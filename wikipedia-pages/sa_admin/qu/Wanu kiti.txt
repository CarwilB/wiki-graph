== Runakuna ==
Wanu kitipiqa [[Puruha]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Wanu kitillipi, Rusaryu kitillipi, Ilapu kitillipi, San Andrés kitillipi, San Isidro de Patulu kitillipi, Valparaíso kitillipipas kawsanku.

==Pulitika Rakiy==
Chunka hukniyuq [[kitilli]]nmi kan: 
* iskay llaqtamanta kitilli: [[Wanu kitilli]], [[Rusaryu kitilli (Chimpurasu)|Rusaryu kitilli]]  (''El Rosario'')
* isqun chakramanta kitilli: Guanando, Ilapu, La Providencia, San Andrés, San José de Chazo, San Gerardo de Pacaicahuan, San Isidro, Santa Fe de Galán, Valparaiso.
