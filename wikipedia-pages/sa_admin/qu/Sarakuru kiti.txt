== Runakuna ==
Sarakuru kitipiqa [[Sarakuru runa|Sarakuru]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa kay kitillikunapi kawsanku: Sarakuru kitilli, Tenta kitilli, Paraiso de Celén kitilli, San Antonio de Cumbe kitilli, El Tablón kitilli, Llusapata kitilli, San Pablo de Tenta kitilli, Selva Alegre kitilli, Urdaneta kitilli. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=286&Itemid=658&lang=kw Saraguro llakta] (kichwa simi) </ref>  <ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20081210084917/http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/sarakuro.htm www.codenpe.gov.ec/sarakuro.htm / Sarakuru] (kastilla simi)</ref>

==Pulitika Rakiy==
11 [[kitilli]]nmi kan: 
* huk llaqta kitilli: Sarakuru kitilli
* 10 chakrapura kitilli: El Paraíso de Celén, El Tablón, Manú, Lluzhapa, San Antonio de Cumbe, San Pablo de Tenta, San Sebastián de Yúluc, Selva Alegre, Urdaneta, '''Sumaypamba'''.
