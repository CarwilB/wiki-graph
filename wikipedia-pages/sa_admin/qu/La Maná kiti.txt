== Runakuna ==
La Maná kitipiqa [[Pansaliyu]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa aswanta [[La Maná kitilli]]pi, [[Guasaganda kitilli]]pi, [[Pukayaku kitilli]]pipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=77&Itemid=114 www.codenpe.gov.ec/panzaleo.htm / Pansaliyu] (kastilla simi) </ref>
