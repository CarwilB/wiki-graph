== Runakuna ==
Pedro Moncayo kitipiqa [[Kayampi]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykuna kay kitillikunapi kawsanku: Tawakuntu kitilli, La Esperanza kitilli, Tukachi kitilli, Malchinguí kitilli, Tupikachi kitilli. <ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20100223025319/http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=73&Itemid=130 www.codenpe.gov.ec / Kayampi]</ref>
== Wiñay kawsay ==
==Pulitika Rakiy==
Pichqa [[kitilli]]nmi kan:
* huk llaqta kitilli: Tawakuntu
* tawa chakrapura kitilli: La Esperanza, Malchingui, Tukachi, Tupikachi.
