==Wiñay kawsay==
Llunpayaq shamuran Piruw presisdente, Ramón Kastilla. Pay ashiq chamuran shumaq hipsahta, kaypa shutin karan Margarita Mariluz. Ishkan kutikaypatin, hankata tikrashana kaykar, warmikaq wañuskiran. Kastilla, llakisha,  hapallan Limaqman aywakuran.
