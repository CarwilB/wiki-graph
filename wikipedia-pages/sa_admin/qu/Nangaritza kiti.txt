== Runakuna ==
Nangaritza kitipiqa [[Sarakuru runa|Sarakuru]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Guayzimi kitillipi Zurmi kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=286&Itemid=658&lang=kw Saraguro llakta] (kichwa simi) </ref>

==Pulitika Rakiy==
Iskay [[kitilli]]nmi kan: 
* huk llaqta kitilli: Guayzimi kitilli 
* huk chakrapura kitilli: Zurmi kitilli.
