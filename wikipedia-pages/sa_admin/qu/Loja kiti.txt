== Runakuna ==
Loja kitipiqa [[Sarakuru runa|Sarakuru]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa San Lukas kitillipi kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=286&Itemid=658&lang=kw Saraguro llakta] (kichwa simi) </ref>

* 13 chakrapura kitilli: Chuquiribamba, Chantaco, El Cisne, Gualel, Jimbilla, Malacatos, Quinara, Santiago, San Pedro de Vilcabamba, San Lucas, Taquil, Willkapampa, Yangana.
