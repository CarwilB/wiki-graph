== Runakuna ==
Zamora kitipiqa [[Sarakuru runa|Sarakuru]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Guadalupe kitillipi Imbana kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=286&Itemid=658&lang=kw Saraguro llakta] (kichwa simi) </ref>

==Pulitika Rakiy==
Qanchis [[kitilli]]nmi kan, huk llaqta kitillim, Zamora, suqta chakrapura kitillipas: Cumbaratza, Guadalupe, Imbana, Sabanilla, San Carlos de las Minas, Timbara.
