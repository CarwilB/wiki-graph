== Runakuna ==
Nabón kitipiqa [[Kañari]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Nabón kitillipi, El Progreso kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20061206225327/http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/kanari.htm www.codenpe.gov.ec/ Kañari] (kastilla simipi)</ref>

Nabón kitillipiqa tawa kichwa llaqtakuna tiyanku: Shiña, Chunasana, Puka, Murasluma.

==Pulitika Rakiy==
Tawa [[kitilli]]nmi kan: Nabón, Kuchapata, El Progreso, Chaya (icha Las Nieves).
