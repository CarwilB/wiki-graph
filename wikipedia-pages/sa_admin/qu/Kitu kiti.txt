== Allpa saywachi ==
[[Rikcha:Cloud forest Ecuador.jpg|thumb|thumb|left|150px|Santa Lusiya, [[Nanegal kitilli]]]]

* '''[[Urqu]]kuna:''' [[Atakasu]] - [[Pichincha]] (Wawa Pichincha nina urqu, Ruku Pichincha urqu) - [[Italó]] nina urqu
* '''[[Mayu]]kuna:''' Saluya mayu (''Saloya'') - Alampi mayu (''Alambí'')
* '''[[Qucha]]kuna:''' Boyeros qucha - Yuyus qucha (''Yuyos'')
* '''[[Phaqcha]]kuna:''' Chirapi phaqcha - Pirawa phaqcha (''La Piragua'')
