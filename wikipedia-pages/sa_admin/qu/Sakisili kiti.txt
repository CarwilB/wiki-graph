== Runakuna ==
Sakisili kitipiqa [[Pansaliyu]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Sakisili kitillipi, Kanchawa kitillipi, Chantilin kitillipi, Kuchapampa kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=77&Itemid=114 www.codenpe.gov.ec/panzaleo.htm / Pansaliyu] (kastilla simi) </ref>

==Pulitika Rakiy==
