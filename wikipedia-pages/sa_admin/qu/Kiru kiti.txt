== Runakuna ==
Kiru kitipiqa [[Tunkurawa Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Kiru kitillipi, Rumipampa kitillipi, Yanayaku kitillipipas kawsanku.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20081105102931/http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/kichwatun.htm Tunkurawa Kichwa runa] (kastilla simipi)</ref>

* iskay chakrapura kitilli:  Rumipampa, Yanayaku-Muchapata (''Yanayacu-Mochapata'').
