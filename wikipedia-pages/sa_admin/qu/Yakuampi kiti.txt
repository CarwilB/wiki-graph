== Runakuna ==
Yakuampi kitipiqa [[Sarakuru runa|Sarakuru]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa 28 de Mayo kitillipi, La Paz kitillipi  Tutupali kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=286&Itemid=658&lang=kw Saraguro llakta] (kichwa simi) </ref>
