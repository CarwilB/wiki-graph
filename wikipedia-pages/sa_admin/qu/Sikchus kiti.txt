== Runakuna ==
Sikchus kitipiqa [[Pansaliyu]] [[Kichwa runa]]kunam tiyanku. Paykunaqa Sikchus kitillipi, Chugchilán kitillipi, Isinliví kitillipi, Pampas kitillipi, Palo Quemado kitillipipas kawsanku. <ref> [http://www.codenpe.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=77&Itemid=114 www.codenpe.gov.ec/panzaleo.htm / Pansaliyu] (kastilla simi) </ref>
