''' Lerdo ''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ciudad Lerdo]]. The municipality covers an area of 1868.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 141,043,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Lerdo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=012|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304132256/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=012|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 129,191 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Lerdo|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10012a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 15, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070510063930/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10012a.htm|archivedate=May 10, 2007}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the city of Lerdo had a population of 79,669.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Lerdo, the municipality had 485 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Nazareno, Durango|Nazareno]] (7,515), [[Ciudad Juárez, Durango|Ciudad Juárez]] (7,069), [[La Loma, Durango|La Loma]] (4,045), [[León Guzmán, Durango|León Guzmán]] (3,335), [[Carlos Real (San Carlos), Durango|Carlos Real (San Carlos)]] (3,021), [[El Huarache (El Guarache), Durango|El Huarache (El Guarache)]] (2,709), [[Villa de Guadalupe, Durango|Villa de Guadalupe]] (2,376), classified as urban, and [[Juan E. García, Durango|Juan E. García]] (2,457), [[Sapioris, Durango|Sapioris]] (1,866), [[San Jacinto, Durango|San Jacinto]] (1,661), [[Los Ángeles, Durango|Los Ángeles]] (1,616), [[La Goma, Durango|La Goma]] (1,522), [[Ejido 21 de Marzo, Durango|Ejido 21 de Marzo]] (1,408), [[Seis de Enero, Durango|Seis de Enero]] (1,372), [[Álvaro Obregón, Durango|Álvaro Obregón]] (1,359), [[La Luz, Durango|La Luz]] (1,285), [[Picardías, Durango|Picardías]] (1,194), [[Las Cuevas, Durango|Las Cuevas]] (1,125), and [[El Rayo, Durango|El Rayo]] (1,030), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
