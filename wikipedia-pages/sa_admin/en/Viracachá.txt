'''Viracachá''' is a town and municipality in the [[Márquez Province]], [[Colombia]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Viracachá is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at {{convert|22|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] and the small urban center at {{convert|2520|m|ft}} above sea level. Other parts of Viracachá reach {{convert|3200|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[Siachoque]], [[Rondón, Boyacá|Rondón]] and [[Soracá]] in the north and east, in the south [[Ciénega, Boyacá|Ciénega]] and Ciénega, Soracá and [[Ramiriquí]] in the west.<ref name=websiteViracacha>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150530131154/http://www.viracacha-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Viracachá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
About the name Viracachá exist different interpretations. One interpretation says the name is derived from [[Quechuan languages|Quechua]] and means "air of the lake".<ref name=websiteViracacha/> Another attributes it to [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] meaning "lord of the enclosure of the wind".<ref>{{cite book |author=Espejo Olaya, Maria Bernarda |year=1999 |url=http://cvc.cervantes.es/lengua/thesaurus/pdf/54/TH_54_003_424_0.pdf |title=Notas sobre toponimia en algunas coplas colombianas |trans-title=Notes about toponomy of some Colombian ballads - Thesaurus |volume=Tomo LIV, Núm. 3. |page=1126 |language=es}}</ref><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Viracachcá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
In the centuries before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] Viracachá was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Due to its proximity to the northern capital [[Tunja|Hunza]], Viracachá was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]''. Modern Viracachá was founded by Dominican missionaries on February 15, 1556.<ref name=websiteViracacha/>

== Economy ==
Viracachá is a rural village where most of the people work in the tertiary sector of services. Also [[agriculture]] and chicken farming is executed. The town does not have a bank.<ref name=websiteViracacha/>
