'''Port-Salut''' ({{langx|ht|Pòsali}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 73,845 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Port-Salut Arrondissement start with the number 82.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Port-Salut Municipality, Sud, Haiti|Port-Salut]]
* [[Saint-Jean-du-Sud]]
* [[Arniquet]]
