'''Cordeiro''' ({{IPA|pt|koʁˈdejɾu}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 22,041 (2020) and its area is 116&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

The literal translation of the city's name in English is "[[wiktionary:Lamb|lamb]]". Among the city's monuments and attractions are the Methodist church, the park of exhibitions and the Cordeiro Social Club.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?lang=&codmun=330150&search=rio-de-janeiro%7Ccordeiro |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em SĂntese |publisher=Cidades.ibge.gov.br |date= |accessdate=2022-08-26}}</ref> The city is known for the Cordeiro Agricultural, Commercial and Industrial Exhibition (''Expo Cordeiro''), considered the oldest exhibition of its kind in Brazil, and for its textile industry.
