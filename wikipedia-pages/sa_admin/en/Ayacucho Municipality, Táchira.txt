The '''Ayacucho Municipality''' is one of the 29 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] that makes up the western [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Táchira (state)|Táchira]] and, according to the [[2011 Venezuelean census]], the municipality had a population of 58,697. The mayor of the municipality is Yoira Vargas De Meza of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela|PSUV]] party since June 2025 and the town of [[Colón, Venezuela|Colón]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Ayacucho Municipality.

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Ayacucho Municipality was 58,697 people, accounting for 4.9% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref> The majority (81.21%) of the municipality's population is in the town of [[Colón, Venezuela|Colón]], the [[municipal seat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/tachira/2003__ayacucho/|title=Urban Localities in Ayacucho (Táchira, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Ayacucho as 62,156 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.72% since 2011 and showing a population density of 122.6 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2003__ayacucho/|title=Ayacucho (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 49.7% men (29,514) and 50.3% women (29,821). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 66.9% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 25.5% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 7.6%. The municipality is mostly urbanized, with 85.5% of inhabitants (50,760) living in [[urban centers]] compared to just 14.5% (8,575) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (59.1%) and [[Mestizo]] (39%). Minority groups included 0.6% [[Afro-Venezuelans]], a small [[Indigenous peoples|indigenous]] population of 26 individuals, and 1.2% belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 93.8%, with 3,068 inhabitants of Ayacucho not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>
