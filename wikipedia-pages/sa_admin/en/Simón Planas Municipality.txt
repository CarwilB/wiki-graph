The '''Simón Planas Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Lara (state)|Lara]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 35,170.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Sarare]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Simón Planas Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Lara.zip {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The municipality was officially founded in 1990.

==Demographics==
The Simón Planas Municipality, according to a 2011 population census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 35,802 (up from 29,521 in 2000).  This amounts to 2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|43.53|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Simón Planas Municipality is Naudy Jesús Ledezma Canelón, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 61% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio SIMON PLANAS | language=es | trans-title=Mayor of Municipality SIMON PLANAS | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis=0&edo=11&mun=9&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229230739/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis=0&edo=11&mun=9&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 - Directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - Directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-16 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Sarare, Buría, and Gustavo Vegas León.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
