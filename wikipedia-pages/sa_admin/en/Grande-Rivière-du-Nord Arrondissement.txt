'''Grande-Rivière-du-Nord''' ({{langx|ht|Grann Rivyè dinò}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord Department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 64,613.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> The arrondissement consists of the [[Grande-Rivière-du-Nord]] and [[Bahon, Haiti|Bahon]] communes.

Postal codes in the arrondissement start with the number 13.{{Citation needed|date=January 2024}}
