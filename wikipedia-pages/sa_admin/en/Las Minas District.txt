'''Las Minas District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Herrera Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 7,945.<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> The district covers a total area of 437&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the city of [[Las Minas, Herrera|Las Minas]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
Las Minas District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*Las Minas (capital)
*[[Chepo, Herrera|Chepo]]
*[[Chumical]]
*[[El Toro, Herrera|El Toro]]
*[[Leones, Herrera|Leones]]
*[[Quebrada del Rosario]]
*[[Quebrada El Ciprián]]
