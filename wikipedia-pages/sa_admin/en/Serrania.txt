'''Serrania''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020, its population was estimated to be 7,668.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/serrania/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> 
The city has an area of 211,48 square kilometers. The [[population density]] is about 37/km<sup>2</sup>. The elevation is 895 meters.

The city belongs to the mesoregion of Sul/Sudoeste de Minas and to the microregion of [[Alfenas]].
