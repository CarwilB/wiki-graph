'''General Güemes''' is the largest and the northernmost [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 62,000 inhabitants in an area of  25,487&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Juan José Castelli, Chaco|Juan José Castelli]], which is located around 1,305&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
