'''Fray Mamerto Esquiú''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]]. This provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|280|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San José de Fray Mamerto Esquiú|San José]]. The department is named after friar [[Mamerto Esquiú]] (1826-1883).
