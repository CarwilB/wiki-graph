'''Tasquillo''' ([[Otomi language|Otomi]]: '''Maxei''') is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. it is located km 5 from the [[Mexican Federal Highway 45]] and km 10 from the nearby city of [[Ixmiquilpan]] in the central west part of the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]].

It has not been possible to clarify whether the word ''Tasquillo'' is of origin Otomí or Nahuatl.<ref name=":2" />

== History ==

=== Pre-Colonial Period ===
Tasquillo was populated by a few ethnic groups which were the [[Nahuas]], [[Toltec]]s and especially the [[Otomi|Otomí]] groups who have given the name "''Mashei''" to the place.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Historia |url=https://tasquillo.gob.mx/historia |access-date=2023-10-25 |website=tasquillo.gob.mx}}</ref> 
Among the most important historical events are the discoveries of curious junk, metals, carved stones or sticks and tools of men who were buried in clay pots. It is said that
these relics are found in the ''[[Huemac]]''.<ref name=":2" />

=== 15th - Early 20th Century ===
In the 15th century under the domination of the [[Mexica]] culture it was named ''Taxco''. Later, with the Spanish conquest in the 16th century, it was given the name "''town of the natives of San Bernardino''," subject to a commission from the [[Marquess|Marquis]] of [[Zúñiga (surname)|Zúñiga]].<ref name=":0" /> and during this time the Spaniards settled at a point called ''Donijá'', which is located southeast of the main square.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Tasquillo Hidalgo |url=http://hidalgotierramagica.jimdofree.com/destinos/corredor-balnearios/tasquillo/ |access-date=2023-10-25 |website=Hidalgo Tierra Mágica |language=es-ES}}</ref>

In the 17th century it was given the name of ''Taxquillo'', a word composed from [[Nahuatl]] or [[Otomi]]. In Nahuatl the word comes from the game of [[Mesoamerican ballgame|''tlachtli'']] which means "''ball game''", In its Otomi dialect the meaning would be "''Place of Goats''", in Spanish the word of ''illo'' means diminutive or small, which gives it another meaning "''Small place of the game of ball"'' or ''"Small place of goats"''.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite book |last=Andrews |first=Enriqueta |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=RwiSvgAACAAJ |title=Vocabulario otomí [de Tasquillo, Hidalgo]: primera parte, Otomí-Español ; segunda parte, Español-Otomí |date=1950 |publisher=Instituto Lingüístico de Verano |language=es}}</ref><ref name=":2" />

According to what is known by the mayoralties, in the 18th century it was not considered among the towns to citizens due to its lack of importance and poverty-rate, since it was simply a neighborhood and municipal seat of the town-hall of [[Ixmiquilpan]].<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://web.uaemex.mx/identidad/docs/cronicas/2021/TOMO_XIX/6.Hernandez-Leal.pdf | title=Tasquillo hidalgo, cuna de héroes anónimos | language=es | trans-title=Tasquillo Hidalgo, cradle of anonymous heroes}}</ref><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2" />

=== 1826 - 1920===
The constitution of the municipality of Tasquillo <ref name=":1" /> is achieved as a town hall belonging to the [[Huichapan]] district of the [[Tula de Allende|Tula prefecture]] on 15 February 1826.

From 1861 to 1869, Tasquillo is recorded as a municipality forming part from the [[Zimapan (municipality)|Zimapán district]] of the [[State of Mexico]] and eventually becoming a municipality of the [[Ixmiquilpan]] district belonging to the [[Tula de Allende|Tula department]] of the State of
Mexico. Once the State of Hidalgo was established in 1869, Tasqullio was designated as the head of the Municipality of its name for the [[Zimapan (municipality)|Zimapan District]], And until 1920 Tasquillo was recorded as a free municipality.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web | url=http://docencia.uaeh.edu.mx/estudios-pertinencia/docs/hidalgo-municipios/Tasquillo-Enciclopedia-De-Los-Municipios.pdf | title=Enciclopedia de los municipios de hidalgo Tasquillo | website=docencia.uaeh.edu.mx}}</ref>

===Late 20th Century - Present===

In 1983 The name of the municipality of Tasquillo is modified replacing ''Taxqullio.'' <ref name=":2" />

Municipal President María de Jesús Chávez ([[Institutional Revolutionary Party|PRI]]) died of [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico|COVID-19]] on January 30, 2021. She took office on December 15, 2020.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Montoya |first1=Juan Ricardo |title=La Jornada - Muere alcaldesa de Tasquillo, Hidalgo, por Covid-19 |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/01/30/estados/muere-alcaldesa-de-tasquillo-hidalgo-por-covid-19/ |access-date=January 30, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=January 30, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
=== Cerro Juarez ===
The [[Cerro Juarez]] peak has an elevation of 3,041.29&nbsp;m (9,978&nbsp;ft).<ref>{{Cite book |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=sIUNAAAAYAAJ&q=Cerro+Ju%C3%A1rez+Tasquillo+Hidalgo |title=Mexico by Motor |date=1950 |publisher=American Automobile Association. |language=en}}</ref> located in the geographic region of the [[Mezquital Valley]].

=== River Tula ===
The [[Tula River]], a tributary from the [[Moctezuma River]] that flows to [[Zimapán|Zimapan]] towards the city of [[Ixmiquilpan]], the river passes in-between Cerro Juarez and the town.<ref>{{Cite book |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=o5P3gOQM-LMC&dq=rio+tula+tasquillo&pg=PA218 |title=Proceedings of The Academy of Natural Sciences (Vol. XCIX, 1947) |publisher=Academy of Natural Sciences |isbn=978-1-4379-5500-2 |language=en}}</ref>

== The Town ==

[[file:V2TasquillioWelcomesYou.jpg |thumb|Main road into Tasquillo where the following says above “Welcome Tasqullio”|255x255px]]

Tasquillo covers an area of 167&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with the town having a population of only 4,030 as of 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tasquillo (Hidalgo) Tasquillo |url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tasquillo/ |access-date=2023-10-24 |website=mexico.PueblosAmerica.com |language=en-US}}</ref>
[[File:Parroquia-san-bernardino-de-siena-tasquillo-scaled.jpg|left|thumb|250x250px|Parroquia of San Bernardino de Siena]]

=== Parroquia San Bernardino de Siena ===
The Parroquia of San Bernardino de Siena is a church located in the center of the town. Which was founded in 1560 and dedicated to the Italian saint [[Bernardino of Siena]]. The church has [[Baroque architecture|baroque]] style architecture and is located in the center of the city. The parish is known for its annual festival in honor of San Bernardino of Siena, which is celebrated on May 20.<ref>{{Cite web  |date=2023-01-15 |title=Parroquia San Bernardino de Siena (Tasquillo)- Horario de misas y servicios |url=https://iglesiasymisas.com.mx/hidalgo/tasquillo/parroquia-san-bernardino-de-siena-tasquillo/ |access-date=2023-10-26 |website=iglesiasymisas.com.mx |language=es}}</ref>

== Municipality ==
The municipality itself has an area of 240.01&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> within the [[Mezquital Valley]] with the municipality having a total population of 17,451 as of 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tasquillo: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tasquillo |access-date=2023-08-18 |website=Data México |language=en}}</ref> It borders with the municipalities of [[Tecozautla]], [[Zimapán]], [[Ixmiquilpan]], [[Alfajayucan]], [[Alfajayucan]] and [[Tecozautla]].
