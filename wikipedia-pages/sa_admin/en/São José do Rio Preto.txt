'''São José do Rio Preto''' ({{IPA|pt|sɐ̃w ʒuˈzɛ du ʁiw ˈpɾetu}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[São Paulo (state)|state of São Paulo]], Brazil. It is located in the north/northwest region of the state, approximately {{convert|445|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the city of [[São Paulo]] and {{convert|700|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Brasília]]. With a population of 504,166 inhabitants,<ref name="IBGE2025"/> by the 2025 estimative of the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], it is the 10th largest city in São Paulo and the 48th [[List of municipalities in Brazil by population|largest in Brazil]]. It has an area of 431.30&nbsp;km²; the urban area, with 124.79&nbsp;km², is the 7th largest in the state and the 30th largest in the country. By the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 IBGE census]], the city had a population of 480,383 inhabitants, with a population density of 1,112.7 people/km².<ref name="CensoIBGE2022"/>

The Metropolitan Region of São José do Rio Preto, with 1,010,633 inhabitants in 2025, is the 30th largest metropolitan area in Brazil, according to the IBGE.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://portal017.com/ibge-regiao-metropolitana-de-rio-preto-supera-1-milhao-de-habitantes-em-2025/ |title=IBGE: Região Metropolitana de Rio Preto supera 1 milhão de habitantes em 2025 |publisher=Portal 017 |access-date=September 10, 2025}}</ref>  In 2020, the [[Mesoregion of São José do Rio Preto]] had a population of 1,569,220; the [[Microregion of São José do Rio Preto]] had a population of 763,534.<ref>{{cite web|title=Censo 2010 of the IBGE|url= http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/sinopse_tab_zip.shtm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110614221649/http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/sinopse_tab_zip.shtm|archive-date=2011-06-14|access-date=2023-08-28}}</ref>

The city was founded in 1852 and elevated to municipality in 1894<ref name="EconConjec2025"/> and has an economic history closely tied to the [[tertiary sector]] and the [[primary sector]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nature and economic progress on the Northwest region of São Paulo (1910–1920) |url=https://www.scielo.br/j/his/a/fr6jB8XzBPHPmPFDRxMSk9S/?lang=en&format=pdf}}</ref> São José do Rio Preto celebrates its anniversary on March 19, [[Saint Joseph's Day]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2012-01-18 |title=Aniversário de Rio Preto será comemorado com shows, seminário e exposições |url=http://www.gruponoticia.com.br/view/?id=18812 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118045519/http://www.gruponoticia.com.br/view/?id=18812 |archive-date=2012-01-18 |access-date=2022-07-17 |website=Groupo Notica}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Vista parcial São José do Rio Preto SP em 1909.jpg|thumb|left|300x300px|São José do Rio Preto in 1909.]]
Prior to the 19th century, the region of São José do Rio Preto was inhabited by the [[Kaingang people|Kaingang]] [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil|indigenous people]]. Over time, their numbers gradually declined as a result of migration, exploratory expeditions known as "[[Bandeirantes|bandeiras]]" and [[miscegenation]] with other groups.<ref>{{cite web |title=The advance in Kaingang lands of São Paulo's West. |url=http://www.portalkaingang.org/index_historia_5.htm |access-date=8 July 2011 |publisher=Portal Kaingang |language=pt}}</ref>

The city was established on March 19, 1852, by João Bernardino de Seixas Ribeiro, a [[Internal migration|migrant]] from [[Minas Gerais]]. Luiz Antônio da Silveira, a local farmer, generously contributed a portion of his land to establish the new city as a tribute to the patron saint, [[Saint Joseph]].<ref name="History">{{cite web |title=History on the Prefecture's Official Site |url=http://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/PortalGOV/do/conhecendoCidade?op=viewForm&coConteudo=11166&coEstruturaPai=12 |access-date=14 June 2011 |publisher=Riopreto.sp.gov.br |language=pt}}</ref>

The municipality of São José do Rio Preto was officially founded in 1894 and the territory ceased to be a part of [[Jaboticabal]]; it was a large territory, limited by the rivers [[Paraná River|Paraná]], [[Rio Grande (Paraná River tributary)|Grande]], [[Tietê River|Tietê]] and [[Turvo River (Grande River tributary)|Turvo]], comprising approximately 26,000&nbsp;km² of land.<ref name="EconomicConjecture2024">{{cite web |title=Economic Conjecture 2024 |url=https://novopainel.riopreto.sp.gov.br/uploads/Conjuntura_Economica_Ingles_compressed_5aa8f56c12.pdf |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |publisher=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=24 September 2025 |language=English |format=PDF}}</ref>
[[File:Mapa da Região no do Estado de S. Paulo Município de Rio Preto - 1, Acervo do Museu Paulista da USP.jpg|thumb|left|Map of the city of Rio Preto in 1917.]]
The railway reached the city in 1912, following the expansion of the Araraquarense Railway (Estrada de Ferro [[Araraquara]]). After the completion, the railway played a crucial role in the development of the city; it transformed the city into a terminal point of [[railroad transport]], of people abroad and agricultural products of Rio Preto and neighboring cities, turning Rio Preto, in 1929, into a growing urban core with circa 27,800 inhabitants<ref name="EconomicConjecture2024"/>

Between 1906 and 1944, the city was known by the [[toponym]] ''Rio Preto''; in 1944, a proposal was made to change the name of the city to ''Iboruna'', meaning "Black River" in the [[Tupi language]], but the proposal was not approved.<ref>{{cite web|title=Iboruna, 158 years|url=http://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/novoportal/Opiniao/Artigos/6832,,Iboruna,+158+anos.aspx|publisher=Diarioweb|access-date=10 July 2011|language=pt|date=21 March 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120326195058/http://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/novoportal/Opiniao/Artigos/6832,,Iboruna,+158+anos.aspx|archive-date=2012-03-26|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[File:Mercado Municipal, São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|left|Municipal Market, inaugurated in 1944.]]
In 1943, the Dr. Fernando Costa Municipal Public Library was inaugurated and in July 19, 1944, the Municipal Market was inaugurated, still in operation.<ref>{{cite web |title=Mercadão comemora 81 anos com programação gratuita neste sábado, 19/7 |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/mercadao-comemora-81-anos-com-programacao-gratuita-neste-sabado |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |publisher=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> In January 1973, the Humberto Sinibaldi Neto Municipal Theater was inaugurated; also in 1973, the construction of the new Cathedral of Saint Joseph began, now the seat of the [[Archdiocese of São José do Rio Preto]].<ref>{{cite web |title=História da Antiga Diocese de São José do Rio Preto |url=https://arquidiocesesjrp.org.br/historia-da-diocese-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto_antiga/ |website=Arquidiocese de São José do Rio Preto |publisher=Arquidiocese de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

In July 19, 1980, in the second term of mayor Adail Vetorazzo, the actual building of the Dr. Fernando Costa Municipal Public Library was inaugurated.<ref name="Library">{{cite web |title=Coluna detalha hoje a construção da Biblioteca Municipal |url=https://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/opiniao/riopretoemfoco/coluna-detalha-hoje-a-construc-o-da-biblioteca-municipal-1.81155 |website=Diário da Região |publisher=Diário da Região |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=21 March 2020}}</ref>

===Toponym===
The name São José do Rio Preto is derived from the combination of the words "São José" ([[Saint Joseph]]) and "[[Preto River (São Paulo)|Rio Preto]]" (Black River), which translates literally as "Saint Joseph of the Black River".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Toponymy and Origins |url=https://library.stanford.edu/all/?q=%22Tanabe%2C+Mari%22 |access-date=2022-08-19 |website=illuminatelocks.com}}</ref> Saint Joseph is the city's patron saint and the Preto is the major river that runs through the city.<ref>{{Cite web |title=História da Diocese de São José do Rio Preto_ANTIGA |url=https://bispado.org.br/historia-da-diocese-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto_antiga/ |access-date=2022-07-18 |website=Diocese de São José do Rio Preto/SP |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

==Geography==
São José do Rio Preto is located in the north/northwest part of the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in a region between the rivers [[Rio Grande (Paraná River)|Grande]], [[Paraná River|Paraná]] and [[Tietê River|Tietê]]. The ecological makeup of the region blends elements of the [[Cerrado]] (Brazilian savanna) with remnants of the [[Atlantic Forest]]. In terms of topography, the area is characterized by gently rolling hills, with an average elevation of {{convert|489|m|ft|abbr=on}}.<ref name="Conjunt" />

===Parks===
[[File:Capivaras no Parque da Represa de São José do Rio Preto - panoramio.jpg|thumb|left|[[Capybara]]s and other animals at the Reservoir Park.]]
The São José do Rio Preto Reservoir Park is located at the eastern part of the city. It was established in 1955 with the aim of enhancing the [[water supply]] and conserving the [[natural environment]] along the banks of the Rio Preto. In 2011, new walking tracks and bike tracks were added, spanning the entire length of the park. Presently, the park boasts over 10,000 native species of local fauna, as well as [[kiosk]]s and free [[outdoor gym]] equipment.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Represa municipal |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/6768/}}</ref> 
[[File:Lago 3 da Represa de São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|right|Third lake of the Reservoir Park.]]
The city has two educational ecological parks: the Educational Ecological Park “Danilo Santos De Miranda” and the Educational Ecological Park “Dr. Joaquim de Paula Ribeiro”, wooded areas with walking tracks, children's playgrounds, focused on the appreciation and integration of the [[Natural environment|environment]], [[leisure]] and[education.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/cartaservicos/educacao/parque-ecologico-educativo |title=Parques Ecológicos Educativos |publisher=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |language=pt |access-date=September 11, 2025}}</ref>
[[File:Zoobotânico de São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|left|Municipal Zoo of São José do Rio Preto.]]
The Municipal Zoo, ''Zoobotânico Municipal'', founded in 1973, counts with a native forest reserve and a [[zoo]] with approximately 300 animals of 70 species in an urban area of 14 [[hectares]] with remnants of [[Atlantic Forest]] and [[Cerrado]] vegetation; it safeguards wild animals that are victims of [[Wildlife smuggling|trafficking]], run overs and fires, as well as fostering orphaned cubs.<ref>{{cite web |title=Zoobotânico de Rio Preto |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/zoobotanico |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=24 September 2025}}</ref>

São José do Rio Preto is the third city with the highest urban [[afforestation]] rate in Brazil; 97.78% of the city's inhabitants lives on streets with trees, according to the 2022 IBGE census.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio Preto é a 3ª cidade do País com mais árvores em vias |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/rio-preto-e-a-3-cidade-do-pais-com-mais-arvores-em-vias |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=19 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=17 April 2025}}</ref>

===Climate===
The climate in São José do Rio Preto is classified as [[Tropical savanna climate|tropical]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Aw]]), characterized by hot and rainy summers, as well as mild and dry winters.

The average annual temperature is around {{convert|23|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. The city typically receives an average annual [[precipitation]] ranging from {{convert|1300|to|1,500|mm|in|abbr=on}}. In 2008, the recorded rainfall reached {{convert|1593|mm|in|abbr=on}}.<ref name="Conjunt">{{cite web|title=Economic Conjuncture of the City, 2010|url=http://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/PortalGOV/do/subportais_Show?c=146|work=Conjuntura econômica de São José do Rio Preto – 2010|language=pt|access-date=15 June 2011}}</ref>

==Economy==
[[File:RicardoBoni aerea S.J.RioPreto-SP (27715337648).jpg|thumb|left|Swift, old historic factory, now a [[Culture|cultural]] and educational center.]]
The [[tertiary sector]] is the main sector of the economy of São José do Rio Preto, representing 84,53% of the total income; the [[secondary sector]] corresponds to 15,19%.<ref name="Seade">{{cite web |title=Municipal Profile at SEADE |url=http://www.seade.gov.br/produtos/perfil/perfilMunEstado.php |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110823080724/http://www.seade.gov.br/produtos/perfil/perfilMunEstado.php |archive-date=23 August 2011 |access-date=5 July 2011 |publisher=Fundação SEADE |language=pt}}</ref> The [[primary sector]] have a small portion of the GDP, accounting for less than 1%. The primary agricultural products in the city includes [[sugarcane]], [[Natural rubber|rubber]], oranges, and maize.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Schneider |first=Ronald M. |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=nQHFDwAAQBAJ&dq=oranges+maize+s%C3%A3o+jos%C3%A9+do+rio+preto&pg=PA8 |title=Brazil: Culture And Politics In A New Industrial Powerhouse |date=2018-02-22 |publisher=Routledge |isbn=978-0-429-97057-3 |language=en}}</ref>
[[File:Riopreto Shopping Center. Entrada da Avenida Brigadeiro Faria Lima, Bairro Jardim Morumbi - panoramio.jpg|thumb|right|Riopreto Shopping Center.]]
In 2021, the GDP of São José do Rio Preto reached [[Brazilian real|R$]]20.9&nbsp;billion, with R$14.5&nbsp;billion from the tertiary sector, R$2.1&nbsp;billion from the secondary sector, R$64.1&nbsp;million from the primary sector and R$1.8&nbsp;billion from [[public administration]].<ref name="EconConjec2025"/> From January to October 2025, exports totaled US$54.9&nbsp;million (R$300.3&nbsp;million), a 35% increase compared to 2024.<ref>{{cite web |title=Exportações de Rio Preto crescem 38% em outubro |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/exportacoes-de-rio-preto-crescem-38-em-outubro |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=9 November 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=7 November 2025}}</ref>

Being a services and commerce center, São José do Rio Preto has five shopping malls: Riopreto Shopping Center,<ref>{{cite web |title=Riopreto Shopping Center |url=https://www.riopretoshopping.com.br/shopping |website=Riopreto Shopping Center |access-date=15 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> inaugurated in 1988, Praça Shopping,<ref>{{cite web |title=Praça Shopping |url=https://pracashopping.com.br/ |website=Praça Shopping |access-date=15 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> inaugurated in 1998, Plaza Avenida Shopping,<ref>{{cite web |title=Plaza Avenida Shopping |url=https://www.plazaavenida.com.br/shopping.asp |website=Plaza Avenida Shopping |access-date=15 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> inaugurated in 2007, Shopping Cidade Norte,<ref>{{cite web |title=Shopping Cidade Norte |url=https://www.shoppingcidadenorte.com.br/shopping.php |website=Shopping Cidade Norte |access-date=15 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> inaugurated in 2012 and Shopping Iguatemi,<ref>{{cite web |title=Shopping Iguatemi São José do Rio Preto |url=https://iguatemi.com.br/saojosedoriopreto |website=Shopping Iguatemi São José do Rio Preto |access-date=15 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> inaugurated in 2014.<ref>{{cite web |title=Chegada de shopping consolida Rio Preto como potência no comércio |url=https://diariodaregiao.com.br/artigo/chegada-de-shopping-consolida-rio-preto-como-potencia-no-comercio-1.2045366 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=23 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=18 March 2025}}</ref>

The [[industrial complex]] of the city comprises three industrial districts and thirteen mini-districts, with the primary employers being [[Small and medium enterprises|small and medium-sized businesses]].<ref name="EconConjec2025" />

The Technological Park Vanda Karina Simei Bolçone was inaugurated in 2018 and is specialized in the areas of health, [[biotechnology]], [[agribusiness]] and design.<ref>{{cite web |title=Parque Tecnológico de Rio Preto é inaugurado |url=https://www.al.sp.gov.br/noticia/?id=375976 |website=Assembleia Legislativa do Estado de São Paulo |access-date=19 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=2 January 2017}}</ref>

According to the Brazilian Human Development Index ([[Human Development Index|HDI]]), São José do Rio Preto was classified as having a high level of development in 2010, with a value of 0.797. This ranking places it at the 50th position out of 5,565 municipalities in Brazil, and 27th out of 645 municipalities in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ranking |url=http://www.atlasbrasil.org.br/2013/ranking |access-date=2022-07-15 |archive-date=2013-08-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130809104924/http://www.atlasbrasil.org.br/2013/ranking |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2025, according to the Firjan Municipal Development Index, São José do Rio Preto was the 8th most developed city in Brazil and the 4th most developed in the state of São Paulo.<ref>{{cite web |title=São José do Rio Preto é 8ª cidade mais desenvolvida do país |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/sao-jose-do-rio-preto-e-8-cidade-mais-desenvolvida-do-pais |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=9 May 2025}}</ref>

===Ethnicities===
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] census of 2022, 67.53% of the population was [[White Brazilians|White]], 25.67% was [[Pardo Brazilians|Mixed]], 5.88% was [[Afro-Brazilians|Black]], 0.85% was [[Asian Brazilians|Asian]] and 0.06% was [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil|Indigenous]].<ref name="CensoIBGE2022"/>

Around 45% of the population of São José do Rio Preto are descendants of Italians who came to the region from the end of the 19th century, around 1880. Since then, the presence of the [[Culture of Italy|Italian culture]] has marked the life of the region and its roots can be seen in the names of streets and [[Neighbourhood|neighborhoods]], in customs, in [[italian language|language]], in [[Catholic Church|religion]], in [[Italian art|art]] and the [[Italian cuisine|gastronomy]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Unesp atua no ensino de italiano em escolas de Rio Preto |url=https://www.ibilce.unesp.br/#!/noticias/v/id::1728/unesp-atua-no-ensino-de-italiano-em-escolas-de-rio-preto/ |website=Instituto de Biociências, Letras e Ciências Exatas – Câmpus de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=15 October 2025 |page=Portuguese |date=5 April 2017}}</ref>

===Religion===
[[File:RicardoBoni Basílica Nossa Senhora da Conceição Aparecida S.J. do Rio Preto SP (40161219864).jpg|thumb|right|Basilica Church of Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception of Aparecida.]]
The city is home to the [[archdiocese of São José do Rio Preto]], created a metropolitan archdiocese by [[Pope Leo XIV]] on May 22, 2025. The diocese is a heritage site situated at the same location where the first chapel was built in 1852. The cathedral serves as the epicenter of the city and houses various artworks, including a sculpture of Saint Joseph dating back to the 19th century.<ref>{{Cite web |title=História de S. José do Rio Preto |url=http://historiabruno.blogspot.com/p/historia-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto.html}}</ref>

According to the 2010 census, the city's population consists of the following religious affiliations: [[Catholic Church|Roman Catholics]] (58.7%), [[Protestantism in Brazil|Brazilian Protestants]] (23.79%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (6.81%), individuals with no religious affiliation (5.57%), [[Jehovah's Witnesses]] (1.47%), [[Atheism|Atheists]] (0.38%), members of the [[The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints]] (0.18%), [[Buddhism|Buddhists]] (0.18%), practitioners of [[Umbanda]] and [[Candomblé]] (0.17%), [[Eastern Orthodox Christians]] (0.07%), and Jews (0.03%).<ref>{{Cite web |date=2015-07-16 |title=Censo 2010 – Lista municípios e religiões |url=http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/censo2010_listareligioes_view.php?editid1=3825 |access-date=2022-07-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150716025710/http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/censo2010_listareligioes_view.php?editid1=3825 |archive-date=2015-07-16 }}</ref>

===Public Security===
[[File:Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto, SP.jpg|thumb|200x200px|City Hall.]]
In 2018, São José do Rio Preto experienced its highest number of [[homicide]]s in 16 years, with 45 recorded murders compared to 47 in 2002.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Santos |first=Marco Antonio dos |title=Rio Preto tem ano mais violento desde 2002 |url=https://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/cidades/riopreto/rio-preto-tem-ano-mais-violento-desde-2002-1.174971 |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=Diário da Região |language=pt-BR}}</ref> The following year, in 2019, the number of homicides decreased to 24 but then increased to 30 in the subsequent year. On the other hand, the number of [[Robbery|robberies]] saw a slight decrease in 2020, with 968 reported cases compared to 1,258 in the previous year.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Carvalho |first=Rone |title=Número de homicídios aumenta, enquanto casos de roubos e furtos caem em Rio Preto |url=https://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/cidades/numero-de-homicidios-aumenta-enquanto-casos-de-roubos-e-furtos-caem-em-rio-preto-1.17611 |access-date=2022-08-20 |website=Diário da Região |date=27 January 2021 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

==Infrastructure==
[[File:Estação de Tratamento de Água de São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|left|Water Treatment Plant.]]
According to the IBGE census of 2022, São José do Rio Preto had 220,173 households with an average number of residents per household of 2.57 people; by the same census, 99.97% of the households had [[bathrooms]], 95.42% had connection to the [[sewage]] network, 99.86% had [[waste collection]] and 91.38% had [[Internet access]].<ref name="CensoIBGE2022"/>
In 2023, according to a study by the Instituto Trata Brasil, São José do Rio Preto had the best [[sanitation]] between de 100 [[List of municipalities in Brazil by population|largest cities in Brazil]], including 100% of the population with access to treated water.<ref>{{cite web |title=Primeira posição e feito inédito: conheça a cidade com melhor desempenho no Ranking do Saneamento 2023 |url=https://tratabrasil.org.br/primeira-posicao-e-feito-inedito-conheca-a-cidade-com-melhor-desempenho-no-ranking-do-saneamento-2023/ |website=Instituto Trata Brasil |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=23 March 2023}}</ref> The city treats 100% of the collected sewage.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio Preto passa a tratar 100% do esgoto coletado |url=https://semae.riopreto.sp.gov.br/rio-preto-passa-a-tratar-100-do-esgoto-coletado/ |website=SeMAE |publisher=SeMAE – Serviço Municipal Autônomo de Água e Esgoto |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=23 March 2023}}</ref>

São José do Rio Preto has no [[favelas]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio Preto sem favela |url=https://diariodaregiao.com.br/artigo/rio-preto-sem-favela-1.993070 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=29 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=6 August 2022}}</ref> The city's last substandard settlement was Favela Marte, which was restructured and urbanized through a pioneering project in Brazil that replaced [[shack]]s without infrastructure with 239 homes for 743 people and inaugurated in 2024 with complete infrastructure.<ref>{{cite web |title=Pioneiro no Brasil, projeto que reconstruiu favela com mais de 200 casas é entregue no interior de SP |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-jose-do-rio-preto-aracatuba/noticia/2024/09/27/pioneiro-no-brasil-projeto-que-reconstruiu-favela-com-mais-de-200-casas-e-entregue-no-interior-de-sp.ghtml |website=Diário da Região |access-date=30 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=27 September 2024}}</ref>

===Health===
[[File:Hospital de Base de São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|left|Base Hospital.]]
The city's main [[public hospital]] is the Hospital de Base (Base Hospital), the second largest hospital in the [[state of São Paulo]] and one of the largest in Brazil. The hospital complex has 8,800 employees, including 1,082 physicians, 641 [[Residency (medicine)|residents]] in 48 [[Medical specialty|specialties]], 3,689 [[nursing]] professionals and 3,388 professionals from various health, [[Health administration|administrative]], and support areas. The institution has 1,318 [[beds]], 1,048 of which are in the ward and 268 in the [[Intensive care unit|ICU]], in addition to the largest [[Surgery|surgical]] center in the [[interior of São Paulo]], with 46 rooms for highly complex procedures and [[Robotic surgery|robotic surgeries]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Hospital de Base de São José do Rio Preto (SP) formaliza adesão ao Mais Acesso a Especialistas |url=https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2025/fevereiro/hospital-de-base-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto-sp-formaliza-adesao-ao-mais-acesso-a-especialistas |website=Ministério da Saúde |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=21 February 2025}}</ref>  Additionally, catering specifically to the healthcare needs of children, the Hospital da Criança e Maternidade (HCM) was established in 2013 and is located next to the Hospital de Base, standing as the largest [[Children's hospital|pediatric hospital]] in Brazil, with 255 beds.<ref>{{Cite web |last=CardioPedBrasil |title=CardioPedBrasil® {{!}} CardioPedBrasil – Centro do Coração da Criança – Hospital da Criança e Maternidade de São José do Rio Preto/ SP ganha selo internacional de qualidade |url=https://www.cardiopedbrasil.com.br/noticias/cardiopedbrasil---centro-do-coracao-da-crianca---hospital-da-crianca-e-maternidade-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto-sp-ganha-selo-internacional-de-qualidade |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=www.cardiopedbrasil.com.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
Other public hospitals are the [[Santa Casa da Misericórdia|Santa Casa de Misericórdia]] de São José do Rio Preto,<ref>{{cite web |title=Santa Casa de Misericórdia de São José do Rio Preto: Institucional |url=https://www.santacasariopreto.com.br/a-santa-casa/ |website=Santa Casa de Misericórdia de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> the Municipal Hospital Dr. Domingo Marcolino Braile<ref>{{cite web |title=Hospital Municipal Dr. Domingo Marcolino Braile: Sobre |url=https://www.hmriopreto.com.br/sobre |website=Hospital Municipal Dr. Domingo Marcolino Braile |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and the State Hospital João Paulo II.<ref>{{cite web|title=Hospital Estadual João Paulo II|url=http://www.franciscanosnaprovidencia.org.br/associacao/nossasobras_detalhes/26/hospital-estadual-jo%C3%A3o-paulo-2%C2%BA |website=Associação e Fraternidade São Francisco de Assis na Providência de Deus |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

The [[private hospital]]s of the city are the Beneficência Portuguesa, that has a [[heliport]],<ref>{{cite web |title=Hospital Beneficência Portuguesa |url=https://beneriopreto.com.br/a-beneficencia-portuguesa/ |website=Beneficência Portuguesa |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> the Austa Hospital,<ref>{{cite web |title=Austa Hospital: Institucional |url=https://austahospital.com.br/institucional/ |website=Austa Hospital |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> the Hospital Santa Helena<ref>{{cite web |title=Hospital Santa Helena |url=https://hospsantahelena.com.br/ |website=Hospital Santa Helena |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and the Hospital Rio Preto Hapvida.<ref>{{cite web |title=Hapvida NotreDame Intermédica inaugura Hospital Rio Preto |url=https://medicinasa.com.br/inauguracao-hospital-rio-preto/ |website=Medicina S/A |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

===Education===
In 2018, primary schools in São José do Rio Preto reported a total enrollment of 48,200 students, while secondary schools recorded 15,335 enrollments. The year 2017 witnessed a total of 27,352 enrollments in higher education institutions.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-07-31 |title=São José do Rio Preto Educa |language=pt-br |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=educa&codmun=354980&nomemun=S%25E3o%2520Jos%25E9%2520do%2520Rio%2520Preto |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731131718/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=educa&codmun=354980&nomemun=S%25E3o%2520Jos%25E9%2520do%2520Rio%2520Preto |archive-date=2013-07-31 |access-date=2023-06-12}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Conjuntura Econômica |language=pt-br |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/arquivosPortalGOV/planejamento/132497_Conjuntura%202018-.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211229151640/https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/arquivosPortalGOV/planejamento/132497_Conjuntura%202018-.pdf |archive-date=2021-12-29 |access-date=2023-06-12 |date=2018}}</ref>

The city is supported by a network of 36 [[State school|public schools]] and 51 private schools, providing education from [[preschool]] to secondary levels. Furthermore, the municipality operates 153 public schools, including elementary, [[Middle school|middle]], and high schools.<ref name="Conjunt" />

[[File:JBC 2023 - Pontos de Vista de um Palhaço. Marcos Morelli SMCS. 26-01-2023 (52652453863).jpg|thumb|left|[[São Paulo State University]] (UNESP).]]

It has a campus of the [[São Paulo State Technological College|FATEC]], which offers programs in technology. The city has a campus of the [[Federal Institute of Education, Science and Technology]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Campus São José do Rio Preto celebra inauguração da nova biblioteca |url=https://www.ifsp.edu.br/ultimas-noticias/5062-campus-sao-jose-do-rio-preto-celebra-inauguracao-da-nova-biblioteca/ |website=Instituto Federal de São Paulo |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=12 May 2025}}</ref> From 2026, São José do Rio Preto will have a campus of the [[Federal University of São Carlos]] with nine courses.<ref>{{cite web |title=Conselho da UFSCar confirma nove cursos para novo campus em Rio Preto; veja lista |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-jose-do-rio-preto-aracatuba/noticia/2025/05/30/conselho-da-ufscar-confirma-sete-cursos-para-novo-campus-em-rio-preto-veja-lista.ghtml |website=G1 |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=30 May 2025}}</ref>

Additionally, the city houses a number of [[private universities]], like UNIP, UNIRP, Faceres, UNORTE and UNILAGO.<ref>{{cite web |title=Enade – Rio Preto |url=https://dhojeinterior.com.br/tres-cursos-em-rio-preto-atingem-nota-maxima-no-enade/ |access-date=15 June 2011}}</ref>

The old railway station, inaugurated in 1912 and deactivated in 2001, became the Railway Museum in 2024. ===Air===
[[File:Inauguração da ampliação e reforma do Terminal de Passageiros e do estacionamento do Aeroporto de São José do Rio Preto (37768849794).jpg|thumb|right|Prof. Eribelto Manoel Reino State Airport.]]
The [[São José do Rio Preto Airport]], Professor Eribelto Manoel Reino, founded in 1959, provides commercial flights operated by three airlines: [[Azul Brazilian Airlines|Azul]], [[Gol Linhas Aéreas Inteligentes|GOL]] and [[LATAM Airlines|LATAM]]. Of the 27 regional airports in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], São José do Rio Preto has the largest passenger traffic; in 2024, it registered the movement of more than 768 thousand passengers, a growth of 5.7% compared to 2023.<ref>{{cite web |title=Com crescimento de 5,7%, Aeroporto de Rio Preto registra movimento de mais de 768 mil passageiros em 2024 |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-jose-do-rio-preto-aracatuba/noticia/2025/01/26/com-crescimento-de-57percent-aeroporto-de-rio-preto-registra-movimento-de-mais-de-768-mil-passageiros-em-2024.ghtml |website=G1 |access-date=2 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=26 January 2025}}</ref> The airport transported 649 tons of cargo in 2024; in 2023, it transported 518 tons of cargo.<ref name="EconConjec2025">{{cite web |title=Economic Conjecture 2025 |url=https://novopainel.riopreto.sp.gov.br/uploads/Conjuntura_Economica_2025_Ingles_processed_9f150f31f3.pdf |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=14 October 2025 |language=English |format=PDF}}</ref> It connects the city to various destinations including [[São Paulo]], [[Campinas]], [[Guarulhos]], [[Cuiabá]] and [[Porto Seguro]]. In 2022, it had a monthly average of over 55 thousand passengers on scheduled flights.<ref>{{cite web |title=Aeroporto de São José do Rio Preto |url=https://aeroportospaulistas.com.br/sao-jose-do-rio-preto/ |website=Aeroportos Paulistas – ASP |access-date=25 September 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==Media==
The major daily newspaper in São José do Rio Preto is the ''Diário da Região'', founded in 1950.<ref>{{cite web |title=A história que escrevemos |url=https://diariodaregiao.com.br/opiniao-e-colunas/a-historia-que-escrevemos-1.2079341 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=22 July 2025}}</ref>

São José do Rio Preto is the headquarters of ''[[Redevida]]'', a brazilian [[catholic]] [[Television broadcaster|television network]]. Redevida offers a diverse range of programming, catering to various interests and demographics. Its content includes Catholic-oriented shows such as TV masses, novenas, and rosaries, as well as news programs, variety shows, sports coverage, and more. The channel reaches audiences throughout Brazil via its affiliates and satellite services, covering a significant portion of the country.<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Fern |first1=Por |last2=Geronazzo |first2=o |date=2020-06-23 |title=Rede Vida comemora 25 anos de serviço à evangelização com identidade católica |url=https://osaopaulo.org.br/destaque/rede-vida-comemora-25-anos-de-servico-a-evangelizacao-com-identidade-catolica/ |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=Jornal O São Paulo |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações de São Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp – April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-06-14|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012. The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History – Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-06-14|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

==Culture==
===Music===
The Symphony Orchestra of São José do Rio Preto (Orquestra Sinfônica de São José do Rio Preto) is a nonprofit organization founded in 1942 and holds the distinction of being the oldest orchestra in the city. It has also been recognized as part of the [[Cultural heritage]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lei Ordinária 13690 2020 de São José do Rio Preto SP |url=https://leismunicipais.com.br/a1/sp/s/sao-jose-do-rio-preto/lei-ordinaria/2020/1369/13690/lei-ordinaria-n-13690-2020-dispoe-sobre-o-tombamento-da-orquestra-sinfonica-de-sao-jose-do-rio-preto-e-da-outras-providencias?r=c |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=leismunicipais.com.br |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

===Literature===
[[File:Biblioteca Municipal de São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|left|Dr. Fernando Costa Public Library.]]
The Dr. Fernando Costa Municipal Public Library currently houses approximately 46,000 volumes. It was officially inaugurated in 1943; the actual building was inaugurated in July 19, 1980, in the second term of mayor Adail Vetorazzo.<ref name="Library"/><ref>{{Cite web |title=[Coluna da Mi Machado] Bibliotecas do Brasil – São José do Rio Preto/SP – Minha Vida Literária |url=https://www.minhavidaliteraria.com.br/2013/12/04/coluna-da-mi-machado-bibliotecas-do-brasil-sao-jose-do-rio-pretosp/ |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=www.minhavidaliteraria.com.br}}</ref> The city also has three smaller public libraries and a [[bookmobile]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Bibliotecas Municipais são opção de cultura e lazer no fim de férias |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/rededebibliotecasmunicipais |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=13 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

===Theaters===
[[File:Teatro Paulo Moura São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|Paulo Moura Municipal Theater.]]
São José do Rio Preto has the Humberto Sinibaldi Neto Municipal Theater, the Paulo Moura Municipal Theater, the Nelson Castro Municipal Theater and the Waldemar de Oliveira Verdi [[Industrial Social Services|SESI]] Theater.<ref>{{cite web |title=Sesi recebe espetáculo 'Emaranhada' no final de semana |url=https://diariodaregiao.com.br/cultura/sesi-recebe-espetaculo-'emaranhada'-no-final-de-semana.1756996919490 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=13 October 2025 |date=4 September 2025 |language=Portuguese }}</ref> Additionally, the city hosts the International Theater Festival (FIT), that completed 56 years in 2025.<ref>{{cite web |title=FIT – Festival Internacional de Teatro de São José do Rio Preto/SP |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/fitriopreto |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>

===Museums===
The city has the Railway Museum,<ref>{{cite web |title=Museu Ferroviário vai preservar memórias e equipamentos da era do trem |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/museu-ferroviario-vai-preservar-memorias-e-equipamentos-da-era-do-trem |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=8 November 2024}}</ref> inaugurated in 2024 in the old railway station, the José Antônio da Silva Museum of [[Primitivism|Primitivist]] Art (MAP), the [[Naïve Art]] Museum (MAN)<ref>{{cite web |title=Secretaria de Cultura |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/secretarias/cultura |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=26 September 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> and the Image and Sound Museum (MIS).<ref>{{cite web |title=Museu da Imagem e do Som: uma Viagem no Tempo |url=https://bemestar.diariodaregiao.com.br/cultura/museu-da-imagem-e-do-som:-uma-viagem-no-tempo.1726301707210 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=2 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=15 September 2024}}</ref>

===Events===
[[File:Lançamento do FIT 2023 em São José do Rio Preto.jpg|thumb|Launch of the International Theater Festival in 2023.]]
* '''Brazilian January of Comedy''': it is a festival of theatrical shows focused on comedy. It lasts eight days, always in January, and the performances are held at the Humberto Sinibaldi Neto Municipal Theater. The first edition was held in 2003.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cultura divulga programação do Janeiro Brasileiro da Comédia 2025 |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/janeirobrasileirodacomedia2025 |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=24 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=13 January 2025}}</ref>
* '''Alberto Bertelli Lucatto Exhibition Grounds''': where the EXPO Rio Preto takes place every year; it is dedicated to the [[agribusiness]] and is the largest [[Agricultural show|agricultural fair]] in Northwest [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]]. It is one of the main ones in Brazil's countryside and had its 62nd edition in 2025.<ref>{{cite web |title=EXPO Rio Preto oferece atrações para toda a família |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/expo-rio-preto-oferece-atracoes-para-toda-a-familia |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=24 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=9 September 2025}}</ref> In April 2026 hosted a [[Guns N' Roses]] show to 30 thousand people.<ref>{{cite web |title=Com hits consagrados, Guns n' Roses levanta o público em Rio Preto
 |url=https://www.diariodaregiao.com.br/cidades/com-hits-consagrados-guns-n-roses-levanta-o-publico-em-rio-preto.1775648656758 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=9 April 2026 |language=Portuguese |date=8 April 2026}}</ref>
* '''International Theater Festival (FIT)''': it is organized by the prefecture in partnership with [[Serviço Social do Comércio|SESC]] and happens in July. Created in 1969 as the National [[Amateur theatre|Amateur Theater]] Festival, in 2001 it expanded its borders and assumed an international dimension.<ref>{{cite web |title=FIT – Festival Internacional de Teatro de São José do Rio Preto/SP |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/fitriopreto |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=24 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
* '''[[Música popular brasileira|Brazilian Popular Music]] Festival''': the Vinícius Nucci Cucolicchio Brazilian Popular Music Festival happens in October in three days at the Humberto Sinibaldi Neto Municipal Theater.<ref>{{cite web |title=Secretaria de Cultura |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/secretarias/cultura |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=31 October 2025 |language=Portuguese}}</ref>
* '''Rio Preto Country Bulls''': it is a [[rodeo]] festival that happens every year in July. In 2025, it was attended by 100 thousand people in five days. It counts with shows from famous [[Sertanejo music|sertanejo]] artists.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rio Preto Country Bulls encerra edição 2025 com público de cerca de 100 mil pessoas ao longo de cinco dias |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/sao-jose-do-rio-preto-aracatuba/noticia/2025/07/14/rio-preto-country-bulls-encerra-edicao-2025-com-publico-de-cerca-de-100-mil-pessoas-ao-longo-de-cinco-dias.ghtml |website=G1 |access-date=31 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=14 July 2025}}</ref>
* '''[[Carnival]] of São José do Rio Preto''': it is a street event, open to the public and held annually during Carnival.<ref>{{cite web |title=Carnaval Rio Preto será de folia em blocos |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/noticias/carnaval-rio-pretense-sera-de-muita-folia-em-blocos |website=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |access-date=25 October 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=24 February 2025}}</ref>

==Sports==
The city is the home of two professional [[Association football|football]] clubs, namely [[América Futebol Clube (SP)|América Futebol Clube]] and [[Rio Preto Esporte Clube]]. América Futebol Clube typically plays their matches at the [[Teixeirão]] Stadium, which has a seating capacity of over 32,000 people.<ref>{{cite web |title=Capacidade dos estádios da A-2 |url=http://www.futebolinterior.com.br/campeonato/paulista-serie_a2-2008/31744+Capacidade_dos_estadios_pode_tirar_acesso_de_times_da_A2 |access-date=6 July 2011 |publisher=Futebol Interior |language=pt}}</ref> Rio Preto Esporte Clube competes at the [[Estádio Anísio Haddad|Anisio Haddad Stadium]] (Rio Pretão) with a capacity of 17,000.

[[File:Jogos Abertos 2024. SMEL 15-12-2024 (54208757193).jpg|thumb|left|Centro Regional de Eventos.]]
The major public sports arena is the Centro Regional de Eventos (Regional Events Center), with a capacity of 5,600 attendants.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.riopreto.sp.gov.br/interesses/estrutura-para-eventos#regional |title=Centro Regional de Eventos |publisher=Prefeitura de São José do Rio Preto |language=pt |access-date=September 11, 2025}}</ref> The Centro Esportivo Integrado do Eldorado (Eldorado's Integrated Sports Center) has an official [[Running track|athletics track]] and other sports facilities.<ref>{{cite web |title=Pista de atletismo do Eldorado, em Rio Preto, volta a receber competição |url=https://diariodaregiao.com.br/esportes/pista-de-atletismo-do-eldorado-em-rio-preto-volta-a-receber-competic-o-1.1002707 |website=Diário da Região |access-date=15 November 2025 |language=Portuguese |date=9 September 2022}}</ref>

São José do Rio Preto is also the home of ''Rio Preto Weilers'', an [[american football]] team. Established in 2010 by a group of passionate enthusiasts, their aim was to promote the sport in the city and other regions of Brazil. The Weilers participate in the [[Superliga Nacional de Futebol Americano|Liga BFA – Elite]], and have already secured two national titles and one state title.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Rio Preto Weilers |url=https://www.salaooval.com.br/times/rio-preto-weilers/ |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=Salão Oval |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Fernandes |first=Henrique |date=2019-07-18 |title=Rio Preto Weilers se consolida como melhor do Estado de São Paulo |url=https://www.gazetaderiopreto.com.br/esportes/noticia/2019/07/rio-preto-weilers-se-consolida-como-melhor-do-estado-de-sao-paulo.html |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=Gazeta de Rio Preto |language=pt-br}}</ref> Moreover, the team actively supports alternative sports groups for individuals with [[Disability|disabilities]], including [[wheelchair basketball]], and swimming. These groups consists of members from the philanthropic organization ''Clube Amigos dos Deficientes'' (Friends of the Disabled Club), who also competes at the national level.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2012-01-18 |title=CAD – Clube Amigos dos Deficientes |url=http://www.cad.org.br/noticias |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118011409/http://www.cad.org.br/noticias |archive-date=2012-01-18 |access-date=2022-07-15}}</ref>
