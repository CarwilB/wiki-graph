'''San Miguel''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]  for "[[Michael (archangel)|Saint Michael]]") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It was founded on August 10, 1896.
[[File:Universidad de Valparaíso - A740054.jpg|thumb|[[University of Valparaíso]]]]
== History ==
The history of San Miguel dates back to the 16th century when Gaspar Banda, assistant to the conquistador [[Diego de Almagro]], created a hermitage to Saint Michael the Archangel in this territory in 1575. In 1881, the Iglesia de San Miguel Arcángel was built on the same site as the 1575 hermitage.

At the beginning of the 19th century, the place emerged as an agricultural area, with wheat, barley and [[Chilean wine|wine plantations]]. The land was owned by [[Bernardo O'Higgins]], liberator and father of the Chilean nation, who used it as his vacation home. 

[[Ramón Subercaseaux Vicuña|Ramón Subercaseaux]] brought wine strains directly from [[Bordeaux]], [[France]], and his wines acquired international prestige, notably at the [[Exposition Universelle (1889)|Exposition Universelle of 1889]] in [[Paris]]. His 1840 house is still preserved as a cultural center and is open to the public. 

The [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of San Miguel was founded on August 10, 1896. At the beginning of the 20th century, the area began to be urbanized in the style of a [[Garden city movement|European garden city]], with large mansions, parks, squares and many trees in the streets. In later years, development followed a more middle-class urban style. 

In the civic square in front of the city hall, a statue of [[José Miguel Carrera]]. The statue was designed by [[Augustin-Alexandre Dumont|Auguste Dumont]], the same sculptor who made the statue of [[Napoleon]] on the [[Place Vendôme|Vendôme Column]] in [[Paris]].

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], San Miguel is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The alcalde's office (''alcaldía'') is located at ''Gran Avenida No. 3418''. The 2024–2028 alcalde is Carol Bown Sepúlveda ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]). The communal council has the following members:
* Luis Sanhueza Bravo ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Felipe Guevara Stephens ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Claudia Rojas Miranda ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Eva Merino Garcés ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Claudio Escobar Oyarzún (Ind)
* Gabriel Zúñiga Aravena ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Viviana Llambias Miranda ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Carla Santana Bustamante ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])

San Miguel belongs to the 28th [[Electoral divisions of Chile|electoral division]] (together with [[Pedro Aguirre Cerda, Chile|Pedro Aguirre Cerda]] and [[Lo Espejo]]) and the  8th [[Electoral divisions of Chile|senatorial constituency]] (Santiago East). It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] of the [[National Congress of Chile|National Congress]] by the deputies [[Guillermo Teillier|Guillermo Teillier del Valle]] ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]]) and Pedro Browne Urrejola (RN). It is represented in the Senate by senators [[Soledad Alvear]] (PDC) and [[Pablo Longueira]] (UDI).

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Miguel spans an area of {{convert|9.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 78,872 inhabitants (37,836 men and 41,036 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 4.8% (3997 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The [[demonym]] for a person from San Miguel is ''sanmiguelino'' for a man or ''sanmiguelina'' for a woman.
