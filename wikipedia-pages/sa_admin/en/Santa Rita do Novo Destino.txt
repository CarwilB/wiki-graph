'''Santa Rita do Novo Destino''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northeastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location==

Santa Rita do Novo Destino is 82&nbsp;km. east of [[Ceres, Goiás|Ceres]] and  15&nbsp;km. north of [[Goianésia]]. Highway connections from [[Goiânia]] are made by taking  GO-080 / [[Nerópolis]] / [[São Francisco de Goiás]] / BR-153 / [[Jaraguá, Goiás|Jaraguá]] / GO-080 / [[Goianésia]] / GO-438.  [https://web.archive.org/web/20160303190259/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Seplan]

Neighboring municipalities:  [[Hidrolina]], [[São Luíz do Norte]], [[Barro Alto, Goiás|Barro Alto]], and [[Santa Isabel, Goiás|Santa Isabel]]

==Demographics==

*Urban population: 1,156
*Population density: 3.53 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup> (2007)
*Population growth rate: 1.56% 2000/2007

==The Economy==

The economy is based on [[subsistence agriculture]], cattle raising, services, public administration, and small transformation industries.  
In 2006 there were 476 farms with a total area of 70,126 hectares.  Of this pasture land made up 48,000 hectares.  There were 48,000 head of cattle.  The main crops were rice, sugarcane (2,450 hectares), manioc, corn (1,000 hectares), and soybeans (1,500 hectares).

==Health and Education==

The adult literacy rate was 78.8% (2000) (national average was 86.4%) and the infant mortality rate was 33.10 (2000) (national average was 33.0).  In 2007 there were no hospitals and there were 5 schools.
