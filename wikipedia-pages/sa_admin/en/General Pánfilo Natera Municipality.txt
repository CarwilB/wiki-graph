'''General Pánfilo Natera''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|50|km}} east of [[Zacatecas (city)|the state capital]]. It is named after {{ill|Pánfilo Natera|es}}, commander of the Central Division of the [[Constitutional Army]] in the [[Mexican Revolution]], and governor of Zacatecas in 1915 and from 1940 to 1944.<ref>{{cite book |last=Rodríguez Valadez |first=Juan Manuel |title=Zacatecas: Historia de las instituciones jurídicas |url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/2.pdf |publisher=[[Senate of the Republic (Mexico)]] |location=Mexico |date=16 August 2010 |chapter-url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/11.pdf |chapter=Gobernantes de Zacatecas |language=es |access-date=16 June 2021}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of General Pánfilo Natera is located at an elevation between {{convert|2000|and(-)|2700|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in southeastern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Villa González Ortega]] to the southeast, [[Ojocaliente, Zacatecas|Ojocaliente]] to the southwest, [[Trancoso Municipality, Zacatecas|Trancoso]] to the west, and [[Guadalupe Municipality, Zacatecas|Guadalupe]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32016.pdf |title=General Pánfilo Natera, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=16 June 2021}}</ref> It also borders the municipality of [[Villa de Ramos]] in the state of [[San Luis Potosí]] to the northeast. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|442.1|km2}} and comprises 0.6% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 47.2% of the land in Pánfilo Natera is used for agriculture. The remainder of the land comprises [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (35.3%), grassland (7.6%), [[mesquite]] shrubland (4.5%), and urban areas (1.3%). The municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of [[Llanos el Salado|El Salado]]. Apart from a small mountainous area in the north of the municipality, its terrain is generally flat. There are no permanent rivers in the municipality. <ref name=compendium/>

Pánfilo Natera has a temperate semi-arid climate with dry winters. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The municipality of General Pánfilo Natera is situated in an area that was originally inhabited by the [[Guachichil]], one of the [[Chichimeca]] nations. The first Spanish settlement in the area was established in 1763. After Mexican independence, the area was first part of the state of San Luis Potosí, but was annexed to the municipality of Ojocaliente in Zacatecas in 1857.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32016a.html |title=General Pánfilo Natera |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=16 June 2021 |archive-date=24 February 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210224175617/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32016a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

On 21 July 1928, the municipality of La Blanca was established, separating it from Ojocaliente. The municipality was renamed in honour of General Pánfilo Natera on 11 November 1964.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
The municipal government of General Pánfilo Natera comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and thirteen trustees (''regidores''), eight elected by [[relative majority]] and five by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Freddy González Vázquez.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=16 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipality of General Pánfilo Natera recorded a population of 23,526 inhabitants living in 6040 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |page=46–47 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2016 |accessdate=16 June 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 22,346 inhabitants in Pánfilo Natera.<ref name=snim/>

[[INEGI]] lists 44 localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which three are classified as urban:<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=016 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de General Pánfilo Natera |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=16 June 2021}}</ref>
* the municipal seat, also called General Pánfilo Natera, which recorded a population of 4894 inhabitants in the 2020 Census;
* El Saucito, located {{convert|8|km}} north of the municipal seat, which recorded a population of 3370 inhabitants in the 2020 Census; and
* San José el Saladillo, located {{convert|7|km}} east of the municipal seat, which recorded a population of 2959 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
General Pánfilo Natera is primarily an agricultural municipality. The main crops grown are corn, beans and chiles.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 49|Federal Highway 49]] runs through the municipality, connecting it to the cities of [[Zacatecas, Zacatecas|Zacatecas]] in the west and [[San Luis Potosí, San Luis Potosí|San Luis Potosí]] in the southeast.
