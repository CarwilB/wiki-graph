'''Actopan''' is a [[municipality (Mexico)|municipality]] located at {{coord|19|30|N|96|37|W|}} in the montane central zone in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 50&nbsp;km from the state capital of [[Xalapa]]. Actopan, the municipal seat, is a small city located near the capital of the state. The region is one of the most important producers of mangoes in the state.

=== Geographic limits ===
The municipality of '''Actopan''' is delimited to the east by the [[Golfo de Mexico]], to the south by the [[Emiliano Zapata, Veracruz|Emiliano Zapata]], to the west by the [[Naolinco]], and to the north by [[Alto Lucero de Gutiérrez Barrios|Alto Lucero]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30004a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Actopan |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es |access-date=2009-06-16 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070403065221/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30004a.htm |archive-date=2007-04-03 }}</ref> It is watered by the river Actopan, which it is born in Cofre de Perote, ends in the [[Gulf of Mexico]], forming the Barra de Chachalacas.

===Weather===
The weather in ''' Actopan ''' is warm and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, Actopan had a population of 41,762 inhabitants in 194 localities with 10% of the population residing in the municipal seat (4,607 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSACTOPAN>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Actopan.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Actopan CENSUS]</ref> Other localities includes Mozomboa (3,329 hab.), Coyolillo (2,394 hab.), Tinajitas (2,038 hab.) and Santa Rosa (1,975 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSACTOPAN/>

==Agriculture==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] chayote squash and the most important the [[mango]] production.

==Celebrations==
In '''Actopan''', the celebration in honor to San Francisco de Asís, Patron of the town, takes place in October, and the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe takes place in December, also a Mexican celebration called El dia de los muertos which means Day Of the dead.
