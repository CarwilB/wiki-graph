'''Breña''' ({{IPA|es|ˈbɾeɲa|lang}}) is a [[List of districts of Lima|district of Lima]], [[Peru]]. It is the second smallest district of the city,<ref>{{Cite news |title=Este es el distrito más grande y el más pequeño de Lima |date=2025-01-16 |url=https://elcomercio.pe/respuestas/trends/cual-es-el-distrito-mas-grande-y-pequeno-de-lima-la-respuesta-te-sorprendera-tdpe-noticia-2/ |work=[[El Comercio (Peru)|El Comercio]]}}</ref> and its creation dates back to the demolition of the [[Walls of Lima|city walls]] ordered by president [[José Balta]] during the late 19th century, which led to the [[Urbanization|broad expansion]] of the city.

== Etymology ==
The district is named after an hacienda of the same name that used to be located in the area.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Breña: por qué se llama así este distrito y cuál es su historia |last=Jimenez |first=Mery |date=2024-04-05 |url=https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/04/05/brena-por-que-se-llama-asi-este-distrito-y-cual-es-su-historia/ |work=[[Infobae]]}}</ref>

== History ==
On December 2, 1869, president [[José Balta]] signed the decree that ordered the demolition of the [[Walls of Lima|city walls]], constructed during the city's [[Viceroyalty of Peru|Spanish period]] to protect it from possible attacks by pirates. According to an 1880 map by P. V. Jouanny, the city ended to the west at the [[Avenida Alfonso Ugarte|Avenida de la Circunvalación]], which had been built over the space occupied by the former walls.

The district was created through Law-Decree N° 11059, signed by president [[Manuel A. Odría]] on July 15, 1949. It is named after the former ''hacienda'' of the same name. The district originally included the neighbourhoods of Breña, Chacra Colorada, Garden City, Azcona, Chacra Ríos, Dos de Mayo, Conde de las Torres and Wiese, being reduced in size 10 years later, with only Breña, Chacra Colorada, Garden City and Azcona remaining in the district.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Historia |url=http://www.munibrena.gob.pe/distrito/historia.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161227061028/http://www.munibrena.gob.pe/distrito/historia.html |archive-date=2016-12-27 |website=Municipalidad de Breña}}</ref>

== Politics ==
Breña is under the jurisdiction of its own [[Municipalities of Peru|district municipality]], as well as that of the [[Metropolitan Municipality of Lima]].
