'''Chiriquí Grande  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chiriquí Grande District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. It is the seat of Chiriquí Grande District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|58.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,014 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|51.2|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 7,637; its population as of 2000 was 2,069.<ref name=inec/> The town is located on the southern shore of [[Chiriquí Lagoon]] and its port serves as the northern terminus of the [[Trans-Panama pipeline]].
