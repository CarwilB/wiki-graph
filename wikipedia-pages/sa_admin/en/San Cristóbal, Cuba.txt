'''San Cristóbal''' is a municipality and city which since 2011 has been included in [[Artemisa Province]] of Cuba. Spread over an area of {{cvt|934.9|km2}}, it is located on the foothills of the [[Sierra de los Órganos]] (Organ mountains) in western Cuba. The city was founded by [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonial settlers as an agricultural center. 
The municipality became internationally significant in October 1962 during the [[Cuban Missile Crisis]], after Soviet missile sites were first photographed by United States reconnaissance aircraft at San Cristóbal.

==History==
The city was founded during the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonial period with the first settlers coming from [[Cadiz]] in Spain and the [[Canary Islands]]. The early settlers developed it into an agricultural region, growing [[tobacco]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://havanatimes.org/diaries/fabiana-del-valle/san-cristobal-from-cultural-flourishing-to-decline/|title=San Cristobal: From Cultural Flourishing to Decline |work=Havana Times|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> In the 18th century, several [[coffee]] plantations were established due to the presence of fertile soil in the region.<ref name="Cuba"/>{{rp|page=197}} San Cristóbal was one of the termini of an old military road built during the beginning of the Cuban Republic.<ref name="Cuba"/>{{rp|page=329}}

=== Cuban missile crisis ===
[[File:Cuba Missiles Crisis U-2 photo.jpg|thumb|left|Reconnaissance image of missile bases under construction at San Cristóbal taken on 14 October 1962]]

The municipality became internationally significant in October 1962 during the [[Cuban Missile Crisis]]. The first photographic evidence of the Soviet missile launch sites were discovered on 14 October 1962, when a [[Lockheed U-2]] aircraft piloted by [[Richard Heyser]] captured images of a missile construction site at San Cristóbal.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://future.state.gov/educators/slideshow/cuba/cuba2.html|title=Cuban Missile Crisis|publisher=U.S. Department of State|access-date=6 May 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100527171609/http://future.state.gov/educators/slideshow/cuba/cuba2.html|archive-date=27 May 2010}}</ref> This led to the further [[naval blockade]] of Cuba by the United States, and its subsequent face-off with the Soviet Union. The crisis was resolved later after the Soviet missiles were withdrawn and the United States withdrew its naval blockade in 1963.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://history.state.gov/milestones/1961-1968/cuban-missile-crisis|title=Cuban Missile Crisis|work=[[Government of United States]]|access-date=1 May 2021}}</ref>

==Geography==
San Cristóbal is located in the [[Artemisa Province]] and covers an area of {{cvt|934.9|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/artemisa/2211__san_crist%C3%B3bal/|title=San Cristóbal |work=City Population|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|work=Statoids|date=July 2003|access-date=1 June 2007}}</ref> It is located on the foothills of the [[Sierra de los Órganos]] (Organ mountains) in western Cuba.<ref name="Cuba">{{cite web|url=https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/41267/pg41267-images.html|title=The History of Cuba, vol. 5|author=Willis Fletcher Johnson}}</ref>{{rp|page=14}} about {{cvt|92|km}} west of the Cuban capital of [[Havana]].<ref name="Cuba"/>{{rp|page=329}} The [[San Cristóbal Municipal Museum]] was established on 31 May 1983.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnpc.cult.cu/institucion/391|title=Museo Municipal de San Cristóbal|lang=es|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had a reported population of 71,098 inhabitants in 2022. The population consisted of 35,641 males and 35,457 females. About 11,784 inhabitants (16.4%) were below the age of fourteen. Majority (68.7%) of the population was classified as urban, and the rest (31.3%) as rural.<ref name="CP"/>
