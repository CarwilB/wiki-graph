'''Capaya''' is a town and [[parishes of Venezuela|parish]] in  [[Acevedo Municipality, Miranda]], Venezuela. It is located about {{convert|12|mi|km}} east north east of the Caribbean Sea. Capaya is at an elevation of {{convert|259.2|ft|m|abbr=off|sp=us}} in relation to sea level. As of 2011 census the town's population was 13,151.<ref name="INE">{{Cite web |date=2014 |title=XIV CENSO NACIONAL DE POBLACIÓN Y VIVIENDA: Resultados por Entidad Federal y Municipio del Estado Miranda | language=Spanish |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/miranda.pdf |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística}}</ref>
