'''La Capilla''' is a town and municipality in the [[Eastern Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is situated at an altitude of {{convert|1600|m|ft}} in the [[Tenza Valley]].<ref name=websiteLaCapilla>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150602142705/http://www.lacapilla-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website La Capilla]</ref>

== Borders ==
{{main|Tenza Valley}}
* North with [[Úmbita]] and [[Pachavita]]
* South with [[Tenza]] and [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]
* West with Cundinamarca
* East with Pachavita and Tenza

== Etymology ==
La Capilla was originally called La Capilla de Tenza, after the [[Virgin of Candelaria|Our Lady of Candle]] [[Marian apparition|appeared]] in the outskirts of the village. Because of this, a chapel was built; hence the name Capilla.<ref name=websiteLaCapilla/>

== History ==
The first inhabitants of the region of La Capilla were the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], who were organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. The people of the area of La Capilla were loyal to the ''[[cacique]]s'' of [[Garagoa]], [[Sutatenza]] and [[Somondoco]] in the [[Tenza Valley]]. [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|Conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] and his troops passed through this valley in 1537, on their way to the domains of the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. Modern La Capilla was founded on November 13, 1793, by Juan de la Cruz Aguirre.<ref name=websiteLaCapilla/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of La Capilla is agriculture, with [[cucumber]]s, [[tomato]]es and [[bean]]s cultivated.<ref name=websiteLaCapilla/>
