'''Paso de Indios Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].
[[Image:LosAltares-96.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Highway 25, near ''Los Altares'']]
The provincial subdivision has a population of about 1,905 inhabitants in an area of 22,300 km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Paso de Indios]], which is located around 1,729 km from the [[Capital federal]].
