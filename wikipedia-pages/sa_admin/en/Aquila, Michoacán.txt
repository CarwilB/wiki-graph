'''Aquila''' ({{IPA|es|aˈkila}}) is a town in the southwest part of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Michoacán]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Aquila: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/aquila |access-date=2024-11-05 |website=Data México |language=en}}</ref> It is the municipal seat for the municipality of Aquila.  It is 23&nbsp;mi/43&nbsp;km southeast of [[Tecoman]]. Its population was 1,915 in 2002. It is located at Latitude: 18°35'N Longitude: 103°31'W and at elevation 989&nbsp;ft/510 m. It has harsh terrain.  Agriculture consists of small farming, mainly for [[subsistence economy|subsistence]]. There is one paved road south to Mexico Highway 200 (5&nbsp;mi/11&nbsp;km away).
