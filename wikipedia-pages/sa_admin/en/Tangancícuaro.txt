'''Tangancícuaro''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]].

The municipal seat is the City of Tangancícuaro de Arista.

==Geography==
Tangancícuaro is at an altitude of 1,700 meters above sea level, with an area of 387.95&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its population was estimated at 33,815 in 1996 and has a slightly negative population growth attributed to emigration to the interior and outside of the country.

It is bordered on the north by [[Zamora (Michoacán)|Zamora]], [[Jacona, Michoacán|Jacona]] and [[Tlazazalca]], to the east by [[Purépero]] and [[Chilchota (municipality)|Chilchota]], to the south by [[Charapan]], [[Los Reyes, Michoacán|Los Reyes]]  and [[Tingüindin]], and to the west by [[Tangamandapio]].

Its distance to the Michoacán State Capital is 134 kilometers.

Natural features include the Duero River, Lake Junguarán, Lake Camécuaro, and Lake Cupatziro.
==Economy==
The economy is based on agriculture with some industry and tourism.
==Tourism==
Tourist destinations include [[Lago de Camécuaro National Park]], the location of Lake Camécuaro (''Lago de Camécuaro'').
