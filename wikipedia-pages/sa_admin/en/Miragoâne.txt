'''Miragoâne''' ({{IPA|fr|miʁaɡɔan}}; {{langx|ht|Miragwàn}}) is a coastal [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in western [[Haiti]] and the capital of the [[Nippes]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]]. It is also the headquarters of the [[Miragoâne Arrondissement]]. It is regarded as one of the major ports in the trade in used goods.  Bales of used clothing, shoes, appliances and used cars arrive at the port from [[Miami]] and other U.S. cities. Local merchants in the [[informal sector]] buy boxes and bales of used goods to sort and resell them in street markets. Inexpensive merchandise is thus dispersed around Haiti.

==History==
Miragoâne was founded in the 17th century by the English.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Miragoane in the past |url=http://www.miragoane.org/la-ville-d-hier/ |access-date=2025-12-07 |website=www.miragoane.org |language=en}}</ref> It was once used as a rest stop for pirates.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Neglected Haitian city bears scars, pain of poverty |url=https://www.latinamericanstudies.org/haiti/miragoane.htm |access-date=2025-12-07 |website=www.latinamericanstudies.org}}</ref> The city is named after the nearby lake, the Étang de Miragoâne, from the Taino name Miraguano.<ref name=":0" /> 

The port was used by [[Reynolds Metals]] aluminum for export of bauxite which was mined inland between the 1960s and 1980s. It has been the site of a proposed collaboration between [[Max Hardberger]] and the Bigio family's [[GB Group (conglomerate)|GB Group]] for further port development.

In 1963, Miragoâne was devastated by [[Hurricane Flora]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Raymont |first=Henry |date=October 11, 1963 |title=Haiti Storm Relief Lags; Economy Is Threatened |url=https://www.nytimes.com/1963/10/11/archives/haiti-storm-relief-lags-economy-is-threatened.html |access-date=December 7, 2025 |work=The New York Times}}</ref> 

In the midst of [[Haitian crisis (2018–present)|Haiti's gang crisis]], Miragoâne's prosecutor, [[Jean Ernest Muscadin]], has become a prominent Haitian figure due to his approach towards gang members, leading to some calls for him to run for president in 2026.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Saint-Louis |first=Fredner Cayemitte, Daniella |date=2025-11-18 |title=Blood on his hands, votes in his future? Muscadin’s popularity surges as UN accuses him of extrajudicial killings |url=https://haitiantimes.com/2025/11/18/muscadin-un-report-extrajudicial-killings/ |access-date=2025-12-07 |website=The Haitian Times |language=en-US}}</ref>

==2024 explosion==
On September 14 2024, A fuel tank overturned and exploded while bystanders were trying to collect gasoline killing 26 people and injuring 40. <ref>{{Cite web | title=The number of people killed in Haiti after a tanker truck exploded rises to 26 {{!}} AP News | url=https://apnews.com/article/haiti-truck-explosion-killed-miragoane-56e3fe9bf34bdf3535a5daca98e6ced3 | access-date=2025-10-05 | website=apnews.com}}</ref>
