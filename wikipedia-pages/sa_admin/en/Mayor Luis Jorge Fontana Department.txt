'''Mayor Luis Jorge Fontana''' is a southern [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 53,500 inhabitants in an area of  3,708&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Villa Ángela]], which is located around 182&nbsp;km from the provincial capital. The department takes its name from [[Luis Jorge Fontana]], the first governor of [[Chubut Province|Chubut]] and an explorer of the region.
