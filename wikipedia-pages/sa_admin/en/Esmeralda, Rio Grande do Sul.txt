'''Esmeralda''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It was raised to municipality status in 1963, the area being taken out of the municipality of [[Vacaria]].<ref name="lei4616"/>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,287.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/esmeralda/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
