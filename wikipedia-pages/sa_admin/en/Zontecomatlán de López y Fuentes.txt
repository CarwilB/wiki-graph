''' Zontecomatlán ''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 378&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 216.33 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|20|46|N|98|20|W|}} in the [[Huasteca]] region of the state.

== Name ==
The name comes from the [[Nahuatl]] language, Tzon-tecoma-tlan; 'head,' 'big round gourd' 'place' that means "place of the big heads". The suffix "de López y Fuentes" honours [[Gregorio López y Fuentes]], a writer and journalist born here in 1897.<ref name="e-local"/>

==Geography==

The municipality of Zontecomatlán is bordered to the north by the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]], to the north-east by [[Benito Juárez (municipality of Veracruz)|Benito Juárez]] and to the south by [[Texcatepec]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30202a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Zontecomatlán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-18|archive-date=2006-11-16|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061116011647/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30202a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Climate===

The climate in Zontecomatlán is warm-humid with an average temperature of 18°C, with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[marijuana]], [[maize]], [[beans]], [[green chile]], [[coffee]], [[orange fruit]] and [[sugarcane]].

==Celebrations==

Every October,  a festival is held to celebrate San Francisco de Asís, patron of the town and in December there is a festival held in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].
