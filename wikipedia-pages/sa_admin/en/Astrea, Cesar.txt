'''Astrea''' is a municipality and town of the [[Department of Cesar]] in northern [[Colombia]].<ref name="gobcesar.gov.co">[http://www.gobcesar.gov.co/infonoticia.asp?idnot=979 {{in lang|es}} Government of Cesar: Astrea] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071023211304/http://www.gobcesar.gov.co/infonoticia.asp?idnot=979 |date=October 23, 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality of Astrea limits to the north with the municipality of [[El Paso, Cesar|El Paso]] and northwest with [[Magdalena Department]]; to the south with the municipality of [[Chriguana, Cesar|Chiriguana]]. It has an extension of {{cvt|580|km2}}. The average temperature throughout the year is 30&nbsp;°C.

The region is part of the geological Zambrano formation, formed in the [[Neogene]] presenting a gray-greenish [[clay]].<ref>[http://ssiglims.igac.gov.co/ssigl/geoportal_de_colombia/galeria_icde/ICDE/INGEOMINAS/DEPARTAMENTALES/Cesar.pdf {{in lang|es}} Agustin Codazzi Geographic Institute: Geological Map of Cesar]{{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==

Founded by a farmer named [[Martín Mier Batista]] in 1936 when a group of farmers from the [[Magdalena Department]] arrived seeking better pastures.<ref name="gobcesar.gov.co" />

After the creation of the Department of Cesar in 1964, Astrea became a corregimiento of the municipality of [[Chimichagua]]. On November 26, 1984, Astrea segregated from Chimichagua and became a municipality of the Department of Cesar by Ordinance 013 of 1984.<ref name="gobcesar.gov.co" /><ref>{{in lang|es}} Cesar 30 Años de Progreso - Gobernación del Cesar (1997). -Page 21-]</ref>

==Economy==

[[Ranching]] and [[agriculture]] are the main economic activity in the region having the town of Astrea as the main urban center of the municipality. Agricultural products are commercialized in the town such as; [[Cassava|yuca]], [[sesame]], [[maize|corn]] and [[common bean]] among others.<ref name="gobcesar.gov.co" />

==Culture==

Astrea celebrates the ''Día de San Antonio de Pádua'' ([[Anthony of Padua|Saint Anthony of Pádua]]) Day in the corregimiento of Arjona, every year on June 16. Astrea also celebrates a traditional feast honoring the ''Día de San Martín'' ([[St. Martin's Day]]) every November 11.<ref name="gobcesar.gov.co" />
