==Neighboring municipalities==
Neighboring municipalities are [[Altar, Sonora|Altar]] to the northwest, [[Hermosillo]] to the south, [[Trincheras]] to the east, and [[Caborca]] and the [[Gulf of California]] to the west.  The town of Pitiquito is located in the north of the municipality on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highway 2]].
