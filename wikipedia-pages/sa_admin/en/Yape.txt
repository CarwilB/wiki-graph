'''Yape''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 187 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 229; its population as of 2000 was 159.<ref name=inec/>   It is located along the [[Tuira River]], and not served by any paved roads.<Ref name="villages">(30 April 2008).
