'''Utcubamba''' (hispanicized spelling), in [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Utkhupampa''' (''utkhu'' [[cotton]], ''pampa'' a large plain,<ref>{{Ref Laime}}</ref><ref>Diccionario Quechua - Español - Quechua, Academía Mayor de la Lengua Quechua, Gobierno Regional Cusco, Cusco 2005 (Quechua_Spanish dictionary)</ref> "cotton plain"), is one of seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]]. It was created by Law#-23843 on May 30, 1984. Its capital is [[Bagua Grande]] and its principal attraction is the Tourist Corridor of Utcubamba where the valley becomes notably closer forming "the [[canyon of Utcubamba]]". These conditions modify the climate in a substantial way and create a radical ecological shift. The  area is fresh and fragrant and the [[Orchidaceae|orchid]]s that are bountiful are unique in the world. Notably picturesque cascades are observed in the rocky vertical walls that the river has created. There are [[hot spring]]s a few meters from El Ingenio bridge.

The selection of this corridor lies in the beauty of the scenery and in the tourist activities that can be generated in it, like [[canoeing]], minor [[watercraft]] navigation, [[fishing]] and [[Water sport (recreation)|recreation sports]].
