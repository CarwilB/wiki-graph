'''{{lang|pt|Congonhas do Norte}}''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of {{lang|pt|[[Minas Gerais]]}}. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] {{lang|pt|[[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]]}} and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of {{lang|pt|[[Conceição do Mato Dentro]]}}.
