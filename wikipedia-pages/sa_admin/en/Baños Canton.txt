'''Baños Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Tungurahua Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Baños de Agua Santa|Baños]]. The population of Baños Canton at the 2010 census was 20,018.,<ref>[http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/wp-content/descargas/Manu-lateral/Resultados-provinciales/tungurahua.pdf Official Ecuador census]</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php?adm1id=1802|title=Ecuador: Municipal Division (Provinces and Parishes) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map}}</ref> and in 2011 its population had risen to 21,140<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-admin.php?adm2id=1802|title=Baños de Agua Santa (Canton, Ecuador) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|website=www.citypopulation.de|language=en|access-date=2018-04-11}}</ref>
