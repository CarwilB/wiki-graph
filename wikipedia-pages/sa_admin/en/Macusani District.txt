'''Macusani District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Carabaya Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its seat is [[Macusani]].

== Geography ==
The district lies along the [[Cordillera Carabaya]], a Peruvian Andean range that runs north–northeast of the town of Macusani.<ref>{{cite web |title=Mapa: Ubicación geográfica del ámbito de influencia de la cordillera Carabaya |url=https://www.gob.pe/institucion/inaigem/informes-publicaciones/783286-mapa-ubicacion-geografica-del-ambito-de-influencia-de-la-cordillera-carabaya |website=Gob.pe |publisher=INAIGEM |date=March 1, 2018 |language=es |access-date=3 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite encyclopedia |title=Cordillera de Carabaya |url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Cordillera-de-Carabaya |encyclopedia=Encyclopædia Britannica |access-date=3 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Cordillera Carabaya – Inventario Turístico |url=https://consultasenlinea.mincetur.gob.pe/fichaInventario/index.aspx?cod_Ficha=437 |website=Mincetur |publisher=Ministerio de Comercio Exterior y Turismo (Perú) |language=es |access-date=3 October 2025}}</ref>
The highest local summit is [[Allincapac]], at about {{convert|5,780|m|ft|abbr=on}}.<ref>{{cite journal |last=Biggar |first=John |title=South America, Peru, Cordillera Carabaya, Various Ascents |journal=American Alpine Journal |year=2006 |url=https://publications.americanalpineclub.org/articles/12200625202/South-America-Peru-Cordillera-Carabaya-Various-Ascents |access-date=3 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Peru 2015–16: Area Notes |url=https://www.alpinejournal.org.uk/Contents/Contents_2017_files/AJ%202017%20312-325%20Peru%20Area%20Notes.pdf |website=Alpine Journal |year=2017 |access-date=3 October 2025}}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (70.66%) learnt to speak in childhood, 28.51% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
