'''Malvinas Argentinas Partido''' is a ''[[partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], in the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area. It has an area of {{convert|63.8|km2|sqmi|1|abbr=on}} and  according to the preliminary results of the 2010 Census, the population was 321,833 inhabitants. ({{census-ar|2010}}).<ref name=Cen2010 /> Its capital is the city of Malvinas Argentinas,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.malvinasargentinas.gob.ar/ellugardelafamilia|publisher=Partido de Malvinas Argentinas|language=es|accessdate=20 January 2026|title=Nuestro Distrito}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://normas.gba.gob.ar/documentos/VJJ5pRSM.html|title=Ley 15209|date=2012|publisher=Gobierno de la provincia de Buenos Aires|language=es|accessdate=20 January 2026}}</ref> and its largest city is [[Grand Bourg]].

Its name reflects Argentina's disputed claims of [[Falkland Islands sovereignty dispute|sovereignty]] over the [[Falkland Islands]] (''Islas Malvinas'' in Spanish). It was created on October 20, 1994 by Provincial Law #11551, seceding from the ''partido'' of [[General Sarmiento Partido|General Sarmiento]], which was dissolved the following year.

Its former capital, [[Los Polvorines]], was founded on the [[Ferrocarril General Manuel Belgrano]] railway, along with the neighboring town of [[Del Viso]]. The union of the lands around these two train stations originated the district.

By Decree #4520, the federal government on 17 December 1908, created the train stop ''Los Polvorines'', which name came from the ''Sargento Cabral'' munitions depot (''polvorín'' in [[Spanish language|Spanish]]), established by the [[Argentine Army]] nearby.
