'''Taquaruçu do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,077.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/taquarucu-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It is located at a latitude of 27º24'00" South and a longitude of 53º28'02" West, being at an altitude of 545 meters above sea level.
