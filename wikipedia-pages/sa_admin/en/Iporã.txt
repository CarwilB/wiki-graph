'''Iporã''' is a [[municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northwest of the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], approximately sixty kilometers from the border with [[Paraguay]]. Its population in 2020 was estimated to be 13,782.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/ipora/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
