'''Nueve de Julio Partido''' (9 of July) is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the central north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 46,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4230|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Nueve de Julio, Buenos Aires|9 de Julio]], which is around {{convert|262|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Name==
The name 9 de Julio was inspired by July 9, 1816, [[Argentine Declaration of Independence|Argentine Independence Day]], the date that Argentina gained its independence from the [[Spanish Empire]].
