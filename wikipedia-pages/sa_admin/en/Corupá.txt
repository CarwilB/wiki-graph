'''Corupá''' is a municipality of the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], located in the [[South Region of Brazil]]. It has approximately 16,000 inhabitants distributed over 407&nbsp;km². It is located in the Valley Itapocu near [[Joinville]]. Its economy is based on the metallurgical industry and in the production of bananas.
