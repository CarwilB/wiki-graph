'''Camargo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The population in 2020 was 2,742, in a land area of {{Convert|138.1|km²||}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/camargo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==History==
The early European migrants here arrived in the region around [[Marau]] from the [[Antônio Prado]] region, but their [[:it:Imigração italiana no Rio Grande do Sul|earlier European origins]] were in [[Italy]].  The area was one of [[Plainplains]] interspersed with low hills and bordering a river, was at the time inhabited by [[Caboclo|Caboclo people]].  The river was called the Camargo, and that is the name that was adopted for the European settlement.

By the early twentieth century, the Italian immigrants had imported the customs and habits of their ancestral homes, and the settlement was being laid out and organized along traditional Italian lines.
