'''Plaisance''' ({{IPA|fr|plɛzɑ̃s|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Eihel-plaisance.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Plezans}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Plaisance Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 50,367 inhabitants.

Morne Bedoret is a nearby peak which is 543 m high.<ref>[http://peakery.com/morne-bedoret/ Morne Bedoret] accessed 20 May 2012</ref> Fort Bedourete was here.
