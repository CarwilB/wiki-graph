'''Barracão''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|1332|ha|adj=on}} [[Espigão Alto State Park]] created in 1949.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PES de Espigão Alto – ISA}} |title=PES de Espigão Alto|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/586372|access-date=2017-01-16}}</ref>
