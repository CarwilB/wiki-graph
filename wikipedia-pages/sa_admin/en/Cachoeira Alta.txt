'''Cachoeira Alta''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in south [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  The population was 12,666 inhabitants in 2020 in a total area of 1659.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.  Cachoeira Alta has one of the largest cattle herds in the state.

==Location==
Cachoeira Alta is located in the [[Quirinópolis Microregion]] in an unpopulated area of the south of the state.  Some of the largest municipalities in total area are located here.  There are road connections with [[Aparecida do Rio Doce]], 57&nbsp;km. to the north; and [[Paranaiguara]], 37&nbsp;km. to the southeast.

The distance to the state capital, [[Goiânia]], is 356&nbsp;km.  Highway connections are made by BR-060 / [[Guapó]] / [[Indiara]] / [[Acreúna]] / [[Rio Verde, Goiás|Rio Verde]]  / GO-174 / GO-422 / [[Aparecida do Rio Doce]] / [[BR-364]]. See [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin]

Neighboring municipalities are:
*north and east: [[Quirinópolis]] and [[Aparecida do Rio Doce]]
*south and west: [[Caçu]]
*east:  [[Paranaiguara]]

==The economy==
The economy is based on cattle raising for meat and milk production, and agriculture, especially the planting of corn and rice.  The cattle herd was 163,000 head including 12,000 milking cows in 2006.

==History==
The first European settlement on record occurred in 1876 when Manoel Batista Barroso, travelling medicine salesman, and Gabriel Paula do Amaral, settled on the right bank of the Cachoeira Alta River, five kilometers above where it meets the Rio Claro.  In 1920 the town had grown and was officially called Cachoeira Alta, because of the river.  In 1931 it became a district belonging to [[Rio Verde, Goiás|Rio Verde]], being raised to municipal status in 1953.
