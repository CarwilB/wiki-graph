'''Mecatlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north zone of the state, about 327&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 48.53 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|20|13|N|97|41|W|}}.

Mecatlán is delimited to the north by [[Coyutla]], to the north-east by [[Chumatlan]], to the north-west by [[Coahuitlan]] to the south-east by [[Coxquihui]] and to the south-west by [[Filomeno Mata]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30103a.htm | title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Mecatlán | publisher=Gobierno Estatal | work=veracruz.gob.mx | access-date=2009-06-10 | language=es}}</ref>

According to the INEGI official statistics, in 2020 year the total population in Mecatlán was about 12,799 persons (49.2% men and 50.8% women). Compared with the 2010 year, the total population has grown about an 8.39%. It produces principally [[maize]] and [[coffee]].

In Mecatlán, in September there is a celebration in honor of [[Michael (archangel)|archangel Michael]], patron of the town.

The weather in Mecatlán is warm all year, with rains in summer and autumn.
