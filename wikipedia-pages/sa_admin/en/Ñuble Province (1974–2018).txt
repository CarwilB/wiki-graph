'''Ñuble Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Ñuble}}, {{IPA|es|ˈɲuβle|pron}}) was one of the [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Bío Bío Region|Bío Bío]] (VIII). It used to span an area of {{convert|13178.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and it was administratively constituted by 21 communes. It has in 2017 a population of 441,604 inhabitants. Its capital was the city of [[Chillán]]. On the 6th of September of 2018, the province became the [[Ñuble Region]].

==History==
The province was created in 1974 from the territory of the historical [[Ñuble Province (1848-1974)|province of Ñuble]]. Two years later, the communes of [[Coelemu]] and [[Ránquil]] from Concepción Province, and the commune of [[Tucapel]] from [[Bío Bío Province]], were added.
 
Following the 8.8 magnitude [[2010 Chile earthquake|earthquake and tsunami]], at least eight small communities and two towns were abandoned by residents, who took up makeshift camps in the hills, fearful of further tsunamis. Over 800 residences in the town of [[Quirihue]] were destroyed, leaving little for townspeople to return to.<ref>{{cite news|work=[[Reuters]] |url= http://uk.reuters.com/article/idUKN03219039._CH_.2420 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160303220636/http://uk.reuters.com/article/idUKN03219039._CH_.2420 |url-status= dead |archive-date= 3 March 2016 |title=Fearing tsunamis in Chile, hundreds hide in hills |author= Mica Rosenberg |date=3 March 2010 |accessdate= 4 March 2010}}</ref>

In 2015 the Ñuble Region law which converts the Ñuble Province into a [[Ñuble Region|Region]], was proposed.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.latercera.com/noticia/senado-aprueba-proyecto-crea-region-nuble/|title=Senado aprueba proyecto que crea la región de Ñuble|publisher=}}</ref> In August 2017 a law to create the new territorial division was promulgated by the President of the Republic in Chillán.<ref name="latercera">{{cite web|title=Con firma de Bachelet hoy nace Región de Ñuble|url=http://www.latercera.com/noticia/firma-bachelet-hoy-nace-region-nuble/|website=latercera|accessdate=21 August 2017}}</ref><ref name="biobiochile">{{cite web|title=Presidenta Bachelet firma decreto: Ñuble se convierte en la XVI región del país|url=http://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/nacional/chile/2017/08/19/region-del-nuble-presidenta-bachelet-tendra-intensa-agenda-en-la-nueva-capital-regional.shtml|website=biobiochile.cl|accessdate=21 August 2017}}</ref> It became operational on 6 September 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.leychile.cl/Navegar?idNorma=1107597|title=LEY-21033 05-SEP-2017 MINISTERIO DEL INTERIOR Y SEGURIDAD PÚBLICA - Ley Chile - Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional|date=5 September 2017|publisher=}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Ñuble was a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]] of Chile, governed by a provincial [[governor]] who was appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]].
 
===Communes===
The province used to comprise 21 [[Communes of Chile|communes]], each governed by a municipality consisting of an elected [[alcalde]] and [[municipal council]].
