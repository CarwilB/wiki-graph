The '''municipality of Ombúes de Lavalle''' is one of the municipalities in the [[Colonia Department]], [[Uruguay]]. Its seat is the city of [[Ombúes de Lavalle]].

== Location ==
The municipality lies in the north section of the department, and limits to its southeast with the [[Municipality of Colonia Miguelete|Colonia Miguelete]] Municipality and to its north with [[Soriano Department]].

== History ==
The municipality of Ombúes de Lavalle was created by Departmental Law N° 014/2013 of 22 March 2013 of the Departmental Board of Colonia, before the proposal of the [[Intendant (government official)|Intendant]] of Colonia, following the provisions of the Law N° 18567 and its later reforming Laws.<ref name=L18567>{{cite web|url=https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/leyes-originales/18567-2009|title=Ley N° 18567 (Documento original)|language=es|publisher=IMPO|accessdate=17 August 2020}}</ref><ref name=L18653>{{cite web|url=https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/leyes-originales/18653-2010|title=Ley N° 18653 (Documento original)|language=es|publisher=IMPO|accessdate=17 August 2020}}</ref>

== Territory ==
According to the Departmental Law N° 014/2013 the limits of the municipality's territory  match those of the NGA and NGB constituencies of Colonia Department.<ref name=DEC0142013>{{Cite web |url=http://www.juntacolonia.gub.uy/images/stories/decretos/2013/014.pdf |title=Decreto 014/2013 |language=es|accessdate=17 August 2020|publisher=Junta Departamental de Colonia |date=22 March 2013}}</ref><ref name=L19319>{{cite web|url=https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/leyes/19319-2015|title=Ley N° 19319. APROBACION DE LA NOMINA DE MUNICIPIOS Y DISTRITOS ELECTORALES|language=es|publisher=IMPO|accessdate=17 August 2020}}</ref>

== Authorities ==
The authority of the municipality is the Municipal Council, integrated by the Mayor (who presides it) and four Councilors.
