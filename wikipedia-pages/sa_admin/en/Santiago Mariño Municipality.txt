The '''Santiago Mariño Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 211,010.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Turmero]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Santiago Mariño Municipality and '''[[Chuao]]'''  where some of the finest [[cocoa bean]]s in the world are produced.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is named for Venezuelan independence hero [[Santiago Mariño]].

==Demographics==
The Santiago Mariño Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 191,731 (up from 165,436 in 2000).  This amounts to 11.5% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|385.78|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Chuao==
'''Chuao''' is a small village founded in the 16th century famous in the world for its [[Theobroma cacao|cacao]] plantations. The village is surrounded by [[mountain]]s and dense [[rainforest]]s to the south [[Caribbean Sea]] near the [[Henri Pittier National Park]]. There is no road access and visitors must come by boat from the town of Puerto Colombia along the coast, or by foot, crossing the mountains and the luxurious [[cloud forest]] from [[Turmero]] near [[Maracay]].

In the Chuao plantation there are currently pure Criollo and [[Hybrid (biology)|hybrid]] varieties of cacao being grown. Criollo beans from Chuao are of very high quality, and are considered Venezuela's finest beans together with [[Porcelana Blanca]] beans from [[Lake Maracaibo]] (another genetically pure variety of Criollo).  [[Amedei]], an Italian chocolate maker, and [http://www.chocolatenaive.com Chocolate NAIVE], a Lithuanian bean-to-bar chocolate maker, offer chocolate bars made with Chuao cacao. Naive was the winner of the European gold medal  at the [http://www.internationalchocolateawards.com International Chocolate Awards] 2014.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://chocolatenaive.com/awards/ |title=Awards &#124; Naive |access-date=2014-07-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140604071740/http://chocolatenaive.com/awards/ |archive-date=2014-06-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.internationalchocolateawards.com/2014/05/european-semi-final-winners-2014/|title = European Semi-final 2014 Winners - International Chocolate Awards}}</ref>

In November 2000, the cacao beans coming from Chuao region were awarded an [[Appellation d'origine contrôlée|appellation of origin]] under the title '''"Cacao de Chuao"''' (from Spanish ''Cacao de Chuao'')<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.wipo.int/en/web/ip-advantage/w/stories/branding-matters-the-success-of-chuao-cocoa-bean|title=Branding Matters: The Success of Chuao Cocoa Bean|website=ip-advantage}}</ref> effectively making this one of the most expensive and sought after types of cacao.
