'''Jaíba''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil]]ian [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  {{as of|2020}} the population was 39,388 in a total area of {{cvt|2,733|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/jaiba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The elevation is {{convert|475|m}}.  It became a municipality in 1993. The postal code (CEP) is 39508–000.

Jaíba is part of the statistical microregion of [[Janaúba]]. It is connected by paved MG-401 to the regional center of Janaúba to the south. The distance is {{cvt|63|km}}.

With irrigation Jaíba has managed to produce a range of agricultural products. The main economic activities are cattle raising (62,000 head in 2006) and farming with production of bananas ({{cvt|2,000|ha}}), citrus fruits, mangoes ({{cvt|200|ha}}), rice, beans ({{cvt|3,480|ha}}), corn ({{cvt|1,175|ha}}), and sorghum ({{cvt|500|ha}}). In 2006 there were 2,173 rural producers with a total area of {{convert|118,741|ha}}. Cropland made up {{convert|18,000|ha}} and natural pasture {{convert|57,000|ha}}. There were only 186 tractors.

In the urban area there were two financial institutions {{as of|2006|lc=y}}. There were 852 automobiles, giving a ratio of about one automobile for every 35 inhabitants. The Gross Domestic Product was R$117,218,000 (2005). Health care was provided by 12 public health clinics and one hospital with 25 beds (2005).
