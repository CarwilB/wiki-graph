'''Ajuterique''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua (department)|Comayagua]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|61.67|km2}} and had a population of 11,356 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
The town of Santiago de Axuterique was established in 1550, about a [[League (unit)|league]]'s distance from the cathedral at [[Comayagua]]. It was granted to Marcos de Reyna, with the land measurements were carried out by Josef Calderón de la Barca. It was placed under the protection of [[James the Great|San Santiago]], with the friar Francisco de Cañaveral in charge of it. On 30 January 1693, five and half [[Caballería|caballerías]] of land was sold for 440 [[Tostones#Other uses of the term|Tostones]]. It suffered damage during an earthquake in 1774. The town was elevated to a status of a municipality in 1865. During the [[Second Honduran Civil War]] in 1924, forces of Gregorio Ferrera fought against the government forces of president [[Vicente Tosta Carrasco]] at Ajuterique. The town suffered damages due to flooding of the Ganzo River, several times since the mid 20th century.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Ajuterique, Comayagua|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Comayagua/Ajuterique.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Ajuterique|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-ajuterique/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Ajuterique is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua Department|Comayagua]] in Honduras.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://proceso.hn/impulsa-desarrollo-en-ajuterique-comayagua-con-millonaria-inversion-social/|title=Impulsan desarrollo en Ajuterique, Comayagua, con millonaria inversión social|work=Proceso Digital|date=27 February 2017|access-date=26 November 2025|language=es|trans-title=They promote development in Ajuterique, Comayagua, with millionaire social investment}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|61.67|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/comayagua/0302__ajuterique/|title=Ajuterique, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Ajuterique has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|23.75|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|274.89|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/comayagua/ajuterique|title=Ajuterique, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Ajuterique had a total population of 11,356 inhabitants, of whom 5,517 (48.6%) were men and 5,839 (51.4%) were women. About 50.1% of the population was classified as urban and 5,669 residents (49.9%) lived in the rural areas.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 3,780 individuals (33.3%) were aged 0–14 years, 6,932 individuals (61.0%) were aged 15–64, and 645 individuals (5.7%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 22.4 years and the mean age was 26.6 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 13.8%, slightly lower than the departmental average of 14.6%. The municipality had 2,552 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.4 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
