The '''Arzobispo Chacón Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 15,850.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sintesise Stadistica |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls |website=www.ine.gob.ve}}{{dead link|date=November 2023}}</ref> The town of [[Canaguá]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Arzobispo Chacón Municipality.<ref name="ine.gob.ve">{{Cite web |title=Merida Division |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip |website=www.ine.gob.ve}}{{dead link|date=November 2023}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Arzobispo Chacón Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 15,850 (up from 15,586 in 2000). This amounts to 1.9% of the state's population.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Poblacion 5 |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls |website=www.ine.gob.ve}}{{dead link|date=November 2023}}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|9.4|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Poblacion 4 |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls |website=www.ine.gob.ve}}{{dead link|date=November 2023}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Arzobispo Chacón Municipality is Carlos Andrès Chacòn Mora, elected on October 31, 2004, with 61% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Regionales 2004 |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=3&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |website=www.cne.gob.ve}}</ref> He replaced Gerardo Duran shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into seven [[parish]]es; Capital Arzobispo Chacón, Capurí, Chacantá, El Molino, Guaimaral, Mucutuy, and Mucuchachí.<ref name="ine.gob.ve" />
