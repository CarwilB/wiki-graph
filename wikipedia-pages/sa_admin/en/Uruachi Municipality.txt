'''Uruachi ''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Uruachi]]. The municipality covers an area of 3,058.31&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 8,200,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Uruachi|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=066|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=27 February 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210227071252/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=066|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 7,934 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Uruachi|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08066a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223204135/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08066a.htm|archive-date=February 23, 2007|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Uruachi had a population of 1,199.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Uruachi, the municipality had 422 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
