'''Corongo''' is one of 20 provinces of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]]. Its capital is [[Corongo]] located at an altitude of 3,141&nbsp;m (10,305&nbsp;ft).

Corongo is situated in a calm valley where the river of the same name runs. It is bordered by the following provinces: to the north and to the west by [[Pallasca Province|Pallasca]], to the east by [[Sihuas Province|Sihuas]] and to the south by [[Huaylas Province|Huaylas]] and [[Santa Province|Santa]]. At the time of census 2005 the province had 7,786 inhabitants,<ref>[http://www1.inei.gob.pe/ www1.inei.gob.pe, Banco de Información Distrital]</ref> distributed in the urban zone and mainly in the countryside.

Corongo has a great variety of places to visit, especially lakes located in the high parts of the province, and the thermal baths of Aticara and Pacatqui.

The geographical coordinates are 8º30'34" S and 77º54'37" W.

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (90.35%) learnt to speak in childhood, 9.44% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
