'''Pinheiros''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 27,327 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|975|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>  The capital of this municipality is located at an altitude of 130 meters above sea level.

The municipality contains the {{convert|2357|ha}} Córrego do Veado Biological Reserve, a strictly protected area named after the [[Veado River (Santo Antônio River)|Veado River]], which flows through the reserve from west to east.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Reserva Biológica do Córrego do Veado – Viarural}}|url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-do-veado/|title=Reserva Biológica do Córrego do Veado|work=Viarural|accessdate=2016-04-15|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160423135655/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-do-veado/|archive-date=2016-04-23|url-status=usurped}}</ref><ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ...
