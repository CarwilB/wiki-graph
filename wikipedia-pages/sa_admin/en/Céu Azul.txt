'''Céu Azul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in the [[Southern Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="ibge"/>

Céu Azul covers {{Convert|1,179.5|km2|mi2}}, has a population of 11,819, and population density of 9.35 resident per square kilometer. {{Convert|852|km2|mi2}} of the city is preserved as [[Atlantic Forest]] and is part of [[Iguaçu National Park]].<ref name="ibge"/><ref name="p"/>

The western region of Paraná was the target of logging companies in the 1950s. The company Pinho e Terras Ltda. installed a colony in the area in 1952 and brought workers, mainly from the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The first residents were primarily of German and Italian origin.<ref name="p"/>
