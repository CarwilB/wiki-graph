'''Iguaraci''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2020, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 12,247 inhabitants and the total area is 838.12&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/iguaracy/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The municipality, has one of the best children's [[mortality rate]] (5.7) of all state and, one of the best in Brazil.

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Ingazeira]], [[Tuparetama]] and [[Tabira, Pernambuco|Tabira]]   ('''N''');  [[Custódia]] and [[Sertânia]]   ('''S''');  [[Tuparetama]] and [[Paraíba]] state  ('''E''');  [[Afogados da Ingazeira]]    ('''W''')

* Elevation - 571 m

* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - Hot tropical
* Annual average temperature - 23.8 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 356&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Iguaraci''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goats]], [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chicken]]s;  and plantations of [[maize|corn]] and [[bean]]s.
