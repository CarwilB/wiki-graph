'''Manaquiri''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]].

==Demography==
Its population was 33,049 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/manaquiri/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 3,976&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Manaquiri is located in a portion of the southern [[Várzea forest]] of the [[Amazon River]]. The main waterway of the Amazon is located around 8 km (5 mi) north of the municipality.

==Transport==
By road, Manaquiri is connected via the {{interlanguage link|AM-354|pt}} highway.
