'''Guadalupe Victoria''' is a [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[states of Mexico|state]] of [[Puebla]]. According to the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|National Statistics Institute]] (INEGI), it had a population of 15,041 inhabitants in the 2005 census. Its total area is 239.83&nbsp;km². It is named after [[Guadalupe Victoria]], the first [[president of Mexico]].

Its geographical coordinates are 19° 17′ North, and  97° 20′ West. Its average altitude is {{convert|2440|m|ft|0}} [[above sea level]]. Its highest elevation is the [[rhyolitic]] [[twin peak|twin]] [[Lava dome|dome]] [[volcano]] [[Las Derrumbadas]] (3480 m).
