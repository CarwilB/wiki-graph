== Demographics and data ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (72.16%) learnt to speak in childhood, 27.31% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref> According to the Foreign Trade Society of Peru (Comex), the district had one of the highest incomes in Peru as of 2023 due to mining and governmental contracts, though its citizens live in poor conditions due to the mismanagement of funds.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Nomberto |first=Isabel |date=2023-07-29 |title=Ni San Isidro ni La Molina: ¿cuál es el distrito más rico del Perú? |url=https://larepublica.pe/datos-lr/respuestas/2023/07/27/ni-san-isidro-ni-la-molina-cual-es-el-distrito-mas-rico-del-peru-san-marcos-ancash-evat-1303172 |access-date=2023-07-30 |website=[[La República]] |language=es}}</ref> The majority of citizens do not have access to sewer drainage, nearly 15% of children suffer from malnutrition and 90% of roads are unpaved.<ref name=":0" />
