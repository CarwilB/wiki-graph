'''Tauramena''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Tauramena has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with moderate to little rainfall from December to March and heavy to very heavy rainfall from April to November.
