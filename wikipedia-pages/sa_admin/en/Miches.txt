'''Miches''' is a town located in [[El Seibo]] province in the northeast coast of the [[Dominican Republic]]. The community sits between the northern slopes of the [[:es:Cordillera Oriental (República Dominicana)|Cordillera Oriental]] and the southern shore of the [[Samaná Bay]].<ref>ESSEG http://cees.columbia.edu/programs/esseg</ref>

==Origin of name==
In 1936, the community formerly known as '''El Jovero''' changed its name -through Act No. 1181, published in the Official Gazette No. 4956-, in honor of the Dominican General of French origin [[:es:Eugenio Miches|Eugenio Miches]].<ref>Moní Reyes, Ceferino (2011). [http://www.agn.gov.do/sites/default/files/libros/pdfs/vol%20121._miches._historia_y_tradiccion._ceferino_moni_reyes.pdf Miches, historia y tradición] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170202205931/http://www.agn.gov.do/sites/default/files/libros/pdfs/vol%20121._miches._historia_y_tradiccion._ceferino_moni_reyes.pdf |date=2017-02-02 }} (in Spanish). Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic; Archivo General de la Nación.</ref>

==Economic activity==
Miches has traditionally been a rural community in one of the least densely populated regions of the Dominican Republic. Agriculture, particularly the cultivation of rice, coconut, cocoa and tubers like [[yautía]] and [[ñame]], cattle ranching, [[artisanal fishing]], local handicrafts and small businesses are the main economic engines of the area.

Due to its geographical location, with the sea to the north and a mountain range to the south, Miches has historically lived isolated.{{citation needed|date=April 2017}} In recent years,{{when|date=April 2017}} improvements have been seen in the roads connecting [[Punta Cana]], [[Santa Cruz de El Seibo]] and [[Sabana de la Mar]], which have opened a new channel of communication that favors the diversification of the economy and an incipient development of tourism.<ref>[http://www.elcaribe.com.do/2015/01/16/medina-inaugura-carretera-bavarouvero-altomiches Danilo Medina inaugura carretera Bávaro-Uvero Alto-Miches]; El Caribe 16/01/2015 || [http://www.diariolibre.com/noticias/danilo-inaugura-las-carretera-el-seibo-miches-FIDL995671 Danilo Medina inaugura la carretera El Seibo-Miches]; Diario Libre 03/02/2015 || [http://www.diariolibre.com/noticias/dejan-en-funcionamiento-la-carretera-miches-sabana-de-la-mar-MI3039970 Dejan en funcionamiento la carretera Miches-Sabana de la Mar]; Diario Libre 15/03/2016</ref>

== Tourism development ==
Miches has a great tourism potential.{{citation needed|date=April 2017}} The extension of its beaches, and its irregular coasts creating unique landforms, providing all the means for potential tourism.<ref>Moní Reyes, Ceferino (2011). [http://www.agn.gov.do/sites/default/files/libros/pdfs/vol%20121._miches._historia_y_tradiccion._ceferino_moni_reyes.pdf Miches, historia y tradición] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170202205931/http://www.agn.gov.do/sites/default/files/libros/pdfs/vol%20121._miches._historia_y_tradiccion._ceferino_moni_reyes.pdf |date=2017-02-02 }} (in Spanish). Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic; Archivo General de la Nación.</ref> The area has mangroves, coconut trees, mountains, rivers, waterfalls, caves and tropical forests.{{citation needed|date=April 2017}} The construction of new roads and the announcement of major projects have fostered the development of tourism in Miches, with the appearance of small local businesses (such as restaurants, rental cabins, boat tours, horseback rides on the beach) and the creation of tourism clusters.{{citation needed|date=April 2017}}

Miches will be the location of the project Tropicalia, a [[sustainable tourism]] and real estate development of Cisneros Real Estate, a division of [[Grupo Cisneros|Cisneros]]. Tropicalia will entail an investment of US$310 million and will generate around 1,800 jobs.<ref>[https://www.forbes.com.mx/la-apuesta-inversion-mas-importante-grupo-cisneros/#gs.wP2_5M8 La apuesta de inversión más importante de Grupo Cisneros]; Forbes 09/05/2016</ref> Since 2008, Tropicalia promotes, through Fundación Tropicalia, the sustainable social and economic development of Miches, working in the areas of environment, education, productivity, and socio-cultural advocacy.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fundaciontropicalia.com/en/about-fundacion-tropicalia/|title = Fundación Tropicalia - About us}}</ref>
