'''Porto de Pedras''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈpoɦtʊ ˈdɪ ˈpɛd(ɾ)ɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northern coast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 7,701 in 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/porto-de-pedras/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Its area is {{cvt|266|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
