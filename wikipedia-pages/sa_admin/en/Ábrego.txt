'''Ábrego''' ({{IPA|es|ˈaβɾeɣo}}) is a Colombian [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and town located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Norte de Santander]]. The urban centre is situated at an altitude of {{convert|1398|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]].<ref name=WebsiteAbrego>{{in lang|es}} {{URL|http://www.abrego-nortedesantander.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml|Official website Ábrego}}</ref>

== History ==
Various indigenous communities, such as the Oroques, Turmeros, Seborucos, and Carasicas, inhabited the territory. The first denomination of this place was Llano de los Orejones in 1530 by [[Ambrosius Ehinger|Ambrosio Alfínger's]] troops. On July 26, 1580, a wooden cross was erected in the middle of the valley of this municipality for the first Eucharist. From that moment, the place began to be called "Llanos de la Cruz". The settlement of the municipality began when the sisters Ana María and Josefa de la Encarnación Maldonado Quintero donated several lands to raise the municipality's population; from that moment, they began to build streets and squares. March 12, 1810, is the date of the municipality's founding. On April 14, 1930, by ordinance number 32, the name of the municipality was changed to Abrego to honor the nortesantadereana woman who is considered a heroine in the department, [[Mercedes Abrego|Mercedes Abrego de Reyes]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Archivo de la Alcaldía Municipal de Ábrego (Norte de Santander, Colombia)|url=http://censoarchivos.mcu.es/CensoGuia/archivodetail.htm?id=1762724|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Censo-Guía de Archivos de España e Iberoamérica|language=es|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101001029/http://censoarchivos.mcu.es/CensoGuia/archivodetail.htm?id=1762724 |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
About 3 hectares of the municipality are located in the Santurbán moor, which is a water source for the departments of Norte de Santander and Santander.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2018|title=Informe económico de los municipios de la jurisdicción de la Cámara de Comercio de Ocaña|url=https://camaraocana.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Estudio-economico-2017.pdf|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Cámara de Comercio de Ocaña|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101161926/https://camaraocana.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Estudio-economico-2017.pdf |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref> Part of the municipality is located in a valley between the Oroque and Frío rivers.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2014|title=Cartilla Ábrego|url=https://consornoc.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/cartilla-abrego-1.pdf|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Corporación Nueva Sociedad de la Región Nororiental de Colombia|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101181848/https://consornoc.org.co/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/cartilla-abrego-1.pdf |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref>

== Economy ==
The economy of the municipality is primarily centered around agriculture. The main crop grown is onions, with other products including tomatoes, cocoa, coffee, corn, beans, sugar cane, and tobacco.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Carrascal |first=Blanca Liliana Velásquez |last2=Álvarez |first2=Melisa Daniela Verjel |last3=Bayona-Vergel |first3=Valentina |last4=Hoyos-Patiño |first4=Johann Fernando |last5=Sayago-Velásquez |first5=Jeimmy Estefany |date=2020-07-01 |title=Impacto económico en los agricultores por la falta de una plaza de mercado en el municipio de Abrego, Norte de Santander |url=https://revistas.ufps.edu.co/index.php/RC/article/view/2903 |journal=Reflexiones contables (Cúcuta) |language=es |volume=3 |issue=2 |pages=40–50 |doi=10.22463/26655543.2903 |issn=2665-5543 |s2cid=235856516 |doi-access=free}}</ref>  

As of 2017, there were 677 commercial establishments in the municipality, including clothing stores, hairdressers, cafes, and bakeries.<ref>{{Cite report |url=https://repository.usta.edu.co/handle/11634/10890 |title=Plan estratégico para el mejoramiento de la malla vial rural del municipio de Ábrego, Norte de Santander al año 2019 |last=Acevedo Martínez |first=Martha Janeth |last2=Verjel Pérez |first2=Lina Fernanda |date=2017 |publisher=Universidad Santo Tomás |language=es |hdl=11634/10890}}</ref>

== Heritage Sites ==
Ábrego has several temples, such as the Parish of Santa Bárbara, which is the first hermitage of the town completed in 1765,<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Carrascal |first1=Marilce Pacheco |last2=Quintero |first2=Damarys Vergel |date=2015-06-30 |title=Atractivos turísticos de Ocaña y su región |url=https://revistas.ufps.edu.co/index.php/profundidad/article/view/2254 |journal=Revista Científica Profundidad Construyendo Futuro |language=es |volume=2 |issue=2 |pages=72–77 |doi=10.22463/24221783.2254 |s2cid=257458200 |issn=2422-2518|doi-access=free }}</ref> which was founded by Juan Quintero Príncipe and Cristóbal Arévalo.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Iglesias y Templos en Municipio de Ábrego|url=https://www.ocanaturistica.com/abrego/atractivos-turisticos/iglesias-templos/|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Ocaña Turística|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160612202043/http://www.ocanaturistica.com:80/abrego/atractivos-turisticos/iglesias-templos/ |archive-date=2016-06-12 }}</ref> It became a parish on September 5, 1807, in which José María Fernández Carvajalino was the first parish priest.<ref name=":0" /> On December 31, 2003, by decree 1144, the church is considered a property of cultural interest of departmental character.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|last=Paéz|first=Luis|date=2007|title=Guía Turística de la Región de Ocaña|url=http://www.mariojavierpacheco.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/GU%C3%8DA-TUR%C3%8DSTICA-DE-LA-REGI%C3%93N-DE-OCA%C3%91A-1x.pdf|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Biblioteca Mario Javier Pacheco García|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101231427/http://www.mariojavierpacheco.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/GU%C3%8DA-TUR%C3%8DSTICA-DE-LA-REGI%C3%93N-DE-OCA%C3%91A-1x.pdf |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref> Another temple in the municipality is the Divino Niño Church, which it was created by the parish priest Diógenes Sanabria, in 2009, the building was consecrated.<ref name=":0" />

Another tourist attraction is the Pozo del Burro ({{Lit|The Donkey's Well|i=yes|lk=yes}}), in which according to a local legend, a traveler was traveling through this place with his donkey carrying gold, but he drowned in the river.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Miustes|first=Alexandra|date=2021-03-12|title=Ábrego: el bello valle de Norte de Santander|url=https://atntelevision.co/2021/03/12/abrego-el-bello-valle-de-norte-de-santander/|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=ATN TV|language=es|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210312151859/https://atntelevision.co/2021/03/12/abrego-el-bello-valle-de-norte-de-santander/ |archive-date=2021-03-12 }}</ref> In addition to Piedras Negras ({{Lit|Black Stones|i=yes|lk=yes}}), which are a pile of stones scattered over approximately 3 hectares. Some theories suggest that they are of volcanic origin or that they are fragments of an asteroid, there is also an oral tradition that says that they were used by the Orokes for rituals in the nular phase.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Las piedras negras más inexplicables del mundo están en Ábrego|url=https://www.laopinion.com.co/region/las-piedras-negras-mas-inexplicables-del-mundo-estan-en-abrego|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=La Opinión|language=es-co|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101014243/https://www.laopinion.com.co/region/las-piedras-negras-mas-inexplicables-del-mundo-estan-en-abrego |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref>

== Natural Reserves ==
One of the natural reserves of Ábrego is the Juridiscciones Moor has about 2800 hectares, specifically in the Pan de Azúcar Lagoon, where the Oroque and Frío rivers are born, which form the Algodonal river.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Campesinos le dan vida al páramo Jurisdicciones |url=https://www.laopinion.com.co/region/campesinos-le-dan-vida-al-paramo-jurisdicciones |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=La Opinión |language=es-co}}</ref> In December 2014, the Juridiscciones, Santurbán and Berlín Moors were delimited by resolution 2090, in this moor complex there are several lagoon, which are water sources for several municipalities of Norte de Santander, including Ábrego.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2018-03-18 |title=Páramo Jurisdicciones – Santurbán - Berlín (PJSB) (Libro Orientador) |url=https://santurban.minambiente.gov.co/images/Puntos_ineludibles/T_1_1_Libreto_Tcnico_Delimitacion_2090.pdf |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=[[Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development (Colombia)|Colombian Ministry of Environment and Sustainable Development]] |language=es}}</ref>

== Education ==
The first educational establishments in the municipality date back to 1844.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2010|title=Plan de Ordenación y Manejo de la Cuenca Hidrográfica del Río Algodonal|url=https://corponor.gov.co/publica_recursos/pomca/algodonal/PLAN_DE_ORDENACION_Y_MANEJO_CUENCA_RIO_ALGODONAL.pdf|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-02|website=Corporación Autónoma Regional de la Frontera Nororiental|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200726232448/https://corponor.gov.co/publica_recursos/pomca/algodonal/PLAN_DE_ORDENACION_Y_MANEJO_CUENCA_RIO_ALGODONAL.pdf |archive-date=2020-07-26 }}</ref> The municipality has a mega school called Carlo Julio Torrado Peñaranda School which was inaugurated in 2016, the mega school has three computer rooms, physics, chemistry and biology laboratories, also in 2016, the school had 1600 primary and high school students studying in it.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Ábrego: 1.600 estudiantes estrenan megacolegio|url=https://www.laopinion.com.co/region/abrego-1600-estudiantes-estrenan-megacolegio|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=La Opinión|language=es-co|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211101155001/https://www.laopinion.com.co/region/abrego-1600-estudiantes-estrenan-megacolegio |archive-date=2021-11-01 }}</ref> The municipality also has another school called Institución Educativa Colegio Santa Barbara, founded in 1966. In 2016, the 50th anniversary of the institution was celebrated in the main park of the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|title=La i. e. colegio santa bárbara celebró, con alegría y jolgorio, sus bodas de oro.|url=http://www.colsaba.edu.co/noticias/celebracion-50-anos-colsaba/|url-status=live|access-date=2021-11-01|website=Colegio Santa Barbara|language=es|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211102181403/http://www.colsaba.edu.co/noticias/celebracion-50-anos-colsaba/ |archive-date=2021-11-02 }}</ref>
