'''Lagoa dos Três Cantos''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil. As of 2020, the estimated population was 1,607.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/lagoa-dos-tres-cantos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Regional language==

Like in many communities in the state of Rio Grande do Sul, [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]] has been part of its history since pioneering days.
