'''Medina''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|meˈd͡ʒĩnɐ}}) is a Brazilian municipality located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in the [[Jequitinhonha River]] valley. According to the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], the population of the municipality was 20,156 in a total area of 1,435 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Medina (MG) {{!}} Cidades e Estados {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/mg/medina.html |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Jequitinhonha and the microregion of Pedra Azul.

== History ==
The municipality originated from a village formed by a group of indigenous and slaves led by the Spaniard Leandro de Medina, who arrived in the region in May 1824. Since the day of their arrival coincided with the feast of [[Rita of Cascia|Saint Rita of Cascia]], this was the name given to the place. In 1847, the first mass was celebrated in the chapel of Saint Rita, built on land donated by the farmer Maria Gonçalves. The village of ''Santa Rita'' was elevated to the category of district, separating from [[Minas Novas]] and annexed to the municipality of Fortaleza (currently [[Pedra Azul]]), with the name of ''Santa Rita de Medina''. In 1938, it was elevated to the status of municipality and was renamed ''Medina'', and the ''[[comarca]]'' was established in 1948.<ref>{{Cite web |title=As Minas Gerais - Medina |url=https://www.asminasgerais.com.br/?item=CONTEUDO&codConteudoRaiz=993 |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=www.asminasgerais.com.br}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality is located in the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Pedra Azul]], which is part of the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It belongs to the Microregion of Pedra Azul and the [[Mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]]. Its population censused in 2022 was 20,156 inhabitants.<ref name=":0" /> 

=== Hydrography ===
The municipality is bathed by the [[Jequitinhonha River]] and its tributary the São Pedro River, and several streams, the most important being the Sapucaia, Dos Cães, São Camilo and Ribeirão streams, the latter being responsible for supplying the city.

== Economy ==
The city's [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] is around R$253.1 million [[Brazilian real|reais]], with 41% of the added value coming from services, followed by public administration (40.4%), industry (12%) and agriculture (6.6%).<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Economia de Medina - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/medina---mg |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref> The municipality's [[Gross domestic product|GDP per capita]] is R$12.2 thousand reais.<ref name=":1" />

== Culture ==
The city holds the festival of the patron saint Rita of Cascia and the city's anniversary every year on May 22nd.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Portal Minas |title=Portal Minas Gerais - FESTA DA PADROEIRA SANTA RITA DE CÁSSIA E... |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/eventos/medina/festa-da-padroeira-santa-rita-de-cassia-e-aniversario-da-cidade-de-medina |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref> The biggest festival held by the municipality is the Festa da Cabeça da Leitoa (lit. "Pig's Head Festival") held annually during the [[Festa Junina|June festivals]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Portal Minas |title=Portal Minas Gerais - FESTA DA CABEÇA DA LEITOA |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/eventos/medina/festa-da-cabeca-da-leitoa |access-date=2025-01-30 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref>
