'''Otzolotepec''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico State|municipality]] in [[Mexico (state)|Mexico State]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  127.95&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 67,611.<ref>{{cite web|title=Otzolotepec |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15067a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=June 6, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070303115429/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15067a.htm |archive-date=March 3, 2007 }}</ref>
