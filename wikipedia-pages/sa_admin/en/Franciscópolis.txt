'''Franciscópolis''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 5,338 people living in a total area of 715&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE 2020"/> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1997.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008}}</ref>

==Geography==
Franciscopolis is located on at an elevation of 380 meters, 70&nbsp;km east of [[Teófilo Otoni]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 546&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Malacacheta]], [[Poté]], [[Água Boa, Minas Gerais|Água Boa]], [[Itambacuri]] and [[Teófilo Otoni]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |archive-date=2008-06-13 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|18|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 9 million from services, 1 million from industry, and 7 million from agriculture. There were 612 rural producers on 50,000 hectares of land. Only 22 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 1,500 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were coffee, sugarcane, beans and corn. There were 33,000 head of cattle (2006). There were no banks (2007) and 85 automobiles (122 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 66 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Health and education==
There was 1 health clinic. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni. Educational needs were met by 3 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 1 pre-primary school.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
