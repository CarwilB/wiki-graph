'''Rambala ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chiriquí Grande District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|33.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,682 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|50.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 10 of March 7, 1997; this measure was complemented by Law 5 of January 19, 1998 and Law 69 of October 28, 1998.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2023 was 1,365.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Wayback Machine|url=https://www.inec.gob.pa/archivos/P053342420231213140620Cuadro%2004.pdf|website=www.inec.gob.pa|access-date=2025-11-30|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20241113021307/https://www.inec.gob.pa/archivos/P053342420231213140620Cuadro%2004.pdf|archive-date=2024-11-13}}</ref>
