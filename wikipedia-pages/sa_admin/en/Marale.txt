'''Marale''' is a municipality in the department of [[Francisco Morazán (department)|Francisco Morazán]], [[Honduras]].

==Location==
Marale is bordered on the north by the municipality of [[Yoro]], south by the municipalities of [[El Porvenir, Francisco Morazán|El Porvenir]] and [[San Ignacio, Francisco Morazán|San Ignacio]], to the east by the municipalities of [[Mangulile]], [[Yocón]] and [[Orica, Francisco Morazán|Orica]], and west by the municipalities of [[Sulaco, Yoro|Sulaco]], [[Yorito]] and [[San José del Potrero]].<ref>http://www.ordenamientoterritorial.hn/images/creacion_municipios/francisco_morazan/marale.pdf{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} Territorial organization of Honduras</ref> It is surrounded by several ranges of hills, known as El Portillo, La Loma de la Cruz and Mud Loma.

==History==
Marale was founded in 1820, and in 1889 became one of the municipalities of the Cedros District.<ref>Fiallos, Carmen. 1989. "Municipalities of Honduras."</ref>

==Villages==
The municipality of Marale encompasses the following eleven villages:<ref>National Institute of Statistics, Honduras. (Census 2001)</ref>
* Marale (county seat)
* El Carrizal
* El Panal
* El Paraíso
* La Esperanza o El Cacao
* La Travesía
* Las Casitas
* Las Lagunas
* Los Naranjos
* Los Planes
* Río Abajo

==Economy==
The main source of livelihood in the parish is agriculture, especially the production of corn and beans.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Culture==
The parish is mainly [[Roman Catholic]]. 
The new year is marked with the celebration of [[La Feria (celebration)|La Feria]], in honour of [[Cristo negro]] (Black Christ).{{cn|date=January 2023}}
