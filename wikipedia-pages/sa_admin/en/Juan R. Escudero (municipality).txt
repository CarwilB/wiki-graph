''' Juan R. Escudero ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Tierra Colorada]]. The municipality covers an area of 652.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 22,805.<ref>{{cite web|title=Juan R. Escudero|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12039a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070510091908/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12039a.htm|archive-date=May 10, 2007}}</ref>
