==Political division==
The province is divided into seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: {{lang|es|distrito}}), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Acomayo District|Acomayo]] ([[Acomayo]])
* [[Acopia District|Acopia]] ([[Acopia, Peru|Acopia]])
* [[Acos District|Acos]] ([[Acos, Peru|Acos]])
* [[Mosoc Llacta District|Mosoc Llacta]] ([[Mosoc Llacta]])
* [[Pomacanchi District|Pomacanchi]] ([[Pomacanchi]])
* [[Rondocan District|Rondocan]] ([[Rondocan]])
* [[Sangarará District|Sangarará]] ([[Sangarará]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (87.48%) learnt to speak in childhood, 12.25% of the residents started speaking in [[Spanish language|Spanish]].<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
