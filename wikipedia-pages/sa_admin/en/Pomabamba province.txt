'''Pomabamba''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Puma Pampa''') is one of twenty provinces of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]]. It was formerly called ''Pumapampa'' (Quechua for "[[cougar]] plain") and today it is also known as "The City of [[Cedar wood|Cedars]]". It is located at the left bank of the Pomabamba river, at {{convert|3063|m|sp=us}} above sea level. It was created by law on February 21, 1861.

In this vast region some [[archaeological site]]s like Ichik Yaynu, Willka Marka, Runa Marka and the gigantic stone constructions of [[Yaynu]] were found with features of the [[Recuay Culture]].

For these ancient copper-colored and fecund places, one of the biggest pre-inca confederations was created, the powerful confederation of conchucos. It was an iron agglomeration of people that presented a hard and brave resistance to the [[Inca]] and Spanish armies. During the Republic, Marshal [[Ramón Castilla]] visited Pomabamba and fell in love with the beautiful [[Ancash]] woman from [[Llumpa District|Llumpa]], Margarita Mariluz.

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (84.83%) learnt to speak in childhood, 14.90% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
