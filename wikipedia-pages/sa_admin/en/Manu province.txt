'''Manu''' is one of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Madre de Dios Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}, Retrieved January 5, 2008</ref> The capital of the province is the city of [[Salvación]].
