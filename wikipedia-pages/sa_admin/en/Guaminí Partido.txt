'''Guaminí Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the west of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4840|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Guaminí]], which is {{convert|492|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==
The economy of Guaminí partido is dominated by the cultivation of [[wheat]], [[maize]], [[soya bean]]s, [[sunflowers]], [[alfalfa]], [[sorghum]] and [[oats]].

Other industries include beef and dairy farming and related farming services.
