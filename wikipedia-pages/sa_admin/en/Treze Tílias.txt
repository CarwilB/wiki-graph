'''Treze Tílias''' ([[German language|German]]: ''Dreizehnlinden'', literally "Thirteen [[Tilia|Lindens]]") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], [[South Region, Brazil|South Region]], [[Brazil]]. It covers about 185.205&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and sits about 470&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Florianópolis]]. The municipality population estimate for 2020 is 7,991. Treze Tílias was originally created on 13 October 1933, the result of an emigration scheme supported by the Austrian government in Vienna. ''Dreizehnlinden'' is known for its maintenance of Austrian culture and language until the present as well as its resistance to Nazi-Germany take-over after [[Anschluss]] in 1938.<ref>{{Cite web |title=1933, Dreizehnlinden |url=https://hdgoe.at/1933_dreizehnlinden |access-date=2024-11-03 |website=hdgö - Haus der Geschichte Österreich |language=de}}</ref>

Founded by [[Austrian people|Austrian]] immigrants, the large majority from [[Tyrol (state)|Tyrol]] and [[Vorarlberg]], Treze Tílias exhibits an Alpine-influenced [[timber framing]] style of architecture. Most residents speak [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] and the [[southern Austro-Bavarian]] dialect of [[Austrian German]].{{cn|date=November 2022}} The economy of Treze Tílias is based on [[agriculture]], [[tourism]], and [[woodworking]].
