'''Cássia dos Coqueiros''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,505 (2020 est.) in an area of {{Cvt|192|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6G0 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 890 m.
