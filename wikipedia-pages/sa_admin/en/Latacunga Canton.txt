'''Latacunga Canton''' is one of seven [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]]s of the [[Cotopaxi Province]] in [[Ecuador]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 143,979.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>  Its capital is the town of [[Latacunga]].

== Subdivision ==
The capital of the canton consists of the following urban parishes: Eloy Alfaro (San Felipe), Ignacio Flores (La Laguna), Juan Montalvo (San Sebastián), La Matríz, San Buenaventura. 

The canton is divided into the following rural parishes: 
* [[Toacaso Parish|Toacaso]]
* [[San Juan de Pastocalle Parish|San Juan de Pastocalle]]
* [[Mulaló Parish|Mulaló]]
* [[Tanicuchí Parish|Tanicuchí]]
* [[Guaytacama Parish|Guaytacama]]
* [[Alaques Parish|Alaques]]
* [[Poaló Parish|Poaló]]
* [[11 de noviembre Parish|Once de Noviembre]]
* [[Belisario Quevedo Parish, Cotopaxi|Belisario Quevedo]]
* [[Joseguango Bajo Parish|Joseguango Bajo]]
