'''Guanambi''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Bahia]], [[Brazil]], with an area of 1,272.367&nbsp;km²,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/geociencias/areaterritorial/area.php?nome=Guanambi&codigo=&submit.x=0&submit.y=0|title=IBGE :: Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|work=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]]|access-date=July 28, 2017|language=pt-br}}</ref> and with 84,928 inhabitants in 2020,  according to estimates by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]],<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/guanambi/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> making it the 20th most populous municipality of [[Bahia]]. It was founded in 1919 and Nilo Coelho is its current mayor.

Guanambi is home of the largest wind complex in [[Latin America]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Inaugurado na Bahia o Maior Complexo Eólico da América Latina|url=http://www.ouvidoriageral.ba.gov.br/2012/07/10/inaugurado-na-bahia-o-maior-complexo-eolico-da-america-latina|access-date=July 28, 2017|language=pt-br|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150403182934/http://www.ouvidoriageral.ba.gov.br/2012/07/10/inaugurado-na-bahia-o-maior-complexo-eolico-da-america-latina/|archive-date=April 3, 2015}}</ref>

==Transportation==
It is 796&nbsp;km (approximately 498 miles) south-west of Salvador, the capital being connected by the roads BR-030, BR-262 and [[BR-324]], representing a strong influence in the commercial. The city is served by [[Guanambi Airport]].
