'''Tepetlixpa''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico (state)|State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|46.68|km2|abbr=on}} and borders on the state of [[Morelos]]. 

In 2000, the municipality had a total population of 16,871.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tepetlixpa |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15094a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=6 June 2009 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070518064243/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15094a.htm |archivedate=18 May 2007 }}</ref> By 2020, the figure had risen to 20,500.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tepetlixpa: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tepetlixpa |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=1 January 2025}}</ref>
 
==Nepantla de Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz==
The municipality is home to Nepantla de Sor Juana Inés de la Cruz, formerly known as San Miguel Nepantla, the hometown of Sor [[Juana Inés de la Cruz]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Descarga de Información correspondiente a la Localidad 150940002|url=http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/AHL/realizaBusquedaurl.do?cvegeo=150940002|publisher=INEGI|accessdate=10 November 2011}}{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref> 

At his request, the ashes of Argentine-Mexican poet [[Juan Gelman]] were scattered in Nepantla.<ref>{{cite web|title=Cenizas de Gelman descansan en la tierra de Sor Juana|url=http://archivo.eluniversal.com.mx/cultura/2014/cenizas-gelman-sor-juana-nepantla-980922.html|publisher=[[El Universal (Mexico City)|El Universal]]|accessdate=19 July 2015}}</ref>
