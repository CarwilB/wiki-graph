'''Ignacio Warnes '''is one of the fifteen provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz]] [[Departments of Bolivia|Department]] and is situated in the department's central parts. The province name honors Colonel [[Ignacio Warnes]] (1772–1816), a military leader in the South American war of independence.

== Location ==
''Ignacio Warnes Province'' is located between 17° 00' and 17° 42' [[Latitude|South]] and between 62° 42' and 63° 14' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 85&nbsp;km from North to South, and 70&nbsp;km from West to East. [[Piray River|Río Piray]] is forming the province border in the West and [[Río Grande (Bolivia)|Río Grande]] in the East.

The province is situated in the Bolivian lowlands and borders [[Obispo Santistevan Province]] in the Northwest, [[Sara Province]] in the West, [[Andrés Ibáñez Province]] in the South, and [[Ñuflo de Chávez Province]] in the East.

== Population ==
The population of Ignacio Warnes Province has increased by circa 75% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 38,285 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 54,593 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2012: 108,888 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Warnes (Province, Bolivia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/santa_cruz/0702__warnes/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
*2024: 161,311 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref name=":0" />

46.2% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

The [[literacy]] rate of the province is 96.9%. ''(2012)''<ref name=":0" />

95.2% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 14.2% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 4.1% [[Guarani language|Guaraní]], and 1.3 speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]]. ''(1992)''

41.9% of the population have no access to electricity, 47.9% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

88.4% of the population are Catholics, 8.8% are Protestants. ''(1992)''
