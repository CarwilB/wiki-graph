'''Campanário''' is a [[Brazil]]ian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 3,730 people living in a total area of {{cvt|441|km2}}.<ref name="IBGE 2020"/> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1943.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008}}</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of Campanário is located at an elevation of 268 meters on the important federal highway BR-116 between [[Governador Valadares]] and [[Teófilo Otoni]]. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Itambacuri]] (W and N), [[Frei Gaspar]] (NE), [[Pescador]] (E), [[Jampruca]] (S).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/govvaladares/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-31 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080917184637/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/govvaladares/ |archive-date=2008-09-17 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
Services and agriculture are the main economic activities. The GDP in 2005 was approximately {{BRLConvert|15|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 9 million reais from services, 1 million reais from industry, and 4 million reais from agriculture. There were 190 rural producers on 19,000 hectares of land (2006). Approximately 450 persons were occupied in agriculture. The main crops are sugarcane, beans, and corn. There were 23,000 head of cattle, of which 3,700 were milk cows (2006). Only 25 of the rural properties had tractors in 2006.<ref name="IBGE 2008">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

There were no banks (2007) In the vehicle fleet there were 161 automobiles, 10 trucks, 12 pickup trucks, 4 buses, and 70 motorcycles (2007).<ref name="IBGE 2008"/>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of {{BRLConvert|133|year=2000|showdate=no}} was well below the state average of {{BRLConvert|276|year=2000|showdate=no}}. [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with {{BRLConvert|435|year=2000|showdate=no}}. The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with {{BRLConvert|73|year=2000|showdate=no}}.

In terms of HDI, the highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06}}</ref>
