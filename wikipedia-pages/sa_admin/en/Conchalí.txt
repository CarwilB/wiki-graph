'''Conchalí''' ({{IPA|es|kontʃaˈli|lang|Conchalipronunciation.ogg}}) is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], within the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It is located in the northern part of the city of [[Santiago]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Conchalí spans an area of {{convert|70.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 133,256 inhabitants (64,973 men and 68,283 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 12.9% (19,663 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> Its 2006 population was projected at 120,151 persons.<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |accessdate=13 September 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archivedate=August 23, 2010 }}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Conchalí is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a [[alcalde|mayor]] who is directly elected every four years. The current mayor is René de la Vega (Ind) since 2016.<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:
* Paula Pérez Espinoza ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Hernán Corona Sánchez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Patricia Molina Molina ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]])
* Lissette Ponce Palacios ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Martín Muñoz Olivares ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Krishna Narváez Espinoza ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Miguel Astudillo Cáceres ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Marjorie Melo Valenzuela ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Conchalí is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Karla Rubilar ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and [[Daniel Farcas Guendelman]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 17th electoral district, (together with [[Renca]] and [[Huechuraba]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Andrés Allamand Zavala ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
