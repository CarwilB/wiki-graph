'''''Santo Antônio do Sudoeste''''' ({{lit|Saint Anthony of the Southwest}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]] with a population of 20,261 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/santo-antonio-do-sudoeste/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is on the border between [[Argentina]] and Brazil, opposite the Argentine city of [[San Antonio, Misiones]]. The [[San Antonio River (South America)|San Antonio River]] separates the two cities.
