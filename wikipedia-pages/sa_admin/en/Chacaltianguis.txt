'''Chacaltianguis''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south of the state of [[Veracruz (state)|Veracruz]]. It has a surface of 557.69 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|18|N|95|50|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Chacaltianguis is delimited to the north by [[Cosamaloapan]], to the east and south-east by [[José Azueta, Veracruz|José Azueta]], to the west by [[Tuxtilla]], and to the south-west by [[Oaxaca]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30054a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chacaltianguis|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070227143938/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30054a.htm|archive-date=2007-02-27|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Chacaltianguis is warm all year with rain in summer.

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[beans]]. 
==Culture==
In May, the celebration of the [[Mango]] festival takes place; this is the main festivity of the town.
