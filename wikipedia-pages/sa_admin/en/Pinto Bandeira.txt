'''Pinto Bandeira''' is a [[List of municipalities in Rio Grande do Sul|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was 3,036&nbsp;(2020) and its area is 105&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=431454|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Rio Grande do Sul {{!}} Pinto Bandeira|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-29}}</ref> The municipality was established in January 2013.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/com-5-novos-municipios-brasil-agora-tem-5570-cidades-7235803|title=Com 5 novos municípios, Brasil agora tem 5.570 cidades|language=pt-br}}</ref>
