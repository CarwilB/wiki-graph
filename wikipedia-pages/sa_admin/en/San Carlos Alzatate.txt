'''San Carlos Alzatate''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jalapa Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|80.2|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 22,264 inhabitants.

==History==
"Alzatate" is derived from [[Nahuatl]] and means "river heron". The region was inhabited by indigenous [[Pipil people|Pipil]] and [[Poqomam people|Poqomam]] people before the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonization. The settlement was formed when the indigenous people settled in the region to escape enslavement during the Colonial rule.<ref name="Hist"/>

Initially, it was part of the [[Potrero Grande]] hacienda, in the department of [[Santa Rosa Department|Santa Rosa]]. It was known as Santa Cruz Alzatate, and was renamed as San Raymundo Alzatate in honor of [[Raymond of Peñafort]]. In 1938, it was renamed as San Carlos Alzatate, as it is currently known.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de San Carlos Alzatate, Jalapa|trans-title=History of San Carlos Alzatate municipality|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/historia-de-el-municipio-de-san-carlos-alzatate-jalapa/|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
San Carlos Alzatate is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipalities]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jalapa Department|Jalapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|80.2|km2}}.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de San Carlos Alzatate, Jalapa|trans-title=Geography of San Carlos Alzatate municipality|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/geografia-de-el-municipio-de-san-carlos-alzatate-jalapa/|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Carlos Alzatate (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jalapa/2105__san_carlos_alzatate/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|126|km}} from the national capital [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|37|km}} from the departmental seat of [[Jalapa, Jalapa|Jalapa]]. It borders the municipalities of Jalapa to the north, [[Casillas, Santa Rosa|Casillas]], [[San Rafael Las Flores]] and [[Jutiapa]] to the south, and [[Mataquescuintla]] and [[San Rafael Las Flores]] to the west.<ref name="Geo"/>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1379.63|m}} above sea level, the municipality has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|20|C}}. It receives about {{cvt|138.83|mm}} of rainfall annually and has an average of 182.22 rainy days per year.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Carlos Alzatate|work=Weather and Climate|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jalapa/san-carlos-alzatate|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
San Carlos Alzatate had an estimated population of 22,264 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 11,121 males and 11,143 females. About 34.8% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.7% was over the age of 65 years. About 46.9% of the population was classified as rural, while 53.1% lived in urban areas. Most residents (94.8%) were born in the same municipality.<ref name="CP"/>

[[Xinca people|Xinca]] (92.2%) formed the predominant ethnic group, with [[Ladino people|Ladino]] (5.9%) forming a minor group. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.1%) is the most widely spoken language. The literacy rate is about 80.7%.<ref name="CP"/>
