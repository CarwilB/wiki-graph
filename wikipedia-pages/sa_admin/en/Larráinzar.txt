'''Larráinzar''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is the town of [[San Andrés Larráinzar]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 20,349,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Larráinzar|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=049|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=21 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151021171056/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=049|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 16,538 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Larráinzar |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07049a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas |accessdate=July 27, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930015034/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07049a.htm |archivedate=September 30, 2007 }}</ref> The population in 2020 grew to 31,259.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Larráinzar: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/larrainzar |access-date=2025-07-05 |website=Data México |language=en}}</ref> It covers an area of 171.04&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the town of Larráinzar (San Andrés Larráinzar) had a population of 2,364.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Larráinzar, the municipality had 77 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
