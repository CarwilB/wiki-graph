'''Quesada''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|116.9|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 24,020 inhabitants.

==History==
According to the historical records, it was named by the Quezada family due to its resemblance to their native estate of Quezada in Spain. It was later acquired by the writer [[José Milla y Vidaurre]], who retain the name for the place. However, the local inhabitants later began calling it Quesada with an "s" because the estate produced the best cheese in the region. The municipality of Quesada was created by official decree on 18 June 1897, during the presidency of [[José María Reina Barrios]].<ref name="Info">{{cite web|title=Historia del Municipio de Quesada|url=https://www.muniquesada.laip.gt/index.php/files/1127/29-INFORMACION-ADICIONAL/1iE0kkAxX199_g9JWasxaBsvTQ481DOcm/29A-HISTORIA-DEL-MUNICIPIO-DE-QUESADA-.pdf|work=Municipalidad de Quesada|access-date=24 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=3,4}}

==Geography==
Quesada is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|116.9|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Quesada (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2217__quezada/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/entregan-proyecto-de-pavimentacion-en-aldea-bordo-alto-quesada/|title=Entregan proyecto de pavimentación en aldea Bordo Alto, Quesada|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=25 February 2026|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located in the western part of the department, about {{cvt|19|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Jutiapa]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Casillas, Santa Rosa|Casillas]] to the north, [[San José Acatempa]] and [[Jalpatagua]] to the west, and Jutiapa to the south and east.<ref name="Info"/>{{rp|page=1}}

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|983.68|m}} above sea level, Quesada has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The district has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|25.31|C}}. Quesada typically receives about {{cvt|78.89|mm}} of precipitation and has 124.48 rainy days (34.1% of the time) on average annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/quesada|title=Quesada|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Quesada had an estimated population of 24,020 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 11,987 males and 12,033 females. About 27.5% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 7.2% was over the age of 65 years. About 88.8% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (11.2%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (87.6%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 86.2%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (58.4%) and [[Xinca people|Xincas]] (40.3%) are the major ethnic groups. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
