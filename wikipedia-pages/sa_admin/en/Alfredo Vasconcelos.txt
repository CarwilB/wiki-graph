'''Alfredo Vasconcelos''' is a city in the south of the [[Federative units of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2025 its population was estimated to be 7,236 in a total area of {{cvt|130.8|km2}}.<ref name="IBGE" /> It belongs to the [[Barbacena]] IBGE statistical microregion. It lies just north of Barbacena on the important BR-040 highway, which links [[Brasília]] to [[Rio de Janeiro]].
