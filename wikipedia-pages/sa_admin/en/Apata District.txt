'''Apata District''' is one of thirty-four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Jauja Province]], located at an elevation of 3.340 min the [[Department of Junín|Department of Junin]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It encompasses an area of 421.62&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It was elevated to district level in November 16, 1864, during the presidency of [[José Balta]].
