'''Siachoque''' is a town and municipality in the [[Central Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Siachoque is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|21|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. It borders [[Toca, Boyacá|Toca]] in the north, [[Rondón, Boyacá|Rondón]] and [[Viracachá]] in the south, in the east Toca, [[Pesca]] and Rondón and in the west [[Soracá]] and [[Chivatá]].<ref name=websiteSiachoque>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.siachoque-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Siachoque]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Siachoque comes from the [[Chibcha language]] of the [[Muisca people]] who inhabited the central highlands of present-day Colombia before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]]. It is composed of the words ''Si''; "here", ''a''; "from, taste, smell", ''chó''; "good" and ''que''; "vigorous fortress", translating as "place of good smells and strong and vigorous cultures" or "Land of the vigorous taste".<ref name=websiteSiachoque/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Siachoque] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Siachoque was part of the loose [[Muisca Confederation]], the former country in the Colombian [[Andes]] and the ''[[cacique]]'' of Siachoque was loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. At the defeat of the latter the municipality became part of the [[New Kingdom of Granada]] and modern Siachoque was founded on August 2, 1556 by [[friar]] Jerónimo de Peralta.<ref name=websiteSiachoque/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Siachoque are [[agriculture]]; [[potato]]es, ''[[Solanum phureja]]'' (''papa criolla''), [[maize]], [[oat]], [[barley]] and [[wheat]], [[livestock]] farming and processing of meat and dairy products.<ref name=websiteSiachoque/>
