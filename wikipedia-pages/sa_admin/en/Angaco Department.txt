'''Angaco''' is a department of the province of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]], Argentina.<ref>{{cite journal | last=Perucca | first=Laura P. | last2=Moreiras | first2=Stella M. | title=Liquefaction phenomena associated with historical earthquakes in San Juan and Mendoza Provinces, Argentina | journal=Quaternary International | publisher=Elsevier BV | volume=158 | issue=1 | year=2006| doi=10.1016/j.quaint.2006.05.023 | pages=96–109}}</ref> It is located in the center east of the province, especially in a desert landscape with [[mountains]]. Characterized by their remarkable wine production.

In November 2023, the Chinese mining company Hua Lian Mining announced the exploration of lithium in the San Juan province, specifically in an area that includes Angaco and Mogna.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Angaco y Mogna en le mira de una empresa china para la exploración de litio |url=https://www.diariohuarpe.com/nota/minera-china-anuncio-la-exploracion-de-litio-en-angaco-y-mogna-2023112418300 |access-date=2025-02-10 |website=Diario Huarpe |language=es}}</ref> 

== Toponymy ==
'''Angaco home''' means Araucanian [[water]] or streams that are at the foot of a hill.
