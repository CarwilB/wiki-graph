'''Antolín del Campo''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Antolín del Campo}}) is a municipality of [[Isla Margarita]] in the state of [[Nueva Esparta]], [[Venezuela]], named in honour of Lieutenant Colonel [[Francisco Antolín del Campo]], a hero of the [[Battle of Matasiete]] during the [[Venezuelan War of Independence]]. The municipality occupies an area of 72 km² with a population of 28,089 inhabitants in 2011 and the capital is [[La Plaza de Paraguachí]].

==Name==
The municipality was named in honour of Lieutenant Colonel [[Francisco Antolín del Campo]], a hero of the [[Battle of Matasiete]] during the [[Venezuelan War of Independence]]. There are tributes to him through several local landmarks, including a public [[plaza]] inaugurated in 1994, a commemorative golden [[bust (sculpture)|bust]], and an educational institution named ''Unidad Educativa Básica Antolín del Campo''.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://albaciudad.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Nueva-Esparta-AntolindelCampo.pdf|title=Muncipio Antolín del Campo|trans-title=Antolín del Campo Municipality|lang=es|website=Alba Ciudad 96.3 FM|publisher={{ill|Ministry of Culture (Venezuela)|es|Ministerio del Poder Popular para la Cultura (Venezuela)|lt=Ministry of Culture}}|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Antolín del Campo Municipality was 28,089 people, accounting for 5.64% of the total population of the state of [[Nueva Esparta]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/17__nueva_esparta/|title=Nueva Esparta (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> All of the population resides in [[La Plaza de Paraguachí]], the [[municipal seat]] of the municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/nuevaesparta/1701__antol%C3%ADn_del_campo/|title=Urban Localities in Antolín del Campo (Nueva Esparta, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Antolín del Campo as 34,734 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 21.7% since 2011 and showing a population density of 482.5 inhabitants/km² in an area of 72 km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/nueva_esparta/1701__antol%C3%ADn_del_campo/|title=Antolín del Campo (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population showed an even balance with 14,346 men (50.7%) and 13,948 women (49.3%). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 68.1% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 24.6% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for the remaining 7.2%. All of the municipality's inhabitants live in the [[urban center]] of the capital city [[La Plaza de Paraguachí]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (48.5%) and [[Mestizo]] (46.1%). Minority groups included 2.2% [[Afro-Venezuelans]], 2.4% belonging to other ethnic groups, and 0.7% identifying as [[indigenous peoples|indigenous]]. The [[literacy rate]] was 97.1%, with 677 inhabitants of Antolín del Campo not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Economy==
Within the Antolín del Campo's economy, the coastal town of [[Manzanillo, Venezuela|Manzanillo]] relies on [[artisanal fishing]], where local livelihoods depend primarily on abundant [[sardine]] catches and seasonal [[octopus]] harvesting. However, this sector faces significant financial strain due to low profit margins driven by third-party resellers, high equipment maintenance costs, and reported extortion by regional security forces.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://elsoldemargarita.com.ve/posts/post/id:191910/Para-pescadores-de-Manzanillo|title=Para pescadores de Manzanillo, la sardina es una bendición |trans-title=For fishermen in Manzanillo, sardines are a blessing|lang=es|first=Elías|last=Marcano|newspaper=[[El Sol de Margarita]]|date=1 July 2017|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>
