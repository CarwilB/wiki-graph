'''Bezerros''' ({{IPA|pt|biˈzeʁus}}) is a [[municipality]] in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]], near the city of [[Gravatá]]. Its population estimate  was 65.007 (2025) and its area is 497,156 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" /> Also known as '''Papangu city''', because a festive tradition in which the people dress masks of all types during the [[Carnival]] festivities. During the carnival time, it is one of the most visited cities in the interior of the [[Pernambuco|state]]. Also, it was a center of [[coffee]] production, an activity which is no longer important in the region. It is much visited during Carnival; tourists and locals love to see papangus parading in city streets.
