'''San Juan de Lopecancha''' is a district of [[Luya Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-hallan-nuevo-complejo-arqueologico-provincia-amazonense-luya-316619.aspx|title=Hallan nuevo complejo arqueológico en provincia amazonense de Luya|work=[[Andina (news agency)|Andina]]|date=11 September 2010|access-date=11 December 2025|language=es}}</ref> One of its major points of interest are the ruins of Chachapoya.

San Juan de Lopecancha borders at the north with the [[Tingo District]]; at the East with [[Magdalena District, Chachapoyas]] and [[La Jalca District|La Jalca District, Chachapoyas]]; at the South with the [[Santo Tomás District, Luya]]; and at the west with [[María District]].
