'''Reforma''' is a [[city]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 40,711,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Reforma|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=074|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=2 October 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211002100716/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=074|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 34,809 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Reforma|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07074a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=August 1, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930024227/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07074a.htm|archive-date=September 30, 2007}}</ref> It covers an area of 434.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the city of Reforma had a population of 26,257.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Reforma, the municipality had 33 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[El Carmen (El Limón), Chiapas|El Carmen (El Limón)]] (2,182), [[Rafael Pascacio Gamboa, Chiapas|Rafael Pascacio Gamboa]] (1,133), and [[Miguel Hidalgo, Chiapas|Miguel Hidalgo]] (1,056), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

On May 8, 2019, an illegal gasoline truck exploded in Reforma.<ref>[https://www.infobae.com/america/mexico/2019/05/09/explota-ducto-de-pemex-en-chiapas-se-reportan-heridos/ Explotó ducto de Pemex en Chiapas: reportan varios muertos] Infobae, 9 May 2019  [https://www.diariodemexico.com/explota-ducto-de-pemex-en-chiapas-durante-robo-de-combustible Explota ducto de Pemex en Chiapas durante robo de combustible] Diario de México, 9 Jan 2019</ref> Mayor Herminio Valdez Castillo said the explosion occurred in an uninhabited area and there were no victims.<ref>{{cite web|website=Forbes México|title=Estalla ducto de Pemex en Chiapas; señalan a huachicoleros|language=es|trans-title=Pemex pipeline explodes in Chiapas; point to huachicoleros|date=May 9, 2019 |access-date= Feb 2, 2020|url=https://www.forbes.com.mx/estalla-ducto-de-pemex-en-chiapas-no-se-reportan-victimas/}}</ref>
