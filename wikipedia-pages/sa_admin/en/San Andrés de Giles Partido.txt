'''San Andres de Giles Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]]. The capital of the partido is [[San Andrés de Giles]], {{convert|103|km}} west of [[Buenos Aires]] and {{convert|170|km}} west of the provincial capital [[La Plata]]. The provincial subdivision has a population of 20,829 inhabitants (2001) in an area of {{convert|1132|km2}}.

It is bordered by [[Exaltación de la Cruz Partido|Exaltación de la Cruz]] in the northeast, [[Mercedes Partido|Mercedes]] in the south, [[Luján Partido|Luján]] in the southeast, [[Suipacha Partido|Suipacha]] in the southeast, [[Carmen de Areco Partido|Carmen de Areco]] in the east and [[San Antonio de Areco Partido|San Antonio de Areco]] in the northeast.
