'''Paltas Canton''' is located to the south of [[Loja, Ecuador|Loja]] city<ref>{{Cite web |title=Paltas Canton, Loja Province, Ecuador |url=https://www.mindat.org/loc-250226.html |access-date=2022-09-02 |website=www.mindat.org}}</ref> and is one of the oldest cantons of province of the same name. It was named after the Paltas, a community who used to live over the new city now called Catacocha in [[Ecuador]].

[[Shiriculapo]] it is a pick of the mountain which holds the capital, [[Catacocha]].  A nice temperature but mostly foggy during night, even though down the valley of Casanga, Macandamine, Yamana, Playas y Naranjo has a temperature that goes over the normal at its capital.
