'''Bolívar Partido ''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' ([[second level administrative subdivision]]) located in central [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The ''partido'' has <span style="white-space:nowrap">5,027&nbsp;km²&nbsp;(1,941&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span> and a population of 32,442 ({{census-ar|2001}}), and its capital is [[San Carlos de Bolívar]].
