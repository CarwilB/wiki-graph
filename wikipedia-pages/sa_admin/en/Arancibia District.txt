'''Arancibia''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Puntarenas (canton)|Puntarenas]] canton, in the [[Puntarenas Province|Puntarenas]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |date=19 March 2019 |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |trans-title=Official declaration for administrative purposes, the approval of the Administrative Territorial Division of the Republic No. 41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |accessdate=26 September 2020 |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |lang=es}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica |date=8 March 2017 |publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional |isbn=978-9977-58-477-5 |trans-title=Administrative Territorial Division of the Republic of Costa Rica |lang=es}}</ref>
== History ==
Arancibia was created on 9 November 2000 by Ley 8044.<ref name="dta2017" />
== Geography ==
Arancibia has an area of {{cvt|45.04|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |trans-title=Area in square kilometres, according to province, canton and administrative district |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |archive-date=24 October 2020 |accessdate=26 September 2020 |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |lang=es}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:560}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
