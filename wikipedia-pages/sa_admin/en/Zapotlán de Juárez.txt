''' Zapotlán de Juárez ''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 131.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 16,493.<ref>{{cite web|title=Zapotlán de Juárez|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13082a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 27, 2008|archive-date=January 29, 2007|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129103150/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13082a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In this municipality is produced agricole products and meat.

The municipality has 20&nbsp;km of the Mexico-Pachuca Highway and has another 18&nbsp;km of federal roads between the three principal towns of the municipality (Zapotlán, [[San Pedro Huaquilpan]] and [[Acayuca]]).

The municipality is located in the southern part of Hidalgo and borders on the municipalities of [[Pachuca]] to the North, [[Tolcayuca]] to the South and West, [[Zempoala]] to the East, and [[Tezontepec]] to the South.
