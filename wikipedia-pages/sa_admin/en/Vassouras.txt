'''Vassouras''' ({{IPA|pt|vɐˈsowɾɐs|lang}}, lit. 'Brooms') is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 37,083 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|552|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

Vassouras was the setting for the [[Bangu, Rio de Janeiro|Bangu]] neighborhood portrayed in the 1970 Brazilian film ''[[Meu Pé de Laranja Lima (film)|My Sweet Orange Tree]]'', as well as the fictional town of "Florença" depicted in the 2001 miniseries ''[[Presença de Anita]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-03-06 |title=Dica da Folha - Folha Fluminense .: Jornalismo com Responsabilidade Social :. |url=http://folhafluminense.com/page/dicas.asp?categoria=5 |access-date=2023-08-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160306111934/http://folhafluminense.com/page/dicas.asp?categoria=5 |archive-date=2016-03-06 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Xavier |first=Nilson |title=Presença de Anita |url=http://teledramaturgia.com.br/presenca-de-anita/ |access-date=2023-08-07 |website=Teledramaturgia |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
