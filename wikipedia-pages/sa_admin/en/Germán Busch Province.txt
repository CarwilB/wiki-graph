'''Germán Busch''' is a [[province]] in the [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]], [[Bolivia]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=admins5 |date=2015-12-29 |title=Provincia Germán Busch (Mapa) |url=https://www.educa.com.bo/geografia/provincia-german-busch-mapa |access-date=2026-01-01 |website=www.educa.com.bo |language=es}}</ref> Most of the surface is covered by the [[Pantanal]], the largest wetland area in the world.

==History==
The province was founded on 30 November 1984 by rearranging the provinces of [[Ángel Sandoval Province|Ángel Sandoval]] and [[Chiquitos Province|Chiquitos]]. It is named after [[Germán Busch]], a former Bolivian military officer and president of Bolivia who is regarded as a war hero for his role in the [[Chaco War]] between Bolivia and [[Paraguay]].

==Subdivision==
The province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].<ref name=":0" />
