'''Una''' is a city in [[Bahia]], [[Brazil]]. Its population in 2020 was 18,544 inhabitants. It is located about {{convert|36|mi|km|abbr=on}} south from [[Ilheus]].

The municipality was founded on August 2, 1890. The GDP per capita is [[Brazilian real|R$]] 2,649 (IBGE / 2005).
[[Una-Comandatuba Airport]] serves both the city of Una and Transamérica Resort, located at nearby Comandatuba Island.

==Geography==

The climate is hot and humid, with annual rainfall over {{convert|1800|mm}}.
The temperature varies from {{convert|19|to|28|C}}, with an average of {{convert|24|C}}.
The municipality contains the {{convert|18715|ha}} [[Una Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1980.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Una|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=202|access-date=2016-04-29}}</ref>
It contains most of the {{convert|23262|ha}} [[Una Wildlife Refuge]], which surrounds the biological reserve.<ref>{{citation |title=As limitações administrativas da união no refúgio de vida silvestre de Una, Sul da Bahia|journal=Direito Ambiental e sociedade|volume=2 |issue=1|year=2012|page=302
