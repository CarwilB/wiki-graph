'''Taulabé''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua (department)|Comayagua]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|219.6|km2}} and had a population of 24,235 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History == 
In 1582, the indigenous people who resided in the Tencoa Valley, along the banks of the [[Ulúa River]] migrated and settled on the banks of the Tamalito River. During the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonial occupation, it became a trading center with the native population in the central Honduras. Hence, Taulabé is derived from [[Lenca language]] word "Tarule" or "Taulepa", which translates to "meeting place of roads and travelers". After Honduran independence in 1821, there was significant migration to the town from other parts of the country, and in 1864, they purchased land from the government for settlement. In 1969, the construction of Northern Highway, a main arterial road, began, and helped in the commercial development of the region. Till 1987, it was part of the municipality of [[Siguatepeque]], and it was established as a new municipality on 20 May 1987.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Taulabe, Comayagua|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Comayagua/Taulabe.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Taulabe|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-taulabe/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Taulabe is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua Department|Comayagua]] in Honduras.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://proceso.hn/honduras-registra-150-municipios-libres-de-incendios-forestales-en-2025/|title=Honduras registra 150 municipios libres de incendios forestales en 2025|work=Proceso Digital|date=30 May 2025|access-date=26 November 2025|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|219.6|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/comayagua/0321__taulab%C3%A9/|title=Taulabe, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Taulabe has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|23.23|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|268.77|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/comayagua/taulabe|title=Taulabe, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Taulabé had a total population of 24,235 inhabitants, of whom 11,842 (48.9%) were men and 12,393 (51.1%) were women. About 7,033 (29.0%) of the population was classified as urban and 17,202 residents (71.0%) lived in the rural areas.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 8,746 individuals (36.1%) were aged 0–14 years, 14,073 individuals (58.1%) were aged 15–64 (consisting of 11,968 individuals aged 15–49 and 2,105 individuals aged 50–64), and 1,416 individuals (5.8%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20.8 years and the mean age was 25.8 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 14.8%, slightly higher than the departmental average of 14.6%. The municipality had 5,503 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.3 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

The economy is dependent on [[agriculture]] and major produce include [[lemon]], [[sugercane]], and [[coffee]]. [[Livestock]] rearing and honey collection are also prevalent in the region.<ref name="Red"/>
