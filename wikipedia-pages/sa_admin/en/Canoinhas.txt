'''Canoinhas''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]], in the north plateau of the State of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. Its latitude is 26°10'38" South, its longitude is 50°23'24" West, and its altitude is 839 meters. The population was estimated in 2020 at 54,480 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/canoinhas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The area of the city is 1143.6 square kilometers.

The city's economy primarily depends on [[agriculture]] and [[logging]].

The city is also known worldwide as the world capital of Erva Mate, a herb used in a traditional drink called [[chimarrao]], the herb [[Ilex Paraguariensis]] has been a main export of the region for quite some time.

==History==
Francisco de Paula Pereira is known to be the founder of the town, it was a popular stop for immigrants from [[Paraná (state)|Parana]] looking for the famous ''Erva Mate''
