'''Huanza District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The ''Cordillera de la Corte'' traverses the district. Some of the highest peaks are [[Chunta (Junín-Lima)|Chunta]] and [[Kashpi]] on the northeastern border of the district.
