'''Venha-Ver''' (lit. "come see") is the westernmost city in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]]. The city's name means "Come-to-see-it" in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]. The highest point of the state is located there, in the [[Serra do Coqueiro]] mountain range, at the triple border of Rio Grande do Norte, [[Paraíba]] and [[Ceará]].
