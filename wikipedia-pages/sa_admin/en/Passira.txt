'''Passira''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[pɐˈsiɾɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==

* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Limoeiro]] and [[Salgadinho, Pernambuco|Salgadinho]]   ('''N'''); [[Gravatá]], [[Bezerros]] and [[Pombos]]   ('''S''');  [[Cumaru]]   ('''W''');  [[Feira Nova, Pernambuco|Feira Nova]] and [[Glória do Goitá]]   ('''E''')
* Area - 329.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 176 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe river]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] hot
* Average annual temperature - 24.1&nbsp;°C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 115&nbsp;km
* Population - 28,894 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/passira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Passira''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[tomato]]es, [[Maize|corn]] and [[bean]]s, and the rearing of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]] and [[horse]]s.
