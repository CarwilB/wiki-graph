'''Chalcatongo de Hidalgo''' (also, '''Chalcatongo''' and '''Villa Hidalgo''') ({{langx|mix|Ñuundaya|}}, 'place of the underworld'<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Whittaker |first1=Gordon |title=The Study of North Mesoamerican Place-Signs |journal=INDIANA - Estudios Antropológicos sobre América Latina y el Caribe |date=1 January 1993 |volume=13 |page=31 |doi=10.18441/ind.v13i0.9-38 |url=https://journals.iai.spk-berlin.de/index.php/indiana/article/view/1812}}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the [[States of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Oaxaca]]. It is part of the [[Tlaxiaco District]] in the south of the [[Mixteca Region]].<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20026a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128225645/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20026a.htm|archive-date=January 28, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

It is the birthplace of former [[Governor of Oaxaca|Governor]] [[Ulises Ruiz Ortiz]].
