'''Arauá''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ɐɾɐˈwɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 9,947 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|193|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/araua/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
