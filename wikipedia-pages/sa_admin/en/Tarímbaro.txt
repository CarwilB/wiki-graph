'''Tarímbaro''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. The municipality has an area of 258.57 square kilometres (0.43% of the surface of the state) and is bordered to the north by [[Copándaro]], [[Meson Nuevo]] and [[Cuitzeo]], to the east by [[Álvaro Obregón (municipality)|Álvaro Obregón]], to the south by [[Morelia]] and [[Charo, Michoacán|Charo]], and to the west by [[Chucándiro (municipality)|Chucándiro]].  The municipality had a population of 51,479 inhabitants according to the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web

==Economy==
The economy of the municipality is mostly based on agriculture and ranching.

==History==
In [[pre-Hispanic]] times the region of present-day Tarímbaro was inhabited by the semi-[[nomadic]] [[Chichimeca]] (who knew the area "place of the willows") and the [[Purépecha people]] of Central Mexico.

As of March 3, 2021, the municipality had reported 625 confirmed cases and 77 deaths from the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus (COVID-19) en Tarímbaro, Michoacán |url=http://www.gncys.com/covid19/covid19-municipio.aspx?edoid=MN&munid=088 |website=gncys.com |access-date=March 5, 2021 |language=es-mx |date=1 February 2020}}</ref> Five-time mayor Baltazar Gaona Sánchez, 80 ([[Party of the Democratic Revolution|PRD]]), became victim number 78 on March 5.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Martínez Elorriaga |first1=Ernesto |first2= |title=Fallece presidente municipal de Tarímbaro por Covid-19 |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/03/05/estados/fallece-presidente-municipal-de-tarimbaro-por-covid-19/ |access-date=March 5, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=March 5, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>
