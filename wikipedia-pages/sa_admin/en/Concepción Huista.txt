'''Concepción Huista''' ({{IPA|es|konseβˈsjon ˈwista}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]].
