'''Pacatuba''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern Portuguese pronunciation: </small>[pɐkɐˈtubɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 14,540 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/pacatuba/panorama |access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|364|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|5547|ha}} [[Santa Isabel Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1988.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Santa Isabel|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=199|accessdate=2016-04-17}}</ref>
