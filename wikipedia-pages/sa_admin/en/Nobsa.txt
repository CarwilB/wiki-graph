'''Nobsa''' is a town and municipality in [[Sugamuxi Province]], part of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]] [[Departments of Colombia|department]]. The urban centre is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at {{convert|125|km|mi}} from the national capital [[Bogotá]] at an elevation of {{convert|2510|m|ft}}. Other parts of the municipality range in elevation between {{convert|2500|m|ft}} and {{convert|3300|m|ft}}. The [[Chicamocha River]] flows through Nobsa. The municipality borders [[Santa Rosa de Viterbo, Boyacá|Santa Rosa de Viterbo]] and [[Floresta, Boyacá|Floresta]] in the north, [[Corrales, Boyacá|Corrales]] and [[Tópaga, Boyacá|Tópaga]] in the east, [[Tibasosa]] and Santa Rosa de Viterbo in the west and Tibasosa and Santa Rosa de Viterbo in the south.<ref name=websiteNobsa>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.nobsa-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Nobsa] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150604005259/http://www.nobsa-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-06-04 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Nobsa is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Decent bath of today".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Nobsa] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], the area of Nobsa was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a loose confederation of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Nobsa was ruled by the ''[[Tundama]]'' of [[Duitama|Tundama]].

Modern Nobsa was founded on January 9, 1593 by Jerónimo Holguín and Misael Millán.<ref name=websiteNobsa/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Nobsa are [[agriculture]], [[livestock]] farming, [[mining]] (predominantly [[limestone]]) and the production of wooden furniture.<ref name=websiteNobsa/>
