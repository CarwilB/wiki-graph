'''San Gregorio''' is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the far south of Chile. It is part of [[Magallanes Region]] and [[Magallanes Province|Province]], and is administered by the municipality of the same name located in [[Punta Delgada, Chile|Punta Delgada]], the principal town in the commune (the coordinates are those of Punta Delgada).

The comuna is on the north shore of the [[Strait of Magellan]]; between the Strait, to the south, and the border with [[Argentina]], to the north. At the [[Primera Angostura]], south of the town of Punta Delgada, there is a [[ferry]] crossing to [[Primavera, Chile|Primavera]] commune on [[Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego|Tierra del Fuego Island]]. The good wind resource attracted a [[Wind power in Chile|wind power]] project, scheduled for 2025.<ref>{{cite web |title=Minister Jobet announces new green hydrogen project in Magallanes that will be the largest in Chile |url=https://chilereports.cl/en/news/2021/12/07/minister-jobet-announces-new-green-hydrogen-project-in-magallanes-that-will-be-the-largest-in-chile |language=en |date=7 December 2021}}</ref>

==Features==
In the settlement of Estancia San Gregorio ({{coord|-52.566944|-70.07|region:CL-MA_scale:10000|format=dms}}), some {{convert|40|km|mi|abbr=on}} southwest of Punta Delgada, there is an [[estancia]], and several imposing buildings dating from 1882.

Nearby are wrecks of two 19th-century [[cargo ship]]s.<ref>{{cite book |title=Rough Guide to Chile |last=Graham |first=Melissa |year=2003 |page=470 |isbn=1-84353-062-7}}</ref> {{ship||Ambassador|clipper|2}} was a sailing tea [[clipper]], built in [[London]] in 1869 and beached at San Gregorio in 1896.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.histarmar.com.ar/InfHistorica/Last%20Port%20of%20Call/Ambassador.htm |title=Last port: Ultimo Puerto de Amarre – Ambassador |last=Seidel |first=Guido |publisher=Histarmar – Historia y Arqueologia Marítima |language=es |accessdate=5 February 2015}}</ref> ''Amadeo'' was a [[steamship]], built in [[Liverpool]] in 1884 and beached at San Gregorio in 1932.<ref>{{cite book |last=Heaton |first=Paul M |year=2004 |title=Lamport & Holt Line |publisher=P.M. Heaton Publishing |place=Abergavenny |isbn=1-872006-16-7 |pages=29, 34, 35}}</ref>

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Gregorio spans an area of {{convert|6883.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 1,158 inhabitants (886 men and 272 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population fell by 29.5% (485 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= INE/>

The population is mostly descended from [[Croats|Croatian]], [[Spanish people|Spanish]] and other [[Europe]]ans.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.hacerfamilia.net/new/index.asp?pag=articulos&id=687 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20070515130645/http://www.hacerfamilia.net/new/index.asp?pag=articulos&id=687 |url-status=dead |archive-date=2007-05-15 |title=San Gregorio |language=es }}</ref>

==Administration==
As a comuna, San Gregorio is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012–2016 alcalde is Edgar Cárcamo Alderete.<ref name=Munitel/><ref name=Official/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Gregorio is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region).
