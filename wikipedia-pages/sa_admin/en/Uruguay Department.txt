The '''Uruguay Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Uruguay''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Entre Ríos Province|Entre Ríos]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.revistachacra.com.ar/agricultura/55747/|title=Entre Ríos: cuánto debe cosechar un productor de soja para cubrir costos|work=Revista Chacra|date=25 January 2024|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located in the south-east of the province, beside the [[Uruguay River]].

The head town is [[Concepción del Uruguay]].
