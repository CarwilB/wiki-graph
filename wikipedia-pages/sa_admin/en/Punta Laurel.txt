'''Punta Laurel''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Bocas del Toro District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|71.9|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,730 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|24|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 692; its population as of 2000 was 966.<ref name=inec/>
