'''Jocón''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Yoro Department|Yoro]], Honduras.

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Jocón municipality had a population of 9,591. Of these, 98.78% were [[Mestizo]], 0.76% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.30% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.15% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.01% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
