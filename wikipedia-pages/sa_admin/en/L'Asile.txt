'''L'Asile''' ({{IPA|fr|lazil}}; {{langx|ht|Lazil}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Anse-à-Veau Arrondissement]], in the [[Nippes]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 41,073 inhabitants in 2015.

The name comes from French meaning "the asylum". Probably it was used for an asylum during the [[Saint-Domingue|colonial era]].
