'''Jalacingo''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central area of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], in the region called the Capital, on the border with the state of [[Puebla]]. It is located at the coordinates [https://geohack.toolforge.org/geohack.php?language=es&pagename=Jalacingo&params=19.8_N_-97.3_E_type:city 19°36'N 97°13'W], the city has a height of 1860 meters above sea level, while the height of the municipality varies from 600 to 2900 meters above sea level.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 46,794 inhabitants in 39 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSJALACINGO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Jalacingo.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Coacoatzintla CENSUS]</ref> About 30% resides in the municipal seat; other localities include Mixquiapan (3,067 hab.), Orilla del Monte (2,680 hab.), Ocotepec (2,559 hab.), and Vista Hermosa (2,198 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSJALACINGO/>
