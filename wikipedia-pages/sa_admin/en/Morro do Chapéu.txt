'''Morro do Chapéu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Bahia]], 386&nbsp;km [[Ordinal directions|northwest]] of its capital, [[Salvador, Bahia|Salvador]], 1012 meters above the [[sea level]], because of which it is one of the coolest towns in Bahia, with temperatures occasionally falling below 10&nbsp;°C in the winter.  Its estimated population in 2020 is 35,440.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/morro-do-chapeu/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Overview==
The municipality contains the {{convert|46000|ha}} [[Morro do Chapéu State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PES Morro do Chapéu – ISA}}
 |title=PES Morro do Chapéu|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591490|access-date=2017-01-21}}</ref>
Standing at [[Chapada Diamantina National Park|Chapada Diamantina]] region, its main attractions are [http://www.morroacontece.com/2016/08/pontos-turisticos-de-morro-do-chapeu.html Ferro Doido] [http://www.fotosdachapada.com] [[waterfall]] and Brejões [[cave]], both among the most beautiful natural monuments in Bahia, but are almost unexplored by tourists given to the city's lack of infrastructure. There are several other waterfalls and caves around the town.

==Climate==
Morro do Chapéu has a [[tropical savanna climate]] with warm summers and mild to warm, dry winters.
