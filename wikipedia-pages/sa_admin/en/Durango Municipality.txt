'''Durango''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Durango, Durango|Victoria de Durango]], the state capital. The municipality covers an area of 10,041&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 582,267,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web |title=Durango |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=005 |work=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL) |access-date=23 April 2014 |archive-date=4 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304041252/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=005 |url-status=live }}</ref> up from 526,659 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=Durango |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10005a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=November 18, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070528124241/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10005a.htm |archive-date=May 28, 2007}}</ref> 

The municipality had 1,323 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Victoria de Durango, Durango|Victoria de Durango]] (518,709), [[El Nayar, Durango|El Nayar]] (3,308), [[Cinco de Mayo, Durango|Cinco de Mayo]] (2,249), classified as urban, and [[La Ferrería (Cuatro de Octubre), Durango|La Ferrería (Cuatro de Octubre)]] (2,021), [[José María Pino Suárez, Durango|José María Pino Suárez]] (2,014), [[Colonia Hidalgo, Durango|Colonia Hidalgo]] (1,986), [[Llano Grande, Durango|Llano Grande]] (1,938), [[Sebastián Lerdo de Tejada, Durango|Sebastián Lerdo de Tejada]] (1,712), [[Villa Montemorelos, Durango|Villa Montemorelos]] (1,617), [[Banderas del Águila, Durango|Banderas del Águila]] (1,274), [[José Refugio Salcido, Durango|José Refugio Salcido]] (1,262), [[Santiago Bayacora, Durango|Santiago Bayacora]] (1,218), [[Cinco de Febrero, Durango|Cinco de Febrero]] (1,131), [[José María Morelos y Pavón (La Tinaja), Durango|José María Morelos y Pavón (La Tinaja)]] (1,072), and [[El Arenal (San Jerónimo), Durango|El Arenal (San Jerónimo)]] (1,015), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
