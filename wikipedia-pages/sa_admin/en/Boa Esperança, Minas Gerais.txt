'''Boa Esperança''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] from the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].
The municipality is located by the [[Serra da Boa Esperança]], which became celebrated through [[Serra da Boa Esperança (song)|song that brings its name]], compounded by [[Lamartine Babo]] and performed by several singers. Across its territory passes [[Rio Grande (Minas Gerais)|Rio Grande]], important to the development of the region. The economy of Boa Esperança is based on [[coffee]] plantations and [[tourism]].

==History==
The municipality of Boa Esperança ({{langx|en|Good Hope}}) came up with [[gold rush]] of the late 19th century. The first settlement was established in 1778, and in 1804 it was called '''Dores do Pântano'' ({{langx|en|Pain Swamp}}).  In 1814 started the construction of the Church, and at that time arose the first [[coffee]] plantations, which marked a phase of wealth and progress for the [[parish]]. In 1866 the parish was elevated by provincial law to the status of [[village]]. On 15 November 1868, the [[seat of local government]] was built and the village was elevated by Provincial Law No. 1611 to the status of [[municipality]]. In 1957–1958, with the construction of the [[Furnas Dam]] and subsequently of the Furnas Lake, the town began to grow as a result of [[tourism]].

==Geography==
Boa Esperança is located in the southern-[[Tropic of Capricorn|tropical]] part of Brazil, at 21 degrees, 5 minutes, 24 second south, and 45 degrees, 33 minutes, 57 seconds west, at an altitude of {{Convert|775|m|ft}}, in the internal part of the [[State of Minas Gerais]]. It covers an area of {{Convert|860.669|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="area" />

===Climate===
Situated on the southern outskirts of the tropical zone and under the influence of the high elevation of the region, the climate type is [[Tropical savanna climate|tropical]] [[mesothermal]] with an average annual rainfall of about {{Convert|1,500|mm|in}}.  The annual temperature is {{Convert|19|C|F}}.  Summer and spring are the hottest seasons with a maximum daily temperature ranging from {{convert|25|to|29|C|F}}. November, December, and January are the warmest months reaching {{Convert|36|-|37|C|F}} and with a minimum temperature of {{Convert|9|–|10|C|F}}.

===Rivers===
[[Grande River (Paraná)|Grande River]], a tributary  of the [[Paraná River]], Ribeirão São Pedro, Ribeirão das Três Pontas, Ribeirão Marimbondo, Ribeirão da Maricota. The [[Furnas Dam]] borders the municipality.

===Fauna and flora===
Boa Esperança is home to several species of [[bird]]s such as [[darter]]s, [[heron]]s, [[roadside hawk]]s, [[moorhen]]s, [[lapwing]]s, [[jabiru]]s, [[kingfisher]]s, and [[grassquit]]s.{{Citation needed|date=March 2024}}

Among the mammals in the area there are [[capybara]], [[brocket deer]], [[monkey]], [[fox]], [[otter]], [[paca]], [[agouti]], [[megabat]], [[skunk]], and others.  Some of these species may have migrated from other areas.

In this area there are [[forest]]s and [[araucaria]]s rich in vegetation, including [[mimosa]], [[cedrus]], [[cinnamomum]], [[sassafras]], [[manilkara bidentata]], [[Morus (plant)|morus]], [[Hymenaea courbaril]], [[copaiba]], [[Swietenia macrophylla]], and [[Balfourodendron riedelianum]]. In [[riparian forest]]s there are [[Croton (plant)|croton]], [[inga]], and [[pine]].  Scattered trees, [[shrub]]s and sub-shrubs present [[Annona crassiflora]], [[Stryphnodendron adstringens]], [[Stenocalyx dysentericus]], [[Tabebuia ochracea]], [[Kielmeyera coriacea]].

==Economy==
Boa Esperança is an agro-pastoral Municipality. It produces [[Food grain|grain]], [[rice]], [[garlic]], [[potato]]es, [[bean]]s, [[maize]], [[sugarcane]], [[cassava]], [[soybean]], [[tomato]]es, several species of [[fruit]], and [[coffee]].  Coffee is the main product and it has a good level of production.  It is consumed domestically and exported to other countries. [[Livestock]], [[domestic pig]]s, and [[poultry]] are some of the oldest economic activities in the area. Trade, the "royalties" paid by Furnas Central Electric, and tourism are among the main activities of the [[Tertiary sector of the economy|tertiary sector]].
