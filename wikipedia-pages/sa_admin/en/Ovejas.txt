'''Ovejas''' is a [[town]] and municipality located in the [[Sucre Department]], northern [[Colombia]] where the traditional [[Cumbia]] (also called Gaita) festival "Francisco Llirene" takes place in October.<ref name = "festival">[http://www.musicastradicionalescolombianas.org/network-festivals/caribbean-node/francisco-llirene-gaitas-national-festival?lang=en Information on the Francisco Llirene Festival]</ref>
