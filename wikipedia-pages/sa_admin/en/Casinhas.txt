'''Casinhas''' or '''little houses''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kaˈzĩj̃ɐ(s)]}}) (population: 13,468)<ref name="IBGE" /> is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has 125.28 sq/km of total area.

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]]  ('''N'''), [[Surubim]]   ('''S''')   [[Bom Jardim, Pernambuco|Bom Jardim]] and [[Orobó]]  ('''E''');  [[Vertentes do Lério]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 125.28&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 390 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and [[Goiana River]]s
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hipoxerófila]]
* Annual average temperature - 23.3 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 133&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Casinhas''' is [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s and [[sheep]].
