'''Pilar''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[piˈla]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 35,212 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/pilar/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|249|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> On March 28, 1876, Pilar carried out the last official [[Capital punishment|execution]] of Brazil when it hanged the slave [[Francisco (slave)|Francisco]].<ref>RIBEIRO, João Luiz. No Meio das Galinhas as Baratas Não Têm Razão: A Lei de 10 de Junho de 1835 - Os Escravos e a Pena de Morte no Império do Brasil (1822-1889). Rio de Janeiro, Editora Renovar, 2005.</ref><ref>LIMA Júnior, Félix. Última Execução Judicial no Brasil. Maceió: Edufal, 1979</ref>
