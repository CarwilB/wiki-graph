'''Condorcanqui''' is a province of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://elperuano.pe/noticia/285691-alistan-33-acciones-contra-la-mineria-ilegal-en-amazonas|title=Alistan 33 acciones contra la minería ilegal en Amazonas|work=[[El Peruano]]|date=24 February 2025|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> It was created by law 23832 of May 18, 1984, based on territories of the [[Bagua Province|province of Bagua]], covering the basins of the rivers [[Santiago River (Peru)|Santiago]], [[Cenepa River|Cenepa]] and [[Marañón River|Marañon]]. The province was named in honor to [[Tupac Amaru II]]

Its principal route is the fluvial one, it lasts three days of navigation to come to [[Santa María de Nieva|Santa Maria de Nieva]], capital of the province, furrowing the waters of the [[Marañón river]].
