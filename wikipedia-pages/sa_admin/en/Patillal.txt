'''Patillal''' is a village and [[corregimiento]] in the municipality of [[Valledupar]] within the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Cesar Department|Cesar]]. The town lies on the steps of the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] with a semi-arid terrain. 

==Geography==

The corregimiento of Patillal borders to the north with the [[Guajira Department]], to the west with the corregimiento of [[La Mina, Valledupar|La Mina]], to the southwest with the corregimiento of [[Los Haticos]], to the south with the corregimiento of [[Rio Seco, Valledupar|Rio Seco]] and [[La Vega Arriba]] and to the east with the corregimiento of [[Badillo]].{{fact|date=June 2020}} 

==History==
{{Update|section|date=June 2020}}
The region of what is now Patillal was a [[cattle]] [[herd]] owned by María Antonia de las Nieves de Maestre and set as corregimiento before 1960.<ref>[http://www.elpilon.com.co/noticias/not00008520.htm {{in lang|es}} El Pilon: Valledupar: Un vasto territorio]{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Culture==
{{Update|section|date=June 2020}}
The village is mostly known for its [[vallenato]] musical traditions and for being the cradle to many musicians of this genre, such as [[Rafael Escalona]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Martin |first=Douglas |date=2009-05-19 |title=Rafael Escalona, Colombian Folk Balladeer, Dies at 81 |url=https://www.nytimes.com/2009/05/19/arts/music/19escalona.html |access-date=2024-06-07 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref> The village celebrates the [[vallenato]] music [[Festival of the Land of Composers]] ({{langx|es|Festival Tierra de Compositores}}) and the traditional Roman Catholic mass of venerating the Virgin of Las Mercedes every September 24 of each year.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Festival Tierra de Compositores |url=https://elpilon.com.co/tag/festival-tierra-de-compositores/ |access-date=2024-06-07 |website=El Pilón {{!}} Noticias de Valledupar, El Vallenato y el Caribe Colombiano |language=en}}</ref>
