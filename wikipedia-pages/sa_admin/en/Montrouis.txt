'''Montrouis''' is a coastal [[communal section]] in [[Haiti]], located in the department of [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fallingrain.com/world/HA/11/Bois_Moquette.html |title=Montrouis, Haiti Page |accessdate=7 May 2014}}</ref> south of [[Saint-Marc]]. Montrouis is one of the most important beach tourism destinations in Haiti, with several well renowned hotels and resorts, including the [[Moulin-sur-Mer]]. The town is located on the [[Côtes-des-Arcadins]], one of Haiti's longest stretches of pure white sand beaches. It is also an exceptional place for sailing and fishing.

==25th Session of the IARP General Assembly==
The 25th session of the [[Amateur radio international operation]] general assembly was held in Montrouis in December 1994. This session adopted a convention allowing amateur radio operators to operate in an IARP member country temporarily while using a permit issued by another member country.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.oas.org/ext/en/main/oas/our-structure/agencies-and-entities/citel/Alliances/protocol-of-amendment-to-the-inter-american-convention-on-the-international-amateur-radio-permit-iarp-1994|title=Protocol of Amendment to the Inter-American Convention on the International Amateur Radio Permit (IARP) - 1994|accessdate=28 Dec 2023}}</ref>
