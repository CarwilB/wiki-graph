'''Vicente Noble''' is a municipality in [[Barahona Province|Barahona]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]].

It is bordered on the north by [[Padre Las Casas, Dominican Republic|Padre Las Casas]] municipality,  [[Azua Province]] and [[Vallejuelo]] in [[San Juan Province (Dominican Republic)|San Juan Province]]; to the east by the [[Azua de Compostela]] municipality; to the south by the [[Jaquimeyes]] section in Barahona Province; and to the west by [[Tamayo, Dominican Republic|Tamayo]] municipality, [[Neiba]] municipality, and the [[Yaque del Sur River]] that acts as a limit for both these municipalities.

Vicente Noble was founded by two families from Azua de Compostela. The town was given various names until it was called Vicente Noble by [[List of Presidents of the Dominican Republic|President]] [[Rafael Trujillo]] in commemoration of the actions of General Vicente Noble in battles against the Haitians.

The Vicente Noble municipality is primarily agricultural. The crops of highest productivity are [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]], banana, [[coconut]], tomatoes, [[Capsicum|pepper]], [[eggplant]], [[yucca]], rice, [[beans]], maize, "[[guandules]]" and [[sweet potato]].

Thousands of emigrants from Vicente Noble now live in Spain and the United Kingdom and do domestic work and construction, and many others practicing nursing, teaching and few have become professional actors, runners and footballers. Their remittance of money constitutes an important source of income for the community.
