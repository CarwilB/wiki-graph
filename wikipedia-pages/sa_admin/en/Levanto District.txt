'''Levanto''' is a district of the province of [[Chachapoyas Province|Chachapoyas]].

==History==
The area has been inhabited since pre-Hispanic times.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Campos-Napán |first1=Carlos E. |date=2020 |title=APROXIMACIONES HISTÓRICAS Y ARQUEOLÓGICAS A LA OCUPACIÓN INKA Y LOS CAMINOS PREHISPÁNICOS DE LEVANTO (AMAZONAS, PERÚ) |url=https://www.jstor.org/stable/27082155 |language=es |journal=[[Chungara (journal)|Chungara]] |publisher=[[University of Tarapacá]] |volume=52 |number=3 |via= |access-date=10 November 2024 }}</ref> It was one of the principal centers in the province of Chachapoyas. In 1538 [[Alonso de Alvarado]] founded the city of [[Chachapoyas, Peru]]. There are an enormous quantity of archaeological sites, including nine archaeological sites of major importance.

==Demographics==
According to the census carried out in 1993 and his projection. The district of Levanto has a population of 1,170 inhabitants for 2000 of whom 51% are men and the difference of 49% are women; most of the population is located in the Urban area with 57%; and in Rural area 43%, being his valuation of population growth raised in the order of 2.6%. 58% of the population is under the age of 25.
