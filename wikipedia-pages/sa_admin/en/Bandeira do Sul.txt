'''Bandeira do Sul''' is a  [[municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 5,778 people, living between 660 and 989 meters elevation.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/bandeira-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The area of Bandeira do Sul is {{cvt|46.9|km2}}. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of Sul e Sudoeste de Minas and to the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of [[Poços de Caldas]].
