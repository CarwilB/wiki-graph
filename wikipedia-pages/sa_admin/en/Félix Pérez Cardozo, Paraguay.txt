'''Félix Pérez Cardozo''' is a town and district located in the [[Guairá Department]] of Paraguay. It is located approximately {{cvt|186|km|abbr=on}} from the national capital [[Asunción]]. In this town, its inhabitants are dedicated to the cultivation of sugar cane and tobacco, as well as crafts, especially Ao po'i embroidery.

==History==
Félix Pérez Cardozo was founded by Governor Agustín Fernando de Pinedo on 15 September 1773; it was initially known by the name of Hyaty. In 1957, a decree by the Executive Branch changed the name of the town to [[Félix Pérez Cardozo]] in honor of the great Paraguayan harpist who was born in this place.<ref>{{cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=XqMlCgAAQBAJ&dq=F%C3%A9lix+P%C3%A9rez+Cardozo&pg=PA134|title=The Paraguayan Harp: From Colonial Transplant to National Emblem|first=Alfredo|last=Colman|page=134}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the data provided by the General Directorate of Statistical Surveys and Census, Félix Pérez Cardozo had a population of 6,156 inhabitants in 2017. Carrying out a relationship with the total population of the district, it had been apparent that 88.75% of the population resided in the rural areas.<ref name=":0"/>

==Transport==
The district of Félix Pérez Cardozo is served by the two highways. The first one is a land path connecting the city to the district of [[Yataity]]. The other one is a paved path connecting the city of [[Villarrica, Paraguay|Villarrica]] to the city of [[Paraguarí]] ([[Paraguarí Department]]).<ref>{{cite web|title=Rutas del Paraguay - Edicion Impresa - ABC Color|url=http://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/escolar/rutas-del-paraguay-572133.html|accessdate=1 February 2019|language=es|first=Paola|last=Dalles|date=14 May 2013|publisher=ABC Color}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Mapa del Paraguay - Red vial 2018|url=https://www.mopc.gov.py/mopcweb/userfiles/files/REDVIAL_NACIONAL_2018.jpg|work=www.mopc.gov.py|accessdate=1 February 2019|publisher=MOPC - Ministerio de Obras Publicas y Comunicaciones del Paraguay|language=es|archive-date=2 February 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190202041958/https://www.mopc.gov.py/mopcweb/userfiles/files/REDVIAL_NACIONAL_2018.jpg|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=MOPC elabora mapa actualizado de rutas del Paraguay|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/mopc-elabora-mapa-actualizado-rutas-del-paraguay-n773147.html|accessdate=1 February 2019|language=es|date=7 March 2014|publisher=Ultima Hora}}</ref>
