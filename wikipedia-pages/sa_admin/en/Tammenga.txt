'''Tammenga''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 15,819.<ref name="census"/> The resort has been named after Henderijkes Tammenga (1815-1864), who after his first plantation did not succeed, started farming in a [[swamp]]y area near Paramaribo in 1852.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.boeroes.nl/tammenga/tammenga.htm|title=Tammenga|website=Boeroes|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Most of the area was sold to [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]] indentured workers in the late 19th century.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-west|title=Paramaribo West|website=Vakantie Arena|language=nl|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>
