'''  Santa Cruz Amilpas ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in southeastern [[Mexico]] and is the second smallest municipality in Oaxaca, only being bigger than [[Natividad, Oaxaca|Natividad]]. However, it is also the most densely populated municipality in Oaxaca. The municipality covers an area of 2.27 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM20oaxaca/municipios/20375a.html|title=Enciclopedia de los municipios y delegaciones de México}}</ref> 
It is part of the [[Centro District, Oaxaca|Centro District]] in the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales region]].
In 2010, the municipality had a population of 10,120.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/48211/Oaxaca_375.pdf|title=Informe anual sobre la situación de pobreza y rezago soical}}</ref>
