'''Ixhuatlán del Café''' is a city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It serves as the municipal seat of the municipality of the same name.

The municipality covers a total surface area of 134.07&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and, in the 2020 [[census]], reported a population of 23,232.
It is located at {{coord|19|03|N|96|59|W|}}.

Its chief products are [[maize|corn]], [[coffee]] and [[fruit]]s.
