}}'''Providencia''' ({{IPA|es|pɾoβiˈðensja}}, [[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "providence") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Metropolitan Region|Santiago City]]. It is bordered by the communes of Santiago to the west, [[Recoleta, Chile|Recoleta]] to the northwest, [[Las Condes]] and [[Vitacura]] to the northeast, [[La Reina]] to the east, and [[Ñuñoa]] to the south. It belongs to the [[Northeastern zone of Santiago de Chile]]. It has 143,974 inhabitants.<ref name="2024census" />

Providencia is home to a large [[Upper middle class|upper-middle]] to [[Upper class|upper-class]] population and it holds the region's highest percentage of population over 60 (22%). It contains many high-rise apartment buildings as well as a significant portion of Santiago's commerce. It is notable for its large, old, and elegant houses, once inhabited by the Santiago elite and now mostly used as offices. The municipality is also home to many embassies, including those of [[Canada]], [[Poland]], [[Hungary]], [[Italy]], [[France]], [[Egypt]], [[Russia]], [[Japan]], [[China]], and [[Uruguay]]. Providencia has one of the highest [[Human Development Index|human development indexes]] of any city in Latin America.[[File:Providencia - 2012 - Vista a Plaza Baquedano desde Cerro San Cristóbal.jpg|thumb|[[Torre Telefónica Chile]] and [[Plaza Baquedano]]]]

=== Immigration ===
As of the 2024 census, immigrants make up 11.2% of the population - 7.6% are from [[South America]], 1.1% from [[North America]], 1.8% from [[Europe]], 0.5% from [[Asia]], 0.05% from [[Africa]], and 0.1% from [[Oceania]].<ref name="2024census" />

== Administration ==
As a commune, Providencia is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The current alcalde is [[Jaime Bellolio|Jaime Bellolio Avaria]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]). The communal council has the following members:
 
* María Eugenia Pino García ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Cristóbal Lagos González ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Isabel Labbé Martínez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* María Carolina Plaza Guzmán (Ind/[[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Valentina Alarcón Chávez ([[Evópoli]])
* María Fernanda Maquieira Borgoño ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Mauricio Labbé Barría ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* María Antonieta Saa Díaz ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Rodrigo Valenzuela Báez ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Solange Wolleter Dávila ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])

Providencia belongs to Electoral District No. 10, with [[Santiago (commune)|Santiago]], [[Ñuñoa]], [[La Granja, Chile|La Granja]], [[Macul]] and [[San Joaquín]], and to the 7th ([[Senate of Chile|Senatorial]]) District ([[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]).
It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] of the [[National Congress of Chile|National Congress]] by the deputies [[Gonzalo Winter]] ([[Social Convergence|CS]]), Jorge Alessandri Vergara ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]), [[Johannes Kaiser (Chilean politician)|Johannes Kaiser]] ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]]), [[María Luisa Cordero]] (IND), Lorena Fries (IND), [[Helia Molina]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]), Alejandra Placencia ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) and [[Emilia Schneider]] ([[Comunes (political party)|COM]]).

==Landmarks==
Providencia is home to [[Barrio Bellavista]], a bohemian neighborhood known for its vibrant community of artists and performers. Barrio Bellavista lies in the shadow of [[Cerro San Cristóbal]], a prominent hill in the city topped by a 22m statue of [[Mary, mother of Jesus|the Virgin Mary]]. The northern part of Providencia serves as its financial and commercial hub, centered around [[Providencia Avenue|Providencia]] and Nueva Providencia (formerly 11 de Septiembre) avenues, while the rest of the commune features high-end residential developments.[[File:Parque La Aviación.JPG|thumb|Aviation Fountain, Providencia Square]][[File:Gran Torre Santiago (39693576311).jpg|thumb|[[Gran Torre Costanera]] and [[Mapocho river]] in [[Uruguay Park]].]]Other notable areas of interest in Providencia include the Metropolitan Zoo; the Teleférico, an aerial gondola that transports passengers from Pedro de Valdivia Norte Street to the top of Cerro San Cristóbal; and the headquarters of several television networks and [[Radio Cooperativa]].
