'''Patamacca''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Marowijne District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 427. Patamacca is a [[tribe|tribal]] area inhabited by [[Maroon (people)|Maroon]]s<ref name="census"/>

In 1773, attempts were made to conquer the Patamacca territory by the [[Society of Suriname]], but did not yield results, and the area was left in the hands of the Maroons.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://archive.org/stream/GeschiedenisVanSuriname/GeschiedenisVanSuriname_djvu.txt|title=Geschiedenis Van Suriname|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl|date=1861}}</ref>

The main economic activity in the Patamacca area was the [[palm oil industry]],<ref name="radio">{{cite web|url=https://www.thecaribbeanradio.com/suriname-palm-oil-industry-may-rise-again-after-setbacks/|title=Suriname palm-oil industry may rise again after setbacks|website=The Caribbean Radio|access-date=21 May 2020}}</ref> During the 1960s<ref name="suriname_nu"/> {{ill|Bruynzeel Suriname Wood Company|nl|Bruynzeel Suriname Houtmaatschappij|lt=Bruynzeel}} started a large-scale wood plantation in the resort.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://edepot.wur.nl/365746 |title=HUMAN INTERFERENCE IN THE TROPICAL RAINFOREST ECOSYSTEM|website=[[Anton de Kom University]] via [[Wageningen University]]|access-date=21 May 2020}}</ref> There is a large [[granite]] mine in the resort which is operated by Grassalco.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://grassalco.com/about-us#company|title=Grassalco N.V. Ons bedrijf|access-date=21 May 2020|website=Grassalco|language=nl|archive-date=18 May 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190518133250/https://grassalco.com/about-us#company|url-status=dead}}</ref> Even though Patamacca did not bear much destruction during the [[Surinamese Interior War]], the civil war did result in refugees, and stopped much of the industry.<ref name="suriname_nu">{{cite web|url=http://www.andasuriname.com/175alg/marowijne02.html|title=Distrikt Marowijne 2|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=11 August 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190811121101/http://www.andasuriname.com/175alg/marowijne02.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> As of 2019, attempts have been made to restart the palm oil industry with Chinese aid.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dagbladdewest.com/2019/06/08/palmolie-of-ordinaire-houtroof/|title=PALMOLIE OF ORDINAIRE HOUTROOF? |website=Dagblad De West|date=8 June 2019 |access-date=21 May 2020}}</ref>

The village of [[Ovia Olo]] is located in the resort.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Plan Bureau| url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Planning Office Suriname - Districts 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|year=2014|language=nl|page=114}}</ref>
