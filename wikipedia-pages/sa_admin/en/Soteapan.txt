'''Soteapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] and city located in the south-central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 260 km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. As of 2000, the municipality counted 27,487 inhabitants within 528.07 km<sup>2</sup>, including about 10,000 in the main city area of Soteapan.

==History==

Historically the city of Soteapan was mentioned as a part of the [[Coatzacoalcos Municipality|municipality of Coatzacoalcos]] by [[Spanish Empire|colonial Spanish]] references, yet was only recognised as a separate Mexican municipality in 1831.

The area in the [[Pre-Columbian era|pre-columbian era]] was an area of contention between the [[Aztec Empire|Aztec empire]] and its southern neighbours.

The area only recently obtained paved road access and is considered to be one of the most [[Poverty in Mexico|impoverished communities in Mexico]]. Its population is composed of mainly of [[Popoluca]], with a small number of [[Nahuas|Nahula]] people. Though many speak the native [[Sierra Popoluca|Popoluca language]], [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the predominant language.

==Geography==

The municipality of Soteapan is delimited to the north by [[Gulf of Mexico]], to the east by [[Mecayapan]], to the south by [[Chinameca, Veracruz|Chinameca]] and [[Acayucan (municipality) |Acayucan]], and to the west by [[Hueyapan de Ocampo]] and [[Catemaco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30149a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Soteapan |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}</ref>

==Economy==
Economically, Soteapan depends on [[small-scale agriculture]] and [[cattle ranching]]. The area invites many tourists due to several waterfalls and views of pristine mountains.

The area is located near the volcanoes of the [[Sierra de los Tuxtlas]] and is located within a large part of the [[Los Tuxtlas Biosphere Reserve]].

==Celebrations==

During March there is a celebration to honour to [[Saint Joseph|San José]], the [[patron saint]] of the town, and in December there is a celebration to honour to [[Our Lady of Guadalupe|Virgen de Guadalupe]].
