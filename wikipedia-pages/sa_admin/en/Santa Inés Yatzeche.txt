'''Santa Inés Yatzeche''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in southeastern [[Mexico]] and is the third smallest municipality in Oaxaca behind [[Santa Cruz Amilpas]] and [[Natividad, Oaxaca|Natividad]].

==History==
The community, which lies on the Atoyac River in the southern [[Valle Grande, Oaxaca|Valle Grande]], has a long history.
Archaeological studies have shown that it was a flourishing center for hundreds of years before the rise of [[Monte Albán]] around 500, when it suffered a loss of 75% of its population.<ref>{{cite book
 |page=91
 |title=Ancient Mesoamerica: A Comparison of Change in Three Regions
 |author1=Richard E. Blanton |author2=Stephen A. Kowalewski |author3=Gary M. Feinman |author4=Laura M. Finsten |publisher=Cambridge University Press
 |year=1993
 |ISBN=0-521-44606-6}}</ref>
==Population==
The community is now relatively poor. As of 2000, only 7% of households earned more than twice the minimum wage. Most of the inhabitants, who speak [[Yatzeche Zapotec|Zapotec]], had not completed six years of education.
The villagers must travel to [[Zimatlán]] to sell produce, and to [[Oaxaca, Oaxaca|Oaxaca City]] to obtain wage-paying work.<ref>{{cite web
 |url=http://www.utexas.edu/utpress/excerpts/excohcul.html
 |title=The Culture of Migration in Southern Mexico
 |publisher=University of Texas Press
 |author=Jeffrey H. Cohen
 |accessdate=2010-07-10}}</ref>
As of 2005, the total population was 975, of whom 908 spoke an indigenous language, living in 210 homes.
==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 11.48&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an average elevation of 1,460 meters above sea level. It is part of the [[Zimatlán District, Oaxaca|Zimatlán District]] of the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales]] region.<ref name=inafed>{{cite web

The climate is temperate and main economic activity is agriculture and livestock husbandry.<ref name=inafed/>
