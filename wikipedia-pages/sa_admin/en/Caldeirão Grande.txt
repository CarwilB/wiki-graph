'''Caldeirão Grande''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Bahia]]. Its estimated population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 13,391 inhabitants, up from 12,491 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name="perfil">[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/caldeirao-grande/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It has an area of {{convert|458.31|km2}}.<ref name="perfil"/> {{as of|2020}}, the mayor was [[Candido Pereira da Guirra Filho]].<ref name="perfil"/>
