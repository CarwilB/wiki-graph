'''Barreirinha''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 33,722 (2022) and its area is 5,751km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/ |access-date=2026-06-08 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Transport ==
The locality is served by the [[Barreirinha Airport]].
