'''Champerico''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Retalhuleu Department|Retalhuleu department]] in southwestern [[Guatemala]]. It is a popular vacation spot for Guatemalans in the region.  The picturesque [[beach]] town was founded on the [[Pacific Ocean]] in 1872,<ref>{{cite book|author1=Iain Stewart|author2=Mark Whatmore|title=The Rough Guide to Guatemala|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=zS3TjIGbOXkC&pg=PA236|year=2002|publisher=Rough Guides|isbn=978-1-85828-848-2|page=236}}</ref> and the paved road to [[Retalhuleu]] makes Champerico one of the most important ports on the Pacific.<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |last=Goldberg |first=Maren|encyclopedia=Encyclopaedia Britannica |title=Champerico |url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Champerico |access-date=27 March 2020 |date=1 June 2015 |publisher=Encyclopaedia Britannica, Inc.}}</ref>  The port handles mostly [[coffee]], [[timber]] and [[sugar]].<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20100425023718/http://www.mychamperico.com/    visita champerico ]</ref> Its population in 2023 is 39,666.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Champerico (Municipality, Guatemala) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/retalhuleu/1107__champerico/ |access-date=2023-11-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
