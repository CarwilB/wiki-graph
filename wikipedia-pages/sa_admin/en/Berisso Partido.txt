'''Berisso Partido''' is a small eastern [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 80,092 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|135|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Berisso]], which is around {{convert|57|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The district forms part of the historical territory of [[Gran La Plata]], to the south of Buenos Aires.

Berisso is home to [[Club Atlético Villa San Carlos]], a football club who currently play in the regionalised [[Primera B Metropolitana|3rd division]] of Argentine football.
