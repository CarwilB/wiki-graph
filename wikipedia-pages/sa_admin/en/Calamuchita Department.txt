The '''Calamuchita Department''' ({{langx|es|Departamento Calamuchita}}) is a subdivision ([[departments of Argentina|department]]) of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lmdiario.com.ar/contenido/395211/el-domingo-25-de-junio-seran-las-elecciones-provinciales-en-cordoba|title=El domingo 25 de junio serán las elecciones provinciales en Córdoba|work=La Nueva Mañana|date=20 March 2023|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The department is located in the center-west of the province and includes important tourist destinations, especially along the Calamuchita Valley. The center of this region is the city of  [[Embalse, Córdoba|Embalse]] home of one of the two functional [[nuclear power plant]]s in Argentina (see [[Embalse nuclear power plant]]).
