'''Ubiretama''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The name comes from the [[Tupi language]]. It is located 499&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]] and east of [[Argentina]]. The population is 1,983 (2020 est.) in an area of 126.69&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/YD9 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>
