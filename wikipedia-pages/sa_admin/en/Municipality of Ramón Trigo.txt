The '''municipality of Ramón Trigo''' is one of the municipalities of [[Cerro Largo Department]], [[Uruguay]], established on 3 April 2013. Its seat is the town of [[Ramón Trigo]].

== History ==
By Decree No. 11/2013 of 3 April 2013, the Departmental Board of Cerro Largo, after the initiative of the Intendant of the Department, allowed the creation of a new municipality based on [[Ramón Trigo]] settlement,<ref name=JDCL-Dec11-2013 /> authorized by Law No. 18567 that allowed the creation of municipalities around settlements with less than 2000 inhabitants, provided this was passed by the Departmental Board after the initiative of the Intendant of the Department.<ref name=Law18567 /> At that time the territorial jurisdiction reached the urban and suburban area of the cadastral town.<ref name=JDCL-Dec11-2013 />

By Law No. 19313 of 27 March 2015, in compliance with the ruling of Law No. 19272, was ratified its creation and assigned the electoral constituency series GFD of Cerro Largo Department as defined by the Electoral Board of Cerro Largo.<ref name=Law19272 /><ref name=Law19319 />

On 30 October 2018, the territorial reach of the municipality was expanded to match the whole territory of the electoral constituency GFD, ordered by the Departmental Board Decree No. 28/2018.<ref name=JDCL-Dec28-2018 />

== Location ==
This municipality is located at the northwest side of Cerro Largo Department, reaching an area of about {{convert|1270.5|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name=PerfilOD /> It borders to the northeast with [[Municipality of Isidoro Noblía|Isidoro Noblía]] municipality, to the east with [[Municipality of Bañado de Medina|Bañado de Medina]] municipality, to the southeast with [[Municipality of Fraile Muerto|Fraile Muerto]] municipality, to the southwest with [[Municipality of Tres Islas|Tres Islas]] municipality and to the northwest with [[Rivera Department|Rivera]] and [[Tacuarembó Department]]s. Its seat is the town of [[Ramón Trigo]].

== Authorities ==
The authority of the municipality is the Municipal Council, integrated by the Mayor (who presides it) and four Councilors.
