'''Lámud''' is one of 23 districts of [[Luya Province]] in [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]].

Lámud is located in the mountainous region, and features ruins and caves of the [[Chachapoya culture]]. Lámud also is the head office of the government of the Province of Luya.

The public holiday of Lámud is celebrated on September 14, which is the holiday of the [[Lord of Gualamita]].

Lámud borders with [[San Cristóbal District, Luya|San Cristóbal District]] to the north, [[Valera District]] (Bongará Province) to the northeast, [[Huancas District]] to the southeast (Province of Chachapoyas), [[Luya District]] to the southeast, and with [[Trita District]] to the south-west.
