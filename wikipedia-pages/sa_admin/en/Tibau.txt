'''Tibau''' is the northernmost city in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]]. It is also the last coastal city (going east–west) of that state before [[Ceará]]. One of the main sources of income for the city is within the fishing and farming industry.
