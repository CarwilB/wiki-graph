'''Pajapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] and city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south zone of the state of Veracruz, about {{convert|496|km|abbr=on}} from the state capital of [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{convert|305.98|km2|abbr=on}}, and is located at {{coord|18|16|N|94|41|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Pajapan is delimited to the north by [[Mecayapan]] to the east by the [[Gulf of Mexico]] and [[Coatzacoalcos]], to the south by [[Chinameca, Veracruz|Chinameca]] and [[Cosoleacaque]] and to the west by [[Mecayapan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30122a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20070626042748/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30122a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 26, 2007|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Pajapan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-12}}</ref> By decree of June 22, 1889, the limits were established between the municipalities of Mecayapan and Pajapan. By a decree of October 6, 1898, Pajapan was annexed as a community within the municipality of Minzapan.{{citation needed|date=January 2017}}

The weather in Pajapan is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.{{citation needed|date=January 2017}}

==Agriculture==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]], [[watermelon]], [[mango]] and [[orange fruit]].{{citation needed|date=January 2017}}

==Celebrations==
In Pajapan, in May takes place the celebration in honor to San Juan de Dios, Patron of the town.{{citation needed|date=January 2017}}
