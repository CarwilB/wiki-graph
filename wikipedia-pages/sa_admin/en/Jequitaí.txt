'''Jequitaí''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 the population was 7,468 in a total area of 1,268&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/jequitai/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1948.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/jequitai/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204104439/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/jequitai/ |archive-date=2008-02-04 |url-status=dead }} {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204104439/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/jequitai/ |date=2008-02-04 }}</ref><ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Jequitaí is located on the [[Jequitaí River]], a tributary of the [[São Francisco River]] at an elevation of 517 meters.  It is east of the nearest population center, [[Pirapora]], which lies at a distance of 66&nbsp;km.  [[Montes Claros]] is 103&nbsp;km. to the northeast.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Pirapora]], [[Claro dos Poções]], [[Coração de Jesus]].  Jequitaí is part of the statistical microregion of Pirapora.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080705172912/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |archive-date=2008-07-05 |url-status=dead }} {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080705172912/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |date=2008-07-05 }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising (41,000 head in 2006) and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$30,637,000.  The most important agricultural crops are bananas, coconuts, rice, sugarcane, beans and corn.  There were 314 automobiles.  In the rural area there were 514 producers.  The total area of agricultural land was 45,000 hectares in 2006, of which 8,500 ha. were planted in crops.  As of 2006 there were 99 tractors.  In the urban area there was one banking agency in 2006.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==
This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.

*State ranking: 521 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 2,903 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000
