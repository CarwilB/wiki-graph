The '''Francisco del Carmen Carvajal Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 14,653.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=8 November 2015 |archive-date=15 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Valle de Guanape]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Francisco del Carmen Carvajal Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Francisco del Carmen Carvajal Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 12,936 (up from 11,654 in 2000).  This amounts to 0.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|103.49|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Francisco del Carmen Carvajal Municipality is Franklyn Guillen, re-elected 23 November 2008 with 55% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales – 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into two parishes; Valle de Guanape and Santa Bárbara (previous to 27 June 1995, the Francisco del Carmen Carvajal Municipality, or Carvajal Municipality as it was known back then, contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
