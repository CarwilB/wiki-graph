The '''Antonio Díaz Municipality''' is one of the four  [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Delta Amacuro]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 26,655.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Curiapo]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Antonio Díaz Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Deltaamacuro.zip {{dead link|date=May 2016|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Antonio Díaz Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 27,693 (up from 21,664 in 2000).  This amounts to 17.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2016|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1.22|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2016|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Antonio Díaz Municipality is Amado Antonio Heredia Bolaño, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 71% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio:Antonio Diaz |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/23/alcaldedis=0&edo=23&mun=3&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303193111/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/23/alcaldedis=0&edo=23&mun=3&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |archive-date=March 3, 2016 |website=cne.gob.ve}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=ELECCIONES 30 DE JULIO DE 2000 DIRECTORIO DE ALCALDES ELECTOS |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 |accessdate=March 15, 2008}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into six [[parish]]es; Curiapo, Almirante Luis Brión, Francisco Aniceto Lugo, Manuel Renaud, Padre Barral, and Santos de Abelgas (previous to February 25, 1995, the Antonio Díaz Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
