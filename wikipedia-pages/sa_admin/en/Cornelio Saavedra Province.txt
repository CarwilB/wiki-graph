'''Cornelio Saavedra''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the north-eastern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Potosí Department]]. The capital of the province is [[Betanzos, Bolivia|Betanzos]] with 4,168 inhabitants in the year 2001.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo National Institute of Statistics of Bolivia]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110924073930/http://www.ine.gob.bo/ |date=September 24, 2011 }}</ref>

The province is named after the president of the Argentine [[First Junta]], [[Cornelio Saavedra]], who was born in [[Otuyo]], 	Betanzos Municipality.

==Location==
Cornelio Saavedra province is one of sixteen provinces in the Potosí Department. It is located between 18° 57' and 19° 44' [[Latitude|South]] and between 64° 48' and 65° 39' [[Longitude|West]]. It borders [[Chuquisaca Department]] in the northeast, [[Chayanta Province]] in the north, [[Tomás Frías Province]] in the west, and [[José María Linares Province]] in the south and southeast. The province extends over {{convert|95|km|abbr=on}} from east to west and {{convert|105|km|abbr=on}} from north to south.

==Division==
The province comprises three separate [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Quechua languages|Quechua]], spoken by 78%, while 47% of the population speaks [[Spanish Language|Spanish]]. The population increased from 52,659 inhabitants (1992 census) to 58,706 (2001 census), an increase of 11.5%.

80% of the population have no access to electricity, 94% have no sanitary facilities. 69% of the population are employed in agriculture, 1% in mining, 10% in industry, 20% in general services. 89% of the population are Catholics, 8% Protestants.

The people are predominantly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent.<ref name="desc">obd.descentralizacion.gov.bo/municipal/fichas/ (inactive)</ref>
