'''Pimentel District''' is one of twenty [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Chiclayo Province|Chiclayo province]] in [[Peru]]. The beach (''Playa Pimentel'') and reed watercraft [[Caballito de totora|caballitos de totora]] are among its most popular attractions.<ref>[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. {{in lang|es}} ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}'' Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
