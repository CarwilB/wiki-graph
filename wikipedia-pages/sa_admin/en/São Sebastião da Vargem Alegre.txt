'''São Sebastião da Vargem Alegre''' (commonly abbreviated as S.S.V.A.) is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of Muriaé.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,024.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sao-sebastiao-da-vargem-alegre/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The municipality was founded by the "Law number 12.030, of december 21st 1995", emancipated from [[Miraí]].<ref>{{Cite web |publisher=Prefeitura de São Sebastião da Vargem Alegre |date=15 August 2024 |title=Prefeitura SSVA - Sobre História |url=https://saosebastiaodavargemalegre.mg.gov.br/historia |access-date=15 August 2024 |website=Prefeitura SSVA}}</ref> Nearly 1,210 people voted regarding the act of emancipation, with an approval rate of 81.8%.

== Early history ==
Prior to the invasion of [[Portuguese colonization of the Americas|Portuguese colonizers]], the region where the city is now located was inhabited by the indigenous ethnicity known by the [[exonym]] "[[:pt:Coroados|Coroados]]". Circa 1903, the first milestone in the history of the city is established, the arrival of Manoel Rodrigues da Silva and his family, having acquired the initial terrain of 15 coffee [[Alqueire|alqueires]], where the first house was built.{{Short description|Brazilian municipality}}
