'''Cedros''' ({{IPA|es|ˈseðɾos}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://elmundo.hn/sismo-de-magnitud-3-5-se-registra-en-cedros-francisco-morazan/|title=Sismo de magnitud 3.5 se registra en Cedros, Francisco Morazán|work=ElMundo.hn|date=4 August 2023|access-date=23 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

It is located 77 kilometers (paved road) from Tegucigalpa in an area of rolling hills with tropical pine forests.{{cn|date=November 2025}}
