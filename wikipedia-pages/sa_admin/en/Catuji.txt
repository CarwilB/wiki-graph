'''Catuji''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 6,257 people living in a total area of 421&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/catuji/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1993.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008}}</ref> Its name comes from the [[Tupi language]], catú-g-y, meaning "good river" or "good water".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://redescobrindoosvales.tur.br/vale-do-mucuri/microrregiao-de-teofilo-otoni/catuji/historia.php|title=História de Catuji: História, Geografia, Cultura e Turismo {{!}} Projeto Redescobrindo os Vales|access-date=2024-11-30|website=redescobrindoosvales.tur.br}}</ref>

The region is famous for its unique rock formations of which Pedra do Chifre and Pedra de Santa Rosa are the most interesting.

==Geography==
Catuji is located on highway BR-116 at an elevation of 625 meters, 72&nbsp;km north of [[Teófilo Otoni]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 513&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: Teófilo Otoni, [[Itaipé]], [[Novo Oriente de Minas]] and [[Padre Paraíso]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |archive-date=2008-06-13 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|18|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 12 million from services, 2 million from industry, and 3 million from agriculture. There were 600 rural producers on 13,000 hectares of land. Only 7 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 1,700 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were bananas, coffee, sugarcane, beans and corn. There were 7,000 head of cattle (2006). There were no banks (2007) and 166 automobiles (145 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 40 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Health and education==
There were 4 health clinics. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni. Educational needs were met by 17 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 1 pre-primary school.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
