'''Platón Sánchez''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. 
It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. The municipal seat is the town of [[Platón Sánchez, Veracruz|Platón Sánchez]].

==Geography==
The municipality covers a total area of {{cvt|227.84|km2}} and  is located on [[Mexican Federal Highway 105|Federal Highway 105]]. 

===Borders===
It borders on the municipalities of [[Tempoal Municipality|Tempoal]] (north), [[Tantoyuca Municipality|Tantoyuca]] (east), [[Chalma Municipality|Chalma]] (south) and [[Chiconamel Municipality|Chiconamel]] (south-east). To the south it borders on the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]].

==Products==
Major products are corn, cereal, sugar, and fruits.

==History==
The municipality's name honours {{ill|Rafael Platón Sánchez|es}} (1831&ndash;1867), a native of the area who fought in the [[Battle of Puebla]] of 5 May 1862 and later chaired the court martial that sentenced [[Maximilian of Mexico|Emperor Maximilian]] and his generals [[Miguel Miramón]] and [[Tomás Mejía]] to death by firing squad in [[Santiago de Querétaro]] on 19 June 1867. The municipality was established with Platón Sánchez's name in 1868.

In 1938 Platón Sánchez (together with [[Chiconamel (municipality)|Chiconamel]]) lost territory which then formed the new municipality of [[Chalma (municipality)|Chalma]].
