'''Vinces Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[ Los Ríos Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Vinces]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 61,565.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
