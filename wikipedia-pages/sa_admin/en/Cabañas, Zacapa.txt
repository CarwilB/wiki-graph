'''Cabañas''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n department of [[Zacapa Department|Zacapa]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/conozca-la-historia-de-cabanas-municipio-zacapaneco-que-recientemente-celebro-su-aniversario/|title=Conozca la historia de Cabañas, municipio zacapaneco que recientemente celebró su aniversario|work=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=10 April 2022|access-date=19 June 2025|language=es}}</ref>

It is a small town of about 10,000, and income is mostly from [[Agriculture|farming]].

The [[2008 Aéreo Ruta Maya crash]] of a [[Cessna 208]] after taking off from [[La Aurora International Airport]] (GUA / MGGT) close to Cabañas killed 11 passengers and crew.
