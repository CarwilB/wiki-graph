'''Passo de Camaragibe''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈpasʊ ˈdi kɐmɐɾɐˈʒiːbi]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northern coast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 15,258 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/passo-de-camaragibe/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 187&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
