'''Florentino Ameghino Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northwest of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 8,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1824|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Florentino Ameghino, Buenos Aires|Florentino Ameghino]], which is {{convert|430|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

== Name ==

Florentino Ameghino Partido is named after Argentine [[natural history|naturalist]], [[paleontology|paleontologist]], [[anthropology|anthropologist]] and [[zoology|zoologist]] [[Florentino Ameghino]].
