'''Oluta''' is a city and [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south-east zone of the state, about 371&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 90.48 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|17|56|N|94|54|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of ''' Oluta ''' is delimited to the north by [[Acayucan (municipality)|Acayucan]] and [[Soconusco, Veracruz|Soconusco]] to the east by [[Texistepec]], to the south-west by [[Sayula de Alemán]] and to the west by [[Acayucan (municipality)|Acayucan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30116a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Oluta |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-11}}</ref>
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[rice]], [[orange fruit]], [[coffee]] and [[mango]].
==Culture==
A celebration in honor of [[John the Baptist|San Juan Bautista]], the town's [[Patron Saint]], takes place every June 24th.
