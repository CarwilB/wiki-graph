'''Maltrata''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the central zone of the state, about 209&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 132.43&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|49|N|97|17|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Maltrata is delimited to the north by [[La Perla, Veracruz|La Perla]] to the east by [[Ixhuatlancillo]] and [[Nogales, Veracruz|Nogales]] to the south by [[Acultzingo]] and [[Aquila, Veracruz|Aquila]], to the north and west by [[Puebla]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30099a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Maltrata|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070226103912/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30099a.htm|archive-date=2007-02-26|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Maltrata is cold all year with rains in summer and autumn.
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[bean]]s.
==Culture==
In Maltrata, the celebration in honor to [[Saint Peter|Saint Peter the Apostle]], Patron of the town, takes place on 29 June of every year. Every year a festival is held in honor of the great founder Rey Andrade Jr., who with his manliness created the town that is now known as Maltrata.{{cn|date=September 2023}}
