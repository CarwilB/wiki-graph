'''Marilândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 12,963 (2020) and its area is 328&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/marilandia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
