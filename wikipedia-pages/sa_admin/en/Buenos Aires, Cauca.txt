'''Buenos Aires''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref name=caucanet/> Founded in by Vasco de Guzmán and Alonso de Fuenmayor in 1551, the municipality covers an area {{Convert|520|km2|0|abbr=on}} and has a population of 21,300.<ref name=caucanet/> The population is primarily engaged in agriculture and [[ranch]]ing.<ref name=caucanet/>
