'''Ranchuelo''' is a town and municipality in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1734<ref name=guije/> and has a municipal population of 50,708, of which about 15,000 in the town itself.

==History==
Originally named Boca de Ranchuelo, the settlement was founded by Dionisio Consuegra on October 1, 1734. In 1856 it was linked to the [[Ferrocarriles de Cuba|national rail network]], and in 1879 gained the municipal status.<ref name=guije/>

==Geography==
[[File:Ranchuelo Parque Marti.jpg|thumb|left|[[José Martí]] Square]]
[[File:Jicotea City Limits Sign.jpg|alt=Jicotea City Limits Sign|thumb|Jicotea sign leading into the village on the Carretera Central del Cuba highway.]]
The town is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Norte, Poza de la China, Sitio Viejo and Sur.<ref name="guije">{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vranchuelo/index.htm|title=Ranchuelo|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-09|language=es}}</ref>

Ranchuelo is 7&nbsp;km from San Juan de los Yeras, 9 from Esperanza, 16 from [[Cruces, Cuba|Cruces]], 28 from [[Santa Clara, Cuba|Santa Clara]], 43 from [[Cienfuegos]] and 256 from [[Havana]]. It borders with the municipalities of [[Santo Domingo, Cuba|Santo Domingo]], [[Cifuentes, Cuba|Cifuentes]], Santa Clara, [[Manicaragua]], Cruces and [[Lajas, Cuba|Lajas]].<ref>{{OSM|r|5884799|Ranchuelo}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Ranchuelo had a population of 50,708.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|556|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-09}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|106.2|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Transport==
Ranchuelo is served by the [[Autopista A1 (Cuba)|A1 motorway]] at the homonym exit, also known as "Ranchuelo-Cienfuegos".<ref>{{OSM|n|1308266444|Ranchuelo (A1 exit)}}</ref> It counts a railway station on the [[Santa Clara railway station (Cuba)|Santa Clara]]-[[Cienfuegos]] line, that is also the western terminus of a [[Rail freight transport|freight line]] to [[Mataguá]].<ref>{{OSM|n|2925075943|Ranchuelo railway station}}</ref>

Esperanza, the most populated municipal hamlet, is crossed in the middle by the "[[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]]" state highway.
