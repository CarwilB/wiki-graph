'''Nova Friburgo''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈnɔvɐ fɾiˈbuʁɡu|lang}}; {{langx|de|Neufreiburg}} {{IPA|de|nɔʏˈfʁaɪbʊʁk|}}; {{langx|en|"New [[Fribourg]]"}}), commonly referred to as just '''Friburgo''', is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] in southeastern [[Brazil]]. It is located in the mountainous region, in the Center Mesoregion of the state, {{convert|136|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the capital [[Rio de Janeiro (city)|Rio de Janeiro]]. The town is {{convert|846|m|ft|abbr=on}} above sea level, with a population of 189,939 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) and its area is 933&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

== History ==
[[File:colonizacaonf.jpg|thumb|left|Nova Friburgo during Swiss and German settlement, 1820–1830.]]
Up to the 19th century, the region of the present Nova Friburgo was inhabited by Coroado [[Purí people|Purí]] [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil|Indians]].<ref>''Os índios de Sumidouro''. Available in http://sumidouro.chez.com/indios.htm. Access on 9 September 2012.</ref><ref>''Nova Friburgo entra para a história''. Available in http://www.swissinfo.ch/por/specials/Suicos_do_estrangeiro/Nova_Friburgo_entra_para_a_historia.html?cid=875208. Access on 9 September 2012.</ref> In 1818, King [[John VI of Portugal|John VI]] was interested in improving the relationship with Germany, in order to obtain support against the French empire. He then proposed a planned settlement that would promote the civilization in Brazil. A royal decree of May 1818, authorized the [[Canton of Fribourg]] of [[Switzerland]],<ref>{{Cite web|date=2019-04-16|title=Schweizer Auswanderer und ihre Liebe zur Heimat|url=https://blog.nationalmuseum.ch/2019/04/schweizer-auswanderer/|access-date=2022-02-22|website=Blog zur Schweizer Geschichte - Schweizerisches Nationalmuseum|language=de-DE}}</ref> to establish a colony of 100 Swiss families in the Morro Queimado Farm, in [[Cantagalo, Rio de Janeiro|Cantagalo]] District, a place with climate and natural characteristics similar to those in their country. 

Between 1819 and 1820, the region was settled by 265 Swiss families, in total 1,458 immigrants. It was named Nova Friburgo (New Fribourg) by the Swiss after the homeland of most of the families.

Following the [[Independence of Brazil]] in 1822, the Imperial Government continued the policy of populating the nation by attracting [[Europe]]an [[colonization]]. Eighty [[German Brazilian|German]] families previously assigned to settlements in the Province of [[Bahia]], for unknown reasons ended up in Nova Friburgo, where they arrived on the 3 and 4 May 1824.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.luteranos.com.br/201/historico.html|url-status=unfit|access-date=2023-09-25|title=Histórico: Comunidade Luterana de Nova Friburgo|lang=pt-br|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20030910211539/http://www.luteranos.com.br/201/historico.html|archive-date=10 Sep 2003}}</ref> Similar arrivals of [[Italian Brazilian|Italians]], [[Portuguese Brazilian|Portuguese]] and a minority of [[Syrian Brazilian|Syrians]] led to such population increases that the once village was elevated to city status on 8 January 1890.

In 1872, the Baron of Nova Friburgo brought to the region the Leopoldina Railroad, to allow for the flow of the coffee from Cantagalo. Agriculture was the basis of economic activity until 1910, when the arrival of industrialists pioneered the development of an industrial sector still thriving to the present day. Of similar importance was the relative proximity to [[Niterói]] and [[Rio de Janeiro]] and the improvement of transport and communication links such as paved roads and telegraph. This encouraged a small tourist industry to grow, which, together with local commerce, became the main source of income for the city.

== Geography ==
[[File:Nova Friburgo by Sentinel-2.png|left|thumb|Nova Friburgo seen from [[Sentinel-2]]]]
According to the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) in 2022, the municipality covers an area of 935.429 km², of which 43.43 km² is urban.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cidade e Estados |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/rj/nova-friburgo.html? |access-date=2026-06-20 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}</ref> It is located at 22° 16' 55" S south latitude and 42° 31' 51" W west longitude. Its neighboring municipalities are: [[Cachoeiras de Macacu]], [[Silva Jardim]], [[Casimiro de Abreu, Rio de Janeiro|Casimiro de Abreu]], [[Macaé]], [[Trajano de Moraes|Trajano de Morais]], [[Bom Jardim, Rio de Janeiro|Bom Jardim]], [[Duas Barras]], [[Sumidouro]], and [[Teresópolis]].<ref name=":52">{{Cite web |title=A cidade |url=https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/pagina/view/1/a-cidade |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251214075511/https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/pagina/view/1/a-cidade |archive-date=December 14, 2025 |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=Nova Friburgo |publisher=Prefeitura Municipal de Nova Friburgo |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

According to the regional division in effect since 2017, established by the IBGE,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ibraim Rodrigues da Silva Medina |title=Divisões Regionais do Brasil {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20190407130601/https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |archive-date=2019-04-07 |access-date=2026-06-20 |website=www.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref> the municipality belongs to the Petrópolis Intermediate Geographic Region and the Nova Friburgo Immediate Geographic Region.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Base de dados por municípios das Regiões Geográficas Imediatas e Intermediárias do Brasil |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls |access-date=2026-06-20 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística}}</ref> Until then, under the system of micro-regions and meso-regions, it was part of the Nova Friburgo micro-region, which in turn was part of the Centro Fluminense meso-region.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2016 |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip |access-date=2026-06-20 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística}}</ref>

=== Topography, hydrography, and the environment ===
[[File:Rio Bengalas.jpg|alt=Rio Bengalas|left|thumb|Rio Bengalas]]
The terrain of Nova Friburgo is predominantly mountainous, featuring hills and peaks, and the elevation is over 200 meters above sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=.: Projeto I-FLORA - Instituto de Química da UFRJ :. |url=https://i-flora.iq.ufrj.br/sobrerj_afisicos.html |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20250528063537/https://i-flora.iq.ufrj.br/sobrerj_afisicos.html |archive-date=2025-05-28 |access-date=2026-06-04 |website=i-flora.iq.ufrj.br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date= |title=NOVA FRIBURGO - TCE-RJ |url=https://www.tcerj.tc.br/documents/10180/1092028/Estudo%20Socioecon%C3%B4mico%202008%20-%20Nova%20Friburgo.pdf |access-date=June 4, 2026 |publisher=TCE-RJ}}</ref>  The highest point is Pico Maior de Friburgo, which rises to 2,366 meters<ref>{{Cite web |last=Serrana |first=Do G1 Região |date=2014-11-03 |title=Pico Maior em Nova Friburgo, RJ, é a maior montanha da 'Serra do Mar' |url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/regiao-serrana/noticia/2014/11/pico-maior-em-nova-friburgo-rj-e-maior-montanha-da-serra-do-mar.html |access-date=2026-06-04 |website=Região Serrana |language=pt-br}}</ref>, while the minimum altitude is 846 meters.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-09-06 |title='É uma cicatriz que não se fecha': produtores de Nova Friburgo usam técnicas sustentáveis para evitar que tragédia se repita 13 anos depois |url=https://g1.globo.com/economia/agronegocios/globo-rural/noticia/2024/09/06/e-uma-cicatriz-que-nao-se-fecha-produtores-de-nova-friburgo-usam-tecnicas-sustentaveis-para-evitar-que-tragedia-se-repita-13-anos-depois.ghtml |access-date=2026-06-04 |website=G1 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The main watercourse that flows through Nova Friburgo is the [[Bengalas River]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Minas |first=Do G1 Sul de |date=2013-01-15 |title=Aumento de nível dos rios deixa cidades em estado de atenção |url=https://g1.globo.com/mg/sul-de-minas/noticia/2013/01/aumento-de-nivel-dos-rios-deixa-cidades-em-estado-de-atencao.html |access-date=2026-06-04 |website=Sul de Minas |language=pt-br}}</ref> The municipality is part of the [[Rio Grande (Dois Rios)|Grande River]] basin.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-02-25 |title=Para onde vão as águas de Nova Friburgo? |url=https://avozdaserra.com.br/colunas/historia-e-memoria/para-onde-vao-aguas-de-nova-friburgo-0 |access-date=2026-06-04 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref>

Sometimes, during the rainy season, the rivers that flow through the municipality, especially the Bengalas River, experience rising water levels, causing flooding along their banks, which necessitates an effective flood warning system. The city was one of the areas most affected by the [[January 2011 Rio de Janeiro floods and mudslides|2011 floods]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-02-16 |title=Em 2011, chuvas que atingiram região serrana do RJ deixaram quase mil mortos |url=https://www.cnnbrasil.com.br/noticias/em-2011-chuvas-que-atingiram-regiao-serrana-do-rj-deixaram-quase-mil-mortos/ |access-date=2026-06-22 |website=CNN Brasil |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
=== Climate ===
[[File:Vista de Catarcione 2.jpg|left|thumb|Rain in the Catarcione neighborhood on November 29, 2024]]
According to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen-Geiger climate classification]], the climate is characterized as mild mesothermal (Cfb).<ref>{{Cite web |title=SUBMISSION OF PAPERS/POSTERS - suporte,+Gerente+da+revista,+1935-10738-1-SP-1.pdf |url=https://ocs.ige.unicamp.br/ojs/sbgfa/article/download/1935/1994 |access-date=June 21, 2026}}</ref> According to data from the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET) covering the period from 1961 to 2003, the lowest temperature recorded in Nova Friburgo was 1 °C on four occasions: twice in June 1968, on the 1st and 28th, and the other two on June 7, 1969, and July 22, 1981. However, outside of that period, more recently, the absolute minimum temperature in the municipality was −1.1 °C on September 4, 2011, and August 8, 2014, at the institute’s automatic weather station.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Serrana |first=Juliana ScariniDo G1 Região |date=2014-08-08 |title=Friburgo, RJ, registra a temperatura mais baixa dos últimos quatro anos |url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/regiao-serrana/noticia/2014/08/inverno-em-nova-friburgo-rj-e-o-mais-rigoroso-em-quatro-anos.html |access-date=2026-06-22 |website=Região Serrana |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=INMET :: Tempo |url=https://tempo.inmet.gov.br/ |access-date=2026-06-22 |website=tempo.inmet.gov.br}}</ref>

=== Districts ===
The municipality is divided into eight districts: Sede, [[Riograndina]], [[Campo do Coelho]], [[Amparo (Nova Friburgo)|Amparo]], [[Lumiar (Nova Friburgo)|Lumiar]], [[Conselheiro Paulino]], [[São Pedro da Serra (Nova Friburgo)|São Pedro da Serra]], and [[Muri (Nova Friburgo)|Muri]].<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |title=Meio Ambiente e Desenv. Urbano Sustentável - Distritos e Bairros» |url=https://novafriburgo.rj.gov.br/paginas-centralizadas/10_32_Nova-Friburgo-Distritos-e-Bairros.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20240730211901/https://novafriburgo.rj.gov.br/paginas-centralizadas/10_32_Nova-Friburgo-Distritos-e-Bairros.html |archive-date=2024-07-30 |access-date=2026-06-10 |website=novafriburgo.rj.gov.br}}</ref> According to city hall, in 2022 the city consisted of 40 neighborhoods,<ref name=":3" /> the most populous of which were Olaria (15,103 residents), Centro (14,233 residents), and Conselheiro Paulino (12,943 residents).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Distritos e bairros de Nova Friburgo |url=https://novafriburgo.rj.gov.br/uploads/pagina/arquivos/Populacao-Bairros-Censo-IBGE-2022SEMMADUS.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240730211530/https://novafriburgo.rj.gov.br/uploads/pagina/arquivos/Populacao-Bairros-Censo-IBGE-2022SEMMADUS.pdf |archive-date=July 30, 2024 |access-date=June 9, 2026 |publisher=Prefeitura Municipal de Nova Friburgo}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
{{Historical populations|1970|90420|1980|123386|1991|167081|2000|173418|2010|182082|2022|189939|title=Nova Friburgo|type=Brazil|align=right|width=|state=|shading=|percentages=|footnote=}}
In the [[2022 Brazilian census]], the population was estimated at 189 939 inhabitants according to the census conducted that year by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE). Of the total, 87 254 were men and 94 828 were women.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Panorama do Censo 2022 |url=https://censo2022.ibge.gov.br/panorama/ |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=Panorama do Censo 2022 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> According to the 2010 census, 159 372 inhabitants lived in the [[urban area]] and 22 710 in the [[rural area]] (16.1%).<ref>{{Cite web |title=2010 Census |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/nova-friburgo/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true |access-date=2026-05-02 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

The median age in the city was 40 years. About 30.56% of the population was between 0 and 14 years old, and 40.43% was 60 years or older.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pesquisa Censo 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/nova-friburgo/pesquisa/10102/122229 |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

The population consisted of 125,897 white people, 18,825 Black people, 301 Asian people, 44,773 mixed-race people, and 132 Indigenous people.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Demografia - Nova Friburgo - RJ |url=https://relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/longevidade/demografia/BRA003033049/nova-friburgo---rj |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240117164028/https://relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/longevidade/demografia/BRA003033049/nova-friburgo---rj |archive-date=January 17, 2024 |access-date=May 2, 2026 |website=Centro de Inovação Sesi - Longevidade e Produtividade}}</ref>

=== Languagues ===
The main language spoken in Nova Friburgo is [[Brazilian Portuguese]], which is the only official language of Brazil, according to the [[Brazilian Constitution of 1988|1988 Constitution]].<ref>According to the [http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/ConstituicaoCompilado.htm Brazil Constitution, article 13]:  "''A língua portuguesa é o idioma oficial da República Federativa do Brasil."''</ref>

=== Immigration ===
{{See also|Swiss Brazilians|Immigration to Brazil}}

Between 1819 and 1820, approximately 1,682 immigrants (261 families) from various cantons in Switzerland settled in the Fazenda do Morro Queimado region.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Histórico |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/ |access-date=2026-06-19 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Imigração Suíça no Brasil |url=https://suicosdobrasil.org.br/imigracao-suica-no-brasil/ |access-date=2026-06-19 |website=Suiços do Brasil |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=200 anos de história |url=https://www.filantropicasuica.org.br/200-anos-de-historia |access-date=2026-06-19 |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-05-17 |title=Imigração suíça: uma história de coragem e sacrifícios |url=https://acervo.avozdaserra.com.br/noticias/imigracao-suica-uma-historia-de-coragem-e-sacrificios |access-date=2026-06-19 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Nova Friburgo, um Novo Mundo, chegada em 03/05/1824 |url=http://www.schuindt.com.br/Historia/HistNovaFriburgo.html |access-date=2026-06-19 |website=www.schuindt.com.br}}</ref>

The German immigrants arrived in Nova Friburgo on May 3, 1824. The original group consisted of 342 people, including farming families and former German mercenaries who had fought in the [[Cisplatine War]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-03-11 |title=A imigração alemã e seu legado |url=https://avozdaserra.com.br/colunas/historia-e-memoria/imigracao-alema-e-seu-legado |access-date=2026-06-19 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Politics ==
[[File:Prefeitura Municipal de Nova Friburgo - Nova Friburgo - 20221116203444.jpg|alt=Nova Friburgo City Hall|left|thumb|[[Nova Friburgo City Hall]]]]
Municipal administration in Nova Friburgo is carried out by the [[Executive Branch|Executive]] and [[Legislative Branch|Legislative]] powers. The Executive is led by the mayor, supported by a cabinet of secretaries. The first mayor was Everard Barreto de Andrade.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-10-31 |title=Prefeitos de Nova Friburgo |url=https://acervo.avozdaserra.com.br/colunas/historia-e-memoria/prefeitos-de-nova-friburgo |access-date=2026-04-27 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref> The mayor elected in the 2024 municipal elections was Johnny Maycon, re-elected by [[Liberal Party (Brazil, 2006)|Partido Liberal]] with 64,99% of valid votes for his second consecutive term, alongside Rodrigo Ascoly ([[Brazilian Democratic Movement|MDB]]) as [[Deputy mayor|vice-mayor]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-12-31 |title=Prefeito de Nova Friburgo (RJ) toma posse nesta quarta (1º); veja lista de vereadores eleitos |url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/regiao-serrana/eleicoes/2024/noticia/2024/12/31/prefeito-de-nova-friburgo-rj-toma-posse-nesta-quarta-1o-veja-lista-de-vereadores-eleitos.ghtml |access-date=2026-04-27 |website=G1 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> The council is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for administration and the Executive, particularly the participatory budget (Budget guidelines law).<ref>{{Cite web |title=lei-242006.pdf |url=https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/uploads/pagina/arquivos/lei-242006.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220302120924/https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/uploads/pagina/arquivos/lei-242006.pdf |archive-date=2022-03-02 |access-date=2026-04-27 |website=www.pmnf.rj.gov.br}}</ref> As of January 2024, the city had 62 920 voters, according to the [[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Estatísticas do eleitorado – Consulta por município/zona eleitoral |url=https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/estatisticas-de-eleitorado/consulta-por-municipio-zona |access-date=2026-04-27 |website=Justiça Eleitoral |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Economy ==
[[File:Avenida Alberto Braune 2.jpg|thumb|Alberto Braune Avenue, Nova Friburgo's main commercial and economic hub]]
Nova Friburgo has a diverse local economy and is regarded as the capital of lingerie.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-06-16 |title=Agora é Lei: Nova Friburgo é oficialmente a Capital Nacional da Moda Íntima |url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/regiao-serrana/noticia/2024/06/16/agora-e-lei-nova-friburgo-e-oficialmente-a-capital-nacional-da-moda-intima.ghtml |access-date=2026-06-10 |website=G1 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> According to the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE), the municipality’s gross domestic product (GDP) in 2023 was R$ 67.367.566,82, ranking 23º in Rio de Janeiro and 228º in Brazil. In 2023, the per capita GDP was R$ 38.789,12, ranking 45º in Rio de Janeiro and 2078º in Brazil.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/ |access-date=2026-06-10 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/ |access-date=2026-06-10 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref name="IBGE" />

The largest sectors are the lingerie, agribusiness and agriculture sectors, as well as eco-tourism and winter tourism.<ref name=":5">{{Cite web |title=A cidade |url=https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/pagina/view/1/a-cidade |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251214075511/https://www.pmnf.rj.gov.br/pagina/view/1/a-cidade |archive-date=December 14, 2025 |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=Nova Friburgo |publisher=Prefeitura Municipal de Nova Friburgo |language=pt-BR}}</ref> 

The city’s main commercial and economic hub is Alberto Braune Avenue, renowned as a hub for shopping, businesses, banks and bustling activity. The avenue is home to the city’s highest concentration of shops.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/index.php/biblioteca-catalogo?view=detalhes&id=418311|access-date=2026-03-13|website=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca|language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/index.php/biblioteca-catalogo?view=detalhes&id=418311|access-date=2026-03-13|website=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca|language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2015-05-02|title=Projeto de lei prevê revitalização da Avenida Alberto Braune|url=http://acervo.avozdaserra.com.br/noticias/projeto-de-lei-preve-revitalizacao-da-avenida-alberto-braune|access-date=2026-03-13|website=Jornal A Voz da Serra|language=pt-br}}</ref>

The largest employer is the [[Stam Metalúrgica]], located in the district of Conselheiro Paulino, which received the Anamaco Award in 2025. The company has more than 1,500 employees.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-11-17 |title=Stam é destaque no Prêmio Anamaco 2025 |url=https://avozdaserra.com.br/noticias/stam-e-destaque-no-premio-anamaco-2025 |access-date=2026-06-14 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref>
[[File:Giraffas em Nova Friburgo.jpg|alt=Giraffas in Cadima Shopping|thumb|Giraffas in Cadima Shopping]]
One of the main shopping centres is Cadima Shopping, located in the city centre; it is one of the busiest in the region and opened in 1998. It is home to major retailers and brands such as Lojas Americanas, [[Casa & Video|Casa e Video]], [[Lojas Renner|Renner]], [[Burger King]], [[Cacau Show]], [[Giraffas]] and [[Subway (restaurant)|Subway]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=November 22, 2023|title=25 anos Cadima Shopping: o maior shopping da região revitaliza seus espaços|url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/regiao-serrana/especial-publicitario/condominio-do-cadima-shopping/noticia/2023/11/22/25-anos-cadima-shopping-o-maior-shopping-da-regiao-revitaliza-seus-espacos.ghtml|access-date=2026-03-13|website=G1|language=pt-BR}}</ref> Another shopping centre is Friburgo Shopping, located in the same neighbourhood and opened in 1997. It houses major retailers and brands such as [[McDonald's|McDonald’s]], Bob’s and Burger King.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Friburgo Shopping|url=https://acianf.com.br/friburgo-shopping|access-date=2026-03-13|website=ACIANF|language=pt-br}}</ref>

=== Health care ===
[[File:Hospital Municipal Raul Sertã.jpg|thumb|Raul Sertã Hospital]]
The healthcare network includes 22 primary care clinics, one health center, four general hospitals, two specialized hospitals, and three psychosocial care centers (CAPS).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Infraestrutura Urbana & Social - Nova Friburgo - RJ |url=https://relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/longevidade/infraestrutura_urbana_e_social/BRA003033049/nova-friburgo---rj |url-status=dead |access-date=May 24, 2026 |website=Centro de Inovação Sesi - Longevidade e Produtividade}}</ref> 

The municipality is home to several major hospitals. One of them is Raul Sertã Hospital, the city’s main and most important hospital, which is administered by the Nova Friburgo municipal government and serves as a referral center for the interior of Rio de Janeiro. Raul Sertã Hospital was inaugurated on April 28, 1918.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-07-04 |title=A história do Hospital Municipal Raul Sertã |url=https://avozdaserra.com.br/noticias/historia-do-hospital-municipal-raul-serta |access-date=2026-05-24 |website=Jornal A Voz da Serra |language=pt-br}}</ref> Another major hospital is São Lucas Hospital, which opened on April 17, 1966.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sobre |url=https://www.saolucasfriburgo.com.br/sobre |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20251008030352/https://www.saolucasfriburgo.com.br/sobre |archive-date=2025-10-08 |access-date=2026-05-24 |website=www.saolucasfriburgo.com.br |language=pt}}</ref>

=== Emergency services ===
Visible policing is carried out by the Rio de Janeiro State Military Police ([[Military Police of Rio de Janeiro State|PMERJ]]), through the 11th Battalion, headquartered in the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Pmerj |first=Cetic |title=Batalhão da sua área |url=https://sepm.rj.gov.br/batalhao-da-sua-area/ |access-date=2026-05-31 |website=PMERJ |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Fire service is provided by the Rio de Janeiro State Military Fire Department, 6th Military Fire Brigade, headquartered in the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Estrutura – CBMERJ |url=https://www.cbmerj.rj.gov.br/sobre-o-cbmerj/institucional/estrutura/ |access-date=2026-05-31 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
