'''General Madariaga Partido''' ({{IPA|es|xeneˈɾal maðaˈɾjaɣa}}) (also known as ''General Juan Madariaga Partido'') is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] located on the Atlantic coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Visita a la ciudad de General Madariaga |url=https://www.welcomeargentina.com/pinamar/visita-ciudad-madariaga.html |website=WelcomeArgentina |access-date=25 June 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Reseña Histórica |url=https://www.turismo.madariaga.gob.ar/patrimonio/resenia |website=DIRECCIÓN DE TURISMO Municipalidad de General Juan Madariaga |access-date=25 June 2025}}</ref>

This provincial subdivision had a population of about 22,600 inhabitants in 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Provinces, Departments and Partidos |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/argentina/admin/ |website=CityPopulation |access-date=25 June 2025}}</ref> The area is {{convert|286.3|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}.

==Economy==
The economy of General Madariaga Partido is concentrated around agricultural production, although in the summer vacation season (December–February) tourism offers a valuable addition to the economy.
