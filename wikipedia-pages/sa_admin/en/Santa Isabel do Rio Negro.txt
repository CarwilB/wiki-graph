'''Santa Isabel do Rio Negro''' (''Saint Isabel of Black River'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 25,865 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/santa-isabel-do-rio-negro/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|62846|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> [[Pico da Neblina]], the highest mountain in Brazil at 2,994 metres (9,822 feet), is located in the municipality. The Municipality was formerly called Tapuruquara.

==Geography==
[[Pico da Neblina]], the highest mountain in Brazil at 2,994 metres (9,822 feet), is located in the municipality. However, the peak is {{convert|180|km}} in a straight line from the urban seat of the municipality, inaccessible except through the [[Amazon rainforest]], and in both a [[Pico da Neblina National Park|national park]] and a [[Yanomami]] reservation under federal control and with restricted access.

The municipality also contains part of the [[Amazonas National Forest]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional do Amazonas|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=114|accessdate=2016-05-19}}</ref>
==Transportation==
The city is served by [[Tapuruquara Airport]].
