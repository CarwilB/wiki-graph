{{Other uses|Zaragoza (disambiguation)}}
'''Zaragoza''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|297|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Xalapa]] and {{convert|10|km}} southwest of the city of [[Minatitlán, Veracruz|Minatitlán]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Zaragoza is located on the coastal plain of southern Veracruz. It borders the municipalities of [[Cosoleacaque]] to the east and south, [[Jáltipan]] to the west, and [[Oteapan]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Zaragoza, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=6 March 2018 |archive-date=4 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804015215/http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|264.418|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.03% of the state's area.<ref name=snim/> 

Zaragoza's terrain is flat and is composed of pastureland (51%), farmland (36%) and built-up areas (14%). It is located in the [[Coatzacoalcos River]] basin.<ref name=compendium/>

Zaragoza's climate is humid with rain throughout the year. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|24|and(-)|26|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1900|and(-)|2100|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
It is conjectured that a group of [[Popoluca]] settlers from [[Oteapan]] founded a settlement in this location in the early 19th century, which was then called San Isidro Xumuapan.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Zaragoza was incorporated on 1 December 1868 as a municipality in the canton of Minatitlán in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=121, 318 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0 |archive-date=2018-02-09 |access-date=2018-03-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180209182348/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Minerva Martínez Ramírez.<ref>{{cite news |url=http://expresionveracruz.com/2017/06/10/entregan-constancia-de-mayoria-a-alcaldesa-electa-de-zaragoza-por-la-coalicion-pan-prd/ |title=Entregan constancia de mayoría a alcaldesa electa de zaragoza por la coalición pan-prd |language=es |newspaper=La Expresión de Veracruz |date=10 June 2017 |access-date=6 March 2018 |archive-date=8 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180308041505/http://expresionveracruz.com/2017/06/10/entregan-constancia-de-mayoria-a-alcaldesa-electa-de-zaragoza-por-la-coalicion-pan-prd/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Zaragoza recorded a population of 10,720 inhabitants living in 2393 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=199 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Zaragoza |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=6 March 2018 |archive-date=7 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180307150915/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=199 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 11,354 inhabitants in Zaragoza, of whom 93.58% reported being of [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|Indigenous]] ancestry and 11.45% reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African]] ancestry.<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 3401 people or 32% of the population in Tepetzintla reported speaking an Indigenous language, of which 3341 spoke Nahuatl.<ref name=snim/>

There are 18 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Zaragoza, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 9639 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/> In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipiality of Zaragoza recorded a population of 11,899 inhabitants, of whom 52.63% are female, and 47.37% are male.<ref>{{Cite web |title=¿Cuál es la población de Zaragoza? |url=https://wkese.com/es/CUAL_ES_LA_POBLACION_DE_ZARAGOZA.html |access-date=2024-08-28 |website=wkese.com}}</ref>

==Economy==
Many of Zaragoza's inhabitants work in nearby cities such as Minatitlán, Jáltipan or Cosoleacaque, either in the service industry or for [[Pemex]]-related companies. The farming of pigs and cattle also contributes to the local economy.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.legisver.gob.mx/fiscalizacion/Planes%20Municipales/PlanesMunicipales2014pdf/PDM%20zaragoza.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal de Zaragoza, Veracruz |language=es |pages=98–102 |publisher=Municipal council of Zaragoza, Veracruz |access-date=6 March 2018}}</ref>
