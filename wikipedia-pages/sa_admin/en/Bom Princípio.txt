'''Bom Princípio''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is known in alla [[Brazil]] as ''[[Strawberry]] Land''.

It was raised to municipality status in 1992, the area being taken out of the municipalities of [[São Sebastião do Caí]] and [[Montenegro, Rio Grande do Sul|Montenegro]]. Part of its initial area subsequently formed the municipalities of [[Tupandi]] and [[São Vendelino]].<ref name="historico">{{Cite web|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/riograndedosul/bomprincipio.pdf|title=Histórico: Bom Princípio|publisher=IBGE|language=pt|access-date=27 January 2020}}</ref>
