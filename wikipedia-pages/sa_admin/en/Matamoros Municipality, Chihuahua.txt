''' Matamoros ''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Mariano Matamoros, Chihuahua|Mariano Matamoros]] (aka "Villa Matamoros"). The municipality covers an area of 
1,139.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 4,499,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Matamoros|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=044|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304091959/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=044|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 4,304 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Matamoros|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08044a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617065819/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08044a.htm|archivedate=June 17, 2011}}</ref> 

The municipality had 109 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 population in parentheses) was: [[Mariano Matamoros, Chihuahua|Mariano Matamoros]] (2,615), classified as urban.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
