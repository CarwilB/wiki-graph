'''Unión de Tula''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in the Sierra de Amula region of the state of [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|443|km2|abbr=on}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 13,133.<ref>{{cite web|title=Unión de Tula |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14110a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=April 20, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601195744/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14110a.htm |archive-date=June 1, 2007 }}</ref>

==Population==
The municipality has a population of 13,799 (2020 census), which includes 6,684 males and 7,115 females. About 4,664 people of this population are minors, 9,135 are adults, and 2,490 of these adults are over 60 years old.<ref name="pop"/>

==History==
Tula is referred to either Tula or Tvla because of the last names of the founders. Topete, Villaseñor, Lazcano and Arreola. Tula was conquered by Francisco Cortes of San Buenaventura in 1524. Later in 1872 a new construction began and there was a second parish church. Before this region was conquered by the Spanish it was populated by indigenous tribes. These tribes depended on Amollan Manor or Amula. Amollan Manor or Amula were like leaders to these people could rely on.<ref name="Union de Tula">{{cite web|title=UNION DE TULA|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM14jalisco/municipios/14110a.html|publisher=Secretaria de Gobernacion|access-date=12 April 2013}}</ref>

==Housing==
About 3,416 homes are registered in Union de Tula, 3,391 are congeneric to normal homes or apartments. Of these, 177 do not have a floor, and 39 have only one room. The economy situation in Tula is bad only 450 homes have their own computer, 2,544 own a washing machine and 3,237 homes have at least one or more televisions. Out of all 3,416 homes 3,212 homes have sanitary installations, 3,283 are connected to the public water supply, and 3,331 have access to electricity.<ref name=union>{{cite web|title=Union de Tula (Municipality)|url=http://www.en.nuestro-mexico.com/Jalisco/Union-de-Tula/|publisher=PHP Link Directory|access-date=12 April 2013}}</ref>

==Sightseeing==
Some places you can visit are [[Camino a Santa Ana]], which is a little road the most people run on for exercise, because cars rarely pass by there. There is also "El Tepehuaje" which is somewhat like a park. El Tepehuaje has many trees and it even has a soccer field for kids to enjoy and it also has a great view.<ref name="El Occidental">{{cite web|title=Vamos a Union de Tula|url=http://www.oem.com.mx/eloccidental/notas/n1519571.htm|publisher=Organizacion Editorial Mexicana|access-date=13 April 2013}}</ref>
