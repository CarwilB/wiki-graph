'''San Nicolás de los Arroyos Partido''' is the northernmost [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 200,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1183|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[San Nicolás de los Arroyos]], which is around {{convert|221|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
