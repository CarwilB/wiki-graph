'''Maciel''' is a district located in the north western part of the [[Caazapá Department]] of [[Paraguay]].

==Location==
The district is located in the north western part of the department and shares border with two districts of Caazapá ([[Caazapá]] and [[Doctor Moisés S. Bertoni]]), one of the [[Paraguarí Department]] ([[Mbuyapey]]) and two of the [[Guairá Department]] ([[Borja District|Borja]] and [[Iturbe (Paraguay)|Iturbe]]).

==Population==
At the 2002 census Maciel had a population of 3,957<ref>Archivo Nacional de Datos. ''[https://anda.ine.gov.py/anda/index.php/catalog/136/variable/V255 Censo Nacional de Población y Viviendas. Año 2002]''</ref>, an increase of 0.4% since 1992.
