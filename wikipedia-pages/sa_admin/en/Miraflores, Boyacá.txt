'''Miraflores''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Lengupá Province]].

Miraflores has been the site of the [[Tabebuia rosea|ocobos]] (Tree)
