'''Tarso''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], Colombia.  Located at an elevation of 1,235&nbsp;m (4,052&nbsp;ft) above sea level, it lies in the subregion of [[Southwestern Antioquia]].

==History==
In 2000, in Tarso, the [[United Self-Defense Forces of Colombia|AUC]] executed Luis Emilio Corrales Londoño, the head of the community action committee [[El Cedrón]].<ref name="Hristov2014">{{Cite book|author=Jasmin Hristov|title=Blood and Capital: The Paramilitarization of Colombiadate=31 July 2014| date=31 July 2014 |publisher=Ohio University Press|isbn=978-0-89680-466-1|page=111}}</ref>
