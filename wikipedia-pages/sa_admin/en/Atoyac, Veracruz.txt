'''Atoyac''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 196 km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 171.09 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|55|N|96|46|W|}}.

The municipality of is delimited to the north by [[Tepatlaxco]], to the east by [[Paso del Macho]], to the south-east by [[Cuitláhuac, Veracruz|Cuitláhuac]], to the south by [[Yanga, Veracruz|Yanga]], to the west by [[Amatlán de los Reyes]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30021a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Atoyac |publisher= Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=2009-06-04 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117185902/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30021a.htm |archive-date=2006-11-17 }}</ref>

It produces principally [[maize]] and [[beans]].
