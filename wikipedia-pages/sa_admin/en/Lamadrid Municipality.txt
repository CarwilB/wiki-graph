'''Lamadrid''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Lamadrid]]. The municipality covers an area of 506.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 1,708.<ref>{{cite web|title=Lamadrid|url=http://www.elocal.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05016a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=November 8, 2008}}{{Dead link|date=February 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
