'''Canto del Llano ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Santiago District, Veraguas|Santiago District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 25,627 as of 2014.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 1 of January 7, 1993.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 23,654.<ref name=inec/>
