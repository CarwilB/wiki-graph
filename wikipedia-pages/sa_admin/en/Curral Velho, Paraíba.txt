'''Curral Velho''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the micro-region of [[Itaporanga, Paraíba|Itaporanga]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraíba]]. The population is 2,512 (2020 est.) in an area of 222.96 km².<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/JAN Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 338 m.
