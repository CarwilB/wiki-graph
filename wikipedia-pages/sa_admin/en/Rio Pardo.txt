'''Rio Pardo''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 34,654 (2022 census) in an area of 2051&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6TE Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 41 m.
