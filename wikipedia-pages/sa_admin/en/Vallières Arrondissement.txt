'''Vallières''' ({{langx|ht|Valyè}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 71,851 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Vallières Arrondissement start with the number 24.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Vallières, Nord-Est|Vallières]]
* [[Carice]]
* [[Mombin-Crochu]]
