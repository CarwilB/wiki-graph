'''Fundão''' ({{IPA|pt|fũˈdɐ̃w}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] in the state of [[Espírito Santo]]. Part of the [[Greater Vitória]] metropolitan region, the Intermediate Region of Vitória, and the Immediate Region of Vitória, it is located north of the state [[Vitória, Espírito Santo|capital]], approximately {{convert|53|km|mi}} away. Covering an area of {{convert|288.724|km2|sqmi}}, of which {{convert|0.9|km2|sqmi}} is urban, its population was recorded as 18,014 inhabitants by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) in 2022.

The average annual temperature in the municipal seat is {{convert|23.2|C|F}}, with the predominant vegetation being [[Atlantic Forest]]. With an [[urbanization rate]] of approximately 84%,<ref name="urb_ibge_2010">{{cite web|url=http://www.censo2010.ibge.gov.br/sinopse/index.php?uf=32|title=Sinopse do Censo Demográfico 2010 - Espírito Santo|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|year=2010|access-date=26 January 2012}}</ref> the municipality had five healthcare facilities in 2009. Its [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) is 0.718, classified as high.

Fundão was emancipated from the former municipality of [[Nova Almeida]] in 1923. Today, it comprises the city of Fundão and the districts of Praia Grande, Timbuí, and Irundi. The municipality's name originates from the Fundão River, which flows through the seat. The primary economic activity is [[coffee production]], though the industrial sector contributes the largest share to the municipal [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP).

As part of the Caminho dos Imigrantes tourist route,<ref name="ig">{{cite web|url=http://turismo.ig.com.br/destinos_nacionais/2010/01/30/caminhos+do+imigrante+historias+e+natureza+no+espirito+santo+9356088.html|title=Caminhos do Imigrante: Histórias e Natureza no Espírito Santo|last=CASTELLO BRANCO|first=Fernanda|publisher=iG|access-date=28 January 2011}}</ref> Fundão is home to the Pico do Goiapaba-Açu, a granite peak rising {{convert|850|m|ft}} where the Goiapaba-Açu Municipal Park is located. Praia Grande is a major tourist attraction in the region, drawing visitors from [[Minas Gerais]] and other parts of Espírito Santo. Between December and January, the festivals of Saint Benedict and Saint Sebastian are held in Timbuí and Fundão, featuring performances by ''congo'' bands.

== History ==
=== Origins ===
[[File:Fundão pré-emancipação.jpg|thumb|left|The Fundão Cultural House, on the right, still as the Agostini family residence, and the old railway before 1922, when the municipal seat was [[Nova Almeida]].]]
Fundão historically succeeds the former municipality of Nova Almeida. Nova Almeida was established on the banks of the [[Reis Magos River]] as the Aldeia dos Reis Magos on 6 January 1557, by [[Jesuits]] and the [[Temiminó]] tribe, led by [[cacique]] Maracaiaguaçu, father of [[Arariboia]].<ref name="clerio">{{Cite book |last=BORGES |first=Clério José |title=História da Serra |publisher=Canela Verde |year=2009 |edition=3rd |location=Serra |pages=11, 34, 39, 40, 43, and 65 |language=Portuguese}}</ref> In 1757, Nova Almeida was elevated to a parish and town, and in 1760, to a ''[[comarca]]''.<ref name="emb">{{Cite book|title=Enciclopédia dos Municípios Brasileiros|language=Portuguese|location=[[Rio de Janeiro]]|publisher=[[Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]]|year=1959|pages=80–82, 149|volume=22|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/monografias/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/EMB-Enciclopedia%20dos%20Municipios%20Brasileiros/EMB_Volume22_ES_RJ.pdf}}</ref> Due to various factors, from the 1820s onward, Nova Almeida's population, which exceeded 8,000 inhabitants, began to decline, leading to the town's decay.<ref name="emb" /><ref name="cam">{{cite web|url=http://www.ape.es.gov.br/diccionario_CAM.htm|title=Diccionario Historico, Geographico e Estatistico da Provincia do Espirito Santo|last=MARQUES|first=César Augusto|date=6 July 1876|publisher=Arquivo Público Estadual (Espírito Santo)|pages=7, 8, 9|access-date=24 August 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090714131905/http://www.ape.es.gov.br/diccionario_CAM.htm|archive-date=2009-07-14|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The first [[Italian immigration to Brazil|Italian immigrants]], the Agostini family, arrived in the current Fundão region in 1875 from [[Tyrol]], settling in Três Barras (now Irundi). In the following decades, dozens of other families arrived in Fundão directly from [[Italy]] or were relocated from other regions, particularly [[Santa Teresa, Espírito Santo|Santa Teresa]].<ref name="italianos">{{cite book|last=NOVAES|first=Maria Stella de|publisher=Instituto Jones dos Santos Neves|year=1980|page=55, 56|volume=1|title=Os italianos e seus descendentes no Espírito Santo}}</ref> During the construction of the [[Vitória-Minas Railway]] in the interior of Nova Almeida, workers perished in the waters of the [[Fundão River]], named for its depth before the end of the 19th century.<ref name="cam" /> In the same location, at the Taquaraçu Farm owned by pioneer Cândido Vieira, the village of Fundão emerged.<ref name="emb" />

=== Administrative formation ===
[[File:Vista de Fundão em 1926.png|thumb|left|View of Fundão in 1926.]]
Various sources provide conflicting and contradictory information regarding the municipality's administrative formation. The seat was transferred to Timbuí in the early 20th century. The Fundão City Council's page<ref name="historiaCamara" /> and a message from [[Nestor Gomes]], president of Espírito Santo, to the state Legislative Congress on 3 May 1923,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://brazil.crl.edu/bsd/bsd/u1412/000006.html|title=Mensagem apresentada pelo presidente Nestor Gomes ao Congresso Legislativo do Estado do Espirito Santo, em 3 de Maio de 1923|publisher=Center for Research Libraries|access-date=21 June 2012|archive-date=26 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150926114309/http://brazil.crl.edu/bsd/bsd/u1412/000006.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> indicate that the municipality was renamed Timbuí or Timbuhy. However, the 1920 census<ref name="censos" /> does not reference this name. Under [https://pt.wikisource.org/wiki/Lei%20Estadual%20do%20Espírito%20Santo%201383%20de%201923 state law 1,383] of 5 July 1923,<ref name="educar">{{Cite journal|date=25 April 1994|title=Municípios do Espírito Santo|journal=A Gazeta|pages=14–16}}</ref><ref name="lei1383" /><ref name="lei1383dm" /><ref name="cmm">{{Cite book|title=Coleção de Monografias Municipais - Nova Série|language=Portuguese|location=[[Rio de Janeiro]]|publisher=[[Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]]|date=29 January 1985|volume=269}}</ref><ref name="gazetinha" /> the "former municipality of Nova Almeida"<ref name="lei1383" /><ref name="lei1383dm" /> had its seat transferred from Timbuí to Fundão,<ref name="historiaCamara" /> renaming the municipality Fundão and elevating the new seat to the status of a town. The law took effect on 1 January 1924,<ref name="lei1383">ESPÍRITO SANTO (state). ''Law 1,383/1923'': Transfers the municipal seat and elevates a settlement to the category of town. Espírito Santo Government: Vitória, 1923</ref><ref name="lei1383dm">{{cite journal|last=|first=|date=6 July 1923|title=Law No. 1,383|journal=Diário da Manhã|pages=1|location=Vitória|doi=|url=http://memoria.bn.br/DocReader/572748/18886|access-date=27 June 2017}}</ref> when the Fundão City Council was inaugurated in the new seat.<ref name="historiaCamara" /> Contradictorily, sources such as the ''Enciclopédia dos Municípios Brasileiros''<ref name="emb" /> and Fundão's own coat of arms and flag<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/norma_detalhe_resultado.asp?id_lei=699&exibe=1|title=Law No. 579, of August 12, 1985 - Establishes the official flag of the municipality of Fundão|publisher=Fundão City Council|access-date=5 December 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150926143017/http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/norma_detalhe_resultado.asp?id_lei=699&exibe=1|archive-date=2015-09-26|url-status=dead}}</ref> cite 5 July 1933, rather than 1923, as the municipality's creation date.

By decree-law of 2 March 1938, Fundão was elevated to city status. Later that year, on 11 November, the district of Nova Almeida was transferred to the municipality of Serra. At the same time, the district of Três Barras (now Irundi) was transferred from Santa Teresa to Fundão.<ref name="emb" /><ref name="historiaCamara">{{cite web|url=http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/historia.asp?id_municipio=16|title=History of the Municipality|access-date=13 May 2010|publisher=Fundão City Council|language=pt|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130801061819/http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/historia.asp?id_municipio=16|archive-date=2013-08-01|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2002, municipal law 229 was enacted, proposing to rename Fundão to Balneário Reis Magos, contingent on a referendum approved by the [[Legislative Assembly of Espírito Santo]].<ref name=lei229>{{cite web|url=http://www.legislacaoonline.com.br/cmfundao/images/leis/image/L2292002.pdf|author=FUNDÃO|title=Law 229, of November 5, 2002|publisher=Legislação Online - Fundão City Council|access-date=2 April 2014|archive-date=7 April 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140407075311/http://www.legislacaoonline.com.br/cmfundao/images/leis/image/L2292002.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality’s area is {{convert|279.648|km2|sqmi}}, representing 0.6069% of Espírito Santo’s territory, 0.0302% of the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region of Brazil]], and 0.0033% of Brazil’s total territory.<ref name="cnm">{{cite web |title=Dados gerais - Fundão - ES |url=https://cnm.org.br/municipios |access-date=14 January 2013 |publisher=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CMN)}}</ref> Of this, {{convert|0.9|km2|sqmi}} is within the urban perimeter.<ref name="ems">{{cite web|url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/es.html|title=Espírito Santo|publisher=Embrapa Satellite Monitoring|access-date=7 July 2011}}</ref>

Fundão is situated in the Intermediate Region of Vitória and the Immediate Region of Vitória.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/apps/regioes_geograficas/|title=Regiões Geográficas|access-date=30 August 2017|publisher=IBGE|last=|first=|year=2017}}</ref> It borders [[Ibiraçu]] to the north, [[Aracruz, Espírito Santo|Aracruz]] to the northeast, the [[Atlantic Ocean]] to the east, [[Serra, Espírito Santo|Serra]] to the south, [[Santa Leopoldina]] to the southwest, and [[Santa Teresa, Espírito Santo|Santa Teresa]] to the west,<ref name="inventario" /> and is {{convert|53|km|mi}} from Vitória.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/TabelaDistancias.pdf|title=Tabela - Distâncias|publisher=Departamento de Estradas de Rodagem do Espírito Santo|access-date=27 August 2012|archive-date=16 September 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120916100027/http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/TabelaDistancias.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Topography and hydrography ===
Despite its relatively small area, Fundão extends from the coast to the Serra do Castelo, a prominent geographical feature in central Espírito Santo.<ref name="inventario">{{cite web |last1=FONSECA |first1=Maria Angélica |last2=MOREIRA |first2=João Vicente Pedrosa |last3=SIMÕES |first3=Eduardo Rodrigo Donatelli |last4=VICENTE |first4=Célia Regina Bigossi |year=2005 |title=Inventário da oferta turística do município de Fundão |url=http://vix.sebraees.com.br/arquivos/biblioteca/invent_fund%C3%A3o.pdf |access-date=15 November 2009 |publisher=Sebrae |archive-date=3 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303173228/http://vix.sebraees.com.br/arquivos/biblioteca/invent_fund%C3%A3o.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its terrain is highly undulating, with 61.53% of its area having a slope of less than 30%.<ref name="infomun" /> The eastern region consists of lowlands and a nearly straight coastline, except for the Flecheira point, while the western region features elevations shaped by crystalline rocks.<ref name="cmm" /> Located {{convert|13|km|mi}} from the seat, on the border with Santa Teresa, is the Pico do Goiapaba-Açu, a [[granite]] formation<ref name="cmm" /> rising {{convert|850|m|ft}},<ref name="mcnaught">{{cite web|url=http://www.cce.ufes.br/observatorio/noticias/CometaMcNaughtEspiritoSanto.html|last=MALACARNE|first=Marcio|title=Cometa McNaught Visível do Espírito Santo|publisher=Observatório Astronômico da Ufes|year=2007|access-date=26 October 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090510143355/http://www.cce.ufes.br/observatorio/noticias/CometaMcNaughtEspiritoSanto.html|archive-date=2009-05-10|url-status=dead}}</ref> offering views of the region between Greater Vitória and [[Linhares]].<ref name="inventario" />
[[File:Vista Goiapaba-açu 2.jpg|center|800px|thumb|{{center|The terrain of Fundão and Santa Leopoldina as seen from Pico do Goiapaba-Açu.}}]] 
[[File:Rio Fundão.JPG|thumb|The [[Fundão River]], which gives the city its name.]]

The municipality’s soil is predominantly dystrophic red-yellow [[latosol]] and red-yellow [[Podzol|podzolic]] soil,<ref name="cmm" /> [[clay soil|clayey]], with medium to low fertility and a [[Soil pH|pH]] of 5.0.<ref name="educar" /><ref name="infomun">{{Cite book|title=Informações Municipais|last=ESPÍRITO SANTO|publisher=Secretaria de Estado de Ações Estratégicas e Planejamento - Departamento Estadual de Estatística|year=1993|volume=2|location=Vitória|pages=329–342|access-date=|chapter=Fundão}}</ref> It has moderate depth, good drainage, and low susceptibility to [[erosion]]. There are patches of organomineral soils, poorly developed, typical of flooded plains, acidic, with considerable but agriculturally limited mineral reserves due to excessive [[soil moisture]] in ''gley'' and organic soils. There are also patches of sandy soil, deep, with high [[Saturated and unsaturated compounds|aluminum saturation]], high acidity, [[Leaching (chemistry)|leaching]], low moisture retention, and very low fertility in their [[quartz]]ose sands and hydromorphic podzols. [[Outcrop|Outcrops]] without vegetation also occur.<ref name="cmm" />

Fundão’s coastline is ideal for [[fishing]] and [[Underwater diving|diving]] due to natural [[reef]]s.<ref name="inventario" /> Praia Grande, one of the region’s most visited resorts during summer, has shallow, calm waters. At the mouth of the [[Reis Magos River]], its [[estuary]] and a [[mangrove]] area are located. Less frequented by tourists and with fine sands, Enseada das Garças features [[restinga]] vegetation, while Praia do Rio Preto, on the border with Aracruz, hosts Atlantic Forest reserves.<ref name="inventario" />

The Reis Magos River originates in Santa Teresa, crosses Fundão’s seat as the Fundão River, and empties between Praia Grande and Nova Almeida, flowing from west to east. Its main tributaries are the Carneiro, [[Timbuí River|Timbuí]], and Piabas rivers. In the lower lands of the Reis Magos River Basin, [[rice]] and [[bean]] cultivation is practiced, alongside [[Coffee bean|coffee]] in other areas. Waterway transport is limited to small boats due to [[siltation]].<ref name="inventario" />

=== Climate ===
[[File:Enchente Fundão 2012.JPG|thumb|left|Flood in Fundão, resulting from heavy rains in January 2012.]]
Fundão’s [[climate]] is [[Tropical wet and dry climate|tropical wet and dry]] (type Aw according to the [[Köppen climate classification]]), with reduced rainfall in [[winter]] and an average annual temperature of {{convert|23.2|C|F}}, characterized by dry, mild winters and rainy [[summer]]s with high temperatures.<ref name="cmm" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> The warmest month, February, has an average temperature of {{convert|25.7|C|F}}, with an average high of {{convert|30.4|C|F}} and a low of {{convert|21.1|C|F}}. The coldest month, July, averages {{convert|20.6|C|F}}, with highs of {{convert|25.1|C|F}} and lows of {{convert|16.5|C|F}}.<ref name="sebrae98" />

The average annual precipitation is {{convert|1200|mm|in}}, with the driest month receiving {{convert|52|mm|in}}.<ref name="geoes">{{Cite book|last=MORAES|first=Cícero|title=Geografia do Espírito Santo|language=Portuguese|location=Vitória|publisher=Fundação Cultural do Espírito Santo|year=1974|page=69}}</ref> The rainiest month averages {{convert|277|mm|in}}. During the rainy season, [[flood]]s and [[landslide]]s are common in some areas.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2009/10/554989-rio+fundao+chega+ao+nivel+de+alerta+de+enchente.html|title=Rio Fundão chega ao nível de alerta de enchente|date=28 October 2009|access-date=22 May 2012|archive-date=1 November 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091101023629/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2009/10/554989-rio+fundao+chega+ao+nivel+de+alerta+de+enchente.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2010/11/691893-chuva+deixa+fundao+embaixo+d+agua.html|title=Chuva deixa Fundão embaixo d'água|publisher=Gazeta Online|date=8 November 2010|access-date=22 May 2012|archive-date=3 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303165508/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2010/11/691893-chuva+deixa+fundao+embaixo+d+agua.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=11 January 2012 |title=Internautas mostram em fotos e vídeos os estragos causados pela chuva no Espírito Santo nesta quarta-feira |url=https://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/vc-no-g1-es/fotos/2012/01/internautas-registram-cenas-dos-transtornos-causados-pela-chuva-no-es.html |access-date=22 May 2012 |publisher=G1}}</ref> The highest recorded temperature was {{convert|39|C|F}}, and the lowest was {{convert|7|C|F}}.<ref name="cmm" />

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Restinga Praia Grande Fundão.JPG|thumb|right|[[Restinga]] vegetation in Praia Grande.]]
Much of Fundão’s Atlantic Forest has been replaced by agricultural and livestock areas. Common in the coastal plain are mangroves along the Reis Magos River and [[restinga]] along the coast. Native riverbank vegetation has been substituted with pastures and residential areas, causing erosion.<ref name="inventario" />

[[File:APA Goiapaba-Açu.jpg|thumb|left|Goiapaba-Açu Environmental Protection Area.]]
The municipality’s fauna is typical of the [[Atlantic Forest]], including [[spider]]s, [[crab]]s, [[butterfly|butterflies]], and [[dragonfly|dragonflies]] among [[invertebrates]]; [[snake]]s, [[tortoise]]s, and [[lizard]]s among [[reptiles]]; [[frog]]s and [[toad]]s among [[amphibians]]; [[parakeet]]s, [[pigeon]]s, [[thrush (bird)|thrushes]], [[tanager]]s, and [[toucan]]s among [[birds]]; and [[Giant otter|river otters]], [[capybara]]s, [[otter]]s, and [[marmoset]]s among [[mammals]].<ref name="inventario" />

To preserve Fundão’s environment, the Goiapaba-Açu Municipal Park was established in 1991,<ref>{{cite web |title=Law 295, of December 16, 2004 - Names Asturiano Benito Castaño the Goiapaba-Açu Municipal Park |url=https://fundao.legislacaocompilada.com.br/Arquivo/Documents/legislacao/html/L2952004.html?identificador=370034003A004C00 |access-date=24 August 2012 |publisher=Fundão City Council}}</ref> and in 1994, the Goiapaba-Açu [[Environmental protection area (Brazil)|Environmental Protection Area]] (APA) was created, spanning Fundão and Santa Teresa around the municipal park, managed by the Espírito Santo Institute of Environment and Water Resources (Iema).<ref name="apa">{{cite web |title=APA de Goiapaba-Açu |url=https://iema.es.gov.br/APA_Goiapaba_Acu |access-date=24 August 2012 |publisher=Instituto Estadual de Meio Ambiente e Recursos Hídricos}}</ref> The APA hosts 133 bird species, including the [[robust woodpecker]] (''Campephilus robustus'') and the [[bare-throated bellbird]] (''Procnias nudicollis'').<ref name="apa" /> Notable plant species in the region include bromeliads, orchids, [[cinnamon]], [[Hymenaea courbaril|courbaril]], [[Pterodon emarginatus|sucupira]], and [[Platycyamus (plant)|pau-pereira]]. Amphibians are also prominent in Goiapaba-Açu’s fauna.<ref>{{cite book|last=RAMOS|first=Aparecida Demoner|author2=GASPARINI, João Luiz|title=Anfíbios do Goiapaba-Açu|publisher=Gráfica Santo Antônio|year=2004|pages=75|location=Vitória}}</ref>

The Augusto Ruschi Forest Garden, the first model of forest gardens proposed by Espírito Santo at [[Earth Summit|Eco-92]],<ref name="horto">{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2012/06/noticias/especiais/1261945-20-anos-apos-eco92-programa-de-reflorestar-cidades-capixabas-passa-longe-de-ser-bom-exemplo.html|title=20 anos após Eco92, programa de reflorestar cidades capixabas passa longe de ser bom exemplo|publisher=Gazeta Online|date=5 June 2012|access-date=2 September 2012|archive-date=9 June 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120609062718/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2012/06/noticias/especiais/1261945-20-anos-apos-eco92-programa-de-reflorestar-cidades-capixabas-passa-longe-de-ser-bom-exemplo.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> is located next to the Josel de Oliveira Sports Gymnasium. Spanning 4.5 hectares of reforested pastures, it includes a nursery for seedlings of [[Cedrus|cedar]], [[cherry]], [[Cariniana|jequitibá]], sucupira, [[peroba]], landscaping species, and fruit trees such as [[avocado]], blackberry, coffee, [[Spondias mombin|yellow mombin]], [[cashew]], [[jackfruit]], [[Syzygium jambos|rose apple]], [[Syzygium cumini|Malabar plum]], [[mango]], and tangerine.<ref name="educar" /><ref name="gazetinha" /><ref name="inventario" />

In 2010, Fundão’s population consisted of 9,016 mixed-race individuals (52.96%), 6,488 whites (38.11%), 1,426 blacks (8.38%), 75 Asians (0.44%), and 20 indigenous people (0.12%).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=result_univer_censo2010&banco=cidadesat&codmun=320220|title=Fundão - Censo Demográfico 2010: Características da População e dos Domicílios: Resultados do Universo|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=24 May 2012}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
While developed within a predominantly Catholic social framework, Fundão today is home to various Protestant denominations. The city is home to diverse Protestant or reformed faiths, with the [[Assemblies of God]] being the largest Christian denomination in the municipality.

According to the 2010 census conducted by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, the population of Fundão is composed of: [[Catholicism|Catholics]] (61.69%), [[Protestantism|Evangelicals]] (28.03%), [[Irreligion|people with no religion]] (6.49%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (1.22%), and 0.52% are divided among other religions.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?c=2103&z=t&o=3|title=Tabela 2103 - População residente, por situação do domicílio, sexo, grupos de idade e religião|publisher=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática|access-date=29 June 2012}}</ref>

== Politics ==
[[File:Prefeitura de Fundão.jpg|thumb|left|Headquarters of the city hall and the Fundão City Council.]]
Under the [[Constitution of Brazil|1988 Constitution]], municipal administration is carried out by the [[executive branch]] and the [[legislative branch]].<ref name="lom" />

The first representative of the executive branch and mayor of the municipality was Hermínio Jorge de Castro, appointed shortly after the municipality’s emancipation.<ref name="historiaCamara" /><ref name="annuario">{{cite book |last= |first= |url= |title=ANNUARIO do Espírito Santo e Norte do Brasil |publisher=IBGE |year=1938 |isbn= |editor= |edition= |volume= |location=Vitória |pages=109–112 |language= |chapter= |issn= |id= |quote=''“Sr. Herminio Jorge de Castro succeeded in definitively transferring the seat to the Villa de Fundão during the government of Dr. Florentino Avidos”'' |access-date=}}</ref> In recent years, the position was temporarily held from 1 January – 26 October 2017, by Eleazar Ferreira Lopes (then affiliated with PCdoB), who was on leave from the presidency of the City Council.<ref name="tol010117">{{cite web|url=http://www.tribunaonline.com.br/fundao-e-muqui-a-espera-de-prefeitos/|title=Fundão e Muqui à espera de prefeitos|last=França|first=Brunella|publisher=Tribuna Online|date=1 January 2017|access-date=1 January 2017|archive-date=3 January 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170103004027/http://www.tribunaonline.com.br/fundao-e-muqui-a-espera-de-prefeitos/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Following a supplementary mayoral election on 1 October 2017, the new mayor, Pretinho Nunes (PDT), was sworn in on 27 October of the same year.<ref name=":1" /> Since 1 January 2021, the municipality has been governed by Gilmar de Souza Borges, who has previously served as mayor on three other occasions.<ref name=":2">{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/es/espirito-santo/noticia/2021/01/01/prefeito-e-vereadores-de-fundao-tomam-posse-veja-lista-de-eleitos.ghtml|title=Prefeito e vereadores de Fundão tomam posse; veja lista de eleitos|access-date=2021-01-02|website=G1|date=January 2021 |language=pt-br}}</ref>

The legislative branch consists of the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of eleven councilors elected for four-year terms (in accordance with Article 29 of the Constitution<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/Constituicao.htm#art29iv|title=Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil: Artigo 29|publisher=Planalto|access-date=8 May 2011}}</ref>) and is structured as follows: two seats for [[Cidadania]]; two for [[Podemos (Brazil)|Podemos]]; two for the [[Brazilian Socialist Party]] (PSB); two for the [[Republicans (Brazil)|Republicans]]; one for [[Patriota]]; one for the [[Democratic Labour Party (Brazil)|Democratic Labour Party]] (PDT); and one for the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD).<ref name=":2" /> The council is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for the administration and the executive branch, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).<ref name="lom" />

The municipality of Fundão is governed by an organic law.<ref name="lom">{{cite web|url=http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/lom.pdf|title=Lei Orgânica|publisher=Fundão City Council|access-date=8 August 2012|archive-date=15 April 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100415152238/http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/LOM.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> It serves as the seat of the Fundão judicial district.<ref name="cgj">{{cite web|url=http://www.cgj.es.gov.br/Comarcas/|title=Comarcas|publisher=Corregedoria Geral da Justiça do Espírito Santo|access-date=8 August 2012}}</ref> According to the Espírito Santo Regional Electoral Court (TRE-ES), the municipality had 14,125 registered voters in May 2017.<ref name="eleitorado">{{cite web|url=http://www.tre-es.jus.br/eleitor/estatisticas/evolucao-do-eleitorado|title=Eleitorado atual|publisher=Espírito Santo Regional Electoral Court|access-date=20 June 2017}}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
Fundão is divided into three districts in addition to the municipal seat: Praia Grande, Timbuí, and Irundi. According to the 2010 census, the seat had 8,744 inhabitants; Praia Grande, 5,349; Timbuí, 2,400; and Irundi, 532.<ref>{{cite web|url=ftp://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Censos/Censo_Demografico_2010/Sinopse/Regioes_Metropolitanas/sinopse_rm_tab_3_8.zip|date=20 April 2011|access-date=30 April 2011|publisher=IBGE - File Server|last=|first=|title=Sinopse RM tab 3 8}}</ref> The Jones dos Santos Neves Institute suggests there are nine neighborhoods in the seat, six in Praia Grande, and one in Timbuí, totaling 16 across the municipality.<ref name="ijsnkmz">{{cite web|url=http://www.ijsn.es.gov.br/ConteudoDigital/20120703_Bairros_CGeo_IJSN.kmz|title=Detalhes Limite de Bairros - ES|publisher=Jones dos Santos Neves Institute|format=[[Keyhole Markup Language|kmz]]|access-date=14 January 2013}}</ref>

The [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) of Fundão is distinguished by its industrial sector.<ref name="pibibgecsv">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=134&codmun=320220|title=3202207_pibmunic.csv|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=11 September 2013}}</ref> According to 2018 data from the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality recorded a GDP of R$475.925 million. The per capita GDP stood at R$22,597.47.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/>

=== Primary sector ===
[[File:Café conilon Timbuí.JPG|thumb|left|A reference unit for [[Coffea canephora|conilon]] coffee in Timbuí.]]
Agriculture and livestock form the sector with the smallest contribution to Fundão’s economy. Out of the city’s total GDP, R$28.954 million comes from the gross value added by agriculture and livestock.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> Despite its lower GDP contribution, Fundão’s economy is rooted in agriculture,<ref name="brasis" /> particularly [[coffee production]].<ref name="educar" /><ref name="seag">{{cite web|url=http://www.seag.es.gov.br/?p=1522|title=Produtores de Fundão recebem treinamento sobre poda em lavoura cafeeira|publisher=Secretaria de Estado da Agricultura, Abastecimento, Aquicultura e Pesca|access-date=10 August 2012}}</ref> According to 2015 IBGE data, the municipality maintained a herd of 11,617 cattle, 639 pigs, 126 sheep, 305 horses, 13 goats, 7,400 poultry (including 3,700 hens), and 3,050 quails. In 2015, the city produced 3.468 million liters of milk from 2,141 cows, alongside 56,000 dozen chicken eggs, 46,000 dozen quail eggs, and 90,000 kilograms of honey.<ref name="pecuaria">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/v4/brasil/es/fundao/pesquisa/18/16459?detalhes=true|title=Pecuária|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=20 June 2017}}</ref> Temporary crops primarily include cassava (1,200 tons), sugarcane (1,080 tons), and watermelon (36 tons).<ref name="lavtemp">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/v4/brasil/es/fundao/pesquisa/14/10193?detalhes=true|title=Produção Agrícola Municipal 2015|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=20 June 2017}}</ref> Permanent crops mainly consist of coffee (4,571 tons), bananas (1,905 tons), and tangerines (374 tons).<ref name="lavperm">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/v4/brasil/es/fundao/pesquisa/15/11863?detalhes=true|title=Produção Agrícola Municipal 2015|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=20 June 2017}}</ref>

According to the 2017 Agricultural Census, of the 453 rural establishments, 306 focused on permanent crops, 22 on temporary crops, 102 on livestock and other animal husbandry, 3 on forestry (planted forests), and 2 on aquaculture.<ref name=":4">{{cite book|url=https://datasebrae.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Fund%C3%A3o.pdf|title=Panorama do Município - Fundão|last=Sebrae|year=2021}}</ref> The municipality’s livestock sector is mixed (for both meat and dairy), with predominance in the coastal region.<ref name="educar" />
[[File:Columbia Tecnologia.jpg|thumb|left|The Columbia Tecnologia factory produces equipment for oil exploration.]]
[[File:Centro de Fundão.jpg|thumb|right|Presidente Vargas Street in downtown Fundão.]]

=== Secondary sector ===
R$148.277 million of the municipal GDP comes from the gross value added by industry (secondary sector), currently the largest contributor to Fundão’s GDP.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> Since 2004, this figure has grown due to [[royalties]] from [[petroleum]].<ref name="incaper">{{cite web|url=http://www.incaper.es.gov.br/proater/municipios/Centro_cerrano/Fundao.pdf|title=Programa de Assistência Técnica e Extensão Rural Proater 2011-2013|publisher=Incaper|year=2011|access-date=26 January 2012|archive-date=17 December 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131217221734/http://www.incaper.es.gov.br/proater/municipios/Centro_cerrano/Fundao.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Tertiary sector ===
R$261.537 million of the municipal GDP is generated by services (tertiary sector).<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> According to the federal government’s Business Map, as of August 2020, the city had 2,453 companies. The 2018 Annual Social Information Report (RAIS) indicated that the municipality employed 3,041 people, with 1,388 in small businesses, 889 in medium-sized businesses, none in large companies, and 764 in public administration.<ref name=":4" /> According to the Espírito Santo Institute for Educational and Industrial Development (Ideies), as of 2021, the average monthly salary in the municipality was R$1,760.58.<ref name=":4" />

== Urban infrastructure ==
=== Healthcare ===
In 2012, the municipality had five healthcare facilities, all municipally operated, offering six beds for [[inpatient care]]. The city also provides outpatient services, including basic medical specialties and dental care, through the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS).<ref name="ibgesaude">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=320220&nomemun=Fund%E3o|title=Serviços de Saúde - 2009|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=12 August 2012}}</ref> Fundão has Primary Healthcare Units and a Diagnostic and Therapy Support Service Unit. As As of December 2012, Fundão had 50 doctors, six dentists, four physiotherapists, three speech therapists, four pharmacists, three psychologists, five nursing assistants, ten nurses, and 30 nursing technicians, totaling 115 healthcare professionals. The municipality is part of the Vitória Regional Health District.<ref name="datasus">{{cite web|url=ftp://ftp.datasus.gov.br/caderno/geral/es/ES_Fundao_Geral.xls|title=ES Fundão Geral|publisher=Datasus|access-date=12 August 2012}}</ref> In 2010, there were 5,278 women of childbearing age (10 to 49 years).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=sinopse_censo2010&codmun=320220&nomemun=Fund%E3o|title=Fundão - Sinopse Censo 2010|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=12 August 2012}}</ref> In 2008, 220 live births were recorded, with 18.6% of mothers under 20 years old.<ref name="datasus" /> Without hospitals,<ref name="datasus" /> the city relies on the Dr. César Agostini Mixed Healthcare Unit, managed by the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cadernos Regionais - Região Metropolitana |url=https://saude.es.gov.br/Media/sesa/Planejamento/Plano_diretor_regionalizacao/Caderno_PDR_Metropolitana.pdf |access-date=12 August 2012 |publisher=Secretaria de Estado de Saúde}}</ref>

=== Education ===
[[File:EEEFM Nair Miranda.jpg|thumb|right|Nair Miranda State Elementary and Secondary School.]]
In 2012, the municipality recorded approximately 3,542 enrollments, 239 teachers, and 16 public schools, with no federal or private schools.<ref name="IBGEeducacao">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=117&codmun=320220|title=3202207 educa|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=14 October 2014}}</ref> According to 2010 census data, the [[illiteracy]] rate among individuals over 15 years was 9.5%; for those aged 15 to 24, 1.2%; for those aged 25 to 59, 8.0%; and for those over 60, 27.1%.<ref name="ibge_ind_soc">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=ind_soc_munic_censo2010&banco=cidadesat&codmun=320220|title=Indicadores sociais municipais: uma análise dos resultados do universo do Censo Demográfico 2010 -Fundão|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=24 May 2012}}</ref>

=== Public safety ===
[[File:Acidente Fundão.jpg|thumb|right|Accident on BR-101 in Fundão in 2011, resulting in five deaths.<ref name="acidente">{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2011/06/noticias/a_gazeta/dia_a_dia/878037-colisao-com-veiculo-que-levava-pacientes-mata-5-e-fere-9.html|title=Colisão com veículo que levava pacientes mata 5 e fere 9|publisher=A Gazeta|date=13 June 2011|access-date=12 May 2012}}</ref>]]
As in many growing Brazilian municipalities, crime poses a challenge in Fundão. In 2012, there were four firearm homicides, yielding a firearm homicide rate of 22.7 per 100,000 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://mapadaviolencia.org.br/tabelas2015/mapaViolenca2015_alfaMun_total.xlsx|title=Mapa da Violência 2015|publisher=Mapa da Violência|access-date=28 September 2015|archive-date=29 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150929055027/http://mapadaviolencia.org.br/tabelas2015/mapaViolenca2015_alfaMun_total.xlsx|url-status=dead}}</ref> While such figures may be less reliable in smaller cities,<ref name="viome">{{cite web|url=http://mundoestranho.abril.com.br/materia/qual-a-cidade-mais-violenta-do-brasil|title=Qual a cidade mais violenta do Brasil?|last=MOTOMURA|first=Marina|publisher=Mundo Estranho|date=18 December 2012|access-date=11 April 2013}}</ref> Fundão neighbors [[Serra, Espírito Santo|Serra]], which, with over 300,000 residents, recorded a 2010 homicide rate of 99.9 per 100,000, the third highest in Brazil for cities of its size. Moreover, the [[Greater Vitória]] metropolitan area, including Fundão, ranked as the 15th most violent region globally in 2014 for areas with over 300,000 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.seguridadjusticiaypaz.org.mx/sala-de-prensa/1165-por-cuarto-ano-consecutivo-san-pedro-sula-es-la-ciudad-mas-violenta-del-mundo|title=Por cuarto año consecutivo, San Pedro Sula es la ciudad más violenta del mundo|publisher=Seguridad, Justicia y Paz|date=19 January 2015|access-date=28 September 2015|archive-date=22 November 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211122172455/http://www.seguridadjusticiaypaz.org.mx/sala-de-prensa/1165-por-cuarto-ano-consecutivo-san-pedro-sula-es-la-ciudad-mas-violenta-del-mundo|url-status=dead}}</ref> In April 2013, Fundão’s police force comprised six military police officers, with two assigned to each urbanized district, a number deemed insufficient by the president of the Timbuí Residents’ Association.<ref name="pmg1">{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/noticia/2013/04/casal-tenta-fugir-de-assalto-e-e-baleado-em-fundao-es.html|title=Casal tenta fugir de assalto e é baleado em Fundão, ES|last=ZANOTTI|first=Álvaro|publisher=G1|date=10 April 2013|access-date=10 April 2013}}</ref>

=== Services and communications ===
Water supply and sewage collection across the city are managed by the Espírito Santo Sanitation Company (Cesan).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cesan.com.br/page.php?4|title=Abrangência|publisher=Companhia Espírito-Santense de Saneamento|access-date=20 August 2012|archive-date=5 August 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120805233401/http://www.cesan.com.br/page.php?4|url-status=dead}}</ref> Electricity distribution in Fundão is handled by EDP Escelsa, serving multiple municipalities in Espírito Santo.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.escelsa.com.br/energia/empresa/area_concessao/area_concessao.asp|title=Área de concessão|publisher=EDP Escelsa|access-date=20 August 2012|archive-date=27 January 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127094636/http://www.escelsa.com.br/energia/empresa/area_concessao/area_concessao.asp|url-status=dead}}</ref> Dial-up and [[broadband]] (ADSL) internet services are offered by various free and paid internet service providers. Mobile phone services are provided by multiple operators. Fundão’s area code ([[direct distance dialing|DDD]]) is 27,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.telelistas.net/templates/ddd_resultado.aspx?estado=es&localidade=Fund%E3o&ddd=&postCadastro=1|title=DDD & DDI - Resultado - Fundão|publisher=Telelistas|access-date=20 August 2012}}</ref> and its postal code ([[Código de Endereçamento Postal|CEP]]) is 29185-000.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.buscacep.correios.com.br|title=BuscaCEP|publisher=Correios|access-date=20 August 2012}}</ref> Fundão is home to Rádio Mais 98.5 FM.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/noticia_exibe.asp?id=348|title=Sessão da Câmara transmitida pela Rádio Mais FM 98.5|publisher=Câmara Municipal de Fundão|date=31 March 2011|access-date=21 August 2012|archive-date=31 July 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731105957/http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/noticia_exibe.asp?id=348|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radiomais98.com/|title=Rádio Mais 98,5 FM|publisher=Rádio Mais 98,5 FM|access-date=21 August 2012}}</ref>

=== Transportation ===
[[File:EFVM Fundão 3.jpg|thumb|right|Passenger train of the Vitória-Minas Railway passing through downtown Fundão.]]
Fundão is served by the [[Vitória-Minas Railway]], via the Fundão Station, inaugurated on 15 May 1905, with daily passenger and cargo trains to [[Belo Horizonte]] and [[Vitória, Espírito Santo|Vitória]].<ref name="efb-f">{{cite web|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efvm/fundao.htm|title=Fundão|last=GIESBRECHT|first=Ralph Mennucci|access-date=2 May 2011|publisher=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil|language=Portuguese}}</ref> The railway is vital for the city and its residents, offering a safer and more affordable alternative to road transport.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efvm/fundao.htm|title=Fundão -- Estações Ferroviárias do Estado do Espírito Santo|access-date=2 October 2020|website=www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br|archive-date=20 February 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200220121625/http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efvm/fundao.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> In the Timbuí district, the Timbuí Station operated from 29 December 1904, until at least the 1960s.<ref name="lom" /><ref name="efb-t1">{{cite web|last=GIESBRECHT|first=Ralph Mennucci|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efvm/timbui.htm|title=Timbuí|access-date=2 May 2011|publisher=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref name="efb-t2">{{cite web|last=GIESBRECHT|first=Ralph Mennucci|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efvm/timbui-nova.htm|title=Timbuí (nova)|access-date=2 May 2011|publisher=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref name="books">{{cite book|last1=Gerodetti|first1=João Emílio|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=j_T1noNaMC4C|title=As ferrovias do Brasil nos cartões-postais e álbuns de lembranças|access-date=2 May 2011|publisher=Google Livros|pages=336|language=Portuguese|last2=Cornejo|first2=Carlos|date=2005 |isbn=978-85-89820-02-8 }}</ref>

By road, Fundão is accessible via [[BR-101 (Brazil highway)|BR-101]], connecting the seat to Serra and Ibiraçu; ES-010, linking Praia Grande to [[Aracruz, Espírito Santo|Santa Cruz]] and Nova Almeida; ES-124, connecting Praia Grande to Santa Rosa (Aracruz); ES-261, linking the seat to Santa Teresa and Santa Rosa; and ES-264, connecting Timbuí to Santa Leopoldina and Nova Almeida.<ref name="dermapa">{{cite web |year=2010 |title=Mapa do Sistema Rodoviário |url=https://der.es.gov.br/Media/der/P%C3%A1ginas/Mapa%20Rodovi%C3%A1rio/MAPA_DER_ES_2016_1.pdf |access-date=2025-10-08 |publisher=Departamento de Estradas de Rodagem do Estado do Espírito Santo}}</ref><ref name="dersis1">{{cite web|url=http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/SistemaRodoviarioEstadual.pdf|title=Revisão e atualização do sistema rodoviário estadual|publisher=Departamento de Estradas de Rodagem do Estado do Espírito Santo|year=2008|access-date=10 May 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120219021001/http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/SistemaRodoviarioEstadual.pdf|archive-date=19 February 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="dersis2">{{cite web|url=http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/ListaRodovias.pdf|title=Relatório de Trechos|publisher=Departamento de Estradas de Rodagem do Estado do Espírito Santo|date=23 March 2009|access-date=10 May 2012|archive-date=25 April 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120425041735/http://www.der.es.gov.br/download/ListaRodovias.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[File:BR-101 Fundão.JPG|thumb|left|BR-101 interchange in downtown Fundão.]]
The BR-101 section through Fundão becomes problematic during high-traffic periods, such as holidays, due to its intersection and traffic lights in the city center. These conditions can cause congestion stretching up to 10 kilometers, disrupting local traffic.<ref name="cbnbr1">{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2013/02/cbn_vitoria/reportagens/1409831-br-101-norte-estrutura-precaria-e-responsavel-por-acidentes-e-congestionamentos.html|title=BR 101 Norte: estrutura precária é responsável por acidentes e congestionamentos|last=FÉLIX|first=Tiago|publisher=CBN Vitória|date=27 February 2013|access-date=28 March 2013|archive-date=29 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150929024204/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2013/02/cbn_vitoria/reportagens/1409831-br-101-norte-estrutura-precaria-e-responsavel-por-acidentes-e-congestionamentos.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="g1br1">{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/noticia/2012/12/prf-registra-engarrafamento-de-10km-na-br-101-em-fundao-no-es.html|title=PRF registra engarrafamento de 10km na BR-101 em Fundão, no ES|publisher=G1 Espírito Santo|date=22 December 2012|access-date=28 March 2013}}</ref><ref name="cbnbr2">{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2011/12/a_gazeta/minuto_a_minuto/1070043-semaforo-provoca-engarrafamento-quilometrico-na-br-101-em-fundao.html|title=Semáforo provoca engarrafamento quilométrico na BR 101 em Fundão|date=26 December 2011|access-date=28 March 2013|publisher=CBN Vitória|archive-date=29 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150929035631/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2011/12/a_gazeta/minuto_a_minuto/1070043-semaforo-provoca-engarrafamento-quilometrico-na-br-101-em-fundao.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="g1br2">{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/noticia/2012/02/br-101-norte-tem-quase-5-km-de-engarrafamento-em-fundao-es.html|title=BR-101 Norte tem quase 5 km de engarrafamento em Fundão, ES|publisher=G1 Espírito Santo|date=18 February 2012|access-date=28 March 2013}}</ref>

[[File:Transcol ES.jpg|thumb|right|Transcol bus on Line 886 along ES-010 in Praia Grande, heading toward [[Jacaraípe, Espírito Santo|Jacaraípe]].]]
Intermunicipal public transport is provided by the Transcol system, with lines 854 and 886 connecting Praia Grande and Direção to the Jacaraípe Terminal, and line 888 linking Timbuí to the Laranjeiras Terminal.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/es/espirito-santo/noticia/nova-linha-do-transcol-que-vai-ate-timbui-es-comeca-a-circular-na-proxima-semana.ghtml|title=Nova linha do Transcol que vai até Timbuí, ES, começa a circular na próxima semana|date=29 June 2018|access-date=29 June 2018|publisher=G1}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://tribunaonline.com.br/nova-linha-do-transcol-com-destino-a-timbui-comeca-a-funcionar-na-proxima-segunda|title=Nova linha do Transcol com destino a Timbuí, começa a funcionar na próxima segunda|date=28 June 2018|access-date=29 June 2018|publisher=Tribuna Online|archive-date=29 June 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180629161157/https://tribunaonline.com.br/nova-linha-do-transcol-com-destino-a-timbui-comeca-a-funcionar-na-proxima-segunda|url-status=dead}}</ref> Selective transport is also available via line 1805, connecting Praia Grande to Vitória’s bus terminal.<ref name="ceturb">{{cite web|url=http://www.ceturb.es.gov.br/default.asp?pagina=15980|title=Horários|publisher=Companhia de Transportes Urbanos da Grande Vitória|access-date=21 May 2012}}</ref> In 2012, a law established municipal public transport lines between Fundão and Timbuí, and between Timbuí and Praia Grande via Fundão.<ref name="lei818">{{cite web |author=FUNDÃO |title=Lei 818, de 5 de janeiro de 2012 - Cria as linhas de transporte público (Fundão x Timbuí e vice-versa e Fundão x Praia Grande e vice-versa), bem como estabelece o regime de exploração do Serviço de Transporte Coletivo por meio de auto-ônibus e dá outras providências |url=https://fundao.legislacaocompilada.com.br/norma.aspx?id=2351&situacao=2&ano=2012 |access-date=21 May 2012 |publisher=Câmara Municipal de Fundão}}</ref> Fundão is served by the Fundão Bus Station, offering routes to Vitória, Santa Teresa, northern and northwestern Espírito Santo, eastern Minas Gerais, and southern Bahia, and the Praia Grande Bus Station, with routes to Vitória and Rio de Janeiro.<ref name="inventario" />

The municipal vehicle fleet in 2012 totaled 6,601 vehicles, including 3,290 cars, 362 trucks, 113 tractor-trailers, 672 pickup trucks, 279 vans, 55 minibuses, 1,291 motorcycles, 215 scooters, 43 buses, 34 wheeled tractors, 21 utility vehicles, and 226 other types of vehicles.<ref name="ibgefrota">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/grafico_cidades.php?lang=&codmun=320220&idtema=139&search=espirito-santo|title=Espírito Santo » Fundão » frota - 2014 - gráfico|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|access-date=28 September 2015}}</ref>

==Culture==
Among the recreational venues in Fundão are the Expedicionários Square, located in Downtown, and various clubs. The Fundão Horse Club organizes horseback rides; the Tropical Campestre Club hosts sports and recreational facilities; and the Maracaiá Club, owned by the Comercial Futebol Clube,<ref name="secult2" /> serves as a downtown nightclub operating on weekend nights with a capacity for 200 people.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> Additionally, [[forró]] dances are held on weekends at Cabana do Matoso in the Encruzo community.<ref name="incaper" />

===Arts===
[[File:Casa da Cultura de Fundão.JPG|thumb|left|Fundão Culture House.]]
Fundão has architectural structures dating back to the [[Brazilian coffee cycle|coffee cycle]]. In the seat, the former Agostini family residence, now the Fundão Culture House, was designated a cultural heritage site of Espírito Santo in 1986.<ref name="brasis">{{cite web|url=http://www.revelandoosbrasis.com.br/blog/municipio2/fundao-2/|title=Fundão|publisher=Revelando os Brasis|access-date=10 August 2012|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250611000000/http://www.revelandoosbrasis.com.br/blog/municipio2/fundao-2/|archive-date=11 June 2025}}</ref> The two-story building, constructed in 1882 on the Taquaraçu farm by Cândido Vieira, was purchased at auction by the Agostini family in the early 20th century and used by the Angelo Agostini e Cia. coffee export company, managed by merchant and banking representative Hipólito Agostini. The building was connected by telephone to the Agostini farm in the rural area. At the time, the ground floor served as a commercial space, while the upper floor was a residence. In 1925, it was the home of physician and politician César Agostini.<ref name="historiaCamara" /> Until the 1970s, the family used the ground floor as an office.<ref name="educar" /> Later, the building was acquired by the municipal government. It was restored in 1985 and 1986 to house a craft shop, museum, and cultural event space, with its exterior paved with stones and a new external staircase built.<ref name="inventario" /><ref>{{cite book|publisher=CONSELHO Estadual de Cultura; UNIVERSIDADE Federal do Espírito Santo|title=Catálogo de Bens Tombados no Espírito Santo|location=Vitória|pages=47–48}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|last=ESPÍRITO SANTO (estado). Secretaria de Estado da Cultura. Conselho Estadual de Cultura|title=Arquitetura|location=Vitória|publisher=Governo do Estado do Espírito Santo|year=2009|pages=90–92|url=http://secult.es.gov.br/_midias/pdf/3909-4b86811f9d3e1.pdf|access-date=10 August 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130722195750/http://secult.es.gov.br/_midias/pdf/3909-4b86811f9d3e1.pdf|archive-date=2013-07-22|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[File:Fundão Igreja São José e Escadaria.jpg|thumb|right|Chrysantho de Jesus Rocha Staircase, in front of the São José Parish Church.]]
Also part of the municipality’s civil architecture is the former Augusto Agostini farm, now the Pé de Serra ranch in Três Barras, which houses a late 19th-century mansion used for coffee trading before becoming a residence. From this location, one can view and access the Goiapaba-Açu peak. Located in front of the Parish Church, the Chrysantho de Jesus Rocha Staircase, built in a [[Baroque Revival architecture|neo-Baroque style]] resembling a chalice with a host,<ref name="magda">{{cite web |author=GRAZZIOTTI |first1=Vanilda Scopel |last2=SCHIMITEL |first2=Lindaura Wagner |last3=RESENDE |first3=Evânia Tofoli de |last4=TOFOLI |first4=Magda Brommonschenkel |date=21 June 2007 |title=Um passeio por Fundão... do passado ao presente |url=http://magdaprojetosepesquisa.blogspot.com.br/ |access-date=10 August 2012 |publisher=Magda Projetos e Pesquisas}}</ref> features gardens and a small grotto with saint statues. The staircase is used to raise the masts of Saint Benedict and Saint Sebastian by ''congo'' bands.<ref name="inventario" /> It is named in honor of the father of [[Archdiocese of Mariana|Mariana’s archbishop]] and former president of the [[Episcopal Conference of Brazil]], the Fundão native [[Geraldo Lyrio Rocha]].<ref name="magda" /><ref name="glr">{{cite web|url=http://www.arqmariana.com.br/?page_id=7|title=Dom Geraldo|publisher=Arquidiocese de Mariana|access-date=10 August 2012|archive-date=5 June 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120605004158/http://www.arqmariana.com.br/?page_id=7|url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[File:Congo Fundão.JPG|thumb|left|São Benedito Congo Band at the São Benedito and São Sebastião Festival in Fundão, 2010.]]
Examples of local religious architecture include:<ref name="inventario" />
* '''Our Lady of Victory Chapel''': Built in 1878 in Irundi, with a view of Goiapaba-Açu. It has its own cemetery with about ten tombstones of former residents.
* '''Bom Jesus da Lapa Church''': From the early 20th century, located in Irundi. Built on a rock, facing a former community.
* '''Our Lady of Penha Church''': In Timbuí’s central square.
* '''São José Parish Church''': Atop the Chrysantho de Jesus Rocha Staircase, significantly altered from its original structure. Most visited during the São Benedito festival.
* '''Santo Antônio Church''': In the seat.<ref name="infomun" />

[[File:Tapioca no Encruzo.jpg|thumb|right|Tapioca production in the Encruzo community.]]
Local music and dance are represented by ''congo capixaba'' bands, which perform on festive days. Fundão’s bands include the São Benedito and São Sebastião Children’s Congo Band in Timbuí; the São Benedito and São Sebastião Congo Band in Campestre I; the Konfogo Cultural Congo Band in Downtown; and the São Sebastião and São Benedito Congo Band of Irundi and Piabas in Três Barras.<ref name=":3">{{cite book|title=Atlas do Folclore Capixaba|publisher=Sebrae|year=2009|location=Vitória}}</ref> Additionally, in 2013, [[gospel music]] was officially recognized as a cultural manifestation of the municipality.<ref name="gospel">{{cite web|url=http://camarafundao.es.gov.br/sno/atas/ATA_36-2013.pdf|title=Ata da 36ª sessão - ordinária|publisher=Câmara Municipal de Fundão|date=18 November 2013|access-date=10 December 2013|archive-date=12 December 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131212090122/http://camarafundao.es.gov.br/sno/atas/ATA_36-2013.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Italian colonization contributed to local cuisine with [[polenta]], [[jabuticaba]] wine, and milk liqueur. [[Moqueca]] is also consumed due to indigenous influence. The municipality is also known for homemade biscuits, crystallized sweets,<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="infomun" /> [[tapioca]] (in the rural Encruzo community),<ref name="gazetinha2">{{cite journal|title=Cidade às margens do Reis Magos|journal=A Gazeta|volume=A Gazetinha|date=10 July 1999}}</ref> cheeses, and [[requeijão]].<ref name="incaper" /> Fundão’s crafts include items made from [[Typha domingensis|southern cattail]] fibers, cane, wood, [[liana]], coconut, and [[banana tree]], as well as shells, fabric, porcelain, plaster, canvas, and glass, used for fabric and porcelain paintings, embroidery, jewelry, baskets, sieves, brooms, and decorated glassware. The municipality has a deactivated cinema.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="infomun" />

===Tourism===
Fundão’s tourism is unique in Espírito Santo for offering both beach and mountain attractions.<ref name="brasis" /> Accessible via the ES-010 highway, the coastline features natural reefs ideal for diving and fishing, compact sands, [[restinga]], and chestnut trees. The main attraction is Praia Grande, also known as Joaripe, a five-kilometer beach with murky waters, frequented by visitors from Minas Gerais and other Espírito Santo cities.<ref name="seculo">{{cite journal|last=FERNANDES|first=Flávia|year=2002|title=Fundão, um lugar com jeito de moeda|journal=Século|issue=25|pages=48–55|publisher=M4|location=Vitória|url=http://www.seculodiario.com/seculo/pdf/revista25/revista.pdf|access-date=19 July 2011}}</ref> The Reis Magos River estuary is located there. Praia Grande offers lodging and dining options with guesthouses, hotels, camping, rental properties, restaurants, bars, and kiosks. Due to restricted beachfront urbanization, its natural features are preserved. During Carnival, this beach attracts as many as 100,000 people who come to enjoy the music. Its calm waters are ideal for water sports and boat tours.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" />
[[File:Praia Grande Fundão.jpg|center|600px|thumb|Praia Grande, Fundão’s main beach.]]
The Rio Preto Beach, on the border with Aracruz, and Enseada das Garças are less frequented, with fine sands. Rio Preto Beach, 100 meters from ES-010, is well-preserved with [[Atlantic Forest]] reserves and high waves suitable for [[surfing]].<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> Enseada das Garças, between Flecheira and Fortaleza points and 500 meters from ES-010, has abundant seaweed and a rocky, muddy bottom.<ref name="sebrae98" /> It features bars, restaurants, and camping but suffers from disorganized occupation, compromising its original characteristics.<ref name="inventario" /> It is more visited in summer by tourists.<ref name="sebrae98" />

Just 50 meters from ES-010, the small Porto da Lama cove has coarse sands and, due to mangrove landfills, non-native vegetation, but also calm waters and restinga. It is less frequented, located between two reef points, with a 250-meter stretch and a sandy, muddy bottom, serving as an anchorage for fishing boats, hence its name. Its waters are clear in summer and murky in winter.<ref name="sebrae98" />

[[File:Goiapaba-Açu 3x4.jpg|right|thumb|Goiapaba-Açu peak seen from Fundão.]]
In the municipality’s interior, the Goiapaba-Açu Peak, accessible via ES-261, houses a native plant nursery and a micropropagation laboratory for orchids, bromeliads, and fruit plants. The park features trails, natural lookouts, waterfalls, springs, valleys, and an events center for visitors. From its summit, several regional cities are visible.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> It is also possible to enjoy adventure tourism in the park, in the form of hang gliding, paragliding, and enduro.<ref name="incaper" />

Five kilometers from the seat via ES-261 toward Praia Grande, the Fundão or Jarrão Waterfall attracts up to 200 visitors daily on weekends,<ref name="incaper" /> especially in summer.<ref name="educar" /> Located in a rocky section of the Fundão River on Nilsinho’s property, it features several one-meter-high falls and a dam 150 meters away. It has a navigable 10-meter-wide pool and a 15-meter-wide, 500-meter-long rapid. The dam contains introduced fish species such as peacock bass, armored catfish, carp, tilapia, and Malaysian prawns, which reproduce among cattails in March, when fishing is prohibited. Due to the city's sewage system, the waterfall is not suitable for swimming. The site includes a bar, fishing facilities, a campground, and an old working distillery.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" />

[[File:Mexericas Fundão.jpg|thumb|left|Tangerine stalls, a symbol of Fundão.]]
In Timbuí, the dam of the former hydroelectric plant, three kilometers from BR-101, has a one-meter waterfall and small rapids among rocks, suitable for bathing and fishing.<ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> In Duas Bocas, four kilometers from the seat, the Salto Waterfall, formed by an old hydroelectric dam with a one-meter drop, has murky, cold waters and coarse sand banks, favorable for bathing and leisure activities.<ref name="sebrae98" /> Other waterfalls exist in the municipality,<ref name="infomun" /> such as on the Piabas River.<ref name="2007ag11" />

Fundão is known for roadside commerce. Since the late 1960s, stalls along BR-101 sell fruits, particularly [[Ponkan|ponkans]],<ref name="educar" /><ref name="gazetinha" /> a municipal symbol.<ref name="2007ag8">{{cite journal|title=A história de uma terra rica em cultura|journal=A Gazeta|date=6 July 2007|pages=8|volume=Fundão - 74 anos de emancipação|location=Vitória}}</ref> Also on BR-101, since the 1960s, Dona Jandyra’s sweets are sold, including [[Cocada|cocadas]], jams, liqueurs (made from araçaúna, fig, banana, [[Genipa americana|genipap]], mallow, and rose or carnation petals), pickles, cakes, candies, and crystallized sweets, made from [[Scarlet eggplant|gilo]], papaya, guava, fig, tomato, carrot, beetroot, jackfruit, orange, and genipap.<ref name="educar" /><ref name="inventario" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> Since March 1994, the Lorena dairy, located on ES-261 toward Santa Teresa, has been artisanally producing cheeses such as [[cottage cheese]], [[Minas cheese]], [[mozzarella]], [[provolone]], palm heart cheese, and cabacinha cheese.<ref name="educar" />

Annual events include Catholic festivals in neighborhoods and rural communities, [[Festa Junina|June festivals]],<ref name="infomun" /><ref name="sebrae98" /> the Political Emancipation Festival in July,<ref name="sebrae98" /> New Year’s Eve, and Carnival in Praia Grande,<ref name="sebrae98" /> and the São Benedito and São Sebastião festivals in Timbuí (December to January) and Fundão (20 January),<ref name="emb" /><ref name="secult2">{{cite web|url=http://secult.es.gov.br/?id=/mapa_cultura/materia.php&cd_matia=567|title=Mapa da Cultura - Fundão|publisher=Secretaria de Estado da Cultura|access-date=21 August 2012}}</ref><ref name="2007ag11">{{cite journal|title=Cultura, turismo e aventura|journal=A Gazeta|date=6 July 2007|pages=11|volume=Fundão - 74 anos de emancipação|location=Vitória}}</ref> featuring ''congo'' bands and processions.<ref name="sebrae98" /> These festivals attract about 2,000 people each.<ref name=":3" /> From 1993 to 1997, the Tangerine Festival was held in May.<ref name="gazetinha" />

===Sports===
The municipality was home to the [[American football]] teams Fundão Spartans, runner-up in the 2010 state Muqueca Bowl championship,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://futebolamericanoes.blogspot.com/|title=Campeonatos|publisher=Liga Espiritossantense de Futebol Americano}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2011/10/gazetaesportes/futebol_americano/984089-especial-fundao-spartans-volta-ao-cenario-do-futebol-americano-estadual-apos-longo-hiato.html|title=ESPECIAL: Fundão Spartans volta ao cenário do futebol americano estadual após longo hiato|last=GOZZER|first=Vitor|publisher=Gazeta Online|date=6 October 2011|access-date=9 October 2011|archive-date=3 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303170701/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2011/10/gazetaesportes/futebol_americano/984089-especial-fundao-spartans-volta-ao-cenario-do-futebol-americano-estadual-apos-longo-hiato.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> and Antares American Football (now Cabritos American Football), runner-up in the 2012 Espírito Santo American Football Championship and participant in the 2012 Touchdown Tournament.<ref name="ge1">{{cite web|url=http://globoesporte.globo.com/es/noticia/2013/03/antares-f-retorna-vitoria-e-inicia-treinos-para-o-torneio-touchdown.html|title=Antares F.A. retorna a Vitória e inicia treinos para o Torneio Touchdown|last=NOVO|first=Sidney Magno|publisher=Globo Esporte|date=3 March 2013|access-date=25 March 2012}}</ref>

In soccer, the Comercial Futebol Clube maintains a training school for children.<ref name="inventario" /> One of its alumni is [[Luan Garcia]], a defender who played for [[CR Vasco da Gama|Vasco da Gama]]<ref name="luanag">{{cite web|url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2013/04/esportes/futebol/1432941-de-ferias-em-fundao-capixaba-luan-sonha-em-assumir-lugar-do-mito-no-coracao-da-torcida.html|title=De férias em Fundão, capixaba Luan sonha em assumir lugar do Mito no coração da torcida|last=CASTRO|first=Luciana|publisher=A Gazeta|date=21 April 2013|access-date=29 April 2013|archive-date=13 March 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140313103738/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2013/04/esportes/futebol/1432941-de-ferias-em-fundao-capixaba-luan-sonha-em-assumir-lugar-do-mito-no-coracao-da-torcida.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="luange">{{cite web|url=http://globoesporte.globo.com/futebol/times/vasco/noticia/2011/12/joia-2012-comparado-mauro-galvao-luan-sonha-ser-novo-dede.html|title=Joia 2012: comparado a Mauro Galvão, Luan sonha ser novo Dedé|last=CAVALIERI|first=Rafael|publisher=Globo Esporte|date=29 December 2011|access-date=29 April 2013}}</ref><ref name="luanln">{{cite web|url=http://www.lancenet.com.br/vasco/Luan-deixa-lagrimas-passado-inesquecivel_0_783521759.html|title=Luan deixa lágrimas no passado e vive dia inesquecível|last=CIANTAR|first=Rodrigo|publisher=Lancenet|date=1 October 2012|access-date=29 April 2013}}</ref> and the [[Brazil national under-23 football team]], and who won a gold medal at the [[Football at the 2016 Summer Olympics – Men's tournament|2016 Summer Olympics]].<ref name="luanolimp">{{cite web|url=http://www.folhavitoria.com.br/esportes/noticia/2016/08/espirito-santo-entra-para-a-historia-com-dois-ouros-na-olimpiada-rio-2016.html|title=Espírito Santo entra para a história com dois ouros na Olimpíada Rio 2016|publisher=Folha Vitória|date=22 August 2016|access-date=22 August 2016}}</ref>

Fundão is home to the Josel de Oliveira Sports Gymnasium, near the forest nursery, with a capacity of 5,000 seats,<ref name="inventario" /> and, in the Oseias neighborhood, the Manoel de Almeida Matos Stadium, owned by the Comercial Futebol Clube.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/projetos_tramitando.asp?id_proposicao=470|title=Indicação 36/2006|publisher=Câmara Municipal de Fundão|access-date=10 August 2012|archive-date=3 September 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140903085101/http://www.camarafundao.es.gov.br/projetos_tramitando.asp?id_proposicao=470|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Holidays===
In Fundão, [[Good Friday]] and [[Saint Joseph's Day|St. Joseph's Day]] (19 March) are municipal holidays, with the Executive Branch able to decree up to twelve optional holidays per year, as per municipal law No. 1,266/2021.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.legislacaocompilada.com.br/fundao/Arquivo/Documents/legislacao/html/L12662021.html|title=LEI Nº 1266/2021 16/03/2021|access-date=6 December 2021|website=www3.camarafundao.es.gov.br}}</ref> According to federal law No. 9,093 12 September 1995, municipalities may have up to four municipal holidays, including Good Friday.<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web|url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm|title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado? folga automática pode gerar alteração contratual|author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão|publisher=Guia Trabalhista|language=Portuguese|access-date=21 February 2011}}</ref><ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaLei">{{cite web|url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm|title=Lei Nº 9.093, de 12 de setembro de 1995|author=Presidência da República|access-date=21 February 2011}}</ref>
