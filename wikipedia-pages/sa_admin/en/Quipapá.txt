'''Quipapá''' is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 186&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2025]]) population of 17,731 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Panelas]]   ('''N''');  [[Alagoas]]  state   ('''S''');  [[São Benedito do Sul]]  ('''E'''); [[Canhotinho]] and [[Jurema, Pernambuco|Jurema]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 230.61&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 462 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] and [[Mundaú River|Mundaú rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Clima - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 22.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 186&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Quipapá''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s, [[manioc]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
