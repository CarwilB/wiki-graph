'''São Francisco de Itabapoana''' ({{IPA|pt|sɐ̃w fɾɐ̃ˈsisku dʒi itɐbapuˈɐ̃nɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 42,210 (2020) and its area is 1,111&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
