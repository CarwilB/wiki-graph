'''Belén de Umbría''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. At a distance of approximately 70 kilometers from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Belén de Umbría |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100233/belen-de-umbria/ |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref> In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 25,276.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Belén de Umbría, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/belen-de-umbria |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
The name Belén was chosen by a bishop who visited these lands in 1902, because they say that when he arrived at the village he commented: "this place looks like a manger, it should be called Bethlehem" and Umbria in homage to the Umbra, one of the most prominent tribes of this region. It was founded on August 10, 1890 and at this time it has more than 25,000 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |last=admin |date=2015-11-01 |title=Belen de Umbría, 125 Años de historia alrededor de la caficultura |url=https://www.destinocafe.com/?p=1700 |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Destino Café |language=es}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Belén de Umbría is situated 1,634 [[metres]] above sea level, due to that elevation the town has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average annual temperature of 20°C.<ref name=":0" />
