'''Supía''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]]. Located along the [[Colombian coffee growing axis]], it was part of the "Coffee Cultural Landscape" [[UNESCO]] [[World Heritage Site]] in 2011. <ref name = "unesco" >{{cite web |title = Coffee Cultural Landscape |url = http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1121 |website = UNESCO World Heritage List |publisher = United Nations Educational, Scientific, and Cultural Organization |access-date = 25 Apr 2011}}</ref>
