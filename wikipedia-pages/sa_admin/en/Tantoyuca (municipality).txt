'''Tantoyuca''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. The municipal seat is [[Tantoyuca, Veracruz]].

In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 97,949 (up from 89,492 in 1995), of whom 23,893 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tantoyuca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30155a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=2008-11-06|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070127023713/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30155a.htm|archive-date=27 January 2007}}</ref> 
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 40,864 (47.92%) spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Wastek language|Wastek]] (Huasteco).

The municipality of Tampico Alto covers a total surface area of 1,205.84&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
