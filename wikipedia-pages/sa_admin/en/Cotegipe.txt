'''Cotegipe''' (former '''Campo Largo''') is a [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] and a [[List of municipalities in Bahia|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Bahia]] in the north-east region of [[Brazil]].  Part of the Extremo Oeste Baiano [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]], the town was founded in 1820 and had a population of 13,769 as of the 2020.<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008" />
