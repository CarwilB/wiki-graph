'''Monterrey Municipality''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Nuevo León|51 subdivisions]] of the [[Nuevo León|State of Nuevo León]], [[Mexico]]. Its municipal seat is located in the City of [[Monterrey]].

The municipal government is headed by the [[municipal president of Monterrey]] (mayor of Monterrey).
