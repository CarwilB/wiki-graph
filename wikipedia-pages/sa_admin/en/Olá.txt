'''Olá  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Olá District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,419 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Olá District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,255; its population as of 2000 was 1,326.<ref name=inec/>
