'''Ibirajuba''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ibiɾɐˈʒubɐ]}}) is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located 182&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the capital of the state of Pernambuco, it has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 7,140 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State – [[Pernambuco]]
* Region – [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries – [[Altinho, Pernambuco|Altinho]] and [[Cachoeirinha, Pernambuco|Cachoeirinha]]   ('''N''');  [[Lajedo, Pernambuco|Lajedo]]    ('''S''' and '''W''');  [[Panelas]] and [[Jurema, Pernambuco|Jurema]]    ('''E''').
* Area – 189.59&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation – 612 m
* Hydrography – [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una River]]
* Vegetation – [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate – Transition between [[tropical]] (Hot and humid)  and, [[semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature – 22.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] – 182&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Ibirajuba''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[maize|corn]], [[beans]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[pigs]], [[goats]] and [[poultry]].
