'''Enrique Baldivieso''' (or: Enrique Valdivieso) is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Potosí Department|Potosí]]. It is named after the former vice president [[Enrique Baldivieso]]. Its seat is [[San Agustín, Bolivia|San Agustín]] with a population of 533 during the census of 2001<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo National Institute of Statistics of Bolivia]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110924073930/http://www.ine.gob.bo/ |date=September 24, 2011 }}</ref> situated on the bank of the [[Turuncha River]] in San Agustín Canton.

== Location ==
Enrique Baldivieso province is one of sixteen provinces in the Potosí Department. It is located between 21° 03' and 21° 51' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 07' and 67° 48' [[Longitude|West]]. In the east, north, west and south it is almost completely encircled by the [[Nor Lípez Province]], only in the southeast it is bordered by the [[Sur Lípez Province]] for 20&nbsp;km.

The province extends over 120&nbsp;km from northwest to southeast at an average width of 35&nbsp;km.

== Division ==
The province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''San Agustín Municipality'''. It is identical to Enrique Baldivieso Province. The province is subdivided into four [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]]:
*[[Alota Canton|Alota]] - 533 inhabitants ''(2001)''
*Cerro Gordo - 292 inhabitants
*[[San Agustín Canton|San Agustín]] - 567 inhabitants
*Todos Santos - 248 inhabitants

== Population ==
The main language is [[Quechua languages|Quechua]], spoken by 96%, while 86% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]]. The population rose from 1,313 inhabitants (1992 census) to 1,640 (2001 census), an increase of 25%. 46.5% of the population are younger than 15 years old.

99.7% of the population have no access to electricity, 99% have no sanitary facilities. 72% of the population are employed in agriculture, 28% in general services. 95% of the population are Catholic, 2% Protestant.
