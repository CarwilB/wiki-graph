'''Tekom Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Yucatán (state)|Yucatán]]. Tekom is also the name of the municipality's largest settlement and seat of the municipal government (''[[cabecera municipal]]''). In the XII national census (''[[Censo General de Población y Vivienda]]'') conducted by [[INEGI]] in 2000, Tekom municipality recorded a population of 2,660 inhabitants. In the intermediate census count undertaken by INEGI in 2005 (the ''[[Conteo de Población y Vivienda|II Conteo de Población y Vivienda]]''), the total municipal population was recorded as 2,933 inhabitants,<ref>INAFED (2005); INEGI (2006, pp.60–61)</ref> 2,413 of whom<ref>Aged five years or older</ref> spoke one of the recognised [[indigenous languages of Mexico]] ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]).<ref>INEGI (2006, pp.60–61)</ref>

The municipality is located in the southeastern portion of the state in the north-central [[Yucatán Peninsula]], and covers a total area of some {{convert|201.83|km2|mi2}}. The terrain is a virtually flat plain, with a median elevation of {{convert|27|m|ft|0}} [[above mean sea level]].<ref name="INAFED 2005">INAFED (2005)</ref> As with most of the Yucatán Peninsula there are no surface rivers or creeks, but there are subterranean water-filled sinkholes (''[[cenote]]s'') some of which are naturally exposed at the surface. Average annual rainfall, which is regular in the summer months, amounts to approximately {{convert|82|mm|in|0}}. The climate is classed as subhumid.<ref name="INAFED 2005"/>

Tekom was created as a ''[[municipio libre]]'' of Yucatán state on 1 January 1920. In 1957 the southern half of Tekom's territory was partitioned off to form a new municipality, [[Chikindzonot]].<ref name="INAFED 2005"/>
