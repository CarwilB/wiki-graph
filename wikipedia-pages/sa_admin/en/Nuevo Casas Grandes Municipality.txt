'''Nuevo Casas Grandes''' is a Mexican [[Municipalities of Chihuahua|municipality]], located in the state of [[Chihuahua (state)|Chihuahua]], northwestern Mexico.  The capital and seat of the [[municipality]] is the city of [[Nuevo Casas Grandes]], which has the same name as the surrounding municipality.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 59,337.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Nuevo Casas Grandes|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=050|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Nuevo Casas Grandes had a population of 55,553.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Nuevo Casas Grandes, the municipality had 158 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.

It has only one airport and is seven hours behind the Greenwich Meridian.<ref name="SEDESOL" />

As of March 2, 2021, Nuevo Casas Grandes reported 696 confirmed cases and 72 deaths from the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus (COVID-19) en Nuevo Casas Grandes, Chihuahua |url=http://www.gncys.com/covid19/covid19-municipio.aspx?edoid=CH&munid=050 |website=www.gncys.com |access-date=March 5, 2021 |language=es-mx |date=1 February 2020}}</ref>

Yuriel Armando González Lara ([[Institutional Revolutionary Party|PRI]]), [[2021 Mexican local elections|candidate for municipal president]], was assassinated on March 4, 2021.<ref name="assassinated_mar4">{{cite news |last1=Villalpando |first1=Rubén |title=La Jornada - Asesinan a candidato a alcaldía en Nuevo Casas Grandes, Chihuahua |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/03/05/estados/asesinan-a-candidato-a-alcaldia-en-nuevo-casas-grandes-chihuahua/ |access-date=March 5, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |date=March 5, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>
