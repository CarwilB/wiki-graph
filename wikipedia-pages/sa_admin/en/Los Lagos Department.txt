'''Los Lagos''' is a department located in the south of [[Neuquén Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://sanmartinadiario.com.ar/actualidad/24682-remataran-propiedades-que-el-ex-secretario-de-kirchner-tenia-en-san-martin.html|title=Rematarán propiedades que el ex secretario de Kirchner tenía en San Martín|work=San Martín a Diario|date=22 June 2019|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
The Department limits with [[Lácar Department]] at north, [[Rio Negro Province]] at the east and southeast, with [[Chile]] at southwest and east.
