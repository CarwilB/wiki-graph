'''Turvo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in the [[Southern Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=412796|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Paraná {{!}} Turvo|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-15}}</ref>

==History==
Turvo became municipality in 1 February 1983.

==Climate==
Turvo has [[oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb''), The average temperature on summer are warm, it reaches 22°C, on winter it reaches 18°C. [[Frost]] are really common on winter.
