'''Tamalín''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the north zone in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It has a surface of 417.85 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|21|20|N|97|49|W|}}. By Decree of November 13, 1875 there was raised in municipality Tamalín's congregation that concerned to Tantima's municipality.{{clarification needed|date=June 2015}}

==Geography==

Tamalín lies on the west shore of [[Tamiahua Lagoon]]. The municipality is delimited to the north and north-west by [[Ozuluama de Mascareñas (municipality)|Ozuluama]], to the east by [[Tamiahua]], and to the south by [[Tancoco]], [[Naranjos Amatlán]] and [[Chinampa de Gorostiza]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30150a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tamalín |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}</ref>

The weather in Tamalín is very warm and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[watermelon]] and [[orange fruit]].

==Celebrations==

In '''Tamalín''', in July  takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe, Patron of the town.
