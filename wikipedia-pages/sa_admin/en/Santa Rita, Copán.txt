'''Santa Rita''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Copán department|Copán]].

== History ==
The municipality of Santa Rita is believed to have been founded in 1700, where it was then known as Aldea Cashapa. However, the area was not recognized as a municipality until 1875.<ref>Fiallos, Carmen. 1989. "Los Municipios de Honduras".</ref>
