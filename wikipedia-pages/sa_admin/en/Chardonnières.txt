'''Chardonnières''' ({{IPA|fr|ʃaʁdɔnjɛʁ}}; also '''Les Chardonnières'''; {{langx|ht|Chadonyè}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Chardonnières Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. Chardonnière is the largest producer of [[grape]]s in Haiti.<ref name="ht">[http://www.haititourisme.gouv.ht/pages/1/13-gastronomie.php Haiti Ministry of Tourism - Gastronomy]</ref> A Festival of the Grape (''Festival du raisin des Chardonnières'') is annually held.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-11625-haiti-social-3rd-edition-of-the-festival-of-the-grape-of-chardonnieres.html |title=Haiti - Social : 3rd Edition of the Festival of the Grape of Chardonnières |editor=Press |date=19 July 2014 |publisher=Haiti Libre |access-date=5 June 2017}}</ref>

==History==
The town was originally named ''Chardonnières'' by [[buccaneer]]s or [[filibuster]]s.<ref name="ht"/> In its [[Saint-Domingue|colonial days]], grapes had reached the tables of the [[List of French monarchs|kings of France]]. Today, grapes are used by the Chardonnesiens to welcome visitors to express their generosity and hospitality.<ref name="ht"/>

==Geography==
Chardonnière is located about 72 kilometers from [[Les Cayes]], and minutes from [[Port-à-Piment]].<ref name="ht"/>

==Infrastructure==
The [[Catholicism|Catholic]] [[Parish in the Catholic Church|parish]] of Chardonnière was erected in 1897 and consecrated to [[Saint Anne]].<ref name="ht"/>
