'''Lavras do Sul''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southwestern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. It is in the [[Campanha Meridional]] [[micro-region]] and the [[Sudoeste Rio-Grandense]] [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|meso-region]]. The population is 7,444 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|2600.60|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1VIK Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The [[Camaquã River]] flows through the municipality.
