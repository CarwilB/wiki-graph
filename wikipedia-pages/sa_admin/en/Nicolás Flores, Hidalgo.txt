'''Nicolás Flores''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|393.2|km2}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 6,202.<ref>{{cite web|title=Nicolás Flores|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13043a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129102739/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13043a.htm|archivedate=January 29, 2007}}</ref> In 2017 there were 3,346 inhabitants who spoke an [[Indigenous languages of Mexico|indigenous language]],<ref>{{cite web|work=Sistema Integral de Información del Estado de Hidalgo|title=Información Estadística Básica: Carpeta Municipal Nicolás Flores|url=http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/040%20La%20Misi%C3%B3n.pdf|publisher=Secretaria de Planeación Desarrollo Regional Regional y Metropolitano|accessdate=22 March 2017|format=PDF|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171230230105/http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/040%20La%20Misi%C3%B3n.pdf|archive-date=30 December 2017}}</ref> primarily [[Mezquital Otomi]].<ref>{{cite book|last1=Báez Cubero|first1=Lourdes|last2=Garret Ríos|first2=Gabriela|last3=Pérez González|first3=David|last4=Moreno Alcántara|first4=Beatriz|last5=Fierro Alonso|first5=Ulises Julio|last6=Hernández García|first6=Milton Gabriel|title=Los pueblos indígenas de Hidalgo: Atlas etnográfico|date=2012|editor=Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia|location=México, D.F; México|isbn=978-607-484-357-6|edition=Primera|url=http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|format=PDF|pages=32–35|access-date=2019-05-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190725220343/http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|archive-date=2019-07-25|url-status=dead}}</ref>
