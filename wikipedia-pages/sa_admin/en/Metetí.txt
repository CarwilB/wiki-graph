'''Metetí ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 7,976 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 58 of July 29, 1998, owing to the Declaration of Unconstitutionality of Law 1 of 1982.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 6,244.<ref name=inec/> It is the last major town on the [[Inter-American Highway]] before the highway ends at [[Yaviza]].<ref name=moon>Friar, William. ''Panama''. [[Moon Publications]] (2008), p. 422–23. {{ISBN|1-59880-085-X}}.</ref> Most of the town's residents are originally from [[Chiriquí Province]].<ref name=moon/> The town has a bank with an ATM, a few restaurants and hotels, a police station, and a gas station.<ref name=moon/>
