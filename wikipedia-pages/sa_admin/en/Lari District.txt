'''Lari District''' is one of twenty [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Caylloma Province|Caylloma]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Ch'ila mountain range|Ch'ila]] mountain range traverses the district. One of the highest elevations of the district is [[Nevado Mismi|Mismi]] at {{convert|5597|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} above sea level.
