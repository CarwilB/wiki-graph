'''Castrovirreyna''' is one of seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] located in the [[Huancavelica Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Castrovirreyna]].

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (77.20%) learnt to speak in childhood, 22.30% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
