'''Sozoranga Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Loja Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Sozoranga]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 7,994.<ref>[http://www.sozoranga.gob.ec Canton del Ecuador] at sozoranga.gob.ec</ref>
