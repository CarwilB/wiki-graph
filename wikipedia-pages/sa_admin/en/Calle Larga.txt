'''Calle Larga''' ("long road" in Spanish) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and one of four [[Communes of Chile|commune]]s in the [[Los Andes Province, Chile|Los Andes Province]] of central [[Chile]]'s [[Valparaíso Region]].

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Calle Larga is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]], administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]], every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Nelson Esteban Venegas Salazar, and the council has the following councilors:<ref name="Official"/>
* Ruth Sabina Guerra Guzmán ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* Víctor Manuel Jara Lopez ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Manuel Amar Amar ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Luis Alberto Gormas Pfeng ([[Independent Democrat Union|UDI]])
* José Francisco Leiva Muñoz ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Carls Patricio Izquierdo Llanos (PDC)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Calle Larga is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] as a part of the 11th electoral district (together with [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]], [[San Esteban, Chile|San Esteban]], [[San Ignacio, Chile|San Ignacio]], [[Rinconada]], [[San Felipe, Chile|San Felipe]], [[Putaendo]], [[Santa María, Chile|Santa María]], [[Panquehue]], [[Llaillay]], [[Catemu]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).

==Geography==
Calle Larga spans an area of {{convert|321.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> The commune is bordered to the west by the commune of [[Rinconada]], to the north and east by the commune of [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]] and to the south by the [[Colina, Chile|Colina]] commune of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Calle Larga commune had 10,393 inhabitants; of these, 5,447 (52.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,946 (47.6%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 5,351 men and 5,042 women.<ref name="INE"/>
