'''Megantoni District''' is one of fourteen districts of [[La Convención Province]] in the [[Cusco Region]] of [[Peru]]. Megantoni occupies the northernmost part of La Convención. The district was created in 2016 from land previously belonging to [[Echarate District]]. Camisea is the capital of Megantoni and has a population of about 500 people. As of 2017, the district was not connected to the rest of Peru by road. The [[Urubamba River]] bisects the district and is a major avenue of transportation.<ref>Dourojeanni, Marc (2017), "Megatoni (Cusco): Desafios y oportunidades del distrito mas joven y con mas canon del pais," ''SPDA: Actualidad Ambiental'', [https://www.academia.edu/33306413/Megantoni_Cusco_Desaf%C3%ADos_y_oportunidades_del_distrito_m%C3%A1s_joven_y_con_el_mayor_canon_del_pa%C3%ADs], accessed 19 Jul 2019</ref>

==Protected and indigenous lands== 
Ninety-four percent of the land of Megantoni District is protected for conservation or allocated to [[indigenous people]]. The majority of this land is "almost untouched" [[tropical rainforest]] and [[Peruvian Yungas]] characteristic of the [[Amazon Basin]] and the eastern flanks of the [[Andes]].<ref>[https://www.academia.edu/33306413/Megantoni_Cusco_Desaf%C3%ADos_y_oportunidades_del_distrito_m%C3%A1s_joven_y_con_el_mayor_canon_del_pa%C3%ADs Dourojeanni]</ref> Protected lands include the [[Pongo de Mainique]], a scenic [[water gap]] where the Urubamba River cuts through the [[Vilcabamba mountain range]] on the southern border of Megantoni. East of the Pongo, the [[Megantoni National Sanctuary]] straddles the border with Echarate. The Kugapakari-Nahua-Nanti Indigenous Territorial Reserve covers most of the eastern one-half of the district. The [[Otishi National Park]] and the [[Machiguenga Communal Reserve]] straddle the western border of the district. Both the reserves were created to protect the lands and the "voluntary isolation" of some groups of indigenous people.<ref>MacDonald, Theodore (2014), "Testing Free, Prior, and Informed Consent," ''Harvard Review of Latin America,'' [https://revista.drclas.harvard.edu/book/%E2%80%9Cuncontacted%E2%80%9D-peruvians], accessed 18 July 2019</ref>

==Geography and climate==
Most of Megantoni District consists of hills and lowland plains, but the [[Vilcabamba mountain range]] on the western border of the district rises to a maximum elevation of {{convert|3385|m|ft}}. Mountains along the southern boundary reach a maximum elevation of {{convert|1606|m|ft}}.<ref>Topographical map, [https://mapper.acme.com/?ll=-11.45,-73.034&z=12&t=T&marker0=-11.45,-73.034,Megantoni%20District%2C%20La%20Convenci%C3%B3n%20Province], accessed 19 Jul 2019</ref> 
Elevations from {{convert|1000|to|3500|m|ft}} are part of the ecoregion of "high jungle" or [[Peruvian Yungas]].<ref>"World Wildlife Fund," [https://www.worldwildlife.org/ecoregions/nt0153], accessed 29 Jul 2019</ref> Below elevations of {{convert|1000|m|ft}} the vegetation is typical of the [[Southwest Amazon moist forests]] ecoregion.<ref>"World Wildlife Fund," [http://www.eoearth.org/article/Southwest_Amazon_moist_forests], accessed 19 Jul 2019</ref> The Pongo de Mainique on the southern border of Megantoni is a global [[biodiversity hotspot]]. According to the [[Wildlife Conservation Society]], "the lowland rainforests and mid-montane [[cloud forest]]s within a radius of {{convert|5|mi|km|spell=in}} of the Pongo possibly comprise the single most biologically-diverse site on the face of the Earth."

Camisea, the capital of Megantoni, has an Am (tropical monsoon) climate according to the [[Köppen climate classification]] system. Although there is little temperature variation among months, the austral summer receives much more precipitation than the austral winter. Under the [[Trewartha climate classification]] system, the climate is Araa: tropical rain forest with high temperatures year around.

==Camisea Gas Field==
[[File:MINISTRO VALAKIVI VISITÓ EL EJE ENERGÉTICO DE LA CONVENCIÓN EN EL CUSCO (VRAEM) (20368067219).jpg|thumb|250px|left|The Malvinas Processing Plant on the Urubamba River.]]
Megantoni is the location of most of the [[Camisea Gas Field]], a controversial four billion dollar project exploiting [[natural gas]] resources. The Malvinas Gas Processing Plant, {{convert|13|km|mile}} south of Camisea, produces natural gas and sends it via a pipeline which crosses the Andes and continues westward to the [[Pacific Ocean]] coast. The gas is consumed domestically and exported from the port of [[Pisco, Peru|Pisco]].<ref>"10 Years of Camisea: The natural gas revolution in Peru," (2015) ''Oil and Gas Year,'' [https://www.theoilandgasyear.com/articles/10-years-of-camisea-the-natural-gas-revolution-in-peru/], accessed 19 Jul 2019</ref>

Exploration for natural gas has extended into the protected and indigenous lands of Megantoni posing a risk to the survival and livelihoods of the indigenous people and the tropical forests. "Inevitably," in the opinion of one scholar, a road will be built to connect Megantoni to the rest of Peru and stimulate an influx of farmers, miners, and drug traffickers who will encroach on the forests and indigenous people of the district.<ref>[https://www.academia.edu/33306413/Megantoni_Cusco_Desaf%C3%ADos_y_oportunidades_del_distrito_m%C3%A1s_joven_y_con_el_mayor_canon_del_pa%C3%ADs Dourojeanni]</ref>
