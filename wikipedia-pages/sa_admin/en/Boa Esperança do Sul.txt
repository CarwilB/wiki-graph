'''Boa Esperança do Sul''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].  The population in 2020 was 15,018 and the area is {{Convert|691.017|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="area">[http://www.seade.gov.br/produtos/perfil/perfilMunEstado.php Área territorial oficial] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110823080724/http://www.seade.gov.br/produtos/perfil/perfilMunEstado.php |date=August 23, 2011 }}</ref>  The elevation is {{Convert|490|m|ft}}.

==History==
Boa Esperança do Sul ({{langx|en|Good Hope of the South}}) was known as '''São Sebastião de Boa Esperança''' ({{langx|en|St. Sebastian of Good Hope}}) since the late 19th-century. In the late 1800s, the area now known as Boa Esperança do Sul was a vast expanse of farmland and forests. It was sparsely populated, with only a few scattered settlements. The region's fertile soil and abundant natural resources attracted pioneers from various parts of Brazil, many of whom were seeking to escape the poverty and hardship of their homelands.In 1880, a small settlement known as Capela de São Sebastião de Boa Esperança was established. The settlement grew rapidly as more people arrived, drawn by the promise of a prosperous future. In July 1895, it became a district, with the name of '''Boa Esperança'''.  In 1944, it was renamed Boa Esperança do Sul.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Rodrigues dos Santos |first=Cássia Betania |date=2021-01-06 |title=EVIDÊNCIAS DE UM CRIME: O FECHAMENTO DA ESCOLA MUNICIPAL BOA ESPERANÇA / Evidence of a Crime: The Closing of the Municipal School Boa Esperança / Evidencia de un delito: El cierre de la Escuela Municipal Boa Esperança |journal=Revista Nera |issue=56 |pages=93–112 |doi=10.47946/rnera.v0i56.7131 |issn=1806-6755|doi-access=free }}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Ricaurte-Quijano |first1=Paola |last2=Carli-Álvarez |first2=Arianna |date=2016-10-01 |title=The Wiki Learning Project: Wikipedia as an Open Learning Environment |journal=Comunicar |volume=24 |issue=49 |pages=61–69 |doi=10.3916/c49-2016-06 |issn=1134-3478|doi-access=free |hdl=10272/12721 |hdl-access=free }}</ref>

==Geography==
Boa Esperança do Sul is located in the  southern-[[subtropic]]al part of Brazil, at 21 degrees, 59 minutes, 33 second south, and 48 degrees, 23 minutes, 27 seconds west, at an altitude of {{Convert|490|m|ft}}, in the internal part of the [[State of São Paulo]].  It covers an area of {{Convert|691.017|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="area" />
