'''La Unión District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Piura Province|Piura]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the law Ley N° 5951 of December 28, 1927 during the government of President [[Augusto Leguía]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Ley N° 5951|url=http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/05951.pdf|accessdate=June 4, 2019|language=es}}</ref>
