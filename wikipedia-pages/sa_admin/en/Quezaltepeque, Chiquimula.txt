'''Quezaltepeque''' ({{IPA|es|kesalteˈpeke}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]], with a population of 28,075 (2018 census)<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/ Citypopulation.de] Population of departments and municipalities in Guatemala</ref> and an area of 239 km<sup>2</sup>, in the [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The local economy is based on agriculture. Agricultural products include coffee, maize and beans.  As of 1993, there was a working telegraph terminal there in the post office.

==Etymology==
''Quetzaltepēc'' is [[Nahuatl]] for "At the [[quetzal]]-feather hill".{{citation needed|date=September 2013}} The [[British people|British]] spelled it '''Quesaltpeque,''' in the 19th century.<ref name=Baily>{{cite book|last=Baily|first=John|title=Central America; Describing Each of the States of Guatemala, Honduras, Salvador, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica|year=1850|publisher=Trelawney Saunders|location=London|page=88|url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/7306/view/1/88/}}</ref>

==Population==

As of 1850, the municipality had an estimated population of 4,000.<ref name=Baily/>
