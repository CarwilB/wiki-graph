'''Francisco León''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]]. It covers an area of 114.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. In 1982 parts of the municipality were buried in the eruption of [[El Chichón Volcano]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 7,000,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Francisco León|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=033|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 5,236 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Francisco León|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07033a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / [[Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas]]|access-date=July 25, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617053203/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07033a.htm|archive-date=June 17, 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The municipality had 50 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) was: [[San Miguel la Sardina, Chiapas|San Miguel la Sardina]] (1,106), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
