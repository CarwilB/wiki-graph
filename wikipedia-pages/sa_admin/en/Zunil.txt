'''Zunil''' ({{IPA|es|suˈnil}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]] with a surface area of {{convert|80|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="inforpessca">[https://web.archive.org/web/20070606233912/http://www.inforpressca.com/zunil/ Zunil at Inforpressca] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070606233912/http://www.inforpressca.com/zunil |date=June 6, 2007 }}</ref> The town of Zunil is located {{convert|9|km|mi}} from the city of [[Quetzaltenango, Quetzaltenango|Quetzaltenango]], on the bank of the [[Salamá River]].<ref name="Cat36">Cattelan 2004, p.36.</ref> Zunil has an altitude of approximately {{convert|2075|m|ft}} [[above mean sea level]].<ref name="Cat36"/> The population of the municipality, which is 100% indigenous, was 14,118 at the 2018 census. The inhabitants speak [[Kʼicheʼ language|Kʼicheʼ]] and [[Spanish language|Spanish]].<ref name="inforpessca"/>
There are thermal baths with volcanic water around the town, for example [[Fuentes Georginas]] and [[Almolonga, Quetzaltenango|Almolonga]].

==Religion==
Zunil possesses active worship of '''''San Simón''''' (also known as [[Maximón]]), a life-sized mannequin representing a [[Maya peoples|Maya]] god, sitting in a wooden throne, which is moved to a different house every year, and many people visit and leave gifts at his shrine.

==Namesakes==
[[Zunil (crater)|A crater]] on [[Mars]], which may be the source of [[Mars meteorite]]s, is named after the village.<ref>{{cite journal|author=McEwan, A.S.|url=http://www.mars.asu.edu/christensen/classdocs/mcewen_zunil_Icarus_2005.pdf|title=The rayed crater Zunil and interpretations of small impact craters on Mars|journal=[[Icarus (journal)|Icarus]]|volume=176|pages=351–381|year=2005|access-date=2006-09-08|doi=10.1016/j.icarus.2005.02.009|bibcode=2005Icar..176..351M|issue=2|display-authors=etal}}.</ref>
