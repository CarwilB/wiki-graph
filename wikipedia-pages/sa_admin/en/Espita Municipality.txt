'''Espita Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/divismpal.cfm?c=1206&e=31&CFID=1993439&CFTOKEN=83113576 |title=Aspectos geográficos de Yucatán. División Municipal |access-date=August 17, 2010 |author=[[INEGI|Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722225830/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/divismpal.cfm?c=1206&e=31&CFID=1993439&CFTOKEN=83113576 |archive-date=July 22, 2011 }}</ref> Its municipal seat is located in the [[Espita|City of Espita]] and is located on the ''Litoral Oriente'' (East Coast) or Region 1 of the state.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Regionalización de Yucatán |author=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/yucatan/regi.htm |publisher=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |access-date=May 10, 2010 |language=es |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110616191413/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/yucatan/regi.htm |archive-date=June 16, 2011 }}</ref> In 2005, the municipality had the 77th highest [[Human Development Index|HDI]] in the state with 0.7320,<ref>{{Cite web |author=[[United Nations Development Programme]] |url=http://www.undp.org.mx/IMG/pdf/Yucatan.pdf |title=Indicadores de Desarrollo Humano y Género en México 2000-2005 |access-date=August 12, 2010 |language=es |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722230804/http://www.undp.org.mx/IMG/pdf/Yucatan.pdf |archive-date=July 22, 2011 }}</ref> the same as [[Honduras]] in 2007.<ref>{{Cite web|author=United Nations Development Programme |url=http://hdr.undp.org/en/media/HDR_2009_EN_Complete.pdf |title=2009 Human Development Report |access-date=August 12, 2010 |page=182}}</ref>

==Geography==
This municipality is located in the eastern-part of the state.  It is located between latitudes 20° 53' and 21° 08' north and longitudes 88° 16' and 88° 27' west.<ref name="municipios"/>

It borders the following municipalities: to the north Sucilá to the south Tinum and Uayma, on the east Temozón and Calotmul and the west Dzitas - Cenotillo.<ref name="municipios"/>

=== Communities ===
The Espita municipality has 17 communities and the municipal seat.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.yucatan.gob.mx/estado/municipios/ver_municipio.jsp?id=32 |title=Información del municipio |author=www.yucatan.gob.mx |access-date=August 18, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101203132103/http://yucatan.gob.mx/estado/municipios/ver_municipio.jsp?id=32 |archive-date=December 3, 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2005, only 7 of its 17 communities had more than 200 inhabitants. Together these 7 communities had a population of 3,605, which together with the municipal seat (10,758), was 14,363, i.e. the 99.52% of the total municipal population.
