'''Beaumont''' ({{IPA|fr|bomɔ̃|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Manestra-Beaumont.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Bomon}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Corail Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 31,580 inhabitants in 2015.

Locations in Beaumont commune include: [[Beaumont City|Beaumont]], [[Flandre, Beaumont, Haiti|Flandre]] and Lacadome.
