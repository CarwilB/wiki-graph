'''San Miguel el Alto''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 787&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 26,971.<ref name="pop"/> 

The municipality includes the town of [[San José de los Reynoso]].

==In popular culture==
It was the setting and filming location for the 1957 film, ''[[Los chiflados del rock and roll]]'', starring [[Luis Aguilar (actor)|Luis Aguilar]].

==Fiestas==
San Miguel is known for its beautiful [[patronal festival]] that lasts from 19 September to 29 September. The celebration is in honor of Saint Michael the Arch Angel. Many events like horse races, artist performance, fireworks, and the coronation of the fiesta queen take place.{{cn|date=April 2023}}
