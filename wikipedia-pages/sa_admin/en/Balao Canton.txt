'''Balao Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Guayas Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Balao]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 20,523.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Censos |first=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y |title=Población y Demografía |url=https://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/censo-de-poblacion-y-vivienda/ |access-date=2022-11-27 |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |language=es}}</ref>
