'''Luís Correia''' is one of the four coastal cities of [[Piauí]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|313800|ha|adj=on}} [[Delta do Parnaíba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Delta do Parnaíba (Federal) – ISA}}
 |title=APA Delta do Parnaíba (Federal)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/585933|access-date=2017-02-09}}</ref>
The municipality also contains part of the {{convert|1592550|ha}} [[Serra da Ibiapaba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Serra da Ibiapaba – ISA}}
 |title=APA Serra da Ibiapaba|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/594860|access-date=2017-02-10}}</ref>
