'''La Pintada''' ({{IPA|es|la pinˈtaða}}); is a town{{Citation needed|date=March 2026}} and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Southwestern Antioquia|southwest region]] of the [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia Department of Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Semana |date=2024-11-24 |title=Nace museo de la historia de las víctimas en La Pintada, Antioquia |url=https://www.semana.com/confidenciales/articulo/nace-museo-de-la-historia-de-las-victimas-de-la-guerra-en-la-pintada-antioquia/202441/ |access-date=2026-03-25 |website=Semana - Últimas Noticias de Colombia y el Mundo |language=spanish}}</ref> It is located 79 miles from [[Medellín]] at an altitude of 600 m (2,000 ft) above sea level. It borders the [[Fredonia, Antioquia|Fredonia]] and [[Santa Bárbara, Antioquia|Santa Bárbara]] municipalities to the north. The [[Cauca River]] flows through the municipality.

==Places of interest==
* Slopes of the Poblanco and Arma rivers
* Yellow hill
* Alejandro López railway station
* El Salto del Caballo
* Acapulco beach
