'''La Chapelle''' ({{IPA|fr|la ʃapɛl|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Anonymât (Kvardek du)-La Chapelle.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Lachapèl}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Saint-Marc Arrondissement]], in the [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
