'''Gorbea''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in [[Cautín Province]], [[Araucanía Region]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Gorbea spans an area of {{convert|694.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 15,222 inhabitants (7,609 men and 7,613 women). Of these, 9,413 (61.8%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 5,809 (38.2%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 3.9% (570 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

The commune's largest settlements are Gorbea, [[Lastarria, Chile|Lastarria]] and [[Quitratué]].<ref name="INE2017">{{cite web |url=https://geoarchivos.ine.cl/File/pub/Cd_Pb_Al_Cs_2019.pdf |title=Ciudades, pueblos, aldeas y caceríos 2019 |publisher= National Statistics Institute|date=2019 |access-date=November 1, 2020|language=es}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Gorbea is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2016-2020 alcalde is Guido Siegmund González ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Gorbea is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Manuel García ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Fernando Meza ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) as part of the 52nd electoral district, together with [[Cunco, Chile|Cunco]], [[Pucón]], [[Curarrehue]], [[Villarrica, Chile|Villarrica]], [[Loncoche]] and [[Toltén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by José Garcia Ruminot (RN) and Eugenio Tuma Zedan ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 15th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-South).
