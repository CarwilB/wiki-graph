'''Moreno Department''' is a department of [[Santiago del Estero Province]], [[Argentina]]. The capital lies at [[Quimilí]].

The department covers an area of 16,127&nbsp;km².<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/86000C22.xls |title=Provincia segun departamento |publisher=Indec |date=29 July 2010 |access-date=2010-07-29 }}{{Dead link|date=September 2024 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The population as of 2001 was 28,053.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/86000LC121.xls |title=Archived from source |publisher=Indec |date=28 September 2007 |access-date=2010-07-29 |archive-date=28 September 2007 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928010644/http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/86000LC121.xls |url-status=dead }}</ref> The department contains the following municipalities:
