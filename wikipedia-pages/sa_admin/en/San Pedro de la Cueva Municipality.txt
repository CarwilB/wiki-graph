'''San Pedro de la Cueva''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=4 January 2010}}</ref>

Its seat is [[San Pedro de la Cueva]].

The municipal area is 1,926.36 km<sup>2</sup>. The population in 2020 was 1,458.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Pedro de la Cueva: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/san-pedro-de-la-cueva |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities are [[Moctezuma Municipality|Moctezuma]] and [[Tepache Municipality|Tepache]] to the north, [[Sahuaripa Municipality|Sahuaripa]] to the east, [[Bacanora Municipality|Bacanora]] to the south, and  [[Villa Pesqueira]] to the southwest.
