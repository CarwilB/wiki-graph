'''Chachapoyas''' is a province of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]]. The province of Chachapoyas was a part of the department of Trujillo (according to the [[Decree|supreme decree]] of February 12, 1821) being its capital the [[Chachapoyas, Peru|city of Chachapoyas]].

After the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|department of Amazonas]] was created, by law of November 21, 1832, it became a province of the Amazonas region, and the [[Chachapoyas, Peru|city of Chachapoyas]] remained a regional capital. Its principal quarters are:

*To the north: [[Luya Urco, Peru|Luya Urco]] and [[Santo Domingo, Peru|Santo Domingo]] 
*To the south: [[Yanco, Peru|Yanco]] and [[La Laguna, Peru|La Laguna]].

[[Image:Iglesia-chiliquin_chachapoyas_amazonas_peru.jpg|thumb|220px|left|The church of Chiliquín, Amazonas.]]

A big part of the province is constituted by soils of [[Puna (ecoregion)|puna]], located between {{convert|4000|and|5000|m}} in the oriental districts of [[Chiliquín, Peru|Chiliquín]], [[Quinjalca, Peru|Quinjalca]] and [[Granada District|Granada]].

Two principal rivers cover its territory: the [[Utcubamba River|Utcubamba]], which runs from south to north and which right margin is dedicated to the agriculture in diverse form; and the [[Sonche river|Sonche]], which runs from east to west and it is born from the meeting of several [[Stream|creek]]s that go down the heights of [[Molino Pampa, Peru|Molino Pampa district]]. This river flows into the [[Utcubamba River|Utcubamba]] along with the [[Huancachaca river|Huancachaca]]. It has a short trip, its beaches are narrow and its areas are unusable because its slopes are rocky and steep.

In [[Shihual (Peru)|Shihual]], a [[Magdalena (Peru)|Magdalena]]'s district, there are many [[calcareous]] caverns,<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Estrada |first=Arturo Ruiz |title=Las cavernas y el poblamiento prehispánico de la provincia de Chachapoyas1 |url=https://www.researchgate.net/figure/La-caverna-de-Shihual-presenta-algunos-tramos-con-pasos-estrechos-que-dan-acceso-a_fig2_332196213 |journal=Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos}}</ref> which [[stalactites]] and [[stalagmite]]s formations serve as habitat to diverse night birds.

[[La Jalca (Peru)|La Jalca]] has the privilege of being the first Spanish city that has been founded in the region: in effect, captain [[Alonso de Alvarado]], conqueror of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas]], on September 5, 1538 founded there the first city of [[Chachapoyas, Peru|San Juan de la Frontera de los Chachapoyas]], which then would be moved to the place that it occupies now.

[[Image:Fiestaspatronales_magdalena_chachapoyas_amazonas_peru2.jpg|thumb|220px|right|The town of Magdalena celebrating its festivities.]]

For Peter Lerche, an anthropologist, in [[La Jalca (Peru)|la Jalca]] there is the legitimate [[Peruvian Ancient Cultures|Andean pre-Hispanic indigenous Peruvian culture]], with traditional towns like [[Conila (Peru)|Conila]], [[Levanto District|Levanto]], [[Colcamar (Peru)|Colcamar]], [[Luya Province|Luya]]. Fruit of his investigations initiated in 1980, he has published "Chachapoyas, travelers' guide". In this book he proposes some tourist circuits. The first one of them, the shortest, can take in average 5 days, and the others, for more adventurous spirits, can take in average 15 days.

Historically, the Chachapoya were in conflict with the Inca and also the Spanish colonisers.<ref>{{Cite book |last1=Alconini |first1=Sonia |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=vnhUDwAAQBAJ&dq=chachapoyas+is+a+province+of+the+Amazonas&pg=PA436 |title=The Oxford Handbook of the Incas |last2=Covey |first2=R. Alan |date=2018-04-02 |publisher=Oxford University Press |isbn=978-0-19-090803-4 |language=en}}</ref>
