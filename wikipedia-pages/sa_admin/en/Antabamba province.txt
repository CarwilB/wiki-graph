'''Antabamba''' is one of the seven provinces of the [[Apurímac Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Antabamba]].

==Boundaries==
*North: province of [[Grau Province|Grau]]
*East: province of [[Cotabambas Province|Cotabambas]] and [[Cusco Region]]
*South: [[Arequipa Region]]
*West: province of [[Aymaraes Province|Aymaraes]]

== Geography ==
The province of Antabamba covers an area of {{convert|1242.33|km2}}. The [[Wansu mountain range|Wansu]] mountain range traverses the province. == Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (77.12%) learnt to speak in childhood, 22.26% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language and  0.28% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]]  ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
