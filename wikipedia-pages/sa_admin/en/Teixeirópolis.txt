'''Teixeirópolis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 4,233 (2020) and its area is 460&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> which makes it the smallest municipality in that state.

== Geography ==
It is located at latitude 10º55'03" south and longitude 62º14'58" west , at an altitude of 260 meters <ref>{{Cite web |title=PREFEITURA MUNICIPAL DE TEIXEIRÓPOLIS |url=https://www.teixeiropolis.ro.gov.br/ |access-date=2025-12-10 |website=PREFEITURA MUNICIPAL DE TEIXEIRÓPOLIS |language=pt-br}}</ref>.
