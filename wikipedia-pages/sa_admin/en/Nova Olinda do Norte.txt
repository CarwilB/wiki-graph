'''Nova Olinda do Norte''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 38,026 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/nova-olinda-do-norte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 5,609&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> The town is located on the banks of the lower [[Madeira River]].

The municipality contains a small part of the {{convert|827877|ha|adj=on}} [[Pau-Rosa National Forest]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Pau-Rosa – ISA}}|language=pt
