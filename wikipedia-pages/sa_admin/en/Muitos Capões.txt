}}'''Muitos Capões''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It was raised to municipality status in 1992, the area being taken out of the municipalities of [[Vacaria]], [[Lagoa Vermelha]] and [[Esmeralda, Rio Grande do Sul|Esmeralda]].<ref name="lei10651">{{Cite web|url=http://leisestaduais.com.br/rs/lei-ordinaria-n-10651-1995-rio-grande-do-sul-cria-o-municipio-de-muitos-capoes|title=Lei Nº 10651: Cria o Município de Muitos Capões|publisher=Leis Estaduais|lang=pt|date=28 December 1995}}</ref>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,173.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/muitos-capoes/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It contains the [[Aracuri-Esmeralda Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=50
