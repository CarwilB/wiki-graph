'''San Miguel Guancapla''', also known as '''San Miguelito''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsam miɣeˈlito}}), is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

== History ==
In the population count of 1791 there was a town with the name of Guancapla in the parish of Intibucá. It is thought that Guancapla was founded in [[1589]]. The town was declared a municipality in 1870. When the department of Intibucá was created in 1883, Guancapla was one of the municipalities that formed the District of La Esperanza, with the name of San Miguel Guancapla. Recently for some time it was known as San Miguelito.<ref>Fiallos, Carmen. 1989. "The Municipalities of Honduras".</ref>

While for a time the locality was called ''San Miguelito'', Decree 166-84, promulgated by the Government of the Republic under the administration of [[Roberto Suazo Cordova]] on October 2, 1984, officially designated the name of the municipality as "San Miguel Guancapla".   Guancapla means "En los Guacales", which refers to a tree that produces a round fruit of woody pericarp, often used for containers.

== Geography ==
Located in a flat area, the municipality borders on the north with the municipalities of [[San Juan, Intibucá|San Juan]] and [[Yamaranguila]], to the south with the municipality of [[Dolores, Intibucá|Dolores]], to the east with the municipality of Yamaranguila and to the west with the municipality of [[Erandique]].{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Miguel Guancapla municipality had a population of 7,368. Of these, 73.54% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (73.22% [[Lenca]]), 16.72% [[Mestizo]], 5.02% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 4.06% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.66% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>

== Villages ==

The municipality has the following five villages (or ''aldeas''):<ref>Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas, Honduras. (Censo 2001)</ref>
* San Miguelito or San Miguel Guancapla (capital of the municipality)
* Chupucay or Resina
* San Antonio
* Segua
* Cofradía
