'''Santa Ana''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán, Honduras|Francisco Morazán]].

The location of the crash of [[Central American Airways Flight 731]] was El Espino in Santa Ana.<ref>"[http://www.dgachn.org/Documentos/Investigacion%20de%20%20Accidentes/Informe%20final%20correcto%20HR-AUQ.pdf DGAC-ACCID-01-2011, CENTRAL AMERICAN AIRWAYS, MATRICULA HR-AUQ]" ([https://web.archive.org/web/20131230233045/http://www.dgachn.org/Documentos/Investigacion%20de%20%20Accidentes/Informe%20final%20correcto%20HR-AUQ.pdf Archive]) [[Dirección General de Aeronáutica Civil (Honduras)|Dirección General de Aeronáutica Civil]]. p. 2/21 (INTRODUCCION{{sic}}). "El accidente ocurrió en El Espino, Jurisdicción de Santa Ana, Francisco Morazán."</ref>
