'''Ferros''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the microregion of [[Itabira]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 9,696.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/ferros/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

From 2001 to 2023, São Pedro dos Ferros lost 4.28 [[hectare|kha]] of [[tree cover]], equivalent to a 30% decrease in tree cover since 2000, and 2.28 [[Tonne|Mt]] of CO₂e emissions.<ref name="GFW">{{cite web |url=https://www.globalforestwatch.org/dashboards/country/BRA/13/755/?location=WyJjb3VudHJ5IiwiQlJBIiwiMTMiLCI3NTUiXQ%3D%3D |title=Summary - São Pedro dos Ferros, Minas Gerais, Brazil. |website=Global Forest Watch |access-date=September 30, 2024}}</ref>

[[Roberto Drummond]] was born in Ferros.
