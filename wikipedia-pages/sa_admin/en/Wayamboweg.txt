'''Wayamboweg''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Saramacca District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 1,560. The largest ethnic group is [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]].<ref name="census"/> The resort is mainly agricultural. <ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf| title=Structuur analyse|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> A rice project has started in late 2017.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2017/09/29/november-2017-start-experiment-met-zaaizaad-in-saramacca/|title=November 2017 start experiment met zaaizaad in Saramacca|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

In the early 21st century, a high school has been opened. The resort is located near the ocean, and tourism facilities are being developed in the resort.<ref name="planning"/> [[Staatsolie]] is drilling for oil in the Wayambozwamp.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2017/11/07/boerencollectief-noord-saramacca-zal-staatsolie-voor-gerecht-slepen/|title=Boerencollectief Noord Saramacca zal Staatsolie voor gerecht slepen|website=Dagblad Suriname|date=7 November 2017 |access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The village of [[Smithfield, Suriname|Smithfield]] is located in the area.
