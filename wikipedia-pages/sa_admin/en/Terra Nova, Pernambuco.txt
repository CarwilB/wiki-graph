'''Terra Nova''' ({{Literal translation|new land}}) is a municipality in the Brazilian state of [[Pernambuco]]. 578.5&nbsp;km from the state's capital, [[Recife]], it is located in the [[Semi-arid climate|semi-arid]] [[Sertão]]. The estimated population in 2025, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 9,231 inhabitants and the total area is 318.709&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" /> The municipality was created in 1958 by state law.<ref>{{Cite web|title=IBGE Cities|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/terra-nova/historico|access-date=2021-04-30|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

Its current [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] ({{Langx|pt-BR|prefeita}}) is Aline Freire of [[Avante (political party)|Avante]], elected in [[2020 Brazilian municipal elections|2020]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Prefeita e vereadores de Terra Nova tomam posse; veja lista de eleitos|url=https://g1.globo.com/pe/petrolina-regiao/noticia/2021/01/01/prefeita-e-vereadores-de-terra-nova-tomam-posse-veja-lista-de-eleitos.ghtml|access-date=2021-04-30|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in are [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[chicken]]s and plantations of [[onion]]s, [[tomato]]es and [[rice]].
