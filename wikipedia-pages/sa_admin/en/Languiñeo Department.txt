'''Languiñeo Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 3,017 inhabitants in an area of 15,339&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Tecka]], which is located around 1,899&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
