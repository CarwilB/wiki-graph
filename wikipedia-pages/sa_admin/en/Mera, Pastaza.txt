'''Mera''' is a town in the eastern foothills of the [[Ecuador]]ian [[Andes]]. It is also the name of the [[Cantons of Ecuador|Canton]] in [[Pastaza Province]] of which it is a part.

It lends its name to [[Shell Mera]], a larger town 4 miles to the east.
