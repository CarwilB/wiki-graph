'''Lucerna''' ({{IPA|es|luˈseɾna}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Ocotepeque department|Ocotepeque]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Lucerna municipality had a population of 5,861. Of these, 99.76% were [[Mestizo]], 0.14% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.05% [[White Latin Americans|White]] and 0.05% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
