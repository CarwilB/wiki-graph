'''Tupanatinga''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[tupɐnɐˈtĩɡɐ]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 306&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 27,551 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/tupanatinga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Sertânia]]   ('''N''');  [[Itaíba]]    ('''S''');  [[Buique]]   ('''E''');   [[Ibimirim]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 795.64&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 710 m
* Hydrography - [[Moxotó River|Moxotó]] and [[Ipanema River|Ipanema rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 22.3 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 306&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Tupanatinga''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially  [[beans]], [[cashew nuts]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[goats]], [[sheep]], [[pigs]], [[horses]] and [[poultry]].
