'''Ángel Albino Corzo''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], in southern [[Mexico]]. It covers an area of 1748.81&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its municipal seat is the town of [[Jaltenango de la Paz]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 26,628,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Angel Albino Corzo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=008|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 21,848 as of 2005.<ref>
{{cite web|title=Ángel Albino Corzo|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07008a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=July 25, 2008}}</ref>

The municipality had 199 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: Jaltenango de la Paz (10,427), [[Nueva Palestina, Chiapas|Nueva Palestina]] (3,475), classified as urban, and [[Querétaro, Chiapas|Querétaro]] (2,203), [[Francisco I. Madero, Chiapas|Francisco I. Madero]] (1,819), [[Nueva Colombia, Chiapas|Nueva Colombia]] (1,568), and [[Nueva Independencia, Chiapas|Nueva Independencia]] (1,283), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The municipality was created on 24 February 1933 during the administration of [[Governor of Chiapas|Governor]] [[Victórico R. Grajales]]. It was named for 19th-century Chiapas politician [[Ángel Corzo|Ángel Albino Corzo]] (after whom [[Chiapa de Corzo, Chiapas|Chiapa de Corzo]] was also named).
