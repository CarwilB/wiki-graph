'''Sánchez Carrión''', known until 1976 as '''Huamachuco''', is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]]. It is named in honour of [[José Faustino Sánchez Carrión]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Huamachuco]]. About 30 miles away is [[Marcahuamachuco]],<ref>Topic, Theresa Lange (1991). "The Meaning of Monuments at Marcahuamachuco," 55th Annual Meeting of the Society for American Archaeology. New Orleans: Abril</ref> a prehistoric political and religious centre of a culture that throve AD 350-1100.
