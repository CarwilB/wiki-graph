'''General Lagos''' ([[Indigenous language|indigenous]] name: '''Takura''')<ref name=senadis>{{cite web |url=http://www.senadis.gob.cl/region/arica_y_parinacota/d/noticias/5638/arica-y-parinacota-directora-regional-de-senadis-se-reune-con-alcalde-de-general-lagos|title= Región de Arica y Parinacota, Arica y Parinacota: Directora Regional de Senadis se reúne con alcalde de General Lagos|last= |first= |date= |website= |publisher=SENADIS, Ministerio de Desarrollo Social|access-date=July 21, 2017|quote=}}</ref> is [[Chile]]'s northernmost [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Parinacota Province]], [[Arica and Parinacota Region]]. Its [[capital (political)|capital]] is [[Visviri]].

The area, once part of [[Peru]], was captured by Chilean forces in the [[War of the Pacific]] by its namesake and commander of Chilean troops in the [[Battle of Arica]], General [[Pedro Lagos]].

[[Tacora]] volcano lies in this commune.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], General Lagos had 1,179 inhabitants (761 men and 418 women), all listed as living in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 16.5% (167 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, General Lagos is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde was Gregorio Mendoza Chura.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], General Lagos is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Nino Baltolu ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Orlando Vargas ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 1st electoral district, which includes the entire [[Arica and Parinacota Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Fulvio Rossi Ciocca ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Jaime Orpis Bouchon ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 1st senatorial constituency ([[Arica and Parinacota Region]] and [[Tarapacá Region]]).
