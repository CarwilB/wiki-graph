'''Vertientes''' is a municipality and town in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Vertientes had a population of 48,821.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=City Population}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|2005|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| access-date=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|24|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
