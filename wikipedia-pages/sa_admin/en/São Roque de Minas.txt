'''São Roque de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was 7,076 people living in a total area of 2,100&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sao-roque-de-minas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the meso-region of Oeste de Minas and to the micro-region of Piumhi.  It became a municipality in 1938.<ref>[https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of São Roque de Minas is located at an elevation of 818 meters on a plateau about 10 kilometers east of the [[Serra da Canastra National Park]] entrance.  Most of the municipality is occupied by the national park, which contains the source of the [[São Francisco River]].   The park contains 71,525 hectares  with a perimeter of 173.4&nbsp;km.<ref>[https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/1123/ UNESCO]</ref>
<ref>[http://www.serracanastra.com.br/ Serra da Canastra]</ref>

Neighboring  municipalities are: [[Medeiros, Minas Gerais|Medeiros]] (N), [[Bambuí]] (E),  [[Piumhi]] (SE), [[Vargem Bonita]] and [[Delfinópolis]] (S) [[Conquista, Minas Gerais|Conquista]] and [[Tapira, Minas Gerais|Tapira]] (W).<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/piui/ Citybrazil] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080928120745/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/piui/ |date=2008-09-28 }}</ref>

The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 334&nbsp;km.  The distance to regional center [[Piumhi]] is 58&nbsp;km.  In 2000 the connecting roads were not paved.

==Economic activities==
Services and agriculture are the most important economic activities.  A regional cheese called "queijo da canastra" is produced here.  The GDP in 2005 was approximately R$56 million, with 2 million reais from taxes, 20 million reais from services, 2 million reais from industry, and 31 million reais from agriculture.  There were 778 rural producers on 139,000 hectares of land.  160 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 2,200 persons were dependent on agriculture.  The main crops are coffee,  rice, beans, and corn.  There were 55,000 head of cattle (2006).<ref>[https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

There were no banks (2007) and 1,029 automobiles (2007), giving a ratio of 6 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 4 public health clinics and 1 hospital with 14 beds (2005).  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to [[Passos, Minas Gerais|Passos]], [[Piumhi]] or  [[Franca]].  Educational needs of 1,250 students were met by 7 primary schools, 2 middle schools, and 5 pre-primary schools.<ref>[https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.766 (2000)
*State ranking: 186 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 1426 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000 
*Literacy rate: 88%
*Life expectancy: 74 (average of males and females)<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>
*Infant mortality rate: 27.2 in 1,000 live births.<ref>[http://www.serracanastra.com.br/saoroque/saoroque.html São Roque]</ref>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of R$256.00 was below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.  [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with R$435.00.  The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with R$73.00.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>
