'''Tandil Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] (Administrative territorial entity of Buenos Aires Province, Argentina) of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 108,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4935|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Tandil]], which is around {{convert|350|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
