'''San Pedro de Urabá''' ({{IPA|es|sam ˈpeðɾo ðe wɾaˈβa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the Urabá Antioquia sub-region.

==Climate==
San Pedro de Urabá has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] (Am) with moderate to little rainfall from December to March and heavy rainfall in the remaining months.
