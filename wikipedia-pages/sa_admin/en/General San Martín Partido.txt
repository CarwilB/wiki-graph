'''General San Martín Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area, immediately to the north-west of the [[Capital federal]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 422,830 inhabitants<ref name=Cen2010>{{Cite web |url=http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |title=2010 Census provisional results |access-date=2011-03-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120901061446/http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |archive-date=2012-09-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> in an area of {{convert|56|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is also named [[San Martín, Buenos Aires|San Martín]].

==Name==
The partido (district) and its capital are named in honor of General [[José de San Martín]], who led Argentina against the [[Spanish Empire]] in the [[Argentine War of Independence]].

==Sports==
The partido is home to [[Primera División Argentina|Primera División]] [[football (soccer)|football]] team [[Chacarita Juniors]], and to [[Primera D Metropolitana|fifth Division]] [[football (soccer)|football]] team [[Club Social y Deportivo Central Ballester]].

In [[rugby union]] San Martín partido is home to [[CEC Liceo Militar]].
