'''Andes''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and town in the [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Part of the sub-region of [[Southwestern Antioquia]], it is located on the western Colombian [[Andes]] [[mountain range]].  Andes was founded on 13 March 1852 by Pedro Antonio Restrepo Escobar. Its elevation is 1,360 metres above sea level with an average temperature of 22&nbsp;°C.  The distance reference from [[Medellín]] city, the capital of [[Antioquia Department]], is 117&nbsp;km and it has a total area of 402.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.  This town is well known for being the place where [[Gonzalo Arango]] a writer, philosopher and Antioquian journalist was born.  The more significantly source of its economy is agriculture, mainly coffee cultivation.

==Climate==
Andes has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Af'') with consistent temperatures, cool nights, and abundant rainfall.
