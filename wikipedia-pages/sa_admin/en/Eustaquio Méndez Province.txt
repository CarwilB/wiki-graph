'''Eustaquio Méndez''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the north-western parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Tarija Department|Tarija]].

==Location==
''Eustaquio Méndez'' province is one of six provinces in the Tarija Department. It is located between 20° 57' and 21° 36' [[Latitude|south]] and between 64° 23' and 65° 15' [[Longitude|west]].

The province borders [[Chuquisaca Department]] in the north and west, [[José María Avilés Province]] in the south, [[Cercado Province (Tarija)|Cercado Province]] in the south-east, and [[Burnet O'Connor Province]] in the east.

The province extends over 90&nbsp;km from north to south, and 105&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Population==
The principal [[language]] of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 99.7% and 0.4% [[Guarani language|Guaraní]].

The population increased from 29,868 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 32,038 (2001 census), an increase of 7.3%. - 46.9% of the population are younger than 15 years old.

78.1% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 77.8% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

67.8% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.1% in [[mining]], 5.9% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 26.2% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

95.1% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 3.1% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).
