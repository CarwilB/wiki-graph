'''Dos de Abril''' (April 2) is a south western [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 7,500 inhabitants in an area of  1,594&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Hermoso Campo]], which is located around 1,100&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
