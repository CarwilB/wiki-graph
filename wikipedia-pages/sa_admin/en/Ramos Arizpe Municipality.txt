'''Ramos Arizpe''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]] in the metropolitan area of [[Saltillo]]. The municipality covers an area of 5306.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 122,243.<ref name="rapido"/>
