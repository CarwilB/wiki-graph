'''Jacaré dos Homens''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒɐkɐˈɾɛ dʊˈzõːmi]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 5,219 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/jacare-dos-homens/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 142&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=3 August 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=9 January 2007 }}</ref>
