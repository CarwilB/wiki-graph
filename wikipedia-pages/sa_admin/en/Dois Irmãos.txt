'''Dois Irmãos''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Its original German name was ''Baumschneis''.<ref>http://www.doisirmaos.rs.gov.br/historia.php{{dead link|date=December 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> It is situated at 52 kilometers from [[Porto Alegre]].

== Publishing ==
The [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] [[Subscription business model|subscription]] book club [[Minha Biblioteca Católica]] was created there.

==Economics==
The municipality, part of the [[Vale dos Sinos]] region, stands out in the economic development of the Brazilian footwear industry, with several industries in this sector.<ref>[https://doisirmaos.atende.net/cidadao/pagina/economia-da-regiao doisirmaos.atende.net]</ref>

Production began with the arrival of German immigrants, who, in addition to being farmers, were dedicated to crafts.
