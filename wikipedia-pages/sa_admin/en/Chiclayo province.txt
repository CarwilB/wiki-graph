'''Chiclayo''' '''Province''' is one of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Lambayeque Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Liz |title=183° Aniversario de la Provincia de Chiclayo - ULADECH CATÓLICA: Universidad Católica Los Ángeles de Chimbote |url=https://www.uladech.edu.pe/en/uladech-catolica/noticias-2020/item/3712-183-aniversario-de-la-provincia-de-chiclayo |access-date=2022-05-31 |website=www.uladech.edu.pe |language=es-es |archive-date=2022-10-01 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221001121217/https://www.uladech.edu.pe/en/uladech-catolica/noticias-2020/item/3712-183-aniversario-de-la-provincia-de-chiclayo |url-status=dead }}</ref> The province was established on April 18, 1835 by President [[Felipe Santiago Salaverry|Felipe Salaverry]], partitioning the southern region of [[Lambayeque province|Lambayeque Province]]. Following the fall and execution of Salaverry for his [[Coup d'état|coup]] during the [[Peruvian Civil War of 1835-1836]], the province's status was reverted back to Lambayeque. This lasted until a new decree issued by President [[Agustín Gamarra]] on March 22, 1839, which definitively confirmed the creation of the province. At the time, its jurisdiction also encompassed what are now the provinces of [[Pacasmayo province|Pacasmayo]] and [[Chepén province|Chepén]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Centurión |first=Freddy R. |date={{Date|2024-12-31}} |title=Apuntes de Historia y Derecho del Perú: Sesquicentenario del departamento de Lambayeque |url=https://apuntesderechoehistoria.blogspot.com/2024/12/sesquicentenario-del-departamento-de.html |url-status=live |access-date={{Date|2026-01-09}} |website=Apuntes de Historia y Derecho del Perú |language=Es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |date={{date|1839-03-22}} |orig-date= |title=Decreto 22 de marzo de 1839: Erigiendo la provincia de Chiclayo |url=https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/LeyesXIX/1839020.pdf |url-status=live |access-date={{date|2026-03-10}} |location=Lima |language=es |publication-place=Lima}}</ref>

==Population==
The province has an estimated province of 729,433 inhabitants.

==Capital==
The capital of this province is the city of [[Chiclayo]], which is the fourth largest in [[Peru]].

==Relief==
The terrain of the province is mainly flat, with smooth slopes which rise from west to east. Here can be observed slight undulations and uneven elevations formed by continuous alluvial actions, both natural and man-made. The cultive lands have been object of an intense work of leveling to facilitate the irrigation.
