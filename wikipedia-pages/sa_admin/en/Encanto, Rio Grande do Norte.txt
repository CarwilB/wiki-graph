'''Encanto''' (lit. "charm") is a municipality located in [[Rio Grande do Norte]], [[Brazil]], located within the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of ''Serra de São Miguel'' and [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of ''Oeste Potiguar''. According to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], the estimated population in 2020 was 5,668 inhabitants. The territorial area is {{cvt|126|km2}} and was created in 1963.
