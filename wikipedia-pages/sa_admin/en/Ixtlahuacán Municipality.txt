'''Ixtlahuacán''' is a [[municipality (Mexico)|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Colima]], being the least populous and most sparsely populated municipality in Colima. 
The municipal seat lies at [[Ixtlahuacán]]. The municipality covers an area of 468.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 2,484.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ixtlahuacán|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06006a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=November 9, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210811/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06006a.htm|archive-date=September 27, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Its residents are called Ixtlahuaqueños. It is one of the most remote ''municipios'' of the state.  It is located between [[Tecoman]] and the city of [[Colima (city)|Colima]]<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://129.113.50.6/libserv/speccoll/gentilic.html |title=Gentilicios Mexicanos<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=2008-04-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080915071711/http://129.113.50.6/libserv/speccoll/gentilic.html |archive-date=2008-09-15 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>Enciclopedia de México en CD ROM, Dataconsult S.A. de C.V., 1999, Disk 1</ref>
