'''Peto Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. It has a municipal seat of the [[Peto, Yucatán|same name]] and is located in the centre-south portion of the Yucatán, 135&nbsp;km to the south-east of state capital [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].

==Communities==
The municipality is made up of 74 communities, the most important include the following:

*[[Peto, Yucatán|Peto]] (Municipal Seat)
*[[Xoy, Yucatán|Xoy]]
*[[Yaxcopil]]
*[[Tixhualatun]]
*[[Progresito]]

== Maya sites ==
Inside the municipality some ancient maya sites are located, including [[Witzinah]], Tixualahtún and Xoy.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipios de Yucatán: Peto |url=https://www.yucatan.gob.mx/estado/ver_municipio.php?id=58#:~:text=Arqueol%C3%B3gicos%3A%20Calotmul%2C%20Tixualaht%C3%BAn%20y%20Xoy,la%20Virgen%20de%20la%20Estrella. |website=Gobierno del estado de Yucatán}}</ref>
