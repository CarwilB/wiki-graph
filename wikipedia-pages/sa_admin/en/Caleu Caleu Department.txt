'''Caleu Caleu Department''' ({{langx|es|Departamento Caleu Caleu}}) is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in southeastern [[La Pampa Province]], [[Argentina]]. The name means [[Laridae|gull]] in the [[Mapuche language]]. To the north, it borders [[Hucal Department]], to the east, [[Buenos Aires Province]], to the south, [[Río Negro Province]], and to the west, [[Lihuel Cahel Department]].

Many of the borders of the municipalities in the region do not align with those of the departments. Caleu Caleu includes part (including the capital) of the municipality of [[La Adela]] and part of the municipalities of [[Bernasconi, Argentina|Bernasconi]], [[General San Martín, Argentina|General San Martín]] and [[Jacinto Aráuz]], which have their capitals in another department.

[[Anzoategui, La Pampa|Anzoategui]] is also in the department.
