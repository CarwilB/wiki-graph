'''Reitoca''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://hch.tv/2023/03/30/por-gestion-de-hch-ninos-de-reitoca-francisco-morazan-reciben-importante-donativo-de-utiles-escolares/|title=¡Por gestión de HCH! Niños de Reitoca, Francisco Morazán reciben importante donativo de útiles escolares|work={{ill|HCH (Honduras)|es}}|date=30 March 2023|access-date=23 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

The name derives from ''Rerituca'', which means "Place of the soft [[juncus]]" in [[Nahuatl]].{{cn|date=November 2025}}
