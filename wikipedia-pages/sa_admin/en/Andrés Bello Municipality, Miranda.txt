'''Andrés Bello''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 25,208.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls Cuadros] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[San José de Barlovento]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Andrés Bello Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 }}</ref> The municipality is one of a number in Venezuela named "[[Andrés Bello Municipality (disambiguation)|Andrés Bello Municipality]]", in honour of the writer [[Andrés Bello]].

==Demographics==
The Andrés Bello Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 25,208 (up from 21,725 in 2000).  This amounts to 0.9% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls Cuadros] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|221.12|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls Cuadros] ine.gob.ve  {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Andrés Bello Municipality is Albaro Ramón Hidalgo Rudas, elected on October 31, 2004, with 50% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=14&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio. Andres Bello
|website=cne.gob.ve|language=es|access-date=28 August 2023}}</ref> He replaced Ramon Lobo shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; San José de Barlovento and Cumbo.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
