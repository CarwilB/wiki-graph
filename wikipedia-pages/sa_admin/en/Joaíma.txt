'''Joaíma''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 15,455 people living in a total area of {{cvt|1668|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/joaima/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Almenara]]. Joaíma is located 27&nbsp;km. south of the Jequitinhonha River. BR-116 is 58&nbsp;km. to the west. The elevation is 294 meters. It became a municipality in 1948. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Jequitinhonha]], [[Felisburgo]], [[Monte Formoso]], and [[Ponto dos Volantes]].

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising and the cultivation of coffee, sugarcane and corn. The GDP in 2006 was {{BRLConvert|43727|k|year=2006|showdate=no}}. There were 02 banking agencies {{as of|2007|lc=y}}. In the same year there were 392 automobiles, which was a ratio of one automobile for every 50 people. In the rural area there were 970 farms of which only 21 had tractors. Most of the farming practiced is on the subsistence level. There were 62,000 head of cattle in 2006.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-0 }}</ref> This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
