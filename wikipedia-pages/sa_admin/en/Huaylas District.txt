The '''Huaylas District''' ({{langx|es|'''Distrito de Huaylas'''}}) is the smallest of 10 districts of the [[Huaylas Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of [[Huaylas, Peru|Huaylas]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.munihuaylas.gob.pe/ |title=Official website of the Huaylas district |access-date=2020-01-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070820025134/http://www.munihuaylas.gob.pe/ |archive-date=2007-08-20 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Location==
The district is located in the north-western part of the province at an elevation of 2,721m. The village of Huaylas is located 45&nbsp;km from the city of [[Caraz]].
