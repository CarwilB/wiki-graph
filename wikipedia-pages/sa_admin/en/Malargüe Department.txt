'''Malargüe''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the south west of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]]. Its borders are [[San Rafael Department|San Rafael]] in the north, [[La Pampa Province]] in the east, [[Neuquén Province]] in the south and [[Chile]] to the west.

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 23,000 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|41,317|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Malargüe]], which is located around {{cvt|1,180|km}} from the federal capital [[Buenos Aires]].

==Geography==
The south of Malargüe Department is considered the northern tip of [[Patagonia]] and is home to many [[nature reserve]]s such as [[Reserva Provincial La Payunia|La Payunia]], [[Castillos de Pincheira]], [[Caverna de las Brujas]] and [[Llancanelo Lagoon|Laguna de Llancanelo]].

===Districts===
The department is divided into four [[Administrative divisions of Argentina|districts]]:

*[[Agua Escondida, Mendoza|Agua Escondida]]
*[[Malargüe]]
*[[Río Barrancas]]
*[[Río Grande, Mendoza|Río Grande]]

===Hydrology===
Malargüe has many hydric resources. Among its most important rivers are the [[Grande River (Argentina)|Grande River]], the [[Colorado River (Argentina)|Colorado River]], the [[Barrancas River]] and the [[Malargüe River]], and the lagoons of Llancanelo, [[Negra Lagoon|Negra]] and [[Niña Encantada Lagoon|Niña Encantada]].

==Science==
Malargüe is home to the [[Pierre Auger Observatory]], an observatory designed to detect [[ultra-high-energy cosmic ray]]s.

==Tourism==
The department has many exciting natural landmarks and is well suited for adventure tourism such as mountaineering, paragliding, rafting and kayaking. Here are located such exciting landmarks as Castillos de Pincheira, Tigre Cave, Brujas Cave and Pozo de las Ánimas - two closely located, up to 101 m deep sinkholes, where the wind often creates a howling sound.

Malargüe Department is home to the famous skiing resort of [[Las Leñas]].
