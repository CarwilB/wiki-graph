'''Ayacucho''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|9681|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the east with the departments of [[Junín Department, San Luis|Junín]] and [[Libertador General San Martín Department, San Luis|San Martín]], to the south with [[Coronel Pringles Department, San Luis|Coronel Pringles]] and [[Belgrano Department, San Luis|Belgrano]], to the west with the provinces of [[Mendoza Province|Mendoza]] and [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]], and to the north with San Juan, [[La Rioja Province (Argentina)|La Rioja]] and [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]], which makes it the only department in Argentina which borders four provinces.
