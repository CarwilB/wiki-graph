'''Taisha Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Morona-Santiago Province]].  Its capital is the town of Taisha.  Its population at the 2001 census was 13,078.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/|title=Resultados}}</ref>

*[[Mestizo]]  3.8%
