'''Los Andes Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Los Andes}}) is one of eight [[Provinces of Chile|province]]s of the central [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Valparaíso Region|Valparaíso]] (V). The city of [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]] is the capital of the province.

==Administration==
As a [[province]], Los Andes is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]], governed by a provincial delegate who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]]. Edith Quiroz Ortiz, appointed by president [[Sebastián Piñera|Piñera]] on April 22, 2010, was the first woman to hold the office of Governor of Los Andes. The presidential provincial delegate appointed by president [[Gabriel Boric]] is [[Cristian Aravena Reyes]], a Socialist.<ref name="Official"/>

===Communes===
The province is composed of four [[Communes of Chile|communes]] ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''comunas''), each governed by a municipality consisting of an [[alcalde]] and [[municipal council]]:
*[[Calle Larga]]
*[[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]]
*[[San Esteban, Chile|San Esteban]]
*[[Rinconada]]

==Geography and demography==
The province spans a landlocked area of {{convert|3,054.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, the second largest in the Valparaíso Region. According to the 2002 census, Los Andes is the sixth most populous province in the region with a population of 91,683. At that time, there were 74,104 people living in [[urban area]]s, 17,579 people living in [[rural area]]s, 46,325 men and 45,358 women.<ref name="INE"/>
