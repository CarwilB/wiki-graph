'''Angaraes''' is one of seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] located in the [[Huancavelica Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Lircay, Peru|Lircay]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': province of [[Acobamba Province|Acobamba]]
*'''East''': [[Ayacucho Region]]
*'''South''': province of [[Huaytará Province|Huaytará]]
*'''West''': province of [[Huancavelica Province|Huancavelica]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into twelve [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

* [[Anchonga District|Anchonga]] ([[Anchonga]])
* [[Callanmarca District|Callanmarca]] ([[Callanmarca]])
* [[Ccochaccasa District|Ccochaccasa]] ([[Ccochaccasa]])
* [[Chincho District|Chincho]] ([[Chincho]])
* [[Congalla District|Congalla]] ([[Congalla]])
* [[Huanca-Huanca District|Huanca-Huanca]] ([[Huanca-Huanca]])
* [[Huayllay Grande District|Huayllay Grande]] ([[Huayllay Grande]])
* [[Julcamarca District|Julcamarca]] ([[Julcamarca]])
* [[Lircay District|Lircay]] ([[Lircay, Peru|Lircay]])
* [[San Antonio de Antaparco District|San Antonio de Antaparco]] ([[Antaparco]])
* [[Santo Tomás de Pata District|Santo Tomás de Pata]] ([[Santo Tomás de Pata]])
* [[Secclla District|Secclla]] ([[Secclla]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (78.63%) learnt to speak in childhood, 21.12% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
