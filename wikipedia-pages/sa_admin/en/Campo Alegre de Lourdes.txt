'''Campo Alegre de Lourdes''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Campo Alegre de Lourdes covers {{convert|2,781.170|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 28,820 with a population density of 11 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located at the north of the state on the border of Bahia and [[Piauí]].<ref name="h"/><ref name="i"/>
