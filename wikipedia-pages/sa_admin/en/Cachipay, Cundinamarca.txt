'''Cachipay''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Tequendama Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Cachipay borders [[Quipile]] in the west, [[Zipacón]] in the east, [[Anolaima]] in the north and [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] in the south. The urban centre is located {{convert|53|km|mi}} east of Bogotá.<ref name=websiteCachipay>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.cachipay-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cachipay]{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Cachipay is probably derived from the [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] name for a fruit similar to the typical Colombian fruit [[Bactris guineensis|corozo]].<ref name=websiteCachipay/>

== History ==
The history of the present municipality of Cachipay dates back at least to the [[Herrera Period]], from which [[ceramic]]s have been found in the current ''vereda'' Tocarema, dated at 2750 years [[Before Present|BP]] (750 BCE).<ref>{{citation |last=Argüello García |first=Pedro María |year=2015 |type=PhD |title=Subsistence economy and chiefdom emergence in the Muisca area. A study of the Valle de Tena (PhD) |url=http://d-scholarship.pitt.edu/24491/1/Arguello_P._Subsistence_economy.pdf |publisher=[[University of Pittsburgh]] |pages=1–193 |accessdate=2016-07-08}}</ref>
The region before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] was inhabited by the [[Panche people]], who were in constant conflict with the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] inhabiting the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] to the east.

Tocarema was the site of the [[Battle of Tocarema]] on August 20, 1538, where an alliance of Muisca [[guecha warrior]]s and troops of the Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] confronted and submitted the Panche, due to their superior weaponry, [[cavalry]] and the dogs trained for war.<ref name=websiteCachipay/>

Modern Cachipay was not founded until November 26, 1982.<ref name=websiteCachipay/>
