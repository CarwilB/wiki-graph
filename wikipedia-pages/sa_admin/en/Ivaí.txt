==Climate==
The climate is [[Oceanic climate|highland subtropical climate]] but always humid ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb''), with an average temperature of 21.6&nbsp;°C, some lower areas can transit to a [[humid subtropical climate]] (''Cfa''), transition found in the Ivaí Valley.<ref name = wb>{{Cite web|url=http://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weatherall.php3?s=598022&units=|title=Ivaí, Parana Travel Weather Averages (Weatherbase)|website=Weatherbase|access-date=2019-05-29}}</ref>
