'''Paratebueno''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. It is 90 km (56 mi) outside the capital city of Bogotá.

==Population==
There were 8,043 residents as of the 2018 census, updated November 2019.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Información general: Paratebueno: Población total 2018 |url=https://sitios.dane.gov.co/cnpv/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250505081135/https://sitios.dane.gov.co/cnpv/ |archive-date=2025-05-05 |access-date=2025-06-08 |website=sitios.dane.gov.co |language=es |url-status=live }}</ref>

== Earthquake ==
On June 8, 2025, Paratebueno was the epicenter of a [[2025 Cundinamarca earthquake|6.4 magnitude earthquake]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-08 |title=Vías rotas y colapso de viviendas dejó el temblor en Paratebueno y más municipios |url=https://www.elespectador.com/bogota/vias-rotas-y-colapso-de-viviendas-dejo-el-temblor-en-paratebueno-y-mas-municipios/ |access-date=2025-06-08 |website=El Espectador |language=spanish}}</ref> Hundreds of homes, as well as multiple schools and churches, were damaged in the Santa Cecilia area of Paratebueno and in the nearby town of Medina.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-06-09 |title=Temblor en Paratebueno: 80% de las viviendas en Santa Cecilia tienen pérdida total |url=https://www.elespectador.com/colombia/cundinamarca/temblor-en-colombia-80-de-las-viviendas-en-santa-cecilia-tendra-perdida-total/?cx_artPos=2&cx_testVariant=cx_1&cx_testId=59 |access-date=2025-06-09 |website=El Espectador |language=spanish}}</ref>

==Climate==
Paratebueno has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall in all months except January.
