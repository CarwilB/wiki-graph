'''Santa Bárbara de Carhuacayan District''' or '''Santa Bárbara de Carhuacayán District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Yauli Province]] in the [[Junín Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Puwaq Hanka mountain range|Puwaq Hanka]] mountain range traverses the district. One of the highest mountains of the district is [[Allqay]] at {{convert|5359|m|ft|abbr=on}}.
