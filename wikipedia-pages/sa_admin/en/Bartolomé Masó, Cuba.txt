'''Bartolomé Masó''' ({{IPA|es|baɾtoloˈme maˈso}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Granma Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
The municipality was named after the Cuban patriot [[Bartolomé Masó|Bartolomé Masó Márquez]].<ref>{{cite book |last1=Domingo |first1=Alfredo Mateo |title=Historia de la división político-administrativa de la isla de Cuba, 1607-1976 |date=1977 |publisher=Editorial Arte y Literatura |page=113 |url=https://www.google.com/books/edition/Historia_de_la_divisi%C3%B3n_pol%C3%ADtico_admin/BkKwAAAAIAAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&bsq=%22Bartolom%C3%A9+Mas%C3%B3%22+%2B+%22lleva+el+nombre%22&dq=%22Bartolom%C3%A9+Mas%C3%B3%22+%2B+%22lleva+el+nombre%22&printsec=frontcover |access-date=8 July 2025 |language=es}}</ref> It is the home of the ''[[Comandancia General de La Plata]]'', headquarters of the [[Cuban Revolution|Cuban revolutionaries]] around [[Fidel Castro]] and today a museum, located in the [[Sierra Maestra]] mountains.<ref>{{cite web |title=Comandancia General de La Plata, Granma |url=https://www.visitarcuba.org/comandancia-general-de-la-plata-granma |website=www.visitarcuba.org |access-date=8 July 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Bartolomé Masó had a population of 46,711.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|629|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=6 October 2007| archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20071012141831/http://statoids.com/ycu.html| archivedate= 12 October 2007 | url-status= live}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|74|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. In 2009, around 73% of Bartolomé Masó's territorial area of 634 square kilometers was rural and difficult to access.<ref>{{cite news |title=Tierra de epopeyas. |url=http://www.cnctv.cubasi.cu/bartolomemaso.php |access-date=8 July 2025 |work=CNC TV |date=15 May 2009 |archive-date=15 May 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090515120116/http://www.cnctv.cubasi.cu/bartolomemaso.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Earthquakes==
On 10 November 2024, a 6.8 magnitude earthquake struck the coast of southeastern Cuba at UTC 05:00.<ref name="usgs">{{cite web |title=M 6.8 - 42 km SSW of Bartolomé Masó, Cuba |url=https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/us7000nr0v/executive |website=earthquake.usgs.gov |access-date=8 July 2025}}</ref> The location of the earthquake was measured to be just about 40 miles away SSW of Bartolomé Masó.<ref name="usgs" /> The earthquake's strength was felt along the coast of Cuba, which had already been experiencing flash flooding due to [[Hurricane Oscar]], and major destruction.<ref>{{cite news |title=Cubans tested to the limit as earthquake, hurricanes and total blackout hit within days |url=https://www.miamiherald.com/news/nation-world/world/americas/cuba/article295400954.html |access-date=8 July 2025 |work=Miami Herald |date=11 November 2024}}</ref> [[First Secretary of the Communist Party of Cuba]] [[Miguel Díaz-Canel|Miguel Díaz-Canel Bermúdez]] said on [[Twitter|X]] that the quakes damaged homes and powerlines and caused landslides in the coastal provinces of Santiago de Cuba and Granma.<ref name="nyt">{{cite news |last1=Davis |first1=William B. |last2=Dong |first2=Madison |last3=Jones |first3=Judson |last4=Keefe |first4=John |last5=Malsky |first5=Bea |last6=Rosenberg |first6=Carol |title=Maps: Strong Quakes Strike Near Cuba |url=https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2024/11/10/world/americas/quake-tracker-cuba.html |access-date=8 July 2025 |work=The New York Times |date=10 November 2024}}</ref> He urged people in those areas to stay outside in open spaces stating, "The first and most essential thing is to save lives."<ref name="nyt" />

The town was again hit by an earthquake with a magnitude of 3.3 in February 2025, with the epicenter being 24 kilometers from Bartolomé Masó.<ref>{{cite news |title=Cuban authorities report a perceptible earthquake near Granma |url=https://en.cibercuba.com/noticias/2025-02-25-u1-e129488-s52968-nid297750-autoridades-cubanas-reportan-sismo-perceptible-cerca |access-date=8 July 2025 |work=CiberCuba |date=25 February 2025 |language=en}}</ref>
