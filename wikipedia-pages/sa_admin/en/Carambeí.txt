== Etymology ==
The name Cararambí derives from the [[Guarani language|Guarani]] ''karumbe'' (turtle) and "y" (water, river): the turtles river.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Vicente Ferreira |first=João Carlos |url=https://www.iat.pr.gov.br/sites/agua-terra/arquivos_restritos/files/documento/2020-07/origens_significados_nomes_municipios_pr.pdf |title=Cidades Brasileiras, origem e significado de seus nomes, Paraná |year=2006 |location=Curitiba |pages=78 |language=portuguese |trans-title=Brazilian cities, origin and meaning of their names, Paraná}}</ref>

==History==
Carambeí was founded in 4 April 1911, and became a municipality on 13 December 1995.

==Climate==
Carambeí is classified as [[oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: ''Cfb''). In summer, the temperature rises to 27°C, whereas winters are somewhat cold, the temperature reaching 10°C. [[Frost]] is common in the winter.
