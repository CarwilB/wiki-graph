The '''Anzoátegui Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 17,030.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Cojedes, Cojedes|Cojedes]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Anzoátegui Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Anzoátegui Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 16,333 (up from 14,354 in 2000).  This amounts to 5.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|19.65|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

|website=gob.ve|language=es|access-date=26 August 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Cojedes and Juan de Mata Suárez.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
