'''Passa-Quatro''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 16,393 (2020 est.) in an area of 277.22&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1DM2 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is part of the microregion of [[São Lourenço, Minas Gerais|São Lourenço]].

==History==
The origin of the name comes from the explorations of the [[bandeirante]] [[Fernão Dias Paes Leme]] who was given instructions to pass over a river four times, thus "Passa Quatro".  The first inhabitants settled the region in 1674. The region wasn't populated until the second half of the 19th century when it became a district in 1854.  In 1884, the Minas-Rio railway was constructed by the [[England|English]] and spurred its development.  Emperor Dom [[Pedro II of Brazil|Pedro II]] elevated the town to a city in 1888.

==Geography==
Mountains include the fourth tallest peak in Brazil, the Pedra da Mina (2,798 m) located within Serra Fina and the point of [[Mantiqueira Mountains|Serra da Mantiqueira]] where the boundary of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] is located.  The Pico dos Três Estados is another landmark located on the boundaries of São Paulo, Minas Gerais and [[Rio de Janeiro]].  There are dozens of other peaks over 2,000 m.

The city was declared a hydro-mineral [[spa]] in 1941 due to its various fountains of torio-[[radioactive]] waters, which are reputedly sought out for curing liver and kidney problems. The winter is dry and the summer mild with temperatures varying between 16 and 22 degrees Celsius.  In winter they can drop to 0 degrees with strong frost. This rural area has views of four of the ten highest peaks in the country.{{citation needed|date=January 2019}} Trout fishing is possible all year. A tourist train service (narrow gauge) was inaugurated in the beginning of 2004 between the cities of Passa Quatro and [[Manacá]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|335|ha|adj=on}} [[Passa Quatro National Forest]], created in 1968.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt

The forest is controlled by [[IBAMA]] and has native forest, exotic species, lakes, and the Iporã waterfall.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Floresta Nacional de Passa Quatro – ICMBio}}|language=pt|title=Floresta Nacional de Passa Quatro|publisher=[[ICMBio]]|url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/visitacao1/unidades-abertas-a-visitacao/2590-floresta-nacional-de-passa-quatro|access-date=2016-09-29|archive-date=2019-08-07|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190807085416/http://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/visitacao1/unidades-abertas-a-visitacao/2590-floresta-nacional-de-passa-quatro|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===New Climber Point===
Passa Quatro has a newer point of [[vine|climber]] at Brazil, compared to [[Siurana (Tarragona)|Siurana]]: [[Gruta de Passa Quatro]] (Cave of Four Passes).
There are a big negative and the local climbers are working to open the roads in this place. (The climbing point is in Passa Vinte, not in Passa Quarto)
