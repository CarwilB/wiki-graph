'''Cajueiro da Praia''' is one of the four coastal cities of [[Piauí]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|313800|ha|adj=on}} [[Delta do Parnaíba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Delta do Parnaíba (Federal) – ISA}}
 |title=APA Delta do Parnaíba (Federal)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/585933|access-date=2017-02-09}}</ref>

The city is home to the '''Cashew of A Praia''' (''Cajueiro da Praia''), which is either the largest [[cashew|cashew tree]] in the world, at {{convert|8800|m2|acre}}, or the second largest, after the [[Cashew of Pirangi]]. The tree has been studied by the [[State University of Piauí]].<ref>{{cite web|URL=http://cidadeverde.com/noticias/212509/setur-confirma-cajueiro-no-piaui-como-o-maior-do-mundo-com-8800m|title=Setur confirma cajueiro no Piauí como o maior do mundo com 8.800m² |author=Cidade Verde |date=2016-02-05|access-date=2018-08-21|language=pt}}</ref>
