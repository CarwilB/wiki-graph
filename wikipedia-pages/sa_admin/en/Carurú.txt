'''Carurú''' is a town and municipality located in the [[departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Vaupés Department|Vaupés]], [[Colombia]]. The town is on the north bank of the [[Vaupés River]] in the [[Amazon natural region]] of the country.

The town is served by the [[Carurú Airport]] and there are no road connections to the rest of the country, the only waterway is the Vaupés River.
