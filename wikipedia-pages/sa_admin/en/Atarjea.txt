''' Atarjea''' is a Mexican city and [[Municipalities of Guanajuato|municipality]] located in the easternmost region of the state of [[Guanajuato]], within the Sierra Gorda range. The municipality has an area of 318 square kilometres (1.04% of the surface of the state) and is bordered to the north by the state of [[San Luis Potosí]], to the east and south by the state of [[Querétaro]], and to the west by [[Xichú]]. The municipality had a population of 5,198 according to the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=2007-03-22 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070328224939/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archivedate=28 March 2007 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |title=Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |accessdate=2007-03-22 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070317205520/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |archivedate=17 March 2007 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The name of the municipality is of [[Otomi people|Otomí]] origin and means "Where they go to drink water."

The municipal president of Atarjea and its many smaller outlying communities is Guadalupe Flores Loyola.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://v2.pan.org.mx/pics/gobierno/edo_gto/atarjea.htm |title=Información General |accessdate=2007-06-25 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070703095238/http://v2.pan.org.mx/pics/gobierno/edo_gto/atarjea.htm |archivedate=2007-07-03 }}</ref>
