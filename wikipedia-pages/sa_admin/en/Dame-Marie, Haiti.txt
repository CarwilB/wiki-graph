'''Dame-Marie''' ({{IPA|fr|dam maʁi|-|Fr-Paris--Dame-Marie.ogg}}; {{langx|ht|Dam Mari}}) is a small seaside [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] located on the western tip of [[Haiti]], in the arrondissement of [[Anse-d'Hainault Arrondissement|Anse-d'Hainault]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].  The municipality's population according to the 2015 census is 38,747 inhabitants. Unlike other cities in Haiti, nearly all the roads in Dame Marie are paved. Dame Marie is relatively well-developed and has an association for most of the existing trades and professions.  Agriculture and fishing are the economic mainstays in the community. The land and sea do offer much potential, but profit margins are barely beyond sustenance due to outmoded equipment, lack of proper storage and poor transportation.
