'''Itatiaia''' ({{IPA|pt|itɐtʃiˈajjɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. With a 2021 population estimate of 32,312, it is the 1,097th-most populous municipality in Brazil.<ref>{{Cite web |date=23 August 2021 |title=Estimativas da população residente no Brasil e unidades da federação com data de referência em 1º de julho de 2021 |url=https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Estimativas_de_Populacao/Estimativas_2021/estimativa_dou_2021.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210827121204/https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Estimativas_de_Populacao/Estimativas_2021/estimativa_dou_2021.pdf |archive-date=27 August 2021 |access-date=17 February 2023 |website=IBGE}}</ref> Located in the [[Mantiqueira Mountains|Mantiqueira mountain range]] and on the border of the states of Rio de Janeiro and [[Minas Gerais|Minais Gerais]], it covers an area of {{cvt|241.035|km2|sqmi}}.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021 |title=Cidades e Estados: Itatiaia |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/rj/itatiaia.html |access-date=18 February 2023 |website=IBGE}}</ref>

The oldest national park in Brazil, [[Itatiaia National Park]] (''Parque Nacional do Itatiaia''), is partially located within Itatiaia.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Parque Nacional do Itatiaia |url=https://parquedoitatiaia.tur.br/ |access-date=18 February 2023 |website=BR Parques}}</ref> The municipality also contains part of the {{cvt|8036|ha|acre|adj=on}} [[Pedra Selada State Park]], created in 2012.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Pedra Selada – INEA}}|language=pt|title=Parque Estadual da Pedra Selada|publisher=INEA: Istituto Estadual do Ambiente (RJ)|url=http://www.inea.rj.gov.br/Portal/Agendas/BIODIVERSIDADEEAREASPROTEGIDAS/UnidadesdeConservacao/INEA_008422|access-date=2016-10-01|archive-date=2016-09-15|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160915163750/http://www.inea.rj.gov.br/Portal/Agendas/BIODIVERSIDADEEAREASPROTEGIDAS/UnidadesdeConservacao/INEA_008422|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
''Itatiaia'' is a [[Guarani language|Guaraní]] term meaning "pointed stone," formed from the words ''itá'' ("stone") and ''atîaîa'' ("pointed").

== History ==
Itatiaia's current territory was originally inhabited by the [[Tupi people|Tupi]], [[Purí people|Purí]], and [[Coroado]] indigenous tribes. The first Europeans arrived in the region in the 17th century, most being [[bandeirantes]] in search of gold.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=historia – Itatiaia |url=https://itatiaia.rj.gov.br/historia |access-date=2023-02-18 |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Green |first=James N. |display-authors= |date=2021 |title=Bandeirantes, Natives, and Indigenous Slavery |url=https://library.brown.edu/create/fivecenturiesofchange/chapters/chapter-2/native-populations/ |website=Brazil: Five Centuries of Change |publisher=Brown University Library Center for Digital Scholarship}}</ref> The demand for gold trade routes from [[Minas Gerais]] to the ports of [[Angra dos Reis]] and [[Paraty|Parati]] encouraged expeditions over the Mantiqueira mountain range through what are now the municipalities of [[Mauá]] and Itatiaia.<ref name=":0" />

With the shuttering of the region's gold mines, the district's landowners transitioned to sugarcane and coffee farming. One of the municipality's largest such farms was owned by politician [[Irineu Evangelista de Sousa, Viscount of Mauá|Irineu Evangelista de Sousa]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=portal@viscondedemaua.com.br |date=2021-06-17 |title=Irineu Evangelista de Sousa: O Barão de Mauá » Visconde de Mauá |url=https://www.viscondedemaua.com.br/artigo/barao-de-visconde-de-maua/ |access-date=2023-02-18 |website=Visconde de Mauá |language=pt-BR}}</ref> The farm's lands are now part of Itatiaia National Park, its forests having been preserved due to Sousa's avoidance of coffee monoculture farming methods.<ref name=":0" />

In 1832, a justice of the peace and notary public were installed in the district. On April 5, 1839, what is now Itatiaia was officially split off from the municipality of [[Resende, Rio de Janeiro|Resende]] and given the name ''Campo Belo'' ("Beautiful Field"). That same year, the district's first ecclesiastical [[curate]] was installed.<ref name=":0" />

On December 31, 1943, the district's name was changed from Campo Belo to Itatiaia.<ref name=":0" />

The district of Itatiaia was elevated to the status of municipality on July 6, 1988.<ref name=":0" />

== Tourism ==
[[File:Neve itatiaia22.jpg|thumb|Snow in Itatiaia National Park, 1985. That year, temperatures in the park reached a record low of {{cvt|-13|C}}.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Globo |first=Acervo-Jornal O. |title=No Sul do Estado do Rio, turistas fazem bonecos de neve no inverno de 1985 |url=https://acervo.oglobo.globo.com/em-destaque/no-sul-do-estado-do-rio-turistas-fazem-bonecos-de-neve-no-inverno-de-1985-12534049 |access-date=2023-02-18 |website=Acervo |language=pt-br}}</ref>]]
Itatiaia's natural beauty and pleasant weather attract thousands of visitors each year, with many rock climbing and hiking in the surrounding mountains.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Turismo Itatiaia {{!}} Turismo Itatiaia |url=https://turismoitatiaia.com.br/ |access-date=2023-02-18 |website=turismoitatiaia.com.br |language=pt}}</ref>

In addition to the Itatiaia National Park, Itatiaia's other tourist destinations include the former Finnish colony of [[Penedo, Itatiaia|Penedo]] and the villages of Maromba and [[Maringá]], both located in the region of [[Visconde de Mauá, Resende|Visconde de Mauá]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Atrativos Turísticos – Itatiaia |url=https://itatiaia.rj.gov.br/atrativos-turisticos |access-date=2023-02-18 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

=== Itatiaia National Park ===
[[File:Mirante do ultimo adeus.jpg|thumb|View from Mirante do ultimo adeus., [[Itatiaia National Park]]]]
[[Itatiaia National Park]], located partially in the municipality, is a tourist attraction and Brazil's first national park. It was created in 1937 under then-president [[Getúlio Vargas]].<ref name=":0" />

=== Pico das Agulhas Negras ===
{{unreferenced section|date=December 2025}}
[[File:Pico das agulhas negras22.jpg|thumb|Pico das Agulhas Negras]]
The Agulhas Negras peak, with a height of {{cvt|2,790.94|m}}, is the highest point in the state of Rio de Janeiro and the fifth highest in Brazil.

Although it is located in a [[Tropics|tropical zone]], the peak usually registers negative temperatures during the winter and occasionally snow in years exceptionally more humid.

=== Penedo ===
{{unreferenced section|date=December 2025}}
[[File:Cachoeira véu de noiva.jpg|thumb|Waterfall véu de noiva, Itatiaia National Park]]
Penedo is a neighborhood and ecological park of the municipality of Itatiaia. It is the main [[Scandinavian Brazilians#Finnish|Finnish]] colony of Brazil, which still shows remarkable in the architecture of residences and commercial buildings, as well as in the local culture.

[[Scandinavian Brazilians#Immigration|Finnish immigration in Brazil]] was the migration movement in the twentieth century of Finns to the interior of the state of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, where they created a settlement called Penedo at the foot of the Itatiaia National Park in the city of Itatiaia.

Tourism in Penedo is a true vocation of Penedo, with a network of 52 hotels and 39 restaurants, snack bars, and bars.

=== Education ===
The municipality of Itatiaia contains a system of public and private education, with twenty municipal schools, one state school, and five private schools.

=== Public security ===

==== Military police ====
The ostensible policing of the city is carried out by the 2nd Company of the 37th [[Military Police of Rio de Janeiro State|Military Police Battalion of the State of Rio de Janeiro]] (37º BPM / 2ªCia), with headquarters in the city center, counting Itatiaia, with a Community Policing Station in the region of Penedo.

==== Civil police ====
The [[Civil Police of Rio de Janeiro State|Civil Police of the State of Rio de Janeiro]] maintains in the municipality the 99th Police Station (99th DP), subordinated to the 9th Regional Police Coordination of the Interior (9th CRPI).

==== Municipal Guard and Civil Defense ====
The city also has a Civil Defense team, to monitor and assist the population in the event of natural disasters, as well as maintains a Municipal Guard, responsible for monitoring the public patrimony and organizing the traffic in the city.

==== Fire Department ====
It was inaugurated by the Rio de Janeiro State Fire Brigade (CBMERJ), on July 27, 2016, at 1:00&nbsp;p.m., an operational unit in the municipality of Itatiaia. The headquarters has a hybrid vehicle and a staff of 24 soldiers. The unit is located between the Municipal Hospital and Maternity Dr. Manoel Martins de Barros and the 99th Police Department, in the neighborhood of Jardim Itatiaia.

There are {{cvt|2,615.48|m2}} of land, which were ceded by the Itatiaia City Hall, with {{cvt|407|m2}} of total constructed area. The work, financed with resources of the fire rate, received an investment of R $647 thousand.

The unit is commanded by Lieutenant Colonel Mauro Junior and is subordinate to the 23rd GBM - Resende.

The barracks is {{cvt|4.7|km}} from the entrance of the Itatiaia National Park, which will enable faster rescue from forest fires.
