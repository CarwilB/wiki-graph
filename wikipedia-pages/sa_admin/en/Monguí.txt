'''Monguí''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the [[Sugamuxi Province]] a subregion of Boyaca.

It was founded in 1601.<ref name="Alcaldía">{{cite web |url=http://www.mongui-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |title=Información general de Monguí |editor=Alcaldía del municipio |access-date=26 October 2017 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150604004027/http://www.mongui-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |archive-date=4 June 2015 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Monguí was named a ''[[Pueblo Patrimonio (Colombia)|Pueblo Patrimonio]]'' (heritage town) of Colombia in 2010. It is among 11 municipalities nationwide that were selected to be part of the ''Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio'' original cohort.<ref>{{Cite web|date=Dec 2020|title=GUÍA: Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia|url=https://fontur.com.co/sites/default/files/2020-12/GUIA_PUEBLOS_PATRIMONIO.pdf|access-date=29 Mar 2021|website=Fondo Nacional de Turismo de la República de Colombia - FONTUR}}</ref>
