'''Maneiro''' is one of 11 municipalities that make up [[Isla Margarita]] in the state of [[Nueva Esparta]], [[Venezuela]]. The capital is [[Pampatar]].<ref name=DPT>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |title=División Político Territorial de la República Bolivariana de Venezuela |access-date=25 October 2015 |date=September 2013 |website=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística (Venezuela)|INE]] |archive-date=24 March 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230324042920/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |url-status=dead}}</ref> In the 2011 census (published 2014) Maneiro had a population of 48,952 people.<ref name=censo>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/nuevaesparta.pdf |title=Resultados por entidad federal y municipios del Estado Nueva Esparta |website=Censo nacional de población y vivienda 2011 |access-date=25 October 2015 |date=December 2014 |editor=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística (Venezuela)|INE]] |archive-date=1 March 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210301103755/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/nuevaesparta.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>
