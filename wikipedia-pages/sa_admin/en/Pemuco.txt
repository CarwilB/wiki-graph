'''Pemuco''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Diguillín Province]], [[Ñuble Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Pemuco spans an area of {{convert|562.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 8,821 inhabitants (4,578 men and 4,243 women). Of these, 3,844 (43.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,977 (56.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 4.8% (408 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pemuco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Julio Muñoz Salazar ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pemuco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Carlos Abel Jarpa  ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) and Rosauro Martínez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 41st electoral district, together with [[Chillán]], [[Coihueco]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[San Ignacio, Chile|San Ignacio]], [[El Carmen, Chile|El Carmen]], [[Yungay, Chile|Yungay]] and [[Chillán Viejo]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Victor Pérez Varela ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mariano Ruiz-Esquide Jara (PDC) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).
