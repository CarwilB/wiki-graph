'''Calzada District''' is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Moyobamba Province|Moyobamba]] in [[Peru]]. Calzada is a small town and has 3,586 inhabitants according to a [[2017 Peru Census|2017 census]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]].
