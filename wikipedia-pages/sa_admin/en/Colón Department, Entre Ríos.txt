The '''Colón Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Colón''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Entre Ríos Province|Entre Ríos]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-east of the province, beside the [[Uruguay River]].

The department has 75,305 inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2022}}. The head town is [[Colón, Entre Ríos|Colón]] (population 21,000). Other cities and towns are [[San José, Entre Ríos|San José]], [[Ubajay]], [[Villa Elisa, Entre Ríos|Villa Elisa]], [[Pueblo Liebig]], [[La Clarita]], [[Arroyo Barú]], [[Pueblo Cazes]], Hocker and Hambis.
