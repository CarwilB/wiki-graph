'''Mistrato''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. About 86 km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]]. In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 17,527.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Mistrató, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/mistrato |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Mistrató has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average annual temperature of 20°C.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Mistrató |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100239/mistrato/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>
