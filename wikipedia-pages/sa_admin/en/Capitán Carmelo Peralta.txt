'''Capitán Carmelo Peralta''', commonly known as Carmelo Peralta, is a Paraguayan city and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] situated on the banks of the [[Paraguay River]], on the border with [[Porto Murtinho]], [[Mato Grosso do Sul]], [[Brazil]], approximately 716 km from [[Asunción]], in the [[Departments of Paraguay|department]] of [[Alto Paraguay Department|Alto Paraguay]], [[Paraguay]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.stp.gov.py/v1/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/Informativo-DGGR-06-2019-4.pdf|title=Informativo DGGPR No 06/19|last=|first=|date=|website=STP ~ Paraguay|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=24 May 2019}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/paraguay-admin.php|title=Paraguay: Administrative Division (Departments and Districts) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map|website=www.citypopulation.de|access-date=2019-05-24}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Mariela |title=Ley Nº 3471 / CREA EL MUNICIPIO DE CARMELO PERALTA EN EL XVII DEPARTAMENTO ALTO PARAGUAY Y UNA MUNICIPALIDAD CON ASIENTO EN EL PUEBLO DE CARMELO PERALTA. |url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/1023/crea-el-municipio-de-carmelo-peralta-en-el-xvii-departamento-alto-paraguay-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-el-pueblo-de-carmelo-peralta |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Baccn |language=es}}</ref>

It is known as "The Gateway to the Pantanal" or "The Pantanal City," as from here, visitors can access the [[Pantanal]], which is the largest wetland in the world, shared between Paraguay, Brazil, and [[Bolivia]].<ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g1mOZ_RN0G8 |title=Paraguay Salvaje: Carmelo Peralta, la puerta al Pantanal paraguayo - Parte 1 (Episodio Completo) |language=en |access-date=2024-04-07 |via=www.youtube.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=DIGITAL |first=ADN |date=2019-05-05 |title=Carmelo Peralta, conocido como la entrada al “Pantanal Chaqueño” |url=https://www.adndigital.com.py/carmelo-peralta-conocido-como-la-entrada-al-pantanal-chaqueno/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=ADN Digital |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Duarte |first=Juana |date=2022-06-23 |title=El proyecto “Vida en el Pantanal” ofrece cursos de comunicación audiovisual en Carmelo Peralta |url=https://rcc.com.py/chaco/el-proyecto-vida-en-el-pantanal-ofrece-cursos-de-comunicacion-audiovisual-en-carmelo-peralta/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=RCC |language=es}}</ref>

In this locality, the [[Route 15 (Paraguay)|PY15]] "Bioceanic" route begins, crossing the Paraguayan Chaco from east to west, ending at the town of [[Pozo Hondo]], on the border with [[Argentina]].

== Toponymy ==
It is named in honor of Carmelo Peralta, a distinguished [[Armed Forces of Paraguay|Paraguayan military]] aviator from [[Quiíndy|Quiindy]], who served during the [[Chaco War]] with the rank of Captain in the [[Paraguayan Air Force]].

He was later promoted to Major and served as the personal pilot for President [[José Félix Estigarribia]] and his wife Julia Miranda Cueto, with whom he died in an air crash on September 7, 1940, at the age of 30. He was posthumously promoted to the immediately superior rank of Lieutenant Colonel.<ref>{{Cite web |title=La Nación / Paraguay en la historia: un accidente aéreo privó al país de su mayor genio militar |url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/pais/2020/09/07/paraguay-en-la-historia-un-accidente-aereo-privo-al-pais-de-su-mayor-genio-militar/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=www.lanacion.com.py |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xvsmDCidtdk |title=Carmelo Peralta - Un emblemático aviador paraguayo |language=en |access-date=2024-04-07 |via=www.youtube.com}}</ref>

== History ==
The first settlers arrived in 1898, initially comprising 12 families, among which a Brazilian woman named Margarita was the first to settle on the island now bearing her name, Margarita Island.<ref>{{Cite web |last=lanaciongn |date=2018-06-27 |title=Diario HOY {{!}} Isla Margarita: el día a día de una comunidad que se encuentra bajo agua |url=https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/isla-margarita-el-dia-a-dia-de-una-comunidad-que-se-encuentra-bajo-agua |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Diario HOY |language=es}}</ref>

A Paraguayan map from 1933 (during the Chaco War) shows the area of Carmelo Peralta named as Puerto Eduardo, functioning as a cattle ranch.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Milda |title=El Paraguay Occidental – Imagoteca |url=https://imagoteca.com.py/el-paraguay-occidental-2/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |language=en-US}}</ref>

The current town of Carmelo Peralta was officially founded in 1940, opposite the Brazilian locality of Porto Murtinho.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2017-05-03 |title=Carmelo Peralta conmemora décimo año de emancipación |url=https://www.ultimahora.com/carmelo-peralta-conmemora-decimo-ano-emancipacion-n1082902 |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Última Hora |language=es}}</ref>

During that time, the Anglo-Argentine company The River Plate Quebracho Company S.A. also operated in the area, extracting [[Quebracho tree|quebracho trees]] for the production of [[tannin]], a substance used for converting raw animal hides into leather in a process known as [[Tanning (leather)|tanning]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Comunidades ayoreo de Puerto María Auxiliadora en alerta |url=https://ddhh2021.codehupy.org.py/comunidades-ayoreo-de-puerto-maria-auxiliadora-en-alerta/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Mariela |title=Ley Nº 4230 / AUTORIZA AL PODER EJECUTIVO – MINISTERIO DEL INTERIOR A TRANSFERIR A TITULO GRATUITO A FAVOR DEL INSTITUTO NACIONAL DE DESARROLLO RURAL Y DE LA TIERRA (INDERT) A LOS FINES DE LA REFORMA AGRARIA, UN INMUEBLE INDIVIDUALIZADO COMO FINCA N° 14.565, PROPIEDAD DEL ESTADO PARAGUAYO, UBICADO EN PUERTO LA ESPERANZA (EX PUERTO SASTRE) |url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/3630/ley-n-4230-autoriza-al-poder-ejecutivo-ministerio-del-interior-a-transferir-a-titulo-gratuito-a-favor-del-instituto-nacional-de-desarrollo-rural-y-de-la-tierra-indert-a-los-fines-de-la-reforma-agraria-un-inmueble-individualizado-como-finca-n-14565-propiedad-del-estado-paraguayo-ubicado-en-puerto-la-esperanza-ex-puerto-sastre |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Baccn |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2010-08-30 |title=Quebracho es la especie chaqueña más exportada |url=https://www.ultimahora.com/quebracho-es-la-especie-chaquena-mas-exportada-n353474 |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Última Hora |language=es}}</ref>

In 1948, the Apostolic Vicariate of the Paraguayan Chaco was created, with its first bishop being Monsignor Ángel Muzzolón, a Salesian of [[Uruguay|Uruguayan]] origin. During his episcopate from 1956 to 1962, the first contacts were made with the [[Ayoreo]] indigenous people near Fortín Madrejón in the Boreal Chaco. In 1963, due to droughts, the Salesians moved their mission to Puerto María Auxiliadora, 30 km south of Carmelo Peralta, along the Paraguay River near Cerro Siete Cabezas, and the evangelized Ayoreo indigenous people relocated there.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Vicariato Apostólico del Chaco |url=https://episcopal.org.py/?page_id=1054 |access-date=2024-04-07 |language=es}}</ref>

In 1974, an attempt was made to establish a Paraguayan colony named Colonia Francisco Careaga Chávez near Cerro Siete Cabezas, but ten years later, most of the lands were sold to Brazilian businesses.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |title=Disposiciones 2023 |url=https://www.ssme.gov.py/vmme/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=2147&Itemid=775 |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=www.ssme.gov.py}}</ref>

Carmelo Peralta was administratively part of the district of [[Puerto Casado]] until May 2, 2008, when it became a municipality, according to Law No. 3,471.

== Geography ==
It is located on the western (right) bank of the Paraguay River, bordering Porto Murtinho, Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil. It borders Coronel Cabral and Toro Pampa to the northwest, Puerto Guaraní and Fuerte Olimpo to the north, and Porto Murtinho in Brazil to the northeast, east, and southeast, and to the south with Puerto María Auxiliadora and Puerto La Esperanza, to the southwest with Coronel Paulino Alén (Cruce Paragro) and to the west with Mayor Hilario Amarilla (Cruce 65), respectively.

== Economy ==
The majority of the inhabitants of Carmelo Peralta engage in cattle ranching, agriculture, fishing, tourism, handicraft sales, boat trips, and trade with Brazil.

== Demographics ==
The population comprises Paraguayan [[Criollo people|criollos]], indigenous people from the Ayoreo ethnicity, and Brazilian ranchers; it has a population of around five thousand inhabitants.

In addition to its main urban center, the municipality also includes more remote populations within its territory, such as:

* Puerto La Esperanza (formerly Puerto Sastre) with 800 inhabitants. 
* Colonia Monseñor Ángel Muzzolón with 50 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cae adolescente por crimen de una pareja - Judiciales y Policiales - ABC Color |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/judiciales-y-policiales/cae-adolescente-por-crimen-de-una-pareja-1760705.html |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=www.abc.com.py |language=es}}</ref>
* Puerto María Auxiliadora (Cucaani Indigenous Community) with 50 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-05-04 |title=El territorio del pueblo ayoreo soporta la mayor deforestación del mundo - Iniciativa Amotocodie |url=https://www.iniciativa-amotocodie.org/2021/05/04/el-territorio-del-pueblo-ayoreo-soporta-la-mayor-deforestacion-del-mundo/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |language=es}}</ref><ref name=":0" />

== Infrastructure ==
Currently, with the paving of route PY15 and the ongoing construction of the Bioceanic Bridge with Brazil, the district is rapidly developing, attracting many companies investing in the area, creating real estate developments, hotels, restaurants, warehouses, minimarkets, service stations, mechanical workshops, and retail stores for imported products, among others.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pantanal Inn: una puerta al mayor sistema de humedales del mundo en Alto Paraguay |url=https://infonegocios.com.py/infoviajes/pantanal-inn-una-puerta-al-mayor-sistema-de-humedales-del-mundo-en-alto-paraguay |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=infonegocios.com.py}}</ref>

The locality also has several public institutions, such as schools, colleges, health centers, a police station, a justice of the peace court, and churches, among others.

It has a small dirt airstrip about 1,300 meters long for regional air transport. There are plans to build a modern airport outside the city in the future, featuring an asphalt runway, which will serve as the main air terminal for cargo and passenger transport in the region.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ferreira |first=Noelia |date=2022-10-11 |title=Indert otorgó a la Dinac predio donde se levantará el futuro aeropuerto de Carmelo Peralta |url=https://www.ip.gov.py/ip/indert-otorgo-a-la-dinac-predio-donde-se-levantara-el-futuro-aeropuerto-de-carmelo-peralta/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=.::Agencia IP::. |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Planifican la instalación de moderno aeropuerto en el Chaco Paraguayo |url=https://infonegocios.com.py/plus/planifican-la-instalacion-de-moderno-aeropuerto-en-el-chaco-paraguayo |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=infonegocios.com.py}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Carmelo Peralta tendría aeropuerto de todo tiempo - Interior - ABC Color |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/interior/carmelo-peralta-tendria-aeropuerto-de-todo-tiempo-1826869.html |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=www.abc.com.py |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Duarte |first=Alba |date=2022-10-12 |title=Otorgan a la Dinac predio donde levantarán el moderno aeropuerto de Carmelo Peralta |url=https://npy.com.py/2022/10/otorgan-a-la-dinac-predio-donde-levantaran-el-moderno-aeropuerto-de-carmelo-peralta/ |access-date=2024-04-07 |website=Noticias Paraguay |language=es}}</ref>
