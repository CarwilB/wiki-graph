'''Sepahua''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in southern [[Atalaya Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is bordered by the [[Junín Region]] on the west, the district of [[Raymondi District|Raymondi]] on the north, the [[Echarate District]] on the east, and  national land  on the south.
