'''Arcahaie''' ({{langx|ht|Lakayè}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 198,551 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015 |date=March 2015 |website=IHSI |language=fr |trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |archive-date=2015-11-06 |url-status=dead |access-date=8 May 2017 }}</ref> Postal codes in the Arcahaie Arrondissement start with the number 64.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes: 
* [[Arcahaie]] 
* [[Cabaret, Ouest|Cabaret]]
