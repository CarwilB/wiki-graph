'''Jiménez''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Cartago Province|Cartago]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019 |archive-date=24 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240224063957/https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Juan Viñas District|Juan Viñas]] district.

== History ==
Jiménez was created on 19 August 1903 by decree 84.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
Jiménez has an area of {{cvt|286.43|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:862}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The Turrialba River forms the northern boundary of the canton of Jiménez, with the [[Reventazón River]] and [[Atirro River]] establishing the eastern border and the [[Pejibaye River]] delineating a major portion of the canton's border on the west.

==Economy==
Its main economic activity is the [[sugar cane]] plantations operated by the Ingenio Juan Viñas, which factory produces more than 20,000 tons of sugar annually.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.juanvinas.com/ingenio.html|title = Juan Viñas}}</ref>
