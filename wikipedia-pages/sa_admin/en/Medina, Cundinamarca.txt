'''Medina''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]].

==Climate==
Medina has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall in all months except January.
