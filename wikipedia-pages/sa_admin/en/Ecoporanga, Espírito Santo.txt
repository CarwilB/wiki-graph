'''Ecoporanga''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 22,835 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|2285|km2}}.<ref name="IBGE"/> It is located in north Espírito Santo near [[Bahia]] and [[Minas Gerais]].
