'''Olho d'Água Grande''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈojʊ ˈdaɡwɐ ˈɡɾɐ̃di]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 5,128 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/olho-dagua-grande/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|119|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
