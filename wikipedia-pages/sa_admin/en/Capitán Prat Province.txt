'''Capitán Prat Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Capitán Prat}}) is a [[Provinces of Chile|province]] in the [[Aysén Region]] of Chile. Its capital is [[Cochrane, Chile|Cochrane]]. Covering an area of {{cvt|37043.6|km2}}, it had a population of 4,610 inhabitants to the [[2024 Chilean census]]. It is named after [[Arturo Prat]].

==History==
The region was originally occupied by indigenous groups, with archeological evidence pointing to human habitation dating back 7,700 years. [[Cochrane, Chile|Cochrane]] was established as a settlement in 1929 by cattle ranchers, and was officially founded in March 1954.<ref name="MI">{{cite web|url=https://dppcapitanprat.dpp.gob.cl/conozca-la-provincia/|lang=es|title=Conozca la provincia|trans-title=Know the Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Cochrane">{{cite web|url=https://www.rutadelosparques.org/en/cochrane/|title=Cochrane|work=Ruta de los parques|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> The [[Aysén Region]] was established on July 10, 1974, by Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> The region is divided into four provinces&ndash;Capitán Prat, [[Aysén Province|Aysén]], [[Coyhaique Province|Coyhaique]] and [[General Carrera Province|General Carrera]], which are further divided into ten [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_de_aysen_del_general_carlos_ibanez_del_campo/|lang=es|title=Region de Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|trans-title=Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Capitán Prat Province is one of the four provinces of the [[Aysén Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It covers an area of {{cvt|37043.6|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> It is located in the southernmost end of the Aysén Region in the [[Chilean Patagonia]].<ref name="MI"/> About 45% of the land of the province is situated in the [[Baker River (Chile)|Baker]] and Easter River basins. Of the remaining land, 30% are coastal lands, and 25% consists of coastal islands.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/42|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Capitán Prat Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Capitán Prat Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 

The province is nicknamed as "Province of Glaciers" due to the presence of mountains, ice fields, and glaciers. Major glaciers include Calluqueo, Steffen, and Jorge Montt Glaciers.  Lake Cochrane National Reserve, and Patagonia National Park form part of the province. [[O'Higgins/San Martín Lake|O'Higgins Lake]], the deepest lake in the Americas, is located near the O'Higgins Glacier.<ref name="MI"/>

[[Cochrane, Chile|Cochrane]], located on the banks of the Baker River, is the capital of the province.<ref name="Cochrane"/><ref name="MI"/> Cochrane has a [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|8.97|C}}. The region receives approximately {{cvt|3.29|cm}} of rainfall annually on average.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/aysen/cochrane|title=Cochrane|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Capitán Prat is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into three [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'')&ndash;Cochrane, [[O'Higgins, Chile|O'Higgins]] and [[Tortel]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MN"/>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 4,610 inhabitants. The population consisted of 2,379 males (51.6%) and 2,231 females (48.4%). About 19.9% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 68.6% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 11.5% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 2,703 inhabitants (58.6%) and a rural population of 1,907 inhabitants (41.4%). Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 4,521 inhabitants (98.1%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 3,376 inhabitants (73.2%), while 1,234 inhabitants (26.8%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 1,955 adherents (53%), followed by 1,217 inhabitants (33%) indicating no religious affiliation, and [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 429 adherents (11.6%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/113__capit%C3%A1n_prat/|title=Capitan Prat Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
