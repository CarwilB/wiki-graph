'''Mogi Guaçu''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 153,658 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) in an area of {{cvt|813|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/233P3 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city is at an average elevation of {{cvt|591|m}} above sea level. Mogi Guaçu is a place name that probably originates from the [[Tupi language]]. It means "large river of snakes".<ref>[[Eduardo de Almeida Navarro|Navarro, E. A.]] ''[[Dictionary of Old Tupi|Dicionário de tupi antigo: a língua indígena clássica do Brasil]]''. São Paulo. Global. 2013. p. 588.</ref> The city was founded on April 9, 1877.

The municipality contains the {{convert|469|ha}} [[Mogi-Guaçu Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation area created in 1942.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Núcleo de Pesquisa Reserva Biológica de Mogi Guaçu|publisher=Secretaria do Meio Ambiente do Estado de São Paulo |url=http://botanica.sp.gov.br/mogi-guacu/|access-date=2016-04-28}}</ref>

The [[Autódromo Velo Città]] is a motorsport racetrack opened in 2012, located {{cvt|20|km}} northeast of the city of Mogi Guaçu.

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia Telefônica Brasileira|Companhia Telefônica Brasileira]] until 1973, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações de São Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-27|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-27|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
