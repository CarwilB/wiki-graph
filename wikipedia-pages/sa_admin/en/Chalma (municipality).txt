'''Chalma''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. The municipal seat is the village of [[Chalma, Veracruz]]. 

The municipality of Chalma covers a total surface area of {{cvt|199.05|km2}}.

===Settlements in the municipality===
*[[Chalma, Veracruz|Chalma]] (municipal seat; 2005 population 2,555)
*[[Chapopote]] (population 2,916)
*[[San Pedro Coyutla]] (1,396)
*El Pintor (925)
*[[La Laja (Chalma)|La Laja]] (360)
*[[Aquixcuatitla]] (200)

==Demographics==
In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 13,067, of whom 2,555 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chalma|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30055a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=2008-11-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070528163101/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30055a.htm|archive-date=2007-05-28|url-status=dead}}</ref> 
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 4,992 spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Nahuatl]].
