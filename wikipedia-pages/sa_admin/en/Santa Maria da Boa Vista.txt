'''Santa Maria da Boa Vista''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 42,782 and <!--, the density is /km²-->the area is 3000.77&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[São Francisco Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]] and [[Santa Cruz, Pernambuco|Santa Cruz]]   ('''N''');  Bahia state  ('''S''');  [[Orocó]]  ('''E''');  [[Lagoa Grande, Pernambuco|Lagoa Grande]]  ('''W''')
* Area - 3001.2&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 361 m
* Hydrography - [[Brigida River|Brigida]] and [[Garças River|Garças]] rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]].
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] ( [[Sertão]]) - hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 26.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 616&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Santa Maria da Boa Vista''' are commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]] and [[donkey]]s;  and plantations of irrigated [[grape]]s, [[mangoes]] and [[banana]]s.
