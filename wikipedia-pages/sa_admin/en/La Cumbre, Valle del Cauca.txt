'''La Cumbre''' (The Summit) is a town and municipality in [[Colombia]], northwest of [[Cali]], in the [[Valle del Cauca Department]]. It is located in the [[Cordillera Occidental (Colombia)|West Andes]] above the city of [[Yumbo]].

La Cumbre is known for its simplicity, lack of hustle and bustle and small-town way of life.  Many day tourists from Cali travel to La Cumbre by car or tourist train. The weather is cooler, and considered a welcome respite from the sunny and often hot weather of Cali. The most important industry in the area is agriculture.

== Polito-Geographical Limits ==

This municipality is surround by the following municipalities of Valle del Cauca Department:
* North: [[Restrepo, Valle del Cauca|Restrepo]].
* South: [[Yumbo]] and [[Cali]].
* East: [[Vijes]]
* West: [[Dagua]]

== Areas of the Municipality ==

The [[Corregimientos of Colombia|Corregimiento]] of Bitaco occupies the southernmost portion of the municipality. Within this area is the section called [[Chicoral, Valle del Cauca|Chicoral]] which is home to the Hindú Tea Plantation and the Bitaco Forest Reserve.  This area connects to the west with [[Dapa, Valle del Cauca|Dapa]] through a foggy, forested pass that crosses into the Cauca Valley.
