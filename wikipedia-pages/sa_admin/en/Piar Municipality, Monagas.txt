'''Piar Municipality''' is a municipality in [[Monagas State]], Venezuela.<ref>{{cite book|title=Minerals Yearbook, 2005, V. 3, Area Reports, International, Latin America and Canada|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=gPpxZeKwHaIC|date=2007-11-16|publisher=United States Department of the Interior|pages=16.2|quote=In June, the Venezuelan National Assembly approved the construction of a cement plant in the municipality of Piar in the State of Monagas.}}</ref> In the north it borders [[Caripe Municipality]].

== History ==

On 2021, Mariangellys Tillero was elected Major.<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-11-22|title=Distribución de alcaldías en Monagas: PSUV 9 y Oposición 4|url=https://elperiodicodemonagas.com.ve/monagas/distribucion-de-alcaldias-en-monagas-psuv-9-y-oposicion-4/|access-date=2022-01-30|website=El Periódico de Monagas Noticias de Maturín Monagas Venezuela|language=es}}</ref>

==Climate==
Minimum monthly temperature in the Piar municipality lies between 14.3 and 23.1&nbsp;°C, while maximum temperature is between 23.9 and 33.3&nbsp;°C. Total annual precipitation fluctuates between 1083 and 1898&nbsp;mm. Most rain falls between June and August, while the driest quarter comprises February to April.<ref>{{cite journal|last1=Ferrer-Paris|first1=José R.|title=Caracterización ambiental de la ruta de NeoMapas: NM17 ''Maturín'', estado Monagas (CNEB e21)|journal=Figshare|date=2017|doi=10.6084/m9.figshare.4745674}}</ref>
