'''Sayula''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the plains of the [[Sotavento zone]] in the central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 382&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface area of {{convert|640.76|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}. It is located at {{coord|17|53|N|94|57|W|}}.

==Geography==

The municipality of '''Sayula''' is delimited to the north by [[Acayucan (municipality)|Acayucan]], to the east by [[Oluta]] and [[Texistepec]], to the south by [[Jesús Carranza, Veracruz|Jesús Carranza]] and to the west by [[San Juan Evangelista]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30144a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Sayula|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15|archive-date=2007-02-28|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070228140934/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30144a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>  It is watered by rivers of the San Juan, which is a tributary of the river Papaloapan and for other tributaries of the Río Chiquito, and the brazo de Coatzacoalcos.

The weather in Sayula is warm  and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]], [[orange fruit]], [[coffee]], [[mango]] and [[green chile]].

==Celebrations==

In '''Sayula''', in May takes place the celebration in honor to San Isidro Labrador, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.

== Notable people ==

* [[Miguel Alemán Valdés]] (1900-1983), [[president of Mexico]] from 1946-1952
