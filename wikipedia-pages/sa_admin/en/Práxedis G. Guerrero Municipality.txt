'''Práxedis G. Guerrero''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Práxedis G. Guerrero, Chihuahua]]. The municipality covers an area of 808.97&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and stands on the [[US-Mexico border|US border]] close to [[Ciudad Juárez]].

==Name==
The municipality's name was given to it by the [[Congress of Chihuahua|State Congress]] in December 1933, to honor the [[Mexican Revolution|Revolutionary]] leader [[Práxedis G. Guerrero]], who was killed in action in [[Janos, Chihuahua]], on 30 December 1910. It was previously known as '''San Ignacio'''.

==History==
Originally San Ignacio or San Ignacio de Loyola, founded after the Guadalupe/Hidalgo Treaty by missionaries coming from central north east Nuevo Mejico, later known as New Mexico.

==Demographics==

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 4,799,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Praxedis G. Guerrero|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=053|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> dramatically down from 8,514 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Práxedis G. Guerrero|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08053a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 19, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223200906/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08053a.htm|archive-date=February 23, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/rutinas/ept.asp?t=mpob93&c=3839&e=08 Población total por municipio según grandes grupos de edad, 2005] [[INEGI]].</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Práxedis G. Guerrero had a population of 2,128.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Práxedis G. Guerrero, the municipality had 85 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 population in parentheses) was: [[El Porvenir, Chihuahua|El Porvenir]] (1,253), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

Práxedis G. Guerrero has a very low population that rarely grows. In fact, much of the population, above all those of school age, leave for nearby [[Ciudad Juárez]], due to the higher level of economic activity there.

===Climate and ecology===
The climate is extremely arid, with very high temperatures in the summer and very low temperatures in the winter, with extreme recorded temperatures of 43°C and -23°C. It has very low levels of precipitation.

Its flora are plants typical of the desert, and its fauna includes species such as the [[Cougar|puma]] and the [[coyote]].
