'''Bainet''' ({{IPA|fr|benɛ}}; {{langx|ht|Benè}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Bainet Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 86,755 inhabitants as of 2015.

== History ==
In late 1791 and early 1792, during the early [[Haitian Revolution]], slaves in Bainet rebelled as part of the Trou Coffy uprising led by [[Romaine-la-Prophétesse]].<ref>{{cite book| last = Rey | first = Terry | title = The Priest and the Prophetess: Abbé Ouvière, Romaine Rivière, and the Revolutionary Atlantic World | publisher = Oxford University Press | year = 2017 | isbn = 978-0190625849 | pp=36, 55 }}</ref>

The commune's administrative offices were destroyed as a result of the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]]. As a result, from 2010 to 2016, city officials worked in prefabricated premises funded by the [[United Nations Stabilisation Mission in Haiti]] (MINUSTAH). MINUSTAH ultimately funded the creation of a new town hall, which was inaugurated in October 2016.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-10-01 |title=Haiti - Reconstruction : New town hall in Bainet |url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-16284-haiti-reconstruction-new-town-hall-in-bainet.html |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Haiti Libre}}</ref>

== Notable person ==
The Haitian artist and photographer [[Gérald Bloncourt]] was born in Bainet.<ref>Eleanor Ingalls Christensen, ''The Art of Haiti'' (1975), page 64</ref>
