'''Ceguaca''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|61.2|km2}} and had a population of 4,948 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. It was officially established on 19 January 1895.

== History ==
"Ceguaca" means "place of corn cob holders" in [[Spanish language|Spanish]].<ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-ceguaca/|title=Municipality of Ceguaca|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality was officially established on 19 January 1895.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Ceguaca, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Ceguaca.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Ceguaca is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] to the north, [[San Francisco de Ojuera]] to the south, [[Concepción del Sur]] to the east, and [[Santa Rita, Honduras|Santa Rita]] to the west.<ref name="Red"/><ref name="Edu"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|61.2|km2}} and has a mean altitude of {{cvt|504|m}}.<ref name="Edu"/> 

== Administrative divisions ==
The municipality comprises five aldeas (villages) and their associated caseríos (hamlets).<ref name="INE2013">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/255-Santa-Barbara-Ceguaca.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 255, Municipio de Ceguaca , Departamento de Santa Bárbara|date=February 2025|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=20}}

== Demographics ==
At per the 2013 census, Ceguaca had a population of 4,948 inhabitants, of whom 2,583 (52.2%) were male and 2,365 (47.8%) were female, with a masculinity index of 109.2 men per 100 women. The entire population was classified as rural, with no urban population recorded in the census. The population density was 80.85 inhabitants per square kilometre, and the intercensal population growth rate between the 2001 and 2013 census was 1.6%.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1605__ceguaca/|title=Ceguaca|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

About 5.5% of the population were classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigneous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 34.3% of the population were aged 0–14, 58.5% were aged 15–64, and 7.2% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 22.1 years and the mean age was 27.3 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the 2013 census recorded an illiteracy rate of 25.8%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} The municipality had 1,156 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.2 persons per occupied dwelling.{{rp|page=23, 27}}
