'''La Democracia''' is a town in the state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]], [[Venezuela]]. It is part of the [[Lander, Venezuela|Lander Municipality]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Miranda, estado {{!}} Fundación Empresas Polar|url=https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/m/miranda-estado/|access-date=2021-03-17|website=bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org}}</ref> In 1875, Venezuelan lithographic printer [[Antonio Damirón]] donated land from his estate in Miranda to create the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Damirón, Antonio {{!}} Fundación Empresas Polar|url=https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/dhv/entradas/d/damiron-antonio/|access-date=2021-03-17|website=bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org}}</ref>
