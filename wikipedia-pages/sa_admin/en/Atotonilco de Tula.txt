'''Atotonilco de Tula''' is a city that serves as the municipal seat for one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]].  It is located in the southwestern part of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]]. The municipality lies at a southern pass leading out of the [[Mezquital Valley]], approximately 86 kilometers north of [[Mexico City]] and about 180&nbsp;km northeast of the state capital, [[Pachuca de Soto]]. 

The name comes from [[Nahuatl]] and means "place of [[hot spring]]s"; in the [[Otomi language]], it is called ''Padehe''. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|31|km2}}. As of the 2010 census, it had a total population of 31,078.<ref>{{cite web|title=Atotonilco de Tula |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13013a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=December 27, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061129064350/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13013a.htm |archivedate=November 29, 2006 }}</ref> It is now part of the [[Tula de Allende]] built-up (or metropolitan) area.

== Geography ==

It is located between the parallels  99° 27’ 51” and 99° 07’ 32”  west longitude, and 20° 05’ 35” and 18° 55’ 22” north latitude. Atotonilco borders. It covers a total surface area of {{cvt|192.7|km2}} at an altitude of 6,634&nbsp;ft.  In the year 2010 census by [[INEGI]], it reported a population of 17,055.<ref>[http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=13&mun=005 Atotonilco de Tula municipality] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160617182427/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=13&mun=005 |date=2016-06-17 }} SEDESOL, Catálogo de localidades.</ref>

The town of Atotonilco de Tula, the municipal seat, has governing jurisdiction over the following communities: Vito, Boxfi, and Progreso.<ref name="INEGI">{{cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |author=INEGI |title=Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005 INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=2008-10-25 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070328224939/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archivedate=2007-03-28 }}</ref> The total municipality extends 96.37 and borders with the municipalities of [[Tepeji del Río]], [[Tula de Allende]], [[Atitalaquia]], [[Ajacuba]] and the [[state of México]] ([[Huehuetoca]], [[Tequixquiac]] and [[Apaxco]]).

The municipal seat is in a small, elongated valley but most of the municipality is inside of the [[Mezquital Valley]].{{cn|date=July 2018}} The highest [[mountain]] the Picacho in the [[Sierra Tezontlalpa]], it rises {{convert|2800|m}} above sea level,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tequixquiac.gob.mx/medio-fisico/|title=Medio Físico – Ayuntamiento de Tequixquiac|website=www.tequixquiac.gob.mx|access-date=2016-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161129171743/http://www.tequixquiac.gob.mx/medio-fisico/|archive-date=2016-11-29|url-status=dead}}</ref> on the border between the municipalities of San Agustín Tlaxiaca and [[Atitalaquia]].

===Flora & fauna===
Atotonilco de Tula municipality is a rural territory of Central Mexican Plateau, here there is a diversity in plants and animals of semi-desertic climate (Mezquital Valley).

The native animals are [[cacomistle]], [[skunk]], [[gopher]], [[bobcat]], [[falcon]], [[Virginia opossum]], [[rabbit]], [[Mexican gray squirrel]], [[Domesticated turkey|turkey]], [[Violetear|colibri]], [[turkey vulture]], [[northern mockingbird]], [[rattlesnake]], [[Pituophis|pine snake]], xincoyote, [[red warbler]], [[rufous-crowned sparrow]], [[lesser roadrunner]], [[great horned owl]], [[frog]], [[toad]], [[red ant]], [[bee]], and others.
