'''General Sánchez Cerro''', known as '''Puquina''' until 1936, is the smallest of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Moquegua Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inei.gob.pe INEI] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/19970412040139/http://www.inei.gob.pe/ |date=1997-04-12 }}</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.muniperu.net IPAM]</ref> The capital of the province is Omate. The province was named after the former Peruvian army officer and president [[Luis Miguel Sánchez Cerro]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[Arequipa Region]]
*'''East''': [[Puno Region]]
*'''South''': [[Mariscal Nieto Province|province of Mariscal Nieto]]
*'''West''': [[Arequipa Region]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], however, is the language which the majority of the population (60.36%) learnt to speak in childhood, 37.45% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language and 1.98% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
