The '''Campo Elías Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 100,192.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Ejido, Mérida|Ejido]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Campo Elías Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
==Demographics==
The Campo Elías Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 100,192 (up from 84,476 in 2000).  This amounts to 11.9% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|164.5|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Campo Elías Municipality is Jesús Alberto Rodríguez Velázquez (Toto), elected on July 27, 2025 with 87% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D12%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180929125405/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D12%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |archive-date=2018-09-29 |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=2008-03-25 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into seven [[parish]]es; Fernández Peña, Matriz, Montalbán, Acequias, [[Jaji, Venezuela|Jají]], La Mesa, and San José del Sur.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
