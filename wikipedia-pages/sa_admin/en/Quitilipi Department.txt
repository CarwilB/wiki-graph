'''Quitilipi''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 32,000 inhabitants in an area of  1.545 km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Quitilipi]], which is located some 1.160 km from the [[Buenos Aires|City of Buenos Aires]].
