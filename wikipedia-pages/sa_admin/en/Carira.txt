'''Carira''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kaˈɾiɾɐ|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 22,239 (2020) and its area is {{convert|636.5|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Carira has a population density of 34 inhabitants per square kilometer. Carira is located {{convert|112|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/>
