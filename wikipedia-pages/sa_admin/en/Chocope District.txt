'''Chocope''' is a district of the [[Ascope Province]], in the [[La Libertad Region]] of [[Peru]].

In 1940 there were 1,302 people in Chocope.<ref>Columbia-Lippincott Gazetteer. (New York: Columbia University Press, 1952) p. 403</ref>
