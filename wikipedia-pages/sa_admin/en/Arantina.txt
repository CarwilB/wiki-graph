'''Arantina''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 2,787 people living in a total area of {{Cvt|89.382|km2}}. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of Sul and Sudoeste de Minas and to the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of [[Andrelândia]].

The municipality has an average annual median temperature of 21.2&nbsp;°C and the vegetation is predominantly that of the South American [[Atlantic Forest]]. With 98% of the population living within the urban area, the city had two health facilities in 2009. The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) of the municipality is 0.736, considered average in relation to the state.

The settlement of the municipality first began in the second half of the 19th century, with the construction of a stretch of the [[Estrada de Ferro Oeste de Minas]] railway. Notable features of the municipality include the handcrafts it produces and annual events such as the [[Carnival]] of Arantina.

==History==
[[File:Arantina.JPG|thumb|[[Level crossing]] in Arantina.]]
The area of the current municipality first began to be settled during the 19th century, when the [[Estrada de Ferro Oeste de Minas]] (EFOM) railroad was being constructed. The railroad was intended to link the south of [[Goiás]] state with the [[Angra dos Reis]] municipality in [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] state, passing through [[Barra Mansa]], [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|RJ]] and operating both passenger and cargo trains. In Arantina there was a junction between the EFOM line and the Barra Line (the name is derived from [[Barra do Piraí]] municipality), connecting [[Bom Jardim de Minas]] municipality to the Barra Mansa line, now defunct.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_linha_barra/bomjardim.htm|title=Bom Jardim de Minas|website=Railway Stations of Brazil|access-date=2 October 2010|language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Arantina (antina Arantes) |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_tronco/arantina.htm |website=Railway Stations of Brazil |access-date=2 October 2010|language=pt}}</ref>

A small town emerged around the Arantes railway station, then situated in the municipality of Mantiqueira (today [[Bom Jardim de Minas]]). For several years the town that is today Arantina belonged to Bom Jardim de Minas. The town was known as Várzea do Paiol. In the current seat of the municipality there was a storehouse to store the corn harvested by farmer Juca Pereira, owner of much of the municipality's land.<ref name=":0">{{cite web|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/minasgerais/arantina.pdf|title=Arantina - Histórico|website=Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Brazil|access-date=22 July 2013|language=pt}}</ref>

The town consolidated itself with the manufacturing of [[ceramic]]s. Given the economic growth, by state law no. 1058 of 31 December 1943, the district of Bom Jardim de Minas was created, which received its name from the railway station. Arantina was emancipated by state law no. 2764 of 30 December 1962, being officially incorporated on 1 March 1963.<ref name=":0" />

== Geography ==
[[File:Grota, Arantina MG - panoramio.jpg|thumb|Hilly terrain in the rural zone.]]
The area of the municipality, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) is 89.42&nbsp;km²,<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/arantina/panorama|title=Arantina|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=21 March 2019}}</ref> with 0.3682&nbsp;km² constituting the urban area. It is situated at a latitude of 21°54'40"S and a longitude of 44°15'21"W and is 323&nbsp;km south of [[Belo Horizonte]], the state capital of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its neighboring municipalities are [[Bom Jardim de Minas]] to the east, [[Andrelândia]] to the northwest, and [[Liberdade, Minas Gerais|Liberdade]] to the south.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/arantina.htm|title=Arantina - MG|website=Cidades.net|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013|archive-date=9 September 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130909215908/http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/arantina.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

According to the regional division of 2017, instituted by the IBGE,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html|title=Regional Division of Brazil|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=21 March 2019}}</ref> the municipality belongs to the Immediate and Intermediate Geographic Region of Juiz de Fora.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls|title=Database of the Municipalities of the Immediate and Intermediate Geographic Regions of Brazil|date=2017|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=10 February 2018}}</ref> Before this time, the municipality was included in the Microregion of Andrelândia and the Mesoregion of South and Southwest Minas.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip|title=Brazilian Territorial Division 2016|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=21 March 2019}}</ref>

=== Terrain and Hydrography ===
The terrain of the municipality of Arantina is predominantly hilly. Approximately 60% of the territory is covered in hilly areas, while about 30% is more mountainous, and the remaining 10% is generally flat. The highest elevation is 1,379 m found at the Serra do Caxambu and the lowest elevation is 1,030 m at the [[base level]] of the Capixingui stream in the Turvo Pequeno river. The central part of the town is at 1,050 m.<ref name=":1" />

The territory is covered in various small rivers and streams, principally the Capixingui stream and the Turvo Pequeno river, making up part of the [[Rio Grande (Paraná River)|Rio Grande]] river basin.<ref name=":1" /> The Turvo Pequeno divides the municipality, and there are drainage projects for its sub-basin. Much of the water extracted from it is supplied to the towns of Arantina and [[Andrelândia]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.correiodopapagaio.com.br/arantina/noticias/deputado_duarte_bechir_visita_arantina|title=Deputy Duarte Bechir visits Arantina|date=11 July 2013|work=Correio do Papagaio|access-date=22 July 2013|language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.abaixoassinado.org/abaixoassinados/8326|title=Revitalization and Preservation of the Rio Turvo Pequena in Andrelândia|date=27 February 2011|website=Abaixo Assinado|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref>

=== Climate ===
The climate of Arantina is characterized, according to the IBGE, as [[Tropical climate|tropical]] (type [[Subtropical highland|Cwb]] according to the [[Köppen climate classification]]).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif|title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> It has an average annual temperature of 21.2&nbsp;°C with dry and cool winters and rainy summers with mild temperatures.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima|title=Climate|date=6 January 2010|website=Brazilian Government|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://archive.today/20111010113851/http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima|archive-date=10 October 2011|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg|title=Brazil - Climates|website=IBGE Library|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130620052525/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg|archive-date=20 June 2013|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> The hottest month, January, has an average temperature of 24.1&nbsp;°C, average high temperature of 29.4&nbsp;°C and average low temperature of 18.9&nbsp;°C. The coldest moth, July, has an average temperature of 17.3&nbsp;°C, with 24.8&nbsp;°C and 9.8&nbsp;°C as the average high and low temperatures respectively. Autumn and spring are transitional seasons.<ref name="Jornal do Tempo">{{cite web |url=http://jornaldotempo.uol.com.br/climatologia.html/Arantina-MG/ |title=Climatology of Arantina - MG |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia |access-date=October 2, 2010 |language=pt}}</ref>

The average annual precipitation is 1412.9&nbsp;mm, with August being the driest month when there is only 13.9&nbsp;mm of rainfall. In December, the rainiest month, the average rainfall is 259.8&nbsp;mm.<ref name="Jornal do Tempo" /> In recent years, however, hot and dry days during the summer have become more frequent and it is not rare for the temperature to exceed 28&nbsp;°C, especially between July and September. In August of 2010, for example, the precipitation in Arantina was 0&nbsp;mm.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://snirh.ana.gov.br/Aplicativos/grafico/graficoChuva.php?tipo=Chuvas&estacao=2144010&ano=2010&mes=8&xtela=1024&ytela=738|title=Rainfall - Daily Averages - 08/2010|website=Agência Nacional de Águas (ANA)|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://archive.today/20111102194438/http://snirh.ana.gov.br/Aplicativos/grafico/graficoChuva.php?tipo=Chuvas&estacao=2144010&ano=2010&mes=8&xtela=1024&ytela=738|archive-date=2 November 2011|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> During the dry season and long [[Indian summer]]s in the rainy season, [[Wildfire|brush fires]] are commonly recorded in the hills, principally in the rural zone of the municipality, which contribute to [[deforestation]] and to the release of [[Air pollution|pollutants]] into the atmosphere, harming the quality of the air.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.sbpcnet.org.br/livro/63ra/resumos/resumos/6186.htm|title=Mapeamento das áreas municipais queimadas na região da Bacia Hidrográfica do Rio Paraibuna MG/RJ através do processamento digital de imagens do satélite Landsat 5|date=15 July 2011|website=Sociedade Brasileira Para o Progresso da Ciência (SBPC)|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.acessa.com/cidade/arquivo/noticias/2013/02/05-prefeitos-da-regiao-participam-de-evento-sobre-prevencao-de-incendio/|title=Prefeitos da região participam de evento sobre prevenção de incêndio|last=Moreira|first=Andréa|date=5 February 2013|website=Acessa|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref>

=== Flora and Fauna ===
The vegetation of the town is divided into two classes of native ecological formations: the forest ([[Atlantic Forest]]) and the open clearings. Exotic vegetation and [[monoculture]]s also occur. These classes, associated with the rugged topography, result in a diverse vegetation cover.<ref>{{Cite book|last=Teixeira|first=Felipe Faria|title=Curso de Turismo|year=2012|location=Bom Jardim de Minas|language=pt}}</ref> In the Atlantic Forest, trees such as the [[Tabebuia]], [[Dalbergia nigra|Brazilian rosewood]], angico, [[Tibouchina mutabilis|manacá-da-serra]], [[Araucaria angustifolia|Brazilian pine]], and [[Cedrus|cedar]] stand out.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://biomaurbano.webnode.com/news/arvores-tipicas-da-mata-atlantica/|title=Árvores típicas da Mata Atlântica|date=24 July 2009|website=Bioma Urbano|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120711082141/http://biomaurbano.webnode.com/news/arvores-tipicas-da-mata-atlantica/|archive-date=11 July 2012|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref>

In 2010, the population of the municipality was calculated by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]] (IBGE) (''Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics'') to be 2,823 inhabitants. According to the census of that year, 1,415 inhabitants were men and 1,408 were women. Also according to the same census, 2,633 inhabitants lived in the urban zone and 190 in the rural zone.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_minas_gerais.pdf|title=Censo 2010 - Minas Gerais|date=29 November 2010|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120106235254/http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_minas_gerais.pdf|archive-date=6 January 2012|access-date=11 December 2010}}</ref> According to statistics divulged in 2018, the municipal population was 2,804 inhabitants.<ref name=":2" />

In 2010, according to data from the IBGE census of that year, the racial composition of Arantina was 2,196 white (77.79%), 216 black (7.65%), 1 yellow (Asian) (0.04%), and 410 brown (multiracial) (14.52%).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=19&i=P&c=3145|title=opulação de Arantina por raça e cor|website=Sidra (Sistema IBGE de Recuperação de Dados Automática)|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> As for the [[Regions of Brazil|region]] of birth, 18 were born in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region]] (0.63%), 2,792 in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]] (98.91%), and 5 in the [[South Region, Brazil|South Region]] (0.18%). 2,404 inhabitants were native to the [[States of Brazil|State]] of [[Minas Gerais]] (90.3%) and, of this total, 1,382 were born in Arantina (48.96%).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=5&i=P&c=1505|title=Tabela 1505 - População residente, por naturalidade em relação ao município e à unidade da federação - Resultados Gerais da Amostra|date=2010|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=5 August 2013}}</ref> Among the 367 natives of other units of the [[Brazil|federation]], [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] was the state with the greatest presence at 270 people (9.58%). Following was [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], with 60 residents (2.13%), and [[Espírito Santo]] with 6 residents (0.20%).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=5&i=P&c=631|title=Tabela 631 - População residente, por sexo e lugar de nascimento|date=2010|website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|access-date=6 August 2013}}</ref>

The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) of Arantina is considered medium by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP), being a value of 0.697 (the 240th highest in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]). Most of the town's indices are close to or above the national average according to the UNDP. The education index was 0.641, life expectancy was 0.834, and the median income was 0.633.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.pnud.org.br/arquivos/ranking-idhm-2010.pdf|title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil|date=29 July 2013|website=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130806022754/http://www.pnud.org.br/arquivos/ranking-idhm-2010.pdf|archive-date=6 August 2013|access-date=8 August 2013}}</ref> According to the IBGE, in the year 2003, the [[Gini coefficient]], which measures economic inequality, was 0.39, with 1.00 being the worst number and 0.00 being the best.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=310360&nomemun=Arantina|title=Indicadores sociais dos municípios brasileiros|website=Cidades - IBGE|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> In that year, the incidence of poverty as measured by the IBGE was 42.15% with the lower limit of the incidence of poverty being 29.79%, the higher limit of incidence being 27.35%, and the subjective incidence being 36.50%.<ref name=":3" />

According to the data of the 2010 census released by the IBGE, the population of Arantina is composed of: 2,553 [[Catholic Church|Catholics]] (90.42%), 214 [[Protestantism|Protestants]] (7.58%), 35 [[Irreligion|non-religious]] (1.24%), 7 [[Kardecist spiritism|spiritualists]] (0.26%), and 0.5% divided among other religions.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=14&i=P&c=2094|title=Tabela 2094 - População residente por cor ou raça e religião|date=2010|website=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (Sidra)|language=pt|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref>

== Politics and Administration ==
The administration of the municipality is carried out by executive and legislative powers. The executive power is exercised by the [[Mayors in Brazil|Mayor]], aided by his cabinet of secretaries. The current mayor of Arantina is Francisco Carlos Ferreira Alves, of the [[Democrats (Brazil)|Democrats (DEM)]], elected in the municipal elections of 2016 with 52.14% of the vote and sworn in on 1 January 2017 with Deputy Mayor Adriano by his side.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/caca/|title=Cacá 25|date=2 October 2016|website=Elections 2016|language=pt|access-date=21 March 2019}}</ref> The legislative power, on the other hand, is constituted by the Municipal Chamber composed of nine [[Alderman|Aldermen]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.eleicoes2012.info/candidatos-vereador-arantina-mg/|title=Candidatos a Vereador Arantina/MG|date=7 October 2012|website=Elections 2012|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303183609/http://www.eleicoes2012.info/candidatos-vereador-arantina-mg/|archive-date=3 March 2016|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref> It is up to this body to draft and vote on laws fundamental to administration and to the executive, especially the [[Participatory budgeting|participatory budget]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074|title=O Poder Público Municipal à frente da obrigação constitucional de criação do sistema de controle interno|last=Lima|first=Flávio Henrique M.|date=8 February 2006|website=JusVi|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111115120521/http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074|archive-date=15 November 2011|access-date=22 July 2013}}</ref>
