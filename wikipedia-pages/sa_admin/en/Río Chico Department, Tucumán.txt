'''Río Chico Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 52,925 (2001) and an area of 585&nbsp;km². The seat of the department is in [[Aguilares, Tucumán|Aguilares]].
