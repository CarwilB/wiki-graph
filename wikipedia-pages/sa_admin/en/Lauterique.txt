'''Lauterique''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[La Paz department, Honduras|La Paz]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Lauterique municipality had a population of 2,986. Of these, 99.80% were [[Mestizo]], 0.13% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.07% [[White Latin Americans|White]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
