'''Ibarama''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1848|ha}} [[Quarta Colônia State Park]], created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Quarta Colônia – SEMA}}
 |title=Parque Estadual Quarta Colônia|publisher=SEMA: Secretaria do Ambiente e Desenvolvimento Sustentável
 |url=http://www.sema.rs.gov.br/parque-estadual-quarta-colonia|access-date=2017-01-20}}</ref>
The municipality is partly flooded by the reservoir of the [[Dona Francisca Hydroelectric Plant]] on the upper [[Jacuí River]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Dona Francisca – OSAB}}|title=Dona Francisca|publisher=OSAB: Observatório Sócio-Ambiental da Baragens|url=http://www.observabarragem.ippur.ufrj.br/barragens/9/dona-francisca|access-date=2017-01-20|archive-date=2019-05-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190523184533/http://www.observabarragem.ippur.ufrj.br/barragens/9/dona-francisca|url-status=dead}}</ref>
