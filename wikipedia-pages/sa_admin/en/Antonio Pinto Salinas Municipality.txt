The '''Antonio Pinto Salinas Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 26,073.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls Poblacion] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The town of [[Santa Cruz de Mora]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Antonio Pinto Salinas Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip Merida] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
[[File:Vistapanoramicadesantacruz.JPG|thumb|220px|left|Santa Cruz de Mora.]]
The Antonio Pinto Salinas Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 26,073 (up from 23,905 in 2000).  This amounts to 3.1% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls Poblacion] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|74.9|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls Poblacion] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
{{update section|date=October 2023}}
The mayor of the Antonio Pinto Salinas Municipality is Jesús Alexis Rodríguez, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 55% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=11&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio 1625|website=gob.ve|access-date=6 October 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Capital Antonio Pinto Salinas, Mesa Bolívar, and Mesa de Las Palmas.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
