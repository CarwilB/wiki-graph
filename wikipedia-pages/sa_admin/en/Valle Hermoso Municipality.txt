'''Valle Hermoso Municipality''' (Beautiful Valley) is one of the [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipalities]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. The seat is at the city of [[Valle Hermoso, Tamaulipas|Valle Hermoso]]. At the census of 2010 the city had a population of 48,918 inhabitants, while the municipality had a population of 63,170.  The municipality has an area of 916.43&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its largest other localities are the towns of [[Anáhuac, Tamaulipas|Anáhuac]] in the northeastern part of the municipality and [[El Realito]] to the west of the city of Valle Hermoso.
