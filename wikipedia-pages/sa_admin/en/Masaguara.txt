'''Masaguara''' ({{IPA|es|masaˈɣwaɾa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Masaguara municipality had a population of 15,892. Of these, 88.16% were [[Mestizo]], 9.87% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (9.65% [[Lenca]]), 1.72% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.23% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.02% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
