'''Hato Rey Central''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], Puerto Rico. It is one of three barrios commonly known together as [[Hato Rey]]. With a population density of 16,155.3 per square mile. It has a land area of 1.03 sq mi and a [[2010 United States Census|2010 Census]] population of 16,640.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico: 2010:population and housing unit counts | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

Hato Rey Central was a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] of the former municipality of [[Rio Piedras, Puerto Rico|Río Piedras]], before it was merged with San Juan in 1951.

It is bounded by [[Hato Rey Norte]] to the west, by barrio [[Universidad, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Universidad]] to the south, by [[Oriente, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Oriente]] to the east, and by [[Santurce, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Santurce]] to the north. The [[Caño Martín Peña]] separates Hato Rey Central from Santurce.

== Landmarks and places of interest ==
* [[Martín Peña Bridge]] (shared with [[Hato Rey Norte]]), [[Art Deco]]-style bridge that connects Hato Rey Central with [[Santurce, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Santurce]].
* ''[[Milla de Oro]]'', the [[central business district]] of San Juan.
* [[Polytechnic University of Puerto Rico]]'s main campus.

== Transportation ==
Although none of the [[Tren Urbano]] stations are technically located in Hato Rey Central, the [[Piñero station|Piñero]], [[Domenech station|Domenech]], [[Roosevelt station (Puerto Rico)|Roosevelt]] and [[Hato Rey station|Hato Rey]] metro stations are located very close (in some cases even steps away) to the barrio. The area is also served by the [[Puerto Rico Metropolitan Bus Authority|Metropolitan Bus Authority]] (AMA).
