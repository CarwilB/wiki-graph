'''Padre Carvalho''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  In 2020 the population was 6,423 in a total area of {{cvt|450|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/padre-carvalho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1997.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/josenopolis/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204104459/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/josenopolis/ |archive-date=2008-02-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Geography==
Padre Carvalho is located in the foothills of the Serra do Espinhaço mountains southwest of  [[Salinas, Minas Gerais|Salinas]] at an elevation of 750 meters.  It is north of the Rio Jequitinhonha and just east of the Rio Vacaria, a tributary of the Jequitinhonha.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Grão Mogol]], [[Josenópolis]], [[Rubelita]], [[Fruta de Leite]] e [[Rio Pardo de Minas]].

Padre Carvalho is part of the statistical microregion of Grão Mogol.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080224162737/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |archive-date=2008-02-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising on a modest scale (2,400 head in 2006) and [[subsistence agriculture]].  The GDP in 2005 was R$ 11,849,000.  The most important agricultural crops are bananas, coffee, peanuts, sugarcane, and corn.  In the rural area, there were 281 producers.  The total area of agricultural land was 10,000 hectares in 2006, of which 2,200 ha. were in crops.  As of 2006, there were only 02 tractors.  In the urban area, there were no banking agencies in 2006.<ref name="IBGE"/>
