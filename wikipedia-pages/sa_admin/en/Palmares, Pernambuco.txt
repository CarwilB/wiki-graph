[[File:Casa de Cultura Palmares.jpg|thumb|right|240px|Palmares Cultural House]]
'''Palmares''' is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], with 63,500 inhabitants, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]].<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/palmares/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Its main activity is the combined field of agriculture and livestock ({{langx|pt|agropecuária}}). It received its name from the [[Portuguese people]] who named it that due to the number of palm trees in the area where run-away slaves had created approximately 16 [[quilombo]]s, led by [[Zumbi]].

* Distance to [[Recife]] - 120&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Palmares''' are based in industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]] especially, [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and creations of [[cattle]], [[horse]]s and [[mule]]s.
