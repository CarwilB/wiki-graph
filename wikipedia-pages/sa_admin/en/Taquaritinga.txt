'''Taquaritinga''' is a city in the Central North area of the State of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 57,364 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|594|km2}}.<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/528 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The population is formed mostly by [[Europe]]an descendants ([[Italy|Italians]], [[Spain|Spaniards]], and [[Portugal|Portuguese]]). [[Afro-Brazilian]]s and [[Japanese-Brazilian]]s are important minority groups.

The climate is tropical of altitude with dry mild winter and hot rainy summer. The economy of the city is based in agrobusiness (sugarcane, orange, lemon, and fruits) and services. The city has an old and regionally famous music school (Conservatório Santa Cecília), an ETEC (Technical School), and three colleges. The most important of them, [[São Paulo State Technological College|FATEC]], is a public one with courses on technology. [[Clube Atlético Taquaritinga]], founded in 1942, is the [[football (soccer)|football]] club of the city. The club plays its home matches at [[Taquarão|Estádio Adail N. da Silva]], which has a maximum capacity of approximately 20,000 people.

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_Telefônica_Brasileira|Companhia Telefônica Brasileira]] until 1973, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-27|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-27|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

==Notable people==
Taquaritinga was the birthplace of:

* [[José Paulo Paes]] (b. July 22, 1926 - d. October 9, 1998) was a poet, translator, literary critic and essayist.
* [[Valmir (footballer, born 1969)|Valmir]] Furlani (b. 1969), footballer
* [[Luiz Araújo (footballer)|Luiz Araújo]] (b. 1996): footballer for [[Atlanta United]]

The Catholic church in the municipality is part of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Jaboticabal]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.catholic-hierarchy.org/country/r10br.html|title=Sul 1 Region of Brazil [Catholic-Hierarchy]|access-date=2025-03-30|website=www.catholic-hierarchy.org}}</ref>

These denominations are growing more and more throughout Brazil.
