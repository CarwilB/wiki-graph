'''Caibaté''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 4,823 (2020 est.) in an area of 259.66&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/XOZ Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The name comes from the [[Tupi language]].  It is located 524&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
