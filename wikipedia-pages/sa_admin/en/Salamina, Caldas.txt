'''Salamina''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]].  It is located north of the department and has a township called San Felix. It was declared a national monument and historic heritage of mankind.
[[File:Parque principal salamina.JPG|thumb|left|Main Park in Salamina]]
Salamina was founded in 1825 by Fermin Lopez,  Francisco Marulanda and others. Salamina is known as the "city of light" because there Caldas has produced many poets, musicians, actors and writers. It is also known as "Mother of Nations" because it was one of the centers from where the founders and first settlers of [[Neira, Caldas|Neira]], [[Aranzazu, Caldas|Aranzazu]], [[Manizales]], Santa Rosa de Cabal, Pereira, Armenia, among others parted. It was elevated to a municipality in 1861.

It has a population of about 20,000 inhabitants, an area of 40,354 hectares and an average temperature of 22&nbsp;°C (71.6&nbsp;°F). Its economy is based on coffee, dairy and livestock.

Salamina was named a ''[[Pueblo Patrimonio (Colombia)|Pueblo Patrimonio]]'' (heritage town) of Colombia in 2010. It is the only municipality in the ''[[Colombian coffee growing axis|Eje Cafetero]]'' that was selected to be part of the ''Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio'' original cohort.<ref>{{Cite web|date=Dec 2020|title=GUÍA: Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia|url=https://fontur.com.co/sites/default/files/2020-12/GUIA_PUEBLOS_PATRIMONIO.pdf|access-date=29 Mar 2021|website=Fondo Nacional de Turismo de la República de Colombia - FONTUR}}</ref> In addition, the town was named part of the "Coffee Cultural Landscape" [[UNESCO]] [[World Heritage Site]] in 2011.<ref name = "unesco" >{{cite web |title = Coffee Cultural Landscape |url = http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1121 |website = UNESCO World Heritage List |publisher = United Nations Educational, Scientific, and Cultural Organization |access-date = 25 Apr 2011}}</ref>
