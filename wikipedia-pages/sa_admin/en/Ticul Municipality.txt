'''Ticul de Morales Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. It is located in the western part of the state at ({{coord|20|24|N|89|32|W|type:city}}) 100&nbsp;km south of the state capital city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]]. The municipality, which has an area of {{convert|355.12|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}, in the 2005 census reported 25,621 inhabitants. The city of [[Ticul]] which is the municipal seat had a population of 21,147, the ninth-largest community in the state in population. The majority are ethnically [[Maya peoples|Maya]]. Its largest other towns are [[Pustunich]] and [[Yotholín]].
