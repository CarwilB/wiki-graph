'''San Miguel District''' is one of thirteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[San Miguel Province|San Miguel]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> San Miguel was founded by [[Francisco Pizarro]] in 1532 and is thus the oldest European town in Peru.<ref>Edward Hyams and George Ordish, ''The Last of the Incas'' (London: Longmans, 1963), 139.</ref>
