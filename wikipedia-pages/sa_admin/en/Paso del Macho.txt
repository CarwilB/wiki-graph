'''Paso del Macho''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the central zone of the state, about {{cvt|70|km|mi}} from the state capital, [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of {{convert|323.26|km2}}. It is located at {{coord|18|58|N|96|43|W|}}.

==Geography==

The municipality of Paso del Macho is delimited to the north by [[Zentla Municipality|Zentla]] to the east by [[Carrillo Puerto (municipality)|Carrillo Puerto]] to the south by [[Cuitláhuac, Veracruz|Cuitláhuac]], to the west by [[Atoyac (municipality)|Atoyac]] and to the north-west  by [[Tepatlaxco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30125a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111222700/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30125a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=11 January 2013|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Paso del Macho|website=e-local.gob.mx|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=Spanish|access-date=12 June 2009}}</ref> The municipality is located in the central zone of the state; its soil presents some irregularities without importance.

The weather in Paso del Macho is warm and dry  all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[sugarcane]], [[coffee]] and [[mango]].

==Celebrations==

In November takes place the celebration in honor to San Martín{{which|date=September 2020}}, patron of the town, and on the 12th of December takes place the celebration in honor of the [[Our Lady of Guadalupe|Virgen de Guadalupe]]<!-- !2nd of December in all of Mexico -->.
