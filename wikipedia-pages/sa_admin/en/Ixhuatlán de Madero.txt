'''Ixhuatlán de Madero''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north zone of the state, about 376&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]], in the region called Huasteca Baja. It is one of 212 municipalities in Veracruz. It has an area of 598.81&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|20|41|N|98|01|W|}}.
==Geography==
The municipality of ''' Ixhuatlán de Madero ''' is bordered on the north by [[Chicontepec de Tejeda]], on the east by [[Temapache]], on the south by [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]] State and [[Puebla]] State, and on the west by [[Tlachichilco]] and [[Benito Juárez (municipality of Veracruz)|Benito Juárez]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30083a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ixhuatlán de Madero|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=9 June 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070509031516/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30083a.htm|archive-date=9 May 2007|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

The area's climate is warm and rainy, with more rain in summer and autumn.
==Products==

Its principal agricultural products are [[maize]], [[beans]], [[watermelon]], [[green chile]], [[coffee]] and [[orange fruit]].
==Culture==
The town's main celebrations occur in May (5-15) to note the Batalla de Puebla, and in July to honor to San Cristobal, the town's Patron Saint.
