'''Itapuca''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]].

It is located at 28º53'43" S latitude and 52º02'12" W longitude, with an elevation of 660 meters above sea level. Its population in 2020 was estimated at 2,065 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/itapuca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It has a surface area of 184.48&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
