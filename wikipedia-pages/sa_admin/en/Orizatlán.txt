'''Orizatlán''' (officially: '''San Felipe Orizatlán''') is a town in the north of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]]. It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding [[municipalities of Hidalgo|municipality]] of the same name.

The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|323.59|km2}}<ref name=medio>{{cite web |title=Medio Físico |url=https://orizatlan.gob.mx/tu-municipio/medio-fisico |website=Municipio de San Felipe Orizatlán |access-date=21 October 2025}}</ref> and, in the 2020 census, reported a population of 38,492, up from 38,472 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Felipe Orizatlán: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/san-felipe-orizatlan |website=Data México |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=21 October 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=San Felipe Orizatlán |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13046a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=27 December 2008}}</ref>
It is located on Hidalgo's borders with [[Veracruz]] and [[San Luis Potosí]].<ref name=medio/>

==Toponymy==

Its name is due in its first part to the patron saint [[Philip of Jesus|San Felipe de Jesús]] and in the second part to the Greek root ''oryza'' (rice) and the [[Nahuatl]] ''tlan'' (place), so it is understood that it means "place of rice".<ref group=note>The etymological origin of the name of a municipality can have different interpretations or have an uncertain origin, this is the most common or most accepted by the municipal government and its residents, according to the ''Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Mexico.''</ref><ref name="etym">{{cite web|author= State Center for Municipal Studies of the State of Hidalgo|year=1988|title=The Municipalities of Hidalgo|series=Encyclopedia Collection of the municipalities of Mexico}}</ref><ref name=gob>{{cite web|title=San Felipe Orizatlán|url=http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/san_felipe_orizatlan|work=[[Municipalities of Hidalgo]]|accessdate=22 March 2017}}</ref>

==History==

The first inhabitants of the area were Huasco{{clarify|date=October 2025}} indigenous people who settled in the area before the conquest. Originally, San Felipe Orizatlán was [[Indian political society]] belonging to the jurisdiction of [[Huejutla]]. The municipal seat was founded in the 16th century by indigenous people and Spaniards, thus carrying out an evangelization. In 1870, the state of Hidalgo declared Orizatlán a [[Municipal seat|municipal seat]].
