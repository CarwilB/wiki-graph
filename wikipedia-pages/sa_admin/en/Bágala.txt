'''Bágala''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Boquerón District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|43.3|sqkm|sqmi}}, and had a population of 5,113 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|118.0|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,653; its population as of 2000 was 2,038.<ref name=inec/>
