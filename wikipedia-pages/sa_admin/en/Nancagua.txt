'''Nancagua''' ({{IPA|es|naŋˈkaɣwa}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Colchagua Province]], [[O'Higgins Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] conducted by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Nancagua spanned an area of {{convert|111.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and had 15,634 inhabitants (7,959 men and 7,675 women). Of these, 9,264 (59.3%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,370 (40.7%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 8.5% (1,220 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Nancagua is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2021-2024 alcalde is Mario Bustamante.
