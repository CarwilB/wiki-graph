'''Lo Espejo''' is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]], southwest of [[Santiago Centro]]. It is the most [[population density|densely populated]] commune in the country.

==Demographics==
According to the 1999 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Lo Espejo spans an area of {{convert|7.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 112,800 inhabitants (55,478 men and 57,322 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 6.1% (7,275 persons) between the 1990 and 1999 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The 2006 population was projected at 106,819 persons, a 5.3% decline.<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |accessdate=13 September 2010 |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archivedate=23 August 2010 }}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=September 2010}}

==Administration==
As a commune, Lo Espejo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcaldesa is [[:es:Javiera_Reyes_Jara|Javiera Reyes Jara]] ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]]).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcaldesa |url=https://www.loespejo.cl/alcaldesa/ |access-date=2022-03-16 |website=Municipalidad Lo Espejo |language=es}}</ref> The communal council has the following members:<ref>{{Cite web |title=Concejo Municipal |url=https://www.loespejo.cl/concejo-municipal/ |access-date=2022-03-16 |website=Municipalidad Lo Espejo |language=es}}</ref>
* Carolina Sagredo Ugarte ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Elizabeth Henríquez Leiva ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Francisca Ganga Zúñiga (Ind/[[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Javiera López Layana ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Cecilia Benavides León ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Carolina Sagredo Ugarte ([[Social Green Regionalist Federation|FRVS]])
* Carlo Acosta de la Fuente ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Lorena Galleguillos Oliva (Ind/[[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Lo Espejo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pedro Browne ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Guillermo Teillier ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) as part of the 28th electoral district (together with [[Pedro Aguirre Cerda, Chile|Pedro Aguirre Cerda]] and [[San Miguel, Chile|San Miguel]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Soledad Alvear]] ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]]) and [[Pablo Longueira]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
