'''Piaçabuçu''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[piɐˈsɐbʊˈsu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. It is the southernmost municipality in Alagoas, and lies near both [[São Francisco River]] and the [[Atlantic Ocean]]. Its population was 17,848 (2020) and its area is 240&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php] - [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]]</ref>
