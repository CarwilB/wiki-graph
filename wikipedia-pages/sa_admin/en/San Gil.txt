'''San Gil''' is a town and municipality in the [[Department of Santander]] in northeastern [[Colombia]], located roughly 300&nbsp;km (192&nbsp;mi) from [[Bogotá]] and 95&nbsp;km from the department's capital, [[Bucaramanga]]. As of 2020, San Gil had a population of 51,874 within the urban area, making it the third largest urban area in the department, after Bucaramanga and [[Barrancabermeja]]. Founded in 1689, San Gil is over 300 years old. It was officially named the tourist capital of the region in 2004, thanks to its outdoor activity opportunities such as [[rafting]], [[caving]], [[kayaking]] and [[hiking]].

[[Image:Sangil1.jpg|thumb|right|View of the main square of San Gil, Santander, 1999.]]

== History ==
San Gil's history goes back to pre-Columbian times, when it was inhabited by native [[indigenous peoples|indigenous]] people called the [[Guane people|Guanes]]. Spanish conquest during the colonial period nearly eradicated the local tribes. The town was officially founded on March 17, 1689, by Don Gil Cabrera Dávalos and Leonardo Correa de Betancourt. According to official sources, San Gil played an important role during the [[Colombian Declaration of Independence|Colombian independence]] period. "Comuneros" rebels came from nearby regions, united, traveled to the capital, Bogota, and fought for the nation's independence, a process which occurred during the early 1820s.<ref name=histmain>{{cite web|last=San Gil Municipality Official Page|title=Historia de San Gil (History of San Gil)|url=http://www.sangil.gov.co/sangilturistica/historia.htm}}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Demographics ==

San Gil's population, as of 2005, was 44,959, (including the rural area). The majority (36,748) reside in the urban area. The population as of 2020 was estimated to be 59,670,<ref name="Censo"/><ref name="pop"/> an increase of roughly 14,500 people, but showing a tendency to decrease in rural areas, decreasing from 6,240 to a projected 5,601.
Ninety-eight percent of houses have access to electricity, 90.3% to [[Tap water|running water]], and 84.1% to a [[sewage system]].<ref name=dane>{{cite web|last=Dane|title=Boletín Censo General 2005|url=http://www.dane.gov.co/files/censo2005/PERFIL_PDF_CG2005/68679T7T000.PDF|accessdate=13 April 2012}}</ref>

== Tourism ==

In 2004, San Gil was named the tourist capital of the region.<ref name="histmain"/><ref name=editref>{{cite web|last=San Gil Tourism Secretary|title=San Gil, Tierra de Aventura|url=http://www.sangil.com.co/|accessdate=12 April 2012}}</ref>  The area offers several outdoor activity opportunities, such as rafting (grade 1–5), kayaking, hiking, and caving.<ref name=legitrese>{{cite web|last=Moomazza|title=Adventure Capital of Colombia...San GIl|url=http://journals.worldnomads.com/moomazza/story/71780/Colombia/Adventure-capital-of-ColombiaSan-Gil|accessdate=12 April 2012}}</ref> Local companies offer rafting packages through various rivers depending on skill level. Parque el Gallineral is a popular destination with its characteristic moss-covered trees resembling beards. The park has a number of paths running through its 10-acre area and by the city's main river, Rio Fonce.<ref name=gallineralref>{{cite web|last=Caputo|first=Lorraine|title=Parque Natural El Gallineral, City Park|url=http://www.vivatravelguides.com/south-america/colombia/eastern-colombia/san-gil/san-gil-activities/parque-natural-el-gallineral/|accessdate=12 April 2012}}</ref> The park's name comes from the 1867 characteristic ''gallinero'' trees planted throughout the whole area. San Gil's main square is called ''Parque la Libertad'' (Liberty Park), which is the most common meeting place in the town and a hub for night life. The town's main cathedral, built in 1791 and remodeled in 1965, is located in this area. Parque Nacional del Chicamocha (Chicamocha National Park or PANACHI) is another Eco-tourist park located roughly 1 hour away from San Gil, placed on a scenic spot in the Chicamocha [[Canyon]]. The park has a museum, hiking trails, rafting, [[paragliding]] and other outdoor activities. It also has a 6.3&nbsp;km (3.91&nbsp;mi) long [[Aerial lift|cable car]], one of the longest in the world in its category,<ref name=teleferico>{{cite web|last=Unknown|title=Teleférico sobre el Cañón del Chicamocha. Impresionante obra en un bello lugar|url=http://www.noticiascaracol.com/nacion/galeria-teleferico-sobre-el-canon-del-chicamocha-impresionante-obra-un-bello-lugar|publisher=Noticias Caracol (News Channel Official Page)|accessdate=26 April 2012}}</ref> offering a ride across the canyon and into the [[plateau]] called Mesa de los Santos.
Barichara, a small colonial town roughly 20 minutes away from San Gil, is another popular destination.  Situated at the rim of the Chicamocha Canyon, it offers a scenic view across the canyon and the Suarez River.  It is known for its well preserved colonial architecture and stone streets.<ref name=link>{{cite web|last=Colombia Link|title=San Gil|url=http://www.colombialink.com/01_INDEX/index_turismo/destinos/sangil.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070428202844/http://www.colombialink.com/01_INDEX/index_turismo/destinos/sangil.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=April 28, 2007|publisher=Colombia Link|accessdate=26 April 2012}}</ref>

== Economy ==

Besides tourism, the region's economy relies heavily on [[agriculture]].  Products such as [[tobacco]], [[sugar cane]] and [[coffee]] are commonly grown in farms nearby<ref name=economiaref>{{cite web|last=San Gil Municipality|title=Economía del Municipio ( Municipality's Economy)|url=http://www.sangil.gov.co/sangilturistica/economia.htm|accessdate=13 April 2012}}</ref>  which are mainly commercialized in the town's main market plaza.
One of the region's most historically important entrepreneurs was  José María Rueda Gómez, known as "Conde de Cuchicute", who, in spite of his eccentricity, pushed the development of coffee production, and helped establish the first bank and a hydroelectric power plant for the region.<ref name=conde>{{cite web|last=Rodríguez, Gómez|first=Juan Camilo|title=Conde de Cuchicute|url=http://www.banrepcultural.org/blaavirtual/biografias/conde-de-cuchicute|publisher=Biblioteca Virtual Luis Angel arango|accessdate=26 April 2012}}</ref>

== Transport ==

San Gil is very well connected to the rest of the country. By air, it is connected through  "[[Los Pozos Airport|Los Pozos]]" a small regional airport for chartered flights and seasonal commercial flights

By land Berlinas del Fonce, Copetran, Omega, Reina and Cootrasangil among other companies ensure connectivity with Bogota and Bucaramanga.
