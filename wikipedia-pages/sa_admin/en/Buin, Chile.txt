'''Buin''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]], in the [[Maipo Province]], Metropolitan Region of Santiago, which forms part of [[Greater Santiago, Chile|Greater Santiago]]. It is a city composed of the towns of [[Maipo, Chile|Maipo]], Viluco, Linderos, Valdivia de Paine, Alto Jahuel, Los Guindos, El Recurso, Olate City, Buinsky city and Campusano. Buin is located about {{convert|35|km|0|abbr=on}} south of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]] in the [[Maipo Valley]] wine region.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Buin spans an area of {{convert|214.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 63,419 inhabitants (31,440 men and 31,979 women). Of these, 53,506 (84.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,913 (15.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 20.1% (10,627 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> In the 2017 census, Buin's population had increased to 96,614, with 47,575 men and 49,039 women.<ref name="bcn">{{cite web |title=Buin Reporte Comunal |url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/reportescomunales/comunas_v.html?anno=2021&idcom=13402 |publisher=Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile |access-date=27 December 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Buin is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Ángel Bozán Ramos (IND).<ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:
* Patricio Silva Gonzalez ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Hernán Henriquez Parrao ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Miguel Araya Lobos ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Ramon Calderón Hormazábal ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Sandra Meneses Tapia ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Nicolás Romo Contreras ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Buin is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Ramón Farías ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and José Antonio Kast (UDI) as part of the 30th electoral district, (together with [[San Bernardo, Chile|San Bernardo]], [[Paine, Chile|Paine]] and [[Calera de Tango]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín (PPD) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
