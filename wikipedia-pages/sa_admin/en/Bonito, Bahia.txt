== History ==
The municipality of Bonito, located in the [[Chapada Diamantina]] region, originated in the late 19th century. At the time, a road connected neighboring towns, and the area where the city now stands became a resting point for travelers, thanks to the presence of a large tree that provided shade. This place, which initially served as a shelter, was gradually inhabited by immigrants, forming a small community.<ref name=":0">{{cite web|access-date=2025-01-11 |title=História de Bonito Bahia |url=https://www.alliferschool.com/2021/04/historia-de-bonito-bahia.html}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

At the beginning of the 20th century, the town consisted of just a few residences. The holding of a regular fair, organized by the residents, marked the local economic development, reducing the dependence on travel to neighboring towns. Over time, the town continued to grow, attracting new residents from other regions.<ref name=":0" />

Agriculture was a key factor in the development of the area, especially coffee cultivation. The introduction of modern cultivation techniques and adaptation to local conditions allowed the consolidation of a promising agricultural activity. Financing projects and technical analyses ended up favoring the expansion of plantations, which contributed significantly to economic and population growth.<ref name=":0" />

Religion also played an important role in the formation of the community. Masses and religious celebrations marked the strengthening of social ties, culminating in the construction of a chapel with the collective support of the inhabitants.<ref name=":0" />

It was created as a district with the name of Bonito (former village) by State Law No. 4,031 of May 14, 1982, and integrated into the municipality of [[Utinga]].

It was elevated to the category of municipality with its current name by State Law No. 5,021 of June 13, 1989, separated from the municipality of Utinga. The municipality was established on January 1, 1990, with the inauguration of the first councilors and the first mayor, with his deputy, democratically elected.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2018-10-31 |title=Histórico do município (IBGE) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/bonito/historico |work=www.ibge.gov.br}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

== Geography ==
It is located at [[Latitude]] 11º58'10" [[South]] and [[Longitude]] 41º15'57" [[West]], at an elevation of 990 meters.

=== Hydrography ===
[[File:Cachoeira da Cacique.jpg|left|thumb|Image of Cacique Waterfall, located near the village of Botafogo, Bonito.]]
The municipality is located in the Paraguaçu River basin and is bathed by the Bonito, Tijuco, das Lajes and Sohen Rivers.<ref>{{cite web |title=Bonito-BA |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_municipais/estimativas_populacionais/2014/BA/bonito_2014_v1.pdf |access-date=11 January 2025 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |language=Brazilian Portuguese |format=PDF}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=October 2005 |title=DIAGNÓSTICO DO MUNICÍPIO DE BONITO |url=https://rigeo.sgb.gov.br/bitstream/doc/16796/1/Rel_Bonito.pdf |access-date=11 January 2025 |website=Serviço Geológico do Brasil |format=PDF}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Bonito Chapada Diamantina, Bahia, Cirquito Chapada Norte, Guia de turismo de Bonito :: Ibicoara Chapada Diamantina |url=https://www.ibicoarachapadadiamantina.com.br/products/bonito-chapada-diamantina-bahia-cirquito-chapada-norte-guia-de-turismo-de-bonito/ |access-date=2025-01-12 |website=www.ibicoarachapadadiamantina.com.br}}</ref> It is also rich in [[waterfall]]s, with highlights including the ''Soltinha Waterfall'' and the ''Cacique Waterfall'', tourist attractions in the region.

== Political-Administrative Organization ==
The [[Municipalities of Brazil|Municipality]] of Bonito has a political-administrative structure composed of the [[Executive (government)|Executive Branch]], headed by a [[Mayor]] elected by [[universal suffrage]], who is directly assisted by municipal secretaries appointed by him, and the [[Legislature|Legislative Branch]], institutionalized by the [[Câmara municipal|City Council]] of Bonito, a [[collegiate body]] representing the citizens that is composed of 11 councilors also elected by universal suffrage.<ref>MEIRELLES, Hely Lopes. ''Direito municipal brasileiro''. 18. ed. São Paulo: Malheiros, 2017.</ref>
