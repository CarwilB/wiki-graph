'''Golfito''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Puntarenas Province|Puntarenas]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019 |archive-date=24 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240224063957/https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Golfito District|Golfito]] district.

== History ==
Golfito was created on 10 June, 1949 by decree 532.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
Golfito has an area of {{cvt|1753.96|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:24}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The canton encompasses the southernmost Pacific coast of Costa Rica, north from Punta Gorda Hill at the [[Panama]] border, on the east side of the [[Gulf of Dulce|Golfo Dulce]].

== Districts ==
The canton of Golfito is subdivided into the following [[Districts of Costa Rica|districts]]:
* [[Golfito District|Golfito]]
* [[Guaycará District|Guaycará]]
* [[Pavón District|Pavón]]

[[Puerto Jiménez District|Puerto Jiménez]] was the second district of the canton, which was segregated to become [[Puerto Jiménez (canton)|Puerto Jiménez]] canton on 8 April 2022.
