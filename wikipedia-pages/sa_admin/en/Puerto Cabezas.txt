'''Puerto Cabezas''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpweɾ.to ka.ˈβe.sas}}; also known as '''Bragman's Bluff''' in [[English language|English]], or '''Bilwi''' in [[Miskito language|Miskito]]) is a municipality and city in [[Nicaragua]]. It is the capital of [[Miskito people|Miskito nation]] in the [[North Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region]].<ref>{{Cite book |last=Arghiris |first=Richard |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=NaV8AwAAQBAJ&dq=Bilwi&pg=PA289 |title=Nicaragua Footprint Handbook |date=2014-04-10 |publisher=Footprint Travel Guides |isbn=978-1-907263-89-7}}</ref>

==Name==
Puerto Cabezas is Bilwi in [[Miskito language|Miskito]].{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=68}} The area was called Bragman's Bluff by English traders.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=71}}

==History==
The earliest known mention of Puerto Cabezas was by an English pirate in the 17th century. The village was one of eight English settlements in the region before being evacuated in 1787.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=71}} In 1849, a British vice-consul and King [[George Augustus Frederic]] visited the area during their travels of the [[Mosquito Coast]].{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=72}} Puerto Cabezas was a fishing village prior to the arrival of American companies.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=68}}

Bragman’s Bluff Lumber Company established Puerto Cabezas as a [[company town]].{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=108}} The company received a grant of 50,000 hectares of land, which were inhabited by [[Miskito people]], from the Nicaraguan government{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=109}} on 28 January 1921.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=70}} The United States maintained a consulate in Puerto Cabezas from 1931 to 1940.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=110}}

The deep water port of Puerto Cabezas was a transportation hub in the area and superior to the shallow water port of [[Bluefields]].{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=121}} The United States launched the [[Bay of Pigs Invasion]] from Puerto Cabezas.{{sfn|Pineda|2006|p=126}}

[[Hurricane Felix (2007)|Hurricane Felix]] hit Puerto Cabezas on September 4, 2007, killing about 100 people.<ref>{{cite news| title=Deadly hurricanes lash central America |work=[[Agence France-Presse]] |url=http://afp.google.com/article/ALeqM5iIG4Rl9AaW-bYPhvI7U_3IcSTZKA |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071209035326/http://afp.google.com/article/ALeqM5iIG4Rl9AaW-bYPhvI7U_3IcSTZKA |archive-date=9 December 2007}}</ref> [[Hurricane Eta]] hit the city on November 3, 2020, causing extensive damage.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.cnbc.com/2020/11/03/hurricane-eta-slams-into-northeast-nicaragua-as-category-4-storm.html|title=Hurricane Eta pounds Nicaragua as Category 4 storm|publisher=[[CNBC]]|website=cnbc.com|date=November 3, 2020|access-date=November 3, 2020}}</ref>

The majority of the inhabitants speak the Miskito language.<ref>{{cite journal |last=Jamieson |first=Mark |title=Linguistic Innovation and Relationship Terminology in the Pearl Lagoon Basin of Nicaragua |journal=[[Journal of the Royal Anthropological Institute]] |publisher=[[Wiley-Blackwell]] |date=1998 |volume=4 |issue=4 |doi=10.2307/3034829 |jstor=3034829 |url=https://www.jstor.org/stable/3034829 |url-access=subscription|page=718}}</ref>

==Climate==
Puerto Cabezas has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]] ''Am'') with significant rainfall year round, and a short dry season in March and April. Even so, these months see an average rainfall of {{convert|48|mm|in|1}} and {{convert|54|mm|in|1}}.<ref name="hko" /> The average temperature ranges from {{convert|24.5|C|F|1}} in February to {{convert|27.8|C|F|1}} in May.<ref name="hko" /> The average annual rainfall is {{convert|2799|mm|in|0}}, while 198 days receive measurable rain during an average year.<ref name="hko" />
