'''Hualhuas District''' is one of twenty-eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huancayo Province|Huancayo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It is located approximately 7 kilometers north of [[Huancayo]] and is heavily [[Indigenous peoples of Peru|native]].

According to [[José María Arguedas]] in 1975, all of the town of Hualhuas was dedicated to the [[Andean textiles|textile weaving]] of rugs and tapestries, be it "casually or professionally". This business is sold to customers in urban areas and tourists.<ref name="Formación de una cultura nacional indoamericana">{{cite book |author1=[[José María Arguedas]] |title=Formación de una cultura nacional indoamericana |page=135 |url=https://www.google.com/books/edition/Formaci%C3%B3n_de_una_cultura_nacional_indoa/ |access-date=19 January 2024}}</ref>

As of 2016, it was ruled by a mayor and five [[regidores]].<ref name="BdPp">{{cite web |title=Municipalidad del Distrito de Hualhuas |url=http://bancodepoliticosperuanos.com/bppe/municipalidad-del-distrito-de-hualhuas/ |website=Banco de Politicos peruanos |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160805020433/http://bancodepoliticosperuanos.com/bppe/municipalidad-del-distrito-de-hualhuas/ |archive-date=5 August 2016}}</ref>
