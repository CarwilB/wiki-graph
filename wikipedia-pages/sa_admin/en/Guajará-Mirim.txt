'''Guajará-Mirim''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rondônia]]. It is located at an [[altitude]] of 128 meters. Its population was 46,556 (2020) and its area is 24,856&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6SU]</ref>

==Geography==
===Location===

Guajará-Mirim lies along the [[Mamoré River]], just across the [[Bolivia]]n border town of [[Guayaramerín]]. It was once the southern terminus of the [[Madeira-Mamoré Railroad|Estrada de Ferro Madeira-Mamoré]] (the Madeira-Mamoré Railway), which was inaugurated in 1912.  It is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Guajará-Mirim]].

===Conservation===

The municipality contains the {{convert|22540|ha|adj=on}} [[Traçadal Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected area that was created in 1990.{{sfn|REBIO do Traçadal – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains 2.33% of the {{convert|216568|ha|adj=on}} [[Guajará-Mirim State Park]], created in 1990.{{sfn|PES de Guajará-Mirim – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains the {{convert|46438|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Ouro Preto Biological Reserve]], created in 1990.{{sfn|REBIO do Rio Ouro Preto – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains 73.45% of the {{convert|204632|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Ouro Preto Extractive Reserve]], also created in 1990.{{sfn|RESEX do Rio Ouro Preto – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
The municipality contains 47.5% of the {{convert|146400|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Cautário State Extractive Reserve]], created in 1995.{{sfn|RESEX Rio Cautário (Estadual) – ISA}}
It contains the {{convert|73818|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Cautário Federal Extractive Reserve]], created in 2001.{{sfn|RESEX do Rio Cautário – ISA |loc=Informações gerais}}

== Consular representation ==
[[Bolivia]] has a Consulate in Guajará-Mirim, Brazil, whereas Brazil has a Consulate in neighboring [[Guayaramerín]], Bolivia.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cancilleria.gob.bo/webmre/node/505|title=Representación de Bolivia en el exterior|website=Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores|language=es|access-date=12 October 2024}}</ref>
