''' Acuña''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ciudad Acuña]], which contained over 98% of the [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality's]] population in 2010. The [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] covers an area of 11,487.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is located on the [[U.S.-Mexico border|international border between Mexico and the USA]], here formed by the [[Río Bravo del Norte]] (Rio Grande), adjacent to the U.S. state of [[Texas]].

In 2015, the [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] had a total population of 181,426 inhabitants. Acuña is currently the fastest growing city in Mexico.<ref>[http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/default.aspx?src=487&e=5] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141106213934/http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/default.aspx?src=487&e=5 |date=2014-11-06 }},"Censo de Población y Vivienda 2010:
  Acuña, Coahuila de Zaragoza," accessed November 6, 2014</ref>
