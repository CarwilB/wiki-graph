'''Guadalupe y Calvo''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Guadalupe y Calvo]]. The municipality covers an area of 9,165.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 53,499,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guadalupe y Calvo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=029|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304092926/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=029|url-status=live}}</ref> up from 51,854 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Guadelupe y Calvo|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08029a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal|accessdate=September 28, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120124015244/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08029a.htm|archive-date=January 24, 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Geography==

===Towns and villages===
As of 2010, the town of Guadalupe y Calvo had a population of 5,816.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Guadalupe y Calvo, the municipality had 1,416 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Baborigame, Chihuahua|Baborigame]] (3,294), classified as urban, and [[Atascaderos, Chihuahua|Atascaderos]] (1,559), [[Las Yerbitas (Aserradero), Chihuahua|Las Yerbitas (Aserradero)]] (1,200), and [[Turuachi, Chihuahua|Turuachi]] (1,131), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
