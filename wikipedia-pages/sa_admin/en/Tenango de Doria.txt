'''Tenango de Doria''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|210.7|km2|sp=us}}. 

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 17,503.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tenango de Doria|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/hidalgo/13060__tenango_de_doria/|website=CityPopulation.de|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística Geografía e Informática, México |access-date=February 22, 2022}}</ref> In 2017 there were 5,030 inhabitants who spoke [[Indigenous languages of Mexico|an indigenous language]],<ref>{{citation |language=es |work=Sistema Integral de Información del Estado de Hidalgo|title=Información Estadística Básica: Carpeta Municipal Tenango de Doria|url=http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/060%20Tenango%20de%20Doria.pdf|publisher=Secretaria de Planeación Desarrollo Regional Regional y Metropolitano|access-date=22 March 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171230230158/http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/060%20Tenango%20de%20Doria.pdf|archive-date=30 December 2017}}</ref> primarily [[Sierra Otomi]] and [[Nahuatl]].<ref>{{cite book|language=es|last1=Báez Cubero|first1=Lourdes|last2=Garret Ríos|first2=Gabriela|last3=Pérez González|first3=David|last4=Moreno Alcántara|first4=Beatriz|last5=Fierro Alonso|first5=Ulises Julio|last6=Hernández García|first6=Milton Gabriel|chapter=Los pueblos indígenas de Hidalgo|title=Atlas etnográfico|date=2012|edition=First|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia|location=México, D.F; México|isbn=978-607-484-357-6|pages=32–35|url=http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|archive-date=25 July 2019|access-date=14 May 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190725220343/http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The municipality is located to the east of Hidalgo between the parallels 20° 15' and 20° 25' of north [[latitude]]; the meridians 98° 05' and 98° 20' of west [[longitude]]. Its elevation is between {{convert|700|and(-)|2800|m|sp=us}} [[Height above mean sea level|above sea level]].<ref name="geo compendio">{{Citation |date=2010 |title=Compendio de información geográfica municipal 2010 |chapter=Tenango de Doria; Hidalgo |url=https://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/13/13060.pdf |access-date=March 16, 2024 |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (INEGI) |language=es}}</ref> This municipality has an area of {{convert|176.61|km2|sp=us}} and accounts for 0.85% of the state’s area, within the geographical region known as Sierra de Tenango.<ref>{{Citation |date=2017-01-18 |title=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |chapter=Estado de Hidalgo: Regionalización – Hidalgo |chapter-url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/regionalizacion.html |access-date=2024-03-16 |language=es |archive-date=2017-01-18 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20170118044857/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/regionalizacion.html}}</ref>

== Toponymy ==
The name ''Tenango'' (or {{lang|nah|Tenanco|}}) is of [[Nahuatl]] origin and means 'in the place of walls', from {{langx|nah|tenamitl||wall}} and the [[locative]] element {{lang|nah|co}}. The  suffix ''de Doria'' was added in honor of {{ill|Juan Crisóstomo Doria|es}}, the first governor of the state of Hidalgo.<ref>{{Cite book |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=L6NRMwEACAAJ |title=Historia de las divisiones territoriales de los municipios del Estado de Hidalgo |date=2007 |publisher=Instituto de Estudios Legislativos |language=es}}</ref>

=== Symbols ===
The municipality's emblem represents a wall with upward projections that resemble battlements, with a lower wall with four circles.<ref>{{Citation |title=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |chapter=Estado de Hidalgo: Tenango de Doria, Hidalgo |chapter-url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/municipios/13060a.html |access-date=2024-03-18  |archive-date=2019-11-02 |language=es |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20191102083647/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/municipios/13060a.html }}</ref> 

== Geography ==
=== Terrain ===
Tenango de Doria is located within the provinces of [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] (99.0%) and the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] (1.0%); within the subprovince of Carso Huasteco (99.0%), Plains and Sierras of Querétaro and Hidalgo (1.0%). Its [[terrain]] is mainly [[Mountain chain|sierra]] ([[Montane ecosystems|mountainous]]) (98.0%), with some [[plain]]s (1.0%), and [[plateau]]s (1.0%). Of the higher elevations found in the municipality, the {{lang|es|cerros}} (hills) of Estribo, Brujo, Debosda el Crío, Macho and the Cuchilla are all at higher than {{convert|1000|m|sp=us}} above sea level.<ref name="E delos M">{{Citation |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios |chapter=Tenango de Doria |publisher=Gobierno del Estado de Hidalgo |chapter-url=http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/files/tenango_de_doria.pdf |access-date=2024-03-18 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20170420143933/http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/files/tenango_de_doria.pdf |archive-date=2017-04-20 |language=es}}</ref> 

Its [[geology]] corresponds to the [[Jurassic]] period (34.0%), [[Cretaceous]] period (33.0%), [[Neogene]] period (31.64%), and [[Triassic]] period (1.0%).<ref name="geo compendio" /> For [[igneous rock]] types, the percentages are as follows: acidic tufts (29.64%), and basalt (3.0%). For [[sedimentary rock]] types: limestone (33.0%), sandstone shale (27.0%) limestone-shale (6.0%), and sandstone conglomerate (1.0%). Regarding [[edaphology]], the soil is classified mainly as [[luvisol]] (86.64%), [[regosol]] (5.0%), and [[cambisol]] (8.0%).<ref name="geo compendio" />

=== Hydrology ===
This municipality is located in the [[hydrology|hydrological]] region of Tuxpan-Nautla (97.0%), and of the [[Pánuco River]] (3.0%); in the basins of the Tuxpan River (76.0%), Cazones River (21.0%), and the Moctezuma River (3.0%); in the sub-basin of the [[Pantepec River]] (76.0%), the San Marcos River (21.0%), and [[Metztitlán]] River (3.0%). 

The water streams that flow through this area are: <ref name="E delos M" /> {{hlist|Tenango |{{lang|es|la Ardilla}} ('the Squirrel') |San Francisco| {{lang|es|Agua Grande}} ('large water') |Carrizal |Arenal |Camarones |Cuarco |{{lang|es|los María}} ('the María') |{{lang|es|Cerro Viejo}} ('old hill') |{{lang|es|Cerro Blanco}} ('white hill') |{{lang|es|Pie del Cerro}} ('foot of the hill') |and Mesillas.}}

== Demographics ==
According to the results presented by the Census of Population and Housing 2020 from [[National Institute of Statistics and Geography]] (INEGI), the municipality has a total of 17,503 inhabitants, with 8,395 men and 9,108 women. There is a [[population density]] of {{convert|99.1|PD/km2|sp=us}}, with half of the population being 27 years or younger. There are 92 men for every 100 women.<ref>{{Cite book |editor=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (INEGI) |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Hidalgo: Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |trans-title=Socio-demographic overview of Hidalgo: Population and Housing Census 2020 |publisher=Gobierno del Estado de Hidalgo |language=es |url= https://inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825197865.pdf}}</ref>

The percentage of the population that speaks an [[indigenous language]] is 26.36%. Mainly [[Sierra Otomi]] is spoken in this municipality (98.6%), with some people speaking [[Nahuatl]] (0.9%). The percentage of the population that is considered [[Afro-Mexicans|Afro-Mexican]] or [[Afro-Descendant|Afro-descendant]] is 1.61%.

== Arts and culture ==
[[File:Tenango Dishes.jpg|thumb|Tenango embroidery style on clay dishes]]
Tenango de Doria is renowned for its vibrant [[Tenango  embroidery]] and textiles. Tenangos (or tenancos) are art pieces which are a part of the municipality's history. It is most notable as it has spread to other parts of Mexico, and the world. Not only that, but the Tenango embroidery style has expanded to be applied to other art mediums. Local artisans in Tenango de Doria have used these colorful designs and applied to them to "[[Ceramic art|barro]]" (clay) dishware, such as plates and teapots.<ref>{{cite news |date=20 April 2023 |title=Tenango de Doria: A cultural gem of Mexico's Sierra Madre |url=https://www.theyucatantimes.com/2023/04/tenango-de-doria-a-cultural-gem-of-mexicos-sierra-madre/ |access-date=20 October 2023 |work=The Yucatán Times}}</ref>

Tenango de Doria's center of community is found in its [[Town hall|municipal hall]] that has been renovated over the years. This building decorated with flora and fauna that is depicted in the traditional Tenango style reflects the municipality's identity and community. There are large, colorful, Tenango de Doria letters that are set up in front of the city hall's entrance and library. The street that the city hall is located at is also where weekly "plazas" (markets) are set up: each Sunday, vendors set up their stalls and townspeople join to buy products. These goods range from small toys and ice cream, to fresh fruit and meat, to sponges and shoes.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Remodelación de la plaza principal de Tenango de Doria |url=https://m.facebook.com/AyuntamientoTenango/videos/remodelaci%C3%B3n-de-la-plaza-principal-de-tenango-de-doria/916046205947598/ |access-date=2024-03-18 |website=m.facebook.com |language=en}}</ref>

Other notable features and touristic attractions in this town are the Church of San Agustín, built in 1891, and the {{lang|es|cerros}} (hills) Brujo, el Arco, and el Cirio.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tenango de Doria Hidalgo |url=http://hidalgotierramagica.jimdofree.com/destinos/corredor-de-los-4-elementos/tenango-de-doria/ |access-date=2024-03-19 |website=Hidalgo Tierra Mágica |language=es-ES}}</ref>
