'''Cáqueza''' ({{IPA|es|kaˈkesa}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The town, located on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], is {{convert|39|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. The municipality borders [[Fosca, Cundinamarca|Fosca]] in the south, [[Ubaque]] in the north, [[Une]] and [[Chipaque]] in the west and [[Quetame]] in the east.<ref name=websiteCaqueza>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20140310084902/http://www.caqueza-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cáqueza]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Cáqueza comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Region or enclosure without forest".<ref name=websiteCaqueza/>

== History ==
The area of Cáqueza in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] and Cáqueza was ruled by the ''[[zipa]]'' based in [[Bogotá|Bacatá]].

Modern Cáqueza was founded on October 23, 1600 by Luis Enríquez.<ref name=websiteCaqueza/>

== Economy ==
Main economic activities in Cáqueza are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Important agricultural products are [[bean]]s, [[maize]], [[pea]]s and [[onion]]s.<ref name=websiteCaqueza/>
