'''Suitucancha''' (Hispanicized spelling of the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] term '''Suyt'u Kancha''', ''suyt'u'' rectangular, ''kancha'' corral, square,<ref name=laime/> "rectangular corral (or square)") is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Yauli Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its seat is Suitucancha.
