'''Pumanque''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Colchagua Province]], [[O'Higgins Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Pumanque spans an area of {{convert|440.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 3,442 inhabitants (1,793 men and 1,649 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population fell by 8.8% (331 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pumanque is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2021-2024 mayor is Gonzalo Baraona Bezanilla.
