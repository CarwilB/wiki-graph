'''El Asintal''' ({{IPA|es|el asinˈtal}}) is a town, with a population of 17,388 (2018 census),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ Citypopuation.de] Population of cities & towns in Guatemala</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Retalhuleu Department|Retalhuleu department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The municipality cover an area of 74 km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 36,882 (2018 census).<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/ Citypopulation.de] Population of departments and municipalities in Guatemala</ref>

The ruins of the [[Mesoamerica]]n city of [[Takalik Abaj]] are within the municipality.
