'''Betéitiva''' ({{IPA|es|beˈtejtiβa}}) a town and municipality in the [[Valderrama Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre of Betétiva is situated at {{convert|250|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]], {{convert|110|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] and {{convert|45|km|mi}} from [[Sogamoso]]. The municipality, located on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], borders in the north [[Belén, Boyacá|Belén]], in the northeast [[Paz de Río]], in the east [[Tasco, Boyacá|Tasco]], in the southeast [[Corrales, Boyacá|Corrales]] and in the west with [[Busbanzá]] and [[Cerinza]].<ref name=websiteBeteitiva>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.beteitiva-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Betéitiva]{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Betéitiva is derived from the [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] name of the ''[[cacique]]'' Betacín of the village and means "chief of the anchovy". According to [[Muisca mythology|Muisca myths]], Betacín fell in love with the daughter of the ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]] and after her dismissal he retreated in the area now known as Betéitiva.<ref name=websiteBeteitiva/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Betéitiva] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Betéitiva before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Different [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] were reigning the central highlands of the Colombian [[Andes]] and Betéitiva was ruled by ''cacique'' Betacín.

Modern Betéitiva was founded on October 14, 1556 by Juan Salamanca.<ref name=websiteBeteitiva/>

== Economy ==
[[Potato]]es and [[dairy]] farming are the most important economical activities of the municipality.<ref name=websiteBeteitiva/>
