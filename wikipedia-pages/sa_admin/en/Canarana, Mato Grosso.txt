'''Canarana''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]].

==Transportation==
The city is served by [[Canarana Airport]].

==Climate==
According to data from the [[National Institute of Meteorology (Brazil)|National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET), since 1995 the lowest [[temperature]] recorded in Canarana was {{convert|10.3|C}} on 4 July 2011,<ref name="Temp-mín-INMET">{{cite web |url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83270&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=06/02/1995&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,,1,,,,,,,,,,,,, |title=BDMEP – historical series – daily data – minimum temperature (°C) – Canarana |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia |access-date=26 June 2018}}</ref> and the highest reached {{convert|42|C}} on 25 September 2010.<ref name="Temp-máx-INMET">{{cite web |url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83270&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=06/02/1995&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,1,,,,,,,,,,,,,, |title=BDMEP – historical series – daily data – maximum temperature (°C) – Canarana |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia |access-date=26 June 2018}}</ref> The highest accumulated [[precipitation (meteorology)|precipitation]] in 24 hours was {{convert|147.2|mm|in}} on 8 February 2004.
