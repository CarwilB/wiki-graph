'''Jinotepe''' ({{IPA|es|xinoˈtepe}}) is a [[city]] and [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Carazo (department)|Carazo]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. The [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] inhabitants of Jinotepe are the [[:es: Chorotegas|Chorotegas]] and [[Nicarao people|Nahuas]].

It borders with [[Managua]], [[Masaya]], [[Granada, Nicaragua|Granada]], and [[Rivas, Nicaragua|Rivas]].

==Toponymy==
Jinotepe comes from the [[Nawat language]], and is named after the [[Nahuas|Nahua]] chiefdom of Xinotepet, which was located in the present-day department of Carazo.<ref>Vida de González Dávila, Gil. Ávila, c. 1480 – 21.IV.1526. ==Geography==
===Climate===
Jinotepe, like much of Western Nicaragua except for the Sierras, has a [[tropical climate]] with constant temperatures averaging between {{convert|28|and|32|C|F}}. Under [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen’s climate classification]], the city has a [[tropical wet and dry climate]]. A distinct dry season exists between November and April, while most of the rainfall is received between May and October. Temperatures are highest in March and April, when the sun lies directly overhead and the summer rainfall has yet to begin.
