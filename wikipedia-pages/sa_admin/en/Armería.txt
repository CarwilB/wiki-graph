'''Armería''' is a municipality in the south-central part of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Colima]]. '''Ciudad de Armería''' is a city and the seat of Armería [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]]. The municipality reported 29,599 inhabitants in the 2015 census and has an area of 341.60&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (131.89 sq mi).<ref name="census2015">{{cite web |url=http://www.cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/col/poblacion/default.aspx?tema=me&e=06|title=Número de habitantes|page=|publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=2017-07-15}}</ref> Its municipal seat is [[Ciudad de Armería]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06001a.htm |title=Armería |language=Spanish |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=2007-12-28 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930032920/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06001a.htm |archivedate=2007-09-30 }}</ref>

===Ciudad de Armería===
'''Ciudad de Armería''' ('''Armería''') is a city in the south-central part of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Colima]]. It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding [[Armería Municipality]]. With a 2005 census population of 14,091, Ciudad de Armería is the fifth-largest community in the state in terms of population.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070316010215/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |date=2007-03-16 }}, [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática]] (INEGI). Accessed on line December 28, 2007.</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06001a.htm Armería] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930032920/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06001a.htm |date=September 30, 2007 }} in the ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Accessed on line December 28, 2007.</ref>
