'''Sololá''' is a city in [[Guatemala]]. It is the capital of the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Sololá Department|Sololá]] and the administrative seat of Sololá [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]]. It is located close to [[Lake Atitlán]].

The name is a Hispanicized form of its pre-Columbian name, one spelling variant of which is Tz'olojya.<ref name="muni_history" /> The urban center has about 14,000 people, but the municipality also includes four village communities — Los Encuentros, El Tablón, San Jorge la Laguna, and Argueta — as well as 59 smaller rural communities.<ref name="muni_history">{{cite web |url=http://inforpressca.com/solola/historia.php |title=Sololá - Historia |access-date=2009-04-20 |publisher=Inforpressca.com }}</ref>

Sololá is situated at 2,114 [[metre|m]] altitude on a mountainside overlooking [[Lake Atitlán]], some 600 meters below. It is a highland market center and is the headquarters for the Catholic [[Diocese|Bishopric]] encompassing the Sololá and [[Chimaltenango Department|Chimaltenango]] Departments.

Almost all residents of Sololá are [[Kaqchikel people|Kaqchikel Maya]], except in Argueta, where most are [[K'iche' people|K'iche' Maya]].<ref name="muni_history" />  A large percentage of both men and women continue to wear traditional Mayan clothing.

Sololá is home to many international organizations including [https://web.archive.org/web/20110818014428/http://www.mannaproject.org/guatemala Manna Project International], [[Friendship Bridge (non-profit)|Friendship Bridge]], [http://www.mayanfamilies.org Mayan Families] and [http://www.lovefutbol.org/#about-us Love Futbol].

== Climate ==
The climate is a [[subtropical highland]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cwb''), very similar to that of the Asian [[monsoon]]s with months almost without any rain. Most of the precipitation is in the summer and early fall. Temperatures during the year have low fluctuation, with a relatively cold low average in winter (7.4&nbsp;°C in January) and summers are cool with April the hottest month having a high of 23.2&nbsp;°C.<ref name="en.climate-data.org">{{Cite web|url=https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/guatemala/solola/solola-1005364/|title=Sololá climate: Average Temperature, weather by month, Sololá weather averages - Climate-Data.org|website=en.climate-data.org|access-date=2019-02-18}}</ref>
