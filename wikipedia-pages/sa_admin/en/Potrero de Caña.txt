'''Potrero de Caña''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tolé District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|20.1|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 337 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|16.8|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,751; its population as of 2000 was 458.<ref name=inec/>
