'''São João''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃w ˈʒuɐ̃w]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 240&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 22,899 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/sao-joao/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Jupi]] and [[Jucati]]   ('''N''');  [[Palmeirina]]    ('''S''');  [[Angelim]]   ('''E''');   [[Garanhuns]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 244.44&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 716 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 21.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 240&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''São João''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially  [[beans]], [[manioc]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[chickens]].
