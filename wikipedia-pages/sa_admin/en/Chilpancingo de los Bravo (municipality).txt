''' Chilpancingo de los Bravo  ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat is [[Chilpancingo de los Bravo]].

==Geography==
The municipality is within the [[Sierra Madre del Sur]] mountain range. It covers an area of {{convert|2338.4|km2|mi2}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 214,219.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chilpancingo de los Bravo|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12029a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070510100349/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12029a.htm|archive-date=May 10, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>
