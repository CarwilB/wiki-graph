'''Campo Belo''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] for "beautiful field") is a city located in [[Minas Gerais]] state, in [[Brazil]]. The estimated population in 2020 was 54,186 inhabitants, and the total area of the municipality was {{convert|528.225|km2}}.  It was founded in 1879.

== Geography ==

=== Climate ===
Campo Belo's climate can be classified as [[tropical altitude]], with yearly average temperature of {{cvt|23.5|°C}}. The [[Köppen climate classification]] of the region is Cwa (Tropical on high altitudes, humid/warm summer and a dry/cool winter).
