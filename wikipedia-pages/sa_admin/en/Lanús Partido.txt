[[File:Centro de Lanús.jpg|thumb|right|The main avenue, Hipólito Yrigoyen, in Lanús district.]]
'''Lanús''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], at the south of the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban conglomerate neighbouring [[Buenos Aires]] city.

The partido has an area of {{convert|45|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and a population of 453,500 ({{census-ar|2010}}).<ref name=Cen2010 /> Its capital is the city of [[Lanús]].

Lanús Partido is connected to the Buenos Aires city across the [[Puente Valentín Alsina|Valentín Alsina Bridge]] over the [[Riachuelo River]]. The name of the partido comes from the former land owner [[Anacarsis Lanús]], who was a pioneer in the urbanization of the area.

==Sports==
The partido is home to [[Club Atlético Lanús]], a [[football (soccer)|football]] club playing in the [[Primera Division Argentina|Argentine Primera Division]].
