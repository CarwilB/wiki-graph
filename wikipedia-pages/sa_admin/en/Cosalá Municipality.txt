'''Cosalá''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sinaloa]] in northwestern [[Mexico]], being the least populous municipality in Sinaloa.<ref>{{cite web |title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128142000/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |archivedate=January 28, 2007 }}</ref>

==Political subdivision==
Cosalá Municipality is subdivided in 5 ''sindicaturas'':
*Cosalá
*Guadalupe de los Reyes
*La Ilama
*San José de las Bocas
*Santa Cruz de Alaya
