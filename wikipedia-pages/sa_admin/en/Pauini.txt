'''Pauini''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]].

The population of Pauini was 19,522 in 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/pauini/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
Its area is {{convert|43263|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|256000|ha|adj=on}} [[Purus National Forest]], created in 1988.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA do Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5112|accessdate=2016-09-08}}</ref>
It holds 8% of the {{convert|311000|ha|adj=on}} [[Mapiá-Inauini National Forest]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA Mapiá-Inauini – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA Mapiá-Inauini|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4213|accessdate=2016-09-08}}</ref>
It also contains 8% of the {{convert|604209|ha|adj=on}} [[Médio Purus Extractive Reserve]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Médio Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Médio Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/uc/4548|accessdate=2016-09-03}}</ref>
