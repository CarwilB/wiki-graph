'''Sagua de Tánamo''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsaɣwa ðe ˈtamano}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Holguín Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
The [[Sierra Cristal National Park]] ({{langx|es|Parque Nacional Sierra Cristal}}) is partly located in the Sagua de Tánamo municipality and partly in neighboring [[Mayarí, Cuba|Mayarí]].<ref name=rhc>{{Cite web|url=http://www.radiohc.cu/espanol/medioambiente/parques/sierracristal1.htm |title=Parque Nacional Sierra Cristal |author=Radio Habana |access-date=2007-10-09 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071004222518/http://www.radiohc.cu/espanol/medioambiente/parques/sierracristal1.htm |archive-date=October 4, 2007 |language=es}}</ref>

The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Barrederas, Bazán, Cananovas, Catalina, Esterón, Juan Díaz, Miguel, Pueblo, Sitio and Zabala.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/osagua/index.htm|title=Sagua de Tánamo|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-05|language=es}}</ref> Carpintero, la Rosa, Rio Grande, La Guira, Naranjo.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Sagua de Tánamo had a population of 44,872.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|704|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|64|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
