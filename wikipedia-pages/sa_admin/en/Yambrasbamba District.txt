'''Yambrasbamba''' is a district of [[Bongará Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref name="Amazonas">{{cite web|url=http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|title=Provincias y Distritos|date=n.d.|language=es|website=Gobierno Regional de Amazonas|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130615121036/http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|archive-date=15 June 2013|url-status=live|access-date=15 November 2014}}</ref> The district occupies an area of 1.715,96&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It was created on December 26, 1870, and has a population of approximately 12,000 inhabitants.
The capital of the district is the city of [[Yambrasbamba]], at an elevation of 1,903 meters on above sea level. The city has an attractive ambience with varieties of [[orchid]]s and large trees. It is crossed by the [[Imaza River]].
The population of the district is primarily rural and of mixed origin. The population devotes themselves to raising [[cattle]], to the production of milk and to the culture of [[yacón]], [[maize|corn]], [[carrot]]s, [[swallows]] and [[coffee]].
