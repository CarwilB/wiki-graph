'''Solidaridad''' ({{lit|solidarity}}) is one of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|eleven municipalities]] that make up the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Quintana Roo]]. Its municipal seat is the town of [[Playa del Carmen]].

Solidaridad is home to the [[Maya civilization|Maya]] archaeological site of [[Xcaret]].<ref name="Municipalities of Quintana Roo">{{cite web

==Geography==

The municipality has some small but rich continental sites including the ecotourism park of [[Xcaret]]. Prior to the creation of the [[Tulum (municipality)|Municipality of Tulum]], the municipality was situated between latitudes 20° 45' and 19° 46' north and longitudes 86° 57' and 88° 05' west, and had an area of {{Convert|4245.67|km2}}. Today, it borders the municipalities of [[Benito Juárez, Quintana Roo|Benito Juárez]] and [[Lázaro Cárdenas, Quintana Roo|Lázaro Cárdenas]] to the north and Tulum to the south; and is separated in most places from its neighboring municipality to the east ([[Cozumel]]) by a stretch of the [[Caribbean Sea]] known as the Cozumel Channel.

The ecotourism park of [[Xel-Ha]] and the [[Calica]] [[limestone]] quarry are not part of Solidaridad as they belong to the neighboring municipality of [[Cozumel]]. These are the only two mainland locations for the municipality of [[Cozumel]] with sizes of {{Convert|90|ha}} and {{Convert|11.9|ha}} respectively. All of the [[limestone]] from [[Calica]] is exported to the US, and the quarry has its own docks and also serves visiting cruise ships.<ref name="Municipalities of Quintana Roo"/>

===Orography and hydrography===

Like most of the [[Yucatan Peninsula]], Solidaridad is almost entirely flat with a gentle slope towards the sea, so from west to east, most of the area never reaches an altitude above {{Convert|25|m}} above sea level.

Like the rest of the peninsula's surface the land has a [[limestone]] base that does not allow the formation of surface water flows such as rivers and streams, the water instead form flows in underground rivers that sometimes rise to the surface in the [[cenote]]s. Lakes and [[cenote]]s are the major water bodies of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|municipality]].

==History==
Solidaridad is one of the youngest municipalities in the country: it was formed on July 28, 1993, by decree of the [[Congress of Quintana Roo|State Congress]] during the administration of [[Mario Villanueva Madrid]]. At this time the municipality of [[Cozumel]] was split into two, with the mainland section constituting the new municipality of Solidaridad.

On March 13, 2008, Solidaridad was split in two when the new [[Tulum (municipality)|Municipality of Tulum]] was formed, as a result its land area and population were adjusted.<ref>{{Cite web | title = Tulum se convierte en el noveno municipio de Quintana Roo | url = http://www.jornada.unam.mx/ultimas/2008/03/13/tulum-se-convierte-en-el-noveno-municipio-de-quintana-roo/?searchterm=Municipio%20de%20Tulum | accessdate = 2008-03-13}} {{Dead link|date=September 2010|bot=H3llBot}}</ref>

==Demographics==

The municipality has a population of 159,310 according to the results of the Population and Housing Census of 2010 conducted by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|National Institute of Statistics and Geography (Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía)]], of these, 83,468 are men and 75,842 are women.<ref name="2010 census tables: INEGI"/> By this time, Tulum Municipality was already separated from Solidaridad.

Currently most of its population are immigrants, mainly from the southeastern states of Mexico; but also, according to the [[Instituto Nacional de Migración|National Institute of Migration, Mexico (Instituto Nacional de Migración de México)]], the municipality has 12 percent of its population of foreign origin.
