'''Pimienta''' is a town, with a population of 13,530 (2023 calculation),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Cortés department|Cortés]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://portalunico.iaip.gob.hn/portal/index.php?portal=95|title=IAIP - Alcaldía  Municipal de Pimienta|accessdate=November 20, 2018|surname=IAIP|website=portalunico.iaip.gob.hn|language=es|archive-date=November 20, 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181120221341/https://portalunico.iaip.gob.hn/portal/index.php?portal=95|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://www.hondurasensusmanos.com/0504-pimienta/#1458164463528-ad3d422e-d0e05a0b-c485911a-f8218cea-039dbc8f-a7f4b86a-1cdd|title=0504 Pimienta – Honduras en sus manos|accessdate=November 20, 2018|website=www.hondurasensusmanos.com|language=es}}</ref> The name means "[[Capsicum|pepper]]" in [[Spanish language|Spanish]].

== History ==
The municipality of Pimienta was created on January 10, 1927. It was named for its abundance of pepper trees.<ref name=":1" />

In 1879 Pimienta was a small collection of buildings, and by the census of 1887 it was a small village in the municipality of Villanueva. In the 1895 national census, Pimienta belonged to the municipality of [[Potrerillos, Cortés|Potrerillos]].<ref name=":1" /> In 1924, with the opening of a railway bridge connecting Potrerillos, Pimienta became a place of great commercial growth, thus inducing banana producing companies to set up a pay office there.<ref name=":0" />

On June 7, 1934 an enormous swelling of the river Ulúa destroyed a large part of the town of Pimienta, therefore in 1936 the population was moved to a new site which became known as Nueva Pimienta (New Pimienta), the new head town of the municipality.<ref name=":0" />

== Geography ==
Pimienta is bounded by the municipalities of [[San Manuel, Cortés|San Manuel]] to the north, [[Villanueva, Cortés|Villanueva]] to the north and west, [[Potrerillos, Cortés|Potrerillos]] to the south, and [[El Progreso]], [[Yoro Department]], to the east. The [[Ulúa River]] passes through Pimienta municipality. Its climate is predominantly hot, with the warmest months being March and June.<ref name=":1" />
