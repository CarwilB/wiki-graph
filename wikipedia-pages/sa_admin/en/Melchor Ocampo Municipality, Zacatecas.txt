'''Melchor Ocampo''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|250|km}} north of [[Zacatecas City]], the state capital. It is named after [[Melchor Ocampo]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Melchor Ocampo lies at an elevation between {{convert|1200|and(-)|2800|m}} in the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] in northern Zacatecas. It borders [[Mazapil Municipality]] in Zacatecas to the south, and the Coahuilan municipalities of [[Viesca Municipality|Viesca]] to the west, [[Parras Municipality|Parras]] to the north, and [[Saltillo Municipality|Saltillo]] to the east.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32027.pdf |title=Melchor Ocampo, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|1881.4|km2}} and comprises 2.5% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 1% of the land in Melchor Ocampo is used for agriculture, and another 1% is covered by forest, but the [[Meseta Central matorral]] is the predominant land cover (98%) in the municipality. Melchor Ocampo is situated in the drainage basin of the {{ill|Laguna de Viesca|es}} in the [[Bolsón de Mapimí]].<ref name=compendium/>

Melchor Ocampo's climate ranges from semiarid to arid desert. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|22|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|200|and(-)|600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Melchor Ocampo is located in territory that was inhabited by the [[Guachichil]] and {{ill|Irritila|es}} peoples prior to Spanish contact.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32027a.html |title=Melchor Ocampo |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> Permanent settlement in the area dates to 1872 with the founding of the San Pedro ''[[hacienda]]'', where a railway station was built in 1903.

The municipality of San Pedro Ocampo was established on 19 August 1916, and on 9 January 1935, it changed its name to Melchor Ocampo. Deposits of gold, silver and lead were discovered in the municipality in the 1930s.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Melchor Ocampo comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''),<ref name=encyclopedia/> and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://igualdad.ine.mx/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Lineamientos-de-Paridad-Zacatecas.pdf |title=Lineamientos para el registro de candidaturas a cargos de elección popular de los partidos políticos y coaliciones |language=es |pages=12–13 |date=27 November 2017 |publisher=Instituto Electoral del Estado de Zacatecas |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is Rodolfo Cisneros Gallegos.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Melchor Ocampo recorded a population of 2736 inhabitants living in 721 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=68–69 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=2 January 2022}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 2662 inhabitants in Melchor Ocampo.<ref name=snim/>

There are 28 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Melchor Ocampo, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=027 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Melchor Ocampo |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> It recorded a population of 698 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy==
In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 30% of Melchor Ocampo's workforce was employed in the primary sector, 44% in the secondary sector, 5% in commerce, and 21% in services.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 10.5: Población ocupada por municipio y su distribución porcentual según sector de actividad económica, al 15 de marzo de 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Zacatecas 2017 |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092047.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The main crops grown are alfalfa, and [[Opuntia|cactus pear]] for fodder.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Melchor-Ocampo.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 027 Melchor Ocampo |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |date=17 June 2021 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> A small mine in the municipality called La Fé del Norte produces 120 tonnes of zinc–lead ore per day as of 2020.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sgm.gob.mx/pdfs/ZACATECAS.pdf |title=Panorama Minero del Estado de Zacatecas |language=es |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=December 2020 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>
