'''Junín''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|Department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|2476|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the west with the Department of [[Ayacucho Department, San Luis|Ayacucho]], to the south with [[Libertador General San Martín Department, San Luis|San Martín]] and [[Chacabuco Department, San Luis|Chacabuco]], to the north with the provinces of [[Córdoba Province, Argentina|Córdoba]].
