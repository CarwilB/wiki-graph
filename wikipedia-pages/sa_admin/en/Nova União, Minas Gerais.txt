'''Nova União''' is a [[municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira. {{As of|2025}}, the estimated population was 6,107.<ref name="IBGE" />
