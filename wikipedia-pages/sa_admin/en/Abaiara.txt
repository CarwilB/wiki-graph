'''Abaiara''' is a [[city]] in the region of the [[Kiriri people]], in the state of [[Ceará]], Brazil. The population in 2020 was 11,853 people.

== Etymology ==
The city was first named, in 1873, "Distrito de São Pedro", as a tribute to the Brazilian emperor [[Pedro II of Brazil|Pedro II]]. In 1938 the name was changed to "Pedro Segundo". In 1943 the city received its current name, "Abaiara", this name in [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupi]] means "the lord of men", through the combination of ''abá'' ("man") and ''îara'' ("lord") (still as a reference and tribute to Pedro II).<ref>{{cite book |last=Navarro |first=Eduardo de Almeida |title=[[Dicionário de Tupi Antigo|Dicionário de tupi antigo: a língua indígena clássica do Brasil]] |publisher=Global |location=São Paulo |year=2013 |page=541 |language=pt}}</ref>

==History==
The first inhabitants of the region were the indigenous [[Kariri people|Kariri]], of [[Tapuia]] origin. They were a strong and warlike people who became notable in colonial history for their resistance to the advance of the [[Colonial Brazil|Portuguese]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Nimuendajú |first=Curt |url=http://portal.iphan.gov.br/indl/pagina/detalhes/1563 |title=Mapa Etno-histórico de Curt Nimuendajú |date=2017 |access-date=2024-06-29}}</ref>

The lands located at the foothills of the [[Chapada do Araripe]] were inhabited by the [[Kariri people|Kariri]] Gauariú.<ref>Sebok, Lou. ''Atlases published in the Netherlands in the rare atlas collection''. National Map Collection (Canada), Ottawa.</ref><ref>Aragão, R. B. ''Índios do Ceará e Topônimos indígenas''. Fortaleza: Barraca do Escritor Cearense, 1994.</ref>

Abaiara, as an urban nucleus, emerged in the 19th century, when two farmers, José Leite da Cunha and José Júlio Sampaio, settled in the area with cattle ranches.<ref name="citybrazil.com.br">{{cite web |title=História da cidade |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/ce/abaiara/historia-da-cidade |website=City Brazil |access-date=2013-12-20 |language=pt}}</ref>

== Political evolution ==
The first political event occurred just after the creation of the ''Distrito da Paz'' on March 27, 1873. Abaiara was first elevated to the condition of '''Vila''' on December 20, 1938, under the name of ''Pedro Segundo''. The name was not very popular with the inhabitants and was soon replaced with the current name on December 30, 1943. Abaiara was elevated to the status of municipality on March 25, 1957.

== Religion ==
The first non-indigenous religious event took place when Father José Antônio de Araújo erected the first church which later became the central point in the city. With the growth of the city and the natural degradation of the first church, it became obvious that a new church was necessary. This second was built next to the first one (to avoid hurting the feelings of the population). The second church was dedicated to the ''Immaculate Heart of Mary'' and was made possible due to the actions of José Júlio Sampaio. However, the official saint remained ''Sacred Heart of Jesus''.

== Geography ==
=== Climate ===
The climate is tropical hot semi-arid with relatively high rainfall of 1,256&nbsp;millimetres,<ref name="ReferenceA">Fundação Cearense de Meteorologia e Recursos Hídricos – [[FUNCEME]].</ref> with precipitation concentrated between January and April.<ref>Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais – [[INPE]].</ref> Average temperatures range between 24 and 26&nbsp;°C.<ref name="Clima_do_Ceará">{{cite web |url=http://www.accuweather.com/pt-br/br/ceara/abaiara/quick-look.aspx |title=Temperatura |year=2011 |publisher=AccuWeather Global |access-date=2011-12-07 |language=pt}}</ref>

=== Hydrography and water resources ===
The main water sources are the Rompe Gibão stream and the Sabonete, São Pedro, and Jitirama streams.<ref name="ceara.com.br">{{cite web |url=http://www.ceara.com.br/m/abaiara/index.htm |title=Abaiara |website=Ceará.com.br |language=pt}}</ref>

=== Relief and soils ===
The main elevations are the São Felipe Mountains, the Mãozinha Mountains, and the Araripe Mountains.<ref name="ceara.com.br" />

=== Vegetation ===
Most of the territory is covered by thorny deciduous forest ([[caatinga]] woodland), while higher areas in the southwestern part of the municipality contain tropical semi-deciduous rainforest (dry forest).<ref name="ReferenceA" />

== Economy ==
The economy of the municipality of Abaiara is based on family farming, agriculture, livestock farming, and local commerce.<ref>Data collected from information provided by local residents.</ref>
