'''Terra Boa''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the micro-region of [[Campo Mourão]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. The population is 17,200 (2020 est.) in an area of 320.85 km².<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23A94 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 825 m.  The neighbouring municipalities are [[Cianorte]], [[Engenheiro Beltrão]], [[Jussara]] and [[Peabiru]].
