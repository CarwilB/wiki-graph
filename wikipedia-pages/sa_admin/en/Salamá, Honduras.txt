'''Salamá''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]].
[[File:Salama-Muni-Olancho-Honduras.png|thumb|right|300px|Map of Salamá Municipality]]

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Salamá municipality had a population of 7,542. Of these, 99.23% were [[Mestizo]], 0.62% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.12% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]]  and 0.03% [[White Latin Americans|White]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
