'''Capixaba''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kapiˈʃabɐ}}) is a  [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 12,008 and its area is 1,713&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|931537|ha|adj=on}} [[Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use environmental unit created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Extrativista Chico Mendes|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=222|accessdate=4 June 2016}}</ref>
