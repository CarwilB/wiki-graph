'''Montanha''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 18,894 (2020) and its area is 1,099&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/montanha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> In the [[Portuguese language]], "montanha" means "[[mountain]]".
