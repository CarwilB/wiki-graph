'''Magdalena''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the central zone of Veracruz, about 125&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 28.79 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|45|N|97|03|W|}}.
==Geography==
Magdalena is delimited to the north by [[San Andrés Tenejapan]] and [[Ixtaczoquitlán]], to the east and south by [[Tequila, Veracruz|Tequila]] and to the west by San Andrés Tenejapan.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30098a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Magdalena|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=10 June 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117190016/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30098a.htm|archive-date=17 November 2006|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Magdalena is cold all year with rain in summer and autumn.

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[coffee]].
==Culture==
A celebration in honor of Santa María Magdalena, patron saint of the town, takes place in July.
