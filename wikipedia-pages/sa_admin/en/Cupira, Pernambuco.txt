'''Cupira''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kuˈpiɾɐ]}}) (population 24,173)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/cupira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has 105.92 sq/km of total area.

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Agrestina]], [[Altinho]] and [[São Joaquim do Monte]] ('''N'''); [[Lagoa dos Gatos]] ('''S'''); [[Belém de Maria]] ('''E'''); [[Panelas]] ('''W''').
* Area - 105.92&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 416 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] river
* Vegetation - Subcaducifólia forest
* Annual average temperature - 23.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 170.5&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Cupira are commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s; and plantations of [[sweet potatoes]], [[beans]] and [[manioc]].
