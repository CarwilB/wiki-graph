'''Mucajaí''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|mukɐʒaˈi}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the midwest of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 18,172 (2020) and its area is 12,461&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/HWE|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Roraima {{!}} Mucajaí|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-06-15}}</ref>

Mucajaí started as Colônia Agrícola Fernando Costa, an agricultural colony, in 1951. In 1980, Apiaú, a larger scale colony, was nearby.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mucajai.rr.gov.br/historia-do-municipio2/ |title=História do Município|website=Municipality of Mucajaí|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> In 1982, it became an independent municipality and renamed Mucajaí.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/mucajai/historico |title=Mucajaí |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the [[Roraima National Forest]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Características da UC|publisher=ICMBio |url=http://flonarr.blogspot.ca/p/caracteristicas-da-uc.html|accessdate=2016-05-19}}</ref> In 2018, a mob attacked and expelled about 300 Venezuelan exiles after a local resident of Mucajaí was killed in a fight with a Venezuelan.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.esquerdadiario.com.br/Xenofobia-em-RR-moradores-expulsam-venezuelanos-de-predio-abandonado-e-ateiam-fogo-a-seus-pertences |title=Xenofobia em RR, moradores expulsam venezuelanos de prédio abandonado e ateiam fogo a seus pertences|website=ESQUERDA DIÁRIO|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
