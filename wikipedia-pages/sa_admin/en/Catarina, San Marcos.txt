'''Catarina''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is recognised as different municipality on 20 January 1925, when it split from [[Malacatán]] municipality.

==Climate==

Catarina has [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Am'').
