'''Celica''' is a canton in [[Loja Province]], [[Ecuador]]. Its seat is [[Celica, Ecuador|Celica]]. The canton is located in the west of the province and is bordered by the cantons of [[Puyango Canton|Puyango]] and [[Paltas Canton|Paltas]] in the north, [[Sozoranga Canton|Sozoranga]] in the east, [[Macará Canton|Macará]] in the south, and [[Pindal Canton|Pindal]] and [[Zapotillo Canton|Zapotillo]] in the west. It covers 521.9 km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 2,500 m.
