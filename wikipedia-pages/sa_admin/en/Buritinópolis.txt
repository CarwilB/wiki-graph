'''Buritinópolis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in eastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  It is the poorest municipality in the state.

==Location==
Buritinópolis is located in the [[Vão do Paranã]] statistical micro-region in  the area near [[Simolândia]] and [[Alvorada do Norte]].  It is close to the important  BR-020 highway, which connects [[Brasília]] with [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]].  Neighboring municipalities are [[Posse, Goiás|Posse]],
[[Simolândia]], [[Alvorada do Norte]], [[Mambaí]], and [[Damianópolis]].  The distance to [[Goiânia]] is 466&nbsp;km.  Highway connections are made by  BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / [[Alexânia]] / BR-060 / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / [[Formosa, Goiás|Formosa]] / BR-020 / [[Simolândia]] / GO-236.<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin Transporte] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=February 5, 2009 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising for meat and farming, especially soybeans and corn.  Most of the inhabitants either have no work or are employed in subsistence farming.  In 2007 there were 15 commercial units and no banks.  The absence of investments in the town leaves the inhabitants without the chance of a job and many, mainly the young, leave the town to try to find a job that would allow them to survive.   There were no hospitals in 2007.  There were 100 automobiles in 2007, which was one automobile for every 34 inhabitants.

==Health and education==
*Literacy rate: 71.9 in 2000.
*Infant mortality rate in 2000: 50.06
*Hospitals:  none
*Schools: 8 with 1,240 students <ref name="Sepin"/>
*Ranking on the United Nations Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.603 (in the lower tiers)
*State ranking:  242/242  (the worst in the state)
*National ranking: 4,612/5,507 <ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhgo.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In Buritinópolis, there is no bank branch, no restaurant, and no hotel.  The two supermarkets are comparable to the little grocery stores in the slums of the large urban centers and the bars can only provide income for their owners.  The agricultural activities provide few jobs since the small rural landowners practice subsistence farming and the large landowners opt for extensive cattle raising, which requires few workers.

Urban erosion is a worry for all.  It has already destroyed a large part of the main avenue that runs parallel to BR-020, the highway that cuts the city in two.  With no money to build drains and pave the streets on both side of the highway, the city government is waiting for  help from the state government.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.chapadaodoceu.go.gov.br/OPopular2003-01-12%20IDH.htm |title=Comparing Buritinopolis with Chapadão do Ceu |access-date=2006-03-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060404204303/http://www.chapadaodoceu.go.gov.br/OPopular2003-01-12%20IDH.htm |archive-date=2006-04-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
