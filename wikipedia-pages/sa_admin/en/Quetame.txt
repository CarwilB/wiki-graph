'''Quetame''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The urban centre of Quetame is located at {{convert|62|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]] at an altitude of {{convert|1496|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[Fómeque]] in the north, [[Fosca, Cundinamarca|Fosca]] and [[Cáqueza]] in the west, the department of [[Meta Department|Meta]] in the east and in the south with [[Guayabetal]].<ref name=websiteQuetame>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.quetame-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Quetame] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150517053321/http://www.quetame-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-17 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Quetame comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Our farmfields on the mountains".<ref name=websiteQuetame/>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Quetame was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Quetame was loyal to the ''[[cacique]]'' of [[Ubaque]].<ref name=websiteQuetame/>

Modern Quetame was founded on June 26, 1826, by Josè Joaquin Guarín.<ref name=websiteQuetame/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Quetame is [[agriculture]] with products [[bean]]s, [[sagú]], [[maize]], [[pea]]s, [[arracacha]] and others.<ref name=websiteQuetame/>

== Earthquake ==
On May 24, 2008, there was a magnitude 5.5 [[2008 El Calvario earthquake|earthquake]] with its [[epicentre]] in Quetame that caused at least 3 deaths and destroyed 40% of the buildings in the village. The tremor was also felt in [[Bogotá]] and [[Villavicencio]].<ref>[https://earthquake.usgs.gov/eqcenter/recenteqsww/Quakes/us2008skbn.php/ USGS data earthquake 2008] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080526040842/http://earthquake.usgs.gov/eqcenter/recenteqsww/Quakes/us2008skbn.php/ |date=May 26, 2008 }}</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.eltiempo.com/nacion/2008-05-24/ARTICULO-WEB-NOTA_INTERIOR-4206638.html/ A 15 ascienden los muertos que dejó temblor del sábado en el centro del país] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080821212828/http://www.eltiempo.com/nacion/2008-05-24/ARTICULO-WEB-NOTA_INTERIOR-4206638.html |date=2008-08-21 }} - [[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]]</ref>
