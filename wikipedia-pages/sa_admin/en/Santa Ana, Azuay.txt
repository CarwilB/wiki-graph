'''Santa Ana''' is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] in [[Cuenca Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of 45.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and according to the 2001 Ecuadorian census it had a population total of 4,739.<ref name="Ajupa">{{cite web|url=http://www.ajupa.gov.ec/parroq.aspx?cant=1&parr=64|title=Parroquia Santa Ana|publisher=Asociación de Juntas Parroquiales Rurales del Azuay|accessdate=February 26, 2010}}</ref>
