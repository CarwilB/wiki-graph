'''Lambaré''' ({{IPA|es|lambaˈɾe}}) is a city in [[Central Department]], [[Paraguay]], forming part of the [[Gran Asunción|Greater Asuncion]] metropolitan area. It covers an area of {{cvt|27|km2}} and had a population of 127,150 inhabitants according to the 2022 national census, making it the [[List of cities in Paraguay by population|eighth-most populous city]] in the country.

==History==
Before the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonialists arrived in Paraguay in the early 16th century, the area was inhabited by Carian people, part of [[Guaraní people|Guaranís]]. There were several tribes, lead by tribal chieftains. The expedition of [[Juan de Ayolas]] arrived in 1535 with 160 men, at a natural port used by the Cario, situated in a small bay at the foot of a hill. That port, known as Casaccia, is considered the site of the first Spanish landing, later became part of the territory of [[Asunción]] and is identified with the early history of Lambaré. The place was commanded by the chieftain Avambaré, whose name later became the name of the settlement and later was transformed in Lambare.<ref name="ABC2018">{{cite news|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/comercial/56-aniversario-lambare/formaba-parte-de-un-territorio-dominado-por-los-guaranies-1709307.html|title=Formaba parte de un territorio dominado por los guaraníes|trans-title=Part of a territory dominated by the Guarani people|work=[[ABC Color]]|date=5 June 2018|access-date=20 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

In 1766, Lambaré was officially founded by [[Carlos Morphi]], the [[Governorate of Paraguay|Governor of Paraguay]] in service of the Spanish Crown. Following the independence of Paraguay, it was created as an independent municipality, which was formalised by Law No. 791 enacted on 5 June 1962 by the government of [[Alfredo Stroessner]]. Mariano Escauriza Fernández served as the first president of the municipal council. On 31 July 1965, it was elevated to a first-category municipality by Executive Decree No. 12756.<ref name="ABC2018"/> The parish church of Lambaré was erected in the colonial period, first functioning as a chapel and from 1769 appearing in records as a vicariate of the cathedral. In 1849, president [[Carlos Antonio López]] announced the completion of the temple of Lambaré.<ref name="ABC2018"/>

==Geography==
Lambaré is located in the [[Central Department]] of Paraguay, forming part of the [[Gran Asunción|Greater Asuncion]] metropolitan area. It covers an area of {{cvt|26.77|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/cities/?cityid=1476|title=Lambaré|work=City Population.de|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.mindat.org/loc-282525.html|title=Lambare|work=Mineral Data|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2022 census carried out by the [[List of national and international statistical services#Americas|Instituto Nacional de Estadística]] (INE), Lambaré had a total population of 127,150 inhabitants, making it the [[List of cities in Paraguay by population|eighth-most populous city]] in the country. The population consisted of 65,799 females and 61,351 males. The median age of the population was 33 years.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="UH">{{cite news|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/top-10-de-las-ciudades-mas-pobladas-del-paraguay-segun-el-censo-2022|title=El top 10 de las ciudades más pobladas del Paraguay, según el Censo 2022|work=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=31 August 2023|access-date=20 May 2026|lang=es|trans-title=The Top 10 of Paraguay’s most populous cities, according to the 2022 Census}}</ref>
