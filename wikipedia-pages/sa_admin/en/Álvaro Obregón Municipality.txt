'''Álvaro Obregón''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://sipse.com/ciencia-y-salud/descubren-mexico-tratamiento-dental-mas-antiguo-america-217423.html|title=Descubren en Michoacán la endodoncia más antigua de América|publisher={{ill|Sipse|es}}|date=11 August 2016|access-date=16 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located north of the state and about {{convert|26|km|mi|0}} from the city of [[Morelia]]. It has an area of {{convert|162.64|km2}} and had a population of 18,696 inhabitants as of 2005. The municipality is named in honor of [[Álvaro Obregón]], the President of Mexico from 1920 to 1924.

The municipality contains 35 localities, which include [[Álvaro Obregón, Michoacán|Álvaro Obregón]], [[Tzintzimeo]], [[El Calvario, Michoacán|El Calvario]], [[Felipe Carrillo Puerto, Michoacán|Felipe Carrillo Puerto]], and [[Lázaro Cárdenas, Álvaro Obregón, Michoacán|Lázaro Cárdenas]].
