The '''Municipality of Centro''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Tabasco|17 municipalities]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tabasco]]. Its municipal seat is located in the city of [[Villahermosa]]. The municipality had a 2010 census population of 640,359 inhabitants, 353,577 (55.2%) of whom lived in its municipal seat, Villahermosa.

The municipal government is headed by the [[municipal president]] (mayor) of Centro.

==Towns and villages==
The municipality has the distinction of having 72 towns (localities) of over 1,000 inhabitants, and 116 towns of over 500 inhabitants, in both cases more than any other municipality in Mexico.
