'''Vargeão''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the [[South Region, Brazil|south region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |year=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20%28pelos%20dados%20de%202000%29.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref> It is located in the [[Xanxerê|Immediate Geographic Region of Xanxerê]] and the [[Chapecó|Intermediate Geographic Region of Chapecó]] and at 298 miles away from the capital of Santa Catarina, [[Florianópolis]].

Its population, according to the [[Census|demographic census]] of the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], carried out in 2022, is 3 634 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Index of /Censos/Censo_Demografico_2022/Populacao_e_domicilios_Primeiros_resultados |url=https://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Censos/Censo_Demografico_2022/Populacao_e_domicilios_Primeiros_resultados/ |access-date=2023-09-13 |website=ftp.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

The town is located on the southern rim of an [[impact crater]], the [[Vargeão Dome]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Geossit - Visualizar Geossítio |url=https://www.sgb.gov.br/geossit/geossitios/ver/1953 |access-date=2023-09-13 |website=www.sgb.gov.br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal |last=Kazzuo-Vieira |first=César |last2=Crósta |first2=Alvaro Penteado |last3=Gamboa |first3=Fernando |last4=Tygel |first4=Martin |date=September 2009 |title=Caracterização geofísica da estrutura de impacto do domo de Vargeão, Brasil |url=https://www.scielo.br/j/rbg/a/7JVQ8Xy3nx5jYYnNr3Prbhh/abstract/?lang=pt |journal=Revista Brasileira de Geofísica |language=pt |volume=27 |pages=375–388 |doi=10.1590/S0102-261X2009000300006 |issn=0102-261X|doi-access=free }}</ref> Vargeão's city hall is located inside the crater. The local population is well aware of the impact origin of the structure, to the point that the town's official nickname is Meteor City (''Terra do Meteorro'').

Its inhabitants are predominantly of [[Italians|Italian]] descent. A large floodplain (in portuguese: várzea) in the area gave rise to the name Vargeão (very large floodplain, in portuguese).
