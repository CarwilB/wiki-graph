'''Pedro Brand''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Santo Domingo Province|Santo Domingo province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]]. Within the municipality there are two [[municipal district]]s (''distritos municipal''): [[La Cuaba]] and [[La Guáyiga]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113 |title=Listado de Codigos de Provincias, Municipio y Distritos Municipales, Actualizada a Junio 20 del 2006 |author=Oficina Nacional de Estadistica, Departamento de Cartografia, Division de Limites y Linderos |language=es |access-date=2007-01-24 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070314215735/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113 |archive-date=March 14, 2007 }}</ref>
