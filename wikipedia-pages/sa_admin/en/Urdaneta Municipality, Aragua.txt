'''Urdaneta''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 21,271.<ref>{{cite web| url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx | title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended | website=geohive.com | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150710071738/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx | archive-date=2015-07-10}}</ref> The town of [[Barbacoas, Aragua|Barbacoas]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Urdaneta Municipality.{{cn|date=January 2024}}

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Urdaneta Municipality (disambiguation)|Urdaneta Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Rafael Urdaneta]].{{cn|date=January 2024}}

==Demographics==
The Urdaneta Municipality, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 21,271 (up from 18,734	 in 2001). The municipality's population density is {{convert|10.51|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>{{cite web| url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/aragua/0515__urdaneta/ | title=Urdaneta (Municipality in Aragua) | access-date=2024-01-25}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Urdaneta Municipality is Sotero González, re-elected in 2021, with 49.41% of the vote, after the previous mayor Antonio Lugo deceased.<ref>{{cite web| url=https://www2.cne.gob.ve/rm2021 | title=Consejo Nacional Electoral  | language=es | access-date=2024-01-25}}</ref><ref>{{cite web| url=https://elperiodiquito.com/aragua/9110/fallecio-alcalde-del-municipio-urdaneta-antonio-lugo/ |title=Alcalde de Urdaneta falleció tras sufrir un infarto (Mayor of Urdaneta died after suffering a heart attack) | language=es | access-date=2024-01-25}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Capital Urdaneta, Las Peñitas, San Francisco de Cara, and Taguay.<ref name="resource1">{{cite web| url=https://www.venciclopedia.org/index.php?title=Municipio_Urdaneta_(Aragua) | title=Municipio Urdaneta (Aragua) | language=es | access-date=2024-01-25}}</ref>
