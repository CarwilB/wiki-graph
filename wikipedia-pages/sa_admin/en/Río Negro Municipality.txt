The '''Río Negro Municipality''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Río Negro}}) is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the southern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Amazonas (Venezuelan state)|Amazonas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 2,300.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[San Carlos de Río Negro]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Río Negro Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Amazonas.zip {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
{{Unreferenced section|date=December 2022}}
The town of San Carlos de Río Negro was founded in 1759 around a mounted camp by the expedition captained by José Solano that moved to that area in the extent of the works of exploration of the limits between the Crowns of [[Portugal]] and [[Spain]] requested by the [[Treaty of Madrid (13 January 1750)|Treaty of Madrid]].  José Solano set up his exploration base in that place, in the margins of the [[Rio Negro (Amazon)|Rio Negro]], and there he settled with the few men that had survived the ascent along the [[Orinoco|Orinoco River]], since most, including the famous Swedish botanist [[Pehr Löfling]] that accompanied the expedition, they had succumbed prey of the tropical diseases, especially the [[yellow fever]].

San Carlos de Río Negro was visited from May 7 to May 10, 1800, for the expedition of [[Alexander von Humboldt]] and [[Aimé Bonpland]], constituting the most southern point of their (périplo) for the [[Amazon Basin]].

==Demographics==
The Río Negro Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 2,660 (up from 2,314 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.9% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.1|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Río Negro Municipality is Luis Alirio Avaristo, elected on October 31, 2004, with 37% of the vote.  Avaristo had been serving as the acting mayor of the Río Negro Municipality since August 13, 2002, following the resignation of Pedro Zerpa.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Rio Negro | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=4&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163156/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=4&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 - directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-05 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Capital Río Negro, Solano, Casiquiare, and Cocuy (previous to December 18, 1997, the Río Negro Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
