'''Campohermoso''' ({{IPA|es|kampoeɾˈmoso}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Lengupá Province]].

==Climate==
Campohermoso has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with moderate rainfall from December to March and heavy to very heavy rainfall in the remaining months.
