'''Campo do Brito''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈkɐ̃pu du ˈbɾitu}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Sergipe]]. 

Campo do Brito has a population of 18,218 (2020) and covers an area of {{convert|202|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/campo-do-brito/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
The municipality contains part of the [[Serra de Itabaiana National Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
