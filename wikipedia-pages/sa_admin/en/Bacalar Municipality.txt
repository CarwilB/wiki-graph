'''Bacalar''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|eleven municipalities]] of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[list of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Quintana Roo]].  The municipal seat and most populous town is the eponymous [[Bacalar]].  The municipality was formed on February 2, 2011, when it separated from the [[Othón P. Blanco, Quintana Roo|Municipality of Othón P. Blanco]].

==Demographics==
As 2020, Bacalar Municipality had a population of 41,754 inhabitants. [[Bacalar]], the municipal seat, had a population of 12,527. Other localities include Limones (2,739 hab.), Maya Balam (2,675 hab.), Los Divorciados (1,249) and Reforma (1,213 hab.)<ref name=CENSOBACALAR2020>[http://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/quintanaroo/23010__bacalar/ Censo Bacalar 2020] INEGI</ref>
