'''Lunahuaná''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in the middle [[Cañete Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is bordered by [[Imperial District]] on the west, [[San Vicente de Cañete District]] on the north,  [[Pacarán District]] on the east, and [[Chincha Province]] on the south.
