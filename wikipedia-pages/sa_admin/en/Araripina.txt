}}'''Araripina''' is a Brazilian municipality in the state of [[Pernambuco]]. Has an estimated population in 2025 of 90,504 inhabitants according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]].<ref name="IBGE" /> Total area of 1.847,5&nbsp;km{{sup|2}} and is  located in the [[Pernambuco|state]] mesoregion of [[Sertão]], at 622 meters above the sea level and 683&nbsp;km West from the state capital, [[Recife]].
The exploration of [[gypsum]] (95% of the Brazilian reserves) and calcarium is the base of the local economy and at the homonymous microregion.

==Economy==

{{See also|Largest Cities of Northeast Region, Brazil}}
The main economic activities in '''Araripina''' are based in extraction of gypsum and no metallic minerals; and primary sector especially creation of goats, donkeys, pigs and farms with beans, manioc and corn. Araripina is located in the microregion with its name, which contains 95% of the Brazilian reserves of [[Gypsum]].

==Sports==
The main sports in Araripina is [[Association football|football]] which is represented by [[Araripina Futebol Clube]], currently playing the [[Campeonato Pernambucano]]. Their stadium is known as ''Chapadão do Araripe'' which has 5000 seats.

==Transportation==
Araripina is served by [[Araripina Airport|Comte. Mairson C. Bezerra Airport]].
