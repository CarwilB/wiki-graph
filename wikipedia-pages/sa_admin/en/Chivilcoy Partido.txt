'''Chivilcoy Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northern area of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 60,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2075|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Chivilcoy]].
