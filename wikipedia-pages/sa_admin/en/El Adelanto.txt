'''El Adelanto''' ({{IPA|es|el aðeˈlanto}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|247.7|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 8,547 inhabitants.

==History== 
El Adelanto was earlier known as Aldea El Sitio, and was part of the municipality of Zapotitlán. It was officially established by the governmental decree enacted on 23 August 1882, and became a municipality on 23 March 1893. In 1925, the municipal boundaries were modified and delimited.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de El Adelanto|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of El Adelanto municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-el-adelanto-jutiapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
El Adelanto is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|247.7|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=El Adelanto (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2209__el_adelanto/|access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> It is located in southeastern Guatemala, {{cvt|153|km}} from the national capital [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|36|km}} from the departmental seat of [[Jutiapa]]. It borders the municipality of [[Jutiapa, Jutiapa|Jutiapa]] to the north, [[Yupiltepeque]] to the east, [[Zapotitlán, Jutiapa|Zapotitlán]] to the south, and [[Comapa, Jutiapa|Comapa]] to the southwest.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de El Adelanto, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/geografia-del-municipio-de-el-adelanto-jutiapa/|trans-title=Geography of El Adelanto municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level, El Adelanto has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification|Classification]]: Am). The district has an annual average temperature of {{cvt|27.17|C}}. El Adelanto receives about {{cvt|84.7|mm}} of precipitation and has 133.66 rainy days (36.62% of the time) on average annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/el-adelanto|title=El Adelanto|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
El Adelanto had an estimated population of 8,457 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 4,306 males and 4,151 females. About 28.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 7.3% was over the age of 65 years. About 36% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (64%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (87.6%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 79.1%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (87.4%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (12.1%) forming a significant minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>

The people of El Adelanto are known as "ticuqueros", as they earlier went to Jutiapa or the nearby El Salvador to procure tucuco, a food item made of maize and beans.<ref name="Hist"/> The economy is mainly dependent on [[agriculture]], majorly maize, and beans. [[Livestock]], horses, small fowl are also reared on a smaller scale.<ref name="Hist"/>
