'''Baños de Agua Santa''' ({{IPA|es|ˈbaɲos ðe ˈaɣwa ˈsanta}}), commonly referred to as '''Baños''', is a city in eastern [[Tungurahua Province]] of [[Ecuador]]. Baños is the second most populous city in Tungurahua, after the capital [[Ambato, Ecuador|Ambato]], and is a major tourist center. Baños is known as the "Gateway to the [[Amazon rainforest|Amazon]]," as it is the last city still located in [[Geography of Ecuador#La Sierra|the mountain region]] before reaching the jungle and other towns that are located in the [[Amazon River basin]].

Baños is located at an elevation of 1,820 metres (5,971 feet) on the northern foothills of the [[Tungurahua]] volcano, whose activity has been characterized by frequent powerful ash explosions and lava flows that can be seen from Banos.<ref>[http://www.volcanodiscovery.com/tungurahua.html Tungurahua on volcanodiscovery.com]</ref>

==Etymology==
[[File:Banos churchVirgendeAguaSanta court.JPG|thumb|right|Court of La Iglesia de la Virgen de Agua Santa in Baños]]
Baños de Agua Santa (Spanish for'' Baths of Holy Water<ref>[http://www.pachamama.org/banos-ecuador Baños at pachamama.org]</ref>'') is named after the hot springs located around the city which have a reputation of having healing properties due to the various minerals they contain.

==History==
The city is also a [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] religious center, as some Catholic believers say that the [[Blessed Virgin Mary|Virgin Mary]] appeared nearby a [[waterfall]]. Due to this belief, a sculpture of the virgin, called ''Virgen de Agua Santa'', was placed in the city's [[cathedral]].

The history of the town has been intimately linked to the highly-active [[Tungurahua]] volcano. In October 1999, all 17,000+ residents were forced to evacuate the city for weeks.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.hoy.com.ec/noticias-ecuador/evacuacion-sin-campanas-11726-11726.html Article on hoy.com.ec] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090409102501/http://www.hoy.com.ec/noticias-ecuador/evacuacion-sin-campanas-11726-11726.html |date=April 9, 2009 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Baños is located on the northern [[piedmont (disambiguation)|foothills]] of the [[Tungurahua]] volcano.

Along with the town [[Pillaro]], Baños is known as a good starting point for exploring the remote [[Llanganates National Park]] and its attractions including Cerro Hermoso. The city itself has a main central park across from the cathedral.

Nearby waterfalls include Virgen de Agua Santa, Inés María, Agoyán, El Manto de la Novia, Pailón del Diablo and Machay.

==Economy==
Baños is a popular tourist destination in Ecuador<ref>[http://www.vivatravelguides.com/south-america/ecuador/andean-highlands/banos/ Viva Travel Guides<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> due to its natural environment (it is home to more than 60 waterfalls) and its adventure sports.<ref name="banios webpage">[http://www.banios.com www.banios.com]</ref> Locals and visitors alike undertake [[rafting]], [[kayaking]], [[canyoning]], [[bridge jumping]], hiking, biking and horseback riding.<ref name="banios webpage" />

Baños is also known for its production of [[Taffy (candy)|taffy]] ({{langx|es|melcocha}} {{IPA|es|melˈkotʃa|}}) made from [[sugarcane|cane]] sugar, and for small shops that sell [[parrot]] sculptures made of [[Balsa Tree|balsa]] wood.

==Climate==

Under the [[Köppen climate classification]], Baños has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Köppen: Cfb or Cwb).
