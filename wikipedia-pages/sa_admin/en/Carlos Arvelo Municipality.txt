The '''Carlos Arvelo Municipality''' (named after [[Carlos Arvelo|Dr. Carlos Arvelo]]) is one of the 14 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Carabobo]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 150,277.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx|title=www.geohive.com geregistreerd via Argeweb - webhosting en domeinregistratie|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[Güigüe]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Carlos Arvelo Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Carabobo.zip{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Geography ==
The Carlos Arvelo Municipality is located in the southeastern part of [[Carabobo|Carabobo State]]. It is limited to the north by the Lake of Valencia and the Municipality the Guayos, to the west the Municipality of Valencia, to the South the State of Guárico and to the east the State of Aragua.<ref>{{cite web|url= http://www.carabobo.gob.ve/geografia-del-estado/|title= Geografia del estado|access-date= 2018-01-19|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20180112020412/http://www.carabobo.gob.ve/geografia-del-estado/|archive-date= 2018-01-12|url-status= dead}}</ref>

The Carlos Arvelo Municipality is integrated by the Güigüe Urban Parish (capital Güigüe) and the Belén Non-Urban Parish (capital Belen) and the Tacarigua Non-Urban Parish (capital Tacarigua). The north of the municipality is mostly plain, unlike the central and southern part of the municipality, which is mountainous.{{citation needed|date=January 2022}}

===Climate===
Minimum monthly temperature in the Carlos Arvelo municipality lies between 12.3 and 22.1&nbsp;°C, while maximum temperature is between 24.4 and 33.9&nbsp;°C. Total annual precipitation fluctuates between 932 and 1395&nbsp;mm. Most rain falls between June and August, while the driest quarter comprises January to March.<ref>{{cite journal|last1=Ferrer-Paris|first1=José R.|title=Caracterización ambiental de la ruta de NeoMapas: NM11 ''Güigüe'', estado Carabobo (CNEB e12)|journal=Figshare|date=2017|doi=10.6084/m9.figshare.4745545}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Carlos Arvelo Municipality, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 150,277 (up from 124,344 in 2001).  This amounts to 6.7% of the state's population.<ref name= a>{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/carabobo.pdf|title=XIV CENSONACIONAL DEPOBLACIÓN Y VIVIENDA|access-date=2018-01-19|archive-date=2015-11-13|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151113151309/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/carabobo.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|180|PD/sqkm}}.<ref name= a />
