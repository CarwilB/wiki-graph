'''Guasca''' is a Colombian town and municipality in the [[Guavio Province]], part of the [[Cundinamarca Department]] located approximately 55&nbsp;km from [[Bogotá]] passing through the town of [[La Calera, Cundinamarca]] or 65&nbsp;km passing through [[Sopó]]. Guasca borders the municipalities [[Tocancipá]] and [[Guatavita]] in the north, [[Junín, Cundinamarca|Junín]] in the east, in the south [[La Calera, Cundinamarca|La Calera]] and in the west [[Sopó]].<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.guasca-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml Official website Guasca] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170711120205/http://guasca-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml |date=2017-07-11 }} - accessed 05-05-2016</ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] of the central highlands of the Colombian [[Andes]], the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], the area was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] who spoke [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]. Guasca was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' who was loyal to the ''cacique'' of [[Guatavita]].<ref name=website/> In the [[Muisca religion|religion]] of the Muisca, the [[Siecha Lakes]] were considered sacred.<ref>{{cite book |author=Ocampo López, Javier |authorlink=Javier Ocampo López |year=2013 |title=Mitos y leyendas indígenas de Colombia |trans-title=Indigenous myths and legends of Colombia |location=Bogotá, Colombia |publisher=Plaza & Janes Editores Colombia S.A. |isbn=978-958-14-1416-1 |page=226 | language=es}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
According to [[friar]] and [[List of Muisca scholars|Muisca scholar]] [[Bernardo de Lugo]] the name Guasca is derived from ''guâ'', "mountain range" and ''shucâ''; "skirt". The name thus means "skirt of the mountain range", indicating the position of the village with respect to the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian Andes.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://muysca.cubun.org/Gram%C3%A1tica_de_Lugo/fol_7r Etymology Guasca according to Bernardo de Lugo] - accessed 05-05-2016</ref>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Guasca are [[agriculture]]; [[potato]]es, [[carrot]]s, flowers and [[strawberry|strawberries]] and [[dairy]] farming.<ref name=website/>

== Tourism ==
Guasca is known for [[ecotourism]] and has access to [[Chingaza Natural National Park]]. Other natural areas are [[Los Encenillos]] and the [[Siecha Lakes]].

== Born in Guasca ==
* [[Mariano Ospina Rodríguez]] - president of the [[Granadine Confederation]]

== Trivia ==
* The plant ''[[Galinsoga parviflora]]'' is called "Guasca(s)" in Colombia and an essential ingredient of the soup [[ajiaco]]
* The spiders ''[[Anapis guasca]]'',<ref>{{wikispecies-inline|Anapis}}</ref> ''[[Deinopis guasca]]'',<ref>{{wikispecies-inline|Deinopis}}</ref> and moth ''[[Dognina guasca]]''<ref>{{wikispecies-inline|Dognina}}</ref> are found in and named after Guasca
