'''Granados''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sonora]], located approximately {{convert|180|km}} northeast of [[Hermosillo]], the state capital. It is named after {{ill|José Joaquín Granados y Gálvez|es}}, the second bishop of Sonora from 1788 to 1794.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM26sonora/municipios/26028a.html |title=Granados |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=8 January 2022 |archive-date=13 December 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211213153802/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM26sonora/municipios/26028a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Granados lies at an elevation between {{convert|400|and(-)|1900|m}} in the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] in eastern Sonora. It borders the municipalities of [[Huásabas Municipality|Huásabas]] to the north, [[Bacadéhuachi Municipality|Bacadéhuachi]] to the east, [[Divisaderos Municipality|Divisaderos]] to the south, and [[Moctezuma, Sonora|Moctezuma]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=https://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/26/26028.pdf |title=Granados, Sonora |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|363.9|km2}} and comprises 0.2% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

Granados lies in the valley of the [[Bavispe River]], which is flanked by the Sierra de Huasabas to the west and the [[Sierra de Bacadéhuachi]] to the east.<ref>{{cite book |last=Bandelier |first=A. F. |author-link=Adolph Francis Alphonse Bandelier |title=Final Report of Investigations Among the Indians of the Southwestern United States, Carried on Mainly in the Years from 1880 to 1885: Part II |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=4BYTAAAAYAAJ&pg=RA1-PA499 |year=1892 |publisher=[[Archaeological Institute of America]] |page=499 |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref> The land cover in Granados mainly comprises [[Sonoran–Sinaloan transition subtropical dry forest|subtropical forest]] (67%), desert grassland (15%) and foothills thornscrub (13%). The northern portion of the Bavispe River valley near the municipal seat of Granados is used for farmland.<ref name=compendium/>
===Climate===
Granados has a semi-arid climate. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|22|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The [[Opata people|Opata]] are the indigenous inhabitants of the Granados valley.<ref>{{cite book |last=Hinton |first=Thomas B. |title=A Survey of Indian Assimilation in Eastern Sonora |url=https://repository.arizona.edu/handle/10150/615817 |location=Tucson |year=1959 |publisher=[[University of Arizona Press]] |series= Anthropological papers of the University of Arizona |volume=4 |page=24 |isbn=978-0-8165-0131-1 |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref> The settlement of Granados was founded in 1823 by members of the Durazo family from Moctezuma and their friend Ramón Arvizu.<ref name=encyclopedia/> 

On 3 December 1862, the municipality of Granados was established in the district of Moctezuma. It became an independent municipality in 1916. From 31 December 1930 to 16 April 1932 it was merged with the municipality of [[Cumpas Municipality|Cumpas]].<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1997 |title=División territorial del estado de Sonora de 1810 a 1995 |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222536/702825222536_27.pdf |language=es |page=170 |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1513-8 |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Granados comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and three trustees (''regidores'') elected by [[relative majority]].<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite web |url=https://contraloria.sonora.gob.mx/documentos/compendio-legislativo-basico-estatal/leyes/4721-ley-de-gobierno-y-administracion-municipal-1/file.html |title=Ley de Gobierno y Administración Municipal |language=es |at=Article 30 |date=9 August 2021 |publisher=Government of Sonora |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is José Vinicio Durazo Durazo.<ref>{{cite web |date=8 June 2021 |url=https://www.lavozdesonora.com/vernoticias.php?artid=6128&cat=1 |title=Alcaldías ganadas por los partidos en la contienda para el periodo 2021-2024, de acuerdo al PREP |language=es |website=La Voz de Sonora |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Granados recorded a population of 1009 inhabitants living in 342 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825197995.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Sonora. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=72–73 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=8 January 2022}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 1150 inhabitants in Granados.<ref name=snim/> The municipal seat, also known as [[Granados, Sonora|Granados]], is the only inhabited locality in the municipality.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 35% of Granados's workforce was employed in the primary sector, 15% in the secondary sector, 13% in commerce, and 34% in services.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 10.5: Población ocupada por municipio y su distribución porcentual según sector de actividad económica, al 15 de marzo de 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Sonora 2017 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/Productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825094904.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref> Cattle farming is the main economic activity.<ref name=atlas>{{cite web |url=http://www.ars.gob.mx/municipioshtml/GRANADOS/granados.html |title=Granados |language=es |website=Atlas Estatal de Riesgos para el Estado de Sonora |publisher=Unidad Estatal de Protección Civil, Sonora |access-date=8 January 2022}}</ref>

A paved road runs from Granados north to [[Huásabas]], where it intersects with the highway to Moctezuma and Hermosillo. There is also an airstrip in Granados.<ref name=atlas/>
