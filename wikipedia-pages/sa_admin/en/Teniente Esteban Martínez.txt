'''Teniente Esteban Martínez''' is a [[district]] and locality in the [[Presidente Hayes Department]] of Paraguay. It is located 300&nbsp;km from [[Asunción]] and has a population of 3340 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.dgeec.gov.py/Publicaciones/Biblioteca/proyeccion%20nacional/Proyeccion%20Distrital.pdf|title=Proyección de la población por sexo y edad, según distrito. Revisión 2015|website=Dgeec.gov.py|pages=32–37|accessdate=11 November 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151016011537/http://www.dgeec.gov.py/Publicaciones/Biblioteca/proyeccion%20nacional/Proyeccion%20Distrital.pdf|archive-date=16 October 2015}}</ref> It was established as a category by means of law 3000/06.<ref>{{cite web|url = http://www.tsje.gov.py/legislacion/resoluciones/2007/resoluciones-10_0.html|title = Resolución TSJE N° 10/2007|accessdate = 19 April 2012|website=Tsdje.gov.py}}</ref> On December 30, 2021, a maximum temperature of {{convert|45.3|C|1}} was registered and on January 26, 2022, a maximum temperature of {{convert|45.7|C|1}} was registered, the latest is the highest in meteorological history of Paraguay.
