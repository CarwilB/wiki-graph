'''Azurduy Province'''<ref>{{GEOnet2|32FA88405DDA3774E0440003BA962ED3|Provincia Azurduy (Approved)}}, [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]]</ref> (full official name: '''Província de Juana Azurduy de Padilla''') is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Chuquisaca Department]] in [[Bolivia]]. Its seat is the town of [[Azurduy, Bolivia|Azurduy]]. It was named in honor of revolutionary guerrilla [[Juana Azurduy de Padilla]].  In the 2024 census it had a population of 24,331.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolivia: Administrative Division (Departments and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/ |access-date=2024-09-10 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Censo de Poblacion y Vivienda - 2012: Chuquisaca |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística de Bolivia |url=http://censosbolivia.ine.gob.bo/localidades/c_listado |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722212933/http://censosbolivia.ine.gob.bo/localidades/c_listado |archivedate=22 July 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
