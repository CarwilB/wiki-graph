'''Istmina''' is a small [[city]] and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].  The city of Istmina was founded in 1834 by Juan Nepomuceno Mosquera, initially with the name of San Pablo.  The name was changed in 1903 to Istmina, a contraction of isthmus and minas (mines), two characteristics of the area.  The municipality covers {{Convert|1880|km2|sqmi|0}}, with an average elevation of {{Convert|79|m|ft|0}}, and an average temperature of {{Convert|26.7|C|F|0}}.  Istmina is located {{Convert|75|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from the departmental capital, [[Quibdó]].
