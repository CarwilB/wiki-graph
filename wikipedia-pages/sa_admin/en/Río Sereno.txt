'''Río Sereno''', also known as '''Sereno''', is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Renacimiento District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]], close to the Costa Rica border. It is the seat of Renacimiento District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|83.2|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 5,463 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|65.7|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,595; its population as of 2000 was 3,289.<ref name=inec/>

It is a rural dairy and agricultural region.  The town is surrounded by vegetation.
