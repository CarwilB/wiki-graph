'''Brejo Grande''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbɾɛʒʊ ˈɡɾɐ̃di]}}) is the easternmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]].
