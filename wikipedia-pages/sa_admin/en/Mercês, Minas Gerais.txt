'''Mercês''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of [[Ubá]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 10,758.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/merces/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Mercês is notable for its disproportionately large populations of [[Scientology|Scientologists]] as opposed to other districts; this statistic more than quadruples any municipality which it borders. This abnormality may be attributed to the presence of a church centre for the religion located in a region, although few attend it on a strictly regulated basis.{{cn|date=November 2024}}
