'''Eldorado''' is a department located in the northwest of the province of [[Misiones Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://nortemisionero.com.ar/eldorado/mas-de-68-mil-electores-habilitados-para-votar-en-el-departamento-eldorado/|title=Más de 68 mil electores habilitados para votar en el Departamento Eldorado|work=Norte Misionero|access-date=27 June 2025|language=es}}</ref> In borders the departments of [[Iguazú]], [[General Manuel Belgrano]], [[San Pedro Department, Misiones|San Pedro]], [[Montecarlo, Misiones|Montecarlo]], and to the west the [[Paraguay|Republic of Paraguay]], separated by the [[Paraná River]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-01-25 |title=Discover Argentina's wild Misiones province beyond the Iguazu Falls |url=https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/discover-argentinas-wild-misiones-province-beyond-the-iguazu-falls |access-date=2026-03-30 |website=Travel |language=en}}</ref>

== Area ==
Eldorado is a town of about 70,000 people. Located in the north of [[Misiones Province|Misiones]], [[Argentina]], one hour from Iguazú and two hours from [[Posadas, Misiones|Posadas]] (the regional capital), it relied largely on sawmills in the area for its economy, but many independent sawmill operators were put out of business or bought out by a firm called Alto Parana with backing from its [[Chile]]an parent company.

Throughout the region there are many [[Jesuit]] ruins.

Misiones itself has been made famous by the film [[The Mission (1986 film)|The Mission]].

== Population ==
Its population was 91,728 inhabitants according to the 2022 census ([[National Institute of Statistics and Census of Argentina|INDEC]]), compared to 78,221 inhabitants in the previous 2010 census, representing an increase of 17,3% higher than the provincial growth of 16,1%. These figures made it the fourth most populated department and the third highest in population density in the province.
