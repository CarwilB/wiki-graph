'''Las Vigas de Ramírez''' is a city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].<ref>{{Cite book |last1=Yáñez-Arancibia |first1=A. |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=67ylBYamP7UC |title=Ecological Dimensions for Sustainable Socio Economic Development |last2=Dávalos-Sotelo |first2=R. |last3=Day |first3=J. W. |date=2014-05-13 |publisher=WIT Press |isbn=978-1-84564-756-8 |pages=498 |language=en}}</ref> It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] of the same name.
The city was established by decree on 23 November 1523, named after the teacher {{ill|Rafael Ramírez Castañeda|es}} (1884–1959).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Las Vigas de Ramírez |url=https://veracruz.mx/destino.php?Municipio=132 |access-date=2022-12-05 |website=veracruz.mx |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Rafael Ramírez Castañeda Maestro normalista revolucionario, impulsor de la educación rural en todo México |url=https://www.cndh.org.mx/noticia/rafael-ramirez-castaneda-maestro-normalista-revolucionario-impulsor-de-la-educacion-rural |website=Comisión Nacional de los Derechos Humanos |access-date=8 January 2024}}</ref>

==Geography==
It is located 40&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Xalapa]]; it stands on the [[Mexico City]] to [[Veracruz, Veracruz|Veracruz]] railway and on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highways 180 and 122]].

{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 20,300 inhabitants in 47 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSLASVIGAS>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Las-Vigas-de-Ram%C3%ADrez.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Las Vigas de Ramírez CENSUS]</ref> Las Vigas de Ramírez, the municipal seat, it's the only considered urban with a population of 9,924 inhabitants.<ref name=2020CENSUSLASVIGAS/> Other localities include Barrio de San Miguel (1,762 hab.), El Paisano (1,114 hab.), El Llanillo Redondo (1,113 hab.) and Toxtlacuaya (657 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSLASVIGAS/>

==Economy==
Major agricultural products include corn, coffee, fruits, and rice.
