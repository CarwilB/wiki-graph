'''Campestre de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in central [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location==
There are municipal boundaries with:
*north:  [[Santa Bárbara de Goiás]]
*south:  [[Guapó]]
*east:  [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]]
*west:  [[Palmeiras de Goiás]]

Campestre is 56 kilometres west of the state capital, [[Goiânia]] and 26 kilometers west of [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]].  Connections are made by GO-060 / [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]] / GO-050.

==History==
Campestre de Goiás began in 1949 when Dimas and Antônio Viera settled with their families along the banks of the Córrego Campestre.  In 1963 it was raised to a district of [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]], and in the same year was dismembered to create a new municipality.
