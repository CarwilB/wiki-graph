'''Tuparetama''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 8,261 inhabitants and the total area is 188.428&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[São José do Egito]]    ('''N''');  [[Iguaraci]]    ('''S''');  [[Paraíba]] state   ('''E''');   [[Ingazeira]] and [[Iguaraci]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 185.54&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 560 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot
* Annual average temperature - 23.5 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 377.4&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Tuparetama''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pigs]]s, [[goats]], [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[beans]] and [[maize|corn]].
