'''Motozintla''' ('''Motozintla de Mendoza''') is a [[Settlement classification in Mexico|city]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]].

Motozintla occupies part of Mexico's [[Mexico-Guatemala border|border with Guatemala]] and is adjacent to the municipalities of [[Siltepec]] (to the north), [[Escuintla]] and [[Huixtla]] (to the west), [[Tuzantán]] and [[Tapachula]] (to the south), and [[Mazapa de Madero]] and [[El Porvenir, Chiapas|El Porvenir]] (to the northeast). It covers an area of 782.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 69,119,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Motozintla|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=057|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 59,875 as of 2005.<ref name=Gob>
{{cite web|title=Motozintla|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07057a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=August 1, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517230548/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07057a.htm|archive-date=May 17, 2011}}</ref>

The municipality had 416 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: Motozintla de Mendoza (23,755), classified as urban, and [[Belisario Domínguez, Chiapas|Belisario Domínguez]] (2,011), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
