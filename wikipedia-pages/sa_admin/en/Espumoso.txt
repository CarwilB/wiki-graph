'''Espumoso''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It belongs to the mesoregion "Nordeste Rio Grandense" and the microregion of "Cruz Alta" along with other 13 municipalities: [[Alto Alegre, Rio Grande do Sul|Alto Alegre]], [[Boa Vista do Cadeado]], [[Boa Vista do Incra]], [[Campos Borges]], [[Cruz Alta, Rio Grande do Sul|Cruz Alta]], [[Fortaleza dos Valos]], [[Ibirubá]], [[Jacuizinho]], [[Jóia|Joia]], [[Quinze de Novembro]], [[Saldanha Marinho]], [[Salto do Jacuí]] and [[Santa Bárbara do Sul]]. The estimated population in 2020 was 15,591 inhabitants,<ref name="PopStats">
 
 [https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/espumoso/panorama IBGE 2020]
</ref>
and its total area was 783.065&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>
  IBGE. Diretoria de Pesquisas - DPE -  Coordenação de População e Indicadores Socias - COPIS.
</ref>

In October 2016, Brazilian people voted in order to choose mayors and aldermen.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2016/calendario-eleitoral|title=Calendário Eleitoral|website=Eleições 2016|publisher=Tribunal Superior Eleitoral|access-date=September 17, 2016|archive-date=December 18, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151218030016/http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/eleicoes-2016/calendario-eleitoral|url-status=dead}}</ref> In Espumoso, Douglas Fontana ([[Democratic Labour Party (Brazil)|PDT]]) was elected mayor with 42.28% of the valid votes. The municipal assembly members elected were: Joacir Carmo Sonda (PDT), Zé Carlos (PDT), Marcel Rotta Simon (PDT), Ana Mari Vogel ([[Progressistas|PP]]), Leo Gugel (PDT), Jadir José de Oliveira (PP),  João Valério Mocelin ([[Brazilian Social Democracy Party|PSDB]]), Juliano de Oliveira Fiuza ([[Brazilian Democratic Movement|PMDB]]), and Roberto Iopp  ([[Workers' Party (Brazil)|PT]]).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/candidatos-espumoso-rs/|title=Eleições 2016}}</ref> The municipal political scene has been occupied by two political parties along the recent history, PP and PDT, even though there have been other minor political forces in the city.
