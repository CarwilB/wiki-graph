'''Ullum''' is a department of the [[Argentina|Argentinean]] province of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]]. It is located in the center of the province and its landscape is dominated by mountains and low vegetation. Its seat is [[Villa Ibáñez]]. It is also characterized by the production of [[grape]]s and [[fruit]]. The [[San Juan River (Argentina)|San Juan River]] is dammed by the [[Ullum Dam]].

== Origin of name ==
The name of this department is of Indian origin, translated as ''head of the penis''. This may refer to the place where rituals were held for aspiring young [[Indigenous people of the Americas|Aboriginal]] warriors or to the fertility of the land in the area, alluding directly to male fertility.<ref name="Acosta2000">{{cite book | title=San Juan, Nostra Tierra | publisher=Ediciones Argentina | author=Acosta, Graciela Liliana | year=2000 | location=San Juan | language = Spanish}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The department is located in the center west of the [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan Province]], northwest of the [[San Juan, Argentina|City of San Juan]]. It has a surface area of 4,391 square kilometers. It is bordered by:
* [[Jáchal Department]] to the north
* [[Zonda Department]] and [[Rivadavia Department, San Juan|Rivadavia Department]] to the south
* [[Albardón Department]] to the east
* [[Calingasta Department]], [[Zonda Department]] and [[Iglesia Department]] to the west.

Ullum  is entirely located on the center and foothills of the San Juan Cordillera. Between the mountains are prominent hills: the Lomadas de Las Tapias, Talacasto, Dehesa and Villicum to the east and the Sierra del Tigre and Sierra de la Invernada to the west. In the south of the department the San Juan River is dammed by the Ullum Dam.

The climate is [[arid]] with little precipitation. Temperature ranges from {{convert|35|C|F}} in summer (with highs up to {{convert|40|C|F}}) to {{convert|16|C|F}} during the dry winters, with lows of {{convert|-8|C|F}}. The climate is influenced by the Ullum dam lake and by occasional snowfall in the Talacasto area.

The flora is characterized by [[xerophyte]]s. The fauna includes species such as [[hare]]s,
[[Cougar|puma]], [[viscacha]]s, [[Elegant Crested Tinamou]]s, [[guanaco]]s, various forest birds and [[Neotropical silversides|silversides]].
