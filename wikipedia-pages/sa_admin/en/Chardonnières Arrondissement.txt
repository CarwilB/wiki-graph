'''Chardonnières''' ({{langx|ht|Chadonyè}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of Haiti. Spanning approximately {{cvt|382|km²}}, it comprises three communes&ndash;[[Chardonnières]], [[Les Anglais]], and [[Tiburon, Sud|Tiburon]]. As of 2015, it had a population of 78,410 inhabitants. It stretches from the coastline of the [[Caribbean Sea]] into the [[Massif de la Hotte]] highlands. The economy is based on agriculture, livestock, fishing, and crafts. The annual Grape Festival is a popular celebration.

== Geography ==
Chardonnieres is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of Haiti.<ref name="Min">{{cite web|url=https://www.mindat.org/feature-3727902.html|title=Chardonnieres|work=Mineral Data|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> The commune is located along the coast between Port-à-Piment and Les Anglais.<ref name="Grape">{{cite web|url=https://ayibopost.com/grape-production-a-traditional-crop-threatened-in-the-south-of-haiti/|title=Grape production: a traditional crop threatened|work=AyiboPost|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The landscape transitions from coastal plains into elevated terrain, including the hills of Randel and the foothills of [[Massif de la Hotte]], reaching elevations up to {{cvt|817|m}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://fr-be.topographic-map.com/map-7hl6t6/Chardonni%C3%A8res/|title=Chardonnières |work=Topographic‑Map.com|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The region has a hot [[tropical climate]].<ref name="Min"/> The region was affected during the [[2021 Haiti earthquake]] and went through extensive rebuilding efforts.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.unicef.org/lac/en/stories/six-months-after-earthquake-mothers-chardonnieres-fight-their-childrens-health|title=UNICEF reports on post-earthquake community recovery|work=UNICEF|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref>

== Demographics and economy ==
As of 2015, the population was 78,410 inhabitants. Approximately 43% of the local population is under the age of 18.<ref name="Pop">{{cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|work=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|archive-date=6 November 2015|url-status=dead|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Chardonnières is recognized as Haiti’s primary grape-producing region, with inhabitants traditionally cultivating grapes on domestic arbors.<ref name="Grape"/> The annual Festival du Raisin (Grape festival) in July promotes local viticulture and cultural heritage.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-11625-haiti-social-3rd-edition-of-the-festival-of-the-grape-of-chardonnieres.html|title=3rd Edition of the Festival of the Grape|work=Haiti Libre|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref>
