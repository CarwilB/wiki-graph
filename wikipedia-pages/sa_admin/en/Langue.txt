'''Langue''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle (department)|Valle]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|136.1|km2}} and had a population of 20,944 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
The name "Langue" means "place of canes and reeds" and has its roots in the [[Nahuatl]] and [[Lenca]] languages. The region was inhabited by indigenous groups before the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] arrived, with the earliest mention dating back to 1591, which mentions the town of "San Antonio de Langue". The prefix "San Antonio" was added to the original name, as it was a common practice during the colonial period. The indigenous settlements had been relocated several times over the years, and the Spanish settlers later populated the town, which served as a base for [[gold]] mining in the region. In the 1791 population census, it is mentioned as a town in the curacy of [[Goascorán]] and was part of the municipality of [[Tegucigalpa]]. In the political division of 1896, it is mentioned as a municipality in the Goascorán district. It was elevated to the status of a city on 7 March 1950.<ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Langue|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-langue/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Langue, Valle|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Valle/Langue.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Langue is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle Department|Valle]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Aramecina]] and [[Curaren]] to the north, [[Nacaome]] to the south, [[San Francisco de Coray]] to the east, and [[Goascorán]] and Aramecina to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|136.1|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1707__langue/|title=Langue, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Langue has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Aw). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|31.3|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|203.86 |mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/valle/langue|title=Langue, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Langue had a total population of 20,944 inhabitants, of whom 10,358 (49.5%) were men and 10,586 (50.5%) were women. About 21.4% of the population was classified as urban and 16,471 residents (78.6%) lived in the rural areas.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1707__langue/|title=Langue, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 7,631 individuals (36.4%) were aged 0–14 years, 11,889 individuals (56.8%) were aged 15–64, and 1,424 individuals (6.8%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20.6 years and the mean age was 26.4 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 20.4%, higher than the departmental average of 17.7%. The municipality had 4,335 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.7 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
