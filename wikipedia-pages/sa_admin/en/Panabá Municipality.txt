'''Panabá Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]].<ref name = "panaba">{{cite web
 | title = Panabá
 | work = Municipalities of Yucatan (in Spanish)
 | publisher = Yucatan State Government
 | url = http://www.yucatan.gob.mx/estado/municipios/ver_municipio.php?id=57
 }}</ref> It is located in the eastern region of the [[Yucatán Peninsula]]'s northern coast. Panabá is also the name of the municipality's largest township, which is also its municipal seat (''[[cabecera municipal]]''). The municipality borders [[San Felipe Municipality, Yucatán|San Felipe]] and [[Río Lagartos Municipality|Rio Lagartos]] to the north, [[Tizimín]] to the east, [[Sucilá Municipality|Sucilá]] to the south and the municipalities of [[Dzilam de Bravo Municipality|Dzilam de Bravo]] and [[Dzilam González Municipality|Dzilam González]] to the west.

==Political regionalisation==

The municipality belongs to the First Federal Electoral District and the Tenth Local Election District.<ref name = "panaba"/>

== Foundation ==
{{Unsourced section|date=March 2025}}
Throughout the colonial period, Panabá was under the jurisdiction of [[Valladolid]] through [[Tizimín]]. During the nineteenth century Panabá was part of the municipality of [[Tizimín]] until 1918.

==Indigenous groups==
{{Unsourced section|date=March 2025}}
The population of indigenous language ([[Yucatec Maya]]) speakers (5 years and over) is 2,465 people.

==Demographic trends==

According to the Census of Population and Housing made in June 2010 by the [[INEGI]], the total population of the municipality was 7,461 people, of whom 3,718 are male and 3,713 are female. The total population of the town represents 0.32 per cent of the total population of the state.<ref name = "panaba"/>

==Education, health and housing==

The town has 4 levels of schools: [[preschool]], [[primary school|primary]], [[secondary school|secondary]] and high school. There is a library located in the headquarters. A literacy campaign made by the National Institute for Adult Education ([[Instituto Nacional para la Educación de los Adultos|INEA]]) succeeded in increasing literacy in the municipality. In 1980, there was 663 people counted as illiterate and by 1986 this number was reduced to 136. According to the 2010 census, the literacy rate for people between 15 and 24 years of age was 97.2%.<ref name = "panaba"/>
