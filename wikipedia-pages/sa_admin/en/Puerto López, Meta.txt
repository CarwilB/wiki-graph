''' Puerto Alfonso López''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpweɾto ˈlopes}}) is a small town and municipality in the [[Orinoquía Region (Colombia)|Orinoquía Region]] ([[Llanos]] region) of [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is known as '''Puerto López''' and is a port on the [[Meta River|Meta]] river. The town is located approximately 100 kilometers east of the town of [[Villavicencio]]. Its main industries are agriculture and cattle. 

Puerto Lopez has been said to be the "belly button",<ref name=Porup>{{Cite book|author1=Porup, Jens  |author2=Kohn, Michael|year=2010|title=
Lonely Planet Colombia (Country Guide)|location=Footscray, Victoria, Australia|publisher=Lonely Planet|page=[https://books.google.com/books?id=Kw78V–p1aY4C&pg=PA135 135]|isbn=978-1-74104-827-8}}</ref> or [[geographical center]], of Colombia. Nearby is the Obelisco del Alto de Menegua, a 30-meter-tall<ref name=Porup /> obelisk that marks the intersection of 4 degrees north latitude and 72 degrees west longitude.<ref>{{Cite web|date=29 May 2008|title=Emisión: “4-72, La Red Postal de Colombia”|language=Spanish|publisher=Amigos de la Filatelia Temática en Colombia (Friends of Thematic Philately in Colombia)|url=https://www.afitecol.com/?p=66}}</ref>

==History==
The town was established on 1 May 1935. It was centered on the river port and pre-existing village. On 3 May 1937, the Intendencia for Meta Department ordered the creation of the Yacuana District (''corregimiento'') whose capital is Puerto Alfonso Lopez. The municipality was established on 3 July 1955, in accordance with National Decree No. 2543 of 1945.
