'''Carolina''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Para District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 343. Most of the inhabitants are [[indigenous people|indigenous]].<ref name="census"/>

== Settlements ==
The resort is home to the settlement of [[Jodensavanne]], a Jewish autonomous area which was settled from 1652 onwards, and abandoned after a fire in 1832.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/201cult/joodsgem01.html|title=Jodensavanna en de Joodse gemeenten|access-date=18 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref>

The village of [[Redi Doti]] (also Redidoti) is located near the site of Jodensavanne. The village has been founded in 1930, and is home to an indigenous population of about 100 people of mixed [[Kalina people|Carib]] and [[Arowak]] background.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.jodensavanne.sr.org/smartcms/default.asp?contentID=607|title=Education Redi Doti|website=Jodensavanne.sr|access-date=18 May 2020}}{{Dead link|date=November 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

[[Blaka Watra]] is a [[Stream|creek]] with very dark, near black water. Former Prime minister [[Johan Adolf Pengel]] built his country residence near the creek. The site was opened to the public in 1970, and became an important recreation area. During the [[Surinamese Interior War]] it was the scene of heavy fighting. <ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/de-afobakaweg|title=De Afobakaweg|website=Vakantie Arena|language=nl|access-date=22 May 2020|archive-date=7 June 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200607181941/http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/de-afobakaweg|url-status=dead}}</ref> It has been restored, and regained its status as a recreation area.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN  2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Blaka Watra is located near the indigenous village [[Cassipora]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://fathh.com/suriname/nieuws/44913/het-inheemse-dorp-redi-doti-in-het-district-para-maakt-zich-klaar-voor-het.html|title=Het inheemse dorp Redi Doti in het district Para maakt zich klaar voor het|website=De Ware Tijd via Fathh|access-date=10 January 2022|language=nl|archive-date=12 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211212180457/https://fathh.com/suriname/nieuws/44913/het-inheemse-dorp-redi-doti-in-het-district-para-maakt-zich-klaar-voor-het.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The [[Ndyuka people]] used to have five settlements on the [[Commewijne River]]. The most important were {{ill|Java, Suriname|nl|Java (Suriname)|lt=Java}} and {{ill|Peninica|nl|Peninica (plaats)}}.<ref name="newmont">{{cite web|url=https://s24.q4cdn.com/382246808/files/doc_downloads/operations_projects/south_america/documents/Sabajo_Project_Historical-Narrative-baseline-study.pdf |title=A Historical Narrative of Traditional Lands around the Newmont Sabajo Project|author=Marie-Josee Artist and Florence Rijsdijk|date=March 2018|publisher=[[Newmont]]|access-date=11 January 2022}}</ref>{{rp|21}} The villages were deserted during the Surinamese Interior War, and as of 2018, have very few permanent residents.<ref name="newmont"/>{{rp|25}}

== Transport ==
The resort Carolina is connected with the [[Avobakaweg]] to [[Paramaribo]] via the Carolinabrug. The bridge used to be located in the village of Carolina, however it was damaged, and new bridge has been built near Red Doti in 2014.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.waterkant.net/suriname/2014/12/24/ingebruikname-van-de-nieuwe-carolinabrug/|title=Ingebruikname van de nieuwe Carolinabrug|website=Waterkant|access-date=18 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The road used to continue to [[Snesiekondre]] on the [[Marowijne River]],<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.srherald.com/suriname/2020/09/09/rehabilitatie-weg-naar-mapanegebied-aangevangen/ |title=Rehabilitatie weg naar Mapanégebied aangevangen|website=Suriname Herald|access-date=11 January 2022|language=nl}}</ref> however the bridge over the Commewijne River near Java had been destroyed during the war, and has not been rebuilt.<ref name="newmont"/>{{rp|25}}
