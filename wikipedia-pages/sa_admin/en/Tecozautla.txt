'''Tecozautla''' ([[Otomi language|Otomi]]: '''Masofo''') is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 575.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The name derives from the [[Nahuatl]] words "tetl", meaning "stone"; "cozaqui", meaning "yellow thing"; and "tla", meaning "place of"; making the entire meaning of Tecozautla "place where yellow earth abounds".<ref name=":0">{{cite web|last=Tourism|first=Secretariat of|title=Tecozautla, Hidalgo|url=http://www.gob.mx/sectur/articulos/tecozautla-hidalgo|accessdate=2021-05-25|website=gob.mx|language=en}}</ref>

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 31,609.<ref>{{cite encyclopedia|title=Tecozautla |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13059a.htm|encyclopedia=Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Mexico|publisher=[[National Institute for Federalism and Municipal Development]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref> In 2017 there were 2,415 inhabitants who spoke an [[Indigenous languages of Mexico|indigenous language]],<ref>{{cite web|work=Integral Information System of the State of Hidalgo|title=Basic Statistical Information: Tecozautla Municipal Folder|url=http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/059%20Tecozautla.pdf|publisher=Secretaria de Planeación Desarrollo Regional Regional y Metropolitano|accessdate=22 March 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171230225941/http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/059%20Tecozautla.pdf|archive-date=30 December 2017}}</ref> primarily [[Mezquital Otomi]].<ref>{{cite book|last1=Báez Cubero|first1=Lourdes|last2=Garret Ríos|first2=Gabriela|last3=Pérez González|first3=David|last4=Moreno Alcántara|first4=Beatriz|last5=Fierro Alonso|first5=Ulises Julio|last6=Hernández García|first6=Milton Gabriel|title=Los pueblos indígenas de Hidalgo: Atlas etnográfico|date=2012|editor=Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia|location=México, D.F; México|isbn=978-607-484-357-6|edition=Primera|url=http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|pages=32–35|archive-date=2019-07-25|access-date=2019-05-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190725220343/http://www.difusion.inah.gob.mx/images/ebook/Atlas/atlas-hidalgo/files/assets/common/downloads/LOS%20PUEBLOS%20IND.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> The town was declared a "[[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]]" in 2015.<ref name=":0" />

==Toponymy==

The name ''Tecozautla'' comes from the [[Nahuatl]] ''tetl'' -stone-, ''cozauqui'' -yellow thing- and ''tla'' which means -place of-; which together means "Place where the yellow earth abounds".<ref group=note>The etymological origin of the name of a</ref><ref name="etym">{{cite book|author=State Center for Municipal Studies of the State of Hidalgo|year=1988|title=The Municipalities of Hidalgo|series=Encyclopedia Collection of the municipalities of Mexico|publisher=Ministry of the Interior}}</ref><ref name=gob>{{cite web|title=Tecozautla|url=http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/tecozautla|work=[[Municipalities of Hidalgo]]|publisher=[[Government of the state of Hidalgo]]|accessdate=March 22, 2017}}</ref>

==Geography==

{{See also|Geography of the state of Hidalgo}}

=== Relief and hydrographic ===

As for [[physiography]] it is located within the province of the [[Neovolcanic Axis]]; within the subprovince of Sierras y Llanuras de Querétaro and Hidalgo.<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> 

As for its [[geology]] it corresponds to the [[Neogene]] period (91.86%), [[Cretaceous]] (4.0%) and [[Quaternary]] (2.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> With Extrusive igneous type rocks: basalt (29.86%), acid tuff (26.0%), volcanoclastic (12.0%), basic tuff-basic volcanic breccia (5.0%), acid tuff-acid volcanic breccia (5.0%) and rhyolite-acid tuff (2.0%); sedimentary: sandstone–conglomerate (12.0%) and limestone (4.0%); soil: alluvial (2.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> Regarding [[pedology]] the dominant soil is [[phaeozem]] (45.86%), [[vertisol]] (26.0%), [[calcisol]] (19.0%) and [[leptosol]] (7.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo />

With regard to [[hydrology]] it is positioned in the hydrological region of the [[Pánuco River]]; in the basins of the [[Moctezuma River]]; within the sub-basins of the [[Tula River]] (35.0%), [[San Juan River (Querétaro)|San Juan River]] (30.0%), [[Tecozautla River]] (21.0%) and [[Alfajayucan River]] (14.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> It also has a dam, wells and springs.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

===Weather===

The following climates are located in the municipal territory with their respective percentage: Semi-dry semi-warm (54.0%) and semi-dry temperate (46.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> With an average annual climatological temperature of 17&nbsp;°C with a total annual precipitation of 517 millimeters.<ref name=INAFED />

===Ecology===

The [[flora]] in the municipality is made up of a variety of trees such as oak, oyamel, biznaga, pitaya, huizache, maguey, cactus, organ and a large number of pirul trees, and some fruit trees.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH /> The [[fauna]] is made up of rabbits, rattlesnakes, hares, squirrels, ocelots, hawks, opossums, armadillos, deer, chameleons, coyotes, buzzards, insects and arachnids.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />
