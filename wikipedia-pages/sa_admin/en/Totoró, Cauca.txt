'''Totoró''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Totoró has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb''). It has moderate rainfall year-round. It is the cloudiest and least sunny town in the world.
