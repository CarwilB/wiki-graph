'''Cajolá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]].  In 2023, the municipality of Cajolá had a population of 19,547,  8,776 males to 10,771 females.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cajolá (Municipality, Guatemala) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/quetzaltenango/0907__cajol%C3%A1/ |access-date=2023-11-02 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
