'''Balboa''' is an island [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Panamá Province]] in [[Panama]], covering the offshore [[Pearl Islands]] lying in the Gulf of Panama southeast of Panama City. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 2,336;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 3,332.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of 333&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the town of [[San Miguel, Panamá Province|San Miguel]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
Balboa District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[San Miguel, Panamá Province|San Miguel]] (capital)
*[[La Ensenada]]
*[[La Esmeralda, Panama|La Esmeralda]]
*[[La Guinea]]
*[[Pedro González, Panama|Pedro González]]
*[[Saboga]]
