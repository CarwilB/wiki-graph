'''Batayporã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population is 11,349&nbsp;(2020) and its area is 1,828&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The town was founded in 1954 by [[Jan Antonín Baťa]] as part of his expansion of the [[Bata Corporation|Bata shoe company]], founded by his brother [[Tomáš Baťa|Tomáš]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Bata's World – Brazil |url=http://world.tomasbata.org/america/brazil/ |website=world.tomasbata.org |access-date=8 September 2023}}</ref>

Batayporã is surrounded by agricultural land. It is located south of [[Anaurilândia]] at the junction of MS-395 and MS-134 (BR376), paved single-lane highways.{{Citation needed|date=September 2023}}

Other towns in Brazil linked to Baťa include [[Anaurilândia]], [[Batatuba]], [[Bataguassu]], and [[Mariápolis]].
