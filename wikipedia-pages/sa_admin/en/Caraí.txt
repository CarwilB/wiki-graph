'''Caraí''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 23,780 people living in a total area of 1,240&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. The elevation of the municipal seat is 750 meters. It became a municipality in 1948.

The economy is based on cattle raising, services, and [[subsistence agriculture]], with the main crops being coffee (2,600 ha.), rice, beans, sugarcane, and corn. The cattle herd had 17,000 head in 2006. In 2005 there were 2633 rural producers but only 20 tractors. 8,700 people were dependent on agriculture. {{as of|2005}} there were 8 public health clinics, with none carrying out diagnosis and complete therapy. There was one hospital with 35 beds. Educational needs were met by 29 primary schools, 3 middle schools and 7 nursery schools. There were 231 automobiles in 2006, giving a ratio of 90 inhabitants per automobile (there were 525 motorcycles). There was 1 bank in 2007.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities are: [[Padre Paraíso]] - [[Catuji]] - [[Itaipé]] - [[Novo Cruzeiro]] - [[Araçuaí]]. The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]], the state capital, is 536&nbsp;km. The distance to the nearest major population center, [[Teófilo Otoni]], is 80&nbsp;km.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.aondefica.com/centerleft_mg.asp |title=Distancias Minas Gerais |access-date=2008-07-25 |archive-date=2010-07-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100707145655/http://www.aondefica.com/centerleft_mg.asp |url-status=usurped }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/aracuai/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-07-25 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080501121853/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/aracuai/ |archive-date=2008-05-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Social Indicators ==

Caraí is ranked low on the MHDI and was one of the poorest municipalities in the state and in the country in 2000.
*MHDI: .636 (2000)
*State ranking: 775 out of 853 municipalities
*National ranking: 4,001 out of 5,138 municipalities in 2000
*Life expectancy: 67
*Literacy rate: 65
*Combined primary, secondary and tertiary gross enrolment ratio: .787
*Per capita income (monthly): R$84.00 <ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>
*Degree of urbanization: 31.94
*Percentage of urban residences connected to sewage system: 73.30
*Infant mortality rate: 35.44 <ref>[http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/CARAI.pdf Indicadores de Saúde] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161204/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/CARAI.pdf |date=July 6, 2011 }}</ref>

The above figures can be compared with those of [[Poços de Caldas]], which had an MHDI of .841, the highest in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The highest in the country was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in the state of [[São Paulo]] with an MHDI of .919. The lowest was [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] with an MHDI of .467 out of a total of 5504 municipalities in the country {{as of|2004|lc=y}}.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
