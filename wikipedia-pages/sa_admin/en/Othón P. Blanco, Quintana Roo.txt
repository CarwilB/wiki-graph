'''Othón P. Blanco''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|eleven subdivisions (''municipios'')]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of  [[Quintana Roo]]. It had a {{MX wikidata|population_as_of}} population of {{MX wikidata|population_total}} people. Its municipal seat is the city of [[Chetumal]], which also serves as the state capital. The municipality is named after [[Othón P. Blanco Núñez de Cáceres]].

The municipal government is headed by the municipal president of Othón P. Blanco (mayor of Othón P. Blanco, aka mayor of Chetumal).

It was formerly the fifth-largest municipality in land area in Mexico at {{convert|17,189.7|km2}},<ref name="e-local.gob.mx"/> occupying more than a third of the entire state.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://comunicacionopb.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=36&Itemid=37 |title=Othón P. Blanco, Ubicación Geográfica |access-date=2009-11-15 |archive-date=2009-07-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090704014532/http://www.comunicacionopb.org/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=36&Itemid=37 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It lost about 40% of its territory when the [[Bacalar Municipality]] was created out of Othón P. Blanco on February 2, 2011.

==Ecosystems==
The vegetation found in the municipality of Othon P. Blanco is mostly of medium forest, spanning most of the interior of the municipality and there are more representative plant species are the [[sapodilla]], the ramon, the [[guayabillo (disambiguation)|guayabillo]]<!--Spanish WP links to a DAB page--> and [[Chaco (plant)|chaco]], more isolated areas within the municipality is populated by high forest, where you can find the siricote, the palo de tinte and [[mahogany]], to the southwest of the town are engaged in areas of rainfed agriculture and irrigation, the main crop of [[sugarcane]], along with the Caribbean coast can be traced mainly [[mangroves]].<ref>{{cite web |title = Quintana Roo. Mapa de Agricultura y Vegetación |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/qroo/agri.cfm |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20120127094800/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/qroo/agri.cfm |archivedate = 2012-01-27 }}</ref>

The fauna is rich and varied, among the main species is the [[manatee]], marine mammal that lives in bays and lagoons lictors and has become a symbol of the region, and one can find species such as [[agouti]], wild boar, [[white-tailed deer]], [[otter]]s, [[turtle]]s and birds.

For the protection of animal and plant species in Othon P. Blanco there are three zones of ecological reserve, the area of Protection of Flora and Fauna Uaymil located on the northeast coast of the territory, near Mahahual, and the Manatee Sanctuary in the Bay of Chetumal and the Biosphere Reserve Banco Chinchorro.

==Tourism==

Located on the coast, the eco resort of Kabah-na offers only four cabins in a hexagon shape to circulate the air and thatched roofs which keep the interior temperature cool. A complex system of solar panels provides energy 24 hours a day as well as hot water. The resort offers restaurant, beach bar, outdoor showers, hammocks, beach volleyball, canoes, snorkeling equipment and more.<ref name="kabahna">{{cite journal |date=December 2009|title=Kabah-na |journal=Mexico Desconocido |volume=394 |page=33 |publisher=Grupo Editorial Impresiones Aéreas |location=Mexico City |issn=1870-9397 |language=Spanish }}</ref>

At Laguna de Bacalar is Rancho Encantado which is located on the edge of a lagoon. It consists of twelve cabins palm-thatched roofs, handmade furniture and decorated with Mexican accents. The cabins are surrounded by tropical gardens in which live more than 150 species of birds among the tropical fruit orchards.<ref name="encantado">{{cite journal |date=December 2009|title=Rancho Encantado |journal=Mexico Desconocido |volume=394 |page=33 |publisher=Grupo Editorial Impresiones Aéreas |location=Mexico City |issn=1870-9397 |language=Spanish }}</ref>
