'''Tierra Nueva''' is a town and [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipality]] in the central [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |archive-date=September 19, 2004 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040919125340/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |title=Estado De San Luis Potosí |accessdate=2023-05-16}}</ref> It has close to 5,000 inhabitants and is at 1,780 meters above sea level.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tierra-nueva-5/|title = Tierra Nueva (San Luis Potosí) Tierra Nueva}}</ref>

In 2023, Tierra Nueva was designated a [[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]] by the Mexican government, recognizing its cultural and historical importance.<ref>{{Cite web |title=These are Mexico's newest Magical Towns |url=https://www.travelweekly.com/Mexico-Travel/Insights/Mexico-names-new-Magical-Towns |access-date=2025-09-15 |website=www.travelweekly.com |language=en}}</ref>
