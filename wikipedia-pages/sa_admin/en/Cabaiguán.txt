'''Cabaiguán''' ({{IPA|es|kaβajˈɣwan}}) is a [[municipio|municipality]] and town in the [[Provinces of Cuba|province]] of [[Sancti Spiritus Province|Sancti Spíritus]], [[Cuba]]. With an urban population of 42,075<ref>Data referred to the main town, whole municipality counts 63,981 inhabitants</ref> is the [[List of cities in Cuba|3rd largest town]] of the province.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cabaiguán (Municipality, Cuba) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/sancti_sp%C3%ADritus/2804__cabaigu%C3%A1n/ |access-date=2024-02-05 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> It is bordered to the north by the municipality of [[Yaguajay, Cuba|Yaguajay]], to the east with the municipality of [[Taguasco]], to the south with the municipality of [[Sancti Spíritus]] with the Tuinicú River serving as its limit, to the southwest with [[Fomento]] and to the west and northwest with [[Placetas]], a municipality within the province of [[Villa Clara Province|Villa Clara]]. It has a territorial area of 598.98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Overview==
It was founded in 1894,<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1926. The municipality is divided into the ''consejos populares'' (i.e. "popular councils") of Cabaiguán and the villages of Neiva, Pedro Barba, [[Guayos]] and [[Santa Lucía, Cabaiguán|Santa Lucía]].<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url= http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vcabaiguan/index.htm| title=Cabaiguán| author=Guije.com| accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Cabaiguán had a population of 63,981.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|597|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=https://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|110|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. Cabaiguan was known as the Cuban epicenter of migration from the Canary Islands.

==Economy==
Cabaiguán is best known for its cigars; and there is a small cigar factory where various brands of international renown are produced. It also has an oil refinery built in 1947 and the Nieves Morejon quarry, where the Provincial Prison is located.

==Transport==
The town straddles the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] highway and is served by the [[Autopista A1 (Cuba)|A1 motorway]]. It has a railway station on the central [[Havana]]-[[Santiago de Cuba|Santiago]] line.
