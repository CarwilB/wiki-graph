'''San Rafael Pie de la Cuesta''' ({{IPA|es|san rafaˈel pje ðe la ˈkwesta}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]].

A small town of 4,320 residents.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://allthecities.com/cities/san-rafael-pie-de-la-cuesta|title=San Rafael Pie de La Cuesta in Guatemala: general information, weather, map, photo and video|website=AllTheCities|language=en-US|access-date=2020-03-24}}</ref> Red tiled roofs and abundant flowering trees are found there. It is located 13&nbsp;km southwest of San Marcos. The English translation of the town's name is "At the foot of the steep slope."  That is because the town is found along a winding, steep road from San Rafael which rises from 900 meters to 2500 meters in only 15&nbsp;km.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.cayaya-birding.com/san_rafael_pie_de_la_cuesta.htm|title=CAYAYA BIRDING hotspot: Refugio del Quetzal San Rafael Pie de la Cuesta|website=www.cayaya-birding.com|access-date=2020-03-24}}</ref> The town is in the northern department of San Marcos, Guatemala, bordering Mexico. San Rafael Pie de la Cuesta is warmer than the rest of the department. As its name implies, San Rafael Pie de la Cuesta lies at the base of the [[Sierra Madre de Chiapas|Sierra Madre]] mountain range. There are many [[Coffee production in Guatemala|coffee plantations]] in the area.

== Bird sanctuary ==
The municipality oversees a bird reserve called El Refugio del Quetzal. It is part of the Tacan-Tajumulco Important Bird Area and designated by BirdLife International.<ref name=":0" />
