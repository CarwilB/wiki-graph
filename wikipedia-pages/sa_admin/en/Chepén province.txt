'''Chepén''' is one of the twelve [[provinces of Peru|provinces]] that make up the [[La Libertad Region]] of [[Peru]]. It is bordered on the north by the [[Lambayeque Region]], on the east by the [[Cajamarca Region]], on the south by the [[Pacasmayo Province|province of Pacasmayo]], and on the west by the [[Pacific Ocean]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Chepén]].
