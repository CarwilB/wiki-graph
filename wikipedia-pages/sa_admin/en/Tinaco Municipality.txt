The '''Tinaco Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 32,564.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Tinaco]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Tinaco Municipality.{{Citation needed| date=August 2024}}

==Demographics==
The Tinaco Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 31,371 (up from 27,203 in 2000).  This amounts to 10.4% of the state's population. The municipality's population density is {{convert|28.57|PD/sqkm}}.{{Citation needed| date=August 2024}}

==Government==
The mayor of the Tinaco Municipality is Teodoro Venancio Bolívar Caballero, elected on October 31, 2004, with 65% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | title=Alcalde de Municipio - TINACO | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303195313/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2016-03-03}}</ref>   He replaced Enrique Centeno shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 - Directorio de Alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - Directory of elected Mayors |access-date=2008-03-14 |archive-date=2006-10-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]es; General en Jefe José Laurencio Silva (the parish's name was officially changed on December 30, 1994, it was formerly known as Tinaco Parish).{{Citation needed | date=August 2024}}
