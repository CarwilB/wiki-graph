'''Ahuacatlán''' ({{IPA|es|awakaˈtɬan}}) is both a municipality and a municipal seat in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Nayarit]]. The municipal seat had a population of 8,819 in 2005. [https://web.archive.org/web/20070502033408/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/nayarit/mpios/18002a.htm]

In 2023, Ahuacatlán was designated a [[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]] by the Mexican government, recognizing its cultural and historical importance.<ref>{{Cite web |title=These are Mexico's newest Magical Towns |url=https://www.travelweekly.com/Mexico-Travel/Insights/Mexico-names-new-Magical-Towns |access-date=2025-09-15 |website=www.travelweekly.com |language=en}}</ref> 

== Geography ==
It is located in the southeastern part of the state and has boundaries with the following municipalities:  in the north with [[San Pedro Lagunillas]], [[Santa María del Oro, Nayarit|Santa María del Oro]] and [[Jala, Nayarit|Jala]]; in the south with the state of [[Jalisco]] and the municipality of [[Amatlán de Cañas]]; in the east with  [[Ixtlán del Río]], [[Jala, Nayarit|Jala]] and [[Amatlán de Cañas]]; and in the west with [[San Pedro Lagunillas]].

The area of the municipality is 466.60 square kilometers and the population was 14,114 in 2005.  The municipal seat had a population of 8,819 in 2005. [https://web.archive.org/web/20070502033408/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/nayarit/mpios/18002a.htm]

== Etymology ==
The name Ahuacatlán, means "place where there is a lot of avocado", coming from the [[Nahuatl]] words  "Tlan", which means place; and "Ahua" which means "avocado".

== Economy ==
The main economic activity is agriculture with the growing of [[avocado]]s, [[sugarcane]], [[maize]], [[sorghum]], [[peanut]]s, and [[bean]]s.  There is also cattle and pig raising. Small industries produce [[molasses]], [[tequila]], and building materials.
