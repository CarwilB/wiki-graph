'''Cardenal Caro Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia Cardenal Caro}}<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.leychile.cl/Navegar?idNorma=7020 | title=Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional &#124; Ley Chile }}</ref>) is one of the three [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the central [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Libertador General Bernardo O'Higgins Region|O'Higgins]] (VI). The capital of Cardenal Caro is [[Pichilemu]].<ref name="Civica">{{cite web|url=http://www.gobernacioncardenalcaro.gov.cl/ino_civica.html |title=Información Cívica |year=2010 |publisher=Government of Cardenal Caro Province |language=es |access-date=2010-11-09 |location=[[Pichilemu]], [[Chile]] |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707011013/http://www.gobernacioncardenalcaro.gov.cl/ino_civica.html |archive-date=2011-07-07 }}</ref>

==Name==
The province is named after [[Cardinal (Catholicism)|Cardinal]] [[José María Caro Rodríguez]], native of Pichilemu, and who was the first Cardinal of Chile.

==History==
On July 13, 1973, [[President of Chile|President]] [[Salvador Allende|Salvador Allende Gossens]] decreed the creation of the [[Cardenal Caro Department]]. The decree was published in the [[Diario Oficial de la República de Chile]] in August of the same year, making it official.<ref name="Saldías"/> [[Marchigüe]] was declared the capital. However, the coup d'état that occurred in September of that year made the decree "dead text".<ref name="Saldías"/>

The province of Cardenal Caro was created on October 3, 1979 by General [[Augusto Pinochet]].<ref name="Saldías"/> The communes of [[Litueche]] (formerly El Rosario), [[La Estrella, Chile|La Estrella]], [[Marchigüe]], [[Paredones]], and [[Pichilemu]], originally from [[Colchagua Province]]; and [[Navidad, Chile|Navidad]], originally from [[San Antonio Province]], formed the province.<ref name="Saldías"/>

==Administration==
As a province, Cardenal Caro is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]] of Chile, governed by a provincial delegate who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]]. Before 2021, the province was administered by the [[governor of Cardenal Caro]], also appointed by the president of the Republic. Since that year, it is administered by the [[presidential provincial delegate of Cardenal Caro]]. The province is composed by six [[Communes of Chile|communes]] ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''comunas''), each of which is governed by a popularly elected [[alcalde]].

==Geography and demography==
According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|3324.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> and had a population of 41,160 inhabitants (22,127 men and 19,033 women), giving it a population density of {{convert|12.4|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.  Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 11.2% (4,151 persons). persons).<ref name= "INE"/>
