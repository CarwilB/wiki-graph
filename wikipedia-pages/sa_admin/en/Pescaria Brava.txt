'''Pescaria Brava''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the [[South Region, Brazil|South region]] of [[Brazil]]. The municipality was established in January 2013.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.clicrbs.com.br/diariocatarinense/jsp/default.jsp?uf=2&local=18&section=Pol%EDtica&newsID=a2632015.xml|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120724125532/http://www.clicrbs.com.br/diariocatarinense/jsp/default.jsp?uf=2&local=18&section=Pol%EDtica&newsID=a2632015.xml|url-status=dead|archive-date=July 24, 2012|title=Santa Catarina ganha mais dois municípios|publisher=[[Diário Catarinense]]|date=August 26, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Novos municípios catarinenses, Pescaria Brava e Balneário Rincão, vivem a primeira eleição municipal|url=http://diariocatarinense.clicrbs.com.br/sc/politica/eleicoes-2012/noticia/2012/10/novos-municipios-catarinenses-pescaria-brava-e-balneario-rincao-vivem-a-primeira-eleicao-municipal-3910290.html|publisher=[[Diário Catarinense]]|date=October 7, 2012|language=pt|access-date=January 15, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141218033358/http://diariocatarinense.clicrbs.com.br/sc/politica/eleicoes-2012/noticia/2012/10/novos-municipios-catarinenses-pescaria-brava-e-balneario-rincao-vivem-a-primeira-eleicao-municipal-3910290.html|archive-date=December 18, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref>
