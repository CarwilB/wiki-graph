'''Santa María de la Paz''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|165|km}} southwest of the state capital of [[Zacatecas City]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Santa María de la Paz is located at an elevation between {{convert|1800|and(-)|2700|m}} in the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] in southwestern Zacatecas. It borders the municipalities of [[Tepechitlán]] to the north, [[Jalpa, Zacatecas|Jalpa]] to the northeast, [[Apozol Municipality|Apozol]] to the east, [[Juchipila]] to the southeast, [[Teúl de González Ortega Municipality|Teúl de González Ortega]] to the south,  and [[Florencia de Benito Juárez|Benito Juárez]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32058.pdf |title=Santa María de la Paz, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|278.4|km2}} and comprises 0.4% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 29.1% of the land in Santa María de la Paz is used for agriculture. The remainder of the land comprises forest (42.7%), grassland (27.7%), and grassland (0.4%). The municipality is situated in the drainage basin of the [[Río Grande de Santiago]]. Most of the municipality is drained by the Tlaltenango River, a tributary of the [[Bolaños River]], while the eastern fifth of the municipality is drained by the [[Juchipila River]].<ref name=compendium/> There is a small reservoir named El Izote located immediately east of the municipal seat.<ref name=plan>{{cite web |url=http://www.santamariadelapazzac.gob.mx/PDM%20SANTA%20MARIA%20DE%20LA%20PAZ%20LISTO.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |pages=19, 31–32 |publisher=Santa María de la Paz |date=January 2017 |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref>

Santa María de la Paz has a temperate climate with dry winters. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
After the [[Mixtón War]], the friar {{ill|Michele da Bologna|es|Miguel de Bolonia}} founded the hospital of ''Nuestra señora de la Limpia Concepción'' {{circa|1542}} at what is now Santa María de la Paz.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32058a.html |title=Santa María de la Paz |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref><ref name=history>{{cite web |url=http://www.santamariadelapazzac.gob.mx/historia.html |title=Historia |language=es |publisher=Municipality of Santa María de la Paz |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref>

Santa María de la Paz was first established as a municipality in the ''partido'' of Tlaltenango<ref name=amador>{{cite book |last=Amador |first=Elias |title=Elementos de geografia del estado de Zacatecas |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=AUwTAAAAYAAJ |publisher=Tip. de la Escuela de artes y oficios en Guadalupe |location=Zacatecas |date=1894 |pages=72, 78 |language=es |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref> from 1869 to 1905.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=history/> It recorded a population of 2500 inhabitants in 1894.<ref name=amador/> Afterwards it was subsumed under the municipality of Teúl, which changed its name to Teúl de González Ortega in 1935. The 1944 Zacatecas Constitution established the ''congregación'' of Ignacio Allende in Teúl de González Ortega.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref> On 26 October 2004, the state government approved the reestablishment of the municipality of Santa María de la Paz from the ''congregación'' of Ignacio Allende, which took effect on 1 January 2005.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.congresozac.gob.mx/lviiilegislatura/content/Direccion%20Juridica/Decretos/decreto20.htm |title=Decreto #20 |language=es |publisher=[[Congress of Zacatecas]] |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Santa María de la Paz comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is José Manuel González Dorado.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Santa María de la Paz recorded a population of 2767 inhabitants living in 860 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=96–97 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=10 October 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 2821 inhabitants in Santa María de la Paz.<ref name=snim/>

There are 19 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Santa María de la Paz, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=058 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Santa María de la Paz |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=10 October 2021}}</ref> It recorded a population of 1995 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The main economic activities in Santa María de la Paz are agriculture and cattle farming. The main crop grown is corn.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=plan/>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 23|Federal Highway 23]] runs north–south through the municipality, connecting it to [[Jerez de García Salinas|Jerez]] and [[Fresnillo]] in the north, and [[Zapopan]] and [[Guadalajara]] in the south.
