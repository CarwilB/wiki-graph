'''Sopachuy''' is a town located in [[Tomina Province]] in the [[Chuquisaca Department]] in south-central [[Bolivia]]. It is the seat of the [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]] of the same name. At the time of the [[2001 Bolivian census|2001 census]], the municipality had 7,241 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.udape.gob.bo/portales_html/portalSIG/atlasUdape1234567/atlas04_2003/CUADROS/Poblacion.htm |title=Municipios: Población Total Área y Sexo, Censo 2001 |language=es |trans-title=Municipalities: Total Population Area and Sex, 2001 Census |access-date=17 August 2024}}</ref>

Sopachuy was founded on 30 October 1581, and was the site of the [[Battle of Sopachuy]] in 1817.
