'''Valdivia Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Valdivia}}; {{IPA|es|balˈdiβja|pron}}) is one of two [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the southern [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Los Ríos Region|Los Ríos]] (XIV). The provincial capital is [[Valdivia (city)|Valdivia]]. Located in the province are two important rivers, the Calle-Calle / [[Valdivia River]] and the [[Cruces River]]. It is part of Northern Patagonia and its wild virgin forest embrace the Patagonian Cordillera following the river Calle Calle down to the Pacific Ocean. It is known in Patagonia by the term "Bosque Valdiviano", referring to the primitive forest of Valdivia with its native trees. These forests are present in some parts of Northern Patagonia, both in Chile and Argentina.

According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|10197.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> and had a population of 259,243 inhabitants (128,972 men and 130,271 women), giving it a population density of {{convert|25.4|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. Of these, 192,066 (74.1%) lived in urban areas and 67,177 (25.9%) in rural areas. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 10.9% (25,547 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

== Culture ==
The Valdivia International Film Festival (FICV) is the most important film event in Chile, one of the most important worldwide and in Latin America. It has been held since 1994, generally during the month of October.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-07-06 |title=Festival Internacional de Cine de Valdivia |url=https://ficvaldivia.cl/ |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=FICValdivia |language=es-ES}}</ref>

The Valdivia Book Fair is organized annually by the city's Municipal Cultural Corporation, with the support of the Los Ríos Governor's Office, in the Saval Park.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Todo lo que necesitas saber sobre la 29° Feria del Libro de Valdivia |url=https://www.diariofutrono.cl/noticia/actualidad/2021/11/todo-lo-que-necesitas-saber-sobre-la-29-feria-del-libro-de-valdivia |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=Diario Futrono |language=es}}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=April 2026}} In this context, several authors born in the [[Los Ríos Region]] stand out, such as [[Maha Vial]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Marimón |first=Guido Macari |date=2020-10-27 |title=Muere la poeta y actriz Maha Vial |url=https://www.latercera.com/culto/2020/10/27/muere-la-poeta-y-actriz-maha-vial/ |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=La Tercera}}</ref> Iván Espinoza Riesco, [[José Baroja]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fernández |first=Antonio Cazás |title=“La escritura le aportó sentido, coherencia e identidad a mi vida” |url=https://www.elcorreogallego.es/el-correo-2/la-escritura-le-aporto-sentido-coherencia-e-identidad-a-mi-vida-AP10794051 |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=www.elcorreogallego.es |language=es-ES}}</ref> Aldo Astete Cuadra, Efraín Miranda Cárdenas, by name a few.{{Citation needed|date=April 2026}}

The Valdivia International Sculpture Symposium contest is listed as one of the most important events in Chile and prestigious in Latin America.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Padilla |first=Leslie Cares |title=Convocatorias Simposio Internacional de Escultura Valdivia 2022 |url=https://www.ccm-valdivia.cl/web/index.php/component/k2/item/127-convocatorias-simposio-internacional-de-valdivia-2022 |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=www.ccm-valdivia.cl |language=en-gb |archive-date=2022-08-08 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220808151213/https://www.ccm-valdivia.cl/web/index.php/component/k2/item/127-convocatorias-simposio-internacional-de-valdivia-2022 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The Valdivia International Jazz Festival was born in July 2000. Today it is considered the oldest festival in Chile and one of the most important in this musical genre in the Southern Cone.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Festival Internacional de Jazz de Valdivia |url=https://comunidadcreativalosrios.cultura.gob.cl/catalogo/festival-internacional-de-jazz-de-valdivia/ |access-date=2023-07-20 |website=comunidadcreativalosrios.cultura.gob.cl}}</ref>
