'''Tafí del Valle Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 13,883 (2001) and an area of 2,741&nbsp;km². The seat of the department is in [[Tafí del Valle]].
