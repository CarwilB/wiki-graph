'''Villa Díaz Ordaz''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Tlacolula District]] in the east of the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales Region]].The name of the town was formally called Santo Domingo del Valle.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 5,859.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20560a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=June 12, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20041211142122/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20560a.htm|archive-date=December 11, 2004|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref>
