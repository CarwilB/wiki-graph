'''Serro''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[metropolitan area]] of [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of [[Conceição do Mato Dentro]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 20,940.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/serro/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

In colonial times the city was known by the name ''Vila do Príncipe'' (''{{Langx|en|Prince Town}}''). In this city were born [[Emerico Lobo de Mesquita]], one of the most prominent composers of the [[Classicism]] movement in Brazil, and [[Gomes Carneiro]], a general who fought on the [[Paraguayan War]] and on the [[Federalist Revolution]].

Serro is well known for its traditional cheese, cultural richness, colonial influence and unique environment.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|4696|ha|adj=on}} [[Pico do Itambé State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual do Pico do Itambé – IEF}}|title=Parque Estadual do Pico do Itambé|publisher=IEF: Instituto Estadual de Florestas|url=http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/areas-protegidas/206?task=view|access-date=2016-12-19|archive-date=2019-03-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190327211616/http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/areas-protegidas/206?task=view|url-status=dead}}</ref>
