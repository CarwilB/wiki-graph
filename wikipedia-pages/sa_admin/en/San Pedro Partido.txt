'''San Pedro Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] located in the north of the [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[Provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Buenos Aires province|Buenos Aires]]. Its capital city is [[San Pedro, Buenos Aires|San Pedro]]. With an area of {{cvt|1,322|km2}}, the population of the partido was 55,234 ({{census-ar|2001}}).

==Economy==

The economy of San Pedro Partido is dominated by farming, and the main agricultural products include [[wheat]], [[soybean]] and [[fruit]].

Other sources of revenue include factories, service industries and [[ecotourism]].
