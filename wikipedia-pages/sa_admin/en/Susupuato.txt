'''Susupuato''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. The municipality has an area of 273.33 square kilometres (0.46% of the surface of the state)<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan Michoacán] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517162648/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_michoacan |date=2011-05-17 }} ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Retrieved on October 6, 2007</ref> and is bordered to the north by the municipality of [[Juárez, Michoacán|Juárez]], to the east  and south by the state of [[Estado de Mexico|México]], and to the west by [[Tuzantla]]. The municipality had a population of 7,703 inhabitants according to the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=2007-10-06 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2013-04-06 |df= }}</ref>  Its municipal seat is the city of '''Susupuato de Guerrero'''.

In [[pre-Columbian]] days the region was home to [[Purépecha people|Purépecha]], [[Mazahua people|Mazahua]], [[Otomi people|Otomi]] and [[Nahua peoples|Nahua]] people.
