'''Samayac''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It lies at an elevation of 615 metres above [[sea level]] and covers an area of 24.3 km².
