}}'''Simonésia''' is a municipality in the state of [[Minas Gerais]], Brazil. It lies in the mesoregion of Zona da Mata and the microregion of Manhuaçu. The municipality was officially created on 31 December 1943.<ref name="admin">{{Cite web |title=Formação Administrativa – Simonésia |url=https://ciscaparao.mg.gov.br/simonesia |website=CIS Caparaó |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

== Geography and demographics ==
Simonésia is located in the Zona da Mata region of eastern Minas Gerais. It covers an area of approximately 487.85 km² and sits at altitudes ranging from about 598 m (e.g., Cachoeira da Neblina) up to around 1,647 m at its mountainous headwaters.<ref name="topo">{{Cite web |title=Plano de Manejo da RPPN Mata do Sossego – Simonésia/MG |url=https://gov.br/icmbio/pt-br/assuntos/biodiversidade/unidade-de-conservacao/unidades-de-biomas/cerrado/lista-de-ucs/rppn-estacao-biologica-mata-do-sossego/arquivos/rppn_mata_sossego_pmplanodemanejo.pdf |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

According to estimates, the population was about 19,736 in 2020.<ref name="pop2020">{{Cite web |title=Simonésia – Municipal data |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/brazil/regiaosudeste/admin/minas_gerais/3167608__simon%C3%A9sia/ |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

Residents are known as ''simonesienses''.<ref name="gent">{{Cite web |title=Conheça a Cidade – Simonésia |url=https://cmsimonesia.mg.gov.br/a-cidade/conheca-a-cidade |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

== History ==
The region’s initial settlement around a river valley, later called São Simão, is dated to around 1855 when explorers arrived.<ref name="hist1">{{Cite web |title=Simonésia – Turismo em Minas Gerais |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/destinos/simonesia |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref> The district was elevated and subsequently the municipality of Simonésia was created by Decree-Law 1.058 of 31 December 1943, having been desegregated from the municipality of Manhuaçu.<ref name="admin"/>

== Economy and environment ==
The economy of Simonésia is based significantly on agriculture, especially coffee cultivation, with complementary activities in livestock and services.<ref name="econ">{{Cite web |title=Simonésia – Portal Naeco |url=https://naeco.com.br/simonesia-mg |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

The municipality holds part of the private natural heritage reserve (RPPN) known as the [[Sossego Forest Biological Station]] (Estação Biológica Mata do Sossego), which protects [[Atlantic Forest]] remnants, mountainous terrain and rich biodiversity.<ref name="env">{{Cite web |title=Sossego Forest Biological Station |url=https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sossego_Forest_Biological_Station |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>

== Tourism and culture ==
Simonésia offers natural attractions including waterfalls (such as the Cachoeira do Marreco) and forested hillsides, and hosts cultural events like its Carnival and the “Simonésia Fest”.<ref name="tour">{{Cite web |title=Simonésia – Turismo em Minas Gerais |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/destinos/simonesia |access-date=2025-11-09}}</ref>
