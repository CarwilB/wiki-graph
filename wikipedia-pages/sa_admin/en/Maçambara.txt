'''Maçambará''' is a small [[Brazil]]ian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 4,562 (2020 est.) in an area of 1,682.82&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2AFA Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 110 m. It is located west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]] and northeast of [[Alegrete]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|4392|ha|adj=on}} [[São Donato Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1975 that protects an area of wetlands on the Butuí River, a tributary of the [[Uruguay River]].<ref>{{citation|title=Inaugurado em São Borja Escritório da Reserva Biológica do São Donato|publisher=Secretaria do Ambiente e Desenvolvimento Sustentável do Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://www.sema.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu=4&cod_conteudo=2937|date=27 May 2002|access-date=2016-04-19|language=pt|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160504223425/http://www.sema.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu=4&cod_conteudo=2937|archive-date=4 May 2016}}</ref>
