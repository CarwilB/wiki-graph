'''Lalaquiz District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of [[Huancabamba Province]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. == History ==
Lalaquiz District was created by law on December 30, 1983, in [[Fernando Belaúnde]]'s term.
