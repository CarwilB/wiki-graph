'''Novo Aripuanã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]].

==History==

The region was originally inhabited by the Toras, Barés, Muras, Urupás, Araras and other indigenous peoples.
The first records of European penetration to the Madeira River are from 1637, when [[Pedro Teixeira]] travelled from [[Belém]] to [[Quito]] in [[Ecuador]].{{sfn|Novo Aripuanã, Amazonas - AM:  Histórico – IBGE}}
The municipality of Novo Aripuanã was created by state law 96 of 19 December 1955 from parts of the municipalities of Borba and Manicoré.
It contained the district of Foz do Aripuanã with the sub-districts of Alvorada, Manicorezinho and Itapinima, and the district of Sumaúma with the sub-districts of Alvorada, Manicorezinho and Itapinima.{{sfn|Novo Aripuanã, Amazonas - AM:  Histórico – IBGE}}
The town of Foz do Aripuanã was elevated to the status of a city, named Novo Aripuanã.
The first prefect of the municipality, Wilson Paula de Sá, took office on 10 February 1956.{{sfn|Novo Aripuanã, Amazonas - AM:  Histórico – IBGE}}

==Geography==
===Location===

Novo Aripuanã has an area of {{convert|41.191|km2}}.
The population as of 2020 was 26,046.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/novo-aripuana/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> 
The seat of the municipality is located where the [[Aripuanã River]] merges into the [[Madeira River]].

===Conservation===

The municipality contains 8% of the {{convert|2467244|ha|adj=on}} [[Apuí Mosaic]], a jointly-managed collection of conservation units.{{sfn|MOS do Apuí – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains the {{convert|83381|ha|adj=on}} [[Manicoré State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2005.{{sfn|FES de Manicoré – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains 39% of the {{convert|283117|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Madeira Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2006.{{sfn|RDS do Rio Madeira – ISA}}
It contains the {{convert|589611|ha|adj=on}} [[Juma Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2006 to support sustainable extraction of forest resources by the traditional population.{{sfn|RDS do Juma – ISA}}
It contains 67% of the [[Campos Amazônicos National Park]], a {{convert|961318|ha|adj=on}} protected area created in 2006 that protects an unusual enclave of [[cerrado]] vegetation in the Amazon rainforest.{{sfn|PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos – ISA}}
It contains the {{convert|72,296|ha|adj=on}} [[Guariba State Park]], created in 2005.{{sfn|PES do Guariba – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains 28% of the {{convert|150465|ha|adj=on}} [[Guariba Extractive Reserve]], also created in 2005.{{sfn|RESEX do Guariba – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}

The municipality  contains about 45% of the {{convert|359138|ha|adj=on}} [[Manicoré Biological Reserve]], created by decree in May 2016 in the week before the provisional removal of president [[Dilma Rousseff]].{{sfn|REBIO do Manicoré – ISA}}
It also contains 29% of the {{convert|896411|ha|adj=on}} [[Acari National Park]], which was created at the same time.{{sfn|PARNA do Acari – ISA}}
The municipality contains about 74% of the {{convert|751302|ha|adj=on}} [[Aripuanã National Forest]], a sustainable development unit also created at that time.{{sfn|FLONA do Aripuanã – ISA}}
