'''Adolfo Alsina''' is a western [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of the [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], found at coordinates {{coord|37|10|S|62|44|W}}.

It has a population of 16,245, an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">5,875&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(2,268&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span>, and the capital is [[Carhué]].

==History==
Adolfo Alsina Partido was founded on January 21, 1877.
