'''Itapuã do Oeste''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 10,641 (2020) and its area is 4,081&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 24% of the fully protected {{convert|71161|ha|adj=on}} [[Samuel Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Samuel Estação Ecológica|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5936|access-date=2016-05-14}}</ref>
