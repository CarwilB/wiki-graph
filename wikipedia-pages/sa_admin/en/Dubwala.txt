'''Tubualá''' or '''Dubwala''' is an island town in the [[Guna Yala]] [[Provinces and regions of Panama|province]] of [[Panama]]. It is less than {{convert|1|km|1}} off the mainland shore.

Tubualá is served by [[Tubualá Airport]].
