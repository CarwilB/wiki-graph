'''Zaruma Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[El Oro Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Zaruma]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 23,407.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

Zaruma was named a ''[[Pueblos Mágicos (Ecuador)|Pueblo Mágico]]'' (magical town) by the Ecuadorian Ministry of Tourism (MINTUR) in 2019. It is one of five communities across the country that inaugurated the programme that year.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Zaruma declarada Pueblo Mágico de Ecuador – Ministerio de Turismo|url=https://www.turismo.gob.ec/zaruma-declarada-pueblo-magico-de-ecuador/|access-date=2021-04-02|language=es}}</ref>
