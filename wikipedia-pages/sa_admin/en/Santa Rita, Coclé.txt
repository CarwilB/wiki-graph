'''Santa Rita''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Antón District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|43.5|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,562 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|58.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,065; its population as of 2000 was 2,368.<ref name=inec/>
