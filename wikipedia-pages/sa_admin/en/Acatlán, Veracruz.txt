'''Acatlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] and village located in the central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 35&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. The municipality has an area of 18.1 km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2020, the municipality had a population of 3,441 inhabitants, and more than 94% of the population resided in the municipal seat.<ref name=2020CENSUSACATLAN>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Acatl%C3%A1n.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Acatlán CENSUS]</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality of '''Acatlán'''  is delimited to the east by the [[Tepetlán]], to the south by the [[Naolinco]], to the west by the [[Miahuatlán (Veracruz)|Miahuatlán]], and to the north by [[Chiconquiaco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30177a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Acatlán |publisher=Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=2009-06-16 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070401104127/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30177a.htm |archive-date=2007-04-01 }}</ref> It is watered by the rivers Actopan and Pájaro Verde.
