'''Arapuá''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northwest of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 2,835 people living in a total area of {{cvt|172|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/arapua/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaíba (mesoregion)|Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaiba]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Patos de Minas]].  It became a municipality in 1962.

==Geography==
Arapuá  is located at an elevation of 637 meters southeast of [[Patos de Minas]].  It is between the [[Paranaíba River]] and the Abaeté River.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Carmo do Paranaíba]] (N), [[Tiros]] (E), [[Matutina]] and [[Rio Paranaíba]] (S), and [[Carmo do Paranaíba]] (W).

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$ R$23 million, of which 8 million came from services, 5 million came from industry, and 9 million came from agriculture.  Arapuá is in the top tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  {{as of|2007}} there were no banking agencies in the town.  There was a small retail infrastructure serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  There were 395 automobiles in all of the municipality (2007), about one for every 08 inhabitants.<ref name="ibge.gov.br">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

In the rural area there were 373 establishments occupying 13,000 hectares (planted area, 1,000 ha and natural pasture 11,000 ha.) (2006).  About 750 persons were employed in agriculture.  39 of the farms had tractors, a ratio of one in ten farms.  There were 10,000 head of cattle in 2006.  The main crops were coffee, rice and corn.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 02 health clinics and 01 hospital with 12 beds.  In the educational sector there was 01 primary school.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>
