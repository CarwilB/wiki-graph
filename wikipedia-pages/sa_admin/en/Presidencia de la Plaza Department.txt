'''Presidencia de la Plaza''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 12,000 inhabitants in an area of  2,284&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Presidencia de la Plaza]], which is located around 1,120&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

==Settlements==

*[[Fortin Aguilar]]
*[[Paso de Oso]]
*[[Presidencia de la Plaza]]

==Attractions==
The [[Parque Nacional Chaco]] (Chaco National Park) is partially situated in the Presidencia de la Plaza Department. The park consists of woodland, rivers and lakes. Native Fauna include [[Tapir]]s, [[Monkey]]s, [[Armadillo]]s and [[Viscacha]]s.
