'''La Jagua del Pilar''' ({{IPA|es|la ˈxaɣwa ðel piˈlaɾ}}), literally "The [[Genipa americana|Jagua]] of the [[Our Lady of the Pillar|Pillar]]", is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]]. The municipality has a total area of 187&nbsp;km² and located some 450 meters over sea level and with an average temperature of 27&nbsp;°C. La Jagua del Pilar became a municipality of La Guajira in 1998.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co">{{cite web | title=Government of La Guajira: La Jagua del Pilar | website=www.laguajira.gov.co | url=http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/lajagua.htm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928001806/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/lajagua.htm | archive-date=28 September 2007 | url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality of La Jagua del Pilar is located in the southern area of the [[Guajira Peninsula]] limiting to the north with the municipality of [[Urumita]]; east with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]] and to the south and west with the [[Department of Cesar]] covering a total area of 152&nbsp;km². The area of the municipality is mostly flat with some mountains near the [[Serranía del Perijá]] mountain range and crossed by the [[Perijá River|Perija]] and [[Marquezote River|Marquezote]] rivers and the [[Los Martires stream]], among others. The municipality seat is located some 450 m above sea level.<ref>[http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Geography]. ''www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co''. {{webarchive | url=https://archive.today/20120804042805/http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m | date=4 August 2012 }}</ref>

===Climate===

The average temperature in the municipality is 28&nbsp;°C throughout the year, presenting two rainy season  and two dry seasons. Climate also varies depending on mountain altitude.<ref>[http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Climate]. ==History==

The area of the present-day municipality of La Jagua del Pilar was populated by indigenous peoples pertaining to the [[Wayuu]] and [[Wiwas]] ethnic groups. During the period of Independence from Spain the area was a farm owned by [[María Concepción Loperena|María de la Concepción Loperena Ustariz]], from [[Valledupar]], one of the few women who supported the independence movement of [[Simón Bolívar]]. Loperena donated more than a hundred horses to the Patriot army. She also donated her state to build a new settlement which was named "''La Jagua del Pedregal''". The new settlement was established by Don Manuel José Fernández de Castro and Don Bartolo Ustariz on October 12, 1795. The name was later changed to La jagua del Pilar to honor Virgin Mary, [[Our Lady of the Pillar]].<ref name="lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co">[http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m History]. ''www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co''. {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120804042805/http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |date=4 August 2012 }}</ref>

On May 6, 1998, the corregimiento of La Jagua del Pilar became a municipality, segregating from the municipality of [[Urumita]] by ordinance 018 of the same year declared by the [[Department Assembly of La Guajira]] and is covered also by Law 14 of 1969 also known as the "Frontiers Law" for having boundaries with [[Venezuela]].<ref name="lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co"/><ref>[http://ssiglims.igac.gov.co/ssigl/datosLimites/44-ORD-018-1998a-Jagua%20Pilar.txt ORDENANZA NUMERO 018 DE 1998]. ==Economy==

The economy is based primarily on agriculture.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co"/> [[Cattle]] ranching is practiced mostly extensively and in [[latifundio]]s. The main agricultural products are [[cotton]], [[maize]], [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]] and [[Cassava|yuca]].<ref>[http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Economy]. ''www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co''. {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120804042805/http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |date=4 August 2012 }}</ref>

==Culture==

{{see also|List of festivals in La Guajira}}

La Jagua del Pilar venerates the "''Virgen del Carmen''" (Virgin of Carmen) a local Roman Catholic tradition on July 16 of each year. Every October 12 of each year the municipality also celebrates the [[Our Lady of the Pillar]] along the [[Vela del Marquezote festival]].<ref>[http://www.lajaguadelpilar-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Culture].
