'''Chicla District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Huarochirí Province]], located in the [[Department of Lima]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the Law No. 11981 on March 4, 1953, during the presidency of [[Manuel A. Odría]], and encompasses an area of 244.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
