'''Santo Antônio do Lisboa''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning [[Saint Anthony of Lisbon]]) is a [[List of cities in Brazil|town]] in the eastcentral part of the state of [[Piauí]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 6,441 (2020 est.) in an area of 387.40&nbsp;km².<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2BB9 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 237 m.
