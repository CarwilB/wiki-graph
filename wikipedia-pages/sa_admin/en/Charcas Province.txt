'''Charcas''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Potosí Department]]. Its capital is [[San Pedro de Buena Vista]] (955 inhabitants in 2001).<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo National Institute of Statistics of Bolivia]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110924073930/http://www.ine.gob.bo/ |date=September 24, 2011 }}</ref>

==Location==
Charcas province is one of sixteen provinces in the Potosí Department. It is located between 17° 57' und 18° 36' [[Latitude|South]] and between 65° 21' und 66° 19' [[Longitude|West]]. It borders [[Bernardino Bilbao Province]] in the north, [[Alonso de Ibáñez Province]] in the northwest, [[Rafael Bustillo Province]] in the southwest, [[Chayanta Province]] in the south, and [[Cochabamba Department]] in the east.

The province extends over 125&nbsp;km from East to West and 100&nbsp;km from north to south.

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Quechua languages|Quechua]], spoken by 98%, while 34% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]]. The population increased from 31,233 inhabitants (1992 census) to 38,174 (2001 census), an increase of 22.3%. Capital of the province is [[San Pedro de Buena Vista|San Pedro]] (955 inhabitants).

10% of the population have no access to electricity, 10% have no sanitary facilities. 72.0% of the population are employed in agriculture, 0.5% in mining, 12.2% in industry, 15.3% in general services. 99% of the population are Catholics.

The people are predominantly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent and descendants of the Spanish.<ref name="desc">obd.descentralizacion.gov.bo/municipal/fichas/ (inactive)</ref>
