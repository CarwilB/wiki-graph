'''São Francisco do Sul''' (literally "South St. Francis") is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. It covers an area of 540&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (208 miles<sup>2</sup>) and had an estimated population of 53,746 in 2020.

==Location==
It was founded as a village by the [[Portuguese Empire|Portuguese]] in 1658. {{citation needed|date=August 2020}} Despite the date of its foundation, there's a previous record that a famous navigator left [[Honfleur]], [[Normandy]] in France by the year of 1503 and arrived at São Francisco do Sul, at the Bay of Babitonga, [[Binot Paulmier de Gonneville|Jean Binot Paulmier De Gonneville]] is also recognised by many historians as one of the first, if not the first, Europeans to arrive. {{citation needed|date=August 2020}} It is situated on the northern end of the [[Island of São Francisco]] at the entrance to the [[Babitonga Bay]].

São Francisco do Sul is an important part of the Brazilian infrastructure. It provides large bulk shipping facilities and is served by the major container shipping lines connecting with the South American east and west coasts, Africa, Asia, North and Central America, the Caribbean and Europe.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.terminalsc.com.br/en/institucional/terminal.php |title=Página inicial | publisher=Terminal Portuário Santa Catarina |access-date=2012-10-29 |archive-date=2016-03-03 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303215002/http://www.terminalsc.com.br/en/institucional/terminal.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>

During the past decade, São Francisco do Sul has become a popular tourism destination for South American and international guests.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.msccruisesusa.com/us_en/Cruise-Destinations/South-America/Brazil/Sao-Francisco-Do-Sul.aspx |title=MSC Cruises (USA): Sao-Francisco-do-Sul South America Cruise Deals |website=www.msccruisesusa.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101228193409/http://www.msccruisesusa.com/us_en/Cruise-Destinations/South-America/Brazil/Sao-Francisco-do-Sul.aspx |archive-date=2010-12-28}}</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|6667|ha|adj=on}} [[Acaraí State Park]], created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Acaraí – FATMA}}|language=pt|title=Parque Estadual Acaraí|publisher=FATMA|url=http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-acarai|access-date=2016-11-29|archive-date=2019-09-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190904051729/http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-acarai|url-status=dead}}</ref>
