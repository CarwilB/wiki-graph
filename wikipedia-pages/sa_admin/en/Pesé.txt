'''Pesé  ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pesé District]], [[Herrera Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 2,565 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of [[Pesé District]].<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,362; its population as of 2000 was 2,547.<ref name=inec/>
