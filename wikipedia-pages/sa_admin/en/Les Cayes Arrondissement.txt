'''Les Cayes''', often referred to as '''Aux Cayes''' ({{langx|ht|Okay}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 346,276 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015 |date=March 2015 |website=IHSI |language=fr |trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |archive-date= 6 November 2015 |url-status=dead |access-date=8 May 2017 }}</ref>
Postal codes in the Les Cayes Arrondissement start with the number 81.

The arrondissement consists of the following municipalities:
* [[Les Cayes]]
* [[Camp-Perrin]]
* [[Chantal, Haiti|Chantal]]
* [[Île-à-Vache]] (island)
* [[Maniche, Haiti|Maniche]]
* [[Torbeck]]
