'''Boyacá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Márquez Province]], part of the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], [[Colombia]]. It is located approximately {{convert|15|km|mi}} from the city of [[Tunja]], the capital of the department. Boyacá limits [[Tunja]] and [[Soracá]] in the north, [[Jenesano]] in the south, [[Nuevo Colón, Boyacá|Nuevo Colón]] and [[Ramiriquí]] in the west and [[Ventaquemada]] in the east.<ref name=websiteBoyaca>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.boyaca-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Boyacá] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150530101655/http://www.boyaca-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml# |date=2015-05-30 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name of both the municipality and the department, Boyacá, comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Region of the blankets" or "Enclosure of the ''cacique''".<ref name=websiteBoyaca/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Boyacá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Boyacá in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' of nearby [[Tunja|Hunza]], the [[Muisca rulers|ruler]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], who were organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]].

Modern Boyacá was founded on August 8, 1537, by Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] who was searching for the [[Muisca mythology|mythical]] ''[[El Dorado]]''.<ref name=websiteBoyaca/>
