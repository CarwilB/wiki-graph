'''San Pedro de Huaca Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Carchi Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Huaca, Carchi|Huaca]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 6,856<ref>[https://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and in 2010 was 7,624. The area is {{convert|31|sqkm|sqmi}}. 

The canton is located in the [[Andes]].  The town of Huaca has an elevation of {{convert|2932|m|ft}} above sea level. 

The canton is divided into two parishes: Huaca (Waka) and Mariscal Sucre.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php|title=Ecuador: Municipal Division (Cantons and Parishes) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map|website=www.citypopulation.de|language=en|access-date=2018-10-19}}</ref>
