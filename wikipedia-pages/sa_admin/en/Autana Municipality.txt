The '''Autana Municipality''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Autana}}) is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the southern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Amazonas (Venezuelan state)|Amazonas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 8,352.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The town of [[Isla Ratón]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Autana Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Amazonas.zip {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The shiretown of the Autana Municipality, Isla de Ratón, was founded by a group led by Pedro Loroima in 1943.{{cn|date=December 2022}}

==Demographics==
The Autana Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of [[Venezuela]], has a population of 8,181 (up from 6,524 in 2000).  This amounts to 5.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.5|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Autana Municipality is Bernabe Arana, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 45% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Resultados generales del Municipio AUTANA |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=5&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Samariapo, Sipapo, Munduapo, and Guayapo (before December 18, 1997, the Autana Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
