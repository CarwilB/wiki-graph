'''Seybaplaya''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "''[[Ceiba pentandra|ceiba]]'' beach") is a [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Campeche]], located {{convert|27|km}} southwest of the state capital at [[San Francisco de Campeche]]. Its creation was approved in 2019 and came into force on 1 January 2021.

==Geography==
The municipality of Seybaplaya is located on the west coast of the [[Yucatán Peninsula]] on the [[Gulf of Mexico]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Campeche Municipality|Campeche]] to the north and [[Champotón Municipality|Champotón]] to the south. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|289.8|km2}}.<ref name=creation/>

Seybaplaya has a [[tropical savanna climate]] with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> The average annual temperature is about {{convert|26|C}}.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==History==
The conquistador [[Francisco Hernández de Córdoba (Yucatán conquistador)|Francisco Hernández de Córdoba]] reached the location of what is now Seybaplaya in 1517. During the era of Spanish colonization, the coast of Campeche was frequently raided by pirates, and the town of Seybaplaya was sacked in 1680 and 1748. A town named Seyba Cabecera was founded {{convert|15|km}} inland of Seybaplaya in 1682 and counted 1217 inhabitants in 1790. It was abandoned after being struck by epidemics of cholera in the 1850s and smallpox in 1915. Its ruins are visible from the road east of Seybaplaya between Xkeulil and Hobomó.<ref name=history>{{cite web |url=https://seybacronista.wordpress.com/2013/10/02/historia-de-seybaplaya/ |title=Historia de Seybaplaya |language=es |website=Cronista de Seybaplaya |date=2 October 2013 |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref><ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/municipios/04004a.html |title=Champotón |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Campeche |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=17 October 2020 |archive-date=25 May 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180525154054/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/municipios/04004a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

When Campeche first organized its administrative divisions in 1861, Seybaplaya was made a ''municipalidad'' in the ''partido'' of Champotón. In 1915 it became a municipal section (''sección municipal'') of the municipality of Champotón, with its own directly elected municipal board (''junta municipal'').<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/gobierno.html |title=Gobierno |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Campeche |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=17 October 2020 |archive-date=10 October 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010174010/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/gobierno.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>  On 28 February 2019 the [[Congress of Campeche|Campeche state legislature]] approved the dissolution of the municipal section and the creation of the municipality of Seybaplaya.<ref>{{cite news |last=Alonzo |first=Lourdes |date=28 February 2019 |url=https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/campeche-tendra-un-nuevo-municipio |title=Y el nuevo municipio de Campeche es... |language=es |newspaper=El Financiero |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref> The decree establishing the municipality was gazetted on 26 April 2019 and came into force on 1 January 2021.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=26 April 2019 |title=Decreto Número 44 |url=http://periodicooficial.campeche.gob.mx/sipoec/public/periodicos/201904/PO0919SS26042019.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Campeche, Segunda Sección |pages=1–2 |access-date=17 October 2020}}</ref>

==Administration==
{{update section|date=May 2025}}
Seybaplaya will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2021. Until then, its administration is being carried out by the members of the former municipal board of Seybaplaya, elected when it was still subordinate to Champotón.<ref name=creation/> The current president is Diego Pablo Palomo Ku.<ref>{{cite news |date=16 July 2018 |url=https://www.novedadescampeche.com.mx/estado/escarcega/agradece-al-pueblo-su-voto-de-confianza/ |title=Agradece al pueblo su voto de confianza |language=es |newspaper=Novedades Campeche |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref>{{Update inline|date=April 2026}}

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Seybaplaya recorded a population of 13,783 inhabitants.<ref name=ageeml/>

There are 56 localities in the municipality,<ref name=ageeml/> of which two are classified as urban:
*The municipal seat, also named Seybaplaya, which recorded a population of 8,711 inhabitants in the 2010 Census, and
*Villa Madero, which recorded a population of 3,954 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=004 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Champotón |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Seybaplaya's economy is mainly dependent on commerce and services connected with its small port,<ref name=creation/> which moved 17,000 tonnes of cargo in 2016.<ref name=survey>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/biblioteca/ficha.html?upc=702825095109 |title=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Campeche 2017 |page=337 |language=es |publisher=[[INEGI]] |date=2017}}</ref> A ''[[maquiladora]]'' operated by [[Delta Apparel]] is located in the municipality, and fishing and agriculture are also practised. The municipality has a high school, a health centre, a police station and a cemetery. [[Mexican Federal Highway 180]] runs along the coast of the municipality, and a section of the toll expressway [[Mexican Federal Highway 180D|180D]] runs parallel to it inland, connecting it to the city of [[Campeche (city)|Campeche]] in the northeast.<ref name=creation/>
