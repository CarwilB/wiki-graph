'''Rio Preto da Eva''' (''Black River of Eve'' in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located just east of [[Manaus]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 34,106 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/rio-preto-da-eva/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|5813|km2|mi2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality contains most of the [[Biological Dynamics of Forest Fragments Project Area of Relevant Ecological Interest]], created in 1985.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ARIE Proj. Dinâmica B. ... ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=ARIE Proj. Dinâmica B. de Fragmentos Florestais|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
|url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5006|accessdate=2016-10-18}}</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|27342|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Urubu State Forest]], created in 2003.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Rio Urubu – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Rio Urubu|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5750|accessdate=2016-10-20}}</ref>
