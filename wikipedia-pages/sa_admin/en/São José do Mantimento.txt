'''São José do Mantimento''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Manhuaçu. As of 2020, the estimated population was 2,806.<ref>{{Cite web |title=São José do Mantimento |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sao-jose-do-mantimento/panorama |access-date=2025-03-24 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>
