'''Jacobo Hunter District''' is one of the 29 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Arequipa Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Distrital de Jacobo Hunter, [http://www.munihunter.gob.pe Municipalidad Distrital Jacobo Hunter] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150402125351/http://www.munihunter.gob.pe/ |date=2015-04-02 }}, Retrieved November 28, 2007.</ref>

The district is named after Jacob Dixon Hunter (Jacobo Dixon Hunter) who was a Scottish doctor (born in [[Madison, Indiana]] (USA) on 8 September 1837. His father was working there as a Presbyterian pastor. His parents returned to Scotland and he grew up in [[Dollar, Clackmannanshire]] (his father's home town) and obtained an MD from [[University of Edinburgh]] in 1858. His MD thesis was on [[psoriasis]] and [[Leprosy|lepra]]. In 1859 he took a position as a ship's doctor and went to South America. He arrived in [[Callao]] on the first steam-powered ship to arrive there from Europe and decided to remain in South America. In 1861 he moved to [[Arequipa]] in Peru where he practiced medicine until his death in 1926.
