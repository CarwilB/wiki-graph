'''Segundo''' (''Barrio Segundo'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Along with [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]], [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]], [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], and [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], Segundo is one of the municipality's [[Ponce, Puerto Rico#Barrios|six core urban barrios]]. It was organized in 1878.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref> Barrio Segundo has 3 subbarrios: Baldority de Castro (or just "Baldorioty"), Clausells,{{efn|According to Eduardo Questell Rodriguez, Pedro Clausells (ca. 1840 - ca. 1910) was a land surveyor from Ponce (See Eduardo Questell Rodriguez, Historia de la Comunidad Bélgica de Ponce, a partir de la Hacienda Muñiz y Otros datos. Ponce, Puerto Rico: Editores Mariana. 2018. pages 59-60.) He may have been a member of the family that owned Finca Clausells located in the subbarrio by that name (see Office of Head Start - Grantee Profile. Current Legally Approved Service Area: Municipality of Ponce, Puerto Rico. ==Location==
Segundo is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and northwest of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

==Boundaries==
Barrio Segundo is bounded on the North by Cinco Street, Pico Dulce Street, and [[Cruceta El Vigía|Paseo de la Cruceta]], on the South by Villa Street, on the West by Global Street, and on the East by [[Paseo Atocha|Atocha]], Plaza Munoz Rivera, and Plaza Degetau Streets.<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=12 August 2010 }} Retrieved February 17, 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Segundo is bounded in the North by [[Portugués Urbano]], in the South by [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]] and [[Canas Urbano]], in the West by Canas Urbano, and in the East by [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], and [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]].

The communities of Clausells, Ferran, and Tamarindo are located in Segundo.

==Notable landmarks==
Segundo is home to a large number of Ponce's landmarks and historic sites. [[Plaza Las Delicias]], [[25 de Enero Street]], and [[Paseo Atocha]], are located there.

The NRHP-listed [[Parque de Bombas]], [[Nuestra Señora de la Guadalupe Cathedral]], [[Armstrong-Poventud Residence]], [[Casa Wiechers-Villaronga]] (Architecture Museum), [[Panteón Nacional Román Baldorioty de Castro]], [[Albergue Caritativo Tricoche]],  [[Miguel C. Godreau Residence|Casa Miguel C. Godreau]], and [[Residencia Subirá|Subira House]] are all located in Barrio Segundo.
