The '''Simón Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 421,424.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city of [[Barcelona, Anzoátegui|Barcelona]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Simón Bolívar Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Simón Bolívar Municipality (disambiguation)|Simón Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].

==Demographics==
The Simón Bolívar Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 428,391 (up from 378,285 in 2000).  This amounts to 29.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|251.11|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Simón Bolívar Municipality is Inés Sifontes, elected on November 23, 2008 with 47% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=DivulgaciÃ³n Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101202220028/http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | access-date=2024-12-27 | archive-date=2010-12-02}}</ref> She replaced José Pérez Fernández shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into six [[parish]]es; El Carmen, San Cristóbal, Bergantín, Caigua, El Pilar, and Naricual.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
