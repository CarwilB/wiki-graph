'''Colchane''' is a [[Chile]]an village and commune in [[Tamarugal Province]], [[Tarapacá Region]].

The commune is located in the [[Andes|Andean]] [[altiplano]], bordering [[Bolivia]]. It also includes the localities of [[Isluga, Chile|Isluga]], Enquelga, Cariquima and Chijo.

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Colchane had 1,728 inhabitants (995 men and 733 women),  and it is entirely [[rural area|rural]]. The total population grew by 4,79% (79 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> However, broken down by sex, only the male population increased (85 more men), while the female population decreased (6 fewer women).<ref name= "INE"/> It has the country's highest rate of people living under the poverty line at 34.56% (Encuesta Casen, 2006).{{Citation needed|date=November 2010}}

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Colchane is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Colchane is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marta Isasi ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) and Hugo Gutiérrez ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) as part of the 2nd electoral district, which includes the entire [[Tarapacá Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[José Miguel Insulza]] (PS, 2018–2026) and José Durana (UDI, 2018–2026) as part of the 1st senatorial constituency ([[Arica and Parinacota Region]] and [[Tarapacá Region]]).
