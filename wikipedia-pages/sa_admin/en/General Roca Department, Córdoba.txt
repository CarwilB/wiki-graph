'''General Roca Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 33,323 inhabitants in an area of 12,659&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Villa Huidobro]].<ref>{{Cite book |last=Cohen |first=Saul Bernard |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=C6PLxz8EMk0C&dq=General+Roca+Department&pg=PA688 |title=The Columbia Gazetteer of the World: A to G |date=2008 |publisher=Columbia University Press |isbn=978-0-231-14554-1 |language=en}}</ref>
