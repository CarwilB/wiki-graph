'''Doctor González''' is a municipality and town located in the northeastern Mexican state of [[Nuevo León]]. It has a territorial extension of 701.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipal seat has 404 [[metres]] above sea level. The municipality is located 48&nbsp;km northeastwards [[Monterrey]]. The municipality was named in honor of [[José Eleuterio González]].

==Geography==
It borders with the municipalities of [[Higueras, Nuevo León|Higueras]] and [[Cerralvo Municipality|Cerralvo]] to north, [[Pesquería, Nuevo León|Pesquería]] and [[Los Ramones, Nuevo León|Los Ramones]] to south, [[Cerralvo Municipality|Cerralvo]] to west, and [[Marín, Nuevo León|Marín]] to west.

==History==
The first settlement in what is today the municipality of Doctor González took place in October 21, 1694, founded by Spanish settlers as [[Hacienda]] de Ramos, the history of the region where the municipality is now located lies within the [[Salinas Valley]], a historic region in the former [[New Kingdom of León]] which extended throughout the [[Salinas River (Nuevo León)|Salinas River]] comprising several municipalities in northern Nuevo León, an important region during the colonization of Nuevo León.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.nl.gob.mx/?Article=17758&ArtOrder=ReadArt&P=leerarticulo&Page=1 |title=Sala de prensa - Contenido - Gobierno del Estado de Nuevo León, México |publisher=Nl.gob.mx |date= |accessdate=2013-09-08}}</ref> The word 'Salinas' designated for the region, was because of the large area characterized by salinity on their lands, useful for livestock and which settled a large number of ranches and farms during the XVII, XVIII and XIX. In 1883 Hacienda de Ramos, was created as a municipality and renamed as [[Doctor González]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mundoregio.com/nuevoleon/municipios/doctor-gonzalez-nuevo-leon.html |title=Dr. Gonzalez Nuevo Leon - Conoce Nuevo Leon con Mundoregio.com - Monterrey, Nuevo Leon, Mexico |publisher=Mundoregio.com |date= |accessdate=2013-09-08}}</ref>
