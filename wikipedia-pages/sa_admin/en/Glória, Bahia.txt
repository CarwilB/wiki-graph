'''Glória''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Glória covers {{convert|1,255.56|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 15,234 with a population density of 13 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located on the border of the states of Bahia,  [[Pernambuco]], and [[Alagoas]] on the banks of the [[Moxito River]], now a lake as the result of the construction of [[Moxito Hydroelectric Power Plant]].<ref name="i">{{cite web | url = http://cod.ibge.gov.br/YV5 | title = Glória | year = 2015 | publisher = Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística | location = Brasília, Brazil | access-date = 2015-08-19 | language = pt}}</ref>

==Languages==
