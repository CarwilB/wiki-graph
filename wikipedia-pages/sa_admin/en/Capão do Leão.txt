'''Capão do Leão''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[municipality]] in the southern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 25,409 (2020 est.) in an area of 785.37&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/63V Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city hosts the main campus of [[Universidade Federal de Pelotas]], the largest higher education institution in the southern portion of Rio Grande do Sul.
