'''Ananás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 9,492 (2020) and its area is 1,577&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1E15]</ref>

The municipality contains 52% of the {{convert|18608|ha|adj=on}} [[Lago de Santa Isabel Environmental Protection Area]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Lago de Santa Isabel – ISA}}
