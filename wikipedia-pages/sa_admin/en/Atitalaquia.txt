'''Atitalaquia''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|64.2|km²|abbr=on}}.

As of 2010 census, the municipality had a total population of 26,749.<ref>{{cite web|title=Atitalaquia |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13010a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=December 27, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070527203744/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13010a.htm |archivedate=May 27, 2007 }}</ref> Atitalaquia is now part of the [[Tula de Allende]] built-up area and while [[urbanization]] is still growing, about to be included in Mexico City Metro Area.{{what?|date=December 2025}}
