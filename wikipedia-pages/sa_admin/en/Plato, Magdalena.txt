'''Plato''' is a town and municipality in [[Magdalena Department]] in [[Colombia]].

* Area: 1,457&nbsp;km².
* Elevation: 20 meters
* Population: 66,362
** Rural: 18,625
** Urban: 47,737
* Agricultural products: livestock, corn, beans, tomatoes, yuca, tobacco

==Points of interest==
* Alligator Man Park
* Statue of Bolivar
* Statue of the Virgin of Carmen
