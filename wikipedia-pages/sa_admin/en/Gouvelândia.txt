'''Gouvelândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in south [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  Gouvelândia is a large producer of sesame and peanuts.

==Location==
Gouvelândia is located in the [[Quirinópolis Microregion]], 35 kilometers west of the most important city, [[Quirinópolis]].  The Rio dos Bois, a tributary of the [[Paranaíba river|Paranaíba]] crosses the region.  The southern boundary is with the São Simão artificial lake and the border with the state of [[Minas Gerais]].

The distance to state capital, [[Goiânia]], is 299&nbsp;km.  Highway connections from [[Goiânia]] are by GO-040 / [[Aragoiânia]] / [[Cromínia]] / GO-215 / [[Pontalina]] / GO-040 / [[Aloândia]] / [[Bom Jesus de Goiás]] / BR-452 / [[Itumbiara]] / GO-206 / BR-483 / [[Cachoeira Dourada]] / [[Inaciolândia]]. See [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin] for all the distances.

Neighboring municipalities are:
*north:  [[Castelândia]]
*south:  [[Santa Vitória, Minas Gerais|Santa Vitória]], Minas Gerais
*east: [[Inaciolândia]]
*west:  [[Quirinópolis]]

==The economy==
The main economic activities were cattle raising and agriculture.  Agricultural production was based on cattle raising (73,000 in 2006), cotton, peanuts, sesame seeds, corn, and soybeans.

==Health and education==
There were 04 schools with 1,640 students (2006).  There were no hospitals. 
*Literacy rate: 84.0%
*Infant mortality rate: 24.07 in 1,000 live births
*Ranking on the Municipal [[Human Development Index]]:  0.754
*State ranking:  114 (out of 242 municipalities in 2000)
*National ranking:  2,228 (out of 5,507 municipalities in 2000)  For the complete list see [https://web.archive.org/web/20060519192034/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhgo.htm Frigoletto]

Gouvelândia first became a district of [[Quirinópolis]] in 1963, gaining municipal status in 1983.
