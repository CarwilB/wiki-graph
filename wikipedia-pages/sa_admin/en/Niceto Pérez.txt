'''Niceto Pérez''' ({{IPA|es|niˈseto ˈpeɾes}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Guantánamo Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located {{convert|10|km|mi}} west of the provincial capital, [[Guantánamo, Cuba|Guantánamo]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Niceto Pérez had a population of 16,181.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|640|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|25|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
