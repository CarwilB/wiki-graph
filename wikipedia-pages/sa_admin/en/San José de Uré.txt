'''San José de Uré''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Córdoba Department]], northern [[Colombia]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Freixes |first=Josep |date=24 October 2024 |title=Colombian Towns Included in UNESCO’s Slave Route Project |url=https://colombiaone.com/2024/10/14/colombia-towns-unesco-slave-route/ |access-date=2026-03-22 |website=Colombia One}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Santaeulalia |first=Inés |date=27 December 2021 |title=The Colombian coca farmers who said no to drugs |url=https://english.elpais.com/usa/2021-12-27/the-colombian-coca-farmers-who-said-no-to-drugs.html |access-date=2026-03-22 |work=El País}}</ref> The town has historical roots in the colonial agriculture and mining industries.<ref name=":0" /> In 2024, it was announced as one of two Colombian towns included in the UNESCO program "The Roads of Enslaved People: Resistance, Freedom and Heritage".<ref name=":0" />
