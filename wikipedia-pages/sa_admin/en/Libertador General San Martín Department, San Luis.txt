'''Libertador General San Martín''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|Department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.infobae.com/politica/2025/05/10/elecciones-2025-las-bancas-en-juego-en-las-cuatro-provincias-que-tiene-comicios-locales-este-domingo/|title=Elecciones 2025: las bancas en juego en las cuatro provincias que tiene comicios locales este domingo|first1=Mariel|last1=Fitz Patrick|first2=Sandra|last2=Crucianelli|work=[[Infobae]]|date=9 May 2025|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

With an area of {{convert|3021|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the Department of [[Junín Department, San Luis|Junín]], to the east with [[Chacabuco Department, San Luis|Chacabuco]], to the south with [[Coronel Pringles Department, San Luis|Coronel Pringles]] and to the west with [[Ayacucho Department, San Luis|Ayacucho]].
