'''Peralta''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Azua Province|Azua]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]].<ref>{{cite web

The village resides between 5,000 and 7,000 feet in the mountains. It is about a 40 to 50 minutes trip up and down the mountain to the city of [[Azua de Compostela]]. The village is primarily focused on [[agriculture]], livestock, and fruits. Electricity is available for all inhabitants of Peralta. However, it does go out from time to time. [[Power outage|Blackout]]s can range from an hour to the whole day.{{cn|date=February 2021}}

There are two rivers in the village, and many of the villagers bathe in the river even though they have access to running water. There are a total of four major ''[[barrio]]s'': Los Jobos, El Higüero, El Mercado, Camboya.
