'''Dilermando de Aguiar''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

== Paleontology ==
In this city there are [[outcrop]]s of [[Sanga do Cabral Formation]], the (Railroad abandoned between Dilermando de Aguiar and [[São Gabriel, Rio Grande do Sul|São Gabriel]]). [[Early Triassic]] age.
