'''Navarro''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 16,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1630|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Navarro, Buenos Aires|Navarro]], which is around {{convert|100|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
