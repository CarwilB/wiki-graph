'''Huimanguillo''' is a [[Municipalities of Tabasco|municipality]] in the south-eastern [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tabasco]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tabasco/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 4, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070302103940/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tabasco/ |archivedate=March 2, 2007 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . --->

The city of Huimanguillo is the municipal seat and the largest city in Tabasco's Chontalpa region. It is located at a distance of 67 km from the city of [[Villahermosa]], state capital.
