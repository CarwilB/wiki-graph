'''Cauquenes Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Cauquenes}}) is one of four [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Maule Region]] in Chile. It spans an area of {{cvt|3027.2|km2}}, and has its capital at the city of [[Cauquenes]]. It had a population of 60,822 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
The [[Maule Region]] was established in 1970. It is divided into four provinces&ndash;Cauquenes, [[Curicó Province|Curicó]], [[Linares Province|Linares]], and [[Talca Province|Talca]], which are further divided into 30 [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_del_maule/|lang=es|title=Region de Maule|trans-title=Maule Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Cauquenes Province is one of the four provinces of the [[Maule Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It covers an area of {{cvt|3027.2|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> About 60% of the land area belongs to the [[Maule River]] basin, with the remanining land majorly forming the coastline.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/19|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Cauquenes Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Cauquenes Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

Cauquenes is located in the southern part of the [[Maule Valley]], a wine making region in Chile. The region has clayey and [[pumice]] based soils, where [[red wine]] grapes are grown.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.winetourism.com/wine-appellation/maule-valley/|title=Maule valley|work=Wine tourism|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

The province has a warm [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|17.81|C}}. The region receives approximately {{cvt|8.05|cm}} of rainfall annually on average.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/maule/cauquenes|title=Cauquenes|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Cauquenes is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into three [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'')&ndash;
[[Cauquenes]], [[Chanco, Chile|Chanco]] and [[Pelluhue]].<ref name="Admin"/> Its capital is the city of [[Cauquenes]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://postal-codes.cybo.com/chile/cauquenes/|title=Cauquenes|work=Cybo|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 60,822 inhabitants. The population consisted of 31,671 females (52.1%) and 29,151 males (47.9%). About 17.4% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 63.7% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 18.9% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 41,357 inhabitants (68%) and a rural population of 19,465 inhabitants (32%). Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 59,837 inhabitants (98.4%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 58,813 inhabitants (96.7%), while 2,007 inhabitants (3.3%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 33,137 adherents (66.1%), followed by [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 8,880 adherents (17.7%), and 7,038 inhabitants (14%) indicating no religious affiliation.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/082__arauco/|title=Arauco Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
