The municipality contained the {{convert|86|ha}} [[Carmo da Mata Biological Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|title=CADASTRO DAS ÁREAS NATURAIS PROTEGIDAS EM MINAS GERAIS|last1=Camargos|first1=Regina M. F.|first2=Mônica|last2=Torrent Lanna|url=http://www.igc.ufmg.br/geonomos/PDFs/4_2_55_67_Camargos.pdf|access-date=2016-04-25|journal=Geonomos|volume=4|issue=2|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131126044117/http://www.igc.ufmg.br/geonomos/PDFs/4_2_55_67_Camargos.pdf|archive-date=2013-11-26|url-status=dead}}</ref>
An audit in 2012 stated that some biological reserves created in 1974 on state-owned land no longer qualified as conservation units. These included Carmo da Mata, [[Colônia 31 de Março Biological Reserve|Colônia 31 de Março]] and others.<ref>{{citation|year=2012|language=pt|page=25
