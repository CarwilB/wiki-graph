'''Bagadó''' ({{IPA|es|baɣaˈðo}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Encuesta SMART Bagadó |url=https://www.unicef.org/colombia/encuesta-smart-bagado |access-date=2025-08-24 |website=UNICEF {{!}} Para cada infancia |language=es}}</ref> It is the site of the [[Tahamí people|Tahami]] Indigenous reservation.<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 July 2024 |title=The joy of football is the remedy for peace in Bagadó |url=https://colombia.unmissions.org/en/joy-football-remedy-peace-bagad%C3%B3 |access-date=2025-08-24 |website=UN Verification Mission in Colombia}}</ref>
