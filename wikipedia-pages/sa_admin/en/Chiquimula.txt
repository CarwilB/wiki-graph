'''Chiquimula''' is a city in [[Guatemala]]. It is the capital of the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula]] and the municipal seat for the surrounding [[municipality]] of the same name.
It is located some 174&nbsp;km from [[Guatemala City]] and within Guatemala known as "La perla del oriente" (the pearl of the east).

==History==
{{expand section|date=January 2021|small=no}}

In 1851, during the Battle of La Arada Guatemalan military won over El Salvador and Honduras military forces, which is why Chiquimula was named "Ciudad Procer" Hero City.

==Population==
Chiquimula is the most populous city of eastern Guatemala. The official population of the city was 37,602, according to the 2002 census. {{As of| 2018}} the population had increased to 111,505.<ref name=irwin>{{cite book|title=Central America; Describing Each of the States of Guatemala, Honduras, Salvador, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica |chapter=State of Guatemala |page=88|chapter-url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/7306/view/1/88/}}</ref>

==Sports==
[[Sacachispas]] football club play in the [[Liga Nacional de Guatemala]], they top-highest football division in Guatemala. Their home stadium is the [[Estadio Las Victorias]].

The club has also played in the ''[[Liga Nacional de Guatemala|Liga Mayor]]'', having reached their best position in the 1995–96 season, when they were runners-up.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesg/guat96.html Guatemala 1995/1996] - RSSSF</ref> They have been playing in the [[Primera División de Ascenso|second division]] since 2000.
