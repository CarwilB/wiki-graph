'''Tetela de Ocampo ''' is a [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=January 4, 2010}}</ref><!-- 

Tetela de Ocampo, means in náhuatl «abundance of stone mountains», «teteles» or 《temples where treasures are hidden or when sacrifices are made》. Nowadays, its official name is Tres Veces Heroica Tetela de Ocampo (Three Times Heroic Tetela de Ocampo),it was until the middles of the XIX Century when it was called Tetela del Oro (Tetela of the Gold) after the gold abundance in the mountains of this town. Tetela is a Mexican municipality in the State of Puebla. It is located at the heart of the Sierra Norte de Puebla, and surrounded by jungly vegetation , right in the middle of big mounts such as the “Zotolo”, “Zoyayo” (“Place where palm trees are abundant”), and “Coyoco” (“Place where coyotes are abundant”).
