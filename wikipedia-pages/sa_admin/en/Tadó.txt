'''Tadó''' ({{IPA|es|taˈðo}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Tadó has an extremely wet [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with very heavy to extremely heavy rainfall year-round.
