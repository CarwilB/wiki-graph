'''San Alberto''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], Paraguay. It occupies an area of {{cvt|1025|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 11,162 individuals.

==Geography==
San Alberto is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|1025|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1018__san_alberto/|title=San Alberto District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|70|km}} from [[Ciudad del Este]], and {{cvt|411|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of [[Asuncion]].<ref name="HN">{{cite web|title=Con múltiples actos San Alberto cumplió 21 años|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/con-multiples-actos-san-alberto-cumplio-21-anos-n579166|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=San Alberto celebrated 21 years with multiple events}}</ref><ref name="LN">{{cite web|title=San Alberto: polo y capital agrícola del norte del Alto Paraná|work=La Nación|url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2026/02/07/san-alberto-polo-y-capital-agricola-del-norte-del-alto-parana/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=San Alberto: agricultural hub and capital of northern Alto Paraná}}</ref>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|345.27|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|25.61|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|21.39|cm}} and has 173.26 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/alto-parana/san-alberto|title=San Alberto, Climate|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, Minga Porã district had a population of 11,162 inhabitants of which 5,621 were males and 5,541 were females. About 36% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (64%) lived in urban areas. About 24.5% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.4% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/> The patron saint festival is celebrated on 15 November annually.<ref name="HN"/>

==Economy==
San Alberto is known the "Agricultural Capital" of Paraguay. The district's economy is predominantly dependent on [[agriculture]], particularly large-scale [[soybean]] production. Most of the cultivable land is dedicated to soybean cultivation with [[wheat]] and [[oilseed]] cultivated on rotational basis. Farming drives all related local economic activity in the district. Agricultural expansion has made San Alberto one of the fastest-growing districts in the region, and it attracts workers from nearby municipalities and districts.<ref name="LN"/>
