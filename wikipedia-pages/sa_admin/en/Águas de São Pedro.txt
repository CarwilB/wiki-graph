'''Águas de São Pedro''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈaɡwɐz dʒi sɐ̃w ˈpedɾu|-|Aguas de Sao Pedro.ogg}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] located {{convert|184|km|mi|abbr=off}} from the state capital.{{refn|group=note|name=Distances|Queried data in the online system (Web Routes) of the Department of Highways ({{langx|pt|Departamento de Estradas de Rodagem}}) of the Logistics and Transport Secretariat of the State of São Paulo.<ref>{{cite web |title=DER Roteamento |url=http://200.144.30.104/website/webrota/viewer.htm |website=Department of Highways (DER) |publisher=São Paulo State Government |trans-title=DER Routing |language=Portuguese |access-date=17 September 2012}}</ref>}} At only {{convert|3.61|km2|sqmi|2|abbr=off}}, it is the second-smallest Brazilian municipality in terms of area,<ref name=IBGE0 /> and had an estimated population of 3,521 {{As of|2020|lc=y}}.<ref name=IBGE1 /> Águas de São Pedro means "Waters of Saint Peter". Its name is derived from the [[mineral spring]]s in its territory and their location, which before the city's founding were part of the municipality of [[São Pedro, São Paulo|São Pedro]] ([[Saint Peter]]).

The average annual temperature in the city is {{convert|22.4|C|F|1|abbr=off}},<ref name=Cepagri /> and most of the municipality's vegetation consists of reforested area. In 2016 there were 2,491 vehicles in the city.<ref name=Fleet>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Frota &#124; Veículo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/22/28120 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Fleet &#124; Vehicle |language=Portuguese |access-date=15 February 2016}}</ref> Exclusively an urban area, with no rural areas, the city had four health facilities in 2009.<ref name=Health>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Serviços de saúde &#124; Estabelecimentos de Saúde |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/32/28163 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Health services &#124; Health Establishments |language=Portuguese |access-date=9 February 2019}}</ref> Its [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) is 0.854, the second highest in the state of São Paulo, as well as the second highest in Brazil, surpassed only by [[São Caetano do Sul]].<ref name=MHDI />

Águas de São Pedro was incorporated in the 1940s. The city is known for its mineral waters of medicinal value, their natural sources popular tourist attractions. One of the springs, ''Fonte Juventude'', has the second most highly sulfurous water in the world.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=80}} It also has two large parks, Dr.{{nbsp}}Octavio Moura Andrade Park and the ''Parque das Águas'' "José Benedito Zani", and the municipal mini-garden, all important green areas of the city.

The municipality is located in the region of Itaqueri Ridge{{snds}}{{langx|pt|Serra do Itaqueri}}; ''Itaqueri'' means "lying stone" in [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupí–Guaraní]]<ref name=Itaqueri>{{cite news |date=9 August 2013 |title=Região da Serra do Itaqueri |url=http://www.folhadesaopedro.com.br/noticia/744 |newspaper=Folha de São Pedro |trans-title=Region of the Itaqueri Ridge |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160309040105/http://www.folhadesaopedro.com.br/noticia/744 |archive-date=9 March 2016 |access-date=9 February 2019}}</ref>{{snds}}in the south-central part of the state of São Paulo. It is a [[planned city]] and since its founding has been a tourist destination.

Until 1800 the region of [[São Pedro, São Paulo|São Pedro]] and its enclave was [[virgin forest]]. The first known people to set foot in Águas de São Pedro territory were, as in most municipalities in the [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] countryside, ''[[bandeirantes]]'' seeking precious stones, especially [[gold]], opening many roads and routes in the dense forest. One of these routes, called ''Caminho do Picadão'' (Way of the Forest Trail), started in [[Itu, São Paulo|Itu]], passed through [[Piracicaba]] and advanced towards the hinterlands of [[Araraquara]].<ref name=PortalAguasAngelo>{{cite web |title=O início – Ângelo Franzin |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=5 |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=The beginning – Ângelo Franzin |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070921175826/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=5 |archive-date=21 September 2007 |access-date=27 January 2016}}</ref> For years, many farms were established in the region, until in 1883, São Pedro was detached from Piracicaba and became politically independent.<ref name=PortalAguasAngelo />

The economy at that time was based on [[coffee]] production, and many Italian families settled in these regions to work under partnership contracts to replace the [[Slavery in Brazil|slave labor]]. In this way, the Italian immigrant Angelo Franzin arrived in Brazil in 1887, going to work on a farm called "Recreio", owned by João Rezende da Cruz; just a year later Franzin would run other farms, like Santa Rita, Santa Eulália and Rosário.<ref name=PortalAguasAngelo /> After many years of work, he purchased land with his brother Jácomo and decided to try coffee planting. The first properties they acquired were the farms Palmeiras and Limoeiro, followed by the lands of Floresta Escura, Gonçalves, Tuncum and Araquá, as well as houses, lots, and two machines to process coffee beans.<ref name=PortalAguasAngelo />

=== Search for oil and the discovery of springs ===

In the 1920s [[Júlio Prestes]], the governor of São Paulo, began exploration of the [[Hydrocarbon exploration|oil prospecting]] area in São Pedro. His efforts failed to find petroleum, and the equipment was abandoned, but still gushing out [[mineral water]]. Subsequently, other attempts were made to find oil at greater depths, and again no oil was found.<ref name=CityHallHistory>{{cite web|title=História |url=http://aguasdesaopedro5.tempsite.ws/portal/2009/08/historia/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=History |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119133529/http://aguasdesaopedro5.tempsite.ws/portal/2009/08/historia/ |archive-date=19 January 2012 |access-date=28 January 2016 |df=dmy }}</ref> One [[Drilling rig|oil rig]] structure still remains; it is called the ''Torre de Petróleo Engenheiro Ângelo Balloni'' (Engineer Ângelo Balloni Oil Tower).

Years later, in 1934, Franzin, owner of some of the land in the area, built a simple [[bath house]] (currently known as the "Youth Fountain") in one of the springs where he bathed, the waters of which had a characteristic smell of [[sulfur]]. A year later, a group of townspeople bought a lot measuring {{convert|100000|m2|sqft|-2}} around the original wood bath house, where they built a [[health resort]]. It was composed of twelve masonry bathtubs.<ref name=CityHallHistory /> In that same year, Octavio Moura Andrade decided to build a [[spa]] there, giving it the name of ''Caldas de São Pedro'', and, along with his brother, Antonio Joaquim de Moura Andrade, established the company ''Águas Sulfídricas e Termais de São Pedro [[S.A. (corporation)|S.A.]]'' (Saint Peter Thermal and Sulphydric Waters S.A.).

For four years, the Institute for Technological Research (IPT) of the [[University of São Paulo]] conducted a series of studies of those waters. Generally, water from great depths has a high concentration of substances that may be harmful to human beings, and its [[pH]] may not be suitable for bathing.<ref name=CityHallHistory /> In 1940 the results were published in Bulletin 26 of the IPT. The waters were deemed good for bathing, and their medicinal properties were studied by Professor João de Aguiar Pupo, Dean of the Faculty of Medicine of São Paulo.<ref name=CityHallHistory />

=== Foundation and incorporation ===
Recognizing the importance of the thermal springs in the region, the São Paulo State Government on 19 June 1940 founded<ref>{{cite web |title=DECRETO N. 11.168, DE 19 DE JUNHO DE 1940 |url=http://www.al.sp.gov.br/repositorio/legislacao/decreto/1940/decreto-11168-19.06.1940.html |website=Legislative Assembly of the State of São Paulo |trans-title=DECREE NO. 11,168, OF 19 JUNE 1940 |language=Portuguese |access-date=27 May 2015 |quote=Creates the Hydromineral and Climatic Spa of 'Águas de São Pedro.'}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=23 June 1940 |title=A Estancia Hydro-mineral e Climatica 'Aguas de São Pedro' |trans-title=The Hydromineral and Climatic Spa "Aguas de São Pedro" |url=http://memoria.bn.br/DocReader/DocReader.aspx?bib=090972_09&PagFis=2081 |language=pt-br |newspaper=Correio Paulistano |issue=25859 |page=2 |access-date=27 May 2015 |via=Biblioteca Nacional}}{{cbignore}}</ref> the {{lang|pt|Estância Hidromineral e Climática de Águas de São Pedro}} (Hydromineral and Climatic Spa of Águas de São Pedro). Despite this, the city celebrates its birthday on 25 July,<ref>{{cite web |title=LEI Nº 293-C de 15 de Agôsto de 1968 |url=https://consulta.siscam.com.br/camaraaguasdesaopedro/arquivo?id=3588 |website=Águas de São Pedro Municipal Council |publisher=SINO Informática |trans-title=LAW NO. 293-C of 15 August 1968 |language=Portuguese |format=PDF |access-date=7 February 2016 |quote=The foundation of the municipality of Águas de São Pedro will be celebrated on July 25, instead of April 2.}}</ref> the date in 1940 of the founding of the first major building in the city, the ''Grande Hotel'' (now ''Grande Hotel São Pedro''). The municipality of Águas de São Pedro was incorporated by State Law No.{{nbsp}}233, of 24 December 1948.<ref name=Incorporation /><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.al.sp.gov.br/documentacao/municipios-paulistas|title=Municípios Paulistas|access-date=2024-06-26|website=www.al.sp.gov.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref>

=== Construction of the spa town and urban planning ===

[[File:Mapa Águas de São Pedro A.png|thumb|right|alt=Map detailing the boundaries of the territory of Águas de São Pedro, showing the streets, green areas, lakes and a river|Map in 1990]]

To promote the development and exploitation of the medicinal waters in an economically viable way, Octavio Moura Andrade conceived and designed a city focused on [[hydrotherapy]] and residential purposes: a [[spa town]]. Águas de São Pedro was created as a hydromineral spa, fully planned and aiming to serve both those who needed treatment and tourists looking for fun and leisure. The [[urban planner]] Jorge de Macedo Vieira was chosen to integrate the use of space with the mineral waters, topography, soil, and climate, conducting a two-year-long detailed study of the area prior to designing the city. It was only in 1940 that the project was completed, being recorded in the Real Estate Registry Office of the District of São Pedro under number{{nbsp}}1, in accordance with the requirements of the Brazilian Federal Decree-Law No.{{nbsp}}58, of 10 December 1937.<ref name=PortalAguasPlanning>{{cite web |title=Planejamento e Construção da Estância |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=19 |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=Planning and Construction of the Spa |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070802220430/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=19 |archive-date=2 August 2007 |access-date=28 January 2016}}</ref>

As part of this project several buildings were constructed, including a large luxury hotel to welcome tourists, as well as a casino,<ref>{{cite web |title=A Construção do Grande Hotel |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=84 |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=The Construction of Grande Hotel |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070802215509/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=84 |archive-date=2 August 2007 |access-date=29 January 2016}}</ref> one of the first in the country with activity regulated by the government.<ref>{{cite web |title=A importância do Cassino |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=26 |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=The importance of the Casino |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070802215248/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=26 |archive-date=2 August 2007 |access-date=29 January 2016}}</ref> A sanitation system was constructed, with the technical firm Saturnino de Brito from [[Rio de Janeiro]] hired to study and control the sanitation system in the area around the ''Grande Hotel''. Several roads were reconstructed, including an {{convert|8|km|mi|0|adj=on}} road connecting São Pedro to the springs that can handle the traffic of heavy vehicles; construction of an [[airport]] (now [[São Pedro Airport (Brazil)|São Pedro Airport]]) in a 40-''[[alqueire]]'' area (239.2{{nbsp}}[[acre]]s, or 96.8{{nbsp}}[[hectare|ha]]) with four runways, a departure lounge, electricity, telephone, running water, hangar and refueling station. Energy infrastructure was improved. Because São Pedro's power grid was in disrepair, a private power line was built connecting São Pedro to the construction works of the spa, and in the ''Grande Hotel'' was installed an emergency [[Power station|powerhouse]] with two [[Diesel fuel|diesel]] generators capable of supplying the hotel and the town.<ref name=PortalAguasPlanning />

The city became one of the members of the ''Região Turística da Serra do Itaqueri'' (Touristic Region of the Itaqueri Ridge),<ref name=ActionsTourism>{{cite web |title=Ações da Secretaria Municipal de Turismo |url=http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/2010/09/acoes-da-secretaria-municipal-de-turismo/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Actions of the Tourism Municipal Secretariat |language=Portuguese |access-date=5 February 2011 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20121129081218/http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/2010/09/acoes-da-secretaria-municipal-de-turismo/ |archive-date=29 November 2012 |df=dmy-all }}</ref> composed of twelve municipalities.<ref name=Itaqueri /> With the large influx of tourists, the need developed for improvements in the commercial sector, prompting renovation of the ''Rua do Comércio'' (Trade Street, old nickname for the street João Batista Azevedo).<ref name=ActionsTourism />

== Geography ==

{{wide image|Aguas de Sao Pedro Landscape 00.jpg|1200px|View of Águas de São Pedro city center|alt=A panorama of several buildings in the city center of Águas de São Pedro}}

The municipality of Águas de São Pedro covers {{convert|3.612|km2|sqmi|1}}, the second-smallest Brazilian city in area, only larger than [[Santa Cruz de Minas]].<ref name=TheCity>{{cite web |title=A Cidade |url=http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/sobre |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=The City |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130724052000/http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/sobre |archive-date=24 July 2013 |access-date=5 February 2016}}</ref> It represents 0.0015% of the state territory and 0.0004% of the area of [[Southeast Region, Brazil|southeastern Brazil]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Dados Gerais – Águas de São Pedro – SP |url=http://portal.cnm.org.br/v4/v11/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100135007 |publisher=National Confederation of Municipalities |trans-title=General Data – Águas de São Pedro – SP |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160309024018/http://portal.cnm.org.br/v4/v11/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100135007 |archive-date=9 March 2016 |access-date=6 August 2019}}</ref> The municipality does not have a [[rural area]], only an urban perimeter.<ref name=TheCity />

Águas de São Pedro is divided into four [[neighborhood]]s: ''Jardim Jerubiaçaba'' (Garden ''Jerubiaçaba''; ''Jerubiaçaba'' means "loyalty" in [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupí–Guaraní]]) in the northeast, ''Jardim Iporanga'' (Garden ''Iporanga''; ''Iporanga'' means "beautiful river" in Tupí–Guaraní) in the east, ''Centro'' (Center), and ''Jardim Porangaba'' (Garden ''Porangaba''; ''Porangaba'' means "nice view" in Tupí–Guaraní) in the south.<ref name=PortalAguasThings>{{cite web |title=Coisas para Fazer |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/coisas_fazer.asp |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=Things to Do |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101203110740/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/coisas_fazer.asp |archive-date=3 December 2010 |access-date=3 February 2016}}</ref>

The city is located at [[latitude]] 22°35'50.422" south and [[longitude]] 47°53'02.309" west,<ref name=Locations /> and at a distance of {{convert|184|km|mi|0}} northwest of the [[São Paulo]] state capital.{{refn|group=note|name=Distances}} Its only adjacent municipality is São Pedro,<ref name=TheCity /> from which is one of the four [[enclave]]s of Brazil, besides [[Arroio do Padre]], [[Ladário]] and [[Portelândia]].

=== Geomorphology and hydrology ===

[[File:Ribeirão Araquá Águas de São Pedro 01.JPG|thumb|left|alt=River surrounded by floodplain vegetation and silted banks|Araquá River]]

The [[geomorphology]] of the area of Águas de São Pedro is characterized by [[Terrain|reliefs]] of low, soft [[hill]]s{{snd}}separated by valleys without major river plains{{snd}}and it is slightly rugged, with sites that only rarely exceed {{convert|200|m|ft|0}} of unevenness. The municipality is at an altitude of {{convert|515|m|ft|0}} above sea level,<ref name=Locations /> and located in a geographic region called the São Paulo State Peripheral Depression, near the border with the Western Plateau, in an area of basaltic [[cuesta]]s.<ref name=PortalAguasCharacteristics>{{cite web |title=Características Gerais |url=http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=20 |website=Portal de Águas |trans-title=General Characteristics |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060509020550/http://www.portaldeaguas.com.br/portal/det_servico.asp?cod_sub_menu=20 |archive-date=9 May 2006 |access-date=6 February 2016}}</ref>

Águas de São Pedro is located in the so-called Piramboia Formation (''Piramboia'' means "[[South American lungfish|snake fish]]" in Tupí–Guaraní), one of the five stratigraphic subdivisions of the [[Paraná Basin]], all formed at different periods, in which [[Triassic]] and [[Cretaceous|Early Cretaceous]] ages [[sediment]]s predominate. The sediments are composed of thin to medium [[arenite]]s, white-orange to reddish in color, and with tangential [[cross-bedding]] in a mid-size to large base; these features are indicative of temporary rivers in the past in a [[Semi-arid climate|semiarid]] environment.<ref name=PortalAguasCharacteristics />

The city is situated in the central part of the Medium Tietê Zone, which occupies about {{convert|15000|km2|sqmi|0}}, or two-fifths of the total area of the Peripheral Depression. It is bounded by Araquá River (''Araquá'' means "hole of the world" in Tupí–Guaraní<ref>{{cite news |last=Jorge |first=J. David |date=6 March 1938 |title=Significação dos nomes de origem Tupy (ou Nheêngatú) applicados ás ruas de São Paulo |trans-title=Significance of names of Tupi origin (or Nheêngatú) applied to the streets of São Paulo |url=http://memoria.bn.br/DocReader/DocReader.aspx?bib=090972_08&PagFis=23031 |language=pt-br |newspaper=Correio Paulistano |issue=25149 |page=17 |access-date=18 May 2015 |via=Biblioteca Nacional |quote=It seems that the original name of Araraquara was Araquá or Aracoára, meaning: hole or pit of the world. From ára – aurora, light, sun, world, day; and quara, coára or quá – hole, orifice, pit.}}</ref>), which has a general north–south route, and also by the Limoeiro and Das Palmeiras lakes.<ref name=TheCity /> The region where Águas de São Pedro is located is also part of the [[Drainage basin|watershed]] of the [[Piracicaba River (São Paulo)|Piracicaba River]].<ref name=PortalAguasCharacteristics /> The Piracicaba basin extends over an area of {{convert|12568.72|km2|sqmi|0}},<ref>{{cite web |title=Caracterização dos Recursos Hídricos |url=http://www.comitespcj.org.br/images/Download/PB/PB0407_Cap08.pdf |website=Consortium of the Watershed of the Rivers Piracicaba, Capivari and Jundiaí |page=195 |trans-title=Characterization of Water Resources |language=Portuguese |access-date=12 February 2019}}</ref> covering the southeastern state of São Paulo and the southern end of [[Minas Gerais]].

According to the [[Köppen climate classification]], Águas de São Pedro has a [[tropical savanna climate]] (Aw),<ref name=Cepagri /><ref>{{cite web |title=Classificação Climática de Koeppen do Estado de São Paulo |url=http://www.cpa.unicamp.br/imagens/classkoeppensp2.gif |website=Center for Meteorological and Climatic Researches Applied to Agriculture – Unicamp |publisher=State University of Campinas |trans-title=Koeppen Climate Classification for the State of São Paulo |format=GIF |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170813060514/https://www.cpa.unicamp.br/imagens/classkoeppensp2.gif |archive-date=13 August 2017 |access-date=12 February 2019}}</ref> close to the [[humid subtropical climate]] (Cfa),<ref>{{cite web |title=World Map of Köppen–Geiger Climate Classification |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |website=World Maps of Köppen-Geiger climate classification |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health |format=GIF |access-date=4 February 2016}}</ref> with a decrease in [[rainfall]] during [[winter]], an annual average temperature of {{convert|22.4|C|F|0}}, dry and mild winters (with occurrence of light [[frost]]s on a few days of the season), and rainy [[summer]]s with high temperatures. The hottest month, February, has an average temperature of {{convert|25.2|C|F|0}}, with an average maximum of {{convert|30.9|C|F|0}} and a minimum of {{convert|19.5|C|F|0}}. The coldest month, July, has an average temperature of {{convert|18.7|C|F|0}}, with {{convert|25.9|C|F|0}} and {{convert|11.4|C|F|0}} being the maximum and minimum averages, respectively.<ref name=Cepagri /> Fall and spring are transitional seasons.

The total annual rainfall is measured at {{convert|1307.5|mm|in|0}}, and July is the driest month, with {{convert|26.7|mm|in|0|abbr=on}} of precipitation. In January, the wettest month, the average is {{convert|221.5|mm|in|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name=Cepagri /> Since the early 2000s, however, hot and dry days during winter have been increasingly frequent not only in Águas de São Pedro, but also in much of the state of São Paulo, often surpassing {{convert|30|C|F|0}}, especially between the months of July and September. During the dry season and in the long ''[[Indian summer|veranicos]]'' in the middle of the rainy season, there have been increasing reports of smoke from burned-over land in sugarcane plantations and scrubland, mainly in the rural area around the city, leading the [[Federal courts of Brazil|federal court]] to prohibit such activity in the region.<ref>{{cite news |date=13 August 2013 |title=Queima da cana é proibida na região de Piracicaba pela Justiça Federal |url=http://g1.globo.com/sp/piracicaba-regiao/noticia/2013/08/queima-da-cana-e-proibida-na-regiao-de-piracicaba-pela-justica-federal.html |work=G1 |publisher=Globo Comunicação e Participações S.A. |trans-title=Sugarcane burning is prohibited in the region of Piracicaba by the Federal Court |language=Portuguese |access-date=5 February 2016}}</ref> [[Hailstorm]]s are not common in the city, but one of the most recent occurred on 17 February 2010.<ref>{{cite news |date=18 February 2010 |title=Vento forte e chuva com granizo provocam pânico em Águas de São Pedro |url=http://www.folhadesaopedro.com.br/pg_noticias_det.php?id_noticia=7008,2 |newspaper=Folha de São Pedro |trans-title=Strong wind and rain with hail cause panic in Águas de São Pedro |language=Portuguese |access-date=3 February 2016}}{{dead link|date=February 2016}}</ref>

=== Ecology and environment ===

The original vegetation in the area of Águas de São Pedro was the [[cerrado]], a mixed formation classified into two strata: the upper stratum, composed of trees with variable height of from {{convert|3|to|6|m|ft|0}}, with [[Crown (botany)|crowns]] often sparse and spaced apart, and the lower stratum, composed of continuous coverage of grasses and other plants less than a meter tall, and trees with trunks and twisted and gnarled branches, thick [[Bark (botany)|bark]], large leaves, and thorns. This natural vegetation, however, is quite devastated.<ref name=PortalAguasCharacteristics /> Today the vegetation is predominantly planted. The city has {{convert|69.58|ha|acre|0}}, 17.40% of its total area, of [[reforestation|reforested]] areas, mostly concentrated in Dr.{{nbsp}}Octavio Moura Andrade Park in the northwest, and {{convert|2.06|ha|acre|0}}, or 0.69% of its total area, of [[floodplain]] vegetation, all concentrated on the banks of the Araquá River in the southeast portion of the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |title=Mapa Florestal dos Municípios do Estado de São Paulo / Águas de São Pedro |url=http://s.ambiente.sp.gov.br/sifesp/aguasdesaopedro.pdf |website=Forestal Institute of São Paulo State |publisher=São Paulo State Government |trans-title=Forestal Map of São Paulo State Municipalities / Águas de São Pedro |language=Portuguese |access-date=6 August 2019}}</ref>

Half of the housing units of the city (50.6%) are owned by vacationers.<ref name=Vacationers>{{cite news |title=Metade das casas da cidade é de veranistas |newspaper=A Tribuna de São Pedro |date=28 January 2012 |trans-title=Half of the houses of the city are from vacationers |language=Portuguese |page=A12 }}</ref> Many people stay in the municipality only on weekends and holidays and have their homes just for recreation and as a real estate investment.<ref name=Vacationers />

In the year 2000, 305 people (16.2% of the city's population) stated having some type of [[disability]], almost two percent higher than the national average.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tabela 2111 – População residente por tipo de deficiência, situação do domicílio, sexo e grupos de idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/2111#/n1/all/n6/all/v/all/p/all/c134/2791,95295,95747/c1/0/c2/0/c58/0/d/v93%200,v1000093%202/l/v,p+c134+c1,t+c2+c58/resultado |publisher=IBGE System of Automatic Recovery (SIDRA) |trans-title=Table 2111 – Resident population by type of disability, state of domicile, sex and age groups |language=Portuguese |access-date=12 February 2019}}</ref>

The Municipal Human Development Index (MHDI) of Águas de São Pedro, is at 0.854, considered very high by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP), and is the second highest in the state of São Paulo, as well as the second highest in Brazil. Considering only education, the index is at 0.825, compared to 0.637 for Brazil as a whole; the longevity index is 0.890 (0.816 for Brazil); and the income index is 0.849 (0.739 for Brazil).<ref name=MHDI /> The city ranks high in the majority of indicators, and above the national average in all indicators, according to the UNDP.

The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], is 0.40,<ref name=IBGE3>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Mapa de Pobreza e desigualdade &#124; Incidência da pobreza |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/36/30246 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Map of Poverty and Inequality &#124; Incidence of poverty |access-date=8 March 2019}}</ref> on a scale in which 1.00 is the worst and 0.00 is the best. The history of the Catholic Church in Águas de São Pedro started when Mrs. Maria Julia das Dores Andrade, mother of the city founder, asked him to build the "House of God" along with the construction of the ''Grande Hotel''.<ref name=Catholic>{{cite web |title=Histórico da Paróquia de Águas de São Pedro |url=http://www.regiaopastoralsaopedro.org.br/aguas/index.jsp |website=São Pedro Pastoral Region of the Diocese of Piracicaba |trans-title=Historic of the Águas de São Pedro Parish |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101219225012/http://www.regiaopastoralsaopedro.org.br/aguas/index.jsp |archive-date=19 December 2010 |access-date=23 August 2009}}</ref> The highest place of the city was selected to erect it.

In 1946 a chapel was completed, its architecture based on a chapel seen by Dr.{{nbsp}}Octavio in the city of Rio de Janeiro.<ref name=Catholic /> The chapel was dedicated to Our Lady of the [[Immaculate Conception]]. In 1954 Águas de São Pedro was removed from the City of São Pedro [[Parish (Catholic Church)|parish]], and on 29 May of that same year, the present-day Immaculate Conception Parish was founded by [[Don (honorific)|Don]] Ernesto de Paula.<ref name=Catholic /> The parish is subordinate to the [[Diocese of Piracicaba]].

Years after the foundation of the parish, a new site near the central area of the city was selected for the construction of a new church, but those plans were not completed because [[Canon (priest)|Canon]] Marcos Van Inn, the creator of the project, died. Then the parochial hall of the city was nominated by the Bishop of Piracicaba, Don Aníger Maria Melillo, to be adapted into the city's Mother Church, as it remains today. The title of Immaculate Conception was transferred from the old chapel to the Mother Church, and the chapel is now dedicated to [[Our Lady of Aparecida|Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception of the Apparition]].<ref name=Catholic />

==== Protestant Church ====
The most diverse evangelical beliefs are present in the city, mainly [[Pentecostalism|Pentecostal]], including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assemblies of God in Brazil]] (the largest evangelical church in the country),<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://confradesp.com.br/campos-eclesiasticos/|title=Campos Eclesiásticos|date=2018-12-10|access-date=2024-06-22|website=CONFRADESP|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ad.org.br/locations/|title=Arquivos: Locais|access-date=2024-06-22|website=Assembleia de Deus Belém – Sede|language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Christian Congregation in Brazil]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br/localidade|title=Localidade - Congregação Cristã no Brasil|access-date=2024-06-22|website=congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> among others. These denominations are growing more and more throughout Brazil.

== Economy ==

[[File:Feira de Artesanato ASP.JPG|thumb|right|alt=Six small chalets in a pedestrian street|Small craft shops in the city]]

According to data for 2016 from the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE), the municipality had a [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) of [[Brazilian real|R$]]132,616,560.00, of which R$7,533,360.00 were product taxes net of subsidies, and the GDP per capita was R$41,378.02.<ref name=IBGE4>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios &#124; PIB a preços correntes |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/38/46996 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities &#124; GDP at Current Prices |access-date=1 April 2019}}</ref> Also according to the IBGE, in 2016 the city had 225 local units (shops) and a total of 1,569 employed persons, with 1,271 of them salaried. [[Salary|Salaries]] along with other compensation totaled R$32,625,000.00 and the average monthly wage of the municipality was 2.2 [[minimum wage]]s.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Cadastro Central de Empresas &#124; Unidades locais |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/19/29761 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Central Register of Enterprises &#124; Local Units |access-date=1 April 2019}}</ref>

The city does not have a rural area, so the [[primary sector]] does not factor in the local GDP. Although the municipality does not depend on any major industry,<ref name=TheCity /> the gross value added to the GDP from the [[secondary sector]] was R$10,504,940.00.

R$94,367,210.00 of the municipal GDP is from services, currently the major source of GDP of Águas de São Pedro.<ref name=IBGE4 /> A major part of the [[tertiary sector]] is tourism, and the city's economy is geared exclusively toward this sector. The municipality of Águas de São Pedro is integrated with the Tourism Region of the Itaqueri Ridge and has as its main attraction its medicinal mineral waters. According to city officials, the new tourism routes go through regionalization.<ref name=ActionsTourism /> Tourists attracted by the natural attractions also help to stimulate the commercial sector of the city. The busiest retail centres are Carlos Mauro Avenue and Trade Street, which after renovations costing R$302,000.00<ref>{{cite web |title=Construção do calçadão deve começar no ano que vem |url=http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/2010/12/calcadaoem2011/ |publisher=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Construction of the pedestrian street should begin next year |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130620215333/http://www.aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/portal/2010/12/calcadaoem2011 |archive-date=20 June 2013 |access-date=15 February 2016}}</ref> has become a [[pedestrian street]].

According to the Brazilian Secretary of Foreign Trade (SECEX), in 2016 the exports of Águas de São Pedro totaled only US$838, all originated from Australia. Among the products exported are [[Bijou (jewellery)|imitation jewelry]], [[Basket weaving|woven baskets]] and plastic [[lid]]s.{{refn|group=note|Queried data in the online database of DataViva.<ref>{{cite web |title=DataViva |url=http://dataviva.info/en/build_graph/secex/4sp100100/all/all?view=Imports/Exports&graph=tree_map |website=DataViva |publisher=Secretary of Foreign Trade (SECEX) |access-date=7 March 2019 }}</ref>}}

The holidays also tend to bring more income to the city. In the 2016 [[Brazilian Carnival|Carnival]], for example, the Municipal Secretary of Tourism said that the influx of tourists would result in the addition of R$5{{nbsp}}million into the local economy.<ref>{{cite news |date=6 February 2016 |title=Carnaval lota hotéis e aumenta oferta de emprego na região de Piracicaba |url=http://g1.globo.com/sp/piracicaba-regiao/noticia/2016/02/carnaval-lota-hoteis-e-aumenta-oferta-de-emprego-na-regiao-de-piracicaba.html |work=G1 |publisher=Globo Comunicação e Participações S.A. |trans-title=Carnival packs hotels and increases labor supply in the region of Piracicaba |language=Portuguese |access-date=15 February 2016}}</ref> Several economic programs were created to highlight the Tourism Region, attract more tourists, and improve the commercial and lodging sectors.<ref name=ActionsTourism />

=== Tourism ===

On weekends the city receives an influx of tourists that numbers up to twice the number of year-round residents{{snd}}about 6,000 tourists. On the long holidays, the number of tourists can reach 30,000.<ref>{{cite web |title=A 3 horas de SP, Águas de São Pedro tem 2ª melhor água sulfurosa do mundo |last=De Souza |first=Rose Mary |url=http://vidaeestilo.terra.com.br/turismo/brasil/sudeste/a-3-horas-de-sp-aguas-de-sao-pedro-tem-2-melhor-agua-sulfurosa-do-mundo,fca85b4418381410VgnVCM20000099cceb0aRCRD.html |date=4 October 2013 |website=Terra |trans-title=Three hours distant from São Paulo, Águas de São Pedro has the 2nd best sulphurous water in the world |language=Portuguese |access-date=15 February 2016 |archive-date=9 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160309031107/http://vidaeestilo.terra.com.br/turismo/brasil/sudeste/a-3-horas-de-sp-aguas-de-sao-pedro-tem-2-melhor-agua-sulfurosa-do-mundo,fca85b4418381410VgnVCM20000099cceb0aRCRD.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Águas de São Pedro is one of eleven municipalities considered hydromineral spas by the government of the state of São Paulo, by fulfilling certain prerequisites set by state Law. Federal Law no.{{nbsp}}2,661 from 3 December 1955 states: "It is considered thermomineral, hydromineral, or simply [[mineral spa]] the location recognized by state law, and which has springs of thermal or mineral, natural waters exploited in compliance with the provisions of this law and the Federal Decree-Law no.{{nbsp}}7,841, from 8{{nbsp}}August 1945."<ref>{{cite web |title=LEI Nº 2.661, DE 3 DE DEZEMBRO DE 1955 |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/1950-1969/L2661.htm |website=Presidency of the Republic |trans-title=LAW NO. 2,661, OF 3 DECEMBER 1955 |language=Portuguese |access-date=2 April 2019}}</ref> This recognition ensures these municipalities a larger budget from the state to invest in regional tourism. Also, the city has the right to add before its name the title of hydromineral spa, the term by which the city is to be designated by both the municipal administration as well as the state references.<ref>{{cite web |title=Estância hidromineral |url=http://aguasdesaopedro5.tempsite.ws/portal/2009/08/estancia-hidromineral/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Hydromineral spa |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121124001647/http://aguasdesaopedro5.tempsite.ws/portal/2009/08/estancia-hidromineral/ |archive-date=24 November 2012 |access-date=6 February 2016}}</ref>

=== Mineral springs ===

[[File:Fontanário Águas de São Pedro 00.JPG|thumb|right|alt=Metal drinking fountain with three taps and information signs over each of them|Drinking fountain with the three types of mineral water of the city]]

Águas de São Pedro is popular for its mineral waters, as its name indicates. There are three fountains in the municipality: ''Fonte Juventude'' (Youth Fountain), ''Fonte Gioconda'' (''Gioconda'' Fountain), and ''Fonte Almeida Salles'' (Almeida Salles Fountain), all with a much higher mineralization than most other Brazilian mineral springs.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=41}} Of these three, only the last two have a natural outcrop, as the ''Juventude'' fountain water comes from the [[artesian well]] at an old oil exploration site.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=41}} The water in the ''Fontanário Municipal'' (Municipal Fountain) is potable, and for baths, there is the ''Spa Thermal Dr. Octavio Moura Andrade'', the municipal bath house.<ref>{{cite web |title=SPA Thermal |url=http://aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/guia-turistico/spa-thermal/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Thermal SPA |language=Portuguese |access-date=14 February 2016}}</ref>

The water of the ''Fonte Juventude'' is collected at a depth of {{convert|348.59|m|ft|-1}}; it is the most highly sulfurous water in Brazil and the [[Americas]] and is the second in the world in sulfurous components (only behind the Pergoli fountain in [[Tabiano Terme|Tabiano]], [[Italy]]),{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=80}} with 34.3 [[milligram]]s of sulfur oxygenated compounds,{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=44}} 4{{nbsp}}mg of [[hydrogen sulfide]], and 53{{nbsp}}mg of [[sodium hydrosulfide]] and [[sodium sulfide]] per liter of water.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=80}} It is indicated for immersion baths and treatment of [[skin disease]]s.

''Fonte Gioconda'' has a high [[sodium sulfate]] content (42&nbsp;mg/100 mL), and is therefore indicated in the treatment of [[stomach disease]]s.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=47}} The fountain was named in honor of the Giocondo family, who ceded the land around the spring to Octavio Moura Andrade.

The ''Fonte Almeida Salles'' is named in honor of the homonymous doctor of the Secretariat of Agriculture of the State of São Paulo who first visited it and tested the medicinal qualities of the water. It is a [[sodium bicarbonate]] water indicated for stomach and liver diseases.{{sfn|Camargo|1990|p=47}}

Idealized by José Palma in 2002,<ref>{{cite web |title=Caminho do Sol – História |url=http://www.caminhodosol.org/paginas.php?i=66 |website=Caminho do Sol |trans-title=Way of the Sun – History |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150717091105/http://www.caminhodosol.org/paginas.php?i=66 |archive-date=17 July 2015 |access-date=30 January 2016 |via=Internet Archive}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Caminho do Sol um pedaço do Céu em Águas de São Pedro |url=http://www.folhadesaopedro.com.br/pg_noticias_det.php?id_noticia=5116,5 |date=26 July 2008 |newspaper=Folha de São Pedro |trans-title=Way of the Sun: a piece of the sky in Águas de São Pedro |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120303104401/http://www.folhadesaopedro.com.br/pg_noticias_det.php?id_noticia=5116,5 |archive-date=3 March 2012 |access-date=10 February 2010 |via=Internet Archive}}</ref> the ''Caminho do Sol'' (Way of the Sun) is a preparatory way to the [[Way of St. James]], and it is recognized by the [[Xunta de Galicia]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Resolución do 25 de febreiro de 2008, da Dirección Xeral de Patrimonio Cultural, pola que se inscribe a Asociación Instituto Caminho do Sol no Rexistro de Entidades de Promoción do Camiño de Santiago. |url=https://www.xunta.gal/dog/Publicados/2008/20080327/Anuncio2F8EA_gl.html |website=Xunta de Galicia |trans-title=Resolution of 25 February 2008, from the Directorate for Cultural Heritage, which registers the Association Institute Way of the Sun in the Registry of Promotion Entities of the Way of St. James. |language=Galician |access-date=10 April 2013}}</ref> The route ends at the city of Águas de São Pedro, in the ''Casa de Santiago'' (House of St.{{nbsp}}James) located in the ''Mini Horto''. Several pilgrims, with bicycles or hiking, come to the city every year along the way as a religious pilgrimage or tourism. The way is {{convert|241|km|mi|-1}} long and passes through 12 municipalities in the following order: [[Santana de Parnaíba]], [[Pirapora do Bom Jesus]], [[Cabreúva]], Itu, [[Salto, São Paulo|Salto]], [[Elias Fausto]], [[Capivari]], [[Mombuca]], [[Saltinho, São Paulo|Saltinho]], Piracicaba, São Pedro, and Águas de São Pedro.

=== Arts and crafts ===

In the field of [[performing arts]], between 2006 and 2010, an ongoing Theatre Exhibition of São Pedro and Águas de São Pedro was held. In 2010 the project won the "Competition to Support Festivals of Art in the State São Paulo" that was promoted by the Secretariat of State for Culture, part of the "Cultural Action Program" organized by the ''Associação Cultural Arte'' (Art Cultural Association), which ensured that the event had full and unrestricted support from the governments of the two cities.<ref>{{cite web |title=Mostra de Teatro agitou São Pedro |url=http://www.agitosaopedro.com.br/materias/mostra-de-teatro-agitou-sao-pedro/23 |website=Agito São Pedro |publisher=Agito Brasil |trans-title=Theatre Exhibition excited São Pedro |language=Portuguese |access-date=7 February 2016}}</ref>

[[Craft]]s are also one of the most spontaneous forms of cultural expression of Águas de São Pedro. In the city it is possible to find a variety of crafts, made with regional raw materials and created in accordance with the local [[culture]] and way of life. The "ART'S TRAMA{{snd}}Artisans Association of São Pedro and Region", along with other institutions such as the municipal government or the Superintendence of Craft Work in Communities (SUTACO),<ref>{{cite web |title=Municípios Cooperados – Espaço do Artesão |url=http://www.sutaco.com.br/mc_espaco_a.html |website=Superintendence of Craft Work in Communities |trans-title=Cooperated Municipalities – Artisan Space |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110831133404/http://www.sutaco.com.br/mc_espaco_a.html |archive-date=31 August 2011 |access-date=11 February 2016 |via=Internet Archive}}</ref> bring together many craftsmen from the region and provide space for production, exhibition and sale of crafts such as quilts and crochet table runners, flowers produced with dry corn leaves, and woven pieces produced on looms. Usually, this material is sold at bazaars (like the ones promoted by the municipal Social Solidarity Fund<ref>{{cite news |title=Fundo Social de Solidariedade realiza bazar de artesanato em Águas de São Pedro |date=12 December 2015 |newspaper=Jornal O Regional |location=São Pedro, São Paulo |trans-title=Social Solidarity Fund will hold craft bazaar in Águas de São Pedro |language=Portuguese |issue=513 |page=10 |quote=…the Hidromineral Spa of Águas de São Pedro will hold from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, at the Convention Center, another Bazaar of the Social Solidarity Fund.}}</ref>), fairs, exhibitions or craft stores.

=== Sports ===

In 1941 the ''Grande Hotel'' hosted the first major chess competition in Brazil, the ''[[Águas de São Pedro–São Paulo 1941 chess tournament|Torneio Internacional de Águas de São Pedro]]'' (Águas de São Pedro International Tournament).<ref>{{cite web |title=Clube de Xadrez São Paulo – breve histórico |url=http://www.cxsp.com.br/?page_id=378 |website=São Paulo Chess Club |trans-title=São Paulo Chess Club – brief history |language=Portuguese |access-date=2 April 2019}}</ref>

More recently, Águas de São Pedro has sent delegations to participate in the ''Jogos Regionais do Idoso'' (Senior Regional Games, known by the [[acronym]] JORI) and the ''Jogos Abertos do Idoso'' (Senior Open Games, known by the acronym JAI). In 2018 56 athletes were sent to participate in the 22nd JORI,<ref>{{cite web |date=16 January 2018 |title=Terceira Idade de Águas participa dos 22º Jori em Lençóis Paulista |url=http://aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/2018/01/16/terceira-idade-de-aguas-participa-dos-22o-jori-em-lencois-paulista/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Senior Citizens of Águas participate in the 22nd JORI in Lençóis Paulista |language=Portuguese |access-date=1 April 2019}}</ref> a competition with participation of athletes from 40 municipalities, and Águas de São Pedro attained 11th place.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 April 2018 |title=Equipe de Águas é tetracampeã no vôlei e cidade leva bronze na natação nos Jori |url=http://aguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/2018/04/05/equipe-de-aguas-e-tetracampea-no-volei-e-cidade-leva-bronze-na-natacao-nos-jori/ |website=Águas de São Pedro City Hall |trans-title=Águas team is four-time champion in volleyball and city takes bronze in swimming in the JORI |language=Portuguese |access-date=1 April 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=21 January 2018 |title=BOLETIM OFICIAL 5 |url=https://apl2.lencoispaulista.sp.gov.br/jori/Content/Boletins/46/46.pdf |website=Lençóis Paulista City Hall |trans-title=OFFICIAL REPORT 5 |language=Portuguese |access-date=1 April 2019 |archive-date=2 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201002100845/https://apl2.lencoispaulista.sp.gov.br/jori/Content/Boletins/46/46.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Government ==
{{See also|List of mayors of Águas de São Pedro}}

[[File:Prefeitura de ASP.JPG|thumb|left|alt=Several twinned buildings in a square with an ATM and among them the city hall, the municipal council hall, a bakery, and a shop|City Hall (white and blue building at left) and the Municipal Council (yellow building in the center)]]

The municipal administration is formed by the mayor and nine ''vereadores'' (councillors), who are elected for a term of four years. The installation of the Municipal Council and the inauguration of the first Sanitary Mayor, Carlos Mauro, appointed by the State Governor, took place on 2 April 1949.<ref>{{cite web |title=A Câmara |url=http://www.camaraaguasdesaopedro.sp.gov.br/Pagina/Listar/2 |website=Águas de São Pedro Municipal Council |trans-title=The Council |language=Portuguese |access-date=24 February 2016}}</ref> The mayor holds [[executive power]] and has nine secretariats under his command: Administration, Education, Finances, Tourism, Environment and Safety, Health, Social and Thermal Promotion, Public Works, and Urban Services.<ref name=Administration /> They assist the mayor in his government, planning, developing, orienting, coordinating and implementing policies in their respective areas. The [[legislative power]] is represented by the [[unicameral]] Municipal Council and its councillors, who are responsible for legislating and supervising the actions of the City Hall. The municipality is governed by the Organic Law of the Municipality of the Spa of Águas de São Pedro, from 5 April 1990.

There is no [[courthouse]] in Águas de São Pedro; the municipality is part of the district court of São Pedro.<ref>{{cite web |date=12 September 2015 |title=TJSP instala 2ª Vara da Comarca de São Pedro |url=http://www.tjsp.jus.br/Noticias/noticia?codigoNoticia=27968&Id=27968 |website=São Paulo Court of Justice |trans-title=São Paulo Court of Justice Installs 2nd Court of the District of São Pedro |language=Portuguese |access-date=6 August 2019 |quote=…for the implementation of the 2nd Court, which was awaited for years by the people of São Pedro, Águas de São Pedro and Santa Maria da Serra, which belong to the district. }}</ref> According to the [[Regional Electoral Courts|Regional Electoral Court of São Paulo]], the city had in 2014 2,881 voters,<ref>{{cite web |date=22 September 2014 |title=614 mil eleitores paulistas utilizarão a biometria em outubro |url=http://www.tre-sp.jus.br/imprensa/noticias-tre-sp/2014/Setembro/mais-de-610-mil-eleitores-paulistas-utilizarao-a-biometria-em-outubro |website=Regional Electoral Court of São Paulo |trans-title=614,000 voters of the São Paulo state will use biometrics in October |language=Portuguese |access-date=24 February 2016 |archive-date=9 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160309080149/http://www.tre-sp.jus.br/imprensa/noticias-tre-sp/2014/Setembro/mais-de-610-mil-eleitores-paulistas-utilizarao-a-biometria-em-outubro |url-status=dead }}</ref> a number greater than the number of inhabitants. This happens because the city has many vacationers and students that end up transferring their electoral register to the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |date=3 October 2010 |title=Nº de eleitores em Águas de São Pedro é maior do que a população |url=http://www.tvb.com.br/Nº+DE+ELEITORES+EM+AGUAS+DE+SAO+PEDRO+E+MAIOR+DO+QUE+A+POPULACAO/2.8,3647 |website=TVB |trans-title=No. of voters in Águas de São Pedro is greater than the population |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160306014101/http://www.tvb.com.br/N%C2%BA+DE+ELEITORES+EM+AGUAS+DE+SAO+PEDRO+E+MAIOR+DO+QUE+A+POPULACAO/2.8,3647 |archive-date=6 March 2016 |access-date=7 April 2019}}</ref>

=== Politics ===

The current mayor is Paulo Sergio Barboza de Lima, a member of the [[Brazilian Social Democracy Party]], and the [[vice mayor]] is Celso Henrique de Azevedo Marques, affiliated with the [[Party of the Republic]].<ref name=Administration /> The Municipal Council is composed as follows: two seats are held by the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD); two seats by the Brazilian Social Democracy Party (PSDB); one seat by the Party of the Republic (PR); one seat by the [[Democrats (Brazil)|Democrats]] (DEM); one seat by the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]] (PT); one seat by the [[Popular Socialist Party (Brazil)|Popular Socialist Party]] (PPS); and one seat by the [[Brazilian Republican Party]] (PRB).<ref name=Legislature />

In the last elections in 2018, the most successful party in Águas de São Pedro was the [[Social Liberal Party (Brazil)|Social Liberal Party]], getting the most votes in all positions except for state representatives. Of the total votes, it won 64.03% (1st round) and 78.64% (2nd round)<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados: Presidente &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Eleições 2018 |url=https://especiais.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2018/resultados/municipios-sao-paulo/aguas-de-sao-pedro-sp/presidente/ |website=Gazeta do Povo |date=10 July 2018 |publisher=Grupo Paranaense de Comunicação |trans-title=Results: President &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Elections 2018 |language=Portuguese |access-date=7 April 2019}}</ref> of the votes for the presidential candidate; 28.87% of the votes for the senator candidate;<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados: Senador &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Eleições 2018 |url=https://especiais.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2018/resultados/municipios-sao-paulo/aguas-de-sao-pedro-sp/senador/ |website=Gazeta do Povo |date=10 July 2018 |publisher=Grupo Paranaense de Comunicação |trans-title=Results: Senator &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Elections 2018 |language=Portuguese |access-date=7 April 2019}}</ref> and 26.09% of the votes for candidates for federal deputy.<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados: Deputado Federal &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Eleições 2018 |url=https://especiais.gazetadopovo.com.br/eleicoes/2018/resultados/municipios-sao-paulo/aguas-de-sao-pedro-sp/deputado-federal/ |website=Gazeta do Povo |date=10 July 2018 |publisher=Grupo Paranaense de Comunicação |trans-title=Results: Federal Deputy &#124; Águas de São Pedro (SP) &#124; Elections 2018 |language=Portuguese |access-date=7 April 2019}}</ref>

=== Transportation ===

[[File:Terminal Rodoviário ASP 00.jpg|thumb|right|alt=Asphalted street with a bus terminal in the background with a parked bus|Bus station of Águas de São Pedro]]

In 1892 a [[railway station]] was opened in the town of São Pedro; it belonged to the ''Companhia União Sorocabana e Ytuana'', which later served as a route for those who wanted to reach Águas de São Pedro. Between 1907 and 1919 it was part of the Sorocabana Railway, and until 1966 it belonged to the Sorocabana Railroad, part of the already defunct Piracicaba Line. The station was deactivated at the end of the 1960s, when the first paved roads were built in São Pedro and its enclave.<ref>{{cite web |title=São Pedro |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/s/spedro.htm |website=Train Stations of Brazil |language=Portuguese |access-date=22 February 2016}}</ref> Today, only one highway passes through the city, the [[Rodovia SP-304|SP-304]], [[Rodovia]] [[Geraldo de Barros]]. === Health care ===

In 2009 the municipality had four health care facilities, two private and two public (the Municipal First Aid Clinic and the Basic Health Unit Eurides de Lima "Dinho Barbosa"). The city also has outpatient care with medical care in basic specialties, dental care with a dentist, and access to services in the ''[[Sistema Único de Saúde]]'' (Unified Health System, known by the acronym SUS).<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em Síntese &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Serviços de saúde &#124; Estabelecimentos de Saúde |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/aguas-de-sao-pedro/pesquisa/32/28163 |website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Brazil in Brief &#124; São Paulo &#124; Águas de São Pedro &#124; Research &#124; Health services &#124; Health Establishments |language=Portuguese |access-date=12 April 2019}}</ref> In December 2009 Águas de São Pedro had sixteen doctors, four general practitioners, four nursing assistants, three nurses, two nursing technicians, two physiotherapists and eight people distributed in other categories, for a total of 39 health care professionals.<ref>{{Cite FTP |title=Caderno de Informações de Saúde |url=ftp://ftp.datasus.gov.br/caderno/geral/sp/SP_Aguas_de_Sao_Pedro_Geral.xls
