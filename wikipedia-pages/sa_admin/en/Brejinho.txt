'''Brejinho''' (''Little Swamp'') is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 8,033 and <!--, the density is /km<sup>2</sup>-->the area is 106.066&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]] state   ('''N''' and '''W'''); [[São José do Egito]]  and [[Terezinha, Pernambuco|Terezinha]]  ('''S''');   [[Itapetim]]  ('''E''').
* Area - 106.3&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 737 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 22.5 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 410&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activity in '''Brejinho''' is [[agribusiness]], especially the breeding of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]];  and planting of [[maize|corn]] and [[beans]].
