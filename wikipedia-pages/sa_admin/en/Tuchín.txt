== Geography ==
Tuchín is located in the northeast of [[Córdoba Department|Córdoba]] at an elevation of 106 meters above sea level, 127 km from [[Monteria, Colombia|Montería]]. The municipality borders San Antonio de Palmito and [[Sincelejo]] in the [[Sucre Department|Department of Sucre]] to the north; San Andrés de Sotavento to the east and south; and Chimá and Momil to the west.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Equipo de Internet para la Rendición de Cuentas - http://www.iprc.org.co |title=Nuestro Municipio |url=http://www.tuchin-cordoba.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170118191628/http://www.tuchin-cordoba.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |archive-date=2017-01-18 |access-date=2026-05-11 |website=www.tuchin-cordoba.gov.co |language=es}}</ref>

== Population ==
It has a population of 52,000, the majority of whom belong to the indigenous Zenú ethnic group.<ref name=":0" />
