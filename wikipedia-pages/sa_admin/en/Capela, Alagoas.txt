'''Capela''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kaˈpɛlɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 16,979 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/capela/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|205|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=IBGE - Cidades@ |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] |access-date=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
