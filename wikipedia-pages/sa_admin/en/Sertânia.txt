'''Sertânia''' is a city  in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], was 34,271. Its total area is 2409.51&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Iguaraci]] and [[Paraíba]] state (N); [[Arcoverde]], [[Buíque]], [[Ibimirim]] and [[Tupanatinga]] (S);  [[Custódia]]   (W); Paraíba (E)
* Area - 2421.51&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 558 m
* Hydrography - [[Moxotó River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi-arid]], hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 23.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 309&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Sertânia are based in industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[cattle]], [[sheep]] (over 85,000), [[pig]]s, [[goat]]s (over 120,000), [[horse]]s, and [[donkey]]s; and plantations of [[maize|corn]], [[tomato]]es and [[bean]]s.
