'''Loreto'''  ({{IPA|es|loˈɾeto|Loreto Pronunciation.ogg}}) is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Baja California Sur]]. It was created in 1992 from the partition of the neighboring [[Comondú Municipality]]. The municipal seat is in the town of [[Loreto, Baja California Sur|Loreto]], which was the former capital of [[Las Californias]] during [[Spain|Spanish]] colonial times.

The 2020 census reported a population of 18,052, about 94% of whom lived in the town of Loreto. The municipality has an area of {{convert|4,311|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Demographics==

As 2020, the city of Loreto with a population of 16,311 concentrates 94% of the municipal population. There are other 136 small communities, which the largest are [[Ligüí, Baja California Sur|Ligüí]] (248 hab.), [[Ensenada Blanca, Baja California Sur|Ensenada Blanca]] (227 hab.), [[Puerto Agua Verde, Baja California Sur|Puerto Agua Verde]] (210 hab.), [[San Javier, Baja California Sur|San Javier]] (155 hab.) and [[Nopoló, Baja California Sur|Nopoló]] (103 hab.).<ref name=CENSOLORETO2020/>
