'''Port-à-Piment''' ({{IPA|fr|pɔʁ a pimɑ̃}}; {{langx|ht|Pòtapiman disid}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Côteaux Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2009, the commune had a population of 17,207.
