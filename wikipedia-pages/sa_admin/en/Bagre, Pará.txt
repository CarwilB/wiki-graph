'''Bagre''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 31,325 people. The area of the municipality is 4,397.290&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Portel''.
