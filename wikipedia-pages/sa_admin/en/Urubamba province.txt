'''Urubamba''' is one of thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Cusco Region]] in the southern highlands of [[Peru]].

== Geography ==
The province is bounded to the north by the [[La Convención Province|province of La Convención]], to the east by the [[Calca Province|province of Calca]], to the south by the [[Cusco Province|province of Cusco]] and the [[Anta Province|province of Anta]], and to the west by the [[La Convención Province|province of La Convención]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: ''distrito''), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Chinchero District|Chinchero]] ([[Chinchero]])
* [[Huayllabamba District, Urubamba|Huayllabamba]] ([[Huayllabamba, Urubamba|Huayllabamba]])
* [[Machupicchu District|Machupicchu]] ([[Machupicchu, Peru|Machupicchu]])
* [[Maras District|Maras]] ([[Maras, Peru|Maras]])
* [[Ollantaytambo District|Ollantaytambo]] ([[Ollantaytambo]])
* [[Urubamba District|Urubamba]] ([[Urubamba, Peru|Urubamba]])
* [[Yucay District|Yucay]] ([[Yucay]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (51.34%) learnt to speak in childhood, 46.78% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
