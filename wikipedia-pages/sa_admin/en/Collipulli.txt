'''Collipulli''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] forming part of the [[Malleco Province]] in the [[Araucanía Region]] in southern [[Chile]]. Its name means "coloured lands" in the [[Mapuche]] language spoken in the area. It has a population of 22,354 (2005) and an area of {{convert|1296|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. Its current mayor is Manuel Macaya Ramírez.

==History==
On 22 November 1867, the city was founded as a military fort during the [[Occupation of Araucanía]]. No traces of the fort remain today. The fort was protected by defensive ditches and wooden stakes, and was supported by a series of bridges placed over the [[Malleco River]] and its tributaries. Other forts were established in the area, and settlers soon followed, developing the land for agriculture.

[[File:Pmalleco.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The [[Malleco Viaduct]], a National Monument located in Collipulli.]]
The [[Malleco Viaduct]], today a National Monument, was officially opened on 26 October 1890, by President [[Manuel Balmaceda]]. The viaduct served as a rapid and effective transportation system for trade and commerce. It sustained some damage in the major earthquake of 27 February 2010.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.lanacion.cl/teeerreemooootooo/noticias/2010-02-27/214421.html |title=lanacion.cl: TEEERREEMOOOOTOOO |website=www.lanacion.cl |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100304074919/http://www.lanacion.cl/teeerreemooootooo/noticias/2010-02-27/214421.html |archive-date=2010-03-04}} </ref>

===Present day===
Collipulli's economy relies on farming, forestry, and services. Although the majority of the land is suitable for agriculture, an increasing area has been given to forestry in recent years. Mario Garbarini helped to found the firefighters company and was well known in Collipulli.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Collipulli spans an area of {{convert|1295.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 22,354 inhabitants (11,106 men and 11,248 women). Of these, 16,006 (71.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,348 (28.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 1.8% (413 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Collipulli is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Leopoldo Rosales Neira ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Collipulli is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gonzalo Arenas ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mario Venegas ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 48th electoral district, together with [[Angol]], [[Renaico]], [[Ercilla, Chile|Ercilla]], [[Los Sauces]], [[Purén]], [[Lumaco]] and [[Traiguén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alberto Espina Otero ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Jaime Quintana Leal ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 14th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-North).
