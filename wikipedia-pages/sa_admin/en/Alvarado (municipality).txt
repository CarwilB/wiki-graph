'''Alvarado''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. The municipality is part of the state's [[Papaloapan Region (Veracruz)|Papaloapan Region]] and its municipal seat is established at the homonymous city of [[Alvarado, Veracruz|Alvarado]].

==History==
In 1816, the citizens of the village of San Cristóbal de Alvarado sought the designation of ''Town'' from [[Ferdinand VII of Spain|Ferdinand VII]], [[Monarchy of Spain|King]] of Spain. The King granted the village the title of Town on 8 September 1816 by Royal Decree, at a cost of 92,800 [[Spanish maravedí|maravedís]]. Almost two years later, on 5 September 1818, Ferdinand VII authorized the first Honorable ''Ayuntamiento'' or [[city council]]. On 3 October 1818, the first City Council was established with [[Tomás Hondal]] serving as first Mayor.<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30011a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Alvarado |publisher=Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=29 January 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070314043017/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30011a.htm |archive-date=14 March 2007 }}</ref>

Once the [[Mexican War of Independence|Independence War]] was over, the State enacted its own Constitution in which the state territory was divided in 4 [[Department (administrative division)|departments]] and 12 [[Canton (administrative division)|cantons]]. The municipality of Alvarado was assigned to the Canton of Veracruz in the Department of Veracruz.

==Geography==
[[File:Alvarado, VER-Satellite.jpg|thumb|left|250px|[[Alvarado Lagoon]] complex and the [[Papaloapan River]]]]
The municipality has an area of {{convert|826.9|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}<ref name="inegiMexCifras"/> and an elevation of {{convert|10|m|ft|abbr=on}}. It is located in the state's [[Papaloapan Region (Veracruz)|Papaloapan region]] and is bordered by the municipalities of [[Boca del Río, Veracruz|Boca del Río]], [[Tlalixcoyan]], [[Medellín Municipality, Veracruz|Medellín]], [[Ignacio de la Llave (municipality)|Ignacio de la Llave]], [[Ixmatlahuacan]], [[Acula (municipality)|Acula]], [[Tlacotalpan (municipality)|Tlacotalpan]] and [[Lerdo de Tejada]].

===Hydrography===
The municipality is crossed by the [[Papaloapan River|Papaloapan]] and [[Blanco River (Veracruz)|Blanco]] rivers, both of which drain into the [[Alvarado Lagoon System|Alvarado Lagoon complex]].

The Alvarado Lagoon complex is formed, from north to south, by the Camaronera, Buen País, Alvarado and [[Tlalixcoyan lagoon]]s.

===Orography===
Alvarado is located in the state's central [[coast]]al zone, within the [[Sotavento plains]]. [[Dune]]s are common in rural and urban areas; an example of this is the neighborhood of {{lang|es|Lomas del Rosario}} ({{lit|[[Rosary]] Hills}}), built on the Rosary dunes which are inside the city of Alvarado.

===Climate===
In this region there is hot/sub-[[humidity|humid]] —the wetter of the sub-humids— weather with abundant rains in summer. The maximum average temperature is {{convert|26|C|F|abbr=on}} and {{convert|18|C|F|abbr=on}} as minimum average temperature, varying from {{convert|5|C|F|abbr=on}} to {{convert|7|C|F|abbr=on}} every year. The dry season is from January to May and the period of rains starts in June. The ''norte''{{efn|A {{lang|es|norte}} ({{lit|north}}) is, most of the time, a cold wind mass coming from the North that can reach sustained [[wind speed]]s and [[Wind gust|gusts]] of well over {{convert|60|km/h|mph|abbr=on}}. {{lang|es|Nortes}} can last from a couple of hours to several days.}} season starts in November and intensifies in January.<ref name="contribution664">{{cite web|url=http://biblioweb.dgsca.unam.mx/cienciasdelmar/instituto/1989-2/articulo347.html|title=Anales del Instituto de Ciencias del Mar y Limnología|last=Reguero|first=Martha|author2=García-Cubas, Antonio|work=Moluscos de la Laguna de Alvarado, Veracruz: Sistemática y Ecología|publisher=[[National Autonomous University of Mexico|UNAM]]|language=es|access-date=29 January 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090220124220/http://biblioweb.dgsca.unam.mx/cienciasdelmar/instituto/1989-2/articulo347.html|archive-date=20 February 2009|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Infrastructure ==
===Roads===
The municipality is connected mainly through two [[Mexican Federal Highway]]s<ref>{{cite web|publisher=Dirección General de Servicios Técnicos, [[Secretaría de Comunicaciones y Transportes]]|title=Datos Viales de Veracruz|url=http://dgst.sct.gob.mx/fileadmin/Viales_2011/30_VERACRUZ.pdf|year=2011|language=es|pages=2–3|access-date=2012-08-07|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120514175732/http://dgst.sct.gob.mx/fileadmin/Viales_2011/30_VERACRUZ.pdf|archive-date=2012-05-14}}</ref> and a few state and municipal roads.
* [[Mexican Federal Highway 180|Highway 180]] covers much of the municipality from north to south, connecting Alvarado with the municipalities of Medellín and Boca del Río to the north, and Lerdo de Tejada to the south. Prior to the building of the Alvarado toll bridge in the 1960s, ferries were used at the Papaloapan River's mouth to cross the river.
* [[Mexican Federal Highway 175|Highway 175]] starts at Buenavista, at the south end of the municipality, connecting with Tlacotalpan to the west. Highway 175 passes over an upstream section of the Papaloapan River via the Tlacotalpan toll bridge.

Regarding state and municipal roads, the small network connects a few locations in the north and northwest areas of the municipality.

{{As of|2020}}, Alvarado had a population of 57,305 and 216 localities. The municipal seat, [[Alvarado, Veracruz|Alvarado]] is the biggest locality with a population of 20,797. Other important localities are [[Antón Lizardo, Veracruz|Antón Lizardo]] (6,284 hab.), Lomas del Sol (2,177 hab.), Playas del Conchal (1,889 hab.) and Paso Nacional (1,828 hab.).<ref name="cuadernillos2020"/>

== Government ==

=== Municipal ===
Local government, or {{lang|es|Ayuntamiento}} ({{langx|en|[[Council|Municipal council]]}}), is led by the {{lang|es|Presidente municipal|}} ({{langx|en|[[Mayor]]|lit=municipal president}}) with support of the [[Syndic]], and the {{lang|es|Regidores}} ({{langx|en|councillors|lit=regents}}). The mayor is elected by [[Direct election|popular election]] for a 4-year term with non-limited, non-consecutive re-elections.

=== State and Federal ===
The municipality is part of the XXIII State Electoral district<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ley de División Territorial del Estado de Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave, para la elección del Poder Legislativo del mismo |url=http://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Documentos/Estatal/Veracruz/wo77248.pdf |access-date=6 August 2022 |publisher= Government of Veracruz |page=3 |language=es |publication-date=14 July 1988}}</ref> and XIX Federal Electoral District.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sistema de Información Geográfica Electoral |url=https://cartografia.ife.org.mx/sige7/?infogeo=RSM |access-date=2022-08-06 |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional Electoral|INE]]|language=es}}</ref>
