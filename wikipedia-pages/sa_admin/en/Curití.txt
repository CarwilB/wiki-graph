'''Curiti''' is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in North-Eastern [[Colombia]]. The area is famous for crafts made from fique, or agave sisal.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.vivatravelguides.com/south-america/colombia/eastern-colombia/san-gil/near-san-gil/curiti/|title = VIVA Travel Guides}}</ref>

Curiti is the birthplace of [[Alejandro Galvis Galvis]], a renowned politician and publisher.
