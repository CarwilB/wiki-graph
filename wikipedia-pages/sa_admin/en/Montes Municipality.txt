'''Montes''' is a municipality of [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]], [[Venezuela]]. It has six [[Parishes of Venezuela|parishes]], and the [[municipal seat]] is in [[Cumanacoa]]. As of 2021, it has a population of 69,696.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones Regionales y Municipales – Municipio Montes |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/web/mapas/mapas_cargos_a_elegir/documentos/17_ce_sucre_concejales_2021.pdf |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral |access-date=5 February 2024 |pages=13–14 |date=30 November 2021 |archive-date=5 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240205201929/http://www.cne.gob.ve/web/mapas/mapas_cargos_a_elegir/documentos/17_ce_sucre_concejales_2021.pdf |url-status=live }}</ref>
