'''San Jerónimo District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Cusco Province]] in [[Peru]].
<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>  It is home to the Universidad Andina del Cusco.  According to the 2007 census, there are 31,687 residents in the district.  A large part of the population communicates in [[Quechua languages|Quechua]].                        On August 9, 1970, [[LANSA Flight 502]] crashed over the district. All but one of the 99 people on board were killed, with another 2 people on the ground.
