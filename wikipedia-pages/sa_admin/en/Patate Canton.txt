'''Patate Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Tungurahua Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Patate]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 13,497.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inec.gob.ec/cpv/ |title=Estadísticas de la Semana |access-date=2014-02-26 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140226070247/http://www.inec.gob.ec/cpv/ |archive-date=2014-02-26 }} at statoids.com</ref>

The still active [[Tungurahua| Tungurahua volcano]] is situated within the canton.

Patate (town and canton) was declared a ''[[Pueblos Mágicos (Ecuador)|Pueblo Mágico]]'' (magical town) by Ecuador's Ministry of Tourism (MINTUR) in 2019. It was the first community in the country to be awarded this distinction.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Patate es oficialmente un 'Pueblo Mágico' del país|url=https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/patate-oficialmente-pueblo-magico-pais.html|access-date=2021-04-02|website=El Comercio}}</ref>
