'''Tombos''' is a  [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in southeast [[Minas Gerais]] state, [[Brazil]]. It is located in the [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] region and its population was approximately 7,850 inhabitants in 2020 (IBGE).
