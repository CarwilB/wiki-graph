'''Jangada''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]].

Noteworthy is the presence of the archaeological site [[Abrigo de Santa Elina]], with human remains as old as 27,000 years ago.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2023-10-09 |date=2022-04-15 |language=pt-BR |title=Jangada – Sítio Arqueológico Santa Elina {{!}} ipatrimônio |url=https://www.ipatrimonio.org/jangada-sitio-arqueologico-santa-elina/}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
