'''Nova Xavantina''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]] in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf | title = Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009| publisher = Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) | date = August 14, 2009 | access-date=December 17, 2009 | format = [[PDF]]|language=pt }}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |date=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20%28pelos%20dados%20de%202000%29.htm |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref>

== History ==
The area now comprising Nova Xavantina was originally inhabited by the [[Xavante]], a [[Jê languages|Jê]]-speaking people who once occupied a large territory in eastern Mato Grosso.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Historia de Nova Xavantina MT - Ache Tudo e Região |url=https://www.achetudoeregiao.com.br/mt/nova_xavantina/historia.htm |access-date=2023-01-04 |website=www.achetudoeregiao.com.br}}</ref> In 1660, the ''[[Bandeirantes|bandeirante]]'' [[Bartolomeu Bueno da Silva]] arrived in the area, capturing and enslaving much of the indigenous Xavante population.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nova Xavantina, primeira cidade da Roncador-XIngu – Rota Brasil Oeste |url=http://www.brasiloeste.com.br/2001/05/historia-nova-xavantina/ |access-date=2023-01-04 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The modern municipality can be traced to the 1944 Roncador-Xingu Expedition, part of the larger [[March to the West (Brazil)|March to the West]] policy enacted by [[Getúlio Vargas]] during the [[Vargas Era|Estado Novo]] period.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mt/nova-xavantina/historico |access-date=2023-01-04 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|title=História|lang=pt-br}}</ref> Until this point, much of Brazil's interior was sparsely populated.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Velasco |first=Valquiria |title=Marcha para o oeste do Brasil - História |url=https://www.infoescola.com/historia-do-brasil/marcha-para-o-oeste/ |access-date=2023-01-04 |website=InfoEscola |language=pt-BR}}</ref> In 1944, the expedition arrived at the [[Rio das Mortes]], building a settlement on the south bank which they named Vila da Xavantina and a settlement on the north bank called Nova Brasília, which were both later incorporated as a district of the [[Barra do Garças]] municipality.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=nox |title=DADOS HISTÓRICOS DE NOVA XAVANTINA-MT/CÂMARA MUNICIPAL |url=https://www.novaxavantina.mt.leg.br/institucional/historia/dados-historicos-de-nova-xavantina-mt |access-date=2023-01-04 |website=Câmara Municipal de Nova Xavantina-MT |language=pt-br}}</ref>

Beginning in the 1970s, the region underwent significant population growth, emerging as a major center for agricultural production in Mato Grosso. Agricultural colonization projects brought immigrants to Mato Grosso from other regions of Brazil, primarily the [[South Region, Brazil|south]].

In 1971, a bridge across the [[Rio das Mortes]] was opened, forming a crucial connection between the much larger city of [[Barra do Garças]] to the south and less-developed areas north of the [[Rio das Mortes]], and connecting the settlements of Nova Brasília and Vila da Xavantina.<ref name=":0" /> Development in the municipalities to the north of Nova Xavantina in the [[Araguaia River|Araguaia]] valley such as [[Água Boa, Mato Grosso|Água Boa]], [[Canarana, Mato Grosso|Canarana]], [[Querência]], [[Vila Rica, Mato Grosso|Vila Rica]], was facilitated by the bridge and made Nova Xavantina an important center for agricultural expansion.<ref name=":2" />  The populations of both settlements began to grow dramatically throughout the 70s as agricultural production expanded. In 1980, Vila da Xavantina and Nova Brasília were joined to form a new municipality, independent of Barra do Garças, called Nova Xavantina.<ref name=":1" />

Agriculture has continued to expand throughout the 80s, 90s, 2000s, and 2010s, driving continued population growth and economic development in Nova Xavantina.<ref name=":2" /> A campus of the [[State University of Mato Grosso|State University of Mato Grosso (''Unemat'')]] was founded in 1991, providing another reason for immigration to the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |title=UNEMAT |url=http://portal.unemat.br/?pg=universidade&conteudo=1 |access-date=2023-01-04 |website=UNIVERSIDADE DO ESTADO DE MATO GROSSO |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Located at 14º40'24"S and 52º21'11"W, Nova Xavantina lies in the [[cerrado]] ecoregion. Under the [[Köppen climate classification]], Nova Xavantina has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: Aw) and is very warm year-round with a rainy season from approximately October to March and a dry season from April to September. It is within the bounds of the [[Amazônia Legal|Brazilian Legal Amazon]]. The [[Rio das Mortes]], a major tributary of the [[Araguaia River|Rio Araguaia]] flows through the center of Nova Xavantina. Though technically in the Amazon Time Zone (UTC-4), Nova Xavantina and other cities in the Araguaia valley unofficially observe Brasília Time (UTC-3). Since the 1980s, Nova Xavantina and the surrounding region have undergone extensive deforestation due to agricultural expansion.

== Economy ==
Nova Xavantina is located in the Amazon-Cerrado transition region, one of the world's largest agricultural frontiers. Crop production, primarily soybeans and corn, and cattle ranching constitute the majority of land in the municipality and agriculture makes up the largest sector of the economy. Due primarily to agricultural expansion, the area has undergone a significant amount of deforestation since the 1980s. 

Another economic driver in Nova Xavantina is tourism, primarily cerrado ecotourism. The city is famous for its many waterfalls, and for recreation on the Rio das Mortes, including swimming, boating, and fishing. 

== Education ==
A campus of the [[State University of Mato Grosso|State University of Mato Grosso (''Unemat'')]] is located in Nova Xavantina, offering undergraduate degrees in Agronomy, Biological Sciences, Civil Engineering, and Tourism, and a graduate degree in Ecology and Conservation.
