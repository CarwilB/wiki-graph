'''Progreso Municipality''' (In the [[Spanish language]]: “progress”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing  270.10&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 25&nbsp;km north of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
Progreso is one of the youngest towns in the Yucatán.<ref name=municipios /> [[Juan Miguel Castro]] Martín, owner of several sisal haciendas, including an estate called [[Hacienda San Pedro Chimay]] was the founder of the Port of Progreso.<ref name="Chimay GOY">{{cite web|title=Hacienda San Pedro Chimay|url=http://www.haciendasenyucatan.com/spedrochimay/|website=Haciendas en Yucatan|publisher=Gobierno del Estado de Yucatán|access-date=10 May 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es}}</ref> He began urging development of a new port in 1840 to further the [[henequen]] trade.<ref name="Juan Miguel Castro">{{cite journal|title=Ayuntamiento Constitucional de Progreso|journal=Diario Oficial del Gobierno del Estado|date=12 April 2006|pages=1–3|url=http://www.yucatan.gob.mx/docs/orden_juridico/Yucatan/Acuerdos/nr842rf1.pdf|access-date=5 June 2015|publisher=Ayuntamiento de Progreso|location=Progreso, Mexico|language=es}}{{Dead link|date=May 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The town was authorized under President [[Ignacio Comonfort]] on 25 February 1856 and was officially founded on 1 July 1871 as the [[Progreso, Yucatán|Port of Progreso]].<ref name=municipios />

On 8 January 1875, the first City Council was formed and the town was elevated to a villa, soon after decreed a city by the state government, and construction of the city hall commenced. In 1881, freight and passenger rail service began and in 1893, the lighthouse opened.<ref name=municipios />

In 1936, the Danish company "Christiane & Nielsen" began construction of malecón (sea wall), which was completed in 1947. It was upgraded in 1968<ref name=municipios /> In 2011, work began to rebuild the malecón and upgrade the entrance of the port.<ref name="new malecón">{{cite news|last1=Escárcega|first1=Mary|title=Yucatán construye Malecón en Puerto Progreso|url=http://www.eluniversal.com.mx/notas/751628.html|access-date=5 June 2015|publisher=El Universal|date=14 March 2011|location=Mexico City, Mexico|language=es}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a term of three years. The president appoints nine Councilpersons to serve on the board for three year terms, as the Secretary and councilors who oversee police and police stations; health and public works; transportation and education; special events; disability regulations; recruitment; and parks and public gardens.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Progreso|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=5 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=8 May 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140508064449/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is Progreso, Yucatán. ==Local festivals==
Every year a [[carnival]] is celebrated from 15 to 30 April and 1 June is a fiesta for the Navy. On 7 August, the feast of San Telmo, patron of fishermen, is recognized.<ref name=municipios />
