'''Burdett O'Connor'''<ref name="INE Census 2001">[http://www.ine.gov.bo/comunitaria/comunitariaVer.aspx?Depto=06&Prov=06&Seccion=00 Burnet O'Connor population data from 2001 Census]{{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}. [[Instituto Nacional de Estadistica de Bolivia]].</ref> is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern part of [[Tarija Department]] in [[Bolivia]], named after [[Francisco Burdett O'Connor]], a chronicler of the South American War of Independence and the making of [[Tarija, Bolivia|Tarija]].{{Citation needed|date=November 2009}}

==Location==
Burdett O'Connor province is one of six provinces in the Tarija Department. It is located between 20° 53' and 21° 58' [[Latitude|south]] and between 63° 36' and 64° 26' [[Longitude|west]].

The province borders [[Chuquisaca Department]] in the north, [[Eustaquio Méndez Province]] in the north-west, [[Cercado Province (Tarija)|Cercado Province]] in the west, [[Aniceto Arce Province]] in the south-west, and [[Gran Chaco Province]] in the south and east. 

The province extends over {{convert|140|km}} from north to south, and {{convert|100|km}} from east to west.

== Population ==
The main language of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 98.4%, while 10.3% of the population speak [[Guarani language|Guaraní]], also 1.8 speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], and 0.3% [[Aymara language|Aymara]] (by western immigrants). 

The population increased from 17,763 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 19,339 (2001 census), an increase of 8.9%. 47.5% of the population are younger than 15 years old. 

89.5% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 84.5% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]]. 

51.6% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 14.6% in [[mining]], 7.0% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 26.8% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

89.9% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 4.0% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

== Division ==
In contrast to the neighbouring provinces, the province comprises only one [[municipio]] which is '''Entre Ríos'''.
It is identical to the Burdett O'Connor Province.
