'''Moron''' ({{IPA|fr|mɔʁɔ̃|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-DenisdeShawi-moron.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Mowon}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jérémie Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of Haiti. It has 31,157 inhabitants (in 2015).

Villages located within the commune include: Moron, [[Gros Morne, Grand'Anse|Gros Morne]], [[La Roche, Grand'Anse|La Roche]], [[Layalace]], [[Lenaire]], [[Plik]] and [[Sources Chaudes]].
