'''Aldama''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Juan Aldama, Chihuahua|Juan Aldama]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 22,302,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Aldama|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=002|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=1 May 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210501084905/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=002|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 19,879 as of 2005.<ref>
