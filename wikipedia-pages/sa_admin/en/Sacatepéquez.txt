'''Sacatepéquez''' ({{IPA|es|sakateˈpekes}}) was a city in [[Guatemala]] from November 21, 1542 until July 29, 1773 when it was destroyed by the [[Santa Marta earthquake]]. Sacatepéquez means "grass hill" and gave its name to the [[Sacatepéquez Department]], founded in 1839.<ref>{{cite book |title=The concise Oxford dictionary of world place names |date=2020 |publisher=Oxford University Press |location=Oxford |isbn=9780191905636 |edition=Sixth |url=https://www.oxfordreference.com/display/10.1093/acref/9780191905636.001.0001/acref-9780191905636-e-9127 |chapter=Sacatepéquez, Guatemala |quote=A department, established in 1839, named after a city of the same name, meaning ‘Grass Hill’, which was destroyed in an earthquake in 1773}}</ref>{{Additional citation needed|date=November 2025}}

Sacatepéquez and [[Antigua Guatemala]] were the two municipalities belonging to the department of [[Chimaltenango (department)|Chimaltenango]].

On September 12, 1839, the department of Sacatepéquez was created, getting its name from the former city. [[Antigua Guatemala]] was made the capital.
