'''Concepción del Norte''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|135|km2}} and had a population of 8,989 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== Geography ==
Concepción del Norte is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Petoa]] and [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] to the north, [[Chinda]] to the south and west, and the department of [[Villa Nueva]] and [[San Antonio de Cortés]] to the east.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Concepción del Norte, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Departamentos/16%20Santa%20Barbara/Concepci%C3%B3n%20del%20Norte.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Concepcion del Norte|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-concepcion-norte/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|135|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1607__concepci%C3%B3n_del_norte/|title=Concepción del Norte|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|290.7|m}} above sea level, Concepción del Norte has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|22.98|C}} and receives about {{cvt|315.01|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/concepcion-del-norte|title=Concepcion del Norte, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Concepción del Norte had a total population of 8,989 inhabitants, of whom 4,741 (52.7%) were men and 4,248 (47.3%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=20}}

About 0.5% of the population were classified as Indigenous and the rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 37.7% of the population were aged 0–14 years, 55.3% were aged 15–64, and 7.1% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20 years and the mean age was 26.3 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 25.7%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 1,946 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.6 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19}}
