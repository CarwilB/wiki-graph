'''Sasaima''' ({{IPA|es|saˈsajma}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The municipality was established in 1605 by Alonso Vasquez de Cisneros.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sasaima-cundinamarca.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m1I1--&m=f |title=Información General > Nuestro municipio - Sasaima |date=29 March 2010 |accessdate=19 June 2010 |archive-date=14 July 2012 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120714075325/http://sasaima-cundinamarca.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m1I1--&m=f |url-status=dead }}</ref> The mayor is Gonzalo Parra Bohorquez.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sasaima-cundinamarca.gov.co/nuestraalcaldia.shtml?apc=a1I1--&m=q |title=¿Quiénes somos? > Nuestra Alcaldia - Sasaima |date=29 March 2010 |accessdate=19 June 2010 |archive-date=15 July 2012 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120715071500/http://sasaima-cundinamarca.gov.co/nuestraalcaldia.shtml?apc=a1I1--&m=q |url-status=dead }}</ref>
