'''Iñaquito''' is a parish of [[Quito]], [[Ecuador]], located toward the north of the city.{{cn|date=February 2024}} There is a large concentration of malls in the area,<ref>{{cite book |last1=Birnbaum |first1=Alexandra M. |title=Birnbaum's South America, 1993 |date=14 August 1992 |publisher=HarperCollins Publishers Limited |isbn=978-0-06-278052-2 |page=429 |language=en}}</ref> and the population is wealthy relative to the rest of Quito.{{cn|date=February 2024}}

This is also home to a large international community in Ecuador, primarily Americans. Various mission agencies have their headquarters for Ecuador here, including [[HCJB]] and [[Youth World]]. To cater to this international community, the Christian & Missionary Alliance started the Alliance Academy, now called [[Alliance Academy International]], which is currently a school containing around 500 students K-12, the majority being Ecuadorian and American but with students representing around 20 countries. There is also a church in English for this missionary community - English Fellowship church.{{cn|date=February 2024}}
