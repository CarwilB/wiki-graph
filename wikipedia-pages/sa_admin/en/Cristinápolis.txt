'''Cristinápolis''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kɾistʃiˈnapolis|lang}}) is the southernmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 18,029 in the year 2020, and its area is {{cvt|251.3|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>
