'''San Sebastián de Buenavista''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. Founded in 1748 by [[Fernando de Mier y Guerra]] with the name San Sebastián de Melchiquejo, but was later changed on November 15, 1957 when it was erected municipality.
