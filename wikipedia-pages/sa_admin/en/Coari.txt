'''Coari''' (''Choary'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]].

==Geography==

The municipal seat of Coari is one of the largest cities of the [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas state]]. It is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Coari]]. The area has reserves of oil and [[natural gas]].
Coari is served by [[Coari Airport]] located 6&nbsp;km from downtown Coari. [[Porto Urucu Airport]] located in the district of Porto Urucu 470&nbsp;km away serves the population residing and working for [[Petrobras]].

The municipality is in the [[Juruá-Purus moist forests]] ecoregion.<ref>{{citation |title=South America: Amazon Basin, northwestern Brazil (NT0133)
 |last=Sears|first=Robin |publisher=WWF: World Wildlife Fund
 |url=http://www.worldwildlife.org/ecoregions/nt0133|accessdate=2017-03-28}}</ref>
It contains part of the [[Amanã Sustainable Development Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Amanã – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS Amanã|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/337|accessdate=2016-05-05}}</ref>
It contains a small portion of the {{convert|1008167|ha|adj=on}} [[Piagaçu-Purus Sustainable Development Reserve]], established in 2003.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Piagaçu-Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS Piagaçu-Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5047|accessdate=2016-08-20}}</ref>
The municipality contains 66.15% of the {{convert|217486|ha|adj=on}} [[Catuá-Ipixuna Extractive Reserve]], established in 2003 as the first extractive reserve in the state of Amazonas.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Catuá-Ipixuna – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Catuá-Ipixuna|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental 
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/uc/1686|accessdate=2016-08-30}}</ref>
