'''Albertina''' is a city in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 its population was estimated to be 3,011.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/albertina/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Founded: 30 December 1962.
