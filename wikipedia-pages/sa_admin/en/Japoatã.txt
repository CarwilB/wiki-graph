'''Japoatã''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʒapoɐˈtɐ̃}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 13,429 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/japoata/panorama |access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|420|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2016-08-03 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>
