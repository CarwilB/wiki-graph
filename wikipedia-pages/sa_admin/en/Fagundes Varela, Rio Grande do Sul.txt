'''Fagundes Varela''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It lies at an elevation of 610 m, covers an area of 132.32&nbsp;km², and has a population of 2,741 (2020 estimate).<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/fagundes-varela/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
