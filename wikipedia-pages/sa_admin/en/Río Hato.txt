'''Río Hato''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Antón District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It is home to the [[Río Hato Airport]]. It has a land area of {{convert|140|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 15,701 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|112.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 8,888; its population as of 2000 was 10,886.<ref name=inec/>
[[File:Rio hato army air base.jpg|left|thumb|Rio Hato army air base in 1940]]
It is the site of [[Scarlett Martínez International Airport]], a small international airport primarily used by Canadian leisure airlines.
