'''Flora''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 19,538.<ref name="census"/> Flora is both the name of the resort and the neighbourhood within the resort. Flora was founded as a [[wood]] plantation in 1809.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/f/flora|title=Plantage Flora|website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

{{anchor|Uitvlugt}}The neighbourhood Uitvlugt is located in the resort.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2018/01/10/politieressorten-paramaribo-elk-versterkt-met-8-aspirant-agenten/|title=Politieressorten Paramaribo elk versterkt met 8 aspirant-agenten|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=3 June 2020}}</ref> Uitvlugt used to be a stronghold of the [[Boeroes]], the white farmers,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://plantagejagtlust.nl/?p=136|website=Plantage Jagtlust|title=BOEROES|access-date=3 June 2020|language=nl}}</ref> and is still a wealthy neighbourhood.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001199801_01/_oso001199801_01_0012.php|title=OSO. Tijdschrift voor Surinaamse taalkunde, letterkunde en geschiedenis. Jaargang 17|date=1998|access-date=3 June 2020|language=nl|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature}}</ref>
