'''Nuevo Celilac''' ({{IPA|es|ˈnweβo seliˈlak}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]]. The municipality was officially created on 29 December 1888. It covers an area of {{cvt|168.2|km2}} and had a population of 8,086 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census.

==History==
In 1791, the region was mentioned as "Celilaca", a settlement of the parish of Tencoa. It was later called as "San Pedro Celilac". According to local accounts, the settlement was affected by a [[cholera]] epidemic in 1860, after which the surviving inhabitants relocated to Tuliapa, which later became the present-day town of Nuevo Celilac. The municipality was officially created on 29 December 1888, and the first municipal authorities were appointed on 20 February 1889. It included the territories of San Pedro Celilac, San Marcos de Jalapa, and San Jerónimo del Pinal. The name derives either from a [[Mexican people|Mexican]] word meaning "in the water of the snails", or the [[Lenca]] term meaning "place of water of the reeds".<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Nueva Celilac, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Nueva-Celilac.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-Nuevo-Celilac/|title=Nueva Celilac|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Naranjito is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[San José de Colinas]] to the west and to the south, [[San Nicolás, Santa Bárbara|San Nicolás]] to the east, and [[Atima]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|168.2|km2}}, and is located to the south of the [[Jicatuyo River]].<ref name="Edu"/>

== Administrative divisions ==
The municipality comprises seven aldeas (villages) and their associated caseríos (hamlets).<ref name="INE2013">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/265-Santa-Barbara-Nuevo-Celilac.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 265, Municipio de Nuevo Celilac, Departamento de Santa Bárbara|date=February 2025|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=20-21}}

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Nuevo Celilac had a population of 8,086 inhabitants, of whom 3,950 were male and 4,137 were female.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}}<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1628__nueva_frontera/|title=Naranjito|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> The urban population was 1,758 inhabitants, while the rural population was 6,328 inhabitants. By broad age group, 2,991 inhabitants were aged 0–14 years, 3,760 were aged 15–49 years, 769 were aged 50–64 years, and 566 were aged 65 years and over.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} Of the overall population, 3.35% was classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> Among the population aged 15 years and over, the illiteracy rate was 24.8%, which was higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}}

== Culture ==
The annual patronal festival of Nuevo Celilac is celebrated on 29 and 30 June in honour of [[Saint Peter]] and [[Saint Paul]]. The National Folk Festival of the Tusa is held annually in September, and promotes local cultural and artistic traditions, parades, and competitions. The Jewish cultural group "Los Judíos", whose traditions date back to 1838, was reorganized in 1983 and participate in local festivities. The "Ocotillo" is a local tradition, during which people cut and transport a [[pine tree]] to the town square, where an effigy of [[Judas]] is hung during celebrations. The municipality is associated with the folk music group "Los Caramberos de Celilac", which has participated in the Cervantino Festival in Mexico.<ref name="Edu"/>
