'''Cruz del Eje Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lmdiario.com.ar/contenido/395211/el-domingo-25-de-junio-seran-las-elecciones-provinciales-en-cordoba|title=El domingo 25 de junio serán las elecciones provinciales en Córdoba|work=La Nueva Mañana|date=20 March 2023|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 52,172 inhabitants in an area of 6,653 km², and its capital city is [[Cruz del Eje]], which is located around 861 km from [[Capital Federal]].

==1908 earthquake==
On 22 September 1908 the region was struck by an earthquake measuring 6.5 on the [[Richter scale]]. The epicentre was around {{coord|30|30|0|S|64|30|0|W|}} and it was felt as far as 100 km away.
