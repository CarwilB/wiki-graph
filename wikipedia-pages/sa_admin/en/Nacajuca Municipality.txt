'''Nacajuca''' is a [[Municipalities of Tabasco|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tabasco]].<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tabasco/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=January 4, 2010}}</ref>

The seat is [[Nacajuca]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the north of the state of Tabasco in the Chontalpa region, bordering the municipalities of [[Jalpa de Méndez]], [[Centla Municipality|Centla]], [[Centro Municipality|Centro]] and [[Cunduacán]]. Its territory measures {{cvt|488.37|km2}} or 2.1% of the state, ranking 13th out of 17 municipalities in size.<ref name="encmuc">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb/ELOCAL/EMM_tabasco |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130221131634/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb/ELOCAL/EMM_tabasco |url-status=dead |archive-date=February 21, 2013 |title=Nacajuca |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=INAFED Instituto para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal and SEGOB Secretaría de Gobernación |year=2010 |location=Mexico |language=Spanish |accessdate=January 18, 2012 }}</ref> There are 72 total communities,<ref name ="microrreg">{{cite web |url= http://cat.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/default.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&valor=27013&varent=27&varmun=013 |title= Nacajuca Resumen Municipal |publisher= SEDESOL |location= Mexico |trans-title=Nacajuca Municipality Summary |language=Spanish |accessdate=January 18, 2012 }}</ref> which include one city (the seat), eleven communities, 28 ranches, fourteen [[ejido]]s, three congregations and three rural subdivisions. It has six regional development centers in which most of the area's economic and social activities are concentrated: Lomitas, Sandial, Taxco, Oxiacaque, Guatacalca and Mazateupa. The municipal government consists of a municipal president, a treasurer, two secretaries, ten representatives called regidores and nine departments.<ref name ="encmuc"/>

===Communities===
In addition to the seat, the municipality has a number of other notable communities. The Bosque de Saloya is a residential development located on the municipality's border with the Centro (Villahermosa) municipality. It has a population of over 8,000 as well due to its proximity to the capital, separated from it only by the Carrizal River. Due to population pressures, there are many irregular settlements in the area. It is considered to be part of the Villahermosa metropolitan area. Lomitas is located 23&nbsp;km from the municipal seat with a population of about 2,900 people. Its main economic activity is agriculture. Guatacalco is located 18&nbsp;km from the municipal seat with a population of about 2,700 people. Its main economic activities are agriculture and handcrafts. Samarkanda is located four km from the municipal seat with a population of about 2,750 people. Its main economic activities are livestock and agriculture. Sandial is located 27&nbsp;km from the municipal seat with a population of about 2,300 people. Its main economic activity is livestock. Tapotzingo is located five km from the municipal seat with a population of about 2,300 people, Its main economic activity is agriculture. Saloya, 1ª Secc is located four km from the municipal seat with a population of about 1,450 people.

==Education==
The municipality has 152 schools and other educational installations which serve about 24,000 students. There are 64 preschools, 67 primary schools, 16 middle schools, five high schools, one vocational education center, thirteen laboratories, twelve workshops, 29 school libraries and four public libraries.<ref name ="encmuc"/> In 2010, the Instituto Tecnológico de Nacajuca was opened in order to decentralized higher education in the state.<ref name ="arranca">{{cite news |title= Arranca en septiembre Instituto Tecnológico de Nacajuca: AGM |url= http://impreso.milenio.com/node/8794142 |newspaper=Milenio |location=Mexico City |date=July 4, 2010 |accessdate=January 18, 2012 |language=Spanish |trans-title=Instituto Tecnológico de Nacajuca starts up in September }}</ref>

==Transportation==
The municipality has 124.7&nbsp;km of paved major roadways including 105.9&nbsp;km of federal and state highways and 18.8&nbsp;km of rural roads. There are forty six vehicular bridges. The main highways include: Federal Highway 187 [[Comalcalco Municipality|Comalcalco]] - Jalpa - Nacajuca, Federal Highway 180 Cunduacán –Jalpa de Méndez – Nacajuca and Federal Highway 180 Santa Cruz – [[Paraíso, Tabasco|Paraíso]] – Jalpa de Méndez – Nacajuca.<ref name ="encmuc"/>

==Economy==
Its main economic activities are livestock and agriculture.<ref name ="encmuc"/> Saloya is noted for its restaurants which serve traditional dishes, especially freshwater fish.<ref name ="laregion">{{cite web |url= http://www.laregion.com.mx/tabasco/guia/municipios/nacajuca.php |title= La Guía de Nacajuca |publisher= La Región newspaper |location=Tabasco, Mexico |language=Spanish |accessdate=January 18, 2012 }}</ref> Mazateupa is located four km from the municipal seat with a population of about 1,700 people. Its main economic activity is the making of handcrafts.<ref name ="encmuc"/>
