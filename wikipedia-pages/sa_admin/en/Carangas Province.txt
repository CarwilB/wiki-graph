'''Carangas''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]].

==Location==
''Carangas'' province is one of sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 59' and 18° 54' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 09' and 67° 45' [[Longitude|West]].

It borders [[San Pedro de Totora Province]] in the northwest, [[Sajama Province]] in the west, [[Litoral Province (Bolivia)|Litoral Province]] in the southwest, [[Sud Carangas Province]] in the southeast, [[Saucarí Province]] in the east, and [[Nor Carangas Province]] in the northeast.

The province extends over 105&nbsp;km from North to South, and 75&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Population==
{{update|section|date=February 2025}}
The main language of the province is [[Aymara language|Aymara]], spoken by 94%, while 84% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 13% [[Quechua language|Quechua]].

The population increased from 7,930 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 10,505 (2001 census), an increase of 32.5%. - 43.1% of the population is younger than 15 years old.{{when|date=February 2025}}

92.5% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 98.1% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]].{{when|date=February 2025}}

79.8% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.1% in mining, 3.5% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 16.6% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

72% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 23% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
