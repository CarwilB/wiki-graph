'''Incahuasi''' is a town and municipality in [[Bolivia]], located in the [[Nor Cinti Province]] in the southwest of the [[Chuquisaca Department]]. The municipality covers an area of 1,598.03 km² and has a population of 16,311 (according to the 2024 INE Census).<ref name="resultados2024"/> The town is 426 km from [[Sucre]], the country's capital, and is connected by road.

The municipality of Incahuasi was created on January 26, 1970, during the government of [[Alfredo Ovando Candia]].<ref name=aniversario>{{cite news|access-date=12 July 2019 |date=26 January 2018 |title=Incahuasi cumple 48 años hoy y se enfoca en caminos y agua |url=https://www.deloscintis.com.bo/actualidad/incahuasi/incahuasi-cumple-48-anos-hoy-y-se-enfoca-en-caminos-y-agua.html |work=DeLosCintis}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
