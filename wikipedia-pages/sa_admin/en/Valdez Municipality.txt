'''Valdez''' is a municipality of [[Sucre State]], [[Venezuela]]. It has four [[Parishes of Venezuela|parishes]], and the municipal seat is in [[Güiria]]. As of 2021, it has a population of 50,862.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones Regionales y Municipales – Municipio Valdez |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/web/mapas/mapas_cargos_a_elegir/documentos/17_ce_sucre_concejales_2021.pdf |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral |access-date=5 February 2024 |pages=19–20 |date=30 November 2021}}</ref>
