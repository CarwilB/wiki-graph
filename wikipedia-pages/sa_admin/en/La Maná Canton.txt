'''La Maná Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]] in the [[Cotopaxi Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[La Maná]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 32,115.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
