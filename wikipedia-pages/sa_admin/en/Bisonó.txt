'''Navarrete''' ('''Municipio de  villa Bisonó''') is a municipality in northwestern [[Dominican Republic]], created in 1961.<ref name="listado" /><ref name="MP-Bisono">{{Cite web|title=Villa Bisonó|publisher=Gobernación Provincial de Santiago|url=http://gobernaciondesantiago.gob.do/los-municipios/villa-bisono/|access-date=2013-01-09|archive-date=2015-05-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150518152130/http://gobernaciondesantiago.gob.do/los-municipios/villa-bisono/|url-status=dead}}</ref> It lies approximately {{convert|25|km|mi}} from [[Santiago de los Caballeros]], Dominican Republic's second largest city.  The administrative centre is in the town of Villa Bisonó.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bisono |first=Ayuntamiento |title=ayuntamientovillabisono.gob.do - Historia |url=https://ayuntamientovillabisono.gob.do/historia/}}</ref>

==Geography==
Bisonó is located in the [[Cibao Valley]] of the [[Yaque del Norte River]].  Before the clearance for agriculture and grazing, the area was forested, being in a subtropical dry forest ecosystem. The northern part of the municipality grades into the foothills of the [[Cordillera Septentrional]].<ref name="MP-Bisono" />

The southern boundary of Bisonó municipality is formed by the Yaque del Norte River. Across the river to the south is the municipal district of La Canela, to the southwest and west is the province of [[Valverde Province|Valverde]], to the north is the municipality of Altamira of [[Puerto Plata Province]], to the southeast is the municipality of [[Villa Gonzalez]] and to the northeast is the municipal district of El Limon.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Santiago: División Territorial|year=2009|publisher=Proyecto de Actualización Y Digitalización Cartográfica Nacional, Departamento de Cartografía, Oficina Nacional de Estadística, Secretaría de Estado de Economía|url=http://www.one.gob.do/mapa_cartografia/documentos.php?f=pdf/santiago/provincia/SANTIAGO.pdf}}{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> 

===Settlements===
The major town is Villa Bisonó, often known as Navarrete. It was formally recognized in 1956 and named after a local land owner José Elías Bisonó. The town is at the junction of highways [[DR-1]] and [[DR-5]]. The town has several neighborhoods (barrios), including Barrio 27 de Febrero, Barrio Los Candelones, Bario Duarte, Bario La Mella, Barrio Nuevo (Jeremias), Barrio Rotonda, Barrio San Miguel, Barrio Trinitaria, and Jalisco.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Villa Bisonó|publisher=Bing Maps|url=http://www.bing.com/maps/default.aspx#JnE9LlZpbGxhJTJiQmlzb25vJTdlc3N0LjAlN2VwZy4xJmJiPTIwLjE3MTAxMTU4NzQ3ODMlN2UtNjguMzcyMjY0ODYyMDYwNiU3ZTE3LjM2Nzk5NDUzMDc0NzQlN2UtNzMuOTUzMzE5NTQ5NTYwNg==}}</ref><ref name="alfabetismo">{{Cite web|at=7939&ndash;7953|title=Poblacion de 3 años y más por alfabetismo, según region, provincia, municipio, distrito municipal, seccion, barrio o paraje |trans-title=Population of 3 years and up for literacy by region, province, municipality, municipal district, sector, neighborhood and locale |publisher=Oficina Nacional de Estadística (ONE), Secretaría de Estado de Economía |url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?module=uploads&func=download&fileId=651 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110521055045/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?module=uploads&func=download&fileId=651 |archivedate=21 May 2011 |format=XLS}}</ref>

Other settlements include Las Atravesadas, Cañada Bonita, Ponton, Pontoncito, El Estacion and Villanueva.<ref name="alfabetismo" />

==History==
In 1939 the area, known as Navarrete, was placed under the rural administration of the municipality of Santiago. In 1956 the settlement of Villa Bisonó, Navarrete, was recognized. In the late 1950s the area was upgraded to a municipal district under the name Navarrete, but still within the Santiago municipality. In 1962 the area was elevated to the category of municipality within the province of Santiago, with the name "Bisonó", although often referred to as "Villa Bisonó" or "Navarrete".<ref name="listado" /><ref name="MP-Bisono" />

==Government==
Outside Villa Bisonó the municipality is divided into seven rural sectors with forty-seven designated locations.  The seven sectors are: Cañada Bonita, Mejía de Navarrete, Villa Heneken or Pontoncito, Villanueva, Estancia del Yaque, La Lomota, and Vuelta Larga.<ref name="MP-Bisono" />

Since 2002, Amantina Gómez, of the Partido de la Liberación Dominicana (PLD), as well as Tito bueno from the partido revolucionario moderno (PRM) has been in charge of the municipal government.<ref name="MP-Bisono" />
