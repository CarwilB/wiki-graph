'''Campos Novos''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], in the [[South Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. It belongs to the Chapecó Intermediate Geographic Region and the Joaçaba-Herval d'Oeste Immediate Geographic Region, situated approximately 370 km west of the state [[Florianópolis|capital]]. The municipality covers an [[area]] of about {{convert|1717|km2|abbr=on}}, with an estimated population of 36,932 inhabitants in 2022, making it the 41st most populous municipality in the state.

The region was first explored in the 18th century, initially inhabited solely by the [[Kaingang]] indigenous people. Established in 1881, Campos Novos experienced significant population growth in the early 20th century due to the arrival of immigrants seeking employment and refugees from the [[Contestado War]]. The municipality's agricultural potential was revealed during this period, propelling it to the forefront of crop production in Santa Catarina, with major crops including [[maize]], [[soybean]], [[bean]], [[wheat]], and [[barley]], while also excelling in [[livestock]] and [[beekeeping]].

Campos Novos boasts several cultural and historical attractions, including the São João Batista Parish Church, the Coronel Gasparino Zorzi Cultural Center, and the Pilgrimage of [[Our Lady of Aparecida]], which attracts an average of 70,000 devotees. The Campos Novos Hydroelectric Plant, constructed in 2006, generates a quarter of Santa Catarina's energy supply.

== Etymology ==
The vast expanse of land and its extensive properties naturally attracted immigrants from [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], and even neighboring landowners from [[Lages]], who sought land for [[cattle ranching]]. Subsequently, the immense fields gave rise to the municipality's name.<ref>{{Harvtxt|Carneiro|2006|p=56}}.</ref>

== History ==
=== Origins ===
[[File:Campos Novos Museu Histórico Municipal equipamentos para animais de montaria e tração.JPG|thumb|left|Equipment for riding and draft animals used by settlers, displayed at the Sebastião Paz de Almeida Historical and Archaeological Museum]]

Like other municipalities in the broader Western Santa Catarina region, the history of Campos Novos has its roots in the earliest settlement experiences of the [[South Region, Brazil|Southern Region of Brazil]]. Before the emergence of settlements in 1650 at [[São Francisco do Sul|São Francisco]], [[Florianópolis|Desterro]], and [[Laguna, Santa Catarina|Laguna]]—efforts led by settlers from São Paulo who ventured by sea in search of new opportunities<ref>{{cite web |url=http://multirio.rio.rj.gov.br/historia/modulo01/rio_da_prata.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120207204546/http://multirio.rio.rj.gov.br/historia/modulo01/rio_da_prata.html |archive-date=February 7, 2012 |title=Toward the Rio da Prata |author=Rio de Janeiro Municipal Education Secretariat |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>—Western Santa Catarina was already experiencing the proximity of [[Spanish people|Spaniards]], who, alongside [[Jesuits]], explored the region stretching from the [[Iguazu River]] to the [[Uruguay River]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://multirio.rio.rj.gov.br/historia/modulo01/band_apres.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110811231830/http://multirio.rio.rj.gov.br/historia/modulo01/band_apres.html |archive-date=August 11, 2011 |title=Captive Bandeiras and Contract Sertanism |author=Rio de Janeiro Municipal Education Secretariat |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Later, in 1663, the [[Bandeirantes|''bandeirante'']] [[António Raposo Tavares|Antônio Raposo Tavares]] traversed these lands and, allied with the Coroado indigenous groups, began aggressively pursuing indigenous settlements, a consequence of the [[Catechesis|catechetical]] endeavors of those priests. Until 1770, however, when they ceased traveling south due to the allure of [[gold]] in the Goiás and Mato Grosso [[Captaincies of Brazil|captaincies]], the settlers from São Paulo had little to exchange for these lands, at least in terms of regional colonization efforts.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web|url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12715&fa=2371|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731135852/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12715&fa=2371|archive-date=July 31, 2013|title=History|author=Municipal Government|access-date=July 21, 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.caestamosnos.org/Brasil_1500/Bloco3.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130622122743/http://www.caestamosnos.org/Brasil_1500/Bloco3.html |archive-date=June 22, 2013 |title=Bandeirantes - Brazil's Great Explorers |author=Carlos Leite Ribeiro |publisher=Cen Portal |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Harvtxt|Ferreira|1959|p=60–61}}.</ref>

The historical narrative of this municipality begins to take shape with the expedition led by Major Atanagildo Martins, guided by Jongong in 1814, aimed at establishing contact with the Missions. Deviating from the planned route due to the fear instilled in his guide by the [[Guarani people|Guarani]] indigenous groups, this expedition reached the [[Campos de Cima da Serra|Vacaria fields]], after traversing the fields where Campos Novos now stands. It is likely, however, that certain landowners from [[Lages]] were already permanently established there by 1839.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /><ref name=":0" />

João Gonçalves de Araújo, a rural landowner in [[Curitibanos]], discovered Campos Novos. Enchanted by the smoke from the fires set by the indigenous people, he organized an expedition and headed toward the Espinilho Ridge. Thus, the first settlers established themselves on the land, soon aided in the occupation efforts by Gaucho fugitives from the [[Ragamuffin War|Farroupilha Revolution]]. Among them, Chico Ferro, Chivida, and Miguel dos Anjos were associated with the initial endeavors that gave rise to this municipality. In 1848, settlers from [[São Paulo Province|São Paulo]] reappeared, conquering the São Jorge fields. Together with newcomers from [[Curitiba]], [[Palmas, Paraná|Palmas]], [[Lages]], [[Guarapuava]], and the [[Pampas|Gaucho fields]] of the Rio Grande do Sul Province, they formed a crucial foundation for the community's organization.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /><ref name=":0" />

The colonization did not begin precisely where the prosperous city of Campos Novos now stands. Initially, it took place about one kilometer from the city, near a stream. Salvador Vieira, moving away from the nascent settlement, built the first house within the perimeter of this thriving city. Shortly afterward, with the settlement already demarcated, Domingos Matos Cordeiro began constructing the São João Batista Parish Church.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /><ref name=":0" />

=== Administrative formation ===
[[File:Campos Novos Prefeitura Municipal.JPG|thumb|left|Current city hall building, which was inaugurated in 1975]]

By Provincial Law No. 377, dated June 16, 1854, the district of Campos Novos, which had existed for some years, was emancipated from the Village of Nossa Senhora dos Prazeres to form a separate parish. Its first authorities were João Fernandes da Caripuna, a native of [[Captaincy of Pernambuco|Pernambuco]], and Domiciano de Azevedo. In 1869, under Law No. 625 of June 11, Campos Novos, along with Palmas and [[Curitibanos]], formed a district of the newly established Curitibanos municipality. Subsequently, by Law No. 923 of March 30, 1881, the Campos Novos district was elevated to the status of a municipality named São João dos Campos Novos; concurrently, the São João Batista parish of Campos Novos was recognized as a village. Its first Intendant was Colonel Manoel Ferreira da Silva Farrapo.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /><ref name=":0" />

Until 1933, the municipality of Campos Novos shared borders with [[Lages]], [[Curitibanos]], Cruzeiro do Sul (now [[Joaçaba]]), [[Porto União]], and the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], covering an area of approximately 15,000 km² at the time. On March 25, 1934, by Decree No. 408, the districts of [[Rio das Antas]] and [[Caçador]] were detached; in 1943, its territory was further reduced with the emancipation of the districts of [[Herval d'Oeste|Herval]], Rio Uruguai, Rio Bonito, and [[Videira|Perdizes]]. Conversely, the districts of Ypira and [[Ouro]] were annexed to its territory, only to be detached again in 1949, along with Piratuba, Ypira, and parts of Tupitinga, [[Capinzal]], and Ouro.<ref name=":0" /> In 1997, Campos Novos lost a portion of its territory to the establishment of the municipality of [[Zortéa]].<ref name="IBGE_Histórico">{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/santacatarina/camposnovos.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731125716/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/santacatarina/camposnovos.pdf |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Campos Novos - History |author=Encyclopedia of Brazilian Municipalities |date=2007 |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Today, there are seven remaining districts besides the seat: Bela Vista, Dal Pai, Espinilho, Encruzilhada, Guarani, Ibicuí, and Leão.<ref name="IBGE_Sinopse">{{cite web|url=http://www.censo2010.ibge.gov.br/sinopseporsetores/?nivel=st |title=Sectoral synopsis |author=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) |date=November 16, 2011 |access-date=July 23, 2012}}</ref><ref name="Prefeitura_Distritos">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=22789&fa=2373 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731140305/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=22789&fa=2373 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Districts |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 23, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== After emancipation ===
[[File:Campos Novos Museu Histórico Municipal armas de fogo.JPG|thumb|left|Firearms used in the [[Contestado War]], displayed at the Sebastião Paz de Almeida Historical and Archaeological Museum]]

In 1893, with an invasion led by Colonel Demétrio Ramos, the municipality's territory felt the repercussions of a civil war. The village was attacked in the early hours of May 19 of that year. Caught off guard, the square's defenders, including local residents, sought refuge with their families in the residence of Colonel Henrique Rupp, which, due to its high-quality construction, provided adequate protection against the invaders. With the defense organized, Lieutenant-Colonel Atanázio de Matos, accompanied by six soldiers, was tasked with retaking the Intendancy, courageously expelling the revolutionaries. A new battle ensued to drive the attackers from their entrenched position, resulting in another victory for the defenders, causing a disorganized retreat of the assault forces. The resistance of Campos Novos led to the death of five brave defenders who fell in the line of duty.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.euachei.com.br/educacao/historia/guerra-do-contestado/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230729194122/https://www.euachei.com.br/educacao/historia/guerra-do-contestado/ |archive-date=July 29, 2023 |title=Contestado War |author=tripod.com |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name=":0" />

[[File:Campos Novos Praça Central.JPG|thumb|right|Lauro Müller Square, in 2011]]
[[File:Igreja Matriz de São João Batista, Campos Novos SC.JPG|thumb|left|São João Batista Parish Church, in 2016]]

At the beginning of the 20th century, the city underwent a period of development with the arrival of immigrants drawn by the prospect of better living conditions in Brazil. Primarily Germans and Italians, they initially contributed to agriculture and the construction of the São Paulo-Rio Grande Railway, and later, after the 1920s, to the establishment of small industries.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /> Poles, Russians, and Lebanese also arrived, along with a significant number of descendants of Germans and Italians, mainly from Rio Grande do Sul, and Caboclos who settled after the end of the [[Contestado War]].<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

Due to the city's demographic growth, infrastructure investments became necessary. The early 20th century saw the arrival of the railway and small industries. In 1922, the first medical clinic was established at Dr. José Athanásio's house, where the Dr. José Athanásio Hospital now operates. On March 29, 1954, the Auxiliadora School was founded, and in 1957, Rádio Cultura de Campos Novos was established. Agriculture was strengthened with the creation of Copercampos on November 8, 1970, which initiated [[cooperativism]]. Today, the city is a major grain producer, particularly of maize, soybean, bean, wheat, and barley, and is notable for its livestock, especially dairy production.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

== Geography ==
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], the municipality's area is {{convert|1659.625|km2|abbr=on}}, with {{convert|4.5211|km2|abbr=on}} constituting the [[urban area]] and the remaining {{convert|1655.1|km2|abbr=on}} forming the [[rural area]], making it the third largest in Santa Catarina, surpassed only by [[Lages]] and [[São Joaquim]].<ref name="Embrapa_SC">{{cite web |url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/sc.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131020015712/http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/sc.html |archive-date=October 20, 2013 |title=Santa Catarina |author=Embrapa Satellite Monitoring |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It is located at 27°24′07″ [[latitude]] south and 51°13′30″ [[longitude]] west, approximately 370 kilometers west of the [[Florianópolis|capital]] of Santa Catarina. It borders [[Erval Velho]], [[Ibiam]], and [[Monte Carlo, Santa Catarina|Monte Carlo]] to the north; [[Barracão, Rio Grande do Sul|Barracão]] ([[Rio Grande do Sul]]), [[Celso Ramos]], and [[Anita Garibaldi, Santa Catarina|Anita Garibaldi]] to the south; [[Vargem, Santa Catarina|Vargem]], [[Brunópolis]], and [[Abdon Batista]] to the east; and [[Capinzal]], [[Zortéa]], [[Ouro]], [[Lacerdópolis]], and [[Herval d'Oeste]] to the west.<ref name="Prefeitura_Chegar">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=24337&fa=2372 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731131635/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=24337&fa=2372 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=How to get there? |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 21, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Geomorphology and hydrography ===
[[File:CamposNovos DamFilledUp.jpg|thumb|left|Reservoir at the Campos Novos Hydroelectric Plant]]

The city's terrain is predominantly gently undulating, deep, and well-drained, offering favorable physical conditions for root development. It is minimally susceptible to [[erosion]] and supports the use of agricultural machinery and implements. In some areas of the municipal territory, there are stronger undulations, which are more prone to erosion and pose greater challenges to agricultural mechanization, particularly where the soil type is [[Cambisol]], containing stones. Although much of the municipality's area has terrain suitable for agriculture, Campos Novos' soils are acidic, often contaminated with exchangeable aluminum, and have limited nutrient reserves. However, when properly managed, they become suitable for both annual crops and less intensive uses such as fruit cultivation, pasture, and reforestation.<ref name="Prefeitura_Solo">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12788&fa=2372&PHPSESSID=qpuv7ie6psthmtss53m3v0rsp1 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731134154/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12788&fa=2372&PHPSESSID=qpuv7ie6psthmtss53m3v0rsp1 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Soil |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city is part of the Lages Plateau hydrographic region and the [[Canoas River (Santa Catarina)|Canoas River]] [[hydrographic basin]], one of the main basins in Santa Catarina. The Canoas River flows through Campos Novos and eleven other municipalities, and upon merging with the [[Pelotas River]], it forms the [[Uruguay River]].<ref name="Prefeitura_Hidrografia">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12789&fa=2372&PHPSESSID=n76temj9ms6nt0d0u3956qfpa4 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731134454/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12789&fa=2372&PHPSESSID=n76temj9ms6nt0d0u3956qfpa4 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Hydrography |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The Campos Novos Hydroelectric Plant, operational since 2007, generates a quarter of Santa Catarina's energy consumption.<ref name="Enercan_UHE">{{cite web |url=http://www.enercan.com.br/site/interno.php?it=1&conteudo=usina&sub=0 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120730201059/http://www.enercan.com.br/site/interno.php?it=1&conteudo=usina&sub=0 |archive-date=July 30, 2012 |title=Plant |author=Campos Novos Energia (Enercan) |date=2011 |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Climate ===
[[File:Campos Novos Agricultura.JPG|thumb|right|Crops in Campos Novos on a sunny day]]

According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], Campos Novos' climate is classified as [[Humid subtropical climate|humid subtropical]] (type ''Cfa'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen system]]),<ref name="vetmedunivienna">{{cite web |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120718194422/http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-date=July 18, 2012 |title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |author=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> characterized by high [[relative humidity]] (RH) and an average annual [[temperature]] of around {{convert|17|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, with mild summers and cold winters.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/62twu0PtQ |archive-date=November 2, 2011 |title=Climate |author=Portal Brasil |date=January 6, 2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130620052525/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-date=June 20, 2013 |title=Brazil - Climates |author=IBGE Library |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="Prefeitura_Clima">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12787&fa=2372 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731135922/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12787&fa=2372 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Climate |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> With over {{convert|2200|hours|abbr=on}} of annual [[insolation]], the area receives approximately {{convert|2100|mm|in|abbr=on}} of rainfall per year, evenly distributed throughout the year, without a distinct [[dry season]].<ref name="NCB_1981-2010">{{cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/portal/index.php?r=clima/normaisclimatologicas|title=Brazil Climatological Normals|publisher=National Institute of Meteorology|access-date=May 14, 2018|archive-date=September 22, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190922133747/http://www.inmet.gov.br/portal/index.php?r=clima%2Fnormaisclimatologicas|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In recent years, however, hot and dry days during [[Indian summer|Indian summers]] have become increasingly frequent, often exceeding {{convert|28|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, particularly in summer. During these dry periods, [[wildfires]] in rural areas are common, contributing to [[deforestation]] and the release of pollutants into the [[atmosphere]], further degrading [[air quality]]. These fires are often intentional, used to prepare land for cultivation, but they are recurrently prohibited.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.enercan.com.br/site/exibe_noticia.php?id=367 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110916034926/http://www.enercan.com.br/site/exibe_noticia.php?id=367 |archive-date=September 16, 2011 |title=Enercan advises on wildfires |author=Campos Novos Energia (Enercan) |date=February 1, 2011 |access-date=June 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Frost]]s are not uncommon, occurring on average 12 to 22 times per year.<ref name="Prefeitura_Clima"/> While [[snow]] is less frequent, official records note occurrences on July 12, 2000<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.engeplus.com.br/conteudo.php?int=noticia&codigo_not=24251 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924000805/http://www.engeplus.com.br/conteudo.php?int=noticia&codigo_not=24251 |archive-date=September 24, 2015 |title=Snow falls in ten Santa Catarina cities |author=Ariadne Niero |date=August 4, 2010 |publisher=Engeplus |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and, with significant intensity, on July 22, 2013.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sma.fundacaoabc.org.br/download/file/L3Zhci93d3cvX2ZpbGVzL3NtYS9tb25pdG9yYW1lbnRvL2JvbGV0aW5zL2lubWV0Ly8yMDEzL0JPTE0wNzIwMTMucGRm |title=Monthly agroclimatological bulletin - July 2013 |author=[[National Institute of Meteorology]] (Inmet) |date=August 1, 2013 |access-date=September 12, 2013 |archive-date=February 23, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140223000339/http://sma.fundacaoabc.org.br/download/file/L3Zhci93d3cvX2ZpbGVzL3NtYS9tb25pdG9yYW1lbnRvL2JvbGV0aW5zL2lubWV0Ly8yMDEzL0JPTE0wNzIwMTMucGRm |url-status=dead }}</ref>{{Weather box

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Campos Novos Araucária.JPG|thumb|left|[[Araucaria angustifolia|Brazilian pines]] in Campos Novos]]

The original and predominant vegetation in the municipality is the pine forest or [[Araucaria Forest|Araucaria forest]], although parts of the native forest have been replaced by agriculture, cattle pastures, or cleared and later [[reforestation|reforested]]. Until the 1980s, the timber industry was a primary source of income for Campos Novos.<ref name="Prefeitura_Solo"/> The construction of the Campos Novos Hydroelectric Plant required the flooding of several native forest areas and surrounding farms along the Canoas River.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.midiaindependente.org/pt/blue/2005/03/310646.shtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111214092432/http://midiaindependente.org/pt/blue/2005/03/310646.shtml |archive-date=December 14, 2011 |title=The problems of the Campos Novos Dam |author=CNI Brasil |date=March 19, 2005 |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

To combat deforestation and the destruction of green areas, the municipal government has implemented programs such as Permanent Preservation Areas, which are vegetated strips between the hydroelectric plant's reservoir and surrounding properties, ranging from 30 to 100 meters wide. Deforested areas used for the plant's construction are being restored and converted into [[riparian forest]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=2366&fa=1&cd=129521 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731132221/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=2366&fa=1&cd=129521 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Mayor participates in the launch of the APP Conservation Program |author=Municipal Government |date=March 2, 2012 |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Additionally, the Rio Canoas State Park, established on May 27, 2004, spans {{convert|1200|ha|abbr=on}} and is dedicated to preserving the native forest near the hydroelectric plant.<ref name="referencia_fatma">{{cite web |url=http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=83&Itemid=162 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121025053950/http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=83&Itemid=162 |archive-date=October 25, 2012 |title=Rio Canoas State Park |author=Environmental Foundation (FATMA) |access-date=July 24, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Some remnants of the [[Araucaria moist forests]] remain today,<ref name="referencia_fatma"/> along with fields formed by layers of [[grasses]], interspersed with shrubby or arboreal species, and scattered in [[Gallery forest|gallery forests]] or [[copses]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.oca.net.br/index.php/mata-atlantica/ecossistemas-catarinenses |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120729080909/http://www.oca.net.br/index.php/mata-atlantica/ecossistemas-catarinenses |archive-date=July 29, 2012 |title=Santa Catarina Ecosystems |author=Santa Catarina Environment Observatory |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipal area has recorded 44 species of orchids and bromeliads.<ref>{{cite journal|author=José Geuster Cleiton|title=Distribution of orchids and bromeliads in western Santa Catarina, southern Brazil|journal=Biota Amazônia|url=http://periodicos.unifap.br/index.php/biota/article/view/1116/pdf_190|author2=Favretto, Mario Arthur|year=2014|volume=4|issue=4|pages=52–59}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|first1=Mario Arthur|last1=Favretto|title=Orchids and bromeliads of the Rio do Peixe Valley, Santa Catarina, Brazil|year=2011|isbn=978-85-915509-2-0|first2=Geuster|last2=Cleiton José|url=https://pt.scribd.com/doc/188135400/Orquideas-e-Bromelias-do-Vale-do-Rio-do-Peixe-Santa-Catarina|pages=146|archive-date=2016-03-03|access-date=2025-06-25|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303190533/https://pt.scribd.com/doc/188135400/Orquideas-e-Bromelias-do-Vale-do-Rio-do-Peixe-Santa-Catarina|url-status=dead}}</ref> Regarding fauna, over 80 bird species have been recorded in the municipal area,<ref>{{cite journal|last=Favretto|first=Mario Arthur|title=Ornithological observations in western Santa Catarina - part I|journal=Atualidades Ornitológicas|url=https://pt.scribd.com/doc/188750413/Observacoes-ornitologicas-no-oeste-de-Santa-Catarina-parte-I|author2=Geuster, Cleiton José|issue=143|pages=49–43|year=2008}}</ref><ref>{{cite journal|last=Favretto|first=Mario Arthur|title=Ornithological observations in western Santa Catarina - part II|journal=Atualidades Ornitológicas|url=https://pt.scribd.com/doc/188751158/Observacoes-ornitologicas-no-oeste-de-Santa-Catarina-parte-II|author2=Geuster, Cleiton José|year=2008|issue=144}}</ref> Regarding [[herpetofauna]], recorded species include the Amazon lava lizard (''[[Tropidurus torquatus]]''), striped worm lizard (''[[Ophiodes striatus]]''), Argentine giant tegu (''[[Tupinambis merianae]]''), worm lizard (''[[Amphisbaenia|Amphisbaena sp.]]''), Wagler's snake (''[[Xenodon merremii|Waglerophis merremii]]''), Neuwied's false fer-de-lance (''[[Xenodon neuwiedii]]''), coral snake (''[[Micrurus altirostris]]''), military ground snake (''[[Liophis miliaris]]''), jararaca (''[[Bothrops jararaca]]''), and Brazilian keelback (''[[Helicops infrataeniatus]]'').<ref name="referencia_viarural">{{cite web |url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-rio-canoas/default.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120504011141/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-rio-canoas/default.htm |archive-date=May 4, 2012 |title=Via Rural: Rio Canoas State Park |author=Via Rural |access-date=July 24, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2010, the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) counted the municipality's population at {{formatnum:32829}} inhabitants, ranking it as the 40th most populous in the state and the second most populous in the Curitibanos Microregion (behind only [[Curitibanos]]), with a [[population density]] of 19.9 inhabitants per km².<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_santa_catarina.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101206221757/http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_santa_catarina.pdf |archive-date=December 6, 2010 |title=2010 Population Census |author=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) |date=November 29, 2010 |access-date=December 11, 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to the 2010 census, {{formatnum:16257}} inhabitants were men and {{formatnum:16572}} were women. The same census reported that {{formatnum:27065}} inhabitants lived in the [[urban area]] and {{formatnum:5764}} in the [[rural area]].<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010" /> Statistics released in 2013 estimated the municipal population at {{formatnum:34386}} inhabitants.

The Municipal [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Campos Novos is considered high by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP), with a value of 0.742 (the 719th highest in Brazil). The city has most indicators close to the national average according to the UNDP. The education index is 0.658, the longevity index is 0.861, and the income index is 0.721.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/>

=== Poverty and inequality ===
[[File:Amostra da população de Campos Novos SC.jpg|thumb|left|Sample of the population during a street event]]

According to the IBGE, in 2003, the [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], was 0.40, with 1.00 being the worst and 0.00 the best.<ref name="GINI" /> That year, the poverty incidence, as measured by the IBGE, was 34.36%, with a lower limit of 23.29%, an upper limit of 45.44%, and a subjective poverty incidence of 22.35%.<ref name="GINI">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Brazilian municipalities' social indicators |author=Cities@ - IBGE |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref>

From 1991 to 2010, the proportion of people with a per capita household income of up to half a minimum wage decreased by 68.0%. In 2010, 80.7% of the population lived above the poverty line, 12.1% were at the poverty line, and 7.1% were below it.<ref name="ODM_1">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69M3CosyT |archive-date=July 22, 2012 |title=1 - End hunger and poverty |author=ODM Portal |date=2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref> In 2000, the richest 20% of the city's population accounted for 61.9% of the total municipal income, 28 times higher than the 2.2% share of the poorest 20%. In 1991, the poorest 20% had a 3.2% share, indicating an increase in social inequality from the early 1990s to 2000.<ref name="ODM_1"/> Also in 2000, according to the municipal government, there were no records of [[slum|slums]] or [[stilt house|stilt houses]], only instances of illegal lots. Currently, there is specific municipal legislation addressing land regularization, but no dedicated plan or program for land regularization.<ref name="ODM_7">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/7-qualidade-de-vida-e-respeito-ao-meio-ambiente/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69M3E2mVB |archive-date=July 22, 2012 |title=7 - Quality of life and environmental respect |author=ODM Portal |date=2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
Reflecting Campos Novos' cultural diversity, the city is home to a variety of religious practices. While it developed on a predominantly Catholic social foundation due to colonization and immigration—and the majority of people in Campos Novos still identify as Catholic—today, dozens of different Protestant denominations, as well as [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritism]] and [[Spiritualism (movement)|Spiritualism]], can be found. In recent decades, Buddhism and Eastern religions have grown in the city. There are also notable Jewish, Mormon, and Afro-Brazilian religious communities. According to the 2000 IBGE census, the population of Campos Novos consists of: [[Roman Catholicism|Catholics]] (85.70%), [[Protestantism|Evangelicals]] (12.09%), [[Irreligion|people with no religion]] (0.98%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (0.52%), and others distributed among various religions (0.71%).<ref name="SIDRA_Religião">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=2094&z=cd&o=11&i=P |title=Resident population by color or race and religion |author=Sidra (IBGE Automatic Data Retrieval System) |date=2000 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref>

==== Roman Catholic Church ====
[[File:Campos Novos Igreja Matriz.JPG|thumb|right|São João Batista Parish Church in Campos Novos]]

According to the [[Catholic Church]]'s division, the municipality is part of the [[Diocese of Joaçaba]], established on June 12, 1975, after being separated from the dioceses of [[Diocese of Lages|Lages]], [[Diocese of Chapecó|Chapecó]], and [[Diocese of Caçador|Caçador]]. It is subordinate to the [[Archdiocese of Florianópolis]] and encompasses thirty other municipalities. The diocese's creation was driven by the rapid population growth in the region in the early 20th century, which fostered the development of Catholicism.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.catedraljoacaba.com.br/diocese.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111005030555/http://www.catedraljoacaba.com.br/diocese.php |archive-date=October 5, 2011 |title=Diocese |author=Diocese of Joaçaba |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city's main religious landmarks are the São João Batista Parish Church and the Our Lady of Aparecida Sanctuary. The parish church, located in the city center opposite Lauro Müller Square, is notable for its interior adorned with wooden sculptures, stained glass, and paintings.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1467 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731131136/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1467 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=São João Batista Parish Church |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The sanctuary is renowned for the Pilgrimage of [[Our Lady of Aparecida]], held annually on October 12, the saint's day, attracting an average of 70,000 devotees.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1466 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731134844/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1466 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Our Lady of Aparecida Sanctuary |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Conducted since 1977, the pilgrimage covers three kilometers from the parish church to the sanctuary.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www1.an.com.br/1999/out/13/0ger.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121102122016/http://www1.an.com.br/1999/out/13/0ger.htm |archive-date=November 2, 2012 |title=Campos Novos procession gathers 50,000 |author=A Notícia |date=October 13, 1999 |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==== Protestant churches ====
Despite the Catholic majority, the city is home to a wide range of Protestant or Reformed denominations, including the Sara Nossa Terra Evangelical Community, Maranatha Christian Church, [[Lutheran Church]], [[Presbyterian Church]], [[Methodist Church]], [[Anglican Episcopal Church]], [[Baptist church|Baptist churches]], [[Assembleias de Deus|Assemblies of God]], [[Seventh-day Adventist Church]], [[World Church of God's Power]], [[Universal Church of the Kingdom of God]], [[Christian Congregation in Brazil]], among others.<ref name="SIDRA_Religião"/> According to the 2000 IBGE data, 12.09% of the population were Protestant, with 8.72% belonging to Pentecostal evangelical churches, 1.68% to mission evangelical churches, and 1.69% to other evangelical religions.<ref name="SIDRA_Religião"/>

There are also Christians from various other denominations, such as [[Jehovah's Witnesses]] (0.03% of inhabitants) and members of [[The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints]] (0.12%).<ref name="SIDRA_Religião"/>

=== Ethnicities ===
[[File:Campos Novos Casarão antigo.JPG|thumb|left|Historic mansion in the city built in the Dutch architectural style]]

According to IBGE census data from 2010, the population of Campos Novos consisted of 24,178 white people (73.66%), 802 Black people (2.44%), 220 Asian people (0.67%), 7,561 multiracial people (23.03%), and 63 Indigenous people (0.19%).<ref name="SIDRA_Raça">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=3175&z=cd&o=5&i=P |title=Resident population by color or race, household situation, sex, and age |author=Sidra (IBGE Automatic Data Retrieval System) |date=2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref> According to IBGE data, there were 25 immigrants from other parts of Santa Catarina and Brazil in 2010.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=11&i=P&c=3172 |title=Table 3172 - International emigrants by sex, according to Major Regions and Federation Units of previous residence |author=Sidra (IBGE Automatic Data Retrieval System) |date=2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref> Conversely, 25 people left Campos Novos for other countries, with four going to [[Italy]] (16.0%), four to [[Angola]] (16.0%), and three to [[Canada]] (12.0%).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=11&i=P&c=3173 |title=Table 3173 - International emigrants by sex, according to continents and foreign destination countries |author=Sidra (IBGE Automatic Data Retrieval System) |date=2010 |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref>

Immigration was more common in the early 20th century, contributing to agriculture and industrial development after 1930. Many immigrants sought employment, particularly in farming, and bolstered commerce. Campos Novos, like parts of Southern and Southeastern Brazil, received waves of immigrants from various regions, notably Italians, Spaniards, Germans, Dutch, Poles, Russians, and Lebanese. Additionally, people from other Santa Catarina cities, Paraná, São Paulo, and Northeastern Brazil relocated to the region, fleeing the [[Contestado War]].<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

== Politics and administration ==
[[File:Campos Novos Câmara de Vereadores.jpg|thumb|right|Campos Novos Municipal Chamber, seat of the [[legislative branch]]]]

Municipal administration is carried out by the [[executive branch]] and the [[legislative branch]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130212201150/http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074 |archive-date=February 12, 2013 |title=The Municipal Public Authority leading the constitutional obligation to create the internal control system |author=Flávio Henrique M. Lima |date=February 8, 2006 |publisher=JusVi |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The first to govern the municipality was Colonel Manoel Ferreira da Silva Farrapo, who took office immediately after the city's emancipation.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /> The current mayor is Silvio Alexandre Zancanaro, from the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD), who won the [[2016 Brazilian municipal elections]] with {{formatnum:11826}} votes (57.70% of voters).<ref name="Terra_Prefeito">{{cite web |author= |date=October 7, 2016 |title=Zancanaro 55 |url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/zancanaro/ |access-date=February 20, 2017 |website=Elections 2016 |publisher=}}</ref>

The [[legislative branch]] consists of the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of nine councilors elected for four-year terms (as stipulated in Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]]<ref name="Constituição">{{cite web |url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130111082108/http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |archive-date=January 11, 2013 |author=DJI |title=Federal Constitution - CF - 1988 |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>), with five seats held by the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement|Brazilian Democratic Movement Party]] (PMDB), three by the [[Progressistas|Progressive Party]] (PP), and one by the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]] (PT).<ref name="vereadores">{{cite web |author= |date=October 7, 2016 |title=Candidates for Councilor Campos Novos/SC |url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/candidatos-vereador-campos-novos-sc/ |access-date=February 20, 2017 |website=Elections 2016 |publisher=}}</ref> The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for administration and the executive branch, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).

The city is governed by an organic law, promulgated and effective from March 30, 1990,<ref name="Orgânica">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=16482&fa=3&cd=6061 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731141228/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=16482&fa=3&cd=6061 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Municipal Organic Law |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and serves as the seat of the Campos Novos Judicial District.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tj.sc.gov.br/mutirao/conciliadores/camposnovos.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731125035/http://www.tj.sc.gov.br/mutirao/conciliadores/camposnovos.html |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Conciliators of the Campos Novos Comarca |author=Santa Catarina Judiciary |access-date=July 22, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In June 2012, the municipality had {{formatnum:25049}} voters, representing 0.529% of Santa Catarina's total.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.gov.br/eleicoes/estatisticas-do-eleitorado/quantitativo-do-eleitorado/consulta-quantitativo |title=Quantitative Consultation |date=August 15, 2011 |author=[[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE) |access-date=July 22, 2012}}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
[[File:Campos Novos Centro.JPG|thumb|left|Downtown Campos Novos]]

Campos Novos is divided into eight districts: the Seat, Bela Vista, Dal Pai, Espinilho, Encruzilhada, Guarani, Ibicuí, and Leão. The Seat was the most populous, with {{formatnum:23359}} inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE_Sinopse"/> The 20th century saw the creation and elevation of several municipal districts to city status, with the last territorial changes occurring on December 29, 1995, with the emancipation of the [[Zortéa]] district under state law No. 10051,<ref name="IBGE_Histórico"/> and on April 3, 2000, with the creation of the Encruzilhada district under Law No. 2590.<ref name="Prefeitura_Distritos"/>

According to the municipal government, the city is further divided into nine neighborhoods (Centro, Jardim Bela Vista, Ernesto Zortea, Nossa Senhora Aparecida, Nossa Senhora de Lourdes, São Sebastião, Santo Antônio, Senhor Bom Jesus, and Boa Vista); six [[social housing]] complexes (Colina das Flores, Eldorado, Integração, Morada do Sol, Nova Zelândia, and Pedacinho do Céu); and fifteen rural communities, five in the Encruzilhada district (Santo Antônio da Palmeira, Entrada do Pelotas, Colônia Pinhal, Nossa Senhora de Lourdes, and Faé) and ten in Guarani (Santa Bárbara, Pinhal Preto, São José, Caxambu, Linha Durigon, Santa Lúcia, Monte Verde, Assentamento 30 de Outubro, Colônia Hipólito, and Linha Martinelli).<ref name="Prefeitura_Bairros">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=22788&fa=2373 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731134346/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=22788&fa=2373 |archive-date=July 31, 2013 |title=Neighborhoods |author=Municipal Government |access-date=July 23, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Economy ==
The [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) of Campos Novos is the largest in the Curitibanos Microregion and the 464th in Brazil.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> According to 2011 IBGE data, the municipal GDP was R$ {{formatnum:910300|thousands}}, with {{formatnum:102147|thousands}} from net product taxes minus subsidies at current prices, and the [[GDP per capita]] was R$ {{formatnum:27523.96}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web|url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=420360&idtema=125&search=santa-catarina |title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities |author=Cities@ - IBGE |date=2011 |access-date=February 7, 2014}}</ref>

In 2010, according to the IBGE, the city had {{formatnum:1153}} local units and {{formatnum:1106}} active businesses and commercial establishments. {{formatnum:7477}} workers were classified as total employed personnel, with {{formatnum:6452}} as salaried employees. [[Wages]] and other remunerations totaled {{formatnum:105427|thousands}} [[Brazilian real|reais]], with an average monthly salary of 2.4 [[minimum wages]] across the municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=empresas&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Central Business Register Statistics |author=Cities@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=July 23, 2012}}</ref> Agriculture has always been a primary income source, with commerce and industry gaining prominence since the 1930s. Easy access via highways to major ports on the Santa Catarina coast and various Brazilian and [[Mercosur]] cities facilitates agricultural exports.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

Agriculture is the least significant sector of Campos Novos' economy. Of the city's total GDP, {{formatnum:195019}} thousand [[Brazilian real|reais]] represents the gross added value of agriculture and livestock.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> According to IBGE data from 2010, the municipality had approximately {{formatnum:53457}} cattle, {{formatnum:1721}} horses, 180 buffaloes, {{formatnum:145290}} pigs, {{formatnum:1123}} goats, and {{formatnum:9700}} sheep. There were {{formatnum:1979033}} poultry, of which {{formatnum:1978619}} were roosters, hens, chickens, and chicks, and 414 thousand were laying hens, producing {{formatnum:7215}} thousand dozen eggs. Additionally, {{formatnum:5860}} cows were milked, yielding {{formatnum:21085}} thousand liters of milk. Four thousand sheep were sheared, producing a total of {{formatnum:9500}} kilograms of wool. The municipality also produced 31 thousand kilograms of honey.<ref name="Agropecuária">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2010&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Pecuária 2010 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>
[[File:Avicultura em Santa Catarina.JPG|thumb|left|Poultry farming in Campos Novos]]

In temporary farming, the main crops are [[corn]] ({{formatnum:140400}} tons produced and 18,000 hectares cultivated), [[soybean]] (132,000 tons produced and 40,000 hectares planted), and [[wheat]] ({{formatnum:30800}} tons produced and 11,000 hectares cultivated).<ref name="IBGE_Lavoura">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2010&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Lavoura Temporária 2010 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> In permanent farming, notable crops include [[yerba mate]] (240 tons produced and 60 hectares harvested), oranges (200 tons produced and 20 hectares harvested), and grapes (108 tons produced and 27 hectares harvested).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavperm2010&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Lavoura Permanente 2010 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

The city is regarded as the "Santa Catarina granary," being one of the state's leading producers of corn, soybean, beans, wheat, and barley. Agriculture, alongside commerce, is the primary source of income for the municipal economy. Several agricultural cooperatives operate in the city, including Copercampos, established on 8 November 1970, Cooperativa Camponovense (Coocam), founded in 1993, and Apicampos, which supports the [[beekeeping]] sector, another prominent activity in Campos Novos. The development of agriculture in the city was significantly influenced by the emergence of these cooperatives starting in the 1970s, which provided substantial support to farmers and ranchers.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

[[File:Campos Novos Cooperativa.JPG|thumb|right|Copercampos headquarters]]
;'''Secondary sector'''
The industry sector, in 2011, was the second most significant contributor to the municipal economy. {{formatnum:198024}} thousand reais of the municipal GDP came from the gross added value of industry ([[Secondary sector of the economy|secondary sector]]).<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> Industrial development began to take shape during the 1930s and 1940s, gaining momentum with the arrival of Italian and German descendants. Today, the city aims to industrialize the large volume of raw materials produced within its territory, excelling in areas such as metallurgy, wood processing, clothing manufacturing, and paper production.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

The municipality has a designated area for industrial construction and investment, located along the [[BR-470 (Brazil highway)|BR-470]] highway, with 12 lots of up to 5,000 m². At EXPOCAMPOS, held biennially in May, the city showcases some of its primary achievements in industry, commerce, agribusiness, and craftsmanship.<ref name="Prefeitura_Indústria">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=14494&fa=11200 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731133857/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=14494&fa=11200 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Indústria |author=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

[[File:Campos Novos Agência da Caixa Econômica Federal 05.05.2011.JPG|thumb|left|[[Caixa Econômica Federal]] branch]]
;'''Tertiary sector'''
The provision of [[Tertiary sector of the economy|services]] contributes {{formatnum:415110}} thousand reais to the municipal GDP, making it the largest source of Campos Novos' GDP.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> Commerce in the city began to develop and become more significant in the municipal economy during the first half of the 20th century, driven by the growing presence of Italian and German descendants, as was the case with the industrial sector.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

Campos Novos is currently considered the main commercial hub of the Association of Municipalities of the Southern Plateau of Santa Catarina (AMPLASC), an organization that supports the commercial sector of the municipality and its region. Other entities, such as the Campos Novos Chamber of Retail Managers (CDL) and the Campos Novos Commercial and Industrial Association (ACIRCAN), have also contributed to commercial development by supporting small and medium-sized businesses. According to statistics from the Secretariat of Industry and Commerce, a municipal entity that coordinates commercial and industrial activities, the secondary and tertiary sectors together accounted for approximately 700,000 establishments in Campos Novos.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

=== Healthcare ===
[[File:Campos Novos Hospital 05.05.2011.JPG|thumb|right|Dr. José Athanásio Hospital]]
In 2009, the municipality had 21 healthcare facilities, including hospitals, [[emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[dentistry|dental services]], with 13 being public and seven private. These facilities provided 171 hospital beds, with 95 in public institutions and 76 in private ones.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Serviços de Saúde 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> In 2011, 99.3% of children under one year old had up-to-date vaccination records.<ref name="ODM_Perfil"/> In 2010, 459 births were recorded,<ref name="ODM_Perfil">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhDrQoc |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=Perfil municipal |author=Portal ODM |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> with an infant mortality rate of zero per thousand children under one year old.<ref name="ODM_4">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhEwrgO |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=4 - reduzir a mortalidade infantil |author=Portal ODM |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> Additionally, 100% of live births were attended by healthcare professionals.<ref name="ODM_5">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/5-melhorar-a-saude-das-gestantes/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhFsvDX |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=5 - melhorar a saúde das gestantes |author=Portal ODM |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> In the same year, 20.3% of pregnant women were under 20 years old.<ref name="ODM_5"/> A total of {{formatnum:5558}} children were weighed by the [[Programa Saúde da Família|Family Health Program]], with 0.4% found to be malnourished.<ref name="ODM_1"/>

The Municipal Health Secretariat, directly linked to the municipal government, is responsible for maintaining and operating the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS) and developing policies, programs, and projects to promote municipal health.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12831&fa=2385&PHPSESSID=2hdid3m1nm820ut04kn79uqq67 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731131641/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12831&fa=2385&PHPSESSID=2hdid3m1nm820ut04kn79uqq67 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Secretaria Municipal de Saúde |author=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Among the basic support and care services are the Family Health Program (PSF), Paraná Medical Assistance (PAM), and the Mobile Emergency Care Service (SAMU).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12934&fa=2389 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090627211753/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12934&fa=2389 |archive-date=27 June 2009 |title=Projetos e Realizações -> Saúde |author=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The Dr. José Athanásio Hospital, with 89 beds (79 under SUS), is managed by the state.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cnes.datasus.gov.br/cabecalho_reduzido.asp?VCod_Unidade=4203602379767 |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhOtqte |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=Identificação |author=DATASUS |date=22 July 2012 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

=== Education ===
[[File:Campos Novos Biblioteca Pública Municipal.JPG|thumb|left|Municipal Public Library]]
The [[Basic Education Development Index]] (IDEB) average for public schools in Campos Novos in 2009 was 4.1 (on a scale from 1 to 10), with 5th-grade students scoring 4.1 and 9th-grade students scoring 3.7; the national average for municipal and state schools was 4.0. In private institutions, the municipal index rises to 6.2 (6.4 for 5th-grade students and 5.9 for 9th-grade students).<ref name="ODM_2">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/2-educacao-basica-de-qualidade-para-todos/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhQ9G9W |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=2 - educação básica de qualidade para todos |author=Portal ODM |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

In 2009, the municipality had approximately {{formatnum:7008}} enrollments in public and private schools.<ref name="IBGE_Educação"/> According to IBGE, in the same year, of the 34 elementary schools, six were state-run, 25 were municipal, and three were private. Of the 68 secondary schools, 32 were state-run, three were municipal, and 33 were private.<ref name="IBGE_Educação"/> In 2000, 14.1% of children aged 7 to 14 were not attending elementary school. The completion rate for youths aged 15 to 17 in that year was 67.2%. The literacy rate for the population aged 15 or older in 2010 was 98.7%.<ref name="ODM_2"/> In 2006, for every 100 girls in elementary school, there were 112 boys.<ref name="ODM_2(2)">{{cite web|url=http://www.portalodm.com.br/relatorios/3-igualdade-entre-sexos-e-valorizacao-da-mulher/sc/campos-novos |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhRB22z |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=3 - igualdade entre os sexos e valorização da mulher |author=Portal ODM |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

The Municipal Secretariat of Education and Culture aims to coordinate and provide administrative and pedagogical support to the Campos Novos school system.<ref name="Prefeitura_Educação">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12830&fa=2385 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731130835/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12830&fa=2385 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Secretaria Municipal de Educação e Cultura |author=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Examples of programs coordinated by the Secretariat include the Education for Youth and Adults (EJA), a free education network for adults who have not completed elementary school; the Special Education network, where students with physical disabilities are taught by specialized teachers; and the National School Library Program.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12938&fa=2389 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090627211426/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12938&fa=2389 |archive-date=27 June 2009 |title=Educação |author=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Public safety and crime ===
[[File:Campos Novos Delegacia da PMSC.JPG|thumb|right|PMSC station]]
Public safety in Campos Novos is provided by various organizations. The Municipality maintains a [[Municipal Guards|Municipal Guard]], tasked with protecting municipal assets, services, and facilities, and collaborating with municipal inspection agencies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Campos%20Novos&state=sc |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121209012458/http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Campos%20Novos&state=sc |archive-date=9 December 2012 |title=Lei Orgânica do município de Campos Novos |author=Leis Municipais |date=21 November 2000 |access-date=9 December 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The Municipal Civil Defense Council (Comdec), established by Ordinance Law 2,373 on November 24, 1997, is responsible for preventive, assistance, recovery, and rescue operations in situations involving public risk. It supports the Fire Department, which focuses on rescue operations and emergencies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=2366&fa=1&cd=33832 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731132605/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=2366&fa=1&cd=33832 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Corpo de Bombeiros e Defesa Civil palestram sobre como agir em situações de desastres naturais |author=Solange Nohatto |date=4 March 2009 |publisher=Prefeitura |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The Military Police is a state force responsible for policing, patrolling banks and the environment, and overseeing prisons, schools, and special events. The force also carries out social integration activities.<ref name="Sargento">{{cite web|url=http://www.sargentosoares.com.br/noticia/1179/Deputado_discute_seguran%C3%A7a_p%C3%BAblica_em_Campos_Novos.html |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhWXM6q |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=Deputado discute segurança pública em Campos Novos |author=Sargento Soares |date=20 April 2011 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> The [[Civil Police (Brazil)|Civil Police]] focuses on investigating and addressing crimes and infractions.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pc.mg.gov.br/internas/servicos/iServicos.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120507162025/http://www.pc.mg.gov.br/internas/servicos/iServicos.php |archive-date=7 May 2012 |title=Serviços |author=Polícia Civil de Minas Gerais |date=2007 |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Various efforts have been undertaken by state and municipal authorities to enhance city safety, such as increasing police personnel<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.jornalpauta.com.br/new.php?id_materia=12868 |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NhYE1g5 |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=PM terá 40 novos soldados na região do meio-oeste |author=Jornal Pauta |date=22 August 2011 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> and holding public safety meetings and lectures.<ref name="Sargento"/>

=== Housing, services, and communications ===
[[File:Campos Novos SAMAE.JPG|thumb|left|Headquarters of the Municipal Autonomous Water and Sewage Service (SAMAE)]]
In 2010, according to IBGE, the city had {{formatnum:10526}} permanent private [[housing unit|households]], including {{formatnum:9719}} [[house]]s, 786 [[apartment]]s, 13 houses in villages or condominiums, and eight rooms or shanties. Of the total households, {{formatnum:7485}} were owned properties, with {{formatnum:7058}} fully paid, 427 under acquisition, and {{formatnum:1820}} [[Renting|rented]]; {{formatnum:1206}} properties were provided, with 542 by employers and 664 through other means. Fifteen were occupied in other ways. Most of the municipality has access to treated water, [[electricity]], [[sewage]], [[municipal solid waste|urban cleaning]], [[landline telephone|fixed telephony]], and [[mobile telephony]]. In that year, 83.89% of households were served by the [[Water supply network|general water supply network]]; 85.25% had some form of waste collection, and 59.14% had sewage disposal through the general sewer or stormwater network.<ref name="IBGE_DomicídiosPop">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=result_univer_censo2010&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Censo Demográfico 2010: Características da População e dos Domicílios: Resultados do Universo |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

The first water supply system in Campos Novos was established through funding from the [[Inter-American Development Bank]] (IDB) and the [[National Health Foundation]] (FUNASA), which initially managed it. On 8 August 1966, the Municipal Autonomous Water and Sewage Service (SAMAE) was created to support the system. In 1999, FUNASA ceased operations, and the administration of the service was transferred to the municipality. Today, the city has a water treatment plant (ETA) located in the Ibicuí district, also managed by SAMAE.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.classificadosmercosul.com.br/samae/historico.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150602161415/http://www.classificadosmercosul.com.br/samae/historico.htm |archive-date=2 June 2015 |title=O SAMAE |author=Serviço Autônomo Municipal de Água e Esgoto (SAMAE) |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
[[File:Campos Novos Subestação Eletrobrás.JPG|thumb|right|[[Eletrobras]] substation]]

The [[Celesc|Santa Catarina Electric Power Company]] (CELESC) is responsible for electricity supply in Campos Novos and several other municipalities in the state.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://novoportal.celesc.com.br/portal/index.php/noticias/819-horario-de-verao-termina-no-domingo-dia-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150602161334/http://novoportal.celesc.com.br/portal/index.php/noticias/819-horario-de-verao-termina-no-domingo-dia-26 |archive-date=2 June 2015 |title=Horário de verão termina no domingo, dia 26 |author=Centrais Elétricas de Santa Catarina (CELESC) |date=23 February 2012 |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2010, 99.8% of households in the municipality were served by the electricity service.<ref name="IBGE_DomicídiosPop"/> The city's electricity is generated by the Campos Novos Hydroelectric Power Plant, managed by Campos Novos Energia S.A., which also supplies 25% of Santa Catarina's energy needs.<ref name="Enercan_UHE"/> Internet services, including dial-up and [[broadband]] ([[Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line|ADSL]]), are provided by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|ISPs]]. Mobile phone services are offered by several operators, and some areas have [[wireless communication|wireless]] internet access.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.abusar.org.br/prov_wireless.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120712230219/http://www.abusar.org.br/prov_wireless.html |archive-date=12 July 2012 |title=Provedores de Todo o Brasil |author=Associação Brasileira dos Usuários de Acesso Rápido |access-date=23 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The area code ([[direct distance dialing|DDD]]) for Campos Novos is 049,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://dddcidade.com.br/campos%20novos/ |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69NsoUkMj |archive-date=23 July 2012 |title=DDD |author=DDD Cidade |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref> and the [[postal code]] ([[Código de Endereçamento Postal|CEP]]) is 89620-000.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correios.com.br/ |title=CEP de cidades brasileiras |author=[[Correios]] |access-date=23 July 2012}}</ref>

Several television channels are available in the [[Very High Frequency|VHF]] and [[Ultra High Frequency|UHF]] bands, along with two newspapers. The first [[radio broadcasting|radio station]] in the city, Rádio Cultura de Campos Novos, was founded in 1957 to meet the growing population's need for information and cultural access.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

=== Transportation ===
[[File:Campos Novos Terminal Rodoviário.JPG|thumb|left|Campos Novos Bus Terminal]]
The municipality was once served by railways. A railway station was located in what is now the Leão district, inaugurated on 23 February 1926 as part of the São Paulo-Rio Grande Railway. It operated until 1997, when the railway was abandoned by its operator, the [[RFFSA|Federal Railway Network]] (RFFSA), and was later demolished.<ref name="Estações_Leão">{{cite web |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/pr-tronco/leao.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120727161716/http://estacoesferroviarias.com.br/pr-tronco/leao.htm |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Leão |author=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil |date=29 August 2010 |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The decline of railways was due to the expansion of highways and airports. By land, the municipality is situated at the intersection of the [[BR-282 (Brazil highway)|BR-282]], [[BR-470 (Brazil highway)|BR-470]], SC-455, SC-458, and SC-456 highways, connecting it to nearby towns, the state capital, major Brazilian cities, and other [[Mercosur]] cities.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /> Campos Novos has a [[bus terminal]] located in the city center, primarily connecting to various cities in Santa Catarina and Rio Grande do Sul.<ref name="Cacador_Campos">{{cite web |url=http://www.cacador.com.br/conttur/campos.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121106211357/http://www.cacador.com.br/conttur/campos.htm |archive-date=6 November 2012 |title=Campos Novos - Tradição e Cultura |author=Caçador.com |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city also has a public transportation system linking the downtown area to neighborhoods and districts with bus stops established by the municipality, regulated by Law No. 2364 of 9 October 2001.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/961920/lei-2364-01-campos-novos-0 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121209013652/http://www.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/961920/lei-2364-01-campos-novos-0 |archive-date=9 December 2012 |title=Lei nº 2364 de 09 de outubro de 2001 do Campos Novos |author=JusBrasil |date=9 October 2001 |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Several airports operate near Campos Novos, such as the [[Joaçaba Airport]] {{airport codes|JCB|SSJA}}, located in [[Joaçaba]], approximately 50 km from the city center.<ref name="Cacador_Campos"/>

In 2010, the municipal vehicle fleet consisted of {{formatnum:14873}} vehicles, including {{formatnum:9380}} cars, 877 trucks, 241 tractor-trucks, {{formatnum:1529}} pickups, 324 vans, 59 minibuses, {{formatnum:1442}} motorcycles, 356 mopeds, 92 buses, two wheel tractors, 52 utility vehicles, and 519 other types of vehicles.<ref name="IBGE_Frota">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=frota&codmun=420360&nomemun=Campos%20Novos |title=Frota 2010 |publisher=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=24 July 2012}}</ref> Dual carriageways, paved roads, and numerous traffic lights facilitate traffic flow, but the increase in vehicles over the past decade has led to increasingly slow traffic, particularly in the municipal seat. Additionally, finding parking spaces in the commercial center has become challenging, causing some losses to local businesses.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.clmais.com.br/informacao/38325/r%C3%B3tula-e-congestionamento-na-br-282 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160311095109/http://clmais.com.br/informacao/38325/r%c3%b3tula-e-congestionamento-na-br-282 |archive-date=11 March 2016 |title=Rótula e congestionamento na BR-282 |author=Thomas Michel |date=9 July 2012 |publisher=CL News |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Culture ==
The Municipal Secretariat of Education and Culture oversees the cultural sector in Campos Novos, aiming not only to support education but also to plan and implement cultural policies through programs, projects, and activities that foster cultural development.<ref name="Prefeitura_Educação"/>

=== Performing arts and events ===
[[File:Campos Novos Museu Histórico Municipal.JPG|thumb|right|Sebastião Paz de Almeida Historical and Archaeological Museum]]
The Cid Caesar de Almeida Pedroso Cultural Foundation, established by Law No. 2,050 of 17 March 1994, is a cultural institution linked to the culture secretariat, housed in the former city hall building constructed in 1919, which served as the seat of the executive and legislative branches until 1975. The building was transformed into the Coronel Gasparino Zorzi House of Culture, serving as a venue for various cultural activities such as music, visual arts, dance, theater, workshops, and events. The Foundation also manages the Dr. Waldemar Rupp Historical Archive, the municipal public historical archive, and the Sebastião Paz de Almeida Historical and Archaeological Museum.<ref name="Prefeitura_CasaCultura">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1465 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731130617/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1465 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Fundação Cultural Camponvense Cid Caesar de Almeida Pedroso |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

To promote local socioeconomic development, the culture secretariat, often in collaboration with other local institutions and companies, has increased investment in festivals and events. Notable annual events include the Copercampos Field Day, showcasing Copercampos’ work in February or March;<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/evento/detalhe/codEvento/36 === Attractions ===
[[File:Campos Novos Casa do Artesanato.jpg|thumb|left|House of Handicrafts]]
The city offers several inns, farms, and hotels in rural areas, focusing on [[ecotourism]]. The Cervo and Santa Mônica Farms and the Triunfo Farmstead offer hiking trails, camping areas, horseback riding, and fishing facilities.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1488 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731130552/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1488 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Fazenda do Cervo |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1495 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731125324/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1495 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Fazenda Santa Mônica |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1485 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731130928/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1485 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Granja Triunfo |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Visitors can also schedule a tour of the Campos Novos Hydroelectric Power Plant.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1482 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731130209/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/1482 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Usina Hidrelétrica de Campos Novos |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In the Barra do Leão district, the Leonense Hot Springs offer sulfurous [[hot springs]] with an average temperature of 33°C, featuring camping areas and indoor pools. These waters are believed to aid in treating rheumatism, arthritis, stomach issues, skin conditions, fatigue, and insomnia.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/11071 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731124123/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/11071 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Termas Leonense |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In the urban area, the city boasts several attractions of historical and cultural significance. Notable sites include the São João Batista Parish Church, located opposite Lauro Müller Square, known for its interior adorned with wooden sculptures, stained glass, and paintings; the Our Lady of Aparecida Sanctuary;<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/Atrativos/20/Turismo%20Religioso |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731141338/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/Atrativos/20/Turismo%20Religioso |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Turismo Religioso |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and the Coronel Gasparino Zorzi House of Culture, which is housed in a building that served as the city hall, inaugurated in 1919 and designated as a municipal historical heritage site.<ref name="Prefeitura_CasaCultura"/>

=== Sports ===
[[File:Campeonato de carrinhos de rolimã em Campos Novos SC.jpg|thumb|right|Street [[soapbox car]] championship held in 2010]]
The Secretariat of Sports and Leisure regularly organizes various sporting events, such as the Amateur [[Association football|Soccer]] Championship, the Centenary Cup, the Quadrangular de Bolão Tournament, the [[Bocce]] Tournament, the [[Volleyball]] Tournament, and the Rádio Cultura Futsal Cup. The ACAMB (Campos Novos Basketball Association) collaborates with the municipality to organize events such as the Regional Soccer Championship, Regional and State [[Karate]] Championships, the State [[Judo]] Championship, and the Athletics Championship. The secretariat also maintains sports schools for children, with approximately 900 participants, and organizes the Campos Novos School Games (JECAM), involving around {{formatnum:1500}} students from municipal, state, and private schools annually.<ref name="Prefeitura_Esporte">{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12937&fa=2389 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090628171110/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12937&fa=2389 |archive-date=28 June 2009 |title=Secretaria de Esporte e Lazer |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city’s main soccer team is Clube Atlético Camponovense, founded on 3 August 1991, which plays its matches at the Cid Pedroso Stadium, with a capacity of approximately 4,000 people.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.golaco.kit.net/main223.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303181113/http://www.golaco.kit.net/main223.html |archive-date=3 March 2016 |title=Camponovense |author=Golaço |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Other sports facilities include the Osni Jacomel Gymnasium, the Humberto Calgaro and Juvelino Fernandes Gymnasiums, some of which feature skate parks, tennis courts, street basketball courts, and beach volleyball courts.<ref name="Prefeitura_Esporte"/>

=== Holidays ===
Campos Novos observes three municipal [[public holiday|holidays]], as defined by the Municipal Organic Law, along with eight national holidays and additional optional holidays. The municipal holidays are the city’s emancipation day on 30 March; the day of the patron saint [[John the Baptist]] on 24 June; and the day of [[Our Lady of Aparecida]] on 12 October.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12950&fa=2375 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731124844/http://www.camposnovos.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=12950&fa=2375 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Feriados |author=Prefeitura |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to federal law No. 9,093, enacted on 12 September 1995, municipalities may have up to four religious municipal holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120626150006/http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |archive-date=26 June 2012 |title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado? folga automática pode gerar alteração contratual |author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |publisher=Guia Trabalhista |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaLei">{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120829143920/http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |archive-date=29 August 2012 |title=Lei n.º 9.093, de 12 de setembro de 1995 |author=Presidência da República |access-date=24 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
