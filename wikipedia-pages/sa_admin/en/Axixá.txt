'''Axixá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Maranhão]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]]. The main town lies on the left bank of the [[Munim River]], upstream from [[Icatu]].<ref>{{Google maps | url = https://www.google.com/maps/place/2%C2%B050'13.0%22S+44%C2%B003'10.0%22W/@-2.820181,-44.0465809,16909m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x0!8m2!3d-2.836944!4d-44.052778?hl=en | access-date =9 February 2017}}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1,535,310|ha|adj=on}} [[Upaon-Açu/Miritiba/Alto Preguiças Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1992.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Upaon-Açu/Miritiba/Alto Preguiças – ISA}}|language=pt
