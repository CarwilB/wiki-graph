'''Tlaxcoapan''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 79.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tlaxcoapan, Hidalgo (Mehiko) Postal Code Listahan - Cybo |url=https://postal-code.cybo.com/mehiko/tlaxcoapan/ |access-date=2024-10-06 |website=postal-code.cybo.com |language=tl}}</ref>

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 26,758.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tlaxcoapan  |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13074a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref> It is now part of [[Tula de Allende]] built-up (or metro) area.

==Toponymy==

The word ''Tlanalapa'' comes from the [[Nahuatl]] ''Tachtli'' 'ball game', ''atl'' 'water' and ''pan'' 'on'; so its meaning is "On or in the river of the ball game".<ref group=note>The etymological origin of the name of a municipality can have</ref><ref name="etym">{{cite book|author=Centro Estatal de Estudios Municipales del Estado de Hidalgo|date=1988|publisher=Secretaría de Gobernación|series=Colección Enciclopedia de los municipios de México|title=Los Municipios de Hidalgo}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref name=gob>{{cite web|access-date=22 March 2017|publisher=Gobierno del estado de Hidalgo|title=Tlaxcoapan|url=http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/tlaxcoapan|work=Municipios de Hidalgo}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Another meaning could be that it decomposes into ''Tlachco'' (from ''tlachtli'' 'ball game' and the locative ''-co'' 'en') 'in the ball game' or literally 'ball game court'; ''atl'' 'water', and the locative suffix ''-pan'' 'over'; so<ref>{{cite web|title=Gran Diccionario del Náhuatl - Tlachcho|url=http://www.gdn.unam.mx/diccionario/tlachco/66566}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
