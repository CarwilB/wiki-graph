'''Uruoca''' is a [[municipality]] of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Ceará]]. Its estimated population is 13,915 inhabitants. It is part of the microregion of Coreaú, which is one of the seven microregions that make up the mesoregion of Noroeste Cearense.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1592550|ha}} [[Serra da Ibiapaba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Serra da Ibiapaba – ISA}}
