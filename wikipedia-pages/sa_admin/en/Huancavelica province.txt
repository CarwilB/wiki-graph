'''Huancavelica''' is one of seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] located in the [[Huancavelica Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Huancavelica]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': province of [[Tayacaja Province|Tayacaja]]
*'''East''': provinces of [[Acobamba Province|Acobamba]], [[Churcampa Province|Churcampa]] and [[Angaraes Province|Angaraes]]
*'''South''': provinces of [[Huaytará Province|Huaytará]] and [[Castrovirreyna Province|Castrovirreyna]]
*'''West''': [[Lima Region]] and [[Junín Region]]

== Geography ==
There are a couple of large lakes in the province like [[Anqasqucha (Huancavelica)|Anqasqucha]], Astuqucha, [[Chiliqucha]], Chunchuqucha, [[Kanllaqucha]], [[Milluqucha]], [[Papaqucha (Huancavelica)|Papaqucha]], [[Qiwllaqucha (Huancavelica)|Qiwllaqucha]], [[Tipiqucha (Huancavelica)|Tipiqucha]], [[Warmiqucha]] and [[Ñawinqucha (Huancavelica)|Ñawinqucha]] some of which belong to the largest lakes of Peru.

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (55.06%) learnt to speak in childhood, 44.70% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
