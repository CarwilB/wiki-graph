'''Engenheiro Beltrão ''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. 
It is about {{convert|64|km}} southwest of the city of [[Maringá]]. It was founded in 1954
It has an area of {{convert|467.470|km2|sqmi}}.
It has a [[Humid subtropical climate]].
As of 2020 the estimated population was 13,981.

==Geography==

Engenheiro Beltrão is in the state of Paraná, Brazil.{{sfn|Geonames}} It has an area of {{convert|467.470|km2}} as of 2018.{{sfn|IBGE}} 
The elevation above sea level is about {{convert|520|m}}.
The municipality is on the left bank of the [[Ivaí River]], in the central region of Paraná, to the northwest.
It {{convert|55|km}} from [[Maringá]], {{convert|450|km}} from [[Curitiba]] and {{convert|350|km}} from [[Foz do Iguaçu]].{{sfn|IBGEhis}}

==History==

Colonization of the region began in the 1930s, when peasants accompanied by their families moved into the fertile lands, which they bought at low prices and with easy payment terms, and cleared the virgin forest to make way for agriculture.{{sfn|Prefeitura de Engenheiro Beltrão}}
The Sociedade Técnica e Colonizadora Engenheiro Beltrão Ltda acquired a huge area of land before 1950 in the east of the municipality of [[Peabiru]] and the northeast of [[Campo Mourão]], between the rivers [[Rio Mourão|Mourão]] and Ivaí.
The company cleared, colonized and developed coffee plantations in the region, which would become the present municipality of Engenheiro Beltrão.
The land was divided by the highway built from [[Campo Mourão]] to [[Maringá]].
The company founded the town of Engenheiro Beltrão on that road, named after the company's director Francisco Gutierrez Beltrão.{{sfn|IBGEhis}}

On 14 November 1951 the land was made the district of Engenheiro Beltrão within the municipality of Campo Mourão.
On 14 December 1951 the municipality of Peabiru was split off from Campo Mourão, including the district of Engenheiro Beltrão.
On 26 November 1954 Engenheiro Beltrão was split from Peabiru as a separate municipality.
The first elections of the municipal executive were held on 3 October 1954.
Various other territorial splits and additions were made since then.
As of 14 May 2001 the municipality had 6 districts: Engenheiro Beltrão, Figueira do Oeste, Ivailândia, Sertãozinho, Sussuí and Triângulo.{{sfn|IBGEhis}}

==Demographics==
The population in the 2010 census was 13,906.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
The estimated population as of 2019 was 14,000.{{sfn|IBGE}}
Population density as of 2010 was {{convert|2975|PD/km2|PD/sqmi}}.{{sfn|IBGE}}
As of 2010, 97.8% of the population had attended school between the ages of 6 and 14. 
Also as of 2010, the municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.730.
This compares to 0.489 in 1991 and 0.650 in 2000.{{sfn|IBGE}}

On the 2010 census religion was reported as [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] by 11,211 people, [[Evangelicalism|Evangelical]] by 2,220 people and [[Animism]] by 20 people.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

==Economy==

Agriculture is still the main economic activity in the region.{{sfn|Dados Estatísticos}}
The land is very fertile, and produces a wide variety of crops, including grains and sugarcane.
There is extensive chicken farming.{{sfn|Engenheiro Beltrão : Governo}}
Industries include clothing production, which is growing, and the alcohol industry.{{sfn|Dados Estatísticos}}
In 2017, the average monthly salary was 1.9 minimum wages. 
Employed people were 17.8% of the  total population. 
Households with monthly income of up to half a minimum wage per person represent 30.2% of the population.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
