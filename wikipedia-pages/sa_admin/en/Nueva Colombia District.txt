'''Nueva Colombia''' is a ''[[Districts of Paraguay|distrito]]'' in the [[Cordillera Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|80.02|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 4,602 individuals. It was officially established on 23 June 1955.

==History==
Nueva Colombia was formerly part of the [[Altos District]].<ref name="UHNC">{{cite web|title=El apacible Rincón Cordillerano se va modernizando|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/el-apacible-rincon-cordillerano-se-va-modernizando-n2980701|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=The peaceful Cordilleran corner is becoming modernized}}</ref><ref name="UH">{{cite web|title=Nueva Colombia, joven distrito en proceso de modernización|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/nueva-colombia-joven-distrito-proceso-modernizacion-n2906116|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Nueva Colombia, young district in modernization process}}</ref> An Economic and Administrative Board was established in 1919, with some autonomous powers to govern the settlement.<ref name="ABC">{{cite web|title=Nueva Colombia, ciudad pujante y en crecimiento|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/interior/nueva-colombia-ciudad-pujante-y-en-crecimiento-199537.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Nueva Colombia, growing and thriving city}}</ref> It was formed as a colony in 1922 and was established as a district by Law No. 260 on 23 June 1955.<ref name="UHNC"/><ref name="ABC"/><ref name="ABCNC">{{cite web|title=Después de tres años vuelven a realizar desfile estudiantil por aniversario de Nueva Colombia|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2023/06/23/despues-de-tres-anos-vuelven-a-realizar-desfile-estudiantil-por-aniversario-de-nueva-colombia/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Student parade returns for Nueva Colombia anniversary after three years}}</ref>

The name which means "New Colombia", was given as a sign of its relationship with Colombia. The streets are often lined with Paraguayan and Colombian flags.<ref name="UHNC"/><ref name="ABC"/>

==Geography==
Nueva Colombia is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Cordillera Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|881|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/cordillera/0313__nueva_colombia/|title=Nueva Colombia District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|43|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of [[Asuncion]].<ref name="NC">{{cite web|title=Turismo y producción apuntalan el crecimiento de Nueva Colombia|work=La Nación|url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios_edicion_impresa/2025/08/17/turismo-y-produccion-apuntalan-el-crecimiento-de-nueva-colombia/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Tourism and production support growth of Nueva Colombia}}</ref> The district is divided into seven companies. It borders [[Loma Grande District|Loma Grande]] and Altos to the east, [[Emboscada, Paraguay|Emboscada]] and [[San Bernardino, Paraguay|San Bernardino]] to the west, [[Arroyos]] to the south.<ref name="UHNC"/>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|121.81|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|27.74|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|16.21|cm}} and has 142.32 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/cordillera/nueva-colombia|title=Nueva Colombia, Weather|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
As per the 2022 census, Nueva Colombia district had a population of 4,602 inhabitants of which 2,392 were males and 2,210 were females. About 73.5% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (26.5%) lived in urban areas. About 22.8% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 11.6% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>
