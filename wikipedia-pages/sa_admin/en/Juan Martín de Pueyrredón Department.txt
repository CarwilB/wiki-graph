'''Juan Martín de Pueyrredón''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of the [[San Luis Province|province of San Luis]], [[Argentina]]. Its [[capital city|headtown]] is the city of [[San Luis, Argentina|San Luis]], which is also the largest and capital city of the province of San Luis.

With an area of {{convert|13120|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with [[Belgrano Department, San Luis|Belgrano Department]], to the east with [[Coronel Pringles Department, San Luis|Coronel Pringles]], to the south with [[Gobernador Dupuy Department, San Luis|Gobernador Dupuy]], and to the west with [[Mendoza Province]].

Until 2010, as per Law #V-0106-2004 (5490)<ref>[http://www.diputadossanluis.gov.ar/CD_Digesto/Leyes/L-V-0106-2004.htm Ley Nº V-0106-2004 (5490) División política departamental de San Luis] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080502184739/http://www.diputadossanluis.gov.ar/CD_Digesto/Leyes/L-V-0106-2004.htm |date=2008-05-02 }}</ref> its name was '''La Capital Department''' ({{langx|es|'''Departamento La Capital'''}}) but according to law #V-0748-2010, the name has been changed to '''Juan Martín de Pueyrredón'''.<ref>[http://www.diputados.sanluis.gov.ar/diputadosasp/paginas/NormaDetalle.asp?NormaID=812 Ley Nº V-0748-2010]</ref>
