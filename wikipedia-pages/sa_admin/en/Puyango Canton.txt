'''Puyango''' is a canton of the [[Loja Province|Province of Loja]], [[Ecuador]]. It is located in the west of the province, and borders the cantons of [[Paltas Canton|Paltas]], [[Celica Canton|Celica]], [[Pindal Canton|Pindal]], and [[Zapotillo Canton|Zapotillo]].
