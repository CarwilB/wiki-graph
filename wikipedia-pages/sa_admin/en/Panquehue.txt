'''Panquehue''' ({{IPA|es|paŋˈkewe}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[San Felipe de Aconcagua Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]].

==Geography==
Panquehue spans an area of {{convert|121.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Panquehue spans an area of {{convert|121.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 6,567 inhabitants (3,312 men and 3,255 women). Of these, 2,904 (44.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,663 (55.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 11.3% (667 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Panquehue is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]], administered by a communal council (''consejo comunal''), which is headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. The current alcalde is Luis Reinaldo Pradenas Moran ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]). The communal council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Patricio Zedán Zárate (UDI)
* Héctor Bazáes Leiva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* René Ahumada Ortega ([[Christian Democratic Party|DC]])
* Marcelo Olguín Moreno ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Panquehue is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marco Antonio Núñez ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Gaspar Rivas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 11th electoral district, together with [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]], [[San Esteban, Chile|San Esteban]], [[Calle Larga]], [[Rinconada]], [[San Felipe, Chile|San Felipe]], [[Putaendo]], [[Santa María, Chile|Santa María]], [[Llaillay]] and [[Catemu]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Ignacio Walker|Ignacio Walker Prieto]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Lily Pérez San Martín]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).{{Update after|2021|9|5}}
