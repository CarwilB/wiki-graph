'''Eloxochitlán de Flores Magón''', formerly known as '''San Antonio Eloxochitlán''', is a city and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Oaxaca]]. 
It is part of the [[Teotitlán District, Oaxaca|Teotitlán District]] in the north of the [[Cañada, Oaxaca|Cañada Region]]. Approximately 96% of the inhabitants of the municipality speak the indigenous [[Tecóatl Mazatec]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sil.org/americas/mexico/popoloca/mazateca-eloxochitlan/00i-MazatecoEloxochitlan-maa.htm |title=The Mazatec language of Eloxochitlán de Flores Magón (Maa) |website=www.sil.org |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120503085223/http://www.sil.org/americas/mexico/popoloca/mazateca-eloxochitlan/00i-MazatecoEloxochitlan-maa.htm |archive-date=2012-05-03}} </ref>

The municipality encompasses approximately 28&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and, as of 2005, had a population of 4,042.

==The town==

Franciscans arrived in about 1827 and founded the town, the first in Mazatec territory to be built by outsiders.<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |title=Eloxochitlán de Flores Magón |encyclopedia=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=State of Oaxaca / [[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |year=2005 |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20029a.htm |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20060428063649/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20029a.htm |archivedate=2006-04-28 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city is best known as the birthplace of the [[Mexican Revolution]]ary journalist and activist [[Ricardo Flores Magón]], after whom the municipality was renamed.

==The municipality==

As municipal seat Eloxochitlán has governing authority over the following communities:

Agua Ancha, Agua Colorada, Agua de Cuchara (Agua de Cucharón), Agua de Cueva, Agua de Máscara, Agua Escalera, Agua Iglesia, Agua Torcida, Barrio la Escopeta, Barrio Moctezuma, Camote Español, Cerro de Plaza, Colonia las Flores, Corral, El Cimiento, El Maguey, La Raya San Pedro, La Tranca, Peña Colorada, Plan de Cebolla (Cebollín), Puente de Fierro, Puerto Rosete, Rincón de las Flores (Rincón Brujo), and San José Buenavista
