==Location==
Sabana Llana Norte is located east of [[Oriente, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Oriente]] [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]], north of the municipality of [[Trujillo Alto]] and west of the municipality of [[Carolina, Puerto Rico|Carolina]]. It is bordered by the [[Laguna San José (Puerto Rico)|San José Lagoon]] on top, its closest body of water.
