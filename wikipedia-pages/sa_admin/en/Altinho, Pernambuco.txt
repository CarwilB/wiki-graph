'''Altinho''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[awˈtĩj̃u]}}) is a municipality/city in the state of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population in [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]], according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 20,674 inhabitants and the total area is 450.17&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Caruaru]] and [[São Caetano, Pernambuco|São Caetano]]   ('''N''');  [[Cupira, Pernambuco|Cupira]], [[Panelas]] and [[Ibirajuba]]   ('''S''');  [[Agrestina]]    ('''E''');   [[Cachoeirinha, Pernambuco|Cachoeirinha]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 452.52&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 454 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] and [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]]
* Clima  -  [[semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 23.1 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 163&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Altinho''' are related with commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creations of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[pigs]] and [[chickens]].
