'''Irecê''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Bahia]], Brazil. {{as of|2020}} it had a population of 73,524 people. {{convert|476|km}} by road northwest of [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]]. Located in the northern region of Chapada Diamantina.  It is an agricultural town known for growing [[Black turtle bean|black beans]] and being a wine-producing region.<ref name="Mitchell1981">{{cite book|last=Mitchell|first=Simon|title=The Logic of Poverty: The Case of the Brazilian Northeast|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=aa89AAAAIAAJ&pg=PA24|access-date=12 June 2013|date=1 January 1981|publisher=Routledge|isbn=978-0-7100-0637-0|page=24}}</ref>

==History==
Irecê was discovered on 21 February 1807, and became municipality on 2 August 1926.

==Climate==
Irecê is classified as [[Semi-arid climate|hot semi-arid climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: ''BSh''), the average temperature on summer is 30°C, on winter is warm, the temperature reaches to 16°C.

== Notable landmarks ==
It is the home of the "Academia Ireceense de Letras e Artes." It was also the site the [[Universal Church of the Kingdom of God]] chose for an industrial [[kibbutz]].<ref name="MeyerMoors2005">{{cite book|last1=Meyer|first1=Birgit|last2=Moors|first2=Annelies|title=Religion, Media, and the Public Sphere|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=bU7eHrQyHsUC&pg=PA65|access-date=12 June 2013|date=1 December 2005|publisher=Indiana University Press|isbn=978-0-253-11172-2|page=65}}</ref>
