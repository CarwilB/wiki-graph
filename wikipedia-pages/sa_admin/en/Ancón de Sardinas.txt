'''Ancón de Sardinas''' ('Sardinas Bight') is a [[Bight (geography)|bight]] in the [[Pacific Ocean]], located on the border between [[Colombia]], which is to the north ([[Nariño Department]]), and [[Ecuador]], which is to the south ([[Esmeraldas Province]]).<ref>[https://archive.today/20120630122902/http://tierra.meteored.com/tierra_Bahia+de+Ancon+de+Sardinas_3689918_CO_esp.html Información de Bahía de Ancón de Sardinas]</ref> In this place begins the maritime delimitation of the [[Colombia–Ecuador border|border between Colombia and Ecuador]], which consists of a single treaty signed on August 23, 1975<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.armada.mil.co/index.php?idcategoria=17903 |title=Convenio sobre Delimitación de Áreas Marinas y Submarinas y Cooperación Marítima entre las Repúblicas de Colombia y del Ecuador |author=Armada Nacional de Colombia |accessdate=17 April 2017 |archive-date=14 November 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111114144740/http://www.armada.mil.co/index.php?idcategoria=17903 |url-status=dead }} ([http://www.law.fsu.edu/library/collection/LimitsinSeas/maps/ls69small.html ver mapa] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131110060657/http://www.law.fsu.edu/library/collection/LimitsinSeas/maps/ls69small.html |date=2013-11-10 }})</ref> and, from the intersection of the extension of the land border and the confluence of the territorial areas of both countries, a midpoint is marked in the bay; From here to the [[Mataje River]], which flows into it, a line is drawn that terminates the boundary.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.eluniverso.com/2012/06/13/1/1355/ecuador-colombia-fijan-punto-limite-maritimo.html |title=Ecuador y Colombia ratifican punto de límite marítimo |publisher=Periódico El Universo |accessdate=19 October 2011}}</ref>
