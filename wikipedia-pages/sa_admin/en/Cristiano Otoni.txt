'''Cristiano Otoni''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] [[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Conselheiro Lafaiete]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 4,719.<ref name="IBGE" />
