'''Cañete''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chile]], located in the [[Arauco Province]] of the [[Biobío Region]]. It is located 135&nbsp;km to the south of [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]].<ref>[http://mapasdechile.com/distancia.htm Mapas de Chile] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101220105319/http://mapasdechile.com/distancia.htm |date=20 December 2010 }} - Distances between cities</ref> Cañete is known as a "Historic City" ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''ciudad histórica'')<ref>{{cite news|title=Alcalde de Lebu molesto con el gobierno por falta de compromiso con la capital provincial|url=http://www.araucotv.cl/noticias/alcalde-de-lebu-molesto-con-el-gobierno-por-falta-de-compromiso-con-la-capital-provincial/|accessdate=10 May 2016|agency=Arauco TV|date=1 August 2014|language=Spanish}}</ref> as it is one of the oldest cities in the country. The [[Battle of Tucapel]] and [[Pedro de Valdivia]]'s death happened near the city's current location. Cañete was also an important location in the [[Arauco War]].

== History ==

=== Etymology ===

Cañete was founded under the name of "Cañete de la Frontera" in 1558. The city was established by Governor [[García Hurtado de Mendoza, 5th Marquis of Cañete|García Hurtado de Mendoza]] and named in honor of his father, [[Andrés Hurtado de Mendoza, 3rd Marquis of Cañete]]. The marquis' title originated from the Castile town [[Cañete, Cuenca|Cañete]].

=== Fort Tucapel ===
The area was known as Tucapel, meaning ''taking by force'' in [[Mapudungun]]. In October 1553, [[Pedro de Valdivia]] founded the [[Fort Tucapel]], next to the present city of Cañete. Two months later, native [[Mapuche]]s destroyed the fort. Valdivia returned on 25 December 1553, to rebuild the fort, but the [[Spain|Spanish]] army was once again defeated in the [[Battle of Tucapel]], an early battle in the [[Arauco War]]. Valdivia was captured and killed.

=== ''Cañete de la Frontera'' ===

In January 1558, Governor [[García Hurtado de Mendoza, 5th Marquis of Cañete|García Hurtado de Mendoza]] founded the city as ''Cañete de la Frontera'' three kilometers to the east of the present location of the city. Hurtado de Mendoza named [[Alonso de Reinoso]] as [[corregidor (position)|corregidor]] of Cañete. The Mapuches, led by [[Caupolican]], besieged the city, but they were defeated in the [[Caupolican#The Battle of Cañete Fort|battle of the Fort of Cañete]].

Subsequent military defeats forced governor [[Francisco de Villagra]] to abandon the city in January 1563.  Three years later, a new city was started under the orders of [[Rodrigo de Quiroga]] but it did not prosper either due to constant attacks by the Mapuches.

=== Refounding ===

The present city was founded on 12 November 1868 by colonel [[Cornelio Saavedra Rodríguez]] as part of the [[Occupation of the Araucanía|pacification of Araucanía]].

=== 21st century ===

On 12 November 2006 a major car accident occurred near Canete when a bus with 28 people fell into the River Tucapel and 19 passengers died. 17 of the victims were soldiers who were members of the band Reinforced Regiment No. 7 "Chacabuco" Concepción. The group was traveling to a celebration for the anniversary of the founding of Cañete. The press titled the accident "''tragedy of Cañete''," like the [[tragedy of Antuco]]. [[President of Chile|President]] [[Michelle Bachelet]], the [[Ministry of National Defense (Chile)|minister of National Defense]] [[Vivianne Blanlot]] and [[List of commanders-in-chief of the Chilean Army|Commander-in-Chief]] [[Óscar Izurieta]] traveled to [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]] for the funerals.

The [[2010 Chile earthquake|2010 Chilean earthquake]] caused major damage to the city. The Kallvu Llanka hospital, which was being constructed, was severely impacted, delaying its opening from 8 October 2010 till 22 October 2013.<ref>{{cite web|title=Inauguración Hospital Kallvu Llanka|url=http://www.cañete.cl/leenota.php?noti=1548|archive-url=https://archive.today/20160527013232/http://www.xn--caete-pta.cl/leenota.php?noti=1548|url-status=dead|archive-date=27 May 2016|website=cañete.cl|publisher=Municipalidad de Cañete|accessdate=30 May 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=Presidente Piñera inauguró Hospital Intercultural de Cañete|url=http://noticias.terra.cl/chile/presidente-pinera-inauguro-hospital-intercultural-de-canete,76aa2de2031e1410VgnCLD2000000ec6eb0aRCRD.html|accessdate=30 May 2016|agency=Terra|date=22 October 2013|language=Spanish}}</ref>

== Demography ==

According to the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]]'s 2002 [[census]], Cañete spans over an area of {{convert|760.4|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 31,270 inhabitants (15,625 men and 15,645 women). 63.4% (19,839) live in [[urban area]]s and 36.6% (11,431) reside in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 6.6% (1,947 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

Projections indicated that the population in 2009 was 33,535 inhabitants.<ref name="Official"/>

== Administration ==

Cañete is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], the smallest administrative subdivision in Chile. It is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile|administrative division]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (mayor) who is directly elected by the public every four years and leading a six-member council.<ref>{{cite web|title=Saludo de Alcalde - Municipalidad de Cañete|url=http://www.cañete.cl/paginanormal.php?pagina=5|accessdate=10 May 2016|language=Spanish}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Concejo Municipal - Municipalidad de Cañete|url=http://www.cañete.cl/paginanormal.php?pagina=6|accessdate=10 May 2016|language=Spanish}}</ref> The municipality of Cañete is a member of the Association of Municipalities of the [[Arauco Province]], also known as Arauco 7.<ref>{{cite web|title=Asamblea - Arauco 7|url=http://arauco7.cl/sitio/?page_id=26|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160603124916/http://arauco7.cl/sitio/?page_id=26|url-status=dead|archive-date=3 June 2016|accessdate=10 May 2016|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Cañete is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Manuel Monsalve (PS) and Iván Norambuena (UDI) as part of the 46th electoral district, together with [[Lota, Chile|Lota]], [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], [[Arauco, Chile|Arauco]], [[Curanilahue]], [[Los Álamos]], [[Contulmo]] and [[Tirúa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Víctor Pérez Varela (UDI) and [[Felipe Harboe|Felipe Harboe Bascuñán]] (PPD) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).

== Transportation ==

[[Las Misiones Airport]] is located in northern Cañete.

The city also has several bus terminals downtown, including Pedro de Valdivia terminal, Jeldres terminal, and Biobio-Jota Ewert terminal. Intercity buses run to Santiago, Concepción, and Temuco.

== Human resources ==

=== Education ===

Cañete has 34 primary and secondary schools, of which 24 are public. Top schools include Liceo Gabriela Mistral, Instituto San José and Liceo B-56.<ref>{{cite news|title=Cañete: Liceo Gabriela Mistral e Instituto San José celebran resultados del Simce|url=http://www.araucotv.cl/noticias/canete-liceo-gabriela-mistral-e-instituto-san-jose-celebran-resultados-del-simce/|accessdate=10 May 2016|agency=AraucoTV|language=Spanish}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=SIMCE: Liceo B-56 entre los mejores del país|url=http://www.araucotv.cl/videos/simce-liceo-b-56-entre-los-mejores-del-pais/|accessdate=10 May 2016|agency=AraucoTV|date=2 May 2011|language=Spanish}}</ref> Higher education institutions include the Technological Institute of the [[Catholic University of the Most Holy Conception]] and the CFT Lota Arauco of the [[University of Concepción]].

=== Public health ===

The Intercultural Hospital Kallvu Llanka ([[Mapuche language|Mapudungun]] for "The Universe's Jewel")<ref>{{cite web|title=Inauguraron el hospital Kallvu Llanka, el primer establecimiento intercultural|url=http://www.hospitalaria.cl/portada/noticias/nacional/313-inauguraron-el-hospital-kallvu-llanka,-el-primer-establecimiento-intercultural.html|website=Hospitalaria.cl|accessdate=10 May 2016|language=Spanish|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160513022429/http://www.hospitalaria.cl/portada/noticias/nacional/313-inauguraron-el-hospital-kallvu-llanka,-el-primer-establecimiento-intercultural.html|archive-date=13 May 2016|url-status=dead}}
Its name ''Kallvu Llanka'' is translated to Spanish as ''joya del universo''.</ref> is located on route P-60-R, in northern Cañete. It offers traditional medicine while channeling and honoring [[mapuche]] culture through aspects such a circular waiting rooms similar to [[ruka (Mapuche)|ruka]]s. The hospital was opened in 2013.

== Culture ==

=== Museums ===

The Mapuche Museum of Cañete is located in the southern part of the city and has been open since 1968.

=== Rural culture ===

Every year, between January and February the Agricultural, Livestock and Forestry Fair of Cañete (Spanish: ''Feria Agrícola, Ganadera y Forestal,'' FAGAF) takes place on the Anike farm two kilometers from the city. The fair is considered the largest in the region and one of the most important agricultural events in [[Chile]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Lanzamiento de Feria Agrícola y Forestal de Cañete se realizará este jueves|url=http://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/2013/01/30/lanzamiento-de-feria-agricola-y-forestal-de-canete-se-realizara-este-jueves.shtml|accessdate=4 June 2016|agency=BioBio Chile|date=30 January 2013|language=Spanish}}</ref> It usually hosts more than one hundred exhibitors and features activities such as loose mare threshing, agricultural and forestry machinery exhibitions and gastronomy contests, amongst other activities.<ref>{{cite news|title=Más de 100 expositores de todo el país llegaron a Cañete para la tradicional Fiesta Agrícola y Ganadera|url=http://www.soychile.cl/Arauco/Sociedad/2015/01/27/301533/Mas-de-100-expositores-de-todo-el-pais-llegaron-a-Canete-para-la-tradicional-Fiesta-Agricola-y-Ganadera.aspx|accessdate=4 June 2016|agency=SoyChile|date=27 January 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref> Additionally, the fair incorporates the local culture by showcasing Mapuche and Chilean farm traditions. Since 2015, the fair has been held in conjunction with Cañete Week which features singers, music groups and comedians.<ref>{{cite news|title=Noche de Brujas, Los Vásquez y Jorge Alís: Los artistas que estarán en la Feria Agrícola de Cañete|url=http://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/2015/01/14/noche-de-brujas-los-vasquez-y-jorge-alis-los-artistas-que-estaran-en-la-feria-agricola-de-canete.shtml|accessdate=4 June 2016|agency=BioBioChile|date=14 January 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Another event, the Farmer Fest of Cayucupil, takes place 14&nbsp;km from the city center in a rural area.

=== Movies ===

Cañete is continuing to grow its film and entertainment presence. The [[Lebu International Film Festival]] is headquartered in the city.<ref>{{cite web|title=Programa FICIL Cañete|url=http://www.cinelebu.cl/wp/?page_id=1522|website=Cinelebu.cl|accessdate=7 July 2016|language=Spanish}}</ref> Also, [[Jorge Olguín (director)|Jorge Olguín]]'s 2014 movie ''[[Whispers of the Forest]]'' was filmed around Cañete, mainly in Reussland Park and Morales Castle, 10&nbsp;km north of the city.<ref>{{cite news|title=En tétrico palacio abandonado de Cañete será filmada primera película 3D chilena|url=http://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/2011/08/22/en-tetrico-palacio-abandonado-de-canete-sera-filmada-primera-pelicula-3d-chilena.shtml|accessdate=7 July 2016|agency=Lanalhue Noticias|publisher=BioBioChile|date=22 August 2011|language=Spanish}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=Los Gritos del Bosque, la primera película chilena que se realiza en 3D|url=http://www.latercera.com/noticia/cultura/2013/10/1453-549403-9-los-gritos-del-bosque-la-primera-pelicula-chilena-que-se-realiza-en-3d.shtml|accessdate=7 July 2016|publisher=La Tercera|date=30 October 2013|language=Spanish|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305021352/http://www.latercera.com/noticia/cultura/2013/10/1453-549403-9-los-gritos-del-bosque-la-primera-pelicula-chilena-que-se-realiza-en-3d.shtml|url-status=dead}}</ref>
