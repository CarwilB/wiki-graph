The '''Palavecino Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Lara (state)|Lara]] and, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 174,099.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Cabudare]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Palavecino Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Lara.zip {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Palavecino Municipality, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 174,099 (up from 155,653 in 2007). This amounts to 9.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|353.76|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Palavecino Municipality is Mirla Vires, elected in 2017 with 64.85% of the vote and 59.23% abstention.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Palavecino | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D11%26mun%3D5%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229230732/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D11%26mun%3D5%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-16 | url-status=live }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Cabudare, José Gregorio Bastidas, and Agua Viva
