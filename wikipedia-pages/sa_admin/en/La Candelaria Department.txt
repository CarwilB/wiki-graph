'''La Candelaria''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|1525|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north and east with [[Bolivia]], to the east with [[Rosario de la Frontera Department]], to the west with [[Guachipas Department]], and to the south with  [[Tucumán Province]].
