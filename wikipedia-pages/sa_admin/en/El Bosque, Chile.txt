'''El Bosque''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "the forest") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|14.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], El Bosque spans an area of {{convert|14.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 175,594 inhabitants (86,435 men and 89,159 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 1.6% (2,740 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, El Bosque is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 mayor is Sadi Melo Moya ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]). The communal council has the following members:
* Simón Melo Contreras ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Lorena Downey Belmar ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Nicol Sepúlveda Hernández ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Patricia del Carmen Coñoman Carrillo ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Carlos Contreras Muñoz ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Rafael Valenzuela Pérez ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Rosa Peralta Valenzuela ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Patricia Arriagada Núñez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], El Bosque is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Tucapel Jiménez ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Iván Moreira ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 27th electoral district, (together with [[La Cisterna]] and [[San Ramón, Chile|San Ramón]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Soledad Alvear Valenzuela ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pablo Longueira Montes ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).

== Transportation ==
The [[El Bosque Airport|El Bosque airport]] is a military airport located in the commune.
