'''Santa Teresa District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[La Convención Province]] in the [[Cusco Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Santa Teresa, Peru is situated 6.5&nbsp;km northwest of [[Machu Picchu]] and is at the axis of several important routes leading to this archeological centre, including alternative paths ways to the ruins (previously the railway was the only means of reaching Machu Picchu). In 1998, a landslide completely buried the town and destroyed the bridge that connected it to Machu Picchu and Cusco, however the bridge has subsequently been rebuilt.

== Geography ==
The [[Willkapampa mountain range|Willkapampa]] mountain range traverses the district. One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Kiswar (Peru)|Kiswar]] at {{convert|5771|m|ft|abbr=on}}. The highest peak in the range is [[Salkantay]] at 6,271&nbsp;m (20,574&nbsp;ft). Other mountains are listed below:<ref name=neate/><ref name=escale/><ref name=amelunxen/>
