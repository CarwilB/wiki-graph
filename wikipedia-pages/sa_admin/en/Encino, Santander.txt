'''Encino''' is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Encino has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with cool to pleasant mornings, warm to very warm afternoons, and very heavy rainfall year round.
