'''Bom Conselho''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbõ kõˈseju]}}) ("Good Advice") is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has 786.2 sq/km of total area.

==Geography==

* State – [[Pernambuco]]
* Region – [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries – [[Saloá]] and [[Terezinha, Pernambuco|Terezinha]] (N); [[Alagoas]] state (S); [[Lagoa do Ouro]] (E); [[Iati]] (W).
* Area – 786.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation – 654 m
* Hydrography – [[Ipanema River]]
* Vegetation – [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Annual average temperature – 22.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] – 266&nbsp;km
* Population - 44,294 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]])

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Bom Conselho are related to commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[sheep]], and [[horse]]s, and plantations of [[sweet potatoes]], [[beans]] and [[banana]]s.
