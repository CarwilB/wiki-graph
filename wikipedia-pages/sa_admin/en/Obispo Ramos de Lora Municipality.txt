The '''Obispo Ramos de Lora Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 25,002.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Santa Elena de Arenales]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Obispo Ramos de Lora Municipality.<ref name="ine">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Obispo Ramos de Lora Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 25,002 (up from 21,496 in 2000). This amounts to 2.96% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|77.9|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Obispo Ramos de Lora Municipality is Humberto Gómez, re-elected on October 31, 2004 with 49% of the vote.<ref name="cne">{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=12&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio|publisher=cne.gob.ve|accessdate=2016-06-15}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es;  Capital Obispo Ramos de Lora, Eloy Paredes, and San Rafael de Alcázar.<ref name="ine"/>
