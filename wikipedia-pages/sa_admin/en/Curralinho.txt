'''Curralinho''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 34,994 people. The area of the municipality is 3.617,240&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Furos de Breves''.

==Geography==

The municipality is on the island of [[Marajó]] to the northwest of [[Belém]] in the delta region where the [[Amazon River|Amazon]] and [[Tocantins River|Tocantins]] rivers empty into the [[Atlantic Ocean]]. It contains part of the {{convert|194868|ha}} [[Terra Grande-Pracuúba Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Extrativista Terra Grande-Pracuúba|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente

It is contained within the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
