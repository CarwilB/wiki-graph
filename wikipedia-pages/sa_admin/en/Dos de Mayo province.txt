'''Dos de Mayo''' (literally ''May 2'') is one of eleven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Huánuco Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[La Unión, Huánuco|La Unión]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[Huamalíes Province|province of Huamalíes]]
*'''East''': [[Leoncio Prado Province|province of Leoncio Prado]]
*'''South''': provinces of [[Huánuco Province|Huánuco]],  [[Yarowilca Province|Yarowilca]] and [[Lauricocha Province|Lauricocha]]
*'''West''': [[Ancash Region]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], however, is the language which the majority of the population (59.82%) learnt to speak in childhood, 39.87% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
