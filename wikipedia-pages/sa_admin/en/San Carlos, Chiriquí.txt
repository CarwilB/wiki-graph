'''San Carlos''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[David District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|44.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 4,487 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|100.4|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,543; its population as of 2000 was 3,181.<ref name=inec/>
