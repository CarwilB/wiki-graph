'''Ancasti''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the southern part of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 4,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|2,413|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Ancasti]], which is located around {{cvt|1,070|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].
