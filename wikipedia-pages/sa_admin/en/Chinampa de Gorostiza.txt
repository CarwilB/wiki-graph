'''Chinampa de Gorostiza''' is a town and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. 

==History==
The municipality was created in 1874 as '''San Antonio Chinampa'''. It adopted its current name in 1932 to honour [[Manuel Eduardo de Gorostiza]] (1789–1851), a playwright, 
diplomat and cabinet minister.<ref name="PMD">http://www.veracruz.gob.mx/finanzas/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2022/PMD/PMD%20Chinampa%20de%20Gorostiza.Veracruz.2022-2025.pdf {{dead link|date=May 2025}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Gorostiza, Manuel Eduardo de (1789-1851) – Guía de Memorias de Hacienda de México (1822-1910)|url=https://memoriasdehacienda.colmex.mx/mhwp/?page_id=7670|access-date=2021-02-08|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
It is located in the north of the state of Veracruz, about {{convert|220|km|mi|0}} from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{convert|152.99|sqkm|sqmi|2}}. It is located at {{coord|21|22|N|97|44|W|}}.

The municipality of ''' Chinampa de Gorostiza ''' is bordered to the south by [[Tantima]] and [[Tuxpam de Rodríguez Cano|Tuxpam]], to the east by [[Tamiahua]] and to the north by [[Tamalín]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30060a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111230722/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30060a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2013-01-11|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chinampa de Gorostiza|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05}}</ref>

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[Orange (fruit)|orange]] and [[mango]].

==Culture==
Festivities take place in June in honour of [[Anthony of Padua]], the town's patron saint.<ref name="PMD"/>
