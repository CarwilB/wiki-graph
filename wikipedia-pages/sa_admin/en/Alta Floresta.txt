'''Alta Floresta''' (Portuguese for "High Forest") is a municipality in [[Mato Grosso]], [[Brazil]]. It is located at around {{coord|9|53|10|S|56|5|14|W |region:BR_type:city |display=inline,title}}.

The municipality is served by [[Alta Floresta Airport|Piloto Osvaldo Marques Dias Airport]].

The municipality contains a small part of the {{convert|184900|ha}} [[Cristalino State Park]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Infraestrutura do Módulo ... PPBio}}|language=pt
