'''Atalaia do Norte''' is the most western [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. As of 2020, its population was 20,398 and its area is 76,355&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref> thus making it the third largest municipality in Amazonas and the seventh largest in Brazil.

==Geography==
The area of this municipality includes the [[Vale do Javari]], an indigenous reserve which is inhabited until the present day by isolated Amerindians also called ''"arrow peoples"'', indigenous ethnic groups that have had little or no contact with the rest of the world.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2011/jun/22/new-tribe-discovered-amazon?INTCMP=SRCH |title=Uncontacted tribe found deep in Amazon rainforest |accessdate=2020-05-08 |publisher=The Guardian UK |last=Phillips |first=Tom |date=2011-06-22}}</ref> The town is located at the start of the [[Itaquai River]].
