'''Pachitea''' is one of eleven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Huánuco Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is [[Panao]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': province of [[Huánuco Province|Huánuco]]
*'''East''': province of [[Puerto Inca Province|Puerto Inca]]
*'''South''': [[Pasco Region]]
*'''West''': province of [[Ambo Province|Ambo]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into four districts, which are:

* [[Chaglla District|Chaglla]] ([[Chaglla]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], however, is the language which the majority of the population (52.69%) learnt to speak in childhood, 46.98% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
