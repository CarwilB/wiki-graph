'''Huacaybamba District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huacaybamba Province|Huacaybamba]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created in 1857 during the presidency of [[Ramón Castilla]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.iperu.org/distrito-de-huacaybamba-provincia-de-huacaybamba|title=Distrito de Huacaybamba, provincia de Huacaybamba, región Huánuco|date=2016-11-21|website=Portal iPerú|language=es|access-date=2019-11-17}}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]]  is the language which the majority of the population (65.93%) learnt to speak in childhood, 33.42% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
