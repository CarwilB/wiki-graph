The '''Huata District''' ({{langx|es|'''Distrito de Huata'''}}) is the smallest of the 10 districts of the [[Huaylas Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of [[Huata]].<ref>[http://www.munihuata.gob.pe Official web site of the Huata District in the Huaylas province] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070424024032/http://www.munihuata.gob.pe/ |date=2007-04-24 }}</ref>

==History==
The district was founded on January 2, 1857.

==Location==
The district is located in the central part of the province at an elevation of 2,736m in the black mountains ({{langx|es|Cordillera Negra}}), neighboring district is the [[Caraz District|Caraz district]] in which the provinces capital [[Caraz]] is located, 17&nbsp;km from the village of Huata.

==Political division==
The Huata District is divided into 1 [[village]] ({{langx|es|pueblos}}, singular: {{lang|es|pueblo}}), 6 [[hamlet (place)|hamlets]] ({{langx|es|caseríos}}, singular: {{lang|es|caserío}}) and 40 ({{langx|es|anexos}}, singular: {{lang|es|anexo}}):

==Capital==
The capital of the Huata district is the city of '''Huata'''.

==Climate==
The district has a temperate and dry climate with a temperature varying from 12 to 19 degrees Celsius.
