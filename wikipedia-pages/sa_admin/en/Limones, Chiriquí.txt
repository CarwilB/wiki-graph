'''Limones''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Barú District, Chiriquí|Barú District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|53.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,040 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|19.3|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 974; its population as of 2000 was 948.<ref name=inec/>

==Climate==
Limones has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] (Am) with moderate to little rainfall from December to April and heavy to very heavy rainfall from May to November.
