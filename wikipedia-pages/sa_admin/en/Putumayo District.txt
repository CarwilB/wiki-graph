'''Putumayo District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Putumayo Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Putumayo District belonged to Maynas Province until Putumayo Province was created on May 5, 2014, in President [[Ollanta Humala]]'s term.

== Geography ==
It has an area of 34,942.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
