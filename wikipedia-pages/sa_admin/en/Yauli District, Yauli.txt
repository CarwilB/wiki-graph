'''Yauli District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Yauli Province]], located in the [[Department of Junín]] in the central highlands of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the Law No. 6468 in November 7, 1847, during the presidency of [[Ramón Castilla]].
