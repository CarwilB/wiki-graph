'''Quebrangulo''' ([[Brazilian Portuguese]]: [[Help:IPA/Portuguese|/kebɾɐ̃ˈgulu/]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 11,248 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/quebrangulo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 300&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|4382|ha}} [[Pedra Talhada Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation |title=Plano Operativo de Prevenção e Combate aos Incêndios Florestais da Reserva Biológica de Pedra Talhada |date=November 2006 |author=Helaeson de Almeida |language=pt |url=http://www.ibama.gov.br/phocadownload/prevfogo/plano_operativo_reserva_biolgica_de_pedra_talhada.pdf |access-date=2016-04-16 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120824182250/http://www.ibama.gov.br/phocadownload/prevfogo/plano_operativo_reserva_biolgica_de_pedra_talhada.pdf |archive-date=2012-08-24 }}</ref>
