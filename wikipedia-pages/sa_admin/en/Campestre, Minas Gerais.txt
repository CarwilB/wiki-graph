'''Campestre''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the south of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population is 21,054 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|54|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/campestre/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
