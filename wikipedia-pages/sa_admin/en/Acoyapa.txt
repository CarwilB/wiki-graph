'''Acoyapa''' is a town and a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupied an area of {{cvt|1382|km2}}, and as per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 20,465 individuals. It was a major commercial and transportation hub in the 19th century, and briefly served as the capital of the department. The economy is primarily dependent on [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing.

==Etymology==
The origin of the name "Acoyapa" has several theories. The name might derive from "Acoyaph" which means "place from above" or "branch of the heaven".<ref name="fact sheet">{{cite web |title=Municipios - Chontales |url=http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/documentos/CHONTALES/acoyapa.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090616205534/http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/documentos/CHONTALES/acoyapa.pdf |archive-date=16 June 2009 |access-date=16 June 2009 |work=Instituto Nicaragüense de Fomento Municipal |language=es}}</ref><ref name="MAPA">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/municipio-de-acoyapa|title=Municipio de Acoyapa|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 
As per some historians, it might have come from the indigenous [[Nahuatl]] term meaning "river of tears" while others meaning include "place of peccaries", "watchtower", and "moonstone".<ref name="Cul">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/acoyapa-culture/|title=Culture, Municipio de Acoyapa|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>  

==History==
As per archaeological evidence, stone sculptures, tools, and other objects have been found, indicating that the region might have been occupied by a disappeared civilization of people associated with the Chontal cultural group earlier. The present settlement of Acoyapa was developed in the mid-17th century, when the Spanish relocated from the abandoned settlement of Nueva Jaén at the confluence of [[Lake Nicaragua]] and the [[San Juan River (Nicaragua)|San Juan River]]. In the 19th century, it was a major commercial center and transportation hub, linking several mining towns.<ref name="Cul"/> It has a church built during the Spanish colonial occupation, dedicated to [[Saint Sebastian|San Sebastián]].<ref name="VN">{{cite web|url=https://www.visitanicaragua.com/en/chontales/|title=Chontales|work=Visit Nicaragua|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> Acoyapa was the capital of [[Chontales Department]], when it was established,<ref name="MAPA"/><ref name="VN"/> and the capital alternated with [[Juigalpa]] between 1858 and 1877 before Juigalpa became the permanent capital.<ref name="Cul"/>

== Geography ==
Acoyapa is located in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|1382|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/ |title=Administrative Divisions of Nicaragua |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality is bordered by  [[Juigalpa, Chontales|Juigalpa]], [[San Pedro de Lóvago]], and [[Santo Tomás, Chontales|Santo Tomás]] municipalities to the north, by [[Villa Sandino]] and [[El Coral]] municipalities to the east, by [[El Almendro]] and [[Morrito]] municipalities to the south, and by [[Lake Nicaragua]] to the west.<ref name="MAPA"/> It is located {{cvt|32|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Juigalpa]] and {{cvt|170|km}} from the Nicaraguan capital of [[Managua]].<ref name="MAPA"/><ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipios |url=http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/municipios.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110724143945/http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/municipios.html |archive-date=24 July 2011 |access-date=24 July 2011 |work=Instituto Nicaragüense de Fomento Municipal |language=es}}</ref> The El Nancital archipelago forms part of the municipality.<ref name="VN"/> A {{cvt|153|km}}-long road, completed in 2009, connects the town to the Costa Rican border.<Ref>{{cite web|url=https://opecfund.org/where-we-work/list/acoyapa-costa-rican-border-road-integration-project|title=Acoyapa-Costa Rican Border Road Integration Project|work=OPEC|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics and economy ==
As per 2023 estimate, Acoyapa has a population of 20,412 individuals of whom 10,152 were males and 10,313 were females. The urban population was 10,436 (50.8%) and the rest of the population was classified as living in rural areas.<ref name="CP">{{cite web |title=Acoyapa (Municipality, Nicaragua) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/chontales/6535__acoyapa/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |website=City Population}}</ref>

The economy of the municipality is based on [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing. Major agricultural produce include [[maize]], beans, [[rice]], and [[sorghum]]. Fishing is practised along the coast.<ref name="MAPA"/><ref name="VN"/> In the mid 20th century, it was known as the "landowner's capital" due to its large cattle ranches.<ref name="Cul"/>
