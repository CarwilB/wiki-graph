'''Anapia District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Yunguyo Province|Yunguyo]] in [[Puno Region]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Anapia District was created by Law No. 236062 (18, 1983), in second term of Fernando Belaúnde Terry.
