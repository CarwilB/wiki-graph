'''Florida''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|Province]] in the [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[Samaipata, Bolivia|Samaipata]]. The province was created by law on December 15, 1924.

==Division==
The province is divided into four [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].

==Places of interest==
* The archaeological site of [[El Fuerte de Samaipata|Fort Samaipata]] ''(El Fuerte de Samaipata)'' which was declared a [[UNESCO World Heritage Site]] in 1998 and [[Amboró National Park]] are the principal tourist attractions of the province.
* [[Laguna Volcán]]
* ''Las Cuevas.'' There are several pools and beaches where the locals swim and disport themselves.
