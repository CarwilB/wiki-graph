== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]]  is the language which the majority of the population (80.45%) learnt to speak in childhood, 19.28% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>{{Cite web |title=INEI - Redatam - 2007 |url=http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C |access-date=2022-08-08 |website=iinei.inei.gob.pe}}</ref>
