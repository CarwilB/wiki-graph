'''Cañuelas Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 42,575 in an area of {{convert|1200|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its administrative centre is [[Cañuelas]], which has a population of around 24,380.<ref>{{cite web|title=Provincia de Buenos Aires Según Localidad|url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/07000LC121.xls|work=Población Censada en 1991 y Población por Sexo en 2001|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos|accessdate=28 February 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928010656/http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/07000LC121.xls|archive-date=28 September 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Sports==
Cañuelas is home to [[Cañuelas FC]], a [[Football (soccer)|football]] club currently playing in the regional [[Primera C Metropolitana|4th Division]] of Argentine football.
