'''Guardarraya''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Patillas, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 1,603.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó. ==History==
Guardarraya, located where the [[Cordillera Central (Puerto Rico)|Cordillera Central mountains]] meet the [[Caribbean Sea]], in 1765 was known for its agriculture, fishing and production of aromatic oils. Guardarraya is where the legendary pirate [[Pirata Cofresi|Roberto Cofresí]] was captured and turned over to authorities.<ref name="PBS">{{cite web |title=La Historia de Puerto Rico a través de sus Barrios: Barrio Guardarraya de Patillas [The History of Puerto Rico through its Barrios: Guardarraya Barrio of Patillas]| url=https://www.pbslearningmedia.org/resource/09670610-27a9-4d87-bb79-3e47d1adeffb/barrios-de-puerto-rico-barrio-guardarraya-de-patillas/ |website=www.pbslearningmedia.org |publisher=Fundación Puertorriqueña de las Humanidades |access-date=29 August 2020 |language=es |format=video}}</ref>

Guardarraya was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. In 1899, the [[United States Department of War]] conducted a [[census]] of Puerto Rico finding that the population of Guardarraya barrio was 1,013.<ref name="OfficeSanger1900">{{cite book|author1=Joseph Prentiss Sanger|author2=Henry Gannett|author3=Walter Francis Willcox|title=Informe sobre el censo de Puerto Rico, 1899, United States. ==Sectors==
Barrios (which are, in contemporary times, roughly comparable to [[minor civil divisions]])<ref name="Barrio-Pueblo">{{cite web |title=US Census Barrio-Pueblo definition |url=https://factfinder.census.gov/help/en/barrio.htm |website=factfinder.com |publisher=US Census |access-date=5 January 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170513190743/https://factfinder.census.gov/help/en/barrio.htm |archive-date=13 May 2017 |url-status=dead }}</ref> in turn are further subdivided into smaller local populated place areas/units called '''sectores''' (''sectors'' in English).
