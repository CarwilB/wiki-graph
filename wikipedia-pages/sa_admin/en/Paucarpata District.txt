'''Paucarpata District''' is one of the twenty-nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Arequipa Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Distrital de Paucarpata, [http://www.munipaucarpata.gob.pe Municipalidad Distrital de Paucarpata], Retrieved November 16, 2007.</ref>  It is noted for its terraced agricultural hills, called ''[[anden]]es'' in Spanish.
