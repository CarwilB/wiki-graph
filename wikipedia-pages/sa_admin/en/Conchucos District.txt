'''Conchucos District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Pallasca Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is Anka Pata at approximately {{convert|4600|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Pallasca Province (Ancash Region)</ref>

{{columns-list|colwidth=15em|

*  Hatun Hirka

* Parya Chuku
* Parya Qucha
* Pillu Kunka
* Pinqullu
* Qullpa Raqra
* Sapallu
* Sinqa
* Tawlli
* Wachu Mach'ay
* Wanaku
* Wiqu Runtu
* Yuraq Yaku
}}

Some of the lakes in the district are [[Challwaqucha (Ancash)|Challwaqucha]], [[Kinwaqucha (Ancash)|Kinwaqucha]], [[Llamaqucha]], [[Paryaqucha (Pallasca)|Paryaqucha]] and [[T'uruqucha (Ancash)|T'uruqucha]].

== Etymology ==
Conchucos means 'country of water' in [[Kulyi language]] (''kon'' water, ''chucu'' country).<ref>Torero, Alfredo: ''Idioma de los Andes'' (2002)</ref><ref>{{Cite journal|last=Palomino|first=Rodolfo|title=Las etimologías toponímicas del Inca Garcilaso|url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/39344891|journal=Revista Andina |issn=0259-9600 |issue=38 |year=2004 |page=9|volume=3}}</ref>
