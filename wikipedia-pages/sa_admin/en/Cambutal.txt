'''Cambutal ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tonosí District]], [[Los Santos Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 511 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 58 of July 29, 1998, owing to the Declaration of Unconstitutionality of Law 1 of 1982.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 452.<ref name=inec/>

The annual fishing tournament is one of the largest in Panama.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Guide |first=Playa Cambutal |title=Events Calendar — Playa Cambutal, Panama |url=https://playacambutalguide.com/calendar |access-date=2026-05-25 |website=Playa Cambutal Guide |language=English}}</ref>
