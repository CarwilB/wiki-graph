'''Río Segundo Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://prensa.cba.gov.ar/informacion-general/departamento-rio-segundo-la-provincia-avanza-en-la-consolidacion-de-la-red-de-municipios-cooperativos/|title=Departamento Río Segundo: La Provincia avanza en la consolidación de la Red de Municipios Cooperativos|website=prensa.cba.gov.ar|publisher=Government of Córdoba|access-date=18 June 2025|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population in 2001 of 95,803 in an area of {{convert|4,970|km²}}. Its [[capital city]] is [[Villa del Rosario, Córdoba|Villa del Rosario]], which is located {{convert|648|km}} from the [[Buenos Aires]].
