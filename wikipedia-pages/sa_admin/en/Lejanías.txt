'''Lejanías''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Lejanías has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to extremely heavy rainfall year round.
