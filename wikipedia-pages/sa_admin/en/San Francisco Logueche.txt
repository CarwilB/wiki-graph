'''  San Francisco Logueche ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{Convert|76.55|km2|abbr=on}}. 
It is part of the [[Miahuatlán District, Oaxaca|Miahuatlán District]] in the south of the [[Sierra Sur de Oaxaca|Sierra Sur Region]].<ref>{{Cite encyclopedia

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 2,240.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Francisco Logueche|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20146a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>

[[Amatlán Zapotec]] is spoken in the town. It is located "at the foot of a very tall mountain" called  ''Yiroos'' in Zapotec.<ref>{{Cite web
