'''Yerba Buena''' is a department of [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.eltucumano.com/noticia/actualidad/302079/cuantos-habitantes-tiene-cada-departamento-de-tucuman-se-publicaron-los-resultados-definitivos-del-censo-2022|title=¿Cuántos habitantes tiene cada departamento de Tucumán?: se publicaron los resultados definitivos del Censo 2022|work=El Tucumano|date=17 July 2024|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The department comprises the city of [[Yerba Buena, Tucumán|Yerba Buena]] and two "comunas rurales" (rural communities): ''[[Cebil Redondo]]'' and ''San Javier'', with a joint population of 63,707 (2001). Lying at the feet of the Aconquija Range, it is located next to [[San Miguel de Tucumán]] on the east, Tafi Viejo on the north and northwest, and Lules on the south and southwest.
