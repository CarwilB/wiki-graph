'''Arroyito''' is a city and district located in the [[Concepción Department (Paraguay)|Concepción Department]] of Paraguay. It is one of the newest districts in the country, created in November 2016.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/arroyito-convertido-en-distrito-1540198.html|title=Arroyito ahora es un distrito de Concepción|publisher=ABC Color}}</ref>
