'''Silca''' is a municipality in the [[Olancho Department]] of central Honduras. As of 2023, its population was estimated at 8,354.

== History ==

The time in which it was founded is unknown. In 1684, it is known that it already existed. In 1878, it was given the category of Municipality.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Archived copy | url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Silca-Olancho-2018.pdf | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250612210150/https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Silca-Olancho-2018.pdf | archive-date=2025-06-12}}</ref>

According to the Historian of Silca during an interview done by “GuayapeTV” he said that the History of the people of Silca started first when it was inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples|Indigenous tribes]]. after the conquest of the Spanish, Silca was a hub for [[Enslaved Africans]] brought by the Spanish for the gold washings of [[Olancho Department|Olancho]], Many of them being from [[The Belgian Congo]], and [[Senegal]]. Later on many escaped Africans settled in Silca. According to the Historian, the people of Silca are descendants of [[Spaniards]], [[West Africa]]ns, [[Central Africa|Central Africans]], and [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous Americans]]. This is the reason people who are from Silca are called Congos as a nickname.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Olancho {{!}} Encyclopedia.com | url=https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/olancho | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200718102743/https://www.encyclopedia.com/humanities/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/olancho | access-date=2025-08-14 | archive-date=2020-07-18}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web | title=Archived copy | url=https://bibliotecavirtual.clacso.org.ar/ar/libros/lasa98/England-Anderson.pdf | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250522222349/https://bibliotecavirtual.clacso.org.ar/ar/libros/lasa98/England-Anderson.pdf | archive-date=2025-05-22}}</ref>

Silca is a testimony of the history of Honduras.
