'''Antônio Prado de Minas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=yes}} is estimated to be 1,587 people living in an area of {{cvt|85.04|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/antonio-prado-de-minas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Muriaé]] within the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]].
