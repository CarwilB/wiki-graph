'''San José''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|223.8|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 69,316 inhabitants.

==History==
In the 19th century, [[Iztapa]] was the only port in the region, which existed for a while, and the trade was largely conducted from [[Acajutla]] (now in [[El Salvador]]). After the independence of Guatemala, the government planned for a new port in 1851. Based on a report by the committee appointed to study the same, the government issued decree No.62 on 12 March 1852, which moved the port of Iztapa to El Zapote effective 1 January 1853, and the port was named as San Jose de Guatemala. The port was moved to the new city on 13 July 1853. On 2 January 1875, San José was elevated as a municipality.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de San Jose|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of San Jose municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-del-municipio-de-san-jose-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Municipalidad de San José |url=http://www.munisanjose.com/ |access-date=10 February 2024 |work=Municipalidad San José, Escuintla |language=es |trans-title=Municipality of San Jose}}</ref> 

==Geography==
San Jose is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|223.8|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Jose (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0509__san_jos%C3%A9/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the northern part of the department, about {{cvt|57|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|112|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Masagua]] and [[La Democracia, Escuintla|La Demorcracia]] to the north, [[Iztapa]] to the east, [[La Gomera, Escuintla|La Gomera]] to the west, and Pacific Ocean to the south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de San Jose|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of San Jose municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-san-jose-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1.98|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> [[Puerto San Jose]] has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|25.204|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|118.52|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/escuintla/puerto-san-jose |title=Puerto San Jose |work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 69,316 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 35,406 males and 33,910 females. About 28.4% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.0% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (62%) was classified as rural, while 38% lived in urban areas. About 79.8% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (96.3%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (2.9%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 87.6%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (97.9%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/> 

==Economy==
[[Agriculture]] and agro-based industries are the major contributor to the economy, along with tourism and port services. The municipality contains [[Puerto Quetzal]], Guatemala's largest Pacific port. The port generates more than seven million quetzales in income annually and employs an estimated 4,000 people.<ref name="Info">{{cite web|title=Economia del municipio de San Jose|work=Deguate|trans-title=Economy of San Jose municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/economia-del-municipio-de-san-jose-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>
