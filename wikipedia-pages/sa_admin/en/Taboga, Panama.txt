'''Taboga   ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Taboga District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 731 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Taboga District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,199; its population as of 2000 was 908.<ref name=inec/>
