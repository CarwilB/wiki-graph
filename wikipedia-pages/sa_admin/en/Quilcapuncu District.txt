'''Quilcapuncu District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[San Antonio de Putina Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital]'' {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}. {{in lang|es}}. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Quilcapuncu District was created in November 26, 1986, in Alan García's term.
