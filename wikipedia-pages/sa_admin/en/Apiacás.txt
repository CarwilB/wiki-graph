'''Apiacás''' is the northernmost municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]]. It is the only city in a territorial "[[Salient (geography)|edge]]" of Mato Grosso that "pierces" the boundary between [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] and [[Pará]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|19,582|km2}} [[Juruena National Park]], one of the largest conservation units in Brazil.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional do Juruena|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=281|access-date=2016-05-23}}</ref>
