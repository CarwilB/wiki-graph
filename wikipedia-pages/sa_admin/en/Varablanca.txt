'''Varablanca''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Heredia (canton)|Heredia]] canton, in the [[Heredia Province|Heredia]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |lang=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

The Varablanca head city is located between [[Poas Volcano]] and [[Barva Volcano]] in the [[Heredia Province]] of [[Costa Rica]]. It is an important cross-road location for all major traffic connecting the northern parts of Costa Rica, especially the [[Sarapiqui River]] delta area with the major population area of the [[San José, Costa Rica|San Jose]] Central Valley. On January 8, 2009, it was struck by a powerful 6.1 magnitude earthquake known as the [[2009 Cinchona earthquake|Cinchona earthquake]].

== History ==
Varablanca was created on 5 July 1971 by Decreto 1819-G. Segregated from districts [[Puerto Viejo, Sarapiquí|Puerto Viejo]] and [[Dulce Nombre de Jesús District|Dulce Nombre de Jesús]].<ref name="dta2017" />
