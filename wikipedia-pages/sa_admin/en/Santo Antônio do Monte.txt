'''Santo Antônio do Monte''' ([[Brazilian Portuguese|Portuguese]], ''St. Antony of the Hill'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the central part of the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 28,427 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|1125.78|km2}}.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/IJ4 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city is located 194&nbsp;km west of state capital [[Belo Horizonte]]. The city is also known as Samonte. 
Santo Antonio do Monte was founded in the 18th century and it became a municipality on November 16, 1875. The main economic activity of the city is the production of fireworks. Nowadays there are in the city about 70 firework factories.
