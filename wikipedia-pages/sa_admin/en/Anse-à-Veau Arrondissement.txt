'''Anse-à-Veau''' ({{langx|ht|Ansavo}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nippes|Nippes Department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 153,639 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015 |date=March 2015 |website=IHSI |language=fr |trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |archive-date=2015-11-06 |url-status=dead |access-date=8 May 2017 }}</ref> Postal codes in the Anse-à-Veau Arrondissement start with the number 75.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Anse-à-Veau]]
* [[Arnaud, Nippes|Arnaud]]
* [[L'Asile]]
* [[Petit-Trou-de-Nippes]]
* [[Plaisance-du-Sud]]
