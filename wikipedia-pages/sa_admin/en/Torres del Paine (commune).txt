}}'''Torres del Paine''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the inland of [[Última Esperanza Province]] and [[Magallanes Region]]. The commune is administered by the [[municipality]] in [[Cerro Castillo, Chile|Cerro Castillo]], the major settlement in the commune. [[Torres del Paine National Park]] lies within the commune.  ''Paine'' means "blue" in the native [[Tehuelche language|Tehuelche]] (Aonikenk) language and is pronounced ''PIE-nay''.<ref>Abraham, Rudolf (2011). ''Torres del Paine: Trekking in Chile's Premier National Park''. Milnthorpe: Cicerone Press. [https://books.google.com/books?id=kuFiVD-SbzQC&q=pine-ay p. 17]. {{ISBN|978-1-84965-356-5}}. Retrieved 4 August 2015.</ref>

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Torres del Paine spans an area of {{convert|6469.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 739 inhabitants (543 men and 196 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 53.3% (257 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Torres del Paine is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Anahi Cardenas Rodríguez ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Torres del Paine is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region).
