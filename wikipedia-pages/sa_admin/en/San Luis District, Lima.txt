'''San Luis''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of the [[Lima Province]] in [[Peru]]. In San Luis the National Sports Village is located in charge of the Peruvian Sports Institute, which has facilities for the practice of various sports (athletics, cycling, volleyball, swimming, softball and baseball). In addition, within its facilities is the headquarters of the Peruvian Olympic Committee and the Peruvian Football Federation (training place of the Peruvian Football Team, both senior and junior categories.

== History ==
It is part of city of [[Lima]]. Officially established as a district on May 30, 1968, by President [[Fernando Belaúnde]].

The district's [[postal code]] is '''30'''.

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 3.49&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 175 meters above [[sea level]].

===Boundaries===
* North: [[El Agustino]]
* East: [[Ate District|Ate]]
* South: [[San Borja District|San Borja]]
* West: [[La Victoria District, Lima|La Victoria]]

==Demographics==
According to the [[Peru 2005 Census|2005 census]] by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 46,258 inhabitants, a population density of 13,254.4 persons/km<sup>2</sup> and 11,901 households.
