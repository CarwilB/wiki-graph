'''Puerto López Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Manabí Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[Puerto López]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 16,626.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110628180130/http://www.statoids.com/yec.html |date=28 June 2011 }} at statoids.com</ref>
