'''Cruzeiro do Sul ''' ({{IPA|pt|kɾuˈzejɾu du ˈsuw}}, ''[[Crux|Southern Cross]]'') is a [[municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. 
It has an area of {{convert|259.103|km2|sqmi}}.
It has a [[Humid subtropical climate]].
As of 2020 the estimated population was 4,449.

==Geography==

Cruzeiro do Sul is in the state of Paraná, Brazil.{{sfn|Geonames}} It has an area of {{convert|259.103|km2}} as of 2019.{{sfn|IBGE}} The elevation above sea level is about {{convert|465|m}}.{{sfn|Geonames}}

==Climate==

The [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen climate type]] is Cfa : [[Humid subtropical climate]].{{sfn|mindat}}
The average annual temperature is {{convert|23|C}}.{{sfn|NASAearth}}
The average annual rainfall is {{convert|1,934|mm}}.{{sfn|NASArain}}

==Demographics==

The population in the 2010 census was 4,563.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
The estimated population as of 2019 was 4,469.{{sfn|IBGE}}
Population density as of 2010 was {{convert|17.61|PD/km2|PD/sqmi}}.{{sfn|IBGE}}
As of 2010, 98.5% of the population had attended school between the ages of 6 and 14. 
Also as of 2010, the municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.713.
This compares to 0.437 in 1991 and 0.582 in 2000.{{sfn|IBGE}}

On the 2010 census religion was reported as [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] by 3,409 people, [[Evangelicalism|Evangelical]] by 805 people and [[Animism]] by 59 people.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

In 2017, the average monthly salary of formal workers was 2.2 minimum wages. 
Formally employed people were 12.1% of the total population. 
Households with monthly income of up to half a minimum wage per person represent 32.7% of the population.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
