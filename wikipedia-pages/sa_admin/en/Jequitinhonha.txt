'''Jequitinhonha''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʒekitʃiˈɲõɲɐ|lang}}) is a Brazilian municipality located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]].

==Geography==

The population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 25,474 people living in a total area of 3,518&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="ibge"/> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Jequitinhonha and to the microregion of [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/felisburgo/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204101147/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/felisburgo/ |archive-date=2008-02-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Jequitinhonha is located on the [[river]] of the same name. The elevation is 223 meters. It became a municipality in 1911. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]] is 582&nbsp;km.

Neighboring municipalities are: [[Joaíma]], [[Felisburgo]], [[Rubim]], [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]] and [[Pedra Azul]].

The municipality contains most of the {{convert|50,890|ha}} [[Mata Escura Biological Reserve]], created in 2003.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|REBIO da Mata Escura – ISA}}
 |title=REBIO da Mata Escura |publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591160|access-date=2017-02-23}}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities are cattle raising and the cultivation of [[coffee]], [[sugarcane]] and [[Maize|corn]]. The GDP in 2006 was R$66,471,000. There were 02 banking agencies {{as of|2007|lc=y}}. In the same year there were 699 automobiles, which was a ratio of one automobile for every 30 people. In the rural area there were 1,905 producers of which only 51 had tractors. There were 88,000 head of cattle in 2006.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref> This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
