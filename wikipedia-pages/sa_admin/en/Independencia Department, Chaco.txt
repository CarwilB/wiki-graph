'''Independencia''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 20,500 inhabitants in an area of  1,871&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Campo Largo, Chaco|Campo Largo]], which is located around 1,229&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
