'''Coyame del Sotol''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern Mexico. The municipal seat is [[Coyame]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|7,877|km2|abbr=on}}.

As of the 2010 census, the municipality had a total population of 1,681.<ref>{{cite web|title=2010 census tables: INEGI |url=http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 |accessdate=29 April 2011 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130502025339/http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 |archivedate=May 2, 2013 }}</ref><ref name="SEDESOL">{{cite web|title=Coyame del Sotol|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=015|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref>

The municipality had 232 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

==Name==
"Coyame" is the [[Apache language|Apache]] toponym of a nearby [[Arroyo (watercourse)|arroyo]].
"Sotol" is the local name for ''[[Dasylirion wheeleri]]'', a flowering desert plant, and for [[sotol]], a distilled [[mezcal]]-like spirit made from it.
