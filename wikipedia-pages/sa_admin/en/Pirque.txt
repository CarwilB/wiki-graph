'''Pirque''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpiɾke|lang}}) is a commune of [[Chile]] in [[Cordillera Province, Chile|Cordillera Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]; it is located 2.8 kilometers southeast of [[Puente Alto]] and 21.3 kilometers south-southeast of the center of [[Santiago]]. It is situated at the base of the [[Cajón del Maipo]], and is the home of the [[Concha y Toro]] wine company.

==Demographics==
[[Image:Santa María de Pirque Chile.JPG|thumb|left|200px|Almenar de Pirque High School]]
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Pirque spans an area of {{convert|445.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 16,565 inhabitants (8,384 men and 8,181 women). Of these, 9,651 (58.3%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,914 (41.7%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 45.7% (5,197 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pirque is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Jaime Escudero Ramos (Ind). The communal council has the following members:
* Sergio Ulloa Galdames ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Betzabe Muñoz Herrera ([[Radical Party of Chile|PR]])
* Claudio Arredondo Medina ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Luis Batalle Pedreros ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Leopoldo Pérez Lahsen ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Viviana Petric Meneses ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pirque is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Mr. Osvaldo Andrade ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Mr. Leopoldo Pérez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 29th electoral district, (together with [[Puente Alto]], [[San José de Maipo]] and [[La Pintana]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Soledad Alvear Valenzuela ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Ena Von Baer ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
