'''Huaraz''' is one of twenty provinces of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]]. It was created on August 5, 1857, during the presidency of [[Ramón Castilla]].<ref>Alberto Tauro del Pino, ''Enciclopedia Ilustrada del Perú'', vol. VIII, p. 1220.</ref>  Geographically, the province is located over the [[Callejón de Huaylas]] and the western slopes of the [[Cordillera Negra]].

The Regional Museum of Archaeology is located in the [[Huaraz District|Huaraz district]]. Some other highlights of the province are the Pumacayán hill, the [[hot springs]] of Monterrey (at 6&nbsp;km or 4&nbsp;mi from the city) and the Willkawain archaeological sites,<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Valverde |first1=Guido |last2=Barreto Romero |first2=María Inés |last3=Flores Espinoza |first3=Isabel |last4=Cooper |first4=Alan |last5=Fehren-Schmitz |first5=Lars |last6=Llamas |first6=Bastien |last7=Haak |first7=Wolfgang |date=2016-06-01 |editor-last=Achilli |editor-first=Alessandro |title=Ancient DNA Analysis Suggests Negligible Impact of the Wari Empire Expansion in Peru's Central Coast during the Middle Horizon |journal=PLOS ONE |language=en |volume=11 |issue=6 |article-number=e0155508 |doi=10.1371/journal.pone.0155508 |issn=1932-6203 |pmc=4889149 |pmid=27248693|bibcode=2016PLoSO..1155508V |doi-access=free }}</ref> at {{convert|13|km|0}} to the north of Huaraz, in village of Paria, in the [[Independencia District, Huaraz|Independencia district]].

At 30 kilometres (20&nbsp;mi) from Huaraz, by the route [[Huaraz]]–[[Casma]] that crosses the [[Cordillera Negra]], there is a place named Punta Callan in the summit of this [[mountain range]]. It offers a panoramic sight of the Cordillera Blanca and the Callejón de Huaylas.

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] and [[mestizo]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (63.43%) learnt to speak in childhood, 36.28% of the residents started speaking using the [[Huaylas Quechua|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
