'''San Luis Jilotepeque''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jalapa Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|208.8|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 27,307 inhabitants.

==History==
Jilotepeque is derived from the [[Nahuatl]] word "Xilotepeque" meaning "land of tender corn". The prefix San Luis is the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] reference to [[Louis IX of France]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de San Luis Jilotepeque|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of San Luis Jilotepeque municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/historia-de-el-municipio-de-san-luis-jilotepeque-jalapa/|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The municipality is associated with the archaeological site of El Durazno in [[San Pedro Pinula]], considered a former [[Maya people|Mayan]] fortress. In 1530, it was relocated to the valley of the present-day San Luis Jilotepeque. From 1551 it belonged to the district of [[Chiquimula]], and in 1873 it became part of the newly formed [[Jalapa department]] through government decree No. 170 enacted on 24 November 1873.<ref name="Hist"/>

==Geography==
San Luis Jilotepeque is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jalapa Department|Jalapa]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Luis Jilotepeque (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jalapa/2103__san_luis_jilotepeque/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|208.8|km2}} and forms part of Region IV (Southeastern Guatemala).<ref name="CP"/><ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de San Luis Jilotepeque, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/geografia-de-el-municipio-de-san-luis-jilotepeque-jalapa/|trans-title=Geography of San Luis Jilotepeque municipality|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> The municipal seat is located at the junction of National Route 18, about {{cvt|143|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|41|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Jalapa]]. It borders the municipalities of [[San Diego, Zacapa|San Diego]] and [[San José La Arada]] to the north, [[Ipala, Guatemala|Ipala]] to the east, [[San Manuel Chaparrón]] to the south, and [[San Pedro Pinula]] to the west.<ref name="Geo"/>

It lies at an elevation of {{cvt|789.61|m}} above sea level. The municipality has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|19.63|C}}. The municipality receives about {{cvt|136.27|mm}} of rainfall annually and has 178.87 rainy days per year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jalapa/san-luis-jilotepeque|title=San Luis Jilotepeque|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
San Luis Jilotepeque had an estimated population of 27,307 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 13,411 males and 13,896 females. About 24.9% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 7.5% was over the age of 65 years. About 52.7% of the population was classified as rural, while 47.3% lived in urban areas. Most residents (88.9%) were born in the same municipality.<ref name="CP"/>

Maya (65%) formed the major ethnic group, followed by [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (34.6%). [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (94.3%) was the most spoken language, with small minorities speaking indigenous languages such as [[Kaqchikel language|Kaqchikel]] and [[Mam language|Mam]]. The literacy rate of the municipality was 77.7%.<ref name="CP"/>
