'''Escuque''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 165&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 32,901 inhabitants according to the 2017 census.
