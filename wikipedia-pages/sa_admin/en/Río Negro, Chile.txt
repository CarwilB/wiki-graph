'''Río Negro''' ({{IPA|es|ˈri.o ˈneɣɾo}}, English: ''Black River'') is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Osorno Province]], [[Los Lagos Region]]. The city is located 38&nbsp;km south of [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]] and 6&nbsp;km west of  [[Chile Route 5|Route 5]]. It has an area of 1,266&nbsp;km² and a population of 14,732 inhabitants (7,204 women and 7,528 men according to census 2002).

The commune is bordered on the north by [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]] and [[San Juan de la Costa]], on the east by [[Puerto Octay]], on the west by the Pacific Ocean, and on the south by [[Purranque, Chile|Purranque]].

== History ==
In the 19th century, the commune was a property located between Chahuilco and [[Purranque]] called ''Curileufu'' by the [[Huilliche people|Huilliche]].  The town was founded on lands donated by Jose Miguel Alderete in the year 1896. The first [[alcalde]] (mayor) was Pedro González.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Río Negro spans an area of {{convert|1265.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 14,732 inhabitants (7,528 men and 7,204 women). Of these, 6,583 (44.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 8,149 (55.3%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 8.1% (1294 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Río Negro is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Carlos Schwalm Urzúa ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).  The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Carlos Kusch ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Pedro Carreño ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* Jaime Ramos ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]-[[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Patricio Alvarado (Ind.-[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Juan Saldivia Quintullanca (Ind.-[[Regionalist Party of the Independents|PRI]])
* Rosiel del Río (Ind.-UDI)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Río Negro is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Fidel Espinoza (PS) and Carlos Recondo (UDI) as part of the 56th electoral district, together with [[Puyehue, Chile|Puyehue]], [[Purranque]], [[Puerto Octay]], [[Fresia, Chile|Fresia]], [[Frutillar]], [[Llanquihue, Chile|Llanquihue]], [[Puerto Varas]] and [[Los Muermos]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina (PS) and Carlos Kuschel Silva (RN) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
