'''Cuyabeno Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Sucumbíos Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Tarapoa]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 6,643.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
