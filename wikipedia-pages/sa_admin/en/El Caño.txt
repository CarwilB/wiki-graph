'''El Caño''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Natá District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|86|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,351 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|38.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,936; its population as of 2000 was 3,276.<ref name=inec/>

El Caño is named after a nearby waterfall found in a creek whose waters end in a nearby river.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The Mystery of El Cano, Panama's Unknown Civilization • Neperos |url=https://www.neperos.com/article/sac1e803fe20f6d9 |website=Neperos.com |language=en}}</ref>

==Archaeological significance==
In 2010, at El Caño, a Pre-Columbian cemetery was discovered. It contained numerous preserved bodies and gold artifacts from a pre-Columbian culture.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The Mystery of El Cano, Panama's Unknown Civilization • Neperos |url=https://www.neperos.com/article/sac1e803fe20f6d9 |website=Neperos.com |language=en}}</ref>
