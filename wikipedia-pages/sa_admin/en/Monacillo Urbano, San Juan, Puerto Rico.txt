'''Monacillo Urbano''' is one of 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] in the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], [[Puerto Rico]]. The [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] was originally part of [[Monacillo, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo]]. In 2010, it had a population of 22,342 living in a land area of 3.23 square miles (8.68&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>). Monacillo Urbano is surrounded by [[Gobernador Piñero, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Gobernador Piñero]] barrio to the north, [[El Cinco, San Juan, Puerto Rico|El Cinco]] barrio to the east, [[Monacillo, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo]] barrio to the south, and the municipality of [[Guaynabo, Puerto Rico|Guaynabo]] to the west.<ref>{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó.
