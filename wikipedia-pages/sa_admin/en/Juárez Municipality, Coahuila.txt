'''Juárez''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in Northeastern [[Mexico]] with Don Margarito Garcia as Municipal President (January 2010 – July 2010).<ref>[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]]. Principales resultados por localidad 2005 (ITER). Retrieved on November 9, 2008</ref> The municipal seat lies at [[Juárez, Coahuila|Juárez]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|2971.3|km2|abbr=on}}. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 1,393.<ref>{{cite web|title=Juárez|url=http://www.elocal.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05015a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=November 8, 2008}}{{Dead link|date=February 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
