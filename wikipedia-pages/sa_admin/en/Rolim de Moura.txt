'''Rolim de Moura''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 55,407 (2020) and its area is 1,457.885&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

== History ==
In 1979, the ''Rolim de Moura Colonization Project'' was launched, aimed at the settlement of surplus settlers in the [[Amazon rainforest]] from the extension of the Integrated Colonization Project GY Paraná or Ji-Paraná. This was implemented in this area by [[Instituto Nacional de Colonização e Reforma Agrária|INCRA]], which distributed plots of land in rural areas to thousands of families. From then on, people who arrived by the hundreds began to build the city, initially made up of shacks.

Rolim de Moura was elevated to the category of municipality through State Decree Law nº 71, of August 5, 1983.<br>
Its first mayor was Valdir Raupp de Matos, elected on December 9, 1984, and who took office on January 1, 1985.<ref>[http://rolimdemoura.ro.gov.br/ Prefecture website]</ref>

The name of the city was given in honor of Dom [[Antônio Rolim de Moura Tavares]], Viscount of Azambuja and second governor of the captaincy of [[Mato Grosso]], for his services rendered to the region of the [[Guaporé River|Guaporé]] valley.
