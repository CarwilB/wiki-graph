'''Mário Campos''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Belo Horizonte.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/mario-campos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

== Demographics ==
The population of Mário Campos reached approximately 16,705 in 2025.<ref name="IBGE" />
