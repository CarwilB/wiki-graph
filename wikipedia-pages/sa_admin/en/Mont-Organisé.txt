'''Mont-Organisé''' ({{IPA|fr|mɔ̃t‿ɔʁɡanize}}; {{langx|ht|Montòganize}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Ouanaminthe Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 17,189 inhabitants.

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of two [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Savanette (communal section)|Savanette]], urban and rural, containing the town of Mont-Organisé
* [[Bois Poux]], rural
