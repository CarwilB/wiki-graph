'''Itapecerica''' ({{IPA|pt|itapeˈsɛɾikɐ}}, {{IPA|pt|itapeˈsɛɾika|}}) is a municipality located in the center of the [[states of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The population is 21,761 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|1041|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/18C0 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city belongs to the meso-region of Oeste de Minas and to the micro-region of [[Formiga, Minas Gerais|Formiga]].  It was founded in 1789.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==History==
Candindé, an extinct [[Jê languages#Varieties|Jê language]], was once spoken in the Itapecerica Valley.<ref name="Loukotka">{{cite book |last=Loukotka |first=Čestmír |author-link=Čestmír Loukotka |title=Classification of South American Indian languages |url=https://archive.org/details/classificationof0007louk |url-access=registration |publisher=UCLA Latin American Center |year=1968 |location=Los Angeles}}</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of Itapecerica is located at an elevation of 825 meters east of Formiga and southeast of [[Divinópolis]].  Neighboring  municipalities are:  [[Divinópolis]] (N), [[Cláudio, Minas Gerais|Cláudio]] (E), [[Camacho, Minas Gerais|Camacho]] (S), [[Oliveira, Minas Gerais|Oliveira]] (SE), and [[Formiga, Minas Gerais|Formiga]] (W).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/formiga/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-21 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204111907/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/formiga/ |archive-date=2008-02-04 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
Services, mining, and industry are the most important economic activities.  There are large deposits of [[graphite]] in the region, which is used in making pencils. In 2005 there were 25 extractive industries (mining) employing 404 persons and 89 transformation industries employing 628 persons.  The GDP in 2005 was approximately R$121 million, 9 million reais from taxes, 67 million reais from services, 24 million reais from industry, and 19 million reais from agriculture.  There were 1,203 rural producers on 86,000 hectares of land (2006).  127 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 2,500 persons were involved in agriculture.  The main crops are coffee, sugarcane, and corn.  There were 41,000 head of cattle (2006), a large percentage of which were dairy cattle.  Poultry raising was also important.<ref name="IBGE"/>

There were 4 banks (2007).  The motor vehicle fleet had 2,525 automobiles, 258 trucks, 196 pickup trucks, and 1,155 motorcycles (2007).<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 11 public health clinics and 1 hospital with 59 beds (2005).  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Formiga or Divinópolis.  Educational needs of 4,600 students were met by 19 primary schools, 6 middle schools, and 4 pre-primary schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>

*National ranking: 1514 out of 5,138 municipalities in 2000

*Life expectancy: 72 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of R$223.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.  [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with R$435.00.  The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with R$73.00.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
