'''Girardota''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Girardota is part of [[The Metropolitan Area of the Aburrá Valley]]. Its population was estimated to be 54,439 in 2020.
