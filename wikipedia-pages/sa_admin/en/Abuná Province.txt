'''Abuná''' is one of the five provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Pando Department|Pando]] and is situated in the department's northern central parts. It has its name from [[Abuná River|Río Abuná]], the border river to neighbouring [[Brazil]].

== Location ==
Abuná province is located between 9° 53' and 11° 06' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 27' and 67° 42' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 120–250&nbsp;km from North to South, and 350&nbsp;km from West to East.

The province is situated in the [[Amazon Rainforest|Amazon]] lowlands of Bolivia and borders [[Brazil]] in the North, [[Nicolás Suárez Province]] in the Northwest and West, [[Manuripi Province]] in the South, and [[Federico Román Province]] in the East.

== Population ==
The population of Abuná Province has increased by 40% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 2,652 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 2,996 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 3,475 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2024: 5,439 inhabitants ([[census]])

45.4% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

52.3% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 2.8% speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]], 2.0% [[Quechua language|Quechua]], and 52.8 speak other [[Indigenous people|indigenous]] languages. ''(1992)''

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 47.4%. ''(1992)''

87.6% of the population have no access to electricity, 83.6% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

83.5% of the population are Catholics, 15.4% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipios]]:
*[[Santa Rosa del Abuná Municipality]] - 3,090&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> - 2,097 inhabitants (''2001'')
*[[Ingavi Municipality]] - 4,378&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> - 899 inhabitants (''2001'')
