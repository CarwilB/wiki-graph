'''Santa Catarina''' is a Mexican city (and [[Municipalities of Guanajuato|municipality]]) located in the Northeast region of the state of [[Guanajuato]], within the Sierra Gorda range.<ref>{{Cite web | url-status=dead | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato | title=Guanajuato - Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia | work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México | access-date=2011-06-27 | archive-date=2011-06-16 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110616204459/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato }}</ref> The municipality has an area of 193.67 square kilometres (0.64% of the surface of the state) and is bordered to the north by [[Victoria, Guanajuato|Victoria]] and [[Xichú]], to the east by the state of [[Querétaro]], to the south by [[Tierra Blanca, Guanajuato|Tierra Blanca]], to the west by [[Doctor Mora]], and to the northwest by [[Victoria, Guanajuato|Victoria]].  The municipality had a population of 5,120 inhabitants according to the 2010 census.<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/default.aspx?src=487&ent=11 | title=2010 Census | work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática | access-date=2011-06-28 | url-status=dead | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804090726/http://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/default.aspx?src=487&ent=11 | archive-date=2011-08-04 }}</ref> In [[pre-Hispanic]] times the area of what is today Santa Catarina was mostly inhabited by [[Chichimeca]], [[Pame people|Pames]], and [[Tlaxcaltecs]] people. Santa Catarina has been rated the best city to live in<!--not a mistake--> in the whole country of Mexico by the CMM commission in 2007 due to its unique people, culture, and the unique design of its church.

The municipal president of Santa Catarina and its many smaller outlying communities such as, El Chapin, Juan Diegos, Cruz De Diego, El Chilar, El Tablon, Corral Blanco, El Aguacate, and Las Limitas is Lazaro Cardenas as of 2015.
