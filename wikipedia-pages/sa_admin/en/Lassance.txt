'''Lassance''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  In 2020 the population was 6,503 in a total area of {{cvt|3213|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/lassance/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1953.<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/lassance/ Citybrazil]</ref><ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Lassance is located on federal highway BR-496 at an elevation of 504 meters.  It is south of the nearest population center, [[Pirapora]] and north of [[Corinto, Minas Gerais|Corinto]].  The municipality is crossed by the Rio das Velhas, a tributary of the [[São Francisco River]].  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Buritizeiro]], [[Várzea da Palma]], [[Buenópolis]], and [[Confria]].  Lassance is part of the statistical microregion of Pirapora.  It is 263&nbsp;km. from the state capital of [[Belo Horizonte]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-05 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080705172912/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |archive-date=2008-07-05 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising (35,000 head in 2006), mining, industry, and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$ 36,064,000.  The most important agricultural crops are bananas, papaya, mangoes, rice, sugarcane, manioc, beans and corn.  There were 247 automobiles.  In the rural area there were 450 producers.  The total area of agricultural land was 88,000 hectares in 2006, of which 4,500 ha. were planted in crops.  As of 2006 there were 124 tractors.  In the urban area there were no banking agencies in 2006.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==
This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.

*State ranking: 630 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
