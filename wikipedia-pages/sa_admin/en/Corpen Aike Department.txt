'''Corpen Aike Department''' is one of the seven [[Departments of Argentina|department]]s in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has an area of 26,350 km<sup>2</sup>. The seat of the department is in [[Puerto Santa Cruz]]. It borders the departments of [[Río Chico Department, Santa Cruz|Río Chico]] and [[Magallanes Department|Magallanes]] to the north, [[Lago Argentino Department|Lago Argentino]] to the west, and [[Güer Aike Department|Güer Aike]] to the south, and has coastlines on the [[Atlantic Ocean]] to the east. It is traversed from west to east by the [[Santa Cruz River (Argentina)|Santa Cruz]] and [[Chico River (Santa Cruz)|Chico]] rivers.

== Demographics ==
The department had a population of 15,171 in the final figures from the 2022 Census (INDEC, 2022), which represented a 36.76% increase from the 11,093 inhabitants recorded in the previous 2010 census (INDEC, 2010).<ref name="Census 2022">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos de la Republica Argentine, Census of 16 May 2022.</ref>

== Protected Natural Areas ==
In the Corpen Aike department is located [[Monte León National Park]], covering approximately 62,000 hectares, which was established on October 20, 2004.

On that date, the law creating the new national park was sanctioned, making it at the time the first continental marine park in Argentina.
