'''Aliança''' (''Alliance'') is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], Brazil,  82 kilometres from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Aliança has an estimated population of 37,349 inhabitants ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2025]]).<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Ferreiros, Pernambuco|Ferreiros]] and [[Itambé, Pernambuco|Itambé]]   ('''N''');  [[Nazaré da Mata]]    ('''S''');  [[Condado, Pernambuco|Condado]]  ('''E'''); [[Timbaúba]] and [[Vicência]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 272.73&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 123 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 82&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Aliança''' are based in industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]] (over 948,000 tons); and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
