'''Port-Margot''' ({{IPA|fr|pɔʁ maʁɡo}}; {{langx|ht|Pò Mago}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Borgne Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 36,937 inhabitants.

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of six [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Grande Plaine (communal section)|Grande Plaine]], rural
* [[Bas Petit Borgne]], urban (town of Port-Margot) and rural
* [[Corail (communal section)|Corail]], rural
* [[Haut Petit Borgne]], rural
* [[Bas Quartier]], urban (Bayeux neighborhood) and rural
* [[Bras Gauche]], urban (Petit Bourg de Port-Margot neighborhood) and rural
