'''Tahuanía District''' is one of the four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Atalaya Province|Atalaya]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080102023316/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-01-02 }}''. Retrieved April 7, 2008.</ref>

The district was created by Law No. 9815 of 2 July 1943, during the government of President [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].<ref>[http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/09815.pdf «Ley N° 9815»]</ref>

In this district of the [[Peruvian Amazon]] lives the [[Annex: Indigenous Peoples of Peru|ethnic]] [[Pano people|Pano]] group [[Shipibo-Conibo]] self-named ''Joni''.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Pueblos Indígenas Amazónicos|url=https://www.peruecologico.com.pe/etnias_mapa.htm|access-date=2020-07-18|website=www.peruecologico.com.pe}}</ref>
