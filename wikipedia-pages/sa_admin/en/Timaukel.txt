}}'''Timaukel''' is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[Chile]]an part of [[Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego|Tierra del Fuego Island]]. It is part of [[Tierra del Fuego Province, Chile|Tierra del Fuego Province]] in [[Magallanes Region]]. The commune is administered by the municipality in [[Cameron, Chile|Villa Cameron]], which is the main port and settlement within the commune.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Timaukel spans an area of {{convert|10995.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 423 inhabitants (376 men and 47 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 68% (171 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

It is a distinctly rural commune, almost deserted, most of the population is of [[British Chilean|English origin]],{{Citation needed|date=December 2010}} who live on farms and is devoted to agriculture, there are also some sawmills. In recent years tourism has become important.

==Administration==
As a commune, Timaukel is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012–2016 alcalde is Alfonso Simunovic Ojeda.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Timaukel is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region).
