'''São Bento do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]], pop. 85,421 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/sao-bento-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> located in southern [[Brazil]], in the northeast part of the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], just south of the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]].

== Industry ==

São Bento do Sul is considered to be an industrialized city.

There are many strong industries active in the region but the municipality is best known for its production of [[furniture]] and [[textile]]s.

== Pioneer days ==

São Bento do Sul was first settled by the ''Kolonisations Verein von 1849, in Hamburg'', a 'for profit' colonization enterprise (as opposed to the State of provincial colonization efforts that were also taking place at that time) established in [[Hamburg]], [[Germany]].

Today there are different festivals celebrating the local population's [[immigrant]] roots: [[German people|German]], [[Italian people|Italian]], [[Polish people|Polish]], [[Czech people|Czech]], and others.
