'''Jopala ''' is a [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070405081753/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |archive-date=April 5, 2007 }}</ref>

The municipality covers an area of 170.3 km², and has a total population of 12,131 person.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Jopala (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/puebla/21089__jopala/ |access-date=2025-04-27 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
