The '''Muñoz Municipality''' is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Apure]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 27,542.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Bruzual]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Muñoz Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Apure.zip {{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is named for Major General {{ill|José Cornelio Muñoz|es}}, leader of a division of independence forces during the [[Venezuelan War of Independence]].

==Demographics==
The Muñoz Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 30,197 (up from 26,141 in 2000).  This amounts to 6.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|3.81|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
