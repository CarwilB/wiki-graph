'''Huarmey''' is one of 20 provinces located in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. It takes around 4 hours to arrive to Huarmey from [[Lima]] by bus and its main economic activities include agriculture, fishing, tourism,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Huarmey Province 2022 Top Things to Do - Huarmey Province Travel Guides - Top Recommended Huarmey Province Attraction Tickets, Hotels, Places to Visit, Dining, and Restaurants |url=https://www.trip.com/travel-guide/destination/huarmey-province-1861562/ |access-date=2022-07-15 |website=TRIP.COM |language=en-XX}}</ref> and mineral shipping.

==Political division==
Huarmey is divided into five [[district]]s, which are:
* [[Cochapeti District|Cochapeti]] 
* [[Culebras District|Culebras]] 
* [[Huarmey District|Huarmey]] 
* [[Huayan District|Huayan]] 
* [[Malvas District|Malvas]]
