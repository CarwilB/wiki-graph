'''José María Avilés''' (or: ''Avilez'') is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the western parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Tarija Department|Tarija]].

==Location==
''Avilés'' province is one of six provinces in the Tarija Department. It is located between 21° 28' and 22° 05' [[Latitude|south]] and between 64° 29' and 65° 25' [[Longitude|west]].

The province borders [[Eustaquio Méndez Province]] in the north, [[Potosí Department]] in the west, [[Argentina]] in the south, [[Aniceto Arce Province]] in the south-east, and [[Cercado Province (Tarija)|Cercado Province]] in the north-east.

The province extends over 85&nbsp;km from north to south, and 115&nbsp;km from east to west.

The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]]:
