'''El Guacamayo''' is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
El Guacamayo has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with warm afternoons, cool to comfortable mornings, and very heavy rainfall year-round.
