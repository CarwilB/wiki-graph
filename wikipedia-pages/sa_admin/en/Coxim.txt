'''Coxim''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 33,459 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|6412|km2}}. It was founded in 1729.

It is located at the confluence of the [[Coxim River|Coxim]] and [[Taquari River|Taquari]] rivers. Its economy is based on tourism, fishing and animal husbandry.
