'''Iguatemi''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 16,176&nbsp;(2020) and its area is 2,947&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
