'''Casas Grandes Municipality''' is located in the northern [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Chihuahua (state)|Chihuahua]]. The [[municipal seat]] is the town of Casas Grandes, Chihuahua.

The [[pre-Columbian]] archaeological zone [[Casas Grandes]] and its central site, after which the municipality is named, is located within the municipality's territory. The site of Casas Grandes, alternatively known as Paquimé, is one of the most significant pre-Columbian cultural and archaeological sites in the region of northwestern Mexico and the southwestern United States known in some archaeological contexts as the [[Oasisamerica]] culture area.

==Geography==
The municipality of Casas Grandes is situated in the northwestern sector of Chihuahua state, the largest [[states of Mexico|state of Mexico]] by area. [[Pueblo de Casas Grandes|(Pueblo de) Casas Grandes]], the municipal capital and its most populous ''localidad'' (locality or settlement), is approximately {{convert|359|km|mi}} to the northwest of the state's capital, the city of [[Chihuahua, Chihuahua|Chihuahua]]. Casas Grandes is bounded by the Chihuahuan municipalities of [[Janos Municipality|Janos]] to the north, [[Galeana, Chihuahua|Galeana]], [[Nuevo Casas Grandes Municipality|Nuevo Casas Grandes]] and [[Madera Municipality|Madera]] to the east, and [[Ignacio Zaragoza Municipality|Ignacio Zaragoza]] to the south. On its western flank, Casas Grandes adjoins municipalities within the state of [[Sonora]].<ref>INAFED (2005)</ref>

===Demographics===
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 10,587,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Casas Grandes|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=013|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=22 October 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141022005524/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=013|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 8,413 as of 2005.

As of 2010, the town of Casas Grandes had a population of 5,256.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Casas Grandes, the municipality had 296 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Mata Ortiz|Juan Mata Ortíz (Pearson)]] (1,182) and [[Colonia Juárez, Chihuahua|Colonia Juárez]] (1,035), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
