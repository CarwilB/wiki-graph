'''Nogoyá''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of the province of [[Entre Ríos Province|Entre Ríos]], [[Argentina]].<ref name=":cp">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-admin.php|title=Argentina: Administrative Division|publisher=citypopulation}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book|url=http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|title=CENTRO LATINOAMERICANO Y CARIBEÑO DE DEMOGRAFÍA (CELADE)|publisher=World Bank|year=1970|archive-date=15 January 2019|access-date=5 February 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190115132401/http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|url-status=dead}}</ref> It is the eighth smallest province with an area of {{cvt|4282|km2}} and the seventh least populated, with 39 026 inhabitants according to the 2010 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.entrerios.gov.ar/dec/publicaciones/CENSO%202010/P1-P_entre_rios.xls|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141110195559/http://www.entrerios.gov.ar/dec/publicaciones/CENSO%202010/P1-P_entre_rios.xls|title=Provincia de Entre Ríos|lang=es|format=XLS |archive-date=2014-11-10|date=2014-11-10|access-date=2019-02-05}}</ref>

It is bordered to the west by the Diamante department, to the north by the Paraná and Villaguay departments, to the south by the Victoria and Gualeguay departments and to the east by the Tala department. The department is crossed by the Nogoyá stream.

According to the methodology used by the INDEC for the 1991, 2001 and 2010 censuses, the Nogoyá department comprised 8 localities: Aranguren, Betbeder or Villa Matilde, Don Cristóbal (in the jurisdiction of Don Cristóbal 2 °), Febre, Hernández, Lucas González, Nogoyá, XX of September.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/30000LC121.xls|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071215055925/http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/30000LC121.xls|title=Provincia de Entre Ríos según localidad|lang=es|format=XLS|archive-date=2007-12-15|date=2007-12-15|access-date=2019-02-05}}</ref>
