'''Espírito Santo do Turvo''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].  The population in 2020 was 4,878 and the area is {{Convert|193.655|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="area">{{Cite web |author=IBGE |author-link=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/geociencias/areaterritorial/area.php?nome=Esp%EDrito+Santo+do+Turvo&codigo=&submit.x=26&submit.y=9 |title=Área Territorial Brasileira – Consulta por Município – Resultados |language=Portuguese |trans-title=Brazilian Territorial Area - Browse by Municipality - Results |date=10 October 2002 }}</ref>  The elevation is {{Convert|520|m|ft}}.

==History==
The donation of a tract of land for the construction of a church is the landmark in the history of this city.  On 23 March 1878, after significant growth Espírito Santo do Turvo ({{langx|en|Holy Spirit of Turvo}}) received the title of [[Freguesia (Portugal)|Freguesia]], a secondary [[local administrative unit]] in [[Portugal]], by Law No. 08, and was marked as a Religious Heritage Site.  Seven years later, in March 1885, the Law No. 20 raised it to the level of [[Town]].

==Geography==
Espírito Santo do Turvo is located in the  southern-[[subtropic]]al part of Brazil, at 22 degrees, 41 minutes, 31 second south, and 49 degrees, 25 minutes, 48 seconds west, at an altitude of {{Convert|520|m|ft}}, in the internal part of the [[State of São Paulo]].  It covers an area of {{Convert|193.655|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="area" />

==Economy==
Its [[economy]] is based on [[agriculture]] with large production of [[peanut]]s and [[cotton]].  Espírito Santo do Turvo is an important junction to the south, [[Mato Grosso]], and other regions.

Almost every month some members of the Association of Women of Espírito Santo do Turvo journey  more than 300 kilometers to the São Paulo State capital to exhibit and sell their artisan work.  Their proceeds combined with a donation from the Levy Straus Foundation is being used to fund their headquarters. <ref>{{Cite web |title=Environmental education against poverty |url=https://revistapesquisa.fapesp.br/en/environmental-education-against-poverty/ |access-date=2026-06-10 |website=revistapesquisa.fapesp.br |language=en-US}}</ref>
