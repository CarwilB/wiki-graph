'''Calca''' is one of thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Cusco Region]] in the southern highlands of [[Peru]]. Its seat is [[Calca, Peru|Calca]].

== Geography ==
The province is bounded to the north by the [[La Convención Province|province of La Convención]], to the east by the [[Paucartambo Province|province of Paucartambo]], to the south by the [[Cusco Province|province of Cusco ]] and the [[Quispicanchi Province|province of Quispicanchi]], and to the west by the [[Urubamba Province|province of Urubamba]].

The [[Urubamba River|Willkanuta River]] which flows through the [[Sacred Valley]] is one of the most important rivers of the province.

==Political division==
The province is divided into eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: ''distrito''), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Calca District|Calca]] ([[Calca, Peru|Calca]])
* [[Coya District|Coya]] ([[Coya, Calca|Coya]])
* [[Lamay District|Lamay]] ([[Lamay, Peru|Lamay]])
* [[Lares District|Lares]] ([[Lares, Peru|Lares]])
* [[Pisac District|Pisac]] ([[Pisac]])
* [[San Salvador District|San Salvador]] ([[San Salvador, Peru|San Salvador]])
* [[Taray District|Taray]] ([[Taray, Peru|Taray]])
* [[Yanatile District|Yanatile]] ([[Quebrada Honda, Peru|Quebrada Honda]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (69.92%) learnt to speak in childhood, 29.47% of the residents started speaking in [[Spanish language|Spanish]].<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>

== Archaeological sites ==
The archaeological complex of [[Pisac|P'isaq]] including [[Inti Watana, Calca|Inti Watana]] is one of the prominent sites with    remains of the [[Inca Empire|Inca]] period in the province. Other archaeological sites are [[Chawaytiri]], [[Llamayuq]], [[Qhapaq Kancha]] and [[Mawk'ataray]].
