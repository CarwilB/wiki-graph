'''Triunfo''' is a municipality in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeastern]] [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Pernambuco]]. The estimated population in 2020, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE)]] was 15,243. The area of the municipality is 191.52&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>{{Cite web|title=IBGE Cities: Triunfo|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/triunfo/panorama|access-date=2021-04-27|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and in 2010 the population density was 78 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=":0">{{Cite book|url=http://www.bde.pe.gov.br/ArquivosPerfilMunicipal/TRIUNFO.pdf|title=Perfil Municipal: Triunfo|publisher=Assembleia Legislativa de Pernambuco|year=2016}}</ref>
Triunfo sits at an elevation of {{Convert|1004|m|ft}} in a forested part of the [[Sertão]], and is the highest municipality in Pernambuco.

Far inland and with a milder climate than the surrounding semi-arid plateau, the city has the nickname "Oasis of the Sertão",<ref>{{Cite web|last=Vasconcelos|first=André|date=2016|title=TRIUNFO- PE: A CONSTRUÇÃO DE UM "OÁSIS" NO SERTÃO NORDESTINO|url=http://www.editorarealize.com.br/revistas/conidis/trabalhos/TRABALHO_EV064_MD1_SA14_ID1726_24102016202954.pdf|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181027061550/http://www.editorarealize.com.br/revistas/conidis/trabalhos/TRABALHO_EV064_MD1_SA14_ID1726_24102016202954.pdf|archive-date=2018-10-27|access-date=2018-10-26}}</ref> and tourism is a significant part of the economy. Its current [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] is Luciano Fernando de Sousa (better known as Luciano Bonfim) of the [[Avante (political party)|Avante]] party, elected in [[2020 Brazilian municipal elections|2020]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Luciano Bonfim, do Avante, é eleito prefeito de Triunfo|url=https://g1.globo.com/pe/caruaru-regiao/noticia/2020/11/15/luciano-bonfim-do-avante-e-eleito-prefeito-de-triunfo.ghtml|access-date=2021-04-28|website=G1|date=15 November 2020 |language=pt-br}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Triunfo''' are tourism, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]];  and plantations of [[guava]]s and [[sugarcane]].
