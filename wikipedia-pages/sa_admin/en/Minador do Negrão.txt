'''Minador do Negrão''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[minɐˈdo ˈdʊ neˈɡɐ̃w]}})  is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 5,322 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/minador-do-negrao/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|167|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?lang=_EN&codmun=270530&search=alagoas|minador-do-negrao] (in Portuguese)</ref>
