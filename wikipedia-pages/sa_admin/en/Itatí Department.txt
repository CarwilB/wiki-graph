'''Itatí Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 8,774 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|890|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Itatí, Corrientes|Itatí]], which is located around {{cvt|1,095|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
