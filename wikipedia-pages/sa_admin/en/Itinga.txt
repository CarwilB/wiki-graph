'''Itinga''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|iˈt͡ʃĩɡɐ}}) is a Brazilian municipality located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in the [[Jequitinhonha River]] valley. {{As of|2020}} the population was 15,022 in a total area of 1,641&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="ibge"/> The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Jequitinhonha]] and to the microregion of [[Araçuaí]]. The elevation of the municipal seat is 269 meters. It became a municipality in 1943. Between the municipalities of [[Araçuaí]] and Itinga is located the largest [[lithium]] mine in Brazil,<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Lítio pode impulsionar o desenvolvimento do Vale do Jequitinhonha - Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |url=https://www.almg.gov.br/comunicacao/noticias/arquivos/Litio-pode-impulsionar-o-desenvolvimento-do-Vale-do-Jequitinhonha/#:~:text=Polo%20da%20extra%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20l%C3%ADtio,para%20reverter%20essa%20contradi%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20hist%C3%B3rica. |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Portal da Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-19 |title=A corrida pela riqueza do lítio na pequena Araçuaí - revista piauí |url=https://piaui.folha.uol.com.br/a-corrida-pela-riqueza-do-litio-na-pequena-aracuai/ |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=revista piauí - _pra quem tem um clique a mais}}</ref> explored and extracted by Sigma Lithium Resources.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sigma Lithium Resources |url=https://sigmalithiumresources.com/ |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Sigma Lithium |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name "Itinga" is a term of [[Tupi language|Tupi]] origin that means "white water", through the combination of the terms ''<nowiki/>'y'' ("water") and ''ting'' ("white").<ref>{{Cite web | title=CURSO DE TUPI ANTIGO | url=https://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060207160819/http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm | access-date=2026-01-18 | archive-date=2006-02-07 | url-status=live }}</ref>

== History ==
The lands where the municipality of Itinga is located today were inhabited by the [[Aimoré|Botocudo]] tribes. Rock inscriptions by these groups are found in caves in the municipality.<ref name="Tecnologia">{{Cite web |last=Tecnologia |first=Digitaliza |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Itinga |url=https://itinga.mg.gov.br/cidade |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=itinga.mg.gov.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Non-indigenous settlement in the area began in 1810, when [[Alférez|''alferes'']] Julião Fernandes Leão set up barracks along the Jequitinhonha River. Later, landowners built a chapel dedicated to [[Anthony of Padua|Saint Anthony of Padua]], which gave rise to the city. Settlement in the area was encouraged due to its location on the banks of the Jequitinhonha River, as it was a meeting point for ''[[tropeiro]]s'' and canoeists, and because it was home to a textile factory that operated between 1880 and 1928.<ref name="Tecnologia"/>

Itinga was part of Araçuaí until 1943, when it became a municipality.<ref name="Tecnologia"/>

== Geography ==
According to the regional division in force since 2017, established by the IBGE, the municipality belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]] and [[Immediate Geographic Region of Araçuaí]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2017">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |title=Base de dados por municípios das Regiões Geográficas Imediatas e Intermediárias do Brasil |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Until then, with the divisions into microregions and mesoregions in force, it was part of the microregion of Araçuaí, which in turn was included in the [[Jequitinhonha (mesoregion)|mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

The city is crossed by the Jequitinhonha River and is accessed via the [[BR-367 (Brazil highway)|BR-367]] highway.

== Economy ==
The city's [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] is around R$148.3 million [[Brazilian real|reais]], with 47.9% of the added value coming from public administration, followed by services (28.7%), industry (13.3%) and agriculture (10.2%). The [[Gross domestic product|GDP per capita]] of Itinga is R$9.8 thousand.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Economia de Itinga - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/itinga---mg |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref>

== Culture ==
Itinga is renowned for its clay crafts, especially in the village of Pasmadinho, which is a town made up of artisans who produce and sell clay pots and utensils.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tolentino |first=Eustáquio |date=28 December 2018 |title=Eustáquio Tolentino Espinosa: 2115 - A arte e o amor transformam Pasmadinho |url=https://eustaquiotolentinoespinosa.blogspot.com/2018/12/2115-arte-e-o-amor-transformam.html |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=Eustáquio Tolentino Espinosa}}</ref> [[:pt:Ulisses Mendes|Ulisses Mendes]], one of the main artisans of the Jequitinhonha Valley, lives in the town.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Brasil |first=Arte Popular Do |title=Arte Popular do Brasil: Ulisses Mendes |url=https://artepopularbrasil.blogspot.com/2010/12/ulisses-mendes.html |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=Arte Popular do Brasil}}</ref>

The city's anniversary on December 31 is the biggest [[New Year's Eve]] celebration in the region, with nationally famous artists performing.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Portal Minas |title=Portal Minas Gerais - ANIVERSARIO DA CIDADE DE ITINGA |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/eventos/padre-paraiso/aniversario-da-ciadade-de-itinga |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref> The city also celebrates Carnival<ref>{{Cite web |title=Itinga Vale Do Jequitinhonha - CarnaItinga 2023! 🎉 #CarnaItinga2023 #itingavaledojequi {{!}} Facebook |url=https://www.facebook.com/ItingaValeDoJequi/photos/carnaitinga-2023-carnaitinga2023-itingavaledojequi/6159483027430431/ |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=www.facebook.com |language=en}}</ref> and, in September, the [[:pt:Nossa Senhora da Ajuda|Nossa Senhora da Ajuda]] festival transforms the streets into open-air markets during the day, along with musical performances at night.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Instagram |url=https://www.instagram.com/bosquinhoitinga/reel/C-DQ3iKOpou/ |access-date=2024-08-24 |website=www.instagram.com}}</ref>
