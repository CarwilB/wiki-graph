'''Catas Altas da Noruega''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] [[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Conselheiro Lafaiete]]. As of 2025, the estimated population was 3,124.<ref name="IBGE" />
