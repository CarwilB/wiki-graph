'''Tununguá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the subregion of the [[Western Boyacá Province]]. Tununguá is situated in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] at a relatively low altitude of {{convert|1246|m|ft}}. The municipality borders the department of [[Santander Department|Santander]] in the north and east and the Boyacá municipalities [[Pauna]] (south and west) and [[Briceño, Boyacá|Briceño]] (south).<ref name=websiteTunungua>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tunungua-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tununguá]</ref>

== History ==
Tununguá is located in the valley of [[Chiquinquirá]], a town {{convert|53|km|mi}} to the east and the area was inhabited before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] (east) and [[Muzo people|Muzo]].

Tununguá is named after ''[[cacique]]'' Tununguá, who was said to be a descendant of the children of ''Fura'' and ''Tena'', from the Muzo myth [[Muzo people|Furatena]].<ref name=websiteTunungua/><ref name=EtymTunungua>{{in lang|es}} [http://historiadeboyacapueblos.weebly.com/significado-de-boyacaacute-y-los-nombres-de-los-pueblos-en-lengua-chibcha.html Etymology Tununguá]</ref><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Municipalities Boyacá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

Tununguá formed a larger municipality with neighbouring Briceño and was properly founded on November 30, 1962.<ref name=websiteTunungua/>

== Economy ==
Around 85% of the economy of Tununguá comes from [[agriculture]]; main products [[coffee]], [[guayaba]], [[banana]]s, [[yuca]], [[orange (fruit)|oranges]], [[mandarin orange|mandarines]], [[lemon]] and others at smaller scale such as [[guanábana]], [[peanut]]s and [[cocoa bean|cacao]]. The remaining 15% comes from [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteTunungua/>
