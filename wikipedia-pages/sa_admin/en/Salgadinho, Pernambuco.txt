'''Salgadinho''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[sɐwɡɐˈdĩj̃u]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 124.6&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 343,200 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/salgadinho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[João Alfredo]]   ('''N''');  [[Passira]]    ('''S''');  [[Limoeiro]]   ('''E''');   [[Surubim]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 88.81&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 232 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 27.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 124.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Salgadinho''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[goats]] and [[poultry]].
