'''Tempoal''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. The municipal seat is [[Tempoal de Sánchez, Veracruz|Tempoal de Sánchez]].

In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 33,107 
(down from 35,600 in 1995), 
of whom 12,237 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tempoal|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30161a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=6 November 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617081548/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30161a.htm|archivedate=17 June 2011}}</ref> 

Of the municipality's inhabitants, 3252 (9.33%) spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Wastek language|Wastek]] (Huasteco); the municipality's name comes from the Wastek ''tam-puhal'', meaning "place of toads".<ref>https://www.tempoal.gob.mx/tu-municipio/nomenclatura</ref> 

The municipality of Tempoal covers a total surface area of {{cvt|1487.15|km2}}.
