'''Valença''' ({{IPA|pt|vaˈlẽsɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was estimated at 76,869 in 2020 and its area is {{convert|1,305|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>  The city is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Valença]].

Valença has five districts: ''Conservatória'' ("City of Serenades"), ''Barão de Juparanã'' ("City of the Barons),'' Parapeúna'', ''Santa Isabel do Rio Preto'' and ''Pentagna''.
Today its economy is geared especially for agriculture and the existing university center in the municipal headquarters.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|5952|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra da Concórdia State Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra da Concórdia – Inea}}|title=Parque Estadual da Serra da Concórdia|publisher=Inea: Instituto Estadual do Ambiente|url=http://www.inea.rj.gov.br/Portal/Agendas/BIODIVERSIDADEEAREASPROTEGIDAS/UnidadesdeConservacao/INEA_008599#/Sobreoparque|access-date=2017-01-18|archive-date=2017-01-19|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170119053741/http://www.inea.rj.gov.br/Portal/Agendas/BIODIVERSIDADEEAREASPROTEGIDAS/UnidadesdeConservacao/INEA_008599#/Sobreoparque|url-status=dead}}</ref>
