==History==
On 22 April 1500 a Portuguese fleet under the command of the explorer [[Pedro Álvares Cabral]], seen by many as the first European to arrive in Brazil, recorded their sighting of [[Monte Pascoal]] which became a European settlement and, subsequently, the municipality of [[Prado (Bahia)|Prado]].  Itamaraju became and until 1961 remained a piece of the territory of Prado.  On 5 October 1961 Itamaraju formally obtained municipal autonomy, its separation from Prado led by José Gomes de Almeida and Antônio Fontes Mascarenhas.
