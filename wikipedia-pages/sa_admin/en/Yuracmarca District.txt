'''Yuracmarca''' or '''Yuraq Marka''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''yuraq'' white, ''marka'' village, "white village") is one of 10 districts of the [[Huaylas Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of Yuracmarca.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://web.archive.org/web/20070521203700/http://www.muniyuracmarca.gob.pe/ Official web site of the Yuracmarca district] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070521203700/http://www.muniyuracmarca.gob.pe |date=May 21, 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The district is located in the eastern part of the province at an elevation of 1,518m, 122&nbsp;km from the regions capital [[Huaraz]] and 55&nbsp;km from the province's capital [[Caraz]].

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (50.35%) learnt to speak in childhood, 49.19% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
