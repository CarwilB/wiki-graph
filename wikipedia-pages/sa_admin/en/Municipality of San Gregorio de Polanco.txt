The '''municipality of San Gregorio de Polanco''' is one of the municipalities of [[Tacuarembó Department]], Uruguay, established on 15 March 2010. Seated in the [[San Gregorio de Polanco|town of the same name]], the municipality is situated north of the [[Río Negro (Uruguay)|Negro River]] and west of the [[Tacuarembó River]]. It is known as "Ciudad Museo", due to its extensive open-air visual arts.

== History ==
The municipality of San Gregorio de Polanco was created by Law No. 18,653 of 15 March 2010, as part of Tacuarembó Department, that intended to cover the electoral constituency identified by series TED,<ref name=Law18653 /> the same territory as the Ninth Judicial Section of Tacuarembó. This new entity created under the new system of second level administrative divisions of Uruguay, replaced the former Non-Autonomous Local Board of San Gregorio de Polanco,<ref name=DiarioOficialn /> which the former system was criticized due to their lack of self-governance and submission to departmental authorities.<ref name=CatolicaPanorama />

== Geography ==
The municipality, part of [[Tacuarembó Department]], is located at its south side, north of the [[Río Negro (Uruguay)|Negro River]] and west of the [[Tacuarembó River]]. The municipality has a total area of 444.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and it has 3722 inhabitants according to the 2011 national census, with a population density of 8.4 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name=PerfilO />

==Symbols==
The municipality of San Gregorio de Polanco does not possess a traditional [[coat of arms]], utilizing a stylized logo for official identification instead. The logo consists of three disconnected, pointed rectangles in yellow, blue, and green, each bearing one of the letters "SGP." This design is intended to reflect the artistic identity of the town.<ref name="FlagLogo">{{cite web|url=https://www.crwflags.com/Fotw/flags/uy-ta-.html|title=Municipalities of Tacuarembó Department (Uruguay)|first=Rob|last=Raeside|website=CRW Flags|date=3 January 2026}}</ref>

The municipal flag consists of a white field with the logo centered. As of 2015, the version displayed at the municipal building included the text ''"MUNICIPIO SAN GREGORIO DE POLANCO''" above the logo and the motto "''Ciudad Museo''" centered below.<ref name="FlagLogo"/>

== Settlements ==
The following settlements are part of this municipality:
* [[San Gregorio de Polanco]] (seat)
* Paso Hondo
* Los Furtados
* Rolón
* Cañadas del Estado
* Rincón de Alonso
* Paso Real

== Authorities ==
The authority of the municipality is the Municipal Council, integrated by the Mayor (presiding over it) and four Councilors, all of whom are elected by direct popular vote every five years. According to the law, the Council is responsible for proposing local budgetary programs, managing [[public services]], and ensuring the preservation of local historical sites. Additionally, the Council is required to hold annual public hearings to promote transparency and [[citizen participation]] in [[local governance]].<ref name=Law19272 />
