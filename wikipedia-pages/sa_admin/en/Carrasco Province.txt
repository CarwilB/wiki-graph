'''Carrasco''' is a province in the [[Cochabamba Department]] in central [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[Totora, Cochabamba|Totora]].

== The people ==
The people are predominantly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechuas|Quechuan]] descent.<ref>obd.descentralizacion.gov.bo</ref> There are also groups of [[Yuracaré people|Yuracaré]] along [[Chapare River]] in the municipalities Chimoré and Puerto Villarroel.<ref>[http://www.amazonia.bo/mas_detalle_proi.php?id_contenido=29 www.amazonia.bo] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110209054907/http://www.amazonia.bo/mas_detalle_proi.php?id_contenido=29 |date=2011-02-09 }}</ref>
