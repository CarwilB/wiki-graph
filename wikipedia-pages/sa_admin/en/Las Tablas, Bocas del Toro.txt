'''Las Tablas''' is a [[town]] and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in the [[Changuinola District]] of [[Bocas del Toro Province]] of [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|97.3|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 9,286 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|95.4|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 40 of April 30, 2003.<ref name=inec/>
