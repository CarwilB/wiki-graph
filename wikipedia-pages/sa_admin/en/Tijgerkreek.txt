'''Tijgerkreek''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Saramacca District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 3,244, the majority being [[Javanese Surinamese|Javanese]], and [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]].<ref name="census"/> The resort is mainly agricultural. In the 21st century, there has been an emphasis on [[peanut]]s. The development of a modern agricultural centre is being planned.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The resort is named after the creek Tijgerkreek which is a tributary of the [[Saramacca River]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.delpher.nl/nl/kranten/view?query=tijgerkreek&coll=ddd&sortfield=date&identifier=ddd:010509810:mpeg21:a0007&resultsidentifier=ddd:010509810:mpeg21:a0007&rowid=4 |title=Binnenlandse Berigten |work=De West-Indiër |date=29 July 1874 |language=nl |access-date=23 June 2022}}</ref> however the main settlement is '''Sidoredjo''' which was incorporated as a village in 1937.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.delpher.nl/nl/kranten/view?query=Sidoredjo&coll=ddd&page=1&facets%5Bspatial%5D%5B%5D=Suriname&sortfield=date&identifier=ddd:010345792:mpeg21:a0071&resultsidentifier=ddd:010345792:mpeg21:a0071&rowid=2 |title=Gewestelijk bestuur |work=Suriname : koloniaal nieuws- en advertentieblad |date=22 May 1939 |language=nl |access-date=23 June 2022}}</ref>
