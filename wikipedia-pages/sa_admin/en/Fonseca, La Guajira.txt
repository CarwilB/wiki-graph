'''Fonseca''' is a municipality located in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]]. The town celebrates the [[Festival del Retorno]] in honor of [[Augustine of Hippo|St Augustine]] with religious celebrations, [[vallenato]] music events and others.

==Geography==
The municipality of Fonseca has a total area of {{convert|622|km2|sqmi|0|disp=or}} at an altitude of {{convert|11|m|ft|0|disp=or}} above sea level at the seat of the municipality. The municipality is on a depression in the valley of the [[Ranchería River]] which flows through the municipality from west to east, between the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] and the [[Serranía del Perijá]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Gobernación de la Guajira |url=https://laguajira.gov.co/municipios/fonseca.htm |access-date=2026-05-17 |website=laguajira.gov.co}}</ref><ref>[http://fonseca-guajira.gov.co/apc-aa-files/62613232343862363735333365653231/PLAN_DE_DESARROLLO_2004___2007__FONSECA.pdf {{in lang|es}} Fonseca Developing Plan 2004: Area and Location]{{Dead link|date=December 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Información del Municipio |url=https://www.fonseca-guajira.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Informacion-del-Municipio.aspx |access-date=2026-05-17 |website=www.fonseca-guajira.gov.co |language=es}}</ref>

Fonseca's northern borders meet the municipality of [[Riohacha]] and the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta mountain range; to the south, the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]] and the Serranía del Perijá mountains; to the east, the municipality of [[Barrancas, La Guajira|Barrancas]]; and to the west, the municipality of [[San Juan del Cesar]].<ref name=":1" />

The average temperature throughout the year is {{convert|28|°C|1|disp=or}}, varying according to altitude due to the mountainous environment.<ref name=":1" /> The municipality's climate ranges from [[semi-arid climate#Hot semi-arid climates|hot semi-arid]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''BSh''), as at the weather station of La Paulina, to [[tropical savanna climate|tropical savanna]] (''Aw'') in wetter locations. There is a long, though not intense, [[wet season]] from April to November and a [[dry season]] from December to March with very little or no rain.

==History==
The area of Fonseca has been inhabited by different indigenous groups including [[Chimila people|Chimila]], [[Tupe people|Tupe]], [[Wayuu]], [[Cariachile people]] and [[Motilon people|Motilon]].<ref name=":0" />

The date of Fonseca's original founding is uncertain, and there are two hypotheses about the foundation of Fonseca. One suggests that it was the [[Catalans|Catalan]] colonizer Agustín Fonseca and, and the other, that it was the Italian José Agustín Parodi.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co">{{Cite web |title=Gobernación de la Guajira |url=https://laguajira.gov.co/municipios/fonseca.htm |access-date=2026-05-17 |website=laguajira.gov.co}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Pasado, Presente y Futuro |url=https://www.fonseca-guajira.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Pasado-Presente-y-Futuro.aspx |access-date=2026-05-17 |website=www.fonseca-guajira.gov.co |language=es}}</ref>

Agustín Fonseca was the leader of a group of adventurers who settled by the Ranchería river, until a rainy season lead to the river flooding the area and bringing diseases. They then moved to a dryer location, on what is supposedly the present-day main plaza of Fonseca.

José Agustín Parodi was a captain of the Spanish Crown and arrived by orders of the [[Spanish monarchy]].<ref name="ReferenceA">{{Cite web |url=http://fonseca-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |title=Fonseca: History |language=es |access-date=2008-03-26 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120709043157/http://fonseca-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |archive-date=2012-07-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 1773, Fonseca was registered as a jurisdiction of [[Santa Marta]]. On June 13, 1829, Decree 1954 established Fonseca as a municipality of the Intendencia of La Guajira.<ref name="ReferenceA" /><ref name=":0" /> In 1954, La Guajira was re-established as an Intendencia and, in 1964, La Guajira separated from Magdalena and became its own Department.<ref>{{Cite web |title=La Guajira – SOCIEDAD GEOGRÁFICA DE COLOMBIA |url=https://sogeocol.edu.co/esgeo/departamentos/la-guajira/ |access-date=2026-05-17 |language=es-CO}}</ref>

===Colombian armed conflict===

{{Main|Colombian armed conflict}}

Since the 1970s, Barrancas has been influenced by the [[Colombian armed conflict]] because of its strategic location between the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta, the Serranía del Perijá mountain ranges and the border with Venezuela. The [[Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia]] (FARC), through its [[Caribbean Bloc of the FARC-EP|Caribbean Bloc]]'s 59th, 19th and 41st fronts, and the [[National Liberation Army (Colombia)|National Liberation Army]] (ELN) guerrilla [[Gustavo Palmesano Front]] have practiced selective assassinations, kidnappings, extortions, forcedly recruitments, town sieges, arms and illegal drugs trafficking among others against the Colombian government and the civilian population.<ref name="derechoshumanos.gov.co">{{Cite web |title=Guajira |url=http://www.derechoshumanos.gov.co/observatorio/departamentos/2007/guajira.pdf |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20080529031021/http://www.derechoshumanos.gov.co/observatorio/departamentos/2007/guajira.pdf |archive-date=2008-05-29 |access-date=2026-05-17 |website=www.derechoshumanos.gov.co}}</ref>

The [[United Self-Defense Forces of Colombia]] (AUC) appeared in the area in the early 2000s led by alias [[Jorge 40]]. After a violent presence in the area, but also dissipating the guerrilla presence, the AUC demobilized in 2006.<ref name="derechoshumanos.gov.co"/>

==Politics==

===Administrative divisions===

Fonseca comprises three {{lang|es|corregimientos}}: Conejo, El Hatico and Sitio Nuevo. It also has eight {{lang|es|Inspecciones de Policía}}: Bangañitas, El Confuso, Los Altos, Sabaneta, Los Pondores, Cardonal, Trigo and Cañaboba. There are twenty {{lang|es|veredas}}: El Porvenir, El Potrero, Jaguey, Puyalito, El Puy, Potrerito, Los Toquitos, Hatico Viejo, La Yaya, San Agustín, Puerto López, Las Bendiciones, Las Marimondas, Las Colonias, La Villa, Guamachal, Mamarongo, El Chorro and Mamonal.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |date=2004 |title=Plan de Desarrollo, Municipio de Fonseca |url=https://repositoriocdim.esap.edu.co/server/api/core/bitstreams/ae8df0a6-68b5-4f1e-99f0-e23a784939d7/content |url-status=live |access-date=2026-05-15 |website=Repositorio CDIM}}</ref>

It has 32 urban neighborhoods, and several rural communities including La Mana, La Union, Arroyo Hondo, Sabana el Medio San Agustin, El Puy, El Toco, Los Roquitos and others. There is one indigenous reserve called [[Resguardo Indígena de Mayabangloma]].<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |date=2024-06-03 |title=Fonseca, Potencia del Desarrollo |url=https://www.fonseca-guajira.gov.co/Transparencia/PlaneacionGestionyControl/PLAN%20DE%20DESARROLLO%20MUNICIPAL%20FONSECA%20POTENCIA%20DEL%20DESARROLLO%20%202024-2027.pdf |url-status=live |access-date=2026-05-16 |website=fonseca-guajira.gov.co}}</ref>

==Culture==

Fonseca celebrates the ''[[Festival del Retorno]]'' (Festival of the Return) every year in which people from Fonseca living in other towns, regions or countries are celebrated for their return to town.<ref name="mifonseca.net">[http://www.mifonseca.net/joomla/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=16&Itemid=28 {{in lang|es}} mifonseca.net: Festival del Retorno] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070225033833/http://www.mifonseca.net/joomla/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=16&Itemid=28 |date=2007-02-25 }}</ref>
