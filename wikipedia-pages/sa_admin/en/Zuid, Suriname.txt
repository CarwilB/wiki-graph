'''Zuid''' (English: South) is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Para District]]. Its population at the 2012 census was 6,113. The main ethnic groups are [[Indigenous peoples in Suriname|Indigenous]], [[Afro-Surinamese people|Creoles]], and mixed race.<ref name="census"/>

Villages which are located in Zuid are [[Berlijn, Suriname|Berlijn]], [[Cabendadorp]], [[Hollandse Kamp]], [[Matta, Suriname|Matta]], [[Onverdacht]], [[Pikin Saron]], [[Sabakoe]], [[Witsanti]] and [[Zanderij]] which is near the [[Johan Adolf Pengel International Airport]], the main airport of [[Suriname]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/302ges/archi161.html|title=Samenvattingen|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=18 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Republiek==
{{anchor|Republiek}}Republiek is a village, and a holiday resort in Zuid. Republiek consists of the two wood plantation ''Mijnhoop'' and ''Valkenburg'' which were merged in 1884 into Republiek.<ref name="intro">{{cite web|url=https://www.republiek.sr/intro.html|title=Plantage Republiek|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Republiek.sr}}</ref> The name is based on Post Republiek, a [[outpost (military)|military outpost]] which already existed in 1793. The military outpost was manned by 24 [[Freedman|freed slaves]] at the time.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/kemp009siri01_01/kemp009siri01_01_0003.php|title=Surinaamsche Staatkundige Almanach voor den Jaare 1793|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1905, the first part of the [[Lawa Railway]] between [[Paramaribo]] and Republiek opened.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://groenroodwit.nl/begin-en-eindstation-onverwacht/|title=Begin- en eindstation Onverwacht|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Groen Rood Wit|date=6 April 2013 }}</ref> The railway station turned Republiek in a holiday resort.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/kemp009siri01_01/kemp009siri01_01_0003.php|title=Sirito. 50 Surinaamse vertellingen|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1987, the train station closed, but Republiek remained a holiday resort.<ref name="Snijders">Armand Snijders: [http://www.parbode.com/reportage/item/497-de-flop-van-lely De flop van Lely.] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160417204301/http://www.parbode.com/reportage/item/497-de-flop-van-lely |date=2016-04-17 }} Parbode, Surinames Magazine, 1 April 2008.</ref> The village is home to about 50 people.<ref name="intro"/> Since 1933, Republiek provides the drinking water for [[Paramaribo]] and [[Wanica District|Wanica]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.swm.sr/over-ons/geschiedenis/meer-over-geschiedenis/|title=Meer over geschiedenis|website=Surinaamsche Waterleiding Maatschappij|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=4 April 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220404144444/https://www.swm.sr/over-ons/geschiedenis/meer-over-geschiedenis/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
