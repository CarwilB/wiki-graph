'''Abriaquí''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Its population was 4,200 in 2002. 

==Climate==
Abriaquí has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cfb) with heavy rainfall year-round.
