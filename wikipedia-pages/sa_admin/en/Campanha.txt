'''Campanha''' is a town ''(município)'' in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 its population was estimated at 16,762 inhabitants.  The town is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Campanha]].
