'''Almirante Brown''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of the [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], located at the south of the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area, at coordinates {{coord|34|47|S|58|24|W}}.

It has an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">129.33&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(49.9&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span> and 555,731 inhabitants ({{census-ar|2010}}),<ref name=Cen2010 /> and its capital is [[Adrogué]].

==Name==
The partido is named after [[Ireland|Irish]]-born General [[William Brown (admiral)|William Brown]] who led the Argentine navy in the [[Argentine War of Independence]] and helped the country gain its independence from the [[Spanish Empire]].
