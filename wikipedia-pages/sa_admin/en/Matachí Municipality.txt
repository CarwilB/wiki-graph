'''Matachí''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Matachí]]. The municipality covers an area of 829.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 3,104,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Matachí|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=043|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305011028/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=043|url-status=dead}}</ref> down from 3,169 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Matachí|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08043a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223203729/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08043a.htm|archive-date=February 23, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Matachí had a population of 1,710.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Matachí, the municipality had 78 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
