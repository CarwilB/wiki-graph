'''Aracoiaba''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Ceará]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region]] of [[Brazil]]. With an area of {{Convert|643.988|km²|sp=us}}, of which {{Convert|6.6403|km²}} is urban, it is located 79 km from [[Fortaleza]], the state capital, and 1,613 km from [[Brasília]], the federal capital. Its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 demographic census]] was 25,553 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), ranking as the 74th most populous municipality in the state of Ceará.

== Geography ==
The territory of Aracoiaba covers {{Convert|643.988|km²|sp=us}},<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |title=Brasil / Ceará / Aracoiaba |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/panorama |access-date=2025-06-18}}</ref> of which {{Convert|6.6403|km²}} constitutes the urban area.<ref name="Tabela/8418">{{cite web |year=2019 |title=Tabela 8418 - Áreas urbanizadas, Loteamento vazio, Área total mapeada e Subcategorias |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/8418#/n3/23/n6/2301208/v/12749/p/all/d/v12749%204/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=16 December 2024 |publisher=IBGE |language=pt-br}}</ref> It sits at an average altitude of 102 meters above [[sea level]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Lino |first=Cristina Bezerra |date=30 January 2020 |title=O acesso à informação como instrumento de controle social: a aplicação da lei de acesso à informação nos municípios de Aracoiaba e Barreira |url=https://repositorio.unilab.edu.br/jspui/handle/123456789/4199 |journal=Universidade da Integração Internacional da Lusofonia Afro-Brasileira |page=13 |access-date=18 December 2024}}</ref> Aracoiaba borders these municipalities: to the north, [[Baturité]], [[Redenção, Ceará|Redenção]] and [[Barreira, Ceará|Barreira]]; to the south, [[Ibaretama]]; to the east, [[Ocara]]; and to the west, Baturité again.<ref name="Oliveira2007">{{cite journal |url=https://repositorio.ufc.br/bitstream/riufc/45704/1/2007_tcc_wmpoliveira.pdf |title=Entrando em foco: O desenvolvimento de uma empresa pelas lentes do posicionamento |date=2007 |access-date=December 17, 2024 |journal=Universidade Federal do Ceará |last=Oliveira |first=Waldiana Mariana Paz de |location=Fortaleza |page=30}}</ref> These lands were granted to the captain-major in 1735, who settled there and prospered, attracting other residents to the region.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.municipios-ce.com.br/dados_cli_14.html|title=Prefeitura Municipal de Aracoiaba|access-date=2024-12-17|website=www.municipios-ce.com.br}}</ref> The city is located 79 km from the state capital [[Fortaleza]],<ref name="distancia">{{cite web |title=Distância entre Aracoiaba - Ceará e Fortaleza - CE |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-aracoiaba-ce-brazil-ate-fortaleza-ce-brazil |access-date=14 December 2024}}</ref> and 1,613 km from the federal capital [[Brasília]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Distância entre Brasília e Aracoiaba |url=https://www.distanciasentrecidades.com/pesquisa?from=Brasilia%2C%20Brazil&to=Aracoiaba%2C%20Brazil |access-date=25 July 2014}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
In the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], the municipality had a population of 25,553 inhabitants (54.1% living in the urban area),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/c1/all/c2/all/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c1,t+c2/result|title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação do domicílio e sexo|date=2022|access-date=December 22, 2024|author=IBGE}}</ref> the municipality ranked only 74th in the state that year,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122232|title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2022|date=2022|access-date=2024-09-06|author=IBGE}}</ref> with 50.18% female and 49.82% male,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/c2/all/c287/100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/result|title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade|date=2022|access-date=2024-12-16|author=IBGE}}</ref> resulting in a sex ratio of 99.27 (9,927 men for every 10,000 women),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122248|title=População residente / Sexo / Razão de sexo|date=2022|access-date=2024-09-06|author=IBGE}}</ref> compared to 25,391 inhabitants in the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]], when it held the 75th state position.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true&tipo=ranking&indicador=27665|title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2010|date=2010|access-date=2024-09-06|author=IBGE}}</ref> Between the censuses of 2010 and 2022, the population of Aracoiaba registered a growth of just over 0.6%, with an annual geometric growth rate of 0.07%.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Taxa de crescimento geométrico ( Unidade: % ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122278 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Regarding [[age group]] in the 2022 census, 67.5% of the inhabitants were between 15 and 64 years old, 20.9% were under fifteen, and 11.61% were 65 or older.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/c2/6794/c287/6653,49108,49109,60040,60041,93070,93084,93085,93086,93087,93088,93089,93090,93091,93092,93093,93094,93095,93096,93097,93098,100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/result|title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade|date=2022|access-date=December 22, 2024|author=IBGE}}</ref> The [[population density]] in 2022 was 39.68 inhabitants per square kilometer (eighty-ninth highest in Ceará),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122231|title=Território / Densidade demográfica ( Unidade: habitantes por quilômetro quadrado )|access-date=2024-09-06|author=IBGE}}</ref> with an average of 3.01 inhabitants per household.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4712#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/d/v5930%202/l/v,p,t/result|title=Tabela 4712 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, Moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados e Média de moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados|access-date=2024-01-06|author=IBGE}}</ref>

The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) was considered medium, according to data from the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). According to the 2010 report published in 2013, its value was 0.615, ranking 87th in the state (out of 184 municipalities) and 3,796th nationally (out of 5,565 municipalities), and the [[Gini coefficient]] rose from 0.41 in 2003<ref name="Gini2003">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/aracoiaba/pesquisa/36/30246?ano=2003|title=Mapa de pobreza e desigualdade|date=2003|access-date=2024-12-21|author=IBGE}}</ref> to 0.51 in 2010.<ref name="Gini2010">{{cite web|url=https://ceara.dieese.org.br/ws2/tabela/ceara/indice-de-gini|title=Índice de Gini|date=2010|access-date=2024-12-21|website=Observatório da Agricultura Familiar do Ceará|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240624234612/https://ceara.dieese.org.br/ws2/tabela/ceara/indice-de-gini|archive-date=June 24, 2024}}</ref> Considering only the longevity index, its value is 0.759, the income index is 0.550, and the education index is 0.556.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web|url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx|title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil|date=2010|access-date=October 11, 2010|work=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano|publisher=United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610114916/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx|archive-date=June 10, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
