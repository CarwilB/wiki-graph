'''Tahuamanu''' is the smallest of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Madre de Dios Region]] of [[Peru]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into three [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:
* [[Iberia District|Iberia]]
* [[Iñapari District|Iñapari]]<ref>{{cite news|url=https://noticias.madrededios.com/articulo/local-sucesos/inician-trabajos-remodelacion-plaza-armas-inapari/20170704004829012264.html|title=Inician trabajos de remodelación de la Plaza de Armas de Iñapari|work=Radio Madre de Dios|date=4 July 2017|access-date=11 December 2025|language=es}}</ref>
* [[Tahuamanu District|Tahuamanu]]
