'''Nacaome''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle (department)|Valle]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|589.2|km2}} and had a population of 58,345 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
Nacaome was established in 1535, when two rival indigenous tribal groups, the [[Mangue language|Cholulas]] and [[Lenca people|Chaparrastiques]], came together to form a new settlement along the [[Nacaome River]]. The name "Nacome" means "union of two races" in [[Nahuatl]].<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite book|author=Banegas, Ramon|title=Historia de Nacaome|publisher=Banco Central de Honduras|date=August 1996|lang=es|page=5}}</ref>

The Spanish colonilaists arrived in 1535 and established the first church. It was elevated to a municipality in the same year, and became a city in 1845.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Nacome, Valle|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Valle/Nacome.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Nacome|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-nacome/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Nacome is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle Department|Valle]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Langue]] and [[San Francisco de Coray]] to the north, [[Pespire]] and [[San Lorenzo, Honduras|San Lorenzo]] to the east, [[Amapala]] to the south and [[Goascorán]] and [[Alianza, Honduras|Alianza]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|589.2|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1701__nacaome/|title=Nacome, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Nacome has a [[tropical savannah climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Aw). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|28.46|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|185.35|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/valle/nacome|title=Nacome, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Nacaome had a total population of 57,345 inhabitants, of whom 27,814 (48.5%) were men and 29,531 (51.5%) were women. About 42.4% of the population was classified as urban and 33,012 residents (57.6%) in the rural areas.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 20,567 individuals (35.9%) were aged 0–14 years, 33,130 individuals (57.8%) were aged 15–64, and 3,648 individuals (6.4%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20.8 years and the mean age was 26.1 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 16.9%, lower than the departmental average of 17.7%. The municipality had 12,351 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.5 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

==Economy==
The major economic activities include [[agriculture]], majorly [[maize]], [[melon]]s, and beans, [[shrimp]] production, and [[salt]] farming. In May 2015, a solar power plant with an annual capacity of 100 megawatts was installed in Nacome, which is one of the largest in the region.<ref name="Red"/>
