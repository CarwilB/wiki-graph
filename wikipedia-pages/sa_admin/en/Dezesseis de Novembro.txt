'''Dezesseis de Novembro''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning November 16) is a [[municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,378 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|216.85|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1A37 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is {{convert|535|km|mi}} west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].

It produces the most [[alfalfa]] crops in Brazil which earns it the nickname the "Alfalfa Capital of Brazil".
