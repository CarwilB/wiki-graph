'''Tobatí District''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Cordillera Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. As per the 2022 census, the district had a population of 27,435 individuals. 

==Etymology==
The name derives from the combination of [[Guaraní people|Guaraní]] usage words of "toba" meaning face and the last part of "moroti" as a suffix, which means white. As per one version, this is attributed to the description of the indigenous inhabitants of the region, who had a pale colored face. As per another version, the name came from the whitish-looking mineral [[kaolin]], found in the region, and used extensively as a raw material for [[pottery]] and building construction.<ref name="ABC">{{cite web|title=La bella Tobatí cumple 478 años de fundación|work=ABC digital|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/centinela/la-bella-tobati-cumple-478-anos-de-fundacion-1638122.html|date=6 October 2017|access-date=1 June 2021|trans-title=The beautiful Tobatí celebrates 478 years of foundation}}</ref>

==History==
Tobatí is one of the oldest settlements in Paraguay, and was founded on 3 October 1539.<ref name="ABC"/><ref name="UH">{{cite web|title=Tobatí festeja sus 470 años de fundación|work=Ultimahora|url=
https://www.ultimahora.com/tobati-festeja-sus-470-anos-fundacion-n261459|access-date=1 June 2021|trans-title=Tobatí celebrates its 470 years of foundation}}</ref> The region was occupied by the Carias of the Guarani people, who were attacked repeatedly by the Chaqueños. Hence, conquisdator [[Domingo Martínez de Irala]], who made an alliance with the Carians, defended them, and later moved them east. They were later settled in Tobatí, on the banks of the Manduvirá River.<ref name="ABC"/> It is the birthplace of [[Pedro Juan Caballero (politician)|Pedro Juan Caballero]], the leader of Paraguayan independence movement.<ref name="UH"/>

==Geography==
Tobati is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Cordillera Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|286.3|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/cordillera/0318__tobat%C3%AD/|title=Tobati|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|72|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of [[Asuncion]], and {{cvt|17|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Caacupé]].<ref name="ABC"/>

==Demographics==
As per the 2022 census, Tobati district had a population of 27,431 inhabitants of which 14,303 were males and 13,132 were females.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/cordillera/0318__tobat%C3%AD/|title=Tobati|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is the second largest district by population in the department of Cordillera.<ref name="UH"/> About 47.6% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (52.4%) lived in urban areas. About 27.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 8.9% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>
