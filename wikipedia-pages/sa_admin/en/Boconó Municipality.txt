'''Boconó''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 1,365&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 100,240 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.
