'''Doutor Maurício Cardoso''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 4,462.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/doutor-mauricio-cardoso/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality would be partially flooded by the proposed [[Panambi Dam]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos}}|language=pt |title=Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos|publisher=Eletrobras CGTEE|date=7 April 2014
