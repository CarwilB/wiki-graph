''' Acatepec ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Acatepec]].  The municipality covers an area of 599&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 32,792, up from 28,525 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Acatepec|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=12&mun=076|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=17 April 2014|archive-date=24 October 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024063532/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=12&mun=076|url-status=dead}}</ref>
