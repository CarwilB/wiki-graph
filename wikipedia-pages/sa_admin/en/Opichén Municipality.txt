'''Opichén Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "inside the cave or the well") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]  containing 268.25&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|75|km}} south of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and was located in the chieftainship of Tutul Xiu. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos, such as in Iñigo de Sugasti in 1607; Pedro de Santo Domingo Campos and Diego Hidalgo Bravo in 1639; Juan Esteban de Aguilar, Cristóbal Matías Hidalgo Bravo, and Juan Esteban Tello de Aguilar in 1652; and Ana de Vaneda Villegas in 1705.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825 the area was assigned to the Camino Real<ref name="municipios"/> under the  [[Maxcanú Municipality]]. In 1988 the area was confirmed as its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|access-date=18 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=es|date=1996|pages=98-124|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archive-date=23 December 2015}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public cleanliness, parks and gardens, public monuments.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Opichén|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=18 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Opichén]], Yucatán. The municipality has 12 other populated places<ref name=governance /> including Calcehtók, Cok, Kaukiriche, Mena, Ojina, San Antonio, San Esteban, Santa Rita, Santa Rosa, Siuch, and Rancho Xikim. ==Local festivals==
Every year from 23 to 27 February is a celebration for the pueblo; from 8 to 12 June is the festival to honor of St. Barnabas; and at the end of August the town holds the feast of San Bartolo.<ref name="municipios"/> 

==Tourist attractions==
* Church of San Bartolomé, built in the eighteenth century 
* Chapel of the Mejorada, built in the eighteenth century
* Hacienda Calcehtoc
