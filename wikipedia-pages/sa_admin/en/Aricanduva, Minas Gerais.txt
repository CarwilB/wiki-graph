'''Aricanduva''' is a municipality in the northeast of the Brazilian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. {{as of|2020}} the population was 5,269 in a total area of {{cvt|243|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/aricanduva/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The elevation is 682 meters. It is part of the IBGE statistical meso-region of [[Jequitinhonha]] and the micro-region of [[Capelinha]]. It became a municipality in 1995.

==Economy==
The economy is based on cattle raising and agriculture, with the main crops being coffee, sugarcane, and corn. There were plantations of eucalyptus trees for charcoal production. In 2005 there were 622 rural producers but only 7 tractors. {{as of|2005}} there were no hospitals and 3 public health clinics. Educational needs were met by 11 primary schools and 1 middle school. There were 114 automobiles in 2006, giving a ratio of 45 inhabitants per automobile. There were no banks in 2007.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Neighboring municipalities are: [[Capelinha]], [[Itamarandiba]] and [[São Sebastião do Maranhão]]. The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 419&nbsp;km. The nearest major population center, Capelinha, is 15&nbsp;km. on secondary roads.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/capelinha/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-07-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080426213918/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/capelinha/ |archive-date=2008-04-26 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Municipal [[Human Development Index]]==
Aricanduva is ranked very low on the MHDI and was one of the poorest municipalities in the state and in the country in 2000. 
*MHDI: .636 (2000)
*State ranking: 773 out of 853 municipalities
*National ranking: 3,996 out of 5,138 municipalities in 2000
*Life expectancy: 70
*Literacy rate: 67 (''For the complete list see'' [https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto])
*Combined primary, secondary and tertiary gross enrolment ratio: .728
*Per capita income (monthly): R$80.00 <ref name="frigoletto.com.br">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>
*[[Urbanization]] rate: 24.91
*Percentage of urban residences connected to sewage system: 65.70
*Infant mortality rate: 65.93 (the national average was 18.91).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/ARICANDUVA.pdf |title=Indicadores de Saúde |access-date=2008-07-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161053/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/ARICANDUVA.pdf |archive-date=2011-07-06 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The above figures can be compared with those of [[Poços de Caldas]], which had an MHDI of .841, the highest in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The highest in the country was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in the state of [[São Paulo]] with an MHDI of .919. The lowest was [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] with an MHDI of .467 out of a total of 5504 municipalities in the country {{as of|2004|lc=y}}. At last count Brazil had 5,561 municipalities so this might have changed at the time of this writing.<ref name="frigoletto.com.br"/>
