'''San José El Idolo''' ({{IPA|es|saŋ xoˈse el ˈiðolo}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]]{{cn|date=August 2024}} in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.google.com/maps/place//@14.45,-91.416667,12z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0x0!5m1!1e4?hl=en | title=Google Maps }}</ref> It has an area of {{convert|82|km²|0|abbr=on}} and is at an altitude of 170 m. It contains 11,000 people.
