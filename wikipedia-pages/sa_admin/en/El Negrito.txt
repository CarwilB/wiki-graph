'''El Negrito''' ({{IPA|es|el neˈɣɾito}}) is a town, with an urban population of 13,260 (2023),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> and a [[municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Yoro Department|Yoro]], [[Honduras]].

== Demographics ==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, El Negrito municipality had a population of 45,363. Of these, 95.35% were [[Mestizo]], 3.33% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 1.03% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (0.50% [[Tolupan]], 0.19% [[Lenca]], 0.15% [[Chʼortiʼ people|Chʼortiʼ]], 0.11% [[Nahuas|Nahua]]), 0.24% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.05% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
