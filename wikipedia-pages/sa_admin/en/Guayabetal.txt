Guayabetal is a [[Colombia|Colombian]] [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the department of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], located in [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]],65 kilometers southeast of [[Bogotá]].

== History ==
Guayabetal was formed on October 12, 1941, in the place it currently occupies during the 1920s and 1930s, within the jurisdiction of [[Quetame]], and was consolidated with the road to [[Villavicencio]]. By Ordinance No. 29 of June 30, 1944, it was established as an inspection area and Ordinance No. 28 of January 14, 1980, created the municipality of Guayabetal. Its first mayor was Manuel Gilberto González. On March 29, 1949, by [[Diocese|Archdiocesan]] decree, the church of Our Lady of Carmen of Guayabetal was erected as a parish of the [[Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Bogotá|Archdiocese of Bogotá]].

On June 28, 1974, near Guayabetal on the Highway to the [[Llanos|Llano]], the Quebrada Blanca Tragedy occurred when a landslide buried more than 500 people waiting to pass, after the road was blocked by a previous landslide.

In January 2018, while construction work was being done on the viaduct at the Chirajara site southeast of Guayabetal, the structure [[Chirajara bridge collapse|collapsed]], causing significant damage and killing 9 workers.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Becerra |first=Julián Amorocho |date=2018-01-31 |title=El puente Chirajara se cayó por un error de diseño en una viga |url=https://www.elcolombiano.com/colombia/error-de-diseno-la-causa-del-desplome-del-puente-chirajara-XA8112167 |access-date=2024-03-31 |website=www.elcolombiano.com |language=es-ES}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The municipality has a significant deficiency in the employment sector due to its large expanse of rugged terrain that prevents the full utilization of arable land. Moreover, due to the major tourist attraction of the central city, such as Villavicencio, the economy generally revolves around the Llano Road (restaurants, cafeterias, workshops, lodgings, etc.).

This is the main source of income for the inhabitants of the municipal seat. Only small producers who have some way to commercialize their products do so in Villavicencio, while others are affected by the purchase of intermediaries who acquire their products at low prices. The rural population is dedicated to agriculture, mainly growing coffee and beans, which forms the economic base of the peasant farmer.

== Transport ==
Guayabetal can be reached from Bogotá (Usme locality) via National Route 40 to the southeast or by the same road from Villavicencio to the west, directly arriving at the urban center of Guayabetal.
