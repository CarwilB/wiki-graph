'''Arcabuco''' is a town and municipality in the [[Ricaurte Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Arcabuco is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2739|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[Moniquirá]] and [[Gámbita]] in the north, [[Villa de Leyva]] and [[Chíquiza]] in the south, [[Cómbita]] in the east and [[Gachantivá]] and Villa de Leyva in the west. The department capital [[Tunja]] is {{convert|34|km|mi}} to the south.<ref name=websiteArcabuco>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150603090158/http://www.arcabuco-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Arcabuco]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Arcabuco comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means either "Place of the intricate scrublands" or "Place enclosed by the hills".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Arcabuco] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Arcabuco in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Their territory was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a loose collection of [[Muisca rulers|rulers]]. Arcabuco was part of the ''[[zaque|zacazgo]]'', with the ''[[zaque]]'' based in [[Tunja|Hunza]].

Modern Arcabuco was founded on October 22, 1856 by Celedonio Umaña and Leopoldo Rodríguez.<ref name=websiteArcabuco/>

== Economy ==
The economy of Arcabuco is centered around [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Apart from the agricultural products [[potato]]es and [[strawberry|strawberries]], the town is known as a large producer of [[almojábana]]s.<ref name=websiteArcabuco/>
