'''Miguel Peña''' is by far the largest civil parish in the municipality of [[Valencia (Venezuela)|Valencia]], [[Carabobo]], and one of the most populous in [[Venezuela]].
It is located in the southern part of the city, and it has most of Valencia's poorest [[slum]]s. It has a surface area of some 200&nbsp;km² for a density of some 2,500 inhabitants per km².<ref>http://www.palimpalem.com/5/parroquiando/index.html?body2.html History of Miguel Peña (in Spanish)</ref>

[[Image:Parroquia Miguel Peña - Municipio Valencia - Carabobo - Venezuela.svg|thumb|right|300px|Civil parish Miguel Peña in [[Carabobo]]]]

==General==

The area was named after [[Miguel Peña (politician)|Miguel Peña]] (1781–1833),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.notitarde.com/portadas/ediciones/aniver/aniver2004/cosiata.html |title=La historia oculta de Valencia · Edición Aniversaria · |website=www.notitarde.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061116090131/http://www.notitarde.com/portadas/ediciones/aniver/aniver2004/cosiata.html |archive-date=2006-11-16}} </ref> a well-known politician from Valencia who was active in the formation of Venezuela in the early part of the 19th century.

The parish has undergone a dramatic population growth in the last 40 years from internal immigration, particularly poor farmers coming from the South and from [[Caracas]] and immigration from other Latin American countries. Most people have come attracted by job opportunities linked to Valencia's industrial development.

It is one of the most dangerous regions in [[Carabobo]].<ref>http://www.eluniversal.com/2008/11/10/sucgc_art_en-carabobo-homicidi_1137374.shtml Crime in Carabobo, El Universal (Spanish)</ref>

=== History ===

Around 1936, there was already a small settlement known as Cruz de Legua where now the Lomas de Funval urbanization is located. Still in the 1940s, the area was used for farming. There were mainly large haciendas and some scattered houses where poor farmers lived.

Some urbanizations started to be built in the fifties and forties on the Northern part when Valencia started to become an industrial city. The main areas were Urbanización Mendoza, Urbanización El Palotal, Urbanización Ricardo Urriera and Urbanización Lomas de Funval. Some bridges were rebuilt giving access to Santa Rosa, El Boquete and Las Dos Bocas (currently the El Ahorcado bridge). There was almost no planning in the process.

The civil parish Miguel Peña was officially created by state law on 4 August 1971. Some avenues were built in the seventies, like the Aranzazu, the Sesquicentenaria, the [[Lisandro Alvarado]] and the Enrique Tejera avenues. Also a new road toward the South, to El Paíto, was made.

The chaotic urban expansion accelerated up in the 1980s and 1990s, leading to a collapse of services and increased criminality.
<ref>[http://www.palimpalem.com/5/parroquiando/index.html?body2.html History of Miguel Peña]</ref>

== Administration and politics ==

Miguel Peña is a [[civil parish]] and as such has little administrative independence. It belongs to the Municipality of Valencia. The governing body is a '''junta parroquial''' with relatively little powers.
There have been long discussions about making it an independent municipality, but this has not come to effect.
,<ref>[http://historico.notitarde.com/2004/03/17/valencia/valencia2.html News on new law for administrative divisions in Carabobo (Notitarde, Spanish)]</ref><ref>[http://historico.notitarde.com/2004/03/16/valencia/valencia8.html Oswaldo Feo Caballero discusses the politics of making Miguel Peña a municipality (Notitarde, Spanish)]</ref>

The area tends to vote for the Chávez system more than others in the region. In the [[2007 Venezuelan constitutional referendum|referendum for the constitutional reform]] of 2 December 2007, 53.95% of the voters there supported the reform against 40.78 for the whole [[Valencia Municipality, Carabobo|municipality of Valencia]].<ref>http://www.cne.gov.ve/divulgacion_referendo_reforma/index.php?cod_estado=07&cod_municipio=09&cod_parroquia=04 Results of constitutional referendum of 2 December 2007 in the Miguel Pena region</ref>

== Divisions ==

The region has grown without much planning. Some of the main areas are:

* The Fundación Mendoza (Mendoza Foundation): This urbanization was financed by the Mendoza entrepreneur and was destined mainly for his workers.
* El Palotal urbanization: This is an urbanization built by the Banco Obrero (Worker's Bank) for the working class. Houses were assigned during [[Rómulo Betancourt]]'s government mostly to people who had opposed the [[Marcos Pérez Jiménez]] dictatorship.
* La Antena
* Las Flores
* La Unidad
* Ruiz Pineda
* Santa Inés
* Barrio (Slum) La Victoria
There are many other sectors that tend to be more precarious. Many of these areas lack services like electricity or proper water canalization.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://historico.notitarde.com/1998/06/10/valencia/valencia13.html |title=notitarde.com - valencia |website=historico.notitarde.com |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20030504154633/http://historico.notitarde.com/1998/06/10/valencia/valencia13.html |archive-date=2003-05-04}} </ref>

Some of the main avenues are:

* Avenida Las Ferias
* Avenida Lisandro Alvarado,
* Avenida Aranzazu
* Avenida la Romana
* Avenida Sesquicentenario
* Vía el Paito, which was built on the ancient [[Spanish Road|Spanish road]] towards the town of Tocuyito and further to the Southwest.

== Culture and education ==

There is a smaller public library, Miguel Colombet, at Calle Sucre cruce con Branger.

There are two main secondary schools: Liceo Zuloaga and Liceo Ponce Bello. There are also dozens of basic schools.

== Transportation ==

The area is mostly served by buses from different bus companies.

=== Metro stations ===

[[Valencia Metro (Venezuela)|Valencia's underground line]] starts in this parish. The community has two underground stations:  
*Estación La Monumental
*Estación Las Ferias.

== Health ==
In spite of the size of the area, there is only one hospital and that is for pediatric purposes only, the Hospital Maternidad del Sur. Otherwise patients need to be treated in the Hospital de Carabobo or in smaller health centres.
