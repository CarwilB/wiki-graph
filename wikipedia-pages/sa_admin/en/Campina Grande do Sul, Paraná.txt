'''Campina Grande do Sul, Paraná''' is a [[municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. Its population was 43,685 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|539.861|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/campina-grande-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains 10% of the {{convert|2699|ha|adj=on}} [[Roberto Ribas Lange State Park]], created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Roberto Ribas Lange – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PES Roberto Ribas Lange|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/593802|access-date=2016-11-17}}</ref>
It contains part of the {{convert|4334|ha|adj=on}} [[Pico Paraná State Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|publisher=State of Paraná
