'''Jateí''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 4,021&nbsp;(2020) and its area is {{cvt|1928|km2}}.

The municipality contains 57% of the {{convert|73345|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Ivinhema State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES do Rio Ivinhema – ISA}}|language=pt
