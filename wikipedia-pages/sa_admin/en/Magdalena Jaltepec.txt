''' Magdalena Jaltepec ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in southwestern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 184.99&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is part of the [[Nochixtlán District]] in the southeast of the [[Mixteca Region, Oaxaca|Mixteca Region]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 3,463.<ref name="Enciclopedia">{{cite web |title=Magdalena Jaltepec |language=es |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20046a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=2021-03-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128225653/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20046a.htm |archive-date=January 28, 2007 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

The community's 16th-century church boasts a number of colonial-era polychrome and other ''[[Santo (art)|santos]]'' (statues of Roman Catholic saints).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.aug.edu/augusta/santos/jaltepec/index.html |title=Santos in Oaxaca's Ancient Churches: Santa Magdalena Jaltepec |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120720234852/http://www.aug.edu/augusta/santos/jaltepec/index.html |archive-date=2012-07-20 |access-date=2021-03-06}}</ref>
