'''Madre de Dios''' ({{IPA|es|ˈmaðɾe ðe ðjos}}) is one of the five provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Pando Department|Pando]] [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] and is situated in the southern parts of the department. Its name derives from [[Madre de Dios River]] on the northern border of the province.

== Location ==
Madre de Dios province is located between 10° 54' and 12° 28' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 05' and 67° 58' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over a length of 390&nbsp;km from northeast to southwest, and up to 150&nbsp;km from northwest to southeast.

It is situated in the [[Amazon Rainforest|Amazon]] lowlands of Bolivia and borders [[Manuripi Province]] in the north, [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in the southwest, and [[Beni Department]] in the southeast.

== Population ==
The population of Federico Román Province has increased by 50% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 8,097 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 9,521 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 11,220 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2024: 31,473 inhabitants ([[census]])
 
49.8% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

99.3% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 0.7% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 0.1% [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 5.6 speak other [[indigenous language]]s. ''(1992)''

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 82.4%. ''(1992)''

94.7% of the population have no access to electricity, 71.8% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

80.2% of the population are Catholics, 18.8% are Protestants. ''(1992)''
