'''San Bernardino''' ({{IPA|es|sam beɾˈnaɾðino}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is known for Los Shapos, a longtime religious and cultural tradition celebrated on 16 July that involves music, decorations, and marching the streets dressed in various costumes.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.prensalibre.com/ciudades/suchitepequez/los-shopos-una-tradicion-que-identifica-a-san-bernardino/|title=Los Shapos, una tradición que identifica a San Bernardino|first=Cristian|last=Soto|work=[[Prensa Libre (Guatemala)|Prensa Libre]]|date=16 July 2017|access-date=5 December 2025|language=es}}</ref>
