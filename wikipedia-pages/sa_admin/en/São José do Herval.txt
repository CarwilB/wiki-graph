'''São José do Herval''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]], commonly known as ''Dead Donkey'' (Burro Morto) by the inhabitants of the region.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 1,943.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/sao-jose-do-herval/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
