'''Sain Alto''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|110|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Zacatecas City]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Sain Alto is located at an elevation between {{convert|1900|and(-)|3000|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in northwestern Zacatecas. It borders the municipalities of [[Río Grande Municipality, Zacatecas|Río Grande]] to the north, [[Fresnillo]] to the south, and [[Sombrerete, Zacatecas|Sombrerete]] to the west.<ref name="compendium">{{cite web |year=2009 |title=Sain Alto, Zacatecas |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32040.pdf |access-date=11 June 2021 |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |publisher=INEGI |language=es}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|1415.0|km2}}<ref name=cifras/> and comprises 1.9% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Sain Alto comprises grassland (51%), forest (23%), and [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (6%). Another 19% of the land is used for agriculture.<ref name=compendium/> The municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of the [[Aguanaval River]], which flows south to north through the eastern part of the municipality. A portion of the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] known as the Sierra de Chapultepec runs through the southwestern part of the municipality. The range contains small deposits of mercury and tin, which were exploited between the 1940s and 1970s.<ref name="encyclopedia">{{cite web |title=Sain Alto |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32040a.html |access-date=11 June 2021 |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |language=es}}</ref><ref name=minerals>{{cite report |url=https://mapserver.sgm.gob.mx/InformesTecnicos/InventariosMinerosWeb/T3212AGTG0001_01.PDF |title=Inventario físico de recursos minerales de la carta Mesillas F13-B35 |language=es |pages=10, 24 |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=December 2012 |access-date=11 June 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite report |last1=Foshag |first1=William F. |last2=Fries Jr. |first2=Carl |url=https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/0935c/report.pdf |title=Tin deposits of the Republic of Mexico |language=es |pages=162–163 |publisher=[[USGS]] |series=Geologic Investigations in the American Republics |year=1942 |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref>

===Climate===
Apart from the mountains in the south where the climate is classified as temperate, Sain Alto has a semi-arid climate with dry winters.<ref name=compendium/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|700|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The name of the municipality derives from the Spanish word Saín,<ref>{{cite book |last=Carrillo Cázares |first=Alberto |title=El debate sobre la guerra chichimeca, 1531-1585 |volume=2 |page=565 |year=2000 |publisher={{ill|El Colegio de Michoacán|es}}, {{ill|El Colegio de San Luis|es}} |location=[[Zamora, Michoacán|Zamora]] |isbn=970-679-031-4 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=RT7zAIvkyZ4C&pg=PA565 |access-date=21 June 2021}}</ref> a group of [[Zacateco]] people who established the settlement of Sain Alto sometime around 1535–1540.<ref name=encyclopedia/> These people were led by a ''[[cacique]]'' named Saín Alonso. The town's full name was originally "San Sebastián con el renombre de Saín", but it eventually became known as San Sebastián de Sain Alto and later just Sain Alto. The earliest Spanish ''conquistadors'' to come into contact with the people of Zaín were {{ill|Ginés Vázquez de Mercado|es}} in 1552 and 1553, and [[Francisco de Ibarra]] in September 1554.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

The first Zacatecas state constitution of 1825 named Sain Alto as a municipality within the ''partido'' of Sombrerete, later the district of Sombrerete. Sain Alto became a free municipality on 19 August 1916.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7}}</ref> Mercury mining began in the municipality in 1934, and accounted for 12% of Mexico's mercury production in the years of 1939 and 1940.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Sain Alto comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and thirteen trustees (''regidores''), eight elected by [[relative majority]] and five by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is José Luis Salas Cordero.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=11 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipality of Sain Alto recorded a population of 21,844 inhabitants living in 5504 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=94–95 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2016 |accessdate=22 January 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 21,533 inhabitants in Sain Alto.<ref name=cifras/>

[[INEGI]] lists 53 localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Sain Alto, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 5295 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The main crops grown in Sain Alto are corn and beans. Livestock ranching is another important agricultural activity, cattle, goats and horses being the commercially most important animals.<ref name=minerals/>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 45|Federal Highway 45]] runs through the municipality, connecting it to Sombrerete and [[Durango City]] in the west, and Fresnillo and Zacatecas City in the southeast.<ref name=minerals/> [[Mexican Federal Highway 49|Federal Highway 49]] defines the eastern border of the municipality.
