'''Parramos''' is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|27.7|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 21,102 inhabitants.

==History==
Parramos was founded in 1533 along the Paraxaj River. Parramos is derived from the [[Kaqchikel language]] words "pa" and "ramos" roughly translating to "in branches". The settlement was destroyed by an earthquake in September 1874. In 1876, the inhabitants transferred to nearby Valley of the Pines, and the town plan was developed by Martínez Flores.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Parramos|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Parramos municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/historia-del-municipio-de-parramos-chimaltenango/|access-date=30 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Parramos is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|27.7|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Parramos (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/chimaltenango/0414__parramos/|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|60|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|7|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Chimaltenango]]. It borders [[El Tejar]] to the north, [[San Andrés Itzapa]] to the northwest, [[Pastores]] and [[Santa Catarina Barahona]] to the east, [[Santa Catarina Barahona]] and [[San Antonio Aguas Calientes]] to the south, and [[San Andrés Itzapa]] to the west.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Parramos|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Parramos municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/geografia-del-municipio-de-parramos-chimaltenango/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Municipios de Chimaltenango|year=2010 |location=Guatemala|url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|access-date=8 June 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608155133/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|archive-date=8 June 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1810.62|m}} above sea level, Parramos has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|21.49|C}} and receives about {{cvt|149.19|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/chimaltenango/parramos|title=Parramos climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 21,102 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 10,646 males and 10,456 females. About 28.9% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.0% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (75.2%) was classified as urban, while 24.8% lived in rural areas. About 68.9% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (49%) and [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (49%) formed the two largest ethnic groups. The municipality had a literacy rate of 86.7%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (91.1%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
