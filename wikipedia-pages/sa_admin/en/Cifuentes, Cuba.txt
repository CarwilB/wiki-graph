'''Cifuentes''' ({{IPA|es|siˈfwentes}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]] founded in 1819<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1919.

==Demographics==
In 2022 the municipality of Cifuentes had a population of 26,135, having dropped from 41,789 in 1976.<ref name=pop>{{Cite web| url=http://www.one.cu/publicaciones/cepde/poblacion%201976%202009/poblacion%201976%202009.pdf|title=Población de Cuba, Provincias y Municipios 1976-2009 Edición 2010
|year= 2010| access-date=2010-09-01|language=es}}</ref><ref name="citypopulation2">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|512|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-09}}</ref> it had a population density of {{convert|51|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

== History ==
The municipality was divided into the [[barrio]]s of Alacrán, Amaro, Barro, Cabecera Norte, Cabecera Sur, Este, Oeste and Sitio Grande until 1976.<ref name="guije">{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vcifuentes/index.htm|title=Cifuentes|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-09|language=es}}</ref>

== Transportation ==
Cifuentes is on a rail line starting in [[Santa Clara, Cuba|Santa Clara]], going to Crucero Margot, to [[Luis Arcos Bergnes, Cuba|Luis Arcos Bergnes]], [[Vega Alta, Cuba|Vega Alta]], [[Canoa, Cuba|Canoa]], [[Tuinicú (Encrucijada)|Tuinicu]], [[Constancia, Cuba|Constancia]], [[Encrucijada]], Mata, El Vaquerito, Aguada la Piedra, Cifuentes, San Diego del Valle, Conyedo, and finally back to Santa Clara.<ref>{{Citation |last=lezumbalaberenjena |title=Composed road map from a tour guide (1980's) |date=2017-11-09 |url=https://www.flickr.com/photos/lezumbalaberenjena/44038904924/ |access-date=2023-04-12}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2019 |title=Road guides of Cuba |url=http://itravelnetwork.us/document/guides/GC_2019_English.pdf}}</ref>
