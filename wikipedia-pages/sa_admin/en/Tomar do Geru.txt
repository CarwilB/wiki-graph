'''Tomar do Geru''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|tõˈmɐ ˈdʊ ʒeˈɾu}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 13,535 (2020) and it covers {{convert|305|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Tomar do Geru has a population density of 43 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located {{convert|131|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]]. <ref name="i"/> The [[Church of Nossa Senhora do Socorro, Tomar do Geru|Church of Nossa Senhora do Socorro]] was built by the [[Society of Jesus]] in 1688. It was listed as a historic structure by the Brazilian [[National Institute of Historic and Artistic Heritage]] (IPHAN) in 1943.<ref name="tg1"/>
