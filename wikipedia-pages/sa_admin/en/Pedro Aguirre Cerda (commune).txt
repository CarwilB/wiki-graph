'''Pedro Aguirre Cerda''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpeðɾo aˈɣire ˈseɾða}}) is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It is named after President [[Pedro Aguirre Cerda]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Pedro Aguirre Cerda spans an area of {{convert|9.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 114,560 inhabitants (55,382 men and 59,178 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 12.2% (15,881 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pedro Aguirre Cerda is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 mayor was Claudina Núñez Jiménez ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> During this period, the communal council had the following members:
* Gloria Rodríguez Calderón ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Juan Rozas Romero (Ind)
* Eduardo Pastene Azola ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Elizabeth Jiménez Oliva (Ind)
* Carmen Salinas Jara ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Eduardo Cancino Cáceres ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Rodrigo Lagos Fuentes ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Manuel Aguilar Gálvez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pedro Aguirre Cerda is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pedro Browne ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Guillermo Teillier ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) as part of the 28th electoral district, (together with [[San Miguel, Chile|San Miguel]] and [[Lo Espejo]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Montes ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Manuel José Ossandón ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
