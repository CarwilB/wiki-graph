The '''Sosa Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 24,142.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[Ciudad de Nutrias]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Sosa Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Sosa Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 27,445 (up from 21,960 in 2000).  This amounts to 3.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|7.7|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Sosa Municipality is Francisco Ramon Ramírez, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 41% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=8&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |title=Alcalde de Municipio |publisher=Cne.gob.ve |date= |accessdate=2019-07-16}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=17 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archive-date=2 October 2006 |url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Ciudad de Nutrias, El Regalo, Puerto de Nutrias, and Santa Catalina.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
