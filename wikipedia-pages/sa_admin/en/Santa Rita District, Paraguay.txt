'''Santa Rita''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], Paraguay. It occupies an area of {{cvt|593.3|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 27,249 individuals. It was established by Law 58/90 effective on 16 March 1990.


==History==
The settlement of Santa Rita was initially founded in 1973 by immigrants from the southern region of Brazil. It was earlier part of [[Domingo Martínez de Irala, Paraguay|Domingo Martínez de Irala]] district, and was elevated as a district by the Law 58/90 enacted on 16 January 1990.<ref name="ABC">{{cite web|title=La Capital del Progreso celebra su 31er aniversario|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2021/01/17/la-capital-del-progreso-celebra-su-31e-aniversario/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=The Capital of Progress celebrates its 31st anniversary}}</ref><ref name="ABC3">{{cite web|title=Conmemoran hoy 33 años de Santa Rita|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2023/01/16/conmemoran-hoy-33-anos-de-santa-rita/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Santa Rita commemorates 33 years today}}</ref> The city experienced rapid population growth due to further immigration and economic development thanks to the fertile agricultural lands and it is known as the "Capital of Progress".<ref name="ABC"/><ref name="ABC1">{{cite web|title=Santa Rita Ciudad progresista|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/abc-revista/santa-rita-ciudad-progresista-632061.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Santa Rita Progressive City}}</ref>

==Geography==
Santa Rita is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|593.3|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1013__santa_rita/|title=Santa Rita District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located in the southern part of the department and is surrounded by other municipalities with high agricultural production, such as [[Santa Rosa del Monday]], [[Iruña]], [[Naranjal District|Naranjal]], [[San Cristóbal District, Paraguay|San Cristobal]], and [[Tavapy]].<ref name="ABC"/>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|121.5|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|28.34|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|10.24|cm}} and has 137.19 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/alto-paraguay/estancia-santa-rita|title=Santa Rita District|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, Santa Rita district had a population of 27,249 inhabitants of which 13,609 were males and 13,640 were females. About 31.8% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (68.2%) lived in urban areas. About 25.7% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.2% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

==Economy==
The economy was predominantly dependent on [[agriculture]], mainly [[soybean]], [[wheat]], [[maize]], [[sunflower]], and [[canola]]. Other economic activities and industries developed around the same.<ref name="ABC"/><ref name="ABC3"/> The Santa Rita Expo, held annually, is the second largest agribusiness exhibition in Paraguay.<ref name="ABC3"/>
