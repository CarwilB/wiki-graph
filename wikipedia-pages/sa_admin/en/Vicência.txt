[[Image:Vicencia 0445.jpg|thumb|Bust of Vicência Barbosa de Melo (ex-owner of the city)]]
'''Vicência''' is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is 81&nbsp;km away from the state capital [[Recife]]. It has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 32,772 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/vicencia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

* Elevation - 119 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.3 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 81&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Vicência''' are based in food & beverage industry and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
