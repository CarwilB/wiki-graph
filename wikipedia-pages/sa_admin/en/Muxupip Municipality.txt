'''Muxupip Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “buried bread”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing {{Convert|54.93|km2}} of land and located roughly {{Convert|30|km}} northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/> It is bounded on the north by [[Motul, Yucatán|Motul]] town, on the south by [[Cacalchén Municipality]] and on the east and the west by [[Yaxkukul Municipality]] and [[Tixkokob Municipality]].<ref name=Yucatan>{{Cite web|url=http://www.yucatan.gob.mx/estado/municipios/ver_municipio.php?id=54|title=Muxupip|language=es|access-date=8 August 2015|publisher=Gobierno del Estado de Yucatán}}</ref>

==Geography==
The topography of the municipality is at an average elevation of {{Convert|8|m}}. The water resources in the municipality is only of underground water known locally as ''cenotes'', as there no surface water sources within its limits. The predominant climate here is of warm sub-humid conditions. Precipitation occurs in the months from May to July and the rainfall is only {{Convert|45| mm}}. The average annual temperature is {{Convert|26.2|C}}. The annual average relative humidity is 66% in March and 89% in December.<ref name=Pip>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/municipios/31054a.html|title=Muxupip|language=es|access-date=8 August 2015|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México}}</ref>

===Wildlife===
The flora recorded here consists of mostly deciduous forest, excluding the agricultural land. The fauna consists of birds such as the tzutzuy, [[quail]], and [[dove]],  [[chachalaca]]. Mammals found here are [[opossum]], [[rabbits]] and [[bats]]. Snakes and lizards are the reptiles reported here.<ref name=Pip/>

==History==
During pre-Hispanic times, the town fell within the provinces under the chieftainship of Ceh Pech. According to archival documents in 1579 it was established Encomienda.<ref name=Yucatan/>  After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/> In 1700, Simón Osorio was the encomendero.<ref name="Robleda (2003)">{{cite book|last1=Robleda|first1=Gabriela Solís|title=Bajo el signo de la compulsión|date=2003|publisher=M. A. Porrúa|location=México|isbn=978-9-707-01310-0|edition=1.|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=kW_6AgKrNZEC&pg=PA173|access-date=3 July 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the [[Motul Municipality]]. In 1927 it was designated as its own municipality.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The present president is Dose Del Carmen Escareno Ake (2012–2015).<ref name=Yucatan/> The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, parks and gardens and public lighting.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Muxupip| url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=3 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

The municipality belongs to the Second Federal Electoral Local District and the Ninth Ward.<ref name=Yucatan/>

==Local festivals==
Every year from 24 to 27 January is the celebration for the town's patroness, the Virgin of the Assumption. There is also a fiesta held on 25 August in honor of St. James.<ref name=municipios />

==Tourist attractions==
The notable monuments in the municipality are the Temple of Santiago, the Chapel and the Holy Cross dated to the seventeenth century and the main building of the [[Hacienda Catzmil]].<ref name=Yucatan/>
