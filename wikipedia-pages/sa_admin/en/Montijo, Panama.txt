'''Montijo  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Montijo District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 2,288 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Montijo District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 4,341; its population as of 2000 was 4,545.<ref name=inec/>
