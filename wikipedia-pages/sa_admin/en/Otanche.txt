'''Otanche''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the subregion of the [[Western Boyacá Province]].

==Climate==
Otanche has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> with mild temperatures and high amounts of rainfall throughout the year.
