'''Vallegrande''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Around 2012 it held 0.5% of the [[Bolivian wine|wine]] and [[singani]] grapes planted in Bolivia, far behind the main wine-making districts of the [[Central Valley of Tarija]] and [[Cinti Valley]].<ref name=br99>{{Cite book |title=Clima, zonificación y tipicidad del vino en regiones vitivinícolas iberoamericanas |year=2012 |pages=99-104|url=https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ernesto-Uliarte/publication/261947152_Clima_Zonificacion_y_Tipicidad_del_Vino_en_Regiones_Vitivinicolas_Iberoamericanas/links/5925c3ec458515e3d44fc839/Clima-Zonificacion-y-Tipicidad-del-Vino-en-Regiones-Vitivinicolas-Iberoamericanas.pdf#page=355 |language=es|location=Madrid|editor-last=Tonietto|editor-first=Jorge|editor-last2=Sotés Ruiz|editor-first2=Vicende|editor-last3=Gómez-Miguel|editor-first3=Vicente D.|chapter=El clima vitícola de las regiones productoras de uvas para de Bolivia|author-last=Antelo Bruno|author-first=Luis}}</ref>
