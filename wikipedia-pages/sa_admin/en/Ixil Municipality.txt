'''Ixil Municipality''' ({{IPA|yua|iˈʃil|}}, in the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “place of bristles”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 134.13&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 25&nbsp;km north of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
In ancient times, the area was part of the chieftainship of [[Ceh Pech]] until the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]]. At colonization, Ixil became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/>

In 1821, Yucatán was declared independent of the Spanish Crown. In 1905, Ixil belonged to the Coastal region headquartered in Izamal. In 1850, Ixil withdrew from the coastal region and became aligned with [[Tixkokob Municipality|Tixkokob]]. In 1918, it became head of its own municipality and was separated from Tixkokob.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a term of three years. The president appoints Councilpersons to serve on the board for three year terms, as the Secretary and councilors of public works, security, cemeteries, nomenclature, parks and public gardens, street lighting and public health.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Ixil|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/municipios/31020a.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=4 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=22 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211222050133/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/municipios/31020a.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />
