'''San Pedro Yaneri''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 79.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 943, of whom 807 spoke some indigenous language.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20335a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>

The municipality is one of nine that make up the  "Rincon de Ixtlán", the only one that is not also within the municipality of [[Ixtlán de Juárez]].
It is part of the [[Ixtlán District, Oaxaca|Ixtlán District]] in the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca|Sierra Norte]] region.
