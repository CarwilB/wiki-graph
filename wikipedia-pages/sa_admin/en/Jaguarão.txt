'''Jaguarão''' ({{IPAc-en|ˌ|ʒ|ɑː|g|w|ə|ˈ|r|aʊ|n}} {{respell|ZHAH|gwə|ROWN}}; {{IPA|pt|ʒaɡwɐˈɾɐ̃w}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southern Brazilian state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]] located on the shores of the [[Jaguarão River]], bordering [[Uruguay]].

It lies a short distance inland from the [[Lagoa Mirim|Mirim Lagoon]].

==Geographical and historical proximity to Uruguay==

In 1865 it was the site of the [[Battle of Jaguarão]], fought against Uruguayan forces.

Located in the extreme south of Brazil and the border with [[Uruguay]], the [[Baron of Mauá International Bridge]] links it with [[Río Branco]].

==Consular representation==
[[Uruguay]] has a Consulate in Jaguarão.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://busquedaconsulados.mrree.gub.uy/busq_mrree/servlet/hresultabusqmision?61,2,0|title=Directorio de oficinas consulares Uruguayas en el mundo|website=Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores|language=es|access-date=13 October 2022}}</ref>
