'''Presidente Juscelino''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was 3,606 living in a total area of 696&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/presidente-juscelino/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

Named after the former president of Brazil [[Juscelino Kubitschek]], Presidente Juscelino is located at an elevation of 596 meters, 40&nbsp;km. west of [[Curvelo]] on highway [[BR-259]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 216&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Corinto, Minas Gerais|Corinto]], [[Curvelo]] and [[Felixlândia]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172032/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |date=2008-04-10 }}</ref> 

The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations is especially important. The GDP in 2005 was R$21 million, with 9 million from services, 4 million from small industries, and 7 million from agriculture. There were 286 rural producers on 35,000 hectares of land. Approximately 1000 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were beans and corn. There were 23,000 head of cattle (2005). There were no banks (2007), only 

34 farms had tractors (2006) and 218 automobiles (108 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 20 inhabitants per automobile.<ref name="IBGE" />

There is only one health clinic. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Curvelo. There are only five primary schools, one middle school, and two pre-primary schools.<ref name="IBGE" />

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.654 (2000)
*State ranking: 733 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 3,707 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000
*Literacy rate: 77%
*Life expectancy: 65 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In 2000, the per capita income of R$97.00 was well below the state and national averages of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.

The highest-ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
