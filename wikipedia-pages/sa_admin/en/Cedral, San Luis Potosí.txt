'''Cedral''' is a town and [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipality]] in the central [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128021947/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |archivedate=January 28, 2007 }}</ref> As of the year 2020, it has a total population of 19,840.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cedral (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/san_luis_potos%C3%AD/24007__cedral/ |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Cedral is located on the northern part of [[San Luis Potosí]], along the [[Mexican Federal Highway 62]]. It borders [[Doctor Arroyo, Nuevo León|Doctor Arroyo]] in the east, [[Matehuala]] and [[Villa de La Paz]] in the south, as well as [[Catorce]] and [[Vanegas]] in the west. Its average elevation is 1,853 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cedral topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-l3zcf3/Cedral/?center=23.9461,-100.6842 |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref>
