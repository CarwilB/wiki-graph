'''Capital'''<ref>{{cite web |title=Capital (Department, San Juan, Argentina) |url=https://citypopulation.de/en/argentina/admin/san_juan/70028__capital/ |website=citypopulation.de |access-date=24 January 2025}}</ref> is a department in [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan Province]], Argentina.

== Geography ==
The capital of San Juan has an area of {{cvt|30|km2}}. To the north bordering department [[Chimbas Department|Chimbas]], on the east by [[Santa Lucía Department|Santa Lucía]], to the west and south by [[Rivadavia Department, San Juan|Rivadavia]] [[Rawson Department, San Juan|Rawson]]. The approximate population of the department of capital is 118,500 inhabitants.

== Population ==

According to estimates from [[INDEC]], the national institute responsible for censuses, it had 116,511 inhabitants in 2005.
