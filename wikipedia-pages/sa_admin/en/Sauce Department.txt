'''Sauce Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,151 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|795|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Sauce, Corrientes|Sauce]], which is located around {{cvt|795|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
