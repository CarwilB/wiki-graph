'''Luis Moya''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|50|km}} southeast of the state capital at [[Zacatecas (city)|Zacatecas]]. It is named after {{ill|Luis Moya (military officer)|lt=Luis Moya|es|Luis Moya (militar)}} (1855–1911), a colonel in the [[Mexican Revolution]] who was posthumously given the rank of [[brigadier general]] in 1939.<ref>{{cite book |title=Diccionario de generales de la Revolución |volume=2 |url=https://inehrm.gob.mx/work/models/inehrm/Resource/305/1/images/dic_grales_rev_t2.pdf |publisher={{ill|Instituto Nacional de Estudios Históricos de las Revoluciones de México|es}} |location=Mexico |date=2014 |pages=696–699 |language=es |isbn=978-607-9276-49-2 |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Luis Moya is located at an elevation between {{convert|1900|and(-)|2400|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in southeastern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Cuauhtémoc Municipality, Zacatecas|Cuauhtémoc]] to the northwest, [[Ojocaliente, Zacatecas|Ojocaliente]] to the northeast, [[Noria de Ángeles]] to the east, and [[Loreto, Zacatecas|Loreto]] to the southeast.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32025.pdf |title=Luis Moya, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=[[INEGI]] |year=2009 |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref> It also borders the municipalities of [[Cosío]], [[Rincón de Romos]], and [[Tepezalá]] in the state of [[Aguascalientes]] to the south. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|176.9|km2}} and comprises 0.2% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 73% of the land in Luis Moya is used for agriculture. [[Meseta Central matorral|Matorral]] (24.7%) and urban areas (1.1%) cover much of the rest of the municipality. Luis Moya is drained by the San Pedro or Aguascalientes River, a tributary of the [[Río Verde (Jalisco)|Río Verde]] in the [[Lerma River|Lerma]]–[[Río Grande de Santiago|Santiago]] river basin. The San Pedro River forms part of the border between Luis Moya and the municipality of Cosío in Aguascalientes.<ref name=compendium/>

===Climate===
Luis Moya has a temperate semi-arid climate with dry winters. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Luis Moya was originally founded as a settlement in 1692 with the name of San Francisco de los Adames.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32025a.html |title=Luis Moya |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

After Mexican independence, San Francisco de los Adames was part of the ''partido'' of Pinos in the state of [[San Luis Potosí]]. The [[Federal Constitution of the United Mexican States of 1857|Constitution of 1857]] formalized the transfer of the municipality of San Francisco de los Adames to Zacatecas. It became a free municipality under the name of San Francisco de los Adame on 19 August 1916. On 9 January 1935, the municipality changed its name in honour of Luis Moya.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |pages=59, 117|location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Luis Moya comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and ten trustees (''regidores''), six elected by [[relative majority]] and four by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Guadalupe Silva Medina.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Census, Luis Moya recorded a population of 13,184 inhabitants living in 3267 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=64–65 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=19 June 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 12,234 inhabitants in Luis Moya.<ref name=snim/>

There are 47 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Luis Moya, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=025 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Luis Moya |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref> It recorded a population of 6983 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The economy of Luis Moya is diversified between the primary, secondary and tertiary sectors.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Luis Moya Municipal Council |url=http://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Documentos/Estatal/Zacatecas/Todos%20los%20Municipios/wo104929.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal del Municipio de Luis Moya, Zac. 2013 - 2016 |language=es |pages=10–11 |newspaper=Suplemento al Periódico Oficial |publisher=Government of Zacatecas |volume=124 |issue=5 |date=15 January 2014 |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref> Agricultural activities include the farming of dairy cattle, whose production supplies companies such as [[Grupo Lala|Lala]] and [[Nestlé]];<ref name=plan/> and crops such as alfalfa, fodder corn and oats, grapes, and tomatoes.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Luis-Moya.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 025 Luis Moya |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref> Manufacturing activities include food processing, metalworking and textile production.<ref name=plan/>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 45|Federal Highway 45]] runs through the northern part of the municipality, connecting it to the cities of Zacatecas to the northwest and [[Aguascalientes (city)|Aguascalientes]] to the south. A segment of [[Mexican Federal Highway 71|Federal Highway 71]] branches off from Highway 45 at the municipal seat of Luis Moya and runs south to [[San Francisco de los Romo]] in Aguascalientes. The [[Ferromex]]-owned railroad which runs between the cities of Aguascalientes and Zacatecas forms part of the municipality's western border.
