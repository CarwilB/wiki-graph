'''Ayahualulco''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz (state)|Veracruz]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located about 80&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{cvt|148.06|km2}}. It is located at {{coord|19|21|N|97|09|W|}}.

The municipality of '''Ayahualulco''' is delimited to the north by [[Perote (Veracruz)|Perote]], to the north-east by [[Xico, Veracruz|Xico]], to the east by [[Ixhuacán de los Reyes]] and to the south by [[Puebla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30025a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ayahualulco |publisher=[Gobierno Estatal] |language=es |access-date=2009-06-04 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117185926/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30025a.htm |archive-date=2006-11-17 }}</ref>

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 27,127 inhabitants in 34 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSAYAHUALULCO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Ayahualulco.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Ayahualulco CENSUS]</ref> Ayahualulco, the municipal seat had a population of 2,823 inhabitants, other localities include Los Altos (4,556 hab.), Xololoyan (2,274 hab.), El Triunfo (2,015 hab.) and Apanteopan (1,658 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSAYAHUALULCO/>

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[potatoes]].

==Culture==
In '''Ayahualulco''', in June takes place the celebration in honor to San Juan Bautista, Patron of the town.
