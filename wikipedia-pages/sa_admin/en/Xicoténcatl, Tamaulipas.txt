'''Xicoténcatl''' is a city and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. On March 15, 1751, it was founded as Villa de Escandón.  On October 27, 1828, it was renamed Xicoténcatl.<ref>[http://www.xicotencatl.gob.mx/municipio/historia.htm Bienvenidos] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120818053252/http://www.xicotencatl.gob.mx/municipio/historia.htm |date=August 18, 2012 }}</ref>
