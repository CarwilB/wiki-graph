'''Acostambo District''' is one of sixteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Tayacaja Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is Chikuru Punta at approximately {{convert|4200|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Tayacaja Province (Apurímac Region)</ref>

{{columns-list|colwidth=20em|
* Chawpi Urqu
* Hatun Pampa
* Inka Pukyu
* Kiswar Q'asa
* Llama Kancha
* Muyu Urqu
* Phiruruyuq
* Saywa
}}
