'''Carlos Manuel de Céspedes''',<ref name=statoids/><ref>Source: ''Mapa de Carreteras de Cuba'' (Road map of Cuba). Ediciones GEO, [[Havana]] 2011 - {{ISBN|959-7049-21-X}}</ref> also shortened as '''Céspedes''', is a town and municipality in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was named for the independence fighter [[Carlos Manuel de Céspedes]] (1819-1874).

==Geography==
It is located in the western part of the province, along the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] highway. The municipality is bordered by [[Florida, Cuba|Florida]], [[Esmeralda, Cuba|Esmeralda]], [[Primero de Enero]] and [[Baraguá]] (both in [[Ciego de Ávila Province]]). The municipality includes some villages, such as [[Magarabomba]].{{citation needed|date=October 2023}}

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Carlos M. de Cespedes had a population of 22,842.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|653|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|35|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Transport==
Céspedes is crossed by the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] highway and counts a railway station on the [[Havana]]-[[Santiago de Cuba]] line. A planned extension of the [[Autopista A1 (Cuba)|A1 motorway]] that will span the entire island will intersect the town.
