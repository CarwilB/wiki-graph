'''Caraá''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]] where [[Rio dos Sinos]]' river rises. It was originally part of the city [[Santo Antônio da Patrulha]], emancipated on December 28, 1995.{{citation needed|date=November 2018}}

== History ==
First inhabitants of Caraá were the indigenous that gave name to the place. Caraá is a type of bamboo that was very abundant in there.
