'''São José do Norte''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 27,721.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/sao-jose-do-norte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
