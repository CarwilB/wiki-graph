'''Alkmaar'''{{efn|{{langx|srn|Gudufrow}}, {{langx|hns|Alakhmár}} or {{lang|hns|Alakmár}}, {{langx|jvn|Likmár}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|title=Verjavaanste toponiemen in Suriname en hun herkomst|trans-title=Javanese-influenced place names in Suriname and their origin|first=Hein|last=Vruggink|publisher=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|date=1987|lang=nl}}</ref>}} is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Commewijne District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 5,561.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/ysr.html Statoids.com]</ref>

==History==
The plantation was named after the city of [[Alkmaar]] in the Dutch province of [[North Holland]] where Jacobus Hengeveldt, the founder was born.<ref name="plantage"/> The city was named after the plantation. Alkmaar has regional significance as a government post and a medical centre. The [[Moravian Church]] (EMEA) is an important center of Christian consignment among the Hindus, and started when P.M. Legêne arrived in Alkmaar. The church was built in 1923, partly financed by fundraising from the Netherlands. A children's home was also setup.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://dpc.uba.uva.nl/cgi/i/image/image-idx?type=boolean;view=reslist;c=surinamica;;rgn1=surinamica_key;q1=Weeshuizen|title=Suriname 1599-1975: Weeshuizen|website=University of Amesterdam|access-date=12 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

In the 18th century Alkmaar became a notable [[coffee]] producing location. Construction of the plantation was consigned to James Hengeveldt in 1745. After the completion of Fort at New Amsterdam in 1746, the land at the mouth of the  [[Commewijne River]] was cleared to make way for the plantation which opened in 1747.<ref>[http://nationaalarchief.sr/geschiedenis/plantages/beneden-commewijnerivier/alkmaar/ National Archives of Suriname] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071010005634/http://nationaalarchief.sr/geschiedenis/plantages/beneden-commewijnerivier/alkmaar/ |date=2007-10-10 }}, {{in lang|nl}}</ref> Charles Godeffroy then took over the plantation.<ref name="plantage">{{Cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/a/alkmaar|title=Plantage Alkmaar|website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=12 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1884, the plantation ceased its operation due to low sugar prices.<ref name="encycl-1916">{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 355 - Handels-Maatschappij (de Nederlandsche)|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=11 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1894, the government bought the grounds in order to distribute it to small farmers.<ref name="plantage"/> The motivation was to entice the indentured workers from [[Java]] to remain in Suriname.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://dbnl.org/tekst/binn011sran01_01/binn011sran01_01_0007.php|title=Sranan. Cultuur in Suriname page 53|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|access-date=12 May 2020}}</ref> The Javanese still form the largest ethnic group.<ref name="census"/> In 1908, there were 699 farmers producing 20,000&nbsp;kg of cacao, 50,000&nbsp;kg of rice, and 57,000 bunches of bananas.<ref name="plantage"/>

The village and former [[sugarcane]] factory of [[Marienburg, Suriname|Mariënburg]] is located close to Alkmaar.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/301ges/amarienb01.html|title=Marienburg Suriname|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref> The villages of [[Johan & Margaretha]] and [[Frederiksdorp]] on the other side of the river, can be reached by a ferry from Mariënburg.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.frederiksdorp.com/nl/over-ons/|title=Over Ons|website=Frederiksdorp.com|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=2 December 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221202151512/https://frederiksdorp.com/nl/over-ons/|url-status=dead}}</ref> The coffee plantation [[Katwijk, Suriname|Katwijk]] is located in Alkmaar.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.maphill.com/suriname/commewijne/alkmaar/katwijk/ |title=Katwijk Maps|website=Map Hill|access-date=1 March 2021}}</ref>
