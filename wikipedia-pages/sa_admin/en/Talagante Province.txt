'''Talagante Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Talagante}}, {{IPA|es|talaˈɣante}}) is one of six [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] in central [[Chile]]. The [[Capital (political)|capital]] is the city of [[Talagante]], located approximately {{convert|35|km|0|abbr=on}} southwest of the national capital of [[Santiago]]. The most northeastern part of the province is included in the Santiago [[conurbation]].

==Administration==
As a province, Talagante is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]] of Chile, governed by a provincial delegate who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]].

===Communes===
The province comprises five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''comunas''), each governed by a municipality consisting of an [[alcalde]] and [[municipal council]]:
*[[Isla de Maipo]]
*[[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]]
*[[Padre Hurtado, Chile|Padre Hurtado]]
*[[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]]
*[[Talagante]]

==Geography and demography==
The province spans an area of {{convert|582.3|sqkm|km2|0}}, the smallest in the Santiago Metropolitan Region. According to the 2002 census, Talagante was the fourth most populous province in the region with a total population of 188,572. At that time, there were 188,572 people living in [[urban area]]s, 28,877 living in [[rural area]]s, 107,935 men, and 109,514 women.<ref name="INE"/>
