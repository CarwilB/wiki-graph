'''Tumbao''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Maunabo, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 593. A new barrio, appearing in the 2010 census, Tumbao barrio was formed from part of [[Palo Seco, Maunabo, Puerto Rico|Palo Seco]] barrio.
