== Geography ==
Some of the highest elevations of the district are [[Suparawra]] at {{convert|5029|m|ft|abbr=on}} and Q'illu Urqu at approximately {{convert|5000|m|ft|abbr=on}}, both on the border with the [[Upahuacho District]]. ==History==
For a detailed account of the history of this city (up to c. 1951) 'La Monografia de la Provincia de Parinacochas' is a very good source. The two volume book details not only historical, but cultural information.<ref>"La Monografia de la Provincia de Parinacochas", OCLC RECORD: [https://www.worldcat.org/oclc/19319571 19319571]</ref>
