'''Pila Partido''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 3,000 in an area of {{convert|3453|km2|abbr=on|0}}, making it one of the most sparsely populated ''partidos'' in Buenos Aires Province. 

The capital city is [[Pila, Buenos Aires|Pila]], which is around {{convert|188|km|abbr=on|0}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
