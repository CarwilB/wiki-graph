'''Chepigana District''' ({{IPA|es|tʃepiˈɣana}}) is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Darién Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 27,461,<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 13, 2009}}</ref> and had risen to 30,110 in the 2010 census,<ref name=census/> prior to the creation of Santa Fe District. Since the separation of Santa Fe District, Chepigana covers a total area of {{convert|4625.1|km2}}.<ref name=area/> The capital lies at the town of [[La Palma, Darién|La Palma]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

On 14 July 2017, the [[National Assembly (Panama)|National Assembly]] decreed the separation of seven ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'' from Chepigana District to create [[Santa Fe District, Darién|Santa Fe District]],<ref name=decree2017>{{cite news |date=14 July 2017 |title=Ley 57 de 14 de julio de 2017 |url=https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/28322_A/62762.pdf |language=es |newspaper=Official Digital Gazette of Panama |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref> which took effect in 2018.<ref name=decree2018>{{cite news |date=15 February 2018 |title=Ley 8 de 14 de Febrero de 2018 |url=https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/28465_A/66082.pdf |language=es |newspaper=Official Digital Gazette of Panama |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref>
