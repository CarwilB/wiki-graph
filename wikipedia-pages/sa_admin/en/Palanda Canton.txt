'''Palanda Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Zamora-Chinchipe Province]].  Its capital is the town of {{Interlanguage link multi|Palanda, Ecuador|lt=Palanda|es|Palanda}}. Its population at the 2001 census was 7,066.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==History==
This canton has a long history, from the earliest known humans occupation<ref>{{cite web|url=http://palanda.arqueo-ecuatoriana.ec/complejo-cultural-mayo-chinchipe|title=Mayo-Chinchipe - Proyecto Zamora Chinchipe|access-date=2010-05-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141129074259/http://palanda.arqueo-ecuatoriana.ec/complejo-cultural-mayo-chinchipe|archive-date=2014-11-29|url-status=dead}}</ref> to one of the latest inca battle, just before the colonial time.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://palanda.arqueo-ecuatoriana.ec/san-agustin|title=San Agustín - Proyecto Zamora Chinchipe|access-date=2010-05-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131001075008/http://palanda.arqueo-ecuatoriana.ec/san-agustin|archive-date=2013-10-01|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Parishes ==
The canton is administratively divided into 4 parishes
* [[Vergel Parish|Vergel]]
* [[Palanda Parish|Palanda]]
* [[El Porvenir Parish|El Porvenir]]
* [[Valladolid Parish|Valladolid]]

==Archaeology==
The site of [[Santa Ana (La Florida)]] is located just to the north of Palanda.<ref>[https://palanda.arqueo-ecuatoriana.ec/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/valdez_uso_social_cuencaMC.png area map]</ref> This is an important archaeological site going back as early as 3,500 BC. 

The work in this area was started in 2002 by a team of French and Ecuadoran archaeologists.
