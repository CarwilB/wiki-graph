'''Mi Perú''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Callao]], [[Peru]]. Initially a [[Pueblos jóvenes|slum]], it was elevated to a [[Populated centres of Peru|populated centre]] and was ultimately made a district in 2014.

== History ==
Mi Perú was part of [[Ventanilla District]] until May 17, 2014, when it was created as District by Law N° 30197.<ref>[http://www.educacionenred.pe/noticia/?portada=53770#ixzz3t0NcpAaM Educación en red]</ref>

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 2.47&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 34 meters above [[sea level]].

===Boundaries===
*East: [[Puente Piedra District]] 
*North, west and south: [[Ventanilla District]]

== Authorities ==
As of 2015 the [[mayor]] of the district was Reynaldo Encalada Tovar.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://rpp.pe/politica/elecciones/partido-de-acuna-gana-elecciones-en-el-distrito-de-mi-peru-noticia-917696|work=RPP|place=Peru|date=2015-11-29|title=Callao: Partido de Acuña gana elecciones en distrito de Mi Perú}}</ref>
Irvin Chavez Leon was mayor in 2025, serving a term from 2023-2026.<ref name=councilweb>{{cite web|title=Municipilidad distrital de Mí Peru|url=https://munimiperu.gob.pe/main/|access-date=2025-09-15}}</ref>
