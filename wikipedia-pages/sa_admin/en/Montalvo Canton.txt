'''Montalvo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Los Ríos Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Montalvo, Ecuador|Montalvo]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 20,067.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

[[Petita Palma]] who was known as the "black marimbera" formed a group called ''Tierra Caliente''.<ref name=eldid>{{Cite web |last=S.A |first=El Diario, Grupo Ediasa |date=2015-08-14 |title=Petita nació en Carondelet |url=https://www.eldiario.ec/noticias-manabi-ecuador/364621-petita-naci-en-carondelet/ |access-date=2024-02-02 |website=El Diario Ecuador |language=spanish}}</ref> One of her songs ''Andarele'' became well known. The ''Andarele'' has a back story from its origins in the Montalvo Canton. It is said to come from a particular area were the musicians would play the ''Andarele'' into the night. People would arrive and be entranced so that they stayed. At dawn some would still want to stay, while others would say "lets go".<ref name=eldid/>
