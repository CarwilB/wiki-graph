'''Terra de Areia''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|113|ha}} [[Mata Paludosa Biological Reserve]], created in 1998, a fully protected conservation unit.<ref>{{citation|language=pt
