'''Trou-du-Nord''' ({{IPA|fr|tʁu dy nɔʁ}}; {{langx|ht|Twou dinò}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Trou-du-Nord Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. 
It has a population of 37,405.

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of three [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Garcin, Haiti|Garcin]], rural
* [[Roucou, Haiti|Roucou]], urban and rural, containing the city of Trou-du-Nord
* [[Roche Plate]], rural

== Notable residents ==
*[[Jovenel Moïse]], (1968-2021), politician, [[President of Haiti]], 2017-2021
