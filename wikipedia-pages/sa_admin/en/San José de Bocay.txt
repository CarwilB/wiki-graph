'''San José de Bocay''' is a town and a [[municipality]] in the [[Jinotega (department)|Jinotega]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]], [[Nicaragua]].

With an area of 3,990.40&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, the municipality of San José de Bocay was officially created on March 13, 2002, as a result of the division of the [[El Cuá]]-Bocay municipality. As of 2006, San José de Bocay is the newest municipality in [[Nicaragua]]. San José de Bocay is located near the Rio Bocay river and is along the NN-66 road.
