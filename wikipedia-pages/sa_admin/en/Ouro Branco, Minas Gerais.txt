'''Ouro Branco''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the ''Mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte'' and to the ''Microregion of Conselheiro Lafaiete''. As of 2025, the city has a population of 40,638 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" /> The name "Ouro Branco" means "White Gold", as opposed to "Black Gold" - [[Ouro Preto]]. The city was an important base on the transportation route from the mines of [[Minas Gerais]] to the coast.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?lang=&codmun=314590&search=minas-gerais%7Couro-branco |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em SĂntese |publisher=Cidades.ibge.gov.br |date= |accessdate=2022-08-26}}</ref>
