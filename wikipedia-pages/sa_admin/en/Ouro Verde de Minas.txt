'''Ouro Verde de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was estimated to be 5,914 people living in a total area of 174&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/ouro-verde-de-minas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Vale do Mucuri and to the micro-region of Teófilo Otoni. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Geography==
Ouro Verde de Minas is located at an elevation of 549 meters, 49&nbsp;km. southeast of [[Teófilo Otoni]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 494&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Teófilo Otoni]], [[Ataléia]], and [[Frei Gaspar]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |date=2008-06-13 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. A large percentage of the population lives in the rural area and is engaged in subsistence farming. The GDP in 2005 was approximately {{BRLConvert|17|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 10 million from services, 1 million from industry, and 5 million from agriculture. There were 605 rural producers on 12,000 hectares of land. Only five farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 1,700 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main cash crop was coffee while sugarcane, beans and corn were grown on a small scale. There were 16,000 head of cattle (2006). There was one bank (2007) and 192 automobiles (254 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 34 inhabitants per automobile.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==
There were three health clinics. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni. Educational needs were met by 20 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 1 pre-primary school.<ref name="IBGE"/>
