'''Yamaranguila''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] of about 21,000 residents in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

Yamaranguila is known as the coldest municipality in the country. Due to the climate, it is one of the few places where [[strawberries]] can be cultivated. Largely populated by the [[Lenca people|Lenca]] indigenous population, Yamaranguila is host to the traditional Guancascos parade during its feria in early December. Most residents have both electricity and running water in the central part of town, while the 24 surrounding villages are found in various stages of development.

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Yamaranguila municipality had a population of 21,025. Of these, 89.86% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (89.55% [[Lenca]]), 9.45% [[Mestizo]], 0.35% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.32% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.03% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
