The [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of '''Coronel Suárez''' ([[German language|German]]: {{lang|de|Partido Oberst Suárez}}) is a subdivision of the [[Buenos Aires Province|Province of Buenos Aires]] in [[Argentina]].  In the south-central part of the province, it was created in 1882 by the provincial government when they divided the territory of Tres Arroyos into the partidos of Coronel Suárez, [[Tres Arroyos Partido|Tres Arroyos]] and [[Coronel Pringles Partido|Coronel Pringles]].

It has a population of  about 37,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|6000|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Coronel Suárez]], which is {{convert|550|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

Its population is mainly of [[Volga German]] descent.
