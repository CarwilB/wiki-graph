'''Tenabo''' ([[Yucatec Maya]]: "place where they measure by hand-spans") is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. It is situated on the coast in the north of the state. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Tenabo]], 36&nbsp;km from [[List of capitals in Mexico|state capital]] [[Campeche, Campeche|Campeche, Camp]]. As of 2015, the municipality had a total population of 10,665.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

==Geography==

The municipality of Tenabo borders to the north with [[Hecelchakán Municipality]], to the east with [[Hopelchén Municipality]], to the south with [[Campeche Municipality]], and to the west with the [[Gulf of Mexico]].

It covers 882&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, accounting for 1.5% of the state's total surface area.

==Demographics==
[[File:Iglesia de tinun.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Church at Tinún]]
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 9,736.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Tenabo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=008|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=21 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151021171157/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=008|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Tenabo had a population of 7,543.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Tenabo, the municipality had 90 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
