'''Universidad''', located in [[Río Piedras, Puerto Rico|Rio Piedras]], is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico]].<ref name=Census>[https://www.census.gov/prod/cen2000/phc-3-53-eng.pdf Puerto Rico 2000: Population and Housing Unit Counts]</ref>

It originally belonged to the municipality of [[Río Piedras, Puerto Rico|Río Piedras]]. When Río Piedras was annexed to San Juan in 1951, Río Piedras's [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] Universidad became one of San Juan's barrios. Universidad is notable for being the location of the main campus of the [[University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus|University of Puerto Rico at Río Piedras]]. The underground section of the [[Tren Urbano]] with the [[Universidad (Tren Urbano station)|Universidad station]] is located below [[Avenida Juan Ponce de León|Ponce de León Avenue]], to the west of the neighborhood.

==Notable event==
On October 14, 2019, the Ernesto Ramos Antonini [[Public housing in Puerto Rico|public housing residence]] (located near the university) was the scene of a mass shooting, with more than 1000 shots fired with six people dead. Puerto Rico has struggled with escalating [[Crime in Puerto Rico|criminality]], mainly due to the illegal drug trade.<ref name="NYT 10_19">{{Cite web|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2019/10/15/us/puerto-rico-shooting.html|title=6 Killed in Shootout as Violence Rattles Puerto Rican Capital|first=Patricia|last=Mazzei|date=October 15, 2019|via=NYTimes.com}}</ref>
