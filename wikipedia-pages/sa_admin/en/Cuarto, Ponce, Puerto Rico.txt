'''Cuarto''' (''Barrio Cuarto'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Together with [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]], [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], and [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], Cuarto is one of the municipality's [[Ponce, Puerto Rico#Barrios|six core urban barrios]]. It was created in 1878.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
Cuarto is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and southeast of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

==Boundaries==
[[File:Antiguo Casino de Ponce, in Ponce, Puerto Rico (IMG 2934).jpg|thumb|left|[[Old Ponce Casino|Casino de Ponce]], an important historic landmark, is located in Barrio Cuarto.]]

It is bounded on the North by Comercio/[[Francisco Parra Duperón]] Street, on the South by [[Ramón Power y Giralt|Ramón Power]] Street, on the West by [[Eugenio Maria de Hostos]] Avenue and Marina Streets, and on the East by [[Rio Portugues]].<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=August 12, 2010 }} Retrieved February 17, 2010.</ref>  In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Cuarto is bounded in the North by Barrio [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], in the South by [[Canas Urbano]], in the West by [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]], and in the East by [[San Antón]].

==Features and demographics==
Cuarto has {{convert|0.2|sqmi|m2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area.  In 2000, the population of Cuarto was 3,011. The population density in Cuarto was 18,303 persons per square mile.<ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Cuarto+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved, February 17, 2010.</ref>

In 2010, the population of Cuarto was 1,999 persons, and it had a density of 12493.8 persons per square mile.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico:2010:population and housing unit counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

The communities of Belgica<ref>[http://www.letsgotoponce.com/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=161&Itemid=81 ''10,000 New Housing Units Expected for Ponce Historical District.'' Belgica sector to be renovated.] Retrieved April 15, 2010.</ref> and El Bosque are found in Barrio Cuarto as are several other smaller communities.

==Landmarks==
Cuarto is home to [[Parque de la Abolicion]] on its southwestern edge. The [[NRHP]]-listed [[Casa Paoli]], [[Old Ponce Casino]], [[Ponce Massacre Museum]], and [[Iglesia de la Santisima Trinidad|Iglesia de la Santísima Trinidad]] are all located in Barrio Cuarto. The Puerto Rico Islamic Center at Ponce (see side photo) is also located in Barrio Cuarto.
