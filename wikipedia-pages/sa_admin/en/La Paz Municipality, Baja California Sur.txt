'''La Paz''' is a [[municipality (Mexico)|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Baja California Sur]]. Its area of {{convert|20,275|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} makes it the municipality in Mexico with the fourth-largest area.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Municipios_de_mayor_y_menor_extension_territo Los Municipios con Mayor y Menor Extensión Territorial] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070303113550/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Municipios_de_mayor_y_menor_extension_territo |date=2007-03-03 }}, [[Instituto Nacional Para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]], [[SEGOB]] (Mexico.)  Accessed on line 15-II-2008.</ref>  It had a population of 290,286 inhabitants in the 2015 census.<ref name="saludbcs1">{{cite web |url=http://www.saludbcs.gob.mx/estadistica/Tarjetas%20Estadisticas%20Ejecutivas%202015.pdf |title=Poblacion Por Municipio, Superficie, Densidad De Poblacion |date= |accessdate=2018-06-17 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180121071415/http://www.saludbcs.gob.mx/estadistica/Tarjetas%20Estadisticas%20Ejecutivas%202015.pdf |archive-date=2018-01-21 |url-status=dead }} {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180121071415/http://www.saludbcs.gob.mx/estadistica/Tarjetas%20Estadisticas%20Ejecutivas%202015.pdf |date=2018-01-21 }}</ref>  Its municipal seat, also named [[La Paz, Baja California Sur|La Paz]], is the state capital.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 292,241.<ref name=CENSOLAPAZ2020>[https://biblioteca.setuesbcs.gob.mx/administrador/biblioteca/publicaciones/pdf/Estrategico_2023_La_Paz.pdf 2020 La Paz Census]</ref> The city of La Paz had a population of 250,241. Other than the city of La Paz, the municipality had 1,018 localities, the largest of which (with 2020 populations in brackets) were: [[Todos Santos, Baja California Sur|Todos Santos]] (7,185 hab.), [[El Centenario, Baja California Sur|El Centenario]] (6,221 hab.), classified as urban, and [[El Pescadero, Baja California Sur|El Pescadero]] (4,245 hab.), [[Chametla, Baja California Sur|Chametla]] (3,045 hab.), [[Melitón Albáñez Domínguez, Baja California Sur|Melitón Albáñez Domínguez]] (2,409 hab.) and [[Los Barriles, Baja California Sur|Los Barriles]] (1,674 hab.), classified as rural.<ref name=CENSOLAPAZ2020/>
