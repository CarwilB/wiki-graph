The '''Mario Briceño Iragorry Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 99,852.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[El Limón, Aragua|El Limón]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Mario Briceño Iragorry Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip {{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is named for the writer [[Mario Briceño Iragorry]].

==Demographics==
The Mario Briceño Iragorry Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 103,269 (up from 98,429 in 2000).  This amounts to 6.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1948.47|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Mario Briceño Iragorry Municipality is Carlos Javier Velarde, elected on October 31, 2004, with 52% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Mario briceño iragorry | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D4%26mun%3D13%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163549/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D4%26mun%3D13%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17 | access-date=2008-03-15 | url-status=live }}</ref>   He replaced Pedro Maurera shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de Julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Capital Mario Briceño Iragorry and Caña de Azúcar (created January 30, 1995).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
