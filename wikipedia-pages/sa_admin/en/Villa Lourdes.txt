'''Villa Lourdes ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Los Santos District]], [[Los Santos Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,075 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 58 of July 29, 1998, owing to the Declaration of Unconstitutionality of Law 1 of 1982.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 995.<ref name=inec/>
