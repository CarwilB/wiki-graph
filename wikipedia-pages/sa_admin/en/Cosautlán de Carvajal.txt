'''Cosautlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the montane central zone of the state, about 23.5&nbsp;km from [[Xalapa]], the state capital. It has a surface of 72.38 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|20|N|96|59|W|}}. The town has 2224 men and 2393 women.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/cosautlan-de-carvajal/|title=COSAUTLÁN DE CARVAJAL (Cosautlán de Carvajal, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave)|work=mexico.PueblosAmerica.com|access-date=2018-07-20|language=en-US}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Cosautlán is delimited to the north by [[Teocelo]], to the south by [[Tlaltetela]], to the east by [[Tlaltetela]], to the south by [[Oteapan]], and to the west by [[Ixhuacán de los Reyes]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30046a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Cosautlán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061026074326/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30046a.htm|archive-date=2006-10-26|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Cosautlán is cold all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 16,167inhabitants in 29 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSCOSAUTLAN>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Cosautl%C3%A1n-de-Carvajal.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Cosautlán de Carvajal CENSUS]</ref> About 25%, resides in the municipal seat; other localities include Limones (2,369 hab.), Piedra Parada (1,474 hab.), San Miguel Tlapéxcatl (1,098 hab.) and Emiliano Zapata (1,094 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSCOSAUTLAN/>

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[beans]].

==Culture==
In Cosautlán, in May takes place the celebration in honor to San Isidro Labrador, Patron of the town.
