'''García''',<ref>{{Cite web |title= |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |access-date=2023-12-31 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230324042920/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |archive-date=2023-03-24 }}</ref> officially '''José María García''' is a municipality located in the eastern part of Margarita Island, bordered by the Caribbean Sea and with the municipalities of Arismendi, Mariño and Díaz, in Venezuela. The municipal capital is [[El Valle del Espíritu Santo]].
