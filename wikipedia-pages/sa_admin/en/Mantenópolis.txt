'''Mantenópolis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 15,503 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|321|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/mantenopolis/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is a small town located in a valley with green mountains rising above it.  It has a central square with municipal offices- e.g., police, public works, and school within a short walking distance of each other.  There are many farms with livestock and crops in the neighboring countryside.  An extraordinarily large number of people from Mantenopolis who migrate to the United States reside on the Island of Martha's Vineyard in the state of [[Massachusetts]].
