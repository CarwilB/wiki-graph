'''Sucre''' is one of the 11 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 20,359.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Maripa, Venezuela|Maripa]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Sucre Municipality.{{Citation needed | date=December 2024}}

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]].

==Demographics==
The Sucre Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 27,218 (up from 20,108 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.8% of the state's population. The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.59|PD/sqkm}}.{{Citation needed | date=December 2024}} 

==Government==
The mayor of the Sucre Municipality is Juan Carlos Figarella Díaz, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 47% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis=0&edo=6&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | title=Alcalde de Municipio - SUCRE | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303200418/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis=0&edo=6&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2016-03-03}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones 30 de Julio de 2000 - Directorio de alcaldes electos |access-date=2008-03-18 |archive-date=2006-10-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four (five if you count the Capital Sucre section) [[parish]]es; Aripao, Guarataro, Las Majadas, and Moitaco.
