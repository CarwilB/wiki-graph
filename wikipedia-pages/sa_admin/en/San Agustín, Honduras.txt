'''San Agustín''' ({{IPA|es|san aɣusˈtin}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Copán Department|Copán]]. It is situated {{convert|36.4|km}} by road northwest of [[Santa Rosa de Copán]].

==History==
In the Territorial Political Division of 1896, San Agustín had the status of a small village (aldea). It was promoted to the status of municipality in 1930, though in 1940 it formed part of Santa Rosa, until it finally became a municipality again in 1957.<ref> Fiallos, Carmen. 1989. "Los Municipios de Honduras"</ref><ref name="Orden"/>

==Geography==
The municipality borders the municipality of [[Concepción, Ocotepeque|Concepción]] to the north, the municipalities of [[La Unión, Copán|La Unión]] and [[Cucuyagua]] to the south, the municipality of [[Santa Rosa de Copán]] to the east  and the municipality of [[Santa Rita, Copán|Santa Rita]] to the west.<ref name="Orden">[http://www.ordenamientoterritorial.hn/images/creacion_municipios/copan/san_agustin.pdf  Limites de Municipio] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190809191202/http://www.ordenamientoterritorial.hn/images/creacion_municipios/copan/san_agustin.pdf |date=2019-08-09 }} (in Spanish) Ordenamientoterritorial.hn, accessed 9 August 2019.</ref>

The municipality contains four villages: San Agustín (the capital), Cerro Negro, El Descombro, and Granadillal.<ref>Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas, Honduras. 2001 census. (in Spanish)</ref>

==Crafts and landmarks==
The area is a producer of pottery.<ref>John M. Weeks, Jane A. Hill, [https://books.google.com/books?id=yaGdAAAAMAAJ&q=San+Agust%C3%ADn,+Cop%C3%A1n The Carnegie Maya: the Carnegie Institution of Washington Maya research program, 1913-1957], p.192</ref> The main town contains the church Iglesia Asambleas de Dios Aposento and a small guesthouse, Rancho Anita.
