'''Fray Justo Santa María de Oro''' is the most south western [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 10,500 inhabitants in an area of 2,205&nbsp;km², and its capital city is [[Santa Sylvina]], which is located around 1,000&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

It takes its name from [[Justo de Santa María de Oro]].
