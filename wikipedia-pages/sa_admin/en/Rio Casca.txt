'''Rio Casca''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Ponte Nova. As of 2020, the population was 13,473 in a total area of {{cvt|384|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/rio-casca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
