'''Senador Salgado Filho''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,770 (2020 est.) in an area of 147.21 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1WC8 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located 492&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]] and east of [[Argentina]].
