'''Río Cuarto''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Río Cuarto (canton)|Río Cuarto]] canton, in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Río Cuarto was created on 11 October 2018 by Acuerdo Ejecutivo N°044-2018-MGP.<ref name="dta2017" />

Río Cuarto has an area of {{formatnum:}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:430}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

For the [[Costa Rica 2011 Census|2011 census]], Río Cuarto had not been created, but in the records there was a population of {{formatnum:11074}} inhabitants. However, its inhabitants were part of [[Río Cuarto (canton)|Río Cuarto]] canton when it was a district of [[Grecia (canton)|Grecia]] canton, and therefore this figure includes the population of the [[Santa Rita District, Río Cuarto|Santa Rita]] and [[Santa Isabel District, Río Cuarto|Santa Isabel]] districts created in 2018.<ref name="censo2011">{{cite web |language=es |title=Censo. 2011.
