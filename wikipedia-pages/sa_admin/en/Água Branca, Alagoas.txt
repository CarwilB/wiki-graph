'''Água Branca''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈaɡ͡wa ˈbɾɐ̃kɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population 20,230 (2020) and its area is 455&nbsp;km².<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
