'''Wenceslau Braz''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020, its population was estimated to be 2,548.

==History==
===Hydroelectric plant===
Between 1922 and 1932, German engineers constructed a 3.4 MW hydroelectric power station (Hidrelétrica Bicas do Meio) for the Itajubá Piquete power grid company (REPI). Two [[Overhead power line|transmission line]]s, mounted on steel lattice poles, supplied energy to the towns of [[Piquete]] and [[Itajubá]] and nearby weapon manufacturers.<ref>[https://www.wenceslaubraz.mg.gov.br/portal/servicos/1002/sobre-a-cidade Prefeitura de Wenceslau Braz - MG - Sobre a Cidade]</ref>
