'''Las Piedras District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Tambopata Province|Tambopata]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Bordered by the Rio Mavila on the northern boundary and the [[Las Piedras River (Peru)|Rio Las Piedras]] to the south, the district comprises typical uninhabited lowland neotropical [[rainforest]];<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Tobes |first1=Ibon |last2=Ramos-Merchante |first2=Adrián |last3=Araujo-Flores |first3=Julio |last4=Pino-del-Carpio |first4=Andrea |last5=Ortega |first5=Hernán |last6=Miranda |first6=Rafael |date=2021 |title=Fish Ecology of the Alto Madre de Dios River Basin (Peru): Notes on Electrofishing Surveys, Elevation, Palm Swamp and Headwater Fishes |journal=Water |language=en |volume=13 |issue=8 |pages=1038 |doi=10.3390/w13081038 |issn=2073-4441|doi-access=free |hdl=10171/60364 |hdl-access=free }}</ref> largely moist broadleaf evergreen or semi-evergreen with overstorey canopy and emergent crowns; medium layer canopy; lower canopy; and shrub level and understory.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Wade |first1=Timothy |last2=Riitters |first2=Kurt |last3=Wickham |first3=James |last4=Jones |first4=K. Bruce |date=2003-09-04 |title=Distribution and Causes of Global Forest Fragmentation |url=https://www.ecologyandsociety.org/vol7/iss2/art7/ |journal=Conservation Ecology |language=en |volume=7 |issue=2 |doi=10.5751/ES-00530-070207 |hdl=10535/2530 |issn=1195-5449|hdl-access=free |url-access=subscription }}</ref>

The forest-structure is influenced by the flood regimes of the [[Las Piedras River (Peru)|Las Piedras River]], a highly meandering, white-water affluent of the [[Madre de Dios River]].<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Hamilton |first1=Stephen |last2=Kellndorfer |first2=Josef |last3=Lehner |first3=Bernhard |last4=Tobler |first4=Mathias |date=2007-09-01 |title=Remote sensing of floodplain geomorphology as a surrogate for biodiversity in a tropical river system (Madre de Dios, Peru) |url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/223625844 |journal=Geomorphology |volume=89 |issue=1–2 |pages=23–38 |doi=10.1016/j.geomorph.2006.07.024|bibcode=2007Geomo..89...23H }}</ref>
