'''Guaraqueçaba''' is the easternmost municipality in the Brazilian state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], and one of the few coastal municipalities of the state. The short Ararapira River marks the border with [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].

Guaraqueçaba has historical significance, being among the first Portuguese settlements in the state of Paraná in 1545.

In 1938 the municipality of Guaraqueçaba was abolished and its territory was absorbed into that of Paranaguá. However, in 1947 Guaraqueçaba had it municipal rights restored.

==Conservation==

The municipality contains the {{convert|819|ha|adj=on}} [[Salto Morato Private Natural Heritage Reserve]], created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Reserva Natural Salto Morato – FGBPN}}|language=pt
 |title=Reserva Natural Salto Morato|publisher=FGBPN: Fundação Grupo Boticário de Proteção à Natureza
 |url=http://www.fundacaogrupoboticario.org.br/pt/o-que-fazemos/reservas-naturais/pages/reserva-natural-salto-morato.aspx|access-date=2016-11-18}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|400|ha|adj=on}} [[Sebuí Private Natural Heritage Reserve]], created in 1999.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RPPN Reserva Ecológica Sebuí – ICMBio}}|language=pt
 |title=RPPN Reserva Ecológica Sebuí |publisher=[[Chico Mendes Institute for Biodiversity Conservation]] (ICMBio)
 |url=http://sistemas.icmbio.gov.br/simrppn/publico/detalhe/205/|access-date=2016-11-15}}</ref>
It contains the [[Guaraqueçaba Ecological Station]] in the coastal mangrove area.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Guaraqueçaba|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=54|access-date=2016-05-07}}</ref>
It also holds 53% of the {{convert|34179|ha|adj=on}} [[Bom Jesus Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit established in 2012.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|REBIO Bom Jesus – ISA}}|language=pt |title=REBIO Bom Jesus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/583144|access-date=2016-06-05}}</ref>
