'''Entre-Ijuís''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 8,938 (according to IBGE 2010 census) in an area of 552.60&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23HX5 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It takes its name from the [[Ijuí River|Ijuí Grand River]], on which it is situated. It is located 435&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].

== History ==
'''Entre-Ijuís district''' is created in 1948, by Municipal Law nº 3 of August 26 of the same year. The municipality of Entre-Ijuís was created by state law nº 8558 of April 13, 1988 year upon majority votes in favor of emancipation on plebiscite held on December 20, 1987, year.<ref>{{Cite web |title=A Cidade - Prefeitura Municipal de Entre-Ijuís |url=https://www.entreijuis.rs.gov.br/cidade |access-date=2022-04-20 |website=www.entreijuis.rs.gov.br}}</ref>
