'''Hurlingham Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. It is in the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] [[urban area]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 176,505 inhabitants<ref name=Cen2010 /> in an area of {{convert|35.43|km2|sqmi|1|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Hurlingham, Buenos Aires|Hurlingham]], which is {{convert|23|km|mi|1|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]]. Hurlingham is known for the [[Hurlingham Club (Argentina)|Hurlingham Club]], a [[sports]] and [[polo]] club named after the [[Hurlingham Club]] in [[Fulham]], [[England]].
