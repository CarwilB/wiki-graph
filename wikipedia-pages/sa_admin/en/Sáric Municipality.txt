'''Sáric''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |title=Archived copy |access-date=4 January 2010 |archive-date=2 December 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131202235700/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its northern boundary is the [[U.S. state]] of [[Arizona]]. The population of the municipality (urban and rural areas) was 2,703 in 2010 living in an area of {{cvt|1676.23|km2|sqmi}}. The elevation is around 800 meters. At the city of [[Sáric]], the municipal seat, it is 780 meters.

==Name==
The Spanish name of the town derives from the [[O'odham language|O'odham]] name, ''Ṣaʼalk'', which means "mountain pass".<ref>[https://books.google.com/books?id=ZboXayslB7sC&dq=%22mountain+pass%22&pg=PA64 Estudios de lingüística y sociolingüística], 1994. {{ISBN|9686569324}}</ref>
==Geography==
Boundaries are with [[Pima County, Arizona|Pima County]] and [[Santa Cruz County, Arizona|Santa Cruz County]] in the U.S. state of [[Arizona]] to the north; [[Nogales, Sonora|Nogales]] to the east; [[Tubutama]] to the south; [[Altar Municipality|Altar]] to the west; and [[Magdalena de Kino]] to the southeast.   

The only settlements of over 100 population were Sáric (892) {{coord|31|06|11|N|111|22|40|W|scale:10000_source:INEGI}}, '''El Sásabe''' (1,295) {{coord|31|28|19|N|111|32|39|W|scale:10000_source:INEGI}}, and Cierro Prieto (353) {{coord|31|01|13|N|111|23|43|W|scale:10000_source:INEGI}}, as enumerated in the 2010 census.
There is an [[US-Mexico border|international border]] [[Sasabe Port of Entry|crossing]] leading to [[Sasabe, Arizona]].  

The land is high desert with extreme temperatures in the summer months.  The [[Altar River]] has its source north of the municipality in the Arizona mountains and flows south.  
==Economy==
The desert lands are poor and agriculture can only be practiced in the Altar River valley where wheat, corn, alfalfa, sorghum, and rye grass are grown.  Extensive cattle raising is carried out.
