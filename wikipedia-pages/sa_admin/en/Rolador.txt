'''Rolador''' is a [[municipality]] of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,296 (2020 est.) in an area of 295.01 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/44JS Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located 524&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
