The '''Arismendi Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 23,727.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archive-date=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Arismendi, Barinas|Arismendi]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Arismendi Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip Division Barinas] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Arismendi Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 20,305 (up from 18,941 in 2000). This amounts to 2.7% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls Cuadros] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|2.8|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls Cuadros] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Arismendi Municipality is Ramón Frías, elected on October 31, 2004, with 61% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=1&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio Arismendi
|website=cne.gob.ve|access-date=11 January 2024}}</ref> He replaced Noheli Abreu shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archive-date=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Arismendi, Guadarrama, La Unión, and San Antonio.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
