'''San Bernardo del Viento''' ({{IPA|es|sam beɾˈnaɾðo ðel ˈβjento}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Córdoba Department]], northern [[Colombia]]. It is best known for its elegant spices and unique style of dance. 

According to 2020 estimates, the population of San Bernardo del Viento was 36,512.
