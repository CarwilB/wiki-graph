'''Chumatlan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. 
==Geography==
It is located in the middle of the state, about 105&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 36.19 km2. It is located at {{coord|20|12|N|97|36|W|}}. The weather in Chumatlan is warm all year with rains in summer.

The municipality is delimited to the north by [[Coyutla]], to the north-east by [[El Espinal, Veracruz|Espinal]], to the south-west by [[Mecatlán]] and to the east by [[Coxquihui]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30064a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chumatlan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117004812/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30064a.htm|archive-date=2006-11-17|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The weather in Chumatlan is warm all year with rains in summer.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[coffee]].

==Culture==
A celebration in honor to Virgen de la Natividad, patron of the town, takes place in September.
