'''Naranjal''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "orange grove") is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|10|km}} south of the city of [[Córdoba, Veracruz|Córdoba]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Naranjal is located in central Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|600|and(-)|1500|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Fortín]] to the north, [[Amatlán de los Reyes]] to the northeast, [[Coetzala]] to the southeast, [[Zongolica]] to the south, [[Tequila, Veracruz|Tequila]] to the southwest, and [[Ixtaczoquitlán]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Naranjal, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|18.599|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.03% of the state's area.<ref name=snim/> 

The municipality is located on the southern bank of the [[Río Blanco (Veracruz)|Río Blanco]] and is watered by that river and its tributaries.<ref name=compendium/><ref name=encyclopedia/> Most of the land in Naranjal (90.41%) is used for agriculture. The dominant soils in the municipality are [[luvisol]]s, [[acrisol]]s and [[vertisol]]s.<ref name=compendium/>

Naranjal's climate is humid with rain falling mostly in the summer. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|18|and(-)|24|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|2400|and(-)|2600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Naranjal established its first town council in 1814.<ref name=encyclopedia/> On 28 March 1831 it was incorporated under the name of San Cristóbal Naranjal as a municipality in the canton of Orizaba in the state of Veracruz. Naranjal became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=113, 236 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Karla Nancy Abad Sosa.<ref>{{cite news |last=Lara |first=Brenda |url=https://www.faroveracruz.com/single-post/2017/12/29/Nancy-Abad-Sosa-Tomara-Protesta-con-Compromiso-y-Gratitud-a-su-Gente |title=Nancy Abad Sosa Tomara Protesta con Compromiso y Gratitud a su Gente |language=es |newspaper=Faro Veracruz |date=29 December 2017 |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Naranjal recorded a population of 4507 inhabitants living in 1049 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=107 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Naranjal |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 4559 inhabitants in Naranjal, of whom 71.11% reported being of [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|Indigenous]] ancestry and 19.61% reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African]] ancestry.<ref name=survey/> 

There are 12 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Naranjal, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2261 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Naranjal is farming. Coffee, bananas and oranges are the main crops grown, comprising respectively 80%, 10% and 7% of the agricultural land in the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://naranjalver14.files.wordpress.com/2014/06/pmd-naranjal-web.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |page=34 |publisher=Municipal council of Naranjal, Veracruz |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref>
