'''Santa Fe''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Colón (department)|Colón]].

Notable people from Santa Fe include [[Miriam Miranda]], a Honduran human rights activist who advocates for the land rights of the [[Garifuna|Garífuna]] people.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://nobelwomensinitiative.org/meet-miriam-miranda-honduras/|title=Meet Miriam Miranda, Honduras - Nobel Women's Initiative|date=2015-12-09|work=Nobel Women's Initiative|access-date=2018-08-05|language=en-US}}</ref>
