'''Santa Lucía''' is a central department of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan Province]] in Argentina.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.canal13sanjuan.com/san-juan/el-peronismo-gano-en-12-de-los-19-departamentos-de-san-juan-_a68fec2cb3c9bd063e329c15f|title=El peronismo ganó en 12 de los 19 departamentos de San Juan|work=Diario 13 San Juan|date=26 October 2025|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 43.565 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|45|km2}}, and its capital city is Santa Lucía, which is located around {{cvt|1,150|km}} from Capital Federal.
