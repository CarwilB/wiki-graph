'''Sabá''' is a town, with a population of 17,080 (2023 calculation),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Colón department, Honduras|Colón]].

==Sports==
The local football club, [[Unión Sabá]], play in the [[Liga Nacional de Ascenso de Honduras|Honduran second division]]. They play at the Estadio Municipal.
