'''Santiago Apoala''' is a village and municipality in the state of [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]], located north  of [[Oaxaca, Oaxaca|Oaxaca city]].  
It is part of the [[Nochixtlán District]] in the southeast of the [[Mixteca Region]].

The name “apoala” is from [[Nahuatl]] meaning “water that destroys”.<ref name="Guide">{{cite web|url=http://oaxaca-travel.com/guide/ecotourism.php?section=ecotourism&lang=es&getdoc=true&atractivo=06.01.03.04|title=Oaxaca’s Tourist Guide|access-date=2008-02-23|archive-date=2014-02-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140202174552/http://oaxaca-travel.com/guide/ecotourism.php?section=ecotourism&lang=es&getdoc=true&atractivo=06.01.03.04|url-status=dead}}</ref> It is {{convert|1970|m|-1|sp=us}} above sea level. It is part of the [[Mixtec]] area of Oaxaca state and is also known by its Mixtec name of “Yutatnoho” which means “river where the lords come from”. Its foundation date is unknown but local legends say this was one of the first areas settled by the Mixtecs.<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20451a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México Oaxaca}}</ref>

There are two waterfalls nearby called '''Cascadas de Apoala''' and '''Cola de Serpiente''' (snake’s tail). Each is more than {{convert|50|m|sp=us}} high and there are number natural springs in the area.<ref name="MexDesOax">{{cite journal |last= Quintanar Hinojosa |first=Beatriz  |date=August 2007 |title=La Mixteca y sus fabulosos conventos |journal=Guía México Desconocido: Oaxaca |volume= 137  |pages=82  }}</ref>

==The municipality==
As municipal seat, the village of Santiago Apoala has governing jurisdiction over the following communities:

Arriba de la Peña, Jazmín Morelos, San Antonio Nduayaco, Tierra Colorada, Unión Buena Vista (Yodocanit o Yodocani), and Unión Palo Solo
