'''Sáchica''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] of [[Colombia]] situated approximately {{convert|34|km|mi|abbr=on}} west of [[Tunja]] in the [[Ricaurte Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Sáchica borders [[Sutamarchán]] and [[Villa de Leyva]] in the north, in the east [[Chíquiza]], [[Samacá]] and [[Ráquira]] in the south and in the west Ráquira and Sutamarchán.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.sachica-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Sáchica] - accessed 07-05-2016</ref> 

== History ==
In the centuries before the arrival of the Spanish [[conquistador]]es, Sáchica was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. Evidence of long inhabitation has been found in the form of [[petroglyph]]s made by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] who were organized in the [[Muisca Confederation]]. The Muisca had their own [[Muisca religion|religion]] where their main gods were [[Sué]] (the Sun) and [[Chía (goddess)|Chía]]; the Moon. In Sáchica monuments to both celestial bodies have been constructed.

Modern Sáchica was founded on July 16, 1556, by Juan Velasco and Carlos Rojas.<ref name=website/> In 1574 a total of 2500 indigenous people were living in Sáchica, presently only 5% is indigenous, the remainder [[mestizo]].<ref name=website/>

In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca, Sáchica means "our present domain",<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Sáchica] - accessed 07-05-2016</ref> "fortress" or "mansion of the sovereign".<ref name=website/>

=== Rock art ===
In a rock shelter in Sáchica, rock art in the form of pictographs has been discovered. The archaeologist [[Eliécer Silva Celis]] pioneered in the study of them in the 1960s. Later research has been performed by [[Carl Henrik Langebaek]], Diego Martínez, Álvaro Botiva, Pedro Argüello García, and others. The black, red and white rock art is present at an altitude of {{convert|2210|m|ft|abbr=on}} at {{coord|73|31|15.926|W|5|35|29.058|N}} and shows human faces, Suns, maize, eyes, mountains, masks, and other figures.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.rupestreweb.info/pictografiassachica.html Las pictografias de Sáchica desde las narraciones orales de sus vecinos] - Rupestreweb</ref> The rock art has been produced in rock shelters of [[Lower Cretaceous]] formations.<ref>Silva Celis, 1962, p.12</ref>

== Paleontology ==
[[Fossil]]ized skeleton of [[pliosaurid]] ''[[Sachicasaurus vitae]]'' almost 10 m long was found in the Lower [[Cretaceous]] ([[Barremian]]) deposits near Sáchica. The generic name of this animal [[List of flora and fauna named after the Muisca|refers to Sáchica]] while the species epithet is meaning "life" in reference to "the vitality that this fossil has given to the Sáchica village since its discovery". Despite its large size, the found individual of ''Sachicasaurus'' is not an adult.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Páramo Fonseca |first1=María Eurídice |last2=Benavides Cabra |first2=Cristian David |last3=Gutiérrez |first3=Ingry Esmirna |year=2018 |title=A new large pliosaurid from the Barremian (Lower Cretaceous) of Sáchica, Boyacá, Colombia |url=https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/esrj/article/view/69916/pdf |journal=Earth Sciences Research Journal |volume=22 |issue=4 |pages=223–238 |doi=10.15446/esrj.v22n4.69916 |s2cid=135054193 |doi-access=free |bibcode=2018ESRJ...22..223P |access-date=2023-11-08 |archive-date=2023-04-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230407081106/https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/esrj/article/view/69916/pdf |url-status=live }}</ref>

The [[Ichthyosauria|ichthyosaur]] ''[[Platypterygius sachicarum]]'' (now ''[[Kyhytysuka sachicarum]]''), found in the [[Paja Formation]] of Villa de Leyva, has also been named after Sáchica.

== Economy ==
Sachiquense economy is based on religious [[tourism]], [[agriculture]]; onions and [[tomato]]es, and [[mining]]; [[gypsum]], [[marble]] and [[clay]].<ref name=website/>
