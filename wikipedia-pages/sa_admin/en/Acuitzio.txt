'''Acuitzio Municipality''' is a  [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] located in the central region of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Michoacán]].

==Demographics==
The municipality had a population of 10,052 inhabitants according to the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=2007-10-05 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2013-04-06 }}</ref>  Its municipal seat is the city of [[Acuitzio del Canje]].

==History==
A religious center near Acuitzio named '''Coatepec''' was founded by the [[Aztecs]] sometime in the 12th century. It is believed that the Aztecs abandoned the center to migrate to a place their Gods had mandated.<ref>[http://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Estatal/MICHOACAN/Municipios/Acuitzio/AcuitzioPlan01.pdf Ordenjuridico.gob.mx]{{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} ''"Antecedentes Históricos".'' Retrieved on October 5, 2007.</ref> Acuitzio is a word of [[Purépecha language|Purépecha]] origin that means "Place of the snakes".<ref>[http://www.patzcuaro.info/recorridos/acuitzio-patzcuaro.html Acuitzio-Pátzcuaro] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080704062623/http://www.patzcuaro.info/recorridos/acuitzio-patzcuaro.html |date=July 4, 2008 }} ''"Historia".'' Retrieved on October 5, 2007.</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality has an area of 180.13  square kilometres (0.30% of the surface of the state) and is bordered to the north and northwest by [[Morelia Municipality]], to the east by [[Madero Municipality]], to south by [[Tacámbaro Municipality]], and to the southwest by [[Huiramba Municipality]].
