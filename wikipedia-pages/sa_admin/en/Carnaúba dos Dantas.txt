'''Carnaúba dos Dantas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of Brazil. With an area of {{Convert|246.308|km²|sp=us}}, of which {{Convert|2.0242|km²}} is urban, it is located 234,1 km from [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]], the state capital, and 1.606 km from [[Brasília]], the federal capital. Its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 demographic census]] was 7.922 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), ranking as the 78nd most populous municipality in the state of Rio Grande do Norte.

== History ==
In the arid and challenging landscape of the [[Sertão|''sertões'']] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]], stands majestically Carnaúba dos Dantas, a municipality that embodies the saga of colonization and the flourishing of a resilient cultural identity. This is the story of a land forged by the courage of pioneers and enriched by the diversity of its origins.<ref name=":0">{{Cite book |last=Cascudo |first=Luiz da Câmara |author-link=Câmara Cascudo |url=http://archive.org/details/nomes-da-terra-lui-s-da-ca-mara-cascudo |title=Nomes da terra: Luís da Câmara Cascudo |date= |publisher=Fundação José Augusto |year=1968 |language=pt |via=the [[Internet Archive]]}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=História |url=https://www.carnaubadosdantas.rn.gov.br/historia/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240309134916/https://www.carnaubadosdantas.rn.gov.br/historia/ |archive-date=March 9, 2024 |access-date=March 9, 2024 |website=Prefeitura Municipal de Carnaúba dos Dantas |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The first records date back to ancient times, when the [[Kiriri people|Kiriri]], natives of the region, dominated the vast expanses of the Acauã Backlands. However, the true story begins to take shape with the arrival of the colonizers, among them Lieutenant [[Francisco Fernandes de Souza]], around 1700.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" />

It is with the prominent figure of [[Caetano Dantas Correia]] that Carnaúba dos Dantas truly begins to take shape. Described by historians like [[Luiz da Câmara Cascudo]] in his book "[[Nomes da Terra]]",<ref name=":0" /> Caetano Dantas Correia, originally from [[Pernambuco]], founded the Carnaúba Farm around 1740. His entrepreneurial vision and commitment to progress were fundamental in establishing the foundations of this community.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" />

The Carnaúba Farm not only became a center of economic activity but also a reference point for the growth and development of the region. Under the leadership of [[Antônio Dantas de Maria]], a direct descendant of Caetano Dantas, the [[chapel of São José]] was built in the early 20th century, marking the emergence of the settlement around it.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" />

The name ''Carnaúba dos Dantas'' pays homage to the rich cultural and historical heritage of this municipality. The imposing [[Copernicia prunifera|carnaúba]] trees, abundant in the region, symbolize the intrinsic connection with nature, while the surname "[[Dantas]]" evokes the lineage of the founders and the values they represented.<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" />

== Geography ==
[[File:CARNAÚBA DOS DANTAS - RN.jpg|thumb|View of the city of Carnaúba dos Dantas by [[aerial photography]]]]
The territory of Carnaúba dos Dantas covers {{Convert|246.308|km²|sp=us}},<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Brasil / Rio Grande do Norte / Carnaúba dos Dantas |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/panorama |access-date=2025-06-18}}</ref> of which {{Convert|2.0242|km²}} constitutes the urban area.<ref name="Tabela/8418">{{cite web |last=IBGE |year=2019 |title=Tabela 8418 - Áreas urbanizadas, Loteamento vazio, Área total mapeada e Subcategorias |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/8418#/n3/23/n6/2402402/v/12749/p/all/d/v12749%204/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=16 December 2024 |publisher=}}</ref> It sits at an average altitude of 306 meters above [[sea level]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Rio Grande do Norte |url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/rn.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070612212935/http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/rn.html |archive-date=2007-06-12 |access-date=2025-06-18 |website= |publisher=[[Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation]]}}</ref> Carnaúba dos Dantas borders these municipalities: to the north, [[Acari, Rio Grande do Norte|Acari]], [[Currais Novos]], and the state of [[Paraíba]]; to the south, [[Parelhas]] and Paraíba; to the east, Paraíba; and to the west, [[Jardim do Seridó]] and Acari.<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante">{{cite web |author=Institute of Economic Development and Environment of Rio Grande do Norte (IDEMA-RN) |date=2013 |title=Perfil do seu município: Carnaúba dos Dantas |url=http://adcon.rn.gov.br/ACERVO/idema/DOC/DOC000000000016640.PDF |url-status= |archive-url= |archive-date= |access-date=10 February 2022}}</ref> The city is located 175 km from the state capital Natal,<ref name="distancia">{{cite web |title=Distância entre Natal - RN, Brazil e Carnaúba dos Dantas - RN, Brazil |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-natal-rn-brazil-ate-carnauba-dos-dantas-rn |access-date=14 December 2024 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref> and 1,606 km from the federal capital [[Brasília]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Distância entre Brasilia - DF, Brazil e Carnaúba dos Dantas - Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-brasilia-df-brazil-ate-carnauba-dos-dantas-rn-brazil |access-date=25 July 2014 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref> Under the [[Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions|territorial division established in 2017]] by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality belongs to the immediate geographical region of Currais Novos, within the intermediate region of Caicó.<ref name="MesoMicro">{{cite web |last=IBGE |date=2024 |title=DTB_2024.zip |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/ |access-date=29 April 2025 |publisher= |language=pt-br |format=[[Zip (file format)|Zip file]]}} RELATORIO_DTB_BRASIL_2024_MUNICIPIOS.ods within zip file.</ref> Previously, under the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] and [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] divisions, it was part of the microregion of Seridó Oriental in the mesoregion of Central Potiguar.<ref>{{cite journal |year=1990 |title=Divisão regional do Brasil em mesorregiões e microrregiões geográficas |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |url-status=live |journal=Biblioteca IBGE |publisher=IBGE |volume=1 |page=44–45 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170925170313/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |archive-date=25 September 2017 |access-date=25 September 2017}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_DTB_20082">{{cite web |date=1 July 2008 |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/Organizacao/Divisao_Territorial/2008/DTB_2008.zip |access-date=11 October 2008 |work=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}</ref>

Its terrain is mainly formed by [[plateau]]s, and its climate is classified as [[Semi-arid climate|semi-arid]], with an annual rainfall precipitation of 550&nbsp;mm.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Taveira |first=Marcelo da Silva |date=2019 |title=Inventário Turístico 2019 |url=http://geoparqueserido.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Inventario-da-Oferta-Turistica-do-Municipio-de-Carnauba-dos-Dantas-2019.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240307212452/http://geoparqueserido.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Inventario-da-Oferta-Turistica-do-Municipio-de-Carnauba-dos-Dantas-2019.pdf |archive-date=March 7, 2024 |access-date=March 9, 2024 |website=geoparqueserido.com.br}}</ref> The region is characterized primarily by two types of [[caatinga]] vegetation. The Hyperxerophilous Caatinga consists of drier vegetation, dominated by an abundance of cacti and low, scattered plants. The Subdesertic Caatinga of Seridó represents the driest type of vegetation in the state, composed of sparse shrubs and small trees with pronounced xerophytic adaptations.<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" />

Among the most common plant species in these environments are ''[[Aspidosperma pyrifolium|pereiro]]'' (''Aspidosperma pyrifolium''), ''[[Cnidoscolus quercifolius|faveleiro]]'' (''Cnidoscolus quercifolius''), ''[[Pilosocereus pachycladus|facheiro]]'' (''Pilosocereus pachycladus''), ''[[Bromelia laciniosa|macambira]]'' (''Bromelia laciniosa''), ''[[Cereus jamacaru|mandacaru]]'' (''Cereus jamacaru''), ''[[Pilosocereus polygonus|xique-xique]]'' (''Pilosocereus polygonus''), and ''[[Mimosa tenuiflora|jurema-preta]]'' (''Mimosa tenuiflora'').<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" /> Carnaúba dos Dantas is located in an area susceptible to desertification classified as "Very Severe" according to the National Plan to Combat Desertification. It includes a conservation area, the Private Natural Heritage Reserve – RPRN Sernativo – created by Federal Ordinance No. 1922 of June 5, 1996. This reserve aims to manage land use and protect the [[Caatinga|Caatinga ecosystem]], covering an area of 378.5 hectares with a legal reserve of 75.7 hectares.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Carnaúba dos Dantas Rio Gr. do Norte Região Nordeste Do Brasil |url=https://www.visiteobrasil.com.br/nordeste/rio-grande-do-norte/polo-serido/carnauba-dos-dantas |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240309141654/https://www.visiteobrasil.com.br/nordeste/rio-grande-do-norte/polo-serido/carnauba-dos-dantas |archive-date=March 9, 2024 |access-date=March 9, 2024 |website=Visite o Brasil}}</ref>

The municipality of Carnaúba dos Dantas lies entirely within the watershed of the Piranhas-Açu river basin, being bathed by the sub-basin of the Carnaúba River. The main tributaries are the streams Malhada Vermelha, do Ermo, Boa Vista, and do Olho d'água. The largest reservoir in the municipality is the public reservoir Monte Alegre, with a capacity of 1,421,600 cubic meters of water, fed by the Olho d'água River.<ref>{{Cite web |first= |date=2012 |title=CARNAÚBA DOS DANTAS |url=http://www.idema.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/idema/socio_economicos/arquivos/Perfil%202008/Carnauba%20do%20Dantas.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120308185015/http://www.idema.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/idema/socio_economicos/arquivos/Perfil%202008/Carnauba%20do%20Dantas.pdf |archive-date=March 8, 2012 |access-date=March 9, 2024 |website=idema.rn.gov.br |publisher=Instituto de Desenvolvimento Sustentável e Meio Ambiente do Rio Grande do Norte – IDEMA}}</ref>

== Demography ==
In the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], the municipality had a population of 7,992 inhabitants and ranked 82nd in the state that year (out of 167 municipalities),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122232 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with 50.28% female and 49.71% male,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2402402/v/all/p/all/c2/all/c287/100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=2024-12-16}}</ref> resulting in a sex ratio of 98.90 (9,890 men for every 10,000 women),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Sexo / Razão de sexo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122248 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> compared to 7,429 inhabitants in the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]] (81.14% living in the urban area),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação do domicílio e sexo |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#/n6/2402402/v/all/p/all/c1/all/c2/all/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c1,t+c2/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> when it held the 86th state position.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2010 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true&tipo=ranking&indicador=27665 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Between the 2010 and 2022 censuses, the population of Carnaúba dos Dantas changed at an annual geometric growth rate of 0.61%.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Taxa de crescimento geométrico ( Unidade: % ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122278 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Regarding [[age group]] in the 2022 census, 68.55% of the inhabitants were between 15 and 64 years old, 19.89% were under fifteen, and 11.57% were 65 or older.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2402402/v/all/p/all/c2/6794/c287/6653,49108,49109,60040,60041,93070,93084,93085,93086,93087,93088,93089,93090,93091,93092,93093,93094,93095,93096,93097,93098,100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> The [[population density]] in 2022 was 32.45 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Território / Densidade demográfica ( Unidade: habitantes por quilômetro quadrado ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122231 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> There were 2,800 housing units with an average of 2.85 inhabitants per household.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 4712 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, Moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados e Média de moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4712#/n6/2402402/v/all/p/all/d/v5930%202/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=2024-01-06}}</ref>

The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) was considered medium, according to data from the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). According to the 2010 report published in 2013, its value was 0.659, ranking 20th in the state and 2,924th nationally (out of 5,565 municipalities),<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web |date=2010 |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610114916/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |access-date=October 11, 2010 |work=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano |publisher=United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)}}</ref> and the [[Gini coefficient]] rose from 0.4 in 2003<ref name="Gini2003">{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2003 |title=Mapa de pobreza e desigualdade |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/carnauba-dos-dantas/pesquisa/36/30246?ano=2003 |access-date=2024-12-21}}</ref> to 0.4 in 2010.<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Índice de Gini da renda domiciliar per capita segundo Município |url=http://tabnet.datasus.gov.br/cgi/ibge/censo/cnv/ginibr.def |access-date=17 June 2025 |website=DataSUS}}</ref> Considering only the longevity index, its value is 0.769, the income index is 0.624, and the education index is 0.577.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010" />
