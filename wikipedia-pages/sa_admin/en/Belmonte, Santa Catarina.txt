'''Belmonte''' is a municipality ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]]),'' in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. The name of the mayor is Genesio Bressain. It is located at 26º50'29" S, 53º34'32 W, and as of 2020 it has an estimated population of 2,709 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/belmonte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
