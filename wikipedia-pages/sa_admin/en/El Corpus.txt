''' El Corpus''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Choluteca department|Choluteca]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.hn/index.php/component/content/article?id=103|title=Departments and Municipals|website=National Institute of Statistics|language=Spanish}}</ref>

== History ==
The Spanish founded El Corpus after a mine called Clavo Rico in 1585. El Corpus became a municipality on July 27, 1827.<ref>{{Cite web|title=El Corpus, Choluteca – INE|url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/2018/09/21/el-corpus-choluteca-2018/|access-date=2021-04-18|website=www.ine.gob.hn}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, 75% of the population works in agriculture. Thirty-four percent uses some type of private water system, and 46% uses oil lamps for light. Ninety-five percent use firewood for cooking. Sixty-two percent has a basic education level and 5% of houses has at least one car.<ref>{{Cite web|title=CENSO DE POBLACIÓN Y VIVIENDA – INE|url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/censo-de-poblacion-y-vivienda/|access-date=2021-04-18|website=www.ine.gob.hn}}</ref>
