'''Trujillo''' is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]].

== Boundaries ==
It borders to the north with the province of [[Ascope Province|Ascope]], to the east with the province of [[Otuzco Province|Otuzco]], to the south with the province of [[Virú Province|Virú]], and to the west with the [[Pacific Ocean]].

== Political division ==
The province has an area of {{convert|1768.65|km2|sp=us}} and is divided into twelve districts. Nine of these are part of the [[Trujillo metropolitan area (Peru)|Trujillo metropolitan area]]. The districts of the province are:

* [[Trujillo District|Trujillo]]
* [[Alto Trujillo District|Alto Trujillo]]
* [[El Porvenir District, Trujillo|El Porvenir]]
* [[Florencia de Mora District|Florencia de Mora]]
* [[Huanchaco District|Huanchaco]]
* [[La Esperanza District, Trujillo|La Esperanza]]
* [[Laredo District|Laredo]]
* [[Moche District|Moche]]
* [[Poroto District|Poroto]]
* [[Salaverry District|Salaverry]]
* [[Simbal District|Simbal]]
* [[Víctor Larco Herrera, Peru|Víctor Larco Herrera]]

== Capital ==
The capital of the province is the city of [[Trujillo, Peru|Trujillo]], the third largest in the country.
