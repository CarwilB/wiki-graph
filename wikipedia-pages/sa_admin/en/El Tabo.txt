'''El Tabo''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[San Antonio Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]]. The commune  spans an area of {{convert|98.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> This town is a traditional   beach for middle class families.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], El Tabo has 7,028 inhabitants (3,537 men and 3,491 women). Of these, 6,604 (94%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 424 (6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 55.7% (2,515 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

===Notable residents===
*[[Nicanor Parra]], poet (in Las Cruces area)

==Administration==
As a commune, El Tabo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Emilio Jorquera Romero ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]). The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Edgardo Gómez Bravo ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Fernando García Jofré ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* Osvaldo Román Arellano ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Arturo Aravena Cisternas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Richard Copier Garrido ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* José Muñoz Osorio ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], El Tabo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by María José Hoffmann (UDI) and Víctor Torres (PDC) as part of the 15th electoral district, (together with [[San Antonio, Chile|San Antonio]], [[Santo Domingo, Chile|Santo Domingo]], [[Cartagena, Chile|Cartagena]], [[El Quisco]], [[Algarrobo, Chile|Algarrobo]] and [[Casablanca, Chile|Casablanca]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Francisco Chahuán Chahuán (RN) and [[Ricardo Lagos Weber]] (PPD) as part of the 6th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Coast).
