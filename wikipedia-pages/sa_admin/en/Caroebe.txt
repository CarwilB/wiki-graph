'''Caroebe''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kaɾoˈɛbi}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 10,656 (as of 2022) and its area is 12,066&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is the easternmost municipality in Roraima and the only one to border the state of [[Pará]]. Careoebe became an independent municipality in 1994.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/caroebe/historico |title=Caroebe |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

==Climate==
Caroebe has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with moderate rainfall from September to March and heavy rainfall from April to August.
