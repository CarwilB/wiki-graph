[[File:MinasGerais Municip Monjolos.svg|thumb|right|250px|Location of Monjolos in the state of Minas Gerais]]
'''Monjolos''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Home - Prefeitura Municipal de Monjolos - MG |url=https://prefeituramonjolos.mg.gov.br/ |access-date=2022-08-18 |website=prefeituramonjolos.mg.gov.br}}</ref> Its population in 2020 was 2,202, with a total area of {{convert|652|km²}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/monjolos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref> 

Monjolos is located at an elevation of {{convert|560|m}} east of the Rio das Velhas, an important tributary of the [[São Francisco River]]. It is connected to the nearest major population center, [[Curvelo]], by state highway MG-220. In 2001, this highway was not paved. The distance to Curvelo is {{convert|50|km}}, and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is {{convert|247|km}}. Neighboring  municipalities are [[Santo Hipólito]], [[Gouveia, Minas Gerais|Gouveia]], [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]], and [[Augusto de Lima, Minas Gerais|Augusto de Lima]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172032/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |date=10 April 2008}}</ref>

The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations is also important. The GDP in 2005 was R$12 million, with 6 million from services, 700 thousand from industry and 5 million from agriculture. There were 164 rural producers on {{convert|17,000|hectare}} of land. Only 22 farms had tractors in 2006.  Approximately 600 persons were dependent on agriculture; the main crops were beans and corn. There were 17,600 head of cattle in 2006. There were no banks in 2007 and 29 automobiles and 36 motorcycles, giving a ratio of 79 inhabitants per automobile.  This is much lower than the state average.<ref name="IBGE"/> 

There were two health clinics. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Montes Claros or Curvelo. Educational needs were met by two primary schools, a middle school and a pre-primary school.<ref name="IBGE"/> 

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.676 in 2000
*State ranking: 651 out of 853 municipalities in 2000
*National ranking: 3,353 out of 5,138 municipalities in 2000 
*Literacy rate: 76%
*Life expectancy: 65 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In 2000, the per capita income of R$145.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00, respectively.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally, the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent{{when|date=March 2021}} statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country - 0.467 - putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
