'''Alto Verá''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|886.8|km2}} and had a population of 11,503 inhabitants as per the 2022 census. It was established in December 1984, and was named as Alto Verá in January 1990.

==History==
Alto Verá district was established as per Law No.1101 on 20 December 1984 as "Doña Heriberta Stroessner de Iglesias" during the presidency of [[Alfredo Stroessner]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite news|url=https://www.adndigital.com.py/alto-vera-la-extrema-pobreza-al-podio-las-mas-progresistas/|title=Alto Verá, de la extrema pobreza al podio de las más progresistas|work=ADN Digital |date=27 January 2019|access-date=30 May 2026|lang=es|trans-title=Alto Verá, from extreme poverty to the podium of the most progressive |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230524160922/https://www.adndigital.com.py/alto-vera-la-extrema-pobreza-al-podio-las-mas-progresistas/| archive-date=24 May 2023}}</ref><ref name="Stat">{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ypy.html|title=Districts of Paraguay|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref> It was renamed to Alto Verá by Law No. 66 enacted on 19 January 1990.<ref name="Hist"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://paraguay.justia.com/nacionales/leyes/ley-66-jan-19-1990/gdoc|title=Ley N° 66/89 que restituye y asigna nuevas denominaciones a algunos distritos del país|work=Justia Paraguay|date=19 January 1990|access-date=30 May 2026|lang=es|trans-title=Law No. 66/89 restoring and assigning new names to certain districts of the country}}</ref>

==Geography==
Alto Verá is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] in Paraguay.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/|title=Paraguay, Administrative Division|work=City Population|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It occupies an area of approximately {{cvt|886.8|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/itap%C3%BAa/0725__alto_ver%C3%A1/|title=Alto Verá (District, Paraguay)|work=City Population|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It is in the north-central part of the department, approximately {{cvt|95|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]], and {{cvt|406|km}} from the national capital of [[Asuncion]].<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="ABC">{{cite news|url=https://www.abc.com.py/viajes/2022/02/23/itapua-cinco-destinos-destinos-poco-conocidos-y-paradisiacos-para-hacer-turismo-interno/|title=Itapúa: cinco destinos poco conocidos y paradisiacos, para hacer turismo interno|work=[[ABC Color]]|date=23 February 2022|access-date=30 May 2026|lang=es|trans-title=Itapúa: five little-known and paradisiacal destinations for domestic tourism}}</ref>

The [[San Rafael National Park]] is a protected reserve area in the district, and has several waterfalls.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="ABC"/> The Kanguery Biological Station is located within the national park. The Arroyo cavern is formed by a rock crevice, which leads to a {{cvt|20|ft}} high waterfall.<ref name="ABC"/>

Alto Vera is located at an elevation of {{cvt|298.47|m}} above sea level. It has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|23.63|C}}, and a precipitation of {{cvt|176.55|mm}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/itapua/alto-vera|title=Alto Vera|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics and economy==
As per the 2022 census, Alto Verá had a population of 11,503 inhabitants, a decrease compared to 13,799 inhabitants recorded in the 2002 census. As per the 2022 census, the district had 6,115 male inhabitants and 5,388 female inhabitants in 2022. Majority (91.3%) of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (8.7%) lived in the urban areas. About 32% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 7.3% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

The economy is majorly dependent on [[agriculture]], particularly the production of [[soybeans]].<ref name="Hist"/>
