'''Los Hidalgos''' is a small town in the [[Dominican Republic]] which straddles the border between the [[Puerto Plata Province]] and [[Valverde Province]]. The northern part is called El Mamey and the southern part Altos de los Acosta.The nearest major town is [[Santiago de los Caballeros]], about {{convert|60|km|abbr=on}} east.
