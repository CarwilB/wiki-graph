'''Río Grande''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Río Grande is located in northern Zacatecas. It borders the municipalities of [[Miguel Auza Municipality|Miguel Auza]], [[Juan Aldama Municipality|Juan Aldama]], and [[General Francisco R. Murguía Municipality|General Francisco R. Murguía]] to the north, [[Villa de Cos]] to the east, [[Cañitas de Felipe Pescador Municipality|Cañitas de Felipe Pescador]] to the southeast, [[Fresnillo]] and [[Saín Alto Municipality|Saín Alto]] to the south, and [[Sombrerete, Zacatecas|Sombrerete]] to the west.<ref name=infocard/> It covers an area of {{convert|1842.931|km2}} and comprises 2.4% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/>

Over 96% of the municipality's area lies in the drainage basin of the [[Aguanaval River]], the main watercourse in the area which flows south to north through the municipality.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Río Grande, Zacatecas |language=es |publisher=INEGI |accessdate=19 January 2017}}</ref> This river was known as the Río Grande in the 16th and 17th centuries despite its modest size.<ref>{{cite book |last=Bakewell |first=P. J. |title=Silver Mining and Society in Colonial Mexico, Zacatecas 1546-1700 |pages=[https://archive.org/details/silverminingsoci0000bake/page/2 2]–3 |url=https://archive.org/details/silverminingsoci0000bake |url-access=registration |publisher=[[Cambridge University Press]] |date=1971 |isbn=0521082277}}</ref> The highest point in the municipality is the Cerro Tetilla Grande located at {{coord|23|41|N|102|51|W|region:MX|display=inline}} with an elevation of {{convert|2370|m}} above sea level.<ref name=notebook/>

Río Grande's climate is [[Semi-arid climate|semiarid]] temperate with an average annual temperature of {{convert|19|C}}.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average annual precipitation ranges between 300 and 600 millimetres.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Prior to the arrival of the Spanish, the Río Grande area was inhabited by [[Chichimeca]] peoples such as the [[Guachichil]] and {{Ill|Irritila|es}}. Oral history holds that a Spanish settlement was founded at Río Grande on 18 August 1562. The town was recognized by the [[Royal Audiencia of Guadalajara]] on March 5, 1689 under the name Santa Elena de Río Grande.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32039a.html |title=Río Grande |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=19 January 2018}}</ref>

Río Grande was first incorporated as an ''[[ayuntamiento]]'' in the ''partido'' of [[Nieves, Zacatecas|Nieves]] in Zacatecas on 29 October 1833. It became a free municipality on 19 August 1916.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=59, 117 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and fourteen trustees (''regidores''), eight elected by [[relative majority]] and six by [[proportional representation]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://riograndezac.gob.mx/informatica/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/BANDO-DE-POLICIA-Y-BUEN-GOBIERNO-DEL-MUNICIPIO-DE-RIO-GRANDE-ZACATECAS.pdf |title=Bando de Policía y Buen Gobierno del Municipio de Río Grande, Zacatecas |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=Government of Río Grande |date=4 December 2017 |accessdate=19 January 2018}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is Mario Cordova Longoria.<ref name=officialsite/>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Río Grande recorded a population of 62,693 inhabitants living in 15,628 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=039 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Río Grande |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |accessdate=19 January 2018}}</ref> It recorded a population of 63,880 inhabitants in the 2015 Intercensal Survey.<ref name=survey/>

There are 58 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> two of which are classified as urban:
*Río Grande, the municipal seat located on the Aguanaval River, which recorded a population of 32,944 inhabitants in the 2010 Census; and
*Loreto, a suburb of the municipal seat located to its northeast, which had 2637 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Río Grande is agriculture.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Beans are its main crop; the municipality is located in the middle of Mexico's main bean-producing region.<ref>{{cite book |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/380/702825118396/702825118396_2.pdf#[3,{%22name%22:%22Fit%22} |title=El Frijol en el Estado de Zacatecas |page=19 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=1997 |accessdate=19 January 2018 |isbn=970-13-1677-0}}</ref> The [[gross domestic product]] of the municipality was unofficially estimated at 2655.8 million [[Mexican peso]]s in 2010.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=González-Estrada |first1=Adrián |last2=Gallegos-Cedillo |first2=Gilberto |date=2014 |title=The gross domestic product of municipalities of Mexico: II. Estates M-Z |url=http://www.scielo.org.mx/pdf/remexca/v5n8/v5n8a6.pdf |journal=Revista Mexicana de Ciencias Agrícolas |volume=5 |issue=8 |pages=1405–1421 |accessdate=19 January 2018}}</ref>
