'''Chapantongo ''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. 

The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|298.1|km2}}. In the 2020 [[INEGI]] census, the municipality reported a total population of 12,967.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chapantongo|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/hidalgo/13017__chapantongo/|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220223175957/https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/hidalgo/13017__chapantongo/ |archive-date=2022-02-23 |work=CityPopulation.de|publisher=Source: Instituto Nacional de Estadística Geografía e Informática, México (web).|accessdate=February 23, 2022}}</ref>
