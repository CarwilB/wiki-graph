'''Nobres''' is a city in the state of [[Mato Grosso]], [[Brazil]]. It is located approximately 140 kilometers from [[Cuiabá]] on the south slopes of the [[Serra Azul]].

Tourists are encouraged to visit the region for its beautiful waterways and limestone caves.<ref name="Chapada Explorer">[http://ecopantanal.com.br/Chapada-dos-Guimaraes/Chapada-dos-Guimaraes-chapada-pantanal-639.html ''Nobres Chapada Explorer'']</ref> 

The municipality contains the {{convert|12513|ha}} [[Gruta da Lagoa Azul State Park]] (Cave of the Blue Lagoon State Park), created in 1999.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Gruta da Lagoa Azul – ISA}}|language=pt |title=PES Gruta da Lagoa Azul|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental|url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/2731|access-date=2016-08-02}}</ref> The rock deposits in the area have also been used to study post-glacial carbonates.<ref name="Science">{{Cite web |url=http://www.vssagi.com/igcp478/AbstractsVSSAGI/307.pdf |title=''Chemostratigraphy of Ediacaran Post-Glacial Carbonates'' Figueiredo M.F. et al. |access-date=2010-02-23 |archive-date=2020-08-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200806144052/http://www.vssagi.com/igcp478/AbstractsVSSAGI/307.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality contains most of the {{convert|11328|ha}} [[Águas do Cuiabá Ecological Station]], a fully protected conservation unit in the [[cerrado]] biome.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=ESEC Águas do Cuiabá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/37|access-date=2016-05-24}}</ref> 

In 2016, its population was estimated at 14.938 people.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?lang=&codmun=510590&search=&#124;&#124;infogr%E1ficos:-informa%E7%F5es-completas|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Mato Grosso {{!}} Nobres {{!}} Estimativa da População 2016|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2017-06-29}}</ref> The two major religious faiths are [[Roman Catholic]] and [[Protestant]].<ref name="Collins Maps Information">[http://www.collinsmaps.com/maps/Brazil/Mato-Grosso/Nobres/P663529.00.aspx Collins Maps information page]</ref>
