'''Guaçuí''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 31,122 (2020) and its area is 469&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/C1O]</ref>

The city's economy is based mainly on coffee production and livestock farming.<ref>[https://www.jornalfato.com.br/cidades/sabado-foi-dia-de-negocios-da-pecuaria-e-cafe-em-guacui,427717.jhtml Sábado foi dia de negócios da pecuária e café em Guaçuí]</ref>
