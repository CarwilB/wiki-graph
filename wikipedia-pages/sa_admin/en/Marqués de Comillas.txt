'''Marqués de Comillas''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]]. Its municipal seat is [[Zamora Pico de Oro]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 9,856,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Marqués de Comillas|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=116|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305201520/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=116|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 8,580 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Marqués de Comillas|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07116a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / [[Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas]]|accessdate=July 25, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930023642/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07116a.htm|archivedate=September 30, 2007}}</ref> It covers an area of 933&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality had 28 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: Zamora Pico de Oro (1,734), classified as urban, and [[Emiliano Zapata, Chiapas|Emiliano Zapata]] (1,082), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
