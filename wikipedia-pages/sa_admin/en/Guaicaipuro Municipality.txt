'''Guaicaipuro''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 280,687.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Los Teques]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Guaicaipuro Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 }}</ref> The municipality is named for the sixteenth century ''[[cacique]]'' [[Guaicaipuro]].

==History==
The city of Los Teques was founded in 1777 and was named after the Aractoeques Carabs, an indigenous tribe that once inhabited the area.  On February 13, 1927, the capital of Miranda was moved to this city from [[Petare]] (before being in Petare, the capital of Miranda was in [[Ocumare del Tuy]]).{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Geography==
Temperature: Varies from 18 and 26 degrees Celsius.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
The Guaicaipuro Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 280,687 (up from 240,731 in 2000).  This amounts to 9.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|424.64|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Guaicaipuro Municipality is Francisco Garcés, elected on December 8, 2013, with 52% of the vote.{{citation needed|date=September 2023}} The municipality is divided into seven [[parish]]es; Los Teques, [[Altagracia de La Montaña]], Cecilio Acosta, El Jarillo, Paracotos, San Pedro, and Tácata.<ref name=autogenerated1 />

==Transportation==
On November 3, 2006, President [[Hugo Chávez]] inaugurated the [[Los Teques Metro]].  This metro system is connected to the [[Caracas Metro]].
