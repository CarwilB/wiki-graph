'''Municipality A''' is one of the eight municipalities into which the department of [[Montevideo Department|Montevideo]], in [[Uruguay]], is administratively divided. It is headquartered on Avenida Carlos María Ramírez corner Rivera Indarte in the city of [[Montevideo]].<ref>[http://www.observa.com.uy/Municipales2010/nota.aspx?id=96530&ex=33&ar=1&fi=39 Information on the eight new municipalities of Montevideo at observa.com.uy]{{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100807185801/http://www.observa.com.uy/Municipales2010/nota.aspx?id=96530&ex=33&ar=1&fi=39|date=2010-08-07}}</ref>

It was created through decree 11,567 of September 13, 2009,<ref>[http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/_web/leyes/2009/09/CM831.pdf Presidency of Uruguay: Law No. 18,567 of September 13, 2009]</ref> later ratified.<ref>[http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/_web/leyes/2010/02/CM909.pdf Law 18,644 of February 12, 2010]{{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100326124707/http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/_web/leyes/2010/02/CM909.pdf|date=2010-03-26}}</ref><ref>[http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/sci/leyes/2010/03/CM3.pdf Law 18,653 of March 15, 2010]</ref>
