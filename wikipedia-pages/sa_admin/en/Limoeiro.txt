'''Limoeiro''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[limoˈeɾu]}}) is a city in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Vicência]]   ('''N''');  [[Passira]] and [[Feira Nova, Pernambuco|Feira Nova]]  ('''S''');  [[Carpina]], [[Lagoa do Carro]] and [[Buenos Aires, Pernambuco|Buenos Aires]]   ('''E'''); [[Bom Jardim, Pernambuco|Bom Jardim]], [[Salgadinho, Pernambuco|Salgadinho]] and [[João Alfredo]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 269.97&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 138 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and Tracunhaém rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caducifólia]] forest
* Climate - hot, tropical, and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.1 °C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 91 km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Limoeiro are based in industry, commerce, tourism and [[agribusiness]]; especially plantations of [[banana]]s and [[sugarcane]], and the raising of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s and [[chicken]]s.
