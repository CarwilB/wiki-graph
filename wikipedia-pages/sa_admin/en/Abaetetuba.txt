The city of '''Abaetetuba''' is located in [[Pará]] State, [[Brazil]]. The population of the municipality is 158,188 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]).<ref name="IBGE" /> It is located in the [[Northern Region of Brazil|northern region of Brazil]] and in the northeastern part of Pará, at a [[latitude]] of 01°43'05" [[south]] and [[longitude]] 48°52'57" [[west]].<ref name="ibge1">{{cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/abaetetuba/panorama | title=State of Pará, Municipality of Abaetetuba, Panorama |date=2015 |publisher=IBGE |accessdate=9 May 2018}}</ref><ref name="geografos">{{cite web | url=http://www.geografos.com.br/cidades-para/abaetetuba.php |title=Abaetetuba, Pará - PA |publisher=Geógrafos |accessdate=9 April 2018}}</ref> It lies on the right bank of the Tocantins River mouth. Abaetetuba is the hub city of the Baixo Tocantins Region and the 6th most populous city in the state. The municipality consists of two districts: Abaetetuba (headquarters) and [[Beja (Abaetetuba)|Beja]] ([[resort]], which was initially the indigenous region of Samaúma).<ref>{{Cite web|title=FRANCINETI MARIA RODRIGUES CARVALHO|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/abaetetuba/panorama|url-status=live|access-date=2021-07-12|website=cidades|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20180410134841/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/abaetetuba/panorama  |archive-date = 10 April 2018  }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
"Abaetetuba" comes from the [[Tupi language]] and means "group of true men", from the words ''abá'' ("man"), ''eté'' ("true") and ''tyba'' ("group").<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm |title=Curso de Tupi Antigo |access-date=2012-09-10 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120809053512/http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm |archive-date=9 August 2012 }}</ref>

== History ==

First settlements in the region date to 1635, when [[Order of Friars Minor Capuchin|Capuchin]] friars originating in the convent of Una in [[Belém]] joined a village of nomadic indigenous tribes in a settlement with the name of '''Samaúma''', though later baptized as the district of '''Beja''' by governor [[Francisco Xavier de Mendonça Furtado]].{{fact|date=May 2026}}

Later, in 1724, [[Francisco de Azevedo Monteiro]], dedicated to the exploration of local amazonian spices and goods, acquired a [[Sesmaria]] in the region, founding a settlement on the shores of the river Maratauíra in a location protected from the tides by the island of Sirituba, which would later form the urban center of the current city.<ref>{{Cite web|title=History of the Municipality (Official City Website)|access-date=2022-01-25|url=https://www.abaetetuba.pa.gov.br/omunicipio.php|language=pt}}</ref>

Known then as '''Abaeté''', the settlement was granted the status of autonomous municipality and separated from the capital of [[Belém]] in 1880 through law nº 973, Abaeté would later be granted the status of city in 1895 through state law nº 324 and finally change its name to Abaetetuba on 30 December 1943, through decree-law nº 4.505.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Comarcas in the State of Pará|access-date=2022-01-25|url=https://www.tjpa.jus.br/PortalExterno/indexComarcas.xhtml|language=pt}}</ref>
