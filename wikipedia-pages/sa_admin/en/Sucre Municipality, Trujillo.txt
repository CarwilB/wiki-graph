'''Sucre''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 214&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 30,715 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following four parishes:

*El Paraíso
*Junín
*Sabana de Mendoza
*Valmore Rodríguez
