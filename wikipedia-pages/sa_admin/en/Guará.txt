'''Guará''' is a municipality situated in the northern part of the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 21,308 (2020 est.) in an area of 362 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/EGI Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 573&nbsp;m.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=351770 IBGE — Cidades] {{in lang|pt}}</ref>

This place name comes from the [[Tupi language]] for two animals common in the region, the [[maned wolf]] (''Chrysocyon brachyurus'') and the [[scarlet ibis]] (''Eudocimus ruber'').<ref>{{citation | author=[[Henrique de Beaurepaire-Rohan]] | entry=Guará | title=Diccionario de Vocabulos Brazileiros | publisher=Imprensa nacional | year=1889 | page=71a | url=https://archive.org/details/diccionariodevo00rohagoog}}</ref>
