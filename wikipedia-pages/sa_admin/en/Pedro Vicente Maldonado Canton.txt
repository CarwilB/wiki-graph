'''Pedro Vicente Maldonado''' is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] in the west of the province of [[Pichincha (province)|Pichincha]] in [[Ecuador]]. It is named after the 18th-century Ecuadoran scientist [[Pedro Vicente Maldonado]]. [[Arashá Spa]], a tropical forest resort, is located in this canton.<ref name="Louis2009">{{cite book|last=Louis|first=Regis St.|title=Ecuador and the Galápagos Islands|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=LcRxfTdcQsgC&pg=PA149|access-date=22 November 2011|date=1 August 2009|publisher=Lonely Planet|isbn=978-1-74104-828-5|page=149}}</ref>
