'''San Juanito''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]]. San Juanito has an elevation of 2,018 metres. San Juanito is situated southwest of Vda. El Tablón, and south of Alto Buenavista.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Juanito |url=https://en.mapy.cz/zakladni?x=-73.6559198&y=4.4761135&z=10&source=osm&id=13195751 |access-date=2022-03-30 |website=Mapcarta |language=en}}</ref>
