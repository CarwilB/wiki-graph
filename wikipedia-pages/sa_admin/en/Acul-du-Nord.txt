'''Acul-du-Nord''' ({{IPA|fr|akyl dy nɔʁ}}; {{langx|ht|Akil dinò}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Acul-du-Nord Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].

== History ==
In 2010, Acul-du-Nord mayor Patrick Julien announced that elections would be cancelled due to violence, telling ''[[Agence France-Presse]]'' that "Men armed with machetes ransacked six polling stations".<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010-11-28 |title=Haiti candidates denounce election |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2010/11/28/haiti-candidates-denounce-election |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Al Jazeera |language=en}}</ref>

Following the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]], the [[United Nations Stabilisation Mission in Haiti]] (MINUSTAH) funded the construction of a new Court of Peace building in the commune.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011-09-10 |title=Haiti - Reconstruction : Inauguration of the new Court of Peace of Acul-du-Nord |url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-3772-haiti-reconstruction-inauguration-of-the-new-court-of-peace-of-acul-du-nord.html |access-date=2023-12-19 |website=Haiti Libre}}</ref>

== Communal sections ==
The commune consists of six [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Camp-Louise]], urban (Camp Louise neighborhood) and rural
* [[Bas de l'Acul]], urban (town of Acul du Nord) and rural
* [[Mornet]], rural
* [[Grande Ravine (communal section)|Grande Ravine]], rural
* [[Coupe à David]], rural
* [[La Soufrière (communal section)|La Soufrière]], urban (Soufrière neighborhood) and rural
