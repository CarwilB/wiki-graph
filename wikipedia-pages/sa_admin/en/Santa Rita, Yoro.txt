'''Santa Rita''' is a town and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Yoro department|Yoro]]. The [[Humuya River]] passes through it.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.laprensa.hn/honduras/535140-97/habilitan-paso-en-el-rio-humuya-de-santa-rita |title=Habilitan paso en el río Humuya de Santa Rita|author=Efrain V. Molina|work=Diario La Prensa |language=es |accessdate=24 November 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.laprensa.hn/honduras/valledesula/431149-98/multaran-a-los-que-contaminan-el-rio-humuya |title=Multarán a los que contaminan el río Humuya|author=Efrain Molina|work=Diario La Prensa |language=es |accessdate=24 November 2015}}</ref>

The town of Santa Rita has a population of 15,230 (2023 calculation).<ref>{{cite web |title=Honduras |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ |website=Citypopulation.de |access-date=1 July 2023}}</ref>

In 1684, it was a hamlet called Benque La Laguneta. It later became the village of Santa Rita El Negrito. The place was established as a town in 1959 with part of the municipality of [[El Negrito]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Santa Rita municipality had a population of 20,301. Of these, 89.73% were [[Mestizo]], 9.20% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.77% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.17% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.12% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
