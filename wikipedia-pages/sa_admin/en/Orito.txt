'''Orito''' is a town and municipality in the [[Putumayo Department]], [[Republic of Colombia]]. The town is just south of the confluence of the [[Patascoy River|Patascoy]] and [[Luzonyaco River]]s. It is located {{convert|43|km}} north of the border with [[Ecuador]].

The town is served by [[Orito Airport]].

==Climate==
Orito has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
