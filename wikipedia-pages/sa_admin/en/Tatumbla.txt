'''Tatumbla''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].

Tatumbla is located in the mountains above [[Tegucigalpa]], about a 45-minute bus ride.  

As of 2013 census, the population of Tatumbla is 7,197.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Localities in Tatumbla (Francisco Morazán, Honduras) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map, Location, Weather and Web Information |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/franciscomorazan/0825__tatumbla/ |access-date=2024-08-06 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
