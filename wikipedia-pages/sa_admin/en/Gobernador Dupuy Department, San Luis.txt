'''Gobernador Dupuy''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref name=":cp">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-admin.php|title=Argentina: Administrative Division|publisher=citypopulation}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book|url=http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|title=CENTRO LATINOAMERICANO Y CARIBEÑO DE DEMOGRAFÍA (CELADE)|last=|first=|publisher=World Bank|year=1970|isbn=|location=|pages=|archive-date=2019-01-15|access-date=2019-02-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190115132401/http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|url-status=dead}}</ref>

With an area of {{convert|19632|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it is the biggest department in the province. It borders to the north with the departments of [[Juan Martín de Pueyrredón Department|Juan Martín de Pueyrredón]] and [[General Pedernera Department|General Pedernera]], to the east with the provinces of [[Córdoba Province, Argentina|Córdoba]] and [[La Pampa Province|La Pampa]], to the south with La Pampa and to the west with [[Mendoza Province]].
