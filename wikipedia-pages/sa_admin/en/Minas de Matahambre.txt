'''Minas de Matahambre''' ({{IPA|es|minas ðe mataˈambɾe}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Pinar del Río Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was declared a National Monument of Cuba.<ref name=cnpc>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cnpc.cult.cu/cnpc/monumen/Pag002.htm |title=National Monuments in Cuba |author=National Council for Cultural Heritage |accessdate=2007-10-09 |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071211213023/http://www.cnpc.cult.cu/cnpc/monumen/Pag002.htm |archivedate=2007-12-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality faces the [[Gulf of Mexico]] to the north, where the [[cay]]s of the [[Colorados Archipelago]] are developed off-shore. It is bordered by the municipalities of [[Mantua, Cuba|Mantua]], [[Guane]], [[San Juan y Martínez]] and [[Viñales]].

The municipality includes the villages of Baja, Cabezas, Pons, La Sabana, Río del Medio, [[Santa Lucía (Minas de Matahambre)|Santa Lucía]], Sitio Morales and Sumidero.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Minas de Matahambre had a population of 30,946.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|858|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|36|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Culture==
The [[Minas de Matahambre Municipal Museum]] holds objects and photography collections of local history.
