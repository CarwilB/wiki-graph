'''San Francisco de la Paz''' ({{IPA|es|saɱ fɾanˈsisko ðe la ˈpas}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the centre of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]].

First known as ''San Francisco Zapote'', the name was changed by bishop Francisco de Paula Campoy y Pérez, for the actual name. The town is located in a small valley formed by the hills "La Cruz" (The Cross) and "Cayo Blanco" (White Cay) in the North and the hill "El Ocotal" in the South.

The extension of San Francisco de la Paz is 540.2&nbsp;km².

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Francisco de la Paz municipality had a population of 19,216. Of these, 98.64% were [[Mestizo]], 0.51% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.50% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.23% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.12% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
