'''General Pedernera''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|15057|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the department of [[Coronel Pringles Department, San Luis|Coronel Pringles]] and [[Chacabuco Department, San Luis|Chacabuco]], to the east with [[Córdoba Province, Argentina|Córdoba Province]], to the south with [[Gobernador Dupuy Department, San Luis|Gobernador Dupuy]] and to the west with [[Juan Martín de Pueyrredón Department|Juan Martín de Pueyrredón]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=INDEC|date=October 14, 2017|title=Provincia según departamento. Población, superficie y densidad. Años 1991 y 2001|url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/74000C22.xls|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171014034823/http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/74000C22.xls|archive-date=2017-10-14|url-status=live}}</ref>

Its name pays homage to Lieutenant-general [[Juan Esteban Pedernera]] who fought with the [[Regiment of Mounted Grenadiers]] during the [[Argentine War of Independence]] and became the interim president of the [[Argentine Confederation]].

On October 14, 2009, the government of San Luis passed the decree of need and urgency No. 2884 MGJyC 09, which created the municipality of the Ranquel people-nation in 66.000 hectares of the General Pedernera Department, so as to provide a settlement for members of the [[Ranquel]] native people. In addition, a delegate was designed to manage the municipality, organized according to the traditions and customs of the Ranquel people.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Cámara de Diputados de la Provincia de San Luis|url=https://diputados.sanluis.gob.ar/|access-date=2021-10-22|website=diputados.sanluis.gob.ar}}</ref>       

According to the 2010 national census, there are 125.470 inhabitants in the department.<ref>{{Cite web|last=INDEC|date=December 22, 2010|title=Censo 2010 Argentina- San Luis: departamentos|url=http://www.censo2010.indec.gov.ar/preliminares/cuadro_sanluis.asp|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101222011910/http://www.censo2010.indec.gov.ar/preliminares/cuadro_sanluis.asp|archive-date=2010-12-22|url-status=live}}</ref>
