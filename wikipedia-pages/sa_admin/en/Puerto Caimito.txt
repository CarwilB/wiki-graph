'''Puerto Caimito''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[La Chorrera District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 16,951 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,623; its population as of 2000 was 7,198.<ref name=inec/>  It was the boyhood home of [[New York Yankees]] [[relief pitcher]] [[Mariano Rivera]].
