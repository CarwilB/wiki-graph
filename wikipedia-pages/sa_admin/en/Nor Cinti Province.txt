'''Nor Cinti''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Chuquisaca Department|Chuquisaca]]. Its capital is [[Camargo, Chuquisaca|Camargo]].
