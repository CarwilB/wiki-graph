'''Morro Redondo''' (English: ''Rounded Hill'') is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 6,589 (2020 est.) in an area of 244.65&nbsp;km².<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/1E4K Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located 35&nbsp;km (21.75&nbsp;mi) from [[Pelotas]].

==Demographics==

[[File:Morro Redondo.JPG|left|thumb|The countryside around Morro Redondo]]
[[File:Morro Redondo city hall.JPG|left|thumb|City hall]]
Much of the population have Portuguese origin mainly from the [[Azores]] and [[Germany|Germans]] mainly from [[Pomerania]], the remainder were from [[Italy]].

==Geography==
Most of the area of the municipality are above 200&nbsp;m (656.16&nbsp;ft) of elevation.  A vegetation of this area is mainly composed of fields with low and herbaceous vegetation ([[pampa]]). Small groves of cultivated trees ([[Salix babylonica|Babylon willow]], [[eucalyptus]], [[pine]], [[cypress]], [[acacia]], [[Populus|poplar]] and [[platanus]]) and native trees ([[Cockspur coral tree]] and [[araucaria angustifolia]]) are also found.

The climate is subtropical or temperate, with pleasant summers, cool winters and frequent [[frost]]s (with an average of 20 per year). The hottest month is January with an average temperature of 21.7&nbsp;°C (71.06&nbsp;°F), and the coldest month is July with an average temperature of 11.7&nbsp;°C (53.06&nbsp;°F). The wettest month is February, with 150&nbsp;mm (5.9&nbsp;in) of precipitation. The average annual temperature in the municipality is 16.7&nbsp;°C (62.06&nbsp;°F). The average annual precipitation is 1,400&nbsp;mm (55.11&nbsp;in).
