'''Vilcas Huamán''' (from ''[[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Willka Waman''''') is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] located in the eastern part of the [[Ayacucho Region]] in [[Peru]].

==Boundaries==
*North: [[Huamanga province]]
*East: [[Apurímac Region]]
*South: [[Sucre province]]
*West: [[Cangallo province]] and [[Víctor Fajardo province]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into eight districts (Spanish: distritos, singular: distrito), each of which is headed by a mayor (alcalde). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:
* [[Vilcas Huamán District|Vilcas Huamán]] ([[Vilcashuamán]])
*[[Accomarca District|Accomarca]] (Accomarca)
*[[Carhuanca District|Carhuanca]]

*[[Huambalpa District|Huambalpa]]
*[[Independencia District, Vilcas Huamán|Independencia]]
*[[Saurama District|Saurama]]
*[[Vischongo District|Vischongo]] (Vischongo)

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are primarily [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (89.62%) learned to speak in childhood, 10.06% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>

== Archaeology ==
Some of the most important archaeological sites in the province are [[Usnu, Ayacucho|Usnu]], [[Inti Watana, Ayacucho|Inti Watana]], [[Pilluchu]], [[Pukara, Vilcas Huamán|Pukara]] and [[Puntay Urqu]].
