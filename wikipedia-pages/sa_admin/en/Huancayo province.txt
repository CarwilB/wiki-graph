'''Huancayo''' is a province of [[Peru]]. It is one of the 9 provinces comprising the [[Junín Region]]. It borders to the north with the [[Concepción Province, Peru|province of Concepción]], the east with the [[Satipo Province|province of Satipo]], the south with the [[Huancavelica Region]] and the west with the [[Chupaca Province|province of Chupaca]]. The province has an approximate population of 545,615 inhabitants.<ref name="GRJ" /> The capital of the province is the city of [[Huancayo]].
