'''Chilibre''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Panamá District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 53,955 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 27,135; its population as of 2000 was 40,475.<ref name=inec/>

==Climate==
Chilibre has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Am'').
