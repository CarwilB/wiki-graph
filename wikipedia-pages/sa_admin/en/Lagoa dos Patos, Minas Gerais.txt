'''Lagoa dos Patos''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 the population was 4,082 in a total area of 599&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/lagoa-dos-patos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1962.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/lagoadospatos/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080907190118/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/lagoadospatos/ |archive-date=2008-09-07 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

Lagoa dos Patos is located about 20&nbsp;km. east of the [[São Francisco River]].  It is 68&nbsp;km. from the nearest major population center, [[Pirapora]] at an elevation of 690 meters.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Jequitaí]], [[Várzea da Palma]], [[Buritizeiro]], [[Ibiaí]], and [[Coração de Jesus]].  Lagoa dos Patos is part of the statistical microregion of [[Pirapora]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080705172912/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/pirapora/index.htm |archive-date=2008-07-05 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The most important economic activities are cattle raising and subsistence farming.  The GDP in 2005 was R$14,041,000.00.  There were no banking agencies in the town in 2007, while there were 81 automobiles, one of the lowest ratios in the state.  In the rural area there were 248 establishments on a total area of 41,000 hectares, of which 4,000 hectares were planted in crops.  Around 900 people were working in agriculture.  There were 23,000 head of cattle.  The main crops were rice, beans, and corn.

Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In 2005 there were 03 health clinics  and no hospitals.
