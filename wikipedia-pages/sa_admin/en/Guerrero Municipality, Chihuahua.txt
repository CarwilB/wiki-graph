'''Guerrero''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Vicente Guerrero, Chihuahua|Vicente Guerrero]] ({{a.k.a.}} '''Ciudad Guerrero'''). The municipality covers an area of 5,603.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 39,626,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guerrero|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=031|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305014322/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=031|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 37,249 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Guerrero|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08031a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=September 29, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617063643/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08031a.htm|archivedate=June 17, 2011}}</ref> 

The municipality had 822 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[La Junta, Chihuahua|La Junta]] (8,930), [[Vicente Guerrero, Chihuahua|Vicente Guerrero]] (7,751), [[Tomochi, Chihuahua|Tomochi]] (2,818), classified as urban, and [[Basúchil, Chihuahua|Basúchil]] (1,451), and [[Orozco (San Isidro Pascual Orozco), Chihuahua|Orozco (San Isidro Pascual Orozco)]] (1,263), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
