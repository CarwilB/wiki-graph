'''Los Andes''' (i.e.: ''The Andes'') is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]]. It is the second largest by area in the province, after [[Rivadavia Department, Salta|Rivadavia Department]], and its capital is the town of [[San Antonio de los Cobres]].

== Geography ==

===Overview===
The department is located in the western area of the province, on the eastern side of the [[Andes]], and includes the [[Puna de Atacama]]. It borders with [[Antofagasta Region]] ([[Chile]]), the provinces of [[Jujuy Province|Jujuy]] and [[Catamarca Province|Catamarca]], and the departments of [[La Poma Department|La Poma]], [[Cachi Department|Cachi]] and  [[Molinos Department|Chicoana]]. The territorial strip linking the northern and southern side of La Poma Department separates Los Andes from [[Rosario de Lerma Department]].
