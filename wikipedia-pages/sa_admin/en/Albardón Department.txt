'''Albardón''' ({{IPA|es|alˈβoɾðon}}) is a department of the [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan Province]] ([[Argentina]]). It is located in the center south of the same, which emphasizes a desert landscape with mountains and numerous plantations, highlighting the presence of prestigious wineries known nationally.<ref>{{cite web | title=Albardón Department, San Juan Province, Argentina | website=Mindat.org | date=9 February 2024 | url=https://www.mindat.org/loc-239820.html | access-date=9 February 2024}}</ref>

== Toponymy ==
The word Albardón, in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], represents a kind of "[[Earth]] [[Rope|cord]]" (''en español cordón de tierra)'' or hill that comes up on a piece of land. According to the tradition, this is what the Spanish saw on the north side of the San Juan River, and according to this feature defined the zone name.
