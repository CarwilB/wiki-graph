'''Gómez Palacio''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Gómez Palacio, Durango|Gómez Palacio]]. The municipality covers an area of 990.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 327,985,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Gómez Palacio|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=007|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=14 April 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150414001344/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=007|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 304,515 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Gómez Palacio|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10007a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=November 18, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070512174200/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10007a.htm|archive-date=May 12, 2007}}</ref>

In 2010, the city of Gómez Palacio had a population of 257,352.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Gómez Palacio, the municipality had 583 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[San Felipe, Durango|San Felipe]] (4,552), [[La Popular, Durango|La Popular]] (3,406), [[Pastor Rouaix, Durango|Pastor Rouaix]] (2,696), classified as urban, and [[San Ramón, Durango|San Ramón]] (2,624), [[Transporte, Durango|Transporte]] (2,624), [[El Vergel, Durango|El Vergel]] (2,257), [[Esmeralda, Durango|Esmeralda]] (2,249), [[San José de Viñedo, Durango|San José de Viñedo]] (1,984), [[Venecia, Durango|Venecia]] (1,861), [[Arcinas, Durango|Arcinas]] (1,629), [[La Flor, Durango|La Flor]] (1,593), [[Santa Cruz Luján, Durango|Santa Cruz Luján]] (1,585), [[Jiménez (Jiménez Uno), Durango|Jiménez (Jiménez Uno)]] (1,568), [[Seis de Octubre, Durango|Seis de Octubre]] (1,528), [[Huitrón, Durango|Huitrón]] (1,511), [[Villa Gregorio García, Durango|Villa Gregorio García]] (1,446), [[Arturo Martínez Adame, Durango|Arturo Martínez Adame]] (1,251), [[Eureka de Media Luna (Eureka), Durango|Eureka de Media Luna (Eureka)]] (1,201), [[Puente de la Torreña, Durango|Puente de la Torreña]] (1,177), and [[California, Durango|California]] (1,032), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
