'''Coalcomán de Vázquez Pallares''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in the [[Sierra Madre del Sur]] mountain range, in the southwest region of the [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Michoacán]] in central-western Mexico.

The municipality has an area of 2,881.57 square kilometres (4.89% of the surface of the state)<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan Michoacán] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517162648/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan |date=2011-05-17 }} ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Retrieved on October 8, 2007</ref> and is bordered to the north by the state of [[Jalisco]] and the municipality of [[Tepalcatepec]], to the east by [[Arteaga, Michoacán|Arteaga]] and [[Aguililla]], to the south by [[Aquila, Michoacán|Aquila]], and to the west by [[Chinicuila]]. The municipality had a population of 18,156 inhabitants according to the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=2007-10-08 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2013-04-06 }}</ref>  Its municipal seat is the city of the same name.

Coalcomán is a word of [[Nahuatl]] origin that means "Snake" or "Snake with Hands", according to some authors.<ref>[http://www.aguilillamich.com/Aguilillamich/AGUILILLA/HISTORIA/HISTORIA/HIST.html Aguililla, Michoacán] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071214033317/http://www.aguilillamich.com/Aguilillamich/AGUILILLA/HISTORIA/HISTORIA/HIST.html |date=2007-12-14 }} ''Pueblo Náhuatl''. Retrieved on October 8, 2007</ref>
