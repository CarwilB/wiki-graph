'''El Valle de Antón''', generally called '''El Valle''', or Anton's Valley in English, is a [[town]] of 7,600 in the [[Coclé]] [[Provinces and regions of Panama|province]] of [[Panama]].

==Geography==

The town is located in the flat wide caldera of the 6&nbsp;km wide [[El Valle (volcano)|El Valle volcano]] that is inactive; there is evidence that it erupted as recently as about 300,000 years ago <ref>Defant et al. 1991: Andesite and dacite genesis via contrasting processes: the geology and Geochemistry of El Valle Volcano, Panama; Contrib. Mineral. Petrol., v. 106, p. 309-324</ref>


==Environment==
[[File:Atelopus zeteki1.jpg|thumb|left|[[Panamanian golden frog]], critically endangered]]
Because of its elevation (600 m), the area is cooler than the Panamanian lowlands.<ref name=f226>Friar, p. 226.</ref> Natural attractions near El Valle include the Chorro El Macho waterfall, Las Mozas waterfall, the "square" trees behind Hotel Campestre, and a group of small thermal pools (which consist of three cement pools of mineralized water that varies in color depending on the specific minerals present at a given moment).<ref>Friar, pp. 226, 229, 231.</ref> The area around the town is also known for being one of the last habitats of the critically endangered [[Panamanian golden frog]].<ref name=f226/> Some of the forests around the town are protected areas.<ref name=f226/>

===Important Bird Area===
El Valle is home to around 500 species of birds. A 10,600&nbsp;ha site encompassing the forests and volcanic peaks north of the town, forming the upper watersheds of several rivers, has been designated an [[Important Bird Area]] (IBA) by [[BirdLife International]] because it supports significant populations of [[black guan]]s, [[olive-sided flycatcher]]s and [[golden-winged warbler]]s.<ref name=bli>{{cite web |url= https://datazone.birdlife.org/site/factsheet/el-valle-de-ant%C3%B3n-iba-panama |title= El Valle de Antón|author=<!--Not stated--> |date=2024|website= BirdLife Data Zone|publisher= BirdLife International|access-date= 2024-09-23}}</ref>

==Demographics==
El Valle had a population of 7,602 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> In 2000 its population was 6,175;  in 1990 it was 4,900.<ref name=inec/>

==Features==
El Valle has one main road, called Avenida Central or Calle Central, which runs east–west across the town.<ref>Friar, p. 226-27.</ref> A main landmark is the town's public market, which is open seven days a week, although it is sometimes referred to as El Valle's Sunday Market.<ref>Friar, pp. 226, 229.</ref> El Valle has a very small historical and geological [[museum]], as well as a small [[zoo]] (El Nispero, named after a type of tree), a small [[serpentarium]], [http://www.butterflyhavenpanama.com a butterfly house], and an orchid conservation center, called [[APROVACA]], which displays over 100 native local [[orchid]] species.<ref>Friar, pp. 226-29.</ref> There are also several petroglyphs west of town that are known as La Piedra Pintada.<ref>{{cite web|title=La Piedra Pintada|url=http://www.anywherepanama.com/destinations/el-valle-de-anton}}</ref>

Some of the people who own properties in the heart of El Valle are wealthy people from [[Panama City]] who use the area as their vacation or weekend home.<ref name=f230>Friar, p. 230.</ref> Popular activities in the area include cycling, hiking, horseback riding, and birdwatching.<ref name=f230/>

The town is about 25&nbsp;km off the [[Interamerican Highway]] by a two-lane road. The road is generally in good condition, with a few potholes that are repaired regularly. Buses, usually mini-buses, to [[Panama City]] are frequent and take approximately 2.5 hours.  As of 2014, the last direct bus to Panama City leaves El Valle at 3pm with a fare of $4.25. To reach other locations in Panama, it is generally necessary to take a bus to San Carlos and transfer there. As of 2014, the last bus from El Valle to San Carlos leaves at 6pm.<ref>Friar, p. 236.</ref>
