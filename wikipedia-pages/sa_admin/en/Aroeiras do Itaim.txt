'''Aroeiras do Itaim''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Piauí]]. The municipality was founded on 1 January 2005. There are 2,551 people residing in the city, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]].
