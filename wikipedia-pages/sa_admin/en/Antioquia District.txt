'''Antioquia District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

==Population==
The population of the district is 1,225 people as of the [[2017 Peru Census|2017 census]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/censos2007/tabulados/?id=ResultadosCensales XI Censo de Población y VI de Vivienda 2007] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121011062227/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/censos2007/tabulados/?id=ResultadosCensales |date=2012-10-11 }} INEI</ref>
