'''Turi''' is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] in [[Cuenca Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of 26.2&nbsp;km² and according to the 2001 Ecuadorian census it had a population total of 6,692.<ref name="unofficialguide">{{cite web|url=http://www.ajupa.gov.ec/parroq.aspx?cant=1&parr=69|title=Parroquia Turi|publisher=Asociación de Juntas Parroquiales Rurales del Azuay|accessdate=February 26, 2010}}{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref> It is perched on a hill with a public park that features an overlook of the city of [[Cuenca, Ecuador|Cuenca]]; perhaps and the only site where the entire city can be observed while still on the ground.

There are artisan shops outside of the church that sell boots and hats, while a number of souvenir stalls dot the roadway.

The Turi Church and observation point is a stop on the Cuenca bus tour.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.unofficialguide.ph/turi/|title=Turi Church and Observation Point|publisher=Unofficial Guide, Philippines|accessdate=March 23, 2018}}</ref>
