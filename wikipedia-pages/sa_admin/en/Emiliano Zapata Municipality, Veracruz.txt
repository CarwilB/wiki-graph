'''Emiliano Zapata''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located 2&nbsp;km from [[Jalapa-Enríquez]] on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highways 180 and 190]]. The municipality is named for the hero of the [[Mexican Revolution]], [[Emiliano Zapata]]. The municipal seat is at [[Dos Ríos, Veracruz|Dos Ríos]].

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 85,489 inhabitants in 141 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSEMILIANO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Emiliano-Zapata.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Emiliano Zapata CENSUS]</ref> Just a fraction (1,698 hab.) resides in the municipal seat, Dos Ríos. The biggest localities are Jacarandas (11,864 hab.), Rinconada (8,005 hab.), La Pradera (7,500 hab.), La Estanzuela (4,951 hab.) and El Carrizal (4,714 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSEMILIANO/>
