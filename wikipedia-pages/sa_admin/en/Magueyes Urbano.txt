'''Magueyes Urbano''' is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s in the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Along with [[Canas Urbano]], [[Machuelo Abajo]], [[Portugués Urbano]], and [[San Antón]], Magueyes Urbano is one of the municipality's five originally rural barrios that are now also part of the urban zone of the city of Ponce. The name of this barrio is of native Indian origin.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 54.</ref> It was created in 1953.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
Magueyes Urbano is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and northwest of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

==Boundaries==
It is bounded on the North by J. J. Cartagena Street (Las Delicias community), Ruth Fernandez Boulevard, and Camino de Ponce (Golf Club) Road, on the South by [[Puerto Rico Highway 123|PR-123]], Ausencia Street, and La Gloria Street, on the West by the hills west of PR-123, and on the East by Novedades Street, the hills east of [[Ponce Cement, Inc.|Ponce Cement]] and the future western branch of [[Puerto Rico Highway 9|PR-9]].<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ Government of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico. ==Features and demographics==
Magueyes Urbano has {{convert|1.2|sqmi|km2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area.  In 2000, the population of Magueyes Urbano was 1,332 persons, and it had a density of 1,070 persons per square mile.<ref>[http://factfinder.census.gov/servlet/GCTTable?_bm=y&-geo_id=05000US72113&-_box_head_nbr=GCT-PH1&-ds_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U&-redoLog=false&-format=CO-2&-mt_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U_GCTPH1_CO2 U.S. Census Bureau. Census 2000. Ponce and Its Barrios. (Ponce Municipio, Puerto Rico -- County Subdivision and Place. GCT-PH1. Population, Housing Units, Area, and Density: 2000. Data Set: Census 2000 Summary File 1 (SF 1) 100-Percent Data)] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20200210210755/http://factfinder.census.gov/servlet/GCTTable?_bm=y&-geo_id=05000US72113&-_box_head_nbr=GCT-PH1&-ds_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U&-redoLog=false&-format=CO-2&-mt_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U_GCTPH1_CO2 |date=10 February 2020 }} Retrieved February 24, 2010.</ref><ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Magueyes+Urbano+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved, February 24, 2010.</ref>

In 2010, the population of Magueyes Urbano was 1,132 persons, and it had a density of 912.9 persons per square mile.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico:2010:population and housing unit counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

Major roads in Magueyes Urbano are [[Puerto Rico Highway 123|PR-123]] and [[Puerto Rico Highway 9|PR-9]].

==Notable landmarks==
Barrio Magueyes Urbano is home to [[Ponce Cement, Inc.]] and the [[NRHP]]-listed [[Cementerio Catolico San Vicente de Paul]].
