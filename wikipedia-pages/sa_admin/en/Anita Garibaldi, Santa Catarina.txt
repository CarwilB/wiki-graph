'''Anita Garibaldi''' is a municipality located in the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], [[Brazil]]. The municipality  covers about 588.612&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and has 6,957 inhabitants. It is named after [[Anita Garibaldi]], the Brazilian wife and comrade-in-arms of Italian revolutionary [[Giuseppe Garibaldi]].
