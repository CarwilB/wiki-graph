'''Bakkie''' (old name: '''Reynsdorp'''<ref name="museum"/>) is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] and town in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Commewijne District]]. Its population was 447 at the 2012 census (541 at the 2004 census).<ref>Census 2012 [http://www.statistics-suriname.org/index.php/statistieken/downloads/category/30-censusstatistieken-2012# ABS Suriname] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141113143359/http://www.statistics-suriname.org/index.php/statistieken/downloads/category/30-censusstatistieken-2012 |date=2014-11-13 }}</ref><ref>Census 2004 [http://www.statoids.com/ysr.html Statoids.com]</ref> Around 1902, the coffee [[plantation]] ''Reynsdorp'' was bought by the government and parceled out in small allotments for immigrants who had served out their contracts.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://nationaalarchief.sr/geschiedenis/plantages/matapicakreek/reynsdorp/index.nl.html |title=National Archives of Suriname |access-date=2010-01-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090318022143/http://nationaalarchief.sr/geschiedenis/plantages/matapicakreek/reynsdorp/index.nl.html |archive-date=2009-03-18 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The resort lies along the [[Atlantic Ocean]] coastline. The resort of Bakkie can only be reached by boat.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

{{anchor|Museum}}
The Museum of Bakkie, located in the village Reynsdorp (now better known as Bakkie) has an impressive collection of old bottles, prints, maps, paintings and many utensils.<ref name="museum">{{cite web|url=http://www.warappakreek.com/?page_id=42&lang=nl&phpMyAdmin=bc400b6448ad4a27f7bff0a597d08479&phpMyAdmin=2U5hdadNvf%2CEOljv1AhyS86QTEf|title=Museum|website=Warappakreek|access-date=14 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

Other villages in the Bakkie resort are [[Alliance, Suriname|Alliance]], [[Ephrata, Suriname|Ephrata]], and [[Kronenburg, Suriname|Kronenburg]]
