'''Costa Rica''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 21,142&nbsp;(2020) and its area is {{cvt|5723|km2}}.

The municipality contains 17.3% of the {{convert|30619|ha}} [[Nascentes do Rio Taquari State Park]], created in 1999.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES das Nascentes do Rio Taquari – ISA}}|language=pt
