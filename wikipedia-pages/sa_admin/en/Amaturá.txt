'''Amaturá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 11,736 (2020),<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/amatura/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> up from 8,694 in 2008, but its area has remained constant at 4,759&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] - {{cite web
 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php 
 |title=IBGE - Cidades@ 
 |publisher=www.ibge.gov.br 
 |accessdate=2008-06-15 
 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php 
 |archivedate=11 June 2008 
 |url-status=dead 
}}</ref>

The municipality contains parts of the [[Jutaí-Solimões Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Jutaí-Solimões|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=56|accessdate=2016-05-04}}</ref>
