'''Cutias''', formally known as '''Cutias do Araguari''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kuˈtʃi.ɐz du aɾɐɡwaˈɾi|lang}}), is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 6,101<ref name="pop">[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/cutias/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|2,115|km2|mi2}}. The name of the municipality comes from a rodent of the same name, known in English as the [[agouti]]. Cutias do Araguari was elevated to the level of municipality on 1 May 1992.<ref name="mun"/><ref name="gov"/>

==Geography==
[[File:Araguari River Cutias Amapá Brazil.jpg|thumb|left|Araguari River in Cutias]]
Cutias is bordered by Pracuúba and Amapá to the north, Macapá to the south, Macapá to the east, and Ferreira Gomes to the west. It is {{Convert|120|m|ft}} from the state capitol of [[Macapá]]. Cutias is dominated by the [[Araguari River (Amapá)|Araguari River]] and its tributaries, which partially submerges the area for nearly half the year. It is very flat, with the highest point reaching only {{Convert|3|m|ft}} above sea level. Cutias has a wet, tropical climate; it averages 2,800&nbsp;mm of rain per year, and has an average humidity of 80%.<ref name="mun"/><ref name="gov"/>

==Economy==
The economy is based on maize and [[manioc]] production, raising livestock, and fishing.<ref name="history">{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/cutias-do-araguari |title=Cutias do Araguari|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> In 2016, Cutias had the second largest amount of [[Bison|buffalo]]s in Brazil.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.beefpoint.com.br/ibge-rebanho-de-bovinos-tinha-21823-milhoes-de-cabecas-em-2016/ |title=IBGE: rebanho de bovinos tinha 218,23 milhões de cabeças em 2016|website=Beef Point Brazil|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> Cutias do Araguari is the largest exporter of ''pirarucú'' ([[arapaima]]) in Brazil, an endangered fish noted for its boneless steaks. The common agouti, from which the municipality takes its name, is also used in local cuisine.<ref name="mun"/><ref name="gov"/>

==Tourism==
The town became known during the ''[[pororoca]]'' craze which attracted surfers to the Araguari River which has large [[tidal bore]]s.<ref name="history"/>
