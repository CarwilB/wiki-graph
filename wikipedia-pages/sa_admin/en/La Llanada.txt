'''La Llanada''' is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
La Llanada has a warm [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with heavy rain for the majority of the year and a drier season from June to September.
