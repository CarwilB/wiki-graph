'''Atalaya''' is the largest of four [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Ucayali Region]], in the central [[Amazon rainforest]] of [[Peru]].

==Languages==

According to the [[Peru 2007 Census|2007 census]], [[Spanish language|Spanish]] was spoken as a first language by 49.1% of the population, while 37.0% spoke [[Asháninka language|Asháninka]], 1.7% spoke [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 0.2% spoke [[Aymara language|Aymara]], 11.9% spoke other indigenous languages and 0.1% spoke foreign languages.

==Political division==
The province is divided into four [[district]]s ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: ''distrito''), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Raymondi District|Raymondi]] ([[Atalaya, Ucayali|Atalaya]])
* [[Sepahua District|Sepahua]] ([[Sepahua]])
* [[Tahuania District|Tahuania]] ([[Bolognesi, Ucayali|Bolognesi]])
* [[Yurua District|Yurua]] ([[Breu]])
