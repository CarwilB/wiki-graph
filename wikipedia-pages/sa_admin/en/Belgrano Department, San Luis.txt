'''Belgrano''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|6626|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the departments of [[Ayacucho Department, San Luis|Ayacucho]], to the east with Ayacucho and [[Coronel Pringles Department, San Luis|Coronel Pringles]], to the south with [[Juan Martín de Pueyrredón Department|Juan Martín de Pueyrredón]], and to the west with [[Mendoza Province]].
