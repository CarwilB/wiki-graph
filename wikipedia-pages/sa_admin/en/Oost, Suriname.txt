'''Oost''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Para District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 8,016.<ref name="census"/> The main town in the resort is [[Paranam]]. Oost is also home to the former [[leprosy|leper]] colony [[Bethesda, Suriname|Bethesda]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://geotargit.com/index.php?qcountry_code=SR&qregion_code=11&qcity=Bethesda|title=Where is Bethesda in Suriname?|access-date=23 May 2020|website=Geo Targit}}</ref> The Paranam alumina refinery is located near the town of Paranam.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.industryabout.com/country-territories-3/2123-suriname/aluminium-industry/32382-suralco-paranam-alumina-refinery|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170918062509/http://www.industryabout.com/country-territories-3/2123-suriname/aluminium-industry/32382-suralco-paranam-alumina-refinery|url-status=usurped|archive-date=September 18, 2017|title=Suralco - Paranam Alumina Refinery|access-date=23 May 2020|website=Industry About}}</ref> The Accaribo area is becoming a tourist spot with the main attractions being White Beach and Caribo Beach Resort.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Planning Office Suriname - Districts|website=Planning Office Suriname|language=nl|access-date=23 May 2020}}</ref>

The former capital of Suriname, [[Torarica]], is located in the Oost Resort. It was settled by [[Portuguese Jews]] in 1629. One origin offered for its name that it is a Portuguese coinage meaning "Opulent [[Torah]]".<ref>{{Cite book|title = Suriname|url = https://books.google.com/books?id=FtTeBgAAQBAJ|publisher = Bradt Travel Guides|date = 2015-02-01|isbn = 9781841629100|first = Philip|last = Briggs}}</ref> By 1665, the village of [[Paramaribo]] had expanded and quickly outranked Torarica.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/301ges/aparbo02.html|title=Paramaribo Suriname 2|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref>

The [[Lokono]] village of [[Powakka]] is located in the resort.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Planning Office|year=2014|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Planning Office Suriname - Districts|website=Planning Office Suriname|page=147|language=nl}}</ref>
