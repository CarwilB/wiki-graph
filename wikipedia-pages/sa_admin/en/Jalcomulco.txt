'''Jalcomulco''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], founded in 1825.

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the central zone of the state, about {{convert|29|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Xalapa]], the state capital and {{convert|359|km|mi|abbr=on}}, from [[Mexico City]]. It has a land area of 72.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality of Jalcomulco is bounded to the north by [[Emiliano Zapata, Veracruz|Emiliano Zapata]], to the east by [[Apazapan (municipality)|Apazapan]], to the south and south-west by [[Tlaltetela]], and to the north-west by [[Coatepec, Veracruz|Coatepec]].<ref name="e-local">{{Cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30088a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Jalcomulco|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10|archive-date=28 March 2007|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070328110558/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30088a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Climate===
The [[weather]] in Jalcomulco is warm all year with [[rain]] in summer and autumn.

Annual [[Precipitation (meteorology)|precipitation]] is 1,125&nbsp;mm.

The average temperature is 24&nbsp;°C, (75&nbsp;°F).

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 5,054 inhabitants in just 4 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSJALCOMULCO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Jalcomulco.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Jalcomulco CENSUS]</ref> The localities are Jalcomulco (3,150 hab.), Santa María Tatetla (1,707 hab.), Tacotalpan (153 hab.) and Rivera del Río (44 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSJALCOMULCO/>

==Economy==
The [[region]] principally produces [[maize]], [[coffee]], [[sugarcane]], and [[mango]]. It also produces [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].

Since the early 1990s [[ecotourism]] has been an important part of the economy of the region, with more than a dozen [[hotel]]s and professional dealers of [[adventure travel]].

==Culture==
In May, a celebration takes place in Jalcomulco to honor [[John the Baptist|San Juan Bautista]], the [[Patron saint]] of the town.

==Sports and activities==
[[File:Rafting Rio Salvaje.JPG|thumb|Rafting in Río Antigua]]
[[File:Bridge over Rio de los Pescados (Río Antigua), Jalcomulco.jpg|thumb|Footbridge across Río Antigua (Río de los Pescados Section), Jalcomulco]]
The Río de los Pescados section of the [[Rio Antigua]] is a [[whitewater]] river that flows through the town of Jalcomulco and is commercially [[Rafting|rafted]].{{citation needed|date=September 2023}}

Other activities to practice in town are [[rappel]]ling, [[climbing]], [[hiking]], [[tyrolean crossing]], [[mountain biking]], [[landscape photography]], and [[temazcal]].{{citation needed|date=September 2023}}

==Filming location==
Two-thirds of the scenes of the movie ''[[Romancing the Stone]]'' (1984) was filmed in this area.{{citation needed|date=September 2023}}
