'''San Lorenzo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Esmeraldas Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[San Lorenzo, Ecuador|San Lorenzo]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 28,180<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
