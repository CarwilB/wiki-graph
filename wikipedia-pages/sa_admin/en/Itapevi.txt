'''Itapevi''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[São Paulo Metropolitan Region|western area of the São Paulo Metropolitan Region]], in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], in the [[Southeast Region of Brazil|Southeast Region]] of Brazil. Originally a village of [[Cotia]], it developed following the arrival of the [[Estrada de Ferro Sorocabana]] in the 19th century and became an emancipated municipality in 1959 after a [[plebiscite]]. It occupies an area of 82.658 km² (31.9 sq mi), and its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 Census]] was 232,297 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Itapevi (SP) {{!}} Cidades e Estados {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/sp/itapevi.html |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> It is located 35 kilometers (21.7 mi) from the state capital, [[São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Distância São-Paulo > Itapevi, São-Paulo, BRA – Linha aérea, rota de condução, ponto intermédio |url=https://pt.distance.to/S%C3%A3o-Paulo/Itapevi,S%C3%A3o-Paulo,BRA |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=Calculador de distâncias |language=pt-pt}}</ref> It is also located 1,017 km (631 mi) from the federal capital, [[Brasília]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Distância Brasília, Distrito-Federal, BRA > Itapevi, São-Paulo, BRA – Linha aérea, rota de condução, ponto intermédio |url=https://pt.distance.to/Bras%C3%ADlia,Distrito-Federal,BRA/Itapevi,S%C3%A3o-Paulo,BRA |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=Calculador de distâncias |language=pt-pt}}</ref>

It is also known as the "Itacity", "City of New Roofs", the "City of Roses", and the "City of Hope".

The formation of the village began around the 18th century, and the first building in the city was a house built around 1720 and used by [[Bandeirantes]]. The earliest settlers were probably members of the Abreus family.

The name of '''District of Itapevy''' (or Itapevi) was associated with the [[noble title]] of '''[[Emílio Mallet|Baron of Itapevi]]''', granted on December 28, 1878, to the military [[Emílio Mallet|Emílio Luís Mallet]], officer of the Brazilian Imperial Army and later patron of the Arm of [[Artillery]] of the [[Brazilian Army]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Archivo nobiliarchico brasileiro/Itapevy (Barão de) – Wikisource |url=https://pt.wikisource.org/wiki/Archivo_nobiliarchico_brasileiro/Itapevy_(Bar%C3%A3o_de) |access-date=May 20, 2026 |website=pt.wikisource.org |language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Historic |url=https://www.11gaaae.eb.mil.br/index.php/institucional/historico |access-date=May 20, 2026}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The toponym comes from the [[Tupi language]] word ''Itapevi'' and means "river of flat stones", according to two books: ''Vocabulário [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupi-Guarani]] – Português'', by Prof. Silveira Bueno (Brasilivros Editora), and ''A Origem dos Nomes dos Municípios Paulistas'' (Imprensa Oficial do Estado de São Paulo, 2003), by Enio Squeff and Helder Perri Ferreira.

The latter work states: "Itapevi (from Tupi ''itá peb'y''): river of flat stone slabs, from ''ita-peba'' (flat stone slab) and '''y'' (river or [[water]]s)".<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca |url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/biblioteca-catalogo.html?id=32452&view=detalhes |access-date=February 18, 2026 |website=IBGE {{!}} Biblioteca |language=pt-br}}</ref>

==History==
On July 10, 1875, the train station of [[Cotia]] (Sorocabana) was inaugurated, around which formed the core of Itapevi.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Diário Oficial do Estado de São Paulo |url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1893/diario%2520oficial/novembro/25/pag_8663_B0OOEC9KUDV7Ge3Q2FACHQFQ8D0.pdf&pagina=8663&data=25/11/1893&caderno=Di%C3%A1rio%20Oficial&paginaordenacao=108663 |access-date=December 21, 2025 |website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br}}</ref> In 1895, the Italian Giulio Michaeli opened a quarry for the production of paving stones, attracting families of Italian immigrants, such as Belli, Michelotti and Silicani.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Correio Paulistano (SP) – 1880 a 1888 – DocReader Web |url=https://memoria.bn.gov.br/DocReader/DocReader.aspx?bib=090972_04&pagfis=10276 |access-date=December 21, 2025 |website=memoria.bn.gov.br}}</ref>

In 1912, Joaquim Nunes Filho (Nho Quim), from Cotia city, purchased the Sítio Itapevy, with 152 bushels. This place covering all the current city center. Nunes became a local political boss, for his ties to the former PRP ([[Paulista Republican Party|Partido Republicano Paulista]]). He managed the elevation of the village to district Cotia on October 12, 1920.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.al.sp.gov.br/repositorio/legislacao/lei/1920/lei-1741-19.10.1920.html |title=LEI N.1.741, DE 19 DE OUTUBRO DE 1920 |access-date=December 21, 2025 |website=www.al.sp.gov.br}}</ref> Nunes has brought electricity in 1929 and the installation of the first telephone in 1930.

Nevertheless, the reference of the place was still under the name of [[Cotia]] train station, removing the possibility of identifying own the place.

In 1940, he arrived in Itapevi Carlos de Castro, businessman. Knowing that the Nunes family had pretensions to sell the land that belonged to Joaquim Nunes (who died in 1941), he acquired vast tract of land, giving rise to the allotment of Parque Suburbano and Jardim Bela Vista. It was from there that accelerated the process of urbanization of the place.

At the time, the train station was still called Cotia and the headquarters of the future city was known as Vila Cotia. With this, they created enormous confusion up to the postal and telegraph service in the district. In 1945, Carlos de Castro got with then Minister João Alberto that the station had its name changed to Itapevi. The people celebrated this decree.

On May 10, 1952, Carlos de Castro met with three other residents of the district: Nicolau Leonardo, Raul Leonardo and Ezequiel Dias Siqueira. Together, they drafted a petition to the [[Assembléia Legislativa do Estado de São Paulo]] for the emancipation of the district. Taking more than a thousand signatures, but even so, they were defeated by the fact that Itapevi was very near Cotia and that residents were indebted to the city. They had to wait five years for the project to be voted on again. From then and now, in a spirit of emancipation throughout this region, members of the society of the time initiated the movement for autonomy in the district, causing the population to commit mass in the process. Its founders were men like himself Carlos de Castro, Americo Christianini, Cezário de Abreu, Bonifácio de Abreu, Rubens Caramez (then councilor of Cotia and who later became the first mayor of Itapevi), Raul Leonardo (the only emancipatory still alive), Jose dos Santos Novaes, Antônio Pedra Pereira and many others.

In 1958, about nine hundred people opted for emancipation, against only thirty unwilling to accept autonomy in a referendum. That same year was formalized by Governor [[Jânio Quadros]] the law that created the city of Itapevi. The city was officially founded in the following year, on February 18, 1959, by Governor [[Carvalho Pinto]]. The first mayor was Rubens Caramez, who won the elections against Carlos de Castro.

=== COHAB === 
[[File:(2021.04.09)  Ponte do Novo Acesso da Cohab (51106013180).jpg|thumb|300x150px|Cohab New Access Bridge]]
Around the 1980s, the São Paulo Metropolitan Housing Company – COHAB, launched the Presidente Housing Complex [[Tancredo Neves]], increasing the city's demographic density. And in the 1990s, the city received three more housing complexes, these built by the Housing and Urban Development Company – CDHU, from the state government. As a result, there was strong migration to the city.

==Geography==
===Physical setting===
The municipality is located at coordinates 23°32'56" S, 46°56'03" W. The total area of the municipality is 82,658 km², ranking 600th in the list of the largest cities in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]]. Of the entire area of the municipality, 37,498 km² are urban areas, ranking 266th on the list of the largest in Brazil. It is limited to the municipality of [[Santana de Parnaíba]] to the north and northwest, [[Barueri]] to the northeast, [[Jandira]] to the east, [[Cotia]] to the south and southeast, [[Vargem Grande Paulista]] to the southwest and [[São Roque, São Paulo|São Roque]] to the west.

Itapevi is under the control of the [[Atlantic Forest|Atlantic Plateau]], where we can see the following types of relief: floodplains, alluvial plains, hills and [[mountain]]s. Serra do Itaqui extends along the border with the municipality of [[Santana de Parnaíba]], where the Aldeia da Serra region is located. The city's ground zero is located 35 kilometers west of the capital of São Paulo, and the current urban center is located in the floodplain of the Barueri-Mirim river, a tributary of the [[Tietê River]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Histórico de Itapevi |url=https://www.itapevi.sp.leg.br/historico-de-itapevi |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20251222234016/https://www.itapevi.sp.leg.br/historico-de-itapevi |archive-date=December 22, 2025 |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=Camara Municipal de Itapevi |language=pt-br}}</ref>

===Climate===
Itapevi climate is subtropical, temperate, warm the coldest month being July and warmest in February. Rainfall is abundant, amounting to an annual average of 1,324&nbsp;mm. According to [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]], Itapevi can be classified as a [[humid subtropical climate]] "Cfa".

== Culture ==
=== Music ===
In addition to the trap artist [[Thiago Veigh|Veigh]],<ref>{{Cite web |date=June 26, 2023 |title=Veigh: Fenômeno do trap já trabalhou com telemarketing e hoje fatura R$ 120 mil por show |url=https://g1.globo.com/pop-arte/musica/noticia/2023/06/26/veigh-fenomeno-do-trap-ja-trabalhou-com-telemarketing-e-hoje-fatura-r-120-mil-por-show.ghtml |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=G1 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> who achieved national and international recognition, the city is also the birthplace of other notable artists, including performers from the [[Hip-hop|hip hop]] movement and groups dedicated to samba. The Trap Fest event, for example, demonstrates the strength of the musical genre in the region. To promote samba de roda, the Samba da Casa 26 group organizes performances that celebrate the genre and serve as a meeting point for the community.

=== Visual arts ===
In 2018, the [[Guinness Book]] officially recognized the largest graffiti wall in the world as being located in the city, on the banks of the [[Rodovia Castelo Branco|Rodovia Castello Branco (SP-280)]], at the Cacau Show factory, depicting the process from planting to harvesting cocoa.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Redação |date=March 27, 2017 |title=Eduardo Kobra faz o maior mural do mundo em rodovia brasileira |url=https://opopular.com.br/magazine/eduardo-kobra-faz-o-maior-mural-do-mundo-em-rodovia-brasileira-1.1247620 |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=O Popular |language=pt-br}}</ref> Measuring approximately 5,772.11 m², the mural was created by artist [[Eduardo Kobra]], who already held the record for a graffiti wall produced in 2016 on the eve of the Olympic Games in [[Rio de Janeiro]].

==Economy==
[[File:2017-02-10 Prefeito visita Eurofarma.jpg|thumb|[[Eurofarma]] complex in '''Itapevi''']]
[[File:Eduardo Kobra, Chocolate Factory of Cacau Show, Itapevi, São Paulo, Brazil.jpg|thumb|Headquarters and main factory of [[Cacau Show]], located in Itapevi]]
Itapevi's economy comes from the services and commerce sector (71.5%), industry (18.8%) and public administration (9.7%). The municipality concentrates almost all its population in urban areas, resulting in reduced agricultural activity. In 2023, the municipality closed with a GDP of R$15.6&nbsp;billion (U$2,9&nbsp;billion) and per capita of R$67.220,40 (U$12.870,24), placing it among the 150 richest municipalities in Brazil.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/economicas/contas-nacionais/9088-produto-interno-bruto-dos-municipios.html?t=pib-por-municipio&c=3522505 |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

The Exame Magazine pointed Itapevi among the 10 cities with the best economic development in the country, and as the first in the state of São Paulo. The ranking is part of the story "The best cities for business'.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.itapevinoticias.jor.br/index.php/itapevi/3045-itapevi-esta-entre-as-10-cidades-com-melhor-desenvolvimento-economico-no-pais-aponta-revista-exame|title=Itapevi está entre as 10 cidades com melhor desenvolvimento econômico no país, aponta Revista Exame|access-date=June 7, 2014|publisher=ItapeviNotícias|archive-date=July 14, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140714210531/http://www.itapevinoticias.jor.br/index.php/itapevi/3045-itapevi-esta-entre-as-10-cidades-com-melhor-desenvolvimento-economico-no-pais-aponta-revista-exame|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Itapevi has a well diversified industrial park, with an emphasis on the pharmaceutical industry. Large companies are located within the municipality, such as [[Henkel]], Jaraguá, [[Cacau show]], Casa Suíça, [[Eurofarma]], [[Alpla]], [[Wyeth]], Blanver, Bomi Brasil, [[Leonardo (company)|Leonardo S.P.A]], [[Pfizer]], Biolab, [[Levi Strauss & Co.|Levi Strauss & Co. Brasil]] and others.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=January 15, 2026 |language=en-US |title=Leonardo in Brazil: the new Service and Logistics Centre to enhance helicopter services |url=https://helicopters.leonardo.com/en/focus-detail/-/detail/leonardo_itapevi_brazil |website=helicopters.leonardo.com}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Onde Encontrar – Casa Suiça |url=http://casasuica.com.br/onde-encontrar/ |access-date=February 17, 2026 |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Lugares incríveis: como é e o que tem no escritório da Cacau Show |url=https://vocerh.abril.com.br/voce-rh/lugares-incriveis-como-e-e-o-que-tem-no-escritorio-da-cacau-show/ |access-date=February 17, 2026 |website=Você RH |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[Companhia Telefônica Brasileira]] until 1973, when it began to be served by [[Telesp|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp – April 12, 1973|access-date=May 27, 2024|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, the company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company currently operates mobile phone, fixed-line, internet (fiber optics/4G), and television (satellite and cable) services.<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History – Telefônica RI|access-date=May 27, 2024|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
