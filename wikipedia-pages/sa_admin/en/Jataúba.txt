'''Jataúba''' is a city in the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]] state (N); [[Belo Jardim]] (S); [[Brejo da Madre de Deus]] and [[Santa Cruz do Capibaribe]] (E); [[Poção]] and Paraíba (W)
* Area - 719.22&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 516 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Clima - [[semi-arid]], hot
* Annual average temperature - 22.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 225&nbsp;km
* Population - 16,328 (2025)<ref name="IBGE" />

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Jataúba are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[pig]]s.
