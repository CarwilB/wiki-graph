'''Pebas District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Mariscal Ramón Castilla Province|Mariscal Ramón Castilla]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |language=Spanish |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] |title=Banco de Información Distrital |accessdate=June 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |archivedate=April 23, 2008}}</ref> Its name is derived from [[Peba language|Peba]], an indigenous ethnic group and language.
