''' Eduardo Neri ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal capital is [[Zumpango del Río]].  The municipality covers an area of 1289.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

It was named for jurist and congressman {{ill|Eduardo Neri Reynoso|es|Eduardo Neri}}, born in Zumpango.<ref>{{cite web |title=Neri Reynoso, Eduardo |url=https://enciclopediagro.mx/biografias/neri-reynoso-eduardo/ |publisher=Enciclopedia Guerrerense |access-date=10 August 2024}}</ref>

In 2005, the total population was 40,328.<ref>{{cite web|title=Eduardo Neri|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12075a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617101819/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12075a.htm|archive-date=June 17, 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The contract for the Canadian-owned mine ''Los Filos,'' consisting of {{convert|1,500|ha|acre}} (700 ha of common use and 800 ha owned by 173 ''[[ejidatarios]]'' was canceled on September 2, [[2020 in Mexico|2020]]. Some 1,300 miners were blocked from entering the site. The ejiidatarios claim that the mining company, Equinox Gold, has racist and discriminatory policies, and that it regularly ignores health and environmental protections. Eight villagers in Carrizalillo died from cancer in 2019, and some babies have been born with deformities related to pollution. 

The mavericks claim that the company has had a racist and discriminatory attitude towards the inhabitants and has violated contract clauses related to the protection of health and the environment. Eight villagers died from last year, apparently from cancer and some babies were born with malformations from pollution. Twenty-one miners have been infected by [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico|COVID-19]]. Both the mining company and the landowners have been extorted into monthly payments by local criminal gangs. Section 17 of the ''Sindicato Nacional de Trabajadores Mineros, Metalúrgicos, Siderúrgicos y Similares de la República Mexicana'' (mineworkers' union), based in [[Taxco]], has appealed to Governor [[Héctor Astudillo Flores]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Ocampo Arista |first1=Sergio |title=Ejidatarios terminan contrato y cierran mina canadiense |url=https://www.msn.com/es-mx/noticias/mexico/ejidatarios-terminan-contrato-y-cierran-mina-canadiense/ar-BB18HUGX?ocid=msedgdhp |website=www.msn.com |publisher=La Jornada |access-date=September 3, 2020}}</ref>
