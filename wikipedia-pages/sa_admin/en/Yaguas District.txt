'''Yaguas''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of the [[Putumayo Province]] in [[Peru]], and one of the four districts that comprise that province.

== History ==
Yaguas was part of [[Maynas Province]] until April 10, 2014 when it was created as District by Law N° 30186 as part of Putumayo Province.<ref>[http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/30186.pdf Leyes congreso Perú]</ref>

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of  18 059,27 km². Its administrative center is located 107 meters above [[sea level]].
