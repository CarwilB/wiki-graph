'''Arivechi''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is at [[Arivechi]].

==Geography==
===Borders===
It has boundaries to the north, east, and south with the [[Sahuaripa Municipality|municipality of Sahuaripa]] and to the west with the [[Bacanora Municipality|municipality of Bacanora]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM26sonora/municipios/26005a.html|title=Arivechi|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y delegaciones de México|publisher=[[INAFED]]|access-date=21 January 2022|language=es|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161011065245/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM26sonora/municipios/26005a.html|archive-date=11 October 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Area===
The area of the municipality (urban and rural) is 738.8 square kilometers.

==Population==
In the 2005 census, the population was 1,280 inhabitants, 866 of whom lived in the municipal seat. [http://www.inegi.gob.mx/inegi/default.aspx]. In the 2020 census, the population of the municipality was 1,177. [https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/ccpv/2020/]
