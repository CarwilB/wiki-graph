'''Calera''' is one of the 58 [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipalities]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]]. It is located on the central part of the state of Zacatecas and it is bounded by the municipalities of [[Municipality of General Enrique Estrada|General Enrique Estrada]], [[Municipality of Fresnillo|Fresnillo]], [[Municipality of Villa de Cos|Villa de Cos]], [[Pánuco (municipality of Zacatecas)|Pánuco]], [[Morelos (municipality of Zacatecas)|Morelos]] and [[Municipality of Zacatecas|Zacatecas]]. The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|389|km2|sqmi|sp=us}}.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32005a.htm |title="Calera", Encyclopedia de Municipios  |access-date=2007-07-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070608031922/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32005a.htm |archive-date=2007-06-08 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality comprises 0.5% of the area of the state of [[Zacatecas]].

==History==
Calera was born as a point of pass from [[Fresnillo]] and [[Zacatecas, Zacatecas|Zacatecas]]. It was named in honor of the hero of independence [[Víctor Rosales]].

== Geography ==
Calera is located on the central part of the state of Zacatecas and it is bounded by the municipalities of [[Municipality of General Enrique Estrada|General Enrique Estrada]], [[Municipality of Fresnillo|Fresnillo]], [[Municipality of Villa de Cos|Villa de Cos]], [[Pánuco (municipality of Zacatecas)|Pánuco]], [[Morelos (municipality of Zacatecas)|Morelos]] and [[Municipality of Zacatecas|Zacatecas]]. The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|389|km2|sqmi|sp=us}}.

==General information==
Calera is one of the fastest-growing cities in Zacatecas Mx., with more than 38,189 people. It is the only municipality in Zacatecas that has an International Airport, Freeway and Railroad. Calera is the most important industrial area in Zacatecas, making floors. wood furniture, juice, purified water, dry chili processing and seeds. Calera is also home to [[Corona (beer)|Corona]], the largest brewer in Latin America. 97% of Calera's population is Roman Catholic and 3% is Protestant.

Calera was decreed as a municipality by the recommendation of Benito Juárez; he went through these lands when he was fighting for the Republic. It was in Calera that Pancho Villa slept and planned the taking of Zacatecas (Toma de Zacatecas).{{cn|date=November 2022}}

==Population==
In the 2005 census, Apulco reported a population of 36,106.<ref name=pop2005>[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110613183420/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |date=2011-06-13 }} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática]] (INEGI)</ref> Of these, 29,626 lived in the municipal seat and the remainder lived in surrounding rural communities.
