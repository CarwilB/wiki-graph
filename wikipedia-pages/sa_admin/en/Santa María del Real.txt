'''Santa María del Real''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Santa María del Real municipality had a population of 10,636. Of these, 87.83% were [[Mestizo]], 8.17% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 2.18% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 1.81% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (1.09% [[Chʼortiʼ people|Chʼortiʼ]], 0.39% [[Lenca]]) and 0.01% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
