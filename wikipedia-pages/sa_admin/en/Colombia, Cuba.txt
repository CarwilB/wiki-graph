'''Colombia''' is a municipality and town in the [[Las Tunas Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in the western part of the province, {{convert|10|km|mi}} south of [[Guáimaro, Cuba|Guáimaro]]. Río Tana flows through the community.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Colombia had a population of 31,482.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|563|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=https://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|56|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
