The '''Mato District''' ({{langx|es|'''Distrito de Mato'''}}) is one of 10 districts of the [[Huaylas Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of [[Villa Sucre]].<ref>[http://www.munimato.gob.pe Official web site of the Mato District in the Huaylas province] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070313211604/http://www.munimato.gob.pe/ |date=2007-03-13 }}</ref>

==History==
The district was founded on January 2, 1857.

==Location==
The district is located in the west-central part of the province at an elevation of 2,239m in the black mountains ({{langx|es|Cordillera Negra}}), neighboring district is the [[Caraz District|Caraz district]] in which the provinces capital [[Caraz]] is located, 12&nbsp;km from the village of Villa Sucre. The regions capital [[Huaraz]] is located at 79&nbsp;km from Villa Sucre.

==Political division==
The district is divided into 1 [[village]] ({{langx|es|pueblos}}, singular: {{lang|es|pueblo}}), 4 [[hamlet (place)|hamlets]] ({{langx|es|caseríos}}, singular: {{lang|es|caserío}}) and 19 ({{langx|es|anexos}}, singular: {{lang|es|anexo}}):
