'''Petrolina de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in central [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location==
Petrolina de Goiás is located 81&nbsp;km. almost due north of [[Goiânia]], the state capital.  It is 23&nbsp;km. southwest of [[São Francisco de Goiás]], 23&nbsp;km. northwest of [[Ouro Verde de Goiás]], 21&nbsp;km. north of [[Damolândia]], and 23&nbsp;km. east of [[Santa Rosa de Goiás]].
*Distance to regional center ([[Anápolis]]):  54&nbsp;km.
*Highway connections from [[Goiânia]]:  state highway GO-80 north from [[Goiânia]], through [[Nova Veneza, Goiás|Nova Veneza]] and [[Ouro Verde de Goiás]], and then 23 kilometers northeast. [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Distancias Rodoviarias]

Neighboring municipalities:  [[Santa Rosa de Goiás]], [[Jesúpolis]], [[São Francisco de Goiás]], and [[Ouro Verde de Goiás]], [[Pirenópolis]], [[Inhumas]] and [[Itauçu]].

==History==
Petrolina de Goiás owes its origin to the fertility of the soils of the [[basin]] of the Córrego Descoberto.  Joaquim Pedro and his family donated lands in 1919 to create the settlement and build a chapel to honour Santa Maria Eterna.  The first name was "Descoberto" but this was changed to "Petrolina" in honour of the founder, Pedro.  In 1932 it became a district in the municipality of [[Jaraguá, Goiás|Jaraguá]].  In 1943 the name was changed to "Goialina", only to be changed again in 1948 to Petrolina de Goiás.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-02-06 |title=Petrolina de Goiás {{!}} Prefeitura Municipal |url=https://www.petrolina.go.gov.br/historia/ |access-date=2024-08-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200206032739/https://www.petrolina.go.gov.br/historia/ |archive-date=2020-02-06 }}</ref>

==The economy==
The economy is based on agriculture, cattle raising, services, public administration, and small transformation industries.
*Number of industrial establishments: 16
*Number of retail establishments: 69
*Dairies: Coop. Agropecuária Regional Rio das Almas Ltda. (22/05/2006)
*Banking establishments:  Banco do Brasil S.A., Banco Itaú S.A.
*Automobiles:  991

The cattle herd had 61,800 head in 2007.  The main crops cultivated were pineapples, garlic, rice, bananas, manioc, corn, and tomatoes.

==Education and Health==
{{Update|date=June 2014}}
There were 9 schools and 2,560 students in 2006.  The adult literacy rate was 82.0% (2000) (the national average was 86.4%).  There was one hospital with 23 beds in 2007.  The infant mortality rate was 18.74 (2000) (the national average was 33.0).
