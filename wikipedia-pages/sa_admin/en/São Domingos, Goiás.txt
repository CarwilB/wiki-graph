'''São Domingos''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northeastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  It is a large producer of beef cattle but is one of the poorest towns in the state.

==Location==
São Domingos is located in the [[Vão do Paranã]] statistical micro-region and is 400&nbsp;km. from [[Brasília]] and 641&nbsp;km. from the state capital, [[Goiânia]].  It is the furthest municipality in the state from [[Goiânia]]
 
Highway connections are made by BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / BR-060 / [[Alexânia]] / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / GO-118 / BR-010 / [[São João d'Aliança]] / [[Alto Paraíso de Goiás]] / BR-010 / GO-118 / [[Teresina de Goiás]] / [[Monte Alegre de Goiás]] / GO-447 / [[Vazante]] / [[Divinópolis de Goiás]] / GO-463. (64&nbsp;km. [[unpaved road]]) <ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin Anuario de Transporte] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=2009-02-05 }}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities are [[Divinópolis de Goiás]] (north);  [[Guarani de Goiás]] (south); [[Iaciara]] (west); the state of [[Bahia]] (east).

The area is very rich in wood, especially cedar, aroeira, ipê, peroba, braúna, [[vinhático]] and umburana.  Vegetation is typically savanna (cerrado) with pequi and buriti trees.  The climate is humid tropical with an average annual temperature of 26&nbsp;°C.

==History==
The settlement of this town began at the end of the eighteenth century on the banks of the present-day São Domingos River.  The first settlement was called "Arraial Velho" but it soon fell into ruin.  In 1821 the Portuguese Domingos and José Valente arrived seeking gold.  They brought an image of São Domingos Gusmão, which gave the name to the town and the river that passes nearby.   The town grew and in 1835 it became a district of [[Arraias]], which is now part of [[Tocantins]]  In 1854 it became a municipality, one of the oldest in the state of [[Goiás]].<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/ Sepin Histórico dos Municípios] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070115162332/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/ |date=2007-01-15 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The economy is based on subsistence agriculture, cattle raising (120,000 head in 2004), services, public administration, and small transformation industries.  There was one bank: Banco do Brasil S.A. (Agosto/2007).

==Tourism==
The topography of the region is very mountainous, with the municipal seat lying near the Serra Geral mountains.  The site was chosen for the filming of a TV miniseries called Grande Sertão Veredas.  Nearby is a state park called Parque Estadual da Terra Ronca,<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20040828231213/http://www.eco.tur.br/ecoguias/terraronca/areas/parques/prqestadual.htm Eco.tur.br]</ref> which contains Angelica cavern,<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20040411223846/http://www.eco.tur.br/ecoguias/terraronca/ecopontos/cavernas/angelica.htm Eco.tur.br]</ref> whose 14&nbsp;km. extension makes it one of the longest in the country.  The park gets its name from a cavern, Terra Ronca, which produces a sound caused by an underground river.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20040411235819/http://www.eco.tur.br/ecoguias/terraronca/ecopontos/cavernas/terraronca.htm Eco.tur.br.]</ref>
