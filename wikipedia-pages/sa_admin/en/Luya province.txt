'''Luya''' is a province located in the south and west part of the [[Department of Amazonas, Peru|department of Amazonas]] in [[Peru]]. 

Its principal rivers are:

*[[Marañón River|Marañon]] that flows from south to northwest and forms its extensive west limit with the [[Cajamarca Region|department of Cajamarca]]
*[[Utcubamba River|Utcubamba]], which runs from south to north-east and forms its east boundary with the provinces of [[Chachapoyas Province|Chachapoyas]] and [[Bongará Province|Bongará]]
*[[Magunchal River|Magunchal]], which is born in the heights of the [[Colcamar (Peru)|district of Colcamar]], runs from south to north and ends into the [[Utcubamba River|Utcubamba]], forming its most extensive and rich agricultural zone.

The province was created by law on February 5, 1861, has 23 districts and its capital is [[Lamud]].<ref>{{Cite book |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=KUgiAQAAIAAJ |title=Trade Promotion Series |date=1925 |publisher=U.S. Government Printing Office |pages=520 |language=en}}</ref>
