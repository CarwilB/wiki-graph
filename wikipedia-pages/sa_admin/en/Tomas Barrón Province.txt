'''Tomás Barrón''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]].

==Location==
Tomás Barrón province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 31' and 17° 44' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 20' and 67° 34' [[Longitude|West]]. It has been named after Colonel Tomas Barrón.

The province borders [[La Paz Department (Bolivia)|La Paz Department]] in the North and West, and [[Cercado Province (Oruro)|Cercado Province]] in the South and East.

The province extends over 30&nbsp;km from North to South, and 25&nbsp;km from East to West.

==Population==
Main [[language]] of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 87%, while 82% of the population speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]] and 8% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992).

*The population increased from 5,045 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 5,424 (2001 census), an increase of 7.5%. - 48% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).
*26% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 95% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).
*49% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 1% in [[mining]], 11% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 39% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).
*66% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 27% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).( which have all been  driven out since 1992){{citation needed|date=May 2025}}

==Division==
The province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Eucaliptus Municipality''', which is identical to the Tomás Barrón Province.
