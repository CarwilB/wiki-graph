'''Tercero''' (''Barrio Tercero'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Together with [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]], [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]], [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], and [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], Tercero is one of the municipality's [[Ponce, Puerto Rico#Barrios|six core urban barrios]]. It was organized in 1878.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html# |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
Tercero is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and east of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

Barrio Tercero is bounded on the North by Isabel Street, on the South by Comercio/[[Francisco Parra Duperón]] Street, on the West by Plaza Degetau and Plaza Munoz Rivera Streets, and on the East by [[Rio Portugues]].<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=12 August 2010 }} Retrieved February 17, 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Tercero is bounded in the North by Barrio [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], in the South by [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]], in the West by [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], and in the East by [[Machuelo Abajo]] and [[San Antón]].

==Features and demographics==
In 2000, Tercero had {{convert|0.1|sqmi|m2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area. The population of Tercero was 773. The population density in Tercero was 10,134 persons per square mile.<ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Tercero+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved, February 17, 2010.</ref>

In 2010, it had {{convert|0.08|sqmi|m2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area. ==Notable landmarks==
Tercero is home to many city landmarks.<ref>[http://www.visitaaponce.com/oficina_permisos/oot/Plan_Area/106A.pdf ''Plan de Area de los Centros Urbanos de Ponce: Map 106A.'']{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }} Office of Territorial Ordering. Autonomous Municipality of Ponce. Ponce, Puerto Rico. Retrieved 5 August 2011.</ref>  The Antonio Arias Ventura promenade, [[Ponce History Museum]], [[Tricentennial Park (Ponce, Puerto Rico)|Tricentennial Park]], [[Teatro La Perla]], and the [[Museum of Puerto Rican Music]] are all located in Barrio Tercero. Also, the [[NRHP]]-listed [[Banco de Ponce (building)|Banco de Ponce]], [[Ponce High School]], [[Banco Crédito y Ahorro Ponceño (building)|Banco Crédito y Ahorro Ponceño]], and [[Salazar-Candal House]] are found in Barrio Tercero as well.
