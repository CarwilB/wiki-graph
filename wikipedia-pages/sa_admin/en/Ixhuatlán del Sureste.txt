'''Ixhuatlán del Sureste''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south-east zone of Veracruz, about {{convert|320|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Xalapa]], the state capital. The total area of the municipality is {{convert|276.37|sqkm|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

The municipality is delimited to the north by [[Coatzacoalcos]], to the east and south by [[Moloacán]], to the south-west by [[Minatitlán, Veracruz|Minatitlán]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30082a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ixhuatlán del Sureste|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-09|archive-date=2007-01-28|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128070245/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30082a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather tends to be warm year-round with rains in summer and autumn. The chief agricultural products are [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]] and [[Orange (fruit)|oranges]].

In August there is a celebration in honor of [[Saint Christopher]], the town's [[patron saint]].
