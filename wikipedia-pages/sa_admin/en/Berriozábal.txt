'''Berriozábal''' is a [[city]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], in southern [[Mexico]]. It covers an area of 352&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 43,179,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Berriozábal|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=012|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304140832/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=012|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 28,719 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Berriozábal|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07012a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=July 25, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927205936/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07012a.htm|archive-date=September 27, 2007}}</ref>

In 2010, the city of Berriozábal had a population of 28,128.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Beside the city of Berriozábal, the municipality had 485 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Santa Inés Buenavista, Chiapas|Santa Inés Buenavista]] (1,559), [[Ignacio Zaragoza, Chiapas|Ignacio Zaragoza]] (1,354), and [[Las Maravillas, Chiapas|Las Maravillas]] (1,339), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
