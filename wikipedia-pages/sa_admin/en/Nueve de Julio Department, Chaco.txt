'''Nueve de Julio ''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 27,000 inhabitants in an area of  1,130&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Las Breñas]], which is located around 1,130&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
