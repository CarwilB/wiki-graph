'''Santa Lucía Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Guayas Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[Santa Lucía, Ecuador|Santa Lucía]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 33,868.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
