'''San Juan del Sur''' is a [[municipality]] and coastal town on the [[Pacific Ocean]], in the [[Rivas (department)|Rivas]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] in southwest [[Nicaragua]]. It is located {{convert|140|km|mi}} south of [[Managua]]. San Juan del Sur is popular among surfers and is a vacation spot for many Nicaraguan families and foreign tourists.

Its population is approximately 15,553, consisting largely of families engaged in fishing, tourism or food and beverage industry and foreigners from the [[United States]], [[Canada]] and [[Europe]]. While the local economy  was, for many years, based on fishing and shipping, it has shifted towards tourism  in the last 20 years. In October 2002, to mark the city's 150th anniversary, it was formally designated the "Port of San Juan del Sur" and a "Tourism City of Nicaragua."<ref>{{cite news

San Juan del Sur, set beside a crescent-shaped bay, was a popular resting place for gold prospectors headed to [[California]] in the 1850s. Correspondingly, San Juan del Sur served as a hub for [[Cornelius Vanderbilt#Oceangoing steamship lines|Cornelius Vanderbilt Lines]].

A statue of the [[Christ of the Mercy]] sits above town on the northern end of the bay. The Mirador del Cristo de la Misericordia sits on one of the highest points on the bay and is one of the tallest Jesus statues in the world.<ref name="auto">{{cite web|url=https://esperanzamarket.com/blogs/news/92465923-top-three-things-to-do-in-san-juan-del-sur-nicaragua|title=Top Three Things To Do In San Juan Del Sur Nicaragua|last=EsperanzaMarket|access-date=2016-06-15|archive-date=2016-09-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160914162235/https://esperanzamarket.com/blogs/news/92465923-top-three-things-to-do-in-san-juan-del-sur-nicaragua|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The town is near the site where ''[[Survivor: Nicaragua]]'' and ''[[Survivor: Redemption Island]]'' were filmed.<ref name="PRLog">{{cite news|url=http://www.prlog.org/10682088-nicaragua-to-host-two-seasons-of-hit-tv-series-survivor.html|publisher=PR Log|date=May 16, 2010|title={{-'}}Nicaragua to host two seasons of hit TV series Survivor|access-date=May 16, 2010}}</ref> Those seasons aired in 2010 and 2011, respectively. The 29th U.S. Survivor season, ''[[Survivor: San Juan del Sur]]'' (2014), was also filmed there,<ref>{{cite magazine|url=http://ew.com/article/2014/05/21/survivor-san-juan-del-sur-blood-vs-water-jeff-probst/|title='Survivor: San Juan del Sur -- Blood vs. Water': Jeff Probst gives intel on NEXT season|magazine=[[Entertainment Weekly]] |date=21 May 2014}}</ref> as was Season 30, ''[[Survivor: Worlds Apart]]'', which aired in 2015. San Juan del Sur has become a hot spot for international surfing competition.<ref name="auto"/>

== Nicaragua Canal ==
San Juan del Sur is the port on the Pacific that has been focus of many historic and modern day [[Nicaragua Canal]] proposals because of its location on the narrow isthmus of Rivas between the [[Pacific Ocean]] and [[Lake Nicaragua]].

== The 1992 Nicaraguan tsunami==
{{Main|1992 Nicaragua earthquake}}

On September 2, 1992, a magnitude 7.0 earthquake off the central coast of Nicaragua generated a [[tsunami]] that devastated many communities along the Pacific Coast. Run-up values along the coast ranged from 2m in the north and south to up to 10m in some central locations. Run-up values in San Juan del Sur were approximately 5m. As a result of the tsunami an estimated 60% of homes in the community were destroyed, approximately 800 residents were displaced,  and  businesses and homes along the Malecón suffered severe damage.

==Geography==

===Climate===
The climate for San Juan del Sur is a [[tropical savanna climate]] (''Aw'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification]]), with constant and very warm temperatures throughout the year. The dry season lasts from December to May, with a substantial and heavy rainy season throughout the remainder of the year, with an average yearly rainfall of {{convert|1,710|mm|in|0|disp=or}}.
