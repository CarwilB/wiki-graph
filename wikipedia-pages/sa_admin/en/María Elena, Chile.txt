'''María Elena''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Tocopilla Province]], [[Antofagasta Region]]. According to the 2012 census, the commune population was 4,593 and has an area of {{convert|12197.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

== History ==
Maria Elena is named after Mary Ellen Comdon, the wife of the first [[Chile saltpeter|saltpeter]] refinery works (''oficina salitre'') manager, [[Elias Anton Cappelen Smith|Elias Cappelen]]. Originally, the name of the works was "Coya Norte". Situated on land purchased from the Treasury in 1924, the plant was opened in 1926 using a [[sodium nitrate]] extraction system patented by the Guggenheim Brothers which had replaced the Shanks system. It was laid out on the basis of the [[flag of the United Kingdom]]{{Citation needed|reason=This sounds like an urban myth. Grid plan with diagonals. |date=January 2023}}. Together with the former refinery at Pedro de Valdivia built in 1931, it was the largest saltpeter works with a combined output of over one million tons per year.

In 1965 both were taken over by the state, through the ''[[Sociedad Química y Minera|Chemicals and Mining Company]]'' (SQM), which was privatized in 1980. This privatization explains why most land and buildings are owned by SQM and not by individuals or the Chilean state.

In 1996, it became the only active mining community in the country after the closure of the saltpeter works at Pedro de Valdivia, following which many workers and their families were relocated to Maria Elena.

On November 14, 2007, the area was devastated by a 7.7 [[Moment magnitude scale|M<sub>w</sub>]] [[2007 Tocopilla earthquake|earthquake]] with an epicenter {{convert|80|km|0|abbr=on}} north of Maria Elena. Almost all the town's old houses collapsed or were made uninhabitable, and the local hospital also almost completely collapsed, though with no loss of life or injuries.<ref name="PensaTocopilla">{{Cite web |url=http://www.prensatocopilla.cl/prontus4_nots/antialone.html?page=http://www.prensatocopilla.cl/prontus4_nots/site/artic/20071115/pags/20071115094341.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707012216/http://www.prensatocopilla.cl/prontus4_nots/antialone.html?page=http://www.prensatocopilla.cl/prontus4_nots/site/artic/20071115/pags/20071115094341.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 7, 2011 |title=El hospital se vino abajo |accessdate=27 October 2010 |date=15 November 2007 |language=es }}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 1992 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] María Elena had 7,530 inhabitants; of these, 7,412 (98.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 118 (1.6%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 4,298 men and 3,232 women. By 2002, the population had fallen 44.9% (124,130 persons{{Inconsistent|date=October 2018|reason=The population numbers cited are inconsistent with those reported in the foregoing sentence and in the table below.}}) since the 1992 census of 13,660{{Inconsistent|date=October 2018|reason=The population numbers cited are inconsistent with those reported in the foregoing sentence and in the table below.}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, María Elena is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Jorge Godoy Bolvarán ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]), and the council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/><ref name="Munitel"/>
* Carlos Hidalgo Marfull
* Nelson Avendaño Véliz
* Jaqueline Godoy Soto
* Jorge Ramirez Plaza
* Carlos Ardiles Aceituno
* Andrés Concha F.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], María Elena is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marcos Espinosa ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) and Felipe Ward ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 3rd electoral district, (together with [[Tocopilla]], [[Calama, Chile|Calama]], [[Ollagüe, Chile|Ollagüe]] and [[San Pedro de Atacama]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Cantero Ojeda ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) and [[José Antonio Gómez]] (PRSD) as part of the 2nd senatorial constituency (Antofagasta Region).
