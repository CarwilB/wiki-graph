'''Guararé District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Los Santos Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 9,485.<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|216|km2}}. The capital lies at the city of [[Guararé]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
Guararé District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[Guararé]] (capital)
*[[El Espinal, Los Santos|El Espinal]]
*[[El Macano]]
*[[Guararé Arriba]]
*[[La Enea]]
*[[La Pasera]]
*[[Las Trancas]]
*[[Llano Abajo]]
*[[El Hato]]
*[[Perales, Los Santos|Perales]]
