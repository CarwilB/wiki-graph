'''Valle de Agua Arriba ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Changuinola District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|70.3|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,163 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|45|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 18 of February 26, 2009.<ref name=inec/>
