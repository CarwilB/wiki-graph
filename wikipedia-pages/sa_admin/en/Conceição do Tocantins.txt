'''Conceição do Tocantins''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 4,087 (2020) and its area is 2,501&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref> Geographically, the city is located about 400 meters above sea level in the Tocantins-Araguaia river basin. It has a warm tropical climate with a clear rainy season. The area is also one of the least populated parts of the state, with a population density of about 1.5 people per square kilometer.<ref>{{Cite web |title=O saneamento em CONCEIÇÃO DO TOCANTINS {{!}} TO {{!}} Municípios e Saneamento {{!}} Instituto Água e Saneamento |url=https://www.aguaesaneamento.org.br/municipios-e-saneamento/to/conceicao-do-tocantins |access-date=2026-05-25 |website=IAS - Instituto Água e Saneamento |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
