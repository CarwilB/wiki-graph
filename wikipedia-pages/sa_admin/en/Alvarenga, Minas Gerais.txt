'''Alvarenga''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020 its population is estimated to be 3,844.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alvarenga População |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/alvarenga/panorama |access-date=2024-09-20 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

Alvarenga became a municipality on December 30, 1962.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alvarenga História |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/alvarenga/historico |access-date=2024-09-20 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>
