'''San Joaquín''' is a [[Municipalities of Querétaro|municipality]] in the central  [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Querétaro]].<ref name="INFDM">{{Cite web|title=Querétaro: San Joaquín |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |language=es |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx:80/work/templates/enciclo/queretaro/Mpios/22015a.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050616074600/http://www.e-local.gob.mx:80/work/templates/enciclo/queretaro/Mpios/22015a.htm |archive-date=16 June 2005 |url-status=dead |df=dmy}}</ref> <!-- As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . --->

==History==
Before the [[Spanish Empire|Spanish]], the site at "Ranas" was a mining center, principally for [[cinnabar]] which was used as a pigment. The "Ranas" site was first mined about 400C.E.<ref name="INAH">{{Cite web|title=Zona Arqueológica de Ranas |language=es |date=11 January 2018 |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Antropología e Historia]] |url=http://inah.gob.mx/zonas/34-zona-arqueologica-de-ranas}}</ref> In 1622 the village of "San Nicolás Tolentino en el Paraje de las Rana" was founded.<ref name="INAH" /> In 1682 the community of Ojo de Agua de Ranas was founded.<ref name="INFDM" />

In 1724 Viceroy [[Juan de Acuña, 2nd Marquis of Casa Fuerte]] granted the first land in the municipality.<ref name="INFDM" /> In 1728 the first Spanish mine was opened at [[San Juan Nepomuceno, Mexico|San Juan Nepomuceno]].<ref name="INFDM" />

In 1864 the name "San Joaquín" was first used on a map.<ref name="INFDM" />

==Geography==
The municipality of San Joaquín covers an area of {{cvt|275|km2}}, and is located between 99° 22' and 99° 39' West longitude, and between 21° 07' and 20° 53' North latitude.<ref name="INFDM" /> Altitude varies from a low of 600&nbsp;meters to heights of 2,700&nbsp;meters.<ref name="INEGI-1">{{Cite web|title=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos: San Joaquín, Querétaro |language=es |year=2005 |publisher=[[National Institute of Statistics and Geography]] {INEGI) |location=Mexico |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/22/22015.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180802183037/http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/22/22015.pdf |archive-date=2 August 2018 |url-status=live |df=dmy}}</ref>
