'''Quebrada Arenas''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for ''sandy creek'') is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico]]. Located in the southwest corner of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], it is the only rural [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the [[municipality]]. Quebrada Arenas is totally outside San Juan's municipal urban zone according to the last [[2010 United States Census|census]]. In 2010 it had a population of 2,747 and a land area of {{convert|2.46|sqmi|km2}}, resulting in a population density of 1,116.7 residents per square mile (431.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>), the lowest of any barrio in San Juan.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico: 2010 Population and Housing Unit Counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|page=32|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

Quebrada Arenas has boundaries with the municipalities of [[Aguas Buenas, Puerto Rico|Aguas Buenas]] and [[Caguas, Puerto Rico|Caguas]] to the south, and [[Guaynabo, Puerto Rico|Guaynabo]] to the west, and is bounded by [[Caimito, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Caimito]] barrios to the east and [[Tortugo, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Tortugo]] barrio to the north.
