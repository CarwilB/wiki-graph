'''Miacatlán''' is a [[city]] and municipal seat of the municipality of Miacatlán in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Morelos]]. It stands at {{coord|18|46|20|N|99|21|17|W}}.

To the north is the [[State of Mexico]] and the municipality of [[Temixco]], to the south [[Puente de Ixtla]], [[Mazatepec]] and [[Tetecala]], to the east [[Xochitepec]], and to the west [[Coatlán del Río]] and the State of Mexico. The city serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding municipality of the same name. The municipality reported 26,713 inhabitants in the 2015 census.
<ref name="cuentame.inegi.org.mx">http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/mor/poblacion/default.aspx (Dec 13, 2018)</ref>  The altitude of Miacatlan is 1,054 meters above sea level and it covers 233,644.30&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of territory, and it is 40&nbsp;km from [[Cuernavaca]].<ref name=municipios />

The toponym ''Miacatlán'' comes from a [[Nahuatl]] name ''Mitl'' (arrow), and ''Acatl'' (rod or cane), and ''Tlan'' (place), and means "place of abundant reeds for arrows". This is probably in reference to the two lakes in the municipality, ''Coatetelco'' and ''El Rodeo''.<ref name=inafed>{{cite web|url=http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM17morelos/municipios/17015a.html |title=Estado de Morelos: MIACATLÁN|publisher=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=Dec 20, 2018|language=Spanish|trans-title=State of Morelos: Miacatlan}}</ref>

The archaeological site of [[Coatetelco archaeological site|Coatetelco]] is also in Miacatlan.<ref name=inafed />

==History==
The history of the municipality of Miacatlán goes back to the pre-Hispanic era, it was part of a political and religious center, tributary to [[Xochicalco]], but later it belonged to the [[Cuernavaca|Cuauháhuac]] manor. Miacatlán was conquered by the [[Aztec]]s, which made it belong to [[Tenochtitlán]]. During the colonial period, it was called ''San Francisco Miacatlán'' and became part of the ''Marquesado del Valle de Oaxaca''.<ref name=municipios />

In 1823 Miacatlan was part of the municipality of [[Mazatepec]], [[State of Mexico]]. When Morelos became a state in 1869, Miacatlán acquired the category of municipality, including the towns of ''Coatetelco'' and ''Palpan de Barandas'', the ranches of ''Nexapa'' and ''Ojo de Agua'', as well as the Haciendas of ''Acatzingo, La Nigua'', and ''Miacatlán''.<ref name=municipios />

During the [[Mexican Revolution]], General Pedro Ojeda fled to the Hacienda de Miacatlán after the ''Siege of Cuernavaca'' (1914). Also General ''Francisco V. Pacheco'' was shot on orders of General [[Emiliano Zapata]] at ''El Zapote'' in 1917.<ref name=inafed />

''Abel Espin Garcia'' of ''Juntos por Morelos'' (Together for Morelos coalition) was elected Presidente Municipal (mayor) in the election of July 1, 2018.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://impepac.mx/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/Candidatos-electos-2018.pdf |title=Candidatos electos 2018 |website=impepac.mx |access-date=Dec 14, 2018|language=es}}</ref>

The community of [[Coatetelco, Morelos|Coatetelco]] became a separate municipality on January 1, 2019.<ref>https://www.diariodemorelos.com/noticias/habr%C3%A1-partir-de-2019-4-municipios-ind%C3%ADgenas-en-morelos (Dec 20, 2018)</ref>

==Communities==
* ''Miacatlan'' is the municipal seat. The town has 7,212 inhabitants and is located at 1,005 meters (3,927&nbsp;ft.) above sea level. There are three preschools (two public, one private), five elementary schools (four public, one private), two middle schools (one public, one private), and two high schools, grades 10-12 (one public, one private).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/miacatlan/|title=Miacatlán|accessdate=February 11, 2019 |language=Spanish|publisher=Pueblos America|trans-title=Miacatlan}}</ref> It is located 44&nbsp;km south of Cuernavaca and 132&nbsp;km from Mexico City.
* ''El Mirador'' has 1,392 inhabitants. It is located at 1,053 meters (3,455&nbsp;ft.) above sea level.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/el-mirador-118/|publisher=Pueblos America|title=El Mirador (Miacatlan)|accessdate=February 11, 2019|language=Spanish|trans-title=The Mirador (Miacatlan)}}</ref> It is one km from the municipal seat.
* ''[[Xochicalco]] (Cirenio Longares)'' has 1,361 inhabitants. It is located next to El Rodeo at 1,170 meters (3,839&nbsp;ft.) above sea level. is an agricultural community; many of its other residents work in Cuernavaca. The archaeological site of the same name is located near the community.<ref name=inafed /> There is one Indigenous school, one preschool, one elementary school, and one middle school.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/xochicalco-cirenio-longares/|title=Xochicalco (Cirenio Longares)|accessdate=February 11, 2019|language=Spanish|publisher=Pueblos America|trans-title=Xochicalco (Cirenio Longares}}</ref> It is located 8&nbsp;km from the municipal seat.
* ''El Rodeo'' has 1,278 inhabitants. It is located next to Xochicaclo at 1,096 meters (3,596&nbsp;ft.) above sea level. There are two preschools (one public, one private), one elementary school (grades 1-6), and one middle school (grades 7-9).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/el-rodeo-73/|title=El Rodeo|accessdate=February 11, 2019|language=Spanish|publisher=Pueblos America|trans-title=The Rodeo}}</ref> El Rodeo is an agricultural community; many of its other residents work in Cuernavaca. The lake by the same name was formed by damming the Rio Tenembe.<ref name=inafed /> It is 5&nbsp;km from the municipal seat.
* ''Palpan de Baranda'' has more than 1,000 inhabitants. It is a farming and ranching community located 20&nbsp;km from the municipal seat of Miacatlan.<ref name=inafed /> It is located at 1,611 meters (5,285&nbsp;ft.) above sea level. There is one preschool, one elementary school, and one middle school.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/palpan-de-baranda/|title=Palpan de Baranda|accessdate=February 11, 2019|language=Spanish|publisher=Pueblos America|trans-title=Palpan de Baranda}}</ref>
* Other communities are very small. ''Tlajotla'' is considered the most beautiful town in the municipality.<ref name=inafed /> It has 148 inhabitants and is located at 1,641 meters (5,384&nbsp;ft.) above sea level. There is one preschool, one elementary school, and one middle school.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tlajotla/|title=Tlajotla|accessdate=February 11, 2019 |language=Spanish|publisher=Pueblos America|trans-title=Tlajotla}}</ref> It is 23&nbsp;km from the municipal seat.

===Coatetelco archaeological zone===
[[Coatetelco archaeological site|Coatetelco]] is a pre-Hispanic archaeological site located next to the ''Laguna de Coatetelco'' (Lake Coatetelco) two km from ''Alpuyeca''. Evidence of nomadic groups goes back 25,000 years, with small farming communities settled in during the Early and Mid-Preclassic Periods (900-500 BCE). The Coatetelco apogee was in the Late Preclassical period (500-150 BCE) with communities of 250 to 500 inhabitants. Excavations demonstrate human occupation influenced by [[Teotihuacan]] (450-600 CE).

[[Xochicalco]] dominated the area during the Epiclassic Period (700 – 900 CE), after which Coatetelco once again became the leading community in the area. Nearly all the standing architecture at the site was built during the Late Postclassical Period (1350–1521).<ref name=municipios>{{cite web|url=http://www.municipios.mx/morelos/miacatlan/ |title=Miacatlán|publisher=Municipios.mx|accessdate=Dec 20, 2018|language=Spanish|trans-title=Miacatlan}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://infomorelos.com/arqueolo/tlaruinas.html |title=Tlaruinas|website=infomorelos.com|archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20100920074317/http://infomorelos.com/arqueolo/tlaruinas.html|access-date=Dec 20, 2018|archive-date= 2010-09-20}}</ref>

Most of the excavation was carried out by Raúl Arana. There is a small pyramid, a ball court, and a circular platform dedicated to the god of wind, ''Ehécatl''. The tallest structure is the ''Cuauhtlitzin'' temple, which has a staircase with lateral arches. There was also a female stone figure that was called Cuauhtlitzin.

There is a museum on the site.<ref name=municipios />

==Culture==
*Feast of ''Santo Tomás Apóstol'', December 21 (Miacatlan). Fireworks, mechanical rides,  ''Corridas de Toros''. There is a religious festival on February 11.
*Feast of the Immaculate Conception, Ash Wednesday (Palpan). ''Corridas de Toros''.

'''Music''' There are four brass bands that play at the fiestas of ''Santo Tomás, Molina, La Sumaya'', and ''Santa Cecilia''.

'''Handicrafts'''. Miacatlan produces ceramics and Palpan produces embroidery.

'''Food'''. Regional foods include goat and mutton ''barbacoa'' and pork ''cochinitas''. ''Tamales de Mojarra'' (a type of fish) in Coatetelco. Cheese and mezcal-based drinks in Palpan.<ref name=inafed />
