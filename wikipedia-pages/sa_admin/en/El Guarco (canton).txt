'''El Guarco''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Cartago Province|Cartago]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Tejar District|Tejar]] district.

==Toponymy==
Named after [[El Guarco]], a [[cacique]] in the colonial era.

== History ==
El Guarco was created on 26 July 1939 by decree 195.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
El Guarco has an area of {{formatnum:167.69}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:1526}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

El Guarco is an elongated canton that stretches southeast from its capital city to encompass a swatch of the [[Cordillera de Talamanca]] (Talamanca Mountain Range). The [[Pan-American Highway]], locally designated as [[National Route 2 (Costa Rica)|Route 2]], delineates the canton's lengthy southwestern border.
