'''Tarauacá''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|taɾɐwaˈka}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northwest of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Tarauacá has a population of 43,151 people<ref name="ibge">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=120060 ibge.gov.br]</ref> and has an area {{convert|20171|sqkm|sqmi|0|sp=us}}.<ref name="ibge" />

==Geography==

The municipality contains 38% of the [[Alto Tarauacá Extractive Reserve]], created in 2000.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Alto Tarauacá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Alto Tarauacá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/282|accessdate=2016-08-07}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|216062|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Gregório State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2004.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Rio Gregório – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Rio Gregório|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5348|accessdate=2016-07-01}}</ref>
It also contains the {{convert|143897|ha|adj=on}} [[Mogno State Forest]] and the {{convert|126360|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Liberdade State Forest]], two other sustainable use units created on the same date.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Mogno – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Mogno|publisher=ISA:Instituto Socoambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4572|accessdate=2016-07-01}}</ref><ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Rio Liberdade – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Rio Liberdade|publisher=ISA: Instituto Sociambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5429|accessdate=2016-07-01}}</ref>

== Economy ==
In 2017, Tarauacá had per capita GDP of 11.763,89 R$, total revenue of 71.657.950 R$, and total expenses of 63.519.410 R$.<ref name="cidades">{{cite web |title=Panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ac/tarauaca/panorama |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |access-date=20 April 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180901113326/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ac/tarauaca/panorama |archive-date=September 1, 2018 |language=pt-br |url-status=live}}</ref>

==Transportation==
Tarauacá is served by [[Tarauacá Airport|José Galera dos Santos Airport]]. It is a small airport.
