'''Tocache'''  is one of 10 [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[San Martín Region]] in northern [[Peru]].

== History ==
The province of Tocache was created by Law Dec 6, 1984, in Fernando Belaúnde's [[Term of office|term]].

== Boundaries==
*'''North:''' [[Mariscal Cáceres Province|province of Mariscal Cáceres]]
*'''East:''' [[Bellavista Province|province of Bellavista]]
*'''South:''' [[Huánuco Region]]
*'''West:''' [[Ancash Region]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into five districts.
* [[Nuevo Progreso District|Nuevo Progreso]] ([[Nuevo Progreso, Peru|Nuevo Progreso]])
* [[Pólvora District|Pólvora]] ([[Pólvora]])
* [[Shunte District|Shunte]] ([[Tambo de Paja]])
* [[Tocache District|Tocache]] ([[Tocache]])
* [[Uchiza District|Uchiza]] ([[Uchiza]])
