'''Bolognesi''' is one of 20 provinces of the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]].

==Overview==
The province originally was part of the province of [[Cajatambo Province|Cajatambo]] (part of [[Lima Region]] since 1916) until 1903, when it was split off and named after Col. [[Francisco Bolognesi]], the hero of the [[Battle of Arica]]. In 1990, the [[Ocros Province|province of Ocros]] split off from Bolognesi.

== Geography ==
The area of the province comprises parts of four [[Andes|Andean]] mountain ranges with snow-covered mountains: the [[Cordillera Blanca]], the [[Cordillera Negra]], the [[Wallanka mountain range|Wallanka]] mountain range and the [[Waywash mountain range|Waywash]] mountain range. == Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (84.05%) learnt to speak in childhood, 15.43% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013  }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
