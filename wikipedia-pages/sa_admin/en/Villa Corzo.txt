'''Villa Corzo''' is a [[city]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]]. The city of Villa Corzo (the municipal seat is located at (16 ° 11'N 93 ° 16'W / 16,183, 93,267 and 580 meters.)

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 74,477,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Villa Corzo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=107|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304095848/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=107|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 68,685 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Villa Corzo, Chiapas, México|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07107a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=August 10, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927203849/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07107a.htm|archive-date=September 27, 2007|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref> It covers an area of 4,026.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the city of Villa Corzo had a population of 10,841.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Villa Corzo, the municipality had 1,985 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[El Parral, Chiapas|El Parral]] (10,865), [[San Pedro Buenavista, Chiapas|San Pedro Buenavista]] (8,969), [[Revolución Mexicana, Chiapas|Revolución Mexicana]] (7,989), [[Valle Morelos, Chiapas|Valle Morelos]] (3,328), [[Nuevo Vicente Guerrero, Chiapas|Nuevo Vicente Guerrero]] (2,906), classified as urban, and [[Jericó (Porvenir), Chiapas|Jericó (Porvenir)]] (2,467), [[1ro. de Mayo, Chiapas|1ro. de Mayo]] (2,381), [[Emiliano Zapata, Chiapas|Emiliano Zapata]] (1,496), [[Manuel Ávila Camacho, Chiapas|Manuel Ávila Camacho]] (1,362), and [[Monterrey, Chiapas|Monterrey]] (1,086), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
