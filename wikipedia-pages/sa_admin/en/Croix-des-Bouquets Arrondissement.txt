'''Croix-des-Bouquets Arrondissement''' ({{langx|fr|Arrondissement de Croix-des-Bouquets}}, {{langx|ht|Awondisman Kwadèboukè}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest Department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 474,806 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|archive-date=2015-11-06|url-status=dead|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Croix-des-Bouquets Arrondissement start with the number 63.

The arrondissement consists of the following [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]]s:
* [[Croix-des-Bouquets, Ouest|Croix-des-Bouquets]]
* [[Thomazeau]]
* [[Ganthier]]
* [[Fonds-Verrettes]]
* [[Cornillon, Ouest|Cornillon]]
* [[Canaan, Haiti|Canaan]]
