'''Porto Walter''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈpoʁtu ˈwawteʁ, - ˈvawteʁ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 12,241 and its area is 6,136&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality contains 27% of the {{convert|846633|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Divisor National Park]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA da Serra do Divisor – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
