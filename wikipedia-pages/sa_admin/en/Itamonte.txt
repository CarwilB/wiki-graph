'''Itamonte''' is a city in the south of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was 15,714 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|432|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cod.ibge.gov.br/CM7]</ref>

==Geography==

Itamonte is a touristic city in the [[Serra da Mantiqueira]] mountains. The territorial area is {{cvt|430.597|km2}} big. The city's current mayor is Alexandre Augusto Moreira Santos, with Marcia Castilho being deputy mayor.

The municipality contains 34.54% of the {{convert|22917|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Papagaio State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES da Serra do Papagaio – ISA}}|language=pt
