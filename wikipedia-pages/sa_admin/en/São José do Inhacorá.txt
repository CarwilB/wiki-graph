'''São José do Inhacorá''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Originally, this little city was a district of [[Três de Maio]] and obtained its emancipation in 1992.{{citation needed|date=December 2014}}

The city is considered the less dangerous in Brazil to live, and it shares this status with [[Borá]]. Violence is virtually eradicated (0 deaths per 100 thousand inhabitants).{{citation needed|date=December 2014}}
