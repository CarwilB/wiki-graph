'''Mercedes Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the eastern part of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 62,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1050|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Mercedes, Buenos Aires|Mercedes]], {{convert|100|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Geography==
Mercedes Partido is situated in the Undulated Pampa (''Pampa Ondulada''), one of the subregions of the [[Pampa]]s. It owes its name to a series of undulations, or the way the terrain descends gradually to the banks of the [[Paraná River]]. It extends north of the [[Salado River (Buenos Aires)|Salado River]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], from [[Samborombón Bay]] to the northeast, to the provinces of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]] and [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]].

==Climate==
As in the rest of the Undulated Pampa, Mercedes's climate is [[Humid Pampa|humid-temperate]], characterized by:

* Four distinct seasons. 
* The moderating influence on temperatures by being near to the [[Atlantic Ocean]].
* The free circulation of Atlantic humid winds, due to the flat terrain.
