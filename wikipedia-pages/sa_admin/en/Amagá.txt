'''Amagá''' ({{IPA|es|amaˈɣa}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the subregion of [[Southwestern Antioquia]]. The area was known for its coal and iron ore production and it hosted early iron works.<ref>Bell, P. L. (1921) ''Colombia: A Commercial and Industrial Handbook'' (Special Agents Series #206) Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, United States Department of Commerce, Government Printing Office, Washington, D.C., [https://books.google.com/books?id=HexJAAAAMAAJ&pg=PA234] page 234]</ref> Ex-[[President of Colombia|Colombian president]] [[Belisario Betancur]] was born in this location. As of 2015, the population comprised 29,555 people.

==Notable places==
The Forge (La Ferrería) produced the first steel in Antioquia and operated from 1864 to 1931.  Its ruins are now a Rural National Monument.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20081206090518/http://www.cotelcoantioquiachoco.org/sitioantioquia/atractivos_ant_detalle_atractivo.php?id1=29&nombre1=Tur%EDsticos,+recreativos+y+culturales+del+Suroeste&id2=71&nombre2=Amag%E1 "Amagá: Atractivos Naturales y Recreativos"] Cotelco (Asociación Hotelera de Colombia), in Spanish, accessed 17 June 2010</ref> There is also a very famous touristic place which is called "El viaducto" (the path). {{citation needed|date=June 2023}}
