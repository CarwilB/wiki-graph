''' Huejuquilla El Alto  ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]] in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]], being the northernmost municipality in Jalisco. The municipality covers an area of 769.5 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 7,926.<ref>{{cite web|title=Huejuquilla El Alto|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14042a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 13, 2009|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061129060536/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14042a.htm|archive-date=November 29, 2006}}</ref>
