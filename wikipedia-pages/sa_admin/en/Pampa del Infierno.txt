'''Pampa del Infierno''' is a town in [[Chaco Province]], Argentina. It is the head town of the [[Almirante Brown Department]]. The town was founded on December 7, 1927.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pampa del Infierno, una apuesta al presente y futuro empresarial |url=https://www.diarionorte.com/293156-pampa-del-infierno-una-apuesta-al-presente-y-futuro-empresarial |last= |first= |access-date=2025-03-30 |website=Diario Norte |date= |language=es}}</ref>

In May, Pampa del Infierno hosts the annual goat festival ("Fiesta Provincial del Chivo").<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pampa del Infierno se prepara para el Festival Provincial del Chivo 2024 |url=https://chaco.gob.ar/noticia-pampa-del-infierno-se-prepara-para-el-festival-provincial-del-chivo-2024-09-17-19-55 |last= |first= |access-date=2025-03-30 |website=Gobierno de Chaco |date=2024-09-17 |language=es}}</ref>

[[MSU Argentina]]'s solar park, the largest in Chaco Province, was built along National Route No. 16, in Pampa del Infierno. It covers an area of 320 hectares, features 223,000 solar panels, and, since 2024, provides energy to around 300,000 homes.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Secheep y MSU conectaron el parque solar de Pampa del Infierno a la red eléctrica nacional |url=https://chaco.gob.ar/noticia-secheep-y-msu-conectaron-el-parque-solar-de-pampa-del-infierno-a-la-red-electrica-nacional-2024-08-19-13-21 |last= |first= |access-date=2025-03-30 |website=Gobierno de Chaco |date=2024-08-19 |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=El parque solar Pampa del Infierno obtuvo la habilitación comercial para operar |url=https://www.diarionorte.com/274980-el-parque-solar-pampa-del-infierno-obtuvo-la-habilitacion-comercial-para-operar%C2%A0 |last= |first= |access-date=2025-03-30 |website=Diario Norte |date= |language=es}}</ref>
