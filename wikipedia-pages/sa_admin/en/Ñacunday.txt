'''Ñacunday''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] and [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] in the [[Alto Paraná]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It was officially founded on 7 December 1973. The [[Ñacunday National Park]] is located in the district, which hosts the {{cvt|43|m}} tall Salto Ñacunday waterfall.

==History==
The municipality was formerly known as Puerto Paranambú and was officially created on 7 December 1973 by Law No. 426.
<ref name="ADN">{{cite web|title=Ñacunday "esconde" una de las maravillas naturales del Paraguay|work=ADN digital|url=http://www.adndigital.com.py/nacunday-esconde-una-las-maravillas-naturales-del-paraguay/|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20181216184542/http://www.adndigital.com.py/nacunday-esconde-una-las-maravillas-naturales-del-paraguay/|archive-date=16 December 2018|access-date=1 June 2021|trans-title=Ñacunday "hides" one of the natural wonders of Paraguay}}</ref> The district was created by Law No. 1097 of 20 December 1984, which formally established its boundaries and incorporated it as a third-category municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://paraguay.justia.com/nacionales/leyes/ley-1097-dec-20-1984/gdoc/|title=Ley 1097|date=20 December 1984|trans-title=Law 1097|work=Justia Paraguay|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Nacunday is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]].<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1008__%C3%B1acunday/|title=Nacunday |work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|65|km}} south of the city of [[Ciudad del Este]]. It is known as the "Waterfall Capital" due to the presence of several waterfalls in the region, the prominent of which is the Salto Ñacunday, which is {{cvt|43|m}} high and {{cvt|70|m}} wide. Most of the waterfalls are formed by the Ñacunday and Yacu’i Guasu rivers, both of which are tributaries of the [[Paraná River]]. The [[Ñacunday National Park]], located in the district, was established in 1973.<ref name="UN">{{cite web|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/nacunday-la-joya-del-alto-parana-pone-sus-fichas-en-el-turismo-sostenible|title=Ñacunday, la joya del Alto Paraná, pone sus fichas en el turismo sostenible|lang=es|access-date=1 April 2026|trans-title=Ñacunday, the jewel of Alto Paraná, puts its chips in sustainable tourism}}</ref>

==Demographics==
As per the 2022 census, Nacunday had a population of 6,468 inhabitants of which 3,394 were males and 3,074 were females. About 85.4% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (14.6%) lived in urban areas. About 32.3% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.5% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

==Economy==
The economy of the district is mainly dependent on [[agriculture]]. Major agricultural produce include [[soybean]], [[wheat]], [[maize]] and [[fruits]] including [[orange (fruit)|orange]], [[grapefruit]], and [[peach]]. There are also plantations cultivating [[peppermint]], [[cassava]], and [[potato]]. Cattle rearing and the associated dairy industry is also present in the region.<ref name="ADN"/> The district also attracts tourists due to the presence of several waterfalls including the Salto Nacunday.<ref name="UN"/>
