'''Aculco''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in the [[Atlacomulco Region]] of the [[State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The name comes from [[Nahuatl]].<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15003a.htm |language=es |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de Mexico Estado de Mexico Aculco |access-date=2008-11-27 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070527201737/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15003a.htm |archive-date=May 27, 2007}}</ref>{{Explain|date=December 2021}} The municipal seat is the town of [[Aculco de Espinoza]], although both the town and municipality are commonly referred to as simply "San Jerónimo Aculco".

The municipality is 110 kilometers away from Mexico City, and is known for its artisans and cheeses, according to ''[[El Heraldo de México|El Heraldo]]''.<ref name="ref2"/> Aculco currently has a population of over 44,823 inhabitants.<ref name="Aculco Municipality"/><ref name="Enciclopedia"/> The mayor or municipal president of Aculco is Jorge Alfredo Osnornio.<ref name="ref2">{{cite web |url=http://www.heraldo.mx/presentan-cartel-de-corrida-mixta-para-la-feria-de-aculco-estado-de-mexico/ |title=Presentan cartel de Corrida Mixta para la Feria de Aculco, Estado de México {{!}} El Heraldo de Aguascalientes |website=www.heraldo.mx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190913230041/http://www.heraldo.mx/presentan-cartel-de-corrida-mixta-para-la-feria-de-aculco-estado-de-mexico/ |archive-date=2019-09-13}}</ref>

==History==
Aculco was founded approximately in 1110 [[AD]] by the [[Otomi people|Otomi]]es, despite the fact that its name comes from [[Nahuatl]]. After being a village inhabited by Otomies for long time, it became a region dominated by the [[Mexica]]s, who lived there many years before the founding of [[Tenochtitlan]].<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

With the arrival of the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] in 1540, construction on the church and the convent of San Jeronimo began. In November 1810, [[Miguel Hidalgo]] and his contingent, who began the [[Mexican War of Independence]], arrived in the region. Aculco was also where insurgents, led by Don Miguel Hidalgo, lost a battle against the troops of Felix Ma. Calleja.<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

During the nineteenth century, the town hall and the first primary school in the region were constructed. Public baths and a municipal pool were also built.{{Citation needed|date=December 2021}}

On February 19, 1825, it became a municipality. In 1914 and 1915, Aculco was the scene of clashes led by the revolutionaries’ [[carrancistas]], [[villistas]] and [[Liberation Army of the South|Zapatistas]].<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

==Geography==
[[File:Cascada - panoramio (1).jpg|thumb|left|Concepción waterfall.]]

The municipality of Aculco has a geographical extent of {{convert|484.7|km2}}. That represents 2.18% of [[Mexico State]], of which it is a part of.<ref name="Enciclopedia"/> Aculco borders [[Polotitlán]] in the north, [[Querétaro]] in the south, and [[Acambay]] and [[Timilpan]] in the south.<ref name="Aculco">{{cite web|url=http://www.boonic.com/enciclopedia/62411.php|title=Aculco|access-date=2008-01-30 |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070907005643/http://www.boonic.com/enciclopedia/62411.php |archive-date = 2007-09-07}}</ref> It is approximately 100 kilometers away from or over 1 hour from Toluca, [[Toluca]].

===Climate===
Aculco has a relatively cool, humid climate, with rain in the summer. The annual average temperature is of {{convert|13.2|°C}}, with the lowest temperature occurring in the winter. The rain season starts at the end of March or beginning of April and lasts until October or November.<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

=== Flora and fauna ===
[[File:Papamoscas Negro, Black Phoebe, Sayornis nigricans (12889281965).jpg|thumbnail|180px|left|[[Sayornis nigricans|Black phoebe]], a native bird.]]

There is a diversity in plants and animals of [[temperate climate]] and [[semi-arid climate]] (Mezquital Valley).

Native animals include: [[cacomistle]], [[skunk]], [[gopher]], [[Virginia opossum]], [[rabbit]], [[Mexican gray squirrel]], [[Domesticated turkey|turkey]], [[Violetear|colibri]], [[turkey vulture]], [[northern mockingbird]], [[rattlesnake]], [[Pituophis|pine snake]], [[Sayornis nigricans|black phoebe]], [[rufous-crowned sparrow]], [[great horned owl]], [[axolotl]], [[frog]], [[toad]], [[red ant]], [[bee]], and others.

==Economy==
The main economic activity of Aculco is the [[ranching]] of animals like pigs and sheep. The municipality's principal products include [[milk]], [[fur]], [[wool]] and [[Egg (food)|egg]]. Aculco's inner commerce principally occurs at the [[Traditional fixed markets in Mexico|municipal market]], while its outer commerce is with [[Toluca]], [[Mexico City]] and [[Querétaro]].

Another major economic activity of Aculco is the exploitation of natural resources like sand, with [[quarries]] present in the region.<ref>{{cite book |last=Gaspar Sampayo |first=Domingo |title=Monografía Municipal - Aculco |year=1986 |publisher=Gobierno del Estado de Mexico |location=Mexico |isbn=968-841-079-9 }}</ref> The municipality is also home to a metal structure manufacturing company.<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

Aculco is famous for the dairy products it produces. In 2019, Aculco has about 60 artisan workshops for cheese and dairy production.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.altoromexico.com/index.php?acc=noticiadprint&id=35318|title = El Payo y Armillita actuarán en Aculco &#124; al Toro México &#124; Medio de comunicación taurino: Toros, Toreros, noticias y actualidad taurinas}}</ref>

===Tourism===
Aculco contains a number of notable geographic features. Two cascades are located in the area: "Tixhiñu" and "la Concepción". Rivers including the Río Ñadó and Río Prieto also flow through the municipality.

Aculco is home to numerous historic sites, including the San Jerónimo and the "Garrido Varela" [[bullring]],<ref name="Aculco"/> as well as the former residence of [[Miguel Hidalgo]] y Costilla. Two busts are located in Aculco in the memory of [[Benito Juarez]] and Miguel Hidalgo.<ref name="Enciclopedia"/>

==Demographics==
According to [[INEGI]], in 2005, Aculco has a population of over 40,000. The ethnic composition is mainly Otomi.<ref name="Enciclopedia">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15003a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |access-date=2008-01-26 |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070930023626/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15003a.htm |archive-date = 2007-09-30}}</ref>

Over 16,000 people in the region are illiterate.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sitesmexico.com/directorio/m/municipio-aculco-mexico.htm |title=Sitios de México |access-date=2008-01-31 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091205164749/http://www.sitesmexico.com/directorio/m/municipio-aculco-mexico.htm |archive-date=2009-12-05 }}</ref>

==Culture==
The foundation of the Aculco municipality is celebrated on February 19.<ref name="ref"/> In March and April, the municipality holds a scenic performance of Semana Mayor that starts on [[Holy Thursday]], in honor of Señor Nenthé.<ref name="ref"/> There is a festival to the Saint Patron [[Saint Jerome]] on September 30, including regional fairs, dances, and food.<ref name="ref">{{Cite web|url=https://www.contrareplica.mx/nota-Pueblo-Magico-Aculco-Estado-de-Mexico20191252|title = Pueblo Mágico Aculco, Estado de México}}</ref>
