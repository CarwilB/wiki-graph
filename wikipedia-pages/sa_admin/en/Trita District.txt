'''Trita''' is a district of [[Luya Province]] in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]]<ref>{{Cite web|title=Amazonas (Region, Peru) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|url=http://www.citypopulation.de/en/peru/admin/01__amazonas/|access-date=2021-05-25|website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> in [[Peru]]. Trita offers several attractive places for tourists such as the ruins of the [[Chachapoya culture]].

Among the traditional dishes of the district are [[purtumute]] and other regional foods.

The district is bordered to the north by the [[Lámud District]] and to the south by the [[Luya District]].
