'''Río Lagartos Municipality''' ([[Spanish language]]: "river of alligators") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 249.09&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|215|km}} northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], it was part of the chieftainship of Ecab.<ref name="municipios"/> After colonization, it became the Spanish colony's northeastern port. Before 1580, Antonio Rodríguez convinced the Yucatecan Governor Guillén de las Casas to appoint him as guard of the port. Rodríguez negotiated with the chieftains Chuyubchuen, Kikil, Panabá and Sucopó to provide him with indigenous workers to mine salt, fish or make ashes for soap. He secured almost a monopoly on the native workers requiring any Spaniard wanting to open a new enterprise to negotiate with him to rent workers.<ref name="Rugeley (2014)">{{cite book|last1=Rugeley|first1=Sergio Quezada ; translated by Terry|title=Maya lords and lordship : the formation of colonial society in Yucatán, 1350-1600|date=2014|publisher=University of Oklahoma Press|location=Norman|isbn=978-0-806-14579-2|pages=67–69|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=P2W9AgAAQBAJ&pg=PA69|access-date=7 August 2015}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Valladolid Municipality, Yucatan|Valladolid Municipality]]. In 1905, it was assigned to the [[Tizimín Municipality]] and in 1988 it was confirmed as head of its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|access-date=7 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=es|date=1996|pages=110, 116, 124|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archive-date=23 December 2015}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of health, education, public security and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Río Lagartos | url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=7 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es}}</ref>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Río Lagartos]], Yucatán. The municipality has 36 populated places besides the seat including Las Coloradas, El Edén, Paraíso, San Pablo, Santa Cruz, Santa Pilar Trejo, Santa Rita, Serrano, Tacxahan, and Zabich.
