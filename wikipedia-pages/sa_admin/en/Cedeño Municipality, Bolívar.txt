The '''Cedeño Municipality''' is one of the 11 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]]. According to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 67,000.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Caicara del Orinoco]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Cedeño Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Bolivar.zip {{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Cedeño Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 88,296 (up from 69,664 in 2000).  This amounts to 5.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1.92|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Cedeño Municipality is Luis Guillermo Troconiz Marquez, elected on October 31, 2004, with 25% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio CEDEÑO |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D6%26mun%3D2%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303174537/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D6%26mun%3D2%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |archive-date=2016-03-03 |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=2025-07-19 }}</ref>  He replaced Ismael Ortuño shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into five (six if you count the Capital Cedeño section) [[parish]]es; [[Altagracia (Cedeño)|Altagracia]], Ascensión Farreras, Guaniamo, La Urbana, and Pijiguaos.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
