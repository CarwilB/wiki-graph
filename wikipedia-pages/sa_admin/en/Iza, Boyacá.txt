'''Iza''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]]. Iza is located near the [[Tota Lake]] and part of the [[Sugamuxi Province]], a subregion of Boyacá. Iza is located in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|13|km|mi}} from [[Sogamoso]] and {{convert|90|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. The municipality borders [[Firavitoba]] and Sogamoso in the north, Sogamoso and [[Cuítiva, Boyacá|Cuítiva]] in the east, in the west [[Pesca]] and Firavitoba, and in the south Cuítiva.

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], the area of Iza was inhabited first by indigenous groups during the [[Herrera Period]] and later by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in the [[Muisca Confederation]]. Iza was ruled by the ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]], modern neighbouring Sogamoso. According to [[Muisca mythology]], the messenger god [[Bochica]] is said to have lived outside Iza in a cave.

In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca ''za'' means "night", and ''Iza'' means "place of healing".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Iza] - accessed 06-05-2016</ref>

Modern Iza was founded on January 2, 1554. The town is nicknamed ''Nido Verde de Boyacá''; "Green Nest of Boyacá".

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Iza are [[mining]] ([[coal]] and [[pozzolan]]) and [[agriculture]].
