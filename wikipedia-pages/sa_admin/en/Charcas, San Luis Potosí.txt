'''Charcas''' is a town and [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128021947/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |archivedate=January 28, 2007 }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of 4.911 km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 14,117 people.<ref>{{cite web |title=Charcas |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/sanluispotosi/charcas/240150001__charcas/ |website=City Population |access-date=13 October 2022}}</ref> Charcas is just south of the [[Tropic of Cancer]]. Due to its elevation of {{convert|2219|m}}, the climate is mild.

The town was founded in 1578 by Spanish conquistadors.{{cn|date=May 2023}}
