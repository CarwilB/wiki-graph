'''Motavita''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Central Boyacá Province]]. It covers approximately {{convert|62|sqkm|sqmi|0}}.<ref name=websiteMotavita>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.motavita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Motavita] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310085041/http://www.motavita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2014-03-10}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Motavita comes from the [[Chibcha language]] and means "washing place to prepare for growing crops", indicating a ceremonial use.<ref>{{Cite book|author=López, Javier Ocampo |year=2001 |title=El imaginario en Boyacá: la identidad del pueblo boyacense y su proyección en la simbología regional (Volume 2: El imaginario colectivo en los pensadores boyacenses) |location=Bogotá, Colombia |publisher=Universidad Distrital "Francisco José de Caldas" |page=84 |isbn=978-958-9160-90-9}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Motavita is bordered to the north by [[Arcabuco]] and [[Cómbita]], to the east and south by [[Tunja]], and to the west by [[Sora, Boyacá|Sora]] and [[Chíquiza]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Motavita: Generalidades: 1.2  Limites (Motavita: Overview: 1.2 Borders)|language=Spanish|publisher=Government of Boyacá|page=1|url=http://www.boyaca.gov.co/recursos_user/documentos/editores/71/1%20generalidades%2020.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
In the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], Motavita was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Motavita was an important settlement in the [[coca]] [[Muisca economy#Trade|trade with surrounding indigenous groups]], such as the [[Muzo people]] who populated the western parts of Boyacá. Motavita was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' of nearby [[Tunja|Hunza]]. [[Conquistador]] [[Hernán Pérez de Quesada]], brother of [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]], reached Motavita in 1540.<ref name=websiteMotavita/> In 1776 the first evangelisation of the Catholic Church was established by [[friar]] Juan Pérez.<ref name=websiteMotavita/> Modern Motavita was founded on 23 December 1816 and its first mayor was Marino Monrroy.<ref name=websiteMotavita/>

== Divisions ==
Motavita was divided into five [[cadastral]] [[vereda]] (townships): Centro, Carbonera, Ristá, Salvial and Sote Panelas; however, Sote Panelas had four more veredas split off from it, namely: Frutillal, Versalles, Quebrada Honda and Barro Hondo, thus making nine vereda.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Motavita: Generalidades: 1.3  División Política (Veredas)|language=Spanish|publisher=Government of Boyacá|page=4|url=http://www.boyaca.gov.co/recursos_user/documentos/editores/71/1%20generalidades%2020.pdf}}</ref>
