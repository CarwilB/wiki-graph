The '''Crespo Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Lara (state)|Lara]] and, as of the 2011 census, the municipality has a population of 49,958.<ref name="census"> {{cite report |date=December 2014 |title=XIV Censo Nacional de Población y Vivienda: Resultados por Entidad Federal y Municipio del Estado Lara |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/lara.pdf |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, República Bolivariana de Venezuela |language=es}} </ref>{{rp|10}} The town of [[Duaca]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Crespo Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Lara.zip {{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Crespo Municipality, according to a 2011 population census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 49,958 (up from 42,432 in 2001). This amounts to 2.8% of the state's population. <ref name="census" /> The municipality's population density is {{convert|56.93|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Crespo Municipality is Miguel Valecillos Paul, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 83% of the vote.<ref> {{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis=0&edo=11&mun=1&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |title=Alcalde de Municipio: Crespo}} </ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones 30 de Julio de 2000 |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Fréitez and José María Blanco.<ref name="census" />{{rp|44}}
