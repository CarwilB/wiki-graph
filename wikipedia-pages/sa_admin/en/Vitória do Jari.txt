'''Vitória do Jari''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|viˈtɔɾi.ɐ du ʒaˈɾi}}; 'Jari's Victory') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southernmost tip of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 16,254<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/vitoria-do-jari/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|2,509|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Vitória do Jari has a population density of 5.9 inhabitants per square kilometer. The town is located on the [[Jari River]] on the other side of [[Munguba]],<ref name="digital"/> and was originally called '''Beiradinho'''.

==History==
[[File:Jari3.JPG|thumb|left|Celulose plant in Munguba and Vitória do Jari on the [[Jari River]] (1995)]]
The town started as a [[shanty town]] for the [[Jari project]]. It was originally called Beiradinho.<ref name="jus">{{cite web|url=https://www.tjap.jus.br/portal/presidencia/comarcas/16-comarca-de-vitoria-do-jari.html|title=Informações da Comarca de Vitória do Jarí|website=Tribunal de Justiça do Estado do Amapá|access-date=3 April 2021|language=pt|archive-date=22 June 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210622180401/https://www.tjap.jus.br/portal/presidencia/comarcas/16-comarca-de-vitoria-do-jari.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> People in the informal economy who worked in [[Munguba]] could not afford housing in the Munguba or [[Monte Dourado]].<ref>{{cite thesis|url=https://ufdcimages.uflib.ufl.edu/UF/E0/00/33/60/00001/silva_n.pdf |title=Like a Murure: Social Change in a Terra-Firme Community on the Amazon Estuary|year=2008|author=Neila Soares da Silva|page=57|publisher=University of Florida}}</ref> In 1994, the town was renamed Vitória do Jari and became an independent municipality.<ref name="jus"/>

==Geography==

The municipality contains 17% of the {{convert|501771|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Cajari Extractive Reserve]], created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Rio Cajari – ISA}}|language=pt

==Economy==
The economy is based on agricultural with an emphasis on corn, bananas, and watermelons, and cattle and buffalo ranches.<ref name="amapa">{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/vitoria-do-jari |title=Vitória do Jari|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> CADAM, a [[kaolinite|kaolin]] mining company is a major employer in the region.<ref name="digital">{{cite web|url=http://www.amapadigital.net/vitoria_jari.php|title=Vitória do Jari|website=Amapá Digital|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

==Jarilândia==
{{anchor|Jarilandia}}
Jarilândia is the location where ''[[Gmelina arborea]]'' was planted on a large scale for the [[Pulp (paper)|pulp]] industry as part of the [[Jari project]]. In 1969, planting began, but soon the trees failed to grow.<ref>{{cite journal|url=https://www.jstor.org/stable/42607638 |title=GMELINA – LARGE SCALE PLANTING, JARILANDIA, AMAZON BASIN|author=A. GREAVES |year=1979|journal=The Commonwealth Forestry Review|volume=4 |issue=178 |pages=267–269 |jstor=42607638 |url-access=subscription}}</ref> The project was cancelled in 1982.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1992/08/29/us/daniel-ludwig-billionaire-businessman-dies-at-95.html |title=Daniel Ludwig, Billionaire Businessman, Dies at 95|work=The New York Times |date=29 August 1992 |access-date=1 April 2021 |last1=Pace |first1=Eric }}</ref>
