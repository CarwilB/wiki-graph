''' Cuatro Ciénegas ''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Cuatrociénegas de Carranza]]. The municipality covers an area of 7860.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 12,715.<ref>{{cite web|title=Cuatrociénegas|url=http://www.elocal.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05007a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=November 8, 2008}}{{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

Cuatrociénegas is famous for its [[Endemism|endemic]] [[species]] and has been awarded the "'''''Pueblo Mágico'''''" (Magic Town) category<ref>{{Cite web|title = Pueblos Mágicos|url = http://www.sectur.gob.mx/pueblos-magicos/|website = www.sectur.gob.mx|access-date = 2016-02-18|first = Secretaría de|last = Turismo}}</ref> by the tourism department.
