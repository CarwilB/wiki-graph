'''Guadalupe''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The capital lies at [[Guadalupe, Chihuahua|Guadalupe]]. The municipality covers an area of 6,200.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 6,458,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guadalupe|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=028|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=2 May 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180502210816/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=028|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Other than the town of Guadalupe, the municipality had 195 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
