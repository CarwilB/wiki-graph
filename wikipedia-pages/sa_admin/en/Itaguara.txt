'''Itaguara''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population in 2020 was 13,435.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/itaguara/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

== Toponym ==
''Itaguara'' is a coinage from [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupi–Guarani]], meaning "wolf rock", by combining  ''itá'' (rock) and ''guará'' (Wolf).
