'''Cerro Largo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northwestern part of the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. It occupies an area of 174.64&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (67.43&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi) at 28º09'0" south 54º45'0" west,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.travelmath.com/city/Cerro+Largo,+Brazil |title=The latitude and longitude of Cerro Largo, Brazil is: |publisher=travelmath |access-date=9 June 2011}}</ref> at an elevation of 211&nbsp;m (692&nbsp;ft) above sea level. Its population {{As of|2020|lc=on}} was 14,189 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/cerro-largo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Regional language==
Cerro Largo was settled soon after 1900 mostly by [[German-Brazilian|German Brazilians]] from the old German settlements on the eastern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], the ''Altkolonie'' (in [[German language|German]]). [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] is the official language but [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]] is still spoken by many citizens in the area. It is the birthplace of São Paulo's current archbishop, Cardinal [[Odilo Scherer]]. In 2012 the state chamber of deputies voted unanimously in favor of recognizing this Germanic dialect an official historical culture good to be preserved.
