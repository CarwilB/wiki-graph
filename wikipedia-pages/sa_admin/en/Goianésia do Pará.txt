'''Goianésia do Pará''' is a [[municipality]], and [[municipal seat]] in the state of [[Pará]], Brazil. In 2020 the population was 41,081. The municipal area is 7,024&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city of Goianésia do Pará is approximately 103 m above [[sea level]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=150309|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Pará {{!}} Goianésia do Pará|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-19}}</ref>
