'''Buritis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 40,356 (2020) and its area is 3,266&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/5EO]</ref> The city consists largely of unpaved, dirt packed roads.{{citation needed|date=December 2024}} There is one large grocery store of the Goncalves chain.{{citation needed|date=December 2024}} Buritis has one stoplight. Surrounding and outlying farmers rely on Buritis to supply goods and replenish supplies.{{citation needed|date=December 2024}} The city has a democratic government with a mayor and eight sitting Council members for each of the eight sectors. The climate is tropical/equatorial with few variations in temperature and climate from month to month.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://en.allmetsat.com/climate/brazil.php?code=82825|title = Climate : Porto Velho, Rondônia Brazil}}</ref>
