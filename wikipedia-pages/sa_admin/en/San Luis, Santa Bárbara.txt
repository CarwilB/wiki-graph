'''San Luis''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|381.3|km2}} and had a population of 24,606 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. The economy is dependent primarily dependent on [[agriculture]] especially [[coffee]], and [[livestock]] rearing.

== History ==
In the 1887 census, San Luis was listed as part of [[San Jose de Colinas]], and later it was listed under the District of Hills.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de San Luis, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/San-Luis.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San Luis is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-san-luis-santa-barbara/|title=Municipality of San Luis|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[San Marcos, Santa Bárbara|San Marcos]] and [[Macuelizo]] to the north, [[Atima]] and [[Nuevo Celilac]] to the south, San José de Colinas and [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] to the east, and Macuelizo, [[Protección]] and [[Naranjito, Honduras|Naranjito]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|381.3|km2}} and has an average elevation of {{cvt|1036|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/>

== Demographics ==
At the time of the 2013 census, San Luis had a total population of 24,606 inhabitants, of which 12,885 (52.4%) were male and 11,721 (47.6%) were female. Of the total population, 5,857 (23.8%) were classified as urban and 18,749 (76.2%) lived in the rural areas.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} Of the population, 0.34% were classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1620__san_luis/|title=San Luis|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

By broad age group, 37.4% of the population were aged 0–14, 56.8% were aged 15–64, and 5.7% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 20.1 years and the mean age was 25.6 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (15,394 persons), the illiteracy rate was recorded as 29.2%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} The municipality had 5,732 occupied private dwellings, with an average household size of 4.2 persons.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=23,27}}

== Economy ==
The economy is dependent primarily dependent on [[agriculture]] especially [[coffee]], and [[livestock]] rearing.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web |url=http://care.ca/field/SanLuis/sanluis_e.shtm |title=The Story of San Luis Coffee in Honduras |access-date=1 June 2007 |work=Care |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071211172231/http://care.ca/field/SanLuis/sanluis_e.shtm |archive-date=11 December 2007}}</ref> Other agricultural produce from the region include beans, [[maize]], and fruits such as [[banana]]s.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>
