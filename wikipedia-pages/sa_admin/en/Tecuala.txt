'''Tecuala''' is both a municipality and a town in the Mexican state of [[Nayarit]], on the Pacific coast. The population of the municipality was 42,237 in a total area of {{Cvt|1,137|km2}} (2000) while the population of the town and municipal seat was 14,584 (2000). One of the longest beaches in the world, Playa Novillero, is located here.<ref>{{cite web|author=Sujaysen|title=The 10 Longest Beaches in The World|url=http://newsflavor.com/opinions/the-10-longest-beaches-in-the-world/|publisher=[[NewsFlavor]]|access-date=6 August 2012|date=25 December 2010|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120626013023/http://newsflavor.com/opinions/the-10-longest-beaches-in-the-world/|archivedate=26 June 2012}}</ref>

"Tecuala" is the Castilian form of the Tecuallan word, which means ''"place of many wild animals"''.<ref name="archive.vn_2013-02-21">{{Citation | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM18nayarit/municipios/18016a.html| archive-url=https://archive.today/20130221080929/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM18nayarit/municipios/18016a.html| url-status=dead| archive-date=February 21, 2013| title=Nayarit-Tecuala.| newspaper=archive.vn| date= February 21, 2013| accessdate=May 21, 2020}}</ref>
== Geography ==
The municipality of Tecuala is located in the northern part of the state of [[Nayarit]], between parallels 22° 14', and 22° 34' north latitude and meridians 105° 14' and 105° 45' west longitude. It is bounded in the north by the state of [[Sinaloa]] and the municipality of [[Acaponeta]]; in the south by the municipalities of [[Santiago Ixcuintla]] and [[Rosamorada]], in the east by the municipality of [[Acaponeta]] and in the west by the [[Pacific Ocean]].

Most of the land is flat and tending to mangrove swamps in the west, forming part of the national protected zone called Marismas Nacionales.<ref name=NatGeo>{{NatGeo ecoregion|id=nt1420|name=Marismas Nacionales-San Blas mangroves}}</ref> The most important river is the [[Acaponeta River|Acaponeta]], which flows into the [[Agua Brava lagoon]].<ref name=NatGeo/>

=== Climate ===
The climate is hot, tropical, subhumid, with the rainy season from July to September, and the hottest months from June to August. The average annual rainfall is 1,200&nbsp;mm. and the average annual temperature is 22&nbsp;°C, varying between 26&nbsp;°C and 18&nbsp;°C.

== Economy ==
The economy is based on agriculture with the main crops being sorghum, beans, tobacco, corn, chili peppers, watermelon and [[Pachyrhizus erosus|jícama]]. There is also widespread cattle raising. Shrimp farming is carried out in the many lagoons.

It is said, that former governor of [[Sinaloa]] state; [[Antonio Toledo Corro]] and Monterrey-based developers Grupo Novisa who apparently own the peninsula have a very aggressive plan with the idea to start a new era for the new Riviera which will employ hundreds of people and increase the living standards of the people who live within the area.{{Citation needed|date=April 2018}}

== Tourism ==
The popular Novillero beach is listed in ''[[Guinness World Records]]'' for being one of the longest beaches in the world—82 kilometers. The Acaponeta River flows into the ocean here and nearby is the Agua Brava lagoon, a place of great fishing and shrimp production. In San Felipe Aztatán, one can visit a monument to the origins of the [[Mexican people]].

Today, Tecuala is the new target for real estate developers focused on tourist matters. Playa Novillero, especially the "Peninsula", which has a total area of approximately {{convert|3460|acre|ha|adj=on}} with ocean and river front, has very attractive plans within the next months to initiate a new concept of a tourist project.
