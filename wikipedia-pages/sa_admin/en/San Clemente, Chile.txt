'''San Clemente''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] administered by the [[municipality]] of San Clemente, located in the [[Talca Province]] of [[Chile]]'s [[Maule Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Clemente spans an area of {{convert|4503.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 37,261 inhabitants (18,988 men and 18,273 women). Of these, 13,398 (36%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 23,863 (64%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 2.3% (847 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Clemente is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008–2012 alcalde is Oscar Galvez Rebolledo ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Clemente is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Empedrado, Chile|Empedrado]], [[Pencahue]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[Pelarco]], [[Río Claro, Chile|Río Claro]] and [[San Rafael, Chile|San Rafael]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
