'''Tibirita''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Tibirita is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|125|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. It borders [[Villapinzón]] in the north, in the east [[La Capilla]] ([[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]), in the south [[Guateque]] (Boyacá) and in the west [[Manta, Cundinamarca|Manta]], [[Machetá]] and [[Chocontá]].<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tibirita-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tibirita] - accessed 07-05-2016</ref>

== History ==
The area of Tibiritá was populated by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] in the 1530s. The village was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]], present day capital of Boyacá Tunja. Modern Tibiritá was founded on July 9, 1553 by Miguel de Ibarra.<ref name=website/> The name Tibiritá is [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]].

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Tibiritá is [[dairy]] farming.<ref name=website/>
