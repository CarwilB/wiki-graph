'''Santa Cruz de Cocachacra District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Huarochirí Province]], located in the [[Department of Lima]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the Law No. 13261 on October 29, 1959, during the second presidency of [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].
