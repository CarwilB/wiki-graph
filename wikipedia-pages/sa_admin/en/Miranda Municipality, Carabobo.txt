'''Miranda''' is one of the 14 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Carabobo]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 29,092. <ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref>  The town of Miranda is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Miranda Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Carabobo.zip{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> 

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named [[Miranda Municipality (disambiguation)#Venezuela|Miranda Municipality]] for independence hero [[Francisco de Miranda]].

==Demographics==
The Miranda Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 27,609 (up from 23,869 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|171.48|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
