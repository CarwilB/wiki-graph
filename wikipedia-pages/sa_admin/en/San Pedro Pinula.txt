'''San Pedro Pinula''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jalapa Department|Jalapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|515.9|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 79,597 inhabitants.

==History==
The region was earlier occupied by [[Poqomam people]], and the name is derived from the [[Poqomam language]], with "pinole" referring to traditional flour and "ha" or "ja" means water or land, roughly translating to "water of pinole" or "land of pinole".<ref name="Hist"/> The name has the prefix "San Pedro" in honor of [[Saint Peter]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wisdomlib.org/names/san-pedro|title=San Pedro|work=Wisdom library|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The municipality was created in 1836 as a part of Jalapa circuit in district of [[Chiquimula]]. It was later incorporated into the [[Jalapa Department|department of Jalapa]] when the department was officially created in 1873.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=San Pedro Pinula|url=https://www.guatevalley.com/municipio/san-pedro-pinula-jalapa|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es|work=Guate Valley}}</ref>

==Geography==
San Pedro Pinula is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jalapa Department|Jalapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|515.9|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Perdor Pinula (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jalapa/2102__san_pedro_pinula/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1379.63|m}} above sea level, the district has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|21.64|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|15.03|cm}} and has 197.24 average rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jalapa/san-pedro-pinula|title=San Pedro Pinula|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
San Pedro Pinula had an estimated population of 79,597 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 39,540 males and 40,057 females. About 35.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.5% was over the age of 65 years. About 85.6% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (14.4%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (95.2%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 68.7%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (89.3%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (10.0%) forming a significant minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.2%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
