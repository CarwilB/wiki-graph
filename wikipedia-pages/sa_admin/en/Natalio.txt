'''Natalio''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is located in the South-East of the country, with the town center located just 10&nbsp;km from the [[Paraná River]] and the border with [[Argentina]]. The district lies 45&nbsp;km off Route 6 which connects departmental capitals [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]] and [[Ciudad del Este]]. The town is located in a fertile agricultural region. Local crops are soy, corn, wheat, [[Tung oil|tung]], [[sorghum]], and cotton. Natalio's slogan is "the Capital of Soy".
