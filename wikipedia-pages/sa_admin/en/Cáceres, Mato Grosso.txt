'''Cáceres''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Mato Grosso]]. It covers an area of 24,000 km<sup>2</sup> and as of 2022 had an estimated population of 89,681.

The town is sited on the [[Paraguay River]], and hosts a popular fishing festival each September.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.smh.com.au/travel/travel-news/thousands-of-piranhas-infest-beach-bite-swimmers-20111117-1nk6v.html|title=Thousands of piranhas infest beach, bite swimmers|date=17 November 2011|work=Sydney Morning Herald|publisher=[[Fairfax Media]]|access-date=4 May 2014}}</ref>
The municipality contains part of the [[Serra das Araras Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica da Serra das Araras|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=75|access-date=2016-04-19}}</ref>
It also contains part of the [[Taiamã Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Taiamã|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=62|access-date=2016-04-18}}</ref> 
The municipality contains the {{convert|114000|ha}} [[Guirá State Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Guirá – Via Rural}}|title=Parque Estadual Guirá|publisher=Via Rural|url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-guira/default.htm|access-date=2016-12-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161220080345/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-guira/default.htm|archive-date=2016-12-20|url-status=usurped}}</ref>

Cáceres was the starting point of the [[Roosevelt–Rondon Scientific Expedition]].<ref>{{citation|language=en|title=Roosevel-Rondon Scientifi Expedition Website|url=http://www.theodore-roosevelt.com/trbrazil.html|access-date=2017-03-27}}</ref>

Cáceres is the administrative headquarters of the [[Mato Grosso State University]] and has 13 graduation courses and 3 post graduation courses.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|title=UNEMAT Website|url=http://portal.unemat.br/?pg=campus&idc=2|access-date=2017-03-27}}</ref>
