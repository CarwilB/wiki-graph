'''Canelinha''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the [[South Region, Brazil|South region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf | title = Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009| publisher = Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) | date = August 14, 2009 | access-date=December 17, 2009 | format = [[PDF]]|language=pt }}</ref> Canelinha is a small town, located {{convert|15.2|km}} west of [[Tijucas]] and {{convert|9.9|km}} by road east of [[São João Batista, Santa Catarina|São João Batista]].<ref>{{Google maps | url =https://maps.google.com/maps?ll=-27.283333,-48.783333&q=loc:-27.283333,-48.783333&hl=en&t=h&z=12  | accessdate =16 May 2013}}</ref> It has an altitude of 17 metres and its population according to the 2020 estimate is 12,398 inhabitants. It has land area of {{cvt|151.409|km2}}. The municipality was established on 23 December 1962.<ref name="GomesBayer2000">{{cite book|last1=Gomes|first1=Luiz|last2=Bayer|first2=Marcos|title=Muitas histórias de Tijucas|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=qqfuAAAAMAAJ|access-date=16 May 2013|year=2000|publisher=Editora Insular|page=65}}</ref>

==Economy==
Canelinha is noted for its ceramics and its large number of potteries, an industry which is very important to the local economy.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.guiamais.com.br/busca/ceramica-canelinha-sc|title=Cerâmica" em Canelinha|publisher=Guiamais.com|access-date=16 May 2013}}</ref> It also produces clothing, knitwear, shoes, and agricultural products. The locality of Areão in the municipality also contains a motocross track, the [[Motódromo Arthur Jachovicz]], which is a venue for the [[Campeonatos Catarinense e Brasileiro de motocross]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.canelinha.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/205|title=Motódromo Arthur Jachovicz|publisher=Municipal Government of Canelinha|access-date=16 May 2013}}</ref>
