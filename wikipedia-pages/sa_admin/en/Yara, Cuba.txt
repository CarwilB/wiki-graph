'''Yara''' is a small town and municipality in the [[Granma Province]] of [[Cuba]], located halfway between the cities of [[Bayamo]] and [[Manzanillo, Cuba|Manzanillo]], in the [[Gulf of Guacanayabo]]. Yara means "place" in the [[Taíno language]].<ref name=taino>{{Cite web|url=http://www.taino-tribe.org/tslist-y.htm |title= Taíno Dictionary |access-date=2013-07-30|language=es}}</ref>

==History==
The Taíno Cacique (chief) [[Hatuey]] was burnt at the stake in Yara, on February 2, 1512, after he organized a guerrilla war against the Spaniards. Hatuey is known as "Cuba's First National Hero".<ref>Running Fox, 'The Story of Cacique Hatuey, Cuba's First National Hero', La Voz del Pueblo Taíno (The Voice of the Taíno People) (United Confederation of Taino People, U.S. Regional Chapter, January 1998)</ref> This action gave birth to one of Cuba's major myths; ''"La Luz de Yara"'', [[Light of Yara|The Light of Yara]].<ref name=LuzdeYara>The Light of Yara.{{Cite web |url=http://blog.guayaberascubanas.com/2012/11/normal-0-false-false-false-en-us-x-none.html |title=Legend of the Light of Yara |access-date=2013-07-30 |archive-date=2013-05-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130529001104/http://blog.guayaberascubanas.com/2012/11/normal-0-false-false-false-en-us-x-none.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

On October 10, 1868, the beginning of the [[Ten Years' War]] in Cuba occurred and is known as ''El Grito de Yara'' ([[Cry of Yara|The Cry of Yara]]) and was the beginning of the First Cuban War of Independence.

Yara was established as a municipality in 1912, when [[Manzanillo, Cuba|Manzanillo]] was split up.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Yara, Yara Arriba, [[Veguitas]], Buey de Gallego, Coco, Caboa, Cabagán, Calambrosio, Canabacoa, Cayo Redondo, José Martí, Los Cayos, Mateo Romás and Sofía.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/omanzanillo/index.htm#pyara|title=Yara|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-06|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Yara had a population of 53,818.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|576|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|93|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
