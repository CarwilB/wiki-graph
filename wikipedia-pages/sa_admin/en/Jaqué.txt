'''Jaqué''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]], located {{convert|38|km}} west of the Panama-[[Colombia]] border. The closest neighboring settlement is [[Puerto Piña]], that is about {{convert|8|km}} north.<ref>[https://www.google.com/maps/@7.5509764,-78.1564553,19982m/data=!3m1!1e3 Google Maps - Jaqué]</ref>

==Transportation==
Jaqué is within the [[Darién Gap]] of the [[Pan American Highway]], and no permanent roads connect it with the rest of Panama. The main means of transportations are by plane and by boat. The town is served by the [[Jaqué Airport]].

==Geography==
Jaqué is on the Pacific coast next to the mouth of the [[Jaqué River]] in Darién Province. The immediate area around Jaqué is mainly low lying with [[mangrove]] swamps and [[Tropical and subtropical moist broadleaf forests|tropical rain forest]]. Further inland are highlands with [[temperate broadleaf and mixed forests]].

===Climate===
Jaqué has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'').<ref name="climate"/> The mean temperature is {{convert|26.6|C|F|1}} with a record high of {{convert|37|C|F|1|disp=or}} and low of {{convert|15|C|F|disp=or}}. The hottest month is March with a mean of {{convert|27.1|C|F|1|disp=or}}, and the coolest December with an average of {{convert|26|C|1|disp=or}}<ref name="climate"/> The two seasons are the wet season and the dry season. The wet season starts in April until December with {{convert|2736|mm|in|2}} of rainfall on average.<ref name="climate"/> The dry starts in January lasting until March. The amount of precipitation drops drastically to less than {{convert|30|mm|in|2}} in February and March with an average dry season total of {{convert|119.9|mm|in|2}}.<ref name="climate"/> On average there are 114 days with rain a year.<ref name="climate"/> Humidity is usually high at or above 80 percent year round with an average of 85.8 percent.<ref name="climate"/> March during the dry season being the lowest at 81.6 percent, and September during the wet season being the highest at 88.2 percent.<ref name="climate"/>

==Demographics==
