'''Otuzco''' is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.businessempresarial.com.pe/la-libertad-supera-los-600-medios-de-comunicacion/|title=La Libertad supera los 600 medios de comunicación|work=Business Empresarial|date=13 October 2023|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital of this province is the city of [[Otuzco]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

* [[Otuzco District|Otuzco]]
* [[Agallpampa District|Agallpampa]]
* [[Charat District|Charat]]
* [[Huaranchal District|Huaranchal]]
* [[La Cuesta District, Otuzco|La Cuesta]]
* [[Mache District|Mache]]
* [[Paranday District|Paranday]]
* [[Salpo District|Salpo]]
* [[Sinsicap District|Sinsicap]]
* [[Usquil District|Usquil]]
