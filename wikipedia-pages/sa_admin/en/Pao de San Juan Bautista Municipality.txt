The '''Pao de San Juan Bautista Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 16,810.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[El Pao, Cojedes|El Pao]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Pao de San Juan Bautista Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Pao de San Juan Bautista Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 15,730 (up from 13,840 in 2000).  This amounts to 5.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|3.09|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pao de San Juan Bautista Municipality is Juan de la Cruz Aparicio Rojas, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 68% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Pao de San Juan Bautista | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163429/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17 | access-date=2008-03-14 | url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-14 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]; El Pao.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
