''' San Juan del Río ''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.maplandia.com/mexico/durango/san-juan-del-rio/san-juan-del-rio/ |title=San Juan del Río Map on Maplandia}}</ref> The municipal seat is at [[San Juan del Río, Durango|San Juan del Rio]]. The municipality covers an area of 1279&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a population of 11,855,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=San Juan del Río|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=028|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 10,364 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Juan del Río |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10028a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=19 December 2008}}</ref> 

The municipality has 109 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were San Juan del Río del Centauro del Norte (2,912), classified as urban, and Diez de Octubre (San Lucas de Ocampo) (1,500), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
