''' Etzatlán  ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 337.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 17,564. 

It is home to the world’s largest crochet canopy, as certified by The Guinness Book of World Records.
<ref>{{cite web|title=Etzatlán|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14036a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 13, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070331092330/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14036a.htm|archive-date=March 31, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2020, the Chilean writer [[José Baroja]] dedicated a story entitled Etzatlán to this.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Etzatlán, por José Baroja |url=http://letras.mysite.com/jbar250121.html |access-date=2022-04-22 |website=letras.mysite.com}}</ref>
