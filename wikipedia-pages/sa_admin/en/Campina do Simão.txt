'''Campina do Simão''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in the [[Southern Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. The municipality was first settled at the beginning on the 20th century, and is named for its first resident, José Simão, also known as Jeca Simão. Campina do Simão was originally part of the municipality of [[Guarapuava]]; it became a separate district of Guarapuava in 1964, and an independent municipality on January 1, 1997.<ref name="ibge"/>

Campina do Simão covers {{Convert|448.424|km2|mi2}}, has a population of 3,859, and population density of 9.09 resident per square kilometer.<ref name="ibge"/>
