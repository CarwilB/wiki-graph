'''Antônio Dias''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 9,275 people living in a total area of {{cvt|877.844|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/antonio-dias/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Vale do Rio Doce and to the microregion of [[Ipatinga]].
