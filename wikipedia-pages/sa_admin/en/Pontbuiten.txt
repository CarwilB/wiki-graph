'''Pontbuiten''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 23,211.<ref name="census"/>

Pontbuiten was founded in the mid 20th century by the Pont family, and was intended for agriculture.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-zuid|title=Paramaribo-Zuid|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Vakantie Arena}}</ref>
