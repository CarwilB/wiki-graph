'''General Belgrano''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 10,500 inhabitants in an area of  1,218&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Corzuela]], which is located around 1,250&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

The department is named in honour of [[Manuel Belgrano|General Manuel Belgrano]] (June 3, 1770 – June 20, 1820), an Argentine economist, Lawyer, Politician and military leader.
