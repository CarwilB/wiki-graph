'''Cabricán''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The head town of Cabricán is situated at an altitude of 2,525 [[metre|m]] above sea level.

Cabrican is the location of Roman Catholic radio station Radio Mam. The station primarily broadcasts in the [[Mam language]].
