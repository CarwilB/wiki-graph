'''San Esteban''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the northeast of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]], west of [[Dulce Nombre de Culmí]], east of [[Gualaco]] and north of [[Catacamas]].<ref>[https://www.google.com/maps/@15.2123034,-85.771298,6000m/data=!3m1!1e3 Google Maps - San Esteban]</ref> [[Costa Rica]]n author [[Oscar Núñez Oliva]] set his 2000 novel ''Los Gallos de San Esteban'' in the municipality.<ref name = "heredia">[http://heredia-costarica.zonalibre.org/archives/2009/09/oscar-nunez-olivas.html Óscar Núñez Olivas]</ref><ref name = "pendienteinter">[http://pendientedemigracion.ucm.es/info/especulo/numero29/onunez.html Una perspectiva sobre la realidad centroamericana en las novelas de Oscar Núñez Olivas:  Una conversación]</ref>

==History==
The municipality was named in honor of friar [[:es:Esteban Verdalet|Esteban Verdelete]], a missionary who collaborated in the evangelism of the people of the region.

It was founded in 1808 by friar [[José Antonio Liendo y Goicoechea]] from [[Guatemala]]. It was populated by indigenous people at that time.<ref>{{cite book | author=Fiallos, Carmen | title=Los Municipios de Honduras | publisher=Universidad Nacional Autónoma de Honduras |language=es |trans-title=The Municipalities of Honduras |location=Tegucigalpa, Honduras | year=1991 | edition=Editorial Universitaria}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality is bordered to the north by the municipalities of [[Trujillo, Honduras|Trujillo]] and [[Iriona]], South by the municipality of [[Catacamas]], East by the municipality of [[Dulce Nombre de Culmí]] and West by the municipality of [[Gualaco]]. It stretches from the top the Agalta valley and crossed by the Grande Agalta river.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Esteban municipality had a population of 25,572. Of these, 90.33% were [[Mestizo]], 6.11% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (5.84% [[Pech people|Pech]]), 2.79% [[White Latin Americans|White]],  0.36% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.41% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
