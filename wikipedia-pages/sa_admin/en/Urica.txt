'''Urica''' is a town in the north-east of Venezuela. It is the capital of [[Urica Parish]], in [[Pedro María Freites Municipality]], in [[Anzoátegui State]] in Venezuela. Its population according to the Census of 2011 is 4,517.<ref>{{cite web|title=Anzoátegui (Venezuela): Municipalities & Urban Localities - Population Statistics in Maps and Charts|url=http://www.citypopulation.de/php/venezuela-anzoategui.php|website=www.citypopulation.de|accessdate=15 August 2017}}</ref>

==Geography==
Urica is 45&nbsp;km southeast of the population center of [[Santa Inés, Venezuela|Santa Inés]]. It is situated on a plain bathed by the waters of the Rivers Urica and Amana, surrounded by the livestock farming areas within [[Anzoátegui]] and known for large-scale fruit production. Of geological interest is the Urica fault.<ref name="(França)Barcelona2005">René Manceda, [https://books.google.com/books?id=hu1Seu6toDwC&pg=PA471 "The western end of the Serrania del Interior, Venezuela: A review"], in: ''6th International Symposium on Andean Geodynamics'' (ISAG 2005, Barcelona), Extended Abstracts. pp.468–71. IRD Editions. {{ISBN|978-2-7099-1575-5}}.</ref>

==History==
Urica was founded by Spaniards in the mid-18th century. Several notable battles have taken place in the vicinity, such as those of 1814 during the [[Venezuelan War of Independence]], in which forces led by [[José Félix Ribas]] and [[José Francisco Bermúdez]] fought against the royalist commander [[José Tomás Boves]]. The battle of Curareque took place on 12 September of that year, and on 5 December the [[Battle of Urica]], in which Boves was killed:<ref name="Marley1998">{{cite book|author=David Marley|title=Wars of the Americas: A Chronology of Armed Conflict in the New World, 1492 to the Present|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=rdvp3cGJUZoC&pg=PA396|year=1998|publisher=ABC-CLIO|isbn=978-0-87436-837-6|page=396}}</ref><ref name="SlattaGrummond2003">{{cite book|author1=Richard W. Slatta|author2=Jane Lucas De Grummond|title=Simon Bolivar's Quest for Glory|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=hG7uDMcZmfAC&pg=PA111|year=2003|publisher=Texas A&M University Press|isbn=978-1-60344-729-4|pages=111–12}}</ref> his tomb is in the village.  On 27 April 1870 the Battle of Altagracia was fought, and on 20 February 1902 the Battle of the Bridge.

In 1879, the National Congress of the Republic created the State of Oriente, whose capital would be Urica. This new territorial division, covering the present-day states of Anzoátegui, [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]] and [[Monagas]], remained in force until 1881 when it was re-named [[Bermúdez (state)|Bermúdez]] state, and in 1898 became Sucre state.{{cn|date=March 2023}}
