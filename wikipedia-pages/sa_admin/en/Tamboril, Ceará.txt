'''Tamboril, Ceará''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Ceará]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web|date=August 14, 2009|title=Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf|access-date=December 17, 2009|publisher=Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|language=pt|format=[[PDF]]}}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web|year=2000|title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil|url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm|access-date=December 17, 2009|publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, (IBGE)|language=pt}}</ref> It has an area of about 2046.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and is located at a [[latitude]] of 04º49'56" South and [[longitude]]  40º19'14" West, at an altitude of 322 meters.<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008" />  The estimated population of Tamboril in 2020 was 26,225.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|date=August 29, 2020|title=Population Estimate 2020|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/tamboril/panorama|access-date=November 28, 2020|website=IBGE}}</ref>

Tamboril contains 8 [[district]]s:<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009" /><ref name=":0" />

* Boa Esperança
* Carvalho
* Curatis
* Holanda
* Oliveiras
* Sucesso
* Açudinho
* Tamboril (Main district)

Tamboril is about 54 kilometers away from the municipality of [[Crateús]], the regional capital of Ceará.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Distance from Crateús to Tamboril|url=http://br.distanciacidades.net/distancia-de-crateus-a-tamboril|access-date=November 28, 2020|website=Distancia Cidades|language=pt}}</ref>
