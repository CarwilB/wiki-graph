'''Santo Antônio de Pádua''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈsɐ̃twɐ̃ˈtõɲu dʒi ˈpadwɐ}}, ''[[Saint Anthony of Padua]]'') is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]]  located in the northeastern part of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 42,594 (2020) and its area is 612&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

==Coat of arms==

The coat of arms of Santo Antônio de Pádua features eight stars representing eight of its districts on a navy blue field of the shield with a tree on the right and a fountain in the middle.  The municipality's name is centered in the shield and it runs diagonally from bottom left to top right.  The shield is bordered with four orange rectangles on each side.  The crown is on the top of the coat of arms.
