'''San Juan de Bigote District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Morropón province]], located in the [[department of Piura]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref>

It borders to the north with the [[Yamango District]], to the northwest with [[Buenos Aires District, Morropón|Buenos Aires District]], to the west, southwest and south with [[Salitral District, Morropón|Salitral District]], to the southeast with [[Canchaque District]] in the [[Huancabamba province]]; and to the east and northeast again with Yamango District.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Juan de Bigote en el departamento de Piura - Municipio y municipalidad de Perú |url=https://www.distrito.pe/distrito-san-juan-de-bigote.html |access-date=2025-09-02 |website=www.distrito.pe |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
The district of San Juan de Bigote was created in December 29, 1986, during the [[First presidency of Alan García|first presidency]] of [[Alan García|Alan García Pérez]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Justia Perú :: Federales > Leyes > 24627 :: Ley de Perú |url=https://peru.justia.com/federales/leyes/24627-dec-29-1986/gdoc/ |access-date=2025-09-02 |website=peru.justia.com |language=es}}</ref>
