'''Feira Grande''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈfeɾɐ ˈɡɾɐ̃di]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western region of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 22,178 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/feira-grande/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|156|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/home.php cidades.ibge.gov.br]</ref>
