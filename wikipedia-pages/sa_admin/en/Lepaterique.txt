'''Lepaterique''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].

==Military base==
A military base located in Lepaterique was used during the 1980s by the [[Contra (guerrillas)|Contras]] and by the [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[Batallón de Inteligencia 601|601 Intelligence Battalion]], which was involved in [[Operation Condor]].

In August 2005, Honduran media revealed that [[Triple Canopy, Inc.]], a [[private military contractor]], was using the base to train mercenaries before they were sent on mission in [[Iraq War|Iraq]].<ref>[http://www.wsws.org/articles/2005/dec2005/merc-d28.shtml Latin American mercenaries guarding Baghdad's Green Zone<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
