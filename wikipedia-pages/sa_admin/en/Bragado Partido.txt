'''Bragado Partido''' is a  [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of around 40,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2230|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Bragado]], which is around {{convert|210|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
