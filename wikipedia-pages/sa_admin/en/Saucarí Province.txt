'''Saucarí''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the central area of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]]. Its seat is [[Toledo, Oruro|Toledo]].

==Location==
Saucarí province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 50' and 18° 47' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 47' and 67° 17' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders [[Cercado Province (Oruro)|Cercado Province]] in the north and east, [[Nor Carangas Province]] in the northwest, [[Carangas Province]] in the west, [[Sud Carangas Province]] in the south, and [[Poopó Province]] in the southeast.

The province extends over 115&nbsp;km from North to South, and 40&nbsp;km from East to West.

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 84%, 83% of the population speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]] and 37% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992).

The population increased from 5,569 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 7,763 (2001 census), an increase of 39%. - 33.6% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).

82% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 96% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

75.2% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 2.6% in [[mining]], 5.6% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 16.6% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

83% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 14% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Toledo'''. It is identical to the province Suacarí.
