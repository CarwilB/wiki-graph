'''Planalto''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]], located at {{Coord|27|19|44|S|53|03|31|W|type:city(11702)_region:BR}}, at an [[elevation]] of 568 meters above sea level. It has a total [[area]] of 237.35 km<sup>2</sup>. Its estimated population in 2020 was 10,019 [[inhabitant]]s.

The [[city]] is located in the confluence of the [[Rio Uruguay]] and [[Rio do Mel]] rivers, close to the border with the Brazilian state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].

The municipality is part of the [[Hidrominerais]] region, in the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of [[Frederico Westphalen]]. It is located in the [[perimeter]] of the underground [[Aquífero Guarani]].
