'''Rufino''' is a city in the [[Provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], Argentina. It has 19,211 inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2022}}. It lies on the southwest of the province, {{convert|260|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from the main [[metropolitan area]] of the province [[Greater Rosario]],{{convert|416|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from the provincial capital [[Santa Fe, Argentina|Santa Fe]], near the borders with [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]] (west) and [[Buenos Aires Province|Buenos Aires]] (south), on the intersection of National Routes [[National Route 33 (Argentina)|33]] and [[National Route 7 (Argentina)|7]].

The town was founded by Gerónimo Segundo Rufino in 1886, as the railway line that linked [[Diego de Alvear, Santa Fe]], to [[Villa Mercedes, San Luis]], was inaugurated. The plans for the new town were approved by the governorship of Santa Fe on 29 March 1889, which is acknowledged as the  official foundation date.
