'''Novo Horizonte''' ([[portuguese language|portuguese]] for "New Horizon") is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the west of the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. It is situated on a [[latitude]] of 26° 26' 40" South, and a [[longitude]] of 52° 50' 01" East, at an altitude of 710 metres. It was created in 1992 out of the existing municipality of [[São Lourenço do Oeste]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.leisestaduais.com.br/sc/lei-ordinaria-n-8530-1992-santa-catarina-cria-o-municipio-de-novo-horizonte|title=Lei promulgada nº 8530, cria o município de Novo Horizonte|publisher=Legislação Estadual de Santa Catarina|language=pt|date=9 January 1992}}</ref> The population was estimated at 2,404 inhabitants in 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/novo-horizonte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The municipality covers 151&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Neighbouring towns==
Novo Horizonte is bordered by the following municipalities: 
* [[São Lourenço do Oeste]]
* [[Jupiá]]
* [[Galvão]]
* [[Coronel Martins]]
* [[Santiago do Sul]]
* [[Formosa do Sul]]
