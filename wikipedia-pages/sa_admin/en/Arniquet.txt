'''Arniquet''' ({{IPA|fr|aʁnikɛ}}; {{langx|ht|Anikè}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Port-Salut Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2015, the commune had 29,180 inhabitants.
