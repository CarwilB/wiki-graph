'''Capão do Cipó''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 3,699 (2020 est.) in an area of 1,120.87&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/13MB Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The economy is mainly agricultural and it contains 500 square kilometres of [[soybean]] production.
