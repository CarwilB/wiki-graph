'''Puerto de Mejillones''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the western parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]]. It is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. Its seat is [[La Rivera]].

==Location==
The province is located between 18° 53' and 19° 16' [[Latitude|South]] and between 68° 10' and 68° 26' [[Longitude|West]]. It is surrounded by [[Sabaya Province]] in the north, west and east, and it borders the Republic of [[Chile]] in the southwest. 
The province extends over 45&nbsp;km from north to south, and 35&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Demographics==
Main language of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 97.0%, while 66.6% of the population speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]] and 8.6% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992). 

The population increased from 751 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 1,130 (2001 census), an increase of 50%.   

42.6% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).  

74% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 73% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992). 

83.8% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 1.0% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 15.2% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

74% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 18% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).
