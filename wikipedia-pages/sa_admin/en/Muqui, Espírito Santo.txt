'''Muqui''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 15,526 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|327|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|10459|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra das Torres Natural Monument]], created in 2010 to protect the mountain peaks in the area.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese|last=Hartung Gomes|first=Paulo Cesar|title=Lei Nº 9.463|date=11 June 2010|url=http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|accessdate=2016-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161028020315/http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|archive-date=28 October 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The word ''muqui'' is of indigenous origin, meaning "between hills", referring to its mountainous geography.
