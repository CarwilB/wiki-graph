'''Coyotepec''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico State|municipality]] in the [[State of Mexico]], [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  12.30&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and, in 2005, had a total population of 39,341.<ref>{{cite web|title=Coyotepec |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15023a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=June 6, 2009 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070525233922/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15023a.htm |archivedate=May 25, 2007 }}</ref> 

The name comes from the [[Nahuatl]] ''coyotl'' (coyote) and ''tepetl'' (hill), with the locative suffix ''c'': thus, "hill of the coyote" or "place of coyotes".<ref>{{cite web |title=Nomenclatura |url=https://www.coyotepec.gob.mx/tu-municipio/nomenclatura |website=Municipio de Coyotepec}}</ref>
