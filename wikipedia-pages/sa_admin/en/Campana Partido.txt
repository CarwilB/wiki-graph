'''Campana Partido''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: Partido de Campana) is a  [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north-east of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of around 84,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|982|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Campana, Buenos Aires|Campana]], which is {{convert|75|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==
The economy of Campana went through significant decline in the late 20th century, but the town and its partido are going through an economic and cultural resurgence, which is reflected by its recent growth in population.{{cn|date=February 2024}}

==Sports==
Campana is home to [[Villa Dálmine|Club Villa Dálmine]], a football club that currently plays in Argentina's regionalised [[Primera B Nacional|2nd division]], and to [[Club Atlético Puerto Nuevo|Puerto Nuevo]], an older club that plays in [[Primera D Metropolitana|5th division]].
