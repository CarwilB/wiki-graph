'''Ocampo''' is a  city (and [[Municipalities of Guanajuato|municipality]]) located in the northwest region of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Guanajuato]].  The municipality has an area of 1,019.49  square kilometres (3.7% of the surface of the state) and is bordered and to the south and east by [[San Felipe, Guanajuato|San Felipe]], to the north by the state of [[San Luis Potosí]], to the west by the state of [[Jalisco]]. The municipality had a population of 23,500 inhabitants according to the 2010 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=2007-03-22 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2013-04-06 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato | title=Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia | work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México | access-date=2007-03-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070422213557/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato| archive-date=2007-04-22}}</ref>

The municipality is named after [[Melchor Ocampo]],  a 19th-century liberal statesman.

The municipal president of Ocampo and its many smaller outlying communities is Francisco Pedroza<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://guanajuato.contralinea.com.mx/archivo/2006/agosto/htm/PAN_voto_conservador.htm |title=PAN, truinfa voto conservador {{!}} Revista Contralínea GUANAJUATO, periodismo de investigacion. Mayo 2006 Mexico {{!}} Agosto 2006<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=2007-06-24 |archive-date=2007-09-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928050531/http://guanajuato.contralinea.com.mx/archivo/2006/agosto/htm/PAN_voto_conservador.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref>

As of 2008 the largest group of immigrants from Ocampo go to [[Dallas]], and the second-largest go to [[Chicago]].<ref>{{cite news|author=Hernández, Macarena |author2=Gary Jacobson |url=http://www.dallasnews.com/sharedcontent/dws/news/longterm/stories/060908dnmeteslmexico.3d5cf84.html |title=Education a challenge in small Mexican community with strong ties to Dallas |newspaper=[[The Dallas Morning News]] |date=2008-06-09 |access-date=2016-12-28 |url-status=bot: unknown |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611130006/http://www.dallasnews.com/sharedcontent/dws/news/longterm/stories/060908dnmeteslmexico.3d5cf84.html |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
