The '''Justo Briceño Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 6,472.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Torondoy]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Justo Briceño Municipality.<ref name="Merida_zip">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
On September 23, 1867, it was established as Torondoy [[Parish (administrative division)|Civil Parish]] . In 1904, Torondoy District was established, comprising the parishes of Piñango, Santa Apolonia and Torondoy. On January 19, 1935, Torondoy District was renamed to Gómez District, but this change was short-lived, and in 1936, it was renamed Justo Briceño District. In 1988, it separated from the [[Tulio Febres Cordero Municipality]] and adopted the name of Justo Briceño Autonomous Municipality, and in 1992, dropped the title of Autonomous to become Justo Briceño Municipality.

A historical border conflict has existed over the border between the [[Zulia State]] and [[Mérida State]] parts of town.{{citation needed|date=August 2011}}<!-- based on the map, no part of this municipality even appears to touch the state border! -->

==Geography==
It is a completely mountainous region. Most of the territory lies between 1,150m and 2,000m of altitude, with altitudes exceeding 4,500m near the borders with Rangel and Libertador municipalities, resulting in snowy areas. The average annual temperature is 16&nbsp;°C (60.8&nbsp;°F) with average rainfall of 1,250mm. The main waterways include Torondoy, Macumana, Macumpate and Arenoso rivers and El Molino creek.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
The Justo Briceño Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 6,472 (up from 5,355 in 2000).  This amounts to 0.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|12.7|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The mayor of the Justo Briceño Municipality is Víctor Luis Matheus López, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 51% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - JUSTO BRICEÑO | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120314125402/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-03-14}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 Directory of elected mayors | accessdate=March 15, 2008 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=October 2, 2006}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es;  Capital Justo Briceño and San Cristóbal de Torondoy.<ref name="Merida_zip"/>
