'''Vista Alegre''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northern part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,739 (2020 est.) in an area of 77.46 km².<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/11W2 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 546 m.
