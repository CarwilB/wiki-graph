'''Puerto Libertador''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpweɾto liβeɾtaˈðoɾ}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Córdoba Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

According to 2020 estimates, the population of Puerto Libertador was 55,622.
