'''Hernando Siles''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] of the [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Chuquisaca Department|Chuquisaca]], [[Bolivia]]. In the 2024 census it had a population of 31,688.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolivia: Administrative Division (Departments and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/ |access-date=2024-09-10 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Censo de Poblacion y Vivienda - 2001: Chuquisaca |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística de Bolivia |url=http://www.ine.gob.bo/comunitaria/comunitariaVer.aspx?Depto=01&Prov=00&Seccion=00 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722212933/http://www.ine.gob.bo/comunitaria/comunitariaVer.aspx?Depto=01&Prov=00&Seccion=00 |archivedate=22 July 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It covers an area of 5,473&nbsp;km², giving it a [[population density]] of 6.67/km². Its capital is [[Monteagudo, Bolivia|Monteagudo]].

== Subdivision ==
