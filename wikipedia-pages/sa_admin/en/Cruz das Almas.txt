'''Cruz das Almas''' (translation: Souls' Cross) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]], in the state of [[Bahia]], in [[Brazil]]. Founded in 1897, it is home to the [[Federal University of Recôncavo da Bahia]] and is one of the main cities of [[Bahia]]. 

In 2022, it had a population of 60,348 inhabitants.<ref name="auto"/> Its climate is [[tropical]], hot and humid. The economy is based on [[agriculture]], especially [[tobacco]], [[citrus fruits]] (oranges and Tahiti lime) and [[cassava]] production. 

It is also home of the [[Embrapa]] Cassava & Fruits.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.embrapa.br/mandioca-e-fruticultura|title=Embrapa Mandioca e Fruticultura - Portal Embrapa|website=www.embrapa.br}}</ref> Cruz das Almas is well known for its [[Festa Junina|Saint John Festival]] in June, where fireworks are used as "espadas" in a beautiful but dangerous game.
