'''Chitagá''' ({{IPA|es|tʃitaˈɣa}}) is a [[Colombian municipality]] and town located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[North Santander]]. The town has a population of 11,684 people and an elevation of 2,350 metres above sea level.
