'''Gonzanamá''' is a canton in the [[Loja Province|Province of Loja]], [[Ecuador]]. It is located in the south-east of the province, bordered by the cantons of [[Catamayo Canton|Catamayo]], [[Loja (canton)|Loja]], [[Quilanga Canton|Quilanga]], [[Calvas]], and [[Paltas]].  It is the "agricultural, farming, and craftsmanship capital of Loja."  Gonzanamá Canton covers 681.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 2,045 m, with a population of 12,247.  The principal town is [[Gonzanamá]].
