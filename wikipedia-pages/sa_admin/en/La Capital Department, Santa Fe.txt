The '''La Capital Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento La Capital''''', {{IPA|es|depaɾtaˈmento la kapiˈtal}}) is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-east of the province. It limits with the [[Paraná River]] in the east; and from there (going clockwise) with the departments of [[San Jerónimo Department|San Jerónimo]] (south), [[Las Colonias Department|Las Colonias]] (west), [[San Justo Department, Santa Fe|San Justo]] (north) and [[Garay Department|Garay]] (northeast).

The department has about 490,000 inhabitants, making it the second most populated in the province after the [[Rosario Department]]. Its head town is [[Santa Fe, Argentina|Santa Fe]], which is also the provincial capital (population 370,000). Other cities and towns are [[Arroyo Aguiar]], [[Arroyo Leyes]], [[Cabal, Argentina|Cabal]], [[Campo Andino]], [[Emilia, Argentina|Emilia]], [[Gobernador Candioti]], [[Laguna Paiva]], [[Llambi Campbell]], [[Monte Vera]], [[Nelson, Argentina|Nelson]], [[Recreo, Santa Fe|Recreo]], [[San José del Rincón, Argentina|San José del Rincón]], [[Santo Tomé, Santa Fe|Santo Tomé]], and [[Sauce Viejo, Argentina|Sauce Viejo]].
