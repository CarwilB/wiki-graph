'''Zarcero''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019 |archive-date=24 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240224063957/https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

Hiking trails, pure water creeks, friendly people, landscapes and natural attractions are features of the area.

==Toponymy==
The name means an abundance of the [[Rubus ulmifolius|elmleaf or thornless blackberry]] plant, in Spanish called {{lang|es|zarzamora}}.

It was originally given the name "Alfaro Ruiz" in remembrance of [[Second Battle of Rivas#Juan Alfaro Ruiz|Juan Alfaro Ruíz]], a hero from the [[Filibuster War|National Campaign of 1856]],<ref>{{Cite book |title=Historical Dictionary of Costa Rica |last=Creedman |first=Theodore S |publisher=The Scarecrow Press |year=1991 |isbn=9780810822153 |location=London |pages=41–42}}</ref> or Filibuster War. The head city of the canton is the eponymous [[Zarcero]] district.

== History ==
Zarcero was created on 21 June 1915 by decree 27.<ref name="dta2017" /><ref>{{Cite book |title=Centenario de Zarcero: un cantón pintoresco |last=Murillo Vargas |first=Juan Ramón |publisher=Editorial Universidad Estatal A Distancia |year=2015 |location=Costa Rica |isbn=9789968481175 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
Zarcero has an area of {{cvt|155.13|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:1777}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

To the north, the region borders canton [[San Carlos (canton)|San Carlos]], to the south it borders canton [[Naranjo (canton)|Naranjo]], to the east it borders canton [[Sarchí (canton)|Sarchí]] and to the west it borders the canton [[San Ramón (canton)|San Ramón]].

The canton itself is diamond-shaped, with the Espina River forming the border on the northwest and the southwest sides. The [[:es:Río Toro (Costa Rica)|Toro River]] forms the southeastern border, and the La Vieja River borders the northeast.

==Economy==
The economy of the canton is based on agriculture, primarily coffee and vegetables such as carrots, lettuce, potatoes and [[Cucurbita ficifolia|chiverre]].<ref>{{Cite web |url= http://digitalcommons.macalester.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1011&context=macabroad |title=Neoliberalism and Social Response: A Preliminary Study of the Peasantry in Alfaro Ruiz, Costa Rica |last=Robson |first=Pål André |date=May 2009 |access-date=August 5, 2017}}</ref> Livestock and their products, especially "natilla Zarcero", a form of sour cream that originated in this area, make up another large portion of the economy. There are also smaller pastry, bread and other agriculturally-based industries in Zarcero. Tourism likewise has an impact on the economy. Two protected areas help attract tourist attention, a section of [[Juan Castro Blanco National Park]] and [[El Chayote Protected Zone]].<ref name="accvc">{{cite web |title=El Chayote Protected Zone, National Parks Costa Rica |url=http://www.costarica-nationalparks.com/elchayoteprotectedzone.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040924033346/http://www.costarica-nationalparks.com/elchayoteprotectedzone.html |url-status=usurped |archive-date=September 24, 2004 |website=www.costarica-nationalparks.com |publisher=Costa Rica National Parks |access-date=August 5, 2017}}</ref>

One feature of the canton is Evangelista Blanco Brenes Park, a park with [[topiary]]-designed bushes located in front of the Iglesia de San Rafael Arcángel in the center of the city of Zarcero.
