'''Luquillo''' ({{IPA|es|luˈkiʎo}}) is a [[Luquillo barrio-pueblo|town]] and [[Municipalities of Puerto Rico|municipality]] of [[Puerto Rico]] located in the northeast coast, northwest of [[Fajardo, Puerto Rico|Fajardo]]; and east of [[Río Grande, Puerto Rico|Rio Grande]]. Luquillo is spread over 5 barrios and [[Luquillo barrio-pueblo|Luquillo Pueblo]] (the downtown area and the administrative center of the city). It lends its name to the [[Sierra de Luquillo]], where [[El Yunque National Forest]] is located. It is part of the [[Fajardo Metropolitan Statistical Area]].

The city of Luquillo is 26 square miles and it sits on 12 miles of [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] coastline. It is nestled between the blue waters of the Atlantic and the [[El Yunque National Forest]], a [[rainforest]], giving it a diverse and unique ecology. Luquillo marks the beginning of the [[Northeast Ecological Corridor]] Nature Reserve which runs down the coast from downtown Luquillo all the way down to the Seven Seas Beach in [[Fajardo, Puerto Rico|Fajardo]]. During certain times of the year, it is not unusual to encounter rare or [[endangered species]] of fauna (like the [[leatherback turtle]]) while visiting in Luquillo.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.luquillopr.com/ |title=Luquillo PR 2015: Everything You Need To Know About Luquillo |access-date=2018-12-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181019121823/http://luquillopr.com/ |archive-date=2018-10-19 |url-status=live }}</ref>

==History==
Luquillo was founded in 1797 and is known as "''La Capital del Sol''" (Capital of the Sun), "''La Riviera de Puerto Rico''" (the Puerto Rican Riviera), and "''Los Come Cocos''" (the [[coconut]] eaters). Legend says that the town was named after the Indian cacique ''Loquillo'', who died a few years after the last Indian rebellion in 1513. However, the word Luquillo most likely originates from ''Yukiyu'', the Taino name for [[El Yunque (Puerto Rico)|El Yunque]], most likely meaning "white mountain" (i.e. foggy mountain).In 1989, [[Hurricane Hugo]] damaged the town.

On September 20, 2017 [[Hurricane Maria]] struck the island of Puerto Rico.  In Luquillo, rivers were breached, there were landslides, the electrical power collapsed, and 1100 homes were destroyed or damaged. Incidents of looting and assaults were reported in the aftermath of the storm.<ref>{{cite news | title=María, un nombre que no vamos a olvidar. ===Special Communities===
{{See also|Puerto Rico Office for Socioeconomic and Community Development}}
{{lang|es|Comunidades Especiales de Puerto Rico}} (Special Communities of Puerto Rico) are marginalized communities whose citizens are experiencing a certain amount of [[social exclusion]]. A map shows these communities occur in nearly every municipality of the commonwealth. There are 14 beaches in Luquillo including {{lang|es|La Pared}}.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/nota/las1200playasdepuertorico-1216285/|title=Las 1,200 playas de Puerto Rico [The 1200 beaches of Puerto Rico]|date=April 14, 2017|website=Primera Hora|language=es|access-date=December 12, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191212200804/https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/nota/las1200playasdepuertorico-1216285/|archive-date=December 12, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> {{lang|es|La Pared}}, which translates to "The Wall", is considered a dangerous beach.<ref>{{cite web | title=Conoce las 11 playas más peligrosas de Puerto Rico [Know the 11 most dangerous beaches in Puerto Rico]| website=El Nuevo Día | date=2018-07-04 | url=https://www.elnuevodia.com/noticias/locales/fotogalerias/conoce-las-11-playas-mas-peligrosas-de-puerto-rico/ | language=es | access-date=2021-05-13}}</ref> The coastal highway going east from [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], leads to [[La Monserrate Beach|Luquillo Beach]], officially known as La Monserrate Beach (''Balneario de la Monserrate''). ==Government==
{{main|Mayoralty in Puerto Rico}}
Like all municipalities in Puerto Rico, Luquillo is administered by a mayor. The current mayor is [[Jesús Márquez Rodríguez]], who was first elected at the [[Puerto Rican general election, 2012|2012 general election]].

The city belongs to the [[Puerto Rico Senatorial district VIII]], which is represented by two Senators. In 2024, [[Marissa Jiménez]] and [[Héctor Joaquín Sánchez Álvarez]] were elected as District Senators.<ref name="g754">{{cite web | title=CEE Event | website=elecciones2024.ceepur.org | url=https://elecciones2024.ceepur.org/Escrutinio_General_121/index.html#es/default/SENADORES_POR_DISTRITO_Carolina_VIII.xml | access-date=May 12, 2025}}</ref>

==Transportation==
[[File:Entrada a Barrio Sabana, Luquillo, Puerto Rico.jpg|thumb|Entrance sign for Sabana barrio and PR-983]]
There is no public transportation in Luquillo, and residents and visitors rely on [[Uber]] or Luquillo Taxi & Tours Puerto Rico LLC [[Luquillo Taxi]] for service.

There are 20 bridges in Luquillo.<ref name="bridge">{{cite web|title=Luquillo Bridges|url=http://bridgereports.com/pr/luquillo/|website=National Bridge Inventory Data|publisher=US Dept. of Transportation|access-date=20 February 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190221224233/http://bridgereports.com/pr/luquillo/|archive-date=21 February 2019|url-status=live}}</ref>

[[Puerto Rico Highway 3|PR-3]] is the main road through Luquillo. Other municipality roads include PR-983, PR-988, PR-991 and PR-940.<ref name="bridge" />

==Symbols==
The {{lang|es|municipio}} has an official flag and coat of arms.<ref>{{cite web | title=Ley Núm. 70 de 2006 -Ley para disponer la oficialidad de la bandera y el escudo de los setenta y ocho (78) municipios. | website=LexJuris de Puerto Rico | url=https://www.lexjuris.com/LEXLEX/Leyes2006/lexl2006070.htm | language=es | access-date=2021-06-15}}</ref>

===Flag===
It consists of three horizontal stripes, the blue top and green bottom are double of width of the yellow central stripe. Blue makes reference to the sky and the sea; yellow represents the sand of its beaches and green represents the vegetation of the [[El Yunque (Puerto Rico)|mountains]]. In the center stripe resides the coat of arms of the municipality superimposed and surrounded by two palm tree leaves crossed at the bottom.<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020">{{cite web | title=LUQUILLO | website=LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico | date=19 February 2020 | url=http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/LUQUILLO.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200219000410/http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/LUQUILLO.html | archive-date=19 February 2020 | url-status=live | language=es | access-date=17 September 2020}}</ref>

===Coat of arms===
In a gold background a centered mountain range with three green mountains is accompanied at the bottom by a bay with blue and silver waves; the top portion of the shield in blue, has three iris branches. Above the shield resides a three tower gold crown. Surrounding the shield by its flanks are two palms trees leaves crossed at the bottom.<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020" />
