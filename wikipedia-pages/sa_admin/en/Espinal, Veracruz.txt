'''Espinal''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] that is located in the central area of the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]] in the [[Totonaca Region]]. It is located at coordinates 20° 16' north latitude and 97° 24' west longitude, and has a height of 100 m.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.espinal.gob.mx/ |title=Official website of municipality |access-date=2019-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081125092626/http://www.espinal.gob.mx/ |archive-date=2008-11-25 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The municipality is made up 82 localities where 24,823 people live.
