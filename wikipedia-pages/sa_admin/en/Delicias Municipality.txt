'''Delicias''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The capital lies at [[Delicias, Chihuahua|Delicias]]. The municipality covers an area of 335.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 137,935,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Delicias|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=021|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304084908/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=021|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 127,211 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Delicias|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08018a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal|accessdate=August 22, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223203237/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08018a.htm|archivedate=February 23, 2007}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Delicias had a population of 118,071.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Delicias, the municipality had 687 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Colonia Revolución, Chihuahua|Colonia Revolución]] (3,995), [[Miguel Hidalgo, Chihuahua|Miguel Hidalgo]] (2,850), classified as urban; and [[Colonia Campesina, Chihuahua|Colonia Campesina]] (2,365), [[Colonia Nicolás Bravo (Kilómetro Noventa y Dos), Chihuahua|Colonia Nicolás Bravo (Kilómetro Noventa y Dos)]] (1,772), [[Colonia Terrazas, Chihuahua|Colonia Terrazas]] (1,602), and [[Colonia Abraham González (La Quemada), Chihuahua|Colonia Abraham González (La Quemada)]] (1,404), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
