'''Gachantivá''' is a town and municipality in the [[Ricaurte Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Gachantivá is located at altitudes ranging from {{convert|2000|m|ft}} to {{convert|3300|m|ft}} on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] and borders [[Villa de Leyva]] in the south, [[Santa Sofía, Boyacá|Santa Sofía]] in the west, [[Arcabuco]] in the east and [[Moniquirá]] in the north.<ref name=websiteGachantiva>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150603092627/http://www.gachantiva-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Gachantivá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Gachantivá comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "chief of the Gacha".<ref name=websiteGachantiva/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Gachantivá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Gachantivá before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. The ''[[cacique]]'' of Gachantivá was loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]].

Modern Gachantivá was founded in 1715 by Juan José Neira.<ref name=websiteGachantiva/>

== Economy ==
The main economical activities of Gachantivá are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Among the agricultural products cultivated are [[maize]], [[potato]]es, [[yuca]], [[coffee]], [[blackberry|blackberries]], [[bean]]s and [[pea]]s.<ref name=websiteGachantiva/>
