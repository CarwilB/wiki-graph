'''Itaguaçu da Bahia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. It covers {{convert|4,451.270|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 14,542 with a population density of 3.3 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
