'''Noord''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Para District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 9,703.<ref name="census"/> Noord is mainly an agricultural area, and has a school.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The main town in the Noord resort is [[Onverwacht, Suriname|Onverwacht]] which is also the capital of the District.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/para01.html|title=Distrikt Para|website=Suriname.nu|language=nl|access-date=13 May 2020}}</ref> The indigenous village of [[Bernharddorp]] is one of the larger settlements in the resort. The village has a population of about 1,800 people as of 2020,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://vids.sr/about/dorpsbesturen/|title=Dorpen en Dorpsbesturen|website=Vereniging van Inheemse Dorpshoofden in Suriname|access-date=4 February 2020|language=nl|archive-date=8 October 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211008160839/https://vids.sr/about/dorpsbesturen/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
