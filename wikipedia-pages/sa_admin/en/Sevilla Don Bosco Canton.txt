'''Sevilla Don Bosco''' is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] in the [[Ecuador]]ian [[Provinces of Ecuador|province]] of [[Morona-Santiago Province|Morona Santiago]]. It is located about {{convert|240|km}} south of [[Quito]], the national capital. Established in 2024, it is Ecuador's newest canton.

==Geography==
Sevilla Don Bosco is located in the [[Ecuadorian Amazon]] on the east bank of the [[Upano River]] across from [[Macas (city)|Macas]], the capital of Morona Santiago. From there it extends east and south into the {{ill|Cordillera de Kutukú|de}}. It borders the canton of [[Huamboya Canton|Huamboya]] to the north, the [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] of Cuchaentza (an exclave of [[Morona Canton|Morona]]) to the northeast, and the cantons of [[Taisha Canton|Taisha]] to the east, [[Tiwintza Canton|Tiwintza]] to the south, [[Logroño Canton|Logroño]] to the southwest, [[Sucúa Canton|Sucúa]] to the west, and the main part of [[Morona Canton|Morona]] to the northwest.<ref name=expresate>{{cite web |date=27 February 2023 |title=Sevilla Don Bosco inicia su camino hacia la cantonización |url=https://www.expresatems.com/historia-principal/sevilla-don-bosco-inicia-su-camino-hacia-la-cantonizacion/ |language=es |website=Exprésate Morona Santiago |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref><ref name=law>{{cite web |url=https://www.registroficial.gob.ec/index.php/registro-oficial-web/publicaciones/suplementos/item/21874-quinto-suplemento-al-registro-oficial-no-676 |publisher=[[National Assembly (Ecuador)]] |title=Ley para la Creación del Cantón Sevilla Don Bosco en la Provincia de Morona Santiago |work=Registro Oficial |date=5 November 2024 |issue=676 |at=Quinto Suplemento |language=es |access-date=2 January 2025 |last1=Gutierrez |first1=Moises }}</ref>

The Cordillera de Kutukú has been designated an [[Important Bird Area]]<ref>{{cite web |url=https://datazone.birdlife.org/site/factsheet/cordillera-de-kutuk%C3%BA-iba-ecuador/text |title=Cordillera de Kutukú  |language=es |publisher=[[BirdLife International]] |year=2005 |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref> and it lies within the boundaries of the [[Kutukú-Shaimi Protective Forest]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Granizo |first=Tarsicio |url=https://wwf.panda.org/discover/knowledge_hub/where_we_work/amazon/amazon_views/the_kutuku_shaimi_protection_forest___an_area_to_discover_/ |title=The Kutukú-Shaimi Protection Forest – An Area to Discover |publisher=[[World Wildlife Fund]] |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref> The sustainable management of the Forest is led by the indigenous [[Shuar]] people, but the Forest is also completely covered by mining concessions.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Palacios Orejuela |first1=Iván Fernando |last2=Rodríguez Espinosa |first2=Fabián |year=2019 |title=Economic valuation of environmental goods and services of the Protector Forest Kutukú – Shaimi, SE Ecuador |url=https://www.proquest.com/docview/2501927486 |access-date=2 January 2025 |journal=International Journal of Energy, Environment and Economics |volume=27 |issue=2 |pages=117–132 |doi=10.3406/bifea.1981.1538|id={{ProQuest|2501927486}} |url-access=subscription }}</ref>

==History==
The [[Upano Valley site]] of [[Kilamope]] is located in the present-day boundaries of Sevilla Don Bosco. Active between 500 BCE and 300–600 CE, the Upano Valley cultures are currently the earliest urbanized agrarian societies known to have existed in the [[Amazon rainforest]].<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Rostain |first1=Stéphen |last2=Dorison |first2=Antoine |last3=de Saulieu |first3=Geoffroy |last4=Prümers |first4=Heiko |last5=Le Pennec |first5=Jean-Luc |last6=Mejía Mejía |first6=Fernando |last7=Freire |first7=Ana Maritza |last8=Pagán-Jiménez |first8=Jaime R. |last9=Descola |first9=Philippe |title=Two thousand years of garden urbanism in the Upper Amazon |journal=Science |volume=383 |issue=6679 |date=12 January 2024 |issn=0036-8075 |doi=10.1126/science.adi6317 |pages=183–189 |pmid=38207020 |bibcode=2024Sci...383..183R |s2cid=266933753 }}</ref><ref>{{cite journal |last=Wade |first=Lizzie |date=11 January 2024 |title=Laser mapping reveals oldest Amazonian cities, built 2500 years ago |url=https://www.science.org/content/article/laser-mapping-reveals-oldest-amazonian-cities-built-2500-years-ago |access-date=2 January 2025 |journal=[[Science (journal)|Science]] |doi=10.1126/science.zzti03q |archive-date=12 January 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240112000516/https://www.science.org/content/article/laser-mapping-reveals-oldest-amazonian-cities-built-2500-years-ago |url-status=live |url-access=subscription }}</ref>

In 1576, acting on orders from [[Juan de Salinas y Loyola]], Captain José de Villanueva Maldonado founded the settlement of Sevilla de Oro on the east bank of the Upano River, which was the source of the first part of Sevilla Don Bosco's name. In 1599, Sevilla de Oro was destroyed by the [[Shuar]] people.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Taylor |first1=Anne-Christine |last2=Descola |first2=Philippe |year=1981 |title=El conjunto jívaro en los comienzos de la conquista española del Alto Amazonas |language=es |url=https://www.persee.fr/doc/bifea_0303-7495_1981_num_10_3_1538 |access-date=2 January 2025 |journal=Bulletin de l'Institut Français d'Études Andines |volume=10 |issue=3–4 |pages=7–54 |doi=10.3406/bifea.1981.1538}}</ref>

In 1929, the [[Salesians of Don Bosco]] at the [[Christian mission|mission]] of Macas started visiting the Shuar on the east bank of the Upano. They founded the mission of Sevilla Don Bosco on 2 May 1943.<ref>{{cite book |last=Garzón Vera |first=Blas |title=El Modelo Educativo Salesiano: Repercusiones en la cultura shuar 1893-1970 |language=es |trans-title=The Salesian Educational Model: Repercussions in the Shuar Culture 1893-1970 |publisher=Editorial Universitaria Abya-Yala |date=10 December 2017 |pages=81–84 |location=Quito |url=https://dspace.ups.edu.ec/handle/123456789/19066 |isbn=978-9978-10-289-3 |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref> On 28 May 1958, the parish of Sevilla Don Bosco was established in the province of Morona Santiago.<ref name=expresate/>

In 2019, Sevilla Don Bosco's parish government petitioned the office of the [[president of Ecuador]] to start the process of converting the parish into a canton, after two earlier requests to do so had failed.<ref name=expresate/> In 2022, the president asked the [[Constitutional Court of Ecuador]] to rule on the constitutionality of the proposed referendum on the issue, which the court approved. On 5 February 2023, 82.66% of Sevilla Don Bosco's population voted in favour of becoming a canton. On 10 October 2024, the [[National Assembly (Ecuador)|National Assembly]] approved the conversion of Sevilla Don Bosco into Ecuador's 222nd canton, which went into force on 5 November 2024.<ref name=law/> Sevilla Don Bosco is scheduled to hold its first elections as an independent canton on 17 August 2025.<ref>{{cite web |date=2 December 2024 |url=https://www.eloriente.com/articulo/sevilla-don-bosco-elegira-autoridades-el-17-de-agosto-de-2025/48151 |title=Sevilla Don Bosco elegirá autoridades el 17 de agosto de 2025 |language=es |website=El Oriente |accessdate=2 January 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2022 Ecuadorian Census, the then-parish of Sevilla Don Bosco recorded a population of 18,647 inhabitants.<ref name=census2024/> Sevilla Don Bosco includes over forty settlements, of which 85% are [[Shuar]] communities.<ref>{{cite web |date=16 May 2024 |url=https://notiregionecuador.com/tramitan-proyecto-de-ley-que-crea-canton-sevilla-don-bosco-en-provincia-de-morona-santiago/ |title=Tramitan proyecto de Ley que crea cantón Sevilla Don Bosco en provincia de Morona Santiago |language=es |publisher=NotiRegiónEcuador |accessdate=2 January 2025}}</ref>

==Economy==
Sevilla Don Bosco's economy is primarily based on agriculture, and its main exports are [[cassava]] and [[Xanthosoma|malanga]].<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Zurita Vaca |first1=Mauricio |last2=Amboya Soque |first2=Renato |last3=Barba Castro |first3=Elizabeth |date=25 March 2016 |title=Infraestructura Vial y crecimiento económico: Caso Parroquias Sevilla Don Bosco y San Isidro, Provincia de Morona Santiago, Ecuador |language=es |trans-title=Road Infrastructure and Economic Growth: The Case of Sevilla Don Bosco Parish and San Isidro, Morona Santiago Province, Ecuador |url=https://dialnet.unirioja.es/servlet/articulo?codigo=5763176 |journal=Revista Investigaciones Altoandinas |volume=18 |issue=1 |pages=83–92 |issn=2313-2957 |doi=10.18271/ria.2016.182 |access-date=2 January 2024}}</ref> The Shuar continue to carry out subsistence farming, hunting and fishing activities in the forest, and are developing initiatives to attract [[sustainable tourism]].<ref>{{cite web |date=November 2022 |url=https://www.proamazonia.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/DON-BOSCO-espan%CC%83ol-V4-GC-NOV_2022_proamazonia.pdf |title=Plan de Vida de la Asociación Sevilla Don Bosco |language=es |publisher=PROAmazonía |accessdate=2 January 2025}}</ref>
