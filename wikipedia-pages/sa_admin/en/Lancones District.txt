'''Lancones District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Sullana Province|Sullana]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref>

In 2016, the Lancones District recorded a temperature of {{convert|39.8|C|F|abbr=on}}, which is the joint highest temperature to have ever been recorded in Peru along with [[Chulucanas]].<ref>{{Cite web|last=PERU21|first=NOTICIAS|date=2019-01-26|title=Récords de calor en el norte {{!}} OPINION|url=https://peru21.pe/opinion/records-calor-norte-455686-noticia/|access-date=2022-01-25|website=Peru21|language=es}}</ref>
