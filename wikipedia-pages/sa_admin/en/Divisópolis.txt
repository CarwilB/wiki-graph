'''Divisópolis''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].

The population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 11,211 people living in a total area of 566&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/divisopolis/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Jequitinhonha and to the microregion of [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/divisopolis/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172002/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/divisopolis/ |archive-date=2008-04-10 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Divisópolis is located on the state boundary of [[Minas Gerais]] and [[Bahia]]. It is 26&nbsp;km. south of the Bahian city of [[Encruzilhada]]. Highway connections are poor making the region isolated and impoverished. The distance to the state capital [[Belo Horizonte]] is 551&nbsp;km.

Neighboring municipalities are: [[Pedra Azul]], [[Divisa Alegre]], [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]], [[Bandeira, Minas Gerais|Bandeira]], and [[Mata Verde]].

The history of the municipality began with the arrival of the tax collectors Sérgio Chaves and João Antônio Dutra  to these lands known as  Gerais, in 1936. The region was known as Boca da Mata and, due to the belief that the town had no future, it was called Só se vendo (we have to see), later becoming Sósseveno in 1953. In the same year it became a district of Almenara, separating in 1992.<ref>[http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/3050/divisopolis-mg.html Divisopolis, informações]</ref>

The location is in a mountainous area with an elevation of 910 meters.  The main economic activities are cattle raising and the cultivation of coffee, sugarcane and corn. Coffee is the main crop with 2,650 hectares planted in 2006.  The GDP in 2006 was R$24,039,000. There was 01 banking agency {{as of|2007|lc=y}}.  In the same year there were 79 automobiles.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
