'''Içara''' is a city in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. It is located 170&nbsp;km south of [[Florianópolis]], the state capital and around 890&nbsp;km south of [[São Paulo]]. It is the Brazilian capital of the [[honey]] and [[tobacco]] industries, but is also very strong in plastic, [[ceramic]] [[tiles]] and [[chemicals]].

Nearby cities are [[Siderópolis]], [[Cocal do Sul]], [[Morro da Fumaça]], [[Maracajá]], [[Araranguá]], [[Nova Veneza, Santa Catarina|Nova Veneza]], [[Forquilhinha]] and [[Criciuma]].

Several small towns lie around Içara forming a metropolitan area of 250 thousand people. The most important are:
*Criciuma (larger city)
*[[Urussanga]]
*Nova Veneza
*[[Nova Trento]]
*Siderópolis
*[[Orleans, Santa Catarina|Orleans]]
*Araranguá
*[[Timbe do Sul]]
