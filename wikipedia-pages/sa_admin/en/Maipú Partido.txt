'''Maipú Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the east-central part of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 10,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2640|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Maipú, Buenos Aires|Maipú]].

==Economy==
The economy of Maipú Partido is dominated by agriculture, the mainstays of the agricultural production being arable crops, cattle, and dairy products.
