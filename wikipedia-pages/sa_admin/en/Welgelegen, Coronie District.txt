'''Welgelegen''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Coronie District]]. Its population was 593 at the 2012 census.<ref name="census"/> Welgelegen began as a [[cotton]] [[plantation]] in 1824.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/w/welgelegenco|title=Plantage Welgelegen (Kust in Coronie)|website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The church of Welgelegen was [[consecration|consecrated]] on 1 November 1883 but was not originally built at its current location; it was first erected at Cardross Park and later moved. The church is no longer in service.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.coronie.nl/wordpress/?page_id=64|title=Welgelegen|website=Coronie.nl|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The villages of [[Jenny, Suriname|Jenny]] and [[Mary's Hope]] are also located in the Welgelegen resort.

==Peruvia Nature Reserve==
{{anchor|Peruvia}}The Peruvia Nature Reserve was founded in 1986.<ref name="srnangsu">{{cite web|url=https://sites.google.com/site/sranangsu/natuur|title=Natuur|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> It is located near the mouth of the [[Coppename River]], and covers an area of 31,000 [[hectare]]s.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The reserve contains [[Mauritia flexuosa|moriche palms]], [[Hura crepitans|Sandbox tree]] forests, and is home to the [[Blue-and-yellow macaw]].<ref name="srnangsu"/>
