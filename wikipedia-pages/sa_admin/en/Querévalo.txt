'''Querévalo''' is a village and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Alanje District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It is located near Canas Blancas, on both banks of the Río Chico or Stone.  It has a land area of {{convert|36.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,006 as of 2023, giving it a population density of {{convert|54.7|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2010 was 1,751; its population as of 2000 was 1,536.<ref name=inec/>

Within the township there are 2 elementary schools, one of which is located in the Querévalo header and one in River Edge, a Corregiduría, 2 Public Health Post, among other infrastructure. The water system is based on deep wells, which are administered by township residents through health committees.{{citation needed|date=May 2015}}

The main economic activities are agriculture site and livestock, these being segmented according to the location of the territory (left or right margin of the Río Chico).{{citation needed|date=May 2015}}
