'''Alpatlahuac''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 161&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 75.7 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|07|N|97|06|W|}}.

The municipality of '''Alpatlahuac''' is delimited to the south by [[Coscomatepec]] and to the north by the [[Calcahualco, Veracruz|Calcahualco]].<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-mexico.gob.mx/work/EMM04/Veracruz/mpios/30008a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Alpatlahuac |publisher=Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=2009-05-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090421091942/http://www.e-mexico.gob.mx/work/EMM04/Veracruz/mpios/30008a.htm |archive-date=21 April 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

It produces [[maize]] and [[rice]].
