'''Tarata''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Tarata province|Tarata]], a province of the [[department of Tacna]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its capital is [[Tarata, Peru|Tarata]].

== History ==
Following the 1883 [[Treaty of Ancón]], Chile established a [[Tacna Province (Chile)|province]] to administer its newly acquired territory south of the [[Sama River]]. Due to differing interpretations of the rivercourse, the area's administration by Chile proved controversial. Tarata was made a sub-delegation of [[Tacna Department (Chile)|Tacna]], with its jurisdiction including the former Peruvian districts of Tarata, [[Tarucachi District|Tarucachi]] and [[Estique District|Estique]].<ref>{{Cite book |title=In the Matter of the Arbitration Between the Republic of Chile and the Republic of Peru: With Respect to the Unfulfilled Provisions of the Treaty of Peace of October 20, 1883, Under the Protocol and Supplementary Act Signed at Washington July 20, 1922. Opinion and Award of the Arbitrator |last=Coolidge |first=Calvin |publisher=Government Printing Office |year=1925 |location=Washington, D.C. |pages=55 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=7P9mAAAAMAAJ}}</ref> The sub-delegation was [[Tarata Department (Chile)|elevated to department]] from 1911 to 1921, after which it was disestablished and handed over to Peru in 1925.
