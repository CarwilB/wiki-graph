'''Girau do Ponciano''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒiˈɾɐw ˈdʊ põsiˈɐ̃nu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 36,102 (2022)<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/girau-do-ponciano/panorama |access-date=2025-07-16 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|502|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
