'''Boca do Acre''' (''Mouth of Acre'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 34,635 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/boca-do-acre/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 22,349&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

The municipality holds 92% of the {{convert|311000|ha|adj=on}} [[Mapiá-Inauini National Forest]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA Mapiá-Inauini – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA Mapiá-Inauini|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4213|accessdate=2016-09-08}}</ref>
It also contains the {{convert|133637|ha|adj=on}} [[Arapixi Extractive Reserve]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Arapixi – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Arapixi|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/877|accessdate=2016-09-08}}</ref>
