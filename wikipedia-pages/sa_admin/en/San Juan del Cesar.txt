'''San Juan del Cesar''' is a municipality and town located in the [[La Guajira Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Etymology==
San Juan Bautista del Cesar name in {{langx|en|John the Baptist}} and ''Chet-tzar'' or ''Sasare'' indigenous language for calm water, the name of the [[Cesar River]].

==History==

The region San Juan del Cesar was inhabited by [[Arhuacos]] [[Indigenous peoples in Colombia|indigenous tribe]], led during that time by a [[Cacique]] named "''Marocazo''". It was founded on June 24, 1701 by Captain [[Salvador Felix Arias Pereira]] with the name of Villa of San Juan Bautista, over Mount Yiyirigak by the [[Cesar River]]. Arias-Pereira was in a mission to "pacify" the indigenous tribes in rebellion; the Tupes, Coyaimas, Conopans and Marocazos.<ref name="sanjuandelcesar-laguajira.gov.co">{{Cite web |url=http://sanjuandelcesar-laguajira.gov.co/apc-aa-files/36613366383038373634353838316266/PLAN_DE_DESARROLLO_2.004___2.007.pdf |title=San Juan del Cesar Territorial Development Plan: General Information |access-date=2008-03-30 |language=es |archive-date=2007-10-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071027110501/http://sanjuandelcesar-laguajira.gov.co/apc-aa-files/36613366383038373634353838316266/PLAN_DE_DESARROLLO_2.004___2.007.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The Villa of San Juan immediately became a pass for people traveling between [[Valledupar]] and the port of [[Riohacha]], back then an important commercial route flowing with cattle and agricultural products. The first church was built by [[Priest]] Gonzalo Suárez Oñate.

==Geography==

The municipality of San Juan del Cesar is located between the mountain ranges of the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] and the [[Serranía del Perijá]] by the [[Cesar River]] and close to the [[Ranchería River]]. It borders to the north with the municipality of [[Riohacha]] and [[Distracción, La Guajira|Distracción]], to the south with the municipalities of [[Villanueva, La Guajira|Villanueva]], [[El Molino, La Guajira|El Molino]] and the [[Cesar Department]]. To the east with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]], to the west again with the municipality of Riohacha, [[Dibulla, La Guajira|Dibulla]] and Cesar Department covering a total area of 1,415&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The urban area of San Juan del Cesar covers 10% of this area; 141&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and the rural area 90%; 1,274&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality is part of the Upper Valley of the [[Cesar River]] (Spanish: ''Alto Valle del Río César''), where the Cañaverales [[tributary]] is located.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Suárez |first=César Normando |date=1979-01-01 |title=El alto valle del río Cesar |url=https://revistas.unal.edu.co/index.php/recs/article/view/11038 |journal=Revista Colombiana de Sociología |language=es |volume=1 |issue=1 |pages=103–127 |issn=2256-5485}}</ref>

===Climate===
The average temperature is 27°C year round.

== Economy ==
San Juan del Cesar together with Riohacha and Dibulla, are one of the production zones in which the [[Yam (vegetable)|yam]], also known as Ñame, is cultivated in the department.<ref name=":2">{{Cite journal |last=Reina-Aranza |first=Yuri Carolina |date=2012-06-14 |title=El cultivo de ñame en el Caribe colombiano |url=https://repositorio.banrep.gov.co/bitstream/handle/20.500.12134/3097/dtser_168.pdf |language=es |location=Bogotá, Colombia |doi=10.32468/dtseru.168}}</ref> San Juan del Cesar is one of the largest producers of yam in the Colombian Caribbean region in which 85 hectares of yam production has been reported.<ref name=":2" />

== Heritage sites ==
In 2021, the municipality of San Juan del Cesar was selected by the National Tourism Fund (FONTUR) among the 25 municipalities of the "Pueblos que Enamoran" initiative, due to its historical sites and tourist attractions, in particular, La Ruta del Cacique ({{Lit|The Cacique's Route|i=yes|lk=yes}}).<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2021-08-01 |title=San Juan del Cesar, uno de los 25 'Pueblos que enamoran' del país |url=https://www.eltiempo.com/colombia/otras-ciudades/san-juan-del-cesar-tierra-de-vallenato-y-lindos-paisajes-607552 |access-date=2022-08-11 |website=[[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]] |language=spanish}}</ref>

=== Churches ===
In 1973, the community of the municipality decided to raise funds for the construction of a new modern church. The old church was demolished, in the meantime the parish priest Manuel Antonio Dávila ordered to build a small chapel near the cemetery of the municipality, in order to preserve the images of the old church and perform masses there while the new church was being built. The construction of this temple, which today is called the San Juan Bautista Parish, was carried out by the architect from Barranquilla, Carlos Makis Ordóñez, and lasted between 1944 and 1945.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Parroquia San Juan Bautista de San Juan – Diócesis de Valledupar |url=https://diocesisdevalledupar.org/parroquia-san-juan-bautista-de-san-juan/ |access-date=2022-08-09 |website=[[Roman Catholic Diocese of Valledupar]] |language=es}}</ref>

The parishes of San Juan Bautista, El Divino Niño and Nuestra Señora del Carmen located in the town of San Juan del Cesar are supported by the municipal administration for religious events.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Daza |first=Hermes |date=2017-04-13 |title=San Juan del Cesar, una tierra bendecida |url=https://elpilon.com.co/san-juan-del-cesar-una-tierra-bendecida/ |access-date=2022-08-09 |website=El Pilón |language=es}}</ref> Also the municipality has several churches categorized as assets of cultural interest by the Ministry of Culture, such as the Lagunita church, which was designated as a material heritage site in July 2003,<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2020 |title=Lista de Bienes declarados Bien de Interés Cultural de Ámbito Nacional |trans-title=List of Properties declared of Cultural Interest at the National Level |url=https://www.mincultura.gov.co/prensa/noticias/Documents/Patrimonio/BIENES%20DE%20INTER%C3%89S%20CULTURAL%20DEL%20%C3%81MBITO%20NACIONAL_mayo%202020.pdf |access-date=2022-01-16 |website=[[Ministry of Culture (Colombia)|Colombia Ministry of Culture]] |archive-date=2022-01-16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220116172233/https://www.mincultura.gov.co/prensa/noticias/Documents/Patrimonio/BIENES%20DE%20INTER%C3%89S%20CULTURAL%20DEL%20%C3%81MBITO%20NACIONAL_mayo%202020.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> where eucharistic celebrations are held in honor of the patron saint, San Rafael Arcángel.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-10-24 |title=En la parroquia de Lagunita celebran en honor a San Rafael Arcángel |url=https://www.periodicolaguajira.com/index.php/la-guajira/81-municipio/39831-en-la-parroquia-de-lagunita-celebran-en-honor-a-san-rafael-arcangel |access-date=2022-08-09 |website=Periódico La Guajira |language=es}}</ref> Another parish that has been considered a cultural property of cultural interest since 2003 is the San Francisco de Asis Church in the corregimiento of Pondores, south of the municipal capital.<ref name=":0" />

=== Museums ===
The municipality has the Manuel Antonio Dávila House of Culture, which is attached to the office of Social Management and the Secretariat of Government and Education,<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Sevilla |first=Kaleth Valero |date=2021-04-01 |title=Con concurso de fotografía, San Juan del Cesar busca recuperar su historia |url=https://www.diariodelnorte.net/?p=62660 |access-date=2022-08-10 |website=Diario del Norte |language=es}}</ref> which was declared a national heritage site in July 2003 along with the churches of the corregimientos of the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Ley 835 de 2003 Congreso de la República - Colombia |url=https://www.redjurista.com/Documents/ley_835_de_2003_congreso_de_la_republica.aspx |access-date=2022-08-10 |website=Red Jurista |language=es-co}}</ref> In this cultural events have been held such as events of historical photographs of the municipality.<ref name=":1" /> The municipality also contains other heritage sites such as the Ventana Marroncita ({{Lit|The Brown Window|i=yes|lk=yes}}) which is part of the Cacique route, this place inspired Diomedes Diaz's album "Tres Canciones",<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2015-04-30 |title=La Ruta del Cacique, un rincón con mucho corazón |url=https://caracol.com.co/radio/2015/04/30/regional/1430381340_741317.html |access-date=2022-08-10 |website=Caracol Radio |language=es}}</ref> this museum located in Corregimiento La Junta, contains clothing and photos of Diomedes.<ref>{{Cite web |last=León |first=Laura Ramírez |title=La Junta, la tierra del Cacique Diomedes Díaz |url=https://www.radionacional.co/cultura/la-junta-la-tierra-del-cacique-diomedes-diaz |access-date=2022-08-11 |website=[[Radio Nacional de Colombia]]}}</ref> The Corregimiento de La Junta, also has a gallery of Diomedez's eldest daughter, Rosa Elvira Diaz Mejia with personal belongings.<ref>{{Cite web |title=En La Junta recuerdan a Diomedes Díaz con sus versos del alma |url=https://www.laopinion.com.co/entretenimiento/en-la-junta-recuerdan-diomedes-diaz-con-sus-versos-del-alma |access-date=2022-08-11 |website=La Opinión |language=es-co}}</ref>
