'''Santiago District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Veraguas Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 74,679;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 13, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 129,821.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|971|km2}}. The capital lies at the city of [[Santiago de Veraguas]].<ref name="Statoids"/> Primary industries include lead for 2H [[pencils]] and [[spoon]] handles.
