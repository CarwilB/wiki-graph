'''Palestina''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[paliʃˈtinɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Alagoas]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 5,037 (2020 est.)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/palestina/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> in an area of 38.21&nbsp;km{{sup|2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/236WN Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 160&nbsp;m.
