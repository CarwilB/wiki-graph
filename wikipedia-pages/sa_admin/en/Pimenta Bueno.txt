'''Pimenta Bueno''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 36,881 (2020) and its area is 6,241 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cod.ibge.gov.br/IY4]</ref>

The municipality contained the {{convert|27860|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Roosevelt State Forest]], created in 1990 and cancelled in 2010.<ref>*{{citation|language=pt
 |author=João Aparecido Cahulla|title=Mensagem no 090 de 31 de Maio de 2010|date=31 May 2010
 |url=http://ditel.casacivil.ro.gov.br/cotel/Livros/Files/LC584-PL.pdf|accessdate=2016-11-01}}</ref>

The city is served by [[Pimenta Bueno Airport]].
