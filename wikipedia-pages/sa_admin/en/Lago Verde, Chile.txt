'''Lago Verde''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located at the headwaters of the [[Cisnes River]] in [[Coyhaique Province]], [[Aisén Region]]. The commune is administered by the municipality of Lago Verde, the principal settlement.

==History==
At the time of European arrival, the indigenous people in the area were the [[Tehuelche people|Tehuelche]], who were nomadic [[hunter gatherer]]s,<ref name="Hist">{{Cite web|title=Historia de Lago Verde|publisher=Municipalidad de Lago Verde |url=http://lagoverdeaysen.cl/2015/?page_id=61 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150815215201/http://lagoverdeaysen.cl/2015/?page_id=61 |archivedate=15 August 2015 |url-status=live}}</ref> however, the area has been occupied since at least 2,800 [[Before Present|BP]], and likely back as far as the end of the [[Last glacial period|last ice age]] (11,400 BP).<ref>{{Cite journal|author1=Méndez M. |author2=César A. |author3=Reyes B. |author4=Omar R. |year=2008 |title=Late Holocene human occupation of the Patagonian forests: A case study in the Cisnes river basin |journal=Antiquity |volume=82 |issue=317 |pages=560–570|doi=10.1017/S0003598X00097222 |hdl=10533/141906 |hdl-access=free }}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal|author1=Scheinsohn, Vivian |author2=Szumik, Claudia |author3=Leonardt, Sabrina |author4=Rizzo, Florencia |year=2015 |title=The "Hidden" Code: Coding and Classifying in Rock Art: The Case of Northwestern Patagonia |journal=Journal of Archaeological Method and Theory |volume= 23|issue=2 |pages= 500–519|doi=10.1007/s10816-015-9249-8 |s2cid=144009669 |url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/276477676|hdl=11336/40636 |hdl-access=free }}</ref> Some Europeans settled in the area in the early twentieth century, beginning in 1914 there were settlers in the upper reaches of the [[Cisnes River]]. The grant for the [[estancia]] "Sociedad Ganadera Cisnes" of over {{Convert|1000|sqkm|sqmi|0}} was made in the early 1920s. It encompassed almost all of what is now the La Tapera district. Nonetheless, the area was not formally organized until the establishment of a settlement, Villa La Tapera, on 26 December 1926.<ref name="Hist" />

In 1936 land for the establishment of the town of Lago Verde was set aside. In 1950, a young Frenchman, Edward Simon. purchased several hectares in the town and established a hotel, the "Cacique Blanco", with the ranching and farming activities to support it. In 1966 Villa La Tapera achieved town status. In 1979 the commune of Lago Verde was created. In 1981 Lago Verde became a municipality. And in 1982 Villa Amengual was founded.<ref name="Hist" />

In 1992 the road to [[La Junta, Aysén|La Junta]] was completed with a bridge over the Rosselot River.  This increased communications within the region.<ref name="Hist" />

According to the 2012 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Lago Verde has 869 inhabitants, with an area of {{convert|5622.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} which makes the commune an entirely [[rural area]].<ref name="INE" /> The population of the commune has been declining; it was 1,062 in the 2002 census, and 1,168 in the 1992 census.<ref name="Comuna-Plan" />

==Administration==
As a commune, Lago Verde is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="Munitel" /><ref name="Official" /> The 2012-2016 alcalde is Nelson Opazo López.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Presentación del Alcalde |publisher=Municipality of Lago Verde |language=es |url=http://lagoverdeaysen.cl/2015/?page_id=70 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150821171648/http://lagoverdeaysen.cl/2015/?page_id=70 |archivedate=21 August 2015 |url-status=live}}</ref> The previous governor was Gaspar Aldea Cadagán.<ref name="Comuna-Plan" />

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Lago Verde is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Alinco ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and David Sandoval ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 59th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Aisén Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Antonio Horvath Kiss (RN) and Patricio Walker Prieto (PDC) as part of the 18th senatorial constituency (Aisén Region).
