'''José María Vargas Municipality''' is one of the 29 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] that makes up the western [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Táchira]] and, according to the [[2011 Venezuelean census]], the municipality had a population of 10,230. The mayor of the municipality is Carlos Pérez Chacón of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela|PSUV]] party since August 2025 and the town of [[El Cobre, Táchira|El Cobre]] is the municipal seat of the José María Vargas Municipality.

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the José María Vargas Municipality was 10,230 people, accounting for 0.85% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref> Under half (43.9%) of the municipality's population is in the town of [[El Cobre, Táchira|El Cobre]], the [[municipal seat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/tachira/2013__jos%C3%A9_mar%C3%ADa_vargas/|title=Urban Localities in José María Vargas (Táchira, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of José María Vargas as 10,816 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.7% since 2011 and showing a population density of 127.2 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2003__ayacucho/|title=José María Vargas (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 52.3% men (5,162) and 47.7% women (4,717). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 65.4% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 28.9% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 5.8%. The municipality is more rural than urbanized, with 56.1% of inhabitants (5,542) living in [[rural areas]] compared to 43.9% (4,337) in [[urban centers]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (60%) and [[Mestizo]] (38.7%). Minority groups included 0.4% [[Afro-Venezuelans]] and 0.8% belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 92.2%, with 623 inhabitants of José María Vargas not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>
