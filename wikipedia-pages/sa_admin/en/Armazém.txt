'''Armazém''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. Is located at latitude 28º15'43" south and longitude 49º01'03" west, with an elevation of 30 meters. The city's streets are embellished with manicured gardens. Armazém has an area of 138.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and was emancipated from the city of [[Tubarão]] on 19 December 1958.
