'''Juan de Mena''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of [[Cordillera, Paraguay|Cordillera Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It is named after the Spanish Renaissance poet [[Juan de Mena]], and is located 88 metres above sea level.{{Citation needed|date=June 2025}} The district is adjacent to the [[Arroyos y Esteros]] district,<ref>{{Cite news |date=February 19, 2021 |title=Investigadores analizan cadena de producción de caña de azúcar orgánica |trans-title=Researchers analyze organic sugar cane production chain |url=https://www.ip.gov.py/ip/2021/02/19/investigadores-analizan-cadena-de-produccion-de-cana-de-azucar-organica/ |access-date=June 20, 2025 |work= |publisher=Agencia de Información Paraguaya |language=es}}</ref> and is surrounded by the lowlands of the [[Manduvirá River|Manduvira]], [[Yhaguy River|Yhaguy]], and Hondo rivers.{{Citation needed|date=June 2025}}

== Demographics ==
As of the 2022 census, there were 6,341 people in the district: 3,163 (49.9%) were male and 3,178 (50.1%) were female, with a median age of 28.<ref name="INE" />
