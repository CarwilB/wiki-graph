'''San Víctor''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Espaillat Province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113|title=Listado de Codigos de Provincias, Municipio y Distritos Municipales, Actualizada a Junio 20 del 2006|author=Oficina Nacional de Estadistica, Departamento de Cartografia, Division de Limites y Linderos|language=Spanish|accessdate=2007-01-24|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070314215735/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113|archivedate=2007-03-14}}</ref> The District Municipality became a municipality on May 23, 2013.<ref>http://www.diarioacontecer.com/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=158:san-victor-se-convierte-en-el-5to-municipios-de-la-provincia-espaillat&catid=4:actualidad&Itemid=8 Nuevo municipio separado de Municipio Moca</ref>

As of the 2012 census estimate, the municipality, then a municipal district of the [[Moca, Dominican Republic|Moca]] municipality, had 79,851 inhabitants; 32,501 living in the [[List of cities in the Dominican Republic|city]] itself, and 47,350 in its rural [[district]]s (''secciones'').
