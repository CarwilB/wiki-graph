The '''Obispos Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 37,493.<ref name="geohive">{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx|title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics|publisher=geohive.com|accessdate=2016-06-15}}</ref> The town of [[Obispos]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Obispos Municipality.<ref name="ine">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Obispos Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 31,694 (up from 26,595 in 2000). This amounts to 4.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|18.1|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Obispos Municipality is Luis Manuel Zambrano Volcan, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 54% of the vote.<ref name="cne">{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=5&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio|publisher=cne.gob.ve|accessdate=2016-06-15}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Obispos, El Real, La Luz, and Los Guasimitos.<ref name="ine"/>
