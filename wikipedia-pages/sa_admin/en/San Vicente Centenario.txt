'''San Vicente Centenario''' ({{IPA|es|sam biˈsente senteˈnaɾjo}}) is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|40.8|km2}} and had a population of 3,601 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. It was officially established on 18 January 1922.

== History ==
San Vicente Centenario was officially created as a municipality on 18 January 1922.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de San Vicente Centenario, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/San-Vicente-Centenario.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-san-Vicente-centenario/|title=Municipality of San Vicente Centenario|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San Vicente Centenario is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[San Nicolás, Santa Bárbara|San Nicolás]] to the north, [[Arada, Honduras|Arada]] to the south and west, and [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] to the east.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|40.8|km2}} and has an average elevation of {{cvt|243|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/>

== Administrative divisions ==
The municipality comprises a single aldea (villages) and its associated caseríos (hamlets).<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/><ref name="INE2013">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/275-Santa-Barbara-San-Vicente-Centenario.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 275, Municipio de San Vicente Centenario, Departamento de Santa Bárbara|date=February 2025|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=20}}

== Demographics ==
As per the 2013 census, San Vicente Centenario had a total population of 3,601 inhabitants, making it the smallest municipality by population in the department.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} The population consisted of 1,834 (50.9%) males and 1,767 (49.1%) females, with a masculinity index of 103.7 men per 100 women. Of the total population, 3,326 (92.4%) lived in the urban while 275 (7.6%) lived in the rural areas. The population density was 88.26 inhabitants per km<sup>2</sup>. The intercensal population growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 2.7% per year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1625__san_vicente_centenario/|title=San Vicente Centenario|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 32.2% of the population were aged 0–14, 61.2% were aged 15–64, the highest in the department, and 6.6% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 23.0 years and the mean age was 27.7 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (2,442 persons), the 2013 census recorded an illiteracy rate of 22.5%, slightly higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} Of the total population, 70.85% were [[Mestizo]], 26.35% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 2.39% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.39% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.03% others.<ref>{{cite report|url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=Bases de datos en línea|lang=es|access-date=1 June 2020}}</ref> The municipality had 914 occupied private dwellings, with an average household size of 3.9 persons, the second lowest in the department.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=23,27}}
