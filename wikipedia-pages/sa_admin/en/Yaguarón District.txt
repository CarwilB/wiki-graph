'''Yaguarón''' is a [[district]] in [[Paraguarí Department]], [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|253|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 29,242 individuals.

==History==
Yaguarón was established as a settlement of indigenous people in 1586. As per writer [[:es:Narciso Ramón Colmán|Narciso Ramón Colmán]], Yaguarón was occupied by the [[Guaraní people]], and was founded in 1539 by [[Domingo Martínez de Irala]]. As accurate documentation does not exist regarding its creation, the municipal authorities set as a foundational date on 10 October, according to municipal ordinance No. 16/2016. [[Franciscan]] missionaries Luis de Bolaños and Alonso de San Buenaventura established a mission that was installed around the Buenaventura temple whose construction had begun in 1755 and culminated in 1772.<ref>{{cite web|title=Preparan desfile estudiantil y expo por los 483 años de fundación de Yaguarón|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2022/10/04/preparan-desfile-estudiantil-y-expo-por-los-483-anos-de-fundacion-de-yaguaron/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Student parade and expo prepared for the 483rd anniversary of the founding of Yaguarón}}</ref>

==Geography==
Yaguairón s a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Paraguarí Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|253|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/paraguar%C3%AD/0915__yaguar%C3%B3n/|title=Yaguairón District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> In 2020, plans were announced to restore forested areas in Yaguarón.<ref name="g148">{{cite book | last1=Quevedo-Fernández | first1=Maria Laura | last2=Silva-Imas | first2=Haroldo Nicolás | last3=Pérez-de-Molas | first3=Lidia Florencia | last4=Gamarra-Ruiz-Díaz | first4=Lila Mabel | last5=González | first5=Alba Liz | last6=Amarilla-Rodríguez | first6=Stella Mary | last7=Villalba-Marin | first7=Lucia Janet | title=Ecological Connectivity of Forest Ecosystems | chapter=Paraguay: Toward a Landscape Restoration of the Paraguayan Atlantic Forest | publisher=Springer Nature Switzerland | publication-place=Cham | date=2025 | isbn=978-3-031-82205-6 | doi=10.1007/978-3-031-82206-3_33 | doi-access=free | page=591–608}}</ref>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|88.04|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|27.16|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|15.87|cm}} and has 139.37 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/central/yaguaron|title=Yaguairón|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, Yaguarón district had a population of 29,242 inhabitants of which 14,657 were males and 14,585 were females. About 64.9% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (35.1%) lived in urban areas. About 25.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 10.5% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

==Culture==
The Temple of San Buenaventura in Yaguarón is one of Paraguay’s most important historical and cultural monuments, combining [[Guaraní people|Guarani]] and [[Franciscan]] heritage. It was built in the 18th century, and is renowned for its Baroque-Guaraní architecture with ornate wooden carvings, religious sculptures, and gilded altarpieces. Its bell tower served for both religious purposes and as a lookout point during periods of conflict. The church remains a major pilgrimage destination where thousands gather annually for religious celebrations honoring the Virgin of the Assumption. It was declared as a historical heritage monument by Law No. 2206/2003.<ref name="UHYaguaronHistoria">{{cite web|title=La rica historia del templo de Yaguarón, un tesoro histórico y cultural del Paraguay|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/la-rica-historia-del-templo-yaguaron-un-tesoro-historico-y-cultural-del-paraguay|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=The rich history of the Yaguarón temple, a historical and cultural treasure of Paraguay}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Solicitan reconocimiento de la Unesco|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2020/07/23/solicitan-reconocimiento-de-la-unesco/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Request submitted for UNESCO recognition}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Iglesia de Yaguarón forma parte de circuito turístico|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/articulos/iglesia-de-yaguaron-forma-parte-de-circuito-turistico-297043.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Yaguarón church forms part of tourist circuit}}</ref>
