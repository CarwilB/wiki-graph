'''Iracema''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|iɾaˈsemɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the midwest of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 12,296 (2020) and its area is 14,413&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2PK|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Roraima {{!}} Iracema|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-06-15}}</ref> Iracema started as an agricultural community. It became an independent municipality in 1994.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/iracema/historico |title=Iracema |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The town is located on the [[BR-174]] highway, and is known for its waterfall.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.oliberal.com/conexaoamz/cachoeiras-da-amazonia-um-lado-da-floresta-que-voce-precisa-conhecer-1.242012|title=Cachoeiras na Amazônia: deleite para os olhos e purificação para as almas|website=OLiberal|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt|archive-date=15 April 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210415092826/https://www.oliberal.com/conexaoamz/cachoeiras-da-amazonia-um-lado-da-floresta-que-voce-precisa-conhecer-1.242012|url-status=dead}}</ref>
