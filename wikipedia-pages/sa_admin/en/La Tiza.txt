'''La Tiza''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Las Tablas District]], [[Los Santos Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,702 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,167; its population as of 2000 was 1,662.<ref name=inec/>

== Climate ==
The average temperature is 25 °C. The warmest month is March, at 28 °C, and the coolest is October, at 22 °C. The average rainfall is 2,084 mm per year. The wettest month is June, at 363 mm of rain, and the driest is March, at 1 mm.
