'''Nueva Frontera''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It was officially established on 1 August 1998. It covers an area of {{cvt|155.46|km2}} and had a population of 13,069 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census.

== History ==
Nueva Frontera was created by Decree No. 192-97 of the [[National Congress (Honduras)|National Congress]] of Honduras, which was published in the official gazette on 17 December 1997. The decree established the municipality under the given name, which was earlier known as "Trascerros" based on a suggestion by Solomón Fajardo.<ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Nueva Frontera|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-nueva-frontera/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Nueva Frontera|url=https://www.edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Nueva-Frontera.html|lang=es|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The municipality was officially established on 1 August 1998.<ref name="Edu"/><ref>{{cite web| url=http://rds.hn/index.php?documento=1390 | title=Perfil del Municipio de Nueva Frontera | access-date=1 June 2014| work=fuente es Alcaldía Municipal | date=21 May 2003 |lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Nueva Frontera is a municipality in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Azacualpa]] to the north, [[Macuelizo]] to the south and east, and [[Florida, Copán|Florida]] to the west. It also shares land border with Guatemala to the west.<ref name="Edu"/> The municipality is spread over an area of {{cvt|155.46|km2}}, about {{cvt|87|km}} from [[San Pedro Sula]] and {{cvt|326|km}} from [[Tegucigalpa]].<ref name="Edu"/>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Nueva Frontera had a population of 13,069 inhabitants, of whom 5,418 were male and 6,578 were female. It had an urban population of 4,565 inhabitants and a rural population was 8,503 inhabitants.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1628__nueva_frontera/|title=Nueva Frontera|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} By broad age group, 41.5% of the population were aged 0–14, 53.3% were aged 15–64, and 5.3% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 18.4 years and the mean age was 24.1 years.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} Of the population, 0.28% were classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> Among the population aged 15 and over, the illiteracy rate was 28.4%, which was higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}}
