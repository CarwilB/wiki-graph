'''Gongogi''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. It covers {{convert|195.39|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 6,985 with a population density of 41 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/> It was named after the [[Gongogi River]] which forms the southern border of the municipality.
