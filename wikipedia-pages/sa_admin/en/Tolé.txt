'''Tolé  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tolé District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It is the seat of Tolé District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|76.9|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,240 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|42.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 5,292; its population as of 2000 was 3,156.<ref name=inec/>
