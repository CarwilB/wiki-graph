'''La Magdalena''' is an [[Electoral district|electoral parish]] (parroquia electorale urban) or district of [[Quito]], the [[capital city]] of [[Ecuador]].  The parish was established as a result of the October 2004{{when|date=December 2012}} [[Elections in Ecuador|political elections]] when the city was divided into 19 urban electoral parishes.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.lonelyplanet.com/worldguide/ecuador-and-the-galapagos-islands/quito/history |title=Lonely Planet Ecuador |publisher=lonelyplanet.com |access-date=2008-04-12 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080929061032/http://www.lonelyplanet.com/worldguide/ecuador-and-the-galapagos-islands/quito/history |archive-date=2008-09-29 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
