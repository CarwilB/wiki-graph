'''San Pablo''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[Osorno Province]] in the [[Los Lagos Region]]. It is located 27&nbsp;km from the city and provincial capital of [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]]. The commune is mostly [[rural]]. Its activity is almost entirely agricultural and ranching. In the commune also performs [[Agritourism]].

== History ==
San Pablo was in the [[Osorno Province|Department of Osorno]] and administratively dependent of the [[Municipality]] of Osorno. On 22 December 1891, the Municipal Building decree establishing the [[City]] of San Pablo, based in San Pablo, whose territory corresponded to the subdelegations 8a, 9a, 10a, 11a and 12a of the Department of Osorno. The first [[mayor]] of San Pablo was Guillermo Heufemann.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-24975.html San Pablo] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090108160720/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-24975.html |date=2009-01-08 }}</ref>

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Pablo spans an area of {{convert|637.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 10,162 inhabitants (5,210 men and 4,952 women). Of these, 3,478 (34.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,684 (65.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 9.1% (1016 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Pablo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Omar Alvarado.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Pablo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Javier Hernández ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Sergio Ojeda ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 55th electoral district, together with [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]] and [[San Juan de la Costa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Kuschel Silva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
