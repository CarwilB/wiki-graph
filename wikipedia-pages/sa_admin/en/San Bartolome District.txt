'''San Bartolome District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Huarochirí Province]], located in the [[Department of Lima]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the Law No. 12001 in November 9, 1953, during the presidency of [[Manuel A. Odría]]. It encompasses an area of 43.91&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
