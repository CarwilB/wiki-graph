'''El Cenepa''' is a district of the province of [[Condorcanqui]] in [[Peru]]. The [[district]] of '''El Cenepa''', created on September 1, 1941 by Law Nº 9364, belongs to the [[province]] of [[Condorcanqui]], department of [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas]]. It has an area of 5,558&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which represents 24% of the territory of [[Alto Marañón]]. El Cenepa has 8,363 inhabitants, and a population density of 1.5 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.

Localities within the district include: 
* [[Shamatak]]
