'''Muisne''' is a coastal town in the southwest of the province of [[Esmeraldas Province|Esmeraldas]] in northwestern [[Ecuador]], with a population of 5,574 in the last Ecuadorian census in 2022.<ref name="pop"/> It is the seat of the namesake canton.

The town is located on the northern tip of the small coastal island known as Isla de Muisne in the [[Ensenada de Mompiche]] bay. The channel of the [[Muisne River]] estuary between the mainland and the eastern side of the island is crossed mainly by small [[outboard motor|outboard-motor]] [[boat]]s for passengers and by [[barge]]s for cars and small trucks.

On the western side of the island there is a beach which attracts both local and foreign tourists.

The canton of Muisne contains several other parroquias (small villages or parishes), including Pedro Carlo, San Francisco del Cabo (the oldest parish), Bunche, Union of Daule, Tola, Maldonado, Bellavista, and [[Daule, Esmeraldas, Ecuador|Daule]].
