'''Lobos Partido''' is a [[partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 33,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1725|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Lobos]], {{convert|115|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
