'''Ibarra Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Imbabura Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Ibarra, Ecuador|Ibarra]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 153,256<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and 181,175 in the 2010 census.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ecuador: Municipal Divisions |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/ecuador-parish-admin.php |access-date=2024-08-28}}</ref> The area of the canton is {{convert|1137|sqkm|sqmi}}.

Ibarra is located in the [[Andes]] region of northern Ecuador. The capital city is situated at an altitude of {{convert|2225|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>

The canton is divided into seven parishes: [[Ambuquí]] (Ampuki), Angochagua (Ankuchawa), Carolina (Karulina), [[La Esperanza, Ecuador|La Esperanza]], Lita, Salinas, [[San Antonio de Ibarra]].<ref>https://www.citypopulation.de-php/ecuador-parish-admin.php {{Dead link|date=March 2022}}</ref>
