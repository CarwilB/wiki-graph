'''Xexéu''' is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]] {{convert|143|km|abbr=on}} from the state capital [[Recife]]. It was first municipality in Latin America to use ecological asphalt.

==Geography==

* State: [[Pernambuco]]
* Region: [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries: [[Palmares, Pernambuco|Palmares]]  ('''N''');  [[Alagoas]]  ('''S''');  [[Maraial]]  ('''W'''); [[Água Preta]]   ('''E''')
* Area: {{convert|110.8|km2|abbr=on}}
* Elevation: {{convert|200|m|abbr=on}}
* Hydrography: [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] river
* Vegetation: [[Subperenifólia]] forest 
* Climate: Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature: {{convert|24.5|C|F|abbr=on}}
* Distance to [[Recife]]: {{convert|143|km|abbr=on}}
* Population - 11,699 (2025)<ref name="IBGE" />

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Xexéu are based in agriculture, especially [[sugarcane]].
