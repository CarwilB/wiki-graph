'''Urubamba District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Urubamba Province|Urubamba]] province in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Urupampa mountain range|Urupampa]] mountain range traverses the district. One of highest peaks of the district is [[Ch'iqun]] at {{convert|5530|m|ft|0|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL map of the Urubamba Province (Cusco Region)</ref>

* Aqu Q'asa
* [[Puka Q'asa (Urubamba)|Puka Q'asa]]
* [[Pumawanka|Pumawank'a]]
* [[Qhapaq Saya]]
* [[Sut'uq]]
* Taruka Kancha
* T'uruhana
* Uman Urqu
