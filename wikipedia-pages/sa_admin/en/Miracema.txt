'''Miracema''' ({{IPAc-en|ˌ|m|iː|r|ə|ˈ|s|ɛ|m|ə}} {{respell|MEE|rə|SEH|mə}}; {{IPA|pt|miɾɐˈsemɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro state]]'s northwestern region, bordering [[Minas Gerais]] state, [[Brazil]] and with a population of 27,154 inhabitants living in an area of 302.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its main economic activities are agriculture and milk production.

==History==
In the early 20th century, [[coffee]] was the city's most important economic activity, and Rio de Janeiro state was the greatest coffee producer of the country.

==Sports==
Brazilian [[football (soccer)|football]] manager [[Aymoré Moreira]], and his brothers, [[Zezé Moreira]] and [[Cruzeiro Esporte Clube#Former coaches|Ayrton Moreira]], who were also managers, were born in the city. ''Miracema Futebol Clube'' is a city's football club.
