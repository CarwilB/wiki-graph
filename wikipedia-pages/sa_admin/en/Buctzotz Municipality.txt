'''Buctzotz Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "dress made of hair") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 543.45&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|95|km}} northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>
It contains several churches and a hospital, Centre de Salud Buctzotz, in the eastern part of the main town.

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but during the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], it became part of the [[encomienda]] system and [[Francisco de Montejo the Younger]] was the first encomendero.<ref name="Rugeley (2014)">{{cite book|last1=Rugeley|first1=Sergio Quezada; translated by Terry|title=Maya lords and lordship : the formation of colonial society in Yucatán, 1350-1600|date=2014|publisher=University of Oklahoma Press|location=Norman|isbn=978-0-806-14579-2|page=130 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=P2W9AgAAQBAJ&pg=PA130|access-date=7 August 2015}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the coastal region partition of [[Izamal Municipality]]. In 1867 it was transferred to the [[Temax Municipality]] and in 1988 was confirmed as head of its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|access-date=6 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=es|date=1996|pages=101, 110, 123|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archive-date=2015-12-23|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 1913, Buctzotz was the site of a battle of the [[Mexican Revolution|revolutionary]] forces under the command of the General Juan Campos.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has seven councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, public services, ecology, parks, public sanitation, nomenclature and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Buctzotz|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=6 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Local festivals==
Every year from 8 to 11 January the town celebrates a festival in honor of the town patroness, the Immaculate Conception and hosts a pilgrimage in her honor between 22 and 30 August. There is also a fiesta held for Santa Clara from 13 to 25 August held annually.<ref name="municipios"/>
