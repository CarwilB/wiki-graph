'''Carmen del Paraná''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is located in the southern part of the country, about 350 kilometers south of the national capital, [[Asunción]] and 35 kilometers northwest of the department capital, [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]]. Founded in 1843 by [[Carlos Antonio López]] and [[Mariano Roque Alonso]], the district is historically revered as the "Cradle of Independence of Paraguay" due to the pivotal [[Battle of Tacuarí]] in 1811.

Covering an area of 302.5 square kilometers, Carmen del Paraná has a predominantly [[urban area|urban]] and [[working age]] population of 6,417 inhabitants per the [[2022 Paraguayan census]]. Economically, the district is a major agricultural hub famously known as the "Capital of Rice," hosting three of Paraguay's largest rice processing mills. In recent years, infrastructure developments following the construction of the [[Yacyretá Dam]] have transformed the district into a popular summer destination, drawing thousands of tourists to its 10-kilometer waterfront and beaches.

==History==
[[File:Carmen del Parana Iglesia Grecorromana Ucraniana.jpg|thumb|Ukrainian Church in Carmen de Parana]]
Carmen del Paraná was founded on 24 April 1843 by decree of [[Carlos Antonio López]] and [[Mariano Roque Alonso]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://nanduti.com.py/carmen-del-parana-suspende-sus-festejos-por-los-183-anos-debido-al-pronostico-de-lluvias|title=Carmen del Paraná suspende sus festejos por los 183 años debido al pronóstico de lluvias|trans-title=Carmen del Paraná suspends its 183rd anniversary celebrations due to the rain forecast|lang=es|newspaper=[[Ñanduti (newspaper)|Ñanduti]]|date=24 April 2026|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> The district is known as the "Cradle of Independence of Paraguay" due to the [[Battle of Tacuarí]] that took place on 9 March 1811, which was a definitive step towards achieving the country's independence in May 1811.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.eby.gov.py/en-conmemoracion-a-la-batalla-de-tacuary-eby-inaugurara-plaza-historica/|title=En conmemoración a la batalla de Tacuary, EBY inaugurará plaza histórica|trans-title=In Commemoration of the Battle of Tacuary, EBY to Inaugurate Historical Plaza|lang=es|publisher=[[Yacyretá Binational Entity]]|date=6 March 2013|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to data from the [[General Directorate of Statistics of Paraguay]], the population of Carmen del Paraná grew from 6,165 inhabitants recorded in the [[2002 Paraguayan census]] to 6,417 inhabitants recorded in the [[2022 Paraguayan census]], representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.20% over the two decade period. Across the district's total area of 302.5 square kilometers, this population size yields a [[population density]] of 21.21 people per square kilometer. The 2022 census data revealed a highly balanced [[gender]] distribution, consisting of 3,210 males and 3,207 females, each comprising approximately 50% of the population. Structurally, the population is predominantly urban and [[working age]]. Approximately 70.1% (4,499 people) reside in [[urban centers]] compared to 29.9% (1,918 people) in [[rural areas]], while 64.5% (4,136 people) fall into the 15-64 age bracket, with children aged 0-14 making up 24.4% (1,566 people) and elderly residents aged 65 and older accounting for the remaining 11.1% (715 people).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/itap%C3%BAa/0707__carmen_del_paran%C3%A1/|title=Carmen del Paraná (District in Paraguay)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=13 December 2024|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

==Economy==
The inhabitants of Carmen del Paraná mainly work in the [[agriculture]], with extensive plantations of [[rice]], [[soybeans]], and [[wheat]], and in [[livestock]] on [[cattle ranch]]es.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://informacionpublica.paraguay.gov.py/public/1183043-Itapuacaracteristicaspdf-Itapuacaracteristicas.pdf|title=Departamento de Itapúa antecedentes históricos|trans-title=Itapúa Department: Historical Background|lang=es|website=Portal Unificado de Acceso a la Información Pública|publisher=[[Ministry of Information and Communication Technologies (Paraguay)|Ministry of Information and Communication Technologies]]|access-date=20 May 2026|quote=Carmen del Paraná se caracteriza por la producción de arroz; tiene una parte alta hacia el este que se utiliza para la producción de soja y trigo; tiene, además, campos ganaderos. [Carmen del Paraná is known for its rice production; it has a higher area to the east used for soybean and wheat production; it also has cattle ranches.]}}</ref> The district is considered the "Capital of Rice" due to three of the largest [[rice mill|rice processing mills]] in the country are located there, with the district being a major rice producer since 1940.<ref name="ABC7Apr13">{{cite news|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/empresas-y-negocios/carmen-del-parana-554537.html|title=Carmen del Paraná|lang=es|newspaper=[[ABC-TV (Paraguayan TV channel)|ABC]]|date=7 April 2013|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref>

Following infrastructure developments by the [[Yacyretá Binational Entity]] to compensate for lost territory due to the construction of the [[Yacyretá Dam]], the district emerged as a popular summer destination by 2013. Its 10-kilometer waterfront and three beaches attracted up to 15,000 weekend tourists, creating a shift toward a seasonal coastal lifestyle despite a local deficit in hotels and restaurants.<ref name="ABC7Apr13"/>
