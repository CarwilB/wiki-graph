'''Florentino Ameghino''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]]. It is located on the [[Atlantic]] coast of [[Argentina]]

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 1,484 inhabitants in an area of 16,088&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Camarones, Chubut|Camarones]], which is located around 1,654&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

The department is noted for its wealth of [[geology|geological]] and [[paleontology|paleontological]] features. Specimens from the region are on display in a number of museums worldwide.

The department is named in honour of [[Florentino Ameghino]] (September 18, 1854 – August 6, 1911), an Argentine [[natural history|naturalist]], [[paleontology|paleontologist]], [[anthropology|anthropologist]] and [[zoology|zoologist]]

==Economy==

The economy of Florentino Ameghino and its head town is dominated by tourism. People are attracted to the region by its lengthy unspoiled coastline, its geography and fauna, especially the penguin colonies.
