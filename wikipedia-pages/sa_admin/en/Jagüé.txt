'''Jagüé''' is a municipality and village in Vinchina Department, [[La Rioja Province (Argentina)|La Rioja Province]] in northwestern [[Argentina]].<ref>[http://www.mininterior.gov.ar/ Ministerio del Interior] {{in lang|es}}</ref>. Its population is 230 as of 2010.

Well-preserved [[fossil]] remains of ''[[Huayracursor jaguensis]]'', one of the earliest [[bagualosauria]]n [[Sauropodomorpha|sauropodomorph]] dinosaurs, were found here, and the species was named after the village of Jagüé in 2025.<ref name="Huayracursor">{{Cite journal |last1=Hechenleitner |first1=E.
