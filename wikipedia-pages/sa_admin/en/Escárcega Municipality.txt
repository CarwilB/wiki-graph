'''Escárcega''' is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Escárcega]].

==Geography==

The municipality of Escárcega borders to the north with the municipality of [[Champotón Municipality|Champotón]], 
to the east with the municipality of [[Calakmul (municipality)|Calakmul]], to the south with the municipality of [[Candelaria (municipality)|Candelaria]], and to the west with the municipality of [[Carmen (municipality)|Carmen]].

It covers 4,569.64&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, accounting for 8.0% of the state's total surface area.

==History==
The municipality was created on  1 January 1991. It took its name from its municipal seat, which was in turn named for [[Tlaxcala]]n railwayman [[Francisco Escárcega]], who built the [[Ferrocarril del Sureste]] that runs from [[Coatzacoalcos|Coatzacoalcos, Ver.]], to [[Campeche, Campeche|Campeche, Camp]].

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 54,184.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Escárcega|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=009|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=2 May 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180502211026/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=009|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Escárcega had a population of 29,477.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Escárcega, the municipality had 539 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: División del Norte (3,259), classified as urban, and La Libertad (1,462), Matamoros (1,453), [[Haro, Campeche|Haro]] (1,092), [[Don Samuel, Campeche|Don Samuel]] (1,081), and [[Altamira de Zináparo, Campeche|Altamira de Zináparo]] (1,040), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

== Transportation ==
Escárcega territory is a major hub of both roads and railways with these being the main driver of development in the region. Before their existence Escárcega was virtually depopulated.

=== Roads ===
The main roads connecting the municipality of Escárcega are Federal Highway 186 and Federal Highway 254. The Federal Highway 186 is the primary means of transportation and economic activity in the territory. The Federal Highway 254 is a small road that connects to 186.<ref name=Comunicaciones>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/camp/viascomunicacion.cfm?c=1217&e=04&CFID=2236814&CFTOKEN=47443121 |title = Mapa con Principales Carreteras |accessdate = 15 July 2008 |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20120306044508/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/camp/viascomunicacion.cfm?c=1217&e=04&CFID=2236814&CFTOKEN=47443121 |archive-date = 6 March 2012 |url-status = dead }}</ref>

The introduction of the railroad in the mid-1930s was the real engine of development in the state of Campeche where the present day municipality of Escárcega lies. The railway connected the Yucatán Peninsula with the rest of the country for the first time in the Mexico's history. The railway passes through the municipality of Escárcega southwest to northeast with the main station in the municipalities seat, Escárcega. To the north it connects with the city of Campeche and then Mérida, and southward with Tenosique, Tabasco. The total length of railways in the municipality is 6769&nbsp;km.<ref name=Comunicaciones />

Currently, Escárcega is home to the [[Escárcega railway station]], which is served by the [[Tren Maya]].
