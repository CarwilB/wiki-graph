'''O'Higgins''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Capitán Prat Province]], located around [[O'Higgins Lake]] in the south east of [[Aisén Region]]. The commune is administered by the municipality in [[Villa O'Higgins]], the principal settlement.

O'Higgins contains 4,338—or 28.5%—of Aysén Region's 15,240 [[hectare|ha]] of ''[[Sphagnum]]'' bogs.<ref name=VillHen>{{Cite book |title=Funciones y servicios ecosistémicos de las turberas de Sphagnum en la Región de Aysén |last=Villaroel |first=Dagoberto |publisher=[[Instituto de Investigación Agropecuaria]]:  Centro Regional de Investigación Tamel Aike |location=Coyhaique |pages=21-47 |language=Spanish |last2=Henríquez |first2=José Manuel |last3=Domínguez |first3=Erwin |last4=Silva |first4=Fernán |last5=Martínez |first5=MaríaPaz |last6=Báez |first6=John |editor-last=Domínguez |editor-first=Erwin |chapter=Distribución geográfica de turberas de Sphagnum en la región de Aysén |editor-last2=Martínez |editor-first2=María Paz}}</ref>

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], O'Higgins spans an area of {{convert|8182.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 463 inhabitants (277 men and 186 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 37.4% (126 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, O'Higgins is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is José Fica Gómez ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]).{{citation needed|date=July 2023}}

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], O'Higgins is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Alinco (PDC) and David Sandoval ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 59th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Aisén Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Antonio Horvath Kiss ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Patricio Walker Prieto (PDC) as part of the 18th senatorial constituency (Aisén Region).
