'''Cambuci''' ({{IPA|pt|kɐ̃buˈsi}}, ''Cambuci Berry'') is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 15,514 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|562|km2}}. The calculated population for 2009 was 18,256. It is a popular destination for tourists.<ref>[[Argentina Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
