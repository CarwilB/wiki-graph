'''Páez''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpaes}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Lengupá Province]].

The town is named after [[José Antonio Páez]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://situr.boyaca.gov.co/municipio-de-paez/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240218132748/https://situr.boyaca.gov.co/municipio-de-paez/ |title=Municipio de Páez |language=es |website=situr.boyaca.gov.co |date= |archive-date=18 February 2024}}</ref>
