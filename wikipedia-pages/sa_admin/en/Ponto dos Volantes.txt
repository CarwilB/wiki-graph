'''Ponto dos Volantes''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈpõtu duz voˈlɐ̃t͡ʃis}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. The population according to the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]] was 10,883 inhabitants in an area of 1,212.413 km² (approx. 468 sq mi).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ponto dos Volantes - panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/ponto-dos-volantes/panorama |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> It became a municipality in 1995.

== History ==

In the early 20th century, the site of what is now Ponto dos Volantes was a small rural settlement known as Barra dos Pilões, named for its location at the confluence of several [[Stream|streams]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Câmara Municipal de Ponto dos Volantes - História da Câmara |url=https://www.camarapontodosvolantes.mg.gov.br/historia |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=www.camarapontodosvolantes.mg.gov.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref> The modern town, however, only began to form with the construction of the [[BR-116 (Brazil highway)|BR-116]] highway in the 1940s. A small [[Bar (establishment)|bar]] was opened at the construction site, which soon became a popular meeting point for workers, local farmers, and prostitutes. Once the highway was completed, some of the workers decided to settle there permanently, founding the town and giving it the new name "Ponto dos Volantes"—which literally translates to "Drivers' Stop" or "Point of the Drivers."

Ponto dos Volantes was established as a settlement of [[Itinga]] in 1943. It was later elevated to a [[District#Brazil|district]] in 1985, and finally became an independent municipality in December 21, 1995.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Assembleia Legislativa de Minas |date=1995-12-21 |title=Lei nº 12.030, de 21/12/1995 - Texto Original - Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |url=https://www.almg.gov.br/legislacao-mineira/texto/LEI/12030/1995/ |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Portal da Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Ponto dos Volantes is located in the [[Jequitinhonha River|Jequitinhonha Valley]]. According to the 2017 regional division by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality is part of the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni|Intermediate]] and [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni|Immediate Geographic Regions of Teófilo Otoni]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2017">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |title=Base de dados por municípios das Regiões Geográficas Imediatas e Intermediárias do Brasil |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Prior to this, under the previous microregion and mesoregion system, it belonged to the microregion of Araçuaí, which was itself part of the [[Jequitinhonha (mesoregion)|mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

== Economy ==
The [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] of Ponto dos Volantes is approximately [[Brazilian real|R$]] 122.6 million, with public administration accounting for the largest share of value added at 52.4%. This is followed by the services sector (35%), agriculture and livestock (8.6%), and industry (4.1%).<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Economia de Ponto dos Volantes - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/ponto-dos-volantes---mg |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref> The GDP per capita in Ponto dos Volantes is R$ 10,000.<ref name=":0" />

== Culture ==
Ponto dos Volantes is the municipality where [[:pt:Isabel_Mendes_da_Cunha|Isabel Mendes da Cunha]] lived, a celebrated artisan renowned for her clay sculptures depicting women. Recognized as one of the most famous craftswomen in Minas Gerais, she became known as the "Dollmaker of the Jequitinhonha Valley."<ref>{{Cite web |title=A história de vida de Izabel Mendes da Cunha: A bonequeira do Vale do Jequitinhonha |url=https://museudapessoa.org/historia-de-vida/a-bonequeira-do-vale-do-jequitinhonha |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Museu da Pessoa |language=pt}}</ref> Each year, the district of Santana do Araçuaí hosts the Santana do Araçuaí Crafts Fair, considered the largest of its kind in the Jequitinhonha Valley. The fair serves as a vital platform, showcasing the work of local artisans and featuring performances by artists with national reputations.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Portal Minas |title=Portal Minas Gerais - XV FEIRA DO ARTESANATO |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/eventos/ponto-dos-volantes/xv-feira-do-artesanato |access-date=2025-10-21 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref>
