'''Três de Maio''' (''English: "Third of May"'') is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. It is located at {{coord|-27.752602|-54.243707}}.

It has an area of 422,2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located 343 m over [[sea level]]. As of 2020 its population was estimated to be 23,876 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/tres-de-maio/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Its climate is mild with temperatures of 20&nbsp;°C (68&nbsp;°F) to 35&nbsp;°C (95&nbsp;°F) in summer and 0 (32&nbsp;°F) to 15&nbsp;°C (59&nbsp;°F) in winter.
