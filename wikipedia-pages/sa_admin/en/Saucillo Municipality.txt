''' Saucillo''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Saucillo]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|2,116.16|km2}}.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 32,325,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Saucillo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=062|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 28,508 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title= Saucillo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08062a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Saucillo had a population of 11,004.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Saucillo, the municipality had 451 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Naica, Chihuahua|Naica]] (4,938), [[Las Varas, Chihuahua|Las Varas]] (2,410), classified as urban, and [[Estación Conchos, Chihuahua|Estación Conchos]] (1,670), and [[Orranteño, Chihuahua|Orranteño]] (1,641), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
