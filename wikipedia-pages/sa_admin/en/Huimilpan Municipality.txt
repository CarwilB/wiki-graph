'''Huimilpan''' is a [[Municipalities of Querétaro|municipality]] in the central [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Querétaro]].<ref>{{cite web |title=QUERÉTARO |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/queretaro/ |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517170314/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/queretaro/ |archivedate=May 17, 2011 |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]}}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of 388.1 km². As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 36,808.--> The municipal seat is at [[Huimilpan]]. Other populated places in Huimilpan include [[Apapátaro]].
