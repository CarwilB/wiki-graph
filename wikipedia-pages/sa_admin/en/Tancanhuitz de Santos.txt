'''Tancanhuitz''' is a town and one of the 58 [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipalities]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]] in central [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=2023-04-17 |language=es |title=Estado De San Luis Potosí |archive-date=September 19, 2004 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040919125340/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |url-status=unfit |date=2002}}</ref>  It is located in the southeastern part of the state, approximately {{convert|330|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the city of [[San Luis Potosí, San Luis Potosí|San Luis Potosí]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|134.05|km2}}.

== Name ==
The name Tancanhuitz comes from the [[Wastek language]], and means ''Place of Flowers'' or ''Canoe of yellow flowers''.<ref name="pacificosur">{{cite web|title=División Municipal|url=http://pacificosur.ciesas.edu.mx/diagnosticoestatal/slp/conte02.html|publisher=Pacificosur|accessdate=November 29, 2008|language=es|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20090221192724/http://pacificosur.ciesas.edu.mx/diagnosticoestatal/slp/conte02.html|archivedate=February 21, 2009}}</ref>

The name Tancanhuitz was already in use by the time of the 1826 constitution of San Luis Potosí, which named Tancanhuitz as one of ten sections of the state.<ref name="inafed">{{cite web|title=Tancanhuitz de Santos|url=http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM24sanluispotosi/municipios/24012a.html|publisher=INAFED Instituto para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal|accessdate=November 6, 2018|language=es|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20180501084012/http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM24sanluispotosi/municipios/24012a.html#|archivedate=2018-05-01|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="constitucion1826">{{cite web|title=Constitucion Política del Estado Libre de S. Luis Potosí (1826)|year = 1826|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=XTsbVMINn58C|accessdate=November 6, 2018|language=es|quote=[Art.] 7. En lo sucesivo se dividirá el Estado en los partidos siguientes: á saber, en los de Catorce, Guadalcazar, San Luis, Santa Maria del Rio, Ojo-caliente, Rioverde, Tancanhuitz, Valle del Maiz, Venado, y Villa de Valles.}}</ref> In 1932, the state government established the location as a city with the name '''Pedro Antonio Santos'''.<ref name="inafed"/>  The name was officially changed to '''Ciudad Santos''' in 1975, then to '''Tancanhuitz de Santos''' in 1981.<ref name="inafed"/>  Another change in 2003 established the current official name as '''Tancanhuitz'''.<ref name="pacificosur"/><ref name="inafed"/>
==Demography==
As of the 2005 census, it had a total population of 20,495, of which 10,180 were men and 10,315 were women.<ref>{{dead link|date=November 2018}}{{cite web|title=Principales resultados por localidad 2005 (ITER)|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/default.asp?s=est&c=10395|publisher=INEGI|accessdate=November 29, 2008|language=es|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110613185456/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/default.asp?s=est&c=10395|archivedate=June 13, 2011}}</ref>

== Geography ==
=== Location ===
Tancanhuitz is located in the southeastern part of the state, at 21° 36’ northern latitude, and 98° 58’ western longitude.  It is at an average height of 200 meters above sea level.

The municipality is bordered by the municipalities of [[Aquismón]] and [[Tanlajás]] to the north, [[Tanlajás]] and [[San Antonio, San Luis Potosí|San Antonio]] to the east, [[Huehuetlán]] and [[Coxcatlán Municipality, San Luis Potosí|Coxcatlán]] to the south, and [[Aquismón]] to the west.

=== Orography and Hydrography ===
The territory covered by the municipality is mostly rugged, although there is a small flat region to the north of the municipal capital. *The geology of the region is classified as [[Mesozoic]],* and the land is used primarily for raising livestock, forestry, and agriculture.<ref>{{cite web|title=Elevaciones Principales|url=http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/slp/eleva.cfm|publisher=INEGI|accessdate=November 29, 2008|language=es|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20090221153850/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/slp/eleva.cfm|archivedate=February 21, 2009}}</ref> The municipality belongs to the Panuco hydrologic region.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mapa de Regiones Hidrológicas|url=http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/slp/rh.cfm?c=444&e=24|publisher=INEGI|accessdate=November 29, 2008|language=es|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20090207211637/http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/slp/rh.cfm?c=444&e=24|archivedate=February 7, 2009}}</ref>  Its water resources are supplied by the [[Oxitipa River|Oxitipa]], [[Tampaón River|Tampaón]], and [[Coy River|Coy]] Rivers.  There are also some seasonal creeks there, such as the Tancanhuitz, as well as several springs.
