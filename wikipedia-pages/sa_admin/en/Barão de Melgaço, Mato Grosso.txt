The municipality contains 43% of the {{convert|108960|ha|adj=on}} [[Encontro das Águas State Park]], created in 2004.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Encontro das Águas – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PES Encontro das Águas|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/2581|access-date=2016-08-02}}</ref>

The city is named for [[Augusto Leverger, Baron of Melgaço]], a [[Brazilian Navy]] admiral during the [[Paraguayan War]].<ref name="City of Barão de Melgaço">{{cite web|url=https://digital.amm.org.br/Barao-de-melgaco |title=Dados Gerais |publisher= City Hall of Barão de Melgaço |access-date=March 10, 2024 |language=pt }}</ref>
