'''San Isidro''' ({{IPA|es|san iˈsiðɾo}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Isidro municipality had a population of 4,387. Of these, 91.18% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (90.77% [[Lenca]]), 7.20% [[Mestizo]], 1.32% [[White Latin Americans|White]] and 0.30% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
