'''Canindé''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Ceará]]. It is the 11th most populous city in the state. It is known for being religious, especially during the annual Saint Francis Party that lasts an entire week. The municipality receives millions of tourists every year, and the most visited places are the Saint Francis statue and the Saint Francis Church.

==History==

[[File:Prédio Vicentino, Canindé, Ceará, Brasil .JPG|thumb|left|Vincentino Building.]]
[[File:Canindé 03.JPG|thumb|left|Canindé City Hall]]
{{See also|History of Brazil}}

The Curu River and the Choró River were inhabited by Native Brazilians, the Tapuia. After the 17th century, Portuguese colonizers started habituating the land.

In 1775, Xavier de Medeiros, right at the border of the Canindé River, started building a church for Saint Francis. Nowadays, the church is at the same place; however, it was totally reformulated by the Italian architect Antonio Mazzoti and the painter George Kau. In 1818, Canindé became a village, and on July 29, 1846, the Municipality of Canindé was declared.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.santuariodecaninde.com/caninde/historia/|title = História}}</ref>

==Geography==

{{See also|Geography of Brazil}}

===climate===

Canindé has a tropical semiarid climate with temperatures that goes from 25&nbsp;°C to 32&nbsp;°C <ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ipece.ce.gov.br/publicacoes/perfil_basico/pbm-2010/Caninde.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-11-06 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141106133912/http://www.ipece.ce.gov.br/publicacoes/perfil_basico/pbm-2010/Caninde.pdf |archive-date=2014-11-06 }}</ref>

=== Soil ===
The soil in Canindé is composed of non-calcium type (55.14%), litholic (12.52%,), and others (4.04%).

===Fauna===
Canindé has a semiarid climate and so most of its fauna is composed of animals that are well adapted to it like Canindé birds, armadillos, lizards and sparrow-hawks.

==Politics==

On June 21, 1847 it was installed the city building in Canindé Downtown.

==Education==
[[File:Ifce caninde maquete.jpg|thumb|left|IFCE Canindé.]]

Canindé has three public high schools: the Paulo Sarasate School, Frei Policarpo School and the School of Professional Education Capelão Frei Orlando<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.crede07.seduc.ce.gov.br/index.php/municipios/caninde |title=Governo do Estado do Ceará |access-date=2014-11-06 |archive-date=2014-11-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141106144945/http://www.crede07.seduc.ce.gov.br/index.php/municipios/caninde |url-status=dead }}</ref> and one private high school, theCNEC-Canindé. The city also host a headquarters of the Federal Institute of Education (IFCE).
