'''Calvillo''' ({{IPA|es|kalˈβiʝo|lang|Calvillo.ogg}}) is a [[Municipalities of Aguascalientes|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Aguascalientes]]. The city of [[Calvillo, Aguascalientes|Calvillo]], the second largest in the state, serves as the municipal seat. It is located to the western part of the state, at about 52 kilometers of the [[Aguascalientes (city)|city of Aguascalientes]]. It stands at  {{coord|21|51|N|102|43|W|}} in the southwestern corner of the state. It is bounded by [[Jalisco]] and [[Zacatecas]]. The municipality, which has an area of 931.26&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (359.56 sq mi), reported a population of 58,250 by 2020. The town of [[Ojocaliente]] is another significant community in the municipality. Calvillo is one of the richest, most prosperous municipalities in Aguascalientes.

During the French invasion of Mexico, the north part of the valley of Huajuacar in which Calvillo lays, was occupied by the French.  At the time, they intermixed with the descendants of Spaniards which already inhabited the south part of the valley. This fact made Calvillo the municipality with most Caucasian-origin inhabitants in the state, compared with the other municipalities.

Calvillo is the largest guava producer in Mexico and famous for its superb confectionery and liqueurs made from this fruit, which you can sample at the Guava Fair held during the first week of December.

The economy in Calvillo is centered on the guava, although there is other agriculture and a great deal of commerce.  Calvillo is better known as the world capital of the guava.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 54,136.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Calvillo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=003|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304131410/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=003|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Calvillo had a population of 19,742.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Calvillo, the municipality had 307 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Ojocaliente, Aguascalientes|Ojocaliente]] (6,914), classified as urban, and [[El Cuervero (Cuerveros), Aguascalientes|El Cuervero (Cuerveros)]] (2,350), [[Valle Huejúcar (Fraccionamiento Popular), Aguascalientes|Valle Huejúcar (Fraccionamiento Popular)]] (1,991), [[El Terrero de la Labor|La Labor]] (1,988), [[Malpaso, Aguascalientes|Malpaso]] (1,697), [[San Tadeo, Aguascalientes|San Tadeo]] (1,507), [[Chiquihuitero (San Isidro), Aguascalientes|Chiquihuitero (San Isidro)]] (1,318), [[La Panadera, Aguascalientes|La Panadera]] (1,212), [[Mesa Grande, Aguascalientes|Mesa Grande]] (1,149), [[Jaltiche de Arriba, Aguascalientes|Jaltiche de Arriba]] (1,114), and [[Crucero las Pilas, Aguascalientes|Crucero las Pilas]] (1,031), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
