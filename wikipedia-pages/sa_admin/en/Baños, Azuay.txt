Baños is a parish located to the south west in the canton Cuenca, province of Azuay, Ecuador. The parish has an area of 327.3 km<sup>2</sup> and the altitude varies from 2050 to 4200 meters above sea level, with an average temperature of 14°C. It is located in the foothills of the mountain range of [[El Cajas National Park|El Cajas]], surrounded by high-altitude areas as: Chanchán, Nero, Yanasacha, Sunsún and Huasiloma. According to the Ecuador census of 2010 had a total population of 16,8611 inhabitants that correspond to 3.33% of the total population of the canton Cuenca.<ref name="Ajupa">{{cite web|url=http://www.ajupa.gov.ec/parroq.aspx?cant=1&parr=51 |title=Parroquia Baños |publisher=Asociación de Juntas Parroquiales Rurales del Azuay |accessdate=February 17, 2010 }}{{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
Baños as remote human settlement to the pre-colonial period, due to the existence of natural resources such as thermal waters, so it should be populated by the Cañaris. At the beginning of the seventeenth century in the colonial era, after being conquered by Spaniards who exploited gold and silver mines in the area, intermarriage between the colonizers and the native population and the purchase and sale of land started, which made possible the population growth and the division of sectors in the parish. In the republican era, in 1824, Baños was inferred as a rural parish in the city of Cuenca, but it was not until September 1, 1852, that it became legally legalized as a rural parish of the Cuenca canton.<ref name="Ajupa"/><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.eltelegrafo.com.ec/noticias/septimo/1/la-parroquia-cuencana-banos-atrae-con-sus-aguas-termales-a-viajeros|title=La parroquia cuencana Baños atrae con sus aguas termales a viajeros|last=mundo|first=El Telégrafo-Noticias del Ecuador y del|date=2017-03-26|website=El Telégrafo - Noticias del Ecuador y del mundo|language=es-es|access-date=2019-06-29}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://parroquiabanos.gob.ec/resena-de-banos/|title=Reseña de Baños|website=GAD Parroquial de Baños|language=es|access-date=2019-06-29}}</ref>

==Geography==
The town of Baños is located just 8 kilometres southwest of the city of [[Cuenca, Ecuador|Cuenca]]. It borders [[San Joaquín, Ecuador|San Joaquín]] parish to the north, [[Tarqui, Cuenca Canton|Tarqui]] and [[Victoria del Portete]] to the south, [[San Gerardo, Ecuador|San Gerardo]], [[Chumblín]] and [[San Fernando Canton|San Fernando]] and [[Zhaglli]] to the east, and with [[Chaucha]] to the west.<ref name="Ajupa"/> Baños covers an area of 327.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which accounts for 10.6 percent of the total area of the canton of Cuenca.<ref name="Ajupa"/>

== Education and health ==
The parish has 18 public educational establishments, of which 8 correspond to high school, 8 to elementary school and the 2 remaining to secondary education. These institutions are located in the parish center, Nero, Minas, Narancay Alto, Huizhil, Narancay Bajo and Misicata.

Baños belongs to District No. 2 and receives public health services at the Carlos Elizalde Center,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.salud.gob.ec/?s=Carlos+Elizalde|title=Resultados de búsqueda para "Carlos Elizalde" – Ministerio de Salud Pública|website=www.salud.gob.ec|access-date=2019-06-29}}</ref> which provides medical, dental and psychological consultations, as well as prenatal, delivery and postpartum care.

==Economy==
The economy of the parish is based around retailing and tourism which accounts for most of the population, followed by agriculture and livestock which accounts for 24 per cent of the population, manufacturing industries (18%) and construction industries (17%).<ref name="Ajupa"/> The main crops include [[maize]], [[beans]], [[potatoes]] and other vegetables.<ref name="Ajupa"/> Clothes and furniture are amongst the most common products manufactured in Baños. The service sector provides jobs in the accommodation and restaurant services, transport and shops to cater for tourists visiting Cuenca and the surrounding canton.<ref name="Ajupa"/> However, unemployment is a problem within the parish and led to 936 people migrating from the parish between November 1996 and 2001, mostly to the [[United States]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/c/document_library/get_file?folderId=45235&name=DLFE-5133.pdf |title=Azuay por Dentro |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadistica y Censo (Ecuador) |page=19 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20091113151857/http://www.inec.gov.ec/c/document_library/get_file?folderId=45235&name=DLFE-5133.pdf |archivedate=2009-11-13 }}</ref>

Between 2005 and 2009 the provincial government of Azuay Province invested US $1,650,000 in public works in the parish, including road infrastructure, educational facilities, sanitation, and socio-economic projects, some of them being water development schemes.<ref name="Ajupa"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.azuay.gov.ec/noticias.aspx?Tipo=1&Id=431 |title=Gobierno Provincial del Azuay saluda a Baños en sus 157 años de parroquialización |publisher=Prefectura del Azuay |date=January 10, 2010 |accessdate=February 17, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110719065801/http://www.azuay.gov.ec/noticias.aspx?Tipo=1&Id=431 |archivedate=July 19, 2011 }}</ref>
