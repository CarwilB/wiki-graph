'''Camacho''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 2,869 people living in a total area of {{cvt|222|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/camacho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the meso-region of Oeste de Minas and to the micro-region of [[Formiga, Minas Gerais|Formiga]].  It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="ibge.gov.br">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=IBGE - Cidades |work=ibge.gov.br |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of Camacho is located at an elevation of 957 meters southeast of [[Formiga, Minas Gerais|Formiga]].  Neighboring  municipalities are:  [[Itapecerica, Minas Gerais|Itapecerica]] (N and NW),  [[Oliveira, Minas Gerais|Oliveira]] (E), and [[Candeias (Minas Gerais)|Candeias]] (S and W).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/formiga/|title=CityBrazil - Percorrendo o Brasil de A a Z|work=citybrazil.com.br|access-date=2008-08-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204111907/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/formiga/#|archive-date=2008-02-04|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Economy==
Services and agriculture are the most important economic activities.  The GDP in 2005 was approximately R$28 million, 9 million reais from services and 17 million reais from agriculture.  There were 546 rural producers on 15,000 hectares of land (2006).  42 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 1,800 persons were involved in agriculture.  The main crops are coffee, rice, beans, and corn.  There were 6,000 head of cattle (2006).<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

There were no banks (2007).  The motor vehicle fleet had 299 automobiles, 51 trucks, 35 pickup trucks, and 245 motorcycles.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 2 public health clinics (2005).  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to [[Formiga, Minas Gerais|Formiga]] or [[Belo Horizonte]].  Educational needs of 700 students were met by 4 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 1 pre-primary school.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.698 (2000)
*State ranking: 554 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 3026 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*Literacy rate: 78%
*Life expectancy: 70 (average of males and females)<ref name="frigoletto.com.br">{{cite web|url=http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm|title=Frigoletto|work=frigoletto.com.br|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm|archive-date=2011-07-06}}</ref>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of R$121.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.  [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with R$435.00.  The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with R$73.00.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="frigoletto.com.br"/>
