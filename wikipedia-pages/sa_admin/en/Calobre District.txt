'''Calobre District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Veraguas Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 12,184;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 13, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) ia 12,159.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of 806&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the town of [[Calobre]].<ref name="Statoids"/>
