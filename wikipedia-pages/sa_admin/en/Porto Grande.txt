'''Porto Grande''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈpoʁtu ˈɡɾɐ̃dʒi|pron}}; {{langnf|pt||Grand Harbor}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 22,452<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/porto-grande/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 4,425&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=160053|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Amapá {{!}} Porto Grande|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-17}}</ref>

==Overview==
Porto Grande became an independent municipality in 1993.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/porto-grande/historico |title=Porto Grande|website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

The area around Porto Grande was first explored by gold miners, however nowadays it has become one of the biggest food producers in Amapá. It is a poor region where many homes do not have sewage system, and a quarter of the population does not have access to clean drinking water, however Porto Grande has one of the lowest illiteracy rates of Brazil.<ref name="globo">{{cite news|url=http://g1.globo.com/jornal-nacional/noticia/2010/09/porto-grande-e-maior-produtora-de-alimentos-do-amapa.html |title=Porto Grande é a maior produtora de alimentos do Amapá|website=Globo|access-date=3 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

The Annual Pineapple Festival in September is a popular tourist attraction. Other attractions are the spa near the [[Araguari River (Amapá)|Araguari River]].<ref name="amapa"/> As of 2021, a regional hospital was being constructed in Porto Grande.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/noticia/1502/obras-do-hospital-regional-de-porto-grande-chegam-a-65-do-cronograma |title=Obras do Hospital Regional de Porto Grande chegam a 65% do cronograma|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

==Geography==
It has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] with a short dry season.<ref name="amapa"/> 

The municipality contains 7.72% of the {{convert|2369400|ha|adj=on}} [[Amapá State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Amapá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Amapá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/430|accessdate=2016-07-06}}</ref>

==Economy==
Porto Grande is a major fruit producer in particular [[pineapple]]s and [[Orange (fruit)|oranges]]. Other economic activities are livestock like cattle, buffalo and pig, palm oil and pine plantations.<ref name="amapa">{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/porto-grande |title=Porto Grande|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

==Transport==
Porto Grande is connected to the [[BR-156]],<ref name="amapa"/> and the [[BR-210]] highways.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://informacoesdobrasil.com.br/rua/ap/porto-grande/rodovia-br-210+162 |title=Rodovia Br 210, Porto Grande - AP|website=Informações do Brasil|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
