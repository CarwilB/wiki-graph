'''Schroeder''' (Schröder) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the [[South Region, Brazil|South region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf | title = Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009| publisher = Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) | date = August 14, 2009 | access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt }}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |date=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref>{{Excessive citations inline|date=September 2021}}

It was named after [[Christian Mathias Schröder]], a German senator who organized a colony in the area.

== History ==
The origin of the municipality's name stems from senator [[Christian Mathias Schröder]], born in the city of [[Hamburg]], located in the north of [[Germany]]. Schroeder is also informally known by the name of S''chroeder Straße'', mainly by the older portion of the population that still speak German. The majority of the town's inhabitants are descendants from Germans originating in the northern region. The presence of other ethnic groups, mainly the Italians, also contributed to the city's history.

The history of the municipality begun with the marriage of [[Princess Francisca of Brazil|Princess Francisca Carolina Joana Leopoldina Romana Xavier de Paula Micaela Rafaela Gabriela Gonzaga de Bragança]] (1824-1898) and [[François d'Orléans, Prince of Joinville|Prince François Ferdinand Philippe Louis Marie d'Orléans]] (1818-1900). In the 1st of may of 1843, the princess Francisca Carolina, daughter of [[Pedro I of Brazil]], married the prince of Joinville, François Ferdinand, son of the king of the French [[Louis Philippe I|Louis Philippe]], and received as a wedding dowry a nearby stretch of land, the colony of São Francisco, area which would be split up into several municipalities along the posterior century, including the town of São Francisco do Sul. In 1846 the engineer Jerônimo Coelho traveled to the area to make the demarcation of the lands.

In 1848, the king of the French Louis Philippe was dethroned and his son François took refuge in England. After beginning to suffer from financial struggles, he sold part of the land to the owner of the Hamburgian Colonizer Society (Sociedade Colonizadora Hamburguesa), the German senator Christian Mathias Schroeder, eight of the 25 leagues received as a dowry. The senator then launched a settling project in part of the received land. Part of the colonists acquired lands near the area where the Schroeder I neighbourhood would be established<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2015-06-30 |title=Histórico - Histórico - Município de Schroeder |url=http://www.schroeder.sc.gov.br/cms/pagina/ver/codMapaItem/32646#.VZKcvBtVhHw |work=www.schroeder.sc.gov.br}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>, beginning the colonization of the lands that today make up the city of Schroeder.

With the increase in population, Schroeder was created as a Guaramirense district by law nº 424 of 31st of July of 1959 of [[Guaramirim]], with Sir Helmuth Moritz Germano Hertel appointed to the role of district intendant. The district would only be promoted into the status of municipality in October 3rd of 1964, through a decree signed by governor Celso Ramos. Sir Paulo Roberto Geipnel was named as the provisional mayor and stayed in the role until November 14th of 1965, when the first elected mayor, Sir Ludgero Tepasse, elected in the 3rd of October of the same year, would take over.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2015-06-30 |title=Schroeder - Germania de Sempre |url=http://www.germaniadesempre.com.br/site/schroeder/ |work=www.germaniadesempre.com.br}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
