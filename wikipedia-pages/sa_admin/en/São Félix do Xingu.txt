'''São Félix do Xingu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Pará]] in the [[Northern Region, Brazil|Northern region]] of [[Brazil]].

The city is served by [[São Félix do Xingu Airport]].

With an area of {{convert|84212.932|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name ="IBGE_Área">
{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/geociencias/cartografia/default_territ_area.shtm |title=Área territorial oficial |author=IBGE |accessdate=2010-12-05|date=2002-10-10|publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]]}}</ref> it is the third largest municipality in Pará and the sixth largest in Brazil.

==Conservation==

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|99271|ha}} [[Tapirapé Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Tapirapé|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=211|accessdate=2016-04-18}}</ref>
It contains part of the [[Tapirapé-Aquiri National Forest]], a {{convert|196,504|ha}} sustainable use conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Tapirapé-Aquiri – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA do Tapirapé-Aquiri|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6634|accessdate=2016-05-30}}</ref>
It also contains part of the {{convert|3373134|ha}} [[Terra do Meio Ecological Station]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica da Terra do Meio|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=47|accessdate=2016-05-29}}</ref>
The municipality contains 51% of the {{convert|445408|ha}} [[Serra do Pardo National Park]], also created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA da Serra do Pardo – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PARNA da Serra do Pardo|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/pt-br/uc/6266|accessdate=2016-08-08}}</ref>
