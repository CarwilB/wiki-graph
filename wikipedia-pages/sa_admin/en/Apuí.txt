'''Apuí''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 22,359 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/apui/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 54,240&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Gold rush==

The municipality shot to fame in December 2006, when a Brazilian maths teacher by the name of Ivani Valentim da Silva posted descriptions of miners scooping up thousands of dollars in gold in the area. In just three months, between 3,000 and 10,000 people poured into the area, cutting down trees, diverting streams and digging wildcat mines. The city was nicknamed ''Eldorado do Juma'' after the mythical [[El Dorado]].{{citation needed|date=July 2016}}

==Geography==
===Conservation===

The municipality contains 92% of the {{convert|2467244|ha|adj=on}} [[Apuí Mosaic]], a jointly-managed collection of conservation units.{{sfn|MOS do Apuí – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It contains the {{convert|808312|ha|adj=on}} [[Sucunduri State Park]], created in 2005.{{sfn|PES do Sucunduri – ISA}}
It also contains the {{convert|492905|ha|adj=on}} [[Sucunduri State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2005.{{sfn|FES do Sucunduri – ISA}}
It contains 72% of the {{convert|150465|ha|adj=on}} [[Guariba Extractive Reserve]], also created in 2005.{{sfn|RESEX do Guariba – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
It also contains the {{convert|113,606|ha|adj=on}} [[Bararati Sustainable Development Reserve]], created at the same time.{{sfn|RDS Bararati – ISA |loc=Informações gerais}}

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1958200|ha|adj=on}} [[Juruena National Park]], one of the largest conservation units in Brazil, created by decree on 5 June 2006.{{sfn|Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional do Juruena}}
It also contains part of the {{convert|896411|ha|adj=on}} [[Acari National Park]] created by president [[Dilma Rousseff]] in 2016 in the last week before her provisional removal from office.{{sfn|PARNA do Acari – ISA}}
The municipality contains about 17% of the {{convert|751302|ha|adj=on}} [[Aripuanã National Forest]], a sustainable development unit created at the same time.{{sfn|FLONA do Aripuanã – ISA}}

==Transportation==
The city is served by [[Apuí Airport]].
