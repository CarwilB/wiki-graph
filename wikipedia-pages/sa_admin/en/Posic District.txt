'''Posic District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Rioja Province|Rioja]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It is famous for the archaeological research conducted at the prehistoric site of Posic. The excavations at Posic has contributed with at least two major results. 1) The Incas' presence in the area can now be dated to 1470 AD. 2) One of the main Inca roads can be traced as far into the highlands and jungles as to Posic. Here the road is present and furthermore is a Tambu.<ref>{{Cite web|title=The Forgotten Amazon|url=https://xn--wadskjrforlag-8fb.dk/the-forgotten-amazon/|website=Wadskjær Forlag|language=da-DK|access-date=2020-04-30}}</ref>
