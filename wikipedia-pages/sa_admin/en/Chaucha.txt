'''Chaucha''' is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] in [[Cuenca Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of 337.2 km² and according to the 2001 Ecuadorian census it had a population total of 1,633.<ref name="Ajupa">{{cite web|url=http://www.ajupa.gov.ec/parroq.aspx?cant=1&parr=53|title=Parroquia Chaucha|publisher=Asociación de Juntas Parroquiales Rurales del Azuay|accessdate=February 17, 2010}}{{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
