'''Champotón Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipality]] within the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of Campeche, including the city of [[Champotón, Campeche|Champotón]] and the surrounding area.  In 2010 the municipality of Champotón had a population of 83,021 inhabitants. It has an areal extent of 6,088.28&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (2,350.7 sq mi).

The municipality also includes the offshore [[Cayos Arcas]], about 140&nbsp;km northwest of [[Campeche]] and 150&nbsp;km north of the mainland coast of Champotón.

==Demographics==
As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 78,170.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Champotón (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/campeche/04004__champot%C3%B3n/ |access-date=2024-08-10 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Champotón had a population of 30,881.<ref name="SEDESOL">{{cite web |title=Champotón |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=004 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201027231243/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=004 |archive-date=27 October 2020 |accessdate=23 April 2014 |work=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)}}</ref> Other than the city of Champotón, the municipality had 864 localities, such as (with 2010 populations in parentheses): [[Santo Domingo Kesté, Campeche|Santo Domingo Kesté]] (3,763), [[Carrillo Puerto, Campeche|Carrillo Puerto]] (2,829), [[Sihochac, Campeche|Sihochac]] (2,731), [[Ley Federal de Reforma Agraria, Campeche|Ley Federal de Reforma Agraria]] (2,435), classified as urban, and [[Xbacab, Campeche|Xbacab]] (1,649), [[San Pablo Pixtún, Campeche|San Pablo Pixtún]] (1,498), [[Maya Tecún I, Campeche|Maya Tecún I]] (1,254), [[Hool, Campeche|Hool]] (1,181), [[La Joya, Campeche|La Joya]] (1,007), and [[Nayarit Castellot]] (299).<ref name=SEDESOL/>
