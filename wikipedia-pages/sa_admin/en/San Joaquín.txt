'''San Joaquín''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]  for [[Joachim|Saint Joachim]]) is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It is part of [[Greater Santiago]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Joaquín spans an area of {{convert|9.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 97,625 inhabitants (46,708 men and 50,917 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 14.4% (16,392 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> Its 2006 projected population was 87,035.<ref name="sir">[http://sir.mideplan.cl/ Sistema de Información Regional], Ministry of Planning of Chile.  {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423113455/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Joaquín is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Sergio Echeverría García.<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:
* Nelly Santander Marín (IND)
* Ramón Ortiz Seguel ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Víctor Contreras Vicencio (IND)
* Gustavo Arias Campos ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Elizabeth Galleguillos Medina ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Cristóbal Labra Bassa ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Manuel León Huerta ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* José Contreras Silva ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Joaquín is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Felipe Salaberry ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and [[Ximena Vidal]] (PPD) as part of the 25th electoral district, (together with [[Macul]] and [[La Granja, Chile|La Granja]]).{{Update after|2023|8|3}} The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Soledad Alvear]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Pablo Longueira]] (UDI) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
