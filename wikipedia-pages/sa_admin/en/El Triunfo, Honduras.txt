'''El Triunfo''' is a town, with a population of 10,930 (2023 calculation),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Choluteca department|Choluteca]].

This small town is located close to the Nicaraguan border.
