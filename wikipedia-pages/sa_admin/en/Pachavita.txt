'''Pachavita''' is a town and municipality in the [[Neira Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at an altitude of {{convert|1985|m|ft}} and at a distance of {{convert|85|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. The municipality borders [[Chinavita]] in the north, [[Tenza]] and [[La Capilla]] in the south, [[Garagoa]] in the east and [[Úmbita]] in the west.<ref name=websitePachavita>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.pachavita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Pachavita] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150602075158/http://www.pachavita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-06-02 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Pachavita is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]; ''pacha'' = man, lord and ''vita'' = point, summit, peak; "Peak of the man".<ref name=websitePachavita/> Another meaning is "Proud chief".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Pachavita] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Before the arrival of the Spanish [[conquistador]]es, the area of Pachavita was part of the loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. The [[Muisca people|Muisca]] had different [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] and the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]] ruled over Pachavita.

Modern Pachavita was founded on November 17, 1716.<ref name=websitePachavita/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Pachavita are [[agriculture]] and [[dairy]] farming with products [[yuca]], [[maize]], [[banana]]s, [[arracacha]], [[bean]]s, [[lulo]] and [[cucumber]]s, eggs, [[pea]]s and [[cheese]].<ref name=websitePachavita/>
