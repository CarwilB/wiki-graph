'''São Domingos do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. It is situated at a latitude of 28º31'51 South and a longitude of 51º53'16" West. As of 2020, it has an estimated population of 3,083 inhabitants on an area of 78.67&nbsp;km².<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/sao-domingos-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Its current mayor is Domingos Scartazzini.{{cn|date=December 2024}}
