'''Sámara''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Nicoya (canton)|Nicoya]] canton, in the Guanacaste province of Costa Rica.<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Sámara was established on 26 November, 1971 by Decreto Ejecutivo 2075-G. It was split from [[Nicoya District|Nicoya]].<ref name="dta2017" />

== Geography ==
Sámara has an area of {{formatnum:109.19}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:3}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

==Villages==
{{Unreferenced section|date=May 2025}} 
The administrative center of the district is the town of Sámara.

Other villages in the district are Bajo Escondido, Barco Quebrado, Buenavista, Buenos Aires, Cambutes, Cangrejal, Cantarrana, Chinampas, Esterones, Galilea, Palmar, Panamá, Playa Buena Vista, Primavera, Pueblo Nuevo, Samaria, Santo Domingo, Taranta, Terciopelo, and Torito.

== Transportation ==
=== Road transportation ===
{{Unreferenced section|date=May 2025}} 
The district is covered by the following road routes:
*[[File:CR RNS 150.svg|25px]] [[National Route 150 (Costa Rica)|National Route 150]]
*[[File:CR RNS 160.svg|25px]] [[National Route 160 (Costa Rica)|National Route 160]]
*[[File:CR RNT 934.svg|25px]] [[National Route 934 (Costa Rica)|National Route 934]]

Sámara is located approximately 35&nbsp;km from [[Nicoya]], the economic and administrative hub of the region.

The paving of the final 35&nbsp;km of the drive, Route 150 from [[Nicoya]] to Sámara on through to [[Puerto Carrillo|Carrillo]] was completed in early 2006.

The small [[Carrillo Airport]] is about 5&nbsp;km east of Sámara.
