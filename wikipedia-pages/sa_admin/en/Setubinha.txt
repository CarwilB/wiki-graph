'''Setubinha''' is a  [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northeast of the Brazilian state of [[Minas Gerais]].  As of 2020 the population was 12,378 in a total area of {{cvt|536|km2}}.<ref name= "IBGE 2020"/> The elevation is 729 metres. It is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]].

The economy is based on cattle raising and agriculture, with the main crops being coffee, bananas, sugarcane, and corn.  As of 2005 there were no hospitals and 3 public health clinics.  In 2006 there were 14 primary schools and 2 middle schools.{{cn|date=April 2025}}
