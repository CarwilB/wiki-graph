'''Sulina ''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. 
It has an area of {{convert|170.759|km2|sqmi}}.
It has a [[humid subtropical climate]].
As of 2020 the estimated population was 2,930.

==Geography==

Sulina is in the state of Paraná, Brazil.{{sfn|Geonames}} It has an area of {{convert|170.759|km2}} as of 2019.{{sfn|IBGE}} The elevation above sea level is about {{convert|497|m}}.{{sfn|Geonames}}

===Climate===

The [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen climate type]] is Cfa : [[humid subtropical climate]].{{sfn|mindat}}
The average annual temperature is {{convert|20|C}}.{{sfn|NASAearth}}
The average annual rainfall is {{convert|2,634|mm}}.{{sfn|NASArain}}

==Demographics==

The population in the 2010 census was 3,314.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
The estimated population as of 2019 was 2,981.{{sfn|IBGE}}
Population density as of 2010 was {{convert|19.88|PD/km2|PD/sqmi}}.{{sfn|IBGE}}
As of 2010, 99.2% of the population had attended school between the ages of 6 and 14. 
Also as of 2010, the municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.693.
This compares to 0.397 in 1991 and 0.614 in 2000.{{sfn|IBGE}}

On the 2010 census religion was reported as [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] by 2979 people, [[Evangelicalism|Evangelical]] by 395 people and [[Animism]] by 0 people.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

In 2017, the average monthly salary of formal workers was 2.3 minimum wages. 
Formally employed people were 12.8% of the total population. 
Households with monthly income of up to half a minimum wage per person represent 29.9% of the population.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

==Health and sanitation==

2.8% of households have adequate sanitation, 74.2% of urban households are on public roads with afforestation and 16.4% of urban households are on public roads with adequate urbanization (presence of manhole, sidewalk, pavement and curb).{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
