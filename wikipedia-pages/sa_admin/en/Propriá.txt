'''Propriá''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|pɾopɾiˈa|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 29,692 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/propria/panorama |access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|95|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

==Sports==
There are two football clubs in Propriá. [[América Futebol Clube (SE)|América]] and [[Esporte Clube Propriá|Propriá]]. América owns [[Estádio José Neto|José Neto Stadium]], which has a maximum capacity of 3,000 people, and Propriá owns [[Estádio Constantino Tavares|Constantino Tavares Stadium]], which has a maximum capacity of 5,000 people. Also, both clubs plays at [[Estádio João Alves Filho|João Alves Filho Stadium]], which has a maximum capacity of 3,000 people and is owned by Sergipe's state government.

==Religion==
Propriá is the seat of a Roman Catholic diocese, which covers 8,181 square kilometers (3,159 square miles), including the 95 square kilometers (36.67 square miles) of the municipality.  The total population of the diocese is approximately 347,000 of which approximately 77% are Catholic.<ref>Annuario Pontificio, 2017, p.587</ref>  The bishop is Vítor Agnaldo de Menezes <ref>{{Cite web|url=https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/it/bollettino/pubblico/2017/10/25/0737/01610.html|title=Rinunce e nomine}}</ref>
