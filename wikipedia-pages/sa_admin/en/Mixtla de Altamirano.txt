'''Mixtla de Altamirano''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is in the central zone of the state, about {{convert|247|km|mi}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of {{convert|60.87|km2}}.
==Geography==
The municipality of Mixtla de Altamirano is delimited to the north by [[Texhuacán]], to the east by [[Zongolica]], to the south by [[Tehuipango]] and to the west by [[Astacinga]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30104a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111230900/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30104a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2013-01-11|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Mixtla de Altamirano|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=Spanish|accessdate=2009-06-11}}</ref>

The weather in Mixtla de Altamirano is very cold all year with rains in summer and autumn.
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[coffee]].
==Culture==
In Mixtla de Altamirano, a celebration to honor San Andrés Apostol, patron of the town, takes place in December.
