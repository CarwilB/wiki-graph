'''Santa Vitória''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the west of the Brazilian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. {{as of|2020}}, the population was estimated at 19,872. It became a municipality in 1948.

Santa Vitória is located at an elevation of {{convert|498|m|ft}}, to the south of the great reservoir of [[São Simão Dam]] in the [[Triângulo Mineiro]]. It belongs to the statistical microregion of [[Ituiutaba]]. Neighboring municipalities are [[São Simão, Goiás|São Simão]], [[União de Minas]], [[Limeira do Oeste]], [[Campina Verde]] and [[Gurinhatã]].

==History==
Settlement began in the beginning of the nineteenth century with the cattle ranch called ''São Jerônimo''. The region was occupied by the Caiapó Indians who were dominated and expelled. The region was covered by forests and malaria was endemic. The first church appeared in 1904, followed by the cemetery in 1905. Santa Vitória belonged to the municipality of Ituiutaba, becoming a separate municipality in 1948.<ref>{{cite web |title=O Município |url=https://www.santavitoria.mg.gov.br/o-municipio/ |website=Prefeitura Municipal de Santa Vitória |trans-website=Santa Vitória City Hall |trans-title=The Municipality |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref>

==Economy==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce and agriculture. The GDP in 2017 was R$721,332,990.00.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios &#124; PIB a preços correntes |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/38/46996 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities &#124; GDP at current prices |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref> Santa Vitória is in the top tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development. It is in a region of good soils, adequate rainfall, and abundance of surface water. As of 2018 there were three bank branches in the town.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Instituições financeiras &#124; Agências |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/29/21910 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; Financial institutions &#124; Branches |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref> There was a small retail commerce serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands. In the rural area there were 972 establishments giving employment to 4,757 persons.<ref name="AgriculturalCensus">{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Censo Agropecuário &#124; Características dos estabelecimentos |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/24/76693 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; Agricultural Census &#124; Characteristics of establishments |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref> 411 of the farms had tractors.<ref name="AgriculturalCensus"/> There were 11,079 vehicles throughout the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Frota de veículos &#124; Veículo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/22/28120 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; Vehicle fleet &#124; Vehicle |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref> There were 210,939 head of cattle in 2017.<ref name="AgriculturalCensus"/> The crops with a planted area of more than {{convert|100|ha|0|abbr=off}} were beans, corn, sorghum, soybeans ({{convert|680|ha|0|abbr=on}}), and sugarcane.<ref name="AgriculturalCensus"/>

Cattle production is important with a large herd. Cattle are raised for meat and milk. Eighteen producers are specialized in milk production. In 2006 there were 23,000 dairy cattle. There is also a substantial swine industry.<ref>{{cite web|title=ASPECTOS GEOGRÁFICO E ECONÔMICOS |url=http://www.santavitoria.mg.gov.br/index/aspectosgeograficoseeconomicos.htm |website=Prefeitura Municipal de Santa Vitória |trans-website=Santa Vitória City Hall |trans-title=GEOGRAPHICAL AND ECONOMIC ASPECTS |language=pt |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090201021342/http://www.santavitoria.mg.gov.br/index/aspectosgeograficoseeconomicos.htm |archive-date=1 February 2009 |url-status=dead |access-date=12 November 2020 |df=dmy }}</ref>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 21 facilities, one of which is a hospital with 29 beds.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Serviços de saúde &#124; Estabelecimentos de Saúde |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/32/28163 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; Health services &#124; Health Facilities |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref> In the educational sector there were 11 primary schools and 3 secondary schools.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE &#124; Cidades@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Pesquisa &#124; Censo escolar - sinopse &#124; Ensino básico |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-vitoria/pesquisa/13/78117 |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |trans-website=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |trans-title=IBGE &#124; Cities@ &#124; Minas Gerais &#124; Santa Vitória &#124; Research &#124; School census - synopsis &#124; Basic education |language=pt |access-date=12 November 2020}}</ref>
