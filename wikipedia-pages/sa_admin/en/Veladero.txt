'''Veladero ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tolé District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|51.1|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,727 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|33.8|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 10 of March 7, 1997; this measure was complemented by Law 5 of January 19, 1998 and Law 69 of October 28, 1998.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 1,575.<ref name=inec/>

The mountain peak [[Baboso]] is nearby.
