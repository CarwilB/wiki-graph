'''Felisburgo''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].The population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 7,504 people living in a total area of 594&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/felisburgo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Almenara]]. Its elevation is 638 meters. It became a municipality in 1962.

==Economics and geography==
The main economic activities are cattle raising and the cultivation of coffee, sugarcane and corn. The GDP in 2006 was {{BRLConvert|19983|k|year=2006|showdate=no}}. There was 01 banking agency {{as of|2007|lc=y}}. In the same year there were 167 automobiles, which was a ratio of one automobile for every 400 people. In the rural area there were 273 producers of which only 33 had tractors. There were 27,000 head of cattle in 2006.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref> Neighboring municipalities are: [[Rio do Prado]], [[Rubim]], [[Jequitinhonha]], [[Joaíma]] and [[Fronteira dos Vales]]. This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
