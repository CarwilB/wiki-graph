'''Muna Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: "soft water") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 270.81&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly 50&nbsp;km south of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, though it existed before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], as part of the chieftainship of Tutul Xiu. At colonization, Muna became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/> The areas encompassing Muna and [[Dzán Municipality]] were joined for a time during the encomienda system. The first encomendero was Castilla in 1549 and it then passed to Alonso Rosado and Diego Rosado. By 1607 the encomendero was Pedro Rosado. In 1625, the trusteeship passed to Diego de Jáuregui and Francisca Rosado and in 1629 to Sebastián de Mendoza and Diego de Mendoza.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5 |page=496 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA36|accessdate=1 August 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the low sierra partition of [[Mama Municipality, Yucatán|Mama Municipality]].<ref name=municipios /> In 1867 it was transferred to the [[Ticul Municipality]] and confirmed as its own municipality in 1988.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|accessdate=3 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=Spanish|date=1996|pages=111, 123|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=23 December 2015}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has nine councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of education, agricultural development, public lighting, events, roads, cemeteries, maintenance, ecology and parks.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Muna | url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=3 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance/>

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance/>

==Local festivals==
Every year from 12 to 15 August the town celebrates a festival in honor of its patroness, the Virgin of the Assumption.<ref name="municipios"/>
