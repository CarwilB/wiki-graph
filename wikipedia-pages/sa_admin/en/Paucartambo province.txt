==Political division==
The province is divided into six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: {{lang|es|distrito}}), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Caicay District|Caicay]] ([[Caicay]])
* [[Challabamba District|Challabamba]] ([[Challabamba]])
* [[Colquepata District|Colquepata]] ([[Colquepata]])
* [[Huancarani District|Huancarani]] (Huancarani)
* [[Kosñipata District|Kosñipata]] ([[Pillcopata]])
* [[Paucartambo District, Paucartambo|Paucartambo]] ([[Paucartambo, Paucartambo|Paucartambo]])

== Ethnic groups ==

The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] ethnicity. According to the 2007 national census, [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the first language of the great majority of the population (85.56%); 13.51% of the residents learned [[Spanish language|Spanish]] as their first language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
