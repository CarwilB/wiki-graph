'''Torbeck''' ({{IPA|fr|tɔʁbɛk}}; {{langx|ht|Tòbèk}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Les Cayes Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 76,083 inhabitants.
