'''San Fernando Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Azuay Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[San Fernando, Azuay|San Fernando]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 3,961.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> San Fernando's main attraction is its famous lagoon, [[:es:Laguna_de_Busa|Laguna de Busa]]. The main source of income is agriculture and livestock.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Fernando (Canton, Ecuador) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/ecuador/admin/azuay/0107__san_fernando/ |access-date=2025-10-22 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> The town itself is mainly rural, and the townspeople are mainly elders, 65+ years old. 

== Town's history ==
It is named after Saint Fernando, whose celebration day is on May 30th. <ref>{{Citation |title=Ferdinand III of Castile |date=2025-06-14 |work=Wikipedia |url=https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ferdinand_III_of_Castile&oldid=1295602268 |access-date=2025-10-22 |language=en}}</ref> The primary language spoken is Spanish.
