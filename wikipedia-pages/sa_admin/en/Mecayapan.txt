'''Mecayapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] and city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south-east zone of the State, about 428&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 523.96 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|13|N|94|50|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Mecayapan is delimited to the north by [[Gulf of Mexico]] to the east by [[Pajapan]], to the south by [[Chinameca, Veracruz|Chinameca]] and to the west by [[Soteapan]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30104a.htm | title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Mecayapan | publisher=Gobierno Estatal | work=veracruz.gob.mx | access-date=2009-06-10 | language=es | archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111230900/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30104a.htm | archive-date=2013-01-11 | url-status=dead }}</ref>
=== Climate ===

The weather in Mecayapan is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

== Economy ==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[green chile]].

== Culture ==

In Mecayapan, the celebration in honor to Santo Santiago Jacobo, the [[patron saint]] of the town, is held every May.
