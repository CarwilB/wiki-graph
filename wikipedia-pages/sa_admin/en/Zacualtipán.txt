'''Zacualtipán''' (formally: '''Zacualtipán de Ángeles''', for [[Felipe Ángeles]], born there in 1868)<ref>{{cite journal|last=Meade|first=Everard Kidder|title=The Passion of the Mexican Revolution: The Trial and Execution of Felipe Ángeles|journal=Journal of Historical Biography|date=Spring 2010|volume=7|pages=30–99|url=https://www.ufv.ca/jhb/Volume_7/Volume_7_Meade.pdf}}</ref> is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 241.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 38,155,<ref>{{cite web |title=Zacualtipán de Ángeles: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/zacualtipan-de-angeles |website=Data México |publisher=Secretaría de Economía}}</ref> up from 25,987 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=Zacualtipan |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13081a.htm |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070124120636/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13081a.htm |archive-date=January 24, 2007 |access-date=December 27, 2008 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]}}</ref>

==History==
The town of Zacualtipán was the site of the [[action of Sequalteplan]] on February 25, 1848.  It was a surprise attack by a mounted American force under Gen. [[Joseph Lane]] that defeated a Mexican guerrilla force under [[Celedonio Dómeco de Jarauta]] during the [[Mexican–American War]].<ref name="Wilcox">{{Cite book |last=Wilcox |first=Cadmus M. |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=wyMuAAAAYAAJ |title=History of the Mexican War |date= |publisher=Church News Publishing Company |year=1892 |location=Washington, D.C. |pages=532-535 |language=en |author-link=Cadmus M. Wilcox}}</ref>
