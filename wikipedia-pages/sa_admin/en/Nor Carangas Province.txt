'''Nor Carangas''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]]. Its seat is [[Huayllamarca]].

==Location==
Nor Carangas province is one of sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 46' and 18° 09' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 13' and 67° 41' [[Longitude|West]]. 

The province borders [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in the north, [[San Pedro de Totora Province]] in the west, [[Carangas Province]] in the south, [[Saucarí Province]] in the southeast, and [[Cercado Province, Oruro|Cercado Province]] in the northeast. 

The province extends over 70&nbsp;km from northwest to southeast, and 25&nbsp;km from northeast to southwest.

==Population==
Main [[idiom]] of the province is [[Aymara language|Aymara]], spoken by 96%, while 84% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 14% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992). 

The population increased from 4,900 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 5,790 (2001 census), an increase of 18.2%. - 41.9% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).  

99.7% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 98.7% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992). 

84.5% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.1% in [[mining]], 3.1% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 12.3% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

80% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 15% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Huayllamarca Municipality'''.
It is identical to Nor Carangas Province.
