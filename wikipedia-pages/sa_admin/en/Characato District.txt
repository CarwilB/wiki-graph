'''Characato''' is a district of [[Arequipa Province]], [[Peru]]. It is located to the southeast of [[Arequipa]], at an average elevation of 3,505 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Characato topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-phswdn/Characato/ |access-date=12 September 2023 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref> As of the year 2022, it had a population of 17,706.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Characato (District, Peru) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/peru/arequipa/admin/arequipa/040105__characato/ |access-date=12 September 2023 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
