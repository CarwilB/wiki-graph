'''Tinjacá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the subregion of the [[Ricaurte Province]]. Tinjacá is located on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|54|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. It borders [[Sutamarchán]] in the north, [[Ráquira]] in the south, [[Sáchica]] in the east and in the west [[Chiquinquirá]] and [[Saboyá]].<ref name=websiteTinjaca>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tinjaca-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tinjacá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
Tinjacá is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Enclosure of the powerful lord".<ref name=websiteTinjaca/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Municipalities Boyacá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Tinjacá before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a loose confederation of different [[Muisca rulers]]. The ''[[cacique]]'' of Tinjacá was loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. Modern Tinjacá was founded on November 7, 1555.<ref name=websiteTinjaca/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Tinjacá are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Important agricultural products are [[onion]]s, [[tomato]]es, [[peach]]es, [[potato]]es, [[maize]] and [[pea]]s.<ref name=websiteTinjaca/>
