'''Guadalupe Etla''' is a village and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Oaxaca]]. The municipality has a total area of 17.86&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

Guadalupe Etla is part of the region of [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|the central valleys of Oaxaca]].

Etla means "abounding beans" and comes from the [[Nahuatl]] words E ~ "bean" and Tia ~ "abundance"; and Guadalupe in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].

According to [[INEGI]] the total population of Guadalupe Etla in 2010 was 2,433.<ref>{{cite web|title=Guadalupe Etla|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20033a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20041211140451/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20033a.htm|archive-date=December 11, 2004|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==History== 
It is known that the chief Sebastián Ramírez de León together with other people donated the land where the municipality is located today, in addition to purchasing land from the town of Guelache.<ref>{{Cite web|author=Aguilar Genaro, Eduardo|title=Inventory of the municipal archive Guadalupe Etla, Oaxaca |oclc=69648083|isbn=968-5937-54-0|accessdate=February 12, 2020|url=https://www.adabi.org.mx/vufind/Record/adabi272475|archivedate=February 12, 2020|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200212193939/https://www.adabi.org.mx/vufind/Record/adabi272475|url-status=dead}}</ref>
