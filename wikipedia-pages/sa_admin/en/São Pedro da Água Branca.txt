'''São Pedro da Água Branca''' is the westernmost municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Maranhão]], bordering both [[Pará]] and [[Tocantins]] states.

The city has one of the most important railway stations on the [[Carajás Railway]] line.
