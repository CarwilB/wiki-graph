'''Mantua''' ({{IPA|es|ˈmantwa}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Pinar del Río Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==History==
{{cleanup section|date=February 2025|reason=It was "founded" twice?}}
It was founded in 1605 by Italian shipwrecked sailors as Mantua, Cuba. It was founded in 1719 under the name Guane del Norte. In 1866 it was established as a municipality.<ref name=guije/> The settlement of Mantua is a National Monument of Cuba.<ref name=cnpc>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cnpc.cult.cu/cnpc/monumen/Pag002.htm |title=National Monuments in Cuba |author=National Council for Cultural Heritage |accessdate=2007-10-09 |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071211213023/http://www.cnpc.cult.cu/cnpc/monumen/Pag002.htm |archivedate=2007-12-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Arroyos, Bartolo, Cabezas, Coronel Pozo (Lázaro), Fidel Pedraja, Guayabo, Macurijes, Mantua, Montezuelo and Pablo Suárez.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/pmantua/index.htm|title=Mantua|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

[[Mantua Municipal Museum]] is located in the José Martí street.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Mantua had a population of 23,233.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|915|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|25|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Transport==
The town is the western endpoint of the "[[Circuito Norte]]" (CN) highway.
