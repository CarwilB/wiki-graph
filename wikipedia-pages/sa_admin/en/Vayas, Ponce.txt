'''Vayas''' is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the [[municipality]] of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Along with [[Playa (Ponce)|Playa]], [[Bucana]], [[Canas (Ponce)|Canas]] and [[Capitanejo (Ponce)|Capitanejo]], Vayas is also one of Ponce's five coastal barrios. Together with Capitanejo, Vayas is also one of two rural coastal barrios in the municipality.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 22.</ref> It was organized in 1831, at which time it was divided into Vayas Norte and Vayas Sur; in 1878 the two were subsequently merged into today's Vayas.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==History==
Barrio Vayas is one of the oldest barrios in Ponce, dating from the 1780s. Its name can be seen spelled as "Bayas", "Vallas, and even "Ballas", but the official name is nowadays spelled "Vayas". It is believed that name came from the stakes used by farmers to mark the extension of their lands, in the times when those lands were used for pasturing herds.<ref>Eli Oquendo-Rodriguez. ''Barrios de Ponce: Noticias y microhistorias de ocho comunidades ponceñas en la historia, siglos XVI al XIX.'' 2019. p. 207. {{ISBN|9781076759153}}</ref>

==Location==
Vayas is a rural, coastal barrio located in the southern region of the municipality, east of downtown Ponce, and southeast of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]]. It is located at 18.0107°N 66.5567°W, with an elevation of 43 feet.<ref>[http://www.trails.com/topo.aspx?panerror=true&z=19&e=759173.152588761&n=1993318.14420084&s=50&size=s&style=drgsr U.S. Geological Survey. Topographic Map of Ponce. Map Styles: Map and Shaded. Trails.com. From: USGS. Topo Map. Projection: NAD83/WGS84. ''Coordinates and Elevation of Barrio Vayas.''] Retrieved 25 February 2010.</ref> The [[toponymy]], or origin of the name, is related to the edges or boundary (a type of fencing) to cordon off a land area or barrio.<ref>Government of the Municipality of Ponce. Periódico "El Señorial".  Special issue: Carnaval Ponceño 2013. February 2013. Page 17. Ponce, Puerto Rico.</ref>

==Features and demographics==
Unlike most other barrios of Ponce, Vayas' landscape is entirely flat. Vayas has the third longest coastline of all five of Ponce's coastal barrios, after [[Canas (Ponce)|Canas]] and [[Playa (Ponce)|Playa]].  Vayas has {{convert|7.1|sqmi|km2|sigfig=2}} of land area and {{convert|3.4|sqmi|km2|sigfig=2}} of water area.<ref>[http://factfinder.census.gov/servlet/GCTTable?_bm=y&-geo_id=05000US72113&-_box_head_nbr=GCT-PH1&-ds_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U&-redoLog=false&-format=CO-2&-mt_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U_GCTPH1_CO2 U.S. Census Bureau. Census 2000. Ponce and Its Barrios. (Ponce Municipio, Puerto Rico -- County Subdivision and Place. GCT-PH1. Population, Housing Units, Area, and Density: 2000. Data Set: Census 2000 Summary File 1 (SF 1) 100-Percent Data)] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20200212033726/http://factfinder.census.gov/servlet/GCTTable?_bm=y&-geo_id=05000US72113&-_box_head_nbr=GCT-PH1&-ds_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U&-redoLog=false&-format=CO-2&-mt_name=DEC_2000_SF1_U_GCTPH1_CO2 |date=12 February 2020 }} Retrieved 25 February 2010.</ref><ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Vayas+Urbano+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved 25 February 2010.</ref>

In 2000, the population of Vayas was 1,338 persons, and it had a density of 188 persons per square mile.<ref name="trails.com"/><ref name="ponceweb.org">[http://www.ponceweb.org/Anuncio/Barriosdeponce.pdf Autonomous Municipality of Ponce. Office of Territorial Planning. (Municipio Autonomo de Ponce. Oficina de Ordenacion Territorial.) Mapa de Localizacion Limite de Barrios de Ponce.] Retrieved 20 February 2010.</ref>

In 2010, the population of Vayas was 901 persons, and it had a density of 127.1 persons per square mile.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico:2010:population and housing unit counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

The main roads serving barrio Vayas are [[Puerto Rico Highway 52|PR-52]] and [[Puerto Rico Highway 1|PR-1]].
