The municipality contains part of the {{convert|823843|ha}} [[Serra das Confusões National Park]], created in 1998, which protects an area of the [[Caatinga]] biome.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional Serra das Confusões|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=146|access-date=2016-06-09}}</ref>

==Name==
''Bom Jesus'' means "Good [[Jesus]]" in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]].

==Geography and climate==
Bom Jesus has a rather dry [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Aw''). It received national recognition in November 21, 2005 when it was measured the temperature of 44.7&nbsp;°C (112.4&nbsp;°F) in the town, the highest ever officially recorded in Brazil until November 2023.<ref name="tempbrazil">{{cite web|url=http://noticias.terra.com.br/brasil/interna/0,,OI771459-EI306,00.html |title=Piauí tem a temperatura mais alta em 96 anos (Piauí has the highest temperature in 96 years) |publisher=Terra |date=26 November 2005 |access-date=2010-10-01}}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the [[Uruçui-Una Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Uruçuí-Una|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=65|access-date=2016-04-23}}</ref>
