'''Daniel Alcides Carrión''' is the smallest of three provinces that make up the [[Pasco Region]] in [[Peru]]. It was named after the medical student [[Daniel Alcides Carrión]]. The capital of this province is [[Yanahuanca]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[Huánuco Region]]
*'''East''': [[Pasco Province|province of Pasco]]
*'''South''': [[Pasco Province|province of Pasco]]
*'''West''': [[Lima Region]]

== Geography ==
The [[Rawra mountain range|Rawra]] mountain range and the Rumi Cruz mountain range traverse the province. One of the highest peaks of the province is [[Puywanqucha]] at about {{convert|5200|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} above sea level.
