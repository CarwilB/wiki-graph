'''Santiago Suchilquitongo''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 44.65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Etla District]] in the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales region]].
As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 8,518.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20483a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 15, 2009|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20041211135701/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20483a.htm|archivedate=December 11, 2004}}</ref>
