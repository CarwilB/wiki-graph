'''El Rodeo''' ({{IPA|es|el roˈðeo}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]], founded on 12 March 1834. Since 1954 it is known as '''San Jose el Rodeo'''.
