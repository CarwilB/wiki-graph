'''Pau dos Ferros''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] in the interior of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]], in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region of Brazil]]. Covering an area of 260&nbsp;km², it is located 389&nbsp;km from [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]], the state capital. Emancipated from [[Portalegre, Rio Grande do Norte|Portalegre]] in the 19th century, the name refers to a tree, likely an [[Microdesmia rigida|oiticica]], which, due to its large size, provided shade and served as a resting place for cowboys who passed through the area, marking their brands with iron on the trunks of these trees, thus initiating the region's settlement.

As the main city of the Alto Oeste region,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correiodatarde.com.br/editorias/correio_politico-60721|title=Rosalba enfrenta crise para acomodar aliados no interior do Estado|author=Souza, Fabiano|date=2011-02-01|publisher=Correio da Tarde|access-date=2011-09-10}}</ref> its population in the [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 census]] was 30,479 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), making it the 18th most populous municipality in Rio Grande do Norte. However, due to the city's regional hub influence, which extends to over thirty neighboring municipalities, approximately 50,000 people pass through the municipal seat daily.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=315 |title=Pau dos Ferros: Serviços polarizados para outras 36 cidades |author=Paulo, João |date=2010-07-17 |publisher=Portal Pau dos Ferros |access-date=2011-03-04 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/5wweDjkha?url=http://paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=315 |archive-date=2011-03-05 |url-status=live }}</ref>

The municipality boasts several historical and cultural attractions, including the Dr. Pedro Diógenes Fernandes Public Reservoir, which supplies the city's water, and the Nossa Senhora da Conceição Church, built in 1738 and established as a parish church in 1756. Additionally, the Obelisk at Praça Monsenhor Caminha was erected to commemorate the centenary of political emancipation and the bicentenary of the parish. Cultural highlights include the Intermunicipal Fair of Education, Culture, Tourism, and Business of Alto Oeste Potiguar (FINECAP), a significant cultural and economic exposition, and the festivities honoring the patron saint [[Immaculate Conception|Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception]], held from late November to early December.

== History ==

=== From origins to emancipation ===
For a long time, the area of present-day Pau dos Ferros was inhabited by the Panati indigenous people. Between the late 17th and early 18th centuries, cowboys and travelers crossing the ''[[sertão]]'' discovered a watercourse, later named the [[Apodi River]], surrounded by large, leafy trees that soon became resting spots during long, exhausting journeys. Along this river, trading posts were established, where cattle were sold and branded on the trunks of these trees.<ref name="Portal_História">{{cite web|url=http://www.paudosferros.com/cidade/history.php|title=História do Município|author=Silva, Edivan|publisher=Portal Pau dos Ferros|access-date=2011-03-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100206024803/http://www.paudosferros.com/cidade/history.php|archive-date=2010-02-06}}</ref>

[[File:Antonio rocha pitta.jpg|thumb|left|Colonel Antônio da Rocha Pita]]
In 1717, during the [[Colonial Brazil|colonial period]], Manoel Negrão became the first grantee of a ''sesmaria'', which was later donated to Colonel Antônio da Rocha Pita, who owned vast lands in the provinces of Ceará and Rio Grande do Norte. Upon his death in 1733, this ''sesmaria'', named "Pau dos Ferros," was inherited by his children Francisco da Rocha Pita, Luiz da Rocha Pita Deusdará, Simão da Fonseca, and Maria Joana, all of whom were pioneers in establishing a small settlement with many mud houses around the ''sesmaria''. The most significant pioneer was the farmer Francisco Marçal, who founded a cattle ranch and, through great efforts, was responsible for building a chapel in 1738, which became the parish church of a large ''[[freguesia]]'' on December 19, 1756, with the creation of the Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception Parish, with [[Immaculate Conception|Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception]] as its patron saint.<ref name="Portal_História"/><ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS">Andrade, Manoel Caminha Freire de et al. '''Commemorative Magazine of the Bicentenary of the Parish and Centenary of the Municipality of Pau dos Ferros (1756-1856-1956)'''. 2nd ed. [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]]: Sebo, 2015. 206 p.</ref><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade">Barreto, José Jácome. '''Pau dos Ferros''': History, Tradition, and Reality. Natal: Clima, 1987.</ref><ref name="HisPDF_2013">{{cite book|author=Souza Neto, Manoel Cavalcante de|title=Pau dos Ferros under the shade of the oiticica|publisher=Offset Editora|year=2013|pages=142|isbn=978-85-65739-49-8}}</ref>

In 1761, the settlement was incorporated into the village of [[Portalegre, Rio Grande do Norte|Portalegre]], which, being located in the mountains 33 kilometers from Pau dos Ferros, hindered local commerce and access for residents. In the western zone of Rio Grande do Norte, only three settlements existed at the time: [[Apodi, Rio Grande do Norte|Apodi]], Portalegre, and Pau dos Ferros, with the latter experiencing steady growth due to its strategic location between two large mountain ranges. Meanwhile, two other mountain settlements, [[Luís Gomes]] and [[São Miguel, Rio Grande do Norte|São Miguel]], began to gain prominence.<ref name="Portal_História"/>

In the 19th century, starting in 1841, residents of the settlement gathered 492 signatures in a petition to elevate Pau dos Ferros to the status of a village. The proposal was submitted to the provincial assembly and approved by the Statistics and Justice Committee but failed to pass in the plenary session. A second attempt in 1847, also unsuccessful, was made when deputy João Inácio de Loiola Barros proposed transferring the village seat from Portalegre to Pau dos Ferros on October 21. On April 12, 1853, provincial deputies Luiz Antônio de Brito Guerra (Baron of Assu) and Elias Antônio Cavalcanti de Albuquerque introduced a new proposal to create a village named Vila Cristina, which was also rejected.<ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" />

Finally, on August 23, 1856, deputy Bevenuto Vicente Fialho presented a new proposal to the provincial assembly in Natal. This project was approved and became provincial law no. 344, sanctioned on September 4 by Governor Antônio Bernardo Passos, elevating the settlement to village status, separating it from Portalegre nearly a century after the parish’s creation. The name "Pau dos Ferros" derives from a tree, likely an [[Microdesmia rigida|oiticica]], marked with iron brands by cowboys who rested under its expansive shade after long journeys.<ref name="Portal_História"/><ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

=== From emancipation to centenary ===

On January 19, 1857, the municipality was officially established in a solemn session at the municipal council, with Manoel Silvestre Ferreira as its first president. On December 1, 1859, the city’s free market was created, which was briefly interrupted but restored in 1868 and has since been held weekly on Saturdays.<ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/> In 1872, during the first [[1872 Brazilian census|national census]], Pau dos Ferros was the third most populous municipality in the province, with 18,637 inhabitants, surpassed only by [[Nova Cruz, Rio Grande do Norte|Nova Cruz]] (20,466) and [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]] (19,126).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv25477_v11_rn.pdf|title=1872 Brazilian Census|access-date=2021-02-04}}</ref> On August 8, 1873, provincial law no. 683 established the Pau dos Ferros District, separated from the Imperatriz district, now [[Martins, Rio Grande do Norte|Martins]].

Following a major [[cholera]] [[epidemic]] in 1862, which killed 199 people according to official records, Pau dos Ferros was struck by a [[Grande Seca|severe drought]] in 1877, lasting three years—the worst in Brazil’s history—followed by outbreaks of [[measles]], [[smallpox]], and [[dysentery]], which further decimated the population due to the lack of hospitals and healthcare facilities.<ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/> Earlier, in 1876, Pau dos Ferros lost territory with the emancipation of São Miguel, which became a municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/sao-miguel/historico|title=Brazil / Rio Grande do Norte / São Miguel / History|author=IBGE|access-date=2022-02-04}}</ref> In 1890, [[Luís Gomes]] followed suit.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/luis-gomes/historico|title=Brazil / Rio Grande do Norte / Luís Gomes / History|author=IBGE|access-date=2022-02-04}}</ref>

From 1889, the year of the [[Proclamation of the Republic (Brazil)|Proclamation of the Republic]], construction began on a reservoir, inaugurated on March 25, 1897, and named "25 de Março." Proposed in 1888 by municipal intendant Joaquim José Correia, it supplied water to the local population for decades. Later, the Gangorra Reservoir, now in Rafael Fernandes, was also built. In the early 20th century, in 1908, Joaquim Correia initiated the construction of a school, named after him and inaugurated in 1911, with Orlando Correia as its first director and Idalina Gurjão and Maria Luíza as its first teachers, who served for many years.<ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

In the late 1910s, one of the bloodiest events in Pau dos Ferros’ history occurred, the "1919 massacre," stemming from a local musical group, the Dr. Guilherme Lins Philharmonic Band, composed of opposing political factions. On April 3, 1919, during a meeting at José Ayres Afonso’s residence, disputes escalated into a violent clash. The situation worsened with the arrival of the police, leading to a shootout with rifles, resulting in deaths and injuries among participants. Consequently, Colonel Joaquim Correia left Pau dos Ferros and settled in Natal. On December 2, 1924, the village of Pau dos Ferros was elevated to city status by state law no. 593, signed by Governor José Augusto Bezerra de Medeiros.<ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

[[File:Busto de Monsenhor Caminha, Pau dos Ferros (RN).jpg|left|thumb|Bust of Manoel Caminha Freire de Andrade (1908–2003), vicar of the Pau dos Ferros parish from 1940 to 1991, at Monsenhor Caminha Square]]
In 1926, the bandit [[Virgulino Ferreira da Silva]], known as ''Lampião'', and his gang entered Pau dos Ferros, committing various thefts, as the city had only one civil policeman at the time. In 1929, [[Picuí]] native Francisco Dantas de Araújo became the first mayor of Pau dos Ferros, overseeing the construction of the current municipal headquarters in 1930, located on Getúlio Vargas Avenue. That same year, following the [[Revolution of 1930|October 24 Revolutionary Movement]], he was deposed, and João Escolástico Bezerra de Medeiros took over, introducing electricity to Pau dos Ferros through a coal-powered plant. Another significant event in the 1930s was the election of Pau dos Ferros native Rafael Fernandes Gurjão to the state governorship by the [[Legislative Assembly of Rio Grande do Norte]], serving from 1935 to 1943.<ref name="Portal_História" /><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

In 1939, Francisco Fernandes Sena became mayor, overseeing the construction of a public slaughterhouse and the first police station in 1940. On February 11, 1940, Father Manoel Caminha Freire de Andrade was appointed parish vicar, becoming a [[Canon (title)|canon]] in 1954 and a [[monsignor]] in 1966, serving until 1991 when he retired due to health issues.<ref name="HisPDF_2013"/> From January 31 to March 15, 1946, Eulália Fernandes Bessa became the first woman to serve as mayor, appointed by federal intervener Miguel Seabra Fagundes.

In the early 1950s, during Dr. Licurgo Nunes Ferreira’s administration (1948–1953), construction began on the municipal pavilion at Praça da Matriz, later named "Cônego Caminha," becoming a key leisure area. Its upper floor housed a dance hall where the band ''Os Brasas'' performed. His administration also saw the construction and inauguration of the 31 de Março School, now the Dr. José Fernandes de Melo State School. In 1953, construction began on the Patronato, inaugurated on July 19, 1954, by the Daughters of Charity. That same year, the parish vicar brought a statue of [[Our Lady of Fátima]] from [[Portugal]], marking one of the municipality’s most significant religious events, and through community efforts, built a chapel in her honor on 13 de Maio Street, inaugurated in 1956. In 1955, the [[Banco do Nordeste]] agency arrived in the municipality.<ref name="PAU DOS FERROS 100 ANOS" /><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

=== From the centenary to the present day ===
[[File:Obelisco, Pau dos Ferros (RN).jpg|thumb|Obelisk of Pau dos Ferros, inaugurated in 1956, commemorating the municipality’s centenary and the parish’s bicentenary]]

On September 4, 1956, Pau dos Ferros celebrated its centennial of political emancipation. That year, several events marked the city’s centenary and the parish’s bicentenary, including the inauguration of the Obelisk at Monsenhor Caminha Square on December 14. Designed by Bahian architect Oscar de Sousa Lelis at the request of then-mayor José Fernandes de Melo, the monument was a highlight of the celebrations. In July 1956, the Pau dos Ferros Centenary Hospital was founded, with Nelson Maia as its founder and director.<ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" />

In 1958, Mayor Paulo Diógenes built the current "4 de Setembro State School" and over 40 kilometers of roads and streets, including the Independence Avenue. On January 1, 1961, Dom Eliseu Simões Mendes founded the Santa Luzia de Marilac Maternity, maintained by the parish’s social assistance league. From 1963 to 1969, during Pedro Diógenes Fernandes’ administration, notable achievements included the creation of the municipal flag, the installation of the first telephones, and the construction of the Pau dos Ferros Dam, which began in 1965 and was completed in 1967. In 1966, the city’s main entertainment venue, Cine São João, opened on Rua 7 de Setembro, at a time when television was not yet available in the city.<ref name="Portal_História"/><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" />

During José Edmilson de Holanda’s first term (1970–1973), Pau dos Ferros received electricity from the Rio Grande do Norte Energy Company (COSERN). In 1970, a severe drought prompted aid from the National Department of Works Against Droughts (DNOCS), which built the city’s [[water treatment plant]], featuring a 19-meter-high water tank with a 500 [[cubic meter]] capacity. On February 28, 1971, the TELERN (Rio Grande do Norte Telecommunications) facilities were inaugurated. Other notable achievements included the opening of the 9 de Janeiro Stadium, commemorating [[Clube Centenário Pauferrense]]’s victory in the first Matutão championship on January 9, 1972, and the Jaime de Aquino Municipal Forum, which has since housed the Pau dos Ferros district court. After José Edmilson’s departure, José Fernandes de Melo returned as mayor until 1977. On September 28, 1976, state decree-law no. 15 established the State University of Rio Grande do Norte (UERN) campus in the municipality, named "Professora Maria Elisa de Albuquerque Maia."<ref name="Portal_História"/><ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade" />

In the 1980s and 1990s, key developments included the inauguration of the municipal [[bus terminal]] in 1984; the launch of the first radio station, AM Cultura do Oeste, on April 1, 1986; the first visit by a [[President of Brazil]], [[José Sarney]], on October 28, 1987; the paving of [[BR-405 (Brazil highway)|BR-405]] along Independence Avenue; and the creation of the Municipal Culture Fair (FECUM) in 1994, which evolved into the Intermunicipal Fair of Education, Culture, Tourism, and Business of Alto Oeste Potiguar (FINECAP) in 1997.<ref name="Portal_História"/><ref name="HisPDF_2013"/>

In the 2000s and 2010s, significant events included the construction of the Nossa Senhora da Conceição Events Square and the reconstruction of the old Matriz Square, which became Monsenhor Caminha Square, inaugurated during a mass on June 27, 2009. Additionally, two federal [[higher education]] institutions were established: the Federal Institute of Rio Grande do Norte (IFRN) and the [[Federal Rural University of the Semi-arid Region]] (UFERSA) in 2009 and 2012, respectively.<ref name="HisPDF_2013"/> The region’s first major [[shopping mall]], Plaza Shopping Center, was inaugurated in January 2019.<ref name="nome">Cavalcante, T. F. F.; Lima Neto, J. C.; Lima, D. F.; Sousa Junior, A. M. ''Spatial dynamics and urban (im)mobility in the center of Pau dos Ferros/RN''. '''Brazilian Journal of Planning and Development''', v. 11, n. 2, p. 427–457, 2022.</ref>

In the 2020 municipal elections, Marianna Almeida Nascimento won with 54.22% of the valid votes, becoming the first woman elected by direct vote<ref>{{cite web |url=https://noticias.uol.com.br/eleicoes/2020/11/15/apuracao-prefeitura-pau-dos-ferros-rn-resultado-1-turno.htm |title=Marianna Almeida (PSD) é eleita prefeita de Pau dos Ferros (RN) |date=2020-11-15 |website=[[Universo Online|UOL]] |access-date=2024-06-30 }}</ref> and the second woman to hold the office since 1946. On June 24, 2021, Pau dos Ferros was visited by [[Jair Bolsonaro]],<ref>{{cite web |url=https://agenciabrasil.ebc.com.br/politica/noticia/2021-06/presidente-assina-ordem-de-servico-para-construcao-do-ramal-do-apodi |title=Presidente assina ordem de serviço para construção do Ramal do Apodi |date=2021-06-24 |website=[[Agência Brasil]] |publisher=[[Empresa Brasil de Comunicação]] |access-date=2024-06-30 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://agorarn.com.br/ultimas/marianna-almeida-e-eleita-primeira-prefeita-mulher-de-pau-dos-ferros/ |title=Marianna Almeida é eleita primeira prefeita mulher de Pau dos Ferros |date=2020-11-15 |website=Agora RN |access-date=2024-06-30 }}</ref> the second Brazilian president to visit the city. Marianna was re-elected in 2024 with the highest vote count in the municipality’s history, securing over 11,000 votes (63% of valid votes).<ref>{{cite web |last1=Norte |first1=Redação Tribuna do |url=https://tribunadonorte.com.br/eleicoes-2024/marianna-almeida-e-reeleita-prefeita-de-pau-dos-ferros/ |title=Marianna Almeida é reeleita prefeita de Pau dos Ferros |work=Tribuna do Norte |date=2024-10-06 |access-date=2025-06-16}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://opotengi.com.br/marianna-almeida-conquista-vitoria-historica-em-pau-dos-ferros/ |title=Marianna Almeida conquista vitória histórica em Pau dos Ferros |author=Jales, João Paulo |date=2024-10-10 |newspaper=O Potengi |access-date=2025-06-16}}</ref>

The local [[terrain]] lies within the Sertaneja Depression, a transitional area between the Apodi Plateau and the [[Borborema Plateau]]. The [[geology]] features metamorphic rocks forming the [[crystalline basement]], dating from the middle [[Precambrian]], between one and 2.5 billion years old. The most common soil type is red-yellow eutrophic [[podzol]], with significant drainage, gentle slopes, high [[Soil fertility|fertility]], and medium texture, which may or may not include gravel.<ref name="Pau dos Ferros" /> Smaller areas have non-calcareous brunisol and [[lithosol]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.uep.cnps.embrapa.br/solos/rn/paudosferros.pdf|title=Exploratory Map - Soil reconnaissance of the municipality of Pau dos Ferros, RN|author=[[Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation]] (EMBRAPA)|access-date=2014-06-22|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151222133509/http://www.uep.cnps.embrapa.br/solos/rn/paudosferros.pdf|archive-date=2015-12-22}}</ref> In the new Brazilian soil classification, the latter is now called neosol, while the others are classified as [[luvisol]].<ref>Jacomine, Paulo Klinger Tito. [https://web.archive.org/web/20140701032225/https://ainfo.cnptia.embrapa.br/digital/bitstream/item/19350/1/Jacomine.pdf The new Brazilian soil classification]. '''Annals of the Pernambuco Academy of Agricultural Science''', v. 5, p. 161–179. [[Recife]]: 2008.</ref>

These less-developed soils are covered by [[Caatinga|hyperxerophilous caatinga]], a low-growing [[vegetation]] typical of the ''[[sertão]]'', leafless during the [[dry season]]. Common species include ''Caesalpinia pyramidalis'', ''[[Pilosocereus pachycladus]]'', faveleiro (''[[Cnidoscolus quercifolius]]''), ''Ziziphus joazeiro'', jurema preta (''[[Mimosa hostilis]]''), quince (''[[Cydonia oblonga]]''), mofumbo (''[[Combretum leprosum]]''), oiticica (''[[Licania rigida]]''), pink ipê (''[[Handroanthus impetiginosus]]''), ''[[Aspidosperma pyrifolium]]'', and dildo cactus (''[[Pilosocereus polygonus]]''). Much of the original vegetation has been cleared for agriculture, particularly [[cotton]] cultivation.<ref name="Pau dos Ferros: história, tradição e realidade"/>

Pau dos Ferros is traversed by the [[Apodi River|Apodi-Mossoró River]], with its entire territory within the homonymous [[hydrographic basin]].<ref name="Bacias hidrográficas">{{cite web|url=http://www.semarh.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/semarh/sistemadeinformacoes/consulta/cBaciaDetalhe.asp?CodigoEstadual=01|title=Apodi/Mossoró Basin|author=Secretariat of State for Environment and Water Resources (SEMARH-RN)|access-date=2012-01-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140201103914/http://www.semarh.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/semarh/sistemadeinformacoes/consulta/cBaciaDetalhe.asp?CodigoEstadual=01|archive-date=2014-02-01|url-status=dead}}</ref> Other watercourses include the Cajazeiras, Capa, Estrema, Meio, and Retiro streams. The main reservoir is the Dr. Pedro Diógenes Fernandes Public Reservoir (''Açude Pau dos Ferros''), built by the National Department of Works Against Droughts (DNOCS) and inaugurated in 1967, located three kilometers from the urban area with a capacity of 54,846,000 m³.<ref name="Pau dos Ferros"/><ref name="Barragem de Pau dos Ferros">{{cite web|url=http://www.semarh.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/semarh/sistemadeinformacoes/consulta/cResFichaTecnica.asp?IdReservatorio=6|title=Technical Data Sheet of the Pau dos Ferros Reservoir|author=SEMARH-RN|access-date=2012-01-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140201103917/http://www.semarh.rn.gov.br/contentproducao/aplicacao/semarh/sistemadeinformacoes/consulta/cResFichaTecnica.asp?IdReservatorio=6|archive-date=2014-02-01|url-status=dead}}</ref> Other reservoirs with capacities of 100,000 m³ or more include the 25 de Março (8,181,000 m³), Capa (640,800 m³), and Benevides José Gonçalves (129,760 m³).<ref name="Pau dos Ferros"/>

The [[climate]] is [[Hot semi-arid climate|hot semi-arid]]<ref name="Pau dos Ferros"/><ref>{{cite web |title=Municipalities located in the Semi-arid region |url=http://www.banconordeste.gov.br/content/aplicacao/Investir_no_Nordeste/Mapa_do_Semi_Arido/gerados/rn_apresentacao.asp |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061005085424/http://www.banconordeste.gov.br/content/aplicacao/Investir_no_Nordeste/Mapa_do_Semi_Arido/gerados/rn_apresentacao.asp |archive-date=2006-10-05 |access-date=2014-06-22}}</ref> (''BSh'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification]]), with high temperatures year-round<ref>{{cite journal|url=http://www2.uol.com.br/omossoroense/040307/conteudo/regional.htm|title=Mossoró, Pau dos Ferros, and Caicó are the hottest cities in RN|author=Costa, Márcio|date=2007-03-04|journal=O Mossoroense|access-date=2014-08-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140903133927/http://www2.uol.com.br/omossoroense/040307/conteudo/regional.htm|archive-date=2014-09-03|url-status=dead}}</ref> and precipitation in the form of [[rain]], concentrated in the first half of the year, with little to no rain in the latter half. According to data from the Rio Grande do Norte Agricultural Research Company (EMPARN) for 1911–1985 and from 1993 onward, the highest 24-hour rainfall recorded in Pau dos Ferros was 147.8&nbsp;mm on April 15, 1982. The record monthly rainfall was 548.9&nbsp;mm in March 1981, and the wettest year was 1974, with 1,505.2&nbsp;mm.<ref name="meteorologia.emparn.rn.gov.br">{{cite web|url=https://meteorologia.emparn.rn.gov.br/relatorios/relatorios-pluviometricos?tipo=acumuladaCorrente|title=Relatório pluviométrico|author=Empresa de Pesquisa Agropecuária do Rio Grande do Norte (EMPARN)|access-date=2022-01-05}}</ref> Since August 2019, the lowest temperature recorded in Pau dos Ferros was on July 22, 2020, with a minimum of 17.7 °C, while the highest reached 41.1 °C on October 31, 2023.<ref name="siemu.ufersa.edu.br">{{cite web|url=https://siemu.ufersa.edu.br/hobolink/paudosferros|title=Hobolink: Ufersa Pau dos Ferros|access-date=2022-02-04}}</ref>

The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of the municipality is considered medium, according to the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). In 2010, reported in 2013, its value was 0.678, ranking 10th in Rio Grande do Norte and 2,481st in Brazil. The longevity index was 0.803, the income index 0.666, and the education index 0.584.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/arquivos/ranking-idhm-2010.pdf |title=Ranking IDH-M 2010 |date=2010 |work=Atlas of Human Development in Brazil|publisher=[[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP) |access-date=2013-08-03 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130806022754/http://www.pnud.org.br/arquivos/ranking-idhm-2010.pdf|archive-date=2013-08-06}}</ref>

From 2000 to 2010, the [[Gini index]] decreased from 0.57 to 0.54, and the proportion of people with a per capita household income of up to R$140 dropped from 38.6% to 20.6%, a 46.6% reduction. In 2010, 79.4% of the population lived above the [[poverty line]], 13.2% between the indigence and poverty lines, and 7.4% below the indigence line. The richest 20% accounted for 58.9% of the municipality’s total income, nearly 17 times that of the poorest 20%, at 3.5%.<ref name="AtlasBrasil_PaudosFerros-RN">{{cite web|url=http://www.atlasbrasil.org.br/perfil/municipio/240940|title=Pau dos Ferros, RN|access-date=2022-02-04}}</ref><ref name="ODS-01">{{cite web|url=http://rd.portalods.com.br/relatorios/9/erradicacao-da-pobreza/BRA002024101/pau-dos-ferros---rn|title=SDG 01 Poverty eradication|access-date=2013-08-03}}</ref>

== Politics ==
According to the organic law of Pau dos Ferros, enacted on April 2, 1990, the municipality’s powers are the [[Executive (government)|executive]] and [[Legislature|legislative]], with representatives elected by [[direct vote]] for four-year terms. The executive is led by the [[mayor]], supported by a freely appointed cabinet of secretaries. The legislative is exercised by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], which oversees the executive and drafts and votes on essential laws, including the annual budget law and the multi-year plan.<ref name="Lei-orgânica">{{cite web|url=https://www.paudosferros.rn.gov.br/arquivos/170/LEIS_LO_1990_0000001.pdf|title=ORGANIC LAW OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF PAU DOS FERROS|publisher=Pau dos Ferros Municipal Council|date=1990-04-02|access-date=2013-05-22|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190421220434/https://www.paudosferros.rn.gov.br/arquivos/170/LEIS_LO_1990_0000001.pdf|archive-date=2019-04-21}}</ref> Since 2025, the municipality has 13 councilors.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/rn/rio-grande-do-norte/eleicoes/2024/noticia/2024/12/31/prefeita-de-pau-dos-ferros-rn-toma-posse-nesta-quarta-1o-veja-lista-de-vereadores-eleitos.ghtml|title=Mayor of Pau dos Ferros (RN) takes office this Wednesday (1st)|date=2024-12-31|access-date=2025-02-20}}</ref>

Several municipal councils are active, including civil defense, children’s and adolescents’ rights, elderly rights, education, environment, and guardianship.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/pau-dos-ferros/pesquisa/1/74454?ano=2014|title=MUNIC - Profile of Brazilian Municipalities|author=IBGE|date=2014|access-date=2017-01-18}}</ref> Pau dos Ferros is home to a state judiciary district, covering [[Água Nova]], Encanto, Francisco Dantas, Rafael Fernandes, [[Riacho de Santana, Rio Grande do Norte|Riacho de Santana]], and São Francisco do Oeste.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://leisestaduais.com.br/rn/lei-complementar-n-643-2018-rio-grande-do-norte-altera-a-lei-complementar-estadual-n-643-de-21-de-dezembro-de-2018-que-regula-a-divisao-e-a-organizacao-judiciarias-do-estado-do-rio-grande-do-norte|title=COMPLEMENTARY LAW No. 643, OF DECEMBER 21, 2018|access-date=2024-09-08}}</ref> According to the [[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE), Pau dos Ferros belongs to the 40th electoral zone of Rio Grande do Norte, with 21,054 voters in December 2024, representing 0.7956% of the state’s electorate.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sig.tse.jus.br/ords/dwapr/r/seai/sig-eleitor-eleitorado-mensal/home?session=104021167841524|title=Electorate|author=[[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE)|access-date=2025-06-06}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The [[Gross Domestic Product]] of Pau dos Ferros in 2016 was R$431,562,010, the 19th largest in Rio Grande do Norte and 1,421st in Brazil, with R$225,853,220 from the [[tertiary sector]]; R$128,579,650 from administration, healthcare, education, and social security; R$46,785,570 in [[taxes]]; R$25,195,280 from [[Secondary sector of the economy|industry]]; and R$5,148,300 from the [[primary sector]]. The [[GDP per capita]] was R$14,287.29.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/pau-dos-ferros/pesquisa/38/46996|title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities|author=IBGE|access-date=2019-04-21}}</ref>

In 2010, among the municipal population aged 18 or older, 58% were [[Workforce|economically active]] and employed, 33.8% were inactive, and 8.3% were active but unemployed. Among the active employed population in the same age group, 49.07% worked in [[Service (economics)|services]], 22.42% in [[commerce]], 10.21% in [[agriculture]], 8.16% in [[construction]], 5.16% in [[manufacturing industries]], and 0.42% in [[public utilities]].<ref name="AtlasBrasil_PaudosFerros-RN"/> Population growth in Pau dos Ferros necessitated the expansion of banking services for low-income families through [[Government of Brazil|federal government]] policies, which was implemented.<ref name="Portal_Elaboração">{{cite web|url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=164|title=Elaboration begins in April|author=Paulo, João|date=2010-03-26|publisher=Portal Pau dos Ferros|access-date=2011-02-28|archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/5wq7Ymqnh?url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=164|archive-date=2011-02-28|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/rn/rio-grande-do-norte/noticia/2014/03/distrito-industrial-de-monte-alegre-rn-e-inaugurado.html|title=Monte Alegre Industrial District, RN, is inaugurated|date=2014-03-15|publisher=G1|access-date=2015-06-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140318030720/https://g1.globo.com/rn/rio-grande-do-norte/noticia/2014/03/distrito-industrial-de-monte-alegre-rn-e-inaugurado.html|archive-date=2014-03-18}}</ref>

According to the IBGE, in 2017, the municipality had a herd of 18,000 [[poultry]] (chickens, roosters, and chicks), 8,500 [[cattle]], 6,500 [[sheep]], 2,500 [[goats]], 2,000 [[pigs]], and 900 [[equines]].<ref name="IBGE-pecuária-2017"/> Regarding the cultivation of temporary crops that year, 24 tons of [[corn]] and 8 tons of [[bean]]s were produced.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/pau-dos-ferros/pesquisa/14/10193|title=Agricultural Production - Temporary Crops|author=IBGE|access-date=2019-04-21}}</ref> In terms of permanent crops, 8,000 [[coconut]]s, 34 tons of [[banana]]s, 13 tons of [[mango]]es, and 1 ton of [[cashew nuts]] were produced.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/pau-dos-ferros/pesquisa/15/11863|title=Agricultural Production - Permanent Crops|author=IBGE|access-date=2019-04-21}}</ref> Additionally, 1,829,000 liters of [[milk]] from 3,200 milked cows and 1,300 kilograms of [[honey]] were produced.<ref name="IBGE-pecuária-2017">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/pau-dos-ferros/pesquisa/18/16459|title=Livestock|author=IBGE|access-date=2019-04-21}}</ref>

=== Healthcare ===
[[File:Secretaria Municipal de Saúde, Pau dos Ferros (RN).jpg|thumb|left|280px|Current headquarters of the Municipal Health Department (SESAU), located at the same site as the former Citizen Service Center]]

The healthcare network in Pau dos Ferros includes twelve primary healthcare units (UBS), one psychosocial care center (CAPS), and two hospitals,<ref>{{cite web |title=Infraestrutura Urbana & Social - Pau dos Ferros - RN |url=https://paudosferros.rn.gov.br/secretaria.php?sec=7 |access-date=February 4, 2021 |work=Centro de Inovação Sesi - Longevidade e Produtividade}}</ref> namely the Dr. Nelson Maia Hospital,<ref>{{cite web |title=HOSPITAL DR NELSON MAIA |url=https://cnes2.datasus.gov.br/Mod_Ambulatorial.asp?VCo_Unidade=2409402407868 |access-date=February 4, 2022}}</ref> the oldest, maintained by the Pau dos Ferros Centenário Hospital Association, and the Dr. Cleodon Carlos de Andrade Regional Hospital (HRCCA), inaugurated on March 10, 1990, offering exclusive service to users of the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS) and operating 24 hours a day with on-call services.<ref>{{cite web |date=2020-08-07 |title=Governo do RN investe na melhoria da saúde no Alto Oeste |trans-title=RN government invests in improving healthcare in the Upper West region |url=http://www.saude.rn.gov.br/Conteudo.asp?TRAN=ITEM&TARG=237185&ACT=&PAGE=&PARM=&LBL=Materia |access-date=May 23, 2013 |language=pt}}</ref> Adjacent to the HRCCA is the headquarters of the VI Regional Public Healthcare Unit of Rio Grande do Norte (VI URSAP), which serves 36 other municipalities in the state.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.saude.rn.gov.br/content/aplicacao/sesap/imprensa/enviados/noticia_detalhe.asp?nCodigoNoticia=29616|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140203091731/http://www.saude.rn.gov.br/content/aplicacao/sesap/imprensa/enviados/noticia_detalhe.asp?nCodigoNoticia=29616|archive-date=2014-02-03|title=Unidades Regionais de Saúde contribuem para fortalecimento do SUS no RN|date=February 10, 2012|publisher=Secretaria de Saúde Pública do Rio Grande do Norte|access-date=May 23, 2013}}</ref>

In April 2010, the municipality's professional healthcare network consisted of 198 [[physician]]s, 138 nursing assistants, fifty nursing technicians, 31 [[nurse]]s, 26 [[pharmacist]]s, 26 [[dentist]]s, eleven [[physiotherapist]]s, nine [[speech therapist]]s, eight [[social worker]]s, seven [[nutritionist]]s, and six [[psychologist]]s, totaling 510 professionals.<ref>{{cite web|url=ftp://ftp.datasus.gov.br/caderno/geral/rn/RN_Pau_dos_Ferros_Geral.xls|title=Cadernos de Informações de Saúde - Pau dos Ferros/RN|publisher=DATASUS|access-date=January 31, 2014}}</ref> In the same year, the [[life expectancy]] at birth was 73.2 years, the [[infant mortality]] rate was 18.2 per thousand births, and the [[fertility rate]] was 2.1 children per woman.<ref name="AtlasBrasil_PaudosFerros-RN"/> According to data from the [[Ministry of Health (Brazil)|Ministry of Health]], 56 cases of [[HIV/AIDS|AIDS]] were recorded in Pau dos Ferros between 1990 and 2016, and from 2001 to 2012, {{formatnum:3166}} cases of [[Dengue fever|dengue]] and four cases of [[leishmaniasis]] were reported.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://rd.portalods.com.br/relatorios/12/saude-e-bem-estar/BRA002024101/pau-dos-ferros---rn|title=ODS 03 Saúde e bem estar|access-date=January 31, 2014|archive-date=April 4, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230404193651/http://rd.portalods.com.br/relatorios/12/saude-e-bem-estar/BRA002024101/pau-dos-ferros---rn|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The education factor of the HDI in the municipality reached a score of 0.584 in 2010,<ref name="AtlasBrasil_PaudosFerros-RN"/> while the [[literacy rate]] of the population over ten years old, as indicated by the 2010 census, was 83% (78.8% for men and 87% for women).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/1383#/n6/2409407/v/all/p/all/c2/all/d/v1646%201/l/v,p+c2,t/resultado|title=Tabela 1383 - Taxa de alfabetização das pessoas de 10 anos ou mais de idade por sexo|date=2010|author=IBGE|access-date=July 19, 2014}}</ref> The completion rates for elementary (ages 15 to 17) and high school (ages 18 to 24) were 54.4% and 40.4%, respectively, with a literacy rate of 95.7% for the population aged 15 to 24.<ref name="ODS-04">{{cite web|url=http://rd.portalods.com.br/relatorios/13/educacao-de-qualidade/BRA002024101/pau-dos-ferros---rn|title=ODS 04 Educação de qualidade|access-date=July 19, 2014|archive-date=April 4, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230404193650/http://rd.portalods.com.br/relatorios/13/educacao-de-qualidade/BRA002024101/pau-dos-ferros---rn|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2010, Pau dos Ferros had an expected duration of schooling of 10.15 years, higher than the state average (9.54 years). The percentage of children aged five to six in school was 85.64%, and for those aged eleven to thirteen attending elementary school, it was 88.69%. Among young people, the proportion of those aged fifteen to seventeen with completed elementary education was 57.15%, and for those aged 18 to 20 with completed high school, it was 34.58%. Considering only the population aged 25 and older, 40.31% had completed elementary education, 29.45% had completed high school, 22.46% were [[illiterate]], and 9.31% had completed [[higher education]].<ref name="AtlasBrasil_PaudosFerros-RN"/>

In 2018, the age-grade distortion among elementary school students, meaning those older than the recommended age, was 7.2% for the early years and 23.2% for the final years, with a distortion rate of 25.2% in high school.<ref name="ODS-04"/> In the same year, the municipality had a network of 24 [[elementary school]]s (with 213 teachers), 20 preschools (51 teachers), and five high schools (108 teachers).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?codmun=240940&idtema=156|title=Rio Grande do Norte » Pau dos Ferros » ensino - matrículas, docentes e rede escolar - 2015|author=IBGE|access-date=January 23, 2017|archive-date=2017-02-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170202100928/http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?codmun=240940&idtema=156}}</ref>

In terms of [[higher education]], the municipality has three public [[higher education institution]]s: University of the State of Rio Grande do Norte (UERN), Federal Institute of Rio Grande do Norte (IFRN), and [[Federal Rural University of the Semi-arid Region]] (UFERSA), the first being state-run and the latter two federally funded. Among private institutions are the Catarina de Siena Nursing School, the Evolução do Alto Oeste Potiguar College (FACEP), the Potiguar University (UnP), and Anhanguera University.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://emec.mec.gov.br/|title=Cadastro Nacional de Cursos e Instituições de Educação Superior|access-date=May 18, 2021}}</ref>

=== Crime and public safety ===
[[File:VII BPM em Pau dos Ferros (RN).jpg|thumb|left|280px|Headquarters of the 7th Military Police Battalion of Rio Grande do Norte “Coronel André Fernandes”]]

According to the 2016 Violence Map, with data from 2014, published by the Sangari Institute, among municipalities with more than 20,000 inhabitants, the [[homicide]] rate by [[firearm]]s in Pau dos Ferros was 14.9 per 100,000 inhabitants, ranking 36th in the state and 964th nationally.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.mapadaviolencia.org.br/mapa2016_armas.php |title=2016 {{!}} Homicídio por armas de fogo no Brasil |publisher=Instituto Sangari |date=2016 |access-date=April 21, 2019 |archive-date=April 29, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190429115552/http://www.mapadaviolencia.org.br/mapa2016_armas.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>

To strengthen security and reduce crime, the 7th Military Police Battalion (VII BPM), based in Pau dos Ferros, collaborates with local authorities to implement measures related to [[public safety]],<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=633 |title=Prefeito reúne autoridades para debater segurança pública no município de Pau dos Ferros |author=HOLANDA, Jean Carlos |date=January 19, 2011 |publisher=Portal Pau dos Ferros |access-date=February 28, 2011 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/5wqdk535x?url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=633 |archive-date=March 1, 2011 |url-status=live }}</ref> such as the establishment of a municipal guard.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camarapaudosferros.com.br/site/page/noticia.php?id=304|title=Antônio Avelino solicita, também, a implantação da Guarda Municipal de Pau dos Ferros|date=May 28, 2015|publisher=Câmara Municipal de Pau dos Ferros|access-date=September 1, 2015|archive-date=2015-09-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150923195959/http://www.camarapaudosferros.com.br/site/page/noticia.php?id=304}}</ref> There are also initiatives to improve the working conditions of police officers and the infrastructure available for their activities, including vehicles, weapons, and facilities used by the force.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correiodatarde.com.br/editorias/correio_politico-60459 |title=Prefeito de Pau dos Ferros exige mais segurança para região |publisher=Correio da Tarde |date=January 20, 2011 |access-date=February 28, 2011 |archive-date=2012-10-31|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121031010849/http://www.correiodatarde.com.br/editorias/correio_politico-60459}}</ref>

Recently, the Pau dos Ferros city government, in partnership with the civil and military state police and the telecommunications provider Brisanet, installed a 24-hour video surveillance system with cameras at strategic points in the city, inaugurated in December 2018, with the operations room located at the VII BPM facilities.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.paudosferros.rn.gov.br/informa.php?id=285|title=Segurança: Prefeitura de Pau dos Ferros intensifica instalação de câmeras do sistema de videomonitoramento|date=November 9, 2018|publisher=Prefeitura Municipal de Pau dos Ferros|access-date=April 21, 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalacontecern.com.br/site/noticia/em-pau-dos-ferros-prefeitura-inaugura-central-de-videomonitoramento|title=Em Pau dos Ferros, Prefeitura inaugura Central de Videomonitoramento|date=December 20, 2018|access-date=April 21, 2019|archive-date=2019-04-21|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190421204322/http://www.portalacontecern.com.br/site/noticia/em-pau-dos-ferros-prefeitura-inaugura-central-de-videomonitoramento}}</ref>

== Culture and leisure ==
The Municipal Secretariat of Culture and Tourism (SECULT) is the municipal government body responsible for education, culture, and tourism in Pau dos Ferros, tasked with organizing cultural and tourism-related activities and projects.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.secultpdf.com.br/#!quemsomos/cjg9|title=Quem somos|access-date=September 1, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303191025/http://www.secultpdf.com.br/#!quemsomos/cjg9|archive-date=2016-03-03}}</ref> Pau dos Ferros is recognized as the ''National Capital of Language and Literature Studies'', a title conferred during the VII National Colloquium of Professors of Portuguese Language and Literature Teaching Methodology, held in August 2010 at the Campus of UERN in the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=379 |title=Pau dos Ferros vira capital nacional dos estudos de língua e literatura |author=Paulo, João |date=August 11, 2010 |publisher=Jornal de Fato |access-date=March 2, 2011 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/5wv00DUqp?url=http://paudosferros.com/info/noticia.php?id=379 |archive-date=March 3, 2011 |url-status=live }}</ref>
