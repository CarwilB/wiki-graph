'''Campos do Jordão''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈkɐ̃puz du ʒoʁˈdɐ̃w}}) is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in southeastern [[Brazil]]. It is part of the [[Metropolitan Region of Vale do Paraíba e Litoral Norte]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lei Complementar nº 1.166, de 09 de janeiro de 2012 |url=http://www.al.sp.gov.br/leis/legislacao-do-estado/ |access-date=2025-09-03 |website=www.al.sp.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref> The population is 52,405 (2020 est.) in an area of {{convert|290.52|km2|mi2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2368S Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city is situated {{convert|1,628|m|ft}} above sea level and is the highest city in Brazil.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE disponibiliza coordenadas e altitudes para 21.304 localidades brasileiras |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/presidencia/noticias/noticia_visualiza.php?id_noticia=2021&id_pagina=1 |publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |trans-title=IBGE provides coordinates and altitudes to 21,304 Brazilian locations |language=pt |access-date=September 10, 2012}}</ref>

There are numerous outdoor activities for tourists and winter residents. These include [[hiking]], [[mountain climbing]], treetop cable swings (''arborismo''), [[horseback riding]], and [[All-terrain vehicle|ATV]] and [[motorbike]] riding. July, of [[winter]] season vacations, sees an enormous influx of visitors (more than quadrupling the city's population), due in part to the winter [[festival]] of [[classical music]].{{Citation needed|date=March 2019}}

Its attractions throughout the year include German, Swiss and Italian cuisine restaurants, bars, and a cable car. There are many ''pousadas'' ([[Public house#Inns|inn]]s) and [[chalet]]s. Also, in order to cater to the large number of visitors, several bars, lounges, discos and [[Nightclub|clubs]] open during the winter months.{{Citation needed|date=March 2019}}

==Economy==
The city's economy is based mainly on [[tourism]]; due to its location at high elevation in the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]], and traditional European-style architecture. Buildings are mostly [[vernacular architecture]] from [[Architecture of Germany|German]], [[Switzerland|Swiss]], or [[Architecture of Italy|Italian]] models. Many of the wealthiest residents in the state of São Paulo have winter [[country house]]s here.

Despite the high income of many visitors, the [[Human Development Index|HDI]] (0.820 in 2004) of Campos do Jordão is not very high because the owners of the houses in the best neighbourhoods are not regular inhabitants; these houses are used only during the holidays. The city can be reached from [[São Paulo]] mainly by road through the [[Rodovia Floriano Rodrigues Pinheiro]]. There is also a picturesque railroad from [[Pindamonhangaba]], used mostly by tourists. At the end of the main road going through Campos do Jordão, there is a state park called Horto Florestal.

==Geography==
The city is located in the northeastern side of the State of São Paulo, bordering [[Minas Gerais]] in the north. Campos do Jordão is at a distance of 180{{nbsp}}km (112 miles) from the [[São Paulo|City of São Paulo]], 334{{nbsp}}km (208 miles) from the [[Rio de Janeiro|City of Rio de Janeiro]], and 486{{nbsp}}km (302 miles) from [[Belo Horizonte]]. The [[Mantiqueira Mountains]] provide unique panoramic views, and the municipality's region still has undeveloped old growth [[Atlantic Forest]] [[habitat]]. The native [[Araucaria angustifolia|Brazilian Paraná pine]] (''Araucaria angustifolia'') is found here.

[[Image:Camposdojordao-01.jpg|thumb|250x250px|left|A shopping mall in a Swiss-inspired style|alt=]]The municipality contains the {{convert|8341|ha|adj=on}} [[Campos do Jordão State Park]], created in 1941.<ref>{{citation|title=Sobre o Parque|url=http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-campos-do-jordao/sobre-o-parque/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161001201540/http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-campos-do-jordao/sobre-o-parque/|url-status=dead|publisher=SAP: Sistema Ambiental Paulista|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Sobre o Parque – SAP}}|access-date=2016-09-27|archive-date=2016-10-01}}</ref> It contains the {{convert|503|ha|adj=on}} [[Mananciais de Campos do Jordão State Park]], created in 1993 to protect the water supply of the municipal seat.<ref>{{citation|title=Parque Estadual Mananciais de Campos do Jordão|url=http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-mananciais-campos-do-jordao/sobre/|publisher=SAP: Sistema Ambiental Paulista|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Mananciais de Campos do Jordão – SAP}}|access-date=2016-10-01|archive-date=2017-05-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170504171755/http://www3.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-mananciais-campos-do-jordao/sobre/|url-status=dead}}</ref> It also contains the {{convert|28800|ha|adj=on}} [[Campos do Jordão Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1984.<ref>{{Cite web |title=APA Campos do Jordao - Sistema Ambiental Paulista - Governo de SP |url=http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/apa-campos-do-jordao/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161002155713/http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/apa-campos-do-jordao/ |archive-date=2016-10-02 |access-date=2025-09-03 |website=www.ambiente.sp.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref>

Campos do Jordão is located on a crystalline plateau where the High Felds are located (in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]: Altos Campos) formed from the [[quaternary]], increasing the geomorphological risks with the increase of [[urbanization]] and seen inadequate occupations as in straight slopes.<ref>{{Cite journal|last1=Hiruma|first1=Silvio Takashi|last2=Modenesi-Gauttieri|first2=May Christine|date=2004-12-01|title=A expansão urbana no planalto de Campos do Jordão: diagnóstico geomorfológico para fins de planejamento|journal=Revista do Instituto Geológico|language=en|volume=25|issue=1–2|pages=1–28|doi=10.5935/0100-929X.20040001|issn=2176-1892|doi-access=free}}</ref> These areas may be at risk of [[landslide]]. The municipality has rounded topos and amphitheaters where organic [[clay]] is found due to [[Erosion|erosive processes]] and due to this constitution its characteristic is the concentration of [[water]].<ref>{{Cite journal|last1=Coutinho|first1=Marcos Pellegrini|last2=Londe|first2=Luciana de Resende|last3=Soriano|first3=Érico|date=2017-09-08|journal=Boletim de Geografia|language=pt|volume=35|issue=3|pages=12–25|doi=10.4025/bolgeogr.v35i3.31826|issn=2176-4786|title=Percepção do Risco de Moradores de Áreas Suscetíveis a Movimentos de Massa No Município de Campos do Jordão-Sp|doi-access=free}}</ref>

A former [[Governor (Brazil)|state governor]] had a winter residence in the city, the Boa Vista Palace, which is now a museum in the city.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.acervo.sp.gov.br/acervo_english/palacios/palacio_boavista.html|title=Palácio Boa Vista|publisher=[[The Artistic-Cultural Collection of the Governmental Palaces of the State of São Paulo]]|trans-title=Boa Vista Palace|archive-url=https://archive.today/20121129051318/http://www.acervo.sp.gov.br/acervo_english/palacios/palacio_boavista.html|archive-date=2012-11-29|url-status=dead|access-date=September 10, 2012}}</ref>

Campos do Jordão features a [[Temperateness|temperate]] [[oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'', [[Trewartha climate classification|Trewartha]]: ''Cfll''),<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.mindat.org/feature-3467684.html|title=Climatic characteristics of Campos do Jordão|access-date=2025-03-02 |website=Mindat}}</ref> characterized by warm to mild summers, cool winters and a fairly comfortable and pleasant climate year-round.<ref name=":12">{{Cite web |last=Velloso Galvão |first=Marília |date=January–March 1967 |title=Regiões bioclimáticas do Brasil |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/periodicos/115/rbg_1967_v29_n1.pdf |publisher=Revista Brasileira de Geografia (v.29, n. 1)}}</ref><ref name=":22">{{Cite web |title=Campos do Jordão, Sao Paulo Köppen Climate Classification (Weatherbase) |url=http://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather-summary.php3?s=598699&cityname=Campos+do+Jord%25E3o%252C+Sao+Paulo%252C+Brazil&units= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220613042744/http://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather-summary.php3?s=598699&cityname=Campos+do+Jord%25E3o%252C+Sao+Paulo%252C+Brazil&units= |archive-date=June 13, 2022 |access-date=2019-03-09 |website=Weatherbase}}</ref><ref name=":0" />

Despite being situated at a higher elevation than some of the highest portions of the [[Santa Catarina (state)|state of Santa Catarina]], the city still enjoys comparatively warmer winters on average, given its lower latitude. Over extended periods of time, the area also sees sporadic snowfall, which is associated with the drier mid-year air currents. Given its mild-temperature [[montane ecosystem]], the [[Araucaria angustifolia|Araucaria pine]], commonly associated with [[South Region, Brazil|Brazil's southernmost region]], naturally grows around the area, specially along the higher grounds of the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite journal |last1=Dubreuil |first1=Vincent |last2=Fante |first2=Karime Pechutti |last3=Planchon |first3=Olivier |last4=Neto |first4=João Lima Sant'anna |date=2018-09-24 |title=Os tipos de climas anuais no Brasil : uma aplicação da classificação de Köppen de 1961 a 2015 |journal=Confins. Revue franco-brésilienne de géographie / Revista franco-brasilera de geografia |volume=37 |language=fr |issue=37 |doi=10.4000/confins.15738 |issn=1958-9212 |doi-access=free|hdl=11449/186498 |hdl-access=free }}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Velloso Galvão |first=Marília |date=January–March 1967 |title=Regiões bioclimáticas do Brasil |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/periodicos/115/rbg_1967_v29_n1.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220629150738/https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/periodicos/115/rbg_1967_v29_n1.pdf |archive-date=June 29, 2022 |publisher=Revista Brasileira de Geografia (v.29, n. 1)}}</ref><ref name=":22"/> The city's average annual [[evapotranspiration]] rate is one of the lowest in the [[São Paulo (state)|state of São Paulo]], which can lead to water shortages during the drier periods of the year.<ref>{{Cite web |title=CIIAGRO - Centro integrado de informações agrometeorológicas |url=http://www.ciiagro.sp.gov.br/climasp/conceituacao.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220614234407/http://www.ciiagro.sp.gov.br/climasp/conceituacao.html |archive-date=June 14, 2022 |access-date=2019-03-09 |website=www.ciiagro.sp.gov.br}}</ref>

The average annual precipitation is approximately 1850{{nbsp}}mm (72.83{{nbsp}}in), with the majority of it falling in December and January.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |title= INMET - Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia |url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/portal/index.php?r=clima/normaisclimatologicas |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190330102127/http://www.inmet.gov.br/portal/index.php?r=clima/normaisclimatologicas |archive-date=March 30, 2019 |access-date=2019-03-09 |website=www.inmet.gov.br}}</ref> The averaged daily  mean temperature for the city  is 14.5{{nbsp}}°C (58.1{{nbsp}}°F); while there are many colder cities in Brazil, the prolonged duration of cooler weather in Campos do Jordão tends to result in lower average temperatures than other municipalities like [[Curitiba]], [[Lages]] or [[Canela, Rio Grande do Sul|Canela]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.las.inpe.br/~ppci/welcome_arquivos/DDValeriano%20-%20OBT%20dez2013.pdf|title=Análise multiescala da distribuição espacial da Araucaria angustifolia (Bertol.) Occasionally, average winter temperatures may also rise and remain above freezing, as was the case in 1999.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.revistaturismo.com.br/Dicasdeviagem/camposdojordao.htm|title=Revista Turismo - Additionally, while recorded dates for snowfall are not unanimously agreed upon, the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET), in operation since 1944, has not documented any instances of snowflakes in the area.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bartolomei |first=Marcelo |title=Folha Online - Cotidiano - Meteorologistas divergem sobre possibilidade de neve em Campos do Jordão - 21/07/2000 |url=https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/cotidiano/ult95u5377.shtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190328070320/https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/cotidiano/ult95u5377.shtml |archive-date=March 28, 2019 |access-date=2019-03-09 |website=www1.folha.uol.com.br}}</ref>

According to data from INMET, the record low temperature ever recorded in Campos do Jordão was -7.2{{nbsp}}°C (19.04{{nbsp}}°F), on June 6, 1988.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/inicio.php|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados diários - temperatura mínima (°C) - Campos do Jordão|website=www.inmet.gov.br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> Furthermore, according to the Forecast Center (CPTEC), the winter for that year was also the coldest on record. In contrast, between 2008 and 2018, the lowest temperature recorded was a considerably warmer -3.8{{nbsp}}°C (25.16{{nbsp}}°F), indicating that [[urbanization]] can significantly lessen colder conditions.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/vale-do-paraiba-regiao/inverno/2018/noticia/inverno-de-1988-foi-o-mais-rigoroso-da-historia-em-campos-do-jordao-diz-cptec.ghtml|title=Inverno de 1988 foi o mais rigoroso da história em Campos do Jordão, diz Cptec|website=G1|date=11 July 2018 |language=pt-br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> Prior to 1988, unofficial records show even lower temperatures: -7.4{{nbsp}}°C (18.68{{nbsp}}°F) on June 26, 1918, -8{{nbsp}}°C (17.6{{nbsp}}°F) on July 25, 1923,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.camposdojordaocultura.com.br/categoria-det.asp?id_cat=70&Categoria=Inverno+em+Campos+do+Jord%25E3o|title=Fotografias - Inverno em Campos do Jord%E3o - Campos do Jordão Cultura|website=www.camposdojordaocultura.com.br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> and -8.7{{nbsp}}°C (16.34{{nbsp}}°F) in July 1926.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://meteorologiaeclima.blogspot.com.br/2010/09/temperatura-de-87-graus-abaixo-de-zero.html|title=Temperatura de 8,7 graus abaixo de zero em Campos do Jordão no ano de 1926|last=Alberto|first=Carlos|date=September 17, 2010|website=Meteorologia e Clima}}</ref> During the same period, the record high temperature was 30.5{{nbsp}}°C (86.9{{nbsp}}°F) on September 17, 1961. Temperatures also reached 30{{nbsp}}°C (86{{nbsp}}°F) on two other occasions: September 21, 1961, and October 14, 1963, though limited data is available.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/inicio.php|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados diários - temperatura máxima (°C) - Campos do Jordão|website=www.inmet.gov.br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> During winter, colder temperatures are more common in June and July, even though theytypically rise above freezing in the afternoon, while ultimately remaining cool. September and April are generally considered to be consisted of more pleasant days, with temperatures above 30{{nbsp}}°C (86{{nbsp}}°F) being rare occurrences.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|url=https://pt.weatherspark.com/y/30407/Clima-caracter%C3%ADstico-em-Campos-do-Jord%C3%A3o-Brasil-durante-o-ano|title=Clima característico em Campos do Jordão, Brasil durante o ano - Weather Spark|website=pt.weatherspark.com|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref>

The highest 24-hour precipitation total in Campos do Jordão was 146.7{{nbsp}}mm (5.77{{nbsp}}in) on March 10, 1965. Other significant precipitation events include 129{{nbsp}}mm (5.07{{nbsp}}in) on October 14, 1995, 121.1{{nbsp}}mm (4.76{{nbsp}}in) on January 24, 1964, 118.2{{nbsp}}mm (4.65{{nbsp}}in) on March 8, 1966, 111.4{{nbsp}}mm (4.38{{nbsp}}in) on December 24, 1971, 108.4{{nbsp}}mm (4.26{{nbsp}}in) on May 25, 2005, 106.4{{nbsp}}mm (4.18{{nbsp}}in) on December 14, 1971, 104.2{{nbsp}}mm (4.10{{nbsp}}in) on December 2, 1963, 102.8{{nbsp}}mm (4.04{{nbsp}}in) on November 20, 1971, and 101.2{{nbsp}}mm (3.98{{nbsp}}in) on December 22, 1966.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/inicio.php|title=BDMEP - série histórica - precipitação (mm) - Campos do Jordão|website=www.inmet.gov.br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> The highest ever precipitation month in Campos do Jordão was December 1971, with a recorded total of 606.6{{nbsp}}mm (23.88{{nbsp}}in).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/inicio.php|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados mensais - precipitação total (mm) - Campos do Jordão|website=www.inmet.gov.br|access-date=2019-03-09}}</ref> Interestingly, [[Christmas|Christmas Day]] has the highest average rainfall in Campos do Jordão, with a 75% chance of precipitation; this trend of high rainfall continues throughout December and January.<ref name=":4" /> It is also noted that 75% of total annual rainfall in Campos do Jordão occurs between spring and summer, although variations in altitude and the urban distribution amongst valleys elicit small fluctuations in the total precipitation across the municipality, with humidity levels decreasing towards the interior of the [[plateau]].<ref>{{Cite journal|last1=Nunes|first1=Lucí Hidalgo|last2=Modenesi-Gauttieri|first2=May Christine|date=1998-12-01|title=Processos geocriogênicos quaternários nas cimeiras da Mantiqueira, com considerações climáticas|journal=Revista do Instituto Geológico|language=pt|volume=19|issue=1–2|pages=19–30|doi=10.5935/0100-929X.19980003|issn=2176-1892|doi-access=free}}</ref>

The local seasonal variation of [[Cloud cover|cloudiness]] is also remarked upon: from the beginning of April to the middle of October, the city sees the most [[sunlight]], with the end of August experiencing the least cloud cover, allowing for up to 70% clear skies. The remainder of the year tends to be cloudier, with mid-January seeing up to 77% overcast skies. The duration between the shortest and longest days of the year typically aligns with astronomical cycles.{{citation needed|date=September 2025}}

For the latter half of the year, stronger winds are usually recorded, particularly between September and October, with average speeds of 8{{nbsp}}km/h (4.97{{nbsp}}mph). In contrast, the period between February and March is characterized by calmer winds, with average speeds of 6{{nbsp}}km/h (3.73{{nbsp}}mph). Northernly winds prevail for about two-thirds of the year, especially from late April all through September. Easternly winds, the second most common, occur for nearly four months of the year.<ref name=":4" />

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was serviced by [[:pt:Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo|Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo]] until 1975, when it began being serviced by [[:pt:Telecomunicações de São Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-26|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In turn,  this company was acquired by Telefónica in July 1998, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
