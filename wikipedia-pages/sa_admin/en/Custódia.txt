'''Custódia''' is a ciy in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 39,609 inhabitants and the total area is 1404.1&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Iguaraci]] and [[Carnaíba]]    ('''N''');  [[Floresta, Pernambuco|Floresta]] and [[Ibimirim]]   ('''S''');  [[Sertânia]]   ('''E''');   [[Betânia, Pernambuco|Betânia]] and [[Flores, Pernambuco|Flores]]  ('''W''').
* Area - 1404.1&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 542 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River|Pajeú]] and [[Moxotó River|Moxotó rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot
* Annual average temperature - 24.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 334.4&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Custódia''' are based in food & beverage industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pigs]], [[horse]]s, [[chicken]]s;  and plantations of [[onion]]s, [[bean]]s and [[tomato]]es.
