==History==
Limaní was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. In 1899, the [[United States Department of War]] conducted a [[census]] of Puerto Rico finding that the population of Limaní barrio was 1,075.<ref name="OfficeSanger1900">{{cite book|author1=Joseph Prentiss Sanger|author2=Henry Gannett|author3=Walter Francis Willcox|title=Informe sobre el censo de Puerto Rico, 1899, United States. ==Notable residents==
* José Esteban Lopez Maldonado, "Agricultural Son of Puerto Rico" is Puerto Rico's youngest farmer and agricultural entrepreneur. He launched the Esteban Bianchi Maldonado Agricultural School in 2017 ({{lang|es| Escuela Agrícola Esteban Bianchi Maldonado}}) when he was 14 years old.<ref>{{cite web | title=NRCS Supports Local Youth Project " Aprendiendo Haciendo" |publisher=NRCS Caribbean Area | date=19 July 2020 | url=https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/wps/portal/nrcs/detail/pr/newsroom/stories/?cid=nrcseprd1383651 | access-date=9 July 2020}}{{dead link|date=June 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}<br>- {{cite web | last=Berly | first=Glorimar Muñoz | title=Mucho más que frutos cosecha el agricultor más joven de la montaña | website=La Perla del Sur | date=8 July 2020 | url=https://www.periodicolaperla.com/mucho-mas-que-frutos-cosecha-el-agricultor-mas-joven-de-la-montana/ | language=es | access-date=9 July 2020}}<br>- {{cite web |title=Genius Farmer Boy |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxuyOrl6EqY |website=YouTube |publisher=Global Eyewitness |accessdate=9 July 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | title=José Esteban cosecha los frutos de la solidaridad | website=Para la Naturaleza | url=https://www.paralanaturaleza.org/jose-esteban-cosecha-los-frutos-de-la-solidaridad/ | language=es | access-date=January 18, 2024}}</ref>
