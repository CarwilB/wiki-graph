'''San Miguel Panán''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsam miˈɣel paˈnan}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is situated at 730 m above [[sea level]]. It contains 15,000 people. It covers a terrain of 40&nbsp;km².
