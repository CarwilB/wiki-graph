'''Abricots''' ({{IPA|fr|abʁiko|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Pamputt-abricots.wav}}, {{lit|Apricots}}; {{langx|ht|Abriko}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jérémie Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. Its Taino-name was Mamey which means apricot the fruit. The town is nicknamed le Paradis des Indiens (Indians' Paradise).

Villages located within the municipality include: Abricots, [[Anse du Clerc]], [[Anse Josep]], [[L'Homond]], [[Latitte]], [[Louissant]], [[Morne Bijote]], [[Poyrette]], [[Sajote]], and [[Saint-Victor, Grand'Anse|Saint-Victor]]
