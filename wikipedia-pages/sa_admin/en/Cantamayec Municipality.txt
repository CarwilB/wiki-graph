'''Cantamayec''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] language: ''Zuelania guidonia'':<ref name="scientific name Zuelania guidonia">{{cite web|title=Tamay |url=http://www.bioarbol.com/en/tamay.php |website=Bioarbol |publisher=Bioarbol |accessdate=1 August 2015 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304124344/http://www.bioarbol.com/en/tamay.php |archivedate=4 March 2016}}</ref> "four tamay<ref name="municipios"/> trees") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. It covers an area of 502.02&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is located roughly 110&nbsp;km southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, though it existed before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]].<ref name="municipios"/> At colonization, Cantamayec became part of the [[encomienda]] system with the first recorded encomendero being Gonzalo Méndez in 1549. It passed to Gonzalo Méndez de Sandoval in 1607, to Pedro de Loaysa in 1625 and then to Manuel Carrillo de Albornoz in 1648.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5|page=522 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA36|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the lower partition of [[Sotuta Municipality]]. In 1917, it was designated as its own municipality.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, parks and gardens, nomenclature and public monuments.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Cantamayec|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=1 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Cantamayec]], Yucatán.  There are 24 populated areas of the municipality.<ref name=governance /> The most notable, after the seat, include Actún-May, Chác, Chac-Motul, Chacxúl, Chalanté, Chichican, Chimay, Cholul, Dolores, Dzán, Dzidzilché, Jesús Man, Kambul, Kojobchaká, Kulkabapop, Múch, Nenelá, Nicté, Revolución, San Carlos, San Isidro, San José Xikó, San Juan, San Martín, San Pedro, Santa Candelaria, Sodzil, Talek, Tixcacal Pérez, Trinidad, Yokdzonot and Yuyumal.
