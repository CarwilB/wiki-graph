'''Pracuúba''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|pɾakuˈubɐ|native}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the mideast of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. It became an independent municipality in 1992.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://pracuuba.portal.ap.gov.br/conteudo/cidade/historia |title=História|website=Municipality of Pracuúba|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The town is located {{Convert|235|km|mi}} from the state capitol of [[Macapá]] and can be accessed from the [[BR-156]] highway.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/pracuuba |title=Pracuúba|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

==Geography==
The population of Pracuúba is 5,246 and its area is {{convert|4,957|km2|mi2}}..<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/pracuuba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The name of the municipality comes from a tree common to the area known as the ''pracuubeira''. The economy of Pracuúba relies of [[artisanal fishing]] and raising livestock, primarily [[American Bison|buffalo]]. It also produces timber and [[açaí palm|açaí]].<ref name="gov"/>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|392469|ha|adj=on}} [[Lago Piratuba Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1980.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Lago Piratuba|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=209|accessdate=2016-04-27}}</ref>
It also contains 4.52% of the {{convert|2369400|ha|adj=on}} [[Amapá State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Amapá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Amapá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/430|accessdate=2016-07-06}}</ref>
It contains 52.85% of the {{convert|460353|ha|adj=on}} [[Amapá National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Amapá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA do Amapá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/398|accessdate=2016-07-06}}</ref>

==Cujubim==
{{anchor|Cujumbin}}
Cujubim is an agricultural village located in the municipality. Pedro Maciel Filho, a native of Cujubim, wanted a school in his village where agricultural techniques were taught. Filho donated land on which the school could be constructed.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://www.consed.org.br/central-de-conteudos/escola-de-pracuuba-e-homenagem-a-memoria-de-agricultor-que-levou-educacao-para-comunidade |title=Escola de Pracuúba é homenagem à memória de agricultor que levou educação para comunidade |website=Consed.org|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> On 9 January 2021, the Pedro Maciel Filho School was opened in Cujubim.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/noticia/0901/governador-inaugura-escola-na-comunidade-cujubim-em-pracuuba |title=Governador inaugura escola na comunidade Cujubim, em Pracuúba|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
