'''Villa de Pozos''' is a [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]]. It is located about {{convert|10|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[San Luis Potosí (city)|San Luis Potosí]]. After being absorbed by the municipality of San Luis Potosí in 1946, it was reestablished as an independent municipality in 2024.

==Geography==
Villa de Pozos borders the municipalities of [[San Luis Potosí (city)|San Luis Potosí]] to the southwest, [[Soledad de Graciano Sánchez]] to the northwest, [[Cerro de San Pedro]] to the north, and [[Zaragoza, San Luis Potosí|Zaragoza]] to the east. Located on the [[Mexican Plateau]] at an elevation of around {{convert|1800|to|2000|m}}, Villa de Pozos has a [[semi-arid climate]], receiving {{convert|300|to|400|mm}} of rain per year.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/24/24028.pdf |title=San Luis Potosí, San Luis Potosí |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref>

==History==
In 1592, the ''[[hacienda]]'' of Real de San Francisco de los Pozos was founded by Diego de Tapia, son of [[Fernando de Tapia]], to supply the miners at [[Cerro de San Pedro]] with food and other necessities.<ref>{{cite news |date=20 June 2022 |last=Álvarez |first=Miguel Ángel |title=Villa de Pozos antes no. ¿Ahora sí? |url=https://elheraldoslp.com.mx/new/2022/06/20/villa-de-pozos-antes-no-ahora-si/ |language=es |newspaper=El Heraldo de San Luis Potosí |accessdate=3 January 2025}}</ref> After Mexico became independent, Pozos was one of the original municipalities established by the state of San Luis Potosí on 19 July 1826. It was elevated to town (''villa'') status on 26 April 1830. It remained an independent municipality until 10 October 1946, when it was integrated into the municipality of San Luis Potosí as a borough (''delegación'').<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de San Luis Potosí División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |trans-title =State of San Luis Potosí Territorial Division from 1810 to 1995 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222512/702825222512_1.pdf |access-date=3 January 2025 |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=978-970-13-1511-8}}</ref> It has been alleged that {{ill|Gonzalo N. Santos|es}}, the [[Institutional Revolutionary Party|PRI]] governor of San Luis Potosí at the time, took this action because Pozos was a centre of [[National Synarchist Union|Synarchist]] activity.<ref>{{cite news |date=12 June 2022 |last=Ochoa |first=Adriana |title=El fabuloso municipio 59 |url=https://pulsoslp.com.mx/lacabala/el-fabuloso-municipio-59/1498704 |language=es |newspaper=Pulso |accessdate=3 January 2025}}</ref> 

In 1995, the citizens of Villa de Pozos petitioned the state of San Luis Potosí for the reestablishment of the municipality, which the state approved in 2022. However, in 2004, the [[Supreme Court of Mexico|Supreme Court of Justice of the Nation]] struck down the act reestablishing the municipality on technical grounds.<ref name=application/> Another request was submitted in 2022, and on 22 July 2024, the [[Congress of San Luis Potosí|San Luis Potosí state congress]] approved the reestablishment of Villa de Pozos as a municipality.<ref name=decree/><ref name=elsol>{{cite news |last=Rocha |first=Raymundo |date=22 July 2024 |url=https://oem.com.mx/elsoldesanluis/local/villa-de-pozos-quien-y-como-se-gobernara-en-el-nuevo-municipio-de-slp-13393565 |title=Villa de Pozos: ¿quién y cómo se gobernará en el nuevo municipio de SLP? |language=es |publisher=El Sol de San Luis |accessdate=3 January 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
Villa de Pozos will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2027.<ref name=elsol/> An interim administration led by María Teresa de Jesús Rivera Acevedo was appointed by the state in August 2024.<ref>{{cite web |date=16 August 2024 |url=http://lviii.congresosanluis.gob.mx/content/congreso-del-estado-designa-integrantes-de-concejo-municipal-de-vpozos |title=Congreso del Estado designa integrantes de Concejo Municipal de V.Pozos. |language=es |publisher=Congress of San Luis Potosí |accessdate=3 January 2025}}</ref> The municipal government of Villa de Pozos consists of a [[municipal president]], two councillors ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndicos''), and twelve trustees (''regidores''), the councillors and one of the trustees being elected by [[relative majority]], and the other trustees by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=decree/>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the borough (''delegacíon'') of Villa de Pozos in San Luis Potosí recorded a population of 148,165 inhabitants.<ref name=application/> The neighbourhoods of Laguna de Santa Rita and San Nicolás de los Jassos recorded populations of 13,315 and 2,885 respectively in 2020.<ref name=marco/> In 2002 the population of the borough was estimated at approximately 61,000.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/ |title=San Luis Potosí |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |year=2002 |access-date=3 January 2025 |archive-date=2 September 2006 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060902144132/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sanluispotosi/}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Villa de Pozos are cattle farming, manufacturing and commerce.<ref name=application/><ref name=encyclopedia/>
