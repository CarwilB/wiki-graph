'''Japi''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Norte]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 4,995 (2020 est.) in an area of 189&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3MZ Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located in the micro region [[Borborema Potiguar]].
