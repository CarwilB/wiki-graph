'''Bocas del Toro''' ({{IPA|es|ˈbokas ðel ˈtoɾo}}), also known colloquially as '''Bocas Town''', is the capital of the [[Panama]]nian province of [[Bocas del Toro Province|Bocas del Toro]] and the district of [[Bocas del Toro District|Bocas del Toro]]. It is a town and a tourist resort located on the southern tip of [[Colón Island]] in the [[Bocas del Toro Archipelago]] in the [[Caribbean Sea]]. Bocas Town had 12,996 residents in 2008.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.contraloria.gob.pa/inec/Publicaciones/17-03-03/211-03.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2010-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101113203656/http://www.contraloria.gob.pa/inec/Publicaciones/17-03-03/211-03.pdf |archive-date=13 November 2010 }}</ref>

Bocas del Toro is served by [[Bocas del Toro "Isla Colón" International Airport]] which hosts daily commuter flights from and to [[Panama City]] and [[San José, Costa Rica|San José]] in neighboring [[Costa Rica]].

==Population and tourism==
The corregimiento of Bocas del Toro has a land area of {{convert|67.3|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 7,366 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|109.4|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 5,274; its population as of 2000 was 4,020.<ref name=inec/>

Relatively few Panamanians live on the island, opting for cheaper housing on the mainland. Mainland residents working on Colón Island travel by boat. Bocas del Toro is a popular tourist destination year-round. The town is small enough that most places are within walking distance. The streets are arranged in a grid. Avenidas (avenues) run east to west and calles (streets) run north to south.

==Climate==
Bocas del Toro features a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af''). The area does not have a predictable dry season. The driest months are October, January, and March.<ref name=frommers>{{cite book|title=Frommer's Panama|edition=2nd|year=2009|isbn=978-0-470-37183-1}}</ref> During the course of the year Bocas del Toro sees a copious amount of precipitation, averaging {{Convert|3458|mm|in|2|disp=or}} of rain per year.
Temperatures are consistent all year, with maxima of {{Convert|83|to|90|F|1}} and minima of {{Convert|71|to|75|F|1}}. Sunrise is normally around 6 am, and sunset is around 6 pm local time. These times vary only slightly during the year.

==Transportation==
[[File:Casa en Bocas del Toro, Panama.jpg|thumb|Stilt houses in Bocas del Toro]]

Bocas del Toro is accessible by air or boat.<!-- dead link: <ref name=tortuga> [http://www.hotelplayatortuga.com/playatortuga/index.php/pages/questions Playa Tortuga Hotel FAQs]. Retrieved 25 May 2009.</ref> --> Most visitors fly into [[Bocas del Toro "Isla Colón" International Airport]] from [[Costa Rica]] or [[Panama City]]. Ferries connect [[Almirante, Bocas del Toro|Almirante]] to Bocas del Toro. Buses run between [[David, Chiriqui|David]], [[Almirante, Bocas del Toro|Almirante]], [[Changuinola]], and the [[Costa Rica]]n border. Islands and islets in the [[Bocas del Toro Archipelago]] are accessible only by private boat or water taxi. Many small communities line the string of islands and very few have roads. Many companies provide boat service to various islands and diving spots.<ref name=frommers />

==Infrastructure==
[[File:Bocas del Toro Archipelago map.png|thumb|300px|right|Bocas del Toro Archipelago]]

Bocas del Toro lacks some basic infrastructure. A generator plant provides power to the towns of Isla Colon, Carenero and Bastimentos. The community does have a waste water (sewage) treatment plant, but not the same quality as found in the United States. The town lacks a water filtration or treatment system.  Both the drinking water and the sea water have very high levels of fecal coliform bacteria.<ref>[http://www.iadb.org/NEWS/detail.cfm?Language=En&artType=PR&artid=4268&id=4268&CFID=18684459&CFTOKEN=92420230 Infrastructure development in Bocas del Toro]. Retrieved 25 May 2009.</ref>

==Health care==

Health care is available, but facilities are limited. A public health clinic operates in town.<!-- dead link: <ref name=tortuga /> -->

Common medical problems include food and waterborne diseases, insect bites, sunburns, heat stress/stroke, and dehydration.{{citation needed|date=November 2021}}

==Attractions==
* The [[Bastimentos Island National Marine Park]] is about a twenty-minute boat ride away, and is a major destination for [[ecotourism|ecotours]].
* [[Isla Colon]] has numerous white sand beaches. Swimmers should be aware of dangerous [[riptide]]s.
* Scuba Diving
Bocas del Toro is still relatively unknown as a dive destination. However, it offers a great diversity of aquatic life around its islands.{{citation needed|date=May 2013}}
* Surfing
Bocas del Toro offers a wide range of surf spots for different levels of experience, among them Black Rock, Carenero, and Wizard Beach, as well as Bastimentos Island. November to April are considered to be peak season, as well as summertime. Bocas del Toro also hosts (inter)national surf competitions.{{citation needed|date=May 2013}}
