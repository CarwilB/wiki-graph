'''San Andrés''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the department of [[El Petén]] in [[Guatemala]]. The municipality is formed by the town of San Andrés, located on the north-western shore of [[Lake Petén Itzá]], and 55 rural communities, with a total population of 20,295 people (census 2002).<ref>{{cite web |author= |title=Municipalidad de San Andrés, Petén - Diagnóstico Institucional |url=http://www.marn.gob.gt/documentos/AMPI/B/B7/B7.2/B7.2.2.doc. |publisher=marn.gob.gt |location=Guatemala |year=2007 |accessdate=2009-07-26 |format=doc |language=Spanish }}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The municipality was created in 1962 and has a territory 8,874&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, equivalent to 25% of the department of El Petén. In the 1990s, more than 93% (8,288&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>) of the municipality's territory was declared protected nature reserve by the central government.<ref name="Morales">{{cite web |author=Morales Santizo, Karla Lorena |title=Municipio de San Andrés, Departamento de Petén - "Administración del Riesgo" |url=http://biblioteca.usac.edu.gt/EPS/03/03_0665_v16.pdf |publisher=USAC |location=Guatemala |year=2008 |accessdate=2009-07-26 |format=pdf |language=Spanish}}</ref>
