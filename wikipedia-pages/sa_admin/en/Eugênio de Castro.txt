'''Eugênio de Castro''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the northern part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,352 (2020 est.) in an area of 419.32 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/9AHF Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located 435&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
