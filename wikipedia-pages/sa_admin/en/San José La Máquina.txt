'''San José La Máquina''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez]], located approximately {{convert|26|km}} south of the departmental capital of [[Mazatenango, Suchitepéquez|Mazatenango]].

==Geography==
San José La Máquina is located on the Pacific coastal plain of southern Guatemala. It borders the Suchitepéquez municipalities of [[Cuyotenango]] to the north, [[San Lorenzo, Suchitepéquez|San Lorenzo]] to the northeast, and [[Mazatenango, Suchitepéquez|Mazatenango]] to the southeast, and the municipality of [[San Andrés Villa Seca]] in [[Retalhuleu Department]] to the west.<ref name=economics>{{cite report |author=College of Economic Sciences |url=http://biblioteca.usac.edu.gt/EPS/03/03_0950_v1.pdf |title=Diagnóstico socioeconómico, potencialidades productivas y propuestas de inversión: municipio de San José La Máquina, departamento de Suchitepéquez |language=es |publisher=[[Universidad de San Carlos de Guatemala]] |accessdate=7 November 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|147|km2}}.<ref name=general/>

The terrain of San José La Máquina is flat, with an elevation ranging from {{convert|51|to|95|m}} above sea level, and has been almost completely deforested for use as agricultural land.<ref name=economics/>

San José La Máquina is irrigated by the [[Icán River|Icán]] and [[Sís River|Sís]] Rivers which form its eastern and western borders respectively. Both rivers have their source in the slopes of the [[Volcán Santo Tomás|Santo Tomás]] volcano, and are highly polluted from sewage and refuse.<ref name=economics/>

San José La Máquina has a [[tropical savanna climate]]. The temperature varies from {{convert|21|–|24|C}} in the wet season from May to October to {{convert|31|–|38|C}} in the dry season from November to April. Annual precipitation is about {{convert|2400|mm}}.<ref name=economics/>

==History==
San José La Máquina stands on land that was previously part of a [[guayacán]]-producing ''[[hacienda]]'' called "Trapiche Grande" which was owned by Queen [[Wilhelmina of the Netherlands]]. The land from this ''hacienda'' was expropriated from 1950 to 1955 as part of the [[Decree 900]] land reforms, and divided between the departments of Suchitepéquez and Retalhuleu. The subdivision located in Suchitepéquez was given the name La Máquina ("the machine"), after a belt conveyor was found abandoned there on the road between Cuyotenango and Tulate. The machine was repaired by the locals and used to build access roads in the area.<ref name=general/>

From 1980 onwards, the local inhabitants made repeated efforts to elevate San José La Máquina to the level of a municipality. They finally succeeded on 12 February 2014, when the [[Congress of the Republic of Guatemala]] decreed its separation from the municipality of Cuyotenango.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=3 March 2014 |title=Decreto Número 7-2014 |url=https://www.congreso.gob.gt/assets/uploads/info_legislativo/decretos/2014/07-2014.pdf |language=es |newspaper=[[Diario de Centro América]] |access-date=7 November 2020}}</ref> The decree was gazetted in the ''[[Diario de Centro América]]'' on 3 March 2014.<ref name=economics/>

==Government==
Alberto Martínez López has served as mayor of San José La Máquina since its establishment in 2014. He was reelected in 2016 and 2020.<ref name=general/>

==Demographics==
In the 2018 Guatemalan Census, which had an estimated underenumeration rate of 9.0%,<ref>{{cite web |last=Brinkhoff |first=Thomas |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ |title=Guatemala |work=City Population |date=23 January 2020 |accessdate=7 November 2020}}</ref> San José La Máquina recorded a total population of 23,062 inhabitants.<ref name=census/> The [[National Institute of Statistics (Guatemala)|National Institute of Statistics]] subsequently estimated the population of San José La Máquina in 2018 to be 24,969.<ref name=population/>

The largest inhabited locality in the municipality is the municipal seat, also called San José La Máquina, with a projected population of 4396 in 2015.<ref name=economics/>

Spanish is spoken by over 95% of the population.<ref name=economics/>

==Culture==
The most important festival in San José La Máquina is the [[Saint Joseph's Day|feast day of Saint Joseph]], which has been celebrated there since 1951.<ref name=general/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
San José La Máquina's economy relies heavily on agriculture. The main cash crop is corn, followed by sesame and tobacco. Other crops of local significance include watermelons, bananas and peanuts. Cattle farming is also a major contributor to the municipal economy.<ref name=economics/>

The road network of San José La Máquina is in poor condition. Departmental road SCH-7, the main thoroughfare in the municipality, is only partially paved. It connects Cuyotenango with the beach town of [[Tulate]] in San Andrés Villa Seca.<ref name=economics/>
