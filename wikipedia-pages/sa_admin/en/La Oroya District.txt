'''La Oroya District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Yauli Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is a region that is rich in [[zinc]], [[lead]] and [[copper]].

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is Hatun Punta at approximately {{convert|4400|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Yauli Province (Junín Region)</ref>
