'''Diamantino''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Mato Grosso]] in [[Brazil]]. It has a population of 22,178 and is located near [[Diamantino River]]. It lies {{convert|1837|ft|m}} above sea-level, and sits at the foot of the [[Mato Grosso plateau]]. Its history dates back to 1730, when it was established as a [[gold]] [[mining]] settlement. In 1746, [[diamond]]s were discovered, causing the town's population to swell and prosper. However, the quantity of diamonds was greatly overestimated, and the town's population steadily declined.<ref>{{Cite EB1911|wstitle=Diamantino |volume=8 |page=157}}</ref>

Near the city lies the [[Pole of inaccessibility#South America|South American pole of inaccessibility]], which means that no place in South America is as far from the nearest ocean as this point. The closest ocean is the [[Pacific Ocean]], at the Peruvian-Chilean border.{{Citation needed|date=November 2015}}
