'''Nor Lípez''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Potosí Department|Potosí]]. Its seat is Colcha "K", also called Villa Martín. The majority of the area of the province was titled as the Nor Lípez [[Native Community Land]] on 19 April 2011. One of the largest mines of  Bolivia, the [[San Cristóbal mine (Bolivia)|San Cristóbal Mine]], is located near San Cristóbal in Colcha "K" municipality.

==Location==
The province is one of sixteen provinces in the Potosí Department. It is located between 20°&nbsp;27' and 22°&nbsp;01' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66°&nbsp;18' and 68°&nbsp;35' [[Longitude|West]].

It is bordered by the [[Daniel Campos Province]] to the north, the Republic of [[Chile]] to the west, the [[Enrique Baldivieso Province]] and [[Sur Lípez Province]] to the south, [[Sud Chichas Province]] to the east, and the [[Antonio Quijarro Province]] to the northeast.

The province extends over 270&nbsp;km from east to west and 210&nbsp;km from north to south.

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].

==Population==
The population increased from 8,320 (1992 census) to 10,460 inhabitants (2001 census), an increase of 25,7%.

About 96% of the population have no access to electricity, 93% have no sanitary facilities. 61% of the population are employed in agriculture, 9% in mining, 2% in industry, 28% in general services. 90% of the population are Catholics, 6% are Protestants.

The people are predominantly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechuas|Quechuan]] descent.<ref>obd.descentralizacion.gov.bo</ref>
