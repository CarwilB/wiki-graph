'''Santa Rita''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|83.6|km2}} and had a population of 3,975 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. It was accorded municipality status on 22 October 1900.

== History ==
Santa Rita was a village in the municipality of [[San Francisco de Ojuera]] until it was elevated to a municipality status on 22 October 1900.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Santa Rita, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Santa-Rita.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-santa-rita-santa-barbara/|title=Municipality of Santa Rita|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Santa Rita is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] to the north, San Francisco de Ojuera to the east and south, [[El Níspero]] and [[San Rafael, Honduras|San Rafael]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|83.6|km2}} and has an average elevation of {{cvt|364|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/>

== Administrative divisions ==
The municipality comprises five aldeas (villages) and their associated caseríos (hamlets).<ref name="INE2013">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/274-Santa-Barbara-Santa-Rita.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 274, Municipio de Santa Rita, Departamento de Santa Bárbara|date=February 2025|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=20}}

== Demographics ==
As per the 2013 census, San Marcos had a total population of 3,975 inhabitants, of whom 2,082 were male and 1,983 (49.0%) were female. Of the total population, 44.7% was classified as urban and 55.3% lived in the rural areas.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1624__santa_rita/|title=Santa Rita|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

Of the population, 0.18% were classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 39.6% of the population were aged 0–14, 64.5% were aged 15–64, and 6.5% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 19.6 years and the mean age was 25.5 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the illiteracy rate was 21.9%, slightly lower than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}}
