}}'''Martínez de la Torre''' is a city and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] of the same name located in the central part of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. The city had a 2020 census population of 64,692, while the municipality had a population of 108,842. There is a total area of 815.13&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (314.72 sq mi) in the municipality. The largest other community in the municipality is the town of [[Independencia, Veracruz|Independencia]].

Martínez de la Torre is on the [[Nautla River]], also known as the Filobobos.
