'''Coyutla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in central zone of the state, about 110&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 312.56 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|20|15|N|97|39|W|}}.

The municipality is delimited to the north and to the west by [[Puebla]] State, to the east by [[Espinal, Veracruz|Espinal]], to the south by [[Chumatlan]], and to the south-west by [[Coahuitlan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30051a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Coyutla|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117004712/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30051a.htm|archive-date=2006-11-17|url-status=dead}}</ref>

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[chili pepper]].

A celebration in honor of San Pedro and San Pablo, patrons of the town, takes place in June.

The weather in Coyutla is warm-medium all year with rains in summer and autumn. A river runs nearby which has attractive views.{{cn|date=July 2023}}
