'''Metapa''' ('''Metapa de Domínguez''') is a [[town]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 5,033,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Metapa|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=055|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from  4,794 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Metapa|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07055a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=August 1, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070325152645/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07055a.htm|archive-date=March 25, 2007}}</ref> It covers an area of 101.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality had 7 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) was: Metapa de Domínguez (2,610), classified as urban.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
