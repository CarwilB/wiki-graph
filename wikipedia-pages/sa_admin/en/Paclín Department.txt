'''Paclín''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.pagina12.com.ar/582689-el-avistaje-de-murcielagos-como-oferta-turistica|title=El avistaje de murciélagos como oferta turística|work=[[Página 12]]|date=28 August 2023|access-date=30 June 2025|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 4,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|985|km2}}, and its capital city is [[La Merced, Catamarca|La Merced]], which is located around {{cvt|60|km}} from the provincial capital.{{cn|date=June 2025}}
