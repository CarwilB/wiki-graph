'''Não-Me-Toque''' (literally ''Don't-Touch-Me'' in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]) is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]].

== History ==
The city's current territory was only explored and occupied when [[Portugal|Portuguese]] descendants established their farms on the place, in the second decade of the 19th century. By the end of the 19th century, families of [[Netherland|Dutch]] immigrants also occupied the southern part of the territory, in 1824. In 1949, the first [[Netherlands|Dutch]] families arrived to the town, making the city one of the starting points of the Dutch immigration on southern Brazil.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://naometoquers.com.br/o-municipio/historico/|title=Histórico - Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|work=Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|access-date=2018-04-18|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

There is no exact information of the name's origins, but there are two theories: the abundance of the ''Dasyphyllum spinescens'' plant, which name is "[[Noli me tangere|touch-me-not]]" in Portuguese; or the establishment of the farm "Fazenda Não-Me-Toque", founded on July 20, 1885.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://naometoquers.com.br/o-municipio/origem-do-nome/|title=Origem do Nome - Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|work=Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|access-date=2018-04-18|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Because of the constant humiliation the citizens of the city suffered because of the name, it was renamed ''Campo Real'' (Royal Field) in December 1971, until it was changed back after a plebiscite in April 1977.<ref>{{Cite book|chapter-url=https://books.google.com/books?id=EXH7rYWqiQYC&q=N%C3%A3o-me-toque&pg=PT44|title=Beleza interior: uma viagem poética pelo Rio Grande do Sul|last=Carpinejar|first=Fabrício|publisher=Arquipélago|year=2012|isbn=9788560171286|location=Brazil|language=pt|chapter=A cidade que defende o nome}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Não-Me-Toque is located in the region of the Planalto Médio, in the micro-region of the Alto Jacuí, Rio Grande do Sul. It is 471&nbsp;km (292&nbsp;mi) away from the coast (or the nearest coastal city).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.rotamapas.com.br/distancia-entre-nao-me-toque-e-sao-lourenco-do-sul|title=Distância entre a cidade de Não-Me-Toque RS e São Lourenço do Sul RS|website=ROTAMAPAS.COM.BR|language=pt-BR|access-date=2018-04-18}}</ref> It is neighbored by the city of [[Carazinho]] on the north (the largest city nearby),<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cidade-brasil.com.br/municipio-nao-me-toque.html|title=Município de Não-Me-Toque|website=www.cidade-brasil.com.br|language=pt|access-date=2018-04-18}}</ref> [[Lagoa dos Três Cantos]] on the south, [[Santo Antônio do Planalto]] on the east and [[Colorado, Rio Grande do Sul|Colorado]] on the west. It is 280&nbsp;km (173&nbsp;mi) away from the state's capital, [[Porto Alegre]].<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://naometoquers.com.br/o-municipio/localizacao/|title=Localização - Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|work=Não-Me-Toque - Prefeitura Municipal|access-date=2018-04-18|language=pt-BR}}</ref>
