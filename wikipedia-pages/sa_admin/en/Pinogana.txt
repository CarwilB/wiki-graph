==Transportation==
In late 2025 the community was connected by road and added to the North American Road Network with construction of bridges and road over the Chucunaque and Turia River from Yaviza. Planning for the road started in 2021.<ref>https://eldigitalpanama.com/licitan-proyecto-carretero-desde-yaviza-hasta-pinogana/</ref><ref>https://pan.news.o-abroad.com/~/economy/203920-en-mop-reports-74-progress-in-the-construction-of-the-yaviza-pinogana-road-in-darien.html</ref>

This is the first community connected to the North American road network since [[Whatì]], [[Northwest Territories]] in 2021.
