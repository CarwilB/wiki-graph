'''El Valle del Espíritu Santo'''<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.venezuelatuya.com/margarita/elvalleeng.htm | title=VenezuelaTuya }}</ref> is a village near [[Porlamar]], in the [[Nueva Esparta]] state of [[Venezuela]]. Founded as the capital of the island in 1529, El Valle del Espíritu Santo is just north of Porlamar. It is the birth town of [[Santiago Mariño]] and his sister, [[Concepción Mariño]], as well as where the patron of the island, the [[Virgen del Valle|Virgin of El Valle]] appeared. The village has a pink-and-white church honoring the Virgin of El Valle. Visitors go to the village all year-round, but especially on her feast day in early September.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.fodors.com/world/south-america/venezuela/isla-margarita/feature_30007.html |title=El Valle del Espíritu Santo & la Asunción &#124; Isla Margarita Features &#124; Fodor's Travel Guides |access-date=2012-02-08 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100510142821/http://www.fodors.com/world/south-america/venezuela/isla-margarita/feature_30007.html |archive-date=2010-05-10 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.venezuelatuya.com/margarita/elvalleeng.htm | title=VenezuelaTuya }}</ref>

== The Virgin of El Valle Church ==
The [[basilica of Our Lady of El Valle]] is a building with two main towers. On the inside, it is very well illuminated. The figure of the virgin is in the altar, venerated by all the locals and all the fishers from the country, because she is their patron. The windows are simple and bright. The Diocesan Museum is located next to the church.

== Santiago Mariño Museum ==
The Santiago Mariño Museum is located in front of the church. This is the town were Santiago Mariño, one of the independence heroes, was born. The museum is simple, but interesting. The construction is a typical colonial house with an internal garden. In the rooms, there are many paintings and furniture from the independence years. The museum is only open in the morning.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://insel-margarita-venezuela.de/margarita-island/el-valle6.html | title=Margarita Island Venezuela Caribbean }}</ref>
