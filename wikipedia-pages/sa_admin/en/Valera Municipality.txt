'''Valera''' is one of the twenty [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] in the [[Venezuela|Venezuelan]] state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]].<ref name="DPT">{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf|title=Territorial Political Division of the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela|publisher=[[National Institute of Statistics (Venezuela)]]|accessdate=December 13, 2018|archive-date=December 11, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201211040255/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> Its capital is the city of [[Valera]]. In the 2011 census, its population was 250,000.<ref name="2011Census" /> It has an area of {{Cvt|240|km2|}}, giving it a [[population density]] of 567.2 inhabitants per km². 

The municipality contains six parishes, Juan Ignacio Montilla, La Beatriz, La Puerta, [[Mendoza del Valle del Momboy]], Mercedes Díaz and San Luis.
