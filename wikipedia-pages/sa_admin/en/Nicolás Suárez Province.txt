'''Nicolás Suárez''' is one of the five provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Pando Department|Pando]] [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] and is situated in the department's northwestern parts. Its name honors [[Nicolás Suárez Callaú]] (1861-1940) who owned major parts of today's ''Pando'' and ''[[Beni Department|Beni]]'' Departments in the times of the [[caoutchouc]]-boom.

== Location ==
''Nicolás Suárez'' Province is located between 10° 39' and 11° 27' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 33' and 69° 34' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over a length of 370&nbsp;km from Northeast to Southwest, and up to 100&nbsp;km from North to South.

The province is situated in the [[Amazon Rainforest|Amazon]] lowlands of Bolivia and borders [[Brazil]] in the North, [[Peru]] in the West, [[Manuripi Province]] in the South, and [[Abuná Province]] in the East.

== Population ==
The population of Nicolás Suárez Province has increased by almost 200% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 18,447 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 29,536 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 39,577 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2010: 51,377 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosPando.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística - Proyecciones] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081118153156/http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosPando.pdf |date=2008-11-18 }}</ref>
*2024: 69,378 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nicolás Suárez (Province, Bolivia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/pando/0901__nicol%C3%A1s_su%C3%A1rez/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
 
42.4% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

84.7% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 4.9% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], and 3.1% speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ''(1992)''.

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 98.3%. ''(2012)''

51.9% of the population have no access to electricity, 33.0% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

83.9% of the population are Catholics, 13.8% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
