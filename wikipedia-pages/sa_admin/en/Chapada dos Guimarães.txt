'''Chapada dos Guimarães''' is a municipality located in central Brazil, 62&nbsp;km from the city of [[Cuiabá]], the capital of [[Mato Grosso]] State. It is home to the [[Chapada dos Guimarães National Park]]. Outside this town is the geographic center of South America.

The municipality contains 77% of the {{convert|3534|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio da Casca Ecological Station]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC do Rio da Casca – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=ESEC do Rio da Casca|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5331|access-date=2016-07-18}}</ref>
The municipal seat contains the {{convert|31|ha}} [[Quineira State Park]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PES Quineira – ISA}}
