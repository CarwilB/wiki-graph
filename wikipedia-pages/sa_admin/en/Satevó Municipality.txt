'''  Satevó ''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[San Francisco Javier de Satevó]]. The municipality covers an area of 2,185.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 3,662,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Satevó|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=061|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305071240/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=061|url-status=dead}}</ref> down from 3,856 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Satevó|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08061a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617074342/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08061a.htm|archivedate=June 17, 2011}}</ref>

The municipality had 244 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

Satevó began as a Jesuit mission to the [[Tarahumara]] in about 1640.<ref>Edward C. Spicer, ''Cycles of Conquest'' (Tucson: University of Arizona Press, 1962) p. 29</ref>
