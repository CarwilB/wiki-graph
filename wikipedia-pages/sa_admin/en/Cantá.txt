'''Cantá''' ({{IPA|pt|kɐ̃ˈta}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the mideast of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 18,799 (2020) and its area is 7,665&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/5ZW|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Roraima {{!}} Cantá|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-06-15}}</ref>

The municipality of Cantá was established in 1995.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/canta/historico |title=Cantá |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> It is an agricultural community based on [[dairy production]], [[livestock]] and [[banana]]s.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://portal.rr.gov.br/index.php/component/k2/item/2841-trafegabilidade-governo-inicia-recuperacao-de-mais-duas-vicinais-no-canta |title=TRAFEGABILIDADE Governo inicia recuperação de mais duas vicinais no Cantá|website=Government of Roraima|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

The municipality is known for its nature. Tourist attractions include the Serra Grande mountains, and the waterfalls of Véu de Noiva.<ref>{{cite news|url=http://redeglobo.globo.com/redeamazonica/tv-roraima/noticia/2013/09/roraima-tv-fala-comunidade-visita-municipio-do-canta-em-roraima.html |title=Roraima TV: Fala Comunidade visita município do Cantá, em Roraima|website=Globo|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> Cantá can be reached from [[Boa Vista, Roraima|Boa Vista]] via the {{ill|BR-432|pt}} highway.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://estradas.com.br/obras-na-br-432-em-roraima-ira-reduzir-trajeto-de-manaus-a-boa-vista-em-60-km/ |title=Obras na BR-432/RR irão reduzir trajeto de Manaus a Boa Vista em 60 km|website=Estradas|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

The Malacacheta Indigenous Territory is located in the municipality. The {{convert|28,658|ha}} territory is home to [[Wapishana]] Amerindians. <ref>{{cite web|url=https://terrasindigenas.org.br/pt-br/terras-indigenas/3751 |title=Terra Indígena Malacacheta|website=Terras Indigenas|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The Tabalascada Indigenous Territory is shared by the Wapishana and the [[Macushi]] people, and measures {{convert|13,037|ha}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://terrasindigenas.org.br/es/terras-indigenas/3861 |title=Terra Indígena Tabalascada|website=Terras Indigenas|access-date=10 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
