'''Dores do Rio Preto''' is the westernmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 6,771 (2020) and its area is 153.106&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The village Pedras Meninas in Dores is the location for the entrance to [[Caparaó National Park]] and the [[Pico da Bandeira]] from Espirito Santo.
