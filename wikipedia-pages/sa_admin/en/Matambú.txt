'''Matambú''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Hojancha (canton)|Hojancha]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

It is the only [[Mangue language|Chorotega]] [[Indigenous territories of Costa Rica|indigenous territory]].<ref>{{Cite thesis |url=https://nmbu.brage.unit.no/nmbu-xmlui/handle/11250/187790 |hdl=11250/187790 |title=Indigenous peoples in Costa Rica and El Diquis hydroelectric project: indigenous identity, consultation and representation |last=Vaage |first=Kari |year=2011 |type=MSc thesis |publisher=Norwegian University of Life Sciences |location=Ås }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.uned.ac.cr/extension/images/ifcmdl/02._Censo_2011._Territorios_Indigenas.pdf|title=Territorios Indígenas|date=2011|website=Instituto nacionale de estadistica y census|access-date=28 January 2019}}</ref>

== History ==
Matambú was created on 25 July 2017 by Ley 9463.<ref name="dta2017" /><ref>{{cite web |title=Creación del Distrito N° 5 del cantón de Hojancha Matambú |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_norma.aspx?param1=NRM&nValor1=1&nValor2=84677&nValor3=109338&strTipM=FN |access-date=16 May 2020}}</ref><ref name="presidencia">{{cite web |title=Territorio indígena de Matambú será el quinto distrito de Hojancha |url=https://www.presidencia.go.cr/comunicados/2017/07/territorio-indigena-de-matambu-sera-el-quinto-distrito-de-hojancha/ |access-date=16 May 2020}}</ref>
