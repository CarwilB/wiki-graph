'''Jerez''' ({{IPA|es|xeˈɾes}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jutiapa Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|46.72|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 7,006 inhabitants.

==History==
The municipality of Jerez was earlier known as Chingo due to its location on the slopes of the volcano of the same name. The town was founded in the 18th century, and was named Jerez in honor of the Nicaraguan general [[Máximo Jerez]]. It is cited as part of the Mita department as per the government decree on 27 August 1836. On 8 May 1852, when the department of Jutiapa was created by government decree, and Jerez became part of it. It was officially declared as a municipality on 5 October 1892 during the reign of president [[José María Reyna Barrios]].<ref name="Info">{{cite report|url=http://biblioteca.usac.edu.gt/EPS/03/03_0033_v11.pdf|title=Municipio de Jerez|lang=es|work=Biblioteca de la Universidad de San Carlos de Guatemala|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>{{rp|page=14}}

==Geography==
Jerez is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|46.72|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Jerez (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2211__jerez/|access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/llevan-a-cabo-jornadas-medicas-en-el-municipio-jerez-jutiapa/|title=Llevan a cabo jornadas médicas en el municipio de Jerez, Jutiapa|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|first=Mariana L.|last=Escobedo|date=22 February 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level, Jerez has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The district has an annual average temperature of {{cvt|27.58|C}}. Jerez typically receives about {{cvt|85.98|mm}} of precipitation and has 135.67 rainy days (37.17% of the time) on average annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/jerez|title=Jerez|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Jerez had an estimated population of 7,006 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 3,498 males and 3,508 females. About 24.9% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 10.5% was over the age of 65 years. About 40.7% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (59.3%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (80.1%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 85.6%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (95.6%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (2.6%) forming a minor minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.7%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
