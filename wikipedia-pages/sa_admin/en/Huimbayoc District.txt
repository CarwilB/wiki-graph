'''Huimbayoc''' or '''Wimpayuq''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]]) is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[San Martín Province|San Martín]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Part of the district is located in the [[Cordillera Azul National Park]].
