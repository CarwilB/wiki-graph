'''Burruyacú Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the northwest of the [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. According to the 2001 census, its population was 32,936. The department seat is the town of [[Burruyacú]].

==Geography==
The department has a total area of 3,605&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, making it the largest department in the province. It has a mountainous west formed by the Sierra de Medinas. East of these mountains is a fertile and sparsely populated plain where ranching and agriculture dominate the land use.
