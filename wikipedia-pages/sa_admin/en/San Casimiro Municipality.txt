The '''San Casimiro Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 25,540.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref>  The town of [[San Casimiro]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the San Casimiro Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The San Casimiro Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 26,030 (up from 23,212 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|52.27|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the San Casimiro Municipality is Johnny Martinez, elected on October 31, 2004, with 44% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=4&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |title=Alcalde de Municipio |publisher=Cne.gob.ve |date= |accessdate=2019-07-17}}</ref>  He replaced Luis Rodriguez shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=17 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archive-date=2 October 2006 |url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Capital San Casimiro, Güiripa, Ollas de Caramacate, and Valle Morín.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
