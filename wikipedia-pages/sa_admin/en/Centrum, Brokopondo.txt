'''Centrum''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Brokopondo District]]. Its population at the 2012 census was 4,482.<ref name="census"/> Centrum can be reached by car via the [[Avobakaweg]] from [[Paranam]] to [[Afobaka]],<ref name="planning"/> or by boat via the [[Suriname River]].<ref name="suriname_nu"/>

==Towns==
{{main|Brokopondo}}
The main town in the Centrum resort is [[Brokopondo]] which is also the capital of the Brokopondo District. [[Afobaka]] is located in the resort. There are several other tiny villages which are still [[Tribe|tribal]]: Asigron, Baling Soela, Boslanti, Compagniekreek, Drepada, and Tapoeripa.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Onderzoek-Regionaal-Plan-Brokopondo-2015-2040.pdf|website=Planning Office Suriname|title=REGIONAAL PLAN BROKOPONDO|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

In 1960, an agriculture pilot project was started in Brokopondo and the village created for the project was named Brokobaka after Brokopondo and Afobaka.<ref name="suriname_nu">{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/brokopondo01.html|title=Distrikt Brokopondo|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref> The project lead to a [[palm oil]] plantation and factory in Victoria, [[Klaaskreek]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/p/phedra|title=Plantages / P / Phedra |website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=15 May 2020}}</ref> however the [[Surinamese Interior War]] and [[lethal yellowing]] resulted in the closure of the factory in 1996.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2016/11/14/oliepalmproductie-zou-geen-prioriteit-moeten-zijn/|title=Oliepalmproductie zou geen prioriteit moeten zijn|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
