'''Huarochirí''' ([[Quechua languages|Quechua]]: ''Waruchiri'')<ref>{{Ref Laime}}</ref> is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Department of Lima|department of Lima]], [[Peru]]. From the administrative point of view of the [[Catholic Church in Peru]], it forms part of the [[Territorial Prelature of Yauyos]]. Its capital is [[Matucana, Peru|Matucana]]. The western section is part of the [[Lima Metropolitan Area]].

== Geography ==
The La Viuda and [[Paryaqaqa mountain range|Paryaqaqa]] or Waruchiri mountain ranges and the ''Cordillera de la Corte'' traverse the province. One of the highest peaks of the province is [[Paryaqaqa]] at {{convert|5750|m|ft|0}} above sea level.
