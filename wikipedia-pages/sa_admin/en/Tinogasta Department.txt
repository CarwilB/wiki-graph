'''Tinogasta''' is a western [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 22,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|23,582|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Tinogasta]], which is located around {{cvt|1,415|km}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
