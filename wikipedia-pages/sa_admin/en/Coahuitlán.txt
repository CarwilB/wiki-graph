'''Coahuitlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is in the north zone of the state, about {{convert|120|km|abbr=on}} from [[Xalapa]], the state capital. It has a surface of {{convert|95.41|sqkm|abbr=on}}. It is at {{coord|20|16|N|97|43|W|}}.

Coahuitlán is delimited to the north and to the east by [[Coyutla]], to the south-east by [[Chumatlan]], to the south by [[Filomeno Mata]] and to the west by [[Puebla]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30037a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Coahuitlán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117004554/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30037a.htm|archive-date=2006-11-17|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Due to the weather, which is warm all year and rains in summer, the main production is of [[maize]] and [[beans]].{{citation needed|date=July 2016}}

In Coahuitlán, in March and April, a celebration in honor of [[Santo Entierro]] takes place.{{cn|date=July 2023}}
