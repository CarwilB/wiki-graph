'''São Domingos do Prata''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 17,327.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sao-domingos-do-prata/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
