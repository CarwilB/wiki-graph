'''San Luis''' is a municipality and town in the [[Pinar del Río Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is centered mainly on agriculture ([[tobacco]], [[rice]], fruit crops), [[Livestock|stock]] raising.

==History==
It was founded in 1827,<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1879, when it split from [[San Juan y Martínez]]. Until 1977, its territory included the seaport village of [[La Coloma]], currently part of Pinar del Río.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/pluis/coloma/index.htm La Coloma] on guije.com</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality is located west of [[Pinar del Río]] and includes the villages of Barbacoa, Barrigonas, Buenavista, El Corojo, El Retiro, Llanadas, Palizada, Santa María and Tirado.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/pluis/index.htm|title=San Luis|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of San Luis had a population of 31,242.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|765|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|41|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
