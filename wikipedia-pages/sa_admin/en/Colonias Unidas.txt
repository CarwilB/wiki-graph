'''Colonias Unidas''' are a collection of settlements in the [[Itapúa Department]] of southern [[Paraguay]] founded by German-speaking immigrants in the early 1900s, including the districts of [[Hohenau, Paraguay|Hohenau]], [[Obligado]], [[Bella Vista, Itapúa|Bella Vista]].

The population of Colonias Unitas is 36,862 per the 2022 census, with 12,809 in Hohenau, 13,606 in Obligado, and 10,447 in Bella Vista.<ref>{{Cite web |title=City Population: Check for Humans |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/checkhuman.html?url=%2Fen%2Fparaguay%2Fadmin%2F07__itap%25C3%25BAa%2F |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>  

== History ==
The first of the Colonias Unidas, Hohenau, was founded on March 14, 1900 by [[German Brazilians|German-Brazilian]] colonists.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-08-30 |title=Colonias Unidas, una historia de inmigrantes y pujanza en la vitrina gracias al Rally |url=https://es-us.noticias.yahoo.com/deportes/colonias-unidas-historia-inmigrantes-pujanza-145221360.html |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=Yahoo News |language=es-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=La Nación / Colonias Unidas: la historia y la cultura son opciones turísticas en Itapúa |url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/pais/2022/12/03/colonias-unidas-la-historia-y-la-cultura-son-opciones-turisticas-en-itapua/ |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=www.lanacion.com.py |language=es}}</ref> Additional German colonists later founded the neighboring municipalities of Obligado in 1912 and Bella Vista in 1918.<ref name=":0">{{Cite book |last=Hebblethwaite |first=Margaret |title=Paraguay |date=July 2010 |publisher=Bradt Travel Guides |isbn=978 1 84162 315 3 |edition=1st |pages=202-203}}</ref><ref>{{cite book |last=Fretz |first=J. Winfield |title=Immigrant group settlements in Paraguay: A study in the sociology of colonization |publisher=Bethel College |year=1962 |location=North Newton, KS |page=73}}</ref> Initially a colony, Hohenau was recognized by the Paraguayan government as an official district in 1959, followed by Obligado in 1980, and Bella Vista in 1960. 

In 1953, 78 farmers formed a cooperative organization called the Cooperativa Colonias Unidas, which supports farming operations, operates fuel stations and grocery stores, and provides [[health care]] to the Colonias Unidas.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cooperativa Colonias Unidas cumple 71 años de vida institucional |url=https://www.masencarnacion.com/articulo/cooperativa-colonias-unidas-cumple-71-anos-de-vida-institucional |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=www.masencarnacion.com |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Sobre Nosotros {{!}} Cooperativa Colonias Unidas |url=https://www.colonias.com.py/nosotros/ |access-date=2026-05-07 |language=es}}</ref> 

== Economy and culture ==
[[File:Colonias Unidas sign.jpg|thumb|176x176px|A sign in Hohenau displays information about the history of the Colonias Unidas.]]
The economy of Colonias Unidas is largely centered on agricultural crops including [[yerba mate]], with Bella Vista known as "Capital Nacional de la Yerba Mate".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ciudad centenaria, fundada por inmigrantes - 103 años de Bella Vista Sur - ABC Color |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/comercial/103-anos-bella-vista-sur/2021/10/12/ciudad-centenaria-fundada-por-inmigrantes/ |access-date=2026-05-07 |website=www.abc.com.py |language=es}}</ref> Additional exports include [[Soybean|soybeans]], [[Dairy farming|dairy]], and [[Pig farming|pork]].

Each district is governed by its own mayor and municipal board, with elections for the five board members occurring every five years, most recently October 10, 2021.

=== Culture ===
[[File:Spanish_and_German_sign_in_Hohenau.jpg|thumb|A bilingual German and Spanish sign on the door of a store in Hohenau instructs visitors to push.]]
Colonias Unidas residents typically speak [[Paraguayan Spanish]] with some speaking [[German language|German]], [[Guarani language|Guarani]], and other languages. The colonies celebrate an annual festival in September as well as a local variant of [[Oktoberfest]] called Choppfest, and they hosted the 2025 World Rally Championship. Cultural institutions include the Centro Cultural de Edwin Krug, a museum in Hohenau with artifacts from early settlers.
