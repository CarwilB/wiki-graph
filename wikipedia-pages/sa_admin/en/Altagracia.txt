'''Altagracia''' ({{IPA|es|altaˈɣɾasja}}) is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Rivas Department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

It is the largest town on the island of [[Ometepe]], above the town of [[Moyogalpa]]. Its original name was Astagalpa, meaning ‘house of the herons’ in [[Nahuatl]].<ref name="VN"/> This city also has a ferry port, Puerto de Gracia, that services Granada and San Carlos near the Rio San Juan. However, due to seasonal water level fluctuations, this port and the nightly ferry crossings are often seasonally cancelled.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ometepeislandinfo.com/Ferry-Schedule|title=Ometepe Island Info - Daily Ferry & Lancha Schedule|website=ometepeislandinfo.com|language=en|access-date=2017-02-06}}</ref>

==History==
Altagracia is home to a cathedral which was built in 1924. The city has recently installed a [[sculpture park]] next to the cathedral that contains important pre-Columbian art made from [[basalt]] rock. The figures are of great size, and represent human figures and their [[alter egos]], mainly the [[eagle]] and [[jaguar]].

Also present in Altagracia is the Museum of Ometepe (''Museo de Ometepe'') with a large [[archaeological]] and [[ethnographic]] collection.

==Tradition==
The city patron is [[San Diego de Alcala|San Diego de Alcalá]] and his festival, Fiesta de San Diego,  is celebrated from November 12–18.<ref name="VN">[http://www.vianica.com/visit/ometepe Explore Nicaragua: Ometepe Island<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> The traditional dance called ''baile del Zompopo'' is performed on the 17th of November. This tradition is mixed with the celebration of [[Xolotl]], who inhabited Nicaragua in [[pre-Columbian]] times before the Spaniards arrived. When the [[Franciscan]] arrived in Ometepe in 1613, this tradition was already existent, although they brought with themselves an image of San Diego de Alcalá and with time they obtained his figure and replaced Xolotl.
