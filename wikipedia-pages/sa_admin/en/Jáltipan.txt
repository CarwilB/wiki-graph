'''Jáltipan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the southern part of the state, about 380&nbsp;km from [[Xalapa]], the state capital. It has a surface of 331.48 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|17|58|N|94|43|W|}}.

The municipality of Jáltipan is delimited to the north by [[Chinameca, Veracruz|Chinameca]], to the east by [[Oteapan]], [[Zaragoza, Veracruz|Zaragoza]], [[Cosoleacaque]] and [[Hidalgotitlán]], and to the west by [[Texistepec]] and [[Soconusco, Veracruz|Soconusco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30089a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Jáltipan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070127051620/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30089a.htm|archive-date=2007-01-27|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Jáltipan is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]], and [[Orange (fruit)|orange]]s.
==Culture==
In February the town holds a celebration in honor of {{lang|es|Virgen de la Candelaria}}, patron saint of the town.
==History==
The town was severely damaged during an [[1959 Coatzacoalcos earthquake|earthquake in 1959]].<ref name=NGDC>{{citation|title=Significant Earthquake Database|url=https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/hazel/view/hazards/earthquake/event-more-info/4201|author=National Geophysical Data Center / World Data Service (NGDC/WDS)|publisher=[[National Geophysical Data Center]], [[National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration|NOAA]]|doi=10.7289/V5TD9V7K|year = 1959|type = Data Set}}</ref>

==Transportation==

Since 22 February 2024, Jaltipan has been the site of a [[Tren Interoceánico]] station.<ref>{{cite journal|first=Santos|last=López Celdo|title=Entra en operación estación de Jáltipan del Tren Interoceánico|journal=Diario del Istmo|date=22 February 2024|language=es|url=https://diariodelistmo.com/regional/entra-en-operacion-estacion-de-jaltipan-del-tren-interoceanico/50493995|access-date=13 May 2024}}</ref>
