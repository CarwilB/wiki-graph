'''San Lorenzo''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Morovis, Puerto Rico]]. San Lorenzo has six sectors and its population in 2010 was 1,209.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó. Berrios. |url=https://archive.org/details/nuevageografad00pic/page/247|publisher=San Juan Editorial Universitaria, Universidad de Puerto Rico,1969}}</ref><ref name="Law2015">{{cite book|author=Gwillim Law|title=Administrative Subdivisions of Countries: A Comprehensive World Reference, 1900 through 1998| url =https://books.google.com/books?id=nXCeCQAAQBAJ|access-date=25 December 2018|date=20 May 2015|publisher=McFarland|isbn=978-1-4766-0447-3|page=300}}</ref><ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico: 2010 Population and Housing Unit Counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

==History==
San Lorenzo was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. Clandestine car races took place in San Lorenzo for many years until an accident on July 18, 1998, claimed the lives of five people. Legal action was taken against police for not having stopped the races from occurring. The races had been very popular, with 5,000 to 6,000 spectators on hand, until they were ended.<ref>{{cite web | title=2016 DTS 232 COLON CHEVERE V. CLASS OTERO, ELA, POLICIA Y OTROS, 2016TSPR232 -Jurisprudencia del Tribunal Supremo de P.R. | website=LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico | url=http://www.lexjuris.com/LexJuris/tspr2016/lexj2016232.htm | language=es | access-date=24 June 2020}}</ref>

San Lorenzo in Morovis was one of the areas of Puerto Rico heavily impacted by [[Hurricane Maria]] on September 20, 2017, with washed away bridges and loss of power for months.<ref name="Tama_Oct_17">{{cite news |last1=Tama |first1=Mario |title=Puerto Rico's long road to recovery |url=https://www.cbsnews.com/pictures/hurricane-maria-puerto-ricos-long-road-to-recovery/7/ |access-date=15 January 2019 |work=Getty Images |agency=CBS News |date=October 17, 2017}}</ref>

San Lorenzo was left isolated when Hurricane Maria washed away their main bridge with the [[PR-567]] highway, into and out of the barrio. An old highway which sits directly on the waters of the river is sometimes passable albeit dangerous. The old road always being washed by the river, was at times passable and was being used by residents, (including school buses), until the construction of a temporary bridge was completed in April, 2018. The alternative to traversing the bridge, or the old highway over the river, is an hour-long drive.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.elnuevodia.com/noticias/locales/nota/aisladoelbarriosanlorenzo-2368702/|title=Aislado el barrio San Lorenzo|date=25 October 2017|website=El Nuevo Dia}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://jornadapr.com/noticias/locales/1024/abren-puente-del-barrio-san-lorenzo-en-morovis/|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180407225253/http://jornadapr.com/noticias/locales/1024/abren-puente-del-barrio-san-lorenzo-en-morovis/|url-status=usurped|archive-date=April 7, 2018|title=Abren puente del barrio San Lorenzo en Morovis|first=Francisco Quiñones|last=Maldonado|website=Jornada Noticiosa}}</ref><ref name="Univision Video">{{cite web |title=Un puente caído empeora la situación de los residentes de Morovis en Puerto Rico [A fallen bridge worsens the situation for residents of Morovis, Puerto Rico] |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_Z_GHeVDKs |website=YouTube |publisher=Univision Noticias |access-date=15 June 2020 |language=es |date=17 September 2017}}</ref>

The construction of a bridge for the isolated community was completed in April 2018 and the bridge was named {{lang|es|La Esperanza}} (Hope) in 2019.<ref>{{cite news| title=Develan puente ‘La Esperanza’ en Morovis | newspaper=La Perla del Sur | date=17 March 2019 | url=https://www.periodicolaperla.com/develan-puente-la-esperanza-en-morovis/ | language=es | access-date=15 June 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Abren puente en Morovis |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fjtQc96wegg |via=YouTube |newspaper=Jornada PR |access-date=15 June 2020| date=5 April 2018}}</ref>

==Geography==
San Lorenzo barrio is south of [[Torrecillas, Morovis, Puerto Rico|Torrecillas]], west of [[Morovis Sur]] and [[Río Grande, Morovis, Puerto Rico|Río Grande]] and north of [[Pasto, Morovis, Puerto Rico|Pasto]] and [[Vaga, Morovis, Puerto Rico|Vaga]] barrios. San Lorenzo's west border is with the municipality of [[Ciales, Puerto Rico|Ciales]].
