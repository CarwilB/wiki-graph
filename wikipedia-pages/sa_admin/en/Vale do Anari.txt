'''Vale do Anari''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 11,377 (2020) and its area is 3,135&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

==History==
The municipality was instated by law n°572 (22 June 1994), signed by governor Oswaldo Piana Filho, with its area taken from [[Machadinho d'Oeste]] municipality.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|346861|ha}} [[Jaru Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1984.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Jaru|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=208|access-date=2016-04-27}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|537|ha}} [[Seringueira Extractive Reserve]], one of a number of small sustainable use units in the region whose primary product is rubber.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Seringueira – ISA}}|language=pt |title=RESEX Seringueira|publisher=ISA:  Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6057|access-date=2016-06-07}}</ref>
The municipality also contains the [[Aquariquara Extractive Reserve]], also created in 1995.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Aquariquara – ISA}}|language=pt |title=RESEX Aquariquara|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental|url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/743|access-date=2016-08-30}}</ref>
