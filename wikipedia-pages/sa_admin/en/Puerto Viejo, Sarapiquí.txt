'''Puerto Viejo de Sarapiquí''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Sarapiquí (canton)|Sarapiquí]] canton, in the [[Heredia Province|Heredia]] province of [[Costa Rica]]. Spread over an area of {{cvt|428.17|km2}}, it is the largest city in the canton. It is located at the foothills of the [[Cordillera]] mountains, in the northeastern part of the country. The economy is dependent mainly on [[agriculture]] and [[tourism]].

== Demographics and economy ==
As per 2020 estimate, there were 29,785 inhabitants, including 15,453
males and 14,332 females. About 8,712 (29.2%) of the population was below the age of fourteen. Majority (64.2%) of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (35.8%) as urban.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/costarica/distr/admin/sarapiqu%C3%AD/41001__puerto_viejo/|title=Puerto Viejo|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 

The economy of the town is dependent on [[agriculture]] and tourism. The surrounding areas of the town has several [[banana]] and [[pineapple]] plantations. The rainforests in the region, the nearby protected areas, and activities along the river, contribute to tourism. The rivers in the region serve as a major lifeline and are used to transport agricultural produce and people.<ref name="Visit"/><ref name="Costa"/>
