'''Cachi''' is a department in the east of [[Salta Province]], Argentina. Its capital is the town of [[Cachi, Argentina|Cachi]]. The total population was 2,189 as of 2015.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cachi Department, Salta Province, Argentina |url=https://www.mindat.org/loc-239507.html |access-date=2022-05-02 |website=www.mindat.org}}</ref>
