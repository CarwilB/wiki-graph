'''Corozal''' ({{IPA|es|koɾoˈsal}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Sucre Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

==Transportation==
Corozal is served by [[Las Brujas Airport (Colombia)|Las Brujas Airport]].
