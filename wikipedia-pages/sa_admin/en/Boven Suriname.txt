'''Boven Suriname''' (also '''Upper Suriname''') is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Sipaliwini District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 17,954. Almost its entire population consists of [[Maroon (people)|Maroons]]<ref name="census"/>

The resort is home to many small [[tribal]] villages. The main village is [[Pokigron]]. Pokigron is located at the end of a paved road via [[Brownsweg]] to the [[Avobakaweg]],<ref name="pokigron_paved">{{cite news|url=https://www.indianfeelings.nl/suriname-ontvangt-geld-van-china-om-financiering-project/|title=Suriname ontvangt geld van China om financiering project|website=Indian Feelings|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Villages to the south of Pokigron can only be accessed by boat.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://vivesonderwijsinsuriname.weebly.com/blog/welcome-to-the-jungle|title=Welcome to the Jungle|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl|website=Vives Onderwijs in Suriname}}</ref> The settlements on the right bank of the [[Upper Suriname River]] are usually adherents to the [[Winti]] (Afro-Surinamese) religion, while the left bank is mainly [[Christianity|Christian]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dbnl.org/tekst/kemp009gesc02_01/kemp009gesc02_01_0001.php|title=Een geschiedenis van de Surinaamse literatuur. Deel 2|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|date=2002|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

Aurora can also be accessed from the [[Laduani Airstrip]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ourairports.com/airports/GY-LDO/|title=Ladouanie Airport — Aurora, Sipaliwini, Suriname|website=Our Airports|access-date=20 May 2020}}</ref> Botopasi, Djumu, Goddo, and Pikin Slee are served by the [[Botopasi Airstrip]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ourairports.com/airports/GY-BTO/|title=Botopasi Airport — Botopasi, Sipaliwini, Suriname|website=Our Airports|access-date=20 May 2020}}</ref> Kajana is served by the [[Cayana Airstrip]].<ref name="kajana">{{cite web|url=https://kajana-school-suriname.com/1336115.htm|title=Kajana School Suriname|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

Even though Boven Suriname had long been settled by the Maroons, it wasn't until 1908 that an expedition led by [[Johan Eilerts de Haan]] set out to find the source of the [[Suriname River]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dbnl.org/tekst/_nee003191301_01/_nee003191301_01_0046.php|title=Neerlandia. Jaargang 17|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl|date=1913}}</ref>
