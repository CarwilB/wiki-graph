'''Concepción Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Concepción}}) is one of the three [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] in the [[Bío Bío Region]] of Chile. Its capital [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]], is one of the largest urban areas in Chile. The province spans an area of {{cvt|3439|km2}}, and had a population of 1,025,448 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
The region was earlier populated by indigenous [[Mapuche people]], and the city of [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]] was established on 5 October 1550 by Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Pedro de Valdivia]] as a base for slave labor and a port. However, the region was the site of constant skirmishes between the Mapuche and the Spanish, and was burnt down multiple times. The Spanish fortified the region, and the [[Biobio River]] formed a natural boundary between the Mapuche and Spanish occupied regions. It later developed into a major [[agriculture]] and pastoral center.<ref name="Brit">{{cite web|url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Concepcion-Chile|title=Concepcion|work=[[Britannica]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-573.html|title=Concepción colonial|trans-title=Colonial conception|lang=es|work=Mermoria Chilena|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

The [[Biobio Region]] was established on 10 July 1974, by Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> The region is divided into three provinces&ndash;Concepción, [[Biobío Province|Biobío]], and [[Arauco Province|Arauco]], which are further divided into 33 [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_del_biobio/|lang=es|title=Biobío Region|trans-title=Biobío Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Concepción Province is one of the three provinces of the [[Biobio Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It covers an area of {{cvt|3439|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> Almost half of the land area consists of coastal islands, while the remaining land forming part of the [[Biobio River|Biobio]], [[Carampangue River|Carampangue]] and [[Itata River]] basins.<ref name="MI">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/1|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Concepción Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Concepción Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> Concepción is vulnerable to natural hazards such as [[earthquakes]] and high [[tidal waves]], and has suffered damages due to these events in the past.<ref name="Brit"/><ref name="Hist"/>

Concepción has a warm [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|13.3|C}}. The region receives approximately {{cvt|83.9|cm}} of rainfall annually on average, with majority of the rain falling in the winter months. The summer season extends from December to March, is relatively dry.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://en.climate-data.org/south-america/chile/viii-region-del-biobio/concepcion-3984/|title=Concepcion province|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Concepcion is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into 12 [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'')&ndash;[[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]], [[Coronel, Chile|Coronel]], [[Chiguayante]], [[Florida, Chile|Florida]], [[Hualpén]], [[Hualqui]], [[Lota, Chile|Lota]], [[Penco]], [[San Pedro de la Paz]], [[Santa Juana, Chile|Santa Juana]], [[Talcahuano]], and [[Tomé]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MI"/> Its capital Concepción, located close to the mouth of the Biobio river, is one of the largest urban areas in Chile, and a major commercial center in the region.<ref name="Brit"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gochile.cl/en/concepcion/|title=Concepcion|work=Go Chile|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 1,025,448 inhabitants. The population consisted of 534,672 females (52.1%) and 490,776 males (47.9%). About 16.9% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 68.4% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 14.7% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 988,383 inhabitants (96.4%) and a rural population of 37,065 inhabitants (3.6%). Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 988,483 inhabitants (96.4%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 950,138 inhabitants (92.7%), while 75,247 inhabitants (7.3%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 324,722 adherents (38.2%), followed by [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 279,892 adherents (32.9%), and 212,450 inhabitants (25%) indicating no religious affiliation.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/082__arauco/|title=Arauco Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
