'''Bombinhas''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the [[South Region, Brazil|South region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web|url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200614144326/https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |url-status=dead |archive-date=June 14, 2020 |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081002024524/http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |archive-date=October 2, 2008 }}</ref> It is the smallest municipality of that state in terms of area.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|17104|ha|adj=on}} [[Marinha do Arvoredo Biological Reserve]], established in 1990.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica Marinha do Arvoredo|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=214 |access-date=2016-04-27}}</ref>
