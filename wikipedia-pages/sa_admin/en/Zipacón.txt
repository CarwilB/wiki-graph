'''Zipacón''' ({{IPA|es|sipaˈkon}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Western Savanna Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The urban centre of Zipacón is situated at an altitude of {{convert|2550|m|ft}} on the [[Bogotá savanna]], the southern flatlands of the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. Zipacón borders [[Anolaima]], [[Facatativá]], [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] and [[Bojacá]].<ref name=websiteZipacon>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.zipacon-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Zipacón] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150520172747/http://www.zipacon-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-20 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Zipacón comes from [[Chibcha language|Muysccubun]] and means "crying of the ''[[zipa]]''".<ref name=websiteZipacon/>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Zipacón was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Zipacón was the site of meditation for the ''zipa''. The settlement was at the border with the [[Panche people|Panche]], eternal enemies of the Muisca. It was in Zipacón where the Panche invaded when the Spanish [[list of conquistadors in Colombia|conquistadors]] were conquering the Bogotá savanna.<ref name=PueblosSagipa>{{in lang|es}} [https://pueblosoriginarios.com/biografias/sagipa.html ''Zipa'' Sagipa] - Pueblos Originarios</ref>

The oldest evidences for [[Muisca agriculture|agriculture]] of [[potato]]es on the Bogotá savanna has been unearthed in Zipacón and dated at 3200 years [[Before Present|BP]].<ref name=Garcia_p63>García, 2012, p.63</ref> The settlement was inhabited since the [[Herrera Period]], at least since 3270 BP.<ref name=Nieto_p96>Nieto Escalante et al., 2010, p.96</ref> [[Muisca art#Rock art on the Altiplano|Rock art]] has been discovered in Zipacón.<ref name=ICANH>{{in lang|es}} [https://www.icanh.gov.co/?idcategoria=5157 Siteos arqueológicos] - [[ICANH]]</ref><ref name=Munoz_p10>Muñoz Castiblanco, 2006, p. 10</ref><ref name=Botiva>Martínez & Botiva, 2004a</ref><ref>López Estupiñán, 2011</ref><ref>Martínez & Botiva, 2004b, p. 15</ref>

Modern Zipacón was founded on July 5, 1561, by José Antonio Rubio.<ref name=websiteZipacon/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Zipacón are agriculture and livestock farming.<ref name=websiteZipacon/>
