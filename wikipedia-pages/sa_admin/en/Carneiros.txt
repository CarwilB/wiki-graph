'''Carneiros''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kɐhˈneɾu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 9,159 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/carneiros/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|113|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
