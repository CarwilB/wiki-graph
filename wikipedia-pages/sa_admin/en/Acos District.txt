'''Acos District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Acomayo Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Maranniyuq]] at {{convert|4520|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Acomayo Province (Cusco Region)</ref>

* [[Hatun Urqu (Acomayo)|Hatun Urqu]]
* Inti Qhawarina
* Kunkayuq
* Markan Urqu
* Nina Urqu
* Wayna Pata

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (88.55%) learnt to speak in childhood, 11.11% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población</ref>
