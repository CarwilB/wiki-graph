'''Esquipulas''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Matagalpa (department)|Matagalpa]] department of [[Nicaragua]].

== Geography ==
To the north, Esquipulas borders the municipalities of [[San Dionisio, Nicaragua|San Dionisio]] and [[Matagalpa]], and to the south [[Boaco]] and San José de los Remates.<ref name='INIFOM'>{{Cite web |url=http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/municipios.html |title=Instituto Nicaragüense de Fomento Municipal |access-date=2011-12-03 |archive-url=https://wayback.archive-it.org/all/20111203185150/http://www.inifom.gob.ni/municipios/municipios.html |archive-date=2011-12-03 |url-status=live }}</ref>

=== Climate ===
The municipality enjoys mild temperatures with a rainy season from May - December and a dry season between January and April.
