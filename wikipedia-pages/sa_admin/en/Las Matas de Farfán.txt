'''Las Matas de Farfán''' is a town in the [[San Juan Province (Dominican Republic)|San Juan Province]], [[Dominican Republic]]. It is the birthplace of a number of current and former [[Major League Baseball]] players including [[Jean Segura]], [[Juan Encarnación]], [[Roberto Novoa]], [[Odalis Perez]], [[Ramón Santiago]], and [[Valerio de los Santos]]. During the [[Haitian occupation of Santo Domingo|1822–44 Haitian occupation]], the town was officially designated as '''Las-Mathas'''.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-04-03 |title=Relation: Las Matas de Farfán (7400868) |url=https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/7400868 |access-date=2024-04-18 |website=OpenStreetMap |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=PDF oficial del gobierno de República Dominicana |url=http://www.lmd.gob.do/transparencia/phocadownload/Publicaciones/Division-Territorial-2015.pdf}}</ref>

The [[Furnia de Catanamatias]], in the mountain range above the town, is the deepest cave in the Dominican republic (380m).<ref name="mondo">{{cite journal |last1=Bozzer |first1=Luigino |last2=Muscio |first2=Giuseppe |title=Haitises 1988 |journal=Mondo Sotterraneo |date=1989 |volume=XIII |pages=11–28}}</ref>
