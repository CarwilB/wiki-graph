'''Pirambu''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern Portuguese pronunciation: </small>[piɾɐ̃ˈbu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 9,359 (2020) and its area is 218&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=January 9, 2007}}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|5547|ha}} [[Santa Isabel Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1988.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Santa Isabel|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=199|accessdate=2016-04-17}}</ref>
