'''Quimilí''' is a town in [[Santiago del Estero Province]], Argentina. The capital city of the [[Moreno Department]], it lies about 200&nbsp;km east of the provincial capital city, [[Santiago del Estero]], and 70&nbsp;km west of the border with [[Chaco Province]], to which it is connected by National Route 89.

The [[National Institute of Statistics and Census of Argentina|INDEC]] census of 2001 recorded a total population of 10,959 inhabitants for Quimilí.
