'''União dos Palmares''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[uniˈɐ̃w ˈduɦ pawˈmɐɾi(s)]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 65,790 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/uniao-dos-palmares/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|428|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> Surrounding agricultural land is largely dedicated to sugar cane and cattle.<ref>http://wikimapia.org/4101240/pt/Uni%C3%A3o-dos-Palmares | Wikimapia: União dos Palmares</ref> At one time, when the city was an active rail stop with regular passenger service,<ref>http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/alagoas/uniao.htm | Estações Ferroviárias (Rail Stations) do Brasil- UNIÃO DOS PALMARES</ref> it was named simply União due to its rail junction joining Alagoas and [[Pernambuco]]. The name was changed in 1944 to reflect its historic significance.<ref>http://www.a-brasil.com/uniaodospalmares/index.htm {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140804102824/http://a-brasil.com/uniaodospalmares/index.htm |date=2014-08-04 }} | União dos Palmares - Guía Turística</ref> The city is increasingly seeing domestic and foreign tourist drawn by historical and natural features that are now protected in Parque Nacional [[Serra da Barriga]] and Parque Memorial Quilombo dos Palmares.

==History==

The city of '''União dos Palmares''' has a historic importance to [[Afro-Brazilians]]. On a nearby hill was a [[Quilombo|mocambo]] or,  in more recent terms, a [[quilombo]]. These were centers of African slaves fleeing and resisting [[slavery]]. Known as the [[Quilombo dos Palmares]] this was the largest and most organized<ref Name=qdp>http://www.articlemyriad.com/69.htm {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070226225557/http://www.articlemyriad.com/69.htm |date=2007-02-26 }} | Oppression and Rebellion: Slavery in Brazil and The Quilombo at Palmares</ref> of the many quilombos in Brazil. The most famous figure of the Quilombo dos Palmares was [[Zumbi| Zumbi dos Palmares]]. The quilombo reached a population somewhere around 30,000 composed of former slaves and some Amerindian allies. Between 1672 and 1696 it withstood repeated attacks by colonial forces until it fell to regular forces with artillery.<ref Name=qdp />

==Present Day Relationship to History==
The city is the gateway to the Parque Memorial Quilombo dos Palmares<ref>http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=projeto_parque_quilombo_palmares{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} | Parque Memorial Quilombo dos Palmares</ref> in the Parque Nacional Serra da Barriga<ref>http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=historia_serra_barriga {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070518042606/http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=historia_serra_barriga |date=2007-05-18 }}  Serra da Barriga</ref> and described as "A Terra da Liberdade" (A land of liberty).<ref>http://www.ale.al.gov.br/www/uniao.htm{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} | Cidades Históricas - União dos Palmares (Assembléia Legislativa de Alagoas)</ref> Memorial celebrations attract visitors and the park is a natural area preserving endangered [[Atlantic semi-deciduous forests]] of the [[Mata Atlântica]]. The park attracts visitors both domestic and foreign to tourist accommodations around the city.<ref>http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=turismo_onde_hospedar | Tourismo</ref> The park and city were given publicity by a 20 November 2003 ceremonial opening by the President of Brazil.<ref>http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=historia_serra_barriga {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070518042606/http://www.quilombodospalmares.org.br/index.php?sec=historia_serra_barriga |date=2007-05-18 }} | Serra de Barriga History</ref>
