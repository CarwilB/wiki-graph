'''Arroio dos Ratos''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. Thanks to its soil, is nationally known as the ''National Cradle of the Coal industry''. The city is also known as the ''National Capital  of the Watermelon''.
