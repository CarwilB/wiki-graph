'''Tilarán''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Tilarán District|Tilarán]] district.

== History ==
Tilarán was created on August 21, 1923 by decree 170.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
Tilarán has an area of {{cvt|638.39|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020}}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:579}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The canton surrounds [[Lake Arenal]] except for the lake's southeast end, which belongs to the province of [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]]. The northern border is in the [[Cordillera de Guanacaste]], touching the Corobicí River at its northernmost limits. The southern part of the canton is in the [[Cordillera de Tilarán]] (mountain range).
