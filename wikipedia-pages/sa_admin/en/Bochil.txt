'''Bochil''' is a [[settlement classification in Mexico|city]] and  [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], in southern [[Mexico]]. It covers an area of {{cvt|372.7|km2}}. Bochil serves as the head town for the [[Second Federal Electoral District of Chiapas]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 30,642,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Bochil|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=013|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 22,722 as of 2005.<ref> {{cite web |title=Bochil|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07013a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=2008-07-25|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210323/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07013a.htm|archive-date=2007-09-27}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the city of Bochil had a population of 12,404.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Bochil, the municipality had 83 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Ajiló, Chiapas|Ajiló]] (1,271), [[El Copal, Chiapas|El Copal]] (1,133), and [[Luis Espinoza, Chiapas|Luis Espinoza]] (1,107), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
