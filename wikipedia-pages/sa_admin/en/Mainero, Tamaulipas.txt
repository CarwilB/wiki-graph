'''Mainero''' is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] located in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. Its capital is called Villa Mainero and had 338 inhabitants during a 2020 [[INEGI]] [[census]]. The municipality as a whole had 2&nbsp;048 inhabitants. Other localities include José Guadalupe Mainero, Guadalupe Mainero and Guadalupe Mainero.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2020 |title=¿Cuántos habitantes tiene... - Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 - Mainero |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/cpv/2020/resultadosrapidos/default.html?texto=Mainero |access-date=2023-10-14 |website=www.inegi.org.mx}}</ref> All named after Guadalupe Mainero (1856–1901), a Mexican journalist, teacher and [[governor]] of Tamaulipas between May 4, 1896 and July 31, 1901.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tampico |first=Alfredo Márquez {{!}} El Sol de |title=Guadalupe Mainero, de periodista a gobernador de Tamaulipas |url=https://www.elsoldetampico.com.mx/local/guadalupe-mainero-de-periodista-a-gobernador-de-tamaulipas-7630979.html |access-date=2023-10-14 |website=El Sol de Tampico {{!}} Noticias Locales, Policiacas, sobre México, Tamaulipas y el Mundo |language=es}}</ref>
