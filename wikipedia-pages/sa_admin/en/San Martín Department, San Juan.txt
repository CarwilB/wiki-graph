'''San Martín''' is a department in the east of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]] province ([[Argentina]]). It is predominantly a landscape of [[mountains]] in the east and significant production of [[wine]].

The department was created on 19 September 1942 from a division of other lands, taking its name from [[José de San Martín]].<ref>{{cite web|last1=Roco Navea |first1=Celeste |url=https://www.tiempodesanjuan.com/departamentales/san-martin/san-martin-el-hermano-menor-tres-que-se-volvio-sinonimo-prosperidad-n354023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240110033235/https://www.tiempodesanjuan.com/departamentales/san-martin/san-martin-el-hermano-menor-tres-que-se-volvio-sinonimo-prosperidad-n354023 |title=San Martín, el "hermano menor de tres" que se volvió sinónimo de prosperidad |language=es |publisher=Tiempo de San Juan |date=18 July 2023 |archive-date=10 January 2024}}</ref> In 1996, it became the second controlled appellation established in Mendoza alongside the renowned Luján de Cuyo.
