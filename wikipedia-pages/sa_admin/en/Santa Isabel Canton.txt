'''Santa Isabel Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Azuay Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Santa Isabel, Azuay|Santa Isabel]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 18,015.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados|title = Resultados}}</ref>
*[[Mestizo]]  92.5%
*[[White Latin American|White]]  4.4%
*[[Afro-Ecuadorian]]  2.2%
*[[Indigenous peoples in Ecuador|Indigenous]]  0.5%
*[[Montubio]]  0.2%
*Other  0.2%
