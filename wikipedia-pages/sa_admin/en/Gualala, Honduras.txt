'''Gualala''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|66.33|km2}} and had a population of 5,191 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census.

== History ==
The municipality of Gualala was earlier known as "Gualatan" and "Gualalatepequez", a name derived from the indigenous language meaning "Abundance of Good Lands".<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Gualala, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Gualala.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-gualala/|title=Municipality of Gualala|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Gualala is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Ilama, Honduras|Ilama]] to the north, [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Barbara]] to the south, [[San José de Colinas ]] to the east, and Ilama to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|66.33|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1611__gualala/|title=Gualala|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/261-Santa-Barbara-Gualala.pdf|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Gualala, Santa Bárbara|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|784.3|m}} above sea level, Gualala has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|21.07|C}} and receives approximately {{cvt|288.85| mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/gualala|title=Gualala|work=Weather and climate|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Gualala had a total population of 5,191 inhabitants, if whom 2,737 (52.7%) were men and 2,454 (47.3%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural, with no urban population recorded in the census. The intercensal growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 1.7%.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

About 2.3% of the population were classified as [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest as non-indigneous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 32% of the population were aged 0–14 years, 57.7% were aged 15–64, and 9.3% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 24.8 years and the mean age was 29.6 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 25.7%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 1,266 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.1 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}}

== Sports ==
The football club, [[Atlético Gualala]], which competes in the Honduran second division, the [[Liga de Ascenso de Honduras]], is based out of the city.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>
