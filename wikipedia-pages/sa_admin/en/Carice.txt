'''Carice''' ({{IPA|fr|kaʁis}}; {{langx|ht|Karis}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Vallières Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 10,180 inhabitants.

== Communal sections ==
The commune consists of two [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Bois Camelle]], rural
* [[Rose Bonite]], urban and rural, containing the town of Carice
