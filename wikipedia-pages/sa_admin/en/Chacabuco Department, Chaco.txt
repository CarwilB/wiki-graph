'''Chacabuco Department''' is a western [[departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 28,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{convert|1,378|km²|sqmi|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Charata]], which is located around {{convert|1,100|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the [[Capital federal]].
