'''Sopetrán''' is a municipality of [[Colombia]], located in the subregion west of the state of [[Antioquia, Colombia|Antioquia]]. It is bounded to the north by the municipality of [[Olaya (Colombia)|Olaya]], to the east by the municipality of Belmira, to the south by the municipalities of [[San Jerónimo, Colombia|San Jerónimo]] and [[Ebéjico]], and to the west by the municipality of [[Santa Fe de Antioquia]]. The population was 13,748 at the 2018 census. It is 59 kilometers from the city of [[Medellín|Medellin]], the state capital of Antioquia. The municipality of Sopetrán has an area of 223 square kilometers.

{{As of|2007}}, Sopetrán was one of three municipalities along the [[Route of the Sun]] in western Antioquia{{citation needed|date=July 2020}}, along with the municipalities of San Jerónimo and Santa Fe de Antioquia, with which Sopetrán shares much of its history{{citation needed|date=July 2020}}. Since the opening of the West Fernando Gomez Martinez Tunnel in 2006, tourism in the region has tripled{{citation needed|date=July 2020}}. The area is particularly suitable for [[ecotourism]].

== History ==
Originally, the indigenous communities of the [[Nutabe]]s and [[Tahamí people|Tahamíes]] inhabited the municipality of Sopetrán. Before the arrival of the [[Spain|Spanish]], the region was called {{proper name|Los Guamas}}. On February 22, 1616, the municipality of Sopetrán was officially established by Francisco Herrera and Campuzano, a native of [[Alcalá de Henares|Alcala de Henares]] in Spain. Herrera worshipped Our Lady of Sopetrán, and he is believed to have named the town after her.

Formerly, the town has been known by the names of {{proper name|Las Guamas}},<ref>{{Cite web|title=ANTIOQUIA-SOPETRÁN|url=http://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/ANTIOQUIA/MUNICIPIOS/SOPETRAN/SOPETRAN.htm|access-date=2020-07-11|website=www.colombiaturismoweb.com}}</ref> Vice Parish of Our Lady of Saladito of Cordoba, and Parish of Our Lady of the Assumption and Sopetrán.<ref>{{Cite book|last=Santoro|first=Nicholas Joseph|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=ZXWRgP-0KBkC&q=sopetran+former+name&pg=PA347|title=Mary in Our Life: Atlas of the Names and Titles of Mary, the Mother of Jesus, and Their Place in Marian Devotion|date=2011-08-12|publisher=iUniverse|isbn=978-1-4620-4022-3|language=en}}</ref>

The origin of the name {{proper name|Las Guamas}} was the lands that had been settled by the indigenous {{proper name|Guamas}} or Guacas many centuries prior, who engaged in agriculture, hunting, fishing, and salt mining. The latter caught the Spaniards' attention, who had been transporting salt from the sea for domestic use and to feed the newly imported cattle.

In 1814, Sopetrán achieved the status of a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]].

== Climate == 
The town is located 716 meters above sea level. The town's climate is defined as tropical. During the summer, there is considerable rainfall, but the winter has comparatively little rainfall. The average temperature is around 24.9&nbsp;°C (76.8&nbsp;°F), and the average annual rainfall is 1360&nbsp;mm (53.5 inches).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://en.climate-data.org/south-america/colombia/antioquia/sopetran-50303/|title=Sopetrán climate: Average Temperature, weather by month, Sopetrán weather averages - Climate-Data.org|website=en.climate-data.org}}</ref>
