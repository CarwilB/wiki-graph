'''Jilotepec''' ([[Nahuatl]]: "on the hill of [[:wikt:jilote|jilotes]]"<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30093a.html |title=Jilotepec |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=27 February 2018 |archive-date=29 January 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190129062847/http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30093a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|11|km}} north-northwest of the state capital [[Xalapa]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Geography==
The municipality of Jilotepec is located in central Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|860|and(-)|1900|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Coacoatzintla]] to the north, [[Naolinco]] to the northeast, Xalapa to the southeast, [[Banderilla, Veracruz|Banderilla]] to the south, [[Rafael Lucio]] to the southwest, and [[Tlacolulan]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Jilotepec, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=27 February 2018 |archive-date=4 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804015215/http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|56.437|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.1% of the state's area.<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC/> 

The municipality is drained by upper tributaries of the [[Actopan River]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The perennial Naolinco River forms part of Jilotepec's border with the municipality of Naolinco.<ref name=compendium/> 

Jilotepec's climate is generally humid with rain throughout the year. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|22|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1400|and(-)|1600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Jilotepec was founded by the [[Totonac people]] in 980 as part of the confederation of Tlacolulan. The [[Teochichimeca]]s controlled Jilotepec from 1380 to 1489, when Jilotepec switched its allegiance to the [[Aztec Empire]]. A [[hacienda]] was established at La Concepción at the end of the 16th century. The parish of Jilotepec was inaugurated in 1811.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

On 28 March 1831, Jilotepec became a municipality in the canton of Xalapa in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=111, 218 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0 |archive-date=2018-02-09 |access-date=2018-02-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180209182348/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> From 1995 to the beginning of the 21st century a wave of [[Emigration from Mexico|emigration]] occurred from central Veracruz to the United States; inhabitants from Jilotepec in particular preferred to emigrate to [[New York City]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Perez Monterosas |first=Mario |date=2003 |title=Las redes sociales de la migracion emergente de Veracruz a los Estados Unidos |language=es |url=http://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?pid=S1665-89062003000100006&script=sci_abstract |journal=Migraciones Internacionales |volume=2 |issue=1 |pages=136–160 |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Sergio Fernández Lara.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.jilotepec.gob.mx/transparencia/ |title=Portal Modelo de Transparencia Municipal |language=es |publisher=Órgano de Fiscalización Superior del Estado de Veracruz |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref>

In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipality of Jilotepec recorded a population of 16,585 inhabitants living in 3474 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Jilotepec.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Jilotepec CENSUS]</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 16,682 inhabitants in Jilotepec, of which 3.97% reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African descent]].<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC/> 

There are 34 localities in the municipality,<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC/> of which two are classified as urban:
* The municipal seat, also called Jilotepec, which recorded a population of 4,309 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.
* La Concepción, located {{convert|6|km}} east of the municipal seat, which recorded 3,707 inhabitants in 2020.<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC/>

Other localities include Vista Hermosa (1,649 hab.), Paso de San Juan (1,214 hab.) and El Pueblito (1,198 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSJILOTEPEC/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Jilotepec is agriculture, involving mainly the cultivation of coffee, sugarcane, corn and beans. Cattle and pigs are also raised.<ref name=plan>{{cite web |url=http://www.legisver.gob.mx/fiscalizacion/Planes%20Municipales/PlanesMunicipales2014pdf/PMD%20JILOTEPEC%20al%2015%20ABRIL%202014.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |page=42 |publisher=Municipal council of Jilotepec, Veracruz |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref>
