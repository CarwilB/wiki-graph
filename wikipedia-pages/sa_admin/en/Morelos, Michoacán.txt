'''Morelos''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|40|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Morelia]]. The municipal seat is at [[Villa Morelos]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Morelos is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northern Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|2100|and(-)|2700|m}}. It borders the Michoacanese municipalities of [[Puruándiro]] to the northwest, [[Huandacareo, Michoacán|Huandacareo]] to the east, [[Chucándiro]] to the southeast, [[Huaniqueo]] to the southwest, and [[Jiménez, Michoacán|Jiménez]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16054.pdf |title=Morelos, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |accessdate=27 December 2020}}</ref> It also borders the municipality of [[Yuriria]] in [[Guanajuato]] to the northeast. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|183.927|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.31% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Morelos consists of temperate forest (30%), grassland (13%), and tropical forest (5%). Another 49% of the land is used for agriculture and 2% consists of urban areas.<ref name=compendium/> Morelos is located in the [[Lerma River]] basin. There are small reservoirs named Epitacio Huerta and Caballerias in the municipality, which are used for agricultural irrigation.<ref name=plan/>

Morelos has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The place now known as Morelos was originally called Huango, and was part of the [[Purépecha Empire]] in [[Pre-Columbian Mexico|pre-Hispanic Mexico]]. Huango was one of the 61 municipalities created in Michoacán in 1831. In 1898, the municipal seat was renamed Huango de Morelos. In 1902 the seat was renamed Villa Morelos and the municipality itself renamed Morelos.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=158–159 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is José Guadalupe Coria Solís.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |accessdate=27 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Morelos recorded a population of 8091 inhabitants living in 2123 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=054 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Morelos |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |accessdate=27 December 2020}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 7806 inhabitants in Morelos.<ref name=survey/>

There are 31 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Villa Morelos is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2446 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Morelos is agriculture. Corn is the main crop, followed by forage oats.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Morelos Municipal Council |url=https://drive.google.com/open?id=1UCxxMkItn9S6425LxfHqP01wbTPMLFBt |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=28 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 3a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=23 March 2016 |accessdate=27 December 2020}}</ref>
