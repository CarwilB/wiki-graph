'''Marco''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Ceará]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region]] of [[Brazil]]. With an area of {{Convert|573.61|km²|sp=us}}, of which {{Convert|4.4206|km²}} is urban, it is located 191 km from [[Fortaleza]], the state capital, and 1,645 km from [[Brasília]], the federal capital. Its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 demographic census]] was 25,799 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), ranking as the 72nd most populous municipality in the state of Ceará.

== Geography ==
The territory of Marco covers {{Convert|573.61|km²|sp=us}},<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |title=Brasil / Ceará / Marco |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/panorama |access-date=2025-06-18}}</ref> of which {{Convert|4.4206|km²}} constitutes the urban area.<ref name="Tabela/8418">{{cite web |year=2019 |title=Tabela 8418 - Áreas urbanizadas, Loteamento vazio, Área total mapeada e Subcategorias |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/8418#/n3/23/n6/2307809/v/12749/p/all/d/v12749%204/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=16 December 2024 |publisher=IBGE}}</ref> It sits at an average altitude of 29 meters above [[sea level]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Sousa |first=Paulo José Moura |date= |title=Memorial descritivo das instalações de gases medicinais do Hospital Municipal Manuel Jaime Neves Osterno no município de Marco - CE |url=https://marco.ce.gov.br/arquivos_download.php?pg=licitacao&id=545&subid=3936 |journal=Prefeitura Municipal de Marco |page=2 |access-date=16 June 2025}}</ref> Marco borders these municipalities: to the north, [[Bela Cruz]]; to the south, [[Senador Sá]] and [[Morrinhos, Ceará|Morrinhos]]; to the east, [[Acaraú]], Bela Cruz again, and Morrinhos; and to the west, [[Granja, Ceará|Granja]] and Senador Sá again.{{Sfn|Silva|2002|p=28}} The city is located 191 km from the state capital [[Fortaleza]],<ref name="distancia">{{cite web |title=Distância entre Marco - Ceará e Fortaleza - CE |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-fortaleza-ce-brazil-ate-marco-ceara-ce |access-date=14 December 2024}}</ref> and 1,645 km from the federal capital [[Brasília]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Distância entre Brasília e Marco |url=https://www.distanciasentrecidades.com/pesquisa?from=Brasilia%2C%20Brazil&to=Marco%2C%20Brazil |access-date=25 July 2014}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
Between the censuses of [[2010 Brazilian census|2010]] and [[2022 Brazilian census|2022]], the population of Marco registered a growth of just over 4.4%, with an annual geometric growth rate of 0.36%.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Taxa de crescimento geométrico ( Unidade: % ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122278 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> With 25,799 inhabitants in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]] (62.48% living in the urban area),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação do domicílio e sexo |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#/n6/2307809/v/all/p/all/c1/all/c2/all/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c1,t+c2/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> the municipality ranked only 72nd in the state that year,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122232 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with 50,47% male and 49,53% female,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/c2/all/c287/100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/result |access-date=2024-12-16}}</ref> resulting in a sex ratio of 101.9 (1,019 men for every 1,000 women),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Sexo / Razão de sexo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122248 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> compared to 24,703 inhabitants in the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]], when it held the 76th state position.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2010 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true&tipo=ranking&indicador=27665 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Regarding [[age group]] in the 2022 census, 70.65% of the inhabitants were between 15 and 64 years old, 22.28% were under fifteen, and 7.07% were 65 or older.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2307809/v/all/p/all/c2/6794/c287/6653,49108,49109,60040,60041,93070,93084,93085,93086,93087,93088,93089,93090,93091,93092,93093,93094,93095,93096,93097,93098,100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> The [[population density]] in 2022 was 44.98 inhabitants per square kilometer,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |title=Território / Densidade demográfica ( Unidade: habitantes por quilômetro quadrado ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122231 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with an average of 2.93 inhabitants per household.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |title=Tabela 4712 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, Moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados e Média de moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4712#/n6/2301208/v/all/p/all/d/v5930%202/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=2024-01-06}}</ref>

The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) was considered medium, according to data from the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). According to the 2010 report published in 2013, its value was 0.615, ranking 87th in the state (out of 184 municipalities) and 3,796th nationally (out of 5,565 municipalities), and the [[Gini coefficient]] rose from 0.43 in 2003<ref name="Gini2003">{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2003 |title=Mapa de pobreza e desigualdade |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/marco/pesquisa/36/30246?ano=2003 |access-date=2024-12-21}}</ref> to 0.54 in 2010.<ref name="Gini2010">{{cite web |date=2010 |title=Índice de Gini |url=https://ceara.dieese.org.br/ws2/tabela/ceara/indice-de-gini |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240624234612/https://ceara.dieese.org.br/ws2/tabela/ceara/indice-de-gini |archive-date=June 24, 2024 |access-date=2024-12-21 |website=Observatório da Agricultura Familiar do Ceará}}</ref> Considering only the longevity index, its value is 0.734, the income index is 0.564, and the education index is 0.553.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web |date=2010 |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610114916/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |access-date=October 11, 2010 |work=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano |publisher=United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)}}</ref>
