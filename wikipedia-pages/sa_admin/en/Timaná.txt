'''Timaná''' is a town and municipality in the [[Huila Department]], [[Colombia]]. The municipality is located in the south of Huila at an altitude of {{convert|1100|m|ft}} and {{convert|166|km|mi}} southwest of the capital [[Neiva]].<ref name=websiteTibana>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.timana-huila.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tibaná]</ref>

== History ==
The region of Timaná before the [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|conquest in Colombia]] was inhabited by the [[Timana people|Timana]], bordered to the north by the {{ill|Yalcón|es|Yalcón}} and farther north, the [[Paez people|Paez]] and [[Pijao people|Pijao]].
 
Modern Timaná was founded on December 18, 1538 by [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|Pedro de Añasco]], soldier in the army of [[Sebastián de Belalcázar]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.banrepcultural.org/blaavirtual/revistas/credencial/sept2001/ciudades.htm Fundacioned de ciudades y poblaciones] - [[Banco de la República]]</ref>
