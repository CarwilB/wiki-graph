'''12 de Octubre''' is a western [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 20,000 inhabitants in an area of  2,576&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[General Pinedo, Chaco|General Pinedo]], which is located around 1,060&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
