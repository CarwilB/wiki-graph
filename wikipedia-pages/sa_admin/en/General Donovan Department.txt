'''General Donovan''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]], named after General [[:es:Antonio Dónovan|Antonio Donovan]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 13,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{convert|1,487|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Makallé]], which is located around 
{{convert|1,060|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the national capital, [[Buenos Aires]].
