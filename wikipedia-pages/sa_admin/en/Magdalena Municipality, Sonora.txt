'''Magdalena'''<ref>{{cite web |title=Ley de Gobierno y Administracion Municipal, Art. 9 |url=https://gestion.api.congresoson.gob.mx/publico/media/consulta?id=33117 |publisher=[[Congress of Sonora]]}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]]  in northwestern [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=31 July 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=4 January 2010 }}</ref>
In the 2020 Census, the municipality reported a total population of 33,049, up by 11.2% from the 2010 result.<ref>{{cite web |title=Magdalena: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/magdalena-26036 |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref>

The municipal seat is the town of [[Magdalena de Kino]]. It is a selected [[Pueblo Mágico]] for its scenic historical qualities.

The tomb of Spanish colonial missionary [[Eusebio Kino]] is located here.

== Geography ==
It is located in the northern part of the State of Sonora, 50 [[miles]] (80 kilometers) south of the border with the United States; its extreme coordinates are 30° 26'- 31° 06' North latitude and 110° 44'- 111° 18' West longitude, its altitude fluctuates between a minimum of 1640 feet above sea level (500 meters asl) and a maximum of 6890 feet asl (2100 meters asl). The municipality has an area of 563.8 square miles (1,460.23 square kilometers) that represent 3% of the total area of Sonora.

It is bordered to the North by the municipality of Nogales, to the South by the municipality of Santa Ana (Sonora), to the East by the municipality of Ímuris and the municipality of Cucurpe, and to the West by the municipalities of Tubutama and Sáric.
