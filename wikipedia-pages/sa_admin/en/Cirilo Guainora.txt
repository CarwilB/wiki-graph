'''Cirilo Guainora  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Cémaco District]], [[Comarca Emberá]], [[Panama]] with a population of 8,703 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Cémaco District.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 22 of November 8, 1983.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,428; its population as of 2000 was 2,015.<ref name=inec/>
