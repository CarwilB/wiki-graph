'''Houttuin''' ({{Literal translation|wood garden}}) is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Wanica District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 15,656. The largest ethnic group are the [[Indo-Surinamese]].<ref name="census"/> The resort is named after the sugar [[plantation]] {{lang|nl|Houttuyn}}, which was founded in 1737.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/h/houttuin|title=Plantage Houttuin|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname Plantages}}</ref> The resort was mainly agricultural,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://wanica.nl/Joomla/distrikt-wanica/geschiedenis-van-distrikt-suriname?showall=1|title=Geschiedenis|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Wanica.nl|archive-date=23 April 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210423051607/https://wanica.nl/Joomla/distrikt-wanica/geschiedenis-van-distrikt-suriname?showall=1|url-status=dead}}</ref> and is planned to remain an agricultural centre.<ref name="planning"/> In the early 21st century, it has seen a steady population growth, and is becoming suburban with housing projects like ''{{ill|Tout Lui Faut|nl}}''.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Structuur Analyse IV|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Planning Office Suriname}}</ref>

[[Staatsolie]] is operating an [[oil refinery]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hydrocarbons-technology.com/projects/staatsolies-tout-lui-faut-refinery-expansion/|title=Staatsolie's Tout Lui Faut Refinery Expansion|website=Hydro Carbons Technology|access-date=23 May 2020}}</ref> and a [[Thermal power station|thermal power plant]] in Houttuin.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/surinamenieuws/bruto-omzet-staatsolie-us-500-miljoen-in-2019-su/|title=Bruto Omzet Staatsolie US 500 miljoen in 2019|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
