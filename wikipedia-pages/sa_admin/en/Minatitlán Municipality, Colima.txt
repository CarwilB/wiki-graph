'''Minatitlán''' is a [[municipality (Mexico)|municipality]] of the Mexican state of [[Colima]].  Its municipal seat is the city of [[Minatitlán, Colima]].  Its principal economic activities are farming, ranching and [[mining]].  Minatitlán borders the state of [[Jalisco]] to the northwest, the municipality of [[Manzanillo Municipality, Colima|Manzanillo]] to the southwest, and the municipalities of [[Coquimatlán (municipality)|Coquimatlán]], [[Villa de Álvarez (municipality)|Villa de Álvarez]], and [[Comala (municipality)|Comala]] to the southeast.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06008a.htm |title=Minatitlán, Colima |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=2007-12-28 |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080313173235/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/Colima/Mpios/06008a.htm |archivedate=2008-03-13 }}</ref>

The municipality is Mexico's largest single source of iron ore.
