'''Antonio José de Sucre''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 81,665.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Socopó]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Antonio José de Sucre Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]] (the others include only his surname, as "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]").

==Demographics==
The Antonio José de Sucre Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 74,968 (up from 63,947 in 2000).  This amounts to 9.9% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|25.2|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Antonio José de Sucre Municipality is Salvador Guerrero, elected on October 31, 2004, with 46% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=10&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|access-date=26 April 2023|title=Alcalde de Municipio|website=cne.gob.ve|date=May 2004|language=es}}</ref> He replaced Trino Hurtado shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Ticoporo, Andrés Bello, and Nicolás Pulido.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
