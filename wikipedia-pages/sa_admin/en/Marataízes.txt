'''Marataízes''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located by the [[Atlantic Ocean]] in southern [[Espírito Santo]], [[Brazil]]. It has a population of 38,883 and holds as such the highest density in the state. Marataízes is a [[tourist city]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|42.20|ha}} [[Falésias de Marataízes Natural Monument]], created in 2008 to protect the cliffs to the south of the town.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Monumento Natural Municipal Falésias de Marataízes – MERCOSUR}}|language=pt|title=Monumento Natural Municipal Falésias de Marataízes|publisher=MERCOSUR|url=http://mercosurambiental.net/p_11.a_1555/MONUMENTO_NATURAL_MUNICIPAL_FAL%C3%A9SIAS_DE_MARATA%C3%ADZES.html|accessdate=2016-10-24|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161024224345/http://mercosurambiental.net/p_11.a_1555/MONUMENTO_NATURAL_MUNICIPAL_FAL%C3%A9SIAS_DE_MARATA%C3%ADZES.html|archive-date=2016-10-24|url-status=dead}}</ref>
