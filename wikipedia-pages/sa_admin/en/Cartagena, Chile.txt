'''Cartagena''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[San Antonio Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|245.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==History==
In the seventeenth century the area surrounding the town became a major producer of wheat, which was shipped to Peru and Spain via the port of Cartagena.

From 1870, Cartagena became a popular residence for the Chilean intellectual elite who built holiday houses and made it a fashionable seaside resort. Cartagena acquired its status as a commune in 1901.

==Demographics==
[[File:Playa Grande de Cartagena (Chile).JPG|thumb|150px|left|Playa Grande of Cartagena.]]
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Cartagena had 16,875 inhabitants; of these, 15,302 (90.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 1,573 (9.3%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 8,396 men and 8,479 women. Cartagena accounts for 1.10% of the regional population.<ref name="INE"/> The demonym for a man from Cartagena is ''Cartagenino'' and ''Cartagenina'' for a woman.

==Administration==
As a commune, Cartagena is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Oscaldo Cartegena Polanco ([[Independent (politician)|Independent]]). The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Jesús Cartagena García ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* David Jiménez Mira ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Jaime Arriola Cortez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Jorge Castro Retamal (RN)
* Teresa Olivares Alvarez (PDC)
* Juan Cárdenas Palacios (Ind./Pro-[[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Cartagena is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by María José Hoffmann ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Víctor Torres ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 15th electoral district, (together with [[San Antonio, Chile|San Antonio]], [[Santo Domingo, Chile|Santo Domingo]], [[El Tabo]], [[El Quisco]], [[Algarrobo, Chile|Algarrobo]] and [[Casablanca, Chile|Casablanca]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Francisco Chahuán Chahuán (RN) and [[Ricardo Lagos Weber]] (PPD) as part of the 6th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Coast).
