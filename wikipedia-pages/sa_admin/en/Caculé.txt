'''Caculé''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Bahia]], which possesses 23,291 inhabitants {{as of|2020|lc=y}}, according to [[IBGE]].<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/cacule/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is situated 750 kilometers southwest from [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]]. Its economy is based mostly on [[agriculture]]. The climate in Caculé is warm and dry during the day, being cooler at night, at about 15&nbsp;°C. The "Rio Antônio" (river Antônio) crosses the city. Although it presently has no operational railroad station, Caculé is midpoint on the [[Ferrovia Centro Atlântica]] railroad route connecting [[Montes Claros]] to [[Salvador, Bahia]].
