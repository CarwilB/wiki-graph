==Overview==
The name comes from the Portuguese ''[[freguesia]]'' of {{Interlanguage link multi|Malta, Vila do Conde|pt|3=Malta (Vila do Conde)|lt=Malta}}, in [[Vila do Conde]].

It ranks 12th by [[Human Development Index|HDI]] among [[List of municipalities in Paraíba|Paraiban municipalities]]; 4th in greater Patos. The per capita income for the municipality is [[Brazilian real|R$]] 12,683.28 (2020).<ref name="IBGE_Infográficos"/> The city is the seat of the Santuário do Divino Pai Eterno, the major sanctuary in the state.<ref name="Santuário Pai Eterno">{{cite web |url=http://www.comunidadesmcj.org.br/santuario-pai-eterno |title=Santuário Pai Eterno |date=July 19, 2016 |publisher=Santuário Pai Eterno |accessdate=July 17, 2011|language=Portuguese}}</ref>

==History==
At the end of the 17th century, the Captain {{Interlanguage link multi|Teodósio de Oliveira Ledo|pt|3=Teodósio de Oliveira Ledo}} led a group of Pêgas Indians to settle the land and begin farming.

The founding of Malta is credited to João Fernandes de Freitas, who built a chapel in 1830 in honor of [[Immaculate Conception]] and developed the core that later would be the village and then the town of Malta. The fertile soil, attracted many livestock farmers, who organized farms both for creation and for the cultivation of cotton.<ref name="IBGE_Infográficos"/>

==Geography==
Malta is located on a [[depression (geology)|depression]] that is surrounded by the Serra do Pico (Portuguese for ''Pico ridge'') to the north and Serra Macapá (''Macapá ridge'') to the south, part of the vast region known as the Depressão sertaneja, with an average elevation of around 350 meters (1,148&nbsp;ft) above sea level – though at a distance of about 220 kilometers (136&nbsp;mi) from the Atlantic Ocean (at [[Macau, Rio Grande do Norte]]). Neighbouring municipalities are [[Vista Serrana]] (North), [[São José de Espinharas]] (Northeast), [[Patos, Paraíba|Patos]] (East), [[Santa Teresinha, Paraíba|Santa Teresinha]] and [[Catingueira]] (South) and [[Condado, Paraíba|Condado]] (West).<ref name="IBGE_Infográficos"/>

===Climate===
The town of Malta experiences a tropical wet and dry climate with a short rainy season (Koppen Aw) from February to April. During this period when the equatorial rainband associated with the highly seasonalized positioning of the [[Intertropical Convergence Zone]] is over the city, warm to hot temperatures and abundant equatorial rainfall prevail. Conversely, the dry season dominates the remaining majority of the year, with abundant sunshine prevalent from May to January. The wettest month is March with an average monthly total of 215.9&nbsp;mm (8.50&nbsp;in) of precipitation, while the driest month is August with an average total of average precipitation total of 2.3&nbsp;mm (0.09&nbsp;in).

Malta's population (''Maltenses'') is made up of two main ethnic groups: [[Europeans]] (mainly [[Portuguese people|Portuguese]]), and [[African people|Africans]].

Roman Catholic and Protestant are the dominant religions in Malta. Data from a 2010 census on religious affiliation indicates that approximately 97 percent of the population is Christian.<ref name="census2010" /> The composition of the maltense population is:
* Roman Catholicism, 89.76%
* Pentecostal, 5.26%
* Protestan, 1.50% and
* no religion, 2.94%.

==Economics==
The service sector is the largest component of Malta GDP at 67 percent, followed by the industrial sector at 21 percent. Agriculture represents 12 percent of GDP (2012).<ref name="PIB2012">[http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=250880&idtema=91&search=paraiba|malta|censo-demografico-2010:-resultados-da-amostra-religiao- IBGE – Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística Infográficos: Despesas e receitas orçamentárias e PIB]. ===Power plants===
There are two solar parks in development in the town. When finished, it will be the Paraíba's largest solar power park. It has allotted 60 MW of solar power capacity to 2 solar project developers, the ''Usina Fotovoltaica Malta'' and the ''Usina Fotovoltaica Angicos I''. This is expected to bring in investments of R$300&nbsp;million.<ref>Governo Federal.
