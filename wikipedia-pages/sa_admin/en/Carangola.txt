'''Carangola''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]]. It is a university town, a main urban hub, and a commercial and industrial region.
