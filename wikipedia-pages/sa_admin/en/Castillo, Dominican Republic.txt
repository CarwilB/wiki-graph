'''Castillo''' is a municipality of the [[Duarte Province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Official pdf government Division-Territorial-2015 |url=http://www.lmd.gob.do/transparencia/phocadownload/Publicaciones/Division-Territorial-2015.pdf |website=lmd.gob.do}}</ref>

It was named after General Manuel María Castillo, hero of the [[Dominican Restoration War|Dominican Restoration]].

The first inhabitants came to the area between 1718 and 1730 from foreign and neighboring communities, such as [[San Francisco de Macorís]], [[Salcedo, Dominican Republic|Salcedo]], [[Moca, Dominican Republic|Moca]], and [[Concepción de la Vega|La Vega]].
