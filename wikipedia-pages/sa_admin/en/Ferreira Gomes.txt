'''Ferreira Gomes''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|feˈʁejɾɐ ˈɡomis}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 7,967<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/ferreira-gomes/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|5,047|km2|mi2}}.<ref name="i"/> The town began as the military headquarters of [[Pedro II of Brazil|Pedro II]] and was founded by João Ferreira Gomes. In 1989, it became an independent municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/ferreira-gomes/historico |title=Ferreira Gomes|website=IBGE|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

== Nature ==
The municipality contains 3.64% of the {{convert|2369400|ha|adj=on}} [[Amapá State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Amapá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Amapá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/430|accessdate=2016-07-06}}</ref>
It also contains 44.07% of the {{convert|460353|ha|adj=on}} [[Amapá National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Amapá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA do Amapá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/398|accessdate=2016-07-06}}</ref>

== Economy ==
[[File:Ferreira Gomes - State of Amapá, Brazil - panoramio (1).jpg|thumb|left|Road in Ferreira Gomes]]
The economy is mainly based on agriculture and [[livestock]] in particular cattle and buffalo. The main agricultural products are [[cassava]], corn and bananas.<ref name="hist">{{cite web|url=http://www.ferreiragomes.ap.gov.br/conteudo/sobre-o-municipio/historico |title=Historico|website=Municipality of Ferreira Gomes|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> In the 1970s, pine plantations were established for the [[Pulp (paper)|pulp]] industry.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/ferreira-gomes |title=Ferreira Gomes|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> Ferreira Gomes is located on the [[BR-156]] highway.<ref name="hist"/>

== Hydroelectric Plant ==
{{anchor|UHCN}}
The Coaracy Nunes Hydroelectric Plant (''Usina Hidrelétrica Coaracy Nunes'') is a [[hydroelectric power plant]] located on the [[Araguari River (Amapá)|Araguari River]].<ref name="reg"/> It is near Vila do Paredão.<ref name="diario"/> Construction started in the 1960s, and was completed in 1978. The plant consists of a 24.9 km<sup>2</sup> dam. Originally it only supplied to cities of [[Macapá]] and [[Santana, Amapá|Santana]], but was later expanded for the whole region.<ref name="reg">{{cite web|url=https://files.comunidades.net/robsontavares/8_oferta_e_demanda_de_energia_no_amapa.pt.en.pdf |title=ENERGY SUPPLY AND DEMAND IN AMAPÁ|year=2017|publisher=REGMPE|pages=2-3}}</ref> In 2015, the output was 78 [[Megawatt|MW]].<ref name="diario">{{cite news|url=https://www.diariodoamapa.com.br/cadernos/cidades/usina-coaracy-nunes-completa-39-anos-de-funcionamento/ |title=Usina Coaracy Nunes completa 39 anos de funcionamento|website=Diário do Amapá|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
