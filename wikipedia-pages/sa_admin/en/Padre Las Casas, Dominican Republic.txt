'''Padre Las Casas''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Azua Province|Azua]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]].  It includes the municipal districts (''distritos municipal'') of La Siembra, Las Lagunas, and Los Fríos.<ref>{{cite web

Padre Las Casas is located in the foothills of the [[Cordillera Central, Hispaniola|Cordillera Central]]. The economy of the town is as a service depot for local farmers and farmers for quite a distance into the mountains to which it is the easiest point of contact. There is also a large service economy and an efficient bus cooperative that connects mainly with Azua and the towns along the way. Full-time employment for males runs about 50% but many males do some kind of work but much of this pays less than a dollar an hour, while entrepreneurs in retail and other small businesses may easily make the owner a 200–500 weekly income. The disparity is great even in the microcosm of this small town.
