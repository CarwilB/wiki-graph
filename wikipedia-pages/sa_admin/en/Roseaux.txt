'''Roseaux''' ({{IPA|fr|ʁozo}}; {{langx|ht|Wozo}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Corail Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 35,756 inhabitants in 2015.

Settlements in Roseaux: 
[[Annete]], [[Boise Sec]], [[Fond d'Acaque]], [[Gomier]], [[Grand Vincent]], [[La Basulle]], [[Nan Plingue]] and [[Roseaux City|Roseaux]]
