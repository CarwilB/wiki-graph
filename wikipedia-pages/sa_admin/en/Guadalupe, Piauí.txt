'''Guadalupe''' is a municipality of the Brazilian state of [[Piauí|Piaui]]. It is located in latitude 06° 47' 13" south and longitude 43° 34' 09" west, with an elevation of 177 meters.

The [[Boa Esperança Hydroelectric Power Plant]] is in Guadalupe, and provides electricity to the state of [[Piauí]].
