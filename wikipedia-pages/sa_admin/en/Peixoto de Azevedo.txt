'''Peixoto de Azevedo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]]. Peixoto de Azevedo was the ancient location of the [[Panará people|Panará indigenous people]]. They were expelled during the 19th century [[gold rush]] in the region. From the 20th century, the Panará people were relocated to the Kapoto-Jarinã [[Indigenous territory (Brazil)|indigenous territory]] created by the [[Villas-Bôas brothers]].

The nearby Alta Floresta Gold Province comprises 38 gold mines.<ref>[http://www.mindat.org/loc-46364.html Alta Floresta Gold Province]</ref>

On September 29, 2006, [[Gol Transportes Aéreos Flight 1907]] crashed approximately 200 kilometers (120&nbsp;mi) east of the city center.

The geographic center of Brazil is located within the municipality.
