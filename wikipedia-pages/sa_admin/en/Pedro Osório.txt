'''Pedro Osório ''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southeastern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 7,706 (2020 est.) in an area of 608.79&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1SR4 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The municipality was founded on April 3, 1958 from parts of the municipalities of [[Canguçú]] and [[Arroio Grande]]. [[Cerrito, Rio Grande do Sul|Cerrito]] was separated in 1997.<ref>[http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=430512&search=rio-grande-do-sul|cerrito|infograficos:-historico IBGE, History of Cerrito]</ref>

The municipality is by the [[Piratini River (São Gonçalo Channel)|Piratini River]].  Its population is largely of [[Italy|Italian]] and [[Lebanon|Lebanese]] descent.
