'''Ipirá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Bahia]], Brazil, with a population of 59,435.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/ipira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It was founded in 1855.
