'''Mâncio Lima''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈmɐ̃sju ˈlimɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Located deep within the [[Amazon rainforest]], it is the country's westernmost municipality and the state's northernmost. {{As of|2022}}, it had a population of 19,294.

Mâncio Lima grew out of a small village named Vila Japiim. The population ballooned with the arrival of settlers from northeast Brazil, who sought a better livelihood in the area amid the [[Amazon rubber cycle|Brazilian rubber booms]] of the 20th century. Vila Japiim became a municipality in 1976 and was renamed Mâncio Lima after one of the settlers who came from the northeast.

== Geography ==
Mâncio Lima is situated in the [[Juruá River|Juruá]] river valley within the [[Amazon rainforest]].<ref name="Muniz">{{cite news |last=Muniz |first=Tácita |title=Nascida às margens do Rio Moa, Mâncio Lima, cidade mais ocidental do país, celebra 47 anos com avanços e se tornando referência turística |url=https://agencia.ac.gov.br/especial-nascida-as-margens-do-rio-moa-mancio-lima-cidade-mais-ocidental-do-pais-celebra-47-anos-com-avancos-e-se-tornando-referencia-turistica/ |access-date=24 June 2025 |work=Agência de Noticias do Acre |publisher=[[Government of Brazil]] |date=30 May 2024 |language=pt-BR}}</ref> Covering an area of {{convert|4,672|km2}}, it contains 32% of the {{convert|846633|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Divisor National Park]], a protected area created in 1989.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Olson |first1=Sarah H. |last2=Gangnon |first2=Ronald |last3=Silveira |first3=Guilherme Abbad |last4=Patz |first4=Jonathan A. |title=Deforestation and malaria in Mâncio Lima County, Brazil |journal=Emerging Infectious Diseases |pages=1108–1115 |doi=10.3201/eid1607.091785 |date=July 2010 |volume=16 |issue=7 |pmid=20587182 |pmc=3321904 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=PARNA da Serra do Divisor |trans-title=Serra do Divisor National Park |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/pt-br/uc/6170 |access-date=23 June 2025 |publisher=Socioenvironmental Institute |language=pt}}</ref> It is the westernmost municipality of Brazil and the northernmost of Acre.<ref name="Querolaine">{{cite news |last=Querolaine |first=Aline |title=Governador Gladson Camelí prestigia 48º aniversário de Mâncio Lima e reforça compromisso com o desenvolvimento do município |url=https://agencia.ac.gov.br/governador-gladson-cameli-prestigia-48o-aniversario-de-mancio-lima-e-reforca-compromisso-com-o-desenvolvimento-do-municipio/ |access-date=24 June 2025 |work=Agência de Noticias do Acre |publisher=[[Government of Brazil]] |date=31 May 2025 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== History ==
Mâncio Lima emerged from a village named Vila Japiim. During the [[Amazon rubber cycle|rubber booms]] of the 20th century, a large number of migrants arrived from northeast Brazil, predominantly from the state of [[Ceará]], and settled as [[Rubber tapping|rubber tappers]] or farmers. Vila Japiim was consequently elevated to the status of a municipality on 30 May 1976 and renamed Mâncio Lima, after Mâncio Agostinho Rodrigues de Lima, a pioneer from Ceará.<ref name="Muniz"/>

== Demographics ==
The 2022 census recorded a population of 19,294 in Mâncio Lima.<ref name="IBGE">{{cite web |title=Mancio Lima |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ac/mancio-lima/panorama |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |access-date=23 June 2025 |language=pt}}</ref> The local population includes a number of Indigenous peoples such as the [[Poyanawa language|Poyanawa]], as well as the descendants of rubber tappers who immigrated from the northeast during the rubber booms.<ref name="Muniz"/>

== Economy ==
The economy of Mâncio Lima is based primarily on agriculture, with [[cassava]] and [[Coffee bean|coffee]] being the main crops. In recent years, the municipality has seen growth in tourism, especially [[ecotourism]], due to its location within Serra do Divisor National Park.<ref name="Muniz"/><ref>{{cite news |last1=Azevedo |first1=Pablo |title=Mâncio Lima celebra 47 anos com mais de R$ 35 milhões em investimentos, frutos da parceria entre governo do Estado e prefeitura |url=https://agencia.ac.gov.br/mancio-lima-celebra-47-anos-com-mais-de-r-35-milhoes-em-investimentos-frutos-da-parceria-entre-governo-do-estado-e-prefeitura/ |access-date=24 June 2025 |work=Agência de Noticias do Acre |publisher=[[Government of Brazil]] |date=29 May 2024 |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
