'''Concordia''' ({{IPA|es|koŋˈkoɾðja}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the south west of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]], north of [[Campamento, Honduras|Campamento]], west of [[Juticalpa, Olancho|Juticalpa]] and south of [[Guayape]] and [[Salamá, Olancho|Salamá]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Concordia municipality had a population of 8,188. Of these, 96.93% were [[Mestizo]], 2.28% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.64% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.15% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
