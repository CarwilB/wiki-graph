'''Lajinha''' is a municipality in the northeastern part of the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the population was estimated to be 19,918. It has an area of {{Convert|431.92|km2}}.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/131C Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The town borders the state [[Espírito Santo]] to the east.
