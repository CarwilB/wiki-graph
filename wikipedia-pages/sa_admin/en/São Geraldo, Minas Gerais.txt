'''São Geraldo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 12,562.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sao-geraldo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It was founded on 7 March 1949.
