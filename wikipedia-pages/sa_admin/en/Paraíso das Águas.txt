'''Paraíso das Águas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 5,654 (2020) and its area is {{Convert|5032|km2|0|abbr=on}}. The municipality was established in January 2013.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/com-5-novos-municipios-brasil-agora-tem-5570-cidades-7235803|title=Com 5 novos municípios, Brasil agora tem 5.570 cidades|language=pt-br}}</ref>
