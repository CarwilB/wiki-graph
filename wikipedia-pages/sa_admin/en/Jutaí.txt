'''Jutaí''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population is 13,886 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/jutai/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 69,552&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=June 11, 2008 }}</ref> making it the fifth largest municipality in Amazonas by area and the ninth largest in Brazil.

==Geography==

The municipality is in the [[Juruá-Purus moist forests]] ecoregion.<ref>{{citation |title=South America: Amazon Basin, northwestern Brazil (NT0133)
 |last=Sears|first=Robin |publisher=WWF: World Wildlife Fund
 |url=http://www.worldwildlife.org/ecoregions/nt0133|accessdate=2017-03-28}}</ref>
It contains parts of the [[Jutaí-Solimões Ecological Station]].{{sfn|Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Jutaí-Solimões}}
It contains the {{convert|275533|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Jutaí Extractive Reserve]], created in 2002.{{sfn|RESEX do Rio Jutaí – ISA |loc=Informações gerais}}
The municipality contains the {{convert|2450380|ha|adj=on}} [[Cujubim Sustainable Development Reserve]], established in 2003.{{sfn|RDS Cujubim – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
This is the largest conservation unit in Amazonas and the largest sustainable development reserve in the world.{{sfn|RDS Cujubim – ISA|loc=Características}}
