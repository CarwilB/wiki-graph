'''Epitacio Huerta''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|105|km}} northeast of the state capital of [[Morelia]]. It is named after General {{ill|Epitacio Huerta|es}},<ref name=encyclopedia/> who fought in the [[Mexican–American War]] and under [[Juan Álvarez]] in the [[Revolution of Ayutla]], and served as [[governor of Michoacán]] from 1858 to 1862.

==Geography==
The municipality of Epitacio Huerta is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northeast Michoacán at an altitude between {{convert|2300|and(-)|3100|m}}. It borders the [[Querétaro|Queretan]] municipalities of [[Huimilpan]] to the north and [[Amealco de Bonfil]] to the northeast, the Michoacanese municipalities of [[Contepec]] to the southeast and [[Maravatío]] to the southwest, and the [[Guanajuato|Guanajuatean]] municipalities of [[Jerécuaro]] to the southwest and [[Coroneo]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16031.pdf |title=Epitacio Huerta, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=25 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|424.839|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.72% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, farmland covered 59% of Epitacio Huerta's land area, while grasslands covered 22% and forests cover 14%.<ref name=compendium/> 
===Hydrography===
The reservoir of the Tepuxtepec Dam on the [[Lerma River]] forms part of Epitacio Huerta's southern border with Contepec. Built from 1926 to 1950, the dam and its reservoir are used for hydroelectricity generation, flood control and irrigation.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://amotepux.blogspot.com/2013/02/presa-tepuxtepec.html |title=Presa Tepuxtepec |language=es |date=19 February 2013 |access-date=3 May 2018}}</ref>
===Climate===
Epitacio Huerta's climate is temperate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|16|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
At the time of Spanish contact, the area of what is now Epitacio Huerta was a buffer zone between the [[Purépecha Empire]] to the west and the [[Aztec Empire]] to the east, and was inhabited by the [[Guamare]] and [[Pame people|Pame]] peoples. By 1632 the ranch of San José Buena Vista was established in what was then the ''[[hacienda]]'' of Coroneo. In 1822, San José Buena Vista was itself recorded as a ''hacienda'' attached to [[Tlalpujahua]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

In 1962, San José Buena Vista was renamed in honour of General Epitacio Huerta. Effective 31 March 1962, it and the surrounding localities were separated from Contepec to form the municipality of Epitacio Huerta, in accordance with Decree No. 83 of 1 March 1962.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=71, 81, 132 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4 |archive-date=2019-04-12 |access-date=2020-12-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190412021950/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is [[Margarito Fierros]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=25 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Epitacio Huerta recorded a population of 16,218 inhabitants living in 3992 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=017 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Epitacio Huerta |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=25 December 2020 |archive-date=4 May 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180504091206/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=017 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 16,622 inhabitants in Epitacio Huerta.<ref name=survey/>

There are 82 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Epitacio Huerta, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 1222 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Agriculture is the main economic activity in Epitacio Huerta.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Sand and gravel are mined at Palos Altos, located {{convert|6|km}} north of the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite report |url=https://mapserver.sgm.gob.mx/InformesTecnicos/InventariosMinerosWeb/T1607BUGJ0004_01.PDF |title=Inventario físico de recursos minerales del municipio Epitacio Huerta, Mich. |language=es |pages=22, 24 |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=August 2007 |access-date=25 December 2020}}</ref>
