'''Sagrada Familia''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] meaning "Holy Family") is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Curicó Province]], [[Maule Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Sagrada Familia spans an area of {{convert|548.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 17,519 inhabitants (9,108 men and 8,411 women). Of these, 5,080 (29%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 12,439 (71%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 3.7% (625 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Sagrada Familia is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Francisco Meléndez Rojas ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Sagrada Familia is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Roberto León ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Celso Morales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 36th electoral district, together with [[Curicó]], [[Teno, Chile|Teno]], [[Romeral]], [[Molina, Chile|Molina]], [[Hualañé]], [[Licantén]], [[Vichuquén]] and [[Rauco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
