'''Telsen Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 1,788 inhabitants in an area of 19,893&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Telsen]], which is located around 1,494&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]]. It has an elevation of 611 meters.<ref>{{Cite web |title=GeoNames.org |url=https://www.geonames.org/3834535/departamento-de-telsen.html |access-date=2026-02-26 |website=www.geonames.org}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
In 2010 the population was 1,644. The population of the department, as of 2022, is 1,681 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Telsen (Department, Chubut, Argentina) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/argentina/admin/chubut/26105__telsen/ |access-date=2023-10-29 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> The gender demographics are 875 males to 748 females. It currently has a population density of 0.08 per square kilometer.
