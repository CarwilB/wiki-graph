'''Bambamarca District''' is one of three [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Hualgayoc Province|Hualgayoc]] in [[Peru]],<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> with 167 pueblos.<ref name="pp">{{cite web | url=http://www.pueblosdelperu.org/cajamarca/hualgayoc/bambamarca | title=Pueblos de Bambamarca | publisher=Pueblos de Perú | date=n.d. | access-date=5 May 2016}}</ref> The area is rich in mineral reserves and mining prospects, including the [[Conga Project]], which is threatening to affect water resources such as the Namocoha lake.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://peru.com/actualidad/nacionales/cajamarca-conmemoran-ano-resistencia-contra-proyecto-conga-noticia-105885 | title=Cajamarca: Conmemoran un año de resistencia contra proyecto Conga | publisher=Peru.com | date=24 November 2012 | language =es|access-date=5 May 2016}}</ref>
