'''Santa Cruz Xitla''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Miahuatlán District]] in the south of the [[Sierra Sur de Oaxaca|Sierra Sur Region]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 3,933.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20384a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>

Municipal president Pedro Modesto Santos died of [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico|COVID-19]] in September [[2020 in Mexico|2020]]. He was the eighth municipal president in Oaxaca to die of the virus.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Rodríguez |first1=Óscar |title=Muere por coronavirus alcalde de Santa Cruz Xitla, Oaxaca |url=https://www.msn.com/es-mx/noticias/mexico/muere-por-coronavirus-alcalde-de-santa-cruz-xitla-oaxaca/ar-BB19midU?ocid=msedgdhp |accessdate=Sep 24, 2020 |work=www.msn.com |publisher=Milenio |date=September 23, 2020}}</ref>
