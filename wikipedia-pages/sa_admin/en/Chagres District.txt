''' Chagres District'''  ({{IPA|es|ˈtʃaɣɾes}}) is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Colón Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 9,191;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 11, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 11,342.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of 445&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the town of [[Nuevo Chagres]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
Chagres District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[Nuevo Chagres]] (capital)
*[[Achiote, Colón|Achiote]]
*[[El Guabo, Colón|El Guabo]]
*[[La Encantada, Colón|La Encantada]]
*[[Palmas Bellas]]
*[[Piña, Colón|Piña]]
*[[Salud, Colón|Salud]]
