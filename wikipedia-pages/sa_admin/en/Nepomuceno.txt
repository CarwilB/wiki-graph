'''Nepomuceno''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The population in 2020 was 26,826 in a total area of {{cvt|584|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/nepomuceno/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The maximum elevation is {{cvt|1,100|m}} and the minimum is {{cvt|850|m}}.

The city belongs to the IBGE statistical microregion of [[Varginha]]. It lies {{cvt|12|km}} west of the important highway BR-381 (Fernão Dias). Distances to major and nearby cities are:
*[[Belo Horizonte]] – 233&nbsp;km
*[[São Paulo]] – 330&nbsp;km
*[[Rio de Janeiro]] – 396&nbsp;km
*[[Brasília]] – 1085&nbsp;km
*[[Três Pontas]] – 54&nbsp;km
*[[Três Corações]] – 80&nbsp;km
*[[Varginha]] – 81&nbsp;km
*[[Lavras]] – 25&nbsp;km
The climate is humid, with mild summers, subject to sporadic periods of drought and with mild winters. The annual rainfall is 1,600&nbsp;mm.

== Temperature ==
Annual average: 21.2 C
Annual average maximum: 27.8 C
Annual average minimum: 13.5 C

The main source of income is the cultivation of coffee, which represents 70% of the economy. There is also production of corn, rice, and beans.
Another important activity is poultry raising, producing around one million eggs a day. In 2006 there were 1,080 rural producers with an area of 34,213 hectares of agricultural land. Cultivated land made up 16,000 hectares. There were 4,800 workers in agriculture.

In the health sector there were 12 health clinics and 1 private hospital, with 47 beds (2005). In the school system there were 31 primary schools, of which 3 were private, and 2 middle schools, of which 1 was private. In 2005 there were 114 attending an extension of a private university.
