'''Diego de Almagro''' ({{IPA|es|ˈdjeɣo ðe alˈmaɣɾo}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chañaral Province]], [[Atacama Region]]. The commune has an area of {{convert|18663.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> The area is named after [[Diego de Almagro]], a [[conquistador]]. Until 1977, the city was named ''Pueblo Hundido'' ("Sunk Town").<ref>{{Cite web |date=2013-10-24 |title=Su Historia ~ Municipalidad Diego de Almagro |url=http://www.diegodalmagro.cl/p/su-historia.html |access-date=2022-12-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131024105541/http://www.diegodalmagro.cl/p/su-historia.html |archive-date=2013-10-24 }}</ref>

Various mines and mineral prospects lie in the commune including the copper mines of [[Esperanza mine|Esperanza]]<ref name=later>{{Cite news |title=Enami recibe más de 50 interesados en licitación para vender o arrendar mina Esperanza en Atacama |last=Guillou |first=Víctor |date=2024-06-13 |url=https://www.latercera.com/pulso-pm/noticia/enami-recibe-mas-de-50-interesados-en-licitacion-para-vender-o-arrendar-mina-esperanza-en-atacama/66WHHHHRHJEFRAL7XV2ULPEWFU/ |access-date=2025-08-27 |work=[[La Tercera]] |language=es}}</ref> and [[El Salvador mine|El Salvador]] and the [[Polymetallic ore|polymetallic]] [[Santo Domingo mine|Santo Domingo project]].<ref name="NSen">{{Cite news |date=September 26, 2024 |title=Santo Domingo Project, Chile |url=https://www.nsenergybusiness.com/projects/santo-domingo-project-chile/?cf-view |access-date=July 21, 2025 |work=NS Energy}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Diego de Almagro had 18,589 inhabitants (10,031 men and 8,558 women). Of these, 17,674 (95.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 915 (4.9%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 32.4-32.4% (8926 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Diego de Almagro is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (mayor) who is directly elected every four years.
