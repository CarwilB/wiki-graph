'''San Isidro''', also referred to as '''San Isidro de Heredia''',<ref>{{cite web |title=Inicio |url=https://www.munisanisidro.go.cr |website=www.munisanisidro.go.cr |publisher=Municipalidad de San Isidro de Heredia |access-date=27 April 2024 |language=Spanish}}</ref> is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Heredia Province|Heredia]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |lang=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[San Isidro District, San Isidro|San Isidro]] district.

== History ==
San Isidro was created on 13 July 1905 by decree 40.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
San Isidro has an area of {{cvt|26.96|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:1337}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The canton is in the highlands northeast of the provincial capital city of [[Heredia, Costa Rica|Heredia]]. The Pará Blanca River forms its far northeastern border.
