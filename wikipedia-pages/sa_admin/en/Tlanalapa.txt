'''Tlanalapa''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 156.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 8,062.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tlanalapa|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/hidalgo/tlanalapa/130720001__tlanalapa/|work=CityPopulation.de|publisher=Source: Instituto Nacional de Estadística Geografía e Informática, México (web).|accessdate=February 23, 2022}}</ref> 

==History==
This was the center of the '''Tlanalapan''' state prior to the Spanish incursion.  It was a dependency of [[Otompa]].  By 1521 the area was taken over by the Spanish.  During the colonial period it was part of [[Apa y Tepeapulco]] except for a short time around 1545 when it was the center of a separate jurisdiction.<ref>Peter Gerhard, ''Guide to the Historical Geography of New Spain'' (Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, 1972) p. 53</ref>
