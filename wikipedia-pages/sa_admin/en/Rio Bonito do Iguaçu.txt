'''Rio Bonito do Iguaçu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in the [[Southern Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf | title = Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009| publisher = Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) | date = August 14, 2009 | access-date=December 17, 2009 | format = [[PDF]]|language=pt }}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |year=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20%28pelos%20dados%20de%202000%29.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref>

== History ==
''Rio Bonito'' means ''nice river'' and ''Iguaçu'' in [[Tupi language|Tupi]] means ''big water'': ''y'' (water) and ''gûasu'' (''big'').<ref>[http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm ''The name of Rio Bonito''] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120809053512/http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm |date=2012-08-09 }}</ref>

=== 2025 tornado ===
{{Main|2025 Rio Bonito do Iguaçu tornado}}

On [[2025 Southern Brazil tornado outbreak|November 7, 2025]], a [[tornado]] hit Rio Bonito do Iguaçu, leaving a trail of destruction, six confirmed deaths, and dozens injured. The Paraná Environmental Technology and Monitoring System preliminarily classified the tornado as an [[Fujita scale|F3]], with winds exceeding 250 km/h.<ref name="G1">{{cite web |url=https://g1.globo.com/pr/campos-gerais-sul/noticia/2025/11/08/tornado-que-atingiu-parana-e-classificado-como-ef3-simepar.ghtml |title=Tornado that hit Paraná is classified as EF3, says Simepar |date=November 8, 2025 |website=G1 |accessdate=November 8, 2025}}</ref> However, experts from [[PREVOTS]], Simepar, and MetSul Meteorologia, rated the tornado as an F4 a few days later.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Robles |first=Carlos |date=2025-11-10 |title=Tornado kills 6 and injures hundreds in southern Brazil, rated preliminary F4 |url=https://bnonews.com/index.php/2025/11/tornado-kills-6-and-injures-hundreds-in-southern-brazil-rated-preliminary-f4/ |access-date=2025-11-11 |website=BNO News |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Blašković |first=Teo |date=2025-11-10 |title=Rio Bonito do Iguaçu tornado upgraded to EF-4, placing Brazil among sites of 2025’s most violent tornadoes worldwide |url=https://watchers.news/2025/11/10/rio-bonito-do-iguacu-tornado-upgraded-to-ef-4-placing-brazil-among-sites-of-2025s-most-violent-tornadoes-worldwide/ |access-date=2025-11-11 |website=The Watchers |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last1=Deslandes |first1=Fernanda |title=Simepar final technical report upgrades the classification of tornadoes that struck 11 municipalities in Paraná |url=https://www.preventionweb.net/news/simepar-final-technical-report-upgrades-classification-tornadoes-struck-11-municipalities |website=PreventionWeb |access-date=2 March 2026 |language=EN |date=26 November 2025}}</ref>
