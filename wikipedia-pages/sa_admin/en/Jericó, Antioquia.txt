'''Jericó''' is a town, [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and Catholic bishopric in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]]. It is part of the subregion of [[Southwestern Antioquia]]. The distance reference from [[Medellín]] city, the capital of the department, is 104&nbsp;km (64.6 miles).<ref>Medellín Travel. [http://medellin.travel/MedellinTravelWeb/locations/119/jerico-antioquia?lang=en "Medellín Travel: Jericó"]{{Dead link|date=January 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}. Retrieved on July 11, 2018.</ref> It lies 1,910 m (6,266 ft) above sea level. 

== History ==
The town was founded on September 28, 1850, by pioneer Santiago Santamaría. It was established as [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] on 1851. During a territorial rearrangement on the country on 1908, Jericó became the capital of the homonymous department until 1911.<ref>Marca Colombia. [http://www.colombia.co/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Jerico.pdf "Jericó: Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia"]</ref>

In 2013, Jericó became the third municipality in Antioquia to be named a ''[[Pueblo Patrimonio (Colombia)|Pueblo Patrimonio]]'' (heritage town) of Colombia.<ref>{{Cite web|date=Dec 2020|title=GUÍA: Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia|url=https://fontur.com.co/sites/default/files/2020-12/GUIA_PUEBLOS_PATRIMONIO.pdf|access-date=29 Mar 2021|website=Fondo Nacional de Turismo de la República de Colombia - FONTUR}}</ref>

== Religion ==
[[File:Catedral de Jericó-antioquia.JPG|left|thumb|Jericó Cathedral]]
Its Catedral de Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes, dedicated to [[Virgin of Mercy|Our Lady of Mercy]], is the episcopal [[cathedral]] see of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Jericó]] (founded 1915, when the first cathedral was built). Due to some damages, the first church had to be replaced by the present cathedral, since 1949.<ref name=jerico>Alcaldía de Jericó. [http://www.jerico-antioquia.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml "Nuestro Municipio: Información General"] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151203205236/http://www.jerico-antioquia.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-12-03 }}</ref> The town is the birthplace of [[Laura of Saint Catherine of Siena]], canonized as saint by [[Pope Francis]] on 2013.<ref>Wyss, Jim. [https://www.miamiherald.com/latest-news/article1951475.html "Madre Laura named Colombia’s first saint"], ''[[The Miami Herald]]'', Miami, May 12, 2013. Retrieved on July 10, 2018.</ref>
