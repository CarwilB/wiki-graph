'''Torreón''' is one of the [[municipalities of Coahuila|municipalities of Coahuila de Zaragoza]], a state in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The city of [[Torreón]] is the municipal seat.<ref>"Municipal seat" is the translation for the Spanish ''cabecera.''</ref> The [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] covers an area of 1947.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 720,848.

[[File:Torreon climas.svg|thumb|The municipality of Torreón is divided into two sections, with a southern exclave separated from the main urban area by the municipalities of [[Lerdo Municipality|Lerdo]], [[Matamoros Municipality, Coahuila|Matamoros]] and [[Viesca Municipality|Viesca]].]]
