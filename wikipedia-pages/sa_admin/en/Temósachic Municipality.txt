''' Temósachic''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Temósachic]]. The municipality covers an area of 5,361.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 6,211,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Temósachic|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=063|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> down from 6,319 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=  Temósachic |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08063a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008}}</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Temósachic had a population of 1,841.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Temósachic, the municipality had 329 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
