'''  San Francisco Telixtlahuaca ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 79.1 km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Etla District, Oaxaca|Etla District]] in the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales region]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 10,278.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Francisco Telixtlahuaca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20150a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>
