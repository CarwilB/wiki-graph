'''El Alto''' is an eastern  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 3,400 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|2,327|km2}}, and its capital city is [[El Alto (Catamarca)|El Alto]].
