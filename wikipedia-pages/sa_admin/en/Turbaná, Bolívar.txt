'''Turbaná''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[Bolívar department]] in [[Colombia]]. Most of the people living here depend on agriculture and cattle raising for their main income. There is no sewer system or dump and waste is disposed of in streams and roadways. The temperature averages from {{convert|27|to|30|C|F}}. Turbaná borders [[Turbaco]] and [[Cartagena, Colombia|Cartagena]] in the north, [[Arjona, Bolívar|Arjona]] in the south and Arjona and Turbaco in the east.
