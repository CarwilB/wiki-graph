'''Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]], located approximately {{convert|138|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez]]. Its inhabitants are of Guatemalan origin and speak the [[Mam language]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.capitanluisvidal.gob.mx/mmunicipio/mhistoria |title=Historia |language=es |publisher=Municipality of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal |accessdate=25 October 2020 |archive-date=28 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201028205616/http://www.capitanluisvidal.gob.mx/mmunicipio/mhistoria |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal is located in the [[Sierra Madre de Chiapas]] in the southern part of the state. It borders the municipalities of [[Ángel Albino Corzo]] to the north, [[Honduras de la Sierra]] to the east, [[Escuintla, Chiapas|Escuintla]] to the southeast, [[Acacoyagua]] to the south, [[Mapastepec]] to the west, and [[Montecristo de Guerrero]] to the northwest.<ref name=plan>{{cite report |author=Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal Municipal Council |url=https://files.transparenciachiapas.org.mx/capitanluisangelvidal/2020/78/1/1a/PLAN_DE_DESARROLLO_CAPITAN_LUIS_A._VIDAL.pdf |title=Plan municipal de desarrollo visíon 2030 del municipio de Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal, Chiapas |language=es |date=14 June 2019 |accessdate=25 October 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|225.24|km2}}.<ref name=decree/>

The terrain of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal is rugged, ranging from {{convert|900|to|2300|m}} in elevation, and is primarily covered by [[Central American pine-oak forests|pine-oak forests]].<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM07chiapas/municipios/07080a.html |title=Siltepec |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Chiapas |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=24 October 2020 |archive-date=4 July 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704101031/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM07chiapas/municipios/07080a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> The western part of the municipality lies in [[El Triunfo Biosphere Reserve]], while the eastern part lies in the Pico El Loro-Paxtal Ecological Conservation Area.<ref name=decree/>

The climate is generally temperate. The average annual rainfall is {{convert|1536|mm}}, with most of it falling between May and October.<ref name=plan/>

==History==
The community of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal was founded in 1954 and was originally part of the municipality of Siltepec. On 18 August 2017, the [[Congress of Chiapas]] approved the separation of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal and its surrounding communities from Siltepec to create a new municipality.<ref name=creation>{{cite news |last=Burguete Cal y Mayor |first=Araceli |date=22 August 2017 |url=https://www.chiapasparalelo.com/opinion/2017/08/nuevos-municipios-en-chiapas-un-expediente-abierto/ |title=Nuevos municipios en Chiapas: un expediente abierto |language=es |newspaper=Chiapas Paralelo |accessdate=25 October 2020}}</ref> The decree establishing Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal and [[Rincón Chamula San Pedro]] as new municipalities was gazetted on 6 September 2017.<ref name=decree/>

==Administration==
In 2018, Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal elected its first municipal president, Emiselda González Roblero.<ref>{{cite news |date=13 December 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-chiapas-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Chiapas 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |accessdate=25 October 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal recorded a total population of 3653 inhabitants.<ref name=ageeml/>

There are 49 localities in the municipality, of which only the municipal seat, also named Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal, is designated as urban. It recorded a population of 563 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=ageeml/>

==Economy==
Coffee production is the predominant economic activity in Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal. Dairy cattle are also raised.<ref name=plan/>
