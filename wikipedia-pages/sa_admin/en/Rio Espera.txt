'''Rio Espera''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].

As of 2025, the town has 5,447 inhabitants and is 238&nbsp;km to the southeast of [[Belo Horizonte]].<ref name="IBGE" /> The town was founded in 1710 when a group of [[Portugal|Portuguese]] explorers, coming from [[São Paulo]], stopped beside a brook to wait for their companions. The site was originally known as Arraial de Espera.
