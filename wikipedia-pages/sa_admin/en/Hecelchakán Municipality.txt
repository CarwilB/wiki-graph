'''Hecelchakán''' ({{IPA|es|xeseltʃaˈkan}}) is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Hecelchakán]].

The municipality had a 2005 census population of 28,306, with 10,285 in the municipal seat. The municipality covers an area of 1,331.99&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (514.284 sq mi) and includes numerous smaller outlying communities, the largest of which is the town of [[Pomuch]].

The name comes from the [[Yucatec Maya]] words ''jeʼelel'' (rest) and ''chakʼan'' (savanna).

==Geography==
Hecelchakán borders to the north with [[Calkiní Municipality]], to the south with [[Tenabo Municipality]], to the east with [[Yucatán (state)|Yucatán State]] and [[Hopelchén Municipality]], and to the west with the [[Gulf of Mexico]], with 24&nbsp;km of shoreline.

It is at an average altitude of 10 m above sea level.

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 28,306.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Hecelchakán|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=005|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=21 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151021171126/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=005|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Hecelchakán had a population of 10,285.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Hecelchakán, the municipality had 80 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Pomuch, Campeche|Pomuch]] (8,694), classified as urban, and [[Pocboc, Campeche|Pocboc]] (1,624), [[Cumpich, Campeche|Cumpich]] (1,587), [[Campo Menonita Yalnón, Campeche|Campo Menonita Yalnón]] (1,151), and [[Santa Cruz, Campeche|Santa Cruz]] (1,118), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

==History==
On 7 December 1915, when decree no. 51 approved a new law of interior administration, Hecelchakán became one of the eight free municipalities of the new state of Campeche.  That law came into effect on 1 January 1916.
