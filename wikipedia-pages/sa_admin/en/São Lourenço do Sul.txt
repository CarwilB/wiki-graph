'''São Lourenço do Sul''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]].  The municipality had 43,111 inhabitants in the last Census (2010). Its population in 2020 was estimated in 43,540 inhabitants. It is located at the west bank of the big lagoon [[Lagoa dos Patos]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|7993|ha|adj=on}} [[Camaquã State Park]], created in 1975.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual do Camaquã – SEMA}}
