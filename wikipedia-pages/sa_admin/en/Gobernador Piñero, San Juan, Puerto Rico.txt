'''Gobernador Piñero''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico]]. With a population of 36,995 ([[2020 United States census|2020]]) living in a land area of {{convert|4.44|sqmi|km2}}, it is [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]]’s second most populated [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrio]] after [[Santurce, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Santurce]], and the fourth largest in land area.

This district is named after [[Jesús T. Piñero]] who was the first and only native Puerto Rican [[governor of Puerto Rico|governor of the territory]] under the American colonial administration in 1946.

== Geography ==
Gobernador Piñero is bounded to the north by the [[San Juan Bay]] and Port and [[Hato Rey Norte, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Norte]] barrio, with the barrios of [[Hato Rey Sur, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Sur]] and [[El Cinco, San Juan, Puerto Rico|El Cinco]] to the east, by [[Monacillo Urbano, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo Urbano]] to the south, and by the [[Guaynabo, Puerto Rico|municipality of Guaynabo]] to the west.<ref name="2010 Census">{{cite book|url=https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|title=Puerto Rico: 2010 Population and Housing Unit Counts.pdf|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau|year=2010}}</ref>

Gobernador Piñero includes the areas of [[Puerto Nuevo (Hato Rey)|Puerto Nuevo]] (including Puerto Nuevo Norte), Villa Borinquen, [[San Patricio State Forest|Bosque Urbano de San Patricio]], Caparra Terrace and Centro Médico.

== Landmarks and places of interest ==
* [[Luis Muñoz Marín Park]], one of the main city parks of San Juan.
* [[Port of San Juan]], partially located in the barrio at [[Puerto Nuevo (Hato Rey)|Puerto Nuevo]].
* San Juan Medical Center or ''[[Centro Médico de Rio Piedras]]''.
* [[San Patricio State Forest]], also known as the San Patricio Urban Forest, one of the [[List of Puerto Rico state forests|20 state forests of Puerto Rico]].
* [[University of Puerto Rico, Medical Sciences Campus]].

== Transportation ==
The area is served by the [[Martínez Nadal station|Martínez Nadal]] (bordering with the [[Guaynabo, Puerto Rico|municipality of Guaynabo]]), [[Las Lomas station|Las Lomas]], [[San Francisco station (Puerto Rico)|San Francisco]] and [[Centro Médico station (Puerto Rico)|Centro Médico]] metro stations.
