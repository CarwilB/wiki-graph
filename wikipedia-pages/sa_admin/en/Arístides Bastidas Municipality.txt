'''Arístides Bastidas''' is one of the 14 municipalities of the state of [[Yaracuy]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality is located in northern Yaracuy, occupying an area of 74 km² with a population of 21,546 inhabitants as of the 2011 census. The [[municipal seat]] is in [[San Pablo, Yaracuy|San Pablo]]. The municipality was established in 1993 in honour of Venezuelan journalist and scientist [[Arístides Bastidas]], born locally in San Pablo.

==Name==
Arístides Bastidas Municipality was established in 1993 in honour of Venezuelan journalist and scientist [[Arístides Bastidas]] who was born locally in [[San Pablo, Yaracuy|San Pablo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://albaciudad.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/Yaracuy-AristidesBastidas_Sucre.pdf|title=Municipios Arístides Bastidas y Sucre|trans-title=Arístides Bastidas and Sucre Municipalities

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Arístides Bastidas Municipality was 21,546 people, accounting for 3.36% of the total population of the state of [[Yaracuy]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/22__yaracuy/|title=Yaracuy (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> An overwhelming majority of the population (95%) resides in [[San Pablo, Yaracuy|San Pablo]], the [[municipal seat]] of the municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/yaracuy/2201__ar%C3%ADstides_bastidas/|title=Urban Localities in Arístides Bastidas (Yaracuy, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Arístides Bastidas as 25,103 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 1.9% since 2011 and showing a population density of 339.2 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/yaracuy/2201__ar%C3%ADstides_bastidas/|title=Arístides Bastidas (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population showed an even balance with 10,303 men (50.3%) and 10,182 women (49.7%). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 64.1% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 30.4% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for the remaining 5.5%. The municipality is very highly urbanised, with 95% of the inhabitants (19,459) living in [[urban centers]] compared to only 5% (1,026) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[Mestizo]] (61.7%) and [[White people]] (32.9%). Minority groups included 5% [[Afro-Venezuelans]] and 0.3% belonging to other ethnic groups, with 9 individuals identifying as [[indigenous peoples|indigenous]]. The [[literacy rate]] was 93%, with 1,151 inhabitants of Arístides Bastidas not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>
