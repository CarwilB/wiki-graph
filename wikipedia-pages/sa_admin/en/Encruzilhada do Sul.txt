'''Encruzilhada do Sul''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
=== Climate ===
The climate is [[Humid subtropical climate|humid subtropical]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfa''), with hot summers and mild winters, with rare occasions at 0&nbsp;°C or below, typical of areas at low altitudes in the subtropics. It is similar to other cities of [[Rio Grande do Sul]] and [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] on the plains or with cities in [[Eastern states of Australia|eastern Australia]]. The climate is sunny and humid all year round.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather-summary.php3?s=598666&cityname=Encruzilhada+do+Sul,+Rio+Grande+do+Sul,+Brazil&units=metric|title=Encruzilhada do Sul, Rio Grande do Sul Köppen Climate Classification (Weatherbase)|website=Weatherbase|access-date=2018-12-24}}</ref>
