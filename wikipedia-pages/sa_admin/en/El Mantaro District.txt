'''El Mantaro District''' is one of thirty-four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Jauja Province]], located in the Department of Junín in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the Law No. 4404 in November 23, 1921, during the presidency of [[Augusto B. Leguía]].  It encompasses an area of 17.76&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
