== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] descent. [[Aymara language|Aymara]] is the language which the majority of the population (67.84%) learnt to speak in childhood, 31.34% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language and  0.56 	% using [[Quechua language|Quechua]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
