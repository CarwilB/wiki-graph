'''Mato Verde''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. It was founded in 1953. The population was 12,412 as of 2020 and the area was {{cvt|664|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/mato-verde/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation of the city is 541 metres.  The postal code (CEP) is 39527-000.

==Geography==
Mato Verde is part of the statistical microregion of [[Janaúba]].  It is surrounded by the following municipalities: [[Monte Azul]], [[Porteirinha]], [[Rio Pardo de Minas]], [[Catuti]] and [[Pai Pedro]].
It is connected by paved road (BR-122) to the regional center of Janaúba to the south.  The distance is 80&nbsp;km.   To the north lies the municipality of [[Monte Azul]], a distance of 32&nbsp;km.  The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 504&nbsp;km.  The distance to [[Montes Claros]] is {{convert|226|km}}.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|49,830|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra Nova State Park]], created in 2003.<ref>{{citation|title=Parque Estadual de Serra Nova: natureza mágica|date=5 November 2009|last=Diniz|first=Ana Elizabeth|language=pt|journal=Revista Ecológico|url=http://www.revistaecologico.com.br/materia.php?id=13&secao=156&mat=173|access-date=2017-01-30|archive-date=2017-02-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170202055952/http://www.revistaecologico.com.br/materia.php?id=13&secao=156&mat=173|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The most important touristic site is the waterfall called Cachoeira de Maria Rosa, which is located 12 kilometers from the urban area.

==Economy==
Inadequate rainfall, isolation, and poor soils make this one of the poorest municipalities in the state and in the country.  The main economic activities are cattle raising (13,500 head in 2006) and farming with modest production of cotton, rice, beans, corn, and sorghum.  In 2006 there were 1,002 rural producers with a total area of 39,851 hectares.  Cropland made up 4,300 hectares and natural pasture 24,500 hectares.  There were only 56 tractors a ratio of one for every 200 farms.  In the urban area there was one financial institution as of 2006.  There were 668 automobiles, giving a ratio of about one automobile for every 19 inhabitants.  The Gross Domestic Product was R$38,291,000 (2005). Health care was provided by 9 public health clinics.  There was one hospital with 17 beds.
