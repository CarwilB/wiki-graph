''' Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 14 municipalities of the state of [[Yaracuy]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality is located in northwestern Yaracuy, occupying an area of 1087 km² with a population of 33,006 inhabitants in 2011. The [[municipal seat]] is located in [[Aroa, Venezuela|Aroa]]. The mayor is Sol Colmenárez of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela|PSUV]] party, since 2021.

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Bolívar  Municipality (disambiguation)|Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/media/1038/gv_t5_c38_p240_343_lres_single_preview.pdf|title=Estado Anzoátegui|trans-title=Anzoátegui|lang=es|first=Ninoska|last=Cilento|website=BiblioFEP|publisher=[[Empresas Polar]]|date=|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Bolívar Municipality was 33,006 people, accounting for 5.14% of the total population of the state of [[Yaracuy]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/22__yaracuy/|title=Yaracuy (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> Less than half of the population (47.56%) resides in [[Aroa, Venezuela|Arora]], the [[municipal seat]] of the municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/yaracuy/2202__bol%C3%ADvar/|title=Urban Localities in Bolívar (Yaracuy, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Bolívar as 37,905 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 1.7% since 2011 and showing a population density of 34.87 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/yaracuy/2202__bol%C3%ADvar/|title=Bolívar (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population showed an even balance with 16,032 men (51.4%) and 15,153 women (48.6%). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 62.6% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 31.6% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for the remaining 5.8%. The municipality is quite balanced in its rurality, with 52.6% of the inhabitants (16,389) living in [[rural areas]] compared to 47.4% (14,796) in [[urban centers]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[Mestizo]] (59.9%) and [[White people]] (33%). Minority groups included 6.1% [[Afro-Venezuelans]] and 0.9% belonging to other ethnic groups, with 26 individuals identifying as [[indigenous peoples|indigenous]]. The [[literacy rate]] was 88.5%, with 2,850 inhabitants of Bolívar not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Government==
The mayor of Bolívar Municipality is Sol Colmenárez of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela]]. She was first elected in November 2021.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.elnacional.com/2021/11/lista-de-las-alcaldias-y-sus-alcaldes-segun-el-cne-ultima-actualizacion/|title=Lista de las alcaldías y sus alcaldes según el CNE (última actualización)|trans-title=List of municipalities and their mayors according to the CNE (latest update)|lang=es|newspaper=[[El Nacional (Venezuela)|El Nacional]]|date=25 November 2021|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> She was re-elected in July 2025, winning 93.91% of the votes.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://yaracuyaldia.com/yaracuy/politica/comenzo-el-proceso-de-proclamacion-de-los-alcaldes-y-concejales-en-yaracuy/|title=Comenzó el proceso de proclamación de los alcaldes y concejales en Yaracuy|trans-title=The process of proclaiming the mayors and councilors in Yaracuy has begun.|lang=es|first=Patricia|last=Torres|newspaper=[[Yaracuy al Día]]|date=30 July 2025|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>
