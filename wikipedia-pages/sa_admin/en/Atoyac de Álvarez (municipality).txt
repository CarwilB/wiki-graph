'''Atoyac de Álvarez ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Atoyac de Álvarez]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|1,638.4|km2|sqmi}}.

==Geography==

Atoyac is located on the [[Costa Grande]] of the state of Guerrero, about {{convert|84|km|mi|sp=us}} west of the port of [[Acapulco]], which is the main city of the state. It has an area of {{cvt|1,638.4|km2|sqmi}}. It limits to the north with [[San Miguel Totolapan (municipality)|San Miguel Totolapan]], [[Ajuchitlán del Progreso (municipality)|Ajuchitlán]] del Progreso and [[General Heliodoro Castillo]]; to the east with [[Coyuca de Benítez (municipality)|Coyuca de Benítez]]; to the west with [[Tecpan de Galeana (municipality)|Tecpan de Galeana]] and to the south with [[Benito Juárez Municipality, Guerrero|Benito Juárez]]. The extreme coordinates of the municipality are 17° 03' - 18° 32' north latitude, and 100° 05' - 100 ° 34' west longitude.

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 60,680,<ref>{{cite web |title=Atoyac de Álvarez: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/es/profile/geo/atoyac-de-alvarez |website=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=9 November 2023}}</ref> up from 58,452 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Atoyac de Álvarez |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12011a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=December 23, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930030543/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12011a.htm |archive-date=September 30, 2007 }}</ref>
