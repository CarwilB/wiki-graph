'''Gonzaga''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], in [[Brazil]].  It is situated in the  [[Governador Valadares]] [[metropolitan area]].

Its population was estimated at 6,171 in 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/gonzaga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It is [[Jean Charles de Menezes]]'s native city.

The [[mayor]] of Gonzaga is currently Julio Maria de Sousa.
