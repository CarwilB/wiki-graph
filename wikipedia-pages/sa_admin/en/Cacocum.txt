'''Cacocum''' is a municipality and town in the [[Holguín Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
It is located south of the city of [[Holguín]] and east of the [[Frank País Airport|Frank País International Airport]] towards the city of [[Bayamo]] on the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] Highway. The area includes extremely flat ground stretching south to the municipality's boundaries with [[Granma Province]]. Its territory is bounded on the west by [[Las Tunas Province]], and on the east with the [[Báguanos, Cuba|Báguanos]] municipality, its nearest neighbor.

[[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] and the Central Railroad pass near the Cacocum townsite, the most populated settlement of this municipality. Two local [[sugar mill]]s, called ''Antonio Maceo'' and ''Cristino Naranjo'' represent Cacocum's main economic activity.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Cacocum had a population of 39,153.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|661|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|59|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
