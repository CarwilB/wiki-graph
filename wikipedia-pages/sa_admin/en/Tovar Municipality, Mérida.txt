'''Tovar Municipality''' is a municipality in [[Mérida State]], [[Venezuela]]. The population of Tovar measured by the 2013 census was 41,867.<ref>{{cite web|title=Estado Merida : Primeros Resultados XIII Censo General de Poblacion y Vivienda (Merida State : First Results XIII General Census of Population and Housing|url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/censo/fichascenso/tiro.asp?cod_entidad=14|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadistica|accessdate=12 September 2012|date=November 2002}}</ref> It is the birthplace of [[Major League Baseball]] player, [[Johan Santana]].<ref>{{cite book|last=Friedman|first=Ian C.|title=Latino Athletes|year=2007|publisher=Facts on File|location=New York|isbn=9780816063840|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=PiTnVkcqmiEC&dq=%22Tovar%2C+M%C3%A9rida%2C+Venezuela%22&pg=PA216|accessdate=12 September 2012|page=216}}</ref>

People native to Tovar are referred to as Tovareños. There is a recent movement to preserve some vintage photos of the town and its people.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://tovarmemoriacolectiva.blogspot.com/|title=Tovar - Memoria Colectiva}}</ref>
