'''Santa Cruz Balanyá''' ({{IPA|es|ˌsanta ˈkɾus βalanˈɟʝa}}) is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|14.16|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 11,144 inhabitants.

==History==
Balanyá was part of the Sacatepéquez region, inhabited by the  [[Kaqchikel people]]. During the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonial occupation, the prefix "Santa Cruz" was added, as per the practice of assigning Christian names to indigenous settlements. In the 18th century, Archbishop Fray Francisco Larras (1768–1770) visited the settlement and noted that it was an annex of the parish of San Juan Comalapa and the population consisted of approximately two hundred families and five hundred inhabitants, mostly made up of indigenous groups with a small [[Ladino people|Ladino]] population. He also described the area as agricultural growing [[maize]] and [[wheat]], and noted that the people wore thick clothing and head coverings due to the cold weather.

After the independence of Guatemala, during the promulgation of the political constitution on 11 October 1825, Balanyá was placed within the eighth district of Comalapa. It was later attached to the circuit of Comalapa on 27 August 1836. When the Chimaltenango department was officially established on 12 September 1839, Santa Cruz Balanyá was incorporated into it.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Santa Cruz Balanyá|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/historia-del-municipio-de-santa-cruz-balanya-chimaltenango/|access-date=30 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Santa Cruz Balanyá is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|14.16|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Santa Cruz Balanyá (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/chimaltenango/0410__santa_cruz_balany%C3%A1/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It is located at a distance of {{cvt|28|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Chimaltenango]] and {{cvt|81|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Tecpán Guatemala|Tecpán]] to the north, [[Zaragoza, Chimaltenango|Zaragoza]] and [[Comalapa]] to the east, [[Patzicía]] to the south, and [[Patzún]] to the west.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Santa Cruz Balanyá|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Santa Cruz Balanyá municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/geografia-del-municipio-de-santa-cruz-balanya-chimaltenango/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|2072.38|m}} above sea level, Santa Cruz Balanyá has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|20.96|C}} and receives about {{cvt|145.49|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/chimaltenango/santa-cruz-balanya|title=Santa Cruz Balanyá climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 11,144 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 5,667 males and 5,477 females. About 30.0% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.4% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (83.7%) was classified as urban, while 16.3% lived in rural areas. About 85.8% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality, while 14.2% were born in other municipalities in Guatemala or abroad. [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (96.3%) formed the major ethnic group, while [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (3.6%) formed a minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 91%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (79.2%) was the most spoken language, with [[Kaqchikel language|Kaqchikel]] spoken by 19.4% of the population.<ref name="CP"/>
