'''Bataguassu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. In 2020, its population was 23,325. Its area is {{cvt|2417|km2}}. The town was founded in 1932 by [[Jan Antonín Baťa]] as part of his expansion of the [[Bata Corporation|Bata shoe company]], founded by his brother [[Tomáš Baťa|Tomáš]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Bata's World – Brazil |url=http://world.tomasbata.org/america/brazil/ |website=world.tomasbata.org |access-date=8 September 2023}}</ref>

Other towns in Brazil linked to Baťa include [[Anaurilândia]], [[Batatuba]], [[Mariápolis]], and [[Batayporã]].
