'''Cercado''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in [[Cochabamba Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[Cochabamba]], which is also the capital of the department.

== Subdivision ==
The province consists of one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Cochabamba Municipality'''. It is identical to the province.

== The people ==
The people are predominantly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechuas|Quechuan]] and [[Aymara people|Aymaran]] descent.
