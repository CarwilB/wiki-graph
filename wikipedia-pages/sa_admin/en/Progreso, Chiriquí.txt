'''Progreso''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Barú District, Chiriquí|Barú District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|55.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 11,402 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|205|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 13,107; its population as of 2000 was 10,103.<ref name=inec/> Its inhabitants are mainly engaged in [[Sowing|planting]] and harvesting [[Rice]].
