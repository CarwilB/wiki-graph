'''Itaquitinga''' is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 84&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2025]]) population of 17,142 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Condado, Pernambuco|Condado]]   ('''N''');  [[Igarassu, Pernambuco|Igarassu]]    ('''S''');  [[Goiana]]   ('''E''');  [[Nazaré da Mata]] and [[Tracunhaém]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 103.44&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 88 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.3 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 84&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Itaquitinga''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]] (over 477,000 tons) and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[chickens]].
