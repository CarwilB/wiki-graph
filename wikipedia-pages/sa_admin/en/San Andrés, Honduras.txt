'''San Andrés''' is a municipality in the Honduran [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Lempira (department)|Lempira]].

It is notable as the principal site in Honduras for the mining of [[andesite]] [[opal]]s from Las Colinas deposit near Social, which is in the municipality. This mining is largely performed by [https://www.gold.org/esg/artisanal-and-small-scale-gold-mining artisanal miners], rather than on an industrial scale.<ref>[https://minerals.usgs.gov/minerals/pubs/country/2004/camermyb04.pdf United States Geological Survey]</ref>

It is approximately 120 km from [[Gracias]] city. It is about 1 hour away from [[Erandique]] city.

== History ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
In the census of 1801 it was no Social "Guaxinlaca" village, under the delegation of "Gracias a Dios". In the national division of 1889, it appeared as a municipality of Erandique district.

== Geography ==
Since it is located on the mountains, the main forests are Pines and Oaks. In 2020, it had 12.4 kha of natural forest.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Vizzuality |title=San Andrés, Honduras, Lempira Deforestation Rates & Statistics {{!}} GFW |url=https://www.globalforestwatch.org/country/HND/13/16/ |access-date=2025-05-28 |website=www.globalforestwatch.org |language=en}}</ref> Its elevation above sea level is the proper one for coffee plantations. Its mountains and hills are very high and steep. It has several water spring running down the steep hills. One curiosity is a huge rock monolith on the way to the departmental capital, which proves the volcanic origins of the rocks in this municipality and the rest of the department.

== Boundaries ==

*South: [[Gualcince]] municipality.
*East: [[Erandique]] municipality. 
*West: [[Valladolid, Lempira|Valladolid]], [[Tambla]] and [[Tomalá]] municipalities.
*Surface Extents: 243 square km

== Resources ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
[[Image:San Andres,Lempira 6.JPG|240px|thumb|right|[[Autumn]] in San Andrés' forests]]
The main product of this municipality is [[coffee]], taking advantage of its elevation from sea level and soil conditions. [[Vegetable|Vegetables]], [[Maize|corn]], and [[Bean|beans]] are second in importance, and then follows [[cattle]] raising and [[milk]] products. These last items are mostly for local consumption. The exploitation of the forest is rational yet. There is electricity, but they sometimes have problems with it, and the absence can last up to 3 days. There is also coverage of mobiles services. And in some houses, people sell gasoline and diesel fuel.

===Demographics===
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Andrés municipality had a population of 13,151. Of these, 52.51% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (52.45% [[Lenca]]), 47.17% [[Mestizo]], 0.27% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.04% [[White Latin Americans|White]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
