'''Lomas de Zamora''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' (district) of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], and part of the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] [[urban agglomeration]].

It has an area of {{convert|89|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}} and a population of 613,192 ({{census-ar|2001}}),<ref name=Cen2010 /> the second-most populous partido in the Greater Buenos Aires agglomeration. The local government's seat is at the city of [[Lomas de Zamora]].
