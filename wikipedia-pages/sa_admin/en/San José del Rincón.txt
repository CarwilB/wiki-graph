'''San José del Rincón''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[State of Mexico]]. Its inhabitants are referred to as ''monteros''.<ref name=municipio>{{cite web |url=http://www.sanjosedelrincon.gob.mx/pagina/resena-del-ayuntamiento-toponimia-y-denominacion |title=San José del Rincón |publisher=Municipal Government of San José del Rincón |accessdate=4 November 2017}}</ref>

==Geography==
San José del Rincón is located in the western part of the State of Mexico on its border with [[Michoacán]]. From north to south, it borders the municipalities of [[El Oro de Hidalgo|El Oro]], [[San Felipe del Progreso]], [[Villa Victoria]] and [[San José Villa de Allende|Villa de Allende]] in the State of Mexico, and the municipalities of [[Tlalpujahua]], [[Senguío]], [[Angangueo]], [[Ocampo Municipality, Michoacán|Ocampo]] and [[Zitácuaro]] in Michoacán. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|489|km2}}.<ref name=infocard/> The highest point in the municipality is the Cerro de Cabrero in the south, at {{convert|3260|m}} above sea level.<ref name=background>{{cite web |url=https://www.sanjosedelrincon.gob.mx/archivos/AntecedentsdelMunicipio.pdf |title=Antecedents del Municipio |publisher=Municipal Government of San José del Rincón |date=2017 |accessdate=4 November 2017}}</ref>

The northern part of the municipality lies within the [[Lerma River]] basin, while the southern part lies in the [[Cutzamala River]] sub-basin of the [[Balsas River]] system, and is drained by the Arroyo Grande<ref name=background/> which flows southeast into Villa Victoria Reservoir, which is part of [[Mexico City]]'s [[Water management in Greater Mexico City|water supply]].

Forests cover 30.7% of San José del Rincón and are mostly located in the mountainous western part of the municipality,<ref name=encyclopedia/> where the predominant trees are ''[[oyamel]]'' and pine.<ref name=municipio/> These forests are protected as part of the [[Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve]].<ref name=background/> The rest of the land in the municipality is mainly used for agriculture and livestock grazing. Soils in the municipality are mostly [[andosol]]s with isolated areas of [[planosol]]s in the northeast and [[cambisol]]s in the east.<ref name=background/>

The climate is temperate sub-humid with rainy summers. Average temperatures range between 12 and 18 degrees Celsius. Frost can occur between December and March; February and March bring winds and dust. More than 100 mm of rain falls in each of the months from June to September.<ref name=background/>

==History==
The municipality of San José del Rincón was created by government decree on 2 October 2001,<ref name=municipio/> which went into force on 1 January 2002.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Its territory previously comprised the western part of the municipality of San Felipe del Progreso.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
Municipal elections are held every three years. The current president of the municipality is Jesús Rolando Rangel Espinosa of the [[Institutional Revolutionary Party|PRI]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of San José del Rincón<ref name=municipio/> recorded a population of 91,345 inhabitants living in 17,707 households.<ref name=infocard/> Its annual growth rate of 2.7% was the highest in the State of Mexico.<ref name=mvt>{{cite news |url=http://mvt.com.mx/san-jose-del-rincon-el-municipio-mas-pobre-y-el-que-mas-se-reproduce/ |title=San José del Rincón: el municipio más pobre y el que más se reproduce |publisher=MVT |date=7 December 2010 |accessdate=4 November 2017}}</ref><ref name=sinembargo>{{cite news |last1=Olvera |first1=Dulce |last2=Flores |first2=Linaloe R. |url=http://www.sinembargo.mx/10-06-2017/3237411 |title=El mexiquense rico le dio la espalda al PRI, y el PRI sacó votos a los distritos marginados |publisher=Sin Embargo |date=10 June 2017 |accessdate=4 November 2017}}</ref> It recorded a population of 93,878 inhabitants in the 2015 Intercensal Survey.<ref name=survey/> 

There are 137 localities in the municipality,<ref name=background/> three of which are classified as urban:
*San José del Rincón Centro, which recorded a population of 1635 inhabitants in the 2010 Census and serves as the municipal headquarters; 
*Guarda de la Lagunita, also known as Las Canoas, which had 2543 inhabitants in 2010; and 
*San Miguel Agua Bendita, which had 2500 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name=infocard/>

In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 89.67% of people in the municipality identified themselves as [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|indigenous]].<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 11,191 people or 12% of the population in San José del Rincón reported speaking an indigenous language, of which 10,916 spoke [[Mazahua language|Mazahua]].<ref name=snim/>

==Economy==
San José del Rincón is one of the poorest municipalities in the State of Mexico.<ref name=mvt/><ref name=sinembargo/> Its main economic activities are agriculture, animal husbandry, commerce and handicrafts.<ref name=background/>
