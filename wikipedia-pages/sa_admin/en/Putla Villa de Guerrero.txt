'''Putla Villa de Guerrero''' or simply '''Putla''' ({{langx|mix|Ñuucaa}}, 'Place  of  Copper' or {{lang|mix|Ñuuñuma}}, 'Place of Smoke'<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Whittaker |first1=Gordon |title=The Study of North Mesoamerican Place-Signs |journal=INDIANA - Estudios Antropológicos sobre América Latina y el Caribe |date=1 January 1993 |volume=13 |page=33 |doi=10.18441/ind.v13i0.9-38 |url=https://journals.iai.spk-berlin.de/index.php/indiana/article/view/1812}}</ref>), is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the State of [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]]. 
It is part of [[Putla District, Oaxaca|Putla District]] in the west of the [[Sierra Sur de Oaxaca|Sierra Sur Region]].

The name Putla is derived from the [[Nahuatl]] name Puctitlán, meaning ''place of mist''. The Villa de Guerrero part is in honor of [[Vicente Guerrero]], a hero of the [[Mexican War of Independence]]. Putla became the seat of its municipality in 1907 and is located 374&nbsp;km from the city of [[Oaxaca, Oaxaca|Oaxaca]].<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20073a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México, Oaxaca, Putla Villa de Guerrero |accessdate=2008-02-24 |work= |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601195135/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20073a.htm |archive-date=2007-06-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

It connects the [[Mixteca]] region with the coast of [[Oaxaca]] and is a commercial center for the [[Mixtec|Mixtecs]], [[Amuzgo people|Amuzgo]]s, and [[Chatinos|Chatino]]s that live in the area.<ref name="MexDesOax">{{cite journal |last= Quintanar Hinojosa |first=Beatriz  |date=August 2007 |title=De la Mixteca a la costa |journal=Guía México Desconocido: Oaxaca |volume= 137  |pages=84  |url= http://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx }}</ref>

==Demography== 
Within Putla, there are 7.75k people that speak indigenous languages. These include mostly [[Trique languages|Triqui]] and [[Mixtec languages|Mixtec]] dialects, as well as [[Zapotec languages|Zapotec]], [[Nahuatl]], [[Amuzgo language|Amuzgo]], [[Chatino language|Chatino]], and others.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Putla Villa de Guerrero: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://datamexico.org/en/profile/geo/putla-villa-de-guerrero |access-date=2023-04-11 |website=Data México |language=en}}</ref>

== Education ==
When it comes to pursuing higher education, the majority of those in Putla pursue a career in teaching at different levels; 30.8% are educated for preschool level education, 38.4% are trained for primary level education, and 30.8% are educated to teach specific subjects.<ref name=":0" />
