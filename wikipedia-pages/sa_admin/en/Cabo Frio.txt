'''Cabo Frio''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈkabu ˈfɾi.u}}, ''Cold Cape'') is a tourist destination located in the state of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]].

Cabo Frio is unique that it offers both Ocean and Lake beaches.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cabo Frio Travel Guide – Beaches, Attractions & Dining |url=https://www.kupi.com/en-ae/explore/brazil/cabo-frio |access-date=2026-06-03 |website=Kupi.com |language=en}}</ref>

The Brazilian coast runs east from Rio de Janeiro to Cabo Frio where it turns sharply north. North of Cabo Frio is [[Cabo de São Tomé]].

It was named after the geography of its location, a [[Cape (geography)|cape]], and because the water temperature is colder than in nearby cities (''[[wikt:frio#Portuguese|frio]]'' is Portuguese for "cold"). This city features beaches with white fine sand. Due to a lack of [[mica]], the sand stays cool in the sun.

{{as of|2025}}, Cabo Frio's estimated population is 238,438 and its area is 410&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

== Demography ==
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], in 2010, Cabo Frio had 90,831 men (48.7%) and 95,396 women (51.3%). The municipality has demonstrated rapid demographic growth over the decades.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/cabo-frio/pesquisa/20/29767?ano=2010 |access-date=2025-03-21 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

=== Ethnicities ===
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], the municipality is made up of 88,701 whites, 72,561 mixed race, 23,555 blacks, 1,109 Asians, and 301 indigenous people.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/cabo-frio/panorama |access-date=2025-03-21 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

* [[White people|Whites]] - 47.7%
* [[Pardo Brazilians|Pardos]] - 39%
* [[Black people|Blacks]] - 12.6%
* [[Asian people|Asians]] - 0.6%
* [[Indigenous peoples|Indigenous]] - 0.1%

=== Religion ===
According to the IBGE, the municipality is made up of 78,061 evangelicals, 64,006 Catholics, 32,894 non-religious people (including atheists and [[Agnosticism|agnostics]]), 6,539 [[Kardecist spiritism|spiritualists]], and 6,413 people who profess other religions, such as [[Judaism]], [[Buddhism]], [[Islam]], [[Exotericism|esotericism]], and [[neopaganism]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=cabofrio.net.br |title=cabofrio.net.br {{!}} Guia de empresas e serviços em Cabo Frio |url=https://cabofrio.net.br/ |access-date=2025-08-13 |website=cabofrio.net.br |language=pt}}</ref>

* [[Evangelicalism|Evangelicals]] - 41.7%
* [[Catholic Church|Roman Catholics]] - 34.3%
* [[Non-religious]] (including [[Atheism|atheists]] and [[Agnosticism|agnostics]]) - 17.6%
* [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] - 3.5%
* Other religions - 3.4%
