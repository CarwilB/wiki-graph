'''Canaã''' (Portuguese for "[[Canaan]]") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 4,548 (2020 est.) in an area of 175 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23NQ3 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>
