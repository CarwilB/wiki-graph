'''San José Independencia''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 58.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is part of the [[Tuxtepec District]] of the [[Papaloapan Region]].

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 4,251,<ref>{{cite web |title=San José Independencia: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/san-jose-independencia |publisher=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=20 April 2024}}</ref> up from 3,689 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=San José Independencia|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20169a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=12 June 2009}}</ref>
