'''Juárez''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ciudad Juárez]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|4853|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

In the 2010 [[INEGI]] Census, the municipality reported a total population of 1,332,131, of whom 1,321,004 (over 99%) lived in the municipal seat.<ref>
{{cite web|title=Juárez|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08037a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617074026/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08037a.htm|archivedate=June 17, 2011}}</ref>

The municipality is named for 19th-century [[President of Mexico|president]] [[Benito Juárez]], as is the city of Ciudad Juárez.
