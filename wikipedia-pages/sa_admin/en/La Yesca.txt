'''La Yesca''' ({{ipa|es|la ˈɟʝeska}}) is a [[municipality]] and the municipal seat of the same in the Mexican state of [[Nayarit]], being the easternmost and least densely populated municipality in Nayarit. The population of the municipality was 12,025 (2005).  The population of the town and municipal seat was 356 inhabitants in 2005. The population density was 7 inhabitants per square kilometer, one of the lowest in the state.

==Geography==
La Yesca municipality covers {{convert|4,316.311|km2|sqmi}}, having the second largest area for municipalities in Nayarit.<ref name="Gómez-Reyes">{{Cite journal |last=Gómez-Reyes |first=Eugenio |display-authors=etal |year=2015 |title=Water balance of La Yesca municipality, Nayarit, México / El balance hídrico en el municipio de La Yesca, Nayarit, México |journal=Revista Bio Ciencias |volume=3 |issue=3 |pages=228–246 |url=http://dspace.uan.mx:8080/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/407/Water%20balance%20of%20La%20Yesca%20municipality%2C%20Nayarit%2C%20M%C3%A9xico.pdf |doi=10.15741/revbio.03.03.09 |doi-access=free |access-date=2018-08-12 |archive-date=2018-08-12 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180812083105/http://dspace.uan.mx:8080/xmlui/bitstream/handle/123456789/407/Water%20balance%20of%20La%20Yesca%20municipality,%20Nayarit,%20M%C3%A9xico.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> Located at the southern end of the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]], it is mostly mountainous terrain. It lies between 21º 10’ and 22º 00’ North latitude and 103º 43” and 104º 33’ west longitude. It is bordered to the southwest by the municipalities of [[Santa María del Oro, Nayarit|Santa María del Oro]], [[Jala, Nayarit|Jala]] and [[Ixtlán del Río]], to the northwest by the municipality of [[El Nayar]], and to the north, east and south by the state of [[Jalisco]].<ref name="Gómez-Reyes" /><ref>See map at {{Cite map|title=Nayarit |url=http://www.maps-of-mexico.com/nayarit-state-mexico/nayarit-state-mexico-map-main.shtml }}</ref>

Mountainous zones comprise 95% of the area. The main elevations are Sierra el Pinabete at 1,420&nbsp;m, the [[Pajarito Mountains, Nayarit|Sierra Pajaritos]] with an elevation of 2,500&nbsp;m and the Sierra de Álica with an elevation of 2,200&nbsp;m.{{cn|date=May 2026}}

The rivers are the [[Río Grande de Santiago]], Bolaños, Camotlán, Huaynamota and Jora Viejo.{{cn|date=May 2026}}

==Demographics==
The census of 1995 registered 4,350 indigenous inhabitants, which made up 35.92% of the population. La Yesca had the second largest indigenous population in the state after [[El Nayar]].

== Economy ==

There is at least one active mine for gold and silver located on a 6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> concession. The project includes two previous producing mines and a modern milling facility capable of milling 200 tonnes per day of feed. Approximately 200,000 tonnes of tailings are available to process immediately, with the tailings containing good grades of both gold and silver.<ref>[http://www.infomine.com/index/properties/LA_YESCA.html mine info]</ref>
