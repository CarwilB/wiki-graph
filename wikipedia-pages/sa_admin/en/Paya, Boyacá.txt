'''Paya''' is a town and municipality in the [[La Libertad Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Paya limits [[Pisba]], [[Labranzagrande]] of Boyacá and [[Támara, Casanare|Támara]], [[Nunchía]] and [[Yopal]] ([[Casanare Department|Casanare]]).<ref name=websitePaya>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150530081858/http://www.paya-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Paya]</ref>

== Etymology ==
Paya in [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] means "People of hope".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Paya] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] in the 1530s, Paya was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]].

Modern Paya was founded on September 14, 1600.<ref name=websitePaya/>

== Economy ==
The majority of the economy of Paya comes from [[horticulture]] (47%). Other areas of income are [[livestock]] farming (37%) and [[agriculture]] (16%).<ref name=websitePaya/>
