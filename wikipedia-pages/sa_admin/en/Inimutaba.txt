'''Inimutaba''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 7,560 living in a total area of 529&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/inimutaba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]].  It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
Inimutaba is located at an elevation of 630 meters on highway [[BR-259]] ([[Felixlândia]] to [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]]). The nearest major population center is [[Curvelo]].

The distance to [[Curvelo]] is 6&nbsp;km; and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 175&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Curvelo]] and [[Presidente Juscelino]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172032/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |date=2008-04-10 }}</ref>

== History ==
Inimutaba began in the nineteenth century with the construction of a textile factory called Fiação e Tecelagem Cedro e Cachoeira.  The name of the town is derived from this industry as it means "village of weavers" in the indigenous language.  In the early nineties the factory closed and the town went through a period of economic depression.

== Economy ==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture.  The production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations is also important.  The GDP in 2005 was R$29 million, with 15 million from services, 6 million from industry, and 5 million from agriculture.  There were 222 rural producers on 19,000 hectares of land.  Only 40 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 1,700 persons were dependent on agriculture.  The main crops were sugarcane, beans, and corn.  There were 19,000 head of cattle (2006).  There were no banks (2007) and 382 automobiles (353 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 17 inhabitants per automobile.
<ref name="IBGE"/>

== Health and education ==
There were 2 health clinics in 2005.  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Belo Horizonte or Curvelo.  Educational needs were met by 7 primary schools, 1 middle schools, and 4 pre-primary schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>
