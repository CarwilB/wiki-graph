'''San Juan Tecuaco''' ({{IPA|es|saŋ ˈxwan teˈkwako}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Santa Rosa Department, Guatemala|Santa Rosa]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcaldes electos en el departamento de Santa Rosa |url=http://www.municipalidadesdeguatemala.info/alcaldes-16/alcaldes-sta-rosa-16.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151030183607/http://www.municipalidadesdeguatemala.info/alcaldes-16/alcaldes-sta-rosa-16.php |archive-date=2015-10-30 |access-date=2025-06-21 |website=www.municipalidadesdeguatemala.info |language=en}}</ref>

==History==
The municipality was founded around 1800 by the [[Xinca people|Xincas]], an almost extinct tribe indigenous to the area.
San Juan Tecuaco is located in the southeast of Guatemala, in the department of Santa Rosa.
Within 82 square km it is surrounded to the west and south by [[Chiquimulilla]], to the east by [[Oratorio]] and [[Pasaco]] and to the north by [[Santa Maria Ixhuatan]].

The Democratic local government is represented by the Major, ([[Alcalde]]) followed by the vice, and three councilors.
The fair ([[feria]]) Bailes, Misa, and Jaripeos, starts on January 23 and ends on January 27 in honor of [[Saint John the Evangelist|San Juan Evangelist]].

==Etymology==
Tecuaco means Tec= tex (Snake), and uaco=cuacl (stone) or place of the snake of stone, a type of [[rattlesnake]].
