'''Tilarán''' is a small town and a district in [[Guanacaste Province]] in Costa Rica. It is the seat of the [[Tilarán Canton]] located in the hills overlooking the west shore of [[Lake Arenal]]. It is connected by road to [[El Silencio, Costa Rica|El Silencio]], and by the 142 road down through the [[Cordillera de Tilarán]] hills to [[Tejona]].<ref>{{cite book|author=Baker, C.P.|title=Costa Rica|publisher=[[DK (publisher)|Dorling Kindersley]] Eye Witness Travel Guides|year=2005|pages=150}}</ref> The area between Tilaran and Tejona is one of the most important wind farms in Costa Rica and turbines are prominent on the landscape. Animal husbandry also forms an important part of the local economy.<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The area between Tilaran and Tejona is one of the most important [[wind farms]] in Costa Rica and turbines are prominent on the landscape. [[Animal husbandry]] also forms an important part of the local economy.
