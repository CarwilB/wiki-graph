'''General Las Heras Partido''' is a small [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 13,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|76|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[General Las Heras, Buenos Aires|General Las Heras]], which is {{convert|67|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The partido and its main town are named after [[Juan Gregorio de las Heras]], a hero of the [[Argentine War of Independence]] and governor of Buenos Aires.
