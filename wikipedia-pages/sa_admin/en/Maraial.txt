'''Maraial''' is a city in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]], 154&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Recife]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Jaqueira]]  ('''N''');  [[Alagoas]] state   ('''S''');  [[Xexéu]] and [[Catende]]  ('''E'''); [[São Benedito do Sul]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 192.25&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 212 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] river
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.6 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 154&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Maraial''' are based in food and beverage industry and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s, [[pineapple]]s, [[cattle]] and [[goat]]s.
