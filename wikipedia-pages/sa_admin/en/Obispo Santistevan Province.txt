'''Obispo Santistevan''' (or: ''Santiesteban'') is one of the fifteen provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz]] [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] and is situated in the department's western parts. The province was founded on 2 December 1941, and its name honors [[Obispo Santistevan]] (1843–1931), famous supporter of the building of [[Santa Cruz de la Sierra|Santa Cruz]] Cathedral.

== Location ==
''Obispo Santistevan'' Province is located between 16° 02' and 17° 24' [[Latitude|South]] and between 63° 02' and 64° 15' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over a length of 280&nbsp;km from Northwest to Southeast, and up to 45&nbsp;km from West to East. The province's border to the neighbouring provinces in the East and Northeast is formed by [[Río Grande (Bolivia)|Río Grande]].

The province is situated in the Bolivian lowlands and borders [[Ichilo Province]] in the Northwest, [[Sara Province]] in the Southwest, [[Ignacio Warnes Province]] in the Southeast, [[Ñuflo de Chávez Province]] in the East, and [[Guarayos Province]] in the Northeast.

== Population ==
The population of Obispo Santistevan Province has increased by 75% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 104,660 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 142,786 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2012: 181,198 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2024: 196,101 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolivia: Administrative Division (Departments and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

44.7% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

98.2% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 27.6% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 1.9% [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 1.2 speak [[Guarani language|Guaraní]]. ''(1992)''

35.5% of the population have no access to electricity, 34.2% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

86.5% of the population are Catholics, 9.8% are Protestants. ''(1992)''
