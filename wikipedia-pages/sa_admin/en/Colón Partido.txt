'''Colón Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the northern border of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 23,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1022|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Colón, Buenos Aires|Colón]], which is around {{convert|260|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
