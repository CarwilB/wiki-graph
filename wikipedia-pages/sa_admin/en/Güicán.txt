'''Güicán de la Sierra''', also known as '''Nuestra Señora de la Candelaria de Güicán''', is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. It is close to the national natural park [[El Cocuy National Park]].<ref>{{Cite web

== Etymology ==
The municipality was named after Güicán tribe, an indigenous people who inhabited this region and is related to the [[U'wa people]]. The name Güicán means "in the fence of the wife" in the indigenous language.<ref>{{Cite web |title=BOYACÁ-GÜICÁN |url=http://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/BOYACA/MUNICIPIOS/GUICAN/GUICAN.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230405203102/http://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/BOYACA/MUNICIPIOS/GUICAN/GUICAN.htm |archive-date=2023-04-05 |access-date= |website=www.colombiaturismoweb.com |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
[[File:Guican monumento.jpg|thumb|Monument to the indigenous dignity of Güicán]]
The conquistador of this territory was [[Hernán Pérez de Quesada]], who arrived with his troops in search of gold. The aborigines withdrew behind the Sierra Nevada to escape from the Spanish.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Fajardo |first=José del Rey |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=70li2ei5hmQC&dq=miguel+blasco+jesuita&pg=PA133 |title=Los Jesuitas en Venezuela: Las misiones germen de la nacionalidad |date=2006 |publisher=Universidad Catolica Andres |isbn=978-980-244-519-6 |page=133 |language=es}}</ref> The municipality was founded by a [[Jesuits|Jesuit]] friar Miguel Blasco on February 26, [[1756]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nuestro Municipio, Información general |url=http://www.guican-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |access-date=2023-04-05 |website=www.guican-boyaca.gov.co |language=es |archive-date=2015-05-29 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150529171904/http://www.guican-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality borders [[Chiscas]] and [[Cubará]] on the north, [[Fortul]] and [[Saravena]] on the east, [[Tame, Arauca|Tame]] and [[El Cocuy]] on the south, as well as [[Panqueba]] and [[El Espino, Boyacá|El Espino]] on the west. Its average elevation is at 3,492 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Güicán topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-3mnb18/G%C3%BCic%C3%A1n/ |access-date=2023-04-05 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Güicán has a [[Tundra climate|Tundra Climate]] (ET). It receives the most amount of rainfall in April, with 244 mm of average precipitation; and the least in January, with 83 mm of average precipitation.
