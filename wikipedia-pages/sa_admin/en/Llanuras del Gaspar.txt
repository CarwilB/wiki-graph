'''Llanuras del Gaspar''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Sarapiquí (canton)|Sarapiquí]] canton, in the [[Heredia Province|Heredia]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |lang=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>
== History ==
Llanuras del Gaspar was created on 6 October 1999 by Decreto Ejecutivo 28137-G.<ref name="dta2017" />
== Location ==

It is located in the northern region of the country and borders the district of Puerto Viejo to the south and west, with the province of Limón to the east and Nicaragua to the north.
Its head, the village of La Aldea, is located 40&nbsp;km (18 minutes) NE of Puerto Viejo and 122&nbsp;km (2 hours 59 minutes) to the NE of San José the capital of the nation.

== Geography ==

Llanuras del Gaspar has an area of {{cvt|267.74|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:20}} metres.<ref name="divadm" /> It is the smallest district of the canton by surface.

It presents a plain territory by dominated by the plains of Sarapiquí.

== Demography ==

The district has 1,160 inhabitants, making it the third-most populous of the canton.<ref name="GR2">{{cite web |url=http://www.inec.go.cr/system/files_force/documentos/inec_institucional/estadisticas/resultados/repoblaccenso2011-15.pdf.pdf?download=1 |publisher=INEC |format=pdf |title=2011 Census Population Report |df= |access-date=2017-08-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170807232834/http://www.inec.go.cr/system/files_force/documentos/inec_institucional/estadisticas/resultados/repoblaccenso2011-15.pdf.pdf?download=1 |archive-date=2017-08-07 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Economy ==

As in its neighboring district of Cureña, agriculture (banana, pineapple, yucca and plantain) is the basis of the local economy.

La Aldea, its head, has education and health services, is the last thanks to the presence of an BTIHC (Basic Team for Integral Health Care) or EBAIS, for its acronym in Spanish.
