'''Pointe-à-Raquette''' ({{IPA|fr|pwɛ̃t a ʁakɛt}}; {{langx|ht|Pwentarakèt}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Gonâve Island#Administrative Division|La Gonâve Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 22,886 inhabitants.
