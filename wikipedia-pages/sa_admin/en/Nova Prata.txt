'''Nova Prata''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the mountainous [[Serra Gaúcha]] region of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], in southern Brazil. It is in the Guapore micro-region of the Nordeste Rio-Grandense meso-region of the state.

==Geography==
The city of Nova Prata is situated at about {{Coord|28|47| 0|S|51|36|40|W|region:BR_type:city(22257)}}, at an altitude of 820 meters. The municipality occupies an area of 258.864&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup><ref name=ibge/> or 259&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name= prata/> In 2020 the population was estimated to be 27,648. Temperature ranges from a minimum of 14&nbsp;°C to a maximum of 28&nbsp;°C with an annual average of 20&nbsp;°C.

Nova Prata is surrounded by the municipalities of: [[André da Rocha]] and [[Guabiju]] to the north; [[Fagundes Varela]] and [[Vila Flores]] to the south; [[Nova Bassano]], [[Nova Araçá]] and [[Vista Alegre do Prata]] to the west; and [[Protásio Alves]] to the east.
