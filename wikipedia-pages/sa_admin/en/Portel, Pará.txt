'''Portel''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 62,945 people. The area of the municipality is 25,384.779&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Portel''.
