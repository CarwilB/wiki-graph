'''Logroño Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Morona-Santiago Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Logroño, Ecuador|Logroño]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 4,621.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> The current town of Logroño is located in the vicinity of the lost 16th century mining town of Logroño de los Caballeros.<ref>[https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2017-12-20/he-struck-it-rich-in-ecuador-now-he-s-looking-for-the-lost-cities-of-gold Bloomberg features/2017-12-20/ He Struck It Rich In Ecuador Now He Is Looking For The Lost Cities-of Gold]</ref>
