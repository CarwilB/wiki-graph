'''Capiatá''' ({{IPA|es|ka.pjaˈta}}) is a city and a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in [[Central Department]], [[Paraguay]]. It had a population of 236,999 inhabitants according to the 2022 national census, making it the [[List of cities in Paraguay by population|fourth-most populous city]] in the country.

==History==
Documents in the Archivo Nacional de Asunción from the early seventeenth century refer to Villa de Capiatá. Following the foundation of [[Asunción]] on 15 August 1537, the Cabildo and Regimiento were created to oversee the city's welfare and the Villa de Capiatá fell under its jurisdiction. Capiatá was influenced by the [[Franciscans]] and its strategic location near Asuncion and as a part of other towns in the Franciscan circuit contributed to it becoming one of the most densely settled areas of the region. It grew around a chapel dedicated to [[Virgin of Candelaria]].<ref name="Mun">{{cite web|url=https://municipalidadcapiata.gov.py/institucional/historia-del-municipio/|title=Historia del Municipio|work=Municipalidad de Capiatá|access-date=20 May 2026|lang=es|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20240304210323/https://municipalidadcapiata.gov.py/institucional/historia-del-municipio/|archive-date=4 March 2024|trans-title=History of the municipality}}</ref>

Capiata officially became a city on 20 May 1878, when the municipal board was established. It was elevated as a municipality in 1968, and Luis Alberto Rachit Fiandro became the first Mayor.<ref name="Mun"/>

==Geography==
Capiatá is located in the [[Central Department]] of Paraguay. It covers an area of {{cvt|88.2|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/central/1102__capiat%C3%A1/|title=Capiata|work=City Population.de|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.mindat.org/feature-3439214.html|title=Capiata|work=Mineral Data|access-date=20 May 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|20|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of Asuncion. It borders seven cities&ndash;[[Areguá]], [[Itauguá]], [[Julián Augusto Saldívar]], [[Luque]], [[Ñemby]], [[San Lorenzo, Paraguay|San Lorenzo]] and [[Ypané]].<ref name="Mun"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2022 census, Capiatá had a total population of 236,999 inhabitants, making it the [[List of cities in Paraguay by population|fourth-most populous city]] in the country. The population consisted of 118,679 females and 118,320 males. The median age of the population was 29 years.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="UH">{{cite news|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/top-10-de-las-ciudades-mas-pobladas-del-paraguay-segun-el-censo-2022|title=El top 10 de las ciudades más pobladas del Paraguay, según el Censo 2022|work=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=31 August 2023|access-date=20 May 2026|lang=es|trans-title=The Top 10 of Paraguay’s most populous cities, according to the 2022 Census}}</ref> The entire population was classified as urban. About 24.7% of the population was aged below 14 years, and 6.8% were more than 65 years.<ref name="CP"/>
