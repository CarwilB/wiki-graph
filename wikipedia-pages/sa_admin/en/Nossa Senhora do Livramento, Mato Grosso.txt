'''Nossa Senhora do Livramento''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the state of [[Mato Grosso]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 13,104 (2020 est.) in an area of 5076.78 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/232NP Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 232 m.
