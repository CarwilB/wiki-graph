'''Villa Hidalgo Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. It is part of the [[Villa Alta District]] in the center of the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca|Sierra Norte Region]]. Its municipal seat is [[Hidalgo Yalalag]], which is located {{convert|104|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Oaxaca City]].<ref name=encmuc>{{cite web |title= Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México - Villa Hidalgo Yalalag |publisher= INAFED |url= http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM20oaxaca/municipios/20038a.htmll |accessdate= 9 June 2014 }}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

As of 2020, the municipality had a population of 1,885.<ref name="census2020">{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/scitel/Default?ev=9|title=Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 - SCITEL|language=es|publisher=INEGI|access-date=2021-02-26}}</ref>
