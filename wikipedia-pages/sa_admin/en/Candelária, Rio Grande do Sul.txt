'''Candelária''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

== Paleontology ==
[[File:Museu Aristides Carlos Rodrigues.JPG|thumb|left|[[Museum Aristides Carlos Rodrigues]]]]
8&nbsp;km west of Candelária there is an outcrop of [[Caturrita Formation]] where various [[Triassic]] fossils have been collected, especially small [[cynodonts]] like ''[[Riograndia]]''. The discovery of ''Riograndia'' in 2001 made it possible to better study the complex of anatomical changes involved in the transition from cynodonts to mammals.<ref name="Bonaparte2001">{{cite journal|author=Jose F Bonaparte, Jorge Ferigolo, Ana Maria Ribeiro|year=2001|url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229744390|title=A Primitive Late Triassic ‘ictidosaur’ from Rio Grande Do Sul, Brazil|journal=Palaeontology|volume=44|issue=4|pages=623–635|doi=10.1111/1475-4983.00194}}</ref>

The [[Museum Aristides Carlos Rodrigues]], in this city, has fossils.
