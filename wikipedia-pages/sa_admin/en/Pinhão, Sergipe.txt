'''Pinhão''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|pĩˈj̃ɐ̃w}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 6,627 (2020) and its area is {{convert|156.1|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Pinhão has a population density of 42 inhabitants per square kilometer. Pinhão is located {{convert|80.7|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/>
