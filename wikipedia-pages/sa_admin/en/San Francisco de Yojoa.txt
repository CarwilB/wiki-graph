'''San Francisco de Yojoa''' ({{IPA|es|saɱ fɾanˈsisko ðe ʝoˈxoa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Cortés department|Cortés]]. Its religious composition is majority [[Christians|Christian]], with a lesser presence of Christian [[Evangelic]]s.

The town is located in the region around [[Lake Yojoa]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Vigilancia de la calidad del agua: invirtiendo en el fortalecimiento de las capacidades del personal - OPS/OMS {{!}} Organización Panamericana de la Salud|url=https://www.paho.org/es/noticias/10-9-2021-vigilancia-calidad-agua-invirtiendo-fortalecimiento-capacidades-personal|access-date=2021-11-23|website=www.paho.org|language=es}}</ref>
