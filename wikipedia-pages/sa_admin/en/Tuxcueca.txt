'''Tuxcueca''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 132.4 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2018, the municipality had a total population of 5,765. The largest town of the municipality, [[San Luis Soyatl%C3%A1n]], and the town of Tuxcueca are both located on the south shore of [[Lake Chapala|El Lago de Chapala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Tuxcueca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14107a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 20, 2009|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070517082909/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14107a.htm|archive-date=May 17, 2007}}</ref>
