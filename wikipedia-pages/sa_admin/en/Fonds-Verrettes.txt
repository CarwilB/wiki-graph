'''Fonds-Verrettes''' ({{IPA|fr|fɔ̃ vɛʁɛt}}; {{langx|ht|Fonvèrèt}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Croix-des-Bouquets Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 40,224 inhabitants and lies adjacent to the [[Dominican Republic–Haiti border]].
