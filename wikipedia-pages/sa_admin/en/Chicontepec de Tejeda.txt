'''Chicontepec''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north of the State of Veracruz. It has a surface area of {{convert|978|sqkm|sqmi|0}}. It is located at {{coord|20|58|N|98|10|W|}}.
==Geography==
The municipality of Chicontepec is delimited to the north by [[Tantoyuca]] and [[Ixcatepec]], to the east by [[Temapache]] and [[Tepetzintla, Veracruz|Tepetzintla]], to the south by [[Ixhuatlán de Madero]], and to the west by [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30058a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chicontepec|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070519090459/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30058a.htm|archive-date=2007-05-19|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Chicontepec is very warm all year round with rain in summer.

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[bean]]s and [[Orange (fruit)|oranges]].

==Culture==
Many inhabitants speak [[Huasteca Nahuatl]] and practice indigenous customs.<ref>{{cite web|title=The Value of El Costumbre and Christianity in the Discourse of Nahua Catechists from the Huasteca Region in Veracruz, Mexico, 1970s–2010s|url=https://www.academia.edu/35580492/11delaCruz_WordsandWorlds.pdf|publisher=Academia.edu|author=Abelardo de la Cruz|access-date=22 July 2018}}</ref>

The celebration in honor of [[James the Great|Santiago Apóstol]], Patron of Chicontepec, takes place in July.
