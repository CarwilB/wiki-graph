'''Tapauá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 17,015 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/tapaua/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 89,324&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> making it the third largest municipality in Amazonas and the fifth largest in Brazil, comparable to  [[South Carolina]] or [[Jordan]].

==Geography==
===Conservation===
The municipality contains the {{convert|233864|ha|adj=on}} [[Abufari Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected area.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Abufari|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=194|accessdate=2016-04-24}}</ref>
It contains 30.35% of the {{convert|1008167|ha|adj=on}} [[Piagaçu-Purus Sustainable Development Reserve]], established in 2003.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Piagaçu-Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS Piagaçu-Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5047|accessdate=2016-08-20}}</ref>
The municipality contains about 9% of the [[Balata-Tufari National Forest]], a {{convert|1079670|ha|adj=on}} sustainable use conservation unit created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Balata-Tufari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=FLONA de Balata-Tufari|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1184|accessdate=2016-06-03}}</ref>
The municipality also contains about 94% of the [[Nascentes do Lago Jari National Park]], an {{convert|812745|ha|adj=on}} protected area established in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Iquiri – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=FLONA do Iquiri|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3067|accessdate=2016-06-02}}</ref>
It contains 0.33% of the {{convert|604209|ha|adj=on}} [[Médio Purus Extractive Reserve]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Médio Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Médio Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/uc/4548|accessdate=2016-09-03}}</ref>
It contains 98% of the {{convert|881704|ha|adj=on}} [[Tapauá State Forest]], created in 2009.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES Tapauá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES Tapauá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6607|accessdate=2016-09-02}}</ref>
