'''Marsella ''or Marseille''''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]] in [[Colombia]]. About 30&nbsp;km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]]. In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 17,208.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Marsella, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/marsella |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
It was founded in 1915 by Pedro Pineda, Nepomuceno Correa and José Bedoya, among others.

Initially it was known as Villa Rica and in 1864, by the will of General Tomás Cipriano de Mosquera, it was added: Segovia. On April 8, 1915, Villa Rica de Segovia was renamed Marseille and finally July 18, 1915 was recognized as the date of its foundation as a municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Marsella – Risaralda un destino lleno de vida |url=https://risaraldaundestinollenodevida.com/new/marsella/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |language=es-CO}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Marsella has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average annual temperature of 18&nbsp;°C.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Marsella |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100238/marsella/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>

== Tourism ==
[[File:Casa de la cultura Marsella.jpg|left|thumb|House of culture]]
Marsella is a colonial town is famous for its botanic garden [http://www.jardinbotanicomarsella.com/ Jardin Bontanico Marsella Alejandro Hombolt] which was set up to promote ecological sustainability in the area, with a range of plants and wildlife as well as an education section with hands on science demonstrations of things like parabolic disks for bouncing sounds across the countryside, and the world's first catapult museum, set up in collaboration with local schools to preserve wildlife by getting children to donate their catapults to the museum.

The town also has an historic cemetery [http://marsella-risaralda.gov.co//sitio.shtml?apc=mGNuestro%20Municipio-1-&x=1915486 Cementerio Jesus Maria Estrada], a colonial-style cultural centre and church in the main square and traditional galleria (market).
