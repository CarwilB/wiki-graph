{{Short description|Municipality in Mosquitia}}
'''Pearl Lagoon''' is a municipality located in the [[South Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region]] of [[Nicaragua]].<ref>{{Cite book | url=https://books.google.com/books?id=3x5T23r6nUUC&dq=pearl+lagoon+english+bank&pg=PA289 |title = National Integration and Contested Autonomy: The Caribbean Coast of Nicaragua|isbn = 9780875868233|last1 = Baracco|first1 = Luciano|year = 2011}}</ref> It is named after the [[Pearl Cay Lagoon]], a prominent coastal lagoon within its territory. As of 2022, Pearl Lagoon Municipality had a population of 21,360.

==History==
{{One source|section|date=November 2021}}
[[File:18th century cannon on Pearl Lagoon municipal wharf.jpg|left|thumb|18th century cannon at the Municipal Wharf of Pearl Lagoon]]
Pearl Lagoon, along with the eastern half of present-day Nicaragua, was a [[British protectorate]] from 1655 until 1860, a period when the region was called the [[Mosquito Coast]].

In the period before 1894, eastern Nicaragua was a British protectorate, ruled through a line of Miskito kings. About a century ago, Pearl Lagoon was considered to be the second capital of the [[Mosquito Coast|Moskitian Kingdom]] when the last Miskito King took up residence in the town of Pearl City, the capital of the municipality.

At that time, Pearl Lagoon was called "the Granada of the Mosquitia" by one of the [[conquistadores]] of the Pacific Coast because of its beauty and the booming ship traffic.

An 18th-century cannon situated on the municipality wharf of Pearl Lagoon has an inscription commemorating battles between liberal and conservative factions.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.nicatour.net/es/nicaragua/caribe/laguna_de_perlas.asp|title=Tours hotels packages and transports of Nicaragua by Nicatour|website=www.nicatour.net|access-date=2007-02-19|archive-date=2007-02-12|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070212063441/http://www.nicatour.net/es/nicaragua/caribe/laguna_de_perlas.asp|url-status=dead}}</ref> The cannon was never fired in anger, it was used only for celebrations.

==Culture==
{{One source|section|date=November 2021}}
Most of the inhabitants are [[Creole peoples|Creole]], but a large number of [[Miskito people|Miskito]], [[Mestizo]] and some [[Garifuna people|Garifuna]] are also present. The [[Mestizo]] people started migrating in the late 90s, some from the city of [[El Rama]] and some from the northern part of the municipality of Pearl Lagoon.

Similar to [[Bluefields]], May Pole is a big celebration among the people. Now within the same month of May, people from all over the municipality would come together in the community of Pearl Lagoon to celebrate and share their different cultures and traditions.

The main communities are Haulover, Kakabila, Marshall Point, Pearl Lagoon, Orinoco, La Fe, Raitipura, Brown Bank, [[Tasbapauni]], Set Net Point, Wawashan, San Vicente, Awas and Pedregal.<ref>{{cite web| url = http://www.laprensa.com.ni/archivo/2006/marzo/09/servicios/guiaturistica/90696.shtml| url-status = dead| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070218044514/http://www.laprensa.com.ni/archivo/2006/marzo/09/servicios/guiaturistica/90696.shtml| archive-date = 2007-02-18| title = La Prensa, El Diario de los Nicaraguenses - Servicios - Guía Turística}}</ref>

==Sports==
[[File:The Harry and Maggie Taylor municipal stadium.jpg|left|thumb|Harry and Maggie Taylor Municipal Stadium]]
Pearl Lagoon was the home to four local baseball teams that played during the dry seasons on Sundays. The four teams were: Sweet Pearly, First Stop, The Young Brave, and the Haulover Tigers.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.moon.com/planner/nicaragua/mustsees/pearllagoon.html |title=Pearl Lagoon, Nicaragua |access-date=2007-02-19 |archive-date=2007-01-12 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070112013043/http://www.moon.com/planner/nicaragua/mustsees/pearllagoon.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Although those teams no longer exist, baseball is still one of the main sports in the community which is also played in the dry season on Sundays at the Harry and Maggie Taylor municipal stadium.

==Transportation==
[[File:Highway between Pearl Lagoon and Haulover.jpg|thumb|right|Highway between Pearl Lagoon and Haulover]]
The municipality of Pearl Lagoon is located just north of Bluefields, the capital of the South Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region, and can be reach by boat and Panga boat.

In 2006, the IDR initiated the construction of a new rural road of 17&nbsp;km from [[Kukra Hill]] to Pearl Lagoon. The road is now reconstructed and as of today, it permits direct access to Managua from Pearl Lagoon<ref>http://www.idr.gob.ni/notas%20de%20prensa/laguna%20de%20perla.pdf{{Dead link|date=February 2022 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> and was financed by the [[World Bank]] (WB) through the [[International Development Association]] (IDA).

For more than 10 years, there has been a bus that travels every day from Pearl Lagoon to [[Kukra Hill]] and back. Around three years ago, the owner of that same bus started a new destination directly from Pearl Lagoon to [[Managua]] one time per week.

== Education ==
In Pearl Lagoon, there are three schools. The Beulah Light Burn school which is only for primary and pre-escolar students, the Pearl Lagoon National Institute only for Secondary students and the Pearl Lagoon Academy of Excellence (PLACE) for pre-escolar, primary and secondary students.
