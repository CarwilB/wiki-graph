'''Ñuu Savi''' ([[Mixtec languages|Mixtec]]: "people of the rain"<ref>{{cite web |url=https://mixtec.sdsu.edu/index.html |title= Andua u' (¿Quién es esa gente? Who are these people?) |publisher=[[San Diego State University]] |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. It is located {{convert|85|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Chilpancingo]]. Its creation from the municipality of [[Ayutla de los Libres (municipality)|Ayutla de los Libres]] was approved in 2021 and went into force on 21 May 2022.

==Geography==
The municipality of Ñuu Savi is located in the [[Costa Chica of Guerrero|Costa Chica]] region of eastern Guerrero. It borders the municipalities of [[Acatepec]] to the north, [[San Luis Acatlán (municipality)|San Luis Acatlán]] to the east, [[Cuautepec (municipality)|Cuautepec]] to the south, and Ayutla de los Libres to the west. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|361.94|km2}}.<ref name=creation/>

Ñuu Savi has a [[subhumid temperate climate]] with rain in the summer. Average temperatures range between {{convert|20|and(-)|28|C}}, and average annual precipitation is between {{convert|1300|and(-)|2000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/12/12012.pdf |title=Ayutla de los Libres, Guerrero |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref>

==History==
[[Coapinola]], the municipal seat of Ñuu Savi, is the oldest settlement in the area, and was once a centre of [[Mixtec]] local government.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Betancourt Radilla |first1=Rosendo |last2=Tizapa Lucena |first2=Emiliano |date=21 June 2022 |title=Del gobierno ñuu'savi prehispánico, la lucha por la educación bilingüe, al sistema propio de elección |url=https://suracapulco.mx/impreso/1/del-gobierno-nuusavi-prehispanico-la-lucha-por-la-educacion-bilingue-al-sistema-propio-de-eleccion/ |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref> The area fell under control of the [[Mexica Empire]] in 1487 and was incorporated into the province of Ayotlán (present-day Ayutla) on the border with [[Yopitzinco]], the territory of the [[:es:Cultura yope|Yope people]].<ref name=oeem>{{cite journal |last1=Tapia Gómez |first1=José Carmen |last2=Días Vázquez |first2=Rosalba |date=January–June 2021 |title=Hilario Ramírez Morales y la OEEM. Resistencia cultural mixteca en Ayutla de los Libres, Guerrero |language=es |trans-title=Hilario Ramírez Morales and the OEEM. Mixteca Cultural Resistance in Ayutla de los Libres, Guerrero |journal=Revista Iberoamericana de las Ciencias Sociales y Humanísticas |url=https://www.ricsh.org.mx/index.php/RICSH/article/view/241/1037 |volume=10 |issue=19 |pages=238–260 |doi=10.23913/ricsh.v10i19.241 |access-date=20 November 2023 |doi-access=free}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Schmal |first=John P. |url=http://www.somosprimos.com/schmal/guerreroindigenous.htm |title=Indigenous Guerrero: A Remnant of the Aztec Empire |publisher=[[San Diego State University]] |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref>

The perpetual marginalization of the Mixtec inhabitants of Ayutla in the following centuries ultimately led to a political awakening and demands for indigenous self-government. This process began in the 1970s through the efforts of local activists such as [[Hilario Ramírez Morales]], who was assassinated in 1981.<ref name=oeem/> It gained momentum from the [[Zapatista uprising]] of 1994, which led to reprisals by the Mexican government, including incidents of [[forced sterilization]],<ref name=kau>{{cite news |last=Sirenio Pioquinto |first=Kau |translator-last=Noyes |translator-first=Allana |date=21 December 2015 |title=Indigenous peoples of Ayutla, dying of neglect |url=https://www.americas.org/indigenous-peoples-of-ayutla-both-harassed-and-neglected/ |publisher=MIRA |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref> and the massacre of eleven indigenous leaders in El Charco by the [[Mexican Army]] in 1998.<ref>{{cite web |date=5 December 2001 |title=Military Injustice |url=https://www.hrw.org/report/2001/12/05/military-injustice/mexicos-failure-punish-army-abuses |publisher=[[Human Rights Watch]] |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref><ref name=sur2022>{{cite news |last1=Betancourt Radilla |first1=Rosendo |last2=Tizapa Lucena |first2=Emiliano |date=27 June 2022 |title=Con la creación del nuevo municipio Ñuu' Savi se arranca el corazón del gobierno indígena en Ayutla |url=https://suracapulco.mx/con-la-creacion-del-nuevo-municipio-nuu-savi-se-arranca-el-corazon-del-gobierno-indigena-en-ayutla/ |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref> Security improved in the area after 2012, when the local community established its own [[Grupos de autodefensa comunitaria|self-defence group]] affiliated with the {{ill|Coordinadora Regional de Autoridades Comunitarias-Policía Comunitaria|es|lt=CRAC–PC}}.<ref name=kau/>

A further step toward self-government took place in 2018, when Ayutla became the first municipality in Guerrero to hold nonpartisan elections governed by [[customary law]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Tabares Merino |first=Gema |date=July–December 2022 |title=Del H. Ayuntamiento a la Honorable Casa de los Pueblos: Concejo Municipal Comunitario de Ayutla de los Libres |language=es |trans-title=From the H. Townhall to the Honorable People’s House: Municipal Community Council of Ayutla de los Libres |journal=Alteridades |url=https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S0188-70172022000200059 |volume=32 |issue=64 |pages=59–70 |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref> After more consultation with the local population, on 31 August 2021 the [[Congress of Guerrero|Guerrero state legislature]] approved the creation of the municipality of Ñuu Savi comprising the Mixtec communities of Ayutla de los Libres.<ref name=creation/> The state constitutional amendment establishing the municipality was passed on 13 January 2022 and went into force on 21 May 2022.<ref name=sur2022/><ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=20 May 2022 |title=DECRETO NÚMERO 161 POR EL QUE SE APRUEBA LA ADICIÓN DE LOS NUEVOS MUNICIPIOS DE SANTA CRUZ DEL RINCÓN, SAN NICOLÁS, ÑUU SAVI Y LAS VIGAS AL ARTÍCULO 27 DE LA CONSTITUCIÓN POLÍTICA DEL ESTADO LIBRE Y SOBERANO DE GUERRERO. EN EL LUGAR QUE CONFORME AL ORDEN ALFABÉTICO LES CORRESPONDE. |url=https://periodicooficial.guerrero.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/P.O-40-20-MAYO-20221.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Guerrero |pages=9–24 |access-date=21 November 2023}}</ref>

==Administration==
Ñuu Savi will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2024. An interim administration was appointed in August 2023, led by municipal president Donaciano Morales Porfirio.<ref>{{cite web |date=17 August 2023 |url=https://congresogro.gob.mx/63/inicio/2023/08/17/designa-el-congreso-al-ayuntamiento-instituyente-del-nuevo-municipio-de-nuu-savi/ |title=DESIGNA EL CONGRESO AL AYUNTAMIENTO INSTITUYENTE DEL NUEVO MUNICIPIO DE ÑUU SAVI |language=es |website=Congress of Guerrero |accessdate=20 November 2023}}</ref> The municipal government of San Nicolás comprises a [[municipal president]], a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and six trustees (''regidores'').<ref>{{cite news |last=Chávez |first=Lourdes |date=16 November 2023 |url=https://suracapulco.mx/impreso/2/aprueba-iepc-composicion-de-ayuntamientos-incluidos-los-cuatro-nuevos-municipios/ |title=Aprueba IEPC composición de ayuntamientos, incluidos los cuatro nuevos municipios |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |location=Chilpancingo |accessdate=24 November 2023}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Ñuu Savi recorded a population of 11,099 inhabitants.<ref name=creation/> The municipal seat of Coapinola recorded a population of 387 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=coapinola/> The most populous localities in the municipality are Ahuacachahue and La Concordia, which recorded populations of 1505 and 1125 inhabitants respectively in the 2020 Census.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=120120003 |title=Ahuacachahue (Ndog'yo Itún Tichi), Ayutla de los Libres, Guerrero (120120003) |language=es |website=México en Cifras |publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=20 November 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=120120015 |title=La Concordia, Ayutla de los Libres, Guerrero (120120015) |language=es |website=México en Cifras |publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=20 November 2023}}</ref>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Ñuu Savi is one of the poorest municipalities in Guerrero. It lacks a hospital, and only primary and ''[[telesecundaria]]'' schooling are available.<ref>{{cite news |date=25 August 2023 |title=Se instala hoy el nuevo municipio Ñuu' Savi: "vamos a nacer como uno de los más pobres" |url=https://suracapulco.mx/se-instala-hoy-el-nuevo-municipio-nuu-savi-vamos-a-nacer-como-uno-de-los-mas-pobres/ |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |access-date=20 November 2023}}</ref> The local economy is based on [[subsistence agriculture]]: corn, beans and sugarcane are the main crops.<ref name=kau/>
