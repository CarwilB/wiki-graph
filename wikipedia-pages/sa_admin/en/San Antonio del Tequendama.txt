'''San Antonio del Tequendama''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Tequendama Province]] part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The municipality is located along the ''Serranía de Subía'' in the Tena Valley and borders [[Tena, Cundinamarca|Tena]] and [[Bojacá]] in the north, Bojacá and [[Soacha]] in the east, [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] and [[El Colegio]] in the west and in the south Soacha and [[Granada, Cundinamarca|Granada]].<ref name=websiteSanTequendama>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.sanantoniodeltequendama-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website San Antonio del Tequendama]{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name ''Tequendama'' means in the [[Chibcha language|Muysccubun]], the language of the Muisca: "he who precipitated downward".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://transcripcionesreveladoras.blogspot.com.co/2013/07/historia-de-colombia-la-civilizacion.html Etymology Tequendama] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170813184313/http://transcripcionesreveladoras.blogspot.com.co/2013/07/historia-de-colombia-la-civilizacion.html |date=2017-08-13 }}</ref>

== History ==
This municipality was formed on the site of Cubsió, a place of reunion of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] originally from [[Bojacá]] and [[Madrid, Cundinamarca|Serrezuela]]. Cubsió was already populated by indigenous peoples from the [[Herrera Period]] with the oldest evidence dated at 1865 years [[Before Present|BP]] (2nd century AD) of a site a few meters from the current course of the [[Bogotá River]].<ref>{{cite thesis |author=Argüello García, Pedro María |year=2015 |type=PhD |title=Subsistence economy and chiefdom emergence in the Muisca area. A study of the Valle de Tena | url=http://d-scholarship.pitt.edu/24491/1/Arguello_P._Subsistence_economy.pdf | publisher=[[University of Pittsburgh]] |pages=1–193}}</ref>

Around 1470 the terrains of San Antonio de Tequendama were ruled by the ''[[cacique]]'' of [[Fusagasugá]], who fought with the [[Panche people|Panche]] against [[Saguamanchica]] in the [[Battle of Pasca]].<ref name=websiteSanTequendama/>

In 1539, [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] organised an expedition down the Bogotá River where he sent his soldier [[Juan de Céspedes Ruiz|Juan de Céspedes]].<ref name=websiteSanTequendama/>

The modern town was founded by Críspulo Corredor in 1857. This municipality has changed names multiples times, San Antonio, San Antonio de Tena, and its actual name San Antonio del Tequendama.<ref name=websiteSanTequendama/>

== Tourism ==
The [[Chicaque Natural Park]] is an [[ecotourism|ecotouristic]] area within San Antonio del Tequendama.<ref name=websiteSanTequendama/>
