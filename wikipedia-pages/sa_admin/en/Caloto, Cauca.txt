'''Caloto''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It was founded on June 29, 1543 by Sebastián de Belalcázar.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pasado, Presente y Futuro |url=https://www.caloto-cauca.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Pasado-Presente-y-Futuro.aspx |access-date=2023-02-11 |website=www.caloto-cauca.gov.co}}</ref>
