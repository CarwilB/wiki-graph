'''Pehuajó Partido''' ({{IPA|es|pewa'xo}}) is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeast  of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 40,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4531|km2|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Pehuajó]], which is around {{convert|389|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
