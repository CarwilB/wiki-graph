'''Congonhas''' ('''Congonhas do Campo''' until 1948) is a historical [[Brazil]]ian city located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. It is situated {{convert|90|km}} south from [[Belo Horizonte]], the capital of state of Minas Gerais, by the highway BR-040. As of 2025, the city had a population of 55,272.<ref name="IBGE" />

Located in a region rich in iron ore, it is surrounded by several mining dams. Two of them overflowed in January 2026. 
<ref name="ER"> {{cite news| url = https://entrepriseromande.ch/web/er/w/br%C3%A9sil-il-pleut-des-barrages-miniers-d%C3%A9bordent%E2%80%A6 |language=fr | title = Il pleut, des barrages miniers débordent | author = [[Pierre Cormon]]| newspaper =Entreprise romande| date = 13 February 2026| accessdate = 17 February 2026}}</ref>

It is a well preserved historic city and retains the characteristics of a [[baroque architecture|baroque]] city, with its [[Church (building)|church]]es, [[building]]s and [[museum]]s.

Other historical cities in Minas Gerais are [[Ouro Preto]], [[São João del-Rei]], [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]], [[Mariana, Minas Gerais|Mariana]], [[Tiradentes, Minas Gerais|Tiradentes]] and [[Sabará]].

==Sanctuary of Bom Jesus de Matosinhos==
The city is known for its [[basilica]] - the {{lang|pt|[[Santuário do Bom Jesus de Matosinhos]]}}, commissioned by Portuguese adventurer and miner Feliciano Mendes in 1757 and completed in 1775.<ref>{{Cite book|title = South America on a Shoestring|url = https://books.google.com/books?id=rnFth62xGKcC|publisher = Lonely Planet|date = 2007-01-01|isbn = 9781741044430|language = en|first = Danny|last = Palmerlee}}</ref><ref name = "unesco">{{cite web |url= http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/334 |title= Sanctuary of Bom Jesus do Congonhas |website= UNESCO World Heritage List |publisher= UNESCO| access-date= 20 Mar 2021}}</ref> In the ramped [[Courtyard|forecourt]] of the basilica are twelve [[soapstone]] sculptures depicting Twelve Prophets, crafted by [[Aleijadinho]], one of the best artists in the [[baroque]] style in the world.<ref name = "unesco" /> The [[Twelve Prophets of Aleijadinho|twelve sculptures of old testament prophets]] around the terrace are considered one of his finest works. Around the forecourt are six chapels with painted wooden scenes depicting the [[Stations of the Cross]]. In 1985 the Sanctuary was named a [[World Heritage Site]] by [[UNESCO]] for its exemplary Baroque architecture and art.<ref name = "unesco" />
