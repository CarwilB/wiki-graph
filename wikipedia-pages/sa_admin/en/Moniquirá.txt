'''Moniquirá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the subregion of the [[Ricaurte Province]]. It is known for its "bocadillos" and "panelitas de leche" (both little sweet cakes). Moniquirá borders [[San José de Pare]] in the north, [[Togüí, Boyacá|Togüí]] and [[Arcabuco]] in the east, [[Gachantivá]] and [[Santa Sofía, Boyacá|Santa Sofia]] in the south and in the west with the department of [[Santander Department|Santander]].<ref name=websiteMoniquira>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20140310085148/http://www.moniquira-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Moniquirá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca, Moniquirá means "place of bath".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Moniquirá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Before the arrival of the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish]] [[conquistador]]es, Moniquirá was who were ruled by the ''[[cacique]]'' of [[Susa, Cundinamarca|Susa]], part of the [[Muisca Confederation]]. [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] passed through Ubaza, part of Moniquirá on March 16, 1537.<ref name=websiteMoniquira/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Moniquirá is [[agriculture]]; [[coffee]], [[sugar cane]] and [[maize]]. The bocadillos industry provides employment for over 800 people.<ref name=websiteMoniquira/>
