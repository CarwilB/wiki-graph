'''Gameleira de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in central [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  The population in 2020 was 3,880 in a total area of 595 square kilometers.

==Geographical Information==
The distance to the state capital, [[Goiânia]], is 99&nbsp;km.  Highway connections from [[Goiânia]] are made by BR-457 / GO-010 / [[Bonfinópolis]] / [[Leopoldo de Bulhões]] / [[Silvânia]] / GO-437. (18&nbsp;km unpaved).

Gameleira has municipal boundaries with [[Silvânia]], [[Abadiânia]], and [[Anápolis]].

'''Agriculture'''

Agriculture is based on cattle raising and growing of [[Maize|corn]], [[sugarcane]], [[rice]], and [[soybeans]].

In 2004 the cattle herd was 20,250 head, of which 6,000 were milking cows.  The main agricultural products in planted area were soybeans (25,000 hectares), corn (1,200 hectares), beans, sorghum, tomatoes, and citrus fruits. 
*Number of farms, total area and workers: 529 on 61,520 ha. with 1,300 workers.
*Land distribution: 32,000 ha. were planted in permanent or perennial crops and 19,511 ha. were natural pasture.  91 farms had a total of 222 tractors (2006).
