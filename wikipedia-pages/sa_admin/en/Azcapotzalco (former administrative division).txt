'''Azcapotzalco''' was a [[municipalities of Mexico|Mexican municipality]]. It was disestablished in 1928, when the municipalities of the [[Mexican Federal District|Federal District]] were replaced by [[boroughs of the Mexican Federal District|boroughs]] (''delegaciones''). There is now a [[Azcapotzalco|borough of the same name]].
