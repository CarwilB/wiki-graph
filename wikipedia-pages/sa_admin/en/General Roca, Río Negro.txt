'''General Roca''' is a city in the northeast of the [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Río Negro Province|Río Negro]], northern [[Patagonia]]. It was founded on September 1, 1879, by Colonel [[Lorenzo Vintter]], on the order of [[War Minister]] [[Julio A. Roca]],<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.generalroca.gov.ar/historical-tourism/|title=Historical Tourism : Municipio General Roca|language=en|access-date=2018-12-12}}</ref> during the [[Conquest of the Desert]]. The place of the first settlement was known by native [[mapuche]] people as Fiske Menuco, which means "deep water".<ref name=":0" /> It was destroyed in 1899 by a flooding of the [[Río Negro (Argentina)|Río Negro]], and had to be rebuilt {{convert|5|km}} northwest in higher lands.

Its present population is approximately 102,750 (according to {{census-ar|2022}}), making it the second most populated city in the province after [[Bariloche]], and the second most important in the [[Río Negro (Argentina)#Alto Valle|Alto Valle]] after [[Neuquén]]. The main activity around the city is the intensive agriculture under [[irrigation]], which made possible an intense agro-industrial activity. The main crops are [[pear]]s and [[apple]]s. The city hosts the annual National Festival of the Apple, which is held in early February.

General Roca, named after [[Julio A. Roca]], is located {{convert|1200|km}} from [[Buenos Aires]], {{convert|505|km}} from [[Bahía Blanca]], {{convert|513|km}} from [[Viedma]] and {{convert|400|km}} from the deepwater port of [[San Antonio Este]]. The city is connected to the east by [[National Route 22 (Argentina)|Argentine National Route 22]], and is also crossed by provincial routes 6 and 65, which connect it to other cities in the Alto Valle, with the south of the province, and with the [[La Pampa Province]]. Through the centre of the city drives the wide rail train lane that joins [[Zapala]] with Bahía Blanca and Buenos Aires. The local airport connects General Roca with other points of the country, mainly with Buenos Aires and [[Mendoza, Argentina|Mendoza]]. Nonetheless, due to lack of maintenance, the airport has not been used since the year 2000.

Together with [[Villa Regina]], [[Allen, Río Negro|Allen]], [[Cinco Saltos]], [[Cipolletti]], [[Neuquén]] and many other smaller towns, they constitute the ''lineal'' urbanization of the ''Alto Valle'' of the [[Río Negro (Argentina)|Negro River]]. The city is surrounded by a patchwork of [[irrigated land]] totaling {{convert|100,000|ha|acre}}. Besides apple and pear [[orchard]]s, there are vineyards and other establishments producing peaches and a variety of other fruits and vegetables. Within the industrial sector, the fruit-refrigerating storehouses, fruit and vegetable packaging, and other agriculture-related industries stand out.

=== "Old Town" ===

The place of the first settlement where General Roca was founded, near Río Negro shoreline, is known as "Old Town". Several buildings dating back to the time before the flooding and subsequent move of the town can be still visited:

* San Miguel School
* María Auxiliadora Sanctuary
* Old María Auxiliadora School
* Villegas Square
* Stefenelli Train Station<ref name=":0" />

==2016 meteor event==

In the afternoon of 20 June 2016, a series of loud blasts could be heard in General Roca, causing buildings to shake and windows to rattle, but police, firefighters and emergency workers could not find any evidence of an explosion or natural calamity. No damage was reported. The [[Astronomy|astronomical]] [[observatory]] in nearby [[Neuquén]] later revealed that the cause had been a [[meteor]] that burst in the atmosphere over the city, at an estimated speed of {{convert|2400|kph|mph}}. Astronomer Roberto Figueroa estimated that the meteor measured approximately {{convert|12|m|ft}} across and probably broke on atmospheric entry, falling mostly as ash, but some larger fragments could have reached the lower atmosphere and caused an audible boom.<ref name="toi2016">{{cite news |last=Agence France Presse |url=http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/tech/tech-news/What-was-that-explosion-A-meteor-it-turns-out/articleshow/53341831.cms |title=What was that explosion? A meteor, it turns out |work=[[Times of India]] |date=2016-07-22 |access-date=2016-07-22}}</ref>

==Geography==
===Climate===
General Roca has a [[semi-arid climate]] with warm to hot summers, combined with cool winters. Rainfall is sparse year round, resulting in a relative arid influence of the climate, being located in the [[Andes|Andean]] [[rain shadow]].
