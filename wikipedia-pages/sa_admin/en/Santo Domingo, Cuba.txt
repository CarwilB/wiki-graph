'''Santo Domingo''' is a municipality and town in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]].

It was founded in 1819<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1879.

==Geography==
The town is divided into the wards (''[[barrio]]s'') of Este (east) and Oeste (west). The municipality includes the villages of Álvarez, Amaro, Arenas, Baracaldo, Bermejal, [[Cascajal]], Cayo Bejuco, Cerrito, [[George Washington (Santo Domingo, Cuba)|George Washington]], Jiquiabo, La Criolla, Las Casimbas, [[Manacas]], [[Mordazo]], Puerto Escondido, Río, Rodrigo, Sabino Hernández, San Bartolomé, San Juan de Amaro, San Marcos, Ulacia (Carlos Baliño), Ventiseis de Julio, Yabucito. Until the 1977 municipal reform, Santo Domingo included Jicotea too, nowadays part of [[Ranchuelo]] municipality.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vsanto/index.htm|title=Santo Domingo|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-09|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{OSM|r|5884805|Santo Domingo}}</ref> 

The wards ("consejos populares" in spanish) of the municipality are [[Cascajal]], [[Mordazo]], [[Sabino Hernández, Cuba|Sabino Hernández]], [[Manacas, Cuba|Manacas]], [[George Washington, Cuba|Washington]], [[La Palma y El Jardín]] (main town of Santo Domingo), [[26 de Julio (Santo Domingo)|26 de Julio]], [[Baliño, Cuba|Baliño]], and [[Rodrigo-Amaro]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sobre Nosotros |url=https://www.somossantodomingo.gob.cu/nuestra-region/sobre-el-municipio/sobre-nosotros |archive-date=2023-05-27 |website=Somos Santo Domingo |language=es |access-date=2022-10-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230527024541/https://somossantodomingo.gob.cu/nuestra-region/sobre-el-municipio/sobre-nosotros |url-status=dead }}</ref> 

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Santo Domingo had a population of 46,984.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|883|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-09}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|53|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
