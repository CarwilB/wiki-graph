'''Carrasco Bonito''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Carrasco Bonito's population was at 4,130 in 2020 and it has an area of 193&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cod.ibge.gov.br/B3D]</ref>

The municipality contains 94% of the {{convert|9280|ha|adj=on}} [[Extremo Norte do Tocantins Extractive Reserve]], created in 1992.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Extremo Norte do Tocantins – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Extremo Norte do Tocantins|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
