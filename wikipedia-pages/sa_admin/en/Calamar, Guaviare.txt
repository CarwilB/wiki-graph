'''Calamar''' is a town and municipality in the [[Guaviare Department]], [[Colombia]].

Ten former members of [[Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia]] (FARC) were killed in a military bombing on Calamar on March 3, [[2021 in Colombia|2021]]. Three “dissidents” were captured.<ref>{{cite news |title=Ten rebels killed, three captured in Colombia military bombing |url=https://www.reuters.com/article/us-colombia-rebels/ten-rebels-killed-three-captured-in-colombia-military-bombing-idUSKCN2AV01Q |access-date=March 4, 2021 |work=Reuters |date=3 March 2021 |language=en}}</ref>
