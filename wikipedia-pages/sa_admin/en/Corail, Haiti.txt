'''Corail''' ({{IPA|fr|kɔʁaj|-|Fr-Paris--corail.ogg}}; {{langx|ht|Koray}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Corail Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 19,566 inhabitants in 2015.

Locations within Corail commune include:
[[Corail City|Corail]], [[D'lagon]], [[Fond Roge]], [[Gros Basin, Corail, Haiti|Gros Basin]], [[Layon Fon]], [[Miyu, Grand'Anse|Miyu]], [[Nan Miel]] and [[Troupeau Corail Haiti|Troupeau]].
