'''Santo Antônio do Palma''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning "Saint Anthony of the palm") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northern part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,123 (2020 est.) in an area of 126.09&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2KXK Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 669 m.
