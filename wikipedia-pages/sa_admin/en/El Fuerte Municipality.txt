'''El Fuerte''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sinaloa]] in northwestern [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128142000/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |archivedate=January 28, 2007 }}</ref> Its seat is [[El Fuerte, Sinaloa|El Fuerte]] city.

== Political subdivision ==
El Fuerte Municipality is subdivided in 7 ''sindicaturas'':
*Mochicahui
*Charay
*San Blas
*Tehueco
*Tetaroba
*Chinobampo
*Jahuara II
