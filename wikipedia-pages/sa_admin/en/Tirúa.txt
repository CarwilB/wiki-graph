'''Tirúa''' ({{IPA|es|tiˈɾu.a}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of towns in Chile|town]] in [[Arauco Province]], [[Biobío Region]]. The [[2010 Chile earthquake]] led to a permanent uplift of Tirúa of about 180 cm above sea level, the highest recorded uplift related to the earthquake.<ref name=Quezadaetal2020>{{Cite journal|title=Unexpected coseismic surface uplift at Tirúa-Mocha Island area of south Chile before and during the Mw 8.8 Maule 2010 earthquake: a possible upper plate splay fault|journal=[[Andean Geology]]|url=http://www.andeangeology.cl/index.php/revista1/article/view/V47n2-3057/pdf|last=Quezada|first=Jorge|volume=47|last2=Jaque|first2=Edilia|publisher=[[National Geology and Mining Service]]|issue=2|doi=10.5027/andgeoV47n2-3057|year=2020|last3=Catalán|first3=Nicole|last4=Belmonte|first4=Arturo|last5=Fernández|first5=Alfonso|last6=Isla|first6=Federico|doi-access=free|hdl=11336/128966|hdl-access=free}}</ref> This caused a large islet to form at the mouth of [[Tirúa River]] next to the town.<ref name=Quezadaetal2020/> The [[Misión Jesuita Mapuche]] is based in Tirúa.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Tirúa spans an area of {{convert|624.4|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 9,664 inhabitants (5,028 men and 4,636 women). Of these, 2,508 (26%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,156 (74%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 10.6% (928 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Tirúa is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2017-2021 alcalde is Adolfo Millabur Ñancuil (Ind).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Tirúa is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Manuel Monsalve Benavides ([[Socialist Party of Chile]]) and Iván Norambuena Farías ([[Independent Democratic Union]]) as part of the 46th electoral district, together with [[Lota, Chile|Lota]], [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], [[Arauco, Chile|Arauco]], [[Curanilahue]], [[Los Álamos]], [[Cañete, Chile|Cañete]] and [[Contulmo]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Felipe Harboe|Felipe Harboe Bascuñán]] from [[Partido por la Democracia|PPD]] and [[Víctor Pérez Varela]] of [[Unión Demócrata Independiente|UDI]] as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).
