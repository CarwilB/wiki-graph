'''La Merced District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Aija Province]] of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]]. It is located between 77°43 ´ 57 " S and 77°35 ´ 51 " W with an average altitude of 3,272 m.s.n.m. Its population was 1582 as of the [[2017 Peru Census|2017 census]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados Definitivos de los Censos Nacionales 2017: Ancash |url=https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1552/ |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] |access-date=January 12, 2023 |page=I: 128 |language=Spanish}}</ref>
