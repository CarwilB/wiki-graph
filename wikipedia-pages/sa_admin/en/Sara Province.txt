'''Sara''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[Portachuelo]].

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
