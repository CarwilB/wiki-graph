'''General Bernardino Caballero''' (or simply '''Caballero''') is a town and a [[district]] in the [[Paraguarí Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is named after [[Bernardino Caballero]], President of Paraguay from 1880 until 1886.

==Location==

Caballero is located in the north-west region of the [[Paraguarí department]], 101&nbsp;km from [[Asunción]], the capital of the Paraguay; and about 30&nbsp;km from the city of [[Paraguarí]].

To get there one should take the [[Route 1 (Paraguay)|national route number 1]] to [[Paraguarí]], and there take a detour to Gral. Bernardino Caballero.

==Geography==

The topography of the district is characterized by hills, streams and swamps. Borders are the following:

* At North it borders with the districts of [[Valenzuela, Paraguay|Valenzuela]] and [[San José de los Arroyos]].
* At South it borders with the [[Acahay]] and [[La Colmena (Paraguarí)|La Colmena]] districts.
* At East it borders with [[Ybytimí]] district.
* At West it borders with [[Sapucaí]] district.
