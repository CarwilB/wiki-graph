The '''Juan José Mora Municipality''' is one of the 14 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Carabobo]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 69,236.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref>  The town of [[Morón, Venezuela|Morón]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Juan José Mora Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Carabobo.zip {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Juan José Mora Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 65,239 (up from 57,701 in 2000).  This amounts to 2.9% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|144.02|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Juan José Mora Municipality is Matson Caldera, elected on November 23, 2008, with 52% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=07&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref>  He replaced José Gregorio Frías shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Morón and Urama.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
