'''Algarrobo''' ({{IPA|es|alɣaˈroβo}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[San Antonio Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]]. Located on the country's central coast, it is a popular summer resort for the population of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]]. It is an upscale community near [[El Quisco]] and [[Valparaíso]]. The [[San Alfonso del Mar]] resort, located north of the city, is home to the world's largest outdoor pool.<ref name="ngbig">Big Dipper, [[National Geographic Magazine]], April 2010, Technology, page 30</ref>

[[Islote Pájaros Niños]] is a nature sanctuary on a rocky islet close to the shore, which is home to colonies of [[Humboldt penguin]]s, [[Peruvian pelican]]s, and [[Guanay cormorant]]s.<ref name = Birdlife>BirdLife International (2023) [http://datazone.birdlife.org/site/factsheet/isla-pájaro-niño-de-algarrobo-iba-chile/ Important Bird Areas factsheet: Isla Pájaro Niño de Algarrobo]. Retrieved 24 April 2023.</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Algarrobo spans an area of {{convert|175.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 8,601 inhabitants (4,369 men and 4,232 women). Of these, 6,628 (77.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 1,973 (22.9%) lived in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 44.1% (2,633 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Algarrobo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Jorge Luis Pizarro Romero. The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Hipólito Aravena Escobar. ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* Manuel Catalan Aranda. (PDC)
* Javier Fuentes Torrealba ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Luís Núñez Berríos. (RN)
* Antonieta Sandoval Solís. (PDC)
* José Yanez Maldonado ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Algarrobo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by María José Hoffmann ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Víctor Torres (PDC) as part of the 15th electoral district (together with [[San Antonio, Chile|San Antonio]], [[Santo Domingo, Chile|Santo Domingo]], [[Cartagena, Chile|Cartagena]], [[El Tabo]], [[El Quisco]] and [[Casablanca, Chile|Casablanca]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Francisco Chahuán Chahuán (RN) and [[Ricardo Lagos Weber]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 6th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Coast).
