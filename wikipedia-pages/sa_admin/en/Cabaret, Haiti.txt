'''Cabaret''' ({{IPA|fr|kabaʁɛ|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-WikiLucas00-cabaret.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Kabarè}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Arcahaie Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2009, the commune had 62,063 inhabitants.<ref name="IHSI"/> During his dictatorship, [[François Duvalier]] renamed it '''Duvalierville''' ({{IPA|fr|dyvaljevil}}; {{langx|ht|Divalyevil}}); in 1961 a construction project was begun. Construction eventually stopped, but the name was kept until Duvalier's successor, his son [[Jean-Claude Duvalier]], fled the country in 1986.
