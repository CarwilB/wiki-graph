'''Catas Altas''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] [[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Itabira]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 5,421.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/catas-altas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
