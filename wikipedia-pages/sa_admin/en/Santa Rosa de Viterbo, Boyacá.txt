'''Santa Rosa de Viterbo''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the [[Tundama Province]], a subregion of Boyacá.

[[Viterbite]], not a mineral but a mixture of [[allophane]] and [[wavellite]], occurs in and was named after this municipality.

== History ==
According to the municipality's archived historical summary, Santa Rosa de Viterbo was founded on 19 May 1690.<ref name="mun-archive">{{cite web |title=Nuestro Municipio |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160204230603/http://www.santarosadeviterbo-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |publisher=Alcaldía de Santa Rosa de Viterbo |language=es |access-date=2026-02-09}}</ref> In 1905 it became the capital of the former Department of Tundama, and in 1908 it became the capital of the former Department of Santa Rosa.<ref name="mun-archive" />

== The "Aerolito" ==
{{main|Santa Rosa de Viterbo meteorite}}
Municipal historical records state that the aerolite was found near Tocavita in early 1810 by Cecilia Corredor, served for years as an anvil in a local blacksmith shop, and was installed on a stone column in the main square on 8 September 1877.<ref name="mun-archive" />

The National Museum of Colombia reports that Mariano Eduardo de Rivero and Jean-Baptiste Boussingault acquired it for the museum in 1823, and that the main mass reached the museum in 1906.<ref name="mnc-aerolito">{{cite web |title=Impresión 3D del aerolito de Santa Rosa de Viterbo |url=https://www.museonacional.gov.co/micrositios1/2021/Museomediaticas/Impresion_3D.html |publisher=Museo Nacional de Colombia |language=es |access-date=2026-02-09}}</ref> The same source describes the Bogotá specimen as an ataxite, classified by the Smithsonian in 1940, composed primarily of iron and nickel, with a mass of 411 kg.<ref name="mnc-aerolito" />

Municipal records indicate transfer to Bogotá during the presidency of Rafael Reyes in 1908.<ref name="mun-archive" /> Radio Ambulante's archival reporting describes this transfer and the later dispersal of fragments to international collections, including Harvard University, the Smithsonian Institution, the Natural History Museum in London, and the Field Museum in Chicago.<ref name="ra-treasure">{{cite web |title=The Treasure That Fell From The Sky |url=https://radioambulante.org/en/translation/the-treasure-that-fell-from-the-sky-translation |website=Radio Ambulante |date=2024-04-30 |access-date=2026-02-09}}</ref> The Meteoritical Bulletin Database lists Santa Rosa as an iron meteorite (IC) with a total known weight of 825 kg.<ref name="metbull">{{cite web |title=Santa Rosa |url=https://www.lpi.usra.edu/meteor/metbull.php?code=23266 |website=Meteoritical Bulletin Database |publisher=Meteoritical Society / Lunar and Planetary Institute |access-date=2026-02-09}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
DANE's 2025 municipal projections estimate 13,468 inhabitants in Santa Rosa de Viterbo (8,392 in the municipal seat and 5,076 in rural population centers).<ref name="dane-pped" />

== Institutions ==
The National Police's Escuela Rafael Reyes is located in Santa Rosa de Viterbo, Boyacá.<ref name="pnc-reyes">{{cite web |title=Escuela Rafael Reyes |url=https://www.policia.gov.co/directorio/escuela-rafael-reyes |publisher=Policía Nacional de Colombia |language=es |access-date=2026-02-09}}</ref>
