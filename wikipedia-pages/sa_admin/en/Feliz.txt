'''Feliz''' is a municipality in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. (Municipalities, in this case, are sections of cities/villages in Brazil, which are like counties in the United States of America.)  The population of Feliz was 13,640 as of 2020. Feliz was founded in February 1959. The area of Feliz is approximately {{convert|37|mi2}}. Feliz is in the Brazilian region of [[South Region, Brazil|Sul]], which is the southernmost region of Brazil.
