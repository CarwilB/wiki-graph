'''Canchayllo District''' is one of thirty-four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Jauja Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its seat is Canchayllo.

== Geography ==
The district lies in the [[Nor Yauyos-Cochas Landscape Reserve]]. The [[Paryaqaqa mountain range|Paryaqaqa]] mountain range traverses the district. The highest mountain of the district is [[Paryaqaqa]] (Tulluqutu) at {{convert|5750|m|ft|0}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref name=escale/>
