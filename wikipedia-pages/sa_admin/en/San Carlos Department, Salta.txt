'''San Carlos''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], in [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|5125|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the east with the [[La Viña Department]], to the southeast with [[Cafayate Department]], to the northeast with [[Chicoana Department]], to the north with [[Cachi Department]], to the west with [[Molinos Department]] and to the south and southwest with the province of [[Catamarca Province|Catamarca]].
