'''Coruripe''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kʊɾʊˈɾipi]}}) is a  [[municipality]] located in the southern coast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 57,294 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/coruripe/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 913&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref> It is the largest municipality in Alagoas by area, but among the largest municipalities of each Brazilian state, it is the smallest. It is situated at the edge of Coruripe river.
