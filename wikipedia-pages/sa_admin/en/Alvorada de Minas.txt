'''Alvorada de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. {{as of|2020}} its population is estimated to be 3,605.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/alvorada-de-minas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Diamantina]].
