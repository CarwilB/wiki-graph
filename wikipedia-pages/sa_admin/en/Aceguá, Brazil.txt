'''Aceguá''' ({{IPA|pt|a.seˈɡwa|lang|Br-Aceguá.oga}}) is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

== Location ==
It is located in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], on the border with [[Uruguay]].

== Paleontology ==
Here are [[outcrop]]s located between the City of Aceguá and [[Bagé]], located along the highway ''BR-153''. They are [[Rio do Rasto Formation]] and Late [[Permian]] age.
