'''Las Rosas''' is a city in the southwest of the {{province|Santa Fe|Argentina}}, located {{convert|165|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Santa Fe, Argentina|the provincial capital]]. It has about 13,689  inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2010}}, and it is the head town of the [[Belgrano Department, Santa Fe|Belgrano Department]], which also comprises the municipalities of [[Armstrong, Santa Fe|Armstrong]], [[Bouquet, Argentina|Bouquet]], [[Las Parejas]], [[Montes de Oca, Argentina|Montes de Oca]], and [[Tortugas, Argentina|Tortugas]].

Las Rosas was founded in 1889 by Guillermo Kemmis & Dickinson bros, and was officially recognized on January 1, 1882, becoming a city on August 20, 1967.

Summers there are warm, humid, wet, and mostly clear and the winters are short, cold, dry, and partly cloudy. Over the course of the year, the temperature typically varies from 41&nbsp;°F to 87&nbsp;°F and is rarely below 30&nbsp;°F or above 95&nbsp;°F.
