'''Santana do Paraíso''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the interior of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]] of the country. It is situated in the [[Vale do Rio Doce (mesoregion)|Vale do Rio Doce]] and is part of the [[Vale do Aço metropolitan area|Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region]], approximately {{convert|240|km|mi}} east of the [[Belo Horizonte|state capital]]. The municipality covers an [[area]] of just over {{convert|276|km2|sqmi}}, with {{convert|11|km2|sqmi}} constituting the [[urban area]], and its population was estimated at 48,286 inhabitants in 2024.

The exploration of the region where the city now stands began in the 19th century, through expeditions ordered by [[John VI of Portugal]] aimed at occupying the area, which became feasible only after the evangelization of local indigenous peoples by Guido Marlière after 1819. Marlière also facilitated the construction of roads, later used by ''[[tropeiro]]s''. Near the present-day city center, at Cachoeira do Engenho Velho, a rest stop was established, which evolved into a farm and commercial hub, around which the settlement known as Taquaraçu formed. This settlement became the district of Santana do Paraíso in 1892.

The area experienced further development due to the influence of industrial complexes in the current Vale do Aço region during the 1940s and 1950s. In 1959, it was selected as the site for the [[Vale do Aço Regional Airport|Ipatinga Airport]], which remains the only airport in the region. Its proximity to [[Ipatinga]], the headquarters of [[Usiminas]], spurred urban growth and the formation of a new urban nucleus in a [[conurbation]] with the neighboring city, parallel to the original district seat. With its status as a [[Commuter town|dormitory city]] and recent industrial investments within its territory, emancipation occurred in 1992. The sustained industrial activity in the region contributed to the establishment of the Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region, one of the main urban centers in the state's interior.

Beyond its economic and demographic significance, the municipality is home to numerous trails, forests, lagoons, and waterfalls open to visitors. A key landmark in the city is the Santana Mother Church, maintained by the local parish. Cultural expressions include a ''congado'' group, [[handicraft]]s, and festive events such as the city’s anniversary celebrations, the Feast of the Divine, and the day of [[Saint Anne]], the municipal patron saint.

== History ==
=== Colonization of the region ===
[[File:Monumento ao Tropeiro, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|left|Tropeiro Monument on [[List of state highways in Minas Gerais|MG-232]]]]
The area of present-day Santana do Paraíso saw little significant colonization until the mid-19th century, except for the presence of Nack-ne-nuck, descendants of the [[Botocudo]]s. Expeditions in search of precious metals also reached the areas near the [[Doce River]], but exploration only began after 1809, under orders from [[John VI of Portugal]], who sought to civilize the indigenous peoples of the [[Vale do Rio Doce (mesoregion)|Vale do Rio Doce]]. Consequently, the lands were transferred to Antônio Rodrigues Taborda, who led the occupation efforts. However, resistance from the natives, dense forests, and the prevalence of [[tropical disease]]s hindered exploration.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web|url=http://www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6499/Historia |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150910174602/http://www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6499/Historia |archive-date=September 10, 2015 |title=In the shade of waterfalls |author=Jakson Goulart |work=Prefeitura Municipal de Santana do Paraíso |date=August 22, 2011 |publisher=Prefeitura |access-date=July 2, 2016}}</ref>

In 1819, Frenchman Guido Marlière was appointed administrator of the Doce River region, achieving pacification of the indigenous peoples and opening roads, many of which were later used by [[Tropeiro|''tropeiros'']].<ref name="CaminhosGerais_N21_P17">Caminhos Gerais Magazine, no. 21, p. 17.</ref><ref name="CaminhosGerais_N35_P23">Caminhos Gerais Magazine, no. 35, p. 23.</ref> One such road, connecting [[Antônio Dias]] and [[Coronel Fabriciano]] to [[Ferros]], passed by Cachoeira do Engenho Velho, near the current center of Santana do Paraíso, where a rest stop was established. This place became known as Taquaraçu, meaning "large bamboo," and evolved into a reference point and commercial center, marking the beginning of a settlement.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

=== Population and economic expansion ===
[[File:Edificação com escrito 1952 no Centro de Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|left|Building with the inscription "1952" in downtown Santana do Paraíso]]
The first commercial hub in Taquaraçu was the Cachoeira do Engenho Farm, which over time was equipped with a mill, coffee and rice cleaning machines, and grain warehouses. The fertile land, abundant game, and numerous watercourses suitable for fishing attracted new residents.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> Expeditions followed the course of the Achado Stream and its tributaries around 1845 to explore local lands. On August 16, 1885, a group of residents donated land in the name of [[Saint Anne]], intended for housing the poor. On the same occasion, the first church of the settlement, dedicated to [[Vincent de Paul|Saint Vincent]], was consecrated.<ref name="UFOP_59a62">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|pp=59–62}}</ref>

Due to the settlement’s growth, municipal law no. 26, dated November 16, 1892, established the district, named Ipanema and subordinated to [[Itabira]]. Under state law no. 556, dated August 30, 1911, the district was renamed Santana do Paraíso and transferred to Ferros, but it was later reassigned to the municipality of [[Mesquita, Minas Gerais|Mesquita]] by state law no. 843, dated September 7, 1923.<ref name="IBGE_História"/> The name "Santana do Paraíso" references the patron saint Saint Anne and the natural attractions, reminiscent of a "[[paradise]]". The area saw further development influenced by the establishment of [[Aperam South America|Acesita]] in [[Timóteo (municipality)|Timóteo]] in the 1940s and [[Usiminas]] in [[Ipatinga]] in the 1950s.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name="UFOP_59a62"/>

The proximity of Santana do Paraíso to the so-called [[Vale do Aço metropolitan area|Vale do Aço]], combined with easy access to Ipatinga via [[List of state highways in Minas Gerais|MG-232]], facilitated the expansion of the original district’s urban core. Meanwhile, a new development zone emerged in another part of the municipal territory, near the border with Ipatinga, intersected by [[Rodovia Fernão Dias|BR-381]] and [[BR-458 (Brazil highway)|BR-458]]. Along these highways, numerous subdivisions were established, particularly after the 1980s,<ref name="UFOP_59a62"/> driven also by the construction of the [[Ipatinga Airport]] in Santana do Paraíso territory in 1959, near BR-381.<ref name="Unileste_Regional">{{cite web |url=http://issuu.com/danielalima53/docs/tcc_pronto |title=Vale do Aço Regional Airport |author=Daniele Lima |date=2015 |pages=16–22 |publisher=Centro Universitário Católica do Leste de Minas Gerais (Unileste) |access-date=July 2, 2016 |archive-date=March 13, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160313013433/http://issuu.com/danielalima53/docs/tcc_pronto |url-status=dead }}</ref> With the creation of these new neighborhoods, a [[conurbation]] with Ipatinga’s urban area emerged, parallel to the original seat of Santana do Paraíso,<ref name="UFOP_59a62"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=74661 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160702214543/http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=74661 |archive-date=July 2, 2016 |title=RMVA has more than 470,000 inhabitants |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=August 30, 2013 |access-date=July 2, 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> integrating the municipality into the Vale do Aço urban agglomeration.<ref name="PDDI1_42a49">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=42–49}}</ref>

=== Emancipation and the 21st century ===
[[File:Avenida Minas Gerais no B. Veraneio, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|left|Minas Gerais Avenue (MG-232) in the Veraneio neighborhood]]
Due to its border with Ipatinga’s urban perimeter and easy access to it, Santana do Paraíso recorded the highest population growth rates in the Vale do Aço between the 1990s and 2000s.<ref name="PDDI1_42a49"/> The establishment of an [[industrial district]] in the Ipatinga Airport region also encouraged the settlement of companies within the municipal territory.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> This development led to emancipation through state law No. 10,704, dated April 27, 1992, and published on April 28, 1992, which came into effect on January 1, 1993.<ref name="IBGE_História"/> In areas previously used for [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]], there was a gradual shift to [[eucalyptus]] cultivation to supply the [[pulp mill]]s of Cenibra in [[Belo Oriente]], with reforestation areas covering over 40% of Santana do Paraíso territory by the early 2010s.<ref name="PDDI1_42a49"/>

As a [[Commuter town|dormitory city]] close to Usiminas,<ref name="ABEP_15">{{Harvtxt|ref=Dinâmica|ABEP|2012|p=15}}</ref> the municipality received investments and revenues from the company, such as airport operations and the construction of an industrial landfill alongside the [[Landfill|sanitary landfill]] of the Vale do Aço Waste Center (CRVA), inaugurated in 2003.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=56388 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160702214741/http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=56388 |archive-date=July 2, 2016 |title=Region produces 750.7 tons/day of waste |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=July 26, 2011 |access-date=July 2, 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="UFOP_89e90">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|pp=89–90}}</ref> In the 2000s, there was a plan to build an industrial unit in the municipality, but the project was halted due to the [[Great Recession|2008–2012 financial crisis]].<ref name="JVA_Obras">{{cite web |url=http://www.jvaonline.com.br/ler_noticia.php?id=90469 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140113204341/http://www.jvaonline.com.br/ler_noticia.php?id=90469 |archive-date=January 13, 2014 |title=ALMG commission discusses airport works |author=Vale do Aço Newspaper |date=April 19, 2011 |access-date=July 2, 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

Alongside the municipality’s economic growth, the conurbation with the Vale do Aço agglomeration, whether related to local companies or not, led to disorganized occupations, deforestation, and precarious subdivisions since the 1990s, particularly in the city’s periphery. Given the sudden population increase and relatively recent emancipation, Santana do Paraíso lacked sufficient resources and infrastructure to implement necessary public policies. Thus, the municipality’s urban growth was not matched by socioeconomic development capable of meeting the population’s needs.<ref name="UFOP_21a22">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|pp=21–22}}</ref>

=== Topography and hydrography ===
The landscape's composition is the result of [[fluvial erosion]] and its action on [[Precambrian]] [[Granite gneiss|granite-gneiss]] rocks.<ref name="CBH-Doce_Caracterização">{{cite web|url=http://www.riodoce.cbh.gov.br/bacia_caracterizacao.asp |title=Characterization of the Hydrographic Basin |author=Doce River Hydrographic Basin Committee |access-date=October 13, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141006105252/http://www.riodoce.cbh.gov.br/bacia_caracterizacao.asp |archive-date=6 October 2014 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Average altitudes range from 200 meters in the south, near the [[Doce River]] course; to 850 meters in the Serra do Achado region in the northwest, reaching up to {{convert|1100|m|ft}} at the source of the Achado Stream. The city center is situated at 300 meters altitude. The southern areas feature plains and slightly undulating terrain, while the northwest, nearing the Serra do Achado, becomes markedly rugged.<ref name="UFOP_21a22" /><ref name="UFOP_85">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|p=85}}</ref> Santana do Paraíso lies within the [[Doce River Basin|Doce River basin]], which separates the municipality from the territories of [[Caratinga]] and [[Ipaba]]. The municipal territory encompasses four main sub-basins, all draining into the Doce River: the Bueiro Stream and Entre Folhas Stream, and the Achado Stream ({{convert|144.4|km²|sqmi}}) and Garrafa Stream ({{convert|45.7|km²|sqmi}}).<ref name="PDDI1_111a114">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=111–114}}</ref> Only the latter two cover parts of the urban perimeter. The Achado Stream originates in a rugged area and, besides being home to hundreds of other springs in its sub-basin, features several waterfalls along its course.<ref name="UFOP_85" /> This stream is a tributary of the Taquaraçu Stream, another significant watercourse in the municipality’s rural zone, which flows into the Doce River.<ref>{{Cite book|last=Ezezinos |first=Karen Esteves |year=2021 |title=Diagnosis of the Achado Stream microbasin in the municipality of Santana do Paraíso/MG and proposals for spring revitalization |page=16 |publisher=Universidade Federal de Itajubá (UNIFEI) |url=https://repositorio.unifei.edu.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/3185/1/Dissertacao_Karen_Esteves_Ezezinos_rev_final%20-%20Karen%20Esteves%20Ezezinos.pdf |access-date=June 8, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250430193949/https://repositorio.unifei.edu.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/3185/1/Dissertacao_Karen_Esteves_Ezezinos_rev_final%20-%20Karen%20Esteves%20Ezezinos.pdf |archive-date=April 30, 2025}}</ref>

=== Neighboring municipalities and metropolitan region ===
{{Main|Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region}}
Santana do Paraíso borders the municipalities of Belo Oriente to the north, Ipaba to the east, Caratinga to the southeast, Ipatinga to the south, and [[Mesquita, Minas Gerais|Mesquita]] to the west.<ref name="cidadesnet.com">{{cite web |url=http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/santanadoparaiso.htm |title=Santana do Paraíso - MG |author=Cidades.Net |access-date=October 13, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130909192820/http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/santanadoparaiso.htm |archive-date=September 9, 2013 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The region’s rapid growth has blurred political boundaries, forming the [[Vale do Aço metropolitan area|Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region]] (RMVA), which includes Santana do Paraíso alongside the cities of Coronel Fabriciano, Ipatinga, and Timóteo, plus 24 other municipalities in the so-called [[Metropolitan belt (Brazil)|metropolitan belt]]. As mentioned, Santana do Paraíso serves as a dormitory city for the RMVA while being home to the region’s only airport and offering the most land suitable for urban expansion. The region gained international recognition because of the major companies that are based there, such as Cenibra (in Belo Oriente), [[Aperam South America]] (in Timóteo), and Usiminas (Ipatinga), all with significant export volumes. Despite its recent settlement, it is one of the state’s key inland urban hubs.<ref name="ABEP_15"/>

=== Climate ===
[[File:Vista de Santana do Paraíso MG a partir da estrada da Serra da Viúva.jpg|thumb|left|Partial view of the city from the Serra da Viúva road during a winter sunset]]
Santana do Paraíso has a [[tropical savanna climate]]<ref name="IBGE_Clima">{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.br/visual_site/map/GEBIS/Brazil_climate.jpg |title=Brazil - Climate |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |publisher=IBGE Library | access-date=July 4, 2016 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131012015042/http://biblioteca.br/visual_site/map/GEBIS/Brazil_climate.jpg |archive-date=October 12, 2013 |url-status=dead}}</ref> (type ''Aw'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]), with an average annual [[temperature]] of {{convert|24|C|F}} and average rainfall of {{convert|1150|mm}} per year, concentrated between October and April. The [[wet season]] comprises the hotter months, while the dry season covers milder months. [[Autumn]] and [[Spring (season)|spring]] serve as transitional seasons.<ref name="Climate-Data.org_Santana-do-Paraíso-MG"/><ref name="Weatherspark">{{cite web |url=https://pt.weatherspark.com/y/30690/Clima-característico-em-Santana-do-Paraíso-Brasil-durante-o-ano |title=Average weather conditions in Santana do Paraíso |author=Weather Spark |access-date=September 7, 2020 }}</ref> The transition between dry and wet periods is marked by [[thunderstorm]]s and high [[thermal amplitude]], especially from late [[winter]] to spring.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0029508-primavera-pode-trazer-temporais |title=Spring may bring thunderstorms |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=September 13, 2010 |access-date=September 7, 2020 }}</ref>

[[Precipitation (meteorology)|Precipitation]] primarily falls as [[rain]], occasionally as [[hail]], and may be accompanied by [[lightning]]s and strong [[Wind gust|gusts]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.climatempo.com.br/noticia/2016/01/27/chuva-raios-e-vento-forte-em-ipatinga-mg--5154 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20200907125229/https://www.climatempo.com.br/noticia/2016/01/27/chuva-raios-e-vento-forte-em-ipatinga-mg--5154 |archive-date=September 7, 2020 |title=Rain, lightning, and strong winds in Ipatinga (MG) | author=Marcelo Pinheiro |date=January 27, 2016 |publisher=Climatempo |access-date=September 7, 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://minasgerais.ig.com.br/2020-02-13/12297285.html |title=Cities in MG on alert for heavy rainfall in the coming hours; see which ones |author=O Tempo Newspaper |date=February 13, 2020 |access-date=September 7, 2020 }}</ref> According to the Atmospheric Electricity Group of the [[National Institute for Space Research]] (ELAT/INPE) in 2018, the municipality has a lightning density of 2.007 strikes per km²/year, ranking 513th in the state and 3,966th nationally.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inpe.br/webelat/homepage/menu/noticias/ranking.de_municipios.php |title=Municipality ranking |author=National Institute for Space Research (INPE) |publisher=Atmospheric Electricity Group |access-date=August 30, 2018}}</ref> [[Fog]] occurs with high [[Relative humidity|humidity]] and low temperatures.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0028499-at-cold-polar-air-front-drops-temperatures-in-vale-do-aco |title=Cold polar air front action lowers temperatures in Vale do Aço |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=May 17, 2010 |access-date=September 7, 2020 }}</ref> However, low humidity levels may occur during the dry season or prolonged [[Indian summer]]s.<ref name="Metar"/> During these periods, dry air combined with [[pollution]] increases pollutant concentration, worsening air quality.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0029357-ar-dry-requires-care |title=Dry air requires care |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=August 26, 2010 |access-date=September 7, 2020}}</ref><ref name="Diário_Queimadas">{{cite web|url=http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/96304.html |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160704160715/http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/96304-6/cidades/alerta-para-o-tempo-seco |archive-date=July 4, 2016 |title=Alert for dry weather |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=August 14, 2015 |access-date=July 4, 2016}}</ref>

The prevailing [[wind]] originates from the east, with the windiest period between August 1 and November 29, averaging 10.7 kilometers per hour, slightly stronger in September and October. During the calmer period from March to June, the average speed ranges between 8 and 9 kilometers per hour.<ref name="Weatherspark"/> At [[Ipatinga Airport]], extreme temperature records range from {{convert|6|C|°C}} on May 19, 2022, to {{convert|40|C|°C}} on October 31, 2012, and November 18, 2023.<ref name="Metar">{{cite web|url=https://www.wunderground.com/history/daily/br/santana-do-para%C3%ADso/SBIP |title=Historical weather for SBIP |author=Weather Underground |access-date=July 4, 2016}}</ref><ref name="CPTEC_BD">{{cite web|url=http://bancodedados.gov.br/ |title=Metar data - === Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Zona rural de Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Pastures in the rural zone of Santana do Paraíso]]
The native vegetation belongs to the [[Atlantic Forest]] domain, with few fragmented areas remaining amidst [[Reforestation|reforested]] zones, pastures, and the urban perimeter.<ref name="PDDI1_64a65">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=64–65}}</ref><ref name="PDDI1_118a120">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=118–120}}</ref> However, the predominant land use is monoculture reforestation with [[eucalyptus]], covering a larger area than the original biome, aimed at supplying raw material for the Cenibra pulp mill. In 2009, eucalyptus plantations occupied {{convert|9608.59|ha}} or 34.97% of Santana do Paraíso’s territory. That year, {{convert|187.36|ha}} (0.68%) were covered by watercourses, and {{convert|742.89|ha}} (2.7%) were urbanized areas.<ref name="FlorestasMG">{{Cite web|url=http://geosisemanet.net.br/inventario_bi/Interface/Forest_Inventory.html?application=Forest_Data.xml&object=municipality&id_marker=15833&id=3169406&layer_id=4&table=public.municipality# |title=Minas Gerais Forest Inventory - Query by Municipality |author=Minas Gerais Forest Inventory |access-date=July 4, 2016}}</ref> By 2014, eucalyptus plantations covered {{convert|140.830|km²}} (50.72% of the municipal area), while native Atlantic Forest spanned {{convert|10.220|km²}} (3.62% of the total area).<ref name="PDDI1_118a120"/>

Santana do Paraíso has an [[Environmental protection area (Brazil)|environmental protection area]] (APA), which, together with neighboring protected areas such as the [[Serra dos Cocais]] in Coronel Fabriciano and other APAs in Ipatinga and Belo Oriente, forms an [[ecological corridor]] to the [[Rio Doce State Park]] (PERD),<ref name="PDDI1_120a124">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=120–124}}</ref> the largest Atlantic Forest remnant and one of the state’s main lake systems.<ref name="PDDI1_64a65"/> However, parts of local APAs are used for pastures or eucalyptus cultivation.<ref name="PDDI2_358a360">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv2|PDDI|2014|pp=358–360}}</ref> The Santana do Paraíso APA, created by municipal decree no. 066 on May 10, 1999, spans {{convert|19.12|km²}} and includes, besides Atlantic Forest remnants, several lagoons and waterfalls open to visitors.<ref name="Universalia_APA">{{cite web |url=http://www.universalisconsultoria.com.br/projetos/0027.pdf |title=Santana do Paraíso APA |author=Universalis |date=April 2008 |access-date=October 14, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160310114455/http://www.universalisconsultoria.com.br/projetos/0027.pdf |archive-date=March 10, 2016}}</ref>

Key environmental issues in the city include flooding, which causes significant damage in low-lying, densely populated areas during the rainy season, and landslides on hills and slopes.<ref name="UFOP_91">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|p=91}}</ref> These problems are often caused by residential construction on hillsides and high-risk zones, as well as waste and sewage dumped into streams and rivers. The city center and the Águas Claras neighborhood, located along watercourses, are typically among the hardest-hit areas by floods.<ref name="UFOP_113">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|p=113}}</ref> Forest fires destroy native vegetation, degrading soil quality and further impairing [[air quality]], compounded by [[Air pollution|atmospheric pollution]] from Vale do Aço mills.<ref name="Diário_Queimadas"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=30649 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120730030248/http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticias.aspx?cd=30649 |archive-date=July 30, 2012 |title=Research on industrial pollution in Vale do Aço |author=Diário do Aço Newspaper |date=September 27, 2009 |access-date=October 14, 2010 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Indicators and inequality ===
[[File:Rua do B. Alto Santana, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|left|Residential street on a hill in the Alto Santana neighborhood]]
The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Santana do Paraíso is considered medium by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] ([[United Nations Development Programme|UNDP]]), with a value of 0.685 (the 2,309th highest in Brazil). Most of the city’s indicators are close to or below the national average, according to the UNDP. The education index is 0.552, the longevity index is 0.878, and the income index is 0.663.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> From 2000 to 2010, the proportion of people with a per capita household income of up to half a minimum wage decreased by 69.0%. In 2010, 88.9% of the population lived above the [[poverty line]], 7.3% were at the poverty line, and 3.8% were below it.<ref name="ODM_1">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-end-hunger-and-poverty/BRA003031695/santana-do-paraiso---mg |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160702204544/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/BRA003031695/santana-do-paraiso---mg |archive-date=July 2, 2016 |title=1 - end hunger and poverty |author=Portal ODM |access-date=July 2, 2016}}</ref> The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], was 0.431, where 1.00 is the worst and 0.00 is the best.<ref name="ODM_Perfil">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA003031695/santana-do-paraiso---mg |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160702204656/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA003031695/santana-do-paraiso---mg |archive-date=July 2, 2016 |title=Municipal profile |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=July 2, 2016}}</ref> The share of the richest 20% of the population in the city’s total income was 47.0%, 8.9 times higher than that of the poorest 20%, which was 5.3%.<ref name="ODM_1"/>

The rapid population growth following Santana do Paraíso’s emancipation meant that urban expansion was not accompanied by sufficient socioeconomic development to meet the population’s needs, leading to significant disorganized occupations and precarious subdivisions, particularly in the city’s periphery. Many subdivisions suffer from steep slopes, roads without drainage, and inadequate earthworks, making housing construction challenging or resulting in improper occupations. In 2012, about 55% of approved lots in the municipality remained unoccupied.<ref name="UFOP_21a22"/>

== Subdivisions ==
[[File:Vista parcial do B. Jardim Vitória a partir do Bom Pastor, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Partial view of the Jardim Vitória neighborhood, which forms a [[conurbation]] with Ipatinga.]]
Santana do Paraíso is not divided into districts. The municipality is organized into 12 neighborhoods within its urban perimeter. It also has ten rural communities and the Industrial District.<ref name="UFOP_58">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|p=58}}</ref> Most neighborhoods in the central area are older and house about 40% of the municipal population, according to the IBGE in 2010. This zone includes the Alto Santana, Centro, Josefino Anício dos Reis, Oliveira, Residencial Paraíso, São Francisco, São José, Vale do Paraíso, and Veraneio neighborhoods.<ref name="UFOP_89a91">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|pp=89–91}}</ref>

Generally, irregular subdivisions and hillside occupations are concentrated in the central area, which also contains watercourses used for sewage disposal.<ref name="UFOP_90a94">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|pp=90–94}}</ref> Outside the central region and near Ipatinga’s urban perimeter, the Águas Claras and Industrial neighborhoods, established before Santana do Paraíso’s emancipation, account for about 39% of the population.<ref name="UFOP_89a91"/> The Ipaba do Paraíso neighborhood lies on the border with [[Ipaba]], along the Doce River, and comprises about 7% of the population.<ref name="UFOP_89a91"/><ref name="UFOP_23">{{Harvtxt|ref=UFOP|Neto|2012|p=23}}</ref>

In conurbation with Ipatinga are several relatively recent subdivisions, established after the 1990s, including Cidade Nova, Jardim Vitória, Parque, Parque Caravelas, Residencial Betânia, and Residencial Bom Pastor. These areas have many vacant lots or ongoing constructions, but most new subdivisions feature considerable infrastructure. Notable are the small farms, primarily for high-standard residences, such as Chácaras Aeroporto, Chácaras do Vale, Chácaras do Vale Expansão, and Chácaras Paraíso I and II.<ref name="UFOP_89a91"/>

== Economy ==
[[File:Comércio no Centro de Santana do Paraíso MG2.JPG|thumb|left|Commerce in downtown Santana do Paraíso]]
In the [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP) of Santana do Paraíso, the industrial and service sectors stand out. According to 2021 [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] data, the municipality’s GDP at current prices was R$750,462,520.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2021"/> R$85,949,020 came from taxes on products net of subsidies at current prices, and the per capita GDP was R$20,818.42.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2021">{{cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/38/46996 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20250102142958/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/38/46996 |archive-date=January 2, 2025 |title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities - 2021 |author=IBGE Cidades |date=2021 |access-date=January 2, 2025}}</ref> In 2010, 67.30% of the population over 18 was [[Workforce|economically active]], with an unemployment rate of 10.39%.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> Notably, about 50% of the population commuted to another municipality for work, given the proximity and easy access to industrial complexes in other Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region municipalities.<ref name="PDDI2_304">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv2|PDDI|2014|p=304}}</ref>

In 2022, [[Salary|salaries]] and other remunerations totaled R$176,901,000, with an average monthly salary of 2 [[minimum wage]]s. There were 1,552 local units.<ref name="IBGE_Empresas">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/19/143491 |title=Central Business Register |author=IBGE Cidades |date=2022 |access-date=January 2, 2025 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20250102143156/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/19/143491 |archive-date=January 2, 2025}}</ref> According to the IBGE in 2010, 67.93% of households survived on less than one minimum wage per resident (5,361 households), 26.62% had between one and three minimum wages per person (2,101 households), 2.31% earned between three and five wages (182 households), 0.72% had incomes above five minimum wages (57 households), and 2.41% had no income (190 households).<ref name="SIDRA_Rendimento">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=4&i=P&c=3261 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160703133733/http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=3261&i=P&opc386=2&nome=on&qtu8=137&digt102=&qtu14=3&notarodape=on&tab=3261&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&qtu11=658&qtu23=294&nivt=0&orp=4&qtu3=27&qtu13=47&opv=2&orc386=3&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&qtu2=5&opn10=0&qtu15=3&sev=96&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5565&qtu102=14208&opn13=0&poc386=2&sep=26272&orn=1&digt10=&opn11=0&qtu7=36&opn23=0&sec386=9680&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=Santana+do+Para%C3%ADso&opn102=0&OpcCara=104&proc=1&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on&qtu10=10282&digt11=&opn7=0&decm=99 |archive-date=July 3, 2016 |title=Permanent private households by nominal monthly household income per capita classes |author=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (SIDRA) |date=2010 |access-date=July 3, 2016}}</ref>

=== Industry and services ===
[[File:Galpões no Distrito Industrial de Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|left|Warehouses in Santana do Paraíso’s Industrial District, along [[BR-458 (Brazil highway)|BR-458]].]]
In 2021, industry was the second-largest contributor to the municipal economy, with R$182,546,570 of the gross added value from the secondary sector.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2021"/> In 2010, 0.40% of workers were employed in extractive industries and 21.48% in manufacturing.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> Santana do Paraíso has an [[industrial district]], established in 2000, covering about {{convert|920965|m²}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.codemig.com.br/distritos-industriais |archive-url=https://archive.today/20160703130124/http://www.codemig.com.br/distritos-industriais |archive-date=July 3, 2016 |title=Map of industrial districts |author=Minas Gerais Economic Development Company (Codemig) |access-date=July 3, 2016}}</ref>

Among other industries, Santana do Paraíso is known for furniture manufacturing, food and beverage production, metalworking, and the Cimentos Cauê factory,<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/> as well as [[eucalyptus]] extraction to supply the Cenibra pulp mill in Belo Oriente.<ref name="PDDI1_42a49"/> In 2023, according to the IBGE, 135,600 cubic meters of eucalyptus logs were extracted, with 99.63% destined for paper and pulp production.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/16/12705 |title=Vegetable extraction and silviculture |author=IBGE Cidades |date=2023 |access-date=January 2, 2025 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20250102144042/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/16/12705 |archive-date=January 2, 2025}}</ref>

In 2010, 12.97% of the employed population was engaged in the construction sector, 1.35% in public utilities, 11.99% in commerce, and 36.17% in the service sector.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas" /> In 2021, {{formatnum:298909.26}} thousand [[Brazilian real|reais]] of the municipal GDP came from the gross value added by the service sector, and {{formatnum:170478.84}} thousand reais from the gross value added by public administration.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2021"/> Commercial activity in Santana do Paraíso is relatively modest compared to other municipalities in the Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region. However, the city’s primary economic corridors include a segment encompassing Getúlio Vargas and Minas Gerais avenues and Sagrados Corações Street in the Centro district, where public services, commercial establishments, educational institutions, and healthcare facilities are concentrated.<ref name="PDDI1_163a165">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=163–165}}</ref>

=== Education ===
[[File:Fachada da Escola Estadual Albertino Ferreira Drumond, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Facade of the Albertino Ferreira Drumond State School]]
In education, the [[Basic Education Development Index]] (IDEB) average score for early-year [[primary education]] students in Santana do Paraíso’s public schools was 6.2 in 2023, on a scale from 1 to 10, while final-year primary students scored 4.3.<ref name="IBGE_SantanadoParaíso"/> In 2022, 33.19% of children aged zero to three, 88.04% of those aged four to five, 98.9% of those aged six to 14, and 89.98% of adolescents aged 15 to 17 were enrolled in schools. === Housing, services, and communications ===
[[File:Aterro sanitário da Central de Resíduos do Vale do Aço, Santana do Paraíso MG.jpg|thumb|left|[[Landfill]] of the Vale do Aço Waste Center (CRVA)]]
In 2022, Santana do Paraíso had {{formatnum:16720}} occupied permanent private households. Of these, {{formatnum:11331}} were houses (67.77%), {{formatnum:5338}} were apartments (31.93%), 33 were houses in villages or condominiums (0.20%), 13 were [[tenement]]s (0.08%), and five were degraded or unfinished structures (0.03%).<ref name="IBGE_Censo2022"/> Of the occupied households, {{formatnum:11617}} were owned (69.47%), with {{formatnum:9310}} fully paid and {{formatnum:2306}} under acquisition; {{formatnum:4581}} were [[Renting|rented]] (27.40%); 496 were loaned or ceded (2.97%); and 28 were occupied in other forms (0.17%).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9930#/n6/3158953/v/all/p/all/c65/95810/c63/1055,1058,4343,73126,73553,73554,95826/c125/2932/d/v1000381%202/l/v,p+c65+c63,t+c125/resultado |title=Tabela 9930 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, por número de cômodos, condição de ocupação do domicílio e tipo do domicílio |author=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (SIDRA) |date=2022 |access-date=January 2, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250102144835/https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9930#/n6/3158953/v/all/p/all/c65/95810/c63/1055,1058,4343,73126,73553,73554,95826/c125/2932/d/v1000381%202/l/v,p+c65+c63,t+c125/resultado |archive-date=January 2, 2025}}</ref>

Water supply and sewage collection services are provided by the Minas Gerais Sanitation Company (Copasa).<ref name="PDDI1_198a202"/> In 2022, according to IBGE, 85.49% of households had access to the general water network as their primary supply source, and 99.99% had exclusive-use bathrooms. Regarding [[sewage disposal]], 90.81% of households were served by the general or stormwater network.<ref name="IBGE_Censo2022"/> Much of the water used to supply the city and the Vale do Aço region comes from an alluvial aquifer located underground, extracted and treated at Copasa’s [[water treatment plant]] in the Amaro Lanari neighborhood of Coronel Fabriciano. However, Santana do Paraíso has five smaller collection and treatment points serving certain areas of the municipality.<ref name="PDDI1_198a202">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv1|PDDI|2014|pp=198–202}}</ref> In March 2024, the [[sewage treatment plant]] (ETE) of the Garrafa Stream began operations, increasing the percentage of treated wastewater in the municipality from 1.5% to 80%, according to Copasa.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0114463-ete-garrafa-entra-em-operacao-em-santana-do-paraiso |title=ETE Garrafa entra em operação em Santana do Paraíso |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=March 26, 2024 |access-date=April 1, 2024 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20240401202859/https://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0114463-ete-garrafa-entra-em-operacao-em-santana-do-paraiso |archive-date=April 1, 2024}}</ref>

Electricity supply is managed by the [[CEMIG|Minas Gerais Energy Company]] (Cemig), which serves much of the state.<ref name="cidadesnet.com" /> In 2010, 99.6% of households had access to the electrical network, according to IBGE data.<ref name="PDDI3_580">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv3|PDDI|2014|p=580}}</ref> Regarding waste disposal, 99.16% of households were served by [[Waste collection|collection services]] in 2022, according to the census,<ref name="IBGE_Censo2022"/> with waste directed to the Vale do Aço Waste Center (CRVA). Operational since 2003, it is located near the Águas Claras neighborhood and also receives waste from other Vale do Aço municipalities and surrounding areas.<ref name="PDDI1_198a202"/> [[Waste picker]]s maintain an informal [[Waste sorting|waste-sorting]] system for [[Recycling|recyclable materials]].<ref>{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv2|PDDI|2014|pp=431–433}}</ref>

== Security and crime ==
[[File:Delegacia de Polícia Civil de Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Police station of the Civil Police in Santana do Paraíso]]
Public safety in Santana do Paraíso is provided by various organizations.<ref name="PDDI3_814">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv3|PDDI|2014|p=814}}</ref> The [[Military Police of Minas Gerais State]], a state-level force, is responsible for policing cities, patrolling banks, protecting the environment, securing prisons, ensuring school safety, and providing security for special events. The force also carries out social integration initiatives.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.policiamilitar.mg.gov.br/portal-pm/12rpm/conteudo.action?conteudo=82&tipoConteudo=itemMenu |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20120805205259/https://www.policiamilitar.mg.gov.br/portal-pm/12rpm/conteudo.action?conteudo=82&tipoConteudo=itemMenu |archivedate=5 August 2012 |title=12ª Região da Polícia Militar |author=Polícia Militar do Estado de Minas Gerais (PMMG) | work=Polícia Militar de Minas Gerais |accessdate=12 July 2016}}</ref> The municipality is served by the Ipatinga Military Police Battalion.<ref name="PDDI3_569a570">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv3|PDDI|2014|pp=569–570}}</ref> The Civil Police of Minas Gerais aims to combat and investigate crimes and infractions.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pc.mg.gov.br/internas/servicos/iServicos.php |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20130924204210/http://web.archive.org/web/20070302103809/http://www.pc.mg.gov.br/internas/servicos/iServicos.php |archivedate=24 September 2013 |title=Serviços |author=Polícia Civil do Estado de Minas Gerais (PCMG) |date=2007 |accessdate=12 July 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The city's Civil Police station constitutes an Integrated Public Safety Area (AISP), which is subordinate to the Ipatinga Regional Civil Police Department.<ref name="PDDI3_569a570"/> The Civil Defense, under the municipal government, also operates in the area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6569/Organograma-Prefeitura|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160711165705/http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:EUaYUKNwWPUJ:www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6569/Organograma-Prefeitura+&cd=2&hl=pt-BR&ct=clnk&gl=br |archivedate=11 July 2016 |title=Organograma Prefeitura |author=Prefeitura | work=Prefeitura Municipal de Santana do Paraíso |date=23 November 2011 |accessdate=12 July 2016}}</ref>

In 2013, the Military Police recorded a total of 91 violent crimes, including 60 robberies (65.9%), 16 attempted [[homicides]] (17.6%), and 15 homicides (16.5%).<ref name="PDDI3_530a531">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv3|PDDI|2014|pp=530–531}}</ref> In 2022, three homicides were recorded, a decrease from five in 2021. The worst year from 2000 to 2022 was 2011, with 15 incidents.<ref name="IPEA_AtlasdaViolência">{{cite web|url=https://www.ipea.gov.br/atlasviolencia/dados-series/328 |title=Homicídios - município |author=Atlas da Violência |publisher=Instituto de Pesquisa Econômica Aplicada (Ipea) |accessdate=6 September 2024 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20240906222134|archivedate=6 September 2024}}</ref> Some homicides are linked to [[drug trafficking]], which also contributes to other crimes, as users often steal and rob to sustain their addictions.<ref name="JVA_Batalhão">{{cite web |url=http://www.jvaonline.com.br/ler_noticia.php?id=108151 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20140301152733/http://www.jvaonline.com.br/ler_noticia.php?id=108151 |archivedate=1 March 2014 |title=Combate ao tráfico é maior desafio de novo Batalhão |author=Jornal Vale do Aço |date=9 February 2014 |accessdate=12 July 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The Industrial and Cidade Nova neighborhoods are identified by the police as the most violent, where the majority of incidents in the municipality are concentrated.<ref name="PDDI3_540">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv3|PDDI|2014|p=540}}</ref>

== Transport ==
[[File:Terminal de passageiros do Aeroporto de Ipatinga, Santana do Paraíso MG2.JPG|thumb|left|Passenger terminal of the [[Vale do Aço Regional Airport]], located in Santana do Paraíso.]]
Air transport is available through the [[Ipatinga Airport]] {{Airport codes|IPN|SBIP}}, known as the Vale do Aço Regional Airport, located in Santana do Paraíso. Built by Usiminas, it is situated approximately {{convert|3|km}} from the center of the neighboring city and serves the entire Vale do Aço Metropolitan Region with daily flights to the [[Greater Belo Horizonte|Belo Horizonte Metropolitan Region]], although it has offered other destinations in the past.<ref name="Unileste_Regional"/> The municipality is also crossed by the [[Vitória-Minas Railway]] (EFVM),<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.diariodocomercio.com.br/noticia.php?id=32680 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160712215538/http://www.diariodocomercio.com.br/noticia.php?id=32680 |archivedate=12 July 2016 |title=Vale inicia obra de duplicação da EFVM |author=Aparecida Lira |date=29 October 2011 |publisher=Jornal Diário do Comércio |accessdate=12 July 2016}}</ref> which enables passenger rail transport through the station located in Ipaba do Paraíso.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/ler_noticia.php?id=21503&t=prefeito-destaca-facilidades-para-escoamento-da-producao |title=Prefeito destaca facilidades para escoamento da produção |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=9 July 2008 |accessdate=3 April 2017 |archivedate=3 April 2017|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20170403153948}}</ref>

The municipal vehicle fleet in 2023 consisted of {{formatnum:13408}} vehicles, including {{formatnum:7205}} cars, {{formatnum:3694}} motorcycles, 868 pickup trucks, 368 trucks, 360 vans, 236 trailers, 213 mopeds, 100 scooters, 96 tractor-trucks, 84 utility vehicles, 58 buses, 56 minibuses, 56 semi-trailers, eight wheel tractors, five tricycles, and one sidecar.<ref name="IBGE_Frota">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/22/28120 |title=Frota |author=IBGE Cidades |date=2023 |accessdate=2 January 2025 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20250102144955/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santana-do-paraiso/pesquisa/22/28120 |archivedate=2 January 2025}}</ref> In 2024, a {{convert|10|km}} bike lane was paved along the [[List of state highways in Minas Gerais|MG-232]], connecting the Industrial neighborhood to the center of Santana do Paraíso.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0119674-ciclovia-entre-o-industrial-e-santana-do-paraiso-ja-esta-pavimentada |title=Ciclovia entre o Industrial e Santana do Paraíso já está pavimentada |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=29 October 2024 |accessdate=2 January 2025 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20250102183234/https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0119674-ciclovia-entre-o-industrial-e-santana-do-paraiso-ja-esta-pavimentada |archivedate=2 January 2025}}</ref> However, sidewalk infrastructure is deficient in many neighborhoods, with issues such as irregularities, obstacles, and lack of pavement continuity.<ref name="PDDI2_326a327">{{Harvtxt|ref=PDDIv2|PDDI|2014|pp=326–327}}</ref> The municipality is intersected by the federal highways [[Rodovia Fernão Dias|BR-381]], the main route to the state capital and [[Espírito Santo]]; and [[BR-458 (Brazil highway)|BR-458]], connecting the Vale do Aço to the [[BR-116 (Brazil highway)|BR-116]]. However, only the MG-232 directly serves the city center, linking it to Ipatinga and extending to the [[MG-010 (Minas Gerais highway)|MG-010]], near the Belo Horizonte Metropolitan Region.<ref name="Google">[http://maps.google.com.br/ Google Maps]. Accessed on 12 July 2016.</ref> Univale Transport provides direct bus lines connecting the main neighborhoods of the municipality to Ipatinga.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/88329-6/cidades/aumento-nas-passagens-intermunicipais |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160712215927/http://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/88329-6/cidades/aumento-nas-passagens-intermunicipais |archivedate=12 July 2016 |title=Aumento nas passagens intermunicipais |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=4 December 2014 |accessdate=12 July 2016}}</ref>

== Culture ==
[[File:Salto de parapente na Serra da Viúva, Santana do Paraíso MG.jpg|thumb|right|[[Paragliding]] jump at Serra da Viúva]]
[[File:Vista do Tijota Hotel Fazenda, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Tijota Hotel Fazenda, a guesthouse in the rural area of the municipality.]]
Santana do Paraíso has a municipal culture council, established in 2006, which is deliberative and composed of equal representation from government and civil society.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2012&node=638&ordem=6.2&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193235/http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2012&node=638&ordem=6.2&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Conselho municipal de cultura e de preservação do patrimônio |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2012 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref> There are also municipal laws for the protection of tangible cultural heritage, managed by a department of the municipal government responsible for cultural affairs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2012&node=632&ordem=6.1&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193343/http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2012&node=632&ordem=6.1&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Órgão gestor e legislação da cultura |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2012 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref> The municipality's cultural policy score for the ICMS Cultural calculation, valid for 2024, was 1.50 on a scale from 0 to 4. The total score, considering factors such as conservation, expenditure, and the number of registered or listed heritage assets, was 8.35.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.iepha.mg.gov.br/images/ICMS_2023/Pontuacao_DEFINITIVA_exercicio_2024-v1.1_-_com_logos.pdf |title=ICMS Critério do Patrimônio Cultural |author=Instituto Estadual do Patrimônio Histórico e Artístico de Minas Gerais (IEPHA) |page=31 |accessdate=15 August 2023 |archivedate=15 August 2023|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20230815111757}}</ref>

[[theater]], [[dance]], [[choir]], [[folklore]], and [[music]], according to the IBGE in 2012.<ref name="IBGE_Grupos">{{cite web|url=http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2005&node=3762&ordem=8.3&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193602/http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2005&node=3762&ordem=8.3&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Grupos artísticos |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2012 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref> [[Handicrafts]] are one of the most spontaneous forms of cultural expression in Santana do Paraíso, with the main artisanal activities, according to the IBGE, being [[embroidery]] and work with [[recyclable materials]].<ref name="IBGE_Artesanato">{{cite web|url=http://munic.ibge.gov.br/ver_tema.php?periodo=2012&node=679&ordem=6.4&munic=315895&uf=31&nome=Santana%20do%20Para%C3%ADso |title=Principais atividades artesanais |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2012 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref> In rural areas, family farming stands out with the production of [[sausages]], [[honey]], [[coffee]], [[sweets]], [[biscuits]], [[bread]], ''[[requeijão]]'', and ''[[avoador]]'', among other homemade foods. At times, the municipality's artisanal production is exported to neighboring municipalities.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.plox.com.br/cultura/paraiso-e-destaque-na-2a-feira-da-agricultura-familiar-em-caratinga |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193710/http://www.plox.com.br/cultura/paraiso-e-destaque-na-2a-feira-da-agricultura-familiar-em-caratinga |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Paraíso é destaque na 2ª Feira da Agricultura Familiar em Caratinga |author=Plox |date=22 June 2015 |accessdate=3 July 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

The city has a rich and diverse folklore. One of its main cultural manifestations is the Congado de Nossa Senhora do Rosário, a traditional ''marujada'' group that sings marches in honor of [[Our Lady of the Rosary]] during festive occasions in the city. Founded by a group of farmers in the 1960s, it faced the risk of disbanding after the death of Albertino Daniel, one of its pioneers, but persisted with around 20 members, according to 2011 information.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> Through the [[Rouanet Law]], [[Usiminas]], in partnership with the municipal government, occasionally brings a diverse program to Santana do Paraíso, including workshops, cultural performances, and cinema. These events take place in schools and the city's main squares, open to the general public.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.plox.com.br/cultura/circuito-usiminas-de-cultura-movimenta-ipatinga-e-santana-do-paraiso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193809/http://www.plox.com.br/cultura/circuito-usiminas-de-cultura-movimenta-ipatinga-e-santana-do-paraiso |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Circuito Usiminas de Cultura movimenta Ipatinga e Santana do Paraíso |author=Plox |date=22 March 2016 |accessdate=3 July 2016 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Landmarks and events ===
[[File:Salto de parapente na Serra da Viúva, Santana do Paraíso MG2.jpg|thumb|left|Horizon of the municipality seen from Serra da Viúva]]
Santana do Paraíso is part of the Mata Atlântica de Minas Gerais Tourist Circuit, officially established in 2010 by the Minas Gerais State Tourism Secretariat to promote tourism in the region.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.turismo.mg.gov.br/images/stories/circuitos/lista-circuitos.pdf |title=Circuitos Turísticos de Minas Gerais |author=Secretaria de Turismo de Minas Gerais |date=18 March 2013 |accessdate=3 July 2016 |archivedate=19 October 2014|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20141019075824 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The main natural attractions of the municipality are the numerous trails, forests, lagoons, and waterfalls in the rural area, some equipped with infrastructure for visitors.<ref name="Férias_Paraíso">{{cite web|url=http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/3785/santana-do-paraiso-mg.html |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20130101003439/http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/3785/santana-do-paraiso-mg.html |archivedate=1 January 2013 |title=Principais Pontos Turísticos |author=Ferias Tur |accessdate=14 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703194023/http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/103384-25/o-giro/santana-do-paraiso-tem-varias-cachoeiras-fantasticas |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Santana do Paraíso tem várias cachoeiras fantásticas |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=14 May 2016 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref> Among the most visited waterfalls are Engenho, Cima, Bela Vista, and Bitinga.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6551/Beleza-natural |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160711212116/http://www.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/6551/Beleza-natural |archivedate=11 July 2016 |title=Beleza natural |author=Prefeitura |work=Prefeitura Municipal de Santana do Paraíso |date=21 August 2011 |accessdate=12 July 2016}}</ref> Many of its small rivers are suitable for extreme sports such as ''aqua-[[trekking]]'',<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.plox.com.br/caderno/educa%C3%A7%C3%A3o/alunos-de-turismo-participam-de-acqua-trekking-em-santana-do-para%C3%ADso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20120907035630/http://www.plox.com.br/caderno/educa%C3%A7%C3%A3o/alunos-de-turismo-participam-de-acqua-trekking-em-santana-do-para%C3%ADso |archivedate=7 September 2012 |title=Alunos de Turismo participam de acqua trekking em Santana do Paraíso |author=Plox |date=15 May 2009 |accessdate=14 October 2010 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and there are rock formations used for [[climbing]] and [[paragliding]] jumps.<ref name="Diário_21aniversário"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/101946-3/policia/resgate-na-serra-do-paraiso |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703194255/http://diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/101946-3/policia/resgate-na-serra-do-paraiso |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Resgate na serra do Paraíso |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=14 March 2016 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref>

One of the sites used for paragliding is Serra da Viúva, which reaches an altitude of 900 meters. The José Paulino dos Santos Paragliding Ramp, located in this geological formation, is a reference for the sport in the region and has attracted practitioners from other states and countries. It is open to visitors and also offers scenic views.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://turismo.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/detalhe-da-materia/info/encontro-voo-livre-serra-da-viuva/6677 |title=Encontro Voo Livre Serra da Viúva |author=Prefeitura |date=20 November 2013 |accessdate=6 September 2024 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20240907022358/https://turismo.santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/detalhe-da-materia/info/encontro-voo-livre-serra-da-viuva/6677 |archivedate=7 September 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0115276-santana-do-paraiso-completa-32-anos-em-28-de-abril-de-2024 |title=Santana do Paraíso completa 32 anos em 28 de abril de 2024 |author=Jornal Diário do Aço |date=28 April 2024 |accessdate=6 September 2024 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20240907022428/https://www.diariodoaco.com.br/noticia/0115276-santana-do-paraiso-completa-32-anos-em-28-de-abril-de-2024 |archivedate=7 September 2024}}</ref> Another geographical landmark used for tourism is Pedra dos Tancredos, located near the city center. It serves as a city reference point and is used for climbing.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/atracoes/santana-do-paraiso/pedreira-dos-tancredos |title=Pedreira dos Tancredos |author=Secretaria de Estado de Cultura e Turismo de Minas Gerais |accessdate=2 January 2025 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20250102150425/https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/atracoes/santana-do-paraiso/pedreira-dos-tancredos |archivedate=2 January 2025}}</ref>
[[File:Pistas de pedestres e ciclistas na Praça de Eventos Dona Maria do Carmo, Santana do Paraíso MG.JPG|thumb|right|Pedestrian and cyclist paths at Dona Maria do Carmo Events Square]]

In the urban area, notable assets listed as [[cultural heritage]] by the municipal government include the Santana Mother Church, located in Praça da Matriz in the city center, maintained by the Santana Parish; the [[Moraceae|mulberry tree]] on Minas Gerais Avenue, a landmark at the city's entrance via MG-232; and the Ipaba do Paraíso Manor, which served as a residence for railway workers involved in the construction of the [[Vitória-Minas Railway]] (EFVM) in the region and later functioned as a school and health center.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> Both Praça da Matriz and Dona Maria do Carmo Events Square, both in the city center, are also city landmarks and frequently used to host events.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.itatiaia.com.br/valedoaco/2023/09/16/praca-matriz-recebe-festival-paraiso-gastronomico-neste-final-de-semana |title=Praça Matriz recebe Festival Paraíso Gastronômico neste final de semana |author=Gabriel Barroso |date=16 September 2023 |publisher=Rádio Itatiaia |accessdate=2 January 2025 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20250102150441/https://www.itatiaia.com.br/valedoaco/2023/09/16/praca-matriz-recebe-festival-paraiso-gastronomico-neste-final-de-semana |archivedate=2 January 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://vox97.com.br/noticia/3465/praca-de-eventos-dona-maria-do-carmo-e-reinaugurada-em-santana-do-paraiso |title=Praça de Eventos Dona Maria do Carmo é reinaugurada em Santana do Paraíso |author=Vox |date=5 July 2024 |accessdate=2 January 2025 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20250102150714/https://vox97.com.br/noticia/3465/praca-de-eventos-dona-maria-do-carmo-e-reinaugurada-em-santana-do-paraiso |archivedate=2 January 2025}}</ref>

=== Holidays ===
In Santana do Paraíso, there are two municipal [[holidays]] and eight national holidays, in addition to optional holidays. The municipal holidays are the city's anniversary, celebrated on 28 April; and the day of [[Saint Anne]], the municipal patron saint, observed on 26 July.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/16807/FERIADOS-NO-MUNICIPIO-PARA-O-ANO-DE-2015 |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20160703193122/http://webcache.googleusercontent.com/search?q=cache:Z8GNtbfSAgwJ:santanadoparaiso.mg.gov.br/Materia_especifica/16807/FERIADOS-NO-MUNICIPIO-PARA-O-ANO-DE-2015+&cd=2&hl=pt-BR&ct=clnk&gl=br |archivedate=3 July 2016 |title=Feriados no município para o ano de 2015 |author=Prefeitura | work=Prefeitura Municipal de Santana do Paraíso |date=2 June 2015 |accessdate=3 July 2016}}</ref>
