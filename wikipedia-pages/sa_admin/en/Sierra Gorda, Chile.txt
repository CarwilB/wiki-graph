'''Sierra Gorda''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Antofagasta Province]], [[Antofagasta Region]]. The total population was 1,516 as of the 2012 census.<ref>[http://www.geohive.com/cntry/Chile_ext.aspx 2012 Chile census] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170307204316/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/Chile_ext.aspx |date=2017-03-07 }}</ref> The two main settlements are the villages of Sierra Gorda (pop. 428) and Baquedano (pop. 825).

The [[Sierra Gorda mine|Sierra Gorda copper mine]] is located 2{{nbsp}}km north-west of the village of Sierra Gorda. Due to the mining business, Sierra Gorda has the eighth highest average household income in the country ([[Purchasing power parity|PPP]] US$34,842 in 2006).<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20071213091927/http://www.mideplan.cl/casen/modulos/comunal/2006/comunal2006cuadro4.xls Encuesta Casen, 2006], Ministry of Planning. PPP data from [http://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/weo/2008/01/weodata/weorept.aspx?sy=2006&ey=2006&scsm=1&ssd=1&sort=country&ds=.&br=1&pr1.x=28&pr1.y=13&c=228&s=PPPEX&grp=0&a= International Monetary Fund], April 2008.</ref>

The village of Sierra Gorda stages a yearly summer music festival called ''Festival del Desierto'' ("Festival of the Desert"), which attracts thousands of people from nearby towns, and even from neighboring countries such as [[Peru]] and [[Bolivia]].<ref name="latercera">La Tercera, February 10, 2008 print edition, page 68.</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Sierra Gorda had 2,356 inhabitants (1,791 men and 565 women), and is an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 65.3% (931 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Sierra Gorda is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2021-2024 alcalde is José Guerrero Venegas.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Sierra Gorda is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pedro Araya ([[Regionalist Party of the Independents|PRI]]) and Manuel Rojas ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 4th electoral district, together with [[Antofagasta]], [[Mejillones]] and [[Taltal]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Jorge Soria ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]], 2018–2026) and [[Luz Ebensperger]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]], 2018–2022) as part of the 2nd senatorial constituency (Antofagasta Region).

== 2008 ''Quantum of Solace'' incident ==
While filming for the [[James Bond]] film ''[[Quantum of Solace]]'' was under way in the village, the mayor of Sierra Gorda, [[Carlos López Vega]], drove his car onto the set because the crew had not asked his permission to film in his commune.<ref>{{cite news | author = Simon Gardner | title = James Bond has new nemesis: irate Chilean mayor | agency=Reuters | date = 2 April 2008 | url = http://uk.reuters.com/article/topNews/idUKN0237294620080402 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20080406135540/http://uk.reuters.com/article/topNews/idUKN0237294620080402 | url-status = dead | archive-date = 6 April 2008 | accessdate=13 September 2008}}</ref><ref>{{cite news | title = 'Mayor' protests on Chile 007 set |publisher=[[BBC News Online]] | date = 2 April 2008 | url = http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/entertainment/7326582.stm | accessdate=3 April 2008}}</ref>
