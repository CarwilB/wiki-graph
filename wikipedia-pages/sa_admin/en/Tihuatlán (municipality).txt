'''Tihuatlán''' is a city and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about {{convert|316|km|abbr=on}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. The municipality has a surface of {{convert|828.29|km2|abbr=on}}. The municipality has a population of 92,726 inhabitants as 2020, however, only 16 % resides in the municipal seat.<ref name=CENSUSTIHUATLAN2020>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2021/06/TIHUATLÁN_2021.pdf Censo Tihuatlán 2020] CEEIG</ref>
==Name==
The name comes from the Nahuatl language  ''cihua-tlan'', which means "place of women".<ref>{{cite web |title=Tihuatlán |url=https://veracruz.mx/destino.php?Municipio=175 |website=Veracruz: Gobierno del Estado}}</ref>

==Geography==
Tihuatlán  is delimited to the north by the municipalities of [[Temapache]] and [[Tuxpan Municipality, Veracruz|Tuxpan]], to the east by [[Papantla]], [[Poza Rica]] and [[Cazones de Herrera]], to the south by [[Coatzintla]], and to the south-west by the state of [[Puebla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30175a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tihuatlán |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> It is watered by the rivers Cazones and Tontepec, which end in the [[Gulf of Mexico]].

The weather in Tihuatlán is warm and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==
It primarily produces [[maize]], [[beans]], [[green chile]] and [[orange fruit|oranges]].

==Celebrations==
{{unreferenced section|date=December 2010}}
In Tihuatlán, in October takes place the celebration in honor of [[Saint Francis of Assisi]], Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].
