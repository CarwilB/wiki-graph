'''São Vicente Ferrer''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃w viˈsẽti fɛˈhɛ]}})  is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]]. According with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] in 2025, has an estimated population of 17,177 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" /> It has a total area of {{convert|110.49|km2}}.

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Macaparana]]  ('''N'''), [[Machados]]   ('''S''')   [[Vicência]]  ('''E''');  [[Orobó]] and [[Paraíba]] state  ('''W''').
* Area - 110.49&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 419&nbsp;m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Annual average temperature - 23.1&nbsp;°C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 131&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''São Vicente Ferrer''' are related with commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creations of [[cattle]]; and plantations of [[banana]]s, [[grapes]] and [[sugarcane]].
