The '''San Jerónimo Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento San Jerónimo''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the {{province|Santa Fe|Argentina}}. It is located in the center-south of the province. It limits with the [[Paraná River]] in the east; and from there (going clockwise) with the departments of [[San Lorenzo Department|San Lorenzo]] and [[Iriondo Department|Iriondo]] (south), [[Belgrano Department, Santa Fe|Belgrano]] (southwest), [[San Martín Department, Santa Fe|San Martín]] (west), and [[Las Colonias Department|Las Colonias]] and [[La Capital Department, Santa Fe|La Capital]] (north).

The department has over 77,000 inhabitants. Its head town is [[Coronda]] (population 17,000). Other cities and towns are [[Arocena, Argentina|Arocena]], [[Barrancas, Santa Fe|Barrancas]], [[Bernardo de Irigoyen, Santa Fe|Bernardo de Irigoyen]], [[Campo Piaggio]], [[Casalegno, Argentina|Casalegno]], [[Centeno, Argentina|Centeno]], [[Desvío Arijón]], [[Díaz, Argentina|Díaz]], [[Gaboto]], [[Gálvez, Santa Fe|Gálvez]], [[Gessler, Argentina|Gessler]], [[Larrechea]], [[Loma Alta, Argentina|Loma Alta]], [[López, Santa Fe|López]], [[Maciel, Argentina|Maciel]], [[Monje, Argentina|Monje]], [[Pueblo Irigoyen]], [[San Eugenio, Argentina|San Eugenio]], [[San Fabián, Santa Fe|San Fabián]], [[San Genaro]], and [[San Genaro Norte]].
