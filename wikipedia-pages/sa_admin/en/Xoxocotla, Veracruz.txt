'''Xoxocotla''' ({{audio|Xoxokotla.ogg|pronunciation}}; [[Nahuatl]]: "place of green pines" or "place of old [[ocote]]s"<ref name=encyclopedia/>) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|103|km}} south of the state capital [[Xalapa]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Xoxocotla is located in the [[Sierra de Zongolica]], which are foothills of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] in central Veracruz. It lies at an altitude between {{convert|2100|and(-)|2700|m}}. It borders the Veracruzian municipalities of [[Soledad Atzompa]] to the northwest, [[Atlahuilco]] to the northeast, and [[Tlaquilpa]] to the east, as well as the municipality of [[Vicente Guerrero, Puebla|Vicente Guerrero]] in the state of [[Puebla]] to the south.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Xoxocotla, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=5 March 2018 |archive-date=4 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804015215/http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|37.398|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.05% of the state's area.<ref name=snim/>

The land in Xoxocotla is divided between farmland (50%), forest (44%) and urban areas (6%). Soils in the municipality consist of [[luvisol]]s (73%) and [[regosol]]s (21%). The municipality is drained by tributaries of the [[Blanco River (Veracruz)|Blanco River]], which itself drains into [[Alvarado Lagoon]] at the mouth of the [[Papaloapan River]].<ref name=compendium/>

Xoxocotla has a temperate climate with rain in the summer. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|100|and(-)|1100|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The municipality of was incorporated on 30 November 1870, forming part of the canton of Zongolica in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=121, 315 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0 |archive-date=2018-02-09 |access-date=2018-03-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180209182348/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Magdaleno Juárez Pérez.<ref>{{cite news |last=Huerta |first=Verónica |url=https://formato7.com/2017/12/14/aceptan-alcaldes-de-morena-formar-parte-del-grupo-de-coordinacion-veracruz/ |title=Aceptan alcaldes de Morena formar parte del Grupo de Coordinación Veracruz |language=es |publisher=Formato 7 |date=14 December 2017 |access-date=5 March 2018 |archive-date=6 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180306142255/https://formato7.com/2017/12/14/aceptan-alcaldes-de-morena-formar-parte-del-grupo-de-coordinacion-veracruz/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Xoxocotla recorded a population of 5163 inhabitants living in 1137 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=137 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Xoxocotla |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=5 March 2018 |archive-date=1 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180301225014/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=137 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 5446 inhabitants in Xoxocotla, 92.29% of whom reported being of Indigenous ancestry and 37.33% of whom reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African]] ancestry.<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 1163 people or 23% of the population in Xoxocotla reported speaking an Indigenous language, 1118 of which spoke Nahuatl.<ref name=snim/>

There are 17 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Xoxocotla, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2362 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Important economic activities in Xoxocotla include pig farming and the growing of corn and beans.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2016/05/Xoxocotla.pdf |title=Cuadernillos municipales, 2016: Xoxocotla |language=es |page=6 |publisher=SEFIPLAN Veracruz |access-date=5 March 2018}}</ref>
