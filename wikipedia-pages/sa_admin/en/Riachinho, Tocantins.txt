'''Riachinho''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]] in the [[Northern Region, Brazil|Northern region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=171855|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Tocantins {{!}} Riachinho|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-30}}</ref>
