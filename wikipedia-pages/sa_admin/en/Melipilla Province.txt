'''Melipilla Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Melipilla}}) is one of six [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] in central [[Chile]]. It spans an area of {{cvt|4065.7|km2}}. Its capital is [[Melipilla]]. It had a population of 205,718 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
Chile was re-organized into twelve regions excluding the Santiago Metropolitan Area by Law No. 575 enacted on 10 July 1974.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> The [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] was officially created as per Law No.3260, enacted on 3 March 1980, and was made up of six provinces including Melipilla.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://nuevo.leychile.cl/servicios/Consulta/Exportar?radioExportar=Normas&exportar_formato=pdf&nombrearchivo=DL-3260_01-ABR-1980&exportar_con_notas_bcn=False&exportar_con_notas_originales=False&exportar_con_notas_al_pie=False&hddResultadoExportar=7083.1980-04-01.0.0%23|lang=es|title=Establece regimen de gobierno y administracion de la region metropolitana de Santiago|trans-title=Establishes the government and administration system of the Santiago metropolitan region|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> Melipilla is derived from [[Mapudungun]] language, and means "four Pillans", where [[Pillan]] is a spirit or demon.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.definition-of.com/Melipilla|title=Melipilla|work=Definition-of|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="PC">{{cite web|url=https://postal-codes.cybo.com/chile/melipilla/|title=Melipilla|work=Cybo|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Melipilla Province is one of the six provinces of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] in Chile.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Santiago-region-Chile|title=Santiago Region|work=[[Britannica]]|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="Info">{{cite web|url=https://dppmelipilla.dpp.gob.cl/conozca-la-provincia/|title=Conozca la provincia|trans-title=About the province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It covers an area of {{cvt|4065.7|km2}},<ref name="INE"/> and occupies nearly 26% of the area of the metropolitan region.<ref name="Info"/> About 85% of the land area of the province belongs to the [[Maipo River|Maipo]] and [[Rapel River]] basins, while the remaining are coastal areas.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/24|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Melipilla Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Melipilla Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> About {{cvt|63000|ha}} of the province is covered by forests, which forms sixteen percent of the total land area.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.globalforestwatch.org/dashboards/country/CHL/14/4/?gfwfires=true&map=eyJjYW5Cb3VuZCI6dHJ1ZX0%3D|title=Melipilla Province|work=Global Forest Watch|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

The region has a warm [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|16.24|C}}, and an average precipitation of {{cvt|3.3|cm}} annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/region-metropolitana-de-santiago/melipilla|title=Melipilla|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Melipilla is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'')&ndash;[[Curacaví]], [[María Pinto]], [[Melipilla]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]], and [[Alhué]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="Info"/><ref name="MN"/> The city of Melipilla serves as the capital of the province.<ref name="Info"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ycl.html|title=Province of Chile|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 205,718 inhabitants. The population consisted of 104,615 females (50.9%) and 101,103 males (49.1%). About 19.2% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 66.3% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 14.5% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 124,588 inhabitants (60.6%) and a rural population of 81,130 inhabitants (39.4%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/135__melipilla/|title=Melipilla Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 190,655 inhabitants (92.7%). Indigenous people formed 12,643 inhabitants (6.1%) of the population, while 193,058 inhabitants (93.9%) identified themselves as non-indigenous. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 108,357 adherents (65.6%), followed by [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 19,458 adherents (11.8%), and 32,836 inhabitants (19.9%) indicating no religious affiliation.<ref name="CP"/> [[Food processing]], [[cheese]] production, and [[wine]] making are the major industries in the region.<ref name="Info"/>
