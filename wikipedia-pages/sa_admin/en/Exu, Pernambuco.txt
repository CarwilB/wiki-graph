'''Exu''' is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], Brazil. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 33,486 and <!--, the density is /km²-->the area is 1336.7&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" /> Exu is the birthplace of the famous [[Luiz Gonzaga]] (1912/1989) which was a very prominent Brazilian [[Folk music|folk]] singer, songwriter, musician and poet. He is considered to be responsible for the promotion of northeastern music throughout the rest of the country. He is also known as the "king of [[Baião (music)|baião]]" and "Gonzagão".

==Geography==

* State – [[Pernambuco]]
* Region – [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries – Ceará state ('''N'''); [[Granito]] ('''S'''); [[Bodocó]] ('''W'''); [[Moreilândia]] ('''E''')
* Area – 1474&nbsp;km²
* Elevation – 523 m
* Hydrography – [[Brigida River]]
* Vegetation – [[Caatinga]] and [[Subperenifólia]] forest.
* Climate – transition between tropical hot and [[semi arid]]- hot and dry
* Annual average temperature – 24.8 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] – 607&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Exu''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of cattle, pigs, goats; and plantations of [[Maize|corn]], [[bean]]s, [[manioc]], coffee and tobacco .
