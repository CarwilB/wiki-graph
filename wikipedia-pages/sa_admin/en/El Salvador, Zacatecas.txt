'''El Salvador''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|260|km}} northeast of [[Zacatecas City]], the state capital.

==Geography==
The municipality of El Salvador lies at an elevation between {{convert|1800|and(-)|2900|m}} in the foothills of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] in northeastern Zacatecas. It borders the municipalities of [[Concepción del Oro Municipality|Concepción del Oro]] in Zacatecas to the west, [[Saltillo Municipality|Saltillo]] in [[Coahuila]] to the north, [[Galeana, Nuevo León|Galeana]] in [[Nuevo León]] to the northeast, and [[Vanegas]] in [[San Luis Potosí]] to the southeast.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32041.pdf |title=El Salvador, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|623.9|km2}} and comprises 0.8% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

The land cover in El Salvador is mostly [[Meseta Central matorral]] (81%) with small tracts of forest (11%) and grassland (8%) also present. The municipality is situated in the Matehuala sub-basin of the [[Llanos el Salado]].<ref name=compendium/>
===Climate===
El Salvador's climate ranges from semiarid to arid desert. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|300|and(-)|600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Cave paintings in the municipality demonstrate past occupation of the area by {{ill|Irritila|es}} tribes.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32041a.html |title=El Salvador |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> Colonial settlement of the area dates to the 18th century, when it was part of the Hacienda El Salado. Based in the neighbouring state of San Luis Potosí, this was one of the largest ''[[hacienda]]s'' in Mexico, and was owned by Juan Bustamante, a former governor of that state.<ref>{{cite book |last=Castro |first=Lorenzo |date=1882 |title=The Republic of Mexico in 1882 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=nJYtAQAAMAAJ&pg=PA62 |page=62 |location=New York |publisher=Thompson & Moreau |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> {{ill|Matías Ramos Santos|es|Matías Ramos}}, who was born in El Salvador in 1891, served as [[Governor of Zacatecas]] from 1932 to 1936,<ref>{{cite book |last=Rodríguez Valadez |first=Juan Manuel |title=Zacatecas: Historia de las instituciones jurídicas |url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/2.pdf |publisher=[[Senate of the Republic (Mexico)]] |location=Mexico |date=16 August 2010 |chapter-url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/11.pdf |chapter=Gobernantes de Zacatecas |language=es |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> and as [[Secretariat of National Defense (Mexico)|Secretary of National Defence]] from 1952 to 1958.<ref>{{cite book |last=Quijano Torres |first=Manuel |title=Los Gabinetes en México 1821-2012 |url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/7/3360/10.pdf |publisher=National Institute of Public Administration |location=Mexico |date=August 2012 |work=200 años de Administración Pública en México |volume=3 |page=153 |language=es |isbn=978-607-9026-20-2 |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref>

The ''congregacíon'' of El Salvador was established in the municipality of Concepción del Oro on 14 November 1964. It became an independent municipality a week later on 21 November 1964.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of El Salvador comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''),<ref name=encyclopedia/> and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://igualdad.ine.mx/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Lineamientos-de-Paridad-Zacatecas.pdf |title=Lineamientos para el registro de candidaturas a cargos de elección popular de los partidos políticos y coaliciones |language=es |pages=12–13 |date=27 November 2017 |publisher=Instituto Electoral del Estado de Zacatecas |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is Miguel Coronado Gómez.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=https://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipality of El Salvador recorded a population of 2509 inhabitants living in 733 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=36–37 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=3 January 2022}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 2710 inhabitants in El Salvador.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=027 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de El Salvador |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

There are 24 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called El Salvador, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog/> It recorded a population of 982 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 53% of El Salvador's workforce was employed in the primary sector, 16% in the secondary sector, 6% in commerce, and 24% in services.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 10.5: Población ocupada por municipio y su distribución porcentual según sector de actividad económica, al 15 de marzo de 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Zacatecas 2017 |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092047.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref> Agricultural activities in the municipality include cattle and goat farming.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/El-Salvador.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 041 El Salvador |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |date=17 June 2021 |access-date=3 January 2022}}</ref>

The [[Kansas City Southern de México]] railway runs north to south through the municipality.
