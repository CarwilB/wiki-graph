'''Saint-Louis-du-Nord''' ({{IPA|fr|sɛ̃ lwi dy nɔʁ}}; {{langx|ht|Sen Lwi dinò}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Saint-Louis-du-Nord Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Ouest (department)|Nord-Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
