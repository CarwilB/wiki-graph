'''San Antonio de Chaclla''' is one of the thirty-two districts of the province of [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]], in the [[Department of Lima]], in [[Peru]]. It borders to the north with the districts of [[Santa Rosa de Quives District|Santa Rosa de Quives]] and [[Arahuay District|Arahuay]], both belonging to the [[Canta Province|province of Canta]]; to the east with the districts of [[Huachupampa District|Huachupampa]], [[San Pedro de Casta District|San Pedro de Casta]], and [[Santa Eulalia District|Santa Eulalia]]; to the south with the district of [[Lurigancho-Chosica]], [[Lima Province|province of Lima]]; and to the west with the districts of [[San Juan de Lurigancho]] and [[Carabayllo District|Carabayllo]], both belonging to the province of Lima.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == History ==
Historically, the district is known as San Antonio de Chaclla since the founding of the town of Chaclla, located at 3,420 meters above sea level in the upper basin of the Santa Eulalia River. Within the ecclesiastical division of the [[Catholic Church in Peru|Catholic Church of Peru]], it belongs to Vicariate IV of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Chosica|Diocese of Chosica]]. The district was created by Decree Law No. 10161 on January 5, 1945, during the first government of [[President of Peru|President]] [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].

== Geography ==
It covers an area of 563.59&nbsp;km² and has a population of 37,961 inhabitants according to [[National Registry of Identification and Civil Status|RENIEC]].

== Urban Landmarks ==
In its territory, there are conflict zones bordering some districts of the province of Lima, such as: Annexes 4 (end of Los Jardines Este avenue), 22 (Beyond Montenegro), and 2 (Campoy hills and part of Huachipa) of the peasant community of Jicamarca, which are areas in dispute with the district of San Juan de Lurigancho. Likewise, there is annex 8 (Huachipa), which is the area in conflict with the Lurigancho-Chosica district.

Its de facto capital is annex 8 of Jicamarca. According to the fact that the Jicamarca peasant community legally sold by sectors: (El Cercado 350 hectares, Pedregal, Las Lomas 240 hectares, La Chancadora 200 hectares, El Palomar, El Valle 1, San Isidro 26 hectares, Ana Yauri 1000 hectares and others) in the years 1999 to 2002, as recorded in items registered in SUNARP.
