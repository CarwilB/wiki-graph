'''Sicuani District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Canchis Province|Canchis]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The most important river is the [[Urubamba River|Willkanuta]] which crosses the district from southeast to northwest.<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL maps of the Canchis Province (Cusco Region)</ref>
