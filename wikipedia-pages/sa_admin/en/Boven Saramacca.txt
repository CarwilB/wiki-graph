'''Boven Saramacca''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Sipaliwini District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 1,427.<ref name="census"/> The dominant geographical feature of this resort is the [[Saramacca River]]. The resort is mainly inhabited by [[Maroon people|Maroon]]s of the [[Matawai people|Matawai]] tribe.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Planning Office Suriname - Districts|website=Planning Office Suriname|language=nl|access-date=23 May 2020}}</ref>

The main village is [[Nieuw Jacobkondre]] which can be reached via an unpaved which connects to the [[East-West Link (Suriname)#Southern East-West Link|Southern East-West Link]] and from there to the rest of the country.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.shoeket.com/nieuwsbericht/28783/|title=Weg naar Nieuw Jacobkondre ernstig ingezakt|website=De Ware Tijd via Shoeket.com|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>  The [[Njoeng Jacob Kondre Airstrip]] also serves Nieuw Jacobkondre.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.gcmap.com/airport/SMJK|title=Njoeng Jacob Kondre, Sipaliwini, Suriname|website=GC Map|access-date=26 May 2020}}</ref> [[Poesoegroenoe]] is home to the [[granman]] of the Matawai.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/sipaliwini02.html|title=Distrikt Sipalwini 2|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref>
