'''Borgne''' ({{IPA|fr|bɔʁɲ}}; {{langx|ht|Obòy}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Borgne Arrondissement]], in [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. The [[economy]] there is based on [[agriculture]] and [[fishing|fishery]]. Other nearby places include [[Port-Margot]], [[Robin, Haiti|Robin]], and [[Fond La Grange]]. Borgne is currently in the early stages of developing a "[[sister cities]]" relationship with [[Honeoye Falls]], [[New York (state)|New York]], [[United States]].

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of seven [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Margot (communal section)|Margot]], rural
* [[Boucan Michel]], rural
* [[Petit Bourg de Borgne]], urban (Petit Bourg de Borgne neighborhood) and rural
* [[Trou d'Enfer]], rural
* [[Champagne (communal section)|Champagne]], rural
* [[Molas (communal section)|Molas]], rural
* [[Côte de Fer et Fond Lagrange]], urban (town of Borgne) and rural
