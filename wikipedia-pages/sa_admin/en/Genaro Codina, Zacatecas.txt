'''Genaro Codina''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|30|km}} southwest of the state capital at the [[Zacatecas (city)|city of Zacatecas]]. It is named after {{ill|Genaro Codina|es}}, musician and composer of the state anthem, the "[[March of Zacatecas]]".

==Geography==
The municipality of Genaro Codina is located at an elevation between {{convert|2000|and(-)|3100|m}} in the [[Sierra Fría]] in south-central Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Villanueva, Zacatecas|Villanueva]] to the west, [[Zacatecas City#The municipality|Zacatecas]] to the northwest, [[Guadalupe Municipality, Zacatecas|Guadalupe]] to the north, [[Ojocaliente]] to the northeast, and [[Cuauhtémoc Municipality, Zacatecas|Cuauhtémoc]] to the east. It also borders the municipalities of [[Rincón de Romos]] to the southeast and [[San José de Gracia, Aguascalientes|San José de Gracia]] to the south, which are both in the [[Aguascalientes|state of Aguascalientes]].<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32012.pdf |title=Genaro Codina, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=1 January 2022}}</ref> Genaro Codina covers an area of {{convert|794.8|km2}} and comprises 1.1% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 14% of the land in Genaro Codina is used for agriculture. The remainder of the land cover comprises grassland (50.2%), forest (28.6%), and [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (6.7%). The municipality is situated in the drainage basin of the [[Río Grande de Santiago]]. The eastern two-thirds of the municipality is drained by the San Pedro or Aguascalientes River, a tributary of the [[Río Verde (Jalisco)|Río Verde]], while the western third is drained by the [[Juchipila River]] and its tributaries.<ref name=compendium/>

Genaro Codina's climate ranges from semiarid in most of the municipality to temperate in the mountainous south. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|800|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
At the time of Spanish contact, the area of what is now Genaro Codina was populated by the [[Zacateco]] people. In 1542, the settlement of San José de la Ysla (or Isla) was founded. [[Tlaxcaltec|Tlaxcalan]] colonists joined the settlement in 1591.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32012a.html |title=Genaro Codina |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=1 January 2022 |archive-date=4 July 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704101439/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32012a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

After independence, San José de la Ysla formed part of the ''partido'' or district of Zacatecas in the state of the same name. Its municipal government (''junta municipal'') was first established on 29 October 1833. It dropped the "San" from its name in 1935, becoming known as José de la Isla. It adopted its present name beginning on 1 January 1958, in honour of the composer of the Zacatecan state anthem. A health centre was inaugurated in Genaro Codina in 1964, and electricity and water services followed in 1971.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=1 January 2022}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Genaro Codina comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''),<ref name=encyclopedia/> and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://igualdad.ine.mx/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Lineamientos-de-Paridad-Zacatecas.pdf |title=Lineamientos para el registro de candidaturas a cargos de elección popular de los partidos políticos y coaliciones |language=es |pages=12–13 |date=27 November 2017 |publisher=Instituto Electoral del Estado de Zacatecas |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is Mario Adrián Reyes Santana.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=1 January 2022}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Census, Genaro Codina recorded a population of 8168 inhabitants living in 2054 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=40–41 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=1 January 2022}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 8104 inhabitants in Genaro Codina.<ref name=snim/>

There are 54 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Genaro Codina, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=012 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Genaro Codina |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=1 January 2022 |archive-date=13 November 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211113053340/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It recorded a population of 1428 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 21% of Genaro Codina's workforce was employed in the primary sector, 46% in the secondary sector, 7% in commerce, and 23% in services.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 10.5: Población ocupada por municipio y su distribución porcentual según sector de actividad económica, al 15 de marzo de 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Zacatecas 2017 |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092047.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |access-date=1 January 2022}}</ref> The main crops grown in Genaro Codina are corn (grain and silage) and [[Opuntia|prickly pear]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Genaro-Codina.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 012 Genaro Codina |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |access-date=1 January 2022}}</ref>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 45D|Federal Highway 45D]] runs through the northeastern corner of the municipality, connecting it to [[Guadalupe, Zacatecas|Guadalupe]] and [[Zacatecas (city)|Zacatecas]] to the north, and to the [[Aguascalientes (city)|city of Aguascalientes]] to the south.
