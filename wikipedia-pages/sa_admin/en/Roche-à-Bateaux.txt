'''Roche-à-Bateaux''' ({{IPA|fr|ʁɔʃ a bato}}; {{langx|ht|Wòchabato}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Côteaux Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2015, the commune had 18,394 residents.

==History==
Roche-à-Bateaux was hit by a category 4 tropical storm in 2016, [[Hurricane Matthew]]<ref>{{Cite news |date=2016-10-08 |title=Hurricane Matthew: Haiti south '90% destroyed' |language=en-GB |work=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/world-latin-america-37596222 |access-date=2022-05-21}}</ref> directly passed over the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]] over the course of 36 minutes. 50 people were killed as a result of the 9.8ft (3m) storm surge<ref>{{Cite web |title=Storm Surge Overview |url=https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/surge/#:~:text=Storm%20surge%20is%20an%20abnormal,surge%20and%20the%20astronomical%20tide. |access-date=2022-05-21 |website=www.nhc.noaa.gov}}</ref> which hit the coastal town, strong winds of 145mph also destroyed 62% of homes in the municipality.
