'''Frei Paulo''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|fɾej ˈpawlu|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 15,556 (2020). Frei Paulo covers {{convert|400.4|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population density of 38 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
