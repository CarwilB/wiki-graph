'''Parral''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Linares Province]] of [[Chile]]'s [[Maule Region]].

==Geography==
Parral is located {{convert|40|km|mi|abbr=on}} south of [[Linares, Chile|Linares]] and 97 kilometers south of [[Talca]], on the southern border of the [[Maule Region]] and Linares Province. Parral borders on the north with the communes of [[Longaví]], [[Retiro, Chile|Retiro]] and [[Colbún]] (with which it shares a short border in the [[Andes|cordillera]]na zone); on the west with the commune of [[Cauquenes]] ([[Cauquenes Province]]); on the south with the commune of Ñiquén and [[San Fabián]] and [[Ñuble Region]]); and on the east, with the commune of San Fabián.

The commune of Parral is {{convert|1638.4|km2|mi2|abbr=on}} in area.<ref name="INE"/> The river [[Perquilauquén]] forms its southern border.{{citation needed|date=April 2020}}

==History==
{{expand section|date=April 2020}}
Parral was founded in 1795 by the [[Viceroyalty of Peru|Viceroy of Peru]], [[Ambrosio O'Higgins]].  It was originally named Villa [[Maria Luisa of Parma|Reina Luisa]] del Parral in honor of the wife of [[Carlos IV]], the King of Spain.{{citation needed|date=April 2020}}

Parral is the birthplace of poet [[Pablo Neruda]], winner of the [[Nobel Prize in Literature]] in 1971.

==Demography==
The city of Parral is the second most populous city in Linares Province with a population of more than 26,000. According to the 2002 [[census]], 26,397 (70.0%) live in [[urban area]]s and 11,425 (30.0%) in surrounding [[rural]] areas. The ratio of men to women is 101 to 100. Between the census of 1992 and that of 2002, the population of the municipality fell by 0.6% (245 people), exclusively in the rural population; the city of Parral grew by around 1,900 people (7.7%).<ref name="INE"/> Parral has welcomed immigration from [[Italy]] and [[Germany]].{{citation needed|date=April 2020}}

==Administration==
As a commune, Parral is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Israel Urrutia Escobar ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Parral is represented{{when|date=April 2020}} in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by [[Guillermo Ceroni]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Ignacio Urrutia ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 40th electoral district, together with [[Longaví]], [[Retiro, Chile|Retiro]], [[Cauquenes]], [[Pelluhue]] and [[Chanco, Chile|Chanco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Hernán Larraín]] (UDI) and Ximena Rincón González (PDC) as part of the 11th senatorial constituency (Maule-South).{{citation needed|date=April 2020}}

==Attractions==
[[File:Teatro Parral.JPG|160px|thumb|left|Theater and Public Library of Parral.]]
Natural and historical attractions in the area include:

*Termas de Catillo, [[thermal baths]] located {{convert|27|km|mi|abbr=on}} east of Parral and {{convert|320|m|ft|abbr=on}} above sea level, and the site of a 200-room hotel complex;
*The Digua Dam reservoir, a largely undeveloped recreational and fishing area;
*The Bullileo Dam reservoir and Laguna de Amargo recreational area;
*Fort Viejo, a recreational area and an [[archaeological site]] from the time of the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas]] (''La Conquista'');
*La Balsa, a recreational area near the [[Andes]], with thermal baths nearby.
