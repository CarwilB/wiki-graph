'''Yamparáez''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Chuquisaca Department|Chuquisaca]]. It is divided in two sections: first section with head in the town of Tarabuco, and the second section with its head in Yamparáez.

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
