'''Olivar''' is a commune in [[Chile]], located in the [[O'Higgins Region]], 10&nbsp;km south of [[Rancagua]], and administered by the municipality of Olivar. The main economic activities range from [[agriculture]] and food production, especially [[apple]]s for exportation.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Olivar spans an area of {{convert|44.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 12,335 inhabitants (6,244 men and 6,091 women). Of these, 7,898 (64%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,437 (36%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 8.9% (1,003 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Olivar is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an ''[[alcalde]]'' (mayor) who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 mayor is Maria Estrella Montero Carrasco.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Olivar is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by [[Ricardo Rincón González|Ricardo Rincón]]  ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Felipe Letelier]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 33rd electoral district, together with [[Mostazal]], [[Graneros]], [[Codegua]], [[Machalí]], [[Requínoa]], [[Rengo]], [[Doñihue]], [[Coínco|Coinco]], [[Coltauco]], [[Quinta de Tilcoco]] and [[Malloa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Alejandro García-Huidobro]] (UDI) and [[Juan Pablo Letelier]] ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) as part of the 9th senatorial constituency (O'Higgins Region).
