'''El Dorado''' is one of ten [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[San Martín Region]] in northern [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-san-martin-por-es-tan-cautivante-esta-region-y-fiestas-se-celebran-junio-1032192.aspx|title=San Martín: ¿Por qué es tan cautivante esta región y qué fiestas se celebran en junio?|publisher=[[Andina (news agency)|Andina]]|date=7 June 2025|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Political division==
The province is divided into five districts.
* [[Agua Blanca District|Agua Blanca]] ([[Agua Blanca, San Martín|Agua Blanca]])
* [[San José de Sisa District|San José de Sisa]] ([[San José de Sisa]])
* [[San Martín District|San Martín]] ([[San Martín, El Dorado Province|San Martín]])
* [[Santa Rosa District, El Dorado|Santa Rosa]] ([[Santa Rosa, El Dorado Province|Santa Rosa]])
