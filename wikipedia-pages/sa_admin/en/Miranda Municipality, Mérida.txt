'''Miranda''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 22,879.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Timotes]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Miranda Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named [[Miranda Municipality (disambiguation)#Venezuela|Miranda Municipality]] after the Venezuelan revolutionary and independence hero [[Francisco de Miranda]].
