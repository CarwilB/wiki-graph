'''Areado''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020, its population was estimated to be 15,181.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/areado/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Most of the population lives in the city. The estimated population living in the city was 9,790. This city is the most urban city among the small cities nearby.

The city has an area of 281 square kilometers. The [[population density]] is about 46, which is far more than the nearest city, [[Alterosa]], which has a population density of just 37.

The city is located near the [[Furnas Reservoir|Furnas' dam]].
