The '''Telchac Pueblo Municipality''' (es: Municipio de Telchac Pueblo) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. It is located to the north of the [[Yucatán Peninsula]].

== Toponymy ==
[[File:Downtown of Telchac Pueblo.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Downtown from top of Church]]
The name of municipality comes from the [[Yucatec Maya language|Maya language]] and officially translated into [[Spanish language|Spanish]] means "Rain or necessary water", as the result of the words ''Tel'', contraction of ''teel'', indispensable or necessary and ''chac'', contraction of ''chaac'', rain or water. Erroneously it is said that meaning is "Red Rooster" (Tel means rooster and Chac means red).

The denomination ''"Pueblo"'' ({{langx|en|Town}}), is used to differentiate it from the nearby town called [[Telchac Puerto]] ({{langx|en|Port of Telchac}}). Both were once a single municipality, but in 1932, for political problems, it split into two municipalities. When [[Telchac Puerto]] was part of Telchac Pueblo its name was Miramar, and even now there is an area that keeps that name in Telchac Puerto, Telchac Pueblo kept that area after the separation, but eventually, lost that part and the town lost all the territory bordering the sea.

== Geography ==
Telchac Pueblo is located in the north-central part of the state of [[Yucatán (state)|Yucatán]], and has a land area of 81.75 square kilometers. It shares borders on the north with the [[Telchac Puerto Municipality]], on the east with the [[Sinanché Municipality]], on the south with [[Motul Municipality]] and on the west with [[Dzemul Municipality]].

=== Topography and hydrography ===
As in most of the [[Yucatán Peninsula]], the territory is quite flat, reaching a maximum altitude of 10 meters above sea level, and slopes gently downward from south to north, towards the [[Gulf of Mexico]];<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/eleva.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Elevaciones Principales |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232751/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/eleva.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref> physiographically the whole territory belongs to the ''Yucatecan Carso''.<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/fisio.cfm?c=444&e=09 |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Fisiografía |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20110722165014/http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/fisio.cfm?c=444&e=09 |archivedate = 2011-07-22 }}</ref>

All over town there are no [[river]]s due to high soil [[Permeability (earth sciences)|permeability]],<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/sombreado_ri.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa con Principales ríos |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232846/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/sombreado_ri.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref> this is a characteristic of the Yucatan area: water seeps into the ground where it forms underground rivers that form cenotes, which are outcrops to the surface of these subterranean river, in Telchac Pueblo there are 11 cenotes, 10 of which are open and a 1 semi-open.<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://www.seduma.yucatan.gob.mx/cenotes-grutas/censo-cenotes.php |title = Censo de cenotes y grutas de Yucatán |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Gobierno del Estado de Yucatán, Secretaría de Desarrollo Urbano y Medio Ambiente |archive-date = 2010-05-23 |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20100523182941/http://www.seduma.yucatan.gob.mx/cenotes-grutas/censo-cenotes.php |url-status = dead }}</ref> The entire town is part of the ''Yucatan Basin'' belonging to the ''Northern Yucatan hydrological region''.<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/rh.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Regiones Hidrológicas |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232858/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/rh.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref>

=== Climate and Ecosystems ===
In Telchac Pueblo there are two kind of climate, in the extreme south the climate is classified as ''Warm humid with rains in summer, less humid'', and the rest of the municipality is ''Semidry warm and very warm'';<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/clim.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Climas |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232926/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/clim.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref> annual average temperature recorded is greater than 26&nbsp;°C;<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/temperat.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Temperatura Media Anual |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232734/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/temperat.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref> Average annual precipitation increases from north to south, ergo, away from the coast in successive bands, the first from 500 to 600&nbsp;mm, second from 600 to 700&nbsp;mm, third from 700 to 800&nbsp;mm and the last from 800 to 1,000&nbsp;mm.<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/precipit.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Precipitación Promedio Anual |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714232717/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/precipit.cfm |archivedate = 2007-07-14 }}</ref>

Telchac Pueblo is covered by [[tropical rainforest]], but significant parts have been converted to agricultural and [[urban area]]s.<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/agri.cfm |title = Yucatán. Mapa de Agricultura y Vegetación |accessdate = 2010-05-22 |author = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20090727184130/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/yuc/agri.cfm |archivedate = 2009-07-27 }}</ref> Tree species include  pochote (''[[Ceiba pentandra]]''), cedro (''[[Cedrela odorata]]'') and chacá (''[[Bursera simaruba]]''). Fauna includes [[rabbit]]s, [[opossum]]s, [[pocket gopher]]s, [[eared dove]], [[white-winged dove]], and the endemic species of white [[owl]] and [[Eumomota superciliosa|pájaro tho]].

== Infrastructure ==
[[File:Telchac Pueblo comunicaciones.svg|thumb|right|280px|Infrastructure and communications]]

=== Communications ===
The municipality of Telchac Pueblo is connected to the rest of the state of Yucatan by land. Within its territory there are no ports, airports or airstrips.

The main road is the 172 state-owned, This road connects in north with Telchac Puerto, in south with the city of Motul where it connects with 176 federal highway; the second state-owned road connects in west with [[Dzemul]] and [[Baca, Yucatán|Baca]], in east with [[Sinanché]], [[Yobaín]] and [[Dzidzantún]] where it connects with 178 federal highway. There is also at the southern end of the municipality a secondary road from road 172 that communicates with the village of [[Uci]], which belongs to the neighboring city of [[Motul, Yucatán|Motul]].

=== Education ===
In education the town has two kindergartens, three elementary, one secondary and one high school. There is a library with free online computers and free wireless internet.
[[File:Parque Bandera.jpg|200px|thumb|right|Park Dedicated to the [[Flag of Mexico]].]]

=== Churches ===
The [[Catholic]] Church of San Francisco de Asís (St. Francis of Assisi) was originally dedicated to [[Lawrence of Rome|St. Lawrence Martyr]], it is now dedicated to [[St. Francis of Assisi]], the patron saint of the village. It is located on the east side of the communities center. The exact timeframe of construction is not known, but the building dates from the end of the 17th century. Many theories suggest the year 1693, but is not official.

While the majority of the population is Catholic, other churches are present in the community, including [[Presbyterian]] and [[Evangelical]] churches, a [[Jehovah's Witnesses]] [[Kingdom Hall]] and most recently a [[meetinghouse]] for [[the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints]].

=== Flag Park ===
This park is dedicated to the Mexican flag, which it proudly waves at its center monument. Its also a meeting and gathering point for the "Telchaqueños", most every day and night of the year you can find Telchaqueños out in the park "breathing fresh air" or just chatting, is a deeply rooted tradition among the residents, which in municipalities of the surrounding area is not seen as often. In addition, this park has 4 posts; two of them serve food, where people tend to eat at night, one serves snacks like "granisados" and "Machacados" the 4th is a small convenience store. The park has free wireless internet.

There are also another park called the "park to the mother", a gas station, a bank, three sports fields, two basketball courts and playground

== Economy ==
Most of the town's population is engaged in primary sector of the economy, particularly to [[agriculture]]. [[Animal husbandry]] is also important, particularly [[livestock]].

Trade and manufacturing activity are other important sectors of economic activity in the municipality.

The professionals who work in the city of Mérida, other municipalities, other states or countries, strengthen the local economy via remittances. Professionals who work in the state, usually still live in Telchac Pueblo and only move to work.

== Politics ==
[[File:PalaciodeTelchac.jpg|thumb|right|City Council of Telchac Pueblo]]
The government of the municipality of Telchac Pueblo corresponds to the [[Ayuntamiento]], composed of five council members, the first holds the post of [[Municipal president (Mexico)|Municipal President]] and the second the [[Syndic]]. Of the five council members, three are elected by relative majority and two by proportional representation;<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://www.congresoyucatan.gob.mx/pdf/leyes_2006/ley%20electoral.pdf |title = Ley de Instituticiones y Procedimientos electoral del Estado de Yucatán, Artículo 7, Párrafo IV |accessdate = 2010-05-21 |author = H. Congreso del Estado de Yucatán |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20091228230257/http://www.congresoyucatan.gob.mx/pdf/leyes_2006/ley%20electoral.pdf |archivedate = 2009-12-28 }}</ref> all are elected by direct and secret vote for a period of three years without the possibility of immediate reelection and take office on July 1 of the year in which they are elected.<ref>{{Cite web |url = http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Periodos_de_gobierno_municipal |title = Periodos de gobierno municipal |accessdate = 2010-05-21 |author = Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal |url-status = dead |archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20141017030747/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Periodos_de_gobierno_municipal |archivedate = 2014-10-17 }}</ref>
