'''Simón Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 40,730.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[San Francisco de Yare]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Simón Bolívar Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{Cite web| url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | title=Estado Miranda.- División político territorial, según municipios y parroquias | year=2003 | format=XLS | language=es | trans-title=Miranda State.- Political-territorial division, according to municipalities and parishes | access-date=2008-03-22 | url-status=dead | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | archive-date=2007-12-21}}</ref> The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Simón Bolívar Municipality (disambiguation)|Simón Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].

==Demographics==
The Simón Bolívar Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 40,730 (up from 34,454 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|310.92|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Simón Bolívar Municipality is Justo Hernández, elected on October 31, 2004, with 32% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio SIMON BOLIVAR | language=es | trans-title=Mayor of Municipality SIMON BOLIVAR | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=15&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231805/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=15&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29}}</ref>  He replaced Armando Vargas shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 - Directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - Directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-23 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; [[San Francisco de Yare]] and [[San Antonio de Yare]].<ref name=autogenerated1 />
