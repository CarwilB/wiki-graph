'''Honey''' is a town in the [[Sierra Norte de Puebla|Sierra Norte]] region of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]], on the border with the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |title=Estado de Puebla |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=4 January 2010 |url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070405081753/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |archive-date=5 April 2007 }}</ref> 
It serves as the seat of the surrounding [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] of the same name.

The municipality was established in April 1919 as '''Chila Honey''', honouring Richard Honey (c. 1829–1913), a 19th-century [[English people|English]] immigrant from Cornwall who invested heavily in mining, metallurgy and railways in the area and established a paint factory in the town.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Burton |first1=Tony |title=Did you know? A village named "Honey" |url=https://www.mexconnect.com/articles/1203-did-you-know-a-village-named-honey/ |website=MexConnect |date=14 March 2008}}</ref> In 1993 the name was officially shortened to its present form.<ref>{{cite web |title=Honey |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM21puebla/municipios/21057a.html |website=INAFED |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200807212417/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM21puebla/municipios/21057a.html |archive-date=7 August 2020}}</ref>
