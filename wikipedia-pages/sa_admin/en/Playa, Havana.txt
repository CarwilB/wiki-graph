'''Playa''' is a [[Cuba|Cuban]] municipality, located in the Havana province. It covers an area of 36.8 square kilometers, which makes up 8.95% of the provincial extension.

==Geography==
Playa is the most northwestern of the ''municipios''. It stretches from the [[Almendares River]] in the east, to [[Santa Fe, Havana|Santa Fe]] in the west. 

It includes the upmarket district of [[Miramar, Havana|Miramar]] and the former fishing village of [[Jaimanitas]]. Other districts include Flores, Náutico, Siboney, Kohly and Buenavista. Many societies and venues have been located in the area, including the [[Buena Vista Social Club]].<ref>{{cite book |last1=Arrechea |first1=Carmen Victoria Montejo |title=Sociedades negras en Cuba: 1878-1960 |date=2004 |publisher=Editorial de Ciencias Sociales |isbn=9789590603075 |pages=243, 277 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=fNV-AAAAMAAJ |language=es}}</ref>
