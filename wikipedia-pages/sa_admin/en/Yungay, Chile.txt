'''Yungay''' is a [[Chile]]an town and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Diguillín Province]], [[Ñuble Region]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|823.5|km|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Yungay commune had 16,814 inhabitants; of these, 11,469 (68.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 5,345 (31.8%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 8,565 men and 8,249 women residing in the commune. The commune grew 10% in population between the censuses of 1992 and 2002 (1.0% annual growth).<ref name="INE"/>

== Towns ==
The following are the towns that made up the commune as with their respective populations (according to the 2002 Census):

* Yungay, commune seat, 9288 inhabitants
* Campanario, 2181 inhabitants
* Cholguán, 718 inhabitants
* Ranchillo, 319 inhabitants
* El Roble, 152 inhabitants
* Los Castaños, 142 inhabitants
* Chillancito, 62 inhabitants

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Yungay is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. For the years 2016-2020, the alcalde is Rafael Cifuentes Rodríguez ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]), and the council members are:<ref name="Official"/><ref name="SUBDERE"/><ref>{{cite book |last1=Lomnitz |first1=Larissa |title=Chile's political culture and parties : an anthropological explanation |date=2000 |publisher=University of Notre Dame Press |isbn=026800840X |edition=1 |url=https://archive.org/details/chilespoliticalc0000lomn |url-access=registration }}</ref>
* Juana Sandoval Rojas ([[Christian Democrat Party|PDC]])
* Rodrigo Stuardo Hernández ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Ángelica Cabezas González ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

* Ricardo Ramos Martínez ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]-[[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Yungay (together with [[Chillán]], [[Coihueco]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[San Ignacio, Chile]], [[El Carmen, Chile|El Carmen]], [[Pemuco]] and [[Chillán Viejo]]) is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] as a part of the 41st electoral district. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío Coast).

== Geography ==
Even though the total area of the commune is around 823&nbsp;km squared, only 3.87&nbsp;km squared (0.47% of the total territory) has been urbanized: the cities of Yungay (with 9,288 inhabitants as of 2002) and Campanario. In the 2017 census there were 17,787 total inhabitants in the commune. The Yungay Commune is located on the eastern borders of the region, thus it contains parts of the Cordillera of the [[Andes]], where the altitude reaches between 150 and 2000 meters above sea level. Several rivers flow out of the Andes sector and cross the body of the region. Generally, the land is devoted to agricultural<ref>{{cite web |title=Chile - Agricultural Sector {{!}} export.gov |url=https://www.export.gov/article?id=Chile-Agricultural-Sector |website=www.export.gov |access-date=21 February 2019 |language=en}}</ref> purposes, principally the growth of [[Pinus radiata|California Pine]], [[wheat]], [[oats]], or [[lentils]]; or for [[livestock]] grazing.<ref>{{cite web |title=Ficha nacional y regionales |url=https://www.odepa.gob.cl/estadisticas-del-sector/ficha-nacional-y-regionales |website=ODEPA {{!}} Oficina de Estudios y Políticas Agrarias |language=es-ES}}</ref>

== Library ==
The Yungay Public [[History of libraries in Latin America|Library]] is named after the priest Oreste Montero, who was a great aid for the education in Yungay and left an impression on many generations through his positive nature.
In the present day, the library has grown and offers many new services such as a computer lab that serves many students (from one of the several high schools within the area) who don’t have access to the necessary resources
