'''Tulantepec de Lugo Guerrero''' is one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 89.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 39,561, with 47.1% men and 52.9% women.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Santiago Tulantepec de Lugo Guerrero: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://datamexico.org/en/profile/geo/santiago-tulantepec-de-lugo-guerrero |access-date=2023-06-21 |website=Data México |language=en}}</ref> Compared to 2010, the population in Santiago Tulantepec de Lugo Guerrero increased by 18.1%.
