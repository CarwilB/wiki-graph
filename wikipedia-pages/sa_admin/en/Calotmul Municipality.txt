'''Calotmul Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "place of two conjoined hills") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 361.50&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|185|km}} east of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but during the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], it became part of the [[encomienda]] system<ref name="municipios"/> and the first encomendero was Rodrigo Alvarez in 1549.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-84-00-04399-5 |page=503 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA36|accessdate=1 August 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref> Subsequent encomenderos included Nicolas del Puerto (1665), who was entrusted with 400 indigenous persons; Antonio de la Felguera Castillo (1710), who had charge over 584 natives; and Marco Ayala.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Tizimín Municipality]].<ref name="municipios"/> In 1988 it was confirmed as head of its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|accessdate=7 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=Spanish|date=1996|page=123}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of markets, parks and gardens, and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Calotmul|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=7 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
