}}'''Puyehue''' ({{IPA|es|puˈʝewe}}) is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chile]] located in [[Osorno Province]] of [[Los Lagos Region]]. It is bordered to the south by [[Rupanco Lake]], to the east by the [[Andes]] and [[Argentina]], to the north by [[Puyehue Lake]] and to the west by [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]] commune. [[Cardenal Antonio Samoré Pass]] that links the [[Los Lagos Region]] to Argentina lies in the eastern tip of the commune. The comune is administered by [[Entre Lagos, Chile|Entre Lagos]], the main town in Puyehue.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Puyehue spans an area of {{convert|1597.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 11,368 inhabitants (6,008 men and 5,360 women). Of these, 3,932 (34.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,436 (65.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 1.4% (161 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Puyehue is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Puyehue is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Fidel Espinoza ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Recondo ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 56th electoral district, together with [[Río Negro, Chile|Río Negro]], [[Purranque]], [[Puerto Octay]], [[Fresia, Chile|Fresia]], [[Frutillar]], [[Llanquihue, Chile|Llanquihue]], [[Puerto Varas]] and [[Los Muermos]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina (PS) and Carlos Kuschel Silva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
