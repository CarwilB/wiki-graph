'''Sairé''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[sajˈɾɛ]}}) is a [[municipality]] located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located 110.7&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the capital of the state of Pernambuco, it has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 10,887 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Bezerros]]   ('''N''');  [[Bonito, Pernambuco|Bonito]]    and [[Barra de Guabiraba]]   ('''S''');  [[Gravatá]]   ('''E''');   [[Camocim de São Félix]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 195.46&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 663 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca]] and [[Sirinhaém River|Sirinhaém rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] and [[hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 24.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 110.7&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Sairé''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[tomato]]es, [[tangerine]]s, [[passion fruit]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
