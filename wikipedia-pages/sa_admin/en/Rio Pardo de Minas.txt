'''Rio Pardo de Minas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northeast of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population in 2020 was 31,045 inhabitants in a total area of {{convert|3117|km2|mi2}}.<ref name=ibge>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |title=Rio Pardo de Minas |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |access-date=May 1, 2012 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120430164910/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |archive-date=April 30, 2012 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The elevation is {{convert|751|m|ft}}. It became a municipality in 1831. The postal code (CEP) is 39530–000. The population density was 9.33 inhabitants per km<sup>2</sup> (24.19 per mi<sup>2</sup>) in 2010.<ref name=ibge/> Rio Pardo de Minas is located on dirt roads (MG-342), {{convert|70|km|mi}} northwest of [[Salinas, Minas Gerais|Salinas]]. The Rio Pardo flows through the town. It is part of the statistical microregion of [[Salinas, Minas Gerais|Salinas]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/salinas/|title=Citybrazil}}</ref>

The municipality contains about 29% of the {{convert|38177|ha}} [[Nascentes Geraizeiras Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2014. The reserve protects an area of the [[cerrado]] biome.{{sfn|RDS Nascentes Geraizeiras – ISA}}
The municipality also contains part of the {{convert|49,830|ha}} [[Serra Nova State Park]], created in 2003.{{sfn|Diniz|2009}}

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising (10,000 head in 2006), small industries and agriculture.  The main crops are bananas, citrus fruits, beans, sugarcane, tomatoes, and corn.  In the rural area there were 3,205 producers on 75,000 ha.  Natural pasture made up 18,000 ha, woodland 33,000 ha. and planted area 11,500 ha.  Around 10,500 persons worked in agriculture.  The GDP in 2003 was R$64,482,000 while the per capita GDP was R$2,319.  In 2006 there was one bank.  Health needs were taken care of by 11 municipal health centers, one private health center and one hospital with 42 beds.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Health and education==
This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
