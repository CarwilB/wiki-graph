'''Jucati''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒukaˈti]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located 217&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the capital of Pernambuco. As of the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], it had a population of 11,517.<ref name="IBGEJucati2025" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[São Bento do Una]]   ('''N''');  [[Garanhuns]] and [[São João, Pernambuco|São João]]   ('''S''');   [[Jupi]]  ('''E''');    [[Capoeiras]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 120.65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 820 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] and [[Mundaú River|Mundaú rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Clima - Hot and Humid
* Annual average temperature - 20.8 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 217.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Jucati''' are based in food & beverage industry, commerce  and [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[beans]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
