''' Manuripi ''' is one of the five provinces of [[Pando Department]] in [[Bolivia]] and is situated in the northwest of the department. Its name is derived from the [[Manuripi River]], which passes through the province from west to east.

== Location ==
''Manuripi'' province is located between 10° 49’ and 12° 30' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 00' and 69° 14' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over a length of 370&nbsp;km from Northeast to Southwest, and up to 100&nbsp;km from Northwest to Southeast.

The province is situated in the [[Amazon Rainforest|Amazon]] lowlands of Bolivia and borders [[Abuná Province]] in the North, [[Nicolás Suárez Province]] in the Northwest, [[Peru]] in the West, [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in the South, [[Madre de Dios Province]] in the Southeast, [[Beni Department]] in the East, and [[Federico Román Province]] in the Northeast.

== Population ==
The population of Federico Román Province has increased by 40% over the last two decades:
*1992: 7,360 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 8,230 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 9,505 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2010: 10,164 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosPando.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística - Proyecciones] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081118153156/http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosPando.pdf |date=2008-11-18 }}</ref>
 
47.7% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

98.0% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 0.4% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], and speak 0.2% [[Aymara language|Aymara]]. ''(1992)''

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 75.4%. ''(1992)''

91.0% of the population have no access to electricity, 68.5% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

84.4% of the population are Catholics, 14.6% are Protestants. ''(1992)''
