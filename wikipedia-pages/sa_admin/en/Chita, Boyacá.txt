'''Chita''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the [[Valderrama Province]] a subregion of Boyaca in [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Chita has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> with frequent rain and cool mild temperatures.
