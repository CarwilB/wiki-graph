'''Mixquiahuala''' (officially '''Mixquiahuala de Juárez'''; [[Otomi language|Otomi]]: '''Ntʼähi''') is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 138.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 37,747.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mixquiahuala de Juárez|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13041a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129102718/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13041a.htm|archivedate=January 29, 2007}}</ref>
