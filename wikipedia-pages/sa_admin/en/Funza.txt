'''Funza''' ({{IPA|es|ˈfunsa}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Western Savanna Province]], of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Funza is situated on the [[Bogotá savanna]], the southwestern part of the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2548|m|ft}}. In Funza the [[La Florida (wetland)|La Florida wetland]], part of the [[wetlands of Bogotá]], a remnant of the [[Pleistocene]] [[Lake Humboldt, Colombia|Lake Humboldt]], still exists. The town is part of the [[Metropolitan Area of Bogotá]] and borders [[Madrid, Cundinamarca|Madrid]] and [[Tenjo]] in the north, [[Mosquera, Cundinamarca|Mosquera]] in the south, Madrid in the west and [[Cota, Cundinamarca|Cota]] and the locality [[Engativá]] of the capital [[Bogotá]] in the east. The eastern boundary is formed by the [[Bogotá River]]. Funza is the site of the former main settlement [[Bacatá]] of the [[Muisca Confederation]]. Modern Funza was founded by [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] during the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] on April 20, 1537.<ref name=websiteFunza>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.funza-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Funza] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151221031514/http://www.funza-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-12-21 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Funza comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Powerful lord".<ref name=websiteFunza/>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Funza was an important village in the [[Muisca Confederation]]. This loose confederation of [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] had a southern ruler, the ''[[zipa]]'' based in [[Bacatá]], the present-day municipality of Funza.<ref name=websiteFunza/>

Modern Funza was founded by [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] en route in his search for ''[[El Dorado]]'' on April 20, 1537.<ref name=websiteFunza/>

On January 21, 1972, a [[Líneas Aéreas La Urraca]] flight [[Líneas_Aéreas_La_Urraca#Accidents_and_incidents|crashed in Funza after an explosion on board]].<ref name=ASN210172>{{cite web|url=http://aviation-safety.net/database/record.php?id=19720121-2 |title=Accident description |publisher=Aviation Safety Network |access-date=8 October 2009}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The economy of Funza is based on agricultural and industrial activities. Main agricultural products are [[potato]]es and [[maize]]. Other industries involve plastics and metalcraft production.<ref name=websiteFunza/>
