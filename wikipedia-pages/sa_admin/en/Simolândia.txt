'''Simolândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northeast [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location and Geography==
Simolândia is situated in the northeast of Goiás, at kilometer 250 of the important BR-020 (Belém-Brasília highway).  It forms boundaries with [[Posse, Goiás|Posse]], [[Alvorada do Norte]] and [[Buritinópolis]]. It belongs to the [[Vão do Paranã]] statistical [[micro-region]].

The distance to [[Goiânia]] is 460&nbsp;km.  Highway connections are made by BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / BR-060 / [[Alexânia]] / [[Sobradinho, Federal District|Sobradinho]] / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / [[Formosa, Goiás|Formosa]] / BR-020 / BR-030 / BR-020 / [[Vila Boa, Goiás|Vila Boa]] / [[Alvorada do Norte]].<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin Anuario de Transporte] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=2009-02-05 }}</ref>

The municipality lies mainly on the slopes of the Serra da Chapadinha and the topography is broken by several rivers:  the Corrente, Salobro and Buriti, which are popular with tourists.  The climate is hot and dry.  The vegetation is made up of 60% forest and 40% savanna.  Hardwoods are exported or used in the furniture industry.

==Economic Information==
The economy is based on [[subsistence agriculture]], cattle raising (19,100 head in 2006), services, public administration, and small transformation industries.  There were no banks in 2007.  There were 362 automobiles in 2007.
*Motor vehicles:  443 (automobiles and pickup trucks) 
*Number of inhabitants per motor vehicle: 15
