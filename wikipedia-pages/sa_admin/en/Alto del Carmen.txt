'''Alto del Carmen''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and village in [[Huasco Province]], [[Atacama Region]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|5938.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> The commune has two sub-sectors; [[Del Tránsito River|Valle del Tránsito]] in the northeast and [[Del Carmen River|Valle del Carmen]] in the southwest. The former valley has a high concentration of indigenous [[Diaguita]]s and while in the latter ancestry tied to [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spanish settlers]] is common.

The commune has an economy based on agriculture, in particular grapes for [[Pisco#Chilean pisco|pisco]] and [[Paxarette|pajarete]], and mining. The [[Indigenous peoples in Chile|indigenous]] [[lineage organization]] [[Huascoaltinos]] represents farming interests in Valle del Tránsito. The now inactive [[Gold mining in Chile|gold mine]] of [[Pascua Lama]] lies in the southeastern edge of commune. The gold-mining project of [[El Alto mining project|El Alto]] remains as of January 2026 active in the northeastern part of the commune.<ref name=dfalto>{{Cite news |title=El Alto, el proyecto minero que Barrick quiere desarrollar en reemplazo de Pascua Lama |last=Durante |first=Nicolás |date=2025-04-12 |url=https://dfmas.df.cl/df-mas/por-dentro/el-alto-el-proyecto-minero-que-barrick-quiere-desarrollar-en-reemplazo |access-date=2025-05-21 |work=[[Diario Financiero]] |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |title=Prospección Minera El Alto de Barrick recibe aprobación ambiental en Atacama y avanza hacia su RCA |last=de Vicente |first=Agustín |date=2026-01-19 |url=https://www.reporteminero.cl/noticia/noticias/2026/01/barrick-prospeccion-el-alto-aprobacion-ambiental-atacama-dia |access-date=2026-02-16 |work=Reporte Minero & Energético |language=es}}</ref> The discovery of valuable [[marble|black marble]] in the commune was announced in December 2024.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Expedición descubre Mármol Negro en Atacama: Chile sería de los pocos países en el mundo en explotarlo |last=Vega Martínez |first=César |date=2024-12-19 |url=https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/sociedad/curiosidades/2024/12/19/expedicion-descubre-marmol-negro-en-atacama-chile-seria-el-segundo-pais-del-mundo-en-explotarlo.shtml |access-date=2025-06-22 |work=[[Radio Bío-Bío]] |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |title=Descubren yacimiento de mármol negro en región de Atacama |last=de Vicente |first=Agustín |date=2025-01-06 |url=https://www.reporteminero.cl/noticia/noticias/2025/01/yacimiento-marmol-negro-region-atacama |access-date=2025-05-21 |work=Reporte Minero y Energético |language=es}}</ref>

==Geography, climate and geology==
[[file:CabeceraRioHuasco.jpg|thumb|350px|Birth of [[Huasco River]] at the confluence of [[Del Tránsito River]] (left) and [[Del Carmen River]] (right).]]
Geographically the commune lies in the natural region of [[Norte Chico, Chile|Norte Chico]] and the [[Transverse Valleys]] of Chile.<ref name=ciren/>

The [[climate]] is the lower parts of commune is one of a [[Semi-arid climate|marginal desert]] and at higher elevations it is one of high-altitude marginal desert.<ref name=ciren/> Most soils in the commune are thin, and stony [[aridisol]]s. In the valley floor soils tend to be less stony.<ref name=ciren>{{Cite report |title=Recursos Naturales: Comuna Alto del Carmen |url=https://www.sitrural.cl/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/A_Carmen_rec_nat.pdf |last=CIREN |year=2021 |pages=1–6 |language=es}}</ref> Much of the geology of the commune is made up of [[metamorphic]] and [[plutonic rock|plutonic]] rock of [[Triassic]] or [[Paleozoic]] age. There are also parts where [[sedimentary rock]]s of [[Jurassic]] age are exposed.<ref name=ciren/> Large inverse [[fault (geology)|geological faults]] and associated [[fold (geology)|folds]] exists in the commune.<ref name=ciren/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Alto del Carmen had 4,840 inhabitants (2,629 men and 2,211 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 2% (95 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

The 2024 census showed that the commune is both the poorest and the one with the oldest population (average age 43 years) in Atacama Region.<ref name=zorro>{{Cite news |title=Resultados del censo ratifican la dura realidad que vive Alto del Carmen: es la comuna más longeva y pobre de la región de Atacama |date=2025-04-05 |url=https://www.elzorronortino.cl/actualidad/resultados-del-censo-ratifican-la-dura-realidad-que-vive-alto-del-carmen-es-la-comuna-mas-longeva-y-pobre-de-la-region-de-atacama/ |access-date=2025-05-21 |work=El Zorro Nortino |language=es}}</ref>

As of 2024 a third of the homes in the commune lacks one of more of the following; electricity connection, sewage system or potable water.<ref name=zorro/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Alto del Carmen is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Alto del Carmen is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Mr. Alberto Robles ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) and Mr. Giovanni Calderón  ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 6th electoral district, (together with [[Caldera, Chile|Caldera]], [[Tierra Amarilla, Chile|Tierra Amarilla]], [[Vallenar]], [[Freirina]] and [[Huasco, Chile|Huasco]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Isabel Allende Bussi ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Baldo Prokurica Prokurica ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 3rd senatorial constituency (Atacama Region).
