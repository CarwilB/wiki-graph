'''Ailigandí''' or '''Agligandi''' is a [[town]] and island in the [[Guna Yala]] [[Provinces of Panama|province]] of [[Panama]]. The island is just off the coast<ref>[https://maps.google.com/maps?ll=9.227568,-78.026233&spn=0.016838,0.01884&t=h&z=16 Ailigandí island - Google Maps]</ref> and is served by [[Ailigandí Airport]]. The town is also called '''Manglar''' or ''Mangrove Swamp''.
