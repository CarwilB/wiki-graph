'''Lima Duarte''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. It has a population of 16,724 inhabitants (2020) and a total area of {{convert|848|km2|mi2}} and lies at an elevation of {{convert|860|m|ft|0}} in the foothills of the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]]. It was given city status in 1881.

It is {{convert|330|km|mi}} from the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], and is part of the [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]], a region whose economic and cultural center is [[Juiz de Fora]]. It is connected with this city by highway BR-267.

[[Ibitipoca state park]] is situated within the municipality about {{convert|25|km|mi}} from the city center.

Average maximum temperature: {{Convert|28|C|F|0}};
Average minimum temperature: {{Convert|10|C|F|0}}

Most of the municipality is drained by the [[Paraíba do Sul River]].  The most important river is the Rio do Peixe, a tributary of the [[Paraibuna River (Minas Gerais)|Paraibuna]].

The name of the city has no connection to the famous Brazilian actor [[Lima Duarte]], but was in homage to Conselheiro José Rodrigues de Lima Duarte, political leader from [[Barbacena]], who in the nineteenth century was Minister of the Navy and a Senator of the Empire.

The city's economy is mainly based on tourism and the dairy industry.
