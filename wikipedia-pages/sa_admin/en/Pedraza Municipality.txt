The '''Pedraza Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 65,390.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended|access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of Ciudad Bolivia is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Pedraza Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Pedraza Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 62,847 (up from 52,411 in 2000).  This amounts to 8.3% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|9.4|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pedraza Municipality is Frenchy Tomas Díaz Rodríguez, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 69% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Pedraza | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D5%26mun%3D6%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231113/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D5%26mun%3D6%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-17 | url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | accessdate=March 15, 2008 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=October 2, 2006}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Ciudad Bolivia, Ignacio Briceño, José Félix Ribas, Páez.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
