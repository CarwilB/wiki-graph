'''Aipe''' is a town and municipality in the [[Huila Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is located on the west bank of the [[Magdalena River]] in the [[Cordillera Central (Colombia)|Central Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] at altitudes between {{convert|350|and|2300|m|ft}}. Aipe borders [[Natagaima]], [[Ataco]] and [[Planadas]] of the [[Tolima Department|department of Tolima]] in the north and west, [[Villavieja, Huila|Villavieja]] and [[Tello, Huila|Tello]] in the east, and the departmental capital [[Neiva]] in the south.<ref name=websiteAipe>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150617173840/http://www.aipe-huila.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Aipe]</ref>

== History ==
In early 1741, Doña Teresa and Don Enríque Cortés, descendants of Mr. Jacinto Cortés, gave 80 hectares to establish a new town, and are considered the founders of the Aipe. In 1770, the church hierarchy and government in [[Neiva, Huila|Neiva]] records the construction of the church of Our Lady of Sorrows.

In 1874, Aipe acquired autonomy from Neiva, as a member of the canton district of [[Villavieja, Huila|Villavieja]] and on 8 April 1912 by Ordinance No. 26, was created as a municipality by the Departmental Assembly.

== Geography ==
The municipality of Aipe is located on the left bank of the Magdalena River. It is located in the northwest of the Department of Huila, in the Magdalena Valley, bounded on the north and west by the [[Department of Tolima]], to the east the Magdalena River, and south by the capital of Huila; [[Neiva]].

The municipality has a total area of 801.04 km2 (3.8% total of the department). The lowest level of the municipality is located at the banks of the [[Magdalena River]] at an altitude of {{convert|350|m|ft}} above sea level, with an average annual temperature of {{convert|28.4|C|F}}. The highest point is {{convert|2300|m|ft}} above sea level, located in the vereda La Union (west of the municipal boundary with the [[Tolima Department|Department of Tolima]]), with an average temperature of 13.67&nbsp;°C.

== Paleontology ==
In the [[Loma Gorda Formation|Loma Gorda]] and [[Hondita Formation]]s in Aipe, many [[Late Cretaceous]] ([[Cenomanian]] to [[Coniacian]]) species of [[ammonite]]s have been found. The species ''[[Acanthoceras (ammonite)|Acanthoceras sp.]]'', ''[[Rhynchostreon|Rhynchostreon sp.]]'', ''[[Choffaticeras|Choffaticeras sp.]]'', ''[[Fagesia|Fagesia catinus]]'', ''[[Neoptychites|Neoptychites cf. andinus]]'', ''[[Mitonia|Mitonia gracilis]]'', ''[[Morrowites|Morrowites sp.]]'', ''[[Nannovascoceras|Nannovascoceras sp.]]'', ''[[Quitmaniceras|Quitmaniceras sp.]]'', ''[[Benueites|Benueites sp.]]'', ''[[Mytiloides|Mytiloides kossmati]]'', ''[[Mytiloides|M. goppelnensis]]'', ''[[Anomia (bivalve)|Anomia sp.]]'', ''[[Paramammites|Paramammites sp.]]'', ''[[Hoplitoides|Hoplitoides sp.]]'', ''[[Hoplitoides|H. ingens]]'', ''[[Hoplitoides|H. lagiraldae]]'', ''[[Codazziceras|Codazziceras ospinae]]'', ''[[Allocrioceras|Allocrioceras sp.]]'', ''[[Prionocycloceras|Prionocycloceras sp.]]'', ''[[Prionocycloceras|P. guayabanum]]'', ''[[Reesidites|Reesidites subtuberculatum]]'', ''[[Subprionotropis|Subprionotropis colombianus]]'', ''[[Mytiloides|Mytiloides scupini]]'', ''[[Dydimotis|Dydimotis sp.]]'', ''[[Gauthiericeras|Gauthiericeras sp.]]'', ''[[Anagaudryceras|Anagaudryceras sp.]]'', ''[[Eulophoceras|Eulophoceras jacobi]]'', ''[[Paralenticeras|Paralenticeras sieversi]]'', ''[[Hauericeras|Hauericeras madagascarensis]]'', ''[[Peroniceras|Peroniceras subtricarinatum]]'', ''[[Forresteria|Forresteria sp.]]'', ''[[Barroisiceras|Barroisiceras onilahyense]]'' and ''[[Ankinatsytes|Ankinatsytes venezolanus]]'' have been registered in Aipe.<ref name=Patarroyo2011>Patarroyo, 2011, p.499</ref>
