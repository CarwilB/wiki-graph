'''Huechuraba''' ({{IPA|es|wetʃuˈɾaβa}}) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Huechuraba spans an area of {{convert|44.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 74,070 inhabitants (36,433 men and 37,637 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 19.9% (12,286 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The projected 2006 population was 82,200 (2006 projection)<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |access-date=12 September 2010 |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archive-date=23 August 2010 }}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=September 2010}}

==Administration==
As a commune, Huechuraba is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is elected by popular vote every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Maximiliano Luksic Lederer (Ind/[[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) who was elected for the 2024-2028 term. The communal council has the following members: 
* Jorge Arancibia Labraña ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Fresia Hernández Bravo ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Bárbara Plaza Escobar ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Humberto Allendes Olivares ([[Evópoli]])
* Elisa Kaelin Tello (Ind/[[Liberal Party of Chile (2013)|PL]])
* Fernando Pérez Navarro ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Genaro Román Zamorano ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Javiera Jiménez Palomo ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Huechuraba is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by [[Érika Olivera]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]), [[Jorge Durán Espinoza]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]), [[José Carlos Meza]] ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]]), [[Andrés Giordano|Andrés Giordano Salazar]] ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]]), [[Karol Cariola]] ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]), [[Boris Barrera]] ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) and [[Maite Orsini]] ([[Democratic Revolution|RD]]), as part of the 9th electoral district, (together with [[Conchalí]], [[Quinta Normal]], [[Independencia, Chile|Independencia]], [[Recoleta, Chile|Recoleta]], [[Lo Prado]], [[Cerro Navia]] and [[Renca]]).
