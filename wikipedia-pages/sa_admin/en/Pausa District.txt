'''Pausa District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Paucar del Sara Sara Province|Paucar del Sara Sara]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. The volcano [[Sara Sara]] is situated in the district.
