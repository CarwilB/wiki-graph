'''Clevelândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in the [[Southern Region, Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. Its name is an homage to American president [[Grover Cleveland]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web
