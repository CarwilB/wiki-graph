'''Masagua''' ({{IPA|es|maˈsaɣwa}})   is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|456|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 49,085 inhabitants.

==History==
The first mention of Masagua is from the Spanish colonial texts, which refer to a church dedicated to [[Virgin Mary]]. In the late 18th century, it had a population of 104 individuals from 25 families. In 1825, Masagua became part of the department of Escuintla as per the Constitution of Guatemala. On 1 May 1830, it was elevated as a municipality and the municipality of San Juan Mixtán was added to it in 1883. In 1927, Masagua had a population of 4,830 inhabitants.<ref name="Hist"/>

Masagua derives from [[Nahuatl]], with several theories proposed as to its meaning. It roughly translates to "place of deer" or "deer that flees".

==Geography==
Masagua is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|456|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Masagua (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0505__masagua/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the southeastern part of the department, about {{cvt|12|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|71|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Escuintla]] and [[San Vicente Pacaya]] to the north, [[Puerto San José]] to the south, [[Guanagazapa]] and [[Iztapa]] to the east.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Masagua|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Masagua municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-masagua-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Municipios de Escuintla, Guatemala |url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=7:escuintla |location=Guatemala |website=Secretaría General de Planificación y Programación de la Presidencia de la República |date=n.d. |access-date=13 July 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150714014759/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=7%3Aescuintla |archive-date=14 July 2015 |language=Spanish |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2008, a power generation plant was constructed by [[:es:Jaguar Energy|Jaguar Energy]] in the municipality with an estimated capacity of 300 MW.<ref>{{cite web|last=Solano|first=Luis|title=Jaguar Energy: un multimillonario negocio en juego|url=http://www.albedrio.org/htm/noticias/cmig-104.html|access-date=13 July 2015|website=Albedrío; Centro de Medios Independientes|location=Guatemala|date=13 July 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150714004958/http://www.albedrio.org/htm/noticias/cmig-104.html|archive-date=14 July 2015}}</ref>

===Climate===
Located at an elevation of {{cvt|100|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> Masagua has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|26.27|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|124.35|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/masagua|title=Masagua|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 49,085 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 25,242 males and 23,843 females. About 30.2% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.2% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (62.9%) was classified as urban, while 37.1% lived in rural areas. About 66.2% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (97.1%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (2.8%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 84.9%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.4%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
