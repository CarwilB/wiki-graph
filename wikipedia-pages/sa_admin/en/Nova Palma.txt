'''Nova Palma''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the hydroelectric [[Dona Francisca Dam]] on the upper [[Jacuí River]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Dona Francisca – OSAB}} |title=Dona Francisca|publisher=OSAB: Observatório Sócio-Ambiental da Baragens
 |url=http://www.observabarragem.ippur.ufrj.br/barragens/9/dona-francisca|access-date=2017-01-20}}</ref>
