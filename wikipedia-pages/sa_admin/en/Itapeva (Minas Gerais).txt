'''Itapeva''' is a municipality in the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 9,881 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|177|km2}}.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|180373|ha|adj=on}} [[Fernão Dias Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Fernão Dias – ISA}}|language=pt
