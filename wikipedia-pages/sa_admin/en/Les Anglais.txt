'''Les Anglais''' ({{IPA|fr|lez‿ɑ̃ɡlɛ}}; {{langx|ht|Zanglè}}), French for "The English", is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Chardonnières Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 29,891 inhabitants. The [[Eye (cyclone)|eye]] of [[Hurricane Matthew]] made landfall near Les Anglais on 4&nbsp;October 2016 at 6&nbsp;a.m. [[Eastern Time Zone|EST]]<!-- reference uses 7am EDT (Miami time, where National Hurricane Center is located), but Haiti is one hour behind in Eastern Standard Time --> (11:00&nbsp;a.m. [[UTC]]) as a powerful [[Saffir–Simpson scale#Category 4|Category 4]] [[hurricane]] with winds of 230&nbsp;km/h (145&nbsp;mph).<ref>{{cite web|author1=Avila, Lixion|author-link1=Lixion Avila|title=Hurricane Matthew Intermediate Advisory 25a|url=http://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2016/al14/al142016.public_a.025.shtml?|publisher=[[National Hurricane Center]]|access-date=5 October 2016|date=4 October 2016|quote=Matthew made landfall near Les Anglais about 700 AM EDT (1100 UTC)....Maximum sustained winds remain near 145 mph (230 km/h) with higher gusts.  Matthew is a category 4 hurricane on the Saffir- Simpson Hurricane Wind Scale.}}</ref>
