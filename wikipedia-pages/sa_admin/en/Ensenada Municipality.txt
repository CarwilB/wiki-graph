'''Ensenada''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Baja California]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ensenada |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/bajacalifornia/municipios/02001a.htm |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070224134740/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/bajacalifornia/municipios/02001a.htm |archive-date=24 February 2007 |publisher=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |language=Spanish}}</ref> It is the fourth-largest municipality in the country, with a land area of {{convert|19526.8|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} in 2020,<ref name="Landarea">{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/default.aspx|title=México en cifras - Medio Ambiente - Superficie continental|publisher=INEGI|language=es|access-date=12 February 2021|archive-date=6 February 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210206190746/https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/default.aspx|url-status=live}}</ref> making slightly smaller than the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]] and larger than five other Mexican states.

Located offshore, [[Cedros Island]] and [[Guadalupe Island]] are part of the municipality, making Ensenada the westernmost municipality in Mexico and Latin America.

Incorporated on 15 May 1882 as the northern [[Partido (region)|partido]] of the [[Baja California Territory]], it became a municipality of the state of Baja California on 29 December 1953.<ref name="Incorporationdate">{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Baja California. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=65–66 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222284/702825222284_7.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=978-970-13-1487-6 |access-date=8 March 2021 |archive-date=6 January 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180106063527/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222284/702825222284_7.pdf |url-status=live }}</ref>

The municipality shares borders with every other municipality in the state: [[Tijuana Municipality|Tijuana]], [[Playas de Rosarito Municipality|Playas de Rosarito]] and [[Tecate Municipality|Tecate]] to the north, [[Mexicali Municipality|Mexicali]] and [[San Felipe Municipality, Baja California|San Felipe]] to the east and southeast and [[San Quintín Municipality|San Quintín]] to the south. Its municipal seat is [[Ensenada, Baja California|Ensenada]], a port lying near the northwest corner of the municipality. 
 
In 2009 a massive project was announced to build what would be Mexico's largest port in [[Punta Colonet]], a largely uninhabited area {{convert|80|km|mi|abbr=on}} south of the city of Ensenada, but as of 2025 no construction has begun. 

In February 2020, San Quintín separated from Ensenada and became Baja California's sixth municipality.<ref name="sanquintin">{{cite web|url=https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/san-quintin-sera-sexto-municipio-de-baja-california|title=San Quintín will be the sixth municipality in Baja California|publisher=El Financiero|language=es|access-date=2020-12-30|archive-date=3 March 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210303081419/https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/san-quintin-sera-sexto-municipio-de-baja-california|url-status=live}}</ref> Prior to this, Ensenada was the country's largest municipality. In January 2022, Puertecitos separated from Ensenada to San Felipe and became Baja California's seventh municipality, further reducing the size of Ensenada Municipality.{{fact|date=March 2026}}

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 443,807 in 1,698 localities.<ref name="census2020" />

Ensenada, the seat, has 330,652 residents, and the following are the largest urban communities:
* [[Maneadero|Rodolfo Sánchez Taboada (Maneadero)]] (27,969)
* El Sauzal de Rodríguez (11,371)
* El Zorrillo (8,522)
* San Vicente (5,062)
* Valle de Guadalupe (4,334)
* Valle de la Trinidad (3,381)
* Punta Colonet (3,095)
* Ojos Negros (2,707)
* Poblado Héroes de Chapultepec (2,360).
