'''Capela Nova''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2025|lc=y}} is estimated to be 4,397 people living in an area of 111&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" /> The elevation is 935&nbsp;m.  The city belongs to the mesoregion of Campo das Vertentes and to the microregion of [[Barbacena]], which lies 58&nbsp;km to the south.
