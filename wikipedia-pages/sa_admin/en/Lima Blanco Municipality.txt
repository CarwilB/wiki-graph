The '''Lima Blanco Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 9,454.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref>  The town of [[Macapo]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Lima Blanco Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{dead link|date=December 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Lima Blanco Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, had a population of 9,161 (up from 7,965 in 2000).  This amounted to 3.1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=December 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|72.13|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=December 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Lima Blanco Municipality is José Orangel Sánchez Fernández, elected on October 31, 2004, with 36% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Lima Blanco | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D8%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163448/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D8%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17 | access-date=2008-03-14 | url-status=live }}</ref>   He replaced Moraima Machado shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Macapo and La Aguadita (previous to December 30, 1994, the Lima Blanco Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
