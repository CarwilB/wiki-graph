'''Lobería Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the Atlantic coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 17,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4755|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is the town of [[Lobería]].

==Economy==
Like its neighbouring partidos on the Atlantic coast, the economy of Lobería is dominated by the summer vacation season (December–February), which sees hundreds of thousands of [[Porteño]]s make their way to the Atlantic coastline.{{Citation needed|date=February 2019}}

During the rest of the year the main economic activities are mainly related to farming and cattle breeding.
