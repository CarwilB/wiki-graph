'''Cevicos''' is a [[town]] and municipality in the [[Sánchez Ramírez Province|Sánchez Ramírez]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]]. Municipal population, as of 2012, is of 12,589. Town's proper population is of 4,285.<ref name="censo"></ref>

==Overview==
The town is located in a green plateau between [[Cotuí]] and [[Sabana Grande de Boyá]]. It municipal district (or hamlet) is the village of [[La Cueva, Dominican Republic|La Cueva]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-06-06 |title=Cevicos {{!}} Sanchez Ramirez {{!}} República Dominicana |url=https://www.conectate.com.do/articulo/cevicos-sanchez-ramirez-republica-dominicana/ |access-date=2024-04-21 |language=es}}</ref>

Cevicos produces fruits like [[mango]], [[pineapple]], [[sugar cane]], [[passion fruit]] and many others.
