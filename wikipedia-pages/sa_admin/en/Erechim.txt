'''Erechim''' ({{IPA|pt|eɾe'ʃi᷉}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], located in the [[South Region, Brazil|South Region]] of [[Brazil]]. Recognized as a subregional hub in the country, it serves as the central city of the [[Alto Uruguai]] region in Rio Grande do Sul and is the second most populous city in the northern part of the state, with an estimated population of {{formatnum:105705}} inhabitants in [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]], according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]].<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2022">{{cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/rs/erechim.html|title=Cities and States - Erechim - IBGE|publisher=ibge.gov.br|access-date=17 August 2023}}</ref> According to the Socioeconomic Development Index (IDESE), Erechim ranks as the second most developed city in Rio Grande do Sul among municipalities with over 100,000 inhabitants, leading in the education sector.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticia/10844/28-03-2016/erechim-e-a-2-mais-desenvolvida-do-rs-entre-as-cidades-com-mais-de-100-mil-habitantes|title=Erechim is the 2nd most developed city in RS among cities with over 100,000 inhabitants|publisher=pmerechim.rs.gov.br|access-date=17 August 2023}}</ref> The Atlas of Violence identifies it as the second safest municipality in the state.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ipea.gov.br/atlasviolencia/download/21/atlas-da-violencia-dos-municipios-brasileiros-2019|title=Atlas of Violence in Brazilian Municipalities 2019|date=6 August 2019|access-date=12 August 2019|publisher=Ipea}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Erechim, a safe city|url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticia/13725/07-08-2019/erechim-uma-cidade-segura|website=Erechim, a safe city|access-date=12 August 2019}}</ref> In 2015, Erechim held the 15th position in [[Gross Domestic Product|GDP]] among municipalities in Rio Grande do Sul.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.fee.tche.br/sitefee/download/pib/municipal/tab13_2007.xls |title=Municipal GDP RS - 2007 |publisher=www.fee.tche.br |access-date=}}</ref>

Erechim is one of Brazil’s earliest planned modern cities. Its urban layout drew inspiration from urban planning concepts used in [[Washington, D.C.]] (1791), [[Paris]] (1850), [[Buenos Aires]] (1580), and [[Belo Horizonte]] (1897).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.raizesdosul.com.br/mun136.htm|title=Roots of the South|access-date=12 April 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090707205259/http://www.raizesdosul.com.br/mun136.htm|archive-date=7 July 2009|url-status=dead}}</ref> However, recent modifications to its road network have enhanced traffic flow, characterized by wide streets, a clear hierarchy, and the creation of convergence points through diagonal streets intersecting the basic grid. Key elements of its urban design include a perpendicular street grid intersected by diagonal avenues, uniformly sized city blocks, and a perimeter avenue encircling the city.

== Etymology ==
When Erechim was established as a municipality on April 30, 1918, it was named "Erexim," a term derived from the [[Kaingang language]], meaning "small field," formed by combining ''rê'' or ''erê'' ("field") and ''xim'' ("small").<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalkaingang.org/Lgua_Kaingang.pdf|title=The Kaingang Language|last=Rocha D’Angelis|first=Wilmar|publisher=|access-date=17 August 2023}}</ref> The city's name likely reflects the forests that surrounded it at the time.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> The city has also been known by other names, such as Paiol Grande, Boa Vista, Boa Vista de Erechim, and José Bonifácio, but "Erechim" has endured to the present day.<ref name="IBGE_História"/>

According to the [[Portuguese orthography|orthographic rules]] of the [[Portuguese language]] established in the [[1943 Portuguese Orthographic Form|Orthographic Agreement of 1943]], the toponym should be spelled "Erexim," since the letter "[[x]]" is used for words of [[Kaingang language|Kaingang]] origin. However, the municipality’s name is officially registered with "ch," as seen in all traffic signs, vehicle plates, and official documents.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/como-tudo-comecou |title=History of Erechim |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=11 October 2010 |archive-date=21 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090736/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/como-tudo-comecou |url-status=dead }}</ref>

During the celebrations marking Erechim’s 50th anniversary in 1968, Rubem Safro, popularly known as Buja, referred to the city as the "Capital of Friendship" while hosting a local event. This nickname was soon embraced by the municipality, reflecting the harmonious coexistence of its diverse ethnic communities.<ref name="Prefeitura_Nome">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/origem-do-nome |title=Origin of the Name |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=11 October 2010 |archive-date=21 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090751/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/origem-do-nome |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== History ==
=== Origins ===
Studies conducted by [[Eletrosul]] indicate that the [[Alto Uruguai]] region, where Erechim is located, has been inhabited by humans for at least 10,000 years. Over the past three centuries, the area was primarily home to [[Indigenous peoples of Brazil|indigenous]] [[Kaingang]] peoples, with some [[Guaraní people|Guarani]] groups occupying lower-altitude regions.<ref name="Prefeitura_Nativos">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/nativos |title=Native Peoples |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=9 October 2010 |archive-date=30 December 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201230104018/https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The first non-indigenous settlers in the area now comprising Erechim were likely [[São Paulo (state)|Paulista]] descendants of ''[[bandeirantes]]'', who settled sporadically after receiving land grants from the state government. Their initial occupation was marked by violent conflicts with the Kaingang, whom the [[Portugal|Portuguese]] referred to as "''coroados''."<ref name="Cidades@IBGE_Histórico">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=430700# |title=History |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=1970 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref>

These early settlers established themselves in a forested region intersected by the abundant river network of the western [[Pelotas River|Pelotas]]-[[Uruguay River|Uruguay]] basin. Around 1887, Augusto de Oliveira Penteado, known as Augusto César, along with João Placidino Machado and Antônio Ferreira de Albuquerque, conducted a river exploration. They submitted a detailed report to the [[Passo Fundo]] Municipal Council in late 1888, naming various geographical features.<ref name="Cidades@IBGE_Histórico"/>

In 1908, the Erechim Colony was founded, planned by the Director of Lands and Colonization, Carlos Torres Gonçalves, following [[positivism|positivist principles]] to serve as a model for [[colonization]].<ref name="caron">{{cite journal |last1=Caron |first1=Márcia dos Santos |last2=Tedesco |first2=João Carlos |date=3 April 2012 |title=The Positivist State in Northern RS: The Issue of Land Ownership and the Founding of the Erechim Colony (1890/1910) |url=http://revistas.unisinos.br/index.php/historia/article/view/htu.2012.162.05 |journal=História Unisinos |volume=16 |issue=2 |pages=220–231 |doi=10.4013/htu.2012.162.05 |issn=2236-1782 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304034352/http://revistas.unisinos.br/index.php/historia/article/view/htu.2012.162.05 |archive-date=2016-03-04 |access-date=16 May 2013 |doi-access=free}}</ref> The colony experienced rapid economic growth, facilitated by the presence of a railway and well-planned roads constructed according to the colony’s original design.<ref name=caron/> The colony was officially established in 1910 with the arrival of the first 36 settlers: four families totaling 28 individuals and four single persons.<ref name=caron/>

=== Pioneerism and Immigration ===
[[File:Trilhos de Erechim em 1910.jpg|thumb|left|The Erechim railway station in 1910]]
[[File:Avenida Maurício Cardoso, Erechim RS (década de 1920).jpg|thumb|left|Maurício Cardoso Avenue]]
The region was primarily colonized by immigrants of [[Poland|Polish]] (1918),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-polonesa |title=Immigrants of Polish ethnicity |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=10 October 2010 |archive-date=18 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118151831/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-polonesa |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Germany|German]] (1912),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-alema |title=Immigrants of German ethnicity |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=10 October 2010 |archive-date=18 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118160528/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-alema |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Jew|Jewish]] (1911),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-israelita |title=Immigrants of Jewish ethnicity |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=10 October 2010 |archive-date=19 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119085205/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-israelita |url-status=dead }}</ref> and predominantly [[Italy|Italian]] descent. The first Italian families arrived around 1910 via the railway, significantly shaping the region’s social, cultural, and material character over the years. Many immigrants, particularly Italians, sought better lives for themselves and future generations. Their influence remains evident today, especially in the city’s architecture and cuisine.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-italiana |title=Immigrants of Italian ethnicity |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=10 October 2010 |archive-date=18 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118203644/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/imigracao/imigrantes-etnia-italiana |url-status=dead }}</ref>

By 1910, under the influence of these early immigrants, the colony’s seat had already developed an urban character, with streets laid out and approximately 50 wooden houses constructed, alongside 22 more under construction. The settlement included two lodging barracks for immigrants, an infirmary, a materials warehouse, nine commercial establishments, a barber shop, a tailor shop, three shoe shops, and a butcher shop. The rural area also developed rapidly. By 1914, the initial settlement of the Erechim Colony had become the most prosperous village in the region. On April 20, 1916, the Land and Colonization Commission’s office was relocated from the Erechim settlement to Paiol Grande, the previously designated general headquarters of the colony.<ref name="Cidades@IBGE_Histórico"/>

=== Administrative formation ===
With the growth of the settlement and its economy—driven by agriculture, livestock, commerce, and services—Erechim was elevated to municipality status under state decree No. 2342 on April 30, 1918, signed by [[Borges de Medeiros]], separating it from the municipality of Passo Fundo. Its seat was established in the former settlement of Boa Vista do Erechim, officially established on June 18, 1918.<ref name="IBGE_História"/> Over the years, new districts were added. In the 1920 national census, the municipality was divided into five districts: Boa Vista do Erechim (seat), Erechim, Barro, Erebango, and Marcelino Ramos. By December 31, 1937, it included eleven districts: Erechim, Barro, Marcelino Ramos, Nova Itália, Nova Polônia, Paulo Bento, Quatro Irmãos, Rio Novo, São Valentim, Treze de Maio, and Viadutos. In 1943, the municipality consisted of twelve districts: José Bonifácio (formerly Erechim), Barro, Carlos Gomes (formerly Ribeirão do Torto, previously Nova Polônia), Cotegipe, Marcelino Ramos, Nova Itália, Paulo Bento, Princesa Isabel (formerly Treze de Maio), Quatro Irmãos, Rio Novo, São Valentim, Severiano Almeida (formerly Nova Itália), and Viadutos. By December 1, 1960, it comprised nine districts: Erechim, Barão de Cotegipe, Capo-Erê, Itatiba, Mariano Moro, Nova Itália (formerly Severiano de Almeida), Paulo Bento, Quatro Irmãos, and Três Arroios. The last modification, under state law No. 10762 of April 16, 1996, separated the districts of [[Paulo Bento, Rio Grande do Sul|Paulo Bento]] and [[Quatro Irmãos]], which became independent municipalities. Erechim then consisted of three districts: Capo-Êre, Jaguaretê, and the seat.<ref name="IBGE_História"/>

=== Urban growth ===
Following the completion of official procedures for emancipation, the Secretary of Public Works, Carlos Torres Gonçalves, ordered the planning of the city that would serve as the municipality’s seat. Engineers and surveyors from the land commission, based in the former Erechim settlement (now Getúlio Vargas), began their work using the railway station as the starting point, apparently without prior terrain reconnaissance. The urban plan mirrored that of [[Belo Horizonte]], the capital of Minas Gerais, and incorporated urban planning concepts from [[Washington, D.C.]] (1791) and [[Paris]] (1850). Once the layout was approved, the city’s development commenced with the sale of plots and rapid construction of wooden buildings, particularly along José Bonifácio Avenue, now known as Maurício Cardoso. Thus, Vila Boa Vista emerged, replacing the former Paiol Grande.<ref name="Prefeitura_Desenho">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/tracado-viario/origens-do-desenho-urbano-da-cidade |title=Origins of the City's Urban Design |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=9 October 2010 |archive-date=19 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119082939/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/apresentacao/tracado-viario/origens-do-desenho-urbano-da-cidade |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Over time, urban improvements became necessary. Key projects included leveling the gully along Maurício Cardoso Avenue, channeling streams in the initial blocks of Itália Street and Nelson Ehlers Street, and filling numerous potholes with wood and dirt.<ref name="Prefeitura_Desenho"/>
[[File:Prédio do Relógio.jpg|thumb|right|Financial center in the city center, the Clock Building]]
[[File:Casa de Erechim.jpg|thumb|right|One of the houses built in the 1930s in Erechim, destroyed by a fire in 2010.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.jornalboavista.com.br/site/index.php?n=3980|title=Fire mobilizes firefighters in Erechim |author=José Adelar Ody |date=7 October 2010 |access-date=11 October 2010}}</ref>]]

Later, extensive studies revealed that Erechim was built over uneven terrain. A months-long topographic survey, led by Diumer Schneider, the Malinowski brothers, the Losina brothers, Antônio Bergmann, and Henrique Schwerin, mapped the area at two-meter intervals. Before this survey was completed, the mayor ordered the municipal surveyor to level the pavement, resulting in curbs that reflected the terrain’s imperfections. Numerous administrations unsuccessfully attempted to level the city. Subsequently, a Master Plan was developed to guide urban development. This plan prioritized leveling buildings before streets, leading to structures with multiple floors partially below ground after street leveling to mitigate steep inclines. The Master Plan sparked significant controversy and was ultimately shelved.<ref name="Prefeitura_Desenho"/>

=== Recent history ===
With the growth of Erechim and neighboring cities, the [[List of mesoregions and microregions of Rio Grande do Sul|Erechim Microregion]] was established, encompassing Erechim and 30 other municipalities. The most populous include [[Getúlio Vargas, Rio Grande do Sul|Getúlio Vargas]], [[Aratiba]], [[Barão de Cotegipe]], [[Estação]], [[Gaurama]], [[Campinas do Sul]], [[Erval Grande]], [[Marcelino Ramos]], [[Viadutos]], [[Itatiba do Sul]], [[Áurea]], and [[Severiano de Almeida]]. In 2006, [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] estimated the microregion’s population at approximately 211,228 inhabitants across a total area of 5,745 km². Its average [[Human Development Index|HDI]] was 0.791, with an average per capita [[Gross Domestic Product|GDP]] of R$22,048 in 2003. The microregion is part of the Noroeste Rio-Grandense mesoregion.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sites-do-brasil.com/diretorio/index.php?cat_id=1017 |title=Erechim Microregion |author=Sites do Brasil |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref>

One of the most tragic incidents in Erechim’s history occurred on September 22, 2004, when a bus chartered by the municipality to transport rural students to city schools, carrying 31 passengers, fell into a CORSAN dam.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.db.com.br/noticia/36790.html |title=School bus falls into dam, killing 17 |author=DB Online |date=22 September 2004 |access-date=9 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121116165336/http://www.db.com.br/noticia/36790.html |archive-date=16 November 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It took rescue teams over three hours to pull the bus out of the water.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://noticias.terra.com.br/brasil/interna/0,,OI388522-EI998,00.html |title=School bus falls into dam, killing 17 in RS |author=Terra |work=Terra |date=22 September 2004 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref> The parents of the deceased teenagers were compensated with R$190,000.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/cotidiano/ult95u371591.shtml |title=Court compensates parents of victims in Erechim bus accident |author=Folha Online |date=12 February 2008 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://zerohora.clicrbs.com.br/zerohora/jsp/default.jsp?uf=1&local=1§ion=Geral&newsID=a1762685.xml |title=Families of victims of Erechim bus tragedy to receive compensation for moral damages |author=ClicRBS |date=11 February 2008 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref> The driver, who sustained minor injuries, was arrested in 2005 for speeding.<ref>{{cite web |author= |date=11 February 2008 |title=Court convicts those responsible for bus accident in RS |url=http://g1.globo.com/Noticias/Brasil/0,,MUL295178-5598,00-JUSTICA%2BCONDENA%2BRESPONSAVEIS%2BPOR%2BACIDENTE%2BNO%2BRS.html |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref> Autopsies confirmed that 17 children and teenagers died. Lucas Verazzo, a student, died heroically saving three classmates from drowning before losing his life in an attempt to save a fourth. The three days following the accident were marked by protests, with classes suspended across all city schools.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.daubi.jor.br/jornal-diario-da-manha-noticia-115.htm |title=Erechim Tragedy - The Difficult Recovery |author=Jornal Diário da Manhã |date=28 September 2004 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/cotidiano/ult95u99957.shtml |title=Protest commemorates victims of school bus accident in Erechim |author=Folha.com |date=25 September 2004 |access-date=9 October 2010}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Erechim is situated in the [[Alto Uruguai]] region, located between the [[Uruguay River]] and the [[Ijuí River]], with numerous small and medium-sized streams forming sub-basins that shape its landscape. The municipality has an average elevation of 783 meters.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/geografia/erechim-em-numeros |title=Erechim in Numbers |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=5 October 2010 |archive-date=21 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090809/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/geografia/erechim-em-numeros |url-status=dead }}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Aratiba]] and [[Três Arroios]] to the north; [[Getúlio Vargas, Rio Grande do Sul|Getúlio Vargas]] and [[Erebango]] to the south; [[Gaurama]] and [[Áurea]] to the east; and [[Paulo Bento, Rio Grande do Sul|Paulo Bento]] and [[Barão de Cotegipe]] to the west.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/geografia/localizacao |title=Location and Boundaries |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=5 October 2010 |archive-date=21 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090814/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/geografia/localizacao |url-status=dead }}</ref> Erechim is approximately 370 km from the state capital, [[Porto Alegre]],<ref>{{cite web |url=http://maps.google.com.br/maps?f=d |title=From Erechim - RS to Porto Alegre - RS - Google Maps |access-date=25 December 2009}}</ref> with a road distance of about 330 km.<ref name="ABCR_Erechim-PortoAlegre">{{cite web |url=https://www.achedistancia.com.br/distancia-de-erechim-a-porto-alegre.html|title=Distance between cities (Erechim-Porto Alegre) |author=Ache Distancia |access-date=11 November 2020}}</ref> The region is characterized by [[basalt]] as its geological foundation, forming a deeply dissected plain shaped by tributaries of the Uruguay River.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://coralx.ufsm.br/ifcrs/fisiografia.htm#alto |title=Physiographic Regions |author=Universidade Federal de Santa Maria |access-date=5 October 2010}}</ref>

The municipality covers an area of 430.764 km², accounting for 0.1602% of Rio Grande do Sul’s territory, 0.0764% of the [[South Region, Brazil|South Region]], and 0.0051% of Brazil’s total area.<ref name="CNM_Dados">{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100143136 |title=General Data |author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CNM) |access-date=5 October 2010}}</ref> The urban perimeter spans 14.2925 km².<ref name="Embrapa_Urbanização">{{cite web|url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/rs.html |title=Rio Grande do Sul |author=Embrapa Monitoramento por Satélite|access-date=5 October 2010}}</ref> Erechim is part of the Noroeste Rio-Grandense mesoregion, within division 004, which includes municipalities formerly part of the Erechim Microregion, a designation replaced by [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] in 2017.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |title=Regional Division of Brazil |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |access-date=11 February 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190211143002/https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |archive-date=11 February 2019}}</ref> This new division also places Erechim within the Passo Fundo Intermediate Geographic Region.<ref name="IBGE_Area_Municipios_2017">{{cite web |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/todos-os-produtos-geociencias/15761-areas-dos-municipios.html |title=Areas of Brazilian Municipalities as of July 1, 2017 |work=Area of Municipalities 2017 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=1 July 2017 |access-date=9 February 2019}}</ref>

=== Climate ===
[[File:Neve_em_Erechim_em_1917.jpg|thumb|left|Records of [[snow]] in Erechim in 1917]]
Erechim’s [[climate]] is classified as [[humid subtropical climate|humid subtropical]] (Cfa according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]), falling within climate zone C.<ref name="vetmedunivienna">{{cite web |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |author=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.climabrasileiro.hpg.ig.com.br/dados.htm |title=Climate Information |author=Ig |access-date=25 December 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100412202401/http://www.climabrasileiro.hpg.ig.com.br/dados.htm |archive-date=12 April 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city experiences regular rainfall throughout the year, with an average annual [[temperature]] of approximately {{formatnum:18|°C}}. Winters are cold, though rarely extreme, and summers have moderate temperatures. During winter, polar air masses can lower temperatures below zero, often leading to [[frost]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2011/07/05/geada-cobre-campos-da-regiao/|title=Frost covers fields in the region|date=5 July 2011|access-date=18 July 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://atmosferaonline.com.br/erechim-e-regiao-alto-uruguai-voltam-a-registrar-temperaturas-negativas/|title=Erechim and Alto Uruguai region record negative temperatures again|author=CASTRO, Edson|date=19 August 2017|access-date=18 July 2020}}</ref> [[Autumn]] and [[Spring (season)|spring]] serve as transitional seasons. The annual rainfall exceeds {{formatnum:1800}} millimeters (mm).<ref name="Climatologia"/> Prolonged dry spells, or [[Indian summer|Indian summers]], often result in smoke from [[wildfire]]s in hills and rural areas.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.clicrbs.com.br/pioneiro/rs/plantao/10,2497964,Estiagem-racionamento-de-agua-em-Erechim-comeca-ao-meio-dia.html |title=Drought: Water rationing in Erechim begins at noon |author=Clicrbs |date=4 May 2009 |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.jornalboavista.com.br/site/index.php?n=3738 |title=Wildfires destroy over 11,000 m² of vegetation in five days |author=Alan Dias |date=26 August 2010 |publisher=Jornal Boa Vista |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref> While rare, [[snow]] has been recorded in Erechim in 1918, 1942, 1956, 1957, 1965, 1990, and 2001.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cidadedeerechim.blogspot.com/search/label/Neve%20em%20Erechim |title=Snow in Erechim |author=Blog Cidade de Erechim |access-date=2 May 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/clima |title=Climate |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=4 October 2010 |archive-date=20 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101020123821/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/clima |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Data from the [[automatic weather station]] operated by the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET) in Erechim, active since November 2006, recorded the lowest temperature of {{formatnum:-3.8|[[Celsius|°C]]}} on July 18, 2017, and the highest of {{formatnum:35.6|°C}} on December 29, 2019. The highest 24-hour [[precipitation]] accumulation reached {{formatnum:111.2|mm}} on October 4, 2023.<ref name="INMET-A828-Erechim-RS"/><ref name="Gráficos-INMET-A828-Erechim-RS"/>

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Araucária em Erechim.jpg|thumb|right|[[Araucaria angustifolia|Brazilian pine]], which is typical of the temperate regions of the Rio Grande do Sul Plateau, is abundant in the city.]]

The [[vegetation]] of Erechim comprises a mix of the [[Araucaria moist forests]], or mixed ombrophilous forest, and [[Prairie|prairies]]. The [[Araucaria angustifolia|Brazilian pine]] is one of the city’s most common trees, characteristic of the colder regions of the Rio Grande do Sul Plateau. Recently, the municipal government planted Brazilian pine saplings in Longines Malinowski Park, distributing approximately 1,300 saplings from the municipal forest nursery.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/longines-malinowski-ganha-nova-araucaria |title=Longines Malinowski receives new Brazilian pine |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |date=27 September 2010 |access-date=10 October 2010}}</ref> During Environment Week, activities included trail walks, visits to the sanitary landfill and springs, workshops, artistic performances, and exhibitions, engaging around 1,000 participants.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/semana-do-meio-ambiente-encerra-com-grande-participacao-do-publico |title=Environment Week concludes with strong public participation |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |date=4 June 2010 |access-date=10 October 2010}}</ref>

The municipality faces pollution challenges due to the growing municipal vehicle fleet. The Secretariats of Culture, Sports, and Tourism, and Environment organized the Erechim City Cycling Event Against Pollution, held in observance of Anti-Pollution Day.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/passeio-ciclistico-cidade-de-erechim-contra-a-poluicao-e-neste-domingo |title=The Erechim City Cycling Event Against Pollution is scheduled to take place this Sunday |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |date=10 August 2010 |access-date=10 October 2010}}</ref>

According to the municipal government, environmental licensing is a key component of environmental management, guiding the location, installation, expansion, and operation of potentially polluting enterprises or activities that may cause environmental harm. Environmental licenses in Erechim are issued by the Municipal Secretariat of Environment (SMMA), based on Municipal Law No. 3932 of December 2005, in accordance with State Environmental Council resolutions 102/2005 and 16/2001, and proposals by the Municipal Environmental Protection Council (COMPAM).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/empreendedor/licenciamento-ambiental |title=Environmental Licensing Directorate |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=10 October 2010 |archive-date=1 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101001055223/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/empreendedor/licenciamento-ambiental |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2020, Erechim’s population was estimated at 106,633 inhabitants, making it the 19th most populous municipality in the state,<ref name="População_Sexo"/> with a [[population density]] of 223.11 inhabitants per km². According to the 2000 census, 48.22% of the population were men (43,568 inhabitants) and 51.78% were women (46,779 inhabitants), aligning with the demographic pattern of Rio Grande do Sul. The age structure shows limited participation of youth and elderly, with adults comprising 65.9% of the population.<ref name="a">{{cite web|url=http://pmeonline.erechim.rs.gov.br/downloads/agenda2018.pdf |title=Erechim Data and Planning|publisher=PME Online/Prefeitura de Erechim |access-date=25 December 2009}}</ref> Approximately 90.79% (82,026 inhabitants) lived in the [[urban area]], while 9.21% (14,761 inhabitants) resided in the [[rural area]].<ref name="População_Sexo">{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/demografia/mu_dem_pop_total.asp?iIdMun=100143136 |title=Demography - Total Population |author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CNM) |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/demografia/mu_dem_pop_urbana.asp?iIdMun=100143136 |title=Demography - Urban Population |author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CNM) |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/demografia/mu_dem_pop_rural.asp?iIdMun=100143136 |title=Demography - Rural Population |author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CNM) |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref> In 2020, Erechim had an estimated 79,288 eligible voters.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.jornalbomdia.com.br/noticia/41876/erechim-quase-80-mil-eleitores-votam-em-49-locais-diferentes|title=Erechim: Nearly 80,000 voters cast ballots at 49 different locations|publisher=Jornal Bom Dia|access-date=11 November 2020}}</ref><ref name="Eleitores">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=partpol&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim|title=Political Representation 2006|date=2006|access-date=3 October 2010|author=Cidades@ - IBGE}}</ref>

Erechim’s [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) is considered high by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP), with a value of 0.776 in 2010. In 2007, the longevity index was 0.833, the health index was 0.782, and the education index was 0.716.<ref name="Ipeadata_IDHRS">{{cite web |author= |date=2007 |title=Erechim, RS |url=http://www.atlasbrasil.org.br/perfil/municipio/4307005 |access-date=4 October 2010 |website=Atlas Brasil}}</ref> In 2000, the population consisted of 78,056 Whites (86.40%), 9,983 mixed-race individuals (11.05%), 2,028 Blacks (2.24%), 132 Indigenous people (0.15%), 55 Asians (0.06%), and 93 individuals with no declared race (0.10%).<ref name="SIDRA_Raça">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=2094&z=cd&o=7&i=P |title=Population of Erechim by Race and Color |author=Sidra (Sistema IBGE de Recuperação de Dados Automática) |date=2000 |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
[[File:Catedral Erechim.jpg|thumb|left|The Erechim Cathedral, the main gathering place for [[Catholic Church|Catholics]] in the city]]
Reflecting Erechim’s cultural diversity, the city hosts a variety of religious practices. While it developed within a predominantly [[Catholicism|Catholic]] social framework, numerous [[Protestantism|Protestant]] denominations are now present. The growth of evangelicals has been notable, comprising nearly 11.4% of the population.<ref name="Religião"/>

The [[Judaism|Jewish community]] in Erechim is significant relative to the state, consisting of many professionals and entrepreneurs, with members excelling in cultural, artistic, and academic fields. It is estimated that Jews in the interior of Rio Grande do Sul account for 10% of the state’s Jewish population.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.firgs.org.br/paginas/a-federacao-israelita-do-rio-grande-do-sul-.aspx |title=About the FIRS |author=FIRS |publisher=firgs.org.br |access-date=17 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090528001315/http://www.firgs.org.br/paginas/a-federacao-israelita-do-rio-grande-do-sul-.aspx |archive-date=28 May 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city of Erechim is located in the country with the largest Catholic population in the world. In October 2009, the federal government recognized the legal status of the [[Catholic Church]],<ref>{{cite web |url=http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/mat/2009/10/08/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-767959739.asp |title=Senate approves agreement with the Vatican |author=Cristiane Agostine |publisher=O Globo |date=8 October 2009 |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref> though Brazil is officially a [[secular state]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://jus.com.br/artigos/8519/brasil-estado-laico-e-a-inconstitucionalidade-da-existencia-de-simbolos-religiosos-em-predios-publicos|title=Brazil: Secular State and the Unconstitutionality of Religious Symbols in Public Buildings|last=Fernando Fonseca de Queiroz|date=October 2005|publisher=Jus Navigandi|access-date=21 June 2022}}</ref> The city is home to various Protestant denominations, including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assembly of God]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://aderechim.com.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=67&Itemid=68 |title=History of the Assembly of God in Erechim |author=Igreja Evangélica Assembleia de Deus Erechim/RS |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref> According to the 2000 census by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], Erechim’s population is composed of [[Catholicism|Catholics]] (85.76%), [[Protestantism|evangelicals]] (11.32%), [[Irreligion|non-religious individuals]] (0.90%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (0.71%), and 1.31% adhering to other religions.<ref name="Religião">{{cite web |url=http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/municipioslista.php?a=search&value=1&SearchField=AnyField&SearchOption=Equals&SearchFor=Erechim |title=Religion |access-date=4 October 2010}}</ref>

== Politics and justice ==
The city’s first governor was Intendant Ayres Pires de Oliveira, appointed on June 18, 1918.<ref name="Prefeitura_Prefeitos">{{cite web |url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/pagina/154/intendentes-e-prefeitos|title=Intendants and Mayors|publisher=pmerechim.rs.gov.br|access-date=17 August 2023}}</ref> Following Decree No. 4,666, Erechim’s first [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] was Amintas Maciel, appointed on December 9, 1930.<ref name="Prefeitura_Prefeitos" /> One of the city’s major avenues is named in his honor.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ereshow.com.br/Erechim.php |title=Erechim - XII International Rally of Erechim |author=www.ereshow.com.br |access-date=24 December 2009 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/65Tl5pi34?url=http://www.ereshow.com.br/Erechim.php |archive-date=15 February 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Over 27 terms, 22 mayors have governed Erechim.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/pagina/148/administracao |title=Administration |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Erechim |access-date=9 November 2020}}</ref> In the 2016 election, Luiz Francisco Schmidt of the [[Brazilian Social Democracy Party|PSDB]] won with 39.82% of valid votes, narrowly defeating Ana de Oliveira of the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement|MDB]] (39.80%) by 12 votes, while Flávio Tirello of the [[Brazilian Socialist Party|PSB]] placed third with 20.38%.<ref name="sc">{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/eleicoes/2016/apuracao/erechim.html|title=Results of the 2016 Election Count in Erechim for Mayor and Councilor|access-date=14 September 2018|work=G1|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The [[legislative power]] is represented by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], comprising 17 councilors elected for four-year terms, in accordance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |author=DJI |title=Federal Constitution - CF - 1988 / Art. 29 |access-date=3 October 2010 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/64ioyjB1v?url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |archive-date=16 January 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The chamber’s composition is as follows:<ref name="vereadores">{{cite web |url=http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/rs/86371/index.shtml#vereador |title=First Round Count (Councilors) |author=Terra |date=6 October 2008 |access-date=3 October 2010 |archive-date=6 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101006205644/http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/rs/86371/index.shtml#vereador |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Brazilian Democratic Movement|MDB]] with 3 seats; [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|PT]] with 3 seats; [[Brazilian Social Democracy Party|PSDB]] with 2 seats; [[Democratic Labour Party (Brazil)|PDT]] with 2 seats; [[Progressive Party (Brazil)|PP]], [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|PSD]], [[Brazilian Labour Party (1981)|PTB]], [[Green Party (Brazil)|PV]], [[Solidarity (Brazil)|SD]], [[Communist Party of Brazil|PC do B]], and [[Brazilian Socialist Party|PSB]] with 1 seat each.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.erechim.rs.leg.br/|title=Home — Erechim Municipal Chamber|access-date=2 October 2017|work=www.erechim.rs.leg.br|language=pt-br}}</ref> The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law). Erechim operates under organic laws.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Erechim&state=rs |title=Organic Law of the Municipality of Erechim |author=@LIZ |access-date=3 October 2010 |archive-date=9 December 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121209113739/http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Erechim&state=rs |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city is also the seat of a ''[[comarca]]'', established by Decree No. 4,366 on August 31, 1929, separated from the [[Passo Fundo]] ''comarca''. Law No. 6,535 of January 31, 1973, classified the Erechim ''comarca'' as third-grade with three prosecutor positions.<ref name="comarca">{{cite web |url=http://www.mp.rs.gov.br/atuacaomp/noticias/id7550.htm |title=Public Prosecutor's Office inaugurates new headquarters in Erechim |author=Ministério Público do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul (MP-RS) |date=3 May 2006 |access-date=3 October 2010 |archive-date=19 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119180205/http://www.mp.rs.gov.br/atuacaomp/noticias/id7550.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to the [[Regional Electoral Courts|TRE]]-[[Rio Grande do Sul|RS]] (Regional Electoral Court of Rio Grande do Sul), Erechim had approximately 77,278 voters in 2016.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tre-rs.gov.br/|title=Regional Electoral Court of Rio Grande do Sul|access-date=2 October 2017|work=www.tre-rs.gov.br|language=pt-BR|archive-date=28 September 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170928135518/http://www.tre-rs.gov.br/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
[[File:Centro Erechim.jpg|thumb|right|View of the downtown area of the city]]
When it was established as a municipality, Erechim consisted of a single district: the Seat, created under the name Erechim (formerly a village) by Municipal Act No. 2 on July 27, 1918. Currently, the city is officially divided into three districts: Capo-Erê, Jaguaretê, and Seat. The most recent change in district organization occurred through State Law No. 10,762, enacted on April 16, 1996, which separated the district of [[Paulo Bento, Rio Grande do Sul|Paulo Bento]] from Erechim, elevating it to municipal status.<ref name="IBGE_História">{{cite web|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/riograndedosul/erechim.pdf |title=History |author=Cities@ - IBGE |access-date=October 2, 2010}}</ref>

Located not far from the city center are the main neighborhoods, which house the majority of Erechim’s population. Notable examples include Bela Vista, Linho, Cerâmica, Copas Verdes, José Bonifácio, Koller, Morro da Cegonha, São Cristóvão, Três Vendas, Triângulo, and São Caetano, among others that form part of the city’s strategic map maintained by the municipal government.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/pagina/771/mapa-dos-bairros|title=Map of Neighborhoods|publisher=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=November 11, 2020}}</ref> Most of these neighborhoods are generally considered to be middle class or upper-class areas. Further from the city center, prominent neighborhoods include Aeroporto, Atlântico, Dal Molin, Florestinha, Loteamentos, Paiol Grande, Zimmer, Presidente Vargas, and Progresso, among others. These areas typically have lower average household incomes, with some classified as [[lower-middle class]] neighborhoods.<ref name="Prefeitura_Demografia">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/demografia |title=Demographics |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |access-date=October 2, 2010 |archive-date=October 20, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101020123828/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/demografia |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Economy ==
According to data from the Foundation for Economics and Statistics for 2014, Erechim’s gross domestic product (GDP) was R$4,091,150,000.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.fee.rs.gov.br/indicadores/pib-rs/municipal/destaques/|title=FEE|access-date=October 2, 2017|website=www.fee.rs.gov.br|language=pt-BR|archive-date=October 3, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171003030328/https://www.fee.rs.gov.br/indicadores/pib-rs/municipal/destaques/|url-status=dead}}</ref> The per capita GDP is R$40,207.07.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/erechim/panorama|title=IBGE | access-date=October 2, 2017|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

=== Primary sector ===
Although agriculture contributes the least to Erechim’s GDP, it remains significant due to the diversity of its output. Of the city’s total GDP, R$36,164,000 comes from the gross added value of agriculture.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim|title=Municipal Gross Domestic Product|date=2007|access-date=October 2, 2010|author=Cities@ - IBGE}}</ref> According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], in 2008, the municipality had a livestock inventory of 13,501 [[cattle]], 27,107 [[pig|pigs]], 194 [[horse|equines]], 4 [[mule|mules]], 472 [[goat|goats]], 1,102 [[sheep]], and 492,907 [[poultry]], including 17,044 [[Chicken|hens]], 474,963 [[rooster|roosters]], broilers, and chicks, and 900 [[quail|quails]].<ref name="Agropecuária" /> In 2008, the city produced 12,922,000 liters of milk from 5,800 [[cattle|cows]], 274,000 dozen eggs, 7,829 kilograms of [[honey]], and 1,200 kilograms of [[wool]] from sheep.<ref name="Agropecuária">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2008&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim |title=Livestock |author=Cities@ - IBGE |date=2008 |access-date=October 2, 2010}}</ref> In temporary crops, the main products are [[corn]] (20,300 tons), [[soybean]] (17,280 tons), and [[wheat]] (5,400 tons).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2008&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim |title=Temporary Crops 2008 |author=Cities@ - IBGE |date=2008 |access-date=October 2, 2010}}</ref>

=== Secondary sector ===
The industrial sector contributes R$521,187,000 to the municipal GDP, making it the most prominent economic sector in Erechim. Approximately 700 companies of varying sizes account for 37.96% of the city’s revenue.<ref name="Prefeitura_Economia"/> The Industrial District, established in 1978, is the primary source of wealth in this sector and employs around 5,000 people.<ref name="Prefeitura_Economia"/>

The significant growth of this sector is largely due to the expansion of the industrial park, which has enabled Erechim to grow four times faster than the national average and nearly three times faster than the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]].<ref name="a" /> This growth is also attributed to the shift of ranchers and farmers to industrial activities as a new means of generating wealth. The [[metalworking]] industry has seen the most significant growth, with employment in this sector increasing by an average of 25.4% between 1985 and 2005.<ref name="Prefeitura_Economia"/>

=== Tertiary sector ===
The service sector accounts for R$952,183,000 of Erechim’s GDP.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> This sector is a vital contributor to the city’s economy. According to IBGE, in 2008, Erechim had 5,439 commercial establishments and employed 74,919 workers, with a total of 40,925 people employed and 33,994 salaried workers. Salaries and other compensations totaled R$478,599,000, with an average monthly wage of 2.7 [[minimum wage|minimum wages]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=empresas&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim |title=Central Business Register Statistics |author=Cities@ - IBGE |date=2008 |access-date=October 2, 2010}}</ref> Commercial activity contributes 17.85% to the municipality’s revenue. The tourism sector is increasingly being developed as a potential significant source of income for the population.<ref name="Prefeitura_Economia">{{cite web |title=Economy |url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br//pagina/147/economia |access-date=October 2, 2010 |publisher=Municipal Government of Erechim}}</ref>

== Education ==
Erechim is home to its own federal university, the [[Federal University of Fronteira Sul]] (UFFS), which is renowned for its focus on social inclusion and academic research across the southern region of Brazil.<ref name="uri">{{cite web |url=http://www.uricer.edu.br/inicio |title=URI - Regional Integrated University - Erechim Campus |author=www.uricer.edu.br |access-date=December 25, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100112085920/http://www.uricer.edu.br/inicio |archive-date=January 12, 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city also hosts a campus of the [[Rio Grande do Sul State University]] (UERGS), which offers high-quality bachelor’s degree programs,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/prefeitura-de-erechim-assina-convenio-com-a-uergs |title=Erechim City Hall signs agreement with UERGS |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |date=March 21, 2007 |access-date=December 25, 2009}}</ref> as well as a campus of the [[Federal Institute of Rio Grande do Sul]] (IFRS),<ref>{{cite web|url=https://ifrs.edu.br/erechim/|title=IFRS - Erechim Campus|date=May 11, 2020|access-date=May 11, 2020}}</ref> offering technical, technological, and bachelor’s degree programs. Additionally, the city is home to the Integrated Regional University of Alto Uruguai and Missões (URI).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.uricer.edu.br/site/|title=URI Erechim Page|publisher=uricer.edu.br|access-date=August 19, 2023}}</ref>

According to the Preliminary Course Score (CPC) and the General Index of Evaluated Institutions (IGC) from the Anísio Teixeira National Institute for Educational Studies and Research (Inep), the Federal University of Fronteira Sul is the top university in the Alto Uruguai region. It is also ranked as the fourth-best federal university in Rio Grande do Sul and the 24th in Brazil. In terms of undergraduate program quality, UFFS ranks third nationally.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://jornalboavista.com.br/30112017uffs-e-a-melhor-instituicao-do-alto-uruguai-segundo-o-inep|title=UFFS is the best institution in Alto Uruguai according to Inep|work=Jornal Boa Vista e Rádio Cultura 105.9 Fm|date=November 30, 2017|access-date=May 31, 2020|publisher=Jornal Boa Vista|archive-date=May 13, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210513034909/https://jornalboavista.com.br/30112017uffs-e-a-melhor-instituicao-do-alto-uruguai-segundo-o-inep|url-status=dead}}</ref> Erechim has schools throughout its territory. Due to extensive urbanization, the few residents in the [[rural area]] have easy access to schools in nearby urban neighborhoods. Municipal schools have a slightly lower educational quality compared to state schools, but the city government is conducting studies to improve public municipal education to achieve better results in the Basic Education Development Index (IDEB).<ref name="IDEB_Índice">{{cite web |url=http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |title=Basic Education Development Index (IDEB) - Results and Goals |author=Federal Government |access-date=October 7, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071009195109/http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |archive-date=October 9, 2007 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2008, the municipality had approximately 18,493 enrollments, 1,149 teachers, and 67 schools in both public and private networks.<ref name="IBGE_Educação" />

== Urban infrastructure ==
Erechim boasts robust infrastructure. In 2000, the city had 26,999 dwellings, including [[apartment|apartments]], [[house|houses]], and rooms. Of these, 19,503 were owned properties, with 17,231 fully paid (63.82%), 2,272 in the process of acquisition (8.42%), and 4,894 [[Renting|rented]] (18.83%). Additionally, 2,323 dwellings were provided, with 683 supplied by employers (2.53%) and 1,640 provided through other means (6.07%). Another 279 were occupied in other ways (1.03%).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100143136|title=Infrastructure - Permanent Private Households and Residents|author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref>

The municipality provides treated water, [[electricity]], [[Waste water services|wastewater services]], [[municipal solid waste|urban cleaning]], [[landline telephone|fixed telephony]], and [[mobile telephone|mobile telephony]]. In 2000, 90.11% of households were served by the [[Water supply network|general water supply network]];<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_abastecimento.asp?iIdMun=100143136|title=Infrastructure - Water Supply|author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref> 89.70% of residences had garbage collection,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_lixo.asp?iIdMun=100143136|title=Infrastructure - Waste Management|author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref> and 89.07% had sanitary drainage.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_esgotamento.asp?iIdMun=100143136|title=Infrastructure - Sanitary Drainage|author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref> The city’s [[Gini coefficient]] is 0.40.<ref name="GINI">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim|title=Social Indicators of Brazilian Municipalities|author=IBGE|access-date=October 4, 2010}}</ref>

=== Social infrastructure ===
According to the municipal government, social infrastructure encompasses four key areas: ''[[education]]'', ''[[income]] and [[poverty]]'', ''[[sanitation]]'', and ''[[Health care|healthcare]]''. These are evaluated using the ''Economic and Social Development Index'' (IDESE). On this scale, which ranges from zero to one, Erechim ranks as the tenth municipality overall in the regional classification.<ref name="a" />

==== Income and poverty ====
In the category of ''income and poverty'', Erechim is considered to have a medium level, ranking 96th in the state. Compared to previous years, it was ranked 30th in 1991 and 75th in 2000.<ref name="a" /> There has been a slight reduction in poverty indicators and a notable increase in the number of people experiencing hunger. However, this does not pose a significant risk to the municipality’s development.<ref name="a" /><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/renda-e-pobreza|title=Income and Poverty|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=October 7, 2010|archive-date=October 21, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090845/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/renda-e-pobreza|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The municipal [[per capita income]] is R$13,251.00.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], is 0.40, where 1.00 represents the highest inequality and 0.00 the lowest.<ref name="GINI" /> The poverty incidence, as measured by IBGE, is 26.30%, with a lower poverty threshold of 15.70%, an upper threshold of 22.90%, and a subjective poverty incidence of 19.39%.<ref name="GINI" />

==== Healthcare ====
Based on the Socioeconomic Development Index (IDESE), Erechim demonstrates strong performance in its [[healthcare]] system but ranks poorly statewide, at 302nd place.<ref name="a" /> In recent years, the city has climbed the rankings due to declines in other municipalities. The [[infant mortality rate]] is 3.16 per thousand live births.<ref name="a" /> The municipality has forty healthcare facilities, twelve public and 28 private, including hospitals, [[emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[dentistry|dental services]]. In total, the city has 286 hospital beds, with 153 public and 133 private.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim|title=Health Services 2005|author=Cities@ - IBGE|date=2005|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref>[[File:Hospital de Caridade.jpg|thumb|right|Entrance to the Erechim Charity Hospital]]

Key hospitals in Erechim include:<ref name="Prefeitura_Saúde">{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/saude|title=Health|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=October 7, 2010|archive-date=October 21, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090855/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/saude|url-status=dead}}</ref>
*'''Santa Terezinha Hospital Foundation of Erechim:''' It is a general hospital that serves as a regional reference for the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS) in the area covered by the 11th Regional Health Coordination Office, which includes 31 municipalities. It is a reference for cancer treatment in the northern region of the state, hosting the High-Complexity Oncology Center, which includes [[chemotherapy]], [[radiotherapy]], and a Women’s Reference Center. It has 175 beds, a 24-hour emergency room, a surgical center, an obstetric center, and neonatal, pediatric, and adult general ICUs.<ref name="Prefeitura_Saúde"/>
*'''Erechim Charity Hospital:''' Founded in the 1930s, it has operated as a philanthropic institution since 1951. In January 2004, the Charity Hospital Clinical Center was inaugurated, integrating professionals from various health specialties with the hospital. It currently has approximately 122 beds, 155 medical staff, and 355 employees.<ref name="Prefeitura_Saúde"/>
*'''Primary Healthcare Units:''' Erechim has twelve primary healthcare units located in various neighborhoods across all city regions, serving areas such as Centro, Progresso, São Vicente de Paulo, Paiol Grande, Presidente Vargas, Aldo Arioli, São Cristóvão, Estevan Carraro, Capoerê, Jaguaretê, Atlântico, and Bela Vista.<ref name="Prefeitura_Saúde"/>

In 2009, Erechim gained national attention during the [[2009 swine flu pandemic|swine flu pandemic]] as the site of [[2009 swine flu pandemic in Brazil|Brazil]]’s first fatal case of [[Swine influenza|swine flu]]. The victim, truck driver Vanderlei Vial, who had traveled to [[Argentina]] between May 28 and June 15, 2009, returned to Erechim on June 19 and sought medical attention the following day with symptoms. He was hospitalized on June 23, transferred to [[Passo Fundo]], and passed away on June 28.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://oglobo.globo.com/cidades/mat/2009/06/28/ministerio-da-saude-confirma-primeira-morte-por-gripe-suina-no-pais-no-rio-grande-do-sul-756558058.asp|title=Ministry of Health confirms first swine flu death in Brazil in Rio Grande do Sul|author=Cleide Carvalho|date=June 28, 2009|publisher=O Globo|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/vidaecidadania/conteudo.phtml?tl=1&id=900368&tit=RS-confirma-primeira-morte-por-gripe-A-H1N1-no-Pais|title=RS confirms first H1N1 flu death in Brazil|author=Gazeta do Povo|date=June 28, 2009|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref> The 2009 flu pandemic was a [[pandemic|global outbreak]] of a swine flu variant, with the first cases reported in [[Mexico]] in mid-March 2009. On May 7, Brazil’s [[Ministry of Health (Brazil)|Ministry of Health]] confirmed the first four cases of H1N1 flu in the country.<ref>{{cite web |author= |date=May 7, 2009 |title=Minister confirms first H1N1 flu cases in Brazil |url=http://g1.globo.com/Noticias/Economia_Negocios/0,,MUL1113115-9356,00-MINISTRO+CONFIRMA+PRIMEIROS+CASOS+DE+GRIPE+A+HN+NO+BRASIL.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090819083551/http://g1.globo.com/Noticias/Economia_Negocios/0,,MUL1113115-9356,00-MINISTRO+CONFIRMA+PRIMEIROS+CASOS+DE+GRIPE+A+HN+NO+BRASIL.html |archive-date=August 19, 2009 |access-date=October 7, 2010 |website=G1}}</ref> According to the [[World Health Organization]], the virus caused over 18,000 deaths worldwide since its emergence in April 2009.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.redorbit.com/news/health/1893907/h1n1_still_a_pandemic_says_who/|title=H1N1 Still A Pandemic, Says WHO|author=redOrbit|date=July 20, 2010|access-date=October 7, 2010}}</ref>

==== Sanitation ====
Among the four components of social infrastructure, [[sanitation]] is the least developed in Erechim. Despite this, it is in sanitation that the city holds its highest state ranking, at 11th, due to significant improvements between 1998 and 2004.<ref name="a" /> Federal Law No. 11,445 of 2007 established a new framework for basic sanitation, mandating services for [[drinking water|water supply]], sanitary sewage, urban cleaning with solid waste management, and stormwater drainage in all Brazilian municipalities. This requires the development of a basic sanitation plan, economic feasibility analysis, regulatory standards, public hearings, a tariff system, conditions for [[sustainability]] of economic-financial balance, and contract duration.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/saneamento|title=Sanitation|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=October 7, 2010|archive-date=October 21, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021090850/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-social/saneamento|url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Public safety ===
[[File:Brasão BMRS.PNG|thumb|left|95px|Emblem of the [[Military Brigade of Rio Grande do Sul]]]]
In 1980, Erechim made statewide headlines due to four murders committed by [[serial killer]] [[Luiz Baú]], who had escaped from the Erechim State Prison and stabbed his victims, mostly young boys, with extreme cruelty, earning the nickname "The Monster of Erechim".<ref>{{cite thesis |type=Master’s dissertation |last=Rocha |first=Humberto José da |title=The "Monster of Erechim": A Case Study on the Imagery of Fear |publisher=[[University of Passo Fundo]] |url=http://tede.upf.br/jspui/bitstream/tede/135/1/2007HumbertoJoseDaRocha.pdf |year=2007 |language=pt}}</ref>

As in most medium and large Brazilian municipalities, crime is a challenge in Erechim. In 2006, the [[homicide]] rate was 15.1%. The rate of [[death|fatalities]] by [[firearm]] peaked at 30.2% in 2003, dropped to 7.6% between 2004 and 2005, but rose to 11.9% in 2006. The rate of deaths from traffic accidents increased from 12.1% in 2002 to 29.2% in 2006.<ref name="ritla">{{cite web|url=http://www.ritla.net/index.php?option=com_docman&task=doc_download&gid=242|title=Municipal Database|author=Ritla|date=2008|work=Map of Violence in Brazilian Municipalities-2008|format=[[Microsoft Excel|xls]]|access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref>

To address high crime rates, the municipal government, in collaboration with the [[Military Brigade of Rio Grande do Sul]] (BMRS) and the Municipal Secretariat for Public Safety and Social Protection, established under Article 27 of Law 4,420 of February 11, 2009,<ref>{{cite web |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |title=Municipal Secretariat for Public Safety and Social Protection |url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br//secretaria/13/sec-saude |access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref> has implemented various measures. One such measure is Law No. 4,723, which amended Article 4 of Law No. 3,925 of December 13, 2005, mandating that bars, restaurants, entertainment venues, and similar establishments in Erechim display signs prohibiting the sale of alcohol, cigarettes, and similar products to minors. This has helped reduce incidents involving minors.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/seminario-regional-de-seguranca-publica-e-direitos-humanos-discute-a-violencia-contra-a-mulher|title=Regional Public Safety and Human Rights Seminar Discusses Violence Against Women|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=September 30, 2010|access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref> Another initiative is the Violence Prevention Program (PPV), aimed at reducing violence through a social network that identifies, integrates, and promotes governmental and non-governmental actions to combat and prevent crime, focusing on high-risk neighborhoods and communities.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/erechim-tera-programa-de-prevencao-a-violencia|title=Erechim to Implement Violence Prevention Program|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=June 16, 2009|access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref>

=== Transportation ===
==== Air transport ====
The [[Erechim Airport]] ([[International Air Transport Association|IATA]]: '''ERM''', [[International Civil Aviation Organization|ICAO]]: '''SSER''') faces competition from nearby airports in [[Passo Fundo Airport|Passo Fundo]] and [[Chapecó Airport|Chapecó]], limiting its growth. However, plans are in place to expand and renovate the passenger terminal by 65 m², bringing the total to 330.49 m². This includes accessibility improvements for people with disabilities, air conditioning, fencing, and new furniture, funded through partnerships with SEINFRA (State Secretariat for Infrastructure and Logistics) and the Federal Airport Assistance Program (PROFAA), with contributions from the Erechim municipality.<ref name="Prefeitura_AirPista">{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/restauracao-da-pista-do-aeroporto-de-erechim-e-inaugurada|title=Restoration of Erechim Airport Runway Inaugurated|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=April 9, 2010|access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref> By 2011, the implementation of a Category 4 Firefighting Section was planned.<ref name="Prefeitura_AirPista"/>

A key operator at the airport is [[NHT Linhas Aéreas]], offering flights to [[Porto Alegre]] and [[São Paulo]]. The airline operates a fleet of six [[Let L-410UVP-E20|LET 410 UVP E-20]] aircraft, designed for 19 passengers and two crew members, manufactured in Europe by [[Aircraft Industries]] and equipped with advanced navigation and safety systems. The Erechim route has recently been enhanced with the addition of two new aircraft.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/nht-linhas-aereas-retoma-voos-diarios-em-erechim|title=NHT Linhas Aéreas Resumes Daily Flights in Erechim|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=October 6, 2010|access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref>

==== Urban transport ====
[[File:Onibus-urbano-erechim.jpg|thumb|219px|Bus representing [[public transport]] in the city]]
The first urban transport company in Erechim, "Empresas de Transportes Gaurama Ltda," was established in 1953, operating routes from [[Marcelino Ramos]] and [[Gaurama]] to Erechim.<ref name="tu">{{cite web|url=http://www.urbanoerechim.com.br/urbanonovo/secoes.php?secao=historico|title=Urban Public Transport in Erechim|author=Urbano Erechim|access-date=December 24, 2009}}</ref> From 1954, it began providing urban transport within the city. Until 1960, it served five neighborhoods: Aeroporto, Linho, Cemitério Municipal, Três Vendas, and Piscina Clube.<ref name="tu" /> Since 1980, Gaurama has focused solely on municipal transport after selling its intermunicipal routes.<ref name="tu" /> Currently, nearly all city neighborhoods are covered.<ref name="Prefeitura_Urbano">{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/municipio/infraestrutura-fisica/transporte-urbano|title=Urban Transport|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=December 24, 2009|archive-date=December 30, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201230104033/https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The [[fare]] for public transport aligns with that of the state capital. However, transferring to another bus requires a new ticket. The standard urban transport fare is currently R$4.80.<ref name="tuu">{{cite web|url=http://urbanoerechim.com.br/|title=Ticket Price|access-date=May 3, 2018|publisher=Urbano Erechim}}</ref> A student fare system offers tickets at R$2.40, valid only with a Student Transport Card.<ref name="tuu" />

In 2019, an electronic ticketing system was introduced,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.jornalbomdia.com.br/noticia/27848/urbano-testa-equipamentos-em-erechim|title=Urbano Tests Equipment in Erechim | access-date=August 25, 2022|website=Jornal Bom Dia|language=pt-br}}</ref> using cards, as implemented in many other cities. By 2022, the use of physical tokens for fares was phased out.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/|title=Municipal Law Regulating the Use of Tokens for Urban Public Transport in Erechim Until May 31, 2022|access-date=August 25, 2022|website=Municipal Government of Erechim|language=pt-br}}</ref>

==== Road and rail transport ====
In 2009, Erechim’s vehicle fleet totaled 46,950, including 30,089 cars, 1,651 trucks, 481 tractor-trucks, 3,552 pickups, 213 minibuses, 7,492 motorcycles, 2,948 scooters, 515 buses, and nine wheeled tractors.<ref name="IBGE_Frota">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=frota&codmun=430700&nomemun=Erechim|title=Fleet 2009|author=Cities@ - IBGE|date=2009|access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref> The city’s paved avenues and numerous [[traffic light|traffic lights]] facilitate traffic flow, but the growing number of vehicles over the past decade has led to increasingly slow traffic, particularly in the Seat district. Additionally, finding parking spaces in the commercial center has become challenging, impacting local [[commerce]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ijuhy.com/noticia-ler.php?id=10581|title=At Least Four People Die in Accident on Erechim-Passo Fundo Highway|author=Ijuhi|date=February 12, 2010|access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref>

The Erechim Bus Terminal is a major hub for road transport in northern [[Rio Grande do Sul]] and southern [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], serving destinations in over ten Brazilian states, including [[Mato Grosso]], [[Minas Gerais]], [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Bahia]], and the [[Federal District (Brazil)|Federal District]]. It covers the southern, parts of the southeastern, central-western, and northeastern regions of Brazil, with key operators including Reunidas and Unesul.<ref name="Prefeitura_Urbano"/>

Erechim also has a historic railway station, which played a significant role in the city and region’s development. Inaugurated on August 3, 1910,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/turista/pontos-turisticos/estacao-ferroviaria|title=Railway Station|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|access-date=October 8, 2010|archive-date=October 21, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021091049/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/turista/pontos-turisticos/estacao-ferroviaria|url-status=dead}}</ref> the local railway network is currently abandoned. However, municipalities in the Alto Uruguai and plateau regions have united to demand action from [[América Latina Logística]] (ALL), the company responsible for the regional rail network, regarding the neglected state of the railway in Alto Uruguai and the 386 km stretch to [[Santa Maria, Rio Grande do Sul|Santa Maria]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/audiencia-publica-encaminha-comite-para-ativacao-da-rede-ferroviaria|title=Public Hearing Advances Committee for Railway Network Reactivation|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=May 17, 2010|access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref>

=== Services and communications ===
[[File:Usina Itá.jpg|thumb|left|[[Itá Hydroelectric Power Plant|Itá UHE]], one of the facilities supplying energy to the region]]
Erechim is served by the electric company Rio Grande Energia (RGE) since 1997, covering 262 municipalities, or 51% of Rio Grande do Sul’s total municipalities.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rge-rs.com.br/aRge/quem_somos.asp|title=RGE - Rio Grande Energia|author=Rio Grande Energia (RGE)|access-date=July 15, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100830151514/http://www.rge-rs.com.br/arge/quem_somos.asp|archive-date=August 30, 2010|url-status=dead}}</ref> Power generation is no longer local but comes from the ''Functional Planning Region 9'', which includes major hydroelectric plants such as [[Itá Hydroelectric Power Plant|Itá]] and [[Machadinho Hydroelectric Power Plant|Machadinho]].<ref name="a" /> Along with [[Passo Fundo]], Erechim is among RGE’s highest energy consumers. Existing and planned PCH and MCH transmission lines are expected to meet demand. The [[List of mesoregions and microregions of Rio Grande do Sul|Erechim microregion]] is a significant energy exporter and requires minimal upgrades. However, rural areas generally lack sufficient lighting to maintain temperatures in [[poultry farm|poultry farms]] or [[pigsties]] or to install cooling systems for milk and meat.<ref name="a" /> Approximately 98% of urban residences have electricity, though this drops to 75–95% in the rural area. The CRERAL (Alto Uruguai Regional Rural Electrification Cooperative), founded on July 23, 1969, serves around 6,000 consumers as an alternative distributor.<ref name="a" /><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.creral.com.br/index.php?id_menu=empresa&item=2|title=Area of Operation|author=CRERAL|access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.creral.com.br/index.php?id_menu=empresa|title=About CRERAL|author=CRERAL|access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref>

Water supply for the entire city is managed by the Rio Grande do Sul Sanitation Company (Corsan).<ref>{{cite web |author= |date=March 31, 2010 |title=Hearing Discusses Water Supply in Erechim |url=https://www.sema.rs.gov.br/seminario-discute-abastecimento-publico-em-erechim |access-date=October 10, 2010 |website=SEMA}}</ref> Sewage collection is handled by the municipal government.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/prefeitura-coloca-em-consulta-publica-edital-de-licitacao-que-vai-contratar-empresa-para-prestar-o-servico-de-saneamento-basico-em-erechim|title=City Hall Opens Public Consultation for Bidding to Hire a Basic Sanitation Service Provider in Erechim|author=Municipal Government of Erechim|date=August 5, 2010|access-date=October 10, 2010}}</ref> Internet services include dial-up, [[broadband]] ([[Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line|ADSL]]), and fiber optic, offered by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|ISPs]]. Mobile phone services are provided by multiple operators, with [[3G]] access available since September 1, 2008.<ref name="Baguete_3G">{{cite web|url=http://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/telecom/01/09/2008/claro-3g-chega-a-carlos-barbosa-e-erechim|title=Claro 3G Arrives in Carlos Barbosa and Erechim|author=Gláucia Civa|date=September 1, 2008|publisher=Baguete|access-date=October 10, 2010}}</ref> Erechim’s area code ([[direct distance dialing|DDD]]) is 054,<ref name="Guiatel_DDD">{{cite web|url=http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_rs.htm|title=Rio Grande do Sul|author=Guiatel|access-date=October 10, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110623233209/http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_rs.htm|archive-date=June 23, 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref> and its [[postal code]] ranges from 99700-001 to 99717-999.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correios.com.br/|title=Postal Codes of Brazilian Cities|author=[[Correios]]|access-date=October 10, 2010}}</ref> On January 19, 2009, Erechim, along with 137 other municipalities in Rio Grande do Sul sharing the same DDD, gained access to [[Local number portability|number portability]], allowing users to switch operators without changing their phone numbers.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/geral/19/01/2009/portabilidade-chega-ao-ddd-54|title=Number Portability Arrives in DDD 54|author=Márcia Lima|date=January 19, 2009|publisher=Baguete|access-date=October 10, 2010}}</ref>

Erechim has several circulating newspapers, including Jornal Diário da Manhã, Jornal Boa Vista, Jornal Bom Dia, Jornal Voz Regional, A Região, and O Esporte. The city is also home to six radio stations: Difusão FM 94.9, Virtual FM 104.7, Cultura FM 105.9, Rede Colinas FM 90.7, Difusão Sul-Riograndense AM 650, and TchêErechim AM 1200. Television stations include a local [[RBS TV|RBS]] affiliate, [[RBS TV Erechim]], as well as repeaters for SBT RS, BAND RS, TVE RS, and TV Pampa.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.clicerechim.com.br/|title=ClicErechim Website|author=ClicErechim|access-date=October 10, 2010|archive-date=September 23, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100923120621/http://www.clicerechim.com.br/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Architecture ===
Erechim, particularly its central region, is characterized by a diverse and significant architectural ensemble. According to the municipal government, the most prominent architectural style shaping the city’s urban landscape is [[Art Deco]], first introduced at the International Exhibition of Modern Decorative and Industrial Arts in [[Paris]] in 1925. This style emerged as a bridge between the [[Eclecticism in architecture|eclectic style]] and the then-radical [[modernism]], featuring geometric forms and abstract designs.<ref name="Prefeitura_Déco">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/turista/pontos-turisticos/art-deco |title=Art Deco |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-date=October 21, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101021091023/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/turista/pontos-turisticos/art-deco |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Art Deco’s decorative motifs primarily rely on the geometry of the [[cube]], [[sphere]], and straight line, along with zigzags—horizontal and vertical lines that form stepped patterns inspired by Mesoamerican, indigenous, and African cultures. The style also incorporates maritime prow-like elements supported by mast-like pillars, ellipses, and luxurious materials such as [[marble]] and [[granite]]. Art Deco facades adhere to geometric precision and linear rhythm, with curved lines following a well-defined arc and straight lines, as if drawn with a ruler.<ref name="Prefeitura_Déco"/>

Erechim was one of Brazil’s first modern planned cities. Its urban layout was inspired by planning concepts used in [[Washington, D.C.]] (1791), [[Paris]] (1850), [[Buenos Aires]] (1580), and [[Belo Horizonte]] (1897). The city’s design features wide streets, a strong hierarchy, and diagonal avenues intersecting a grid, creating convergence points. Key elements include a perpendicular street grid intersected by diagonal avenues, uniformly sized blocks, and a perimeter avenue.<ref name="Raízes_Histórico">{{cite web |url=http://www.raizesdosul.com.br/mun136.htm |title=History |author=Raízes do Sul |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090707205259/http://www.raizesdosul.com.br/mun136.htm |archive-date=July 7, 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Traditions, arts, and events ===
[[File:Centro cultural 25 de julho.jpg|thumb|left|25 de Julho Cultural Center, a venue for numerous cultural events]]
Theater and cinema play significant roles in Erechim. Throughout the year, the municipal government, in partnership with local or regional companies, organizes various cultural events. Recently, Erechim earned the nickname “Capital of Amateur Theater in Rio Grande do Sul” for hosting numerous theater-related events.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.daubi.jor.br/16-11-2004-03-administracao-municipal.htm |title=Erechim is the Capital of Amateur Theater in Rio Grande do Sul |author=Daubi Piccoh |date=November 16, 2004 |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-date=January 18, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118183551/http://www.daubi.jor.br/16-11-2004-03-administracao-municipal.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 25 de Julho Cultural Center is a primary venue for these activities. Events promoting local and regional music are also frequently held, such as performances by the choir of the Regional Integrated University of Alto Uruguai and Missões (URI).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/10/08/coral-da-uri-campus-de-erechim-e-atracao-no-cultura-de-erechim-e-10/ |title=URI-Erechim Campus Choir is an attraction at Cultura de Erechim é 10 |author=Diana Rocha |date=October 8, 2010 |publisher=ClicRBS |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-date=March 4, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304120226/http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/10/08/coral-da-uri-campus-de-erechim-e-atracao-no-cultura-de-erechim-e-10/ |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/tag/cultura/ |title=Culture |author=ClicRBS |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-date=October 9, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101009102334/http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/tag/cultura/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city also promotes and encourages artistic work by schoolchildren.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/10/06/a-arte-despertando-talentos-esta-em-exposicao-no-castelinho/ |title="Art Awakening Talents" Exhibition at Castelinho |author=Diana Rocha |date=October 6, 2010 |publisher=ClicRBS |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-date=March 3, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303213449/http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/10/06/a-arte-despertando-talentos-esta-em-exposicao-no-castelinho/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Erechim has a notable tradition of crafts and cuisine. Regional dishes, ranging from [[pie|pies]] and [[bonbon|bonbons]] to homemade meals such as [[rice and beans]], and artisanal products are sold at city stalls, fairs, or recurring events. The Erechim Artisans Association supports local and regional artisans by providing work opportunities.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticia/3704/12-03-2010/bairros-ganham-mais-investimentos-em-pavimentacao |title=Second Edition of "Women in the Square" Held This Sunday |author=Bom Dia Rio Grande |date=March 12, 2010 |access-date=October 8, 2010 }}</ref> Recently, the Municipal Council approved a law granting artisans more space for sales and promotion, including an area in Júlio de Castilhos Square, where the Erechim Artisan House was established.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/artistas-e-artesaos-ganham-espaco-da-julio-de-castilhos |title=Artists and Artisans Gain Space in Júlio de Castilhos |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |date=November 19, 2008 |access-date=October 8, 2010}}</ref>

Erechim also hosts a multi-sector fair known as Frinape, which celebrated its 14th edition in 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.frinape.com.br/sessoes/conteudo/historia#.UmV49XBwrKc|title=History of Frinape|publisher=Frinape|access-date=October 21, 2013|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131021212418/http://www.frinape.com.br/sessoes/conteudo/historia#.UmV49XBwrKc|archive-date=October 21, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Sports ===
[[File:Colosso da Lagoa.jpg|thumb|right|[[Estádio Olímpico Colosso da Lagoa]] during a [[Grenal]] match]]
As in much of Brazil, [[Association football|football]] is the most popular sport in Erechim. The city’s main club is [[Ypiranga Futebol Clube]], commonly known as Ypiranga de Erechim, founded on August 18, 1924. It plays its home games at the [[Estádio Olímpico Colosso da Lagoa]], inaugurated on September 2, 1970, with a capacity of up to 30,000 spectators. The record attendance was approximately 25,000 for a match between Ypiranga and [[SC Internacional|Sport Club Internacional]] on August 18, 1974, which Internacional won 2-0. It is the third-largest stadium in Rio Grande do Sul, behind [[Arena do Grêmio]] and [[Estádio Beira-Rio|Beira-Rio]]. The field measures 110 m by 80 m. The inaugural match was between [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio Foot-Ball Porto Alegrense]] and [[Santos FC|Santos Futebol Clube]], with Santos winning 2-0, with goals by [[Pelé]] (his 1,040th career goal) and Léo.<ref>{{cite web |author=Diana Rocha |date=September 2, 2020 |title=Estádio Olímpico Colosso da Lagoa completa 50 anos de história |trans-title=Colosso da Lagoa Olympic Stadium celebrates 50 years of history |url=https://www.atmosferaonline.com.br/estadio-olimpico-colosso-da-lagoa-completa-50-anos-de-historia/ |access-date=2025-12-10 |website=Atmofesta Online |publisher=ClicRBS |language=pt}}</ref>

Erechim also excels in [[Sport of athletics|athletics]]. The Erechim Runners Club (CORRE) represents the sport, with over 20 athletes, including 10 high-performance competitors in adult and youth categories for both genders. CORRE organizes events and sports projects, ranging from social inclusion to high-performance training, promoting athletics and street running as means of leisure, health, and quality of life.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.webnode.com/w/corre-erechim.webnode.com/ |title=Leisure and High Performance |author=Clube dos Corredores de Erechim (CORRE) |access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref> On December 14, 2010, Erechim hosted the “Stars Game,” a charity match featuring celebrities and musicians against members of the Municipal Council and Ypiranga players.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/12/01/nomes-de-participantes-do-jogo-das-estrelas-sao-divulgados/ |title=Participants' Names for the Stars Game Announced |publisher=wp.clicrbs.com.br |access-date=December 2, 2010 |archive-date=March 4, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304062943/http://wp.clicrbs.com.br/erechim/2010/12/01/nomes-de-participantes-do-jogo-das-estrelas-sao-divulgados/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

A major sporting event is the Erechim City School Olympics, held annually by the municipal government in partnership with the Municipal Secretariat for Culture, Sports, and Tourism. It involves students from municipal schools competing in various sports, including [[futsal]], [[volleyball]], [[basketball]], [[Sport of athletics|athletics]], [[chess]], [[table tennis]], [[swimming]], [[handball]], and [[dominoes]], for participants aged 7 to 15. In 2010, 23 schools participated.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/olimpiada-escolar-contara-com-23-escolas-em-2010 |title=School Olympics to Include 23 Schools in 2010 |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |date=October 8, 2010 |access-date=October 9, 2010 |archive-date=January 19, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120119131019/http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/olimpiada-escolar-contara-com-23-escolas-em-2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/ultimos-acertos-para-a-olimpiada-escolar-de-erechim-ocorrem-terca |title=Final Arrangements for Erechim School Olympics Held Tuesday |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |date=September 24, 2009 |access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref> Another significant event is the [[Rally de Erechim]], an annual international [[Rallying|rally]] competition since 1998, considered Brazil’s largest and one of South America’s most prominent.<ref name="Prefeitura_Rally">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmerechim.rs.gov.br/noticias/erechim-vai-receber-a-maior-prova-de-rali-de-velocidade-do-brasil |title=Erechim to Host Brazil's Largest Speed Rally |author=Municipal Government of Erechim |date=May 4, 2009 |access-date=October 28, 2010}}</ref> The inaugural event featured a starting grid of about ten cars, with only two Erechim teams participating. Winners included Cassandro Maloz and Gustavo Priess, followed by Luiz Tenebro and Marco Marini (category 2.0); Ulisses Berthold and André Pagliosa in category N3, followed by Cláudio Rossi and Fernando Bitencourt; Paulo Lemos and Maria Antonite in A7; and Luis Tedesco and Alberto Blanco in A6. In category 1.6, Fernando Mello and Blender won.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rallybrazil.com/historia09.php |title=History |author=Rally Brazil |access-date=October 8, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120111112516/http://www.rallybrazil.com/historia09.php |archive-date=January 11, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Holidays ===
Erechim observes four [[public holiday|public holidays]], eight national holidays, and four optional holidays. Municipal holidays include the [[Ragamuffin War]] Day on September 20, [[All Souls' Day|All Souls’ Day]] on November 2, and the floating holidays of [[Good Friday]] and [[Feast of Corpus Christi|Corpus Christi]], which is held on the Thursday following [[Trinity Sunday]].<ref>{{cite web |author= |date=October 2005 |title=Municipal Holidays of Rio Grande do Sul |url=http://www.turismo.rs.gov.br/uploads/1227711762Feriados_Munic___26.11.08.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113003326/http://www.turismo.rs.gov.br/uploads/1227711762Feriados_Munic___26.11.08.pdf |archive-date=November 13, 2012 |access-date=October 9, 2010 |website=State Secretariat for Tourism, Sports, and Leisure}}</ref> According to Law No. 9,093 of September 12, 1995, municipalities may have up to four public holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web|url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm|title=Is Carnival a holiday? Taking time off may result in changes to the contract |author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |publisher=Guia Trabalhista |access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref><ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaLei">{{cite web|url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/legislacao/l9093.htm|title=Law No. 9,093 of September 12, 1995 |author=Guia Trabalhista |access-date=October 9, 2010}}</ref>
