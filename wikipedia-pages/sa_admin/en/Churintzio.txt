'''Churintzio''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located {{convert|104|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Churintzio is located in northern Michoacán at an altitude between {{convert|1700|and(-)|2500|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Ecuandureo]] to the west, [[La Piedad]] to the north, [[Zináparo]] to the northeast, [[Penjamillo]] to the southeast, and [[Tlazazalca]] to the south.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Churintzio, Michoacán de Ocampo |language=es |publisher=INEGI |accessdate=24 April 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|230.084|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.4% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/> 

Churintzio is located in the highlands of the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]]. It is watered by intermittent streams and cold springs in the [[Lerma River]] basin.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

Churintzio's climate is temperate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Churintzio was founded in 1543. The place name may derive from the [[Purépecha language|Purépecha]] words ''achuri'', "night", or ''chureni'', "to grow dark".<ref name=encyclopedia/> It became a municipality on 1 March 1904.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=80, 130 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Francisco Javier Pérez Maldonado.<ref>{{cite news |last=Córdoba Hernández |first=Leonardo |url=https://www.am.com.mx/2018/07/04/la-piedad/local/recibe-constancia-de-mayoria-juan-luis-contreras-en-churintzio-4-490110 |title=Recibe constancia de mayoria Juan Luis Contreras en Churintzio |language=es |newspaper=Periódico am |date=4 July 2018 |accessdate=8 December 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Churintzio recorded a population of 5564 inhabitants living in 1654 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=013 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Churintzio |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |accessdate=24 April 2018 |archive-date=25 April 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180425033546/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=013 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 5016 inhabitants in Churintzio.<ref name=survey/>

There are 19 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Churintzio, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2592 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The main economic activities in Churintzio are agriculture and livestock production.<ref>{{cite news |author=Churintzio Municipal Council |url=http://leyes.michoacan.gob.mx/destino/O11320po.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal 2015–2018 |language=es |page=7 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=28 April 2016 |accessdate=24 April 2018}}</ref> A [[maquiladora]] manufactures clothes in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.quadratin.com.mx/politica/buscara-sergio-flores-impulsar-la-economia-en-churintzio-y-numaran/ |title=Buscará Sergio Flores impulsar la economía en Churintzio y Numarán |language=es |newspaper=Quadratin |date=5 April 2018 |accessdate=24 April 2018}}</ref> [[Mexican Federal Highway 15D|Federal Highway 15D]] traverses the municipality from east to west.
