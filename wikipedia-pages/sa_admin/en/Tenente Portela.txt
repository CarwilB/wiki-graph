'''Tenente Portela''' is a city in the north-west region of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], the southernmost state of [[Brazil]], with an estimated population of 13,434 inhabitants. The city's geographical coordinates are {{Coord|27|22|16|S|53|45|30|W|type:city}}, and its elevation is 390 m. Area: 337.5&nbsp;km².
