'''Amaral Ferrador''' ({{IPA|pt|a.maˈɾaw fe.ʁaˈdoʁ|lang|Br-Amaral Ferrador.ogg}}) is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 7,085 (2020 est.) in an area of 506.46&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6DN Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is situated on the [[Camaquã River]].

==Economy==

The main economical activities are trade, agriculture (maize, tobacco and beans) and livestock (cattle, sheep and pork).

==Demography==

Its population consists of 60% Portuguese ancestry, 25% Polish ancestry and 15% [[Afro-Brazilian]]s.
