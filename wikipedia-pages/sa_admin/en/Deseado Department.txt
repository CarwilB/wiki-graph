'''Deseado Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It covers  an area of 63,784 km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 72,953 at the 2001 Census, which rose to 107,630 at the 2010 Census and 126,743 at the 2022 Census. The seat of the department is in [[Puerto Deseado]], with 16,747 inhabitants in 2022, but the largest town is the port of [[Caleta Olivia]], which had 56,312 inhabitants in 2022. Other large towns in 2022 are [[Las Heras, Santa Cruz|Las Heras]] (24,728)
and [[Pico Truncado]] (25,194).
