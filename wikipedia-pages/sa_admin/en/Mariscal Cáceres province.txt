'''Mariscal Cáceres''' ({{langx|es|provincia de Mariscal Cáceres}}) is one of the [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of [[San Martín Region]], [[Peru]]. Its capital is the town of [[Juanjui]]. It was created by a law of the Peruvian congress during the presidency of [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].<ref name=law>{{Cite web |title=Ley N° 9097 |url=https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/09097.pdf|access-date=12 December 2023}}</ref>

The province is named after the 19th-century Peruvian president [[Andrés Avelino Cáceres]].{{cn|date=December 2023}}

==Political division==
Mariscal Cáceres Province is divided into five districts:<ref name=law/>
*[[Campanilla District]], centered in the settlement [[Campanilla]]
*[[Huicungo District]], centered in the settlement [[Huicungo]]
*[[Juanjui District]], centered in the town [[Juanjui]]
*[[Pachiza District]], centered in the settlement [[Pachiza]]
*[[Pajarillo District]], centered in the settlement [[Pajarillo]]
