'''Tonosí  ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tonosí District]], [[Los Santos Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 2,257 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of [[Tonosí District]].<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,910; its population as of 2000 was 2,282.<ref name=inec/>

The town is served by [[Tonosí Airport]].
