'''Modelo''' is a [[city]] in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], in the [[Southern Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. The area was granted municipality status in 1961, its area being taken from the existing municipality of [[São Carlos, Santa Catarina|São Carlos]];<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://leis.alesc.sc.gov.br/html/1961/780_1961_Lei_promulgada.html|title=Lei promulgada nº 780, cria os municípios de Saudades, Pinhalzinho e Modelo|publisher=Legislação Estadual de Santa Catarina|lang=pt|date=7 December 1961}}</ref> Three areas have subsequently been removed to form the new municipalities of [[Bom Jesus do Oeste]], [[Serra Alta]] and [[Sul Brasil]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/santacatarina/modelo.pdf|title=Modelo: Santa Catarina|publisher=IBGE|lang=pt|access-date=26 January 2020}}</ref>
