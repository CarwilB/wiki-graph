'''Vargem Alta''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 21,591 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|415|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the protected area of the [[Frade e a Freira Natural Monument]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt|title=Monumento Natural O Frade e a Freira|publisher=Secretário de Meio Ambiente, Espírito Santo|url=http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/default.asp?pagina=16710|access-date=2016-05-06|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160603133900/http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/default.asp?pagina=16710|archive-date=2016-06-03|url-status=dead}}</ref>
