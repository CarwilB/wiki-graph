'''Papagaios''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was 15,800 in 2020 and its area is {{convert|554|km2|mi2|abbr=off}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/SHI]</ref> Its economy is based on exports of [[slate]].

Papagaios is {{convert|150|km|mi|abbr=off}} away from the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]].
