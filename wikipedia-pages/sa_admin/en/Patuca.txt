'''Patuca''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the south of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]], east of [[Juticalpa, Olancho|Juticalpa]] and south of [[Catacamas]].

It contains the [[Patuca River]], which is the second largest in [[Central America]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://alexagui.bol.ucla.edu/patuca/ |title=Patuca River Campaign |accessdate=2005-10-30 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20060918062212/http://alexagui.bol.ucla.edu/patuca/ |archivedate=2006-09-18 }} Patuca River Campaign</ref>

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Patuca municipality had a population of 26,668. Of these, 98.27% were [[Mestizo]], 0.92% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.63% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.12% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.06% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
