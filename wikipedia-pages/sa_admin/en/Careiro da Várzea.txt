'''Careiro da Várzea''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 30,846 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/careiro-da-varzea/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 2,631&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>  It lies on the south (right) bank of the Amazon opposite [[Manaus]].

Careiro da Várzea is the terminus of the [[BR-319]] road from southern Brazil.  There is a ferry link to Manaus.
