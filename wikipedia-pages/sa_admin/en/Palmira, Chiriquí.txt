'''Palmira''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Boquete District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|57.5|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,776 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|30.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,043; its population as of 2000 was 1,513.<ref name=inec/> There are three urban areas with some limited housing density.  They are Palmira Centro, Palmira Arriba and Palmira Abajo. Palmira has excellent soil, abundant rivers and creeks and grows a variety of vegetables, fruits and coffee.  There is also some meat production.
