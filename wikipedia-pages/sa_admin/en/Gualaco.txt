'''Gualaco''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the north of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]], west of [[San Esteban, Olancho|San Esteban]], north of [[Santa Maria del Real]] and east of [[Guata]].

Gualaco is served by [[Jicalapa Airport]], a grass [[airstrip]] {{convert|3|km}} southeast of town.<ref>[https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=13/15.0243/-86.0737 OpenStreetMap - Gualaco/Jicalapa]</ref>

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Gualaco municipality had a population of 21,863. Of these, 97.11% were [[Mestizo]], 1.76% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.56% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.50% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.07% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
