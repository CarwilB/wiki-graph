The '''San Salvador Department''' (in Spanish, Departamento San Salvador) is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of [[Entre Ríos Province]], [[Argentina]], created in 1995. It is located in the center-east of the province.

The department has 20,854 inhabitants as per the 2022 census [INDEC], which are distributed in 4 districts. The head town is [[San Salvador, Entre Ríos|San Salvador]], which has a population of 13,228. Other towns are [[General Campos]], [[Colonia Baylina]], [[San Ernesto, Entre Ríos|San Ernesto]] and [[Walter Moss]].
