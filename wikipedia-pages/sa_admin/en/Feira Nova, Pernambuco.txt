'''Feira Nova''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈfeɾɐ ˈnɔvɐ]}}) is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. According with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], has an estimated population of 21,427 inhabitants ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]).<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Limoeiro]]  ('''N'''); [[Glória do Goitá]]  ('''S''');  [[Passira]]  ('''W''');  [[Lagoa do Itaenga]]  ('''E''')
* Area - 107.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 154 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] river
* Vegetation - [[Caducifólia]] forest
* Climate - tropical hot and humid
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 78&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Feira Nova''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[sugarcane]] and [[cattle]] and their [[milk]].
