'''La Unión''' is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] in [[Chordeleg Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of 14.1&nbsp;km² and according to the 2001 Ecuadorian census it had a population total of 1,622.<ref name="Ajupa">{{cite web|url=http://www.ajupa.gov.ec/parroq.aspx?cant=11&parr=52|title=Parroquia La Unión|publisher=Asociación de Juntas Parroquiales Rurales del Azuay|accessdate=February 26, 2010}}{{Dead link|date=February 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
