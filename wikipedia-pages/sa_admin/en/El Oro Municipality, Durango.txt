''' El Oro''' ({{IPA|es|el ˈoɾo|lang|El Oro.ogg}}) is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Santa María del Oro, Durango|Santa María del Oro]]. The municipality covers an area of 10,041&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 11,320,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=El Oro|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=018|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=23 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160323182754/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=018|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 10,501 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=El Oro |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10005a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=November 10, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070528124241/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10005a.htm |archive-date=May 28, 2007 }}</ref>

The municipality had 113 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 population in parentheses) was [[Santa María del Oro, Durango|Santa María del Oro]] (5,878), classified as urban.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
