'''Capivari de Baixo''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southern region of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.capivaridebaixo.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item%3D15272%26fa%3D2557 |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Capivari de Baixo / SC |access-date=2013-02-24 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140414004600/http://www.capivaridebaixo.sc.gov.br/conteudo/?item=15272&fa=2557 |archive-date=2014-04-14 }}</ref> In 2010, it had a population estimated at 21,674 inhabitants.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=420395&lang=|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Santa Catarina {{!}} Capivari de Baixo|website=www.cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2017-01-11}}</ref> As of 2020, the population was estimated to be at 25,177. From 1991 to 2000, the demographic growth has been of 1.51% yearly, while the national average was of 1.63%.<ref name=":1" /> From 2000 to 2010, the demographic growth was of 1.56% yearly, while the national average was of 1.17%.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://atlasbrasil.org.br/2013/pt/perfil_m/capivari%20de%20baixo_sc|title=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano no Brasil|date=2013|access-date=January 10, 2016}}</ref> Capivari de Baixo has the largest coal-fired thermoelectric power plant of [[Latin America]], Complexo Termoelétrico Jorge Lacerda.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://diariodosul.com.br/SITE2015/noticia/21169/Cinco-decadas-de-existencia-do-Complexo-Jorge-Lacerda-.html|title=Cinco décadas de existência do Complexo Jorge Lacerda|last=SUL|first=DIARIO DO|website=diariodosul.com.br|access-date=2017-01-11|archive-date=2017-08-12|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170812025352/http://diariodosul.com.br/SITE2015/noticia/21169/Cinco-decadas-de-existencia-do-Complexo-Jorge-Lacerda-.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
