'''Iglesia''' is a department of the province of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.canal13sanjuan.com/san-juan/el-peronismo-gano-en-12-de-los-19-departamentos-de-san-juan-_a68fec2cb3c9bd063e329c15f|title=El peronismo ganó en 12 de los 19 departamentos de San Juan|work=Diario 13 San Juan|date=26 October 2025|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located in the northwest mountainous corner of this province with substantial vegetation. Iglesia is one of the most visited departments in the province, particularly around [[Rodeo, San Juan|Rodeo]].{{Citation needed|date=March 2022}}

== Economy ==
Nearly 30 percent of the cultivated area is dedicated to logging. Next in land area are pasture and seeds (garlic, beans, lettuce, onions, peas). Fruit, cereals, [[vegetables]] and aromatics are minor crops. Local farmers breed goats and sheep on the grassland.{{Citation needed|date=March 2022}}

Significant gold and silver mining occupy the Cordillera iglesiana, including the [[Veladero]] [[Mining|mine]], which began production in 2005. Located 375 kilometers from the city of San Juan, the project is run by Minera Argentina Gold (MAGSA), a subsidiary of [[Barrick Gold]].{{Citation needed|date=March 2022}}
