'''Agualeguas''' is a [[municipality]] located in the northeastern part of the state of [[Nuevo León]], [[Mexico]], ({{coord|26|19|N|99|32|W|}}). The name "Agualeguas" honors the first inhabitants of the region, the [[Gualegua]] tribe. It was recognized as a village on January 7, 1821, by the [[New Spain|colonial government]] when Bro. Diego Velázquez declared it the village of "St. Nicholas of Gualeguas." 

The municipality itself has an area of 980.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is located 180 meters above sea level. According to the 2020 census, the municipality has a population of 3,382 inhabitants, a decrease of 1.8% from its 3,443 inhabitants according to the 2010 census data.
