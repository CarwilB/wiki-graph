'''Cuichapa''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
Cuichapa is located in central zone of the state, about 90 km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 69.92 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|46|N|96|52|W|}}. The municipality of Cuichapa is delimited to the north by [[Yanga, Veracruz|Yanga]], to the north-west by [[Amatlán de los Reyes (municipality)| Amatlán de los Reyes]], to the south by [[Omealca]], and to the west by [[Coetzala]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30052a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20070626042748/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30052a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2007-06-26|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Cuichapa|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08}}</ref> 

===Climate===
The weather in Cuichapa is warm-medium all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[beans]]. 

==Society==
The Patron of the town is [[San Isidro Labrador]], who is honored with a celebration in May.
