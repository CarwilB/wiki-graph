'''Pedro Moncayo''' is a [[canton (administrative division)|canton]] in the north of the [[Pichincha Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The seat of the canton is the city of [[Tabacundo]]. The canton is named after politician [[Pedro Moncayo]]. The canton is separated by the [[Mojanda]] volcano from the canton of [[Otavalo Canton|Otavalo]] in the province of [[Imbabura Province|Imbabura]] in the north. It borders the same-province cantons of [[Cayambe Canton|Cayambe]] and [[Quito Canton|Quito]].
