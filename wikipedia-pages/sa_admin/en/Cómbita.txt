'''Cómbita''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the sub region of the [[Central Boyacá Province]]. Cómbita is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] and borders [[Arcabuco]] and the department of [[Santander Department|Santander]] in the north, [[Sotaquirá]] in the northeast, [[Tuta, Boyacá|Tuta]] and [[Oicatá]] in the east, department capital [[Tunja]] at {{convert|8.5|km|mi}} away and [[Motavita]] in the south and Arcabuco and Motavita in the west.<ref name=websiteCombita>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.combita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cómbita] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150923205549/http://www.combita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-09-23 }}</ref>

== History ==
Cómbita was in the time before the arrival of the Spanish [[conquistador]]es inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. The [[Muisca rulers|ruler]] of the northern Muisca was the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]], modern day Tunja. The ''[[cacique]]'' of Cómbita was loyal to the ''zaque''.<ref name=websiteCombita/>

In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca, Cómbita means either "Hand of the jaguar and wheel of life" or "Force of the summit".<ref name=websiteCombita/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Cómbita] - Excelsio.net</ref>

Modern Cómbita was founded in 1586.<ref name=ElTiempoCombita>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.eltiempo.com/archivo/documento/MAM-521916 Foundation of Cómbita] - [[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]]</ref>

== Economy ==
The economical activities of Cómbita are [[agriculture]]; [[potato]]es, [[barley]], [[wheat]], [[maize]] and [[pea]]s, and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteCombita/>
