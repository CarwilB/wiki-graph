'''Pedro María Ureña Municipality''' is one of the 29 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] that makes up the western [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Táchira]] and, according to the [[2011 Venezuelean census]], the municipality had a population of 52,678. The mayor of the municipality is Jhon Carrillo of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela|PSUV]] party since December 2017 and the town of [[Ureña, Táchira|Ureña]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Pedro María Ureña Municipality.

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Pedro María Ureña Municipality was 52,678 people, accounting for 4.4% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref> Almost all (96.36%) of the municipality's population is in the town of [[Ureña, Táchira|Ureña]], the [[municipal seat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/tachira/2020__pedro_mar%C3%ADa_ure%C3%B1a/|title=Urban Localities in Pedro María Ureña (Táchira, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Pedro María Ureña as 56,142 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.8% since 2011 and showing a population density of 305.1 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2020__pedro_mar%C3%ADa_ure%C3%B1a/|title=Pedro María Ureña (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 49.8% men (25,821) and 50.2% women (26,058). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 68.1% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 27.1% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 4.8%. The municipality is almost entirely urbanized, with 96.4% of inhabitants (50,006) living in [[urban centers]] compared to just 3.6% (1,873) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (58.1%) and [[Mestizo]] (39.2%). Minority groups included 0.9% [[Afro-Venezuelans]], a small [[Indigenous peoples|indigenous]] population of 4 individuals, and 1.8% belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 96.2%, with 1,608 inhabitants of Pedro María Ureña not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pedro María Ureña Municipality is Jhon Carrillo of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela]], since his election in December 2017.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://prodavinci.com/cne-anuncia-resultados-de-las-elecciones-municipales-de-2017/|title= CNE anuncia resultados de las elecciones municipales de 2017 |trans-title=CNE announces results of the 2017 municipal elections|lang=es|newspaper=[[Prodavinci]]|date=11 December 2017|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref> He was re-elected for a third term in July 2025.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/frontera/proclamado-por-tercera-vez-alcalde-de-urena/|title=Proclamado por tercera vez alcalde de Ureña|trans-title=Mayor of Ureña Proclaimed for Third Term|lang=es|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|first=Jonathan|last=Maldonado|date=29 July 2025|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Nueva Arcadia and Pedro María Ureña.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://callejero-venezuela.openalfa.com/municipio-pedro-maria-urena|title= Callejero de Municipio Pedro María Ureña, Estado Táchira |trans-title=Street map of Pedro María Ureña Municipality, Táchira State|lang=es|website=Callejero Venezuela|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>
