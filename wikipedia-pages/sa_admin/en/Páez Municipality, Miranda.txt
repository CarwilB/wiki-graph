'''Páez''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 39,097.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Río Chico, Venezuela|Río Chico]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Páez Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{Cite web| url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | title=Estado Miranda.- División político territorial, según municipios y parroquias, 2003 | format=ZIP | trans-title=Miranda State.- Political territorial division, according to municipalities and parishes, 2003 | language=es | accessdate=2008-03-22 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | archivedate=2007-12-21}}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Páez Municipality (disambiguation)|Páez Municipality]]" for independence hero [[José Antonio Páez]].

==Demographics==
The Páez Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 39,097 (up from 33,259 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|40.6|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Páez Municipality is Emilio Ruiz, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 39% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Paez | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231702/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-23 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into five [[parish]]es; [[Río Chico (parish)|Río Chico]], [[El Guapo, Miranda State|El Guapo]], Tacarigua de La Laguna, [[Paparo]], and San Fernando del Guapo.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
