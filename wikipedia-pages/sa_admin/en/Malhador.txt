'''Malhador''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|mɐʎɐˈdo}}, alternatively {{IPA|mɐjɐˈdo}}) is a [[municipality]] located in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Sergipe]]. It is a little city in a rural area. It is located near [[Itabaiana]]. Its population was 12,653 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|101|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8QD]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the [[Serra de Itabaiana National Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
