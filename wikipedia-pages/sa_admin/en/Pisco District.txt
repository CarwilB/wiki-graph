'''Pisco''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in middle [[Pisco Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is bordered by the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the west, the district of [[San Clemente District|San Clemente]] on the north, the [[Túpac Amaru Inca District]] on the east, and  the [[San Andrés District]]  on the south.
