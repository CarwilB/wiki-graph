'''Indaparapeo''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|25|km}} east of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Indaparapeo is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northeast Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|1900|and(-)|2900|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Zinapécuaro]] to the north, [[Queréndaro]] to the east, [[Tzitzio]] to the south, [[Charo Municipality|Charo]] to the southwest, and [[Álvaro Obregón Municipality|Álvaro Obregón]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16040.pdf |title=Indaparapeo, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|176.985|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.30% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land in Indaparapeo consists of farmland (41%), temperate forest (41%), grassland (9%), rainforest (5%), and urban areas (3%).<ref name=compendium/> About 95% of the entire municipality is located in the [[Lerma River]] basin, while the southernmost part is drained by the [[Purungueo River]], a tributary of the [[Cutzamala River]].

Indaparapeo has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|18|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1200|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In the [[Purépecha language]], Indaparapeo has been translated to mean "place of games" or "place of victory in combat"; the latter would appear to be a reference to the [[Tarascan state| Purépecha]] defeating the [[Aztecs]]. Prior to Spanish contact, Indaparapeo was inhabited by the [[Matlatzinca people]], who were allies of the Purépecha.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

Indaparapeo was one of the 61 municipalities initially created in Michoacán in 1831. Its original extent has been reduced by the creation of several municipalities from its territory, namely Queréndaro in 1921, Álvaro Obregón in 1930 and Tzitzio in 1936.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=82, 141–143 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is María Teresa Pérez Romero.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Indaparapeo recorded a population of 16,427 inhabitants living in 3791 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=040 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Indaparapeo |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=26 December 2020 |archive-date=19 August 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210819233750/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=040 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 16,990 inhabitants in Indaparapeo.<ref name=survey/>

There are 39 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which two are classified as urban:
* the municipal seat also known as Indaparapeo, which recorded a population of 6791 inhabitants in the 2010 Census; and
* San Lucas Pío, located {{convert|5|km}} east of the municipal seat on [[Mexican Federal Highway 126]], which recorded a population of 3056 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Important economic activities in Indaparapeo include agriculture and brickmaking.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite report |url=https://mapserver.sgm.gob.mx/InformesTecnicos/InventariosMinerosWeb/T1607BUGJ0006_01.PDF |title=Inventario físico de recursos minerales del municipio Indaparapeo, Michoacán |language=es |pages=19–23 |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=August 2007 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>
