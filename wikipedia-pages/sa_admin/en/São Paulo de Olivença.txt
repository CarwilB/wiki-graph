'''São Paulo de Olivença''' is a community and a municipality near the western edge of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] near the tri-country border area in [[Brazil]]. The population is 40,073 (2020 est.)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/sao-paulo-de-olivenca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> in an area of 19,746 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/235HS Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The city is served by [[São Paulo de Olivença Airport|Senadora Eunice Michiles Airport]]. This city, along with other surrounding cities, is known for their sand export for the making of cement.

==History==
It was founded in 1689 as a mission by Spanish Jesuit Samuel Fritz. The municipality of [[Santo Antônio do Içá]], located to the north, was separated from this municipality in 1955.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8CO Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística], history</ref> In 2010, the city suffered from a severe landslide, causing road damage. No one was injured or killed, but many homes were lost in the Amazon River.
