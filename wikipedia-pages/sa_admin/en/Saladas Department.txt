'''Saladas Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.cadenaderadios.com.ar/noticia/17279_plan-limn-saladas-sigue-sumando-pequeos-productores.htm|title=Plan Limón: Saladas sigue sumando pequeños productores|work=Cadena de Radios|date=12 November 2021|access-date=14 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 21,470 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|1,981|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Saladas, Corrientes|Saladas]], which is located around {{cvt|930|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
