'''Chiquitos Province''' is one of the fifteen provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz]] [[Departments of Bolivia|Department]], situated in the center of the department. Its capital is [[San José de Chiquitos]].

The province was created on January 23, 1826, during the presidency of [[marshal]] [[Antonio José de Sucre]].

It forms the so-called "[[Chiquitania|Gran Chiquitania]]" together with [[José Miguel de Velasco Province]], [[Ñuflo de Chávez Province]], [[Ángel Sandoval Province]], and [[Germán Busch Province]].

== Location ==
Chiquitos Province is located between 17° 00' and 18° 37' [[Latitude|South]] and between 58° 54' and 62° 45' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 500&nbsp;km from West to East, and up to 220&nbsp;km from North to South.

The province is situated in the Bolivian lowlands and borders [[Ñuflo de Chávez Province]] in the northwest, [[Andrés Ibáñez Province]] in the west, [[Cordillera Province (Bolivia)|Cordillera Province]] in the south, [[Germán Busch Province]] in the southeast, [[Ángel Sandoval Province]] in the east, and [[José Miguel de Velasco Province]] in the north.

== Division ==
Chiquitos Province comprises three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into cantons.

== Population ==
The population of Chiquitos Province has increased by circa 15% over the recent five years, from 59,754 inhabitants (2001 [[census]]) to 68,445 inhabitants (2005 est.).<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=August 25, 2007 }}</ref> A substantial part of the population consists of German speaking [[Russian Mennonite|Mennonites from Russia]].
