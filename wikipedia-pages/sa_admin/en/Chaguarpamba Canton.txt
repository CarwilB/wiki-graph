'''Chaguarpamba''' is a canton in the Province of [[Loja Province|Loja]] Ecuador. Its seat is [[Chaguarpamba]]. The canton is located in the north of the province and is bordered by the cantons of [[Catamayo]], Olmedo, [[Paltas Canton|Paltas]], and the province of [[El Oro Province|El Oro]].  The name is [[Quichua]] for "Valley of [[Jaguar]]s."  It occupies an area of 313.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 1050 m, with a population of 6,857.  Average temperature is between 18 and 24&nbsp;°C

Chaguarpamba is noted for its [[coffee]].
