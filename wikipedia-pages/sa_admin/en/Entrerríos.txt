'''Entrerrios''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Colombia]]n subregion of [[Northern Antioquia]], in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]]. The population was 8,820 at the 2018 census. The population, according to the 2002 census, was 8,305 inhabitants.
