'''Cota''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Central Savanna Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Cota is part of the [[metropolitan area]] of Colombian capital [[Bogotá]] which centre is {{convert|26|km|mi}} away. The urban centre of Cota is located at an altitude of {{convert|2566|m|ft}} and the municipality borders [[Chía, Cundinamarca|Chía]] in the north, [[Funza]] in the south, [[Suba (Bogotá)|Suba]], part of Bogotá in the east and [[Tenjo]] in the west.<ref name=websiteCota>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150520071418/http://www.cota-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cota]</ref>

== History ==
In the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], the [[Bogotá savanna]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in the southern [[Muisca Confederation]]. The [[Muisca rulers|ruler]] of [[Bogotá|Bacatá]] (''[[zipa]]'') controlled Cota. The Muisca called the town ''Gota'', which is either a personal name of derived from ''cota''; "curl". Cota still has a surviving Muisca population.

Modern Cota was founded on November 29, 1604 by Diego Gómez de Mena.<ref name=websiteCota/>

== Economy ==
Cota has a small economy mainly based on [[agriculture]]; [[cabbage]] and [[lettuce]].<ref name=websiteCota/>
