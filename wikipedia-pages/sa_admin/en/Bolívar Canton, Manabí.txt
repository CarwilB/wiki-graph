'''Bolívar''' is a canton located in the northeast of the province of [[Manabí Province|Manabí]], [[Ecuador]]. It borders the canton [[Pichincha Canton|Pichincha]] in the east, the cantons [[Portoviejo]] and [[Junín Canton|Junín]] in the south and the cantons [[Tosagua Canton|Tosagua]] and [[Chone Canton|Chone]] in the north, and cover an area of 524 [[km²]].
