'''Trinidad''' (Spanish for '''''Trinity''''') is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]].  It is well known for its Jesuit Mission, [[La Santísima Trinidad de Paraná|La Misión de la Santísima Trinidad de Paraná]]. In 1993, the mission was declared a [[UNESCO]] World Heritage Site, and is open to visitation by tourists.

==Geography==
Trinidad is located on Ruta 6ta, 30 kilometers north of the department's capital, [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]].  It is surrounded by the following districts:

*[[Ruins of Jesús de Tavarangue|Jesus]] and [[Hohenau, Paraguay|Hohenau]] to the north,
*[[Nueva Alborada]] and [[Hohenau, Paraguay|Hohenau]] to the east,
*[[Nueva Alborada]]  and [[Capitán Miranda]] to the south,
*[[Capitán Miranda]] to the west.

==Population==
According to the 2002 census, by the Dirección General de Estadísticas, Encuestas y Censos, the district had a total of 6,873 inhabitants.  35% (2,417) percent living in the center of the district, and 65% (4,456 ) in rural areas.
