'''Rengo''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[Zona Central, Chile|Zona Central]] of [[Chile]], situated in the [[Cachapoal Province]] of the [[O'Higgins Region]] at a distance of {{convert|28|km|0|abbr=on}} south of the city of [[Rancagua]] and {{convert|114|km|abbr=on}} south of the national capital [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]]. It was named after the courageous [[Toqui]] Rengo for his particular bravery at the [[Battle of Lagunillas]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Rengo spans an area of {{convert|591.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 50,830 inhabitants (25,311 men and 25,519 women). Of these, 37,075 (72.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 13,755 (27.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 16.5% (7,213 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Rengo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Rengo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Felipe Letelier ([[Partido por la Democracia|PPD]]) and Ricardo Rincón  ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 33rd electoral district, together with [[Mostazal]], [[Graneros]], [[Codegua]], [[Machalí]], [[Requínoa]], [[El Olivar, Chile|Olivar]], [[Doñihue]], [[Coínco|Coinco]], [[Coltauco]], [[Quinta de Tilcoco]] and [[Malloa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alejandro Garcia-Huidobro (UDI) and Juan Pablo Letelier Morel ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) as part of the 9th senatorial constituency (O'Higgins Region).
