'''Atalaia''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˌataˈlajɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 47,365 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/atalaia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{convert|532|sqkm|abbr=on}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=11 June 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=11 June 2008 }}</ref>
