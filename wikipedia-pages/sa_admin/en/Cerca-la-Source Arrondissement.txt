'''Cerca-la-Source''' ({{langx|ht|Sèka Lasous}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] (district) in the [[Centre (department)|Centre]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 119,756 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|archive-date=2015-11-06|url-status=dead|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Cerca-la-Source Arrondissement start with the number 54.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Cerca-la-Source]]
* [[Thomassique]]
