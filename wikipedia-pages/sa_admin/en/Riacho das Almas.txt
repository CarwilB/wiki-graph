'''Riacho das Almas''' (population 20,646) is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has 313.99 sq/km of total area.

==Geography==
[[File:Riacho-das-Almas-vista-geral-2.jpg|thumb|right|200px|City overview after the rainy season]]
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Surubim]] and [[Frei Miguelinho]]   ('''N''');  [[Caruaru]]   ('''S''' and '''W''');   [[Bezerros]] and [[Cumaru]]  ('''E''').
* Area - 313.99&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 407 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca]] rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hipoxerófila]]
* Annual average temperature - 23.6 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 152&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Riacho das Almas''' are the [[textile]] industry and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[pineapple]]s and [[manioc]].
