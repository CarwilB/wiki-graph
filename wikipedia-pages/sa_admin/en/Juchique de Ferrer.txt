'''Juchique de Ferrer''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the central zone of the state, about 259.10&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 934.20 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|50|N|96|42|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Juchique de Ferrer is delimited to the north by [[Colipa]] to the east by [[Vega de Alatorre]],  to the south-east by [[Alto Lucero]], to the south by [[Tepetlán]], to the north-west by [[Yecuatla]] and to the south-west by [[Chiconquiaco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30095a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Juchique de Ferrer|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070331093240/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30095a.htm|archive-date=2007-03-31}}</ref>

The weather in Juchique de Ferrer  is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[coffee]] and [[green chile]].
==Culture==
In Juchique de Ferrer, in March takes place the celebration in honor to San José Patron of the town.
