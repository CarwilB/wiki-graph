'''Tizayuca''' is one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central [[Mexico]]. The '''city of Tizayuca''' is the municipal seat. The population of the city is 60,265<ref>{{cite web|title=Tizayuca Population data |url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tizayuca/|work=|publisher=PueblosAmerica.com |accessdate=January 22, 2021}}</ref> and the municipality has 168,302 inhabitants.<ref name="Data México">{{cite web|title=Tizayuca|url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tizayuca|work=Data México|access-date=January 31, 2025}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 76.81&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

It is the only municipality in the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]] that is officially part of the [[Mexico City Metropolitan Area]], while the metro area of [[Pachuca]] surrounds the north and west limits.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 56,573,<ref>{{cite web|title=Tizayuca |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13069a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref> but this has grown rapidly to reach 97,461 at the 2010 Census and 168,302 at the 2020 Census.<ref name="Data México"/>

==History==
Tizayuca was originally a [[Pre-Columbian era|Pre-Columbian]] [[Nahua people|Nahua]] settlement in the [[Valley of Mexico]].
