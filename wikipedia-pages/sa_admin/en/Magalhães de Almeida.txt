'''Magalhães de Almeida''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Maranhão]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]].

==History==

Around 1855, there was a great flood on [[Parnaíba River]], and Barnabé Pereira Mascarenhas went down the river with an [[igara (canoe)|igara]] looking for new lands to live in. He found a hill on the left side of the river and built his new home there.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web |title=História de Magalhães de Almeida |language=pt-BR |website=[[IBGE]] |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ma/magalhaes-de-almeida/historico |access-date=4 January 2026 |archive-date=4 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260104162153/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ma/magalhaes-de-almeida/historico |url-status=live}}</ref>

Returning to his old settlement, people asked Barnabé where he had stablished himself, and he answered he made a "hole" (furo in Portuguese) nearby. The region, thus, was known as Furo.<ref name="ibge"/>

In 1885, Antônio da Silva Lopes, Militão Pereira Mascarenhas and Florindo José da Silva tried to colonize the region, but failed. Furo only developed in 1918, when Benedito Romão de Sousa, Manoel Vasconcelos Leão, Vítor Gonçalves Costa and others moved to the region and built infrastructure, including a church. The region then was renamed Porto de Santo Antônio, as a homage to [[Anthony of Padua]].<ref name="ibge"/> The region was predominantly occupied by fishermen.<ref name="enciclo">{{cite book |last=Silva |first=Rafael Thalysson Costa |title=Enciclopédia dos municípios maranhenses: Região de desenvolvimento do delta das américas |volume=10 |language=pt-BR |publisher=Secretaria de Estado do Planejamento e Orçamento and Instituto Maranhense de Estudos Socioeconômicos e Cartográficos |year=2024 |chapter=Magalhães de Almeida |pages=81-106 |url=https://imesc.ma.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Regiao-de-Desenvolvimento-do-Delta-das-Americas-v102024.pdf}}</ref>

In April 1925, the region was elevated to the status of village by the Governor José Maria Magalhães de Almeida. In 1936, it became the district of Trincheiras, from [[São Bernardo, Maranhão|São Bernardo]]. During the term of the Governor  Sebastião Archer da Silva, it became a municipality through the Law nº 771, from 1 October 1952, that came into effect on 1 January 1953.<ref name="ibge"/><ref name="camara">{{cite web |title=História do Município |language=pt-BR |website=Câmara Municipal de Magalhães de Almeida |url=https://camarademagalhaesdealmeida.ma.gov.br/historia-do-municipio/ |access-date=5 January 2026 |archive-date=5 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260105015714/https://camarademagalhaesdealmeida.ma.gov.br/historia-do-municipio/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

From 1996 on, with the election of João Cândido de Carvalho Neto as mayor, the municipality was predominantly governed by the Carvalho family.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Gomes |first1=Gilberto Wagner Bezerra |last2=Costa |first2=José Orlando |title=Magalhães de Almeida comemora em 1° de outubro 69 anos de sua elevação à cidade. |date=27 September 2021 |language=pt-BR |website=Correio do Norte |url=https://www.portalcorreiodonorte.com.br/magalhaes-de-almeida-comemora-em-1-de-outubro-69-anos-de-sua-elevacao-a-cidade/ |access-date=5 January 2026 |archive-date=5 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260105052518/https://www.portalcorreiodonorte.com.br/magalhaes-de-almeida-comemora-em-1-de-outubro-69-anos-de-sua-elevacao-a-cidade/ |url-status=live}}</ref>

==Geography==

Magalhães de Almeida is located on the Mesoregion of Leste Maranhense and the Microregion of Baixo Parnaíba Maranhense.<ref name="projeto">{{cite journal |last1=Gomes |first1=Érico Rodrigues |last2=Nunes |first2=Ossian Otávio |last3=Filho |first3=José Barbosa Lopes |title=Relatório diagnóstico do município de Magalhães de Almeida |journal=Report |publisher=Project for Registering Underwater Ground Sources |language=pt-BR |url=https://rigeo.sgb.gov.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/060eb12f-bc27-4d96-bfdc-3c2dcafaf3ec/content}}</ref> It's predominant biomes are [[Cerrado]] (77%) and [[Caatinga]] (23%).<ref>{{cite web |title=Magalhães de Almeida – MA |language=pt-BR |website=Infosanbas |url=https://infosanbas.org.br/municipio/magalhaes-de-almeida-ma/ |access-date=7 January 2026 |archive-date=7 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260107205943/https://infosanbas.org.br/municipio/magalhaes-de-almeida-ma/ |url-status=live}}</ref> The municipality has a tropical sub-humid dry climate, with two seasons: rainy (from January to June) and dry (from July to December).<ref name="projeto"/> The [[vegetation]] is predominantly made of [[savanna]] and [[seasonal tropical forest]].<ref name="enciclo"/>

===Hidrography===

Magalhães de Almeida is bathed by the [[Parnaíba River|Parnaíba]] and [[Buriti River (Maranhão)|Buriti River]]s.<ref name="enciclo"/> Both rivers are connected through the Buriti Lagoon.<ref>{{cite web |last=Santos |first=Ivanildo |title=Inclusão social e perservação da Lagoa do Bacuri são estimuladas através da pesquisa com foco ambiental |date=27 August 2013 |language=pt-BR |website=FAPEMA |url=https://www.fapema.br/inclusao-social-e-preservacao-da-lagoa-do-bacuri-sao-estimuladas-atraves-de-pesquisa-com-foco-ambiental/ |access-date=10 January 2026 |archive-date=10 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260110224506/https://www.fapema.br/inclusao-social-e-preservacao-da-lagoa-do-bacuri-sao-estimuladas-atraves-de-pesquisa-com-foco-ambiental/ |url-status=live}}</ref> The [[underground water]] comes from the porous aquifer of Grupo Barreiras and unconsolidated sediments from [[swamp]]s and [[mangrove]] deposits, continental wind deposits and [[alluvial]] deposits.<ref name="projeto"/>

[[Flood]]s are common during the rainy season, isolating some villages and difficulting access to some places, including touristic spots.<ref name="turismo"/>

===Geology===

Magalhães de Almeida is constituted of [[Paleogene]] detritolateritic layers, [[Pleistocene]] [[Aeolian processes|Aeolian layer]]s, and [[alluvial deposit]]s from [[Holocene]].<ref name="enciclo"/>

It's [[geomorphology]] is made of [[river plain]]s, [[plateau]]s from Parnaíba River, and [[Sub-littoral|sub-littoral]] [[tableland]]s. The [[soil]] is made out of [[latosol]]s, neosols and [[plinthosol]]s.<ref name="enciclo"/>

==Demographics==

===Population===

According to [[IBGE]], the population in 2022 was of 13,807,<ref name="ibge2"/> a decline of 21.49% since the last [[census]] in 2010.<ref>{{cite news |title=População de Magalhães de Almeida (MA) é de 13.807 pessoas, aponta o Censo do IBGE |language=pt-BR |date=28 June 2023 |website=[[G1 MA]] |url=https://g1.globo.com/ma/maranhao/noticia/2023/06/28/populacao-de-magalhaes-de-almeida-ma-e-de-13-807-pessoas-aponta-o-censo-do-ibge.ghtml |access-date=17 January 2026 |archive-date=17 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260117172034/https://g1.globo.com/ma/maranhao/noticia/2023/06/28/populacao-de-magalhaes-de-almeida-ma-e-de-13-807-pessoas-aponta-o-censo-do-ibge.ghtml |url-status=live}}</ref>

===Public safety===

During the 80s, [[public safety]] on Magalhães de Almeida and other 28 municipalities was the responsibility of the 3rd. Company (CIA) of the Military Police from the 2nd. Military Police Battalion, headquartered in [[Chapadinha]]. It was made out of one captain, one sergeant, one 3rd. corporal and 15 soldiers.<ref name="safety">{{cite journal |last=Nascimento |first=Suzanne Deyna Ribeiro |title=Redimensionamento da área do 16º Batalhão de Polícia Militar e criação de uma nova unidade policial militar |language=pt-BR |journal=Final Thesis for Specialization in Public Safety Management |publisher=[[Federal University of Maranhão]] |year=2023 |url=https://rosario.ufma.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/7255/1/TCC_SUZANNE_NASCIMENTO.pdf}}</ref>

Since the State Law nº 4,716, from 17 April 1986, CIA became independent, being renamed to  4th. Company of Independent Military Police (CI). CI was responsible for 28 municipalities, and added 112 soldiers in 1993.<ref name="safety"/>

CI was extinguished and substituted by the 6th Battalion of Military Police through the State Law nº 10,155, from 29 October 2014, responsible for 17 municipalities. Four of the municipalities were passed to another battalion through the State Law nº 11,813, from 24 August 2022. Magalhães de Almeida, [[Santana do Maranhão]] and [[São Bernardo, Maranhão|São Bernardo]] are the resonsibility of the 2nd. CIA.<ref name="safety"/>

In 2023, the military police had a base rented by the prefecture, where 9 policemen were allocated. The patrols were mostly made with motorcycles.<ref name="safety"/>

==Urban infrastructure==

===Roads===

Magalhães de Almeida can be reached from MA 110 Road. Its main Avenues are Bendito Romão de Sousa and Getúlio Vargas.<ref name="enciclo"/> According to a 2021 study by the [[Federal University of Maranhão]], the roads are full of holes.<ref name="turismo">{{cite journal |last1=Colasante |first1=Tatiana |last2=Silva |first2=Elizama Portugal da |title=Potencialidades para o desenvolvimento do turismo em Magalhães de Almeida - MA |language=pt-BR |journal=Revista Acadêmica Observatório de Inovação do Turismo |publisher=[[Federal University of Maranhão]] |volume=15 |issue=2 |pages=68–90 |year=2021 |doi=10.17648/raoit.v15n2.6291|doi-access=free }}</ref>

==Economy==

According to IBGE, the [[GDP per capita]] from Magalhães de Almeida was of R$ 13,150.33 (2023) with an [[HDI]] of 0.567 (2010).<ref name="ibge2">{{cite web |title=Panorama de Magalhães de Almeida (2023) |website=[[IBGE]] |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ma/magalhaes-de-almeida/panorama |access-date=17 January 2026 |archive-date=7 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260107221523/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ma/magalhaes-de-almeida/panorama |url-status=live}}</ref> In 2020, the biggest economic sector from the municipality was services, followed by agriculture.<ref name="enciclo"/>

In 2021, the average earning of the municipality was of 1.5 [[minimum wage]]s, with 58.1% of the population earning up until half a minimum wage. The total amount of 1,843 people had formal jobs.<ref name="ibge2"/>

There has been incidences of [[work analogous to slavery]] in the municipality. Between 13 and 27 August 2025, tax auditors rescued 76 people working with [[Copernicia prunifera|carnauba]] straw extraction after an operation from the [[Federal Police of Brazil|Federal Police]] with the [[Public Prosecutor's Office (Brazil)|Public Prosecutor's Office]] and the Federal Public Defender's Office.<ref>{{cite news |title=Auditores-fiscais resgatam 80 trabalhadores em condições análogas à escravidão em duas cidades do MA |date=27 August 2025 |website=[[G1 MA]] |url=https://g1.globo.com/ma/maranhao/noticia/2025/08/27/auditores-fiscais-resgatam-80-trabalhadores-em-condicoes-analogas-a-escravidao-em-duas-cidades-do-ma.ghtml |access-date=17 January 2026 |archive-date=17 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260117165015/https://g1.globo.com/ma/maranhao/noticia/2025/08/27/auditores-fiscais-resgatam-80-trabalhadores-em-condicoes-analogas-a-escravidao-em-duas-cidades-do-ma.ghtml |url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=MTE resgata 80 trabalhadores de situação análoga à escravidão no Maranhão |language=pt-BR |date=28 August 2025 |website=[[Ministry of Labour and Employment (Brazil)|Ministry of Labour and Employment]] |url=https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/noticias-e-conteudo/2025/agosto/mte-resgata-80-trabalhadores-de-situacao-analoga-a-escravidao-no-maranhao |access-date=17 January 2026 |archive-date=17 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260117165834/https://www.gov.br/trabalho-e-emprego/pt-br/noticias-e-conteudo/2025/agosto/mte-resgata-80-trabalhadores-de-situacao-analoga-a-escravidao-no-maranhao |url-status=live}}</ref>

===Agriculture===

Most of the agricultural activity in Magalhães de Almeida is for [[subsistence agriculture|subsistence]], with [[cassava]] being the main product. It is mainly harvested by Trincheiras village and used to produce [[cassava flour|flour]] and sold to [[Ambev]].<ref name="enciclo"/> The municipality is also part of the "[[New Agricultural Frontier]]", regions in Maranhão that are prone to [[mechanized agriculture]].<ref>{{cite book |last1=Terra |first1=Aldemir |last2=Folmer |first2=Ivanio |title=Geografia Rural e Agrária - Reflexões e Práticas |year=2025 |language=pt-BR |publisher=Arco Editores |page=140 |isbn=978-65-5417-560-9 |url=https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Ademir-Terra/publication/396962618_O_PARQUE_COMO_CAMPO_DE_DUNAS_CONFLITOS_E_RESISTENCIAS_DOZE_ANOS_DE_PESQUISAS_NOS_LENCOIS_MARANHENSES/links/695463aa0c98040d4824fe9f/O-PARQUE-COMO-CAMPO-DE-DUNAS-CONFLITOS-E-RESISTENCIAS-DOZE-ANOS-DE-PESQUISAS-NOS-LENCOIS-MARANHENSES.pdf}}</ref> It is used to plant [[Soybean|soybean]] and [[millet]] for [[exportation]] purposes.<ref name="enciclo"/>

Temporary plantations are used to produce cassava, soy, millet, [[bean]]s, [[rice]], [[watermelon]] and [[corn]]. Permanent plantations are used to produce [[papaya]], [[banana]] [[Bahia coconut]], [[cashew nut]]s and [[mango]]. Horticulture is also present on the municipality, with the predominance of [[chives]] and [[coriander]]. In 2024, there were 99 permanent plantations and 676 temporary ones, occupying 615 [[hectare|ha]].<ref name="enciclo"/>

==Government==

===Districts===

Magalhães de Almeida is subdivided in two districts, Magalhães de Lima and Custódio Lima.<ref name="camara"/> Besides the city of Magalhães de Almeida, the municipality is also home of the villages of Alto do Cedro, Aninga, Bacuri, Baixa da Salsa, Boa Vista, Curralinho, Custódio Lima, Entre Ladeiras, Férias, Florzinho, Malhadinha, Melancias, Murici dos Bragas, Nova Vila, Oitis, Santa Maria, Santo Inácio, Tourada, Trincheiras and Vargem Grande and the settlement projects of Canaã and Santo Agostinho.<ref name="enciclo"/><ref>{{cite web |title=Mapa do Município de Magalhães Almeida (MA) |language=pt-BR |date=2022 |website=[[IBGE]] |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_municipais/colecao_de_mapas_municipais/2022/MA/magalhaes_de_almeida/A0_2106300_MM.pdf |access-date=5 January 2026 |archive-date=5 January 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260105020850/https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_municipais/colecao_de_mapas_municipais/2022/MA/magalhaes_de_almeida/A0_2106300_MM.pdf |url-status=live}}</ref>
