'''Lindolfo Collor''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].  It is named after the [[Ministry of Labor and Employment (Brazil)|Minister of Labor]] during the [[Estado Novo (Brazil)|Estado Novo]]. The population is 6,125 (2020 est.) in an area of 32.99&nbsp;km².<ref>[https://archive.today/20150523162649/http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1K0S Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>

==History==
Lindolfo Collor was first colonized by [[German Brazilian|German immigrants]] to Brazil.  It was originally known as "Picada Capivara," a reference to the numerous [[capybara]]s in the region.  When the town was incorporated the name "Lindolfo Collor" was adopted to honor the former Minister of Labor [[Lindolfo Leopoldo Boeckel Collor]], who served in the government of [[Getúlio Vargas]].

==Health==
A study by the Instituto de Pesquisa Econômica Aplicada (Ipea), released in June 2008, named Lindolfo Collor as the second best city in Brazil in terms of public health.  The study compared mortality rates and life expectancy between 1991 and 2000.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://zerohora.clicrbs.com.br/zerohora/jsp/default2.jsp?uf=1&local=1&source=a1990478.xml&template=3898.dwt&edition=10112&section=67 |title=Reportagem: Saúde de Lindolfo Collor entre as 10 melhores do Brasil|access-date=22 June 2008 |author=Zero Hora }}</ref>
