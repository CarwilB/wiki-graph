'''El Santuario''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the subregion of [[Eastern Antioquia]].  El Santuario was founded on 11 March 1765 by Captain Antonio Gómez de Castro. Its elevation is 2,150 meters above sea level, with an average temperature of 17&nbsp;°C. The distance reference from [[Medellín]] city, the capital of [[Antioquia Department]], is 57&nbsp;km and it has a total area of 83.45&nbsp;km². This town is well known for being the place where comedian Guillermo Zuluaga "Montecristo" and actor Crisanto Alonso Vargas "Vargasvil" were born.  The more significant source of its economy is agriculture, mainly vegetables, beans, potatoes and legume cultivation.
