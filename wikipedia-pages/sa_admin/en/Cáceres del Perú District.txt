'''Cáceres del Perú District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Santa Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

The province was named after the Peruvian president [[Andrés Avelino Cáceres]].
