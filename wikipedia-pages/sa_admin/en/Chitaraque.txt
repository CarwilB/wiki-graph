'''Chitaraque''' is a town and municipality in the [[Ricaurte Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is located at a distance of {{convert|104.6|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] and situated at an altitude of {{convert|1575|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. It borders [[Gámbita]], [[Santander Department|Santander]] in the east and south, [[San José de Pare]], [[Santana, Boyacá|Santana]] and [[Togüí]] in the west and [[Suaita]], Santander in the north.<ref name=websiteChitaraque>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150603091330/http://www.chitaraque-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chitaraque]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Chitaraque comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Our vigorous farmfields from before".<ref name=websiteChitaraque/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Chitaraque] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Chitaraque was inhabited by the Poasaque tribe, belonging to the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. It was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]].

Modern Chitaraque was founded on January 1, 1621.<ref name=websiteChitaraque/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Chitaraque are [[agriculture]] ([[panela]] production, [[coffee]], [[banana]]s and [[yuca]]) and milk farming.<ref name=websiteChitaraque/>
