'''Limón'''<ref name="2001 census">{{cite web | url = http://ccp.ucr.ac.cr/bvp/censos/honduras/2001/index.htm | title = XVI Censo Nacional de Población y V de Habitación Honduras 2001 | publisher = El Centro Centroamericano de Población (CCP) | language = Spanish | access-date = 2010-03-25 | archive-date = 2012-02-23 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20120223233033/http://ccp.ucr.ac.cr/bvp/censos/honduras/2001/index.htm | url-status = dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url = http://www.monografias.com/trabajos52/municipios-honduras/municipios-honduras8.shtml#colon | title = Antecedentes históricos de los departamentos y municipios de Honduras: Departamento de Colón | publisher = Monografías.com| language = Spanish}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | url = https://www.angelfire.com/ca5/mas/dpmapas/col/limon/limon.html | title = Municipio de Limón| language = Spanish}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Colón Department (Honduras)|Colón]] in [[Honduras]].

According to official census of 2001, has a population of 8,627 inhabitants.<ref name="2001 census" /> Its territory of {{convert|643.4|km²}} is divided into an ''[[wikt:aldea|aldea]]'' (village) and 23 ''[[wikt:caserío|caseríos]]'' (hamlets). Limón is bordered to the north by the [[Caribbean Sea]], to the south and east by the municipality of [[Iriona]], and to the west by the municipalities of [[Trujillo, Honduras|Trujillo]] and [[Santa Rosa de Aguan]].<ref>[https://www.google.com/maps/@15.8662816,-85.502272,8948m/data=!3m1!1e3 Google Maps - Limón]</ref><ref>[https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=14/15.8660/-85.5022 OpenStreetMap - Limón]</ref>
