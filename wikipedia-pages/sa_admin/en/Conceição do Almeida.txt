'''Conceição do Almeida''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] in the state of [[Bahia]]. Its estimated population as of 2020 was 17,165 inhabitants.
<br>
Conceição do Almeida was organized on 18 July 1890, Its territory used to belong to São Felipe.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://conceicaodoalmeida.ba.gov.br/|title=Prefeitura Municipal de Conceição do Almeida – BA – Imprensa Oficial com Certificação Digital ICP-BRASIL|language=pt-BR|access-date=2019-06-28}}</ref>

The anthem was written by the poet Castro Alves.
