'''Quilmes''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]], Argentina, within the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] [[conurbation]].

It has an area of {{convert|125|sqkm|abbr=out}}, and a population of 580,829 ({{census-ar|2010}}),<ref name=Cen2010 /> making it the third-most populous partido in the Gran Buenos Aires. Named after the [[Quilmes (tribe)|Quilmes Tribe]], its capital is the city of [[Quilmes]]. 

[[File:Municipalidad del Partido de Quilmes (Buenos Aires).jpg|thumb|left|Quilmes Municipal Building (town hall)]]

==Beer==
<!-- Image with inadequate rationale removed: [[File:Cerveza-Quimes-Label-P72300.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Cerveza Quilmes]]'s label.]] -->

Quilmes gives its name to one of Argentina's beers, [[Cerveza Quilmes]], which was originally brewed in the area.

==Sport==
Quilmes is home to two football clubs, [[Quilmes Atlético Club]] of the [[Primera Division Argentina|Primera Division]] and [[Club Atlético Argentino de Quilmes]] of the regionalised [[Primera D Metropolitana|5th Division]]. The city also stands out in many other sports, including [[field hockey]], basketball and [[rugby football|rugby]], among others.

==Christianity==
In 1666 was established the Cathedral of Quilmes (Catedral de Quilmes in Spanish). In 1976 a papal bull created the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Quilmes|Diócesis de Quilmes]]. The first minister was Padre Obispo [[Jorge Novak]], famous for his defense of Human Rights during the [[Proceso de Reorganización Nacional]].

The first [[Rivers of Life]] Church ("[[Ríos de Vida]]" in Spanish) was established in Quilmes in 1967. It is a group of over 80 [[Evangelicalism|evangelical]] churches around the world. [[Rivers of Life]], Quilmes has the largest congregation of all the Rivers of Life churches around the world.

Other notable evangelical churches include the Iglesia del Encuentro and the Iglesia del Puente.
