'''Capitão Enéas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  As of 2020 the population was 15,313 in a total area of 970&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/capitao-eneas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  

Capitão Enéas belongs to the IBGE statistical microregion of [[Montes Claros]].  The distance to this important regional center is 90&nbsp;km.  The municipal seat is located on the railway line that links Minas Gerais to [[Bahia]].  

The main economic activity is cattle raising.  In 2006 there were 68,000 head of cattle.  Important agricultural crops are sugarcane, beans, and corn.  There are several small transformation industries.  There was 1 financial institution in 2007.  In 2007 there were 299 automobiles, giving a ratio of 47 inhabitants for each automobile.
