'''Capital Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]], in which the [[Córdoba, Argentina|provincial capital]] is located. Its boundaries create a square slightly off-center of the province. It is the only provincial department not sub-divided into districts, and while officially the city of Córdoba is the only municipality within the departmental limits, parts of other municipalities reach into it. 

The department has a population of about 1,5 million inhabitants in an area of  562&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its capital city is [[Córdoba, Argentina|Córdoba]], which is also the capital city of Córdoba Province. It is located around 710&nbsp;km from [[Buenos Aires]].

== Location ==
The department is surrounded by the departments of [[Colón Department, Córdoba|Colón]] to the north, and [[Santa María Department, Córdoba|Santa María]] to the south. In its laws, the province defines the limits through a series of straight lines on latitudes and longitudes.<ref>{{Cite web |title=PODER JUDICIAL DE CORDOBA - ARGENTINA |url=http://www1.justiciacordoba.gov.ar/site/Asp/MapaJudDepartamentos.asp |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100510020738/http://www1.justiciacordoba.gov.ar/site/Asp/MapaJudDepartamentos.asp |archive-date=2010-05-10 |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=www1.justiciacordoba.gov.ar}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=CARACTERÍSTICAS FÍSICAS Y POLÍTICAS |url=http://www.cordoba.gov.ar/cordobaciudad/principal2/Docs/observatorio/guia07/CAPITULO1_07.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140108020613/http://www.cordoba.gov.ar/cordobaciudad/principal2/Docs/observatorio/guia07/CAPITULO1_07.pdf |archive-date=2014-01-08 |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=www.cordoba.gov.ar}}</ref> 

Due to the square departmental limits, some of the neighboring towns and cities reach cross the departmental lines. This includes Estacion Juárez Celman, Saldán and [[Villa Allende]] in the north, which all are located in the [[Colón Department, Córdoba|Department Colón]].<ref name=":0" />

== Population ==
According to the 2022 census numbers of [[INDEC]], the department has a population of 1.505.250, who live in around half a million households. Between 2010 and 2022, the population grew by 13 %.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2022 INDEC census data |url=https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c1_6.xlsx |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20260528021059/https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c1_6.xlsx |archive-date=2026-05-28 |access-date=2026-05-30 |website=censo.gob.ar}}</ref>

Most of the population lives in a dense urban zone, with 98 % of households having access to the local water grid<ref>{{Cite web |title=Provincia de Córdoba. Total de hogares, por procedencia del agua, según provisión del agua, por departamento.
