'''Chikindzonot  Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “cenote in the west”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing  352.56&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 170&nbsp;km southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
It is unknown which chieftainship the area was under prior to the arrival of the Spanish. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system which lasted until the end of the colonial era. Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the Valladolid region.<ref name="municipios"/>

During the [[Caste War of Yucatán]], the village was sacked by the rebel indigenous people and the 300 white inhabitants fled. Colonel Eulogio Rosado y Estevéz established a headquarters in 1848, 25 miles away in the town of Ichmul, to try to recover Chikindzonot. In 1849, Lt. Col. Pablo A. González y Osorio was able to retake the town, and thereafter it slowly repopulated.<ref name=municipios />

It was designated as its own municipality in 1957.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has seven councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public security, public sanitation and monuments.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Chikindzonot|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=8 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Chikindzonot]], Yucatán. ==Local festivals==
Every year from the 1 to the 18 February the town holds a traditional village festival and in October, a celebration for its patron saint, St. Francis of Assisi.<ref name=municipios />
