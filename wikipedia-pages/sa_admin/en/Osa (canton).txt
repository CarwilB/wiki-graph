'''Osa''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Puntarenas Province|Puntarenas]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Puerto Cortés District|Puerto Cortés]] district.

== History ==
Osa was created on 29 July, 1940 by decree 185.<ref name="crevoterritorial" /> In pre-[[Pre-Columbian era|Columbia]]n times, Osa was populated by [[Boruca]] which left legacies such as fields and stone walkways.

== Geography ==
Osa has an area of {{formatnum:1930.24}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:24}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The canton begins at the Barú River near [[Dominical, Puntarenas|Dominical]] on the central Pacific coast. It continues as a narrow strip of land southward to include the area around [[Palmar District|Palmar]] and [[Sierpe District|Sierpe]], finally widening to take in the entire neck and upper portion of the Osa Peninsula, where significant gold mining operations are located. As its name suggests, [[humpback whale]]s and [[dolphin]]s migrate into the waters to calve, mate, and to rest. [[Whale watching]] is available along the coasts. [[Isla del Caño]] is part of Osa canton.

==Conservation and tourism==
In the canton there are conservation areas that are part of the [[Osa Conservation Area]], such as [[Corcovado National Park]], [[Ballena Marine National Park]] and [[Piedras Blancas National Park]]. The [[stone spheres of Costa Rica]] are found in this canton.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Spheres Festival |url=https://si.cultura.cr/es/expresiones-y-manifestaciones/festival-de-las-esferas |access-date=2025-09-10 |website=si.cultura.cr}}</ref>
