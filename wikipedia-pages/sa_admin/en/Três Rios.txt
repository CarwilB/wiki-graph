'''Três Rios''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈtɾe(j)z ˈʁi.us}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. As of 2020 its population was an estimated 82,142 inhabitants, and its area is around {{Convert|322|km2|sqmi}}.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Três Rios (RJ) {{!}} Cities and States {{!}} IBGE|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/en/cities-and-states/rj/tres-rios.html|access-date=2021-04-28|website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The current [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] of Três Rios is Joacir Barbaglio Pereira (known as Joa) of the [[Liberal Party (Brazil, 2006)|Liberal Party]], elected in [[2020 Brazilian municipal elections|2020]] to a four-year term.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Três Rios escolhe Joa, do PL, como novo prefeito|url=https://g1.globo.com/rj/sul-do-rio-costa-verde/noticia/2020/11/16/joa-do-pl-e-eleito-prefeito-de-tres-rios.ghtml|access-date=2021-04-28|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-01-12|title=Prefeito - Três Rios|url=https://tresrios.rj.gov.br/index.php/prefeito/|access-date=2021-04-28|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The name Três Rios, meaning "three rivers" in Portuguese, is a reference to the three important rivers in the area:<ref>{{Cite web|last=Teixeira|first=Ezilma Maria|date=2021-01-02|title=Nossa História - Três Rios|url=https://tresrios.rj.gov.br/index.php/nossa-cidade/|access-date=2021-03-25|language=pt-BR}}</ref> the [[Paraíba do Sul]], the [[Piabanha River|Piabanha]], and the [[Paraibuna River (Minas Gerais)|Paraibuna]]. The city itself is located on the Paraíba, and the three rivers meet downstream, to the east. The name "''Esquina do Brasil''" ({{Literal translation|street corner of Brazil}}) was inspired by the words of former president [[Juscelino Kubitschek]],<ref>{{Cite web|title=80º aniversário de Três Rios e tradição de seu carnaval inspiram "Esquina do Brasil" - Entre Rios Jornal|url=https://www.entreriosjornal.com.br/noticia-80-aniversario-de-tres-rios-e-tradicao-de-seu-carnaval-inspiram-esquina-do-brasil-67539|access-date=2021-04-28|website=www.entreriosjornal.com.br}}</ref> referring to the major highways that pass through.

Três Rios hosts a campus of the [[Federal Rural University of Rio de Janeiro]] (UFRRJ), called the ''Instituto Três Rios'', which offers four undergraduate programs.<ref>{{Cite web|title=O Instituto|url=https://itr.ufrrj.br/portal/institucional/instituto/|access-date=2021-04-28|website=Campus Três Rios {{!}} UFRRJ|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

==Sports==
There are three sport clubs in the city. ''[[América Futebol Clube (TR)|América]]'', ''[[Entrerriense Futebol Clube|Entrerriense]]'' and ''Três Rios Futebol Clube''. América's [[association football|football]] section is currently closed, but the club still disputes [[basketball]] competitions. Entrerriense and Três Rios FC's football sections are currently active. Três Rios is currently disputing [[Campeonato Carioca (lower levels)|Campeonato Carioca Third Level]].
