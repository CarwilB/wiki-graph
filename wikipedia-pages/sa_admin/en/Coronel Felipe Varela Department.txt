'''Coronel Felipe Varela''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of the province of [[La Rioja Province, Argentina|La Rioja]] ([[Argentina]]).<ref name=":cp">{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-admin.php |title=Argentina: Administrative Division |website=City Population |access-date=2025-12-19}}</ref><ref>{{cite book |url=http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645 |title=Centro Latinoamericano y Caribeño de Demografía (CELADE) |publisher=World Bank |year=1970 |language=es |access-date=2025-12-19}}</ref> Its chief town is [[Villa Unión]].

The department is named after Argentine [[caudillo]] [[:es:Felipe Varela|Felipe Varela]].<ref>{{cite news |title=La Rioja: El Departamento General Felipe Varela vivió una gran temporada, y ya se prepara para Semana Santa |url=https://www.zonanortehoy.com/turismo/2018/3/5/la-rioja-el-departamento-general-felipe-varela-vivio-una-gran-temporada-ya-se-prepara-para-semana-santa-60108.html |newspaper=Zona Norte Hoy |date=2018-03-05 |language=es |access-date=2025-12-19}}</ref>
