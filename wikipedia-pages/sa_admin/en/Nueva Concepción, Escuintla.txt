'''Nueva Concepción''' ({{IPA|es|ˈnweβa konseβˈsjon}}) is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|558.4|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 83,170 inhabitants.

==History==
Nueva Concepción was earlier part of the municipality of Santa Ana Mixtán, which was merged [[Tiquisate]] on 5 March 1947. The town was designed and established during the reign of  [[Carlos Castillo Armas]] in 1956, and was planned by Jacobo Arbenz Guzmán. It was one of the municipalities that was created by the government decree on 15 February 1974, and became effective from 21 May of the same year.

==Geography==
Nueva Concepción is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|558.4|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Nueva Concepción (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0513__nueva_concepci%C3%B3n/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the northern part of the department, about {{cvt|93|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|150|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Patulul]] to the north, [[La Gomera, Escuintla|La Gomera]] to the east, [[Tiquisate]] to the west, and Pacific Ocean to the south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Nueva Concepción|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Nueva Concepción municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-la-gomera-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|55.3|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> Nueva Concepción has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|25.8|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|122.13|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/escuintla/nueva-concepcion|title=Nueva Concepción|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 83,170 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 42,217 males and 40,953 females. About 29.5% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.8% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (78.4%) was classified as urban, while 21.6% lived in rural areas. About 81.8% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (98.0%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (1.7%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 83.6%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
