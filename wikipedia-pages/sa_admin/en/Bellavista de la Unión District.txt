'''Bellavista de la Unión District''' is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Sechura Province|Sechura]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Bellavista de la Unión District was created by Law January 29, 1965, in [[Fernando Belaúnde]] term.
