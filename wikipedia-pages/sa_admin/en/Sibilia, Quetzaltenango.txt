'''Sibilia''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.prensalibre.com/ciudades/sub_quetzaltenango/videos-asi-se-ven-carreteras-y-campos-en-sibilia-quetzaltenango-al-estar-totalmente-cubiertos-de-granizo/|title=Videos: así se ven carreteras y campos en Sibilia, Quetzaltenango al estar totalmente cubiertos de granizo|first=Pavel|last=Arellano|work=[[Prensa Libre (Guatemala)|Prensa Libre]]|date=10 August 2023|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It covers an area of 28 km² and sits at an elevation of 2800 [[Metre|metres]] above sea level. The town is roughly located 19km northwest of the department capital, [[Quetzaltenango]], and 126km east from the country's capital, [[Guatemala City]]. As of 2023, it has a population of 9,220. The region is known for strong [[Mestizo]] migration to areas of the U.S. like [[Los Angeles]], [[Oklahoma City]], and the South Florida cities of [[West Palm Beach]] and [[Fort Lauderdale]], where they maintain big communities of Sibilia growing.
