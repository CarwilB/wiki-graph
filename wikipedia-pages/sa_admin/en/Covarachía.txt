'''Covarachía''' is a town and municipality in the [[Northern Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is located at {{convert|208|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] at an altitude of {{convert|2320|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. The municipality borders [[San José de Miranda]] and [[Capitanejo, Santander|Capitanejo]] (both [[Santander Department|Santander]]) in the north, [[Tipacoque]] in the south, Capitanejo in the east and in the west the municipalities [[Onzaga]] and [[San Joaquín, Santander|San Joaquín]] (Santander).<ref name=websiteCovarachia>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150602083433/http://www.covarachia-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Covarachía]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Covarachía is a combination of [[Spanish language|Spanish]] and [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]; "cave of the Moon", with ''Chía'' referring to the Moon goddess [[Chía (goddess)|Chía]].<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Covarachía] - Excelsio.net</ref><ref name=websiteCovarachia/>

== History ==
Covarachía was inhabited by indigenous people during the [[Herrera Period]], and later, in the northeasternmost part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], ruled by a ''[[cacique]]''. Covarachía is bordered by the [[Chicamocha River]] and the territories to the east of the town were inhabited by the [[Lache people]]. The [[Muisca people|Muisca]] were the people who lived on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] in the 1530s.

Modern Covarachía, called Ricaurte between 1858 and 1869, was founded on February 10, 1823, by Juan Zámano and Felipe Pérez.<ref name=websiteCovarachia/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Covarachía are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Important agricultural products are [[tobacco]], [[fique]], [[pineapple]]s, [[yuca]], [[maize]], [[pea]]s, [[sugarcane]], [[tomato]]es and [[melon]]s.<ref name=websiteCovarachia/>
