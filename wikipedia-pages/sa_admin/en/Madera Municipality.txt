'''Madera''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in the Sierra Nevada mountains of northern Mexico. The municipal seat lies at [[Madera, Chihuahua|Madera]]. The municipality covers an area of 8,158.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 29,611,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Madera|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=040|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=10 January 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160110075245/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=040|url-status=dead}}</ref> down from 32,031 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Madera|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08040a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927205739/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08040a.htm|archivedate=September 27, 2007}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Madera had a population of 15,447.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Madera, the municipality had 600 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: El Largo (3,522), Nicolás Bravo (1,862), classified as urban, and Las Varas (Estación Babícora) (1,417) and Mesa del Huracán (Chihuahuita) (1,102), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The economy is primarily based on [[logging]], ''madera'' meaning wood in Spanish. There is also some agriculture and cattle ranches in the municipality.

The municipality is also known for its caves and parks and within a 50&nbsp;km radius of the town there are many caves featuring pre-Columbian artworks.

==Crime==
A ''narcofosa'' (mass grave attributed to organized crime) containing the remains of [[Femicides in Ciudad Juárez|women killed]] in 2011 and 2012 was found in Madera in December 2016.<ref>{{cite news |last=Villalpando |first=Rubén |title=La Jornada - Con Corral, 10 mil 42 asesinatos en Chihuahua; 95%, impunes |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/01/11/estados/con-corral-10-mil-42-asesinatos-en-chihuahua-95-impunes/ |access-date=January 11, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=January 11, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>
