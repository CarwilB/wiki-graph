'''Concepción''' is one of nine provinces in the [[Junín Region]] in central [[Peru]]. Its [[Capital (political)|capital]] is [[Concepción, Junín|Concepción]].

Notable sites include [[Santa Rosa de Ocopa District|"El convento de Santa Rosa de Ocopa"]], which contains a vast library and artefacts that were acquired from the first Spanish expeditions to the Peruvian [[Amazon rainforest]].
