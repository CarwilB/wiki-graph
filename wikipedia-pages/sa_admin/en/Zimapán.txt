'''Zimapán''' ([[Otomi language|Otomi]]: '''Mabo̱za''') is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern Mexico. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|860.9|km2}}.

The name Zimapán derives from the [[Nahuatl]] words "cimatl", meaning "cimate" (a root used to ferment [[pulque]]) and "pan", meaning "inside or over"; which translates to "over the cimate" or "in the cimate".<ref>{{Cite web|title=El portal único del gobierno. {{!}} gob.mx|url=https://failover.www.gob.mx/mantenimiento.html|access-date=2021-05-25|website=failover.www.gob.mx|archive-date=2011-01-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110110000632/http://www.presidencia.gob.mx/en/press/?contenido=31365|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Toponimia|url=http://p-zimapan.hidalgo.gob.mx/index.php/municipio/toponimia|access-date=2021-05-25|website=p-zimapan.hidalgo.gob.mx|archive-date=2021-05-25|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210525174304/http://p-zimapan.hidalgo.gob.mx/index.php/municipio/toponimia|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 39,927.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Zimapán (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/_/13084__zimap%C3%A1n/|access-date=2022-02-25|website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> Zimapán has a regional hospital and seven health centers. It also has 32 Casas de Salud, or health houses.<ref name=":1">{{cite web|title=Zimapan  |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13084a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 27, 2008}}</ref>

[[Los Mármoles National Park]] is located in the municipality, northeast of the town in the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]].

== History ==
The land was place for [[Otomi]] and Quinamí indigenous people, which later received influence from [[Olmecs]] and [[Nahuas]].<ref name=":0" /> In 1522, after the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Conquista]], Spanish colonalists founded the city of Zimapán. [[Augustinians|Augustinian]] friars built the first church of Zimapán in the 18th century and later they built another, which building ended in 1822.<ref name=":1" />

[[Vanadium]] was discovered by first time here in 1801 by [[Andrés Manuel del Río]].<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|last=Turismo|first=Secretaría de|title=Zimapán, Hidalgo|url=http://www.gob.mx/sectur/articulos/zimapan-hidalgo|access-date=2021-05-25|website=gob.mx|language=es}}</ref> During the [[Mexican Revolution]], General Otilio Villegas won a battle for the "[[Constitutionalists in the Mexican Revolution|Carrancistas]]" in Zimapán.<ref name=":1" />

The city was declared a "[[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]]" in 2018.<ref name=":2" />
