}}'''Playa Vicente''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the south zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 220&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 2,122.14 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|17|50|N|95|49|W|}}.

==History==
The prehispanic state of [[Guaspaltepec]] was located in the vicinity of Playa Vicente.

Playa Vicente was declared a city by decree on January 14th, 1930 and its name changed to Venustiano Carranza, after [[Venustiano Carranza|the revolutionary leader]]. The former name of Playa Vicente was reinstituted by decree on December 14th, 1937. 

==Geography==

The municipality of '''Playa Vicente''' is delimited to the north by [[José Azueta, Veracruz|José Azueta]] and [[Isla, Veracruz|Isla]], to the east by [[Juan Rodríguez Clara]] and to the south and west by [[Oaxaca]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30130a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Playa Vicente|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-12|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930015125/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30130a.htm|archive-date=2007-09-30}}</ref>  Playa Vicente is watered by the rivers La Lana and Playa Vicente.

The weather in Playa Vicente is warm  and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[orange fruit]], [[coffee]] and [[mango]].

==Culture==

The patron of the town is the Virgen de la Candelaria. A celebration in her honor takes place in February, and a celebration in honor of Virgen de Guadalupe takes place in December.
