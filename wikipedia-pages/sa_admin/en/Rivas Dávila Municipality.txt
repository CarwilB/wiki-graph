'''Rivas Dávila''' is one of the 23 municipalities of the state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 187&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 20,128 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following two parishes, with their capitals listed in parentheses:

*Bailadores ([[Bailadores]])
*Gerónimo Maldonado (La Playa)
