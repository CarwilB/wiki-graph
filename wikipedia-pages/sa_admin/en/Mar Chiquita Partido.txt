'''Mar Chiquita Partido''' is a county (''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'') located in the Atlantic coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

This provincial subdivision has a population of about 18,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|3114|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}} and its [[Administrative centre|county seat]] is [[Coronel Vidal]].

==Economy==
The economy of Mar Chiquita Partido is dominated by agriculture, cattle raising, and the summer tourist season (December-February).
