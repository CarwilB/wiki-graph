'''Federico Román''' (or: ''General Federico Román'') is one of the five provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Pando Department|Pando]] and is situated in the department's northeastern parts. Its name honours General [[Federico Román]].

== Location ==
''Federico Román'' province is located between 09° 41' and 10° 51' [[Latitude|South]] and between 65° 17' and 66° 39' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 150&nbsp;km from North to South, and 200&nbsp;km from West to East.

The province is situated in the [[Amazon Rainforest|Amazon]] lowlands of Bolivia and borders [[Abuná Province]] in the West, [[Manuripi Province]] in the Southwest, [[Beni Department]] in the Southeast, and [[Brazil]] in the East and North.

The border to Beni Department in the South forms [[Beni River|Río Beni]], to neighbouring Brazil [[Madeira River|Río Madeira]] in the East and [[Abuná River|Río Abuná]] in the North.

== Population ==
The population of Federico Román Province has increased by more than 400% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 1,516 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo/indice/poblacion92Proy.aspx?gestion=1992 Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) 1992] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140423142224/http://www.ine.gob.bo/indice/poblacion92Proy.aspx?gestion=1992 |date=April 23, 2014 }}</ref>
*2001: 2,242 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo/comunitaria/comunitariaVer.aspx?Depto=08&Prov=04&Seccion=02 Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) 2001] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304061354/http://www.ine.gob.bo/comunitaria/comunitariaVer.aspx?Depto=08&Prov=04&Seccion=02 |date=2016-03-04 }}</ref>
*2012: 7,034 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>[http://censosbolivia.ine.gob.bo/localidades/c_listado Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) 2012] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722212933/http://censosbolivia.ine.gob.bo/localidades/c_listado |date=2011-07-22 }}</ref>
 
39.6% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

87.9% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 3.1% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 1.2% [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 26.5 speak foreign languages (mainly  [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]], due to the nearness to Brazil) . ''(1992)''

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 72.6%. ''(1992)''

83.1% of the population have no access to electricity, 59.1% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

83.1% of the population are Catholics, 15.2% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
The province comprises three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipios]]:
* 09-0501 [[Nueva Esperanza Municipality]] - 2,068 inhabitants (''census 2012'')
* 09-0502 [[Villa Nueva Municipality]] - 3,275 inhabitants
* 09-0503 [[Santos Mercado Municipality]] - 1,691 inhabitants
