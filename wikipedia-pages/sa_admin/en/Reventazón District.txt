'''Reventazón''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Siquirres (canton)|Siquirres]] canton, in the [[Limón Province|Limón]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |lang=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>
== History ==
The town of Reventazón is mentioned in 1887 as "a point about 40 miles from Limon". A 50 mile-long railway line, "the Reventazon line", is due to link it to Cartago, passing through a rich countryside noted for its sugar-cane and with valuable copper mines as yet not open for the lack of even a mule track.  That line is to have 45 large bridges and 88 small ones; among those is the 600 ft-long Birriz [[viaduct]] with four spans of 150 feet each, resting on iron columns, and in some places 200 feet above the water.<ref>{{cite book |last1= Calvo |first1= Joaquiin Bernardo |year= 1890 |title= The Republic of Costa Rica |location= Chicago / New York |publisher= Rand, McNally & Company |pages= 288, 290 |url= https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/cd/The_Republic_of_Costa_Rica_%28IA_republicofcostar00calv%29.pdf }}</ref> James Livesey and Son are chief engineers for the railway.<ref>{{cite web |title= Costa Rica Railway - Birriz Viaduct |website= liveseyandson.tumblr.com |date= |url= https://liveseyandson.tumblr.com/post/73504128439/costa-rica-railway-birriz-viaduct |access-date= May 14, 2025 }}</ref>

Reventazón District was created on 16 October 2018 by ''Decreto Ejecutivo'' N°040-2018-MGP.<ref name="dta2017" />
