'''Pabellón de Arteaga''' is [[Municipalities of Aguascalientes|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Aguascalientes]]. It stands at {{coord|22|09|N|102|16|W|}} in the central part of the state. The [[Settlement classification in Mexico|city]] of [[Pabellón de Arteaga]] (2010 population: 28,633) serves as its municipal seat. The municipality had a population of 41,862 and an area of 199.33&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (76.97 sq mi). Its largest other town is named [[Emiliano Zapata, Aguascalientes|Emiliano Zapata]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 41,862.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Pabellón de Arteaga|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=006|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=April 23, 2014|archive-date=March 4, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304131347/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=006|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Pabellón de Arteaga had a population of 28,633.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Pabellón de Arteaga, the municipality had 300 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Emiliano Zapata, Aguascalientes|Emiliano Zapata]] (2,995), classified as urban, and [[Las Ánimas, Aguascalientes|Las Ánimas]] (1,794), [[Santa Isabel, Aguascalientes|Santa Isabel]] (1,065), [[Santiago, Aguascalientes|Santiago]] (1,020), and [[San Luis de Letras, Aguascalientes|San Luis de Letras]] (1,018), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
