'''Grande-Rivière-du-Nord''' ({{IPA|fr|ɡʁɑ̃d ʁivjɛʁ dy nɔʁ}}; {{langx|ht|Grann Rivyè dinò}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Grande-Rivière-du-Nord Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|Department]] of [[Haiti]]. [[Jean-Jacques Dessalines]] was born there in 1758 on the Cormiers plantation.<ref>Dayan, Joan (1995) "Haiti, History, and the Gods" pp. 66&ndash;97 ''In'' Prakash, Gyan (editor) (1995) ''After Colonialism: Imperial histories and postcolonial displacements'' Princeton University Press, Princeton, New Jersey, [https://books.google.com/books?id=UPDVd3sYLHQC&pg=PA74 page 74], {{ISBN|0-691-03742-6}}</ref>

The town was established in 1712, and named after a nearby river. In addition to being the birthplace of Dessalines, it is the hometown of [[Jean Price-Mars]], [[Jean-Baptiste Chavannes]], [[Henri Namphy|Henry Namphy]], [[Vilbrun Guillaume Sam]], [[Tirésias Simon Sam]], [[Jean-Baptiste Riché]], and [[Philippe Guerrier]]. Grande-Rivière has retained a significant amount of colonial-era architecture. 

Local landmarks include the Church of [[Rose of Lima|St. Rose of Lima]], first built in 1712, and the Dessalines monument, unveiled in 2022.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Roc |first=Rejy Joseph |date=2023-09-05 |title=Discover the birthplace of Dessalines, Grande-Rivière-du-Nord |url=https://haitiantimes.com/2023/09/05/discover-the-birthplace-of-dessalines-grande-riviere-du-nord/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20250725090754/https://haitiantimes.com/2023/09/05/discover-the-birthplace-of-dessalines-grande-riviere-du-nord/ |archive-date=2025-07-25 |access-date=2026-02-12 |work=The Haitian Times |language=en-US}}</ref> The town is a national hub of [[Cocoa bean|cocoa]] and [[Coffee bean|coffee]] production. A team of Canadian students set up a computer-equipped library in the town in 2017.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Chalifour |first=Annik |date=2018-07-14 |title=Grande-Rivière-du-Nord, une terre haïtienne de caractère |url=https://l-express.ca/grande-riviere-du-nord-une-terre-haitienne-de-caractere/ |access-date=2026-02-12 |website=l-express.ca |language=fr-CA}}</ref>
