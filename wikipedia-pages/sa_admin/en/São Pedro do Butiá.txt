'''São Pedro do Butiá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 2,949.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/sao-pedro-do-butia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Minority language==
Like many towns in the state which were first settled by [[German language|German-speaking]] Brazilians from the ''Altkolonie'' or the old colonies in the East, the regional German language is still present in daily family and community life, if not as much in the public sphere since [[World War II]] due to language repression public policies implemented by the State (i.e. [[Getúlio Vargas|Getúlio Vargas']] aggressive nationalization campaign).

Among other informal names (and often pejorative descriptions), the local dialect is gaining recognition by its formal name, [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]] (international language code: ISO 639-3 hrx), a reference to the [[Hunsrück]] region of southwest Germany, where the language has strong linguistic roots (see: [[West Middle German]] dialects).

In 2012 the state chamber of deputies voted unanimously in favor of recognizing this Germanic dialect an official historical intangible culture good to be preserved. Later, in 2014, the [[National Institute of Historic and Artistic Heritage]] (IPHAN in Portuguese), under the overview of the [[Ministry of Culture (Brazil)|Ministry of Culture of Brazil]], officially announced of a project of recognition, survey and support to the maintenance of the local language.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://e-ipol.org/comunidade-hunsriqueana-comemora-a-aprovacao-do-projeto-para-inventario-de-sua-lingua/ | publisher=IPOL - Instituto de Investigação e Desenvolvimento em Política Linguística | language=pt | title=Comunidade Hunsriqueana comemora a aprovação do Projeto para Inventário de sua Língua | date=26 November 2014 | access-date=25 April 2017}}</ref>

==Pioneer park==
São Pedro do Butiá, also known simplesmente as ''Butiá'' in Portuguese or ''Butioo''<ref>Altenhofen, Cléo Vilson (1995). Hunsrückisch in Rio Grande do Sul - Ein Beitrag zur Beschreibung einer deutschbrasilianischen Dialektvarietät im Kontakt mit dem Portugiesische (Mainzer Studien zur Sprach- und Volksforschung 21). Stuttgart: Franz Steiner Verlag, p. 173.</ref> in German, has a popular pioneer village park called ''Centro Germânico Missioneiro''<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.portaldasmissoes.com.br/site/view/id/539/centro-germanico-missioneiro-de-sao-pedro-do-butia.html | publisher=Portal das Missões | language=pt | title=Centro Germânico Missioneiro de São Pedro do Butiá | access-date=25 April 2017}}</ref> (here ''missioneiro'' is a reference to the old [[Misiones Orientales|Jesuit Missions]], established in the general area during colonial times, around three hundred years before the more modern settlement of the area). The village has many different structures, like a general store, a country school, and some relocated old homes<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.portaldasmissoes.com.br/site/view/id/1692/museu-casa-do-colono.html | publisher=Portal das Missões | title=Museu Casa do Colono | language=pt | access-date=25 April 2017}}</ref> which are all set around a large statue of [[Saint Peter]], the patron saint of this mostly catholic municipality. According to official information, the 30 meters tall statue of St. Peter<ref>[http://www.clicrbs.com.br/zerohora/jsp/default2.jsp?uf=1&local=1&source=a2326724.xml&template=3898.dwt&edition=11279&section=1003 Zeho Hora: ''São Pedro é inaugurado hoje'']</ref> is the same size as the world-famous statue of [[Christ the Redeemer (statue)|Christ the Redeemer]], found at the top of the [[Sugarloaf Mountain]] in the city of [[Rio de Janeiro]].

==Neo-Gothic architecture==
The church of São Pedro Apóstolo (St. Peter Apostle) of São Pedro do Butiá is a very good example of the [[neo-Gothic]] architecture which prevailed and characterizes most of the earlier larger religious structures in the Germanic regions in the Northwest of the state. The main church Paróquia Sagrada Família de Narazé (Sacred Family of Nazareth Church) is a much larger and a two-towered neo-Gothic construction. Located 15 kilometers away from São Pedro do Butiá, the Igreja Matriz, as it is known, was built between 1913 and 1921 and in the same general revival style.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.portaldasmissoes.com.br/site/view/id/1266/igreja-matriz-de-cerro-largo-sagrada-familia-de-na.html | publisher=Portal das Missões | language=pt | title=Igreja Matriz de Cerro Largo Sagrada Família de Nazaré | access-date=25 April 2017}}</ref>
