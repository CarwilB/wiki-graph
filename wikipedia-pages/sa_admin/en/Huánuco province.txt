== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (75.97%) learnt to speak in childhood, 23.74% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>

==Events==
On 21 December 2005, eight Peruvian policemen were killed by [[Shining Path]] terrorists.<ref>{{cite news | url = https://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/americas/4551378.stm | work=BBC News | title=Peru vows to smash 'drug rebels' | date=22 December 2005}}</ref> The policemen were involved in the government's efforts of destroying [[coca]] crops.

On 19 November 2009, Peruvian policemen reported apprehending gang members suspected of killing people in Huanuco Province for their fat and selling the fat on the black market for use in cosmetics in Europe.  Three suspects confessed to killing 5 people and told the police that the fat was worth $60,000 a gallon.  At least 60 people are reported missing this year in province of Huanuco. [https://www.nytimes.com/2009/11/20/world/americas/20peru.html?_r=2&ref=world]
