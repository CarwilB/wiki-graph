'''Olavarría Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the central region of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 104,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|7715|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Olavarría]], which is around {{convert|350|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
