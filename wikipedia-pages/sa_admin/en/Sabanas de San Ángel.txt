'''Sabanas de San Ángel''' ({{IPA|es|saˈβanas ðe san ˈaŋxel}}), Spanish for Savannas of Saint Angel, is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. Founded around 1607 with the name San Antoñito by the [[Spain|Spanish]] Colonizers as a pathway town in the route between [[La Guajira Department]] and the [[Magdalena River]]. On June 24, 1999 the municipality is created with the name of Sabanas de San Angel that segregated from the municipalities of [[Ariguaní]], [[Pivijay]], [[Chibolo]] and [[Plato, Magdalena|Plato]].
