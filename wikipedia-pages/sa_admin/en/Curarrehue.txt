'''Curarrehue''' ({{IPA|es|kuɾaˈrewe}}) is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Cautín Province]] of [[Araucanía Region]], [[Chile]]. The origin of Curarrehue dates back to the [[occupation of Araucanía]] and the [[Conquest of the Desert]] by the Chilean and Argentine army respectively in the 1870s and 1880s when [[Mapuches]] were pushed by the Argentine Army through [[Mamuil Malal Pass]] into the valley of Curarrehue where they settled. 

The town of Curarrehue aside, the second largest settlement in the commune is the village of [[Catripulli]] with 424 inhabitants as of 2017.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://geoarchivos.ine.cl/File/pub/Cd_Pb_Al_Cs_2019.pdf |title=Ciudades, pueblos, aldeas y caceríos 2019 |publisher=National Statistics Institute|date=2019 |access-date=November 1, 2020 |language=es}}</ref>

Geologically the town of Curarrehue is placed on the [[Reigolil-Pirihueico Fault]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Curarrehue spans an area of {{convert|1170.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 6,784 inhabitants (3,586 men and 3,198 women). Of these, 1,862 (27.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,922 (72.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 13.5% (806 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE" />

==Administration==
As a commune, Curarrehue is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Héctor Carrasco Ruiz ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).<ref name="Munitel"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Curarrehue is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Manuel García (RN) and Fernando Meza ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) as part of the 52nd electoral district, together with [[Cunco, Chile|Cunco]], [[Pucón]], [[Villarrica, Chile|Villarrica]], [[Loncoche]], [[Gorbea, Chile|Gorbea]] and [[Toltén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by José Garcia Ruminot (RN) and Eugenio Tuma Zedan ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 15th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-South).

== Geography ==

=== Climate ===
Curarrehue has a cool and humid [[oceanic climate]] (with a cool-summer [[Mediterranean climate|Mediterranean]] tendency), with an average annual precipitation of {{convert|2134|mm|in}}. Summers have mild days and cool nights. Winters are chilly and wetter, with [[snow]]falls. This climate is described by the [[Köppen climate classification]] as ''Cfb''.
