'''Villa Alegre''' (Spanish for "joyous town") a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of cities in Chile|city]] located in [[Linares Province]], [[Maule Region]] of [[Chile]]. With an area of {{convert|189.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, Villa Alegre is the smallest commune in the Maule Region. It sits {{convert|92|m|0|abbr=on}} above sea level.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Villa Alegre spans an area of {{convert|189.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 14,725 inhabitants (7,332 men and 7,393 women). Of these, 5,456 (37.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,269 (62.9%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population fell by 2.8% (425 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==History==
Villa Alegre was founded on May 6, 1891, and was one of the first places in Chile to have an electric [[tram]]way service, in 1915.

==Economy==
Villa Alegre and its neighbor [[San Javier, Chile|San Javier]] are traditional [[winemaking]] areas. The [[Loncomilla river|Loncomilla]] valley, which includes these two communes, is endowed with some of the better [[vineyard]]s and quality wines of the bountiful [[Maule river|Maule Valley]], in the [[Chilean Central Valley|Chile's central valley]] [[viticulture|viticultural]] [[Chilean wine|regions]] or [[appellation]]s.

==Government==
As a commune, Villa Alegre is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Arturo Palma Vilches ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]),<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> and the councilors are:
* María Ignacia González Torres ([[Christian Democrat Party of Chile|PDC]])
* Héctor Del Tránsito Manosalva González ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Manuel Muñoz Bastias (PRSD)
* Justo Rebolledo Araya ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Eduardo Bustamante Maureira (RN)
* Edgardo Bravo Rebolledo (RN)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Villa Alegre is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Jorge Tarud ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Romilio Gutiérrez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 39th electoral district, together with [[Linares (Chile)|Linares]], [[Colbún]], [[San Javier, Chile|San Javier]] and [[Yerbas Buenas]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Hernán Larraín]] (UDI) and Ximena Rincón González (PDC) as part of the 11th senatorial constituency (Maule-South).

== Famous People born in Villa Alegre ==

* [[Malaquías Concha Ortiz]], [[politician]] and social [[reform movement|reformer]].
