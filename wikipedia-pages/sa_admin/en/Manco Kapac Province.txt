'''Manco Kapac''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz]]. Its capital is [[Copacabana (Bolivia)|Copacabana]].

== Subdivision ==
Manco Kapac Province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are partly further subdivided into cantons.
