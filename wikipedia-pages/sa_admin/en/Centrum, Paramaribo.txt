'''Centrum''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in Suriname, located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 20,631.<ref name="census"/> The historical centre of Paramaribo is located within the resort. The city centre is mainly in original condition, contains 291 listed monuments, and was designated in 2002 as a [[Unesco|UNESCO]] [[World Heritage Site]].<ref name="unesco">{{cite web|url=http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/940|title=Historic Inner City Paramaribo|website=Unesco.org|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>

==History==
Paramaribo was founded in 1613 by Stoffel Albertszoon near the [[indigenous people|indigenous]] village Parmurbo.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_bij005191401_01/_bij005191401_01_0007.php|title=Bijdragen en Mededeelingen van het Historisch Genootschap. Deel 35|date=1914|access-date=29 May 2020|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|language=nl}}</ref> Around 1665, during the [[Great Britain|British]] occupation, the village was expanded and quickly outranked the earlier settlement of [[Torarica]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/301ges/aparbo02.html|title=Paramaribo Suriname 2|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref>

The historical centre dates from the 17th and 18th century, and is mainly composed of wooden houses in a plain and symmetrical style, and the street are set in a [[Grid plan|grid structure]].<ref name="unesco"/> The reason for using wood was that the absence of stone in the vicinity, and the clay was not suitable for [[brick]]s. Only important administrative buildings were built from stone shipped from the [[Netherlands]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.src-reizen.nl/bestemmingen/suriname/bezienswaardigheden|title=Bezienswaardigheden in Suriname|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl|website=SRC Reizen}}</ref> 

{{anchor|1821 fire}}
In January 1821, a fire consumed the area around Oranjeplein (current name: Onafhankelijkheidsplein<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dpc.uba.uva.nl/cgi/i/image/image-idx?c=surinamica;q1=C%20R%20%20Singh;rgn1=surinamica_all;start=41;type=boolean;view=reslist|title=Suriname 1599-1975|website=University of Amsterdam|language=nl|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref>) and Waterkant, the oldest part of the city. More than 400 buildings were lost, and it is the reason why the oldest part of the city centre has many 19th-century buildings.<ref name="nu1832">{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/301ges/aparbo05.html |title=Paramaribo Suriname|access-date=29 May 2020| language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref> In 1832, 46 houses burnt down.<ref name="nu1832"/> The slaves [[Kodjo (slave)|Kodjo]], Mentor, and Present were found guilty of [[arson]], and burnt alive.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://deleesclubvanalles.nl/boek/oog-in-oog-met-paramaribo/ |title=Oog in oog met Paramaribo|website=De Lees Club van Alles|access-date=10 March 2021|language=nl}}</ref>

Even though the centre has been constructed using a wide grid, the houses are spaced relatively close together. The reason is that the [[slave]]s, and [[Freedman|freed slaves]] lived in little shacks behind the main [[façade]].<ref name="encycl-1916">{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 552 - Paramaribo|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Frimangron==
{{anchor|Frimangron}}A muddy area to the south of the centre would later be named ''Frimangron'', meaning land of the free men. It was often not found on official maps, but was inhabited by freed former slaves. In 1772 the neighborhood was finally officially listed as neighbourhood F.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://historiek.net/frimangron-slavernij-suriname-karwan-fatah-black/83126/|title=Frimangron en de fundamenten van de vrije Surinaamse gemeenschap|website=Historiek|access-date=14 June 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Sights==
The main sights in the centre are:
* [[Fort Zeelandia (Paramaribo)|Fort Zeelandia]], a [[fortress]] built in 1640.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.suriname.nu/302ges/archi198.html|title=De Architectuur van Suriname|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>
* [[Saint Peter and Paul Cathedral, Paramaribo|Saint Peter and Paul Cathedral]], a wooden [[Roman Catholic]] cathedral, and the world's tallest wooden cathedral.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2010/11/15/grootste-houten-kathedraal-ter-wereld-heropend-in-paramaribo-a1462596|title=Grootste houten kathedraal ter wereld heropend in Paramaribo|website=NRC Handelsblad|url-access=subscription|language=nl|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>
* [[Onafhankelijkheidsplein]], the Independence Square, which contains the [[National Assembly (Suriname)|National Assembly]] and the [[Presidential Palace of Suriname|Presidential Palace]] among others.<ref name="city">{{cite web|url=https://cityofparamaribo.com/read/Worldheritage_area?sublist=18936&css=|title=World Heritage area|website=City of Paramaribo|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>
* The [[Waterkant]], the oldest street of Paramaribo with outstanding architecture near the [[Suriname River]].<ref name="city"/>
* [[Garden of Palms]], a palm tree landscape garden in behind the Presidential Palace.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.lonelyplanet.com/the-guianas/suriname/paramaribo/attractions/a/poi-sig/363444|title=Attractions in Paramaribo|access-date=29 May 2020|website=Lonely Planet}}</ref>
* [[Keizerstraat]], a street where the [[Neveh Shalom Synagogue]] and the [[Mosque Keizerstraat|Ahmadiyya Anjuman Isha’at Islam Mosque]] are adjacent to one-another.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cityofparamaribo.nl/read/keizerstraat|title=Keizerstraat|work=cityofparamaribo.nl|accessdate=29 May 2020}}</ref>
* [[Arya Diwaker]], one of the biggest [[Hindu temple|mandirs (Hindu temples)]] of Suriname.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.hindorama.com/negentig-jaar-arya-dewaker-suriname-s-gangapersad/|title=Negentig jaar Arya Dewaker Suriname|website=Hindorama|access-date=30 May 2020}}</ref>
* [[Centrumkerk]], the main church of the [[Dutch Reformed Church of Suriname]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://geheugen.delpher.nl/nl/geheugen/view?coll=ngvn&identifier=SURI01%3AFOTOAMXX1VAR219 |title=Kerkplein met Hervormde Kerk|website=Het Geheugen|access-date=8 March 2021|language=nl}}</ref>
* [[Court Charity]], a building of the [[Foresters Friendly Society]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://suriname.nu/surinamezoeken/knowledge-base/court-charity/ |title=Court Charity|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=10 March 2021|language=nl}}</ref>
* [[Elisabeth Samson House]], the house of [[Elisabeth Samson]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameview.com/blogs/elisabeth-samson-huis-restauratie-en-conservatie/ |title=Elisabeth Samson Huis: Restauratie en Conservatie |website=Suriname View|access-date=19 March 2021|language=nl}}</ref>
