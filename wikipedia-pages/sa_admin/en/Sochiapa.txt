'''Sochiapa''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|State]] of [[Veracruz]], about 40&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 21.39 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|12|N|96|56|W|}}. For decree of December 10, 1913 suprimió Sochiapa's municipality, passing its territory to form a part of that of Totutla. Later Sochiapa recovered its political category.

==Geography==

The municipality of Sochiapa is delimited to the north and northeast by [[Totutla]], to the southeast by [[Comapa, Veracruz|Comapa]], to the south by [[Huatusco (municipality)|Huatusco]] and to the west by [[Tlaltetela]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30146a.html |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Sochiapa |publisher=[[INAFED]] |language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}</ref> 

It is watered by small creeks which are tributaries of the [[Jamapa River]].

The weather in Sochiapa is cold  and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[coffee]] and [[sugarcane]].

==Celebrations==

In '''Sochiapa''', the celebration in honor to San Juan Nepomuseno, Patron of the town, takes place in May, and the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe takes place in December.
