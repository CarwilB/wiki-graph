'''Águia Branca''' ("white eagle" in [[English Language|English]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west-central portion of the state of [[Espírito Santo]], in southeastern [[Brazil]]. The municipality has a population of 9,631 (2020) and its area is 454 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/BP4]</ref>

==History==
It was first colonized by [[Poland|Polish]] immigrants, in 1928. Polish was widely spoken in the region till the Second World War, when the Brazilian government prohibited education in [[Polish language|Polish]] and [[German language|German]].

==Geography==
Located 226&nbsp;km from the state capital Vitória. Municipalities it shares borders with include: (west) [[Mantenópolis, Espírito Santo|Mantenópolis]] and [[Pancas, Espírito Santo|Pancas]]. (north) [[Barra de Sao Francisco, Espírito Santo|Barra de São Francisco]] and [[Nova Venécia, Espírito Santo|Nova Venécia]]. (south) [[São Domingos do Norte, Espírito Santo|São Domingos do Norte]]. (east) [[São Gabriel da Palha, Espírito Santo|São Gabriel da Palha]].

The municipality is in a markedly mountainous region of the state, and Águia Branca town looks up at great finger shaped stone peaks which surround the area. Municipal maximum height is at 878m above sea level.
