'''Ayacucho Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 19,669 inhabitants in an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">6,785&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(2,619.7&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span>, and its capital city is [[Ayacucho, Buenos Aires|Ayacucho]], which is around {{convert|330|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
