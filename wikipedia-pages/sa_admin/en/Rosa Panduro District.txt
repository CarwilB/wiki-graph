'''Rosa Panduro District''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of the [[Putumayo Province]] in [[Peru]], and one of the four districts that comprise that province.

== History ==
Rosa Panduro was part of [[Maynas Province, Peru|Maynas Province]] until April 17, 2014 when it was created as District by Law N° 30186 as part of Putumayo Province.<ref>[http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/30186.pdf Leyes congreso Perú]</ref> The district is named after a [[Rosa Panduro|housewife]] who [[Battle of Rocafuerte|participated]] in the [[Ecuadorian–Peruvian War]].

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 7,155,65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 34 meters above [[sea level]].

=== Boundaries ===
* By the north: [[Teniente Manuel Clavero District]]; 
* By the est: Colombia Republic;
* By the south and sudwest: [[Putumayo District]] and, [[Napo District]] and [[Torres Causana District]] (Maynas Province).

== Authorities ==
The current [[mayor]] of the district is Delmer Ricopa Coquinche (Movimiento Integración Loretana).<ref>[http://diariolaregion.com/web/candidatos-del-mil-ganaron-en-las-elecciones-municipales-2015/ Diario La Región 2015]</ref>
