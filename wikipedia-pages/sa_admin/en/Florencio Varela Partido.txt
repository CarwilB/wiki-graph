'''Florencio Varela''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the south of [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The partido has an area of {{convert|190|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}} and a population of 423,992 ({{census-ar|2010}}).<ref name=Cen2010>{{Cite web |url=http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |title=2010 Census provisional results |access-date=2011-03-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120901061446/http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |archive-date=2012-09-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its capital is [[Florencio Varela, Buenos Aires|Florencio Varela]].

==Name==
The partido is named in honour of Argentine writer and journalist [[Florencio Varela (writer)|Florencio Varela]].
