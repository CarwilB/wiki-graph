'''Vila Pavão''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 9,244 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|433|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref> [[East Pomeranian]], a dialect of [[Low German]], has co-official status in Vila Pavão.
