'''Babaçulândia''' is a municipality in the state of [[Tocantins]], Brazil.

Babaçulândia was founded on 1 January 1954.
It has an area of {{convert|1788.461|km2}}.
The most recent population figure as of 2020 was 10,666 people.
The municipality includes the buffer zone of the [[Tocantins Fossil Trees Natural Monument]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt|work=Surgiu|author=Tânia Caldas|title=Monumento natural das árvores fossilizadas do Tocantins renova conselho consultivo|date=27 February 2016|url=http://surgiu.com.br/noticia/233049/monumento-natural-das-arvores-fossilizadas-do-tocantins-renova-conselho-consultivo.html|access-date=2016-05-06|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160301111628/http://surgiu.com.br/noticia/233049/monumento-natural-das-arvores-fossilizadas-do-tocantins-renova-conselho-consultivo.html|archive-date=1 March 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
