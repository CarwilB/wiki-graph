'''Carmo''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈkaʁmu}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its nickname is the ''Cidade Bela'' (Beautiful City).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=330120 |title=IBGE &#124; Brasil em SĂntese |publisher=Cidades.ibge.gov.br |date= |accessdate=2022-08-26}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Carmo encompasses an area of about {{convert|320|km2|mi2}}.

Its current population is estimated to be more than 19,000 inhabitants, of which 72.3% live in the suburban area.

The municipality neighbors [[Sumidouro]], [[Cantagalo, Rio de Janeiro|Cantagalo]], [[Duas Barras]], [[Sapucaia, Rio de Janeiro|Sapucaia]], as well as bordering [[Além Paraíba]].

Part of the city is situated in the basin of the [[Paquequer River (Teresópolis)|Paquequer River]], one of the last tributaries of the [[Paraíba do Sul]] that has little aquatic pollution.
