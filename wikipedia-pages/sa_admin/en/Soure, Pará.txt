'''Soure''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northern state of [[Pará]], on the island of [[Marajó]], which is located in the [[Amazon River]] at its mouth. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 25,565 people. The area of the municipality is 3,512.863&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Arari''.

The [[Cambu River]] runs through the municipality for most of its course. The [[Tartarugas River]] forms the border with the municipality of [[Chaves, Pará|Chaves]].<ref name="lisboa">{{cite book | last = Lisboa | first = P.L.B. | title = A Terra dos Aruã: Uma história ecológica do arquipelágo do Marajó | year = 2012 | publisher = Museu Paraense Emílio Goeldi | location = Belém}}</ref>

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=APA Arquipélago do Marajó|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/951|accessdate=2016-06-27}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|27464|ha|adj=on}} [[Soure Marine Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use conservation area created in 2001 that protects the coastal mangroves to the north of the municipal seat and along the north part of the [[Paracauari River]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Marinha de Soure – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Marinha de Soure|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4370|accessdate=2016-06-29}}</ref>
