'''Zamora''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 187,075.<ref name="stats">{{cite web |title=XIV CENSO NACIONAL DE POBLACIÓN Y VIVIENDA Resultados por Entidad Federal y Municipio del Estado Miranda |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/Demografia/CensodePoblacionyVivienda/pdf/miranda.pdf}}</ref> The town of [[Guatire]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Zamora Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">{{cite web |title=Miranda.zip |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archive-date=December 21, 2007 |access-date=March 22, 2008}}</ref> 

==Name==
The municipality is one of several named "[[Zamora Municipality (disambiguation)|Zamora Municipality]]" for the 19th century Venezuelan soldier [[Ezequiel Zamora]].

==History==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
The region was historically an [[agricultural]] area that cultivated various [[export]] products. The population of Guatire has increased rapidly in recent years as an east suburb of [[Caracas]].

==Geography==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
The municipality's northern border, separating it from [[Vargas (state)|Vargas]], is the east part of the Costa (or Caribe) [[Mountain range|Mountain Range]], while the lower Caraballo Ridge comprises its southern border. Grande River (also called Guarenas River or Caucagua River) runs from the west to the southeast. Guatire, the municipality's shire town, is located near the western border.

==Demographics==
The Zamora Municipality, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 187,075 (up from 152,422 in 2001). This amounts to 7% of the state's population.<ref name="stats"/> The municipality's population density in 2011 was {{convert|490|PD/sqkm}}.

==Government==
The mayor of the Zamora Municipality is Raziel Rodriguez, elected in 2021 for 2021-2025 tenure with 46,97% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Divulgaciones |url=https://www2.cne.gob.ve/rm2021 |access-date=2023-09-09 |website=www2.cne.gob.ve}}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Guatire and Bolívar.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
