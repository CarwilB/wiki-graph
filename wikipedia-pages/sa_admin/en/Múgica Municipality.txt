'''Múgica''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Nueva Italia, Michoacán|Nueva Italia]]. The municipality was originally created on February 12, 1942 as the municipality of '''Zaragoza''' then changed to the current name on December 9, 1969.
