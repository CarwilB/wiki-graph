'''Tomayquichua District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Ambo Province|Ambo]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410165254/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-10 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Despite its size and population, it receives some tourism, almost entirely domestic, due to locally popular events such as “El Baile de Negritos”, and the house of “La [[Perricholi]]”.
