''' Casimiro Castillo  ''' is a [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 522.7  km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 18,913.<ref>{{cite web |title=Casimiro Castillo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14021a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=April 13, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070401172924/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14021a.htm |archive-date=April 1, 2007 }}</ref>

Five police officers from Casimiro Castillo were arrested for the murder of a young man on August 26, 2020.<ref>{{cite news |title=Detienen a 5 policías en Jalisco por asesinato de joven detenido |url=https://www.animalpolitico.com/2020/08/detienen-policias-jalisco-asesinato-joven-detenido/ |access-date=January 11, 2021 |work=Animal Político |date=28 August 2020 |language=es |archive-date=19 January 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210119095049/https://www.animalpolitico.com/2020/08/detienen-policias-jalisco-asesinato-joven-detenido/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> The body of Alfredo Sevilla, mayor on leave, was found at the bottom of a gully on March 10, 2021.<ref>{{cite news |title=En el fondo de un barranco hallan muerto a alcalde con licencia de Casimiro Castillo |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/estados/2021/3/12/en-el-fondo-de-un-barranco-hallan-muerto-alcalde-con-licencia-de-casimiro-castillo-259984.html |access-date=March 13, 2021 |work=proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |date=March 12, 2021 |language=spanish}}</ref>

As of March 11, 2021, the municipality reported 118 cases and 31 deaths related to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus (COVID-19) en Casimiro Castillo, Jalisco |url=http://www.gncys.com/covid19/covid19-municipio.aspx?edoid=JC&munid=021 |website=gncys.com |access-date=March 13, 2021 |language=es-mx |date=1 February 2020}}</ref>
