'''Sambú''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 931 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 762; its population as of 2000 was 747.<ref name=inec/>  It is located at the confluence of the Sambú and Sabalo rivers. The town has an airstrip with a paved runway.
