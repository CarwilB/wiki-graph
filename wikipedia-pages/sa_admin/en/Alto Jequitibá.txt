'''Alto Jequitibá''' is a Brazilian municipality in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. It belongs to the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Manhuaçu. The municipality has a population of 8,301 (2020) and its area is {{Cvt|152|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cod.ibge.gov.br/OLO]</ref>

==Geography==
Settled in the highplanes among offshoots from the [[Pico da Bandeira]], the third highest mountain of Brazil nearing 3,000 meters, in the Ocidental side of the [[Caparaó]] range of mountains. It is in the Brazil's Southeast in the bush's zone.

==Demographics==
Alto Jequitibá is one of the few mainly [[Protestant]] cities of Minas Gerais, mainly [[Presbyterian]].
