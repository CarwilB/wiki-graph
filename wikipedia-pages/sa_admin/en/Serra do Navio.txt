'''Serra do Navio''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈsɛʁɐ du naˈviw|lang}}; 'Mountain range of the Ship') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 5,488 (2020 est.)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/serra-do-navio/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 7,713&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=160005|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Amapá {{!}} Serra do Navio|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-17}}</ref> In 1947, [[manganese]] was discovered in the area.<ref name="conflict"/> Serra do Navio<ref name="ibge"/> was built as a planned city to house the workers.<ref name="conflict">{{cite web|url=https://mapa.conflictosmineros.net/ocmal_db-v2/conflicto/view/129 |title=Exploração de manganês pela ICOMI: passivo ambiental para a população de Serra do Navio|website=Conflictos Mineros|access-date=3 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> On 22 June 1993, the capital of the municipality was changed from Água Branca do Amapari to Serra do Novio.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/serra-do-navio/historico |title=Serra do Navio|website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=3 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.diariodoamapa.com.br/cadernos/entrevista/podemos-nunca-mais-ouvir-o-apito-do-trem-entrando-nas-matas-de-serra-do-navio/ |title=“Podemos nunca mais ouvir o apito do trem entrando nas matas de Serra do Navio” |website=Diário do Amapá|access-date=4 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

== History ==
In 1947, [[manganese]] was discovered in the area. [[ICOMI]] was given the concession to exploit the mines, however the scale of the operation was such, that [[Bethlehem Steel]] was given a 49% stake in 1950. The [[Amapá Railway]], and two towns for the workers were constructed: Serra do Navio and Vila Amazonas near [[Santana, Amapá|Santana]] where a harbour was built.<ref name="conflict"/> Serra do Navio was built according to North-American standards and was considered a model town.<ref name="amapa"/>

During the 1980s, the mine produced about 1,000,000 tons of ore,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mining-enc.com/b/brazil/ |title=Brazil|website=Mining Encyclopedia|access-date=3 April 2021}}</ref> however Bethlehem Steel wanted to end the cooperation, because the deposits were being exhausted. The contract was originally set to end in 2003, however in 1996, Bethlehem Steel decided to end the contract.<ref name="conflict"/>

[[File:Serra do Navio Amapá Brazil.jpg|thumb|left]]
The closing of the main mine, led to a steep decrease in population, and the appearance of a [[ghost town]]. Smaller mines still operate in the region. Other economic activities include agriculture, and forestry.<ref name="amapa">{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/conheca/serra-do-navio |title=Serra do Navio|website=Government of Amapá|access-date=3 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

Serra do Navio is located on the [[BR-210]] highway.<ref name="amapa"/> The municipality is subdivided in two districts: The town of Serra do Navio and [[Cachaço, Brazil|Cachaço]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ap/serra-do-navio/historico |title=Serra do Navio|website=IBGE|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
