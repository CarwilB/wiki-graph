'''Upala''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Upala (canton)|Upala]] canton, in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

==History==
Upala was granted the title of "ciudad" (city) by a law of May 4, 1970.

== Geography ==
Upala has an area of {{formatnum:148.65}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:48}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It is in the northwest corner of the [[San Carlos Plain]] (Llanura de San Carlos) in northern Costa Rica, {{Convert|76|km||sp=us}} southeast of [[La Cruz District, La Cruz|La Cruz]], {{Convert|124|km||sp=us}} northwest of [[Ciudad Quesada]], {{Convert|195|km||sp=us}} from the provincial capital city of [[Alajuela]], and {{Convert|214|km||sp=us}} from the national capital city of [[San José, Costa Rica|San José]].

== Economy ==
Upala is a supply center for cattle ranchers and rice growers in the area. A hospital, bank, medical clinic, pharmacy and gas station are here. There is a busy commercial marketplace in the town center where vendors sell fruits, vegetables and a wide array of other home-grown products. Several small restaurants share the market area, and there are other restaurants and a few places to stay in the town.

==Nearby places of interest==

[[Caño Negro Wildlife Refuge]] is a watery lowland of lush sloughs and marshes, a seasonal lake, and abundant wildlife. The area is a birdwatcher's paradise. ({{Convert|37|km||sp=us}} east of Upala via Highway 4 to Colonia Puntarenas and Highway 138 to Caño Negro)
