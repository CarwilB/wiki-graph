The city is served by [[Conceição do Araguaia Airport]].

==Climate==
Conceição do Araguaia experiences a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with hot temperatures, high humidity, and distinct wet and dry seasons.

The city experiences a [[wet season]] from October to April. The [[dry season]] extends from June to September.<ref name="WMONormals"/>
