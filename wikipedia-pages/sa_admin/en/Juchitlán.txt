'''Juchitlán ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 245.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 5,282.<ref>{{cite web|title=Juchitlán|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14052a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 16, 2009|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070528055209/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14052a.htm|archive-date=May 28, 2007}}</ref>
