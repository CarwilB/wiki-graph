'''Patzún''' ({{IPA|es|patˈsun}}) is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|165.6|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 72,873 inhabitants.

==History==
Patzún was inhabited by the [[Kaqchikel people]] since the 12th century at the least, and was part of the [[Iximche]] kingdom. Its name is derived from the words "pa" and "sum" meaning "place" and "sunflower" respectively, roughly translating to the "place of sunflowers". Another meaning that has been attributed is that it came from "pa" and "tsun" meaning "leather".<ref name="Hist"/>

As per local lore, the documents regarding the establishment of the town were hidden by [[Bernard of Clairvaux|San Bernardino]], the patron of the town, behind a red cloth, during the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spanish colonization]] in the early 16th century, and the documents were lost later, and only the cloth is preserved. The [[Franciscans]]  built a church in 1540. The city was destroyed in 1566, and the church and municipal buildings were damaged in 1760, when a cargo of [[gun powder]] exploded. In 1839, Patzún became a municipality in the department of Chimaltenango, when it was created.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Patzún|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Patzún municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/historia-del-municipio-de-patzun-chimaltenango/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Patzún is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|165.5|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Patzún (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/chimaltenango/0407__patz%C3%BAn/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the western corner of the department. It borders the municipalities of [[Tecpán Guatemala|Tecpán]] to the north, [[Pochuta]] and [[Acatenango]] to the south, [[Patzicía]] and [[Santa Cruz Balanyá]] to the east, and [[San Lucas Tolimán]] and [[San Antonio Palopó]] to the west.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Patzún|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Patzún municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/geografia-del-municipio-de-patzun-chimaltenango/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Municipios de Chimaltenango|year=2010 |location=Guatemala|url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|access-date=8 June 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608155133/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|archive-date=8 June 2015|language=es}}</ref>

===Climate===
Located at an elevation of {{cvt|2235.38|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> Patzún has a [[Oceanic climate|subtropical highland climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Cwb). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|15.7|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|1678|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref name="Climate-Data.org"/>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 72,873 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 37,027 males and 35,846 females. About 29.7% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.6% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (54.3%) was classified as urban, while 45.7% lived in rural areas. About 94.9% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (95.1%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (4.7%) forming a significant minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 84.8%, and [[Kaqchikel language|Kaqchikel]] (71.9%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
