'''Castanheiras''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning [[chestnut]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 2,987 (2020) and its area is 893&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/7SM]</ref>
