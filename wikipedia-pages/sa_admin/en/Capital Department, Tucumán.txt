'''Capital Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the center-north of the [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. At the 2001 census, its population was 527,607 and its [[population density]] was 5,862/km<sup>2</sup>.  The city of [[San Miguel de Tucumán]] covers almost all 90&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of the department's area. It is the smallest department in area, but the most populated as well.

==Geography==
The entire department lies on a plain presenting a gentle slope heading toward the [[Salí River]] located at the east end.
