'''Rodríguez de Mendoza''' is a province of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]]. It is located in the southeast part of the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|department of Amazonas]]. It borders on the west with the [[Chachapoyas Province|province of Chachapoyas]] and on the north, east and south with the [[San Martín Region|department of San Martin]]. It was created by law 7626 on October 31, 1932, and its capital is [[Mendoza (Peru)|Mendoza]].

The province has an enormous importance because of the fertility of its soils, its benign climate and the big rivers that pass through its territory. Between these rivers we have [[San Antonio River (Peru)|San Antonio]], [[Aiña river|Aiña]], [[Pachca river|Pachca]], [[Omia river|Omia]] and [[Shocol river|Shocol]] or [[Shocol river|Milpuc]]. In its extensive tropical forests, there are [[spectacled bear]]s and one variety of [[hummingbird]] (''[[Loddigesia mirabilis]]'').
