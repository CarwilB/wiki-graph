'''Tezontepec de Aldama''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. 

The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|133.6|km2}} and, as of 2005, had a total population of 10,723.<ref>{{cite web|title= Tezontepec de Aldama |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13066a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref> It was raised to municipal status in 1869.<ref name="muni"/>

The name "Tezontepec" contains the [[Nahuatl]] words ''tetzontli'' ([[tezontle]], a reddish volcanic rock) and ''tepetl'' (hill), while "de Aldama" commemorates [[Mexican War of Independence|Independence War]] hero [[Juan Aldama]].<ref name="muni">{{cite web |title=Tezontepec: Tu municipio |website=Municipio de Tezontepec de Aldama |url=https://tezontepec.gob.mx/tu-municipio/ |access-date=12 September 2023}}</ref>
