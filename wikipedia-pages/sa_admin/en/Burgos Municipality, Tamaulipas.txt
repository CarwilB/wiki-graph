'''Burgos''' is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] located in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]].

== History ==
On February 20, 1749, the captain José Antonio Leal de León y Guerra founded the village of Nuestra Señora de Loreto de Burgos, populated mainly with families from [[Linares (Nuevo León)| Linares]].
