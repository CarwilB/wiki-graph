'''Livorno'''{{efn|{{langx|srn|Ofriti}}, {{langx|hns|Lafendro}}, {{langx|jvn|Lepondro}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|title=Verjavaanste toponiemen in Suriname en hun herkomst|trans-title=Javanese-influenced place names in Suriname and their origin|first=Hein|last=Vruggink|publisher=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|date=1987|lang=nl}}</ref>}} is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 8,209.<ref name="census"/>

The resort consists of the neighbourhoods ''Livorno'' and ''Bethesda''.<ref name="vakantie">{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-zuid|title=Paramaribo Zuid|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Vakantie Arena}}</ref> Livorno is a former [[sugar]] plantation founded in 1819 by F. Cassali from [[Livorno]], [[Italy]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/l/livorno|title=Plantage Livorno|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname Plantages}}</ref> [[Bethesda, Suriname|Bethesda]] was the location of a [[leprosy|leper]] colony which had been at that location between 1933,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pix4profs.nl/gallery/MAJELLA/|title=MAJELLA STICHTING|website=Pix4Profs|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> and 1962.<ref name="knaw">{{cite web|url=http://resources.huygens.knaw.nl/zendingoverzeesekerken/RepertoriumVanNederlandseZendings-EnMissie-archieven1800-1960/gids/organisatie/organisatie/849906134|title=Repertorium van Nederlandse zendings- en missie-archieven 1800-1960|website=[[Huygens Institute for the History of the Netherlands]]|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Livorno is close to the harbour, and home to small industry.<ref name="vakantie"/>
