'''Montauri''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2022, it has a population of 1,499 people.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/montauri/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Demographics==
{{update section|2019|date=April 2019}}
Montauri it is the only Brazilian town where the entire population reported to be [[White Brazilian|White]], according to the 2000 census. The town has its origin from a group of [[Italian Brazilian|Italian]] [[immigrant]]s who settled in the region in 1904.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/7873/montauri-rs.html|title=Pousadas em Montauri, Hotéis em Montauri - RS - Restaurantes Imobiliárias Fotos - Férias|website=www.ferias.tur.br}}</ref>
