The '''Partido de Bahía Blanca''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of the [[Buenos Aires Province]] is located at the south-west of the province in central [[Argentina]] at coordinates {{coord|38|42|S|62|16|W}}.

The provincial subdivision holds a population of 284,776 inhabitants in an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">2,300&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(888&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span>, and its capital city is [[Bahía Blanca]], on the shore of the [[Atlantic Ocean]].

==Sports==
The city of Bahía Blanca has a strong sporting tradition. It is home to two  [[football (soccer)|football]] clubs, [[Olimpo de Bahía Blanca]] and [[Club Villa Mitre]] ([[Primera B Nacional|second division]]).

The city is also well known for its basketball club Estudiantes which is on the ''Liga Nacional de Basquetbol.''{{cn|date=February 2024}}
