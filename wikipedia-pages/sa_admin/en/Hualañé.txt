'''Hualañé''' ({{IPA|es|walaˈɲe}}) is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Curicó Province]] in [[Chile]]'s seven [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Maule Region|Maule]].

Situated in the [[Mataquito River]] valley, its main activities are the agriculture of tomatoes, potatoes, and grapes for wine. The commune spans an area of {{convert|629.0|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of Population and Housing by the National Statistics Institute (''Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas'' or INE) the Hualañé commune had 9,741 inhabitants; of these, 5,198 (53.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,543 (46.6%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 5,059 men and 4,682 women. The population grew by 4.8% (443 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Hualañé is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Claudio Pucher.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Hualañé is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Roberto León ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Celso Morales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 36th electoral district, together with [[Curicó]], [[Teno, Chile|Teno]], [[Romeral]], [[Molina, Chile|Molina]], [[Sagrada Familia, Chile|Sagrada Familia]], [[Licantén]], [[Vichuquén]] and [[Rauco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
