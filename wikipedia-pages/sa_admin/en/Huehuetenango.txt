'''Huehuetenango''' ({{IPA|es|weweteˈnaŋɡo|}}) is a [[city]] and municipality in the [[highland]]s of western [[Guatemala]]. It is also the capital of the department of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]]. The city is situated {{convert|269|km|mi}} from [[Guatemala City]], and is the last departmental capital on the [[Pan-American Highway]] before the Mexican border at [[La Mesilla]]. Its primary export is coffee.

==Overview==
{{Onesource|section|date=November 2021}}
Huehuetenango (originally called ''Xinabajul'' in the [[Mam language]]) was already a [[Maya civilization|Maya]] settlement before the [[Spain|Spanish]] conquest of the fortified city of [[Zaculeu]], which was the [[Pre-Columbian]] capital of the Mam kingdom situated just a few kilometers from Xinabahul. 'Huehuetenango' means ''place of the ancients (or ancestors)'' in [[Nahuatl language|Nahuatl]], which is the name [[Gonzalo de Alvarado]] adopted from his [[Nahua peoples|Nahua]] allies when Zaculeu and Xinabahul were conquered.

Many people of [[Mam people|Mam]] descent still live in and around Huehuetenango, and the nearby ruins of Zaculeu have become a tourist attraction. The ruins are markedly distinct from other Maya archeological sites; the original unearthed stones, comprising only a small portion of the original structures, were coated with plaster during restoration works carried out in the 1940s. There is also a small museum at Zaculeu that includes statues and small artifacts found on the site.

[[Huehuetenango Airport]] {{airport codes |HUG |MGHT |}} is within the city and has a paved {{convert|825|m}} runway and a small terminal building. There are no scheduled airline flights {{asof|2019|lc=y}}.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.world-airport-codes.com/guatemala/huehuetenango-3039.html|title=Huehuetenango - Guatemala|website=World Airport Codes|accessdate=1 May 2019}}</ref>

==Climate==
The city has a [[Oceanic climate|subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cwb''), with warm summers and mild winters.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://en.climate-data.org/north-america/guatemala/huenuetenango/huehuetenango-53821/|title=Huehuetenango climate: Average Temperature, weather by month, Huehuetenango weather averages - Climate-Data.org|website=en.climate-data.org|access-date=2019-02-15}}</ref>

==Sports==
The city boasts one Guatemalan top level football side [[Deportivo Xinabajul|Xinabajul]] in the [[Liga Nacional de Fútbol de Guatemala]].

==People==
Former [[President of Guatemala|president]] [[Efraín Ríos Montt]] was born in Huehuetenango (in 1926). His younger brother, [[Mario Enrique Ríos Montt]] the emeritus auxiliary bishop of the [[Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Guatemala]] in 1932.
