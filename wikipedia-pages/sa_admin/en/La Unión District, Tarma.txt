'''La Unión District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Tarma Province|Tarma]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>  It is bordered on the north by the districts of Palcamayo and San Pedro de Cajas; on the south by the district of Tarma; on the east by the district of Acobamba; and on the west by the Central Railroad of Cerro de Pasco.
