'''Albán''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]].

==Veredas==
The municipality is divided into 14 veredas.
* Centro
* Chavarro
* Chimbe
* El Entable
* Garbanzal
* Guayacundo Alto
* Guayacundo Bajo
* Java
* Las Marías
* Los Alpes
* Namay Alto
* Namay Bajo
* Pantanillo
* San Rafael
