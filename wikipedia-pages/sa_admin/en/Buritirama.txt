'''Buritirama''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Buritirama covers {{convert|3,941.872|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 21,276 with a population density of 5.4 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
