'''Reynosa''' is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] located in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. It includes the city of [[Reynosa, Tamaulipas|Reynosa]]. In population, both the municipality and the city (locality) are the largest in their respective categories in the state of Tamaulipas.
