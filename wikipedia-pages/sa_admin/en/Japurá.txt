'''Japurá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 2,251 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/japura/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 55,791&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (21,541 sq mi).<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref> It forms the Japurá [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] together with the municipality [[Maraã]] (to the east of the Japurá municipality). The southern border of both the municipality and the microregion is the [[Japurá River]].
[[File: Amazonas Micro Japura.svg|thumb|right|260px| It forms the Japurá [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] together with the municipality [[Maraã]] (to the east of the Japurá municipality).]]
The municipality contains the [[Juami-Japurá Ecological Station]], which covers the entire [[Juami River]] basin.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica Juami-Japurá|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=72|access-date=2016-05-07}}</ref>
The municipality contains 55% of the {{convert|146950|ha|adj=on}} [[Auatí-Paraná Extractive Reserve]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Auatí-Paraná – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX Auatí-Paraná|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/uc/1010|access-date=2016-10-21}}</ref>
It contains part of the {{convert|7999000|ha|adj=on}} [[Alto Rio Negro Indigenous Territory]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Terra Indígena Alto Rio Negro – ISA}}
 |title=Terra Indígena Alto Rio Negro|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental|work=Terras Indígenas no Brasil
 |url=https://terrasindigenas.org.br/en/terras-indigenas/4068|access-date=2017-03-04}}</ref>

==In popular culture==
* Japurá, rendered as "Jaburo," is the location of the main Earth Federation base in the [[Mobile Suit Gundam]] franchise.
