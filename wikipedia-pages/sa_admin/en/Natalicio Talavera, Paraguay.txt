'''Natalicio Talavera''' is a  [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] in the [[Guairá Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. The town is named after 19th-century poet [[Natalicio Talavera (poet)|Natalicio Talavera]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Natalicio Talavera – Municipalidad de Natalicio Talavera – Gobernaciones y Municipios|url=https://www.municipios.gov.py/nataliciotalavera/|access-date=2021-07-21|language=es}}</ref> The [[Tebicuarymí River]] runs near the town.<ref name=":0" />
