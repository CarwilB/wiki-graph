'''Puerto Leguízamo'''<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://puertoleguizamo-putumayo.gov.co/ayuda.shtml?apc=O-h1--&s=Y Puerto Leguízamo official website] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070929114815/http://puertoleguizamo-putumayo.gov.co/ayuda.shtml?apc=O-h1--&s=Y |date=29 September 2007 }}</ref> ({{IPA|es|ˈpweɾto le'ɣisamo}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Putumayo Department]], a southern border region of [[Colombia]]. It is on the north bank of the [[Putumayo River]].

Its Catedral Nuestra Señora del Carmen, dedicated to [[Our Lady of Mount Carmel]], is the [[cathedral]] episcopal see of the Latin Catholic missionary pre-diocesan [[Apostolic Vicariate of Puerto Leguízamo-Solano]].

Puerto Leguízamo is served by [[Caucayá Airport]].

In 2026, [[2026 Colombian Air Force Lockheed C-130 crash|a Lockheed C-130 crashed in the nearby forest]] after departing from Caucayá Airport.

==Climate==
Located just a few kilometres south of the [[equator]], Puerto Leguízamo has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
