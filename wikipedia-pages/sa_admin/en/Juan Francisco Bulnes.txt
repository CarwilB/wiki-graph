'''Juan Francisco Bulnes''' ({{IPA|es|ˈxwaɱ fɾanˈsisko ˈβulnes}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Gracias a Dios (department)|Gracias a Dios]].

The total population of the municipality was 6,407 in 2015. 
The rivers Abajo and Platano along with the lagoon of Ibans form the major water systems of the municipality. Almost all the localities are located next to these water systems with Ibans being the biggest. The only health centre of the municipality is located in Ibans.

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Juan Francisco Bulnes municipality had a population of 6,392.

By 2018, the municipality had a population of 6,440 inhabitants, composed of 3,292 men and 3,149 women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref>{{cite web|author=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE) |date=2018 |editor=INE Honduras |format=PDF |language=es |title=Juan Francisco Bulnes, Gracias a Dios: Indicadores Municipales 2018 |url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Juan-Francisco-Bulnes-Gracias-a-Dios-2018.pdf}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
