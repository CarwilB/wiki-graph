'''Presidente Prudente''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the [[Interior of São Paulo|interior]] of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], approximately 558 kilometers (347 miles) from the state capital, [[São Paulo]].

According to the 2025 estimate by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), Presidente Prudente has a population of 234,706 inhabitants,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/presidente-prudente/panorama |title=POPULAÇÃO |access-date=23 February 2025 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> with a floating population of 402,000 inhabitants,<ref name="IBGE"/> making it the [[List of municipalities in São Paulo|40th most populous municipality in São Paulo]]. The municipality covers a territorial area of 560.637&nbsp;km².<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/sp/presidente-prudente.html |title=Presidente Prudente (SP) | access-date=23 February 2025 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

The municipality comprises the seat and the districts of Ameliópolis, Eneida, Floresta do Sul, and Montalvão,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.igc.sp.gov.br/produtos/arquivos/municipios_e_distritos.pdf |title=Municípios e Distritos do Estado de São Paulo |publisher=IGC – Instituto Geográfico e Cartográfico}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2015/ |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |publisher=IBGE – Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística}}</ref> which are subdivided into 255 neighborhoods.

Presidente Prudente was emancipated from Conceição de Monte Alegre (now [[Paraguaçu Paulista]]) in the early 1920s.<ref>{{cite web |last=Seade |first=Fundação |url=http://produtos.seade.gov.br/produtos/500anos/index.php?arq_htm=historico/hist_41406.htm |title=Fundação Seade |access-date=8 April 2025 |website=produtos.seade.gov.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref> Its name honors the former Brazilian president [[Prudente de Morais]] (1841–1902), who was a lawyer and politician, serving as the first governor of São Paulo during the Republic (1889–1890).

Today, the city is a major industrial, cultural, and service hub in western São Paulo, earning the nickname "Capital of Western São Paulo." According to a study by the [[Getúlio Vargas Foundation]], it ranks as the 27th most promising city in Brazil for building a professional career.

Industrialization in the city began in the mid-1930s, spurred by the economic crisis of 1929, which prompted the exploration of new economic activities. In addition to industry, cotton cultivation was also significant.

The municipality boasts a rich cultural tradition, encompassing crafts, theater, music, and sports. The main football club is [[Grêmio Desportivo Prudente]], which plays at the [[Estádio Paulo Constantino]] (Prudentão). Other notable landmarks include the Parque do Povo and the Teatro Municipal Procópio Ferreira, alongside other theaters and small parks.

== History ==
=== Origins ===
[[File:Xavante.jpg|thumb|left|Representatives of the [[Xavante]] indigenous people, the first inhabitants of the São Paulo region|alt=Indigenous Xavante representatives|177x177px]]

The area now known as the western region of São Paulo was originally inhabited by indigenous [[Xavante]], [[Kaingang]], and [[Guarani people|Guarani]]/[[Pai Tavytera]] populations.

The territory was characterized by forests on reddish purple soils and [[grassland]]s on other soil types. The rise of urban markets in [[Minas Gerais]] led wilderness explorers to settle in the region, investing in cattle ranching. This need for land control sparked conflicts with the region's indigenous peoples.

As gold reserves in Minas Gerais dwindled, a significant migratory wave moved toward São Paulo. This migration intensified as many from Minas Gerais fled conscription for the [[Paraguayan War]], which lasted from December 1864 to March 1870, and continued after the war ended.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web |url=http://www.presidenteprudente.net/historia_de_presidente_prudente.php |title=História de Presidente Prudente |author=Prefeitura |access-date=16 November 2010 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/63c8SFx06?url=http://www.presidenteprudente.net/historia_de_presidente_prudente.php |archive-date=1 December 2011 |url-status=live}}</ref>

In 1893, a path was established between the Campos Novos do Paranapanema region and the [[Paraná River]], connecting it to [[Mato Grosso]]. This region saw the expansion of coffee plantations into the reddish purple soils of the south. The ridges of the Western São Paulo Plateau gained value due to their suitability for coffee cultivation.

The advance of coffee and the resulting expansion of capitalist control encouraged the occupation and growth of the Far Western São Paulo region. Coffee [[Latifundium|landowners]] from older regions moved to Western São Paulo, acquiring land for the purpose of subdividing it into lots, leading to the establishment of [[subsistence agriculture]] properties alongside coffee estates.<ref name="Museu_História">{{cite web |url=http://museu.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/historiapp.php |title=História de Presidente Prudente |author=Museu Municipal de Presidente Prudente |access-date=16 November 2010 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/63c8NVFJh?url=http://museu.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/historiapp.php |archive-date=1 December 2011 |url-status=live}}</ref>

The development of Presidente Prudente was further aided by the railway, which served as the primary means of transporting people and goods for decades. The choice of the urban site was closely tied to the route of the Sorocaba Railway, which followed the ridge lines.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

=== Political and administrative emancipation ===
[[File:Prudentedemorais.jpg|thumb|left|150px|The municipality's name honors former Brazilian president [[Prudente de Morais]]|alt=Portrait of Prudente de Morais]]

With the significant development of the region now encompassing Presidente Prudente, the village of Goulart was established on 14 September 1917 by Colonel Francisco de Paula Goulart.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> This occurred shortly before the territorial and administrative emancipation from Conceição de Monte Alegre (now [[Paraguaçu Paulista]]).<ref name="Museu_História"/>

Much of the territory consisted of subdivided lots. In addition to farmers, merchants emerged, interested in purchasing lots to develop municipal commerce. With the inauguration of regular train services on 19 January 1919, both rural and urban populations grew. Colonel José Soares Marcondes owned the company responsible for selling these lots, the Cia. Marcondes de Colonização, Indústria e Comércio.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

Upon emancipation, the city consisted of a single district, the Seat, created by State Law No. 1,798 on 28 November 1921 and established on 13 March 1923, covering an area of approximately 20,000&nbsp;km² at the time. The ''[[Comarca]]'', of the 4th level, was created by Law No. 1,887 on 8 December 1922.<ref name="IBGE_História" /> Initially named Vila Goulart in honor of Francisco de Paula Goulart, it later adopted its current name to honor former Brazilian president [[Prudente de Morais]].<ref name="Museu_História"/>

=== Economic growth ===
[[File:Presidente Prudente SP - década de 1930.jpg|thumb|Central area of the city in the 1930s|alt=Historical photo of Presidente Prudente's central area]]

Coffee cultivation was the most significant economic activity for the new municipality, driven by landowners, contractors, and settlers. In 1927, an estimated ten million coffee plants were recorded in the municipality. The first half of the 1920s marked the peak of Prudente's coffee production. Its decline was driven by the depletion of the region's sandy soils, [[frost]] that destroyed crops, reduced exports due to changes in coffee quality, competition from other countries, and the [[Great Depression|economic crisis of 1929]].

With the coffee crisis, a shift to cotton was inevitable, supported by favorable national and international conditions. Cotton attracted foreign companies to the western São Paulo region, which traded with and financed small farmers due to high international demand for the product.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

The introduction of cotton and, later, peanut cultivation brought changes to the structure of labor relations in the area. However, it was only in the following decade that cotton cultivation became significant, with a notable increase in planted area as coffee cultivation continued to decline. Other crops such as rice, corn, [[beans]], and [[potatoes]] became the economic foundation for farmers, who used their sales to finance coffee crops, pay for properties, and support their families. The processing of these products occurred in municipalities and settlements, increasing the number of establishments and fostering their growth.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

In the 1940s, 44.7% of the region's usable land consisted of pastures. The growing importance of [[cattle ranching]] in the Alta Sorocabana region was solidified, particularly through the deforestation of previously unexploited areas.<ref name="Museu_História"/> Early industrial activities, initially tied to timber extraction, shifted toward processing raw materials, primarily for cattle production. Surplus products not consumed locally were transported to São Paulo by rail.<ref name="Museu_História"/> In the early 1930s, there were seventeen industrial establishments, which grew to 138 by 1940, employing 655 people. Industrial development complemented the dominant agricultural economy, reinforcing its prominence.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

=== Social development and modern times ===
[[File:Praça Nove de Julho.JPG|thumb|left|Current view of Praça Nove de Julho in downtown Prudente|alt=Modern view of Praça Nove de Julho|277x277px]]

The city's growth necessitated the establishment of the first school, now Escola Estadual Professor Adolpho Arruda Mello, in 1925; the first health facility in 1926; the establishment of the Parish in 1925; and the District Education Inspectorate in 1928, which was transformed into the Office of Education in 1932.

Electricity supply services began in 1924, provided by the Companhia Marcondes de Colonização Indústria e Comércio through a small thermoelectric plant powered by a steam locomotive and a 60 kVA generator, supplying energy from dusk until midnight.

Religious services provided by the [[Catholic Church]] were formalized with the creation of the São Sebastião Parish in 1925, led by Father José Maria Martinez Sarrion, who served as parish priest until his death in 1951. Within a short period, the municipality achieved a significant level of regional political-administrative autonomy.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

At its founding, Presidente Prudente originally spanned approximately 15,600&nbsp;km², with its boundaries reaching the Aguapeí-Peixe watershed to the north, the [[Paranapanema River]] to the south, the municipalities of [[Campos Novos Paulista]] (formerly Campos Novos do Paranapanema) and Conceição do Monte Alegre to the east, and the [[Paraná River]] to the west.

Changes in the territorial-administrative framework progressively reduced the municipality's area due to successive divisions by state laws. By 1960, it reached its current configuration. Throughout its territorial expansion, the city gradually absorbed the rural area. This rural dominance has been increasingly replaced by the urban area to meet the demands of urban expansion, driven by the growth of productive activities (industry, commerce, and services) and the rising demand for housing due to population concentration.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

== Geography ==
[[File:Presidente Prudente (SP) e vizinhos.svg|thumb|Map of Presidente Prudente (SP) in red and neighboring municipalities in blue|alt=Map showing Presidente Prudente and its neighbors]]

Presidente Prudente is located in the western region of São Paulo state, within the [[List of microregions of São Paulo (state)|mesoregion and microregion]] of the same name, approximately 558 kilometers from [[São Paulo]], the state capital, and 979 kilometers from [[Brasília]], the federal capital.<ref name="Distância rodoviária"/> It occupies an area of {{convert|562.974|km2|abbr=on}}, and is bordered by the municipalities of [[Flora Rica]], [[Flórida Paulista]], and [[Mariápolis]] to the north; [[Pirapozinho]], [[Anhumas]], and [[Regente Feijó]] to the south; [[Caiabu]] to the east; and [[Alfredo Marcondes]], [[Álvares Machado]], and [[Santo Expedito]] to the west.<ref name="Museus_História"/>

The municipality lies within the hydrographic basin of the Paraná River, with several sub-basins of small and medium streams playing significant roles in its configuration. It is drained by the Veado and Cedro streams, part of the [[Santo Anastácio River]] basin, and the Onça stream and Mandaguari River, which belong to the [[Rio do Peixe (Paraná River tributary)|Peixe River]] basin. The city's terrain is gently undulating, with frequent [[hills]], most of which are divided by watercourses. The soil is classified as Bauru sandstone, with agricultural characteristics suitable for various crops. The highest point in the city is at 472 meters.<ref name="Museus_História">{{cite web |url=http://museu.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/historiapp.php |title=História de Presidente Prudente |author=Secretaria Municipal de Cultura |access-date=11 November 2010 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140427203253/http://museu.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/historiapp.php |archive-date=27 April 2014}}</ref>

The climate of Presidente Prudente is classified as [[Tropical climate|tropical rainy]] (type ''Aw'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]),<ref name="Unicamp_Köppen">{{cite web |url=http://www.cpa.unicamp.br/outras-informacoes/clima-dos-municipios-paulistas.html |title=Classificação Climática de Köppen do Estado de São Paulo |author=Centro de Pesquisas Meteorológicas e Climáticas Aplicadas à Agricultura (CEPAGRI) |access-date=19 June 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080619011540/http://www.cpa.unicamp.br/outras-informacoes/clima-dos-municipios-paulistas.html |archive-date=19 June 2008}}</ref> characterized by rainy, hot summers and dry, cooler winters, with an average annual temperature of approximately {{convert|23|°C|°F|abbr=on}}.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010">{{cite web|url=https://portal.inmet.gov.br/normais|title=NORMAIS CLIMATOLÓGICAS DO BRASIL|publisher=INMET|access-date=18 July 2020}}</ref> The annual [[precipitation]] index is {{convert|1340|mm|in|abbr=on}}, concentrated in the spring and summer months,<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/> with a significant decrease during winter, when low [[humidity|relative humidity]] levels are common.<ref name="Umidade-baixa">{{cite web |author= |date=27 August 2010 |title=Com 8%, umidade do ar em Presidente Prudente é mais seca do que a do Saara |url=http://noticias.r7.com/sao-paulo/noticias/com-8-umidade-do-ar-em-presidente-prudente-e-mais-seca-do-que-a-do-saara-20100827.html |access-date=11 November 2010 |website=R7}}</ref> The annual [[insolation]] is approximately {{convert|2600|hours|abbr=on}}.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/>

According to data from the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET) from January 1961 to May 2012, the lowest recorded temperature in Presidente Prudente was {{convert|-1.8|°C|°F|abbr=on}} on 18 July 1975,<ref name="BDMEP-INMET"/> a day marked by a [[black frost]] event associated with a major cold wave that kept temperatures extremely low across much of Brazil.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.eco-carroagem.com.br/suacidade_cidade.asp?s=3&ss=1&uf=25&sc=11334 |title=Sobre a cidade de Presidente Prudente |access-date=27 April 2014 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20140427205708/http://www.eco-carroagem.com.br/suacidade_cidade.asp?s=3&ss=1&uf=25&sc=11334 |archive-date=27 April 2014}}</ref> Another sub-zero record was {{convert|-0.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}} on 20 July 1981.<ref name="BDMEP-INMET"/> The highest recorded temperature was {{convert|41.8|°C|°F|abbr=on}} on 6 October 2020.<ref name="INMET-A707-Presidente-Prudente-SP"/>

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Hotel Ibis.JPG|thumb|left|Typical ''[[Cerrado]]'' vegetation during the [[dry season]] in Presidente Prudente, with the Ibis Hotel in the background|alt=Cerrado vegetation with Hotel Ibis|277x277px]]

The rural area of Presidente Prudente is characterized by pastures and sparse tree cover, with the soil covered year-round. The municipality exhibits significant diversity in [[land use]], as older neighborhoods are densely populated with considerable tree cover along streets and in [[backyard]]s. Newer neighborhoods, which form the majority, feature sparse constructions with lawns and scattered tree cover. Housing complexes are densely built. Land use in the city is not highly diversified, with residential use predominating over other uses such as industry, commerce, and services.<ref name="UFSC_IlhasDeCalor">{{cite journal |url=http://www.periodicos.ufsc.br/index.php/geosul/article/view/13307/12269 |title=Intensidade e forma da ilha de calor urbana em Presidente Prudente/SP: episódios de inverno |author=Margarete Cristiane de Costa Trindade Amorim |journal=Geosul |date=2005 |volume=20 |issue=39 |pages=64–82 |publisher=[[Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina]] (UFSC) |access-date=12 November 2010 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6AnhWJMj3?url=http://www.periodicos.ufsc.br/index.php/geosul/article/view/13307/12269 |archive-date=19 September 2012 |url-status=live}}</ref>

To implement a more robust environmental management system, the municipal government, through the Municipal Secretariat of the Environment, invests in cleaning public spaces, including the removal of shrubs, litter, and debris, as well as the eradication of ''[[leucaena]]'' and other non-native species, while creating green areas in the urban zone.

Several vacant lots within the urban perimeter are covered with native ''Cerrado'' vegetation and are maintained and protected by municipal environmental preservation agencies, such as the Municipal Secretariat of the Environment. Parts of neighborhoods such as Jardim Planalto, Brasil Novo, Jardim Paraíso, and Vale do Sol had their lots fenced with wire in 2010.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=5562 |title=Prudente avança na área de proteção ambiental e protege matas nativas na área urbana |author=Prefeitura |date=22 March 2010 |access-date=12 November 2010 |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6AnhcoRGh?url=http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=5562 |archive-date=19 September 2012 |url-status=live}}</ref>

The Municipality of Presidente Prudente has adopted increasingly stringent and contradictory environmental and tax policies. Councilor Valmir da Souza Pinto proposed a bill stipulating that property owners who fail to adopt robust environmental management practices will pay higher property taxes (IPTU). The bill was voted on 17 March 2015 and reported on 31 March of the same year.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/presidente-prudente-regiao/noticia/2015/03/camara-de-pres-prudente-aprova-iptu-ecologico-em-1-discussao.html |title=Câmara de Pres. Prudente aprova 'IPTU Ecológico' em 1ª discussão |date=31 March 2015 |access-date=1 April 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br/index2.php?pag=T1RVPU9EZz1PV0k9T1RrPU9UUT1OMlE9T0dNPU9XST1PR1U9T0dNPU9HWT1PV009T1dZPQ==&&id=5627 |title=Câmara de Prudente aprova 18 projetos em Sessão Ordinária |date=17 March 2015 |access-date=1 April 2015}}</ref>

According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], in 2014, the population of the municipality was {{formatnum:220599}} inhabitants, making it the [[List of municipalities in São Paulo|36th most populous in the state]] with a population density of 367.7 inhabitants per km². According to the 2000 census, 48.22% of the population were male (91,797 inhabitants) and 51.78% were female (97,389 inhabitants). Approximately 97.91% (185,229 inhabitants) resided in the urban area, while 2.09% (3,957 inhabitants) lived in the rural area.<ref name="População_Sexo"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://200.252.8.174/demografia/mu_dem_pop_urbana.asp?iIdMun=100135467|title=Demografia – População Urbana|author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CMN)|accessdate=23 October 2010|archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/69wBcxBrL?url=http://200.252.8.174/demografia/mu_dem_pop_urbana.asp?iIdMun=100135467|archivedate=15 August 2012|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://200.252.8.174/demografia/mu_dem_pop_rural.asp?iIdMun=100135467|title=Demografia – População Rural|author=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CMN)|accessdate=23 October 2010|archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/69wBgjWkr?url=http://200.252.8.174/demografia/mu_dem_pop_rural.asp?iIdMun=100135467|archivedate=15 August 2012|url-status=live}}</ref>

The Municipal [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Presidente Prudente is considered high by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). In 2010, its value was 0.806, ranking it as the [[List of municipalities in São Paulo by HDI|thirteenth highest in the state]], ahead of the capital, São Paulo, which ranks fourteenth.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> In 2010, the social index was 0.785.<ref name="Ipeadata_IDHRS">{{cite web |author=Ipeadata |date=2007 |title=IDHM – Índice de Desenvolvimento Humano Municipal |url=https://www.ipeadata.gov.br/Default.aspx |accessdate=23 October 2010}}</ref> The city has most indicators above the national average, according to the UNDP. The per capita income is 14,652.00 [[Brazilian real|reais]]. The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], is 0.46, where 1.00 is the worst and 0.00 is the best.<ref name="GINI" /> The poverty incidence, as measured by the IBGE, is 14.47%, with a lower limit of 10.10%, an upper limit of 18.85%, and a subjective poverty incidence of 10.84%.<ref name="GINI">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Indicadores sociais dos municípios brasileiros |author=IBGE |accessdate=23 October 2010}}</ref> In 2000, the population of Presidente Prudente consisted of 135,104 Whites (71.41%), 7,045 Blacks (3.72%), 39,965 ''[[Pardo]]s'' (21.12%), 194 Indigenous (0.10%), 5,777 Asians (3.05%), and 1,100 undeclared (0.58%).<ref name="SIDRA_Raça">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=2094&z=cd&o=7&i=P |title=População de Presidente Prudente por raça e cor |author=Sidra (Sistema IBGE de Recuperação de Dados Automática) |date=2000 |accessdate=23 October 2010}}</ref>

== Politics ==
[[File:Prefeitura de Presidente Prudente.JPG|left|thumb|Presidente Prudente City Hall]]
The executive power of the municipality of Presidente Prudente is represented by the [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]], assisted by their cabinet of secretaries.<ref name="LeiOrgânica_PresidentePrudenteSP"/> The first municipal mayor was Pedro de Mello Machado, affiliated with the [[Paulista Republican Party]] (PRP), serving from August to December 1923.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sinomar.com.br/pp_prudente1.asp|title=CONHEÇA PRESIDENTE PRUDENTE|accessdate=27 April 2014|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20140427231202/http://www.sinomar.com.br/pp_prudente1.asp|archivedate=27 April 2014}}</ref> The current mayor, elected in the 2024 municipal elections, is Milton Carlos de Mello, popularly known as Tupã, elected by the [[Republicans (Brazil)|Republicans]] party with 52.81% of the valid votes.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/presidente-prudente-regiao/eleicoes/2024/noticia/2024/10/06/tupa-e-eleito-prefeito-de-presidente-prudente.ghtml|title=Tupã é eleito prefeito de Presidente Prudente | date=6 October 2024|accessdate=3 January 2025|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/presidente-prudente-regiao/noticia/2025/01/01/cerimonia-oficial-empossa-prefeito-vice-prefeito-e-vereadores-para-o-mandato-de-2025-a-2028-em-presidente-prudente.ghtml|title=Cerimônia oficial empossa prefeito, vice-prefeito e vereadores para o mandato de 2025 a 2028 em Presidente Prudente | date=1 January 2025|accessdate=3 January 2025|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref>

The [[legislative power]] is exercised by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of thirteen councilors elected for four-year terms.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br/index2.php?pag=T1RjPU9EZz1PVFU9T0dFPU9EWT1PR0k9T1RZPU9XST1PR1U9T1dVPQ==|title=Vereadores|publisher=Câmara de Presidente Prudente|accessdate=27 April 2014}}</ref> In the current legislature, which began in 2025, the chamber consists of three seats for the Republicans, three for the [[Progressistas]] (PP), two for [[Brazil Union]] and the Social Democratic Party, and one seat each for the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement]] (MDB), [[Podemos (Brazil)|Podemos]] (PODE), and the [[Liberal Party (Brazil, 2006)|Liberal Party]] (PL).<ref>{{cite web|title=CÂMARA MUNICIPAL DE PRESIDENTE PRUDENTE|url=https://www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br/site/Legislaturas/VIGESIMA-PRIMEIRA-CÂMARA---17-LEGISLATURA/?pag=T0dRPU9EZz1PR0U9T0dVPU9UZz1PVE09T0RnPU9XUT1PV1U9T1dRPU9HTT1ZVEE9&view=DET-LEG&idleg=21|website=www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=21 December 2019}}</ref> The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on laws fundamental to the administration and the executive, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).<ref name="LeiOrgânica_PresidentePrudenteSP" />

Presidente Prudente is governed by an organic law, enacted on 5 April 1990,<ref name="LeiOrgânica_PresidentePrudenteSP">{{cite web|url=http://www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br/arquivo_legislativo.php|title=Lei Orgânica do Município de Presidente Prudente|publisher=Câmara de Presidente Prudente|accessdate=24 October 2010|archive-date=6 February 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170206105112/http://www.camarapprudente.sp.gov.br/arquivo_legislativo.php|url-status=dead}}</ref> and serves as the seat of a ''[[comarca]]'', classified as fourth grade.<ref name="IBGE_História"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nossosaopaulo.com.br/Reg_01/Reg01_PresidentePrudente.htm|title=PRESIDENTE PRUDENTE – SP|accessdate=15 August 2012}}</ref> According to the [[Superior Electoral Court]], in November 2019, the municipality had 176,219 registered voters, representing 0.530% of the total voters in the state of São Paulo.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/consulta-quantitativo|title=Consulta Quantitativo|date=November 2019|author=[[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE)|accessdate=20 December 2019|archive-date=10 June 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140610023817/http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/consulta-quantitativo|url-status=dead}}</ref>

== Administrative divisions ==
Presidente Prudente is officially divided into five districts: Ameliópolis, Eneida, Floresta do Sul, and Montalvão, in addition to the seat district. When the city was emancipated, it consisted solely of the seat district, established by State Law No. {{formatnum:1798}} on 28 November 1921, and implemented on 13 March 1923. The Judicial District, classified as fourth grade, was created by Law No. {{formatnum:1887}} on 8 December 1922.<ref name="IBGE_História">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=354140# |title=History |author=Cities@ – IBGE |accessdate=12 November 2010}}</ref>

The city is also subdivided into approximately 220 neighborhoods,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.grandebrasil.com.br/Brasil/Cidades/?Municipio_de_Presidente_Prudente,_estado_de_Sao_Paulo_(SP)+9532&Grupo=4 |title=Presidente Prudente |author=GrandeBrasil.com |accessdate=12 November 2010}}</ref> with the largest and most populous, according to the city hall, being Ana Jacinta.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=4362 |title=Minister promises funds for the Youth Center in Ana Jacinta |author=City Hall |date=21 December 2009 |accessdate=12 November 2010}}</ref> The poorest region of the municipality comprises the neighborhoods Jardim Santa Mônica and Vila Furquim, where many families live in precarious conditions. The eastern part of the city also includes areas marked by a [[social exclusion]] map. In these areas, the levels of exclusion and social vulnerability are stark, with visible manifestations of social issues, where entire families rely on the solidarity and charity of others, as many residents' rights are often disregarded or entirely absent from the community.<ref>{{cite web |author=Casa da Sopa Francisco de Assis |title=Educating for Renewal Project |url=http://www.casofa.org.br/projetosocioambiental.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090410200832/http://www.casofa.org.br/projetosocioambiental.html |archive-date=10 April 2009 |accessdate=12 November 2010}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The gross domestic product (GDP) of Presidente Prudente ranks as the 128th largest in Brazil,<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> with a strong emphasis on the [[service sector]]. According to 2005 data from the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], the municipality had a GDP of R$2,971,249,000.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> Of this total, R$327,020,000 consisted of taxes on products net of subsidies.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities |author=Cities@ – IBGE |date=2005 |accessdate=29 October 2010}}</ref> The per capita GDP is R${{formatnum:14652}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> Among Brazil's 5,565 municipalities, Presidente Prudente holds the 27th position in the ranking of the most promising cities for building a professional career, according to a study by the [[Getúlio Vargas Foundation]], published in Você S.A magazine.<ref name="VocêSA_Médias">{{cite web |url=http://vocesa.abril.com.br/desenvolva-sua-carreira/materia/destaque-medias-485104.shtml |title=Spotlight on Medium-Sized Cities |author=Roseli Loturco |date=7 August 2009 |publisher=Você S.A |accessdate=13 November 2010 |archive-date=11 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110811072411/http://vocesa.abril.com.br/desenvolva-sua-carreira/materia/destaque-medias-485104.shtml |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In 2022, the city exported US$141&nbsp;million in products and imported US$16.6&nbsp;million, resulting in a trade surplus of US$124.7&nbsp;million.<ref name="DIÁRIO DE PRUDENTE">{{cite web|url=https://www.diariodeprudente.com/cidade/prudente-tem-o-melhor-trimestre-de-exportacoes-dos-ultimos-6-anos/|title=Prudente records its best export quarter in the last 6 years|date=13 April 2023 |publisher=Diário de Prudente|accessdate=23 April 2023}}</ref>

Agriculture is the least significant sector of Presidente Prudente's economy. Of the municipality's total GDP, R$23,232,000 represents the gross value added by agriculture.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], in 2008, the municipality had a herd of 52,607 cattle, 1,620 [[Equidae|horses]], 300 pigs, 66 goats, 55 [[Bubalina|buffaloes]], seven donkeys, 181 [[mule]]s, 820 sheep, and 11,500 poultry, including 7,400 [[Chicken|hens]] and 4,100 [[rooster]]s, broilers, and chicks.<ref name="Agropecuária" /> In 2007, the city produced 2,750,000 liters of milk from 3,680 [[Cattle|cows]]. Additionally, 89,000 dozens of chicken eggs and 905 kilograms of [[Honey|bee honey]] were produced.<ref name="Agropecuária">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2008&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Livestock 2008 |author=Cities@ – IBGE |date=2008 |accessdate=29 October 2010}}</ref> In temporary crops, the main products are [[sugarcane]] (200,000 tons), [[sweet potato]] (9,600 tons), and [[cassava]] (1,250 tons).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2009&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Temporary Crops 2009 |author=Cities@ – IBGE |date=2008 |accessdate=29 October 2010}}</ref>

=== Secondary sector ===
The [[Secondary sector of the economy|industrial sector]] is currently the second most significant contributor to Presidente Prudente's economy. R${{formatnum:436375}} of the municipal GDP comes from the gross value added by industry (secondary sector).<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> According to the city hall's registry, there are 445 industries in the municipality. This figure accounts for approximately 58% of the industries affiliated with [[Federação das Indústrias do Estado de São Paulo|Fiesp]]/Ciesp in the Western São Paulo region. Small and micro-enterprises are prominent in the city's industrial landscape. A significant portion of the secondary sector's contribution comes from the four industrial districts, which collectively cover an area of 45 ''[[alqueire]]s''.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.prudensite.com.br/industria.asp |title=Industry |author=Prudente Site |accessdate=29 October 2010 |archive-date=1 November 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091101041332/http://www.prudensite.com.br/industria.asp |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The NIPP I (Antônio Crepaldi Presidente Prudente Industrial Nucleus) spans approximately 20 ''alqueires'' and houses 41 industries from various sectors. These include factories that produce fire extinguishers; furniture, mortar, and beverage production; metal structures; cleaning products; agricultural products; electrical goods; and industrial equipment. The NIPP II (Presidente Prudente Industrial Nucleus) covers 18 ''alqueires'' but is still in the implementation phase. The NIPP III (Belmiro Maganini Non-Polluting Industrial Nucleus) occupies about four ''alqueires'' and hosts 60 industries in sectors such as electronics, road equipment, industrial kitchens, clothing, hospital equipment, metal frames, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, footwear, soft drinks, sawmills, and furniture. The NIPP IV (Antônio Onofre Gerbasi Non-Polluting Industrial District), covering approximately three ''alqueires'', is home to 52 industries, including [[precast concrete]], [[Electric battery|batteries]], seed production, [[Concrete slab|slabs]], [[Mortar (masonry)|mortar]], stone cutting, marble and granite processing, wood, and furniture manufacturing.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=7521 |title=Western Zone Industrial District Concentrates the Largest Number of Industries in Prudente |author=City Hall |date=23 July 2010 |accessdate=29 October 2010}}</ref>

== Infrastructure ==
In 2000, the city had {{formatnum:55178}} [[Housing|households]], including apartments, houses, and rooms. Of these, 39,445 were owned properties, with {{formatnum:32076}} fully paid (58.13%), 7,369 under acquisition (13.75%), and 10,445 [[Renting|rented]] (18.93%). A total of 5,187 properties were provided, with 810 supplied by employers (1.47%) and 4,377 provided in other ways (7.93%). Another 101 were occupied in other forms (0.18%).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100135467 |title=Infrastructure – Permanent Private Households and Residents |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|accessdate=30 October 2010}}</ref> The municipality has treated water, electricity, [[Sewage|sewage systems]], [[Waste management|urban cleaning]], [[Landline|fixed telephony]], and [[Mobile network|mobile telephony]]. In 2000, 96.84% of households were served by the [[Drinking water|general water supply network]];<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_abastecimento.asp?iIdMun=100135467 |title=Infrastructure – Water Supply |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|accessdate=30 October 2010}}</ref> 97.29% of households had garbage collection;<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_lixo.asp?iIdMun=100135467 |title=Infrastructure – Waste Disposal |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|accessdate=30 October 2010}}</ref> and 95.01% of residences had sanitary sewage systems.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_esgotamento.asp?iIdMun=100135467 |title=Infrastructure – Sanitary Sewage |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CMN)|accessdate=30 October 2010}}</ref>

=== Healthcare ===
[[File:Hospital Regional do Câncer de Presidente Prudente.JPG|thumb|left|Esperança Regional Hospital of Presidente Prudente, formerly known as Cancer Hospital]]

In 2005, the municipality had 91 healthcare facilities, with 58 private and 33 public establishments, including hospitals, [[Emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[Dentistry|dental services]]. These facilities provided 1,294 hospital beds, with 71 public and 1,223 private.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Health Services 2005 |author=Cities@ – IBGE |date=2005 |accessdate=30 October 2010}}</ref> The city has eight general hospitals, six public, one private, and one philanthropic. Presidente Prudente also has 1,425 nursing assistants, 408 general practitioners, 236 pediatricians, 221 gynecologist-obstetricians, 218 nurses, and 1,063 professionals in other categories, totaling 3,571 healthcare workers. In 2008, 2,667 live births were recorded, with 8.4% premature, 77.1% delivered by caesarean section, and 13.4% born to mothers aged 10 to 19 (0.3% aged 10 to 14). The crude birth rate is 12.9.<ref name="Datasus_SaúdePP">{{cite web |author=DATASUS |title=Health Information Booklet – General Information |url=http://tabnet.datasus.gov.br/tabdata/cadernos/SP/SP_Presidente_Prudente_Geral.xls |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090920051955/http://tabnet.datasus.gov.br/tabdata/cadernos/SP/SP_Presidente_Prudente_Geral.xls |archive-date=20 September 2009 |accessdate=30 October 2010 |format=[[Microsoft Excel|xls]]}}</ref>

The former University Hospital (now Regional Hospital – HR) was purchased in 2010 for R$78&nbsp;million by the São Paulo State Government, which invested millions in renovations. The HR serves as a reference for 45 municipalities in Western São Paulo and currently has 550 beds, all under the Unified Health System (SUS), including 56 Intensive Care Unit (ICU) beds, with 20 for adults, 10 for coronary care, 6 for pediatrics, and 20 for neonates. It offers medical residency programs in Cardiology, General Surgery, Plastic Surgery, Vascular Surgery, Dermatology, Gynecology and Obstetrics, Infectious Diseases, Neurosurgery, Ophthalmology, Orthopedics, Otorhinolaryngology, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, Urology, ICU, Pediatric ICU, and Neonatology.<ref>{{cite web|url = http://www.hrpresidenteprudente.org.br/pagina.aspx?id=114|title = HR|author = |accessdate = }}</ref>

=== Education ===
[[File:FCT UNESP Presidente Prudente.JPG|thumb|Faculty of Science and Technology, [[São Paulo State University|UNESP]] Presidente Prudente campus]]
[[File:Unoeste - Campus II.JPG|thumb|Block III of UNOESTE Campus II]]

The Municipal Education Secretariat of Presidente Prudente (Seduc), legally established by Law No. 2296/83 on 21 December 1983, provides training courses for teachers in the municipal network, promotes literacy projects, and facilitates the use of municipal school buildings outside class hours and during vacation periods for cultural and recreational activities. The Secretariat also coordinates with federal and state agencies to establish general education and teaching programs.<ref>{{cite web |author=City Hall |title=Education |url=http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/unidade.xhtml?cod=8 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131019125022/http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/unidade.xhtml?cod=8 |archive-date=19 October 2013 |accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref>

Presidente Prudente has schools in all regions of the municipality. Due to extensive urbanization, the few residents in rural areas have easy access to schools in nearby urban neighborhoods. Education in state schools is of a lower standard than in municipal schools, but the city hall is conducting studies to improve public state education to achieve better results in the IDEB (Basic Education Development Index).<ref name="IDEB_Índice">{{cite web |url=http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |title=Basic Education Development Index (IDEB) – Results and Goals |author=Federal Government |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071009195109/http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |archivedate=9 October 2007 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2008, the municipality had approximately 40,639 enrollments, 2,673 teachers, and 193 schools in public and private networks.<ref name="IBGE_Educação"/> The municipal [[Literacy|literacy rate]] is 98%.<ref name="Museu_História"/>

The municipality is also notable for its universities, such as São Paulo State University (Unesp), Union of São Paulo Educational Institutions (Uniesp), and University of Western São Paulo (Unoeste), as well as institutions such as Antônio Eufrásio de Toledo University Center (Toledo), [[São Paulo State Technological Colleges|São Paulo State Technological College]] (Fatec), the National Commercial Learning Service (SENAC), the [[SENAI|National Service for Industrial Training]] (Senai), and Sesi, which together enroll over 80,000 students across more than 110 different undergraduate and postgraduate courses. The city has three universities, two colleges, and seven distance education colleges.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.lucrativa.com.br/faculdades.asp |title=Colleges and Universities in Presidente Prudente |author=Lucrativa |accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref>

=== Science and technology ===
Presidente Prudente is considered one of the most innovative cities in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Ortega |first=João |date=26 April 2019 |title=Presidente Prudente: Proof that Government and Startups Together Generate More Innovation |url=https://www.startse.com/noticia/ecossistema/63949/startup-presidente-prudente-govtech |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220412235802/https://www.startse.com/noticia/ecossistema/startup-presidente-prudente-govtech |archive-date=12 April 2022 |accessdate=28 April 2019 |publisher=StartSe}}</ref> In 2016, a law was enacted to promote and support [[technological innovation]], scientific and technological research, and technological development, aiming to bridge the gap between the municipal government and higher education institutions while facilitating investments in startups, research, and development.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mayor Signs Municipal Innovation Law Bringing Benefits to the Technology Sector – Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall|url=https://presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=32993|website=presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=28 April 2019}}</ref> The law established the Municipal Innovation Fund to provide financial resources for the projects of researchers and entrepreneurs, the Innovation Assistance Grant for individuals developing projects (startups), the Innovation Award to recognize successful initiatives and projects, the Municipal Council for Science, Technology, and Innovation, and the Municipal Innovation Seal for companies contributing to the municipality's development in this area.<ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date=12 April 2016 |title=Innovation Law Provides Incentives for Technological Projects in Prudente |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/presidente-prudente-regiao/noticia/2016/04/lei-de-inovacao-preve-incentivos-projetos-tecnologicos-em-presidente-prudente.html |accessdate=28 April 2019 |website=Presidente Prudente and Region |language=pt-br}}</ref> The city was the first in Brazil to enact a Municipal Innovation Law.

In 2017, the Inova Prudente Foundation, officially the Vicente Furlanetto Foundation for Education, Research, and Innovation of Presidente Prudente (FUNDEPI), was inaugurated. This physical space, equipped with infrastructure and services, aims to integrate startups, entrepreneurs, researchers, and the community, while fostering the development of entrepreneurs and small businesses, applied research for improving or creating new products or processes, and driving innovation.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mayor Signs Law Establishing the Technological Innovation Center in Prudente – Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall|url=https://presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=35680|website=presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=29 April 2019}}</ref> The facility underwent renovations, and in 2018, Brazil's first Municipal Technology and Innovation Coworking Space was opened.<ref>{{cite web|title=Delivery of Inova Prudente Renovation and Coworking Inauguration Set for Monday – Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=40532|website=www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=28 April 2019}}</ref> The space accommodates 120 "coworkers," 200 researchers, and 40 companies.<ref>{{cite web|title=Inova Prudente|url=https://www.inovaprudente.com.br/|website=www.inovaprudente.com.br|accessdate=28 April 2019}}</ref>

In early 2019, the municipal government launched the InovaTec Program, which grants exemptions from municipal fees and taxes and a 60% reduction in the calculation base for the Service Tax (ISS) owed by these companies. In practice, they pay only 2% ISS on monthly revenue, the lowest rate allowed by federal legislation.<ref>{{cite web|title=InovaTec Program Brings Tax Benefits to Prudente Entrepreneurs – Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=43144|website=www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=29 April 2019|date=1 February 2019}}</ref> The Inova Prudente Foundation was recognized as a success case at the GovTech Conference, an event organized by StartSe held in April 2019 in [[São Paulo]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Inova Prudente is a Success Case at the GovTech Conference in SP|url=https://www.imparcial.com.br/noticias/-inova-prudente-e-case-de-sucesso-na-govtech-conference-em-sp,26877|website=imparcial.com.br|accessdate=28 April 2019|last=Jornal O.|first=Imparcial}}</ref>

The municipality is also home to the Presidente Prudente Technological Incubator (INTEPP), established in 2004. Its goal is to support the creation, development, and consolidation of technology-based companies, providing physical infrastructure and services to establish new businesses, attract investments, and capture market share, generating income and jobs.<ref>{{cite web |title=History – INTEPP |url=http://www.intepp.com.br/index.php/historico/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181003180831/http://www.intepp.com.br/index.php/historico/ |archive-date=3 October 2018 |accessdate=29 April 2019}}</ref>

=== Crime and public safety ===
[[File:Brasão PMSP.PNG|thumb|left|Coat of arms of the [[Military Police of São Paulo State|PMSP]]]]

As in most medium and large Brazilian municipalities, crime is also a challenge in Presidente Prudente, although it has the lowest crime rates in São Paulo state. In 2006, the [[homicide]] rate in the municipality was 12 per 100,000 inhabitants. The rate of deaths by firearms, which was 11.8 in 2002, rose to 16.3 in 2003, then stood at 11.7 and 14.6 in 2004 and 2005, respectively, before dropping to 6.0 in 2006. The rate of deaths from traffic accidents, which was 22.7 in 2002, increased to 30.9 in 2006.<ref name="ritla">{{cite web|url=http://www.ritla.net/index.php?option=com_docman&task=doc_download&gid=242|title=Municipal Database|author=Ritla|date=2008|work=Brazilian Municipalities Violence Map-2008|format=[[Microsoft Excel|xls]]|accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref>

The decline in homicides related to urban violence is attributed to measures implemented by the [[Military Police of São Paulo State|São Paulo State Military Police]] (PMSP), such as the Digital Incident Report (RDO), adopted in 46 other municipalities in São Paulo state. The RDO standardizes [[incident report]]s (BOs) filed at police stations via intranet, stores them in databases, and allows access by other police agencies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ssp.sp.gov.br/acoes/acoes_sistemas.aspx |title=Intelligence Systems Map Crime |author=State Secretariat for Public Security |date=27 May 2010 |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/68cVG2Wqv?url=http://www.ssp.sp.gov.br/acoes/acoes_sistemas.aspx |archivedate=22 June 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to the municipal government, the [[Ministry of Cities]] invests little in security in Presidente Prudente.<ref>{{cite web |author=City Hall |date=15 May 2009 |title=Tupã Opens Workshop and Demands Federal Government Action |url=http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/eventos/oficina/noticias.asp?cod=2533 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141205071208/http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/eventos/oficina/noticias.asp?cod=2533 |archive-date=5 December 2014 |accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref>

The Presidente Prudente region has the highest concentration of prisons in Brazil, with a prison population of 18,318 inmates across 21 facilities, which have a capacity of only 13,757, meaning the prison population exceeds capacity by 33.15%.<ref>{{Cite web |date=13 August 2010 |title=70% dos presídios da região de Presidente Prudente estão superlotados |url=http://www.linhadireta.org.br/noticia/p/?id=1360 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151208235349/http://www.linhadireta.org.br/noticia/p/?id=1360 |archive-date=8 December 2015 |access-date=10 September 2025 |website=Linha Direta}}</ref> The establishment of prisons in the interior allowed the state government to address two issues with a single measure: relocating inmates away from large urban centers and meeting demands for new job opportunities in these municipalities. The prisons created an estimated 18,000 jobs for prison facilities, supported by R$230 million in investments.<ref>[http://www.abep.nepo.unicamp.br/encontro2010/docs_pdf/tema_6/abep2010_2657.pdf]. ''UNICAMP – "Prison Cities": Migration and Prisons in São Paulo Regions*'' (accessed on 27 November 2015)</ref>

=== Services and communications ===
[[File:Jardim Alto da Boa Vista (Presidente Prudente) - Agência dos Correios.JPG|thumb|Branch of the [[Correios|Brazilian Post and Telegraph Corporation]] in the municipality]]

The water supply service in Presidente Prudente is managed by the [[Sabesp|São Paulo State Basic Sanitation Company]] (Sabesp). The municipality gets 70% of its water from the [[Rio do Peixe (Paraná River tributary)|Rio do Peixe]], while the remaining 30% is sourced from the [[Santo Anastácio River]] and small underground reservoirs and springs.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://pessoal.educacional.com.br/up/3390001/2001714/t131.asp |title=Water Project |author=Cristo Rei School |accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref> One hundred percent of the urban area is served by the [[Electricity|electricity distribution network]], with 112,454 megawatt-hours consumed annually in 2001 and approximately 180,000 consumers.<ref name="Prudens!te_Perfil"/>

There are still [[Dial-up Internet access|dial-up]] and broadband ([[Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line|ADSL]]) internet services offered by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|access providers]]. In fixed telephony, the city was served by Empresa Telefônica Paulista<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/agosto/24/pag_0023_7N162SEKO0SI5eBAEP27T7ANNEG.pdf&pagina=23&data=24/08/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100023|title=List of Assets of Empresa Telefônica Paulista Incorporated by Telesp|publisher=Official Gazette of the State of São Paulo}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://acervo.estadao.com.br/pagina/#!/19730501-30088-nac-0020-999-20-not|title=Telesp Takes Control of Cia. Telefônica Rio Preto and Empresa Telefônica Paulista|publisher=Acervo O Estado de São Paulo}}</ref> until 1973, when it began to be served by [[Telesp|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]] (TELESP),<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.telesp.com.br:80/paginas/06_casasua/060202_operacional.htm|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/19980114175120/http://www.telesp.com.br:80/paginas/06_casasua/060202_operacional.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=14 January 1998|title=Telesp's Area of Operation in São Paulo|publisher=Official Telesp Page (archived)}}</ref> which built the telephone exchanges still in use today. In 1998, this company was privatized and sold to [[Telefônica Brasil|Telefônica]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://telefonica.mediagroup.com.br/pt/Empresa/Nossa_Historia.aspx|title=Our History|publisher=Telefônica / VIVO}}</ref> and in 2012, the company adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/economia/negocios/noticia/2012/04/telefonica-conclui-troca-da-marca-por-vivo.html|title=Telefônica Completes Brand Change to Vivo|author=Gasparin, Gabriela|date=12 April 2012|publisher=G1}}</ref> for its operations. Mobile telephony services are provided by various operators. There is also [[3G]] access, available in the municipality since 2009,<ref name="Sinomar_3G">{{cite web |url=http://www.sinomar.com.br/portal/conteudo.asp?codigo=2855&page=48 |title=3G Telephony Arrives in Presidente Prudente |author=Sonimar Calmona |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archive-date=28 April 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140428052454/http://www.sinomar.com.br/portal/conteudo.asp?codigo=2855&page=48 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and [[4G]] access, available since 2013.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Notícias de Presidente Prudente e Região {{!}} O Imparcial | url=https://www.imparcial.com.br/site/sistema-4g-comeca-a-funcionar-hoje-em-82-bairros-de-prudente | access-date=8 August 2025 | website=www.imparcial.com.br}}</ref> The area code ([[Direct distance dialing|DDD]]) for Presidente Prudente is 018,<ref name="Guiatel_DDD">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_sp1.htm |title=São Paulo |author=Guiatel |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archiveurl=https://www.webcitation.org/60YEsFvmQ?url=http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_sp1.htm |archivedate=30 July 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref> and the [[Postal code]] (CEP) ranges from 19,000–000 to 19,109–999.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.correios.com.br/|title=Postal Codes of Brazilian Cities|author=Correios|accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref> On 8 January 2009, the municipality began to be served by [[Local number portability|portability]], along with other cities with DDD 018 and states including [[Rio Grande do Sul]] (DDDs 51 and 55), [[Tocantins]] (63), [[Mato Grosso]] (65), and [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] (92 and 97).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/folha/dinheiro/ult91u487660.shtml |title=Portability Arrives Today in Western São Paulo, Porto Alegre, and Manaus |author=Lorenna Rodrigues |date=8 January 2009 |publisher=Folha Online |accessdate=1 November 2010}}</ref>

There are twelve channels in the [[Very High Frequency|VHF]] band – channels 2 to 13 – and 69 in the [[Ultra High Frequency|UHF]] band – channels 14 to 83. There are also [[microwave]] (SHF) and [[satellite]] channels, which offer significantly better frequency and require special receivers. These receivers typically deliver the signal to the television on a VHF channel.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/empresas/midia-e-publicidade/imprensa-e-midia/emissoras-de-televisao/ |title=Television Stations |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archive-date=29 March 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120329225217/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/empresas/midia-e-publicidade/imprensa-e-midia/emissoras-de-televisao |url-status=dead }}</ref> There is also a daily newspaper in Presidente Prudente, "O Imparcial." The city previously had "Oeste Notícias," which was closed by its management on 31 January.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalprudentino.com.br/noticia/noticias.php?id=31551&titulo=jornal-de-prudente-encerra-publicacoes-apos-18-anos |title=Prudente Newspaper Ceases Publication After 18 Years |author=PortalPrudentino|date=31 January 2013 |accessdate=4 February 2013}}</ref> The city has several online portals, the most accessed of which are Portal G1 – Presidente Prudente, Diário de Prudente, and Portal Prudentino. There are also nine radio stations in the city,<ref name="Prudens!te_Perfil">{{cite web |url=http://www.prudensite.com.br/cidade.asp |title=The City – Profile |author=Prudens!te |accessdate=11 November 2010 |archive-date=17 October 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081017000337/http://www.prudensite.com.br/cidade.asp |url-status=dead }}</ref> with the main ones being "101 FM" and "98 FM."<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.lucrativa.com.br/radio.asp |title=Radio Stations in Presidente Prudente |author=Lucrativa |accessdate=1 November 2010 |archive-date=26 March 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090326084256/http://www.lucrativa.com.br/radio.asp |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Transportation ===
[[File:Raposo tavares em PP.jpg|thumb|Raposo Tavares Highway ([[Rodovia Raposo Tavares|SP-270]]), near Presidente Prudente]]
[[File:Estação Ferroviária Presidente Prudente.JPG|thumb|View of the old Presidente Prudente railway station]]

The municipality is served by the Tietê-Paraná Waterway, which facilitates the transport of goods to [[Mercosur]] countries.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-cidade/transportes/ |title=Transportation |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=2 November 2010 |archive-date=31 December 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101231081304/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-cidade/transportes/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> Presidente Prudente is also served by Ferroban (Bandeirantes Railway S.A.) within its territory, on a section formerly belonging to the now-defunct Sorocabana Railway. However, since 1999, there has been no passenger rail transport, only freight transport.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/p/presprudente.html |title=Line History |author=Brazilian Railway Stations |date=7 August 2010 |accessdate=2 November 2010}}</ref> The municipality has easy access to SP-270 ([[Rodovia Raposo Tavares|Raposo Tavares Highway]]), connecting [[São Paulo]] to Presidente Prudente and [[Presidente Epitácio]] to [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]; [[SP-501 (São Paulo highway)|SP-501]] (Júlio Budisk Highway), linking Presidente Prudente to [[SP-294 (São Paulo highway)|SP-294]] – Comandante João Ribeiro de Barros (Alta Paulista – [[Osvaldo Cruz, São Paulo|Osvaldo Cruz]], [[Dracena]], [[Adamantina]]); and [[Rodovia Assis Chateaubriand|SP-425]] (Assis Chateaubriand Highway), connecting Presidente Prudente to [[Santo Inácio]] (PR) and subsequently to [[São José do Rio Preto]] and the border with [[Minas Gerais]]. Additionally, it has access to regionally and nationally significant highways via paved, dual-lane secondary roads. The city is also served by the [[Presidente Prudente Airport|Dr. Adhemar de Barros Airport]] {{Airport codes|PPB|SBDN}}, with a capacity for 178,926 passengers, making it the third largest in São Paulo state.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-cidade/acesso/ |title=Access |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=2 November 2010 |archive-date=13 January 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110113170401/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-cidade/acesso/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The municipal vehicle fleet in 2009 totaled 103,460 vehicles, including 65,001 cars, 3,327 trucks, 576 tractor-trucks, 7,685 pickups, 272 minibuses, 22,169 motorcycles, 3,650 mopeds, 743 buses, and 37 wheeled tractors.<ref name="IBGE_Frota">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=frota&codmun=354140&nomemun=Presidente%20Prudente |title=Fleet 2009 |author=Cities@ – IBGE |date=2009 |accessdate=2 November 2010}}</ref> The city's duplicated and paved avenues and numerous [[traffic light]]s facilitate [[Traffic|traffic flow]], but the increase in the number of vehicles over the past decade has led to increasingly slow traffic, particularly in the seat district. Additionally, finding parking spaces in the commercial center has become challenging, causing some losses to commerce, partly due to merchants and their employees occupying the limited available spaces.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.gruponoticia.com.br/view/?id=13758 |title=Unoeste Exam Candidates Face Rain and Congestion |author=Mônica Berdoni |date=21 November 2009 |publisher=Grupo Notícia |accessdate=2 November 2010 |archive-date=18 January 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118231739/http://www.gruponoticia.com.br/view/?id=13758 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Previously, public transportation in Presidente Prudente was provided by two companies: Transporte Coletivo Presidente Prudente (TCPP) and Pruden Express.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=6849 |title=Urban Passenger Transport Companies Request 31% Fare Increase |author=City Hall |date=11 June 2010 |accessdate=2 November 2010}}</ref> Currently, following a bidding process, only one company, Prudente Urbano, provides the service.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/preskowych/stories/com-tarifa-de-r-350-pruden-express-vence-licitacao-do-transporte-coletivo-em-presidente-prudente.ghtml|title=With a R$3.50 Fare, Pruden Express Wins Presidente Prudente Public Transport Bid|website=G1|language=pt-br|accessdate=13 January 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=37338|title=City Hall Approves Contract with Public Transport Bid Winner – Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall|website=www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br|accessdate=13 January 2019}}</ref> The Municipal Secretariat for Traffic Affairs (SEMAV) is the municipal body responsible for the city's traffic and transportation systems. It regulates and oversees the public transportation system and manages municipal traffic. It comprises departments for Planning, Traffic, Transportation, and Public Safety Cooperation.<ref>{{cite web |author=City Hall |title=Traffic Affairs and Public Safety Cooperation |url=http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/unidade.xhtml;jsessionid=17874D93880AB9D6881A634EA06D681A?cod=5 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303231124/http://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/unidade.xhtml;jsessionid=17874D93880AB9D6881A634EA06D681A?cod=5 |archive-date=3 March 2016 |accessdate=2 November 2010}}</ref>

== Culture and leisure ==
=== Tourism, arts, and events ===
[[File:Centro_Cultural_Matarazzo.JPG|left|thumb|Matarazzo Cultural Center, one of the city's main [[leisure]] venues]]
[[File:Parque Aquático Cidade da Criança.JPG|left|thumb|[[Water park]] at Cidade da Criança, an important recreational space]]

The city has twenty hotels, four movie theaters (820 seats), two theaters (555 seats), seven amphitheaters, five [[Library|libraries]], and two spaces for aeromodeling.<ref name="Prudens!te_Perfil"/> The municipality also has thirteen social clubs, as well as the Eduardo José Farah Municipal Stadium (the largest capacity in Brazil's interior, with 65,000 seats)<ref>{{cite web|url=http://classicoeclassico.sites.uol.com.br/templos/sp/Prudente2.htm |title=Eduardo José Farah Stadium |author=Templos do Futebol |accessdate=4 November 2010}}</ref> and the Quarto de Milha Ranch Covered Arena (the largest covered arena in Latin America).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/passeios/rancho-quarto-milha.html |title=Quarto de Milha Ranch |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=4 November 2010 |archive-date=3 May 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090503041348/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/passeios/rancho-quarto-milha.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Events such as the National Theater Festival (FENTEPP),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.fentepp.com.br/fentepp.asp |title=XVII Fentepp: Open the Door and Enter |author=FENTEPP Website |accessdate=3 November 2010 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100822035911/http://www.fentepp.com.br/fentepp.asp |archivedate=22 August 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> the Nikkei Fest,<ref>{{cite web|title=About Nikkei Fest | url=http://nikkeifest.com.br/sobre/|accessdate=28 April 2019|language=pt-BR}}</ref> the Presidente Prudente Literary Festival (FLITPP),<ref>{{cite web|title=FLITPP Presidente Prudente Literary Festival|url=http://www.flitpp.com.br/|website=FLITPP Presidente Prudente Literary Festival|accessdate=28 April 2019|language=pt-BR}}</ref> the Festival of Nations<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=7192 |title=Festa das Nações Brings Together Cuisine from 14 Countries and Expects 60,000 People Over Four Days |author=City Hall |date=3 July 2010 |accessdate=3 November 2010}}</ref> and the Expo-Prudente,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.paraguacity.com/?id=85-3701 |title=47th Expo-Prudente Runs from September 3 to 14 and Features Nearly 20 Attractions |author=Paraguacity |date=20 August 2010 |accessdate=3 November 2010}}</ref> contribute to the city's role as a cultural hub in the region. For leisure, the city offers the Matarazzo Cultural Center, a complex that includes the Dr. Abelardo de Cerqueira César Municipal Library, Profª Jupyra Cunha Marcondes Municipal Art School, Municipal Art School, Paulo Roberto Lisboa Theater, Sebastião Jorge Chammé Auditorium, Condessa Filomena Matarazzo Cinema, Os Sombras e Os Temperamentais Boulevard, Presidente Prudente Seresteiros Square Boulevard, Francisco Artoni Coreto Square, Bandstand, Multipurpose Rooms for workshops and rehearsals, Galleries, and an Atelier.<ref>{{cite web |author=Presidente Prudente Culture Secretariat |title=Centro Cultural Matarazzo |url=https://turismopp.sp.gov.br/centro-cultural-matarazzo/ |accessdate=3 November 2010}}</ref>

The city also features the Parque do Povo, Cidade da Criança (a 70-hectare green area with infrastructure, including attractions such as a cable car, ecological trail, zoo, playground, kart track, planetarium, astronomical observatory, water park, fishermen's association, Free Environmental School, and two lakes),<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/turismo-natural/parque-ecologico-cidade-crianca.html |title=Cidade da Criança Ecological Park |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=4 November 2010 |archive-date=3 June 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110603101334/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/turismo-natural/parque-ecologico-cidade-crianca.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> the Nelson Bugalho Ecological Park,<ref>{{cite web|title=Presidente Prudente Gains Ecological Park|url=https://www.saopaulo.sp.gov.br/spnoticias/ultimas-noticias/presidente-prudente-ganha-parque-ecologico/|website=São Paulo State Government|date=20 May 2016|accessdate=28 April 2019|language=pt-BR}}</ref> the Procópio Ferreira Municipal Theater<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/teatros/teatro-municipal-procopio-ferreira.html |title=Procópio Ferreira Municipal Theater |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=4 November 2010 |archive-date=29 April 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090429183509/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/teatros/teatro-municipal-procopio-ferreira.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> and the SESC Thermas.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-sesc.html |title=SESC Thermas |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=4 November 2010 |archive-date=5 October 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101005071254/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-sesc.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Sports ===
[[File:Derby Paulista (Corinthians-Palmeiras) Paulistão 2009.jpg|thumb|Match between [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista|Corinthians]] and [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras|Palmeiras]] at the [[Estádio Prudentão|Prudentão]], during the [[2009 Campeonato Paulista]]]]

Following the era of [[Associação Prudentina de Esportes Atléticos|Prudentina]] in the 1960s, the dissolution of the fully professional [[Esporte Clube Corinthians|Corinthians de Prudente]] in 2001, and the brief stint of [[Grêmio Barueri Futebol|Grêmio Prudente Futebol]] in 2010 when it adopted the city's name, Presidente Prudente is now home to [[Grêmio Desportivo Prudente]] and has a stadium, the [[Estádio Prudentão|Eduardo José Farah Municipal Stadium]], known as Prudentão, inaugurated on 12 October 1982, with a current capacity of up to 44,414 people. The first match played there was between [[Santos Futebol Clube]] and the now-defunct [[Esporte Clube Corinthians]], attended by approximately 20,240 spectators, with Santos winning 1–0. The record attendance was for a match between [[Sociedade Esportiva Palmeiras]] and [[Sport Club Corinthians Paulista]], with 45,972 spectators on 3 March 1996, resulting in a 3–1 victory for Palmeiras. In lower divisions, notable teams include [[Oeste Paulista Esporte Clube]] and Presidente Prudente Futebol Clube.

On 23 December 2010, the Flávio Araújo Athlete Training Center was inaugurated, named in honor of the local sports journalist who had a distinguished career in national radio. The center is a reference in sports, offering medical care and physical training facilities, a covered court, and a football field used as a training center for [[Grêmio Desportivo Prudente|Grêmio Prudente]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=10230|title=Official Website of the Presidente Prudente City Hall}}</ref>
[[File:Jardim Alto da Boa Vista (Presidente Prudente) - Quadra de Vôlei de Praia.JPG|thumb|Beach volleyball court in Presidente Prudente, in the Jardim Alto da Boa Vista]]

In other sports, the city is also seeing significant investments in expanding sports facilities, such as the PUM (Multi-Use Park), which includes five covered courts, a [[health club]], and locker rooms for athletes.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/turismo-natural/parque-uso-multiplo.html |title=Multi-Use Park |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=5 November 2010 |archive-date=12 April 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130412020422/http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/turismo-natural/parque-uso-multiplo.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> The Olympic Center has received investments in Olympic disciplines, such as the [[Track and field|athletics]] track and swimming and [[water polo]] pools. The People's Park courts have also been covered, and its two football fields have been upgraded with synthetic turf.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.guiapresidenteprudente.com.br/guia-turismo/passeios/centro-olimpico.html |title=Olympic Center |author=Presidente Prudente Guide |accessdate=5 November 2010}}</ref> The Caetano Peretti Municipal Stadium has accommodations for amateur athletes and a renovated field, with plans for a possible additional athletics track.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.presidenteprudente.sp.gov.br/site/noticias.xhtml?cod=5610 |title=Expansion of the Caetano Peretti Training Center to Benefit Athletes |author=City Hall |date=25 March 2010 |accessdate=5 November 2010}}</ref> The city also has twelve [[gateball]] stadiums, seven sports halls, and four baseball stadiums.<ref name="Prudens!te_Perfil"/>

=== Holidays ===
In Presidente Prudente, there are three [[Public holiday|municipal holidays]], eight national holidays, and three optional holidays. The municipal holidays are: [[Saint Sebastian]]’s Day on 20 January; the city's anniversary on 14 September; and the homage to [[Immaculate Conception|Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception]] on 8 December.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.oabsp.org.br/noticias/2002/02/08/1201/ |title=Federal Justice Releases Municipal Holidays in Courts |author=São Paulo Brazilian Bar Association (OAB-SP) |date=8 February 2002 |accessdate=4 November 2010}}</ref>
