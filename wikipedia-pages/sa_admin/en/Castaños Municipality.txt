'''Castaños ''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Castaños]]. The municipality covers an area of 7860&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 23,871.<ref>{{cite web|title=Castaños|url=http://www.elocal.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05006a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=November 8, 2008}}{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
