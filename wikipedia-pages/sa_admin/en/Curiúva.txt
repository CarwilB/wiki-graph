'''Curiúva''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the interior of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. It belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Ponta Grossa]] and the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Telêmaco Borba]] and is situated northwest of the state capital, approximately {{convert|287|km|mi|abbr=on}} away. It covers an area of {{convert|576.263|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}, of which {{convert|2085.9|m2|sqft|abbr=on}} are in the [[urban area]]. According to 2018 IBGE estimates, its population was {{formatnum:15003}} inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2018"/>

The municipal seat has an average annual temperature of {{convert|19.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, and the predominant vegetation in the municipality is the [[Araucaria moist forests|mixed ombrophilous forest]]. With 68.77% of its residents living in the urban area, the municipality had nine healthcare facilities in 2009. Its [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) is 0.675, which is considered to be in the medium range compared to the state average.

The territory where Curiúva is located began to be colonized around 1947. The original name of Curiúva was Caetê, and its earliest inhabitants were the [[Kaingang]] people. Founded through State Law No. 2 on October 10, 1947, and officially established on October 26 of the same year, it was emancipated from [[São Jerônimo da Serra]]. Today, the main economic activities in Curiúva are agriculture and service provision.

== History ==
=== Origins ===
[[File:João da Silva Machado.jpg|thumb|left|Baron of Antonina.|244x244px]]
The explorations that took place in the mid-19th century, aimed at forging paths into the interior along the [[Tibagi River|Tibagi]] and [[Paranapanema River|Paranapanema]] rivers, are the earliest historical records of what is now the municipality of Curiúva. At that time, at the request of the Baron of Antonina, the explorer Joaquim Francisco Lopes ventured into the tropical forest, crossing the Facão and Caeté ranges. The old trail, connecting the {{Interlanguage link|Campos Gerais do Paraná|lt=Campos Gerais|pt|Campos Gerais do Paraná}} to the then Jataí Military Colony, evolved into a highway due to the constant movement of numerous troops in the opposite direction.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web|url=http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/historico.pdf|title=Histórico do Município do Curiúva|date=1997|access-date=July 25, 2012|author=Prefeitura|archive-url=https://archive.today/20141205013428/http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/historico.pdf|archive-date=December 5, 2014|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Harvtxt|Ferreira|1996|pp=263–264}}</ref>

The first residents of the area were Fortunato Rodrigues Jardim and Antônio Cunha, who owned vast tracts of land in the region. Many travelers passing through the small settlement that formed grew fond of the place and stayed, beginning to establish a new way of life in a land of warm climate and fertile soil.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name=":0" />

There were no doctors in the settlement, and diseases, many transmitted by animals, were a constant issue. Healing was done solely through [[folk healing]] and blessings, with doctors or medicine traders visiting only occasionally, arriving on horseback. In 1912, a police district was established, and on November 1, 1913, the area received its first visit from a priest. In 1922, João Antônio Desidério de Oliveira was appointed as the official postman.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name=":0" />

=== Administrative formation and etymology ===
[[File:Joaquim Carneiro.jpg|thumb|left|upright|Joaquim Carneiro, the first elected mayor of Curiúva from 1947 to 1948.]]
The original name of Curiúva was Caetê, referencing the range of the same name, which in the [[Guarani language]] means "virgin forest." Under this name, the settlement was elevated to the status of a Police District in 1912 by Paraná State Decree-Law No. 985, belonging to the municipality of [[São Jerônimo da Serra]]. By Paraná State Decree-Law No. 199 of December 30, 1943, amended by Decree-Law No. 311 of February 26, 1945, the Caetê District began to form the municipalities of Araiporanga (São Jerônimo) and [[Congonhinhas]]. From then on, the district was renamed Curiúva.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name=":0" />

On October 10, 1947, through Paraná State Law No. 2, approved by the then [[List of governors of Paraná|governor of Paraná]], [[Moisés Lupion|Moysés Lupion de Tróia]], the municipality of Curiúva was founded and officially established on October 26 of the same year. On the same day, Luíz Lemos took office as the first appointed mayor.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> The first elected mayor in 1947 was Joaquim Carneiro,<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> a businessman, ceramist, and pharmacist.<ref name="Joaquim Carneiro">{{Harvtxt|Magda de Castro|2005|p=61}}</ref> However, his term was short-lived, ending the following year,<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name="Joaquim Carneiro" /> as he took his own life, overwhelmed by insurmountable issues in the cooperative he founded.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name="Joaquim Carneiro" /> In 1951, the Sapopema District was created, which was emancipated from Curiúva in 1960 to become a municipality. In 1983, Curiúva lost another portion of its territory to form the municipality of [[Figueira, Paraná|Figueira]]. Today, Alecrim is the only Administrative District.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name=":0" />

The name Curiúva comes from the Guarani language and refers to a variety of [[Araucaria angustifolia|pine tree]], which was abundant in the region during the time of settlement. The term “''curi''” means “pine cone” or “pine nut,” and “''uva''” means “tree.” Thus, the city’s name can be interpreted as “pine wood” or “pine tree.”<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name=":0" /><ref name="curiuva.net">{{cite web|url=http://www.curiuva.net/|title=CURIUVA.NET__________O Site de Curiúva-Paraná__________|publisher=Curiuva.net|access-date=November 9, 2011|archive-date=February 23, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090223183426/http://www.curiuva.net/|url-status=}}</ref>

== Geography ==
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], the municipality’s area is {{convert|576.261|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}, with {{convert|2.0859|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} in the urban area and the remaining {{convert|574.175|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} in the rural area.<ref name="Embrapa_PR">{{cite web|url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/pr.html|title=Paraná|access-date=July 25, 2012|author=Embrapa Satellite Monitoring|archive-url=https://archive.today/69QMGOG4x|archive-date=July 25, 2012}}</ref> It is located at {{coord|24|01|58|S|50|27|28|W|type:city_region:BR-PR}} and is {{convert|195|km|mi|abbr=on}} west of the [[Curitiba|state capital]]. It borders [[Sapopema]], [[Figueira, Paraná|Figueira]], and [[Ibaiti]] to the north; [[Ortigueira, Paraná|Ortigueira]] to the west; [[Telêmaco Borba]] to the south; and Ibaiti and [[Ventania]] to the east.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

According to the [[Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions|regional division in effect]] since 2017, established by the IBGE,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html|title=Divisão Regional do Brasil|author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|date=2017|access-date=February 10, 2019|archive-date=February 10, 2019|archive-url=https://archive.today/20190210133149}}</ref> the municipality belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Ponta Grossa]] and the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Telêmaco Borba]]. Previously, under the division into [[Microregions of Brazil|microregions]] and [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregions]], it was part of the Ibaiti microregion, which was included in the Norte Pioneiro Paranaense mesoregion.<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web|url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip|title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016|author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|date=2016|access-date=February 10, 2019|archive-date=November 3, 2020|archive-url=https://archive.today/20201103132852}}</ref>

=== Geomorphology and hydrography ===
[[File:Curiúva Serra do Caeté.JPG|170px|thumb|left|The Serra do Caeté is the highest point in the municipality.]]
Geologically, the municipality’s terrain is of [[Permo-Carboniferous]], [[Permian]], and [[Paleozoic]]-Permian origin. The soils in the municipality include red-yellow [[acrisol]], [[latosol]], lithic neosol, and haplic [[cambisol]], predominantly with a sandy texture.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> The municipal seat is at an elevation of {{convert|776|m|ft|abbr=on}}. The municipality’s [[terrain]] is quite undulating and almost mountainous, with elevations ranging between {{convert|500|and|900|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Curiúva is located in the Second Paranaense Plateau. The maximum elevation is approximately {{convert|977|m|ft|abbr=on}} at Serra do Caeté, and the minimum is {{convert|510|m|ft|abbr=on}} at the Tibagi River.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

Curiúva is part of the [[Hydrographic basin|hydrographic basins]] of the [[Tibagi River|Tibagi]] and [[Das Cinzas River|Das Cinzas]] rivers. The main rivers that flow through the municipality are not navigable, including the [[Laranjinha River|Laranjinha]], [[Lajeado River (Paraná)|Lajeado]], and [[Das Antas River (Tibagi River tributary)|Das Antas]], except for the Tibagi, which originates in [[Campos Gerais]].<ref name="Prefeitura_História" />

=== Climate ===
According to the IBGE, Curiúva’s [[climate]] is classified as [[Humid subtropical climate|humid subtropical]] (type Cfa according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]),<ref name="vetmedunivienna">{{cite web |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-url=https://archive.today/64OfbLt1y |archive-date=January 2, 2012 |title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health |access-date=July 25, 2012 }}</ref> with an average annual temperature of {{convert|19.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, mild temperatures, and constant rainfall throughout the year.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima |archive-url=https://archive.today/64OfcWMv4 |archive-date=January 2, 2012 |title=Clima |author=Portal Brasil |date=January 6, 2010 |access-date=July 25, 2012 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-url=https://archive.today/64Oid6wNe |archive-date=January 2, 2012 |title=Brasil - Climas |author=Biblioteca IBGE |access-date=July 25, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The warmest month, January, has an average temperature of {{convert|22.5|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, with a maximum of {{convert|27.7|°C|°F|abbr=on}} and a minimum of {{convert|17.3|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. The coldest month, June, averages {{convert|15.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, with a maximum of {{convert|21.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}} and a minimum of {{convert|9.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. [[Autumn]] and [[Spring (season)|spring]] are transitional seasons.<ref name="Clima" />

The average annual precipitation is {{convert|1442.9|mm|in|abbr=on}}, with August being the driest month, receiving {{convert|61.4|mm|in|abbr=on}}. January, the wettest month, averages {{convert|209.0|mm|in|abbr=on}}.<ref name="Clima" /> In recent years, however, hot and dry days during winter have become more frequent, often exceeding {{convert|30|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, especially between July and September. For example, on August 30, 1994, thermometers reached nearly {{convert|36|°C|°F|abbr=on}} in the middle of winter.<ref name="Simepar_Almanaque">{{cite web |url=http://www.simepar.br/tempo/clima/almanaque.jsp |title=Almanaque Climático |author=Instituto Tecnológico SIMEPAR |access-date=July 25, 2012 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120619053334/http://www.simepar.br/tempo/clima/almanaque.jsp |archive-date=June 19, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to the SIMEPAR Technological Institute, the highest recorded temperature was on November 17, 1975, when some areas reached {{convert|41.1|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. The lowest recorded temperature was on July 18, 1975, with an average of {{convert|-3.7|°C|°F|abbr=on}}.<ref name="Simepar_Almanaque"/>

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Curiúva Araucária.JPG|170px|thumb|right|Remnant of ''[[Araucaria angustifolia]]'' in Curiúva.]]
At the time of the [[discovery of Brazil]] in 1500, the municipality was covered by a single vegetation formation, the [[Araucaria moist forests|mixed ombrophilous forest]]. Over time, parts of the native forest gave way to the timber industry and the exploitation of [[Aspidosperma spruceanum|''aspidosperma spruceanum'']], [[pine]], and [[coal]], which are the main plant and mineral resources found in the municipality, as well as to agriculture and pastures for the expansion of livestock farming.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

Coal has been mined since 1941 by the Cambuí Coal Company (Figueira), and there are traces of [[uranium]],<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> although the presence of permanent preservation areas (APP) now limits which areas can be exploited, even on private property.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.jacarezinhonanet.com/php_noticiaimpressao.php?noticia=5387 |archive-url=https://archive.today/69QMLKPIZ |archive-date=July 25, 2012 |title=Colegiado começa analisar autuações para definir multas ambientais |author=Jacarezinho na Net |date=August 3, 2009 |access-date=July 25, 2012 }}</ref> [[Non-governmental organization|Non-governmental organizations]] also contribute to combating illegal deforestation, with projects in the municipality focused on environmental preservation, including native forest restoration, rural property adaptation with technicians and farmers, and environmental education in local schools.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.controlemunicipal.com.br/arquivos/e2d789e75c3d.4.vegeta%C3%A7%C3%A3o.pdf |archive-url=https://archive.today/69QMMIzs4 |archive-date=July 25, 2012 |title=3.4. Vegetação |author=Plano Diretor Municipal de Santo Antônio da Serra |access-date=July 25, 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The same census reported that {{formatnum:9573}} inhabitants lived in the [[urban area]], while {{formatnum:4350}} resided in the [[rural area]].<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010" /> According to 2018 estimates, the municipal population was {{formatnum:15003}}. Of the total population in 2010, {{formatnum:3515}} inhabitants (25.25%) were under 15 years old, {{formatnum:9237}} (66.34%) were aged 15 to 64, and {{formatnum:1171}} (8.41%) were over 65, with a [[life expectancy]] at birth of 72.7 years and a [[fertility rate]] of 2.5 children per woman.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas">{{cite web|url=http://atlasbrasil.org.br/2013/pt/perfil_m/curiuva_pr|title=Perfil - Curiúva, PR|date=2013|access-date=November 27, 2014|publisher=[[United Nations Development Programme|UNDP]]|author=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano|archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203195955/http://atlasbrasil.org.br/2013/pt/perfil_m/curiuva_pr|archive-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref>

The municipal [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Curiúva is considered medium by the [[United Nations Development Programme|UNDP]], with a value of 0.656 (the 2986th highest in Brazil). The city’s indicators are close to the national average according to the UNDP. Considering only the education index, the value is 0.546; longevity is 0.795, and income is 0.649.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010" /> From 2000 to 2010, the proportion of people with a per capita household income of up to half a minimum wage decreased by 69.1%, and in 2010, 87.2% of the population lived above the [[poverty line]], 8.8% were at the poverty line, and 4.1% were below.<ref name="ODM_1">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr|title=1 - acabar com a fome e a miséria|date=2012|access-date=November 27, 2014|author=Portal ODM|archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200055/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr|archive-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], was 0.450, with 1.00 being the worst and 0.00 the best.<ref name="ODM_Perfil">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr|title=Perfil municipal|date=2012|access-date=November 27, 2014|author=Portal ODM|archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200144/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr|archive-date=December 3, 2014}}</ref> The share of the richest 20% of the city’s population in the total municipal income was 50.4%, or 9.7 times higher than the 5.2% share of the poorest 20%.<ref name="ODM_1" />

== Politics and administration ==
Municipal administration is carried out by the [[Executive (government)|executive]] and [[Legislature|legislative]] branches.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074|date=February 9, 2006|access-date=February 22, 2015|publisher=JusVi|author=Flávio Henrique M. Lima|archive-url=https://archive.today/64iovlywZ|archive-date=January 16, 2012|title=O Poder Público Municipal à frente da obrigação constitucional de criação do sistema de controle interno}}</ref> The representative of the executive power in Curiúva, chosen by the people in the [[2024 Brazilian municipal elections|2024 municipal elections]], was Christiano Borges of the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]], who won a total of {{formatnum:3942}} votes (48.55% of the electorate), with Pithi as [[vice-mayor]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/pr/norte-noroeste/eleicoes/2024/noticia/2024/10/07/eleicoes-2024-christiano-borges-do-psd-e-eleito-prefeito-de-curiuva-no-1o-turno.ghtml|title=Eleições 2024: Christiano Borges, do PSD, é eleito prefeito de Curiúva no 1º turno|access-date=May 9, 2025|website=g1|date=7 October 2024 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20250509082550|archive-date=May 9, 2025}}</ref>

The legislative branch consists of the [[Câmara municipal|Municipal Chamber]], composed of nine councilors elected for four-year terms (in accordance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm|access-date=February 22, 2015|author=DJI|archive-url=https://archive.today/64ioyjB1v|archive-date=January 16, 2012|title=Constituição Federal - CF - 1988 / Art. 29|url-status=dead}}</ref> It comprises two seats from the [[Brazilian Woman's Party|PMB]], two from the [[Social Christian Party (Brazil)|PSC]], two from the [[Brazilian Labour Party (1981)|PTB]], two from the [[Brazilian Socialist Party|PSB]], and one from the [[Progressistas|PP]]. The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for the administration and the executive, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).<ref name="prefeito">{{cite web|url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/candidatos-curiuva-pr/|title=Candidatos|access-date=January 10, 2017|publisher=Eleições 2016|archive-url=https://archive.today/20180214014432|archive-date=February 14, 2018}}</ref>

The municipality is part of the Curiúva Judicial District, created by Decree-Law No. 5809 on June 15, 1968, following requests from local and regional authorities and leaders, and officially established on July 15, 1970.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /> It also includes the municipalities of Figueira and Sapopema.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.planejamento.mppr.mp.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=2092 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200316 |archive-date=December 3, 2014 |title=Comarca - Curiúva |author=Ministério Público do Estado do Paraná (MPPR) |access-date=November 27, 2014 }}</ref> There were {{formatnum:11360}} voters in February 2014, representing 0.144% of the total in Paraná.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/consulta-quantitativo |title=Consulta Quantitativo |date=April 12, 2013 |author=[[Superior Electoral Court|TSE]] |access-date=November 27, 2014 |archive-date=October 17, 2013 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20131017010850 }}</ref>

=== Symbols ===
The symbols of the municipality of Curiúva are the flag, coat of arms, and anthem. The coat of arms, flag, and anthem were adopted as symbols by Municipal Law No. 595 on December 1, 1994. The coat of arms is a [[modern French shield]], topped with a chief in [[argent (heraldry)|argent]] featuring the dove of the [[Holy Spirit|Divine Holy Spirit]], symbolizing the municipality’s [[patron saint]]. On the right flank of the shield is a [[fleur-de-lis]], representing social change and the happiness of the people, and on the left, a stylized sun at its zenith, heralding a future of achievements for all. In the lower field of the shield, agriculture, reforestation, and [[Araucaria angustifolia|araucarias]] symbolize key economic activities of the municipality. As supports, two factory [[chimneys]] represent the potential of industrialization, which could make Curiúva one of the most developed municipalities in the region in the near future. [[Coffee]], [[maize]], and [[rice]] with their stalks entwined represent the most important agricultural riches of the municipality. Below the shield is a scroll with the inscriptions 10/10/1947 — Curiúva — 26/10/1947, and above it is a crown with five golden towers.<ref name="Prefeitura_Símbolos">{{cite web|url=http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/municipio/municipio.html|title=Município de Curiúva|access-date=July 25, 2012|author=Prefeitura|archive-url=https://archive.today/69Qja2tnq|archive-date=July 25, 2012}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite law|jurisdiction=Curiúva|title= Lei nº 595|date=December 1, 1994|subject=Dispõe sobre a forma e a apresentação dos símbolos do município de Curiúva e dá outras providências|periodical=Diário Oficial|location=Curiúva|publication-date=December 1, 1994}}</ref>

The flag, designed by Professor José Carlos Pereira, consists of a rectangle with the municipal coat of arms in the center and five vertical stripes: [[vert (heraldry)|vert]] (at the ends), argent (in the center), and [[or (heraldry)|or]] (between the vert and argent stripes).<ref name="Prefeitura_Símbolos" /> Its colors are vert for honor, civility, courtesy, joy, abundance, and hope; or for glory, splendor, wealth, grandeur, and leadership; and argent for peace, friendship, work, purity, and religiosity. The anthem of Curiúva was written by Professor José Carlos Pereira, with music composed by the maestro from Minas Gerais, Sebastião Lima, who settled in Paraná.<ref name=":1" />

== Economy ==
[[File:Curiúva Olaria.JPG|170px|thumb|left|Ceramics industry.]]
In Curiúva’s [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP), [[agribusiness]] and the service sector stand out. According to 2012 IBGE data, the municipality’s GDP was {{BRL|152981000}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> {{BRL|6611000}} came from taxes on products net of subsidies at current prices, and the per capita GDP was {{BRL|10867.46}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3PK1I |archive-url=https://archive.today/6V0flGgjU |archive-date=December 22, 2014 |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2011 |access-date=December 22, 2014 }}</ref> In 2010, 61.76% of the population over 18 was [[Workforce|economically active]], while the unemployment rate was 6.04%.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/>

[[Wages]], along with other remuneration, totaled {{BRL|24590000}}, and the average monthly wage in the municipality was 1.8 [[minimum wage]]s. In 2012, {{BRL|53486000}} of the city’s GDP came from the gross added value of agribusiness,<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> while in 2010, 40.52% of the municipality’s economically active population was employed in this sector.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> According to the IBGE, in 2013, the municipality had {{formatnum:36395}} cattle, 630 buffalo, 500 goats, {{formatnum:1160}} horses, {{formatnum:1000}} sheep, {{formatnum:9280}} pigs, and {{formatnum:45780}} poultry. {{formatnum:2250}} cows were milked, producing {{formatnum:3350000}} liters of milk. Additionally, {{formatnum:13500}} kilograms of honey and {{formatnum:31760}} kilograms of [[silkworm]] cocoons were produced.<ref name="Agropecuária">{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3QXVF |archive-url=https://archive.today/6V0gyVLIX |archive-date=December 22, 2014 |title=Pecuária 2013 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2013 |access-date=December 22, 2014 }}</ref>

In terms of temporary agriculture, the main crops are [[maize]] ({{formatnum:20500}} tons produced and {{formatnum:5000}} hectares cultivated), [[soybean]] ({{formatnum:17350}} tons and {{formatnum:5000}} hectares planted), and [[sugarcane]] ({{formatnum:4000}} tons and 80 hectares), in addition to [[garlic]], [[peanut]], [[rice]], [[onion]], [[bean]], [[cassava]], [[watermelon]], [[tomato]], and [[wheat]].<ref name="IBGE_Lavoura">{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3QXSB |archive-url=https://archive.today/6V0gitvKs |archive-date=December 22, 2014 |title=Lavoura Temporária 2013 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2013 |access-date=December 22, 2014 }}</ref> In terms of [[Permanent crop|permanent crops]], [[coffee]] (444 tons produced and 308 hectares harvested), [[Orange (fruit)|orange]] (300 tons and 20 hectares), and [[lemon]] (180 tons and 10 hectares) stand out, with [[banana]], [[persimmon]], [[papaya]], [[mango]], [[Passion fruit (fruit)|passion fruit]], [[peach]], [[tangerine]], and [[grape]] also cultivated.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3QXS9 |archive-url=https://archive.today/6V0gkiVmx |archive-date=December 22, 2014 |title=Lavoura Permanente 2013 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2013 |access-date=December 22, 2014 }}</ref>

; Secondary and tertiary sectors
[[File:Curiúva Comércio na Avenida Central.JPG|170px|thumb|right|Antônio Cunha Avenue.]]
Industrial production contributed {{BRL|13432000}} to the municipality’s GDP in 2012, primarily from agribusiness and timber extraction, with a smaller presence of mineral, non-metallic product, metallurgical, mechanical, chemical, food, and [[textile industry|textile]] establishments.<ref name="IPARDES_Curiúva"/> According to the IBGE, in 2013, {{formatnum:142458}} m³ of [[firewood]] and {{formatnum:350149}} m³ of logs were extracted, with 32.3% used for [[pulp (paper)|pulp]] production,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3QXX9 |archive-url=https://archive.today/6V0hMSxcJ |archive-date=December 22, 2014 |title=Extração vegetal e silvicultura - 2013 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2013 |access-date=December 22, 2014 }}</ref> and according to 2010 statistics, 8.18% of Curiúva’s workers were employed in the manufacturing sector.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/>

In 2010, 7.73% of the employed population worked in construction, 1.25% in public utilities, 11.58% in commerce, and 26.81% in services,<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> and in 2012, {{BRL|79452000}} of the municipal GDP came from the gross added value of the tertiary sector.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> Since the settlement’s establishment, commerce has been a significant source of income for the population. In 1907, the first merchant, Constante José Borges, arrived in what was then Caetê from Tibagi. From 1913, commerce began to develop and prosper in the municipality, selling a wide variety of products such as food, fabrics, footwear, clothing, hats, hardware, and subsistence goods.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

== Infrastructure ==
=== Housing and crime ===
[[File:Curiúva Delegacia da PMPR.JPG|170px|thumb|left|Detachment of the [[Military Police of Paraná State]].]]
In 2010, the city had {{formatnum:4391}} permanent private [[households]]. Of these, {{formatnum:4375}} were houses, 16 were apartments, and there were no tenement houses. Of the total households, {{formatnum:3196}} were owned properties ({{formatnum:2865}} fully paid and 331 under acquisition); 651 were rented; 541 were ceded (187 by employers and 354 in other ways), and three were occupied in other ways.<ref name="IBGE_Domicílios"/> Some of these residences had access to treated water, [[electricity]], [[wastewater|sewage]], [[solid waste|urban cleaning]], [[fixed telephony]], and [[mobile telephony]]. {{formatnum:3218}} households were served by the [[Water supply network|general water supply network]] (73.28% of the total); {{formatnum:4147}} (94.44%) had exclusive bathrooms; {{formatnum:3346}} (76.2%) were served by some form of garbage collection provided by the city hall; and {{formatnum:4360}} (99.29%) had electricity supply.<ref name="IBGE_Domicílios">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=result_univer_censo2010&codmun=410700&nomemun=Curi%FAva |title=Censo Demográfico 2010: Características da População e dos Domicílios: Resultados do Universo |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=July 26, 2012}}</ref>

In 2008, the rates of [[homicide]],<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/HTN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de homicídio nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=July 26, 2012 |format=xls |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/query?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sangari.com%2Fmapadaviolencia%2Ftabelas2011%2FHTN.xls&date=2011-02-28 |archive-date=February 28, 2011 |url-status= }}</ref> [[suicide]],<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/STN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de suicídio nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=July 26, 2012 |format=xls |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/query?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sangari.com%2Fmapadaviolencia%2Ftabelas2011%2FSTN.xls&date=2011-02-28 |archive-date=February 28, 2011 |url-status= }}</ref> and deaths from traffic accidents<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/TTN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de Óbitos Ac.Transporte nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=July 26, 2012 |format=xls |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/query?url=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sangari.com%2Fmapadaviolencia%2Ftabelas2011%2FTTN.xls&date=2011-02-28 |archive-date=February 28, 2011 |url-status= }}</ref> were low in Curiúva compared to much of Brazil. Under the Brazilian Federal Constitution, the municipality has a [[Municipal Guards|Municipal Guard]], tasked with protecting public assets, services, and facilities.<ref name="segurança">{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/constituicao.htm |archive-url=https://archive.today/62wqysIsY |archive-date=November 4, 2011 |title=Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil de 1988 |author=Presidência da República |date=1988 |access-date=July 26, 2012 }}</ref> Public security services have existed since December 18, 1912, when, through State Decree No. 985, the Caetê Police District was created, with Sub-Commissioner Roberto Mathias taking office under Decree No. 996 on December 27 of the same year.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

=== Healthcare and education ===
[[File:Curiúva Hospital.JPG|170px|thumb|right|Curiúva Municipal Hospital]]
[[File:Curiúva Colégio.JPG|170px|thumb|right|José de Alencar State School]]
In 2009, the municipality had nine healthcare facilities, including hospitals, [[emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[dentistry|dental services]], with four being public municipal facilities and five private. All nine were part of the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=410700&nomemun=Curi%FAva |title=Serviços de Saúde 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=July 26, 2012}}</ref> In 2013, 84% of children under one year old had up-to-date vaccination records.<ref name="ODM_4"/> In 2012, 191 live births were recorded,<ref name="ODM_5">{{cite web |url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/5-melhorar-a-saude-das-gestantes/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200448/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/5-melhorar-a-saude-das-gestantes/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-date=December 3, 2014 |title=5 - melhorar a saúde das gestantes |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=November 27, 2014 }}</ref> with an [[infant mortality]] rate of 15.7 deaths per thousand in that year.<ref name="ODM_4">{{cite web |url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200712/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-date=December 3, 2014 |title=4 - reduzir a mortalidade infantil |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=November 27, 2014 }}</ref> In 2010, 2.67% of girls aged 10 to 17 had children, with an activity rate of 14.02% among girls aged 10 to 14.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> Of the children under two years of age who were weighed by the [[Programa Saúde da Família|Family Health Program]] in 2013, 0.2% were found to be [[malnutrition|malnourished]].<ref name="ODM_1"/>

In the field of education, the [[Basic Education Development Index]] (IDEB) average for Curiúva's public schools in 2013 was 4.8, on a scale ranging from 1 to 10. The score for 5th-grade students (formerly 4th grade) was 5.6, while for 9th-grade students (historically 8th grade), it was 3.9; the national average was 4.9.<ref name="ODM_2">{{cite web |url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/2-educacao-basica-de-qualidade-para-todos/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200523/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/2-educacao-basica-de-qualidade-para-todos/BRA004041096/curiuva---pr |archive-date=3 December 2014 |title=2 - Basic Quality Education for All |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=27 November 2014 }}</ref> In 2010, 4.16% of children aged between six and fourteen were not enrolled in primary education.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/> The completion rate for youths aged 15 to 17 was 61.6%, and the literacy rate for young people and adolescents aged 15 to 24 was 99.0%. In 2013, the age-grade distortion among primary education students, meaning those older than the recommended age, was 13.7% for the early years and 30.6% for the final years, while in secondary education, the distortion reached 27.7%.<ref name="ODM_2"/> Among residents aged 18 or older, 25.99% had completed primary education, 14.53% had completed secondary education, and the population had, on average, an expected 10.95 years of schooling.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas"/>

In 2010, according to data from the demographic census sample, out of the total population, {{formatnum:3868}} inhabitants were attending [[daycare]]s and/or schools. Of this total, 62 attended daycare centers, 201 were in preschool, 66 were in literacy classes, 48 were in youth and adult education, 2,385 were in elementary school, 602 were in high school, 105 were in EJA (Youth and Adult Education) in the former elementary school system, and 108 were in the former secondary school system. Thirty-six were enrolled in higher education specialization courses, and 254 were in undergraduate programs. {{formatnum:10055}} people were not attending educational institutions, with {{formatnum:2031}} never having attended and {{formatnum:8024}} having attended at some point.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1HNSL |archive-url=https://archive.today/20141203200603/http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?codmun=410700&idtema=105 |archive-date=3 December 2014 |title=2010 Demographic Census: Sample Results - Education |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=27 November 2014 }}</ref> In 2012, the municipality had {{formatnum:3263}} enrollments in its educational institutions, with 12 schools offering primary education, seven of which belonged to the state public network and five to the municipal network. Three schools providing secondary education were part of the state network.<ref name="IBGE_Educação"/>

== Transport ==
In December 2011, the municipal vehicle fleet consisted of {{formatnum:4478}} vehicles, including {{formatnum:2343}} cars, 214 trucks, 36 tractor-trucks, 347 pickups, 152 vans, 21 minibuses, {{formatnum:1054}} motorcycles, 132 mopeds, 75 buses, 42 trailers, 51 semi-trailers, one wheeled tractor, and ten utility vehicles.<ref name="IPARDES_Curiúva"/> The city has a [[bus terminal]], the Curiúva Bus Terminal, located in the city center on Edmundo Mercer Street, inaugurated on 29 April 2011 to replace the old one. It primarily connects the municipality to various cities in the states of Paraná and São Paulo.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.npdiario.com/noticia/201/governador-nesta-sexta-feira-em-curiuva |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69Rr4VrFH?url=http://www.npdiario.com/noticia/201/governador-nesta-sexta-feira-em-curiuva |archive-date=26 July 2012 |title=Governor in Curiúva this Friday |author=NP Diário |date=27 April 2011 |access-date=26 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The first roads and vehicles appeared in the late 1940s and early 1950s during Tobias José Borges' administration, with the first bus line being Curiúva/[[Ibaiti]], operated by Expresso Pássaro Brasileiro.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

The municipality is served by PR-160 and PR-090, which intersect within the municipality, as well as PR-962, a small access road to PR-160. The municipality was also served by rail transport from the Paraná-Santa Catarina Railway Network (RVPSC), but the railway passed through the district (now municipality) of Figueira, far from Curiúva’s center. The station, Lysimaco Costa, was inaugurated on 15 September 1948 and was the last stop on the Barra Bonita Branch, connecting Figueira to [[Tomazina]]. It operated until 1969, when train services ceased. The city where the station was located became independent on 20 April 1982.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/pr-ramalbb/lysimacocosta.htm |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69S1dQUK1?url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/pr-ramalbb/lysimacocosta.htm |archive-date=26 July 2012 |title=Lysimaco Costa |author=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil |date=15 January 2011 |access-date=26 July 2012 |url-status=live }}</ref>

Regarding air transport, Curiúva does not have an airfield. The region is served by the [[Telêmaco Borba Airport]], which offers regular flights.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/parana/voe-parana-curitiba-interior/|title=Ten more cities in Paraná's interior will have flights to Curitiba; see price range|date=6 August 2019|author=Katia Brembatti|publisher=Gazeta do Povo|access-date=19 January 2020|archive-date=18 December 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191218183211/https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/parana/voe-parana-curitiba-interior/}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.aen.pr.gov.br/modules/noticias/article.php?storyid=103181&tit=Parana-ganha-10-novas-rotas-de-voos-espalhadas-pelo-Estado|title=Paraná gains 10 new flight routes across the state|date=6 August 2019|author=|publisher=Paraná News Agency|access-date=19 January 2020|archive-date=6 August 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190806215630/http://www.aen.pr.gov.br/modules/noticias/article.php?storyid=103181&tit=Parana-ganha-10-novas-rotas-de-voos-espalhadas-pelo-Estado}}</ref> Other nearby airports include the [[Cornélio Procópio Airport]] and the [[Londrina Airport]].

== Culture ==
=== Festivals and institutions ===

Among the cultural spaces, there are four libraries,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/munic2005/ver_tema.php?tema=t8_2&munic=410700&uf=__&nome= |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69TKqyDbY?url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/munic2005/ver_tema.php?tema=t8_2 |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Cultural facilities |author=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |date=2005 |access-date=27 July 2012 }}</ref> including the Valdeci Camargo de Jesus Library,<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> as well as the Municipal Stadium and the Caetê Sports Gymnasium, although a new gymnasium with official standards is under construction.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://revelia.com.br/?pagina=posts&id=3791&tipo=Curi%FAva |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69TKsF3Nk?url=http://revelia.com.br/?pagina=posts |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Construction begins on football field |author=Revelia |date=25 May 2012 |access-date=27 July 2012 }}</ref> Another space is the Curiúva Sports and Recreational Club (CERC), formed on 6 September 1954 from the merger of the Curiúva Recreational Association (founded on 20 November 1952) and Curiúva EC (established on 2 June 1954). In addition to providing sports facilities, it hosts dances, events, and festivals.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> Amateur sports championships are also occasionally organized, such as the Municipal Futsal Championship<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=b054603368vfb0&id=1207851|title=Municipal and Regional Futsal Championship begins this Saturday|date=27 September 2013|access-date=12 February 2018|publisher=|author=Prefeitura|archive-url=https://archive.today/20180212215101/http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=b054603368vfb0&id=1207851|archive-date=12 February 2018}}</ref> and the Curiúva Regional Championship (also futsal), which includes teams from other cities.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=b054603368vfb0&id=1336863|title=2017 Regional Futsal Championship begins this Saturday|date=23 June 2017|access-date=12 February 2018|publisher=|author=Prefeitura|archive-url=https://archive.today/20180212215101/http://www.curiuva.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=b054603368vfb0&id=1207851|archive-date=12 February 2018}}</ref>

=== Handicrafts ===
[[Handicraft|Handicrafts]] are one of the most spontaneous forms of cultural expression in Curiúva. Throughout the municipality, distinctive artisanal production can be found, made with regional [[raw material]]s and created according to local culture and lifestyle. Associations bring together local artisans, providing spaces for crafting, exhibiting, and selling handmade products. These items are typically sold at fairs, exhibitions, or craft shops.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.npdiario.com/noticia/5065/associacao-de-curiuva-atende-idosos-e-mulheres |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69TKy3h8T?url=http://www.npdiario.com/noticia/5065/associacao-de-curiuva-atende-idosos-e-mulheres |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Curiúva association serves the elderly and women |author=NP Diário |date=13 July 2012 |access-date=27 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The federal Escola Ativa program operates in the city’s rural schools, offering craft and recycling courses to children and adolescents.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.expressonoticias.com.br/images/pdf/aaapagina_1_-_preto.pdf |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69TKz41L4?url=http://www.expressonoticias.com.br/images/pdf/aaapagina_1_-_preto.pdf |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Escola Ativa program revolutionizes rural education in Curiúva |author=Expresso Notícias |date=28 March 2012 |access-date=27 July 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> According to the IBGE, the main artisanal activities in Curiúva include embroidery, works with fruits and seeds extracted from nature, and tapestry.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/munic2005/ver_tema.php?tema=t8_4&munic=410700&uf=__&nome= |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/69TKzzKrN?url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/munic2005/ver_tema.php?tema=t8_4 |archive-date=27 July 2012 |title=Main artisanal activities |author=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |date=2005 |access-date=27 July 2012 }}</ref>

=== Holidays ===
Curiúva has two municipal [[holiday]]s, according to 1997 city government data, in addition to eight national holidays and optional holidays. The municipal holidays are [[Whitsun]], celebrated in May or June, and the city’s political emancipation day on 26 October.<ref name="Prefeitura_História" /> According to Brazil’s Federal Law No. 9093, enacted on 12 September 1995, municipalities may have up to four religious municipal holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6338F8rTG?url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |archive-date=8 November 2011 |title=Carnival - Is it a holiday?
