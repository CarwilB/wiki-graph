'''São Luiz''' or '''São Luiz do Anauá''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈsɐ̃w luˈiz du anɐˈwa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 7.315 and its area is 1,527&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the smallest municipality in that state. São Luiz became an independent municipality in 1982.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/sao-luiz/historico |title=São Luiz |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The municipality can be reached by the [[BR-210]] highway.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://portalamazonia.com/noticias/cidades/onca-pintada-e-encontrada-sem-cabeca-e-patas-em-rodovia-de-roraima |title=Onça-pintada é encontrada sem cabeça e patas em rodovia de Roraima|website=Portal Amazonia|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
