'''San Juan Nepomuceno''' is a city and district in the [[Paraguay|Paraguayan]] department of [[Caazapá Department|Caazapá]]. It has the largest urban conglomeration of the department.

==Geography==
It lies 249 km southeast of the national capital city, [[Asunción]], at the terminus of the Blas Garay highway. Blas Garay is a spur of the national highway Number 8 and forks at a crossroads near the town of [[Ñumí]]. This district is located in the east of the department and shares borders with six districts of [[Caazapá]] (clockwise: N: [[General Higinio Morínigo]], [[Abaí]]; E: [[Tavaí]]; S: [[Yuty]]; W: [[Buena Vista (Paraguay)|Buena Vista]], Caazapá) and one of the [[Itapúa Department|Itapúa]] ([[Alto Verá]]).
