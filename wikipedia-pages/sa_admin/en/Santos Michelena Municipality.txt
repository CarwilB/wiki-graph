The '''Santos Michelena Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]]. According to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 38,574.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[Las Tejerías]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Santos Michelena Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip] {{dead link|date=April 2014}}</ref> The municipality is named for the Venezuelan politician [[Santos Michelena]].

==Demographics==
The Santos Michelena Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 44,409 (up from 38,638 in 2000). This amounts to 2.7% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls] {{dead link|date=April 2014}}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|201.86|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls] {{dead link|date=April 2014}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Santos Michelena Municipality is Reinaldo Lorca, re-elected on October 31, 2004 with 32% of the votes.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=12&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |title= Data|website=www.cne.gob.ve |format=PDF|access-date=2020-11-23}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf|title=Info |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es: Capital Santos Michelena and Tiara.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
