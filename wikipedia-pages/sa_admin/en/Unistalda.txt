'''Unistalda''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 1,995 (2022 census) in an area of 602.39 km².<ref>[https://archive.today/20150510091250/http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2333C Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>  Its elevation is 361 m.  It is located west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]] and northeast of [[Alegrete]].
