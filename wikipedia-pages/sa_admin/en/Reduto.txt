'''Reduto''' is a municipality in the Brazilian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. It lies in the [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] region and belongs to the immediate geographic region of [[Manhuaçu]] and the intermediate region of [[Juiz de Fora]]. The municipality became independent on 21 December 1995 and was officially installed on 1 January 1997.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web |title=Reduto (MG) |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/mg/reduto.html |publisher=IBGE |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref>

== Geography and demographics ==
Reduto covers an area of about 151.86&nbsp;km². The town is located at an average altitude of roughly 600&nbsp;metres above sea level, with higher elevations reaching about 1,140&nbsp;metres in the surrounding hills. According to the 2022 census, the municipality had a population of 7,848 inhabitants, resulting in a population density of approximately 52&nbsp;inhabitants per km².<ref name="ibge" />

The climate is mild and humid, typical of the Zona da Mata region, with well-defined wet and dry seasons. The local vegetation is composed mainly of remnants of [[Atlantic Forest]] and pasturelands.

== History ==
The area began as a rural district called ''Estação'', later renamed ''Reduto'', under the jurisdiction of the municipality of [[Manhuaçu]]. It became a district by Law&nbsp;No.&nbsp;336 on 27 December 1948 and was elevated to municipality status by State Law&nbsp;No.&nbsp;12.030 on 21 December 1995. The municipal government was officially installed on 1 January 1997.<ref name="ibge" />

== Economy and infrastructure ==
Agriculture is the backbone of Reduto’s economy. The municipality is part of one of Brazil’s main coffee-producing belts, with small and medium farms dedicated to arabica coffee cultivation. Cattle ranching, dairy farming and small-scale industries also contribute to local income.<ref>{{cite web |title=Reduto – Perfil Municipal |url=https://investminas.mg.gov.br/municipio/reduto/ |publisher=Invest Minas |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref>

According to the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, Reduto’s [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) was estimated at R$106.5&nbsp;million in 2021, with a per-capita GDP around R$16,700. The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) in 2010 was 0.629, classified as medium.<ref>{{cite web |title=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano no Brasil – Reduto (MG) |url=https://atlasbrasil.org.br/pt/perfil/municipio/3154155 |publisher=PNUD |access-date=9 November 2025}}</ref>

== Culture and administration ==
The municipality is governed by a mayor and a city council. The population maintains strong cultural roots connected to rural traditions, coffee harvest festivals and religious celebrations. The town’s modest size gives it a predominantly agrarian character, with a growing service sector linked to commerce and education.
