'''Huallanca''' (hispanicized spelling) or '''Wallanka''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] for "mountain range"<ref name=laime>{{Ref Laime}}</ref> and the cactus species ''[[Austrocylindropuntia subulata]]'')<ref name=mincetur>{{cite web |url=http://www.mincetur.gob.pe/TURISMO/OTROS/inventario%20turistico/Ficha.asp?cod_Ficha=2901 |title=Jatun Huancagaga |author=<!--Staff writer(s); no by-line.--> |publisher=mincetur |access-date=May 2, 2015 |archive-date=April 17, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150417184536/http://www.mincetur.gob.pe/TURISMO/OTROS/inventario%20turistico/Ficha.asp?cod_Ficha=2901 |url-status=live }}</ref> is a district of the [[Bolognesi Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]].
