'''Cacimba de Dentro''' is a municipality in the state of [[Paraíba]] in northeastern [[Brazil]]. It is located in the mesoregion of [[Agreste Paraibano]] and the microregion of Western Curimataú, 160 km from the state capital, [[João Pessoa, Paraíba|João Pessoa]].
