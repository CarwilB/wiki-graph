'''Vitória das Missões''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 3,092 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|259.61|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1641 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is located 461&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]] and east of [[Argentina]].  Its nickname is "Progress" (''Progresso'' in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]).
