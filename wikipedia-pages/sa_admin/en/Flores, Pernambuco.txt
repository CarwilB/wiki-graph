'''Flores''' (Portuguese for "Flowers") is a municipality ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]])'' in the state of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 20,776 (2025 est.) in an area of 995.56&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]] state and [[Quixaba]]  ('''N''');  [[Betânia, Pernambuco|Betânia]]  ('''S''');  [[Carnaíba]] and [[Custódia]]  ('''E''');  [[Triunfo, Pernambuco|Triunfo]] and [[Calumbi]]  ('''W''')
* Elevation - 466 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]].
* Clima - Semi desertic ( [[Sertão]]) hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 25.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 385&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Flores''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[cattle]] and their [[milk]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]] and plantations with [[bean]]s, [[Maize|corn]] and [[guava]] (goiaba).
