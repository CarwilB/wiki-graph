'''Flexeiras''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[fleˈʃeɾɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 12,807 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/flexeiras/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 316&nbsp;km².<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 38% of the {{convert|6116|ha}} [[Murici Ecological Station]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC de Murici (ESEC) – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=ESEC de Murici (ESEC)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591553|access-date=2016-11-06}}</ref>
