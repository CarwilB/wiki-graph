'''Paso Ancho''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Tierras Altas District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It was established by Law 55 of September 13, 2013.<ref name=law>Ley 55 del 13 de septiembre del 2013.</ref><ref name=prensa>{{cite news|title=Tierras Altas, nuevo municipio chiricano|url=http://impresa.prensa.com/nacionales/Tierras-Altas-nuevo-municipio-chiricano_0_3755624441.html|access-date=May 29, 2015|work=[[La Prensa (Panama City)|La Prensa]]|date=September 17, 2013|archive-date=May 29, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150529065849/http://impresa.prensa.com/nacionales/Tierras-Altas-nuevo-municipio-chiricano_0_3755624441.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Climate==
Paso Ancho has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cwb) with moderate to little rainfall from December to April and heavy to very heavy rainfall from May to November.
