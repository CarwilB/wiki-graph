''' Nombre de Dios  ''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Nombre de Dios, Durango|Nombre de Dios]]. The municipality covers an area of 1,478.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 18,488,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Nombre de Dios|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=016|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=25 January 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200125232353/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=016|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 17,318 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Nombre de Dios|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10016a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 15, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070521210201/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10016a.htm|archive-date=May 21, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Nombre de Dios had a population of 5,302.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Nombre de Dios, the municipality had 110 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[San José de la Parrilla (La Parrilla), Durango|San José de la Parrilla (La Parrilla)]] (1,500), [[Gabriel Hernández (Mancinas), Durango|Gabriel Hernández (Mancinas)]] (1,256), and [[General Francisco Murguía, Durango|General Francisco Murguía]] (1,241), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
