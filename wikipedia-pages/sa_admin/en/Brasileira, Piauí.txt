'''Brasileira''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Piauí]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=220196|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Piauí {{!}} Brasileira|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-11}}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1592550|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra da Ibiapaba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Serra da Ibiapaba – ISA}}
 |title=APA Serra da Ibiapaba|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/594860|access-date=2017-02-10}}</ref>
It contains 26% of the {{convert|7700|ha|adj=on}} [[Sete Cidades National Park]], created in 1961.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PARNA de Sete Cidades – ISA}}
 |title=PARNA de Sete Cidades|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental 
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/596439|access-date=2017-02-10}}</ref>
