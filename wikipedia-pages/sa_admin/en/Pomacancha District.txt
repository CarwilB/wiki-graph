'''Pomacancha''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Puma Kancha''', meaning "[[Puma (genus)|puma]] corral") is one of thirty-four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Jauja Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
