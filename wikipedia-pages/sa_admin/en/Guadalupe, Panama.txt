'''Guadalupe''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[La Chorrera District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 34,242 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 18,015; its population as of 2000 was 26,857.<ref name=inec/> Guadalupe's representative between 2009 and 2012 was Sumaya Judith Cedeño.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://lachorrera.gob.pa/administracion.htm |title=Municipio de la Chorrera |access-date=2014-02-20 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140530052810/http://lachorrera.gob.pa/administracion.htm |archivedate=2014-05-30 }}</ref> It is part of the urban area of [[La Chorrera, Panama|La Chorrera]].<ref> {{cite web |url=http://lachorrera.gob.pa/conoce.htm |title=Municipio de la Chorrera |access-date=2014-02-20 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140205031053/http://lachorrera.gob.pa/conoce.htm |archivedate=2014-02-05 }}</ref>
