'''Jobabo''' is a municipality and town in the [[Las Tunas Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in the southern part of the province, {{convert|34|km|mi|abbr=on}} south of [[Las Tunas (city)|Las Tunas]], the provincial capital.

==Overview==
Jobabo is named from the [[Taíno language|taino]] word jobabol, meaning ''"a place where many jobo trees grow"''.<ref name=periodico26>{{Cite web|url=http://www.periodico26.cu/english/tunas_profile/jobabo.htm |title=Jobabo |author=Periodico 26 |accessdate=2007-10-06 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070623052104/http://www.periodico26.cu/english/tunas_profile/jobabo.htm |archivedate=2007-06-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Jobabo had a population of 40,345.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|884|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|46|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
