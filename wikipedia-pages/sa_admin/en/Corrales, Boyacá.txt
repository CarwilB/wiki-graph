'''Corrales''' is municipality in the [[Tundama Province]] of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], with a population of 2,481 according to the 2005 Census.<ref>[http://www.dane.gov.co/files/censo2005/regiones/boyaca/corrales.pdf dane.gov.co] (DANE 2005)</ref>

==History==

The locality was founded on January 28, 1782 by Vizente de Rivera y Mendoza. Three battles were fought there between 6 and 10 July 1819, during [[Bolívar's campaign to liberate New Granada]].
