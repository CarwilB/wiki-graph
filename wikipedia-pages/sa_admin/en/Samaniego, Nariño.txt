'''Samaniego''' ({{IPA|es|samaˈnjeɣo}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

Samaniego is known as the landscape's city and musical of Nariño. The majority of the city's population is dedicated to coffee and sugar cane to manufacture a handcrafted product named Panela, similar to sugar. Samaniego is known for having the principal youngest musical competition in Nariño, called the Departmental Competition of Musical Bands and Meeting of Dances in Nariño, where 20 musical bands have a competition to be the best band in Nariño and represent Nariño in other band competitions like in the Vega - Cundinamarca, San Pedro - Valle and Paipa - Boyaca.

Eight people were killed in the August [[2020 in Colombia|2020]] [[Massacre of Samaniego]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Pardo |first1=Daniel |title=Una nueva matanza de jóvenes deja en evidencia el recrudecimiento de la violencia en Colombia |url=https://www.bbc.com/mundo/noticias-america-latina-53801935 |website=BBC News Mundo |accessdate=August 25, 2020 |language=es |date=16 August 2020}}</ref> The victims are identified as Brayan Cuaran, Bairón Patiño, Elian Benavides, Daniel Vargas, Laura Riascos, Joan Quintero, Rubén Ibarra, and Óscar Obando.<ref>{{cite web |title=Violencia en Colombia: seis jóvenes fueron asesinados en otra masacre y ya son 27 las víctimas en menos de dos semanas |url=https://www.infobae.com/america/colombia/2020/08/22/violencia-en-colombia-seis-jovenes-fueron-asesinados-en-otra-masacre-y-ya-son-27-las-victimas-en-menos-de-dos-semanas/ |website=infobae |accessdate=August 25, 2020 |language=es-ES |date=August 22, 2020}}</ref>
