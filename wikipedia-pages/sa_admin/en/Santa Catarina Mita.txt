'''Santa Catarina Mita''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|207.2|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 32,172 inhabitants.

==History==
The territory of Santa Catarina Mita was originally inhabited by Pipil and Toltec people. Mita was one of the districts of the [[Chiquimula department]] formed by the government decree on 4 November 1825. On 8 May 1852, Mita district was divided into two districts&ndash;[[Asunción Mita]] and Santa Catarina Mita, and both of them were incorporated as a part of [[Jutiapa department|Jutiapa]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Asunción Mita|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Asunción Mita municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-asuncion-mita-jutiapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> On 9 November 1853 it was officially declared as a municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.guatevalley.com/municipio/santa-catarina-mita-jutiapa|trans-title=History of Santa Catarina Mita|lang=es|title=Historia Santa Catarina Mita|work=Guate Valley|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Santa Catarina Mita is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|207.2|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Santa Catarina Mita (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2203__santa_catarina_mita/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level, Santa Catarina Mita has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The district has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|26.63|C}}. Santa Catarina Mita typically receives about {{cvt|83.03|mm}} of precipitation and has 131.01 rainy days (35.89% of the time) on average annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/santa-catarina-mita|title=Santa Catarina Mita|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Santa Catarina Mita had an estimated population of 32,172 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 15,873 males and 16,299 females. About 26.8% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 8.1% was over the age of 65 years. About 49.9% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (50.1%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (85.2%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 84.1%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (94%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (4.9%) forming a minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.4%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
