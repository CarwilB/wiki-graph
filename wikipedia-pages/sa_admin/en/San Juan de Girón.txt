'''San Juan de Girón''' (better known as '''Girón'''), formerly: Villa de los Caballeros de Girón,<ref>Municipality Identification http://www.giron-santander.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=mIxx-1-&m=f{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> is a municipality of the [[Santander Department]], which is part of the Bucaramanga Metropolitan area in northeastern [[Colombia]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://giron-santander.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m1d1--&m=f#simbolos|title=Información general |work=Nuestro Municipio|access-date=June 1, 2009|language=es|publisher=Alcaldía de Girón}}</ref>

==Pueblo Patrimonio==
Girón was declared a Colombian ''[[Pueblo Patrimonio (Colombia)|Pueblo Patrimonio]]'' (heritage town) by the Colombian government in 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|date=Dec 2020|title=GUÍA: Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia|url=https://fontur.com.co/sites/default/files/2020-12/GUIA_PUEBLOS_PATRIMONIO.pdf|access-date=29 Mar 2021|website=Fondo Nacional de Turismo de la República de Colombia - FONTUR}}</ref>

==National Monument==
The municipality is known [[List of National Monuments of Colombia|National Monument of Colombia]] for its old sector characterized by its Spanish colonial architecture (cobblestone streets, houses with white walls, large dark brown and/or black doors, and clay tile roofs).<ref>{{Cite web|title=Girón - Mi municipio - Pasado, presente y futuro (in Spanish)|url=https://giron-santander.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Pasado-Presente-y-Futuro.aspx|access-date=19 April 2024|website=Alcaldía de Girón, Colombia}}</ref>
