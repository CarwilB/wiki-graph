'''Padilla Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. After the flood at the [[Vicente Guerrero Dam]] in 1970, the seat was moved from '''Padilla''' to the town of [[Nuevo Padilla, Tamaulipas|Nuevo Padilla]].<ref>{{cite web|title=DIAGNÓSTICO Y PLAN MUNICIPAL DE DESARROLLO  RURAL SUSTENTABLE|url=http://www.portalestamaulipas.gob.mx/files/inicio/documents/plan_desarrollo/30.pdf|publisher=Municipio de Padilla - Estado de Tamaulipas|accessdate=24 February 2012|language=es}}</ref>

Padilla is known for being the place where the Mexican liberator and Emperor [[Agustín de Iturbide]] was executed by a firing squad.<ref>{{cite web|last=Rosas|first=Alejandro|title=Sangre en Padilla: la ejecución de Iturbide|url=http://bicentenario.com.mx/?p=15974&cp=all|publisher=Tu Bicentenario 2010|accessdate=6 March 2012|language=es}}</ref> It is also the place where [[Manuel de Mier y Terán]], a Mexican general, took his life with a sword.<ref>{{cite web|last=González Lezama|first=Raúl|title=Manuel de Mier y Terán: la intermitencia de su "eterno descanso"|url=http://www.inehrm.gob.mx/Portal/PtMain.php?pagina=exp-mier-y-teran-articulo|publisher=Secretaría de Educación Pública|accessdate=6 March 2012|language=es|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120129173512/http://www.inehrm.gob.mx/Portal/PtMain.php?pagina=exp-mier-y-teran-articulo|archive-date=29 January 2012|url-status=dead}}</ref> The town was established on 6 January 1749 by [[José de Escandón, 1st Count of Sierra Gorda|José de Escandón]], then a Spanish Indian-fighter in [[New Spain]] and the founder and first governor of the colony of [[Nuevo Santander]]. The town was named after María Padilla, the wife of [[Juan Francisco de Güemes, 1st Count of Revillagigedo|Juan Francisco de Güemes]], the viceroy of New Spain.<ref>{{cite web|title=PADILLA|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tamaulipas/municipios/28030a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120730103944/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tamaulipas/municipios/28030a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=30 July 2012|publisher=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México ESTADO DE TAMAULIPAS|accessdate=6 March 2012|language=es}}</ref> At the time of its establishment, more than 40 people from [[Hidalgo Municipality, Tamaulipas|Hidalgo]], [[Linares, Nuevo León|Linares]], and [[Río Blanco, Veracruz|Río Blanco]] resided in the area.<ref>{{cite web|title=Historia de Padilla|url=http://www.padilla.gob.mx/padilla/historia.htm|publisher=Municipio de Padilla, Tamaulipas|accessdate=6 March 2012|language=es|archive-date=20 August 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100820001652/http://www.padilla.gob.mx/padilla/historia.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> As of 2010, the municipality of Padilla has a population of 14,020.<ref>{{cite web|title=MUNICIPIO DE PADILLA|url=http://www.campotamaulipas.gob.mx/oeidrus/pdfs/basica/padilla.pdf|publisher=Gobierno de Tamaulipas|accessdate=6 March 2012|language=es}}{{Dead link|date=April 2020 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The [[Comisión Federal de Electricidad|Federal Electricity Commission]] (CFE) reported that a [[Power outage|blackout]] that affected 12 states of the country and more than 10 million users on December 28, [[2020 in Mexico|2020]], was caused by a fire in {{convert|30|ha|acre}} of grasslands in Padilla.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Cruz Vargas |first1=Juan Carlos |title=CFE: Apagón masivo fue por incendio de pastizales en Tamaulipas |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/2020/12/30/cfe-apagon-masivo-fue-por-incendio-de-pastizales-en-tamaulipas-255326.html |access-date=January 4, 2021 |work=proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |date=December 30, 2020 |language=spanish}}</ref> However, the Tamaulipas State Civil Protection Coordination rejected the document presented by the CFE, saying the document was falsified.<ref>{{cite news |title=Tamaulipas revira versión de CFE por mega apagón y advierte sobre firma falsa |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/2020/12/30/tamaulipas-revira-version-de-cfe-por-mega-apagon-advierte-sobre-firma-falsa-255327.html |access-date=January 4, 2021 |work=proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |date=December 30, 2020 |language=spanish}}</ref>
