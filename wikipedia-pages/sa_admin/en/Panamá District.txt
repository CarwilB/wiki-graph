'''Panamá''' is a [[districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Panamá Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the 2010 census was 880,691;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=September 23, 2014}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 1,183,333.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of 2,031&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The district seat is [[Panama City]].<ref name="Statoids"/>
