'''Saramacca Polder''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Wanica District]]. Its population at the 2012 census was 10,217. And the majority of the population is of [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]] descent.<ref name="census"/> The Saramacca Polder was created between 1894 and 1904. The main function was agriculture.<ref name="encycl-1916">{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 509 - Nickerie|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=11 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The Commissaris Weytinghweg was built in 1906, and connects the polder with [[Paramaribo]]. Initially the area was mainly used for [[rice]] cultivation, but has become home to companies and suburban housing.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/wanica02.html|title=Distrikt Wanica 2|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref>
