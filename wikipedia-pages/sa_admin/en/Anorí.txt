'''Anorí''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the subregion of [[Northeastern Antioquia]]. The cloud forest near Anorí forms the majority of the range of the [[endemic]], [[critically endangered]] [[chestnut-capped piha]].<ref name="KirwanGreen399">{{cite book |last1=Kirwan |first1=Guy M. |last2=Green |first2=Graeme |date=2011 |title=Cotingas and Manakins |location=Princeton |publisher=Princeton University Press |page=399 |isbn=978-0-691-15352-0}}</ref>

The population was 14,502 at the 2018 census.
