'''Rioblanco''' is a town and municipality in the [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] department of [[Colombia]].  The population of the municipality was 19,090 as of the 2018 census.<ref name="Censo"></ref>

==Climate==
Inírida has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy rainfall except for the period from June to September.
