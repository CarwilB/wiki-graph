'''General La Madrid''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the central region of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4811|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[General La Madrid]].

The Partido and its capital city are named after General [[Gregorio Aráoz de Lamadrid]], a general who fought in the [[Argentine War of Independence]]. He served with Argentine heroes such as [[José de San Martín]], [[Manuel Belgrano]] and [[José María Paz]].
