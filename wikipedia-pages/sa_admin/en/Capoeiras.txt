'''Capoeiras''' is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 252.7&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]]  of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 20,048 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/capoeiras/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[São Bento do Una]]   ('''N''');  [[Garanhuns]]    ('''S''');  [[Jucati]]   ('''E''');   [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]] and [[Caetés, Pernambuco|Caetés]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 335.26&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 888 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River|Mundaú]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]]
* Annual average temperature - 20.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 252.7&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Capoeiras''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[tomato]]es, [[bean]]s, [[manioc]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
