'''Guarani''' is a municipality located in the Zona da Mata within the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 8,918 (2020 est.) in an area of 264&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The average elevation is 440m above the sea level within the urban limits, reaching up to 1,434m in the mountainous area located in the outskirts. The town was named after the biggest native tribe which dwelt in Brazil before the [[Colonial Brazil|Colonial Period]], the [[Guaraní people|Guaranis]].

Guarani's most iconic landscape is the Catholic Parish of the Divine Holy Ghost located in one of the highest points of the town. Surrounded by a small green garden and Marechal Deodoro da Fonseca Square and gathered with astonishing paintings as well as sculptures representing New Testament's passages the church is an attractive building which reminds the baroque churches of older cities, spread throughout Minas Gerais state, settled in the seventeenth century.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guarani.mg.gov.br/a-cidade/historia-da-cidade/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120625050127/http://www.guarani.mg.gov.br/a-cidade/historia-da-cidade/ |archive-date=2012-06-25 |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Guarani – MG » História da Cidade}}</ref> However, the area of Guarani was only settled in the 1830s, and it became a municipality on March 25, 1914, by separation from [[Rio Pomba|Pomba]].<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/5KS3 IBGE], history</ref>
