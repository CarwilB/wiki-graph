'''Trinidad''' ({{IPA|es|tɾiniˈðað}}) is a town, with a population of 20,564,<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/santabarbara/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the Department of Santa Bárbara</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]].

The townspeople claim to have Jewish [[sephardim]] ancestors; it is also home of [[General Knox]], who as a Commerce Secretary for Honduras signed the [[Knox Treaty]] between the [[United States]] and Honduras.

It is also home to a street named after Shimon Agur former Israeli Ambassador and to a sports center named Plaza Jerusalem, because it was built with help from Israel.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Trinidad municipality had a population of 19,593. Of these, 76.06% were [[Mestizo]], 22.44% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.95% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.50% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.06% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref> 

== Arts and culture ==
Trinidad is home to the ''Feria Paseo Real de las Chimeneas Gigantes'' (Royal Walk Fair of the Giant Chimneys), a tradition celebrated every December since 2001. The giant chimneys were inspired by the ancient tradition of celebrating the day of the Immaculate Conception in December. The fair features huge sculptures made out of newspaper, wire, and paint, created by local artists. Each year has a different theme, 2018 theme was ''El Circo'' (The Circus),<ref>{{Cite web|last=seguir|first=Redacción Autor marcado para|title=Deslumbrante cierre del festival de las chimeneas gigantes de Trinidad|url=https://www.laprensa.hn/honduras/deslumbrante-cierre-festival-chimeneas-gigantes-trinidad-PDLP1240719|access-date=2022-02-26|website=www.laprensa.hn|language=es-HN}}</ref> in 2017 was ''Mundo Acuático'' (Waterworld),<ref>{{Cite web|last=seguir|first=Redacción Autor marcado para|title=Trinidad se prepara para el paseo de Chimeneas Gigantes|url=https://www.laprensa.hn/honduras/honduras-cultura-folclore-costumbres-trinidad-chimeneas-esculturas-MYLP1134495|access-date=2022-02-26|website=www.laprensa.hn|language=es-HN}}</ref> in 2016 was ''Defendiendo a la Madre Naturaleza'' (Defending Mother Earth). The three-day event ends with the burning of the giant chimneys.
