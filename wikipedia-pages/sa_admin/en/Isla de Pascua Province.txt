'''Isla de Pascua Province''' (Easter Island Province) is a [[Chile]]an [[provinces of Chile|province]], an administrative division located within the [[Valparaíso Region]]. It has a 163.6&nbsp;km² area and a population of 4800&nbsp;inhabitants. The provincial capital is [[Hanga Roa]]. Besides [[Easter Island]], the province comprises the small and uninhabited [[Isla Salas y Gómez]], located hundreds of kilometers east of Easter Island. It is the only Chilean province located in [[Oceania]] and has a "special regime" status, with similar attributions to regional governments. It was created in 1976, replacing the former [[Isla de Pascua Department]].

== Communes ==
The province includes only one [[Communes of Chile|commune]]: [[Isla de Pascua (commune)|Isla de Pascua]].
