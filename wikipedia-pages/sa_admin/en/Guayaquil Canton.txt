The '''Guayaquil Canton''', officially the '''Municipality of Guayaquil''', is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] in the center of the [[Guayas Province, Ecuador|Guayas Province]] in western [[Ecuador]]. The canton was named after its seat, the city of [[Guayaquil]], the most populous city in Ecuador.

==Political divisions==
The canton is divided into 16 urban parishes and 5 rural parishes. The urban parishes make up the city of Guayaquil.
