'''Purranque''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] in the [[Chile]]an [[Los Lagos Region]], which lies on the [[Pan-American Highway]] about {{convert|65|km|abbr=on}} north of [[Puerto Montt]]. It is part of the [[Osorno Province]].

==History==
The first ethnically identifiable inhabitants of the area were [[Huilliche]]s, an indigenous people, and the land belonged to ''Cacique'' (Chief) Raylef, who was known as "''Cacique Don Raylef de Purranquil''"; Purranquil meaning in [[Mapudungun language]] “bush land”. 

The present city was founded on April 18, 1911 as ""'''Villa Lo Burgos de Purranquil'''"", by [[Tomas Burgos|Tomás Burgos]], in a plot of land {{convert|45|km|abbr=on}} south of [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]], along the railroad line between that city and [[Puerto Montt]]. On November 24, 1941 the town was elevated to the category of city by law 6402.

==Geography==
===Climate===
It is classified as [[oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: ''Cfb''), on summer, the temperature reaches to 23°C, on winter are cold in Purranque, the temperature reaches to 4°C, [[frost]] are common on winter.

==Tourism==
The city is served by [[Canal Bajo Carlos Hott Siebert Airport]], located near [[Osorno, Chile|Osorno]], and the [[El Tepual Airport|El Tepual International Airport]], located near [[Puerto Montt]]. Purranque have the catholic feast of Saint Sebastian, each 20 January.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Purranque spans an area of {{convert|1458.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 20,705 inhabitants (10,354 men and 10,351 women). Of these, 13,265 (64.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,440 (35.9%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 2.6% (529 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Purranque is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Alicia Villar Vargas ([[Republican Party of Chile|PRCH]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Purranque is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Hector Barria Angulo (PDC), Harry Jurgensen (IND), Daniel Lilayu (UDI) and Emilia Nuyado (PS) as part of the 25th electoral district, together with [[Puyehue, Chile|Puyehue]], [[Río Negro, Chile|Río Negro]], [[Puerto Octay]], [[Fresia, Chile|Fresia]], [[Frutillar]], [[Llanquihue, Chile|Llanquihue]], [[Puerto Varas]] and [[Los Muermos]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Fidel Espinoza Sandoval (PS), Ivan MoreiraB Barros ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) ( and Carlos Kuschel Silva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
