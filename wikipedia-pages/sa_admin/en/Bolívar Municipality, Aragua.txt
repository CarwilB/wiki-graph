'''Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 38,047.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[San Mateo, Aragua|San Mateo]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Bolívar Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Bolívar Municipality (disambiguation)|Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].

==Geography==
The municipality is mountainous in the center and north, but in the south it is flat due to the depression formed by [[Lake Valencia (Venezuela)|Lake Valencia]]. The temperature generally varies between 24.5&nbsp;°C and 30&nbsp;°C, while annual precipitation averages 900&nbsp;mm.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Economy==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
Agriculture and industry are the primary sources of income for the municipality, which contains some 3% of the industry of Aragua state. In agriculture, the municipality stands out for its production of [[papaya]] and [[cassava]], in which it ranks 2nd and 3rd, respectively, in the state.

==Demographics==
The Bolívar Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 42,295 (up from 39,260 in 2000). This amounts to 2.5% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|729.22|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Bolívar Municipality is Freddy Arenas, elected on November 23, 2008, with 61% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=04&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> He replaced César Augusto Barrera Ramirez shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]; Capital Bolívar.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
