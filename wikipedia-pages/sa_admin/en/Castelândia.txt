'''Castelândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in southwest [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  It is a large producer of sugar cane and soybeans.

==Location==
Castelândia is located in the southwest of the state, west of the Rio dos Bois, a major tributary of the [[Paranaíba river|Paranaíba]].  It is 54 kilometers northeast of the regional center, [[Quirinópolis]] and 95 kilometers southwest of [[Rio Verde, Goiás|Rio Verde]].  The distance to the state capital, [[Goiânia]], is 282&nbsp;km.  Highway connections are made by GO-040 / [[Aragoiânia]] / [[Cromínia]] / [[Pontalina]] / [[Bom Jesus de Goiás]] / BR-452 / GO-319 / [[Aloândia]]. [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin].  Neighboring municipalities are [[Rio Verde, Goiás|Rio Verde]], [[Maurilândia]], and [[Bom Jesus de Goiás]].

==The economy==
The economy is based on cattle raising (22,000 head in 2006), poultry production, and growing of sugar cane, corn, and soybeans.  There was 01 financial institution in 2007—BRADESCO S.A

==History==
Castelândia was created as a district belonging to the municipality of [[Rio Verde, Goiás|Rio Verde]] in 1963.  In 1993 it was dismembered from Rio Verde to become its own municipality.
