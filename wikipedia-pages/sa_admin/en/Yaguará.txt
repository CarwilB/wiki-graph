'''Yaguará''' ({{IPA|es|ɟʝaɣwaˈɾa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Huila Department]], [[Colombia]]. The urban centre is located at an elevation of {{convert|650|m|ft}} in the [[Magdalena River|Magdalena River Valley]] and the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. The municipality borders [[Teruel, Huila|Teruel]] and [[Palermo, Huila|Palermo]] in the north, [[Gigante, Huila|Gigante]], [[Hobo, Huila|Hobo]] and [[Tesalia]] in the south, [[Campoalegre]] and Hobo in the east and Tesalia and [[Iquira]] in the west. The departmental capital [[Neiva]] is {{convert|49|km|mi}} to the northeast. The [[Betania Reservoir]] is situated within the boundaries of Yaguará.<ref name=websiteYaguara>[http://www.yaguara-huila.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Yaguará]{{Dead link|date=July 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Yaguará is derived from the [[jaguar]].<ref name=websiteYaguara/>

== History ==
In the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], the region of Yaguará was inhabited by the [[Páez people|Páez]]. They defended their territory against the [[Pijao people|Pijao]], who came from nearby [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] and later against the [[Spanish Empire|Spanish]] [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|conquistadors]].<ref name=websiteYaguara/>

Modern Yaguará was founded on March 13, 1623 by Francisco Gómez Quintero, under the command of the governor of Neiva, Diego de Ospina y Medinilla.<ref name=websiteYaguara/>

== Paleontology ==
Fossils of the [[Late Cretaceous]] ([[Turonian]]) [[mosasaur]] ''[[Yaguarasaurus]]'' were found in the [[La Frontera Formation]] in Yaguará and the species was named after the town.<ref name=FWYaguarasaurus>[https://paleobiodb.org/classic/displayCollResults?collection_no=122676 ''Yaguarasaurus columbianus''] at [[Fossilworks]].org</ref>
