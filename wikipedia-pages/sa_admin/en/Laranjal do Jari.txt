'''Laranjal do Jari''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|laɾɐ̃ˈʒaw du ʒaˈɾi}}; 'Jari Orangery') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the state of [[Amapá]], [[Brazil]]. It is the only municipality in the west boundaries of Amapá, except for a small part of [[Vitória do Jari]]. Its population is 35,114 and its area is 30,783&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the largest municipality of Amapá.<ref name="ibge"/>

== History ==
The land was originally inhabited by Amerindians. Later businessmen set up [[Natural rubber|rubber]] plantations. The largest plantation was owned by [[José Júlio de Andrade]] who owned {{convert|35000|km2}} of land which made him the biggest landowner at the time. In 1948, his tenants revolted and he was forced to sell the land to Portuguese businessmen who sold it to [[Daniel K. Ludwig]], an American billionaire,<ref name="history">{{cite web|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/amapa/laranjaldojari.pdf# |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303215907/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/amapa/laranjaldojari.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-date=2016-03-03 |title=Laranjal do Jari|website=[[IBGE]] via WebCitation|access-date=1 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> in 1964.<ref name="upi">{{cite news|url=https://www.upi.com/Archives/1982/01/23/End-of-US-owners-dream-in-the-Amazon-jungle/4107380610000/ |title=End of U.S. owner's dream in the Amazon jungle|website=UPI Archives|access-date=2 April 2021}}</ref>

[[File:Flood Laranjal do Jari Amapá Brazil 1.jpg|thumb|left|Flood in Laranjal do Jari (2018)]]
In 1967, Ludwig conceived the [[Jari project]]. He wanted to replace the [[rainforest]] with ''[[Gmelina arborea]]'' for the [[Pulp (paper)|pulp]] industry.<ref name="history"/> A planned city called [[Monte Dourado]] was built in [[Almeirim, Pará|Almeirim]], however it was unable to provide housing for all the workers, and a [[shanty town]] called Beiradão emerged on the other side of the [[Jari River]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.sjsu.edu/faculty/watkins/jari.htm |title=The Jari Project of Brazil|website=San José State University |access-date=2 April 2021}}</ref> The project turned into a major money losing failure, and in 1982, he sold the land.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/1992/08/29/us/daniel-ludwig-billionaire-businessman-dies-at-95.html |title=Daniel Ludwig, Billionaire Businessman, Dies at 95|website=New York Times|access-date=1 April 2021}}</ref>

In 1987, the land became an independent municipality, and Beiradão was renamed Laranjal do Jari.<ref name="history"/> The city is still mainly a river slum, and suffers from fires, floods, and open sewage.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://core.ac.uk/display/195897682 |title=A realidade socioambiental de Laranjal do Jari/AP: soluções apontadas por moradores na perspectiva da educação popular |author=Eliana do Socorro de Brito Paixão|year=2013|publisher=Editora da Universidade Federal de Uberlandia|language=pt, en}}</ref>

== Transport ==
Laranjal do Jari is connected to the [[BR-156]] and [[BR-210]] highways.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.portal.ap.gov.br/noticia/0507/governo-do-amapa-inicia-pavimentacao-do-trecho-sul-da-br-156 |title=Governo do Amapá inicia pavimentação do trecho sul da BR-156 |website=Government of Amapá|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The city of Monte Dourado can be reached by ferry.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://globoplay.globo.com/v/7007687/ |title=Balsa que faz travessia de laranjal do Jari para Monte Dourado está quebrada, no AP |website=Globo|access-date=2 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

== Geography ==
=== Nature ===
The municipality contains 39% of the {{convert|501771|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Cajari Extractive Reserve]], created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Rio Cajari – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX do Rio Cajari|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5271|accessdate=2016-11-06}}</ref>
It also contains 69% of the {{convert|806184|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Iratapuru Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS do Rio Iratapuru – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RDS do Rio Iratapuru|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5379|accessdate=2016-11-05}}</ref>
A part of the [[Tumucumaque Mountains National Park]] is located within the municipality.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://en.visiteobrasil.com.br/norte/amapa/ecoturismo/conheca/parque-montanhas-do-tumucumaque |title=Tumucumaque Mountains Park|website=Visite O Brasil|access-date=1 April 2021}}</ref>

== Indigenous peoples ==
The Wayampi Indigenous Territory is located in the municipality and is home to the [[Wayampi people|Wayampi]] and [[Aparai people]].<ref name="socio"/> The area inside Laranjal do Jari measures {{convert|3592|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://terrasindigenas.org.br/en/terras-indigenas/3901 |title=Terra Indígena Waiãpi|website=Terras Indigenas|access-date=1 April 2021}}</ref> A group of Wayampi have settled along the [[Amapari River|Amapari]] and Anakui Rivers, however information about the group is very limited.<ref name="socio">{{cite web|url=https://pib.socioambiental.org/en/Povo:Waj%C3%A3pi |title=Wajãpi|website=Socio Ambiental|access-date=1 April 2021}}</ref>
