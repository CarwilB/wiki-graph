'''Barrancas''' is a [[town]] and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]]. The municipality of Barrancas is located to the west of the [[Ranchería River]] in a valley between the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] and [[Serranía del Perijá]] mountains. Barrancas has a total area of 742&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and an elevation of 40 meters above sea level. The average temperature is 28&nbsp;°C throughout the year. It became a municipality in 1892.<ref name="SpanishGobernacion">[http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/barrancas.htm {{in lang|es}} Gobernacion de La Guajira - Barrancas] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070813150039/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/barrancas.htm |date=August 13, 2007 }}</ref>

Its economy is based on agriculture and coal mining which is exploited by the [[Cerrejón]] coal mine.<ref name="SpanishGobernacion" />

==Geography==
Barrancas is located in the [[Guajira Peninsula]] in the [[Caribbean Region of Colombia]], limiting to the east with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]]; to the north with the municipalities of [[Hatonuevo]] and [[Albania, La Guajira|Albania]]; to the south with the municipality of [[Fonseca, La Guajira|Fonseca]] and to the west with the municipality of [[Riohacha]]. The municipality covers a total area of 742&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (286.5 sq mi) and is some 40&nbsp;m (131&nbsp;ft) over the sea level.

The municipality of Barrancas lies in the Valley of Upar formed between the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] (west) and the [[Serranía del Perijá]] (east) and within the basin of the [[Ranchería River]].

===Climate===
The climate present in the region is predominantly hot and dry with no mountainous climate variations, maintaining an average temperature throughout the year of 28&nbsp;°C; while in the lower plains temperature can reach 35&nbsp;°C, while in the mountainous area of the municipality the temperature is milder. During the rainy season temperature in the mountainous part of the municipality can reach between 15 and 18&nbsp;°C while in the dry season 18-24&nbsp;°C. Higher temperature are reached by the end of March, April and May In June and July temperatures drop and in August and September raises. In October and November drops once again and raises in December and January due to the [[trade wind]]s.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://barrancas.galeon.com/ |title=Galeon.com: Barrancas - Climate |access-date=2007-02-15 |language=es |archive-date=2013-04-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130414062324/http://barrancas.galeon.com/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==
Barrancas was founded in 1664, when the Spaniards arrived in the region.<ref name=":0" />

During the war of independence from Spain, on March 26, 1813, the Cabildo of Barrancas insurrected against the local Spanish monarchy authorities destroying images and coat of arms of King [[Fernando VII]]. The cabildo also swore loyalty to [[Simón Bolívar]]. The revolt was led by Eugenio Vidal, Javier López Sierra, Jose Rodriguez Romero, Misael Orozco, Pedro Ojeda, Crisanto Solano, Bienvenido Gomez and Pedro Miguel Garavito.<ref name=":0">{{Harvnb|Castro Trespalacios|1979|p=85}}</ref>

Barrancas was one of the villages part of the [[Province of Padilla]] and became a municipality in 1892.<ref>[http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/barrancas.htm {{in lang|es}} Barrancas: Foundation date] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070813150039/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/barrancas.htm |date=August 13, 2007 }}</ref> It was also one of the five municipalities that grouped to form the [[Department of La Guajira]] in 1954, then called ''Intendencia Nacional de La Guajira'' by orders of then military dictator [[Gustavo Rojas Pinilla]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://barrancas.galeon.com/ |title=Galeon.com: Barrancas - History |access-date=2007-02-15 |language=es |archive-date=2013-04-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130414062324/http://barrancas.galeon.com/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
[[File:Cerrejonmine3.png|thumb|right|250px|Section of the [[Cerrejón]] coal mine open pit near the [[Serranía del Perijá]] mountain range.]]

The municipality of Barrancas is connected through a highway to the department capital, [[Riohacha]] and the main commercial town in the department, [[Maicao]]. Barrancas also has cultural influence and commercial trade related to agriculture from the city of [[Valledupar]] in the [[Department of Cesar]]. Barrancas commercializes in small scale agricultural products such as [[coffee]], [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]], [[cotton]], [[maize]] and [[Cassava|yuca]], but the main economic activity in the municipality is the exploitation of [[coal]] at [[Cerrejón]] coal mine. The Cerrejón coal mine is one of the largest open pit coal mines in the world.<ref>[https://archive.today/20120709121035/http://barrancas-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Barrancas: Economy]</ref>
