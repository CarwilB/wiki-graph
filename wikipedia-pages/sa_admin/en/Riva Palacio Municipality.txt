'''Riva Palacio''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[San Andrés, Chihuahua|San Andrés]]. '''Riva Palacio''' has an area of 808.97&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, it had a population of 8,012,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Riva Palacio|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=054|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305010318/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=054|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 7,811 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title= Riva Palacio|url=
http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08054a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 19, 2008}}</ref> 

The municipality had 155 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The municipality's name is in honour of [[Vicente Riva Palacio]].
