'''Choré District''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[San Pedro Department, Paraguay|San Pedro Department]], [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|703|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 23,548 individuals. The district was officially established on 11 December 1981.

==History==
The original occupants of the regions were the indigenous [[Guaraní people]]. The name Choré came from the tribal chieftain of the indigenous people known as Juan Choré, whose name itself was derived from the name of a wild bird known as "Guyraû Choré".<ref name="ABC37">{{cite web|title=Choré celebra su 37° aniversario de distritación|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/centinela/chore-celebra-su-37-aniversario-de-distritacion-1768901.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Choré celebrates its 37th anniversary as a district}}</ref><ref name="UHChore">{{cite web|title=El Municipio de Choré invierte en obras de empedrados de numerosas calles|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/el-municipio-chore-invierte-obras-empedrados-numerosas-calles-n626999|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=The Municipality of Choré invests in paving works on numerous streets}}</ref>

The region was earlier part of the [[San Estanislao]] district. Following petitions by the locals for the creation of an independent district, the Choré district was officially established on 11 December 1981.<ref name="ABC37"/><ref name="ABC39">{{cite web|title=Conmemoran 39 años de distritación de Choré|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2020/12/12/conmemoran-39-anos-de-distritacion-de-chore/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=39 years of Choré district status commemorated}}</ref>

==Geography==
Choré is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[San Pedro Department, Paraguay|San Pedro Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|703|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/san_pedro/0203__chor%C3%A9/|title=Chore District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|230|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of [[Asuncion]],<ref name="UHChore"/> and separated from the districts of [[Lima]] and [[General Isidoro Resquín]] to the north by the Jejuí River. It borders the districts of [[General Elizardo Aquino]] and [[San Estanislao]] to the south, and [[Guayaibí]] to the west.<ref name="Muncip">{{cite web|url=https://www.municipios.gov.py/chore/|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20240523192650/https://www.municipios.gov.py/chore/|title=Municipio of Chore|lang=es|archive-date=23 May 2024|work=Government of Paraguay|access-date=1 June 2022}}</ref>

It has humid climate with heavy rainfall throughout the year. The average annual temperature is {{cvt|23|C}}, with a maximum of {{cvt|40|C}} and a minimum of {{cvt|1|C}}.<ref name="Muncip"/>

==Demographics and economy==
As per the 2022 census, Choré had a population of 23,548 inhabitants of which 11,957 were males and 11,591 were females. About 85.9% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (14.1%) lived in urban areas. About 28.4% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 9.3% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

The economy is dependent on [[agriculture]] majorly [[tobacco]], and [[livestock]] rearing. Limited fishing is practiced by the people, who reside on the banks of the Jejui River.<ref name="UHChore"/> [[Club Choré Central]] is a football club based out of Choré and plays in the [[Paraguayan Primera División B Nacional]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://versus.lanacion.com.py/versus/2021/07/17/la-ufi-presenta-los-detalles-para-campeonato-nacional-b/|title=La UFI presenta los detalles para Campeonato Nacional B|date=17 July 2021|access-date=1 June 2022|work=Versus|lang=es}}</ref>
