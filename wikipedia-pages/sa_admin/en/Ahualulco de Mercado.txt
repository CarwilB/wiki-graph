'''Ahualulco de Mercado''' is a city and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 274&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 21,465.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ahualulco de Mercado |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14003a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=April 13, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070602171747/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14003a.htm |archive-date=June 2, 2007 }}</ref>

==Name origin==
Ahualulco comes from the Nahuatl word ''Ayahualolco'' which means "place of water crowned" or "place around the water."

== Geography ==

=== Location ===
Ahualulco de Mercado is located in central [[Jalisco]], at coordinates 20° 37'20" to 20° 46'10" north latitude and 103° 52'00" to 104º 04'30" west longitude.

The municipality is bordered on the north by the municipalities of [[Etzatlán]], [[San Juanito de Escobedo]] and [[Tequila]], on the east by the town of [[Teuchitlán]], on the south by the municipalities [[Teuchitlán]] and [[Ameca, Jalisco|Ameca]], and on the west by the municipalities of Ameca and [[Etzatlán]].
It lies on the road from El Refugio to San Marcos and is the most populous town and tourism in this area.

=== Weather ===
The weather is [[semi-arid climate|semi-arid]] with dry winter and spring, and semi-warm, no well-defined thermal change for winter. The average annual temperature is 21.3 °C, with a high of 29.7&nbsp;°C and minimum 13&nbsp;°C. The rainfall occurs between the months of June and July, having a mean of 871.4 mm precipitation, and reaching up to 1,100 during the rainy season. The average annual frost days is 8. The prevailing winds are north towards the northwest.
