'''Pimampiro Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Imbabura Province]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 12,951<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and 12,970 in the 2010 census.<ref>"Ecuador: Municipal Division", https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 10 Jul 2017</ref>

Pimampiro is located in the [[Andes]] of northern Ecuador.   Its capital is the town of [[Pimampiro]] which has an elevation of {{convert|2163|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>  Pimampiro has an area of {{convert|437|sqkm|sqmi}}.
