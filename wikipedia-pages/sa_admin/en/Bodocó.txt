'''Bodocó''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Its population in 2025, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], was an estimated 36,145 and <!--, the density is /km²-->its area is 1621.785&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" /> Bodocó was established in 1909 from territory of the municipality of [[Granito]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|date=July 2016|title=Perfil Municipal: Bodocó|url=http://www.bde.pe.gov.br/ArquivosPerfilMunicipal/BODOC%C3%93.pdf|url-status=live|access-date=2021-05-18|website=Assembleia Legislativa de Pernambuco|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210519042140/http://www.bde.pe.gov.br/ArquivosPerfilMunicipal/BODOC%C3%93.pdf |archive-date=2021-05-19 }}</ref>

Its current [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] ({{Langx|pt-BR|Prefeito}}) is Otávio Augusto Tavares Pedrosa Cavalcante of the [[Brazilian Socialist Party]], elected in [[2020 Brazilian municipal elections|2020]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Prefeito e vereadores de Bodocó tomam posse; veja lista de eleitos|url=https://g1.globo.com/pe/petrolina-regiao/noticia/2021/01/01/prefeito-e-vereadores-de-bodoco-tomam-posse-veja-lista-de-eleitos.ghtml|access-date=2021-05-19|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref>

==Geography==

* Region – [[Sertão]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries – state of [[Ceará]] (N); [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]] (S); [[Exu, Pernambuco|Exu]] and [[Granito]] (E); [[Ouricuri]] and [[Ipubi]] (W)
* Area – 1553.85&nbsp;km²
* Elevation – 443 m
* [[Drainage basin]] – [[Brigida River]]
* Vegetation – [[Caatinga]] (shrubland)
* Climate – [[Semi-arid climate|semi-arid]], hot and dry, [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: BSh
* Annual average temperature – 25.6°C
* Distance to [[Recife]] – 642.6&nbsp;km<ref name=":0" />

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Bodocó are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially the farming of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[horse]]s, [[donkeys]], [[pig]]s, and [[honey]], and cultivation of [[Maize|corn]] and [[manioc]].
