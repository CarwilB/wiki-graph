'''Balboa''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].

Founded by Order No. 02 on 3 November 1967, the municipality covers an area of {{Convert|360|km2|0|abbr=on}} and has a population of 27,030.<ref name=caucanet/> The population is primarily engaged in agriculture and [[ranch]]ing.<ref name=caucanet/>
