'''Altar''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=April 29, 2011 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archivedate=July 31, 2012 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . ---> The municipality had a 2010 census population of 9,049 inhabitants, the vast majority of whom lived in the municipal seat of [[Altar, Sonora|Altar]], which had a population of 7,927 inhabitants. There are no other localities with over 1,000 inhabitants.

==History==
The territory of the municipality was originally inhabited by the [[O'odham]] people. In 1755, the Spanish founded '''Presidio Santa Gertrudis del Altar''' in response to the [[1751 Pima Rebellion]].<ref name="sheridan">{{cite book |last1=Sheridan |first1=Thomas E. |title=Landscapes of Fraud: Mission Tumacácori, the Baca Float, and the Betrayal of the O’odham |date=26 May 2016 |publisher=University of Arizona Press |isbn=978-0-8165-3441-8 |page=48 |language=en}}</ref> It was later renamed to '''Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe del Altar'''.

==Geography==
The total area of the municipality (urban and rural) is 3,944.90 square kilometers.  The municipal population in 2010 was 9,049 inhabitants, with 7,927 (87.6%) living in the municipal seat.  Other settlements are La Cabecera Municipal, Ejido 16 de Septiembre, Ejido Llano Blanco, and Ejido Santa Matilde.

Surrounding municipalities are [[Sáric]], [[Tubutama]], [[Atil, Sonora|Atil]], [[Trincheras]], [[Pitiquito]], [[Caborca]] and [[Oquitoa]].  The northern boundary is with [[Pima County, Arizona|Pima County]] in the [[U.S. state]] of [[Arizona]].

===Climate===
The land lies at an elevation of {{convert|200|-|400|m}} sloping towards the [[Gulf of California]].  
The climate is dry with extremely high temperatures in the summer.  The temperatures sometimes exceed {{convert|131|F}} and is among the hottest places in the world from May to September.

==Economy==
The economy is based on agriculture and cattle raising.  The main crops are wheat, cotton, corn, beans, sorghum, and table grapes. The town is also a staging area for the flow of immigrants that will attempt entry into the United States through the shared [[Sonoran Desert]].
