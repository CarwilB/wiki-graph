'''Juan Vicente Campo Elías''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 5,959 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following two parishes:

* Arnoldo Gabaldón
* Campo Elías
