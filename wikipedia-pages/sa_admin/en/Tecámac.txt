'''Tecámac''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico State|municipality]] in [[State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  {{cvt|157.34|km2}}. The municipal seat lies in the town (locality) of Tecámac de Felipe Villanueva. Its largest town, [[Ojo de Agua, State of Mexico|Ojo de Agua]], with a population of 242,283 inhabitants, is the largest locality in Mexico that is not a municipal seat.

As of the 2020 census, the municipality had a total population of 547,500, with most people residing in [[Ojo de Agua, State of Mexico|Ojo de Agua]].

==Geography==
[[File:OjoDe Agua.JPG|thumbnail|220px|left|Urban park in Ojo de Agua, Tecámac.]]

The town of [[Tecámac de Villanueva]], a municipal seat, has governing jurisdiction over the following communities: Ozumbilla, Santa María Ajoloapan, San Lucas Xolox, Los Reyes Acozac and Ojo de Agua. The total municipality extends 157.34&nbsp;km{{sup|2}} and borders with the municipalities of [[Zumpango]], [[Temascalapa]], [[Tonanitla]], [[Teotihuacán]] and [[Ecatepec de Morelos]].

==Infrastructure==

[[Felipe Ángeles International Airport]] is located within the municipalities [[Zumpango]] and Tecámac.

Tecámac Power Center is an important shopping destination for Tecámac and surrounding municipalities, {{convert|812000|sqft|sqm}} in area, anchored by [[Cinépolis]] cinemas, FunCentral (entertainment area), [[Home Depot]], [[Sam's Club]], [[Suburbia (department store)|Suburbia]], and [[Walmart México y Centroamérica|Walmart]].<ref>[https://fibramacquarie.propertycapsule.com/web/property/94fe63433e8d5b1f90b14a8df7f79872-abcb846fff5308fddfea385bae7a4f96#plans "Tecámac Power Center", Fibra MacQuarie website]</ref><ref>[https://tecamacpowercenter.com.mx Tecámac Power Center official website]</ref>
