'''Ushuaia Department''' ({{langx|es|Departamento Ushuaia}}) is a [[Departments of Argentina|department of Argentina]] in [[Tierra del Fuego Province, Argentina|Tierra del Fuego Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref name=mm />

== Geography ==
The capital city of the department is situated in [[Ushuaia]]. Off the eastern end of the main island, separated by the [[Le Maire Strait]], is [[Isla de los Estados]] (English: Staten Island, from the Dutch ''Stateneiland'').<ref name=mm>{{Cite web|url=https://museomaritimo.com/en/geography|title=Geography of Tierra del Fuego|website=MUSEO MARÍTIMO DE USHUAIA|accessdate=3 July 2025}}</ref> The department neighbours the [[Río Grande Department]] in Tierra del Fuego as well as the Argentine claimed department for the British [[Falkland Islands]] and [[Argentine Antarctica]] claim.<Ref name=mm /> In the department's port office, they stamp passports with the phrase "Fin del Mundo" ([[English language|English]]: End of the World).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.afar.com/places/tourist-office-in-ushuaia-argentina-ushuaia|title=Tourist Office in Ushuaia Argentina|website=AFAR Media|accessdate=3 July 2025}}</ref>

== Military ==
Ushuaia Department has long been a main location for Argentine military bases. During the 1982 [[Falklands War]] with the United Kingdom, the ''[[ARA General Belgrano]]'' sailed from Ushuaia to the [[Falkland Islands]], where [[Sinking of the ARA General Belgrano|it was sunk]] by the [[Royal Navy]] submarine ''[[HMS Conqueror (S48)|HMS Conqueror]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.casarosada.gob.ar/international/latest-news/50466-national-day-of-the-ara-general-belgrano-honor-and-glory-to-our-heroes|title=National Day of the ARA General Belgrano s|website=Casa Rosada|accessdate=3 July 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.portsmouth.co.uk/heritage/belgrano-posed-a-real-threat-to-fleet-1-1271951 |title=Belgrano posed a real threat to fleet |work=Portsmouth News |accessdate=2025-07-03}}</ref> As a result, there was sensitivity around the Falklands War and antipathy towards the British in the department as a result.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.businessinsider.com/bbcs-top-gear-faced-mob-in-argentina-2014-10|title=Top-Rated British Car Show 'Top Gear' Was Chased Out Of Argentina By A Mob|first=Benjamin|last=Zhang|website=Business Insider|accessdate=3 July 2025}}</ref> In 2014, the controversy was restoked when the British television programme ''[[Top Gear (2002 TV series)|Top Gear]]'' drove through Ushuaia Department and were forced to flee due to a mob of Argentine veterans of the Falklands War attacking them. The mob felt that one of their car's numberplates (H982 FKL) was a reference to the Falklands War.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/celebrity-interviews/travel-james-may-presenter/|title=James May: 'Our tour of Argentina was so contentious we were forced to flee with a police escort'|date=23 December 2019|accessdate=3 July 2025|work=The Telegraph}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/media/2014/oct/03/top-gear-argentina-jeremy-clarkson-bbc|title=Top Gear driven from Argentina after Jeremy Clarkson number plate row|first=John|last=Plunkett|date=3 October 2014|accessdate=3 July 2025|work=The Guardian}}</ref>  In 2025, it was announced that the Argentine government had given consideration to a proposal from the [[United States]] to base [[United States Navy]] submarines in Ushuaia with a new naval base there.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://en.mercopress.com/2025/05/05/argentina-us-southcom-chief-passes-through-consequences-to-be-seen-shortly|title=Argentina: US SOUTHCOM Chief passes through – consequences to be seen shortly?|website=MercoPress|accessdate=3 July 2025}}</ref>
