'''Arroio Trinta''' is a municipality in the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] in the South region of [[Brazil]]. The population in 2020 was 3,549 inhabitants.
 
==History==

===Etymology===

There is a degree of curiosity concerning the name of the town.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=420160&search=santa-catarina|title=Cidades@ Histórico do Município|work= EBGE|date= 2016|access-date=27 July 2016}}</ref> It is said that in order to arrive at the city of [[Videira]] (to which the town belonged until 1961), a small river (Arroio) had to be crossed thirty (Trinta) times, and for this reason the name of the town became Arroio Trinta.

===Origin===

The Italian descendants who immigrated to the region of [[Urussanga]] began to colonise Arroio Trinta in 1924. Until then, the population was made up of caboclos.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=420160&search=santa-catarina|title=Cidades@ Histórico do Município|work= EBGE|date= 2016|access-date=27 July 2016}}</ref>

The first [[Watermill|mill]] of Arroio Trinta came a year later after the arrival of the first families and the first [[sawmill]] was established five years later.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=420160&search=santa-catarina|title=Cidades@ Histórico do Município|work= EBGE|date= 2016|access-date=27 July 2016}}</ref>
