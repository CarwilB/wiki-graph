'''Oropeza''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Chuquisaca Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its seat is [[Sucre]] which is also the constitutional capital of Bolivia and the capital of the Chuquisaca Department.

== Places of interest ==
[[File:Incamachay arte Ruprestre Oropeza Chuquisaca Bolivia.jpg|thumb|right|120px|Cave paintings at Inka Mach'ay]]
The archaeological sites of [[Inka Mach'ay, Bolivia|Inka Mach'ay]] and Puma Mach'ay are situated within the province. Inka Mach'ay was declared a National Monument in 1958. <ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.siarb-bolivia.org/eng/parks.htm |title=Archaeological parks in Bolivia |access-date=2010-12-21 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160609155510/http://www.siarb-bolivia.org/eng/parks.htm |archive-date=2016-06-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
