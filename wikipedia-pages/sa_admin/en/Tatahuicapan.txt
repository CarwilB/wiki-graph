'''Tatahuicapan de Juárez''' or '''Tatahuicapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the south-east of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It was created in 1997 with an area of 208.06 km<sup>2</sup>.

==Geography==
The municipality of Tatahuicapan is delimited to the north, south and east by [[Mecayapan]], and to the west by [[Soteapan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30210a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tatahuicapan |publisher=veracruz.gob.mx|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}</ref> The rivers Tatahuicapán, Zapoapan, Piedra Labrada, Texizapan, and Temoloapan run through the region.

===Climate===
The weather in Tatahuicapan is typically warm all year round, with regular [[rain]]s in [[summer]] and [[autumn]].

==Celebrations==
A celebration takes place in March in honour of [[Gabriel|San Gabriel]], [[patron saint]] of the town, and another in December in honour of the [[Virgen de Guadalupe]].
