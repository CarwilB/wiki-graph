'''Elota''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Sinaloa]]. It stands at 
{{coord|23|46|10|N|106|35|0|W|}}.
<!-- 23° 46'10" N. and 106° 35'00" W. -->
The municipality reported 55,339 inhabitants in the 2020 census.

The municipal seat of Elota is the city of [[La Cruz, Sinaloa|La Cruz]].
