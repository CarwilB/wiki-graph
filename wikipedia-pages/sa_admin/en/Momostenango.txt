'''Momostenango''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Totonicapán Department|Totonicapán department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The municipality is situated in the north-west of Totonicapán, in the western highlands of Guatemala.

==Population==
Momostenango's population is predominantly of Maya Kʼicheʼ descent. 70% of the population lives in rural areas and most are small farmers growing maize, beans for their own consumption, and wheat.

The municipality is subdivided in the town of Momostenango, 14 villages and 169 smaller communities called ''caseríos'' or ''parajes''.<ref name="Vinicio">{{cite web |author=Vinicio Alonso, Mario |url=http://biblioteca.usac.edu.gt/EPS/03/03_0646_v6.pdf |format=PDF|title=Municipio de Momostenango, departamento de Totonicapán: Costos y rentabilidad de unidades pecuarias (producción de huevos y galinas |accessdate=23 April 2009 |date=2007 |work= |publisher=Facultad de Ciencias Económicas, USAC }}</ref>

Momostenango is the birthplace of the Maya Kʼicheʼ poet [[Humberto Ak'ab'al]] (1952-2019), who writes in [[Kʼicheʼ language|Kʼicheʼ (Quiché)]] and in [[Spanish language|Spanish]].

==Geography==
Momostenango borders to the north with the municipalities of [[Malacatancito]] ([[Huehuetenango (department)|Huehuetenango]]), [[San Bartolo, Totonicapán|San Bartolo]] and [[Santa Lucía La Reforma]], to its south with [[San Francisco El Alto]] and the municipality of [[Totonicapán]], to the east with [[Santa María Chiquimula]], and to the west with [[San Carlos Sija]] ([[Quetzaltenango (department)|Quezaltenango]]).

The municipality's territory of 305&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> has a mountainous topography with elevations ranging from 1800 m in the north-east, to 3300 m (Cerro Tená) to the west. The mountains on its western border are a watershed for the Salinas and [[Samalá river]] basins. Most of the 49 waterflows are tributaries of the [[Chixoy River]] in the [[Salinas River (Guatemala)|Salinas river]] basin.<ref name="SEGEPLAN">{{cite web |url=http://sinit.segeplan.gob.gt/images/Textos/RedPob/ERPM_QU_COT.pdf |format=PDF |title=Momostenango - Estrategia para la reducción de la pobreza municipal |accessdate=20 February 2008 |last= |first= |year=2002 |work= |publisher=SEGEPLAN }}{{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

Like most of the central highlands, the climate is relatively cold, and is marked by a rainy season starting in mid-May, and a dry season starting in October. Yearly precipitation is around 1200&nbsp;mm, except for the much drier northern part of the municipality, which receives 400–800&nbsp;mm of rainfall.
