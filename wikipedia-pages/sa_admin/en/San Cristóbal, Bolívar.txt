'''San Cristóbal''' is a municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]]. It is located on the south shore of the [[Canal del Dique|Dique Canal]]. As of the year 2018, it has a total population of 8,054.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolívar (Colombia): Urban Localities in Municipalities - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/colombia/bolivar/ |access-date=2023-06-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San Cristóbal is situated on the northern edge of [[Bolívar Department]]. Its average elevation is 7 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Cristóbal topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-s8v8s8/San-Crist%C3%B3bal/ |access-date=2023-06-03 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref>
