'''Venturosa'''({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[vẽtuˈɾɔzɐ]}})  is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the state of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has 338.12 sq/km of total area.

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Caetés, Pernambuco|Caetés]] and [[Capoeiras]]   ('''S''');  [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]] and [[Alagoinha]]   ('''E''');    [[Pedra, Pernambuco|Pedra]]  ('''W''').
* Area - 338.12&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 530 m
* Hydrography - Ipojuca, Ipanema and Una rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Annual average temperature - 23.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 243&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Venturosa''' are industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[bean]]s, [[Maize|corn]]; and  farming of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goat]]s, [[pig]]s and [[chickens]].
