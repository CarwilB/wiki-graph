'''Quehue''' ([[Aymara language|Aymara]] and [[Quechua language|Quechua]]: '''Q'iwi''', meaning curve(d)) is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Canas Province]] in [[Peru]]. Its seat is the village of Quehue.

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Ch'iyar Jaqhi (Cusco)|Ch'iyar Jaqhi]] at {{convert|4654|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Canas Province (Cusco Region)</ref>

* Luntuni
* Qullani Urqu
* Q'asa Saya
* [[Tuqtu (Canas-Chumbivilcas)|Tuqtu]]
* Awki Tutukani
}}

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (97.36%) learnt to speak in childhood, 2.41% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población</ref>
