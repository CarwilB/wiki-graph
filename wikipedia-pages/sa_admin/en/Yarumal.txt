'''Yarumal''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. The municipality (three parishes and 20 villages) has an area of {{cvt|738.3|km2|sqmi}}. The population was 41,542 at the 2018 census. Its average elevation is {{cvt|2,265|m|ft}} above sea level.

It has a [[minor basilica]], [[Basilica of Our Lady of Mercy (Yarumal)|Our Lady of Mercy]], which is a parish church of architectural note.

It gave its name to the [[Yarumal Society for the Foreign Missions]] (M.X.Y./I.M.E.Y.), a [[Medellín]]-based Latin Catholic [[Society of Apostolic Life of Pontifical Right]] for Men, which despite its name is especially active in Colombian missions.

== History ==
Yarumal was founded in 1787 as '''San Luis de Gongora'''. Municipal status was granted in 1821.

=== Name ===
The current name of Yarumal comes from a local plant of the family [[Moraceae]], known in botanical Latin as [[Cecropia peltata]] L.

== Health ==
{{See also|Alzheimer's disease in the Hispanic/Latino population#PSEN1 gene}}

An unusually large proportion of the inhabitants suffer from early-onset [[Alzheimer's disease]], which is caused by the gene mutation E280A. The genetic mutation is thought to have come from a Spanish conquistador. Approximately 5,000 residents will develop early-onset Alzheimer's. Half of the affected residents were shown to have developed symptoms by their early 40s.<ref>{{Cite journal|title = Origin of the PSEN1 E280A mutation causing early-onset Alzheimer's disease|journal = Alzheimer's & Dementia|date = 2014-10-01|issn = 1552-5279|pmc = 4019728|pmid = 24239249|pages = S277–S283.e10|volume = 10|issue = 5 Suppl|doi = 10.1016/j.jalz.2013.09.005|first1 = Matthew A.|last1 = Lalli|first2 = Hannah C.|last2 = Cox|first3 = Mary L.|last3 = Arcila|first4 = Liliana|last4 = Cadavid|first5 = Sonia|last5 = Moreno|first6 = Gloria|last6 = Garcia|first7 = Lucia|last7 = Madrigal|first8 = Eric M.|last8 = Reiman|first9 = Mauricio|last9 = Arcos-Burgos}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal|title = Genetic diagnosis and prognosis of Alzheimer's disease: challenges and opportunities|journal = Expert Review of Molecular Diagnostics|date = 2015-03-01|issn = 1744-8352|pmid = 25634383|pages = 339–348|volume = 15|issue = 3|doi = 10.1586/14737159.2015.1002469|first = Christiane|last = Reitz|pmc=4677675}}</ref>
