'''Brejo da Madre de Deus''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbɾɛʒu ˈdɐ ˈmɐdɾi ˈdi ˈdeus]}}) (''Swamp of the Mother of God'') is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 202&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 51,225 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/brejo-da-madre-de-deus/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is nationally famous for hosting every [[easter]] a large open air theater show designated [[passion of Christ]] in [[New Jerusalem theater|New Jerusalem]].

==Tourism==
In ''Fazenda Nova'', which means ''New Farm'', a district of Brejo da Madre de Deus is located the Park of Sculptures and the famous [[New Jerusalem theater]].
[[Image:Nova-Jerusalém-Fórum-Pilatos.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Roman Forum - stage 6]]
The theater city hosts every year in the [[Easter]] an outdoor theater show designated [[Passion of Christ]] which tell us the history of ''death and resurrection of [[Jesus]]''. It is considered the largest open-air [[theater]] in the world<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.novajerusalem.com.br/2009/# |title=Paixão de Cristo |access-date=2009-09-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090509034225/http://www.novajerusalem.com.br/2009/# |archive-date=2009-05-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref> due to its 100,000 m<sup>2</sup> (1.1 million ft<sup>2</sup>), surrounded by 3500 meters of walls and 70 towers. 
{{Main|New Jerusalem theater}}

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Brejo da Madre de Deus''' are based in tourism, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[tomato]]es; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s and [[poultry]].
