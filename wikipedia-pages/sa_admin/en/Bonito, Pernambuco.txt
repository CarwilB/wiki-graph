'''Bonito''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[buˈnitu]}}) (''Beautiful'') (population 38,117)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/bonito/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco. It has a total area of {{cvt|399.5|km2}}.
[[Image:Municipio de bonito-pe.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Urubu Waterfalls between Bonito and Primavera]]

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Camocim de São Félix]], [[Sairé]] and [[Barra de Guabiraba]] ('''N'''); [[Palmares, Pernambuco|Palmares]] and [[Catende]]('''S'''), [[Cortês]] and [[Joaquim Nabuco, Pernambuco|Joaquim Nabuco]] ('''E'''); [[São Joaquim do Monte]] and [[Belém de Maria]] ('''W''').
* Area - 399.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 443 m
* Hydrography - [[Sirinhaém River|Sirinhaém]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] rivers
* Vegetation - Subcaducifolia Forest
* Climate - Hot and Humid
* Annual average temperature - 21 c
* Main road -  BR 232 and PE 103
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 128&nbsp;km

==Economy==

Principal economic activity for Bonito is the agricultural sector. Sugar cane, cattle, tuber production, poultry and fruit. tourism is an emerging field due to the many natural waterfalls accessible by a newly asphalted highway.

Bonito is experiencing a real estate boom driving up the prices of building lots and rental units, proposed improvements to the main route into the city will further drive development.
