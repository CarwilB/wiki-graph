'''Asientos''' is a [[Municipalities of Aguascalientes|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Aguascalientes]]. It stands at {{coord|22|14|N|102|05|W|}}. The town of [[Real de Asientos]] serves as its municipal seat.

Real de Asientos was declared a ''[[Pueblos Mágicos (Mexico)|Pueblo Mágico]]'' by the Mexican [[Secretariat of Tourism (Mexico)|Secretariat of Tourism (SECTUR)]] in 2006.<ref>{{cite web|title=Real de Asientos, Aguascalientes {{!}} Secretaría de Turismo|url=http://www.sectur.gob.mx/gobmx/pueblos-magicos/real-de-asientos-aguascalientes/|access-date=2021-03-31|language=es-ES}}</ref> As of March 2021 it is one of three Pueblos Mágicos in the state of Aguascalientes.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 45,492.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Asientos|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=002|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=24 February 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180224232650/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=002|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Other than the town of '''Real de Asientos''', the municipality had 250 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Villa Juárez, Aguascalientes|Villa Juárez]] (4,888), [[Ciénega Grande, Aguascalientes|Ciénega Grande]] (3,348), classified as urban, and [[Guadalupe de Atlas, Aguascalientes|Guadalupe de Atlas]] (2,259), [[Lázaro Cárdenas, Aguascalientes|Lázaro Cárdenas]] (1,583), [[Pilotos, Aguascalientes|Pilotos]] (1,331), [[Bimbaletes Aguascalientes (El Álamo), Aguascalientes|Bimbaletes Aguascalientes (El Álamo)]] (1,223), [[Molinos, Aguascalientes|Molinos]] (1,219), [[El Tule, Aguascalientes|El Tule]] (1,189), [[Noria del Borrego (Norias), Aguascalientes|Noria del Borrego (Norias)]] (1,186), [[Licenciado Adolfo López Mateos, Aguascalientes|Licenciado Adolfo López Mateos]] (1,074), and [[Jarillas, Aguascalientes|Jarillas]] (1,041), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The municipality of Asientos is located in the north-east of the state and has a territorial area of 547.74 kilometres squared which is equivalent to 9.84% of the total area of the state and it has an altitude ranging from 1800 to 2700 metres above sea level.

It borders the municipality of [[Tepezalá]] to the north east, the municipality of [[Pabellón de Arteaga Municipality|Pabellón de Arteaga]] to the west, the municipalities of [[San Francisco de los Romo]] and [[Aguascalientes]] to the south east and the municipality of [[El Llano, Aguascalientes|El Llano]] to the south. In the north and east, it borders the state of [[Zacatecas]], specifically the municipalities of [[Loreto, Zacatecas|Loreto]] and [[Villa García]]; and in the south east, it borders the state of [[Jalisco]], with the municipality of [[Ojuelos de Jalisco]].

==Government==
The government of the municipality consists of the City Council, which is made up of a municipal president, a trustee and a council composed of nine members, five elected by relative majority and four by proportional representation. The city council is elected by universal, direct and secret vote in elections held on the first Sunday of July of the corresponding year.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Periodos_de_gobierno_municipal |title=Periodos de gobierno municipal |accessdate= 19 February 2011 |author= Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal |archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20110616191719/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Periodos_de_gobierno_municipal |archivedate= 16 June 2011}}</ref>
