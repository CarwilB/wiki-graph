'''Arauco Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Arauco}}) is one of three [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Bío Bío Region]] in Chile. It spans an area of {{cvt|5643.3|km2}}. Its capital is [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]]. It had a population of 166,231 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
The [[Biobio Region]] was established on 10 July 1974, by Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> The region is divided into three provinces&ndash;Arauco, [[Biobío Province|Biobío]], and [[Concepción Province, Chile|Concepción]], which are further divided into 33 [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_del_biobio/|lang=es|title=Biobío Region|trans-title=Biobío Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Arauco Province is one of the three provinces of the [[Biobio Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It covers an area of {{cvt|5643.3|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> It borders the provinces of Concepción to the north, Biobío to the east, [[Cautín Province|Cautin]] to the south and borders the Pacific Ocean to the west.<ref name="Info"/> Almost half of the land area consists of coastal plains, and roughly quarter of them are made up of offshore islands, with the remaining forming part of the [[Carampangue River|Carampangue]] and [[Lebu River]] basins.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/50|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Arauco Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Arauco Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

The province consists of plain surrounded by the Nahuelbuta Mountains to the east, and low lying hills that slope towards the coast on the west. The region has significant forest cover, with a number of lakes, the major of which are the Lleu-lleu and Lanalhue lakes.<ref name="Info">{{cite report|url=https://theforestsdialogue.org/sites/default/files/field_visit_information_english.pdf|title=Arauco Province|work=Forests dialogue|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> Its capital is at [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], located along the coast.<ref name="EB1911">{{EB1911|inline=y|wstitle=Arauco|volume=2|page=322}}</ref> 

The province has a [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|17.14|C}}. The region receives approximately {{cvt|14.02|cm}} of rainfall annually on average.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/biobio/arauco|title=Arauco province|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a province, Arauco is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into seven [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'')&ndash;[[Arauco, Chile|Arauco]], [[Cañete, Chile|Cañete]], [[Contulmo]], [[Curanilahue]], [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], [[Los Álamos]], and [[Tirúa]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MI">{{cite web|url=https://dpparauco.dpp.gob.cl/conozca-la-provincia/|lang=es|title=Conozca la provincia|trans-title=Know the Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 166,231 inhabitants. The population consisted of 85,512 females (51.4%) and 80,719 males (48.6%). About 19.1% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 66.4% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 14.5% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 118,792 inhabitants (71.5%) and a rural population of 47,439 inhabitants (28.5%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/082__arauco/|title=Arauco Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 164,774 inhabitants (99.1%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 121,533 inhabitants (73.1%), while 44,684 inhabitants (26.9%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] formed the largest religious group with 73,889 adherents (55%), followed by 29,115 [[Roman Catholics]] (21.7%), and 28,132 inhabitants (20.9%) indicating no religious affiliation.<ref name="CP"/> The region has a high rate of [[poverty]] and [[unemployment]]. This Province has historically high poverty levels. According to a 2013 survey, 16.7% of the population were in poverty, higher than the regional (12.3%) and national average (7.8%).<ref name="MI"/><ref name="Info"/>
