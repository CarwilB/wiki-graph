'''Senguio''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|90|km}} east of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Senguio is located at an elevation between {{convert|2100|and(-)|3400|m}} in the [[Sierra de Mil Cumbres]] mountain range in northeastern Michoacán. It borders the Michoacanese municipalities of [[Maravatío]] to the north, [[Tlalpujahua]] to the east, [[Angangueo]] to the south, [[Aporo, Michoacán|Aporo]] to the southwest, and [[Irimbo]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16080.pdf |title=Senguio, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |accessdate=28 December 2020}}</ref> It also borders the municipality of [[San José del Rincón]] in the [[State of Mexico]] to the southeast. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|257.741|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.44% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Senguio consists of temperate forest (48%) and grassland (12%). Another 38% of the land is used for agriculture and 0.8% consists of urban areas.<ref name=compendium/> Most of Senguio drains into the [[Lerma River]], while the southernmost part of the municipality is located in the basin of the [[Cutzamala River]], a tributary of the [[Balsas River]].<ref name=compendium/> The forests in the southeastern part of the municipality are protected as part of the [[Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve]].

Senguio has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|8|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1100|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The place name Senguio is of [[Purépecha]] origin, meaning "limits". Prior to the arrival of the Spanish, Senguio was located in the frontier region between the [[Purépecha Empire| Purépecha]] and [[Aztec Empire]] and was inhabited by [[Mazahua people|Mazahua]] tribes. The area was conquered by [[Cristóbal de Olid]] in 1522, and during the colonial period, the land was taken from the native inhabitants and incorporated into the Spanish ''[[hacienda]]s'', gradually leading to the disappearance of the indigenous peoples.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

After Mexican independence, Senguio was made a part of the municipality of Irimbo in 1831.<ref name=encyclopedia/> It became an independent municipality in 1856. It was re-incorporated into Irimbo in 1863, but re-established as 
a municipality in 1868.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=87, 192–193 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4 |archive-date=2019-04-12 |access-date=2020-12-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190412021950/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Senguio comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Rodolfo Quintana Trujillo.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |accessdate=28 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Senguio recorded a population of 18,427 inhabitants living in 4153 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=080 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Senguio |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |accessdate=28 December 2020}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 19,146 inhabitants in Senguio.<ref name=survey/>

There are 55 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Senguio is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2707 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Agriculture is the main economic activity in Senguio. Crops grown include corn and oats.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Senguio Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/5a-5418-2.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=83 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 5a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=1 January 2019 |accessdate=28 December 2020}}</ref>
