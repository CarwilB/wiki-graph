'''La Estrella''' is a town and [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. La Estrella is part of [[The Metropolitan Area of the Aburrá Valley]]. The population was estimated to be 75,517 in 2020.
