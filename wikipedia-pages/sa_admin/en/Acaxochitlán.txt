'''Acaxochitlán''' is one of the 84 [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipalities]] of the state of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 226.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 34,892. Acaxochitlan has three languages; [[Nahuatl]], [[Spanish language|Spanish]], and [[Otomi language|Otomi]].

In 2023, Acaxochitlan was designated a [[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]] by the federal government, recognizing its cultural and historical importance.<ref>{{Cite web |title=These are Mexico's newest Magical Towns |url=https://www.travelweekly.com/Mexico-Travel/Insights/Mexico-names-new-Magical-Towns |access-date=2025-09-15 |website=www.travelweekly.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Its name derives from the [[Nahuatl]] words "acatl" meaning "cane," and "xochitl" meaning "flower", which form the word "acaxochitl," a term also used to designate a plant belonging to the reed family and its red flowers. The suffix "tlan" means "place." Thus, the name Acaxochitlán translates as "place of abundant Acaxochitl" or "place where the reed bears flowers."<ref>{{cite web|title=Acaxochitlán|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/index.html|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|language=es|access-date=24 August 2014|archive-date=24 September 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924034313/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
