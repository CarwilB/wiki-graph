'''Tumbes''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] in [[Peru]], located in the [[Tumbes Region|region]] of the same name. It borders the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the north, the province of [[Zarumilla Province|Zarumilla]] on the east, the [[Piura Region]] and [[Ecuador]] on the south and the province of [[Contralmirante Villar Province|Contralmirante Villar]] on the west. Its capital is [[Tumbes, Peru|Tumbes]], which is also the regional capital.<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial Tumbes, [http://www.munitumbes.gob.pe Municipalidad Provincial de Tumbes], Retrieved November 7, 2007</ref>

==Boundaries==
* '''North''': [[Pacific Ocean]]
* '''East''': [[Zarumilla province]]
* '''South''': [[Piura Region]] and [[Ecuador]]
* '''West''': [[Contralmirante Villar province]]
