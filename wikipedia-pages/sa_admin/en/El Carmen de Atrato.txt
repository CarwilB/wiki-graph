'''El Carmen de Atrato''' is a municipality and town in the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
El Carmen de Atrato has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') which borders a [[tropical rainforest climate]]. The town’s high altitude makes its climate far more temperate (similar to that of [[Andean Region (Colombia)|the Andean Region]]) than the climate of most of Chocó Department, although it still receives more than twice as much rain as [[Bogotá]].
