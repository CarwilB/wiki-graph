'''Chocontá''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Almeidas Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. It is located on the [[Pan-American Highway]]. In 1938 Chocontá had a population of 2,041.<ref>Columbia-Lippincott Gazetteer. (New York: Columbia University Press, 1952) p. 403</ref>

== Etymology ==
In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca, Chocontá means: "Farmlands of the good ally" or "Garden of the neighbour".<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.choconta-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chocontá] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310084932/http://www.choconta-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=10 March 2014 }}</ref>

== History ==
Chocontá is located in the territories of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] on the border between the area led by the ''[[cacique]]'' of [[Vélez, Santander|Vélez]] and the southern Muisca ''[[zipa]]zgo'' reign. Around 1490 the armies of the northern Muisca led by [[Michuá]] and the warriors of the southern Muisca ruled by [[Saguamanchica]] confronted each other in the bloody [[Battle of Chocontá]].

The [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish]] [[conquistador]]s led by [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] reached the Colombian highlands in 1537. On 9 June 1537, Pentecostal day, the Spanish arrived in Chocontá and Father Fray Domingo de las Casas named it ''Pueblo del Espíritu Santo'' ("Town of the Holy Spirit") with a mass. The village was founded by Tómas López on 6 September 1563 and relocated in 1573.<ref name=website/>

In 1854 the [[list of presidents of Colombia|presidential]] headquarters was located in Chocontá and on 21 April 1854 [[Tomás de Herrera]] was proclaimed president in the rectory of the town. On 9 August 1819 at 2 pm liberator [[Simón Bolívar]] arrived to Chocontá and stayed to rest in what currently is the House of Culture after his triumph, defining the fate of the Colombian Republic.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.choconta-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml#historia History of Chocontá] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310084932/http://www.choconta-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml#historia |date=10 March 2014 }}</ref>

== Trivia ==
* Chocontá is the principal producer of [[strawberry|strawberries]] in Colombia
* The town is nicknamed ''La ciudad satélite de Colombia'' ("The satellite town of Colombia") because of the satellite dishes of Telecom<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://es.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Chocont%C3%A1&oldid=88202406 Version of Spanish language Wikipedia] – accessed 20-04-2016</ref>
* The [[Sisga Reservoir]] is located within Chocontá
* The [[genus]] ''[[Choconta (froghopper)|Choconta]]'' with species ''[[Choconta circulata]]'', found near Bogotá, have been named after Chocontá
