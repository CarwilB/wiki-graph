'''San Esteban''' ("St. Stephen" in Spanish) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Los Andes Province, Chile|Los Andes Province]] of central [[Chile]]'s [[Valparaíso Region]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|1361.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Esteban spans an area of {{convert|1361.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 14,400 inhabitants (7,298 men and 7,102 women). Of these, 7,542 (52.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,858 (47.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 18.5% (2,247 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Esteban is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is René Mardones Valencia ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Official"/>

* Geovanna Serey Lizana

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Esteban is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marco Antonio Núñez ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Gaspar Rivas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 11th electoral district, (together with [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]], [[Calle Larga]], [[Rinconada]], [[San Felipe, Chile|San Felipe]], [[Putaendo]], [[Santa María, Chile|Santa María]], [[Panquehue]], [[Llaillay]] and [[Catemu]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Ignacio Walker Prieto ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Lily Pérez San Martín]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).{{Update after|2021|9|5}}
