'''Doctor Juan Eulogio Estigarribia''', also known as Campo Nueve or Campo 9, is a city in [[Paraguay]]. It is famous for its tourism and for its sports which include a local football team, and for its [[Guarani people|Guarani]] culture sites. The main river in the city is the [[Iguazú River]] and the city is agricultural.
