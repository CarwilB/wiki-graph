'''Doctor Arroyo Municipality''' is a municipality located in the northeastern Mexican state of [[Nuevo León]]. It has a territorial extension of 5,106.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipality was named in honor of [[José Francisco Arroyo de Anda]]. The municipality was renamed on September 22, 1826. The municipality had 33,269 inhabitants in the 2010 census.

==Geography==
It lies in the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]], it borders the municipalities of [[Aramberri]] and [[Galeana, Nuevo León|Galeana]] to the north, [[Mier y Noriega]] and the state of [[San Luis Potosí]] to the south and the municipalities of [[Aramberri]], [[General Zaragoza]], the state of [[Tamaulipas]] to the east. Doctor Arroyo is located in the zone there are great rivers of intermittent streams and a large mountain range, where occasional snowfall occurs in winter.
