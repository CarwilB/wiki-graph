''' Concepción de Buenos Aires  ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  265.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 6,334.<ref>{{cite web|title=Concepción de Buenos Aires|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14026a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=April 13, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129040800/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14026a.htm|archive-date=January 29, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>
