'''Cuautlancingo''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[List of Mexican states|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070405081753/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |archive-date=April 5, 2007 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . ---> It is part of the [[Metropolitan area of Puebla]]. The town is bordered on the north by the state of [[Tlaxcala]], on the east by Tlaxcala and the city of [[Puebla, Puebla|Puebla]], on the south by the municipalities of [[San Pedro Cholula]] and Puebla, and on the west by the municipality of [[Coronango (municipality)|Coronango]].

The Main Plant of [[Volkswagen de México]], [[Volkswagen|VW]]'s biggest plant in the world outside Germany, is located in the north of Cuautlancingo.{{citation needed|date=March 2014}} It has a capacity of 2,500 units per day and employs around 14,000 people.<ref name=Ludwig1>{{cite web|url= http://automotivelogistics.media/intelligence/sharing-logistics-state-expansion |title=Volkswagen & Audi in Mexico part 1: Sharing logistics in a state of expansion |first=Christopher |last=Ludwig |date=14 July 2016|publisher=Automotive Logistics|access-date=13 May 2017}}</ref>

The [[Puebla, Puebla|Puebla]]-area German school, ''[[Colegio Humboldt Puebla]]'' (''Deutsche Schule Puebla''), is in Cuautlancingo.<ref>"[https://www.colegio-humboldt.edu.mx/ Inicio]."
