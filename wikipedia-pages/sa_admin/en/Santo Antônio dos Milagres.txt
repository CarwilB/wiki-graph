'''Santo Antônio dos Milagres''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning "Saint Anthony of the Miracles") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western part of the state of [[Piauí]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,166 (2020 est.) in an area of 33.15 km².
