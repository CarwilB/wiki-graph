'''João Alfredo''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈʒuɐ̃w ɐwˈfɾedu]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 120.6&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 33,328 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/joao-alfredo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Bom Jardim, Pernambuco|Bom Jardim]]   ('''N''');  [[Salgadinho, Pernambuco|Salgadinho]]    ('''S''');  [[Limoeiro]]    ('''E''');   [[Surubim]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 133.52&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 328 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River|Goiana]] and [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 120.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''João Alfredo''' are based in wood & [[furniture]] industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[maize|corn]], [[manioc]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[pigs]] and [[poultry]].
