'''Huarochirí District''' (in Hispanicized spelling) or '''Waruchiri'''<ref>Teofilo Laime Acopa, Diccionario Bilingüe, Iskay simipi yuyay k'ancha, Quechua – Castellano, Castellano – Quechua: '''''waruchiri''''' - ''s. Valle situado en el distrito de matucana, al sur de Lima y al oriente del adoratorio de Pacha kamaq (JL).''</ref> is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]] in [[Peru]]. Despite its name, it is not the provincial seat; the seat is at [[Matucana District|Matucana]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]].
