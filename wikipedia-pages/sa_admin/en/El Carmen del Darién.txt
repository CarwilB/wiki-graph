'''El Carmen del Darién''' is a municipality and town{{Citation needed|date=March 2026}} in the northern part of the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=FM |first=La |date=2017-12-04 |title=Esta tragedia se pudo haber evitado: alcalde de Carmen del Darién, Chocó |url=https://www.lafm.com.co/actualidad/esta-tragedia-se-pudo-haber-evitado-alcalde-de-carmen-del-darien-choco-80831 |access-date=2026-03-26 |website=La FM |language=es}}</ref>

==Climate==
El Carmen del Darién has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year round. The following climate data is for Curbaradó, the capital of the municipality.
