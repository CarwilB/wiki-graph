'''Patagones Partido''' is the southernmost [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 37,646 inhabitants (at the 2022 Census) in an area of {{convert|13570|sqkm|abbr=on}}, making it by far the largest partido in Buenos Aires Province. The capital city of Patagones Partido is [[Carmen de Patagones]], which is around {{convert|960|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

It is bordered on the north by [[Villarino Partido]].

==Economy==

The economy of Patagones Partido is dominated by farming.
