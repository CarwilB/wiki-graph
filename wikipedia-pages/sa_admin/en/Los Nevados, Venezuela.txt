'''Los Nevados''', is a town founded in 1591, located in the [[Sierra Nevada National Park (Venezuela)|Sierra Nevada National Park]] in Mérida, Venezuela, located 2,710 meters above sea level and with a population of 2000 inhabitants.<ref>[http://www.colibri-tours.com/es/los-nevados-merida-venezuela-colibri-tours Tour los Nevados] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140220040835/http://www.colibri-tours.com/es/los-nevados-merida-venezuela-colibri-tours |date=2014-02-20 }}</ref> its name comes from the glaciers of the peaks León, Toro and Espejo that could be seen from Los Nevados before their disappearance in the 1960s . the main activity of the area is tourism, with its horse and donkey rides via the [[Merida cable car|cable car]] station Loma Redonda. Its inhabitants are also engaged in farming, especially the cultivation of potatoes, corn, wheat and garlic.
