'''Contulmo''' ([[Mapudungun]]: "place of transit") {{IPA|es|konˈtulmo|}} is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Arauco Province]], [[Biobío Region]]. Colonized by Germans of [[Berlin]] since 1884.

== History ==
In December 1612, a group made up of three religious [[Jesuit missions|Jesuit missionaries]] and five Mapuche leaders were murdered by a fit of rage by the Mapuche leader [[Anganamón]], from a nearby community, who wanted back some of his wives who had escaped from him. This event marked the end of the so-called "[[Defensive War]]", a military strategy of the [[Spanish Empire]] against the Mapuches in those territories during the [[Arauco War]].<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Redden |first1=Andrew |title=The Best Laid Plans…: Jesuit Counsel, Peacebuilding, and Disaster on the Chilean Frontier; The Martyrs of Elicura, 1612 |journal=Journal of Jesuit Studies |date=10 March 2017 |volume=4 |issue=2 |pages=250–269 |doi=10.1163/22141332-00402006 |url=https://brill.com/view/journals/jjs/4/2/article-p250_5.xmlhttps://brill.com/view/journals/jjs/4/2/article-p250_5.xml |access-date=30 March 2024 |language=en |issn=2214-1324|doi-access=free }}</ref>

In 1884, a group of 48 Protestant German families arrived from Berlin and its surroundings, in the former [[Kingdom of Prussia]]. The German missionary ordained [[Methodist]] pastor in the [[United States]] but with a [[Lutheranism|Lutheran]] training from his childhood and youth, [[Oskar von Barchwitz-Krauser]], held conferences in the German capital to gather people who wanted to emigrate to the [[Araucanía (historic region)|historic region of Araucanía]], in [[Southern Chile]], to found a community governed by Christian principles. The colonizers left from Berlin to [[Hamburg]] to embark to [[Liverpool]], where after making a stop in [[Rio de Janeiro]], they crossed the [[Strait of Magellan]] to reach the Chilean port of [[Talcahuano]] in April 1884.<ref>{{cite book |last1=von Barchwitz-Krauser |first1=Oskar |title=Six years with William Taylor in South America... |date=1885 |url=https://www.loc.gov/item/unk83004412/ |access-date=30 March 2024}}</ref> The urbanization tasks were difficult, in an area in the middle of the [[Chilean Coast Range|Coastal mountain range]] and with dense swamps and wooded vegetation. The first elected mayor of the commune of Contulmo was the German naturalized Chilean [[Paul Kortwich]] in 1918.<ref>{{cite book |title=Breve Historia de Contulmo |url=https://www.archivohistoricoconcepcion.cl/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Breve_Historia_de_Contulmo_Cuadernos_del_Bio-Bio.pdf |access-date=30 March 2024 |language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Geography==
Contulmo spans an area of {{convert|961.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> It is bordered by the commune of [[Cañete, Chile|Cañete]] to the north and northwest, [[Tirúa]] to the southwest, [[Purén]] to the east and [[Lumaco]] to the southeast.

The commune includes part of the lakes [[Lanalhue Lake|Lanalhue]] and [[Lleulleu Lake|Lleulleu]]. The town of Contulmo is surrounded by mountains and hills of the [[Nahuelbuta Range]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Contulmo has 5,838 inhabitants (3,020 men and 2,818 women). Of these, 2,442 (41.8%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,396 (58.2%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 13.3% (898 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Culture==
:''Main articles: [[German Chileans]] and [[German colonization of Valdivia, Osorno and Llanquihue]]''

In architecture and urban design the town shows the German colonization. Every year in April is celebrated the '''"Fest der Kolonisten"''' which means "feast of the colonists" in German, and in December is the '''"Feast of the White Strawberry",''' among other festivities.

==Administration==
[[File:Edificio Ilustre Municipalidad de Contulmo.JPG|thumb|left|Contulmo Town Hall]]
As a commune, Contulmo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2016-2020 alcalde is Mauricio Lebrecht Sperberg ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Contulmo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Manuel Monsalve ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Iván Norambuena (UDI) as part of the 46th electoral district, together with [[Lota, Chile|Lota]], [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], [[Arauco, Chile|Arauco]], [[Curanilahue]], [[Los Álamos]], [[Cañete, Chile|Cañete]] and [[Tirúa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Victor Pérez Varela (UDI) and Mariano Ruiz-Esquide Jara ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).
