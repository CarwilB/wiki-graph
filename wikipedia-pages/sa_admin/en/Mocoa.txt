'''Mocoa''' ({{IPA|es|moˈko.a}}) ([[Kamëntšá_language|Kamëntšá]]: Shatjok<ref>{{cite book |last=O'Brien|first=Colleen Alena |date=2018 |title=A Grammatical Description of Kamsá, A Language Isolate of Colombia |url=http://ling.hawaii.edu/wp-content/uploads/ColleenOBrienFinal.pdf}}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and capital city of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Putumayo Department|Putumayo]] in [[Colombia]]. As per the 2018 census, the population of Mocoa was estimated at 38,457 individuals. The city was founded in 1551 and covers an area of {{cvt|5.613|km2}}.

==Geography==
Mocoa is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and capital city of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Putumayo Department|Putumayo]] in [[Colombia]]. The city was founded in 1551.<ref name="Brit">{{cite web|url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Mocoa|title=Mocoa |work=[[Britannica]] |access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref><ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://colombia.co/en/the-colombian-amazon-orinoco/putumayo/mocoa|title=City of Mocoa|work=Colombia.com|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|5.613|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/colombia/putumayo/mocoa/86001000__mocoa/ |work=Citypopulation.de |title=Mocoa municipality with localities}}</ref> It lies on the fringes of the [[Andes]] mountain range in the [[Caquetá River]] basin. It is surrounded by Amazon rainforests and flat agricultural and grazing lands.<ref name="Brit"/> There are several waterfalls, wildlife reserves, and caves surrounding the city.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://colombia.travel/en/mocoa|title=Mocoa|work=Columbia travel|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

On 31 March 2017, flooding and [[2017 Mocoa landslide|mudslides]] caused significant destruction and left more than 254 people dead, and hundreds missing.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://reliefweb.int/report/colombia/colombia-co-humanitarian-situation-report-2-5-april-2017|title=Colombia CO: Humanitarian Situation Report #2, 5 April 2017|work=Relief Web|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2017/apr/01/colombia-landslide-mocoa-putumayo-heavy-rains|title=Colombia landslide leaves at least 254 dead and hundreds missing|last=Brodzinsky|first=Sibylla|date=2 April 2017|work=The Guardian|access-date=1 June 2017|language=en-GB|issn=0261-3077}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Brocchetto|first1=Marilia|last2=Ramos|first2=Fernando|last3=Sanchez|first3=Ray|title=Mudslides kill more than 200 people in Colombia|url=http://www.cnn.com/2017/04/01/americas/colombia-mudslide-deaths/|work=[[CNN]]|accessdate=1 April 2017}}</ref>

===Climate===
Mocoa has mean yearly temperature of {{cvt|21-21.4|C}} with minimum of {{cvt|16-17|C}} and maximum of {{cvt|28-29|C}}. It receives an annual rainfall of {{cvt|420-460|cm}}, with majority of the rainfall happening from April to July.<ref name="Report">{{cite report|url=https://www.coppergiant.co/documents/Technical-Report-43-101.pdf|title=Technical report|work=Coppergiant|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>{{rp|page=29}}

==Demographics ==
As per the 2018 census, the population of Mocoa was estimated at 38,457 individuals, and increase from approximately 27,104 in 2005.<ref name="CP"/> The population consisted of 18,852 males and 19,605 females. About 8,057 (22.7%) of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 2,139 (9%) was above the age of 65 years.<ref name="CP"/>

== Transport and economy ==
The city is located off the highway connecting [[Pasto]], the capital of [[Narino department]], to [[Puerto Asis]].<ref name="Brit"/> In 2009, the [[San Francisco, Cundinamarca|San Francisco]]-Mocoa road corridor was planned at a cost of US$ 54 million intended to serve as another route connecting the Colombian Pacific coast with the Amazon region.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.iadb.org/en/news/idb-help-colombia-build-san-francisco-mocoa-road|title=IDB to help Colombia build San Francisco-Mocoa road|work=Inter-American Development Bank|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The city is served by the Villa Garzón Airport (Cananguchal Airport), which is well connected by road.<ref name="Report"/>{{rp|page=38}}<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.avionia.com/airport/SKVG|title=Villa Garzón Airport|work=Avionia|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The city serves as the economic center of the department, whose economy is based on [[agriculture]], and livestock rearing.<ref name="Brit"/><ref name="Report"/>{{rp|page=41}}
