'''Loreto''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Zacatecas]], [[Mexico]]. The town is the seventh-largest community in the state, with a 2005 census population of 22,085 inhabitants, while the municipality of which it serves as municipal seat had a population of 43,411.<ref>[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática</ref> The municipality has an area of 427&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (164.87 sq mi).

People celebrate Nuestra Señora de Loreto from 1 to 10 December. It is an agricultural region where people grow primarily lettuce, corn, chili and beans.
This town was founded in 1931 with the name Bimabaletes, and in 1956 its official name changed to Loreto.

It is home to the well known regional band Los 5 de Zacatecas.
