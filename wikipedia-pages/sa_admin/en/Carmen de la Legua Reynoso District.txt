'''Carmen de la Legua Reynoso''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Callao]], [[Peru]]. Its creation dates back to a series of [[Squatting in Peru|informal settlements]] built in the area by inner country migrants during the mid-20th century, ultimately being formally established as a district in late 1964.

== Etymology ==
The district is named in part after a sanctuary dedicated to [[Our Lady of Mount Carmel]], located one [[League (unit)|league]] away from both [[Lima]] and [[Callao]]. Its other name, Reynoso, comes from a [[brickworks]] established by one of the area's first settlers.<ref name=Creacion>{{Cite web |title=Creacion Historica |url=http://www.municarmendelalegua.gob.pe/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=142&Itemid=439 |website=Municipalidad Distrital de Carmen de la Legua Reynoso}}</ref>

== History ==
What would eventually become the district was once covered by shrubland surrounding the road connecting [[Lima]] and [[Callao]], itself surrounded by farmland. The area later known as Reynoso was located next to [[Rímac River]].<ref name=Creacion/> Under the [[Viceroyalty of Peru|Spanish]], a hermitage dedicated to [[John of God]] was built on site by the [[Brothers Hospitallers of Saint John of God|order of the same name]], exactly one [[League (unit)|league]] away from both cities.<ref name=Memoria-2023>{{Cite book |title=Memoria Anual 2023 |publisher=Municipalidad de Carmen de la Legua - Reynoso |year=2023 |language=es |url=https://municarmendelalegua.gob.pe/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MEMORIA-ANUAL-2023.pdf |chapter=I}}</ref> In 1617, a school was established by the [[Carmelites]], and a sanctuary dedicated to [[Our Lady of Mount Carmel]] was built on the site of the former hermitage.<ref name=Memoria-2023/>

Starting in the 20th century, the area became gradually populated by immigrants from the countryside, who [[Squatting in Peru|established settlements]] in the empty farmland surrounding the city. It was officially established under the name in the 1950s by José López Pasos, Alejandro Ladrón De Guevara, and Medina Barios Ántero Lizano, the latter serving as the secretary in charge of zoning and distribution.<ref name=Memoria-2023/> By this time, the area was wholly dedicated to the sale of construction materials,<ref name=Memoria-2023/> and the concession for the extraction and sale of sand—to be used for [[cement]]—had been granted to the ''Confederación de Areneros del Perú''.<ref name=Creacion/> López and his associates established the Reynoso [[brickworks]], also building homes to be able to work at night.<ref name=Creacion/>

The first settlers moved to an area located to the west known as the "Callejón de Reynoso," which eventually became New Reynoso, establishing a homeowners' association headed by López. To the east, other settlers built a slum between Jorge Chávez street and Nicolás Dueñas avenue, known as "La Victoria" (initially "Nuevo Callao"), and whose homeowners' association was headed by Justo Alarcón Rubina.<ref name=Creacion/> Other settlers included refugees from [[Department of Tarapacá (Peru)|Tarapacá]], who left due to the [[War of the Pacific]] and were awarded a lot in the area through a law passed by the government.<ref name=Creacion/><ref name=Memoria-2023/><ref>{{Cite book |title=Analisis de situacion de salud del distrito Carmen de la Legua - Reynoso, 2018 |last=Barrios Hernández |first=Emily Melina |publisher=Dirección Regional de Salud del Callao |year=2019 |pages=13 |language=es |url=https://www.diresacallao.gob.pe/wdiresa/documentos/boletin/epidemiologia/asis/FILE0000312020.pdf |last2=Huamán Salgado |first2=Giajaira Rosa |last3=Sardá Campos |first3=Jared Patrick |chapter=I}}</ref>

The informal settlement of the area led to a dispute with Alberto Cayetano Espantoso Bergmann, the owner of an adjacent estate known as Chacra Puente, during which services were slowly constructed in the area: in 1956, the houses were made of bricks, two wells, six generators, a communal building, two churches, five schools, and one medical outpost.<ref name=Creacion/> In 1964, Juan Razzeto, a deputy representing Callao, presented a project in the Chamber of Deputies at the request of the area's locals. It was approved, and the district was officially established on December 4, through Law No. 15247.<ref name=Creacion/><ref name=Memoria-2023/> In 1966, Marino Peláez Villarreal was chosen as the district's first mayor.<ref name=Creacion/>

A statue dedicated to Our Lady of Mount Carmel was inaugurated in January 2001, being relocated due to the roadworks that aimed to connect [[Jorge Chávez International Airport]] with the rest of the city.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Estatua de la Virgen de Carmen de la Legua será trasladada por obras del Nuevo Aeropuerto Jórge Chávez: ¿dónde será reubicada? |last=Sánchez |first=Jhonatan |date=2023-03-27 |url=https://larepublica.pe/sociedad/2025/03/27/estatua-de-la-virgen-de-carmen-de-la-legua-sera-trasladada-por-obras-del-nuevo-aeropuerto-jorge-chavez-donde-sera-reubicada-evat-1972053 |work=[[La República]]}}</ref>

== Politics ==
Carmen de la Legua Reynoso is under the jurisdiction of its own [[Municipalities of Peru|district municipality]], headed by a mayor.

== Geography ==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 2.12&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 54 meters above [[sea level]].

=== Boundaries ===
* North: [[Callao District]], [[San Martín de Porres District]] (in the [[Lima Province]])
* East: [[Lima District]]
* South and West: Downtown Callao

== Demographics ==
According to the [[Peru 2005 Census|2005 census]] by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 40,439 inhabitants, a population density of 19,075 persons/km<sup>2</sup> and 8,745 households in the district.
