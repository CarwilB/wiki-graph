'''Iztapa''' ({{IPA|es|isˈtapa}}), or '''Puerto de Iztapa''', is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department|Escuintla]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|381.3|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 
20,697 inhabitants.

==History==
When the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonialists arrived in 1527, Iztapa was already an existing settlement, and [[Don Pedro de Alvarado]] commissioned three ships to be built at the port of Iztapa for his subsequent expedition to Peru. The port underwent further work in the late 16th century. On 18 February 1824, a commercial port was established under the name of "Puerto Independencia", which was moved later in the mid 19th century. On 15 March 1833, the government established Iztapa officially and allocated six thousand pesos for the same. As per further decree on 30 April 1834, further land was allocated to develop the cavalry and a road connecting Iztapa and [[Escuintla]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Guanagazapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Iztapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-del-municipio-de-iztapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> [[British people|British]] records from 1850 refer to the place as Yetapa.<ref name="Baily2"/> In 1897, president [[José María Reina Barrios]] attempted to build an inter-oceanic railroad to attract investors during the [[Exposición Centroamericana|Central America Expo]], which included a new terminal at the Iztapa Port.<ref name=macias15julio1897a/>

==Geography==
Iztapa is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|381.3|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Guanagazapa (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0508__guanagazapa/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the southern part of the department, about {{cvt|55|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|130|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Guanagazapa]] to the north, [[Taxisco]] to the east, [[Masagua]] and [[Puerto San José]] to the west, and the Pacific Ocean to the south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Iztapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Iztapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-iztapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|author=SEGEPLAN |title=Municipios de Escuintla, Guatemala |url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=7:escuintla |location=Guatemala |work=Secretaría General de Planificación y Programación de la Presidencia de la República |language=es |access-date=13 July 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150714014759/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=7%3Aescuintla |archive-date=14 July 2015 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Iztapa is considered to be one of the best places in the world to catch [[Sailfish]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Olander |first1=Doug |title=The World's Best Sailfish Spots |url=https://www.sportfishingmag.com/worlds-best-sailfish-spots/ |date=6 June 2019 |publisher=Sport Fishing Magazine |access-date=21 June 2019}}</ref>

===Climate===
Iztapa has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|25.79|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|122.06|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/guanagazapa|title=Guanagazapa|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 20,697 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 10,585 males and 10,112 females. About 27.8% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.7% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (73.3%) was classified as rural, while 26.7% lived in urban areas. About 77.7% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (98%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (1.3%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 86.4%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
