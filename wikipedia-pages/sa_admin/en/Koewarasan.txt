'''Koewarasan''' ({{langx|jvn|ꦏꦸꦮꦫꦱꦤ꧀|Kuwarasan}} {{lit|wellbeing}}) is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Wanica District]]. Its population at the 2012 census was 27,713.<ref name="census"/> It is located to the west of [[Paramaribo]], and is mainly a suburban area. The municipality was founded in 1939, and was originally intended for the [[Javanese people|Javanese]] settlers from [[Java]], however due to [[World War II]], only a single group arrived.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://wanica.nl/Joomla/distrikt-wanica/geschiedenis-van-distrikt-suriname?showall=1|title=Geschiedenis|website=Wanica.nl|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=23 April 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210423051607/https://wanica.nl/Joomla/distrikt-wanica/geschiedenis-van-distrikt-suriname?showall=1|url-status=dead}}</ref> The majority of the population are [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]].<ref name="census"/> Prior to 1939, Koewarasan was an agricultural area used for the cultivation of [[rice]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2016/08/30/concurrentie-in-de-landbouw/|title=Concurrentie in de landbouw|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Sunny Point==
{{anchor|Sunny Point}}The [[Surinamese Interior War]] caused the population of [[Pokigron]] to flee. A large group of civilians [[squatting|squatted]]<ref name="terdege">{{cite news|url=https://www.digibron.nl/viewer/collectie/Digibron/id/41a8a3df99beb854ae5f4a4b45e55375|title=De ondergang van een Surinaams bosnegerdorp|date=2 January 1991|website=Terdege via [[Delpher]]|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref><ref name="sunny_point"/> a terrain on Koewarasan, and have named their village Sunny Point.<ref name="sunny_point">{{cite web|url=https://kansrijksuriname.org/jeugdkoor-in-sunny-point/|title=Wat wij doen in Sunny Point|access-date=20 May 2020|website=Kansrijk Suriname|language=nl}}</ref> A school has been setup by the organisation Kansrijk Suriname, and on 24 March 2018, a [[library]] opened.<ref name="sunny_point"/> On 3 January 2019, 60 families living in Sunny Point-3 faced eviction.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.srherald.com/suriname/2019/01/03/dc-ramai-belooft-spoedige-oplossing-voor-krakers-sunny-point-3/|title=Dc Ramai belooft spoedige oplossing voor krakers Sunny Point-3|website=Suriname Herald|access-date=20 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
