'''Nindirí''' is a town and a  municipality in the [[Masaya (department)|Masaya department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

In 2022 the population was 61,816 in the municipality, and 29,566 in the town. Nindirí is located 3.5&nbsp;km from downtown [[Masaya]] and form the southeastern part of [[Managua|Managua Metropolitan Area]].
