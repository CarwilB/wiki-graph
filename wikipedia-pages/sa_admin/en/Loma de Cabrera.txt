'''Loma de Cabrera''' is the second largest city in the province of [[Dajabón]], Dominican Republic. It is located in the northwest portion of the country, in the [[Cibao]] region.
[[File:BalnearioElSalto.jpg|200px|left|thumb|The Balneario El Salto waterfall in Loma de Cabrera]]
The river named ''Dajabón'', also known as Masacre, runs through Loma de Cabrera. The same river separates the city of [[Dajabón River|Dajabon]] from [[Haiti]]. Loma de Cabrera  has a very popular river falls called "''Balneario El Salto''", which is a very popular tourist place for Dominican residents.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Garabito |first=Francys Josefina Rondón |date=2023-09-11 |title=¿Desde dónde nace el río Masacre y por qué lleva ese nombre? |url=https://cdn.com.do/nacionales/desde-donde-nace-el-rio-masacre-y-por-que-lleva-ese-nombre/ |access-date=2024-04-18 |website=cdn.com.do |language=es}}</ref>
