'''Natales''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "births") is  a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Última Esperanza Province]] in the [[Magallanes and Antartica Chilena Region]] of extreme southern [[Chile]]. Its [[capital (political)|capital]] and only port [[List of cities in Chile|city]] is [[Puerto Natales]].

==Geography==
The commune's area spans {{convert|48974.2|km2|0|abbr=on}},<ref name="INE"/> making it one of the largest communes in Chile, after only [[Antártica]] (which is, however, 25 times Natales' size), and larger than eleven of the country's [[Regions of Chile|16 political regions]]. The [[Cueva del Milodón Natural Monument]] lies {{convert|24|km|0|abbr=on}} northwest of Puerto Natales.

Its capital and only city, [[Puerto Natales]] (2002 population: 16,978), is one of the [[southernmost settlements]] in the world.<ref name="2007INE">{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.cl/canales/chile_estadistico/territorio/division_politico_administrativa/pdf/DPA_COMPLETA.pdf |format=PDF |title=Territorial division of Chile |accessdate=11 September 2010|language=es}}</ref>

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], the Natales commune has 19,116 inhabitants (10,068 men and 9,048 women). Of these, 16,978 (88.8%) lived in [[urban area]] of the city and 2,138 (11.2%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 10.7% (1,841 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses. The commune comprises 96.3% of the total provincial population of 19,855.<ref name= "INE"/> Its population density is only {{convert|0.38|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Administration==
Puerto Natales serves as capital of both the Natales commune and the Última Esperanza Province. As a commune, Natales is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Fernando Paredes Mansilla ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Official"/><ref name="Munitel"/> The regional [[governor]], since 14 July 2021, is Jorge Flies Añón.<ref name="Intendants">{{Cite web |url=https://www.goremagallanes.cl/sitioweb/gobregCL_curriculum.php |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |accessdate=21 September 2021|language=es}}</ref>
* Herminio Fernández García (UDI)
* Ana Mayorga Bahamonde (UDI)
* Tolentino Soto Ríos ([[Christian Democrat Party of Chile|PDC]])
* José Cuyul Rogel ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Alfonso Coñoecar Millalonco ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Felipe Muñoz Muñoz ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Natales is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano Cornejo (PDC) and Gabriel Boric Font ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire Magallanes and Chilean Antarctica Region. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carolina Goic Boroevic (PDC) and Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes Region).
