'''Seropédica''' (''Silk Farm'', {{IPA|pt|seɾoˈpɛdʒikɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in [[Greater Rio de Janeiro]], [[Brazil]], 75&nbsp;km from the [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|state]] capital of [[Rio de Janeiro]].  Its population was 83,092 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|284|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

== Mayor ==
* Lucas Dutra

== Education ==
The municipality is home of the main campus of the [[Federal Rural University of Rio de Janeiro]] (UFRRJ) and an [[Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation|Embrapa]] research institute focused on [[agroecology]].

==Notability==
The bacterium ''[[Herbaspirillum seropedicae]]'' is named after Seropédica, as it was isolated in the locality.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Schmid |first1=Michael |title=The Genus Herbaspirillum |journal=Prokaryotes |date=2006 |page=141 |doi=10.1007/0-387-30745-1_7}}</ref>
