'''Pachalúm''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Quiché Department|El Quiché]].

Pachalúm had been a village in the [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] of [[Joyabaj]], but was recognized as separate municipality in 1986.<ref>Acuerdo Gubernativo 377-86 of the 27th of June 1986, mentioned in  http://pachalum.pa.funpic.org/ {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090522220929/http://pachalum.pa.funpic.org/ |date=2009-05-22 }}</ref>

== History ==
{{See also|Joyabaj|El Quiché}}
The name «Pachalum» originated from the ''Pachecas' game'' which men practiced under the shadow of a "Chalum" tree during the early years of the 20th century.<ref name=Barrios/>

=== During the Spanish colony ===

José de Alvarado —relative of conquistador [[Pedro de Alvarado]]— was given from the [[Kingdom of Guatemala]] authorities more than one hundred fifty acres of land in the [[Joyabaj|Santa María Xoyavaj]] region, dando origen a los establecimientos de colonizadores españoles en la región.  Pachalúm was mentioned for the first time in 1817, as place for sugar production settled by Spanish families that arrived there from [[San Martín Jilotepeque]], [[El Chol|Santa Cruz El Chol]] and from most of the modern [[Baja Verapaz Department]].<ref name=Barrios/>

=== After Independence from Spain ===

After the revolution against field marshal [[Vicente Cerna y Cerna]] in 1871, the families of the revolutionary leaders that helped the Liberal revolt asked [[Joyabaj]] mayor that their settlement became a village, which was granted on 9 August 1872.<ref name=gramajo/>

== Administrative division ==

The municipality has an area of 100 km; besides the municipal capital, there are eight villages, thirteen settlements and five farm fields.<ref name=Barrios>{{cite web|url=http://pachalum.pa.funpic.org/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=50&Itemid=55 |title=Historia de Pachalúm |website=Municipalidad de Pachalúm |location=Guatemala |access-date=9 November 2009 |year=2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090524010931/http://pachalum.pa.funpic.org/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=50&Itemid=55 |archive-date=May 24, 2009 }}</ref><ref name=gramajo>{{cite web|url=http://munipachalum.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/portada.jpg&imgrefurl=http://munipachalum.org/la-municipalidad/oficinas-y-dependencias-municipales/centro-municipal-de-atencion-integral/|title=Historia de Pachalum|author=Barrios Gramajo, Mario Jobel|date=n.d.|website=Municipalidad de Pachalum|location=Guatemala|access-date=21 January 2016}}{{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Topography ==

Pachalúm is located in [[Sierra de Chuacús]], specifically at the bottom of Tuncaj Hill, where several sprinkles come down. It is also close to the [[Motagua river ]] basin, cuyos afluentes, los ríos Las Vegas y Tumbadero, humedecen el terreno de la localidad.<ref name=gramajo/>

== Municipal government and infrastructure ==

The first elections ever held in Pachalúm resulted in victory of Vicente de la Roca in 1986, but his term was not that effective due to the lack of experience on managing a municipality; the next mayor was Rafael Elías who focused on the area development. Afterwards, teach Reynabel Estrada Roca was elected, serving for three terms.<ref name=Barrios/>

Among those projects finished during Estrada Roca's term in office are: 
* paved road between Pachalúm and [[Guatemala City]], via Concuán
* Grocery market building
* Municipal Hotel
* Sports complex
* Urban area pavement
* irrigation project, led by Hugo Lorenzo Duarte Estrada<ref name=gramajo/>

Other important access roads to Pachalúm are:
* Pachalúm -[[Chimaltenango]], via [[San Martín Jilotepeque]], 70% paved
* Pachalum - [[Santa Cruz del Quiché]], 92 km long, with 16 km unpaved
* Pachalum - [[Cubulco]], 20 km long<ref name=Barrios/>
