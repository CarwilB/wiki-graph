'''Vigia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northeastern part of the state of [[Pará]], [[Brazil]].  The town was founded on 16 January 1616.  The population is 54,172 (2020 est.) in an area of 539.08 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/3P8 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>
