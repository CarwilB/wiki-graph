'''Marcovia''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Choluteca department|Choluteca]], on the [[Gulf of Fonseca]].

== History ==
Marcovia was called Pueblo Nuevo until 1882 when it became a municipality and was renamed Marcovia by the president, Marco Aurelio Soto. The majority of the people in Marcovia used to be from El Salvador.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Marcovia, Choluteca – INE|url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/2018/08/29/marcovia-choluteca-2018/|access-date=2021-04-19|website=www.ine.gob.hn}}</ref>

== Demographics{{coord|13|17|N|87|19|W|display=title|region:HN_type:city_source:GNS-enwiki}} ==
According to the 2013 census, 53% of the population works in agriculture. Fifty-five percent use some type of private water system, and 48% uses public electricity to light their houses. Eighty-four percent use firewood for cooking and 13% of houses have at least one car. Eighty percent of Morolica has a basic education level.
