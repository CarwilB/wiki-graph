'''Bongará''' is a province of Peru's [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]].<ref name="Amazonas">{{cite web|url=http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|title=Provincias y Distritos|date=n.d.|language=es|website=Gobierno Regional de Amazonas|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130615121036/http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|archivedate=15 June 2013|url-status=dead|accessdate=15 November 2014}}</ref> It borders to the north with the [[Condorcanqui Province|province of Condorcanqui]], to the east with the [[San Martín Region]] and [[Loreto Region]], to the south with the [[Chachapoyas Province|province of Chachapoyas]] and to the west with the provinces of [[Luya Province|Luya]] and [[Utcubamba Province|Utcubamba]].<ref name="InfoGob">{{cite web|url=http://www.infogob.com.pe/Localidad/ubigeo.aspx?IdUbigeo=010300&IdLocalidad=110&IdTab=0|title=Información Electoral|date=n.d.|language=es|website=INFOgob (Jurado Nacional de Elecciones)|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141115223838/http://www.infogob.com.pe/Localidad/ubigeo.aspx?IdUbigeo=010300&IdLocalidad=110&IdTab=0|archivedate=15 November 2014|url-status=live|accessdate=15 November 2014}}</ref>

It presents a varied geographical configuration. The branches of the [[Andes]] cross it from west to east, dividing it in three regions with proper characteristics:

*A swampy region, humid, that spreads between the narrow valley of the [[Utcubamba river]] and the ''quebrada'' (cliff) called Panhuaico. It has a cold climate. The zones of Jatunpampa, Kurupampa and Santa Cruz are located in this place. This vast region, because of the nature of its soil that has abundant natural [[grassland]]s, is called to be an excellent cattle zone.
*A region with many geographical accidents, stony soils, with high crags which heights fluctuate between 2,000 and 4,000 m. As an example of the important types of this [[orography]], there are the mountain peaks] of Tocarpo, Cashul, Chambarán, Yumbam and Añacaja. This orographical system runs from south to north and its long ride gives place to a deep region (''hoyada''), which serves as a cradle to the picturesque lake [[Pumaqucha (Amazonas)|Pumaqucha]].
*Finally, an oriental branch goes towards the north, framing the [[Chiriaco River|Chiriaco]] or Imaza river and moves by the East. In this place, the rainforest opens itself to the districts of [[Yambrasbamba District|Yambrasbamba]], [[Chisquilla District|Chisquilla]] and [[Corosha District|Corosha]] up to the limits with the [[San Martín Region]]. In this orographical chain there are the mountain peaks of Yerbabuena, Quitaya, Chiquipata, Calpotrero, Quilahuaita, Chonta Pozo and Ventana.

The hydrographic system of this province is represented by the basins of the [[Utcubamba river]] to which numerous gorges come together for both margins and serves as a limit with [[Luya Province]]. The [[Chiriaco River|Chiriaco]] or Imaza river crosses it on its South-Western part. The lake Pumaqucha is the principal part of this hydrography that was already described.
