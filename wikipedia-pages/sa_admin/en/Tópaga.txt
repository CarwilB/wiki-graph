'''Tópaga''' ({{IPA|es|ˈtopaɣa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Sugamuxi Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|Colombian department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Tópaga is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] with the urban center at an altitude of {{convert|2900|m|ft}} while the topography varies over the municipality from {{convert|2400|m|ft}} to {{convert|3200|m|ft}}. It borders [[Nobsa]] and [[Corrales, Boyacá|Corrales]] in the west, in the east [[Mongua]] and [[Gámeza]], in the north Gámeza and in the south [[Monguí]] and [[Sogamoso]]. Department capital [[Tunja]] is {{convert|98|km|mi}} away. [[Chicamocha River]] flows through Tópaga.<ref name=websiteTopaga>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150604024327/http://www.topaga-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tópaga]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Tópaga comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Behind father river".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Tópaga] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Tópaga was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The village was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' with the same name as the village, loyal to the ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]]. The ''iraca'' was educated by messenger god [[Bochica]] according to the [[Muisca religion|religion of the Muisca]].<ref name=websiteTopaga/>

Modern Tópaga was founded in 1593 by Álvaro Leiva.<ref name=websiteTopaga/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Tópaga is [[coal mining]], used to produce electrical energy.<ref name=websiteTopaga/>
