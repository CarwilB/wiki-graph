'''Salto de Agua''' is a [[town]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 57,253,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Salto de Agua|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=077|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304123133/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=077|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 49,300 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Salto de Agua|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07077a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=July 27, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927205644/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07077a.htm|archive-date=September 27, 2007}}</ref> It covers an area of 1289.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

Other than the town of Salto de Agua, the municipality had 387 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Egipto, Chiapas|Egipto]] (1,419), [[Belisario Domínguez, Chiapas|Belisario Domínguez]] (1,393), [[San Miguel, Chiapas|San Miguel]] (1,373), [[Santa María, Chiapas|Santa María]] (1,317), [[Ignacio Zaragoza, Chiapas|Ignacio Zaragoza]] (1,303), [[Río Jordán, Chiapas|Río Jordán]] (1,197), [[Estrella de Belén, Chiapas|Estrella de Belén]] (1,151), [[Jerusalén, Chiapas|Jerusalén]] (1,151), [[Arroyo Palenque, Chiapas|Arroyo Palenque]] (1,137), and [[Cenobio Aguilar (La Trinidad), Chiapas|Cenobio Aguilar (La Trinidad)]] (1,006), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
