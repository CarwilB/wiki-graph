'''Masatepe''' ([[Nawat language|Nawat]]: ''Masatepek''), ({{IPA|es|masaˈtepe}}) is one of the nine municipalities of the [[Masaya Department]] in [[Nicaragua]]. It is located on the plateau of the villages 50 kilometers from [[Managua]] along the road to Masaya. It belongs to the tourist corridor of " Los Pueblos Blancos" on top of the coffee-producing Volcanic Plateau. The origin of the word "Masatepe" comes from the [[Nawat language]] and is named after the [[Nahuas|Nahua]] chiefdom of Masatepek, which was located in the present-day department of Masaya.<ref>Vida de González Dávila, Gil. Ávila, c. 1480 – 21.IV.1526. Descubridor y conquistador. et al., 2012</ref><ref>Historia general y natural de las Indias, islas y tierra-firme del mar océano; por el Capitán Gonzalo Fernández de Oviedo y Valdés; Primer Cronista del Nuevo Mundo; publícala la Real Academia de la Historia.- Tomo IV.- Libro XLII.- Capitulo XIII.</ref><ref>Colonización de américa, cuando la historia marcha, de Oviedo y Valdés, Gonzalo c. 1480 - 1557, 2006</ref> Masatepek is a combination of the Nawat words ''Masat'' (deer),<ref name="Campbell1985">{{cite book|last=Campbell|first=Lyle|title=The Pipil Language of El Salvador|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=N5bmUh7jproC&pg=PA10|date=January 1, 1985|publisher=Walter de Gruyter|isbn=978-3-11-088199-8|pages=10–12}}</ref> and ''-tepek'' (hill).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.revistas.unam.mx/index.php/antropologia/article/view/22958|title= El Nawat de Cuzcatlan}}</ref> The literal translation of Masatepek is "deer hill". The [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] inhabitants of Masaya are the [[Nicarao people|Nahuas]] and the [[:es: Chorotegas|Chorotegas]], and the Nahuas who still inhabit the municipality dominate the [[cocoa bean|cocoa]] production in Masatepe.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://catalogosiidca.csuca.org/Record/UNANL.13389|title= Preparation of a hygiene plan for the Nicaraos microenterprise cocoa industry in the department of Masaya}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cadenacacaoca.info/CDOC-Deployment/documentos/InformeNicaragua.pdf|title= Nicaragua National Report}}</ref>

== History and Etymology ==

The name ''Masatepe'' originates from the Nawat language, and is a combination of the Nawat words ''Masat'' (deer), and ''-tepek'' (hill).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.mined.gob.sv/nawat/modulos/taksalis_1/MODULO%201.pdf#:~:text=El%20n%C3%A1huat%2C%20catalogada%20como%20lengua%20en%20peligro,dialectos%20o%20variaciones%20dialectales%20en%20algunos%20municipios.|title= ¡Hablemos náhuat! (page 64 of 95)}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://revistas.ues.edu.sv/index.php/launiversidad/article/view/998/922|title= PIPIL DIALECTOLOGY}}</ref> Therefore the literal translation of Masatepek is "deer hill", and was named by the [[Nicarao people|Nahuas]] in reference to the [[white-tailed deer]] that inhabit the region.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://nicaragua-travels.com/granada-region/masaya#:~:text=What%20an%20incredible%20opportunity%20to,to%20hundreds%20of%20bat%20nests.|title= Masaya, Nicaragua: town, volcano & crafts market}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://nicaragua-travels.com/blog/news/masaya-volcano-practical-information#:~:text=Despite%20ongoing%20volcanic%20activity%20and,andtrees%20adapted%20to%20arid%20soils.|title= Visit Masaya volcano in Nicaragua: Practical guide}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.nicaliving.com/masaya/volcano-national-park/#:~:text=The%20eruptions%20have%20had%20a,deer%2C%20iguanas%2C%20and%20monkeys.|title= Masaya Volcano National Park}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.inaturalist.org/taxa/522700-Odocoileus-virginianus-nemoralis/browse_photos#:~:text=Photos%20of%20Nicaraguan%20white%2Dtailed,Subspecies%20Odocoileus%20virginianus%20nemoralis)%20%C2%B7%20iNaturalist|title= Photos of Nicaraguan White-tailed deer}}</ref> In addition, Masatepek was a [[Nahuas|Nahua]] chiefdom located in what is now the Masaya department. The Nahuas of Masatepek inhabited [[Nindirí, Masaya|Nindiri]], Niquinohomo, Monimbó, and Masatepe, and coexisted with the [[:es: Chorotegas|Chorotegas]] who also inhabited Masaya, as well as [[Chibchan languages|Chibchan]] minorities who were assimilated into Nahua culture and society.<ref>{{Cite book|title=The Indigenous People of Mesoamerica and Central America|last=Carmack|first=Robert|publisher=Lexington Books|year=2017|isbn=9781498558976|pages=81–83}}</ref><ref>Los Indios precolombinos de Nicaragua y Costa Rica en los siglos XV y XVI, 2009 - Bolaños, Enrique</ref><ref>Historia de la Gran Nicoya en el sur de Mesoamérica, Jiménez-Santana 1997</ref><ref>Colonización de américa, cuando la historia marcha, de Oviedo y Valdés, Gonzalo c. 1480 - 1557, 2006</ref><ref>Historia general y natural de las Indias, islas y tierra-firme del mar océano; por el Capitán Gonzalo Fernández de Oviedo y Valdés; Primer Cronista del Nuevo Mundo; publícala la Real Academia de la Historia. Tomo IV. Libro XLII. Capitulo XIII.</ref>

== Weather and Location ==
Masatepe is located on top of the Plateau of towns, at an altitude of 475m, the total area can be divided into two main different types of biomes:

1- Rain forest (Usually has native trees and Coffee Plantations. Elv: (400 - 500m)

2- Dry Valley. ( can be found near the coast of the Lagoon, and the Masaya Volcano ). Elv: (90 - 250m)

The temperature fluctuates during the year, being mostly warm. During the winter months (November - January) the temperatures become cooler, and there are usually foggy mornings.
