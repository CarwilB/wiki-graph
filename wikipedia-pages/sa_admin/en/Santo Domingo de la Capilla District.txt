'''Santo Domingo de la Capilla District''' is one of fifteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Cutervo Province|Cutervo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

The district of Santo Domingo de la Capilla was created by an Act of January 12, 1956, in the government of President Manuel A. Odria.
In ecclesiastical terms, it is part of the sub-diocese of [[Chota, Peru|Chota]], suffragan of the Archdiocese of [[Piura]].
