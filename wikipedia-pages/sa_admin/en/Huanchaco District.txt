'''Huanchaco District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Trujillo Province, Peru|Trujillo]],<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> located in [[La Libertad Region]], [[Peru]]. The district in the year 2017 had   68,409 inhabitants.<ref>National Institute of Statistics and Information (INEI)- Peru  {{cite web|url=http://desa.inei.gob.pe/censos2007/tabulados/?id%3DResultadosCensales |title=Consulta de Resultados Censales |access-date=2012-11-04 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121011062227/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/censos2007/tabulados/?id=ResultadosCensales |archive-date=2012-10-11 }}</ref>

Huanchaco is one of the tourist districts of [[Trujillo, Peru|Trujillo]], has several tourist attractions, starting with the citadel of [[Chan Chan]], the hill called "Cerro Campana" beaches like Huanchaco beach, Playa Azul, La Ribera, La Poza, El Silencio, El elio, etc. In Huanchaco there are multiple restaurants, hotels, lodges and private residences with ocean views. Huanchaco annually receives tourists from all over the world especially in summer, the horses of reeds or ''"[[caballitos de totora]]"'', among other attractions is most appreciated by holidaymakers.
