'''Confins''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2025 is estimated to be 7,758 people.<ref name="IBGE" /> The area of the municipality is {{cvt|42.008|km2}}. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Belo Horizonte. It is home of the international airport of Belo Horizonte, [[Tancredo Neves International Airport]].
