'''Saña District''', also '''Zaña District''', is one of twenty [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Chiclayo Province|Chiclayo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The town of [[Zaña]] is the capital of the district.
