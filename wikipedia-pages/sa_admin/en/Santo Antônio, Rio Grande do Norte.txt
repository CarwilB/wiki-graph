'''Santo Antônio''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] for "Saint Anthony") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]])'' in the state of [[Rio Grande do Norte]] in [[Brazil]]. The population in 2020 was 24,280 and the area is 301&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The elevation is 92 m.
