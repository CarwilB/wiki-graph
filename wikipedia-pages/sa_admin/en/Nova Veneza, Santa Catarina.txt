'''Nova Veneza''' (a [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] name meaning "New Venice") is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], [[Southern Brazil]]. It has 15,342 inhabitants<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/nova-veneza/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and was settled by immigrants from [[Venice]] in 1891.{{fact|date=May 2023}}

People of Venetian descent make up 95% of the population.<ref>[http://www.portalveneza.com.br/a_cidade.php Nova Veneza] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080819191657/http://www.portalveneza.com.br/a_cidade.php |date=2008-08-19 }}</ref>
