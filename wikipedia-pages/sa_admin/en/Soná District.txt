'''Soná District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Veraguas Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the 2010 Panamanian census was 27,833;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 13, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 29,774.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|1519|km2}}. The capital lies at the city of [[Soná, Panama|Soná]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

It also hosts the Soná Football Club 1835.

The mayor of the city is Aristides Ortiz Arosemena.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.expresionesamp.net/AE02/E70122006/sespec.htm |title=Expresiones_Especial_Playa El Flor: Belleza y Conflicto en la Costa sur  de Veraguas. |publisher=Expresiones.  Para una Sociedad Participativa. |access-date=2 August 2014 | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080219214333/http://www.expresionesamp.net/AE02/E70122006/sespec.htm |archivedate=19 February 2008| url-status=dead}}</ref>

The city is referenced in [[Prison Break]] based on a local urban legend of an "underground prison".
