'''Santa Teresa''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Carazo (department)|Carazo]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It contains 42 communities.{{cn|date=March 2026}}

Olympic middle-distance runner [[Edgar Cortez]] was born in Santa Teresa.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.olympedia.org/athletes/126814|title=Edgar Cortez|publisher=Olympedia|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref>
