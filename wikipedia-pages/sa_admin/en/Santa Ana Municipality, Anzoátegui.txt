The '''Santa Ana Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n [[Anzoátegui State]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 9,636.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Santa Ana, Anzoátegui|Santa Ana]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Santa Ana Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Santa Ana Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 10,919 (up from 9,675 in 2000).  This amounts to 0.7% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|9.22|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
