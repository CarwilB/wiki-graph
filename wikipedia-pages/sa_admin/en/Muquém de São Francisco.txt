'''Muquém de São Francisco''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Muquém de São Francisco covers {{convert|3,853.185|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 11,417 with a population density of 3 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
