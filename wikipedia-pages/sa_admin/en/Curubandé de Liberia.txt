'''Curubandé''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Liberia (canton)|Liberia]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Curubandé was created on 26 November 1971 by Decreto Ejecutivo 2077-G. Segregated from [[Liberia District|Liberia]].<ref name="dta2017" />

== Geography ==
Curubandé has an area of {{formatnum:81.14}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:322}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

==Villages==
Administrative center of the district is Cereceda. Other villages are Colorado and Curubandé.

==Economy==
In recent years, Curubandé has become a center of tourism within Costa Rica. This is due in large part to the nearby [[Rincon de la Vieja National Park]].

Curubandé is also the site of [[Las Pailas Geothermal Central]], a large [[geothermal power]] plant that is being built to provide a large portion of Costa Rica's electric energy needs.
