'''Poço Redondo''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈposu ʁeˈdõdu|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population is 35,122 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|1212|km2}},<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> which makes it the largest municipality in that state.
