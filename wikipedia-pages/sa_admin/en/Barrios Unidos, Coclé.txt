'''Barrios Unidos ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Aguadulce District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|64.5|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 9,390 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|145.5|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 58 of July 29, 1998, owing to the Declaration of Unconstitutionality of Law 1 of 1982.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 8,610.<ref name=inec/>
