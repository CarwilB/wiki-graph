'''Serrinha''' is a city in the [[Bahia|state of Bahia]], in eastern [[Brazil]].

==Geography==

The population is 81,286 people.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/serrinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The mayor is Cyro Oliveira Silva Novais of the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement]].

The city is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Serrinha]].

== History ==
The town's founding date is 13 June 1876.
