'''Santa María Alotepec''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. It is part of the [[Sierra Mixe]] district within the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca]] region.

==Name==
The name Alotepec means "hill of the macaws".<ref name=INF>{{cite web |title=Santa María Alotepec |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20394a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=2010-07-22 |archive-date=2006-04-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060427224342/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20394a.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of {{convert|149.27|km²}} at an altitude of {{convert|1,460|m}} above sea level. Trees include pine, oak and cedars. Fruits include orange, banana, [[Pouteria sapota|mamey sapote]], [[Manilkara zapota|sapodilla]], mango, lime, [[custard apple]] and avocado. Wild animals include foxes, deer, armadillo, [[tepescuincle]], wild boar and anteater. There is a great variety of butterflies.<ref name=INF/>
==Population==
In 2020, the municipality had 806 households with a total population of 2,736, of whom 2,434 spoke an indigenous language, primarily [[Mixe languages]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=INEGI |date= |title=Santa María Alotepec, Oaxaca (20394) |url=https://en.www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas |access-date=2025-11-10 |website=www.inegi.org.mx |language=es}}</ref>

In 2005, the municipality had 682 households with a total population of 2,526, of whom 2,262 spoke an indigenous language. 90% of the population were engaged in agriculture. A few also keep domestic animals.<ref name="INF" />

The [[Union of Indigenous Communities of the Isthmus Region]], a co-operative founded in 1982, assists in the production and distribution of local products, notably coffee, under a [[fair trade]] label.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.gpiatlantic.org/conference/reports/2103uciri.pdf |title=Union of Indigenous Communities of the Isthmus Region|publisher=GPIAtlantic |accessdate=2010-07-18}}</ref>
