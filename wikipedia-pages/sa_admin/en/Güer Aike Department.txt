'''Güer Aike Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It covers an area of 33,841&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and had a population of 113,267 at the 2010 Census which rose to 137,895 at the 2022 Census. The seat of the department is in [[Río Gallegos]] which also has the majority of the population with 95,796 inhabitants in 2010 and 115,524 in 2022. 

[[Río Turbio, Santa Cruz|Río Turbio]] is the second largest town with 11,077 inhabitants in 2022, while [[Veintiocho de Noviembre]] has the next largest population with 9,478 inhabitants in 2022. The department's name means 'large camp' in the [[Tehuelche language|''aonikenk'']] (or 'tehuelche') language of southern [[Patagonia]].
