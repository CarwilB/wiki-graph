'''Maria da Fé''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 14,056 people. The area of the municipality is 203.774&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Minas Gerais|South/South-West of Minas Gerais]] and to the microregion of [[Itajubá]].

== Geography ==
=== Climate ===
Maria da Fé is located in the dominant mesothermic climates in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|southeast]] and [[South Region, Brazil|south of Brazil]], associated with its altitude. The city features a [[Oceanic climate|temperate oceanic climate]]  ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref name="Climate">{{Cite web |title=Médias climatológicas de Maria da Fé - MG |url=https://www.amantesdotempo.com.br/2017/05/medias-de-temperatura-anual-em-maria-da.html|access-date=17 March 2025}}</ref> as well as [[Campos do Jordão]]. The average temperature is 17&nbsp;°C (maximum average 23.3&nbsp;°C and minimum average of 10.1&nbsp;°C), with warm summers but not as hot as places in valleys in the interior of the country and the winters are colds to [[Brazil]] and [[Minas Gerais]]. The total precipitation is 1738&nbsp;mm.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/48186961|title=ESPAÇAMENTO ENTRE PLANTAS NO DESEMPENHO DE JARDIM CLONAL DE CULTIVARES DE OLIVEIRA|website=ResearchGate|language=en|access-date=2018-11-17}}</ref> It is the only city in the state where the average temperature rise trend per decade reaches 1&nbsp;°C.<ref>{{Cite journal|last1=Ávila|first1=Léo Fernandes|last2=Mello|first2=Carlos Rogério de|last3=Yanagi|first3=Sílvia de Nazaré Monteiro|last4=Neto|first4=Olívio Bahia Sacramento|date=2014-06-11|title=Tendências de temperaturas mínimas e máximas do ar no Estado de Minas Gerais|url=https://seer.sct.embrapa.br/index.php/pab/article/view/19032|journal=Pesquisa Agropecuária Brasileira|language=pt|volume=49|issue=4|pages=247–256|doi=10.1590/S0100-204X2014000400002|issn=1678-3921|doi-access=free}}</ref> Being in the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]], [[frost]] is a common event in normal winter. It can go from 10 occasions in several years.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.even3.com.br/Anais/micrometeorologia2017/64018-AVALIACAO-DA-OCORRENCIA-DE-GEADA-NA-SERRA-DA-MANTIQUEIRA-NO-PERIODO-ENTRE-2009-E-2014|title=Avaliação Da Ocorrência De Geada Na Serra Da Mantiqueira No Período Entre 2009 E 2014|work=Even3|access-date=2018-11-17|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

The city is one of the coldest of [[Minas Gerais]] and [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Citation|title=As dez cidades mais frias de Minas Gerais|url=https://www.em.com.br/app/noticia/gerais/2023/04/24/interna_gerais,1485159/as-dez-cidades-mais-frias-de-minas-gerais.shtml}}</ref> According to data from the [[Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia|National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET), referring to the period between 1976 and 2016, the lowest temperature recorded in Maria da Fé was -8.4&nbsp;°C on July 21, 1981,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83015&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1976&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,,1,,,,,,,,,,,,,|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados diários - temperatura mínima (°C) - Maria da Fé|access-date=2018-11-17}}</ref> and the highest reached 34,4&nbsp;°C on January 19, 2015.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83015&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1976&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,1,,,,,,,,,,,,,,|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados diários - temperatura máxima (°C) - Maria da Fé|access-date=2018-11-17}}</ref> The largest accumulated rainfall in 24 hours reached 173&nbsp;mm (mm) on January 3, 2000. Other large accumulations equal or greater than 100&nbsp;mm were 125&nbsp;mm on October 14, 1995, 103.4&nbsp;mm on November 26, 2006, and 100.2&nbsp;mm on February 25, 1995.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83015&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1976&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,,,,,,,,,1,,,,,,|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados diários - precipitação (mm) - Maria da Fé|newspaper=Ministério da Agricultura, Pecuária e Abastecimento|access-date=2018-11-17}}</ref> The lowest relative humidity index reached 15% in the afternoon of August 20, 2005.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=83015&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1976&mRelDtFim=31/12/2018&mAtributos=,,,,1,,,,,,,,,,,,|title=BDMEP - série histórica - dados horários - umidade relativa (%) - Maria da Fé|access-date=2018-11-17}}</ref> In recent records there were years like 2013 where there were 23 nights with [[frost]] but the following year only 3 days, but varies considerably from year to year going from a few days to more than a dozen. The average of the first frost is on May 18 and the last on August 14, and may occur from early April until the end of September.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.climamariadafe.com.br/|title=Estação Meteorológica Sítio da Laje|last=Brzezina|first=Jachym|website=Estação Meteorológica Sítio da Laje|access-date=2019-08-01}}</ref>{{Weather box
