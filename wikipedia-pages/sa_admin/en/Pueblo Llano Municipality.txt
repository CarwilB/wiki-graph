The '''Pueblo Llano Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of 
[[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 
11,706.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Pueblo Llano]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Pueblo Llano Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip{{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The municipality was part of the [[Miranda Municipality, Mérida|Miranda Municipality]] until 14 June 1987, when it became the Autonomous Municipality Pueblo Llano. It dropped "autonomous" from its name in 1992. Pueblo Llano, 105.1 FM, was launched in 1992.

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the north east of Mérida in the Venezuelan Andes. The area is mountainous, with an average altitude of 2300m. Average annual temperatures are around 18&nbsp;°C.

==Demographics==
The Pueblo Llano Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 11,706 (up from 9,792 in 2000).  
This amounts to 1.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population 
density is {{convert|211.6|/mi2|/km2|disp=preunit|people&nbsp;|people|}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pueblo Llano Municipality is Antonio José Santiago Santiago, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 86% of the vote.
