'''San Carlos de Guaroa''' ({{IPA|es|saŋˈkaɾlos ɣwaˈɾoa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Plane crash==
{{main|2019 Laser Aéreo Douglas DC-3 crash}}
On 9 March 2019, a [[Douglas DC-3]] operated by Latinoamericana de Servicios Aéreo (Laser Aéreo) crashed near the town.<ref name="2019-03-09-avherald">{{cite news |last1=Hradecky |first1=Simon |title=Crash: Laser Colombia DC3 near Villavicencio on Mar 9th 2019, lost height on approach |url=http://avherald.com/h?article=4c52f8b2&opt=0 |access-date=11 March 2019 |work=avherald.com |publisher=The Aviation Herald |date=9 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="PP675640">{{cite web |url=https://www.pulzo.com/nacion/estrello-avioneta-departamento-meta-PP657640 |title=Se estrelló avioneta en el Meta: confirman 12 víctimas, entre las que habría una alcaldesa |date=9 March 2019 |trans-title=Plane crashed in Meta: 12 victims confirmed, including a mayor |publisher=Pulzo |language=Spanish |access-date=9 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="2019-03-09-bbc">{{cite news |title=Colombia plane crash 'kills 12' |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-latin-america-47510174 |access-date=11 March 2019 |publisher=BBC News |date=9 March 2019}}</ref> The plane was on a flight from [[Jorge Enrique González Torres Airport]], [[San José del Guaviare]] to [[La Vanguardia Airport]], [[Villavicencio]]. All 14 people on board were killed, including the mayor of [[Taraira]]. The crew were attempting to land at Villavicencio and had just declared an emergency.<ref name="2019-03-09-avherald" />
