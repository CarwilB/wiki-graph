'''Santo Amaro da Imperatriz''', or simply Santo Amaro, is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] located close to the state capital [[Florianópolis]] and is connected to [[Palhoça]] by [[BR-282]] in the northeast.

==Location==

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|84130|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Tabuleiro State Park]], a protected area created in 1975.
The lushly-forested park protects the sources of the [[Vargem do Braço River|Vargem do Braço]], [[Cubatão River (south Santa Catarina)|Cubatão]] and [[D'Una River|D'Una]] rivers, which supply most of the drinking water for greater Florianópolis and the south coast region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra do Tabuleiro – FATMA}}|language=pt|title=Parque Estadual da Serra do Tabuleiro|publisher=FATMA: Fundação do meio Ambiente|url=http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-da-serra-do-tabuleiro|access-date=2016-06-11|archive-date=2016-05-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160510084252/http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-da-serra-do-tabuleiro|url-status=dead}}</ref>
There are several farms in the municipality. The only urban zone is situated in [[Downtown]] Santo Amaro.

==Parties and festivals==
The '''Festa do Divino Espírito Santo''' (Divine Holy Spirit Party) is the main religious celebration of the Florianópolis Region. '''Festa do Milho''' (Corn Festival) is another important festival of Santo Amaro.

==Economy==
Even though Santo Amaro is a small city, important enterprises were founded in the city. '''Imperatriz Supermarkets''' was founded in 1974 and is the most profitable supermarket of Santa Catarina. '''É de Casa Supermarkets''' is also another important supermarkets network. It is also the headquarters of LOJA DA IRRIGAÇÃO, distributor of RAIN BIRD in Santa Catarina.

'''Auto Viação Imperatriz''' is the bus enterprise that drives people to [[Águas Mornas]], [[Palhoça]], [[São José, Santa Catarina|São José]] and [[Florianópolis]]. '''Imperatriz''' is also another important enterprise that manufactures [[ice-cream]].{{cn|date=November 2023}}
