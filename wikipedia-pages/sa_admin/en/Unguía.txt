'''Unguía''' is a municipality and town in the far north of the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Unguía has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with heavy rainfall from April to December and little to moderate rainfall from January to March. It is the driest place in the extremely wet department of [[Chocó Department|Chocó]] with only {{convert|2392|mm|in|1|disp=or}} of rain.
