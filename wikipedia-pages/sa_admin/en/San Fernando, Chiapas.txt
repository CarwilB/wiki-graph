'''San Fernando''' is a [[town]] and [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]]. 

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 41,793,<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Fernando (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/chiapas/07079__san_fernando/ |access-date=2024-02-21 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref><ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=San Fernando|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=079|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304120648/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=079|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 26,436 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Fernando|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07074a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|accessdate=1 August 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930024227/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07074a.htm|archivedate=30 September 2007}}</ref> It covers an area of 258.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the town of San Fernando had a population of 9,651.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of San Fernando, the municipality had 196 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[El Progreso, Chiapas|El Progreso]] (2,704), [[El Copalar, Chiapas|El Copalar]] (2,039), [[Francisco I. Madero, Chiapas|Francisco I. Madero]] (1,993), [[Gabriel Esquinca, Chiapas|Gabriel Esquinca]] (1,968), [[Benito Juárez, Chiapas|Benito Juárez]] (1,488), [[Viva Cárdenas, Chiapas|Viva Cárdenas]] (1,431), [[Álvaro Obregón, Chiapas|Álvaro Obregón]] (1,126), and [[16 de Septiembre, Chiapas|16 de Septiembre]] (1,020), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
