'''Toledo''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-02 |title=TOLEDO |url=https://corregimientos.antioquia.gov.co/toledo/ |access-date=2025-01-02 |website=TOLEDO - Corregimientos de Antioquia}}</ref> Part of the subregion of [[Northern Antioquia]], it lies at an altitude of 1,850 m (6,070 ft).
