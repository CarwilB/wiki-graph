'''Taquara''' is a [[municipality]] in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Taquara is located 72&nbsp;kilometers (44 miles) from [[Porto Alegre]], the state capital. Taquara's population was estimated at 57,584 in 2020.
