'''El Plateado de Joaquín Amaro''' or '''Joaquín Amaro''', also known as '''El Plateado''' and as '''General Joaquín Amaro''', is a municipality and community in [[Zacatecas]], [[Mexico]], located {{convert|105|km|abbr=on}} SW of [[Zacatecas, Zacatecas|Zacatecas City]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Catálogo de Claves de Entidades Federativas, Municipios y Localidades |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/ageeml/ |website=[[INEGI]] |language=es |access-date=March 4, 2026}}</ref>  It is bordered by the municipal divisions of Villanueva, Tabasco, Momax, and [[Tlaltenango de Sánchez Román]].
