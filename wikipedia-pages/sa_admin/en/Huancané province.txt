'''Huancané''' (from [[Aymara language|Aymara]] '''Wanqani''' {{audio|Ay-Wankani.oga|listen}}, meaning "the one with big stones") is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Puno Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}, Retrieved January 2, 2008</ref> The capital of the province is the city of [[Huancané]].

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Aymara language|Aymara]] is the language which the majority of the population (53.26%) learnt to speak in childhood, 30.82% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language and  15.70% using [[Spanish language|Spanish]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
