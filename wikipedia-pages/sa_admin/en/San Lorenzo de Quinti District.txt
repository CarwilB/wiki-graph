'''San Lorenzo de Quinti District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Geography ==
The [[Paryaqaqa mountain range|Paryaqaqa]] or Waruchiri mountain range is the north-eastern border of the district. One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Paryaqaqa]] at {{convert|5750|m|ft|0}} above sea level.
