'''Bela Vista de Minas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2025|lc=y}} is estimated to be 14,332 people.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-12-18 |title=Brazil Cities by Population 2025 |url=https://worldpopulationreview.com/cities/brazil |access-date=2025-12-28 |website=World Population Review |language=en}}</ref> The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] Metropolitana de [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] of [[Itabira]].
