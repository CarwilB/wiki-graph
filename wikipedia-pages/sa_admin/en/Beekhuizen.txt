'''Beekhuizen'''{{efn|{{langx|srn|Bekoysi}}, {{langx|hns|Bekoisi}}, {{langx|jvn|Békoisi}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|title=Verjavaanste toponiemen in Suriname en hun herkomst|trans-title=Javanese-influenced place names in Suriname and their origin|first=Hein|last=Vruggink|publisher=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|date=1987|lang=nl|access-date=11 June 2026|archive-date=25 December 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241225195343/https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|url-status=live}}</ref>}} is a former [[sugarcane]] plantation<ref name="plantation">{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/b/beekhuizen|title=Plantage Beekhuizen|website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=3 October 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221003073312/https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/b/beekhuizen|url-status=live}}</ref> and currently a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 17,185.<ref name="census"/>

The plantation was founded before 1700 by Benjamin Beeke. Around 1850, the [[Moravian Church]] became part owner of the plantation, started a school,<ref name="plantation"/> and freed the slaves working on their part of the plantation, because they were against slavery.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/index/nt00341/86b23911-9f54-4227-871d-6c1e71742e81|title=Suriname en Nederlandse Antillen: Vrijverklaarde slaven (Emancipatie 1863), Voornaam: Thomas Theodor|website=Nationaal Archief.nl|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=4 September 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250904210456/https://www.nationaalarchief.nl/onderzoeken/index/nt00341/86b23911-9f54-4227-871d-6c1e71742e81|url-status=live}}</ref> In the late 19th century, the Saramacca canal was widened, and 8,200 [[hectare]]s could be put under cultivation.<ref name="plantation"/>

At the outbreak of [[World War II]], in May 1940, some Surinamese detainees were interned there, as well as some Germans on a temporary basis until the internment camp at [[Copieweg internment camp|Copieweg]] was completed.<ref>{{cite news |title=Nog meer interneeringen. |url=https://resolver.kb.nl/resolve?urn=MMKB23:001618057:mpeg21:p00003 |work=De West |date=May 31, 1940 |location=Paramaribo |language=nl}}</ref> In 1947, {{ill|Bruynzeel Suriname Wood Company|nl|Bruynzeel Suriname Houtmaatschappij|lt=Bruynzeel}} started a wood factory, and constructed wooden prefabricated houses on an industrial scale.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bruynzeelwoningen.nl/|title=Bruynzeel Woningen|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|website=Bruynzeel Woningen|archive-date=8 August 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200808230031/https://www.bruynzeelwoningen.nl/|url-status=live}}</ref> In 1945, Beekhuizen became a neighbourhood of [[Paramaribo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cityofparamaribo.com/read/Worldheritage_sub?sublist=18936&css=|title=Unesco World Heritage|website=City of Paramaribo|access-date=28 May 2020|archive-date=8 August 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200808234326/https://cityofparamaribo.com/read/Worldheritage_sub?sublist=18936&css=|url-status=dead}}</ref> The [[Zorg en Hoop Airport]] is located in the Beekhuizen resort. The [[Jules Sedney Harbour]], one of the two main cargo harbours of Suriname (the other being in [[Nieuw-Nickerie]]), is also located in the resort.<ref name="surgoed">{{cite news|url=https://www.surgoed.com/nieuwsbericht/het-waaggebouw/|title=Het Waaggebouw|website=SurGoed|access-date=14 March 2021|language=nl|archive-date=12 May 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210512145248/https://www.surgoed.com/nieuwsbericht/het-waaggebouw/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="history">{{cite web|url=https://havenbeheer.com/historie/|title=Geschiedenis|website=Havenbeheer|access-date=8 March 2026|language=nl|archive-date=26 December 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221226152150/https://havenbeheer.com/historie/|url-status=live}}</ref>
