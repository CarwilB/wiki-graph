'''San Francisco''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] within [[Panama City]], in [[Panamá District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 43,939 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 34,262, and its population as of 2000 was 35,751.<ref name=inec/>

On 20 March 1998, San Francisco recorded a temperature of {{convert|40.0|C|F|abbr=on}}, which is the highest temperature to have ever been recorded in Panama.<ref name=ETESA>{{cite web |url=http://www.hidromet.com.pa/temperaturas_extremas.php?idioma=ing |title=Extreme Temperatures |publisher=Empresa de Transmisión Eléctrica S.A |access-date=30 October 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161031151317/http://www.hidromet.com.pa/temperaturas_extremas.php?idioma=ing |archive-date=31 October 2016 |url-status=live }}</ref>
