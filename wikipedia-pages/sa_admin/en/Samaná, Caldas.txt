'''Samaná''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]].

It is about 220&nbsp;km north west of Bogota.

Facilities include the Parque Simon Bolívar park and the [[Fundación Nacional Batuta|Fundacion Batuta]] Caldas academy of music.<ref>{{cite web |title=Fundacion Batuta Caldas |url=http://www.batutacaldas.com/ |website=Batuta Caldas}}</ref>

==Climate==
Samaná has a relatively cool due to altitude and very wet [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af''). It is the wettest place in the department of Caldas.
