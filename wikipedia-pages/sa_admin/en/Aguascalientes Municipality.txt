'''Aguascalientes''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Aguascalientes]]. Its [[municipal seat]] is the city of [[Aguascalientes, Aguascalientes|Aguascalientes]], which is also the state capital. As of 2010, its population was 797,010, most of whom lived in the city of Aguascalientes.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.censo2010.org.mx/ Data] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110406225157/http://www.censo2010.org.mx/ |date=2011-04-06 }}, 2010 census, [[INEGI]]. Accessed on April 9, 2011.</ref>

==Geography==
Aguascalientes is in the south of the valley that divides the state in two and borders the municipalities of [[Municipality of El Llano|El Llano]], [[Jesús María Municipality|Jesús María]], [[Municipality of Calvillo|Calvillo]], [[Municipality of Asientos|Asientos]], and [[Municipality of San Francisco de los Romo|San Francisco de los Romo]] in the state, as well as the state of [[Jalisco]] to the south. Its area is 1,169&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://web.archive.org/web/20070927204101/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/aguascalientes/municipios/01001a.htm Aguascalientes], ''[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Enciclopedia Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930031434/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Enciclopedia |date=2007-09-30 }}'', [[Instituto Nacional Para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]], [[SEGOB]]. Accessed on line November 23, 2007.</ref>

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 797,010.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Aguascalientes|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=001|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=17 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151017015005/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=001|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Aguascalientes had a population of 722,250.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Aguascalientes, the municipality had 799 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Pocitos, Aguascalientes|Pocitos]] (5,169), [[Villa Licenciado Jesús Terán (Calvillito), Aguascalientes|Villa Licenciado Jesús Terán (Calvillito)]] (4,481), [[Norias de Ojocaliente, Aguascalientes|Norias de Ojocaliente]] (3,741), [[Norias del Paso Hondo, Aguascalientes|Norias del Paso Hondo]] (2,539), [[General José María Morelos y Pavón (Cañada Honda), Aguascalientes|General José María Morelos y Pavón (Cañada Honda)]] (2,500), classified as urban, and [[Cartagena, Aguascalientes|Cartagena]] (2,496), [[Jaltomate, Aguascalientes|Jaltomate]] (2,299), [[San Antonio de Peñuelas, Aguascalientes|San Antonio de Peñuelas]] (2,147), [[San Sebastián, Aguascalientes|San Sebastián]] (1,862), [[Peñuelas (El Cienegal), Aguascalientes|Peñuelas (El Cienegal)]] (1,670), [[El Refugio de Peñuelas, Aguascalientes|El Refugio de Peñuelas]] (1,624), [[Montoro (Mesa del Salto), Aguascalientes|Montoro (Mesa del Salto)]] (1,574), [[La Loma de los Negritos, Aguascalientes|La Loma de los Negritos]] (1,519), [[El Salto de los Salado, Aguascalientes|El Salto de los Salado]] (1,436), [[Arellano, Aguascalientes|Arellano]] (1,382), [[San Ignacio, Aguascalientes|San Ignacio]] (1,360), [[Cumbres III, Aguascalientes|Cumbres III]] (1,337), [[Cotorina (Coyotes), Aguascalientes|Cotorina (Coyotes)]] (1,298), [[Lomas del Sur, Aguascalientes|Lomas del Sur]] (1,207), [[Los Caños, Aguascalientes|Los Caños]] (1,150), and [[Centro de Arriba (El Taray), Aguascalientes|Centro de Arriba (El Taray)]] (1,064), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
