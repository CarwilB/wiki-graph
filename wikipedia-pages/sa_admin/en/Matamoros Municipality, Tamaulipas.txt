'''Matamoros''' is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] located in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. Its municipal seat is the city of [[Matamoros, Tamaulipas|Matamoros]].
