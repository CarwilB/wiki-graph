'''Quinchía''', also known as '''''Villa de los Cerros''''' (''Village of the Hills''), is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. About 110&nbsp;km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]]. In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 27,890.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Quinchía, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/quinchia |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
Around 1842, the decimated indigenous population, as a result of the violence against them by the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spaniards]], decided to formalize the organization of the hamlet, creating Quinchía Viejo. On November 29, 1886, the current municipality of Quinchía emerged, by the will of Messrs. Gabriel Vinasco, Zoilo Bermúdez, José Natalio and Protasio Gómez.

Quinchia was officialized on November 29, 1888, also On April 24, 1912, the town was declared a municipality. At the beginning of the 20th century, the Indigenous Reservation would end, through negotiations and all kinds of maneuvers by the [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]] settlers who ended up taking over the lands.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Quinchía |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100242/quinchia/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>

It is located in the [[Colombian coffee growing axis]], part of the "Coffee Cultural Landscape" [[UNESCO]] [[World Heritage Site]] in 2011.

== Climate ==
Quinchía has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average annual temperature of 18&nbsp;°C.<ref>{{Cite web |title=RISARALDA-QUINCHIA |url=https://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/RISARALDA/MUNICIPIOS/QUINCHIA/QUINCHIA.htm |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=www.colombiaturismoweb.com}}</ref>
