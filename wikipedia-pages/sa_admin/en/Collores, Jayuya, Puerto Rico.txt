'''Collores''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Jayuya, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 1,666.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó.
