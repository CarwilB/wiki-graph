'''Jambaló''' ({{IPA|es|xambaˈlo}}) is a municipality and, at the same time, an indigenous reservation of [[Colombia]]. It is located in the northeastern [[Cauca Department|department of Cauca]], 80 kilometers north of the city of [[Popayán]] (the capital of the department). It borders on the north with the municipalities of [[Caloto, Cauca|Caloto]] and [[Toribío, Cauca|Toribío]], on the east with the municipality of [[Páez, Cauca|Páez]], on the south with the municipality of [[Silvia, Cauca|Silvia]] and on the west with the municipalities of [[Caldono, Cauca|Caldono]] and [[Santander de Quilichao]]. The municipal head is the most populous urban center of the territory and is also called "Jambaló", is located in the extreme south, on the limits with the municipality of [[Silvia, Cauca|Silvia]]; However, in the rural area it is where the majority of the population lives (94%). Being Jambaló also indigenous guard, the majority of its inhabitants are of the nasa and misak ethnic groups.{{coord|2|51|N|76|19|W|region:CO_type:adm2nd_source:GNS-enwiki|display=title}}
