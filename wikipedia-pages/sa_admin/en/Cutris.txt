'''Cutris''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[San Carlos (canton)|San Carlos]] canton, in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Cutris was created on 26 November 1971 by Decreto Ejecutivo 2083-G.<ref name="dta2017" />

== Geography ==
Cutris has an area of {{formatnum:848.01}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> which makes it the largest district in the canton by area and an elevation of {{formatnum:65}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It is located in the northern region of the country and borders with 6 districts; [[Pocosol, Costa Rica|Pocosol]] al oeste, [[Aguas Zarcas, Costa Rica|Aguas Zarcas]] y [[Pital, Costa Rica|Pital]] al Este, [[La Palmera, Costa Rica|Palmera]], [[Florencia, Costa Rica|Florencia]] y [[La Fortuna, San Carlos|La Fortuna]].  While to the north it borders Nicaragua.

Its head, the town of Boca Arenal, is located 33.4&nbsp;km (47 minutes) to the N of Ciudad Quesada and 133&nbsp;km (2 hours 34 minutes) to the NW of San Jose the capital of the nation.

It presents a flat relief in the majority of its territory.

== Economy ==

At present, this district makes livestock and citrus cultivation (orange, pineapple and sugarcane), its main activities.

These provide employment to a large number of people, most of whom are Nicaraguan migrants who arrive in these lands attracted by the harvest.

In Boca de Arenal, (head of the district) you can find restaurants, grocery stores, butchers and a mill.
