'''Pinheiral''' ({{IPA|pt|piɲejˈɾaw}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 25,364 (2020) and its area is {{convert|77|sqkm|abbr=on}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

==History==

The city was named after the São José do Pinheiro coffee plantation, owned by the very rich planter and slave trader José de Souza Breves (1795-1879).<ref>{{cite web |language=pt |author=Andreza Baptista |title=Fazenda do Pinheiro |url=https://acasasenhorial.org/acs/index.php/pt/casos-de-estudo/casosdeestudo/678-fazenda-do-pinheiro |website=A Casa Senhorial |access-date=16 October 2024}}</ref> This [[Fazenda|estate]], on which about 500 [[Slavery in Brazil|slaves]] used to work, was "one of the most opulent in Brazil". Nowadays, the [[Afro-Brazilians|descendants of slave]] plan to transform the Park of the Ruins, only vestige of the plantation and its palace,<ref>{{cite web |language=pt |author=Prefeitura Municipal de Pinheiral |title=Fazenda São José do Pinheiro |url=https://pinheiral.rj.gov.br/a-cidade/atracoes-turisticas/fazenda-sao-jose-do-pinheiro/ |website=pinheiral.rj.gov.br |access-date=16 October 2024}}</ref> into a memory centre, including a museum and school of [[jongo]] (dance and musical genre of black communities).<ref>{{cite web |last1=Rogero |first1=Tiago |title=A coffee plantation in Brazil enslaved Africans. Centuries later, their descendants have taken over |url=https://www.theguardian.com/news/article/2024/aug/18/coffee-plantation-brazil-jongo |website=The Guardian |access-date=15 October 2024 |date=18 August 2024}}</ref>
