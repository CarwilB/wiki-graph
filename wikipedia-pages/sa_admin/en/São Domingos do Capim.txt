'''São Domingos do Capim''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|sɐ̃w duˈmĩɡuz du kaˈpĩ}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Pará]]. The estimated population is 32,139 (2020). It has an area of 1677&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=150720|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Pará {{!}} São Domingos do Capim|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-19}}</ref>

The city is well known for hosting a world surfing championship in the [[tidal bore|bore tides]] of the [[Guamá River]], a phenomenon which is locally referred to as the ''[[pororoca]]''. The championships take place between March and April.
