'''Chapab Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “Place of oily water”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 168.62&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 85&nbsp;km south of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name=municipios />

==History==
Its colonial history is unknown prior to the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]]. After colonization, it was part of the [[encomienda]] system during the 18th century. In 1837, it was designated as a puebla under [[Ticul Municipality|Ticul]].<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The president appoints Councilpersons to serve on the board for three year terms, as Secretary, and councilors of Public Works; Ecology; and street lighting.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Chapab|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=4 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

In addition, the municipal agencies ensure the preservation of public order, and apprehend and prosecute criminals as required by authority. They also regulate gaming and the sale of alcohol, and enforce civil registrations and education requirements.<ref name=governance />
