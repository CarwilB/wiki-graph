'''Los Arabos''' is a municipality and town in the [[Matanzas Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in the eastern part of the province, bordering the province of [[Villa Clara Province|Villa Clara]].

==History==
The municipality was divided into the [[Former barrios of Cuba|barrios]] of Cabecera (municipal seat), Macagua, Monte Alto and San Pedro de Mayabón.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/marabos/index.htm|title=Los Arabos|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Los Arabos had a population of 25,702.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|762|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|30|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
