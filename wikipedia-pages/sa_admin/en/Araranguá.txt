'''Araranguá''' is a city located in the southern part of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] state, in the south of [[Brazil]]. It has 68,867 inhabitants and was settled mainly by [[Portugal|Portuguese]] and [[Italy|Italians]]. Araranguá is known as "A Cidade das Avenidas" ("''The City of the Avenues''") because of its wide roads.

==History==
Until the 18th century, the city was inhabited mostly by [[Carijós]] and [[Kaingang]] Indians. These ethnics groups disappeared after the arrival of the first Europeans settlers.

On 11 February 1728, was started the opening of the Caminho dos Conventos way that linked Morro dos Conventos ("''Convents Mountain''") to Curitiba. This way became used mostly by the tropeiros, who carried cattle from [[Rio Grande do Sul]] to [[São Paulo]]. Araranguá became a waypoint for those between [[Viamão]] (former capital of Rio Grande do Sul) and [[Lages]] (in the top of the Planalto Serrano).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ararangua.sc.gov.br/|title=Prefeitura de Araranguá|accessdate=19 February 2024}}</ref>

The first to arrive in Araranguá region were the [[Portugal|Portugueses]], who came from [[Laguna, Santa Catarina|Laguna]] in the beginning of the 19th century. They first settled in the Morro dos Conventos. Later, [[Italy|Italian]], [[Germany|German]], [[Poland|Polish]] and [[Spain|Spanish]] immigrants and the African [[slaves]] arrived in the city.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sc.gov.br/portalturismo/Default.asp?CodMunicipio%3D234%26Pag%3D2 |title=Santa Catarina - Municípios - Araranguá |access-date=2009-01-08 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110524051520/http://www.sc.gov.br/portalturismo/Default.asp?CodMunicipio=234&Pag=2 |archive-date=24 May 2011}}</ref> In 1816 was inaugurated in the village of Canjicas the first chapel in the region. In 1864 was created the first church within the current city of Araranguá.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radarsul.com.br/ararangua/historia.asp |title=(( Radar Sul )) História da Cidade de Araranguá - Santa Catarina - SC - Brasil |access-date=2009-01-08 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090212210529/http://radarsul.com.br/ararangua/historia.asp |archive-date=12 February 2009}}</ref>

The city was founded 3 April 1880, and was named Araranguá after the [[Araranguá River]]. The village was promoted to the condition of city in 1921.

Along the 20th century the city underwent some dismemberments. The city of Criciúma emancipated in 1925 followed by Turvo (1948), Sombrio (1953), Maracajá (1967) and Balneário Arroio do Silva (1997).

==Climate==
Araranguá was struck by the [[Cyclone Catarina]] in the night between 28 and 29 March 2004 with winds up to 150&nbsp;km/h. This event still intrigues scientists.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.apolo11.com/furacao_catarina.php|title=APOLO11.COM - Furacão Catarina, o primeiro furacão brasileiro|website=www.apolo11.com|accessdate=19 February 2024}}</ref>

The city was again struck by at least two [[tornadoes]] in the night of September, the 29th of 2009, the first ever recorded in the urban area, although others have been already spotted in rural areas. A school gymnasium collapsed over a house and many windows were broken in some shops, but there were no victims.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ararangua.net/?acao=noticia&cd_noticia=343&pagina=0|title = Município de Araranguá}}</ref>

*Commerce
*Tourism

== Education ==
The city has a campus of the [[Federal University of Santa Catarina]], inaugurated on 10 August 2009. With headquarters in Mato Alto and Jardim das Avenidas, the campus offers 4 undergraduate programs (Computer Engineering, Energy Engineering, Physiotherapy and Information and Communication Technologies) and 4 masters programs (Rehabilitation Sciences, Energy and Sustainability, Physics Teaching and Information and Communication Technologies).

Also located in Araranguá is a campus of the [[Federal Institute of Santa Catarina]], which offers technical courses in Electromechanics, Fashion and Apparel Production, and undergraduate programs in Fashion Design and Physics .

==Transportation==

===Highways===
Araranguá is accessed by road mainly from the [[BR-101]], an important highway that connects several major Brazilian cities. It lies roughly halfway between [[Porto Alegre]] and [[Florianópolis]], the former being  {{convert|210|km|mi|abbr=on}} away.

===Buses===
The city is served by Viação Cidade<ref>{{Cite web |title=Viação Cidade Ltda |url=https://www.viacidade.com.br/ |access-date=2023-11-06 |website=www.viacidade.com.br}}</ref> for intra-city routes and by União, Pluma, SantoAnjo and Eucatur for inter-city and inter-state routes. Among cities served are [[Porto Alegre]], [[Florianópolis]], [[Curitiba]] and [[São Paulo]] besides most of the neighboring cities and cities along those routes.
