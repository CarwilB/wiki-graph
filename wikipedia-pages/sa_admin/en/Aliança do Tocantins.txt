'''Aliança do Tocantins''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 5,346 (2020) and its area is 1,580&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/ST5]</ref>
