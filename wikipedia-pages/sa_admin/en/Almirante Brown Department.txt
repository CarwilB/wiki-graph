'''Almirante Brown Department''' is the westernmost [[departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 28,000 inhabitants in an area of  17,276&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Pampa del Infierno]], which is located around 1,265&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

The department is named in honour of [[William Brown (admiral)|William Brown]] (1777 -1857), the [[Ireland|Irish]] born commander in chief of the [[Argentine Navy]].

==Attractions==

The city of Pampa del Infierno hosts the annual ''Fiesta Provincial del Chivo'' (Provincial Goat Festival).
