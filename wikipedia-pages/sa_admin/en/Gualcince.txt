'''Gualcince''' ({{IPA|es|ɡwalˈsinse}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Lempira department|Lempira]].

It is one of the several municipalities of the [[Lempira Department|Lempira department]]. It is located 78&nbsp;km from [[Gracias]] city. It is necessary to pass by San Juan, [[Intibuca]] and through the municipalities of [[Santa Cruz, Lempira|Santa Cruz]] and [[San Andrés, Lempira|San Andrés]]. It has 2 accesses, which separate from the main road that leads to southern municipalities. The road is constantly being repaired. There are several sections of this roads that are narrow, so careful driving is advised, especially for buses and trucks.

== History ==
It was first called "Gualán". There is no accurate date for its foundation, but in some old books of real state, it is registered that the Catholic Church was built back in 1,576. In the census of 1,791 it was the capital of the "Cerquin" curato. In the national division of 1889 was one of the municipalities of [[Candelaria, Lempira|Candelaria]] district. It became a municipality in 1840.{{citation needed|date=January 2023}}

== Geography ==
It has huge mountains covered with pine forests. One of them is "Congolon" mountain. These mountain are favorable for coffee plantations and also for water springs.{{citation needed|date=January 2023}}

== Boundaries ==
Its boundaries are: 
*North : [[San Andrés, Lempira|San Andrés]] and [[Erandique]] municipalities.
*South : [[Mapulaca]] and [[Candelaria, Lempira|Candelaria]] municipalities.
*East  : [[Piraera]] municipality.
*West  : [[La Virtud]] municipality.
*'''Surface Extents''': 163&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>

== Resources ==
The Coffee plantations are the main economical activity, and the geography has a lot to do with it. Corn and beans can not be excluded since they are part of the national diet. The cattle raising is only for local consumption. The vegetables are scarce there for they are brought from other municipalities and even from other departments, such as [[Intibuca]].{{citation needed|date=January 2023}}

== Population ==
The cross-breed of Spanish settlers and native Indian represents half of the population. The 2nd half consists of native Indians descendants.

*'''Population''': this municipality had about 9,537 people. The INE used this figure to elaborate estimates, and the number of people in 2008 could be 10,681.{{Update inline|date=April 2026}}
*'''Villages''': 12
*'''Settlements''': 103

===Demographics===
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Gualcince municipality had a population of 11,295. Of these, 73.69% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 20.64% [[Mestizo]], 0.89% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.19% [[White Latin Americans|White]] and 4.59% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>

== Tourism ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
Gualcince displays incredible landscapes of high, steep pine forest mountains, and very old buildings such as the Catholic Church and some houses that are still occupied. It does not have any hostels, but [[Candelaria, Lempira|Candelaria]] is 40 minutes away. Grocery stores make it easier to spend some time there. One popular dish there, are the "Pupusas de Flor de Oroco".

*'''Local Holidays''': December 8; "Inmaculapa Concepcion" day.
