'''Laguna''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southern state of [[Santa Catarina State|Santa Catarina]], 120 kilometers south of the state's capital, [[Florianópolis]], and north east of [[Porto Alegre]]. The population is 46,122 (2020 est.) in an area of 336.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?lang=_PT&codmun=420940&search=santa-catarina&#124;laguna&#124;infograficos:-dados-gerais-do-municipio|title=IBGE :: Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|date=2017}}</ref> The elevation is 2 m. The [[BR-101]] coastal highway passes through the municipality.

The city was founded in 1676 by rural people of the [[Captaincy of São Vicente|''capitania'' of São Vicente]]. In 1714, the locality was recognized as a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]], and in 1847 it received the status of [[city]]. It was the capital of the short-lived [[Juliana Republic]] in 1839. The city's flag is a tricolor with yellow, white and green, and has a coat of arms in the middle.

A pod of [[bottlenose dolphin]]s resident in the sea off Laguna drive fish towards fishermen who stand at the beach in shallow waters. Then one dolphin rolls over, which the fishermen take as sign to throw out their nets. The dolphins feed on the escaping fish. The dolphins were not trained for this behavior; the collaboration has been going on at least since 1847.<ref>''[[The Daily Telegraph]]'' (2006), [https://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/southamerica/brazil/734722/Brazils-sexiest-secret.html "Brazil's sexiest secret"], article retrieved March 20, 2009.</ref><ref>Dr. Moti Nissani (2007) [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ECk0yMifmzw Bottlenose Dolphins in Laguna Requesting a Throw Net] (video). Supporting material for Nissani's presentation at the 2007 International Ethological Conference. Video retrieved March 20, 2009.</ref> [[Southern right whale]]s also can be seen from shores during winter to spring seasons.<ref>Archived at [https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211205/KZe2IrR4yo8 Ghostarchive]{{cbignore}} and the [https://web.archive.org/web/20171224214819/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KZe2IrR4yo8&gl=US&hl=en Wayback Machine]{{cbignore}}: {{cite web| url = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KZe2IrR4yo8| title = BALEIA E SEU FILHOTE PRAIA DO MAR GROSSO LAGUNA | website=[[YouTube]]}}{{cbignore}}</ref>
