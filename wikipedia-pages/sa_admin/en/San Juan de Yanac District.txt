'''San Juan de Yanac District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Chincha Province|Chincha]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

It borders to the north with the districts of Chavín and San Pedro de Huacarpana; to the east and south with the province of Castrovirreyna; and to the west with the districts of Chavín and Alto Larán.

==History==

It was created on February 12, 1965, during the first government of the architect [[Fernando Belaúnde Terry]], by Law No. 15433.
