'''Lajedo''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[laˈʒedu]}}) is a municipality and a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 40,589 (2020 est.) in an area of 189.10 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/lajedo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city area is 3.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Geography==

* Boundaries - [[São Bento do Una]] and [[Cachoeirinha, Pernambuco|Cachoeirinha]]  ('''N''');  [[Canhotinho]]   ('''S''');   [[Ibirajuba]]  ('''E''');  [[Calçado]]   ('''W''').
* Elevation - 661 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] and [[Mundaú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]]
* Annual average temperature - 21.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 192&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Lajedo''' are industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[pig]]s, [[sheep]], [[chicken]]s; and plantations of [[bean]]s and [[manioc]].
