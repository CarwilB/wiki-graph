'''Dom Feliciano''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, it had a population of 15,487 people, of whom 90% are of [[Polish Brazilian|Polish descent]].<ref>[http://www.domfeliciano.rs.gov.br/portal1/municipio/historia.asp?iIdMun=100143123 Prefeitura de D.
