'''Tepetzintla''' ([[Nahuatl]]: "under the hill" or "many small hills"<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM21puebla/municipios/21167a.html |title=Tepetzintla |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=1 March 2018 |archive-date=20 June 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220620212415/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM21puebla/municipios/21167a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|207|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Xalapa]] and {{convert|119|km}} south of the city of [[Tampico]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Tepetzintla is located on the coastal plain of northern Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|80|and(-)|1100|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Tancoco]] to the north, [[Cerro Azul (municipality)|Cerro Azul]] to the northeast, [[Temapache]] to the southeast, [[Chicontepec de Tejeda|Chicontepec]] to the southwest, [[Ixcatepec]] to the west, and [[Chontla]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Tepetzintla, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|227.942|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.3% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/> 

The municipality is mostly located in the [[Tuxpan River (Veracruz)|Tuxpan River]] basin.<ref name=compendium/><ref name=encyclopedia/> Pastureland comprises 65% of Tepetzintla's area while jungle comprises another 28%.<ref name=compendium/>

Tepetzintla's climate is warm and humid with rain falling mostly in the summer. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|22|and(-)|26|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1400|and(-)|1600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
On 1 December 1868 Tepetzintla was incorporated as a municipality in the canton of Túxpan in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918. On 3 December 1963 the municipality of Cerro Azul was separated from Tepetzintla.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=118, 290 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Olga Isela Gerardo Morales.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.lavozdetantoyuca.com/lavoz1661.html/ |title=Gana el PAN en Tepetzintla |language=es |newspaper=La Voz de Tantoyuca |date=4 June 2017 |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Tepetzintla recorded a population of 13,949 inhabitants living in 3460 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=167 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Tepetzintla |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 14,736 inhabitants in Tepetzintla, of whom 74.31% reported being of [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|Indigenous]] ancestry and 13.59% reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African]] ancestry.<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 2005 people or 14% of the population in Tepetzintla reported speaking an Indigenous language, of which 1952 spoke Nahuatl.<ref name=snim/>

There are 62 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Tepetzintla, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 5166 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Beef cattle farming is a major economic activity in Tepetzintla. Agriculture is also important: corn and oranges are the main crops grown, respectively comprising 63% and 33% of the agricultural land in the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tepetzintla.gob.mx/uploads/transparencia/f91b8f959b9858f6114415423c09707a.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |pages=47–48 |publisher=Municipal council of Tepetzintla, Veracruz |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref>
