'''Berbeo''' is a town and municipality in the [[Lengupá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre of Berbeo is located at an altitude of {{convert|1350|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. Berbeo borders [[San Eduardo, Boyacá|San Eduardo]] in the east, [[Zetaquirá]] and [[Miraflores, Boyacá|Miraflores]] in the west, Zetaquirá in the north and Miraflores and [[Páez, Boyacá|Páez]] in the south.<ref name=websiteBerbeo>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20140310085207/http://www.berbeo-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Berbeo]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The municipality was formerly called San Fernando de Aguablanca and Legupá, and since 1913 bears the name Berbeo, after Juan Francisco Berbeo.<ref name=websiteBerbeo/>

== History ==
The area of Berbeo before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[indigenous peoples of Colombia|indigenous]] [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. In Berbeo [[Muisca art#Rock art|petroglyphs]] have been discovered. Modern Berbeo was founded on April 23, 1743, by Jesuits.<ref name=websiteBerbeo/>

== Economy ==
Main activity of Berbeo is [[agriculture]], with [[coffee]] and [[chonto tomato]]es as major products.<ref name=websiteBerbeo/>
