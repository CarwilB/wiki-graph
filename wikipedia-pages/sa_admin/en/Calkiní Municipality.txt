'''Calkiní''' ([[Yucatec Maya]]: "throat of the sun") is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. It is situated at the northern tip of the state, on the central western coast the [[Yucatán Peninsula]]. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Calkiní]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Calkiní borders to the west with the [[Gulf of Mexico]], to the north and east with the state of [[Yucatán (state)|Yucatán]], and to the south with the municipality of [[Hecelchakán]]. 
It covers 1,966.57&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Demographics==
The 2020 census reported a population of 59,232 persons.<ref name=CALKINI2020>{{cite web|title=Localities in Calkiní 2020-INEGI 2020|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/campeche/04001__calkin%C3%AD/}}</ref>  Of these, 15,949 spoke one of several indigenous languages, predominantly [[Yucatec Maya]].

As of 2020, the city of [[Calkiní]] had a population of 14,934.<ref name=CALKINI2020/> Other than the city of Calkiní, the municipality had 202 localities, the largest of which (with 2020 populations in parentheses) were: [[Dzitbalché, Campeche|Dzitbalché]] (13,208), Bécal (6,801), Nunkiní (6,485), Bacabchén (3,128), classified as urban, and Santa Cruz Pueblo (2,286), San Antonio Sahcabchén (2,280), Tepakán (2,193), Santa Cruz Ex-Hacienda (1,421), and Tankuché (1,228), classified as rural.<ref name=CALKINI2020/>

==Heritage==
The [[Maya civilization|Maya]] archaeological site of [[Can-Mayab-Mul]] is in Nunkiní.
