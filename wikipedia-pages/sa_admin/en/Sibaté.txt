'''Sibaté''' ({{IPA|es|siβaˈte}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Soacha Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Sibaté is located on the [[Bogotá savanna]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2700|m|ft}} and a distance of {{convert|27|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. It forms part of the [[Metropolitan Area of Bogotá|metropolitan area]] of the capital. Sibaté borders [[Soacha]] in the north, [[Pasca]] and [[Fusagasugá]] in the south, Soacha in the east and [[Silvania, Colombia|Silvania]] and [[Granada, Cundinamarca|Granada]] in the west.<ref name=websiteSibate>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.sibate-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Sibaté] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150520175455/http://www.sibate-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-20 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Sibaté means in [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] "Leak of the lake", according to [[List of Muisca scholars|scholar]] [[Miguel Triana]].<ref name=websiteSibate/>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], the area of Sibaté was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The Bogotá savanna was ruled by the ''[[zipa]]'' based in [[Bogotá|Bacatá]].

Modern Sibaté was founded on November 24, 1967.<ref name=websiteSibate/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Sibaté are [[agriculture]] ([[potato]]es, [[pea]]s and [[strawberry|strawberries]]) and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteSibate/>
