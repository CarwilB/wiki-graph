'''Ferrier''' ({{IPA|fr|fɛʁje}}; {{langx|ht|Ferye}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Fort-Liberté Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It had 14,642 inhabitants as of 2015.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref>

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of one [[communal section]], namely:
* [[Bas Maribahoux]], urban and rural, containing the town of Ferrier
