'''El Realejo''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chinandega (department)|Chinandega]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

==History==
The town of El Realejo was constructed in 1532, during the first years of [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spanish colonization]]. During this period it served as Nicaragua's principal port, and remained so until the beginning of the 17th century, when [[pirate]] attacks started to threaten the town's commercial life-line. The importance of El Realejo was further diminished by the construction of [[Corinto, Nicaragua|Corinto]], which came to be Nicaragua's new principal port.<ref>[http://vianica.com/visit/chinandega Explore Nicaragua: Chinandega<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>

In 1845 a shipment of weapons for the government in [[León, Nicaragua|León]] was seized by the invading forces of [[Francisco Malespín]] during the ''[[Emiliano Madriz#Guerra de Malesp%C3%ADn|Guerra de Malespín]]''.

== Economy ==
Today, the economy is based on agriculture, fishing, and the raising of animals. El Realejo is characterized by open expanses of tropical savannah that give way to mountains and beaches.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.sister-cities.org/icrc/cityseek/list/display?recID=54293 |title=Sister Cities International<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=2006-12-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100621100213/http://sister-cities.org/icrc/cityseek/list/display?recID=54293 |archive-date=2010-06-21 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
