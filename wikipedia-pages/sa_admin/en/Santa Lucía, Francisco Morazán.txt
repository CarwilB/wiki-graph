'''Santa Lucía''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsanta luˈsi.a}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].

== History ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
[[File:Santa Lucia Strest in Honduras.jpg|thumb|Colonial Street in Santa Lucia]]
There is no exact data on its beginnings, although it is known that it is one of the oldest towns, that it was inhabited before the arrival of the [[Spaniards|Spanish]]. Although some writers have affirmed that mining began in 1580, there are documents that indicate that around 1540 it had already been visited by the first Spanish explorers who were looking for mineral veins and then began to exploit its rich mines.

On November 12, 1820, Santa Lucía was established as a municipality in the Department of [[Tegucigalpa]].

== Tourism ==
[[File:SantaLucia Honduras.jpg|thumb|Santa Lucia lagoon ]]
Santa Lucia is a common tourist destination for those who visit the Tegucigalpa.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.elheraldo.hn/honduras/francisco-morazan-joyas-lista-30-maravillas-bicentenario-independencia-honduras-MQEH1496219|title=Francisco Morazán tiene cinco joyas en la lista de las 30 Maravillas|first=Brayan Jeshua|last=García|work=[[El Heraldo (Tegucigalpa)|El Heraldo]]|date=30 September 2021|access-date=23 November 2025|language=es}}</ref> With a mild climate, the main town can be comfortably explored on foot starting with a lagoon full of life in which you can rent [[Rowing|rowing boats]] to explore it. The historic center of Santa Lucia is one of the best preserved in Honduras.

The town is full of scenic views, including views of Tegucigalpa. Santa Lucia is home to several restaurants and cafes. The town boasts well-preserved original [[Spanish Colonial architecture|colonial structures]], from houses, stone streets, and its church.
