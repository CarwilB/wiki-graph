'''Cucurpe''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead|archive-date=July 31, 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 4, 2010 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². The municipal seat is at [[Cucurpe]].

==Area and population==
The total area of the municipality is 1,778.55 square kilometers, which represents 0.96% of the state total and 0.09% of the national total.  The population of the municipality was 937 inhabitants in the 2000 census and 798 inhabitants in the 2005 second count.<ref name=elocal>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/municipios/26022a.htm Cucurpe] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210041/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/municipios/26022a.htm |date=2007-09-27 }} @ Enciclopedia de los Municipios de Mexico (Spanish)</ref> The population of the municipal seat was 487 in 2000.<ref name="INEGI-Busqueda">[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/lib/buscador/busqueda.aspx INEGI] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070824152945/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/lib/buscador/busqueda.aspx |date=August 24, 2007 }} (Spanish)</ref><ref>[http://galileo.inegi.gob.mx/website/mexico/viewer.htm?bsqTable=77&bsqField=CVEMUN&bsqStr=26022&TName=MGM&seccionB=mdm Map with Marco geoestadístico municipal] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120902174816/http://galileo.inegi.gob.mx/website/mexico/viewer.htm?bsqTable=77&bsqField=CVEMUN&bsqStr=26022&TName=MGM&seccionB=mdm |date=2012-09-02 }} Mapa Digital de México @ INEGI (Spanish)</ref> The negative population growth is due to the high migration from this municipality, the deficient educational infrastructure, and the lack of sources of work.

In the 2020 census, the municipality reported a population of 863.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cucurpe: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/cucurpe |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

==Municipal boundaries==
The municipality has boundaries with [[Imuris]] in the north, [[Arizpe]] in the east, [[Opodepe]] in the south, [[Santa Ana, Sonora|Santa Ana]] in the west, [[Magdalena de Kino]] in the northwest.
