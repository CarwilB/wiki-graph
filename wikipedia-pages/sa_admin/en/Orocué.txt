'''Orocué''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]], [[Colombia]], located on the shore of the [[Meta River]]. Historically, it went by the name San Miguel del Macuco.<ref name="WDL1">{{cite web|title=A Study of the Saliba Language|url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/8992|publisher=World Digital Library|access-date=24 April 2013}}</ref>

It is located 180&nbsp;km from [[Yopal]], and 546&nbsp;km from [[Bogotá]].<ref>[https://www.orocue-casanare.gov.co Municipio de Orocué]</ref>

==Climate==
Orocué has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with moderate to little rainfall from December to March and heavy rainfall from April to November.
