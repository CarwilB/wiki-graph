'''Sabanetas''' (''Barrio Sabanetas'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the [[municipality]] of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Along with [[Magueyes]], [[Tibes]], [[Portugués Rural|Portugués]], [[Montes Llanos]], [[Machuelo Arriba]], [[Maragüez]], and [[Cerrillos (Ponce)|Cerrillos]], Sabanetas is one of the municipality's eight rural interior barrios. The name of this barrio is of native Indian origin.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 54.</ref> It was founded in 1831.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
Sabanetas is located in the southern section of the municipality, east of the traditional city center at [[Plaza Las Delicias]]. The [[toponymy]], or origin of the name, is one that describes an open valley covered by grasslands and few, if any, trees.<ref>Government of the Municipality of Ponce. Periodico "El Señorial".  Special issue: Carnaval Ponceño 2013. February 2013. Page 17. Ponce, Puerto Rico.</ref>

==Boundaries==
It is bounded on the North by barrio [[Cerrillos (Ponce)|Cerrillos]], in the South by barrios [[Bucaná]] and [[Vayas]], in the West by barrios [[Machuelo Abajo]] and [[San Anton]], and in the East by barrios [[Coto Laurel]] and [[Vayas]].

==Features and demographics==
Sabanetas has {{convert|2.7|sqmi|km2|sigfig=2}} of land area and {{convert|0.1|sqmi|m2|sigfig=2}} of water area.  In 2000, the population of Sabanetas was 6,420. The population density in Sabanetas was 2,352 persons per square mile.<ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Sabanetas+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved 14 February 2010.</ref>

In 2010, the population of Sabanetas was 5,534 persons, and it had a density of 2,034.6 persons per square mile.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico:2010:population and housing unit counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

The communities of Campo Alegre, Alta Vista, Villa Flores, and Sabanetas proper are located in barrio Sabanetas.

Major roads serving Barrio Sabanetas are [[Puerto Rico Highway 1|PR-1]], [[PR-52]], and [[PR-10]].

The highest point in Barrio Sabanetas is ''Cerro Los Negrones'' which stands at 397 feet.<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/maptest ''Maptest.''] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100218155723/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/maptest |date=2010-02-18 }} Government of the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
