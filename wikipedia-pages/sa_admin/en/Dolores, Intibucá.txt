'''Dolores''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Dolores municipality had a population of 5,140. Of these, 85.70% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (85.56% [[Lenca]]), 9.48% [[Mestizo]], 3.53% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 1.26% [[Afro-Hondurans|Afro-Honduran or Black]] and 0.02% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
