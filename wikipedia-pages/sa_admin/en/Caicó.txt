'''Caicó''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]]. With an area of {{Convert|1228.583|km²|sp=us}}, of which {{Convert|14.0727|km²}} is urban, it is located 222 km from [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]], the state capital, and 1,572 km from [[Brasília]], the federal capital. Its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 demographic census]] was 61,146 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), ranking as the eighth most populous municipality in the state of Rio Grande do Norte.

== History ==
Around 1700, scouts from the [[Captaincy of Paraíba]] arrived in the region and began relentless pursuits against the Caicó Indigenous people, who lived near the confluence of the [[Barra Nova River|Barra Nova]] and [[Seridó River|Seridó rivers]]. After several violent confrontations, the Indigenous inhabitants were expelled, making way for the arrival of settlers, primarily focused on livestock farming. Small pastoral communities began to form, drawn by the region’s abundant pastureland and water sources.<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante">{{cite web |author=Institute of Economic Development and Environment of Rio Grande do Norte (IDEMA-RN) |date=2013 |title=Perfil do seu município: Caicó |url=http://adcon.rn.gov.br/ACERVO/idema/DOC/DOC000000000016645.PDF |url-status= |archive-url= |archive-date= |access-date=10 February 2022}}</ref>

The first land allotments (known as ''datas'') were requested shortly thereafter, and news of a fertile area suitable for cattle raising attracted settlers from [[Paraíba]], [[Pernambuco]], and [[Portugal]]. Among the earliest documented settlers were Captain Inácio Gomes da Câmara, Lieutenant José Gomes Pereira, and Manoel de Souza Fortes.<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" />

By 1748, the settlement of [[Seridó]] belonged to the parish of [[Piancó]], in the present-day state of Paraíba. On 15 April of the same year, a [[royal charter]] elevated the settlement to an administrative district under the name Caicó—a name derived from the Indigenous tribe that once inhabited the area and meaning "thin scrubland."<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" />

The municipality was officially created by [[Royal order|Royal Order]] on 22 July 1766 and implemented by the Government of Pernambuco on 28 April 1788 under the name ''Vila Nova do Príncipe''. It was granted city status on 16 December 1868, by Provincial Law No. 612. Later, through State Decree No. 12 of 1 February 1890, the municipality was renamed Seridó. The name Caicó was restored by State Decree No. 33, issued on 7 July 1890.<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" />

== Geography ==
The territory of Caicó covers {{Convert|1228.583|km²|sp=us}},<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Brasil / Rio Grande do Norte / Caicó |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/panorama |access-date=2025-06-18}}</ref> of which {{Convert|14.0727|km²}} constitutes the urban area.<ref name="Tabela/8418">{{cite web |last=IBGE |year=2019 |title=Tabela 8418 - Áreas urbanizadas, Loteamento vazio, Área total mapeada e Subcategorias |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/8418#/n3/23/n6/2402006/v/12749/p/all/d/v12749%204/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=16 December 2024 |publisher=}}</ref> It sits at an average altitude of 151 meters above [[sea level]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Rio Grande do Norte |url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/rn.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070612212935/http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/rn.html |archive-date=2007-06-12 |access-date=2025-06-18 |website= |publisher=[[Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation]]}}</ref> Caicó borders these municipalities: to the north, [[Jucurutu]], [[Florânia]], and [[São Fernando]]; to the south, [[São João do Sabugi]] and the state of [[Paraíba]] ([[Várzea, Paraíba|Várzea]]); to the east, [[São José do Seridó]], [[Cruzeta]], [[Jardim do Seridó]], and [[Ouro Branco, Rio Grande do Norte|Ouro Branco]]; and to the west, [[Timbaúba dos Batistas]], São Fernando, and [[Serra Negra do Norte]].<ref name="São Gonçalo do Amarante" /> The city is located 222 km from the state capital Natal,<ref name="distancia">{{cite web |title=Distância entre Natal - RN, Brazil e Caicó - RN, Brazil |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-natal-rn-brazil-ate-caico-rn |access-date=14 December 2024 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref> and 1,572 km from the federal capital [[Brasília]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Distância entre Brasilia - DF, Brazil e Caicó - Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-brasilia-df-brazil-ate-caico-rn-brazil |access-date=25 July 2014 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref>

Under the [[Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions|territorial division established in 2017]] by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality belongs to the immediate geographical region of Caicó, within the intermediate region of Caicó.<ref name="MesoMicro">{{cite web |last=IBGE |date=2024 |title=DTB_2024.zip |url=http://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/ |access-date=29 April 2025 |publisher= |language=pt-br |format=[[Zip (file format)|Zip file]]}} RELATORIO_DTB_BRASIL_2024_MUNICIPIOS.ods within zip file.</ref> Previously, under the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] and [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] divisions, it was part of the microregion of Seridó Ocidental in the mesoregion of Central Potiguar.<ref>{{cite journal |year=1990 |title=Divisão regional do Brasil em mesorregiões e microrregiões geográficas |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |url-status=live |journal=Biblioteca IBGE |publisher=IBGE |volume=1 |page=44–45 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170925170313/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |archive-date=25 September 2017 |access-date=25 September 2017}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_DTB_20082">{{cite web |date=1 July 2008 |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/Organizacao/Divisao_Territorial/2008/DTB_2008.zip |access-date=11 October 2008 |work=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}</ref>

=== Climate ===
Caicó has a [[semi-arid climate#Hot semi-arid climates|hot semi-arid climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]] ''BSh''), subject to variability, and the dry periods (droughts) may last more than one year. Rain falls between February and May, with an average annual precipitation of {{convert|700|mm|in|1|disp=or}}. There are about 3,000 hours of average sunshine annually.<ref name="ClimateData_CaicóRN">{{cite web|url=http://en.climate-data.org/location/765469/|title=Climate: Caicó|publisher=Climate Data|accessdate=August 17, 2015|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150818002659/http://en.climate-data.org/location/765469/|archivedate=August 18, 2015 }}{{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/*/http://en.climate-data.org/location/765469/ |date=* }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.wmdigital.com.br/serido/Caracter%EDsticas/caracteristicas.htm |title=Características da Região do Seridó |language=Portuguese |accessdate=September 7, 2016}}</ref> Beside [[Mossoró]] and [[Pau dos Ferros]], both in Western Rio Grande do Norte, Caicó is one of the hottest cities in the state<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www2.uol.com.br/omossoroense/040307/conteudo/regional.htm|title=Mossoró, Pau dos Ferros e Caicó são as cidades mais quentes do RN|author=Márcio Costa|date=March 4, 2007 |publisher=O Mossoroense|language=Portuguese|accessdate=August 17, 2015 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140903133927/http://www2.uol.com.br/omossoroense/040307/conteudo/regional.htm|archivedate=September 3, 2014 }}</ref> with temperatures reaching up to {{convert|38|°C|°F|0}} during the day.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jornaldehoje.com.br/verao-2013-pode-ser-o-mais-quente-dos-ultimos-anos/ |title=Verão 2013 pode ser o mais quente dos últimos anos|date=December 21, 2012|publisher=Jornal de Hoje|accessdate=August 17, 2015 |language=Portuguese|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150619061131/http://jornaldehoje.com.br/verao-2013-pode-ser-o-mais-quente-dos-ultimos-anos/|archivedate=June 19, 2015}}</ref>

According to the Brazilian National Institute of Meteorology, since 1995 the lowest temperature recorded in Caicó was {{convert|16.3|°C|°F|1}} on March 16, 2008,<ref name="LowTemperateINMET_Caicó-RN"/> and the highest reached {{convert|40|°C|°F}} on January 18, 2003 and January 28, 2007.<ref name="HighTemperateINMET_Caicó-RN"/> The highest cumulative [[rainfall]] recorded in 24 hours was {{convert|171.2|mm|1}} on January 22, 1996.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt.php?&mRelEstacao=82690&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1961&mRelDtFim=31/07/2015&mAtributos=,,,,,,,,,,1,,,,,,|title=BDMEP - Série Histórica - Dados Diários - Precipitação (mm) - Seridó (Caicó)|publisher=Brazilian National Institute of Meteorology|language=Portuguese|accessdate=August 17, 2015}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
In the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], the municipality had a population of 61,146 inhabitants and ranked eighth in the state that year (out of 167 municipalities),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122232 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with 52.42% female and 47.58% male,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2402006/v/all/p/all/c2/all/c287/100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=2024-12-16}}</ref> resulting in a sex ratio of 90.77 (9,077 men for every 10,000 women),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Sexo / Razão de sexo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122248 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> compared to 62,709 inhabitants in the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]] (91.63% living in the urban area),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação do domicílio e sexo |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#/n6/2402006/v/all/p/all/c1/all/c2/all/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c1,t+c2/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> when it held the seventh state position.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2010 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true&tipo=ranking&indicador=27665 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Between the 2010 and 2022 censuses, the population of Caicó changed at an annual geometric growth rate of -0.21%.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Taxa de crescimento geométrico ( Unidade: % ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122278 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Regarding [[age group]] in the 2022 census, 70.84% of the inhabitants were between 15 and 64 years old, 17.02% were under fifteen, and 12.13% were 65 or older.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/2402006/v/all/p/all/c2/6794/c287/6653,49108,49109,60040,60041,93070,93084,93085,93086,93087,93088,93089,93090,93091,93092,93093,93094,93095,93096,93097,93098,100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> The [[population density]] in 2022 was 49.77 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Território / Densidade demográfica ( Unidade: habitantes por quilômetro quadrado ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122231 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> There were 22,067 housing units with an average of 2.75 inhabitants per household.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 4712 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, Moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados e Média de moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4712#/n6/2402006/v/all/p/all/d/v5930%202/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=2024-01-06}}</ref>

The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) was considered high, according to data from the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). According to the 2010 report published in 2013, its value was 0.71, ranking seventh in the state and 1,595th nationally (out of 5,565 municipalities),<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web |date=2010 |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610114916/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |access-date=October 11, 2010 |work=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano |publisher=United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)}}</ref> and the [[Gini coefficient]] rose from 0.47 in 2003<ref name="Gini2003">{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2003 |title=Mapa de pobreza e desigualdade |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rn/caico/pesquisa/36/30246?ano=2003 |access-date=2024-12-21}}</ref> to 0.56 in 2010.<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Índice de Gini da renda domiciliar per capita segundo Município |url=http://tabnet.datasus.gov.br/cgi/ibge/censo/cnv/ginibr.def |access-date=17 June 2025 |website=DataSUS}}</ref> Considering only the longevity index, its value is 0.824, the income index is 0.703, and the education index is 0.619.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010" />
