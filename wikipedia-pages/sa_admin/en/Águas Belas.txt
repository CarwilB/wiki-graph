'''Águas Belas''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈaɡ͡wɐ(s) ˈbɛlɐ(s)]}}) (literally ''Beautiful Waters'') is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]].

==History==

The region where Águas Belas is located was originally inhabited by the [[Tupiniquim]], who were expelled by the [[Carnijó]] tribe.  The first Europeans arrived around 1700.  Until 1904, Águas Belas was a district of [[Buíque]].{{Citation needed|date=March 2026}}

The name "Águas Belas" (Portuguese for "Beautiful Waters") originated from the commentary of a judge who was visiting the city and was impressed by the excellent quality of the water in the town.{{Citation needed|date=March 2026}}

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Águas Belas''' are based in industry, commerce  and [[agribusiness]] especially plantations of [[bean]]s, [[Maize|corn]] and [[manioc]]; and creations of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[horse]]s, [[pig]]s and [[chickens]].
