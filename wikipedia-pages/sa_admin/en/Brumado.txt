'''Brumado''' ({{IPA|pt|bɾuˈma.du|lang|Brumado pronúncia.ogg}}) is a Brazilian municipality in the interior of [[Bahia]], in the Northeast region of the country, precisely in the Center-South mesoregion of the State, in the homonymous microregion to the do municipality name, to the do 555 kilometers from [[Salvador, Bahia|Salvador]], state capital. Its territorial area is 2 207,612 square kilometers, the headquarters's area occupying 2 174 square kilometers, have altitude of 454 meters. According to data from the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE), in 2023 its population was estimated at 70 268 inhabitants. The Human Development Index (HDI) is 0.656 (average).<ref name=IBGE/>

A survey by the Federation of Industries of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] State (FIRJAN) found that Brumado is considered to be the sixth most developed municipality in Bahia, based on the FIRJAN Municipal Development Index (IFDM 2013) published in 2015.<ref name= FIRJAN>{{cite web|url=http://www.firjan.com.br/ifdm/consulta-ao-indice/ifdm-indice-firjan-de-desenvolvimento-municipal-resultado.htm?UF=BA&IdCidade=290460&Indicador=1&Ano=2013|title=''IFDM para Brumado-BA''|access-date=December 4, 2015}}</ref> A query made exclusively by Urban Systems, in January 2016, for [[Exame]] Magazine, concluded that the city is among the 100 best in Brazil to invest, ranking 84th in the ranking, totaling 2 306 points. A study carried out by IBGE, in 2012, concluded that of the 64 602 inhabitants from the time, 50 899 were literate, 31 747 were men and 32 855 were women. The per capita income was R$12,144.11, and in distribution between rural and urban areas, in the private households in the rural area, it was R$217.00, and in the urban area it was R$333.33. The fleet of motor vehicles such as road transport, including motorcycles, as well as tractors and trucks totaled 31 000.<ref name=IBGE/>

The city is known as "the Mining Capital" in Bahia, because it has a large amount of minerals in its subsoil, which is the basis of its economy, and has large mining companies that carry out their extractive activities in Serra das Éguas (in English, something like "Ridge of the Mares"), which, by the way, also is one of the tourist points of the mountainous landscapes of the Municipality.<ref name=Cetem>{{cite web|url=http://mineralis.cetem.gov.br/bitstream/handle/cetem/1116/27.%20MAGNESITA%20(novo1).pdf?sequence=1|title=Magnesita|access-date=April 11, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223235504/http://mineralis.cetem.gov.br/bitstream/handle/cetem/1116/27.%20MAGNESITA%20(novo1).pdf?sequence=1|archive-date=2015-12-23|url-status=dead}}</ref>
Brumado shares boundaries with the Municipalities of [[Livramento de Nossa Senhora]], [[Dom Basílio]], [[Aracatu]], [[Rio de Contas]], [[Malhada de Pedras]], [[Tanhaçu]], [[Ituaçu]], [[Rio do Antônio]], [[Lagoa Real]] and [[Caraíbas]].<ref name=DOficial>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumado.ba.io.org.br/diarioOficial/download/109/880/0|title=''Aspectos Gerais''|access-date=December 20, 2015|archive-date=March 3, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303215617/http://www.brumado.ba.io.org.br/diarioOficial/download/109/880/0|url-status=dead}}</ref> For making border with the Municipality of Rio de Contas along the river with thew same name, consequently also makes border with the Chapada Diamantina. The city has a privileged location allied to other benefits, such as important crossroad such as the State highways BA-262 and BA-148 and federal highway BR-030, also counting on the Centro-Atlântica Railroad (FCA).<ref name=UNEB0>{{cite web|url=http://monografias.brasilescola.uol.com.br/geografia/o-crescimento-urbano-dagradacao-hidrica-no-vetor-norte-.htm|title=''O crescimento Urbano e a Degradação Hídrica no Vetor Norte da Cidade de Brumado-BA''|access-date=November 16, 2015}}</ref>

==History==
===Etymology===
There are several theories about the origin of the name Brumado. According to Teodoro Fernandes Sampaio, the name "Brumado" comes from the expression [[Tupi language]]: ''Itimbopira'' (''Y''-''timbó''-''pyra''), which means misty, covered in fog, but according to José Dias, former priest of the city, the name originates from the Serra das Éguas, when the morning fog descends from it covering the city. Another theory says that the origin of the name Brumado is attributed to the word ''bromine'' - a word that at the time of mining on the [[Rio Brumado]] was used by garimpeiros and bandeirantes to distinguish loss or deceit, mystification or disappearance of gold in the mining site that supposedly had an abundance of that ore.<ref name=PB>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumado.ba.gov.br/historia.php|title =''Origin of the name''|access-date=November 11, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150923194812/http://www.brumado.ba.gov.br/history.php|archive-date=2015-09-23|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Câmara_de_Vereadores_de_Brumado">{{cite web|url=http://www.cmbrumado.ba.gov.br/historiadacamara|title=History of the Chamber|access-date=November 4, 2016|archive-date=April 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160402233618/http://www.cmbrumado.ba.gov.br/historiadacamara|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=UNEB_XX>{{cite web|url=http://www.uneb.br/brumado/dcht/sobre-brumado/|title=About Brumado|access-date=November 4, 2016|archive-date=June 22, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170622192515/http://www.uneb.br/brumado/dcht/sobre-brumado/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Settlement===
[[File:O Sobrado do Brejo, Brumado.jpg|left|thumb|{{center|O Sobrado do Brejo (painting by Alessandro Silva) was a mansion from the 19th century. It was located in Serra Das Éguas in Brumado. It was the residence of the colonels (lords of Sobrado do Brejo). Today, it belongs to Magnesita.}}]]
In the 18th century, captain Francisco de Souza Meira and his team left the captaincy of Minas Gerais (current state of [[Minas Gerais]]), crossing the Brumado river, advancing along the right bank until reaching the mouth of the Do (Santo) Antônio river, arriving to the [[Indigenous (ecology)|indigenous]] territory, [[Tupinambá people|Tupinambas]] fierce, and between them a great fight took place, close to the area where today is the headquarters of the Brumado Municipality. It was the first part taken and received the name "Conquista", later he founded a [[farm]] called Das Éguas Serra. Although these Indians strongly resisted the attacks of the captain and his group, they gradually dispersed, disturbed by the presence of white men.<ref name="Câmara_de_Vereadores_de_Brumado"/>

In the future, the Serra das Éguas farm was acquired by João Antunes Moreira, who in turn left it to his son, the Catholic priest André Antunes da Maia; who finally sold it on June 30, 1749, to José de Souza Meira, son of Francisco de Souza Meira, for $462,700 (1 million, 462 thousand and 700 [[Brazilian Real (former)|réis]]), with 232 head of cattle, 105 head of equine cattle and a black slave called Manuel. In this way, the lands returned to the hands of the Meiras. In 1755, Campo Seco farm was bought by the Portuguese Miguel Lourenço de Almeida ([[familia do Santo Ofício]]), clan of the Canguçu family in the municipality. The Serra das Éguas farm was close to the area that is now the city of Brumado. It was located on the southern slope, the lower part of a [[mountain]], now known as Serra das Éguas, known for its deposits of [[magnesite]] and mineral talc. Over the years, a small village grew about ten kilometers from the city center on the same bank of the river, thanks to the success of the adventurers who worked in crops and livestock, giving life to the village that would later become the seat of the municipality.<ref name="Câmara_de_Vereadores_de_Brumado"/>

===Emancipation===
[[File:Antiga prefeitura municipal de Brumado.jpg|thumb|left|{{center|Old City Hall.}}|220px]]
The region where Brumado in now located belonged to the colonial Comarca (Municipality) of Minas do [[Rio de Contas]] from 1723 to 1810, when created the Municipality of Vila Nova do Príncipe e Santana de Caitité (currently [[Caetité]]). On June 19, 1869, still under the name of Freguesia do Bom Jesus dos Meiras, the then Vice-President of the imperial [[Province of Bahia]], Antônio Ledeslau de Figueiredo Rocha, signed Provincial Decree-Law No. 1091, thus transforming Bom Jesus dos Meiras into a district under the same name, as part of [[Caetité]]. Soon after, Antônio Pinheiro Pinto Canguçu, the second lord of Sobrado do Brejo, contributed financially and with labor to build the Chapel of Bom Jesus, attending the requests of the inhabitants; Today, the old chapel is the mother church of Brumado, that at that time was called Church Senhor do Bonfim, in honor of the patron of the city, Bom Jesus, and had like first vicar the priest Jose Mariano Meira Rocha.<ref name=UNEB_XX/> Bom Jesus dos Meiras separated from Caetité in 1877, receiving the title of [[village]]. The author of the project was a Provincial Deputy of Bahia, Marcolino Moura. The political leader at that time was Colonel Exupério Pinheiro Canguçu, the fourth and last lord of [[Sobrado do Brejo]]. Political emancipation occurred on June 11, 1877(Provincial Law n° 1756). The first City Council of Bom Jesus dos Meiras was inaugurated on February 13, 1878, composed by: Colonel Exupério Pinheiro Canguçu (as president); Belarmino Jacundes Lobo (as secretary); Rufiniano de Moura Amorim (as procuror); Plácido Guedes d'Oliveira (as supervisor); Francisco Alves Piranha (as usher ou chamberlain).<ref name=PB/><ref name="Câmara_de_Vereadores_de_Brumado"/><ref name=UNEB_XX/>

In 1948, Armindo dos Santos Azevedo took office as the first elected mayor by [[Direct election|direct vote]]. The merchant and cattle rancher was elected for three terms: 1948 - 1950, 1955 - 1958 and 1963 - 1966. In its [[management]], the Municipality achieved great advances, such as water supply, [[electricity]] and paving of many streets and squares in the center of city. The energy in Brumado in the 1940s to 1970s only worked until 9:30&nbsp;p.m. It was from then on that the energy began to function in an integral way.<ref name=Angela>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibahia.com/a/blogs/empregos/files/2014/10/CINE-F%C3%81TIMA-MONOGRAFIA-DE-%C3%82NGELA-DOS-SANTOS-MEIRA-OUT.-2014.pdf|title=''O cinema de Brumado Enquanto Espaço de Socialização — o Cine Fátima em Brumado''|access-date=December 1, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160306090233/http://www.ibahia.com/a/blogs/empregos/files/2014/10/CINE-F%C3%81TIMA-MONOGRAFIA-DE-%C3%82NGELA-DOS-SANTOS-MEIRA-OUT.-2014.pdf|archive-date=2016-03-06|url-status=dead}}</ref> In 1962, the districts of Santa Bárbara dos Casados, Olhos d'Água and São Pedro would have their toponyms changed to Ubiraçaba, Itaquaraí and Aracatu, respectively.<ref name=Angela/> From 1967 to 1970, the mayor was elected doctor Juracy Pires Gomes, with the support of [[Business partner|partner]] Armindo dos Santos Azevedo. In his mandate important works were carried out, such as the new city hall, the new municipal market, the city's first public [[library]]; The main one was the dam of the Do Antônio River that would supply the city until the year 2010.<ref name=UNEB_XX/><ref name=Angela/>

==Economic expansion==

At the beginning of the 20th century, the [[economy]] was based on [[agriculture]]: large-scale [[cotton]] farming, [[coffea arabica|coffee]] and [[cereal]]s, and cattle raising, [[goats]], sheep and cattle, favoring the [[export]] of sheep skins raw or tanned, dry leather and salty, as well as lump cotton and live cattle. Cotton was the culture that most contributed to the economic development of the municipality. Between 1930 and 1955, the production was so great that the producers started to benefit it in the field even using handmade machines. Faced with the success of cotton growing, modern cotton factories were installed in the city. The municipality had six plants in operation. Since 1980, the crop has declined, due to several factors, as an interruption of financial aid by the government, (such as crop insurance), [[cotton boll weevil]] (pests that destroy crops), high interest rates for production financing, and of the processing.<ref name=Vera_Marcelo>{{cite web|url=http://www2.desenbahia.ba.gov.br/publitao/arquivos/arquivos/b1879eb7c9c64b7bbcdc5f2342c62cde.pdf|title=Desafios ao fortalecimento da cadeia do algodão: o caso da região oeste|access-date=December 1, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161105033445/http://www2.desenbahia.ba.gov.br/publitao/arquivos/arquivos/b1879eb7c9c64b7bbcdc5f2342c62cde.pdf|archive-date=2016-11-05|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=Cidemar>{{cite web|url=http://www.tribunapopulargbi.com.br/?lk=10&id=331|title=''Zona rural de Brumado sofre com a crise da cotonicultura''|access-date= December 1, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Estado_da_Bahia>{{cite web|url=http://memoria.bn.br/DocReader/Hotpage/HotpageBN.aspx?bib=313394&pagfis=99809&pesq=&url=http://memoria.bn.br/docreader#|title=Bom Jesus dos Meiras|access-date=December 1, 2016}}</ref> Currently, the production of passion fruit has been outstanding, in the district of Itaquaraí, one of the largest production locations in Brazil.<ref name="97_Notícias">{{cite web|url=http://www.97news.com.br/noticias/4966-2015/08/22/itaquarai-ira-sediar-o-primeiro-encontro-dos-produtores-de-maracuja-do-sertao-produtivo|title=''Itaquaraí irá sediar o primeiro encontro dos produtores de maracujá do Sertão Produtivo''|access-date= December 1, 2016}}</ref>

In 1930, two important events marked the economic history of the municipality: the arrival of the mining company Magnesita S.A., today, [[RHI Magnesita]] founded by two French friends naturalized Brazilian and resident in Brazil, Miguel Pierre Cahen and Georges Louis Minviele - in partnership with Antônio Mourão Guimarães -, a politician of the time, and the construction of the Federal Railroad Network (RFFSA), which would facilitate the transportation of the newly discovered ore to the port of Aratu in Salvador. Both events occurred in 1939. The Magnesita company was authorized to operate in 1940 through Decree nº 6220 and since then, it has exploited most of the magnesite and mineral talc reserves contained in Das Éguas Saw. In the 1970s, Brumado arrived at the Brazilian Refractory Products Industries (IBAR S.A.). The company interested in extracting magnesite has established its facilities in the northern part of the saw. In the following years, the municipality witnessed great changes, such as large-scale population growth, construction of houses and commercial establishments. Faced with this great attraction, people from other cities were arriving, commerce strengthened, and the  Brumado city became known by recent events. ==Geography==

The Brumado municipality is located in the Center South (or Southwest) of Bahia, in the region known geographically as the Polygon of the Droughts, in Serra Geral, under the coordinates: 14° 12' 13", south latitude; 41° 39' 55" west longitude, at 454 meters altitude and is 555 kilometers southwest of Salvador. The urban area covers an area of 2 174 square kilometers.<ref name=Sesab>{{cite web|url=http://www1.saude.ba.gov.br/mapa_bahia/municipio.asp?CIDADE=290460|title=''Mapa da saúde''|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-date=January 7, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170107171014/http://www1.saude.ba.gov.br/mapa_bahia/municipio.asp?CIDADE=290460|url-status=dead}}</ref> There is no information if there is a point higher than the Das Éguas Saw peak, probably this is the highest point of the municipality, with 1 100 meters.<ref name=Uesb/><ref name=Senado>{{cite web|url=http://legis.senado.gov.br/legislacao/ListaPublicacoes.action?id=197999|title=Decreto nº 67 747 de 8 de dezembro de 1970|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161109021816/http://legis.senado.gov.br/legislacao/ListaPublicacoes.action?id=197999|archive-date=2016-11-09|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=Apolo_11>{{cite web|url=http://www.apolo11.com/latlon.php?uf=ba&cityid=279|title=Longitude e latitude das cidades brasileiras|access-date= December 2, 2016}}</ref> The municipality limits with Livramento de Nossa Senhora and Dom Basilio, to the [[north]]; Rio de Contas, Ituaçu and Tanhaçu, to the northeast; Aracatu, to the [[east]]; Rio do Antônio and Lagoa Real, to the [[west]]; Malhada de Pedras to the [[southwest]] and the Caraíbas to the [[south]]. Its micro-region has a homonymous name. The urbanization rate is 69.86%, population density is 29.01 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name=IBGE/><ref name=DOficial/>

===Relief and hydrography===
[[File:Rio do Antônio.jpg|thumb|200px|right|{{center|Antônio River, Brumado.}}]]
The Brumado municipality is characterized by mountainous formations typical of the Serra do Espinhaço (Of Espinhaço Mountain), which is the mountainous chain that occupies much of the southwest of the State and which belongs the relief of the municipality. The relief of Brumado is very bumpy, presenting mountainous areas denominated mountains and/or ''altomontanas'', like the Das Éguas Saw and Black stone hill (Morro da Pedra Preta).<ref name=CBPM>{{cite web|url=http://www.cbpm.com.br/paginas/meio_bahia.php|title=''Meio ambiente na Bahia''|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160121124633/http://www.cbpm.com.br/paginas/meio_bahia.php|archive-date=2016-01-21|url-status=dead}}</ref> There are also level areas, which are flat and / or wavy forms, which make the transition between the upper and lower areas, forming species of stairs. Also has flat and flat relief shapes.<ref name=Mariana>{{cite web|url=http://www.twiki.ufba.br/twiki/pub/IGeo/GeolMono20131/mariana_lima.pdf|title=Caracterização hidroquímica da qualidade da água no município de Brumado-BA|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-date=December 24, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224001005/http://www.twiki.ufba.br/twiki/pub/IGeo/GeolMono20131/mariana_lima.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> In general, the Brumadian relief is quite rugged, slightly undulating and presents erosions due to the floods, favorable characteristics for biodiversity, contributing to a wide variety of species, both in flora and fauna.<ref name=Chesf>{{cite web|url=http://www.chesf.gov.br/portal/pls/portal/docs/1/1571216.PDF|title=Relatório de Impacto Ambiental da Linha de Transmissão Brumado a Ibicoara|access-date=August 12, 2015}}</ref>
[[File:Barragem de Cristalandia.jpg|thumb|200px|left|{{center|The dam of Cristalândia.}}]]
The Brumado municipality is part of the [[De Contas River]] basin and of the Do Antônio River sub-basin, which also includes the municipalities: Caculé, Rio do Antônio, Licínio de Almeida, Guajeru and Malhada de Pedras. The main drains of the Do Contas River basin are: Brumado River, São João River, Santa Maria Stream, Do Antônio River and Gavião River; The main tributaries of Antônio River are: Paiol River, Do Salto River and Do Quirino Stream. The Antônio River Basin has an area of 6,540 square kilometers.<ref name=UNEB0/><ref name=Cristiane>{{cite web|url=http://mhn.uepb.edu.br/revista_tarairiu/n7/art4.pdf|title=Levantamento arqueológico no alto curso do Rio Gavião - BA.|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161107012412/http://mhn.uepb.edu.br/revista_tarairiu/n7/art4.pdf|archive-date=2016-11-07|url-status=dead}}</ref> Two of the great tributaries are born in very different places: the Brumado River, affluent of the Contas, is born in the Saw of souls (Serra das Almas) and of Antônio River in General Saw (Serra Geral), in Licínio de Almeida municipality.<ref name=UNEB0/><ref name=Lara_Oliveira>{{cite web|url=http://periodicos.uesb.br/index.php/coloquiobaiano/article/viewFile/2870/pdf_95|title=''Evolução socioeconômica da Microregião de Livramento de Nossa Senhora-BA''|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161107013259/http://periodicos.uesb.br/index.php/coloquiobaiano/article/viewFile/2870/pdf_95|archive-date=November 7, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref> In De Contas River is inserted the dam of Cristalândia Which supplies the city, and in Do Antônio River, the dam of the Antônio River, which supplied the urban population by 2010.<ref name=Embasa>{{cite web|url=http://www.embasa.ba.gov.br/content/barragem-de-cristal%C3%A2ndia-%C3%A9-solu%C3%A7%C3%A3o-para-escassez-de-%C3%A1gua-na-regi%C3%A3o-de-brumado|title=Barragem de Cristalândia é a solução para escassez de água na região de Brumado|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303180210/http://www.embasa.ba.gov.br/content/barragem-de-cristal%C3%A2ndia-%C3%A9-solu%C3%A7%C3%A3o-para-escassez-de-%C3%A1gua-na-regi%C3%A3o-de-brumado|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref> Most rivers are temporary, such as Do Antônio River and São João River. The De Contas River Basin has annual precipitation between 700 and 800&nbsp;mm, with little possibility of major floods. The average temperature is 22&nbsp;°C. It is characterized by two [[biome]]s: [[Caatinga]] and [[Atlantic Forest]] (Mata Atlântica), which have suffered [[deforestation]], to give way to agriculture and urbanization. It covers an area of 4 477.62 square kilometers. The municipality that most demands water from the basin is [[Jequié]].<ref name=Poliana>{{cite web|url=http://www.biblioteca.uesc.br/biblioteca/bdtd/200960112d.pdf|title=Potencial de usos dos recursos hídricos para irrigação na sub-bacia de transição do Rio das Contas|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref> The sub basin of the Do Antônio River has suffered over time with water degradation, due to the intense pollution of its waters, deforestation of the riparian forests and the excess of dams that are built. The main drainage of the sub basin, the river of the same name, has as its main function human supply, with minimum use for [[irrigation]].<ref name=UNEB0/>

===Geomorphology===
[[File:Gráfico de ocorrências de minerais na Serra das Éguas.jpeg|thumb|500px|right|{{center|Graph of occurrences of minerals in Das Éguas Saw.}}<ref name=Ufba0/>]]
Geologically, Brumado is located in the São Francisco Craton, precisely in the Gavião Geological Block, in the Greenstone Belts Umburanas.<ref name=Mariana/><ref name=CBPM0>{{cite web|url=http://www.oocities.org/teomag/teogeo/bahiamapa/greenstone.htm|title=Greenstone belts sequenciais vulcanossedimentares equivalentes|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref> In these areas, the rocks are formed by metavolcanic sequences, that is to say of very old volcanic actions, which favored the formation of [[metamorphic rocks]] of a great quantity of minerals, [[metal]]s and [[Nonmetal (chemistry)|nonmetal]]s such as iron, [[manganese]], [[emerald]]s, [[quartz]], [[granite]]s and different types of ornamental stones.<ref name=CBPM0/> The chart below shows the main occurrences of minerals in the Brumadian underground and their corresponding percentages. In geochronology studies it was verified that the oldest rock formations of [[South America]], with the age of 3.4 to 3.5 billion years, are between the municipalities Brumado municipality and [[Vitória da Conquista]] municipality. In the geological scale, the soil is from the [[Paleoproterozoic]] Age or the Lower Proterozoic Age, equivalent to 1.6 and 2.5 [[Gigaannum]]. It was at this age that the oldest rocks and the richest soils in any and every type of ore were formed. This explains why Das Éguas Saw is so rich in minerals.<ref name=Geologia>{{cite web|url=http://www.oocities.org/teomag/teogeo/bahiamapa/geolbatxt.htm|title=''Geologia da Bahia''|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref> In addition to magnesite, Brumadian soil contains all these minerals already mentioned above, which leads us to conclude that it is one of the richest soils in Brazil and one of the oldest in the world, making it one of the most studied by [[geologists]].<ref name=CBPM0/>

===Climate===
The predominant [[climate]] in Brumado is the [[tropical]] steppe, making a transition with the [[semi-arid climate]], classified as BSh (hot and dry), according to [[Wladimir Köppen]] and [[Rudolf Geiger]], however, there are much lower temperatures at the top of the hills, always citing as an example the Das Éguas Saw and Pedra Preta Hill, one of the highest points of this mountain range. The rainy season is concentrated between October and January, with a difference of 128&nbsp;mm between the driest month,  August and the rainier month, of December. The rest of the year, the rains are scarce. The average temperature is 23.8&nbsp;°C, with an average annual rainfall of 590 to 600&nbsp;mm.<ref name=Cetem/><ref name=Climate0>{{cite web|url=http://pt.climate-data.org/location/43289/|title=Clima de Brumado|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref> same with the scarcity of rainfall, the city already suffered with excesses, as in 1968, when the Do Antônio River suffered a great flood at the point of water reaching the center of the city, after passing over the bridge of the São Félix neighborhood, which Was caught.<ref name=Brumado_Agora1>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/noticias/6633-2013/12/29/brumado-enchentes-historicas-do-rio-do-antonio|title=''Enchentes históricas do Rio do Antônio''|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref><ref name="Brumado_Notícias1">{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/9-2013/03/28/viagem-no-tempo-45-anos-da-enchente-em-brumado#bn-foto-1|title=''45 anos da enchente em Brumado''|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224103954/http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/9-2013/03/28/viagem-no-tempo-45-anos-da-enchente-em-brumado#bn-foto-1|archive-date=December 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Other floods occurred in 1982 and 1992. It also suffered major droughts, as in 1998.<ref name=Embasa/><ref name=Carina0>{{cite web|url=https://repositorio.unb.br/bitstream/10482/8309/1/2010_CarinaGomesMessias.pdf|title=Análise da degradação ambiental da microbacia do Rio do Antônio em Brumado|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223235644/http://repositorio.unb.br/bitstream/10482/8309/1/2010_CarinaGomesMessias.pdf|archive-date=2015-12-23|url-status=dead}}</ref> The months of January of the years of 2015 and 2016 were considered "good of rain", registering 350&nbsp;mm and 530&nbsp;mm, respectively, the latter almost reaching the annual average. The highest accumulated of that month happened in 1985, when it was registered more than 1 100&nbsp;mm.<ref name="Brumado_Notícias2">{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/15571-2016/01/27/acumulado-da-chuva-passa-dos-530-mm-em-brumado-e-ja-e-o-recorde-dos-ultimos-43-anos|title=Acumulado da chuva passa dos 530 mm em Brumado e já é recorde dos últimos 43 anos|access-date=December 2, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161107094148/http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/15571-2016/01/27/acumulado-da-chuva-passa-dos-530-mm-em-brumado-e-ja-e-o-recorde-dos-ultimos-43-anos|archive-date=November 7, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Ecology and environment==

===Fauna and flora===
[[File:Umbuzeiro.jpg|thumb|left|{{center|''Umbuzeiro'', tree of the umbu fruit.}}|200px]][[File:Veado-da-caatinga.jpeg|thumb|left|{{center|Deer of Caatinga.}}|200px]]
Along the municipality, there are four types of vegetation existing in all Brazilian territorial extent: [[Ciliar Forest]], [[Cerrado]], Rupestre Field and Caatinga, which is the predominant [[vegetation]]. In the ridges of the hills and mountains, such as Das Éguas Saw and of Pedra Preta Hill, and on the banks of the rivers are closed and small [[tropical forests]], called "generals". [[Campos rupestres]] (in English Rupestrian field), typical vegetation of montane and higmontanes environments, occur at altitudes above 900 m, as is the case again in the Das Éguas Saw and Da Pedra Preta Hill; Is characterized by the predominance of shrubs and/or herbaceous plants, precisely in the mountainous summits of the mountains.<ref name=CBPM/><ref name=Mariana/><ref name=Chesf/>

The species occurring in the Caatinga are very diversified, some with important commercial and ecological value, such as ''aroeira'' (''[[Astronium urundeuva]]'') and braúna, or baraúna (''[[Schinopsis brasiliensis]]''), angico and black angico (''Anadenanthera macrocarpa'') and umbuzeiro (''Spondias tuberosa''), one of the which is protected by Municipal Law No. 1030 of 1992. Other notable species are the ''juazeiro'' and ''jatobá'', and other species: cassutinga (''[[Caesalpinia microphylla]]''), black maria (''[[Vitex sp.]]''), ''umburana'' or northeastern cumaru (''[[Amburana cearensis]]''), white jurema (''[[Mimosa sp.]]''), among many other creeping and shrub plant.<ref name=Chesf/>[[File:Cougar - panoramio (4).jpg|thumb|{{center|''Puma concolor'', The largest cat in the region.}}|200px]]

Although the municipality has small forest islands called Riparian Forests, often located on the banks of the rivers and streams of the region, the [[fauna]] is composed of animals adapted to the Caatinga. Animal biodiversity often ends up having its natural habitat threatened by miners, ranchers and [[hunter]]s, who aim at territorial expansion, exploitation of natural resources and also illegal trade in animals; Consequently, it ends up entailing a [[predator]]y activity unrestrained and without any control with respect to the ecological imbalance that this action can cause.<ref name=Mariana/><ref name=Chesf/>

Brumado has a very wide biodiversity, both vegetal and animal, ranging from small specimens to medium-sized animals threatened with extinction. In the [[fauna]], a reasonable amount of mammals prevails; At least 148 species were cataloged in the interior of the municipality and in the surrounding regions, practically all threatened with extinction. They are distributed between Cerrado and Caatinga; As the highlight is the puma known as ''suçuarana'' (or [[Jaguarundi]]), being one of the species most endangered and the mocca (''[[Kerodon rupestris]]''), because it is endemic (exclusive of the region). Other examples are: caatinga deer, [[armadillo]] or ''tatupeba'' (''[[Euphractus]]''), [[paca]] (''[[Agouti paca]]''), [[Opossum|possum]] (''Conepatus semistriata''), [[fox]] and [[raccoon]] (''Dusicyon thous''), mallard cat or puma yagouaroundi (''[[Herpailurus yaguaroundi]]''), [[ferret]] (''Galictis vittatus''), [[coati]] (''Nasua nassua''), among others. Disordered hunting of these animals for food consumption is the factor that most contributes to putting animals on the extinction list.<ref name=Chesf/>

There are also a myriad of birds that are part of the fauna; Most of them are targeted by breeders and collectors, favoring the trafficking of these animals. Among the most common and well-known are: [[Atlantic canary|canary]], quail, partridge, northeastern cardinal (''[[Paroaria dominicana]]''), ''Caatinga'' ''bacural'' (''[[Chordeiles pusillus]]''), canan crow (''[[Cyanocorax cyanopogon]]''), mustache(''[[Sporophila lineola]]''), cowgirl-heron (''[[Bubulcus ibis]]''), Great Blue Heron (''[[Casmerdius albus]]''), ''garrincha'' (''[[Thryothorus genibarbis]]''), ''seriema'' (''[[Cariama cristata]]''), fire sanhaço (''[[Piranga flava]]''), blackbird or ''coqui'' (''[[Gnorimopsar chopi]]'' ), white anu (''[[Guira guira]]''), black anu (''[[Crotophaga ani]]''), [[woodpecker]] (''[[Dryocopus lineatus]]''), crack iron (''[[Saltador simillis]]''), vi-vi or fim-fim (''[[Euphonia clorotica]]'') Caatinga (''[[Aratinga cactorum]]''), ''sofrê'' (''[[Icterus jamacaii]]''), canary of the kingdom, canary of the earth, ''sabiá'' and many others. Due to size, quail and partridge are hunted constantly inside the municipality.<ref name=Chesf/> The species of [[amphibian]]s and [[reptile]]s present in the local fauna are small animals, like ''calango'' (''[[Tropidurus torquatus]]''). Of these species, the green calango (''[[Ameiva ameiva]]'') is the most commonly seen because it has no commercial value, is not edible, nor is venomous. The ''teiú'' (''[[Tupinambis merianae]]'') and the armadillo are well-hunted animals. The best-known snake species are rattlesnake (''[[Crotalus durissus]]'') and jararaca (''[[Bothrops jararaca]]''), both are venomous.<ref name=Chesf/>

===Religion===
[[File:IgrejaMatriz-Brumado-BA.jpg|thumb|left|{{center|Mother church.}}|220px]]
Bahia is a very culturally diverse state and when it comes to religion it could not be different. Nevertheless, many who attend [[Catholicism|catholic]] cults also attend cults of religions of African matrices, such as [[umbanda]] and [[candomblé]]. In Brazil, all who are baptized in the Catholic Church are considered Catholic, even if they practice another religion.<ref name=Abril_na_linha>{{cite web|url=http://www.abril.com.br/pagina/principais_religioes.shtml|title=''Principais religiões''|access-date=December 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161126164059/http://www.abril.com.br/pagina/principais_religioes.shtml|archive-date=2016-11-26|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In Brumado, in 2012, according to data from the IBGE, 50 869 people professed Catholicism, 7 422 identified themselves as evangelicals and 994 people identified themselves as spiritists. Of those who professed [[umbanda]] or [[candomblé]], they were not even mentioned;<ref name=IBGE/> But this does not mean that religions of African matrices are not present. There are no numbers on these religions in Brumado, but they do exist, and there is information in the city that is the largest center of candomblé in the world, in the shape of a castle.<ref name=Portal_Brumado0>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/especial-espiritualismo-candomble|archive-url=https://archive.today/20151223193654/http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/especial-espiritualismo-candomble|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 23, 2015|title=Especial espiritualismo candomblé|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref>

For Catholics, they have the traditional processions, such as the procession of St. Sebastian, patron saint of the city. Since 1960, every January 20, Catholic faithful have celebrated St. Sebastian's Day, walking several streets in the city center. It is also celebrated the Day of Santa Rita, with [[processions]].<ref name=TV_Nova_Vida>{{cite web|url=http://redevida.com.br/programa/jctv/paroquia-bom-jesus-realiza-festa-para-santa-rita-em-brumado-ba.html|title=''Paróquia Bom Jesus realiza festa para Santa Rita em Brumado''|access-date=December 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303172659/http://redevida.com.br/programa/jctv/paroquia-bom-jesus-realiza-festa-para-santa-rita-em-brumado-ba.html|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref> Many other religious manifestations are held, mostly by Catholics and sympathizers. For [[Evangelicalism|evangelicals]], the greatest festive religious event is the March for [[Jesus]], which usually takes place in September. Many evangelicals gather in the center of the city to fraternize with their brothers to the sound of [[gospel music]]. Also on Evangelical Day, June 11, which coincides with the date of Brumado's birthday, supporters celebrate in a double dose, reconciling the dates to the two celebrations, with musical shows<ref name=97_News>{{cite web|url=http://www.97news.com.br/noticias/147-2013/09/02/brumado-marcha-para-jesus-foi-um-sucesso|title=''Marcha para jesus foi um sucesso''|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Se_Liga_Brumado>{{cite web|url=http://www.seligabrumado.com.br/2014/06/brumado-minha-terra-minha-historia_11.html?m=1|title=''Brumado, minha terra, minha vida''|access-date=December 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303202246/http://www.seligabrumado.com.br/2014/06/brumado-minha-terra-minha-historia_11.html?m=1|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Politics and administration==
[[File:Prefeitura de Brumado.jpg|thumb|right|{{center|Current City Hall.}}|220px]]
According to the Brazilian Constitution of 1988, Brumado is part of a presidential republic, based on the Democratic government of the United States model. The municipal administration is in charge of the Executive Branch and the Legislative Branch. Until 1930, the Brazilian municipalities were governed by the mayor of the corresponding municipality, called intendants or executive agents. After the Revolution of 1930, the municipal powers were divided between executive and legislative.<ref name="Arquivo_Histórico_do_Legislativo">{{cite web|url=http://www.cmp.sc.gov.br/files/camara/arquivos/5.pdf|title=''Arquivo histórico virtual do Legislativo — história do legislativo no Brasil''|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref><ref name="Conteúdo_jurídico">{{cite web|url=http://www.conteudojuridico.com.br/artigo,um-breve-historico-sobre-as-constituicoes-brasileiras,36050.html|title=''Um breve histórico sobre a Constituição brasileira''|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Vitor_Amorim>{{cite web|url=http://educacao.uol.com.br/disciplinas/historia-brasil/constituicao-de-1988-1-contexto-historico-e-politico.htm|title=''Constituição de 1988, contexto histórico e político''|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref> In 1877, the provincial deputy Marcolino Moura elaborated a project calling for the political emancipation of Brumado, then called Bom Jesus dos Meiras. Brumado, then, emancipated himself from Caetité at the time of coronelism, on June 11 of that year. The city council was created on January 13 of the following year, when Colonel Exupério Pinheiro Canguçu, the fourth and last lord of Sobrado do Brejo, became the first intendant.<ref name=UNEB_XX/>

Currently Brumado is part of the 90th electoral zone of Bahia with more than 66 061 voters, counting with the voters of the municipalities of Aracatu and Malhada de Pedras, of which 47 845 are in Brumado.<ref name=Uol>{{cite web|url=http://placar.eleicoes.uol.com.br/2012/1turno/ba/brumado|title=''Apuração do primeiro turno''|access-date=December 2, 2016}}</ref><ref name=TRE>{{cite web|url=http://www.tre-ba.jus.br/imprensa/noticias-tre-ba/2013/Dezembro/inaugurado-o-novo-forum-eleitoral-de-brumado|title=Inaugurado o novo fórum eleitoral de Brumado|access-date=December 5, 2016|archive-date=March 3, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303172147/http://www.tre-ba.jus.br/imprensa/noticias-tre-ba/2013/Dezembro/inaugurado-o-novo-forum-eleitoral-de-brumado|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=Brumado_Agora2>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/noticias/20993-eleicoes-2016-47845-eleitores-estao-aptos-a-votarem-em-brumado|title=Eleições 2016: 47 845 eleitores estão aptos a votarem em Brumado|access-date=December 5, 2016}}</ref> The legislative chamber is composed of 13 councilors elected from among the 72 candidates who ran in the 2012 elections.<ref name="Câmara_de_Vereadores_de_Brumado"/><ref name="Médio_São_Francisco">{{cite web|url=http://mediosaofrancisco.com/2012/08/01/tse-defere-a-candidatura-de-18-candidatos-a-vereadores-de-brumado/|title=''TSE defere a candidatura de 18 candidatos a vereadores em Brumado''|access-date=December 5, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160220022619/http://mediosaofrancisco.com/2012/08/01/tse-defere-a-candidatura-de-18-candidatos-a-vereadores-de-brumado/|archive-date=February 20, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref> To better serve the electorate, the new headquarters of the electoral forum was inaugurated in 2013.<ref name=TRE/>

==Economy==

Trade is very developed, and the city has seven banking institutions: [[Itaú Unibanco|Itaú Bank]], [[Banco Bradesco|Bradesco Bank]], [[Banco do Nordeste|Do Nordeste Bank]], [[Banco do Brasil|Do Brasil Bank]], [[Caixa Econômica Federal|Caixa Bank]], Sicoob Bank and Do Povo Bank. From the discovery of the mines of magnesite and mineral talc, the city began to develop, and with that the commerce was strengthened.<ref name=Portal_Brumado2>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/brumado---bahia|archive-url=https://archive.today/20151223193659/http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/brumado---bahia|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 23, 2015|title=''Brumado — histórico''|access-date=December 6, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Prefeitura_Municipal1>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumado.ba.gov.br/ler.php?cod=601|title=''Inaugurada em Brumado a agência do Banco do Povo''|access-date=December 6, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303175616/http://www.brumado.ba.gov.br/ler.php?cod=601|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Primary sector===
[[File:Agência Bradesco em Brumado.jpg|thumb|left|{{center|Bradesco Bank In the Armindo Azevedo Square, downtown.}}|220px]]
The [[Primary sector of the economy|primary sector]], which encompasses extractive, [[livestock]] and [[agricultural]] activities, is the sector with the lowest participation in the [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP) of the municipality. It is not such a strong economic base. In 2006, the [[Superintendency of Economic and Social Studies of Bahia]] (SEI) published data, where agriculture and cattle raising appeared in that year with only 5.04% of the municipal GDP. Although this sector has a minimal contribution, it can highlight some important activities.<ref name=Ufba0/> In agriculture, crops such as cotton stand out, with 800 [[hectare]]s planted and harvested; [[Beans]] with 2 000 hectares planted and harvested; [[ricinus]] or ''mamona'', 200 hectares planted and harvested; [[watermelon]] 350 hectares, planted and harvested; And ''[[Spondias tuberosa|umbu]]'', which is cultivated naturally, without human intervention, for being native, therefore, present in all extension of the municipality. Other low-productivity crops are: [[mango]], with 25 hectares planted and harvested, and coconut-bay with 20 hectares planted and harvested. These data are from the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) in 2014.<ref name= IBGE/>

In livestock, cattle with 45 105 heads and ''[[galiform]]'' with a total of 87,654 heads, 37,657 heads of [[chicken]]s. The goats totaled 29,654 heads; Sheep, 13 534 heads; 16 326 heads, according to IBGE data in 2014.<ref name=IBGE/> According to the (SEI), in 2006 the industry accounted for 52.31% of the municipality's GDP. This percentage is obtained as a function of mineral extractive activities by the companies Magnesita S.A., Xilolite S.A., Ibar Nordeste S.A. and Cimento Cimpor. Mining is the largest source of wealth in the municipality and generates employment for the majority of the population.<ref name=Cetem/>

Brumado has rich deposits of magnesite, mineral talc and other minerals such as [[vermiculite]], [[Dolomite (mineral)|dolomite]], rock [[crystal]] and granites of the most varied types. The mineral magnesite is the main product of the mining industry, and of all the mines of this open-air ore existing in the world, Brumado is the third largest. It also has the second largest mineral talc mine in Brazil.<ref name=Cetem/><ref name=Ufba0/>

===Secondary sector===

The [[Secondary sector of the economy|secondary]] sector is closely linked to the primary sector. Since there are few processing industries in the municipality, much of the primary sector's share is consequently integrated into the secondary sector because of mineral extraction - since the mining industries themselves are part of the two sectors - since they extract Ore and manufacture various products from its own extraction, along with other small industries, including cement and [[Metallurgy|metallurgical]] industries, contributing to this sector have great potential. However, mineral extraction potential is not added to 5.04% of the primary sector, but to the secondary sector; If this happened, the primary sector would have a higher percentage. The miners, in addition to extracting the primary resources, manufacture some products for direct consumption, such as medicinal talc, cosmetic talc and processed mineral talc for the manufacture of pulp and an infinity of products for consumption in agribusiness. Given that 52.31% of municipal GDP is mixed with extraction and transformation, it is assumed that this percentage is technically divided between the secondary sector and the primary sector.<ref name=Cetem/><ref name=Ufba0/>

===Tertiary sector===
[[File:Rua comercial em Brumado.jpeg|thumb|180px|right|{{center|Commercial street in Brumado.}}]]
The [[Tertiary sector of the economy|tertiary]] sector accounts for 46.24% of the municipality's GDP, the second largest sector contributing to the municipal economy.<ref name=Ufba0/> Trade plays an important role in the economic development of the city. Many of the trades are associated with the City Council of Shopkeepers (CDL). There are more than 1 800 registered companies in every municipality, of which 1 775 are active; Between industries, stores and service providers. These companies employed 12 677 people who, together with all the employed persons, obtained the value of 14 913, in active companies and with due registration (IBGE / data of 2012).<ref name= IBGE/><ref name=CPRM>{{cite web|url=http://www.cprm.gov.br/rehi/atlas/bahia/relatorios/BRUM028.pdf|title=''Cadastro de fontes de abastecimento por fontes subterrâneas''|access-date=December 6, 2016}}</ref>

Service companies that performed federal and state works beginning in 2011 employed large numbers of people, including from other municipalities. The largest employers of these companies were Grupo Andrade Gutierrez and VALEC, responsible for the construction of the West-East Integration Railway (FIOL).<ref name=Valec>{{cite web|url=http://www.valec.gov.br/noticias/Noticia260413_3|title=''Fiol emprega mão de obra local''|access-date=December 6, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161228185054/http://valec.gov.br/noticias/Noticia260413_3|archive-date=2016-12-28|url-status=dead}}</ref> Until 2013, these companies and their subcontractors played an important role in the local economy, in addition to the miners who invested in their facilities between 2011 and 2015, generating employment for the city's population and also for other neighboring cities.91 In the 2014 and 2015, some companies that provided services to the State had their works suspended several times, which caused financial insecurity for employees and for commerce.<ref name=Brumado_Agora4>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/tag/fiol|title=''Devido a falta de repasse da Valec, operários da Fiol são demitidos''|access-date=December 6, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224103941/http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/tag/fiol|archive-date=December 24, 2015|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref> Service delivery has a greater share absorbed by the mining industry, either by constant services, or by temporary services resulting from casual investments.

Civil construction has also contributed a lot to economic growth and since 2009, projects have been carried out and, although they slowed down in 2015, the city still sees works being carried out, with expectation of other civil construction projects to be executed. The northern part of the city is the one that has contributed the most to small works, due to the large number of houses built recently.<ref name=UNEB0/>

==Infrastructure==

===Cheers===

The city has only one public hospital, however, counting on several health posts, distributed among the neighborhoods. It has 23 units with coverage of the Unified Health System (SUS) that perform care from small to medium complexity. The good thing is that the city can be considered well served by clinics that perform the most diverse types of tests and specific treatments. In 2012, the municipality had a total of 122 health facilities, 2 of which were state, 34 were municipal and 77 were private.<ref name= IBGE/><ref name= SESAB>{{cite web|url=http://www1.saude.ba.gov.br/mapa_bahia/municipio.asp?CIDADE=290460|title=''Mapa da saúde''|access-date=December 7, 2016|archive-date=January 7, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170107171014/http://www1.saude.ba.gov.br/mapa_bahia/municipio.asp?CIDADE=290460|url-status=dead}}</ref>

;Sanitation

The sanitation in the city is satisfactory, but although it has a sewage network, the waste is directly thrown into the bed of the Antônio River, which cuts through the city. The Baiana Water and Sanitation Company (Embasa) does not treat the sewage network, and the city has long been channeling the galleries to the Do Atônio River.<ref name=Carina0/> In 2005, the construction of the Cristalândia dam in the district of the same name began in Rio de Contas after a marked drought between 1998 and 1999. The Brumadian population at that time had the water supply compromised in its totality, being supplied by tank truck. Before the construction of the Cristalândia dam, the city was supplied by the old dam of the Do Antônio River,<ref name=Embasa/> Which was analyzed belatedly and the water declared as unfit for human consumption, because it contains excess of minerals of various types.<ref name=Uesc>{{cite web|url=http://www.uesc.br/cursos/pos_graduacao/mestrado/mdrma/dissertacoes/allansalomao.rtf|title=Estudo da poluição inorgânica do Rio do Antônio e avaliação do risco à saúde pública na população do município de Brumado|access-date=December 7, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224000422/http://www.uesc.br/cursos/pos_graduacao/mestrado/mdrma/dissertacoes/allansalomao.rtf|archive-date=December 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Thanks to the attendance of the public power to the appeal of the population, finally the reservoir was built, and the municipality has not suffered any major problems with water supply.<ref name=Tribuna_da_Bahia>{{cite web|url=http://www.tribunadabahia.com.br/2012/06/07/seca-afeta-tambem-as-areas-urbanas|title=''Seca afeta também as áreas urbanas''|access-date=December 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303183656/http://www.tribunadabahia.com.br/2012/06/07/seca-afeta-tambem-as-areas-urbanas|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=O_Diário_da_Região>{{cite web|url=http://www.odiariodaregiao.com/governos-federal-e-estadual-discutem-novos-investimentos-no-semiarido/|title=Governo Federal e estadual discutem novos investimentos no semiárido|access-date=December 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170612013718/http://www.odiariodaregiao.com/governos-federal-e-estadual-discutem-novos-investimentos-no-semiarido/|archive-date=2017-06-12|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2012, the IBGE indicated that in 17 159 homes had treated water.<ref name= IBGE/> The selective collect is also done. To meet the requirements of Federal Law No. 11445, of January 5, 2007, regulated by Federal Decree 7217 of June 21, 2010 — Which obliges municipalities to create efficient means to eliminate garbage and other waste produced — It is discussed the elaboration of the new sanitation plan for the city.<ref name=Jornal_do_Sudoeste2>{{cite web|url=http://www.jornaldosudoeste.com/noticias/brumado/5508-brumado-vai-comecar-a-construir-o-plano-municipal-de-saneamento-basico-%20saneamento|title=''Brumado vai começar a construir o novo plano de saneamento''|access-date=December 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303195342/http://www.jornaldosudoeste.com/noticias/brumado/5508-brumado-vai-comecar-a-construir-o-plano-municipal-de-saneamento-basico-%20saneamento|archive-date=2016-03-03|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=Creaba>{{cite web|url=http://www.creaba.org.br/noticia/1762/Crea-BA-ajuda-a-elaborar-Planos-de-Saneamento-em-cidades-do-Sudoeste.aspx|title=''Crea ajuda a elaborar plano de saneamento em cidades do Sudoeste''|access-date=December 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224004346/http://www.creaba.org.br/noticia/1762/Crea-BA-ajuda-a-elaborar-Planos-de-Saneamento-em-cidades-do-Sudoeste.aspx|archive-date=December 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In the rural zone, sanitation follows what is common in these localities: Water is stored in artesian wells, small lakes. With the government's encouragement, through the Água para Todos Program (Water for All), cisterns are built on the floor for consumption, or boxes of fiber or [[polyethylene]] are distributed for the storage of water, for human consumption.<ref name=Folha_Regional3>{{cite web|url=http://folharegionalonline.com.br/brumado-recebe-40-cisternas-programa-agua-para-todos/|title=''Brumado recebe cisternas de polietileno''|access-date=December 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160327091842/http://folharegionalonline.com.br/brumado-recebe-40-cisternas-programa-agua-para-todos/|archive-date=2016-03-27|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name=CAR0>{{cite web|url=http://www.car.ba.gov.br/programa/agua-para-todos/|title=''Programa Água para Todos''|access-date=December 9, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161203095203/http://www.car.ba.gov.br/programa/agua-para-todos/|archive-date=2016-12-03|url-status=dead}}</ref>

;Full-time school

The Municipal Assistance Program for Learning (PMAA), which was financed only with municipal funds, began to be implemented between 2009 and 2011, had a high investment and won the Innovation Award in Educational Management of the National Institute of Studies and Research (INEP). Its objective was to implement full-time schools in the municipality. Approved by 80% of the parents of the students, this initiative, besides contributing to their intensive learning, also helps to keep them away from risks,  as contact with drug traffickers, for remaining the rest of the day unoccupied by the streets. Full-time classes have a nine-and-a-half hour period and reconcile morning and afternoon shifts. School reinforcement classes - mainly in mathematics — diversified activities and constant meetings between teachers were responsible for much of the success of the project. A high investment was made to improve the quality of the municipal education network. The number of classrooms doubled, and reform of other schools was carried out. In the enlargement of the network, also were constructed auditoriums, poliesportivas courts with dressing rooms, computer rooms and industrial kitchens. In addition, these schools have four meals a day. Social inclusion is also practiced in these schools, and students with different types of disabilities receive specialized treatment. Although the federal government's incentives are minimal and insufficient, the municipal government has so far sought partnerships and participation in federal education programs such as the National Education Program and the National Literacy Program in the Right Age.<ref name=Revista_Escola_Pública>{{cite web|url=http://revistaescolapublica.com.br/textos/35/educacao-integral-das-ruas-a-escola-300053-1.asp|title=''Das Ruas para a Escola''|access-date=December 11, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151224052725/http://revistaescolapublica.com.br/textos/35/educacao-integral-das-ruas-a-escola-300053-1.asp|archive-date=December 24, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Public security===

The public security in Brumado is in charge of the [[civil police|civil]] and [[military police]] of Bahia, besides the municipal guard. The civil police has a headquarters called the 20th Regional Police Coordination of the Interior (20th Coorpin), and the military police have the 34th Independent Military Police Company (34th CIPM).<ref name=Brumado_Agora8>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/noticias/19897-brumado-34-companhia-independente-de-policia-recebe-15-novos-policiais|title=''Companhia Independente de Polícia recebe 15 novos policiais''|access-date=December 14, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Brumado_Agora6>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/antigo/tag/coordenadoria-regional-de-policia-do-interior/|title=''Novo Coordenador da 20ª COORPIN visita Eduardo''|access-date=December 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161109221003/http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/antigo/tag/coordenadoria-regional-de-policia-do-interior/|archive-date=November 9, 2016|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref><ref name=Brumado_Notícias6>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/antigo/tag/guarda-municipal-de-brumado/|title=''Guarda Municipal será reestruturada''|access-date=December 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161110043320/http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/antigo/tag/guarda-municipal-de-brumado/|archive-date=2016-11-10|url-status=dead}}</ref> Although we do not find the percentage of crimes in the municipality, it is verified that in Brumado there are also big problems, such as the police deficit and the lack of financial and social incentives of the state government. This causes the number of armed robberies to increase significantly. The year of 2013 was the most violent of the last ten, registering 20 homicides.<ref name=Brumado_Agora7>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadoagora.com.br/noticias/11371-2014/11/11/2013-bahia-lidera-numero-de-assassinatos-no-pais;-brumado-registrou-20|title=''Bahia lidera número de assassinatos no país; Brumado registrou 20.''|access-date=December 14, 2016}}</ref><ref name=Brumado_Notícias8>{{cite web|url=http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/1488-2013/08/10/brumado-registra-pelo-menos-um-homicidio-a-cada-quinze-dias|title=''Brumado registra pelo menos um homicídio a cada quinze dias''|access-date=December 14, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161110043317/http://www.brumadonoticias.com.br/noticias/1488-2013/08/10/brumado-registra-pelo-menos-um-homicidio-a-cada-quinze-dias|archive-date=November 10, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Services and communications===

Human development services and public services, such as paving, construction of galleries for sanitary sewage, etc., are under the responsibility of the Municipal Department of Infrastructure.<ref name=Prefeitura_de_Brumado>{{cite web|url=http://brumado.ba.gov.br/stransparencia/Index/Index/0/10/MTQWmVZhW0Mzj|archive-url=https://archive.today/20161109142901/http://brumado.ba.gov.br/stransparencia/Index/Index/0/10/MTQWmVZhW0Mzj|url-status=dead|archive-date=November 9, 2016|title=''Sec. Mun. de Saúde''|access-date=December 14, 2016}}</ref> Essential services such as piped and treated water and electricity are provided by state-owned companies in Bahia. Embasa is responsible for the provision of sewage and water supply services, and the State Electricity Company of Bahia (Coelba) provides public services for the supply of electricity and maintenance of the electricity grid.<ref name=Coelba>{{cite web|url=http://www.amarelasinternet.com/br/brumado/energia_el%C3%89trica_coelba_grupo_neoenergia_luz/coelba_grupo_neoenergia.html|title=''Coelba, Grupo Neoenergia''|access-date=December 14, 2016}}</ref>

The Brumadian population enjoys satisfactory means of communication, as a sign of four cellular operators: [[Claro Telecom Participações S.A.|Claro]], [[Vivo (telecommunications)|Vivo]], [[TIM Brasil|TIM]] and [[Oi (telecommunications)|Oi]];<ref name=Listas.com>{{cite web|url=http://listtas.com/cobertura-celular/Sinal-da-Vivo-em_Brumado-BA_|title=''Cobertura celular. ==Culture and leisure==
===Tourism and events===

At least until 1998, carnival was one of the most traditional events. The story of this event dates back to 1949, when a group of friends decided to join and go out to streets to toy the revelry of [[Momo, Brazil|Momo]]. In 1951, the party started to be held in clubs, taking turns between one and the other.<ref name=BSI>{{cite web|url=http://agentediz.com.br/carnaval-festa-popular/|title=''O carnaval é definitivamente uma festa popular''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref> However, in 1998, the year of the great drought, [[carnival]] happens sporadically, and one of the city's most popular urban [[festivity]] is no longer as traditional as it once was. If we do a search on the carnival of Brumado, we will find more news about the cancellation of the event than the realization of it. Officials claim that the reasons are the lack of funds or the effects of drought.<ref name=G1.Globo>{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/bahia/verao/2015/noticia/2014/12/carnaval-de-brumado-e-cancelado-por-falta-de-verbas-diz-secretaria.html|title=''Carnaval de Brumado é cancelado por falta de verbas''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref>
The city has a certain tourist potential developed by the realization of traditional festivities, like St. John and St. Peter.<ref name=Terra_Notícias>{{cite web|url=http://noticias.terra.com.br/brasil/cidades/vc-reporter-festa-de-sao-joao-movimenta-rodoviarias-da-ba,b0d9eb5e3abda310VgnCLD200000bbcceb0aRCRD.html|title=''Festa de São João movimenta rodoviárias na Bahia''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref><ref name=UOL_Notícias>{{cite web|url=http://atarde.uol.com.br/cultura/noticias/1084561|title=''Brumado celebra São Pedro até domingo''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref> Has a well-structured hotel network.<ref name=O_Brumércio>{{cite web|url=http://www.obrumercio.com.br/guia_comercial.php?id=231|title=''O The Bruman culture is diverse and closely linked to the rural roots, which in a way, whether, want or not, has been suffering decadence. Religious festivals such as Saint John, Saint Peter Revelry of kings have been losing strength. Revelry of kings is also a very traditional party in the municipality, but has greater strength in the rural area. Saint Peter, the feast of Saint Sebastian, Bom Jesus and the feast of Our Lady Aparecida are very traditional religious feasts,<ref name=Festas>{{cite web|url=http://www.destaquebahia.com.br/noticias/aracatu/3655-festival-de-reis-encanta-os-aracatuenses-e-mostra-a-forca-de-uma-tradicao-que-ainda-permanece-viva|title=''Festa de Reis encanta os aracatuenses e mostra a força de uma tradição que ainda permanece viva''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref> but the party that has grown a lot is the cavalcade, which happens from north to south of the municipality, either to fraternize with friends and family, or to enjoy shows of bands and artists of sertaneja music.<ref name=Cavalgada>{{cite web|url=http://www.seagri.ba.gov.br/sites/default/files/calendario_cavalgada_2014.pdf|title=''Calendário de cavalgada 2014''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref><ref name=JOVEM>{{cite web|url=http://www.radiojovemhits.com.br/antigo/noticia/1686/dia-10-tem-a-cavalgada-da-amizade-em-brumado#|title=''Dia 10 tem cavalgada da amizade em Brumado''|access-date=December 15, 2016}}</ref>

===Sports===

The sports achievements in the city are mostly amateur level, such as ''Maratoninha'' (Child marathon), for children up to 12 years, which is held annually on October 12<ref name=Municípios_Baianos>{{cite web|url=http://www.municipiosbaianos.com.br/noticia01.asp?tp=1&nID=22056|title=''12ª Maratoninha será realizada no dia 12 de outubro''|access-date=January 3, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223235627/http://www.municipiosbaianos.com.br/noticia01.asp?tp=1&nID=22056|archive-date=December 23, 2015|url-status=dead|df=mdy-all}}</ref> And football tournaments in the neighborhoods and in the countryside. The most important sporting events in the city are the futsal championships and the Brumado Ecological Race to Rio de Contas. This race is one of the most important cycling competitions in Bahia and is played in several categories, with the participation of athletes from all over the country. The competition is interstate and is recognized by the Federation of Brazilian Cycling and the Brazilian Cycling Confederation, and the score serves to define the national ranking.<ref name=BDON>{{cite web|url=http://www.corridaecologica.com.br/16-corrida-ecologica-brumado-a-rio-de-contas-marcou-a-historia-da-competicao|title=''16ª Corrida Ecológica Brumado a Rio de Contas marcou a história da competição''|access-date=January 3, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303171602/http://www.corridaecologica.com.br/16-corrida-ecologica-brumado-a-rio-de-contas-marcou-a-historia-da-competicao|archive-date=March 3, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Brunadian futsal is one of the main sports practiced in the city, a sport in which Brumado futsal is four times Bahian champion.<ref name=JA>{{cite web|url=http://www.jornalalerta.com.br/2012/12/brumado-e-tetracampeao-baiano-de-futsal/|title=''Brumado é tetracampeão baiano de futsal''|access-date= January 3, 2017}}</ref> Another common sporting event is the capoeira wheels. The athletes are baptized, and graduates, receiving colorful ropes, tied at the waist. These strings correspond to your graduation.<ref name=PTBDO>{{cite web|url=http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/batizado-de-capoeira-e-show-folclorico-do-grupo-topazio-serao-neste-fim-de-semana|archive-url=https://archive.today/20151223193701/http://www.portalbrumado.com.br/batizado-de-capoeira-e-show-folclorico-do-grupo-topazio-serao-neste-fim-de-semana|url-status=dead|archive-date=December 23, 2015|title=''Batizado de capoeira do Grupo Topázio será realizado neste fim de semana''|access-date=January 3, 2017}}</ref>

==Holidays==

There are 15 national holidays in the city, including ten national, three municipal and one state, and one optional point. National holidays are: January 1 (International confraternity), [[Passion of Christ]], April 21 ([[Tiradentes]]), May 1 (World Labor Day), [[Corpus Christi (feast)|Corpus Christi]], September 7 ([[Independence of Brazil]]), October 12 (Day of Our Lady of Aparecida), November 2 (Day of the Dead), November 15 (Proclamation of the Republic) and December 25 ([[Christmas]]).<ref name=Portal_Brasil>{{cite web|url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/governo/2016/01/divulgado-calendario-de-feriados-nacionais-de-2016|title=''Divulgado calendário de feriados nacionais de 2016''|access-date=January 3, 2017}}</ref> The municipalities holidays are: June 11 (emancipation of the municipality), January 20 (Saint Sebastian's Day, patron of the city) and August 6 (Bom Jesus Day, patron saint of the city).<ref name=PMBDOBA>{{cite web|url=http://www.feriadosmunicipais.com.br/bahia/brumado/|title=''Feriados municipais''|access-date=January 3, 2017}}</ref> State holiday, only one: July 2 (Independence of Bahia).<ref name=Velma>{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/bahia/independencia-da-bahia/noticia/2015/07/historiador-conta-curiosidades-sobre-luta-pela-independencia-na-bahia.html|title=''Historiador conta curiosidades sobre a luta pela Independência da Bahia''|access-date= January 3, 2017}}</ref> Optional point: Ash Wednesday (until 2:00&nbsp;p.m.).<ref name=Portal_Brasil/>
