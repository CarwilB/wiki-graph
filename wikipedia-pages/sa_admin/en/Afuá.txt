'''Afuá''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 39,567 people. The area of the municipality is 8,372.772&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Furos de Breves''.

==Geography==
The town of Afuá is situated on an internal river bay on [[Marajó]], where various rivers that flow on this island come together:<ref name="lisboa">{{cite book | last = Lisboa | first = P.L.B. | title = A Terra dos Aruã: Uma história ecológica do arquipelágo do Marajó | year = 2012 | publisher = Museu Paraense Emílio Goeldi | location = Belém}}</ref> 
*[[Afuá River]]
*[[Marajozinho River]]
*[[Cajuúna River]]
*The Santana Channel (''Canal do Santana'') of the [[Cajari River (Marajó)|Cajari River]]
*[[Furo Piraiauara]]
The town is heavily influenced by the tides of these rivers.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Lomba |first1=R.M. |last2=Nobre Jr. |first2=B.B. |date=2013 |title=A relação rural-urbano a partir das cidades ribeirinhas: O papel do comércio popular (feiras) na cidade de Afuá (PA) |url=https://journals.openedition.org/confins/8405 |journal=Confins. Revue franco-brésilienne de géographie |volume=28 |issue= |publisher= |pages= |doi= |access-date=}}</ref> Most of them flow into the [[Vieira Grande Bay]], which connects to the [[Amazon River]]. In the rainy season the waters in the bay rise, causing the water flow in these rivers to change direction, which leads to large-scale flooding of the town.<ref>{{cite thesis |last=Miranda |first=D.S.M. |date=2014 |title=Palafitas Digitais: comunicação, convergência cultural e relações de poder em Afuá |url=https://repositorio.ufpa.br/handle/2011/6212 |work= |degree= |location= |publisher=Federal University of Pará |access-date=}}</ref> 

Because of this, most houses are built on [[stilt house|stilt]]s (called ''palafitas'' in Portuguese)<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Rocha |first1=M.C.S. |last2=Medeiros |first2=V.A.S. |date=2019 |title=Configuração e urbanidade em assentamentos da Amazônia: as lições de Afuá (Pará, Brasil)  |url=https://revistademorfologiaurbana.org/index.php/rmu/article/view/71/36 |journal=Revista de Morfologia Urbana  |volume=7 |issue=1 |publisher= |pages= |doi= |access-date=}}</ref> and traffic is mainly by boat. The town has been off-limits to cars sinse 2002, earning Afuá the epithet "The Venice of Marajó" (''Veneza Marajoara'').<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Dias |first1=M.B. |last2=Silva |first2=M.J.B. |date=2011 |title=Afuá: Veneza marajoara, Pará-Brasil |url=https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/4517/451744820407.pdf |journal=Revista Geográfica de América Central |volume=Número Especial EGAL |issue= |publisher= |pages=1–8 |doi= |access-date=}}</ref> From outside the town is only accessible by boat, though there is an airstrip that is often underwater. The town's mayor has described it as a model for net-zero emissions.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Magolis |first=Mac |date=12 May 2024 |title=A car-free town in the Amazon serves lessons for pedaling to net zero emissions |url=https://www.npr.org/2024/05/12/1248537199/climate-net-zero-amazon-rainforest-brazil-cycling |work=NPR}}</ref>

The various rivers form several islands within the larger island Marajó, such as Afuá Island, Cajuúna Island and Piraiauara Island.<ref name="lisboa"/> Further out, a number of islands in the Vieira Grande Bay are part of the municipality, the largest of which is [[Serraria Island, Pará|Serraria Island]].

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|sqmi|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date= |title=Área de Proteção Ambiental Arquipélago do Marajó |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/arp/1362 |website=Unidades de Conservaçăo no Brasil |location=São Paulo |publisher=Instituto Socioambiental |access-date=}}</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|65181|ha|adj=on}} [[Charapucu State Park]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 2010.<ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date= |title=Parque Estadual Charapucu |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/arp/5130 |website=Unidades de Conservaçăo no Brasil |location=São Paulo |publisher=Instituto Socioambiental |access-date=}}</ref>
