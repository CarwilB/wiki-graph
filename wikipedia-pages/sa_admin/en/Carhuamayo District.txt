'''Carhuamayo''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Qarwa Mayu''' or '''Qarwamayu''': ''qarwa'' yellowish, ''mayu'' river, "yellowish river") is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Junín Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is Yana Hirka at approximately {{convert|4600|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Junín Province (JunínRegion) 2017</ref>

* Puka Shalla
* Rinri Uchku
* Runtunniyuq
* Sankhayuq
* Wanin Punta
