'''Urumita''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]].

==Geography==

The municipality of Urumita is located on the [[Serranía del Perijá]] mountain range with part of its territory covering the valley of the [[Cesar River]] towards the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] mountain range. The municipality seat of Urumita is however located on a flat area some 45&nbsp;km away from the capital of the [[Department of Cesar]], [[Valledupar]] and some 175&nbsp;km from the capital city of the Department of La Guajira, [[Riohacha]].<ref name="urumita-guajira.gov.co">[http://urumita-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Urumita: geography]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

Urumita limits to the north with the municipality of [[Villanueva, La Guajira|Villanueva]] in a 36&nbsp;km borderline; to the south with the municipality of [[La Jagua del Pilar]] sharing approximately 50&nbsp;km; to the east with [[Venezuela]], sharing 10&nbsp;km; and to the west with the municipality of Valledupar sharing 15&nbsp;km covering a total area of 329&nbsp;km².<ref name="urumita-guajira.gov.co"/>

===Climate===
Climate in the municipality of Urumita is very warm to hot with an average temperature throughout the year of {{convert|28|°C|1|disp=or}} varying only slightly over the year.<ref>[http://urumita-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Urumita: climate]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> There is a lengthy rainy season from April to November, with a slightly drier period mid-year, and a short, very [[dry season]] from mid-December to March. Under the [[Köppen climate classification]], Urumita has a [[tropical savanna climate]], labelled ''Aw'' on climate maps.

==History==

Prior to the arrival of the Spanish colonizers, the area of Urumita was inhabited by indigenous peoples pertaining to the [[Chimila people|Chimila]]. At the time of the Spanish arrival in the early 16th Century, the area was governed by a ''[[cacique]]'' named Uruma. After a period of conquest and colonization by the Spanish, on October 3, 1785 Juan Bautista Canalete founded the village of Urumita. The village became part of the jurisdiction of the municipality of [[Villanueva, La Guajira|Villanueva]] within the Government of Santa Marta. It was later made part of the [[Province of Padilla]].<ref name="ReferenceA">[http://urumita-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Urumita: History]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

In 1972 Urumita became an autonomous corregimiento by Ordinance 043 of 1971 until 1978 when the Urumita was segregated from the municipality of Villanueva and became a relative status by Ordinance 016 of the same year. In 1982 the previous ordinance of 1978 is reconfirmed by ordinance 036 of 1982 rectifying the limits with Villanueva.<ref name="ReferenceA"/>

==Culture==

The region has a large presence of [[Calaguala]]s, a plant considered medicinal. It is used in various ways by the population of Urumita, and it is part of its popular culture. The locals celebrate the [[Festival y Reinado de las Flores y la Calaguala]] (Festival and Pageant of the Flowers and the Calaguala) in honor of the plant.<ref>[http://urumita-guajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Urumita: Culture]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}

It is also featured in Gabriel Garcia Marquez's classic novel ''One Hundred Years of Solitude.''</ref>
