'''Aija District''' is one of 5 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in [[Aija Province]], [[Ancash Region]], [[Peru]]. Its population was 2017 as of the [[2017 Peru Census|2017 census]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados Definitivos de los Censos Nacionales 2017: Ancash |url=https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1552/ |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] |access-date=January 12, 2023 |page=I: 122 |language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Geography==
The District of Aija is located on the western slopes of the [[Cordillera Negra]]. It borders on [[Recuay Province]] to the south and east, on [[Coris District]], [[Huacllan District]] and [[Succha District]] to the west, and on [[La Merced District, Aija|La Merced District]] to the north.
