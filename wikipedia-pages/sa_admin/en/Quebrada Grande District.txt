'''Quebrada Grande''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Tilarán (canton)|Tilarán]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019 |archive-date=24 February 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240224063957/https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

==Toponymy==
The translation to English of {{lang|es|Quebrada Grande}} is Big Creek, a self explanatory name of a riverine feature in the area.

==History== 
On 11 June 2020, the [[Cabeceras District, Tilarán|Cabeceras]] district was segregated from Quebrada Grande, by allocating {{convert|51.25|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} from the originally {{convert|85.24|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} of Quebrada Grande.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Marín |first1=Claudia |title=Aprueban creación del distrito octavo del cantón de Tilarán |url=https://www.elmundo.cr/costa-rica/aprueban-creacion-del-distrito-octavo-del-canton-de-tilaran/ |access-date=12 June 2020 |work=elmundo.cr |date=11 June 2020}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Quebrada Grande has an area of {{formatnum:33.72}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:725}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
