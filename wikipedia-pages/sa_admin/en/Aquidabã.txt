'''Aquidabã''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|akidaˈbɐ̃|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 21,681 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|357|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/aquidaba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
