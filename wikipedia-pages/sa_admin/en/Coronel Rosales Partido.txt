'''Coronel Rosales Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the southern coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 61,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1312|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Punta Alta]], which is around {{convert|650|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Attractions==
The coastal village of [[Pehuen Có]] is popular with tourists from [[Provincia de Buenos Aires]] and it benefits from a beautiful beach, woodlands, a shipwreck, and some interesting fossils.{{cn|date=February 2024}}

==Sport==
The city of Punta Alta is home to [[Sporting Punta Alta]], a football club that play in the regionalised [[Torneo Argentino B|4th Division]] of Argentine football.
