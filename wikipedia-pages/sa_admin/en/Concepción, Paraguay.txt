'''Concepción''' ({{IPA|es|konseβˈsjon}}) is a city and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in northern [[Paraguay]] and capital of the [[Concepción department, Paraguay|Concepción Department]]. It is situated next to the [[Paraguay River]].

Founded in 1773 by [[Governorate of Paraguay|Governor]] Agustín Fernando de Pinedo, the town prospered in the early years of the 20th century as a river port and a center for the north of the country, exploiting the new wealth of the [[Gran Chaco]]. The town also played an important role in the [[Paraguayan Civil War (1947)|Paraguayan Civil War of 1947]]. The city is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Concepción en Paraguay]].

==Transportation==
[[Route 5 (Paraguay)|National Route 5]] (in Spanish, Ruta Nacional Número 5 "Gral. Bernardino Caballero") connects the city with [[Pedro Juan Caballero, Paraguay|Pedro Juan Caballero]] and [[Asunción]].

==Climate==
[[File:Concepción Catholic chapel.jpeg|thumb|left|Main Catholic chapel in Concepción]]
Concepción is located in very close proximity to the [[Tropic of Capricorn]], with the latitudinal line lying just south of the city. The city has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with abundant annual precipitation, although possessing a highly prominent summer peak. Summers are very hot, rainy, and often oppressively humid. Severe thunderstorms are very common in the summer and have the capability to dump copious amounts of rainfall in a short period of time, along with destructively high winds and occasional hail. Winter is much drier and considerably cooler, with near drought-like conditions often prevailing.
