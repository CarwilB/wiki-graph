The '''Nueve de Julio Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Nueve de Julio''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the northwestern corner of the province, limiting with the [[Vera Department]] in the east, and with the [[San Cristóbal Department]] in the south. To the north it limits with the province of [[Chaco Province|Chaco]], and to the west with the province of [[Santiago del Estero Province|Santiago del Estero]].

The department is the second largest in the province, but it has only about 28,000 inhabitants. The head town is [[Tostado, Santa Fe|Tostado]] (population 14,000). Other cities and towns are [[Esteban Rams]], [[Gato Colorado]], [[Gregoria Pérez de Denis]], [[Juan de Garay, Santa Fe|Juan de Garay]], [[Logroño, Argentina|Logroño]], [[Montefiore, Argentina|Montefiore]], [[Pozo Borrado]], [[San Bernardo, Nueve de Julio|San Bernardo]], [[Santa Margarita, Argentina|Santa Margarita]], and [[Villa Minetti]].

The name of this department corresponds to the date of the [[Argentine Declaration of Independence]] (9 July 1816) and is often spelled with the actual figure 9 (''Departamento 9 de Julio'').
