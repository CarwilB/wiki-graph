''' La Unión de Isidoro Montes de Oca ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]] and is the westernmost municipality in Guerrero. The municipal seat lies at [[La Unión, Guerrero|La Unión]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|1,142|km²|sqmi}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 25,230.<ref>{{cite web|title=La Unión de Isidoro Montes de Oca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12068a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=23 December 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601185328/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12068a.htm|archive-date=1 June 2007}}</ref>

The main towns are [[La Unión, Guerrero|La Unión]], population 3,079; {{ill|Petacalco|es}}, population 2,671; {{ill|Zacatula, Guerrero|lt=Zacatula|es|Zacatula}}, population 1,417; {{ill|Lagunillas, Guerrero|lt=Lagunillas|es|Lagunillas (La Unión)}}, population 1,371; {{ill|El Naranjito|es|El Naranjito (Guerrero)}}, population 1,167; and Surcua, population 1,046.<ref>[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]]. Principales resultados por localidad 2005 (ITER). Retrieved on 23 December 2008</ref>

==History==

On 26 October 1614, the [[Dutch people| Dutch]] Captain [[Joris van Spilbergen]] – involved in [[Eighty Years War|his country's war with Spain]] – raided with an expedition of five ships this part of the coast of the then [[Spanish Mexico]]. At Zacatula he succeeded in capturing the pearl fishing ship ''San Francisco''.<ref name=Quanchi>Quanchi, ''Historical Dictionary of the Discovery and Exploration of the Pacific Islands'', page 233</ref><ref name=Myers>[https://books.google.com/books?id=-chO8R-3PYC&dq=joris+van+spilbergen&pg=PA459 Myers, Paul A., ''North to California'', Llumina Press, 2004] {{ISBN|9781595262516}}</ref>

On 20 July 1993, the municipality of La Unión acquired its current name in commemoration of locally born [[Mexican War of Independence|Independence War]] hero [[Isidoro Montes de Oca]].<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Guerrero. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url= http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/gro/GUERRERO.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1491-3 |page=193 |access-date=4 September 2025 |archive-date=5 November 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231105052243/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/gro/GUERRERO.pdf |url-status=live }}</ref>
