'''Simiti''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

== History ==
Simití was founded in 1537 by [[Antonio de Lebrija (conquistador)|Antonio de Lebrija]] and [[Juan Maldonado (conquistador)|Juan Maldonado]], two soldiers of the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|expedition]] from [[Santa Marta]] to the [[Muisca Confederation]], led by [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.banrepcultural.org/blaavirtual/revistas/credencial/sept2001/ciudades.htm Fundaciones de ciudades y poblaciones] - [[Banco de la República]]</ref>
