'''Trindade''' is a municipality in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], was 32,257 inhabitants and had a total area of 295.765&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Trindade''' are based in non metallic ([[gypsum]]) industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially rearing of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pigs]], [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[manioc]]. Trindade is located in the micro region of [[Araripina]], which contains 95% of the Brazilian reserves of gypsum.
