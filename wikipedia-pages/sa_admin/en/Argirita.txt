'''Argirita''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 2,704 people living in a total area of {{cvt|159.33|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/argirita/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Cataguases.
