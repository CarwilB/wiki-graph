'''San Juan Quiahije''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. It is part of the [[Juquila District]] in the centre of the [[Costa Region]]. The origin of the name {{lang|ctp|Quiahije}} is not known, some people conjecture it might mean "Stone Forest" in the [[Zapotec language]].<ref name=INF>{{cite web|title=San Juan Quiahije|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20213a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 91.86&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 1,960 metres above sea level. The climate is temperate humid with an average temperature of 16.4°C and annual rainfall of 847&nbsp;mm. The forests contain pines and oaks. Wild fauna include deer, rabbits. iguanas, armadillos, squirrels, badgers, raccoons, wild boars, skunks, wildcats, foxes and coyotes.<ref name=INF/>
==Population==
As of 2005, the municipality had 593 households with a total population of 4,154 of whom 3,517 people spoke the Chatino language or ChaqF tnyaJ.<ref>[[Tomas Cruz Lorenzo]]</ref> [[Tomas Cruz Lorenzo]] was one of the notable leaders in this community. He was killed in Santa Catarina Juquila on September 26, 1989. His assassination is unsolved. Economic activities include farming, animal husbandry and trade.<ref name=INF/> Quiahije is one of the centers of the [[Chatinos|Chatino]] people or ntenB chaqF tnyaJ, related to the [[Zapotec peoples|Zapotec]] but with a distinct language.<ref>{{cite web
