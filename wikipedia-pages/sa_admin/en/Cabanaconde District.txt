'''Cabanaconde''' or '''Qhawana Kunti''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]]) is one of twenty [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the  [[Caylloma Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
One of the highest mountains of the district is [[Hualca Hualca]] at {{convert|6025|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL maps of the Caylloma Province 1, Arequipa Region</ref>

* Chuwa Qucha
* Kuntur
* Kuntur Sinqa
* Lipayuq
* Luk'i Urqu
* Pukarilla
* Puru Pakisqa
* Phuyphu
* Wanqarani
* Yaritayuq
