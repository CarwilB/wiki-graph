'''San Felipe de Jesús''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in northwestern [[Mexico]], being the smallest municipality in Sonora.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=January 4, 2010}}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. The municipal seat is at [[San Felipe de Jesús]].

The municipal area is 152.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, with a population of 416 registered in 2000. [http://www.inegi.gob.mx/inegi/default.aspx]

Neighboring municipalities are  [[Banámichi Municipality|Banámichi]], [[Aconchi Municipality|Aconchi]], [[Opodepe Municipality|Opodepe]], and [[Huépac Municipality|Huépac]].
