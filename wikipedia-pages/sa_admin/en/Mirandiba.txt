'''Mirandiba''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 14,605 inhabitants and the total area is 821.676&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

The municipality was designated a priority area for conservation and sustainable use when the [[Caatinga Ecological Corridor]] was created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|last=Mattos|first=Marluza|title=Ministério cria Corredor Ecológico da Caatinga|year=2006|publisher=Ministério do Meio Ambiente|url=http://www.mma.gov.br/informma/item/3274-ministerio-cria-corredor-ecologico-da-caatinga|access-date=2016-11-01|archive-date=2019-02-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190220002916/http://www.mma.gov.br/informma/item/3274-ministerio-cria-corredor-ecologico-da-caatinga|url-status=live}}</ref>

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[São José do Belmonte]] ('''N''');  [[Carnaubeira da Penha]] ('''S''');  [[Serra Talhada]] ('''E'''); [[Salgueiro]] and [[Verdejante]] ('''W''').
* Area - 809.26&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 450 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot

* Distance to [[Recife]] - 470&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Mirandiba''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pigs]]s, [[cattle]], [[chicken]]s;  and plantations of [[tomato]]es and  [[bean]]s.
