'''Tocancipá''' ({{IPA|es|tokansiˈpa}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Central Savanna Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Tocancipá is situated in the northern part of the [[Bogotá savanna]], part of the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2605|m|ft}}. The capital [[Bogotá]], which [[Metropolitan Area of Bogotá|metropolitan area]] includes Tocancipá, is {{convert|42|km|mi}} to the south. Tocancipá borders [[Gachancipá]] in the north, [[Guasca]] and [[Sopó]] in the south, [[Guatavita]] in the east and [[Zipaquirá]] in the west.<ref name=websiteTocancipa>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tocancipa-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tocancipá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Tocancipá comes from [[Chibcha language|Muysccubun]] and means "Valley of the joys of the ''zipa''".<ref name=websiteTocancipa/>

== History ==
The area of Tocancipá was inhabited early in the [[Template:Timeline of inhabitation of the Altiplano Cundiboyacense|history of inhabitation]] of the Altiplano. The [[List of Muisca and pre-Muisca sites|archaeological site]] [[Tibitó]] is located within the boundaries of Tocancipá and evidence of inhabitation has been dated to 11,740 ± 110 years [[Before Present|BP]].<ref>Aceituno & Rojas, 2012, p.127</ref> At the time of arrival of the Spanish [[conquistador]]es in 1537, Tocancipá was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a loose confederation of different [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The ''[[zipa]]'' of [[Bogotá|Bacatá]] ruled over Tocancipá.

Modern Tocancipá was founded on September 21, 1593 by Miguel de Ibarra.<ref name=websiteTocancipa/>

== Tourism ==
[[Jaime Duque Park]], a family-oriented amusement park, is located in Tocancipá. The town also hosts the [[Autódromo de Tocancipá]], a race track where vintage and GT races are held.
