'''Isla de Maipo''' is a town and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Talagante Province]] in central [[Chile]]'s [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Geography==
Isla de Maipo can be found in the [[Chilean Central Valley]] approximately {{convert|40|km|abbr=on}} southwest of the metropolitan area of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]] and about {{convert|12|km|0|abbr=on}} from the city of [[Talagante]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|189|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> In general, the commune is characterized as predominantly rural, which is reflected in its landscape of colonial houses, cultivated farms, vineyards, natural sites and the predominance of agricultural activities, which is carried out by the majority of its population.<ref name="Municipality">{{Cite web |url= http://www.islademaipo.cl/ |title= Municipality of Isla de Maipo |access-date= 18 July 2010 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20070813234731/http://www.islademaipo.cl/ |archive-date= 13 August 2007 |url-status= dead }}</ref> The Maipo River is the most important in the whole metropolitan area, because it supports the highest population density in Chile and is also the main stream which irrigates the area.<ref name="Municipality"/>

==Traditions==
{{Unsourced|section|date=July 2023}}
One of its traditions is the ''Fiesta de La Virgen de La Merced''. This feast is celebrated the last Sunday of September each year and attracts more than 80,000 pilgrims from all parts of the region, across the country and abroad. 

''La Fiesta de la Vendimia'' (a grape harvest feast) is celebrated every year, calling together local inhabitants and foreign tourists. The festival allows tourists to familiarize themselves with the culinary art of the commune, especially the high quality of its wines. The ''Plaza de Armas'' is converted into a large dining room, where one can hear folk and other varieties of music.

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Isla de Maipo had 36,219 inhabitants (18,051 men and 18,168 women). Of these, 26,941 (74.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,278 (25.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 40.39% (10,421 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 censuses; during this period, the female population surpassed the male population, going from 49% to 50.2%.<ref name= "INE"/>
