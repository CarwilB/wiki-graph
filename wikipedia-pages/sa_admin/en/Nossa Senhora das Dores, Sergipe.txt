'''Nossa Senhora das Dores''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈnɔsɐ sẽˈɲɔɾɐ daz ˈdoɾis|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 26,795 (2020) and its area is 471 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/nossa-senhora-das-dores/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It is named after '[[Our Lady of Sorrows|Nossa Senhora das Dores]]', which translates to 'Our lady of Sorrows', referring to [[Mary, mother of Jesus]].
