'''El Tambo''' is one of seven cantons located in [[Cañar Province]], [[Ecuador]].  It is situated along the [[Ecuador Highway 35|PanAmerican Highway]] in a valley across from the larger town of [[Cañar, Ecuador|Cañar]].  The canton is fully surrounded by [[Cañar Canton]]. The population of El Tambo is a mix of mestizo and indigenous Cañari. The seat of the canton is the town of [[El Tambo, Ecuador|El Tambo]].

The canton was established on 24 January 1991.<ref>{{cite news |title=El Tambo celebra sus 24 años con desfiles y feria artesanal |url=https://www.eluniverso.com/vida-estilo/2015/01/24/nota/4471106/tambo-celebra-sus-24-anos-desfiles-feria-artesanal/ |work=El Universo |date=24 January 2015 |language=es}}</ref> Its name comes from the Kichwa “tambu” which means “place of rest or inn”.

The majority of the population of El Tambo is a mixture of indigenous and mestizo people who are dedicated to agricultural activities, thus taking advantage of the fertility of its soil. El Tambo is distinguished by its cultural and historical wealth. About 30 minutes out of El Tambo is the town of [[Ingapirca]] named after the [[Inca Empire|Incan]] ruins that are there.  The ruins of Ingapirca are the largest Incan ruins in Ecuador.
