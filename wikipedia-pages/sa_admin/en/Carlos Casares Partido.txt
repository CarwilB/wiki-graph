'''Carlos Casares Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 21,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2520|sqkm|abbr=on}}. Its capital city is [[Carlos Casares, Buenos Aires|Carlos Casares]], which is around {{convert|257|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
