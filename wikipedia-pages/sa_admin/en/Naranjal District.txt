'''Naranjal''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], [[Paraguay]]. Spread over an area of {{cvt|627.4|km2}}, it had a population of 6,608 inhabitants as per the 2022 census. It was officially founded on 26 July 1990. It is known for its "Fiesta de la Costilla" (rib festival) celebrated in May annually.

== History ==
Naranje district was carved out of the districts of [[Domingo Martínez de Irala, Paraguay|Domingo Martínez de Irala]] and [[Ñacunday]] on 26 July 1990. It was also declared as a municipality on the same day, and the first mayor was Don Faustino Leiva Azuaga.<ref name="Mun">{{cite web|url=https://www.municipalidadnaranjal.gov.py/la-ciudad-de-naranjal/|title=Ciudad de Naranjal|work=Government of Paraguay|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es|trans-title=City of Naranje}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Naranjal is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the southwestern part of the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]].<ref name="Mun"/><ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1014__naranjal/|title=Naranjal|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|363|km}} from the national capital of [[Asuncion]].<ref name="Mun"/>

== Demographics and culture ==
As per the 2022 census, Naranje had a population of 6,608 inhabitants of which 3,409 were males and 3,199 were females. About 58.2% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (41.8%) lived in urban areas. About 25.7% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.6% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/> The population is multi-ethnic, with significant proportion formed by immigrants from Brazil.<ref name="Mun"/><ref name="LN">{{cite web|url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios_edicion_impresa/2025/11/01/naranjal-la-capital-nacional-de-la-costilla-que-impulsa-el-desarrollo/|title=Naranjal: la Capital Nacional de la Costilla que impulsa el desarrollo|work=[[La Nación (Paraguay)|La Nación]]|date=1 November 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es|trans-title=Naranje: the National Capital of the Rib that drives development}}</ref>

Due to its significant population of Brazilian immigrants, there is Brazilian influence on the local culture and language. The town is known for its ''Fiesta de la Costilla'' (rib festival) held in July annually. During the festival, nearly {{cvt|4,500|kg}} are ribs are cooked for over 12 hours, and thousands of people gather to eat them.<ref name="Mun"/><ref name="LN"/>

== Economy==
The primary economic activity is [[agriculture]], with majority of the people employed in small farms. Major agriculture produce include [[soyabean]], [[wheat]], and [[maize]].<ref name="Mun"/><ref name="LN"/>
