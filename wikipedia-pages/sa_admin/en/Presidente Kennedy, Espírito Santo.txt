'''Presidente Kennedy''' is a coastal [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southernmost portion [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. It covers {{convert|583.9|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 11,658 with a population density of 19 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/> It is located at 55 meters above sea level. It was named after U.S. president [[John F. Kennedy]], in a tribute to his persona. The city received donations from [[Alliance for Progress]] plan.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.presidentekennedy.es.gov.br/new/historia |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Presidente Kennedy |accessdate=2012-04-19 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20120805215550/http://www.presidentekennedy.es.gov.br/new/historia |archivedate=2012-08-05 }}</ref>

In recent years, an [[oil well]] was discovered in the ocean just in front of the city who was awarded with a heat economy. The oil is under the [[pre-salt layer]]. Companies such as the Chinese [[Baosteel]]<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.seculodiario.com/exibir_not.asp?id=4274 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110716033148/http://www.seculodiario.com/exibir_not.asp?id=4274 |archive-date=2011-07-16 |title=SeculoDiario.com - Espírito Santo mais forte na internet}}</ref>{{failed verification|date=October 2016}} operate in the city, and a new [[seaport]] is under construction.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.presidentekennedy.es.gov.br/new/artigo-port_central_kennedy10042012 |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Presidente Kennedy |accessdate=2012-04-19 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120427013047/http://www.presidentekennedy.es.gov.br/new/artigo-port_central_kennedy10042012 |archivedate=2012-04-27 }}</ref>
