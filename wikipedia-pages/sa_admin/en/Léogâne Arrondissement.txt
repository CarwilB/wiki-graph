'''Léogâne''' ({{langx|ht|Leyogàn}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest Department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 509,280 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015 |date=March 2015 |website=IHSI |language=fr |trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |archive-date=6 November 2015 |url-status=dead |access-date=8 May 2017 }}</ref> Postal codes in the Léogâne Arrondissement start with the number 62.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Léogâne]]
* [[Petit-Goâve]]
* [[Grand-Goâve]]

==History==

===2010 7.0 earthquake===
{{further|2010 Haiti earthquake}}

On 12 January 2010, a [[2010 Haiti earthquake|7.0 magnitude earthquake struck]] the arrondissement. The city of [[Léogâne]] was estimated to be 80–90% destroyed.<ref>ABC News, [http://www.abc.net.au/news/stories/2010/01/17/2794043.htm?section=justin 'Haiti Disaster Like "No Other"']{{dead link|date=April 2025|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}, ''AFP'', Lisa Millar, 17 January 2010 (accessed 17 January 2010)</ref> [[Petit Goâve]] was greatly affected by the quake.<ref>CNN, [http://www.cnn.com/2010/WORLD/americas/01/15/earthquake.damage.elsewhere/index.html?hpt=T2 "Outside Port-au-Prince, 'towns are absolutely destroyed'"], ''Khadijah Rentas'', 15 January 2010 (accessed 17 January 2010)</ref> All public buildings in [[Grand-Goâve]] were destroyed.<ref>{{in lang|fr}} La Presse (Montreal), [http://www.cyberpresse.ca/international/amerique-latine/seisme-en-haiti/201001/18/01-940162-deux-quebecoises-sont-mortes-a-grand-goave.php "Deux Québécoises sont mortes à Grand-Goâve"], Émilie Côté, 18 January 2010 (accessed 21 January 2010)</ref>
