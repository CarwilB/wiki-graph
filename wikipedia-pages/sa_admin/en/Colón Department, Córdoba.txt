'''Colón Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of the [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Province of Córdoba]] in [[Argentina]]<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lmdiario.com.ar/contenido/395211/el-domingo-25-de-junio-seran-las-elecciones-provinciales-en-cordoba|title=El domingo 25 de junio serán las elecciones provinciales en Córdoba|work=La Nueva Mañana|date=20 March 2023|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>, which spreads from the Sierras Chicas on the western end, into the [[Pampas]] plains towards the east. 

The provincial subdivision had a population of about 295,725 inhabitants in 2022<ref>{{Cite web |title=Wayback Machine |url=https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c1_6.xlsx |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20231121231455/https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c1_6.xlsx |archive-date=2023-11-21 |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=censo.gob.ar}}</ref>, with an area of {{convert|2568.2|sqkm|sqmi|0|sp=us}}<ref>{{Cite web |title=Wayback Machine |url=https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c2_6.xlsx |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20231121231410/https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_cordoba_est_c2_6.xlsx |archive-date=2023-11-21 |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=censo.gob.ar}}</ref>, and its capital city is [[Jesús María, Córdoba|Jesús María]], which is located {{convert|760|mi|km|0|sp=us}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

== Economy ==
Agriculture and tourism are two of the main branches of the local economy.

=== Agriculture ===
[[Soybean|Soy]] and [[Maize|corn]] are the two main crops grown in the department, in the 2017/2018 season they represented 57% (64265 [[Hectare|hectares]]) and 37.5% (42347 ha) of the total crop-growing surface of the department.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Visor de Mapas |url=https://mapascordoba.gob.ar/viewer/mapa/24 |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=mapascordoba.gob.ar}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Salinas |first=Carlos |date=2019-04-30 |title=¿Cuánto de soja y cuánto de maíz? Conoce el nuevo Mapa de Coberturas Agrícolas - IDECOR |url=https://www.idecor.gob.ar/conoce-el-nuevo-mapa-de-coberturas/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20260314094418/https://www.idecor.gob.ar/conoce-el-nuevo-mapa-de-coberturas/ |archive-date=2026-03-14 |access-date=2026-05-28 |work=IDECOR |language=es-ES}}</ref>

Furthermore, [[livestock]], is kept in the department. This includes [[poultry]]<ref>{{Cite news |last=IDECOR |date=2025-06-25 |title=Producción avícola en Córdoba: nuevo mapa con todos los datos |url=https://www.idecor.gob.ar/produccion-avicola-en-cordoba-nuevo-mapa-con-todos-los-datos/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20260113084346/https://www.idecor.gob.ar/produccion-avicola-en-cordoba-nuevo-mapa-con-todos-los-datos/ |archive-date=2026-01-13 |access-date=2026-05-28 |work=IDECOR |language=es-ES}}</ref>, [[pork]]<ref>{{Cite web |title=Visor de Mapas |url=https://mapascordoba.gob.ar/viewer/mapa/459 |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=mapascordoba.gob.ar}}</ref>, sheep and cows for the meat production.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Visor de Mapas |url=https://mapascordoba.gob.ar/viewer/mapa/459 |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=mapascordoba.gob.ar}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=IDECOR |date=2025-06-04 |title=Carne y leche: 8 mapas de la producción ganadera con datos actualizados |url=https://www.idecor.gob.ar/carne-y-leche-8-mapas-de-la-produccion-ganadera-con-datos-actualizados/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20260315230808/https://www.idecor.gob.ar/carne-y-leche-8-mapas-de-la-produccion-ganadera-con-datos-actualizados/ |archive-date=2026-03-15 |access-date=2026-05-28 |work=IDECOR |language=es-ES}}</ref>

== Mining ==
The department is home to a number of quarries that are mostly used for extracting building materials<ref>{{Cite web |last=Romero |first=Daniel Díaz |date=2026-05-05 |title=Corazón de piedra: la minería que devora las Sierras de Córdoba |url=https://latinta.com.ar/2026/05/05/corazon-piedra-mineria-devora-sierras-cordoba/ |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=La tinta |language=es}}</ref> and that have sparked environmental and labor concerns amongst the municipalities<ref>{{Cite web |last=Noticias |first=Agua de Oro |date=2026-05-28 |title=PREOCUPACIÓN VECINAL POR EL IMPACTO DE LAS CANTERAS EN LAS SIERRAS CHICAS - Agua de Oro Noticias |url=https://aguadeoronoticias.com.ar/2026/05/28/preocupacion-vecinal-por-el-impacto-de-las-canteras-en-las-sierras-chicas/ |access-date=2026-05-29 |language=es-AR}}</ref>

=== Tourism ===
Due to its location in the Sierras Chicas, Colón is a common tourist destination, in particular during the summer months. In 2026, various cities in the department reported large tourist numbers, both from national and international visitors.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cuellar |first=Benita |date=2026-02-03 |title=Turismo en Córdoba: en enero, Río Ceballos encabezó con un 85% la ocupación en Sierras Chicas |url=https://www.lavoz.com.ar/ciudadanos/turismo-en-cordoba-en-enero-rio-ceballos-encabezo-con-un-85-la-ocupacion-en-sierras-chicas_0_GKlY2l4xRR.html |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=www.lavoz.com.ar |language=es-LA}}</ref> Amongst others, the towns of Salsipuedes, Agua de Oro, La Granja, Ascochinga and Jesús María are popular destinations.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Clarín |first=Redacción |date=2022-08-13 |title=5 pueblos, 5 escapadas a las Sierras Chicas de Córdoba |url=https://www.clarin.com/viajes/5-pueblos-5-escapadas-sierras-chicas-cordoba_0_mV18ObuTt7.html |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=Clarín |language=es}}</ref> Many of those places offer various outdoor tourist activities, like hiking or horseback-riding.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sierras Chicas – Córdoba Turismo |url=https://cordobaturismo.gov.ar/sierras-chicas_/ |access-date=2026-05-28 |language=es}}</ref>

Every January, [[Jesús María, Argentina|Jesús María]] hosts the ''Festival Nacional de la Doma y el Folclore'' (Horse Training and Folklore Festival), attracting hundred-thousands of visitors.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Redacción |date=2026-01-25 |title=$106 mil millones como balance final: el impacto económico que dejó Jesús María 2026 tras diez noches de festival |url=https://politico.ar/106-mil-millones-como-balance-final-el-impacto-economico-que-dejo-jesus-maria-2026-tras-diez-noches-de-festival/ |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=Político.ar |language=es}}</ref> Additionally, many of the municipalities in the department organize events to celebrate carnival, attracting tourists as well.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-03 |title=Unquillo: este fin de semana llegará el Carnaval a Sierras Chicas {{!}} Noticias de Sierras Chicas |url=https://elmilenio.info/2025/02/03/unquillo-este-fin-de-semana-llegara-el-carnaval-a-sierras-chicas/ |access-date=2026-05-28 |website=El Milenio |language=es}}</ref>
