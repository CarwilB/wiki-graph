'''Laranja da Terra''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 10,933 (2020) and its area is 458&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/laranja-da-terra/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> [[East Pomeranian]], a dialect of [[Low German]], has co-official status in Laranja da Terra.
