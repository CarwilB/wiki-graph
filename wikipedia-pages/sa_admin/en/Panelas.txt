'''Panelas''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[pɐ̃ˈnɛlɐ(s)]}}) is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], Brazil.<ref>[http://www2.condepefidem.pe.gov.br/web/condepefidem PE State site - City by city profile]</ref> It is located 182.6&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the state capital. Panelas has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 22,991 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Panelas are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]] and [[manioc]], as well as livestock such as [[cattle]], [[goats]], [[sheep]], and [[poultry]].
