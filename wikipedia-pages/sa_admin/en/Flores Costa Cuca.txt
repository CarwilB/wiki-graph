'''Flores Costa Cuca''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The municipality covers an area of 61.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, is situated at an average altitude of 540 metres above sea level and has a population of 21,630.<ref>[http://www.inforpressca.com/florescostacuca/ Flores Costa Cuca, Quetzaltenango<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
