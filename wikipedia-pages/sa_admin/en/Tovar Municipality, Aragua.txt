The '''Tovar Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 14,161.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[Colonia Tovar]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Tovar Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>

==History==
The village of ''La Colonia Tovar'' (''The Tovar Colony'') is located about 60&nbsp;km from [[Caracas]].  The town was named after [[Martín Tovar y Ponte]] who donated the land over 100 years ago.  The town is mainly known for its [[Germanic peoples|Germanic]] characteristics, culture, and a dessert called "golfiado", which is very similar to a [[Cinnamon roll]].  Founded in 1843 by German settlers, the city remained isolated from the rest of the country for decades, a factor that permitted the inhabitants to keep their culture and traditions.  The majority of its residents are descendants of Germans and have a Northern European appearance.  The Alemannic dialect of [[German language|German]], known as ''[[Colonia Tovar dialect|Alemán Coloniero]]'' ("Colonial German"), is nearly extinct.

Today, 6,000 people live in the main village, up from 1,300 in 1963.

Due to the cool climate and pleasant surrounding countryside, it is a popular week-end destination for many visitors from [[Caracas]].  Many houses are weekend retreats and second homes.  There is a wide range of hotels, restaurants and tourist facilities, many of which are only open on the weekend.

The town and surrounding mountainous countryside have a superficial resemblance to Southern Germany.  The visitor will occasionally see Germanic oddities such as waitresses in traditional [[Bavaria]]n dress hawking "[[Black Forest cake|torta selva negra]]" and the odd [[List of international license plate codes|"D" plate]] on some cars, but will be unlikely to find anyone who understands German fluently.

==Geography==
Colonia Tovar, at about 2,000 meters above sea level, has an annual average temperature of 16 degrees [[Celsius]] (61 degrees [[Fahrenheit]]), and at night it can drop to around 5&nbsp;°C.

==Economy==
The cost of living is higher here than elsewhere in Venezuela.  The town depends mostly on tourism and [[peach]] agriculture.  The surrounding cloud forests are protected by the Pico Codazzi Natural Monument, though some signs of deforestation are visible due to the high tourist demand from Caracas, and the extraction of [[Cyatheales|tree fern]], sold dry for growing orchids.  The town is situated on a steep hillside, making the street layout difficult and poorly suited to the heavy weekend traffic.  During the week the town can be virtually deserted.

==Demographics==
The Tovar Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 17,429 (up from 14,798 in 2000).  This amounts to 1% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|77.46|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls]{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Tovar Municipality is Esteban Bocaranda, elected on October 31, 2004, with 33% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=14&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |title=Archived copy |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=14 January 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229230632/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=14&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |archive-date=29 February 2012 |url-status=dead}}</ref> He replaced Alfredo Durr shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-03-15 |archive-date=2006-10-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]; Capital Tovar.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
