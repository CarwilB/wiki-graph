'''Miguel Checa District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Sullana Province|Sullana]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital]''. Retrieved 17 April 2008. {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080417091049/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=17 April 2008 }}</ref> It was created by law on 10 November 1950 by the government of President [[Manuel A. Odría]].<ref>Ley 11515 {{Cite web|title=20.2 Departmento Piura: Dispositivo legal de creación según provincia y distrito |language=es |year=2015 |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática |url=http://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1201/cuadros/piura/piura_20_2.xls |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924034925/http://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1201/cuadros/piura/piura_20_2.xls |archivedate=24 September 2015 |url-status=live }}</ref> The administrative center of the district is the village of Sojo which is approximately {{Convert|15|km|mi|0}} west of the town of [[Sullana]] on the Paita Highway.<ref name="CasaSojo">{{Cite web|title=Casa Hacienda Sojo |date=20 February 2012 |language=es |publisher=Ministerio de Comercio Exterior y Turismo (MINCETUR) |url=http://www.mincetur.gob.pe/TURISMO/OTROS/inventario%20turistico/Ficha.asp?cod_Ficha=403 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150415192853/http://www.mincetur.gob.pe/TURISMO/OTROS/inventario%20turistico/Ficha.asp?cod_Ficha=403 |archivedate=15 April 2015 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Sojo Map — Satellite Images of Sojo |publisher=Maplandia |url=http://www.maplandia.com/peru/piura/sullana/sojo/ }}</ref>

Of interest in Miguel Checa District are the archaeological site and [[huaca]] "La Mariposa", and the colonial mansion "Casa de Sojo".<ref name="CasaSojo" /><ref>{{Cite web|title=19.2 Departmento Piura: Directorio de atractivos naturales y turisticos |year=1997 |language=es |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática |url=http://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/web/biblioineipub/bancopub/Est/lib0358/c1902.htm |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160123185344/http://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/web/biblioineipub/bancopub/Est/lib0358/c1902.htm |archivedate=23 January 2016 |url-status=live }}</ref> The district was named after Miguel Checa who built the "Casa de Sojo" in 1910.<ref name="CasaSojo" />
