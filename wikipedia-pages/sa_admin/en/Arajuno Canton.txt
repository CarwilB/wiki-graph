'''Arajuno Canton''' is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Pastaza Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Arajuno]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 5,150.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
