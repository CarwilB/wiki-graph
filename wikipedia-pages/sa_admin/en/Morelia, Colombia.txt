'''Morelia''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Caquetá Department]], [[Colombia]]. Morelia contains many ecological areas, such as [[Bodoquero River|El Rio Bodoquero]] valley, which attracts the majority of tourists and local residents.<ref>Inventory of Cultural Heritage; Municipality of Morelia; Institute of Culture, Sports, and Tourism of [[Caquetá Department|Caquetá]], accessed May 2, 2011</ref>
