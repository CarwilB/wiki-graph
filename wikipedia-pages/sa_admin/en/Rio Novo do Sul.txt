'''Rio Novo do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 11,626 (2020) and its area is {{Cvt|204|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/rio-novo-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The name of this municipality means "New River of the South" in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]].
