'''Anzoátegui''' ({{IPA|es|ansoˈateɣi}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Tolima Department]] of [[Colombia]]. The population of the municipality was 9,700 as of the 1993 census.

The town of Anzoátegui was founded on 16 July 1895, as the village of ''La Palma''.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://tolima.gov.co/tolima/informacion-general/turismo/1996-municipio-de-anzoategui |title=Municipio de Anzoátegui |trans-title=Municipality of Anzoátegui |language=es |website=tolima.gov.co |access-date=11 June 2025}}</ref>
