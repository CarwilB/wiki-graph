'''Zamora Municipality''' is a municipality in [[Michoacán]], Mexico. The seat is at [[Zamora, Michoacán]].

Municipalities of Zamora:

-Ejidio Independencia La Labor

-Guamuchil

-La Estancia

-Sauz de Magaña

-Atacheo, a town where the entire municipality gathers to celebrate holidays, like Independence Day and religious holidays. Especially on 12 December, when they celebrate día de la Virgin de Guadalupe.

Three police officers were killed and 10 people injured in a shoot-out in Zamora Municipality on May 26, [[2019 in Mexico|2019]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.msn.com/es-mx/noticias/mexico/ataque-deja-tres-polic%C3%ADas-muertos-y-otros-10-heridos-en-zamora-michoac%C3%A1n/ar-AABWE5q|title=Ataque deja tres policías muertos y otros 10 heridos en Zamora, Michoacán|publisher=MSN Noticias|access-date=May 26, 2019 |language=es|date=May 26, 2019|trans-title=Attack leaves three policemen dead and another 10 injured in Zamora, Michoacán}}</ref>
