'''Guarani de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality of Brazil]], located in northeastern [[Goiás]].

==Geography==
Guarani belongs to the [[Vão do Paranã]] statistical microregion. It borders the municipals of [[São Domingos, Goiás|São Domingos]], [[Iaciara]], [[Posse, Goiás|Posse]], as well as the state of [[Bahia]].

Guarani is located south of the Terra Ronca state park and 28 kilometers north of Posse. Guarani is crossed by the Frei River, which has one of the most beautiful waterfalls in the region.

The distance to [[Goiânia]] is 550&nbsp;km. Connections are made by taking	BR-153 / Anápolis / GO-060 / Alexânia / Planaltina / Formosa / GO-020 / BR-030 / Vila Boa / Alvorada do Norte / GO-108 / Posse.<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Transporte] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=2009-02-05 }}</ref>

==History==
The town was first created as a district of the municipality of  [[São Domingos, Goiás|São Domingos]] with the name of Guarani in 1919.  In 1943 the name was changed to Coatiçaba.  In 1963 it was dismembered from São Domingos and formed a new municipality with the name Guarani de Goiás.
