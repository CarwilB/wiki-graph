'''Itaipé''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 12,836 people living in a total area of 483&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/itaipe/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Itaipé is located on at an elevation of 847 meters, 66&nbsp;km. northwest of [[Teófilo Otoni]].  The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 530&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Novo Cruzeiro]], [[Caraí]], [[Catuji]], and [[Ladainha, Minas Gerais|Ladainha]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-08 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |archive-date=2008-06-13 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city's name is of [[Tupi language|Tupi]] origin and means "Path of Stones", due to the presence in the region of many interesting rock formations. The most spectacular is Pedra do Chifre, with an elevation of 1,100 meters, and located on the municipal boundary of Itaipé and Teófilo Otoni. There are also many waterfalls in the region including Cachoeira das Bicas, 3&nbsp;km. from the city center.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.itaipe.net/index.php |title=Itaipé |access-date=2008-08-08 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080603171549/http://www.itaipe.net/index.php |archive-date=2008-06-03 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

The town began as a district of [[Araçuai]] and later [[Novo Cruzeiro]], becoming a municipality in 1962.

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|37|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 20 million from services, 3 million from industry, and 12 million from agriculture. There were 1,232 rural producers on 38,000 hectares of land. Only 16 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 4,100 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were coffee, sugarcane, beans and corn. There were 6,000 head of cattle (2006). There was one bank (2007) and 347 automobiles (556 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 33 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Health and education==
There were 2 health clinic and 1 hospital with 26 beds (2005). Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni. Educational needs were met by 22 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 1 pre-primary school.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>
