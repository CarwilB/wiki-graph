'''Retalhuleu''' ({{IPA|es|re.ta.luˈlew}}) is a city in south-western [[Guatemala]], about {{convert|55|km}} by road southwest of [[Quetzaltenango]]. It is the departmental seat of [[Retalhuleu Department]] as well as the municipal seat of Retalhuleu Municipality. Locally it is nicknamed "Reu."<ref name="Frommers">{{cite web|url=https://www.frommers.com/destinations/retalhuleu/|title=Retalhuleu|publisher=Frommer's|accessdate=15 July 2025}}</ref>

The city had a population of 90,505 at the time of the 2018 census.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ |title= Population of cities & towns in Guatemala|publisher=Citypopulation.de|accessdate=15 July 2025}}</ref> 

==History==
Retalhuleu was established in 1877.<ref name="Frommers"/> The city played a role in the [[Bay of Pigs Invasion]]. For a number of recruits, infantry training was carried out at a CIA-run base code-named ''JMTrax''. The base was on the Pacific coast of Guatemala between [[Quetzaltenango]] and Retalhuleu, in the Helvetia coffee plantation.<ref name="BOPC1961">{{cite book |last1=Quesada |first1=Alejandro de |title=The Bay of Pigs: Cuba 1961 |date=2011 |publisher=Bloomsbury Publishing |isbn=978-1846038198 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=EE-1CwAAQBAJ&dq=JMTrax&pg=PA11 |language=en}}</ref> In summer 1960, an airfield (code-named ''JMadd'', aka ''Rayo Base'') was constructed near Retalhuleu, Guatemala.<ref name="BOPC1961"/><ref>Wise, David; Ross, Thomas (1964). [[The Invisible Government]]. New York: Random House. pp. 23-50</ref> In November 1960, the Retalhuleu recruits also took part in quelling an officers' rebellion in Guatemala, in addition to the intervention of the [[U.S. Navy]].<ref name="revolutionaries">{{cite book|pages=97–99|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=5s4263Q-xGgC&pg=PA98|title=Revolution and revolutionaries: Guerrilla movements in Latin America|isbn=978-0842026260|last1=Castro|first1=Daniel|year=1999| publisher=SR Books }}</ref>

On August 7 and 8, 2021, Retalhuleu was severely affected by rain, which lasted for 36 hours and caused landslides, floods and other damages to Retalhuleu and other Guatemalan cities.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.prensalibre.com/guatemala/comunitario/lluvia-del-fin-de-semana-deja-danos-en-provincia-retalhuleu-y-zacapa-son-de-los-mas-afectados-breaking/|title=Lluvia de 36 horas deja daños: Retalhuleu y Zacapa son de los más afectados|publisher=Prensa Libre|language=Spanish|accessdate=15 July 2025}}</ref>

==Geography and climate==
Retalhuleu lies in south-western Guatemala, approximately {{convert|55|km}} by road southwest of [[Quetzaltenango]], {{convert|27|km}} west of [[Mazatenango]] and about {{convert|37|km}} northeast of the coastal town of [[Champerico]] along Route 95.<ref>{{Google maps | url =https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Quetzaltenango,+Guatemala/Retalhuleu,+Guatemala/@14.6841441,-91.7483117,64847m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m13!4m12!1m5!1m1!1s0x858e97fe3fc89d3f:0x3ba7ff011f0f000f!2m2!1d-91.5231866!2d14.8446068!1m5!1m1!1s0x858ef1dc35723fa9:0x96991609e9dcfced!2m2!1d-91.685788!2d14.5245485?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI1MDcwOS4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D  | accessdate =15 July 2025}}</ref>

Retalhuleu has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Am'') with a very rainy [[wet season]] from April to November, and a [[dry season]] from December to March.<ref name="WMONormals"/>

==Sports==
The local [[Association football|football]] club is [[Deportivo Reu]] which was founded in 2013 after [[Juventud Retalteca]] became defunct.<{{citation needed|date=October 2025}} Deportivo Reu play their local games at the [[Estadio Dr. Óscar Monterroso Izaguirre]] in the [[Primera División de Ascenso]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://stereo100.com.gt/2019/resultados-y-posiciones-en-la-jornada-1-de-la-primera-division-en-guatemala/|title=Resultados y posiciones en la jornada 1 de la Primera División en Guatemala|publisher=Stereo100.com|date=13 January 2019|accessdate=15 July 2025|language=Spanish}}</ref>
