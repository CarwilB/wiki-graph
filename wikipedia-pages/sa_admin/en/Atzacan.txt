'''Atzacan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 198&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 80.61 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|54|N|97|05|W|}}.

The municipality of '''Atzacan''' is delimited to the north by [[Chocaman]], to the east by [[Iztaczoquitlán]], to the south by [[Orizaba]], to the south-west by [[Mariano Escobedo, Veracruz|Mariano Escobedo]], to the west by [[La Perla, Veracruz|La Perla]], to the north-west by [[Coscomatepec de Bravo]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30022a.htm |archive-url=https://archive.today/20070626042748/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30022a.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2007-06-26 |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Atzacan |publisher=[Gobierno Estatal] |language=es |access-date=2009-06-04 }}</ref>

It produces principally [[maize]] and [[beans]].

A celebration in honor of Santa Ana, Patronress, takes place in April.

A large crowd attended the funeral of mayor Octavio Misael Lorenzo Morales on February 8, 2021, who died of [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico|COVID-19]]. As of this date, there have been 106 confirmed cases and ten deaths due to the virus in Atzacan.<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus (COVID-19) en Atzacan, Veracruz |url=http://www.gncys.com/covid19/covid19-municipio.aspx?edoid=VZ&munid=022 |website=gncys.com |access-date=February 9, 2021 |language=es-mx |date=1 February 2020}}</ref> Few of the mourners practiced [[Social distancing measures related to the COVID-19 pandemic|social distancing]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Alcalde veracruzano murió por COVID y salieron a despedirlo cientos de personas sin sana distancia |url=https://www.infobae.com/america/mexico/2021/02/10/alcalde-veracruzano-murio-por-covid-y-salieron-a-despedirlo-cientos-de-personas-sin-sana-distancia/ |access-date=February 9, 2021 |work=infobae |publisher=Infobae |date=February 9, 2021 |language=es-ES}}</ref>
