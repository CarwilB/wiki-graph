The '''Caseros Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Caseros''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the south of the province. Its head town is the city of [[Casilda]] (population 32,000).

Its neighbouring departments are [[Belgrano Department, Santa Fe|Belgrano]] and [[Iriondo Department|Iriondo]] in the north, [[San Lorenzo Department|San Lorenzo]] in the west, and [[Constitución Department, Santa Fe|Constitución]] and [[General López Department|General López]] in the south; to the west its border marks the interprovincial limit with [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]].

The towns and cities in this department are (in alphabetical order): [[Arequito, Santa Fe|Arequito]], [[Arteaga, Argentina|Arteaga]], [[Berabevú]], [[Bigand]], Casilda, [[Chabás]], [[Chañar Ladeado]], [[Gödeken]], [[Los Molinos, Argentina|Los Molinos]], [[Los Quirquinchos]], [[San José de La Esquina]], [[Sanford, Argentina|Sanford]], [[Villada, Argentina|Villada]].
