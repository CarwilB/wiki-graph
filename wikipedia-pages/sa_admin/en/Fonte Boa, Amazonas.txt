'''Fonte Boa''' (literally, "good water spring") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 17,005 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/fonte-boa/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 12,111&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

The city is served by [[Fonte Boa Airport]].

One of the city's best-known attractions is the [https://portalamazonia.com/amazonia-de-a-a-z/pirarucu-o-gigante-da-amazonia/ Pirarucu Festival], which celebrates the annual fishing of the fish.

==Geography==
The municipality contains 45% of the {{convert|146950|ha}} [[Auatí-Paraná Extractive Reserve]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Auatí-Paraná – ISA}}|language=pt
