'''Umbaúba''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ũbaˈubɐ|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 25,550 (2020). Umbaúba covers {{convert|118.86|km2|mi2|abbr=on}} and has a population density of 210 inhabitants per square kilometer. The municipality is located {{convert|95|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/>
