'''Carinhanha''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Carinhanha covers {{convert|2,529.45|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 30,375 with a population density of 12 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located on the banks of the [[Rio São Francisco]] and the [[Carinhanha River]], which also forms the border of the state of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref name="i"/>
