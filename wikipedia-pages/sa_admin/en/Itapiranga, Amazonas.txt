'''Itapiranga''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] [[northern Brazil]] on the left bank of the [[Solimões River]] about 200&nbsp;km east of Manaus. Its population was 9,230 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/itapiranga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 4,231&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Name==
The name is of American Indian origin, and was given to a quarry which has a port. It comes from ''ita'', stone and ''pitanga'', red, so the name means "red stone".{{CN|date=January 2026}}

==Geography==
The municipality contains about 40% of the {{convert|424430|ha|adj=on}} [[Uatumã Sustainable Development Reserve]], which protects the lower part of the [[Uatumã River]] basin.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS do Uatumã – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RDS do Uatumã|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6832|access-date=2016-07-25}}</ref>

==History==
It was founded in 1931 as a suburb of [[Silves, Amazonas|Silves]].
