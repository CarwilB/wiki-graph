'''Armenia''', also known as '''''Armenia Mantequilla''''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for ''[[Butter]] Armenia'') to avoid confusion with other [[Armenia (disambiguation)|Armenias]], is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and town in the western region of the [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It lies at an altitude of 1,800 m (5,900 ft) above sea level.
