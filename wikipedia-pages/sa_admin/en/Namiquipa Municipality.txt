'''Namiquipa''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Namiquipa]]. 

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 22,880,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Namiquipa|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=048|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 20,314 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Namiquipa|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08048a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070529050147/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08048a.htm|archivedate=May 29, 2007}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Namiquipa had a population of 1,752,<ref name=SEDESOL/> up from 1,718 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Namiquipa|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08048a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070529050147/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08048a.htm|archivedate=May 29, 2007}}</ref>  Other than the town of Namiquipa, the municipality had 363 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Santa Ana, Chihuahua|Santa Ana]] (2,978), classified as urban, and [[El Terrero, Chihuahua|El Terrero]] (2,621), [[El Molino, Chihuahua|El Molino]] (2,176), [[Benito Juárez, Chihuahua|Benito Juárez]] (1,967), classified as urban, and [[Cruces, Chihuahua|Cruces]] (1,206), and [[Independencia (Cologachi), Chihuahua|Independencia (Cologachi)]] (1,088), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
