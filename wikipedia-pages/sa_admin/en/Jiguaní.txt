'''Jiguaní''' ({{IPA|es|xiɣwaˈni}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Granma Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located {{convert|25|km|mi}} east of [[Bayamo]], the provincial capital.

==Overview==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Babiney, Baire, Bijagual, La Villa, Los Negros, Maffo, Rihito and Santa Rita.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/ojiguani/index.htm|title=Jiguaní|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-06|language=es}}</ref>

The name "Jiguaní" is of [[Taíno people|Taíno]] origin, and means "height of the river".<ref name=taino>{{Cite web|url=http://www.uctp.org/VocesIndigena.html |title=Taíno Dictionary |author=[[United Confederation of Taíno People]] |accessdate=2007-10-18 |language=Spanish |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071016055722/http://www.uctp.org/VocesIndigena.html |archivedate=2007-10-16 |url-status=dead }}</ref>{{better|date=May 2026}} The town of Jiguaní was a stronghold and a starting point in the [[Cuban War of Independence|independence war]] of 1895.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Jiguaní had a population of 58,982.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|646|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|91|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
