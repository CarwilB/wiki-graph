'''Tenancingo''' is a town and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Tlaxcala|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tlaxcala]]. 

Tenancingo is now considered to be the center for [[Sex trafficking in Mexico|sex trafficking throughout Mexico]], with roots of the practice dating back to the 1970s.<ref>{{cite news|last1=Alejandro|first1=Lopez de Haro|title=Sex Trafficking Ring From Mexico To New York Discovered|url=https://huffingtonpost.com/2012/06/04/sex-trafficking-ring-from_n_1568348.html|work=The Huffington Post|accessdate=6 December 2014|date=4 June 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=de los Reyes|first1=Ignacio|title=Tenancingo, viaje a la capital de la esclavitud sexual en México|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/mundo/noticias/2012/05/120522_trata_mujeres_mexico_eeuu_sexual_pea.shtml|website=BBC Mundo|accessdate=6 December 2014}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last1=Vazquez|first1=Rafael|title=¿Tlaxcala: La Meca de los padrotes?|url=https://www.sdpnoticias.com/columnas/2012/10/31/tlaxcala-la-meca-de-los-padrotes|website=SDP Noticias|accessdate=2 February 2015}}</ref> It is home to local [[organized crime]] operations that work in collaboration with the larger [[Drug cartel|cartels]]: [[Los Zetas]], [[Milenio Cartel|Nuevo Milenio]], [[Knights Templar Cartel|Caballeros Templarios]], and the [[Gulf Cartel]].<ref name="Vanguardia">{{cite web|last1=Embargo|first1=Sin|title=Cárteles ganan 10 mmdd con trata de mujeres; menores entran a "padrotear"|url=https://www.vanguardia.com.mx/cartelesganan10mmddcontratademujeresmenoresentranapadrotear-1779959.html|website=www.vanguardia.com.mx|publisher=Vanguardia|accessdate=2 February 2015}}</ref>

==Sex trade and human trafficking==
Of the 10,000 inhabitants of Tenancingo, it is estimated that 1,000 are sex traffickers.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Trevelyan|first1=Laura|last2=Botti|first2=David|last3=de los Reyes|first3=Ignacio|title=Tenancingo: the town sex trafficking built|url=https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-18078658|website=www.bbc.com|publisher=BBC}}</ref> Local [[sexual exploitation]], [[human trafficking]], [[pimping]] and [[forced prostitution]] industries in Tenancingo are estimated to be worth $1 billion [[USD]] annually,<ref name="Vanguardia"/> with direct ties to the international [[sex trade]].<ref name="Daily News">{{cite news| last1=Erica| first1=Pearson| title=Small Mexican town of Tenancingo is major source of sex trafficking pipeline to New York| url=http://www.nydailynews.com/new-york/small-town-tenancingo-mexico-city-source-new-york-sex-slaves-article-1.1088866| website=www.nydailynews.com/| publisher=Daily News| date=3 June 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite web| last1=Castro Soto| first1=Oscar Arturo| title=LA INICIATIVA POPULAR EN TLAXCALA ACCIONES COLECTIVAS PARA EL COMBATE A LA TRATA DE MUJERES| url=http://idhieibero.org/publicaciones/pdf/iniciativaPopular.pdf|website=idhieibero.org/| publisher=Centro Fray Julián Garcés, Derechos Humanos y Desarrollo Local, A. C.| url-status=dead| archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141209110509/http://idhieibero.org/publicaciones/pdf/iniciativaPopular.pdf| archivedate=2014-12-09}}</ref> These practices have been denounced by dozens of [[Non-governmental agency|NGOs]].<ref name="Daily News"/><ref>{{cite web| last1=Castro Soto| first1=Oscar Arturo| title=LA INICIATIVA POPULAR EN TLAXCALA ACCIONES COLECTIVAS PARA EL COMBATE A LA TRATA DE MUJERES| url=http://idhieibero.org/publicaciones/pdf/iniciativaPopular.pdf|website=idhieibero.org/| publisher=Centro Fray Julián Garcés, Derechos Humanos y Desarrollo Local, A. C.| url-status=dead| archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141209110509/http://idhieibero.org/publicaciones/pdf/iniciativaPopular.pdf| archivedate=2014-12-09}}</ref>

Recent investigations and a documentary ''Pimp City: A Journey to the Center of the Sex Trade'' (2014), have revealed that the small town was identified by the [[United States Department of Justice]] as the leading provider of female sex slaves to the United States.<ref>{{cite web| title=HUMAN TRAFFICKING ASSESSMENT TOOL REPORT FOR MEXICO| url=https://apps.americanbar.org/rol/publications/mexico_2009_htat_en.pdf| website=www.americanbar.org/aba.html| publisher=the American Bar Association| accessdate=6 December 2014| archive-date=3 March 2016| archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303204017/https://apps.americanbar.org/rol/publications/mexico_2009_htat_en.pdf| url-status=dead}}</ref> According to the documentary, the entire political structure and police force of the town are implicated in [[human trafficking]] and [[sex trade]].<ref>{{cite web| last1=Brennan| first1=Alice| title=Prostitution Pipeline to U.S. Begins in Tenancingo, Mexico| url=http://hereandnow.wbur.org/2014/06/30/pimp-city-tenancingo| website=hereandnow.wbur.org/| publisher=Fusion| accessdate=6 December 2014}}</ref> The [[U.N.]] estimates profits for the global human trafficking and sex trade industries at US$32 billion annually, making it the third most profitable [[Black market|illegal global industry]].<ref>{{cite news| last1=Grillo| first1=Ioan| title=The Mexican Drug Cartels' Other Business: Sex Trafficking| date=31 July 2013| url=https://world.time.com/2013/07/31/the-mexican-drug-cartels-other-business-sex-trafficking/| work=[[Time (magazine)|Time]]| access-date=4 February 2015}}</ref>

On October 11, [[2019 in Mexico|2019]], the [[Attorney General of Mexico]] arrested three members of a sex-trafficking ring in Tenancingo.<ref>{{cite web|website=Informador|title=La FGR desarticula banda dedicada a la trata de personas en Tlaxcala|language=es|trans-title=The Attorney General of Mexico dismantles a sex-trafficking ring in Tlaxcala|url=https://www.informador.mx/mexico/La-FGR-desarticula-banda-dedicada-a-la-trata-de-personas-en-Tlaxcala-20191011-0101.html}}</ref>
