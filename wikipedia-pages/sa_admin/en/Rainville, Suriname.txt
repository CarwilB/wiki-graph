'''Rainville''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in Suriname, located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 22,747.<ref name="census"/>

The Rainville resort is home to the [[Paramaribo Zoo]]. The [[zoo]] was opened in 1972. Unlike most zoos, about 75% of the animals are local wild animals who had been either confiscated or were former pets.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2020/03/03/paramaribo-zoo-op-world-wildlife-day-zullen-de-dieren-ook-goed-verzorgd-worden/|title=Paramaribo Zoo: "Op World Wildlife Day zullen de dieren ook goed verzorgd worden"|website=Dagblad Suriname|date=3 March 2020 |access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Combé==
{{anchor|Combe}}
[[File:Figuratieve plattegrond der stad Paramaribo. Blad 80 van de 1e Buitenwijk of Combé. (A.J. Meyer 1905).pdf|thumb|left|Map of Combé (1905)]]
Rainville is home to the Combé neighbourhood. It started as a plantation, and was turned into a village in 1791.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/301ges/aparbo04.html|title=Paramaribo Suriname 4|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The neighbourhood has been named after Nicolaas Combé, a French [[huguenot]] and one of the early colonists, who was persuaded to move to Suriname from [[Berbice]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://suriname.nu/surinamezoeken/knowledge-base/notities/ |title=De Hugenoten|website=Suriname.nu|date=17 September 2020 |access-date=16 March 2021|language=nl}}</ref>

It is the oldest neighbourhood of Paramaribo, and contains small wooden houses with their own character.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-noord|title=Paramaribo-Noord|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website=Vakantie Arena}}</ref> When the [[Centrum, Paramaribo|city centre]] was designated a [[Unesco]] [[World Heritage Site]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/940|title=Historic Inner City Paramaribo|website=Unesco.org|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref> Combé was listed as a buffer area.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cityofparamaribo.com/read/Worldheritage_area?sublist=18936&css=|title=World Heritage Area|website=City of Paramaribo|access-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>
