'''Fort-Liberté''' ({{langx|ht|Fòlibète}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 60,632 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Fort-Liberté Arrondissement start with the number 21.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Fort-Liberté]]
* [[Ferrier, Nord-Est|Ferrier]]
* [[Perches, Nord-Est|Perches]]
