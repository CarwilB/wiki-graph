'''Municipality B''' is one of the eight [[Municipalities of Uruguay|municipalities]] of [[Montevideo]], [[Uruguay]]. It is one of the smallest and central municipalities of the city, occupying part of the southern end of the [[Montevideo Department|department]], on the waters of the [[Río de la Plata]]. A large part of the municipality has an administrative and [[Port of Montevideo|port]] function.

It was created through decree 11,567 of September 13, 2009,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Uruguay Presidencia |url=https://www.gub.uy/presidencia/inicio |access-date=2023-05-10 |website=Uruguay Presidencia |language=es}}</ref> later ratified.<ref>[http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/_web/leyes/2010/02/CM909.pdf Law 18,644 of February 12, 2010]{{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100326124707/http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/_web/leyes/2010/02/CM909.pdf|date=2010-03-26}}</ref><ref>[http://www.presidencia.gub.uy/sci/leyes/2010/03/CM3.pdf Law 18,653 of March 15, 2010]</ref>

== Population ==
According to the 2009 census data, the municipality had an approximate population of 120,000 inhabitants.<ref>[http://www.observa.com.uy/Municipales2010/nota.aspx?id=96530&ex=33&ar=1&fi=39 Information on the eight new municipalities of Montevideo at observa.com.uy]{{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100807185801/http://www.observa.com.uy/Municipales2010/nota.aspx?id=96530&ex=33&ar=1&fi=39|date=2010-08-07}}</ref>

== Neighborhoods ==
{{Main articles|Barrios of Montevideo}}
Each of the municipalities of Montevideo is subdivided into neighborhoods (barrios). Specifically, municipality B is made up of the following neighborhoods: [[Aguada, Montevideo|La Aguada]], [[Barrio Sur, Montevideo|Barrio Sur]], [[Centro, Montevideo|Centro]], Ciudad Vieja, [[Cordón]], [[Palermo, Montevideo|Palermo]] and [[Parque Rodó]].
