'''Necochea''' is a coastal [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] and city area in the southeast of the [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. The seat of the municipality is the port of [[Necochea]].

The partido has a population of around 104,977 people, in an area of {{convert|4455|sqkm|abbr=on}}. In the census of 2001 Neochea Partido had a population density of exactly {{convert|20.00|PD/sqkm}}.

[[Image:Playa-Necochea.JPG|left|250px|Necochea beachfront]]

==Geography==
As of 2001 the district had about 90,000 inhabitants. The district covers an area of {{convert|4455|sqkm|abbr=on}} and lies about {{convert|530|km|abbr=out}} south of [[Buenos Aires]].

==History==
The partido was founded on July 19, 1865, and the settlement was founded on October 12, 1881, by [[Ángel Ignacio Murga]].

The town of Necochea obtained its status as a city on July 26, 1911.
[[Image:Lago-Necochea.JPG|thumb|left|250px|El lago de los Cisnes (Swan's Lake).]]

==Geography==
The partido has a salt-water lake known as ''El Lago de los Cisnes'', ([[Swan's Lake]]).

==Economy==

The economy of Necochea benefits from an influx of tourists from [[Gran Buenos Aires]] during the summer vacation season (December–February).

The main elements of the non-tourist economy are farming, the production of agricultural chemicals and [[biodiesel]].
