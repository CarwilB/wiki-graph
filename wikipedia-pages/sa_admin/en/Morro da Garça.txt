'''Morro da Garça''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/destinos/morro-da-garca |title= Minas Gerais on Administrative City}}</ref> Its population as of 2020 was 2,437 living in a total area of 414&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/morro-da-garca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira (mesoregion)|Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]].  It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Overview==
Morro da Garça is located at an elevation of 600 meters, 15&nbsp;km. west of important highway BR-135.  The distance to [[Curvelo]] is 36&nbsp;km., and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 210&nbsp;km.   Neighboring municipalities are:  [[Corinto, Minas Gerais|Corinto]], [[Curvelo]] and [[Felixlândia]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172032/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |date=2008-04-10 }}</ref>  The name comes from a nearby hill.

The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture.  The production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations is also important.  The GDP in 2005 was R$19 million, with 8 million from services, 1 million from industry, and 8 million from agriculture.  There were 235 rural producers on 27,000 hectares of land.  Only 45 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 700 persons were dependent on agriculture.  The main crops were  beans and corn.  There were 27,000 head of cattle (2006).  There were no banks (2007) and 152 automobiles (110 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 29 inhabitants per automobile.<ref name="IBGE"/>

There were 2 health clinics.  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Curvelo.  Educational needs were met by 4 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 2 pre-primary schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.680 (2000)
*State ranking: 634 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 3,291 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000 
*Literacy rate: 82%
*Life expectancy: 65 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In 2000 the per capita income of R$129.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
