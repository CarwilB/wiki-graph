'''San Sebastián Huehuetenango''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/san-sebastian-huehuetenango-declarado-quinto-municipio-libre-de-pisos-de-tierra/|title=San Sebastián Huehuetenango declarado quinto municipio libre de pisos de tierra|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|first=Brenda|last=Larios|date=18 November 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located approximately 27&nbsp;km. north of the city of Huehuetenango on Inter-American Highway heading toward the Mexican border at La Mesilla. The language spoken is Mayan Mam. The majority of the inhabitants are subsistence farmers.
