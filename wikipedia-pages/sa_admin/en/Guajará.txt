'''Guajará''' (''Guanjeras'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 16,937 in 2020<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/guajara/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 8,904&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2007-02-20 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
