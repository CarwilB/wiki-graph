'''Itaperuna''' ({{IPA|pt|itapeˈɾunɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. It had a population of 101,041 in [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]], and has an area of 1.105,3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{cite web |title=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|url=http://ftp.ibge.gov.br/Estimativas_de_Populacao/Estimativas_2018/estimativa_dou_2018_20181019.pdf |year=2018 |quote=2018 Estimates of Population |access-date=6 March 2019}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Tabela 4709: População residente, Variação absoluta de população residente e Taxa de crescimento geométrico |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4709#resultado |access-date=2023-10-26 |website=sidra.ibge.gov.br}}</ref><!--ref name="IBGE2"/-->

==History==

Itaperuna was founded in 1889, after being separated from the municipality of [[Campos dos Goytacazes]].<ref name="IBGE Cities">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |title=IBGE Cities |author=IBGE |author-link=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120430164910/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |archive-date=2012-04-30}}</ref>

==Location==

Itaperuna is located 230&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Rio de Janeiro]]. Its neighbouring municipalities are:<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/mapas_ibge/pol_estaduais.php |title=Political-Administrative Maps |author=IBGE |author-link=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics}}</ref>

* [[Bom Jesus do Itabapoana]] – north, northeast and east
* [[Campos dos Goytacazes]] – east
* [[Italva]] – east and southeast
* [[Cambuci]] – south
* [[São José de Ubá]] – south
* [[Miracema]] – southwest
* [[Laje do Muriaé]] – west
* [[Patrocínio de Muriaé]] – northwest
* [[Eugenópolis]] – northwest
* [[Antônio Prado de Minas]] – northwest
