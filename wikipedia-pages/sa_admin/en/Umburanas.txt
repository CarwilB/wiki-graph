'''Umburanas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Umburanas covers {{convert|1,775.633|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 19,402 with a [[population density]] of 10.2 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/umburanas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The municipality is located within the [[caatinga]] [[ecoregion]].<ref name="i"/> Umburanas borders on five municipalities: [[Ourolândia]], [[Mirangaba]], [[Campo Formoso]], [[Morro do Chapéu]], and [[Sento Sé]], all in the state of Bahia.
