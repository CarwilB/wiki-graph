'''Puquio District''' is one of twenty-one [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Lucanas Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Some of the largest lakes of the district are [[Apiñaqucha]], [[Islaqucha]], [[Pukaqucha (Ayacucho)|Pukaqucha]] and [[Lake Orconcocha, Ayacucho|Urqunqucha]].
