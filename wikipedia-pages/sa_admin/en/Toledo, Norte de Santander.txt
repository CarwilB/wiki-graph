'''Toledo''' ({{IPA|es|toˈleðo|-|es-Toledo.ogg}}) is a [[Colombian municipality]] and town located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[North Santander]]. It is on the border with [[Boyacá Department]] and has natural gas development in the township of [[Gibraltar, Colombia|Gibraltar]]. In August 2011 the Gibraltar to [[Bucaramanga]] gas pipeline was completed.<ref>{{Cite journal|author=Nogarin, Mauro|date=October 2011|title=Mountainous Terrain Challenges Workers Building Gas Pipeline In Colombia|journal=Pipeline & Gas Journal|volume=238|issue=10|url=http://www.pipelineandgasjournal.com/mountainous-terrain-challenges-workers-building-gas-pipeline-colombia?page=show|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20111013054410/http://pipelineandgasjournal.com/mountainous-terrain-challenges-workers-building-gas-pipeline-colombia?page=show|archivedate=13 October 2011|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Climate==
Toledo has a very wet [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cfb). It has heavy rainfall from November to February and very heavy to extremely heavy rainfall from April to October.
