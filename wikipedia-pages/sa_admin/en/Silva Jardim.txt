'''Silva Jardim''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈsiwvɐ ʒaʁˈdʒĩ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 21,352 (2022) and its area is {{cvt|937|km2}}.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/silva-jardim/panorama |access-date=2023-10-26 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

==Conservation==

The municipality  contains the {{convert|5052|ha|adj=on}} [[Poço das Antas Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1974 from a former experimental agricultural station.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Poço das Antas|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=215|accessdate=2016-04-28}}</ref>
It contains 7.5% of the {{convert|46350|ha|adj=on}} [[Três Picos State Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES dos Três Picos – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PES dos Três Picos|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/597436|accessdate=2016-10-03}}</ref>
It contains part of the [[Central Rio de Janeiro Atlantic Forest Mosaic]] of conservation units, created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese|page=13 |last1=Costa |last2=Lamas |last3=Fernandes |first1=Cláudia |first2=Ivana|first3=Rosan|publisher=Reserva da Biosfera da Mata Atlântica |title=Planejamento Estratégico do Mosaico Central Fluminense|date=December 2010 |url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/images/stories/mosaicos/planejamento-central-fluminense.pdf|accessdate=2016-10-02}}</ref>
