'''Astorga''' is a municipality in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in [[Brazil]].  It was founded in 1951.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 26,209 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/astorga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
