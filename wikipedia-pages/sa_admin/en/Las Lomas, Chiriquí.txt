'''Las Lomas''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[David District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|76.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 18,769 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|245.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 10,615; its population as of 2000 was 13,683.<ref name=inec/> its population as of 2010 was 18,769 its population as of 2023 was 25,297
