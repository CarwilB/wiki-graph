'''São João do Polêsine''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

== Paleontology ==
In this city there are [[outcrop]]s with [[fossil]]s. [[Sauropodomorpha|Sauropodomorph]] [[dinosaur]] ''[[Buriolestes]]'' and [[dinosauromorph]] ''[[Ixalerpeton]]'' were found in the [[Carnian]] (Late [[Triassic]]) deposits.
