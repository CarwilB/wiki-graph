'''La Convención''' is the largest of thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Cusco Region]] in the southern highlands of [[Peru]].

As part of the higher-altitude [[Amazon basin]] at the foot of the Andes, La Convención is one of three Peruvian provinces that prominently figure in national [[coffee]] production, the other being [[Chanchamayo Province|Chanchamayo]] province in [[Junín Region|Junín]] and [[Jaén Province, Peru|Jaén]] province in [[Cajamarca region|Cajamarca]].

== Geography ==
The province of La Convención is bounded to the north by the [[Junín Region]] and the [[Ucayali Region]], to the east by the [[Madre de Dios Region]], to the south by the provinces of [[Anta Province|Anta]], [[Calca province|Calca]] and [[Urubamba province|Urubamba]], and to the west by the [[Ayacucho Region]] and the [[Apurímac Region]].

La Convención is approximately {{cvt|220|km|mi}} long from north to south.  Within that distance, the land of La Convención reaches has a maximum elevation of {{cvt|6264|m|ft}} at [[Salcantay]],<ref>"Peakbagger,"[https://www.peakbagger.com/peak.aspx?pid=8487], accessed 17 Jul 2019</ref> on the border of La Convención, [[Anta Province|Anta]], and [[Urubamba Province|Urubamba]] provinces, and a minimum elevation of {{cvt|284|m|ft}} in the [[Amazon Basin]] along the [[Ucayali River]]. Between the glaciers and tundra of Salcantay and other high mountains to the [[rain forest]]s of the Amazon Basin the topography is extremely rugged and varied.<ref>Google Earth</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens: [[Asháninka people|Asháninka]],<ref>[http://www.peruecologico.com.pe/etnias_ashaninka.htm www.peruecologico.com.pe/ Ashaninka]</ref> [[Matsigenka people|Matsigenka]],<ref>[http://www.peruecologico.com.pe/etnias_machiguenga.htm peruecologico.com.pe] Machiguenga</ref> [[Yine people|Yine]]<ref>[http://www.peruecologico.com.pe/etnias_piro.htm peruecologico.com.pe] Piro</ref> and [[Quechua people|Quechua]]. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (51.98%) learnt to speak in childhood, 39.82% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
