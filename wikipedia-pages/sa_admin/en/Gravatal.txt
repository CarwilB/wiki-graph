Located in a mountainous area,<ref name="turismo"/> Gravatal has springs at 37&nbsp;°C whose water it is divided supplies four local hotels and an aquatic complex.<ref name=Luiz>{{cite web |last1=Antônio del Tedesco |first1=Luiz |title=Gravatal and neighborhood in SC offer thermal waters, mountains, sea and European air |url=https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/turismo/2018/11/gravatal-e-vizinhanca-em-sc-oferecem-aguas-termais-serra-mar-e-ar-europeu.shtml |access-date=30 April 2020 |language=pt |date=28 November 2018}}</ref> The [[spring water]] contains chemicals such as [[fluorine]] and [[lithium]].<ref name="turismo">{{cite web |title=GRAVATAL |url=http://turismo.sc.gov.br/en/cidade/gravatal/ |website=turismo.sc.gov.br |access-date=30 April 2020}}</ref>

Approximately 2,000 people live in the region of the city where the spas are located; in July up to 50 thousand tourists visit the area.<ref name=Luiz/>

==Culture==
800 meters from the centre of the Gravatal, is 'Ecopark Termas' built to promote ideas for sustainable development and well-being. It is 17,000 m<sup>2</sup>, within 7,500 m<sup>2</sup> of preserved forest RPPN (private natural heritage reserve), located on Rua Antônio Pedro Mendonça.<ref>{{cite web |title=Ecopark Thermal |url=http://ecoparktermas.blogspot.com/ |access-date=30 April 2020 |language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=ECOPARK THERMAL |url=http://quetalviajar.com/destinos-de-viagem/brasil/estado-santa-catarina/ecopark-termas-ecologia-e-sustentabilidade-em-gravatal.htm |website=quetalviajar.com |access-date=30 April 2020 |language=pt}}</ref>
