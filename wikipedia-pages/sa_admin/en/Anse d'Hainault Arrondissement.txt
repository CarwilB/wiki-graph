'''Anse d'Hainault''' ({{IPA|fr|ɑ̃s dɛno}}; also '''Anse-d'Ainault'''; {{langx|ht|Ansdeno}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] of the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse Department]] located in southwestern [[Haiti]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pcgn.org.uk/Toponymic%20factfile-Haiti-Jan10.pdf |title=Haiti |work=Toponymic Fact File |publisher=[[Permanent Committee on Geographical Names for British Official Use|Permanent Committee on Geographical Names]] |access-date=7 November 2010 }}</ref> As of 2015, the population was 98,522 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015 |date=March 2015 |website=IHSI |language=fr |trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf |archive-date=2015-11-06 |url-status=dead |access-date=8 May 2017 }}</ref> Postal codes in the Anse d'Hainault Arrondissement start with the number 72.

==Municipalities==
The arrondissement consists of the following municipalities:
* [[Anse d'Hainault]]
* [[Dame-Marie, Grand'Anse|Dame Marie]]
* [[Les Irois]]
