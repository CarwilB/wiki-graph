'''Coqueiral''' is a  [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the south of the Brazilian [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2010 the population was 9,289 in a total area of {{cvt|297|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/coqueiral/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The municipal seat lies at an elevation of 844 meters.  It became a municipality in 1948.

==Geography==
Coqueiral belongs to the IBGE statistical microregion of [[Varginha]].  It is located north of [[Varginha]] and [[Três Pontas]], west of [[Lavras]], and northeast of [[Alfenas]].  The regional center of [[Varginha]] is 57&nbsp;km. away.

==Economy==
The main economic activities are milk production, coffee growing (7,200 hectares in 2006), and the cultivation of beans, corn, and sugarcane. In 2006 there were 808 rural producers with total agricultural area of 22,649 hectares.  More than 2,000 persons worked in agriculture.  In the town itself there was one financial institution.

In the health sector there were 6 public clinics and 1 private clinic.  There were no hospitals, the closest hospital being in [[Três Pontas]], 31&nbsp;km. away.  In the educational sector there were 13 primary schools and 1 middle school.
