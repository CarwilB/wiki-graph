'''Augusto de Lima''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 4,851 living in a total area of 1,250&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/augusto-de-lima/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]]. It became a municipality in 1963.<ref name="ibge.gov.br">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Augusto de Lima is located at an elevation of 700 metres on highway BR-135 between [[Curvelo]] and [[Montes Claros]]. The nearest major population centre is [[Curvelo]]. The town began with the building of the railroad from Belo Horizonte to Montes Claros in 1910. The name is derived from the name of a writer and politician. See [[Augusto de Lima]].

The distance to [[Curvelo]] is 112&nbsp;km; and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 240&nbsp;km.  Neighboring  municipalities are:   [[Buenópolis]], [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]], [[Monjolos]], [[Corinto, Minas Gerais|Corinto]] and [[Lassance]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080410172032/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/curvelo/ |archive-date=2008-04-10 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services and agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was R$39 million, with 24 million from services, 2 million from industry, and 10 million from agriculture. There were 354 rural producers on 14,000 hectares of land. Only 8 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 800 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were mangoes, sugarcane, beans, and corn. There were 27,000 head of cattle (2006). There was 1 bank (2007) and 666 automobiles (366 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 14 inhabitants per automobile. 
<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

==Health and education==
There were 3 health clinics in 2005. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Bocaiúva, Montes Claros or Curvelo. Educational needs were met by 16 primary schools, 2 middle schools, and 9 pre-primary schools.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.690 (2000)
*State ranking: 642 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 3,319 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}} 
*Literacy rate: 77%
*Life expectancy: 65 (average of males and females)<ref name="frigoletto.com.br">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In 2000 the per capita income of R$149.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="frigoletto.com.br"/>
