'''  San Dionisio del Mar ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]].    
It is part of the [[Juchitán District]] in the east of the [[Istmo de Tehuantepec, Oaxaca|Istmo de Tehuantepec region]].
The town is named after its patron saint.<ref name=INF>{{cite web
 |title=San Dionisio del Mar
 |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20130a.htm
 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México
 |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]
 |accessdate=2010-07-28}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 237.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an elevation of 10 meters above sea level and includes a peninsula in the Gulf of Tehuantepec on the Pacific coast.
The climate is warm sub humid with summer rains.
Flora include bushes, mesquite, guanacaste and native grasslands.  
Wild fauna include iguana, armadillo, opossum, pigeon and rook.<ref name=INF/>
==Demography==
As of 2005, the municipality had 1,225 households with a total population of 5,165 of whom 2,639 spoke an indigenous language.<ref name=INF/>
The municipality is home to the [[Huave people]], who speak a unique language unrelated to any other.<ref>{{cite web
 |url=http://www.mexicantextiles.com/grouppages/huave.html
 |title=Huaves of Oaxaca
 |work=Mexican Textiles
 |accessdate=2010-07-28}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activity is fishing. 
The people also practice agriculture, growing maize, sesame, sorghum, squash and fruit, and keep cattle, horses and pigs.<ref name=INF/>
