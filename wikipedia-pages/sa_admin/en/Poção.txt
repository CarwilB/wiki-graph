'''Poção''' is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]]  of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 241&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 11,305 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/pocao/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]] state   ('''N''');  [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]]    ('''S''' and '''W''');  [[Jataúba]]   ('''E''').
* Area - 199.74&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 1000 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]]
* Annual average temperature - 19.5 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 241&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Poção''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[beans]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]] and [[poultry]].
