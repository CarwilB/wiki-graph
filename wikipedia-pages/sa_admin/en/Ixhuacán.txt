'''Ixhuacán''', or '''Ixhuacán de los Reyes''', is a city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].  It is located approximately {{convert|36|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Xalapa Enríquez]]; [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highway 123]] runs through it.  It is bordered by [[Teocelo]], [[Xico (Veracruz)|Xico]] and [[Coatepec, Veracruz|Coatepec]]. [[Coffee]] is the chief product of Ixhuacán de los Reyes.

It serves as the municipal seat for the [[Ixhuacán de los Reyes (municipality)|surrounding municipality of the same name]].

== History ==
In August 1519 [[Hernán Cortés]] passed through Ixhuacán on his way to [[Tenochtitlan]]. Both Cortes and his chronicler [[Francisco Lopez de Gomara]] described the town as having a fort.

== Climate ==
Ixhuacán de los Reyes has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cfb) with cool to mild weather year-round. It experiences heavy monsoonal precipitation from June to September.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 11,387 inhabitants in 141 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSIXHUACAN>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Ixhuac%C3%A1n-de-los-Reyes.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Ixhuacán de los Reyes CENSUS]</ref> About 20% resides in the municipal seat (2,692 hab.). Other localities includes Monte Grande (926 hab.), Barranca Nueva (808 hab.), Tlalchy (625 hab.) and Caltzontepec (554 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSIXHUACAN/>
