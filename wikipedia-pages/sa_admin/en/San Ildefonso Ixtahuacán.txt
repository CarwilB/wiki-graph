'''San Ildefonso Ixtahuacán''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]], situated at 1580 metres above [[sea level]], with the town of Ixtahuacán as the municipal seat. The municipality has a population of 44,424 (2018 census) and covers an area of {{Convert|135|km2|abbr=on}}. The annual festival is January 20–25.
