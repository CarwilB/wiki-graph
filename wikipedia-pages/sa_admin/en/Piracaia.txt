'''Piracaia''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in Brazil. According to the [[2022 Brazilian census]], it had a population of 26,029, a population density of 67.51 inhabitants per square kilometre, and an area of {{convert|385.64|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}; its population was estimated at 26,795 in 2025.<ref name="IBGE-profile">{{cite web |title=Piracaia |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/sp/piracaia.html |website=Cidades e Estados |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |language=pt-BR |access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality was created in 1859 as Santo Antônio da Cachoeira, and its name was changed to Piracaia on 20 August 1906.<ref name="IBGE-history">{{cite web |title=Piracaia, São Paulo: Histórico |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/biblioteca/visualizacao/dtb/saopaulo/piracaia.pdf |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |language=pt-BR |format=PDF |access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>
