'''Chepo''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepo District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 20,420 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Chepo District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 8,971; its population as of 2000 was 12,734.<ref name=inec/>  It is located five kilometers south of the [[Inter-American Highway]].<ref name=moon>Friar, William. ''Panama''. [[Moon Publications]] (2008), p. 422. {{ISBN|1-59880-085-X}}.</ref> It is a sprawling town that is nearly a suburb of [[Panama City]].<ref name=moon/> It contains a gas station, hospital, ATM, and a variety of stores.<ref name=moon/> The Panamanian Minister of Public Work announced a new highway expansion connecting Pacora to Chepo <ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.mop.gob.pa/ministro-arosemena-anuncia-ampliacion-de-la-carretera-pacora-chepo/|title = }}</ref>

The [[Darién Gap]] formerly began at Chepo, where the [[Panamerican Highway]] ended; after jungle, it begins again at [[Turbo, Colombia]]. Chepo was formerly a transshipment point and base for adventure travel outfitters.  Since completion of the {{convert|230|km}} paved highway to [[Yaviza]] about 2008, most of this economic activity has moved to [[Metetí]] or Yaviza.
