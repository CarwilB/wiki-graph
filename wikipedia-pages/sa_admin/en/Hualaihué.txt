'''Hualaihué''' ({{IPA|es|walajˈwe}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in [[Palena Province]], [[Los Lagos Region]]. The communal capital is the town of [[Hornopirén]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Hualaihué spans an area of {{convert|2787.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 8,273 inhabitants (4,457 men and 3,816 women). Of these, 2,406 (29.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 5,867 (70.9%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 2.1% (169 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Hualaihué is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Freddy Ibacache Muñoz ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Hualaihué is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gabriel Ascencio ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Alejandro Santana ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 58th electoral district, together with [[Castro, Chile|Castro]], [[Ancud]], [[Quemchi]], [[Dalcahue]], [[Curaco de Vélez]], [[Quinchao]], [[Puqueldón]], [[Chonchi]], [[Queilén]], [[Quellón]], [[Chaitén]], [[Futaleufú, Chile|Futaleufú]] and [[Palena, Chile|Palena]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Kuschel Silva (RN) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
