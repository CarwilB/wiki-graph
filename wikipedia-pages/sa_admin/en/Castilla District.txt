'''Castilla District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Piura Province|Piura]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref> The district was created in 1861 during the presidency of [[Ramón Castilla]]. In 2007, it had a population of 120,000.<ref>[http://www.citypopulation.de/php/peru.php?cityid=200104 city population]</ref>
