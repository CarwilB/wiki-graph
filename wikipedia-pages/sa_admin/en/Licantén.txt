'''Licantén''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] within the Licantén [[Communes of Chile|commune]], administered by the [[Municipality]] of Licantén within the [[Curicó Province]] in the [[Maule Region]] of [[Chile]]. The commune also include the coastal town of [[Iloca, Chile|Iloca]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Licantén spans an area of {{convert|273.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 6,902 inhabitants (3,654 men and 3,248 women). Of these, 3,974 (57.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,928 (42.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 8.8% (557 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Licantén is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Héctor Quiero Palacios ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Licantén is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Roberto León ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Celso Morales (UDI) as part of the 36th electoral district, together with [[Curicó]], [[Teno, Chile|Teno]], [[Romeral]], [[Molina, Chile|Molina]], [[Sagrada Familia, Chile|Sagrada Familia]], [[Hualañé]], [[Vichuquén]] and [[Rauco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
