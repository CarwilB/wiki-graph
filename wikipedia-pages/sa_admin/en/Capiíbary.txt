'''Capiibary''' is a [[town]] in the [[San Pedro Department, Paraguay|San Pedro]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]].

== Etymology ==
The city gets its name from the Capiibary River, which was named Capiĭbarĭ (compound from ''capiĭbá·r'' "[[capybara]]" and ''ĭ'' "water, river") in [[Classical Guarani]] by the natives due to the abundance of these animals in the river. With time, the original pronunciation got mixed together with the Spanish reading of the name, thus producing the final form "Kapi'ivary," even in modern [[Guarani language|Guarani]].
