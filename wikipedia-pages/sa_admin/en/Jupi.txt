'''Jupi''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒuˈpi]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]] (PE), [[Brazil]].

As of 2025, the estimated population was 16,022.<ref name="IBGE" />
