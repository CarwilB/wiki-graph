'''Imías''' is a municipality and town in the [[Guantánamo Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located on the southern coast of Cuba, bordering the [[Caribbean Sea]] to the south.

==Geography==
The municipality of Imías borders with [[San Antonio del Sur]], [[Baracoa]] and [[Maisí]]. The town is crossed by the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]], a west–east highway spanning the length of Cuba, and counts [[Imías Airport|a little airport]].<ref>[http://airportdatabase.net/cuba/imias-airport_46384.html Imías Airport on Airport Database website]</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Imías had a population of 21,005.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|524|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|40.0|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
