'''Ascope''' is a province in the [[La Libertad Region]], [[Peru]]. It is bordered by the province of [[Trujillo Province, Peru|Trujillo]] on the south; the province of [[Pacasmayo Province|Pacasmayo]] on the north; the provinces of [[Contumazá Province|Contumazá]], [[Otuzco Province|Otuzco]], and [[Gran Chimú Province|Gran Chimú]] on the east; and the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the west. Its capital is [[Ascope]].

From the hierarchical point of view of the Catholic Church, it is part of the Archdiocese of Trujillo.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.iglesiacatolica.org.pe/cep_mapas/mapas_je/trujillo.htm |title=Arzobispado de Trujillo |trans-title=Archbishopric of Trujillo |language=es |website=www.iglesiacatolica.org.pe |access-date=25 June 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081103003437/http://www.iglesiacatolica.org.pe/cep_mapas/mapas_je/trujillo.htm |archive-date=2008-11-03}}</ref>
