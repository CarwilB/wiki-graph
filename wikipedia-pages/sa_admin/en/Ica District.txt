'''Ica District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of [[Ica Province|Ica]] in [[Regions of Peru|region]] of [[Ica Region|Ica]] within [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

The Province capital city of [[Ica, Peru|Ica]] is located in this district. Ica is also the capital of the entire Region of Ica.  Also, close by is the tourist oasis village of [[Huacachina]].
