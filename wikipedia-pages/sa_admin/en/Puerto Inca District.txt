'''Puerto Inca District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Puerto Inca Province|Puerto Inca]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>            On [[Christmas Eve|December 24]] 1971, LANSA sole Lockheed Electra [[LANSA Flight 508|crashed]] in the district. All but [[Juliane Koepcke|one]] of the people on board were killed.
