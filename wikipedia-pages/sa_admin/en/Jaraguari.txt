'''Jaraguari''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 7,342&nbsp;(2021) and its area is {{cvt|2913|km2}}.

==History== 
It was first settled by Brazilians in 1875 when [[Porfirio Alberto de Freitas]], his wife, and his brother in-law settled into Jaraguari Velho (then called Fazenda Estiva). In January 1909, [[José Thomaz Barbosa]] would draw up the borders of the municipality. Later, de Freitas ordered the construction of a church. It was only completed in 1923. It had been named Senhor Divino Espírito Santo, and was destroyed in 1930. 
After that, Japanese settlers arrived and created a separate community.
In 1953, Jaraguari was made a municipality. The name was derived from Araguari, the old hometown of de Freitas. A J was added because Jaragua means grass in [[Brazilian Portuguese]].<ref name="Dados Históricos Do Município">{{cite web |title=DADOS HISTÓRICOS DO MUNICÍPIO |url=https://jaraguari.ms.gov.br/pagina/78_Historia-da-cidade.html |website=Jaraguari Prefeiturea |access-date=17 October 2021}}</ref>

==Deforestation==
Jaraguari is largely covered by forests, but has suffered 1.5% forest lost in the past 20 years, with much of this picking up pace in the 2010s.<ref name="Global Forest Watch">{{cite web |title=Jaraguari, Mato Grosso Do Sul, Brazil Deforestation Rates & Statistics |url=https://www.globalforestwatch.org/dashboards/country/BRA/11/43/?category=forest-change |website=Global Forest Watch |access-date=17 October 2021}}</ref>
