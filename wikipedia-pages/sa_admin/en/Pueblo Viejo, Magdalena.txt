'''Puebloviejo''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. It was founded in 1526 by friar Tomas Ortíz. It was erected a municipality on May 3, 1929. The town lies on a natural thin division between the [[Ciénaga Grande de Santa Marta]] marshes and the [[Caribbean Sea]].
