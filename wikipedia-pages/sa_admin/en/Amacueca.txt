''' Amacueca  ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  124.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 5,065.<ref>{{cite web|title=Amacueca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14004a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 13, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050101015124/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14004a.htm|archive-date=2005-01-01|url-status=dead}}</ref>
