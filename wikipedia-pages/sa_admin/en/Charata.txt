'''Charata''' is a city in the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Chaco Province|Chaco]], Argentina. It has 26497 inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2010}}, and is the head town of the [[Chacabuco Department, Chaco|Chacabuco Department]] and the most important city in the southwest of Chaco, located 280&nbsp;km from the provincial capital [[Resistencia, Chaco|Resistencia]].

== History ==
The city was founded by provincial decree on 4 October 1914.

Charata is the important settlement closest to the [[Campo del Cielo crater]] [[meteorite|meteoric]] dispersion (originated by the impact of a large metallic meteoroid, probably around 3800 BCE).
