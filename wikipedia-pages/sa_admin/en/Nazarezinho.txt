'''Nazarezinho''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraíba]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region]] of [[Brazil]]. As of the year 2021, its total population is 7,271.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nazarezinho (PB) {{!}} Cidades e Estados {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/pb/nazarezinho.html |access-date=2023-05-15 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |date=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |accessdate=December 17, 2009 |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20%28pelos%20dados%20de%202000%29.htm |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Geography ==
Nazarezinho is located on the western part of [[Paraíba]], to the south of Rio Piranhas. Its average elevation is 356 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nazarezinho topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-1rl1rr/Nazarezinho/ |access-date=2023-05-15 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Nazarezinho has a [[Hot semi-arid climate|Hot Semi-arid Climate]] (BSh). It sees the most amount of rainfall in March, with 137 mm of average precipitation; and the least in September, with 1 mm of average precipitation.
