'''Gros-Morne''' ({{IPA|fr|ɡʁo mɔʁn}}; {{langx|ht|Gwo Mòn}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Gros-Morne Arrondissement]], in the [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 155,692 inhabitants (2015). Gros-Morne is a rural city in Haiti. Its name means "Big Mountain."
 
==Economy==
