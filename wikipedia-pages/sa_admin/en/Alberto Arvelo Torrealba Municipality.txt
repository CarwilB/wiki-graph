The '''Alberto Arvelo Torrealba Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 41,232.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Geregistreerd via Argeweb |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref>  The town of [[Sabaneta, Barinas|Sabaneta]] is the [[County seat]] of the Alberto Arvelo Torrealba Municipality. The municipality is named for [[Alberto Arvelo Torrealba]].

==History==
The town of Sabaneta was founded by Juan de Alhama in 1787.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Economy==
The main industry in this municipality is the production of sugar.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
The Alberto Arvelo Torrealba Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 36,363 (up from 33,190 in 2000).  This amounts to 4.8% of the state's population.  The municipality's population density is {{convert|47.3|PD/sqkm}}.

==Government==
The mayor of the Alberto Arvelo Torrealba Municipality is Anibal José Chávez Frías, elected on October 31, 2004, with 99% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=9&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |access-date=2023-02-16 |title=Resultados generales del Municipio ALBERTO ARVELO TORREALBA |website=National Electoral Council }}</ref>  He replaced Noel Zamudia shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones Presidenciales 2006: Resultados |website=CNE Venezuela |access-date=15 March 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archive-date=2 October 2006}}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Sabaneta and Rodríguez Domínguez.
