'''San Bernardo''' ({{IPA|es|sam beɾˈnaɾðo}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
San Bernando has a warm [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with moderate to heavy rainfall year-round. Like most of Andean Nariño Department but unlike most places with a subtropical highland climate, San Bernando has a marked summer minimum in rainfall instead of the more usual winter minimum.
