'''Ospino''' is a town in the [[Venezuela]]n [[States of Venezuela|state]] of [[Portuguesa (state)|Portuguesa]].  This town is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Ospino Municipality and, according to the 2001 Venezuelan [[census]], the municipality has a population of 39,215.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gov.ve/censo/fichascenso/tiro.asp?cod_entidad=18 Primeros Resultados XIII Censo General de Población y Vivienda<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825040625/http://www.ine.gov.ve/censo/fichascenso/tiro.asp?cod_entidad=18 |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
