'''Mucuri''' is a city in the state of [[Bahia]] in [[Brazil]]. The estimated population in 2020 is 42,251 inhabitants. It is the southernmost city in Bahia as well as Brazil's [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast]] region, and the only one from that state to border [[Espírito Santo]].

The city contains part of the {{convert|1,503.75|ha|adj=on}} [[Córrego Grande Biological Reserve]].
The climate is tropical, warm and mostly humid, with one or two dry months.
Average annual temperature is {{convert|22|to|24|C}}, and average annual rainfall is {{convert|1250|to|1500|mm}}.<ref>{{citation|url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-grande/|title=Reserva Biológica do Córrego Grande|work=Viarural|access-date=2016-04-15|language=pt|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160423125035/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-grande/|archive-date=2016-04-23|url-status=usurped}}</ref>

Mucuri is served by [[Mucuri Airport|Max Feffer Airport]].
