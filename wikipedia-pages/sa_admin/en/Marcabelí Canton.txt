'''Marcabelí Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[El Oro Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Marcabelí]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 4,930.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/|title=Resultados}}</ref>
*[[Mestizo]]  85.6%
*[[White Latin American|White]]  5.6%
*Montubio  5.2%
*[[Afro-Ecuadorian]]  3.4%
*[[Indigenous peoples in Ecuador|Indigenous]]  0.1%
*Other  0.1%
