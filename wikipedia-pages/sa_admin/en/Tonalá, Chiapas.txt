'''Tonalá''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]] in southern [[Mexico]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 84,594,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Tonalá|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=097|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 78,438 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tonalá|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07097a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas|access-date=August 18, 2008}}</ref> It covers an area of 1853{{nbs}}km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the city of Tonalá had a population of 35,322.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Tonalá, the municipality had 960 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: Paredón (6,126), Tres Picos (4,403), Cabeza de Toro (3,413), classified as urban, and [[Manuel Ávila Camacho (Ponte Duro), Chiapas|Manuel Ávila Camacho (Ponte Duro)]] (1,778), [[Ignacio Ramírez, Chiapas|Ignacio Ramírez]] (1,689), [[Huizachal, Chiapas|Huizachal]] (1,421), [[Doctor Belisario Domínguez (La Barra), Chiapas|Doctor Belisario Domínguez (La Barra)]] (1,043), [[San Luqueño, Chiapas|San Luqueño]] (1,016), and [[Morelos (Mojarras), Chiapas|Morelos (Mojarras)]] (1,010), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
