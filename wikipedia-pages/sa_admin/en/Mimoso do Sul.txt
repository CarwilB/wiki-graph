'''Mimoso do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 26,115 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|867|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|10459|ha}} [[Serra das Torres Natural Monument]], created in 2010 to protect the mountain peaks in the area.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese|last=Hartung Gomes|first=Paulo Cesar|title=Lei Nº 9.463|date=11 June 2010|url=http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|accessdate=2016-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161028020315/http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|archive-date=28 October 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
