'''Santa Tereza''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil. It was raised to municipality status in 1992, the area being taken out of the municipalities of [[Bento Gonçalves, Rio Grande do Sul|Bento Gonçalves]], [[Garibaldi, Rio Grande do Sul|Garibaldi]] and [[Roca Sales]].<ref name="lei9627">{{Cite web|url=http://leisestaduais.com.br/rs/lei-ordinaria-n-9627-1992-rio-grande-do-sul-cria-o-municipio-de-santa-tereza|title=Lei Nº 9627: Cria o Município de Santa Tereza|publisher=Leis Estaduais|lang=pt|date=20 March 1992}}</ref>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 1,726.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/santa-tereza/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
