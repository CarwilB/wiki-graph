'''Paramacca''', also known as '''Pamacca'''<ref name="planning"/> ({{langx|djk|Paamakaliba}} or just {{lang|djk|Paamaka}}),<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://suriname-languages.sil.org/Aukan/English/AukanEngDictIndex.html|title=Aukan–English Dictionary|publisher=[[SIL Global]]}}</ref> is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Sipaliwini District]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.surinameview.com/districten/|title=Districten|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname View}}</ref> The population is estimated between 1,500 and 2,000 people.<ref name="parbode"/> In 1983, the [[Sipaliwini District]] was created, and the eastern part became the resort of [[Tapanahony]].<ref name="suriname_nu">{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/sipaliwini01.html|title=Distrikt Sipaliwini|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref> The Paramacca resort is the northern part of Tapanahony, and mainly inhabited by the [[Paramaccan people]],<ref name="suriname_nu"/><ref name="gov_sr">{{cite web|url=http://regionaldevelopment.gov.sr/actueel/2019/paamaka-en-ndyuka-leggen-grens-vast/|title=Paamaka en Ndyuka leggen grens vast|website=Regional Development.gov.sr|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=28 January 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220128031825/https://regionaldevelopment.gov.sr/actueel/2019/paamaka-en-ndyuka-leggen-grens-vast/|url-status=dead}}</ref> the border of the resorts is the island of Bofoo Tabiki in the [[Marowijne River]].<ref name="gov_sr"/>

The administrative centre of the resort is located in [[Snesiekondre]], and was completed in 2012.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.starnieuws.com/index.php/welcome/index/nieuwsitem/2215|title=Bestuurscentra Sipaliwini bouwtechnisch opgeleverd|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Star Nieuws}}</ref> The District Commissioner for the resort is Margaretha Malontie.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.gfcnieuws.com/margaretha-malontie-nieuwe-deken-districtscommissarissen/|title=Margaretha Malontie nieuwe deken districtscommissarissen|website=GFC Nieuws|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The Paramaccans were runaway slaves who had made a peace treaty with the [[Surinam (Dutch colony)|Colony]] in 1872.<ref>{{Cite conference|title=Conference on Slavery, Indentured Labour, Migration, Diaspora and Identity Formation.|author=J. Marten and W. Schalkwijk|date=19 June 2018|publisher=[[Anton de Kom University]]|quote=Original publication:Koloniaal Verslag van 1872 page 543}}</ref> In 1879, a group of about 90 Paramaccans led by Apensa created a settlement on an island in the [[Marowijne River]] near the mouth of the Paramacca Creek. The town was named [[Langatabiki]] (Long Island).<ref name="encycl">{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 155 - Boschnegers|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The resort of Paramacca consists of the current area settled by the Paramaccans.<ref name="suriname_nu"/>

==Villages==
The resort is made up of 13 [[tribe|tribal]] villages which are mainly located on the Lower [[Marowijne River]], and is home to the non-tribal village of [[Stoelmanseiland]]. The main village of the resort is Langatabiki which is the residence of the [[granman]] of the Paramaccan people.<ref name="planning"/>

[[Medische Zending]] operates health care clinics in Langatabiki, [[Nason, Suriname|Nason]], and Stoelmanseiland.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|title=Zorggebied|website=Medische Zending.sr|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=30 September 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220930070719/http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Schools are present in Gakaba, Langatabiki, Nason, and Stoelmanseiland.<ref name="planning"/>

==Economy==
A major part of the economy of Paramacca is [[gold prospecting]] by [[Newmont]]. [[Ecotourism]] has become an important addition in the early 21st century.<ref name="parbode">{{cite web|url=https://parbode.com/dc-malontie-paamaka-op-weg-naar-ontwikkeling-sneak-peek/|title=Dc Malontie: 'Paamaka op weg naar ontwikkeling'- Sneak Peek|website=Parbode|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Holiday resorts have opened near the Armina waterfalls, and Stoelmanseiland.<ref name="planning"/> [[Cassave]] is the main agricultural crop.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.starnieuws.com/index.php/welcome/index/nieuwsitem/392|title=Grondenrechten vraagstuk belangrijk voor granman Forster |website=Star Nieuws|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Transport==
The area can be reached by car via an [[unpaved road]] between [[Moengo]] and Langatabiki, from where there is access to the [[East-West Link (Suriname)|East-West Link]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.waterkant.net/suriname/2018/06/10/deel-van-weg-naar-langatabiki-in-zeer-slechte-staat/?cn-reloaded=1|title=Deel van weg naar Langatabiki in zeer slechte staat|website=Waterkant.net|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Plans have been developed to pave the road to [[Moengo]], and built one road from Langatabiki via [[Stoelmanseiland]] to [[Benzdorp]], and another from Langatabiki to [[Brokopondo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://spinlab.vu.nl/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Final_ChristiaanJolly1707736.pdf|title=Ruimtegebruiksmodelleren|date=August 2010|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=[[Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam]]}}</ref>

Paramacca is served by [[Langatabbetje Airstrip]] and [[Stoelmans Eiland Airstrip]] offering [[Blue Wing]] scheduled services from [[Paramaribo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://bluewingairlines.com/|title=Blue Wing Airlines|access-date=23 May 2020|website=Blue Wing Airlines}}</ref>
