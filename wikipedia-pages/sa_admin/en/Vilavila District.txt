'''Vilavila''' (in Hispanicized spelling) or '''Wila Wila''' ([[Aymara language|Aymara]] ''wila'' red, blood-red,<ref>{{Ref Bertonio}}</ref> the [[reduplication]] signifies that there is a group or a complex of something, "a complex of red color") is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Lampa Province|Lampa]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Pariwana]] at {{convert|5022 |m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL maps of the Lampa Province, Puno Region</ref>

* Llanqirani
* Pukarani
* Salla Pata
* Wallatani
* [[Wisa Wisa (Lampa-Melgar)|Wisa Wisa]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (98.12%) learnt to speak in childhood, while only 1.25% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
