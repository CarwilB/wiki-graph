'''Biobío Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Biobío}} {{IPA|es|ˌbi.oˈβi.o|}}) is one of three [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Bio Bío Region|Biobío]] (VIII). Its capital is [[Los Ángeles, Bío Bío|Los Ángeles]] (2002 pop. 94,716). It is bounded on the north, west and south by the provinces of [[Concepción Province, Chile|Concepción]], [[Arauco Province|Arauco]] and [[Malleco Province|Malleco]], respectively, and on the east by [[Argentina]]. It has an area of {{convert|14,987.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} of well-wooded and mountainous country, and exports timber to a large extent.<ref name=EB1911>{{EB1911|inline=1 |wstitle=Bio-Bio (province)|display=Bio-Bio |volume=3 |page=952}}</ref>  The population is 373,981 according to the [[census]] of 2012.
 
The great trunk railway from [[Santiago]] south to [[Puerto Montt]] crosses the western part of the province and also connects it with the port of [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]]. [[Los Ángeles, Bío Bío|Los Ángeles]] lies {{convert|25|km|0|abbr=on}} east of this railway and is connected with it by a branch line.<ref name=EB1911/>

==History==
{{See also|Isla del Laja}}
The Province of Bio-Bío was created on October 13, 1875, as part of the province of Araucan. In 1887, President [[José Manuel Balmaceda]] set aside the [[Malleco Province|Province of Malleco]].  The province was then named for the [[Biobío River]] which flows through it. The Province of Bio-Bío was divided, into three departments:

==Administration==
As a province, Biobío is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]] of Chile, governed by a provincial [[governor]] who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]].

==Geography and demography==
According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|14987.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> and had a population of 353,315 inhabitants (176,960 men and 176,355 women), giving it a population density of {{convert|23.6|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. It is the tenth most populated province in the country. Of these, 245,775 (69.6%) lived in urban areas and 107,540 (30.4%) in rural areas. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 9.1% (29,405 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Bio Bío Valley wine region==
The Bio Bío Valley wine region is located in the [[Provinces of Chile|province]] and [[Regions of Chile|region]] of the same name. One of Chile's southern wine regions, it has become known for its crisp, aromatic wines.<ref>[http://www.wine-searcher.com/regions-bio+bio+valley Bio Bío Valley Wine], wine-searcher.com. 21 August 2013. Retrieved 5 November 2013.</ref>
The region is located at a latitude of 36°S, similar to [[Andalusia|southern Spain]] and [[Monterey]] in California. The majority of its vineyards lie between {{convert|50|m}} and {{convert|200|m}} above sea level with a moderate [[Mediterranean climate]]. It receives {{convert|1,275|mm}} of rain per year, among the highest of all Chilean wine valleys, although winds prevent excessive humidity - a phenomenon that can also be observed in [[Hauts-de-France|northern France]]. For most of the 20th century, the main varieties grown in the Bio Bío valley were [[Moscatel de Alejandria]] and [[Pais]] (known as Missiones in USA), but today, [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], and [[Sauvignon Blanc]] are also grown throughout the valley.{{citation needed|date=December 2022}}

The UK's [[Co-op Food|Co-Op]]'s Bio Bío Valley [[Malbec]] (2014) was awarded silver at the International Wine Challenge and a bronze from ''[[Decanter (magazine)|Decanter]]'' magazine.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.telegraph.co.uk/food-and-drink/wine/susy-atkins-why-the-co-operatives-truly-irresistible-wines-are-e/|title=Susy Atkins: why the Co-operative's Truly Irresistible wines are exactly that|first=Susy|last=Atkins|date=29 June 2016|access-date=18 June 2019|website=telegraph.co.uk}}</ref>
