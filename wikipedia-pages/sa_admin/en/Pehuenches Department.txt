'''Pehuenches''' is a department located in the northeast of [[Neuquén Province]], Argentina.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pehuenches Department, Neuquén Province, Argentina |url=https://www.mindat.org/loc-239785.html |access-date=16 August 2024 |website=mindat.org}}</ref>

==Geography==
The department limits at north with [[Mendoza province]], at northeast with [[La Pampa Province]] and at east with [[Rio Negro Province]], [[Añelo Department]] at south, [[Loncopué Department]] at South-SouthWest, [[Picún Leufú Department]] in the southwest, Minas Department in the northwest.

== Population ==
According to the 2022 Argentine [[Census|National Census]], Pehuenches had a total population of 29,753 people and [[population density]] of 3.6 people per square kilometer.<ref>{{Citation |title=Censo Nacional de 2022 de República Argentina |trans-title=2022 National Census of the Republic of Argentina |trans-chapter=National Census of Population, Houses, and Housing 2022 Table 2.15. |url=https://censo.gob.ar/index.php/datos_definitivos_neuquen/ |chapter=Censo Nacional de Población Hogares y Viviendas 2022, Cuadro 2.15. |chapter-url=https://censo.gob.ar/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/c2022_neuquen_est_c2_15.xlsx |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos de República Argentina |language=es}}</ref>
