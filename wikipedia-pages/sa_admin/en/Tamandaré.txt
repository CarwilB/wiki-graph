'''Tamandarë''' ([[Brazilian Portuguese]]: ''Tamandaré''; [[Old Tupi]]: ''Tamandûaré'', lit. "different") is a coastal [[municipality]] about {{convert|103|km|mi|abbr=on}} south of [[Recife]], the capital city of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Pernambuco]].
[[File:Tamandaré Pernanbuco.jpg|thumb|Tamandaré beach]]
[[File:tamandare beach.jpg|thumb]]

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Rio Formoso]] and [[Sirinhaém]]  ('''N''');  [[Barreiros, Pernambuco|Barreiros]]  ('''S''');  [[Água Preta]]  ('''W'''); [[Atlantic Ocean]]   ('''E''')
* Area - {{convert|190|km2}}
* Elevation - 8 m
* Vegetation - [[Coconut tree]]s and Atlantic forest
* Clima - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.3 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 103&nbsp;km

The municipality contains part of the strictly protected [[Saltinho Biological Reserve]], a {{convert|562|ha}} conservation unit created in 1983.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Saltinho|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=198|access-date=2016-04-17}}</ref>

==Beaches==

{{further|List of beaches in Pernambuco}}
*'''Tamandaré beach'''
Urbanized, has two kilometers long of [[sand]] and a number of tourist facilities. Has small waves and fine sand. Easy to reach from [[Recife]] and [[Caruaru]].

*'''Boca da Barra beach'''
At [[low tide]] is formed [[tide pool|natural pools]]. Suitable for [[open water swimming|swimming]], has a dense [[mangrove]] vegetation in the [[estuary]] of Canoa quebrada River. It's possible to reach the Mamocambilhas beach in [[Barreiros, Pernambuco|Barreiros]] by walking.

*'''Campas beach'''
Three kilometers long, is good for [[open water swimming|swimming]], and in front of the Marinas hotel, the [[ocean|sea]] allows natural [[anchor]]ages of [[boat]]s. It is possible to rent equipment such as [[jetski]], banana boats and [[boat]]s.

*'''Carneiros beach'''
Still almost deserted, just has a few [[summer]] houses and bars. It has five kilometers of [[landscape]] mixed between the [[reef]]s, [[coconut tree]]s and low waves [[water]].

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Tamandaré are based in tourism, [[artisanal fishing]], food and beverage industry and agriculture especially [[coconut]]s.
