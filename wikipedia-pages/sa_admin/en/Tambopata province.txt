'''Tambopata''' is the largest of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Madre de Dios Region]] of [[Peru]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into four [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

* [[Inambari District|Inambari]]<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/06/03/ejercito-del-peru-militares-son-grabados-mientras-cobraban-cupos-a-mineros-ilegales-durante-operativo-en-madre-de-dios/|title=Ejército del Perú: Militares son grabados mientras cobraban cupos a mineros ilegales durante operativo en Madre de Dios|first=Manuel|last=Rojas Berríos|work=[[Infobae]]|date=3 June 2025|access-date=11 December 2025|language=es}}</ref>
* [[Laberinto District|Laberinto]]
* [[Las Piedras District|Las Piedras]]
* [[Tambopata District|Tambopata]]

===Representation===
This province has one communal representative to the regional government.
