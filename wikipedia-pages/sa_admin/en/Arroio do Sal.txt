'''Arroio do Sal''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is about 30&nbsp;km south of [[Torres, Rio Grande do Sul|Torres]] and 30&nbsp;km north of [[Capão da Canoa]], thus in about the northern half of the state's shoreline. It is 175&nbsp;km from [[Porto Alegre]], with which it is connected via BR 101 and BR 290.

Though the population is only about 10,000, during the summer tourism season the number of people increases to about 90,000. It includes 27&nbsp;km of the Atlantic coast and has about 60 beaches.
