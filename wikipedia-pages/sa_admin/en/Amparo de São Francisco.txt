'''Amparo de São Francisco''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ɐ̃ˈpaɾʊ ˈdi ˈsɐ̃w fɾɐ̃ˈsiskʊ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 2,380 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/amparo-do-sao-francisco/panorama |access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|35|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
