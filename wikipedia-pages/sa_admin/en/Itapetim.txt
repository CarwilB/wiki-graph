'''Itapetim''' is the northernmost city in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 14,236 inhabitants and the total area is 411.87 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
Itapetim is located in the [[Sertão]] sub-region, and has the northenmost point in the [[mainland]] Pernambuco: The village of Lagoa de Dentro,<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-09-29 |language=pt-BR |title=Sitio Lagoa de Dentro, Itapetim - PE |url=https://informacoesdobrasil.com.br/rua/pe/itapetim/sitio-lagoa-de-dentro+6299 |website=Informações do Brasil}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> also called Fava de Cheiro (2010 IBGE census)<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-09-29 |date=2010 |language=pt-BR |title=Mapa de Itapetim (Censo IBGE de 2010) |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_para_fins_de_levantamentos_estatisticos/censo_demografico_2010/mapas_municipais_estatisticos/pe/itapetim_v2.pdf |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> or Tombador (2022 IBGE census).<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-09-29 |date=2022 |language=pt-BR |title=Mapa de Itapetim (Censo IBGE de 2022) |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_municipais/estimativas_populacionais/2022/PE/itapetim_v1.pdf |website=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> The municipality of Itapetim is formed by three main districts: the headquarters district, São Vicente and [[Piedade do Ouro]].{{efn|Piedade do Ouro is classified as a district by the Itapetim Municipal Prefecture, but it is not by IBGE}}<ref>{{Cite web |last=julianosbc |date=2023-01-09 |title=Itapetim |url=https://tomeconta.tce.pe.gov.br/itapetim/#:~:text=Seus%20distritos%20s%C3%A3o%20Itapetim%20sede,%20S%C3%A3o%20Vicente%20e%20Piedade |access-date=2024-09-29 |website=TomeConta |language=pt-BR}}</ref> 

Itapetim borders [[Paraíba]] state    ('''N''' and '''E''');   [[São José do Egito]]   ('''S''');   [[São José do Egito]] and [[Brejinho]]   ('''W'''). According to the 2022 IBGE census, its area is 411.9 km². Itapetim also has an elevation of 637 meters and is crossed by the [[Pajeú River]]. It has a [[Semi-arid climate|semi-arid]] and its biome is the [[Caatinga]] ([[Deserts and xeric shrublands|xeric shrublands]]).

==Economy==

[[File:Distrito de Piedade - Itapetim 02.jpg|thumb|Group of abandoned houses on an old small farm in Piedade district, Itapetim municipality.|left]] Main economic activity in '''Itapetim''' is [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[maize|corn]], and [[beans]].
