'''Santa Elena Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Santa Elena Province]].  Its capital is the city of [[Santa Elena, Ecuador|Santa Elena]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 144,076.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
