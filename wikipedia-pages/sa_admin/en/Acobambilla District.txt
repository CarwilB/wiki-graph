== Geography ==
There are a couple of large lakes in the district like [[Anqasqucha (Huancavelica)|Anqasqucha]], Astuqucha, [[Chiliqucha]], Chunchuqucha, [[Kanllaqucha]], [[Milluqucha]], [[Papaqucha (Huancavelica)|Papaqucha]], [[Warmiqucha]], and [[Ñawinqucha (Huancavelica)|Ñawinqucha]].
