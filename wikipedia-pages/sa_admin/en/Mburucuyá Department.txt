'''Mburucuyá Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,012 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|961|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Mburucuyá, Corrientes|Mburucuyá]], which is located around {{cvt|1,010|km}} from [[Capital Federal]] (Buenos Aires).
