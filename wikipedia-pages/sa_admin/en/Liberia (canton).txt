'''Liberia''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Liberia District|Liberia]] district.

== History ==
Liberia was created on 7 December 1848 by decree 167.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
[[Image:Cantón de Liberia.jpg|thumb|left|Map of Liberia canton]]
Liberia has an area of {{formatnum:1436.47}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:193}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
Prominent geologic features of Liberia include Cerro Cacao (Cacao Mountain) and [[Rincón de la Vieja]]. The latter is the center of the [[Rincón de la Vieja Volcano National Park]]. The canton also includes the most visited portion of [[Santa Rosa National Park]] on its northwest border. The Río Salto delineates the southwestern border as far as the [[Tempisque River]], and the Tempisque forms the border on the southeast as far as the Bahía Naranjo (Orange Bay).
