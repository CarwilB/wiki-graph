'''Najasa''' ({{IPA|es|naˈxasa}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Najasa had a population of 14,732.<ref name="citypopulation2">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|885.32|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |author=citypopulation.de |year=2015 |title=Population of Najasa (Municipality) |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/cuba-admin.php?adm2id=3012 |accessdate=March 5, 2017}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|17.6|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
