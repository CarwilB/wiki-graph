'''Guachipas''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|2785|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north and northeast with [[Metán Department]], to the east with the Departments of [[La Candelaria Department|La Candelaria]] and [[Rosario de la Frontera Department|Rosario de la Frontera]], to the south with [[Cafayate Department|Cafayate]] and [[Tucumán Province]], and to the west with [[La Viña Department]].
