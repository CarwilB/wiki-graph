'''Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 47,878.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls]{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Barinitas]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Bolívar Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip]{{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Bolívar Municipality (disambiguation)|Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].

==Demographics==
The Bolívar Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 47,878 (up from 40,863 in 2000).  This amounts to 6.3% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls]{{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|45.7|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls]{{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Bolívar Municipality is Iván Dario Maldonado, elected on October 31, 2004, with 49% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D5%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |title=Archived copy |access-date=2019-10-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303201402/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D5%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |archive-date=2016-03-03 |url-status=dead }}</ref>  He replaced Alberto Melean shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Barinitas, Altamira, and Calderas.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
