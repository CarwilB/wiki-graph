'''El Níspero''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|78.14|km2}} and had a population of 8,109 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
In the 1887 census, El Níspero appears as a village under the jurisdiction of [[Arada, Honduras|Arada]] within [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]]. It was formally constituted as a municipality on 9 May 1917.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de El Níspero, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/El-N%C3%ADspero.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
El Níspero is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.com/en/geography/municipality-el-nispero/|title=Municipality of El Níspero|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Arada, Honduras|Arada]] to the north, [[San Rafael, Honduras|San Rafael]] to the south, [[Santa Rita, Honduras|Santa Rita]] to the east, and [[La Unión, Lempira |La Unión]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|78.14|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1610__el_n%C3%ADspero/|title=El Níspero|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It is located on the banks of the Pajosa River.<ref name="Edu"/>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|615.69|m}} above sea level, El Níspero has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of about {{cvt| 22.33|C}} and receives approximately {{cvt|306.3|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/el-nispero|title=El Níspero climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, El Níspero had a total population of 8,109 inhabitants, of whom 4,189 (51.7%) were men and 3,920 (48.3%) were women. The municipality had an urban population of 2,860 and a rural population of 5,249. The intercensal growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 1.7%.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

About 0.5% of the population were classified as Indigenous and the rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 35.1% of the population were aged 0–14 years, 57.9% were aged 15–64, and 7.0% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 22.2 years and the mean age was 27.0 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 24.7%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 1,964 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.1 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19}}
