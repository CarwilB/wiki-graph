'''Ubaque''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Ubaque borders the municipalities [[Choachí]] in the north, [[Fómeque]] in the east, [[Cáqueza]] and [[Chipaque]] in the south and in the west is the Colombian capital [[Bogotá]] at 56&nbsp;km away.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.ubaque-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml Official website Ubaque] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171013031258/http://ubaque-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml |date=2017-10-13 }} - accessed 04-05-2016</ref>

== History ==
The area around Ubaque was before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] in the 1530s inhabited by the indigenous [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in a [[Muisca Confederation|confederation]]. The capital of the southern Muisca territories was [[Bacatá|Muyquytá]], present-day Funza, to the west of Ubaque with [[Muisca rulers|ruler]] [[Saguamanchica]]. Ubaque was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' who was loyal to the northern Muisca with capital [[Tunja|Hunza]] until Saguamanchica's successor, the brutal leader [[Nemequene]] conquered Ubaque.

The arrival of the Spanish conquerors was revealed to ''[[zipa|psihipqua]]'' [[Tisquesusa|Bogotá]], succeeding the throne after the death of Nemequene. Tisquesusa reigned the southern Muisca at the time of arrival of the Spanish, led by [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]]. Mohan Popón who lived in Ubaque told the Muisca ruler that foreigners were coming and Bogotá would die "bathing in his own blood".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://pueblosoriginarios.com/biografias/tisquesusa.html Tisquesusa would die bathing in his own blood] - Pueblos Originarios - accessed 04-05-2016</ref>

The troops of De Quesada conquered the Muisca Confederation and on October 15, 1651, Ubaque was properly founded.

The etymology of Ubaque is not entirely clear. The name could be derived from ''Ybaque'' (the [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] word for a blooding ''[[Eucalyptus]]'' tree, common in the [[Andes]]) or from the word ''Ebaque''.<ref name=website/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Ubaque, where 98% of the people live in rural areas, is [[agriculture]], particularly [[potato]]es and [[carrot]]s.<ref name=website/>
[[File:2019 Ubaque - Laguna por la tarde.jpg|thumb|left|Lake Ubaque]]

== Lake Ubaque ==
Lake Ubaque or Lake El Cacique is a [[sacred lake]] in the [[Muisca religion|religion]] of the Muisca, located within the boundaries of Ubaque.<ref>Ocampo López, 2007, Ch.V, p.226</ref> It was in Ubaque where the last public religious ceremony of the Muisca was performed, on December 27, 1563.<ref>Londoño, 2001, p.4</ref>
