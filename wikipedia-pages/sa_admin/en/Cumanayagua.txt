'''Cumanayagua''' ({{IPA|es|kumanaˈʝaɣwa}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Cienfuegos Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in a valley near the Guamuhaya Mountains (usually these mountains are referred as [[Escambray Mountains]], {{convert|23|km|mi}} east of [[Cienfuegos, Cuba|Cienfuegos]], the provincial capital.

==History==
[[File:Cumanayagua, Cuba.JPG|thumb|left|A road in town's centre]]
Cumanayagua was inhabited by [[Cubans|Cuban]] [[Taíno]] indigenous people when the Spanish arrived to the island. Though the meaning of the name is uncertain, is known it comes from Taino [[Arawak]] origin. Some scholars believe it means ''"The place filled with flowers of Royal Palms"'' other believe it could mean ''"The place of Cuma and Anayagua"'', Cuma was the aboriginal chief ruler of the region, and Anayagua his daughter and heir to the chiefdom.

The village was established by Spanish and Cuban creole under Colonial times with a longer name and the village was officially established as a town on 3 May 1804, under the name of San Felipe de Cumanayagua. This name was retained until 1878 when it was changed into ''"Santa Cruz de Cumanayagua"'', and finally at the turn of the 20th century got its definitive and shorter Taino name it has today.

==Geography==
Located in the south-eastern corner of the province, by the [[Caribbean Sea|Caribbean Coast]] and at the borders with the province of [[Villa Clara Province|Villa Clara]] and [[Sancti Spíritus Province|Sancti Spíritus]]; the municipal territory includes part of [[Escambray Mountains|Escambray Mountain Range]] and the nature reserves of [[Topes de Collantes]] and [[Pico San Juan Nature Reserve|Pico San Juan]]. The town lies in south of Avilés Reservoir and is crossed by the [[Hanabanilla River]].<ref name=OSM>{{OSM|n|482488265|Cumanayagua}}</ref>

The municipality borders with the ones of [[Cienfuegos]], [[Palmira, Cuba|Palmira]], [[Cruces, Cuba|Cruces]], [[Manicaragua]] and [[Trinidad, Cuba|Trinidad]]. It counts several hamlets (villages and localities), as Arimao, Barajagua, Camilo Cienfuegos, Entronque de Minas, Gavillán, Guajimico, Hoyo de Padilla, La Clara, La Sierrita, Las Moscas, Playa El Inglés, Playa Yaguanabo, San Blas, San Francisco and San Juan.<ref name=OSM/>

==Demographics==
[[File:Cumanayagua.jpg|thumb|right|A stone-made signboard at the entrance of the municipality]]
In 2022, the municipality of Cumanayagua had a population of 47,118.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1099|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|43|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Economy==
[[citrus|Citrus fruits]] and [[coffee]] are grown in the municipality of Cumanayagua.
