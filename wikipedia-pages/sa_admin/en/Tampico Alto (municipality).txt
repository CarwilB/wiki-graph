'''Tampico Alto''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region, at the northern end of [[Tamiahua Lagoon]]. The municipal seat is also called [[Tampico Alto, Veracruz|Tampico Alto]].

In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 11,971 (down from 14,684 in 1980), of whom 2,126 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tampico Alto|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30152a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=6 November 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617081312/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30152a.htm|archive-date=17 June 2011}}</ref> 
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 129 spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Wastek language|Wastek]] (Huasteco).

Tampico Alto Municipality covers a total surface area of 1,027.35&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
