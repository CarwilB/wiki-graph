'''San Juan Lachao''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]].
It is part of the [[Juquila District]] in the center of the [[Costa Region]].<ref name=INF>{{cite web|title=San Juan Lachao|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20202a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>
==Geography==
The municipality covers 190.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of hilly land. The town is at an elevation of 60 meters above sea level.
The climate is mild and humid.
Local flowers include cartucho, [[gladiolus]], [[Tagetes|marigold]], [[bougainvillea]], roses and geraniums. 
The area is well-wooded, with pine, oak, avocado, macuil, cedar, and guanacaxtle cuachepil. 
Mangoes, plums, bananas, oranges, limes and avocados provide fruit. 
Local birds include chachalaca, white-winged dove, pigeon blue, red Palamo, real peak, quail, magpie, woodpecker and parakeet. 
Wild boar, deer, badger, raccoon, squirrel, body spin, opossums, skunks and foxes are found in the forests, and iguana and rattlesnake are also present.<ref name=INF/>
==Population==
As of 2005, the municipality had 739  households with a total population of 3,936 of whom 1,825 spoke an indigenous language.<ref name=INF/>
Some of the people speak the [[Chatino language]], a remote branch of the [[Zapotec language|Zapotecan]] family spoken only in the Juquila district.<ref>{{cite journal

==Economy==
The majority of the population is engaged in growing corn and beans.<ref name=INF/>
The [[Union of Indigenous Communities of the Isthmus Region]], a cooperative founded in 1982, assists in production and distribution of the local products under a [[fair trade]] or organic label.<ref>{{cite web
