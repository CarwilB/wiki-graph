'''Taxisco''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Santa Rosa Department, Guatemala|Santa Rosa]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is located close to the [[Pacific Ocean]].

Taxisco was the birthplace of [[Juan José Arévalo]], who served as [[President of Guatemala|President of the Republic]] from 1945 to 1951.

Prior to the [[Spanish conquest of Guatemala|Spanish Conquest]], Taxisco was occupied by the [[Xinca people]].<ref>Letona Zuleta et al, p.5.</ref>
