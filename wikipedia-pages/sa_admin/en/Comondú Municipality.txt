'''Comondú''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Baja California Sur]]. It had a population of 73,021 inhabitants in 2020.<ref name=CENSOCOMONDU2020>[https://biblioteca.setuesbcs.gob.mx/administrador/biblioteca/publicaciones/pdf/Estrategico_2023_Comondu.pdf 2020 Comondú Census]</ref> With a land area of 16,318.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (7,072.9 sq mi), it is the seventh-largest municipality in area in Mexico. The municipal seat is located in [[Ciudad Constitución]].

The [[Spain|Spanish]] missions of [[Misión San José de Comondú|San José de Comondú]] and [[Misión San Luis Gonzaga Chiriyaqui|San Luis Gonzaga]] are located in this municipality. The [[Rocas Alijos]], a group of tiny uninhabited rocks that are 300&nbsp;km west off the coast, are part of the municipality.

The municipality of Comondú takes its name from an indigenous Californian root: caamanc cadeu, of the Cochimí ethnic group, which means: reed bed in a ravine - The center is divided into two unequal parts; the upper part has a rising sun, on the right a cardón type cactus (cardón). Other villages of relevance Cd. Insurgentes, Puerto San Carlos, Puerto Lopez Mateos, Villa Ignacio Zaragoza, San Juanico, San Isidro, La Purisima, Cadeje, Tepentu, Villa Morelos. Once a prosperous agricultural valley, mainly grains, and a lot of other non seasonal products (Asparagus, Oranges, tomatoes, onion, and cotton).

==Politics==
[[File:San Isidro, West Coastal, Baja California Sur, Mexico.jpg|thumb|left|Cerro El Pilón la Purísima.]]
[[File:Templo en San Miguel de Comondú.jpg|thumb|left|Church in San Miguel de Comondú.]]
The Municipality of Comondú was one of the first three municipalities created in the then Federal South Territory of Baja California as part of a presidential decree of 1971, after a constitutional amendment allowing the creation and election of local councils in the territory, hitherto divided into political delegations. Dated January 1, 1972 Comondú was formally the first City Council. The township government rests with the council; it consists of the Mayor, a trustee and a council composed of fourteen aldermen; the city council is elected for a period of three years by popular vote (direct and secret), starting every April 30 of the election year.

As of March 1, 2021, the municipality reported 1,740 recoveries, 26 active cases, and 158 deaths from the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus – Baja California Sur Situation Report – Información de Coronavirus |url=https://coronavirus.bcs.gob.mx/english/ |website=coronavirus.bcs.gob.mx |publisher=Gobierno de Baja California Sur |access-date=March 1, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>
