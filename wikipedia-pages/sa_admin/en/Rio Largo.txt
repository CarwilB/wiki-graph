'''Rio Largo''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈɦiw ˈlaɦɡu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] made in 1915 located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population estimated was 75,394 (census of 2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/rio-largo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|309|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] – [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

It all started in 1915, Rio Largo became the "village of Santa Luzia do Norte", but in 1938, it became an independent city, being founded '''Rio Largo.'''
