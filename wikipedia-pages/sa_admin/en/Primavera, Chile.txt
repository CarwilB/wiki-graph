'''Primavera''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "spring")  is a [[Communes of Chile|Chilean commune]] in [[Tierra del Fuego Province, Chile|Tierra del Fuego Province]], [[Magallanes and Antartica Chilena Region]], [[Chile]]. It is located on the north of [[Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego|Tierra del Fuego Island]]. Its main settlement is [[Cerro Sombrero]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Primavera spans an area of {{convert|4614.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 1,016 inhabitants (735 men and 281 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population fell by 38% (613 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Primavera is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012–2016 alcalde is Blagomir Brztilo Avendaño.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Primavera is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Enrique Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes and Antartica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic Boroevic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes Region).
