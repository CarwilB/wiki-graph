'''Salvador Alvarado''' ({{IPA|es|salβaˈðoɾ alβaˈɾaðo}}) is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Sinaloa]], being the smallest municipality in Sinaloa. It stands at 
{{coord|25|08|09|N|107|51|45|W|}}.
<!-- {{coord|25|08|09|N|107|51|45|W|}}. -->

The municipality reported 79,085 inhabitants in the 2010 census and has an areal extent of 1,197.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (462.36 sq mi). Its municipal seat is the city of [[Guamúchil]]. The largest other community in the municipality is the town of Benito Juárez (population 5,128).
