'''Tapalqué Partido''' is a central [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 8,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4149|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Tapalqué]], which is around {{convert|273|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
