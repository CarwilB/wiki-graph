'''Colonia Carlos Pellegrini''' is a small town and municipality in [[San Martín Department, Corrientes|San Martín Department]] in [[Corrientes Province]], Argentina. According to the 2001 population census conducted by [[INDEC]] its population was 683 inhabitants.<ref name="Sánchez2006">{{cite book|last=Sánchez|first=Jorge Ezequiel|title=Argentina pueblo a pueblo: Provincia de Córdoba III. Provincia de Corrientes. Provincia de Entre Ríos I|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=BBDjAAAAMAAJ|accessdate=22 December 2011|year=2006|publisher=Clarín|page=1345}}</ref> It was established in 1927. It is connected by road to [[Mercedes, Corrientes|Mercedes]] in the southwest.

The climate of the area is subtropical, prone to storms, with an average summer temperature of 30&nbsp;°C and can fall to −2&nbsp;°C at night at lowest during the winter. The average rainfall is about 2000&nbsp;mm per year.

It lies within the [[Iberá Provincial Nature Reserve]] (Reserva Natural Provincial del Iberá, also known as the Iberá Wetlands), a wetland of 1,300,000 hectares on a peninsula that enters the Iberá lagoon 5,500 ha. It has diverse wildlife, including caiman, swamp deer and over 350 species of birds.
