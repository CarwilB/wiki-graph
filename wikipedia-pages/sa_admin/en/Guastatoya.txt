'''Guastatoya''' is a city in [[Guatemala]]. It is the capital and the most populous city of the department of [[El Progreso Department|El Progreso]]. The Guastatoya Water Park is located in the city. The city was leveled in 1976 by an [[1976 Guatemala earthquake|earthquake]] with a moment magnitude of 7.5. Guastatoya is located 73&nbsp;km from [[Guatemala City]].

==Sports==
[[Deportivo Guastatoya]] football club play in the [[Liga Nacional de Fútbol de Guatemala|first tier of Guatemalan football]]. Their home venue is the [[Estadio David Cordón Hichos]].
