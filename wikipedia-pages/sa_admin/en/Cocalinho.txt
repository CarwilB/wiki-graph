'''Cocalinho ''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso]].
It has an area of {{convert|16,516.319|km2|sqmi}}.
It has a [[tropical savanna climate]].
As of 2020 the estimated population was 5,681.

==Geography==

Cocalinho is in the state of Mato Grosso, Brazil.{{sfn|Geonames}} It has an area of {{convert|16,516.319|km2}} as of 2018.{{sfn|IBGE}} The elevation above sea level is about {{convert|231|m}}.{{sfn|Geonames}}

==Climate==

The [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen climate type]] is Aw : [[Tropical savanna climate]].{{sfn|mindat}}
The average annual temperature is {{convert|27|C}}.{{sfn|NASAearth}}
The average annual rainfall is {{convert|2,024|mm}}.{{sfn|NASArain}}

==Demographics==

The population in the 2010 census was 5490.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
The estimated population as of 2020 was 5,681.{{sfn|IBGE}}
Population density as of 2010 was {{convert|0.33|PD/km2|PD/sqmi}}.{{sfn|IBGE}}
As of 2010, 90.4% of the population had attended school between the ages of 6 and 14. 
Also as of 2010, the municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.660.
This compares to 0.328 in 1991 and 0.500 in 2000.{{sfn|IBGE}}

On the 2010 census religion was reported as [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] by 3,449 people, [[Evangelicalism|Evangelical]] by 1,207 people and [[Animism]] by 19 people.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

In 2017, the average monthly salary was 2.3 minimum wages. 
Employed people were 17.3% of the  total population. 
Households with monthly income of up to half a minimum wage per person represent 37.1% of the population.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

==Health and sanitation==

1.9% of households have adequate sanitation, 81% of urban households are on public roads with afforestation and 0.3% of urban households are on public roads with adequate urbanization (presence of manhole, sidewalk, pavement and curb).{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
Annual hospitalizations due to diarrhea are 6.1 per 1,000 inhabitants.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
