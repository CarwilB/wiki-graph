'''San Francisco del Oro''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]] in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at San Francisco del Oro. The municipality, one of the smallest in Chihuahua, covers an area of 695.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Demography==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 4,753,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=San Francisco del Oro|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=059|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> which is a decrease from the 2005 population of 4,838.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Francisco del Oro|url=
http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08059a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 19, 2008}}</ref>

As of 2010, the municipality of San Francisco del Oro had a population of 4,249.<ref name=SEDESOL/> The municipality has 97 localities, none of which has a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
