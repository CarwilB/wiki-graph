'''Mariscal Cáceres District''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] ''mariscal'' [[marshal]]) is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Camaná Province|Camaná]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080417091049/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-17 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

The district was named after the Peruvian president [[Andrés Avelino Cáceres]].
