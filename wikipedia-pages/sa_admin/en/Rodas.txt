'''Rodas''' ({{IPA|es|ˈroðas}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Cienfuegos Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1859<ref name=guije/> under the name of ''Lechuzo''. In 1879 it was renamed Rodas in honour of capitán general ''Caballero de Rodas''.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s and villages of Cabecera (the town proper), Ariza, [[Cartagena (Rodas)|Cartagena]], Congojas, Jabacoa, [[La Unión, Cienfuegos|La Unión]], Limones, Medidas, Santiago, Soledad and Turquino. Until the 1977 administrative reform, the village of [[Arriete-Ciego Montero]], currently in the municipality of [[Palmira, Cuba|Palmira]], was part of it.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vrodas/index.htm|title=Rodas|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

It is bordered by the municipalities of [[Aguada de Pasajeros]], [[Abreus]], [[Cienfuegos]], [[Palmira, Cuba|Palmira]], [[Lajas, Cuba|Lajas]], [[Santo Domingo, Cuba|Santo Domingo]] (in [[Villa Clara Province]]) and [[Los Arabos]] (in [[Matanzas Province]]).

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Rodas had a population of 32,719.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|552|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|59|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
