'''Honda''' ({{IPA|es|onda|IPA}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] department of [[Colombia]]. The population of the municipality was 24,693 as of the census 2018.<ref name="pop"/> Along with [[Líbano, Tolima|Líbano]], Honda is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic]] [[Diocese]] of [[Diocese of Líbano-Honda|Líbano-Honda]]. The main industries of Honda are tourism, fishing, and cattle-ranching.

==Toponymy (name origin)==

Honda takes its name from the [[Ondaima]]s, the indigenous people that inhabited the banks of the [[Magdalena River]] and the region where the city lies today.

Honda is called "the City of Bridges" with more than 40 of them on the rivers [[Magdalena River|Magdalena]], Gualí, Guarinó, and Quebrada Seca. It is also called the "City of Peace" as it escaped most of the violence of the 1950s.<ref name=ciudad>{{cite web|url=http://www.infocolombiano.com/turinftol3.php|title=HONDA: "Ciudad de la Paz", "Ciudad de los Puentes"|access-date=10 August 2016|publisher=Informativo Colombiano|language=es|archive-date=5 February 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180205001136/http://www.infocolombiano.com/turinftol3.php|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Honda (Colombia).jpg|thumb|left|300px|Honda and the Magdalena in the foreground]]

Honda was founded on August 24, 1539 by Francisco Nuñez Pedroso. The "golden age" of the city was between 1850 and 1910 when the Magdalena river constituted the only means of transportation between the [[Caribbean]] coast and the inland city of Bogotá. The city was the main river port of the country and all the imported goods and articles arrived in [[Bogotá]] through the port of Honda.

Because of its historical importance, Honda was named a [[Pueblo Patrimonio (Colombia)|''Pueblo Patrimonio'']] (heritage town) of Colombia in 2010. As of March 2021, it is the only municipality in the [[Middle Magdalena Valley]] region that has been awarded this distinction.<ref>{{Cite web|date=Dec 2020|title=GUÍA: Red Turística de Pueblos Patrimonio de Colombia|url=https://fontur.com.co/sites/default/files/2020-12/GUIA_PUEBLOS_PATRIMONIO.pdf|access-date=29 Mar 2021|website=Fondo Nacional de Turismo de la República de Colombia - FONTUR}}</ref>
