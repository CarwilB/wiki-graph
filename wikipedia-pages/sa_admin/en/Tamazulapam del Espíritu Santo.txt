''' Tamazulapam del Espíritu Santo ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality is at an altitude of 2,040 meters.<ref>http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM20oaxaca/municipios/20031a.html {{Dead link|date=February 2022}}</ref> It is part of the [[Sierra Mixe]] district within the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca]] Region.

The municipality has a total population of  6,908,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://municipales.siap.gob.mx/oax/mun_20031_TAMAZULAPAMDELESPIRITUSANTO/ |title=Tamazulapam del Espíritu Santo, Oaxaca |website=municipales.siap.gob.mx |access-date=22 May 2022 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130713025422/http://municipales.siap.gob.mx/oax/mun_20031_TAMAZULAPAMDELESPIRITUSANTO/ |archive-date=13 July 2013 |url-status=dead}}</ref> and the town has a population of 2,372.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tamazulapam-del-espiritu-santo/|title=Tamazulápam del Espíritu Santo (Oaxaca) Tamazulápam del Espíritu Santo}}</ref>

Municipal President Artemio Ortiz Ricardez died on May 13, [[2020 in Mexico|2020]] during the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Han muerto 19 ediles a consecuencia de Covid (+Infografía) |url=https://www.msn.com/es-mx/noticias/mexico/han-muerto-19-ediles-a-consecuencia-de-covid-infograf%C3%ADa/ar-BB18nzdT |website=www.msn.com |publisher=24 Horas |accessdate=August 26, 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Fallece el edil de Tamazulápam Mixe por Covid-19 |url=https://imparcialoaxaca.mx/los-municipios/455249/fallece-el-edil-de-tamazulapam-mixe-por-covid-19/ |website=imparcialoaxaca.mx |accessdate=August 26, 2020}}</ref> The town had been placed under quarantine after its first reported case on May 10.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tamazulápam Mixe decreta cuarentena obligatoria tras confirmar su primer caso de Covid-19 |url=https://oaxaca.eluniversal.com.mx/municipios/10-05-2020/tamazulapam-mixe-decreta-cuarentena-obligatoria-tras-confirmar-su-primer-caso |publisher=El Universal |accessdate=August 26, 2020 |language=es |date=10 May 2020}}</ref>
