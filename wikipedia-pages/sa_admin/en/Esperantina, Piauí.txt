The town is the home of International Female Orgasm Day which was established in 2007 by José Arimatéia Dantas Lacerda. The holiday is celebrated each August 8.<ref>[http://www.ibtimes.com/international-day-female-orgasm-brings-pleasure-brazilian-women-742490 International Day Of The Female Orgasm Brings Pleasure To Brazilian Women] International Business Times, 2012-08-08.</ref><ref>[http://www.cosmopolitan.com/cosmo-latina/blog/international-female-orgasm-day What You Should Know About International Female Orgasm Day] Cosmopolitan, 2012-08-09.</ref>
