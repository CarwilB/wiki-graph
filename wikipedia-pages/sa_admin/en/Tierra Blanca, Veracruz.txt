'''Tierra Blanca''' is a city and its surrounding [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] of the same name located in the south-central part of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. At the 2005 census the city had a population of 44,171 inhabitants, and 47,824 in 2015. The city serves as the municipal seat of the municipality, which has an area of 1,363.76 km<sup>2</sup> (526.55 sq mi) and a population of 86,075 inhabitants (2005) and 106,277 (2015). Its largest other community is the town of [[Joachín, Veracruz|Joachín]].

The municipality has been torn by violence in the 2010s and 2020s. On May 30, [[2020 in Mexico|2020]], Francisco Navarette Serna, founder of "El Sol de Terra Blance," was one of seven people killed at a party held during the height of the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico]]. In 2016, Navarette Serna was accused of being the local drug lord responsible for the murder of five men between 16 and 27 years old from [[Acayucan (municipality)|Acayucan]] in February [[2016 in Mexico|2016]]. Their bodies were crushed to avoid identification.<ref>{{cite web |title=Ataque armado deja siete muertos y dos heridos en salón de fiestas en Veracruz |url=https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/estados/ataque-armado-deja-siete-muertos-y-dos-heridos-en-salon-de-fiestas-en-veracruz |website=El Universal |access-date=May 30, 2020 |language=es |date=30 May 2020}}</ref>
