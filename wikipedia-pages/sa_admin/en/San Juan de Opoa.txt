'''San Juan de Opoa''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Copán department|Copán]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://proceso.hn/honduras-registra-150-municipios-libres-de-incendios-forestales-en-2025/|title=Honduras registra 150 municipios libres de incendios forestales en 2025|work={{ill|Proceso Digital|es}}|date=30 May 2025|access-date=26 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

It was one of the first villages founded by the Spaniards in 1526.{{citation needed|date=August 2012}}
