'''Mombin-Crochu''' ({{IPA|fr|mɔ̃bɛ̃ kʁɔʃy}}; {{langx|ht|Monben Kwochi}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Vallières Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 25,113 inhabitants.

== Communal sections ==
The commune consists of two [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Sans Souci (communal section)|Sans Souci]], urban and rural
* [[Bois Laurence]], urban and rural, containing the town of Bois Laurence
