'''Santa María Chiquimula''' is a town, with a population of 15,919 (2018 census),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ Population of cities & towns in Guatemala]</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Totonicapán Department|Totonicapán department]] of [[Guatemala]]. Located in the western highlands of Guatemala at an altitude of 2130 metres.<ref name="Muni_geo"/> The municipality has an area of {{cvt|236|km2}} and a population of 55,013 (2018 census). The Mayan [[Kʼicheʼ language]] is spoken among the indigenous people here, but [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is also widely spoken. The main products of the region are corn and black beans.

==Dialect==

The [[Kʼicheʼ language|Kʼicheʼ]] spoken in Santa María Chiquimula is marked by intervocalic {{IPA|/l/}} transitioning into {{IPA|[ð]}}, especially among women.<ref>{{Cite book|title=Language and Ethnicity among the Kʼicheeʼ Maya|last=Romero|first=Sergio|year=2015|pages=42|language=English|chapter=“Each Town Speaks Its Own Language”: The Social Value of Dialectal Variation in Kʼicheeʼ |isbn=9781607813989}}</ref>
