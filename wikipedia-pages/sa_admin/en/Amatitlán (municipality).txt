'''Amatitlán''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 231&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]] to the south-east. It has a surface of 169.4 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|26|N|95|44|W|}}.

==Geography==
===Borders===
The municipality of Amatitlán is delimited to the north-east by [[Tlacotalpan Municipality|Tlacotalpan]], to the south-east by [[José Azueta, Veracruz|José Azueta]], to the south-west by [[Cosamaloapan]], to the west by [[Ixmatlahuacan]], and to the north-west by [[Acula (municipality)|Acula]].<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30012a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Amatitlán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-05-22|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070528124611/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30012a.htm|archive-date=2007-05-28|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Products==
It produces [[maize]] and [[beans]].
