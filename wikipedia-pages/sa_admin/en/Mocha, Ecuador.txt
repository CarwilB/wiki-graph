'''Mocha''' is a city located in the central [[Andean]] mountain range of [[Ecuador]]. Located in [[Tungurahua Province]], Mocha is the seat of the [[Mocha Canton]]. The current mayor of Mocha is Sr. Orlando Caluña.<ref>''http://www.tungurahua.gov.ec/Autoridades.php'' {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090413224817/http://www.tungurahua.gov.ec/Autoridades.php |date=2009-04-13 }} Authorities of the Honorable Consejo Provincial de Tungurahua 21 July 2009</ref>
