'''Comapa''' (from [[Nahuatl]] ''Komapan'', "in the river of pots"<ref name=encyclopedia/>) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located about {{convert|40|km}} south of the state capital [[Xalapa]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Geography==
The municipality of Comapa is located in the [[Gulf of Mexico]] lowlands of central Veracruz. It borders the municipalities of [[Puente Nacional, Veracruz|Puente Nacional]] and [[Tlacotepec de Mejía]] to the north, [[Totutla]] to the northwest, [[Sochiapa]] to the west, [[Huatusco]] to the southwest, [[Zentla]] to the south, [[Soledad de Doblado]] to the southeast, and [[Paso de Ovejas]] to the northeast.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Comapa, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|311.861|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.4% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/> 

The northern border of the municipality mostly follows the Paso Limón and Panoaya Rivers.<ref name=compendium/> The Panoaya (from [[Nahuatl]] ''Panohaya'', "place where the waters pass"<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tlacotepecdemejia.gob.mx/puente-de-panohaya/ |title=PUENTE DE PANOHAYA |language=es |publisher=Government of Tlacotepec de Mejía |date=16 October 2014 |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref>) is an upper tributary of the Paso de Ovejas River, which itself meets the [[Antigua River|La Antigua River]] at [[La Antigua, Veracruz|La Antigua]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Todd Jr. |first=John |url=http://www.johntoddjr.com/149%20Caminorealmaps/08%20caminobr.htm |title=The Mysterious Bridge in the Jungle |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref> The municipality's southern border is mostly made up by the Chavaxtla and Paso Lagartos Rivers, both tributaries of the [[Jamapa River]].<ref name=compendium/><ref>{{cite encyclopedia |title=Hidrología |encyclopedia=Atlas del patrimonio natural, histórico y cultural de Veracruz |language=es |year=2010 |publisher=Government of Veracruz |url=http://cdigital.uv.mx/bitstream/123456789/9650/1/04HIDROLOGIA.pdf |page=105 |volume=1}}</ref>

Comapa's climate is generally warm and subhumid with rain in the summer. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|18|and(-)|26|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|900|and(-)|1600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In pre-Hispanic times the [[Totonac people]] built a fort at Comapa.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Local tradition has it that Comapa was one of the places where [[Guadalupe Victoria]] hid during his guerilla campaign in the [[Mexican War of Independence]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Flores Rodríguez |first=Miguel Ángel |url=http://diariodehuatusco.blogspot.com/2016/09/comapa-y-su-fortin.html |title=COMAPA Y SU FORTIN |language=es |date=28 September 2016 |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref>

On 28 March 1831, Comapa became a municipality in the canton of Huatusco in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=106, 167 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Carmen Cantón Croda.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://comapa.gob.mx/Transparencia/2 |title=Portal de Transparencia del H. Ayuntamiento de Comapa, Veracruz |language=es |publisher=Órgano de Fiscalización Superior del Estado de Veracruz |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Comapa recorded a population of 18,713 inhabitants living in 4382 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=043 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Comapa |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref> It recorded a population of 19,859 inhabitants in the 2015 Intercensal Survey.<ref name=survey/> 

There are 67 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which two are classified as urban:
* The municipal seat, also called Comapa, which recorded a population of 1421 inhabitants in the 2010 Census; and
* Boca del Monte, which recorded 4832 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name=catalog/>

==Agriculture==
Crops grown in the municipality include coffee, corn and sugarcane.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Comapa is known for its ''comapeño'' peppers, a small orange variety eaten fresh or dried.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.laroussecocina.mx/diccionario/definicion/chile-comapeno |title=Chile comapeño |language=es |website=Larousse Cocina |access-date=9 February 2018}}</ref>
