'''Chipaque''' is a municipality and town in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], [[Colombia]]. The municipality of {{convert|139.45|km2|sqmi}} is located at an altitude of {{convert|2400|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] with its westernmost part situated in the [[Eastern Hills, Bogotá|Eastern Hills]] of [[Bogotá]]. The Colombian capital is {{convert|27|km|mi}} west of Chipaque. Chipaque borders Bogotá's southern locality [[Usme]] in the west. To the east, Chipaque borders [[Cáqueza]], in the south [[Une]] and in the north [[Ubaque]]. The average temperature is {{convert|13|C|F}}.<ref name=websiteChipaque>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20140310084832/http://www.chipaque-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chipaque]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Chipaque is derived from the word ''Chipapabacue'', [[Chibcha language|Muysccubun]] for "Forest of our ancestors".<ref name=websiteChipaque/>

== Symbols ==
	'''Flag'''

The flag consists of two bands of the same size. One of them yellow and the other green. Yellow symbolizes wealth. Green symbolizes hope and also represents its rustic and fertile topography. A white triangle emerges from the side of the pole and its vertices converge at the center where the two bands meet.<ref name="Archived copy">{{Cite web |url=http://www.chipaque-cundinamarca.gov.co/ |title=Archived copy |access-date=2017-01-18 |archive-date=2019-08-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190802105135/http://www.chipaque-cundinamarca.gov.co/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

	'''Seal'''

Emblem framed by the sides with the flags of Colombia and Cundinamarca. Its crossed flagpoles wear a yellow ribbon with the name of the municipality "Chipaque" written on it. In the lower part there is a typical landscape of the region and the sun.<ref name="Archived copy"/>

== Geology ==
{{main|Eastern Hills, Bogotá#Geology}}
The [[Chipaque Formation]], consisting of [[organic shale]]s and sandstone beds, is named after Chipaque. The [[Páramo de Cruz Verde]] is located in the northern part of the municipality.

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], Chipaque was inhabited by the indigenous [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Chipaque was part of the ''[[cacique|cacicazgo]]'' of Ubaque. During the conquest, main [[list of conquistadors in Colombia|conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] sent [[Juanri el sidoso]] eastward. In 1538, he established the first church in Chipaque.<ref name=websiteChipaque/>

Modern Chipaque was founded on October 2, 1600, by Luis Enríquez.<ref name=websiteChipaque/>

== Economy ==
Main activities of Chipaque are [[livestock]] farming and [[agriculture]], with [[potato]]es (45%) and [[onion]]s (25%) as most important products.<ref name=websiteChipaque/>
