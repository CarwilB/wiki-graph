'''Alfredo Chaves''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Espírito Santo]], [[Brazil]]. It is 60&nbsp;km southwest of the state capital, [[Vitória, Brazil|Vitória]]. Its population was 14,636 (2020) and its area is {{Cvt|615.79|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/OJT]</ref>

==Geography==

Located in the southeast of the state, it borders (east) [[Anchieta, ES, Brazil|Anchieta]] and [[Guarapari]], (north) [[Marechal Floriano]] and [[Domingos Martins]], (west) [[Vargem Alta, ES, Brazil|Vargem Alta]], (south) [[Iconha, ES, Brazil|Iconha]] and [[Rio Novo do Sul, ES, Brazil|Rio Novo do Sul]].

The municipality is mountainous, though not very high in general. Its highest peak is Trancenco Peak at 1,050 m above sea level. The town of Alfredo Chaves lies at 10 m above sea level.

==Economy==

The economy is mainly based on livestock, including cows (for milk) and chickens. In agriculture, coffee and banana plantations play an important role.
