'''Pedernales Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Manabí Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[Pedernales, Ecuador|Pedernales]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 46,876.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
