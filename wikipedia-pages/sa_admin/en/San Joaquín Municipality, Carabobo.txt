The '''San Joaquín Municipality''' is one of the 14 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Carabobo]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 64,124.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[San Joaquín, Carabobo|San Joaquín]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the San Joaquín Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Carabobo.zip{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Geography==
It borders [[Aragua State]] to the north, the [[Lake Valencia (Venezuela)|Lake Valencia]] to the south, the [[Diego Ibarra Municipality]] to the east, and the [[Guacara Municipality]] to the west.

==Demographics==
The San Joaquín Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 60,953 (up from 48,946 in 2000).  This amounts to 2.7% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|479.94|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the San Joaquín Municipality is Luis Aguiar, elected on November 23, 2008, with 55% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=07&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02|title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008|website=www.cne.gob.ve}}</ref> He replaced César Emilio Hernández Meza shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into one [[parish]] (San Joaquín).<ref name=autogenerated1 />

==Tradition==
The municipality is well known for its local biscuits, the "''panelas'' of San Joaquín".
