'''Kantunil Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “place of yellow or precious stone”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 153.29&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 65&nbsp;km southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There are no records indicating the history of pre-Hispanic times for this area. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/> The known encomenderos for Kantunil are: Juan de Aguilar (1549)l Francisco López de Cieza (1552), Diego López de Cieza (1607), Pedro de Magaña Pacheco de la Cámara (1632), Pedro de Magaña y Contreras,  José de Arrúe (1648), Alonso Chacón de Aguilar (1664-1688), Ignacio Chacón.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=84-00-04399-5|page=492|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA492|access-date=9 June 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the Coast region with its headquarters in [[Izamal Municipality]]. In 1910, Kantunil and Holcá were the only settlements in the area.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three year term. The town council has four aldermen, who serve as Secretary of the Town Hall; and councilors of heritage and patrimony; parks, public gardens and cemeteries; and public lighting.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Kantunil|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=4 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />
