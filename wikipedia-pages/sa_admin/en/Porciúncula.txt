'''Porciúncula''' ({{IPA|pt|poʁsiˈũkulɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the northernmost point of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 17,288 (2022) and its area is 291.847&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/porciuncula/panorama |access-date=2023-10-26 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>
