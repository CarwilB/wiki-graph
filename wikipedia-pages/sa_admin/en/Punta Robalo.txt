'''Punta Robalo''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chiriquí Grande District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. The corregimiento has a land area of {{convert|51|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 1,164 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|22.8|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,118; its population as of 2000 was 1,673.<ref name=inec/> The town is located on the western coast of [[Chiriquí Lagoon]].
