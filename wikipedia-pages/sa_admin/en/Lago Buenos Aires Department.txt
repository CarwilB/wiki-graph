'''Lago Buenos Aires Department''' is one of the seven [[Departments of Argentina|department]]s in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It covers an area of 28,609&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and had a population of 12,606 at the 2022 Census.<ref name="Census 2022">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos de la Republica Argentine, Census of 16 May 2022.</ref> The seat of the department is in [[Perito Moreno, Santa Cruz|Perito Moreno]].
