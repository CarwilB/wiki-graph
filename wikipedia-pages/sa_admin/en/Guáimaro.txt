'''Guáimaro''' is a town and municipality in the southern part of [[Camagüey Province]] in [[Cuba]]. It is located between the cities of [[Camagüey]] and [[Las Tunas (city)|Las Tunas]].

==History==
Guáimaro features prominently in Cuban history as the place where in 1869, at the beginning of the [[Ten Years' War]], the Revolutionary Army of [[Mambises]] met and created the [[Guáimaro Constitution]] for a new nation free from Spanish colonial oppression. The municipality was created in 1943, when it split from Camagüey.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Camaniguán, Elia, Galbis, Guáimaro, Palo Seco, Pilar and Tetuán.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/cguaimaro/index.htm|title=Guáimaro|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-05|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Guáimaro had a population of 36,114.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1847|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|20|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
