'''Buenópolis''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 10,353 living in a total area of 1,610&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/buenopolis/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Central Mineira]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Curvelo]]. It became a municipality in 1938.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Buenópolis is located at an elevation of 700 meters on highway BR-135.  The nearest major population center is [[Montes Claros]].  The town began with the building of the railroad from Belo Horizonte to Montes Claros in 1910. The name is derived from the name of the state governor at that time, Júlio Bueno Brandão.

The distance to [[Curvelo]] is 138&nbsp;km; and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 272&nbsp;km.   Neighboring  municipalities are:   [[Augusto de Lima, Minas Gerais|Augusto de Lima]], [[Joaquim Felício]], [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]], [[Bocaiúva]], and [[Lassance]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/nanuque/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-01 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080418022843/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/nanuque/ |archive-date=2008-04-18 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
The municipality contains part of the {{convert|22494|ha}} [[Serra do Cabral State Park]], created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra do Cabral – IEF}}
 |title=Parque Estadual da Serra do Cabral|publisher=IEF: Instituto Estadual de Florestas

==Economy==

The main economic activities are services and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$39 million, with 24 million from services, 2 million from industry, and 10 million from agriculture.  There were 354 rural producers on 14,000 hectares of land.  Only 8 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 800 persons were dependent on agriculture.  The main crops were mangoes, sugarcane, beans, and corn.  There were 27,000 head of cattle (2006).  There was 1 bank (2007) and 666 automobiles (366 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 14 inhabitants per automobile.  
<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Social indexes==

There were 3 health clinics in 2005.  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Bocaiúva, Montes Claros or Curvelo.  Educational needs were met by 16 primary schools, 2 middle schools, and 9 pre-primary schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.679 (2000)
*State ranking: 642 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 3,319 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}} 
*Literacy rate: 77%
*Life expectancy: 65 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In 2000 the per capita income of R$149.00 was well below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
