'''Novo Santo Antônio''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] for "New Saint Anthony") is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the state of [[Piauí]]. The population is 3,003 (2020 est.) in an area of 481.71&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/121U Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>

The municipality contains the {{convert|223170|ha|adj=on}} [[Araguaia State Park]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Proteção Integral - SEMA}} |title=Proteção Integral|date=28 May 2013|publisher=SEMA MT |url=http://www.sema.mt.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=583&Itemid=470|access-date=2016-12-03}}</ref>
