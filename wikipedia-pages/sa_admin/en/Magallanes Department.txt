'''Magallanes Department''' is one of the seven [[Departments of Argentina|department]]s in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has an area of 19,805&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and had a population of 12,911 at the 2022 Census.<ref name="Census 2022">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos de la Republica Argentine, Census of 16 May 2022.</ref> The seat of the department is at the town of [[Puerto San Julián]].
