'''Jardinópolis''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], [[Brazil]], in the outskirts of [[Ribeirão Preto]]. The population is 45,282 (2022 est.) in an area of 502&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6M7 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is 590 m above sea level. Jardinópolis is known as an important distribution center of [[mango|mangoes]] in Brazil. The town hosts the "Festa do Senhor Bom Jesus da Lapa" (The Kind Hearted Jesus of Lapa's celebration) religious event, which takes place every year between July 28 and August 6.
