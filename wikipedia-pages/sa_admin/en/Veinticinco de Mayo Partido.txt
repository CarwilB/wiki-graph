'''Veinticinco de Mayo Partido''' (25 May) is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the centre-north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 35,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4769|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Veinticinco de Mayo, Buenos Aires|Veinticinco de Mayo]], which is around {{convert|228|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
