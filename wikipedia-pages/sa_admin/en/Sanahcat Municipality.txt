'''Sanahcat Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. It contains {{Convert|54.93|km2}} of land and is located roughly {{Convert|52|km}} southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/> It is bounded on the north by [[Hocabá Municipality|Hocabá]] &ndash; [[Xocchel Municipality|Xocchel]], on the south by [[Huhí Municipality|Huhí]], on the east [[Kantunil Municipality|Kantunil]], and the west by [[Homún Municipality|Homún]].<ref name=Sana>{{Cite web|url=http://www.yucatan.gob.mx/estado/municipios/ver_municipio.php?id=64|title=Sanahcat|language=es|access-date=8 August 2015|publisher= Gobierno del Estado de Yucatán}}</ref> In the [[Yucatec Maya language]], its name means "legume of Tzalam."

==History==
During pre-Hispanic times, the town existed but it is unclear which chieftainship it was part of. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/> In 1565 the encomienderos were Melchor and Francisco Pacheco.<ref name="Quezada (2014)">{{cite book|last1=Quezada|first1=Sergio|title=Maya lords and lordship: the formation of colonial society in Yucatán, 1350&ndash;1600|date=2014|publisher=University of Oklahoma Press|location=Norman|isbn=978-0-806-14579-2|page=132|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=P2W9AgAAQBAJ&pg=PA132}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its [[independence]] from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the Beneficios Bajos region with its headquarters in [[Sotuta]].<ref name=municipios /> Development of the area started in 1821.<ref name=Sana/> In 1900, it split away from Hocabá. On 29 September 1924 Sanahcat was elevated to a municipality and in 1937 the Haciendas Tixcacal Xtohil and Ancona were withdrawn from its jurisdiction.<ref name=municipios /><ref name=Sana/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works; parks and gardens; and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Sanahcat|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=1 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=8 May 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140508064449/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

==Local festivals==
Every year on the 15th of August a celebration in honor of Our Lady of the Assumption is held.<ref name=municipios />

==Tourist attractions==
* Church of Our Lady of the Assumption built in the 16th century
* Cenote Pishtón 
* Hacienda Tixcacal Leal

On the way to Momain, there is a church at Sanathcat which is roughly finished and is built to a "T-plan". The chapel has an arch feature and has a belfry.{{Sfn|Perry|Perry|2002|p=218}} The church built in the sixteenth century. is dedicated to Our Lady of the Assumption.<ref name=Sana/>
