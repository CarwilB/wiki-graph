'''Cayes-Jacmel''' ({{IPA|fr|kaj ʒakmɛl}}; {{langx|ht|Kay Jakmèl}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jacmel Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 40,348 inhabitants. Cayes-Jacmel is down the road from Cyvadier and home to a lovely public beach and has a remarkable farmers market (Wednesdays and Saturdays). The town is also home to amazing artists and artisans, including work in meticulously created miniature ships. The region's hydroelectric plant (running on a stream only a few inches deep) is nearby.

== History ==
The town of Cayes-Jacmel was founded in 1714.

== Administration ==
The commune is made up of 4 sections:
* Ravine-Normande
* Gaillard
* Haut-Cap-Rouge
* La Selle de Fond-Melon

== Monument and site ==
'''Fort Ogé''' is part of around 20 military installments, built on the Haitian territory after the 1804 independence: a defensive system aimed to stop a possible return of the French, former owners of the Saint-Domingue colony. The Fort Ogé was built under the direction of general [[Magloire Ambroise]]. The ruins of the fort are located inside the commune section of  Cap-Rouge. Its name reminds of [[Vincent Ogé]], one of the main protagonists of the [[Haitian Revolution]].
