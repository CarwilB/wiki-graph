'''Yerbas Buenas''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Linares Province]], [[Maule Region]]. It lies in the geographical center of the country, on the [[fertility (soil)|fertile]] [[Chilean Central Valley|central]] [[plain]], some {{convert|300|km|0|abbr=on}} south of the national [[Capital (political)|capital]] of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]], {{convert|50|km|0|abbr=on}} south of [[Talca]], the [[Maule Region|regional]] capital and {{convert|12|km|0|abbr=on}} north of [[Linares, Chile|Linares]], the [[Linares Province|provincial]] capital.

==Demography==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Yerbas Buenas had a population of 16,134 (8,380 men and 7,754 women). Of these, 1,595 (9.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s (the town of Yerbas Buenas), and 14,539 (90.1%) lived in [[rural area]]s. The population grew 4.1% (634 persons) between the censuses of 1992 and 2002.<ref name="INE"/>

==Geography==
The commune covers an area of {{convert|262.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. Its entire [[territory (country subdivision)|territory]] lies within the central [[plain]] or "depresión intermedia", ([[Chilean Central Valley]]). Yerbas Buenas is bordered on the west by [[San Javier, Chile|San Javier]] and [[Villa Alegre, Chile|Villa Alegre]], on the north by [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], ([[Talca Province]]); on the east, by [[Colbún]], and on the south, by [[Linares, Chile|Linares]].

==Heritage==
Yerbas Buenas is a historical town with a [[cultural heritage]] and distinctive Spanish-style [[architecture]]. The town is a typical example of the preserved rural [[Colonialism|colonial]] architecture found sometimes in small towns and in large ''[[hacienda]]s'' (estates) in [[Latin America]]. Whole streets are composed only of Spanish-style buildings from the colonial period, which is very rare in Chile.

===Historical Museum===
The Historical Museum of Yerbas Buenas is housed in a preserved, colonial building that is not only one of the oldest surviving in the town and province but also a silent witness of the dawn of [[Chilean independence|Chile's independence]]. This was the house where the [[Spain|Spanish]] [[brigadier]] [[Antonio Pareja]], [[commander]] of the [[Royalist (Spanish American Revolution)|royalist army]], stayed on the night of April 26, 1813, the eve of the first significant military encounter between the [[Ferdinand VII of Spain|royalist]] and the [[Patriotism|patriot]] armies in the [[Chilean War of Independence|independence struggle of Chile]]. [[Colonel]] [[Juan de Dios Puga]], commander of the patriot force, was killed in the battle as was one third of his army. This building became a [[National Monuments of Chile|National Historic Monument]] on August 22, 1984 and the sector of the town around the building has been declared a "Typical Zone" (''Zona típica'').

==Administration==
As a commune, Yerbas Buenas is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 [[Mayor]] is Jonathan Norambuena (IND).The municipal council has the following members:<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>
* José Parra Parada ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Rodrigo González Villalobos ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Flavio Hernán Ramos Gutiérrez ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Cristina Zehender Soto ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]])
* Michael Mendez Gonzalez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Cristián Patricio Fuentealba Retamal ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|PR]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Yerbas Buenas is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Jorge Tarud ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Romilio Gutiérrez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 39th electoral district, together with [[Linares (Chile)|Linares]], [[Colbún]], [[San Javier, Chile|San Javier]] and [[Villa Alegre, Chile|Villa Alegre]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Hernán Larraín]] (UDI) and Ximena Rincón González (PDC) as part of the 11th senatorial constituency (Maule-South).
