'''Banderilla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 9&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface area of 22.21&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|35|N|96|56|W|}}.

==Name==
Tradition has it that the name alludes to a small flag (''banderilla'') that thieves would hoist on the summit of the hill of La Martinica as a signal to their accomplices that a valuable goods were being moved along the royal highway.{{citation needed|date=September 2023}}

==Geography==
The municipality of Banderilla is delimited to the north and northeast by [[Jilotepec, Veracruz|Jilotepec]], to the east and the south by [[Xalapa]], and to the southeast and west by [[Rafael Lucio]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30026a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Banderilla |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> To the municipality it is watered by tributary rivers of the [[Sedeño River]], which in turn is a tributary of the [[Actopan River]].

The weather in Banderilla is cold and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 25,993 inhabitants in 29 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSBANDERILLA>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Banderilla.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Banderilla CENSUS]</ref> More than 87% resides in the municipal seat, Banderilla; other localities include La Haciendita (1,265 hab.), Xaltepec (1,040 hab.), Luis Donaldo Colosio (293 hab.) and Fredepo (164 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSBANDERILLA/>

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[sugarcane]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

In ''' Banderilla ''', in March takes place the celebration in honor to San José, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.
