'''San Francisco''' ({{IPA|es|saɱ fɾanˈsisko}}) is a town, with a population of 3,437 (2013 census),<ref name=pop>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/atlantida/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the Department of Atlántida with at least 200 inhabitants</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Atlántida department|Atlántida]]. The largest town of the municipality is Santa Ana, with a population of 6,776 (2013 census).<ref name=pop/>
