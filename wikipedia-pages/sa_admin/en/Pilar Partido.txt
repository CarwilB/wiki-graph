'''Pilar Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northern part of [[Greater Buenos Aires]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 232,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|352|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Pilar, Buenos Aires|Pilar]], which is around {{convert|45|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

It has [[Campus Universidad del Salvador|universities]] and schools.
