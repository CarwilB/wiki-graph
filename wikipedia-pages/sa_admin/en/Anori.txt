'''Anori''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 21,477 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/anori/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 5,795&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 40.11% of the {{convert|1008167|ha|adj=on}} [[Piagaçu-Purus Sustainable Development Reserve]], established in 2003.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Piagaçu-Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS Piagaçu-Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5047|accessdate=2016-08-20}}</ref>
