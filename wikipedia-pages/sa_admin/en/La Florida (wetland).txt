'''La Florida''' is a wetland and park known as the ''Parque Metropolitano La Florida''. La Florida is located across the [[Bogotá River]] from [[Jaboque]] wetland in the municipality [[Funza]], [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], close to [[Cota, Cundinamarca|Cota]]. La Florida does not belong to the protected [[wetlands of Bogotá]].<ref name=HumedalLaFlorida>{{in lang|es}} [http://humedalesbogota.com/humedal-la-florida/ Humedal La Florida website]</ref>

=== Insects ===
The [[dragonfly]] species ''[[Ischnura cruzi]]'' has been registered in La Florida, as well as in [[La Conejera]] and [[Santa María del Lago]].<ref name=IUCNIschnuraCruzi>{{cite iucn |author=Bota-Sierra, C. |author2=Palacino, F. |author3=Rache, L. |date=2016 |title=''Ischnura cruzi'' |volume=2016 |article-number=e.T49254523A49255309 |doi=10.2305/IUCN.UK.2016-1.RLTS.T49254523A49255309.en |access-date=11 November 2021}}</ref>
