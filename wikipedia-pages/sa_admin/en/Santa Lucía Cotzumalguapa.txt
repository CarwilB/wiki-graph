'''Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsanta luˈsi.a kotsumalˈɣwapa}}) is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|529.8|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 129,473 inhabitants.

==History==
Archeological evidence indicates that the region was occupied by [[Pipil people]] who migrated from the north in the mid seventh century CE. They populated the region for around two centuries, and engaged in trade with the [[Aztecs]]. There were skirmishes with the [[Kʼicheʼ people|Kʼiche]] and [[Kaqchikel people|Kaqchikel]] tribes, who lived to the northwest of the region. After the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonialists arrived, they were converted by the Franciscan missionaries, and the region was largely abandoned for almost four centuries. The patron saint festival is celebrated on 13 December annually  in honor of the Virgin of Saint Lucia.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-de-el-municipio-de-santa-lucia-cotzumalguapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Municipio de Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/santa-lucia-cotzumalguapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|480.3|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0502__santa_luc%C3%ADa_cotzumalguap/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the northern part of the department, about {{cvt|32|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|90|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[La Gomera, Escuintla|La Gomera]] and [[Nueva Concepción]] to the south, [[Siquinalá]] to the east, [[Escuintla]] and [[Patulul]] to the west, and [[Yepocapa]] to the north.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-de-el-municipio-de-santa-lucia-cotzumalguapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 129,473 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 66,047 males and 63,426 females. About 28.9% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.4% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (52.2%) was classified as urban, while 47.8% lived in rural areas. About 86.5% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (96.0%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (3.7%) forming a significant minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 87.2%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (97.4%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
