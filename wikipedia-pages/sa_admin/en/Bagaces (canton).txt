'''Bagaces''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Bagaces District|Bagaces]] district.

== History ==
In the [[Pre-Columbia]]n era, Bagaces was home to the [[Nicarao people]] who settled in [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] after migrating from Central and southern [[Mexico]]. Evidence of [[Mesoamerica]]n style markets and Jade showed that Bagaces had flourished during Pre-Columbian times.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.buenastareas.com/ensayos/El-Reino-De-Este-Mundo/563206.html|title= The Kingdom Of This World}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.persee.fr/doc/jsa_0037-9174_1901_num_3_2_3365|title= The Aboriginals of Costa Rica}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv1qr6sk7.7?seq=8#metadata_info_tab_contents/|title = Las culturas indígenas y su medioambiente}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Central-American-Nahua-distribution-based-on-Fowler-1981-469-523-The-enclaves-in_fig1_265144861|title= Central American Nahua}}</ref> Bagaces was created on 7 December 1848 by decree 167.<ref name="crevoterritorial" /> Bagaces is also one of the oldest settlements in early Spanish conquest in the 1540s. It became an important town after 1601 when the "Mule Trail" was established communicating Cartago, the capital of the province, with Guatemala, the capital of the Captaincy General. Bagaces was a necessary night stop and later acquired fame because of its dry bisquist (Biscocho) and cheese (Queso Bagaces), an important supply for the long ride.

== Geography ==
Bagaces has an area of {{cvt|1273.49|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:396}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The Salto River on the west and Tenorio River on the east delineate this canton, with the Tempisque River as the southern border and the northern border high in the Cordillera de Guanacaste. [[Miravalles Volcano]] sits near that border's midway point.
