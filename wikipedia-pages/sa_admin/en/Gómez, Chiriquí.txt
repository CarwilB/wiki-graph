'''Gómez''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Bugaba District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|40.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,702 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|66.2|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,468; its population as of 2000 was 2,422.<ref name=inec/>
