'''Santiago Tetepec''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. It is located in the [[Jamiltepec District]] in the west of the [[Costa Region]].

As of 2020, the municipality had a population of 4,909 people<ref>{{cite web | title=México en cifras | website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (INEGI) | date=March 4, 2020 | url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=20489#collapse-Resumen | language=es | access-date=March 26, 2026}}</ref> and the town had 1,200 people.<ref name="d190">{{cite web | title=México en cifras | website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (INEGI) | date=March 4, 2020 | url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=204890001#collapse-Resumen | language=es | access-date=March 26, 2026}}</ref>
