'''Pachacútec District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Ica Province|Ica]] in [[Peru]].[[File:Pachacutec district Ica Province Ica Region Peru.png|thumbnail|Pachacútec district within Ica Province of Ica Region, Peru.]]

== History ==
Pachacútec District was created by Law 15114 (July 24, 1964).
