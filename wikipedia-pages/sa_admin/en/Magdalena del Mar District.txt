'''Magdalena del Mar''', also known simply as '''Magdalena''', is a seaside [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Lima Province]] in [[Peru]] and one of the districts that comprise the city of [[Lima]]. Its current mayor is Francis Allison Oyague. Magdalena was officially established as a district on May 10, 1920.

==Geography==
With a total land [[area]] of 3.61 km<sup>2</sup>, Magdalena borders the districts of [[San Miguel District, Lima|San Miguel]] on the west, [[Pueblo Libre]] and [[Jesús María District, Lima|Jesús María]] on the north, [[San Isidro District, Lima|San Isidro]] on the east, and the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the south.

For more than fifty years, the eastern border of the district has been disputed with neighboring San Isidro. A judge ordered the councils of both districts to deposit the money of the affected areas' taxpayers in the [[National Bank of Peru]] until this long-standing conflict is resolved.{{citation needed|date=November 2014}}

==Transportation==
The main avenues that connect the district with the rest of the city are the Ejercito avenue, the Brasil avenue, the Javier Prado avenue, the Juan de Aliaga Avenue and the Sucre avenue.

There are many bus routes operated by private companies that go through the district, however, the main publicly owned bus lines are the 201, 202, 204 and 209 line from the Corredor Rojo.

==Demographics==
According to a 2002 estimate by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 52,976 inhabitants and a population density of 14,674.8 persons/km<sup>2</sup>.
