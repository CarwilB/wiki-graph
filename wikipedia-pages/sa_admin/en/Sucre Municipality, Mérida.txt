'''Sucre Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] (''[[municipio]]s'') that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and the municipality has a population of 24,509 according to the 2011 census conducted by the [[National Institute of Statistics (Venezuela)|National Institute of Statistics]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx|title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |website=www.geohive.com | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx | archive-date=2016-03-15}}</ref> The [[municipal seat]] of the Sucre Municipality is [[Lagunillas, Mérida|Lagunillas]].<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no}}</ref>

The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]].{{cn|date=May 2026}}
