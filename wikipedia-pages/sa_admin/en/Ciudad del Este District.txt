'''Ciudad del Este''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], [[Paraguay]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/alto-parana-tiene-cuatro-distritos-segunda-ola-n2925268|title=Alto Paraná tiene cuatro distritos en segunda ola|work=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=29 January 2021|access-date=19 June 2025|language=es}}</ref> The capital is [[Ciudad del Este]].

Along with [[Asunción]], it is the only district in Paraguay with 100% urban population.
