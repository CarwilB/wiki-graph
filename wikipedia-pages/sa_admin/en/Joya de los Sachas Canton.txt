'''Joya de los Sachas''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Orellana Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[La Joya de los Sachas]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 26,363.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
