''' Atenguillo  ''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  610.2 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a population of 4107.<ref>{{cite web|title=Atenguillo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14012a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=April 13, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050728122810/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14012a.htm |archive-date=July 28, 2005 }}</ref>
