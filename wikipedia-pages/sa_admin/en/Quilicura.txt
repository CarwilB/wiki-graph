'''Quilicura''' ({{IPAc-en|k|ɪ|l|i|ˈ|k|ʊər|ə}} {{respell|KIL|i|KOOR|ə}}; {{IPA|es|kiliˈkuɾa}}) is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in capital [[Santiago]]. Founded in 1901, it was originally a satellite town on what were then the outskirts of the city of Santiago. In recent times, ongoing [[urban sprawl]] has quickly urbanized the area, transforming it from what was once prime agricultural land into an integrated part of the Santiago metropolitan area.

==Etymology==
{{Unsourced|section|date=July 2023}}
The origin of the name Quilicura is from the [[Mapuche]] language [[Mapudungun]] and comes from the words ''kila'' meaning three and ''kura'' meaning stone. The "Three Stones" are the three hills which separate the area and form the border with [[Renca]].{{Citation needed|date=February 2026}}

==Location==

Quilicura is located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]], at the northwestern edge of Chile's capital Santiago. It borders [[Renca]] to the south (naturally separated by [[Cerro Renca|Renca Hill]]), [[Pudahuel]] to the west, [[Huechuraba]] and [[Conchalí]] to the east, and [[Lampa, Chile|Lampa]] and [[Colina, Chile|Colina]] to the north.

Quilicura has relatively little green space compared to wealthier neighborhoods in the city, as it remains a predominantly industrial area. A [[Google data centers|Google data center]] operates in the area.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Salamanca |first1=Laura Rodríguez |title=Microsoft, Google say their data centers create thousands of jobs. Their permit filings say otherwise |url=https://restofworld.org/2025/data-centers-jobs-microsoft-google-chile/ |publisher=[[Rest of World]] |date=5 November 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 census, Quilicura spans an area of {{convert|57.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 126,518 inhabitants (62,421 men and 64,097 women). Of these, 125,999 (99.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 519 (0.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 207.7% (85,397 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> According to the 2012 census preliminary data Quilicura is now home to 203,946 residents.<ref name="2012census">{{cite web|url=http://www.censo.cl/2012/08/resultados_preliminares_censo_2012.pdf|title=Resultados Preliminares Censo de Población y Vivienda 2012|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadísticas|date=31 August 2012|language=es|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130116212429/http://www.censo.cl/2012/08/resultados_preliminares_censo_2012.pdf|archive-date=16 January 2013}} Note: Data are preliminary.</ref>

In 2005, the regional quality of life index for Quilicura was rated at a medium level of 72.53, ranking 34th out of 52. In 2003, the commune's [[Human Development Index]] was recorded at 0.782, placing it 19th out of 341.<ref name="auto">"[http://www.desarrollohumano.cl/otraspub/pub12/IDHC%20con%20portada.pdf Las Trayectorias del Desarrollo Humano en las Comunas de Chile (1994-2003)] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010209/http://www.desarrollohumano.cl/otraspub/pub12/IDHC%20con%20portada.pdf|date=July 7, 2011}}." [[United Nations Development Programme|UNDP]], ''Gobierno de Chile, Mideplán''</ref>

In 2011, the average annual household per capita income was estimated at US$39,302 ([[Purchasing power parity|PPP]]).<ref name="casen2011">{{cite web|title=Casen Survey 2011|publisher=Ministry of Social Development of Chile|url=http://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/casen_publicaciones.php?ano=2011|year=2011|access-date=10 September 2012|archive-date=6 October 2012|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121006040327/http://observatorio.ministeriodesarrollosocial.gob.cl/casen_publicaciones.php?ano=2011|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://stats.oecd.org/|title=OECD.StatExtracts|publisher=[[OECD]]|access-date=2012-09-10}} Note: Data refer to "PPPP41: Purchasing Power Partities for actual individual consumption." Figure used is 425.368652.</ref>

According to the 2017 census conducted by the National Institute of Statistics, the population of the commune increased to 210,410 inhabitants, of whom 103,456 (49%) were men and 106,954 (51%) were women, which corresponded to a masculinity index of 96.7.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Censo 2017 |url=http://www.censo2017.cl/ |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=www.censo2017.cl}}</ref>

In recent years, Quilicura has become home of a large [[Haitian diaspora|Haitian community]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2018-09-20 |title=Migración Haitiana en Chile |url=http://www.extranjeria.gob.cl/media/2018/01/Minuta-Haiti.pdf |access-date=2022-08-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180920162415/http://www.extranjeria.gob.cl/media/2018/01/Minuta-Haiti.pdf |archive-date=20 September 2018 |lang=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-10-10 |title=Así vive la 'petit Haití' de Santiago {{!}} Santiago {{!}} La Tercera Edición Impresa |url=http://diario.latercera.com/2011/02/13/01/contenido/santiago/32-59221-9-asi-vive-la-petit-haiti-de-santiago.shtml |access-date=2022-08-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161010125820/http://diario.latercera.com/2011/02/13/01/contenido/santiago/32-59221-9-asi-vive-la-petit-haiti-de-santiago.shtml |archive-date=2016-10-10 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=De Haití a Chile: la formación de un enclave residencial en la periferia de Santiago. |url=https://estudiosurbanos.uc.cl/exalumnos/de-haiti-a-chile-la-formacion-de-un-enclave-residencial-en-la-periferia-de-santiago/ |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=Instituto de estudios Urbanos y Territoriales UC |language=es-ES}}</ref>

==Transport==

Quilicura is located about 12&nbsp;km from [[Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport|Santiago's international airport]] and is served by the Américo Vespucio Norte Highway as well as the [[Pan-American Highway]]. [[Transantiago|RED]] public buses connect Quilicura to the centre of Santiago and run frequently in both directions. The first phase of the [[Santiago Metro Line 3]] connecting the commune with the rest of the network was completed on 22 January 2019, with phase 2 being completed in 2023. Currently, the closest metro station to Quilicura is [[Los Libertadores metro station|Los Libertadores]] which is the northern terminus of Line 3.

On the other hand, the commune belongs to the [[Electoral divisions of Chile|VII Senatorial District]] that represents the entire [[Santiago Metropolitan Region|Metropolitan Region of Santiago]].
