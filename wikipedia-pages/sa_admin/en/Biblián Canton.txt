'''Biblián Canton Ecuador''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Cañar Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Biblián]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 20,500.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> Population has remained unchanged for the past two decades. Most of its original inhabitants have immigrated to the United States and Spain, while internal immigration from other cities in the province has helped keep the population at about 20,000 since the 1980s
