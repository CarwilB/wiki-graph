'''Santa María Ixcatlan''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in southeastern Mexico and is the second least densely populated municipality in Oaxaca behind [[Santa María Chimalapa]]. 
It is part of the [[Teotitlán District, Oaxaca|Teotitlán District]] in the north of the [[Cañada, Oaxaca|Cañada Region]].<ref name=INF>{{cite web

==Geography==
The municipality has an area of 201.58&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an elevation of 1,840 meters above sea level.
In the settled areas the climate is hot and humid with abundant rainfall in summer.
Other areas are arid with little rainfall.
===Flora and fauna===
Trees include morillo, [[oak]] and [[juniper]], and fruit-bearing peach, apricot, pomegranates, figs and [[Crataegus|hawthorn]].  
Local birds include chachalaca, [[quail]], [[Columbidae|dove]], sparrow, goldfinch, orioles, [[hawk]]s and [[eagle]]s. 
Wild life includes coyote, fox, wild cat, wild boar, badger, rattlesnake, coral snake, lizards and scorpions.<ref name=INF/>

==Demographics==
As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 573, of whom 37 people spoke an indigenous language.<ref name=INF/>
The town is the only one inhabited by speakers of the [[Ixcatec language]].  
According to the ''Consejo Nacional para la Cultura y las Artes'', there were only 8 speakers of the [[Ixcatec language|Ixcateco]] (Xwja) language in 2008.<ref>{{cite web

Most of the population is engaged in cultivation of maize, beans, wheat and barley, and also in manufacture of artisan products made from palms. There is small-scale animal husbandry of goat, cattle, horses and donkeys.

==Economy==
[[Mezcal]] is produced in the village.<ref name=INF/>
