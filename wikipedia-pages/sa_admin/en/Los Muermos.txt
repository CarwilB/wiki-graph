'''Los Muermos''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Llanquihue Province]], [[Los Lagos Region]] in southern [[Chile]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Los Muermos spans an area of {{convert|1245.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 16,964 inhabitants (8,939 men and 8,025 women). Of these, 5,707 (33.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 11,257 (66.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 0.5% (90 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Climate==

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Los Muermos is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Emilio González Burgos ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Los Muermos is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Fidel Espinoza ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Recondo (UDI) as part of the 56th electoral district, together with [[Puyehue, Chile|Puyehue]], [[Río Negro, Chile|Río Negro]], [[Purranque]], [[Puerto Octay]], [[Fresia, Chile|Fresia]], [[Frutillar]], [[Llanquihue, Chile|Llanquihue]] and [[Puerto Varas]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina (PS) and Carlos Kuschel Silva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
