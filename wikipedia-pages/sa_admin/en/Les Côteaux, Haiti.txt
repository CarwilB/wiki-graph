'''Les Côteaux''' ({{IPA|fr|le koto}}; {{langx|ht|Koto}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Les Côteaux Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2015, it had a population of 21,302 inhabitants.
