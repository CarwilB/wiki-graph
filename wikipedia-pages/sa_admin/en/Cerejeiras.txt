'''Cerejeiras''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 16,204 (2020) and its area is 2,783&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/HBA]</ref>

The municipality contains 28% of the {{convert|384055|ha|adj=on}} [[Corumbiara State Park]], created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES de Corumbiara – ISA}}|language=pt
