'''Itabaianinha''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|itabajɐˈnĩɲɐ|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. In 2020, its population was 42,166, and its area is {{cvt|493|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/itabaianinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Itabaianinha is noted for its large [[Dwarfism|dwarf]] population. It is sometimes called "a cidade dos anões" ("the city of dwarfs").<ref>{{cite web|last1=Gonzalez-Ramirez|first1=Andrea|title=Inside The City With The World's Largest Population Of People With Dwarfism|url=https://www.refinery29.com/en-us/2016/09/120414/dwarves-dwarfism-genes-brazil-city-photos|website=Refinery29|date=2016-09-01|access-date=2021-06-04}}</ref> The dwarfism is of the proportionate type and arises from a rare genetic mutation.<ref>{{cite journal|last1=Hayashida|first1=CY|last2=Gondo|first2=RG|last3=Ferrari|first3=C|last4=Toledo|first4=SP|last5=Salvatori|first5=R|last6=Levine|first6=MA|last7=Ezabella|first7=MC|last8=Abelin|first8=N|last9=Gianella-Neto|first9=D|last10=Wajchenberg|first10=BL|title=Familial growth hormone deficiency with mutated GHRH receptor gene: clinical and hormonal findings in homozygous and heterozygous individuals from Itabaianinha.|journal=European Journal of Endocrinology|date=June 2000|volume=142|issue=6|pages=557–63|pmid=10822217}}</ref> The population probably originated through [[Consanguine marriage|intrafamily marriages]]. The condition has been shown<ref>{{cite web|title=Human Growth Hormone: Not a Life Extender After All?|url=http://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/news/media/releases/human_growth_hormone_not_a_life_extender_after_all|publisher=Johns Hopkins Medicine|date=26 January 2010}}</ref> to have no effect on normal lifespan. In the late 1990s many residents of Itabaianinha under 15 years started receiving hormonal treatment. The population of dwarfs is dwindling, with only an estimated one hundred remaining, mostly in the village of Carretéis, ten miles from Itabaianinha center.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Paolini|first1=Giulia Valentina|last2=Pitzalis|first2=Bruno|title=City of Dwarfs|url=http://narrative.ly/city-of-dwarfs/|website=Narratively|date=19 November 2014}}</ref>
