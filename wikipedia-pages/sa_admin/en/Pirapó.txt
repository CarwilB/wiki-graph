'''Pirapó''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil. The population is 2,254 (2020 est.) in an area of 295.01&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/117G Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The town is situated by the [[Ijuí River]], close to its confluence with the [[Uruguay River]], which forms the border with [[Argentina]]. It is located 563&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]] and northeast of [[Alegrete]]. 

Like many towns in the state which were first settled by German-speaking Europeans, the German language is still present in daily family and community life, if not as much in the public sphere since [[World War II]]; the regional German dialect is called [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]], as it is a Brazilian variant of the dialect spoken in the [[Hunsrück]] region of southwest Germany. In 2012 the state chamber of deputies voted unanimously in favor of recognizing this Germanic dialect an official historical culture good to be preserved.

The municipality would be partially flooded by the proposed [[Garabí Dam]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos}}|language=pt |title=Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos|publisher=Eletrobras CGTEE|date=7 April 2014
