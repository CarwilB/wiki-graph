'''Omitlán de Juárez''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|110.5|km2}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 7,529.<ref>{{cite web|title=Omitlán de Juárez  |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13045a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 27, 2008}}</ref>

==Toponymy==

The name ''Omitlán'' in the [[Nahuatl]] language means ''Ome'' 'two' and ''tlan'' 'place', so it is understood that it means ''Place of two''.<ref group=note>The etymological origin of the name of a municipality can have various interpretations or have an uncertain origin, this is the most common or most accepted by the municipal government and its inhabitants, according to the ''Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Mexico.''</ref><ref name="etym">{{cite book|author=Centro Estatal de Estudios Municipales del Estado de Hidalgo|date=1988|publisher=Secretaría de Gobernación|series=Colección Enciclopedia de los municipios de México|title=Los Municipios de Hidalgo}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref name=gob>{{cite web|access-date=22 March 2017|archive-date=14 April 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170414081341/http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/omitlan_de_juarez|publisher=[[Gobierno del estado de Hidalgo]]|title=Omitlán de Juárez|url=http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/omitlan_de_juarez|work=[[Municipios de Hidalgo]]}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> The surname ''de Juárez'' was given to him by the former president of Mexico, Benito Juárez García.<ref name=gob />

==Geography==
{{See also|Geography of the state of Hidalgo}}
Omitlán de Juárez stands on [[Mexican Federal Highway 105|Federal Highway 105]], which connects [[Pachuca]], Hidalgo, to [[Tempoal de Sánchez]], Veracruz.

===Terrain and hydrology===

Omitlán de Juárez is located within the province of the [[Neovolcanic Axis]]; within the subprovince of Plains and Sierras of Querétaro and Hidalgo.<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> Its terrain is [[mountain chain|mountainous]] (81.0%) and [[plain]] (19.0%) [[highland]]s.<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> The hills known as Cerro Gordo, Cerro del Gallo and Peña del Zumate stand out.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

Its geolog corresponds to the Neogene period (98.23%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> With extrusive igneous rocks: intermediate volcanic breccia andesite (53.23%), acid tuff (27.0%), acid tuff, acid volcanic breccia (7.0%) and basalt (11.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> Regarding [[soil science]] the dominant soil is [[phaeozem]] (72.23%), [[luvisol]] (17.0%) and [[regosol]] (9.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo />

With regard to hydrology, it is positioned in the hydrological region of the [[Pánuco River|Pánuco]]; in the basins of the [[Moctezuma River]]; within the sub-basins of the [[Amajac River]] (51.0%) and [[Metztitlán River]] (49.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> It has the Amajac and Bandola rivers, the latter of which flows into the Los Angeles Dam. It has two bodies of water.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

===Weather===

The municipal territory is found in the following climates with their respective percentage: Temperate sub-humid with rainfall in summer, more humid (92.0%) and semi-cold sub-humid with rains in summer, more humid (8.0%).<ref name=ProntuarioHidalgo /> With an average annual temperature of 14&nbsp;°C and an annual rainfall of 700 to 1200 millimeters.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

===Flora and fauna===

The flora in the municipality is made up of oak, prickly pear, oyamel, ash, pine, walnut, quebrancha, tepozán, strawberry tree, as well as trees such as peach, pear, plum, blackberry, apple, etc.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH /> The [[fauna]] includes animals such as foxes, crows, squirrels, gophers, rattlesnakes, ouncers and a wide variety of songbirds, as well as insects and arachnids of different species.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

According to the results presented by the 2020 Population and Housing Census of INEGI, the municipality has a total of 9295 inhabitants, 4422 men and 4873 women.<ref name="INEGI 2020">{{cite web|access-date=25 January 2021|author=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|date=2020|title=Principales resultados por localidad (ITER). Hidalgo|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/descarga/ficha.html?tit=325911&ag=0&f=csv}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref name="Panorama INEGI">{{cite book|access-date=20 March 2021|author1=INEGI|date=2021|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]]|title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Hidalgo. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020|url=https://inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825197865.pdf}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> It has a density of 116.6 inhabitants/km², half of the population is 29 years old or younger, there are 90 males for every 100 females.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" />

The percentage of the population that speaks [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]] is 0.31%,<ref group=note>Percentage of population aged 3 years and over. and the percentage of the population that</ref> considers itself [[Afro-Mexican]] or [[African diaspora|Afro-descendant]] is 0.22%.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> It has a [[Literacy rate]] of 99.4% in the population aged 15 to 24, and 92.1% in the population aged 25 and over.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> The percentage of population by level of schooling,.<ref group=note>Percentage of population aged 15 and over</ref> It is 5.5% without schooling, 65.2% with [[basic education]], 20.5% with [[upper secondary education]], 8.7% with [[higher education]], and 0.1% unspecified.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" />

The percentage of the population affiliated with health services is 62.3%. <ref group=note>Includes multiple affiliations.</ref> 27.3% are affiliated with the [[Mexican Social Security Institute|IMSS]], 66.2% with [[INSABI]], 5.8% with the [[ISSSTE]], 0.4% with IMSS Bienestar, 0.1% with the health units of [[PEMEX]], [[Social Security Institute for the Mexican Armed Forces|Defense or Navy]], 1.1% to a private institution, and 0.1% to another institution.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> The percentage of the population with a disability is 5.1%.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> The percentage of population by marital status,.<ref group=note>Percentage of population aged 12 and over</ref> 30.2% are [[married]], 31.9% [[Single person|single]], 26.4% are in common-law unions, 5.0% are separated, 1.2% are divorced, 5.3% are widowed.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" />

For 2020, the total number of inhabited private homes is 2506 homes, representing 0.3% of the state total.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> With an average number of occupants per dwelling of 3.2 people.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /> Houses with partition and block walls predominate.<ref name=basic>{{cite web|access-date=22 March 2017|archive-date=30 December 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171230230125/http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/045%20Omitlan%20de%20Juarez.pdf|author1=Sistema Integral de Información del Estado de Hidalgo|publisher=[[Gobierno del estado de Hidalgo]]|title=Información Estadística Básica: Carpeta Municipal Omitlán de Juárez|url=http://siieh.hidalgo.gob.mx/PDFS/045%20Omitlan%20de%20Juarez.pdf|work=Secretaria de Planeación Desarrollo Regional Regional y Metropolitano}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> In the municipality, for the year 2020, the electric power service covers a coverage of 99.4%; piped water service by 52.1%; [[drainage]] service covers 89.7%; and the health service by 91.9%.<ref name="Panorama INEGI" /><ref name=basic />

==Economy==
{{See also|Economy of the State of Hidalgo}}

In 2015 the municipality had an [[HDI]] of 0.701 <span style="color:#00CC00">'''High''',</span> so [[Annex:Municipalities of Hidalgo by HDI|ranks 45th at the state level]];<ref name=IDH2015>{{cite web|access-date=22 July 2019|author1=COESPO|date=2019|publisher=[[Gobierno del estado de Hidalgo]]|title=Hidalgo. Índice de Desarrollo Humano Municipal 2015|url=http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/pdf/idh%202015_mun_hgo_web.pdf|work=Consejo Estatal de Población|archive-date=27 July 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200727040938/http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/pdf/idh%202015_mun_hgo_web.pdf|url-status=dead}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> and in 2005 it had a GDP of $326,367,496.00 and a GDP per capita of $43,348.00 (current 2005 prices).<ref name=IDH2005>{{cite web|access-date=22 March 2017|archive-date=8 December 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151208141416/http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/descargables/IDH%20municipal%202000-2005.pdf|date=2005|publisher=[[Gobierno del estado de Hidalgo]]|title=Hidalgo. Indicadores sociodemográficos e índices de dedesarrollo humano por municipio, 2000 y 2005.|url=http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/descargables/IDH%20municipal%202000-2005.pdf|work=Consejo Estatal de Población}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref><ref name="SNIM">{{cite web|access-date=3 March 2017|author=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|publisher=[[Gobierno de México]]|title=Sistema Nacional de Información Municipal|url=http://www.snim.rami.gob.mx/|work=[[Secretaría de Gobernación de México|Secretaría de Gobernación]]}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --> To view information, first select the type of information you want, then the state of Hidalgo in the "Federative entity" field, and then choose in the "municipality" field.</ref>

According to the [[National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy]] (Coneval), the municipality registers a [[Marginalization|Marginalization Index]] Medium.<ref name=basic /> 47.5% of the population is in moderate poverty and 15.1% is in extreme poverty.<ref name=basic /> In 2015, the municipality ranked 43rd out of 84 municipalities in the state scale of social backwardness.<ref name=coneval1>{{cite web|author=Coneval |author-link=National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy  |url=http://diariooficial.gob.mx/SEDESOL/2017/Hidalgo_045.pdf|title=Informe Annual Report on the Situation of Poverty and Social Backwardness: Omitlán de Juárez, Hidalgo (2017)|work=[[Secretariat of Social Development (Mexico)|Ministry of Social Development]]|publisher=[[Government of Mexico]]|accessdate=March 3, 2017}}</ref>

As of 2015 data, in terms of agriculture, in this municipality it is mostly corn grain and forage oats, and although in a smaller proportion apples, peaches and barley grain are planted.<ref name=SIIEH /><ref name=basic /> In [[livestock]] the majority of livestock production is sheep and poultry and to a lesser extent goats.<ref name=SIIEH /><ref name=basic />

As of 2015, there are 113 economic units, which generated jobs for 146 people.<ref name=basic /> As far as commerce is concerned, there are two [[tianguis]], seven [[Diconsa]] stores, and one [[Liconsa]] store; as well as a municipal market, and a municipal flea market.<ref name=basic /> According to figures for the year 2015 presented in the Economic Censuses by INEGI, the [[Economically Active Population]] (EAP) of the municipality amounts to 3280 of which 3098 are employed and 182 are unemployed. <ref group=note>Size of the community according to the number of inhabitants for the population aged 12 years and over.</ref><ref name=basic /> 16.30% belong to the [[primary sector]], 33.63% belong to the [[secondary sector]], 49.80% belong to the [[tertiary sector]] and 0.27% did not specify.
