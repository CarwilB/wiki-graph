'''Santa Cruz del Norte''' is a town and municipality in the [[Mayabeque Province]] of [[Cuba]]. Before 2011 it was part of [[La Habana Province]]. It is located on the north shore, between the cities of [[Havana]] and [[Matanzas]], at the mouth of the Santa Cruz River.

==Overview==
[[Image:Roneria Havana Club.JPG|250px|left|thumb|Havana Club distillery]]
The town was founded in 1800, being a [[fishing]] community at that time, and achieved municipality status in 1931.<ref name=guije/>

This is the main home of the [[Havana Club]] [[rum]] distillery; all dark varieties being produced in Santa Cruz del Norte. The [[distillery]] can be visited as part of a tour.

Other industries in the municipality include [[energy|power]] generation, [[petroleum|oil]] extraction and fishing.
{{clear|left}}

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the ''consejos populares'' of Santa Cruz del Norte (the town proper) and the villages of [[Bacunayagua]], [[Boca de Jaruco]], [[Jibacoa]], [[El Fraile (Santa Cruz del Norte)|El Fraile]], [[Arcos de Canasí]], [[El Comino]], [[La Sierra (Santa Cruz del Norte)|La Sierra]], [[Camilo Cienfuegos (Santa Cruz del Norte)|Hershey]] (or Camilo Cienfuegos), [[Loma del Tanque]], [[El Rubio (Santa Cruz del Norte)|El Rubio]] and [[Paula (Santa Cruz del Norte)|Paula]].<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/hsantac/index.htm|title=Santa Cruz del Norte|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>  A spur of the [[Hershey Electric Railway]] connects the town to [[Matanzas]].  This is used mainly by diesel powered freight trains.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Santa Cruz del Norte had a population of 34,907.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|376|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|93|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
