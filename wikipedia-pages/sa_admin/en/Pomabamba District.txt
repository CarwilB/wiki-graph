'''Pomabamba''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Pumaq pampa''', ''pumaq'' [[cougar]], ''pampa'' large plain,<ref>{{Ref Laime}}</ref> "cougar plain") is the largest of 4 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Pomabamba Province]] of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>[http://desa.inei.gob.pe INEI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601020523/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ |date=2007-06-01 }}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (76.16%) learnt to speak in childhood, 23.62% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que apredió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
