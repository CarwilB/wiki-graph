'''Carache''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 957&nbsp;km² with a population of 32,820 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Towns and parishes==
The municipality is divided into the following parishes, each of which contain smaller towns.
* '''[[Carache, Venezuela|Carache]]''': 11,592 (40% of pop.)
* [[La Concepción, Trujillo|La Concepción]]: 5,583 (19%)
* [[Cuicas, Venezuela|Cuicas]]: 5,825 (20%)
* [[Panamericana, Venezuela|Panamericana]]: 1,926 (7%)
* [[Santa Cruz, Trujllo|Santa Cruz]]: 4,125 (14%)
