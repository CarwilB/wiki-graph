'''Aramina''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 5,655 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|203|km2}}.<ref>[https://archive.today/20150919215107/http://cod.ibge.gov.br/YW4 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 614 m.
