'''Bajo Boquete  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Boquete District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], Panama. It is the seat of Boquete District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|18.2|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 4,493 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|246.5|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 10,908; its population as of 2000 was 3,833.<ref name=inec/>
