'''San Rafael''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Talca Province]] in the [[Maule Region]] of [[Chile]]. The town serves as the communal [[Capital (political)|capital]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Rafael spans an area of {{convert|263.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 7,674 inhabitants (3,903 men and 3,771 women). Of these, 3,482 (45.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,192 (54.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 6.5% (465 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Rafael is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcaldesa is Claudia Alejandra Diaz Bravo. ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Rafael is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca (UDI) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Empedrado, Chile|Empedrado]], [[Pencahue]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], [[Pelarco]] and [[Río Claro, Chile|Río Claro]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
