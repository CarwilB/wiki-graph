'''San Patricio del Chañar''' is a town located in [[Añelo Department]], in [[Neuquén Province]], Argentina.

== Economy ==
In San Patricio del Chañar agriculture is one of the most important activities. The main crop is [[grape]]s, used for the production of wine, a new business for the area and the province.

===Energy sources===
The town is provided of electric energy by a small dam located in the area.

==Culture==
The word ''Chañar'' comes from the [[Quechuan languages|Quechuan language]] name for a kind of bush (chical in [[Spanish language|Spanish]]).
