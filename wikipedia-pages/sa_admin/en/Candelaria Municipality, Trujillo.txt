'''Candelaria''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 411&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 27,811 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following seven parishes, with their capitals listed in parentheses:

*Arnoldo Gabaldón (Minas)
*Bolivia (Bolivia)
*Carrillo (Torococo)
*Cegarra (Mitón)
*Chejendé (Chejendé)
*Manuel Salvador Ulloa (Sabana Grande) 
*San José (Las Llanadas)
