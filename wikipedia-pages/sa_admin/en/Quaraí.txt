'''Quarai''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located on the border with [[Uruguay]] on the [[Rio Quaraí]]. Its population is 22,607 (2020 est.) in an area of 3,147.63&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, making it one of the largest municipalities in the state.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/MNI Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 112 m. It is located 590&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Montevideo]], Uruguay and southeast of [[Alegrete]].

==Flag==

Created in 1971, the flag is made up of three stripes, blue, green and white in normal tones, it features a blue and red triangle-rectangle and a quadrilateral green that ascends between the two triangles with a blue triangle and in the left side of the flag, the angle of the green triangle is low and right.  The center is elliptically vertical, in the white panel, the coat of arms added under law 400 21 December 1964.{{fact|date=August 2025}}

==Geography==

The area includes the Cerro do Jarau, a crater with 5.5&nbsp;km in diameter dating back to around 117 million years and Salamanda do Jarau.

==History==

The first inhabitants were [[Amerindian|South American Indian]]s including the [[Jarros]], [[Guenoas]], [[Minuanos]] and the [[Charruas]] people.  The city was created on 8 April 1875.

==The creation==

The diplomatic action of the [[Treaty of Badajoz (1801)|Treaty of Badajoz]] was signed in 1801 in which the then territorial boundary of Rio Grande do Sul became bounded at the [[Rio Quaraí]].  In 1814, its first ranches were founded.{{fact|date=August 2025}}
