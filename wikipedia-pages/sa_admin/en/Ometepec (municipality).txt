''' Ometepec  ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ometepec]].  The municipality covers an area of 1,100.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 55,283.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ometepec|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12046a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617102357/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12046a.htm|archive-date=June 17, 2011}}</ref>
