'''Engenheiro Navarro''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/mg/engenheiro-navarro.html |title=Engenheiro Navarro on IBGE Brazil}}</ref> Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 7,241 people living in a total area of 632&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/engenheiro-navarro/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[North of Minas]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Bocaiúva]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

Engenheiro Navarro is located at an elevation 637 meters on the railroad line that links [[Belo Horizonte]] to [[Montes Claros]]. This line is no longer used for passenger traffic. Highway BR-135, which links Montes Claros to the state boundary with [[Goiás]] passes at a distance of 4 kilometers to the east. The distance to [[Bocaiúva]] is 35&nbsp;km; the distance to [[Montes Claros]] is 85&nbsp;km; and the distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 347&nbsp;km. Neighboring  municipalities are:  [[Bocaiúva]] (N) and (E); [[Joaquim Felício]] (S); [[Francisco Dumont]] and [[Claro dos Poções]] (W).<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/bocaiúva/ Citybrazil]{{Dead link|date=August 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The main economic activities are cattle raising, services, and agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was R$23 million, with 14 million in services, 2 million in industry, and 5 million in agriculture. There were 268 rural producers on 40,000 hectares of land. The main crops were cotton, sugarcane, beans, and corn. There were 27,000 head of cattle (2006).<ref name="IBGE"/>

Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.
