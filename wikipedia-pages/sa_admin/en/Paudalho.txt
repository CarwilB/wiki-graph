'''Paudalho''' is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]].

==Geography==

* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Tracunhaém]] ('''N'''), [[São Lourenço da Mata]], [[Chã de Alegria]], [[Camaragibe]] and [[Glória do Goitá]] ('''S'''), [[Carpina]] and [[Lagoa do Itaenga]] ('''W'''), [[Paulista]] and [[Abreu e Lima]] ('''E''')
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and [[Goiana River|Goiana]] rivers
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - {{convert|25.2|C|F}}
* Distance to [[Recife]] - {{convert|42|km|mi}}

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Paudalho''' are based in extractive industry, commerce and primary sector especially [[sugarcane]] and [[cattle]].
