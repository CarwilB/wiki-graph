'''Capital''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 159,703 inhabitants<ref>[http://www.censo2010.indec.gov.ar/CuadrosDefinitivos/P1-P_Catamarca.pdf 2010 Census] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141205052339/http://www.censo2010.indec.gov.ar/CuadrosDefinitivos/P1-P_Catamarca.pdf# |date=2014-12-05 }} from [[National Institute of Statistics and Census of Argentina|INDEC]] official website.</ref> in an area of {{cvt|684|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San Fernando del Valle de Catamarca]], which is located around {{cvt|1,145|km}} from [[Capital federal|Buenos Aires]].
