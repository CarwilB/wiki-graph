'''Jacmel''' ({{langx|ht|Jakmèl}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 338,728 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref>

Postal codes in the Jacmel Arrondissement start with the number 91.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Jacmel]]
* [[Cayes-Jacmel]]
* [[La Vallée, Sud-Est|La Vallée]]
* [[Marigot, Sud-Est|Marigot]]

==History==

===2010 7.0 earthquake===

==== Recovery ====
In December 2010, the Capponi Construction Group, a Miami-based construction company decided to help with the revitalization of a Jacmel. This initiative was created to help stimulate a self-sustained tourism economy for Haiti. [[Michael Capponi]], founder of the Capponi Group, formed the Jacmel Advisory Council to help revitalize Jacmel, while preserving its arts, culture and traditions. The board promotes best practices to provide a socio-economic system for thousands of Haitians living in the south east region. Capponi Group Haiti is also restoring a 200-year-old coffee sorting house on the port of Jacmel.
{{further|2010 Haiti earthquake}}

[[Jacmel]], a city with many French colonial heritage buildings, was devastated by [[2010 Haiti earthquake|a 7.0 magnitude tremblor]] on 12 January 2010, collapsing much of its heritage architecture.<ref>CNN, "The Situation Room", 15 January 2010</ref>

==Facilities==
The largest health centre/hospital in the region is Saint-Michel in [[Jacmel]]. It was severely damaged in the 12 January 2010 earthquake.<ref>{{in lang|fr}} IRIN, [http://www.irinnews.org/fr/ReportFrench.aspx?ReportId=87948 "HAÏTI: Les craintes d’un tremblement de terre persistent"], ''2 February 2010'' (accessed 2 February 2010)</ref>
