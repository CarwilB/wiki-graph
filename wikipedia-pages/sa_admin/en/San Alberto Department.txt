'''San Alberto Department''' is a [[departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province, Argentina]]. It is composed by many [[municipality|municipalities]] and [[Communes of Argentina|communes]]. Its main industry is [[tourism]] and it is visited by many people each summer.
San Alberto includes cities and towns as [[Mina Clavero]], [[Villa Cura Brochero]] and [[Nono, Córdoba|Nono]].
