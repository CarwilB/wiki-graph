'''Santa Catarina''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Nuevo León|municipalities]] of [[Nuevo León]] in [[Northern Mexico]]. Its municipal seat is located at [[Santa Catarina, Mexico|Santa Catarina]], as municipalities in Mexico are often named after their seats. It is included within the [[Monterrey metropolitan area]].

The municipal government is headed by the municipal president of Santa Catarina (aka mayor of Santa Catarina).
