'''Barroquinha''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Ceará]]. The population was estimated at 15,044 in 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ce/barroquinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|313800|ha}} [[Delta do Parnaíba Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|APA Delta do Parnaíba (Federal) – ISA}}
