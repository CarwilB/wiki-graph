'''Puerto Triunfo''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the [[Magdalena Medio Antioquia]] sub-region.

Near this town is located the ''[[Hacienda Napoles]]'', which was once owned by [[Pablo Escobar]].

==Natural wonders==
* Claro river canyon
* La Danta caves
