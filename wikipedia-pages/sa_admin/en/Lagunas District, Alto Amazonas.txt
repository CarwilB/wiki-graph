'''Lagunas District''' is one of the six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Alto Amazonas Province|Alto Amazonas]] Province, in the Department of [[Loreto Region|Loreto]], in [[Peru]]. It is bordered by the districts of Alto Pastaza, [[Pastaza District|Pastaza]], [[Jeberos District|Jeberos]], Santa Cruz, [[Urarinas District|Urarinas]] and Parinari.

The capital, Lagunas, sits on the [[Huallaga River]]. The nearest road access is in [[Yurimaguas]], situated 90&nbsp;km (56 miles) to the south. To reach Yurimaguas by boat takes at least five hours.

==History==

The settlement of ''Santiago de Lagunas'' was established in 1670 by a priest, ''Juan Lorenzo Lucero''.

==Human Geography==

Lagunas District is home to members of the Kokama and Chayahuita indigenous groups.

==Tourism==

Lagunas is located 12&nbsp;km (7.5 miles) from Ranger Station 8 (PV8) of the Pacaya-Samiria National Reserve. It is therefore a starting point to explore the north-western section of the Reserve.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.perunorth.com/news/2018/3/6/lagunas-the-other-gateway-to-pacaya-samiria|title=Lagunas - The 'Other' Gateway to Pacaya-Samiria|work=Peru North|access-date=2018-03-06|language=en-US}}</ref>
