'''Pallasca''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Pallasqa''') is one of 20 provinces of the [[Ancash Region]] in Peru.

Some of the lakes of the district are [[Challwaqucha (Ancash)|Challwaqucha]], [[Kinwaqucha (Ancash)|Kinwaqucha]], [[Llamaqucha]], [[Paryaqucha (Pallasca)|Paryaqucha]], [[Pusaqqucha]], [[P'itiqucha (Ancash)|P'itiqucha]], [[T'uruqucha (Ancash)|T'uruqucha]] and [[Wayq'uqucha]].
