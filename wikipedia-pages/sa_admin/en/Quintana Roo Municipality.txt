'''Quintana Roo Municipality''' (Named after a patriot, [[Andrés Quintana Roo]], from [[Mérida, Yucatán]], who supported Mexican independence.)<ref name="Name meaning">{{cite web|title=Quintana Roo|url=http://www.history.com/topics/mexico/quintana-roo|website=History|publisher=A&E Television Networks|access-date=9 June 2015}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 139.24&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 104&nbsp;km east of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
In pre-Hispanic times this area belonged to the chieftainship of Cupules. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821. In 1871 the ranch Hobchén which had been part of the [[Dzitás Municipality]] became a pueblo with the name of Quintana Roo. In 1931, it was designated as its own municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four aldermen, who serve as Secretary of the Town Hall; and councilors of public works, public security and potable water.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Quintana Roo|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=4 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Quintana Roo, Yucatán|Quintana Roo]], Yucatán. The other populated areas in the municipality are Carrillo, Dzulotok Pueblo, Maxcapixcil, San Andrés, San Antonio, Santa Inés, San Juan, and Santa María. ==Local festivals==
Every year from 23 to 27 February is a fiesta of the pueblo; from 8 to 12 June the feast in honor of St. Barnabas is celebrated; and the last of August, a celebration for San Bartolomé is held.<ref name=municipios />

==Tourist attractions==
* Church of San Bartolomé, built in the eighteenth century
* The Chapel of La Mejorada, built in the eighteenth century
* Hacienda Calcehtoc
