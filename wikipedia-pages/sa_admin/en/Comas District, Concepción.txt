'''Comas District''' is one of fifteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Concepción Province, Peru|Concepción Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
