'''San Juan de Tarucani District''' ([[Aymara language|Aymara]] '''Tarujani''', ''taruja'' [[deer]],<ref name=bertonio/> ''-ni'' a suffix, "the one with deer") is one of 29 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Arequipa Province|Arequipa]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
