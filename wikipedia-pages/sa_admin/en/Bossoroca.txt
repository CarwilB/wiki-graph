'''Bossoroca''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 6,205 (2020 est.) in an area of 1610.57 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/108S Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The name comes from the [[Guarani language]], and may mean [[erosion]].  It is located 524&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].

==History==

The area was first inhabited by the [[Guarani people|Guarani]] tribes. In the 17th and 18th centuries, [[Misiones Orientales|Jesuit missions]] were established in the area. The municipality of Bossoroca was created on March 4, 1967.
