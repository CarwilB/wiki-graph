'''Jérémie''' ({{langx|ht|Jeremi}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse Department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 238,218 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Anse d'Hainault Arrondissement start with the number 71.

==Communes==
The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Jérémie]]
* [[Abricots]]
* [[Bonbon, Grand'Anse|Bonbon]]
* [[Chambellan]]
* [[Moron, Grand'Anse|Moron]]
* [[Marfranc]]

==History==
In the aftermath of the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]], a food aide convoy transporting aide delivered to [[Jérémie Airport]] through [[Jérémie]], encountered a hijacking attempt by 20 men, on Saturday 30 January 2010.<ref>Newser, [https://www.newser.com/article/d9dk9mb81/food-convoy-attacked-by-armed-group-in-haiti-un-warns-of-volatile-security-situation.html "Food convoy attacked by armed group in Haiti; UN warns of volatile security situation"]{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}, '''Paisley Doods''', ''2 February 2010'' (accessed 3 February 2010)</ref>
