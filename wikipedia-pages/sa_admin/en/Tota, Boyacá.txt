'''Tota''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], [[Colombia]], part of the [[Sugamuxi Province]]. Tota is located approximately 40&nbsp;km from [[Sogamoso]] and the municipality borders in the north [[Cuítiva, Boyacá|Cuítiva]], [[Aquitania, Boyacá|Aquitania]] in the east, [[Zetaquirá]] and [[San Eduardo, Boyacá|San Eduardo]] in the south and westward of Tota the municipality of [[Pesca, Boyacá|Pesca]] is located.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tota-boyaca.gov.co/index.shtml Official website Tota] - accessed 02-05-2016</ref> Nearby and named after the village is [[Lake Tota]], the largest lake of Colombia.

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] of the central highlands of Colombia ([[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]]), the area around Tota was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], [[Muisca rulers|ruled]] by the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]], currently known as Tunja. In the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca, ''Tota'' means "tillage of the river".<ref name=EtymologyMuisca>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Tota] - accessed 02-05-2016</ref>

The first European to enter the territories of the northern [[Muisca Confederation]] was [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]].

== Economy ==
The economical activities of Tota are mainly [[agriculture]] (potatoes, carrots, peas, onions and others), [[livestock]] farming, [[mining]] and [[tourism]] ([[Lake Tota]]).<ref name=website/>
