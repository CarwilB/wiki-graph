'''Independencia''' ({{IPA|es|indepenˈdensja}}, meaning "independence") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]], situated in the [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]] of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. The commune's main thoroughfare is [[Independencia Avenue (Santiago de Chile)|Independencia Avenue]]. It has a population of 116,943.<ref name="2024census" />

== History ==
[[File:Iglesia del Niño Jesús de Praga, Santiago 20200209 01.jpg|thumb|150px|Iglesia del Niño Jesús de Praga]]
[[File:Hospital Clínico de la Universidad de Chile, Independencia, Santiago 20230817 06.jpg|thumb|Universidad de Chile Hospital]]
[[File:Cervecería Ebner, Independencia, Santiago 20220529 01.jpg|thumb|[[Cervecería Ebner]]]]

The [[Hipódromo Chile]] horse racetrack opened in 1906, and hosts the Gran Premio Hipódromo Chile and the Clásico St. Leger. One of the commune's greatest achievements was the construction of [[Estadio Independencia (Chile)|Estadio Independencia]], owned by [[Club Deportivo Universidad Católica]]. The stadium, demolished in 1971, was used for Chilean and Latin American sports between 1945 and 1967.<ref name="Opening">{{cite news |title=La Universidad...|url=https://culturadigital.udp.cl/dev/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/LN_1945_10_13.pdf|language=es|newspaper=[[La Nación (Chile)|La Nación]]|date=13 October 1945|access-date=12 May 2025 |location= Santiago |archive-url=  |archive-date= }}</ref> Another venue built in the commune was the [[Estadio Santa Laura-Universidad SEK|Estadio Santa Laura]], owned by [[Unión Española]], which is still in use today.

== Demographics ==
As of the 2024 census, the commune has a population of 116,943, of which 48.7% are male and 51.3% are female. People under 15 years old make up 17.5% of the population, and people over 65 years old make up 11.0%. 100% of the population is urban.<ref name="2024census" />

=== Immigration ===
As of the 2024 census, Independencia has the highest share of immigrants out of all Chilean communes, as they make up 44.4% of the population - 42.0% are from [[South America]], 2.1% from [[North America]], 0.1% from [[Europe]], 0.1% from [[Asia]], 0.03% from [[Africa]], and 0.004% from [[Oceania]].<ref name="2024census" />

==Administration==
[[Image:Plaza Chacabuco, Independencia, Santiago 20230519 08.jpg|thumb|Plaza Chacabuco]]
As a commune, Independencia is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The alcalde for the 2024-2028 term is Agustín Iglesias Muñoz ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Official"/><ref name="Munitel"/> The communal council has the following members:

* Leyla Pichara González ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Rosa Huilipan Castillo ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* José Miguel Cuevas Fonsea ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Macarena Díaz Rossel ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Manuel Jara Vargas (Ind/[[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Sandra Álvarez Ruíz ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Rodrigo Barco Sánchez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* [[Máximo Breake Díaz]] ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Independencia is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Patricio Hales ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Claudia Nogueira ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 19th electoral district, (together with [[Recoleta, Chile|Recoleta]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín (PPD) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
