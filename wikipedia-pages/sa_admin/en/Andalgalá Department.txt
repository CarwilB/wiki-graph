'''Andalgalá''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 14,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|4,497|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Andalgalá]], which is located around {{cvt|1,435|km}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==

The main contributors to the economy of Andalgalá Department are farming and mining. The [[Capillitas Mine]] is a rich source of [[Rhodochrosite]], (Inca Rosa), as well as 87 other types of mineral, including [[Catamarcaite]], which is named after the Province of Catamarca.
