'''Tatatila''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the mountainous central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 26&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface area of 82.25&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|42|N|97|07|W|}}.

==Geography==

The municipality of Tatatila is delimited to the north by [[Altotonga (municipality)|Altotonga]], to the north-east by [[Tenochtitlán, Veracruz|Tenochtitlan]], to the east by [[Tlacolulan]] and [[Tenochtitlán, Veracruz|Tenochtitlan]], to the south by [[Las Vigas de Ramírez]], and to the west by [[Las Minas, Veracruz|Las Minas]] and [[Villa Aldama]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30156a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tatatila|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16|archive-date=2007-03-29|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070329022136/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30156a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> It is watered by small creeks tributaries of the river Nautla.

The weather in Tatatila is cold and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

In 2020, Tatatila recorded population of 6,041 inhabitantes in 44 localities, all of them classified as rural.<ref name=2020CENSUSTATATILA>http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Tatatila.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Tatatila CENSUS</ref> Tatatila, the municipal seat, contains nearly 20% of the population (1,072 hab.). Other localities include La Mancuerna (737 hab.), Pilhuatepec (532 hab.), Tenexpanoya (497 hab.) and Piedra Parada (483 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSTATATILA/>

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

The celebration in honor of St. Peter the Apostle, patron of the town, occurs at the end of June, and the celebration in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]] occurs in December.
