'''Nosara''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Nicoya (canton)|Nicoya]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916161710/http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book|lang=es|title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf|access-date=2 October 2020|archive-date=22 December 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181222202946/http://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> 

==History==
Nosara was created on 26 January 1988 by Acuerdo 40.<ref name="dta2017" />

==Geography==
Despite the Nicoya peninsula being one of the last locations with large areas of unpaved roads in Costa Rica, Nosara is one of the oldest [[expatriate]] communities in the country, as well as a [[fishing]] and [[cattle ranch|agricultural area]].<ref>[http://www.lonelyplanet.com/costa-rica/peninsula-de-nicoya/nosara-area Lonely Planet - Nosara Area]</ref>

Unlike most coastal tourist towns, Nosara lacks nearly any development directly on the beach as much of it consists of the [[Ostional Wildlife Refuge]] (necessitated by the presence of breeding [[olive ridley]] and [[leatherback]] [[sea turtle]] populations). Nosara is a [[surfing]] destination as Playa Guiones is one of the most consistent waves in the world with over 330 days per year of rideable conditions. Playa Guiones is also considered a mecca for [[yoga]] in Costa Rica<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.ticotimes.net/travel/nosara-yoga/ |title=Yoga mecca Nosara is not a bad place to become one with the cosmos |access-date=2018-08-19}}</ref> and the home of several renowned yoga schools.  Nearby Playa Pelada is also a popular surf spot and a hangout area for locals (known as "Ticos").

The official town center is located 6&nbsp;km inland from the beach. Within "Nosara town" is the [[Market (place)|market]], [[pharmacy]], [[post office]], [[church (building)|churches]], [[school]], [[police]] station, David S. Kitson public [[library]] and [[Red Cross]] clinic (Nosara lacks a hospital facility). There is a Banco Popular branch located in a small commercial area near Playa Guiones.

For the [[Costa Rica 2011 Census|2011 census]], Nosara had a population of {{formatnum:4912}} inhabitants.<ref name="censo2011">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Censo. 2011. Población total por zona y sexo, según provincia, cantón y distrito |url=https://www.inec.cr/censos/censos-2011 |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=28 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200928013144/https://www.inec.cr/censos/censos-2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref> As of 2024, this number is estimated to have almost doubled. 

There are five main oceanfront communities each named after their respective beaches: Playa Nosara, Playa Guiones, Playa Pelada, Playa Garza, and Playa Ostional. These comprise the largest areas of foreign-owned residences, tourist hotels, and restaurants, centered on Guiones and Pelada.  The "suburb" Esparanza de Nosara borders the Playa Guiones area, sitting between its first two beach entrances to the south.  Esparanza hosts the Guiones area's largest market, a school and [[soccer]] (football) field, and a few new housing developments, hotels, and restaurants.

In 2017, Nosara was noted as the fastest-growing surf town in the world by economists from the University of Sydney, Australia, when analyzing over 5,000 surf breaks across the globe.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.theinertia.com/business-media/turns-out-perfect-waves-like-this-in-nosara-costa-rica-are-worth-50-billion-per-year/|title=Turns Out Perfect Waves Like This in Nosara Costa Rica Are Worth $50 Billion Per Year|last=Heyden|first=Dylan|work=The Inertia|access-date=2017-03-15|language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://sydney.edu.au/news-opinion/news/2017/03/14/surf-break-discoveries-create-economic-growth.html|title=Surf break discoveries create economic growth|website=The University of Sydney|access-date=2017-03-15}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.nosararealestatereport.com/nosara-officially-fastest-growing-surf-town-world/|title=Nosara is Officially the Fastest Growing Surf Town in the World - The Nosara Real Estate Report|website=www.nosararealestatereport.com|language=en|access-date=2017-09-05}}</ref>

The [[Nosara River]] goes through the village.

==Villages==
The administrative center of the district is the village of Bocas de Nosara.

Other villages in the district are Ángeles de Garza, Bijagua, Cabeceras de Garza, Coyoles, Cuesta Winch, Delicias, Esperanza Sur, Flores, Garza, Guiones, Ligia, Nosara, Playa Nosara, Playa Pelada, Portal, Río Montaña, Santa Marta and Santa Teresa.

=== Airport===
The district is served by [[Nosara Airport]], located only 15 minutes from Nosara beaches, served by [[Sansa Airlines]].

There is also a taxi service from the major international airports in San Jose and Liberia.

The town is spread out and surrounded by jungle. People who live there usually get around with motorcycles, not a lot of them have cars. As a traveler, cars can be rented but it is not necessary since ATVs or golf carts are cheaper and still can get everywhere. There is also the option to call a [[tuk tuk]], small cars that fit a maximum of three people in the back. They are used as taxis.
