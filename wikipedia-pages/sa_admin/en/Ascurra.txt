'''Ascurra''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] state of [[Brazil]]. Ascurra has a strong [[Italian culture]] due to its colonization in 1876 by farmers from [[Welschtirol]].<ref name="i"/> [[Hermann Blumenau]] (1819–1899) named the municipality after a point in [[Serra do Ibitirapé]], [[Paraguay]] of the same name that was taken by the [[Brazilian Army]] during the [[Paraguayan War]] in 1869.

Ascurra covers {{convert|110.901|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 7,978 with a population density of 70 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>

==Geography==

Ascurra is located {{Convert|40|km|mi}} from [[Blumenau]] and {{Convert|200|km|mi}} from the state capital of [[Florianópolis]]. The [[Itajaí-Açu River]] crosses Ascurra.<ref name="g"/>

==Economy==

Mainly from the [[rice]] agriculture.
