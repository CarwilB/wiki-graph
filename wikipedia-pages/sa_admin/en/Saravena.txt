'''Saravena''' is a city and municipality in [[Arauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is located {{convert|6|km}} south of Colombia's border with [[Venezuela]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.google.com/maps/@6.9527174,-71.8755953,5606m/data=!3m1!1e3|title=Google Maps|website=Google Maps}}</ref> The municipality, with a population of 55,554, has had [[FARC]] and [[National Liberation Army (Colombia)|ELN]] activity as well as a Colombian military presence near the Venezuelan border.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://noticias.caracoltv.com/colombia/viaje-al-triangulo-rojo-de-la-guerra-la-zona-de-arauca-donde-se-siente-el-rigor-del-conflicto|title=Viaje al ‘triángulo rojo de la guerra’, la zona de Arauca donde se siente el rigor del conflicto|date=February 18, 2019|website=Noticias Caracol}}</ref>

It is served by [[Los Colonizadores Airport]].

==Climate==
Saravena has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with moderate to little rainfall from December to March to and heavy to very heavy rainfall from April to November.
