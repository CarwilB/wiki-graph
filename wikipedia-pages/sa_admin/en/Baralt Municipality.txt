'''Baralt Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipality of Venezuela]] in the [[States of Venezuela|state]] of [[Zulia]]. It is located on the eastern coast of [[Lake Maracaibo]] and named after diplomat and writer [[Rafael María Baralt]]. Its [[municipal seat]] is [[San Timoteo, Venezuela|San Timoteo]]. Established as an independent district on 3 April 1948 following a population spike driven by the early 20th-century [[oil boom]], the predominantly urban municipality had a population of 92,080 people as of the 2011 census.

==History==
The Baralt Municipality was officially established on 3 April 1948, when [[Zulia]]'s Legislative Assembly reformed the political-territorial division law to create an independent, autonomous district. Named in honor of the Zulia native, diplomat, and writer [[Rafael María Baralt]], the new territory was carved out of the [[Sucre Municipality, Zulia|Sucre District]], where it had previously existed as the General Urdaneta parish since 1845. The push for autonomy began between 1943 and 1944 when residents of [[San Timoteo, Venezuela|San Timoteo]] and [[Mene Grande]] formed a committee to petition for independence. This movement was heavily driven by the region's massive early 20th-century [[oil boom]], which caused the population to skyrocket from just 1,191 residents in 1920 to nearly 22,000 by 1950. Furthermore, the territory was geographically isolated from the rest of the Sucre District by portions of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]] and lacked a connecting highway until 1956, making it isolated administratively.<ref>{{cite journal|url=https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/8859242.pdf|title=Creación del distrito Rafael María Baralt. 70 años|trans-title=Creation of the Rafael María Baralt District. 70 Years|lang=es|first=Manuel|last=Pérez Gil|journal=Journal of History, Geography, Art, and Culture|pages=145–147|publisher=[[Rafael María Baralt National Experimental University]]|date=July–December 2017|access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Baralt Municipality was 92,080 people, accounting for 2.4% of the total population of the state of [[Zulia]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/23__zulia/|title=Zulia (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref> The capital and [[municipal seat]] of Baralt is the town of [[San Timoteo, Venezuela|San Timoteo]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.paedica.com.ve/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/evoluci%C3%B3n-territorial.pdf|title=Evolución Territorial del Zulia|trans-title=Territorial Evolution of Zulia|lang=es|first=Iván Darío|last=Parra|publisher=[[PAEDICA]]|date=2019|access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref> even though only 5.7% of the municipality's population resides there.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/zulia/2302__baralt/|title=Urban Localities in Baralt (Zulia, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Baralt as 104,429 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 1.6% since 2011 and showing a population density of 47.23 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/zulia/2302__baralt/|title=Baralt (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=25 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 51.1% men (45,926) and 48.9% women (43,921). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 63.7% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 31.5% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for the remaining 4.8%. The municipality is more urbanized than rural, with 74.1% of inhabitants (66,600) living in [[urban centers]] compared to 25.9% (23,247) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[Mestizo]] (57.9%) and [[White people]] (37.8%). Minority groups included 2.8% [[Afro-Venezuelans]] and 1.2% indigenous people as well as another 218 individuals belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 90.8%, with 6,540 inhabitants of Baralt not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>
