'''Andahuaylas''' is the second largest of the seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Apurímac Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Andahuaylas]]. The province is located in the north-western part of the region and measures {{convert|3987.00|km2}}.<ref>[http://www.muniandahuaylas.gob.pe Official web site of the Andahuaylas Province]</ref>

==Boundaries==
*North: [[Chincheros Province|province of Chincheros]] and [[Ayacucho Region]]
*East: provinces of [[Abancay Province|Abancay]] and [[Aymaraes Province|Aymaraes]]
*South: [[Ayacucho Region]]
*West: [[Ayacucho Region]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (73.54%) learnt to speak in childhood, 26.09% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language and  0.19% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]]  ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>

== Archaeology ==
Some of the most important archaeological sites in the province are [[Awkimarka (Apurímac)|Awkimarka]], [[Llamachayuq]] and [[Suntur]].
