'''Serra dos Aimorés''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north-east of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], at its namesake, the [[Serra dos Aimorés]]. Its population as of 2020 was 8,713 living in a total area of 215&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/serra-dos-aimores/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the statistical [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Vale do Mucuri]] and to the statistical [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Nanuque]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref> Before this time, it was a disputed region between the states of Minas Gerais and [[Espírito Santo]].<ref>{{cite book | title=Anuário estatístico do Brasil | publisher=IBGE | year=1953}}</ref>

==Geography==
Serra dos Aimorés is located at an elevation of 209 meters on the state boundary with [[Bahia]]. The nearest major population center is [[Nanuque]]. Most of the population lives in the urban area.

The distance to [[Nanuque]] is 25&nbsp;km; and the distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 616&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Nanuque]], [[Ibirapuã]] (Bahia), and [[Mucuri|Ibiranhém]], (Bahia).<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/nanuque/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080418022843/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/nanuque/ |date=2008-04-18 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and [[subsistence agriculture]]. The GDP in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|44|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 20 million from services, 13 million from industry, and 6 million from agriculture. There were 146 rural producers on 12,000 hectares of land. Only 11 farms had tractors (2006). 800 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main crops were coconuts, sugarcane, beans, and corn. There were 26,000 head of cattle (2006). There was 1 bank (2007) and 382 automobiles (203 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 20 inhabitants per automobile.
<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Social indicators==
The social indicators rank it in the bottom tier of municipalities in the state.
*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.655 (2000)
*State ranking: 731 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 3,699 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000
*Literacy rate: 74%
*Life expectancy: 62 (average of males and females)<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In terms of HDI, the highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

==Health and education==
There were 6 health clinics (2 specialized) (2006). Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Nanuque. Educational needs were met by 10 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 5 pre-primary schools.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>
