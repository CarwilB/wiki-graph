'''Santa Rosa''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the centre of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 16,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|8,510|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Santa Rosa, Mendoza|Santa Rosa]], which is located around {{cvt|975|km}} from the [[Federal Capital]].
