'''Aroma''' is one of the twenty provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]]. It is situated in the southern parts of the department. Its seat is [[Sica Sica]] (Sika Sika).

== Location ==
Aroma province is located between 16° 43' and 17° 35' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 22' and 68° 23' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 120&nbsp;km from north west to south east, and up to 55&nbsp;km from north east to south west.

The province is situated on the Bolivian [[Altiplano]] south east of [[Lake Titicaca]] and borders [[Ingavi Province]] in the north west, [[Pacajes Province]] in the west, [[Gualberto Villarroel Province]] in the south, [[Oruro Department]] in the south east, [[Loayza Province]] in the east, and [[Pedro Domingo Murillo Province]] in the north.

== Population ==
The population of Aroma Province has increased by 80% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 65,730 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 86,480 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2012: 98,205 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2024: 112,015 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolivia: Administrative Division (Departments and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

45.1% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

79.9% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 93.9% speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 6.7% [[Quechua language|Quechua]]. ''(2001)''

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the population of the province is 78.4%. ''(1992)''

82.1% of the population have no access to electricity, 88.0% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

69.2% of the population are Catholics, 25.5% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
The province comprises seven [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipios]] which are further subdivided into cantons.
