'''San Martín Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 12,236 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|6,385|km2}}, and its capital city is [[La Cruz, Corrientes|La Cruz]].
