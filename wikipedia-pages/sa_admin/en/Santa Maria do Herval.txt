'''Santa Maria do Herval''' ([[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch German|Hunsrick]]: Teewald) is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

Santa Maria do Herval is one of 17 municipalities in Brazil where Hunsrick is an official language.
