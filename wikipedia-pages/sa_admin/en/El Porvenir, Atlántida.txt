'''El Porvenir''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''The Future'') is a town, with a population of 5,202 (2013 census),<ref name=pop>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/atlantida/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the Department of Atlántida with at least 200 inhabitants</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Atlántida department|Atlántida]]. The largest town of the Municipality is El Pino, with a population of 6,304 (2013 census).<ref name=pop/>

==Geography==
The municipality is in the department of Atlantida and borders with the city of [[La Ceiba]], [[San Francisco, Atlántida|San Francisco]]. It counts the villages of El Pino<ref>{{Google maps|url=http://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=el+pino+atlantida+honduras&ie=UTF-8&ei=OZmCU4LvLcnXOanEgYgK&ved=0CAYQ_AUoAQ|title=El Pino}}</ref> and La Unión.<ref>{{Google maps|url=http://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=la+union+atlantida+honduras&ie=UTF-8&ei=DpGCU-DVLMS9OaaEgIgK&ved=0CAYQ_AUoAQ|title=La Unión}}</ref><ref>[[:commons:File:HN-AT Municipalities.svg|Municipal map of Atlántida Department]] (El Porvenir is the nr. 2)</ref> Much of El Porvenir extends along a coastline. The beach is a popular place for both locals and visitors, especially during Semana Santa. Most of the area surrounding El Porvenir is dominated by pineapple fields.

== Transportation ==
El Porvenir is serviced by both buses and [[Auto rickshaw|mototaxies]]. Buses come at infrequent intervals and depart for either La Ceiba or La Unión. Moto-taxies can only be taken within the city limits, but numerous taxis travel to La Ceiba and further. The CA-13 highway goes past El Porvenir and connects it to surrounding cities and the nearby [[Golosón International Airport]].{{cn|date=January 2023}}
