'''Nanawa''' is a [[town]] in the [[Presidente Hayes]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]]. The urban area is divided by the Pilcomayo River from the city of [[Clorinda, Formosa|Clorinda]] in the country of [[Argentina]].
