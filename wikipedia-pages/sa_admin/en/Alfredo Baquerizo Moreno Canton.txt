'''Alfredo Baquerizo Moreno''' is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of the [[Provinces of Ecuador|province]] of [[Guayas Province|Guayas]] in the Republic of [[Ecuador]]. Its official name was given in honor of [[Alfredo Baquerizo Moreno]] (1859 – 1951), who served three times as the president of Ecuador. It is also known as '''Juján''', a name which came from a regional plant. The canton covers an area of {{convert|228.6|km2}}. Its [[canton seat]] is also known as [[Alfredo Baquerizo Moreno, Guayas|Alfredo Baquerizo Moreno]] or Juján.

As of the [[census]] of 2001, there were 19,982 people residing within the canton limits.<ref name="INEC">{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inec.gov.ec/c/document_library/get_file?folderId=295154&name=DLFE-13233.pdf ''Cantón A. == Geography ==
The Jujan canton is located in the west of the province of Guayas. It is bordered on the north by [[Los Ríos Province]]; on the south by [[Milagro Canton]]; on the east by the [[Simón Bolívar Canton]]; and on the west by the cantons of [[Samborondón Canton|Samborondón]] and [[Yaguachi Canton|Yaguachi]].
