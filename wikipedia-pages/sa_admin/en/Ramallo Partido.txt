'''Ramallo''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the far north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 29,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1040|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Ramallo, Buenos Aires|Ramallo]], which is around {{convert|250|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Ramallo massacre==

On September 17, 1999, provincial police opened fire on a getaway car from a bank robbery, and two of the hostages in the car were killed.<ref name="lanacion">{{Cite news |url=https://www.lanacion.com.ar/sociedad/investigaran-a-todos-los-efectivos-nid154046/ |title=Investigarán a todos los efectivos |work=[[La Nación]] |date=19 September 1999 |access-date=12 June 2026 |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.lanacion.com.ar/seguridad/masacre-de-ramallo-condenan-al-gobierno-bonaerense-a-indemnizar-a-los-familiares-de-las-victimas-nid06032024/ |title=Masacre de Ramallo: condenan al gobierno bonaerense a indemnizar a los familiares de las víctimas |work=[[La Nación]] |date=6 March 2024 |access-date=12 June 2026 |language=es}}</ref> There are several theories to explain the police action, ranging from incompetence to a scandal involving former president [[Carlos Menem]] and the suspicious death of his son.<ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.pagina12.com.ar/1999/99-09/99-09-19/pag08.htm |title=Duda Mendonça no previó ni a Zulema ni a Ramallo |work=[[Página/12]] |date=19 September 1999 |access-date=12 June 2026 |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |url=https://www.pagina12.com.ar/1999/99-09/99-09-18/pag07.htm |title=El juez es un incompetente |work=[[Página/12]] |date=18 September 1999 |access-date=12 June 2026 |language=es}}</ref><ref name="lanacion" />
