'''Aldama Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], in southern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is '''Aldama, Chiapas'''.

==Demographics==
As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 8,480,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Consulta interactiva de datos |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/olap/consulta/general_ver4/MDXQueryDatos.asp?#Regreso&c= |access-date=2023-05-31 |website=www.inegi.org.mx}}</ref> up from 3,635 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07113a.htm |title=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas |access-date=July 23, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930015002/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07113a.htm |archive-date=September 30, 2007 }}</ref>

As of 2020, the town of Aldama had a population of 2,279. <ref>{{Cite web |title=Aldama (Chiapas) Aldama |url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/aldama/ |access-date=2023-05-31 |website=mexico.PueblosAmerica.com |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Localities==
Other than the town of Aldama, the municipality had 20 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Consulta interactiva de datos |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/olap/consulta/general_ver4/MDXQueryDatos.asp?#Regreso&c= |access-date=2023-05-31 |website=www.inegi.org.mx}}</ref>
