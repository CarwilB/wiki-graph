'''Tlachichilco''' is a small town in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the northern part of Veracruz, in the region known as Huasteca Baja. It is one of the 212 [[municipalities of Veracruz]]. Tlachichilco is made up of 32 small towns in which more than 10,000 people live.<ref>{{cite book |title=Ethnology |last=Monaghan |first=John |author2=Victoria R. Bricker |author3=Barbara Edmonson  |year=2000 |publisher=University of Texas Press |isbn=978-0-292-70881-5 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=RZxCCImjN-gC&dq=Tlachichilco+%2Bveracruz&pg=PA66 |access-date=2008-11-09 |page=66}}</ref>
