'''Marueño''' (''Barrio Marueño'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the [[municipality]] of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Along with [[Anón (Ponce)|Anón]],  [[Coto Laurel]], [[Guaraguao]], [[Quebrada Limón]], [[Real (Ponce)|Real]], and [[San Patricio (Ponce)|San Patricio]], and the coastal barrios of [[Canas (Ponce)|Canas]] and  [[Capitanejo (Ponce)|Capitanejo]], Marueño is one of the municipality's nine bordering barrios. Marueño borders the municipality of [[Peñuelas, Puerto Rico|Peñuelas]]. The name of this barrio is of native Indian origin.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 54.</ref> It was created in 1831.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
[[File:Escena en el Barrio Marueño, Ponce, PR (DSC02300A).jpg|thumb|left|Scene on [[PR-502]] in Barrio Marueño]]
Marueño is located in the northwestern section of the municipality, northwest of the city of Ponce. It is a mostly mountainous barrio, as it sits on the transition zone between the coastal plains and the rugged mountains of the [[Cordillera Central, Puerto Rico|Cordillera Central]]. Its highest point is at Monte Marueño (''Mount Marueño''), which stands at 640 meters (2,100 feet) above sea level.<ref name="elsuralavista.com">{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20130923092122/http://www.elsuralavista.com/periodico/?p=22661 El Sur a la Vista. La Historia de Nuestros Barrios: Barrio Marueño, Ponce. 21 June 2010.]}}</ref> The [[Río Pastillo|Pastillo River]] divides the barrio into two sections of fairly equal size. The river originates high up in the mountains of barrio [[Guaraguao]].

==History==

===Origin of the name===
The name of the barrio has its origins in Europe. Its name is, however, not found in any other part of Puerto Rico.  The barrio, the tallest peak in the barrio, and the river that runs through it are all called Marueño.<ref name="elsuralavista.com"/>

In the [[Aragonese language]], the name "Marueño" means "a bunch of rocks". The name could also be related to another word of Aragonese origin – "murueño" - meaning “the end of a field”, thus it could then refer to some boundary or refer to the low fence walls used to mark the edge of properties.<ref name="elsuralavista.com"/>
