'''Morón''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' ([[second level administrative division]]) of the [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. Located in the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] urban area, its head town is [[Morón, Buenos Aires|Morón]] which is located around {{cvt|17|km}} from Buenos Aires.

The provincial subdivision has a population of 319,934 inhabitants<ref name=Cen2010 /> in an area of  {{cvt|52|km2}}.
