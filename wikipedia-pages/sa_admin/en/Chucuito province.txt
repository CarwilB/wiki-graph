'''Chucuito''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Puno Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}, Retrieved December 29, 2007</ref> The capital of the province is the city of [[Juli, Peru|Juli]].

==History==
Chucuito is cited by ''[[Comentarios Reales de los Incas]]''. On the 1st chapter of the 3rd book of [[Comentarios reales de los incas]], Chucuito is mentioned because of huge monuments and anthropomorphic statues.<ref>[http://bib.cervantesvirtual.com/FichaAutor.html?Ref=64&idGrupo=Todo#1_Todo Inca Garcilaso de la Vega, Comentarios reales de los incas, Lisbona 1609 ]. {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304221542/http://bib.cervantesvirtual.com/FichaAutor.html?Ref=64&idGrupo=Todo#1_Todo|date=2016-03-04}}</ref> The [[Aramu Muru]] monument is located in the province.

==Languages==
According to the [[Peru 2007 Census|2007 census]], [[Aymara language|Aymara]] was spoken by 72.4% of the population as their first language, while 26.8% spoke [[Spanish language|Spanish]], 0.6% spoke [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 0.1% spoke [[Asháninka language|Asháninka]], 0.0% spoke other indigenous languages and 0.0% spoke foreign languages.
