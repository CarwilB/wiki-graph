'''Supatá''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Gualivá Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Supatá is located {{convert|76|km|mi}} north of the Colombian capital [[Bogotá]]. Supatá borders the municipalities [[Pacho]], [[Vergara, Cundinamarca|Vergara]], [[Subachoque]], [[La Vega, Cundinamarca|La Vega]] and [[San Francisco, Cundinamarca|San Francisco]].<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.supata-cundinamarca.gov.co/ Official website Supatá] - accessed 06-05-2016</ref>

== History and geography ==
Before the arrival of the [[Spanish conquest of the Americas|Spanish]] [[conquistador]]es in the central highlands of Colombia, the area around Supatá was inhabited by the [[Panche people]]. In [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] ''Supatá'' means "low and fertile land".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.salsipuedes.org/caminatas-desde-bogota/supata-0 Etymology Supatá] - accessed 29-04-2016</ref> The village is located on the northwestern edge of the [[Bogotá savanna]] at a relatively low elevation of {{convert|1780|m}} above sea level, while other areas within the municipality are much higher, up to a maximum elevation of {{convert|3400|m|ft}}.<ref name=website/>

On the ''El Tablazo'' mountain, at an elevation of {{convert|3285|m}} within the boundaries of Supatá, there's a cave named ''Cueva Chía'' ("Chía Cave"). [[Chía (goddess)|Chía]] was the Moon goddess of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] who frequently invaded the territories of the Panche. It is said the people hid the treasures of Chía in this cave.<ref name=website/>

== Economical activities ==
The main economical activity of Supatá is [[agriculture]], particularly [[coffee]], [[sugar cane]], [[banana]]s and [[yuca]].<ref name=website/>
