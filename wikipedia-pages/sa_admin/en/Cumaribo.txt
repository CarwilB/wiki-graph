'''Cumaribo''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Vichada Department|Vichada]], [[Republic of Colombia]]. Cumaribo was founded by [[Jose Nicolino Mattar]] in 1959.

In 2005 the municipality had an estimated total population of 28,718 inhabitants, 4,312 of these living in the head of the municipality or urban area. With an area of {{convert|65,193|sqkm|sqmi|0|disp=or}}, about the size of [[Lithuania]], it is the largest [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality in Colombia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Cumaribo borders to the north with the other three municipalities of the Department of Vichada; [[La Primavera, Vichada|La Primavera]], [[Puerto Carreño]] and [[Santa Rosalía, Vichada|Santa Rosalia]]; to the east with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]]; to the south with the [[Department of Guainía]]; and to the west with the departments of [[Department of Meta|Meta]] and [[Department of Guaviare|Guaviare]].

Cumaribo is within the [[Orinoquia Region of Colombia]] part of the [[Llanos]] plains that cover part of Colombia and the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]]. Three-quarters of the total area of the municipality of Cumaribo is savanna and the rest is covered by jungle with some small mounts including the [[Mount Vichada]], [[Mount Mona]], [[Mount Matavenia]] and [[Mount Guaripa]].<ref name="cumaribo-vichada.gov.co">[http://cumaribo-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-v1--&m=f {{in lang|es}} Municipality of Cumaribo: General Information – geography] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120709173324/http://cumaribo-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-v1--&m=f |date=9 July 2012 }}</ref>

There are three important rivers basins within the range of the municipality; the [[Orinoco River]], the [[Meta River]] and the [[Guaviare River]]. The Meta river flows into the Orinoco river and the Guaviare river is the main current for the [[Muro River|Muro]], [[Uva River|Uva]] and [[Mataven River|Mataven rivers]]. There are also two main lakes within the municipality; the [[Lake Caimán]] and the [[Lake Sesema]].<ref name="cumaribo-vichada.gov.co"/>

===Climate===
Cumaribo has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with heavy rainfall in all months except January and February. Temperatures are consistently very warm to hot – between {{convert|27|and|30|°C|°F|1}} throughout the year<ref name="cumaribo-vichada.gov.co"/> – and humidity very high.

==History==

Cumaribo was officially founded by colonizers led by [[Jose Nicolino Mattar]] in 1959 but was inhabited previously by indigenous peoples such as the [[Guahibo people|Guahibo]].

===Colombian armed conflict===

{{see also|Colombian armed conflict}}

The area has been under the influence of the [[Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia]] (FARC) guerrilla group due to the large production of [[coca]] leaves in the region and difficult access for the [[Military of Colombia]].  [[Tomás Medina Caracas]], a prominent FARC commander in charge of the [[illegal drug trade in Colombia|illegal drug trade]] for this organization was killed on 1 September 2007 in a [[Colombian Army]] military operation in eastern Colombia within the municipality of Cumaribo near the border with [[Venezuela]].<ref name=ELTIEMPO>[http://www.eltiempo.com/conflicto/noticias/ARTICULO-WEB-NOTA_INTERIOR-3705978.html {{in lang|es}} El Tiempo: El capo de las drogas de las Farc] eltiempo.com Retrieved 3 September 2007.</ref>

==Demography==

According to a census of 2001, the population at the head of the municipality was of 1055 inhabitants, 534 males, 521 females. Children between 0–14 years old was of 418 of which 206 were male and 212 female. Young adults between 15–24 years old is of 208 of which 95 were male and 113 female. Adults between the ages of 25 and 49 years old was of 358 of which 186 were male and 172 female. 49 and older were 71 people of which 47 were male and 24 female. More than half of the population were colonizers, 35% indigenous and a 15% mestizos.<ref name="lablaa.org">{{Cite web |url=http://www.lablaa.org/blaavirtual/tesis/unal/linguist/lin1-60.pdf |title=Luis Angel Arango Library: Diagnóstico sociolingüístico de Cumaribo, zona de contacto indígena – Colono, Vichada |access-date=23 March 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090320092513/http://www.lablaa.org/blaavirtual/tesis/unal/linguist/lin1-60.pdf |archive-date=20 March 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

===Indigenous reserves in Cumaribo===

{{see also|Indigenous peoples in Colombia}}

The municipality of Cumaribo has some 38 indigenous reserves.<ref name="lablaa.org"/> The indigenous are predominantly the [[Guahibo people]], [[Curripaco people|Curripaco]] and [[Piapoco people|Piapoco]] peoples pertaining to the [[Arawak peoples|Arawak]] language family, and the [[Cuiva people|Cuiva]], [[Desana people|Desana]], [[Puinave people|puinave]] and [[Sáliva people|Saliva]] peoples.<ref>[http://cumaribo-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-v1--&m=f {{in lang|es}} Municipality of Cumaribo: General Information – demography] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120709173324/http://cumaribo-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-v1--&m=f |date=9 July 2012 }}</ref>
