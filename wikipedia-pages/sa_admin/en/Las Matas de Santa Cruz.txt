'''Las Matas de Santa Cruz''' is a small [[city]] in the [[Monte Cristi Province|Monte Cristi]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=&men=gcis&lng=en&des=wg&geo=-65&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=x|archiveurl=https://archive.today/20121204181725/http://world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=&men=gcis&lng=en&des=wg&geo=-65&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=x|archivedate=4 December 2012|title=Dominican Republic: largest cities and towns and statistics of their population|website=World Gazeteer|accessdate=9 September 2021|via=Archive Today }}</ref>

==Notable people==
* [[Nelson Cruz]] (born 1980) is a Dominican-American professional baseball designated hitter and right fielder.
* [[Antony Santos]] (born 1967) is a Dominican singer, songwriter, musician, and producer. He is one of the top-selling Bachata artists of all time.
* [[Raulín Rodríguez]] (born June 16, 1971) is a Dominican singer, songwriter, musician, and producer. He is one of the first major bachata artists to have international success and popularize this style of music in the Dominican Republic.
