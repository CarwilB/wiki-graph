'''Narciso Campero''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Cochabamba Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[Aiquile]]. Aiquile lies on one transportation route between the cities of [[Cochabamba]], [[Sucre]] and [[Santa Cruz de la Sierra|Santa Cruz]].

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into cantons.

== 1998 earthquake ==
{{main|1998 Aiquile earthquake}}

Campero was near the [[epicenter]] of an earthquake which struck on May 22, 1998. The shock had a [[Moment magnitude scale|moment magnitude]] of 6.6 and a maximum [[Mercalli intensity scale|Mercalli intensity]] of VIII (''Severe''). It caused over 100 deaths and left thousands homeless. It was the largest shallow earthquake in Bolivia in over 50 years, and was felt as far away as [[Cochabamba]] and [[Sucre]] (approximately 100&nbsp;km). The earthquake caused widespread damage over an area of approximately 100&nbsp;km in diameter.
