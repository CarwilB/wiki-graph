'''Calca District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Calca Province|Calca]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The capital of the district is [[Calca, Peru|Calca]], also known as the capital of the Sacred Valley.

== Geography ==
Some of the highest mountains of the [[Urupampa mountain range|Urupampa]] mountain range, [[Ch'iqun]], [[Q'irayuq]], [[Sawasiray]] and [[Siriwani]], lie in the northwestern part of the Calca District.
