The '''San Sebastián Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 23,279.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx|title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics|website=www.geohive.com|access-date=2016-10-15}}</ref>  The town of [[San Sebastián, Aragua|San Sebastián]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the San Sebastián Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip]{{Dead link|date=October 2016}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The San Sebastián Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 22,906 (up from 20,096 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.4% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls]{{Dead link|date=October 2016}}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|46.65|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls]{{Dead link|date=October 2016}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the San Sebastián Municipality is Carlos Guillermo Miranda Escobar, elected on October 31, 2004, with 43% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|title=Alcalde de Municipio San Sebastian|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=5&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|website=www.cne.gob.ve|accessdate=15 October 2016}}</ref>   He replaced Enrique Barrios shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]es; Capital San Sebastián.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
