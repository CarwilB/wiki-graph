'''Suárez''' ({{IPA|es|ˈswaɾes}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].

In October 2007 a gold mine near the town collapsed, killing at least 24 people.<ref name=bbcoct14>{{cite news |url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/world/americas/7043516.stm |title=Colombia mine collapse kills 24 |date=14 October 2007 |accessdate=26 March 2009 |work=[[BBC News]]}}</ref>{{us?|date=November 2023}}{{relevant?|date=November 2023}}
