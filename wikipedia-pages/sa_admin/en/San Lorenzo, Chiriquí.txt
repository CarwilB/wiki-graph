'''San Lorenzo''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[San Lorenzo District, Panama|San Lorenzo District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|136.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,290 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|16.7|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,621; its population as of 2000 was 1,772.<ref name=inec/>
