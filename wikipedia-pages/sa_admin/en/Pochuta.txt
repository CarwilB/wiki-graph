'''Pochuta''' is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|128|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 11,365 inhabitants.

==History==
Pochuta was earlier known as Caprera, and was part of [[Patzún]]. Caprera was elevated as a municipality on 6 July 1882, and adopted the name "Pochuta". As a honor to the patron [[Saint Michael|San Miguel]], it was known as "San Miguel Pochuta". Pochuta is derived from [[Nahuatl]] word "pachut: or "pochotl" meaning "place of thorns".<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Pochuta|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Pochuta municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/historia-del-municipio-de-pochuta-chimaltenango/|access-date=30 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Pochuta is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|128|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Pochuta (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/chimaltenango/pochuta/|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipality of [[Patzún]], Chimaltenango to the north; to the south with the municipalities of [[Patzún]] to the north, [[Patulul]] to the south, [[Acatenango]] and [[Yepocapa]] to the east, [[San Lucas Tolimán]] to the west.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Pochuta|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Pochuta municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/geografia-del-municipio-de-pochuta-chimaltenango/|access-date=30 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Municipios de Chimaltenango|year=2010 |location=Guatemala|url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|access-date=8 June 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608155133/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|archive-date=8 June 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1810.62|m|ft}} above sea level, Pochuta has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|21.5|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|149.25|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/chimaltenango/pochuta|title=Pochuta climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 11,365 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 5,808 males and 5,557 females. About 30.3% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.8% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (51.4%) was classified as urban, while 48.6% lived in rural areas. About 79.5% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (72.2%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (27.3%) forming a significant minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 80.0%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (85.1%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
