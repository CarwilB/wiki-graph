'''Córdoba''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

In September, 2017, the community suffered 4.2 magnitude earthquake.<ref>{{cite news|title=4,2 Earthquake|url=http://caracol.com.co/emisora/2017/09/23/cartagena/1506166203_153369.html|accessdate=10 December 2017|publisher=Radio Caracol|date=September 23, 2017}}</ref>
