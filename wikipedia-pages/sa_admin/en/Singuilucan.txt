'''Singuilucan''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{Cvt|334.1|km2}}. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 13,143.<ref>{{cite web|title=Singuilucan |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13057a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref>

The name of Singuilucan has been erroneously given as "lugar de lodo o de mucho lodo" (place of silt or too much mud), which in [[Nahuan languages|Nahuatl]] would be ''Zoquilocan''. But the pre-Columbian glyph for this town clearly shows a hill with quelites (''quilitl'' means green herbs, ''tzintli'' in Nahuatl) growing at its base. It also shows a blade of a knife, alluding to the [[Obsidian use in Mesoamerica|obsidian]] blade industry of [[Teotihuacan]], which was based in this area, as well as there is nearby "El Cerro de las Navajas" ("hill of the knives").

So the correct translation of Singuilucan is ''tzinquilocan'', "the place at the base of the mountain of quelites".<ref>[http://singuilucan.hidalgo.gob.mx/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=4&Itemid=9 singuilucansinguilucan.hidalgo.gob.mx] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100213015100/http://singuilucan.hidalgo.gob.mx/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=4&Itemid=9 |date=2010-02-13 }}</ref>
