'''Nossa Senhora de Lourdes''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈnɔsɐ sĩˈj̃ɔɾɐ ˈdi ˈluɦdisʼ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 6,496 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/nossa-senhora-de-lourdes/panorama |access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is 80&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
