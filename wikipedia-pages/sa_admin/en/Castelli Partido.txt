'''Castelli Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the east coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 8,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2100|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Castelli, Buenos Aires|Castelli]], which is {{convert|180|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
