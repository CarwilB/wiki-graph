'''Cañas Dulces''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Liberia (canton)|Liberia]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

The administrative center of Cañas Dulces District is the village of Cañas Dulces. The district has a population of about 3500 people.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20150402103951/http://www.inec.go.cr/A/MT/Poblaci%C3%B3n%20y%20Demograf%C3%ADa/Nacimientos/Publicaciones/C0/01-%202013/Publicaciones%20de%20Estad%C3%ADsticas%20Vitales.pdf Estadísticas Vitales 2013] - INEC</ref>  Cañas Dulces is near [[Rincón de la Vieja Volcano National Park]] and the [[Rincón de la Vieja Volcano]].<ref>{{cite book|title=Volcanoes of the World|author1=Siebert, L. |author2=Simkin, T. |author3=Kimberly, P. |page=387|year=2010|edition=3rd|publisher=University of California Press|isbn=9780520268777}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|title=Costa Rica: Land of Volcanoes|author1=Chavarría-Aguilar, O.L.  |author2=Alvaro Induni, G.E.|pages=79–80|year=2005|edition=2nd|publisher=EUNED|isbn=9789968313667}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|title=Fodor's Costa Rica 2006|editor=Kelly, S.M.|page=[https://archive.org/details/fodors06costaric00kell/page/165 165]|year=2005|publisher=Random House|isbn=9781400015511|url=https://archive.org/details/fodors06costaric00kell/page/165}}</ref><ref>{{cite book|title=Lonely Planet: Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Southern Mexico, Panama|author=Reid, R.|page=[https://archive.org/details/lonelyplanetcent00robe/page/596 596]|edition=5th|year=2004|publisher=Lonely Planet|isbn=9781741040296|url=https://archive.org/details/lonelyplanetcent00robe/page/596}}</ref>

==Wildlife==
Wildlife in Cañas Dulces includes monkeys such as the [[mantled howler]],<ref>{{cite book|title=Primates Of The World: Distribution, Abundance And Conservation|author=Wolfheim, J.H.|page=222|year=1983|publisher=Psychology Press|isbn=9783718601905}}</ref> as well as the [[white-headed capuchin]] and [[Geoffroy's spider monkey]], and birds such as the [[keel-billed toucan]].

== Geography ==
Cañas Dulces has an area of {{cvt|243.39|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:105}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
