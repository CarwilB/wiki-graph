'''Chigorodó''' ({{IPA|es|tʃiɣoɾoˈðo}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is part of the Urabá Antioquia sub-region.

== Transportation ==
It is served by the [[Jaime Ortiz Betancur Airport]], which once had commercial air service to [[Medellin, Colombia|Medellin]] on [[ACES Colombia]] Airlines during the early 1990s.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-05-30 |title=ACES Colombia April 4, 1993 |url=https://departedflights2.com/aces-colombia-april-4-1993/ |access-date=2026-04-10 |website=departedflights2.com |language=en}}</ref>
