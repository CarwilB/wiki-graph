'''Capinota''' is one of sixteen [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]]s in the [[Cochabamba Department]], [[Bolivia]]. Its capital is the city of [[Capinota]]. The province has a projected population over 33,000 inhabitants by 2017.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.bo/index.php/demografia/introduccion-2 Estadísticas Demográficas]</ref> Capinota has three sections and the most populous is Capinota section, followed by Santivanes.

== Subdivision ==
Capinota Province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into cantons.
