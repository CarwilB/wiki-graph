'''Bucaná''' is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Together with [[Canas (Ponce)|Canas]], [[Playa (Ponce)|Playa]], [[Vayas]], and [[Capitanejo (Ponce)|Capitanejo]], Bucaná is one of the municipality's five coastal barrios. The name of this barrio is of native Indian origin.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 54.</ref> It was founded in 1831.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html# |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==History==
Prior to being established as a barrio of Ponce, around 1597, the [[Port of Ponce|bay of Ponce]] had a small place populated by Christian European settlers that was called Bucaná.<ref>[http://www2.pr.gov/oech/oech/Documents/Lo%20mas%20Reciente/Libros%20Municipios/Ponce.pdf ''Ponce: Notas para su Historia (second Edition).''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170207032215/http://www2.pr.gov/oech/oech/Documents/Lo%20mas%20Reciente/Libros%20Municipios/Ponce.pdf# |date=7 February 2017 }} Mariano Vidal Armstrong. Oficina Estatal de Conservacion Historica. San Juan, Puerto Rico. 1989. page 14. Accessed 19 February 2018.</ref> In 1800, Bucaná was known as Coto Bucaná,<ref>[http://edicionesdigitales.info/biblioteca/historiaponce.pdf ''Verdadera Y Auténtica Historia de la Ciudad de Ponce.''] Eduardo Neuman Gandia.  Instituto de Cultura Puertorriqueña. 1913. Page 66. Accessed 17 June 2018.</ref> a type of grant of land suitable for farming to a resident by the Spanish king in recognition for some service provided by the resident to the King.<ref>[http://edicionesdigitales.info/biblioteca/historiaponce.pdf ''Verdadera Y Auténtica Historia de la Ciudad de Ponce.''] Eduardo Neuman Gandia.  Instituto de Cultura Puertorriqueña. 1913. Page 67. Accessed 17 June 2018.</ref>

==Location==
Bucaná is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and southeast of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]]. The [[toponymy]], or origin of the name, alludes to the river that makes its way through it, [[Río Bucaná]].<ref>Government of the Municipality of Ponce. Periodico "El Señorial".  Special issue: Carnaval Ponceño 2013. February 2013. Page 17. Ponce, Puerto Rico.</ref>

==Boundaries==
[[File:Julio Monagas Park in Barrio Bucana, Ponce, PR (IMG 2745).jpg|thumb|left|300px|Entrance to Julio Monagas Park in Barrio Bucana.]]

It is bounded on the North by Marginal Street/PR-578 (one block north of [[Puerto Rico Highway 1|PR-1]]), on the South by the [[Caribbean Sea]], on the West by [[Rio Bucana]], [[Puerto Rico Highway 2|PR-2]] (roughly), [[Rio Portugues]], and the Portugues-Bucana Rivers Channel, and on the East by Bucara Street/Los Caobos Avenue, [[Puerto Rico Highway 52|PR-52]] (roughly), and the Costa Caribe Country Club East Access Road.<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=2010-08-12 }} Retrieved February 20, 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Bucaná is bounded in the North by [[Sabanetas (Ponce)|Sabanetas]], in the South by the Caribbean Sea, in the West by [[San Anton]] and [[Playa (Ponce)|Playa]], and in the East by [[Vayas]].<ref>[http://www.visitponce.com/oficina_permisos/Anuncio/Barriosdeponce.pdf Mapa de Localizacion Limite de Barrios de Ponce. Oficina de Ordenacion Territorial. Municipio Autonomo de Ponce. Ponce, Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110514152103/http://www.visitponce.com/oficina_permisos/Anuncio/Barriosdeponce.pdf |date=2011-05-14 }} Retrieved July 31, 2010.</ref>
