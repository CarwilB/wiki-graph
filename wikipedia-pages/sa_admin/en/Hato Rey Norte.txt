'''Hato Rey Norte''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the [[municipality]] of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], [[Puerto Rico]]. It is one of three barrios commonly known together as [[Hato Rey]]. Hato Rey Norte was part of the former municipality of [[Rio Piedras, Puerto Rico|Rio Piedras]], before it was merged with the municipality of San Juan in 1951.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2019-04-25 |title=Una breve mirada a la historia de Río Piedras |url=https://cauce.uprrp.edu/una-breve-mirada-a-la-historia-de-rio-piedras/ |access-date=2025-06-30 |language=es}}</ref>

It is bounded by [[Hato Rey Central]] to the east, the districts of [[Gobernador Piñero, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Gobernador Piñero]] and [[Hato Rey Sur, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Sur]] to the south, and by [[Santurce, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Santurce]] to the north. The [[Caño Martín Peña]] separates Hato Rey Norte from Santurce.

== Landmarks and places of interest ==

* [[Popular, Inc.|Banco Popular]] Center and [[Caribbean Cinemas|Caribbean Cinema]]'s Fine Arts Café.
* [[List of FBI field offices|FBI San Juan Field Office]] and Degetau Federal Building.
* Hiram Bithorn Stadium, a baseball park listed in the [[National Register of Historic Places]].<ref>{{Citation|title=National Register of Historic Places listings in San Juan, Puerto Rico|date=2021-05-23|url=https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_San_Juan,_Puerto_Rico&oldid=1024704338|work=Wikipedia|language=en|access-date=2021-10-14}}</ref>
* [[José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum]], popularly known as the ''Choliseo'', a basketball arena and San Juan's main concert venue.
* [[Martín Peña Channel]] and boardwalk or lineal park (eastern end).
* ''[[Milla de Oro]]'', the central business district of San Juan.
* [[Plaza Las Américas (Puerto Rico)|Plaza Las Américas]], the largest shopping mall in the [[Caribbean]].
* [[Puerto Rico Police|Puerto Rico Police Department]] Headquarters.
* [[Roberto Clemente Coliseum]], an indoor arena and concert venue.
* [[List of United States federal courthouses|United States Federal Court]] for the District of Puerto Rico.

== Transportation ==
Hato Rey Norte is part of the [[Milla de Oro]] and San Juan [[central business district]], served by the [[Domenech station|Domenech]], [[Roosevelt station (Puerto Rico)|Roosevelt]] and [[Hato Rey station|Hato Rey]] metro stations. The area is also served by the [[Martín Peña Channel]] ferry, and the district is also considered a hub for the [[Puerto Rico Metropolitan Bus Authority|Metropolitan Bus Authority]] (AMA).
