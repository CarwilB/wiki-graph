'''Saloá''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[sɐloˈa]}}) is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is 263&nbsp;km away from the state capital [[Recife]], and has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 15,862 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/saloa/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Paranatama]]   ('''N''');  [[Bom Conselho]] and [[Terezinha, Pernambuco|Terezinha]]   ('''S'''); [[Garanhuns]]    ('''E''');  [[Iati]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 252.08&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 745 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipanema River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] - hot
* Annual average temperature - 20.6 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 263&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Saloá''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[bean]]s, [[tomato]]es, [[coffee]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
