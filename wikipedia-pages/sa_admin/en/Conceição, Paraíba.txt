'''Conceição''' is a municipality ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]])'' in the state of [[Paraíba]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 19,007 (2020 est.) in an area of 579&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is part of the microregion of [[Itaporanga, Paraíba|Itaporanga]].
