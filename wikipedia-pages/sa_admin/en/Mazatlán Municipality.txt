'''Mazatlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sinaloa]] in northwestern [[Mexico]], being the most densely populated municipality in Sinaloa.<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 11, 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128142000/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/ |archivedate=January 28, 2007 }}</ref>

== Political subdivision ==
Mazatlán Municipality is subdivided in 8 ''sindicaturas'':
*[[Villa Unión, Sinaloa|Villa Unión]]
*[[El Recodo, Sinaloa|El Recodo]]
*[[El Quelite, Sinaloa|El Quelite]]
*Mármol
*El Roble
*Siqueros
*La Noria
*El Habal

Other settlements:
*[[El Walamo]]
