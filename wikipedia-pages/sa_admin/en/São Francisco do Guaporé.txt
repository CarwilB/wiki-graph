'''São Francisco do Guaporé''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 20,681 (2020) and its area is 10,960&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/JJG]</ref>

The municipality contains 9% of the {{convert|36442|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra dos Reis State Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Serra dos Reis – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PES Serra dos Reis|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6322|accessdate=2016-08-06}}</ref>
It contains 86% of the {{convert|2244|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra dos Reis A State Park]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PES Serra dos Reis A – ISA}}
 |title=PES Serra dos Reis A|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6334|accessdate=2016-12-20}}</ref>
It holds part of the {{convert|615771|ha|adj=on}} [[Guaporé Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Guaporé|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=206|accessdate=2016-04-26}}</ref>
