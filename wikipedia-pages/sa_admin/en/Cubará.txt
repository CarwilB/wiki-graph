'''Cubará''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]] also referred to as the '''Boyacá Frontier District''' for sharing an international border with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]].

==Climate==
Cubará has a very wet [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af).
