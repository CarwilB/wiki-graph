'''Candarave District''' is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Candarave Province|Candarave]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Etymology ==
Peruvian linguist [[Rodolfo Cerrón-Palomino]] speculates that the name comes from ''kanta-ra-wi'', an [[Aymara language|Aymara]] word meaning "place of many lodgings".<ref>{{Cite journal |title=La naturaleza probatoria del cambio lingüístico: a propósito de la interpretación toponímica andina |journal=[[Pontifical Catholic University of Peru|Voces del Ande: ensayos sobre onomástica andina]] |last=Cerrón Palomino |first=Rodolfo |url=https://repositorio.pucp.edu.pe/items/09fdc3f2-951d-4139-8413-e089c7a68654 |author-link=Rodolfo Cerrón-Palomino |year=2008 |doi=10.18800/9789972428562 |isbn=978-9972-42-856-2|doi-access=free |hdl=20.500.14657/174297 |hdl-access=free }}</ref>
