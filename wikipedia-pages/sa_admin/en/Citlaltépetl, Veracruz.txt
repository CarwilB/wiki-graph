'''Citlaltépetl''' ([[Nahuatl]]: "star mountain") is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|223|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Xalapa]] and {{convert|99|km}} south of the city of [[Tampico]], [[Tamaulipas]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Citlaltépetl is located in central Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|80|and(-)|1300|m}}, with the south being higher in elevation. It borders the municipalities of [[Ozuluama de Mascareñas (municipality)|Ozuluama]] to the northwest, [[Tantima]] to the northeast and east, [[Tancoco]] to the southeast, and [[Chontla]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Citlaltépetl, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 March 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|81.539|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.1% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/> 

The majority of the land in Citlaltépetl (65.55%) is pastureland. The dominant soils in the municipality are [[vertisol]]s and [[regosol]]s.<ref name=compendium/>

Citlaltépetl's climate is warm with rain falling mostly in the summer. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|21|and(-)|25|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1400|and(-)|1600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In pre-Hispanic times the area was inhabited by the [[Huastec people]], and the [[Mexica]] also kept a military presence there.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Citlaltépetl was incorporated on 12 June 1872 as a municipality in the canton of Tampico in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=106, 160–161 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Eutiquia Reyes Santiago.<ref>{{cite news |last=Hernández |first=Adela |url=http://www.sintesisdelgolfo.com/contundente-triunfo-de-eutiquia-reyes-santiago-en-citlaltepetl-veracruz/ |title=Contundente triunfo de Eutiquia Reyes Santiago en Citlaltépetl, Veracruz |language=es |newspaper=Síntesis del Golfo |date=5 June 2017 |access-date=2 March 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Citlaltépetl recorded a population of 11,081 inhabitants living in 2771 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=035 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Citlaltépetl |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=2 March 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 12,109 inhabitants in Citlaltépetl, 81.85% of whom reported being of [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|Indigenous]] ancestry and 4.29% reported being of [[Afro-Mexicans|African]] ancestry.<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 1955 people or 18% of the population in Citlaltépetl reported speaking an Indigenous language, of which 1887 spoke Nahuatl.<ref name=snim/>

There are 30 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Citlaltépec is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 5005 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Citlaltépetl is farming. Corn, coffee and oranges are the main crops grown, and cattle and pigs are raised.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.legisver.gob.mx/fiscalizacion/Planes%20Municipales/PlanesMunicipales2014pdf/CITLALTEPEC_30042014-VF_2.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |pages=61–62 |publisher=Municipal council of Citlaltépetl, Veracruz |access-date=2 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180303110911/http://www.legisver.gob.mx/fiscalizacion/Planes%20Municipales/PlanesMunicipales2014pdf/CITLALTEPEC_30042014-VF_2.pdf |archive-date=3 March 2018 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
