'''La Primavera''' (literally meaning "The Spring" in [[Spanish language|Spanish]]) is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Vichada Department|Vichada]], [[Colombia]].

==History==
La Primavera was founded by colonizers from other regions of Colombia on 15 July 1959. In 1968 La Primavera became a [[Colombian National Police]] Inspection site and in 1972 it became a [[corregimiento]].<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: History] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120711125336/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml |date=2012-07-11 }}</ref>

In 1987 La Primavera became a [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality]], the second in the then Commissary of Vichada. In 1991, the Commissary of Vichada became the Department of Vichada and La Primavera one of its municipalities.<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: History] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120711125336/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml |date=2012-07-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of La Primavera borders to the north with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]] with the [[Meta River|Meta river]] as boundary; to the east with the municipality of [[Puerto Carreño]]; to the south with the municipality of [[Cumaribo]] and to the west with the municipality of [[Santa Rosalia, Vichada|Santa Rosalia]].<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: Geography] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120718192958/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |date=2012-07-18 }}</ref>

The geography of La Primavera is predominantly flat as part of the [[Orinoquía Region of Colombia|Orinoquia region]] in the [[Llanos]] plains. The region is crossed by numerous rivers affluents of the [[Meta River]] basin. The municipality has a total area of {{convert|98,970|sqkm|sqmi|0}} and the seat of the municipality covers an area of {{convert|54,789|sqkm|sqmi|0}}.<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: Geography] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120718192958/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |date=2012-07-18 }}</ref>

===Climate===
La Primavera has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with heavy rainfall from April to November and little to very little rain from December to March.

==Economy==
Ranching is the main economic activity in the municipality of La Primavera. Agriculture is practiced for subsistence and produces mainly [[cotton]], [[maize]] and [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]].<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: History] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120711125336/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/sitio.shtml |date=2012-07-11 }}</ref> The municipality is developing its [[artisan fishing]], commercializing some 30 varieties of fish for export to the Colombian capital city [[Bogotá]]. The government is studying the possibility of exploiting titanium deposits and exploring oil fields.<ref>[http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} La Primavera: Economy] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120718192958/http://laprimavera-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |date=2012-07-18 }}</ref>
