'''Itacarambi''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]].  {{as of|2020}} the population was 18,164 in an area of 1,252&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/itacarambi/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  
*The elevation of the municipal seat is 445 meters.  
*It became a municipality in 1962.  
*The postal code (CEP) is 39470–000.
*Statistical microregion:  [[Januária]]

Icaraí de Minas is on the left bank of the [[São Francisco River]] between Januária and [[Manga, Minas Gerais|Manga]]. It is connected to Januária by paved BR-135.  The distance is 63&nbsp;km.

The economy is based on agriculture with emphasis on cattle raising.  There were 39,000 head in 2006.  The main agricultural crops were corn, manioc, sugarcane, and [[Ricinus|mamona]]—castor oil plant.  The GDP was R$65,000,000 in 2005.
