'''Coyhaique Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Coyhaique}}) is a [[Provinces of Chile|province]] in the [[Aysén Region]] of Chile. Its capital is [[Coyhaique]]. Spread over an area of {{cvt|12942.5|km2}}, it had a population of 58,602 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]]. The province was established by law on 14 July 1974.

==History==
The region was populated by several indigenous groups such as the [[Tehuelche people|Tehuelche]], [[Alacalufe]] and [[Chono people|Chono]], who were nomadic, and used the streams for transportation along the [[Andes]] mountain. As the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] colonialists arrived inteh region, the indigenous population declined. It was repopulated only in the 19th century, when forests were cleared to establish cattle ranches.<ref name="VC"/>

The [[Aysén Region]] was established on 10 July 1974, as per Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It was named is divided into four provinces&ndash;Coyhaique, [[Aysén Province|Aysén]], [[General Carrera Province|General Carrera]] and [[Capitán Prat Province|Capitán Prat]], which are further divided into ten [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_de_aysen_del_general_carlos_ibanez_del_campo/|lang=es|title=Region de Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|trans-title=Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

The name Coyhaique or Coihaique originated from the [[Tehuelche language]] words "koi" meaning "pond" and "aiken" meaning "camp",<ref name="Info">{{cite web|url=https://www.interpatagonia.com/coihaique/index_i.html|title=Coihaique|work=Inter Patagonia|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> roughly translating to a "place with ponds or lagoons".<ref name="RP">{{cite web|url=https://www.rutadelosparques.org/en/coyhaique/|title=Coyhaique|work=La Ruta de los Parques|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Coyhaique Province is one of the four provinces of the [[Aysén Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It spans an area of {{cvt|12942.5|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> The province is situated in [[Chilean Patagonia]], east of the [[Andes]] mountain range.<ref name="VC">{{cite web|url=https://www.visitchile.com/en/tourist-guides/coyhaique-and-puerto-aysen/|title=Coyhaique and Puerto Aisén|work=Visit Chile|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> [[Coyhaique]], which lies at the confluence of the Simpson and Coyhaique rivers, serves as the capital of the region.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Info"/><ref name="RP"/> About 55.7% of the land area of the province belongs to the [[Aysen River]] basin.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/17|lang=es|title=Provincia details, Coyhaique Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Coyhaique Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

===Climate===
The province has a maritime coastal climate towards the west and cold climate towards the eastern border with Argentina. The temperatures are mild with an average annual temperature of {{Cvt|9.13|C}}, and drops below freezing in the winter. The region receives only about {{cvt|3.35|cm}} rainfall on average annually.<ref name="VC"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/aysen/coyhaique|title=Coyhaique|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
Coyhaique is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a governor.<ref name="Gobernadores"/> It is further sub-divided into two [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas''), [[Coyhaique]] and [[Lago Verde, Chile|Lago Verde]],<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MN"/>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the Coyhaique province had a population of 58,602 inhabitants, and is the most populous of the provinces in the Aysen region.<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="CP"/> The population consisted of 30,005 females (51.2%) and 28,597 males (48.8%). About 19.3% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 68.2% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 12.5% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 46,854 inhabitants (80%) and a rural population of 11,748 inhabitants (20%). Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 56,097 inhabitants (95.7%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 43,512 inhabitants (74.3%), while 15,073 inhabitants (25.7%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 24,208 adherents (51.5%), followed by 14,858 inhabitants (31.6%) indicating no religious affiliation, and [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 6,752 adherents (14.4%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/111__coyhaique/|title=Coyhaique Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=
https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
