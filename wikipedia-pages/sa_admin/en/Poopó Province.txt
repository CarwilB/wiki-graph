'''Poopó''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the eastern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]]. Its seat is [[Poopó Municipality|Poopó]].

==Location==
Poopó province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 18° 15' and 18° 50' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 19' and 66° 54' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders [[Pantaléon Dalence Province]] in the northeast, [[Cercado Province, Oruro|Cercado Province]] in the northwest, [[Saucarí Province]] in the west, [[Sud Carangas Province]] in the southwest, [[Eduardo Avaroa Province]] in the south, and [[Potosí Department]] in the east.

The province extends over 70&nbsp;km (43.5 mi) from north to south, and 70&nbsp;km (43.5 mi) from east to west.

== Geography ==
The province lies east of the [[Desaguadero River (Bolivia)|Desaguadero River]] and [[Poopó Lake]].

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Spanish language|Spanish]], spoken by 86%. In addition to Spanish, 78% of the population speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]] and 22% [[Aymara language|Aymara]] (1992).

The population decreased from 17,437 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 14,984 (2001 census), a decrease of 5.2%. - 45.2% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).

44% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 89% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

61% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 12% in [[mining]], 5% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 22% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

86% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 12% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
