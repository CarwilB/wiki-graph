'''Frei Miguelinho''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈfɾej miɡɛˈlĩj̃u]}}) is a city in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Santa Maria do Cambucá]] (N); [[Caruaru]] and [[Riacho das Almas]] (S); [[Surubim]] (E); [[Vertentes]] (W)
* Area - 212.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 370 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] hipoxerófila
* Climate - [[semi-arid]], hot
* Annual average temperature - 23.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 166&nbsp;km
* Population - 15,546 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/frei-miguelinho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Frei Miguelinho are based in [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]] and [[chickens]].
