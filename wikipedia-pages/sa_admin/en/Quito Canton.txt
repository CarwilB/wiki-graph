'''Quito''', officially the '''Metropolitan District of Quito''' ({{langx|es|Distrito Metropolitano de Quito}}), is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] in the province of [[Pichincha Province|Pichincha]], [[Ecuador]].

==Governance==
[[File:Independence Square Historic Center - Plaza de la Independencia en Quito Ecuador.JPG|thumb|[[Plaza de la Independencia|Independence Square]] in the [[Historic Center of Quito]].]]
The '''Municipality of Quito''' (officially the '''Municipality of the Metropolitan District of Quito''') is the governing body of the city of [[Quito]] and the [[Quito (canton)|Metropolitan District]]. Its headquarters are at the Municipal Palace, located on the east side of the [[Plaza de La Independencia]]. The municipality is made of two principal organs: the mayor's office and a fifteen-member city council.

==Population figures==
According to the 2001 census, the total population of Quito (not the city itself) is 1,839,853, with 892,570 men and 947,283 women. According to the 2007 estimates, it is 1,840,000. Of this, the population of the urban parishes (the city of Quito itself) was 1,399,378, and the population of the rural parishes (outside of the city of Quito but still within the canton) was 440,475.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Dcs_tabla%26idTabla%3D82%26tipo%3Dp%26idProvincia%3D17%26idSeccion%3D%26idCiudad%3D51 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050525025510/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=cs_tabla&idTabla=82&tipo=p&idProvincia=17&idSeccion=&idCiudad=51 |archive-date=May 25, 2005 }}</ref> The total population density of the canton is 439.8 inhabitants per km² (1139.1/mi²). The total number of households in the canton is 555,928, with 419,845 in the urban area (the city) and 136,083 in the rural area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Dcs_tabla%26idTabla%3D89%26tipo%3Dp%26idProvincia%3D17%26idSeccion%3D%26idCiudad%3D51 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050525025836/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=cs_tabla&idTabla=89&tipo=p&idProvincia=17&idSeccion=&idCiudad=51 |archive-date=May 25, 2005 }}</ref> Of the 1,407,526 inhabitants of the canton over the age of 12, 639,068 are married, 541,758 are single, 33,116 are divorced, 30,898 are separated, and 47,930 are widowed.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Dcs_tabla%26idTabla%3D88%26tipo%3Dp%26idProvincia%3D17%26idSeccion%3D%26idCiudad%3D51 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060609031931/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=cs_tabla&idTabla=88&tipo=p&idProvincia=17&idSeccion=&idCiudad=51 |archive-date=June 9, 2006 }}</ref>

Overall, the canton's illiteracy rate is 4.3% &ndash; 5.3% for women, 3.2% for men, 3.6% in the urban area (the city), and 6.6% in the rural area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Dcs_grafico%26idGrafico%3D1005%26tipo%3Dp%26idProvincia%3D17%26idSeccion%3D%26idCiudad%3D51 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20050525025744/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=cs_grafico&idGrafico=1005&tipo=p&idProvincia=17&idSeccion=&idCiudad=51 |archive-date=May 25, 2005 }}</ref> The [[unemployment]] rate of the city is 8.9%, and 43.8% are [[underemployment|underemployed]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Denc_tabla%26idTabla%3D295 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20041207222551/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=enc_tabla&idTabla=295 |archive-date=December 7, 2004 }}</ref> The average monthly income is $317.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc%3Denc_tabla%26idTabla%3D299 |title=INEC |access-date=April 19, 2005 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20041207224101/http://www.inec.gov.ec/interna.asp?inc=enc_tabla&idTabla=299 |archive-date=December 7, 2004 }}</ref> <!--Education data at http://www.google.com/search?q=CIA%20factbook%20ecuador-->
