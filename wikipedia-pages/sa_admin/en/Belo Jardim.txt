'''Belo Jardim''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbɛlu ʒɐhˈdĩ]}}) (''Beautiful Garden'') a [[Brazil]]ian municipality in the state of [[Pernambuco]]. It has an estimated population in 2020 of 76,687 and a total area of 647.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/belo-jardim/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It is located at 608 meters above the sea level and 182&nbsp;km away from the state capital, [[Recife]].

The economy is based on [[agribusiness]] (poultry, guava-based products, other food), agriculture (beans, maize, sweet potatoes, banana, coffee, manioc, tomatoes, garlic, sugar cane), and automotive batteries.  The city is the headquarters of Acumuladores Moura S.A. (Baterias Moura).

The city is served by [[Belo Jardim Airport]].

==Economy==

{{Further|Largest Cities of Northeast Region, Brazil}}
The economy is based on agribusiness, agriculture, commerce and automotive batteries.  The city is the headquarters of Acumuladores Moura S.A. (Baterias Moura or ''Auto-batteries Moura'').
