'''Ganthier''' ({{IPA|fr|ɡɑ̃tje}}; {{langx|ht|Gantye}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Croix-des-Bouquets Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 71,261 inhabitants.
