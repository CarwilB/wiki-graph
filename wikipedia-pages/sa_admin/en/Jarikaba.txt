'''Jarikaba''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Saramacca District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 5,963.<ref name="census"/> [[Banana]]s form the backbone of the economy. Food And Agriculture Industries, the main employer, has {{convert|1,200|ha}} under cultivation, and a workforce of more than 1,000 people.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.gfcnieuws.com/bananenproductie-jarikaba-komt-op-gang/|title=Bananenproductie Jarikaba komt op gang|website=GFC Nieuws|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=1 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220701073804/https://www.gfcnieuws.com/bananenproductie-jarikaba-komt-op-gang/|url-status=dead}}</ref> Food And Agriculture Industries used export only to [[Europe]], but as of 2018 supplies much of the [[Caribbean]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.deboodschap.today/jarikaba-in-de-bloei/|title=Jarikaba in de bloei|website=De Boodschap.today|access-date=27 May 2020}}</ref>

The [[Jarikaba Airstrip]] is located near Jarikaba.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://airportguide.com/airport/info/SMJA |title=Jarikaba Airstrip Airport|website=Airport Guide|access-date=4 January 2022}}</ref>
