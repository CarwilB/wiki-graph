'''Nueva Arcadia''' ({{IPA|es|ˈnweβa aɾˈkaðja}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Copán department|Copán]]. La Entrada, with a population of 27,830 (2023 calculation),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> is the largest town in the municipality.
