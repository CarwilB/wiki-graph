'''Tola''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Rivas (department)|Rivas]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. Tola means "Land of Tula", or "Land of the Toltecs" and its etymology derives from [[Nahuatl]] Tōllān, the [[altepetl]] of the [[Mesoamerican chronology|Classic and Post Classic]] [[Toltec Empire]] of central [[Mexico]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcaldiadetola.gob.ni/index.php/conoce-tola/|title = Get to know Tola}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/municipio-de-tola/|title = Municipality of Tola}}</ref> Tola is a direct reference to the Toltec ancestry of the [[Nicarao people|Nahuas]] in Nicaragua.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.lavozdelnorte.com.mx/2014/04/13/migraciones-de-lengua-nahuatl-hacia-centroamerica/|title = Migraciones de lengua Náhuatl hacia Centroamérica}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal|url=https://www.jstor.org/stable/983071|title = Were the Toltecs an Historic Nationality| jstor=983071 | last1=Brinton | first1=Daniel G. | journal=Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society | date=1887 | volume=24 | issue=126 | pages=229–241 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.danielschavelzon.com.ar/?p=565|title = Las migraciones nahuas de México a Nicaragua según las fuentes históricas| date=29 April 2006 }}</ref> The indigenous inhabitants of Tola are the Nahuas.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://territorioindigenaygobernanza.com/web/nnic_09/|title = Nahoas. Territorio indígena y gobernanza}}</ref>

As of 2023, the estimated population of Tola is 23,927.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tola (Municipality, Nicaragua) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/rivas/8005__tola/ |access-date=2024-07-30 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
