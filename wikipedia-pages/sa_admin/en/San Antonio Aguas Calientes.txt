'''San Antonio Aguas Calientes''' ({{IPA|es|san anˈtonjo ˈaɣwas kaˈljentes}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Sacatepéquez]].<ref>[https://www.okantigua.com/visit-san-antonio-aguascalientes-textiles-market/ Visit San Antonio Aguascalientes Textiles Market]</ref>

The municipal seat is the town of San Andrés Ceballos which is known for its weavers. [[Maya peoples|Maya]] women in the area use a backstrap loom to weave traditional patterns. There is a two-story market on the square that sells weavings and other crafts. Several of the women have their looms set up and will demonstrate their skills. There are a couple other stores outside of town that also sell weavings.
