'''Sítio do Mato''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Sítio do Mato covers {{convert|1,627.776|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 13,059 with a population density of 8.1 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
