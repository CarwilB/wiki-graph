'''Ica''' is the largest of five [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Ica Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Ica (city)|Ica]].

[[Huacachina]] is a small town, oasis and resort in this region.

== Political division ==

The province of Ica is divided into fourteen [[district]]s ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: {{lang|es|distrito}}), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''):
