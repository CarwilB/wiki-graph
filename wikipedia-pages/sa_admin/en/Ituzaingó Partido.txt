'''Ituzaingó''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]]. It is in the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area, [[Argentina]], {{convert|10|km|abbr=out}} west of [[Buenos Aires]] city. It has an area of {{convert|38.51|km2|sqmi|1|abbr=on}} and a population of 180,232 ({{census-ar|2022}}).<ref name=Cen2010>{{Cite web |url=http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |title=2010 Census provisional results |access-date=2011-03-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120901061446/http://200.51.91.231/censo2010/ |archive-date=2012-09-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its capital, the city of [[Ituzaingó, Buenos Aires|Ituzaingó]], and the other districts in Ituzaingó Partido were part of the [[Morón Partido]] until 1995. 

==History==

The partido of ltuzaingó stems from the Provincial Law No. 11,610 enacted on December 28, 1994, when [[Eduardo Duhalde]] was governor of the [[Buenos Aires Province|Province of Buenos Aires]]. It allowed the creation of the partidos of [[Hurlingham, Buenos Aires|Hurlingham Partido]] and ltuzaingó from the division of the former partido of [[Morón| Morón Partido]].

During the elections of May 14, 1995 came the first elected officials of ltuzaingó Partido for 1995-1999: Alberto Daniel Descalzo was elected mayor and took office on December 11 that year at a ceremony in the hall acts of the School No. 1. At the same time, the City Council was constituted by [[Horacio Ramiro González]] (First City Council president), Marcelo Nadal, Adalberto Montes de Oca, Luis Sosa, and Jose Gonzalez composing [[Justicialist Party]] bloc. Ricardo Vallarino, Ruben Rosso, and Alberto Fusco composing  electoral coalition Frepaso-Pais bloc, and Yolanda Jaimez and Fernando Miño composing the [[Radical Civic Union]] bloc.<ref>[http://www.miituzaingo.gov.ar/CdelPartido.html Creation of Ituzaingó (in spanish)] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110925033729/http://www.miituzaingo.gov.ar/Cdelpartido.html |date=2011-09-25 }}</ref>

Currently, the mayor (Descalzo Jr.) and his family are under investigation for [[corruption]]. The investigation concerns the access to power and public office for his entire family. <ref>{{Cite web |date=2003-08-05 |title=Investigan al intendente de Ituzaingó por haber nombrado a cuatro parientes |url=https://www.lanacion.com.ar/politica/investigan-al-intendente-de-ituzaingo-por-haber-nombrado-a-cuatro-parientes-nid516927/ |access-date=2026-05-18 |website=LA NACION |language=es}}</ref>

==Insecurity - Crimes==

The homicide rate has historically averaged around 5 cases per 100,000 inhabitants, while urban robbery rates have peaked at over 1,100 cases per 100,000 inhabitants. <ref>{{Cite web |date=2019-08-31 |title=Ituzaingó, con la peor tasa del conurbano |url=https://www.lanacion.com.ar/seguridad/ituzaingo-con-la-peor-tasa-del-conurbano-nid2283236/ |access-date=2026-05-18 |website=LA NACION |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2019-09-05 |title=Ituzaingó es el municipio más violento e inseguro del conurbano |url=https://www.smnoticias.com/noticias/92504 |access-date=2026-05-18 |website=SM Noticias |language=es-AR}}</ref>
