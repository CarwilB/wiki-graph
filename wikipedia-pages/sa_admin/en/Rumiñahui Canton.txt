'''Rumiñahui''' (in Hispanicized spelling, {{IPA|es|rumiˈɲawi|}}) or '''Rumiñawi''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''rumi'' stone, ''ñawi'' eye,<ref>Teofilo Laime Acopa, Diccionario Bilingüe, Iskay simipi yuyay k'ancha, Quechua – Castellano, Castellano – Quechua (Quechua-Spanish dictionary)</ref> "stone eye") is a canton of [[Pichincha Province]] in [[Ecuador]]. Its seat is [[Sangolquí]]. The canton lies southeast of [[Quito Canton]] and forms a suburb of [[Quito]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.fao.org/docrep/w7445e/w7445e03.htm#2.3.%20general%20characteristics%20of%20the%20urban%20socio%20ecosystem%20in%20quito |title=2.3. General characteristics of the urban socio-ecosystem in Quito |website=Urban and Peri-Urban Forestry in Quito, Ecuador: a Case-Study |publisher=[[FAO]] |access-date=15 December 2016}}</ref>

Rumiñahui was named a ''[[Pueblos Mágicos (Ecuador)|Pueblo Mágico]]'' by the Ecuadorian Ministry of Tourism (MINTUR) in 2020. It was the first canton in Pichincha to be awarded that distinction.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Rumiñahui, el primer Pueblo Mágico de Pichincha|url=https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/ruminahui-pueblo-magico-pichincha-turismo.html|access-date=2021-04-02|website=El Comercio}}</ref>

==Parishes==
The canton is made up of the following parishes:
*Urban parishes: [[Sangolquí Parish|Sangolquí]], [[San Pedro de Taboada|Taboada]]
*Rural parishes: [[Cotogchoa]], [[Rumipamba, Rumiñahui|Rumipamba]]
