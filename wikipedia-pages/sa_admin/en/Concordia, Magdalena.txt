'''Concordia''' ({{IPA|es|koŋˈkoɾðja}}) is a town and municipality of the [[Magdalena Department]] in northern [[Colombia]]. Founded by Mandate 007 of June 24, 1999 with portion of territories from the municipalities of [[Cerro San Antonio, Magdalena|Cerro San Antonio]] and [[Pedraza, Magdalena|Pedraza]].
