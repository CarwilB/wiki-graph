'''Cartagena del Chairá''' ({{IPA|es|kaɾtaˈxena ðel tʃajˈɾa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Department of Caquetá]]. The town gained notoriety during the failed [[FARC-Government peace process (1999-2002)]] between the [[Government of Colombia]] and the guerrilla group [[Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia]] (FARC) which still maintains operations and a high influence in the area despite the presence of the [[Military of Colombia|Colombian military]].
