'''Yguazú''' ({{IPA|es|iɣwaˈsu|lang}}; {{langx|gn|Yguasu}} {{IPA|gn|ɨɣʷaˈsu|}}, {{small|after}} [[Iguazu River]], {{lit|Big Water}}), historically known as '''Colonia Yguazú''',<ref name="Muniyguazu">{{cite web |title=Datos del Distrito de Yguazú |trans-title=Data from the District of Yguazú |url=https://muniyguazu.gov.py/v1/index.php/historia |website=muniyguazu.gov.py |publisher=Municipalidad de Yguazú |access-date=19 March 2026 |language=es-PY}}</ref> is a municipality and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of [[Paraguay]]'s [[Alto Paraná Department]],<ref name="Quartucci2022">{{cite web |last1=Quartucci |first1=Soledad |date=29 December 2022 |title=Experience the Guarani World of Yguazu, Paraguay |url=https://latinarepublic.com/2022/12/29/experience-the-guarani-world-of-yguazu-paraguay/ |website=Latina Republic |access-date=1 February 2024}}</ref> founded by [[Japanese Paraguayans|Japanese immigrants]].

Yguazú was founded on 22 August, 1961, as a Japanese agricultural colony,<ref name="Quartucci2022"/><ref name="Muniyguazu"/> and is located 40 kilometers to the West of [[Ciudad del Este]].<ref name="Muniyguazu"/>
