'''Vizcatán del Ene district''' is one of the nine constituent districts of [[Satipo Province]] situated in the eastern part of the [[Department of Junín]], [[Peru]].

== History ==
The district was founded on 2 October 2015 by the government of President [[Ollanta Humala]] by enactment of Law Number 30346.<ref>[http://elcomercio.pe/peru/junin/satipo-promulgan-ley-que-crea-distrito-vizcatan-ene-noticia-1845638 El Comercio: Satipo: promulgan ley que crea distrito de Vizcatán del Ene]</ref><ref name="Ley30346">[http://www2.congreso.gob.pe/Sicr/TraDocEstProc/Contdoc02_2011_2.nsf/d99575da99ebfbe305256f2e006d1cf0/4959198ef4330ea205257d500004f293/$FILE/PL03786100914.pdf Ley de Creación del Distrito de Vizcatán del Ene]</ref>

In May 2021, the [[San Miguel del Ene attack]] was perpetrated in the area, resulting in the deaths of 18 people.
