'''Cabuya''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Antón District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|60.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,119 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|35|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,852; its population as of 2000 was 2,060.<ref name=inec/>
