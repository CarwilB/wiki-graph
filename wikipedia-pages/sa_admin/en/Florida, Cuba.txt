'''Florida''' ({{IPA|es|floˈɾiða}}) is a municipality and city in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located {{convert|40|km|mi|abbr=on}} north-west of [[Camagüey]], along the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]] highway. The city was established in 1907, and the municipality was established in 1924.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url = http://www.cmg.jovenclub.cu/index.php?pag=vew_notice.php&Id_noticia=3711&seccion=Florida|title = Historia del Municipio Florida|date = |accessdate = |website = Joven Club de Computación y Electrónica|publisher = |last = |first = |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20160303220848/http://www.cmg.jovenclub.cu/index.php?pag=vew_notice.php&Id_noticia=3711&seccion=Florida|archive-date = 2016-03-03|url-status = dead}}</ref> Of all the municipalities of the Cuban province of [[Camagüey]], Florida is third in area size.<ref name=":0" /> The name is Spanish for ''Land of flowers''.

==History==
The town was founded in 1907 and, in 1949, the politician Francisco Díaz Marchand presented a project to turn it into an independent municipality. The project did not prosper, but in 1960 Manuel Frías Morales drafted a law to carry out the feasibility studies for the foundation of the [[municipality]], but the opinion was adverse. Later, the Law #30 of June 14, 1971 created the municipality of Florida, one of the youngest of the island.

==Geography==
The municipality is located northwest of its province, next to the borders with [[Ciego de Ávila Province]]. Its elevation is represented by the Urabo Mountain with a height of 132.5 m. The bordering municipalities are [[Baraguá]], [[Carlos Manuel de Céspedes, Cuba|Céspedes]], [[Esmeralda, Cuba|Esmeralda]], [[Camagüey]] and [[Vertientes]].<ref>{{OSM|r|5892223|Florida}}</ref>

The municipal territory includes the villages and the localities of Alberto Becerra, [[Algarrobo (Florida)|Algarrobo]], Conquista, El Angel, El Trece, La Alína, La Caridad, La Esperanza, La Jagua, La Porfuerza, La Serafina, [[La Tomatera]], La Vallita, [[Las Margaritas (Florida, Cuba)|Las Margaritas]], Las Mercedes, Las Parras, Las Tusas, Los Güines, María Dos, María Seis, Manzanillo, Ortigal, Otero, [[Pérez Pérez]], [[Playa Florida]], [[San Antonio (Florida, Cuba)|San Antonio]], San Diego, San Faustino, San Jerónimo, Santa Gertrudis, Urabo and Vista Alegre. Until the 1977 municipal reform, it included the village of [[Magarabomba]] too,<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/cflorida/index.htm|title=Florida|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-05|language=es}}</ref> currently part of Céspedes.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Florida had a population of 69,174,<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> 56,271 of them in the town proper.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Florida (Municipality, Cuba) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/camag%C3%BCey/3009__florida/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1800|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|38|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

== Health ==
Florida has 283 doctors, 187 of them are family physicians. There are 125 health facilities, including hospitals (a general and a pediatric one), clinics, pharmacies and laboratories.<ref name=":0" />

== Education ==
There are 64 educational centers with a total of 15,013 students attending primary, secondary, and [[Pre-tertiary education|pre-university]].<ref name=":0" /> Florida also offers [[night school]]s with a range of educational careers for adults who can not attend during daylight hours.

==Transport==
[[File:Florida, Cuba - panoramio (3).jpg|thumb|right|Florida railway station]]
{{See also|Florida Airport (Cuba)}}
Florida is served by the "Central Railway" (''Ferrocarril Central'') [[Havana]]-[[Santiago de Cuba|Santiago]], with local and long-distance trains. It is crossed by the national highway "[[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]]" (CC) and, the future extension of the [[Autopista A1 (Cuba)|A1 motorway]], will involve the town.
