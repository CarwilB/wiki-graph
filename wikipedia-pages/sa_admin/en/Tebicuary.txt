'''Tebicuary''' is a [[district]] in the [[Guairá Department]] of Paraguay. It is located 132&nbsp;km to the southwest of [[Asunción|Asuncion]] and 30&nbsp;km to the west of [[Villarrica, Paraguay|Villarrica]]. In 2008, it became a district being disengaged from neighboring [[Coronel Martínez]]. It is the most recently created district in the Guairá Department. The first mayor was the businessman Juan Bosch Beynen.<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |last=Barreto |first=Antonio Ramon |date=April 30, 2023 |title=15 años de distritación de Tebicuary, en el Guairá (2) |trans-title=15 year anniversary of the district of Tebicuary, in Guaira (2) |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/cultural/2023/04/30/15-anos-de-distritacion-de-tebicuary-en-el-guaira-2/ |access-date=September 19, 2024 |work=ABC Digital |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
The name Tebicuary derives from the [[Guarani language]] expression ''Teyikuera'' which is a possible reference to the tribes named ''Teyy''. The Teyy people used to inhabit the current departments of [[Misiones, Paraguay|Misiones]] and [[Paraguarí Department|Paraguarí]]. In this area runs a river also named [[Tebicuary River|Tebicuary]] which is a tributary of the [[Paraguay River]]. As the Spanish [[conquistador]]s mispronounced the name of the tribe, the original Guarani name evolved into the current spelling.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Barreto |first=Antonio Ramon |date=April 23, 2023 |title=15 años de distritación de Tebicuary, en el Guairá (1) |trans-title=15 year anniversary of Tebicuary district, in Guaira (1) |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/cultural/2023/04/23/15-anos-de-distritacion-de-tebicuary-en-el-guaira-1/ |access-date=September 19, 2024 |work=ABC Digital |language=es}}</ref>

According to her 1779 will, Juana Ortiz de Zarate, [[Mestizo|mestiza]] daughter of Spanish conquistador and governor [[Juan Ortiz de Zárate]] was mentioned as a resident in an estate located in the ''Partido of Ybytymini''. By that time the area of Ybytymini comprised the current territory of Tebicuary as well as of neighboring district [[Ybytimí]]. Juana had acquired the estate from a royal ensign named Juan Bautista Achard. In 1785, Spanish traveller [[Félix de Azara]] described Ybytymini as "of broad extension" and with a population of 600 people and with crops like [[sugarcane]], [[tobacco]] and [[yucca]].<ref name=":0" />
