'''Chulucanas District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Morropón Province|Morropón]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref>  Its administrative headquarters is in the town of [[Chulucanas]].
