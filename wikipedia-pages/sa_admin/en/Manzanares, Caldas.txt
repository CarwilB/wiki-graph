'''Manzanares''' ({{IPA|es|mansaˈnaɾes}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]].

==Climate==
Manzanares has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb''). It has warm afternoons, pleasant mornings, and heavy rainfall year-round.
