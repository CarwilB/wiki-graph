'''Calumbi'''  is a city  in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], was 5,354 inhabitants. The total area is 179.31&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Triunfo, Pernambuco|Triunfo]] and [[Santa Cruz da Baixa Verde]] (N); [[Betânia, Pernambuco|Betânia]] (S);  [[Flores, Pernambuco|Flores]]   (E);   [[Serra Talhada]]  (W)
* Area - 221.04&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 446 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi-arid]], hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 24.8 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 408&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Calumbi are based in [[agribusiness]], especially the raising of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goat]]s, and [[chickens]], and plantations of [[tomato]]es and [[banana]]s .
