'''Acobamba''' or '''Aqupampa''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''aqu'' sand, ''pampa'' large plain, "sand plain") is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in the middle of [[Tarma Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is bordered by districts of [[Tarma District|Tarma]], [[La Unión District, Tarma|La Unión]]  and [[Palcamayo District|Palcamayo]] on the west, [[Huasahuasi District]] on the north, districts of [[Palca District, Tarma|Palca]] and [[Tapo District|Tapo]] on the east, and  districts of [[Tapo District|Tapo]] and [[Tarma District|Tarma]] on the south.
