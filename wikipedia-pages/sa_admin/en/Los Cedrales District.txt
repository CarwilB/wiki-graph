'''Los Cedrales''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|881|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 11,959 individuals. It was officially established on 16 January 1990.

==History==
Los Cedrales was established officially as a district by the Law 60/89 enacted on 16 January 1990. It was carved out of the districts of [[Presidente Franco District|Presidente Franco]] and [[Domingo Martínez de Irala, Paraguay|Domingo Martínez de Irala]].<ref name="Law">{{cite web|title=Ley N° 60/89 que crea el Distrito Los Cedrales del Departamento de Alto Paraná|work=Justia Paraguay|url=https://paraguay.justia.com/nacionales/leyes/ley-60-jan-16-1990/gdoc/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Law 60/89 creating the District of Los Cedrales}}</ref><ref name="ADN">{{cite web|title=Los Cedrales celebra con entusiasmo su 27 aniversario fundacional|work=ADN Digital|url=http://www.adndigital.com.py/los-cedrales-celebra-con-entusiasmo-su-27-aniversario-fundacional|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200217232940/http://www.adndigital.com.py/los-cedrales-celebra-con-entusiasmo-su-27-aniversario-fundacional/|archive-date=17 February 2020|access-date=23 May 2025|language=es|trans-title=Los Cedrales celebrates its 27th founding anniversary}}</ref>

==Geography==
Los Cedrales is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|403.1|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1010__los_cedrales/|title=Los Cedrales District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is separated from the Presidente Franco and [[Minga Porá District|Minga Pora]] districts to the north by the Monday and Cedroty rivers, and Domingo Martínez de Irala to the south by the Ytuti river, a tributary of the [[Paraná River]].<ref name="Law"/>

Los Cedrales is located at an elevation of {{cvt|221.66|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|23.73|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|19.82|cm}} and has 160.58 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/alto-parana/los-cedrales|title=Los Cedrales, Climate|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, Los Cedrales had a population of 7,258 inhabitants of which 3,769 were males and 3,489 were females. About 58.2% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (41.8%) lived in urban areas. About 26.7% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 7.7% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>

==Culture and economy==
The economy is mainly dependent on [[agriculture]]. Major agricultural produce includes [[soybean]], [[maize]], [[wheat]], and [[sunflower]].<ref name="ADN"/>

The patron saint is Virgin Aparecida, who has a chapel dedicated to her.<ref name="ADN"/> The Los Cedrales historical museum was opened in 2017, and preserves various artifacts related to its history.<ref>{{cite web|title=Inauguran un museo en Los Cedrales que rescata la historia de los pioneros|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/interior/inauguran-un-museo-en-los-cedrales-1628086.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Museum inaugurated in Los Cedrales preserving pioneer history}}</ref>
