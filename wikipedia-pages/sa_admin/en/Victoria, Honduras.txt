'''Victoria''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Yoro department|Yoro]].<ref>[https://www.google.com/maps/@14.93956,-87.391834,3182m/data=!3m1!1e3 Google Maps - Victoria]</ref>

It is served by [[Victoria Airport (Honduras)|Victoria Airport]], a {{convert|700|m}} grass [[airstrip]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Victoria municipality had a population of 33,019. Of these, 88.60% were [[Mestizo]], 7.14% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (6.69% [[Tolupan]]), 3.63% [[White Latin Americans|White]],  0.61% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.02% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
