The '''Junín Municipality''' is one of the 29 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] that makes up the western [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Táchira (state)|Táchira]] and, according to the [[2011 Venezuelean census]], the municipality had a population of 88,699. The mayor of the municipality is Jackson Carrillo of the [[Democratic Unity Roundtable|MUD]] party since November 2021 and the town of [[Rubio, Venezuela|Rubio]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Junín Municipality.

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Junín Municipality was 88,699 people, accounting for 7.4% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref> The majority (81.66%) of the municipality's population is in the town of [[Rubio, Venezuela|Rubio]], the [[municipal seat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/tachira/2014__jun%C3%ADn/|title=Urban Localities in Junín (Táchira, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Junín as 92,805 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.57% since 2011 and showing a population density of 446.2 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2014__jun%C3%ADn/|title=Junín (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 49.4% men (39,864) and 50.6% women (40,816). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 69.1% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 23.8% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 7.1%. The municipality is mostly urbanized, with 88.8% of inhabitants (71,671) living in [[urban centers]] compared to just 11.2% (9,009) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (57.2%) and [[Mestizo]] (41.8%). Minority groups included 0.5% [[Afro-Venezuelans]], a small [[Indigenous peoples|indigenous]] population of 12 individuals, and 0.4% belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 96.6%, with 2,309 inhabitants of Junín not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Government==
The mayor of the Junín Municipality is Jackson Carrillo of the [[Democratic Unity Roundtable]] (MUD) party. He won the election in November 2021 with 37.12% of the vote, beating the sitting mayor Ángel Márquez who received 34.86%.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/politica/con-jackson-carrillo-la-mud-obtuvo-el-triunfo-en-junin/|title=Con Jackson Carrillo la MUD obtuvo el triunfo en Junín|trans-title=With Jackson Carrillo, the MUD Wins in Junín|lang=es|first=Norma|last=Pérez|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|date=24 November 2021|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref> In July 2025, he was re-elected, receiving 56.93% of the vote.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/politica/de-la-mano-de-dios/|title=De la mano de Dios|trans-title=By God's Grace|lang=es|first=Norma|last=Pérez|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|date=4 August 2025|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Bramon, Junín, La Petrolea, and Quinimarí.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://callejero-venezuela.openalfa.com/municipio-junin|title= Callejero de Municipio Junín, Estado Táchira |trans-title=Street map of Junín Municipality, Táchira State|lang=es|website=Callejero Venezuela|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>
