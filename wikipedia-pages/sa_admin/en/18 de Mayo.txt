'''18 de Mayo''' or '''Dieciocho de Mayo''' is a city and municipality in the [[Canelones Department]] of [[Uruguay]].

==Establishment==
After a process of evaluation at departmental level,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.elpais.com.uy/13/03/06/pciuda_700395.asp |title=Aspiran a crear nuevos municipios |language=es |date=6 March 2013 |publisher=[[El País (Uruguay)|El País]]}}</ref> this municipality was created on 15 March 2013.<ref name="30mun">{{cite web |url=http://www.elpais.com.uy/13/03/22/pciuda_704216.asp |title=Crean alcaldía número 30 en zona de villas |language=es |date=22 March 2013 |publisher=[[El País (Uruguay)|El País]]}}</ref>

It includes several towns, belonging until that date to the municipalities of [[Las Piedras, Uruguay|Las Piedras]] and [[Progreso, Uruguay|Progreso]], in the old Route 5 area:<ref name="30mun"/> Villa Alegria, Vista Linda, El Dorado, San Francisco, Villa Cristina, San Isidro, El Dorado Chico and Villa Foresti.<ref name="corresponsal"/>

Its name commemorates the [[Battle of Las Piedras (1811)|Battle of Las Piedras]], which took place in 1811.

There is concern at the political and local level for giving this populous area a city status.<ref name="corresponsal">{{cite web|url=http://elcorresponsal.blogia.com/2008/010701-crearian-una-nueva-ciudad-en-canelones.php |title=Crearían una nueva ciudad en Canelones |accessdate=22 March 2013 |publisher=El Corresponsal|language=es}}</ref>

==Government==
The authority of the municipality is the Municipal Council, composed of the mayor and four councilors.
