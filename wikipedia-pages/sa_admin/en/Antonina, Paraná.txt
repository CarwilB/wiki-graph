'''Antonina''' is a [[municipality]] in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in southern [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 18,949.

==Geography==
Antonina has an area of {{convert|845.853|km2}}. It is located at {{coord|25|6|0|S|48|43|00|W}}.  It is 80&nbsp;km away from [[Curitiba]].

The municipality contains 29% of the {{convert|2699|ha|adj=on}} [[Roberto Ribas Lange State Park]], created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Roberto Ribas Lange – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PES Roberto Ribas Lange|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/593802|access-date=2016-11-17}}</ref> It contains part of the {{convert|4334|ha|adj=on}} [[Pico Paraná State Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|publisher=State of Paraná
 |last1=Lerner |first1=Jaime|first2=José Antonio |last2=Andreguetto|first3=José Cid Filho|last3=Campêlo|title=Decreto 5769 - 05 de Junho de 2002|date=5 June 2002
 |url=http://www.legislacao.pr.gov.br/legislacao/pesquisarAto.do?action=exibir&codAto=33133&indice=1&totalRegistros=5|access-date=2016-11-19}}</ref>
It also holds 30% of the {{convert|34179|ha|adj=on}} [[Bom Jesus Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit established in 2012.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|REBIO Bom Jesus – ISA}}|language=pt |title=REBIO Bom Jesus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/583144|access-date=2016-06-05}}</ref>

==History==
The small town of Antonina was former known as "Capela" – as a chapel was built here in the 18th century. Due to this fact the inhabitants are called "capelistas" to this day. The official name is in honor to [[António, Prior of Crato|King Antonio]] of [[Portugal]].

==Culture==
Antonina hosts a July winter festival promoted by the [[Federal University of Paraná]], which develops several workshops and shows during this time in the village. The carnival takes place within several blocks and features [[samba]] groups.
