'''Cidreira''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cidreira.rs.gov.br/paginas/historia.htm |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Cideira |access-date=2011-04-21 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101115035203/http://www.cidreira.rs.gov.br/paginas/historia.htm |archive-date=2010-11-15 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
It is located at a latitude of 30º09'39" South and a longitude of 50º14'02" West, at an altitude of 60 meters above sea level.  Its estimated population for 2006 was 11,767 inhabitants. It occupies an area of 246,362&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
