'''São Bento do Una''' is a city from the Northeastern Region of [[Brazil]] at the [[Pernambuco]] state. According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], it has an estimated population of 51,165 inhabitants (2025).<ref name="IBGE" /> It is located at [[latitude]] 08°31'22" South and [[longitude]] 36°26'40" West, and at approximately 614 meters above sea level.

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Belo Jardim]]  ('''N'''); [[Jucati]], [[Jupi]] and [[Lajedo, Brazil|Lajedo]]  ('''S''');  [[Capoeiras]], [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]] and [[Sanharó]]  ('''W''');  [[Cachoeirinha]]  ('''E''')
* Area - 727&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 614 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] rivers
* Vegetation - [[Hipoxerófila]] [[caatinga]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 207&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''São Bento do Una''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially plantations of [[bean]]s, [[Maize|corn]] and [[manioc]] and creations of [[livestock]] such as: [[chickens]], [[quail]]s and their [[egg (food)|eggs]], [[cattle]], [[milk]], [[pig]]s, [[sheep]], [[goat]]s and [[donkey]]s .
