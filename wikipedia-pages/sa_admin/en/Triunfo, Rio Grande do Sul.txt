'''Triunfo''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 29,856 (2020 est.) in an area of 818.80&nbsp;km².<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/13R3 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It is situated at the confluence of the rivers [[Taquari River (Rio Grande do Sul)|Taquari]] and [[Jacuí River|Jacuí]]. It is the richest municipality in Brazil, with a per capita income of R$122,750 (US$65,275).{{citation needed|date=October 2015}}
