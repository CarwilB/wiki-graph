'''Roque Peréz Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1600|km2|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Roque Pérez]], which is around {{convert|135|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
