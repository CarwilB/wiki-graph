'''Bolívar''' is a municipality of [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]], [[Venezuela]]. The capital is [[Marigüitar]].<ref name="DPT">{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf|title=División Político Territorial de la República Bolivariana de Venezuela|accessdate=25 October 2015|date=September 2013|agency=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística (Venezuela)|INE]]|archive-date=11 December 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201211040255/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> It was founded in 1713 after many years of missionary work taking place in the area.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.turismosucre.com.ve/pueblosyciudades/mariguitar/|title=Marigüitar • Municipio Bolívar • Estado Sucre|website=www.turismosucre.com.ve|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "Bolívar Municipality" in honor of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].

== Geography ==
The municipality lies about 12 meters above sea level,<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://mapcarta.com/28603988|title=Municipio Bolívar|website=Mapcarta|language=en|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref> with its northern border on the [[Gulf of Cariaco]]. The [[Marigüitar River]] runs through the municipality into the gulf.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://mimariguitar.blogspot.com/|title=Un Poco de Historia|last=Marigüitar|date=2010-12-12|website=Marigüitar|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref> Besides the capital, the municipality has seven main population centers: Guaracayar, Güirintar-Carenero, La Soledad, Sotillo, La chica, Golindano, and Petare.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://ve.geoview.info/marigueitar,1433488807n|title=Marigüitar, Municipio Bolívar, Sucre, Venezuela|website=ve.geoview.info|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref> It is accessible by [[trunk road]] 09 that runs west-east from [[Cumaná]] to [[San Antonio del Golfo]], and by sea from the gulf.<ref name=":0" />

Despite bordering the coast, in 2019 the municipality began to suffer from severe water shortages.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://elpitazo.net/oriente/comunidad-valle-soto-cerro-la-via-cumana-carupano-en-protesta-por-la-falta-de-agua/|title=Comunidad Valle Soto cerró la vía Cumaná-Carúpano en protesta por la falta de agua {{!}} El Pitazo|website=elpitazo.net|date=14 May 2019|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref> There have been infrastructure works since 2000 to improve the safe water flow in the municipality, works which have seen over 15,000 laborers from the local community and which have been largely funded by local export companies.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.redactivateyconstruye.org/Experiencias/Oriente/Identificacion-y-formulacion.aspx?id=Consejo%20Comunal|title=Red Activate y Construye - 14. TOr Identificación y formulación|website=www.redactivateyconstruye.org|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The economy of this municipality is based primarily in fishing,<ref name=":0" /> but there is also some tourism.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=http://aperturaven.blogspot.com/2009/10/se-perdio-por-falta-de-unidad-municipio.html|title=Apertura Venezuela: Se perdió por falta de unidad. Municipio Bolívar, Estado Sucre|last=Aperturaven|date=2009-10-30|website=Apertura Venezuela|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref> Combining both, the municipality also engages in promoting aquatourism.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://sucrecorsotur.blogspot.com/2016/04/la-comunidad-de-la-fragata-del.html|title=Corsotur Sucre: LA COMUNIDAD DE LA FRAGATA DEL MUNICIPIO BOLÍVAR EN EL ESTADO SUCRE SE PREPARA PARA LA CONSOLIDACIÓN DE LA RUTA ACUICULTURÍSTICA|last=|first=|date=2016-04-15|website=Corsotur Sucre|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref>

Based in Marigüitar, are several large fish and seafood packaging companies.<ref name=":0" />

The municipality has some hotel complexes and native cultural food outlets, for the national and international tourists that come because of its beaches and landscapes. There are also about 10 pharmacies in the municipality, which began selling suncream for tourists as well as providing medicines for the locals.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://esp.pharmaciesworldwide.com/farmacias-baratas-en-venezuela/p-farmacias-en-municipio-bolivar-sucre/|title=Farmacias Baratas en Municipio bolívar (sucre) - Directorio - Listado - Guía - farmacias - Baratas - Sucre - Venezuela|website=Pharmaciesworldwide|language=es|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref>

== Government ==
The municipality is governed by a mayor and a council, all of whom are elected and are affiliated with a national party. The current mayor is Héctor Frontado, who was previously the mayor of neighboring municipality Mejía from 2013-17.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://pananoticias.wordpress.com/2013/10/10/san-antonio-del-golfo-esta-resteado-con-hectorfrontado/|title=San Antonio del Golfo está resteado con Héctor Frontado|last=|first=|date=10 October 2013|website=Pananoticias|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131107030526/http://pananoticias.wordpress.com/2013/10/10/san-antonio-del-golfo-esta-resteado-con-hectorfrontado/ |archive-date=2013-11-07 |access-date=11 September 2019}}</ref> In 2017, Cabeza was arrested and later sentenced for a corruption scheme in which his used his office as mayor to illegally obtain expensive goods.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://lanacionweb.com/sucesos/condenan-exalcalde-del-municipio-bolivar-en-sucre-por-venta-ilicita-de-vehiculos/|title=Condenan exalcalde del municipio Bolívar en Sucre por venta ilícita de vehículos|last=ELR|date=2019-04-24|website=Diario La Nación|language=es-VE|access-date=2019-09-11}}</ref>
