'''Famatina''' is an [[Argentina|Argentine]] Department in [[La Rioja Province, Argentina|La Rioja Province]].

== Geography ==
The department borders [[Catamarca Province]] to the north, [[San Blas de los Sauces Department]], [[Castro Barros Department]] and [[Sanagasta Department]] to the east, [[Chilecito Department]] to the south, [[Vinchina Department]] to the west and [[General Lamadrid Department]] to the southwest.

== Population ==
According to estimates for June 2007, the population was 6819 inhabitants.<ref>Dirección General de Estadística de la Provincia de La Rioja [http://www.larioja.gov.ar/estadistica/Principal/dpe.htm] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229073212/http://www.larioja.gov.ar/estadistica/Principal/dpe.htm|date=2012-02-29}}</ref>

Author: Fernando Falke</ref> birds Upucerthia validirostris rufescens, Upucerthia ruficauda famatinae, Cinclodes fuscus riojanus, [[Asthenes modesta serrana]], lizard [[Liolaemus famatinae]] (near extinction), [[Phymaturus mallimacci]] (near extinction); mammals  [[Abrocoma famatina]] (near extinction) and Lagidium viscacia famatinae.<ref name = iucn>{{cite web |url = https://www.iucnredlist.org/details/11148/0 |author1=Dunnum, J. |author2=Vargas, J. |author3=Bernal, N. |author4=Zeballos, H. |author5=Lessa, E. |author6=Ojeda, R. |author7=Bidau, C. |year = 2008 |title=Lagidium viscacia |access-date = 17 November 2010 }}</ref><ref name= AVES>{{cite web|url=http://www.avesargentinas.org.ar/12/noticia.php?id=494#_ftn1  |title=Aves Argentinas |publisher=Asociación Ornitológica del Plata|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120419224311/http://www.avesargentinas.org.ar/12/noticia.php?id=494#_ftn1 |archive-date=2012-04-19 |date=2012-02-03}}</ref>

There are populations of threatened species of the [[Andes]] region ([[Taruca Hippocamelus antisensis]] (a large [[deer]] species), [[Vultur gryphus]] and [[Rhea pennata]].<ref name= AVES/>

Endemic plants include [[Baccharis famatinensis]].<ref name= FALKE/>

===Serranías del Famatina Provincial Wildlife Reserve===
The Reserve was decreed in 2002 by provincial law 7292. However, that reserve was never demarcated or implemented.<ref name= AVES/>
