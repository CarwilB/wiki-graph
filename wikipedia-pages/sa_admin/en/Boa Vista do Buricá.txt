'''Boa Vista do Buricá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]], located at {{Coord|27|40|07|S|54|06|36|W|region:BR_type:city(6468)}}, at an altitude of 291 meters above sea level. It has an area of 108,732 kilometers<sup>2</sup>. The population was estimated at 6,712 in 2020. It has a predominantly agricultural economy, but there is also an industrial park for small and medium enterprises in different sectors. Its population consists mainly of the descendants [[Europe]]an of immigrants, the vast majority of [[Germans|German]]ic origin. German is frequently used.
