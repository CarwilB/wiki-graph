'''Jorge Basadre''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[department of Tacna]], [[Peru]]. Located in the country's southern coast, it is the westernmost province of the department. Its capital is [[Locumba]].

==Etymology==
The province is named after [[Jorge Basadre|Jorge Basadre Grohmann]], one of the country's most notable [[historian]]s, and a native of the city of [[Tacna]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial Jorge basadre. [http://www.munijorgebasadre.gob.pe/ Municipalidad Provincial Jorge basadre]. Retrieved March 10, 2008</ref>

==History==
The province was created on April 21, 1988, through law No. 24799,<ref>{{Cite web|language=es |id=Law No. 24799 |url=http://munijorgebasadre.gob.pe/archivos/creacion1.pdf |title=Crean en el Departamento de Tacna la Provincia de "Jorge Basadre" |trans-title=The Province of "Jorge Basadre" is created in the Department of Tacna |website=munijorgebasadre.gob.pe |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081203081547/http://www.munijorgebasadre.gob.pe/archivos/creacion1.pdf |archive-date=December 3, 2008 |url-status=dead |access-date=March 10, 2008}}</ref> prior to which its territory had belonged to [[Tacna province]].

==Politics==
The province is administered by a municipal government that also administers [[Locumba District]]. The [[Catholic Church in Peru]] administers the province as part of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Tacna and Moquegua]] since 1944.

==Geography==
The province is the site of [[Toquepala mine]].

===Boundaries===
*'''Northwest''': [[Moquegua Region]]
*'''Northeast''': [[Candarave Province]]
*'''Southeast''': [[Tacna Province]]
*'''Southwest''': [[Pacific Ocean]]

==Demographics==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], however, is the language which the majority of the population (79.88%) learnt to speak in childhood, 15.90% of the residents started speaking using the [[Aymara language]] and 4.01% using [[Quechua language|Quechua]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C |title=Censos Nacionales 2007: XI de Poblacion y VI de Vivienda |trans-title=National Census 2007: XI of Population and VI of Housing |work=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110803211256/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C |archive-date=August 3, 2011 |url-status=dead |location=Peru |lang=es}}</ref>
