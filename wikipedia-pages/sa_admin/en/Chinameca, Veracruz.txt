'''Chinameca''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
It is located in the south-east zone of the State of [[Veracruz (state)|Veracruz]], about 290&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{cvt|157.10|km2}}. It is located at {{coord|18|01|N|94|41|W|}}.

Chinameca is delimited to the north by the municipalities of [[Mecayapan Municipality|Mecayapan]] and [[Pajapan Municipality|Pajapan]], to the east by [[Cosoleacaque Municipality|Cosoleacaque]], to the south by [[Oteapan Municipality|Oteapan]], [[Jaltipan de Morelos Municipality|Jaltipan de Morelos]] and [[Soconusco Municipality|Soconusco]] and to the west by [[Soteapan Municipality|Soteapan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30059a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chinameca|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128143148/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30059a.htm|archive-date=2007-01-28|url-status=dead}}</ref>
===Weather===
The weather in Chinameca is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[Orange (fruit)|oranges]].

==Events==
In '''Chinameca''', the celebration in honor to Purísima Concepción, Patron of the town, takes place in December.
