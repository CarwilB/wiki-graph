'''Carnaubeira da Penha''' is a city in the [[Brazil]]ian [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], 498&nbsp;km away from the state's capital, [[Recife]].

==History==
The unclassified extinct language ''[[Uamué language|Aticum]]'' or ''Araticum'' was spoken near Carnaubeira.<ref name="Loukotka">{{cite book |last=Loukotka |first=Čestmír |authorlink=Čestmír Loukotka |title=Classification of South American Indian languages |url=https://archive.org/details/classificationof0007louk |url-access=registration |publisher=UCLA Latin American Center |year=1968 |location=Los Angeles}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The population in 2020, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 13,025 inhabitants and the total area is 1004.67&nbsp;km².<ref>{{Cite web |title=Carnaubeira da Penha |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/carnaubeira-da-penha/panorama |access-date=2024-06-14 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

There are [[Atikum]] and Pankará indigenous peoples living in reserves.

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Carnaubeira da Penha''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s (over 80,000 heads), [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[donkey]]s, [[chicken]]s; and plantations of [[banana]]s, [[tomato]]es and [[guava]].
