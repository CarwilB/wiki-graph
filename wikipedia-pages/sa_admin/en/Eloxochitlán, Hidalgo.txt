'''Eloxochitlán''' is a village and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|200.4|km2}}.

In 2005, the municipality had a total population of 2,417.<ref>{{cite web |title=Eloxochitlán |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13020a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=December 27, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070306105352/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13020a.htm |archive-date=March 6, 2007 }}</ref>

The name contains the [[Nahuatl]] elements ''elotl'' (maize), ''xochitl'' (flower) and ''tlan'' (place).<ref>{{cite web | url=https://ayuntamientoeloxochitlanhgo.gob.mx/historia.html | title=Eloxochitlan Hidalgo &#124; Gobierno municipal }}</ref>
