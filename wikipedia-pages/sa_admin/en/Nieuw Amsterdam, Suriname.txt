'''Nieuw Amsterdam'''{{efn|{{langx|nl|Nieuw-Amsterdam}}, {{IPA|nl|ˌniu ɑmstərˈdɑm|pron}}, {{lit|New Amsterdam}}; {{langx|srn|Nyunfoto}}; {{langx|hns|Kilá}}; {{langx|jvn|Nyumpoto}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|title=Verjavaanste toponiemen in Suriname en hun herkomst|trans-title=Javanese-influenced place names in Suriname and their origin|first=Hein|last=Vruggink|publisher=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|date=1987|lang=nl}}</ref>}} is the capital of the [[Commewijne District]] in [[Suriname]]. It is a small coastal town situated at the confluence of the [[Suriname River]] and [[Commewijne River]], just across from [[Paramaribo]], the country's capital. Its population at the 2012 census was 5,650,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Suriname Ressorts |url=http://www.statoids.com/ysr.html |access-date=2024-09-30 |website=www.statoids.com}}</ref> with around 1,200 people living in the main town, most of whom are of [[Javanese Surinamese|Javanese]] and [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]] origin.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://spangstaging.com/2019/abs/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/census8etn.pdf|title=Census 2012|website=Stang Makandra|access-date=9 May 2020|archive-date=23 December 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211223031229/https://spangstaging.com/2019/abs/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/census8etn.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> It is the location of the historical [[Fort Nieuw-Amsterdam]], today an open-air museum. The town of [[Mariënburg]] with former sugarcane factory is located 3 km from Nieuw Amsterdam and part of the resort.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tripadvisor.nl/Attraction_Review-g1202804-d12300703-Reviews-Marienburg_suikerfabriek-Nieuw_Amsterdam_Commewijne_District.html|title=Marienburg suikerfabriek Nieuw Amsterdam Commewijne District|language=nl|website=Trip Advisor|access-date=17 May 2020}}</ref>

==History==
In 1734, the [[Society of Suriname]] decided to build a fort near the confluence of the Commewijne and Suriname River to defend the [[Surinam (Dutch colony)|Colony of Suriname]] and the capital [[Paramaribo]].<ref name="fort_website">{{Cite web|url=http://www.fortnieuwamsterdam.sr/welkom/geschiedenis/|title=Geschiedenis|language=nl|website=Fort Nieuw Amsterdam|access-date=9 May 2020|archive-date=23 July 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190723092918/http://www.fortnieuwamsterdam.sr/welkom/geschiedenis/|url-status=dead}}</ref> On 8 October 1834, Nieuw-Amsterdam became the capital of the newly formed District of Upper Commewijne.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/usr.html|title=Districts of Suriname |website=Statoids|access-date=9 May 2020}}</ref> Between 1843 and 1854, Nieuw Amsterdam was designated as the location where incoming ships had to [[quarantine]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 594 - Quarantaine|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=9 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1872, part of the fort was converted to a jail, and would remain the only jail in Suriname until 1967.<ref name="fort_website"/> In 1873 the local population started a program of independent agriculture which was encouraged by [[List of colonial governors of Suriname|governor]] van Sypesteyn who designated Nieuw Amsterdam and [[Domburg, Suriname|Domburg]] as suitable locations.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 441 - Landbouw|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=5 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> On 17 January 1888, a decision was taken to lay the first [[telephone]] line between Nieuw-Amsterdam and Paramaribo with branches to [[redoubt]]s Leiden and [[Johan & Margaretha|Frederiksdorp]], and to operate the network from Nieuw-Amsterdam, because it was expected to mainly for military purposes.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 587 - Telefonie|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=9 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

With the completion of the [[Jules Wijdenbosch Bridge]] on 20 May 2000, the [[East-West Link (Suriname)|East-West Link]] was further expanded to Commewijne and [[Marowijne District|Marowijne]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.entoen.nu/nl/suriname/suriname/jules-wijdenboschbrug|title=Wijdenboschbrug|website=Canon van Nederland|language=nl|access-date=9 May 2020}}</ref> There is also ferry service from [[Ferry terminal Leonsberg|Leonsberg]] in Paramaribo.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://groenroodwit.nl/2013/02/fort-nieuw-amsterdam-suriname/ |title=Fort Nieuw Amsterdam: Lichtschepen en kanonnen|website=Groen Rood Wit|access-date=14 January 2022|language=nl}}</ref>
