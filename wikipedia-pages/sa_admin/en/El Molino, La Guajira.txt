'''El Molino''' ({{langx|en|The [[Mill (grinding)|Mill]]}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]].

The town is known for having the colonial church [[Ermita de San Lucas]], considered a National Monument of Colombia.<ref name="es.catholic.net">[http://es.catholic.net/turismoreligioso/762/2013/articulo.php?id=23181 {{in lang|es}} Catholic.net: Ermita de San Lucas en El Molino-Guajira (Colombia)]</ref> The town also celebrates the ''[[Festival del Cantante]]'' (Spanish for Festival of the Singer). It became a municipality in 1989.<ref>[http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/elmolino.htm {{in lang|es}} Government of La Guajira: El Molino] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928001715/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/elmolino.htm |date=2007-09-28 }}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality is within the Valley formed by the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] and [[Serranía del Perijá]] mountains and the [[Cesar River]]. The municipality is located on the mountain steps of the Serranía del Perijá covering a total area of 190&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and at 240 meters over sea level.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co">[http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/elmolino.htm {{in lang|es}} Government of La Guajira: Geography of El Molino] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928001715/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/elmolino.htm |date=2007-09-28 }}</ref>

The municipality of El Molino is located in the southern part of the Department of La Guajira limiting to the west and north with the municipality of [[San Juan del Cesar]]; to the south with the municipality of [[Villanueva, La Guajira|Villanueva]] and to east with the [[Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela]].<ref name="elmolino-laguajira.gov.co">{{Cite web |url=http://elmolino-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |language=es |title=El Molino: Geography |access-date=2008-03-25 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120709045413/http://elmolino-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |archive-date=2012-07-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The [[El Molino River]] is the main source of water for the municipality, flowing down from the Serrania del Perija mountain range and into the [[Cesar River]].<ref name="elmolino-laguajira.gov.co"/>

===Climate===

The average temperature throughout the year is 28&nbsp;°C with two rainy season and two dry seasons.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co"/> Climate varies depending on altitude from steppe to mountainous, ranging between 16&nbsp;°C and 34&nbsp;°C presenting warm, temperate and cold weather.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://elmolino-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |language=es |title=El Molino: Climate |access-date=2008-03-25 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120709045413/http://elmolino-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m |archive-date=2012-07-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==

The village of El Molino was founded in 1595 as Villa del Rosario by the Spanish colonizer [[Pedro Beltrán Valdés]] and became part of the Government of Santa Marta. The name was changed later to "''Población de San Lucas de El Molino''" and Later to simply El Molino.

The first inhabitants in the region, prior to the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas]] were indigenous peoples pertaining to the [[Cariachiles]] ethnic group. Capichin friars named the village "Población de San Lucas de El Molino" because of a [[Wheat]] [[Mill (grinding)]] which was later demolished. Besides Spanish families, El Molino also received colonizers from [[Portugal]], [[France]] and [[Germany]] to a lesser scale.<ref name="es.catholic.net"/>

El Molino became a corregimiento of the municipality of [[Villanueva, La Guajira|Villanueva]] and subsequently became a municipality in 1989. El Molino was officially formed by Ordinance 039 of 2001 segregated from the municipality of [[San Juan del Cesar]] after a popular referendum.
