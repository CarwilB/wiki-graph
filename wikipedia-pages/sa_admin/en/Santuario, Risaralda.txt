'''Santuario''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. About 64 km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]]. In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 12,826.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Santuario, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/santuario |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
Founded in 1906, Santuario was born under the administrative tutelage of [[Anserma, Caldas|Anserma]] and towards part of the state of Cauca. In 1982 it became dependent on the newly created municipality of [[Apía, Risaralda|San Antonio de Apía]], a year later the first school was created and in 1894 it ceased to be a hamlet and was declared a township, becoming a parish in 1906. In 1907, it was erected as a Municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Santuario |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100244/santuario/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>

== Climate ==
Santuario has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average annual temperature of 20°C.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Santuario – Risaralda un destino lleno de vida |url=https://risaraldaundestinollenodevida.com/new/santuario/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |language=es-CO}}</ref>
