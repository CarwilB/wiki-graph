'''Dionísio''' is a municipality in the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] [[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Itabira]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 7,609.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/dionisio/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|35970|ha}} [[Rio Doce State Park]], created in 1944, the first state-level conservation unit in Minas Gerais.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual do Rio Doce – IEF}}|title=Parque Estadual do Rio Doce|publisher=IEF: Instituto Estadual de Florestas MG|url=http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/component/content/195?task=view|access-date=2017-02-06|archive-date=2022-05-27|archive-url=https://archive.today/20220527201233/http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/component/content/195?task=view|url-status=dead}}</ref>
