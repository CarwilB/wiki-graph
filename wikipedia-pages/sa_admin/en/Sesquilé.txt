'''Sesquilé''' is a town and municipality in [[Almeidas Province]] in the [[Cundinamarca Department|department of Cundinamarca]], [[Colombia]]. Sesquilé in the [[Chibcha language]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] means "hot water".<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.sesquile-cundinamarca.gov.co/ Official website Sesquilé] - accessed 06-05-2016</ref>

Sesquilé is adjacent to [[Tominé Reservoir]] and nearby [[Lake Guatavita]], the suspected site of the ''[[El Dorado]]'' legend.
