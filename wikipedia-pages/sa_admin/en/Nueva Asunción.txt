'''Nueva Asunción''' (''New Asunción'') is a Paraguayan district located in the [[Presidente Hayes Department]], on the western bank of the [[Paraguay River]], about 5 km from [[Asunción]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9567/ley-n-6731-crea-el-municipio-de-nueva-asuncion-en-el-xv-departamento-de-presidente-hayes-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-la-localidad-de-chaco-i|title=Ley Nº 6731 / CREA EL MUNICIPIO DE NUEVA ASUNCIÓN EN EL XV DEPARTAMENTO DE PRESIDENTE HAYES Y UNA MUNICIPALIDAD CON ASIENTO EN LA LOCALIDAD DE CHACO-Í.|website=Biblioteca y Archivo del Congreso Nacional|date=4 May 2023|language=es}}</ref>

Previously the area was known as '''Chaco'i''', which means «little chaco» in [[Guaraní language|Guaraní]]. In 2021, it was officially given the name ''Nueva Asunción'', referring to the fact that it is the «new city» that represents the growth and expansion of [[Greater Asunción]] on the other side of the Paraguay River on the [[Chaco (Paraguay)|western region]], a previously depopulated and underdeveloped area.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://infonegocios.com.py/plus/crecer-al-otro-lado-del-rio-como-y-cuanto-costaria-convertir-a-chaco-i-en-la-nueva-asuncion|title=Crecer al otro lado del río: ¿Cómo y cuánto costaría convertir a Chaco’i en la Nueva Asunción?|website=Infonegocios PY|date=4 May 2023|language=es}}</ref>
