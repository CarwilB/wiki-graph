'''Bijagua''' is the fourth [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Upala (canton)|Upala]] canton, in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Bijagua has an area of {{formatnum:186.39}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:430}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It is a small town approximately 26&nbsp;km south of [[Upala]] in Northern plains of [[Costa Rica]].

The town is situated in a valley between the [[Miravalles Volcano|Miravalles]] and [[Tenorio Volcano|Tenorio]] volcanoes.

== Locations ==
* Towns: Altamira, Carlos Vargas.   
* Hamlets: Ángeles, Achiote, Cuesta Pichardo, Chorros, Florecitas, Flores, Higuerón, Jardín, Macho, Pata de Gallo (San Cristóbal) (part), Pueblo Nuevo, Reserva, San Miguel, Santo Domingo, Zapote.<ref name="dta2017" />

== Economy ==
The local economy is primarily farming, including vegetable production and cattle raising. Tourism exists due in part to the [[Old growth forest|primary growth rainforest]] located in the area.<ref>[http://www.heliconiaslodge.com Eco-tourism website]</ref>

Bijagua is approximately 16&nbsp;km from [[Tenorio Volcano National Park]]<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.acarenaltempisque.org/tenorio.htm |title=Tenorio Volcano National Park at acarenaltempisque.org (Spanish site) |access-date=2008-10-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081225143629/http://www.acarenaltempisque.org/tenorio.htm |archive-date=2008-12-25 |url-status=usurped }}</ref> and the [[Celeste River]].
