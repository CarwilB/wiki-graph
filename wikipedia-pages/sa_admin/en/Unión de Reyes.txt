'''Unión de Reyes''' is a municipality and town in the [[Matanzas Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in the western part of the province, {{convert|30|km|mi}} south of [[Matanzas, Cuba|Matanzas]], the provincial capital.

==History==
Unión de Reyes was founded in 1844,<ref name=guije/> and it was established as a municipality on July 1, 1879. Its name derives from ''Unión en el Punto de Reyes'', so named for its location at a railway junction.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Municipios/MunicipioUnion.htm |title=Unión de Reyes |author=Municipalities in Matanzas |access-date=2007-10-02 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060310041001/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Municipios/MunicipioUnion.htm |archive-date=March 10, 2006 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality, originally divided into the [[barrio]]s of Iglesia and Unión,<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/munion/index.htm|title=Unión de Reyes|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref> is divided into 9 ''consejos populares'' (i.e. "people's councils"): the town of Unión de Reyes and the villages of Alacranes, Bermejas, [[Cabezas (Unión de Reyes)|Cabezas]], Cidra, Estante, Juan Ávila, Juan G. Gómez and Puerto Rico.{{citation needed|date=August 2022}}

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Unión de Reyes had a population of 35,021.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|856|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|41|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
