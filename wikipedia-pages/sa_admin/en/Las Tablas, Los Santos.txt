'''Las Tablas''' ({{IPA|es|las ˈta.βlas}}) is the capital of the [[Panama]]nian province of [[Los Santos Province|Los Santos]], with a population of 9,255 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is located a few kilometres inland from the [[Gulf of Panama]] on the [[Azuero Peninsula]]. Las Tablas is a recognised national centre of Panamanian folk: [[Folk art|Art]], [[Folk music|music]], [[gastronomy]], [[architecture]], [[culture]] and [[Folklore|literature]]. [[Belisario Porras]], the only [[President of Panama|Panamanian president]] to serve three terms, was from Las Tablas.
[[File:Parque Belisario Porras Barahona, Ciudad de Las Tablas, Provincia de Los Santos (2025).jpg|thumb|left|[[Belisario Porras Barahona]] Park, popularly known as Porras Park]]

It is known for a lively yearly [[Carnival]], in which the city splits into two competing factions, "Calle Arriba" (Uptown, literally "Street Above") and "Calle Abajo" (Downtown / Street Below), both centred on two streets of the same name. Each faction will have a carnival queen, a parade, fireworks, music, a decorated plaza, food stands, presentations, concerts, surveys, games, contests, etc., all attempting to overpower the other faction's efforts.

==Etymology==
[[File:Iglesia de la Patrona Santa Librada.jpg|thumb|Santa Librada church]]

According to folklore, the town takes its name, which translates as "The Boards", from planks of wood salvaged from a Spanish ship used to construct the first houses in the town.   The ship had run aground on the coast near the city's future location after fleeing Panama City before the arrival of [[Henry Morgan]].
