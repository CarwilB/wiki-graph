'''Alcobaça''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of [[Bahia]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the [[Cassurubá Extractive Reserve]], a {{convert|100768|ha|adj=on}} sustainable use conservation unit that protects an area of mangroves, river and sea where shellfish are harvested.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Cassurubá – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX do Cassurubá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Ambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/584708|access-date=2016-06-22}}</ref>
The Timbebas reef opposite Alcobaça is part of the {{convert|91330|ha|adj=on}} [[Abrolhos Marine National Park]], a conservation unit created in 1983.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Nacional Marinho dos Abrolhos – ICMBio}}|language=pt|title=Parque Nacional Marinho dos Abrolhos|publisher=[[ICMBio]]|url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/o-que-fazemos/visitacao/unidades-abertas-a-visitacao/193-parque-nacional-marinho-dos-abrolhos.html|access-date=2016-04-30|archive-date=2020-09-30|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200930064431/https://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/o-que-fazemos/visitacao/unidades-abertas-a-visitacao/193-parque-nacional-marinho-dos-abrolhos.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
