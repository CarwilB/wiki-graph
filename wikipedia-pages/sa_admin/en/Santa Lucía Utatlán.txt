'''Santa Lucía Utatlán''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsanta luˈsi.awtaˈtlan}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Sololá Department|Sololá department]] of [[Guatemala]].

==Climate==
Santa Lucia Utatlan has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cwb) dry, cold winters and rainy, cool summers.
