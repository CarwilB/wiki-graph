It is located at 26º42'52" from latitude South and 49º29'00" from longitude west, at an altitude of 530 meters. It has a population of 4,115, according to a 2020 [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] estimate. It has an area of 375.758&nbsp;km².
