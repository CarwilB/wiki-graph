The '''Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 16,144.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[El Cantón]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality (disambiguation)|Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan poet [[Andrés Eloy Blanco]].

In 2008, it established a sister-relationship with Dane County, Wisconsin -- http://www.dane-andres.org/

==Demographics==
The Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality, according to a 2017 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 18,816 (up from 15,847 in 2000).  This amounts to 2.5% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls Population data] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|12.6|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls Population data] ine.gob.ve {{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Andrés Eloy Blanco Municipality is Zulay Martínez Fuentes, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 69% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=12&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio|website=cne.gob.ve |language=es|access-date=9 September 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; El Cantón, Santa Cruz de Guacas, and Puerto Vivas.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
