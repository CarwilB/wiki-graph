'''Belmonte''' is a municipality ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]])'' in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Bahia]]. It is located at 15º51'47" S, 38º52'58" W. It is 8 metres above sea level. In 2020 it had an estimated population of 23,437 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/belmonte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality covers a total area of 2016.85&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and was founded in 1764.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|100646|ha|adj=on}} [[Canavieiras Extractive Reserve]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX de Canavieiras – ISA}}|language=pt
