'''San Felipe''' is a town, with a population of 17,360 (2018 census),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ Citypopulaton.de] Population of cities & towns in Guatemala</ref> and a municipality in [[Retalhuleu Department]], situated on the road to [[Quetzaltenango]] between [[El Palmar, Quetzaltenango]] to the north-west and [[San Martin Zapotitlan]] to the south side.

==Geography==
Geographically, the municipality of San Felipe, Retalhuleu comprises the northern tip of the department of [[Retalhuleu]] (borders to the west with department of [[Quetzaltenango]] and to the east with the [[Suchitepéquez Department]]).  Of the population, many people live in the rural areas, namely [[Aldea Vela]], [[Canton Tierra Colorada]] and  [[Aldea El Palmarcito]] among others.
