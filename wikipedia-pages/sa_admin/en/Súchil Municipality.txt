'''Súchil''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Súchil]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|822.9|km2|0|abbr=on}}.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 6,761,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Súchil|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=033|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> down from 6,928 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Súchil|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10033a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=November 18, 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070329021614/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10033a.htm|archive-date=March 29, 2007|df=}}</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Súchil had a population of 4,107.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Súchil, the municipality had 62 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
