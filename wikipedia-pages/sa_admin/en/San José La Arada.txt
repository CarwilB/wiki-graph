'''San José La Arada''' ({{IPA|es|saŋ xoˈse la aɾaða}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It has a population of 8,756 (2018 census)<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/ Citypopulation.de] Population of departments and municipalities in Guatemala</ref> and covers an area of 121 km<sup>2</sup>.
