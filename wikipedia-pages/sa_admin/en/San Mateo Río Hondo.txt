'''San Mateo Río Hondo''' is the name of a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the state of [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]].
It is part of the [[Miahuatlán District, Oaxaca|Miahuatlán District]] in the south of the [[Sierra Sur de Oaxaca|Sierra Sur Region]]. It is 2,300 meters above sea level.  Known in the [[Colonialism|colonial era]] as "''Tetequipa''" in [[Nahuatl]] and "''Yegoyoxi''" in [[Zapotec language|Zapotec]], both names meaning "river of sand." As municipal seat San Mateo Río Hondo has governing jurisdiction over the following communities: Barranca Grande, El Campanario, El Cuachepil, El Encino, El Esfuerzo, El Manzanal, El Naranjal, El Progreso, El Tavel, Falda de Portillo, Horno de Cal, Jalatengo, La Concepción, La Doncella, La Floreña, La Victoria, Las Nubes, Las Tinas, Loma San Marcial, Miramar, Piedra Gentil, Pinabete (Llano de Pinabete), Ranchería Yogoló (Piedra Manchada), Rancho Cañas, Rancho Cerezales, Rancho Madroño (Rancho el Capulín), Rancho Nuevo, Río Cuapinol, Río Grande, Río Molino, Río Pacífico, San Antonio, San Felipe (Manzanillo), San Ildefonso Ozolotepec, San José del Pacífico, San Melchor, San Pablo, Tres Cruces, Yogoló, and Zapotitlán. San Mateo Rio Hondo is divided into three areas: Nochixtlán district, Mixtecan region and San Mateo Sindihui. The municipality has three hills: La Postura, Zaniltepec and Cerro León.
