'''Cerro Corá''' is a city and district and located in the [[Amambay Department]] of [[Paraguay]] on the [[Amambai Mountains]]. It was created by Law 6555, detaching itself from the municipality of [[Pedro Juan Caballero, Paraguay|Pedro Juan Caballero]], which is {{convert|20|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the center of the departmental capital.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2020/05/28/senadores-aprobaron-la-creacion-del-municipio-de-cerro-cora-en-amambay/|title=Senadores aprobaron la creación de municipio de Cerro Corá en Amambay|accessdate=3 August 2020|website=ABC Color|language=es}}</ref> Its urban center has the name of '''Colonia Capitán Raúl Ocampos Rojas''', also known as '''[[Chiriguelo]]'''. It has an area of 1451&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/9276/ley-n-6555-crea-el-municipio-de-cerro-cora-en-el-xiii-departamento-amambay-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-la-colonia-capitan-raul-ocampos-rojas-chiriguelo|title=Ley N° 6555 Crea el municipio de Cerro Corá en el XIII departamento Amambay y una municipalidad con asiento en la colonia Capitán Raúl Ocampos Rojas|accessdate=4 August 2020|website=Biblioteca y Archivo del Banco del Congreso de la Nación|language=es}}</ref>
