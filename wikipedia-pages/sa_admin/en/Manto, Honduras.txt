'''Manto''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Manto municipality had a population of 9,625. Of these, 95.62% were [[Mestizo]], 3.29% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.57% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.51% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
