'''Boca da Mata''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbokɐ ˈdɐ ˈmata]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 27,356 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/boca-da-mata/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 187&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=11 June 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=11 June 2008 }}</ref>
