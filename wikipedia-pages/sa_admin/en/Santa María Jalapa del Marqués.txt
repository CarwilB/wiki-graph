'''Santa María Jalapa del Marqués''' is a small city and a [[municipio (Mexico)|municipiality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Oaxaca]]. They are both part of the [[Tehuantepec District, Oaxaca|Tehuantepec District]] in the west of the [[Istmo de Tehuantepec, Oaxaca|Istmo Region]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Rosete |first1=Erika |title=Santa María Jalapa del Marqués, el pueblo con un embalse que se seca y revela un convento del Siglo XVI |url=https://elpais.com/mexico/2024-02-17/santa-maria-jalapa-del-marques-el-pueblo-con-un-embalse-que-se-seca-y-revela-un-convento-del-siglo-xvi.html |website=El País México |access-date=29 June 2024 |language=es-MX |date=17 February 2024}}</ref>

==The city==
Jalapa del Marqués stands on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highway 190]], on the southern shore of the [[Presa Juárez]] reservoir. The city serves as the municipal seat and administrative centre for the surrounding municipality of the same name.

==The municipality==
In 2020, the population was 11,735 (48.2% men and 51.8% women).<ref name='datamex'>{{cite web |title=Santa María Jalapa del Marqués: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/santa-maria-jalapa-del-marques#population-and-housing |website=Data México |publisher=[[Government of Mexico]] |access-date=29 June 2024 |language=en}}</ref>
Santa María Jalapa del Marqués municipality governs the following communities:

* Cerro del Chivo, Cerro del Marqués
* Colonia Presidente Juárez

* El Tamarindo
* Granja del Ángel
* Guadalupe Victoria

* Llano Grande
* Llano Vería
* Loma Bonita
* Magdalena Guelavence
* Peña San Juan
* Pochotillo
* San Cristóbal
* Santa Elena de la Cruz
* Solo Dios
* Vishiñadu (El Palenque)
