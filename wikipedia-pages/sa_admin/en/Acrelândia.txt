'''Acrelândia''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|akɾeˈlɐ̃dʒi.ɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the easternmost portion of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.google.com/maps/place//@-9.827778,-66.882778,14z/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0x0!5m1!1e4?hl=en | title=Google Maps }}</ref> Its population is 15,490 and its area is 1,808&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the smallest municipality in that state.
