'''Córrego do Ouro''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in eastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location and Highway Connections==
Córrego do Ouro is located in the [[Iporá Microregion]] west of the state capital, [[Goiânia]].  It is 45&nbsp;km. northwest of [[São Luís de Montes Belos]], the largest town in the region.  Goiânia lies at a distance of 171&nbsp;km.  Highway connections from [[Goiânia]] were made by  GO-060 / [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]] / [[Claudinápolis]] (district) / GO-326 / [[Anicuns]] / GO-222 / GO-326 / [[Sanclerlândia]] / [[Buriti de Goiás]] / GO-518. [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Seplan].  Neighboring municipalities:  [[Moiporá]], [[Israelândia]], [[Fazenda Nova]], [[Novo Brasil]], [[Buriti de Goiás]], and [[Adelândia]]

==The economy==
The main economic activities were cattle raising (64,200 in 2006) and agriculture.  Main crops were rice, bananas, and corn.   There was one dairy:  Laticínios Farlat - Indústria e Comércio Ltda (2007)
*GDP (R$1,000.00) in 2005:  18,252
*Per capita GDP (R$1.00) in 2005:  6,577

== History ==
The history of Córrego do Ouro began in 1934 with the dynamism of Padre Alexandre Pereira.   A bridge was built across the Rio Fartura and the population began to grow.  In 1948 it was raised to the status of district belonging to Goiás to be granted autonomy in 1953.
