'''Tepetlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the montane central zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It has a surface of 83.90 km<sup>2</sup>. During the [[Pre-Columbian era|Pre-Hispanic]] epoch it was inhabited by the Totonac, the most ancient date in the one that one alludes as municipality in that of the 1 °. Of December 1868 in the Cologne it named the village San Antonio Tepetlán, for decree of November 5, 1932, the head-board is called Tepetlán, remaining suppressed the first part of the name.

==Geography==

The municipality of ''' Tepetlán ''' is delimited by [[Acatlán, Veracruz|Acatlán]], [[Chiconquiaco]], [[Alto Lucero de Gutiérrez Barrios]] and [[Naolinco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30166a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tepetlán |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> The territory of the municipality is watered by small tributary rivers of the river Actópan.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[green chile]], [[sugarcane]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

In '''Tepetlán''', in June takes place the celebration in honor to San Antonio, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.
