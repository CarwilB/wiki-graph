'''Cubarral''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]]. The municipality of [[Guamal]] borders Cubarral to the north, to the west is bordered by the department of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], to the east by the municipality of [[San Martín, Meta|San Martín]] and to the south by the municipality of [[El Dorado, Meta|El Dorado]]. Furthermore, According to [[National Administrative Department of Statistics (Colombia)|DANE]] projections from the 2005 census, Cubarral would have a population of approximately 5946 people by 2015. 

== Hydrography ==
There are a lot of rivers that cross the municipality of Cubarral, and according to National Environmental Licensing Authority (ANLA) in Spanish, some of the main watercourses are:

* Ariari river
* Humadea river
* Tonoa river

==Climate==
Cubarral has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
