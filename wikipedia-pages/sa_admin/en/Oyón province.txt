'''Oyón''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Department of Lima|department of Lima]], [[Peru]]. From the administrative point of view of the [[Catholic Church in Peru]], it forms part of the [[Diocese of Huacho]]. It measures {{convert|1886|km2}}. The capital of the province is the city of [[Oyón, Peru|Oyón]].
