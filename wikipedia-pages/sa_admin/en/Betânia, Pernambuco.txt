'''Betânia''' (''Bethany'') is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], was 11,961 and <!--, the density is /km²-->the area is 1256,138&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

The municipality contains the {{convert|1485|ha}} [[Maurício Dantas Private Natural Heritage Ecological Reserve]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RPPN Reserva Ecológica Maurício Dantas – ICMBio}}|language=pt
 |title=RPPN Reserva Ecológica Maurício Dantas|publisher=ICMBio: [[Chico Mendes Institute for Biodiversity Conservation]]
 |url=http://sistemas.icmbio.gov.br/simrppn/publico/detalhe/275/|access-date=2016-11-02}}</ref> The municipality was designated a priority area for conservation and sustainable use when the 5,900,000-hectare  [[Caatinga Ecological Corridor]] was created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|language=pt
 |last=Mattos|first=Marluza|title=Ministério cria Corredor Ecológico da Caatinga|year=2006|publisher=Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://www.mma.gov.br/informma/item/3274-ministerio-cria-corredor-ecologico-da-caatinga|access-date=2016-11-01}}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Betânia''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]];  and plantations of [[Maize|corn]], [[tomato]]es and [[bean]]s.
