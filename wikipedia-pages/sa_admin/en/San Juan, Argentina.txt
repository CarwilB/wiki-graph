'''San Juan''' ({{IPA|es|saŋ ˈxwan}}) is the capital and largest city of the [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[Provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]] in the [[Cuyo (Argentina)|Cuyo]] region, located in the Tulúm Valley, west of the [[San Juan River (Argentina)|San Juan River]], at {{convert|650|m|ft|0|abbr=on}}  [[above mean sea level]], with a population of around 112,000 as per the {{census-ar|2001}} (over 500,000 in the [[metropolitan area]]).

In agriculture, [[wine]] is economically important.

The [[1944 San Juan earthquake]] on January 15 killed around 10,000 people and left half of the provincial population homeless.{{cn|date=June 2026}}

==Geography==
The city of San Juan is located in a fertile valley within a rocky mountainous area. Winter temperatures are generally mild, averaging between {{convert|1|C|F|1}} and {{convert|16|C|F|1}}, but can drop below {{convert|-9|C|F|1}}. Summers are hot, with average temperatures between {{convert|19|C|F|1}} and {{convert|35|C|F|1}}, and a record maximum of {{convert|46.7|C|F|1}} on December 20, 1995.<ref name = ORA>

Since very little rain falls in the region, the San Juan River has been dammed to provide a regular source of water to the city. The resulting [[reservoir (water)|reservoir]] is located in [[Ullum]], and is known as the [[Quebrada de Ullum Dam]]. The dam also provides electrical power to the region.

Sixty-five percent of the surrounding area's agricultural production is related to [[Argentine wine|wine production]].

== Notable sites ==

The  [[San Juan de Cuyo Cathedral]] was completed in 1979.

== Wine ==
[[File:Vendimia en San Juan año 1890.jpg|left|thumb|alt=Harvest in the San Juan Region 1890|Harvest in the San Juan Region 1890]]
San Juan is ranked second among the wine-producing provinces of Argentina. It has a cultivated area of 116,700 acres at altitudes of between 1,970 (601m) and 4,590 feet (1399m) above sea level.  This region specializes in [[Syrah]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Douce noir|Bonarda]], [[Chardonnay]] and [[Torrontés]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.winesofargentina.org/argentina/regiones/cuyo/sanjuan/|title=Argentina - Wines Of Argentina|website=www.winesofargentina.org|access-date=6 April 2018}}</ref>
Because of this area's [[Semi-desert|semi-desert climate]], [[viticulture]] is dependent on irrigation from the [[San Juan River (Argentina)|San Juan]] and [[Jáchal River]]s.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.wine-searcher.com/regions-san+juan|title=Wine-Searcher. Human race only please ...|website=www.wine-searcher.com|access-date=6 April 2018}}</ref>
