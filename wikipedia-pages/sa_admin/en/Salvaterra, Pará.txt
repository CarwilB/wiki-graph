'''Salvaterra''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 24,075 people. The area of the municipality is 1,043.504 km<sup>2</sup>. The city is located in the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and the microregion of ''Arari''.

==Conservation==

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.{{sfn|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}
The municipality operates the [[Mata do Bacurizal e do Lago Caraparu Ecological Reserve]], a forest area with a lake just south of the town of Salvaterra.{{sfn|Giovanni Salera Júnior|2012|p=15}}
