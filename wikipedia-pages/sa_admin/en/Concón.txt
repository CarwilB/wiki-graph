'''Concón''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Valparaíso Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]]. It is a major tourist center known for its beaches, ''[[balneario]]s'' (beachside resorts) and night life.

== Geography ==
The commune of Concón spans an area of {{convert|76.0|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> It is located on the Pacific coast north of [[Reñaca beach|Reñaca]], [[Viña del Mar]] and south of [[Quintero]]. The Aconcagua river ends at the north of the town.

Its three main beaches are Playa Negra, Amarilla and Boca. 50 hectares of dunes stretch along and above the coast, though only about 20 are protected, the rest being increasingly encroached by highrise apartment buildings.

== Demographics ==
According to data from the 2017 Census of Population and Housing, the commune of Concón had 42,152 inhabitants; of these, 39,409 (93.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,743 (6.5%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 20,321 men and 21,831 women.<ref name="INE"/> The population grew by 30.61% (9,879 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 censuses. The 2024 projected population was 48,171.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/reportescomunales/comunas_v.html?anno=2024&idcom=5103|title=Reporte Comunal 2024: Concón|work=[[Library of the National Congress of Chile]]|access-date=18 October 2024|language=es}}</ref>

The [[demonym]] for a person from Concón is ''Conconino'' for a man, or ''Conconina'' for a woman.<ref name="INE"/>

== History ==
This location was first mentioned by [[Pedro de Valdivia]] in 1541. It was created as a municipality in 1899 but became part of Viña del Mar in 1927. A 1995 law again turned it into its own municipality.

== Administration ==
As a commune, Concón administered by a [[municipal council]], which is headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. The mayor since 2021 has been Freddy Ramírez Villalobos (Ind). The municipal council for the 2024–2028 term has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Ricardo Urenda Herencia ([[Independent politician|Ind.]]/[[Evópoli]])
* Paulina Zúñiga Guevara ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* María José Aguirre Neuenschwander ([[Independent politician|Ind.]]/[[Evópoli]])
* Jorge Valdovinos Gómez ([[Independent politician|Ind.]]/[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Alberto Fernández López ([[Radical Party of Chile|PR]])
* Elda Arteaga Breiding ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Concón is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Edmundo Eluchans ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Rodrigo González as part of the 14th electoral district, together with Viña del Mar. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Francisco Chahuán Chahuán ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and [[Ricardo Lagos|Ricardo Lagos Weber]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 6th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Coast).
