'''Canela''' is one of four [[Communes of Chile|communes]] in the [[Choapa Province]] of [[Chile]]'s north-central [[Coquimbo Region|IV Coquimbo Region]]. The [[capital (political)|capital]] is the town of [[Canela Baja]].

==Administration==
[[File:Municipalidad canela.jpg|thumb|left|175px|Municipal building at Luis Infante Nº 520 in Canela Baja.]]
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Canela is administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. The municipal building (''alcaldía'') is located at Luis Infante Nº 520 in Canela Baja.<ref name="SUBDERE"/> The commune is administered by alcalde Juan Bernardo Leyton Lemus (Independent-PC) from 2012 to 2016, who won the election with 50,21% of the vote against 41,15% of his nearest rival, former mayor [[Norman Araya Araya]] (Independent). The commune has a total of six councilors:<ref name="Official"/>
# Manuel Ángel Navarro Vega (PC)
# Ana Margarita Vega Flores ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
# Miguel Pereira Bugueño ([[RN, Chile|RN]])
# Darwin Cortés Palma ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
# Néstor Valle Tapia (PC-[[Independent (politician)|Ind.]])
# Gustavo Gallardo Trigo ([[Christian Democrat Party|PDC]])

Canela belongs to the 9th [[Electoral division of Chile|electoral district]] and the 4th Senate District (for that of the Coquimbo Region). It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] of the [[National Congress of Chile|National Congress]] by the Members [[Jorge Insunza Gregorio de las Heras]] (PPD) and [[Luis Lemus]] (PS). Currently, Coquimbo is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Jorge Pizarro]] (PDC) and [[Adriana Muñoz (politician)|Adriana Muñoz]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]).

==Geography==
Canela spans an area of {{convert|2196.6|km|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> The commune is bordered to the west by the [[Pacific Ocean]] and to the north by three communes of the [[Limarí Province]]: [[Ovalle]], [[Punitaqui]], and [[Combarbalá]] to the north and east. To the southeast lies the commune of [[Illapel]], and [[Los Vilos]] borders on the south. Canela Baja is located approximately {{convert|12|km|0|abbr=on}} east of the [[Panamerican Highway]], [[Chile Route 5|Route 5]], at kilometer 299.

==Climate==
The waters of the Canela stream flow into the [[Choapa River]], which drains into the Pacific Ocean. The area's climate is steppe or semiarid. The average annual precipitation is about {{convert|169.51|mm|0|abbr=on}}.

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Canela commune had 9,379 inhabitants; of these, 1,744 (18.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,635 (81.4%) in [[rural area]]s. Thus, the Canela population represents 1.5% of regional population and 11.4% of the provincial population.
