'''Marcavelica District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Sullana Province|Sullana]] in [[Peru]].<ref>[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. (in Spanish) ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital]'',  Retrieved April 17, 2008. {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080417091049/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=17 April 2008 }}</ref>  It was created by law on 25 March 1952 by the government of President [[Manuel A. Odría]].<ref>Ley 11794 {{Cite web|title=20.2 Departmento Piura: Dispositivo legal de creación según provincia y distrito |language=es |year=2015 |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática |url=http://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1201/cuadros/piura/piura_20_2.xls |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924034925/http://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1201/cuadros/piura/piura_20_2.xls |archivedate=24 September 2015 |url-status=live }}</ref>

The headquarters of one of the two dedicated hunting areas in Peru is located in Marcavelica District.<ref name="Angolo">{{Cite web|title=Atractivos y Potencialidades |language=es |url=http://munimarcavelica.gob.pe/atractivos-y-potencialidades/ |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150413212514/http://munimarcavelica.gob.pe/atractivos-y-potencialidades/ |archivedate=13 April 2015|url-status=live}}</ref> Known as "[[El Angolo Hunting enclosed land|El Coto de Caza el Angolo]]", it was formerly, in the 1970s, the estates and cattle ranch of Calixto Romero. Today it is part of the Northwest Peru Biosphere Reserve.<ref name="Barrio">{{Cite magazine|author=Barrio, Javier|year=2004|title=El Angolo refugio de fauna silvestre ("El Angolo, refuge for wildlife") |magazine=Viajeros |volume=7|pages=34–42 |url=https://www.academia.edu/2403382/El_Angolo_refugio_de_fauna_silvestre}}</ref> Consisting of some {{Convert|650|sqkm|sqmi|0}},<ref>{{Cite news|title=El Coto de Caza El Angolo: de visita obligada |year=2007 |newspaper=CrónicaViva |publisher=Universidad Jaime Bausate y Meza |url=http://www.cronicaviva.com.pe/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=20081&Itemid=98 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070502091809/http://www.cronicaviva.com.pe/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=20081&Itemid=98 |archivedate=2 May 2007 |url-status=dead}}</ref> the area is quite mountainous with deep ravines. The area is covered in dry forests, as the area has a low rainfall, but it is subject to heavy mists. Among the animals in the hunting reserve are whitetail deer, red deer, puma, peccary, [[sechuran fox]], [[pampas cat]], and anteaters. Birds native to the area include the condor, king vulture, hawks, canary partridges, pigeons of various kinds, and numerous song birds.<ref name="Angolo" /><ref name="Barrio" /><ref>{{Cite web|author=Saldarriaga, Correa Maria Antoinette |title=Conociendo El Área Natural Protegida De La Región Piura Coto De Caza El Angolo |date=29 August 2012 |publisher=PerúEduca: Proyecto Productos |url=http://www.perueduca.pe/web/proyecto/productos?p_p_id=33&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_state=normal&p_p_mode=view&p_p_col_id=column-1&p_p_col_pos=1&p_p_col_count=2&p_r_p_564233524_tag=%C3%A1rea+natural+protegida |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150413222427/http://www.perueduca.pe/web/proyecto/productos?p_p_id=33&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_state=normal&p_p_mode=view&p_p_col_id=column-1&p_p_col_pos=1&p_p_col_count=2&p_r_p_564233524_tag=%C3%A1rea%20natural%20protegida |archivedate=13 April 2015 |url-status=live }}</ref>
