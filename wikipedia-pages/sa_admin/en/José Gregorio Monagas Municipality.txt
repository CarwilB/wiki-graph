The '''José Gregorio Monagas Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 17,534.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive – Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=15 March 2016 }}</ref> The town of [[Mapire, Anzoátegui|Mapire]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the José Gregorio Monagas Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is named for the nineteenth century Venezuelan President [[José Gregorio Monagas]].

== Demographics ==

The José Gregorio Monagas Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 18,154 (up from 16,533 in 2000). This amounts to 1.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|1.98|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Government ==

The mayor of the José Gregorio Monagas Municipality is Cruz Ojeda, elected on 23 November 2008 with 54% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales – 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> He replaced Rafael Ángel Puerta Alvarez shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into six parishes; Capital José Gregorio Monagas, Piar, San Diego de Cabrutica, Santa Clara, Uverito, and Zuata.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
