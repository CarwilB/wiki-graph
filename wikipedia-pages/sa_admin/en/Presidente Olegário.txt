'''Presidente Olegário''' [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population in 2020 was 19,627 inhabitants in a total area of {{convert|3,531|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/presidente-olegario/panorama IBGE 2020] {{inlang|pt-br}}.</ref>

==Geography==
Presidente Olegário belongs to the [[Paracatu, Minas Gerais|Paracatu]] statistical microregion.  The elevation of the municipal seat is {{convert|947|m|ft|abbr=in}}.  It became a municipality in 1938.  This municipality is located {{convert|28|km|mi|abbr=in}} northeast of [[Patos de Minas]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/presidenteolegario/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20121231200429/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/index.php|date=31 December 2012}} {{inlang|pt-br}}.</ref>  The distance to the capital, [[Belo Horizonte]] is {{convert|433|km|mi|abbr=in}}.

Neighboring municipalities are: [[Lagamar]], [[Lagoa Grande, Minas Gerais|Lagoa Grande]], [[Patos de Minas]], [[Varjão de Minas]], [[João Pinheiro, Minas Gerais|João Pinheiro]] and [[São Gonçalo do Abaeté]].

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising (118,000 head in 2006) and farming.  There was also production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations.   This charcoal is transported to the industrial region near [[Belo Horizonte]] to be used in the steel and iron industry.  The GDP was R$169,963,000 (2005).  There were two banking agencies in 2006.  In the rural area there were 1,790 farms with {{convert|260000|ha|acre}} hectares of agricultural land, {{convert|34000|ha|acre}} of which were planted, {{convert|163000|ha|acre|abbr=values}} were in natural pasture, and {{convert|48000|ha|acre|abbr=values}} were in woodland or forest.  Around 6,500 people were involved in the agricultural sector.  There were 439 tractors, a ratio of one tractor for every 40 farms.  The main crops were cotton, rice, sugarcane, beans, tomatoes, soybeans, and corn.  In the health sector there were 10 health clinics and one hospital with 20 beds.  In Patos de Minas, {{convert|28|km|mi|abbr=in}} away on paved roads there are complete health facilities.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php Statistics from IBGE] {{inlang|pt-br}}.</ref>

The score on the Municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.721.  This ranked Presidente Olegário 457 out of 853 municipalities in the state, with [[Poços de Caldas]] in first place with 0.841 and [[Setubinha]] in last place with 0.568.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150705072500/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm|date=5 July 2015}} {{inlang|pt-br}}.</ref>
