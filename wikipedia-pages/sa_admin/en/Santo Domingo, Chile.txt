'''Santo Domingo''' is a [[Chile]]an coastal [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[San Antonio Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]]. It was founded as '''Rocas de Santo Domingo''' (St. Dominic's Rocks).

== Demographics ==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Santo Domingo spans an area of {{convert|536.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 7,418 inhabitants (3,811 men and 3,607 women). Of these, 4,737 (63.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,681 (36.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 19.3% (1,200 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

== Administration ==
As a commune, Santo Domingo is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde was Fernando Rodríguez Larraín.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Santo Domingo is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by María José Hoffmann ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Víctor Torres ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 15th electoral district, (together with [[San Antonio, Chile|San Antonio]], [[Cartagena, Chile|Cartagena]], [[El Tabo]], [[El Quisco]], [[Algarrobo, Chile|Algarrobo]] and [[Casablanca, Chile|Casablanca]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Francisco Chahuán Chahuán ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and [[Ricardo Lagos Weber]] ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 6th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Coast).
