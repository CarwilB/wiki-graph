'''Diez de Octubre''' is one of the 15 municipalities or boroughs (''[[municipio]]s'' in Spanish) in the city of [[Havana]], [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
It is one of the oldest municipalities of the capital. Its foundation dates from the second half of the 16th Century aimed at populating the city when the [[Canary Islands|Canary Islanders]] emigrate to Cuba.{{citation needed|date=March 2013}}
