'''Brasópolis''' is a municipality in [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Previously called São Caetano da Vargem Grande, it was renamed after its most famous son, [[Venceslau Brás]], ninth President of the Republic, although he was born in neighbouring [[Itajubá]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|180373|ha}} [[Fernão Dias Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Fernão Dias – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=APA Fernão Dias|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/586570|access-date=2016-10-02}}</ref>
