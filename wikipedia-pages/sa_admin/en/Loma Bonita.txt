'''Loma Bonita, Oaxaca '''is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]]. It is part of the [[Tuxtepec District]] of the [[Papaloapan Region]]. As of 2020, the municipality had a population of 40,943.<ref>{{cite web|date=January 2, 2021|title=Loma Bonita|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/oaxaca/20044__loma_bonita/|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=May 24, 2021|work=City Population}}</ref>

==History==
Loma Bonita began as a neighborhood with American residents, who were attracted to the area's fertile land. In 1940, the pineapple industry arrived in  the area.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Loma Bonita, Oaxaca — The Capital of the Pineapple|url=http://tuxtepecturismo.com/en/what-to-do/other-cities-in-the-cuenca-del-papaloapan-region/loma-bonita/|access-date=2021-04-29|website=Tuxtepec Tourism|language=en-US}}</ref> The settlement began as several campsites, established by young Americans in the [[Oaxaca]]/[[Veracruz]] border area.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Ltd|first=rome2rio Pty|title=Discover how to get to Loma Bonita from anywhere.|url=https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Loma-Bonita|access-date=2021-04-14|website=Rome2rio|language=en}}</ref> The town's entrance features a bronze statue of "El Ponitero", a pineapple picker cutting and collecting this fruit in a basket on his back.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|title=Loma Bonita, Oaxaca — The Capital of the Pineapple|url=http://tuxtepecturismo.com/en/what-to-do/other-cities-in-the-cuenca-del-papaloapan-region/loma-bonita/|access-date=2021-04-29|website=Tuxtepec Tourism|language=en-US}}</ref> 

Loma Bonita began as a camp between two villages, Pedro Salomón and Provo Medina, and a railroad connecting [[Veracruz]] to [[Suchiate]]. In the 1940s, the pineapple industry became a major industry. 

== Culture ==
=== Festivals ===
One of the most important local events is the annual Feria de la Piña (Pineapple Festival).<ref name=":0" />

==Geography==
Loma Bonita is home to fertile lands.<ref name=":0" /> The two rivers that cross Loma Bonita are the [[Papaloapan River]] and the Obispo River. The municipality covers an area of 588.15 square kilometres and is located approximately 36 kilometres from the [[Tuxtepec District]] in [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]].

===Climate===
The weather in Loma Bonita is typically warm, with abundant rainfall. The municipality has both a wet and dry season and throughout the year temperatures will vary from 10°C to 31°C.<ref name=":5">{{Cite web|title=Average Weather in Loma Bonita|url=https://weatherspark.com/y/5533/Average-Weather-in-Loma-Bonita-Mexico-Year-Round.|access-date=2021-05-28|website=Weather Spark|language=en-US}}</ref>

== Economy ==
Loma Bonita became a productive place for trade. The Mexican Agricultural and Land Company bought some extensions of Loma Bonita as a business opportunity.

Another agricultural product is [[sugarcane]] as this municipality is located within the Oaxaca region, one of Mexico's top sugarcane-producing areas.<ref name=":4">{{Cite web|title=Mexico's Sugar Industry Struggles to Remain Competitive|url=https://gro-intelligence.com/insights/articles/mexico-sugar-industry-struggles-to-remain-competitive#:~:text=Mexico's%20top%20sugar%20producing%20area,of%2011%20million%20tonnes%20annually.|access-date=2021-05-28|website=Gro Intelligence|language=en-US}}</ref>
