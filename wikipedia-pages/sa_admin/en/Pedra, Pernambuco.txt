'''Pedra''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈpɛdɐ]}}) ('''stone''') is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located  at 255&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco, and has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 22,668 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/pedra/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Arcoverde]] and [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]]   ('''N''');  [[Águas Belas]]    ('''S''');  [[Caetés, Pernambuco|Caetés]], [[Paranatama]] and [[Venturosa]]   ('''E''');   [[Buique]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 803.02&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 593 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipanema River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] - hot
* Annual average temperature - 22.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 255.4&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Pedra''' are based in commerce and  [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[bean]]s, [[tomato]]es, [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[sheep]], [[goat]]s, [[horse]]s and [[chicken]]s.
