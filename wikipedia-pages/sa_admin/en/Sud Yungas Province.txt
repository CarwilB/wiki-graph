'''Sud Yungas''' or '''Sur Yungas''' ([[Aymara language|Aymara]]: ''Aynach Yunka jisk'a'') is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz]]. It was created during the presidency of [[José Manuel Pando]] on January 12, 1900.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.gobernacionlapaz.gob.bo/index.php/conoce/la-paz/estructura/estructura-fisica/290-provincia-sud-yungas.html |title=Official website |access-date=2012-04-14 |archive-date=2012-08-01 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120801011853/http://www.gobernacionlapaz.gob.bo/index.php/conoce/la-paz/estructura/estructura-fisica/290-provincia-sud-yungas.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> The capital of the province is [[Chulumani]].

== Geography ==
The [[Cordillera Real, Bolivia|Cordillera Real]] traverses the province. The highest peaks of the province is [[Illimani]] at {{convert|6438|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} above sea level.
