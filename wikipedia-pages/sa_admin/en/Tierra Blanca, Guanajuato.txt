'''Tierra Blanca''' is a city and municipality in northeastern [[Guanajuato]], Mexico. Covering an area of 391.65 square kilometers (1.09% of the state's total surface area), it shares borders with [[Santa Catarina, Guanajuato|Santa Catarina]] to the north, the state of [[Querétaro]] to the south and east, [[San José Iturbide]] to the west, and [[Doctor Mora]] to the northwest. According to the 2005 census, the municipality had a population of 14,515.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=2007-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archivedate=2013-04-06 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato | title=Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia | work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México | accessdate=2007-03-22}}</ref> In pre-Hispanic times, the area now known as Tierra Blanca was primarily inhabited by the [[Chichimeca]] people.

Tierra Blanca was established and designated as a municipality in 1536 by order of the first viceroy of New Spain, [[Antonio de Mendoza]], making it one of the oldest municipalities in Guanajuato.

The current municipal president of Tierra Blanca and its smaller outlying communities is Ernesto Reyes Pérez.<ref>[http://periodico.guanajuato.gob.mx/archivos/200701301607210.PO_16_3ra_Parte.pdf PO_14_2da_Parte<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722225130/http://periodico.guanajuato.gob.mx/archivos/200701301607210.PO_16_3ra_Parte.pdf |date=2011-07-22 }}</ref>
