'''Texistepec''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the south-east zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 285&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 615.26 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|17|54|N|94|49|W|}}. Texistepec in 1580 concerned to Coatzacoalcos's province.{{clarify|date=June 2015}} In 1831 San Miguel Texistepec was forming a municipality and was possessing 2 square leagues of lands with legal titles, southern people who suffered the batterings of the currents that our Mexican Republic had.

==Name==
The name "Texistepec" comes from the [[Nahuatl]] roots ''te-ksis''- "shell (egg)" + -''tepe''- "mountain" + -''k'': "in".<ref name="Campbell">Campbell, Lyle. ''American Indian languages: the historical linguistics of Native America''. Oxford: Oxford University Press, 1997. P. 403, note 22. {{ISBN|0-19-509427-1}}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality of Texistepec is delimited to the north by [[Oluta]] and [[Soconusco, Veracruz|Soconusco]], to the east by [[Hidalgotitlán (municipality)|Hidalgotitlán]], to the south by [[Jesús Carranza (municipality)|Jesús Carranza]] and to the west by [[Sayula de Alemán]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30172a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Texistepec |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> The municipality is watered by small rivers that are tributaries of the Chiquito, affluent river of the river Coatzacoalcos.

The [[weather]] in Texistepec is very warm and dry all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

Texistepec produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[Orange (fruit)|oranges]], [[rice]] and [[sorghum]].

==Celebrations==

In Texistepec, the celebration in honor of [[San Miguel Arcangel]], Patron of the town, takes place in May and the celebration in honor to [[Virgen de Guadalupe]] takes place in December.
