'''Nogales''' ({{IPA|es|noˈɣales}}) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Quillota Province]] of central [[Chile]]'s fifth [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Valparaíso Region|Valparaíso]].

== Geography ==
The commune of Nogales spans an area of {{convert|405.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Nogales has 21,633 inhabitants (10,786 men and 10,847 women). Of these, 18,698 (86.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,935 (13.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 15.9% (2,964 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Nogales is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]], administered by a communal council (''consejo comunal''), which is headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. The current alcalde is Óscar Cortés Puebla ([[Christian Democrat Party|PDC]]). The communal council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Juan Rivera ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Oscar Mena ([[Independent (politician)|Independent]])
* Jorge Gómez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Josué Godoy ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Jaime Lineros ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]])
* Nelson Verdejo ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Nogales is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Eduardo Cerda ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Andrea Molina]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 10th electoral district, (together with [[La Ligua]], [[Petorca]], [[Cabildo, Chile|Cabildo]], [[Papudo]], [[Zapallar]], [[Puchuncaví]], [[Quintero]], [[Calera, Chile|Calera]], [[La Cruz, Chile|La Cruz]], [[Quillota]] and [[Hijuelas]]).{{Update after|2021|8|18}} The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Ignacio Walker|Ignacio Walker Prieto]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Lily Pérez San Martín]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).{{Update after|2021|9|5}}
