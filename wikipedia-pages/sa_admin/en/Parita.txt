'''Parita  ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Parita District]], [[Herrera Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 3,723 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of [[Parita District]].<ref name=inec/> It was founded in 1556.<ref name=cl>''Columbia-Lippincott Gazetteer'' p. 1432</ref> Its population as of 1990 was 3,257; its population as of 2000 was 3,616.<ref name=inec/>

==History==
Before the Spanish conquest, this town was the capital of the [[Kingdom of Parita]].
