'''Chinautla''' ({{IPA|es|tʃiˈnawtla}}) is a city and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala Department|Guatemala department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The city has a population of 104,972 (2018 census) making it the fourteenth largest city in the country and the seventh largest in the Guatemala Department.<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of the major cities in Guatemala</ref>
