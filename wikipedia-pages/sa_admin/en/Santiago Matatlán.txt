'''Santiago Matatlán''' a.k.a. "World Capital Of [[Mezcal]]" is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 85.48 km<sup>2</sup>.

It is part of the [[Tlacolula District]] in the east of the [[Valles Centrales de Oaxaca|Valles Centrales Region]].
