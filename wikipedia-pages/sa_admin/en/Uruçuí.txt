'''Uruçuí''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Piauí]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref> It is the largest municipality in that state by area.
