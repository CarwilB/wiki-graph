'''Bella Vista Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 35,350 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|1,695|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Bella Vista, Corrientes|Bella Vista]], which is located around {{cvt|891|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].
