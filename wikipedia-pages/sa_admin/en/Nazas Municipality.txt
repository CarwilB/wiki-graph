''' Nazas  ''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Nazas]]. The municipality covers an area of 2,412.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 12,894,<ref>{{cite web |title=Nazas: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/nazas |website=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=8 April 2024}}</ref> up from 12,411 in 2010<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Nazas|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=015|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=22 August 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170822181818/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=015|url-status=dead}}</ref> and 12,166 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Nazas|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10015a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 15, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070309131832/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10015a.htm|archivedate=March 9, 2007}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Nazas had a population of 3,622.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Nazas, the municipality had 78 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[General Lázaro Cárdenas (Pueblo Nuevo), Durango|General Lázaro Cárdenas (Pueblo Nuevo)]] (1,927) and [[Paso Nacional, Durango|Paso Nacional]] (1,366), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The greatest extent of the [[Solar eclipse of April 8, 2024|solar eclipse of 8 April 2024]] was in the village of Guadalupe Hidalgo in the municipality of Nazas, with a totality of about 4 minutes and 28 seconds.<ref>{{cite web |title=Total Solar Eclipse: 8 April 2024 |url=https://www.thesuntoday.org/eclipses/solar/total/2024-04-08/ |website=The Sun Today |access-date=8 April 2024}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last1=Rivera |first1=Ramón |title=Así se vivió el eclipse solar total en Nazas, epicentro en Durango |url=https://www.milenio.com/content/eclipse-solar-mexico-vivio-nazas-durango |access-date=19 April 2024 |work=Milenio |date=8 April 2024}}</ref>
