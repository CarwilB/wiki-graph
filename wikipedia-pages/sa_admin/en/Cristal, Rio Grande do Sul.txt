'''Cristal''' ({{IPA|pt|kɾisˈtaw}}, "crystal") is a Brazilian municipality in the southeastern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 8,067 (2020 est.) in an area of 681.63&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/XKN Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The [[Camaquã River]] flows through the municipality.
