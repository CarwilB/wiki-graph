'''Olmedo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Manabí Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Olmedo, Manabí|Olmedo]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 9,844.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inec.gob.ec/cpv/ |title=Estadísticas de la Semana |access-date=2014-02-26 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140226070247/http://www.inec.gob.ec/cpv/ |archive-date=2014-02-26 }}</ref>
