'''Piraera''' is one of the municipalities of [[Lempira Department|Lempira department]] in [[Honduras]].  It is located on one of the branches of "Congolón" mountain, it is 105&nbsp;km away from the city of [[Gracias, Lempira|Gracias]].

== History ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
Its first inhabitants came from San Antonio, Santa Lucia and Magdalena in [[Intibucá Department]].  In the population record of 1702 there were some people booked. In the census of 1791, it is a town part of "Curato" de Cerquin and in 1889 it was a municipality of Candelaria.  It is supposed that ''Lempira'', the national hero, received the power from "Etempic" chief when the Spanish arrived, lived in this municipality.
[[Image:Piraera,Lempira 2.JPG|300px|thumb|right|The Old Colonial Church]]

== Geography ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
Since it is located on the mountains, the main forests are Pines and Oaks. Its elevation above sea level is the proper one for coffee plantations. Its mountains and hills are very high and steep. It has several water springs running down the steep hills. One curiosity is a huge rock monolith on the way to the departmental capital, which proves the volcanic origins of the rocks in this municipality and the rest of the department.

== Boundaries ==
Its boundaries are: 
*North : [[Gualcince]] and [[Erandique]] municipalities.
*South : [[Intibuca]] department and [[El Salvador]].
*East  : [[San Francisco, Lempira|San Francisco]] municipality and [[Intibuca]] department. 
*West  : [[Candelaria, Lempira|Candelaria]] and [[Virginia, Lempira|Virginia]] municipalities.
*'''Surface Extents''': 179&nbsp;km²

== Resources ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
Coffee is the main product up in Piraera, followed by cattle, milk derivates, corn, beans, and commerce. The trees are plenty and yield some income. It also has several water springs, for people and cattle and for the crops. As the rest of the department it has electricity, Internet access in the Mayor's and with mobile communication services. A few people sell fuels in their houses.

==Population==
The majority of the population is Indigenous. 
*'''Population''': The population in 2001 was 11,420, and according to estimates by the INE Honduras, 14,124 is expected for 2015.
*'''Villages''': 10
*'''Settlements''': 105
===Demographics===
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Piraera municipality had a population of 13,758. Of these, 78.00% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 20.44% [[Mestizo]], 1.50% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.06% [[White Latin Americans|White]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>

== Tourism ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
The traveler must go through [[Santa Cruz, Lempira|Santa Cruz]], [[San Andrés, Lempira|San Andrés]] and [[Gualcince]] municipalities.  The deviation to Piraera is {{convert|85|km}} from Gracias, to the left.{{fact|date=March 2022}}  This dirt road is repaired constantly along the year.  The visitor must be careful during the rainy season due to landslides along the way.{{fact|date=March 2022}} It has little to offer to visitors, it has the typical colonial distribution.  Local activities include hiking in the hills, which have views of the volcanos of [[El Salvador]], and [[Intibuca]] department.{{fact|date=March 2022}} Also, there is a waterfall on the way.{{fact|date=March 2022}}

Local holidays include "Santiago' Day on July 25, and "Sagrado Corazon Day" and "San Jose Day" both on October 19.
