'''Melgaço''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Pará]] in the [[North Region, Brazil|North Region]] of [[Brazil]]. With an area of {{Convert|6,772.764|km²|sp=us}}, of which {{Convert|1.7177|km²}} is urban, it is located 173 km from [[Belém]], the state capital, and 1,503 km from [[Brasília]], the federal capital. Its population in the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 demographic census]] was 27,881 inhabitants, according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), ranking as the 77th most populous municipality in the state of Pará. Melgaço ranked lowest among all Brazilian municipalities in the [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) published by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP) in 2010.

== Geography ==
The territory of Melgaço covers {{Convert|6,772.764|km²|sp=us}},<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |title=Brasil / Pará / Melgaço |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/panorama |access-date=2025-06-18}}</ref> of which {{Convert|1.7177|km²}} constitutes the urban area.<ref name="Tabela/8418">{{cite web |last=IBGE |year=2019 |title=Tabela 8418 - Áreas urbanizadas, Loteamento vazio, Área total mapeada e Subcategorias |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/8418#/n3/23/n6/1504505/v/12749/p/all/d/v12749%204/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=16 December 2024 |publisher=}}</ref> It sits at an average altitude of 12 meters above [[sea level]]. Melgaço borders these municipalities: [[Portel, Pará|Portel]], [[Breves, Brazil|Breves]], [[Bagre, Brazil|Bagre]], [[Gurupá]], and [[Porto de Moz]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Melgaço, PA - Informações sobre o município e a prefeitura |url=https://www.cidade-brasil.com.br/municipio-melgaco.html |access-date=2025-06-17 |website=www.cidade-brasil.com.br}}</ref> The city is located 173 km from the state capital [[Belém]],<ref name="distancia">{{cite web |title=Distância entre Belem - PA, Brazil e Oeiras do Pará - PA, Brazil |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-belem-pa-brazil-ate-melgaco-pa-brazil |access-date=14 December 2024 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref> and 1,503 km from the federal capital [[Brasília]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Distância entre Brasília e Melgaço |url=https://www.distanciaentreascidades.com.br/distancia-de-brasilia-df-brazil-ate-melgaco-pa-brazil |access-date=25 July 2014 |website=Distância Entre as Cidades}}</ref>

Under the [[Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions|territorial division established in 2017]] by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality belongs to the immediate geographical region of Breves, within the intermediate region of Breves.<ref name="MesoMicro">{{cite web |date= |title=DTB_2024.zip |url=http://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2024/ |access-date=29 April 2025 |publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |language=pt-br |format=[[Zip (file format)|Zip file]]}} RELATORIO_DTB_BRASIL_2024_MUNICIPIOS.ods within zip file.</ref> Previously, under the [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]] and [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] divisions, it was part of the microregion of Portel in the mesoregion of Marajó.<ref>{{cite journal |year=1990 |title=Divisão regional do Brasil em mesorregiões e microrregiões geográficas |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |url-status=live |journal=Biblioteca IBGE |publisher=IBGE |volume=1 |page=44–45 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170925170313/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv2269_1.pdf |archive-date=25 September 2017 |access-date=25 September 2017}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_DTB_20082">{{cite web |date=1 July 2008 |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/Organizacao/Divisao_Territorial/2008/DTB_2008.zip |access-date=11 October 2008 |work=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
In the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], the municipality had a population of 27,881 inhabitants and ranked only 77th in the state that year (out of 144 municipalities),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2022 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122232 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with 52.25% male and 47.75% female,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/1504505/v/all/p/all/c2/all/c287/100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=2024-12-16}}</ref> resulting in a sex ratio of 109.43 (10,943 men for every 10,000 women),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Sexo / Razão de sexo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122248 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> compared to 24,808 inhabitants in the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]] (22.18% living in the urban area),<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação do domicílio e sexo |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#/n6/1504505/v/all/p/all/c1/all/c2/all/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c1,t+c2/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> when it held the 88th state position.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Sinopse / População residente ( Unidade: pessoas ) / 2010 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/23/27652?detalhes=true&tipo=ranking&indicador=27665 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Between the 2010 and 2022 censuses, the population of Melgaço registered a growth of just over 12.4%, with an annual geometric growth rate of 0.98%.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=População residente / Taxa de crescimento geométrico ( Unidade: % ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122278 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> Regarding [[age group]] in the 2022 census, 57.89% of the inhabitants were between 15 and 64 years old, 38.54% were under fifteen, and 3.55% were 65 or older.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 9514 - População residente, por sexo, idade e forma de declaração da idade |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/9514#/n6/1504505/v/all/p/all/c2/6794/c287/6653,49108,49109,60040,60041,93070,93084,93085,93086,93087,93088,93089,93090,93091,93092,93093,93094,93095,93096,93097,93098,100362/c286/113635/d/v1000093%202/l/v,p+c2+c287,t+c286/resultado |access-date=December 22, 2024}}</ref> The [[population density]] in 2022 was 4.12 inhabitants per square kilometer,<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |title=Território / Densidade demográfica ( Unidade: habitantes por quilômetro quadrado ) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/10102/122229?tipo=ranking&indicador=122231 |access-date=2024-09-06}}</ref> with an average of 4.85 inhabitants per household.<ref>{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2022 |title=Tabela 4712 - Domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados, Moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados e Média de moradores em domicílios particulares permanentes ocupados |url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/4712#/n6/1504505/v/all/p/all/d/v5930%202/l/v,p,t/resultado |access-date=2024-01-06}}</ref>

The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) is registered as the lowest-ranked in Brazil, according to data from the [[United Nations Development Programme]]. According to the 2010 report published in 2013, its value was 0.418, ranking 144th in the state and 5,565th nationally (out of 5,565 municipalities),<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010">{{cite web |date=2010 |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610114916/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/Ranking-IDHM-Municipios-2010.aspx |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |access-date=October 11, 2010 |work=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano |publisher=United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)}}</ref> and the [[Gini coefficient]] rose from 0.33 in 2003<ref name="Gini2003">{{cite web |author=IBGE |date=2003 |title=Mapa de pobreza e desigualdade |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pa/melgaco/pesquisa/36/30246?ano=2003 |access-date=2024-12-21}}</ref> to 0.55 in 2010.<ref>{{Cite web |last=IBGE |date=2010 |title=Índice de Gini da renda domiciliar per capita segundo Município |url=http://tabnet.datasus.gov.br/cgi/ibge/censo/cnv/ginibr.def |access-date=17 June 2025 |website=DataSUS}}</ref> Considering only the longevity index, its value is 0.776, the income index is 0.454, and the education index is 0.207.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010" />

According to [[BBC News Brasil]], in Melgaço, the school dropout rate among children aged six to fourteen in primary education is 14.6%, while in secondary education it reaches 21.2%. Additionally, 20% of residents aged fifteen or older are illiterate, and the average length of schooling among the population is five years.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Fellet |first=João |last2=Lima |first2=Felix |date=2024-10-14 |title=Eleições municipais 2024: o fosso que separa as vidas nos municípios de melhor e pior IDH do Brasil |url=https://www.bbc.com/portuguese/articles/c0m08e9ymejo |access-date=2025-06-17 |work=BBC News Brasil |language=pt-br}}</ref>
