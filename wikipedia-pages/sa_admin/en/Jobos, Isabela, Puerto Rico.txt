'''Jobos''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Isabela, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 3,446.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó. ==History==
Jobos was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. In 1899, the [[United States Department of War]] conducted a [[census]] of Puerto Rico finding that the population of Jobos barrio was 1,551.<ref name="OfficeSanger1900">{{cite book|author1=Joseph Prentiss Sanger|author2=Henry Gannett|author3=Walter Francis Willcox|title=Informe sobre el censo de Puerto Rico, 1899, United States. ==Places in Jobos==
In 2019, the headquarters for Solar Libre, a new school where students can study solar energy, was established in Jobos. It was established with financial support from Samsung, The Hispanic Federation and NFL football player, [[Victor Cruz (American football)|Victor Cruz]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/nota/victorcruzentregadonativoanuevaescuelaenisabela-1303446/|title = Victor Cruz entrega donativo a nueva escuela en Isabela|date = 21 September 2018}}</ref>

[[Jobos Beach]], known as a world-class surf spot<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/2009/01/25/travel/25puerto-rico.html|title=Affordable Caribbean: Puerto Rico|first=John|last=Haskins|newspaper=The New York Times|date=January 22, 2009}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.surfertoday.com/travel/the-best-surf-spots-in-puerto-rico|title=The best surf spots in Puerto Rico|author=<!--not stated-->|website=SurferToday.com|language=en-us|access-date=2023-10-30}}</ref> and where in February 2013, the [[Rip Curl Pro]] surfing competition was held, is located in Jobos barrio.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.noticel.com/deportes/jobos-recibe-evento-de-surfing-de-clase-mundial-este-fin-de-semana/609019413|title=Jobos recibe evento de surfing de clase mundial este fin de semana|date=February 8, 2013|publisher=noticel.com|language=es|access-date=October 12, 2019|archive-date=October 9, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191009021807/https://www.noticel.com/deportes/jobos-recibe-evento-de-surfing-de-clase-mundial-este-fin-de-semana/609019413|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://enciclopediapr.org/en/marine-route/events-and-sports/windsurfing/|title=Windsurfing|website=Encyclopedia of Puerto Rico|access-date=2019-10-12|archive-date=2019-07-15|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190715021724/https://enciclopediapr.org/en/marine-route/events-and-sports/windsurfing/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

There is a cemetery in Jobos.
