'''Santa Maria do Cambucá''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃ta maˈɾiɐ ˈdu kɐ̃buˈka]}}) is a city in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]]   ('''N''');  [[Frei Miguelinho]]   ('''S''');  [[Surubim]] and [[Vertentes do Lério]]   ('''E''');   [[Vertentes]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 92.14&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 494 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] river
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - [[semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 23.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 153&nbsp;km
* Population - 14,586 (2025)<ref name="IBGESanta2024" />

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Santa Maria do Cambucá''' are general industry,  and [[agribusiness]] especially plantations of [[bean]]s; and farming of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goat]]s and [[chickens]].
