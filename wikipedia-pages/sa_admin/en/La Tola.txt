'''La Tola''' is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
La Tola has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
