'''Xaloztoc''' is a town and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Tlaxcala|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tlaxcala]]. It is one of 60 municipalities in the state. The [[INEGI]] reference number for the municipality is 29039.<ref>{{cite web |title=Estado de Tlaxcala |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040919125451/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tlaxcala/ |archive-date=19 September 2004 |access-date=6 March 2023 |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tlaxcala/ |url-status=live }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². The name derives from the [[Nahuatl]] ''xal'' ("sand"), ''azto'' ("cave") and the locative suffix ''c''.<ref>{{cite web |title=Xaloztoc: Tu Municipio |url=https://www.xaloztoc.gob.mx/municipio |publisher=Ayuntamiento de Xaloztoc |access-date=31 October 2024}}</ref>

==Geography==
In  2020 the municipality of Xaloztoc had 25,607 residents, up 17.6% from the 2010 figure.<ref>{{cite web |title=Xaloztoc: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/xaloztoc |website=Data México |publisher=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=31 October 2024}}</ref> The size of the municipality is about 49 km<sup>2</sup> (19 sq mi). The average elevation is 2,500 meters (8,200 ft) above sea level.

===Climate===
On average, Xaloztoc receives 730.3 mm/m<sup>2</sup> (28.8 in) of precipitation annually. The average yearly temperature for the area is approximately {{convert|14.8|C|F|}}.
