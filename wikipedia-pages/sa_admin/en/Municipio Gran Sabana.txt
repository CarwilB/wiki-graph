The '''Gran Sabana Municipality''' is one of the 11 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 28,450.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Santa Elena de Uairén]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Gran Sabana Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Bolivar.zip {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Gran Sabana Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 34,095 (up from 27,027 in 2000).  This amounts to 2.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1.03|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> As of 2011, the Gran Sabana Municipality has amongst the highest proportions of indigenous people in the country, roughly 77.67%, the majority of which being Pemon.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Angosto-Ferrández |first=Luis F. |date=2022-03-01 |title=Cultural labor and the defetishization of environments: connecting ethnographies of tourism in Venezuela and Chile |url=https://doi.org/10.1007/s10624-021-09639-0 |journal=Dialectical Anthropology |language=en |volume=46 |issue=1 |pages=55–72 |doi=10.1007/s10624-021-09639-0 |issn=1573-0786 |pmc=8601866 |pmid=34815615}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Gran Sabana Municipality is Emilio González, elected in the [[2017 Venezuelan municipal elections]].<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Gran Sabana | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis=0&edo=6&mun=9&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231502/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis=0&edo=6&mun=9&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29}}</ref>  He replaced Manuel Vallés shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 Directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archive-date=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one (two if you count the Capital Gran Sabana section) [[parish]] (Ikabarú).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
