== Demographics ==
Tanquián de Ecosbedo's population has a population of 14,382, 83% of which are [[Catholic Church|Catholic]]. 25% of the population is indigenous or is of indigenous descent.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Towns of Tanquián de Escobedo (Municipality in San Luis Potosí) |url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/san-luis-potosi/tanquian-de-escobedo/ |access-date=2022-11-01 |website=mexico.PueblosAmerica.com |language=en-US}}</ref> Its most populous town of the same name has a population of 9458 according to the 2020 mexican census.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tanquián De Escobedo (San Luis Potosí) Tanquián de Escobedo |url=https://en.mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/tanquian-de-escobedo/ |access-date=2022-11-01 |website=mexico.PueblosAmerica.com |language=en-US}}</ref>[[File:Tanquian en San Luis Potosí.svg|thumb|Tanquian en San Luis Potosí]]
