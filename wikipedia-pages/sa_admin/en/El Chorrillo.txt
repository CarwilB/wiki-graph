'''El Chorrillo''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] within [[Panama City]], in [[Panamá District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 18,302 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/>

==History==
It was founded April 29, 1915, and was originally populated by immigrants working on the construction of the [[Panama Canal]]; many were from the [[Antilles]]. The impoverished area is the hometown of [[Manuel Noriega]] and was heavily damaged during the [[United States invasion of Panama]] in 1989.<ref name="nbcnews">{{cite web|url=http://worldnews.nbcnews.com/_news/2011/12/11/9363044-noriega-returns-to-panama-a-largely-forgotten-man|title=Noriega returns to Panama a largely forgotten man |website= nbcnews.com|access-date=2014-06-02}}</ref> Other former residents of note include boxer [[Roberto Durán]]<ref>{{cite AV media| title= No Mas| type= documentary film|year= 2013| publisher= ESPN}}</ref> and poet Hector Miguel Collado.

Its population as of 1990 was 20,488; its population as of 2000 was 22,632.<ref name=inec/>

==Sport==
El Chorillo is the only district in Panama with two first-division clubs - [[Chorrillo F.C.]] and [[Plaza Amador]] - in the [[Liga Panameña de Fútbol]].

El Chorillo is the birthplace of hall of fame boxer [[Roberto Duran]].
