'''Itambacuri''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|itɐ̃bakuˈɾi}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref>[http://wikimapia.org/1339322/es/Itambacuri-Minas-Gerais-Brasil Wikimapia {{in lang|es}}]</ref> {{as of|2020}} its population is estimated to be 23,209. Itambacuri contains its own airport.<ref>[http://www.world-airport-codes.com/brazil/itambacuri-3204.html World-Airport-Codes.com]</ref>
