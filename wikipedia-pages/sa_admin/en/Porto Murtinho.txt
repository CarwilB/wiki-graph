'''Porto Murtinho''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈpoʁtu muʁˈtʃiɲu}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 17,298&nbsp;(2020) and its area is {{cvt|17735|km2}}.

==Climate==
On 15 November 2023, a maximum temperature of {{convert|43.0|°C}} was registered in Porto Murtinho.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ogimet.com/cgi-bin/gsynres?ind=86833&ano=2023&mes=11&day=17&hora=1&min=0&ndays=30|title= 86833:   Porto Murtinho (Brazil)|author=<!--Not stated--> |date= 15 November 2023|website=ogimet.com |publisher=OGIMET |access-date= 16 November 2023|quote=}}</ref>

==Consular representation==
[[Paraguay]] has a Consulate in Porto Murtinho.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.mre.gov.py/index.php/representaciones/consulados-del-paraguay-en-el-mundo/en-el-brasil|title=Representaciones, Consulados del Paraguay, en el Brasil|website=Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores|language=es|access-date=26 June 2022}}</ref>
