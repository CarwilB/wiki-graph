'''Rafael Delgado''', former known as '''San Juan del Río''', is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the mountainous central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 140&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of {{convert|39.48|km2|mi2}} and, in the 2020 census, reported a population of 24,127.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rafael Delgado: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/rafael-delgado |website=Data México |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=17 December 2024}}</ref>

In 1831, the village of San Juan del Río constituted a municipality. By decree, on November 5, 1932, the municipality adopted its current name, in honor of [[Rafael Delgado (author)|the author Rafael Delgado]], a native of nearby [[Córdoba, Veracruz|Córdoba]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Rafael Delgado, Ver. Informe del Resultado de la Fiscalización Superiopr |url=https://www.orfis.gob.mx/CuentasPublicas/informe2014/archivos/TOMO%20V/VOLUMEN%2011/004%20Rafael%20Delgado.pdf |publisher=ORFIS |access-date=17 December 2024 |quote=Su nombre se establece en honor al gran escritor cordobés Rafael Delgado Sáinz. Por Decreto de 5 de noviembre de 1932 el Municipio de San Juan del Río y la cabecera cambian su nombre al de Rafael Delgado.}}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality is bordered to the north by [[Orizaba]] and [[Ixmatlahuacan]], to the east by [[Ixtaczoquitlán (municipality)|Ixtaczoquitlán]], to the south by [[San Andrés Tenejapan]], [[Tlilapan]] and [[Nogales (municipality of Veracruz)|Nogales]], and to the west by [[Río Blanco, Veracruz|Río Blanco]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30135a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Rafael Delgado |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-12}}</ref> It is irrigated by the Río Blanco river.

The weather in Rafael Delgado is cold and wet all year, with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==
The municipality produces principally [[maize]], [[sugarcane]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==
Every June, a celebration is held in honor of San Juan de la Concepción, patron of the town, and in December the town holds festivities in honor of the Virgin of Guadalupe.
