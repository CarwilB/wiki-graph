The '''Girardot Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 11,906.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The town of [[El Baúl, Cojedes|El Baúl]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Girardot Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Girardot Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 11,579 (up from 10,387 in 2000).  This amounts to 3.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|3.34|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Girardot Municipality is Gustavo Ramón Guillén Brizuela, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 30% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Girardot | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=3&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163444/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=3&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 Directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; El Baúl and Sucre.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
