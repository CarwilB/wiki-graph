'''Queropalca District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Lauricocha Province|Lauricocha]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Waywash mountain range|Waywash]] mountain range traverses the district. The highest peak of the district is [[Yerupaja]] at {{convert|6635|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} which is also the highest elevation of the range. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL map of the Lauricocha Province (Huánuco Region)</ref>

* Chinkana
* [[Kasha (Peru)|Kasha]]
* Kuntur Waqanan
* Llamt'a
* [[Mit'urahu]]
* [[Parya (Peru)|Parya]]
* [[Runtuy]]
* T'uyu Hirka
