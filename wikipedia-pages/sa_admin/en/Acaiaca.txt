'''Acaiaca''' is a city in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 its population was estimated to be 3,994.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/acaiaca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is the birthplace of [[Geovanni Deiberson Maurício|Geovanni]], the attacking midfield [[association football|football]] player formerly of [[Barcelona FC|Barcelona]], [[S.L. Benfica|Benfica]] and [[Hull City]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://globoesporte.globo.com/platb/conexaogontijo/2009/11/17/geovannio-mineiro-que-faz-historia-no-futebol-ingles/|title = Conexão Gontijo » Geovanni, o mineiro que faz história no futebol inglês » Arquivo}}</ref>
