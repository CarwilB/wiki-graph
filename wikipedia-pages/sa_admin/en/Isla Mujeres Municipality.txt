'''Isla Mujeres''' ({{IPA|es|'isla mu'xeɾes}}, [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for ''Island of the Women'') is one of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|eleven municipalities]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Quintana Roo]], located on the [[Yucatán Peninsula]]. Most of the municipality is located on the mainland in the northeastern corner of the state. Its municipal seat, also called [[Isla Mujeres]], is a small town on the island from which it takes its name. It is located about {{convert|13|km|mi}} northeast of [[Cancún]] in the [[Caribbean Sea]], and it is the easternmost municipal seat in Mexico. As of the {{#invoke:Wikidata|getQualifierDateValue|P1082|P585|FETCH_WIKIDATA|y}} census, the town had a population of {{#property:P1082}} inhabitants.<ref name="INEGI">[http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 2010 census tables: INEGI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130502025339/http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 |date=2013-05-02 }}</ref>
