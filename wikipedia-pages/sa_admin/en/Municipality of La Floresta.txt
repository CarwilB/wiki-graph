The '''municipality of La Floresta''' is one of the municipalities of [[Canelones Department]], [[Uruguay]], established on 15 March 2010. Its seat is the city of [[La Floresta, Uruguay|La Floresta]].

== History ==
The municipality was created by the provision of Law No. 18653 of 15 March 2010, as part of Canelones Department, and it includes the CLB and CLC constituencies.<ref>{{cite web|url= http://200.40.229.134/Leyes/AccesoTextoLey.asp?Ley=18653&Anchor=|title= Ley 18.653|accessdate= 20 December 2012|work= Poder Legislativo|date= 15 March 2010|location= Montevideo|archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20131103162225/http://200.40.229.134/Leyes/AccesoTextoLey.asp?Ley=18653&Anchor=|archivedate= 3 November 2013}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= http://www.imcanelones.gub.uy/recursos/descargas/materiales_municipios/Decreto_junta_departamental_76.pdf|title= Decreto Nº 76|accessdate= 20 December 2012|work= Junta Departamental de Canelones|date= 30 December 2009|format= pdf|archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20140202172213/http://www.imcanelones.gub.uy/recursos/descargas/materiales_municipios/Decreto_junta_departamental_76.pdf|archivedate= 2 February 2014}}</ref>

== Location ==
The municipality lies at the southeast side of the Canelones Department. The main activity of the municipality is tourism, as it is made up of a chain of spas on the [[Río de la Plata]].<ref name="MUNLAFLOR">{{cite web |url= http://www.imcanelones.gub.uy/?q=node/4343|title= Municipio de La Floresta|accessdate= 21 December 2012|work= Comuna Canaria}}</ref> 

Its area is 65.5 km².<ref>{{cite web|author=Intendencia de Canelones|title=Municipios de Canelones, Base de Datos|url=http://imcanelones.gub.uy/recursos/descargas/materiales_municipios/Informe.zip|accessdate=28 January 2014|format=zip|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140202172458/http://imcanelones.gub.uy/recursos/descargas/materiales_municipios/Informe.zip|archivedate=2 February 2014}}</ref> The census localities that make it up, had a population of 7,310 inhabitants, in 2011.<ref name=PerfilOD>{{Cite web|url=https://otu.opp.gub.uy/perfiles/canelones/la-floresta|title=La Floresta|publisher=Uruguay Territory Observatory, [[Office of Planning and Budget]]|language=Spanish |accessdate=24 April 2022}}</ref>
