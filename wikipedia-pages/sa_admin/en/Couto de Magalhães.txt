'''Couto Magalhães''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 5,639 (2020) and its area is 1,586&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (612.4&nbsp;mi<sup>2</sup>), a density of 3.07 people/km<sup>2</sup> (8 people/mi<sup>2</sup>). It is located 150&nbsp;m (492.1&nbsp;ft) above sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/to/coutomagalhaes/index.php |title=CityBrazil - Tocantins - Couto de Magalhães |access-date=2008-12-23 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110524181137/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/to/coutomagalhaes/index.php |archive-date=2011-05-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The [[demonym]] is Coutense.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=June 11, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
