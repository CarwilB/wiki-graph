'''El Cocuy''' ({{IPA|es|el koˈkuj}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the sub-region of the [[Gutiérrez Province]]. The national natural park [[El Cocuy National Park]] is nearby.
