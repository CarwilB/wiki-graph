'''Cenotillo Municipality''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "little [[cenote]]") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 614.43&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|115|km}} east of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], it was part of the chieftainship of Cupules. After colonization, it became part of the [[encomienda]] system and some of the first encomenderos were Diego Burgos and don Diego López de Ricalde in 1583,<ref name="municipios"/> followed by Lorenzo Coella in 1627.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5 |page=513 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA36|accessdate=1 August 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref> Subsequent encomenderos included José Domingo Pardío in 1744.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821,<ref name="municipios"/> and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Valladolid Municipality, Yucatan|Valladolid Municipality]]. In 1867, it was assigned to the [[Espita Municipality]] and in 1988 it was confirmed as head of its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995 |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |website=inegi |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=7 August 2015 |location=Aguascalientes, Mexico |language=Spanish |date=1996 |pages=102, 110 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |archivedate=23 December 2015 }}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of sports, parks and gardens, and potable water.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Cenotillo|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=7 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Cenotillo]], Yucatán. The populated areas of the municipality besides the seat include Cantún, Chunyucú, Cunyá, Ebtún, Kakalhá, Karin, Kaxec, Mococa, Muctal, Ocal, Pacel, Palmero, Petil, San Antonio, San Felipe, San Nicolás, San Pedro, San Ruto, Santa Clara, Sihonal, Tixbacab, Tzumbalam, Yodzonot 2 and Yohman. ==Local festivals==
Every year from 3 to 13 August a festival is held in honor of Santa Clara in Cenotillo. In Tixbacab from the 13 to 15 August there is an annual celebration for the Virgin of the Assumption.<ref name="municipios"/>
