'''Araguacema''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 7,155 (2020) and its area is 2,778&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/RDH]</ref>

The municipality contains 17.15% of the {{convert|1678000|ha|adj=on}} [[Ilha do Bananal / Cantão Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Leandro (Ilha do Bananal/Cantão) – ISA}}|language=pt
