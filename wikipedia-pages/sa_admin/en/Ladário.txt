'''Ladário''' ({{IPA|pt|laˈdaɾju}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]].  The municipality of Ladário is surrounded by the municipality of [[Corumbá]] in all directions. Together, Corumbá and Ladário total 121,176 inhabitants.

A town with natural resources such as iron, manganese, limestone, sand and clay, Ladário is located in the western region of the state of Mato Grosso do Sul, in the heart of the [[Pantanal]].
