'''Tonayán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the south zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 20&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 74.03 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|43|12|N|96|54|36|W|}}.
==Name==
The name comes from the language Náhuatl, Tonali-tona-yan; that means “Place where it dawns".

==History==
Tonayán was founded as village between the year 1552 and 1564 when a group of inhabitants of the old village of Misantla established in this place to take care of its boundaries that were feeling threatened by the agrarian invasions of the village of Chapultepec.

==Geography==

The municipality of Tonayán is delimited to the north by [[Tenochtitlán, Veracruz|Tenochtitlán]], to the east by [[Landero y Coss]], to the south-east by [[Naolinco]] and to the west by [[Coacoatzintla]] and [[Tlacolulan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30187a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tonayán |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-17}}</ref> It is watered by small tributary rivers of the river Actopan.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[potatoes]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

In Tonayán, in June takes place the celebration in honor to San Pedro Apostol, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.{{cn|date=September 2023}}
