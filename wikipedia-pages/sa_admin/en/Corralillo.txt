'''Corralillo''' is a municipality and town in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1831<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1879.

==Geography==
Corralillo is located on the northern coast of Cuba, in the north-western corner of the Villa Clara province, bordering the [[Matanzas Province]] to the west and the Bay of Cadiz to the north. The [[cay]]s of Falcones, Blanquizal and Verde of the [[Sabana-Camagüey Archipelago]] as well as a multitude of [[reef]]s are located at sea north of Corralillo.<ref>[https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Cayo%20Blanquizal#map=11/22.9938/-80.2462 Corralillo"], map, on ''openstreetmap.org''.</ref>

The municipality is divided into the ''consejos populares'' (mainly villages) of Ceja de Pablo, Corralillo (municipal seat), Palma Sola, Perú, [[Rancho Veloz]], Sabana Grande, Santa María and [[Sierra Morena, Cuba|Sierra Morena]].<ref name=guije>{{Cite web |title= Corralillo |website= guije.com |language= es |url= http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/corralillo/index.htm |accessdate= 2007-10-09 }} {{Dead link|date=November 2023}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Corralillo had a population of 24,791.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|843|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-09}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|29|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
