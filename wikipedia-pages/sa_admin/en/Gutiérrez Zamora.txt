'''Gutiérrez Zamora''' is a city in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. Gutiérrez Zamora is bordered by [[Papantla]], [[Tuxpan]], [[Poza Rica]] and [[Coatzintla]], and it is served by both the railway and [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highways 180 and 190]].

It was named in honour of  [[Manuel Gutiérrez Zamora]], a native of the port of [[Veracruz, Veracruz|Veracruz]] who served as  [[Governor of Veracruz|governor of the state]] in the mid-19th century.
