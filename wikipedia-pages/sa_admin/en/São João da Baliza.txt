'''São João da Baliza''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈsɐ̃w ʒuˈɐ̃w da baˈlizɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 8.858 (2022) and its area is 4,284&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/HXH|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Roraima {{!}} São João da Baliza|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-06-15}}</ref> São João da Baliza was first settled in 1974 after the [[BR-210]] highway made the area accessible. It was called São João, because three of the initial settlers were called João. Da Baliza refers to a stream near the town.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://sjb.rr.gov.br/novo/nosso-municipio/ |title=NOSSO MUNICÍPIO|website=Municipality of São João da Baliza|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> It became an independent municipality in 1982.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/sao-joao-da-baliza/historico |title=São João da Baliza |website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=15 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
