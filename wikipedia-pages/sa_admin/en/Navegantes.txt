'''Navegantes''' ({{IPA|pt|naveˈɡɐ̃tʃis}}, ''Seafarers'') is a coastal and [[tourist city]] located in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] state, [[Southern Region, Brazil|southern]] [[Brazil]]. The population is 93.619 (2024 est.) in an area of 112.0&nbsp;km².<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1VE Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>

[[Ministro Victor Konder International Airport]] serves the popular beach resort of [[Balneário Camboriú]], and other cities including [[Itajaí]] and [[Blumenau]].

==Economy==
The main activity is fishing industry and ship building.  A private port was built in 2007, served by 4 berths (approximately 900m straight) and focused on refrigerated container cargo, due to Santa Catarina State having the biggest [[meat industry]] in [[Brazil]].  As of 2012, there were 6 cranes, and plans for expansion of the quay.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.tilgroup.com/terminal/port-navegantes|url-status=live|title=Brazil - Portuarios de Navegantes - Port of Navegantes &#124; TIL Group|website=tilgroup.com|accessdate=May 17, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230516234853/https://www.tilgroup.com/our-terminals/navegantes.html|archive-date=May 16, 2023}}</ref>
