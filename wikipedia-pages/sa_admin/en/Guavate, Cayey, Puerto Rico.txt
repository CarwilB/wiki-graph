'''Guavate''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Cayey, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 1,870.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó. ==History==
Guavate was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. In 1899, the [[United States Department of War]] conducted a [[census]] of Puerto Rico finding that the combined population of [[Beatriz, Cayey, Puerto Rico|Beatriz]] and Guavate barrios was 853.<ref name="OfficeSanger1900">{{cite book|author1=Joseph Prentiss Sanger|author2=Henry Gannett|author3=Walter Francis Willcox|title=Informe sobre el censo de Puerto Rico, 1899, United States. ==Tourism==
Guavate is known for its "pork highway" (a stretch of [[Puerto Rico Highway 184]]) and was featured in the Discover Puerto Rico tourism campaign of 2019 for being a must-see place in Puerto Rico because of its tender [[Lechon|pork cuisine]], mild weather and scenic views of [[Sierra de Cayey]].<ref name="Discover PR">{{Cite web|url=https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/list/top-places-to-see-puerto-rico|title=Top Places to See in Puerto Rico|website=Discover Puerto Rico}}</ref><ref>{{cite web | title=Puerto Rico Leisure Guide – 2021 | website=Issuu | url=https://issuu.com/discoverpuertorico/docs/leisure_guide_2021_edited | access-date=January 22, 2022}}</ref> The [[Carite State Forest]] is located nearby.

==Notable residents==
Juan Santos Torres {{lang|es|El Picapiedras de Guavate}} is a well-known Puerto Rican sculptor who lives in and has a workshop in Guavate.<ref name="Discover PR" /><ref name="Picapiedra">{{cite web |title=Puertorriqueñísimo - Cayey - El picapiedras de Guavate  |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0O6sNzAju7o |website=YouTube |publisher=PuertoRicoNetwork |accessdate=14 June 2020|language=es}}</ref><ref name="orlandosentinel.com 2019">{{cite web | title=EN FOTOS: Juan Santos, el Picapiedras de Guavate y su arte en mármol | website=orlandosentinel.com | date=1 July 2019 | url=https://www.orlandosentinel.com/elsentinel/negocios/os-es-picapiedras-guavate-puerto-rico-artesano-20190801-wkmwvaqrxbhl3j34cwrjtaz7pu-photogallery.html | access-date=14 June 2020|language=es}}</ref>
