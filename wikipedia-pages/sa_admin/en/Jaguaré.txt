'''Jaguaré''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 31,039 (2020) and its area is 660&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/jaguare/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|27858|ha}} [[Sooretama Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1982 when two earlier units were merged.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Sooretama|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=201|access-date=2016-04-29}}</ref><ref>{{citation|language=pt
 |title=Reserva Biológica de Sooretama|publisher=ICMBio |url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/rebiosooretama/|access-date=2016-04-17}}</ref>
