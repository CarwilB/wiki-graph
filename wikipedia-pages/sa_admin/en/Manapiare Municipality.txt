The '''Manapiare Municipality''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Manapiare}}) is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the southern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Amazonas (Venezuelan state)|Amazonas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 7,715.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[San Juan de Manapiare]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Manapiare Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Amazonas.zip {{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The town of San Juan de Manapiare was founded by the explorer Don Melicio Pérez in the year 1940.<ref>[http://www.amazonas.gob.ve/amazonas/municipios.html Municipios del Estado Amazonas<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100421163113/http://www.amazonas.gob.ve/amazonas/municipios.html |date=2010-04-21 }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Manapiare Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 9,658 (up from 7,647 in 2000). This amounts to 6.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.3|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Manapiare Municipality is Pastor Nelson Rodrìguez, elected on October 31, 2004, with 60% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Manapiare | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120206165431/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-06}}</ref>  He replaced Benjamin Perez shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Alto Ventuari, Medio Ventuari, and Bajo Ventuari (previous to December 18, 1997, the Manapiare Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
