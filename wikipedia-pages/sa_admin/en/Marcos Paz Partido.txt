'''Marcos Paz''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|''partido'']] in the [[Provinces of Argentina|Argentine province]] of [[Buenos Aires Province|Buenos Aires]]. Its capital city is [[Marcos Paz, Buenos Aires|Marcos Paz]].

Established on 25 October 1878 (provincial law 1244), Marcos Paz is at the eastern border of [[Greater Buenos Aires]] with the rest of the province, although in an administrative sense, it is not considered part of the [[metropolitan area]].

According to the 2001 [[INDEC]] National Census, the ''partido'' has 43,400 inhabitants, and a population density of {{convert|92.34|PD/sqkm}}. Marcos Paz's mayor is Ricardo Pedro Curuchet, of the local ''Partido Vecinalista''.

==Name==
The town of [[Marcos Paz]], which gives its name to the partido, gradually built up around a train station, ''Estación Coronel Doctor Marcos Paz''. The station was named after [[Marcos Paz|Coronel Doctor Marcos Paz]], an Argentine politician and vice president to [[Bartolomé Mitre]] in 1862.
