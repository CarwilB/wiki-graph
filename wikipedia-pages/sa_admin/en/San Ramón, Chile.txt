'''San Ramón''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "Saint Ramón") is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], within the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] of [[Chile]]. It is part of the urban area of [[Santiago, Chile|Greater Santiago]]. It has 76,002 inhabitants.<ref name="2024census" />

==Demographics==
As of the 2024 census, the commune has a population of 76,002, of which 49.2% are male and 50.8% are female. People under 15 years old make up 16.4% of the population, and people over 65 years old make up 16.8%. 100% of the population is urban.<ref name="2024census" />

=== Immigration ===
As of the 2024 census, immigrants make up 8.2% of the population - 6.9% are from [[South America]], 1.1% are from [[North America]], 0.05% are from [[Europe]], 0.06% are from [[Asia]], 0.003% are from [[Africa]], and 0.004% are from [[Oceania]].<ref name="2024census" />

==Administration==
As a commune, San Ramón is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Gustavo Eduardo Toro Quintana ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]]). The communal council has the following members:

* Cristóbal Escobar Salinas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Mario Alarcón León ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Maricel Araya Peñaloza (Ind/[[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]])
* Fidel Contreras Cespedes ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Claudio Tapia Díaz ([[Popular Green Alliance Party|PAVP]])
* Fidel Castro Solís ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Estefany Ñanculef Beltrán (Ind/[[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Jacqueline Sandoval Rozas ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Ramón is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Mr. Tucapel Jiménez ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Mr. Iván Moreira ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 27th electoral district, (together with [[El Bosque, Chile|El Bosque]] and [[La Cisterna]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Soledad Alvear Valenzuela (PDC) and Pablo Longueira Montes (UDI) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
