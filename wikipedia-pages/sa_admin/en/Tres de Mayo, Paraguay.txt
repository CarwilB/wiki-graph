'''Tres de Mayo'''  is a municipality of [[Paraguay]] located in the outskirts of [[Caazapá]]. Formerly it was part of the district of [[Yuty]], but in 2012 it was elevated to the category of [[municipality]]. It has a total area of 743,25 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 17,928 inhabitants in 2016.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.bacn.gov.py/archivos/1264/20141027133154.pdf|title= Ley N.° 4.604|date= 2012|language=es}}</ref><ref name="DGEEC_1">{{cite web|url=http://www.dgeec.gov.py/Publicaciones/Biblioteca/proyeccion%20nacional/Proyeccion%20Distrital.pdf|title=Proyección de la población por sexo y edad, según distrito.
