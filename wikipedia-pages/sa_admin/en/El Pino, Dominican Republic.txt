'''El Pino''' is a community located on the [[Dajabón Province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://ciudadoriental.com/danilo-medina-entrega-1075-titulos-definitivos-de-propiedad-a-parceleros-de-el-pino-dajabon/|title=Danilo Medina entrega 1,075 títulos definitivos de propiedad a parceleros de El Pino, Dajabón|work=Ciudad Oriental|date=3 January 2019|access-date=8 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The most known and inhabited sector is el Sector Los Ramos, where the extensive Rodriguez-Ramos family and their descendents live.
