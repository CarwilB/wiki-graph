'''La Masica''' ({{IPA|es|la maˈsika}}) is a town, with a population of 5,975 (2013 census),<ref name=pop>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/atlantida/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the Department of Atlántida with at least 200 inhabitants</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Atlántida department|Atlántida]]. The largest town of the municipality is San Juan Pueblo, with a population of 8,983 (2013 census).<ref name=pop/>
