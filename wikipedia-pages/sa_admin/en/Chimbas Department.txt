'''Chimbas''' is a department of the province of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.canal13sanjuan.com/san-juan/el-peronismo-gano-en-12-de-los-19-departamentos-de-san-juan-_a68fec2cb3c9bd063e329c15f|title=El peronismo ganó en 12 de los 19 departamentos de San Juan|work=Diario 13 San Juan|date=26 October 2025|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is located on the south bank of the [[San Juan River (Argentina)|San Juan River]], and is part of greater [[San Juan, Argentina|San Juan]]. The department is thoroughly urbanized and [[Industrial sector|industrial]].

== Origin of name ==
This name comes from the [[Basque language]], and translates as "field outside the river" or "opposite side of the river."
