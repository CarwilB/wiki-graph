'''Paya''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 639 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 336; its population as of 2000 was 565.<ref name=inec/>  It lies on the Paya River, a tributary of the [[Tuira River]], and close to the [[Colombia–Panama border]].<ref name="washpost">Yeadon, David (16 August 1992). [https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/travel/1992/08/16/panama-gap-into-the-heart-of-the-darien-by-boat-and-on-foot/bf1561fd-118e-4f2e-89f3-ae66befe4972/ Panama GAp: Into the Heart of the Darien, By Boat and On Foot], ''[[The Washington Post]]''</ref>
