'''Chambellan''' ({{IPA|fr|ʃɑ̃bɛlɑ̃|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-DenisdeShawi-chambellan.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Chanbèlan}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jérémie Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 26,459 inhabitants in 2015.

Villages located within the commune include: [[Babino, Grand'Anse|Babino]], [[Cadette, Haiti|Cadette]], [[Grande Plaine]], [[Granger, Grand'Anse|Granger]], [[La Coude]] and [[Laterrière (Haïti)|Laterriere]].
