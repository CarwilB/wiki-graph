'''Mariscal Nieto''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] ''mariscal'' [[marshal]])  is the largest of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] that make up the [[Moquegua Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inei.gob.pe INEI] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/19970412040139/http://www.inei.gob.pe/ |date=1997-04-12 }}</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.muniperu.net IPAM]</ref> The capital of the province is the city of [[Moquegua]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': province of [[General Sánchez Cerro Province|General Sánchez Cerro]]
*'''East''': [[Tacna Region]]
*'''South''': province of [[Ilo Province|Ilo]]
*'''West''': [[Arequipa Region]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], however, is the language which the majority of the population (79.51%) learnt to speak in childhood, 15.65% of the residents started speaking using the [[Aymara language|Aymara]] language and  4.45% using [[Quechua language|Quechua]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
