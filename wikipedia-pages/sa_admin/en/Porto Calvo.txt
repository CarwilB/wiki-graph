'''Porto Calvo''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈpoʁtu ˈkawvu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Alagoas]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was 24,071 in 2022<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=IBGE. Porto Calvo |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/porto-calvo/panorama |access-date=2023-12-14 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|313|km2}}.<ref name=":0" /> It was founded in 1636.{{Citation needed|date=December 2022}}
