'''Iati''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[iaˈti]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located  at 286&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. It has an estimated population of 17,165 inhabitants ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]).<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Saloá]] (North); [[Alagoas]] state (South); [[Saloá]] and [[Bom Conselho]] (East); [[Águas Belas]] (West).
* Area - 635.14&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 487 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipanema River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 24.2 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 286&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Iati''' are in [[agribusiness]], especially  [[beans]], [[manioc]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[pigs]] and [[poultry]].
