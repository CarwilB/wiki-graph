'''Mezquital del Oro''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|190|km}} southwest of [[Zacatecas City]], the state capital.

==Geography==
The municipality of Mezquital del Oro lies at an elevation between {{convert|900|and(-)|2500|m}} in the [[Sierra Madre Occidental]] in southwestern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Trinidad García de la Cadena, Zacatecas|Trinidad García de la Cadena]] to the west, [[Teúl de González Ortega Municipality|Teúl de González Ortega]] to the north, [[Juchipila]] to the northeast, and [[Moyahua de Estrada]] to the east. It also borders the Jaliscan municipalities of [[Ixtlahuacán del Río]] to the south and [[San Cristóbal de la Barranca]] to the southwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32028.pdf |title=Mezquítal del Oro, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|486.5|km2}} and comprises 0.6% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 5.5% of the land in Mezquital del Oro is used for agriculture. The remainder of the land comprises tropical forest (53.6%), temperate forest (23.6), and grassland (17.2%). The municipality is situated in the drainage basin of the [[Juchipila River]], a tributary of the [[Río Grande de Santiago]]. Much of the municipality lies in the canyon of the [[Mezquital River]], a tributary of the Juchipila.<ref name=compendium/>

Mezquital del Oro's climate ranges from temperate to tropical depending on elevation, with dry winters in either case. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|26|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Mezquital del Oro is located in territory that was inhabited by the [[Caxcan]] people prior to Spanish contact.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32028a.html |title=Mezquital del Oro |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The town of Mezquital del Oro was founded in the 1730s after gold was discovered there.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |last=Amador |first=Elias |title=Elementos de geografia del estado de Zacatecas |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=AUwTAAAAYAAJ |publisher=Tip. de la Escuela de artes y oficios en Guadalupe |location=Zacatecas |date=1894 |page=89 |language=es |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> Mesquital was named as a district in the ''partido'' of Juchipila in 1825. When ''partidos'' were abolished in 1916, Mezquital del Oro became an independent municipality.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> Gold production peaked in the 1910s, and continued until the last mine was abandoned in 1940.<ref>{{cite web |last=Ramírez |first=Rocío |date=11 December 2019 |url=https://imagenzac.com.mx/municipios/quieren-explotar-una-mina-de-oro-en-mezquital-del-oro/ |title=Quieren explotar una mina de oro en Mezquital del Oro |language=es |website=Zacatecas en Imagen |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Mezquital del Oro comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''),<ref name=encyclopedia/> and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://igualdad.ine.mx/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Lineamientos-de-Paridad-Zacatecas.pdf |title=Lineamientos para el registro de candidaturas a cargos de elección popular de los partidos políticos y coaliciones |language=es |pages=12–13 |date=27 November 2017 |publisher=Instituto Electoral del Estado de Zacatecas |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The current president of the municipality is Mónica Rodarte Dávila.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Mezquital del Oro recorded a population of 2451 inhabitants living in 735 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=70–71 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=2 January 2022}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 2584 inhabitants in Mezquital del Oro.<ref name=snim/>

There are 52 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Mezquital del Oro, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=028 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Mezquital del Oro |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> It recorded a population of 1136 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Nowadays, the main economic activity in Mezquital del Oro is agriculture.<ref name=encyclopedia/> In the 2015 Intercensal Survey, 40% of Mezquital del Oro's workforce was employed in the primary sector, 20% in the secondary sector, 7% in commerce, and 32% in services.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 10.5: Población ocupada por municipio y su distribución porcentual según sector de actividad económica, al 15 de marzo de 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Zacatecas 2017 |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092047.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref> The main crops grown in Mezquital del Oro are agave, forage grasses, and corn (grain and silage).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Mezquital-del-Oro.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 028 Mezquital del Oro |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |date=17 June 2021 |access-date=2 January 2022}}</ref>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 23|Federal Highway 23]] runs through the westernmost corner of the municipality, connecting it to [[Jerez de García Salinas|Jerez]] and [[Fresnillo]] in the north, and [[Zapopan]] and [[Guadalajara]] in the south.
