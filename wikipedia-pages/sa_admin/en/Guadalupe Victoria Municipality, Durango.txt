'''Guadalupe Victoria''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ciudad Guadalupe Victoria]]. The municipality covers an area of 767.10&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 36,695.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guadalupe Victoria|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=008|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=21 October 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151021171221/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=008|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Guadalupe Victoria had a population of 16,506.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Guadalupe Victoria, the municipality had 92 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Antonio Amaro (Saucillo), Durango|Antonio Amaro (Saucillo)]] (3,526), [[Ignacio Allende, Durango|Ignacio Allende]] (2,588), [[Ignacio Ramírez, Durango|Ignacio Ramírez]] (2,432), classified as urban, and [[General Calixto Contreras (Colorada), Durango|General Calixto Contreras (Colorada)]] (2,213), [[Felipe Carrillo Puerto (Tarabillas), Durango|Felipe Carrillo Puerto (Tarabillas)]] (2,019), and [[José Guadalupe Rodríguez (Peñuelas), Durango|José Guadalupe Rodríguez (Peñuelas)]] (1,374), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
