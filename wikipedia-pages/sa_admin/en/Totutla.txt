[[File:Cabalgata_en_Totutla.jpg | thumb | right | alt=Traditional Horseback Parade in Honor of Saint James the Apostle on July 25th in the municipality of Totutla | Traditional Horseback Parade in Honor of Saint James the Apostle on July 25th,  Totutla]]
'''Totutla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the south zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 40&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 80.61 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|13|N|96|58|W|}}. The name comes from the language Náhuatl, Toto-tlan; that means "Among the birds".

==Geography==

The municipality of ''' Totutla ''' is bordered to the north by [[Tenampa]], to the north-east by [[Tlacotepec de Mejía]] and to the south-west by [[Chiapas]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30188a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Totutla |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-17}}</ref>

===Climate===
The weather in Totutla is cold and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[coffee]] and [[sugarcane]].

==Celebrations==

In ''' Totutla ''', in July takes place the celebration in honor to Santiago Apostol, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.
