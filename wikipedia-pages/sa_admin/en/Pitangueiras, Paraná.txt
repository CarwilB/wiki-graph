'''Pitangueiras''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 3,262 (2020 est.) in an area of 123&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8EU Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 660 m.
