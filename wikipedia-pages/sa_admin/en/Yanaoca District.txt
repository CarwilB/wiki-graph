'''Yanaoca District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Canas Province]] in [[Peru]]. Its seat is Yanaoca.

== Geography ==

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (88.77%) learnt to speak in childhood, 10.90% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población</ref>
