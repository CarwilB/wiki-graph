'''Villa Aldama''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central zone in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 33&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 78.96&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|39|N|97|14|W|}}. The municipality takes this name in honor of Don [[Juan Aldama]], who was one of the revolutionary commanders in the [[Mexican War of Independence]].

==Geography==

The municipality of Villa Aldama is bordered to the north by [[Las Minas, Veracruz|Las Minas]] and [[Altotonga (municipality)|Altotonga]], to the east by [[Las Vigas de Ramírez|Las Vigas]] and to the south by [[Perote (municipality)|Perote]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30194a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Villa Aldama |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-17}}</ref>

The weather in Villa Aldama is cold and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[potato]]es.

==Celebrations==
Every July, a festival is held to celebrate Saint John the Apostle, patron of the town, and in December there is a festival held in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].
