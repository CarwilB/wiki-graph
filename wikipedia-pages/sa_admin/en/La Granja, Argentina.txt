'''La Granja''' is a town located in the [[Colón Department, Córdoba|Colón Department]] of the [[Córdoba Province, Argentina|province of Córdoba]] in central Argentina, north of the provincial capital [[Córdoba, Argentina|Córdoba]], on the northern end of the [[Sierras de Córdoba#Geography|Sierras Chicas]] mountain range, making it a tourist destination for inner-Argentine tourism.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-29 |title=La Granja |url=https://cordobaturismo.gov.ar/localidades/la-granja/ |access-date=2026-05-26 |language=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The town is located about 53 km north of the city of Córdoba and the provincial road E53 runs through the town, which connects it to the towns of [[Agua de Oro]] to the south and [[Ascochinga]] to the north. In addition to the town center, the localities of Villa Ani Mi and Las Vertientes de la Granja are part of the municipality.

The Tiú Mayu stream flows through the town. Parts of the bed have been declared as a urban natural reserve, providing a short hiking trail.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-29 |title=La Granja – Reserva Tiú Mayú – Córdoba Turismo |url=https://cordobaturismo.gov.ar/experiencias/la-granja-reserva-tiu-mayu/ |access-date=2026-05-26 |language=es}}</ref>

== Population ==
In the 2010 census, the population of La Granja was 2,456 according to the INDEC.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-cordoba.php?cityid=14021160 |title=ARGENTINA: La Granja |publisher=City Population |date=26 December 2013 |accessdate=28 December 2021}}</ref>
By the 2022 census, this number had grown to 4805 inhabitants. 

== Locality ==
Tourism is one of the main sources of economic activity in the town, which offers in particular nature related activities around the river and mountains.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipalidad de La Granja |url=https://www.lagranja.gob.ar/turismo |access-date=2026-05-26 |website=www.lagranja.gob.ar}}</ref> Along the river Tiú Mayu, various natural pools and beaches exist, alongside the La Toma balneario which additionally offers spots for barbecues.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Playas y Balnearios de La Granja, Córdoba, Sierras Chicas, Turismo, Caminatas, Campings, Río, Verano, Tres Cascadas, Balneario La Toma, Naturaleza, Paisajes, Vacaciones, Viajes |url=https://www.turismocordoba.com.ar/lagranja/playas.php |access-date=2026-05-27 |website=www.turismocordoba.com.ar}}</ref> 

The town has a health center with specialists offering appointments on different days<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipalidad de La Granja |url=https://www.lagranja.gob.ar/salud |access-date=2026-05-27 |website=www.lagranja.gob.ar}}</ref> and offers its own public transport service called "Munibus".<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipalidad de La Granja |url=https://www.lagranja.gob.ar/transporte |access-date=2026-05-27 |website=www.lagranja.gob.ar}}</ref>

The town also its own cooperative for providing public services, which was founded in 1952 and also covers the communities in Las Vertientes, Villa Ani Mi and Los Molles.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Historia institucional – Cooperativa de La Granja |url=https://cooplagranja.com/historia-institucional/ |access-date=2026-05-27 |language=es}}</ref> 

Since 1995, the town also has its own volunteer firefighter group.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bomberos Voluntarios de La Granja |url=https://www.bomberosra.org.ar/bomberos/267-bomberos-voluntarios-de-la-granja |access-date=2026-05-27 |website=Bomberos Voluntarios de Argentina |language=es}}</ref>
