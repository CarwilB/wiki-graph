'''Centenário''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].  As of 2022, the estimated population was 2,779.<ref name=":0">{{cite web | title=Centenário (RS) &#124; Cidades e Estados &#124; IBGE | url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/rs/centenario.html }}</ref> Centenário became a municipality on the 20th of March 1992.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |url=https://www.centenario.rs.gov.br/pg.php?area=HISTORIA |access-date=2025-07-16 |website=www.centenario.rs.gov.br}}</ref> Its current mayor is Genoir Marcos Florek.<ref name=":0" /> Centenário's culture has been influenced over time by the high amounts of Polish immigrants who have moved to the region. One such example is the creation of the International Pierogi Festival, held annually.<ref name=":1" />
