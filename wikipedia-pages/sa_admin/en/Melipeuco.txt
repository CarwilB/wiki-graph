'''Melipeuco''' ({{IPA|es|meliˈpewko}}) is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] ({{langx|es|comuna}}) in [[Chile]], located at the foot of the [[Andes]], in the [[Cautín Province|Province of Cautín]], [[Araucanía Region]]. Melipeuco is {{convert|12|km|0|abbr=on}} from the southern entrance of the [[Conguillío National Park]]. The [[Nevados de Sollipulli]], an ice-filled volcanic [[caldera]] surrounded by numerous hot water springs and geysers, lie south of the town. Lake Carilafquén is also located in this area. At the edge of the Nevados de Sollipulli, at the Alpehue intersection {{convert|33|km|0|abbr=on}} away, there are several hot springs with water from {{convert|40|to|70|C|F}}, even in the middle of glacier blocks.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Melipeuco spans an area of {{convert|1107.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 5,628 inhabitants (2,908 men and 2,720 women). Of these, 2,333 (41.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,295 (58.5%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 5.9% (315 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Melipeuco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Eduardo Navarrete Fuentes ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Melipeuco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Enrique Estay (UDI) and Fuad Chahín ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 49th electoral district, together with [[Victoria, Chile|Victoria]], [[Curacautín]], [[Lonquimay]], [[Vilcún]], [[Lautaro, Chile|Lautaro]], [[Perquenco]] and [[Galvarino, Chile|Galvarino]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alberto Espina Otero ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Jaime Quintana Leal ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 14th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-North).
