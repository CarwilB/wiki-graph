'''Bavispe''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web |title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=31 July 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=4 January 2010 }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of 1,723&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="sede">{{cite web|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/zap/datGenerales.aspx?entra=nacion&ent=26&mun=015|title=Bavispe|work=Cédulas de Información Municipal|publisher=[[Secretariat of Welfare (Mexico)|SEDESOL]]|access-date=28 December 2021|language=es|archive-date=7 February 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170207031258/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/zap/datGenerales.aspx?entra=nacion&ent=26&mun=015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 1,454.<ref name=sede/> The municipality has 12 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Bavispe]] (701), classified as urban; and San Miguelito (456), La Mora (218), La Galerita (64), La Vega Azul (5), Oaxaca (3), Los Caballos (2), La Galera (1), Tasabiri (1), El Fuste (1) and Las Cuevas (1), classified as rural.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=26&mun=015|title=Bavispe|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretariat of Welfare (Mexico)|SEDESOL]]|access-date=28 December 2021|language=es|archive-date=7 February 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170207031240/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=26&mun=015|url-status=dead}}</ref>

By the time of the 2020 Census, the municipality's population had fallen to 1,169.<ref>{{cite web |title=Bavispe: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/bavispe |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |accessdate=2 January 2025}}</ref>

==Geography==
Bavispe Municipality borders municipalities in Sonora on the south, west and north, and municipalities in [[Chihuahua (state)|Chihuahua]] on its east.
