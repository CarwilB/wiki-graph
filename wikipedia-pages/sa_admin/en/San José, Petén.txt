'''San José''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Petén Department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/acto-de-inauguracion-del-nuevo-palacio-municipal-en-san-jose-peten/|title=Acto de inauguración del nuevo Palacio Municipal en San José, Petén|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=1 February 2026|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It contains 3,602 people. It lies on the north shore of [[Lake Petén Itzá]] and is located a few kilometers from the [[Mesoamerican chronology|Classic Period]] [[Maya civilization|Maya]] ruin of [[Motul de San José]]. [[El Zotz]] is also located in the municipality.

==Sports==
The town is home to [[Heredia Jaguares de Peten]] of the [[Liga Nacional de Fútbol de Guatemala]], the top level in Guatemalan football since it moved from [[Morales, Guatemala|Morales]] in summer 2008.
