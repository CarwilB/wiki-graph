'''Cruz Alta''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The town is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Cruz Alta]]. Was founded on August 18, 1821.

The [[municipality]] is known to have given rise to many other municipalities as [[Ijuí]], [[Santa Maria, Rio Grande do Sul|Santa Maria]], [[Passo Fundo]], Santo Angelo and their initial territory led to more other, became known by the northwestern mother.<ref name="Data de criação dos municípios">{{cite web|url=http://www.fee.rs.gov.br/feedados/consulta/data_criacao.asp|title=Data de criação dos municípios do RS
|publisher=Fundação de Economia e Estatística
|author=FEE
|access-date=February 11, 2013
}}</ref>
The [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] belongs to the Northwest Mesoregion Riograndense and Microregion Cruz Alta. It is located at [[latitude]] 28 º 38 '19 "south and [[longitude]] 53 º 36' 23" [[west]], with an average elevation of 452 meters above sea level. Access to the city is by [[BR-158]], in the north-south axis, the [[BR-377]] to the [[east]], and also by the [[RS-342]] on the [[west]].

The location of the [[city]] is logistically important, being considered as a major trunk road and rail in the [[north]] central part of the [[States of Brazil|state]], with the presence of a dry port in the northeast of the city.

==Geography==
===Climate===
Cruz Alta experiences a [[humid subtropical climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfa'') with hot summers, mild winters, and ample rainfall throughout the year.
