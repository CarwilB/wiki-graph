'''Requena''' is one of the eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Loreto Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was created by Law No. 9815 on July 2, 1943 by president [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de Requena, ''[http://www.munirequena.gob.pe/index.php?opc1=2 Reseña] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080202033617/http://www.munirequena.gob.pe/index.php?opc1=2 |date=2008-02-02 }}''. Retrieved on November 5, 2007.</ref> Its territory is part of the [[Amazon rainforest]]; as such, it is characterized by high temperatures, heavy rainfall and a wide diversity of plants and animals.<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de Requena, ''[http://www.munirequena.gob.pe/index.php?opc1=turi Requena: el boom del turismo ecológico]''. Retrieved on November 5, 2007.</ref>

==Political division==
The province is divided into eleven districts.

* [[Alto Tapiche District|Alto Tapiche]] ([[Santa Elena, Requena Province|Santa Elena]])
* [[Capelo District|Capelo]] ([[Flor de Punga]])
* [[Emilio San Martín District|Emilio San Martín]] ([[Tamanco]])
* [[Jenaro Herrera District|Jenaro Herrera]] ([[Jenaro Herrera]])
* [[Maquia District|Maquia]] ([[Santa Isabel, Requena Province|Santa Isabel]])
* [[Puinahua District|Puinahua]] ([[Bretaña, Peru|Bretaña]])
* [[Requena District|Requena]] ([[Requena, Loreto|Requena]])
* [[Saquena District|Saquena]] ([[Bagazan]])
* [[Soplin District|Soplin]] ([[Nueva Alejandría]])
* [[Tapiche District|Tapiche]] ([[Iberia, Requena Province|Iberia]])
* [[Yaquerana District|Yaquerana]] ([[Angamos, Requena|Angamos]])
