'''Empedrado''' ({{IPA|es|empeˈðɾaðo}}) is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Talca Province]] of [[Chile]]'s [[Maule Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Empedrado spans an area of {{convert|564.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 4,225 inhabitants (2,222 men and 2,003 women). Of these, 2,499 (59.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 1,726 (40.9%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 7.2% (329 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Empedrado is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Gonzalo Tejos Perez.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Empedrado is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Pencahue]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], [[Pelarco]], [[Río Claro, Chile|Río Claro]] and [[San Rafael, Chile|San Rafael]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
