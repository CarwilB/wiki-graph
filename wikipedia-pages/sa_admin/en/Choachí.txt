'''Choachí''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The municipality borders [[La Calera, Cundinamarca|La Calera]] in the north, [[Ubaque]] in the south, in the east [[Fómeque]] and westward of Choachí is the Colombian capital [[Bogotá]]. The town centre is located at {{convert|38|km|mi}} from the centre of the capital.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.choachi-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website - information Choachí] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170618184905/http://www.choachi-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2017-06-18 }} - accessed 29-04-2016</ref> Within the boundaries of Choachí the [[Páramo de Cruz Verde]] is situated.

== Etymology ==
The name Choachí is derived from the original name in [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]: ''Chi-gua-chí'', which means "Our Mountain of the Moon". The [[Muisca people|Muisca]] had a strong lunar cult and worshipped the goddess of the Moon, [[Chía (goddess)|Chía]] in various places across their territories. It is believed that in Choachí a place of worship to the Moon was located.<ref name=website/>

== Geography ==
Choachí is home to the tallest waterfall in Colombia, La Chorrera, which has a height of 590 m (~1940&nbsp;ft).<ref>{{Cite web |title=La Chorrera Waterfall_Colombia |url=http://www.holidaytravel.cc/Article/travelguides/southamerica/colombia/201602/68850.html |access-date=2023-05-01 |website=www.holidaytravel.cc}}</ref>

== History ==
The area of Choachí was inhabited by the southern [[Muisca people|Muisca]], part of the [[Muisca Confederation]] [[Muisca rulers|ruled]] by the ''[[zipa]]'' of [[Bogotá|Bacatá]]. The modern foundation of the town was early in the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], in 1560, by Antonio Bermúdez, a [[conquistador]] who arrived as part of the army of [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]]. In 1601 a parish was constructed in Choachí.<ref name=website/>

Between the 17th and 19th century the original [[indigenous peoples of Colombia|indigenous population]] almost completely perished.<ref name=website/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Choachí are agriculture, [[livestock]] farming.  Also tourism, mainly from the capital, is a source of income for the local people.<ref name=website/>

== Geology ==
In the area of Choachí [[Cretaceous]] [[sandstone]]s and the organic-rich [[shale]]s of the [[Fómeque Formation]] are [[outcrop]]ping.<ref name=website/>

== Archeology ==
In the first half of the 20th century within Choachí the [[Choachí Stone]] has been found, possibly in a grave. The stone, carved in [[lydite]] may represent the [[Muisca calendar]], a complex [[lunisolar calendar]] used by the Muisca.<ref>Izquierdo, 2009, p. 86</ref> Also [[petroglyph]]s have been discovered in Choachí.
