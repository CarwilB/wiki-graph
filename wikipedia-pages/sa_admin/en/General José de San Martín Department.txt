'''General José de San Martín''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|16257|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with [[Bolivia]], to the east with [[Rivadavia Department, Salta|Rivadavia]], and to the south and west with [[Orán Department|Orán]].
