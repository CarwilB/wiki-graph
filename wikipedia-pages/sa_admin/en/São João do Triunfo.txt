'''São João do Triunfo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the interior of the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. Situated in southeastern Paraná, it is 106 km southwest of the state capital, [[Curitiba]]. The municipality covers an area of {{formatnum:720.407}} km², of which {{formatnum:0.3682}} km² is within the [[urban area]]. According to the 2010 census conducted by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], its population was {{formatnum:13704}} inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010">{{cite web|url=https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/Tabela/608#resultado|date=November 29, 2010|access-date=December 11, 2010|work=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática - SIDRA|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|title=Tabela 608 - População residente, por situação de domicílio e sexo - Sinopse|archive-date=December 28, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161228000330/https://sidra.ibge.gov.br/tabela/608#resultado}}</ref>

The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{formatnum:17.4}} °C. Its predominant [[vegetation]] is the [[Araucaria moist forests|mixed ombrophilous forest]]. In 2009, 29.54% of its inhabitants lived in the urban area, and the municipality had eleven healthcare facilities. Its [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) is 0.679, which is considered to be medium for the state.

The first expedition to the region where São João do Triunfo is located occurred in 1845, when pioneers and their families sought land to settle. In 1864, the allocation of areas for the delimitation of the Rio da Vargem Parish began. Predominantly an agricultural municipality, its most significant products are [[tobacco]], [[yerba mate]], [[maize]], and [[bean]]s, with additional cultivation of soybeans, [[wheat]], and [[potato]]es. The municipality has one administrative district, Palmira. Established by State Law No. 13 on January 8, 1890, and implemented on February 15 of the same year, it was emancipated from [[Palmeira, Paraná|Palmeira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.educadores.diaadia.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=490|title=São João do Triunfo - Educadores|access-date=November 5, 2022|website=www.educadores.diaadia.pr.gov.br|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211208042006/http://www.educadores.diaadia.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=490|archive-date=December 8, 2021}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name honors the [[patron saint]] [[John the Baptist|Saint John the Baptist]] and João Nunes de Souza, the founder of the locality.<ref name=":0">{{Cite book|last=Ferreira|first=João Carlos Vicente|title=O Paraná e seus municípios|publisher=Editora Memória Brasileira|year=1996|location=Maringá|page=638}}</ref> The adjective ''do Triunfo'' was added due to the success achieved by the brave explorers of the Rio da Vargem region. The name João comes from the Hebrew name ''Yohanan'', which means "God's grace."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.etymonline.com/index.php?term=John&allowed_in_frame=0|title=John|access-date=April 20, 2016|publisher=Online Etymology Dictionary|last=Harper|first=Douglas|archive-date=March 24, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160324035714/http://www.etymonline.com/index.php?term=john&allowed_in_frame=0}}</ref> Saint John the Baptist announced the arrival of the [[Messiah]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://verdadeemfoco.com.br/estudo.php?id=97|title=João Batista, O Mensageiro Precursor do Prometido Messias|access-date=April 20, 2016|publisher=Verdade em foco|archive-date=February 17, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160217144321/http://verdadeemfoco.com.br/estudo.php?id=97}}</ref>

== History ==
=== Origins and settlement ===
[[File:São João do Triunfo PR Brasil Casarão histórico (2).jpg|left|thumb|Historical mansion from the late 19th century.]]
The region of São João do Triunfo began to be settled in 1864. It all began when João Nunes de Souza, who lived in [[São José dos Pinhais]], planned to go hunting in the hinterlands. An avid hunter, João de Souza set out from his homeland, traveled along the [[Iguazu River]], and climbed along the banks of the Rio da Vargem to a certain point.<ref name=":1">{{Cite book|last=Ferreira|first=João Carlos Vicente|title=O Paraná e seus municípios|publisher=Editora Memória Brasileira|year=1996|location=Maringá|pages=637–638}}</ref>

There was an abundance of wild game, lush forests, abundant waters, and fertile soil, which captivated the explorer, who decided to settle there. His entire family accompanied him, facing all the dangers inherent in such an endeavor, but they survived. They explored the region, began farming, built trails in the forest, and promoted the area as much as possible.<ref name=":1" />

In 1867, Antonio Dotes arrived at Rio da Vargem (the former name of the area) with other people, and together they built a small village.<ref name=":1" />

Deeply religious, the local population erected a chapel, where the image of [[John the Baptist|Saint John the Baptist]] was enshrined. Several residences were built around the small church, primarily on land donated by the community’s founder. It was not long before the village’s name changed from Rio da Vargem to São João do Triunfo.<ref name=":1" />

=== Administrative formation ===
[[File:São João do Triunfo PR Brasil Antiga Prefeitura Municipal.jpg|left|thumb|Former City Hall, built in 1927.]]
With the new name, on March 16, 1871, through Paraná Provincial Law No. 254, São João do Triunfo was elevated to the status of ''[[freguesia]]''. Through Paraná State Law No. 13, dated January 8, 1890, during the [[First Brazilian Republic|Old Republic]], the municipality of São João do Triunfo was established, with territory emancipated from [[Palmeira, Paraná|Palmeira]]. It was officially implemented on February 15, 1890, with the inauguration of the mayor and councilors.<ref name=":1" />

The territory of São João do Triunfo includes the district of Palmira, which welcomed [[Polish Brazilians|Polish immigrants]] in the early 20th century through the Rio dos Patos and Brumado colonies. Palmira became a municipality on April 10, 1909, through State Law No. 874, and in 1920, it had a population of 2,423 inhabitants. Later, the municipality of Palmira was dissolved and incorporated into São João do Triunfo.<ref name=":1" />

Through Paraná State Law No. 93, dated September 14, 1948, the municipality was elevated to the status of seat of the [[judicial district]], with its implementation on January 25, 1949.<ref name=":1" />

== Geography ==
[[File:São João do Triunfo PR Brasil Rio da Vargem (2).jpg|left|thumb|The Rio da Vargem, a tributary of the [[Iguazu River]], runs through the municipality from north to south.]]

According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], the municipality’s area is {{formatnum:720.407}} km², of which {{formatnum:0.3682}} km² constitutes the [[urban area]].<ref name="Embrapa_PR">{{cite web|url=http://www.urbanizacao.cnpm.embrapa.br/conteudo/uf/pr.html|title=Paraná|access-date=July 25, 2012|archive-url=|archive-date=|author=Embrapa Monitoramento por Satélite}}</ref> It is located at 25°40′58″ [[Latitude|south latitude]] and 50°17′49″ [[Longitude|west longitude]], and it is 106 kilometers southwest of the [[Curitiba|state capital]]. Its neighboring municipalities are: [[Palmeira, Paraná|Palmeira]] ([[North|N]]); [[São Mateus do Sul]] and [[Antônio Olinto, Paraná|Antônio Olinto]] ([[South|S]]); [[Lapa, Paraná|Lapa]] ([[East|E]]); and [[Rebouças]] and [[Fernandes Pinheiro]] ([[West|W]]).<ref name=":2" />

According to the [[Intermediate and Immediate Geographic Regions|regional division]] in effect since 2017, established by the IBGE,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias-novoportal/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/2231-np-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |title=Divisão Regional do Brasil |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |access-date=February 10, 2019 |archive-url= |archive-date=}}</ref> the municipality belongs to the Intermediate and Immediate Geographical Regions of Ponta Grossa.<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2017">{{cite web |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2017/DTB_2017.zip |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2017 |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |access-date=February 10, 2019 |archive-date=November 3, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201103132852/ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2017/DTB_2017.zip }}</ref> Previously, under the division into [[Microregions of Brazil|microregions]] and [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregions]], it was part of the São Mateus do Sul microregion, which was included in the Southeastern Paraná mesoregion.<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web |url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |access-date=February 10, 2019 |archive-date=November 3, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201103132852/ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip }}</ref>

The municipality of São João do Triunfo is part of the Second Paraná Plateau, also known as the Ponta Grossa Plateau, where the [[terrain]] is undulating, and the average altitude ranges from 1,200 to 300 meters above sea level.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=August 9, 2014|publisher=InfoEscola|archive-url=|archive-date=|url=http://www.infoescola.com/parana/geografia-do-parana/|title=Geografia do Paraná|author=Thais Pacievitch}}</ref> The average altitude of the municipal seat is {{formatnum:840}} meters, one of the highest in the state,<ref name="Embrapa_PR" /> and the predominant [[Geologic time scale|geological age]] of the soil belongs to the [[Permian]] period.<ref>{{cite web|date=2006|access-date=August 9, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110310220245/http://www.mineropar.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=26|archive-date=2011-03-10|url=http://www.mineropar.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=26|title=Idades geológicas|author=Secretaria da Indústria, do Comércio e Assuntos do Mercosul do Paraná}}</ref>

The predominant vegetation is the [[Atlantic Forest]],<ref>{{cite web|access-date=August 9, 2014|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/723|title=Infográficos: dados gerais|author=Cidades@ - IBGE}}</ref> with remaining Atlantic forest reserves covering {{formatnum:13288}} hectares in 2011, or 18.4% of the total municipal area.<ref>{{cite web|date=August 20, 2012|access-date=August 9, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140507044742/http://www.sosma.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/Atlas_municipio_completo2012.pdf|archive-date=2014-05-07|url=http://www.sosma.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2012/08/Atlas_municipio_completo2012.pdf|title=Atlas dos Remanescentes Florestais da Mata Atlântica|author=[[SOS Mata Atlântica Foundation]]|page=10}}</ref> The main waterway that runs through the municipal territory is the Rio da Vargem.<ref name=":2">{{Cite map|author=Nonoya Filho|first=José|title=São João do Triunfo|map=Paraná: rodoviário, político, turístico, escolar|scale=1:750,000|publisher=Editora Trieste|location=São Paulo|access-date=}}</ref>

The climate of São João do Triunfo is classified, according to the IBGE, as [[Oceanic climate|oceanic]] (type Cfb according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif|title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification|access-date=March 23, 2014|publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070316010128/http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif|archive-date=2007-03-16|author=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification}}</ref> January, the warmest month, has an average temperature of {{formatnum:21.5}} °C, with a maximum of {{formatnum:27.3}} °C and a minimum of {{formatnum:15.8}} °C. July, the coldest month, has an average temperature of {{formatnum:12.8}} °C, with a maximum of {{formatnum:19.2}} °C and a minimum of {{formatnum:6.5}} °C. [[Autumn]] and [[Spring (season)|spring]] are transitional seasons.<ref name="Clima" />

The average annual precipitation is {{formatnum:1451.0}} mm, with July being the driest month, receiving {{formatnum:72.0}} mm. February, the wettest month, averages {{formatnum:185.0}} mm. In recent years, however, hot and dry days have become increasingly frequent during winter, often exceeding {{formatnum:30}} °C, especially between July and September. [[Frost]]s are common in winter, and between July 22 and 23, 2013, [[freezing rain]] was recorded in the city.<ref>{{cite web|date=July 23, 2013|access-date=May 22, 2016|last=Redação|url=http://www.parana-online.com.br/editoria/cidades/news/683241/?noticia=GEADA+ENTRA+NO+LUGAR+DA+NEVE+NESTA+QUARTA+FEIRA+NO+PARANA|title=Geada entra no lugar da neve nesta quarta-feira no Paraná|work=Paraná Online |archive-date=August 10, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140810024137/http://www.parana-online.com.br/editoria/cidades/news/683241/?noticia=GEADA+ENTRA+NO+LUGAR+DA+NEVE+NESTA+QUARTA+FEIRA+NO+PARANA}}</ref>

== Politics and administration ==
The municipal administration is carried out by the [[Executive (government)|executive]] and [[Legislature|legislative]] powers.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074|date=February 9, 2006|access-date=February 22, 2015|publisher=JusVi|author=Flávio Henrique M. Lima|archive-url=|archive-date=|title=O Poder Público Municipal à frente da obrigação constitucional de criação do sistema de controle interno}}</ref> The representative of the executive power of São João do Triunfo, elected in the [[2024 Brazilian municipal elections|2024 municipal elections]], was Mário Cezar da Silva of the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]], who won a total of {{formatnum:5621}} votes (60.63% of the voters), with Cristian Borges as [[vice-mayor]].<ref name="prefeito">{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/pr/campos-gerais-sul/eleicoes/2024/noticia/2024/10/07/eleicoes-2024-mario-cezar-do-pt-e-eleito-prefeito-de-sao-joao-do-triunfo-no-1o-turno.ghtml|title=Eleições 2024: Mario Cezar, do PT, é eleito prefeito de São João do Triunfo no 1º turno {{!}} Eleições 2024 em Campos Gerais e Sul - PR|date=October 7, 2024|access-date=March 17, 2025|website=G1|language=pt-br}}</ref>

The legislative power, in turn, is constituted by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of 9 councilors elected for four-year terms (in accordance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]])<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm|access-date=February 22, 2015|author=DJI|archive-url=|archive-date=|title=Constituição Federal - CF - 1988 / Art. 29}}</ref> and consists of two seats for the [[Republicans (Brazil)|Republicans]], two for the [[Progressistas]], two for the [[Green Party (Brazil)|Green Party]], one seat for the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]], one for the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]], one for [[Solidarity (Brazil)|Solidarity]], and one for the [[Liberal Party (Brazil, 2006)|Liberal Party]]. It is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for the administration and the executive, especially the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).<ref name="prefeito" />

There were {{formatnum:11082}} voters in April 2016, representing 0.142% of the state’s total.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleitor/estatisticas-de-eleitorado/consulta-quantitativo|date=April 12, 2013|access-date=February 22, 2015|author=[[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE)|title=Consulta Quantitativo|archive-date=March 25, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150325082908/http://www.tse.jus.br/eleitor/estatisticas-de-eleitorado/consulta-quantitativo}}</ref> The municipality of São João do Triunfo is governed by an [[organic law]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.controlemunicipal.com.br/inga/sistema/arquivos/1034/021215100145_lei_organica_sjtrinfo_pdf.pdf|title=Lei Orgânica do Município de São João do Triunfo|access-date=May 24, 2016|publisher=Controle Municipal|archive-date=November 3, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201103132816/http://www.controlemunicipal.com.br/inga/sistema/arquivos/1034/021215100145_lei_organica_sjtrinfo_pdf.pdf}}</ref> and is the only municipality under the jurisdiction of the homonymous [[Comarca|judicial district]], of which it is the seat.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.planejamento.mppr.mp.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=2181|title=Comarca de São João do Triunfo|access-date=May 30, 2016|publisher=Subprocuradoria-Geral da Justiça para Assuntos de Planejamento Institucional do Ministério Público do Paraná|archive-date=July 1, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160701100413/http://www.planejamento.mppr.mp.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=2181}}</ref> It is subdivided into two districts, namely the seat and Palmira.<ref name=":0" />

== Subdivisions ==
When it was emancipated in 1890, São João do Triunfo was made up of several villages. The first to separate and later become a municipality was [[São Mateus do Sul]]. Other cities, such as [[Porto Amazonas]], were also part of the territory. After São João do Triunfo became a municipality, [[Rio Azul, Paraná|Rio Azul]] and [[Rebouças]] also separated. On April 10, 1909, the district of Palmira was created, and in the territorial division of July 1, 1960, which is still in effect, São João do Triunfo consists of two districts: the seat and Palmira.<ref name=":1" /><ref name="IBGE_Histórico" />

The [[urban area]] of the municipality consists only of the Centro, with no other neighborhoods. The [[rural area]] is divided into several farms, villages, and communities, listed as follows: Avencal, Santana, Faxinal dos Fabrícios, Faxinal Cachoeira, Mangueirinha, Bituva dos Machados, Cachoeira, Vitorinópolis, Rio Baio Terceiro, Pinhalzinho, Papuã, Faxinal do Louro, Serrinha, Morro Grande, Boa Vista, Bolo Grande, Taquaruçu, Solidão, Lajinha, Guaiaca, Colônia Bromado, Coxilhão de Santa Rosa, Coxilhão do Meio, Coxilhão das Ameixeiras, Faxinal dos Pereiras, Faxinal dos Rodrigues, Ponte Nova, Mato Queimado, Floresta São Paulo, Porto Feliz, Meia Lua, Barra Bonita, Água Branca, Poços, Salto, Monte Alegre, Fundão, Rodeio, Ladeira, Jacu, and Colônia Lagoa.<ref>{{cite web|url=ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_para_fins_de_levantamentos_estatisticos/contagem_da_populacao_e_censo_agropecuario_2007/mapas_municipais_estatisticos/pr/sao_joao_do_triunfo.pdf|title=São João do Triunfo|date=2007|access-date=January 6, 2017|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística|last=IBGE|archive-date=November 3, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201103132835/ftp://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/cartas_e_mapas/mapas_para_fins_de_levantamentos_estatisticos/contagem_da_populacao_e_censo_agropecuario_2007/mapas_municipais_estatisticos/pr/sao_joao_do_triunfo.pdf}}</ref>

== Economy ==
In the [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP) of São João do Triunfo, the agriculture and service sectors stand out. According to 2016 [[IBGE]] data, the municipality’s GDP was {{BRL|456012.56}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2016">{{cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/sao-joao-do-triunfo/pesquisa/38/46996 |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios - 2016 |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |access-date=February 10, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190210133246/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/sao-joao-do-triunfo/pesquisa/38/46996 |archive-date=February 10, 2019}}</ref> {{BRL|13107.66}} were from taxes on products net of subsidies at current prices, and the per capita GDP was {{BRL|30780.46}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB_2016" /> In 2010, 70.72% of the population over 18 years old was [[Workforce|economically active]], while the unemployment rate was 2.61%.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas" />

[[Wages]], including other remunerations, totaled {{BRL|13095}}, and the average monthly salary in the municipality was 2.0 [[minimum wages]]. There were 209 local units and 206 active companies.<ref name="IBGE_Empresas">{{cite web|url=http://cod.ibge.gov.br/13HYG|date=2012|access-date=May 25, 2016|title=Estatísticas do Cadastro Central de Empresas|author=Cidades@ - IBGE}}</ref> According to the IBGE, 16.31% of households survived on less than one minimum wage per month per resident ({{formatnum:667}} households), 18.9% lived on between one and three minimum wages per person (767), 1.71% received between three and five (70), 0.83% had income above five per month (34), and 2.74% had no income (112).<ref name="SIDRA_Rendimento">{{cite web|url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?c=3261&z=cd&o=7|date=2010|access-date=May 25, 2016|title=Domicílios particulares permanentes, por classes de rendimento nominal mensal domiciliar per capita|author=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (SIDRA)}}</ref>

In 2009, the municipality had eleven healthcare facilities, one private and ten public municipal facilities, including hospitals, [[emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[dentistry|dental services]]. There were 26 hospital beds available.<ref name="IBGE_Saúde">{{cite web|url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/home.php|access-date=February 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150731003318/http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/home.php|archive-date=July 31, 2015|title=Serviços de saúde - 2009|author=Cidades@ - IBGE}}</ref> In 2014, 99% of children under 1 year old were up to date with their vaccinations.<ref name="ODM_4">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/|date=2012|access-date=February 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150103235537/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/|archive-date=January 3, 2015|title=4 - reduzir a mortalidade infantil|author=Portal ODM}}</ref> In 2013, there were {{formatnum:159}} live births, with no [[infant mortality]] of children under five years old per thousand.<ref name="ODM_4" /> In 2010, 4.31% of women aged 10 to 17 had children, with 12.22% of them aged 10 to 14, and the activity rate in this age group was 12.22%.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas" /> 83.0% of children under 2 years old were weighed by the [[Programa Saúde da Família|Family Health Program]] in 2013, with 1.2% of them malnourished.<ref name="ODM_1" />

[[File:São João do Triunfo PR Colégio Estadual Francisco Neves Filho (2).jpg|left|thumb|Francisco Neves Filho State School.]]

In the field of education, the average [[Basic Education Development Index|IDEB]] score among public schools in 2013 was 4.4 (on a scale from 1 to 10), with 5th-grade students scoring 5.3 and 9th-grade students scoring 4.4; the national average was 4.0.<ref name="ODM_2">{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/|date=2012|access-date=February 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150103235537/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/|archive-date=January 3, 2015|title=2 - educação básica de qualidade para todos|author=Portal ODM}}</ref> In 2010, 85.08% of children aged seven to fourteen were not attending elementary school.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas" /> The completion rate for young people aged 15 to 17 was 43.82%, and the [[literacy rate]] for youths and adolescents aged 18 to 24 was 26.40%. The age-grade distortion among elementary school students, i.e., those older than the recommended age, was 10.1% for the initial years and 33.2% for the final years, and in high school, the distortion reached 32.1%.<ref name="ODM_2" /> Among residents aged 18 or older, 29.14% had completed [[elementary education]], 26.40% had completed [[high school]], and the population had an average of 9.88 expected years of study.<ref name="PNUD_Atlas" />

In 2010, according to census sample data, {{formatnum:3704}} inhabitants attended [[daycare]]s and/or schools. Of this total, 44 attended daycares, {{formatnum:283}} were in preschool, {{formatnum:106}} in literacy classes, 27 in youth and adult education, {{formatnum:2395}} in elementary school, {{formatnum:488}} in high school, 93 in elementary adult education, 75 in high school adult education, 25 in higher education specialization, and {{formatnum:169}} in undergraduate courses. {{formatnum:8934}} people did not attend educational institutions, with {{formatnum:1446}} never having attended and {{formatnum:8554}} having attended at some point.<ref>{{cite web|date=2010|access-date=February 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150731003318/http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/home.php|archive-date=July 31, 2015|url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/home.php|title=Censo Demográfico 2010: Resultados da Amostra - Educação|author=Cidades@ - IBGE}}</ref>

The municipality is served by the PR-151 highway, as well as the [[BR-277 (Brazil highway)|BR-277]], which starts in [[Curitiba]] and passes through [[Campo Largo, Paraná|Campo Largo]], [[Balsa Nova]], São Luiz do Purunã, Colônia Witmarsum, [[Porto Amazonas]], and [[Palmeira, Paraná|Palmeira]] (along BR-277), before turning left onto PR-151.<ref name="DNIT">{{cite web |url=http://www.dnit.gov.br/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/pr.pdf |accessdate=July 20, 2011 |publisher=Departamento Nacional de Infraestrutura de Transportes |title=Mapa Multimodal do Paraná |archive-date=February 6, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150206190417/http://www.dnit.gov.br/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/mapas-multimodais/pr.pdf}}</ref>

== Culture ==
[[File:São João do Triunfo PR Brasil Biblioteca Cidadã (2).jpg|thumb|Professora Lina dos Santos Oliveira Public Library]]
To promote local socioeconomic development, the municipal government of São João do Triunfo, in collaboration with local and external institutions, has increased investments in festivals and events. The Department of Education, Culture, and Tourism is the municipal government body responsible for overseeing the cultural sector, with mandatory representation from various fields.<ref>{{cite web |author=Prefeitura |title=SCRETARIA MUNICIPAL DE EDUCAÇÃO, CULTURA E TURISMO |url=https://www.sjtriunfo.pr.gov.br/portal/secretarias/11/secretaria-municipal-de-educacao-cultura-e-turismo |accessdate=May 29, 2016}}</ref>

The only cultural venue in the municipality is the Professora Lina dos Santos Oliveira Public Library.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ipardes.gov.br/cadernos/MontaCadPdf1.php?Municipio=84150 |title=Caderno Estatístico |date=2016 |accessdate=February 7, 2016 |publisher=Instituto Paranaense de Desenvolvimento Econômico e Social |archive-date=July 1, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160701201018/http://www.ipardes.gov.br/cadernos/MontaCadPdf1.php?Municipio=84150}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.bpp.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=43 |accessdate=May 29, 2016 |publisher=Biblioteca Pública do Paraná |title=Bibliotecas Municipais |author=Governo do Paraná |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610095309/http://www.bpp.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=43}}</ref> The sole [[tourist attraction]], the Arco Íris Waterfall, is located in the Coxilhão Santa Rosa community, near the Telepar tower, past the city of [[Palmeira, Paraná|Palmeira]]. It is owned by Paulo Domingos Bedim and is open to visitors.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sjtriunfo.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=cb4ddd5f6epvcb&id=122# |title=Cachoeira Arco Íris |accessdate=May 29, 2016 |last=Prefeitura |archive-date=June 10, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160610094833/http://www.sjtriunfo.pr.gov.br/index.php?sessao=cb4ddd5f6epvcb&id=122}}</ref>

=== Holidays ===
São João do Triunfo observes two municipal holidays, eight national holidays, and several optional holidays. The municipal holidays are the feast day of [[John the Baptist]], celebrated on June 24, and the city’s political emancipation day, celebrated on February 15.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.turismo.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Dias-de-Padroeiros |title=Dias de Padroeiros |accessdate=October 24, 2023 |website=Secretaria do Turismo |language=pt-br |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210613030506/https://www.turismo.pr.gov.br/Pagina/Dias-de-Padroeiros |archive-date=2021-06-13 |url-status=}}</ref> According to Brazil’s Federal Law No. 9,093, enacted on September 12, 1995, municipalities may designate up to four religious municipal holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado? folga automática pode gerar alteração contratual |accessdate=July 27, 2012 |publisher=Guia Trabalhista |archive-url= |archive-date= |author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |url-status=}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |title=Lei n.º 9.093, de 12 de setembro de 1995 |accessdate=July 27, 2012 |archive-url= |archive-date= |author=Presidência da República |url-status=}}</ref>

=== Festivals ===
In São João do Triunfo, families from the communities of Pinhalzinho, Canudos, Cachoeira, Fabrícios, and Rio Baio organize the São Gonçalo Dance. During the festival, participants perform the dance in front of an altar adorned with various images, including that of São Gonçalo.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.prdagente.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=326 |title=Paraná da Gente: festas tradicionais |date=2004 |publisher=Secretaria de Estado da Cultura |accessdate=January 20, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070323140809/http://www.prdagente.pr.gov.br/modules/conteudo/conteudo.php?conteudo=326 |archive-date=2007-03-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

=== Cuisine ===
