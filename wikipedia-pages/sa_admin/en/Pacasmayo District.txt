'''Pacasmayo District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Pacasmayo Province|Pacasmayo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It lies just over 100&nbsp;km north of [[Trujillo, Peru|Trujillo]], in [[km]] number 681 from the [[Panamerican Highway]], is located in the coast beside to the [[Pacific Ocean]]. The city is visited commonly for [[surfers]].
