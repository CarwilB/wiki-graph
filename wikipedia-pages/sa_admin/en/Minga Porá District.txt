'''Minga Porá''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], Paraguay. It occupies an area of {{cvt|881|km2}}. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 11,959 individuals. It was officially established on 4 January 1991.

==History==
The settlement was formed as Malvina Cue when about 27 families arrived at the location on 13 May 1983, and established a settlement, which was named Minga Porã. The people erected a chapel for [[Virgin Mary|Virgen de Fatima]], who is the patron saint of the city.<ref name="ABC38">{{cite web|title=Minga Porã celebra 38 años de la llegada de los primeros pobladores|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/este/2021/05/13/minga-pora-celebra-38-anos-de-la-llegada-de-los-primero-pobladores/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Minga Porã celebrates 38 years since arrival of first settlers}}</ref><ref name="ABC30">{{cite web|title=Minga Porã conmemora 30 años de distritación|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/este/2021/01/04/minga-pora-conmemora-30-anos-de-distritacion/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Minga Porã commemorates 30 years as a district}}</ref><ref name="UHDistrito32">{{cite web|title=Minga Porã y San Alberto recordaron aniversario de creación como distritos|work=Laclave|url=https://www.laclave.com.py/2023/01/06/minga-pora-y-san-alberto-recordaron-aniversario-de-creacion-como-distritos/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Minga Porã and San Alberto commemorated district creation anniversary}}</ref>

Minga Porã was upgraded to the district by the Law 116/90 enacted on 4 January 1991.<ref name="UHDistrito32"/><ref name="Hoy">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Alto Paraná es declarado como "capital de la paraguayidad”|work=Hoy|url=https://www.hoy.com.py/nacionales/2024/05/03/municipio-de-alto-parana-es-declarado-como-capital-de-la-paraguayidad|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Alto Paraná municipality declared "capital of Paraguayan identity"}}</ref> It was split from the [[Hernandarias District|Hernandarias]] and [[Itakyry]] districts.The foundation date is celebrated annually along with the commemorative festival for the patron saint.<ref name="ABC38"/><ref name="ABC30"/>

==Geography==
Minga Porã is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|881|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1016__minga_por%C3%A1/|title=Minga Pora District|work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located about {{cvt|90|km}} from [[Ciudad del Este]],<ref name="ABC38"/><ref name="ABC30"/> and {{cvt|413|km}} from the Paraguayan capital of [[Asuncion]].<ref name="Hoy"/>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|213.54|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|24.38|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|20.365|cm}} and has 164.97 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/alto-parana/colonia-minga-pora|title=Colonia Minga Pora|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, Minga Porã district had a population of 23,548 inhabitants of which 6,178 were males and 5,781 were females. About 78.1% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (21.9%) lived in urban areas. About 30.6% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.1% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>
