'''San Pedro''' ({{IPA|es|sam ˈpeðɾo}}) is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Melipilla Province]] in central [[Chile]]'s [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], San Pedro spans an area of {{convert|787.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 7,549 inhabitants (4,080 men and 3,469 women), and the commune is an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 11.9% (803 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, San Pedro is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Florentino Flores Armijo ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]),<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> and his council members are:<ref name="Municipality">{{Cite web |url= http://www.sanpedrodemelipilla.cl/ |title= Municipality of San Pedro |accessdate=21 July 2010}}</ref>
* Emilio Cerda Sagurie ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Jeremías Vilches Mondaca ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Samuel Espinoza Vilches ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) 
* Avelino Farías Piña ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Juan Ignacio Zúñiga Godoy ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Pedro Ulloa Ulloa ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Pedro is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
