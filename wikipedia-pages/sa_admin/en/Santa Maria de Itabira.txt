'''Santa Maria de Itabira''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 10,857.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-maria-de-itabira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
