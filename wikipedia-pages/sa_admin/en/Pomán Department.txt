'''Pomán''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.infobae.com/sociedad/2023/12/25/video-la-impactante-crecida-de-un-rio-derrumbo-un-puente-en-catamarca/|title=Video: la impactante crecida de un río derrumbó un puente en Catamarca|work=[[Infobae]]|date=25 December 2023|access-date=2 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|4,859|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Saujil]], which is located around {{cvt|158|km}} from the provincial capital.
