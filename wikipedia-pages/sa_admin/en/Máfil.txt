'''Máfil''' ([[Mapudungun]] for ''embraced between rivers'') is a [[List of city in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Valdivia Province]], [[Los Ríos Region]] in southern [[Chile]], about 30&nbsp;km northeast of [[Valdivia, Chile|Valdivia]]. 

The commune is the site of a dairy industry{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=56}} and the town of Máfil a local centre of commerce and services such as [[basic education]] and a [[community health center]].{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=62–63}}<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://saludresponde.minsal.cl/lugares-los-rios/ |title=Lugares Los Ríos |access-date=2023-05-10 |publisher=[[Ministry of Health (Chile)|Ministry of Health]] |language=Spanish}}</ref> Relative to other communes of [[Los Ríos Region]] Máfil stands out for its central position and good road connections to the surrounding communes.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=28}} 

The climate of Máfil is temperate oceanic with at least four dry months.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=34}} A total of 803 mm of precipitation occurs in winter and 189 mm in summer according to averages calculated in 1989.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://observatorio.ciren.cl/profile/clima/mafil-la-union?climaregion=9 |title=Mafil - La Unión |access-date=2023-05-09 |website=Observatorio Institucional |publisher=CIREN |language=Spanish}}</ref> The natural vegetation of Máfil is [[Valdivian temperate forests]].{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=34}}

The main natural resources of Máfil are the forest and their timber, the [[gold mining|gold]] of [[Madre de Dios Mine|Madre de Dios]] and [[coal mining in Chile|coal]] in [[Pupunahue]] and [[Millahuillín]].{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|pp=37–38}}{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=41}} The commune has [[Andosol|soils of volcanic origin]] including [[trumao]]s.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=30}}

==History==
The lands of Máfil were formerly known as Pidey and were chiefly populated by [[Huilliche]]s.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=19}} In the second half of the 19th century, [[German colonization of Valdivia, Osorno and Llanquihue|German farmers settled in Máfil]].{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=54}} The change in name to Máfil is attributed to a land survey map published in 1910 where it appeared.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|p=19}} In 1953 a large coal gasification plant began to be developed in [[Pupunahue]] by [[CORFO]].{{sfn|Aliaga Armijo|Sepúlveda Márquez|2009|pp=17}}{{sfn|Aliaga Armijo|Sepúlveda Márquez|2009|pp=25}} The plant was to gasify coal using German technology from [[World War II]] to then create in the long term a larger plant in [[Magallanes Region]] where coal deposits were larger.{{sfn|Aliaga Armijo|Sepúlveda Márquez|2009|pp=27}} The building of the gasification plant was halted during the government of [[Jorge Alessandri]] and its infrastructure and materials were subsequently either sold, abandoned or stolen.{{sfn|Aliaga Armijo|Sepúlveda Márquez|2009|pp=27}}

The commune was created in 1964 during the government of Jorge Alessandri.{{sfn|Fierro Ascencio|2009|pp=20–21}}

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Máfil spans an area of {{convert|582.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 7,213 inhabitants (3,773 men and 3,440 women). Of these, 3,796 (52.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,417 (47.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 7% (547 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The town of Máfil had 5,237 inhabitants according to the 2017 census.<ref name="INEciudad2017">{{cite web |url=https://geoarchivos.ine.cl/File/pub/Cd_Pb_Al_Cs_2019.pdf |title=Ciudades, pueblos, aldeas y caceríos 2019 |publisher= National Statistics Institute|date=2019 |access-date=November 1, 2020|language=es}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Máfil is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. the alcalde is Claudio Sepúlveda Miranda r.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Máfil is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by [[Marcos Ilabaca]] ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]), [[Gastón von Mühlenbrock]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]), [[Bernardo Berger]] (ind-[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]), [[Patricio Rosas]] (ind-[[Social Convergence|CS]]) and [[Ana María Bravo]] ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) as part of the 24th electoral district (Los Ríos Region). The commune is represented in the Senate during the 2022–2030 period by [[Alfonso de Urresti]] ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]), [[Iván Flores]] ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]]) and [[María José Gatica]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 12th senatorial constituency (Los Ríos Region).
