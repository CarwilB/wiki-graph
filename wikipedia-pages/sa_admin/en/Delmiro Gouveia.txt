'''Delmiro Gouveia''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[dewˈmiɾu go(ʊ)ˈveɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the westernmost point of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 52,262 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/delmiro-gouveia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 609&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality holds part of the {{convert|26736|ha}} [[Rio São Francisco Natural Monument]], which protects the spectacular canyons of the [[São Francisco River]] between the [[Paulo Afonso Hydroelectric Complex]] and the [[Xingó Dam]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Monumento Natural do Rio São Francisco|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=1812|access-date=2016-05-21}}</ref>
