'''Rorainópolis''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʁoɾajˈnɔpulis}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southernmost part of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 32,647 (as of 2022) and its area is 33,594&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipality is crossed by the [[equator]].

==History==
In the 1970s, the regional headquarters of [[Instituto Nacional de Colonização e Reforma Agrária|INCRA]] were established in Rorainópolis along the [[BR-174]] highway. INCRA started to distribute land which attracted settlers from all of Brazil.<ref name="hist"/> In 1988, the name was changed from Vila do Incra to Rorainópolis.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://rr.senac.br/portal/index.php/cep-rorainopolis |title=
CEP - Rorainópolis |website=Senac|access-date=13 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> In 1995, Rorainópolis became an independent municipality, and has become the second most populous of the state of Roraima.<ref name="hist">{{cite web|url=http://www.rorainopolis.rr.gov.br/pagina/2_Historia-de-Rorainopolis-.html |title=História de Rorainópolis |website=Municipality of Rorainópolis|access-date=13 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The {{ill|State University of Roraima|pt|Universidade Estadual de Roraima}} operates a campus at Roraima.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.uerr.edu.br/campus-rorainopolis/ |title=CAMPUS RORAINÓPOLIS|website=Universidade Estadual de Roraima|access-date=13 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

== Nature ==
The [[Anauá National Forest]] is a {{convert|259,400|ha}} [[National forest (Brazil)|national forest]] in Rorainópolis.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.icmbio.gov.br/portal/flona-de-anaua |title=Floresta Nacional de Anauá
|website=ICMBio - FLONA|access-date=13 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> It is a [[protected area]] with sustainable use of natural resources established in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.protectedplanet.net/351714 |title=Floresta Nacional De Anauá|website=Protected Planet|access-date=13 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The [[Baixo Rio Branco-Jauaperi Extractive Reserve]] is a {{convert|581,173|ha}} protected area which has a shared use agreement with [[Waimiri Atroari Indigenous Territory]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/arp/4473 |title=Reserva Extrativista Baixo Rio Branco-Jauaperi|website=Socio Ambiental|access-date=13 April 2021}}</ref> It has been established in 2018.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.protectedplanet.net/555682105 |title=Reserva Extrativista Baixo Rio Branco Jauaperi|website=Protected Planet|access-date=13 April 2021}}</ref>

The nature in Rorainópolis is under threat from [[palm oil]] plantations. Palmaplan has as of 2021, bought 30,000 hectares of land. The cattle ranchers are eager to sell their land, and deforest new areas.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://news.mongabay.com/2021/04/new-palm-oil-frontier-sparks-scramble-for-land-in-the-brazilian-amazon/ |title=New palm oil frontier sparks scramble for land in the Brazilian Amazon|website=Monga Bay|access-date=15 April 2021}}</ref>
