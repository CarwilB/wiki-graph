'''Groot Henar''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Nickerie District]], near the bridge over the Nickerie River.<ref name="planning"/> Its population at the 2012 census was 2,709, most of which are [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]].<ref name="census"/> The resort has been named after the plantation Henar which was turned into small agricultural plots after the abolition of slavery.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://slavernij.eloweb.nl/plantage/pc/html/sur.html|title=Slaverij in Suriname|website=Slavernij Eloweb|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The cultivatable area was increased with the Henarpolder created in the 1920s. 1970 the Europolder was created adding 3,700 hectares to the resort.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://nickerie.nl/nick-sub-geschiedenis.htm#De%20opkomst%20van%20de%20rijstcultuur%20en%20de%20polders|title=De opkomst van de rijstcultuur en de polders|access-date=27 May 2020|language=nl|website=Nickerie.nl}}</ref>

Groot Henar has a school, and a clinic.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Structuur Analyse|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
