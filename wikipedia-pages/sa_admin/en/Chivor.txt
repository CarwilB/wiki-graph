'''Chivor''' is a town and municipality in the [[Eastern Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The mean temperature of the village in the [[Tenza Valley]] is {{convert|18|C|F}} and Chivor is located at {{convert|215|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]].<ref name=websiteChivor>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150602135035/http://www.chivor-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chivor]</ref> Economic activity includes [[emerald]] mining.

== Borders ==
It is bordered to the north by the municipality of [[Macanal]]; to the south by [[Ubalá]], [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], on the east by the municipality of [[Santa María, Boyacá|Santa María]], and the west by the municipality of [[Almeida, Boyacá|Almeida]].<ref name=websiteChivor/>

== Etymology ==
Chivor comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Our farmfields - our mother" or "Green and rich land". The latter refers to the rich emerald deposits.<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Chivor] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Chivor was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]]. The Muisca were organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]] with northern [[Muisca rulers|ruler]] the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]] and the southern ''[[zipa]]'' in [[Bogotá|Bacatá]]. Already in those times the rich emerald deposits were known and mined by the Muisca. The emeralds functioned as offer pieces in the [[Muisca religion]], as decoration and as [[Muisca economy|money]].

The emerald deposits of Chivor were discovered by Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] in 1537 but the mines were abandoned until 1886.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.minadepiedras.com/es/la-esmeralda/yacimientos-de-esmeraldas.html Emerald deposits in Boyacá] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160302141101/http://www.minadepiedras.com/es/la-esmeralda/yacimientos-de-esmeraldas.html |date=2016-03-02 }}</ref>

Modern Chivor was not founded until December 16, 1930, by Florencio Novoa.<ref name=websiteChivor/>

== Economy ==
The main economic activities of Chivor are [[agriculture]] ([[maize]], [[yuca]], [[banana]]s, [[sugarcane]], [[bean]]s, [[chayote]], [[coffee]] and fruits such as [[papaya]]s, [[blackberry|blackberries]] and the typical Colombian fruits [[lulo]] and [[tree tomato]]es) and especially the [[emerald]] mining.<ref name=websiteChivor/> In 2014 emeralds worth 30 million US dollars were extracted in Boyacá. The rich deposits have led to numerous conflicts in the region, including in Chivor.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.elespectador.com/noticias/nacional/nueva-guerra-de-los-esmeralderos-articulo-544477 30 million US dollars of emeralds in 2014 in Boyacá] - [[El Espectador]]</ref>

The ''Gran Esmeralda de Moctezuma'' ("Great Emerald of [[Montezuma (disambiguation)|Moctezuma]]")<!--Left link to disambig page as different Moctezumas are related--> is a mineral of {{convert|21|cm|in}} high, {{convert|17|cm|in}} long and {{convert|16|cm|in}} thick and has been found in Chivor. Currently the emerald is in [[Vienna]], [[Austria]]. Other grand emeralds from Chivor are ''Patricia'' weighing 632 [[Carat (mass)|carats]] ({{convert|126.4|g|oz}}), and ''La Magnífica'' of 1225 carats ({{convert|245|g|oz}}).<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://www.semana.com/nacion/articulo/la-reinsercion-de-los-esmeralderos/17376-3 Gran Esmeralda de Moctezuma and other large emeralds from Chivor] - [[Semana (Colombia)|Semana]]</ref>

The ''Embalse la Esmeralda'' ("Emerald reservoir") producing [[hydroelectric]] energy is governed from Chivor, Macanal and Almeida.
