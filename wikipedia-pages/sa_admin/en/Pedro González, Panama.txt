'''Pedro González''' is one of the [[Pearl Islands]] of [[Panama]]. It is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Balboa District]], [[Panamá Province]] with a population of 263 as of 2010, mostly in the village of Pedro de Cocal.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 440; its population as of 2000 was 247.<ref name=inec/>

It is served by [[Fernando Eleta Airport]].
