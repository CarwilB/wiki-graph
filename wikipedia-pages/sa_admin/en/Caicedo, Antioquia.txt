'''Caicedo''' ({{IPA|es|kajˈseðo}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Quintero |first=Willy Rodriguez |date=2020-09-28 |title=¡Gracias comunidad de Caicedo! Memoria histórica es posible con trabajo articulado |url=https://centrodememoriahistorica.gov.co/gracias-comunidad-de-caicedo-memoria-historica-es-posible-con-trabajo-articulado/ |access-date=2026-03-25 |website=Centro Nacional de Memoria Histórica |language=es}}</ref> It is part of the sub-region of [[Southwestern Antioquia]]. In 2002, the population was 7,608, with a density ratio of 34.5/km'''²''' (89 mi'''²''').
