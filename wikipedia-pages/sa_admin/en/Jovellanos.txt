'''Jovellanos''' is a municipality and town in the [[Matanzas Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Asunción, Isabel, Realengo and San José.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/mjovellanos/index.htm|title=Jovellanos|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

It was founded in 1842 as Corral de la Bemba<ref name=guije/> on the location of an old ranch called ''Bemba''. It took its current name in 1870, in honor of the [[Spain|Spanish]] writer [[Gaspar Melchor de Jovellanos]]. The same year it achieved the status of ''villa'' (town).

Jovellanos is also called "Bemba" by its inhabitants and locals.  "Bemba" means "big lip" in [[Cuban Spanish]]; this term refers to the black population of the city.  To outsiders, it may have negative racial overtones, but in the city this is not the case.  Inside Jovellanos, the black and white population for the most part live harmoniously, side by side. Therefore, "Bemba" is an affectionate name for the town used by locals.{{citation needed|date=May 2014}}

Jovellanos is an [[Industrial sector|industrial]] town, with large and small factories, while [[Sugarcane|sugar cane]] is cultivated in the surrounding areas.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Jovellanos had a population of 57,216.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|505|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|110|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
