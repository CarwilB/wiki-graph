The '''Simón Rodríguez Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 182,474. <ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[El Tigre]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Simón Rodríguez Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
El Tigre was founded on February 23, 1933.  The city started when oil companies found oil in El Tigre and established their offices in Campo Oficina.

==Demographics==
The Simón Rodríguez Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 183,185 (up from 155,178 in 2000).  This amounts to 12.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|260.58|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Simón Rodríguez Municipality is Carlos Hernández, elected on November 23, 2008, with 49% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02|title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008|website=www.cne.gob.ve}}</ref> He replaced Ernesto José Paraqueima Luiggi shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Edmundo Barrios and Miguel Otero Silva.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
