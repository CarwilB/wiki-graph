'''Palenque Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Los Ríos Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Palenque, Los Ríos|Palenque]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 20,658.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
