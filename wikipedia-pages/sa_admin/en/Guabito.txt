'''Guabito''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] and [[town]] in the [[Changuinola District]] of the [[Bocas del Toro Province]] of [[Panama]].<ref name=world>{{citation|url=http://world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=&men=gpro&lng=en&des=wg&geo=-167&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=x&geo=358209738 |title=World Gazetteer - Guabito, Panama |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20130209120639/http://world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=&men=gpro&lng=en&des=wg&geo=-167&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=x&geo=358209738 |archivedate=2013-02-09 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It is a small town located directly across the [[Rio Sixaola]] from [[Sixaola]], [[Costa Rica]]. An elevated former railroad grade and bridge connects Guabito to Sixaola.<ref name=moon>{{citation|title=Moon Panama|publisher=Avalon Travel|date=November 2008|isbn=978-1-59880-085-2|url-access=registration|url=https://archive.org/details/panama0000fria}}</ref> This border crossing is popular with tourists going between [[Costa Rica]] and [[Bocas del Toro Province|Bocas del Toro]],<ref name=fodor>{{citation|title=Costa Rica 2008|edition=2nd|year=2008|publisher=Fodor's Travel|isbn=978-1-4000-1803-1}}</ref> though few tourists stop in Guabito longer than necessary to clear Panamanian customs.<ref name=moon />

Guabito has a land area of {{convert|168.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 8,387 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|49.7|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 11,125; its population as of 2000 was 14,366.<ref name=inec/>
