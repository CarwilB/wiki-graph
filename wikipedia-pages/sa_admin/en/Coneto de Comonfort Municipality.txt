'''Coneto de Comonfort''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Coneto de Comonfort]]. The municipality covers an area of 1324.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2015, the municipality had a total population of 4,390.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web| url=http://www.cuentame.org.mx/monografias/informacion/dur/poblacion/default.aspx?tema=me&e=10 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190411052801/http://www.cuentame.org.mx/monografias/informacion/dur/poblacion/default.aspx?tema=me&e=10 | archive-date=2019-04-11 | title=Número de habitantes}}</ref> The town of Coneto de Comonfort has a population of 858.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Coneto de Comonfort, the municipality had 64 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 population in parentheses) was: [[Nogales, Durango|Nogales]] (1,117), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
