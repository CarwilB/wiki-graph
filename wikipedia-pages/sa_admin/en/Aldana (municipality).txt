'''Aldana''' is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Aldana has a cold [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with moderate rainfall year-round.
