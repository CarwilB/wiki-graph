'''Julcán''' is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.businessempresarial.com.pe/la-libertad-supera-los-600-medios-de-comunicacion/|title=La Libertad supera los 600 medios de comunicación|work=Business Empresarial|date=13 October 2023|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital of this province is the city of [[Julcán]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into four [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

* [[Julcán District, Julcán|Julcán]]
* [[Calamarca District|Calamarca]]
* [[Carabamba District|Carabamba]]
* [[Huaso District|Huaso]]
