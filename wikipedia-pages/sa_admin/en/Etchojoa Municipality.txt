'''Etchojoa''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Sonora|seventy-two municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 31, 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 }}</ref> It is located in the southern part of the state, in the Mayo Valley area and on the coast of the Gulf of California. Its municipal seat and most inhabited town is [[Etchojoa]]. Other important towns include [[Bacobampo]], {{Interlanguage link|Buaysiacobe|lt=Buaysiacobe|es|Buaysiacobe}}, and {{Interlanguage link|Bacame Nuevo|lt=Bacame Nuevo|es|Bacame Nuevo}}. It also includes the rural town of [[Colonia Soto, Sonora|Colonia Soto]]. The municipality was founded on October 15, 1909. 

According to the Population and Housing Census carried out in 2020 by the [[National Institute of Statistics and Geography]] (INEGI), the municipality has a total of 61,309 inhabitants,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Gobierno de Mexíco |date= |title=Etchojoa |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/etchojoa |access-date=May 22, 2024 |website=Gobierno de Mexíco |publisher=Secretaría de Economía}}</ref> ranking 11th among the most populated in the state, and has an area of {{cvt|949.85|km2}}.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Publicaciones y mapas |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/biblioteca/ficha.html?upc=702825197995 |access-date=2024-05-22 |website=www.inegi.org.mx}}</ref> Its [[Gross domestic product|gross domestic product per capita]] is [[United States dollar|USD]] $5,701 and its [[Human Development Index|human development index]] (HDI) is 0.7223. Like most municipalities in Sonora, the name was given because of its municipal head. <ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=26&mun=026|title=Resumen municipal|publisher=[[Secretariat of Welfare (Mexico)|SEDESOL]]|access-date=4 March 2021|language=es}}</ref> <!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². --->
