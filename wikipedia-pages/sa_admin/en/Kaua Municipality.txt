'''Kaua Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "is it bitter?") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 214.60&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|135|km}} southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]]. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos, beginning in 1544 with Juan de la Cruz, passing in 1607 to Juan Gil de la Cruz and Tomé Gil de la Cruz, to Ignacio de Escalante Barroto in 1663<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5|page=503| url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA503|access-date=15 August 2015|language=es}}</ref> and to Diego Escalante from 1700 to 1750.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Valladolid Municipality, Yucatan|Valladolid Municipality]]. In 1928 the area became its own municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of sports, ecology and education.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Kaua|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=15 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Kaua, Yucatán]]. The municipality has 12 populated places<ref name=governance /> besides the seat including Chan Dzonot, Dzeal, Dzibiac, Haimil, Kankab, Knoh, San Nicolás, San Román, Tzcal, Xkoben, X'lapxul and [[Xuxcab]].
