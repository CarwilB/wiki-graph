'''Gualmatán''' (full name, '''San Francisco del Líbano de Gualmatán'''), is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Gualmatán has a comfortable [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with moderate rainfall year-round.
