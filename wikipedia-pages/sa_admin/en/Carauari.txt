'''Carauari''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 28,508 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/carauari/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 25,767&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

The city is served by [[Carauari Airport]].

==Geography==

The town is in the [[Juruá-Purus moist forests]] ecoregion.<ref>{{citation |title=South America: Amazon Basin, northwestern Brazil (NT0133)
 |last=Sears|first=Robin |publisher=WWF: World Wildlife Fund
 |url=http://www.worldwildlife.org/ecoregions/nt0133|accessdate=2017-03-28}}</ref>
The municipality contains about 5% of the [[Tefé National Forest]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Tefé – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA de Tefé|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6656|accessdate=2016-10-19}}</ref>
The municipality contains {{convert|251577|ha|adj=on}} [[Médio Juruá Extractive Reserve]], created in 1997, on the left bank of the meandering [[Juruá River]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Médio Juruá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Médio Juruá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4526|accessdate=2016-06-16}}</ref>
It also contains the {{convert|632949|ha|adj=on}} [[Uacari Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS de Uacari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RDS de Uacari|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6818|accessdate=2016-09-06}}</ref>

===Climate===
Carauari has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with unpleasantly hot and humid weather year-round. Rainfall is significant throughout, although the period from June to August is drier than the remainder of the year.
