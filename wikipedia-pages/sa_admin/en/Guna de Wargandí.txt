'''Guna de Wargandí''' or '''Wargandí''', formerly known as '''Kuna de Wargandí'''<ref>{{Cite web|date=2014-02-22|title=¿Guna, kuna o cuna?: James Howe {{!}} La Prensa Panamá|url=https://www.prensa.com/impresa/opinion/Guna-kuna-cuna-James-Howe_0_3874112623.html|access-date=2021-09-01|website=www.prensa.com|language=es}}</ref> ({{IPA|es|ˈkuna ðe waɾˈɣandi}}) is a ''[[comarca indígena]]'' (indigenous territory) and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]]  in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,914 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 34 of July 25, 2000,<ref name=inec/>  from the province of [[Darién Province|Darién]], from the district of [[Pinogana District|Pinogana]]. It has an area of {{convert|775|km2}}. It is inhabited by the [[Guna people]].

==Geography==
As Wargandí is a sub-provincial ''comarca'' (equivalent to a [[Districts of Panama|district]] rather than a [[Provinces of Panama|province]]), it is not subdivided into districts, although it is subdivided into three communities, [[Mortí]], [[Nurra, Panama|Nurra]] and [[Wala, Panama|Wala]]. It is located in the [[Chucunaque River]] basin. It is a part of the [[Pinogana District]] of the [[Darien Province]].

The community of Nurra serves as its [[de facto]] capital. It has no [[de jure]] capital.
