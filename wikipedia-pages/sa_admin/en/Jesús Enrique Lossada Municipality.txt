'''Jesús Enrique Lossada''' is a [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipality]] in the metropolitan area of [[Maracaibo]], [[Venezuela]]'s second largest city. Jesús Enrique Lossada covers an area of {{convert|3533|km2}}<ref name=area/> and recorded a population of 118,756 in the 2011 Venezuelan census.<ref name=census2011/> It is named after {{ill|Jesús Enrique Lossada|es}} (1892–1948), a distinguished lawyer, politician, professor, and writer from Maracaibo.<ref name=chronicles>{{cite book |editor-last=Dorantes |editor-first=Egli |title=Entre Crónicas y Cronistas |language=es |url=http://150.185.9.18/fondo_editorial/images/PDF/CPCP/ENTRE%20CRNICAS%20Y%20CRONISTAS%20-%20Egli%20Dorantes%20Compilador.pdf |publisher=Universidad Nacional Experimental Rafael María Baralt |year=2017 |isbn=978-980-427-023-9 |accessdate=22 November 2020 }}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref>

==Geography==
[[File:Mapa Jesús Enrique Lossada.PNG|thumb|400px| Map of the ''parroquias'' and major features in the municipality of Jesús Enrique Lossada.]]
Jesús Enrique Lossada lies northwest of [[Lake Maracaibo]] in the [[Maracaibo Basin]]. The municipality borders the municipalities of [[Mara Municipality|Mara]] to the north, Maracaibo and [[San Francisco Municipality, Zulia|San Francisco]] to the east, [[La Cañada de Urdaneta Municipality|La Cañada de Urdaneta]] to the southeast, and [[Rosario de Perijá Municipality|Rosario de Perijá]] to the southwest.<ref name=muni>{{cite report |url=http://www.corpozulia.gob.ve/archivos/JESUS%20ENRIQUE%20LOSSADA%202010-2011.pdf |title=Municipio Jesús Enrique Lossada |language=es |publisher=Corporación de Desarrollo de la Región Zuliana |date=2010 |accessdate=22 November 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181024122952/http://www.corpozulia.gob.ve/archivos/JESUS%20ENRIQUE%20LOSSADA%202010-2011.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> To the west it borders the Colombian [[Departments of Colombia|departments]] of [[Cesar Department|Cesar]] and [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]].

The relief in the eastern part of the municipality is flat, while in the west it elevates to form the mountains of the [[Serranía del Perijá]] on the Colombia–Venezuela border. The western highlands are forested, while the eastern lowlands have been largely converted to agricultural land. The [[Guasare River|Guasare]] and [[Socuy River|Socuy]] rivers originate in the western highlands and flow northeastward into Mara Municipality, where they meet to form the [[El Limón River|Limón River]]. The [[Palmar River]], on which the Tres Ríos Reservoir is located, forms the municipal border between Jesús Enrique Lossada and Rosario de Perijá.<ref name=muni/><ref name=report>{{cite report |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Ambiental/PrincIndicadores/pdf/Informe_Geoambiental_Zulia.pdf |title=Informe Geoambiental: Estado Zulia 2011 |language=es |pages=80–89 |publisher=National Institute of Statistics (Venezuela) |year=2011 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20170713092610/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/Ambiental/PrincIndicadores/pdf/Informe_Geoambiental_Zulia.pdf |archivedate=13 July 2017 |access-date=22 November 2020 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite book |editor-last=Rodríguez Olarte |editor-first=Douglas |title=Ríos en Riesgo de Venezuela |language=es |publisher=[[Universidad Centroccidental Lisandro Alvarado]] |location=Barquisimeto |date=15 February 2017 |volume=1 |pages=16–17, 20 |isbn=978-980-12-9350-7}}</ref>

The wet season in Jesús Enrique Lossada lasts from May to November. Rainfall increases from north to south and from east to west in the municipality, varying from {{convert|500|to|1500|mm}} annually. The average annual temperature is {{convert|26|C}}.<ref name=muni/><ref name=report/>

==History==
Ranches were first established in the late eighteenth century in what is now the territory of Jesús Enrique Lossada. [[Royal Dutch Shell|Shell]] struck oil at La Paz in 1922 and La Concepción in 1924,<ref>{{cite web |title=Historia |url=http://yusmaryurdaneta02.blogspot.com/p/historia.html |language=es |accessdate=22 November 2020}}</ref> and operated the oilfields there until the 1960s.<ref>{{cite web |date=21 April 2007 |title=Hace 40 años La Paz volvió a nacer |url=https://municipiojelossada.blogia.com/ |language=es |accessdate=22 November 2020}}</ref> Jesús Enrique Lossada was part of the former Maracaibo District until 21 June 1989, when it and Maracaibo were separated, each becoming independent municipalities.<ref name=chronicles/>

==Economy==
Jesús Enrique Lossada's economy is primarily based on agriculture. Major crops include corn, cassava, tomatoes, bananas, and other fruits.<ref name=muni/> In particular it is one of the largest producers of grapes in Zulia.<ref name=report/> Livestock produced in the municipality include cattle, pigs, sheep, goats, and poultry.<ref name=muni/><ref name=report/>

==Infrastructure==
Local roads connect Jesús Enrique Lossada with its neighbouring municipalities.<ref name=muni/> Troncal 6 highway passes through the southeastern corner of the municipality as it runs between [[La Villa del Rosario|Villa del Rosario]] in the southwest and San Francisco in the east.

The Tres Ríos Reservoir is located at the confluence of the Palmar, Lajas and Caño Pescado rivers on the border between Jesús Enrique Lossada and Rosario de Perijá. Built in 2006, the dam has a capacity of {{convert|180|e6m3}} and an effective height of {{convert|59|m}}. It supplies water for irrigation and household consumption.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.hidrolago.gov.ve/hidrocuencas.html |date=27 September 2018 |title=Embalse Tres Rios |language=es |publisher=Hidrolago |accessdate=22 November 2020 |archive-date=5 August 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200805121649/http://www.hidrolago.gov.ve/hidrocuencas.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> Jesús Enrique Lossada does not have an extensive water distribution system and water is delivered by truck to a majority of households.<ref name=muni/><ref name=report/> There is no sewer system in the municipality.<ref name=muni/>

The two hospitals in the municipality are both located in La Concepción.<ref name=muni/>
