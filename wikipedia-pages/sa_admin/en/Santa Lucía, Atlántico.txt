'''Santa Lucía''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsanta luˈsi.a}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Atlántico Department|Atlántico]]. The town is located on the north bank of the [[Canal del Dique|Dique Canal]].
