'''San Isidro''' is an affluent [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]] found in the north of [[Greater Buenos Aires]]. Its capital is the city of [[San Isidro, Buenos Aires|San Isidro]]. It is {{convert|21|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from the city of [[Buenos Aires]].

The founder of San Isidro was Domingo de Acassuso, who built a [[cathedral]] in 1706 dedicated to [[Isidore the Laborer|San Isidro Labrador]], having seen him in a dream. Other towns in the municipality are [[Acassuso]], [[Beccar]], [[Boulogne Sur Mer, Buenos Aires|Boulougne]], [[Martínez, Buenos Aires|Martínez]], and [[Villa Adelina]].

The area of the partido is {{convert|51.44|km2|sqmi|1|abbr=on}}. In 2010 there were 291,608 inhabitants.<ref name=Cen2010 /> San Isidro borders on the partidos of [[Tigre Partido|Tigre]], [[Vicente López Partido|Vicente López]], [[General San Martín Partido|San Martín]] and [[San Fernando Partido|San Fernando]].

==Cathedral==
[[Image:20060128 - Catedral de San Isidro (Argentina).jpg|left|thumb|200px|Catedral de San Isidro.]]
The [[San Isidro cathedral]] (''Catedral de San Isidro'' in [[Spanish language|Spanish]]) was completed on July 14, 1898. Constructed in [[Neo-Gothic]] style, it stands {{convert|68|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} tall. It is located opposite Plaza Mitre in San Isidro's historic quarter.

==Sports==

===Football===
San Isidro is home to [[Club Atlético Acassuso]], a football club that currently plays in the regionalised [[Primera B Metropolitana|3rd Division]].

===Horse racing===
San Isidro is home to a historically prominent Jockey Club, that runs San Isidro's world-class [[race track]], the [[Hipódromo de San Isidro]].

===Rugby===
San Isidro is the national capital of Rugby and home to two of the most important rugby clubs in the country - SIC ([[San Isidro Club]]) and CASI ([[Club Atlético San Isidro]]).

===Sailing===
There are a number of sailing clubs on the [[Río de la Plata]].
