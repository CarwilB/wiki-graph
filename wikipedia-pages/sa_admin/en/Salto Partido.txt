'''Salto Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 29,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4455|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Salto, Buenos Aires|Salto]], which is around {{convert|200|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
