'''Carmen''' is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Campeche]]. It is situated at the south-west of the state, on and around the [[Laguna de Términos]]. The municipal seat and largest settlement is [[Ciudad del Carmen]]. As of 2015, the population was 248,303.

The name "Carmen" is in honour of 16 July, the feast of [[Nuestra Señora del Carmen]], after the date in 1717 when Spanish colonial authorities finally expelled the pirates from the island now known as [[Isla del Carmen]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Carmen borders to the north with the [[Gulf of Mexico]]; to the west with the municipality of [[Palizada Municipality|Palizada]]; and to the east with the municipalities of [[Champotón Municipality|Champotón]], [[Escárcega]] and [[Candelaria (municipality)|Candelaria]]. It covers a total surface area of  9,720.09&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 221,094.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Carmen|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=003|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305080421/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=003|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The municipality had 1,886 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Ciudad del Carmen, Campeche|Ciudad del Carmen]] (169,466), [[Sabancuy, Campeche|Sabancuy]] (7,286), [[Isla Aguada]] (6,204), [[Nuevo Progreso, Campeche|Nuevo Progreso]] (4,851), [[San Antonio Cárdenas, Campeche|San Antonio Cárdenas]] (4,206), [[Atasta, Campeche|Atasta]] (2,535), classified as urban, and [[Checubul, Campeche|Checubul]] (1,811), [[Chicbul, Campeche|Chicbul]] (1,692), [[Colonia Emiliano Zapata, Campeche|Colonia Emiliano Zapata]] (1,311), [[El Aguacatal, Campeche|El Aguacatal]] (1,270), [[Mamantel (Pancho Villa), Campeche|Mamantel (Pancho Villa)]] (1,262), [[Licenciado Gustavo Díaz Ordaz (18 de Marzo), Campeche|Licenciado Gustavo Díaz Ordaz (18 de Marzo)]] (1,239), and [[Abelardo L. Rodríguez, Campeche|Abelardo L. Rodríguez]] (1,130), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The 2005 census reported a population of 199,988 persons. Of these, 4,151 spoke one of 25 [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous languages]] used in the municipality, predominantly [[Ch'ol language|Ch'ol]] and [[Yucatec Maya]].
