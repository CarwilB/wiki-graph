'''Caluma Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Bolívar Province, Ecuador|Bolívar Province]].  Its population at the 2010 census was 13,129.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/|title = Resultados}}</ref>
*[[Mestizo]]  91.8%
*[[White Latin American|White]]  3.1%
*[[Indigenous peoples in Ecuador|Indigenous]]  2.3%
*[[Afro-Ecuadorian]]  1.5%
*[[Montubio]]  1.1%
*Other  0.2%
