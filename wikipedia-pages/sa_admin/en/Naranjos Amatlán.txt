'''Naranjos Amatlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north zone of the state, about 402&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. The municipal seat is [[Naranjos, Veracruz|Naranjos]].

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[orange fruit]], [[coffee]] and [[mango]].

==Culture==
In Naranjos Amatlán, in March takes place the celebration in honor to San José de la Montaña, patron of the town.

==Geography==

The municipality has an area of {{convert|200.7|km2}}. It is located at {{coord|21|21|N|97|41|W|}}.

The municipality of Naranjos Amatlán is delimited to the north by [[Chinampa de Gorostiza]] to the east and south-east by [[Tamiahua]], to the south and south-west by [[Tancoco]] and to the west by [[Tamalín]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30013a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111201811/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30013a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2013-01-11|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Naranjos Amatlán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-11}}</ref>

===Climate===
It has a hot-humid-tropical climate, most of the year is hot (from May to October) winter is usually between warm and cool.
Between May and June temperatures usually get around {{convert|104|F|C}}, while in December and January could get lower than {{convert|41|F|C}}.
The heat is really common, including some days in the winter.
When spring is finishing (May–June) is usually very hot and dry, while in the summer is a little bit less but much rainier, fall's beginning October) is still hot, the rest is cooler, cold fronts start that are from Mexico's Gulf.
