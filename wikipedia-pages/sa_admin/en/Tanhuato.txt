'''Tanhuato''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|135|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Tanhuato is located at an elevation between {{convert|1600|and(-)|2000|m}}  in the [[Bajío]] region in northwestern Michoacán. It borders the Michoacanese municipalities of [[Yurécuaro]] to the east, [[Ecuandureo]] to the southeast, [[Ixtlán, Michoacan|Ixtlán]] to the southwest, and [[Vista Hermosa, Michoacán|Vista Hermosa]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16086.pdf |title=Tanhuato, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref> Additionally, the [[Lerma River]] forms the border between Tanhuato and the Jaliscan municipalities of [[Ayotlán]] to the northeast and [[La Barca Municipality|La Barca]] to the northwest. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|228.732|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.39% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Tanhuato consists of tropical forest (31%) and grassland (9%). More than half (57%) of the land is used for agriculture and 1.9% consists of urban areas. The main soil types are [[vertisol]]s (74%) and [[phaeozem]]s (23%).<ref name=compendium/> The Gonzalo Reservoir lies on the border between Tanhuato and Vista Hermosa and has a total capacity of {{convert|7600000|m3}} and a useful capacity of {{convert|6600000|m3}}.<ref>{{cite report |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenido/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092092.pdf |title=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Michoacán de Ocampo 2017 |at=Table 4.17 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2017 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

Tanhuato has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|18|and(-)|22|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The place name Tanhuato is of [[Chichimeca]] origin, meaning "near the hill". In pre-Hispanic times the area was part of the [[Purépecha Empire]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

Tanhuato was one of the original municipalities established in Michoacán in 1831.<ref name=encyclopedia/> The municipality was disestablished and incorporated into Yurécuaro at various periods in the 19th century (1856–1861, 1863–1868, and 1874–1877).<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=88, 202–204 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref> In 1902, the municipal seat was renamed Tanhuato de Guerrero in honour of former Mexican president [[Vicente Guerrero]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Tanhuato comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Héctor Daniel Aranda Pérez.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Tanhuato recorded a population of 15,176 inhabitants living in 3767 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=086 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Tanhuato |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=9 January 2021 }}{{Dead link|date=July 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 15,352 inhabitants in Tanhuato.<ref name=survey/>

[[INEGI]] lists 18 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Tanhuato de Guerrero is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 9546 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Agriculture is the main economic activity in Tanhuato. Major crops grown include wheat, sorghum, corn, vegetables (e.g., chiles<ref>{{cite news |date=16 December 2020 |url=https://www.brunoticias.com/yurecuaro-tanhuato-vista-hermosa-chile/ |title=Yurécuaro, Tanhuato y Vista Hermosa líderes productores de chile en Michoacán |language=es |website=Brunoticias |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref> and hydroponically grown tomatoes), blue agave and beans. Tanhuato is particularly known for its alfalfa and chickpea production. Livestock raised include cattle, pigs, goats, poultry and bees.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Tanhuato Municipal Council |url=http://tanhuato.sisofi.com.mx/uploads/22-05-2020/Plan-de-Desarrollo-2018-2021.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |pages=7–8 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 3a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=25 February 2019 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

The toll expressway [[Mexican Federal Highway 15D|Federal Highway 15D]] runs through the municipality, as does [[Mexican Federal Highway 110|Federal Highway 110]].
