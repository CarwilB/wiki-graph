'''Independencia''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[La Rioja Province, Argentina|La Rioja Province]] ([[Argentina]]).<ref name=":cp">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-admin.php|title=Argentina: Administrative Division|publisher=citypopulation}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book|url=http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|title=CENTRO LATINOAMERICANO Y CARIBEÑO DE DEMOGRAFÍA (CELADE)|publisher=World Bank|year=1970}}</ref>
== History ==
The department was created by Law 108 of {{Start date and age|1864|09|28|df=yes}}. This law also established Patquía as the head town.
[[File:Los Colorados, La Rioja, Argentina.jpg|200px|thumb|left|Independencia]]
{{clearleft}}
== Geography ==
The department has 7,120 km² and borders to the north with the departments of Capital, Chilecito and Coronel Felipe Varela, to the east with the department of Angel Vicente Peñaloza, to the west with the province of San Juan and to the south with the department of Facundo Quiroga.
