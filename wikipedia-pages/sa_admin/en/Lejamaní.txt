'''Lejamaní''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipalities]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua department|Comayagua]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.hn/index.php/component/content/article?id=103|title=Censo Poblacional 2013|last=cruz|first=Eduardo|date=2013|website=www.ine.gob.hn|language=es-es|trans-title=Population Census 2013|access-date=2018-06-13}}</ref>

== History ==
The municipality began as a town entrusted to Don Felipe II, King of Spain in 1630.<ref>Flores, Fredy; Sergio Palacios (2009). ''Honduras Geográfica''. Ediciones Ramsés. p. 77.</ref>
