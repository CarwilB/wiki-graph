'''Madruga''' ({{IPA|es|maˈðɾuɣa}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Mayabeque Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located in the eastern part of the province, between [[Matanzas, Cuba|Matanzas]] and [[Güines, Cuba|Güines]]. It was founded in 1803.<ref name=guije/>

==Geography==
In 1940, the municipality was divided into the [[barrio]]s of Concordia and Cayajabos, Este, Itabo, Majagua, Oeste, Sabana de Robles and San Blas.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/hmadruga/index.htm|title=Madruga|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

After 1973, the municipality includes Madruga; [[Aguacate, Cuba|Aguacate]]; Viviendas Campesinas; La Granja; Cayajabos Pipián and Flor de Itabo.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Madruga had a population of 26,412.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|464|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|57|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
