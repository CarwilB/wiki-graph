'''Mariscal Estigarribia''' ({{IPA|es|maɾisˈkal estiɣaˈriβja}}) is a town in the [[Boquerón, Paraguay|Boquerón]] Department of [[Paraguay]]. It is home to [[Dr. Luis María Argaña International Airport]] (IATA code: ESG).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.aeroflight.co.uk/waf/americas/paraguay/Paraguay-Home.htm |title=Aeroflight Countries of the World – Paraguay |publisher=Aeroflight |accessdate=20 December 2008}}</ref>

==Strategic importance==
The airport was constructed by Paraguayan military engineers between 1977 and 1986, as part of an intended free-trade zone to help in the development of the northern Chaco region.<ref name=USDOS>{{cite news|title=United States Has No Plans for Military Base in Paraguay |publisher=US Department of State |date=11 January 2006 |accessdate=9 June 2025|url=http://www.america.gov/st/pubs-english/2006/January/20060111094434atlahtnevel0.827984.html |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20101128070450/http://www.america.gov/st/pubs-english/2006/January/20060111094434atlahtnevel0.827984.html |archivedate=28 November 2010 }}</ref>

400 US troops arrived in Paraguay in July 2005, shortly after the Paraguayan government signed a [[visiting forces agreement]] with the United States,<ref>{{Cite news|title=U.S. Military Moves in Paraguay Rattle Regional Relations |publisher=[[International Relations Center]] |date=14 December 2005 |accessdate=3 May 2006 |url=http://americas.irc-online.org/am/2991 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070612192202/http://americas.irc-online.org/am/2991 |archivedate=12 June 2007 }}</ref> in support of joint exercises with the Paraguayan military which took place between July 2005 and December 2006.<ref name=USDOS/>

==Climate==
Mariscal Estigarribia lies right on the boundary of a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Aw'') and a [[Semi-arid_climate#Hot_semi-arid_climates|hot semi-arid climate]] (''BSh''). It is characterised by sweltering summers with occasional heavy thunderstorms, and dry winters with very warm afternoons and usually pleasant mornings. The minimum temperature of {{convert|34.0|C|F|1}} recorded on November 13, 2023 in Mariscal José Félix Estigarribia, is considered the highest minimum temperature recorded in South America.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ogimet.com/cgi-bin/gsynres?ind=86068&ano=2023&mes=11&day=13&hora=1&min=0&ndays=30|title= 86068: Mariscal Estigarribia (Paraguay)|author=<!--Not stated--> |date= 8 November 2023|website=ogimet.com |publisher=OGIMET |access-date= 12 November 2023|quote=}}</ref>
