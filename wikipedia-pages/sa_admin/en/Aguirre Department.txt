'''Aguirre Department''' is a department of [[Santiago del Estero Province]], [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nuevodiarioweb.com.ar/provinciales/se-esperan-22-de-maxima-y-hay-19-departamentos-bajo-alerta-por-tormentas.htm|title=Se esperan 22º de máxima y hay 19 departamentos bajo alerta por tormentas|work={{ill|Nuevo Diario|es}}|date=18 August 2025|access-date=3 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital lies at [[Pinto, Santiago del Estero|Pinto]].

The department covers an area of 3692 km². The population as of 2010 was 7,610. Municipalities include [[Argentina, Santiago del Estero|Argentina]], [[Casares, Santiago del Estero|Casares]] and [[Malbrán]].
