'''San Carlos''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[West Panamá Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 15,541;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 24,001.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|338|km2}}.

The capital city of the District is named [[San Carlos, Panama|San Carlos]] as well.<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
San Carlos District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[San Carlos, Panamá Oeste|San Carlos]] (capital)
*[[El Espino, Panama|El Espino]]
*[[El Higo, Panama|El Higo]]
*[[Guayabito]]
*[[La Ermita]]
*[[La Laguna, Panama|La Laguna]]
*[[Las Uvas]]
*[[Los Llanitos]]
*[[San José, Panamá Oeste|San José]]
