== History ==
[[File:Pueblo de Susques.jpg|thumb|The Town of [[Susques]].]]
By the treaty of May 10, 1889 with [[Bolivia]], [[Argentina]] renounced its claim on [[Tarija]] and part of the [[Chaco Basin|Chaco Boreal]], and Bolivia, in compensation, ceded the [[Puna de Atacama]], which was in the power of [[Chile]] after the [[War of the Pacific|Pacific War (1879-1880)]]. This action granted Argentina a territory that was part of the [[Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata]], but which was in fact in the hands of Chile. As Chile [[Puna de Atacama dispute|refused]] to hand over the territories ceded by Bolivia, it was decided to submit the issue to the arbitration of the [[United States|American]] diplomat [[William I. Buchanan|William Insco Buchanan]], who in 1899 awarded three fourths of the disputed territory to Argentina and the rest to Chile. By Law No. 3906 of January 9, 1900, the Territorio Nacional de los Andes (National Territory of the Andes) was organized. By decree of May 12, 1900, the National Executive Branch divided the Territory of the Andes into three administrative departments, the northernmost being that of "Susques or del Norte", which bordered to the east with the province of Jujuy and to the north with Bolivia.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Departamento de Susques |url=https://lugaresquever.com/wiki/departamento-de-susques?spmchkbj=spmprvbj6tci6cRLV2lgB6GivcVhpS5YSF |access-date=2023-10-16 |website=lugaresquever.com |language=es}}</ref>
