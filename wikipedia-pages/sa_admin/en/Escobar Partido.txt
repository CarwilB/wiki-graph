'''Escobar Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northern part of the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area, in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 178,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|277|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Belén de Escobar]], which is {{convert|32|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

Escobar is home to a significant population of [[Japanese Argentines]].
