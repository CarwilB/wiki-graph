'''San Sebastián District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Cusco Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Anawarkhi]] at {{convert|4050|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Cusco Province (Cusco Region)</ref>

* Anka Wachana
* Ñustayuq
* Tawqaray
* [[Wanakawri, Cusco|Wanakawri]]

== Archaeology ==
Some of the most important archaeological sites of the district are [[Chuqik'iraw Pukyu]], [[Inkill Tampu]], [[Pumamarka, San Sebastián|Pumamarka]], [[Puqin Kancha]], [[Qullqapampa]], [[Rumiwasi]] and [[Wayna Tawqaray]].
