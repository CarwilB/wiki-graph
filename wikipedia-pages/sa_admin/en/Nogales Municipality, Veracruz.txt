'''Nogales''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the mountainous western region of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. The municipal seat is the city of [[Nogales, Veracruz|Nogales]].

It is situated at, at an altitude of 1280 m, and covers a total surface area of
77.32&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. In the year 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 31,818.<ref>
{{cite web|title=Nogales |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30115a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=10 November 2008}}</ref>
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 1,286 (4.67%) spoke an [[Indigenous languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Nahuatl]].

The name "Nogales" is the Spanish for [[walnut|walnut trees]].

==History==
This part of the future state of Veracruz was brought under [[Aztec]] sway in or around 1450 under Emperor [[Moctezuma I]]lhuicamina. Following the [[Spanish conquest of Mexico]], the area was awarded to the [[conquistador]] Ojeda el Tuerto. Ojeda introduced [[sugar cane]] into the area, and the San Juan Bautista Nogales sugar mill &ndash; one of the earliest, if not the very first on the American continent &ndash; was later established there.

In 1627, Rodrigo de Viveros y Aberrucia, owner of the sugar mill at the time, was named the First Count of the Valley of [[Orizaba]] by [[Philip III of Spain]].

In 1910, the city of Nogales was awarded the status of a town (''villa'') and, in 1971, city status (''ciudad'').
