'''Maimón''' is a [[town]] and [[List of municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] in the [[Monseñor Nouel Province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]]. Maimón is situated between the Maimón, La Leonora, [[Yuna River|Yuna]] and Zinc rivers, formed by a little valley and mountains in his sides.
The colonies that were established in [[Bonao]] needed to encounter an easy lane to get to [[Concepción de la Vega]], where was settled the general authorities at that time, the first Bonao was established in a little town that today is known as [[Villa Sonador|Sonador]].
