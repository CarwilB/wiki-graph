'''Inzá''' ({{IPA|es|inˈsa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It was part of the [[San Agustín culture]] in the [[Pre-Columbian era]], and home to the [[Tierradentro]] site. It is mostly inhabited by [[Paez people]].
