'''Telpaneca''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Madriz (department)|Madriz]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.el19digital.com/articulos/ver/168201-nuevo-sistema-de-agua-potable-mejora-la-vida-a-familias-de-telpaneca|title=Nuevo Sistema de Agua Potable mejora la vida a familias de Telpaneca|work=[[El 19]]|date=4 September 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It is 218 kilometers from [[Managua]].

== Notable places ==
Notable places in Telpaneca include la cueva del sapo (the frog's cave), la cuesta de la mona (the monkey's slope) and la virgen del chorro (the virgin waterfall). The nearby Mount Malacaste, located at Santo Domingo, offers a fresh environment and an odor of coffee.

== Notable people ==
Among the town's most notable figures are:

* Marcos Iván Vilchez Ruiz, also known as Daz Zuvr. He is a Nicaraguan polymath who stands out as a musician, physicist, mathematician, quantum biology researcher, and author. In the creative field, he is the founder and CEO of Nicánima Estudios, as well as the founder, guitarist, and lead vocalist of the rock band TheVeilers.<ref>{{cite web |author=Rafael Lechado Cruz |date=2026-01-21 |title=Nicánima Estudios: Animación, libros y música inspirados en la mitología e historia nicaragüense |url=https://www.tn8.ni/nacionales/nicanima-estudios-animacion-libros-y-musica-inspirados-en-la-mitologia-e-historia-nicaraguense/ |access-date=2026-05-26 |website=TN8}}</ref>
* Josamary Hazbell Martinez Miranda, member of the Nicaragua National Chess Team, who won 2nd place in the National Chess Competition.<ref>{{cite web |date=2025-05-13 |title=Chess-Results Server Chess-results.com |url=https://s1.chess-results.com/tnr1328321.aspx?lan=2&art=2&rd=4&SNode=S0 |access-date=2026-05-26 |website=Chess-Results}}</ref>
* Josadak Enmanuel Martinez Miranda, member of the Nicaragua National Chess Team, who won 4th place in the National Chess Competition.<ref>{{cite web |date=2025-05-13 |title=Chess-Results Server Chess-results.com |url=https://s1.chess-results.com/tnr1328321.aspx?lan=2&art=2&rd=4&SNode=S0 |access-date=2026-05-26 |website=Chess-Results}}</ref>
* Aldo Josué Aldana, writer of notable books such as ''La casa de piedra.''<ref>{{cite web |date=2025-05-17 |title=La Casa de Piedra: Ideas en torno a la obra de Aldo G. Aldana |url=https://el-viejo-librero.com/2025/05/17/la-casa-de-piedra-ideas-en-torno-a-la-obra-de-aldo-g-aldana/ |access-date=2026-05-26 |website=El Viejo Librero}}</ref>
* Roxana Lisseth Cárdenas Rivas, member of the Nicaragua National Chess Team, Undefeated National Chess Champion in the School Games.<ref>{{cite web |title=FIDE Player Profile |url=https://ratings.fide.com/profile/6112730 |access-date=2026-05-26 |publisher=FIDE}}</ref>
* Guillermo Ardón, introduced electricity and the coffee depulper machine for the first time.
* Chico Barreda, introduced the first mill to the municipality.
* Reynaldo Guillén, recognized local historian.
* Payín Guillén, one of the first musicians in Telpaneca, noted for playing the accordion.
* Esteban Medina, Juan Torrez, Fortino Valles, and Luis Florián, builders of the Cristo Rey Church.
* Chano Valladares, responsible for bringing the newspaper to the locality for the first time.
