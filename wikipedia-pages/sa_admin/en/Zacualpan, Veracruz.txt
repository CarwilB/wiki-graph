'''Zacualpan''' ([[Nahuatl]]: "place above the pyramid") is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|179|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Xalapa]] and {{convert|56|km}} northeast of the city of [[Pachuca]], [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Zacualpan is located in northern Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|400|and(-)|2700|m}}. It borders the Veracruzian municipalities of [[Huayacocotla (municipality)|Huayacocotla]] to the west, [[Texcatepec]] to the north, and [[Tlachichilco]] to the northeast, as well as the Hidalgan municipalities of [[San Bartolo Tutotepec]] to the southwest and [[Agua Blanca de Iturbide]] to the south.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Zacualpan, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=6 March 2018 |archive-date=4 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804015215/http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|264.418|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.4% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/>

Zacualpan is located in the [[Huasteca]] [[Karst]] of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]]. Its land cover is divided between forest (50%), pastureland (25%) and farmland (25%). It is watered by tributaries of the [[Vinazco River]], itself a tributary of the [[Tuxpan River (Veracruz)|Tuxpan River]].<ref name=compendium/>

Zacualpan's climate is humid with rain throughout the year. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|24|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1100|and(-)|2600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The area was originally inhabited by the [[Huastec people]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> Zacualpan was separated from Huayacocotla to form its own municipality on 13 November 1875. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=120, 317 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0 |archive-date=2018-02-09 |access-date=2018-03-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180209182348/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Carina Lugo Barrón.<ref>{{cite news |last=Topete |first=José |url=https://www.alcalorpolitico.com/informacion/pese-a-paridad-de-genero-solo-habra-57-alcaldesas-en-veracruz-235531.html |title=Pese a paridad de género, sólo habrá 57 alcaldesas en Veracruz |language=es |newspaper=Al Calor Político |date=5 June 2017 |access-date=6 March 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Zacualpan recorded a population of 6784 inhabitants living in 1821 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=198 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Zacualpan |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=6 March 2018 |archive-date=7 March 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180307152437/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=198 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 7194 inhabitants in Zacualpan.<ref name=survey/>

There are 69 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Zacualpan, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 633 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Major economic activities in Zacualpan include forestry and farming. Zacualpan produced {{convert|33983|m3}} of timber in 2015, second to Huayacocotla among municipalities in Veracruz.<ref>{{cite book |url=http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2017/01/AEyGE2016.pdf |title=Cuadro 14.1: Volumen de la producción forestal maderable por municipio según grupo de especies |language=es |publisher=INEGI |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave 2016 |isbn=978-607-739-964-3}}</ref> Cattle and pigs are the main livestock raised, while corn, peaches and apples are the main agricultural crops.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2016/05/Zacualpan.pdf |title=Cuadernillos municipales, 2016: Zacualpan |language=es |page=6 |publisher=SEFIPLAN Veracruz |access-date=6 March 2018}}</ref>
