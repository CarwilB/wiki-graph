'''Urrao''' is a town and municipality of [[Colombia]], located in the subregion [[Southwest Antioquia | southwest]] of the department of [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]]. Limited by the north with the municipalities of [[Frontino (Colombia) | Frontino]] and [[Abriaquí]], in the east with the municipalities of [[Abriaquí]], [[Caicedo, Antioquia|Caicedo]], [[Anzá]], [[Betulia,_Antioquia |Betulia]] and [[Salgar]], and in the south and west with [[Vigía del Fuerte]] and the department of [[Chocó Department|Chocó]]. It is the second largest municipality in the department. As of 2015, the population comprised 44,648 people. Professional cyclist [[Rigoberto Urán]] was born and raised here.<ref name="Señal Colombia">{{cite web |date=26 June 2018 |title=¿Qué sería de Rigo sin Urrao? |trans-title=What would Rigo be without Urrao? |url=https://www.senalcolombia.tv/deportes/historia-de-rigoberto-uran-y-urrao |access-date=15 September 2020 |website=Señal Colombia |publisher=Radio Televisión Nacional de Colombia |language=es}}</ref>
==Climate==
The climate is warm and temperate in Urrao. There is significant rainfall throughout the year in Urrao. Even the driest month still has a lot of rainfall. According to Köppen and Geiger, this climate is classified as Cfb. The average temperature in Urrao is 15.7 °C | 60.3 °F. About 7573 mm | 298.1 inch of precipitation falls annually.
