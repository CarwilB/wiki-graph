'''Cabo de Hornos''' (''[[Cape Horn]]'') is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the south of [[Tierra del Fuego]] [[archipelago]], in [[Antártica Province]], [[Magallanes Region]]. The municipality of Cabo de Hornos, located in the town of [[Puerto Williams]], also administers the [[Antártica (Commune)|Antártica]] commune. It is named for [[Cape Horn|Cabo de Hornos, or "Cape Horn", the southern tip of South America]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Cabo de Hornos spans an area of {{convert|15853.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 2,262 inhabitants (1,403 men and 859 women). Of these, 1,952 (86.3%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 310 (13.7%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 24.7% (448 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Cabo de Hornos is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2016-2020 alcalde is Patricio Fernández ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]]). The communal council has the following members:

Daniel Fernando Valdebenito Contreras (PS) Ángela Barría Barrientos (RN) Juan Velásquez (PS) Carolina Guenel González (DC) Francis Delgado Ibaceta (RN) Paola Speake Ojeda (DC)<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Cabo de Hornos is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region).
