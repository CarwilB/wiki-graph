'''Isla de Pascua''' (Easter Island; Rapa Nui) is a [[Chile|Chilean]] [[Communes of Chile|commune]] with a special regime,<ref group="note">Although the [[Chilean Constitution of 1980|Constitution]] establishes the commune of Isla de Pascua has a special regime, the organic law supposed to regulate the area has not yet been dictated.</ref> located within [[Isla de Pascua Province]] in [[Valparaíso Region]]. It is the only commune in Isla de Pascua Province, comprising [[Easter Island]] and [[Isla Salas y Gómez]]. Its capital is [[Hanga Roa]], located in the southwestern area of the main island, where most of the local population resides.

==Local government==
The municipality of Isla de Pascua is led by mayor Elizabeth Arévalo Pakarati, who is accompanied in the Municipal Council by:

* [[Pedro Edmunds Paoa]] (Ind./[[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Omar Alfonso Enrique Veriveri Durán (Ind./[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])

* Ivonne Nahoe Zamora ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Javiera Paricia Lira Hucke ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

== Public services ==
With regard to public health, Hanga Roa Hospital is a healthcare facility that has operated on the island since 1917, originally as a medical outpost. Its current facilities were inaugurated in 2012.

In terms of public order and citizen security, the 6th Easter Island Police Station is a unit of the Carabineros of Chile, under the authority of Valparaíso Prefecture No. 9. In addition, the Chilean Investigative Police (''Policía de Investigaciones de Chile'', PDI) maintains a Criminal Investigation Brigade and is also responsible for immigration entry and exit controls for the island, given its status as a Special Territory.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 August 2018 |title=Nuevo control migratorio para visitar Rapa Nui |url=https://www.pdichile.cl/centro-de-prensa/detalle-prensa/2018/08/01/nuevo-control-migratorio-para-visitar-rapa-nui |access-date=13 December 2025 |website=[[Investigations Police of Chile]] |language=es}}</ref>

The Easter Island Fire Department was founded on 16 August 1972 and consists of a single company that serves the entire municipal area.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cuerpo de Bomberos de Isla de Pascua |url=https://www.bomberos.cl/cuerpo-de-bomberos-de-isla-de-pascua |access-date=13 December 2025 |website=Bomberos de Chile |language=es}}</ref>

The Santiago Katipare Pakarati Ranguitaki Reading Center (''Centro Lector Santiago Katipare Pakarati Ranguitaki''), founded on 26 April 2019, is a public library and cultural center of the municipality, which replaced the former Rapa Nui Municipal Library. In addition, the [[Biblioteca William Mulloy|William Mulloy Library]] houses the personal bibliographic collection of the American anthropologist specializing in Rapa Nui studies.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Biblioteca William Mulloy |url=https://www.museorapanui.gob.cl/biblioteca-william-mulloy |access-date=13 December 2025 |website=Museo de Rapa Nui |language=es}}</ref>
