'''Aquin''' ({{IPA|fr|akɛ̃}}; {{langx|ht|Aken}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Aquin Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It is a port on the south coast of the [[Tiburon Peninsula, Haiti|Tiburon Peninsula]]. It had 104,216 inhabitants in 2015,<ref name=IHSI/> up from 1,799 in 1950.<ref>Columbia-Lippincott Gazetteer, p. 86</ref>
