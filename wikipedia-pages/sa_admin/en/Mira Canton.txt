'''Mira Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Carchi Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Mira, Carchi|Mira]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 12,919<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and was 12,180 in the 2010 census.<ref>"Ecuador: Municipal Division" https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 13 July 2017</ref> The area is {{convert|588|sqkm|sqmi}}.  

The canton is located in the [[Andes]] and western foothills of the Andes on the upper tributaries of the [[Mira River (South America)|Mira River]]. The town of Mira has an elevation of {{convert|2423|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>  

The parishes in the canton are Concepción, Jijón y Caamańo, Juan Montalvo (San Ignacio de Quil), and Mira (Chontahuasi).<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php|title = Ecuador: Municipal Division (Provinces and Parishes) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map}}</ref>
