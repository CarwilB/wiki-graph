'''Coromandel''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]], located in the southeast region of the country, in the [[Brazilian state|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population is estimated at 28,894 people as of 2022.<ref>{{cite web |title=Coromandel |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/coromandel/panorama |website=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |access-date=10 February 2025 |language=pt}}</ref> The municipality ''(município)'' of Coromandel is located 495&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Belo Horizonte]], and 673&nbsp;km from [[São Paulo]]. Coromandel is one of the municipalities of the [[Triângulo Mineiro]]/[[Alto Paranaíba]] micro region and its territorial size is 3,296&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its borders are limited in the north by state of [[Goiás]] and [[Guarda-Mor]] municipality, east by the municipalities of [[Vazante]], [[Lagamar]], [[Patos de Minas]] and [[Guimarânia]], west by the municipalities of [[Abadia dos Dourados]] and [[Monte Carmelo]], and south by Patrocínio municipality. Coromandel has six districts: Alegre, Santa Rosa, Pântano de Santa Cruz, Lagamar dos Coqueiros and Mateiro.

Its [[economy]] is largely promoted by [[cerrado]] coffee agriculture (the legitimate Brazilian coffee for exportation) and [[plantation]]s of [[soybean]] and [[maize|corn]]. Also [[dairy farm]]s supply several dairy products industries in the [[city]]. [[Cattle]] farms supply with its [[livestock]] [[meat]] to industries. Another important part of the economy is [[mining]], for example the [[calcareous]] [[Rock (geology)|rock]]s exploration that is plenty used for [[agriculture]]s to correct soil [[acidity]]. [[Diamond]] exploration has its importance as well, especially for local miners and companies. One of the most important diamonds in the world was discovered in Coromandel in 1938, named as [[Getúlio Vargas Diamond]], it was a pure, rare and precious stone.

The origins of the name Coromandel is not decisively known among its population, but older generations believe the name is connected to the [[Coromandel Coast]], in India, from where ships loaded with slaves would have departed.{{citation needed|reason=|date=July 2016}}
