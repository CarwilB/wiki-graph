'''Agua Prieta''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=4 January 2010 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archivedate=31 July 2012 }}</ref> In 2015, the municipality had a total population of 82,918;<ref name="inegi.org.mx">{{cite web|url=http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/son/poblacion/|title=Número de habitantes. Sonora|website=Cuentame.inegi.org.mx|accessdate=9 July 2018|archive-date=22 October 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171022133712/http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/son/poblacion/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
by 2020, it had risen to 91,929.<ref>{{cite web |title=Agua Prieta: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/agua-prieta |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

The municipal seat is the border city of [[Agua Prieta, Sonora]].
