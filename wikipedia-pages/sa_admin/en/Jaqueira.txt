'''Jaqueira''' is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]]  of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]] with an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 11,644.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/jaqueira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
Jaqueira is located in the Brazilian state of [[Pernambuco]], in the coastal [[Zona da Mata]] region. The city is 147 kilometers away from the state's capital city of [[Recife]]. It is bound by the Lagoa dos Gatos to the north, [[Maraial]] to the south, [[Catende]] to the east, and [[São Benedito do Sul]] to the west. The city has an area of 89.09 square kilometers, at an elevation of 143. The area has a humid, tropical climate, and Jaqueira's annual average temperature is 25.2&nbsp;°C. Near the city are the [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una River]] and the [[Subcaducifólia]] forest.

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Jaqueira are based in [[agribusiness]], particularly [[sugarcane]] and [[banana]]s.
