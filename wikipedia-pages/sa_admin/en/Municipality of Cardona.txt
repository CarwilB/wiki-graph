'''Cardona''' is a [[Uruguay]]an [[Municipalities of Uruguay|municipality]] located in the [[Soriano Department|department of Soriano]]. Its seat is the city of [[Cardona, Uruguay|Cardona]].

== History ==
The municipality of Cardona was created on March 15, 2010, by Law 18653, located within the department of Soriano, comprising the MFA constituency of the department.{{cn|date=January 2023}}
