'''Pestel''' ({{IPA|fr|pɛstɛl}}; {{langx|ht|Pestèl}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Corail Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 44,659 inhabitants in 2015. The commune also includes the [[Grande Cayemite Island|Cayemite Islands]].

==Locations in Pestèl==

Mainland: [[Bourjoly]], [[Fond Gondai]], Jean Bellune, [[Joly Guirbert]], [[La Salle, Grand'Anse|La Salle]], [[La Source, Corail, Haiti|La Source]], [[Lere de L'Eau]], [[Nan Dane]], [[Nan Palmiste]], [[Paviton]], [[Pestèl City|Pestèl]] and [[Plane Martin]], Glode.

[[Les Cayemites|Grande Cayemite]]: [[Anse a Macon]], [[Anse du Nord]], [[Au Bord de l'Etang]], [[Mare Citron]], [[Nan Palmis]] and [[Pointe Sable]].
