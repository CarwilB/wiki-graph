'''Mariscal Ramón Castilla''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for [[Ramón Castilla|Marshal Ramón Castilla]]) is one of the eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Loreto Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was created by Law No. 22728 on October 18, 1979, by President [[Francisco Morales Bermúdez]]. The province was named after [[Ramón Castilla]].

Its territory is mostly flat and covered by the [[Amazon rainforest]].
