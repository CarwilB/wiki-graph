'''Tupungato''' is a [[departments of Argentina|department]] located in the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Mendoza Province|Mendoza]], [[Argentina]]. The cabecera (departmental capital), Tupungato, is located approximately {{cvt|70|km}} south of [[Mendoza, Argentina|Mendoza]] city, in the Valle de Uco.

Its name comes from the [[Tupungato]] volcano (at 6,570 metres, one of Argentina's highest peaks). The volcano's name is said to mean "star viewpoint" in the Huarpe language.{{citation needed|date=December 2017}}

The department is approximately 1,200 metres [[above mean sea level]], and comprises {{cvt|2,485|km2}} (1.6% of the provincial total). According to the {{census-ar|2001}}, the total population of the department was 28,539 with a density of 11.48 inhabitants per km<sup>2</sup>.

The region is mainly dependent on agricultural production, with vineyards being its most important produce, representing 30% of the planted lands. Other regional products are fruit (mainly [[peach]]) and [[garlic]]. The [[wine]] industry, however, is particularly relevant due to the constant investments and intensive labour occupation. Recent developments in the [[economy of Argentina|Argentine economy]] have further fuelled the [[Argentine wine]] industry, attracting foreign investors and tourists to the region.
