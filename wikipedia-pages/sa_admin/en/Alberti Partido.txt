'''Alberti''' is a northern central [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]].

Alberti is located at the south of the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area. It has an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">1,130&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(436&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span> and 10,322 inhabitants ({{census-ar|2001}}).

==Economy==
The economy of Alberti is dominated by agriculture. Its main products are [[wheat]], [[maize]], [[soya bean]]s, [[oat]]s, [[sunflower]]s, [[sorghum]], and industrial crops.{{citation needed|date=February 2024}}
