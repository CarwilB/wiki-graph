'''Quípama''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Western Boyacá Province]].

It is home of one of the largest [[Colombian emeralds|emerald]] mines in the world.

==Climate==
Quípama has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
