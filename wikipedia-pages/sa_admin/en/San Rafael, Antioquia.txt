'''San Rafael''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Part of the subregion of [[Eastern Antioquia]].

The town was founded in 1864 by a group of miners who were attracted to the abundance of gold in the region.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Pazera |first=John and Susan |date=2020-07-02 |title=Two Pleasant Colombian Towns: San Rafael and San Carlos |url=https://latitudeadjustmentblog.com/2020/07/02/two-pleasant-colombian-towns-san-rafael-and-san-carlos/ |access-date=2022-07-13 |website=Latitude Adjustment |language=en-US}}</ref> It is also rich in water resources.<ref name=":0" /> The population was 12,578 at the 2018 census.
