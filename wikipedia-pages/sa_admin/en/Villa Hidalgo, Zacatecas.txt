'''Villa Hidalgo''' is a town and its surrounding [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]]. Prior to 1934, the settlement was known as '''Santa Rita'''.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Rodríguez Hernández |first1=Leonor |title=Villa Hidalgo, Zacatecas: origen y evolución de la antigua estancia de Santa Rita, 1790-1807 |url=http://ricaxcan.uaz.edu.mx/xmlui/handle/20.500.11845/1470 |publisher=Autonomous University of Zacatecas |access-date=13 September 2023}}</ref> 

In 2020, the municipality reported a total population of 19,446.<ref>{{cite web |title=Acerca de Villa Hidalgo |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/es/profile/geo/villa-hidalgo-32054 |website=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=12 September 2023}}</ref>
