'''Santa Maria Madalena''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈsɐ̃tɐ mɐˈɾi.ɐ madɐˈlẽnɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 10,392 (2020) and its area is 816&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|21444|ha|adj=on}} [[Desengano State Park]], created in 1970.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Upam Desengano flagra corte de madeira nativa ... Inea}}|title=Upam Desengano flagra corte de madeira nativa em Santa Maria Madalena|date=14 October 2015|publisher=Inea: Instituto Estadual do Ambiente|url=http://200.20.53.3:8081/Portal/Noticias/INEA0110557|accessdate=2017-01-12|archive-date=2017-01-13|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170113150523/http://200.20.53.3:8081/Portal/Noticias/INEA0110557|url-status=dead}}</ref>
