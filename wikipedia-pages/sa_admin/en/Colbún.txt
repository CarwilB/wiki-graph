'''Colbún''' ([[Mapudungun]]: "Cleaning of the land") is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Linares Province]], [[Maule Region]]. The commune has a population of over 17,000 inhabitants and covers an area of {{convert|2900|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, making it the province's largest. Its capital, the town of Colbún, has 3,679 inhabitants (2002 [[census]]). It is {{convert|7|km|0|abbr=on}} west of the center of [[continental Chile]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Colbún spans an area of {{convert|2899.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 17,619 inhabitants (8,943 men and 8,676 women). Of these, 5,152 (29.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 12,467 (70.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 3.9% (669 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Geography==
The Colbún commune is bordered on the west by [[Yerbas Buenas]]; on the southwest by [[Linares, Chile|Linares]] and [[Longaví]]; on the north by [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]] ([[Talca Province]]); on the east and southeast, by [[Argentina]], and on the south, by [[Parral, Chile|Parral]] and [[San Fabián]]; the latter in [[Ñuble Region]]. The municipality of Colbún occupies the easternmost strip of the [[Linares Province|Province of Linares]], which boasts some of the highest provincial [[Andes|peaks]] and the headwaters of several provincial rivers.

==Foundation and Historical facts==
The municipality of Colbún was officially founded December 30, 1927.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.municipalidadcolbun.cl/Historia%20de%20la%20Comuna.html |title=Municipalidad de Colbún |accessdate=2012-03-04 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120624033340/http://www.municipalidadcolbun.cl/Historia%20de%20la%20Comuna.html |archivedate=2012-06-24 }} (Spanish). Retrieved march 4, 2012</ref> Prior to that date the municipality was called [[Panimávida]] and had its headquarters in that town. The municipality of Panimávida had been founded May 6, 1906.<ref>http://descubrelinares.cl/?page_id=1813 {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120826100233/http://descubrelinares.cl/?page_id=1813 |date=2012-08-26 }} (Spanish) Retrieved march 4, 2012</ref>

From 1913 to 1954 Colbún was the terminus of the narrow gauge [[Putagán—Colbún railway]] originating in the city of [[Linares, Chile|Linares]].<ref>[https://www.flickr.com/photos/29624311@N06/2767060712/ La estacion del tren (Spanish)] Retrieved march 4, 2012</ref>

==Administration==

Colbún is administered by Mayor Pedro Pablo Muñoz (Independent), who has been elected for the period 2021-2024, and the Municipal Council is composed of:

* Ángel Cárter Ramos
* Italo Sepúlveda Oses
* José Olivares Quiroz
* Renato Hernández
* Janette Molina
* Jorge Dedes Franco

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Colbún is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Jorge Tarud ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Romilio Gutiérrez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 39th electoral district, together with [[Linares (Chile)|Linares]], [[San Javier, Chile|San Javier]], [[Villa Alegre, Chile|Villa Alegre]] and [[Yerbas Buenas]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Hernán Larraín]] (UDI) and Ximena Rincón González (PDC) as part of the 11th senatorial constituency (Maule-South).

==Attractions==
In the vicinity of Colbún there are two well-known Chilean [[spa town|spa]]s: the "Termas de [[Quinamávida]]" and the "Termas de [[Panimávida]]". In addition, the municipality concentrates the [[hydroelectric]] industry of the province, with the important [[power station]]s of Colbún and Machicura. The Colbún [[dam]] [[lake]] is a centre for recreation and [[water sports]] although these are highly dependent on water levels, which tend to be erratic.

==Local customs==

===Crafts===
Near Panimávida lies the village of [[Rari (village)|Rari]], famous for its unique [[craft]]s made of horse hair.
