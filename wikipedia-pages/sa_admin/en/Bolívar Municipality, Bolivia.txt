'''Bolívar Municipality'''  is a [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Cochabamba Department|Cochabamba]]. The municipality and [[Bolívar Province, Cochabamba|Bolívar Province]] are identical. Its seat is the city of [[Bolívar (Cochabamba)|Bolívar]], named after [[Simón Bolívar]], a [[Venezuela]]n military and political leader.

== Subdivision ==
The municipality is divided into nine [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
