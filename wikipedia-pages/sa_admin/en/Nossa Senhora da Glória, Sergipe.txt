'''Nossa Senhora da Glória''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈnɔsɐ sẽˈɲɔɾɐ da ˈɡlɔɾi.ɐ|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 37,324 (2020) and its area is {{convert|757.45|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. It has a population density of 48 inhabitants per square kilometer. Nossa Senhora da Glória is located {{convert|126|km2|mi2|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/>
