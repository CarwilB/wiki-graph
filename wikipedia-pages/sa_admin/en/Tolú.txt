'''Tolú''', also known as '''Santiago de Tolú''', is a small town in [[Sucre Department]], northern [[Colombia]] on the Caribbean coast. The municipality has an area of 500&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is named after the Tolú, one of the [[Pre-Colombian era|pre-Columbian]] [[Indigenous peoples of South America|indigenous people]] of the [[Geography of Colombia|North Colombia lowlands]].<ref>{{citation|title=[[Handbook of South American Indians]]|volume=4|pages=329–338|year=1948|editor=[[Julian H. Steward]]|publisher=U.S. Government Printing Office}}</ref>

The municipality of Tolú borders to the North with [[San Onofre, Sucre|San Onofre]], to the East with [[Toluviejo]], to the South with [[Coveñas]], [[Palmito, Sucre|Palmito]] and [[Sincelejo]].
