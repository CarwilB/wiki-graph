'''Bolívar''' is a town and [[municipality]] in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref name=caucanet/> Founded in October 1784 by Domingo Belisario Gómez, the municipality covers an area of {{Convert|746.3|km|0|abbr=on}} and has a population of 57,511.<ref name=caucanet/> The population is primarily engaged in agriculture and [[ranch]]ing.<ref name=caucanet/>
