''' Canelas''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Canelas, Durango|Canelas]]. The municipality covers an area of 683.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

{{As of|2015}}, the municipality had a total population of 4,683.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cuentame.org.mx/monografias/informacion/dur/poblacion/default.aspx?tema=me&e=10|title=Número de habitantes. Durango|access-date=2020-01-16|archive-date=2019-04-11|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190411052801/http://www.cuentame.org.mx/monografias/informacion/dur/poblacion/default.aspx?tema=me&e=10|url-status=dead}}</ref>
It is located in the Sierra Madre Occidental.

==Settlements==
Canelas has some 70 small villages and ranches.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10002a.htm |title=Durango - Canelas |access-date=2008-11-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061124105914/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10002a.htm |archive-date=2006-11-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Some of these locations have sawmills and produce wood. This region of Durango has a variety of minerals. These two natural resources provide many people with jobs in Canelas.{{cn|date=December 2023}}
