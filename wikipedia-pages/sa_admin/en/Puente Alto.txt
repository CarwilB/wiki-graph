'''Puente Alto''' (Spanish: "Tall Bridge") is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]]. It is the capital of the [[Cordillera Province, Chile|Cordillera Province]] in the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]], and is located in the south-east corner of the [[Santiago|Greater Santiago]] conurbation. It has a population of 568,086, making it the [[Communes of Chile#List of communes by region and province|most populous commune of Chile]].

==History==

After 1884, the Province of Santiago was divided into three departments: Santiago, La Victoria, and Melipilla.

In 1891, the "Autonomic Commune Law" was enacted, after which the president issued the Decree for the Creation of Municipalities of Chile.

Following this decree, in 1891, the municipalities of Peñaflor, Talagante, Calera de Tango, San José de Maipo, and Lo Cañas were established in the department of La Victoria. On November 18, 1892, the municipalities of Puente Alto and La Granja were created in the same department, leading to the dissolution of the municipality of Lo Cañas.

Thus, the Municipality of Puente Alto, which included the subdelegations 12th, Puente Nuevo de Pirque, 15th, Lo Cañas, and 16th, El Peral from the Department of La Victoria, was officially established by law on November 18, 1892, and published in the Official Gazette No. 4376 of 1892.

According to the Autonomic Commune Law, the municipality's governing body consisted of at least nine councilors, with the number increasing proportionally to the population, and three mayors. This system granted the municipalities greater autonomy and more powers, allowing them to operate more independently from the central government.

A letter from the Mayor’s Office of Puente Alto to the Governor of the Department of La Victoria, dated May 15, 1894, reveals the first mayors of the commune:

<blockquote>
''“It being convenient for Your Honor to be informed about the organization of the Municipality of Puente Alto for the relations between it and your government, I have the honor to inform Your Honor that in the first ordinary session, Don Carlos Aldunate Solar was appointed as the first mayor, Don Victorino Rojas Magallanes as the second, and Don Rafael Correa Echagüe as the third.''  
''This I communicate to Your Honor for any necessary action.''  
''God guide Your Honor.”''
</blockquote>

As of the 2024 census, the commune has a population of 568,086, of which 48.2% are male and 51.8% are female. People under 15 years old make up 18.4% of the population, and people over 65 years old make up 11.7%. 99.9% of the population is urban and 0.1% is rural.<ref name="2024census" />

=== Immigration ===
As of the 2024 census, immigrants make up 5.2% of the population - 4.5% are from [[South America]], 0.6% from [[North America]], 0.1% from [[Europe]], 0.1% from [[Asia]], 0.005% from [[Africa]], and 0.002% from [[Oceania]].<ref name="2024census2" />

==Administration==
As a commune, Puente Alto is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (mayor) who is directly elected every four years. The mayor for the 2024-2028 term is Matias Toledo (Ind).<ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.mpuentealto.cl/concejo-municipal/|title=Concejales|work=Municipality of Puente Alto|access-date=10 October 2024|language=es}}</ref>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Puente Alto is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Álvaro Carter ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]), Ximena Ossandón ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]), Daniela Serrano (PCCh), Ana María Gazmuri (AH), Mónica Arce Castro (Ind/PH), Pamela Jiles (PH) and Hernán Palma Pérez (PH), as part of the 12th electoral district, (together with [[La Florida, Chile|La Florida]], [[Pirque]], [[San José de Maipo]] and [[La Pintana]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Manuel José Ossandón Irarrázaval (RN), Fabiola Campillai Rojas (Ind), Claudia Pascual (PCCh), Luciano Cruz Coke (EVOP) and Jose Manuel Rojo Edwards (PRCh) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago Metropolitan Region).

==Transportation==

Puente Alto is connected to the rest of Santiago though the [[Red Metropolitana de Movilidad|Red Metropolitana de Mobilidad]], which includes bus lines and [[Santiago Metro Line 4|Line 4]] of the [[Santiago Metro]], which has its southern terminus on [[Plaza de Puente Alto metro station|Plaza de Puente Alto station]], beneath the commune's main square. Travel time to downtown Santiago via the metro is approximately 45 minutes.

== Areas of Interest ==

=== Estadio Municipal ===
Puente Alto's city stadium is located at 450 Nemesio Vicuña and features a football field and an athletics track. The stadium has a capacity of 1900, making it one of the biggest sports centers of the commune.

On May 30, 2011, the city stadium was re-inaugurated by the Mayor Manuel J. Ossandón.

=== <span class="anchor" id="Mampato"></span>Mampato amusement park ===
Mampato is an amusement park with various activities for children and teenagers. Its main attractions include the trains, the trampolines and tricycles. It was inaugurated in 1998 in Las Vizcachas, Puente Alto.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.mampato.cl/historia|title=Mampato, Parques de Diversiones|website=Mampato, Parques de Diversiones|access-date=2017-11-15}}</ref>

=== Pueblito Las Vizcachas ===

"Pueblito las Vizcachas" is a large, well-maintained park in Puente Alto frequented by families and residents in the area. The park opened on December 30, 2015. Visitors have the opportunity to partake in kayaking, interact with farm animals and enjoy other activities in the green areas without paying an entrance fee.
