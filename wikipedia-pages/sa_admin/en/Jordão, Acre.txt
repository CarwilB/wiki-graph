'''Jordão''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʒoʁˈdɐ̃w|lang}}; 'Jordan') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western region of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 9,222  and its area is 5,429&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality contains 62% of the [[Alto Tarauacá Extractive Reserve]], created in 2000.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Alto Tarauacá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Alto Tarauacá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
