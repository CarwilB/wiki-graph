'''Santo Antônio do Paraíso''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning "Saint Anthony of paradise") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,068 (2020 est.) in an area of 165.90&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/235AO Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>
