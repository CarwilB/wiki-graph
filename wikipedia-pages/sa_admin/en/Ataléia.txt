'''Ataléia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of [[Minas Gerais]] in Brazil. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 12,678 people living in a total area of 1,838.384&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1943.<ref name="ibge.gov.br">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Ataléia is located at an elevation of 240 meters, 70&nbsp;km. east of [[Teófilo Otoni]] and a short distance from the boundary with the state of [[Espírito Santo]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 516&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Teófilo Otoni]], [[Carlos Chagas, Minas Gerais|Carlos Chagas]], the state of [[Espírito Santo]], [[Frei Gaspar]] and [[Ouro Verde de Minas]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |archive-date=2008-06-13 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|53|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 34 million reais from services, 7 million reais from industry, and 3 million reais from agriculture. There were 1,211 rural producers on 102,000 hectares of land. Only 52 farms had tractors in 2006. Approximately 3,200 persons were dependent on agriculture.  The main crops were bananas, coffee, sugarcane, beans and corn. There were 111,000 head of cattle in 2006. There were 2 banks: Bradesco and Sicoob in 2007 and 438 automobiles (361 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 3,400 inhabitants per automobile.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

==Health and education==
There were 5 health clinics and 1 hospital with 12 beds. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni.  Educational needs were met by 16 primary schools, 2 middle schools, and 7 pre-primary schools.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>
