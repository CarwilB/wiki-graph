'''Púcuro''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 356 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 358; its population as of 2000 was 295.<ref name=inec/>  It lies along the Púcuro river, which is a tributary of the [[Tuira River|Tuira]].<ref name="geo">[https://figshare.com/articles/journal_contribution/12337895  The Geology of the Darien, Panama, and the late Miocene-Pliocene collision of the Panama arc with northwestern South America], GSA Bulletin (2004) 116 (11-12): 1327–1344</ref>
