'''Colta Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Chimborazo Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[Cajabamba, Ecuador|Cajabamba]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 44,701.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Cities==
Besides the capital, [[Cacha]] is most likely included in the canton as well.
