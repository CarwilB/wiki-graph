'''Tlanchinol''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|380.3|km2}}. The town stands on [[Mexican Federal Highway 105|Federal Highway 105]] in the mountains of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]]. 

The name comes from the [[Nahuatl]] ''Tlanchinolli'' ("burnt house") and ''icpac'' (locative of superposition); hence: "on the burnt house". An earlier form of the name was '''Tlanchinaltic'''.<ref>{{cite web |title=Nomenclatura |url=https://tlanchinol.gob.mx/tu-municipio/nomenclatura |website=Municipio de Tlanchinol |access-date=13 December 2025}}</ref>

The municipality was created in 1869.<ref>{{cite web |title=Cronología de hechos históricos |url=https://tlanchinol.gob.mx/tu-municipio/cronologia-de-hechos-historicos |website=Municipio de Tlanchinol |access-date=13 December 2025}}</ref>
In the 2020 [[INEGI]] census, it reported a total population of 37,722, up from 33,694 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=Tlanchinol: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tlanchinol |website=Data México |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=13 December 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Tlanchinol  |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13073a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]| accessdate=27 December 2008 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20070523121859/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13073a.htm |archive-date=23 May 2007}} </ref>
