'''José Azueta''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
It is located in south zone of the State of [[Veracruz (state)|Veracruz]], about 205&nbsp;km from state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 582.63 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|04|N|95|42|W|}}.
===Borders===
José Azueta Municipality is delimited to the north by [[Tlacotalpan Municipality]] and [[Amatitlán Municipality]] to the east by [[Isla Municipality]], to the south by [[Playa Vicente Municipality]], to the west by [[Chacaltianguis Municipality]], [[Cosamaloapan Municipality]] and [[Oaxaca State]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30089a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: José Azueta|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070127051620/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30089a.htm|archive-date=2007-01-27|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Weather===
The weather in José Azueta is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]],  [[beans]], [[rice]], [[watermelon]], [[green chile]] and [[sugarcane]].

==Events==
In '''José Azueta''', the celebration in honor to Cristo Rey, Patron of the town takes place in November.
