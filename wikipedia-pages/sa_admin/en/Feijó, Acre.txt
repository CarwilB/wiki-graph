'''Feijó''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|fejˈʒɔ|native}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. As of 2022, its population was  35,426. The total land area is 24,202&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The city is served by [[Feijó Airport]].

The municipality contains {{convert|231555|ha|adj=on}} of [[Santa Rosa do Purus National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created  in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Santa Rosa do Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
