'''Zarumilla''' is one of the three [[Provinces of Peru|province]]s of the [[Tumbes Region]] in northwestern [[Peru]]. It borders the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the north, [[Ecuador]] on the south and east, and the [[Tumbes Province]] on the west. 

Its capital is the town of [[Zarumilla]]. Other important settlements in the province include [[Matapalo]] and the border town of [[Aguas Verdes]], which is connected to Ecuador by a bridge.

Zarumilla was established as a province on November 17, 1942.

The [[Zarumilla River]], located in the eastern part of the province, marks the border between Peru and Ecuador. In 1998, during [[El Niño]], the river changed its course, moving {{convert|1|km2|acre}} of land into its eastern margin. Ecuadorians took advantage of this situation, saying that the river is the natural border marker and therefore gives them rights in the affected area. The Peruvians argued that the area is sovereign Peruvian soil.
