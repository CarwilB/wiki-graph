'''São Domingos''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈsɐ̃w dʊˈmĩɡʊs}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Sergipe]].
