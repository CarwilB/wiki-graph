'''Yupiltepeque''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of {{cvt|55.44|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 18,419 inhabitants.

==History==
The name Yupiltepeque is believed to have been derive from the [[Pipil people|Pipil]]-[[Xinka people|Xinka]] language, combining "yupil" meaning "flower village" and "tepeq" meaning "hill", referring to a hill on which king Tepoc resided in the ancient Pipil settlement now identified as Pueblo Viejo village. The Xincas inhabited southeastern Guatemala before the arrival of the Pipils, who migrated into the region between the 7th and 12th centuries and eventually subordinated several Xinca communities. The Xincas then expanded into areas including the present-day Yupiltepeque. During the Spanish conquest, [[Pedro de Alvarado]] invaded the Xinca territory with the support of other indigenous people, and captured the city.

According to the official decree on 27 August 1836, Yupiltepeque was attached to the circuit of Mita in the department of [[Chiquimula]]. Mita was later divided into three districts including [[Jutiapa]] on 23 February 1848, and Yupiltepeque was within the jurisdiction of Jutiapa. While it was briefly returned to the original jurisdiction from 1850 to 1852, the department of Jutiapa was created on 8 October 1852 and included Yupiltepeque. On 11 July 1906, former El Salvadoran president [[Tomás Regalado (Salvadoran politician)|Tomás Regalado]] was killed here during the [[Totoposte Wars|war with Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Yupiltepeque|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Yupiltepeque municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-yupiltepeque-jutiapa/|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Yupiltepeque is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|55.44|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Yupiltepeque (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2206__yupiltepeque/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It is situated about {{cvt|145|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|23|km}} from the departmental seat of [[Jutiapa]]. It borders Jutiapa to the north, [[Jerez, Jutiapa|Jerez]] to the south, [[Asuncion Mita]] and [[Atescatempa]] to the east, and [[Zapotitlán, Jutiapa|Zapotitlan]] and [[El Adelanto]] to the west.<ref>{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Yupiltepeque, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/geografia-del-municipio-de-yupiltepeque-jutiapa/|trans-title=Geography of Yupiltepeque municipality|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical monsoon climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|26.25|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|8.18|cm}} and has 129.11 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/yupiltepeque|title=Yupiltepeque|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Yupiltepeque had an estimated population of 18,419 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 9,259 males and 9,160 females. About 28.4% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.5% was over the age of 65 years. About 82.7% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (17.3%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (88.2%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 83.1%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (76.5%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (22.6%) forming a significant minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.3%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
