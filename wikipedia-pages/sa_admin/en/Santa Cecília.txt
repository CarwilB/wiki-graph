'''Santa Cecília''' is a [[city]] in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], in the [[Southern Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. The municipality was created in 1958 out of the existing municipality of [[Curitibanos]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://leisestaduais.com.br/sc/lei-promulgada-n-348-1958-santa-catarina-altera-a-divisao-territorial-do-estado|title=Lei promulgada nº 348|publisher=Legislação Estadual de Santa Catarina|lang=pt|date=21 June 1958}}</ref>
