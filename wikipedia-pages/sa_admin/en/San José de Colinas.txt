'''San José de Colinas''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|241.5|km2}} and had a population of 18,791 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
San José de Colinas was earlier known as "San Antonio". The municipality was established under the name "Nueva Florida de San José" on 28 January 1812.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=San José de Colinas, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/San-Jos%C3%A9-de-Colinas.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of San José de Colinas|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-san-jose-colinas/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San José de Colinas is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[San Luis, Santa Bárbara|San Luis]] and [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] to the north, [[Nuevo Celilac]], [[Gualala, Honduras|Gualala]], and [[Santa Bárbara (municipality)|Santa Bárbara]] to the south, [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] and [[Ilama, Honduras|Ilama]] to the east, and [[San Luis, Santa Bárbara|San Luis]] to the west. The municipality also along the [[Jicatuyo River|Jicatuyo]] and [[Ulúa River]]s.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|241.5|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1606__san_jos%C3%A9_de_colinas/|title=San Jose de Colinas|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|784.3|m}} above sea level, San José de Colinas has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|21.46|C}} and receives about {{cvt|294.1|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/san-jose-de-colinas|title=San Jose de Colinas, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, San José de Colinas had a total population of 18,791 inhabitants, of whom 9,836 (52.3%) were men and 8,955 (47.7%) were women. The municipality had an urban population of 5,215 (27.7%) and a rural population of 13,576 (72.3%). The intercensal growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 1.7%.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

About 1% of the population were classified as Indigenous and the rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 32.4% of the population were aged 0–14 years, 59.8% were aged 15–64, and 7.9% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 24.9 years and the mean age was 29.2 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 25.9%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 4,855 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 3.8 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19}}

==Economy==
The major economic activity is [[agriculture]], and major crops include [[coffee]], [[sugarcane]], and other fruits and vegetables. [[Livestock]] farming is also practiced, which includes cattle, horses, and poultry. Other economic activity includes handicrafts, especially hats made of junco, a type of reed plant.<ref name="Red"/>
