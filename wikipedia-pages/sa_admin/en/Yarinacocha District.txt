'''Yarinacocha District''' is one of the seven [[districts of Peru]] in [[Coronel Portillo Province]], part of the [[Ucayali Region]]. Its capital is the village of [[Puerto Callao]].<ref>{{cite web |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática |url=http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |title=Banco de Información Digital |language=es |access-date=12 March 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090103230330/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |archive-date=3 January 2009}}</ref>
