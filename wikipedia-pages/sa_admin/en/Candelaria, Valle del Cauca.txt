'''Candelaria''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Valle del Cauca Department|Valle del Cauca]], [[Colombia]].

Candelaria in Colombia, is part of the metropolitan area of the city of [[Cali]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://gittv.univalle.edu.co/publicaciones/Area_metropolitana_web.pdf|title=ANÁLISIS DE ACCESIBILIDAD VIAL PARA EL ÁREA METROPOLITANA DE SANTIAGO DE CALI, COLOMBIA|last=Jaramillo|first=Ciro|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140201225251/http://gittv.univalle.edu.co/publicaciones/Area_metropolitana_web.pdf|access-date=2019-03-19|archive-date=2014-02-01}}</ref> along with the municipalities adjacent to it. Candelaria is located 28&nbsp;km from Cali.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.candelaria-valle.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml|title=Official archives of the Cali Mayor's Office for Candelaria|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160121115638/http://www.candelaria-valle.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml|access-date=2019-03-19|archive-date=2016-01-21}}</ref>

It has small villages such as [[El Tiple]].
