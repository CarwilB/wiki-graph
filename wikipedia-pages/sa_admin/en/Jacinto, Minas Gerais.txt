'''Jacinto''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Jacinto is located on the right bank of the [[Jequitinhonha River]].  It is 44 kilometers east of [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]].

Supposedly, the city name is a homage to an old settler named Jacinto who lived in the city around the 1910s.
