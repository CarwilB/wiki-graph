'''San Sebastián de Mariquita''' is a town and [[Municipio|municipality]] in the [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] department of [[Colombia]], about {{convert|150|km|abbr=on}} northwest of [[Bogotá]]. This town and municipality contains several important Spanish settlements that were located here due to its vicinity to the [[Magdalena River]]. Today, Mariquita is frequented by tourists from the capital visiting attractions like the Medina Waterfalls (''Cataratas de Medina'') and the [[mint (coin)|mint]] (''Casa de la Moneda''). The Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] died there and is buried in the [[Primatial Cathedral of Bogotá]]. Today it is home to large hotels and [[haciendas]], among them Villa de los Caballeros.

The population of the municipality was 32,642 as of the 2005 census.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Resultados censo general 2005 - Población censada después de compensada por omisiones de cobertura geográfica y contingencia de transferencia Mariquita (Tolima)| language=es | trans-title=2005 General census results - Census population after compensation for omissions in geographic coverage and transfer contingency Mariquita (Tolima) | url=http://www.dane.gov.co/files/censo2005/regiones/tolima/mariquita.pdf | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061129165110/http://www.dane.gov.co/files/censo2005/regiones/tolima/mariquita.pdf | archive-date=2006-11-29}}</ref>
