'''Junín''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 35,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|263|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Junín, Mendoza|Junín]], which is located around {{cvt|1080|km}} from the [[Capital Federal]].
