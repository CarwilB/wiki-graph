'''Tenochtitlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], and the name of the main township which serves as the seat of the municipal government. It is located in the foothills of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental|Sierra Madre]], with the township lying at an altitude of approximately 900&nbsp;m (2953&nbsp;ft) [[Height above mean sea level|above mean sea level]].

In the year 2000 census, the municipality reported a total population of slightly over 5600 people. The same census also revealed that the municipality had no hotels, no restaurants, no cafeterias, no bars, and no discothèques or night-clubs.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipio de Tenochtitlan, Veracruz, México |url=http://www.tenochtitlan.gob.mx/secciones.php?seccion=turismo&cx=163 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040810082136/http://www.tenochtitlan.gob.mx/secciones.php?seccion=turismo&cx=163 |archive-date=2004-08-10 |access-date=2025-07-16 |website=www.tenochtitlan.gob.mx}}</ref>

The town and the municipality are both named after [[Tenochtitlan]], the capital of the [[Aztec|Aztec Empire]] prior to the [[Spanish colonisation of the Americas|Spanish conquest]].
