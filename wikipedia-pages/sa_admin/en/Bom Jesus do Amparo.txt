'''Bom Jesus do Amparo''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city is part of the mesoregion [[Belo Horizonte Metro|Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and the microregion of [[Itabira]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 6,133.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/bom-jesus-do-amparo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
