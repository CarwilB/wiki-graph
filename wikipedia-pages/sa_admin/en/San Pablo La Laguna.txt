'''San Pablo La Laguna''' ({{IPA|es|sam ˈpaβlo la laˈɣuna}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Sololá Department|Sololá department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/familias-de-san-pablo-la-laguna-reciben-el-bono-social-para-salud-y-educacion/|title=Familias de San Pablo La Laguna reciben el Bono Social para salud y educación|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=1 October 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> It consists of the village that bears the name San Pablo La Laguna which is situated on the shore of [[Lake Atitlan]] between the villages of [[San Juan La Laguna]] and [[San Marcos La Laguna]].{{cn|date=March 2026}}
