'''San Cristóbal Suchixtlahuaca''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 44.65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Coixtlahuaca District]] in the [[Mixteca Region]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 244.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Cristobal Suchixtlahuaca|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20129a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070126053910/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20129a.htm|archivedate=January 26, 2007}}</ref>
