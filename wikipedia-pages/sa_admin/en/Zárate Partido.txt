'''Zárate Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in Argentina.

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 101,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1202|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Zárate, Buenos Aires|Zárate]], which is around {{convert|86|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
