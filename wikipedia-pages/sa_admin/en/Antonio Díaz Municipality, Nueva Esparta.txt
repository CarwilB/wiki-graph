'''Antonio Díaz''' is one of the eleven municipalities on [[Margarita Island]] in the state of [[Nueva Esparta]], [[Venezuela]].<ref name=DPT>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |title=División Político Territorial de la República Bolivariana de Venezuela |access-date=October 25, 2015 |date=September 2013 |edition=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística (Venezuela)|INE]] |archive-date=December 11, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201211040255/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> It is located in the center-east of the island and covers an area of 165.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. {{As of|2011|post=,}} it had a population of 71,466 inhabitants. The municipality's capital is the town of [[San Juan Bautista, Nueva Esparta|San Juan Bautista]], situated in the valley of the same name.

== History ==
[[File:Pozas_de_San_Juan_Bautísta.jpg|link=File:Pozas_de_San_Juan_Baut%C3%ADsta.jpg|left|thumbnail|144x144px|Ponds of [[Fuentidueño]], freshwater springs in the Valley of San Juan Bautista]]

In 1525, ancient settlers from [[Cubagua Island]], searching for fresh water, arrived in the Valley of San Juan. Attracted by several freshwater springs, mild weather, and fertile lands suitable for raising livestock and planting, they decided to settle there.<ref name="Pedro_Herrera">{{Cite web|url=http://www.elsoldemargarita.com.ve/posts/post/id:151268/Pedro-de-Herrera-y-El-Valle-de-San-Juan|title=Pedro de Herrera y El Valle de San Juan|access-date=November 3, 2020|website=www.elsoldemargarita.com.ve|author=Verni Salazar}}</ref> In 1529, San Juan Bautista was founded by Pedro de Alegría, the first Spanish commune on [[Margarita Island]]. Alegría, who succeeded [[Francisco Fajardo]]<ref name="Pedro_Herrera" /><ref>{{Cite journal|url=https://revistaseug.ugr.es/index.php/cnova/article/view/1642|title=EL GOBIERNO DE LA ISLA MARGARITA EN EL SIGLO XVI: HERENCIA Y PRESENCIA FEMENINA|publisher=Luisa Martinez de Salinas|date=2009|edition=Chronica Nova, Universidad de Valladolid}}</ref> and was the first European settler on the island, established himself in San Juan. He founded a cattle estate while serving as Lieutenant and Governor of Margarita and representing the heirs of Marcelo Villalobos.<ref name=":2">{{Cite journal|url=https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/3203828.pdf|title=EL GOBIERNO DE LA ISLA MARGARITA EN EL SIGLO XVI: HERENCIA Y PRESENCIA FEMENINA|publisher=Luisa Martinez de Salinas|date=2009|edition=Chronica Nova, edición 35|access-date=November 7, 2020}}</ref>
