'''[[Cap-Haïtien]]''' ({{langx|ht|Kap Ayisyen}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]] and is the second-most important city of the country. It is known as the historical and tourism capital of the country. Cap-Haïtien was founded in 1670 by the French settler [[Bertrand d'Ogeron|Bertrand d'Ogeron de La Bouëre]]. As of 2015, the population was 356,908 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|archive-date=2015-11-06|url-status=dead|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> The city is governed by three mayors elected by popular vote every 5 years and also represented in the National Assembly of Haiti with one member elected every 4 years. Postal codes in Cap-Haïtien Arrondissement start with the number 11.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Cap-Haïtien]]
* [[Quartier-Morin]]
* [[Limonade]]
