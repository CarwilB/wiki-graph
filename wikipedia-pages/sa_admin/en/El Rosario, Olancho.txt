'''El Rosario''' ({{IPA|es|el roˈsaɾjo}}) is a town<ref>{{GEOnet2|32FA8835AFB63774E0440003BA962ED3|El Rosario (Approved)}}, [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]] </ref> ({{coord|14|54|18|N|86|42|08|W|display=inline}}) and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]]<ref>{{GEOnet2|32FA8835AFCC3774E0440003BA962ED3|El Rosario (Approved)}}, [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]]</ref> in the west of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]].

It is south-east of [[Yocón]], north-east of [[Guayape]] and north-west of [[Salamá, Olancho|Salamá]].

The town had a lumber mill, school, clinic, and a public library, La Biblioteca Regional de Dr. Dean J. Seibert.

In 2018, El Rosario experienced [[drought]] and [[Harvest|failures]] of the corn and bean crops, as the August rains did not come. Residents have attributed the drought to [[climate change]], and to logging which has depleted the pine forests surrounding the town.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://insideclimatenews.org/news/08072019/climate-change-migration-honduras-drought-crop-failure-farming-deforestation-guatemala-trump|title=Ravaged by Drought, a Honduran Village Faces a Choice: Pray for Rain or Migrate|last=Gustin|first=Georgina|date=2019-07-08|website=InsideClimate News|language=en-US|access-date=2019-08-03}}</ref>

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, El Rosario municipality had a population of 4,224. Of these, 99.48% were [[Mestizo]], 0.31% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.17% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.05% [[White Latin Americans|White]].<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
