'''Río Hurtado''' is one of five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] in the [[Limarí Province]] of [[Chile]]'s north-central [[Coquimbo Region|IV Coquimbo Region]].

==Administration==
As a commune, Río Hurtado is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Río Hurtado is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by members elected in the 5th electoral district. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by members of Parliament elected as part of the 4th senatorial constituency (Coquimbo Region).

==Geography==
Río Hurtado spans an area of {{convert|2,117.2|km2|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Río Hurtado commune had 4,771 inhabitants, all of whom are considered to live in [[rural area]]s, making it the least populous commune in the province. The Río Hurtado population represents 0.79% of regional population and 3.1% of the provincial population.<ref name="INE"/>

==Miscellaneous==
Rio Hurtado hosts the El Sauce astronomical observatory, and in particular the Chilean station of the [[Asteroid Terrestrial-impact Last Alert System]] (ATLAS–CHL, observatory code  [[IAU code#W68|W68]]).
