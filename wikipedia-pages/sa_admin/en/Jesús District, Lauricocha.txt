'''Jesús District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Lauricocha Province|Lauricocha]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Waywash mountain range|Waywash]] mountain range and the [[Rawra mountain range|Rawra]] mountain range traverse the district. One of the highest peaks of the district is ''[[Siula Grande|Siula]]''.
