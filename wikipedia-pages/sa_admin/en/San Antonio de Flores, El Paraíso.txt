'''San Antonio de Flores''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[El Paraíso department|El Paraíso]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://hch.tv/2023/03/21/nueve-meses-sin-alcalde-en-san-antonio-de-flores-el-paraiso-pobladores-exigen-fallo-a-csj/|title=Nueve meses sin alcalde en San Antonio de Flores, El Paraíso, pobladores exigen fallo a CSJ|work=HCH.tv|date=21 March 2023|access-date=26 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

It is located between mountains and it is close to the border with Nicaragua.
