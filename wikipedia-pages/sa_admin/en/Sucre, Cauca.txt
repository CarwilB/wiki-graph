'''Sucre''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It contains roughly 10,00 households. As of 2013, the municipality is frequently hit by [[FARC]] attacks.<ref>{{cite web| url = http://www.elpais.com.co/elpais/judicial/noticias/farc-atacan-con-bomba-alcaldia-sucre-cauca| url-status = dead| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20150611030956/http://www.elpais.com.co/elpais/judicial/noticias/farc-atacan-con-bomba-alcaldia-sucre-cauca| archive-date = 2015-06-11| title = Estación de Policía semidestruida es el resultado de ataques de Farc en Sucre, Cauca {{!}} El País - Noticias de Cali, Valle y Colombia}} </ref>
