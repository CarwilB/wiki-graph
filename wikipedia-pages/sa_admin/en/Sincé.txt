'''Sincé''', also known as '''San Luis de Sincé''', is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Sucre Department|Sucre]],  28&nbsp;km (15&nbsp;mi) southeast of [[Sincelejo]], in northern [[Colombia]]. It has an average Temperature of 26,5°C (80°F).  Its main economic activities are agriculture, stockbreeding and commerce. It was founded November 10, 1775 by Antonio de Torre y Miranda.  Sincé is home to "Hospital Local Nuestra Señora del Socorro" and a Municipal Photo Gallery.

== Celebrations and Events ==
The biggest local celebrations are held in honor of the {{ill|Virgen del Socorro|es}}, the city's patron Saint, and celebrated in September.  Other major celebrations include the {{lang|es|Fiestas en Corralejas}} (Running of the Bulls) in January and {{lang|es|La Semana de la Sinceanidad}} (Sincé Week), commemorating its founding in November.
