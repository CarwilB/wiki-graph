'''Astacinga''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 213 km from state capital [[Xalapa]] to the south. It has a surface of 69.09 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|34|N|97|06|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Astacinga is delimited to the north by [[Tlaquilpa]], to the north-east by [[Mixtla de Altamirano]], to the south-east by [[Tehuipango]] and to the west by [[Puebla]].<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30019a.htm |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120630040729/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30019a.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2012-06-30 |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Astacinga |publisher= Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=2009-05-22 }}</ref>

In Astacinga the ecosystems that coexist in the municipality are the one of cold forest with species like the [[pine]].

==Products==
It produces [[maize]] and [[beans]].
