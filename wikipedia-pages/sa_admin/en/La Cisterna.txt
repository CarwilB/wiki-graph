'''La Cisterna''' ({{IPA|es|la sisˈteɾna}}, [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "the cistern") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] and [[census-designated place|census-designated city]] in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], part of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] of [[Chile]]. Founded on 30 May 1925, it is part of the urban area of [[Greater Santiago]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], La Cisterna spans an area of {{convert|10|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 85,118 inhabitants (40,651 men and 44,467 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population fell by 10.1% (9594 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The 2006 projected population was 78,402.<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |accessdate=13 September 2010 |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archivedate=23 August 2010 }}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=September 2010}}

==Administration==
As a commune, La Cisterna is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Joel Olmos Espinoza (Ind).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:
* Gonzalo Aguayo Gajardo ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Maritza Salas Vega (Ind/[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Cesar Antillanca Urrutia ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Alejandro Urrutia Jorquera ([[Radical Party of Chile|PR]])
* Orlando Morales Becerra ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Iván Borcoski González ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Mónica Quezada González ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Carola Espíndola Castro ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], La Cisterna is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Tucapel Jiménez (PPD) and Iván Moreira ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 27th electoral district, (together with [[El Bosque, Chile|El Bosque]] and [[San Ramón, Chile|San Ramón]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Soledad Alvear]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Pablo Longueira]] (UDI) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
