'''Cañitas de Felipe Pescador''' is one of the 58 [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipalities]] in the state of [[Zacatecas]], Mexico. It is located in the northern part of Zacatecas and is bounded by the municipalities of [[Río Grande Municipality, Zacatecas|Río Grande]], [[Villa de Cos]], and [[Fresnillo]]. The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|482|km2|sqmi}}.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32006a.htm |title=Zacatecas |accessdate=2007-07-31 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930030730/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32006a.htm |archivedate=2007-09-30 }} "Cañitas de Felipe Pescador", Encyclopedia de Municipios</ref>

The [[municipal seat]] is also called '''Cañitas de Felipe Pescador'''.

==History==
The first inhabitants of the Cañitas de Felipe Pescador region were the [[Huichol people|Huichol]]. In 1918, the region became populated with farmers. In 1921, governor [[Donato Moreno]] granted a motion to legally fund the Cañitas region with the [[Eminent domain|expropriation]] of [[haciendas]]{{Clarify|date=January 2011}} as an ''[[ejido]]'', a system of [[communal land]]. This led to the rise of the region to become an Independent Congregation of the Municipality of [[Fresnillo]].

In 1921, the first school was founded with the name of ''Carlos A. Carrillo'', which placed special attention to environmental education. That same year the train station was installed in the city of Cañitas, and the community of La Colorada was moved to the region.

It was not until November 19, 1958, that Cañitas went from being a Congregation to a [[Municipio (Mexico)|Municipality]]. Gilberto Montes Monsiváis was named [[Municipal president]], and that same year elections are held in which Herón Domínguez was declared the winner, and he became the first elected municipal president of Cañitas de Felipe Pescador (1959–1961).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.zacatecas.gob.mx/Municipios/Ca%C3%B1itasHist.htm |title=Municipio de Cañitas de Felipe Pescador |access-date=2019-11-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090313090804/http://www.zacatecas.gob.mx/Municipios/Ca%C3%B1itasHist.htm |archive-date=2009-03-13 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Population==
In the 2005 census, Cañitas de Felipe Pescador reported a population of 7,893.<ref name=pop2005>[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110613183420/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |date=2011-06-13 }} INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática</ref> Of these, 5,950 lived in the municipal seat, and the remainder lived in surrounding rural communities.

===Religion===
Most of the population (93.83%) is [[Roman Catholic Church|Catholic]]; [[Protestant]]s and [[Evangelical]]s follow with 2.87%, and the remainder are [[Seventh-day Adventist Church|Seventh-day Adventist]]s and [[Neo-Pentecostal]]s with 6.17%.
