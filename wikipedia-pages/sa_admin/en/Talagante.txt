'''Talagante''' ({{IPA|es|talaˈɣante}}) is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and the [[capital (political)|capital]] [[List of cities in Chile|city]] of the [[Talagante Province|province of the same name]] in the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] of central [[Chile]]. The word ''Talagante'' in [[Quechua languages|Quechua]] comes from ''talacanta'', meaning "Lazo de Hechicero", which was the proper name of the ''[[curaca]]'', or ruler, who dominated this central valley on behalf of the [[Inca Empire]] during the arrival of the Spaniards.

==Geography==
Talagante can be found in the [[Chilean Central Valley]] at an elevation of {{convert|313|m|0|abbr=on}}, {{convert|35|km}} to the southwest of the national capital of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|125.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Talagante spans an area of {{convert|125.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 59,805 inhabitants (29,468 men and 30,337 women). Of these, 49,957 (83.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,848 (16.5%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 132800% (59,760 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Talagante is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Raúl Leiva Carvajal (IND),<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> and his council members are:<ref name="Municipality">{{Cite web |url= http://www.munitalagante.cl/ |title= Municipality of Talagante |accessdate=23 July 2010}}</ref>
* Luis Silva Pavez ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Sebastián Rosas Guerrero ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Patricio Huerta Moraga ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* María Eugenia Torres Miranda ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Félix Donoso Gómez ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Juan Cartagena Diaz ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Talagante is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).{{cn|date=July 2023}}
