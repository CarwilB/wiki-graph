'''San Lorenzo''' ({{IPA|es|san loˈɾenso}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is situated at 220 m above [[sea level]]. It contains 7,753 people. It covers a terrain of 60&nbsp;km². The annual festival is on February 2.
