'''San Miguel  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Balboa District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,044 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Balboa District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,179; its population as of 2000 was 817.<ref name=inec/>

The town is on the northern shore of [[Isla del Rey, Panama|Isla del Rey]], the largest island in the [[Pearl Islands]] [[archipelago]]. It is served by [[San Miguel Airport (Panama)|San Miguel Airport]].
