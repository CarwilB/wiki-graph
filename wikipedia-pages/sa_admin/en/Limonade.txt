'''Limonade''' ({{IPA|fr|limɔnad|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Poslovitch-limonade.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Limonad}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Cap-Haïtien Arrondissement]], in the  [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 69,256 inhabitants. [[Christopher Columbus]] and his crew celebrated the first Christmas in the Americas at Limonade in 1492.<ref name=test>[http://www.limonadehaiti.com]
 {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090826173547/http://www.limonadehaiti.com/ |date=2009-08-26 }}, ''www.limonadehaiti.com''</ref> Limonade is also the city in which [[François Capois]], a renowned hero of the [[Haitian Revolution]], died.

In 2012, a new university built by the [[Dominican Republic]] was finished near the town, [[Université Roi Henri Christophe]], part of the [[University of Haiti]].<ref name="carib">{{Cite web|url=http://www.caribjournal.com/2012/09/22/haiti-officially-opens-roi-henri-christophe-campus-in-limonade/|title=Haiti Officially Opens Roi Henri Christophe Campus in Limonade|date=22 September 2012}}</ref>
