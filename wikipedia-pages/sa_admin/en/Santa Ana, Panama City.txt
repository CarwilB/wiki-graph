== History ==
During the 18th century, the numerous fires that destroyed part of Panama City created a demographic boom in the Santa Ana community.  Its historic church was moved in 1842. During the [[1925 Tenant Movement]], the US Army was deployed in Santa Ana to contain the demonstrations.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tejeira Davis |first=Eduardo |title=El Casco Antiguo de Panamá |url=http://bdigital.binal.ac.pa/bdp/artpma/casco%20antiguo1.pdf |access-date=2025-11-03 |website=bdigital.binal.ac.pa |language=es}}</ref>

The Malambo orphanage was founded in 1890 in Santa Ana, and moved to a new location in 1995.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2007-12-07 |title=A home for Panama's orphans |url=https://www.theguardian.com/world/2007/dec/07/panama-international-aid-and-development |access-date=2025-11-03 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>
