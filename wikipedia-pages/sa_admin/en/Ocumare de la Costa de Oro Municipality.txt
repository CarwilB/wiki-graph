The '''Ocumare de la Costa de Oro Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 12,816.<ref name="geohive">{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx|title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics|publisher=geohive.com|accessdate=2016-06-15}}</ref> The town of [[Ocumare de la Costa]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Ocumare de la Costa de Oro Municipality.<ref name="ine">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip{{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
Ocumare de la Costa, known for its attractive tourist beaches, hides a great history in its mountains. Today, traveling from other parts of the country to Ocumare is easy compared with previous years; one had to travel by horse and carriage through the mountains (passing through the towns of San Joaquin, El Loro, Hacienda Santa Maria, and Cumboto on the way) in order to get to Ocumare de la Costa. In 1921, the construction of today's modern highway began, and it was completed in 1927.

==Demographics==
The Ocumare de la Costa de Oro Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 9,605 (up from 8,239 in 2000). This amounts to 0.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|28.25|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=February 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Ocumare de la Costa de Oro Municipality is Roberto José Madero, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 40% of the vote.<ref name="cne">{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/4/alcaldedis=0&edo=4&mun=18&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|title=Alcalde de Municipio|publisher=cne.gob.ve|accessdate=2016-06-15}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-03-15 |archive-date=2006-10-02 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]; Capital Ocumare de La Costa de Oro.<ref name="ine"/>
