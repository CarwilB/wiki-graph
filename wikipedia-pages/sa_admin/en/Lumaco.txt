'''Lumaco''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Malleco Province]] in the [[Araucanía Region]] of [[Chile]].  Its name in [[Mapudungun]] means "water of ''[[Luma (plant)|luma]]''". Lumaco is located to {{convert|120|km|0|abbr=on}} northeast of [[Temuco]] and {{convert|52|km|0|abbr=on}} from [[Angol]]. It shares a boundary to the north with the communes of [[Purén]] and [[Los Sauces]], to the east with [[Traiguén]] and [[Galvarino, Chile|Galvarino]], to the south with [[Cholchol]] and [[Carahue]] in [[Cautin Province]], and the west with [[Tirúa]] and [[Contulmo]] in the [[Arauco Province]] of the [[Biobío Region]].

The area of Lumaco and [[Purén]] is rich in [[Mapuche]] and [[Conquest of Chile|early Spanish]] archaeological sites.<ref name=DilleSite>{{cite book |last=Dillehay |first=Tom D. |last2=Paige Silcox |first2=J. |last3= Ocampo E.|first3=Carlos |date=2014|editor-last=Dillehay|editor-first=Tom |title=The Teleoscopic Polity|chapter=Site Distribution and Settlement Patterns |publisher=Springer |pages=255–274 |isbn=978-3-319-03128-6 |author-link=Tom Dillehay }}</ref>

==Geography==
The [[epicenter]] of the [[1960 Valdivia earthquake]], the strongest earthquake ever recorded, was near Lumaco.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Lumaco spans an area of {{convert|1119|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 11,405 inhabitants (6,074 men and 5,331 women). Of these, 4,132 (36.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,273 (63.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 7% (853 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Lumaco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Manuel Painiqueo Tragnolao ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]).<ref name="Munitel"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Lumaco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gonzalo Arenas ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mario Venegas ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 48th electoral district, together with [[Angol]], [[Renaico]], [[Collipulli]], [[Ercilla, Chile|Ercilla]], [[Los Sauces]], [[Purén]] and [[Traiguén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alberto Espina Otero ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Jaime Quintana Leal ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 14th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-North).
