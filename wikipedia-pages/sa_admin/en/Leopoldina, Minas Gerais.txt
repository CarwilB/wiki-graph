'''Leopoldina''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] municipality in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. It belongs to the [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] mesoregion and it located 322 kilometers southeast of [[Belo Horizonte]], the state capital. In 2022, its estimated population was 51 145 inhabitants, according to IBGE. Its territory covers an area of 943&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Leopoldina |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/leopoldina/panorama |access-date=2023-07-31 |website=IBGE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Dados Gerais |url=http://www.leopoldina.mg.gov.br/portal1/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100131440 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118180014/http://www.leopoldina.mg.gov.br/portal1/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100131440 |url-status=dead |archive-date=2012-01-18 |access-date=2010-09-15 |website=City Hall of Leopoldina}}</ref>

The municipal headquarters has an average annual temperature of 21°C and the vegetation is predominantly Atlantic forest. Regarding the automobile fleet, 16,575 vehicles were registered in 2012. The municipality's [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) is 0.726, classified as high.<ref name=":0" /> 

The political emancipation of the municipality took place in 1854, and its name is a tribute to Princess [[Princess Leopoldina of Brazil|Leopoldina de Bragança e Bourbon]], daughter of Emperor [[Pedro II of Brazil|D. Pedro II]].<ref name=":4">{{Cite web |title=Municípios Mineiros |url=http://www.almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado&diretorio=munmg&arquivo=municipios&municipio=38401 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928074207/http://www.almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado&diretorio=munmg&arquivo=municipios&municipio=38401 |archive-date=2007-09-28 |access-date=2010-09-15 |website=Legislative Assembly of Minas Gerais}}</ref> Currently it is formed by the headquarters and the districts of [[Abaíba, Leopoldina|Abaíba]], [[Piacatuba, Leopoldina|Piacatuba]], [[Providência, Leopoldina|Providência]], [[Ribeiro Junqueira, Leopoldina|Ribeiro Junqueira]] and [[Tebas, Leopoldina|Tebas]].<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |title=Denominações Urbanas |url=http://www.almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado&diretorio=durbana&arquivo=denominacoes_urbanas&municipio=877 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081228023727/http://www.almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado |archive-date=2008-12-28 |access-date=2010-09-15 |website=Legislative Assembly of Minas Gerais}}</ref> The city, during the [[Brazilian coffee cycle|coffee cycle]], was one of the most important in the former [[province]] of Minas Gerais. With the great economic [[Wall Street Crash of 1929|crisis of 1929]], the economy of the municipalities of Minas Gerais linked to coffee growing suffered a great impact.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |first=N |title=A Escravidão em Leopoldina |date=16 March 1995 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/1995/03/16/a-escravidao-em-leopoldina/ |access-date=2011-03-17}}</ref> Nowadays, its economy is based on [[dairy farming]], rice cultivation and the service sector.<ref name=":0" />

Leopoldina has cultural, natural and architectural attractions, such as the [[St. Sebastian Cathedral, Leopoldina|Cathedral of St. Sebastian]], the Espaço dos Anjos Museum, the Electricity Museum, the [[Maurício Hydroelectric Power Plant]] reservoir, the Cruzeiro Hill, among others. One of the main events that happen in the city are the Agricultural and Industrial Exhibition, the ''Feira da Paz'' and the traditional Viola and Gastronomy Festival of Piacatuba.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Pontos turísticos |url=http://www.leopoldina.mg.gov.br/portal1/municipio/ponto_turistico.asp?iIdMun=100131440 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118234139/http://www.leopoldina.mg.gov.br/portal1/municipio/ponto_turistico.asp?iIdMun=100131440 |archive-date=2012-01-18 |access-date=2011-03-17 |website=City Hall of Leopoldina}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The [[Toponymy|toponym]] is a tribute to the second daughter of Emperor D. Pedro II, Princess Leopoldina de Bragança e Bourbon. The name was chosen when the town became politically emancipated from the municipality of [[Mar de Espanha]] in 1854. Before that, the area was a district called São Sebastião do Feijão Cru, in reference to both the patron saint of the place and the stream that crosses the territory.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-09-11 |title=Origem imperial: conheça a história por trás do nome da cidade de Leopoldina |url=https://g1.globo.com/mg/zona-da-mata/noticia/2022/09/11/origem-imperial-conheca-a-historia-por-tras-do-nome-da-cidade-de-leopoldina.ghtml |access-date=2023-08-01 |website=G1}}</ref> 

== History ==

=== Origins ===
At the end of the 18th century, the [[Brazilian Gold Rush|gold production]] of the [[Captaincy of Minas Gerais]] went into decline. Some families left the villages in search of fertile land in other parts of the captaincy. They reached the forbidden areas of colonization, then known as ''Sertões do Leste'', an extensive strip of Atlantic forest that extended from the [[Paraibuna River (Minas Gerais)|Paraibuna River]] and [[Estrada Real|the Royal Road]] to the [[Doce River]]; later, this region became known as the Zona da Mata.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Castro |first=Celso Falabella de Figueiredo |title=Os sertões de leste: achegas para a história da Zona da Mata |publisher=Imprensa Oficial |year=1987}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book |last=Mercadante |first=Paulo |title=Os Sertões do Leste. Estudo de uma Região: a Mata Mineira |publisher=Zahar Editores |year=1973}}</ref>

In 1813, the first ''[[Sesmaria|sesmarias]]'' in the territory of the current municipality, which belonged to the term of [[Barbacena]], ''[[comarca]]'' of Rio das Mortes, were donated. However, the oldest references of residents date back to 1824.<ref name=":3">{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=Alguns Fatos da História de Leopoldina |date=30 November 1999 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/1999/11/30/alguns-fatos-da-historia-de-leopoldina-2/ |access-date=2010-09-23}}</ref> The first explorers established themselves with their families on the banks of the [[Feijão Cru Stream]], where a troopers' settlement began to develop near a small chapel made of [[wattle and daub]], erected in 1831 by farmers Francisco Pinheiro de Lacerda and Joaquim Ferreira Brito and dedicated to [[Saint Sebastian]].<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=São Sebastião do Feijão Cru - Vila: 1854 |url=http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/index.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090715021841/http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/index.html |archive-date=2009-07-15 |access-date=2010-09-15}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=75 – O Feijão Cru: antes de ser Villa Leopoldina |date=10 May 2017 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/2017/05/10/75-o-feijao-cru-antes-de-ser-villa-leopoldina/ }}</ref>

Until the arrival of the first landowners, the region was inhabited by the [[Purí people|Puri]] indigenous people. These groups were entrusted with certain services, such as clearing the forest and harvesting the [[Carapichea ipecacuanha|ipecacuanha]], for which they were paid with ''[[cachaça]]''. A considerable part of the Puri population was killed by the diseases brought by the invaders, such as [[measles]], and the other part was expelled and had to leave for the province of [[Espírito Santo]]; by 1865, there were practically no indigenous people left in the area.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=Origem dos Povoadores de Leopoldina |date=12 September 1998 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/1998/09/12/origem-dos-povoadores-de-leopoldina/ |access-date=2010-09-24}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=Indígenas em Leopoldina |date= 19 April 1997|url=https://cantoni.pro.br/1997/04/19/indigenas-em-leopoldina/ |access-date=2010-09-24}}</ref>

In 1831, the [[district]] of São Sebastião do Feijão Cru was created, as part of the municipality of São Manuel do Pomba, now [[Rio Pomba]]. In 1851, the district was transferred to the territory of Mar de Espanha, from which it was emancipated by provincial law No. 666 of April 27, 1854, creating the municipality of Vila Leopoldina.<ref name=":1" /> The same law transferred some districts dismembered from the territory of Presídio, now [[Visconde do Rio Branco, Minas Gerais|Visconde do Rio Branco]], to Leopoldina. At that time, the municipality had a population of 23,000 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lei No. 666 de 27 de abril de 1854 |url=http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/vila.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090715153343/http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/vila.html |archive-date=2009-07-15 |access-date=2010-11-04}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=Recontos de um Recanto – 4: Argirita |date=9 July 2002 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/2002/07/09/recontos-de-um-recanto-4-argirita/ |access-date=2010-11-29}}</ref> 

The formation of the village began around the Rosário Square, from where the streets of Rosário (now Tiradentes Street), Direita (now Gabriel Magalhães Street) and Riachuelo (now Joaquim Ferreira Brito Street) left, constituting the oldest roads in the city.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Cantoni |first1=Nilza |last2=Rodrigues |first2=José Luiz Machado |year=2004 |title=Nossas Ruas, Nossa Gente: Logradouros Públicos de Leopoldina |url=https://pt.scribd.com/doc/89281432/Nossas-Ruas-Nossa-Gente-Logradouros-Publicos-de-Leopoldina |journal=SENAI}}</ref>

=== The coffee cycle ===
[[Coffee growing]] developed in the [[province of Rio de Janeiro]] crossed the [[Paraíba do Sul|Paraíba do Sul river]] and spread into the Zona da Mata through the valleys of the [[Paraibuna River (Minas Gerais)|Paraibuna]], [[Pirapetinga River (upper Paraíba do Sul)|Pirapetinga]] and [[Pomba River|Pomba]] rivers, leading to a fast growth of the area in the second half of the 19th century. Vila Leopoldina was elevated to the category of city by provincial law No. 1116 of October 16, 1861.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lei No. 1116 de 16 de outubro de 1861 |url=http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/cidade.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090715152859/http://www.arquivohistorico-mg.com.br/leopoldina/cidade.html |archive-date=2009-07-15 |access-date=2010-11-04}}</ref> At that time, Latin and French were already taught in the locality. In 1872, by provincial law no. 1867, Leopoldina became the seat of a district.<ref name=":3" />

The municipality also benefited from the construction of the [[Leopoldina Railway|Leopoldina Railroad]], whose tracks reached the city in 1877. Through the railroad, trade with [[Rio de Janeiro]], capital of the [[Empire of Brazil|Empire]], was carried out. In 1879, the first newspaper of the municipality was founded, called ''Leopoldinense''. In April 1881, Leopoldina was visited by Emperor Pedro II.<ref name=":3" />

In 1883, Leopoldina had the second largest slave population in the province of Minas Gerais, behind only [[Juiz de Fora]]. Between the last decade of the 19th century and the first decade of the 20th century, European immigrants arrived in Leopoldina to work in the coffee plantations. An immigrant hostel operated in the town until 1898. In 1910, the Constança Colony was created in the district of Tebas for immigrants, mainly Italians.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cantoni |title=Por que comemorar o Centenário da Colônia Agrícola da Constança? |date=28 May 2009 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/2009/05/28/por-que-comemorar-o-centenario-da-colonia-agricola-da-constanca/ |access-date=2023-08-01}}</ref>

On September 2, 1906, in the district of Piacatuba, the cornerstone of [[Mauricio Hydroelectric Power Plant]] was laid, the first [[Hydroelectricity|hydroelectric power plant]] built in the region, to exploit the hydraulic potential of the ''Cachoeira da Fumaça'', on the [[Novo River (Minas Gerais)|Novo]] River. Two years later, on July 16, 1908, the [[electric power]] arrived in the city.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mais de um Século de História |url=http://www.grupoenergisa.com.br/MinasGerais/Energisa%20Minas%20Gerais/Historia/MaisdeUmSeculodeHistoria.aspx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731201013/http://www.grupoenergisa.com.br/MinasGerais/Energisa%20Minas%20Gerais/Historia/MaisdeUmSeculodeHistoria.aspx |archive-date=2013-07-31 |access-date=2010-10-04 |website=Energisa Minas Gerais}}</ref> Also in 1906, the [[Professor Botelho Reis State School|Leopoldinense Gymnasium]] was founded, which from 1912 onwards offered technical education at the Agricultural School and [[higher education]] at the School of Pharmacy and Dentistry.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Nogueira |first=Natania Aparecida da Silva |title=Leopoldina: instrução, mito político e formação de elites na Zona da Mata Mineira (1895-1930) |publisher=Ed. do Autor |year=2011 |isbn=9788591205707}}</ref> Between 1911 and 1912, the [[Ribeiro Junqueira Bank|Banco Ribeiro Junqueira]] and the Zonna da Matta construction company were founded in the town.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Banco Ribeiro Junqueira |url=http://www.pontodeinformacao.org.br/index_cidade.php?id_cid=9&acao=conteudo&menu=ph&id_cont=187 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140407055058/http://www.pontodeinformacao.org.br/index_cidade.php?id_cid=9&acao=conteudo&menu=ph&id_cont=187 |archive-date=2014-04-07 |access-date=2014-03-31}}</ref> The coffee crisis at the beginning of the 20th century, however, caused serious changes in the economy of the Zona da Mata and the municipality, which began to rely on dairy farming and rice cultivation.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Capri |first=Roberto |title=Banco Ribeiro Junqueira e Praça General Osório |date=21 June 2012 |url=https://cantoni.pro.br/2012/06/21/banco-ribeiro-junqueira-e-praca-general-osorio/ |access-date=2014-03-31}}</ref>

In the period between the [[First Brazilian Republic|First Republic]] and the [[1964 Brazilian coup d'état|military coup of 1964]], some political leaders of Leopoldina achieved projection in Minas Gerais and Brazil, such as Senator [[José Monteiro Ribeiro Junqueira|Ribeiro Junqueira]], Governor [[Clóvis Salgado da Gama|Clóvis Salgado]] and President [[Carlos Luz]]. The [[BR-116 (Brazil highway)|Rio-Bahia Highway]], inaugurated in 1963, benefited the industrialization of the municipality and strengthened it as a communications junction.<ref name=":0" />

=== Administrative division ===
Emancipated from Mar de Espanha in 1854, the municipality was installed on January 20, 1855 covering nine districts: Leopoldina (headquarters), Capivara, Conceição da Boa Vista, Laranjal, Madre de Deus do Angu, Nossa Senhora da Piedade, Bom Jesus do Rio Pardo, Santa Rita do Meia Pataca and São José do Paraíba. Since then, new districts have been created and others emancipated to constitute the municipalities of [[Cataguases]], [[Além Paraíba]] and [[Recreio]].<ref name=":1" /> The last dismemberment occurred by state law No. 2764 of 1962, which emancipated the district of Rio Pardo to create the municipality of [[Argirita]]. Currently, the municipality is formed by six districts: Leopoldina, Abaíba, Piacatuba, Providência, Ribeiro Junqueira and Tebas.<ref name=":2" /><ref name=":0" />

== Geography ==
Leopoldina is located in the Zona da Mata mesoregion and occupies an area of 942&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the most extensive of its micro-region, representing 0.161% of the state of Minas Gerais, 0.102% of the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]] and 0.011% of the entire Brazilian territory. It borders [[Cataguases]] to the north, [[Laranjal, Minas Gerais|Laranjal]] to the northeast, Recreio to the east, [[Pirapetinga]] and [[Estrela Dalva]] to the southeast, [[Volta Grande]] and Além Paraíba to the south, [[Santo Antônio do Aventureiro]] to the southwest, Argirita and [[São João Nepomuceno]] to the west, and [[Descoberto]] and [[Itamarati de Minas]] to the northwest.<ref name=":0" />

=== Landform and hydrography ===
The territory of Leopoldina is located in the [[Paraíba do Sul River Basin]], and the municipal headquarters is crossed by the Feijão Cru Stream. The main rivers that flow through the municipality are the Pomba and Pirapetinga, both tributaries of the Paraíba do Sul, and the Pardo and Novo rivers, tributaries of the Pomba.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Carta do Brasil SF-23-X-D-V-2 |url=https://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/SF-23-X-D-V-2.jpg |access-date=2010-11-16 |website=IBGE}}</ref>

About 80% of the municipality has topography ranging from undulating to hilly and only 20% classified as plain. The [[altitude]] of the municipal headquarters is 225 meters; Alto de Santa Úrsula, the highest point of the municipality, has an altitude of 712 meters.

=== Climate ===
{{Climate chart|Leopoldina|19|29|220|20|29|176|19|29|147|17|27|68|15|27|35|13|24|24|12|24|14|13|25|17|14|25|44|17|27|121|18|28|162|19|28|277|float=right|clear=none|source=[http://www.tempoagora.com.br/previsaodotempo.html/brasil/climatologia/Leopoldina-MG/ Tempo Agora]}}
The climate of Leopoldina is [[Tropical climate|tropical]] (type Aw according to [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]), with an average annual temperature of around 21 °C, dry and mild winters and rainy summers with moderately high temperatures. The warmest months are January, February and March, with an average maximum temperature of 29 °C; in February 2003, a peak temperature of 35 °C was recorded. July is the coldest month, with an average minimum temperature of 12 °C; in July 2003, a low of 9 °C was recorded.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Dados históricos simulados de clima e tempo para Leopoldina |url=https://www.meteoblue.com/pt/tempo/historyclimate/climatemodelled/leopoldina_brasil_3458632 |access-date=2023-08-01 |website=Meteo BLue}}</ref> 

The average annual rainfall is 1,307 mm. The highest rainfall is recorded in the period from October to March, with the winter months characterized by drought. On average, July is the driest month, when only 14.2 mm are recorded, and December is the wettest month, with an average of 277.1 mm.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tempo agora em Leopoldina |url=https://tempoagora.uol.com.br/ |access-date=2011-10-13 |website=UOL}}</ref>

=== Vegetation and soil use ===
The native vegetation of the municipality is the [[seasonal semideciduous forest]], a part of the [[Atlantic Forest|Atlantic forest]] [[biome]], which has been severely deforested. Currently, the seasonal forest occupies an area of 7144 hectares in the territory. Today, most of Leopoldina's area is covered by fields and pastures, which occupy 79,308 hectares. It has two environmental [[Protected areas of Brazil|conservation units]]: the Antônio Andrade Ribeiro Municipal Park, with an area of 31.93 hectares, and the [[Lapinha Biological Reserve]], with 368 hectares.<ref>{{Cite journal |year=2006 |title=Plano de Recursos Hídricos da Bacia do Rio Paraíba do Sul - Diagnóstico dos Recursos Hídricos |url=https://www.ceivap.org.br/downloads/PSR-010-R0.pdf |journal=Coppetec}}</ref>

=== Demography ===
{{Bar chart|float=right|title=Total population of the municipality|label_type=Year|data_type=Population|label1=2010|label2=2000|label3=1991|label4=1980|label5=1970|data1=51.136|data2=50.041|data3=46.442|data4=42.118|data5=41.306}}
In 2010, the population of Leopoldina was estimated by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) at 51,136 inhabitants, with a population density of 54.27 inhabitants per km<sup>2</sup>. According to the 2010 census, 47.99% of the population were men (24 545 inhabitants), 52.01% women (26 591 inhabitants), 89.39% (45 712 inhabitants) lived in the urban area and 10.61% (5 424 inhabitants) in the rural area. Also according to the IBGE, Leopoldina had 37 685 voters in 2006.<ref name=":0" />

The Municipal [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Leopoldina is considered average by the [[United Nations Development Programme|United Nations Development Program]] (UNDP). Its value is 0.778, being the 122nd highest in the entire state of Minas Gerais (out of 853), the 487th of the entire Southeast Region of Brazil (out of 1666 municipalities) and the 1098th of all Brazil (out of 5 507 municipalities). Considering only education (MHDI-E), the index value is 0.854, while Brazil's is 0.849. The longevity index (MHDI-L) is 0.789 (the Brazilian is 0.638) and the income index is 0.691 (Brazil is 0.723). The per capita income is 8,994.55 reais, the adult [[Literacy|literacy rate]] is 89.56% and life expectancy is 72.35 years. According to IBGE, the [[Gini coefficient]], which measures social inequality, is 0.45, with 1.00 being the worst number and 0.00 the best. The incidence of poverty, measured by IBGE, is 30.46%, the lower limit of poverty incidence is 22.02%, the upper limit is 38.90% and the incidence of subjective poverty is 26.38%.<ref name=":0" />

== Politics ==
Local administration is carried out by the executive and legislative branches. The first representative of the executive power and mayor of the municipality was Francisco Andrade Bastos, whose mandate lasted from 1937 to 1945. In twenty mandates, thirteen mayors have passed through the City Hall of Leopoldina.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Silva |first1=Lucilene Nunes |last2=Nogueira |first2=Natania Aparecida |year=2008 |title=Leopoldina: Povoamento, Café e Atualidade |url=https://pt.scribd.com/document/49424489/CARTILHA-copia-pdf |journal=City Hall of Leopoldina}}</ref> The current mayor of the city is Pedro Augusto Junqueira Ferraz (PL), elected with 31.30% of the valid votes (8,926 votes). The legislative branch is made up of the [[Chamber of Deputies (Brazil)|Chamber of Deputies]], composed of 15 councillors elected for four-year terms.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011-02-08 |title=Instituições Governamentais |url=http://www.almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado&diretorio=instgov&arquivo=instituicoes_governamentais |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110208152736/http://almg.gov.br/index.asp?grupo=estado&diretorio=instgov&arquivo=instituicoes_governamentais |archive-date=2011-02-08 |access-date=2011-02-25}}</ref> Leopoldina is also the seat of a ''comarca'', whose territory also includes the municipalities of Recreio and Argirita.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lista de Comarcas do Estado de Minas Gerais |url=http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100825194853/http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |archive-date=2010-08-25 |access-date=2011-02-25}}</ref><ref>{{Cite journal |title=Municípios e Distritos que Integram as Comarcas de Minas Gerais |url=http://www8.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/guia/docs/localidades_MG.pdf |journal=TJMG}}</ref>

== Economy and infrastructure ==
{{Bar chart|title=Gross Domestic Product|float=right|label_type=Year|data_type=Thousand (BRL)|label1=2011|label2=2010|label3=2009|label4=2008|label5=2007|label6=2006|label7=2005|label8=2004|label9=2003|label10=2002|data1=602.572|data2=567.948|data3=514.094|data4=462.060|data5=415.882|data6=388.043|data7=359.829|data8=316.043|data9=267.735|data10=237.022}}
According to 2011 data from IBGE, Leopoldina had a [[Gross domestic product|Gross Domestic Product]] of R$ 602,572 thousand. Of this total, R$ 52 206 thousand are from taxes on products net of subsidies.<ref name=":0" />

The main economic activities in Leopoldina are [[Tertiary sector of the economy|services]] and [[Secondary sector of the economy|industry]], which account for 65% and 17% of the municipality's GDP, respectively. In the industry, the manufacture of textiles, clothing, food and beverages, metal products, rubber and plastic products and non-metallic minerals stand out. Currently, nature tourism is growing in the municipality.<ref name=":8" />

Agriculture has a share of 8.5% in the GDP, with [[dairy farming]] and the raising of chickens and pigs standing out. Leopoldina also produces corn, rice, bananas, sugar cane, among others.<ref name=":4" /> 

The main press organizations in the city are the newspapers, ''Leopoldinense Online'', ''O Vigilante Online'', ''Hora H Magazine'', ''Rádio-Jornal AM'', ''Rádio 104 FM'', ''Rádio Luz FM'' (community).<ref name=":9">{{Cite journal |title=PLANO MUNICIPAL DE EDUCAÇÃO - LEOPOLDINA/MG – 2015 a 2024 |url=https://www.leopoldina.mg.gov.br/abrir_arquivo.aspx/plano_municipal_de_educacao_2015_68_maio_2015?cdlocal=6&arquivo=%7Bad6a88db-e2b5-dc7a-3206-cac4deeecaea%7D.pdf |journal=City Hall of Leopoldina}}</ref>

Leopoldina has 46 health facilities, of which 19 are public. In all, the municipality has 243 hospital beds.<ref name=":0" />

=== Transportation ===
The municipality of Leopoldina is served by federal highways BR-116 (Rio-Bahia), [[BR-120 (Brazil highway)|BR-120]] and [[BR-267 (Brazil highway)|BR-267]] and also by state highway MG-454. The Leopoldina Railroad, now granted to [[Ferrovia Centro-Atlântica]] (FCA), passes through the districts of Providência, Abaíba and Ribeiro Junqueira. [[Leopoldina Airport]] is located in the district of Piacatuba.<ref>{{Cite journal |year=2009 |title=Mapas Multimodais do Brasil por Estado |url=https://bibliotecadigital.economia.gov.br/handle/123456789/294 |journal=Gov.br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Alvim |first=Plínio Fajardo |title=A Estrada de Ferro Leopoldina |url=https://leopoldinense.com.br/video/105/estrada-de-ferro-leopoldina-em-leopoldina-mg |access-date=2022-01-08}}</ref>

In 2012, the municipality's fleet totaled 16,575 vehicles, of which 9,901 were cars, 663 trucks, 262 tractor-trailers, 1,060 pickup trucks, 3,493 motorcycles, 85 buses, 67 minibuses and 1,044 of other types.<ref name=":0" />

=== Education ===
Leopoldina has thirty primary education institutions, of which twenty-four are public; nine secondary education institutions (six public) and four higher education institutions - [[Minas Gerais State University|UEMG]] (public), [[Federal Centers for Technological Education|CEFET]] (public), [[Presidente Antônio Carlos University Center|UNIPAC]] (private) and DOCTUM (private).<ref name=":8">{{Cite web |title=Economia e infraestrutura |url=https://leopoldinense.com.br/conteudo/281/economia-e-infraestrutura |access-date=2023-08-01 |website=Leopoldinense}}</ref><ref name=":9" />

== Culture ==
=== Museums ===
The city center is occupied by the [[Espaço dos Anjos Museum]], a 19th-century building where the poet [[Augusto dos Anjos]], patron of the ''Academia Leopoldinense de Letras e Artes'', lived.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Leopoldina – Espaço Augusto dos Anjos |url=https://www.ipatrimonio.org/leopoldina-espaco-augusto-dos-anjos/#!/map=38329&loc=-21.53068,-42.643044999999994,17 |access-date=2023-08-01 |website=iPatrimonio}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-07-27 |title=Inscrições abertas para o 32º Concurso Nacional de Poesias Augusto dos Anjos em Leopoldina |url=https://www.ovigilanteonline.com.br/site/2023/07/25/inscricoes-abertas-para-o-32o-concurso-nacional-de-poesias-augusto-dos-anjos-em-leopoldina/ |access-date=2023-08-01 |website=O Vigilante Online}}</ref> The district of Piacatuba is the location of the Electricity Museum, next to the Mauricio Power Plant. The city also has libraries, a music conservatory and cultural associations.<ref>{{Cite web |title=PIACATUBA - LUGAR DE GENTE DE BOM CORAÇÃO |url=https://momentosjf.wixsite.com/quintal-da-malu/sobre-piacatuba |access-date=2023-08-01}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
According to the territorial division of the [[Catholic Church]], Leopoldina has been the seat of a [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Leopoldina|diocese]] since 1942, whose episcopal see is the [[St. Sebastian Cathedral, Leopoldina|Cathedral of St. Sebastian]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Diocese de Leopoldina |url=http://www.dioceseleopoldina.com/conteudo/detalhe/1 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130903112321/http://dioceseleopoldina.com/conteudo/detalhe/1 |archive-date=2013-09-03 |access-date=2014-06-11 |website=Diocese of Leopoldina}}</ref> There are seven parishes in the municipality, four of which are located in the headquarters (Saint Sebastian, [[Our Lady of the Rosary]], [[Saint Joseph]] and [[Benedict of Nursia|Saint Benedict]]), one in Piacatuba (Our Lady of Mercy), one in Tebas ([[Anthony of Padua|Saint Anthony]]) and one in Providência (Saint Anthony). The municipality also has temples of other religions, such as evangelical churches. According to information from the 2010 census, conducted by IBGE, most of the population of Leopoldina declares itself Catholic (71.46%), followed by [[Evangelicalism|evangelicals]] (16.62%), [[Kardecist spiritism|spiritists]] (3.71%), no religion (6.01%) and the rest belonging to other religions. Among the evangelicals, the largest share declared themselves linked to the [[Assemblies of God|Assembly of God]] (4.12%), followed by the [[Foursquare Church|Church of the Foursquare Gospel]] (1.30%) and the [[Methodism|Methodist Church]] (1.14%).<ref name=":0" />
