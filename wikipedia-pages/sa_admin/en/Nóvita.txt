'''Nóvita''' is a [[municipality]] and [[town]] in the [[Chocó Department]], [[Colombia]].

It was the first capital of Chocó Province. It was a traditional center for [[gold mining]],<ref>{{Cite web
| last = Paz
| first = Manuel María
| author-link = Manuel María Paz Delgado
| title = Exterior View of the Houses in Nóvita, Province of Chocó
| work = World Digital Library
| access-date = 2014-05-21
| url = http://www.wdl.org/en/item/9056
}}</ref> Many of its residents are [[Afro-Colombian]]s.<ref>{{Cite web
| last = Paz
| first = Manuel María
| title = View from a Street in Nóvita, Chocó Province
| work = World Digital Library
| access-date = 2014-05-21
| url = http://www.wdl.org/en/item/9060
}}</ref>

In the 1850s, supplies, and possibly even people, were brought to the town by [[Porter (carrier)|porters]] due to "the mountainous terrain, very high rainfall, and many rivers" of the surrounding region.<ref>{{Cite web
| title = Porters on the Mountain of Nóvita, Chocó Province
| work = World Digital Library
| access-date = 2014-05-22
| url = http://www.wdl.org/en/item/9065
}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web
| title = Road to Nóvita via Tamaná Mountain, Chocó Province
| work = World Digital Library

}}</ref>
