'''Archidona Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]] located in the [[Napo Province]], with its capital in the town of [[Archidona, Ecuador|Archidona]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 18,551.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
