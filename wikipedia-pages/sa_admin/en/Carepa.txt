'''Carepa''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Carepa is part of the Urabá Antioquia sub-region.

==Climate==
Carepa has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
