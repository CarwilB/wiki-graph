'''Orobó''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ɔɾɔˈbɔ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]]/city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025 was 22,412 inhabitants and the total area is 138,362&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" /> The city has the lowest children's [[mortality rate]] of the state (2.9) and one of the lowest in the whole country.

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]] state   ('''N''');  [[Bom Jardim, Pernambuco|Bom Jardim]]   ('''S''');  [[Machados]] and [[São Vicente Ferrer, Pernambuco|São Vicente Ferrer]]   ('''E''');   [[Casinhas]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 140.78&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 415 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caducifólia]] and [[Subcaducifólia]] forests
* Climate  -  [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 127.4&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Orobó''' are related with  commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creations of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[pig]]s, [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[banana]]s and [[sugarcane]].
