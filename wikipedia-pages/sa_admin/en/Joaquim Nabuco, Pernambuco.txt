'''Joaquim Nabuco''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]]. It has an estimated population of 16,011 inhabitants as of 2020, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]].<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/joaquim-nabuco/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It has a total area of 121,88&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. [[Joaquim Nabuco]] was a famous Pernambuco [[politician|statesman]], who lived in the 19th century.

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Joaquim Nabuco''' are based in food and beverage industry and [[agribusiness]], especially plantations of [[sugarcane]], [[manioc]]; and creations of [[cattle]].
