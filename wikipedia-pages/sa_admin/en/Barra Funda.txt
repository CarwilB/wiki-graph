'''Barra Funda''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 2,551.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/barra-funda/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Barra Funda began to be colonized in 1919 by colonizers from the region of Guaporé, Veranópolis and Caxias do Sul. Until then, the region was inhabited by the [[Guaraní people|Guarani Indians]].

The area was a point of low altitude used by cattle herders to cross a river, which gave rise to the name of the municipality.
