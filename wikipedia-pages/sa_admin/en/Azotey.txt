'''Azotey''' ([[Guarani language|Guarani]]: {{Lang|gn|Asote'y}} {{IPA|gn|asoteˈʔɨ||gn-Asote'y.wav}}) is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Departments of Paraguay|department]] of [[Concepción Department, Paraguay|Concepción]], [[Paraguay]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.anr.org.py/pdftep/informe-final-17-12-2017-y-complementaria-07-01-2018.pdf|title=Elección Interna Partidaria para elegir Candidatos a Presidente y Vice-Presidente de la República; Miembros de las Cámaras de Senadores, Diputados, Parlamentarios del Mercosur; Gobernadores y Miembros deJuntas Departamentales|website=Asociacion Nacional Republicana|access-date=3 February 2019}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/paraguay-admin.php|title=Paraguay: Administrative Division|website=citypopulation|access-date=3 February 2019}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The word ''asote'y'' in Guarani means [[whip]]. It is a compound which agglutinates the Spanish word for whip (''azote'') and the Guarani term for handle (''y'').<ref>{{Cite book |last=[[Antonio Ruiz de Montoya|Ruiz de Montoya]] |first=Antonio |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=xtsVAAAAMAAJ |title=Tesoro de la lengua guarani |date=1876 |publisher=B.G. Teubner |language=es}}</ref>
