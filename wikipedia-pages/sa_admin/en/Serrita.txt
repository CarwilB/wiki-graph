'''Serrita''' is a city  in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 18,764 inhabitants and the total area is 1535.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - Ceará state    ('''N''');  [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]]  and [[Terra Nova, Pernambuco|Terra Nova]]  ('''S''');  [[Salgueiro]] and [[Cedro, Ceará|Cedro]]   ('''E''');   [[Moreilândia]] and [[Granito]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 1603.6&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 419 m
* Hydrography - [[Brigida River|Brigida]] and [[Terra Nova River|Terra Nova rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot
* Annual average temperature - 27.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 535.5&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Serrita''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pigs]]s, [[cattle]], [[horses]], [[donkeys]], [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[onions]], [[beans]] and [[banana]]s.
