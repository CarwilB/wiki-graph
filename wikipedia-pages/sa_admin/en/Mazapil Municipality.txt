'''Mazapil''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]] and the state's largest municipality by area. The [[Peñasquito Polymetallic Mine|Peñasquito mine]], Mexico's largest gold mine, is located in this sparsely populated municipality.

==Geography==
The municipality of Mazapil is located in northern Zacatecas where it borders three other Mexican states. The Zacatecan municipalities it borders are [[Melchor Ocampo Municipality|Melchor Ocampo]] to the north, [[General Francisco R. Murguía Municipality|General Francisco R. Murguía]] to the west, [[Villa de Cos]] to the south and [[Concepción del Oro Municipality|Concepción del Oro]] to the east. Mazapil also borders the municipalities of [[Catorce]], [[Santo Domingo, San Luis Potosí|Santo Domingo]] and [[Vanegas]], to the east, all part of [[San Luis Potosí]]; [[San Juan de Guadalupe Municipality|San Juan de Guadalupe]] in the state of [[Durango]] to the west; and [[Saltillo Municipality|Saltillo]] and [[Viesca Municipality|Viesca]] to the north, both in [[Coahuila]].<ref name=infocard/> Mazapil is Zacatecas's largest and Mexico's twelfth largest municipality,<ref>In 2003 [[INAFED]] reported that Mazapil was Mexico's thirteenth largest municipality by area: {{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Municipios_de_mayor_y_menor_extension_territo/ |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131017053709/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/ELOC_Municipios_de_mayor_y_menor_extension_territo |archive-date=17 October 2013 |title=Los Municipios con Mayor y Menor Extensión Territorial |accessdate=16 January 2018 }} In 2011, the municipality of [[Othón P. Blanco, Quintana Roo|Othón P. Blanco]] in [[Quintana Roo]] lost over 7000 km<sup>2</sup> of its area after the creation of [[Bacalar Municipality]], so that Mazapil now ranks twelfth.</ref> covering an area of {{convert|12143.256|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprising 16.1% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/>

Mazapil is situated in the [[Chihuahuan Desert]] and has an elevation range between {{convert|1300|and(-)|3200|m}}.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Mazapil, Zacatecas |publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=16 January 2018 |archive-date=4 August 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110804015215/http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Matorral]] vegetation predominates in the mostly arid and semi-arid climate, although open [[pinyon-juniper woodland]]s occur in the more elevated regions with their more temperate and partially sub-humid climates.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=cacti>{{cite journal |last1=Brailovsky Signoret |first1=David |last2=Hernandez |first2=Hector M. |date=2010 |title=Mazapil, Zacatecas: Diversity and Conservation of Cacti in a Poorly-Known Arid Region in Northern Mexico |doi=10.2985/015.082.0502 |journal=Cactus and Succulent Journal |volume=82 |issue=5 |pages=197–202 }}</ref> Average annual precipitation in the municipality varies between {{convert|200|and(-)|600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Mazapil's name derives from the [[Nahuatl]] place name ''Mazatlpilli'', itself derived from [[:wikt:en:mazatl|''mazatl'']] "deer" and [[:wikt:en:pilli|''pilli'']] "small".<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/index.html |title=Mazapil |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=16 January 2018 |archive-date=29 May 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160529223640/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/index.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The original inhabitants of the Mazapil valley were nomadic [[Chichimeca]] peoples known to the Spaniards as [[Guachichil]]es. [[Francisco de Ibarra]] reached the area in 1554. In 1562, Pedro de Ahumada y Samano reported finding the valley inhabited by 6000 native warriors armed with bows and arrows. Silver mines were first established in the valley in the late 1560s.<ref name=history>{{cite web |url=http://mazapil.gob.mx/HistoricoTransparencia/About.html |title=Mazapil |publisher=Government of Mazapil |accessdate=16 January 2018}}</ref>

Mazapil was one of Zacatecas's eleven original subdivisions (then known as ''partidos'') when the state's constitution was enacted in 1825.<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |page=59 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 }}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> In 1885 the fall of a [[meteorite]] in the area during the [[Andromedids]] [[meteor shower]] attracted scientific attention, although a cometary origin for the meteorite is now considered unlikely and the timing of its fall has been ascribed to coincidence.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Beech |first1=Martin |date=2002 |title=The Mazapil meteorite: from paradigm to periphery |doi=10.1111/j.1945-5100.2002.tb00845.x |journal=Meteoritics & Planetary Science |volume=37 |issue=5 |pages=649–660 |doi-access=free }}</ref> 

In 1907 [[Gustavo A. Madero]] set up four factories in the municipality for the extraction of [[guayule]] rubber.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Guayule from the Mazapil region was also exported to the US for rubber production during [[World War II]].<ref name=cacti/>

==Administration==
Mazapil's municipal government comprises a president, secretary, councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and six trustees (''regidores'').<ref name=officialsite/> The current president of the municipality is Gregorio Macías Zúñiga.<ref name=officialsite/>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Mazapil recorded a population of 17,813 inhabitants living in 4275 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=026 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Mazapil |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |accessdate=16 January 2018}}</ref> It recorded a population of 17,457 inhabitants in the 2015 Intercensal Survey.<ref name=survey/> 

There are 175 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Mazapil, is classified as urban. Located in the northeast corner of the municipality, it recorded a population of 794 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=infocard/>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Mazapil are mining and agriculture.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

In 2015, the municipality produced {{convert|28543|kg|ozt}} of gold, {{convert|942002|kg|ozt}} of silver, 15,563 tonnes of copper, 85,334 tonnes of lead and 207,844 tonnes of zinc.<ref>{{cite book |title=Cuadro 16.1: Volumen de la producción de minerales seleccionados por municipio 2013, 2014 y 2015 |work=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Zacatecas 2016 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=Spanish |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=978-607-739-849-3 }}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> [[Goldcorp]]'s Peñasquito gold mine, Mexico's largest, is located in Mazapil.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.goldcorp.com/English/portfolio/operations/penasquito/default.aspx |title=Peñasquito |publisher=[[Goldcorp]] |accessdate=17 January 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Jamasmie |first=Cecilia |url=https://www.mining.com/goldcorp-begins-controlled-shutdown-of-mexico-mine-its-largest-operation/ |title=Goldcorp begins controlled shutdown of Mexico mine, its largest operation |publisher=MINING.com |date=13 October 2016 |accessdate=17 January 2018}}</ref> Local communities have protested the mine's activities, citing water overuse and contamination, creation of health problems for locals, and inadequate compensation for workers and landowners.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://micla.ca/conflicts/penasquito/ |title=Peñasquito, Mexico |publisher=MICLA |accessdate=16 January 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |url=https://ecoosfera.com/2016/09/minera-penasquito-amenaza-vida-de-campesinos-en-zacatecas/ |title=Campesinos se manifiestan contra minera canadiense en Mazapil, Zacatecas |language=es |publisher=Ecoosfera |date=28 September 2016 |accessdate=17 January 2018}}</ref>

The main crops in Mazapil are corn and beans. [[Ixtle]] fiber and [[candelilla wax]] are also produced.<ref>{{cite report |date=1982 |title=A Technology Assessment of the Commercialization of Mexican Guayule: Final Report |url=https://hdl.handle.net/10150/304616 |publisher=Office of Arid Lands Studies, University of Arizona; Centro de Investigacion en Quimica Aplicada |pages=155–156 |hdl=10150/304616 |accessdate=16 January 2018}}</ref>
