'''Huiramba''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://mexico.pueblosamerica.com/i/huiramba/|title=HUIRAMBA (Huiramba, Michoacán de Ocampo)|website=Mexico.pueblosamerica.com|access-date=7 August 2018}}</ref> The central settlement is also called Huiramba, and the municipality includes the localities El Pedregal, El Refugio, El Sauz, La Presa, La Reunión, Las Trojes, Los Cerritos and Tupátaro.
