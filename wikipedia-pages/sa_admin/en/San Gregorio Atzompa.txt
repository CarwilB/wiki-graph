'''San Gregorio Atzompa''' is a [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=January 4, 2010}}</ref>

== Towns ==

=== [[:es:Chipilo|Chipilo de Francisco Javier Mina]] ===
Chipilo de Francisco Javier Mina, known as Chipilo, is a small city located 12 kilometers south of the city of [[:es:Estado de Puebla|Puebla]], [[:es:México|México]]. It is located 2150 meters above sea level. Its official name honors the memory of [[:es:Francisco Javier Mina|Francisco Javier Mina]]. The name for people originally from Chipilo is chipileño/chipileña. Most of the inhabitants of Chipilo speak [[:es:Chipileño|chipileño]], a variant of [[:es:Idioma véneto|véneto]].<ref>[http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ipX1y4eL2Rw Video sobre Chipilo]</ref>

== Demography ==
According to the results of the 2020 Population and Housing Census carried out by the [[:es:Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]]. The total population of San Gregorio Atzompa amounts to 9,671 people; of which 48.4% are men and 51.6% are women.
