'''Argelia''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. Founded by Order No. 02 on November 3, 1967, the municipality covers an area of 713&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and has a population of around 20,000.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.caucanet.net.co/nf/municipios/argelia.htm |title=Departemento del Cauca |publisher=Fundefilms |accessdate=30 March 2009 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20081123033244/http://www.caucanet.net.co/nf/municipios/argelia.htm |archivedate=23 November 2008 }}</ref> The population is primarily engaged in agriculture.
