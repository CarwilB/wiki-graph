'''Goascorán''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle (department)|Valle]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|191.1|km2}} and had a population of 14,342 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== Etymology ==
Goascorán might have been derived from the indigenous Uaxcayan language, which means "houses of Guajes", where Guajes refers to native plant species. It might also have been derived from the "Guas" bird, which might refer to the [[raptor (bird)|raptors]] nesting along the [[Gulf of Fonseca]] in the region.<ref name="Red"/>

==History==
The settlement was initially established in 1686 at "Playa Grande del Guayabal". Due to pirate attacks, the town was shifted to a new location called San Jerónimo. However, as the pirate attacks continued, the inhabitatns moved further inland to the current location. It part of [[Comayagua Department]] during the political division of 1825. It was part of a land dispute with the neighboring El Salvador, which was settled by the [[International Court of Justice]] in 1991.<ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Goascorán|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-goascoran/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Goascorán, Valle|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Valle/Goascorán.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Goascorán is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle Department|Valle]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It shares international border with [[El Salvador]], demarcated by the [[Goascorán River]].<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|191.1|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1706__goascor%C3%A1n/|title=Goascorán, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Goascorán has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|30.55|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|198.96|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/valle/goascoran|title=Goascorán, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Goascorán had a total population of 14,342 inhabitants, of whom 6,868 (47.9%) were men and 7,474 (52.1%) were women. About 11% of the population was classified as urban and 12,759 residents (89.0%) lived in the rural areas.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1706__goascor%C3%A1n/|title=Goascorán, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 4,607 individuals (32.1%) were aged 0–14 years, 8,549 individuals (59.6%) were aged 15–64, and 1,186 individuals (8.3%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 23.0 years and the mean age was 28.5 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 16.0%, lower than the departmental average of 17.7%. The municipality had 3,410 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.1 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
