'''Los Salias,''' founded in 1982, is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 68000.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> It's located approximately 14&nbsp;km away from Caracas and it borders with the [[Capital District (Venezuela)|Capital District]] to the north, [[Guaicaipuro Municipality]] to the south, [[Baruta Municipality|Baruta]] to the east and [[Carrizal Municipality|Carrizal]] to the west. Los Salias has an Area of 51&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and a max altitude of 1700 metres above sea level. The town of [[San Antonio de Los Altos]] is the [[municipal seat]] of Los Salias.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 }}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela (INE), Los Salias Municipality, has a population of 68.000. The municipality's population density is {{convert|1333.33|PD/sqkm||abbr=}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Los Salias Municipality is José Antonio Fernández López, elected on December 12 with 59.9% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/resultadosMunicipales2017/ | title=Divulgación Resultados Electorales Municipales y Regionales del Zulia 2017 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into one [[Parishes of Venezuela|parish]] (San Antonio de Los Altos).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
