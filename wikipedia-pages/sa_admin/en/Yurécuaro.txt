'''Yurécuaro''' is a town and a municipality in the region of the Chapala [[cienega]] at the north-west in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Michoacán]],<ref name="census2020">{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/scitel/Default?ev=9|title=Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 - SCITEL|language=es|publisher=INEGI|access-date=2021-01-27}}</ref> at a height of 1530 meters (5065&nbsp;ft) above sea level.  Its limits to the north with the state of [[Jalisco]] separated by the [[Lerma River|Lerma]] river, at west with [[La Piedad]], at south with [[Ecuandureo]] and to the southeast with [[Tanhuato]]. The distance to the state capital [[Morelia]] is {{convert|125|mi}} and to [[Guadalajara]] is {{convert|85|mi}}.

==Etymology==
Yurécuaro comes from the [[Purépecha language|Purepecha]] iorekuarhu meaning "riverside" or "place of growing".

==History==
The city was founded May 22, 1559 by Viceroy Don Luis de Velasco.

==Transportation==
Transport routes in Yurécuaro include:
*Road state 110 east to [[La Piedad]] west [[La Barca (municipality)|La Barca]]
*Road Yurecuaro-La Concepcion, [[Jalisco]]
*[[Zamora, Michoacán|Zamora]]-Yurecuaro toll expressway (under planning)
*Train [[Mexico City|Mexico]]-[[Guadalajara]] and Yurecuaro-Zamora
