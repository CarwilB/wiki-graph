'''Padre Hurtado''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpaðɾe wɾˈtaðo}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in the [[Talagante Province]] of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region|Metropolitan Region]]. It is part of the [[Greater Santiago]] conurbation and is one of its outermost communes. The commune is named in honor of [[Saint Alberto Hurtado]], commonly known as ''Padre Hurtado'' ("Father Hurtado").

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Padre Hurtado spans an area of {{convert|80.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 38,768 inhabitants (19,367 men and 19,401 women). Of these, 34,257 (88.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,511 (11.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 32% (9,396 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Padre Hurtado is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Felipe Muñoz Heredia ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]). The communal council has the following members:
* Paz González Zúñiga ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Marcela Rojas Flores ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Ignacio Arias Díaz (Ind/[[Liberal Party of Chile (2013)|PL]])
* Francisco Garrido Sanhueza ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Daniela Díaz Santibáñez ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Rocío López Céspedes ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Padre Hurtado is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]] and [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
