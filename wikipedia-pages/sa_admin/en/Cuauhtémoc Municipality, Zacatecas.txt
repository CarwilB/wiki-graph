'''Cuauhtémoc''' is one of the 58 [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipalities]] in the [[Political divisions of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]]. It is located in the central part of the state of Zacatecas and it is bounded by the municipalities of [[Ojocaliente, Zacatecas|Ojocaliente]], [[Luis Moya, Zacatecas|Luis Moya]], and [[Genaro Codina, Zacatecas|Genaro Codina]]; it also shares a border with the state of [[Aguascalientes]]. The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|318|km2|sqmi|sp=us}}.<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32007a.htm "Cuauhtémoc"] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930015053/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32007a.htm |date=September 30, 2007 }} [[INAFED]]: ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''.</ref>
