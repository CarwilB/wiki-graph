'''Catende''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]]. Its estimated population in 2020 was 43,340 inhabitants in a total area of 207.24&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/catende/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The economy is based on the cultivation of sugarcane and the production of derived products (sugar and [[ethanol]]).  Catende used to have  the world's largest sugar mill. {{Citation needed|date=March 2008}}

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Bonito, Pernambuco|Bonito]]  ('''N'''); [[Palmares, Pernambuco|Palmares]]  ('''E''');  [[Maraial]] and [[Jaqueira]]  ('''S''');  [[Belém de Maria]]  ('''W''')
* Area - 206.92&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 168 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] river
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 24.8 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 137&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Catende''' are based in industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]] especially [[sugarcane]] and [[cattle]].
