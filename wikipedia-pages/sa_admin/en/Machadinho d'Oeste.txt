'''Machadinho d'Oeste''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 40,867 (2020) and its area is 8,509 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|346861|ha|adj=on}} [[Jaru Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1984.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Jaru|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=208|accessdate=2016-04-27}}</ref>
It also contains 13% of the [[Campos Amazônicos National Park]], a {{convert|961318|ha}} protected area created in 2006 that protects an unusual enclave of [[cerrado]] vegetation in the Amazon rainforest.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1482|accessdate=2016-06-03}}</ref>

The municipality contains 78% of the [[Angelim Extractive Reserve]] and 79% of the [[Ipê Extractive Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Angelim – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Angelim|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/627|accessdate=2016-08-30}}</ref><ref>*{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Ipê – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Ipê|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3033 |accessdate=2016-08-31}}</ref>
It also contains the [[Castanheira Extractive Reserve|Castanheira]], [[Freijó Extractive Reserve|Freijó]], [[Garrote Extractive Reserve|Garrote]], [[Itaúba Extractive Reserve|Itaúba]], [[Jatobá Extractive Reserve|Jatobá]], [[Maracatiara Extractive Reserve|Maracatiara]], [[Massaranduba Extractive Reserve|Massaranduba]], [[Mogno Extractive Reserve|Mogno]], [[Piquiá Extractive Reserve|Piquiá]], [[Roxinho Extractive Reserve|Roxinho]] and [[Sucupira Extractive Reserve|Sucupira]] extractive reserves.
