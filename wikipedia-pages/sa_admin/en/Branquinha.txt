'''Branquinha''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[bɾɐ̃ˈkĩj̃ɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 10,460 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/branquinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 191&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref> It was devastated by flooding in June 2010.
