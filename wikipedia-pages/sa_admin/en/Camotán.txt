'''Camotán''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula department]] of [[Guatemala]].

==History==

===2001 famine===

On 3 August 2001, Camotán municipality declared yellow code in the area when it learned about the desperate situation that the rural communities were facing imminent famine; the root cause of the crisis were the short raining season, and the decline in the international coffee price.<ref name=industriall>{{cite web|url=http://www.industriall-union.org/es/archive/imf/la-hambruna-y-la-sequia-golpean-a-guatemala-ya-hay-48-muertos |title=La hambruna y la sequía golpean a Guatemala: ya hay 48 muertos |website=Industriall global union |date=14 September 2001 |language=es |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151020192444/http://www.industriall-union.org/es/archive/imf/la-hambruna-y-la-sequia-golpean-a-guatemala-ya-hay-48-muertos |archive-date=October 20, 2015 }}</ref> [[Alfonso Portillo]]'s government decreed State of Calamity to get international help; officially, 48 deceased were reported, but there were rumors of hundreds of casualties.<ref name=industriall/>

In March 2022, the Catholic church in the town of Camotan burnt down because lightning stroke it after a severe rainstorm, and the town mayor, Noe Guerra asked for national refund help to rebuild the church, there is no further notice about the plan.

== Population ==

Total population as of 2002 was 35,263, living in 6,479 homes.
