'''São José do Xingu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]], in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |date=July 1, 2008 |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |trans-title=Territorial Division of Brazil |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |access-date=December 17, 2009 |publisher=Territorial Division of Brazil and Territorial Limits, Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web |date=August 14, 2009 |title=Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009 |trans-title=Population estimates for July 1, 2009 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf |access-date=December 17, 2009 |publisher=Population Estimates, Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |language=pt |format=[[PDF]]}}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |year=2000 |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |trans-title=Descending ranking of the HDI-M of municipalities in Brazil |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |access-date=December 17, 2009 |publisher=Human Development Atlas, United Nations Development Program (UNDP) |language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |date=December 19, 2007 |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |trans-title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities 2002-2005 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |access-date=December 17, 2009 |publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) |language=pt}}</ref>

==Plane crash==
{{Main|Varig Flight 254}}
On 3 September 1989, a [[Varig]] [[Boeing 737|Boeing 737-241]] registration PP-VMK, operating [[Varig Flight 254|flight 254]] flying from [[São Paulo-Guarulhos International Airport|São Paulo-Guarulhos]] to [[Val de Cães International Airport|Belém-Val de Cães]] with intermediate stops, crashed near São José do Xingu while on the last leg of the flight between [[Marabá Airport|Marabá]] and Belém due to a [[Aviator|pilot]] [[navigation]]al error, which led to [[fuel exhaustion]] and a subsequent [[belly landing]] into the jungle, 40&nbsp;km from São José do Xingu. Out of 54 occupants, there were 13 fatalities, all of them passengers. Only 7 of the passengers died as a direct result of the crash, the other 6 occupants died after the crash, due to injuries and at least partially caused by the delayed arrival of rescue resources. Since the flight crashed about 1.200 km away from its intended destination, caused by flying into the wrong direction immediately after take-off - first west, then south, instead of the required north-eastern direction - the search and rescue operations focused on a completely wrong region, much closer to the actual destination of Belém. The survivors were discovered only after 4 of the occupants walked for 3 hours through the forest to find help.<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://aviation-safety.net/database/record.php?id=19890903-0 | title=Accident description PP-VMK | publisher=Aviation Safety Network | access-date=25 June 2011}}</ref><ref>{{cite book |last=da Silva |first=Germano |title=O rastro da bruxa: história da aviação comercial brasileira no século XX através dos seus acidentes 1928-1996 |last2=César |first2=Carlos Ari |publisher=EDIPUCRS |year=2008 |isbn=978-85-7430-760-2 |edition=2 |place=Porto Alegre |pages=352–360 |language=pt |trans-title=The witch's trail: history of Brazilian commercial aviation in the 20th century through its accidents 1928-1996 |chapter=Rumo errado |trans-chapter=Wrong direction}}</ref>
