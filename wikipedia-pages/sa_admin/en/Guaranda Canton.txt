'''Guaranda Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]] in the [[Bolívar Province, Ecuador|Bolívar Province]].  Its capital is [[Guaranda]] and its population in the 2001 census is 81,643.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/|title=Resultados}}</ref>
*[[Mestizo]]  49.1%
*[[Indigenous peoples in Ecuador|Indigenous]]  47.0%
*[[White Latin American|White]]  2.3%
*[[Montubio]]  0.8%
*[[Afro-Ecuadorian]]  0.7%
*Other  0.1%
