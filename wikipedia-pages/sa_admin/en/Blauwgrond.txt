'''Blauwgrond''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 31,483.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/ysr.html Statoids.com]</ref> Blauwgrond started as a [[Javanese Surinamese|Javanese]] village to the north of [[Paramaribo]]. During the 1960s and 1970s, large scale building projects turned the village into a neighbourhood of Paramaribo,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dbnl.org/tekst/binn011sran01_01/binn011sran01_01_0007.php|title=Sranan. Cultuur in Suriname|date=1992|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl|website= Digital Library for Dutch Literature}}</ref> and is mainly known as the Javanese culinary centre with many ''[[warung]]s'' and restaurants.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://werkgroepcaraibischeletteren.nl/the-blauwgrond-experience/|title=The Blauwgrond Experience|website=Werkgroup Caraïbische Letteres|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> The resort was called Blauwgrond (English: Blue Ground), because the earth had a bluish colour.

Leonsberg is a former [[coffee]] plantation in the northern part of Blauwgrond. It was founded by A. Léon in 1819.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/l/leonsberg|title=Plantage Leonsberg|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> At Leonsberg there is a [[Ferry terminal Leonsberg|ferry]] to [[Nieuw Amsterdam, Suriname|Nieuw-Amsterdam]].
