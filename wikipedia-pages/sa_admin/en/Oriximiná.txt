'''Oriximiná''' is the westernmost and second-largest (by territorial area) municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Pará]]. It is also the fourth-largest in the country.

==Geography==

The city lies on the [[Trombetas]] river, {{convert|40|km}} northwest of [[Óbidos, Brazil|Óbidos]]. The city is crossed by the [[Equator]].
Oriximiná is served by [[Oriximiná Airport]] located {{convert|8|km}} from downtown Oriximiná. [[Porto Trombetas Airport]] located in the district of Porto Trombetas {{convert|70|km}} away serves the population residing and working for the [https://web.archive.org/web/20080113222124/http://www.mrn.com.br/ Mineração Rio do Norte S/A].

The north of the municipality contains most (75.89%) of the {{convert|4245819|ha|adj=on}} [[Grão-Pará Ecological Station]], the largest fully protected tropical forest conservation unit on the planet.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|title=Estação Ecológica Grão-Pará|publisher=Ideflor-bio (Government of Pará)|url=http://ideflorbio.pa.gov.br/unidades-de-conservacao/regiao-administrativa-calha-norte-iii/estacao-ecologica-grao-para/|access-date=2016-05-12|archive-date=26 September 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200926143414/https://ideflorbio.pa.gov.br/unidades-de-conservacao/regiao-administrativa-calha-norte-iii/estacao-ecologica-grao-para/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The municipality also contains the {{convert|1007580|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Trombetas Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1979 to preserve the ecology and specifically to protect the [[Arrau turtle]].<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Rio Trombetas|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=210|access-date=2016-04-17}}</ref>
The municipality contains part of the [[Saracá-Taquera National Forest]], a {{convert|441283|ha|acre|adj=mid}} sustainable-use conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional de Saracá-Taquera|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=109|access-date=2016-06-01}}</ref>
It contains 88% of the {{convert|3172978|ha|adj=on}} [[Trombetas State Forest]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Trombetas – ISA}}|language=pt |title=FES do Trombetas|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/es/uc/6782|access-date=2016-09-07}}</ref>
