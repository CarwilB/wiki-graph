The city is one of the coldest in Brazil, and sometimes there is a chance of snow.

==History==
Bom Jardim da Serra was a part of [[São Joaquim]] municipality, the city was discovered in 1870, and it became municipality on 26 January 1967.

==Geography==
Located at 1,245 meters above sea level, Bom Jardim da Serra is a calm city, with its 4,772 inhabitants. The average temperature is 5 °C (41 °F) in the winter and 14 °C (57 °F) during the summer. On June 19, 2023, in Bom Jardim da Serra, a temperature of {{convert|-10.9|°C|}} was recorded.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.wunderground.com/dashboard/pws/IBOMJA2/graph/2023-06-19/2023-06-19/daily|title=Personal Weather Station Dashboard {{pipe}} Weather Underground}}</ref> This temperature was the lowest temperature recorded in a standardized station of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina state]], surpassing the −10 °C recorded in [[São Joaquim]], on August 2, 1991.<ref>{{cite tweet|number=1421530925477806082|user=Climaterra|title=FRIO DE -10,4 em SC!! 20 municípios...|date=31 July 2021}}</ref>
