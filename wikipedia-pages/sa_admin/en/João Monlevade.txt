'''João Monlevade''' is a [[Brazil]]ian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the interior of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]] of Brazil. It is situated east of the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], approximately 110 kilometers away. The municipality covers an [[area]] of {{cvt|99.158|km2}}, of which {{cvt|29.1|km2}} is classified as [[urban area]], with a population of 83,360 inhabitants in 2024.

João Monlevade was emancipated in the 20th century, specifically in 1964, with its development largely driven by the establishment of ArcelorMittal Aços Longos (formerly ''Companhia Siderúrgica Belgo-Mineira'') in 1921.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://decsi.ufop.br/hist%C3%B3ria-do-departamento |title=History of the Department |access-date=27 November 2021 |website=decsi.ufop.br |language=pt-br |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211127000000/https://decsi.ufop.br/hist%C3%B3ria-do-departamento |archive-date=27 November 2021}}</ref> The municipality currently comprises nearly sixty neighborhoods,<ref name="GrandeBrasil_JM"/> and it boasts various natural, historical, and cultural attractions, including the São José do Operário Mother Church, constructed in the 1940s, and the Catalan Forge, with its imposing and renowned headquarters (Fazenda Solar), built to house Jean-Antoine Félix Dissandes de Monlevade, the pioneer after whom the municipality is named.

== History ==
=== Origins ===
[[File:Jean monlevade.jpg|thumb|left|Jean-Antoine Félix Dissandes de Monlevade, founder of the city.]]
Until the early 19th century, the region now occupied by João Monlevade was merely dense, untouched forest. In August 1817, the [[France|French]] engineer Jean-Antoine Félix Dissandes de Monlevade arrived in the area. In what was then the Province of Minas Gerais, Jean conducted a [[mineralogy|mineralogical]] and [[geology|geological]] study of the soil, which led to the discovery of extensive [[mineral deposit]]s suitable for [[iron]] production.<ref name="Prefeitura_História">{{cite web |url=http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6507 |title=History of João Monlevade |author=Communication Office of the João Monlevade Municipal Prefecture (AcomPMJM) |date=20 June 2011 |publisher=Prefecture |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110909000000/http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6507 |archive-date=9 September 2011}}</ref>

Following this discovery, the Frenchman traveled through various districts, such as Sabará, Caeté, and São Miguel de Piracicaba, where he acquired several land grants and built a [[Catalan forge]], as well as his residence, the Solar Monlevade, in 1818. He established a factory that achieved great success, becoming one of the largest during the imperial period, producing everything from hoes to bridles.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> In 1935, another major industry, the ''Companhia Siderúrgica Belgo-Mineira'' (now ArcelorMittal Aços Longos), was established with the assistance of engineer Louis Ensch, sparking significant urban development. This period saw the construction of 3,000 homes, the Margarida Hospital, and the São José do Operário Mother Church, alongside improvements in roads, schools, and local commerce.<ref name="BibliotecaIBGE_História">{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/minasgerais/joaomonlevade.pdf |title=João Monlevade - History |author=Encyclopedia of Brazilian Municipalities |date=2007 |publisher=IBGE Library |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110909000000/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/minasgerais/joaomonlevade.pdf |archive-date=9 September 2011}}</ref> The area was then known as the ''Industrial Center of the Rio Piracicaba and Carneirinhos District''.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>

In 2022, a documentary titled [https://colonia.lu/pt-br The Luxembourg Colony], directed by Dominique Santana, was released, chronicling the history of Monlevade from the arrival of Louis Ensch.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.anoticiaregional.com.br/noticia.php?id=18172 |title=Documentary on the Luxembourg Colony |access-date=15 March 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220315000000/https://www.anoticiaregional.com.br/noticia.php?id=18172 |archive-date=15 March 2022}}</ref>

=== Political and administrative evolution ===
The area was originally part of the municipality of [[Rio Piracicaba]]. On 27 December 1948, by state law no. 336, the [[district]] of João Monlevade was created, named in honor of the engineer who pioneered the region. The district was elevated to [[municipality]] status by state law no. 2764 on 30 December 1962 and was officially established on 1 March 1963, consisting solely of the District Seat.<ref name="BibliotecaIBGE_História"/> Full municipal autonomy was achieved on 29 April 1964.<ref name="Câmara_História"/>

The first election was held in 1965, and on 5 December of that year, the first councilors (13 in total, with Sebastião Batista Gomes as president, João Amaro Gomes as vice-president, and Ronaldo Frade as secretary) took office, along with the mayor, Wilson Alvarenga, and his deputy mayor, Josué Henrique Dias.<ref name="Câmara_História">{{cite web |url=http://www.camarajm.mg.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=22&Itemid=34 |title=History |author=Communication Office |date=27 August 2009 |publisher=Municipal Chamber |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110909000000/http://www.camarajm.mg.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=22&Itemid=34 |archive-date=9 September 2011}}</ref> The João Monlevade Judicial District was established in 1975 and installed in 1979.<ref name="Câmara_História"/>

=== Post-emancipation development ===
The city's urban growth necessitated improvements in infrastructure. In addition to the Margarida Hospital and the Monlevade Gymnasium, built in the 1950s, other significant developments during this period included the establishment of the Commercial Association, the Educational Foundation (later FUNCEC), and the installation of Minas Gerais Telecommunications (Telemig) and the [[CEMIG|Energy Company of Minas Gerais]] (Cemig), which brought telephone services and electricity to the city, respectively.<ref name="Geo_História">{{cite web |url=http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/1/Historia-de-Joao-Monlevade.html |title=History of João Monlevade |author=Cultural Project Path of Riches |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110909000000/http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/1/Historia-de-Joao-Monlevade.html |archive-date=9 September 2011}}</ref> Between the 1970s and 1980s, there was increased investment in the cultural sector, including the construction of the Louis Ensch Municipal Stadium.<ref name="Tempos_Estádio">{{cite web |url=http://mavalem.sites.uol.com.br/mg/JoaoMonle.htm |title=Dr. Louis Ensch Municipal Stadium |author=Tempos do Futebol |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110909000000/http://mavalem.sites.uol.com.br/mg/JoaoMonle.htm |archive-date=9 September 2011}}</ref>

Today, the rural landscape is increasingly being replaced by urban development to meet the demands of urban expansion, driven by growth in productive activities (industry, [[commerce]], and services) and rising housing needs due to population concentration. The boundary between rural and urban areas is becoming less distinct, and the rural population is declining each year.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/><ref name="População_Sexo">{{cite web |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/demografia/mu_dem_pop_total.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |title=Demography - Total Population |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CNM) |date=2008 |access-date=10 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110910000000/http://www.cnm.org.br/demografia/mu_dem_pop_total.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |archive-date=10 September 2011}}</ref>

== Geography ==
[[File:Vista parcial João Monlevade MG com pedras ao fundo.jpg|thumb|left|Partial view of the city with rocky formations in the background. The municipality is characterized by mountainous terrain, with rolling hills and mountains.<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/>]]
According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), the municipality spans an area of {{formatnum:99.158}} km²,<ref name="IBGE_JoãoMonlevade"/> of which {{formatnum:29.104}} km² constitutes the [[urban area]].<ref name="Embrapa_Áreaurbana2015"/> It is located at 19°48′36″ south [[latitude]] and 43°10′26″ west [[longitude]], approximately 110 kilometers east of the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]].<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/> Its neighboring municipalities are [[Bela Vista de Minas]] to the east, [[São Gonçalo do Rio Abaixo]] to the west, [[Rio Piracicaba]] to the south, and [[Itabira]] to the north.<ref name="cidadesnet.com">{{cite web |url=http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/joaomonlevade.htm |title=João Monlevade - MG |author=Cidades.Net |access-date=9 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131005005608/http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/joaomonlevade.htm |archive-date=5 October 2013}}</ref>

The city center has an average elevation of 580.57 [[meters]]. The highest point in the municipality is in the Serra do Seara, reaching an elevation of {{formatnum:1340}} meters. The terrain is predominantly mountainous, with 68% of the municipal territory consisting of rolling hills and mountains, 20% undulating areas, and the remaining 12% flat terrain. The lowest elevation is found at the Jacuí Stream.<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/>

The municipality lies within the [[Doce River Basin]], and it is drained by the [[Piracicaba River (Minas Gerais)|Piracicaba]] and Santa Bárbara rivers.<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/> Like most municipalities in Minas Gerais, João Monlevade is surrounded by mountains and rocky formations. Some parts of the city have expanded into the hills without a directed urban planning framework aligned with environmental considerations, leading to geophysical characteristics that cause landslides during the rainy season. In some areas, the lack of green spaces hinders rainwater drainage, resulting in flooding and inundations.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.riodoce.cbh.gov.br/comite/GT-CHEIAS/capitulo4.htm |title=Diagnosis of Disasters Related to Heavy Rainfall |author=Doce River Basin Committee |access-date=10 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110910000000/http://www.riodoce.cbh.gov.br/comite/GT-CHEIAS/capitulo4.htm |archive-date=10 September 2011}}</ref>

Situated within the [[Atlantic Forest]] [[biome]], much of the municipality’s original vegetation was cleared in the 1930s with the establishment of the ''Companhia Siderúrgica Belgo-Mineira''. To mitigate [[environmental impact]]s and prevent potential [[environmental stress]], the company initiated a [[reforestation]] program in the 1940s.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/4/Historia-da-Cia.-Siderurgica-Belgo-Mineira.html |title=History of Companhia Siderúrgica Belgo-Mineira |author=Cultural Project Path of Riches |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/4/Historia-da-Cia.-Siderurgica-Belgo-Mineira.html |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref> Currently, various projects are ongoing or planned, including the annual Week of the Environment, held between late May and early June, which features lectures in schools and for the public, ecological walks, and tree planting across the city.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/noticias/1/660-ief-de-joao-monlevade-comemora-semana-do-meio-ambiente |title=IEF of João Monlevade Celebrates Environment Week |author=Minas Gerais State Forestry Institute (IEF) |date=10 June 2008 |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/noticias/1/660-ief-de-joao-monlevade-comemora-semana-do-meio-ambiente |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/noticias/1/862-nucleo-do-ief-de-joao-monlevade-comemora-semana-do-meio-ambiente |title=IEF Nucleus of João Monlevade Celebrates Environment Week |author=Minas Gerais State Forestry Institute (IEF) |date=1 June 2009 |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/noticias/1/862-nucleo-do-ief-de-joao-monlevade-comemora-semana-do-meio-ambiente |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref>

=== Climate ===
[[File:Tempestade noturna em João Monlevade MG.jpg|thumb|left|Storm with [[lightning]] in the city on a November night.]]
The climate in João Monlevade is classified as [[Humid subtropical climate|humid subtropical]]<ref name="IBGE_Clima">{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |title=Brazil - Climates |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |publisher=IBGE Library |access-date=10 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131012015042/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-date=12 October 2013}}</ref> (type ''Cwa'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]),<ref name="Climatologia_JoãoMonlevadeMG">{{cite web |url=https://pt.climate-data.org/location/24904/ |title=Climate: João Monlevade |author=Climate-Data.org |access-date=20 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180715174810/https://pt.climate-data.org/location/24904/ |archive-date=15 July 2018}}</ref> with an average annual temperature of {{formatnum:21}} °C and an average annual rainfall of {{formatnum:1400}} mm, concentrated between October and April, with December being the wettest month.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/> The [[wet season]] corresponds to the warmer months, while the [[dry season]] includes milder months.<ref name="Weatherspark"/> February, the warmest month, has an average temperature of {{formatnum:23.4}} °C, while July, the coldest month, averages {{formatnum:17.7}} °C.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/> Extreme cold events are rare during [[winter]], with [[autumn]] and [[Spring (season)|spring]] serving as transitional seasons.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/>

With nearly {{formatnum:1970}} hours of [[insolation]] annually, the [[relative humidity]] is relatively high, with an annual average above 75%.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/> [[Fog]] occurs in the mornings of colder months due to high humidity and lower temperatures.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://g1.globo.com/bom-dia-brasil/noticia/2014/12/br-262-e-uma-das-estradas-mais-perigosas-do-pais.html |title=BR-262 is one of the most dangerous roads in the country |author=Bom Dia Brasil |date=23 December 2015 |publisher=G1 |access-date=20 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180720230247/http://g1.globo.com/bom-dia-brasil/noticia/2014/12/br-262-e-uma-das-estradas-mais-perigosas-do-pais.html |archive-date=20 July 2018}}</ref> However, low humidity levels can occur during the [[dry season]] or prolonged [[Indian summer]]s. During these periods, dry air combined with [[pollution]] from vehicles, industries, and wildfires contributes to increased atmospheric pollutant concentration, worsening air quality.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/><ref>{{cite journal |last=Corte |first=Felipe Della |year=2018 |title=A case study on air quality in the municipality of João Monlevade |journal=Universidade Federal de Ouro Preto |pages=35–45; 49 |url=http://www.monografias.ufop.br/bitstream/35400000/925/1/MONOGRAFIA_EstudoCasoQualidade.pdf |access-date=20 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180424224709/http://www.monografias.ufop.br/bitstream/35400000/925/1/MONOGRAFIA_EstudoCasoQualidade.pdf |archive-date=24 April 2018}}</ref> The prevailing wind comes from the east, with the windiest period from 3 August to 28 November, averaging 11.3 kilometers per hour, with a slight peak in September and October. During the calmer period from March to June, the average wind speed ranges between 9 and 10 kilometers per hour.<ref name="Weatherspark">{{cite web |url=https://pt.weatherspark.com/y/30608/Clima-caracter%C3%ADstico-em-Jo%C3%A3o-Monlevade-Brasil-durante-o-ano |title=Average Weather Conditions in João Monlevade |author=Weather Spark |access-date=20 July 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180720230509/https://pt.weatherspark.com/y/30608/Clima-caracter%C3%ADstico-em-Jo%C3%A3o-Monlevade-Brasil-durante-o-ano |archive-date=20 July 2018}}</ref>

According to data from the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET) for the periods 1961–1984, 1986–1991 (until 31 October), 1993 (from 1 April) to 2002, and 2004–2018, the lowest temperature recorded in João Monlevade was {{formatnum:3.5}} °C on 1 June 1979,<ref name="FJP_Anuário1994"/> and the highest reached {{formatnum:37.8}} °C on 10 September 1997.<ref name="Temp-max-INMET"/> The highest 24-hour [[precipitation]] accumulation was {{formatnum:145.2}} mm on 4 January 1997. Other accumulations equal to or exceeding {{formatnum:100}} mm include {{formatnum:115.2}} mm on 20 December 2011, {{formatnum:111}} mm on 17 February 1998, {{formatnum:105.2}} mm on 27 January 1961, {{formatnum:103.9}} mm on 21 February 1964, {{formatnum:103}} mm on 10 November 2009, and {{formatnum:100.3}} mm on 23 January 1977.<ref name="Precip-INMET"/> December 2011, with {{formatnum:616}} mm, was the wettest month on record.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt_mensal.php?&mRelEstacao=83591&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1961&mRelDtFim=30/06/2018&mAtributos=,,,,,,,,,1,,,,,,, |title=BDMEP - Historical series - Monthly data - Total precipitation (mm) - João Monlevade |publisher=National Institute of Meteorology (INMET) |access-date=21 June 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140621000000/http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt_mensal.php?&mRelEstacao=83591&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1961&mRelDtFim=30/06/2018&mAtributos=,,,,,,,,,1,,,,,,, |archive-date=21 June 2014}}</ref>

In 2010, the population of the municipality was recorded by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE) as {{formatnum:73120}} inhabitants, making it the 47th most populous in the state, with a [[population density]] of 739.81 inhabitants per km².<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_minas_gerais.pdf |title=2010 Census - Minas Gerais |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=29 November 2010 |access-date=11 December 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101211000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_minas_gerais.pdf |archive-date=11 December 2010}}</ref> That year, {{formatnum:34963}} inhabitants were male, and {{formatnum:38488}} were female. According to the same census, {{formatnum:73120}} inhabitants lived in the [[urban area]], while 331 resided in the [[rural area]].<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010"/> The population was composed of {{formatnum:28170}} whites (38.27%), {{formatnum:9939}} blacks (13.50%), 663 Asians (0.90%), {{formatnum:34764}} mixed-race (47.23%), and 74 indigenous people (0.10%).<ref name="SIDRA_Raça">{{cite web |url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=3145&z=cd&o=4&i=P |title=Population of João Monlevade by Race and Color |author=Sidra (IBGE Automatic Data Retrieval System) |date=2000 |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=3145&z=cd&o=4&i=P |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref> According to 2018 statistics, the municipal population was {{formatnum:79387}} inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE_JoãoMonlevade"/>

The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) of João Monlevade is considered high by the United Nations Development Programme ([[United Nations Development Programme|UNDP]]), with a value of 0.807, ranking as the 28th highest in Minas Gerais (out of 853 municipalities) and 425th nationally (out of {{formatnum:5507}}). The city’s indicators are generally average and comparable to the national average according to the UNDP.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking of Municipal HDI in Brazil |date=2000 |work=Atlas of Human Development |publisher=[[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP) |access-date=11 October 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081011000000/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |archive-date=11 October 2008}}</ref> The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], is 0.38, where 1.00 is the worst and 0.00 is the best.<ref name="IBGE_Pobreza"/> In 2003, the incidence of poverty, as measured by the IBGE, was 23.68%, with a lower limit of 14.12%, an upper limit of 33.25%, and a subjective poverty incidence of 18.07%.<ref name="IBGE_Pobreza">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Social Indicators of Brazilian Municipalities |author=IBGE |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=mpobreza2003&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
[[File:Vista_da_Igreja_de_São_Luís_Maria_de_Montfort,_João_Monlevade_MG.jpg|thumb|right|Church of [[Louis-Marie Grignion de Montfort]]. Catholicism is the predominant religion in the municipality, as it is throughout Brazil.<ref name="Religião"/>]]
Reflecting the cultural diversity of João Monlevade, the city is home to a variety of religious expressions. While it developed primarily within a [[Catholicism|Catholic]] social framework, numerous [[Protestantism|Protestant]] denominations are present today.<ref name="Religião"/>

Located in the country with the largest Catholic population in absolute numbers, João Monlevade has four parishes (Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception, Our Lady of Fatima, São José Operário, and São Luís Maria de Montfort) and is part of the [[Diocese of Itabira-Fabriciano]]. The [[Catholic Church]] had its legal status recognized by the federal government in October 2009,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/mat/2009/10/08/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-767959739.asp |title=Senate approves agreement with the Vatican |language=Portuguese |author=Cristiane Agostine |publisher=O Globo |date=8 October 2009 |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110808095241/http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/mat/2009/10/08/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-767959739.asp |archive-date=8 August 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref> despite Brazil being an officially secular state.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://jus.com.br/artigos/8519/brasil-estado-laico-e-a-inconstitucionalidade-da-existencia-de-simbolos-religiosos-em-predios-publicos |title=Brazil: Secular state and the unconstitutionality of religious symbols in public buildings |author=Fernando Fonseca de Queiroz |date=October 2005 |publisher=Jus Navigandi |access-date=20 July 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720000000/https://jus.com.br/artigos/8519/brasil-estado-laico-e-a-inconstitucionalidade-da-existencia-de-simbolos-religiosos-em-predios-publicos |archive-date=20 July 2011}}</ref>

The city is home to various Protestant or reformed denominations, including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assembly of God]], Maranatha Christian Church, [[Presbyterianism|Presbyterian Church]], [[Baptist Church|Baptist churches]], the [[Seventh-day Adventist Church]], and the [[Universal Church of the Kingdom of God]], among others. According to the 2000 IBGE census, the population of João Monlevade consists of [[Catholicism|Catholics]] (82.26%), [[Protestantism|evangelicals]] (12.53%), [[Irreligion|people with no religion]] (3.69%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (0.52%), [[Umbanda|Umbandists]] (0.06%), and 0.94% belonging to other religions.<ref name="Religião">{{cite web |url=http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/municipioslista.php?a=search&value=1&SearchField=AnyField&SearchOption=Equals&SearchFor=Jo%E3o+Monlevade |title=Religion |access-date=11 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110911000000/http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/municipioslista.php?a=search&value=1&SearchField=AnyField&SearchOption=Equals&SearchFor=Jo%E3o+Monlevade |archive-date=11 September 2011}}</ref>

== Politics ==
Municipal administration is carried out by the [[executive branch]] and the [[legislative branch]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074 |title=The Municipal Public Power and the Constitutional Obligation to Create an Internal Control System |author=Flávio Henrique M. Lima |date=8 February 2006 |publisher=JusVi |access-date=12 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130212201150/http://jusvi.com/artigos/20074 |archive-date=12 February 2013 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The first leader of the executive branch and [[mayor]] of the municipality was Wilson Alvarenga, elected in December 1965, just over a year after the city’s emancipation.<ref name="Câmara_História"/> Over twenty-nine terms, various mayors have served João Monlevade.<ref name="Câmara_História"/> In 2009, Gustavo Prandini of the [[Green Party (Brazil)|Green Party]] (PV) won the [[2008 Brazilian municipal elections|municipal elections]] in João Monlevade with 37.94% of the valid votes. As the municipality had fewer than 200,000 voters, there was no second round.<ref name="Terra_Eleições2008">{{cite web |url=http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/47236/index.shtml |title=First Round Results |author=Terra |date=6 October 2008 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/47236/index.shtml |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref>

The [[legislative branch]] consists of the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of ten councilors elected for four-year terms (in accordance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]]<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |title=Federal Constitution - CF - 1988 / Art. 29 |author=DJI |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130111082108/http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |archive-date=11 January 2013 |url-status=dead }}</ref>) and is structured as follows:<ref name="vereadores">{{cite web |url=http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/47236/index.shtml#vereador |title=First Round Results (Councilors) |date=6 October 2008 |access-date=13 September 2011 |author=Terra |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/47236/index.shtml#vereador |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> two seats for the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]] (PT), two for the [[National Mobilization Party]] (PMN), two for the [[Brazilian Social Democracy Party]] (PSDB), one for the [[Green Party (Brazil)|Green Party]] (PV), one for the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement Party]] (PMDB), one for the [[Progressive Party (Brazil)|Progressive Party]] (PP), and one for the [[Republic Party]] (PR). The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on laws fundamental to the administration and the executive, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).

The municipality of João Monlevade is governed by an organic law<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.camarajm.mg.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=78&Itemid=88 |title=Organic Law |author=Communication Office |date=6 July 2011 |publisher=Municipal Chamber |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.camarajm.mg.gov.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=78&Itemid=88 |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> and serves as the seat of a [[Comarca|Judicial District]],<ref name="comarca">{{cite web |url=http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |title=List of Judicial Districts of the State of Minas Gerais |author=Minas Gerais State Court of Justice |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100825194853/http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |archive-date=25 August 2010 |url-status=dead }}</ref> with, according to the prefecture, a [[twin town|sister city]], [[Bela Vista de Minas]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=1915 |title=President of the João Monlevade Chamber Receives Councilors from Bela Vista de Minas |author=DeFato Online |date=13 February 2009 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=1915 |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> In 2010, the municipality had {{formatnum:56795}} voters, a 2.74% increase compared to 2008.<ref name="Eleitores">{{cite web |url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=29586 |title=Monlevade has 56,795 voters |author=Cidade Mais |date=20 July 2010 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=29586 |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
[[File:João Monlevade - Carneirinhos.jpg|thumb|right|Carneirinhos neighborhood, central region of João Monlevade.]]
The municipality has no subdivisions into [[district]]s other than the District Seat, and according to the IBGE, there have never been legislative proposals to formalize the creation of additional districts.<ref name="BibliotecaIBGE_História"/> However, the city is informally divided into three regions: the Industrial City, encompassing neighborhoods between the Baú Cemetery and the border with [[Bela Vista de Minas]], including the areas around Baú, Vila Tanque, and Centro Industrial; Carneirinhos, covering the lands from Getúlio Vargas and Wilson Alvarenga avenues to the borders with [[São Gonçalo do Rio Abaixo]] and the vicinity of [[BR-381]]; and Loanda/Cruzeiro Celeste, which includes neighborhoods along BR-381 and Armando Fajardo and Isaac Cassimiro avenues. At the time of emancipation, there was an unofficial division into two regions: Monlevade, comprising the central blocks of the city and the ArcelorMittal plant and its surroundings, and Carneirinhos, which included the rest of the urban perimeter.

Due to the higher population concentration, smaller official groupings exist today. According to the "Grande Brasil" website, João Monlevade comprises 64 [[neighborhood]]s, in addition to subdivisions and residential condominiums.<ref name="GrandeBrasil_JM">{{cite web |url=http://www.grandebrasil.com.br/Brasil/Cidades/0di4/Municipio_De_Joao_Monlevade_Estado_De_Minas_Gerais_mg |title=Municipality of João Monlevade, Minas Gerais (MG) |author=Grande Brasil |access-date=16 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110916000000/http://www.grandebrasil.com.br/Brasil/Cidades/0di4/Municipio_De_Joao_Monlevade_Estado_De_Minas_Gerais_mg |archive-date=16 September 2011}}</ref> In some neighborhoods, street names are themed after the locality, such as in the República neighborhood, where streets are named after [[List of presidents of Brazil|former Brazilian presidents]],<ref name="Geo_História"/> and in the Satélite neighborhood, where streets are named after Northeastern Brazilian capitals.

== Economy ==
The [[Gross Domestic Product]] (GDP) of João Monlevade is the second largest in its microregion, with a focus on the [[tertiary sector|service sector]]. According to 2008 data from the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], the municipality’s GDP was R$&nbsp;{{formatnum:1436358.435|000}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> Of this, {{formatnum:181233}} thousand were from taxes on products net of subsidies at current prices.<ref name="IBGE_PIB">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Gross Domestic Product of Municipalities |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2008 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> The per capita GDP was R$&nbsp;{{formatnum:19260.33}}.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/>

In 2009, there were {{formatnum:35021}} [[Workforce|workers]], of which {{formatnum:19272}} were employed, and {{formatnum:15749}} were salaried employees. [[Wages]] and other remunerations totaled {{formatnum:232025|000}} [[Brazilian real|reais]], with an average monthly salary of 2.6 [[minimum wage]]s across the municipality. There were {{formatnum:2728}} local units and {{formatnum:2654}} active companies.<ref name="IBGE_Empresas">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=empresas&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Statistics of the Central Business Register |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=empresas&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref>

Agriculture is the least significant sector for João Monlevade’s economy.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/> According to the IBGE, in 2009, the municipality had a herd of {{formatnum:1983}} [[cattle]], 70 [[Equidae|horses]], four donkeys, 20 [[mule]]s, 120 [[pig]]s, 40 goats, 20 [[sheep]], and {{formatnum:1350}} [[poultry]], including 500 [[Gallus gallus domesticus|hens]] and 850 roosters, chicks, and pullets.<ref name="Agropecuária"/> In 2009, the city produced 281,000 liters of milk from 240 [[cattle|cows]], 4,000 dozen eggs, and 750,000 kilograms of [[honey]].<ref name="Agropecuária">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2009&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Livestock 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2009&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> In terms of temporary agriculture, the main crops are [[sugarcane]] (480 tons), [[cassava]] (36 tons), and [[corn]] (36 tons).<ref name="IBGE_Lavoura">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2009&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Temporary Agriculture 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2009&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref>
[[File:João Monlevade - State of Minas Gerais, Brazil - panoramio (1).jpg|thumb|View of some buildings of Belgo, a company founded in the 1930s that was vital to Monlevade’s growth.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/>]]

In the industry sector, the primary source of income is ArcelorMittal Aços Longos, part of the world’s largest steelmaking group. This sector was crucial to the emergence and development of the municipality and its surroundings.<ref name="Prefeitura_História"/> João Monlevade also has around {{formatnum:2500}} small industries and service providers, contributing to the municipality’s growth, particularly in forging, service provision, locksmithing, machining, boilermaking, and civil construction.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.gthidro.ufsc.br/2011/08 |title=Governance of Common Goods and the International Year of Forests at the I Regional Environment Forum – João Monlevade/MG |author=Gabriel Coelho |date=1 August 2011 |publisher=Transdisciplinary Research Group on Water and Territory Governance (GTHIDRO) |access-date=13 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110913000000/http://www.gthidro.ufsc.br/2011/08 |archive-date=13 September 2011}}</ref> In 2000, {{formatnum:7254}} people were employed in the industrial sector.<ref name="cidadesnet.com"/>

The [[commerce]] of João Monlevade is concentrated in the Carneirinhos neighborhood, away from the Belgo plant, although smaller commercial hubs exist in neighborhoods such as Loanda and Cruzeiro Celeste. Most of the city’s commerce revolves around clothing and footwear, but there is also significant activity in services, [[electronics]], [[automobiles]], and [[supermarkets]]. As a regional hub due to its diverse commerce, João Monlevade is home to major national retail chains such as Ricardo Eletro, [[Magazine Luiza]], and Ponto Frio.<ref name="Geo_História"/>

== Infrastructure ==
[[File:João Monlevade 2 - Carneirinhos.jpg|left|thumb|Partial view of the central region of João Monlevade]]
In 2000, the municipality had {{formatnum:17357}} [[housing units]], including [[apartments]], [[houses]], and rooms. Of these, {{formatnum:13341}} were owned properties, with {{formatnum:12619}} fully paid (72.70%), 722 under acquisition (4.16%), and {{formatnum:2752}} [[rented]] (15.86%); {{formatnum:1142}} properties were provided, with 94 by employers (0.54%) and {{formatnum:1048}} provided otherwise (6.04%). Another 122 were occupied in other ways (0.70%).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |title=Infrastructure - Permanent Private Households and Residents |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CNM) |date=2000 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> The municipality has access to treated water, [[electricity]], [[sewage]], [[solid waste|urban cleaning]], [[fixed-line telephone|landline telephony]], and [[mobile telephony]]. In 2000, 96.83% of households were served by the [[Water supply network|general water supply network]];<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_abastecimento.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |title=Infrastructure - Water Supply |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CNM) |date=2000 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_abastecimento.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> 92.88% had garbage collected by the cleaning service;<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_lixo.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |title=Infrastructure - Waste Disposal |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CNM) |date=2000 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_lixo.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> and 92.82% had access to the general sewage or stormwater network.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_esgotamento.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |title=Infrastructure - Sewage System |author=National Confederation of Municipalities (CNM) |date=2000 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_esgotamento.asp?iIdMun=100131411 |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref>

=== Healthcare ===
In 2009, the municipality had 54 healthcare facilities, including hospitals, [[emergency department|emergency rooms]], [[health center]]s, and [[dentistry|dental services]], with 17 public and 37 private facilities, all public ones belonging to the municipal network. These facilities provided 102 beds for [[hospitalization]], all in private institutions.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Health Services 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> In 2010, {{formatnum:1010}} live births were recorded,<ref name="IBGE_Síntese">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=sintese&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Summary of Information |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=sintese&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> and 123 deaths were recorded, with 72 males and 51 females.<ref name="IBGE_Morbidades">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=morbid&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Hospital Morbidities 2010 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2010 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=morbid&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref>

The main hospitals in the municipality are [[Unimed (organization)|Unimed]] João Monlevade<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.unimed.com.br/pct/index.jsp?cd_canal=52290&cd_secao=52276 |title=Our Mission |author=Unimed Monlevade Hospital |date=12 September 2006 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.unimed.com.br/pct/index.jsp?cd_canal=52290&cd_secao=52276 |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> and Margarida Hospital.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.viacomercial.com.br/2011/06/17/hospital-margarida-de-joao-monlevade-inaugura-4%C2%BA-andar-do-novo-predio/ |title=Hospital Margarida in João Monlevade opens 12 new beds |author=Via Comercial |date=17 June 2011 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.viacomercial.com.br/2011/06/17/hospital-margarida-de-joao-monlevade-inaugura-4%C2%BA-andar-do-novo-predio/ |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref> The latter stands out as one of the city’s first hospitals, inaugurated by ''Companhia Siderúrgica Belgo-Mineira'' on 16 November 1952 to meet the growing demand driven by the city’s development. Prior to this, medical care was provided in a temporary wooden clinic within the plant. Located in the Vila Tanque neighborhood, it is also one of Monlevade’s main structures.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.fundacaoarcelormittalbr.org.br/arquivos/boletim_111.pdf |title=Hospital Margarida - 55 years |author=ArcelorMittal Inox Brazil Foundation |date=16 November 2007 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110914000000/http://www.fundacaoarcelormittalbr.org.br/arquivos/boletim_111.pdf |archive-date=14 September 2011}}</ref>

== Public safety and crime ==
As in most medium and large Brazilian municipalities, crime remains a challenge in João Monlevade. In 2008, the [[homicide]] rate in the municipality was 24.1 per 100,000 inhabitants, ranking 43rd in the state and 699th nationally.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/HTN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de homicídio nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=14 September 2011 |format=xls |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/HTN.xls}}</ref> The [[suicide]] rate in that year was 5.4 per 100,000 inhabitants, placing it 161st in the state and 1106th nationally.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/STN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de suicídio nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=14 September 2011 |format=xls |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/STN.xls}}</ref> Regarding deaths from traffic accidents, the rate was 17.4 per 100,000 inhabitants, ranking 149th in the state and 1324th nationally.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/TTN.xls |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de Óbitos Ac.Transporte nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |author=Sangari |date=2011 |access-date=14 September 2011 |format=xls |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.sangari.com/mapadaviolencia/tabelas2011/TTN.xls}}</ref>

However, these rates are relatively low compared to other Brazilian cities and to previous years. The 17th Independent Company of the [[Minas Gerais Military Police]] (PMMG), headquartered in the municipality, is responsible for policing and combating crime in João Monlevade and Bela Vista de Minas. According to the battalion, there was a 14.66% decrease in the crime rate from 2009 to 2010.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=29468 |title=Monlevade registra redução de 14,66% no índice de criminalidade |author=Cidade Mais |date=9 July 2010 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=29468}}</ref> According to the PM, the most violent areas in Monlevade are the Novo Cruzeiro and Nova Monlevade neighborhoods.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jopopular.blogspot.com/2010/09/major-afirma-na-cdl-que-criminalidade.html |title=Major afirma na CDL que criminalidade caiu em Monlevade |author=O Popular |date=27 September 2010 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://jopopular.blogspot.com/2010/09/major-afirma-na-cdl-que-criminalidade.html}}</ref>

== Services and communications ==
The water supply service is managed by the Department of Water and Sewage (DAE), which is also responsible for sewage collection.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6556 |title=Engenheiro químico assume gestão do Departamento de Águas e Esgotos |author=Assessoria de Comunicação da Prefeitura Municipal de João Monlevade (AcomPMJM) |date=1 September 2011 |publisher=Prefeitura |access-date=17 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6556}}</ref> In the municipality, as in most of Minas Gerais, electricity supply is provided by the [[CEMIG|Minas Gerais Energy Company]] (Cemig). In 2003, there were {{formatnum:23224}} consumers, and {{formatnum:358274340}} [[Watt-hour|kWh]] of energy was consumed.<ref name="cidadesnet.com" />

Dial-up and [[broadband]] ([[Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line|ADSL]]) internet services are offered by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|providers]]. Mobile phone services are provided by several operators. The area code ([[Direct distance dialing|DDD]]) for Monlevade is 031.<ref name="Guiatel_DDD">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_mg.htm |title=Minas Gerais |author=Guiatel |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=2011-08-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110807064854/http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_mg.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> The [[Postal code|Postal Code]] ([[Código de Endereçamento Postal|CEP]]) for the city ranges from 35930-000 to 35931-999.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correios.com.br/ |title=CEP de cidades brasileiras |author=[[Correios]] |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.correios.com.br/}}</ref> On 19 January 2009, the city began offering <span title="Portability">[[Mobile number portability|number portability]]</span>, along with other cities with the 031 area code. The municipality also has several newspapers in circulation. In 2000, there were three in total. Currently, the regularly circulated newspapers include ''A Notícia'' (since 1984), ''Bom Dia'' (since 1998), ''O Popular'' (since 2003), ''O Celeste'', ''Alô Cidadão'', ''Diário do Vale'', and ''Última Notícia'' (since 2013). In 2001, there were two radio stations, according to the Minas Gerais Radio and TV Association and Minas Gerais Telecommunications.<ref name="cidadesnet.com" /> However, this number has increased over the years. == Transportation ==
The municipal vehicle fleet in 2010 consisted of {{formatnum:28023}} vehicles, including {{formatnum:18019}} cars, {{formatnum:1038}} trucks, 226 tractor-trucks, {{formatnum:2744}} pickups, 544 scooters, 93 minibuses, {{formatnum:4317}} motorcycles, 197 mopeds, 216 buses, three wheeled tractors, 61 utility vehicles, and 565 other types.<ref name="IBGE_Frota">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=frota&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade |title=Frota 2009 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2009 |access-date=1 May 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=frota&codmun=313620&nomemun=Jo%E3o%20Monlevade}}</ref> The city's duplicated and paved avenues and numerous [[traffic light]]s facilitate [[traffic]] flow, but the increase in the number of vehicles over the past decade has led to increasingly slow traffic, particularly in the city center. Additionally, finding parking spaces in the commercial center has become challenging, causing some losses to local businesses.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=24414 |title=Dentista apresenta projeto para trânsito de João Monlevade |author=Cidade Mais |date=16 May 2009 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=24414}}</ref>

The João Monlevade Traffic and Transit Department is responsible for regulating and overseeing the public transportation system, managing traffic, and, through its Traffic Agents, issuing fines to drivers who commit traffic violations.<ref name="DeFato_Reunião">{{cite web|url=http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=15862 |title=Câmara se reúne com Settran para discutir problemas do trânsito em Monlevade |author=Flávia Henriques |date=19 February 2011 |publisher=DeFato Online |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=15862}}</ref> Public transportation is operated by Escon Viação.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=9244 |title=Vale-transporte: 20 anos acompanhando a evolução |author=Cidade Mais |date=27 August 2005 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=9244}}</ref> A project is currently under consideration to create a dedicated bus lane, known as the "Blue Line," on the city's main avenues.<ref name="DeFato_Reunião"/>

João Monlevade is served by the [[Vitória-Minas Railway]] (EFVM), which provides daily passenger rail transport and logistics services for mining operations in neighboring cities. The city's station was inaugurated on 31 August 1935, and the EFVM remains the cheapest and safest way to travel to [[Belo Horizonte]], [[Vitória, Espírito Santo|Vitória]], or other cities with stations or stops.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efcb_mg_ramais/monlevade.htm |title=Monlevade |date=18 December 2010 |access-date=14 September 2011 |author=Estações Ferroviárias do Brasil |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/efcb_mg_ramais/monlevade.htm}}</ref> By road, the municipality has easy access to [[BR-381]], connecting to cities such as [[São Paulo]], Belo Horizonte, and [[Governador Valadares, Minas Gerais|Governador Valadares]]; to [[BR-262]], linking to [[Corumbá]], [[Campo Grande]], [[Uberlândia]], Belo Horizonte, and Vitória; and to MG-129, serving nearby municipalities such as [[Itabira]], [[Rio Piracicaba]], [[Mariana, Minas Gerais|Mariana]], [[Ouro Preto]], and [[Conselheiro Lafaiete]].<ref name="cidadesnet.com" /><ref>[http://maps.google.com.br/ Google Maps] (accessed 14 September 2011)</ref>

The municipality also has a [[bus terminal]], one of the largest and busiest in the region. The João Monlevade Bus Terminal, located on the outskirts of BR-381 in the Santo Hipólito neighborhood, is managed by [[Rede Graal]]. It was inaugurated in February 2004 to replace the old Tancredo Neves Bus Terminal, which closed on 20 February of that year.<ref name="Cidade+_Inauguração">{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=4742 |title=Rodoviária é inaugurada |author=Cidade Mais |date=17 February 2004 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=4742}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=4565 |title=Rodoviária será inaugurada no final do mês |author=Cidade Mais |date=22 January 2004 |access-date=14 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=4565}}</ref>

== Culture ==
The cultural sector in João Monlevade is managed by the Department of Sports and Leisure and the House of Culture Foundation, which aim to plan and implement the municipality's cultural policy through programs, projects, and activities that promote cultural development. These entities are linked to the Mayor's Office, form part of the municipality's indirect public administration, and have administrative and financial autonomy, supported by budget allocations, their own assets, revenue application, and contracts or agreements with other institutions.<ref name="Prefeitura_Secretarias">{{cite web|url=http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6504 |title=Governo - Secretários |author=Assessoria de Comunicação da Prefeitura Municipal de João Monlevade (AcomPMJM) |date=14 June 2011 |publisher=Prefeitura |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.pmjm.mg.gov.br/mat_vis.aspx?cd=6504}}</ref>
[[File:Fest Inverno UFOP Mon.png|left|thumb|220x220px|Logo of the UFOP Winter Festival, which also takes place in Monlevade <ref>[https://ufop.br/noticias/festival-de-inverno reference]</ref>]]
[[File:Anfiteatro do Centro Educacional de João Monlevade.jpg|thumb|Amphitheater of the João Monlevade Educational Center <ref>{{cite web|last1=Bretas|first1=Gustavo|last2=Bretas|first2=Gustavo|url=https://defatoonline.com.br/teatro-de-monlevade-recebe-melhorias/|title=Teatro de Monlevade recebe melhorias|date=2017-11-27|access-date=2022-03-18|website=DeFato Online|language=pt-BR |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/https://defatoonline.com.br/teatro-de-monlevade-recebe-melhorias/}}</ref>]]
Throughout the year, various performances take place at the CEJM amphitheater and in public spaces. There are two active theater companies: Cia. Teatral O Salto<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ciateatralosalto.blogspot.com.br/p/quem-somos.html |archive-url=http://www.webcitation.org/69lyNMEV4 |archive-date=8 August 2012 |title=Quem somos |author=Cia. Teatral O Salto |access-date=8 August 2012 }}</ref> and Cia. do Infinito de Teatro.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.teatrociadoinfinito.blogspot.com.br/2012/07/ocupacao-do-espaco-publico-continua.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140714175207/http://www.teatrociadoinfinito.blogspot.com.br/2012/07/ocupacao-do-espaco-publico-continua.html |archive-date=14 July 2014 |title=A Ocupação do espaço público continua... |author=Cia. do Infinito de Teatro |date=3 July 2012 |access-date=8 August 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> These groups frequently perform shows in the city and have represented Monlevade in other cities across the state.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.teatrociadoinfinito.blogspot.com.br/p/ta-na-midia.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140714181348/http://www.teatrociadoinfinito.blogspot.com.br/p/ta-na-midia.html |archive-date=14 July 2014 |title=Saiu na Mídia |author=Cia. do Infinito de Teatro |date=7 February 2012 |access-date=8 August 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Since October 2011, Cia. Since its inception, the project has visited 10 different squares, attracting thousands of spectators.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=35014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731131912/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=35014 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Domingo na Praça com "O Salto" |author=Cidade Mais |date=24 March 2012 |access-date=8 August 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Alongside this growth in the local scene, the need for a cultural association emerged. In August 2011, the Acordar Cultural Association of Médio Piracicaba was established, encompassing various artistic disciplines, including theater, music, lyrical arts, and visual arts.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ultimanoticia.com.br/ultimanoticia/Portugues/detNoticia.php?cod=12429 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731133611/http://www.ultimanoticia.com.br/ultimanoticia/Portugues/detNoticia.php?cod=12429 |archive-date=31 July 2013 |title=Sistema Nacional de Cultura em discussão |author=Última Notícia |date=13 June 2012 |access-date=8 August 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 2011, the city hosted the 1st Performing Arts Festival, featuring local, regional, and national artists from Minas Gerais and other Brazilian states.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.festivaldeartescenicas.com.br/?page_id=16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121029083520/http://www.festivaldeartescenicas.com.br/?page_id=16 |archive-date=29 October 2012 |title=O Festival |author=Festival de Artes Cênicas |date=8 June 2012 |access-date=8 August 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://coisasdeteatro.blogspot.com/2011/03/festival-de-artes-cenicas-de-joao.html |title=Festival de Artes Cênicas de João Monlevade |author=Ruy Jobim |date=1 March 2011 |publisher=Coisas de Teatro |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://coisasdeteatro.blogspot.com/2011/03/festival-de-artes-cenicas-de-joao.html}}</ref>

The ArcelorMittal Cultural Program, promoted by ArcelorMittal João Monlevade, also organizes theatrical performances for the public.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jaomonlevade.blogspot.com/2010/07/o-programa-arcelormittal-cultural.html |title=O Programa ArcelorMittal Cultural |author=Jão Monlevade Online |date=30 July 2010 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://jaomonlevade.blogspot.com/2010/07/o-programa-arcelormittal-cultural.html}}</ref>

[[Handicraft]]s are another form of spontaneous cultural expression in Monlevade. Throughout the municipality, unique artisanal products are made using regional [[raw material]]s, reflecting local culture and lifestyles. Groups such as the João Monlevade Artisans Association (Artejom) bring together various artisans, providing spaces for the creation, exhibition, and sale of handmade products.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jornalnabocadopovo.blogspot.com/2009/06/associacao-dos-artesaos-toma-posse.html |title=Associação dos Artesãos toma posse |author=Jornal Na Boca do Povo |date=19 June 2009 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://jornalnabocadopovo.blogspot.com/2009/06/associacao-dos-artesaos-toma-posse.html}}</ref> These items are typically sold at fairs, exhibitions, or craft shops. The craft fair at Praça do Povo and Praça 7, held since 2003, is a notable event in the city.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3763 |title=Feira de artesanato na Praça do Povo é aprovada |author=Cidade Mais |date=16 September 2003 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3763}}</ref>

== Tourism and events ==
[[File:Cemitério Histórico de João Monlevade.jpg|thumb|290x290px|Facade of the Historical Cemetery]]
João Monlevade boasts several tourist attractions, including the Serra do Seara, the highest point in the municipality, which offers a runway for [[hang gliding]] and serves as an important ecological and environmental reserve.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=50 |title=Serra do Seara: a invasão dos garimpeiros |author=Cidade Mais |date=31 July 2001 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=50}}</ref>

The Henry Meyers Forest Club hosts dances, parties, environmental visits by organizations, and sports activities such as rappelling and ecological tours, within a 100,000 m² green area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3979 |title=Descontração e aventura junto à natureza |author=Cidade Mais |date=21 October 2003 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3979}}</ref> The Areão Municipal Park, the city’s exhibition park, has accommodated over 25,000 people and includes a small environmental reserve known for its trails.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=17859 |title=Parque do Areão em Monlevade vai abrigar Memorial do Aço |author=Flávia Henriques |date=6 June 2011 |publisher=DeFato Online |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.defatoonline.com.br/noticias/ultimas/?IdNoticia=17859}}</ref>

The Catalan Forge, built by Jean-Antoine Félix Dissandes de Monlevade in the 1910s to settle in the region,<ref name="Cidade+_68">{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3652 |title=Belgo: 68 anos de história e trabalho |author=Cidade Mais |date=29 August 2003 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3652}}</ref> and the Historical Cemetery, constructed in the 19th century for the burial of slaves who worked at the Catalan Forge, are also notable.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=13603 |title=Monlevadenses visitam Cemitério Histórico |author=Cidade Mais |date=3 November 2006 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=13603}}</ref> The São José do Operário Parish Church, founded on 25 September 1948, honors the workers of the Belgo-Mineira Steel Company (now ArcelorMittal). It is the only church in the world built in a "V" shape, resembling a [[chalice]], to celebrate the Allied victory in [[World War II]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/3304/joao-monlevade-mg.html |title=João Monlevade |author=Ferias Tur |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.ferias.tur.br/informacoes/3304/joao-monlevade-mg.html}}</ref> In front of the church, there is a statue of a bishop with hands forming a "V." The external staircase leading to the church is also chalice-shaped. Additionally, a grotto dedicated to [[Our Lady of Lourdes]] is part of the surroundings. The Fazenda Solar Monlevade, owned by Jean Antoine Félix Dissandes de Monlevade, was built in the early 19th century by slaves. This imposing structure dominated the Piracicaba Valley landscape and remains a historical landmark and symbol of the civilization established by the French pioneer.<ref>{{cite web|title=Fazenda Solar|url=http://guidez.com.br/fazenda-solar-v171|website=Guidez|access-date=2015-08-04 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://guidez.com.br/fazenda-solar-v171}}</ref>

|archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=24083}}</ref> Labor Day in May;<ref>{{cite == Sports ==
[[File:Crianças em campinho de João Monlevade MG.jpg|thumb|right|Football is the most popular sport in Brazil.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://mundoestranho.abril.com.br/materia/qual-e-o-esporte-mais-praticado-no-brasil |title=Qual é o esporte mais praticado no Brasil |author=Luiz Fujita |publisher=Mundo Estranho |access-date=30 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://mundoestranho.abril.com.br/materia/qual-e-o-esporte-mais-praticado-no-brasil}}</ref> The image shows children playing football in a local field. The first clubs in Monlevade emerged in the 1940s, and categories for children were established in the 1970s.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/6/Futebol-de-Monlevade.html |title=Futebol de Monlevade |author=Projeto Cultural Caminho de Riquezas |access-date=30 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.morrodogeo.com.br/capitulo/6/Futebol-de-Monlevade.html}}</ref>]]
As in much of Brazil, [[Association football|football]] is the most popular sport in João Monlevade. The city is home to several clubs, such as Real Esporte Clube,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=7246 |title=1ª etapa da sede social do Real Esporte Clube já está concluída |author=Cidade Mais |date=11 January 2005 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=7246}}</ref> founded on 7 September 1957, initially as a football team. In 1977, it became a social and recreational club, and in 1983, the Domingos Silvério Sobrinho Sports Center was established, featuring a football field, [[badminton]] courts, [[swimming pool]]s, [[gym]] rooms, and a [[physiotherapy]] clinic.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.clubedosoficiais.org.br/site/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=153:real-esporte-clube&catid=42:clubes&Itemid=78 |title=Real Esporte Clube - João Monlevade/MG |author=Clube dos Oficiais |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.clubedosoficiais.org.br/site/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=153:real-esporte-clube&catid=42:clubes&Itemid=78}}</ref> Other well-known sports facilities include the Embaúbas Tennis Club<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=1278 |title=Belgo promove 9ª Colônia de Férias |author=Cidade Mais |date=3 July 2002 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=1278}}</ref> and the Arena Esportiva.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=16536 |title=Homenagens marcam inauguração da Arena Esportiva |author=Cidade Mais |date=10 July 2007 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=16536}}</ref>

The Monlevadense Football League (LMF) is the institution responsible for professionalizing and regulating football championships in the city.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3883 |title=Funcec sedia posse da Liga Monlevadense de Futebol |author=Cidade Mais |date=3 October 2003 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=3883}}</ref> Among these, the Monlevadense Football Championship stands out, held annually and involving the city’s main teams.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=16895 |title=Vila Celeste e Laranjeiras lideram campeonato da LMF |author=Cidade Mais |date=7 August 2007 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.cidademais.com.br/noticias/?id=16895}}</ref> The Louis Ensch Municipal Stadium is the main venue for sports activities in the city, with a capacity of approximately {{formatnum:1900}} spectators.

== Holidays ==
João Monlevade has three municipal [[holiday]]s and eight national holidays, in addition to optional holidays. The municipal holidays are: the city’s emancipation anniversary on 29 April; [[Feast of Corpus Christi|Corpus Christi]], held on the Thursday following the Sunday of the [[Trinity (Christianity)|Holy Trinity]]; and the day of [[Immaculate Conception|Our Lady of the Conception]] on 8 December.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=null&mes=null&ano=2010&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar |title=Calendário do Judiciário |author=Tribunal de Justiça de Minas Gerais (TJMG) |date=2011 |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=6 August 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250806032300/http://www.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=null&mes=null&ano=2010&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar}}</ref> According to federal law No. 9,093, enacted on 12 September 1995, municipalities may have up to four municipal holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado? folga automática pode gerar alteração contratual |author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |publisher=Guia Trabalhista |language=Portuguese |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=2012-06-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120626150006/http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaLei">{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |title=Lei n.º 9.093, de 12 de setembro de 1995 |author=Presidência da República |access-date=15 September 2011 |archive-date=2012-08-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120829143920/http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref>
