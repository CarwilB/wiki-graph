'''Porto Real''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈpoʁtu ʁeˈaw}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 19,974 (2020) and its area is 50.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<Ref name=c />

It is the sole location where the [[coastal tapeti]], a species of [[cottontail rabbit]], is known to exist.<ref name="iucn status">{{cite iucn|title=''Sylvilagus tapetillus''|article-number=e.T142542759A165117046|author=Ruedas, L.A.|date=2019|volume=2019|access-date=19 November 2021|doi=10.2305/IUCN.UK.2019-3.RLTS.T142542759A165117046.en|author2=Smith, A.T.}}</ref>

== History ==
The land around Porto Real was first given to Captain-Major Garcia Rodrigues Pais Leme by King [[João V of Portugal]] in 1715. It was not until 1801 that Pais' descendant built a settlement on the land and named it Resende after the [[Count of Resende]]. In 1722, due to an incentive from the [[Marquess of Lavradio]] to exempt anyone who had planted a number [[Coffea|coffee trees]] from military service, the Pais Leme family did so and started a coffee industry there. By 1822, a pier was built into the [[Paraíba do Sul River|Paraíba River]] which started to be used regularly by the [[Emperor of Brazil]], [[Pedro II of Brazil]]. This led to the town being renamed as Porto Real.<ref name=c>{{cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/porto-real/historico |title=Porto Real |language=pt |publisher=IBGE |accessdate=2025-07-07}}</ref>  From there, the [[Province of Rio de Janeiro]] started establishing sugar mills in the town as the coffee industry had started to be less profitable, with the Imperial Brazilian government elevated the town to a Colony, with the aim of bringing in Italian migrants to help work in the sugar industry.<Ref name=c />

By 1892, it had been relegated back to a district within [[Resende, Rio de Janeiro|Resende municipality]]. In 1995, it was severed from Resende and given its own municipal status.<Ref name=c /> In 2011, a [[Citroën]] automobile construction factory had been established within Porto Real with them announcing in 2025 that they would be constructing the [[Citroën Basalt]] car at the factory in Porto Real.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.rj.gov.br/desenvolvimentoeconomico/node/534|title=Stellantis inicia produção de novo SUV, em Porto Real, no Rio de Janeiro &#124; Desenvolvimento Econômico|website=Rio Governement |language=pt|accessdate=7 July 2025}}</ref> In 2021, a new bridge over the river was constructed at Porto Real.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.7segundos.com.br/arapiraca/noticias/2021/12/20/194465-ponte-sobre-rio-itiuba-e-inaugurada-durante-governo-presente-em-porto-real-do-colegio|title=Ponte sobre rio Itiúba é inaugurada durante Governo Presente em Porto Real do Colégio|language=pt|website=7 Segundos|accessdate=7 July 2025}}</ref> In 2024, a large drug smuggling ring was discovered in Porto Real with the [[Civil Police (Brazil)|Brazilian Civil Police]] arresting people after the drugs were discovered being sent via the Brazilian postal service.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://avozdacidade.com/wp/policia-civil-de-porto-real-apreende-encomendas-com-mais-de-30-quilos-de-drogas-enviadas-pelos-correios/|title=Polícia Civil de Porto Real apreende 'encomendas' com mais de 30 quilos de drogas enviadas pelos Correios |publisher=Avozdaciadade|language=pt|first=Por Cyntia|last=Freitas|date=12 November 2024|accessdate=7 July 2025}}</ref>
