The town of '''Villa Madero''' is a municipal seat in the [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] of '''Madero''' in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It was established in 1868 with the name of Cruz de Caminos.<ref name="auto">{{Cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16049a.html|title=Michoacán de Ocampo - Madero|access-date=2022-01-30|archive-date=2021-08-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210823172918/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16049a.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> According to tradition the first inhabitant of the place was a man named Armas, he is now considered the town's founder. The land where the town currently sits belonged to La Hacienda De La Concepcion. The village is the point of intersection of the paths that lead to [[Acuitzio]], Etucuaro, Curuchancio and [[Tacámbaro|Tacambaro]], this is why it became known as Cruz De Caminos (Cross Roads).<ref>{{cite web| url=https://madero.gob.mx/ | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130827014433/https://madero.gob.mx/ | archive-date=2013-08-27 | title=MUNICIPIO DE MADERO | language=es}}</ref>

Cruz de Caminos, was in 1901 part of [[Acuitzio del Canje, Michoacán|Acuitzio Del Canje]]. On July 27, 1914, Cruz de Caminos applied to become a municipality. On October 12, 1914, the agreement to elevate it to a municipality with the name of Madero was ratified.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_4.pdf | title=División Territorial de Michoacán de Ocampo | access-date=2023-12-25 | language=es | archive-date=2024-11-10 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241110134634/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_4.pdf | url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="auto"/> It was named after [[Francisco I. Madero]].<ref name="auto"/>

Villa Madero hosts an annual festival named "La Feria del Mezcal".<ref>{{cite web | url=https://revolucion.news/anuncian-la-feria-del-mezcal-artesanal-en-villa-madero/ | title=Anuncian la Feria del Mezcal Artesanal en Villa Madero | date=12 November 2015 }}</ref> This is about a traditional beverage "[[mezcal]]" produced in Etucuaro, a town that is part of the Madero municipality. During this festival, producers give free samples of their product. There are also traditional food stands, different sports activities, a parade and at night people gather in a ballroom to dance with live music.<ref>Villa Madero: Historia De Un Pueblo De La Sierra Michoacana by Arturo Villaseñor Gómez</ref>

Villa Madero used to be surrounded by pine forests, but as of 2022 is battling [[illegal logging]], [[deforestation]], encroachment by [[Avocado production in Mexico|avocado growers]], and depleted water tables. An attempt to institute citizen patrols such as was successful in [[Cherán]] was foiled by the [[Mexican drug cartel]]. Local farm families are having to haul in water because the avocado growers have diverted streams to water their orchards.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://apnews.com/article/business-world-news-mountains-mexico-caribbean-32a4508534bffb00cf341119de733d5d|title = Mexican town protects forest from avocado growers, cartels|website = [[Associated Press]]|date = 27 January 2022}}</ref>
