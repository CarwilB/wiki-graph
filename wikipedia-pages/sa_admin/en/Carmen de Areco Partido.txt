'''Carmen de Areco''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 14,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1080|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Carmen de Areco]], which is {{convert|140|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
