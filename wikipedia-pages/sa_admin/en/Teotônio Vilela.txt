'''Teotônio Vilela''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[tɛɔˈtõnjw viˈlɛlɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western of the [[Federative units of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 44,372 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/teotonio-vilela/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|298|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

In 2021 [[Bolsonaro]] visited it on his 1000-day-in-office celebration.{{cn|date=October 2023}}
