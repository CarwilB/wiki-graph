'''Pajonal''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Penonomé District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 13,565 as of 2010. Its population as of 1990 was 10,232; its population as of 2000 was 12,097.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=|title=Superficie, población y densidad de población en la República|url=http://www.contraloria.gob.pa/inec/archivos/P3601Cuadro11.xls|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=|website=}}</ref>
