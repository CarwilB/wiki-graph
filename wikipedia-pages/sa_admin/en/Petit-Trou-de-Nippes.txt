'''Petit-Trou-de-Nippes''' ({{IPA|fr|pəti tʁu də nip}}; {{langx|ht|Ti Twou de Nip}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Anse-à-Veau Arrondissement]], in the [[Nippes]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 29,990 inhabitants (2015).

On 14 August 2021 it was near the epicenter of [[2021 Haiti earthquake|a magnitude 7.2 earthquake]] that has killed at least 2,207 people and injured 12,268 more. The earthquake caused irreparable damage to the only health center in Petit Trou de Nippes. In October 2023, a U.S.-based nonprofit named [https://www.locallyhaiti.org/hospital Locally Haiti] began building a new Community Hospital and Health Center in partnership with local leaders. 

In 1963, the commune was devastated by [[Hurricane Flora]], destroying 85% of the village.

The town's name means "Little hole of Nippes".<ref>From Google translate from both Haitian creole and French to English.</ref>
