'''Las Piedras''' ({{IPA|es|las ˈpjeðɾas}}), is a [[Las Piedras barrio-pueblo|town]] and [[Municipalities of Puerto Rico|municipality]] in east [[Puerto Rico]] located in the central eastern region of the island, north of [[Yabucoa, Puerto Rico|Yabucoa]]; south of [[Canóvanas, Puerto Rico|Canóvanas]] and [[Río Grande, Puerto Rico|Río Grande]]; east of [[Juncos, Puerto Rico|Juncos]] and [[San Lorenzo, Puerto Rico|San Lorenzo]]; and west of [[Naguabo, Puerto Rico|Naguabo]] and [[Humacao, Puerto Rico|Humacao]]. Las Piedras has 8 barrios including [[Las Piedras barrio-pueblo|Las Piedras Pueblo]] (the downtown area and the administrative center of the city). It is part of the [[San Juan-Caguas-Guaynabo Metropolitan Statistical Area]].

Las Piedras has many natural attractions such as ''[[Cueva del Indio (Las Piedras)|Cueva del Indio]]'' which contains original [[Taíno|Taino]] caves and paintings that denote some characteristics of the island's natives. Las Piedras is located about 45 minutes from [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], Puerto Rico's capital and 5 minutes from [[Palmas del Mar]], [[Humacao]] which is one of the biggest resorts in the Caribbean.

[[El Toro (Sierra de Luquillo)|El Toro]], the highest point in the [[Sierra de Luquillo]], is located in the boundary between the municipalities of Las Piedras and [[Río Grande, Puerto Rico|Río Grande]].

==History==
Las Piedras was founded in 1793.

Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became a territory of the [[United States]]. In 1899, the [[United States Department of War]] conducted a [[census]] of Puerto Rico finding that the population of Las Piedras was 8,602.<ref name="OfficeSanger1900">{{cite book|author1=Joseph Prentiss Sanger|author2=Henry Gannett|author3=Walter Francis Willcox|title=Informe sobre el censo de Puerto Rico, 1899, United States. War Dept. Porto Rico Census Office|url=https://archive.org/details/informesobreelc00joangoog|year=1900|publisher=Imprenta del gobierno|page=[https://archive.org/details/informesobreelc00joangoog/page/n254 163]|language=es|access-date=2020-03-13|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121115044918/http://archive.org/details/informesobreelc00joangoog|archive-date=2012-11-15|url-status=live}}</ref>

On September 20, 2017 [[Hurricane Maria]] struck Puerto Rico. In Las Piedras, 500 residences lost their roof.<ref>{{cite news | title=María, un nombre que no vamos a olvidar. == Geography ==
Las Piedras is on the eastern side of Puerto Rico but not on the coast. According to the 2010 [[U.S. Census Bureau]], the municipality has a total area of {{convert|33.89 |sqmi}}, of which {{convert|33.88|sqmi}} is land and {{convert|.01|sqmi}} is water.<ref name="PR_Ency">{{cite web|url=https://enciclopediapr.org/en/encyclopedia/las-piedras-municipality/|title=Las Piedras Municipality|publisher=Fundación Puertorriqueña de las Humanidades (FPH)|website=enciclopediapr.org|access-date=2019-03-20|archive-date=2020-08-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200823012551/https://enciclopediapr.org/en/encyclopedia/las-piedras-municipality/|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Culture==
===Festivals and events===
Las Piedras celebrates its [[Fiestas patronales in Puerto Rico|patron saint]] festival in December. The {{lang|es|Fiestas Patronales Nuestra Señora de Asunción}} is a religious and cultural celebration that generally features parades, games, artisans, amusement rides, regional food, and live entertainment.<ref name="PR_Ency" />

Other festivals and events celebrated in Las Piedras include:
*''[[Güiro]]'' Festival - March
*Folk Culture Festival – April
*Cross Festival – May
*Youth Festival – July
*Folk Festival – September
*{{lang|es|Festival Artesanal}} - October<ref>{{cite web | title=Puerto Rico Festivales, Eventos y Actividades en Puerto Rico | website=Puerto Rico Hoteles y Paradores | url=https://www.puertoricohotelesparadores.com/festivales-eventos-actividades | language=es | access-date=2020-07-17 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200226163727/https://www.puertoricohotelesparadores.com/festivales-eventos-actividades | archive-date=2020-02-26 | url-status=live }}</ref>

==Government==
{{Main|Mayoralty in Puerto Rico}}
All municipalities in Puerto Rico are administered by a mayor, elected every four years. The current mayor of Las Piedras is [[Miguel López Rivera]], of the [[New Progressive Party of Puerto Rico|New Progressive Party]] (PNP). He was first elected at the [[Puerto Rican general election, 2008|2008 general elections]].

The city belongs to the [[Puerto Rico Senatorial district VII]], which is represented by two Senators. In 2024, [[Wanda Soto Tolentino]] and [[Luis Daniel Colón La Santa]] were elected as District Senators.<ref>[https://elecciones2024.ceepur.org/Escrutinio_General_121/index.html#es/default/SENADORES_POR_DISTRITO_Humacao_VII.xmll Elecciones Generales 2024: Escrutinio General] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241115225812/https://elecciones2024.ceepur.org/Escrutinio_General_121/index.html#es/default/SENADORES_POR_DISTRITO_Humacao_VII.xmll |date=November 15, 2024 }} on CEEPUR</ref>

===Flag===
The flag of Las Piedras has three horizontal stripes of equal width, the stripe at the top is colored white, the center stripe is colored green and the bottom stripe is colored blue. In the middle of the flag rests an image of the [[Taíno]] sun in yellow.<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020">{{cite web | title=LAS PIEDRAS | website=LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico | date=19 February 2020 | url=http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/LASPIEDRAS.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200219001736/http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/LASPIEDRAS.html | archive-date=19 February 2020 | url-status=live | language=es | access-date=17 September 2020}}</ref>

===Coat of arms===
In a silver field resides a blue monogram of the [[Mary, mother of Jesus|Holy Virgin]], topped by a blue crown. Seven silver stones border the silver field and at the tip of the shield resides the Taíno sun in gold denominated as the "Sun of Las Piedras".<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020" />
