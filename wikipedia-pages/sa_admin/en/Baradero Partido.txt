'''Baradero Partido''' is a  [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 29,562 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1514|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Baradero]], which is to the north west of [[Buenos Aires]].

The district was founded on July 25, 1615, making it one of the oldest partidos in Buenos Aires Province.
