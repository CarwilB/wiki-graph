'''Pencahue''' ({{IPA|es|peŋˈkawe}}) is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chile]], located in [[Talca Province]], in the seventh [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Maule Region|Maule]]. The commune spans and area of {{convert|956.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

Also there are another three places called Pencahue, near to San Vicente de Tagua Tagua, Sexta Región, Chile. Their names are: Pencahue Central, Pencahue Abajo (some people call it only Pencahue Bajo) and Plaza de Pencahue.

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, Pencahue had 8,315 inhabitants; of these, 2,037 (24.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,278 (75.5%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 4,517 men and 3,798 women. The population grew 5.9% (461 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pencahue is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012/2012-2016 alcalde is Lucy Lara Leiva ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).<ref name="Munitel1">{{cite web|url=http://www.munitel.cl/|title=Asociación Chilena de Municipalidades|accessdate=22 January 2010|language=es|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110419202659/http://www.munitel.cl/|archivedate=2011-04-19}}</ref><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pencahue is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Empedrado, Chile|Empedrado]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], [[Pelarco]], [[Río Claro, Chile|Río Claro]] and [[San Rafael, Chile|San Rafael]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
