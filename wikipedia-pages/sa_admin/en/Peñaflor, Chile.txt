'''Peñaflor''' ({{IPA|es|peɲaˈfloɾ}}) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Talagante Province]] in central [[Chile]]'s [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

[[File:Hospital de Peñaflor 20240513.jpg|thumb|Hospital]]

==Geography==
It can be found in the [[Chilean Central Valley]] approximately {{convert|37|km|abbr=on}} southwest of the metropolitan area of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|69.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Peñaflor spans an area of {{convert|69.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 66,619 inhabitants (32,671 men and 33,948 women). Of these, 63,209 (94.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,410 (5.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 32.8% (16,471 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Peñaflor is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Manuel Fuentes Rosales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]),<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> and his council members are:<ref name="Municipality">{{Cite web |url= http://www.penaflor.cl/ |title= Municipality of Peñaflor |accessdate=18 July 2010|language=es}}</ref>
* Jorge Kellendonk Numhauser ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Teresita Acevedo Lara (IND)
* Guillermo Donoso Aguilar ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Jorge Navarrete Urzua ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Juan Plaza Núñez (IND)
* Juan Caroca Suarez ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Peñaflor is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte (UDI) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
