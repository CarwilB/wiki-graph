'''Ciénaga de Zapata''' is one of 14 [[Municipalities of Cuba|municipalities]] of the [[Matanzas Province]], [[Cuba]], and the municipal seat is located at [[Playa Larga]], at the northern end of the [[Bay of Pigs|Bahia de Cochinos]] ("Bay of Pigs"). A large part of the municipality is protected as the [[Zapata Swamp]], after which the municipality is named.

With an area of {{Convert|4162|km2}}, it is the largest municipality of Cuba.<ref name=statoids/>

Among the villages included in the municipal territory, one of the most famous is [[Playa Girón]], site of the [[Bay of Pigs Invasion]] in 1961. Other villages are Bermejas, Buenaventura, Caleta del Rosario, Caleta Sábalo, El Jiquí, El Maíz, El Rincón, Guamá, Guasasa, Helechal, La Ceiba, La Florestal, La Salina, Maneadero, Palpite, Playa Maceo, Playa Maquina, San Blás, San Lázaro, Santo Tomás, and Sopillar.

In 2022, the municipality of Cienaga de Zapata had a population of 10,394.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|4162|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003|accessdate=2007-10-17}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|2.5|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
