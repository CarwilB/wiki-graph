'''Santo Augusto''' is a [[Municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]], in the [[Microregions of Brazil|micro region]] of [[Ijuí]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 13,848.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/santo-augusto/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
