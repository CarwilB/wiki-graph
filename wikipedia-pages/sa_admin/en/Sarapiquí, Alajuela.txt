'''Sarapiquí''' is the name of the district number 14 of the canton of Alajuela, that in turn belongs to the province of Alajuela in Costa Rica.<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

Because it borders on the canton of Sarapiquí, it is often confused with the latter.<ref name="  ">{{cite web |url=http://www.munialajuela.go.cr/documentos/bienesinmuebles/mapas/14.pdf |publisher=Municipality of Alajuela |format=pdf |title=Map of Sarapiquí de Alajuela |access-date=2017-08-28 |archive-date=2017-08-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170828062309/http://www.munialajuela.go.cr/documentos/bienesinmuebles/mapas/14.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== History ==
Sarapiquí was created on 6 November 1922 by Decree 28.<ref name="dta2017" />

== Location ==
The district of Sarapiquí, is located in the northern region of the country and borders the canton of Sarapiquí to the east, Río Cuarto to the northwest, [[Sarchí (canton)|Sarchí]] to the west, and the district of Sabanilla de Alajuela to the south.

Its head, the picturesque and rural village of San Miguel, is located 51&nbsp;km (1 hour 30 minutes) north of the city of Alajuela and 67.9&nbsp;km (1 hour 52 minutes) north of San José, the nation's capital .

The nearest towns to San Miguel are Río Cuarto (city), La Virgen de Sarapiquí (village) and Cariblanco (village).

== Geography == 
Sarapiquí has an area of 113.98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the largest district of the canton by area.<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of 510 metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It presents a mountainous relief in the majority of its territory.

== Economy ==

The economy is mainly based on agriculture and livestock.

In the town of San Miguel de Sarapiquí, financial and health services (Ebais) are offered, both state.

The trade is represented by stores selling groceries, among others.
