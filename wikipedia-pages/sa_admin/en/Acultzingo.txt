'''Acultzingo''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 220&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]] to the south-west.

==Products==
It produces [[maize]], [[rice]] and [[Vicia faba|fabas]].

==Geography==
===Borders===
Acultzingo Municipality is delimited to the east by [[Soledad Atzompan Municipality]], to the south and the west by the state of [[Puebla (state)|Puebla]] and to the north by [[Aquila Municipality]].<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30006a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Acultzingo |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es |access-date=2009-05-20 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061026074144/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30006a.htm |archive-date=2006-10-26 }}</ref>
