'''Sapucaia''' ({{IPA|pt|sapuˈkajɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 18,249 (2020) and its area is 540&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> ''Sapucaia'' is also the local name for the tree ''[[Lecythis zabucajo]]''.
