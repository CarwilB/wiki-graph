'''Habana del Este''' (Spanish for "East Havana"), also spelled '''''La Habana del Este''''', is one of the 15 municipalities or boroughs (''[[municipio]]s'' in Spanish) forming the city of [[Havana]], [[Cuba]].

==Geography==
[[File:Tarara-beach.JPG|thumb|left|180px|[[Tarará]] Beach]]
As its name indicates it is on the eastern side of the city, and includes the overspill towns of Camilo Cienfuegos and [[Alamar]] as well as the beach towns of [[Boca Ciega]], [[Tarará]], [[Santa María del Mar, Havana|Santa María del Mar]] and [[Guanabo, Cuba|Guanabo]].{{cn|date=November 2021}}

===Beaches===
The chain of beaches called the Eastern Beaches ({{langx|es|Playas del Este}}) extend for {{convert|15|miles|km}} along the north coast of Havana City province. The beaches are (named from West to East): [[Tarara, Cuba|Tarará]]; El Mégano; Bacuranao (has a bay shape, thick sand and a small Spanish fortress on its western side); Santa María del Mar; Boca Ciega; [[Guanabo, Cuba|Guanabo]]; La Veneciana and Brisas del Mar. The Eastern Beaches are a popular tourist spot with excellent natural conditions, though tourist facilities are scarce and have yet to be expanded.{{cn|date=November 2021}}
