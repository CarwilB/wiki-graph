'''Remedios  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Remedios District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It is the seat of Remedios District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|43.5|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 908 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|20.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,650; its population as of 2000 was 962.<ref name=inec/>
