'''Ranquitte''' ({{IPA|fr|ʁɑ̃kit}}; {{langx|ht|Rankit}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Saint-Raphaël Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 18,197 inhabitants.

The school in Ranquitte is the Spady-Calhoun school named partly in honor of one of its benefactors, Dr. Steven Spady of [[Hindman, KY]].  There is also a Health Clinic on campus there. Both the school and clinic are overseen by a Haitian staff and maintained by Christian Flights International,<ref>http://www.christianflights.org</ref> a non-profit missionary organization that has been sending trips into this area for over 40 years.
