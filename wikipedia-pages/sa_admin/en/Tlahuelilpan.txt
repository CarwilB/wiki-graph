'''Tlahuelilpan''' ({{IPA|es|tlaweˈlilpan}}; {{langx|nah|Tlaualilpan|lit=place where the lands are irrigated}})<ref>{{cite web
| title=Tlahuelilpan: Toponimia
| url=http://estado.hidalgo.gob.mx/MunicipioDetalle/Tlahuelilpan
| publisher=Hidalgo State Government
| language=es
| accessdate=2019-02-06}}</ref> is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|31.3|km2}}. As of the 2010 census, the municipality had a total population of 17,153.<ref>{{cite web
| title=Tlahuelilpan
| url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM13hidalgo/municipios/13070a.html
| language=es

| publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]
| accessdate=2019-02-06}}</ref>

==History==

On January 18, 2019, the town was the site of a [[2019 Tlahuelilpan pipeline explosion|deadly pipeline explosion]], killing 130 people and causing 48 injuries.<ref>{{Cite news
