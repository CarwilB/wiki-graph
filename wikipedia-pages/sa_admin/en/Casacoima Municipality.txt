The '''Casacoima Municipality''' is one of the four [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Delta Amacuro]]. according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 31,706.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Sierra Imataca, Delta Amacuro|Sierra Imataca]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Casacoima Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Deltaamacuro.zip {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Casacoima Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 27,792 (up from 21,217 in 2000).  This amounts to 17.9% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|9.52|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Casacoima Municipality is Pedro Santaella, elected on October 31, 2004, with 22% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio CASACOIMA |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/23/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D23%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163251/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/23/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D23%26mun%3D4%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |archive-date=2012-02-17 |website=www.cne.gob.ve |access-date=2008-03-14 }}</ref>  He replaced Jose Flores shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into five [[parish]]es; Imataca, Cinco de Julio, Juan Bautista Arismendi, Manuel Piar, and Rómulo Gallegos (previous to February 25, 1995, the Casacoima Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
