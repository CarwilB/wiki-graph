'''Loja Canton''' is located in the southeast of the [[Loja Province|Province of Loja]] bordering the [[Podocarpus National Park]] and the [[Zamora-Chinchipe Province|Province of Zamora-Chinchipe]] in the east and south, and the cantons of Saraguro in the north, Catamayo, Gonzanama, and Quilanga in the west.  The principal city is [[Loja, Ecuador|Loja]] which is also the provincial capital.  It is also home to [[Vilcabamba, Ecuador|Vilcabamba]], the "Valley of Longevity."
