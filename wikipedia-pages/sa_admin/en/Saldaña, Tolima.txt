'''Saldaña''' is a town and municipality in the [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] department of [[Colombia]]. Saldaña is where nearly 30% of Colombian rice is grown, and most of them work in the rice business. The population of the municipality was 14,329 as of the 2017 census.
