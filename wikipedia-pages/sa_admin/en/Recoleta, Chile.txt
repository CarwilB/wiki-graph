'''Recoleta''' is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] in the [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It is located north of [[Santiago (commune)|Santiago Centro]] directly across the [[Mapocho River]]. The commune also bordered by [[Independencia, Chile|Independencia]] and [[Conchalí]] to its west, [[Huechuraba]] to its north and [[Providencia, Chile|Providencia]] and [[Vitacura]] to its east where the [[San Cristóbal Hill]] forms a natural barrier.

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Recoleta spans an area of {{convert|16.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 157,851 inhabitants (77,709 men and 80,142 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 6.5% (9,631 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> Its 2024 projected population is 196,856.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/reportescomunales/comunas_v.html?anno=2024&idcom=13127|title=Reporte Comunal 2024: Recoleta|work=[[Library of the National Congress of Chile]]|access-date=19 October 2024|language=es}}</ref>

== Parks and open space ==
The territory of Recoleta includes the northwest portion of [[Santiago Metropolitan Park]], the [[Cerro Blanco]] and the [[Santiago General Cemetery]].

== Culture ==
The bohemian [[Barrio Bellavista]] straddles the border between Recoleta and Providencia. [[Barrio Patronato]] is a neighbourhood in Recoleta with a commercial district that initially grew with the arrival of Middle Eastern immigrants in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. The [[La Vega Central Market]] also lies in close proximity to the [[Patronato metro station]].

Historical churches are located in Recoleta, including the [[Iglesia de la Recoleta Franciscana]] and the [[Iglesia de la Recoleta Dominica]].

==Administration==
As a commune, Recoleta is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] that is directly elected every four years. The alcalde for the 2024-2028 term is Fares Jadue Leiva ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]). The communal council has the following members:

* Marta Valera Aranda ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Silvana Flores Cruz ([[Democrats (Chile)|DEM]])
* Marcel Rosse Venegas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Alejandra Muñoz Díaz ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Karen Garrido Ganga ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Joceline Parra Delgadillo ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Cristian Weibel Avendaño ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Camila Henríquez Lara ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Recoleta is part of electoral [[Electoral divisions of Chile|district No. 9]]. On the other hand, the commune belongs to the [[Electoral divisions of Chile|VII Senatorial District]] that represents the entire [[Santiago Metropolitan Region|Metropolitan Region of Santiago]].
