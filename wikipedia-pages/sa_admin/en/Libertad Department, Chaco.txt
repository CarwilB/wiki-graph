'''Libertad''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of  1,088&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Puerto Tirol]].
