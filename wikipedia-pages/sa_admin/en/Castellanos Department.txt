The '''Castellanos Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Castellanos''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-west of the province. It has about 162,000 inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2001}}. Its head town is the city of [[Rafaela]] (population 84,000).

Its neighbouring departments are [[San Cristóbal Department|San Cristóbal]] in the north, [[Las Colonias Department|Las Colonias]] in the east, and [[San Martín Department, Santa Fe|San Martín]] in the south. The western limit is the border with the province of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]].
