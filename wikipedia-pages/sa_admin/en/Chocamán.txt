'''Chocamán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 146&nbsp;km south of the [[Veracruz (state)|Veracruz]] state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 41.13 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|01|N|97|02|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of '''Chocamán''' is delimited to the north and north-east by [[Coscomatepec de Bravo|Coscomatepec]], to the north-east and south by [[Fortín de las Flores]], to the south-west by [[La Perla, Veracruz|La Perla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30062a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chocaman|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061117185940/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30062a.htm|archive-date=2006-11-17|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Chocamán is cold all year with rains in summer.
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[sugarcane]] and [[Coffee]].
==Culture==
A celebration in honor of Santa Cruz in May.
