'''Puerto Miranda''' is an oil port situated on the east side of [[Lake Maracaibo]] in [[Venezuela]] opposite the city of [[Maracaibo]] and is operated by the [[Petróleos de Venezuela S.A.|Venezuelan State Oil Company (PDVSA PETROLEO, S.A.)]]<ref>[http://www.agemar.net/popup/07.asp Agemar web site]</ref> It is the largest crude oil export port in South America.

==History==
[[Royal Dutch Shell|Shell]], together with the [[Esso]] and [[Gulf Oil Company|Gulf]] subsidiaries  Creole and Mene Grande, decided to construct a deepwater port inside Lake Maracaibo from which to export their [[crude oil]]s, together with a [[Oil depot|tank farm]] to store crude oils that were later sent to the refineries in [[Amuay]] and Cardon on the [[Paraguaná Peninsula|Paraguaná peninsula]].
Construction of the port started at the end of the 1950s and was completed in 1961.<ref>The Contamination of Lake Maracaibo by Oliver L. Campbell</ref>

==Facilities==
Puerto Miranda has two jetties, each long enough to take two tankers, so that four tankers can be serviced at one time. 
Jetty No. 2 (North) provides two berths, Berth No. 1 and No. 2; Jetty No. 3 (South) provides Berths No. 5 and 6.
All cargo is loaded through {{convert|12|in|mm|adj=mid|-diameter}} [[chiksan]]s at a maximum rate of about 5,500 [[Tonne]]s per hour.
Products supplied are 
* [[Fuel oil#General classification|Fuel Oil No. 2 and No. 6]]
and the following crude oils:
* Laguna
* Tia Juana Heavy
* Bachaquero
* Lagunillas
* Cabimas
* Menemota
* Lagomar
* Lagocinco
* Lagotreco

For a list of crude oil types see [[List of crude oil products#List of crude oil products|the List of crude oil products]]
