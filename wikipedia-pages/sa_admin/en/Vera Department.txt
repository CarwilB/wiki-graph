The '''Vera Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Vera''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-north of the province. It limits [[Chaco Province]] to the north, and the departments of [[General Obligado Department|General Obligado]] (east), [[San Javier Department, Santa Fe|San Javier]] (south-east), [[San Justo Department, Santa Fe|San Justo]] (south), [[San Cristóbal Department|San Cristóbal]] (south-west), and [[Nueve de Julio Department, Santa Fe|Nueve de Julio]] (west).

The Vera Department is the largest department in Santa Fe but also one of the least densely populated, as is true in the north of the province as a whole. It has over 51,000 inhabitants. Its head town is [[Vera, Santa Fe|Vera]] (population 20,000). Other cities and towns are [[Calchaquí, Argentina|Calchaquí]], [[Cañada Ombú]], [[Fortín Olmos]], [[Garabato, Argentina|Garabato]], [[Golondrina, Argentina|Golondrina]], [[Intiyaco, Santa Fe|Intiyaco]], [[La Gallareta]], [[Los Amores, Argentina|Los Amores]], [[Margarita, Argentina|Margarita]], [[Tartagal, Santa Fe|Tartagal]], and [[Toba, Santa Fe|Toba]].
