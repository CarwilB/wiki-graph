'''El Triunfo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Guayas Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[El Triunfo, Ecuador|El Triunfo]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 34,117.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

The Canton was created in 1983, but some of it is disputed with [[La Troncal Canton]] in [[Ca%C3%B1ar Province]].
