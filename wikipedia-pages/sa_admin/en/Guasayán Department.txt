'''Guasayán Department''' is a ''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'' of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santiago del Estero Province|Santiago del Estero]], [[Argentina]].

This region of small mountains in the west of the province is 2,588&nbsp;km² (less than 2% of the province) and has 7,404 inhabitants ({{census-ar|2001}}) (less than 1% of the province's population). Its capital is [[San Pedro de Guasayán]], with 1,715 inhabitants, located 124 kilometres from [[Santiago del Estero]], the provincial capital, on Provincial Route 64. Other villages include Lavalle, Guampaya and Villa Guasayán.<ref name="sde.gov">{{cite web |url=http://www.sde.gov.ar/municipios/guasayan.php |title=Santiago del Estero - Sitio Oficial |website=www.sde.gov.ar |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20051216015801/http://sde.gov.ar/municipios/guasayan.php |archive-date=2005-12-16}} </ref>

The highest peak in the department is ''Sinchi Caña'' at 630 metres, and no river issues from it.<ref name="sde.gov"/> According to some historians, the term ''Guasayán'' could mean "path behind the sierra", or "view from the heights". There are some ruins from Native American inhabitants, especially near Villa Guasayán. [[Diego de Rojas]] is said to have travelled through those lands around 1544.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.elliberal.com.ar/secciones.php?nombre=home&file=verarchivo&id_noticia=05082819P |title=Villa Guasayán ruins |access-date=2006-03-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927143358/http://www.elliberal.com.ar/secciones.php?nombre=home&file=verarchivo&id_noticia=05082819P |archive-date=2007-09-27 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
