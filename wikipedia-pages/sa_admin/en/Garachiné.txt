'''Garachiné''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,878 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,800; its population as of 2000 was 1,944.<ref name=inec/>

== Transportation ==
The village is served by the [[Garachiné Airport]] {{airport codes|GHE}}.
