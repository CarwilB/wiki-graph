'''Santa Rosalía''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsanta rosaˈli.a}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Vichada Department|Vichada]], [[Colombia]].

==Geography==

The area is part of the [[Llanos]] plain shared by Colombia and [[Venezuela]], located within the [[Orinoquía Region of Colombia|Orinoquia region of Colombia]]. The municipality has an average temperature of 28&nbsp;°C throughout the year, persisting a hot and dry climate. The seat of the municipality lies on the riviera of the [[Meta River]] which also serves as boundary between the Department of Vichada and the [[Department of Casanare]].<ref>[http://santarosalia-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Santa Rosalia: geography]</ref>

The municipality of Santa Rosalia limits to the north with the [[Department of Casanare]]; to the east with the municipality of [[La Primavera, Vichada|La Primavera]]; to the south with the municipality of [[Cumaribo]] and to the west with the [[Department of Meta]].

==History==

The area of present-day Santa Rosalia was inhabited by indigenous tribes, predominantly the [[Guahibo people]]. Upon the arrival of the [[Spanish people]] and other Europeans, the region was not colonized until the 16th century. The settlement was established as a port for embarking cattle from the [[hacienda]]s. In 1993 La Primavera was proclaimed municipality.<ref>[http://santarosalia-vichada.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m Santa Rosalia: History]</ref>
