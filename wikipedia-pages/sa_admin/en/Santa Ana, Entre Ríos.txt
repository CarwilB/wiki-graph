'''Santa Ana (Entre Ríos)''' is a village and municipality in [[Entre Ríos Province]] in north-eastern [[Argentina]].<ref>[http://www.mininterior.gov.ar/ Ministerio del Interior] {{in lang|es}}</ref>

The 1979 completion of the [[Salto Grande Dam]] put thousands of acres under water, and Santa Ana shifted to citrus production, which now supports 60% of the population.
