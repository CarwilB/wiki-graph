'''Guamal''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Guamal has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af''). The climate is hot and sometimes humid, though its proximity to the foothills of the Oriental Andes brings mild breezes at nightfall. Average temperature is {{convert|25.1|°C|°F|1|disp=or}}. Although January is almost dry enough for [[tropical monsoon climate|tropical monsoon]] (''Am'') classification, the other eleven months all receive over {{convert|125|mm|in|0|disp=or}} of rain, and for eight months from April to November monthly rainfall consistently exceeds {{convert|340|mm|in|1|disp=or}}.
