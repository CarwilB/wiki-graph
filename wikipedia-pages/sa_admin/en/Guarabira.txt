'''Guarabira''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraíba]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast Region]] of [[Brazil]]. It is located at around {{Coord|6|51|18|S|35|29|24|W}}. It was founded in 1837.  It is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Guarabira]].
