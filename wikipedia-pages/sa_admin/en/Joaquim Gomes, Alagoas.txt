'''Joaquim Gomes''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒuɐˈkĩ ˈɡõmis]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in [[Alagoas]], Brazil. It had a population of 24,081 as of 2020,<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/joaquim-gomes/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and an area of {{Cvt|239|km2}}.<ref>[[IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

The [[extinct language|extinct]] and unattested [[Wasu language]] was spoken in the Serra do Azul of Joaquim Gomes.
