'''Palmeirina''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[pawmeˈɾĩj̃ɐ]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  252&nbsp;km from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco, Palmeirina has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 7,600 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/palmeirina/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[São João, Pernambuco|São João]] and [[Angelim]]   ('''N''');  [[Correntes, Pernambuco|Correntes]] and [[Alagoas]] state   ('''S''');  [[Canhotinho]]  ('''E''');   [[Garanhuns]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 158.01&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 531 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 22.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 252&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Palmeirina''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[beans]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
