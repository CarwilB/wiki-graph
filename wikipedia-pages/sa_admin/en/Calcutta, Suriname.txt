'''Calcutta''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Saramacca District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 1,647, and is mainly inhabited by [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indians]].<ref name="census"/> The town is named after [[Calcutta]], India. From 1873 onwards, indentured workers were recruited from [[India]] to work the plantations. Most of the workers departed from Calcutta. About one third returned to India, but most decided to stay in Suriname.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.lallarookh.nl/hindostaanse-contractarbeiders/|title=Hindostaanse contractarbeiders 1873 – 1920|access-date=23 May 2020|language=nl|website=Lalla Rookh}}</ref>

Calcutta is the place where [[oil]] was discovered in 1965 which marked the beginning of [[Staatsolie Maatschappij Suriname|Staatsolie]], the national oil and gold company.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.staatsolie.com/nl/over-ons/geschiedenis/|title=Staatsolie - Geschiedenis|website=Staatsolie|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Calcutta is a major centre of [[rice]] cultivation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

The resorts also contains the village of [[Boskamp, Suriname|Boskamp]] and the indigenous village of [[Kalebaskreek]]. The former [[leprosy]] colony of [[Batavia, Suriname|Batavia]] is located in the resort.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.bataviasuriname.com/en_US/|title=Batavia Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|website=Batavia Suriname}}</ref>
