'''Tancoco''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 210&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 145.59 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|21|17|N|97|47|W|}}. On the occasion of the development reached by the camp petroleum of Zacamixtle, the municipal head-board was moved to this place. For Decree of December 26, 1929 it returns to the village of Tancoco, its category of Municipality.{{what?|date=December 2025}}

==Geography==

The municipality of Tancoco is delimited to the north by [[Tamalín]] and [[Naranjos Amatlán]], to the east by [[Tamiahua]], to the south by [[Cerro Azul (municipality)|Cerro Azul]] and [[Tepetzintla, Veracruz|Tepetzintla]], to the west by [[Chontla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30153a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tancoco|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}{{Dead link|date=July 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> It is watered by small creeks, which end in Tamiahua's lagoon.

The weather in Tancoco is warm and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[bean]]s and [[orange fruit]].

==Celebrations==

In Tancoco, in February takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de la Candelaria, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.
