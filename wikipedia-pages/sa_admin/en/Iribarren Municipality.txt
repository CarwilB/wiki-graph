The '''Iribarren Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Lara (state)|Lara]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 1,027,022.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The City of [[Barquisimeto]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Iribarren Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Lara.zip{{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Iribarren Municipality, according to a 2011 population census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 996,230 (up from 915,634 in 2000).  This amounts to 56% of the state's population.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/index.php?option=com_content&view=category&id=95&Itemid=26|title=Censos de población y vivienda|access-date=2019-02-08|archive-date=2015-03-16|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150316050254/http://www.ine.gov.ve/index.php?option=com_content&view=category&id=95&Itemid=26|url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|372.11|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Iribarren Municipality is Alfredo Ramos, elected on December 8, 2013. The municipality is divided into 10 [[parish]]es; Catedral, Concepción, El Cují, Juan de Villegas, Santa Rosa, Tamaca, Unión, Aguedo Felipe Alvarado, Buena Vista, and Juárez.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
