'''Tapenagá''' is a southern [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about  4,000 inhabitants in an area of  6,025km², and its capital city is [[Charadai]], which is located around 1,070&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
