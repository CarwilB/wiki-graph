'''Hato Rey Sur''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the [[municipality]] of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], [[Puerto Rico]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://factfinder.census.gov/faces/tableservices/jsf/pages/productview.xhtml?pid=DEC_10_DP_DPDP1&prodType=table|archive-url=https://archive.today/20190521214830/https://factfinder.census.gov/faces/tableservices/jsf/pages/productview.xhtml?pid=DEC_10_DP_DPDP1&prodType=table|url-status=dead|archive-date=May 21, 2019|title=Profile of General Population and Housing Characteristics: 2010|website=Census.gov|accessdate=February 12, 2019}}</ref> It is one of three barrios commonly known together as [[Hato Rey]]. Hato Rey Sur was a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] of the former municipality of [[Rio Piedras, Puerto Rico|Rio Piedras]], before it was merged with the municipality of San Juan in 1951.<ref>[https://enciclopediapr.org/encyclopedia/anexion-de-rio-piedras-a-san-juan/ ''Anexion de Rio Piedras a San Juan.''] Enciclopedia de Puerto Rico. Fundación Puertorriqueña de Humanidades. Accessed 12 February 2019.</ref> Following the annexation of Rio Piedras, the city of San Juan, and its surrounding area now including Rio Piedras, quadrupled its former size.<ref>[http://www.linktopr.com/riopiedras_e.html Includes map of Río Piedras and San Juan before annexation.] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081118194414/http://www.linktopr.com/riopiedras_e.html |date=November 18, 2008 }}</ref>

== Landmarks and places of interest ==

* EDP University, Hato Rey Campus
* Judicial Center of San Juan
* [[Universidad Avenue]], the main nightlife and dining spot for students of the UPR.
* The [[University of Puerto Rico]]'s [[University of Puerto Rico, Río Piedras Campus|main campus]] is also located closeby.

== Transportation ==
Although the only [[Tren Urbano]] station to be fully located within Hato Rey Sur is [[Piñero station|Piñero]], the [[Río Piedras, Puerto Rico|Rio Piedras]] and [[Universidad station (Puerto Rico)|Universidad]] stations also serve the barrio due to their close proximity.
