'''Simoca Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 29,932 (2001) and an area of 1,261&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The seat of the department is in [[Simoca]].
