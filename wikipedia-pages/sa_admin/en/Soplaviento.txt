'''Soplaviento''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]]. The town is located on the south bank of the [[Canal del Dique|Dique Canal]] across from the town of [[San Estanislao, Bolívar|San Estanislao]].
