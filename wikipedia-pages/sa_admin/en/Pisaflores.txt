'''Pisaflores''' is a town is the north-west of the [[Mexican state]] of [[Hidalgo (state)|Hidalgo]]. It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding [[municipalities of Hidalgo|municipality]] of the same name. 

The municipality covers an area of {{Cvt|159.3|km2}} and, as of 2020, had a population of  18,723<ref>{{cite web |title=Pisaflores: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/pisaflores |website=Data México |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=21 October 2025}}</ref> (up from 17,214 in 2005).<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13049a.htm | title=Pisaflores | publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] | work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México | access-date=December 27, 2008 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070320075607/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13049a.htm | archive-date=March 20, 2007 | url-status=dead | language=es}}</ref> 
It is located in the state's [[Huasteca]] region, bordering the states of [[Querétaro]] and [[San Luis Potosí]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Ubicación geográfica de Pisaflores |url=https://www.hidalguia.com.mx/pisaflores/ubicacion.htm |website=Hidalguía |access-date=21 October 2025}}</ref>

Pisaflores also has [[Tepehuas]] who are Eastern Orthodox Christians. (Sandstrom 2005)
