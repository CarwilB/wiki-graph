'''Eduardo Abaroa''', also '''Eduardo Avaroa''', or '''Challapata'''<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gob.bo/publicaciones/visorPdf.aspx?Codigo=040202&tipo=1|title=Santiago de Quillacas|author=<!--Staff writer(s); no by-line.--> |publisher=INE, Bolivia|access-date=October 15, 2015}}</ref> is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the southeastern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]]. Its seat is [[Challapata]]. The province was named after Colonel [[Eduardo Abaroa]], Bolivia's hero of the [[War of the Pacific]].

==Location==
Eduardo Abaroa province is one of sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 18° 39' and 19° 30' [[Latitude|South]] and between 65° 42' and 66° 42' [[Longitude|West]]. 

The province borders [[Poopó Province]] in the northwest, [[Sud Carangas Province]] and [[Ladislao Cabrera Province]] in the west, and [[Potosí Department]] in the south and east. Furthermore, [[Sebastián Pagador Province]] is embedded between the two municipios of Eduardo Abaroa province.

The province extends over 90&nbsp;km from north to south, and 100&nbsp;km from east to west.

== Geography ==
The province lies at [[Poopó Lake]], one of the largest lakes of Bolivia.

One of the highest peaks of the province is [[Jatun Wila Qullu]] at {{convert|5214|m|ft|abbr=on}}. ==Population==
The main language spoken in the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 78.3%, while 71.9% of the population speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]] and 23.0% [[Aymara language|Aymara]]. 

The population increased from 23,147 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 27,675 (2001 census), an increase of 19.6%. - 44.1% of the population are younger than 15 years old.  

82% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 94% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]]. 

68.9% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.5% in [[mining]], 6.9% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 23.7% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

86.6% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 11.9% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
