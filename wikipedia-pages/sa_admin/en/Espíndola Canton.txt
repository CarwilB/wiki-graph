{{Short description|Canton of Loja province, Ecuador}}
[[Image:Loja in Ecuador (+Galapagos).svg|thumb|Loja Province]]Espíndola is a canton in the Province of [[Loja Province|Loja]] [[Ecuador]].  It is located in the south-east, bordered by the cantons of [[Quilanga Canton|Quilanga]] and [[Calvas Canton|Calvas]], the province of [[Zamora-Chinchipe Province|Zamora-Chinchipe]] and the country of [[Peru]].  It covers an area of 632&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 1720 m, with a population of 19,213.  The average temperature is 19.9&nbsp;°C.
