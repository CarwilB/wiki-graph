'''Arataca''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Arataca covers {{convert|435.96|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 10,961 with a population density of {{convert|27|PD/km2|abbr=off|sp=us}}. It consists of two districts: Arataca, the municipal seat, and Itatingui.<ref name="i"/>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|11336|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra das Lontras National Park]], created in 2010.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional da Serra das Lontras|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=1909|access-date=2016-06-09}}</ref>
