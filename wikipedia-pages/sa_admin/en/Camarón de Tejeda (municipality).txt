'''Camarón de Tejeda Municipality''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the state's southeast, in the [[Las Montañas]] region. The municipal seat is the city of [[Camarón de Tejeda]]. It covers a total of 74.38&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

At the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a population of 5,660, of whom 2019 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Camarón de Tejeda|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30007a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=2008-11-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070331124832/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30007a.htm|archive-date=2007-03-31|url-status=dead}}</ref>
==Geography==
The municipality of Camarón de Tejeda is delimited to the east by the [[Soledad de Doblado]], to the south and west by the [[Paso del Macho]], and to the west by [[Zentla Municipality|Zentla]].
==History==
Camarón de Tejeda is famous for the [[Battle of Camarón]], fought on 30 April 1863 between the [[French Foreign Legion]] and the [[Mexican Army]].
