'''San Benito''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[El Petén]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of 112&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and had 29,926 inhabitants at the 2002 Census; the latest official estimate (as at mid 2012) was 59,486 people.

It is located adjacent to departmental capital [[Flores, Guatemala|Flores]].

== History ==
San Benito was Originally founded under the name '''San José de Los Negros''' by the Spaniards who imported enslaved Africans from the Caribbean into the region including an estimated 100 slave fugitives from the neighboring Belize<ref>{{Cite web |title=Paper: From Involuntary to Voluntary Colonists: The Belizean Settlers of San José de Los Negros, Guatemala (133rd Annual Meeting (January 3-6, 2019)) |url=https://aha.confex.com/aha/2019/webprogram/Paper25088.html |access-date=2026-02-18 |website=aha.confex.com}}</ref>, who, upon their arrival declared their desire to convert to Catholicism, a key step in becoming recognized as faithful subjects of Spain. 

With them the Christian Africans brought the image of [[Benedict the Moor|Saint Benedict the Black]], proclaiming him the patron saint of the place. <ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Rodriguez |first=Mireya |title=Municipio de San Benito, Petén |url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/peten/san-benito/ |access-date=2026-02-18 |website=Deguate.com |language=es}}</ref>

In response to protests from the local maya chiefs, Commander José de Gálvez ordered the relocation of the inhabitants to where the Barrio La Ermita is currently located near Petén’s fortress, leading to a name change to '''San Benito de''' '''Los Negros''' shortened to the common name '''San Benito'''.<ref name=":0" />

In 2013 Mayor of San Benito '''[[Sonia Rivera]]''' attended a delegation where she met with Mayor of Belize City '''[[Darrell Bradley]].''' Through acknowledgements of shared heritage and in the presence of congress from Guatemala City they declared San Benito and Belize City sister cities and established a formal friendship.<ref>{{Cite news |date=March 1, 2017 |title=The Ties That Bind Bze And Guat: San Benito Mayor Visits |url=https://www.7newsbelize.com/sstory.php?nid=24861 |access-date=February 2, 2026 |work=7newsbelize.com}}</ref>

==Sports==
[[Deportivo San Benito]] football club have been playing in the [[Primera División de Ascenso|second tier of Guatemalan football]] since they clinched promotion from the third tier after the 1998/1999 season.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesg/guat99.html Guatemala 1998/99] - RSSSF</ref> They play their home games in the [[Estadio Alejandro Ochaeta Requena]].
[[Deportivo Don Bosco]] football club have been playing in the [[tercera División de Ascenso|third tier of Guatemalan football]] since 2015/2016 season.<ref>[https://www.rsssf.org/tablesg/guat99.html Guatemala 1998/99] - RSSSF</ref> They play their home games in the [[Estadio Alejandro Ochaeta Requena]].
