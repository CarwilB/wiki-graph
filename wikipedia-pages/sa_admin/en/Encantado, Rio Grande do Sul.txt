'''Encantado''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is located 25&nbsp;km northeast of [[Teutônia]].<ref name="cidade">{{Cite web |title=Encantado - Informações sobre o município e a prefeitura |trans-title=Municipality of Encantado |url=https://www.cidade-brasil.com.br/municipio-encantado.html |access-date=December 18, 2022 |website=www.cidade-brasil.com.br |at=Cidade Brasil / Estado de Rio Grande do Sul / Município Encantado}}</ref> It is also the location of the ''[[Christ the Protector]]'' statue, which was completed in 2022.<ref>{{cite news |date=April 11, 2021 |title=New Christ statue in Brazil's Encantado to be taller than Rio's |agency=BBC News |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-latin-america-56710354 |access-date=December 18, 2022}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Swati |first=K S |date=May 5, 2022 |title=Brazil's Encantado Town Builds A Statue Of Jesus Christ Taller Than Rio's |url=https://www.outlookindia.com/travel/brazil-s-encantado-town-builds-a-taller-statue-of-jesus-christ-than-rio-s-news-195008 |access-date=December 18, 2022 |website=Outlook |publisher=[[Outlook India]] |language=en}}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities include [[Muçum]], [[Roca Sales]], and [[Vespasiano Correa|Vespasiano Corrêa]].<ref name="cidade" />
