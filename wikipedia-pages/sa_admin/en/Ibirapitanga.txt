'''Ibirapitanga''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] and a town in the State of [[Bahia]], [[Brazil]], located inland from the coast about 45&nbsp;km.<ref>{{GEOnet2|32FA884110653774E0440003BA962ED3|Ibirapitanga (Approved)}}, [[National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|United States National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency]]</ref>  In 2020 the estimated population of the municipality was 23,404 inhabitants.

==History==
The town was founded in 1915 when José Meneses de Oliveira built his home on the banks of the Cachoeira River, and it came to be known as Cachoeira do Pau. Later the name was changed to Ibirapitanga, a word in the [[Tupi language]] for "red wood" that is, [[Paubrasilia|Brazilwood]].<ref name="IBGE-Hist">{{cite web|title=Ibirapitanga, Bahia: historico|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=291270#historico}}</ref>

The municipality was officially created in 1961, carved out of the interior portion of [[Camamu|Camamu municipality]], but came into effect in 1963.<ref name="IBGE-Hist" />

==Administrative divisions==
Ibirapitanga municipality is divided into four parts, the headquarters town of Ibirapitanga, and the rural divisions:  Itamarati, Camamuzinho and Novo Horizonte.

==Geography==
Ibirapitanga is bounded by the municipalities of [[Camamu]] to the east, [[Maraú]] to the southeast, [[Ubaitaba]] to the south, [[Ubatã]] to the west, [[Gandu, Brazil|Gandu]] and [[Piraí do Norte]] to the north and [[Igrapiúna]] to the northeast.

Highway BA-650 runs east to the town of [[Camamu]]. Highway 330 runs west to the towns of [[Ubatã]] and [[Ipiaú]]. Highway 101 runs north to [[Gandu, Brazil|Gandu]] and south to [[Ubaitaba]] and [[Itabuna]].

==Economy==
Ibirapitanga's economy is based on [[Theobroma cacao|cocoa]] agriculture.
