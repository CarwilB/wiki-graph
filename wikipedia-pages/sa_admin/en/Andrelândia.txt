'''Andrelândia''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]] that is located in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|Mesoregion]] of South and Southwest of Minas and hosts the [[Microregions of Brazil|Microregion]] of Andrelândia. It is 300&nbsp;km away from the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]]<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Cidades.Net |title=Andrelândia - MG |url=http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/andrelandia.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090630031943/http://cidadesnet.com/municipios/andrelandia.htm |archive-date=2009-06-30 |access-date=2010-05-07}}</ref> and occupies an area of approximately 1 005&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. In 2014 its population was estimated at 12 507 inhabitants, being the 296th most populous municipality in the state of Minas Gerais and the second of its microregion.

It was founded on July 20, 1868, under the name Vila Bela do Turvo and consisted of five [[district]]s: Turvo, Arantes, Bom Jardim, Madre de Deus do Rio Grande and San Vicente Ferrer. Over the years the districts turned into [[city|cities]], leaving only Andrelândia only the municipal seat. Throughout its history, the municipality had several denominations but has had its current name since state law 1160, of September 19, 1930.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Enciclopédia dos municípios brasileiros - Volume XXIV ano 1958 |year=2007 |title=Andrelândia - MG |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/minasgerais/andrelandia.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706160927/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/minasgerais/andrelandia.pdf |archive-date=2011-07-06 |archive-format=[[Portable Document Format|PDF]] |access-date=2010-07-28 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE)}}</ref>

The city has a great tradition in tourism and many of its old houses are considered historical municipal patrimonies.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Turismo rural |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=127 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210193201/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=127 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-01}}</ref> Other highlights are the religious festivals, such as the Feast of [[Saint Sebastian]], the Feast of Kings, the [[Holy Week]], the Feast of [[Benedict the Moor|San Benedict]], ''[[Feast of Corpus Christi|Corpus Christi]]'' and the feast of the [[patron saint]], [[Our Lady of Vendôme|Our Lady of Porto]], in August.<ref name=":16">{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Turismo de eventos |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=129 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706155740/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=129 |archive-date=2011-07-06 |access-date=2010-08-01}}</ref>

The city is known for being the hometown of the internationally known professor, historian, and academic [[José Murilo de Carvalho]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=José Murilo de Carvalho |url=http://www.academia.org.br/academicos/jose-murilo-de-carvalho/biografia |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191227181025/http://www.academia.org.br/academicos/jose-murilo-de-carvalho/biografia |archive-date=2019-12-27 |access-date=2019-12-27 |website=Academia Brasileira de Letras |language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=josé murilo de carvalho terra natal - Pesquisa Google |url=https://www.google.com/search?q=jos%C3%A9+murilo+de+carvalho+terra+natal |access-date=2019-12-27 |website=www.google.com}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
It was renamed Vila Bela do Turvo, its first name, when it was elevated to the category of village by provincial law No. 1191 of July 27, 1864. It was elevated to the status of city, with the name Turvo, on July 20, 1868. Two years later, by provincial law No. 1644 of September 13, the municipality of Turvo was renamed Porto do Turvo. By state law No. 2 of September 14, 1891, the municipality of Porto do Turvo was renamed again as Turvo. By state law No. 556 of August 30, 1911, the then district of Nossa Senhora da Piedade do Rio Grande (belonging to Andrelândia) was renamed Arantes.<ref name=":1" />

After state law nº 1 160, of September 19, 1930, the municipality of Turvo was renamed Andrelândia, the name that prevails today.<ref name=":1" /> Its toponymy is in honor of farmer [[André da Silveira]], who was one of the first to settle in the region. André's concern was to establish a place of worship for the development of the city and its population, which culminated in the current municipality of Andrelândia.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=História |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=23 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100814213109/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=23 |archive-date=2010-08-14 |access-date=2010-07-28}}</ref>

== History ==

=== Colonization and Development ===
[[Image:Praça Visconde de Arantes 1927, Andrelândia MG.jpg|thumb|Visconde de Arantes Square in 1927|left]]The beginning of the colonization of European origin in the region of the current city of Andrelândia was a consequence of the gold exploitation in the south of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. In the mid-eighteenth century, the region where the city is today was already fully populated by explorers who came to the region in search of fortune.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Souza Miranda |first=Marcos Paulo de |title=Aspectos Históricos da Terra de André |year=1996 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=10}}</ref>

Around 1740, the population flow in the region increased considerably. Many people demarcated an extension of wasteland, settled there and later took care of the legalization of possession through the granting of a letter of ''sesmaria'', which was given by the governor of the [[Captaincy]] of Minas Gerais. The "''Congonhal''" region, which extends over a large area of fertile land on the right bank of the Turvo Grande River and received this name due to the abundance of trees, was one of the most populated areas.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Souza Miranda |first=Marcos Paulo de |title=Aspectos Históricos da Terra de André |year=1996 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=4}}</ref>

In 1749, the farmer André da Silveira, feeling the need for religious assistance, asked the Bishop of [[Mariana, Minas Gerais|Mariana]] for permission to build a church dedicated to [[Our Lady of Vendôme|Our Lady of Porto]] in the place called Turvo Pequeno. Heeded by the ecclesiastical authority, the chapel was built, receiving the Catholic blessing in 1755. Around the small temple, many houses were built, and soon the Arraial do Turvo, primitive name of the settlement, was formed.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Andrelândia.com |title=História |url=http://andrelandia.com/?page_id=2 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100129002408/http://andrelandia.com/?page_id=2 |archive-date=2010-01-29 |access-date=2010-07-28}}</ref><ref name=":3">{{Cite web|title=Histórico |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/historicos_cidades/historico_conteudo.php?codmun=310280 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121105113954/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/historicos_cidades/historico_conteudo.php?codmun=310280 |archive-date=2012-11-05 |access-date=2010-07-28}}</ref> After the construction of the chapel, in 1827, the Arraial do Turvo was already in conditions to be elevated to parish, which in fact occurred, having, from then on, a progressive growth, until it was transformed into a village in 1864, by law nº 1.191 of July 27 of this year.<ref name=":3" />

=== Ethnic Development ===
In the ethnic aspect, the population of Andrelândia has not suffered much miscegenation. A large part of the city has Portuguese ancestry, due to the first inhabitants who came along with the ''[[bandeirantes]]'' from São Paulo and founded the old farms, the first habitational centers, from which the city was born. The closest indigenous tribe was found farther south in Minas Gerais, in the region surrounding the springs of the [[Rio Verde (São Paulo)|Verde]] and [[Baependi River|Baependi]] rivers, near the Papagaio Mountain, around 1700.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=54}}</ref> However, it was only between the 19th and 20th centuries that the first foreigners began to arrive in Andrelândia, mostly Lebanese who came to Brazil in search of wealth. Besides the foreigners who arrived and integrated the population of Andrelândia, many other families, from other cities and regions of Brazil, were also attracted by the riches of the place.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=57}}</ref> From [[Italy]] came the D'Alessandro and Rivelli families; from [[Spain]], Laredo, Casas Martins, and Garrido, and from [[Portugal]], Gaspar and [[Pereira (surname)|Pereira]]. Almost all of them were motivated by the construction of the [[Estrada de Ferro Oeste de Minas|West of Minas Railroad]], inaugurated on June 14, 1914, or were tempted to progress with the benefits resulting from its operation.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=58}}</ref>

There was a small group of people in the city, mainly of European origin, who frequented the market of Andrelândia in the early twentieth century and who, as attested by the elders, were the first street sellers of the current city. These people lived in a region not far from the town, called Congonhal, near the Bandeira Mountain,<ref name=":4">{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=56}}</ref> and for this reason they were called ''Congonheiras''. They traveled around the city selling the harvest of their small crops and were known for not accepting the jobs that were offered to them.<ref name=":4" /> The ''Congonheiros'' still exist, and in much larger numbers, but they have diversified their way of life: they lost the distinction that marginalized them and many moved to the city. They married into other families, learned and dedicated themselves to the most varied trades and professions.<ref name=":4" />

=== Administrative evolution ===
There was one detail missing to elevate the village to the status of municipality.  According to the legislation in force, this could only happen if the population founded, with its own resources, the building of the public jail and the town hall. Then, with the collaboration of Antônio Belfort de Arantes and his son, Antônio Belfort Ribeiro de Arantes, the works could be executed and the small village was raised to the category of municipality on October 21, 1866. On July 20, 1868, the emancipation of Andrelândia took place officially, under the name of Turvo, by provincial law no. 1518.<ref name=":1" />

Under state law No. 2 of September 14, 1891 the district of Nossa Senhora da Piedade do Rio Grande was created and attached to the then municipality of Turvo. By state law No. 556 of August 30, 1911, the district of Nossa Senhora da Piedade do Rio Grande was renamed Arantes, having then five districts: Turvo, Arantes, Bom Jardim, Madre de Deus do Rio Grande and São Vicente Ferrer. By state law No. 843 of September 7, 1923, the Madre de Deus do Rio Grande district was renamed Cianita and Senhor do Bom Jesus do Jardim was renamed Bom Jardim. By State Law No. 148, of December 17, 1938, the district of São Vicente Ferrer was dismembered from Andrelândia and raised to the category of municipality under the name of Francisco Sales. Under the same law, the district of [[Bom Jardim de Minas]] was dismembered from the municipality of Andrelândia, and became a municipality with the same name. By law No. 1039 of December 12, 1953, the districts of Arantes and Cianita were dismembered and elevated to the category of municipalities with the names, respectively, [[Piedade do Rio Grande]] and [[Madre de Deus de Minas]], remaining until today only the municipal seat.<ref name=":1" />

=== Economic Growth ===
[[File:Andrelândia MG 1930.jpg|thumb|Andrelândia in 1930]]
In 1982, the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) verified that there were 71 commercial establishments in the city. This was quite a considerable number for a city of low income and low consumption. Considering the momentum of the first three decades of the 20th century, the city lost in productive activity and monetary circulation, both in the urban and rural areas, due to the rural exodus that, consequently, decreased the labor force that is very difficult in this rugged and hard to reach region. With the low value of production and the high price of seeds and inputs, a natural consequence of a government policy that was not interested in the small producer, the man from the countryside was forced to abandon his work and look for the big urban centers, where he hoped to find some kind of work to maintain himself, or end up increasing the rate of unemployed, underemployed, slum dwellers, hungry, and even criminals.<ref name=":5">{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=87–88}}</ref>

Another event that marked and still reflects the history of the municipal economy is the fact that many industries installed at the end of the 19th century and the first half of the 20th century disappeared, without any plausible explanation. However, the commercial movement in Andrelândia has developed considerably, especially since the early 1990s, which was a visible sign of the city's economic growth and modernization.<ref name=":5" />

=== Recent History ===
With the growth of Andrelândia and nearby cities, the [[Microregions of Brazil|Microregion]] of Andrelândia was created, gathering, besides the municipality, twelve other cities: [[Aiuruoca]], [[Arantina]], [[Bocaina de Minas]], [[Bom Jardim de Minas]], [[Carvalhos, Minas Gerais|Carvalhos]], [[Cruzília]], [[Liberdade, Minas Gerais|Liberdade]], [[Minduri]], [[Passa-Vinte|Passa Vinte]], [[São Vicente de Minas]], [[Seritinga]] and [[Serranos]]. In 2006 its population was estimated at 75,631 inhabitants, by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, in a total area of 5,034.106&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) was 0.740 and the [[Gross domestic product|GDP]]  ''per capita'' was R$3,796.46 in 2003. It is located in the [[Mesoregions of Brazil|Mesoregion]] of South and Southwest of Minas Gerais.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sites do Brasil |title=Microrregião de Andrelândia |url=http://www.sites-do-brasil.com/diretorio/index.php?cat_id=784 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100201205337/http://sites-do-brasil.com/diretorio/index.php?cat_id=784 |archive-date=2010-02-01 |access-date=2010-01-08}}</ref>

Currently, the town has been standing out in its tourism, with many of its old houses built in the 17th and 18th centuries becoming part of the town's historical heritage. Many of its squares and churches also preserve the [[baroque]] style from the time of the clearing of the region.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Turismo urbano |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=121 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210194335/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=121 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-01}}</ref> Besides this, Andrelândia has also been developing its rural tourism, where the main attractions are the old farms, many from the 18th century.<ref name=":2" />

== Geography ==
[[File:Vista panorâmica de Andrelândia.JPG|left|thumb|Partial view of Andrelandia]]
The geography of Andrelândia is homogeneous, with an undulating relief and [[Atlantic Forest|Atlantic]] vegetation. The area of the municipality is 1,004.536&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which represents 0.1713% of the [[Minas Gerais]] territory, 0.1087% of the area of the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast region of Brazil]] and 0.0118% of the whole Brazilian territory.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Confederação Nacional dos Municípios (CMN) |title=Dados gerais Andrelândia - MG |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100131031 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210616165752/https://www.cnm.org.br/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100131031 |archive-date=2021-06-16 |access-date=2010-04-13}}</ref> The relief of Andrelândia is very rugged, corresponding geomorphologically to the Desiccated Plateau of the Upper Rio Grande, the Compartment of the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]] and the [[Aiuruoca River]] Depression, in the so-called Southern Minas Plateau. The confluence of its waters to the Rio Grande is defined between the north-left slope of the Mantiqueira Mountains range and the south of the municipality. The maximum [[altitude]] is 1 535 m. at Natureza Mountains and the minimum altitude is 934 m at Aiuruoca River; the headquarters is at an altitude of 1 000 m.<ref>{{Cite web |last=CityBrazil |title=Relevo |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/geral_detalhe.php?cat=4 |access-date=2010-05-07}}</ref> In the municipality, the relief varies between hilly and undulating, where about 20% of the territory is flat, 20% of the land is hilly, and the remaining 60% is a sea of hills and mountains.<ref name=":0" />

Andrelândia is located in the Mesoregion of Southeastern Minas and Microregion of Andrelândia, with an area of approximately 1,000&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipality is bordered to the north by [[Madre de Deus de Minas]] and [[Piedade do Rio Grande]]; to the northeast by [[Santana do Garambéu]]; to the east by [[Lima Duarte, Minas Gerais|Lima Duarte]]; to the southeast by [[Bom Jardim de Minas]]; to the south by [[Arantina]], [[Liberdade, Minas Gerais|Liberdade]] and [[Seritinga]]; to the southwest by [[Serranos]] and to the northwest by [[São Vicente de Minas|Sao Vicente de Minas]]. Andrelândia is 299 kilometers away from [[Belo Horizonte]], in an axis almost equidistant from [[São João del-Rei|São Joao Del-Rei]], [[Barbacena]], [[Juiz de Fora]] and [[Caxambu]], which allows it to be located, for tourism references, in a delta between the ''Vertentes'' Region, ''Zona da Mata-Sul'' ([[English language|English]]: Forest South Zone) and ''Circuito das Águas'' (English: Water Circuit).<ref name=":0" />

=== Geographic region ===
According to the most modern geographic classification (2017) of [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], Andrelândia is a municipality of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Juiz de Fora]], in the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Juiz de Fora]].<ref>{{Cite Q|Q112576597}}</ref>

=== Hydrography ===
Its waters all flow northward, towards the Rio Grande Valley, forming the headwaters of the [[Río de la Plata Basin|Platine Basin]], through the following courses, which are the main ones: the [[Rio Grande (Paraná River tributary)|Rio Grande]], which northizes the municipality, coming from Bom Jardim de Minas towards Lima Duarte, Santana do Garambéu, Piedade do Rio Grande and Madre de Deus de Minas and receives directly, as tributaries, a multitude of streams, and the [[Capivari River (Tietê River tributary)|Capivari River]] that is born in Andrelândia and flows into the border of Santana do Garambéu with Piedade do Rio Grande.<ref name=":6">{{Cite web |last=CityBrazil |title=Hidrografia |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/geral_detalhe.php?cat=5 |access-date=2010-05-07 }}{{Dead link|date=September 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

On the other side of the municipality is the [[Aiuruoca River]], calmer and with muddy waters, coming from Serranos, through a little uneven region, which has as its most voluminous affluent, by the right margin, the Turvo Grande River, after receiving the waters of the Turvo Pequeno River, also the Barra, Sardinha and Vacas creeks, besides many streams. The Aiuruoca River flows into the Rio Grande, outside the lands of Andrelândia, into the large artificial lake of the Camargos Hydroelectric Power Plant.<ref name=":6" />

=== Climate ===
The climate of Andrelândia is characterized as [[subhumid temperate climate]] (type Cwb according to [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]),<ref>{{Cite web |last=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification. |title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120524200847/http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-date=2012-05-24 |access-date=2010-11-22 |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health}}</ref> with reduced rainfall in the winter and an average annual temperature around 19&nbsp;°C, with dry and cold winters, often with frost in some areas, and humid summers with higher temperatures.<ref>{{Cite web |last=CityBrazil |title=Clima/Temperatura |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/geral_detalhe.php?cat=3 |access-date=2010-05-07 }}{{Dead link|date=August 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> January and February are the warmest months of the year, and June and July the coldest. Fall and spring are transition seasons. The annual rainfall is more than 1,500 millimeters (mm), with December being the month with the highest precipitation,<ref name=":23">{{Cite web |last=Climate-Data |title=Clima: Andrelândia |url=http://pt.climate-data.org/location/176072/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150803001530/http://pt.climate-data.org/location/176072/ |archive-date=2015-08-03 |access-date=2015-08-02}}</ref> which can occur in the form of rain and, on some occasions, hail.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Chuva de granizo assusta moradores de cidades mineiras |url=http://clickcarangola.com.br/site/28/10/2011/chuva-de-granizo-assusta-moradores-de-cidades-mineiras/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150803002526/http://clickcarangola.com.br/site/28/10/2011/chuva-de-granizo-assusta-moradores-de-cidades-mineiras/ |archive-date=2015-08-03 |access-date=2015-08-02 |publisher=Click Carangola |publication-date=2011-10-28}}</ref>

=== Fauna and Flora ===
The native vegetation of the municipality belongs to the [[Atlantic Forest]] domain ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]: ''Mata Atlântica''), where trees such as [[trumpet tree]]s, [[Dalbergia nigra|Brazilian rosewood]], lenten trees, [[Araucaria angustifolia|candelabra trees]], and cedars stand out. The soil is acidic and with little organic matter, together with the dry climate, do not favor the quick growth of vegetation. The main economic activity in the rural area is dairy farming, and the annual burning of pastures is the usual management practice. As a consequence, the region has a high rate of destruction of its original vegetation cover.<ref name=":9">{{Cite web |last=NPA - Núcleo de Pesquisas Arqueológicas do Alto Rio Grande |title=A Criação do Parque Arqueológico da Serra de Santo Antônio |url=http://www.npa.org.br/NPA_parque_arqueologico.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161200/http://www.npa.org.br/NPA_parque_arqueologico.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2011-07-06 |access-date=2010-07-28}}</ref>

The lack of forests and the poverty of the vegetation reduce the fauna to small rodents, very few mammals and wild animals, and small birds. Species of wolves, foxes, field dogs, [[ocelot]]s, [[Wildcat|wild cats]], [[deer]], [[coati]]s, [[paca]], [[capybara]] and [[marmoset]]s can still be found, although in much reduced numbers, thanks to the invasion of their habitat by man. Predatory hunting has also contributed greatly to the disappearance or reduction of certain wild animals. The deer, the [[otter]], the coati, the [[porcupine]], and the capybara are examples of this: they were very common in the region, but are about to become extinct due to predatory hunting. The [[tinamou]], the [[toucan]] and the [[Penelope (bird)|penelope]] may be included in the list of birds doomed to extinction. Of the birds that inhabit the region, the largest are the [[seriema]], the [[vulture]], the [[hawk]], the wild duck, the [[teal]], the ''paturi'', the white [[heron]] and the [[stork]]. The waters of Andrelândia, free of excessive pollution due to the absence of industry, are very good for amateur fishing. The most common fish in its streams and rivers are the ''[[Astyanax (fish)|astyanax]]'' and its many varieties, the [[Phoebetria|sooty albatrosses]] or ''piaba'', the ''mandi'' and its relatives ([[Willow|sallows]], [[catfish]] and [[frogfish]]), the ''tabarana'', the ''capinheiro'', the ''timburé'', the ''pirapetinga'', the [[gilded catfish]], the ''traíra'', the [[Pterophyllum|angelfish]] and the [[tilapia]], the last three, present in the natural lagoons and artificial dams.<ref name=":9" />

To avoid greater environmental problems, the city government, together with other organizations and institutions in the municipality, has been organizing several environmental awareness campaigns, especially in the city's schools. Some, besides stimulating reading, also aim to make students aware of their responsibility for the environment in which they live.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Projeto escolar estimula leitura e discute meio ambiente |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=653 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210194434/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=653 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-21 |publication-date=2010-04-19}}</ref>

=== Religion ===
[[File:Igreja de São Benedito - Andrelândia.JPG|thumb|200x200px|Church of Saint Benedict, located in the Areão neighborhood]]
[[Catholic Church|Catholicism]] in Andrelândia not only interferes in its socio-political-administrative life, but has also accompanied its history since its origins. [[André da Silveira]] felt the need to build a chapel where people could gather, together with their families, to participate in the liturgical acts of the Catholic faith. The chapel of Our Lady of Porto do Turvo, as it had been baptized, was blessed in 1755, by the vicar of [[Aiuruoca]], Father Francisco de Cerqueira Campos.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Almeida |first=Paulo César de |title=Andrelândia Vultos & Fatos |year=1994 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=94}}</ref>

There are several religious manifestations present in the city. Although in recent years the growth of other religious beliefs has been noticed, the municipality still has an eminently Catholic social matrix and 89% of the population shares this faith.<ref name=":11">{{Cite web |title=Religião |url=http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/municipioslista.php?a=search&value=1&SearchField=AnyField&SearchOption=Contains&SearchFor=Andrel%E2ndia |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210164725/http://www.mai.org.br/tabelas/municipios/municipioslista.php?a=search&value=1&SearchField=AnyField&SearchOption=Contains&SearchFor=Andrel%E2ndia |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2012-05-24}}</ref> The city of Andrelândia is located in the most Catholic country in the world in absolute numbers. The Church had its legal status recognized by the federal government in October 2009,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Cristiane Agostine |title=Senado aprova acordo com o Vaticano |url=http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/mat/2009/10/08/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-767959739.asp |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120629125103/http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/mat/2009/10/08/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-767959739.asp |archive-date=2012-06-29 |access-date=2010-07-20 |website=O Globo |publication-date=2009-10-08}}</ref> even though Brazil is currently an officially [[Secularity|secular]] state.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fernando Fonseca de Queiroz |title=Brasil: Estado laico e a inconstitucionalidade da existência de símbolos religiosos em prédios públicos |url=https://jus.com.br/artigos/8519/brasil-estado-laico-e-a-inconstitucionalidade-da-existencia-de-simbolos-religiosos-em-predios-publicos |access-date=2022-02-11 |website=Jus Navigandi}}</ref>

According to 2010 census data,<ref name=":11" /> the population of Andrelândia is composed of:

===== Catholic missionary =====
The [[African Americans|African American]] priest [[Martin de Porres Ward]] was missionary in Andrelândia until his death, in 1999. Ward was given the title of honorary citizen in 1995. The bishop of the Roman Catholic Diocese of São João del-Rei, [[José Eudes Campos do Nascimento]], gave start to the process of recognition of santity of Ward in 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |date=2022-07-27 |title=African-American Catholic priest who served in Brazil could soon be on road to sainthood |url=https://www.blackcatholicmessenger.com/fr-martin-maria-de-porres-ward-ofm-conv-announcement/ |access-date=2023-05-22 |website=Black Catholic Messenger |language=en}}</ref>

== Politics ==

=== Administration ===
According to the [[Constitution of Brazil|1988 Constitution]], Andrelândia is located in a presidential federal republic inspired by the American model. However, the Brazilian legal system follows the [[Germanic law|Roman-Germanic]] tradition of [[positive law]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Organization of American States (OAS) |title=The Brazilian Legal System |url=http://www.oas.org/juridico/mla/en/bra/en_bra-int-des-ordrjur.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181007050843/http://www.oas.org/juridico/mla/en/bra/en_bra-int-des-ordrjur.html |archive-date=2018-10-07 |access-date=2010-07-13 |language=en}}</ref> where municipal administration takes place through the [[Executive (government)|executive]] and [[Legislature|legislative]] branches.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Flávio Henrique M. Lima |title=O Poder Público Municipal à frente da obrigação constitucional de criação do sistema de controle interno |url=http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Paul%EDnia&state=sp |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120908113231/http://www.leismunicipais.com.br/cgi-local/orglaw.pl?city=Paul%EDnia&state=sp |url-status=dead |archive-date=2012-09-08 |access-date=2010-08-13 |publication-date=2006-02-08}}</ref>
[[File:Prefeitura e Câmara Municipal de Andrelândia.JPG|left|thumb|Headquarters of the [[Town hall|City Hall]] and [[Municipal council|City Council]]]]
Before 1930, the municipalities were run by the presidentes of the town councils, also called executive agents. Municipal powers were separated into [[Executive (government)|executive]] and [[Legislature|legislative]] only after the [[Revolution of 1930]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Câmara Municipal de Bragança Paulista |title=Câmara Municipal de Bragança Paulista |url=http://www.camarabp.sp.gov.br/conteudo/camara-municipal-da-estancia-de-braganca-paulista- |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120723004142/http://www.camarabp.sp.gov.br/conteudo/camara-municipal-da-estancia-de-braganca-paulista- |url-status=dead |archive-date=2012-07-23 |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref> In 14 terms of office, 16 mayors passed through the mayoralty. In recent years the position was held by Samuel Isac Fonseca, of the [[Brazilian Social Democracy Party]] (PSDB), elected in Brazil's municipal elections in 2008 with 60.71% of the valid votes (4,624 votes)<ref>{{Cite web |last=Terra |title=Andrelândia MG - Apuração 1º turno |url=http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/40550/index.shtml#prefeito |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121221182808/http://apuracao.terra.com.br/2008/1turno/mg/40550/index.shtml#prefeito |archive-date=2012-12-21 |access-date=2010-08-13 |publication-date=2008-10-06}}</ref> and reelected in the 2012 elections with 45.02% of the intentions (3,392 votes). Since the municipality has less than 200,000 voters, there was no second round.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Eleições 2012 |title=Samuel 45 |url=http://www.eleicoes2012.info/samuel-psdb-45/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130413000517/http://www.eleicoes2012.info/samuel-psdb-45/ |archive-date=2013-04-13 |access-date=2013-01-05 |publication-date=2012-10-07}}</ref>

The legislative branch consists of the [[Municipal council|city council]], composed of nine councilmen elected for four-year terms (in compliance with the provisions of Article 29 of the 1988 Constitution)<ref>{{Cite web |last=DJI |title=Constituição Federal - CF - 1988 / Art. 29 |url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120629125100/http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2012-06-29 |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref> and is composed as follows:<ref>{{Cite web |last=Eleições 2012 |title=Candidatos a Vereador Andrelândia/MG |url=http://www.eleicoes2012.info/candidatos-vereador-andrelandia-mg/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20130412232537/http://www.eleicoes2012.info/candidatos-vereador-andrelandia-mg/ |archive-date=2013-04-12 |access-date=2013-01-05 |publication-date=2012-10-07}}</ref> three seats from the Brazilian Social Democracy Party (PSDB), two seats from the [[Brazilian Labour Party (current)|Brazilian Labor Party]] (PTB), two seats from the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]] (PT); one seat from the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement|Brazilian Democratic Movement Party]] (PMDB); and one seat from the [[Green Party (Brazil)|Green Party]] (PV). It is up to the house to elaborate and vote on fundamental laws for the administration and the Executive, especially the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law). The municipality of Andrelândia is governed by an organic law, which was revised to adapt to the Constitutional Amendment no. 101 of 2000.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura de Andrelândia |title=Câmara |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=161 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110919050156/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=161 |archive-date=2011-09-19 |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref> The city is also the seat of a [[judicial district]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tribunal de Justiça do Estado de Minas Gerais |title=Lista de Comarcas do Estado de Minas Gerais |url=http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100825194853/http://www.tjmg.jus.br/juridico/sf/comarcas.html |url-status=dead |archive-date=2010-08-25 |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref> According to the TRE-MG ([[Regional Electoral Courts|Regional Electoral Court]] of Minas Gerais), the municipality had in 2006 9,315 voters.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Representação Política 2006 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=partpol&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref>

=== "Deer" and "Crabs ===
In the political history of the town, two local parties stood out: "Deer" and "Crabs" The political rivalry between them in Andrelândia has already exceeded a century of existence and remains alive in the minds of most of the inhabitants. Their origins are closely related to two great figures in history: Colonel [[José Bonifácio de Azevedo]] on the one hand and [[Visconde de Arantes]] on the other.<ref name=":12">The Turvo Law Judge, folheto impresso pelo Minas Gerais State em 1899.</ref>

For a long time, Viscount Arantes was the absolute and undisputed leader of Turvo, relying on the totality of its electorate. In 1887 the first rebels and the first subtraction in his votes appeared. In 1890 hundreds of votes appeared against the Viscount, the second and more significant subtraction. In 1894 the supporters of the PRT outnumbered the supporters of the Viscount's party. Tradition has it that one of the majorities of the Viscount's faction, unhappy with this defeat, made the following remark in public: "It is not possible that our votes have diminished so much! We are going backwards! We look like Crabs!". This fact was humorously glossed over in the newspapers of the time. On the other hand, the PRT members were euphorically celebrating the massive vote they managed to get. A real leap, as the deer does when it is being chased. In 1889 José Bonifácio de Azevedo, João Zuquim de Figueiredo Neves and José Ribeiro Salgado, three of the major PRT's leaders, founded in Bahia Farm, municipality of Turvo, the second butter factory in Brazil, called "Deer Butter Factory - Azevedo & Cia", which established the nickname of the supporters of Colonel José Bonifácio's party.<ref name=":12" />

Even today, although there are countless political parties of imposing acronyms in the municipality, in the municipal elections they irreducibly form two blocks: the "deer" and the "crabs", which rival. Such a struggle, however, lost its meaning long ago due to the city's lack of great resources, as the division of its community leads it to stagnation and retrogression.<ref name=":12" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Renato Grandelle |title=Andrelândia, onde os grupos políticos dos 'veados' e dos 'caranguejos' dão as cartas |url=http://oglobo.globo.com/cidades/mat/2010/08/14/andrelandia-onde-os-grupos-politicos-dos-veados-dos-caranguejos-dao-as-cartas-917396997.asp |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100817181848/http://oglobo.globo.com/cidades/mat/2010/08/14/andrelandia-onde-os-grupos-politicos-dos-veados-dos-caranguejos-dao-as-cartas-917396997.asp |archive-date=2010-08-17 |access-date=2010-01-13 |publication-date=2010-08-14}}</ref>

== Subdivisions ==
The municipality of Andrelândia does not have administrative regions. When it was founded it was composed of five districts: Turvo (Headquarters), Arantes, Bom Jardim, Madre de Deus do Rio Grande and São Vicente Ferrer. However, as the years went by, all of them became cities, leaving to Andrelândia only the headquarters, currently its only district.<ref name=":1" />

Some of the poorest neighborhoods are Santos Dumont and Vila Zamoura, where the city government needed to build houses for victims of a heavy rainfall at the end of 2008. Other less developed neighborhoods are also benefiting from the paving of their streets, such as Nossa Senhora de Fátima, Cruzeiro and Santa Clara.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Prefeitura inicia calçamento nos bairros |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=920 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210193648/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=920 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2020-08-22 |publication-date=2009-08-19}}</ref> One of the main neighborhoods is Rosário, which, besides being one of the oldest, is where the municipal unit of the [[Programa Saúde da Família|Family Health Program]] (PSF) is located, which also benefits the neighborhoods Vila Mariana, Serrinha, São José, Cruzeiro, Fábrica, Pedreira, Belo Horizonte I and II, and Santa Clara.<ref name=":15">{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Saúde |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=110 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210194433/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=110 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-07-22}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The [[Gross domestic product|Gross Domestic Product]] - GDP of Andrelândia is the second largest of its microregion,<ref name=":13">{{Cite web|year=2007 |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220616212922/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/?tabela=pibmunic&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%2525EF%2525BF%2525BDndia |archive-date=2022-06-16 |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref> with emphasis on the area of services. In the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] data of 2008, the municipality had R$87 812,889 in its Gross Domestic Product. Of this total, R$3,659 were from taxes on products net of subsidies.<ref name=":13" /> The GDP per capita was R$7,096.56.<ref name=":13" />

Agriculture has reasonable importance in Andrelândia. Of the entire gross domestic product of the city, R$16,990 is the gross added value of agriculture and cattle raising.<ref name=":13" /> The most important crops are rice, beans and corn.<ref name=":21b">{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Produção Agrícola Municipal - Cereais, Leguminosas e Oleaginosas |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=pamclo_2007&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref> According to the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, in 2008 the municipality had a herd of 25 253 cattle, 3 093 pigs, 1 065 horses, 98 sheep, and 21 059 birds, among these 12 465 chickens, 8 594 roosters, chickens and chicks and 161 quails.<ref name=":14">{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Andrelândia - MG |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=prodpec2008&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref> In 2008, the city produced 18,709 liters of milk from 10,274 cows. There were 83,000 dozen hen eggs, 2,000 dozen quail eggs, 3,319 kilos of honeybee, and 40 kilos of wool.<ref name=":14" />

The city's permanent farming produces mainly coffee (358 tons), grapes (140 tons), guava (88 tons) and tangerines (64 tons).<ref>{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Lavoura Permanente 2008 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavperm2008&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref> In temporary farming, mainly corn (15,600 tons), beans (960 tons) and sugar cane (1,600 tons) are produced, the latter being cited as the main agricultural product in Brazil, being cultivated since the time of colonization of the country.<ref>{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Lavoura Temporária 2008 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=lavtemp2008&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Economia BR |title=As exportações brasileiras: o agronegócio |url=http://www.economiabr.defesabr.com/Eco/Eco_exportacao_agro_produtos.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100211212710/http://www.economiabr.defesabr.com/Eco/Eco_exportacao_agro_produtos.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2010-02-11 |access-date=2010-08-29}}</ref>

=== Secondary Sector ===
[[File:Banco do Brasil em Andrelândia-MG.JPG|thumb|200x200px|Banco do Brasil branch in Andrelândia]]
Industry is still very incipient in the municipality, even though it is starting to show signs of improvement, it is mainly limited to the production of dairy products. R$8,352 of the municipal GDP are from the gross added value of industry (secondary sector).<ref name=":13" /> In 2000, the main industrial companies classified according to the number of employees were Flor de Andrelândia Dairy products LTDA (manufacture of food products and beverages) and Seixas Pre-molded Industry and Commerce LTDA (manufacture of non-metallic mineral products). In the same year, 816 people were employed in the industrial sector.<ref name=":0" />

=== Tertiary sector ===
R$43,512 of the municipal GDP is from services (tertiary).<ref name=":13" /> The tertiary sector is currently the main generator of Andrelândia's GDP. According to the IBGE, in 2008 the city had 402 companies and 2,781 workers, of which 1,617 were total employees and 1,164 were salaried employees. Salaries along with other remunerations totaled 9,584 ''[[Brazilian real|reais]]'' and the average monthly salary of the whole municipality was 1.6 minimum wages.<ref>{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Estatísticas do Cadastro Central de Empresas |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=empresas&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220616212943/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/favicon.ico |archive-date=2022-06-16 |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref>

The commercial movement in Andrelândia has expanded greatly since the early 1990s. It was a visible sign of the city's economic growth, modernization, and consequently, progress, despite the slow pace with which it developed before the 1990s. Currently the city's commerce still attracts consumers from neighboring towns for purchases of basic necessities, furniture and household appliances, with only the absence of car dealerships and other highly complex services.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Andrelândia.net |year=2008 |title=Comércio & serviços |url=http://www.andrelandia.net/comserv.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100620083511/http://www.andrelandia.net/comserv.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2010-06-20 |access-date=2010-07-04}}</ref>

=== Technology ===
Some companies in the technology area, mainly service providers, have established their bases in Andrelândia as internet providers and developers of online systems, from where they serve companies from all over [[Brazil]], [[Europe]] and [[Latin America]], such as Dream Net, Futura Web, and others.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Geraldo Almeida |year=2011 |title=Migração Futura Web para Andrelândia |url=http://futuraweb.net.br/2010/index_empresa.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110408103842/http://www.futuraweb.net.br/2010/index_empresa.php |archive-date=2011-04-08 |access-date=2011-05-29}}</ref>

== Urban structure ==

Of this total, 2,569 were owned properties, of which 2,551 were already paid for (74.11%), 18 were being acquired (0.52%), 349 were rented (10.14%), 507 properties were given away, of which 203 were given away by an employer (5.90%), 304 were given away in another way (8.83%) and 17 were given away in another way (0.49%).<ref>{{Cite web |last=Confederação nacional de municípios (CMN) |title=Infra-Estrutura - Domicílios Particulares Permanentes e Moradores |url=http://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100131031 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210616163520/https://www.cnm.org.br/infra/mu_infra_domicilio.asp?iIdMun=100131031 |archive-date=2021-06-16 |access-date=2010-07-23}}</ref>

In security, Andrelândia does not have such high rates of serious crime occurrences because it does not have many inhabitants. === Education ===
Andrelândia has schools in all regions of the municipality. Due to intense urbanization, rural residents have easy access to schools in nearby urban neighborhoods. Education in state schools has a higher level than in municipal schools, but the municipality is creating programs to make state public education better, in order to achieve better results in the Basic Education Development Index.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Governo Federal |title=Índice de Desenvolvimento da Educação Básica (IDEB) - Resultados e Metas |url=http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071009195109/http://ideb.inep.gov.br/Site/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=2007-10-09 |access-date=2010-07-22}}</ref> The municipality, in 2008, had approximately 2,676 enrollments, 167 teachers and 16 schools in the public and private networks.<ref name=":22">{{Cite web|year=2008 |title=Ensino, matrículas, docentes e rede escolar 2008 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=educa&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-22}}</ref>

In 2007, the administration expanded the infrastructure of the schools, which currently have improved service capacity for students. As a result, new transfers and investments from the federal and state governments arrived in the municipality, such as the construction of the first municipal daycare center, the expansion of the João Narciso de Oliveira Municipal School, the creation of the Agroforestry Technical School, and the construction of multi-sports courts.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Escola Técnica Agroflorestal inicia nova turma |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=850 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130927212727/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=850 |archive-date=2013-09-27 |access-date=2010-07-22 |publication-date=2009-07-21}}</ref>

=== Health ===
[[File:Hospital Municipal Dr. José Gustavo Alves.JPG|thumb|Doctor José Gustavo Alves Municipal Hospital]]
Until 2000, before the administration of [[Francisco Rivelli]] (2001-2008), the city had two units of the Family Health Program, both without dental care, one in the city center and the other in the Rosário neighborhood, but the service was not enough to serve the entire population. In view of this, a third unit was built in the Santos Dumont neighborhood, which today serves about 4,000 people who live in the Santos Dumont, Vila Zamoura, Chácara, Areão, and Santa Tereza neighborhoods. To further optimize the service to the population, renovations and expansions were made to the two existing units, which needed urgent maintenance.<ref name=":15" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=UBS do Bairro Rosário é inaugurada |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=806 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210193906/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=806 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-07-22 |publication-date=2009-07-06}}</ref>

The inauguration of the second block of the Doctor José Gustavo Alves Mixed Health Unit, which occurred on October 26, 2007, alleviated the needs of the population of Andrelândia in low and medium complexity care, urgencies and emergencies, besides care related to pregnancy and maternity. Today there are few cases referred to other locations and the Mixed Health Unit has the capacity to solve most of the pathologies that need medical care, including providing 24-hour care.<ref name=":15" /> According to IBGE data in 2005, the city had seven health care facilities, three of them private and four municipal, including hospitals, emergency rooms, health posts, and dental services. The city has 57 beds for hospitalization in health facilities.<ref>{{Cite web|year=2005 |title=Serviços de Saúde 2005 |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/csv.php?tabela=assismed&codmun=310280&nomemun=Andrel%E2ndia |access-date=2010-07-22}}</ref>

=== Services and Communications ===
[[File:Praça Gabriel Ribeiro Salgado à noite.jpg|left|thumb|190x190px|Nighttime lighting at Gabriel Ribeiro Salgado Square: the power supply is provided by [[CEMIG|Minas Gerais Energy Company]] (Cemig)]]
The water supply service, as in most of the state of [[Minas Gerais]], is provided by the Minas Gerais Sanitation Company (Copasa). Sewage collection, on the other hand, is done by the city government itself.<ref name=":0" />

In Andrelândia, as in most of the old cities of the state, the [[Castor oil|castor oil lamp]] was used for the internal lighting of the residences and, in the streets, it was used only on festive days, when all the windows were illuminated. The oldest reference to a public lighting system in this city is from 1881.<ref>"with lamps of fifty candles, lit from dusk until dawn, placed in one hundred and twenty streetlamps spread throughout the streets of the city". Privilege contract for the supply of light and power signed between the Turvense Light and Power Company and the Turvo City Council on 05.02.1918. Book 19 of the Notary's Office of the 2nd Notary of Andrelândia.</ref> On November 22, 1927, the Turvense Light and Power Company was bought for 90 ''[[Portuguese real|contos de réis]]'' by the South Minas Gerais Electricity Company, which extended the transmission line to [[Bom Jardim de Minas]], passing through [[Arantina]]. Later it was replaced by the [[CEMIG|Minas Gerais Energy Company]] (Cemig) which, even today, as in practically the whole state, is responsible for the city's electricity supply service. In 2003 there were 4 378 consumers and 7 173 289 KWh of energy were consumed.<ref name=":0" />

There are still [[Dial-up Internet access|dial-up]] and [[broadband]] ([[ADSL]]) internet services being offered by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|ISPs]]. The area code ([[direct distance dialing]]) of Andrelândia is 35<ref>{{Cite web |last=Guiatel |title=Minas Gerais |url=http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_mg1.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100916051134/http://www.guiatel.com.br/info_mg1.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2010-09-16 |access-date=2010-07-23}}</ref> and the [[ZIP Code|ZIP code]] of the city is 37300–000.<ref>{{Cite web |last=[[Correios]] |title=CEP de cidades brasileiras |url=http://www.correios.com.br/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150222175041/http://www2.correios.com.br/ |archive-date=2015-02-22 |access-date=2010-07-23}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=CityBrazil |title=Andrelândia |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/index.php |access-date=2010-07-23 }}{{Dead link|date=August 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> On January 12, 2009 the city started to be served by telephone number portability, just like the other cities of area code 35. Portability is a service that makes it possible to change the operator without the need to change the number of the cell phone.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Terra |title=Portabilidade começa em cidades de MG, ES e AC |url=http://tecnologia.terra.com.br/interna/0,,OI3301525-EI4796,00-Portabilidade+comeca+em+cidades+de+MG+ES+e+AC.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081110183419/http://tecnologia.terra.com.br/interna/0,,OI3301525-EI4796,00-Portabilidade+comeca+em+cidades+de+MG+ES+e+AC.html |archive-date=2008-11-10 |access-date=2010-07-23 |publication-date=2008-11-02}}</ref> In 2001 there was only one radio station according to the Minas Gerais Association of Radio and TV and Telecommunications of Minas Gerais S.A, but this number has increased over the years. Currently the main ones are Andrelândia [[FM broadcasting|FM]] and Cultura FM Radio.<ref name=":0" />

=== Transports ===
[[File:Centro de Andrelândia.jpg|thumb|[[Level crossing]] in the City Center]]
As the city does not have an abundance of rivers, it does not have much tradition in waterway transport. The closest airport to Andrelândia is Major-Brigadeiro Doorgal Borges, located in [[Barbacena]], 121&nbsp;km away from the city.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Aeroportos do Brasil |title=Aeroporto Major Brigadeiro Doorgal Borges - Barbacena MG |url=http://www.aeroportosdobrasil.com.br/minas_gerais_mg/barbacena_mg/aeroporto_barbacena_mg.php |access-date=2010-08-31 |archive-date=2010-07-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100729131954/http://www.aeroportosdobrasil.com.br/minas_gerais_mg/barbacena_mg/aeroporto_barbacena_mg.php |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipal fleet in the year 2009 was 2,414 vehicles, being 1,648 cars, 120 trucks, four tractor trucks, 198 pickup trucks, 26 minibuses, 378 motorcycles, twenty-four scooters and sixteen buses.

The first roads in the area of the municipality appeared in the beginning of the 20th century, being only a few stones of roads that cut the territory. During the administration of Gabriel Ribeiro Salgado (1923-1926), the construction of the first road for automobiles in Andrelândia began, connecting the city to [[São João del-Rei]]. Currently, the only paved road that serves the city is BR-494 which, passing through [[Arantina]], joins [[BR-267]] ([[Juiz de Fora]]-[[Caxambu]]). BR-494 was paved thanks to the determination of President [[João Figueiredo|João Baptista de Oliveira Figueiredo]], who ordered the Railway Engineering Company S/A (ENGEFER) to proceed with the paving work, when, on April 30, 1981, he was in Andrelândia to inspect the works of the Steel Railroad. A small unpaved stretch until [[São Vicente de Minas]] connects the city to the Presidente Tancredo Neves Highway, paved in almost its entire length, connecting the Historical Circuit to the Water Circuit.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Souza Miranda |first=Marcos Paulo de |title=Aspectos Históricos da Terra de André |year=1996 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=73}}</ref>

Visconde de Arantes provided Andrelândia with the passage of the [[Estrada de Ferro Oeste de Minas|West of Minas Railroad]], which linked São João del-Rei to the Sítio station, currently the city of [[Antônio Carlos, Minas Gerais|Antônio Carlos]]. On June 14, 1914, the first locomotive of the West arrived in the city. From the 1950s on, the railroad system in the region began to feel, as in other parts of the state, the effects of the greater expansion of highways, which absorbed almost all the traffic of passengers and light freight, leaving the railroad practically limited to the transport of [[ore]].<ref>{{Cite book |last=Souza Miranda |first=Marcos Paulo de |title=Aspectos Históricos da Terra de André |year=1996 |location=Juiz de Fora - MG |pages=76–78}}</ref>

Andrelândia is cut by the Trunk Line of the old West of Minas Railroad, which connects the city to [[Araguari]], northbound, and to the [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] cities of Barra Mansa and [[Angra dos Reis]], southbound.<ref>{{Cite web |title=RMV - Tronco -- Estações Ferroviárias do Estado de Minas Gerais |url=https://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_tronco/rmv_tronco.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20030808102314/http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_tronco/rmv_tronco.htm |archive-date=2003-08-08 |access-date=2020-08-21 |website=www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br}}</ref>

Through the railroad, besides transporting ores, for many years the city was also served by passenger transportation. Until August 1996, the ''Trem Mineiro'' of the old [[RFFSA]], operated its trips on weekends, always presenting a great demand of passengers in the connection with [[Ribeirão Vermelho]] and [[Barra Mansa]], causing an intense movement of them in the railway station of Andrelândia.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Andrelândia -- Estações Ferroviárias do Estado de Minas Gerais |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_tronco/andrelandia.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200806062347/http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/rmv_tronco/andrelandia.htm |archive-date=2020-08-06 |access-date=2020-08-21 |website=www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br}}</ref>

After the privatization of the railroad that same year, the long-distance passenger trains stopped circulating in the region and since then, there is only freight train traffic.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Barra Mansa-Lavras -- Trens de passageiros do Brasil |url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/trens_rj/bmansa-lavras.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190913074016/http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/trens_rj/bmansa-lavras.htm |archive-date=2019-09-13 |access-date=2020-08-21 |website=www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br}}</ref>

== Culture and leisure ==

=== Royal Road ===
[[File:Escudo da Estrada Real.png|left|thumb|117x117px|Shield for the "Estrada Real" Project]]
An important stretch of the Royal Road, but still little researched and explored for tourism purposes, is the Trade Path or Black River Path, a variant that was opened around the year 1813 to facilitate the transit of traders and muleteers between [[São João del-Rei]] and [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. This route, which started from New Path in a stretch between the present municipalities of [[Paty do Alferes]] and [[Paraíba do Sul]], headed towards [[Valença, Rio de Janeiro|Valença]] in the state of Rio de Janeiro, then went through the old mining villages of [[Rio Preto, Minas Gerais|Rio Preto]], [[Bom Jardim de Minas]], Turvo (now Andrelândia), [[Madre de Deus de Minas]], Santo Antônio do Rio das Mortes Pequeno and finally reached the village of São João del-Rei.<ref name=":17">{{Cite web |last=NPA - Núcleo de Pesquisas Arqueológicas do Alto Rio Grande |title=Estrada Real |url=http://www.npa.org.br/NPA_estrada_real.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161314/http://www.npa.org.br/NPA_estrada_real.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2011-07-06 |access-date=2010-07-23}}</ref> The Trade Path is a very important variant of the Royal Road and along it there is a huge and varied number of cultural and scenic attractions, besides several places for the practice of the so-called [[ecotourism]]: The beautiful waterfalls and trout breeding ponds in the region between Rio Preto and Bom Jardim de Minas; the colonial architecture, the archaeological sites, the typical sweets and cheese and the quality [[cachaça]] produced in the region of Andrelândia; the century-old farms and churches, the mountain ranges and the folkloric traditions of the Madre de Deus de Minas region.<ref name=":17" />

Recently a project of law included Andrelândia in the route of the Royal Road. To demarcate the location, several totems were placed in the municipality.<ref name=":17" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Andrelândia será incluída na rota comercial da Estrada Real |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=934 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706160249/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=934 |archive-date=2011-07-06 |access-date=2010-07-23 |publication-date=2009-08-18}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Estrada Real |title=Conheça um pouco o "Instituto Estrada Real", sua atuação e seus objetivos |url=http://www.estradareal.org.br/estra_real/index.asp |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101224185052/http://www.estradareal.org.br/estra_real/index.asp |archive-date=2010-12-24 |access-date=2010-07-23}}</ref>

=== Architecture ===
[[File:Igreja Matriz de Andrelândia.jpg|thumb|Entrance of the Mother Church of Andrelândia, Minas Gerais, Brazil]]
The colonial architectural complex of Andrelândia is significant, although it has already suffered very important losses such as the town hall and jail, the town house that belonged to the Baron of Cajurú, the "Passos", among many others. A serious problem is the decharacterization of many colonial houses due to the substitution of the primitive roof tiles by modern French tiles, forming a mixture of styles that has already occurred in the residences that belonged to Major Gustavo Ernesto Alves, to the Viscount of Arantes, and even with the oldest specimen of the city's colonial residential architecture, today represented by a simple remnant, with its worn stone sills denouncing the antiquity of its existence.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Patrimônio histórico e cultural |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=121#2 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210194335/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=121#2 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-14}}</ref>

The city's architecture is highlighted in its centennial mansions, through the actions of the city hall and other agencies, which are concerned with the preservation of the city's history. Among its old buildings, the churches and chapels stand out, many built in the [[baroque]] style.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Portal Descubraminas |title=Casarões do século XVIII |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/atracoes-turisticas/atrativos-diversos |access-date=2010-07-22 |website=CityBrazil }}{{Dead link|date=August 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> Below is a list of the churches:

* Church of [[Our Lady of Vendôme|Our Lady of Porto of the Eternal Salvation]]''':''' is a baroque-style Catholic church, built by André da Silveira in the center of the city. It was planned by architect Lucio Costa at the end of the 18th century. In 1890 Father Francisco Severo Malachias made the first change in the church's architecture; until that year it had no tower. A low tower was built, placed to the left of the building, which lasted until 1918. Between 1904 and 1913, some repairs were made, but only for conservation. In 1991, the last modifications were made: the replacement of the woodwork, renewal of the general painting, and some touch ups necessary for the conservation of the temple.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura de Andrelândia |year=2008 |title=Turismo religioso: Igreja Nossa Senhora do Porto da Eterna Salvação - Igreja Matriz |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=123#1 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706155959/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=123#1 |archive-date=2011-07-06 |access-date=2010-07-21}}</ref>
* Church of [[Our Lady of the Rosary]]: there is a lot of conjecture about the time when the church was built, but it is possible to believe that it didn't happen before 1817, when [[John VI of Portugal|D. João VI]], king of the [[United Kingdom of Portugal, Brazil and the Algarves]], who was staying in Rio de Janeiro with the royal family, fleeing the [[Peninsular War|Napoleonic invasions]] in Europe, granted permission for the slaves to build the church.<ref name=":18">{{Cite web |last=Portal Descubraminas |title=Atrativos Culturais |url=http://citybrazil.uol.com.br/mg/andrelandia/atracoes-turisticas/atrativos-culturais |access-date=2010-07-22 |website=CityBrazil }}{{Dead link|date=August 2023 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
* Church of [[Benedict of Nursia|Saint Benedict]]: is located in the Areão neighborhood. It was built under the guidance of Father José Tibúrcio and its history can be accurately known through the words of the vicar himself in a text that he prepared on the eve of the consecration of this temple. It was inaugurated on September 27, 1936, when the image of the patron saint, Saint Benedict, was transferred from the Mother Church to this church, where it remained until the chapel was torn down and the current church was built. It has modern architecture, imitating the welcoming gesture of Jesus Christ.<ref name=":18" />

=== Handicraft ===
Handicraft is one of the most spontaneous forms of cultural expression in Andrelândia. In various parts of the city, it is possible to find a differentiated handicraft production, made with regional raw materials and created according to the local culture and way of life. This diversity makes the local handicrafts rich and creative. The Association of Artisans of Andrelândia brings together several artisans from the region, making space available for making, exhibiting and selling their handmade products. They produce especially crocheted bedspreads and table runners, flowers produced with dry corn leaf, and pieces produced with looms, among others.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Andrelândia.net |title=Arte e Cultura |url=http://www.andrelandia.net/artecultura.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100913144039/http://www.andrelandia.net/artecultura.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2010-09-13 |access-date=2010-08-14}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Andrelândia.net |title=Artesanato |url=http://www.andrelandia.net/artesanato.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110723013830/http://www.andrelandia.net/artesanato.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2011-07-23 |access-date=2010-08-14}}</ref>
[[File:Fundação Guairá.JPG|left|thumb|240x240px|Guairá Foundation, home of handcrafted artifacts in Andrelândia]]
Usually these pieces are sold at fairs, exhibitions or craft stores. The "''Casa do Artesanato''" ([[English language|English]]: House of Handicraft) represents the point of exhibition and sale of various pieces produced by Andrelândia's artisans. An important sponsor of handicrafts in the municipality is the Social Assistance Reference Center, a project organized by the municipality involving families at risk, who work to strengthen family and community ties. It offers courses in handicrafts, cold porcelain and recycling projects using newspapers, garlic and onion peelings and vegetable fibers.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Inaugurada nova sede do CRAS |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=381 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090313032924/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=381 |archive-date=2009-03-13 |access-date=2010-08-14 |publication-date=2009-02-17}}</ref>

=== Music ===
The music of Brazil was formed mainly from the fusion of European and African elements, brought by the Portuguese colonizers and slaves respectively. The first records of consistent musical activity in Brazil come from the activity of the Jesuit priests, established in the country since 1549.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Berthold |first=Noel |title=A Escultura dos Sete Povos |year=1978 |location=Brasília |pages=26}}</ref> In Andrelândia, besides the most common musical styles in Brazil, classical music also stands out.<ref name=":19">{{Cite web |last=Aristides Ferreira Leite |title=Sociedade Andrelandense Musical São Pio X |url=http://www.andrelandia.net/bandasaopiox.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110723013601/http://www.andrelandia.net/bandasaopiox.htm |url-status=dead |archive-date=2011-07-23 |access-date=2010-08-24}}</ref>

In Andrelândia, throughout the years, several bands based in the city have stood out regionally, with emphasis on the classical style groups, such as the 15 de Agosto, Santa Cecília, the Musical Corporation "''Irmãos Leite''", besides the ''Andrelandense'' Musical Society Saint Pius X, today known as Saint Pius X Band, founded by maestro Rufino on March 4, 1955, and exists until today, being conducted by maestro Wilson Pereira. It was declared of Municipal Public Utility by Municipal Law No. 716 of October 14, 1985 and entity of State Public Utility, according to Law No. 9.203 of June 24, 1986. The corporation has always been present in various cultural, historical, civil and religious moments of Andrelândia and region.<ref name=":19" />

=== Events ===
[[File:Portão de entrada do Parque de Exposições de Andrelândia.jpg|thumb|200x200px|Entrance to the Exhibition Park of Andrelândia, where the city's main events are usually held]]
To stimulate the local socioeconomic development, the Andrelândia City Hall, together or not with local companies, invests in the parties and events segment. These parties often attract people from other cities, requiring a better infrastructure in the municipality and stimulating the professionalization of the sector, which is beneficial not only to tourists, but also to the entire population of the city. The activities take place throughout the year, especially during religious holidays.<ref name=":16" />

==== Religious celebrations ====
They are held during the periods of religious celebrations throughout the year. The main religious events in Andrelândia are the Feast of [[Saint Sebastian]] and the Feast of Kings, in January; Holy Week, in March or April; the Feast of [[Benedict of Nursia|Saint Benedict]], in May; ''Corpus Christi'', in June and the [[Patron saint|Patron Saint]]'s Feast, in August.<ref name=":16" />

==== ''Andrefolia'' ====
The Andrelândia [[Carnival]] is the most popular festival in the city. It is celebrated in the Visconde de Arantes Square, popularly known as the forum square, with ''matinês'' (parade of carnival blocks), music shows and the Saint Pius X Band. ''Andrefolia'' takes people through the streets and makes the revelers happy. The most traditional blocks in the municipality are: ''Nem Ligo do Areão'', ''Unidos do São Dimas'', ''Confetes ao Vento'', and ''Das Virgens''. In some editions, it has attracted more than 10,000 people.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Andrefolia registra recorde de público na região |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=1327 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130927212907/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=1327 |archive-date=2013-09-27 |access-date=2010-08-14 |publication-date=2010-02-26}}</ref><ref name=":16" />

==== Visconde de Arantes Commandery ====
Established by Municipal Law 1 311/2002, the [[Commandery]] honors annually, on July 20, the anniversary of the city's foundation, up to ten citizens in the political, business, social, and cultural fields, who have rendered exceptional services to the local community. The commandery ceremony brings together several local personalities.<ref name=":16" />

==== Farming and Cattle Raising Exhibition of Andrelândia ====
It is held annually on September 7 at the José Athaíde de Oliveira Exhibition Park. It is the largest festival in the city, with an average attendance of about 30,000 people. In the last few years, the structure of the Exhibition Park has been remodeled and expanded to receive a larger number of tourists and more exhibitors. The party lasts five days and has as its trademark, shows by big names in the Brazilian music scene.<ref name=":16" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Exposição de Andrelândia é sucesso de público |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=1019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210193011/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=1019 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-14 |publication-date=2009-09-15}}</ref>

=== Sports ===
As in most of the cities in the country, the most popular sport is soccer, despite its little tradition. In fact, only in schools is sport given its due value as integration and health, even then in a very restricted scope, in physical education classes. Throughout the year small soccer clubs in the city or region, in partnership with the city hall and with the sponsorship of local companies, organize amateur competitions, such as the Regional Soccer Championship, which is the championship with the greatest scope and breadth of participation. Usually, there are a total of 20 teams from several cities in the region.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=LAF promove 24º Campeonato Regional de Futebol |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=928 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210192658/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=928 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-13 |publication-date=2009-08-18}}</ref>

In schools, the city government, together with the State Secretariat of Sports and Youth (SEEJ) and the Municipal Secretariat of Industry, Commerce and Tourism organize several multi-sport events involving children and teenagers from several schools. The goal of these activities is social inclusion through sports, expanding the integral formation and ensuring access to sports, pedagogical, and recreational practices, in addition to health promotion and nutritional complementation.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Semana das Atividades Físicas agita Andrelândia |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=604 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210192833/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?p=604 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-13 |publication-date=2009-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Prefeitura |title=Indústria, Comércio, Turismo e Esportes |url=http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=176 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141210192835/http://www.andrelandia.mg.gov.br/?page_id=176 |archive-date=2014-12-10 |access-date=2010-08-13}}</ref>

=== Holidays ===
In Andrelândia, there are three municipal holidays, eight national holidays and four optional points. The municipal holidays are: ''Corpus Christi'', always held on the Thursday following [[Trinity|Holy Trinity]] Sunday,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Tribunal de Justiça de Minas Gerais (TJMG) |year=2010 |title=Calendário do Judiciário (junho) |url=http://www.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=null&mes=6&ano=2010&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120914114453/http://www.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=null&mes=6&ano=2010&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar |url-status=dead |archive-date=September 14, 2012 |access-date=2010-08-13 }}</ref> the anniversary of the emancipation of Andrelândia on July 20,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tribunal de Justiça de Minas Gerais |url=https://www8.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=28&mes=7&ano=2024&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=www8.tjmg.jus.br}}</ref> and the [[Assumption of Mary|Assumption of Our Lady]] on August 15.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Tribunal de Justiça de Minas Gerais |url=https://www8.tjmg.jus.br/servicos/gj/calendario/index.jsp?tipoFeriado=todos&comarca=28&mes=8&ano=2024&btn_pesquisar=Pesquisar |access-date=2024-08-14 |website=www8.tjmg.jus.br}}</ref> According to law Nº 9.093 of September 12, 1995, municipalities can have a maximum of four municipal holidays, including [[Good Friday]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado?
