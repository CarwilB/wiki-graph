'''Canelones''' is a [[Uruguay]]an [[Municipalities of Uruguay|municipality]] located in the [[Canelones Department|department of Canelones]]. Its seat is the city of [[Canelones, Uruguay|Canelones]].

== Location ==
The municipality is located in the central-western region of the department of Canelones, south of the creek Canelón Grande.

== Populated places in the municipality of Canelones ==
* [[Canelones, Uruguay|Canelones]] (department capital and municipality seat)
* [[Juanicó]]
* Barrio Remanso
* Parada Cabrera
* Villa Arejo
* Paso Palomeque
* Paso Espinosa
