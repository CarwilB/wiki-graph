'''Landero y Coss''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the central zone of the state, about 33&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 21.39 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|44|N|96|51|W|}}.

The municipality of Landero y Coss is delimited to the north-east by [[Misantla]] to the east by [[Chiconquiaco]],  to the south-east by [[Acatlán, Veracruz|Acatlán]], to the south by [[Miahuatlán (Veracruz)|Miahuatlán]] and to the west by [[Tonayan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30096a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120630100434/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30096a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2012-06-30|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Landero y Coss|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10}}</ref>

The weather in Landero y Coss is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

With a population of 1,543 inhabitants {{as of|2020|lc=y}}, Landero y Coss is the smallest municipality of Veracruz in terms of population.<ref name=2020CENSUSLANDERO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Landero-y-Coss.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Landero y Coss CENSUS]</ref> There are only 3 localities, all of them classified as rural: Landero y Coss, the municipal seat, contains more than 70% of the population (1,117 hab.). The other two localities are Buena Vista (421 hab.); and Raicero (5 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSLANDERO/>

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[coffee]] and [[green chile]].

==Culture==
In Landero y Coss, in June takes place the celebration in honor to San Juan Bautista Patron of the town.
