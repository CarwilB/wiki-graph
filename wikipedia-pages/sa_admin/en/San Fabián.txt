'''San Fabián''' is the easternmost of 21 [[Communes of Chile|commune]]s in the [[Punilla Province]] of central [[Chile]]'s. [[Ñuble Region]]. The [[capital (political)|capital]] is the town of [[San Fabián de Alico]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|1568.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

== Administration ==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], San Fabián is administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. For 2024-2028 term, the alcalde is Cristofer Valdés González ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]), and the council members are:<ref name="Council">{{cite web |url=https://datos.sinim.gov.cl/ficha_comunal.php#tab-autoridades |title=Ficha comunal |access-date=12 December 2024 |work=Sistema Nacional de Información Comunal; Subsecretaría de Desarrollo Regional y Administrativo|language=es}}</ref>
* Evelyn Troncoso Sepúlveda ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Maximiliano Quiñones Ceballos ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Jaime Meriño Constanzo ([[Christian Democrat Party|DC]])
* Samuel Muñoz Muñoz (Ind./[[Radical Party of Chile|PR]])
* Carlos Orellana Orellana (PPD)
* César Palavecino Morales (Ind./RN)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Fabián is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marta Bravo Salinas (UDI), [[Frank Sauerbaum|Frank Sauerbaum Muñoz]] (RN), Cristóbal Martínez Ramírez (UDI), Sara Concha Smith ([[Christian Social Party (Chile)|PSC]]), and Felipe Camaño Cárdenas (DC) for the 2022–2026 term, as a part of the 19th electoral district (together with [[Bulnes, Chile|Bulnes]], [[Cobquecura]], [[Coelemu]], [[Ñiquén]], [[Portezuelo, Chile|Portezuelo]], [[Quillón]], [[Quirihue]], [[Ninhue]], [[Ránquil]], [[San Carlos, Chile|San Carlos]], [[San Nicolás, Chile|San Nicolás]], [[Treguaco]], [[Chillán]], [[Chillán Viejo]], [[Coihueco]], [[El Carmen, Chile|El Carmen]], [[Pemuco]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[San Ignacio, Chile|San Ignacio]], and [[Yungay, Chile|Yungay]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Loreto Carvajal|Loreto Carvajal Ambiado]] (PPD) and [[Gustavo Sanhueza|Gustavo Sanhueza Dueñas]] (UDI) for the 2022–2030 term, as part of the 16th senatorial constituency (Ñuble Region).

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2017 Census of Population and Housing, the San Fabián commune had 4,308 inhabitants. The population density of San Fabian is 2,7/km2

==== Socioeconomic inequality ====
According to the data from the [[Encuesta casen|Casen]] 2006 survey has the highest [[Gini coefficient|Gina coefficient]] in Chile, 0.607, followed by the commune of San Pedro de la Paz, in the Bibío Region, with an index of 0,54 . This index measures the inequality in the existing income level, with 0 being the minimum and 1being the maximum.
