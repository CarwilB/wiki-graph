'''Rionegro''' ({{IPA|es|rioˈneɣɾo}}) is a town and municipality in the department of [[Santander Department|Santander]] in northeastern [[Colombia]]. The urban centre of Rionegro is located at an altitude of {{convert|590|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]], {{convert|20|km|mi}} north of the departmental capital [[Bucaramanga]]. The municipality is named after the [[Río Negro, Santander|Río Negro]].<ref name=WebsiteRioNegro>{{in lang|es}} {{URL|http://www.rionegro-santander.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml|Official website Rionegro}}</ref>
