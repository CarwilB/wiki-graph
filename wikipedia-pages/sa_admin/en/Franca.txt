'''Franca''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[São Paulo (state)|state of São Paulo]], Brazil. The city is located in the northeastern portion of the state, 401 km (249 mi) from the state capital [[São Paulo]], and 676 km (420 mi) from [[Brasília|Brasilia]]. It covers a total area of 605.679 km<sup>2</sup> of which 86.92 km<sup>2</sup> comprises the urban area.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ibge-panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/franca/panorama}}</ref> As of the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 Census]], the city's population was 352,536.<ref name="IBGE_Franca" /> 

It is recognized as the "National Capital of Footwear" and "National Basketball Capital" in Brazil, serving as the industrial and economic hub for a region of 19 municipalities, with a combined population of 672,053, between the states of São Paulo and [[Minas Gerais]].

In 2018, the city was ranked as the fifth best place to live in Brazil, and in 2023, as the sixth safest medium sized city in the country.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-09-03 |title=Ranking completo das cidades mais seguras do Brasil |url=https://myside.com.br/guia-imoveis/cidades-mais-seguras-brasil |access-date=2024-11-19 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240903165716/https://myside.com.br/guia-imoveis/cidades-mais-seguras-brasil |archive-date=2024-09-03 }}</ref> It has also maintained its position as one of the Brazilian cities with the best urban sanitation ratios.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE atualiza a listagem dos municípios que integram os recortes territoriais brasileiros {{!}} IBGE |url=https://censos.ibge.gov.br/2013-agencia-de-noticias/releases/24445-ibge-atualiza-a-listagem-dos-municipios-que-integram-os-recortes-territoriais-brasileiros-6.html |access-date=2022-09-19 |website=censos.ibge.gov.br |language=pt-BR |archive-date=2022-09-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220920173107/https://censos.ibge.gov.br/2013-agencia-de-noticias/releases/24445-ibge-atualiza-a-listagem-dos-municipios-que-integram-os-recortes-territoriais-brasileiros-6.html |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Franca drops to 5th position of the best urban sanitation in Brazil, The city of Santos is leading. |url=https://gcn.net.br/noticias/430436/franca/1969/12/franca-cai-para-5-melhor-saneamento-basico-do-pais-santos-lidera }}{{Dead link|date=April 2024 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

Franca was founded as a parish in 1805, initially part of [[Mogi Mirim]] until 1824. Its city status was granted in 1856.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2015-12-12 |title=Memorial: Veja lembranças de Mogi nas décadas de 50 e 60 |url=https://opopularmm.com.br/memorial-veja-lembrancas-de-mogi-mirim-nas-decadas-de-50-e-60-16664 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220920171822/https://opopularmm.com.br/memorial-veja-lembrancas-de-mogi-mirim-nas-decadas-de-50-e-60-16664 |archive-date=2022-09-20 |access-date=2022-09-18 |website=opopularmm.com.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

==History==

The history of Franca begins with the [[Bandeirantes|bandeiras]] of [[Bartolomeu Bueno da Silva]] (The Anhanguera) in the 18th century. 

During the opening of the route known as "Caminho de Goiás" (route to Goiás), new settlements were established along the route to serve as resting / stopping points for the entourages travelling along the way from São Paulo to [[Goiás]], in search of gold.<ref>{{Cite web |title=aspectgeo |url=http://educar.sc.usp.br/biologia/prociencias/cp/Franca/aspectgeo.htm |access-date=2022-09-18 |archive-date=2012-01-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120118114332/http://educar.sc.usp.br/biologia/prociencias/cp/Franca/aspectgeo.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref> 

The settlement where the city is now located was formerly known as ''Pouso dos Bagres'' (stop of the catfishes).

In 1819, Franca was visited by French naturalist [[Augustin Saint-Hilaire|''Auguste de Saint-Hilaire'']] who dedicated the following message:

''"The village of Franca, where I landed, is pleasantly in the midst of vast pastures, in an uncovered region, sown by bushes and cut by deep valleys. This charming village occupies the center of a path of land, wide and rounded, on each side. bathed by a small stream. At the time of my voyage, there were only about fifty houses there, but the sites for the construction of a large number of them were already marked, and it was easy to see that Franca would not take long to acquire greatness.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ceatec_ppgurb_dr_Dirceu_PJ |url=https://repositorio.sis.puc-campinas.edu.br/bitstream/handle/123456789/16106/ceatec_ppgurb_dr_Dirceu_PJ.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y}}</ref>''

The parish of Franca was founded on December 03, 1805, being part of the territory of ''Vila de Mogi Mirim'' until 1824, when it was  emancipated by [[John VI of Portugal|João VI]] and named ''Vila Franca do Imperador'' in honor of the [[Pedro I of Brazil|''Emperor Pedro I of Brazil'']]. It received the status of city on April 24, 1856.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Lei n° 21, de 24/04/1856 |url=https://www.al.sp.gov.br/norma/139894 |access-date=2022-09-18 |website=www.al.sp.gov.br |language=pt-br}}</ref>

Around 1890, Franca was served by the ''Estrada de Ferro Mogiana'', however, the railroad branch was deactivated after the construction of a new branch connecting [[Ribeirão Preto]] to [[Uberaba]]. Due to the expansion of coffee production between the XIX and XX centuries, many italian immigrants settled in Franca, and the first shoe factory emerged in the late 1920s.<ref>{{Cite web |title=coffee, immigrants and enterprises in the northeast of são paulo |url=https://www.hehe.org.br/index.php/rabphe/article/view/239/311}}</ref>

Franca took part in the [[Constitutionalist Revolution|''Constitutionalist Revolution of 1932'']], losing six residents who fought to death for São Paulo.<ref>{{Cite web |title=700 francanos são homenageados nesta 4ª |url=https://gcn.net.br/noticias/256672/franca/2014/07/700-francanos-sao-homenageados-nesta-4 |access-date=2022-09-18 |website=GCN |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Economy ==
Franca is one of the largest footwear producers in Brazil and Latin America, home to thousands of medium and large industries, including those focused on footwear component production and design centers that train new professionals for domestic and international careers. It hosts a diverse industrial sector, including production in metal, furniture, food, and beverages.<ref>{{cite thesis |last=Rezende |first=Vinícius Donizete de |title=Anônimas da história: relações de trabalho e atuação política de sapateiras entre as décadas de 1950 e 1980 (Franca-SP) |publisher=Universidade Estadual Paulista (UNESP) |url=https://repositorio.unesp.br/handle/11449/82848 |year=2006}}</ref> Franca is also located in the Alta Mogiana region, known for its coffee production due to the abundance of fertile soil and a favorable climate for cultivation.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alta Mogiana:Coffee |url=http://www.amsc.com.br/noticias/003.htm |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205050252/http://www.amsc.com.br/noticias/003.htm |archive-date=2009-02-05 |access-date=2022-09-18}}</ref> The city has also had a long-standing jewelry and diamond industry, making it one of Brazil's prominent diamond-cutting centers.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Diamantes e Franca |url=https://gcn.net.br/noticias/46744/franca/2009/08/diamantes-e-franca |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220921210508/https://gcn.net.br/noticias/46744/franca/2009/08/diamantes-e-franca |archive-date=2022-09-21 |access-date=2022-09-18}}</ref>  

In 2021, the estimated [[Gross domestic product]] (GDP) of Franca reached R$11.28 billion.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipalities: GDP |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/franca/pesquisa/38/47001?tipo=ranking |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The GDP per capita was R$31,450.10, while external revenue, including federal and state transfers, accounted for 58.65% of total revenue in 2023. The average monthly earnings of formal workers in 2022 were 2.1 times the value of the national minimum wage in Brazil.<ref>{{Cite web |title=franca/panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/franca/panorama |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

==Geography==
[[File:Região montanhosa próximo a Franca , SP, Brazil 12012019.jpg|thumb|Franca is located on a plateau where ''[[Serra da Canastra National Park]]'' can be sighted.]]
Franca is located in the northeastern region of the state of São Paulo, standing as the fifth highest municipality in the state at an altitude of 1,040&nbsp;m above the sea level. The territory of Franca is covered by sandy soils dominated by the sandstones of [[Bauru Formation|Bauru]], and [[Botucatu Formation|Botucatu]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Geografiaagricola/50 |url=http://observatoriogeograficoamericalatina.org.mx/egal15/Geografiasocioeconomica/Geografiaagricola/50.pdf}}</ref> Vegetation is dominated by grasses, and the forests are restricted to mountainous slopes. The municipality shares borders with its neighbors [[Batatais]], [[Cristais Paulista]], [[Patrocínio Paulista]], [[Ibiraci]] and [[Claraval]]. 

=== Hydrography ===
The basin of Rio Canoas provide water for the city and the surrounding area. The municipality is traversed by various waterways, including the Rio das Canoas, Rio Pouso Alegre, Rio São João, and Ribeirão Salgado.

===Climate===

Franca has a [[tropical savanna climate]] (Aw), with milder temperatures due to its elevation. The city experiences dry winters, rainy summers, and moderate temperatures year-round. Prior to the current climate classification (1961-1990), it was classified as having a [[humid subtropical climate]] (Cwa). The lowest recorded temperature was 0 °C on July 5, 1953, and the highest temperature was 37.8 °C on October 15, 2014.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Normais Climatológicos do Brasil |url=https://accounts.google.com/v3/signin/identifier?dsh=S-1140687269%3A1663519955854797&continue=https%3A%2F%2Fdrive.google.com%2Ffile%2Fd%2F0BwM-LY650N0NRjNkR2V6YXF5bU0%2Fview&followup=https%3A%2F%2Fdrive.google.com%2Ffile%2Fd%2F0BwM-LY650N0NRjNkR2V6YXF5bU0%2Fview&osid=1&passive=1209600&service=wise&flowName=WebLiteSignIn&flowEntry=ServiceLogin&ifkv=AQDHYWqVBB7LqPI0Twg9U1LzpdShQfg29Ui6aiTlhf8Ur1YEx2e9E8c_t8OiN3PNexf94B6jpAxNhg |access-date=2022-09-18 |website=accounts.google.com}}</ref>

Occurrence of rain is high and it is one of the rainiest cities in the State of São Paulo. The highest recorded rainfall reached 146 millimeters on March 28, 1931, and recently, 133.7 millimeters was recorded on November 21, 2018.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Volume de Chuvas – Franca |date=29 January 2020 |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/ribeirao-preto-franca/noticia/2020/01/29/franca-sp-registra-maior-volume-de-chuvas-em-janeiro-no-estado-de-sp-aponta-instituto.ghtml}}</ref>

==Demographics==
As of the 2022 census, Franca had a population density of 582.05 inhabitants per square kilometer (1,507.5/sq mi).<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sp/franca/panorama |access-date=2024-11-19 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The municipality belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Franca]], which encompasses 10 other municipalities with a combined population of 420,000 as of 2017. The region is part of the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Ribeirão Preto]]. 

In 2010, the [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) for Franca was 0.780, placing it in the high human development category. Among its components, the HDI-M for income was 0.755, [[longevity]] was 0.800, and [[education]] was 0.906.

By 2011, the majority of residents lived in urban areas, totaling 315,355, while 5,657 lived in rural zones. The school enrollment rate for children between 6 and 14 years old was 98.2%. The [[life expectancy]] was 73.03 years with a literacy rate of 96.37%. The fertility rate was 2.26 children per woman. The [[infant mortality]] rate was 12.66 per 1,000 live births, equivalent to 1.26%. As of the census of 2022, the infant mortality rate stood at 7.09 per 1,000 live births, while hospitalizations for [[diarrhea]] reached 6.2 per 1,000 residents.<ref name=":0" />

=== Racial and ethnic composition ===
In 2011, the racial and ethnic composition consisted of 69.15% [[White Brazilians|White]], 23.84% [[Pardo Brazilians|Mixed-race]] (Pardo), 6.30% [[Black people|Black]], 0.58% [[Asian Brazilians|Asian]], and 0.12% [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil|Indigenous]].<ref name=":0" /> As of 2022, the population was 63.98% White, 28.26% Mixed-race (Pardo), and 7.48% Black.
