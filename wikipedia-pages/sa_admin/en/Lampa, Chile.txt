'''Lampa''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of cities in Chile|city]] in the [[Chacabuco Province|Chacabuco province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. Lampa is situated near the [[Chicauma]] mountain range, part of which was added to the [[La Campana National Park]].<ref>C. Michael Hogan. 2008</ref>

Lampa (in [[Quechuan languages|Quechua]]: Lampa, ‘the miner’s shovel’) is a [[Communes of Chile|community]] located in the rural zone northwest of [[Santiago]], [[Chile]]. It belongs to the administrative [[Chacabuco Province|providence of Chacabuco]], in the [[Metropolitan Region of Santiago]].

According to the census of 2017, Lampa has a population of 201,034 inhabitants, being the community with the greatest growth in comparison with the census before (2002), when it only had 40228 inhabitants.

In the trimester of the year 2016, Lampa was selected as first place as the community with the most sales in living area, reaching 28%, with respect to Colina and [[Puente Alto]].

The community of Lampa adjoins the communities of [[Tiltil|Til Til]], [[Colina, Chile|Colina]], [[Pudahuel]], [[Curacaví]], [[Quilicura]] and [[Quilpué]].

==Demographics==
Lampa appears in the official census records as early at 1788.<ref>Lyman De Platt. 1998</ref> According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Lampa spans an area of {{convert|451.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 40,228 inhabitants (20,571 men and 19,657 women). Of these, 28,229 (70.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 11,999 (29.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 60.7% (15,195 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

It is a community where the land, is for the most part plains, with certain rolling hills in the northern part.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.gobernacionchacabuco.gov.cl/geografia/|title = Ubicación geográfica}}</ref> Like most of the communities in the region it has a Mediterranean type of vegetation, resulting in a green landscape almost year-round, even though in the [[summer]] there tend to be hot spots. The climate is for the most part is a temperate, [[Mediterranean climate]]<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.adapt-chile.org/web/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/PLCC-LAMPA.compressed.pdf |title=Archived copy |access-date=2019-02-22 |archive-date=2016-08-04 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160804120333/http://www.adapt-chile.org/web/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/PLCC-LAMPA.compressed.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> even though in the zone there are registered extremes throughout the majority of the region. For that reason it can reach temperatures below 0&nbsp;°C  in [[winter]] at the lowest, with frequent frosts, and in summer it can reach 30&nbsp;°C. In the year 2013, Lampa was the coldest community of the Metropolitan Region of Santiago, reaching -8.4&nbsp;°C.
The commerce is gradually progressing, above all in downtown, which is very close to the [[Autopista Central]] and is connected by highway Américo Vespucio Express and Costanera Norte with all the neighborhoods of the Great Santiago. Demographically, it is a growing community, more and more housing complexes are built due to its proximity to [[Santiago, Chile]] through the Autopista Central, Costanera Norte and Amémerico Vespucio Norte Express.
Soon the Highway Norponiente will be built which will connect [[Cerro Navia]] with Lampa from the Enlace Carrascal to the Costanera Sur.

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Lampa is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Graciela Ortúzar Novoa ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Lampa belongs to the [[Electoral districts of Chile|Electoral District]] number eight and to the seventh Senatorial District (Santiago Poniente.) It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile]] by the deputies Joaquín Lavín León de la [[Independent Democratic Union|IDU]], Gabriel Silber Romo del CDP, Pepe Auth Stewart of [[Radical Party of Chile|PRSD]], Patricio Melero Abaroa of the [[Independent Democratic Union|IDU]], Claudia Mix Jiménez of [[Citizen Power]], Mario Desbordes Jiménez of [[National Renewal (Chile)|NR]], [[Carmen Hertz Cádiz]] of the [[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]] and Pbalo Vidal Rojas of [[Democratic revolution|DR]]. At the same time, it is represented in the [[Senate]] by the senators [[Guido Girardi|Guido Girardi Lavín]] of [[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]] and [[Andrés Allamand]] of [[National Renewal (Chile)|NR]].
The well-known municipality of Lampa is directed by the [[mayor]] Graciela Ortúzar Novoa ([[National Renewal (Chile)|NR]]), who is advised by the councilors:

* Juan Amigo Astudillo (IND) 
* Rodrigo Plaza Muñoz ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Carmen Gloria Ruminot Jorquera ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Felipe González Pino (IND)
* Marcos Álvarez Tobar ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Marcelo Jaque Peña ([[Independent Regionalist Party|PRI]])

The regional [[intendant]], appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]], is Fernando Echeverría.<ref name="Intendants">{{Cite web |url=http://www.gobiernodechile.cl/intendentes/ |title=Gobierno de Chile: Intendentes |accessdate=3 December 2010 |language=es |archive-date=15 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130615025111/http://www.gobiernodechile.cl/intendentes/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Lampa is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Mr. Patricio Melero 
(UDI) and Mr. Gabriel Silber (PDC) as part of the 16th electoral district, (together with [[Colina, Chile|Colina]], [[Tiltil]], [[Quilicura]] and [[Pudahuel]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
