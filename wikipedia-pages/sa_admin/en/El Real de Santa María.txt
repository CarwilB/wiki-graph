'''El Real de Santa María ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pinogana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,183 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Pinogana District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,201; its population as of 2000 was 1,185.<ref name=inec/>

The town of El Real is on the Pirre River, a small tributary of the [[Tuira River]]. It is {{convert|17|km}} by boat to the town of [[Yaviza]], the terminus of the [[Pan American Highway]].<ref>{{cite web |title=El Real to Yaviza |url=https://www.google.com/maps/@8.1476703,-77.7238353,12994m/data=!3m1!1e3 |website=Google Maps |access-date=19 November 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=El Real, Yaviza |url=https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=13/8.1427/-77.7227 |website=OpenStreetMap |access-date=19 November 2018}}</ref>  

The town was founded by the Spanish as a fort in 1665 to protect their gold mining interests in the area.<Ref name="founding1">[https://www.redalyc.org/journal/943/94370787011/html/ The history of mining and mineral exploration in Panama: From Pre-Columbian gold mining to modern copper mining],  Boletín de la Sociedad Geológica Mexicana, vol. 72, no. 3, 00012, 2020 (" The deposits were first worked by the Spaniards in about 1665 when the fort and mining town of El Real de Santa Maria was founded on the Río Tuira")</ref> The English Buccaneer [[Bartholomew Sharp]] took over the town and fort in 1680, but found the Spanish had removed most of the gold they hoped to seize, and left after a few days.<ref name="gold1">[https://books.google.com/books?id=Zb401E_G46YC&pg=PA316 A Biographical Sketch of Captain Dampier], ''The United States Services Journal'', p. 316 (1837)</ref>

[[El Real Airport]] is an [[airstrip]] serving the area.
