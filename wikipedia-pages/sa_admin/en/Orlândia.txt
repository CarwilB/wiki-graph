'''Orlândia''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 44,360 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|291.8|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2356R Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 695&nbsp;m (2,280&nbsp;ft).
