'''General Villegas Partido''' is the northwesternmost [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 29,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|7232|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, making it the fourth-largest partido in Buenos Aires Province. Its capital city is [[General Villegas]], which is {{convert|410|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
