'''Sutatenza''' ({{IPA|es|sutaˈtensa}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Eastern Boyacá Province]] of the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], [[Colombia]]. It is located {{convert|125|km|mi}} from the Colombian capital [[Bogotá]] and {{convert|118|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. Sutatenza borders [[Somondoco]], [[Guateque]], [[Tenza]] and [[Garagoa]].<ref name=websiteSutatenza>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.sutatenza-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Sutatenza]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Sutatenza comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means either "Cloud behind the anchovy" or "Descending to the house of the ''cacique''".<ref name=websiteSutatenza/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Sutatenza] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
In the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Sutatenza, situated in the [[Tenza Valley]] to the east of the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' called Tenzuzucá or Tenzucá, loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. [[Conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] in his quest for ''[[El Dorado]]'' and the rich [[emerald]] deposits of the [[Muisca Confederation]] visited Sutatenza in 1537.

Modern Sutatenza was not founded until October 22, 1783.<ref name=websiteSutatenza/>

==Climate==
Sutatenza has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> with mild temperatures and frequent rainfall. A [[dry season]] between December and February produces warmer and drier weather.

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Sutatenza are [[agriculture]] and [[aviculture]]. A small [[halite|salt]] mine is operated in the ''vereda'' Salitre.<ref name=websiteSutatenza/>
