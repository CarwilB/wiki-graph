'''Cachoeirinha''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kaʃueˈɾĩj̃ɐ]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 169&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco.

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Tacaimbó]]   ('''N''');  [[Lajedo, Pernambuco|Lajedo]]    ('''S''');  [[São Caitano]], [[Altinho, Pernambuco|Altinho]] and [[Ibirajuba]]    ('''E''');   [[São Bento do Una]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 179.27&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 536 m
* Hydrography - [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una]] and [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]]
* Annual average temperature - 21.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 169&nbsp;km
* Population - 20,501 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/cachoeirinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Cachoeirinha''' are based in industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially  [[beans]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[pigs]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[horses]] and [[poultry]].
