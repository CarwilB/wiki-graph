'''Chicamán''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Quiché Department|El Quiché]].

Tourists may visit the Canyon El Barbudo and the Quatro Chorros waterfalls.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.inforpressca.com/chicamanq/turismo.php |title=Turismo en el Municipio de Chicamán, Quiché|website=Inforpressca.com|accessdate=2008-01-17|language=es}}</ref> Its population in 2023 is 44,728.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Chicamán (Municipality, Guatemala) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/quich%C3%A9/1419__chicam%C3%A1n/ |access-date=2023-11-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
