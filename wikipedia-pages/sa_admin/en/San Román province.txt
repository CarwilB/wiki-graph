'''San Román''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] in the [[Puno Region]] of [[Peru]]. It borders the provinces of [[Lampa Province|Lampa]], [[Azángaro Province|Azángaro]], [[Puno Province|Puno]], [[Huancané Province|Huancane]], the [[Arequipa Province]] of the [[Arequipa Region]] and [[Moquegua Region]]'s province of [[General Sánchez Cerro Province|General Sánchez Cerro]]. Its capital is the city of [[Juliaca]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into four districts (Spanish: ''distritos'', singular: ''distrito''):
* [[Cabana District, San Román|Cabana]]
* [[Cabanillas District|Cabanillas]] 
* [[Caracoto District|Caracoto]]
* [[Juliaca District|Juliaca]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (61.52%) learnt to speak in childhood, 29.80% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language]] and 8.53% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
