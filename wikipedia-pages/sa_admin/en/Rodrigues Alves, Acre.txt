'''Rodrigues Alves''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ʁoˈdɾiɡiz ˈawvis}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 19,351 according to the 2020 estimates.

{{stack|[[File:West Acre Brazil conservation units 2016.svg|thumb|Conservation units in the west of the state of Acre, Brazil]]}}
The municipality contains 13.45% of the {{convert|846633|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Divisor National Park]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA da Serra do Divisor – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PARNA da Serra do Divisor|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
