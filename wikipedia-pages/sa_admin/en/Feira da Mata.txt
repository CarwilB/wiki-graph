'''Feira da Mata''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Feira da Mata covers {{convert|1,176.105|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 5,661 with a population density of 5 inhabitants per square kilometer. It was originally part of [[Carinhanha]], but became an independent municipality in 1989.<ref name="i"/>

Feira da Mata is located at the junction of BA-601 and BA-594. It is {{Convert|964|km|mi}} from the state capital of Bahia, [[Salvador, Bahia|Salvador]]. The [[Carinhanha River]] forms the southern border of the municipality, which is also the border between the states of Bahia and [[Minas Gerais]].<ref name="i"/>

Feira da Mata was originally home to the [[Kayapo people]]. Manuel Nunes Viana expelled the Kayapo population in the early 18th century and settled in the area.<ref name="i"/>
