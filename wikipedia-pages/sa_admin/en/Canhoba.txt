'''Canhoba''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|kɐ̃ˈɲɔbɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]].<ref>{{cite book | title=Latin American Dendroecology: Combining Tree-Ring Sciences and Ecology in a Megadiverse Territory | publisher=Springer | year=2020 | url=https://books.google.com/books?id=MLHWDwAAQBAJ&pg=PA47 | page=47 | isbn=978-3-030-36930-9 }}</ref> Its population was 4,006 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/canhoba/panorama | website=cidades.ibge.gov.br | access-date=26 January 2021}}</ref> and its area is {{cvt|170|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy | website=www.ibge.gov.br |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>
