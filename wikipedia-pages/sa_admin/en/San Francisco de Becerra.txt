'''San Francisco de Becerra''' ({{IPA|es|saɱ fɾanˈsisko ðe βeˈsera}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the interior of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Olancho department|Olancho]], slightly to the south. It is bordered by [[Juticalpa]] in the west and [[Catacamas]] in the east.

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Francisco de Becerra municipality had a population of 9,475. Of these, 97.20% were [[Mestizo]], 1.56% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.93% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]], 0.30% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and 0.01% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>

==Sports==
Local football club [[Alianza de Becerra]] play in the [[Liga de Ascenso de Honduras|Honduran second division]].
