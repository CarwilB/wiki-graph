'''Santa María Chilapa de Díaz''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western Mexico. 
It is part of the [[Teposcolula District]] in the center of the Mixteca Region.
The name Chilapa means "Water from chiles".<ref name=INF>{{cite web

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 234.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 1900 meters above sea level.
The climate is mild, averaging about 17&nbsp;°C.
===Flora and fauna===
Trees include oak, pine and pitch pine. Wild fauna include coyotes, snakes, lizards and scorpions.<ref name=INF/>

==Demography==
{{Unsourced section|date=March 2025}}
As of 2005, the municipality had 484 households with a total population of 1,687 of whom 151 spoke an indigenous language.

==Economy==
Economic activity includes agriculture (corn, beans and wheat) and some cattle ranching. There is a cooperative that processes sugar cane from the state of [[Veracruz]] for rum and brandy.  In addition there is a factory making polyethylene products and a third dedicated to manufacture and marketing of raffia.<ref name=INF/>

==History ==
The foundation date of the town is unknown because it burned down in 1879 and all the original titles were lost.<ref name=INF/>
