'''San José''' is a town and municipality located in the western portion of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]]. It was originally part of Risaralda Municipality until 1997, when it became an independent municipality within the Caldas Department. The municipality is part of the Lower Western District, 67&nbsp;km from Manizales. Its name derives from the arrival of the founders on the day of Saint Joseph, March 19.{{Clarify|reason=incoherent|date=November 2010}}

The municipality is divided into 18 minor counties or veredas:
Altomira, La Paz, Los Caimos, El Contento, Tamboral, Arrayanes, El Bosque, Morroazul, La Primavera, La Libertad, La Cienaga, La Estrella, Guaimaral, Vaticano, El Pacifico, La Morelia, Pueblo Rico and Buena Vista.
