'''Ipixuna''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 30,436 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/ipixuna/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 13,566&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 58.76% of the {{convert|427004|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Gregório Extractive Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Rio Gregório – ISA}} 
 |title=RESEX do Rio Gregório|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
