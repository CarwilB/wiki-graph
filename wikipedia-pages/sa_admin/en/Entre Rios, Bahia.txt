== Culture ==
Entre Rios hosts several traditional festivities including the Lavagem das Praias (beach cleans) in Porto do Sauipe, Subaúma and Massarandupió, the Barquinha festival in [[Sítio do Mato|Sítio do Meio]] and Lagoa Redonda, in addition to numerous [[patron saint]] festivals.

A local specialty dish is [[wiktionary:arrumadinho|''Arrumadinho'']]'','' which consists of [[Black-eyed pea|black-eyed peas]], sun-dried or salted meat, toasted [[cassava flour]], and a vinegar-based salad, typically served together in a plate or bowl. The dish is widely available in restaurants across the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |access-date=2021-07-24 |first=BeeWeb Agência Digital- |language=pt-br |last=www.beeweb.com.br |title=Entre Rios - Costa dos Coqueiros {{!}} ABIH - Associação Brasileira da Indústria de Hotéis - Bahia |url=https://abihbahia.org.br/localidade/entre-rios |website=Associação Brasileira da Indústria de Hotéis da Bahia - ABIH - BAHIA - BRASIL |archive-date=2021-07-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210724022850/https://abihbahia.org.br/localidade/entre-rios |url-status=dead }}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
== Roads ==
The city center can be accessed via the [[BR-101]] highway towards [[Aracaju]]; the [[Linha Verde (Bahia)|Linha Verde]] to Palame, where the [[BA-400]] connects to Entre Rios; or via the [[BA-093]] highway from [[Simões Filho]].<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{Cite web |last=JP |date=2025-06-06 |title=PEDÁGIO DA BA-093 VAI FICAR 10% MAIS CARO; SAIBA VALORES |url=https://bahiaeconomica.com.br/wp/2025/06/06/pedagio-da-ba-093-vai-ficar-10-mais-caro-saiba-valores/ |access-date=2026-01-13 |website=Bahia Economica |language=pt-br}}</ref>
