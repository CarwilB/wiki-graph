'''Tzitzio''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Michoacán]], located approximately {{convert|30|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Tzitzio is located in the [[Balsas Depression]] in northeast Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|600|and(-)|2400|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Charo Municipality|Charo]] and [[Indaparapeo]] to the north, [[Queréndaro]] to the northeast, [[Ciudad Hidalgo, Michoacán|Hidalgo]] to the east, [[Tuzantla]] to the southeast, [[Tiquicheo]] to the south, [[Madero, Michoacán|Madero]] to the west, and Morelia to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16101.pdf |title=Tzitzio, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |accessdate=29 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|944.133|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 1.61% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Tzitzio consists of temperate forest (63%), tropical forest (33%) and grassland (1%). Another 2% of the land is used for agriculture and less than 0.1% is urbanized.<ref name=compendium/> Most of Tzitzio drains into the [[Purungueo River]], which runs north to south through the municipality and drains into the [[Cutzamala River]] just upstream of {{ill|El Gallo Reservoir|es|Presa El Gallo}}. Some of the western parts of the municipality drain into the [[Carácuaro River]].<ref name=compendium/> The southeastern corner of {{ill|Insurgente José María Morelos National Park|es|Parque nacional Insurgente José María Morelos}} extends into Tzitzio near its municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.gob.mx/semarnat/articulos/parque-nacional-insurgente-jose-maria-morelos |title=Parque Nacional Insurgente José María Morelos |language=es |publisher=[[SEMARNAT]] |date=22 February 2018 |accessdate=29 December 2020}}</ref>

Tzitzio's climate varies from temperate in the north to tropical in the south, with rain falling in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|28|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1300|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Tzitzio is a word of [[Chichimeca]] origin meaning "beautiful place". In 1831 it was a ''tenencia'' in the municipality of Indaparapeo. Its inhabitants produced sugarcane, guava, sapote, and other tropical fruits, and wove ''chiquihuites'' or baskets. It was made an independent municipality in 1936.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=69, 90, 220–221 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Emilia Herrera Sánchez.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |accessdate=26 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Tzitzio recorded a population of 9166 inhabitants living in 2046 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=101 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Tzitzio |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |accessdate=29 December 2020}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 8966 inhabitants in Tzitzio.<ref name=survey/>

[[INEGI]] lists 271 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Tzitzio, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 1072 inhabitants in the 2010 Census. The most populated locality in the municipality is the village of Tafetán, which recorded a population of 1784 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Tzitzio's economy is based on agriculture. Major activities include cattle farming and the cultivation of corn, tomatoes, sugarcane, and fruits such as mango, guava, plum and papaya.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Tzitzio Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/2a-4619-1.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=9 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 2a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=9 May 2019 |accessdate=29 December 2020}}</ref>
