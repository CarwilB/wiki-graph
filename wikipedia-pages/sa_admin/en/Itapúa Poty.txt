'''Itapúa Poty''' is a Paraguayan [[districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the north-central part of the [[Itapúa Department]]. It is situated approximately 25 km from [[Route 6 (Paraguay)|route 6]], which connects the cities of [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]] and [[Ciudad del Este]].

The town borders to the north with [[Tavaí]] ([[Caazapá Department]]), to the south with [[Capitán Meza]] and [[Pirapó, Paraguay|Pirapó]], to the east with [[Edelira]], and to the west with [[Alto Verá]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Itapúa Poty seduce con su Salto Poty y Puente Kyha|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/itapua-poty-seduce-su-salto-poty-y-puente-kyha-n2837476|website=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=12 August 2019|access-date=29 November 2025|language=es|first=Antonio|last=Rolín|archive-date=29 November 2025|url-status=live|trans-title=Itapúa Poty seduces with its Salto Poty and Puente Kyha|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251129181032/https://www.ultimahora.com/itapua-poty-seduce-su-salto-poty-y-puente-kyha-n2837476}}</ref>

== History ==
Itapúa Poty was elevated to district status by Law No. 926 of 6 August 1996, which created the Municipality of Itapúa Poty in the department of Itapúa and established its municipal government in the town of the same name.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Ley Nº 926|url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/10885/ley-n-926-crea-el-municipio-itapua-poty-del-vii-departamento-itapua-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-el-pueblo-de-itapua-poty|website=Biblioteca y Archivo Central del Congreso Nacional|access-date=29 November 2025|language=es|date=14 October 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250717161532/https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/10885/ley-n-926-crea-el-municipio-itapua-poty-del-vii-departamento-itapua-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-el-pueblo-de-itapua-poty|archive-date=17 July 2025|url-status=live|last=Mariela}}</ref>

The district is named after the [[Paraguayan polka|polka]] song "Itapúa Poty", composed by musician Juan Carlos Soria, a native of this community. The name comes from the [[Guaraní language]] and means "flower of Itapúa".<ref>{{Cite web|title=Itapúa Poty celebra 28 años de fundación con importantes avances|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/itapua-poty-celebra-28-anos-de-fundacion-con-importantes-avances|website=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=19 August 2024|access-date=29 November 2025|language=es|first=Por Brand|last=Voice|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251109135031/https://www.ultimahora.com/itapua-poty-celebra-28-anos-de-fundacion-con-importantes-avances|archive-date=9 November 2025|url-status=live|trans-title=Itapúa Poty celebrates 28 years since its founding with significant progress}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2022 census, the district has a total population of 9,273 inhabitants, with a [[median age]] of 28 years. The sex distribution is 4,719 men and 4,554 women. The urban population amounts to 864 people, while the rural population reaches 8,409 inhabitants.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=https://www.ine.gov.py/Publicaciones/Biblioteca/documento/257/Censo%202022%20-%20Estructura%20de%20la%20poblacion%20por%20edad%20y%20sexo.pdf|title=Censo 2022 - Estructura de la población por edad y sexo|access-date=29 November 2025|website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística|trans-title=2022 Census - Population structure by age and sex|language=es}}</ref>

By age groups, 1,628 people are between 0 and 14 years old (219 in urban areas and 1,486 in rural areas), 5,759 people are between 15 and 64 years old (557 urban and 5,202 rural), and 809 people are 65 years of age or older (88 urban and 721 rural).<ref name=":0" />

A total of 2,764 occupied private dwellings are registered, with an average of 3.4 persons per household. Of these, 2,266 have running water and 2,700 have electricity. Regarding access to technology, 2,260 households have a touchscreen mobile phone and 1,813 have an internet connection.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.ine.gov.py/censo2022/documentos/2%20resultados%20finales%20viviendas.pdf|title=Censo 2022 - Caracterización de las viviendas y los hogares|access-date=29 November 2025|website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística|trans-title=2022 Census - Characterization of dwellings and households|language=es}}</ref>

== Tourism ==
The Takuapí waterfall is located in the forests of the district, within the San Rafael Reserve area. The waterfall is over 30 meters high and situated in a rugged landscape with abundant springs that forms part of the [[Tebicuary River]] basin. The area is suitable for eco-adventure activities and sports such as [[rappelling]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=El distrito de Itapuá Poty se abre camino a pasos agigantados|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/el-distrito-itapua-poty-se-abre-camino-pasos-agigantados-n2899326|website=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=10 August 2020|access-date=29 November 2025|language=es|archive-url=https://archive.today/20251129185127/https://www.ultimahora.com/el-distrito-itapua-poty-se-abre-camino-pasos-agigantados-n2899326|archive-date=29 November 2025|url-status=live|trans-title=The district of Itapúa Poty is making great strides}}</ref>
