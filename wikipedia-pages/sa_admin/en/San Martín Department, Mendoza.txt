'''San Martín''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the centre of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 108,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|1,504|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San Martín, Mendoza|San Martín]], which is located around {{cvt|1,100|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].

==Name==

The department and its ''cabecera'' (capital) are named in honour of [[José de San Martín|General San Martín]] (1778–1850), the military leader that led the Argentine forces to victory over the [[Spanish Empire]] during the [[Argentine War of Independence]].

==Sport==

The city of San Martín is home to [[San Martín de Mendoza|Atlético Club San Martín]], a [[football (soccer)|football]] club currently playing in the regionalised [[Torneo Argentino B|4th Division]].
