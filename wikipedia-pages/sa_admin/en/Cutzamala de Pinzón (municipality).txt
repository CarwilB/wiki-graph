''' Cutzamala de Pinzón ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]] and is the northernmost municipality in Guerrero. The municipal seat lies at [[Cutzamala de Pinzón]]. The municipality covers an area of 611.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 20,730.<ref>{{cite web|title=Cutzamala de Pinzón|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12027a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 23, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210639/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12027a.htm|archive-date=September 27, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>
