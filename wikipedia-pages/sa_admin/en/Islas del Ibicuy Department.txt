The '''Islas del Ibicuy Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Islas del Ibicuy''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Entre Ríos Province|Entre Ríos]], [[Argentina]]. It is the ninth largest in the province with 4,500&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and the least populated, with 14,000 inhabitants according to the 2022 census.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.entrerios.gov.ar/dec/publicaciones/CENSO%202010/P1-P_entre_rios.xls |title=INDEC. Censo Nacional de Población, Hogares y Viviendas 2001 y 2010. |accessdate=13 November 2014 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20141110195559/http://www.entrerios.gov.ar/dec/publicaciones/CENSO%202010/P1-P_entre_rios.xls |archivedate=10 November 2014 }}</ref>
