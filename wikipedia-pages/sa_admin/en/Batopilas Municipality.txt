'''Batopilas de Manuel Gómez Morín''' ({{IPA|es|batoˈpilas|lang|Batopilas.ogg}}) is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Batopilas, Chihuahua|Batopilas]]. The municipality covers an area of 2,064.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The name "Batopilas de Manuel Gómez Morín" was adopted in 2017 to honor of [[Manuel Gómez Morín]] (1897-1972), who was born here and was a prominent Mexican lawyer, academician and politician.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 14,362,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Batopilas|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=008|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=11 April 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150411065505/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=008|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 13,298 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web |title=Batopilas |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08008a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=August 18, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070304094917/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08008a.htm |archivedate=March 4, 2007 }}</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Batopilas had a population of 1,220.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Batopilas, the municipality had 603 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
