'''Los Castillos''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Río de Jesús District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 552 as of 2010.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010 |title=SUPERFICIE, POBLACION Y DENSIDAD DE POBLACION EN LA REPUBLICA, SEGUN PROVINCIA, COMARCA INDIGENA, DISTRITO Y CORREGIMIENTO: CENSOS DE 1990 A 2010 |url=https://view.officeapps.live.com/op/view.aspx?src=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.inec.gob.pa%2Farchivos%2FP3601Cuadro11.xls&wdOrigin=BROWSELINK |access-date=13 Jun 2023 |website=contraloria.gob.pa |publisher=[[National Institute of Statistics and Census of Panama]] |format=XLS}}</ref>
