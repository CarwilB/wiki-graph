'''Lagoa Vermelha''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 27,778.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/lagoa-vermelha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
