{{Main|San Juan, Puerto Rico}}

Monacillo is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] in the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico]]. The [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] included the area that now forms [[Monacillo Urbano, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo Urbano]]. In 2010, it had a population of 11,442 living in a land area of 1.15 square miles (2.98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>). Monacillo is surrounded by [[Monacillo Urbano, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo Urbano]] barrio to the north, [[Cupey, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Cupey]] to the east, [[Caimito, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Caimito]] to the south, and the municipality of [[Guaynabo, Puerto Rico|Guaynabo]] to the west.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico: 2010 Population and Housing Unit Counts.pdf | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|page=32|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>
