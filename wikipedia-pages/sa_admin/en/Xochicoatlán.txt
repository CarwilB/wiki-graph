'''Xochicoatlán''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|159.3|km2}}.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 7,015.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Xochicoatlán (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/hidalgo/13079__xochicoatl%C3%A1n/|access-date=2022-02-25|website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

==Toponymy==
The word ''Xochicoatlán'' comes from the [[Nahuatl]] ''Xóchitl'' 'flower' and ''cuauhtl'' 'tree'; so its meaning is "Place of the viper flower".<ref group=note>The etymological origin of the name of a municipality can have different interpretations or have an uncertain origin, this is the most common or most accepted by the government municipality and its residents, according to the ''Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Mexico.''</ref><ref name="etym">{{cite book|author= State Center for Municipal Studies of the State of Hidalgo|year =1988|title= The Municipalities of Hidalgo|series= Encyclopedia Collection of the municipalities of Mexico|publisher= Secretary of the Interior}}</ref><ref name=gob>{{cite web|title=Xochicoatlán|url=http://www.hidalgo.gob.mx/page/municipio/xochicoatlan|work=[[Municipalities of Hidalgo]]|publisher=[[Government of the state of Hidalgo]]|access-date=March 22, 2017}}</ref>

==Geography==
{{See also|Geography of the state of Hidalgo}}

===Terrain and hydrology===

Xochicoatlánis located within the province of [[Sierra Madre Oriental]], within the Carso Huasteco subprovince. Its terrain is [[Mountain chain|mountainous]] or hilly [[highland|upland]]s (70.0%) and [[Plateau|plateaux]] (30.0%).

Its [[geology]] corresponds to the [[Jurassic]] period (46.3%), [[Permian]] (16), [[Triassic]] (13.0%), [[Cretaceous]] (12.0%) and [[neogene]] (12.0%). With extrusive igneous type rocks: acid tuff (7.0%) and basalt (4.3%) Sedimentary: shale-sandstone (38.0%), limestone-shale ( 26.0%), sandstone-conglomerate (13.0%) and limestone (11.0%). Regarding [[pedology]] the dominant soil is [[leptosol]] (35.0%), [[luvisol]] (23.3%), [[umbrisol]] (30.5%), [[cambisol]] (7.5%) and [[regosol]] (3.0%).

Regarding [[hydrology]] it is positioned in the hydrological region of the [[Pánuco River|Pánuco]] (3.0%); in the basins of the [[Moctezuma River]]; within the subbasin of the Los Hules River.

===Climate===

The municipality presents a variety of [[climate]]s: Humid temperate with rain all year round (59.0%); humid temperate with abundant rains in summer (22.0%); humid semi-warm with rain all year round (16.0%); and temperate subhumid with rains in summer, with higher humidity (3.0%).It registers an average annual temperature of 19&nbsp;°C and a rainfall of 1,890 millimeters per year, the rainy period is from June to December .<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

===Ecology===

In [[flora]] it has vegetation made up of broadleaf forest and medium-sized subevergreen forest.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH /> As for [[fauna]] there are wild boar, porcupine, hare duck, falcon and eagle.<ref name=INAFED /><ref name=SIIEH />

==Economy==
{{See also|Economy of the State of Hidalgo}}

In 2015 the municipality had an [[HDI]] of 0.684 <span style="color:#FFCC00">'''Medium''',</span> so ranks 53rd at the state level;<ref name=IDH2015>{{cite web|author1=COESPO|title=Hidalgo. Municipal Human Development Index 2015|url=http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/pdf/idh%202015_mun_hgo_web.pdf|work=Consejo Estatal de Poblacion|publisher=[[Government of the State of Hidalgo]]|date=2019|accessdate=July 22, 2019|archive-date=July 27, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200727040938/http://poblacion.hidalgo.gob.mx/pdf/idh%202015_mun_hgo_web.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> and in 2005 it presented a [[GDP]] of $315,651,036.00 [[Mexican pesos]], and a [[GDP per capita]] of $45,391.00 (current prices of 2005).<ref name=IDH2005>
To view information, first select the type of information you want, then the state of Hidalgo in the "Federative entity" field, and then choose "municipality" in the field.</ref>
According to the [[National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy (CONEVAL)|National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy]] (Coneval), the municipality registers a [[Marginalization|Marginalization Index]] Medium.<ref name=basic /> 39.4% of the population is in moderate poverty and 37.7% is in extreme poverty.<ref name=basic /> In 2015, the municipality ranked 58th out of 84 municipalities in the state scale of social backwardness.<ref name=coneval1>{{cite web|author=[[National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy|Coneval]]|url=http://diariooficial.gob.mx/SEDESOL/2017/Hidalgo_079.pdf|title=Annual Report on the Situation of Poverty and Social Backwardness: Xochicoatlán, Hidalgo (2017)|work=[[Secretariat of Social Development (Mexico)|Ministry of Social Development]]|publisher=[[Government of Mexico]]|accessdate=March 3, 2017}}</ref>

As of 2015, in terms of agriculture, corn, beans, coffee, oranges and piloncillo cane are grown.<ref name=SIIEH /><ref name=basic /> In [[livestock]] cattle are raised for milk and meat, sheep, pigs and goats, laying and fattening birds, as well as turkeys, also producing honey and beeswax.<ref name=SIIEH /><ref name=basic />

In 2015, there were 49 economic units, which generated jobs for 98 people.<ref name=basic /> As far as [[commerce]] is concerned, there is one [[tianguis]], nine Diconsa stores and two [[Liconsa]] dairy shops.<ref name=SIIEH /><ref name=basica /> According to figures for the year 2015 presented in the Economic Censuses by INEGI, the [[Workforce|Economically Active Population]] (EAP) of the municipality amounts to 2094 people, of which 1977 are employed and 117 are unemployed.<ref group=note>Size of the community according to the number of inhabitants for the population aged 12 and over.</ref><ref name=basic /> 42.19% belong to the [[primary sector]], 22.71% belong to the [[secondary sector]], 34.45% belong to the [[tertiary sector]] and 0.65% did not specify.<ref name=basic />
