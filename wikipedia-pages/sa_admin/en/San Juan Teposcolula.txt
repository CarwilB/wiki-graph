'''San Juan Teposcolula''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in the State of [[Oaxaca]], [[Mexico]], often known simply as Teposcolula. The name Teposcolula means “next to the twist in copper”.

It is part of the [[Teposcolula District, Oaxaca|Teposcolula District]] in the center of the [[Mixteca Region, Oaxaca|Mixteca Region]].<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20221a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México, Oaxaca, San Juan Teposcolula |accessdate=2008-02-24 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110616214257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20221a.htm |archivedate=2011-06-16 }}</ref>

==The town==
The town is 2,300 meters above sea level. Long ago it was known as San Juan Itnuyana. The current town was founded in 1561. The main economic activities are logging and the production of [[Mezcal|mescal]].<ref name="EncMuc" />

==The municipality==
As of 2005, the municipality had 349 households with a total population of 1,344, of whom three spoke an indigenous language.
