'''Nuevo Colón''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Márquez Province]]. The urban centre is located at an altitude of {{convert|2500|m|ft}} on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], at a distance of {{convert|34|km|mi}} from the departmental capital [[Tunja]]. Nuevo Colón borders [[Boyacá, Boyacá]], [[Ventaquemada]] and [[Jenesano]] in the north, [[Tibaná]] and [[Turmequé]] in the south, Tibaná in the east and Turmequé and Ventaquemada in the west.<ref name=websiteNuevoColon>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20140310085118/http://www.nuevocolon-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Nuevo Colón]</ref>

== Etymology ==
Nuevo Colón was first called Chiriví, after the name of the area in the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], when it was inhabited by the [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]-speaking [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Chiriví means "our".<ref name=BoyacaNombres>{{in lang|es}} [http://historiadeboyacapueblos.weebly.com/significado-de-boyacaacute-y-los-nombres-de-los-pueblos-en-lengua-chibcha.html El significado del nombre Boyacá y sus pueblos]</ref> It received the name Nuevo Colón in 1907.<ref name=websiteNuevoColon/>

== History ==
Chiriví was part of the domains of the ''[[zaque|hoa]]'' of Hunza before the conquest. [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|Conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] passed through Nuevo Colón in 1537, when he was crossing the [[Tenza Valley]] to discover the [[Colombian emeralds|emerald]] mines of [[Somondoco]], on his way to [[Tunja|Hunza]]. Chiriví, as Nuevo Colón was called, after the foundation of Tunja, became part of the [[encomienda]] of [[Gonzalo Suárez Rendón]]. Nuevo Colón was visited by the first evangelisers in 1556, the first church was constructed in 1776 and modern Nuevo Colón was founded on October 15, 1783. The colour orange in the coat of arms of the municipality refers to the skin colour of the indigenous Muisca.<ref name=websiteNuevoColon/>

==Climate==
Nuevo Colón has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> with mild temperatures. April to November are cool, with frequent precipitation, while December to March are warmer and drier.

== Economy ==
Around 80% of the economy of Nuevo Colón is based on [[agriculture]] of fruits. Main products cultivated are [[prune]]s, [[pear]]s, [[apple]]s, [[peach]]es, and the typical Colombian fruits [[curuba]], [[tree tomato]], [[papayuela]], [[feijoa]], [[Passiflora ligularis|granadilla]] and [[uchuva]]. Additionally [[potato]]es, [[bean]]s and [[maize]] are grown.<ref name=websiteNuevoColon/>
