'''Antônio Carlos''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 11,459 people living in an area of {{cvt|530|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/antonio-carlos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The elevation is 1,058 meters.  The city belongs to the mesoregion of Campo das Vertentes and to the microregion of [[Barbacena]]. The important regional center of Barbacena lies 13&nbsp;km to the north and is connected by highway MG-135. Antônio Carlos is on the railroad line that connects with [[Juiz de Fora]].
