'''Paulo Jacinto''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈpɐwlʊ ʒɐˈsĩtu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 7,560 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/paulo-jacinto/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|108|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>
