'''São Joaquim''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], situated in southern Brazil. Its [[Population (human biology)|population]] in 2020 was 27,139 [[inhabitants]]. Located in the Planalto Serrano, it lies {{convert|136|km|mi|abbr=in}} from [[Tubarão]], {{convert|81|km|mi|abbr=in}} from [[Lages]] and {{convert|227|km|mi|abbr=in}} from [[Florianópolis]]. The town is home to a variety of ethnic groups, including [[Italian Brazilian|Italian]], German, [[Portuguese Brazilian|Portuguese]], [[Japanese Brazilian|Japanese]] and [[Afro-Brazilian|Afro-]]Brazilians. There are also many people from other states in Brazil, especially from [[Rio Grande do Sul]].

==Economy==
The town's [[economy]] was once based on livestock, but recently the cultivation of fruits such as [[apple]]s has become more prominent. Today, São Joaquim is the largest producer of fruit in the state, with over 1000 small producers. Tourism is also important for the town, as it is one of the coldest places in Brazil and sometimes receives [[Snow in Brazil|snowfall]], a rather uncommon occurrence in most of Brazil (although [[Ice pellets|refrozen snow]], not present in most of Brazil, seldom happens in many tropical areas as it does in about half of Southeastern Brazil). The town is also known for its wines, such as Villa Francioni, Joaquim, Quinta da Neve, Núbio and Suzin, which are reaching international recognition, especially in [[East Asia]].

==Geography==
At {{convert|1360.|m}} above sea level, São Joaquim<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.geografos.com.br/cidades-santa-catarina/sao-joaquim.php|title=São Joaquim, Santa Catarina - SC. Coordenadas Geográficas Município Brasileiro São Joaquim.|website=www.geografos.com.br|access-date=2018-10-16}}</ref>) has one of the highest city halls in the country. Its year-round mild climate and cold winter by Brazilian standards (but not restricted temperate species) allows [[apple]] cultivation as in other similar climate areas, leading to the promotion of the National Apple Festival.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ecoviagem.com.br/brasil/santa-catarina/sao-joaquim/|title=São Joaquim - Santa Catarina - A Cidade mais fria do Brasil - EcoViagem|website=ecoviagem.com.br|language=pt-br|access-date=2018-10-09}}</ref> EPAGRI, the state's agricultural agency, has an experimental station where the cultivation of [[Fuji (apple)|Fuji]] apples, brought by Fumio Hiragami brought from [[Curitibanos]], is perfected. The plants come from [[Japan]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.meusroteirosdeviagem.com/2012/06/sao-joaquim-exponeve-e-estacao.html|title=São Joaquim - Exponeve e a Estação Experimental da Epagri {{!}} Meus Roteiros de Viagem|website=www.meusroteirosdeviagem.com|date=7 June 2012 |language=pt-BR|access-date=2018-10-10}}</ref>

Its population was 24,812 inhabitants in the census of 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/censo2010/tabelas_pdf/total_populacao_santa_catarina.pdf|title=População total de Santa Catarina por município}}</ref> The municipality has an area of 1,892.26&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/sao-joaquim/panorama|title=Panorama - São Joaquim|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]]|access-date=2018-10-16}}</ref> It is part of the microregion of Campos de Lages,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://docweb.epagri.sc.gov.br/website_cepa/Dados_regioes/Campos_Lages.pdf|title=MRG - Campos de Lages}}</ref> in the [[Serrana (Santa Catarina)|mesoregion of Serrana]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.cidade-brasil.com.br/mesorregiao-serrana.html|title=Mesorregião Serrana - Informações sobre as cidades desse grupo de municípios|website=www.cidade-brasil.com.br|language=pt|access-date=2018-10-16}}</ref> But since mid-2017 it has been in the immediate region of Lages.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/apps/regioes_geograficas|title=Regiões Geográficas|website=www.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2018-11-04}}</ref>

===Climate===
[[File:Clima.São.Joaquim.SC.jpg|left|thumb|City hall sign indicating an absolute (unofficial) minimum temperature of {{convert|-14.0|C}}]]
Due to its altitude above 4430&nbsp;ft (1350 m) and high latitude, the city has a [[Temperateness|temperate]] [[oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'', [[Trewartha climate classification|Trewartha]]: ''Cflk'') modified for precipitation sufficiently moist in every month, more abundant throughout the year (usually more evenly distributed than in [[Campos do Jordão]]), with mild summers and cool winters with low temperatures - but not below [[Melting point|freezing]]. The average annual temperature is 11.4&nbsp;°C to 14.3&nbsp;°C. The maximum average does not exceed 20&nbsp;°C. At the same time, the minimum averages are above 7&nbsp;°C. The annual precipitation is high, from 1450&nbsp;mm to 1650&nbsp;mm, and about 135 rainy days.<ref>{{Cite journal|last1=Back|first1=Álvaro José|last2=Bruna|first2=Emilio Della|last3=Felipetto|first3=João|date=2014-06-08|title=TENDÊNCIAS NOS ÍNDICES CLIMÁTICOS E AGROCLIMÁTICOS APLICADOS À VIDEIRA NO PLANALTO SERRANO DE SANTA CATARINA|url=https://revistas.ufpr.br/revistaabclima/article/view/33720|journal=Revista Brasileira de Climatologia|language=pt|volume=13|doi=10.5380/abclima.v13i0.33720|issn=2237-8642|doi-access=free}}</ref> Winter precipitation can occur at crucial times in the season, but tend not to be plentiful and cumulative, except on a few occasions.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://pt.weatherspark.com/compare/y/29877~36205/Compara%C3%A7%C3%A3o-das-caracter%C3%ADsticas-meteorol%C3%B3gicas-m%C3%A9dias-em-S%C3%A3o-Joaquim-e-Plymouth|title=Comparação das características meteorológicas médias em São Joaquim e Plymouth|website=pt.weatherspark.com|language=pt|access-date=2018-11-18}}</ref> Along with [[Urupema]], [[Bom Jardim da Serra]] and [[São José dos Ausentes]], São Joaquim is considered the coldest city in Brazil if the maximum average temperature and the number of cold hours are considered.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/cotidiano/2018/06/cidades-de-santa-catarina-travam-guerra-por-titulo-de-mais-fria-do-pais.shtml|title=Cidades de Santa Catarina travam guerra por título de mais fria do país|date=2018-06-21|work=Folha de S.Paulo|access-date=2018-11-18|language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=https://brasil.estadao.com.br/noticias/geral,cidades-de-sc-tem-temperaturas-abaixo-de-6c,70002475217|title=Cidades de SC têm temperaturas abaixo de -6°C - Brasil - Estadão|work=Estadão|access-date=2018-11-18|language=pt-BR}}</ref> During the winter months, [[frost]] is common - every month is subject to the phenomenon, which is more common from May to September (in years of [[La Niña]] from April to October), although strong masses of polar air may enter as early as April. Outside winter, spring and fall may receive frosts due to the prevailing cold dry air.<ref name=":4" /> Frost can occur even in midsummer<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://noticias.uol.com.br/meio-ambiente/ultimas-noticias/redacao/2018/01/30/geada-em-pleno-verao-frente-fria-muda-paisagem-de-sc-com-registro-de-14c.htm|title=Geada em pleno verão. ====Snow and features====
[[File:Snow in São Joaquim (SC) 1988b.jpg|thumb|right|A house covered in snow during the 1988 snowfall]]
São Joaquim is one of the [[Snow in Brazil|few Brazilian cities where snow is relatively common]] every year and is the snowiest city in the country, which makes it a tourist destination. Located in an elevated area, it is an [[urban area]] with the highest number of [[Weather-related cancellation|snowy days]], despite its location in relation to other higher [[rural area]]s.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web|url=https://clmais.com.br/sao-joaquim-e-a-cidade-que-mais-neva-no-brasil-diz-autor/|title=São Joaquim é a cidade que mais neva no Brasil, diz autor – CLMais {{!}} Correio Lageano|website=clmais.com.br|date=19 August 2018 |language=pt-BR|access-date=2018-10-11}}</ref> On July 20, 1957, the city suffered the second largest blizzard in the country, and the largest in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] with 1.3 m of accumulated snow. The city was isolated for a week and received supplies by air.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://g1.globo.com/sc/santa-catarina/noticia/trabalhador-rural-relembra-maior-nevasca-ja-registrada-em-sc-ha-60-anos-foi-emocionante-e-lindo.ghtml|title=Trabalhador rural relembra maior nevasca já registrada em SC, há 60 anos: 'Foi emocionante e lindo'|work=G1|access-date=2018-10-10|language=pt-BR}}</ref> It snowed for seven uninterrupted hours, and continued sporadically in the days following, temporarily closing the [[highway]]s.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://g1.globo.com/sc/santa-catarina/noticia/2014/07/maior-nevasca-de-sc-e-2-maior-do-brasil-faz-57-anos-neste-domingo.html|title=Maior nevasca de SC e 2ª maior do Brasil faz 57 anos neste domingo|date=2014-07-20|work=Santa Catarina|access-date=2018-10-10|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Studies show the increase of the precipitation frequency with the warm phase of the [[Pacific decadal oscillation]] (PDO) and its decrease during the [[El Niño]] and [[La Niña]] episodes. The average is 2.7 days per year in São Joaquim (value close to that of [[Greenville, South Carolina|Greenville]] in the [[United States|US]]) with the majority coming from [[Anticyclone|polar anticyclones]] with an [[extratropical cyclone]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/handle/10183/28490/000694132.pdf?sequence=1|title=Dinâmica e padrões da precipitação de neve no Brasil}}</ref> The maximum gap in the monthly range of [[snow]]fall was between April and October, i.e. between autumn and the first weeks of spring, considering the years 1980 to 2013. In the same interval the year with the most frequency was in 1990, at the same time that there were years without any occurrence. In descending order July and August are the snowiest months, period most affected by cold [[air mass]]es.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.abclima.ggf.br/sbcg2016/anais/arquivos/eixo_6/trabalho%20(1).pdf|title=A CORRELAÇÃO ENTRE AS ONDAS DE FRIO E NEVASCAS EM SÃO JOAQUIM/SC E OS IMPACTOS NA POPULAÇÃO}}</ref> Between 1980 and 2010, the city had 103 cases of snow, if the distribution were even, this would mean that in each year there were at least 3 days with this type of precipitation. In 36% of the cases, there is a causal relationship with the arrival of [[cold wave]]s.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web|url=http://www.labclima.ufsc.br/files/2010/04/Disserta%C3%A7%C3%A3o_Final_RafaelBritoSilveira.pdf|title=ONDAS DE FRIO EM SÃO JOAQUIM - SANTA CATARINA - BRASIL: A SAÚDE COMO FATOR DEPENDENTE DA QUALIDADE DE VIDA}}</ref> A few years that it did not snow are 1971, 1986 and 2005.<ref name=":1" />
