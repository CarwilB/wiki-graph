'''Gastre Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 1,508 inhabitants in an area of 16,335&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Gastre]], which is located around 1,713 km from the [[Capital federal]].
