'''Bonbon''' ({{IPA|fr|bɔ̃bɔ̃|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-WikiLucas00-bonbon.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Bonbon}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jérémie Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. The non-profit organization Sant Demen is establishing a learning center for the Haitian community in the area. It has 8,610 inhabitants in 2015.

The village of [[Desarmeaux]] is also located in the commune.
