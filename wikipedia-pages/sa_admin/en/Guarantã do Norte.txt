'''Guarantã do Norte''' is a municipality located in [[Mato Grosso]] state, [[Brazil]] approximately {{convert|780|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Cuiabá]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=510410|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Mato Grosso {{!}} Guarantã do Norte|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-30}}</ref>

Population: 36,130 inhabitants

Total area: {{convert|2,767|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}

The municipality borders [[Pará]] state. It was granted the status of municipality on 13 May 1986.<ref name=":0" />
