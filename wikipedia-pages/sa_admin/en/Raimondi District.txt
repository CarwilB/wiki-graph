'''Raimondi''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in the middle [[Atalaya Province]] in [[Peru]]. Part of the [[Gran Pajonal]], an elevated plateau occupied primarily by the [[Asháninka]] people. is in Raimondi district.
