'''Succha District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Aija Province]], of the [[Ancash Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}, Retrieved November 10, 2007</ref> The capital of the district is Villa de Succha. Its population was 714 as of the [[2017 Peru Census|2017 census]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Resultados Definitivos de los Censos Nacionales 2017: Ancash |url=https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1552/ |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] |access-date=January 12, 2023 |page=I: 122 |language=Spanish}}</ref>
