'''San Bernardino''' is a residential [[neighbourhood]] of the Venezuelan municipality [[Libertador Municipality (Venezuelan Capital District)|Libertador]] of [[Caracas]], and one of its 32 parishes.

Although it is mostly a middle class residential neighbourhood, San Bernardino also holds the headquarters of [[Banco Mercantil]], [[Banco Venezolano de Crédito]], [[Corpoelec]], and [[Centro Financiero Confinanzas]].{{cn|date=January 2025}}

The [[Quinta de Anauco]], which houses the Caracas Museum of Colonial Art, can also be found in San Bernardino.{{r|universal}}
