'''Villa Isabela''' is a [[town]] in the [[Puerto Plata Province|Puerto Plata]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]].  It lies 9&nbsp;km south of the site of [[La Isabela]], where [[Christopher Columbus]] founded a settlement in 1493.

The archeological site of this town was added to the [[UNESCO]] [[World Heritage]] Tentative List on February 5, 2018 in the Cultural category.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Centre |first=UNESCO World Heritage |title=Sitio Arqueológico de la Villa La Isabela |url=https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6289/ |access-date=2026-03-09 |website=UNESCO World Heritage Centre |language=en}}</ref>
