'''Coatzintla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north of state, about {{Convert|120|km|mi}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It covers an area of {{Convert|235.25|km2|mi2}}. It is located at {{coord|20|29|N|97|28|W|}}. As 2020 the municipality had a population of 55,016, with 34,039 in the municipal seat.<ref name=CENSUSCOATZINTLA2020>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Coatzintla.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf Censo Coatzintla 2020] CEEIG</ref> Other localities includes Geovillas del Real (3,154 hab.), Manuel María Contreras (2,326 hab.), Palma Sola (1,892 hab.) and Corralillos (1,424 hab.).<ref name=CENSUSCOATZINTLA2020/>

The municipality of Coatzintla is delimited to the north by [[Tihuatlán]] and [[Poza Rica]], to the east by [[Papantla]], to the west by the state of [[Puebla]] and to the south-west by [[Coyutla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30040a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Coatzintla|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129121256/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30040a.htm|archive-date=2007-01-29|url-status=dead}}</ref>

It produces principally [[maize]] and [[potatoes]].

Each July in Coatzintla a celebration is held to honor to Señor Santiago, Patron of the town.

The weather in Coatzintla is warm all year with rain in summer and autumn.
