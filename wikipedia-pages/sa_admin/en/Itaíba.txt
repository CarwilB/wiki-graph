'''Itaíba''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[itaˈiˑbɐ]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located 306&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the capital of the state of Pernambuco.  It has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 26,308 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/itaiba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Tupanatinga]]   ('''N''');  [[Alagoas]] state   ('''S''');  [[Buique]] and [[Águas Belas]]   ('''E''');   [[Manari, Pernambuco|Manari]] and Alagoas [[States of Brazil|state]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 1068.29&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 478 m
* Hydrography - [[Ipanema River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 24.1 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 331&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Itaíba''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[beans]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] (over 83,000 heads), [[goats]], [[sheep]], [[pigs]], [[horses]] and [[poultry]].
