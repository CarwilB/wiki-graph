'''Chilcayoc''' (in Hispanicized spelling) or '''Ch'illkayuq''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''ch'illka'' [[baccharis]],<ref>Diccionario Quechua - Español - Quechua, Academía Mayor de la Lengua Quechua, Gobierno Regional Cusco, Cusco 2005: '''''ch'illka''''' - ''s. Bot. (Bacharis molino. Bacharis polyantha kunth).''</ref> ''-yuq'' a [[suffix]] to indicate possession, "the one that has got baccharis" or "the one with baccharis") is the smallest of 11 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Sucre Province]] in the [[Ayacucho Region|Ayacucho region]] in [[Peru]].

==Population==
The population of Chilcayoc is 677 people, 324 men and 353 women.<ref name=inei>[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ INEI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601020523/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ |date=2007-06-01 }}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (86.71%) learnt to speak in childhood, 13.13% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>

==Administrative division==

* Ankasilla ''(Anccasilla)''
* Hatun Rumi ''(Jatun Rumi)''
* Cañana
* Wamrani (Huambrani)
* Willkapampa ''(Vilcabamba)''
