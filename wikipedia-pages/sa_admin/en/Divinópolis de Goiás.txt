'''Divinópolis de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northeastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
Divinópolis de Goiás is part of the [[Vão do Paranã]] statistical micro-region, which consists of 12 municipalities with 88,913 inhabitants in a total area of 17,452.90&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Divinópolis is one of the farthest towns from the state capital and one of the most isolated and poorest. The distance to [[Goiânia]] is 642&nbsp;km. Highway connections are made by BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / [[Alexânia]] / BR-060 / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / GO-430 / GO-118 / BR-010 / [[São João da Aliança]] / [[Alto Paraíso de Goiás]] / [[Teresina de Goiás]] / [[Monte Alegre de Goiás]] / GO-447 / [[Vazante]]. (64&nbsp;km. [[unpaved road]]).<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Transporte] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=February 5, 2009 }}</ref>

It is part of the diocese of [[Formosa, Goiás|Formosa]], a diocese that has an area of 47,604.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 285,427 inhabitants. A curiosity is that there are only 23 priests and 45 nuns for this enormous area.
 
It is bordered in the north by the state of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]] and in the east and south by the municipality of [[São Domingos, Goiás|São Domingos]].

==The economy==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising and modest production of rice, bananas, sugarcane, and corn. In 2007 there were 51 commercial units. There were no banks.

==History==
The settlement of the region began in 1880 with the Galheiros Ranch, owned by Inácia Ferreira Lima, who incentivated the arrival of settlers from the state of [[Bahia]]. Lands were donated to build a chapel to São João Bautista (Saint John the Baptist) and soon houses began to appear. In 1890 the village (povoado) was raised to the status of district belonging to [[São Domingos, Goiás|São Domingos]] with the name of Galheiros, taking the name of the ranch with the same name. In 1958 it separated and became a municipality with the same name. This was changed to Cirinópolis in 1964 in honor of Luis Pereira Cirino, who had fought hard to make it a municipality. Later, in 1989 the name was changed to Divinópolis de Goiás.
