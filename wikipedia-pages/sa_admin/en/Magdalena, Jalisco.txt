'''Magdalena ''' is a town and municipality in the state of [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. Magdalena lies 78 kilometers northwest of [[Guadalajara]]. The municipality covers an area of 293.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It borders the state of [[Nayarit]] to the west, and the town of [[Tequila, Jalisco|Tequila]] to the east. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 18,924.<ref>''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México'', Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal, April 16, 2009.</ref>

== Geography ==
The town is situated on rather high 1350 meters above sea level. It borders mountains to the north and somehow smaller hills to the south.

In the west, there is a big water reservoir called the ''Laguna de Magdalena'', which features many different birds.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Magdalena {{!}} Gobierno del Estado de Jalisco|url=https://www.jalisco.gob.mx/es/jalisco/municipios/magdalena|access-date=2020-07-29|website=www.jalisco.gob.mx}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Avalan importancia arqueológica de exlaguna de Magdalena|url=https://www.milenio.com/politica/comunidad/avalan-importancia-arqueologica-de-exlaguna-de-magdalena|access-date=2020-07-29|website=www.milenio.com}}</ref>

==Gems==
Magdalena is famous for [[fire opal]]s; the whole region accommodates hundreds of mines where [[obsidian]], [[fire opal]]s, and other rocks are produced.<ref>{{cite web |title=World Class Fire Opals Mined Near Puerto Vallarta |url=http://www.banderasnews.com/0810/nb-fireopals.htm |website=BanderasNews}}</ref> In the town, there are several stores selling stones.
