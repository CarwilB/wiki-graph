'''Pensilvania''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] for Pennsylvania) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]].

==Climate==
Pensilvania has a [[subtropical highland climate]] (Cfb) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
