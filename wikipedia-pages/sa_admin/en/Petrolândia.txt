'''Petrolândia''' (''Petroland'', named in honour of [[Dom Pedro II]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is in the São Francisco Region. Petrolândia has a total area of 1056.5 square kilometers and had an estimated population of 36,104 inhabitants in 2025 according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]].<ref name="IBGE" /> It has one of the largest GDP per capita of [[Sertão]] due to the location of one [[hydroelectric]] power plant, property of [[CHESF]].

==Languages==
The [[Kambiwá language]], now extinct, was spoken in the village of Barreira in Petrolândia.<ref name="Meader-Nordeste">{{cite book |last=Meader |first=Robert E. |url=https://www.silbrazil.org/resources/archives/16988 |title=Indios do Nordeste: Levantamento sobre os remanescentes tribais do nordeste brasileiro |year=1978 |publisher=[[SIL International]] |location=Brasilia |lang=pt |archive-date=8 October 2024 |access-date=23 January 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241008181259/https://www.silbrazil.org/resources/archives/16988 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Petrolândia''' are based in commerce, [[energy]] and transportation industry, plus  primary sector especially fruits such as [[coconut]]s, bananas, [[goiaba]]; and creations of goats, sheep, [[bull]]s and chickens. The energy sector due to the presence of [[CHESF|Chesf]] is the most important activity.

==Transport==
From 1881 to 1964 Petrolândia was the [http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/alagoas/petrolandia.htm terminus] of a single track railway, known as the Estrada de Ferro Paulo Afonso. This railway was constructed to bypass rapids and waterfalls on the [[São Francisco River]] by linking the upper limit of navigation of the Lower São Francisco at [[Piranhas, Alagoas|Piranhas]], with the lower limit of navigation of the Upper São Francisco at Petrolândia.
