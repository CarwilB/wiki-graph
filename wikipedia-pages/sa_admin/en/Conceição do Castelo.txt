'''Conceição do Castelo''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in central [[Espírito Santo]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was 12,806 (2020) and its area is 369&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/5W3]</ref>

==History==
The municipality was created when it separated from [[Castelo, Espírito Santo|Castelo]] on 7 May 1964.{{citation needed|date=December 2021}}

==Geography==
Conceição do Castelo is about 120&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Vitória, Brazil|Vitória]]. It stands in the mountainous region of the state, roughly southwest of the capital.{{citation needed|date=December 2021}}

==Economy==
The economy is largely based on agriculture. Some important products are coffee, beans, [[maize]], tomatoes and pigs, as well as cattle.{{citation needed|date=December 2021}}
