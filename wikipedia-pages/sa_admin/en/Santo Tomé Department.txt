'''Santo Tomé Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 54,050 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|7,359|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Santo Tomé, Corrientes|Santo Tomé]], which is located around {{cvt|910|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
