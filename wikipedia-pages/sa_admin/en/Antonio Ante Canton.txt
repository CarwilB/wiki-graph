'''Antonio Ante Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Imbabura Province]].   Its population at the 2001 census was 36,053.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and increased to 43,518 in the 2010 census.<ref>"Ecuador: Municipal Division", https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 10 Jul 2017</ref>  The canton has an area of {{convert|79.79|sqkm|sqmi}}. 

Its capital is the town of [[Atuntaqui]], located {{convert|10|km|mi}} east in straight line distance from the provincial capital of [[Ibarra, Ecuador|Ibarra]].  The province is in the [[Andes]], the capital at an elevation of {{convert|2405|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>

Antonio Ante is divided into five parishes: Atuntaqui (Hauntaki), Imbaya (San Luis de Cobuendo), San Francisco de Natabuela (Natawila), San José de Chaltura, and San Roque.<ref>"Ecuador: Municipal Division," https://citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 13 Jul 2017</ref>
