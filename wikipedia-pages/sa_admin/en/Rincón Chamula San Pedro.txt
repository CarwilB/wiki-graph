'''Rincón Chamula San Pedro''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]], located approximately {{convert|54|km}} north of the state capital of [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez]]. Unlike the [[mestizo]] population of the surrounding towns, its population is mostly indigenous [[Tzotzil]].<ref name=plan/>

==Geography==
The municipality of Rincón Chamula San Pedro is located in the Northern Mountains of Chiapas. It borders the municipalities of [[Ixhuatán, Chiapas|Ixhuatán]] to the north, [[Pueblo Nuevo Solistahuacán]] to the east, [[Jitotol]] to the south, [[Rayón, Chiapas|Rayón]] to the southwest, and [[Tapilula]] to the northwest. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|77.93|km2}}.<ref name=plan/>

The terrain of Rincón Chamula San Pedro is mountainous, ranging from {{convert|1500|to|2400|m}} in elevation. The forests that used to cover the entirety of the municipality have been replaced to a significant extent by farmland and rangeland.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=6 September 2017 |title=Decreto Número 248 |url=http://www.sgg.chiapas.gob.mx/po2012/archivos/descargas.php?f=C-316-06092017-730.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Chiapas, Segunda Sección |access-date=25 October 2020 |archive-date=31 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201031075632/https://www.sgg.chiapas.gob.mx/po2012/archivos/descargas.php?f=C-316-06092017-730.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The climate ranges from temperate to tropical depending on the elevation, with rain falling year-round.<ref name=decree/>

==History==
In the 17th century, the community of Rincón Chamula San Pedro was founded by Tzotzil who had been expelled from [[San Juan Chamula]] for religious reasons.<ref name=plan/><ref name=decree/> It was attached to Pueblo Nuevo Solistahuacán when that municipality was established in 1922.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM07chiapas/municipios/07072a.html |title=Pueblo Nuevo Solistahuacán |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Chiapas |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=25 October 2020}}</ref> On 18 August 2017, the [[Congress of Chiapas]] approved the separation of Rincón Chamula San Pedro and its neighbouring communities to create a new municipality.<ref name=creation>{{cite news |last=Burguete Cal y Mayor |first=Araceli |date=22 August 2017 |url=https://www.chiapasparalelo.com/opinion/2017/08/nuevos-municipios-en-chiapas-un-expediente-abierto/ |title=Nuevos municipios en Chiapas: un expediente abierto |language=es |newspaper=Chiapas Paralelo |access-date=25 October 2020}}</ref> The decree establishing Rincón Chamula San Pedro and [[Capitán Luis Ángel Vidal]] as new municipalities was gazetted on 6 September 2017.<ref name=decree/>

==Administration==
In 2018, Rincón Chamula San Pedro elected its first municipal president, Reynaldo Girón Bautista.<ref>{{cite news |date=13 December 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-chiapas-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Chiapas 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=25 October 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Rincón Chamula San Pedro recorded a total population of 7244 inhabitants,<ref name=ageeml/> 5968 or 82% of which reported speaking an indigenous language, [[Tzotzil language|Tzotzil]] being the main one spoken. Of the population that spoke an indigenous language, 58% also spoke Spanish.<ref name=plan/>

There are nine localities in the municipality, of which only the municipal seat named Rincón Chamula is designated as urban. It recorded a population of 5592 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=ageeml/>

==Economy==
Agriculture and animal husbandry are the major economic activities in Rincón Chamula San Pedro. The main crops grown are corn, beans, potatoes, and onions. At lower elevations, beef cattle and pigs are raised.<ref name=decree/>
