'''Jaime Zudáñez''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Chuquisaca Department|Chuquisaca]]. It was named after [[Jaime de Zudáñez]], emancipation leader born in Chuquisaca in 1772.
