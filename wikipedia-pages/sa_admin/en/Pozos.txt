'''Pozos''' is a  [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] in [[Santa Ana (canton)|Santa Ana]] canton, [[San José Province|San José]] province of [[Costa Rica]]. It was created in 1907. The principal avenue of the district carries the name of Lindora, which is also used to name the district and its surroundings.<ref name=":0">{{citation|url=http://www.inec.go.cr/sites/default/files/documetos-biblioteca-virtual/reanuario2014-2015.pdf|title=Anuario Estadístico 2014-2015|publisher=Anuario Estadístico 2014 - 2015 “Comprendio de datos del país”|access-date=24 June 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.santaana.go.cr/index.php/institucional/el-concejo/concejos-de-distrito/86-conejos-de-distrito-de-lindora|title=Municipalidad de Santa Ana|access-date=8 September 2018|website=www.santaana.go.cr|language=es-es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.santaana.go.cr/index.php/institucional/el-concejo/concejos-de-distrito|title=Municipalidad de Santa Ana|access-date=9 September 2018|website=www.santaana.go.cr|language=es-es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.inec.go.cr/poblacion/estimaciones-y-proyecciones-de-poblacion|title=Estimaciones y Proyecciones de población {{!}} INEC|access-date=24 June 2018|website=www.inec.go.cr|language=es}}</ref><ref name=":03">{{citation|url=http://www.inec.go.cr/sites/default/files/documetos-biblioteca-virtual/reanuario2014-2015.pdf|title=Anuario Estadístico 2014-2015|publisher=Anuario Estadístico 2014 - ==Toponymy== 
{{lang|es|Pozos}} is translated as ''ponds'', of which there were many due to clay terrains at the north part of the district.

== Geography ==
Pozos has an area of {{formatnum:13.34}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:847}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

==Villages==
The district encompasses the Bosques de Lindora, Honduras, Lagos, Montana, Valle del Sol, Villa Real, Villas del Sol and Vista Azul villages.
