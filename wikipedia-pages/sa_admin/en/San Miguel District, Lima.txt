'''San Miguel''' is one of the 43 districts that are part of the Lima province and of the urban area of [[Lima]], [[Peru]]. It is bordered by the districts of [[Bellavista District|Bellavista]] and [[Lima District|downtown Lima]] on the north; [[Pueblo Libre]], [[Magdalena del Mar]] and downtown Lima on the east; the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the south; and the [[La Perla District|La Perla district]] on the west.

San Miguel is an [[upper-class]] (65% of its population) and upper-middle class (35%) district with a very high [[Human Development Index|HDI]] score.<ref>{{cite book |title=Planos estratificados de Lima Metropolitana a nivel de manzanas 2020|url=https://www.inei.gob.pe/media/MenuRecursivo/publicaciones_digitales/Est/Lib1744/libro.pdf|last=Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|location=Lima|page=52|year=2020}}</ref>

==Points of interest==
The [[Parque de las Leyendas]] (the city's [[zoo]]), the parque de la imaginación,<ref>Comisión de Promoción del Perú para la Exportación y el Turismo (2000), Lima y Callao (in Spanish), page 18</ref> the parish church of San Miguel,<ref>[[Google Maps]], accessed on 18 April 2026</ref> and the [[Federico Villarreal National University]] are located in the district.<ref>[https://www.unfv.edu.pe/ Federico Villarreal National University], accessed on 18 April 2026</ref>
