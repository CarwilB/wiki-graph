'''São Miguel dos Campos-AL''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃w miˈgɛw 'dʊɦ ˈkɐ̃pu]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the eastern half of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 61,797 as of 2020 estimates<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/sao-miguel-dos-campos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|361|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
