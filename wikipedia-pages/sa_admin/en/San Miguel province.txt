'''San Miguel''' is one of the thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Cajamarca Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was created by Law No. 15152 on September 29, 1964, by president [[Fernando Belaunde Terry]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de San Miguel, ''[http://www.munisanmiguel-cajamarca.com/h_repub.htm San Miguel en la República] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071113002716/http://www.munisanmiguel-cajamarca.com/h_repub.htm |date=2007-11-13 }}''. Retrieved on November 4, 2007.</ref> It has a [[Mountain|mountainous]] territory which varies in height from 500 to more than 4,000 metres {{nowrap|(1,600–13,000 ft)}} above sea level. As a result, there is a great diversity of [[Climate|climates]] ranging from hot and dry at lower altitudes to cold and rainy at higher levels.<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de San Miguel, ''[http://www.munisanmiguel-cajamarca.com/aspc_geo.htm Aspecto Geofísico]''. Retrieved on November 4, 2007.</ref> Herding is an important economic activity thanks to the existence of extensive [[pasture]]s. Its main product is cow's milk which is either sold outside the province or transformed into dairy products such as cheese. There are several gold mines in production in the higher regions of the province. There's also an important handicraft industry mainly devoted to textiles made out of cotton or wool.<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de San Miguel, ''[http://www.munisanmiguel-cajamarca.com/produccion.htm Producción]''. Retrieved on November 4, 2007.</ref>

==Archaeology==
The oldest known irrigation canals in the Americas are located in the [[Nanchoc District]] of the province of San Miguel. The canals in the [[Zaña Valley]] have been [[radiocarbon]] dated to 3400 BCE, and possibly date to 4700 BCE.<ref name="Dillehay, 2005">{{cite journal |last1=Dillehay |first1=Tom D. |last2=Eling, Jr. |first2=Herbert H. |last3=Rossen |first3=Jack |title=Preceramic irrigation canals in the Peruvian Andes |url=https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/102/47/17241.full.pdf |journal=Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America |year=2005 |volume=102 |issue=47 |pages=17241–17244 |publisher=National Academy of Science |doi=10.1073/pnas.0508583102 |pmid=16284247 |pmc=1288011 |access-date=20 November 2020 |doi-access=free |bibcode=2005PNAS..10217241D }}</ref>
