'''Belisario Boeto''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Chuquisaca Department|Chuquisaca]].

== Subdivision ==
The province consists of only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Villa Serrano Municipality''', which is identical to the province. Belisario Boeto Province is further subdivided into three cantons. The cantons with their seats are:
