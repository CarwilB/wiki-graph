'''Wiwilí de Nueva Segovia''' ({{IPA|es|wiwiˈli ðe ˈnweβa seˈɣoβja}}) is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Nueva Segovia Department]] of [[Nicaragua]], on the west bank of the [[Coco River|Rio Coco]]; [[Wiwilí de Jinotega]] is on the east bank. It borders the [[Bosawas National Natural Resource Reserve]], the largest protected area in Central America.{{cn|date=October 2021}}
