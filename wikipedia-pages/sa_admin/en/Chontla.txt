'''Chontla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the northern part of the state, about 220&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 361.09 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|21|18|N|97|55|W|}}.

==Geography==

===Borders===
Chontla is delimited to the west by [[Tantoyuca]], to the south by [[Ixcatepec]] and [[Tepetzintla, Veracruz|Tepetzintla]], and to the east by [[Citlaltépetl, Veracruz|Citlaltépetl]] and [[Ozuluama de Mascareñas (municipality)|Ozuluama]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30063a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chontla|publisher=[Gobierno Estatal]|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070516085444/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30063a.htm|archive-date=2007-05-16|url-status=dead}}</ref>

===Weather===
The weather in Chontla is warm all year with rains in summer.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[chili pepper]]s.
