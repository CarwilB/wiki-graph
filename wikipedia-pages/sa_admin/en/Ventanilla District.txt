'''Ventanilla''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Callao]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.infobae.com/peru/2024/09/23/ventanilla-la-historia-de-como-paso-de-ser-una-ciudad-satelite-a-convertirse-en-el-distrito-mas-grande-del-callao/|title=Ventanilla: la historia de cómo pasó de ser una ciudad satélite a convertirse en el distrito más grande del Callao|first=Manoel|last=Obando|work=[[Infobae]]|date=29 September 2024|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> Covering more than half of the province's territory, it is Callao's largest district.

The current [[mayor]] of Ventanilla is Pedro Carmelo Spadaro.

==History==
It was officially established as a district on January 28, 1969. The first stone for the building of Ventanilla was placed on September 24, 1960, in what is now the Central Church of Ventanilla San Pedro Nolasco.

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 73.52 km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 71 meters above [[sea level]]. Ventanilla is located in the northern part of the province.

Ventanilla is made up of eight urban zones and more than 160 neighborhoods (''barrios'').

===Boundaries===
* North: [[Santa Rosa District (Lima)|Santa Rosa]] and [[Ancón District|Ancón]] (both in the [[Lima Province]])
* East: [[Puente Piedra District|Puente Piedra]] (Lima Province) and [[Mi Peru District|Mi Peru]] (Callao).
* South: [[Callao District|Downtown Callao]], and [[San Martín de Porres District|San Martín de Porres]] (Lima Province)
* West: [[Pacific Ocean]]

==Demographics==
According to the [[Peru 2005 Census|2005 census]] by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 243,526 inhabitants and a population density of 3312.4 persons/km<sup>2</sup>.
