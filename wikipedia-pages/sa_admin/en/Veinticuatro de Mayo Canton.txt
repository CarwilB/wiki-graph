'''Veinticuatro de Mayo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Manabí Province]].  Its capital is the city of [[Sucre, Manabí|Sucre]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 28,294.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
