'''Chignahuapan'''  ({{IPA|es|tʃiɣnaˈwapan|-|Chignahuapan.ogg}}) is a town in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]]. It serves as the seat of the surrounding [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] of the same name.<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070405081753/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/ |archive-date=April 5, 2007 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . --->
The municipality is the location of many touristic places very well known all over the state such as the [[Inmaculada Concepción, Chignahuapan|Basilica of the Immaculate Conception]], which is the biggest figure of the virgin Mary in Latin America, and also, Las Aguas Termales de Chignahuapan (thermal waters).

The main economy of Chignahuapan is making Christmas [[baubles]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Medina|first=Imelda|title=Tuesday, Nov 27, Mexico|url=http://imgfeed.pictopia.com/u127/search/author/14509/|work=Reuters|publisher=Reuters|access-date=15 December 2011}}{{dead link|date=August 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
