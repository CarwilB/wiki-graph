'''Cochamarca''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Qucha Marka''' or '''Quchamarka''', meaning "lake village") is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the  [[Oyón Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
