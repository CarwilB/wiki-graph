'''Capitán Miranda''' is a [[town]] in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]], about 15&nbsp;km north of the department capital [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]].

The [[Teniente Amin Ayub Gonzalez Airport]] is located just west of the town.
