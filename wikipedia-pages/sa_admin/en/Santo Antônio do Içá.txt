'''Santo Antônio do Içá''' is a community and a municipality in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] near the Colombian border in [[Brazil]]. The population is 21,243 (2020 est.)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/santo-antonio-do-ica/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> in an area of 12,307 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1T1 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The municipality was created in 1955 out of [[São Paulo de Olivença]]. The city is served by [[Ipiranga Airport]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|15000|ha|adj=on}} [[Javari-Buriti Area of Relevant Ecological Interest]], created in 1985.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ARIE Javari-Buriti – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=ARIE Javari-Buriti|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3644|accessdate=2016-10-19}}</ref>
It contains parts of the [[Jutaí-Solimões Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Jutaí-Solimões|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=56|accessdate=2016-05-04}}</ref>
