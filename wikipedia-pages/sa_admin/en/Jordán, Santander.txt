'''Jordán''' ({{IPA|es|xoɾˈðan}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]].

The little town of '''Jordán,''' located in the Colombian Department of '''Santander'''. has a population of around 56/67 people. Not to mention that this little town is located in the middle of the [[Canyon of chicamocha|Cañón del Chicamocha]], The second largest Canyon in the world.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ORGANIZACIÓN COLPARQUES - Paraísos por descubrir en Colombia! |url=http://www.colparques.net/ |access-date=2023-02-25 |website=ORGANIZACIÓN COLPARQUES - Paraísos por descubrir en Colombia!}}</ref> The town is mainly based in agriculture, since it has large fields of fruits like; lemons, Tomatoes and Oranges.
