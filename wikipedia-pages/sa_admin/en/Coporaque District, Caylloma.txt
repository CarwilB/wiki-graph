'''Coporaque District''' is one of twenty [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Caylloma Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Chila mountain range]] traverses the district. One of the highest mountains of the district is [[Qullqiri (Arequipa)|Qullqiri]] at approximately {{convert|5200|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL maps of the Caylloma Province 2, Arequipa Region</ref>

* Hatun Urqu
* Pumachiri
* Uma Qala
* Willkayuq
* Yuraq Qaqa

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (61.18%) learnt to speak in childhood, 37.62% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
