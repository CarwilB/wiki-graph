'''Marigot''' ({{IPA|fr|maʁiɡo|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Lepticed7-marigot.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Marigo}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Jacmel Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 50,739 inhabitants.

In early 1793, during the [[Haitian Revolution]], Marigot's black inhabitants rebelled and built a fort in the city.<ref>{{cite book| last = Rey | first = Terry | title = The Priest and the Prophetess: Abbé Ouvière, Romaine Rivière, and the Revolutionary Atlantic World | publisher = Oxford University Press | year = 2017 | isbn = 978-0190625849 | pp=44, 235 }}</ref> Abbé Aubert, a white priest and leader of the rebellion in the area, also commandeered Marigot's cannons and contributed them to [[Romaine-la-Prophétesse]]'s slave uprising around and siege of [[Jacmel]].{{sfn|Rey|2017|pp=129-130}}

The Haitian writer {{ill|Émile Célestin-Mégie|fr}} was born in Marigot.<ref>Edith Wainwright, ''Culture haïtienne à travers des textes choisis'' (2001), page 72</ref>
