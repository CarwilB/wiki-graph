'''Usumatlán''' ({{IPA|es|usumaˈtlan}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n department of [[Zacapa Department|Zacapa]]. In 2013, the population was 11,045.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://aprende.guatemala.com/historia/geografia/municipio-de-usumatlan-zacapa/|title=Municipio de Usumatlán, Zacapa|first=Luis|last=Valladares|website=aprende.guatemala.com|date=22 February 2024|access-date=19 June 2025|language=es}}</ref>
