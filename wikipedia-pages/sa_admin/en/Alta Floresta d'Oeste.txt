'''Alta Floresta d'Oeste''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈawtɐ floˈɾɛstɐ doˈɛstʃi|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 22,728 (2020) and its area is 7,067&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/ED6]</ref>

It holds part of the {{convert|615771|ha}} [[Guaporé Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Guaporé|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=206|accessdate=2016-04-26}}</ref>
