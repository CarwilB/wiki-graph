'''Cañete''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Department of Lima|department of Lima]], [[Peru]]. From the administrative point of view of the [[Catholic Church in Peru]], it forms part of the [[Territorial Prelature of Yauyos]]. It is bordered by the [[Lima Province|province of Lima]] to the north, the [[Ica Region]] to the south, the provinces of [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí]] and [[Yauyos Province|Yauyos]] to the east, and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the west. Its capital is the town of [[San Vicente de Cañete District]].

San Luis is the capital of [[Afro-Peruvian]] [[folklore]]. Is the most populated province in the department, and its third most important province (after [[Lima Province|Lima]] and [[Huaura Province|Huaura]]).

==Climate==
The weather of the province's coastal area has two well-defined seasons:
* The sunny season, with temperatures of around 30&nbsp;°C (86.4&nbsp;°F), during daytime.
* The "sunless" season (May–December), during which the sun is covered by clouds. The extreme [[humidity]] causes a sensation of cold during this season, even if the temperature never gets lower than 11&nbsp;°C (51.8&nbsp;°F).

==Attractions==
[[File:Cañete River May 2012.jpg|thumbnail|right|Cañete River]]

The coast of Cañete, with beaches such as [[Asia District, Peru|Asia]], Chilca, Puerto Viejo, León Dormido, Costa del Campo,  Totoritas, Chocaya, Chepeconde, Puerto Fiel, Gallardo and Cerro Azul, is one of the province's main touristic attractions. The pleasant valley of [[Lunahuaná]], a paradise for adventure sports lovers, is just half an hour from San Vicente de Cañete. The main attraction is the fast running [[Cañete River]], which features [[rapids]] up to Class IV. Each year, the valley hosts a festival involving rafting, trekking, gliding, mountain biking and fishing competitions. There are a few annual festivals in Cañete. The most important ones are Festival of [[musica negra|Black Art]] in late August and Adventure Sports Festival in Lunahuaná, held in February.
