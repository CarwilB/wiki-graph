== Cocoa Festival ==
Until the 1990s, the area reached levels of extreme poverty, due to the internal armed conflict in Peru, attacking several native communities in the district and leaving 1,500 settlers dedicated to the cultivation of [[coca]].

During the mayoralty of Manuel Gambini and Yonel Mendoza a process of changing crops to cocoa was implemented. <ref>{{Cite web |title=Irazola: Un distrito que curó  las heridas que dejó el terror {{!}} Diario Expreso |url=http://www.expreso.com.pe/noticia/2014/02/01/irazola-un-distrito-que-curo-las-heridas-que-dejo-el-terror |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141006115251/http://www.expreso.com.pe/noticia/2014/02/01/irazola-un-distrito-que-curo-las-heridas-que-dejo-el-terror |archive-date=2014-10-06 |access-date=2025-09-12 |website=www.expreso.com.pe |language=es}}</ref>

From then on, together with the Regional government of Ucayali, the cocoa festival is held annually with activities related to the processes derived from chocolate, parades and beauty pageants. <ref>{{Cite news |last=Peru.com |date=2012-07-27 |title=Inauguran el V Festival Regional del Cacao Irazola 2012 en Ucayali {{!}} Peru.com |url=http://peru.com/2012/07/27/viajes/noticia-de-viajes/inauguran-v-festival-regional-cacao-irazola-2012-ucayali-noticia-77500 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141006091941/http://peru.com/2012/07/27/viajes/noticia-de-viajes/inauguran-v-festival-regional-cacao-irazola-2012-ucayali-noticia-77500 |archive-date=2014-10-06 |access-date=2025-09-12 |work=Perú.com}}</ref>
