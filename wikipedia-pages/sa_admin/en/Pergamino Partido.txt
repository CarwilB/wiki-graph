'''Pergamino Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 100,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2950|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Pergamino]], which is around {{convert|225|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
