'''Isla''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south zone of the state, about 370&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 714.80 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|02|N|95|32|W|}}.

==Etymology==
In Spanish, the word "'''Isla'''" means "'''[[Island]]'''".
==Geography==
The municipality of Isla is delimited to the north by the municipalities of [[Tlacotalpan (municipality)|Tlacotalpan]] and [[Santiago Tuxtla]], to the east by [[Hueyapan de Ocampo]] and [[Juan Rodríguez Clara]], to the south by [[Playa Vicente]] and to the west by [[José Azueta, Veracruz|José Azueta]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30077a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Isla|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070427135757/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30077a.htm|archive-date=2007-04-27|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Isla is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]], [[watermelon]] and [[pineapple]].

==Events==
In ''' Isla''', in May takes place the celebration in honor to Sagrado Corazón, Patron of the town.
