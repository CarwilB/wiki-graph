'''Monte Patria''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and one of four [[Communes of Chile|commune]]s in the [[Limarí Province]] of [[Chile]]'s north-central [[Coquimbo Region]].

==Administration==
As a commune, Monte Patria is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a Communal Council ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''Consejo Comunal''), headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. As of 2010, the alcalde is [[Juan Carlos Castillo]].

Monte Patria belongs to the 9th [[Electoral division of Chile|electoral district]] and the 4th Senate District (Coquimbo Region). It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] of the [[National Congress of Chile|National Congress]] by the Members Adriana Muñoz<!-- not the Cuban middle distance runner --> ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and [[Luis Lemus]] ([[Regionalist Party of the Independents|PRI]]). Currently, Coquimbo is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Jorge Pizarro]] ([[Christian Democrat Party|PDC]]) and [[Evelyn Matthei]] ([[Independent Democrat Union|UDI]]).

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Monte Patria commune had 30,276 inhabitants; of these, 13,340 (44.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 16,936 (55.9%) in [[rural area]]s. Thus, the Monte Patria population represents 5.1% of regional population and 19.2% of the provincial population. At that time, there were 15,351 men and 14,925 women residing in the commune.<ref name="INE"/>
