'''Campo Grande''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈkɐ̃pu ˈɡɾɐ̃di]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. It is situated in the [[Agreste Alagoano]] [[Mesoregions of Brazil|mesoregion]] and belongs to the [[Arapiraca (microregion)|Arapiraca]] [[Microregions of Brazil|microregion]].<ref name="rigeo">{{cite web |title=PROJETO CADASTRO DE FONTES DE ABASTECIMENTO POR ÁGUA SUBTERRÂNEA |url=https://rigeo.cprm.gov.br/jspui/bitstream/doc/15250/1/rel_cadastros_campo_grande.pdf |website=rigeo.cprm.gov.br |access-date=31 May 2023|language=pt}}</ref>

== History ==
Settlers first arrived in Campo Grande during the 18th century attracted by the farmland of the region. Settlers continued to make communities there, but the town was not officially incorporated until June 27, 1957, when the district was founded. However, it remained a part of the municipality of São Brás until the municipality of Campo Grande was founded on May 31, 1960.<ref name="IBGE História">{{cite web |title=História Campo Grande Alagoas - AL |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/campo-grande/historico |website=ibge.gov.br |publisher=IBGE |access-date=31 May 2023|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="Guia do Turista">{{cite web |title=Campo Grande Dicas incríveis da Cidade |url=https://www.guiadoturista.net/america-do-sul/brasil/alagoas/campo-grande |website=guiadoturista.net |access-date=31 May 2023|language=pt}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Campo Grande is located in the state of Alagoas, in the northeastern region of Brazil. It is situated in the Agreste Alagoano, which is known for its diverse flora and fauna.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Galindo Alves de Queiróz |first=Andreza |title=FAUNA NO SERTÃO ALAGOANO: RESGATES E APREENSÕES REALIZADAS EM UM PROGRAMA DE FISCALIZAÇÃO CONJUNTA |url=https://cdn.agenciapeixevivo.org.br/media/2019/06/Fauna-no-sert%C3%A3o-alagoana_resgates-e-apreens%C3%B5es-realizadas-em-um-programa-de-fiscaliza%C3%A7%C3%A3o-conjunta.pdf |access-date=31 May 2023 |language=pt}}</ref> The municipality covers a total area of 170.144 square kilometers.<ref name=":0" />

== Demographics ==
According to the 2021 estimate by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), Campo Grande has a population of 9,567 residents. The municipality has a population density of 53.98 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades@ {{!}} Alagos {{!}} Campo Grande {{!}} Panorama |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/campo-grande/panorama |access-date=2023-05-30 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|language=pt}}</ref>

== Governance ==
The current mayor of Campo Grande is Téo Higino. He won the election by a margin of 9 votes. The previous Mayor, Arnaldo Higino, received 51.4% of the votes, but was convicted of misconduct and thus Téo Higino was elected mayor.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/al/alagoas/noticia/2021/09/12/teo-higino-do-republicanos-e-eleito-prefeito-de-campo-grande-al.ghtml|title=Téo Higino, do Republicanos, é eleito prefeito de Campo Grande, AL|website=globo.com|language=pt}}</ref>
