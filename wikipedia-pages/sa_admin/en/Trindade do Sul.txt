'''Trindade do Sul''' is a municipality in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

== Geography ==
The municipality of Trindade do Sul is a part of the [[Microregion of Frederico Westphalen]]. It is located at a [[latitude]] of 27º31'13" south and a [[longitude]] of 52º53'00" west, being at an [[altitude]] of 640 meters above sea level.

This municipality is divided in several localities, called lines ({{Langx|pt-BR|Linhas}}). Those are:
* Barra Grande
* Barra Seca
* Barrinha
* Baú
* Bonita
* Cachoeira
* Campina de Pedras
* Passo do Lobo
* Caturrita
* Colônia Nova
* Demétrio
* Filisbina
* Gastão
* Girau
* São Paulo
* São Vicente
* Fátima
* Rincão dos Rosas
* Rossetto
* Assentamento 29 de Outubro

== Population ==
According to [[IBGE]] statistics from 2020, Trindade do Sul has a population of 5,791 people.
