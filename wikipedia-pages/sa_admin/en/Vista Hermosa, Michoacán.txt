'''Vista Hermosa''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "beautiful view") is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Michoacán]], located approximately {{convert|150|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Vista Hermosa is located at an elevation between {{convert|1600|and(-)|1700|m}}  in the [[Bajío]] region in northwestern Michoacán. It borders the Michoacanese municipalities of [[Tanhuato]] to the east, [[Ixtlán, Michoacan|Ixtlán]] to the southeast, [[Pajacuarán Municipality|Pajacuarán]] to the southwest, and [[Briseñas]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16105.pdf |title=Vista Hermosa, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref> Additionally, the [[Lerma River]] forms the border between Vista Hermosa and the Jaliscan municipality of [[La Barca Municipality|La Barca]] to the north. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|148.022|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.3% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, 91% of the land in Vista Hermosa is used for agriculture and urban areas cover another 4% of the municipality. [[Vertisol]]s are the main soil type in the municipality.<ref name=compendium/> The {{ill|Duero River (Michoacán)|lt=Duero River|es|Río Duero (Michoacán)}} flows east to west through the southern part of the municipality, and meets the Lerma River at [[Ibarra, Michoacan|Ibarra]] in the adjacent municipality of Briseñas. The Gonzalo Reservoir lies on the border between Tanhuato and Vista Hermosa and has a total capacity of {{convert|7600000|m3}} and a useful capacity of {{convert|6600000|m3}}.<ref>{{cite report |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenido/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/anuarios_2017/702825092092.pdf |title=Anuario estadístico y geográfico de Michoacán de Ocampo 2017 |at=Table 4.17 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2017 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

Vista Hermosa has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|18|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The settlement of Vista Hermosa was organized in the second half of the 19th century from the ''[[hacienda]]s'' of Buenavista and El Molino. Vista Hermosa was established as a municipality on 1 December 1921 from localities previously part of Ixtlán and Tanhuato. The municipal seat was named Vista Hermosa de Negrete after José María Martínez Negrete, who bought the lands of the Molino ''hacienda'' after they were confiscated by the Mexican government and built a school there.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16105a.html |title=Vista Hermosa |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=9 January 2021 |archive-date=11 January 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210111201449/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16105a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 1950, the municipality of Briseñas was separated from Vista Hermosa.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=68, 91, 226 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4 |archive-date=2019-04-12 |access-date=2021-01-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190412021950/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Vista Hermosa comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Rosa Elena de la Cruz Pérez Tello.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Vista Hermosa recorded a population of 18,995 inhabitants living in 4789 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=105 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Vista Hermosa |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 20,624 inhabitants in Vista Hermosa.<ref name=survey/>

[[INEGI]] lists 16 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which two are classified as urban:

*the municipal seat Vista Hermosa de Negrete, which recorded a population of 10,752 inhabitants in the 2010 Census; and
*El Capulín, located {{convert|7|km}} south of the municipal seat, which recorded a population of 2438 in 2010.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Agriculture is the main economic activity in Vista Hermosa. Major crops grown include corn, sorghum, wheat, and vegetables such as chiles.<ref>{{cite news |date=16 December 2020 |url=https://www.brunoticias.com/yurecuaro-tanhuato-vista-hermosa-chile/ |title=Yurécuaro, Tanhuato y Vista Hermosa líderes productores de chile en Michoacán |language=es |website=Brunoticias |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref> The largest employer in the municipality is the meat producer [[Sukarne|SuKarne]], which maintains a herd of 150,000 cattle in the municipality.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Vista Hermosa Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/3a-8919.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=4 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 3a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=19 February 2019 |access-date=9 January 2021}}</ref>

The toll expressway [[Mexican Federal Highway 15D|Federal Highway 15D]] runs through the municipality, as do [[Mexican Federal Highway 35|Federal Highways 35]] and [[Mexican Federal Highway 110|110]].
