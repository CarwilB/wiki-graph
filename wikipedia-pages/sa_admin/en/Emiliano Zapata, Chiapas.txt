'''Emiliano Zapata''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]], located approximately {{convert|33|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Emiliano Zapata is located in the [[Chiapas Depression dry forests|Chiapas Depression]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Acala Municipality|Acala]] to the east, [[Venustiano Carranza, Chiapas|Venustiano Carranza]] to the southeast, and [[Chiapa de Corzo, Chiapas|Chiapa de Corzo]] to the west. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|179.14|km2}}.<ref name=area/>

The rolling terrain of Emiliano Zapata is mostly covered by farmland and pastureland with isolated patches of jungle.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/biblioteca/ficha.html?upc=702825292942 |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Acala, Chiapas |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref> The [[Grijalva River]] forms the northern and southeastern border of the municipality,<ref name=map>{{cite map |url=http://www.ceieg.chiapas.gob.mx/productos/files/MAPASMUNDC/Base_Emiliano_Zapata.pdf |title=Municipio de Emiliano Zapata |year=2019 |scale=1:110,000 |publisher=CEIEG |access-date=2 November 2020 }}{{Dead link|date=November 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref> and the [[Angostura Dam (Mexico)|Angostura Dam]] (officially called the Belisario Domínguez Dam) on that river is located on the border between Emiliano Zapata and Venustiano Carranza.

==History==
On 23 November 2011, the government of Chiapas decreed the creation of the municipalities of [[Belisario Domínguez Municipality|Belisario Domínguez]], [[El Parral, Chiapas|El Parral]], Emiliano Zapata, and [[Mezcalapa]].<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=23 November 2011 |title=Decreto Número 008 |url=http://www.sgg.chiapas.gob.mx/po2006/archivos/descargas.php?f=P.O.%20337%20SEGUNDA%20SECCION.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Chiapas, Segunda Sección |access-date=2 November 2020 }}{{Dead link|date=March 2024 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> Emiliano Zapata was created from the ''ejido'' of 20 de Noviembre which was formerly part of the municipality of Acala.<ref>{{cite news |date=14 January 2012 |title=Four new municipalities change the map of Chiapas |url=https://geo-mexico.com/?p=5696 |work=Geo-Mexico, the geography and dynamics of modern Mexico |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Emiliano Zapata recorded a total population of 9915 inhabitants.<ref name=ageeml/>

There are 142 localities in the municipality, of which only the municipal seat 20 de Noviembre is designated as urban. It recorded a population of 4636 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=ageeml/>

==Infrastructure==
A paved [[spur route]] of [[Mexican Federal Highway 190]] runs from Chiapa de Corzo northwest to southeast through the municipality, crossing over the Grijalva River on the Angostura Dam into Venustiano Carranza.<ref name=map/> [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez International Airport]] is located about {{convert|13|km}} west of 20 de Noviembre.
