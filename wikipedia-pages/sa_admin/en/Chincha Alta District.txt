'''Chincha Alta District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Chincha Province|Chincha]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

One of its iconic sites is the Manor House known as [[Hacienda San José (hotel)|Hacienda San José]].

Mayor: Armando Huamán Tasayco <small>(2019-2022)</small>
