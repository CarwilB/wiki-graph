'''Aramecina''' ({{IPA|es|aɾameˈsina}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle (department)|Valle]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|100.4|km2}} and had a population of 7,173 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
The village of Santa Lucia was established in 1578 in the Apasapo river basin, after the discovery of minerals in the Potosí hills in the region. In 1740, the municipality of Apasapo was established, which later became part of the Valle department in 1893, with Santa Lucia as its seat. In 1907, the municipal seat was relocated to a new settlement, which was named "San Sebastián de Aramecina", with Aramecina meaning  "river of small Magüey plants". During the [[Football War]], in July 1969, El Salvador attacked Honduras. The [[El Salvador Air Force]] bombed Honduras, and the [[El Salvador Army|ground units]] plundered the town, with the majority of the population fleeing east.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Aramecina, Valle|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Valle/Aramecina.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Aramecina|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-aramecina/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Aramecina is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle Department|Valle]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Caridad]] to the north, [[Langue]] to the east, and [[Goascorán]] to the south. It shares an international border with the Republic of [[El Salvador]] to the west, which is demarcated by the [[Goascoran River]].<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|100.4|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1704__aramecina/|title=Aramecina, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|118.94|m}} above sea level, Aramecina has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|28.51|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|185.69|mm}} of precipitation annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/valle/aramecina|title=Aramecina, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Aramecina had a total population of 7,173 inhabitants, of whom 3,432 (47.8%) were men and 3,741 (52.2%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 2,679 individuals (37.3%) were aged 0–14 years, 4,002 individuals (55.8%) were aged 15–64, and 492 individuals (6.9%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20.0 years and the mean age was 26.0 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 18.6%, higher than the departmental average of 17.7%. The municipality had 1,516 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.6 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
