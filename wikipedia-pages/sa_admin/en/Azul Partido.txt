'''Azul''' is a central [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 62,996 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|6615|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Azul, Buenos Aires|Azul]], which is around {{convert|298|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The province was founded on December 16, 1832, and the people are known as ''Azuleños''.
