'''Limache''' is a town and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Marga Marga Province]], [[Valparaíso Region]], [[Chile]]. It is the only Chilean commune with two urban areas: San Francisco de Limache on the north side of the Pelumpén stream, and Limache Viejo on the south. Limache is well-known in Chile for its production of tomatoes, as well as the annual celebration of its patron saint known as the {{ill|Virgin of the 40 Hours|es|Virgen de las 40 Horas}}.

==Geography==
Limache spans an inland area of {{convert|293.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} in Chile's [[Zona Central, Chile|Zona Central]], located {{convert|43|km|0|abbr=on}} east of the [[port city]] of [[Valparaíso]] and {{convert|103|km|0|abbr=on}} northwest of the [[national capital]] of [[Santiago]].

==Culture==
The Feast of the Virgin of the 40 Hours (''La Fiesta de la Virgen de las 40 Horas'') begins in Limache 40 hours before the last Sunday in February with [[Mass (liturgy)|masses]] every hour, culminating on Sunday with a procession through the city.<ref name="Virgin">{{Cite web |url=http://gosouthamerica.about.com/od/holidaysinchile/qt/Virgen40Horas.htm |title=''"Fiesta de la Virgen de las 40 Horas"'' |access-date=10 February 2011 |archive-date=4 January 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110104120254/http://gosouthamerica.about.com/od/holidaysinchile/qt/Virgen40Horas.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] Limache has 46,121 inhabitants (22,353 men and 23,768 women). Of these, 39,169 (84.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 6,952 (15.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 17.6% (6,902 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a township, Limache is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a [[alcalde|mayor]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024–2028 mayor is Luciano Valenzuela Romero ([[Independent politician|Ind.]]/[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]).<ref name="mayor"/> The current municipal council has the following members:<ref>{{cite web|url=https://limache.cl/concejo-municipal/|title=Concejo Municipal 2024-2028|work=Municipality of Limache|access-date=20 August 2025|language=es}}</ref>
* Victoria Ladrón de Guevara Astudillo ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Alexis Ahumada Gómez ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Danilo Sandoval Sarabia (Ind./UDI)
* Matías Irarrázabal Gutiérrez (RN)
* Paula Abarca Carrizo (Ind./RN)
* Álvaro Zamora Pérez ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Limache is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marcelo Schilling ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Arturo Squella ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 12th electoral district, (together with [[Olmué]], [[Villa Alemana]], [[Quilpué]]). The township is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Ignacio Walker|Ignacio Walker Prieto]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Lily Pérez San Martín]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).{{Update after|2021|9|5}}
