'''Itapagipe''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the west of the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  {{as of|2020}} the population was 15,379 in a total area of {{cvt|1795|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/itapagipe/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1885.

==Geography==

Itapagipe is located at an elevation of {{cvt|516|m}} in the south-center of the area known as the [[Triângulo Mineiro]] 37&nbsp;km. west of the Transbrasiliana highway—Br-153.  The Rio Grande, a tributary of the [[Paraná River]], forms its southern boundary.  It belongs to the statistical micro-region of [[Frutal]].  Neighboring municipalities are:
*North: Frutal
*Northwest: [[Campina Verde]]
*West: [[São Francisco de Sales]]
*South: [[Paulo de Faria]], São Paulo<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/frutal/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-06-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080223213042/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/frutal/index.htm |archive-date=2008-02-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Origin of the name==
The name Itapagipe comes from the tupi-guarani and means "hard rock". This name was chosen because of a large rock that covers all of the bed of the Lageado stream, which crosses the town.<ref name="City government site">[http://www.itapagipe.mg.gov.br/oMunicipio.jsp City government site]</ref>

==History==

The district of Lageado, today Itapagipe, became a municipality in 1948, but it had been settled much earlier in 1880.  The first settler, Antônio Gomes Sobreiro, "married" a Caiapó Indian girl and established his ranch in the region.  A chapel was built and a small village began with the name of Santo Antônio do Lageado.

==Economic activities==

The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$ R$147,447.  Itapagipe is in the top tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  {{as of|2007}} there were 02 banking agency in the town.  There was a well-developed retail infrastructure serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  In the rural area there were 1,336 establishments occupying about 3,300 persons.  414 of the farms had tractors, a ratio of one in three.   There were 1,912 automobiles in all of the municipality, about one for every 7 inhabitants—a high ratio for Brazil.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The economic sectors of the town are divided into 285 commercial establishments, 29 industries, 25 services, and 02 banks.  The town had 2,469 houses.  Twelve churches attended to the religious needs of the population.<ref name="City government site"/>

Itapagipe is an important agricultural producer.  There were 183,000 head of cattle in 2006, of which 35,000 head were dairy cows.  In permanent crops there were {{cvt|92|ha}}. planted, while in perennial crops {{cvt|14000|ha}} were planted (2006).
The crops with a planted area of more than {{convert|100|ha}} were pineapple, rice, oranges, sugarcane, corn, sorghum, and soybeans.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==

In the health sector there were 5 health clinics and one hospital with 15 beds.  In the educational sector there were 5 primary schools and 1 middle school.<ref name="IBGE"/>
