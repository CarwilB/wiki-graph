'''Montúfar Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Carchi Province]].  Its capital is the city of [[San Gabriel, Ecuador|San Gabriel]].  The canton's population in the 2001 census was 28,576<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and was 30,511 in the 2010 census.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php |title=Ecuador: Municipal Division (Provinces and Parishes) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |website=www.citypopulation.de |access-date=12 Jul 2017}}</ref>

Montúfar is located in the [[Andes]] of northern Ecuador.  Its capital of San Gabriel has an elevation of {{convert|2878|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>

The canton is subdivided into the parishes of Chitán de Navarrete, Cristóbal Colón, Fernández Salvador, La Paz, Piartal, and San Gabriel.
