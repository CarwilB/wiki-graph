'''Huasco Province''' ({{IPA|es|ˈwasko}}, {{langx|es|Provincia de Huasco}}) is one of three [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] of the northern [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Atacama Region|Atacama]] (III). [[Vallenar]] is the capital city.

==Geography and demography==
According to the 2012 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], the province spans an area of {{convert|18201.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> and had a population of 72,145, giving it a population density of {{convert|3.7|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. The province had a 2002 population of 66,491 Of these, 53,664 (80.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 12,827 (19.3%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 2.7% (1,761 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a [[province]], Huasco is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], which is further divided into four [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas''). The province is administered by a presidentially appointed provincial delegate. [[Rodrigo Loyola Morenilla]] was appointed by [[President of Chile|president]] [[Gabriel Boric]].<ref name="Gobernadores"/>
