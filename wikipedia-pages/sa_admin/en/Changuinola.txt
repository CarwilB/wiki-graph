'''Changuinola''' is a city in the [[Bocas del Toro Province]] of [[Panama]] and seat city of the [[Changuinola District]]. The city attracts tourists as a popular gateway to [[Bocas Town, Bocas del Toro|Bocas Town]] and nearby cities [[Almirante, Bocas del Toro|Almirante]] and [[Chiriqui Grande]].<br>
Changuinola is surrounded by [[Changuinola River]] and extensive banana plantations.

==Demographics==
The corregimiento of Changuinola has a land area of {{convert|96.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 31,223 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|323|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 33,721; its population as of 2000 was 39,896.<ref name=inec/>

==Climate==
Changuinola is a coastal location with a [[tropical climate]], more specifically a trade-wind [[tropical rainforest climate]].  The area does not have a predictable [[dry season]]. The driest times are late August to mid-October and February to March.<ref name=wunder>{{cite web|url=http://www.wunderground.com/NORMS/DisplayIntlNORMS.asp?CityCode=78767&Units=english|title=Changuinola, Panama|accessdate=29 May 2009|publisher=Weather Underground|archive-date=5 March 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200305164315/https://www.wunderground.com/NORMS/DisplayIntlNORMS.asp?CityCode=78767&Units=english|url-status=dead}}</ref> Changuinola is humid. Thundershowers and heavy rain are common.<ref name=frommers>Frommer's Panama (2E, 2009), {{ISBN|978-0-470-37183-1}}.</ref> Normal temperatures are consistent all year, with maxima being {{convert|80|to|84|°F|1}} and minima {{convert|71|to|75|°F|1}}.<ref name=wunder /> Due to its low latitude, sunrise is around 6 AM, and sunset is around 6 PM local time. These times vary slightly during the year. Weather data is collected at [[Changuinola "Capitán Manuel Niño" International Airport|Capitán Manuel Niño International Airport]].<ref name=wunder />

==Transportation==
[[File:El Empame Changuinola.JPG|thumb|El Empalme hill view|200px]]

Changuinola is accessible by air, land, or sea. Visitors can fly into [[Changuinola "Capitán Manuel Niño" International Airport|Capitán Manuel Niño International Airport]] from [[Panama City]] by [[Air Panama]]. It has daily flights but can be delayed due to frequent thunderstorms and heavy rains. Direct flights from the United States, Canada, and Mexico are not available.

Buses run between Changuinola, Almirante, [[David, Chiriqui|David]] and Panama City. When traveling by land, tourists coming from or going to Costa Rica must clear both Costa Rican customs in [[Sixaola]] and Panamanian customs in [[Guabito]]. Buses are more reliable in bad weather.<ref name=frommers />

==Infrastructure==
[[File:El tren en el puente.png|thumb|GE Diesel Electro-motive engine over old Changuinola River Bridge|175px|right]]
Changuinola is divided into small farm communities called "Fincas", since all of the city is surrounded by banana plantations.

Changuinola has one main road that crosses the town from south to north and connects Changuinola to [[Guabito]], at the [[Panama–Costa Rica border]].

The [[Changuinola River]] bridge was built in 2009 and replaced the 1920 railroad bridge next to it.

Changuinola was struck in April 1991 by the 7.7&nbsp;{{M|w|link=y}}  [[1991 Limon earthquake|Bocas del Toro eqarthquake]], and the town was reconstructed in the following years.

==Public Services==
Private and Public health care available. The CSS Changuinola Hospital is located right next to the Police station, and private clinics are also a great choice and available in the city.

Changuinola has several public and private schools, to mention most important IPT Changuinola and Empalme School.

University of Panama extension "CRUB" is located in Empalme, Changuinola
