'''Castilho''' ({{IPA|pt|kasˈtiʎu}}) is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], in Brazil. It has an estimated population of 21,521 (as of 2021)<ref name=IBGE1/> in an area of {{convert|1,065.318|km2|0|abbr=on}},<ref name=IBGE0/> and its elevation is of {{convert|378.46|m|0|abbr=on}} above the sea level.<ref name=Locations/>

The municipality contains 3.69% of the {{convert|9044|ha}} of the [[Aguapeí State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{cite web |title=Parque Estadual do Aguapeí |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/arp/2940 |website=Unidades de Conservação no Brasil |publisher=Instituto Socioambiental |trans-website=Conservation Areas in Brazil |trans-title=Aguapeí State Park |access-date=22 February 2020}}</ref> It contains part of the {{convert|8885.33|ha}} of the [[Mouth of the Aguapeí Private Natural Heritage Reserve]], created in 2010.<ref>{{cite web |title=RPPN SP 2013 – Anuário das Reservas Particulares do Patrimônio Natural Instituídas pela Secretaria do Meio Ambiente do Estado de São Paulo |url=http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/rppn-2013.pdf |website=Secretaria do Meio Ambiente do Estado de São Paulo |trans-website=Secretariat for the Environment of the State of São Paulo |trans-title=PRNH SP 2013 – Yearbook of Private Reserves of Natural Heritage Established by the Secretariat for the Environment of the State of São Paulo |language=Portuguese |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151028192130/http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/rppn-2013.pdf |archive-date=28 October 2015 |url-status=dead |page=49 |access-date=22 December 2020}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_de_Telecomunicações_do_Estado_de_São_Paulo|Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo]] until 1975, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-26|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

==Transportation==
Castilho was served by [[Urubupungá Airport|Urubupungá–Ernesto Pochler Airport]]. It was a support structure for the operation of the Urubupungá complex, formed by the hydroelectric plants [[Ilha Solteira Dam|Ilha Solteira]], [[Engineer Souza Dias Dam|Jupiá]] and [[Três Irmãos Dam|Três Irmãos]] on [[Paraná River|Paraná river]]. However, in 2003, services were suspended due to the bad conditions of the runway and in 2005 the facility was abandoned.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://g1.globo.com/Noticias/SaoPaulo/0,,MUL56344-5605,00-AEROPORTO+NAO+TEM+VOOS+HA+ANOS+NO+INTERIOR+DE+SP.html|title=Aeroporto não tem vôos há 4 anos no interior de SP|website=g1|date=21 July 2007|access-date=20 July 2023|language=pt}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.perfilnews.com.br/aeroporto-urubupunga-e-desativado-por-mas-condicoes/|title=Aeroporto de Urubupungá é desativado por más condições|website=Perfil News|date=15 June 2005|access-date=20 July 2023|language=pt}}</ref>
