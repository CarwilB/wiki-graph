'''Malacacheta''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was estimated to be 18,602 people living in a total area of 719&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/malacacheta/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. It became a municipality in 1924.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Malacacheta is located at an elevation of 432 meters, 90&nbsp;km. west of [[Teófilo Otoni]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 515&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Setubinha]], [[Novo Cruzeiro]], [[Ladainha, Minas Gerais|Ladainha]], [[Poté]], [[Franciscópolis]] and [[Água Boa, Minas Gerais|Água Boa]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-09 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |archive-date=2008-06-13 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. A large percentage of the population is in the rural area and engaged in subsistence farming. The GDP in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|54|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 37 million from services, 4 million from industry, and 10 million from agriculture. There were 960 rural producers on 35,000 hectares of land. Only 25 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 2,700 persons were dependent on agriculture. The main cash crop was coffee while sugarcane, beans and corn were grown on a small scale. There were 33,000 heads of cattle (2006). There was one bank (2007) and 686 automobiles (629 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 26 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Health and education==
There were seven health clinics and two hospitals with 75 beds. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni. Educational needs were met by 23 primary schools, five middle schools, and three pre-primary schools.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Murder of four pre-schoolers in 1955==
In April 1955, a great tragedy took place on Good Friday. On the farm of São João da Mata in the hamlet, Catulé, which is a part of the municipality of Malacacheta, four pre-schoolers were hanged at their feet by members of a religious sect, the Adventist Church of the Promise, and killed with a cane. Seven other children were flogged. Some parents participated diligently; they considered it a magical ritual.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/240771340_A_aparicao_do_demonio_no_Catule|title=A aparição do demônio no Catulé|date=2021-05-11}}</ref>
