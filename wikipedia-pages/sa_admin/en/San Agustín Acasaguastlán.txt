'''San Agustín Acasaguastlán''' ({{IPA|es|san aɣusˈtin akasaɣwasˈtlan}}) is a town and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[El Progreso Department|El Progreso department]] of [[Guatemala]]. At the 2018 census, the population of the town was 17,728 and that of the municipality 45,765.<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities & towns in Guatemala</ref> The ''aldea'' (hamlet) [[Tulumaje]] is in this municipality.
