The '''Píritu Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 23,248.<ref>http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx {{Dead link|date=August 2022}}</ref> The town of [[Píritu, Anzoátegui]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Píritu Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The Píritu Municipality separated in 1993 from the [[Fernando de Peñalver Municipality]].{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
The Píritu Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 24,532 (up from 19,834 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.7% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|109.03|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Píritu Municipality is Rita Jimenez, elected on November 23, 2008, with 47% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> She replaced Antonio Barrios shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Capital Píritu and San Francisco (previous to June 27, 1995, the Píritu Municipality contained only a single parish)
