'''São José do Egito''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 32,508 and the area is 774.037&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Districts - São José do Egito, Bonfim and Riacho do Meio
* Boundaries - [[Brejinho, Pernambuco|Brejinho]] and [[Itapetim]]   ('''N''');  [[Tuparetama]] and [[Ingazeira]]   ('''S''');  [[Paraíba]] state  ('''E''');  [[Santa Terezinha, Pernambuco|Santa Terezinha]] and [[Tabira, Pernambuco|Tabira]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 791.9&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 585 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 23.2 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 394&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''São José do Egito''' are commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[Maize|corn]], [[bean]]s  and [[tomato]]es.
