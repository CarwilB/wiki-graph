'''San Antonio de Ibarra''' is a city in northern [[Ecuador]] that lies at the foot of the [[Imbabura Volcano]] and on the left bank of the [[Tahuando]] River in [[Imbabura Province, Ecuador|Imbabura Province]]. It is about {{convert|7|km|mi}} from the provincial capital of [[Ibarra, Ecuador|Ibarra]] and about {{convert|70|km|mi}} northeast of Ecuador's capital [[Quito]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.touribarra.gob.ec/esp/index.php/san-antonio-de-ibarra|title=San Antonio de Ibarra|website=touribarra.gob.ec|publisher=|accessdate=2 December 2015|archive-date=8 December 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151208044823/http://www.touribarra.gob.ec/esp/index.php/san-antonio-de-ibarra|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==History==
The city was founded in 1683, but there is evidence of human settlement from 100 years before.{{citation needed|date=December 2015}}

== Culture ==

San Antonio is famous for its wood artisans. For this is called also the ''Capital of wood [[artisan]]s''.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/ecuador/san-antonio-ibarra-busca-reflotar.html|title=San Antonio de Ibarra busca reflotar la artesanía|website=elcomercio.com|publisher=|accessdate=2 December 2015}}</ref> [[Pope Francis]], during his July 2015 visit to Ecuador, received a wood sculpture made by Jorge Villalba, born in San Antonio, as a gift.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.ecuavisa.com/articulo/noticias/nacional/111014-san-antonio-ibarra-recibira-al-papa-300-mil-artesanias-madera|title=San Antonio de Ibarra recibirá al papa con 300 mil artesanías de madera|website=ecuavisa.com|publisher=|accessdate=2 December 2015}}</ref>
