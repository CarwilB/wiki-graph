'''Maravilha''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[mɐɾɐˈviɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 9,004 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/maravilha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 279&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>
