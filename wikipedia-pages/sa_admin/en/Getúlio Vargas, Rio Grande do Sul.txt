[[File:Igreja Imaculada Conceição - Getúlio Vargas, RS.jpg|300px|right]]'''Getúlio Vargas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. With an estimated population of 16,184 people in 2020,<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/getulio-vargas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> it occupies an area of 286.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipality was named after the Brazilian [[President of Brazil|President]] of the [[Getúlio Vargas|same name]].
