'''La Huacana Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It has a population of 32,757. Its municipal seat is the [[La Huacana, La Huacana|city of the same name]].

==Etymology==
Huacana is a word of [[Chichimeca]] origin that means "Place of Dresses". Another version as to the origin of the name is that it was described only as "A place where a grey hair is floated on the water" by an indigenous inhabitant when she was asked the region's name.<ref>[http://www.patzcuaro.info/recorridos/santaclara-huacana.html Patzcuaro info] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080704062639/http://www.patzcuaro.info/recorridos/santaclara-huacana.html |date=2008-07-04 }} ''La Huacana''. Retrieved on October 7, 2007</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality has an area of 1,952.60 square kilometres and a population of 32,757 inhabitants according to the 2010 census.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.snim.rami.gob.mx/|website=Sistema Nacional de Informacion Municipal|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y Desarrollo Municipal|accessdate=6 January 2016}}</ref>

La Huacana municipality is located in the southern part of the state 480 meters above sea level, and is bordered to the north by the municipalities of [[Nuevo Urecho]] and [[Ario, Michoacan|Ario]], to the east by [[Turicato]], to the south by [[Churumuco]] and [[Arteaga, Michoacán|Arteaga]], and to the west by [[Múgica]] and [[Apatzingán]]. Its distance from the state capital [[Morelia]] is 161 kilometers. Its surface area is 1,951.91 km<sup>2</sup>, representing 3.32% of the whole state.<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan Michoacán] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517162648/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan |date=2011-05-17 }} ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Retrieved on October 7, 2007</ref> 

Its orography is composed of southern foothills of the transverse volcanic system, the hills of Las Canoas, La Copa, El Estribo, La Sierrita and El Milpillas.  Features of its hydrography are the rivers Huamito, Pastoria and Capirio, and the streams Zapiero, San Pedro Jorullo and San Antonio. The municipality contains Zicuiran dam and some cold water springs and one hot water spring. Its weather is tropical with summer rains and in other parts it is dry steppe. It has an annual rainfall of 800.0 mm and temperatures ranging from 10 to 54 degrees Celsius. Its forest is mostly made up of pine, oak, shrubbery of different types and [[hydrophilic]] vegetation.<ref name=encic>{{Cite web|url = http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16035a.html|title = La Huacana|date = 2010|accessdate = |website = Enciclopedia de los municipios y delegaciones de Mexico|publisher = INAFED|last = Rosales|first = David|archive-date = 2016-03-04|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20160304115121/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16035a.html|url-status = dead}}</ref>
 
==History==
During the wars before the Spaniards arrived, this place was conquered by the [[Tarascan state]], by the successors of Tariacuri: Hiquingare, Tangaxuan and Hirepan. These rulers, in order to ensure the payment of [[tribute]], appointed Cupauxanti as chieftain. In the 16th century, with the arrival of the Spaniards, shortly after the conquest of [[Apatzingan]], La Huacana became an ''[[encomienda]]'' under Juan Pantoja. In 1789 [[Jose Maria Morelos]] was the [[curate]] of the parish of Tamacuaro de la Aguacana.<ref name=encic />

In 1795, the volcano Jorullo erupted, seriously affecting the village.

On 20 November 1861, the State Congress decreed the creation of the municipality of La Huacana, but this was subsequently reversed. The municipality was reinstated on 12 March 1907. 

==Notable people==
* [[Ernesto Rodriguez (Mexico)|Ernesto Rodriguez]], a social activist, who implemented [[Land reform in Mexico#Cardenista land reform 1934 to 1940|land redistribution]] in the area, establishing the first ''[[ejido]]'' in La Huacana in May 1936. He was killed on 4 December 1937 as a result of this land redistribution.
