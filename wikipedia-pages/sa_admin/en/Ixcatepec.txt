'''Ixcatepec''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the north zone of the state, about {{convert|220|km|mi|0}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{convert|229.49|sqkm|sqmi|2}}. It is located at {{coord|21|14|N|98|00|W|}}.

The municipality of Ixcatepec is delimited to the north and north-east by [[Chontla]], to the west by [[Tantoyuca]], to the south by [[Chicontepec de Tejeda]] and to the south-east by [[Tepetzintla, Veracruz|Tepetzintla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30078a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ixcatepec |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-09}}</ref>
The weather in Ixcatepec is very warm to hot all year with rains in summer and autumn.

It produces principally [[maize]], [[bean]]s and [[sugarcane]].

In Ixcatepec, in August takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de la Asunción, Patron of the town.
