'''Divina Pastora''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|diˈvinɐ pɐʃˈtoɾɐ}}, alternatively {{IPA|diˈɦinɐ pɐʃˈtoɾɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. The [[Parish Church of Our Lady of the Divine Shepherd]] (''Igreja Matriz de Nossa Senhora Divina Pastora''), a listed historic structure by the [[National Institute of Historic and Artistic Heritage]] (IPHAN), was constructed in the late 18th century and is located at the east of the city center. Its population was 5,215 (2020)<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE 2020 |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/divina-pastora/panorama |website=IBGE}}</ref> and its area is {{convert|92|km2|mi2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref><ref name="i">{{cite web |url=http://portal.iphan.gov.br/ans.net/tema_consulta.asp?Linha=tc_hist.gif&Cod=1949 |title= Igreja Matriz de Nossa Senhora Divina Pastora |language=pt | place = Brasília |publisher=Instituto do Patrimônio Histórico e Artístico Nacional |accessdate=2017-01-05}}</ref>
