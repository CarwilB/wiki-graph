'''Inhambupe''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref> The town lies along Highway [[BR-110 (Brazil highway)|110]], approximately {{convert|161|km}} by road north of [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]]. In 2022 it had an estimated 33,790 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/inhambupe/panorama|title=Inhambupe|publisher=cidades.ibge.gov.br|accessdate=27 June 2024|language=Portuguese}}</ref> 

==History==
Between 1572 and 1582, an indigenous [[catechesis]] grew on the left bank of the [[Inhambupe River]] (Rio Inhambupe de Cima). The Jesuits opened a school in nearby [[Água Fria]]. The Marshal of the Casa da Torre dos Garcia D'Ávila erected a church of the Divine Holy Spirit in Inhambupe in the 1620s.<ref name= "History"/>

From 1718, the village was part of the Parish of Água Fria, but on April 24, 1728 Vasco Fernandes Cézar de Menezes elevated Inhambupe to the status of town.
The parish of Inhambupe was formally established on November 7, 1816.<ref name= "History">{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/inhambupe/historico|title=Historico|publisher=cidades.ibge.gov.br|language=Portuguese|accessdate=27 June 2024}}</ref>

According to local tradition, at the end of the 19th century, the mystic [[Antônio Conselheiro]] passed through the city, before the [[War of Canudos]]. Municipal Law No.1 of 20 October 1909 established the districts of Barreiro, Bebedouro, Caetitú, Calumbi, Caueira, Curralinho, Encantado, Gibóia, Jacu, Junco, Lagoa, Mulungu, Recreio and Tanquinho. In 1933, it was reduced to 4 districts: Inhambupe, Aporá, Itapororocas and Sátiro Dias. Itapororocas was replaced with Itamira (formerly Serra do Aporã) district in 1938. By State Law No. 1021 of 14 August 1958, the districts of Aporá and Itamira became part of the new Municipality of [[Aporá]].<ref name= "History"/>

==Geography==
The town lies along Highway [[BR-110 (Brazil highway)|110]], approximately {{convert|161|km}} by road north of [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]]<ref>{{Google maps | url =https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Salvador,+State+of+Bahia,+Brazil/-11.783333,-38.35/@-12.3819852,-39.0829553,242520m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m8!4m7!1m5!1m1!1s0x716112050422ebd:0xf71c84369573db9d!2m2!1d-38.5016363!2d-12.9777378!1m0?hl=en&entry=ttufrom  | accessdate =27 June 2024}}</ref> and about {{convert|75|km}} west from the coast.<ref>{{cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=zDsTAAAAYAAJ&dq=Inhambupe&pg=PA540|title=Memoir of the State of Bahia|work=Arquivo Público do Estado da Bahia|author=Francisco Vicente Vianna, José Carlos Ferreira|year=1893|isbn=9780530279152|publisher=Creative Media Partners|page=540}}</ref> The [[Inhambupe River|Inhambupe]] and [[Itapicuru River|Itapicuru]] rivers flow through the municipality.<ref>{{cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=Gd0rAAAAYAAJ&q=Inhambupe|title=Revista de história municipal, Issues 4–7|year=1991|publisher=Centro de Estudos de História Municipal|language=Portuguese|page=26}}</ref>

==Economy and health==
The area is a producer of [[cassava]].<ref>{{cite book|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=U1spAQAAIAAJ&q=Inhambupe|title=Volume 14–15 - Abstracts on Cassava (manihot Esculenta Crantz).|year=1988|publisher= Cassava Information Center, Centro Internacional de Agricultura Tropical|orig-year=1975|page=12}}</ref> The average GDP for capita in 2021 was 15.368,34 R$.<ref name="ecohealth"/>

The average infant mortality in Inhambupe is 17.24 per 1,000 live births, which is ranked 144th out of 417 municipalities in the state, and 1510th out of 5570 nationwide.<ref name="ecohealth">{{Cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/inhambupe/panorama|title=Inhambupe|publisher=cidades.ibge.gov.br|accessdate=27 June 2024|language=Portuguese}}</ref>
