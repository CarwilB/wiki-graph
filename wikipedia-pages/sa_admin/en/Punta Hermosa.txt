'''Punta Hermosa''' is one of 43 [[Districts of Lima|districts]] of the [[Lima Province]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of Punta Hermosa. The district's main attractions are its beaches.

==Geography==
Punta Hermosa borders the [[Pacific Ocean]] on the west; the [[Lurín District|Lurin]], and [[Pachacámac District|Pachacámac]] districts on the north; [[Huarochirí Province|Huarochirí Province's]], district of [[Santo Domingo de los Olleros District|Santo Domingo de los Olleros]] on the east; and the [[Punta Negra District|Punta Negra]] district on the south.

==Political division==
The district is divided into 4 populated centers ({{langx|es|Centros Poblados}}):

* Punta Hermosa
* Capilla Lucumo
* Cucuya
* Pampapacta

==Beach resort==
Punta Hermosa is located 42 km south of [[Lima District|downtown Lima]], to which it is connected by the [[Pan-American Highway]]. The district's main attraction is its beaches, which are visited by thousands of people every summer.  The area, which used to be a resort/vacation district where visitors stayed only temporarily, has recently seen an [[urban area|urban]] expansion and an increase in permanent year-round residents.
