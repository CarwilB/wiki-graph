'''Zacoalco de Torres''', formerly '''Zacoalco''' ({{langx|nah|Tzacoalco}}; "place of closed water"), is a [[town]] and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]] in [[Jalisco]], [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 479.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is the primary production region of the ''equipal''-style wood and pigskin furniture.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 30,528.<ref>{{cite web|title=Zacoalco de Torres|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14119a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=April 20, 2009}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality covers an area of 479.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. To the east lies the largest lake in Mexico, [[Lake Chapala]].
