'''Independência''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 6,109 (2020 est.) in an area of 357.44 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/16WL Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is {{convert|372|m|ft}}.
