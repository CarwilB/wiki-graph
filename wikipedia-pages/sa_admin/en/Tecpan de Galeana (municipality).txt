''' Tecpan de Galeana''' (for [[Hermenegildo Galeana]])<ref>{{cite web |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo Tecpan de Galeana 2021–2024 |url=https://www.ayuntamientotecpan.gob.mx/PlanDeDesarrollo/PlanMunicipalTecpan.pdf |access-date=31 October 2023}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Tecpan de Galeana]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|2,537.8|km²|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 65,237,<ref>{{cite web |title=Técpan de Galeana: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety  |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tecpan-de-galeana |publisher=Secretariat of the Economy |access-date=31 October 2023}}</ref> up from 52,848 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tecpan de Galeana|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12057a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=23 December 2008|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617102655/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12057a.htm|archive-date=17 June 2011}}</ref>
