'''Las Vigas''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. It is located about {{convert|95|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Chilpancingo]]. Its creation from the municipality of [[San Marcos Municipality, Guerrero|San Marcos]] was approved in 2021 and went into force on 21 May 2022.

==Geography==
The municipality of Las Vigas is located in the [[Costa Chica of Guerrero|Costa Chica]] region of eastern Guerrero. It borders the municipalities of San Marcos to the west, [[Tecoanapa (municipality)|Tecoanapa]] to the north, [[Florencio Villarreal]] to the east, and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the south. Most of the municipality lies within the [[drainage basin]] of [[Tecomate Lagoon]], which extends into the southwestern part of the municipality, and is part of the Lagunas Costeras de Guerrero [[Key Biodiversity Area]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.keybiodiversityareas.org/site/factsheet/10024 |title=Key Biodiversity Areas factsheet: Lagunas Costeras de Guerrero |website=World Database of Key Biodiversity Areas |publisher=Key Biodiversity Areas Partnership |access-date=22 December 2023}}</ref> The northeastern part of the municipality lies in the basin of the [[Nexpa River]], which forms part of the border between Las Vigas and Florencio Villarreal.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/12/12053.pdf |title=San Marcos, Guerrero |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=22 December 2023}}</ref>

Las Vigas has a [[subhumid temperate climate]] with rain in the summer. Average temperatures range between {{convert|26|and(-)|28|C}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Las Vigas and its surrounding communities had been demanding the creation of an independent municipal government since at least 2014.<ref>{{cite news |last=Rodríguez Guzmán |first=Alberto |date=16 August 2014 |url=https://suracapulco.mx/impreso/principal/dejan-el-palacio-de-san-marcos-promotores-del-municipio-de-las-vigas-tratara-el-martes-el-cabildo-su-solicitud/ |title=Dejan el Palacio de San Marcos promotores del municipio de Las Vigas; tratará el martes el Cabildo su solicitud |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |location=Chilpancingo |accessdate=22 December 2023}}</ref> On 31 August 2021, the [[Congress of Guerrero|Guerrero state congress]] approved the formation of the municipality of Las Vigas from nineteen localities previously belonging to San Marcos.<ref name=creation/> The state constitutional amendment establishing the municipality was passed on 13 January 2022 and went into force on 21 May 2022.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=20 May 2022 |title=DECRETO NÚMERO 161 POR EL QUE SE APRUEBA LA ADICIÓN DE LOS NUEVOS MUNICIPIOS DE SANTA CRUZ DEL RINCÓN, SAN NICOLÁS, ÑUU SAVI Y LAS VIGAS AL ARTÍCULO 27 DE LA CONSTITUCIÓN POLÍTICA DEL ESTADO LIBRE Y SOBERANO DE GUERRERO. EN EL LUGAR QUE CONFORME AL ORDEN ALFABÉTICO LES CORRESPONDE. |url=https://periodicooficial.guerrero.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/P.O-40-20-MAYO-20221.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Guerrero |pages=9–24 |access-date=23 December 2023}}</ref>

==Administration==
Las Vigas will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2024. The municipal government of Las Vigas will consist of a [[municipal president]], a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and six trustees (''regidores'').<ref>{{cite news |last=Chávez |first=Lourdes |date=16 November 2023 |url=https://suracapulco.mx/impreso/2/aprueba-iepc-composicion-de-ayuntamientos-incluidos-los-cuatro-nuevos-municipios/ |title=Aprueba IEPC composición de ayuntamientos, incluidos los cuatro nuevos municipios |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |location=Chilpancingo |accessdate=22 December 2023}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Las Vigas recorded a population of 9449 inhabitants.<ref name=creation/> The municipal seat, also named Las Vigas, recorded a population of 4762 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825197858.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Guerrero. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |page=134 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=22 December 2023}}</ref>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The flat land of Las Vigas is well suited for farming and animal husbandry. The municipality is irrigated by the Nexpa River and the Revolución Mexicana reservoir on that river.<ref name=creation/> Fishing and artisanal salt production are practised in Tecomate Lagoon.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Villerías Salinas |first1=Salvador |last2=Tello Almaguer |first2=Pedro Vidal |last3=García Castro |first3=Neftalí |date=March 2016 |title=Algunas características ambientales de la laguna de Tecomate, Guerrero, México |language=es |trans-title=Some environmental characteristics of the lagoon of Tecomate, Guerrero, Mexico |journal=Tlamati |volume=7 |issue=1 |pages=11–13 |url=http://ri.uagro.mx/handle/uagro/491 |access-date=22 December 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite journal |last=Good |first=Catharine |date=Spring 1995 |title=SALT PRODUCTION AND COMMERCE IN GUERRERO, MEXICO: An ethnographic contribution to historical reconstruction |journal=Ancient Mesoamerica |volume=6 |issue=1 |pages=1–13 |jstor=26307200}}</ref>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 200]] runs through the municipal seat, connecting it with [[San Marcos, Guerrero|San Marcos]] and [[Acapulco]] to the west, and [[Cruz Grande]] and [[Ometepec]] to the east.

Secondary education is available in Las Vigas, including a preparatory school for the [[Autonomous University of Guerrero]].<ref name=creation/>
