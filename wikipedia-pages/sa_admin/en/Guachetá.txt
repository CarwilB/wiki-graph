'''Guachetá''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Ubaté Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Guachetá is located at {{convert|118|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. It borders the [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]] municipalities of [[Ráquira]] and [[Samacá]] in the north, [[Ubaté]] and [[Lenguazaque]] in the south, [[Ventaquemada]] and Lenguazaque in the east and in the west [[Fúquene]] and Ubaté. Guachetá is located on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at altitudes between {{convert|2600|m|ft}} and {{convert|3500|m|ft}}.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.guacheta-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml#2 Official website Guachetá] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170709074052/http://guacheta-cundinamarca.gov.co/index.shtml#2 |date=2017-07-09 }} - accessed 05-05-2016</ref>

== History ==
[[File:Don Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada.JPG|thumb|left|100px|'''Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada''', founder of Guachetá]]
When the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish]] [[conquistador]]es entered the central highlands of Colombia, they encountered the [[Muisca Confederation]]; territories of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Guachetá was an independent territory within the confederation, led by a ''[[cacique]]''. The Spanish army leader [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] founded Guachetá on March 12, 1537, and initially called it ''San Gregorio'' because of the date. The Muisca spoke [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and Guachetá in Chibcha means "farmlands of the hill".<ref name=website/>
{{clearleft}}

== Muisca myth ==
[[File:Goranchacha El hijo del Sol.jpg|thumb|left|100px|Statue honouring '''Goranchacha''']]
One of the many stories of the [[Muisca mythology|mythology]] of the Muisca is the myth of the virgin maid of Guachetá. The daughter of the ''cacique'' allegedly became pregnant through the forces of the Sun, represented in the [[Muisca religion]] by [[Sué]]. After nine months pregnancy she bore an [[emerald]] that she put between her breasts. From this emerald grew the mythical ''cacique'' [[Goranchacha]]; son of the Sun.<ref>{{cite book |author=Ocampo López, Javier |author-link=Javier Ocampo López |year=2013 |title=Mitos y leyendas indígenas de Colombia |trans-title=Indigenous myths and legends of Colombia |location=Bogotá, Colombia |publisher=Plaza & Janes Editores Colombia S.A. |isbn=978-958-14-1416-1 |page=80 |language=es}}</ref>
{{clearleft}}

== Economy ==
The main economical activities of Guachetá are [[agriculture]], particularly [[potato]]es, [[maize]], [[pea]]s and [[bean]]s,<ref name="website" /> along with [[coal mining]] and [[Dairy|dairy production]].
