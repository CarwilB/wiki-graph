'''Timucuy Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “place of the dove (divine hen)”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing  63.15&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 25&nbsp;km southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
In ancient times, the area was part of the chieftainship of Chakan until the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]]. At colonization, Timucuy became part of the [[encomienda]] system, with a series of encomenderos including Pedro Álvarez in 1549, Gaspar Juárez de Ávila in 1565 and Gertrudis Marín in 1724.<ref name="municipios"/>

In 1821, Yucatán was declared independent of the Spanish Crown.<ref name=municipios /> In 1840, Timucuy was joined with the region of Tecoh. On 24 July 1867, Timucuy was established as a town within the jurisdiction of [[Acanceh Municipality]]. In 1988, it was designated as head of its own municipality.<ref name="CREACION DE LOS MUNICIPIOS">{{cite book|title=División territorial del Estado de Yucatán de 1810 a 1995.|date=1997|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|location=Aguascalientes, Ags.|isbn=970-13-1518-9|pages=101, 73, 109, 124|edition=1.|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|accessdate=5 June 2015|language=Spanish|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=2015-12-23}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a term of three years. The president appoints Councilpersons to serve on the board for three year terms, as the Secretary and councilors of street lighting, ecology and parks and public gardens.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Timucuy|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/municipios/31020a.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=4 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref>
