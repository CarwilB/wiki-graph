'''Imigrante''' (population: 3,080) is a city in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], located in south [[Brazil]], situated in the state's [[Taquari Valley]] region. It was established by [[Ethnic German|German]] and [[Italian people|Italian]] immigrants.  Imigrante calls itself, ''Terra do Cactos'', or Land of the [[Cactus]].

The city is 100 m above sea level. Its climate is subtropical. 

Imigrante's economy centers on the metallurgy industry. 

The mayor is Paulo Gilberto Altmann, of the [[Progressive Party (Brazil)|PP]] party.
