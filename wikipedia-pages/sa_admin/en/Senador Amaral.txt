'''Senador Amaral''' is the second highest city in [[Brazil]] ({{convert|1505|m|ft|abbr=on|disp=sqbr}}),<ref>The highest is [[Campos do Jordão]] at {{cvt|1628|m|ft}}</ref> reaching near {{convert|1620|m|ft|abbr=on}} in some parts in the northern areas, nearly the Ponte Segura district, the highest point is an unnamed mount in Bom Repouso border at approximately {{cvt|1660|m|ft}} above sea level.  It is located in the [[Mantiqueira Mountains]], south of [[Minas Gerais]] state. It is a small city, with a population of 5,361 inhabitants and approximately 2980 people living in the urban area. There was no asphalt or mobile phone signal in the municipality before 2007. Only Cambuí access, [[MG-295]] is asphalted.

== Geography ==
=== Climate ===
Senador Amaral has status of ''Estância Climática'' because of its cold climate, has an average annual temperature of {{convert|18.2|°C}}, and in winter's nights temperatures of around {{convert|0|to|6|°C|°F}} and with a record low of {{convert|-5|°C}}.
