'''Naranjito Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Guayas Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Naranjito, Ecuador|Naranjito]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 31,756.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
