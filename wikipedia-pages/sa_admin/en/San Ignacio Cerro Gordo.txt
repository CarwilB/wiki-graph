'''San Ignacio Cerro Gordo''' is a municipality in [[Jalisco]], [[Mexico]]. It was established by the Congress of the State of Jalisco, on 1 January 2007 out of Arandas.<ref name="SanIgnacio">{{cite web|title=Historia: Antecedentes|trans-title=History: Background|url=http://sanignaciocg.gob.mx/historia/antecedentes|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210121073735/http://sanignaciocg.gob.mx/historia/antecedentes|archive-date=2021-01-21|access-date=2021-02-21|publisher=Municipality of San Ignacio Cerro Gordo|language=es}}</ref> Decree Number 20371 was published in the Periódico Oficial ''El Estado de Jalisco'', on 30 December 2003, three years before the creation of this municipality, to allow its municipal authorities to be elected by the citizens in the [[2006 Jalisco state election|election of municipal presidents on 2 July 2006]].<ref>{{cite web| url=https://periodicooficial.jalisco.gob.mx/sites/periodicooficial.jalisco.gob.mx/files/12-30-03-ix_0.pdf |title=Periódico Oficial ''El Estado de Jalisco''. Decreto No. 20371|language=es|date=30 December 2003 |access-date=10 April 2022}}</ref>
