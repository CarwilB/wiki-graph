'''Guarumal''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Soná District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 3,239 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,726; its population as of 2000 was 3,340.<ref name=inec/>
