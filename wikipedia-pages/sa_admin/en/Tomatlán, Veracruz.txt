'''Tomatlán''' ([[Nahuatl]]: "place of tomatoes"<ref name=encyclopedia/>) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|58|km}} south of the state capital of [[Xalapa]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Tomatlán is located in central Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|1000|and(-)|1500|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Ixhuatlán del Café]] to the northeast, [[Córdoba, Veracruz|Córdoba]] to the southeast, [[Chocamán]] to the southwest, and [[Coscomatepec de Bravo]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Tomatlán, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=3 March 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|18.905|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.03% of the state's area.<ref name=snim/>

Tomatlán is located in the [[Jamapa River]] basin. Agricultural land makes up 87.04% of the municipality's area.<ref name=compendium/>

Tomatlán's climate is humid and semi-warm with rain throughout the year. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1500|and(-)|2000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Located {{convert|3|km|spell=in}} east of the municipal seat of Tomatlán,<ref name=encyclopedia/> the [[Olmec]] site of [[La Yerbabuena]] dates to the Middle and Late [[Formative period]]s (c. 600–400 BCE) and has been identified as a workshop for the production of [[obsidian]] tools.<ref>{{cite book |last=Cobean |first=Robert H. |title=A World of Obsidian: The Mining and Trade of a Volcanic Glass in Ancient Mexico |page=163 |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=u24TngcA49kC&pg=PA165 |date=2002 |publisher=[[INAH]] |isbn=9789701825082}}</ref> In the 15th century a [[Teochichimeca]] settlement called Quechultenango ("wall of [[quetzal]]s") was founded in the area, which in the colonial period became known as San Miguel Quechultenango Tomatlán.

On 18 January 1825 Tomatlán was incorporated as a municipality in the state of Veracruz. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=119, 306 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Nicolas Prado Morales.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tomatlanveracruz.gob.mx/estructura |title=Estructura: Periodo 2018 - 2021 |language=es |publisher=Municipal council of Tomatlán, Veracruz |access-date=3 March 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Tomatlán recorded a population of 6763 inhabitants living in 1749 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=167 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Tomatlán |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=1 March 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 6869 inhabitants in Tomatlán.<ref name=survey/>

There are 10 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Tomatlán, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 4348 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The most important economic activities in Tomatlán are sugarcane production and chicken farming, followed by coffee production and cattle farming.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.invedem.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/26/2014/02/ifm-pmd-tomatlan-11-13.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2011–2013 |language=es |page=39 |publisher=Municipal council of Tomatlán, Veracruz |access-date=3 March 2018}}</ref>
