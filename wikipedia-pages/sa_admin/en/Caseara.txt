'''Caseara''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]]. Its population was 5,442 (2020) and its area is 1,692&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/B3M]</ref>

The municipality contains 10.66% of the {{convert|1678000|ha|adj=on}} [[Ilha do Bananal / Cantão Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Leandro (Ilha do Bananal/Cantão) – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=APA Leandro (Ilha do Bananal/Cantão)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3977|accessdate=2016-10-31}}</ref>
The headquarters and visitor center of [[Cantão State Park]] are located just outside the town of Caseara.  The municipality plans to open a hiking/bicycle trail from the town center to the park's visitor center in 2014.
