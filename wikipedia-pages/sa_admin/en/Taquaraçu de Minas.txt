'''Taquaraçu de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira. As of 2025, the estimated population was 4,394.<ref name="IBGE" />
