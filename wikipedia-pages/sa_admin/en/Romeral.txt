'''Romeral''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Curicó Province]], [[Maule Region]]. The commune spans and area of {{convert|1597.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of Population and Housing by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], the Romeral commune had 12,707 inhabitants; of these, 3,675 (28.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,032 (71.1%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 6,596 men and 6,111 women. The population grew by 10.6% (1,217 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Romeral is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Carlos Cisterna Negrete ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Romeral is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Roberto León (PDC) and Celso Morales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 36th electoral district, together with [[Curicó]], [[Teno, Chile|Teno]], [[Molina, Chile|Molina]], [[Sagrada Familia, Chile|Sagrada Familia]], [[Hualañé]], [[Licantén]], [[Vichuquén]] and [[Rauco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
