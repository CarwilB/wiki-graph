'''Zunilito''' ({{IPA|es|suniˈlito}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is situated at 790 m above [[sea level]]. It covers an area of 78&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
