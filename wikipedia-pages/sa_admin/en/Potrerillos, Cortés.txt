'''Potrerillos''' is a town, with a population of 20,050 (2023 calculation),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Cortés department|Cortés]]. The municipality was founded in 1875.<ref name=":1" />
