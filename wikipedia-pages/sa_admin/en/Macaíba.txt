==Climate==
Macaíba has a rather dry [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''As'') with like most of the ''Nordeste'' coast a strong [[dry season]] from September to January. Although the area is substantially drier than [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]], in accordance with its location in the transition to the ''[[agreste]]'' further west, the climate is hot year round and humidity is generally high particularly in the rainy season.
