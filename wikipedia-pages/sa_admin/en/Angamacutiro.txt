'''Angamacutiro''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. Located in the far north portion of the state, its [[municipal seat]] is the city of Angamacutiro de la Unión. The name ''Angamacutiro'' originates from the [[Purepecha language]],{{cn|date=December 2024}} meaning "place on the edge of the canyon". This name highlights the region's proximity to a canyon's cliff edge, which is a reflection of its geographic features.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Michoacan - Angamacutiro|url=http://inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16004a.html|access-date=2021-10-17|website=inafed.gob.mx}}</ref>

== History ==

Among the peoples subdued by the armies of the  [[Tarascan state|Purépecha State]], is Angamacutiro. In the 16th century, the [[Conquistador|Spanish conqueror]] [[Nuño de Guzmán]], when marching at the head of an expedition that was heading towards [[Nueva Galicia]] passed through this town, taking possession of it improperly, which caused a dispute, since the commander of Huango (Villa Morelos) claimed Angamacutiro as a parcel. It is common knowledge that the Spanish stumping towards the indigenous population had two characteristics, while Nuño de Guzmán resorted to the sword, instructed the friars [[Juan de Padilla]], [[Juan de Badillo]] and [[Bartolomé de Estrada]], to evangelize the population.

Angamacutiro was founded in 1323 by a group of [[Otomi]] indigenous people who came from the [[Guanajuato|State of Guanajuato]].

The municipality was created on December 10, 1831 by the State Congress, it was the scene of several struggles during the war of intervention in Michoacán. In 1866 there was a confrontation between the imperial forces commanded by Gen. Ramón Méndez and the Republican group known as  ''los chinacos'', the latter caused strong setbacks to the forces representing the intervention; in recognition of loyalty to the republic, the State Congress granted it on June 28, 1869, the name of "Villa Unión" is currently known as Angamacutiro de la Unión to its municipal seat.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Michoacan - Angamacutiro|url=http://inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16004a.html|access-date=2021-10-17|website=inafed.gob.mx}}</ref>

== Geography ==

Angamacutiro is located in the extreme north of Michoacán, bordering the state of [[Guanajuato]]. It is bordered by the municipalities of José Sixto Verduzco to the northeast, [[Puruándiro]] to the east, Panindícuaro to the south, [[Penjamillo]] to the west and [[Pénjamo]] (Guanajuato) to the north. The municipality has a total area of {{convert|230.26|km2|sqmi}}, representing 0.39% of Michoacán's total area.

The municipality is located where the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] meets the lowlands of the [[Bajío]]. 

The principal river of Angamacutiro is the [[Lerma River]], which marks the state border with Guanajuato. The Angulo River runs north-south through the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/sombreado_ri.cfm |title = Michoacán. Mapa de Principales Ríos |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714235549/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/sombreado_ri.cfm|archive-date = 14 July 2007}}</ref> All of the smaller streams and rivers are tributaries of these two rivers. 

===Climate and ecosystems===

Angamacutiro has a [[humid subtropical climate]] with rains in the summer.<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/clim.cfm |title = Michoacán. Mapa de Climas |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714235152/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/clim.cfm |archive-date = 14 July 2007 }}</ref> The average annual temperature is between 16 and 24 °C,<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/temperat.cfm |title = Michoacán. Mapa de Temperatura Media Anual |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714235620/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/temperat.cfm |archive-date = 14 July 2007 }}</ref> annual rainfall is between 700 and 800 mm.<ref>{{cite web |url = http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/precipit.cfm |title = Michoacán. Mapa de Precipitación Promedio Anual |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070714235210/http://mapserver.inegi.gob.mx/geografia/espanol/estados/mich/precipit.cfm |archive-date = 14 July 2007 }}</ref>

Native species include plants such as [[Opuntia ficus-indica|nopal]], [[Vachellia farnesiana|huizache]] and shrubs, and animals such as [[Least weasel|weasels]], [[European rabbit|rabbits]], [[Tree squirrel|squirrels]], [[Mephitidae|skunks]], [[American_badger|badgers]], [[Streptopelia|turtledoves]] and [[duck]]s.

==Demographics==

As of the census of 2020, there were 14,943 people residing in the municipality, a 0.18% raise from the 2010 census count; 5,595 men and 6,738 women.<ref name=":0" /><ref>{{cite web |url = http://www.inegi.org.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/rutinas/ept.asp?t=mpob93&s=est&c=3839&e=16 |title = Michoacán. Población total por municipio según grandes grupos de edad, 2005 |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es}}</ref> 36.6% of the population live in towns of more than 2,500 people. Only 0.2% of the population older than 5 years speak an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]].<ref>{{cite web |url = http://www.inegi.org.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/rutinas/ept.asp?t=mpob103&s=est&c=3850&e=16 |title = Michoacán. Indicadores seleccionados de la población por municipio, 2005 |publisher = Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía |language=es}}</ref>

31.4% of the population was under the age of 15 and 10.3% were 64 years or older. 45.4% of the population was male.
