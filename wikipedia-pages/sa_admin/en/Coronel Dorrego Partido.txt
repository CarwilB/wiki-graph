'''Coronel Dorrego Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the southern coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 16,500 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|5818|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Coronel Dorrego]], which is around {{convert|628|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==
The economy of Coronel Dorrego Partido is dominated by agriculture. The mainstays of agricultural production are cereals, dairy products, and beef.{{citation needed|date=May 2018}}

==Tourism==
[[Marisol, Buenos Aires|Marisol]] is a coastal resort which is popular with tourists from [[Gran Buenos Aires]].
