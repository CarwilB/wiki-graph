'''El Peñón''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. It is located at an altitude of {{convert|1310|m|ft}} in the western flanks of the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the [[Colombia]]n [[Andes]]. The municipality, part of the [[Rionegro Province]] borders [[Topaipí]] in the north, [[Vergara, Cundinamarca|Vergara]] and [[Nimaima]] in the south, [[Pacho]] in the east and [[La Peña, Cundinamarca|La Peña]] and [[La Palma, Cundinamarca|La Palma]] in the west. The municipality is {{convert|120|km|mi}} northwest of the capital [[Bogotá]] and thanks to the lower elevation has a temperate climate averaging {{convert|21|C|F}}.

== Geology ==
The [[Lower Cretaceous]] [[El Peñón Formation]] is named after El Peñón.

== History ==
The area of El Peñón before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Panche people|Panche]]. Modern El Peñón was founded in 1822 by Pedro Bustos as El Peñón de Terama, named after a ''[[cacique]]'' of the Panche that resisted the Spanish [[list of conquistadors in Colombia|conquistadors]] and died in 1565.<ref name=ElPenon>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20160215094235/http://www.elpenon-cundinamarca.gov.co/apc-aa/view.php3?vid=1090&cmd%5B1090%5D=x-1090-2984715 191 años de Nuestro Bello Municipio]</ref>
