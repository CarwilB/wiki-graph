'''Anse-à-Galets''' ({{IPA|fr|ɑ̃s a ɡalɛ}}; {{langx|ht|Ansagalèt}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] and city in the [[La Gonâve Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] on [[Gonâve Island]] in [[Haiti]], located to the west-northwest of [[Port-au-Prince]] in the [[Gulf of Gonâve]]. It is the largest commune on the island and has 62,559 inhabitants.

The current{{When|date=July 2021}} mayor of the city of Anse-a-Galets is  Ernso Louissaint.{{citation needed|date=June 2017}}

==Transportation==
The commune is served by [[Anse-à-Galets Airport]] and a public wharf.  The public wharf has daily ferry traffic to the Haitian mainland at the Carries Ferry terminal.{{citation needed|date=June 2017}}
