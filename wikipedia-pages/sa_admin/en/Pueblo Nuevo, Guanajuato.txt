'''Pueblo Nuevo''' is a municipality in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Guanajuato]].  Its borders are [[Salamanca, Guanajuato|Salamanca]], [[Irapuato]], [[Valle de Santiago]], [[Abasolo, Guanajuato|Abasolo]].

Former mayor José Durán González, 67, was ambushed and murdered on September 5, 2017.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.proceso.com.mx/501802/asesinan-a-exalcalde-pueblo-nuevo-guanajuato| url-status = dead| archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20170905185827/http://www.proceso.com.mx/501802/asesinan-a-exalcalde-pueblo-nuevo-guanajuato| archive-date = 2017-09-05| title = Asesinan a exalcalde de Pueblo Nuevo, Guanajuato - Proceso}} </ref>
