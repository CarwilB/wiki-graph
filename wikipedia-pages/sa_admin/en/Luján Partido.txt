'''Luján Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeastern part of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 94,000 inhabitants{{fact|date=November 2014}} in an area of {{convert|800|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Luján, Buenos Aires|Luján]], which is {{convert|67|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==
The economy of Luján Partido is dominated by farming, other aspects include textile and medical manufacturing, and the production of agrochemicals and agricultural machinery.
