'''Sabanalarga''' ({{IPA|es|saβanaˈlaɾɣa}}, Spanish for "Long Plain") is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Atlántico Department]], [[Colombia]]. Founded in 1620 by [[Lucas Dionisio Tesillo y Diego]] and [[Marceliano de Jesús Almanza]]. It became a municipality in 1680.

==Geography==

Sabanalarga is located in the middle of the [[Atlántico Department]]. It is surrounded to the north by the municipalities of [[Usiacurí]], [[Baranoa]], and [[Polonuevo]]; to the east by the municipality of [[Ponedera]]; to the south by the municipalities of [[Candelaria, Atlántico|Candelaria]], and [[Manatí, Atlántico|Manatí]]; to the southwest by the municipality of [[Repelón]] and to the west by the municipalities of [[Luruaco]] and [[Piojó, Atlántico|Piojó]].
