'''Bandeira''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 4,766 people living in a total area of 484&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/bandeira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Almenara]]. Bandeira became a municipality in 1962.

==Economy and geography==
Bandeira is located 37&nbsp;km northeast of [[Almenara, Minas Gerais|Almenara]], just south of the boundary with the state of [[Bahia]]. Highway connections are poor making the region isolated. The elevation was 292 meters. The main economic activities are cattle raising (22,000 head in 2006) and the cultivation of cacao, oranges, sugarcane and corn. The GDP in 2006 was {{BRLConvert|15053|k|year=2006|showdate=no}}. There were no banking agencies {{as of|2007|lc=y}}. In the same year there were 63 automobiles.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref> This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
