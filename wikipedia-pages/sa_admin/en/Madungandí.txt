'''Guna de Madungandí''' or '''Madungandí''', formerly known as '''Kuna de Madugandí'''<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://m.prensa.com/impresa/opinion/Guna-kuna-cuna-James-Howe_0_3874112623.html|title = ¿Guna, kuna o cuna?: James Howe &#124; la Prensa Panamá|date = 22 February 2014}}</ref> is a ''[[comarca#Panama|comarca indígena]]'' (indigenous territory) and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepo District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 4,271 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 24 of January 12, 1996.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 3,305.<ref name=inec/>

The primary ethnicity is [[Guna people|Guna]].

The comarca is not divided into districts; but is divided into 12 communities: Akua Yala, Ibedí, Pintupu, Icandí, Piria, Cuinupdi, Nargandí, Ogobnawila, Diwar Sikua, Capandi and Tabardi. Its capital is [[Akua Yala]].

Its area is 2318.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is located near the [[Bayano River]].

The leader of this region is known as Ariadna Alfonso. It is the most important sahila in the region.
