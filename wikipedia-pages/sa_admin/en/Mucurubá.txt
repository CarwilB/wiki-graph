'''Mucurubá''' is a community in [[Mérida (state)|Mérida state]], [[Venezuela]], in the foothills of the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Cordillera Oriental]]. It is 2,407&nbsp;m above sea level, about 32 kilometers from the city of [[Mérida, Mérida|Mérida]]. It is a parish within the [[Rangel (Venezuela)|Rangel]] Municipality. The name is derived from the words "muco" (place) and "ruba" (a tuber similar to potato) in the language spoken by the  pre-Columbian inhabitants. The settlement was found in 1586 by [[Bartolomé Gil Naranjo]].

The climate is cool and dry, with an average temperature of 16.5&nbsp;°C, suitable for irrigated farming of crops such as potato, carrot, lettuce, garlic, blackberries, strawberries, peaches and figs. In colonial times, corn was an important crop, ground in water mills.<ref name=cibert>{{cite web
