'''Acevedo''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 88,289.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Caucagua]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Acevedo Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web|url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Acevedo Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 88,289 (up from 75,868 in 2000).  This amounts to 3.1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|46.99|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Acevedo Municipality is Juan José Aponte Mijares, elected on October 31, 2004, with 47% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=1&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|year=2004|language=es|title=Alcalde de Municipio. Acevedo|access-date=8 May 2023}}</ref> He replaced Vicente Apicella shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into eight [[parish]]es; [[Caucagua]], [[Aragüita]], Arévalo González, [[Capaya]], [[El Café]], [[Marizapa]], [[Panaquire]], and Ribas.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
