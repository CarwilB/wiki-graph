'''Maranura District''' in [[Peru]] is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[La Convención Province|La Convención]] and is located in the department of [[Cusco]] and remains under the administration of the Regional Government of Cusco .<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

==History==

The '''Maranura District''' was created by the government of President Manuel Prado Ugarteche on March 15, 1961, by Law 13620.<ref name="Promo region cusco">{{cite web|title=Maranura District - La Convención - Cusco|url=http://www.promoregioncusco.com/en/laconv_maranura.php|website=Promo region cusco|accessdate=2 July 2014}}</ref>

==Authorities==

The current mayor of the Maranura District is Francisco Marcavillaca Alvarez of the [[We Are Peru|Democratic Party We Are Peru]]. The district has five [[Alderman|Aldermen]]: Sergio Tapia Caballero (SP), Clara Huamán Castilla (SP), Fredy Alagón Ricalde (SP), Obdulia Palma Ttito (SP), Efrain Yabar Becerra (Acuerdo Popular Unificado).<ref name="Promo region cusco"/>
