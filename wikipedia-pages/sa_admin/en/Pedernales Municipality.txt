The '''Pedernales Municipality''' is one of the four [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Delta Amacuro]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 6,535.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{Dead link|date=July 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Pedernales, Delta Amacuro|Pedernales]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Pedernales Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Deltaamacuro.zip{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The Pedernales District became the Pedernales Municipality in 1994.

==Demographics==
The Pedernales Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 6,535 (up from 5,181 in 2000).  This amounts to 4.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1.85|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/deltaamacuro/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=Cyberbot II |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pedernales Municipality is Selgio Buanerges Ramírez, elected on October 31, 2004, with 40% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Mayor of Municipality PEDERNALES |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/23/alcaldedis=0&edo=23&mun=2&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |access-date=2024-06-27 |website=cne.gob.ve}}</ref>   He replaced Cristobal Jimenez shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=e016.pdf
 |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Pedernales and Luis Beltrán Prieto Figueroa (previous to February 25, 1995, the Pedernales Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
