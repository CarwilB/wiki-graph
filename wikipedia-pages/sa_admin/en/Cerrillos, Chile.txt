'''Cerrillos''' (English: '''Hillocks''') is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in a midtown area of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]] and the southwest of the city — in the [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. The commune was created in 1991 from a subdivision of [[Maipú, Chile|Maipú]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], the commune spans an area of {{convert|21|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has a population of 71,906<ref name="INE"/> living in 19,811 households,<ref name="INEDiv"/> giving it a population density of {{convert|3424.1|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. At that time, there were 34,961 men and 36,945 women. The population fell 1.0% (743 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Cerrillos is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 mayor is Johnny Yáñez (Ind), and the council has the following members:<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>
* Luis Leiva Godoy ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Josefina Osorio Oviedo ([[Popular Green Alliance Party|PAVP]])
* Roxana Muñoz Daza ([[Radical Party of Chile|PR]]
* Juan Jiménez Retamal ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Daivit Mellado Ramírez (Ind)
* Carolina Burgos Alarcón ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Cerillos is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] as a part of the 20th electoral district (together with [[Estación Central]] and [[Maipú, Chile|Maipú]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
