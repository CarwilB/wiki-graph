'''Abreus''' ({{IPA|es|aˈβɾews}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Cienfuegos Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1840.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vabreu/index.htm|title=Abreus|author=Guije.com|accessdate=7 October 2007| archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20071012221925/http://guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vabreu/index.htm| archivedate= 12 October 2007 | url-status= live|language=es}}</ref>

==History==
Abreus was founded as San Segundo de los Abreu in 1840 by Trinidadian landowners during an economic boom of slave-labor sugar cane plantations.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://abreus.gob.cu/nuestra-region/region-cizq/un-poco-de-historia|title=Un poco de historia|date=2019-11-17|language=es|publisher=Government of Abreus|access-date=2021-07-13|archive-date=2024-04-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240414202215/https://abreus.gob.cu/nuestra-region/region-cizq/un-poco-de-historia|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality includes the town of Abreus<ref name=guije/> and the villages of Babiney, Carmelina, Cayo Grande, Charcas, Cienguita, Constancia, Horquitas, [[Juraguá]], [[Matun, Cuba|Matun]], Mijailito, Navarra, Nueva Juraguá, San Ignacio, Simpatía and Yaguaramas. It is bordered by [[Zapata Swamp|Ciénaga de Zapata]] and [[Calimete]], both in [[Matanzas Province]], and by [[Aguada de Pasajeros]], [[Rodas]] and [[Cienfuegos]].

==Demographics==
In 2019, the municipality of Abreus had a population of 30,719; 51% of the population identified as men.<ref name=one2019>{{cite web|url=http://www.onei.gob.cu/sites/default/files/anuario_est_municipal/00_anuario_abreus_.pdf|title=ANUARIO ESTADÍSTICO 2019 CINFUEGOS ABREUS|date=2019|publisher=Oficina Nacional de Estadística|language=es|access-date=2021-07-13|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210713054540/http://www.onei.gob.cu/sites/default/files/anuario_est_municipal/00_anuario_abreus_.pdf|archive-date=2021-07-13}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|579|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}} it has a population density of {{convert|53|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.<ref name=one2019/>
