'''Cucaita''' is a [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Central Boyacá Province]], part of [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]]. The urban centre is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|20|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. Cucaita borders [[Sora, Boyacá|Sora]] in the north, Tunja in the east and south and [[Samacá]] in the south and west.<ref name=websiteCucaita>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.cucaita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cucaita] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150529032211/http://www.cucaita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-29 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Cucaita is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means either "Seminary enclosure" or "Shade of the farming fields".<ref name=websiteCucaita/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Municipalities Boyacá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Cucaita in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organised in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Cucaita was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' of nearby [[Tunja|Hunza]].

Modern Cucaita was founded on August 12, 1556 by [[friar]] Juan de Los Barrios.<ref name=websiteCucaita/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Cucaita are [[agriculture]] (predominantly [[onion]]s and [[pea]]s), [[livestock]] farming and minor [[carbon]] [[mining]].<ref name=websiteCucaita/>
