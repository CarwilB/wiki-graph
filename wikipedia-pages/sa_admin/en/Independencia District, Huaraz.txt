'''Independencia District''' is one of twelve [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huaraz Province|Huaraz]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
The [[Cordillera Blanca]] traverses the district. Some of the highest peaks of the district are [[Pallqarahu]], [[Pukaranra]], [[Ranrapallqa]] and [[Chinchey|Rurichinchay]]. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL map of the Huaraz Province (Ancash Region)</ref>

* [[Churup]]
* [[Hatun Kunka]]
* [[Puka Qaqa Punta]]
* [[Rima Rima]]
* [[Uqshapallqa]]
* [[Tullparahu]]
