'''Turrialba''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Cartago Province|Cartago]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Turrialba District|Turrialba]] district.

== History ==
Turrialba was created on 19 August 1903 by decree 84.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

== Geography ==
Turrialba has an area of {{formatnum:1642.67}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020}}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:768}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

Turrialba is the eleventh largest canton among the eighty two cantons that comprise Costa Rica. The Turrialba River and [[Atirro River]] establish major portions of the canton's western border, and the [[Chirripó River]] delineates its long southeastern border.  The region is home to the active [[Turrialba Volcano]].

== Economy ==
===Tourism===
[[Guayabo National Monument]] is a historic archaeological site located 18&nbsp;km north of Turrialba city, it is located in the [[Santa Teresita District|Santa Teresita]] and [[Santa Cruz District, Turrialba|Santa Cruz]] districts of the canton.  It is one of Costa Rica's only two [[Pre-Columbian era|pre-Columbian]] sites that is open to the public, together with the [[Stone spheres of Costa Rica|Stone Spheres Museum]] in the south of the country.<ref>{{cite web |title=Guayabo National Archeological Monument |url=https://www.visitcostarica.com/en/costa-rica/where-to-go/protected-areas/guayabo-national-archeological-monument |website=Visit Costa Rica |publisher=ICT |access-date=13 November 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Guayabo National Monument |url=http://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/accvc/gnm/Pages/default.aspx |publisher=SINAC |access-date=13 November 2020}}</ref>

The [[Turrialba Volcano National Park]] is located in the canton, reopened after it was closed from 2012 to 2020 due to eruptions.

===Dairy farms=== 
The eponymous [[Turrialba cheese]] is produced in this region.

===Bananas===
Lower Turrialba is within Costa Rica's banana production zone and was important in the origin of [[United Fruit]].
