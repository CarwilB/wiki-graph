'''Apulco''' is one of the 58 [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipalities]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]]. It is located in the southern part of the state of Zacatecas and it is bounded by the municipality of [[Municipality of Nochistlán de Mejía|Nochistlán de Mejía]]. The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|200|km2|sqmi|sp=us}}.<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32002a.htm "Apulco", Encyclopedia de Municipios ] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210331/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32002a.htm |date=September 27, 2007 }}</ref>

The municipal seat is the city of [[Apulco]].

==Population==
In the 2005 census Apulco reported a population of 4,801.<ref name=pop2005>[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070328224939/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |date=March 28, 2007 }} INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática</ref> Of these, 1,453 lived in the municipal seat and the remainder in surrounding rural communities.
