'''Pacasmayo''' is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]];<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.businessempresarial.com.pe/la-libertad-supera-los-600-medios-de-comunicacion/|title=La Libertad supera los 600 medios de comunicación|work=Business Empresarial|date=13 October 2023|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> Pacasmayo is also the name of one of its districts. The capital of this province is the city of [[San Pedro de Lloc]].
