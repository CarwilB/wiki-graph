'''Maní Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: 'Place where everything happened', properly 'to travel or walk to some place’, that is, ''pasar caminando a alguna parte''; ''desfilar''; ''transitar'')<ref>Alfredo Barrera Vásquez, director; Juan Ramón Bastarrachea Manzano and William Brito Sansores, editors: Diccionario maya Cordemex: Maya-Español, Español-Maya, Vol. 2, Mérida, Yucatán, 1980, Ediciones Cordemex</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]  containing 85.59&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly 90&nbsp;km south of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
In the pre-Hispanic period the area was part of the chieftainship of Tutul Xiu and it was a ceremonial center, where the annual festival to [[Kukulcan]] was held. At the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], Maní became part of the [[encomienda]] system<ref name="municipios"/> with its lands assigned to the Spanish Crown, which was still the sole trustee in 1565.<ref name="Quezada (2014)">{{cite book|last1=Quezada|first1=Sergio|title=Maya lords and lordship: the formation of colonial society in Yucatán, 1350-1600|date=2014|publisher=University of Oklahoma Press|location=Norman|isbn=978-0-806-14579-2|page=133| url=https://books.google.com/books?id=P2W9AgAAQBAJ&pg=PA133|access-date=2 August 2015}}</ref>

In 1562, Franciscan priest [[Diego de Landa]] ordered an [[Inquisition]] in [[Maní, Yucatán|Maní]] ending with a ceremony called the [[auto-da-fé]], where the Maya were whipped and ceremonial objects and [[Maya codices]] were burned. Much of the heritage of the Maya was lost due to these actions.<ref name=municipios />

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Tekax Municipality]].<ref name=municipios /> In 1867 it passed to the [[Ticul Municipality]] and in 1931 became the head of its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995 |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |website=inegi |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=31 July 2015 |location=Aguascalientes, Mexico |language=es |date=1996 |pages=111, 166 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |archive-date=23 December 2015 }}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, potable water and ecology.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Maní|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=2 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=8 May 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140508064449/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance/>

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance/>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Maní, Yucatán|Maní]], Yucatán.  Besides the seat, the populated areas of the municipality include:  Miramar, Mópila, Póh Chuu, San Chakán, San José, San Pedro, San Rafael, San Román, San Simón, Santa Amalia, Santa Ana, Santa Antonia, Santa Cruz, Santa Rosa, Santa Teresa, Santa Ursula and Tipikal. ==Local festivals==
Every year from 15 to 24 August 24, a celebration is held in honor of the Virgin of the Assumption and from 27 December to 3 January is a festival for the Virgen de la Candelaria.<ref name="municipios"/>
