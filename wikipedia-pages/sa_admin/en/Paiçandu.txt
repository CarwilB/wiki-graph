'''Paiçandu''' is a city in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] in southern Brazil. Paiçandu was founded in 1948, and emancipated on 19 November 1960. Its population was 41,773 (2020) and its area is 171.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/20A]</ref>

==Geography==
*Altitude: 550 m
*Distance from [[Curitiba]], the state capital: 449&nbsp;km
*Distance from [[Paranaguá]], Paraná's primary seaport: 540&nbsp;km
*Distance from [[Maringá]], site of the nearest major airport: 7&nbsp;km

==Climate==
Paiçandu's [[subtropical climate]] offers hot summers and concentrated [[wet season]]s; Paiçandu has no [[dry season]]. The average high temperature is 22&nbsp;°C. Frost rarely accompanies the winter weather (the average low temperature is 18&nbsp;°C).
