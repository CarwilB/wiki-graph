'''Tetiz Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "place of the sweet potato") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 180.11&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|30|km}} west of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and was located in the chieftainship of Ak Canul. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos, including Pablo de Aguilar and Alonso Hernández between 1700 and 1750.<ref name="municipios"/> 

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825 the area was assigned to the Lower Camino Real under the  [[Hunucma Municipality]]. In 1918 the area was confirmed as its own municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of heritage and public works; police and transit; public services, education and health; and parks, gardens and ecology.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Tetiz|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=18 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
