The '''Guacara Municipality''' is one of the fourteen [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the  [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Carabobo]]. according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 176,218.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Guacara]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Guacara Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Carabobo.zip {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Guacara Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 171,123 (up from 145,132 in 2000).  This amounts to 7.7% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1037.11|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/carabobo/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Guacara Municipality is Jhoan Castañeda.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=07&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref>  He replaced Jose Manuel Flores Salazar shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; [[Ciudad Alianza, Venezuela|Ciudad Alianza]], Guacara, and [[Yagua (Venezuela)|Yagua]].<ref name=autogenerated1 />
