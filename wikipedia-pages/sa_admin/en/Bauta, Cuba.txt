'''Bauta''' is a municipality and town located {{convert|40|km|abbr=on}} southwest of  [[Havana|Havana City]], in the [[Artemisa Province]] of [[Cuba]] since 2010 as a result of the division of what was the [[La Habana Province|Province of Havana]] (Havana Countryside).

==Geography==
Part of the [[Havana]] metropolitan area, the municipality includes the villages of Anafe, Cangrejeras, Cayo La Rosa, Comunidad Ben Tre, Corralillo (Félix E. Alpízar), Lazo de la Vega, Machurrucutu, [[Playa Baracoa]], Pueblo Textil (Ariguanabo), Rosa Marina and San Pedro (General Antonio Maceo).<ref>{{OSM|r|2576648|Bauta}}</ref> [[Río Baracoa|Baracoa River]] crosses the municipality, and Playa Baracoa is established at its mouth at the [[Gulf of Mexico]].

== Demographics ==
In 2022, the municipality of Bauta had a population of 51,300.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|157|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003 |access-date=2007-10-07 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071012141831/http://statoids.com/ycu.html| archivedate= 12 October 2007 |url-status=live}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|330|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Economy==
Bauta is a regional trading center for the local farm community. Regional crops include [[sugarcane]], [[pineapple]], [[tobacco]], [[root vegetable]]s, and [[citrus]] fruits. In recent years Bauta has become a haven for Cuban artists and [[musician]]s.

==Transport==
Bauta, crossed by the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]], is served by [[Autopista A4 (Cuba)|A4 motorway]],<ref>{{OSM|n|455205897|Bauta (A4 exit)}}</ref> passing some km north. From 2014, it counts a railway station<ref>{{OSM|n|3720874897||Bauta railway station}}</ref> on the new [[Havana]]-[[Guanajay]]-[[Artemisa]]/[[Mariel, Cuba|Mariel]] line, part of the [[Havana–Pinar del Río railway|Havana-Artemisa-Pinar del Río line]]. [[Playa Baracoa Airport]], serving Havana, is located in the neighboring municipal territory of [[Caimito, Cuba|Caimito]], but lies in front of the village of [[Playa Baracoa]].<ref>{{OSM|n|455624731|Playa Baracoa}}</ref><ref>{{OSM|n|259849770|Playa Baracoa Airport}}</ref>
