'''Vila Valério''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 14,073 (2020) and its area is 464 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] – {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=August 3, 2014 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|27858|ha}} [[Sooretama Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1982 when two earlier units were merged.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica de Sooretama|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=201|access-date=2016-04-29}}</ref><ref>{{citation|language=pt|title=Reserva Biológica de Sooretama|publisher=ICMBio|url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/rebiosooretama/|access-date=2016-04-17|archive-date=2016-05-06|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160506042954/http://www.icmbio.gov.br/rebiosooretama/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
