'''Iquique Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Iquique}}) is one of two [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] in the northern [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Tarapacá Region|Tarapacá]]. Its capital is the port city of [[Iquique]].

==History==
Until October 2007, the Province of Iquique was composed of 7 communes: Alto Hospicio, Camina, Colchane, Huara, Iquique, Pica and Pozo Almonte, but since then, with the creation of the Region of Arica and Parinacota, much of the province, specifically the municipalities of Huara, Camina, Colchane, Pozo Almonte and Pica, was transferred administratively to Tamarugal Province, leaving Iquique Province consists of two communes.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Geography and demography==
According to the 2012 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|2835.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and had a population of 275,042 inhabitants, giving it a population density of {{convert|76.3|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 30.8% (50,959 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a [[province]], Iquique is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], which is further divided into two [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas''): the capital Iquique and its suburb [[Alto Hospicio]]. The province is administered by the presidentially appointed regional delegate.<ref name="Gobernadores"/>
