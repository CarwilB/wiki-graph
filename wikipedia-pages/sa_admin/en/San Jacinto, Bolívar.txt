'''San Jacinto''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

The [[San Jacinto archaeological site]] is located near the town.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Jacinto 1. Ecología histórica, orígenes de la cerámica e inicios de la vida sedentaria en el Caribe colombiano. ==Culture==

San Jacinto is the most important town in northern Colombia for local, traditional woven textiles such as hammocks and clothing as well as handicrafts. The town the birthplace of musician [[Adolfo Pacheco]],<ref>{{citation |url=https://www.eltiempo.com/colombia/barranquilla/fallecio-adolfo-pacheco-en-barranquilla-tras-accidente-de-transito-737520 |title=Falleció el juglar Adolfo Pacheco tras accidente de tránsito |trans-title=Adolfo Pacheco dies after traffic accident |language=es |work=[[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]] |author=David Alejandro López Bermúdez |date=2023-01-28 |access-date=2025-04-11}}</ref> and of the [[Latin Grammy Award]] winner [[cumbia]] group [[Los Gaiteros de San Jacinto]].
