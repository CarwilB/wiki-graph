'''Villagarzón''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Putumayo Department]], [[Republic of Colombia]].

==Climate==
Villagarzón has a very wet and overcast [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'').
