'''Tierra Oscura ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Bocas del Toro District]], [[Bocas del Toro Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|88.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,661 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|30|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 10 of March 7, 1997; this measure was complemented by Law 5 of January 19, 1998 and Law 69 of October 28, 1998.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 1,950.<ref name=inec/>
