'''Anta''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]]. Its capital is [[Joaquín V. González, Argentina|Joaquín Víctor González]].

With an area of {{convert|21945|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the departments of [[Orán Department|Orán]] and [[Rivadavia Department, Salta|Rivadavia]], to the east with Rivadavia and [[Chaco Province]], to the south with [[Santiago del Estero Province]], and to the west with the departments of [[Metán Department|Metán]] and [[General Güemes Department, Salta|General Güemes]] and with [[Jujuy Province]].
