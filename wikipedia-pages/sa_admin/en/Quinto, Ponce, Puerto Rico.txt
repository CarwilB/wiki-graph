'''Quinto''' (''Barrio Quinto'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].   Together with [[Primero (Ponce)|Primero]], [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]], and [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], Quinto is one of the municipality's [[Ponce, Puerto Rico#Barrios|six original urban barrios]]. It was organized in 1878.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

[[File:Paseo Atocha, Bo. Quinto, Ponce, Puerto Rico, visto desde la Calle Vives, mirando al sur (DSC03207).jpg|thumb|250px|left|[[Paseo Atocha]], the western boundary of Barrio Quinto. Stores on the right are part of [[Barrio Segundo]]]]

==Location==
Quinto is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and northeast of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

==Boundaries==
[[File:Francisco Pancho Coimbre Museum in Ponce (IMG 2949).jpg|thumb|left|200px|[[Francisco Pancho Coimbre Museum]] at [[Lolita Tizol]] and Castillo Streets in Barrio Quinto]]
Barrio Quinto is bounded on the North by Guadalupe Street, on the South by Isabel Street, on the West by [[Paseo Atocha|Atocha]] Street, and on the East by [[Rio Portugues]].<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=12 August 2010 }} Retrieved 18 February 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Quinto is bounded in the North by [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], in the South by [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], in the West by [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], and in the East by [[Machuelo Abajo]].

==Features and demographics==
Quinto has {{convert|0.1|sqmi|m2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area.  In 2000, the population of Qinto was 724. The population density in Quinto was 6,811 persons per square mile.<ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Quinto+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved 17 February 2010.</ref>

==Notable landmarks==
Quinto is home to the [[Museo Francisco Pancho Coimbre]]. Also, the [[Plaza de Mercado de Ponce|Isabel II Marketplace]] (''[[Plaza del Mercado de Ponce]]'') is located here. [[NRHP]]-listed landmarks in Barrio Quinto include the [[Font-Ubides House]], the [[Mercado de las Carnes]] (Plaza Juan Ponce de León), and the old [[Antiguo Cuartel Militar Español de Ponce|Spanish Military Headquarters]]/city jail.
