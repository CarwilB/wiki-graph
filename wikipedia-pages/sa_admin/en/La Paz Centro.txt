'''La Paz Centro''' ({{IPA|es|la pas ˈsentɾo}}) is a town and a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[León Department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

The town is located along the highway between [[Managua]] and [[León, Nicaragua|León]]. The small municipality is known for its small shops and [[pottery]]. La Paz Centro is the home of the [[Asososca Lagoon Natural Reserve]], which is one of the few water reserves in Nicaragua that remains uncontaminated. For decades, La Paz Centro and the bordering town [[Nagarote]] have been in a feud about which town makes the best [[quesillo]]s.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Wei |first=Clarissa |date=October 19, 2015 |title=These Two Towns Are in a Bitter Feud Over a Cheese Taco |url=https://www.vice.com/en/article/these-two-towns-are-in-a-bitter-feud-over-a-cheese-taco/ |access-date=August 8, 2025 |work=Vice Media}}</ref>

The town was the site of a [[Battle of La Paz Centro|battle]] between [[United States Marine Corps|American Marines]] and Nicaraguan Liberal rebels in the aftermath of the [[Nicaraguan Civil War (1926–1927)]] on May 16, 1927, prior to the [[Sandino Rebellion]].
