'''São Gonçalo do Piauí''' (''first part, Portuguese meaning "San Goncalo of Piaui"'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Piauí]]. It is located at an altitude of 270 meters. The population is 5,030 (2020 est.) in an area of 150.22 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23IKX Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> After a plebiscite held in 1995, part of the municipality was split off and became the municipality of [[Santo Antônio dos Milagres]].
