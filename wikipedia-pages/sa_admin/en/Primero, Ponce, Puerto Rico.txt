'''Primero''' (''Barrio Primero'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s of the municipality of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Together with [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], [[Tercero (Ponce)|Tercero]], [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]], [[Quinto (Ponce)|Quinto]], and [[Sexto (Ponce)|Sexto]], Primero is one of the municipality's [[Ponce, Puerto Rico#Barrios|original six core urban barrios]]. It was founded in 1878.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
Primero is an urban barrio located in the southern section of the municipality, within the Ponce city limits, and southwest of the traditional center of the city, [[Plaza Las Delicias]].

==Boundaries==
Barrio Primero is bounded on the North by Villa Street, on the South by Ferrocarril and Dr. Ferran Streets, on the West by [[Puerto Rico Highway 2#Ponce spur|PR-2R]] (Carretera Pámpanos / Calle Coto Canas), and on the East by Eugenio Maria de Hostos Avenue and Marina Street.<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=2010-08-12 }} Retrieved 17 February 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Primero is bounded in the North by Barrio [[Segundo (Ponce)|Segundo]], in the South by [[Canas Urbano]],  in the West by Barrio Canas Urbano, and in the East by Barrio [[Cuarto (Ponce)|Cuarto]]. The communities of Ponce de Leon, Los Maestros, Mariani, and Pedro J. Rosaly are located in Barrio Primero.

==Notable landmarks==
Primero is home to [[Ponce City Hall]] on its northeastern end.
The [[Dora Colón Clavell Urban Park]], is also found in Barrio Primero. The historic, [[NRHP]]-listed, [[Rosaly-Batiz House]], [[Residencia Zaldo de Nebot]] on 27 Marina Street, and the [[Missionary Society of the Methodist Episcopal Church]] on 132 Villa Street, are also in Barrio Primero.
