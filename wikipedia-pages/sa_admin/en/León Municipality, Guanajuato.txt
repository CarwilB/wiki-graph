'''León Municipality''' is a municipality in the state of [[Guanajuato]]. Its municipal seat is located in the city of León. According to the 2020 census, the municipality had a population of 1,721,215.<ref name=inegi_app/>

It is the [[Municipalities of Mexico|third most populous municipality]] in Mexico, behind Mexico City and Tijuana. The city contains about 91% of the municipalities' population.
