'''Larreynaga''' ({{IPA|es|laˈrejnaɣa}}) is the second largest [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[León Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. The municipality seat is the town of Malpaisillo.<ref>[http://vianica.com/sp/nicaragua/leon/larreynaga-malpaisillo/8.5 Larreynaga en vianica.com]</ref> The estimated population of the municipality is 33,117 but it is steadily growing at a rate of 2.22% yearly.<ref name="MF">[http://www.manfut.org/leon/larreynaga.html Larreynaga, Leon, Nicaragua<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>

==History==
The municipality of Larreynaga was founded by Colonel Manuel Ignacio Pereira Quintana on August 11, 1944. The town of Malpaisillo was officially declared a city in September 1968. It got its name "Larreynaga" from [[Miguel Larreynaga]], a Nicaraguan philosopher, [[humanist]], lawyer and poet.<ref name="MF"/><ref>{{cite news | first=Ramón | last=Juárez Salazar | title=Telica cumple 130 años de ser municipio | url=http://archivo.elnuevodiario.com.ni/2001/mayo/14-mayo-2001/departamentos/departamentos4.html | work=El Nuevo Diario | accessdate=2008-01-17 | language=Spanish | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110717185716/http://archivo.elnuevodiario.com.ni/2001/mayo/14-mayo-2001/departamentos/departamentos4.html | archivedate=2011-07-17 }}</ref>

The municipality of Larreynaga is home to the [[Cerro Negro]] volcano, just 10&nbsp;km from the city of Malpaisillo. Cerro Negro has erupted frequently since its first eruption.<ref>{{cite gvp|vn=344070|vtab=Eruptions|name=Cerro Negro}}</ref> The last volcanic activity at Cerro Negro occurred on August 5, 1999, and lasted for a total of two days.

In recent years, the Cerro Negro volcano has been a popular destination for [[sandboarding]].<ref>[http://vianica.com/attraction/7/cerro-negro-volcano Cerro Negro at vianica.com]</ref>
