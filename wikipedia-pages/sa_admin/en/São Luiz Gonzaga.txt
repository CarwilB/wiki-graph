'''São Luiz Gonzaga''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] for [[St. Aloysius Gonzaga]]) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Its population is 33,293 (2020 est.) in an area of 1295.68&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/235H1 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> It was founded in the 17th century as a [[Misiones Orientales|Jesuit mission town]]. It is located 503&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].

==Geography==
===Climate===
São Luiz Gonzaga has a [[humid subtropical climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfa'') with hot summers, mild winters, and ample rainfall throughout the year.
