'''Nuevo Emperador''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Arraiján District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 3,903 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,319; its population as of 2000 was 2,765.<ref name=inec/>
