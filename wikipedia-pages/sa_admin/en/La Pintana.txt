'''La Pintana''' is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. La Pintana is home to Antumapu, the agricultural and veterinary sciences campus of [[University of Chile]], Chile's oldest university.

==Demographics==
According to the 1999 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], La Pintana spans an area of {{convert|30.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 190,085 inhabitants (94,963 men and 95,122 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 12.1% (20,445 persons) between the 1990 and 1999 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The 2006 projected population was 201,183.<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |accessdate=13 September 2010 |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archivedate=23 August 2010 }}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=September 2010}}

==Administration==
As a commune, La Pintana is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Claudia Pizarro Peña ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]]). The communal council has the following members:
* Isaac Núñez Sepúlveda ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Sara Guerrero Martínez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Marcela Poveda Morales ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|DC]])
* René Díaz Jorquera ([[Radical Party of Chile|PR]])
* Iván López Bilbao ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Stephany Hurtado Panes ([[Popular Green Alliance Party|PAVP]])
* Roberto Jaramillo Carreño ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Waleska Salas Núñez ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], La Pintana is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Osvaldo Andrade ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Leopoldo Pérez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 29th electoral district, (together with [[Puente Alto]], [[Pirque]] and [[San José de Maipo]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Soledad Alvear]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Pablo Longueira]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 8th senatorial constituency (Santiago-East).
