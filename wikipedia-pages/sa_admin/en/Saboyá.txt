'''Saboyá''' is a town and municipality in the [[Western Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]].<ref name=websiteSaboya>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.saboya-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Saboyá] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150602123549/http://www.saboya-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-06-02 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Saboyá is an Italian name brought by European colonizers.<ref name=Casa_Saboya>{{in lang|es}} [https://conceptosdelahistoria.com/conceptos-politicos/monarquia/casa-de-saboya/] - conceptosdelahistoria.com </ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] on the central highlands of the Colombian [[Andes]], Saboyá was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' with the same name.

The first [[encomienda|encomendero]] of Saboyá was Pedro de Galeano, brother of [[Martín Galeano]] and soldier in the army of [[Hernán Pérez de Quesada]], brother of Spanish [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]]. Modern Saboyá was founded on October 4, 1556.<ref name=websiteSaboya/>

[[Simón Bolívar]] visited Saboyá on three occasions: January 2, 1821, September 6, 1827 and June 9, 1828.<ref name=websiteSaboya/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Saboyá are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Among the agricultural products [[potato]]es, [[maize]] and the fruits [[curuba]], [[blackberry|blackberries]], [[tree tomato]]es and [[strawberry|strawberries]] are cultivated.<ref name=websiteSaboya/>
