'''Alvinópolis''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. {{as of|2020}} its population is estimated to be 15,169.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/alvinopolis/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira.
