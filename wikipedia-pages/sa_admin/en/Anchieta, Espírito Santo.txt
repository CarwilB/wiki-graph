'''Anchieta''' is a  [[List of municipalities in Espírito Santo|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 29,779 in 2020 and its area is {{cvt|412|km2|sqmi}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/7OT Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]</ref> Its average elevation is {{cvt|2|m|ft}} above sea level.

Formerly known as ''Reritiba'', the city was renamed after the [[Society of Jesus|Jesuit]] [[Spain|Spanish]] missionary and [[Canonization|saint]] [[José de Anchieta]] (1534-1597).
