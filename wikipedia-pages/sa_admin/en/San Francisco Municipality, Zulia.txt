'''San Francisco''' is a [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipality]] in the metropolitan area of [[Maracaibo]], [[Venezuela]]'s second largest city. San Francisco covers an area of {{convert|185|km2}}<ref name=area/> and recorded a population of 446,757 in the 2011 Venezuelan census.<ref name=census2011/>

==Geography==
San Francisco is situated on the Maracaibo Plain on the west shore of Tablazo Strait, which connects [[Lake Maracaibo]] with the [[Caribbean Sea]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Maracaibo Municipality|Maracaibo]] to the north, [[Jesús Enrique Lossada Municipality|Jesús Enrique Lossada]] to the west, and [[La Cañada de Urdaneta Municipality|La Cañada de Urdaneta]] to the south.<ref name=muni>{{cite report |url=http://www.corpozulia.gob.ve/archivos/SAN_FRANCISCO_2010-2011.pdf |title=Municipio San Francisco |language=es |publisher=Corporación de Desarrollo de la Región Zuliana |date=2010 |accessdate=12 October 2020}}</ref>

San Francisco has a semi-arid climate, with an average annual temperature of 27.8 °C and annual rainfall of 700 mm.<ref name=muni/>

==History==
In his 1830 atlas of Venezuela, [[Agustín Codazzi]] recorded a ''Punta de San Francisco'' located between the towns of Maracaibo and La Cañada. San Francisco was first officially recorded as the name of a ''parroquia'' in 1881.<ref>{{cite web |last=Fernández León |first=Julio |date=6 October 2011 |title=HISTORIA DEL MUNICIPIO SAN FRANCISCO (Monografía) |url=http://juliofernandezleon.blogspot.com/2011/10/historia-del-municipio-san-francisco.html |language=es |accessdate=12 October 2020}}</ref> The current municipality was created on 22 January 1995 from the ''parroquias'' of San Francisco, Francisco Ochoa, Marcial Hernandez, and Domitila Flores, all previously part of the municipality of Maracaibo.<ref name=division>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |title=División Político Territorial de la República Bolivariana de Venezuela 2013 |language=es |publisher=National Institute of Statistics (Venezuela) |pages=153–154, 159 |date=September 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200529033749/http://www.ine.gov.ve/documentos/AspectosFisicos/DivisionpoliticoTerritorial/pdf/DPTconFinesEstadisticosOperativa2013.pdf |archive-date=29 May 2020}}</ref>

==Economy==
San Francisco is highly urbanized and its economy is sustained by secondary and tertiary activities.<ref>{{cite journal |last=Ortiz |first=Rosa E. |date=22 January 2014 |title=Caracterización de las Organizaciones No Gubernamentales: El caso de los municipios Maracaibo y San Francisco |url=https://www.redalyc.org/articulo.oa?id=29003207 |language=es |journal=Revista Venezolana de Gerencia |volume=10 |issue=32 |pages=652–674 |accessdate=12 October 2020}}</ref> Major industries include coal, oil, cement, and fish processing.<ref name=muni/>

==Infrastructure==
[[File:General Rafael Urdaneta Bridge view from the lake to Cabimas side.jpg|thumb|The [[General Rafael Urdaneta Bridge]], which connects San Francisco with Santa Rita on the other side of the Tablazo Strait.]] 
[[File:Terminal de buses Simón Bolívar.jpg|thumb|The Simón Bolívar bus terminal.]]
San Francisco is the western terminus of the Troncal 3 highway, which crosses the Tablazo Strait over the [[General Rafael Urdaneta Bridge]] and connects Maracaibo to [[Coro, Venezuela|Coro]], the capital of [[Falcón]], and the town of [[Morón, Venezuela|Morón]] in [[Carabobo]]. San Francisco also lies on the Troncal 6 highway, which runs north-south through Zulia from the border crossing to Colombia at [[Paraguachón]] to [[La Fría]] in [[Táchira]].

Intercity buses stop at the Simón Bolívar bus terminal at the interchange of Troncal 3 with Avenida 5.<ref>{{cite news |date=8 August 2016 |title=Terminal de San Francisco cubre rutas hacia varios estados a precios solidarios |url=https://versionfinal.com.ve/ciudad/terminal-de-san-francisco-cubre-rutas-hacia-varios-estados-a-precios-solidarios/ |language=es |publisher=Versión Final |accessdate=12 October 2020}}</ref> The bus terminal is also the terminus of a circular bus route which serves San Francisco and is operated by Maracaibo's [[Metromara]] bus system.<ref>{{cite web |date=2016 |title=Cuáles son las rutas de BusMetroMara TransGuajira BusUrdaneta y BusLaConcepción |url=https://www.notilogia.com/2016/01/cuales-son-las-rutas-de-busmetromara-transguajira-busurdaneta-y-buslaconcepcion.html |language=es |publisher=Notilogía |accessdate=12 October 2020}}</ref>

[[La Chinita International Airport]], which serves the Maracaibo area, is located in San Francisco.

The municipality has two hospitals.<ref name=muni/>

Recreational areas in San Francisco include the [[Zulia Metropolitan Zoo]] and a botanical garden.<ref name=muni/>
