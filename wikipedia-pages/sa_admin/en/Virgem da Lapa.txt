'''Virgem da Lapa''' ({{IPA|pt|viʁˈʒẽj da ˈlapɐ}}) is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was estimated to be 13,740 people living in a total area of {{convert|871|km²|0|abbr=on}}.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/virgem-da-lapa/panorama |title=IBGE 2020 |access-date=2021-01-11 |archive-date=2020-06-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200630030755/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/virgem-da-lapa/panorama |url-status=live }}</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Jequitinhonha and to the microregion of [[Araçuaí]]. It became a municipality in 1948. Neighboring municipalities are [[Rubelita]], [[Josenópolis]], [[Grão Mogol]], [[Berilo]], [[Francisco Badaró]], [[Araçuaí]], and [[Coronel Murta]].

== Etymology ==
Virgem da Lapa ("Virgin of Lapa") honors Our Lady of Lapa ([[:pt:Nossa_Senhora_da_Lapa|Nossa Senhora da Lapa]]), whose image was discovered near the city in a cave where there is now a sanctuary.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Virgem da Lapa |url=https://www.asminasgerais.com.br/?item=CONTEUDO&codConteudoRaiz=975 |access-date=2026-01-22 |website=AS MINAS GERAIS}}</ref> Our Lady of Lapa is the patron saint of the [[Diocese of Araçuaí]].<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Turismo em Minas Gerais {{!}} Santuário de Nossa Senhora da Lapa |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/atracoes/virgem-da-lapa/santuario-de-nossa-senhora-da-lapa |access-date=2026-01-22 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== History ==
The region that currently corresponds to the municipality of Virgem da Lapa was originally inhabited by the Aranã indigenous people. With the colonization process, the area began to be explored by ''garimpeiros'' ([[Prospecting|prospectors]]) in search of [[precious stones]]. In 1729, the Portuguese landowner Antônio Pereira dos Santos, who held numerous enslaved people, obtained a ''sesmaria'' ([[land grant]]) encompassing the left bank of the [[Araçuaí River]] and the right bank of the [[Jequitinhonha River]], then part of the territory of [[Minas Novas]]. The intensification of prospecting activities and the discovery of an image of Our Lady of Lapa (Nossa Senhora da Lapa) led to the establishment of the settlement of São Domingos do Araçuaí. This community was annexed to the municipality of [[Araçuaí]] in 1891 and later emancipated on December 27, 1948, officially becoming the municipality of Virgem da Lapa.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE Cidades - Virgem da Lapa |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/virgem-da-lapa/historico |access-date=2026-01-22 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

== Geography ==
According to the regional division in force since 2017, established by the IBGE, the municipality belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]] and [[Immediate Geographic Region of Araçuaí]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2017">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |title=Base de dados por municípios das Regiões Geográficas Imediatas e Intermediárias do Brasil |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Until then, with the divisions into microregions and mesoregions in force, it was part of the microregion of Araçuaí, which in turn was included in the [[Jequitinhonha (mesoregion)|mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip |access-date=30 October 2017}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

The Jequitinhonha River flows through the municipality, while the Araçuaí River marks the border with [[Francisco Badaró]]. The main access route to the city is via [[BR-367 (Brazil highway)|BR-367]], which connects to {{Interlanguage link|MG-114|pt}}, which in turn links to LMG-677.

== Economy ==
The [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] of Virgem da Lapa is approximately R$ 162.6 million, with 52.3% of the value added coming from public administration, followed by services (36.1%), agriculture and livestock (7.3%), and industry (4.3%). The [[Gross domestic product|per capita GDP]] of Virgem da Lapa is R$ 13,800.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Economia de Virgem da Lapa - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/virgem-da-lapa---mg |access-date=2026-01-22 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref>

== Culture ==
The main annual festival in Virgem da Lapa is the Festa de Agosto, a celebration in honor of Our Lady of Lapa that attracts many pilgrims and devotees from the region. Avenida Castelo Branco, in the city center, is lined with [[Hawker (trade)|street vendors]], and in the evening, performances by nationally famous artists take place.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2016-08-17 |title=Romeiros e mascates fazem a festa de Virgem da Lapa |url=https://aconteceunovale.com.br/portal/?p=95028 |access-date=2026-01-22 |website=Aconteceu no Vale |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
