'''Santa Maria de Jetibá''' is the name of a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in central [[Espírito Santo]], Southeastern [[Brazil]]. It was founded in 1857 by [[Pomeranians (German people)|Pomeranian]] immigrants who arrived through the state capital [[Vitória, Brazil|Vitória]] by going up the river Santa Maria.

==Geography==
Located in the south-central mountain region of Espírito Santo, this municipality has an area of 735.55 square kilometers. The town of Santa Maria de Jetibá is 720 meters above sea level, and the highest point in the state is Garrafão Peak, at 1200 meters (3937&nbsp;ft) above sea level.

==People==
With a total population of 41,015 (2020), Santa Maria de Jetibá is considered one of the last significant [[East Pomeranian dialect|East Pomeranian]]-speaking communities in the world. Those immigrants ([[Pomeranians (German people)|Pomeranians]]), who came from the historical region Pomerania, now part of Germany and Poland where it is known as Pommern and Pomorze respectively.

In Brazil, many Pomeranians also established communities in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] (see [[Pomerode]]) and [[Rio Grande do Sul]] (see [[Arroio do Padre]]). Santa Maria de Jetibá is a place where the people still speak solely East Pomeranian in their day-to-day life. Due to the isolation of this community, most of the members of the community live in very poor conditions. Due also to financial constraints by the state, very little investment in education is forthcoming, and the language barrier persists to be a real problem for the community. This also hinders their future prospects, of course, by them not having proper education, thus a reasonable expectation in the work market. Those communities are mainly rural.

==Climate==

Santa Maria de Jetibá is in a high valley and the altitude is about 700m above sea level. The elevation of this municipality influences the climate which features a cool and dry winter, while there are wet and hot days with cool nights in summer. Santa Maria de Jetibá features a [[humid subtropical climate]] type [[Köppen climate classification|Cwa]].
