'''Los Reyes''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "the kings") is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located about {{convert|100|km}} south of the state capital [[Xalapa]].<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Geography==
The municipality of Los Reyes is located in the [[Sierra de Zongolica]], which are foothills of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] in central Veracruz. It lies at an altitude between {{convert|1200|and(-)|2600|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Tequila, Veracruz|Tequila]] to the north, [[Zongolica]] to the east, [[Texhuacán]] to the south, [[Tlaquilpa]] to the southwest, and [[Atlahuilco]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Los Reyes, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=28 February 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|33.81|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.05% of the state's area.<ref name=snim/>

The land in Los Reyes is mostly either forested (64%) or used as farmland (33%). [[Luvisol]]s predominate in the municipality. The municipality is located in the [[Papaloapan River]] basin.<ref name=compendium/> [[Mexican Federal Highway 123|Federal Highway 123]] runs through the municipality from northwest to east, forming part of the municipal border between Los Reyes and Atlahuilco.

Los Reyes's climate is generally temperate and humid. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1900|and(-)|2600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The original name for the area was Cozcatlán ([[Nahuatl]]: "place of jewels").<ref name=encyclopedia/> According to the ''[[Historia Tolteca-Chichimeca]]'' a group of Nahuatl-speaking [[Nonoalca]] known as the Cozcatecah settled in the Cozcatlán area around 1050, which formed part of the historical region of Nonoalco.<ref>{{cite journal |last=Sandoval Arenas |first=Carlos O. |date=2017 |title=Displacement and revitalization of the Nahuatl language in the High Mountains of Veracruz, Mexico |doi=10.1177/1474022216628390 |journal=Arts & Humanities in Higher Education |volume=16 |issue=1 |pages=66–81}}</ref>

On 28 March 1831, a municipality called Los Santos Reyes de Coscatla was incorporated in the area, forming part of the canton of Orizaba in the state of Veracruz. On 4 June 1888 Los Reyes annexed the municipality of Atlanca. Los Reyes became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=115, 260 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Eclicerio Tequiliquihua Quiahuixtle.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://losreyesveracruz.gob.mx/portal/Transparencia/2 |title=Portal de Transparencia Ayuntamiento de Los Reyes, Veracruz |language=es |publisher=Órgano de Fiscalización Superior del Estado de Veracruz |access-date=28 February 2018}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Los Reyes recorded a population of 5484 inhabitants living in 1261 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=30&mun=137 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Los Reyes |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=28 February 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 5830 inhabitants in Los Reyes, 98.40% of whom reported being of Indigenous ancestry.<ref name=survey/> In the 2010 Census, 4699 people or 86% of the population in Los Reyes reported speaking an Indigenous language, of which 4478 spoke Nahuatl.<ref name=snim/>

There are 20 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Los Reyes, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 963 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Los Reyes is farming and corn is the main crop grown.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.legisver.gob.mx/fiscalizacion/Planes%20Municipales/PlanesMunicipales2014pdf/PMD_LosReyes.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |page=14 |publisher=Municipal council of Los Reyes, Veracruz |access-date=28 February 2018}}</ref>
