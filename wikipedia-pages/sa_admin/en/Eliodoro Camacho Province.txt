'''Eliodoro Camacho''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in [[Bolivia]] situated at [[Lake Titicaca]]. Its seat is [[Puerto Acosta]].

Camacho Province is situated in the western region of the La Paz Department bordered to the north by the [[Muñecas Province]], to the east by the [[Larecaja Province]], to the south by the [[Omasuyos Province]], to the west by [[Lake Titicaca]] and [[Moho Province]] located in the [[Puno Region]] of [[Peru]].

Camacho Province was created during the presidency of General [[Ismael Montes]] on November 5, 1908,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.lexivox.org/norms/BO-L-19081105-3.html |title=www.lexivox.org |publisher=www.lexivox.org |access-date=2011-11-08}}</ref> named after the Bolivian politician and officer [[Eliodoro Camacho]]. Originally it was composed of the cantons Huaycho, Escoma, Carabuco, Italaque, Mocomoco and Ambaná. Huaycho became the capital of the province under the name "Puerto Acosta" (meaning: "Port Acosta") in honor of the writer Nicolás Acosta (1844 - 1893).<ref>[https://elias-blanco.blogspot.com/2010/06/nicolas-acosta.html Diccionario Cultural Boliviano] (Spanish)</ref>

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into five [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]]<ref>[http://www.bolivia.com/noticIAS/AutoNoticias/DetalleNoticia42293.asp www.bolivia.com] (Spanish)</ref>  which are further subdivided into cantons.
