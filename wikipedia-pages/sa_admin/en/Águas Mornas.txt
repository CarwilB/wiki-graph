'''Águas Mornas''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] in the state of [[Santa Catarina (Brazil)|Santa Catarina]].

The municipality contains part of the [[Rio das Lontras Private Natural Heritage Reserve]], a fully protected area of montane rainforest in the [[Atlantic Forest]] biome.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=RPPN Reserve Ecologica Rio das Lontras|publisher=Associação dos Proprietários de Reservas Particulares do Patrimônio Natural de Santa Catarina |url=http://www.rppncatarinense.org.br/hp/assoc_lontras.asp|accessdate=2016-05-18}}</ref>
It also contains part of the {{convert|84130|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Tabuleiro State Park]], a protected area created in 1975.
The lushly-forested park protects the sources of the [[Vargem do Braço River|Vargem do Braço]], [[Cubatão River (south Santa Catarina)|Cubatão]] and [[D'Una River|D'Una]] rivers, which supply most of the drinking water for greater Florianópolis and the south coast region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra do Tabuleiro – FATMA}}|language=Portuguese |title=Parque Estadual da Serra do Tabuleiro|publisher=FATMA: Fundação do meio Ambiente
 |url=http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-da-serra-do-tabuleiro|accessdate=2016-06-11}}</ref>
