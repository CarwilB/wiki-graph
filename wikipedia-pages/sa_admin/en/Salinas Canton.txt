'''Salinas Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Santa Elena Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[Salinas, Ecuador|Salinas]]. Its population at the 2001 census was 49,572. The actual governor of this city is Paul Borbor Mite who was elected by a democratic process in 2008.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ecuador Cantons |url=https://statoids.com/yec.html |access-date=2025-06-05 |website=statoids.com}}</ref>
