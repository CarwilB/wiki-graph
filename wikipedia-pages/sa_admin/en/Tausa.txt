'''Tausa''' ({{IPA|es|ˈtawsa}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Ubaté Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Tausa is and was an important town on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] due to its [[salt mine]]. It was the third most prolific salt deposit for the original inhabitants of the area; the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Tausa's urban centre is located at an elevation of {{convert|2950|m|ft}} (other parts of the municipality reach elevations of {{convert|3700|m|ft}}) and a distance of {{convert|65|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. The municipality borders [[San Cayetano, Cundinamarca|San Cayetano]], [[Carmen de Carupa]] and [[Sutatausa]] in the north, [[Pacho]] in the west, Sutatausa, [[Cucunubá]] and [[Suesca]] in the east and in the south with [[Nemocón]] and [[Cogua]].<ref name=websiteTausa>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tausa-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tausa]{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Tausa comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "tribute".<ref name=Espejo_p1124>Espejo Olaya, 1999, p.1124</ref>

== History ==
Tausa was inhabited since the [[Herrera Period]]. The town was an important mining location of [[halite]] for the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The ''[[zipa]]'' of [[Bogotá|Bacatá]] ruled over Tausa. The mining activities can be seen in the seal of the village; the pick and spade.

Modern Tausa was founded on August 2, 1600.<ref name=websiteTausa/>

== Economy ==
Main sources of income of Tausa are [[agriculture]], [[livestock]] farming and [[halite|salt]] mining.<ref name=websiteTausa/>
