'''Cémaco District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Comarca Emberá-Wounaan]] in [[Panama]].<ref name="dia">{{cite news|url=https://www.diaadia.com.pa/el-pais/ogullosamente-de-capeti-la-comarca-embera-wounaan-ya-tiene-su-primera-abogada-765224|title=¡Ogullosamente de Capetí! La Comarca Emberá Wounaan ya tiene a su primera abogada|work=día a día Panamá|date=30 March 2023|access-date=8 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital is [[Unión Chocó]].  The area of this ''district'' is 3097.5 sq. kilometers. It is on the eastern part of [[Comarca Emberá-Wounaan]]. The other side of the Comarca is [[Sambú District]].

==Administrative divisions==
Cémaco District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

* [[Cirilo Guainora, Embera (Darién)|Cirilo Guainora]]<ref name=dia/>
* [[Lajas Blancas]]
* [[Manuel Ortega, Emberá|Manuel Ortega]]
