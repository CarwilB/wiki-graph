'''Moxos''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Beni Department]], [[Bolivia]]. It is named after the [[Moxos savanna]].

The province consists of one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''San Ignacio de Moxos Municipality''', which is identical to the province. The province is divided into three [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]]:

* San Francisco Canton - 2,684 inhabitants ''(2001)''
* San Ignacio Canton - 17,387 inhabitants 
* San Lorenzo Canton - 1,572 inhabitants
