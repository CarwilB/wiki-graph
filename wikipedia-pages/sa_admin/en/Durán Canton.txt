'''Durán Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Guayas Province]].  Its capital is the city of [[Durán, Ecuador|Durán]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 178,714.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

The canton was created in 1986 during the presidential period of [[León Febres Cordero]] through a presidential decree.
