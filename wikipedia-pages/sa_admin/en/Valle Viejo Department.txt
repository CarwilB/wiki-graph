'''Valle Viejo''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 24,000 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|540|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San Isidro, Catamarca|San Isidro]].

==Prehistory==

The Valle Viejo region has been populated for at least 8,000 years. Around 1,000 years ago the area was settled by a population of farmers and hunters. The population created buildings, irrigation systems, and various fortifications.
