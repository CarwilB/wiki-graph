'''Río Cuarto Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]]. Its capital city is [[Río Cuarto, Córdoba|Río Cuarto]], which is located around 608&nbsp;km from [[Capital Federal]].
