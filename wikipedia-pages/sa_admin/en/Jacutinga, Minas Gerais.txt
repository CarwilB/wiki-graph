'''Jacutinga''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 26,264.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/jacutinga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

== Other uses ==
Jacutinga is also a type of [[gold]]-[[Bearing (mechanical)|bearing]] [[iron]] [[ore]] found in Brazil.
