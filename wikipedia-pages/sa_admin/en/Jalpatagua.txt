'''Jalpatagua''' ({{IPA|es|xalpaˈtaɣwa}}) is a [[town]] and municipality in the [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|175.8|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 31,858 inhabitants. The municipality lies close to the border with [[El Salvador]].

==History==
Jalpatagua is believed to have been derive from the [[Nahuatl]] language words "jal", "atl", and "patlaguac", meaning aspiration, water, and wide respectively and can be roughly interpreted as "wide river".<ref name="Hist"/>

During the late 16th century, the inhabitants were mostly indigenous Pipil and Mulatto people, while the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] owned agricultural estates in the region. Over time, the agricultural farms developed supported by the rivers, and fertile land, which resulted in further immigration. In the 18th century, Jalpatagua was an important center for cattle trade. Historical records from 1806 refer to the settlement as "Xalpatagua", then belonging to the curato of San Pedro Conguaco in the mayoralty of Escuintla.<ref name="Hist"/>

Administratively, Jalpatagua underwent several jurisdictional changes during the 19th century. It became part of the Jutiapa department in 1852, which later lost that status in 1883. It was finally re-established as a municipality of Jutiapa Department by government decree on 30 January 1886.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Jalpatagua|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Jalpatagua municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-jalpatagua-jutiapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Jalpatagua is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|175.8|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Jalpatagua (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2212__jalpatagua/|access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> It is situated about {{cvt|102|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|37|km}} from the departmental seat of [[Jutiapa, Jutiapa|Jutiapa]]. It borders the municipalities of [[San José Acatempa]] and [[Quesada, Guatemala|Quesada]] to the north, [[Oratorio]] and [[Moyuta]] to the west, and [[Conguaco]] and Moyuta to the east. It shares land border with [[El Salvador]] to the south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Jalpatagua, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/geografia-del-municipio-de-jalpatagua-jutiapa/|trans-title=Geography of Jalpatagua municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|570.18|m}} above sea level, Jalpatagua has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The district has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|27.13|
C}}. Jalpatagua typically receives about {{cvt|84.58|mm}} of and has 133.47 rainy days (36.57% of the time) on average annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/jalpatagua|title=Jalpatagua|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Jalpatagua had an estimated population of 31,858 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 15,910 males and 15,948 females. About 28.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.8% was over the age of 65 years. About 35.8% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (64.2%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (83.3%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 85.6%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (89.6%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (8.3%) forming a significant minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.7%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
