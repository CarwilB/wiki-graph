'''Fortaleza de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southwest of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 4,437 people living in a total area of {{convert|218|km2|0|abbr=on}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/fortaleza-de-minas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the meso-region of Sul e Sudoeste de Minas and to the micro-region of [[Passos, Minas Gerais|Passos]]. It became a municipality in 1963.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of Fortaleza de Minas is located at an elevation of 856 meters just south of the regional center of [[Passos, Minas Gerais|Passos]]. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Pratápolis]] (N and NW), [[Itaú de Minas]] (N), [[Passos, Minas Gerais|Passos]] (NE and E), [[Jacuí]] and [[Bom Jesus da Penha]] (S and SE), [[São Sebastião do Paraíso]] (W).<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20010708001639/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/passos/ Citybrazil]</ref>

==Tourism==
Despite the small area there is a varied landscape with rugged hills, streams, and waterfalls. There are three modest hotels and four restaurants. The São João River is the main river. It has waterfalls, and large rock formations and is used for canoeing and kayaking. Cachoeira do Val is a beautiful waterfall located {{convert|4|km|0|abbr=on}} from the town.<ref>[http://www.fortalezademinas.mg.gov.br/art.asp?art=3 City site]{{Dead link|date=October 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> See [http://www.fortalezademinas.mg.gov.br/art.asp?art=18 Galeria de fotos]{{Dead link|date=October 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} for photos.

==Economic activities==
Mining, services, small industries, and agriculture are the main economic activities. The economy is based on mineral extraction (nickel) and the sulfuric acid industry. There is one large enterprise, Mineração Serra da Fortaleza, of the Votorantim group. In 2005 501 workers were employed in this industry. The GDP in 2005 was approximately R$112 million, with 5 million reais from taxes, 25 million reais from services, 72 million reais from industry, and 9 million reais from agriculture. There were 521 rural producers on 18,000 hectares of land. Approximately 1,800 persons were occupied in agriculture. The main crops are coffee, bananas, sugarcane, rice, beans, and corn. There were 11,000 head of cattle, of which 3,000 were milk cows (2006).<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref><ref>[http://www.fortalezademinas.mg.gov.br/art.asp?art=3 City site]{{Dead link|date=October 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

There is 1 bank (2007) In the vehicle fleet there were 616 automobiles, 48 trucks, 53 pickup trucks, 4 buses, and 212 motorcycles (2007).<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were three health clinics (2005). Educational needs of 850 students were attended to by two primary schools, one middle school, and one pre-primary school.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.765 (2000)
*State ranking: 191 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 1,458 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*Literacy rate: 85%
*Life expectancy: 74 (average of males and females)<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of R$192.00 was below the state average of R$276.00 and below the national average of R$297.00. [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with R$435.00. The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with R$73.00.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>
