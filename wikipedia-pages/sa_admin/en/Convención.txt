'''Convención''' is a [[Colombian municipality]] located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[North Santander]]. It is considered the largest producer of panela in the department. Account, according to figures estimated by the DANE, with a population of 16,605. It is located at 1,076 meters above sea level and has a temperate climate.

==History==
The first conqueror to set foot in these territories was the German [[Ambrosio Alfinger]] in 1530, who perished in combat with the aborigines. Later, Spaniards [[Pedro de Ursúa|Pedro de Ursua]] and Ortón Velázquez founded many settlements in what is now Norte de Santander.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://enciclopedia.banrepcultural.org/index.php?title=Personajes_de_la_Conquista_a_Am%C3%A9rica|title=Personajes de la Conquista a América|work=Banrepcultura (In Spanish)|accessdate=2018-01-10|archive-date=2018-01-24|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180124053223/http://enciclopedia.banrepcultural.org/index.php?title=Personajes_de_la_Conquista_a_Am%C3%A9rica|url-status=dead}}</ref>
