''' Ituzaingó Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 30,565 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|8,613|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Ituzaingó, Corrientes|Ituzaingó]], which is located around {{cvt|1,070|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
