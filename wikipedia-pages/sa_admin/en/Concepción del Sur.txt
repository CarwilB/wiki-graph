'''Concepción del Sur''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|64.74|km2}} and had a population of 5,486 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
In the population census of 1887, Concepción del Sur appeared as a village in Santa Bárbara. The municipality was created on 27 October 1900, when it was carved out of the municipality of [[Ceguaca]].<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Concepción del Sur, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Departamentos/16%20Santa%20Barbara/Concepci%C3%B3n%20del%20Sur.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Concepción del Sur is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Concepción del Sur|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-concepcion-sur/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Santa Bárbara, Santa Bárbara|Santa Bárbara]] to the north, [[San Francisco de Ojuera]] to the south, [[San Pedro Zacapa]] to the east, and [[Ceguaca]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|64.74|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1608__concepci%C3%B3n_del_sur/|title=Concepción del Sur|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|784.3|m}} above sea level, Concepción del Sur has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality's yearly temperature is {{cvt|21.23|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|290.93|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/concepcion-del-sur|title=Concepción del Sur, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Concepción del Sur had a total population of 5,486 inhabitants, of whom 2,819 (51.4%) were men and 2,667 (48.6%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 2,051 individuals (37.4%) were aged 0–14 years, 3,179 individuals (58.0%) were aged 15–64 (consisting of 2,684 individuals aged 15–49 and 495 individuals aged 50–64), and 255 individuals (4.6%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 20.8 years and the mean age was 25.1 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 25.7%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 1,946 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.6 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
