'''Lavalle Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the northeast of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 32,000&nbsp;inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|10,212|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Villa Tulumaya]], which is located around {{cvt|1,095|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].
