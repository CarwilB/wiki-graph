The '''Jiménez Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Lara (state)|Lara]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 97,524.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Quíbor]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Jiménez Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Lara.zip {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Jiménez Municipality, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 100,997 (up from 97,524 in 2007).  This amounts to 5.6% of the state's population.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ine.gov.ve/index.php?option=com_content&view=category&id=95&Itemid=26|title=Censos de población y vivienda|access-date=2019-02-08|archive-date=2015-03-16|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150316050254/http://www.ine.gov.ve/index.php?option=com_content&view=category&id=95&Itemid=26|url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|126.98|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/lara/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Jiménez Municipality is Luis Alberto Plaza Paz, elected on October 31, 2004, with 73% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Jimenez | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D11%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229230714/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/11/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D11%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-16 | url-status=live }}</ref>   He replaced Manuel Diaz shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 Directory of elected mayors | accessdate=2008-03-15 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=2006-10-02}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into eight [[parish]]es; Juan Bautista Rodríguez, Cuara, Diego de Lozada, Paraíso de San José, San Miguel, Tintorero, José Bernardo Dorante, and Coronel.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
