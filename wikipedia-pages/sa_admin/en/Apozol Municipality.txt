'''Apozol''' is one of the 58 [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipalities]] in the state of [[Zacatecas]], [[Mexico]].

It is located in the southern part of the state of Zacatecas and is bounded by the municipalities of [[Municipality of Jalpa|Jalpa]], [[Municipality of Juchipila|Juchipila]], [[Municipality of Nochistlán de Mejía|Nochistlán de Mejía]], [[Municipality of Tepechitlán|Tepechitlán]] and [[Municipality of Teúl de González Ortega|Teúl de González Ortega]].

The municipality covers a total surface area of {{convert|283|km2|sqmi|sp=us}}<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32001a.htm "Apozol", Encyclopedia de Municipios ] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070523172016/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/zacatecas/municipios/32001a.htm |date=May 23, 2007 }}</ref>. The 2005 census reported a population of 5,898.<ref name=pop2005>[http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070328000000/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |date=March 28, 2007 }} INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática</ref>
The municipality accounts for 0.68% of the area of the state of [[Zacatecas]].
