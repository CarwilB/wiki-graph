'''Cansahcab Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: "Place of the four white caves") is a small (146.90&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>) [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] located roughly 50&nbsp;km southeast of the state capital of Mérida.<ref name=municipios />

==History==
Before the arrival of the Spanish, the area belonged to the chieftainship of Ah Kin Chel. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system. One of the encomenderas was Bantulia Sosa de Rivero, who in 1700 had responsibility entrusted for the care of 250 indigenous inhabitants.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the [[Izamal Municipality]]. In 1913 it was designated as its own municipality.<ref name=municipios />

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of ecology, public services and public security.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Cansahcab|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=5 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Local festivals==
Every year from 14 – 16 September, Cansahcab holds an annual fair or "fiesta del pueblo" during which an impromptu bullfighting ring is set up to host much of the festivities which include a [[charreada|charrería]], [[bullfighting]] and [[Folk dance|folkloric dancing]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Independence day|url=https://carlosrosado.blogspot.com/2007/09/independence-day-fried-bull-entrails.html}}</ref> On 4 October, a feast is held in honor of St. Francis of Assisi, patron saint of the town.<ref name=municipios />

==Notable people==
Notable locals include General [[Teodosio Canto]] who was governor of the state of Yucatán from 1882-1886.<ref name="municipios"/>
