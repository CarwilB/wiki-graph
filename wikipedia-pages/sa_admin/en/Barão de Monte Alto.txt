'''Barão de Monte Alto''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population in 2020 was estimated to be 5,354 people living in a total area of {{cvt|199.1|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/barao-de-monte-alto/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of [[Muriaé]].
