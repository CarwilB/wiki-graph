'''Huayllabamba District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Urubamba Province|Urubamba]] in [[Peru]]. The town of [[Huayllabamba, Urubamba|Huayllabamba]] is the capital of the district.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
In [[Quechuan languages|Quechua]], "huaylla" means grassy and "bamba" means plain, e.g. "grassy plain." "Bamba" is a variation of the word "pampa."
