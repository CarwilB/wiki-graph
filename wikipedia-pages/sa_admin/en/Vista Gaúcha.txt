'''Vista Gaúcha''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

Vista Gaúcha is located in the northeast of the province, 480&nbsp;km from the capital, [[Porto Alegre]].

The population of the municipality was 2,855 people, according to the IBGE Demographic estimate of 2020.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/vista-gaucha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Its area is 90.02&nbsp;km², comprising .033% of the province, 0.0157% of the region, and  0.001% of the country.

Its IDH is .0784 per the Atlas de Desenvolvimento Humano/PNUD (2000).
