'''Ñuflo de Chávez'''<ref>[[Ñuflo de Chaves]] (1518-1568) was a Spanish [[conquistador]]. He is best known for founding the city of [[Santa Cruz de la Sierra]].</ref> is one of the fifteen provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]] and is situated in the northern and central parts of the department. The name of the province honors the [[conquistador]] [[Ñuflo de Chaves]] (1518–1556) who founded the city of [[Santa Cruz de la Sierra]]. Its capital is  [[Concepción (Santa Cruz)|Concepción]]. The province was created by law of September 16, 1915, during  the presidency of [[Ismael Montes Gamboa|Ismael Montes]]. Originally it was part of the [[Chiquitos Province]].<ref name="Ñuflo de Chávez">[http://www.santacruz.gob.bo/turistica/provincia/nuflodechavez/index.php Official site of Ñuflo de Chávez Province] (Spanish)</ref>

== Location ==
Ñuflo de Chávez Province is located between 13° 45' and 17° 30' [[Latitude|South]] and between 61° 30' and 63° 25' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over a length of 520&nbsp;km from north to south, and up to 250&nbsp;km from west to east.

The province is situated in the Bolivian lowlands and borders [[Beni Department]] in the north, [[Guarayos Province]] in the north-west, [[Obispo Santistevan Province]] and [[Ignacio Warnes Province]] in the south-west, [[Andrés Ibáñez Province]] and [[Chiquitos Province]] in the south, and [[José Miguel de Velasco Province]] in the east.

The population of the province has increased by more than 100% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 61,008 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 93,997 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 111,813 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística]</ref>
*2024: 129,674 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Ñuflo de Chávez (Province, Bolivia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/santa_cruz/0711__%C3%B1uflo_de_ch%C3%A1vez/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

48.3% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

The [[literacy]] rate of the province is 94.3%. ''(2012)''

87.3% of the population have no access to electricity, 50.4% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

79.5% of the population are Catholics, 17.2% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

In 1992 91.6% of the population spoke [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 30.1% [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 2.0% [[Aymara language|Aymara]] and 1.2 spoke [[Guarani language|Guaraní]]. During the 2001 census Spanish was spoken by 79,377 people, Quechua  by 20,763, Aymara by 1,362, Guarani by 1,002, other native languages by 3,619 and foreign languages by 5,617 people. == Economy ==
The principal economic activities are devoted to [[animal husbandry]], [[dairy farming]], [[forestry]] and [[tourism]]. Another important occupation is [[handicraft]] like the fabrication of [[hammock]]s, [[textile]]s, hats, bags and baskets.<ref name = "Ñuflo de Chávez" />
