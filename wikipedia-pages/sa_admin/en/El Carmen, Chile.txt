'''El Carmen''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of towns in Chile|town]] in [[Diguillín Province]], [[Ñuble Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], El Carmen spans an area of {{convert|664.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 12,845 inhabitants (6,567 men and 6,278 women). Of these, 4,426 (34.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 8,419 (65.5%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 9.3% (1316 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, El Carmen is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Juan Diaz González ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], El Carmen is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Carlos Abel Jarpa  ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) and Rosauro Martínez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 41st electoral district, together with [[Chillán]], [[Coihueco]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[San Ignacio, Chile|San Ignacio]], [[Pemuco]], [[Yungay, Chile|Yungay]] and [[Chillán Viejo]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Victor Pérez Varela ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mariano Ruiz-Esquide Jara ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).
