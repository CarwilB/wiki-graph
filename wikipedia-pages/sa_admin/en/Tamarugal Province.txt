'''Tamarugal Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Tamarugal}}) is one of two [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] in the northern [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Tarapacá Region|Tarapacá]], the other being the [[Iquique Province]]. Tamarugal Province spans most of the breadth of Chile, between the [[Pacific Ocean]] and [[Bolivia]], where it borders on [[Oruro Department]] and [[Potosí Department]]. The capital is the city of [[Pozo Almonte]].

==Name==
The province is named after [[Pampa del Tamarugal]].
Spanish name:
* Provincia de Tamarugal: Used by the government of the province.
* Provincia del Tamarugal: Used by the government of Chile.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.minjusticia.gob.cl/n7361_02-06-2015.html |title=Tarapacá: Plaza de Justicia atendió a usuarios de la Provincia del Tamarugal - Ministerio de Justicia - Gobierno de Chile |website=www.minjusticia.gob.cl |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151016213148/http://www.minjusticia.gob.cl/n7361_02-06-2015.html |archive-date=2015-10-16}} </ref> Derived from the name El Tamarugal.
* Provincia El Tamarugal: Derived from the name El Tamarugal.

==Geography and demography==
According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|39390.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and had a population of 22,531 inhabitants (14,175 men and 8,356 women). It is the seventh largest and ninth least populated province in the country with a population density of {{convert|0.6|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 63.5% (8,748 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
The province is administered by a presidentially appointed delegate. [[Luz Eliana González Millas]] was appointed by [[President of Chile|president]] [[Gabriel Boric]].

==Communes==
As a [[province]], Tamarugal is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], which is further subdivided into five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'').
