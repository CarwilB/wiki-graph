'''Nocupétaro''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|75|km}} south of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Nocupétaro is located in the [[Tierra Caliente (Mexico)|Tierra Caliente]] region of Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|500|and(-)|1800|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Madero, Michoacán|Madero]] to the north, [[Carácuaro]] to the east, [[Turicato]] to the southwest, and [[Tacámbaro]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16057.pdf |title=Nocupétaro, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|547.184|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.93% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Nocupétaro consists of tropical forest (59%), grassland (19%) and temperate forest (14%). Another 7% of the land is used for agriculture and 0.6% consists of urban areas.<ref name=compendium/> Nocupétaro is located in the drainage basin of the [[Tacámbaro River]], a tributary of the [[Balsas River]].<ref name=compendium/>
===Climate===
Most of Nocupétaro has a [[tropical wet and dry climate]] with rain in the summer, while the southernmost portion of the municipality has a [[semi-arid climate]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|20|and(-)|28|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1100|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Nocupétaro has been inhabited since pre-Hispanic times. The name is of [[Chichimeca]] origin and means "place in the valley". The area was evangelized by {{ill|Juan Bautista Moya|es}}, the "Apostle of the Tierra Caliente", in the 16th century.<ref name=encyclopedia/> [[José María Morelos]] served as parish priest of Carácuaro and Nocupétaro from 1799 until 1810, when he joined the rebels in the [[Mexican War of Independence]].<ref>{{cite journal |last=Gutiérrez Escudero |first=Antonio |year=2008 |title=José María Morelos: el siervo de la nación mexicana (I) |journal=Araucaria |language=es |url=https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/2663139.pdf |volume=10 |issue=20 |pages=235–247 |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref> In 1822, Nocupétaro was made part of the ''partido'' of Tacámbaro. It became an independent municipality in 1910.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=160–161 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4 |archive-date=2019-04-12 |access-date=2020-12-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190412021950/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Nocupétaro comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is J. Félix González Gómez.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Nocupétaro recorded a population of 7799 inhabitants living in 1858 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=057 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Nocupétaro |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=28 December 2020 |archive-date=26 August 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210826222055/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=057 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 8195 inhabitants in Nocupétaro.<ref name=survey/>

There are 142 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Nocupétaro de Morelos is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 3260 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
Nocupétaro is one of the poorest municipalities in Michoacán. As of 2017, half of its population lived in extreme poverty.<ref>{{cite news |last=Morales Pérez |first=Uriel |date=18 April 2017 |url=https://www.quadratin.com.mx/politica/en-pobreza-extrema-50-habitantes-nocupetaro/ |title=En pobreza extrema, 50% de habitantes de Nocupétaro |language=es |website=Quadratín |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref> Agriculture is the main economic activity in the municipality. The main crops are corn, sorghum, beans and squash, and livestock such as goats, pigs, cattle and poultry are raised.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=Nocupétaro Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/4a-3319.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=11 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 4a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=22 April 2019 |access-date=28 December 2020}}</ref>
