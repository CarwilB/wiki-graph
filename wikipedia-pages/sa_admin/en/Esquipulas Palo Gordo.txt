'''Esquipulas Palo Gordo''' ({{IPA|es|eskiˈpulas ˈpalo ˈɣoɾðo}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]].  The town was founded on 24 December 1826 year. In the 1920s it was merged with [[San Marcos, San Marcos|San Marcos]], but in 1948 it recovered its autonomy again.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pbase.com/m_escalante_herrera/palo_gordo|title=Breve información del municipio de Esquipulas Palo Gordo|year=2007|access-date=9 September 2015|location=Guatemala|website=Pbase.com|author=Escalante Herrera, Marco Antonio|language=es}}</ref>

== Natural resources ==

[[File:Esquipulas Palo Gordo 02.jpg|thumb|200px|Esquipulas Palo Gordo in 2009.]]

Natural resources in the municipality are mainly cattle, forestry and agriculture.  The main agricultural products are: sugar cane, corn, wheat, potato, and beans; the land is flat and fertile.

==Tourism==
{{See also|Tapachula International Airport|La Aurora International Airport}}

Esquipulas Palo Gordo has several natural attractions; among them is Ixtagel hill and the Quetzal refuge in Fraternidad village, along with a large forest area that covers approximately 50% of the municipal area and has extensive fauna variety.

The municipality celebrates its annual fair in honor of the Black Christ of [[Esquipulas]] between January 12 and 17.
