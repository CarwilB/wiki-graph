'''Vallejuelo''' is a [[town]] in the [[San Juan Province, Dominican Republic|San Juan]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]].

==History==
{{unreferenced section|date=May 2023}}
The first settlers resided in Rio Arriba of the south in 1880. These people had come from the Cibao region. Then, in 1912, several families of Hondo Valle and El Cercado settled in Vallejuelo and adjacent areas.<ref>{{Cite web |title=ayuntamientovallejuelo.gob.do |url=https://ayuntamientovallejuelo.gob.do/historia/ |access-date=September 8, 2025 |website=Ayuntamiento Municipal Vallejuelo}}</ref>
