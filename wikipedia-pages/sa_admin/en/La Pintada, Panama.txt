'''La Pintada ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] and town in [[La Pintada District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It is the seat of La Pintada District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|84.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,882 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|45.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,515; its population as of 2000 was 3,733.<ref name=inec/>

The town is located in the hills {{convert|13|km|abbr=on}} northwest of [[Penonomé District|Penonomé]]. The Coclé del Sur River runs along the edge of the town. Two kilometers from the town are located a variety of pre-Columbian petroglyphs.<ref name=moon/>

The factory for the cigar brand Cigarros Joyas de Panamá is located in the town.  There is also a small artisans' market that sells Panama hats and various local crafts.<ref name=moon>Friar, William. ''Panama''. [[Moon Publications]] (2008), pp 238-39. {{ISBN|1-59880-085-X}}.</ref>
