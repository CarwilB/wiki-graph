'''Peñalolén''' ([[Mapudungun language|Mapudungun]] "fraternal meeting place") is a [[Chile]]an [[communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. It was founded on 15 November 1984.

It is one of the most segregated areas of Santiago with Pre-Cordillera areas such as Las Pircas, Casablanca, and Penalolen Alto having a large upper-middle to high income population similar to [[Las Condes]] or [[Vitacura]]. While the lower areas are more working-class. 

==History==
The commune was founded on 15 November 1984. In 2019, Chilean police arrested a local family, accusing them of drug trafficking. The Peñalolén organization was reported to be one of the largest in the Santiago area, operating throughout the city.<ref>Archived at [https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211205/r5UGNWDrdtM Ghostarchive]{{cbignore}} and the [https://web.archive.org/web/20191030120317/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5UGNWDrdtM&gl=US&hl=en Wayback Machine]{{cbignore}}: {{cite web| url = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5UGNWDrdtM| title = La caída de las "narcoreinas", la banda más grande y peligrosa de la zona oriente de Santiago | website=[[YouTube]]}}{{cbignore}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Peñalolén spans an area of {{convert|54.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 216,060 inhabitants (105,528 men and 110,532 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 20.2% (36,279 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
{{Moresources|section|date=July 2023}}
[[File:Escudo de Peñalolén.svg|thumb|Peñalolén Coat of Arms|235x235px]]
As a commune, Peñalolén is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (mayor) who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is Miguel Concha Manso ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]]). The communal council has the following members:
* Sebastián Villouta Seguel ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Valentina Escobar Petit ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Ignacio Sánchez Pino ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Carla Caro Figueroa ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Begoña Torres Kurth ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Cristián Jofré Delgado ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Iván Tapia Peñaloza ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Daniela López Awad ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Claudio Hernández Olivares ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Juan Cantuarias Cantuarias ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])

===Mayors===
Since 1984, Peñalolén has had six mayors:
*María Angélica Cristi (1984–1989, appointed by Pinochet)
*Carlos Alarcón (1989–1993, appointed by Aylwin)
*Carlos Echeverría (1993–1996)
*Carlos Alarcón (1997–2004)
*[[Claudio Orrego]] (2004-2012)
*Carolina Leitao (2012–2024)
*Miguel Concha Manso (2024-)

===Parliamentary representation===

Peñalolén belongs to Electoral District No. 11 together with the communes of [[Lo Barnechea]], [[Las Condes]], [[La Reina]] and [[Vitacura]] and to the VII Senatorial Circunscription ([[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]).

It is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] of the [[National Congress of Chile|National Congress]] for the period 2022-2026 by the following deputies:
