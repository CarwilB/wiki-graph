'''Ayutla''' ({{IPA|es|aˈʝutla}}) is a [[municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is situated along the [[Suchiate River]] natural border with [[Mexico]] in the southern part of the department. The municipality center is Ciudad Tecún Umán.  There is a combined [[List of road–rail bridges|road and rail bridge known]] as ''[[Puente Rodolfo Robles]]'' linking Ayutla to [[Ciudad Hidalgo, Chiapas|Ciudad Hidalgo]] in [[Chiapas]], [[Mexico]].<ref>[http://wikimapia.org/4346861/es/Puente-Dr-Rodolfo-Robles Dr. Rodolpho Robles Bridge]</ref>

There exists a [[Rail transport in Guatemala#Railway links with adjacent countries|railway]] which was rebuilt in 2019.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.puertochiapas.com.mx/news/362-railway-between-guatemala-and-mexico-connectivity-across-puerto-chiapas|title = Railway between Guatemala and Mexico, connectivity across Puerto Chiapas}}</ref>

In December 2019, the [[United States Department of the Treasury|U.S. Treasury Department]] sanctioned Ayutla's mayor, Erick Zúñiga, under the [[Foreign Narcotics Kingpin Designation Act]], alleging Zúñiga to be "a major drug trafficker," while also alleging that Zúñiga controls a drug trafficking organization and "supplies cocaine to Mexico's [[Sinaloa Cartel]]."<ref>{{cite web|url=https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sm863|title=Treasury Sanctions Guatemalan Mayor and His Drug Trafficking Organization|website= home.treasury.gov|date=December 19, 2019|access-date= December 21, 2019}}</ref>
