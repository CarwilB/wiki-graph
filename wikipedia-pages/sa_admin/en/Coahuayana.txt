'''Coahuayana''' (formally: '''Coahuayana de Hidalgo''') is a city in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It serves as the municipal seat for the surrounding municipality of the same name, which is located on the Pacific Ocean coast and is the westernmost municipality in Michoacán. 

The municipality has an area of 362.34 square kilometres (0.61% of the surface of the state)<ref name="Michoacán">[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan Michoacán] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517162648/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan |date=17 May 2011 }} ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Retrieved on 8 October 2007</ref> and is bordered to the north by the municipality of [[Chinicuila]], to the south by [[Aquila, Michoacán|Aquila]] and the [[Pacific Ocean]], and to the west and northwest by the state of [[Colima]]. The municipality reported a population of 17,022 in the 2020 [[INEGI]] census,<ref>{{cite web |title=Coahuayana: Ficha Municipal de Información 2022 |url=https://cpladem.michoacan.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Coahuayana.pdf |publisher=Michoacán State Government |access-date=7 December 2025}}</ref> up from 11,632 in 2005.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=8 October 2007 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=6 April 2013 |df= }}</ref>  

Coahuayana is a word of [[Nahuatl]] origin that means "Place Where Trees and [[Cucurbita maxima|Squash]] are Abundant".<ref name="Michoacán"/>
