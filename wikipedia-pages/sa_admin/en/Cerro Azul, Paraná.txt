The [[municipality]] contains part of the {{convert|337|ha|adj=on}} [[Campinhos State Park]], created in 1960.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual de Campinhos – IAP}}
 |title=Parque Estadual de Campinhos|publisher=IAP: Instituto Ambiental do Paraná
 |url=http://www.iap.pr.gov.br/modules/ucps/aviso.php?codigo=41&codigo_cat=0|access-date=2017-02-05}}</ref>

Cerro Azul is officially recognized as the National Capital of the [[Mandarin orange|Ponkan Tangerine]] (Capital Nacional da Ponkâ) under Federal Law No. 14,608/2023, due to its significant role in the country's citrus production.<ref>{{cite web |author=Senado Federal |title=Cerro Azul vira capital nacional da mexerica ponkan |url=https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2023/06/21/cerro-azul-vira-capital-nacional-da-mexerica-ponkan |website=Senado Notícias |date=2023-06-21 |access-date=2026-06-07 |language=pt}}</ref>

==History==
Cerro Azul was discovered in 1860 and became a municipality on 27 December 1897.
