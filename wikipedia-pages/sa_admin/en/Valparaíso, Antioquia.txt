'''Valparaíso'''  ({{IPAc-en|lang|ˌ|v|ɑː|l|p|ɑː|r|ɑː|ˈ|iː|s|oʊ}} {{respell|VAHL|pah|rah|EE|soh}};<ref name="Cohen">{{cite encyclopedia|entry=Valparaiso|encyclopedia=The Columbia Gazetteer of the World |edition=Second |volume=3: P to Z |editor-first=Saul B. |editor-last=Cohen |publisher=Columbia University Press |location= New York |date=2008 |page=[https://books.google.com/books?id=C6PLxz8EMk0C&newbks=1&newbks_redir=0&lpg=PR3&pg=PA4062#v=onepage&q&f=false 4062] |isbn=9780231145541}}</ref> {{IPA|es|balpaɾaˈiso}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].
