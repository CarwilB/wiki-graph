'''Tlapacoyan''' is a city and municipality in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about three hours by automobile away from the state capital [[Xalapa]].

In 2020 Tlapacoyan registered  61,377 inhabitants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=División municipal. Veracruz |url=https://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/ver/territorio/div_municipal.aspx |access-date=2024-02-18 |website=cuentame.inegi.org.mx}}</ref>

==Tourism==

===Places to visit===

[[Filobobos River|Filobobos]] is a nearby natural reserve area for exploring nature. The river and rapids lead through a series of caves and beautiful interior waterfalls, and locals at the river entrances arrange short or longer guided rafting adventures through the caves.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011-06-27 |title=Historia De Tlapacoyan {{!}} Turismo En Veracruz |url=https://www.turismoenveracruz.mx/2011/06/historia-de-tlapacoyan/,%20https://www.turismoenveracruz.mx/2011/06/historia-de-tlapacoyan/ |access-date=2024-02-18 |language=es}}</ref>
