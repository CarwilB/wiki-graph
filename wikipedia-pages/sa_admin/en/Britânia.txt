'''Britânia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northwestern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].  The population was 5,797 (2020) in a total area of 1461.2 km<sup>2</sup>.

==Municipal boundaries==
Municipal boundaries:
*North: [[Aruanã]] 
*South: [[Jussara]] 
*East: [[Itapirapuã]] and [[Aruanã]] 
*West: the state of [[Mato Grosso]]

==Highway connections==
A paved highway connects Britânia with [[Santa Fé de Goiás]] and [[Jussara]]. Connections with [[Goiânia]] are made by highways GO-060 / [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]] / [[Claudinápolis]] / GO-326 / [[Anicuns]] / [[Sanclerlândia]] / [[Novo Brasil]] / GO-324 / [[Jussara]] / BR-070 / [[Nova Trindade]] / GO-173.

The main rivers are the Vermelho and the [[Araguaia River|Araguaia]].  There are also several lakes, like the Tigrinho, Açará and the most important for tourist purposes, the Lago dos Tigres, which is used for fishing, water sports and swimming.

==Economic facts==
The economy is based on cattle raising, agriculture, commerce (58 units), and some small transformation industries (11 units). Britânia had a herd of 155,400 in 2006, with 90% being the [[Nelore]] breed.  The land is divided into large properties, which use little labor.  Other important agricultural products are rice, sesame seeds, manioc, and corn.

*Financial institutions in 2004: 01—Bradesco S.A.

==History==
In 1953 Paulo Carlos Schmidt de Vasconcelos, from [[São Paulo]], bought lands from the government and staked out lots in an endeavour called "Loteamento Lago dos Tigres" next to the lake of the same name.  In 1959 another Paulista, Írio Spinar, bought the land and began to sell lots to farmers, who mainly came from São Paulo or [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]].  The settlement grew and in 1958 it became a district of [[Jussara]], later to become a municipality in 1963.
