'''Carlos Tejedor Partido''' is a  [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north-west of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of  about 11,500 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|3933|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Carlos Tejedor, Buenos Aires|Carlos Tejedor]].
