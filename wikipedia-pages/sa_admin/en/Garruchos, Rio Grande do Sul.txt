'''Garruchos''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,886 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|799.85|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/16AP Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 69 m by the Uruguay River. It is located 627&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].

The town is situated by the [[Uruguay River]] which forms the border with [[Argentina]] to the west; an Argentine settlement with the same name, [[Garruchos, Corrientes|Garruchos]], is on the other side.

The municipality would be partially flooded by the proposed [[Garabí Dam]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos}}|language=pt |title=Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos|publisher=Eletrobras CGTEE|date=7 April 2014
