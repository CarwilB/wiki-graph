'''Brandsen''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in northeastern [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. In 2001 the city and district had a population of about 22,500 people. It covers an area of {{convert|1126|sqkm|sqmi|abbr=on}}. The capital is also known as [[Brandsen]].

==History==
Founded on November 21, 1875 it lies about {{convert|70|km|mi|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]]. The area was named after [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[colonel]], [[Federico de Brandsen]], who was killed in the [[battle of Ituzaingó]] in the [[Cisplatine War]].
