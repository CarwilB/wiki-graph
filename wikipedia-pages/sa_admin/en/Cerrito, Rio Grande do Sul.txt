'''Cerrito''' is a Brazilian municipality in the southern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 6,047 (2020 est.) in an area of 451.70&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6L0 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The municipality was formed in 1997 from part of the municipality [[Pedro Osório]].<ref>[http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=430512&search=rio-grande-do-sul|cerrito|infograficos:-historico IBGE, History of Cerrito]</ref>
