'''Narganá''' or '''Yandup''' in the [[Guna language]] is one of the islands belonging to the [[Guna Yala]], an autonomous territory or comarca in [[Panama]]. The culture in Nargana is influenced heavily by Panama and most people there are bilingual speaking both Spanish and the Kuna language. The island has an area of 5.5 [[hectare]]s and is completely overbuilt, and could be walked around in less than 10 minutes. There is a small hospital and school on the island. The nearest populated island, [[Corazón de Jesús (island)|Corazón de Jesús]], is connected to Nargana by a concrete bridge. Both islands are among the most westernized of the [[San Blas Islands]].
