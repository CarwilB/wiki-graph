'''Fram''' (or '''Colonia Fram''') is a city & one of the 30 districts in the [[Itapúa Department]] of [[Paraguay]].

==History==

Fram was founded on March 20, 1927, by [[Norwegians|Norwegian]] Pedro Cristophensen, along with Mateo Sanchez, a native of [[Carmen del Paraná]]. The old name of the place was ''Apereá''.<br />
The residents of Fram are of Slavic origin ([[Polish people|Poles]], [[Russians]] and [[Ukrainians]]), [[Germans|German]] and [[Japanese people|Japanese]].

==Toponymy==
Fram is a [[Norwegian language|Norwegian]] word meaning "Forward" and is the name of the famous ship that brought the Norwegian explorer Fridtjof Nansen and his crew to the Arctic ice in 1893.<br />
Fram also is known as "The Capital of Wheat", a nickname earned by being an area of wheat production, since its founding.

==Demography==

According to the ''Dirección General de Estadísticas, Encuestas y Censos'' the district has a total population of 6,923 inhabitants, of which 3.414 live in its urban area.

==Location==
The city of Fram is situated 46&nbsp;km north-west of the [[Encarnación, Paraguay|Encarnación]], 22&nbsp;km north-east of Carmen del Paraná.<br />
You can get there taking Route Number 1 (taking a detour in [[Coronel Bogado]]) or Route Number 6 (taking a detour in [[Capitán Miranda]]).

==Economy==
The district have experienced significant progress in recent years due to the participation of its citizens in solving community problems. It highlights in particular their [[social insurance]] system of the municipality that provides health coverage to all its inhabitants.<br />
The district of Fram has become a center of national development, an example of community development and self-management. There is also a harmony between the people and the business community.<br />
Its main economic items are farming, agribusiness, livestock, pigs and poultry. A beautiful [[Eastern Orthodox Church|Orthodox church]] completes the landscape of this progressive city.
