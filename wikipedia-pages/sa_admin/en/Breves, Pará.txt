'''Breves''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]], on the island of [[Marajó]]. Its population {{As of|2025|lc=y}} is estimated to be 116,058 people.<ref name="IBGE" /> The area of the municipality is 9,550.454&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Furos de Breves''.

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=APA Arquipélago do Marajó|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/951|access-date=2016-06-27}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|93746|ha|adj=on}} [[Mapuá Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Mapuá – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX Mapuá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4249|access-date=2016-06-30}}</ref>

The city is served by [[Breves Airport]].

In August 1659, the [[Treaty of the Mapuá]] was signed at the [[Mapuá River]] by [[Kingdom of Portugal|Portuguese]] [[Jesuits]] led by [[António Vieira]] with various indigenous peoples who inhabited the [[Marajó Archipelago]].<ref name="steward">{{cite book |last=Steward |first=J.H. |date=1948 |title=Handbook of South American Indians, Volume 3: The Tropical Forest Tribes |url=https://etnolinguistica.wdfiles.com/local--files/hsai:vol3p193-198/vol3p193-198_turiwara.pdf |location= |publisher=Smithsonian Institution |page=194–196 |isbn= |access-date=}}</ref> The ceremony took place near a locality called Vila Amélia ({{Coord|1|7|S|50|18|W|display=inline}}). In the present day, there is still an indigenous cemetery at the spot where the treaty was signed.<ref name="costa">{{cite journal |last1=Costa |first1=E.M. |date=2016 |title=Dos Indígenas, Os Artefatos: a História Dos Povos Indígenas Do Marajó, PA|url=https://1library.org/document/zx5r4eo4-dos-ind%C3%ADgenas-artefatos-hist%C3%B3ria-povos-ind%C3%ADgenas-arquip%C3%A9lago-maraj%C3%B3.html |journal=Revista de Estudos Linguísticos, Literários, Culturais e da Contemporaneidade |volume=18b |issue= |publisher= |pages= |doi= |access-date=}}</ref>

== Climate ==
The climate is [[Tropical rainforest climate|tropical rainforest]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Af''), although August to October is nearly dry enough to be a [[tropical monsoon climate]] (''Am''). Temperatures are hot with extremely humidity all year round, and rainfall ranges from moderate to very heavy.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://pt.climate-data.org/america-do-sul/brasil/para/breves-43947/|title=Clima Breves: Temperatura, Tempo e Dados climatológicos Breves - Climate-Data.org|website=pt.climate-data.org|access-date=2019-03-30}}</ref>
