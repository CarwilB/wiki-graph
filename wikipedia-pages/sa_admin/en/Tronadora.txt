'''Tronadora''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Tilarán (canton)|Tilarán]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> It is located on the west shore of [[Lake Arenal]]. It is connected by road to [[Tejona]] and [http://detilaran.com Tilarán] along Route 142.<ref>{{cite book|author=Baker, C.P.|title=Costa Rica|publisher=[[Dorling Kindersley]] [[Eye Witness Travel Guides]]|date=2005|pages=150}}</ref> The weather is changeable due to the proximity of the confluence of the Pacific coast weather pattern and the inland lake  weather pattern. It is drier and cooler than the Pacific coast due to the elevation of 614 meters (2014 feet).

== Geography ==
Tronadora has an area of {{cvt|142.28|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020}}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:600}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
