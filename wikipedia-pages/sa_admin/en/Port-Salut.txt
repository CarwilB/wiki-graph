'''Port-Salut''' ({{IPA|fr|pɔʁ saly|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Jérémy-Günther-Heinz Jähnick-port-salut.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Pòsali}}) is a coastal [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].

[[File:Port Salut Haïti.JPG|thumb|right|Beach at Port-Salut (2005)]]

Port-Salut is a popular destination for local Haitians, as well as tourists, due to the surrounding beaches.

Port-Salut is the hometown of Haiti's former [[President of Haiti|president]], [[Jean-Bertrand Aristide]], who was born there in 1953,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011-03-18 |title=Aristide retrouve Haïti après sept ans d'exil |url=https://www.lefigaro.fr/international/2011/03/18/01003-20110318ARTFIG00702-aristide-retrouve-haiti-apres-sept-ans-d-exil.php |access-date=2022-05-14 |website=LEFIGARO |language=fr}}</ref> and is also the hometown of former [[Prime minister of Haiti|prime minister]], [[Jean-Marie Cherestal]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Jean Marie Cherestal appelle les acteurs politiques au dialogue |url=https://lenouvelliste.com/public/article/87404/jean-marie-cherestal-appelle-les-acteurs-politiques-au-dialogue |access-date=2022-05-14 |website=Le Nouvelliste |language=en}}</ref>
