'''Abancay''' is one of seven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Apurímac Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Abancay]].

==Boundaries==
*North: [[Cusco Region]]
*East: provinces of [[Cotabambas Province|Cotabambas]] and [[Grau Province|Grau]]
*South: provinces of [[Aymaraes Province|Aymaraes]], [[Antabamba Province|Antabamba]] and [[Grau Province|Grau]]
*West: provinces of [[Andahuaylas Province|Andahuaylas]] and [[Aymaraes Province|Aymaraes]]

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (51.47%) learnt to speak in childhood, 48.06% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language and 0.21% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
