'''Sambú District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Comarca Emberá-Wounaan]] in [[Panama]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.atp.gob.pa/llegada-de-crucero-reactiva-la-economia-en-el-distrito-de-sambu/|title=Llegada de crucero reactiva la economía en el Distrito de Sambú|website=atp.gob.pa|publisher=Autoridad de Turismo de Panamá|date=3 October 2022|access-date=8 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital is [[Puerto Indio]].  The area of this ''district'' is 1296.4 sq. kilometers. It is on the western part of [[Comarca Emberá-Wounaan]]. The other side of the Comarca is [[Cémaco District]].

==Administrative divisions==
Sambú District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'': 

* [[Río Sábalo]]
* [[Jingurudo]]
