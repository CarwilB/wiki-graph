'''Canavieiras''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão The municipality contains part of the {{convert|100646|ha}} [[Canavieiras Extractive Reserve]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX de Canavieiras – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX de Canavieiras|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/584236|access-date=2016-09-14}}</ref>

The town is associated with a nearby gold mine, which has been operating since the 17th century. The mine is currently part of Yamana Gold's Jacobina operation. Traces of Uranium have been identified in the Gold and pyrite ore-bodies.<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Gross |first=W. H. |date=1968 |title=Evidence for a modified placer origin for auriferous conglomerates, Canavieiras Mine, Jacobina, Brazil |url=https://doi.org/10.2113/gsecongeo.63.3.271 |journal=Economic Geology (1968) 63 (3): 271–276 |volume=63 |issue=3 |pages=271-276 |doi=10.2113/gsecongeo.63.3.271 |url-access=subscription }}</ref>
