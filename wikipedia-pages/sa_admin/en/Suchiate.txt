'''Suchiate''' is a [[municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], on [[Mexico-Guatemala border|the border with]] [[Guatemala]]. It is in the [[Soconusco]] region, and is the southernmost municipality both in Chiapas and in all of Mexico. On the eastern edge of the municipality, flowing from north to south, is the [[Río Suchiate]], which forms the international border. Two communities within the municipality are [[border crossing]]s with Guatemala, [[Ciudad Hidalgo, Chiapas|Ciudad Hidalgo]] and [[Talismán, Chiapas|Talismán]]. Ciudad Hidalgo is the municipal seat and has a customs house at the border. Suchiate reported a 2010 census population of 35,056 persons. It has a land area of {{convert|606.1|km²|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 35,056.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Suchiate|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=087|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref>

The municipality had 180 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Ciudad Hidalgo, Chiapas|Ciudad Hidalgo]] (14,606), [[La Libertad, Suchiate|La Libertad]] (4,500), classified as urban, and [[Ignacio López Rayón, Chiapas|Ignacio López Rayón]] (1,573), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
