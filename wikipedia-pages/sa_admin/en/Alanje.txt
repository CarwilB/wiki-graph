'''Alanje  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Alanje District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It is the seat of Alanje District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|21.4|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 3,168 {{as of|2023|lc=y}}, giving it a population density of {{convert|153.2|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2010 was 2,406; its population as of 2000 was 2,703.<ref name=inec/>
