'''Mocorito''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sinaloa]] in northwestern [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Sinaloa|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=January 11, 2010|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070128142000/http://www.e-local.gob.mx:80/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/|archive-date=28 January 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref> It is bordered to the north by the municipality of [[Sinaloa Municipality|Sinaloa]], to the south by [[Navolato Municipality|Navolato]], to the southeast by [[Culiacán Municipality|Culiacán]], to the east by [[Badiraguato Municipality|Badiraguato]] and to the west with [[Salvador Alvarado Municipality|Salvador Alvarado]] and [[Angostura Municipality, Sinaloa|Angostura]]. It encompasses a total of {{cvt|2800.6|km2}} and represents 4.9% of the territory of [[Sinaloa]]. According to the 2020 census it had a population of 40,358, compromising 270 total localities. Major localities include  Pericos, [[Mocorito]], and Caimanero.<!-- The municipality covers an area of  km². As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of . --->

==Flora and Fauna==

The three climatic regions that make up Sinaloa can be seen in Mocorito. [[Tropical savanna climate|Tropical savanna]] in the west, [[Sierra Madre Occidental|mountainous]] in the east, and a [[Sonoran–Sinaloan transition subtropical dry forest|transitional zone]] in the central region. Each has its own vegetation. The mountainous regions are composed of conifers, oaks, and pine trees. In the foothills [[Bignoniaceae|amapas]], [[ebony]], [[List of plants known as cedar|cedar]], and [[juniper |juniper trees]] are commonly found. The coastal region of Mocorito is abundant with various grasses and bushes, [[Mangrove|mangroves]], [[Pithecellobium dulce|Manila tamarinds]], [[mesquite |mesquites]], and [[Ficus maxima|wild fig trees]].

Most arable land in Mocorito is used to farm [[Arable land|temporary crops]]. This includes [[rice]], [[Soybean|soybeans]], [[safflower]], [[wheat]], [[cottonseed]], [[sesame]], [[Bean|beans]], [[cotton]], [[sugarcane]], and [[Maize|corn]]. Fruit trees are also grown; this includes [[melon]], [[watermelon]], [[avocado]], [[mango]], [[Orange (fruit)|orange]], and [[papaya]]. 

[[Cattle]] raised include [[Pig|pigs]], [[Goat|goats]], and [[sheep]]. The wildlife include [[deer]], [[hare]], [[Rabbit|rabbits]], [[Armadillo|armadillos]], [[Iguana|iguanas]], and more. Predators also proliferate in the area, with animals such as [[Wildcat|wildcats]], [[Ocelot|ocelots]], and [[Cougar|mountain lions]] living deep in the mountainous regions.
