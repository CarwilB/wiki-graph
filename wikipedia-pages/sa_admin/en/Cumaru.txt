'''Cumaru''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kumɐˈɾu]}}) is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is 132&nbsp;km away from the state capital [[Recife]], and has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 10,192 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/cumaru/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Surubim]]   ('''N''');  [[Bezerros]]    ('''S''');  [[Passira]]   ('''E''');   [[Riacho das Almas]]     ('''W''').
* Area - 292.24&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 443 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 25.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 132&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Cumaru''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[beans]], [[maize|corn]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[pigs]] and [[poultry]].
