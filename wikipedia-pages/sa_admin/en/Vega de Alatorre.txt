'''Vega de Alatorre ''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central zone in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 60&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 310.92 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|20|01|43.5065|N|96|38|44.0459|W|}}.

In 2017, the total population of the municipality of Vega de Alatorre was 20,490.

==Geography==
The municipality of ''' Vega de Alatorre ''' is bordered to the north by [[Nautla]], to the east by [[Gulf of Mexico]], to the south by [[Juchique de Ferrer]] and [[Alto Lucero de Gutiérrez Barrios]] and to the west by [[Misantla (municipality)|Misantla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30192a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Vega de Alatorre |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-17}}</ref>

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[watermelon]], [[green chile]], [[banana]], and [[orange fruit]].

==Notable people==
*[[Rafael Hernández Ochoa]], Governor of Veracruz from 1974-1980.
