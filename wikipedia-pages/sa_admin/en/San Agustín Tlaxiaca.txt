'''San Agustín Tlaxiaca''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 354.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 27,118.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Agustín Tlaxiaca  |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13052a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 27, 2008}}</ref>

== Geography ==
It is located between the parallels  98° 48’ 20” and 99° 05’ 32”  west longitude, and 19° 57’ 20” and 20° 12’ 22” north latitude. San Agustín Tlaxiaca borders. It covers a total surface area of 354.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.  In the year 2010 census by [[INEGI]], it reported a population of . The total municipality extends 96.37 and borders with the municipalities of [[Ajacuba]], [[Actopan, Hidalgo|Actopan]], [[Pachuca]], [[El Arenal, Hidalgo|El Arenal]], [[Mineral del Chico]], [[Zapotlán de Juárez]], [[Tolcayuca]] and the [[state of México]] ([[Hueypoxtla]]).

The town of San Agustín Tlaxiaca, as the municipal seat, has governing jurisdiction over the following communities: [[Ixcuinquitlapilco]], [[San Juan Solis]], and [[Pozos]].<ref name="INEGI">{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/datos-geograficos/13/13052.pdf |author= INEGI |title= Link to tables of population data from Census of 2009 INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |date=2009 }}</ref>
