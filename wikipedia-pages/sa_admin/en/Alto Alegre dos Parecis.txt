'''Alto Alegre dos Parecis''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈawtu aˈlɛɡɾi dus paɾeˈsis|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 13,255 (2020) and its area is 3,958&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/4M2]</ref>

The municipality contains 20% of the {{convert|384055|ha|adj=on}} [[Corumbiara State Park]], created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES de Corumbiara – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PES de Corumbiara|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/2210|accessdate=2016-10-31}}</ref>
It also contained the {{convert|38950|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra dos Parecis State Park]], created in 1990 and cancelled in 2010.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Legislação Estadual – Ministério Público}}
 |title=Legislação Estadual|publisher=Ministério Público do Estado de Rondônia
 |url=http://www.mpro.mp.br/web/caop-meio-ambiente/legislacao-estadual|accessdate=2016-12-10}}</ref>
