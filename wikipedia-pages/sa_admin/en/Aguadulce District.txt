'''Aguadulce''' ({{IPA|es|aɣwaˈðulse}}) is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Coclé Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 39,290.<ref name="Statoids"> {{cite web |url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html |title=Districts of Panama |publisher=Statoids |access-date=April 10, 2009}}</ref>  The district covers a total area of {{cvt|466|km2}}. The capital lies at the city of [[Aguadulce, Coclé|Aguadulce]].<ref name="Statoids"/>
==Administrative divisions==
Aguadulce District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'': 

*[[Aguadulce, Coclé|San Juan Bautista de Aguadulce]] (capital)
*[[El Cristo, Coclé|El Cristo]]
*[[El Roble, Coclé|El Roble]]
*[[Pocrí, Coclé|Pocrí]]
*[[Barrios Unidos, Coclé|Barrios Unidos]]
