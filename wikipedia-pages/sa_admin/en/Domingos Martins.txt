'''Domingos Martins''' is the name of a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the Brazilian state of [[Espírito Santo]]. According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], the municipality had 33,986 inhabitants. It has a population density of 25.9 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.

==Geography==
Domingos Martins is approximately 60&nbsp;km west of the state capital, [[Vitória, Brazil|Vitória]], and is 542 meters above sea level. Bordering municipalities include [[Castelo, ES, Brazil|Castelo]], [[Venda Nova do Imigrante]] and [[Afonso Cláudio, Espírito Santo|Afonso Claudio]] to the west, [[Santa Maria de Jetiba, ES, Brazil|Santa Maria de Jetiba]] and [[Santa Leopoldina, ES, Brazil|Santa Leopoldina]] to the north, [[Cariacica]] and [[Viana, Brazil|Viana]] to the east, and [[Guarapari]], [[Marechal Floriano]], [[Alfredo Chaves, ES, Brazil|Alfredo Chaves]] and [[Vargem Alta, ES, Brazil|Vargem Alta]] to the south. The municipality itself is again subdivided into 5 districts (''distritos''): Capital (''Sede'' in Portuguese), Aracê, [[Santa Isabel, Brazil|Santa Isabel]], Parajú and Melgaço. The total area occupied by the municipality is 1,225.327&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

It is in the mountainous region of the state and is marketed as the "Green City", since it is surrounded by subtropical rainforest, much of which has disappeared in the rest of the region. Within its borders lie mountains of up to 2000m, amongst which is the well-known [[Pedra Azul State Park]] consisting mainly of a quartzite rock jutting out of the surrounding landscape at 1,822m above sea level. It has a blue tinge to the stone due to the kind of [[lichen]]s which grow on it, which gives the name to the formation and the park.

==History==

This town was founded in 1893 and was then known as '''Campinho'''. Part of the colony of [[Santa Isabel, Brazil|Santa Isabel]], it was first a secondary district but soon outgrew its capital and took the title of capital from it later on. Domingos Martins acquired its present name on 20 December 1921, given in honour of [[Domingos José Martins]] who fought in the [[Pernambucan revolt]] in 1817, when the region sought independence from Brazil. He was accused of treason and executed in [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]] on 12 June 1817.

The [[Lutheran]]-Confessional Evangelical Church in the town square ("Praça" Arthur Gerhardt-Square) was founded on 20 May 1866 and is considered the first evangelical church in Brazil to have a bell tower, as at that time only [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] churches were permitted to build bell towers. A salient feature in the history of the municipality is the presence of [[Pomeranians (German people)|Pomeranian]] immigrants, who came from a region that is now part of Germany and Poland. This population often finds itself in difficult-to-access areas, and many still speak [[East Pomeranian dialect|East Pomeranian]], though today it is thought to be extinct in their homeland.

==Economy==

Because it was colonized mainly by Germanic immigrants, Domingos Martins still has characteristically Germanic architecture and cuisine. This makes it a top tourist destination for [[Capixabas]]. Both tourism and agriculture are important to this municipality. The capital also holds the headquarters of a [[soft drink]] factory, "Refrigerantes Coroa", as well as a few small-scale biscuit factories which borrow their recipes from their Germanic ancestors.
