'''Bom Jesus do Itabapoana''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈbõ(w) ʒeˈzu(j)z dwitɐbapuˈɐ̃nɐ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. It had a population of 35,173 as of 2022, and has an area of 596.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It was founded in 1939, after being separated from the municipality of [[Itaperuna]].<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |title= IBGE Cities |author= IBGE |author-link= Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |url-status= dead |archiveurl= https://web.archive.org/web/20120430164910/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/topwindow.htm?1 |archivedate= 2012-04-30 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The northern border of Bom Jesus do Itabapoana is delimited by the [[Itabapoana River]], which divides the states of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] and [[Espírito Santo]]. The city is located 251&nbsp;km in a straight line from the state capital [[Rio de Janeiro]]. Its neighbouring municipalities are:<ref>{{cite web| url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/mapas_ibge/pol_estaduais.php | title= Mapa Político do Estado do Rio de Janeiro |author= IBGE |author-link= Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics }}</ref>

* [[São José do Calçado]] ([[Espírito Santo|ES]]) – north
* [[Bom Jesus do Norte]] ([[Espírito Santo|ES]]) – north
* [[Apiacá]] ([[Espírito Santo|ES]]) – north
* [[Mimoso do Sul]] ([[Espírito Santo|ES]]) – northeast
* [[Campos dos Goytacazes]] – east and southeast
* [[Itaperuna]] – south and southeast
* [[Natividade, Rio de Janeiro|Natividade]] – west
* [[Varre-Sai]] – northwest
* [[Guaçuí]] ([[Espírito Santo|ES]]) – northwest
