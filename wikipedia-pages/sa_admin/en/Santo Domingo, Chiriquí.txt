'''Santo Domingo''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Bugaba District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|50.9|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,625 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|51.5|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,988; its population as of 2000 was 2,276.<ref name=inec/>
