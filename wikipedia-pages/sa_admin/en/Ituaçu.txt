'''Ituaçu''' (alternately spelled '''Ituassú, Ituassu''' or '''Ituacu''') is a city in the [[Chapada Diamantina National Park|Chapada Diamantina]] region of [[Bahia]], Brazil. The city is 530&nbsp;km from [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]], the capital city of Bahia. It is the birthplace of singer [[Moraes Moreira]], while another singer [[Gilberto Gil]] lived there from 2 months old. The climate is temperate, falling to 12&nbsp;°C during the winter.  Its estimated population in 2020 is 19,030.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/ituacu/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
