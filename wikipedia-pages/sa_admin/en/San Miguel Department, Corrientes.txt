'''San Miguel Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 10,252 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|2,863|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San Miguel, Corrientes|San Miguel]].
