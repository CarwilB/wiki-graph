'''Santa Rosa''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Lima Province]], Peru. Officially established as a district on February 6, 1962. It borders the [[Ancón District]] to the north and the east, the Pacific Ocean on the west, and the [[Ventanilla District]] on the south.

==History==
The district of Santa Rosa was created on February 6, 1962, by Law No. 13982 signed by president [[Manuel Prado Ugarteche]].

It is the district most visited by tourists in Lima North, and the headquarters of more than 500 companies, mostly in the field of transport, restaurants, advertising, internet, real estate and hotels.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe/Biografia.html|title=Biografía|website=www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe|access-date=2018-04-08|archive-date=2018-04-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180409043146/http://www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe/Biografia.html|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe/DesaE.html|title=Desarrollo Económico|website=www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe|access-date=2018-04-08|archive-date=2018-04-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180409043212/http://www.munisantarosa-lima.gob.pe/DesaE.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 21.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 79 meters above [[sea level]].

===Boundaries===
* North and East: [[Ancón District|Ancón]]
* South: [[Ventanilla District|Ventanilla]] (in the [[Callao Region]])
* West: [[Pacific Ocean]]

==Demographics==
Santa Rosa is the sixth least populated district of Lima with 18,751 inhabitants, only surpassed by [[Pucusana District|Pucusana]] (17,786), [[Punta Negra District|Punta Negra]] (8,271), [[San Bartolo District|San Bartolo]] (7,989), [[Punta Hermosa]] (7,895), and [[Santa María del Mar District (Peru)|Santa María del Mar]] (1,675).<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.elpopular.pe/actualidad-y-policiales/2017-01-18-lima-estos-son-los-distritos-mas-y-menos-poblados-de-la-capital-del-peru-foto-y-video|title=Lima: Estos son los distritos más y menos poblados de la capital del Perú (FOTO Y VIDEO)|date=2017-01-18|work=ElPopular_pe|access-date=2018-04-08|language=es}}</ref>

11% of the inhabitants are children under 5 years of age. 11.8% are from 6 to 11 years old, and 11.2% are from 12 to 17 years old.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://conasec.mininter.gob.pe/obnasec/pdfs/Nro.01-DistritoSantaRosa.pdf|title=Information about Santa Rosa District|access-date=2018-04-08|archive-date=2017-07-21|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170721142228/http://conasec.mininter.gob.pe/obnasec/pdfs/Nro.01-DistritoSantaRosa.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>
