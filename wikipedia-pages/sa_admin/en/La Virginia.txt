'''La Virginia''' ({{IPA|es|la βiɾˈxinja}} is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. About 50 km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=La Virginia – Risaralda un destino lleno de vida |url=https://risaraldaundestinollenodevida.com/new/la-virginia/ |access-date=2023-09-27 |language=es-CO}}</ref> In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 28,488.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene La Virginia, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/la-virginia |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
Founded in 1959 by Francisco Jaramillo, Pedro Martínez and Leonardo Villa, among others. This Municipality was in its beginnings a Palenque for black fugitives from slavery. The initial hamlet was called Sopinga and then Nigricia in the mid-19th century. On November 27, 1959, La Virginia was declared a municipality, since it previously belonged to Belalcazar, Caldas.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=La Virginia |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100237/la-virginia/ |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>
