The '''Aricagua Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality had a population of 4,564.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls Poblacion 6] {{dead link|date=October 2016|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref> The town of [[Aricagua]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Aricagua Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">[http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip Merida] {{dead link|date=October 2016|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Aricagua Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, had a population of 4,564 (up from 4,514 in 2000). This amounted to 0.5% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls Poblacion 5] {{dead link|date=October 2016|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|5.8|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls Poblacion 4] {{dead link|date=October 2016|bot=InternetArchiveBot|fix-attempted=yes}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Aricagua Municipality is [[Nelson Jesus Márquez Rojas]], re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 59% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/12/alcaldedis=0&edo=12&mun=22&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |access-date=16 November 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Capital Aricagua and San Antonio.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
