'''Paz de Río''' is a town and municipality in the [[Valderrama Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre of Paz de Río, situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]], is located at an elevation of {{convert|2200|m|ft}}, and the elevation within the municipality ranges from {{convert|2150|m|ft}} to {{convert|3600|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[Sativanorte]] and [[Sativasur]] in the north, [[Betéitiva]] and [[Tasco, Boyacá|Tasco]] in the south, Sativasur and [[Socha]] in the east and [[Belén, Boyacá|Belén]] and [[Tutazá]] in the west.<ref name=websitePazdeRio>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150602234008/http://www.pazderio-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Paz de Río]</ref>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Paz de Río was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The original name of the village, now the name of a ''[[vereda]]'', Chitagoto is taken from ''[[cacique]]'' Chitagoto, who was loyal to the [[Tundama]] of [[Duitama|Tundama]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [https://soyturismo.wordpress.com/category/boyaca/page/3/ Etymology Chitagoto]</ref>

Modern Paz de Río was not founded until July 20, 1935 by Marco Antonio Mejía Gómez.<ref name=websitePazdeRio/>

== Economy ==
The main economical activity of Paz de Río is [[agriculture]], predominantly [[potato]]es and [[onion]]s. Other products are [[maize]], [[wheat]] and [[barley]].<ref name=websitePazdeRio/>
