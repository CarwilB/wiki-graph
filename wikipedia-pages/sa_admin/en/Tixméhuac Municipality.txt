'''Tixméhuac Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "Place of Xmeuac tribe") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 251.65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|110|km}} southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and under the chieftainship of Tutul Xiu. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos, including: Juan Xiu, in 1557; Hernando Xiu, in 1565; and Pablo Cen, in 1579.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821,<ref name="municipios"/> and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Tekax Municipality]].<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|accessdate=10 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=Spanish|date=1996|page=115|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=2015-12-23}}</ref> In 1900 it becomes its own municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, public services and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Tixméhuac | url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=10 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Local festivals==
Every year on 19 September the town celebrates the feast of Saint Michael the Archangel.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Tourist attractions==
* Church of Saint Michael the Archangel, built during the eighteenth century
* archeological sites at Chucub, Kimbilá, Modzil and Nocas
