'''San Andrés Tenejapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the montane central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 85&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 24.68 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|47|N|97|06|W|}}.

==Geography==

The municipality of '''San Andrés Tenejapan''' is delimited to the north by [[Tlilapan]] and [[Rafael Delgado, Veracruz|Rafael Delgado]] to the east by [[Rafael Delgado, Veracruz|Rafael Delgado]], [[Rafael Delgado, Veracruz|Rafael Delgado]] and [[Tlilapan]],  to the south by [[Tequila, Veracruz|Tequila]] and [[Atlahuilco]] and to the west by [[Huiloapan de Cuauhtémoc]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30140a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: San Andrés Tenejapan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  It is watered by small rivers, which are tributaries of the [[Blanco River (Veracruz)|Río Blanco]].

The weather in San Andrés Tenejapan is cold and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]].

==Celebrations==

In '''San Andrés Tenejapan''', the celebration in honor to Virgen de la Candelaria takes place in February, Patron of the town, and the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe takes place in December.
