'''Monte Santo de Minas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the south of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population is 21,513 inhabitants, according to the 2020 estimate. Its area is 594.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and its density is 35.8 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web |url=  https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/monte-santo-de-minas/panorama|title= Monte Santo de Minas|language=Portuguese |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]]) | place = Brasília, Brazil |date=2024 |accessdate=2024-11-24}}</ref>
