'''Coper''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Western Boyacá Province]].

==Climate==
Coper has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
