'''Puerres''' is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Puerres has a comfortable [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'') with moderate rainfall year-round.
