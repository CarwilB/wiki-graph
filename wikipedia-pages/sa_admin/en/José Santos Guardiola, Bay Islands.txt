'''José Santos Guardiola''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Bay Islands Department]] of [[Honduras]].<ref name="Municipio">{{Cite web |url=https://www.municipio.hn/municipio-jose-santos-guardiola.html |title=El municipio de José Santos Guardiola |website=Municipios de Honduras |access-date=13 September 2023 |language=es}}</ref> It is located on the island of [[Roatán]]. The name of the municipality is in honor of [[Brigadier General]] [[José Santos Guardiola|José Santos Guardiola Bustillo]], [[President of Honduras]] between 1856 and 1862. The capital of the municipality is [[Oakridge, Honduras|Oakridge]].

The municipality was created by presidential decree, issued in 1960.
