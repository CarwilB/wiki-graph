'''Buenos Aires''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Rivas (department)|Rivas]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]], located on the western shore of [[Lake Nicaragua]].

==History==
The municipality of Buenos Aires was founded as a pueblo sometime between 1820 and 1838.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://play.google.com/store/books/details?id=8-w8AQAAIAAJ&rdid=book-8-w8AQAAIAAJ&rdot=1 |title=Historia de Nicaragua desde los tiempos más remotos hasta el año de 1852: Volumen 3 |trans-title=History of Nicaragua from the earliest times to the year 1852: Volume 3 |language=es |website=play.google.com |date=1889 |access-date=26 June 2025}}</ref>
