'''Maniche''' ({{IPA|fr|maniʃ}}; {{langx|ht|Manich}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Les Cayes Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 23,934 inhabitants.

The villages of [[Maniche, Maniche, Sud|Maniche]] and [[Madame Jean Pierre]] is located in the commune.
