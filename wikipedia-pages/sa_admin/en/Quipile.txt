'''Quipile''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Tequendama Province]] of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The municipality borders [[Bituima]] and [[Vianí]] in the north, [[Jerusalén, Cundinamarca|Jerusalén]] and [[Anapoima]] in the south, [[Anolaima]], [[Cachipay, Cundinamarca|Cachipay]] and [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] in the east and [[San Juan de Rioseco]] and [[Pulí, Cundinamarca|Pulí]] in the west. The urban centre is located at an altitude of {{convert|2012|m|ft}} at a distance of {{convert|83|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]].<ref name=websiteQuipile>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.quipile-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Quipile] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150522160615/http://www.quipile-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-22 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Quipile comes from the [[Cariban languages|Cariban language]] of the Panche and means "strong and superior place".<ref name=websiteQuipile/> It is derived from Quipili, the name of the ''[[cacique]]'' of the Panche.

== History ==
In the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], Quipile was inhabited by the [[Panche people|Panche]]. They were submitted to the Spanish rule of the [[New Kingdom of Granada]] in the [[Battle of Tocarema]], which took place on August 20, 1538.

The date and name of the founder is unknown but traditionally set at November 12, 1825 by José María Lozano.<ref name=websiteQuipile/>

== Economy ==
Quipile is an agrarian community with main products [[coffee]], [[sugarcane]] and [[banana]]s cultivated.<ref name=websiteQuipile/>

== Paleontology ==
In 1956, fossil remains of ''[[Megatherium]]'' and ''[[Mastodon]]''; [[Pleistocene]] [[megafauna]] dated at 460,000 years [[Before Present|BP]], were discovered within the boundaries of Quipile.<ref name=websiteQuipile/>
