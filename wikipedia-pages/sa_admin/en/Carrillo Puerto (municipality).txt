}}'''Carrillo Puerto''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the central area of the State of Veracruz, at {{coord|18|47|N|96|34|W|}}. It has a surface of 246.76 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 18,888 as of 2020.<ref name="census2020">{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/scitel/Default?ev=9|title=Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 - SCITEL|language=es|publisher=INEGI|access-date=2021-01-27}}</ref> Its seat is '''Tamarindo''' (1,209 inhabitants) and its most populated town is El Palmar (1,259).<ref name="census2020" />

==Geography==
The municipality of ''' Carrillo Puerto ''' is delimited to the north by [[Paso del Macho]], to the north-east by [[Soledad de Doblado]], to the east by [[Cotaxtla]], to the south-east by [[Cuichapa]] and to the west by [[Cuitláhuac, Veracruz|Cuitláhuac]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30031a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Carlos A. Carrillo |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es |access-date=2009-06-05 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061129164227/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30031a.htm |archive-date=2006-11-29 }}</ref>

Weather in Carrillo Puerto is warm and dry most of the year, with a rainy season in Summer.

==Economy==
Its main crops are [[maize]] and [[beans]]. 
==Culture==
The main popular celebration takes place in May, to honor [[Isidore the Laborer]], patron saint of the town.
