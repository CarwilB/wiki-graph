==History==
Assaí was founded in 1932 by [[Japanese Brazilians|Japanese migrants]], becoming a municipality on January 28, 1944. Assaí is among a number of small cities in Brazil founded by Japanese immigrants, including [[Bastos, Brazil|Bastos]] and [[Pereira Barreto]] in [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Story |first=Nick |date=2021-07-14 |title=Location spotlight: The Japanese influence in Brazil |url=https://www.storyproductions.com/location-spotlight-japan |access-date=2025-06-17 |website=Story Productions}}</ref>

The city was initially named '''Assailand''', before ultimately being renamed in 1944.<ref name=":0" />

=== Asian heritage ===
The city has the highest reported percentage of [[Asian Brazilians]] in the country, comprising 11.5% of the population.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-12-22 |title=2022 Census: self-reported brown population is the majority in Brazil for the first time {{!}} News Agency |url=https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/en/agencia-news/2184-news-agency/news/38726-2022-census-self-reported-brown-population-is-the-majority-in-brazil-for-the-first-time |access-date=2025-06-13 |website=Agência de Notícias - IBGE |language=en-GB}}</ref> In 2018, the city inaugurated a Japanese-style castle in a ceremony attended by around 4,000 spectators. The castle was modeled by [[Himeji Castle]] in [[Japan]]'s [[Hyōgo Prefecture]], which itself is a "sister province" of the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]].

== Politics and government ==
In the second round of the [[2022 Brazilian general election]], Assaí voted for [[Jair Bolsonaro]] with 70.43% of the vote, with [[Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]] taking 29.57% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-10-31 |title=Eleições em Assaí (PR): Veja como foi a votação no 2º turno |url=https://g1.globo.com/pr/norte-noroeste/noticia/2022/10/31/eleicoes-em-assai-pr-veja-como-foi-a-votacao-no-2o-turno.ghtml |access-date=2025-06-13 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref>

The city's current mayor is Michel Angelo Bomtempo, a member of the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Prefeitura Municipal de Assaí - PR |url=https://www.assai.pr.gov.br/prefeito |access-date=2025-06-17 |website=assai.pr.gov}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-11-30 |title=Eleições 2024: Tuti (PSD) {{!}} Candidato(a) a prefeito(a) de Assaí-PR {{!}} Folha |url=https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/eleicoes/candidatos/2024/pr/prefeito/tuti-160002144189.shtml |access-date=2025-06-17 |website=Folha de S.Paulo |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
