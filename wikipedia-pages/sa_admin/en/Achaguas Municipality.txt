The '''Achaguas Municipality''' is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Apure]] has an area of 15,754 km<sup>2</sup> and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 58,516. <ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Achaguas]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Achaguas Municipality.<ref>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Apure.zip]{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
The town of Santa Bárbara de la Isla de los Achaguas was founded in 1774 by Fray Alonso de Castro, and its name was taken from the native tribe "The Achaguas".  Since 1835, the miraculous wood-carved figure of "El Nazareno de Achaguas" is venerated in the town's church.  This figure was a donation that [[José Antonio Páez|General José Antonio Páez]] gave to the town in agreement to the victory obtained in the battle against the Spanish for the independence of Venezuela which was to take place in [[Carabobo]].

==Demographics==
The Achaguas Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 65,081 (up from 55,446 in 2000).  This amounts to 13.7% of the state's population.<ref>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls]{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|4.13|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls]{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Achaguas Municipality is Sana Josefina Nakat de Hurtado, who is serving as interim mayor.  She replaced Luis Montoya who had been elected on October 31, 2004, with 71% of the vote.<ref>[http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/ CONSEJO NACIONAL ELECTORAL – RESULTADOS ELECCIONES REGIONALES 2004<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into six [[parish]]es; Urbana Achaguas, Apurito, El Yagual, Guachara, Mucuritas, and Queseras del Medio.<ref>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Apure]{{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
