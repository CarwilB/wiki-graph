'''Castilla''' is one of eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Arequipa Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was named after [[Ramón Castilla]].

== Geography ==
The [[Chila mountain range|Chila]] mountain range traverses the province. One of the highest mountains of the province is [[Coropuna]] at {{convert|6425|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} above sea level. == Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Aymara people|Aymara]] and [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent, as well as [[White Peruvians|European]] descendants of [[Spaniards|Spanish]] settlers. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], is the language which the majority of the population (79.72%) learnt to speak in childhood, 18.52% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language and 1.51% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
