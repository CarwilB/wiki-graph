'''Tibacuy''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]], in [[Sumapaz Province]]. Tibacuy is situated south of the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] at {{convert|87|km|mi}} southeast of the capital [[Bogotá]].<ref name=websiteTibacuy>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tibacuy-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tibacuy] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310085035/http://www.tibacuy-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2014-03-10 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
In the [[Chibcha language]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] and Panche, Tibacuy means "official chief".<ref name=websiteTibacuy/>

== History ==
The area of Tibacuy was inhabited by the Muisca and the [[Panche people|Panche]] with the [[Sutagao people|Sutagao]] living to the southeast. The present town centre is situated at a lower altitude than the original indigenous village. Modern Tibacuy was founded between 13th and 17th of February 1592 by Bernardino de Albornoz.<ref name=websiteTibacuy/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Tibacuy is [[agriculture]], predominantly [[coffee]], [[banana]]s, [[tomato]]es and [[blackberry|blackberries]].

== Archaeology ==
In Cumaca, rural part of Tibacuy, [[petroglyphs]] have been found.<ref name=websiteTibacuy/>
