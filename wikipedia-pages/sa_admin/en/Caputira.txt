'''Caputira''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and the microregion of [[Manhuaçu]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 9,308.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/caputira/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
