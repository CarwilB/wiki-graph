'''Penipe Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Chimborazo Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Penipe]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 6,485.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

The canton consists of one urban parish, [[Penipe Parish]] and six rural ones:
* [[Bayushig Parish]]	
* [[Bilbao Parish]]
* [[La Candelaria Parish, Ecuador|La Candelaria Parish]]	
* [[El Altar Parish]]	
* [[Matus Parish]]	
* [[Puela Parish]]
