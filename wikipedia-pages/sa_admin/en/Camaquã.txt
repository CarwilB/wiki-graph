'''Camaquã''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|7993|ha|adj=on}} [[Camaquã State Park]], which was created in 1975.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual do Camaquã – SEMA}}
