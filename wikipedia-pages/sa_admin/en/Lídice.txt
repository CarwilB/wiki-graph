'''Lídice''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Capira District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 5,307 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,840; its population as of 2000 was 4,711.<ref name=inec/>

The town was renamed on 31 October 1943, from Potrero, to commemorate the town of [[Lidice]] in the [[Protectorate of Bohemia and Moravia]] (the [[Czech Republic]] today) which was [[Lidice massacre|razed to the ground, and its population murdered]] by the [[Schutzstaffel|SS]] on 10 June 1942 as a retribution for the [[assassination]] of [[Reinhard Heydrich]], a highly ranked official.
