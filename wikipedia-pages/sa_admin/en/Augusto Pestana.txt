'''Augusto Pestana''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the State of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. It is located at 28°31′01″S 53°39′01″W, at an altitude of 390 m (1,279 feet). The city is 320&nbsp;km northwest of [[Porto Alegre]], 1,540&nbsp;km southwest of [[Brasília]] and just 120&nbsp;km east of the border with [[Argentina]].<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]</ref>

It was named after [[Augusto Pestana (politician)|Augusto Pestana]] (1868-1934), a Brazilian engineer and politician.

==History==

The area was originally inhabited by [[indigenous peoples of South America]] ("Umbu" tradition) about ten thousand years ago. In 1697, [[Spaniard]] [[Jesuits]] founded a [[Jesuit Reductions|Reduction]] in São João Batista, 40&nbsp;km west of present-day Augusto Pestana. The whole region was eventually annexed by the [[Portugal|Portuguese]] in 1801. One hundred years later, [[Augusto Pestana (politician)|Augusto Pestana]], then director for Public Lands in Northwest [[Rio Grande do Sul]], founded a settlement on the Cadeado Range, between the Conceição and the Ijuizinho rivers (the name "Cadeado", padlock in Portuguese, is a reference to a checkpoint that existed on the road between [[Cruz Alta, Rio Grande do Sul|Cruz Alta]] and [[Santo Ângelo]]).<ref>LAZZAROTTO, Danilo. "História de Ijuí". Ijuí, Museu Antropológico Director Pestana, 1977.</ref>

The first settlers were [[Germany|German]] immigrants, most of them from [[Pomerania]]. During [[World War II]], soldiers from Augusto Pestana fought for Brazil against [[Nazi Germany]]. The list of Pestanense members in the [[Brazilian Expeditionary Force]] (FEB), active in the [[Mediterranean, Middle East and African theatres of World War II|Mediterranean Theatre]] of the conflict, included Artur Goergen, Eugenio Ladvig and Helmuth Matte, now honored with street names in the city. The other FEB veterans were Sebaldo Goergen, Waldemar Senn, Albino Wilhens, Benno Müller, Oswin Wunder, Alípio Schmitt, Osvino Henrique Geiss, Adelino Mathias Dambroz, Emílio Papke, Alfonso Steiernagel e Arnoldo Gustavo Frantz.<ref>Monument to World War II Veterans, Praça Farroupilha.</ref>

In the 1950s and 1960s, the settlement experienced a boom in [[wheat]] production and was incorporated in September 1965 as the municipality of '''Augusto Pestana'''. The first city government was formally installed on 14 May 1966.<ref>[http://www.pmaugustopestana.com.br City Government of Augusto Pestana]</ref>

==Geography==
[[File:AP port.PNG|thumb|150px|left|City map of Augusto Pestana (click to enlarge).]]
Augusto Pestana has a total area of 347,44 km2. It borders the municipalities of [[Ijuí]] and [[Coronel Barros]] to the north, [[Boa Vista do Cadeado]] to the east, [[Jóia]] to the south and [[Eugênio de Castro]] to the west. The municipality is drained by the Ijuizinho and Conceição rivers, tributaries of the [[Ijuí River|Ijuí river]], in the [[Uruguay River|Uruguay]] basin.

The climate is classified as a [[humid subtropical]] (Köppen: Cfa). Summers are typically warm and humid, while winters are cold and windy. The hottest month is January, with an average high temperature of 29.9&nbsp;°C. The coldest month is July, with an average low of 8.4&nbsp;°C.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]</ref>

==Demographics==
[[File:Igreja AP.jpeg|thumb|150px|left|St. Joseph Church, Augusto Pestana.]]
The population of Augusto Pestana was 7,096 at the 2010 Brazilian census. The population density was 20.4 inhabitants per square kilometer. Life expectancy is 75.8 years.

Most Pestanenses are of [[German-Brazilian]], [[Italian-Brazilian]] and [[Portuguese-Brazilian]] ancestry. 66% of them are [[Roman Catholic]], whereas 32% are [[Protestant]], mostly [[Lutherans]].<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]</ref>

==Regional languages==
[[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]] (or ''hunriqueano riograndense'' in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]], Brazil's national language) is one of the names given to a Germanic language spoken in this municipality since pioneer days.<ref>[https://www.ethnologue.com/language/hrx Ethnologue: Languages of the world: Riogrand Hunsrik: A language of Brazil.] Site accessed on July 28, 2017.</ref>

==Economy==
According to the statistics bureau of Rio Grande do Sul, Augusto Pestana's [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) reached 74.9 million US dollars (149.7 million Brazilian reais) in 2010. The GDP per capita was 10.5 thousand US dollars (21 thousand Brazilian reais).

[[Agribusiness]] is the driving force of the local economy. The municipality is a great producer of [[wheat]], [[soy]], [[Maize|corn]], [[beef]] and [[dairy]].<ref>[http://www.fee.tch.br Siegfried Heuser Foundation]{{Dead link|date=May 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Education==

There are seven public schools, one private school and one public library in Augusto Pestana. The city is just 15&nbsp;km away of the main campus of the University of Ijuí, one of the largest universities in southern Brazil.<ref>[http://www.pmaugustopestana.com.br City Government of Augusto Pestana]</ref>

==Transportation==

Augusto Pestana is crossed by the RS 522 State Highway, which connects the city to the BR 295 National Highway. The road distance to the State capital, Porto Alegre, is 405&nbsp;km. There is a commuter bus service between the [[Ijuí]], the major transportation hub in the region. The closest airport is located in Ijuí ([[Ijuí Airport|João Batista Bos Filho Airport]]).<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]</ref>
