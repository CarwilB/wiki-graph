'''Ibirá''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 12,518 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|190|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8IF Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Ibirá is known as a tourist resort, due to the thermal waters.

==Geography==

The municipality of Ibirá is situated in the northern part of São Paulo state. Its main river is the ''Rio do Cubatão'', a tributary of the [[Tietê River]].
