'''Pirapora do Bom Jesus''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. It is part of the [[Metropolitan Region of São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |title=Lei Complementar nº 1.139, de 16 de junho de 2011 |access-date=2015-09-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151001191247/http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |archive-date=2015-10-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The population is 19,178 (2020 est.) in an area of 108.49&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://archive.today/20150909093612/http://cod.ibge.gov.br/HQO Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The name ''Pirapora'' comes from the [[Tupi language]].

==Culture==
Pirapora is the biggest Brazilian [[shrine]] after [[The Basilica of the National Shrine of Our Lady of Aparecida|Aparecida]]. A sculpture of the Good [[Jesus]] is [[veneration|venerated]] here.
