'''Sena Madureira''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈsenɐ maduˈɾejɾɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 46,511 and its area is {{convert|25278|km2}}, making it the largest municipality in the state. It has a climate which combines temperatures of {{convert|78|F}} with humidities in the upper 90s percent, all year round. It is 143&nbsp;km from [[Rio Branco, Acre|Rio Branco]].
The oldest church in Acre state is located in Sena Madureira, the Nossa Senhora da Imaculada Conceição Church (1910).

==Geography==

The municipality contains part of the [[Rio Acre Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica Rio Acre|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=68|accessdate=2016-04-23}}</ref>
It also contains part of the {{convert|931537|ha|adj=on}} [[Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use environmental unit created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Extrativista Chico Mendes|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=222|accessdate=2016-06-04}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|176349|ha|adj=on}} [[Macauã National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 1988.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA do Macauã – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA do Macauã|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/pt-br/uc/4046|accessdate=2016-06-19}}</ref>
It also contains the {{convert|21,148|ha|adj=on}} [[São Francisco National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de São Francisco – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA de São Francisco|publisher=ISA:Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5960|accessdate=2016-06-21}}</ref>
[[File:Parque_Estadual_do_Chandless_(2).jpg|thumb|center|[[Abseiling]] at [[Chandless State Park]], Sena Madureira, Acre, Brazil]]
It contains 98% of the {{convert|750795|ha|adj=on}} [[Cazumbá-Iracema Extractive Reserve]], established in 2002 to support sustainable use of the natural resources by the traditional population.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Cazumbá-Iracema – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Cazumbá-Iracema|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1765|accessdate=2016-06-16}}</ref>
The municipality contains about 12% of the {{convert|695303|ha|adj=on}} [[Chandless State Park]], created in 2004, which protects an area of rainforest with bamboos that contains various endemic species of flora and fauna.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Chandless – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PES Chandless|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1821|accessdate=2016-06-18}}</ref>
It contains about 27% of the {{convert|46064|ha|adj=on}} [[Antimary State Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Antimary|publisher=ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FES do Antimary|publisher=ISA:Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/637|accessdate=2016-07-02}}</ref>
