The city is served by [[Confresa Airport]].

== History ==
The first settlement that led to the formation of the municipality of Confresa was called Vila Tapiraguaia, a fusion of the terms Tapirapé and Araguaia. This was a geographic reference to the Tapirapé and Araguaia Rivers, tributaries forming the Tocantins Basin. The term Confresa refers to the Colonizadora Frenova Sapeva. This company owned the Fazendas Reunidas Nova Amazônia, which included numerous agricultural properties and a distillery. The company is now called Frenova Agropecuária Ltda.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Confresa/MT - O maior município do Norte Araguaia comemora 27 anos de emancipação política e administrativa : |url=https://www.aguaboanews.com.br/noticias/exibir.asp?id=16688 |access-date=2024-06-24 |website=Agua Boa News |language=pt-br}}</ref>

Confresa was established as a district of Santa Terezinha on April 17, 1990. It was elevated to municipality status on December 20, 1991, also incorporating small areas from Luciara and Porto Alegre do Norte. The district headquarters was established on January 1, 1993, and on March 19, 1997, the district of Veranópolis was created. Confresa also includes the following agricultural villages: Jacaré, Valente, Três Flechas, Buriti, Novo Planalto, Pé de Caju, Lumiar, Vila Goiás, Bridão Brasileiro, Santa Marta, Pé de Galinha, and Postinho.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Dados Gerais |url=https://www.confresa.org/portal/servicos/1004/dados-gerais |access-date=2024-06-24 |website=Prefeitura Municipal de Confresa - MT |language=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2020-06-07 |title=Prefeito decreta 'lockdown' em município com 30 mil habitantes e 3 mortes por Covid-19 |url=https://g1.globo.com/mt/mato-grosso/noticia/2020/06/07/prefeito-decreta-lockdown-em-municipio-com-30-mil-habitantes-e-3-mortes-por-covid-19.ghtml |access-date=2024-06-24 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref>
