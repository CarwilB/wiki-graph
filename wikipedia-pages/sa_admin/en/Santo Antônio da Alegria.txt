'''Santo Antônio da Alegria''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northern part of the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 6,977 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|310.29|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6VK Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 791 m. A neighboring municipality is [[São Sebastião do Paraíso]] in [[Minas Gerais]] to the northeast.

== Government ==

* Mayor: Ricardo da Silva Sobrinho (2021/2024)
* Vice Mayor: Denilson de Carvalho (2021/2024)
