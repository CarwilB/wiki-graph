'''Xalatlaco''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in [[Mexico (state)|Mexico State]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  93.23&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is one of the 17 municipalities that [[Mexico City's boroughs|border Mexico City]], bordering the capital city's southwest side.

As of the 2010 census, the municipality had a total population of 26,865 inhabitants.
