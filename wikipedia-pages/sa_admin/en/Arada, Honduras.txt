'''Arada''' ({{IPA|es|aˈɾaða}}) is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|108|km2}} and had a population of 9,622 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. It was officially established on 22 June 1900.

== History ==
Arada was earlier part of the municipality of [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]]. The people petitioned for it to be declared as an independent municipality due to the difficulty in accessing the Santa Bárbara as the [[Ulua River]] ran in between the settlements. It was officially established as a municipality on 22 June 1900, and the first municipal council took office on 4 January 1901 with Agapito Rodríguez as the first mayor.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Arada, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Arada.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-arada/|title=Municipality of Arada|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

"Arada" is derived from [[Spanish language|Spanish]] meaning "plough". The earlier inhabitants were from the nearby El Ocotal village. As the soil at El Ocotal was unsuitable for [[agriculture]], they searched for fertile lands nearby, and found Arada. The farmers traveled daily to work, and returned back to their native village at sunset, and this activity led to the name of the place itself.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>

== Geography ==
Arada is located in the southern part of the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[San Nicolás, Santa Bárbara|San Nicolás]] and [[San Vicente Centenario]] to the north, [[Santa Bárbara, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] to the east, [[El Níspero]] to the south, and [[La Unión, Lempira|La Unión]], and San Nicolás to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|108|km2}} and has an average elevation of {{cvt|432|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>

== Demographics ==
As per the 2013 census, Arada had a total population of 9,622 inhabitants, of which 4,948 (51.4%) were male and 4,674 (48.6%) were female, with a masculinity index of 105.8 men per 100 women. Of the total population, 2,771 (28.8%) lived in the urban and 6,851 (71.2%) in the rural areas. The population density was 89.09 inhabitants per km². The intercensal population growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 1.4% per year.<ref name="CP1">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1602__arada/|title=Arada|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

Of the population, 0.3% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and rest were non-indigenous people.<ref name="CP1"/> By broad age group, 36.3% of the population were aged 0–14, 57.9% were aged 15–64, and 5.8% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 21.3 years and the mean age was 26.3 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (6,134 persons), the 2013 census recorded an illiteracy rate of 26.4%, higher than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} The municipality had 2,312 occupied private dwellings, with an average household size of 4.1 persons.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=23, 27}}
