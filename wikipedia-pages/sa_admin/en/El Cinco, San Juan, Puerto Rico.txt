'''El Cinco''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the [[municipality]] of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], [[Puerto Rico]] and a former [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] of the dissolved municipality of [[Río Piedras, Puerto Rico|Río Piedras]]. As of the [[2020 United States census|2020 United States Census]], it had a population of 6,138 and a land area of 1.34 square miles (3.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>) resulting in a population density of 4,625.4/sq mi (1,785.9/km2).<ref>{{cite web | last=Bureau | first=U.S. Census | title=Explore Census Data | website=Explore Census Data | url=https://data.census.gov/profile/El_Cinco_barrio,_San_Juan_Municipio,_Puerto_Rico?g=060XX00US7212724868 | access-date=June 25, 2025}}</ref>

El Cinco is surrounded by seven barrios: [[Gobernador Piñero, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Gobernador Piñero]], [[Monacillo Urbano, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo Urbano]], [[Monacillo, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Monacillo]], [[Cupey, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Cupey]], [[Sabana Llana Sur, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Sabana Llana Sur]], [[Pueblo, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Río Piedras (pueblo)]] and [[Hato Rey Sur, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Sur]].

== Landmarks and historic sites ==
This barrio is home to numerous sites listed in the [[National Register of Historic Places|US National Register of Historic Places]]. The historic [[Old Piedras River Aqueduct|Río Piedras Aqueduct]], also known as the San Juan Historic Aqueduct, is an old aqueduct and water filtration plant that dates from 1846. The site has been listed in the National Register of Historic Places since 2007.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Antiguo Acueducto del Río Piedras {{!}} National Trust for Historic Preservation|url=https://savingplaces.org/places/antiguo-acueducto-del-rio-piedras|access-date=2021-10-14|website=savingplaces.org|language=en-US|archive-date=2022-01-21|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220121053208/https://savingplaces.org/places/antiguo-acueducto-del-rio-piedras|url-status=dead}}</ref> Other historic sites located in El Cinco are the [[Río Piedras Bridge]], shared with [[Hato Rey Sur]] and dating from 1853, the Rum Pilot Plant located [[University of Puerto Rico]] Experimental Agricultural Station,<ref>{{Citation|title=National Register of Historic Places listings in San Juan, Puerto Rico|date=2021-05-23|url=https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=National_Register_of_Historic_Places_listings_in_San_Juan,_Puerto_Rico&oldid=1024704338|work=Wikipedia|language=en|access-date=2021-10-14}}</ref> and the [[San Juan Botanical Garden]] is located nearby too.

== Transportation ==
The area is served by the ''[[Tren Urbano]]'' [[Rapid transit|metro system]] with the elevated [[Cupey (Tren Urbano station)|Cupey station]] located on the intersection of highways [[Puerto Rico Highway 1|PR-1]] and [[Puerto Rico Highway 176|PR-176]] in El Cinco. Despite the name of the station, it is not located in the barrio of Cupey, although it is indeed located nearby.
