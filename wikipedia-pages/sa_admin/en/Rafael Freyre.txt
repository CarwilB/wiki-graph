'''Rafael Freyre''' is a municipality and town in the [[Holguín Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Overview==
[[File:Rafael Freyre Cuba 2.jpg|thumb|left|Rafael Freyre train station]]
The main municipal settlement (Rafael Freyre itself) developed around the sugar cane factory "Central Santa Lucía".<ref name="Central Santa Lucia">{{Cite web| url=https://www.flickr.com/photos/44316456@N00/2531144033/| title= Sugar Mill| author= MatK2| year= 2008| accessdate=2009-10-12|language=es}}</ref> The only middle school in the area, “ESBU Rafael Freyre Torres,” is attended by a few hundred students from small towns near Los Mineros, La Viuda, Cochico, and other nearby towns.

Some of its most popular destinations are the north coast beaches, such as [[Playa Guardalavaca]] and [[Guardalavaca]], which are both natural white sand beaches in the bordering municipality of [[Banes, Cuba|Banes]].

In a small village called Bariay, there is a monument that marks the arrival of [[Christopher Columbus]].
{{clear left}}

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Rafael Freyre had a population of 53,408.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|620|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|86|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
