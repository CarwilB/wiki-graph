The municipality of Araquari was basically colonized by [[Azorean]] [[European colonization of the Americas|immigrants]], who arrived at the coast of Santa Catarina between 1748 and 1756. Since then, the [[Azores|Azorean culture]] rooted up and walked hand in hand with the most diverse cultures, as with the [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil]] and [[Africa]]ns, both important in this region thus creating a cultural and religious mosaic.

Like many coastal cities Araquari has its founding myth linked to the [[European immigration to Brazil|European occupation process in America]], in the [[Age of Discovery#bob’s ocean blue (1419–1507)|first phase of the age of discoveries]]. According to information from the municipal Department of Culture the European foundation of Araquari is set 40 years after the discovery of Brazil in 1500.{{cn|date=November 2021}}

The Spanish navigator [[Álvar Núñez Cabeza de Vaca]] landed what is now [[Barra Velha]] and encouraged the exploration of the northern region, until then inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indios]]. The expedition consisted of 250 men, 40 horses, some slaves and indoctrinated group of Indios by the [[Jesuits]]. A month later, they came what they called first ''Paranaguá Mirim'', ("small cove") in [[Tupi-Guarani]].{{cn|date=November 2021}}

In 1658, the first [[Portuguese people|Portuguese]] [[Settler|pioneers]] settled in the region inhabited by [[Carijós]], but the actual foundation of the town only happened in 1848, when a Portuguese ship arrived in [[Paraty]] under the command of [[Manuel Vieira de Albuquerque Touvar]], who founded a small colony. Another pioneer, [[Joaquim da Rocha Coutinho]], joined him and the two decided to found a village, but failed to reach an agreement on the site. {{cn|date=November 2021}}

The Judge of the District of São Francisco ruled in favor of Rocha Coutinho who built homes on the banks of the river [[Parati]], surrounding pastures and crops. Both are considered to be the founders of the parish Senhor Bom Jesus do Paraty (''Good Lord Jesus of Paraty'') in 1854, but still part of the municipality of [[São Francisco do Sul]].<ref>[http://www.araquari.sc.gov.br/c/conheca-araquari Conheça Araquari, History of Araquari] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150614005140/http://www.araquari.sc.gov.br/c/conheca-araquari |date=2015-06-14 }}. araquari.sc.gov.br, retrieved 21 July 2015</ref>

==BMW==
[[File:First BMW manufactured in Brazil SAO 2014 0419.JPG|thumb|left|The first BMW manufactured at the Araquari plant]]The town is home of a [[BMW]] automobile plant since October 2014, when the first BMW 328i ActiveFlex car was assembled. Over the course of the next few years, more than [[EUR]] 200 million was invested in the plant, resulting in a production capacity of more than 30,000 vehicles annually. The BMW 328i ActiveFlex car is a [[Flexible-fuel vehicles in Brazil|flex fuel]] version of [[BMW 3 Series (F30)|BMW 3 series]] specifically designed for the Brazilian market, which allows the vehicle to run on any blend of gasoline and ethanol up to [[Neat ethanol fuel|E100 ethanol]].<ref>[https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/pressDetail.html?title=bmw-group-assembles-first-car-in-brazil&outputChannelId=6&id=T0194443EN&left_menu_item=node__4088 BMW Group assembles first car in Brazil]. press.bmwgroup.com, retrieved 21 July 2015</ref> BMW produces the 3 Series, [[BMW X1|X1]], [[BMW X3|X3]], [[BMW X4|X4]] and [[BMW X5|X5]] plug-in hybrid PHEV electrified, same as of what is manufactured in [[Spartanburg, South Carolina]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Boeriu |first=Horatiu |date=2024-04-12 |title=Plant Araquari in Brazil Will Build the BMW X5 PHEV |url=https://www.bmwblog.com/2024/04/12/plant-araquari-in-brazil-will-build-the-bmw-x5-phev/ |access-date=2024-10-10 |website=BMW BLOG |language=en-US}}</ref>
