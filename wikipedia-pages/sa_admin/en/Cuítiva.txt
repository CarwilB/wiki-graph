'''Cuítiva''' is a town and municipality in the [[Sugamuxi Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at an altitude of {{convert|2750|m|ft}} and a distance of {{convert|85|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. The altitudes of the municipality range from {{convert|2727|m|ft}} to {{convert|3200|m|ft}} and it borders [[Iza, Boyacá|Iza]] in the north, [[Sogamoso]] in the east, [[Aquitania, Boyacá|Aquitania]] in the west and [[Tota, Boyacá|Tota]] in the south.<ref name=websiteCuitiva>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.cuitiva-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cuítiva] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150603173659/http://www.cuitiva-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-06-03 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Cuítiva is derived from [[Chibcha language|Muysccubun]] and means "Similarity to the chief".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Cuítiva] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area of Cuítiva before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their loose [[Muisca Confederation]]. Nearby ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]] ruled over Cuítiva.

Modern Cuítiva was founded by [[Juan de San Martín]] on January 19, 1550.<ref name=websiteCuitiva/>

On the central square a monument honouring messenger god in the [[Muisca religion|religion]] of the Muisca; [[Bochica]] has been erected.

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Cuítiva are [[agriculture]]; [[potato]]es, [[pea]]s, [[wheat]], [[maize]] and [[Welsh onion]]s and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteCuitiva/>
