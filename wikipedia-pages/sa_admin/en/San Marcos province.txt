'''San Marcos''' is one of the thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Cajamarca Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was created by Law No. 23508 on December 11, 1982, by president [[Fernando Belaunde Terry]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de San Marcos, ''[http://www.munisanmarcos.gob.pe/historia.php Historia]{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}''. Retrieved on November 4, 2007.</ref> It has a mountainous territory which varies in height from 1,500 to more than 4,000 meters {{nowrap|(5,000–13,000 ft)}} above sea level. The province is crossed by several rivers, the most important of which is the [[Marañón River|Marañón]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Municipalidad Provincial de San Marcos, ''[http://www.munisanmarcos.gob.pe/ubicacion.php Ubicación]{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}''. Retrieved on November 4, 2007.</ref>

* [[Gregorio Pita District|Gregorio Pita]] ([[Paucamarca]])
* [[Ichocán District|Ichocan]] ([[Ichocán]])
* [[José Manuel Quiroz District|José Manuel Quiroz]] ([[Shirac]])
* [[José Sabogal District|José Sabogal]] ([[Venecia, San Marcos Province|Venecia]])
