==Conservation==

The municipality contains roughly half of the {{convert|216601|ha|adj=on}} [[Mulata National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Mulata – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=FLONA de Mulata|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4750|access-date=2016-06-01}}</ref>
The north of the municipality contains part (13.31%) of the {{convert|4245819|ha|adj=on}} [[Grão-Pará Ecological Station]], the largest fully protected tropical forest conservation unit on the planet.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|title=Estação Ecológica Grão-Pará|publisher=Ideflor-bio (Government of Pará)|url=http://ideflorbio.pa.gov.br/unidades-de-conservacao/regiao-administrativa-calha-norte-iii/estacao-ecologica-grao-para/|access-date=2016-05-12|archive-date=2020-09-26|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200926143414/https://ideflorbio.pa.gov.br/unidades-de-conservacao/regiao-administrativa-calha-norte-iii/estacao-ecologica-grao-para/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
It contains 2% of the {{convert|3172978|ha|adj=on}} [[Trombetas State Forest]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES do Trombetas – ISA}}|language=pt |title=FES do Trombetas|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/es/uc/6782|access-date=2016-09-07}}</ref>
