'''Águas de Lindoia''' (''Lindoia Waters'') is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]]. The population is 18,808 (2020 est.) in an area of 60.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/232W27 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística] {{Dead link|fix-attempted=yes|date=February 2024}}</ref> It is a tourist spot in part due to its [[hot spring]]s, being part of the ''Circuito das Águas'', also including the cities of [[Amparo, São Paulo|Amparo]], [[Jaguariúna]], [[Lindoia]], [[Campina do Monte Alegre|Monte Alegre]], [[Pedreira, São Paulo|Pedreira]], [[Serra Negra]] and [[Socorro, São Paulo|Socorro]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.circuitodasaguaspaulista.com.br/ |title=Guia de Hotéis do Circuito das Águas Paulista SP - Hotéis, Turismo e Lazer |publisher=Circuitodasaguaspaulista.com.br |access-date=2013-04-09 |archive-date=2013-04-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130430124041/http://www.circuitodasaguaspaulista.com.br/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

== History ==
Before the 20th-century, the location was only known by adventurers who crossed the woods searching for gold. Having contracted diseases during their journeys, these adventurers found the cure in the warm waters that flowed from the mountains. 

In 1909, an Italian doctor, Francisco Tozzi, learned from his own father, Henrique Tozzi, about the medicinal properties of the springs. Dr Francisco Tozzi lived in [[Serra Negra]], and ended up buying the region auctioned by the government, making the property a public space.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=350050# |title=Cidades@ :: Águas de Lindóia - SP|publisher=IBGE.gov.br|access-date=9 April 2013}}</ref>

== Leisure and business ==
165&nbsp;km from São Paulo City, Águas de Lindoia offers the hot springs, adventure sports, and [[agritourism]]. Its economy is based on tourism.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.censo2010.ibge.gov.br/amostra/ |title=Censo 2010 - Resultados Censo 2010 |publisher=Censo2010.ibge.gov.br |access-date=2013-04-09 |archive-date=2013-04-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130414092603/http://www.censo2010.ibge.gov.br/amostra/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

The city's population is 17,266 inhabitants. There are 1,500 apartments and 25 hotels with the capacity to host 5,000 guests per day, allowing it to host several large Congresses and conventions per year, some nearing in size to 4,000 participants.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.portalaguasdelindoia.com.br/ |title=Portal Águas de Lindóia |publisher=Portalaguasdelindoia.com.br |access-date=2013-04-09 |archive-date=2011-08-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110810025459/http://www.portalaguasdelindoia.com.br/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_Telefônica_Brasileira|Companhia Telefônica Brasileira]] until 1973, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-26|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

== Religion ==
{{see also|Religion in Brazil|Protestantism in Brazil|Roman Catholic Church in Brazil}}
'''[[Christianity]]''' is present in the city as follows:

=== Catholic Church ===
The Catholic church in the municipality is part of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Amparo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.catholic-hierarchy.org/diocese/dsapa.html|title=São Paulo (Archdiocese) [Catholic-Hierarchy]|access-date=2024-06-22|website=www.catholic-hierarchy.org}}</ref>

=== Protestant Church ===
The most diverse evangelical beliefs are present in the city, mainly [[Pentecostalism|Pentecostal]], including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assemblies of God in Brazil]] (the largest evangelical church in the country),<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://confradesp.com.br/campos-eclesiasticos/|title=Campos Eclesiásticos|date=2018-12-10|access-date=2024-06-22|website=CONFRADESP|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ad.org.br/locations/|title=Arquivos: Locais|access-date=2024-06-22|website=Assembleia de Deus Belém – Sede|language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Christian Congregation in Brazil]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br/localidade|title=Localidade - Congregação Cristã no Brasil|access-date=2024-06-22|website=congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> among others. These denominations are growing more and more throughout Brazil.
