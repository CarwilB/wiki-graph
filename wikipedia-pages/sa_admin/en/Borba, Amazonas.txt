'''Borba''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]] in [[northern Brazil]].

==Geography==

Borba is located on the banks of the [[Madeira River]] around {{convert|150|km|mi|abbr=on}} south of [[Manaus]]. Its population was 41,748 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/borba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 44,251&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>  The city is the seat of the [[Territorial Prelature of Borba]].

The municipality contains 46% of the {{convert|283117|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Madeira Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS do Rio Madeira – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS do Rio Madeira|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5487|accessdate=2016-09-01}}</ref>
It contains about 10% of the {{convert|513747|ha|adj=on}} [[Matupiri State Park]], created in 2009 along the [[Matupiri River]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES do Matupiri – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=PES do Matupiri|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4487|accessdate=2016-09-01}}</ref>
It fully contains the {{convert|179083|ha|adj=on}} [[Matupiri Sustainable Development Reserve]], created in 2009.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS do Matupiri – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RDS do Matupiri|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4480|accessdate=2016-09-01}}</ref>
It contain 21% of the {{convert|397557|ha|adj=on}} [[Igapó-Açu Sustainable Development Reserve]], also created in 2009.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Igapó-Açu – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RDS Igapó-Açu|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/2964|accessdate=2016-09-02}}</ref>
Borba also contains about 60% of the {{convert|896411|ha|adj=on}} [[Acari National Park]] created by president [[Dilma Rousseff]] in 2016 in the last week before her provisional removal from office.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA do Acari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=PARNA do Acari|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/605831|accessdate=2016-06-12}}</ref>

==History==
Founded in 1728 as the [[Jesuit]] mission Aldea Trocano by Portuguese Padre [[João Sampaio]]. In 1755, it became the first ''vila'' (Portuguese town) in what was then the new [[captaincy of Rio Negro]], named Borba.

==Economy==
The economy of Borba depends largely on fishing, agriculture, and extractive activities like rubber extraction and cocoa harvesting.

The city is served by [[Borba Airport]].
