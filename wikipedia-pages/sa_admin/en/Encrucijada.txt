'''Encrucijada''' ({{IPA|es|eŋkɾusiˈxaða}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1850<ref name=guije/> and established as a municipality in 1910.

==History==
The municipality was divided into the [[barrio]]s of Centro, [[Paso Real (Encrucijada)|Paso Real]], [[El Santo (Cuba)|El Santo]] and [[Vega Redonda (Cuba)|Vega Redonda]].<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vencrucijada/index.htm|title=Encrucijada|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-09|language=es}}</ref> After the 1977 administrative reform, [[Calabazar de Sagua]], part of Sagua la Grande, became part of it.

==Geography==
The municipality borders with [[Sagua La Grande|Sagua la Grande]], [[Cifuentes, Cuba|Cifuentes]], [[Santa Clara, Cuba|Santa Clara]] and [[Camajuaní]].<ref>{{OSM|n|5884794|Encrucijada}}</ref>

The Municipality has the [[Consejos populares]] (wards) of:
* [[Encrucijada Norte]] (North Encrucijada in English)
* [[Encrucijada Sur]] (South Encrucijada in English)
* [[Constancia (Encrucijada)|Abel Santamaría]]
* [[Dos Hermanas (Encrucijada)|Dos Hermanas]]
* [[La Sierra (Encrucijada)|La Sierra]]
* [[Calabazar de Sagua]]
* [[El Purio (Encrucijada)|El Purio]]
* [[El Santo (Cuba)|El Santo]]
* [[Emilio Córdova (Encrucijada)|Emilio Córdova]]

== Transportation ==
Encrucijada is on a rail line starting in Santa Clara, going to Crucero Margot, to [[Luis Arcos Bergnes, Cuba|Luis Arcos Bergnes]], [[Vega Alta, Cuba|Vega Alta]], [[Canoa, Cuba|Canoa]], [[Tuinicú (Encrucijada)|Tuinicu]], Constancia, Encrucijada, Mata, El Vaquerito, Aguada la Piedra, Cifuentes, San Diego del Valle, Conyedo, and finally back to Santa Clara.<ref>{{Citation |last=lezumbalaberenjena |title=Composed road map from a tour guide (1980's) |date=2017-11-09 |url=https://www.flickr.com/photos/lezumbalaberenjena/44038904924/ |access-date=2023-04-12}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2019 |title=Road guides of Cuba |url=http://itravelnetwork.us/document/guides/GC_2019_English.pdf}}</ref>

The municipality has 3 state highways, being the [[Carretera de Malezas]] (4–311), linking the municipality to the provincial capital, Santa Clara, Highway [[Highway 4–273 (Cuba)|4–273]], from Calabazar de Sagua to the municipality of Cifuentes, and the [[Circuito Norte]] (4–I–3) from Calabazar–Encrucijada–La Sierra and the municipalities of Sagua La Grande and Camajuaní.

==Demographics==
In 2004, the municipality of Encrucijada had a population of 33,641.<ref name=atenas>{{Cite web |url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060714001016/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm |archive-date=2006-07-14 |title=2004 Population trends, by Province and Municipality |author=Atenas.cu |year=2004 |access-date=2007-10-09 |language=es |url-status=dead }}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|345|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-09}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|97.5|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. The population number had dropped to 31,500 in 2022.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
