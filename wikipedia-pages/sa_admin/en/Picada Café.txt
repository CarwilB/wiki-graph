'''Picada Café''' or '''Kaffeeschneiss''' in the local German dialect (i.e. [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]]) is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is located around 80 kilometers from the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]].

==Economy==
The local economy depends largely on small farming;<ref name="Sinésio Geromir Klauck: A História Agrária da Picada do Café / RS">[http://www.lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/handle/10183/54677/000855473.pdf?sequence=1 ''A História Agrária da Picada do Café por Sinésio Geromir Klauck, curso de planejamento e gestão para o desenvolvimento rural - Plageder, Departamento de Ciências Econômicas, Faculdade de Ciências Econômicas, Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul''. Porto Alegre, Aug. 1, 2011.]</ref> and increasingly on national (internal) tourism which in turn revolves around its Germanic roots and traditions, perceived as exotic.

==Minority language==
Since its foundation in 1840s<ref name="Histórico: Picada do Café">[http://www.picadacafe.rs.gov.br/secao.php?pagina=2 Prefeitura Municipal de Picada do Café: Histórico (Website accessed on Nov. 10, 2013).]</ref> and from its pioneering days onward, the [[Riograndenser Hunsrückisch]] language, a Brazilian variety of German largely based on the [[Rhine Franconian dialects|Rhine Franonian]], has been present in this community.

There are many other municipalities with this bilingual profile throughout the state. In 2012 the state chamber of deputies voted unanimously in favor of recognizing this Germanic dialect an official historical culture good to be preserved.<ref name="rslei">[http://www.al.rs.gov.br/legis/M010/M0100018.asp?Hid_IdNorma=58094 LEI  14.061 - DECLARA INTEGRANTE DO PATRIMÔNIO HISTÓRICO E CULTURAL DO ESTADO DO RIO GRANDE DO SUL A “LÍNGUA HUNSRIK”, DE ORIGEM GERMÂNICA]</ref><ref name="rsleijulho2012">[http://www.al.rs.gov.br/legis/M010/M0100099.ASP?Hid_Tipo=TEXTO&Hid_TodasNormas=58094&hTexto=&Hid_IDNorma=58094 LEI Nº 14.061, de 23 de julho de 2012 - Declara integrante do patrimônio histórico e cultural do estado do Rio Grande do Sul a língua hunsrik, de origem germânica]</ref>
