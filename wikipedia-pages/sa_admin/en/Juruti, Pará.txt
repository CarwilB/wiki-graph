'''Juruti''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in western [[Pará]] state, [[Brazil]], on the [[Amazon River]]. This is the site of a new giant [[Alcoa]] [[bauxite]] mine that has changed the face of this previously neglected area.

The city is served by [[Juruti Airport]].
