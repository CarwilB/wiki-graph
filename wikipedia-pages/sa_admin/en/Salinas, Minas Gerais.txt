'''Salinas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northeast of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city is known nationally for the [[cachaça]] production.

==Important facts==
[[Image:CachacaJava.jpg|thumb|150px|right|A bottle of ''[[Cachaça]] Java'', produced in '''Salinas'''.]]
The elevation is 471 meters.  It became a municipality in 1880.  The postal code (CEP) is 39560–000.  Population density was around 18 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.

'''Neighboring municipalities'''
*Neighboring municipalities are: [[Taiobeiras]], [[Rubelita]], [[Santa Cruz de Salinas]], [[Novorizonte]], [[Fruta de Leite]], [[Rio Pardo de Minas]] and [[Comercinho]].

==Climate==
Salinas has a semi-arid climate with high temperatures almost all year round.  There is a period of severe drought and another with torrential and sporadic rains.  The average summer temperature high is 33&nbsp;°C and the average winter high is 18&nbsp;°C.<ref>[http://www.salinas.mg.gov.br/Informa%E7%F5es_Gerais/Informa%E7%F5es_gerais.htm Prefeitura Municipal de Salinas]</ref> The maximum temperature of 41.8&nbsp;°C (107.2&nbsp;°F) was recorded on November 18 and 24, 2023 in Salinas.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ogimet.com/cgi-bin/gsynres?ind=83441&ano=2023&mes=11&day=19&hora=20&min=0&ndays=30|title= 83441:   Salinas (Brazil)|author=<!--Not stated--> |date= 18 November 2023|website=ogimet.com |publisher=OGIMET |access-date= 26 November 2023|quote=}}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising (46,000 head in 2006), small industries and agriculture.  The main crops are bananas, citrus fruits, mangoes, beans, sugarcane, and corn.  The local rum (cachaça) is well known.  In the rural area there were 2,084 producers on 111,541 ha.  Natural pasture made up 62,000 ha, woodland 35,000 ha. and planted area 7,500 ha.  Around 6,000 persons worked in agriculture.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

In the urban area there were 4 banking agencies in 2006.  The GDP in 2005 was R$157,547.<ref name="IBGE"/>

==IBGE statistical microregion==
Salinas is also a statistical microregion ('''53''') consisting of 17  municipalities: [[Águas Vermelhas]], [[Berizal]], [[Curral de Dentro]], [[Divisa Alegre]], [[Fruta de Leite]], [[Indaiabira]], [[Montezuma, Minas Gerais|Montezuma]], [[Ninheira]], [[Novorizonte]], [[Rio Pardo de Minas]], [[Rubelita]], Salinas, [[Santa Cruz de Salinas]], [[Santo Antônio do Retiro]], [[São João do Paraíso]], [[Taiobeiras]], [[Vargem Grande do Rio Pardo]].  The population of this region was 180,136 in 2000 and the area is 17,883.30&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/salinas/ Citybrazil]</ref>

==Health and education==
This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.

*State ranking: 551 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 3,015 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000

*Illiteracy rate: 23.85% (15 years old or older)(2003) The rate for Minas Gerais was 11.96; the rate for Brazil was 13.63

==Transportation==
Salinas is located on highway BR-251.  The distance to [[Montes Claros]] is 210&nbsp;km.  The distance to the Rio-Bahia BR-116 is 108&nbsp;km.
*Important distances: Belo Horizonte/MG 631&nbsp;km; Vit. da Conquista/BA 250&nbsp;km; São Paulo/SP 1210&nbsp;km; Brasília/DF 720&nbsp;km; and Rio de Janeiro/RJ 1070&nbsp;km.

Salinas is served by [[Salinas Airport (Brazil)|Salinas Airport]].
