'''Salcabamba''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Sallqa Pampa''', meaning "wild plain") is one of sixteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Tayacaja Province]]  in the [[Huancavelica Region]]) of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (84.11%) learnt to speak in childhood, 15.49% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
