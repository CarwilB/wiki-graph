'''Orocó''' is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Its population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], is 14,129 and <!--, the density is /km<sup>2</sup>-->its area is 554.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[São Francisco Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]]   ('''N''');  Bahia state  ('''S''');  [[Cabrobó]]  ('''E''');  [[Santa Maria da Boa Vista]]  ('''W''')
* Area - 554.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 349 m
* Hydrography - [[Brigida River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hiperxerófila]].
* Climate - [[Semi arid]] ([[Sertão]]) hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 26.1 C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 576&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Orocó''' are based in general on commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[donkey]]s and [[pig]]s;  and plantations of [[onion]]s, [[melon]]s and [[banana]]s.
