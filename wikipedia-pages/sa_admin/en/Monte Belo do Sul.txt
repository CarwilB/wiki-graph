'''Monte Belo do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]], in the [[Serra Gaúcha]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 2,530, and the area of the municipality was 69.726 km².<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/monte-belo-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
