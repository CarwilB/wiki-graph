'''Palizada''' ({{IPA|es|paliˈsaða|lang|Palizada Pronunciation.ogg}}) is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] that make up the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. It is situated in the far south-west corner of the state. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Palizada, Campeche|Palizada]]. As of 2010, the population was 8,352.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

==Geography==
The municipality of Palizada borders to the west with the state of [[Tabasco]] and to the east with the neighbouring Campeche municipality of [[Carmen Municipality|Carmen]]. It covers a total surface area of 2,071.70 km<sup>2</sup>.

==Demographics==
As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 8,352.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Palizada|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=007|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=25 January 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200125232353/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Palizada had a population of 3,089.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Palizada, the municipality had 256 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
