'''Arrecifes Partido''' is a  [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north-east of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]]. It is at coordinates {{coord|34|04|S|60|07|W}}.

The provincial subdivision has a population of 27,279 inhabitants in an area of <span style="white-space:nowrap">1,183&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;(457&nbsp;sq&nbsp;mi)</span>, and its capital city is [[Arrecifes]], which is around {{convert|170|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The district was founded in 1901, and the people are known as ''arrecifeños''.

==Sports==
Arrecifes is home to [[Club Atlético Almirante Brown]] [[Football (soccer)|football]] club.
