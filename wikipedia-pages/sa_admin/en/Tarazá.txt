'''Taraza''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Bajo Cauca Antioquia|Bajo Cauca]] subregion of [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].  It lies {{convert|222|km}} from the city of [[Medellín]], the departmental capital, and has a land area of {{convert|1569|km2}}.  The municipality was separated from the municipality of [[Cáceres, Antioquia|Cáceres]] in 1979.

In April 2008, 24 people were arrested, 40 were injured and at least one was killed in farmers' protests here, instigated by [[Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia|FARC]], against the eradication of the [[coca]] crop in the area.  In the aftermath of this, the municipality declared a humanitarian crisis.<ref>[http://colombiareports.com/2008/04/24/farmers-protest-leaves-40-injured-in-taraza/ Farmers’ protest leaves 40 injured in Taraza]; 24 March 2008 <!-- Accessed 14 July 2008 --></ref><ref>[http://colombiareports.com/2008/04/25/humanitarian-crisis-in-taraza/ Humanitarian crisis in Taraza; 25 March 2008 <!-- Accessed 14 July 2008 -->]</ref>
