'''Conguaco''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It has an area of {{cvt|149.9|km2}}, and had a population of 30,281 as per 2023 estimate. The municipality lies close to the border with [[El Salvador]].

==History==
Several theories exist as to the origin of the name Conguaco. The name might mean "place of slings or grinding shells". As per another theory, it is derived from "guaco", the name of a medicinal plant used for stomach ailments in the region.<ref name="Hist"/>

The earlier inhabitants of the region were descendants of the [[Nahua people|Pipil]] and Pocoman ethnic groups, and the region was called as "El Cuato de Conguaco". The existing settlement was founded in 1825. Local mythology state that an image of [[Saint Peter|San Pedro]] appeared at a lagoon, and the people built an [[altar]], and a church later. After the church was built, the lagoon disappeared, which enabled the people to establish the settlement.<ref name="Hist"/>

The "pinoleada" is an annual festival celebrated on 24 April in honor of the patron [[Saint Michael|San Marcos]]. As per tradition, the dish of [[pinol]], which is made of [[maize]], and meat, is prepared and distributed to the people. After the feast, the image of San Marcos is taken on a procession.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Conguaco|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Conguaco municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-conguaco-jutiapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Conguaco is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|149.9|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Conguaco (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2213__conguaco/|access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> It is located {{cvt|112|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]] and {{cvt|65|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Jutiapa, Jutiapa|Jutiapa]]. It is bordered by the municipalities of [[Jalpatagua]] to the north and east, and [[Moyuta]] to the west. It shares land border with [[El Salvador]] to the south.<ref>{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Conguaco, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/geografia-del-municipio-de-conguaco-jutiapa/|trans-title=Geography of Conguaco municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level, Conguaco has a Tropical monsoon climate ([[Koppen Classification|Classification]]: Am). The district’s yearly temperature is {{cvt|26.31|C}} and it is 4.59% higher than Guatemala’s averages. Conguaco typically receives about {{cvt|82.03|mm}} of precipitation and has 129.43 rainy days (35.46% of the time) annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/conguaco|title=Conguaco|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Conguaco had an estimated population of 30,281 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 15,215 males and 15,066 females. About 36.7% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.4% was over the age of 65 years. About 87.5% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (12.5%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (96.1%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 77.2%. [[Xinca people|Xincas]] (49.3%) and [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (48.8%) formed the major ethnic groups. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (98.9%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
