'''San Miguel de Urcuquí Canton''', or '''Urkuki''',  is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Imbabura Province]].  Its seat is the town of [[Urcuquí]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 14,381<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and was 15,671 in the 2010 census.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php |title=Ecuador: Municipal Division (Provinces and Parishes) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |website=www.citypopulation.de |access-date=10 Jul 2017}}</ref> The area of the canton is {{convert|735.3|sqkm|sqmi}}.

San Miguel de Urcuquí is located in the [[Andes]] of northern Ecuador. The town and parish of Urcuqui had a population of 4,499 in 2000 and 5,205 in 2010 and has an elevation of {{convert|2295|m|ft}} above sea level. The town is located about {{convert|9|km|mi}} in straight line distance northwest of the city of [[Ibarra, Ecuador|Ibarra]], the capital of Imbabura province.<ref>Google Earth; https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 10 Jul 2017</ref>
