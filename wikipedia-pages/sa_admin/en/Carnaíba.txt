'''Carnaíba''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 19,533 inhabitants and the total area is 432.13&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" /> Its mayor is [[Wamberg Gomes]].

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Solidão]] and [[Paraíba]] state   ('''N''');  [[Custódia]]   ('''S''');  [[Afogados da Ingazeira]]  ('''E''');   [[Flores, Pernambuco|Flores]] and [[Quixaba]]  ('''E''')
* Area - 436.98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 485 m
* Hydrography - [[Pajeú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[Semi arid]]- hot
* Annual average temperature - 24.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 395&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Carnaíba''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[cattle]], [[goats]], [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[Maize|corn]], [[bean]]s, [[manioc]], and [[cashew nuts]].
