'''Máncora District''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in the [[Talara Province]] of the [[Piura Region]] in northwestern [[Peru]]. Its capital is the [[beach resort]] town of [[Máncora]]. It was officially established as a district on November 14, 1908.

==Boundaries==
Las Pocitas Beach is a special place of Mancora District.

* North: [[Zorritos District]] (in the [[Tumbes Region]])
* East: [[Marcavelica District]] ([[Sullana Province]])
* South: [[Los Organos District|Los Órganos District]]
* West: [[Pacific Ocean]]

==Demographics==
According to the [[Peru 2005 Census|2005 Census]], the district has 8,570 inhabitants.
