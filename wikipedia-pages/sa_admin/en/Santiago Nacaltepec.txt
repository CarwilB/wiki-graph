'''Santiago Nacaltepec''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 213,06&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of [[Cuicatlán District]] in the north of the [[Cañada, Oaxaca|Cañada Region]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 1,967.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20478a.htm |title=Santiago Nacaltepec |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=2023-06-12 |archive-date=May 17, 2004 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040517150113/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20478a.htm |language=es-mx}}</ref>
