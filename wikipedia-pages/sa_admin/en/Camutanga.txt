'''Camutanga''' is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 120&nbsp;km from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 202]]5) population of 7,962 people and one of the strongest GDP per capita of [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]].<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/camutanga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref><ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Itambé]] and [[Paraíba]] state    ('''N''');  [[Ferreiros, Pernambuco|Ferreiros]]   ('''S''');  [[Timbaúba]]   ('''W'''); [[Itambé]]   ('''E''')
* Area - 37.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 98 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 25.2 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 120&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Camutanga''' are based in  food and beverage industry, [[commerce]] and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]].
