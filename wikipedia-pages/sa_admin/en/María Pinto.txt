'''María Pinto''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Melipilla Province]] in central [[Chile]]'s [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Administration==
As a commune, María Pinto is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Cesar Araos Aguirre ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]),<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> and his council members are:<ref name="Official"/>
* Mario Donoso Araya ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Elias Jacob Hirmas (IND)
* Carlos Acevedo Barrera ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Margarita Cofré Olguin ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Sonia Vargas Farias ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]])
* Claudia Atabales Alarcón ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], María Pinto is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
