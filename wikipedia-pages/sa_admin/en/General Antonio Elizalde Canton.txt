'''General Antonio Elizalde Canton''' or '''Bucay Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Guayas Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Bucay, Ecuador|General Antonio Elizalde]] or Bucay.  Its population at the 2001 census was 8,696.<ref>[https://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
