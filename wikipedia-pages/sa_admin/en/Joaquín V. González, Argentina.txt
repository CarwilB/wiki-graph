'''Joaquín Victor González''' is a town and municipality in [[Salta Province]] in northwestern [[Argentina]].<ref>[http://www.mininterior.gov.ar/ Ministerio del Interior] {{in lang|es}}</ref> It is located in and is the capital of [[Anta Department]]. It was founded as '''Laguna Blanca''' (Spanish for ''White Lagoon'') but changed to be named after [[Joaquin V. Gonzalez]] after a railroad was built in the area. Its industries are manufacturing of wood-based goods.<ref name="Joaquín V. González">{{cite web |title=Joaquín V. González - Provincia de Salta |url=https://www.edisalta.ar/jvgonzalez.htm |website=www.edisalta.ar |access-date=15 May 2023 |language=es}}</ref> Its population is 21,045 with 10,567 men and 10,478 women.<ref name="municipalidad">{{cite web |title=Joaquín V. González en la region de Salta - Municipio y gobierno municipal de Argentina |url=https://www.municipalidad-argentina.com.ar/municipalidad-joaquin-victor-gonzalez.html |website=www.municipalidad-argentina.com.ar |access-date=15 May 2023}}</ref>
