'''Ilha Comprida''' (Portuguese for "Long Island") is a [[municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. Stretching southwest along the [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic]] coast of the state, it is coextensive with the [[barrier island]] of Ilha Comprida, the longest of its kind in the state ({{convert|74|km|disp=sqbr}}). The municipality was founded in 1993 after the merger of the parts of Ilha Comprida that belonged to the city of [[Cananéia]] with the sections of the island that were administered by the city of [[Iguape]].<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/B3ZM IBGE], history</ref>

The Ilha Comprida has an average annual temperature of 24 °C. The population is 13,419 (2022) in an area of 196.567km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cod.ibge.gov.br/23558 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The island is covered by the [[Ilha Comprida Environmental Protection Area]], which defines zoning and allowable land use in an effort to reduce environmental damage from human occupation.<ref>{{citation |title=A criação da APA de Ilha Comprida e seus desdobramentos no atual processo de desenvolvimento local
|last1=Lima |first1=Cibele Oliveira|first2=Regina Célia de |last2=Oliveira|year=2013
|url=http://observatoriogeograficoamericalatina.org.mx/egal14/Procesosambientales/Impactoambiental/21.pdf|access-date=2016-11-20}}</ref> The municipality contains 55% of the {{convert|455|ha|adj=on}} [[Guará Area of Relevant Ecological Interest]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ARIE Guará (ARIE) – ISA}}|language=pt
|title=ARIE Guará (ARIE)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
|url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/587135|access-date=2016-11-26}}</ref> It is part of the [[Iguape-Cananéia-Paranaguá estuary lagoon complex]].<ref>{{citation|work=Malucos por Aventura|title=CICLOTURISMO Lagamar - Ilha Comprida, Ilha de Cananéia, Ilha do Cardoso, Ilha de Superagui, Ilha das Peças e Ilha do Mel|url=https://malucosporaventura.blogspot.ca/2010/09/cicloturismo-lagamar-ilha-comprida-ilha.html|access-date=2016-11-26}}</ref>

Due to its importance and because it is completely located in an [[Environmental protection area (Brazil)|environmental protection area]], Ilha Comprida was declared a [[Nature reserve|Biosphere Reserve]] by [[UNESCO]]. It preserves the last [[Dune|dunes]] in the state.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-10-22 |title=Ilha Comprida guarda mistérios e as últimas dunas do Estado de SP |url=https://g1.globo.com/sp/campinas-regiao/terra-da-gente/noticia/2021/10/22/ilha-comprida-guarda-misterios-e-as-ultimas-dunas-do-estado-de-sp.ghtml |access-date=2024-02-11 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-06-14|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012. The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-06-14|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
