'''San Cristóbal''' is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] of the [[Alto Paraná Department]], [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|926.8|km2}} and is one of the most isolated districts in the department. As per the 2022 census, it had a population of 8,563 individuals.

==Geography==
San Cristóbal is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|926.8|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web|title=Alto Paraná: distritos, colonización agrícola y expansión del territorio productivo|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/escolar/segundo-ciclo/ciencias-sociales/2016/09/05/decimo-departamento-alto-parana/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Alto Paraná: districts, agricultural colonization and expansion of productive territory}}</ref><ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Cristóbal District population and profile|work=CityPopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/alto_paran%C3%A1/1012__san_crist%C3%B3bal/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=en}}</ref> It is located in a remote and isolated location in southern part of the department.<ref name="ABC">{{cite web|title=San Cristóbal, ciudad aislada|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/tres-fronteras/san-cristobal-ciudad-aislada-1335302.html|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=San Cristóbal, isolated town}}</ref>

It is located at an elevation of {{cvt|282.62|m}} above sea level. The district has a tropical savanna climate ([[Koppen Classification]]: Aw). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|24.11|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|15.18|cm}} and has 143.25 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/paraguay/caazapa/san-cristobal|title=San Cristobal|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Infrastructure and services==
San Cristóbal is characterized by deficient public infrastructure and other services including proper road connections, and healthcare facilities. Due to this isolation, residents and local businesses have often been forced to self-organize support systems, including voluntary monthly contributions to help maintain public operations such as policing due to limited state allocation.<ref name="ABC"/>

==Demographics ==
As per the 2022 census, San Cristóbal district had a population of 8,563 inhabitants of which 4,433 were males and 4,130 were females. About 82.7% of the population was classified rural, and the rest (17.3%) lived in urban areas. About 28.6% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.7% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/> The economy is dependent on [[agriculture]] particularly [[wheat]] production.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios_edicion_impresa/2025/05/09/mas-del-50-del-trigo-nacional-lo-produce-las-cooperativas/|title=Más del 50 % del trigo nacional lo produce las cooperativas|work=[[La Nación (Paraguay)|La Nación]]|date=9 May 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref>
