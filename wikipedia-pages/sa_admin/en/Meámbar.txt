'''Meámbar''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua (department)|Comayagua]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|409.7|km2}} and had a population of 12,522 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
[[Petroglyphs]] and other archeological evidence from the [[pre-Columbian period]] have been found between the villages of Loma Alta and Palmital in the municipality. The history of the first known settlement goes back to 1630 to the indigenous village of Meámbar, which was located east to the current settlement on the right bank of the Piojo River. "Meámbar" roughly translates to "water of the corn and sugarcane" in the [[Mesoamerican language]]. In 1801, Alonso Oseguera de Contreras established the municipality on the west bank of the Piojo River. During the Spanish colonial period, it served as a strategic transit center due to its central location, and became a major commercial hub.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Meámbar, Comayagua|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Comayagua/Meámbar.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Meámbar|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-meambar/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Meámbar is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua Department|Comayagua]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|409.7|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/comayagua/0310__me%C3%A1mbar/|title=Meámbar, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It is located in the northwestern part of the department, and borders the [[Cortés department]] to the north and west. The Laguna Seca Mountain mountains are located in the west central part of the municipality. It is traversed by the Maragua and Piojo rivers, and the water storage area of the Francisco Morazán Dam, which is located in the nearby municipality of [[Santa Cruz de Yojoa]], occupies majority of the north western part of the municipality. Approximately 80% of the land area is covered by broad leaf forests, and the Meámbar Azul National Park is located next to the along [[Lake Yojoa]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>
<ref>{{cite news|url=https://proceso.hn/insep-avanza-con-la-construccion-de-puente-sobre-el-rio-piojo-en-el-municipio-de-meambar-comayagua/|title=INSEP avanza con la construcción de puente sobre el Rio Piojo en el municipio de Meambar, Comayagua|work=Proceso Digital|date=21 October 2016|access-date=26 November 2025|language=es|trans-title=INSEP advances with the construction of a bridge over the Rio Piojo in the municipality of Meambar, Comayagua}}</ref> 

Meámbar has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|23.03|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|266.57|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/comayagua/meambar|title=Meámbar, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Meámbar had a total population of 12,522 inhabitants, of whom 6,398 (51.1%) were men and 6,124 (48.9%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 5,265 individuals (42.0%) were aged 0–14 years, 6,657 individuals (53.2%) were aged 15–64, and 599 individuals (4.8%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 18.0 years and the mean age was 23.5 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 22.2%, significantly higher than the departmental average of 14.6%. The municipality had 2,544 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.8 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

The economy is dependent on [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing. Major agricultural produce include [[sugarcane]], [[coffee]], [[cucumber]], and [[beet]].<ref name="Red"/>
