'''La Florida''' ({{IPA|es|la floˈɾiða}}, [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "the flowery") is an urban-suburban [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in the South East of [[Santiago]]. It is a residential area and its inhabitants are mostly members of a new middle to [[upper middle class|upper-middle class]]. It ranks number 12 out of 346 among Chilean communes in terms of [[Human Development Index]].<ref name="HumanDev"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], La Florida spans an area of {{convert|70.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 382,701 inhabitants. 100% lived in [[urban area]]s and 52.1% were women. It currently has 402,433 inhabitants,<ref name="Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional">{{Cite web|last=Chile|first=BCN Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de|date=June 2020|title=Reportes Estadísticos 2020 de La Florida Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile|url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/reportescomunales/comunas_v.html?anno=2020&idcom=13110|access-date=2021-01-18|website=bcn.cl}}</ref> which makes it the fourth most populous commune in Chile, after [[Puente Alto]], [[Maipú, Chile|Maipú]] and [[Santiago (commune)|Santiago Centro]].

==Administration==
As a commune, La Florida is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. Its mayor for the 2024-2028 term is Daniel Reyes Morales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]). The local council has the following members:

'''Conservatives (6)'''
* Felipe Mancilla Mejías ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Renata Santander Ramírez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Leticia Lagos Vidal ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Hugo Herrera Baeza ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Alejandra Parra Galasso ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Martina Díaz Carrasco ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])

'''Social Democrats (4)'''
* Reinaldo Rosales Méndez ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PDD]])
* Lia Gálvez Fuentes  ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* José Seves Rojas ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Victoria Oyarzún Carvajal ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], La Florida is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gustavo Hasbún ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Camila Vallejo-Dowling ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]]) as part of the 26th District, which consists entirely of the La Florida commune. It is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Manuel José Ossandón (Conservative independent) and Carlos Montes (PS) as part of the 8th Senatorial Constituency (East Santiago).
