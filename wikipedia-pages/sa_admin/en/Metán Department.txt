'''Metán''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], in [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|5235|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the departments of [[General Güemes Department, Salta|General Güemes]] and [[Anta Department|Anta]], to the east with Anta, to the south with [[Santiago del Estero Province]] and [[Rosario de la Frontera Department]], and to the west with [[Guachipas Department]], [[La Viña Department]], and [[Capital Department, Salta|Capital Department]].

== History ==
The department was created during the government of [[Manuel Solá]] (1859-1860) with territories ceded by the [[Rosario de la Frontera Department]].<ref>[http://www.camdipsalta.gov.ar/INFSALTA/Gobernadores2.htm "Gobernadores de Salta 1810- 1900 "] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090604040947/http://www.camdipsalta.gov.ar/INFSALTA/Gobernadores2.htm |date=2009-06-04 }} ''Cámara de Diputados de la Provincia de Salta''</ref>
