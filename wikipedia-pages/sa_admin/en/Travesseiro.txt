'''Travesseiro''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].

==History==
Colonised by [[Germans|German]] and [[Italians|Italian]] immigrants, the municipality freed itself from Arroio do Meio in 1992, the result of an free movement born in the 1980s. The name is due to being it being a place of crossing the Jacaré stream, current [[Forqueta River]], being a point of passage through the water.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://gauchazh.clicrbs.com.br/colunistas/leandro-staudt/noticia/2022/10/por-que-o-nome-de-um-municipio-gaucho-e-travesseiro-cl9fwh1c5005t018wc5wpcak0.html |title= Por que o nome de um município gaúcho é Travesseiro|date=October 2022|accessdate= 16 September 2025}}</ref>
