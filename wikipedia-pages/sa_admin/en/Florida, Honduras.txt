'''Florida''' ({{IPA|es|floˈɾiða}}) is a town, with a population of 6,384 (2013 census),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/copan/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the Department of Copán with at least 200 inhabitants</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the western [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Copán department|Copán]].

Its products include tobacco, corn and cattle.

The [[Fiesta patronal|''feria patronal'']], or fair in honor of the town's patron saint, is towards the end of April when one may enjoy aubades, ''[[carrera de cintas|carreras de cintas]]'', climbing greased poles, the coronation of the queen of the fair, [[rodeo]]s, [[sack race]]s, and so forth.
