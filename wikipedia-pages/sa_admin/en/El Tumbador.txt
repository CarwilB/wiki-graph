'''El Tumbador''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]]. The population is mostly of [[Mam people]], who speak their own language. It was founded in 1878.

The main source of income is agriculture—farming and animal husbandry. People produce mostly coffee, but also [[sugarcane]], beans, [[cassava]], fruits and spices—[[cardamom]] and [[macadamia]]. Cattle, horses, sheep and goats are bred in the area as well.

El Tumbador gave its name to the [[Belgium|Belgian]] [[Non-governmental organization]] [[Tumbador vzw]] that used to support a project in the municipality.
