'''Weg naar Zee''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 16,037, and has a large [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]] population.<ref name="census"/> The name can be translated as "road to sea", and it is indeed the road to the sea. Weg naar Zee is home to the Open Air [[Crematorium]] which opened in 1968, and there is a pilgrimage site for [[Hindu]]s nearby.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surivision.nl/weg-naar-zee/|title=Weg naar Zee|website=Suri Vision|date=12 November 2017 |access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1968, oil had been discovered in Weg naar Zee, and is being exploited by [[Staatsolie]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://columbus-erp.com/operations/suriname/weg-naar-zee/|title=Weg naar Zee|website=Columbus Energy Resources|access-date=28 May 2020|archive-date=28 May 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200528063001/https://columbus-erp.com/operations/suriname/weg-naar-zee/|url-status=dead}}</ref> 

==Environment==
In 2015, a [[mangrove]] conservation effort was launched. The rising sea tide due to climate change, and the decline for the forests, increase the risk of flooding. The project aims to increase the number of mangrove forests near the coast.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fernandesbottling.com/blog/bescherming-van-de-kust-bij-weg-naar-zee/|title=Bescherming van de kust bij Weg naar Zee|website=Fernandes Bottling|date=15 July 2015 |access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> A part of the resort is still an agricultural area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-west|website=Vakantie Arena|title=Paramaribo-West|access-date=28 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> [[Conservation International Suriname]] (CI-Suriname) and partners use sediment-trapping structures (including permeable dams) and mangrove rehabilitation to reduce coastal erosion and support mangrove regrowth.<ref name="Wetlands2016-BWN">{{cite web |title=Building with Nature concept successfully introduced in Suriname |website=Wetlands International |date=14 June 2016 |url=https://www.wetlands.org/blog/building-with-nature-concept-successfully-introduced-in-suriname/}}</ref><ref name="Kosmos2018-Mangroves">{{cite web |title=Mangrove Rehabilitation in Suriname |website=Kosmos Energy |date=30 November 2018 |url=https://www.kosmosenergy.com/spotlight/mangrove-rehabilitation-in-suriname/}}</ref> Studies have discussed lessons from permeable dams on mangrove-mud coasts and mapped mangrove dynamics along the Suriname coast using satellite data.<ref name="WinterwerpPermeableDams2020">{{cite journal
