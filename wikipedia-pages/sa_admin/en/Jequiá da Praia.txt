'''Jequiá da Praia''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒikiˈa ˈdɐ ˈpɾajɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located on the coast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its area is 339 km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref> and in 2020 it had a population of 11,536.

The municipal seat lies on the [[Jequiá River (Alagoas)|Jequiá River]]. The municipality contains the {{convert|10231|ha}} [[Lagoa do Jequiá Marine Extractive Reserve]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Marinha da Lagoa do Jequiá – ISA}}|language=pt
