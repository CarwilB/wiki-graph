'''Pisba''' (sometimes spelled '''Pisva''') is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the subregion of [[La Libertad Province]]. Pisba is situated in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] at altitudes between {{convert|2400|m|ft}} and {{convert|3500|m|ft}}. Distance to [[Sogamoso]] is {{convert|128|km|mi}} and to [[Tunja]] {{convert|176|km|mi}}. The municipality borders [[Paya, Boyacá|Paya]] in the east, [[Labranzagrande]] in the south and [[Mongua]] in the northwest.<ref name=websitePisba>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150530082807/http://www.pisba-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Pisba]</ref> The [[Casanare Department|Casanare]] municipality [[Támara, Casanare|Támara]] borders Pisba in the north.

== History ==
Pisba is located in the east of Boyacá and in this area the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] inhabiting the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] bordered the homelands of the [[U'wa people|U'wa]] in the north and the [[Achagua people|Achagua]] in the east. The name is derived from the [[Chibcha language]] and means "Honourable domain from before".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Pisba] - Excelsio.net</ref> At the arrival of the Spanish, it is said the Muisca worked hard in [[Muisca agriculture|agriculture]], mainly [[maize]], [[yuca]], [[banana]]s and predominantly [[cotton]]. Pisba was ruled by the ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]].

Modern Pisba was founded on April 3, 1629.<ref name=websitePisba/>

== Nature ==
The national park [[National Natural Park Páramo de Pisba|Páramo de Pisba]] is located close to Pisba and named after it.

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Pisba are [[agriculture]], [[silviculture]] and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websitePisba/>
