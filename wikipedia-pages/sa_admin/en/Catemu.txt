'''Catemu''' is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[San Felipe de Aconcagua Province]] of central [[Chile]]'s [[Valparaíso Region]].

==Geography==
Catemu spans an area of {{convert|361.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, the Catemu commune had 12,112 inhabitants; of these, 6,706 (55.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 5,406 (44.6%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 6,172 men and 5,940 women. The population grew by 7.2% (817 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Catemu is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]], administered by a communal council (''consejo comunal''), which is headed by a directly elected [[alcalde]]. The current alcalde is Boris Luksic Nieto. The communal council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* María Salas Herrera
* Pablo Pacheco Delgado
* Aurora Medina Carvajal
* Luís René Carvajal Leiva
* Claudio Núñez Cataldo
* María Sánchez Contreas

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Catemu is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Marco Antonio Núñez ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Gaspar Rivas ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 11th electoral district, together with [[Los Andes, Chile|Los Andes]], [[San Esteban, Chile|San Esteban]], [[Calle Larga]], [[Rinconada]], [[San Felipe, Chile|San Felipe]], [[Putaendo]], [[Santa María, Chile|Santa María]], [[Panquehue]] and [[Llaillay]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Ignacio Walker|Ignacio Walker Prieto]] ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Lily Pérez San Martín]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 5th senatorial constituency (Valparaíso-Cordillera).{{Update after|2021|9|5}}
