'''Pesquería''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] of the state of [[Nuevo León]] in [[Mexico]]. It is located at  25º47´N  100º3´W and {{Convert|330|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.geonames.org/8582461/pesqueria.html|title=GeoNames.org|website=www.geonames.org|language=en|access-date=2017-10-20}}</ref> It is part of the suburban area of the [[Monterrey Metropolitan area]]. Its distance to the [[Monterrey]] city downtown area is {{Convert|36|km|mi}}. To the east of Pesquería is [[Los Ramones]], to the north is Doctor González, to the west are [[Apodaca]] and [[Monterrey International Airport]], and to the south is [[Cadereyta Jiménez, Nuevo León|Cadereyta Jiménez]], all of which are in Nuevo León.

The river [[Pesquería River|Pesquería]] flows through the region.

== Demographics ==
{{See|Koreans in Mexico}}
The creation of a new automotive plant by [[Kia|KIA]] has led to an influx of [[Koreans in Mexico|South Korean immigrants]] and workers to the area. Colloquially, Pesquería has even been referred to as "[[Pescorea]]",<ref>{{cite web|title=Pesquería y su nueva realidad|url=http://www.zocalo.com.mx/new_site/articulo/de-pesqueria-a-pescorea-el-nuevo-pueblo-de-nl|website=Zócalo}}</ref> a [[portmanteau]] of "Pesquería" and "Corea", the Spanish word for Korea.

== Economy ==
The most important economic activities in the municipality of [[Pesquería River|Pesqueria]] are cattle-breeding (about 20,000 hectares) and agriculture (about 10,000 hectares).

Suitable [[clay]] deposits enabled founding of some works producing bricks and tiles of good quality for local consumption as well as for export.

An automotive plant was built by the Korean [[Kia|KIA]] Motors company in 2016 for a planned production of 300,000 cars annually.
