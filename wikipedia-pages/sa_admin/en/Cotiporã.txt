'''Cotiporã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,838.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/cotipora/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

== Gem robbery ==
A group of attackers forced open the door of a gem factory and put on the run from the police, nine people hostage. The attack took place on Sunday the 30 December 2012 in the village of the state of Rio Grande do Sul.<ref>[https://archive.today/20130221022200/http://www.mmail.com.my/story/gang-hits-gem-cutting-plant-brazil-flees-hostages-42153 Gang hits gem-cutting plant in Brazil, flees with hostages]</ref> After the attackers had blown open the door to the factory, the officers opened fire. Killing three attackers, some robbers managed to escape and in a nearby bar and a house they took nine people hostage. Among them were two men, six women and an eleven-year-old girl.<ref>[http://www.straitstimes.com/breaking-news/world/story/gang-hits-gem-cutting-plant-brazil-flees-hostages-20121231 Gang hits gem-cutting plant in Brazil, flees with hostages]</ref> The nine hostages were found on Sunday night unharmed. The police were on Monday the 31 December still after the fugitive offenders.<ref>[http://www.news24.com/World/News/Hostages-taken-from-Brazil-gem-cutting-plant-20121230 Hostages taken from Brazil gem-cutting plant | News24]</ref>
