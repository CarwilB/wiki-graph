'''Ángel R. Cabada''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. The municipal seat is at [[Ángel R. Cabada]].

The municipality of Ángel R. Cabada is located about 207&nbsp;km southeast from the state capital [[Xalapa]], and is in the [[Papaloapan River]] region between [[Lerdo de Tejada, Veracruz|Lerdo de Tejada]] and [[San Andrés Tuxtla]] on the Federal Highway. It is bordered to the north by the [[Gulf of Mexico]], to the south by [[Santiago Tuxtla]], and to the west by [[Saltabarranca]] and [[Lerdo de Tejada, Veracruz|Lerdo de Tejada]].<ref name="elocal">{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30015a.html |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ángel R. Cabada |publisher=[[INAFED]] |language=Spanish |accessdate=22 May 2009}}</ref> 

The municipality produces [[maize]] and [[beans]].
