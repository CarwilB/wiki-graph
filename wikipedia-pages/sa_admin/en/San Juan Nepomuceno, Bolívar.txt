'''San Juan Nepomuceno''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]].  It is named after [[Saint John of Nepomuk]].

==Notable residents==
[[Mayerlis Angarita]] was born here in about 1980. She has won awards for her leadership on women's rights and she has been a mayoral candidate.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Mayerlis Angarita Robles|url=https://lasillavacia.com/quienesquien/perfilquien/mayerlis-angarita-robles|access-date=2021-03-19|website=La Silla Vacía|language=es}}</ref> Artist [[Gabriel Sierra]] was born in 1975 in San Juan Nepomuceno.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Gabriel Sierra |url=https://www.guggenheim.org/artwork/artist/gabriel-sierra |access-date=2023-06-13 |website=The Guggenheim Museums and Foundation |language=en-US}}</ref>
