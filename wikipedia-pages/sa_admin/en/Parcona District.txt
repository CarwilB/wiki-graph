'''Parcona District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Ica Province|Ica]] in [[Peru]].  Parcona is the second smallest of the fourteen districts.  [[File:Parcona district Ica Province Ica Region Peru.png|thumbnail|Parcona district within Ica Province of Ica Region Peru.]]<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080417091049/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-17 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Parcona is an inland small area, 2.5&nbsp;km from the Ica District and inhabits 8,000 people.
