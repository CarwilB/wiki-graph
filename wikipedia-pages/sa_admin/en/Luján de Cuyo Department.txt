'''Luján de Cuyo''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the northwest of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 104,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|4,847|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Luján de Cuyo]], which is located around {{cvt|1,107|km}} from the [[Buenos Aires|Federal Capital]].
