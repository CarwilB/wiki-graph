'''Uibaí''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. It is located about {{convert|508|km}} byroad northwest of [[Salvador, Brazil|Salvador]]. It lies to the northeast of [[Gentio do Ouro]] and about {{convert|30|km}} south-southwest of [[Irecê]], the nearest city. [[Xique-Xique]] and the [[São Francisco River]] is to the northwest.

A [[rural area]], population growth has been slow; in 1950 it had a population of 11,890,<ref name="Silva1961">{{cite book|last=Silva|first=Fernando Altenfelder|title=Xique-Xique e Marrecas, duas comunidades do médio São Francisco|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=5jgsAAAAYAAJ|access-date=12 June 2013|year=1961|publisher=Comissão do Vale do São Francisco|pages=50–51}}</ref> and as of 2020 it had a population of 13,891 people.
