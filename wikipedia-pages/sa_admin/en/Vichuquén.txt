'''Vichuquén''' is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Curicó Province]] of [[Chile]]'s [[Maule Region]].

== History ==
The Spanish arrived along the Lico rivers in 1585, where settlements of the [[Mapuche]] and [[Inca]] already existed. In 1865, Vichuquén founded its capital of the village of the same name.{{Citation needed|date=September 2010}} In 1987, the old part of the town was declared a "Typical Zone Vichuquén" for its colonial style architecture.{{Citation needed|date=September 2010}}

== Geography ==
Vichuquén is located northwest of the Curicó Province. It also has the Vichuquén lakes [[:es:Lago Vichuquén|Lago Vichuquén (es)]] in its boundaries, as well as the [[Laguna Torca National Reserve]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|425.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Vichuquén spans an area of {{convert|425.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 4,916 inhabitants (2,596 men and 2,320 women). Of these, 1,368 (27.8%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,548 (72.2%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 0.3% (15 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Vichuquén is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Román Pavez López ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Vichuquén is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Roberto León ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Celso Morales ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 36th electoral district, together with [[Curicó]], [[Teno, Chile|Teno]], [[Romeral]], [[Molina, Chile|Molina]], [[Sagrada Familia, Chile|Sagrada Familia]], [[Hualañé]], [[Licantén]] and [[Rauco]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
