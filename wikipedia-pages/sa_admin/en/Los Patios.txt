'''Los Patios''' is a municipality and town in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Norte de Santander]], northeastern [[Colombia]]. As of 2012, it has a population of 77,588<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=1330558690&men=gcis&lng=en&des=gamelan&geo=-55&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=&srt=pnan |title=World Gazetteer |archiveurl=https://archive.today/20130217015244/http://www.world-gazetteer.com/wg.php?x=1330558690&men=gcis&lng=en&des=gamelan&geo=-55&srt=npan&col=abcdefghinoq&msz=1500&pt=c&va=&srt=pnan |archivedate=2013-02-17 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==
The history of this community begins in the 17th century. It began as the ''[[hacienda]]'' "Los Patios", which developed into a small country town, which later became a ''[[corregimiento]] de Villa del Rosario'' and still later the 37th municipality of Norte de Santander. According to historical records, the proprietors of the original ''hacienda'' included Juan Aranda, Carlos Matamoros, and Reinaldo Viccini, who engaged in agriculture, principally of [[Cacao bean|cacao]].

Other nearby ''haciendas'' now incorporated into the municipality were La Rinconada (now Tennis Club and the Colegio Santo Ángel), Kilómetro tres (now Urb. La Floresta), Los Colorados over the plain of the River Pamplonita; Los Vados; El Hato, Located on the land that is now the Kilómetro 8 neighborhood; El Suspiro (today Vereda Agua Linda); La Garita, Buenos Aires (near los Vados) and García, owned by Agustín García, now the recreational park San Rafael.
