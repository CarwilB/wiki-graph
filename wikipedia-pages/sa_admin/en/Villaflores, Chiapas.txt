'''Villaflores''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the southern [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], and the name of its largest settlement and seat of the municipal government. Situated in the [[Sierra Madre de Chiapas]] range, the municipality has an area of approximately 1232&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an average elevation of 540m [[above mean sea level]]. <ref>INAFED (2005)</ref>

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 98,618.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Villaflores|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=108|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=2014-04-23|archive-date=2016-03-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304092028/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=108|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Villaflores had a population of 37,237.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Villaflores, the municipality had 1,588 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Jesús María Garza, Chiapas|Jesús María Garza]] (6,724), [[Cristóbal Obregón, Chiapas|Cristóbal Obregón]] (4,664), [[Guadalupe Victoria (Lázaro Cárdenas), Chiapas|Guadalupe Victoria (Lázaro Cárdenas)]] (3,583), [[Benito Juárez, Chiapas|Benito Juárez]] (3,567), [[Ciudad Cuauhtémoc, Chiapas|Cuauhtémoc]] (3,084), [[Nuevo México, Chiapas|Nuevo México]] (3,014), [[Doctor Domingo Chanona, Chiapas|Doctor Domingo Chanona]] (2,962), [[Villa Hidalgo (Villaflores), Chiapas|Villa Hidalgo]] (2,502), classified as urban, and [[Roblada Grande, Chiapas|Roblada Grande]] (1,729), [[Joaquín Miguel Gutiérrez, Chiapas|Joaquín Miguel Gutiérrez]] (1,663), [[Francisco Villa, Chiapas|Francisco Villa]] (1,360), [[Libertad Melchor Ocampo, Chiapas|Libertad Melchor Ocampo]] (1,324), [[Agrónomos Mexicanos, Chiapas|Agrónomos Mexicanos]] (1,202), [[Dieciséis de Septiembre, Chiapas|Dieciséis de Septiembre]] (1,177), [[Ignacio Zaragoza, Chiapas|Ignacio Zaragoza]] (1,055), and [[Calzada Larga, Chiapas|Calzada Larga]] (1,049), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
