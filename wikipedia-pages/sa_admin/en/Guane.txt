'''Guane''' is a municipality and town in the [[Pinar del Río Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It was founded in 1602.<ref name="guije">{{Cite web |author=Guije.com |title=Guane |url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/pguane/index.htm |accessdate=2007-10-07 |language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
The wards of the municipality include Punta de La Sierra, Portales, [[Guane 1]], Guane 2, Combate la Teneria, Isabel Rubio, Sabalo, and Mollna. <ref>{{Cite web |last=Zabala |first=Teresa Padron |date=2012-11-15 |title=La mujer y su participación en los procesos de dirección en el territorio en Guane |url=https://www.monografias.com/trabajos94/mujer-y-su-participacion-procesos-direccion-territorio-guane/mujer-y-su-participacion-procesos-direccion-territorio-guane |access-date=2022-10-20 |website=Monografias.com |language=es}}</ref>

==History==
Guane is a Cuban Indigenous name. Before it was renamed Pinar del Río in 1774, the area was known as the old colonial capital of Guane.

The municipality was formerly divided into the [[Former barrios of Cuba|barrios]] of Cabo de San Antonio y La Fe, Catalina, [[Cortés, Pinar del Río, Cuba|Cortés]], Hato de Guane, Isabel Rubio (Paso Real de Guane), Juan Gómez, La Grifa, Las Martinas, Los Acostas, Manuel Lazo (Cayuco), Portales, Pueblo, Punta de la Sierra, Sábalo and Tenería.<ref name="guije" />

It was the main city of Filipino migrants to Cuba, who were known as '''Chinos Manila''' among the local population. The Filipinos worked the huge tobacco plantations much as they did in their home country, in the present provinces of [[Ilocos Sur]], [[Tarlac]], [[Ilocos Norte]], [[Cagayan]] and [[Isabela (province)|Isabela]].  During that time, the Spanish colonizers had a virtual monopoly of the tobacco trade through their company, the [[Tabacalera]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Guane had a population of 34,922.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|717|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|49|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
