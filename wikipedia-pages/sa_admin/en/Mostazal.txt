'''Mostazal''' ({{IPA|es|mostaˈsal}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Cachapoal Province]], [[O'Higgins Region]]. The capital of the commune is the [[List of cities in Chile|city]] of '''San Francisco de Mostazal'''.

==Geography==
The commune spans an area of {{convert|523.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/> The ''Plaza de San Francisco'' has an elevation of {{convert|500|m|0|abbr=on}}.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Mostazal has 21,866 inhabitants (11,038 men and 10,828 women). Of these, 17,903 (81.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,963 (18.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 20.6% (3,728 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Mostazal is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Sergio Medel Acosta. The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* Santiago Garate
Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Mostazal is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Eugenio Bauer ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Ricardo Rincón  ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 33rd electoral district, together with [[Graneros]], [[Codegua]], [[Machalí]], [[Requínoa]], [[Rengo]], [[El Olivar, Chile|Olivar]], [[Doñihue]], [[Coínco|Coinco]], [[Coltauco]], [[Quinta de Tilcoco]] and [[Malloa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by [[Andrés Chadwick|Andrés Chadwick Piñera]] (UDI) and Juan Pablo Letelier Morel ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) as part of the 9th senatorial constituency (O'Higgins Region).
