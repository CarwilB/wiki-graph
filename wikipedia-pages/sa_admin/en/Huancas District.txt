'''Huancas''' is one of the 21 districts of [[Chachapoyas Province]] in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]] of northern Peru. It is bounded to the north by [[Valera District]], to the east by [[Sonche District]], to the south by [[Chachapoyas District]], and to the west by [[Luya District]] and [[Lámud District]].

== History ==

The district was legally created on 5 February 1861, during the government of President [[Ramón Castilla|Ramon Castilla]].

== Geography ==
[[Image:Huancas sonche chachapoyas amazonas peru.jpg|thumb|150px|Sonche River near Huancas]]
Huancas District covers an area of 49&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and has a population of 1,305.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.infogob.com.pe/Localidad/ubigeo.aspx?IdUbigeo=010108&IdLocalidad=90&IdTab=0|title=INFOgob|website=www.infogob.com.pe|access-date=2016-09-18}}</ref>

The capital is the town of '''Huancas''', which is a 15-minute drive from the city of [[Chachapoyas, Peru|Chachapoyas]]. Huancas is located at a height of 2,558 m (8,392&nbsp;ft) above sea level, in an area where high [[Peruvian Amazon|Amazon rainforest]] drops to the Sonche River, 962 m (3,156&nbsp;ft) below. There is an attractive Colonial church located on the main square; and the citizens are well known for their earthenware (''alfareria'') – including clay cooking pots and jugs (''cantaros'') – that are hand-decorated and fired using open, wood-burning ovens

However, the most notable tourist draw is the stunning viewpoint over the Sonche Canyon, 1&nbsp;km (0.6 miles) east of Huancas. Additionally, 3&nbsp;km (1.9 miles) north of Huancas, is Huanca Urco hill, with views of where the Sonche, Vitaya and [[Utcubamba (river)|Utcubamba Rivers]] meet. On a clear day, the top part of [[Gocta Cataracts|Gocta Falls]] can be seen.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.perunorth.com/huancas/|title=Huancas – Sonche Canyon, Huanca Urco, Vitaya & Utcubamba River|website=Peru North|access-date=2016-09-18}}</ref>

Huancas belongs to the parish of Chachapoyas, and is served by two priests from the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Chachapoyas]].

== Festivals ==

Huancas' patron saint is the [[Lord of Miracles|Lord of the Miracles]], so its annual festival is held on 18 October.
