'''Tixcacalcupul Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "Two mouths of Cupul's well") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 1,164.98&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|180|km}} southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and was located in the chieftainship of  Cupules. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos,<ref name="municipios"/> beginning with Francisco Manrique in 1549, passing to Juan de Cárdenas in 1579, Francisco Mallén in 1607 and later to Francisco Mallén de Rueda. In 1645 the encomenderos were Gaspar de Ayala Pacheco and Francisca Dorantes y Solís and in 1686 passed to Pedro Pardo de Lagos.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5|page=519| url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA503|accessdate=15 August 2015|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|accessdate=15 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=Spanish|date=1996|page=15|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=2015-12-23}}</ref> the area was assigned to the [[Valladolid Municipality, Yucatan|Valladolid Municipality]]. During the [[Caste War of Yucatán]], a battle occurred here in which almost all of the inhabitants were massacred by government troops. In 1918 the area became its own municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, sports, education and health.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Tixcacalcupul| url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=15 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref>
