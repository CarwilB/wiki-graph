'''Brejão''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[bɾɛˈʒɐ̃w]}}) is a municipality/city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population in [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]] was 9,079 and the total area is 159.786&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Garanhuns]]   ('''N''' and '''E''');  [[Lagoa do Carro]]   ('''S''');   [[Terezinha, Pernambuco|Terezinha]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 159.79&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 788 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate  -  [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 22.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 244&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Brejão''' are related with [[agribusiness]], especially creations of [[cattle]]; and plantations of [[bean]]s, [[manioc]], [[coffee]] and [[tobacco]].
