'''Santa Rosa del Mbutuy''' is a [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Caaguazú Department|Caaguazú]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]] that is best described as the 'Cruce del Mbutuy' by residents of the Santa Rosa District, where it is located, because of the intersection of two major roads that the town is built around.

Some of the town's early growth could be attributed to the fact that it was on an asphalt paved road that intersected with a dirt road to the rest of the District of Santa Rosa del Mbutuy, and other departments of the nation.

==Economy==
Much of the town's economy is derived from traveler services such as food and fuel to those that pass through, as well as services to the local agricultural sector.{{cn|date=March 2024}}

==Communication==
Before cellular phone services where available in the area, it was home to one of the two telephone exchanges in the district.{{cn|date=March 2024}}

==Location==
It is located about 3.5 hours by bus north-east of [[Asunción]], the capital city of the country.
