'''Ilama''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|179.4|km2}} and had a population of 9,058 inhabitants as per the 2013 national census. It is situated near the [[Ulúa River]].

== History ==
The municipality was formerly known as "Ilamatepeque", a name derived from [[Nahuatl]] meaning "hill of the old woman" (''cerro de la vieja'' in [[Spanish language|Spanish]]).<ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-ilama/|work=RedHonduras|title=Municipality of Ilama|access-date=26 August 2023}}</ref> As per the documents found in the municipal archives, the municipal charter of Ilama was established on 31 December 1795. The document records the election of the first municipal officers in the ciudad de San Cristóbal de Ilamatepeque.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Ilama, Santa Bárbara|work=Edu-Honduras|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Ilama.html|access-date=1 August 2025|lang=es}}</ref> The patron saint of Ilama is the [[Our Lady of Lourdes|Virgin of Lourdes]], and the annual patron feast (''feria patronal'') is celebrated from 1 to 12 February each year.<ref name="Red"/><ref name="Edu"/>

== Geography ==
Ilama is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1612__ilama/|title=Ilama|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> It is situated on highlands off the [[Ulúa River]]. The municipality borders the municipalities of [[Chinda]] and [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] to the north, [[Santa Bárbara, Santa Bárbara|Santa Bárbara]] to the south, [[San Francisco de Yojoa]] and [[San Antonio de Cortés]] to the east, and [[San José de Colinas]] and [[Gualala, Honduras|Gualala]] to the west.<ref name="Red"/><ref name="Edu"/> It occupies an area of {{cvt|179.4|km2}},<ref name="INE2013">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/262-Santa-Barbara-Ilama.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 262, Municipio de Ilama 16-12, Departamento de Santa Bárbara|date=February 2025|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es}}</ref>{{rp|page=19}} and an average altitude of {{cvt|298|m}}.<ref name="Edu"/>

== Demographics ==
At the time of the 2013 census, Ilama had a total population of 9,058 inhabitants of whom 4,809 (53.1%) were male and 4,249 (46.9%) were female with a [[sex ratio]] of 113.2 men per 100 women. Of the total population, 2,117 (23.4%) lived in the urban and 6,941 (76.6%) in the rural areas.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} By age group, 34.7% of the population were aged 0–14, 58.3% were aged 15–64, and 7.1% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 22.6 years and the mean age was 27.6 years.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19-20}} Among the population aged 15 and over, the 2013 census recorded a [[literacy rate]] of 74.6%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=23}} 

There were 2,603 private dwellings in the municipality, of which 2,155 were occupied. The average household size in occupied private dwellings was 4.2 persons. Of the private dwellings, 2,462 (94.6%) were classified as independent houses (''casas independientes''), and 1,819 (84.4%) were owner-occupied, 99 (4.6%) were rented, and 193 (9.0%) were borrowed or ceded without payment.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=27}} Of the occupied private dwellings, 1,854 (86%) obtained water from the public system, 92 (4.3%) from wells, and 73 (3.4%) from other sources. About 1,629 (75.6%) had access to electric lighting, of which 1,375 were connected to the public electricity system.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=29}}
