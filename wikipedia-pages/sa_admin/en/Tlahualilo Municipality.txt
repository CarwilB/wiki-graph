''' Tlahualilo''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The [[municipal seat]] lies at [[Tlahualilo de Zaragoza]]. The municipality covers an area of 3,709.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 22,244,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Tlahualilo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=036|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 19,882 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tlahualilo|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10036a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=December 19, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927203933/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10036a.htm|archivedate=September 27, 2007}}</ref> 


The municipality had 159 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Tlahualilo de Zaragoza, Durango|Tlahualilo de Zaragoza]] (9,517), [[El Lucero (Arcinas), Durango|El Lucero (Arcinas)]] (2,622), classified as urban, and [[San Francisco de Horizonte (Horizonte), Durango|San Francisco de Horizonte (Horizonte)]] (1,657), [[Jauja, Durango|Jauja]] (1,101), [[Banco Nacional, Durango|Banco Nacional]] (1,072), and [[San Julio, Durango|San Julio]] (1,046), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
