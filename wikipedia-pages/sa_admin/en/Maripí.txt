'''Maripí''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Western Boyacá Province]]. The town hosts an important [[Colombian emeralds|emerald]] mine; [[La Pita]].
