'''Baldim''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2025|lc=y}} is estimated to be 7,595, in a total area of {{cvt|556266|sqkm|0}}.<ref name="IBGE" /> The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] Metropolitana de [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Sete Lagoas]].
