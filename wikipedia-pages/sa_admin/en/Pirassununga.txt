'''Pirassununga''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]], with an altitude of 627 meters. The population is 76,877 (2020 est.) in an area of 727&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2343O Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>

Situated in the southeast region of Brazil, the city is home to many important institutions, one being the [[Brazilian Air Force Academy]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www2.fab.mil.br/afa/|title=AFA - Academia da Força Aérea|website=www2.fab.mil.br|language=pt-br|access-date=2018-11-12}}</ref> All current and future officers of the Brazilian Air Force are trained here. Pirassununga is also home to Fort Anhaguera, which once hosted the [[Brazilian Mechanized Cavalry Regiment|13th Mechanized Cavalry Regiment]] of the Brazilian army.

In addition to being an important region for the Brazilian military, Pirassununga is a hub for the agriculture and environmental sciences of Brazil. The [[University of São Paulo]]'s [https://www.fzea.usp.br/ Faculdade de Zootecnia e Engenharia de Alimentos (FZEA)], which translates to the College of Animal Science and Food Engineering, is located in the city.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fzea.usp.br/en/|title=FZEA-USP - FZEA-USP|website=www.fzea.usp.br|language=en-US|access-date=2018-11-12}}</ref> Pirassununga is also the location of [https://www.icmbio.gov.br/cepta/ CEPTA], the National Research and Conservation Center of Continental Aquatic Biodiversity,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.icmbio.gov.br/cepta/destaques/58-brazilian-action-plans-for-freshwater-fishes.html|title=ICMBio - Centro Nacional de Pesquisa e Conservação de Peixes Continentais - CEPTA - Brazilian Action Plans for freshwater fishes|last=Santos|first=CEPTA - Fernando|website=www.icmbio.gov.br|language=pt-br|access-date=2018-11-12}}</ref> which is associated with the [[Chico Mendes Institute for Biodiversity Conservation|Chico Mendes Institute]], an important research and conservation center in [[Brasília]], the capital of [[Brazil]].

== History ==
Pirassununga was originally home to the [[Tupi people]], an indigenous group in Brazil. They gave the city its name, which means "the place where the fish makes sounds" or "the sound of fish." Every December, the [[Piracema|fish migrate upstream]], causing loud sounds as they fight against the current.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://pirassununga.sp.gov.br/a-cidade/historia-2/|title=História - Prefeitura Municipal de Pirassununga|work=Prefeitura Municipal de Pirassununga|access-date=2018-11-12|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Exports ==

=== Cachaça ===
[[File:Cachaca Sapucaia Pirassununga.jpg|thumb|Cachaça Sapucaia Barrels in Pirassununga|alt=|left]]Pirassununga is home to three sugar cane distilleries, [[Cachaça 51|Pirassununga 51 Cachaça]], [[Cachaça Sapucaia]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://thefatrumpirate.com/sapucaia-florida-ouro|title=Cachaca Sapucaia review |website=thefatrumpirate.com/sapucaia-florida-ouro|date=3 January 2019 |language=en|access-date=2019-01-03}}</ref> and Pirassununga Cachaça.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ministryofrum.com/rumdetails.php?r=378|title=Pirassununga Cachaça - Brazil - review - Ministry of Rum|website=www.ministryofrum.com|language=en|access-date=2018-10-22}}</ref> The most popular brand, Pirassununga 51 Cachaça, was founded in 1959, just one year after Brazil had won its first FIFA Soccer World Cup.<ref>{{Cite web|title=FIFA|url=https://fifa.com/|access-date=2021-10-11|website=fifa.com|language=en}}</ref> Pirassununga 51 Cachaça became a household name in the country during the 70s. "51, uma boa ideia," which translates to "51, a good idea," soon became a phrase in popular culture.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cachaca51.com.br/cachaca|title=Cachaça 51|website=Cachaça 51|access-date=2018-10-22}}</ref> It was not until the 1990s that Pirassununga 51 Cachaça became an export to other parts of the world. [[Cachaça]] is used as the spirit of choice for [[Brazil]]'s national [[cocktail]], the [[caipirinha]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Origem da Caipirinha {{!}} Caipirinha Prendada|url=https://www.caipirinhaprendada.com.br/a-caipirinha/origem-da-caipirinha/|access-date=2021-10-11|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Tourist Spots ==

=== Waterfall Emas ===
[[File:Cachoeira das Emas.jpg|thumb|Waterfall Emas in Pirassununga, [[São Paulo]].|alt=|left]]Waterfall Emas serves as a [[Hydroelectricity|hydroelectric plant]] to power the city of Pirassununga. In addition to supplying the city with electricity, it is a local fishing and boating destination. Houses can be rented nearby for fishermen to be near the water. Around Waterfall Emas, one can find many restaurants serving the local fish, dorado.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cachoeiradeemas.com.br/|title=Cachoeira de Emas Pirassununga – Pontos Turisticos, Restaurantes, Hoteis, Turismo, Peixes|website=cachoeiradeemas.com.br|access-date=2018-10-26}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-06-14|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by [[Telefónica]], which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012. The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-06-14|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

=== Catholic Church ===
The Catholic church in the municipality is part of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Limeira]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.catholic-hierarchy.org/diocese/dsapa.html|title=São Paulo (Archdiocese) [Catholic-Hierarchy]|access-date=2024-06-17|website=www.catholic-hierarchy.org}}</ref>

The most diverse evangelical beliefs are present in the city, mainly [[Pentecostalism|Pentecostal]], including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assemblies of God in Brazil]] (the largest evangelical church in the country),<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://confradesp.com.br/campos-eclesiasticos/|title=Campos Eclesiásticos|date=2018-12-10|access-date=2024-06-17|website=CONFRADESP|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ad.org.br/locations/|title=Arquivos: Locais|access-date=2024-06-17|website=Assembleia de Deus Belém – Sede|language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Christian Congregation in Brazil]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br/localidade|title=Localidade - Congregação Cristã no Brasil|access-date=2024-06-17|website=congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> among others. These denominations are growing more and more throughout Brazil.
