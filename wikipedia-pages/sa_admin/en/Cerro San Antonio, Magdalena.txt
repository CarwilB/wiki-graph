'''Cerro San Antonio''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. Founded on June 13, 1750 by [[Fernando de Mier y Guerra]] near the [[Magdalena River]]. The ''Libertador'' [[Simon Bolivar]] visited this town on May 23, 1813 during the War of Independence of Colombia from Spain.
