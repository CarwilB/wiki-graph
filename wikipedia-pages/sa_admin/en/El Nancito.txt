'''El Nancito''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Remedios District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|35.4|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 607 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|17.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 786; its population as of 2000 was 519.<ref name=inec/>
