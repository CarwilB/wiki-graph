'''Contepec''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]], located {{convert|110|km}} east of the state capital of [[Morelia]], being the easternmost municipality in Michoacán.

==Geography==
The municipality of Contepec is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northeast Michoacán at an altitude between {{convert|2100|and(-)|3200|m}}. It borders the Michoacano municipalities of [[Epitacio Huerta Municipality|Epitacio Huerta]] to the northwest, [[Maravatío]] to the west, and [[Tlalpujahua]] to the southwest, as well as the municipalities of [[Amealco de Bonfil]] in [[Querétaro]] to the northeast, and [[Temascalcingo]] and [[El Oro de Hidalgo]] of the [[State of Mexico]] to the east and southeast.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Contepec, Michoacán de Ocampo |language=es |publisher=INEGI |accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|381.725|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.6% of the state's area.<ref name=survey/>

Farmland covers 64% of Contepec.<ref name=compendium/> The mountains on the municipality's eastern border with Temascalcingo are protected as the Altamirano unit of the [[Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve]], a UNESCO [[World Heritage Site]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/uploads/nominations/1290.pdf |title=Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve: World Heritage Site Nomination Document |page=11 |date=January 2007 |publisher=[[UNESCO]] |accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref>

Tepuxtepec Dam is located on the [[Lerma River]] which flows through the northern and western part of Contepec. Built from 1926 to 1950, the dam and its reservoir are used for hydroelectricity generation, flood control and irrigation.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://amotepux.blogspot.com/2013/02/presa-tepuxtepec.html |title=Presa Tepuxtepec |language=es |date=19 February 2013 |accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref>

Contepec's climate is temperate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|700|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The place name Contepec derives from [[Nahuatl]] ''comitl'', "pot", and ''tepetl'', "hill". In pre-Hispanic times the area was inhabited by [[Guamare]] and [[Pame people|Pame]] indigenous peoples: epidemics caused their extinction in the area by the end of the 16th century. The area was repopulated in the 17th century by [[Otomi]] and [[Mazahua people|Mazahua]] settlers.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

In the first half of the 19th century Contepec was part of the municipality of [[Coroneo]] in [[Guanajuato]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> In 1857 it was separated from Guanajuato and incorporated as a municipality in Michoacán. Between 1935 and 1938 the municipal seat was located at Tepuxtepec. In 1962 the municipality of Epitacio Huerta was separated from Contepec.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=78, 116–117 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Ruben Rodríguez Jiménez.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Census, the municipality of Contepec recorded a population of 32,954 inhabitants living in 7723 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=017 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Contepec |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 34,193 inhabitants in Contepec.<ref name=survey/>

There are 74 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat, also known as Contepec, is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 4184 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Contepec are agriculture and livestock farming.<ref name=plan2012>{{cite news |author=Contepec Municipal Council |url=http://leyes.michoacan.gob.mx/destino/O11959po.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal 2015–2018 |language=es |page=10 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=9 December 2016 |accessdate=3 May 2018}}</ref>
