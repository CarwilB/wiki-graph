==Location==
La Esperanza District is located in the North - Center of Trujillo Province, between latitudes 08° 04′ 30″ south latitude and 79° 02′ 38″ W, at a distance approximately of 01&nbsp;km from the [[Plaza de Armas of Trujillo (Peru)|main square of Trujillo]] city.<ref>{{cite web |year = |url = http://www.peru.gob.pe/docs/PLANES/11314/PLAN_11314_PDDC_(Plan_de_Desarrollo_Distrital_Concertado)_2011-2020_2011.pdf|title = Plan de Desarrollo Concertado de La Esperanza 2011- 2020 |editor = |accessdate =November 23, 2012|language=es}}</ref>
