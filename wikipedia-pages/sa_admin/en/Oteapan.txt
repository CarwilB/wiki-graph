'''Oteapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is in the south-east zone of the state, about 394 km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 27.97 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|00|N|94|40|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Oteapan is delimited to the north by [[Chinameca, Veracruz|Chinameca]] to the east by [[Cosoleacaque]], to the south by [[Zaragoza, Veracruz|Zaragoza]] and to the south-west by [[Jaltipan de Morelos|Jaltipan]] State.<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30120a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Oteapan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-12|archive-date=2007-02-25|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070225114457/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30120a.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The weather in  Oteapan is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.
==Products==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[rice]] and [[green chile]].
==Culture==
In Oteapan, in May takes place the celebration in honor to Santa Cecilia, patron of the town.
