'''Silos''' or '''Santo Domingo de Silos''' is a [[Colombian municipality]] and town located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[North Santander]].

It is the only municipality founded in Colombia by a German, [[Ambrosio Alfinger]], in 1531.
