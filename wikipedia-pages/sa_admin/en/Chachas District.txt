'''Chachas District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Castilla Province|Castilla]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. == Geography ==
The [[Chila mountain range|Chila]] mountain range traverses the district. One of the highest mountains of the district is [[Chila (Castilla)|Chila]]  at {{convert|5654|m|ft|0|abbr=on}} above sea level.
