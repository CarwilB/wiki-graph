'''Canhotinho'''({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kɐ̃j̃oˈtĩj̃u]}})  (''Little Left-handed'') is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is 223&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of Pernambuco. Canhotinho has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 24,773 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Lajedo, Pernambuco|Lajedo]] and [[Jurema, Pernambuco|Jurema]]   ('''N''');  [[Palmeirina]]    ('''S''');  [[Quipapá]] and [[Alagoas]] state  ('''E''');   [[Angelim]] and [[Calçado]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 423.08&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 520 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River|Mundaú]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 21.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 223&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Canhotinho are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[beans]], [[manioc]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
