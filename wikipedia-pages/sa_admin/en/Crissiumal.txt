'''Crissiumal'''{{efn|According to the current [[Portuguese orthography|orthographic norms]] of the [[Portuguese Language Orthographic Agreement of 1990|1990 Orthographic Agreement]], this toponym should be spelled '''Criciumal'''. The use of the letter "[[C]]" is prescribed for words of [[Tupi language|Tupi]] origin. The name derives from Tupi and refers to a family of [[Poaceae|grasses]]. Over the years, the spelling has evolved from ''cresciúmal'' to ''crissiumal'' and finally to ''criciumal''.}} is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], located in the [[List of mesoregions and microregions of Rio Grande do Sul|Northwest Rio Grande do Sul mesoregion]] and the Três Passos Microregion,<ref name=":24" /> at a [[latitude]] of 27°30'00" [[south]] and a [[longitude]] of 54°06'03" [[west]], at an [[altitude]] of 410 meters.<ref name=":24" /> Its estimated population in 2016 was 14,233, covering an area of 363.106 [[square kilometer|km<sup>2</sup>]].<ref name=":30">{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=430600 |access-date=February 3, 2017 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The municipality is traversed by the waters of the [[Uruguay River]]. Its main access is via the [[List of state highways in Rio Grande do Sul|RS-207]] highway, though it is also served by the RS-305.

Crissiumal was originally founded in 1933, with official emancipation occurring in 1954. Its name is believed to originate from ''criciúma'', a plant abundant in the region, which in the Tupi language means a smooth, flexible rod or small bamboo. The service sector currently accounts for 60% of the municipal GDP, serving as the primary source of income.

One of Crissiumal's most prominent and traditional football clubs is the [[Tupi Futebol Clube]], established in May 1949. Crissiumal hosts various events, such as the Feast of [[Our Lady of Navigators]] and Expocris, and features several tourist attractions, including the Castle, the Pioneer Monument, and the Church of the Three Holy Martyrs of the Missions. It is known as the Gaucho capital of agro-industries.<ref name="Conheça Crissiumal">{{cite web |url=http://www.crissiumal-rs.com.br/index.php/conheca-crissiumal |title=Learn About Crissiumal |publisher=Crissiumal Municipal Government |access-date=November 18, 2011 |archive-date=October 10, 2011 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111010141828/http://www.crissiumal-rs.com.br/index.php/conheca-crissiumal |url-status=dead }}</ref> Crissiumal is the 122nd most populous city in the state and the 2133rd in Brazil.<ref name="Cidade de Crissiumal/RS. População e Estatísticas">{{cite web |url=http://www.estadosecidades.com/crissiumal-rs_cidade.aspx |title=City of Crissiumal/RS. Population and Statistics |publisher=States and Cities, Social Network of States and Cities |access-date=November 11, 2011}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The city's name derives from a shrub-like plant common in the region, known as ''criciúma''.<ref name=":36" /> The word ''criciúma'' likely originates from the [[Tupi language]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://bambusc.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2009/06/tupy_guarani_fonte-de-informacoes-sobre-bambus-nativos-no-brasil_tarcisofilgueiras.pdf|title=Tupi-Guarani: Source of Information on Native Brazilian Bamboos|access-date=February 9, 2017|publisher=BambuSC Santa Catarina Bamboo Association|last=Figueiras|first=Tarcísio}}</ref> meaning a smooth, flexible rod or small bamboo.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.bib.unesc.net/biblioteca/sumario/000026/000026E2.html |title=Coal Culture in Criciúma-SC: The Unhe Story|access-date=February 10, 2017|publisher=University of Southern Santa Catarina|last=Philomena|first=Gerson Luis de Boer}}</ref> According to the [[Michaelis (dictionary)|Michaelis Dictionary]], it refers to plants of the genera ''Criciuma'' or ''[[Bambusa]]'', belonging to the [[Poaceae|grass]] family.<ref name=":18">{{cite web|url=http://michaelis.uol.com.br/busca?id=8myd|title=Criciúma|access-date=February 9, 2017|website=Michaelis Online}}</ref> The etymology of the word is unknown,<ref name=":18" /> and the correct spelling is "quiriciúma," as, according to [[Joseph of Anchieta|Anchieta]], there are no consonant clusters in the Tupi language.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.numaboa.net.br/glossarios/indigenas?start=50|title=Indigenous Names and Expressions|access-date=February 9, 2017|website=www.numaboa.net.br}}</ref>

== History ==

=== Colonization and emancipation of Crissiumal ===
[[File:Wendroth09.jpg|left|thumb|Map of the São Pedro do Rio Grande do Sul Province from 1856, showing the [[Uruguay River]] and its right-bank tributaries, suggesting that Crissiumal, due to its proximity to the river, may have been known since that year.]]
The first inhabitants of Crissiumal were the [[Tupi people|Tupi]] indigenous groups.<ref name=":33">{{cite book|last=Pletsch|first=José Raymundo|publisher=Salesian College Dom Bosco|year=1978|location=Santa Rosa|title=History of Crissiumal}}</ref> These indigenous peoples cultivated [[maize]], [[cassava]], and [[Yam (vegetable)|yam]], which they used to produce other foods and low-alcohol beverages.<ref name=":33" /> In the 1930s, two indigenous settlements, each with 60 to 70 families, existed in the areas of Bela Vista and Linha Porto Alegre. In the 1970s, artifacts such as two millstones, a pair of spurs, spears, and caves containing clay ovens were discovered there.<ref name=":33" /> These groups originated from [[Paraguay]], where they had participated in an uprising against the government.<ref name=":33" /> After their failure, they were persecuted and fled to Brazilian territory.<ref name=":33" /> There is no evidence of ''[[bandeirantes]]'' passing through Crissiumal or of [[Jesuit missions among the Guaraní|Jesuit missions]] led by religious orders in the area.<ref name=":33" />

The area where Crissiumal is located was one of the last regions in [[Rio Grande do Sul]] to be settled by European colonizers.<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal">{{cite journal|url=http://rbgdr.net/revista/index.php/rbgdr/article/view/120/111 |title=Environmental Impacts of the Developmental Dynamics of the Northwest Colonial Region of Rio Grande do Sul |author= Dilson Trennepohl |journal=Revista Brasileira de Gestão e Desenvolvimento Regional |date=14 February 2008 |volume=4 |issue=1 |access-date=November 18, 2011}}</ref> Initially, the area was occupied by indigenous groups.<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" /> In the end of the late 19th century, efforts to colonize northern and northwestern Rio Grande do Sul gained momentum through the removal of bureaucratic obstacles, and the establishment of legal and logistical incentives.<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" /> As the lands in the "old colonies" were already occupied, and population growth required new frontiers for settlers' descendants and new immigrants, several colonization projects were implemented in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, facilitating migration to the region.<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" /> In 1890, the "Ijuí Colony" was founded, composed of newly arrived European immigrants, descendants of old colony settlers, and native peoples.<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" /> Closer to Crissiumal, settlers established the "Upper Uruguay Military Colony" in 1879,<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" /> which later, in 1944, became the municipality of [[Três Passos]].<ref name="Ambiente Crissiumal" />
[[File:Crissiumal em 1935.jpg|thumb|463x463px|Crissiumal in 1935, two years after its founding.<ref name=":36" />]]
Settlement and occupation of Crissiumal began in the early 1930s<ref name=":9">{{cite web|url=http://amuceleiro.com.br/?pg=desc-noticias&id=1590|title=Crissiumal Celebrates 59 Years of Emancipation This Friday|access-date=January 31, 2017|website=AMUCELEIRO - Association of Municipalities of the Celeiro Region}}</ref> with colonists from the municipalities of [[Estrela, Rio Grande do Sul|Estrela]], [[Lajeado, Rio Grande do Sul|Lajeado]], [[Sobradinho, Rio Grande do Sul|Sobradinho]],<ref name=":10">{{Cite web |title=Official Website - Crissiumal Municipal Government |url=http://crissiumal-rs3.hospedagemdesites.ws/Site/conheca-crissiumal/23-a-cidade/crissiumal-historico |access-date=January 31, 2017 |website=Crissiumal Municipal Government |archive-date=March 27, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190327092044/http://crissiumal-rs3.hospedagemdesites.ws/Site/conheca-crissiumal/23-a-cidade/crissiumal-historico |url-status=dead }}</ref> [[Venâncio Aires]], and [[Roca Sales]] in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], and from [[Laguna, Santa Catarina|Laguna]], [[Tubarão]], [[Criciúma]], and [[Urussanga]] in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].<ref name=":36">{{cite book|last1=Vicentinini|first1=Jenair|last2=Klingler|first2=Aline Patricia|publisher=SR|year=2011|location=Horizontina|title=Crissiumal: Documentary: Essence of Our History - 78 Years of History, 57 Years of Political Autonomy}}</ref> The first European settler to arrive in Crissiumal was Domingos Maccari, a foreman for the firm Dahne, Conceição & Cia., in 1930.<ref name=":36" /> He was followed by Domingos Meneghel in 1933,<ref name=":9" /> Adolfo Rinaldi, Ivo dos Santos, Albino Löwe, Bernardo Dickel, and Miguel Schutz, some with their families.<ref name=":10" /> On January 6, 1936, the first mass was celebrated, and the first chapel was built on January 23 of the same year.<ref name=":10" /> The fertility of the land attracted relatives and acquaintances of the pioneers, leading to an influx of new settlers.<ref name=":10" /> Roads were carved through the forest, and ox carts and trucks transported the colony's products to larger centers.<ref name=":10" /> In the book ''History of Crissiumal'', José Raymundo Pletsch, based on accounts from Adelina Frieda Schütz, described the scene during Crissiumal's early colonization:

{{quote|At the beginning of settlement (...) there was no school, no doctor, no midwife. (...) Wild animals roamed near homes. It was difficult to sell agricultural products. They could only sell lard and eggs. The supply of basic foodstuffs was also precarious until the opening of the first commercial establishment, owned by Mr. Albino Loew.
As the population grew, various professionals gradually settled in the village of Crissiumal.<ref name=":33" />}}Schwanke and Pohl also describe how the first settlers arrived in Crissiumal:
{{quote|According to Pedro Oswaldo Scheid, newspapers in the Old Colonies advertised fertile land in Crissiumal. The advertisements motivated mainly young couples to venture out, leaving the comfort of their homeland and facing many hardships in the name of progress.
According to Mrs. Hedy Schwingel Hetzel, men usually came alone to inspect the land. After securing a purchase agreement with the Dahne Conceição company, they cleared the forest, burned the vegetation, and built a shack covered with grass, palm leaves, or wooden shingles to house their families. Then they would fetch their wives, children, and sometimes relatives to settle in the new land. Possessions were transported by trucks, ox carts, horses, and buses known as "Mala-Branca".
The pioneers (...) arrived in Crissiumal with some provisions and few household items.
(...)
The Serrana Company bus brought settlers without a fixed schedule due to poor road conditions. The bus, called "Mala-Branca" because it had a designated space for hanging white cotton bags used at the time, carried passengers.
(...)
Settlers chose their plots using a map and were guided through trails marked by surveyors. (...) They carried rice, which they cooked in bamboo tubes at the campsite, and also relied on hunted game. The work was risky due to the dense, extensive forest and the known presence of ferocious animals in the region.<ref name=":37" />}}
[[File:Primeiro veículo motorizado de Crissiumal em 1935.jpg|thumb|The first motorized vehicle in Crissiumal, in 1935.<ref name=":36" />]]
On January 6, 1936, Crissiumal was recognized as a village.<ref name=":9" /> On January 1, 1941, the first baptism took place, and on January 3, the first marriage, between Ruy Mesch and Josefina Focking, was celebrated by the first parish priest, Father Sebastião Rademaker.<ref name=":10" /> In 1944, Crissiumal and Três Passos united to seek emancipation,<ref name=":10" /> and on December 28, 1944, State Decree-Law No. 716 created the municipality of Três Passos<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=431370&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Cpalmeira-das-missoes |access-date=January 31, 2017 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and the Crissiumal District.<ref name=":11">{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&mun=430600&search=rio-grande-do-sul |access-date=January 31, 2017 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> In the 1950 Census, the Crissiumal District had more inhabitants than the municipal seat,<ref name=":10" /><ref name=":11" /> leading to an emancipation movement. In a 1954 plebiscite, the population voted to separate from Três Passos.<ref name=":11" /> State Law No. 2,553, enacted on December 18, 1954, established the municipality of Crissiumal.<ref name=":10" /><ref name=":11" />

=== From emancipation to the 1990s ===
Egon Theophilo Heinsch described the atmosphere of Crissiumal in 1956 in his book ''Crissiumal: A Bit of History: Tupy F.C. and Its Struggle'':
{{quote|Upon arriving in Crissiumal with my family on September 1, 1956, to assume the position of Treasurer of the Municipal Government at the invitation of Mayor Lauro Pedro Thomas, the initial impression was not very favorable: electricity, supplied by the Lunardi Sawmill, was available only until a certain hour at night; well water was brackish; there was no paving: in dry weather, dust prevailed, and when it rained, mud took over. Gradually, however, we adapted.<ref name=":21" />}}
After emancipation, direct elections for mayor and councilors were held on February 20, 1955.<ref name=":33" /> Lauro Pedro Thomaz, the only candidate for mayor, was elected, took office on February 28, 1955, and governed until January 1, 1960.<ref name=":33" /> During his administration, the sub-prefecture building was adapted to house administrative and legislative functions temporarily.<ref name=":33" /> Municipal roads were improved and widened, a city master plan was developed, new teachers were hired, schools were built, the town center and the villages of Humaitá and Vista Nova were electrified, and [[Artesian well|artesian wells]] were drilled for potable water, though no water was found.<ref name=":33" />
[[File:Casa em Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|A [[half-timbered house]] built in 1948.]]
The second mayor, Alcido Brust, was elected by direct and secret vote through the [[Brazilian Labour Party (1981)|PTB]]-PRB coalition.<ref name=":33" /> His administration saw the installation of a municipal telephone network, the construction of a works yard, the installation of a stone crusher, the building of wooden schools funded by the state government called ''brizoletas'', the electrification of Lajeado Crissiumal, Vista Alegre, and Linha Porto Alegre, the construction of a masonry building for the Rocha Pombo School with state funding, and the remodeling of the 25 de Julho Square.<ref name=":33" />

The third mayor, Pedro Harry Hoffmann, governed from 1964 to 1969.<ref name=":33" /> During his term, some streets in the town center were paved and tree-lined, the 25 de Julho Square was completed, the foundation and lower part of the new municipal building were constructed, over 100 telephones were installed, twelve localities were electrified, an ASCAR office was established, an exposition park was built, and fourteen new primary schools were created and constructed.<ref name=":33" />

From 1965, Crissiumal was designated a National Security Area due to its border with [[Argentina]], and mayors were appointed by the [[President of Brazil]].<ref name=":37" /> The first appointed mayor was Benno Bender, who led the municipal executive from July 7, 1971, to August 9, 1975. His administration saw the town center supplied with water by Corsan, the construction of a police station, the Ponche Verde State High School, and several school buildings.<ref name=":17" /> The second appointed mayor, Pedro Osvaldo Scheid, served from August 9, 1975, to August 7, 1980, to August 1. During his administration, the LBA, Cebem, and Apae centers were established, new schools were built, a new municipal building was constructed, a television retransmission tower was installed, a quarry was established, and a [[Banco do Brasil]] branch was opened.<ref name=":17" />

The third appointed mayor, Carlos Willy Grün, served from August 1, 1980, to January 1, 1986. During this period, a sports gymnasium, the Pioneer Monument, and the Rio Grande do Sul Telecommunications Company (CRT) building were constructed, the Direct Dialing System (DDD) was implemented, the first daycare was built, and the second floor of the municipal government building was completed.<ref name=":17" /> After [[History of Brazil (1985–present)|redemocratization]], National Security Areas were abolished, and Crissiumal resumed direct elections for mayors.<ref name=":17" /> Henrique Ebeling served as mayor from 1986 to 1988, during which time Castelo Branco Avenue was paved, bridges and schools were constructed.<ref name=":17" /> Luiz de Rosso served from 1989 to 1992.<ref name=":37" />

=== Administrative formation ===
Before its colonization, the area belonged to [[Porto Alegre]],<ref name=":33" /> then to [[Rio Pardo]] in 1809,<ref name=":33" /><ref name=":8" /> and to Cachoeira in 1819.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.revistas.fee.tche.br/index.php/boletim-geografico-rs/article/download/3322/3394|title=Municipal Evolution: A Geographic Analysis|access-date=March 14, 2017|publisher=Foundation for Economics and Statistics|last=La Salvia|first=Flávia}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ihgrgs.org.br/mapoteca/cd_mapas_rs/CD/imagens/mapas/cap_4/cap_4.1/images/581-325.jpg|title=S São Pedro do Rio Grande do Sul Province 1822|access-date=March 14, 2017|publisher=Historical Institute and Geographical Institute of Rio Grande do Sul|last=Campomar|first=João C.}}</ref> and to Espírito Santo de Cruz Alta by 1834.<ref name=":33" /><ref name=":8">{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?codmun=430600 |access-date=January 30, 2017 |website=www.cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> After colonization, it was part of [[Palmeira das Missões]],<ref name=":8" /> then called Santo Antônio da Palmeira,<ref name=":36" /> and later [[Três Passos]].<ref name=":36" /><ref name=":17">{{cite book|title=Notes on Crissiumal's Past: 1933 - 1993|last=Plletsch|first=Pedro Raymundo|publisher=Sedigraf|year=1995|location=Ijuí|pages=17–19}}</ref><ref name=":8" /> When part of Palmeira das Missões, Crissiumal was included in its 11th District, headquartered in Santa Teresinha.<ref name=":33"/> In 1944, the Crissiumal District was created by State Decree-Law No. 716 of December 28, from parts of the Ivagaci, Três Passos, and [[Campo Novo]] districts.<ref name=":8" /> In 1954, State Law No. 2553 of December 18 created the municipality of Crissiumal, which separated from Três Passos.<ref name=":8" /> In 1955, the districts of Candelária (Municipal Law No. 54, December 12), Esquina Gaúcha (Municipal Law No. 58, December 28), and Planalto (Municipal Law No. 59, December 28) were created, and in 1957, the Lajeado Grande District (Municipal Law No. 179, July 9).<ref name=":8" /> In 1959, Crissiumal lost territory due to the emancipation of [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]].<ref name=":33" /> In 1963, the Candelária District separated to form the municipality of [[Boa Vista do Buricá]].<ref name=":8" /> In 1987, Municipal Law No. 851 of December 12 created the Vista Nova District.<ref name=":8" />

=== Recent history ===
[[File:Vista do centro de Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|View of downtown Crissiumal in 2017, from Santa Rosa Avenue near the Recanto Verde square.]]
Henrique Ebeling was re-elected and served as mayor from 1993 to 1996.<ref name=":37" /> In 1997, Alvício Pereira Duarte took office and governed until 2000.<ref name=":37" /> In 2001, Walter Luiz Heck became mayor, was re-elected, and served until 2008,<ref name=":37" /> when Sérgio Drumm took office in 2009, serving until 2012.<ref name=":36" />

In 1998, the Fonte Nova Pact was created to support small-scale farmers in producing raw materials, agro-industrialization, and food production.<ref name=":20">{{cite web|url=http://redcidir.org/nueva2014/index.php?option=com_phocadownload&view=category&download=444:O%20Desenvolvimento%20de%20um%20Munic%C3%ADpio%20do%20RS&id=22:i-simposio-2008&Itemid=534&start=20&lang=es|title=The Development of a Municipality in Rio Grande do Sul: A Study on the Influence of Cooperativism|access-date=February 2, 2017|last=Sparemberger|first=Ariosto|archive-date=February 3, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170203082852/http://redcidir.org/nueva2014/index.php?option=com_phocadownload&view=category&download=444:O%20Desenvolvimento%20de%20um%20Munic%C3%ADpio%20do%20RS&id=22:i-simposio-2008&Itemid=534&start=20&lang=es|url-status=dead}}</ref> Alongside the program, the Municipal Inspection Service (SIM) was established, issuing Municipal Inspection Permits to certify the quality and origin of industrialized products.<ref name=":20" /> By the end of 2002, the 30 existing agro-industries formed the Cooper Fonte Nova cooperative to strengthen farmers’ activities.<ref name=":20" />

A significant climatic event occurred in Crissiumal on the early morning of June 15, 2005, when hailstones weighing 300 to 500 grams fell, accompanied by winds exceeding 150 km/h.<ref name=":36" /> It was the most severe storm and the greatest disaster in the municipality's history.<ref name=":36" /> According to the GVces website, "Around 3:30 a.m., entire windows were shattered by ice stones the size of oranges. Holes in roofs gave the impression that Crissiumal had been bombed."<ref>{{cite web|url=http://gvces.com.br/bombardeio-de-granizo-em-crissiumal?locale=pt-br|title=Hail Bombardment in Crissiumal - GVces|access-date=March 9, 2017|website=gvces.com.br|author=GVces}}</ref> Over a third of the city was affected, and there was solidarity from residents and neighboring towns, who aided in rebuilding homes.<ref name=":36" /> The Rio Grande do Sul state government sent 600 kg of non-perishable food, 100 blankets, 1,700 pieces of clothing and shoes, ten rolls of tarpaulin, and forage seeds to the municipality.<ref>{{cite journal|title=Governor Visits Affected Areas|journal=Gazeta do Sul|url=http://www.grupogaz.com.br/gazetadosul/tratadas/eo_edicao/1/2005/06/20050618_8f58388a4/pdf/632_20050618_567.pdf|access-date=February 2, 2017}}</ref><ref>{{cite journal|author=PROCERGS|title=Governor Visits Areas Hit by Storm in Crissiumal|journal=Government of Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://www.rs.gov.br/conteudo/169671/governador-visita-locais-atingidos-por-tempestade-em-crissiumal|archive-date=2017-02-03|access-date=2025-06-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170203162415/http://www.rs.gov.br/conteudo/169671/governador-visita-locais-atingidos-por-tempestade-em-crissiumal|url-status=dead}}</ref> Another event occurred in August 2009 when a storm damaged homes and crops and uprooted trees.<ref name=":36" />

In December 2012, the Rio Grande do Sul Civil Police conducted an operation named Patriota, investigating crimes such as embezzlement, fraud, rigged bidding, overpriced purchases of medicines and construction materials for public works, and diversion of materials from the works yard to buy votes in elections.<ref name=":22">{{cite journal|date=December 20, 2012|title=Crissiumal Deputy Mayor, RS, Arrested in Anti-Corruption Operation|journal=Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/noticia/2012/12/vice-prefeito-de-crissiumal-rs-e-preso-em-operacao-contra-corrupcao.html}}</ref> 110 agents executed 25 search and seizure warrants and 13 arrest warrants.<ref name=":22" />

In the [[2012 Brazilian municipal elections|2012 municipal elections]], Walter Luiz Heck was re-elected mayor with 50.46% of valid votes,<ref>{{cite web |title=2012 Election Results in Crissiumal |url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/apuracao/crissiumal.html |access-date=February 28, 2017 |website=G1}}</ref> but his mandate was revoked by the [[Superior Electoral Court]] in February 2015 for offering money and public positions to candidates and councilors from an opposing coalition to withdraw their candidacies and support his campaign.<ref name=":57">{{cite journal|date=February 4, 2015|title=Mayor and Deputy Mayor of Crissiumal Have Mandates Revoked by TSE|journal=Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/noticia/2015/02/tse-cassa-mandatos-de-prefeito-e-vice-de-crissiumal-rs.html}}</ref> Walter was declared ineligible for eight years from 2012, and the president of the City Council, Renato Salling, took office,<ref name=":57" /> governing until Roberto Bergmann, elected in supplementary elections in June 2015, assumed the mayoralty.

== Geography ==

=== Location ===
Crissiumal is located at a [[latitude]] of 27°29'59" [[south]] and a [[longitude]] of 54°06'04" [[west]], at an altitude of 410 meters. It is bordered to the east by [[Três Passos]], to the south by [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]] and [[Nova Candelária]], to the west by [[Horizontina]] and [[Doutor Maurício Cardoso]], and to the north by [[Argentina]] and [[Tiradentes do Sul]].<ref name="Leite Crissiumal">{{cite web|url=http://www.lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/handle/10183/2201/000315862.pdf?sequence=1 |title=Socioeconomic Analysis and Development Perspectives for Milk Producers in Crissiumal - RS |author=Machado, Roberto Tormes |access-date=November 20, 2011}}</ref><ref name="Diagnóstico Local Crissiumal-RS">{{cite web|url=http://portal.saude.gov.br/portal/arquivos/pdf/Diagnostico_Local_Crissiumal-RS.pdf |title=Local Health Diagnosis Crissiumal |author=Ministry of Health |access-date=November 15, 2011}}</ref> The municipality is bounded from east to northwest by the Lajeado Grande River until its confluence with the [[Uruguay River]]. To the northwest, it is delimited by the Uruguay River up to the mouth of the [[Buricá River]], which marks the western boundary. To the south, it is delimited by the Reúno River and its tributaries, and to the east by the Lajeado Jacu.<ref name=":48" /> Crissiumal belongs to the [[List of mesoregions and microregions of Rio Grande do Sul|Northwest Rio Grande do Sul Mesoregion]] and the Três Passos Microregion.<ref name=":24">{{cite web|url=http://www.cnm.org.br/municipios/index/100143/100143111|title=CNM Portal - National Confederation of Municipalities|access-date=February 6, 2017|website=www.cnm.org.br|last=Desenvolvimento|first=CNM}}</ref> The distance to the state capital, [[Porto Alegre]], is disputed and varies by source. According to Pletsch and Vicentini, it is 548 km.<ref name=":36" /><ref name=":17" /> Under the state division of Regional Development Councils, Crissiumal is part of the Colonial Northwest Regional Council (CRNOC), and in the division of municipal associations, it belongs to the Association of Municipalities of the Celeiro Region (Amuceleiro).<ref name=":12">{{cite web |last=Culturais |first=DRPAC - Cultural Promotions Division |title=Crissiumal Presents Its History and Characteristics |url=http://www2.al.rs.gov.br/promocoesculturais/Not%C3%ADcia/tabid/3365/IdMateria/174709/Default.aspx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221013143735/http://www2.al.rs.gov.br/promocoesculturais/Not%C3%ADcia/tabid/3365/IdMateria/174709/Default.aspx |archive-date=2022-10-13 |access-date=January 31, 2017 |website=www2.al.rs.gov.br}}</ref>

=== Topography ===
[[File:Crissiumal no Planalto Norte-riograndense.jpg|thumb|Location of Crissiumal in the North Rio-Grandense Plateau, highlighted in green.]]
The municipality is situated in the North Rio-Grandense Plateau, formed by basaltic lava flows from the [[Mesozoic Era]],<ref>{{cite journal |last=Cardoso |first=Marcelo Pereira |date=July 12, 2011 |title=The Terrain of Rio Grande do Sul |url=http://www.trabalhosescolares.net/o-relevo-do-rio-grande-do-sul/ |journal=School Papers}}</ref><ref name=":25">{{cite web|url=http://ojs.uem.br/ojs/index.php/Percurso/article/viewFile/18060/10215|title=The Terrain of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, and Its Representation in a Model|access-date=February 3, 2017|publisher=Percurso Journal - NEMO|last=Becker|first=Elsbeth Léia Spode}}</ref> extending from southern [[Goiás]] to [[Rio Grande do Sul]], forming Brazil's Southern Plateau.<ref name=":25" /> The terrain ranges from gently undulating to strongly undulating and mountainous,<ref name="Floresta Alto Uruguai"/> with more rugged areas near the [[Uruguay River|Uruguay]], [[Buricá River|Buricá]], Lajeado Grande rivers, and their tributaries.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal"/> Relatively flat areas exist along these watercourses but are limited in extent.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal"/> The lowest altitude, approximately 120 meters, is found in the west at the mouth of the [[Buricá River]], while the highest, reaching 472 meters, is near the boundary with [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]] in the west. The town center's altitude is 410 meters.<ref name=":24" /> The soil derives from basic [[igneous rocks]] of the Serra Geral Formation, with [[basalt]] as the substrate,<ref name="Floresta Alto Uruguai">{{cite web|url=http://cascavel.ufsm.br/revistas/ojs-2.2.2/index.php/cienciaflorestal/article/view/309/176 |title=Phytosociological Analysis of Some Remnant Forest Areas of the Upper Uruguay, Between the Ijuí and Turvo Rivers, in Rio Grande do Sul |author=Sandro Vaccaro, Solon Jonas Longhi |access-date=November 19, 2011}}</ref> predominantly featuring dystrophic red [[latosol]]<ref name=":1">{{cite web |date=March 2021 |title=Environmental Zoning for Silviculture |url=https://sema.rs.gov.br/upload/arquivos/202303/30162724-proposta-atualizacao-zas-2021-rev03.pdf |access-date=January 28, 2017 |publisher=Government of Rio Grande do Sul}}</ref> and [[chernozem]].<ref name=":26">{{cite web|url=http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=819&cod_menu=817&tipo_menu=ECONOMIA&cod_conteudo=1484|title=Socioeconomic Atlas of Rio Grande do Sul|access-date=February 3, 2017|website=www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br|archive-date=February 4, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170204085126/http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=819&cod_menu=817&tipo_menu=ECONOMIA&cod_conteudo=1484|url-status=dead}}</ref> Latosol is deep, well-drained, acidic, and low in fertility, suitable for agriculture if chemically corrected.<ref name=":26" /> Chernozem, dark in the [[A horizon]] due to organic matter, has high fertility and varies from shallow to deep.<ref name=":26" />

=== Hydrography ===
[[File:Rio Lageado Grande.jpg|left|thumb|Lajeado Grande River]]
Crissiumal is situated in the [[Uruguay River]] Basin and the Turvo-Santa Rosa-Santo Cristo Sub-basin.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=802&cod_menu=800&tipo_menu=MEIO&cod_conteudo=1372|title=Socioeconomic Atlas of Rio Grande do Sul|access-date=February 3, 2017|website=www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br|archive-date=February 4, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170204085537/http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=802&cod_menu=800&tipo_menu=MEIO&cod_conteudo=1372|url-status=dead}}</ref> The main rivers are the [[Uruguay River|Uruguay]], [[Buricá River|Buricá]], Lajeado Grande, and Reúno,<ref name="Diagnóstico Local Crissiumal-RS" /> along with smaller watercourses known as ''lajeados'', including Principal, Caçador, Mirim, Teimoso, Fazenda, and Nass.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal" /> The Uruguay River is formed by the confluence of the [[Canoas River (Santa Catarina)|Canoas]] and [[Pelotas River|Pelotas]] rivers at the border between [[Rio Grande do Sul]] and [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.riosvivos.org.br/a/Canal/Rio+Uruguai/542|title=Uruguay River|access-date=February 10, 2017|website=www.riosvivos.org.br|last=Informação|first=Mais Empresas Tecnologia da|archive-date=February 27, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170227215947/http://www.riosvivos.org.br/a/Canal/Rio+Uruguai/542|url-status=dead}}</ref> marking the border between [[Brazil]] and [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Uruguay River |url=http://escola.britannica.com.br/article/483623/Uruguai,%20rio |access-date=February 10, 2017 |website=escola.britannica.com.br}}</ref> It flows into the [[Río de la Plata]] and has major Brazilian tributaries, including the [[Canoas River (Santa Catarina)|Canoas]], [[Pelotas River|Pelotas]], [[Passo Fundo River|Passo Fundo]], [[Chapecó River|Chapecó]], [[Ijuí River|Ijuí]], [[Ibicuí River|Ibicuí]], and [[Quaraí River|Quaraí]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://palmitos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/12091|title=Palmitos Tourism Portal > Attractions > Uruguay River|access-date=February 10, 2017|website=palmitos.sc.gov.br|archive-date=February 11, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170211075853/http://palmitos.sc.gov.br/turismo/item/detalhe/12091|url-status=dead}}</ref> The Lajeado Grande River originates from the confluence of the Lajeado Herval Novo and Lajeado Herval Grande rivers in [[Campo Novo]].<ref name=":37">{{cite book|title=From Criciúma to Crissiumal: Social Studies|last1=Schwanke|first1=Teresinha Maria Lippert|last2=Pohl|first2=Vera Lúcia Bagetti|publisher=SETREM|year=2007|location=Três de Maio}}</ref> Its flow increases as it passes through Crissiumal, [[Três Passos]], and [[Tiradentes do Sul]] before joining the Uruguay River.<ref name=":37" /> The Buricá River, with a length of 195 km, has its main sources in [[Chiapeta|Chiapetta]], and its main tributary, the Inhacorá River, originates in [[Santo Augusto]], Chiapetta, [[São Valério do Sul]], and [[Alegria, Rio Grande do Sul|Alegria]].<ref name=":21a">{{cite web|url=http://www.repositorio.jesuita.org.br/bitstream/handle/UNISINOS/2067/cleria%20bittencourt%20meller.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y|title=Tecer, Lançar e Recolher Redes de Saberes Ambientais de Atores Sociais Envolvidos com a Suinocultura|access-date=March 11, 2017|publisher=Unisinos Digital Repository|last=Meller|first=Cleria Bitencorte}}</ref> These rivers have potential for hydroelectric power generation.<ref name=":1" /> Swine farming pollutes watercourses, and mechanized agriculture accelerates [[erosion]], carrying [[soil]] into rivers.<ref name=":1" />

Part of Crissiumal's territory lies over the Serra Geral I Aquifer System, and part over the Serra Geral II Aquifer System.<ref name=":35">{{cite web|url=http://www.cprm.gov.br/publique/media/mapa_hidrogeologico_RS.pdf|title=Hydrogeological Map of Rio Grande do Sul|date=November 2005|access-date=February 6, 2017|publisher=Mineral Resources Research Company}}</ref> The Serra Geral I Aquifer, covering the southeastern part of the municipality, including the urban area, is capped by thick reddish soil and has a specific capacity of 1 to 4 m<sup>3</sup>/h/m.<ref name=":35" /> The Serra Geral II Aquifer, near the Uruguay River, has a specific capacity below 0.5 m<sup>3</sup>/h/m.<ref name=":35" /> The Serra Geral I Aquifer has high to medium potential for groundwater in rocks with fracture porosity, while the Serra Geral II has medium to low potential.<ref name=":35" />

Crissiumal is part of the [[Atlantic Forest]] [[biome]].<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?lang=&codmun=430600&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Cinfograficos:-dados-gerais-do-municipio |access-date=February 3, 2017 |website=ibge.gov.br}}</ref> It lies in the agroecological region of the Upper Uruguay River Valley and the Três Passos sub-region.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal"/> The vegetation is classified as seasonal deciduous forest.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal"/><ref name="Floresta Alto Uruguai"/> It features a high percentage of species exclusive to the subtropical forest, a relatively low number of tall tree species, and an almost complete absence of [[Epiphyte|epiphytes]].<ref name="Floresta Alto Uruguai"/> Currently, the forest is fragmented into small, scattered patches among crops, primarily [[maize]], [[wheat]], and [[soybean]]. The most abundant species of flora belong to the ''[[Leguminosae]]'', ''[[Boraginaceae]]'', ''[[Sapindaceae]]'', and ''[[Rutaceae]]'' families.<ref name="Floresta Alto Uruguai"/> Vegetation covers 10 to 20% of the municipality's area, with the remainder occupied by annual crops and pastures.<ref name="Leite Crissiumal"/> In 2008, forests covered 11,589.15 hectares, with the rest consisting of exposed soil, fields, water bodies, and urban areas.<ref name=":27">{{cite web|url=https://periodicos.ufsm.br/reget/article/download/5543/3594|title=Dynamics of Forest Cover in Crissiumal-RS Over a Twenty-Year Period|access-date=February 3, 2017|publisher=Electronic Journal of Management, Education, and Environmental Technology|last=Lippert|first=Diogo Belmonte}}</ref> From 1988 to 2008, forest regeneration amounted to 6,271.28 hectares due to the expansion of remaining forest fragments, the emergence of pioneer vegetation in former agricultural areas, and the planting of commercial forests, mainly consisting of ''Eucalyptus'' and ''Pinus'' trees.<ref name=":27" />

Deforestation from 1988 to 2008 totaled 1,508.04 hectares, occurring in areas now used for agriculture.<ref name=":27" /> Among the region's flora species are the silk floss tree (''[[Chorisia speciosa]]''), golden shower (''[[Peltophorum dubium]]''), rosemary (''[[Holocalyx|Holocalyx balansae]]''), grapia (''[[Apuleia leiocarpa]]''), cabreúva (''[[Myrocarpus frondosus]]''), and pink ipê (''[[Tabebuia avellanedae]]'').<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.rbma.org.br/rbma/pdf/caderno_11.pdf|title=The Atlantic Forest Biosphere Reserve in Rio Grande do Sul|access-date=February 15, 2017|publisher=Atlantic Forest Biosphere Reserve}}</ref> The municipality has no permanent preservation areas, so preservation areas are those mandated by the [[Brazilian Forest Code]], which establishes minimum preservation strips along rivers or watercourses based on their width, as well as around lagoons, springs, hilltops, and slopes with inclines exceeding 45%.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2011-2014/2012/lei/L12651compilado.htm|title=L12651compilado|access-date=February 15, 2017|website=www.planalto.gov.br}}</ref>

==== Fauna ====
The fauna includes several [[mammals]], various [[birds]], some [[reptiles]], and [[fish]].<ref name="Diagnóstico Local Crissiumal-RS" /> Notable species in the region include birds such as the [[Caracara (genus)|caracara]] (''Pyrrhocoma rufiops''), roadside hawk ([[Buteo magnirostris|''Buteo maguirostris'']]), white-tipped dove (''[[Leptotila verreauxi]]''), red-crested cardinal (''[[Paroaria coronata]]''), rufous hornero (''[[Furnarius rufus]]''), and campo flicker ([[Campo flicker|''Colaptes campestris'']]); mammals such as the oncilla (''[[Leopardus tigrinus]]''); crab-eating raccoon ([[Crab-eating raccoon|''Procyon cancrivorus'']]), Brazilian guinea pig (''[[Cavia aperea]]''), and six-banded armadillo ([[Six-banded armadillo|''Euphractus sexcinctus'']]); and reptiles including the Amazon false coral snake (''[[Oxyrhopus rhombifer]]''), racer (''[[Mastigodryas]]'' ''sp.''), coral snake (''[[Micrurus]]'' ''sp.''), and common lizard (''Schreibersii'' ''sp.'').<ref name=":48">{{cite web|url=https://portal.tce.rs.gov.br/pmsr/PMRSU/Crissiumal/PMGIRS_Crissiumal_2.pdf|title=Municipal Basic Sanitation Plan and Integrated Solid Waste Management Plan Volume II Diagnosis of the Situation of Basic Sanitation Services|access-date=February 14, 2017|publisher=Rio Grande do Sul Court of Auditors|author=IPOA - Porto Alegre Environmental Institute|archive-date=February 15, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170215021650/https://portal.tce.rs.gov.br/pmsr/PMRSU/Crissiumal/PMGIRS_Crissiumal_2.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> Six species are threatened, three of which are [[amphibians]].<ref name=":1" /> Some fish species spawn in the headwaters of Uruguay River tributaries, such as the Lajeado Grande.<ref>{{cite book|title=Rio Grande do Sul and Ecology: Selected Chronicles of a Contemporary Naturalist|last=Roessler|first=Henrique Luiz|year=2005|edition=2nd|location=Porto Alegre|isbn=85-98053-05-8|publisher=SEMA}}</ref>

==== Environmental issues ====
[[File:Lixo em Crissiumal.jpg|thumb|Improperly disposed waste on a public street in Crissiumal. According to the 2010 Demographic Census, 24 households discarded waste in vacant lots or public spaces in the municipality.<ref>{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=67&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Ccenso-demografico-2010:-resultados-do-universo-caracteristicas-da-populacao-e-dos-domicilios- |access-date=May 7, 2017 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>]]

According to the Socioeconomic Atlas of Rio Grande do Sul, the main environmental problems in the Uruguay River Basin, which includes Crissiumal, are untreated sewage discharge into [[watercourses]], liquid waste disposal from swine and poultry farming, [[deforestation]], groundwater exploitation that may contaminate deeper water sources, [[erosion]], agricultural [[soil compaction]], soil and water contamination by agrochemicals, recurring droughts and floods, and [[siltation]] of water flows.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=802&cod_menu=800&tipo_menu=MEIO&cod_conteudo=1374|title=Socioeconomic Atlas of Rio Grande do Sul|access-date=March 11, 2017|website=www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br|archive-date=March 20, 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160320155604/http://www.atlassocioeconomico.rs.gov.br/conteudo.asp?cod_menu_filho=802&cod_menu=800&tipo_menu=MEIO&cod_conteudo=1374|url-status=dead}}</ref> In the Turvo-Santa Rosa-Santo Cristo sub-basin, there is also occupation of permanent preservation areas and a lack of riparian vegetation.<ref name=":21a" /> Livestock and agriculture, which use large amounts of fertilizers, can disrupt the nitrogen cycle, affecting water quality, and the decomposition of waste such as manure, urine, and feed can pollute air, water, and soil.<ref name=":21a" /> Among the municipality's anthropogenic activities recorded by the FEPAM, the main ones are swine farming, retail fuel storage/trade (gas stations), pesticide storage, water supply systems with dams, and road transport of hazardous products or waste.<ref>{{cite web |title=FEPAM - Henrique Luiz Roessler State Environmental Protection Foundation / RS |url=https://www.fepam.rs.gov.br/quem-somos |access-date=March 11, 2017 |website=www.fepam.rs.gov.br}}</ref> An environmental liability exists in the Industrial neighborhood, where homes were built over a former waste dump, still producing odors, gases, and [[leachate]].<ref name=":48" />

=== Climate ===
[[File:Dia nublado em Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|Cloudy day in Crissiumal, seen from the Vila União square.]]
Crissiumal has a [[humid subtropical climate]].<ref name=":37" /> Its location in the southernmost part of the country leads to four well-defined seasons in terms of temperature, with evenly distributed rainfall throughout the year.<ref name=":110">{{cite journal|date=June 7, 2013|title=Rio Grande do Sul Climate: When to Visit Rio Grande do Sul|journal=Travel Guide|url=http://www.guiaviagem.org/rio-grande-do-sul-clima/}}</ref> Summers, from December to March, are very hot, while winters, from June to September, are moderately cold, with minimum temperatures around 9°C.<ref name=":110" /> Maximum winter temperatures range from 18°C to 20°C.<ref name=":110"/> Spring and fall have mild and pleasant temperatures.<ref name=":110"/> The climate is characterized by abundant precipitation, fog formation, and occasional frosts.<ref name=":0">{{cite web|url=http://revistas.fee.tche.br/index.php/boletim-geografico-rs/article/view/3344/3414|title=Regional Division of Rio Grande do Sul|date=1956|access-date=January 27, 2017|last=Pacheco|first=Maria Fagundes de Souza Doeca}}</ref> The average annual temperature is 18°C,<ref name=":37" /> with absolute maximums exceeding 41°C.<ref name=":0" /> The temperature range spans from 0°C to 38°C.<ref name=":37" /> From June to August, temperatures drop, and severe frosts may occur,<ref name=":37" /> a normal phenomenon given due to the latitude and topography.<ref name=":46">{{cite web|url=http://coralx.ufsm.br/ifcrs/clima.htm|title=Climate|access-date=February 13, 2017|website=coralx.ufsm.br}}</ref> Along the [[Uruguay River]], fog formed by humidity prevents frost, favoring agriculture.<ref name=":37" /> [[Rainfall]] is heavy and well-distributed, with winter months (July to September) being the wettest.<ref name=":110" /> Annual rainfall typically ranges from 1,650 to Several weather events, including snow, frost, drought, flooding, strong winds, and hail, have significantly impacted Crissiumal.<ref name=":37" /> On August 20 and 21, 1965, the region experienced snowfall.<ref name=":39">{{cite web|url=http://www.topsulnoticias.com.br/news/curiosidade-a-neve-de-20-de-agosto-de-1965-no-rs/|title=CURIOSIDADE: A Neve de 20 de Agosto de 1965 no RS :: TopSul Notícias|access-date=2017-02-13|website=www.topsulnoticias.com.br}}</ref> The phenomenon began at ten o'clock on one day and continued into the next, forming snow layers exceeding one meter in some areas.<ref name=":39" /> House and shed roofs collapsed, telephone and power lines snapped, water pipes burst, and livestock and wildlife perished.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.grupogaz.com.br/gazetadaserra/noticia/457561-ha_50_anos_o_centro_serra_estava_tingido_de_branco.html|title=Há 50 anos o Centro Serra estava tingido de branco - Regional {{!}} Jornal Gazeta da Serra|access-date=2017-02-13|website=www.grupogaz.com.br}}</ref> In August 2010, the area saw precipitation of ice pellets, also known as [[graupel]], which, according to meteorologists, is a rare occurrence at lower altitudes.<ref>{{cite news|date=2010-08-04|title=Pelo menos 16 cidades do Sul registraram neve nesta madrugada|newspaper=O Globo|url=https://oglobo.globo.com/brasil/pelo-menos-16-cidades-do-sul-registraram-neve-nesta-madrugada-2970597}}</ref> The most severe drought lasted four months, from late 2004 to mid-2005, prompting a declaration of a state of emergency.<ref name=":37" /> The drought led to the postponement of the start of classes at two municipal schools and caused the total loss of [[soybean]] and [[maize]] crops.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radiogaucha.com.br/especial/rs/intercentenario/19,0,786593,|title=Falta de água faz escolas adiarem início das aulas em Crissiumal {{!}} - Inter Centenario|access-date=2017-02-13|website=www.radiogaucha.com.br}}</ref> The most significant flood occurred in 1983 when the [[Uruguay River]] reached 25 meters.<ref name=":37" /> Another flood took place on June 29, 2014, when the Uruguay River reached 19.77 meters.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.topsulnoticias.com.br/news/clima-tempo-ha-um-ano-crissiumal-e-regiao-enfrentava-a-segunda-maior-enchente-da-historia-do-rioទ11;rio-uruguai-rs/|title=CLIMA TEMPO: Há Um Ano, Crissiumal e Região Enfrentava a segunda maior Enchente da História do Rio Uruguai – RS :: TopSul Notícias|access-date=2017-02-13|website=www.topsulnoticias.com.br}}</ref> In 2005, a hailstorm struck the city and several rural areas.<ref name=":37" /> During this event, approximately 570 people were left homeless, and over 100 were treated for injuries at the Charity Hospital.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.camara.gov.br/sileg/integras/320678.pdf|title=A tempestade que destruiu Crissiumal - ==Demographics==
The population of Crissiumal in 2016, as estimated by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), was 14,233 inhabitants, with a [[population density]] of 38.89 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name=":30" /> According to the 2010 Census, 6,903 inhabitants were male and 7,171 were female.<ref name="IBGE_Cidades">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |title=IBGE Cidades |accessdate=November 12, 2011}}</ref> The same census reported that 43.48% of the population was urban, with 6,124 inhabitants living in urban areas and 7,960 in rural areas.<ref name="IBGE_Cidades"/> The [[life expectancy]] at birth is 75.11 years, and the [[infant mortality]] rate is 8.47 per thousand live births.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fee.rs.gov.br/perfil-socioeconomico/municipios/detalhe/?municipio=Crissiumal|title=FEE|accessdate=2017-02-04|website=www.fee.rs.gov.br}}</ref>

The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of Crissiumal is considered high by the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP). The HDI ranges from 0 to 1, with values closer to 0 indicating lower human development and those closer to 1 indicating higher development.<ref>{{cite journal|date=2013-07-29|title=Porto Alegre is the only city in RS on the list of the 50 with the best HDI in the country|journal=Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/noticia/2013/07/porto-alegre-e-unica-cidade-do-rs-na-lista-das-50-com-melhor-idh-do-pais.html}}</ref> In 2010, Crissiumal's HDI was 0.712.<ref name=":31">{{cite web |title=Crissiumal, RS |url=http://www.atlasbrasil.org.br/perfil/municipio/4306007 |accessdate=February 3, 2017 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano no Brasil}}</ref> Specifically for education, the index value is 0.616,<ref name=":31" /> compared to Brazil's national education index of 0.637.<ref name=":32">{{cite journal|date=2013-07-29|title=Municipal HDI of Brazil grows 47.5% in 20 years, says UNDP|journal=Brasil|url=http://g1.globo.com/brasil/noticia/2013/07/idh-municipal-do-brasil-cresce-475-em-20-anos-aponta-pnud.html}}</ref> The longevity index is 0.835<ref name=":31" /> (compared to Brazil's 0.739<ref name=":32" />), and the income index is 0.702<ref name=":31" /> (compared to Brazil's 0.816<ref name=":32" />). The [[Gini coefficient]], which measures [[social inequality]], is 0.39, where 1.00 represents the highest inequality and 0.00 the lowest.<ref name="GINI" /> The poverty incidence, as measured by the IBGE, is 17.71%, with a lower limit of 11.56%, an upper limit of 23.87%, and a subjective poverty incidence of 12.82%.<ref name="GINI">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/xtras/temas.php?nomemun=Crissiumal&codmun=430600&tema=mpobreza2003&desc=Mapa%20de%20Pobreza%20e%20Desigualdade%20-%20Munic%C3%ADpios%20Brasileiros&legenda=Fonte%3A%20IBGE%2C%20Censo%20Demogr%C3%A1fico%202000%20e%20Pesquisa%20de%20Or%C3%A7amentos%20Familiares%20-%20POF%20%202002%2F2003.%3Cbr%20%2F%3E%20NOTA%3A%20A%20estimativa%20do%20consumo%20para%20a%20gera%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20destes%20indicadores%20foi%20obtida%20utilizando%20o%20m%C3%A9todo%20da%20estimativa%20de%20pequenas%20%C3%A1reas%20dos%20autores%20Elbers%2C%20Lanjouw%20e%20Lanjouw%20%282002%29.%0D%0A&uf=rs&r= |title=Mapa de Pobreza e Desigualdade - Municípios Brasileiros 2003 |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |date=2003 |accessdate=November 15, 2011}}</ref>

Between 1991 and 2000, Crissiumal's population decreased by 3,003 inhabitants. This decline may be attributed to an [[emigration]] flow toward the Metropolitan, North Coast, and Serra regions of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], as between 1995 and 2000, the net migration balance of the COREDE Noroeste Colonial region, to which Crissiumal belonged, was -12,862, while the COREDE regions of Metropolitano Delta do Jacuí, Serra, and Litoral had positive balances ranging from 15,000 to 20,000.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.abep.nepo.unicamp.br/docs/anais/outros/4EncNacSobreMigracao/SCII-3.pdf|title=Population movements in Rio Grande do Sul: an inter- and intra-regional perspective through the Regional Economic Development Councils (Coredes)|accessdate=February 7, 2017|publisher=Universidade Estadual de Campinas|last=Jardim|first=Maria de Lourdes}}</ref>

===Racial composition===
The [[IBGE]] classification by color or race is divided into the following categories: white, brown (''pardo''), black, yellow, and indigenous.<ref name=":21c">{{cite book|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/livros/liv95987.pdf|title=Methodology of the 2010 Demographic Census|publisher=IBGE|year=2016|editor-last=IBGE|location=Rio de Janeiro|isbn=978-85-240-4362-8|issn=0101-2843|edition=2nd|series=Methodological Reports|volume=41}}</ref> The black population corresponds to the combined total of brown and black populations.<ref>{{cite journal|title=IBGE uses black color classification; black group includes blacks and browns - News - UOL Vestibular|journal=UOL Vestibular|url=https://vestibular.uol.com.br/noticias/redacao/2013/05/03/ibge-usa-classificacao-de-cor-preta-grupo-negro-reune-pretos-e-pardos.htm}}</ref> Racial or color composition is determined by self-declaration.<ref name=":21c" /> In 2010, Crissiumal's population consisted of 12,657 whites (89.87%), 1,311 browns (9.31%), and 108 blacks (0.77%).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://informacoesdobrasil.com.br/dados/rio-grande-do-sul/crissiumal/|title=Crissiumal (RS): Summary of Information - Economic, statistical, and political data|accessdate=2017-02-04|website=informacoesdobrasil.com.br}}</ref> The white population is primarily descended from Germans, Italians, and Poles.<ref name=":21b">{{cite web|url=http://www.fahor.com.br/publicacoes/TFC/Economia/2015/Kati_Franciele_Lima_Gihl.pdf|title=Public policies offered in the municipality of Crissiumal - RS to encourage the development of local family agroindustry|accessdate=May 13, 2017|publisher=Faculdade Horizontina|last=Gihl|first=Kati Franciele de Lima}}</ref>

Crissiumal is located in the country with the largest absolute number of Catholics in the world.<ref name=":23">{{cite web|url=http://www.hojemais.com.br/andradina/noticia/articulistas/brasil-maior-pais-catolico-do-mundo-e-um-estado-laico|title=Brazil, the largest Catholic country in the world, is a secular state|accessdate=2017-02-02|website=www.hojemais.com.br}}</ref> However, the [[Constitution of Brazil|1988 Brazilian Constitution]] guarantees [[freedom of religion]], prohibiting any form of [[religious intolerance]] and establishing an official [[Separation of church and state|separation of Church and State]], making Brazil a [[secular state]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.amperj.org.br/artigos/view.asp?ID=99|title=Association of the Public Prosecutor's Office of the State of Rio de Janeiro - Amperj|accessdate=2017-02-04|website=www.amperj.org.br}}</ref> In October 2009, an agreement with the [[Vatican City|Vatican]] was approved by the Senate and enacted by the President in February 2010, recognizing the [[Concordat of 2008|Legal Status of the Catholic Church in Brazil]]. The project faced criticism from parliamentarians who viewed its approval as undermining the secular state.<ref>{{cite journal|date=2009-10-08|title=Senate approves agreement with the Vatican|journal=O Globo|url=http://oglobo.globo.com/economia/senado-aprova-acordo-com-vaticano-3161783}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_Ato2007-2010/2010/Decreto/D7107.htm|title=Decree No. 7107|accessdate=2017-02-04|website=www.planalto.gov.br}}</ref>

Reflecting Crissiumal's cultural diversity, the city is home to a variety of religious expressions. Although it developed within a predominantly [[Catholicism|Catholic]] social framework, dozens of different [[Protestantism|Protestant]] denominations can now be found in the city. Additionally, the growth of evangelicals has been notable, reaching 34.69% of the population.<ref name=":5">{{cite web |title=IBGE |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=91&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Ccenso-demografico-2010:-resultados-da-amostra-religiao- |accessdate=2017-01-30 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> According to data from the 2010 census conducted by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, Crissiumal's population is composed of [[Catholicism|Catholics]] (64.48%), [[Protestantism|Evangelicals]] (34.69%), [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (0.09%), and [[Jehovah's Witnesses]] (0.06%).<ref name=":5" /> The percentage of those with no religious affiliation is 0.36%, lower than the national average of 7.3%.<ref name=":5" /> Among evangelicals, 3,228 belong to the [[Evangelical Lutheran Church of Brazil]], 677 to the [[Brazil for Christ Pentecostal Church]], 310 to the [[Assemblies of God]], and 179 to the [[Foursquare Church]].<ref name=":5" />

==Politics==
Under the [[Constitution of Brazil|1988 Brazilian Constitution]], Crissiumal is part of a [[republic|federative republic]] with a [[presidential system]]. This form of government was inspired by the American model, though Brazil's legal system follows the [[Roman law|Roman]]-[[Germanic law|Germanic]] tradition of [[positive law]].<ref name=":40">{{cite web|url=http://www.oas.org/juridico/mla/en/bra/en_bra-int-des-ordrjur.html|title=The Brazilian Legal System|accessdate=2017-02-01|website=www.oas.org}}</ref> Municipal administration is carried out by the [[Executive branch|executive]] and [[Legislative branch|legislative]] branches,<ref name=":16">{{cite web|url=http://www.camaracrissiumal.com.br/aplicativos/projetoleis/legislaturas/arquivos/Lei%20Organica%20de%20Crissiumal.pdf|title=Organic Law of the Municipality of Crissiumal|accessdate=February 1, 2017|publisher=Câmara de Vereadores de Crissiumal}}</ref> with all members of these branches directly elected.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.citymayors.com/government/brazil_government.html|title=City Mayors: Brazil federal, state and local government|accessdate=2017-02-05|website=www.citymayors.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.jusbrasil.com.br/topicos/295354/eleicao-direta|title=Direct Election|accessdate=2017-02-05|website=Jusbrasil}}</ref>

In Crissiumal, the [[Executive branch|Executive Power]] is represented by the mayor and a cabinet of secretaries, in accordance with the model outlined by the [[Constitution of Brazil|Federal Constitution]]. However, the [[Organic Law|Municipal Organic Law]] stipulates that public administration must provide the population with effective tools to exercise participatory democracy. The city's first mayor was Lauro Pedro Thomas, elected on February 20, 1955, and sworn in on February 28, 1955.<ref name=":17" /> Over thirteen terms, eleven mayors have served Crissiumal.<ref name=":36" /> The mayor until 2020 was Roberto Bergmann, from the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement Party]] (PMDB), elected in a supplementary election in 2015,<ref name=":58">{{cite journal|date=2015-06-14|title=After an off-season election, Crissiumal has a new mayor and vice-mayor|journal=Rio Grande do Sul|url=http://g1.globo.com/rs/rio-grande-do-sul/noticia/2015/06/apos-eleicao-fora-de-epoca-cidade-de-crissiumal-tem-novo-prefeito-e-vice.html}}</ref> re-elected in the [[2016 Brazilian municipal elections]], and sworn in the following year.<ref name=":49">{{cite web|url=http://divulga.tse.jus.br/oficial/index.html|title=TSE - Election Results Disclosure|accessdate=2017-02-14|website=divulga.tse.jus.br}}</ref> He received 5,351 valid votes, representing 55.20% of the votes in the first round.<ref name=":49" /> As the municipality has fewer than 200,000 voters, there was no second round.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/imprensa/noticias-tse/2016/Outubro/confira-as-cidades-onde-havera-segundo-turno-em-30-de-outubro|title=Check the cities where there will be a second round on October 30|accessdate=2017-02-15|website=www.tse.jus.br}}</ref>

The [[Legislative branch|Legislative Power]] is exercised by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of 9 councilors,<ref name="Vereadores">{{cite web |url=http://www.camaracrissiumal.com.br/index.php/2011-08-13-15-09-47 |publisher=Câmara Municipal de Vereadores de Crissiumal - RS |title=Vereadores |accessdate=November 14, 2011}}</ref> elected for four-year terms (in compliance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil]], which sets a maximum of nine councilors for municipalities with up to {{formatnum:15000}} inhabitants).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/constituicao/Constituicao.htm#29 |publisher=planalto.gov.br |title=Constitution of the Federative Republic of Brazil: Article 29 |accessdate=November 14, 2011}}</ref> The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for administration and the [[Executive branch|Executive]], particularly the [[participatory budgeting]] (Budget Guidelines Law). As of 2017, the Chamber consists of three councilors from the [[Brazilian Democratic Movement Party]] (PMDB), two from the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD), one from the [[Communist Party of Brazil]] (PC do B), one from the [[Brazilian Labour Party (1981)|Brazilian Labour Party]] (PTB), one from the [[Progressistas|Progressive Party]] (PP), and one from the [[Brazilian Socialist Party]] (PSB).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.eleicoes2016.com.br/candidatos-vereador-crissiumal-rs/2/|title=Councilors Elected in Crissiumal / RS|accessdate=2017-02-14|website=Eleições 2016}}</ref> The local [[Judiciary|Judicial Power]] is exercised through the Crissiumal Court District, which serves the municipalities of Crissiumal and [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]],<ref>{{cite web |title=Court Districts and Jurisdictional Municipalities |url=http://www.tjrs.jus.br/site/poder_judiciario/comarcas/comarcas_e_municipios_jurisdicionados/ |accessdate=2017-02-02 |website=www.tjrs.jus.br |archive-date=2017-01-31 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170131184924/http://www1.tjrs.jus.br/site/poder_judiciario/comarcas/comarcas_e_municipios_jurisdicionados/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> and was established by State Law No. 2,666, of August 6, 1955, separating from the [[Três Passos]] court district.<ref name=":36" /> Crissiumal is also the seat of the 91st electoral zone, covering the municipalities of Crissiumal, [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]], and [[Sede Nova]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tre-rs.jus.br/apps/zonas/index.php?acao=zona&zona=91|title=TRE-RS - Tribunal Regional Eleitoral do Rio Grande do Sul|accessdate=2017-02-02|website=www.tre-rs.jus.br|archive-date=2017-02-03|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170203075220/http://www.tre-rs.jus.br/apps/zonas/index.php?acao=zona&zona=91|url-status=dead}}</ref> According to the [[Superior Electoral Court]], the municipality had approximately 11,506 voters in 2016.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/estatisticas-eleitorais-2016/eleicoes-2016|title=2016 Electorate|accessdate=2017-02-02|website=www.tse.jus.br|archive-date=2017-07-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170720055405/http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/estatisticas-eleitorais-2016/eleicoes-2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Municipal councils also operate in Crissiumal, complementing the legislative process and the work of municipal secretariats. These councils, mandatorily composed of representatives from various sectors of organized [[civil society]], address diverse issues. The following municipal councils are currently active: Municipal Council for Agricultural, Livestock, and Irrigation Policy,<ref>{{cite ===Law===
[[File:Lei Municipal Número 1 de Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|233x233px|Municipal Law No. 1 of March 2, 1955.]]
Brazilian law is based on the tradition of the [[civil code]], part of the Romano-Germanic legal system. Thus, [[Civil law (common law)|civil law]] concepts prevail over [[common law]] practices.<ref name=":40" /> Most of [[Law of Brazil|Brazilian legislation]] is [[Codification (law)|codified]], although non-codified statutes form a substantial part of the system, playing a complementary role.<ref name=":40" /> The legal system is grounded in the [[Constitution of Brazil|1988 Federal Constitution]], enacted on October 5, 1988, which serves as Brazil's fundamental law.<ref name=":41">{{cite web|url=https://www.oas.org/juridico/mla/pt/bra/pt_bra-int-des-ordrjur.html|title=The Brazilian legal system is based on the Roman-Germanic tradition|accessdate=2017-02-13|website=www.oas.org}}</ref> Municipalities have autonomy to draft their Organic Law and other laws, but this legislative autonomy is limited by the principles of the Federal Constitution and the provisions of the State Constitution to which they belong.<ref name=":41" /> The [[Organic Law]] is a kind of municipal constitution and must be voted on in two rounds with a minimum interval of ten days and approved by two-thirds of the Municipal Chamber members.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.jusbrasil.com.br/topicos/296619/lei-organica-do-municipio|title=Municipal Organic Law|accessdate=2017-02-13|website=Jusbrasil}}</ref> Crissiumal's current Organic Law was enacted on November 25, 2003.<ref name=":16" />

The municipality is responsible for preparing its annual budget, multi-year plan, and budget guidelines law, establishing construction regulations, subdivisions, street layouts, and urban zoning, regulating the use of public spaces, overseeing public transportation and taxi services, and regulating the posting of posters and advertisements.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibam.org.br/media/arquivos/estudos/manual_prefeito.pdf|title=Mayor's Manual|accessdate=February 13, 2017|publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Administração Municipal|archive-date=January 10, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170110180005/http://www.ibam.org.br/media/arquivos/estudos/manual_prefeito.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> According to the [[Supreme Federal Court]], municipalities have the authority to create laws setting the operating hours of commercial establishments, environmental laws within their local interest, and laws extending free urban public transportation.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.stf.jus.br/portal/constituicao/artigobd.asp?item=446|title=The Constitution and the Supreme :: STF - Supreme Federal Court|accessdate=2017-02-13|website=www.stf.jus.br|publisher=Supremo Tribunal Federal}}</ref> Municipalities are also tasked with drafting the Municipal Tax Code, which establishes rules for taxes, fees, and contributions within their jurisdiction, including the IPTU, the ISS, the ITBI, service fees, police service fees, and the special assessment tax.<ref>{{cite web |title=Public Education - Library - Citizenship |url=http://www.educacaopublica.rj.gov.br/biblioteca/cidadania/0062.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090410185202/http://www.educacaopublica.rj.gov.br/biblioteca/cidadania/0062.html |archive-date=2009-04-10 |accessdate=2017-02-13 |website=www.educacaopublica.rj.gov.br}}</ref>

===Symbols===
The symbols of Crissiumal are the flag, the coat of arms, and the anthem. The coat of arms and flag were established as symbols by Municipal Law No. 472, of December 28, 1977.<ref name=":36" /> The coat of arms is a Portuguese shield divided by a black cross, symbolizing the arrival of Jesuits and Spaniards in the municipal territory.<ref name=":36" /> In one section of the shield is a cluster of bamboo, which gave the municipality its name, and in another, a bovine specimen, reflecting the importance of cattle breeding to the municipality.<ref name=":36" /> Below the shield is a ribbon with the inscription 18/12 - Crissiumal - 1955, and above it is a crown with three towers.<ref name=":36" /> The flag's colors are red, representing the tenacity and work ethic of its people, blue, symbolizing the sky that covers the municipality with hope, and white, conveying a message of faith from its inhabitants.<ref name=":36" /> The Crissiumal anthem was created through a contest organized by the Culture Center.<ref name=":36" /> The first stanza of the anthem describes the formation of the first settlement in Crissiumal:
<poem>
:"Amid forests, bamboos, and wilderness,
:A new settlement opened in clearings,
:Treading the northwest region,
:Crissiumal, beautiful and strong, emerged."
</poem>

==Subdivisions==
[[File:Mapa da cidade de Crissiumal.jpg|thumb|400x400px|Map of the urban area of Crissiumal]]
According to Crissiumal's Municipal Organic Law, its territory is divided into three categories: the Urban Perimeter, encompassing the city; the urban circumscription, comprising Districts and neighborhoods; and the Rural Zone, consisting of rural properties and smallholdings.<ref name=":16" /> According to Crissiumal's Master Plan, the municipality is divided into Macro-zones. Macro-zoning establishes rules for territorial organization and aims to define guidelines for the use of territorial planning and land use and occupation zoning instruments.<ref name=":6">{{cite web |title=Crissiumal Master Plan |url=http://transparencia.crissiumal-rs.com.br/Componentes/Leis/arquivos/2.691-2011.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220402113133/http://transparencia.crissiumal-rs.com.br/Componentes/Leis/arquivos/2.691-2011.pdf |archive-date=2022-04-02 |accessdate=January 30, 2017 |publisher=Prefeitura Municipal de Crissiumal}}</ref> The macro-zones include urban macro-zones, rural macro-zones, a tourism macro-zone, and a special macro-zone.<ref name=":6" />

The urban macro-zones consist of the consolidated urban macro-zone, formed by the urban perimeter of the municipal seat, where the majority of the urban population is concentrated, and the controlled-use urban macro-zone, comprising localities considered urban districts of the seat, Esquina Gaúcha, Vista Nova, Lajeado Grande, and Planalto.<ref name=":6" /> The rural macro-zones are characterized by areas suitable for agricultural and livestock activities and other primary sector activities. The rural macro-zones include São Sebastião, Esquina Gaúcha, Três Ilhas, Vista Alegre, Esquina Uruguai, Linha Brasil, Alto Crissiumal, Boa Esperança, Bela Vista Schmidt, Bela Vista Brem, São Vicente, and Vila Bender.<ref name=":6" /> The tourist macrozone is the area designated for tourism. Residential use, tourism, leisure, accommodation facilities, commerce, services, and tourism support are encouraged there.<ref name=":6" /> The special macro-zone corresponds to the industrial area.<ref name=":6" />

===Primary sector===
[[File:Soja em Crissiumal.jpg|thumb|Soybeans are the main temporary crop in Crissiumal. In the foreground, a soybean plantation, and in the background, a view of the city.]]
In Crissiumal, family farming is a tradition, and some farmers still use hoes, animal-drawn plows, sickles, carts, and threshers.<ref name=":37" /> However, modernization has introduced tractors, planters, harvesters, and other machinery.<ref name=":37" /> In [[livestock farming]], two programs support this activity: Via Lácteo, which offers courses, financial assistance, and guidance to dairy producers, and the Criar Program, which assists pig farmers with land leveling, sand, and gravel for constructing pig breeding facilities.<ref name=":37" /> Of the city's total [[Gross Domestic Product|GDP]], 68,456 thousand reais is the gross value added by agriculture in 2014.<ref name=":50" /> According to the IBGE, in 2015, the municipality had a herd of 27,200 [[cattle]], 43,000 [[pigs]], 138 [[horses]], 18 [[goats]], 566 [[sheep]], 23,000 [[chickens]], and 300 [[quails]].<ref name=":51">{{cite web |title=Livestock - 2015 |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=159&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Cpecuaria-2015 |accessdate=2017-02-18 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> In 2015, the city produced 30,000 thousand liters of milk from 10,000 [[cows]].<ref name=":51" /> Additionally, 350,000 dozen chicken eggs, 10,000 kilograms of [[honey]], and 60 kilograms of [[wool]] from sheep were produced.<ref name=":51" /> In temporary agriculture, the main crops produced in 2015 were [[soybeans]] (25,920 tons), [[corn]] (17,991 tons), [[wheat]] (5,775 tons), and [[tobacco]] (1,440 tons).<ref>{{cite web |title=Municipal Agricultural Production - Temporary Crops - 2015 |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=158&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Cproducao-agricola-municipal-lavoura-temporaria-2015 |accessdate=2017-02-18 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The municipality's [[silviculture]] produced 6,500 [[cubic meters]] of [[firewood]] from eucalyptus, occupying 750 hectares, and was the only product of this activity in 2015.<ref>{{cite web |title=Plant Extraction and Silviculture - 2015 |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=160&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Cextracao-vegetal-e-silvicultura-2015 |accessdate=2017-02-18 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The 2006 Agricultural Census recorded 2,615 agricultural establishments with a productive area of 32,020 hectares, including 2,556 individual owners (31,434 hectares), 20 personal partnerships or consortia (243 hectares), 20 corporations (218 hectares), and 4 cooperatives (14 hectares).<ref>{{cite web |title=Agricultural Census - 2006 |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=3&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Ccenso-agropecuario-2006 |accessdate=2017-02-18 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

===Secondary sector===
[[File:Fábrica de Móveis em Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|Furniture industry.]]
The [[secondary sector]] of the economy transforms products from the [[primary sector]] and includes industries, construction, and energy generation.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geografia.seed.pr.gov.br/modules/galeria/detalhe.php?foto=1370&evento=3|title=Secondary Sector of the Economy - Discipline - Geography|accessdate=2017-02-18|website=www.geografia.seed.pr.gov.br|last=CELEPAR|language=pt}}</ref> In 2014, 31,333 thousand reais of the municipal [[Gross Domestic Product|GDP]] came from the gross value added by industry,<ref name=":50" /> representing 10.2% of the total value added in the municipality.<ref name=":52" /> In 2015, Crissiumal had 90 manufacturing industries, of which 85 were micro-enterprises, three were small enterprises, and two were medium and large enterprises.<ref name=":52" /> In the same year, there were also 41 micro-enterprises in construction.<ref name=":52" /> In 1999, a program called Pacto Fonte Nova was established to support small rural producers in producing raw materials, agro-industrializing, and marketing food products, resulting in the creation of 44 agro-industries and 250 direct jobs since that year.<ref name=":37" /> These agro-industries receive subsidies for infrastructure investment and environmental licensing through a municipal program.<ref name=":21b" /> Seventy percent of agro-industry products are sold in local markets or used for school meals.<ref name=":21b" /> In addition to agro-industries, the municipality has industries in footwear, furniture,<ref name=":37" /> metallurgy,<ref name="Conheça Crissiumal" /> and dairy production.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fee.rs.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/20161109livro-apls-a-aglomeraao-produtiva-de-laticnios-fronteira-noroeste-celeiro.pdf|title=The dairy production cluster Fronteira Noroeste-Celeiro|accessdate=February 6, 2017|publisher=Fundação de Economia e Estatística|last=Fauth|first=Elvin M.}}</ref> In May 2017, the first photovoltaic energy generation system was installed in a rural property in Vila Planalto through an initiative by EMATER and the Municipal Agriculture Secretariat.<ref>{{cite journal|date=May 12, 2017|title=First photovoltaic energy generation system installed in the municipality|journal=A Notícia Regional|url=}}</ref>

===Tertiary sector===
The [[tertiary sector]] encompasses services such as commerce, education, healthcare, tourism, and banking. In 2014, 94,122 thousand reais of the municipal GDP came from service provision.<ref name=":50" /> The tertiary sector is a significant contributor to Crissiumal's GDP, accounting for 60% of the value added in 2014.<ref name=":52" /> In that year, Crissiumal had 656 service companies, including 633 micro-enterprises, 22 small enterprises, and one medium and large enterprise.<ref name=":52" /> The Crissiumal Company, founded on September 1, 1939, and renamed [[Viação Ouro e Prata]] in 1948, is based in the municipality.<ref>{{cite journal|title=Viação Ouro e Prata diversifies activities and aims to attract more passengers|journal=Jornal do Comércio|date=6 February 2023 |url=http://jcrs.uol.com.br/site/noticia.php?codn=90117}}</ref>

===Tourism===
Crissiumal is part of the tourist route called Rota do Yucumã.<ref name=":12" /> Located in northwestern Rio Grande do Sul, from the border with [[Argentina]] and the border with [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]] to the Gaucho Middle Plateau, the Rota do Yucumã comprises 30 municipalities and includes, in addition to the Yucumã Falls, rural tourism itineraries, parks and resorts, museums, agro-industries, and monuments.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.turismoyucuma.com.br/a_regiao.php|title=The region|accessdate=2017-02-01|website=Rota do Yucumã|archive-date=2016-10-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161014172314/http://turismoyucuma.com.br/a_regiao.php|url-status=dead}}</ref> The Pacto Fonte Nova Agroindustrial Development Program gave rise to the Mundo Colonial tourist route, attracting thousands of visitors.<ref name=":12" /> Since 2002, the route has been visited by over 250 excursions from the states of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], and [[Mato Grosso]].<ref name=":12" /> This route offers ecological trails, resorts, inns, and dishes from the cuisine of Italian and German immigrants.<ref name=":13">{{cite web|url=http://www.turismoyucuma.com.br/municipios.php?id=11|title=Municipalities Crissiumal|accessdate=2017-02-01|website=Rota do Yucumã|archive-date=2016-12-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161202060204/http://turismoyucuma.com.br/municipios.php?id=11|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Urban infrastructure==
Crissiumal has robust infrastructure. In 2010, there were 4,902 [[housing units]], including [[apartments]], [[houses]], and rooms. Of these, 3,903 were owned properties, with 3,499 fully paid (71.38%), 404 under payment (8.24%), 632 [[rented]] (12.89%), 348 were provided, with 61 by employers (1.24%) and 287 provided otherwise (5.85%), and 19 were occupied in other ways (0.39%).<ref name=":55" /> The municipality has treated water, [[electricity]], [[solid waste|urban cleaning]], [[fixed telephony]], and [[mobile telephony]]. In 2010, 81.13% of households were served by the [[Water supply network|general water supply network]], and 63.12% had garbage collection.<ref name=":55" />

===Security, violence, and crime===
[[File:Brasão BMRS.PNG|left|thumb|117x117px|Coat of arms of the [[Military Brigade of Rio Grande do Sul]]]]
A security force present in the municipality is the Rio Grande do Sul Civil Police,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.policiacivil.rs.gov.br/conteudo/24391/crissiumal/termosbusca=Crissiumal|title=CRISSIUMAL|accessdate=2017-02-02|website=Delegacias e Postos de Polícia|publisher=Polícia Civil do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul}}</ref> primarily responsible for judicial police functions and criminal investigations.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pc.rs.gov.br/conteudo/971/atribuicoes|title=Responsibilities|accessdate=2017-02-02|publisher=Polícia Civil do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul|archive-date=2017-02-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170220105417/http://www.pc.rs.gov.br/conteudo/971/atribuicoes|url-status=dead}}</ref> Another security agency is the [[Military Brigade of Rio Grande do Sul|Military Police]], maintained by the state government, with multiple duties aimed at community integration and protection, including preventive and ostensible policing, environmental patrols, civil defense, support to the [[Brazilian Armed Forces|Armed Forces]], drug enforcement, search and rescue, professional training for at-risk youth, citizenship promotion, basic hygiene education, [[firefighting]] services, and, during summer, [[lifeguard]] services.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.brigadamilitar.rs.gov.br/Site/Institucional/Missao.aspx|title=Military Police - The Community's Force|accessdate=2017-02-02|website=www.brigadamilitar.rs.gov.br|archive-date=2017-02-03|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170203161729/https://www.brigadamilitar.rs.gov.br/Site/Institucional/Missao.aspx|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2012, the Rio Grande do Sul Civil Police conducted an operation named Operação Patriota to combat public fund embezzlement, bidding crimes, and corruption in the purchase of medicines and public works.<ref name=":7">{{cite journal|title=Deputy mayor, doctors, and businessmen arrested in Crissiumal|journal=Zh 2014|url=http://zh.clicrbs.com.br/rs/noticias/noticia/2012/12/vice-prefeito-medicos-e-empresarios-sao-presos-em-crissiumal-3988053.html}}</ref> The six-month investigation executed 25 search and seizure warrants and 13 arrests (five preventive and eight temporary), uncovering over two million [[Brazilian real|reais]] in misappropriated public funds.<ref name=":7" /> The operation involved 110 police officers and resulted in the arrest of the deputy mayor, the former municipal health secretary,<ref name=":7" /> the former public works secretary,<ref name=":22" /> construction entrepreneurs, and two doctors.<ref name=":7" /> One branch of the scheme operated in the healthcare sector, issuing fake requisitions for unperformed tests, billed to an intermunicipal health consortium, with the funds pocketed by group members, including a doctor, laboratories, and municipal administration agents.<ref name=":7" />

=== Transportation ===
[[File:RS 305 em Crissiumal.jpg|thumb|Highway [[List of state highways in Rio Grande do Sul|RS-305]] connecting Crissiumal to [[Horizontina]].]]
The main land access routes are RS-207, which is paved and connects Crissiumal to [[Humaitá, Rio Grande do Sul|Humaitá]] and [[BR-468 (Brazil highway)|BR-468]], and RS-305, which is under paving and links Crissiumal to [[Horizontina]] and [[Três Passos]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.daer.rs.gov.br/upload/arquivos/201607/19120217-mapa-oficial-frente-2015.pdf|title=Mapa Rodoviário 2015|access-date=2017-02-06|publisher=Departamento Autônomo de Estradas de Rodagem do Rio Grande do Sul}}</ref> Part of RS-305, connecting the city to Horizontina and Três Passos, is in poor condition, with potholes, ditches, uneven surfaces, and rocks.<ref name=":02">{{Cite web|url=http://www.jornalcolonial.com/geral/a-situacao-esta-precaria-na-rs-305/|title=A situação está precária na RS 305 – Jornal Colonial|access-date=2017-02-06|website=www.jornalcolonial.com}}</ref> Currently, no work is being done on this highway, despite the Autonomous Department of Highways stating that RS-305 is under paving.<ref name=":02" /> The movement of goods and passengers occurs solely via highways.<ref name=":28" /> The nearest access points to active railways are in [[Santo Ângelo]] and [[Ijuí]], and airports with infrastructure for handling passengers and cargo are located in [[Santa Rosa, Rio Grande do Sul|Santa Rosa]] and [[Ijuí]], in addition to an airfield in [[Campo Novo]], with a 1.02 km paved runway for private aircraft only.<ref name=":28" /> Crissiumal is also close to two ferries on the [[Uruguay River]], one connecting Porto Soberbo in [[Tiradentes do Sul]] to [[El Soberbio]], [[Argentina]]; and another linking [[Barra do Guarita]] to [[Itapiranga, Santa Catarina|Itapiranga]], [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].<ref name=":28" /> The city also has a fourth-class bus station in its downtown area.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www4.daer.rs.gov.br/site/forca_download.php?arquivo=arquivos/coletivos/arquivo29_185.pdf|title=Relação de Estações Rodoviárias Cadastradas no DAER|access-date=2017-02-13|publisher=DAER - Departamento Autônomo de Estradas de Rodagem - RS}}</ref>

The municipal vehicle fleet in 2015 consisted of 9,045 vehicles, including 4,572 cars, 354 trucks, 37 tractor-trucks, 560 pickup trucks, 170 vans, 17 minibuses, 2,875 motorcycles, 232 mopeds, 55 buses, and 139 other types of vehicles.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Frota - 2015 |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/temas.php?lang=&codmun=430600&idtema=153&search=rio-grande-do-sul%7Ccrissiumal%7Cfrota-2015 |access-date=2017-02-12 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> The entity responsible for managing road transportation in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]] is the Autonomous Department of Highways (DAER), a state agency founded in 1937.<ref name=":45">{{Cite web|url=http://www.daer.rs.gov.br/quem-somos|title=Quem Somos - DAER-RS|access-date=2017-02-13|website=www.daer.rs.gov.br|language=pt}}</ref> Its responsibilities include road planning, issuing road regulations, constructing, operating, and maintaining highways, and policing road traffic.<ref name=":45" />

== Culture ==
The culture of the city is heavily influenced by [[Culture of Germany|Germanic culture]], brought and nurtured by [[Germans|German]] immigrants who settled the region. The [[Hunsrik]] dialect, a variety of the [[German language]], is still spoken today by a significant number of people, particularly among the older residents. The city is home to the Municipal Public Library, established in 1955 and named Carlos Laert Public Library in honor of the teacher, poet, and journalist Carlos Laert. Additionally, the Municipal Museum, founded in 1983, houses objects that narrate the history of the municipality and its population.<ref name=":37" /> Crissiumal also features a Gaucho Traditions Center, known as CTG Estância da Saudade, founded in 1966, with the purpose of preserving [[Gaucho]] traditions, history, culture, and customs, including language, clothing, cuisine, and popular arts.<ref name=":36" /> Notable events in the municipality include the Day of Our Lady of Navigators, celebrated on February 2 along the banks of the [[Uruguay River]] at Barra do Lajeado Grande, [[Festa Junina|June festivals]] in June, and celebrations of [[Independence Day (Brazil)|Brazil's Independence Day]] and the [[Ragamuffin War|Farroupilha Revolution]] in September, featuring parades through the city streets.<ref name=":36" /> Another significant event is Expocris, which first took place in 1994 and occurs biennially.<ref name=":36" />

=== Arts ===
[[File:Detalhe do Monumento O Pioneiro em Crissiumal.jpg|left|thumb|Detail of the Pioneer Monument, a sculpture made from scrap metal by the artist Paulo Siqueira.]]
Music has been a part of Crissiumal since its colonization.<ref name=":36" /> The first musical group was the band Vida Azul, followed later by [[jazz]] ensembles such as Jazz Brasil, Jazz Amor da Serra from Linha Principal, Jazz Az de Ouro, and Jazz Flor de Maio. Additionally, Crissiumal's first [[Rock music|rock]] band, Os Podres, emerged in 1989 and disbanded in 2002.<ref name=":36" /> Current bands in the municipality include Danúbio Azul, Musical Som Sete, Musical Calmon,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://crissiumal-rs3.hospedagemdesites.ws/Site/conheca-crissiumal/15-a-cidade|title= Site Oficial - Prefeitura Municipal de Crissiumal - RS {{!}} Conheça Crissiumal|access-date=2017-02-16|website=Prefeitura Municipal de Crissiumal|archive-date=2017-02-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170218063620/http://crissiumal-rs3.hospedagemdesites.ws/Site/conheca-crissiumal/15-a-cidade|url-status=dead}}</ref> Banda Os Bade, and the Crissiumal Municipal Band.<ref name=":36" /> The city is also home to [[troubadour]]s such as Jairo Coelho, who is also a composer, and Ornélio de Souza, known by the nickname Teixeirinha. Furthermore, the Santa Cecília Choir, established in the 1950s, performs sacred and popular music.<ref name=":36" />

In literature, notable writers include José Raymundo Pletsch, a professor and lawyer who authored the books ''História de Criciumal'' and ''Apontamentos Sobre o Passado de Crissiumal''; Jenair Vicentini, a stenographer and teacher who wrote ''Crissiumal: Documentário: Essência da Nossa História''; and Teresinha Schwanke and Vera Pohl, both teachers and co-authors of the book ''Da Criciúma a Crissiumal''.<ref name=":36" />

In dance, the city is home to the Madre Paulina Ethnic Group - GEMP Escola & Cia. de Dança, founded in 1995.<ref name=":36" /> The company has won awards in every competition it has entered, including nine awards for eight choreographies presented at an international competition in 2004.<ref name=":36" /> In 2013, GEMP received the Vitor Matheus Teixeira Award from the [[Legislative Assembly of Rio Grande do Sul]] in recognition of its efforts to promote Rio Grande do Sul's culture.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.topsulnoticias.com.br/news/cultura-/|title=CULTURA: GEMP Escola e Cia. de Dança de Crissiumal Recebe o Prêmio de Melhor Grupo de Show pela Assembléia Legislativa do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul. :: TopSul Notícias|access-date=2017-02-16|website=www.topsulnoticias.com.br}}</ref> In addition to this company, there is also the Madre Paulina Skating Company and the Dance Group of the CTG Estância da Saudade.<ref name=":36" />

=== Language ===
[[File:Lápide em Cemitério em Crissiumal.jpg|thumb|220x220px|Tombstone in Crissiumal's Municipal Cemetery with an epitaph in German, which, loosely translated, reads: "Into your hands I commend my spirit. You have redeemed me, Lord. You, faithful God."]]
Portuguese is the official language of Crissiumal,<ref>{{Cite journal|date=2005-06-01|title=A língua portuguesa no Brasil|journal=Ciência e Cultura|volume=57|issue=2|issn=0009-6725|url=http://cienciaecultura.bvs.br/scielo.php?pid=S0009-67252005000200015&script=sci_arttext}}</ref> spoken by nearly the entire population and virtually the only language used in media, business, and administrative purposes. [[Brazilian Portuguese]] has developed uniquely, influenced by [[Indigenous languages of Brazil|Amerindian]], [[African languages|African]], and other [[Languages of Europe|European languages]].<ref>{{Cite journal|last=|first=|date=|title=Brazil|journal=Ethnologue|url=https://www.ethnologue.com/country/br|access-date=8 March 2017}}</ref> In Rio Grande do Sul, there is a variant of Brazilian Portuguese with distinct lexical characteristics and influences from Italian, Spanish, and German,<ref>{{Cite journal|title=Dialetos brasileiros - Linguística|journal=InfoEscola|url=http://www.infoescola.com/linguistica/dialetos-brasileiros/}}</ref> featuring words such as ''cacetinho'' ([[Pão francês|French bread]]), ''faixa'' (road, asphalt), ''cusco'' (dog), ''sinaleira'' (traffic light), ''bergamota'' (tangerine),<ref name=":47">{{Cite journal|title=Dicionário gauchês: significado dos termos que os gaúchos falam - Terra|journal=Terra|url=https://noticias.terra.com.br/brasil/cidades/dicionario-gaucho/}}</ref> and expressions like "''frio de renguear cusco''," meaning intense, unbearable cold.<ref name=":47" /><ref>{{Cite journal |date=2016-03-15 |title=Expressões gauchescas |url=http://www.rodosoft.com.br/dicas/expressoes-gauchescas-2/ |journal=Rodosoft |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170206112503/http://www.rodosoft.com.br/dicas/expressoes-gauchescas-2/ |archive-date=2017-02-06}}</ref>

As in many municipalities in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], the German language, in its Rio Grande variant, is an integral part of Crissiumal's history since its founding. The dialect spoken in the region is [[Hunsrik|Hunsrückisch]], a variant prevalent in the [[Hunsrück]] region of southwestern [[Germany]]. This dialect is also known as Hunsrück,<ref name=":19">{{Cite web|url=http://www.aguasmornas.sc.gov.br/noticias/838.htm|title=Prefeitura Municipal de Águas Mornas - SC|date=|access-date=2017-02-09|website=www.aguasmornas.sc.gov.br|publisher=|last=|first=}}</ref> Hunsrick, Hunsbucklisch, Hunsriqueano,<ref>{{cite web |last=Habel |first=Jussara Maria |date= |title=Fundamentos para o estudo das línguas dos imigrantes boêmios no Brasil |url=https://www.lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/handle/10183/115688/000956575.pdf?sequence=1 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170820134457/https://www.lume.ufrgs.br/bitstream/handle/10183/115688/000956575.pdf?sequence=1 |archive-date=2017-08-20 |access-date=5 February 2017 |publisher=Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul}}</ref> Hunsrickisch, Brazilianized Hunsrik, Riograndense Hunsrückisch,<ref>{{Cite journal|last=Catarinense|first=Diário|title=Moradores de Biguaçu e Antônio Carlos conservam idioma trazido por alemães|journal=Diário Catarinense|url=http://dc.clicrbs.com.br/sc/noticias/noticia/2013/08/moradores-de-biguacu-e-antonio-carlos-conservam-idioma-trazido-por-alemaes-4236626.html}}</ref> and Riograndenser Hunsrückisch.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.rodrigotrespach.com/2015/03/15/imigracao-alema-no-dialeto-riograndenser-hunsruckisch/|title=Imigração Alemã no dialeto Riograndenser Hunsrückisch|access-date=2017-02-05}}</ref> Speakers of this dialect have adopted Portuguese terms, creating words such as ''fakong'' (faction), ''aviong'' (airplane), ''kamiong'' (truck), and ''milheprot'' (cornbread).<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.dw.com/pt-br/o-alem%C3%A3o-lusitano-do-sul-do-brasil/a-1174391|title=O alemão lusitano do Sul do Brasil {{!}} Cultura {{!}} DW.COM {{!}} 20.04.2004|access-date=2017-02-13|website=DW.COM|last=(www.dw.com)|first=Deutsche Welle}}</ref> Some rarely spoken variants include ''Sächsisch'', ''Österreichisch'', ''Hessisch'', ''Alemannisch'', and ''Berlinisch''.<ref name=":19" /> In 2012, the [[Legislative Assembly of Rio Grande do Sul]] unanimously approved the official recognition of the Rio Grande German dialect as an integral part of the state's cultural heritage.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.al.rs.gov.br/legis/M010/M0100099.ASP?Hid_Tipo=TEXTO&Hid_TodasNormas=58094&hTexto=&Hid_IDNorma=58094|title=Lei nº 14.061, de 23 de julho de 2012|date=|access-date=2017-02-05|website=www.al.rs.gov.br|publisher=Assembléia Legislativa do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul|last=|first=}}</ref>

=== Cuisine ===
A typical dish in the local cuisine is ''[[feijoada]]'', which can be prepared with black beans, pork fat, sausage, dried meats, fresh and dried seasonings, and may be accompanied by white rice and cassava flour.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Culinária Brasileira |url=http://basilio.fundaj.gov.br/pesquisaescolar/index.php?option=com_content&id=549 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170214102155/http://basilio.fundaj.gov.br/pesquisaescolar/index.php?option=com_content&id=549 |archive-date=2017-02-14 |access-date=2017-02-13 |website=basilio.fundaj.gov.br}}</ref> In addition to typical [[Rio Grande do Sul]] dishes such as ''[[churrasco]]'', ''arroz de carreteiro'', and ''[[chimarrão]]'', Crissiumal's cuisine has been influenced by Italian immigrants with dishes such as galeto, pasta, wine, and [[polenta]],<ref name=":38">{{Cite web|url=http://comida.ig.com.br/pelomundo/regiao-sul/4fd6328063a8bb36eb41858c.html#pratos_rs|title=Região Sul - Brasil|access-date=2017-02-11|website=comida.ig.com.br}}</ref> and by German immigrants with sausages, [[sauerkraut]], potato salad, and beer.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://lazer.hsw.uol.com.br/cozinha-brasileira-sul3.htm|title=A influência alemã {{!}} ComotudoFunciona|access-date=2017-02-11|website=lazer.hsw.uol.com.br|language=en}}</ref> Barbecue is made with meat seasoned only with salt, grilled over coals or an open fire, and can be served with cassava flour, mayonnaise salad, and leafy salads with vinaigrette.<ref name=":38" /> ''Arroz de carreteiro'' is a mixture of rice with dried beef or leftover barbecue meat.<ref name=":38" /> ''Chimarrão'', a legacy of the Guarani indigenous people, is an infusion of [[yerba mate]] served in a gourd.<ref name=":38" /> Polenta is a cornmeal paste cooked in a heated pot and can be served soft, firm, grilled, or fried.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://padovanella.com.br/historia-polenta/|title=História da Polenta Italiana {{!}} PaDovaNella Ristorante|access-date=2017-02-11|website=padovanella.com.br}}</ref> Sauerkraut<ref>{{Cite journal|date=2014-10-10|title=Sauerkraut ou Chucrute : Alimento Probiótico|journal=Sauerkraut|url=http://sauerkraut.com.br/sauerkraut-ou-chucrute-alimento-probiotico/}}</ref> is prepared with shredded cabbage and salt, left to ferment in a container for some time.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://comida.umcomo.com.br/receita/como-fazer-chucrute-caseiro-25358.html|title=Como fazer chucrute caseiro - 8 passos (com imagens)|access-date=2017-02-11|website=umcomo.com.br}}</ref>

=== Sports ===
[[File:Treino do Tupi.jpg|left|thumb|Training session of the Tupi football team at the Rubro-negro Municipal Stadium.]]
A popular sport in Crissiumal is [[Association football|football]]. Decades ago, rural communities in the municipality had football teams composed of local residents.<ref name=":37" /> There were so many willing players that the most skilled were selected to form the teams.<ref name=":37" /> Today, due to rural exodus, fewer young people remain in rural areas, making it difficult for communities to form football teams.<ref name=":37" /> Annually, the Municipal Sports Council - CMD organizes an amateur football championship with teams composed of athletes from the municipality and neighboring municipalities.<ref name=":37" /> The championship finals take place at the Rubro-Negro Municipal Stadium.<ref name=":37" /> The CMD and the Municipal Public Employees Association (AFPM) also organize the municipal futsal championship. There are categories for the following groups: under-15 boys, men, women, and seniors.<ref name=":37" /> Since 1987, the Municipal Education Department has held the Inter-school Games at the Municipal Sports Gymnasium in partnership with a local school. The games feature male and female futsal and volleyball in the U-12, junior, and children's categories.<ref name=":37" /> Other sports practiced in the municipality include [[canasta]], [[bocce]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.radioaltouruguai.com.br/?pg=desc-noticia&id=33449|title=ATAC realiza dia 26 de novembro o 20º Encontro Regional dos Técnicos Agrícolas em Crissiumal|date=|access-date=2017-02-12|publisher=Rádio Alto Uruguai {{!}} FM 92,5 - AM 970|last=|first=}}</ref> bocha 48,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.jornalcolonial.com/geral/confira-dos-resultados-do-campeonato-municipal-de-48-de-crissiumal/|title=Confira dos resultados do Campeonato Municipal de 48 de Crissiumal – Jornal Colonial|access-date=2017-02-12|website=www.jornalcolonial.com}}</ref> tabletop bowling,<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.rs.gov.br/conteudo/170511/cinquentenario-de-crissiumal-e-comemorado-com-jogos-sol-a-sol|title=Cinqüentenário de Crissiumal é comemorado com Jogos Sol a Sol|access-date=2017-02-12|website=Governo do Estado do Rio Grande do Sul|last=PROCERGS|archive-date=2017-02-13|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170213163745/http://www.rs.gov.br/conteudo/170511/cinquentenario-de-crissiumal-e-comemorado-com-jogos-sol-a-sol|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.scj.org.br/2015/09/28/festa-do-seminario-sao-miguel/|title=FESTA DO SEMINÁRIO SÃO MIGUEL {{!}} SCJ|access-date=2017-02-12}}</ref> and motocross.<ref>{{Cite journal|title=Classmann prestigia 2ª Etapa Copa Cidade de Motocross em Crissiumal|journal=Jusbrasil|url=https://al-rs.jusbrasil.com.br/noticias/113093828/classmann-prestigia-2a-etapa-copa-cidade-de-motocross-em-crissiumal}}</ref>

In Crissiumal, there is a football club founded on May 1, 1949, named [[Tupi Futebol Clube]].<ref name=":17" /> This team faced [[SC Internacional|Internacional]] in 1970,<ref name=":17" /> [[America Football Club (Rio de Janeiro)|America]] in 1974,<ref name=":36" /> and [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio]] in 1978.<ref name=":36" /><ref name=":17" /> Notable athletes from Crissiumal include [[Cláudio Taffarel|Cláudio André Mergen Taffarel]], born in [[Santa Rosa, Rio Grande do Sul|Santa Rosa]], who played for [[Tupi Futebol Clube|Tupi]], [[SC Internacional|Sport Club Internacional]], [[Parma Calcio 1913|Parma]], [[AC Reggiana 1919|Reggiana]], and the [[Brazil national football team|Brazilian national football team]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/biografias/445/taffarel|title=Taffarel - Futebol - UOL Esporte|website=esporte.uol.com.br|access-date=2017-02-12}}</ref> and [[Danrlei|Danrlei de Deus Hinterholz]], born in Crissiumal, who played for [[Grêmio FBPA|Grêmio]] for ten years, [[Clube Atlético Mineiro|Atlético Mineiro]], the Brazilian national team,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://esporte.uol.com.br/futebol/biografias/2/danrlei|title=Danrlei - Futebol - UOL Esporte|website=esporte.uol.com.br|access-date=2017-02-12}}</ref> and was elected a federal congressman in 2010 and re-elected in 2014.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://terceirotempo.bol.uol.com.br/que-fim-levou/danrlei-4333|title=Danrlei - Que fim levou? - Terceiro Tempo|website=Third Time|access-date=2017-02-12}}</ref> Additionally, [[Beto (footballer, born 1959)|Roberto Gilmar Hinterholz]], who played for Tupi and became a world club champion is also notable.<ref name=":36" />
