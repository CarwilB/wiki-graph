'''Miraflores''' is a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] in the municipality of [[Añasco, Puerto Rico]]. Its population in 2010 was 567.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó. ==Hurricane Maria==
The barrio of Miraflores became inaccessible when [[Hurricane Maria]] hit Puerto Rico on September 20, 2017 and caused landslides and destruction.<ref>{{cite web | title=Añasco urge atención para recuperar carreteras, puentes y alcantarillados | website=La Isla Oeste | url=https://laislaoeste.com/anasco-urge-atencion-para-recuperar-carreteras-puentes-y-alcantarillados/ | language=es | access-date=9 September 2020}}</ref> Five months after the hurricane struck, engineers and officials were grappling with the massive amounts of repairs that were needed to PR-109 in Añasco and multiple other areas of this barrio and of Añasco, as a whole.<ref>{{cite web | title=Añasco urge atención para recuperar carreteras, puentes y alcantarillados | website=La Isla Oeste | date=14 February 2018 | url=https://laislaoeste.com/anasco-urge-atencion-para-recuperar-carreteras-puentes-y-alcantarillados/ | language=es | access-date=9 September 2020}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Añasco Miraflores PR-109 Kilometer 13.4 |url=https://laislaoeste.com/anasco-bo-miraflores-rd-pr-109-km-13-4/ |website=laislaoeste.com |access-date=9 September 2020}}</ref>

==Sectors==
Barrios (which are, in contemporary times, roughly comparable to  [[minor civil divisions]])<ref name="Barrio-Pueblo">{{cite web |title=US Census Barrio-Pueblo definition |url=https://factfinder.census.gov/help/en/barrio.htm |website=factfinder.com |publisher=US Census |access-date=5 January 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170513190743/https://factfinder.census.gov/help/en/barrio.htm |archive-date=13 May 2017 |url-status=dead }}</ref> in turn are further subdivided into smaller local populated place areas/units called '''sectores''' (''sectors'' in English). ==Special communities==
Since 2001 when law 1-2001 was passed,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.lexjuris.com/lexlex/Leyes2001/lex2001001.htm|title=Leyes del 2001|website=Lex Juris Puerto Rico|language=es|access-date=24 June 2019}}</ref> measures have been taken to identify and address the high levels of poverty and the lack of resources and opportunities affecting specific communities in Puerto Rico. Initially there were 686 places that made the list.<ref name="wordpress.com">{{cite web|url=https://cpprbib.wordpress.com/biblioteca-virtual/guias-tematicas/comunidades-especiales/comunidades-especiales-de-puerto-rico/|title=Comunidades Especiales de Puerto Rico|date=8 August 2011|language=es|access-date=24 June 2019}}</ref> By 2008, there were 742 places on the list of {{lang|es|Comunidades especiales de Puerto Rico}}. The places on the list are barrios, communities, sectors, or neighborhoods and in 2004, Miraflores had made the list.<ref name="wordpress.com"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.elnuevodia.com/noticias/locales/nota/evolucionaelproyectodecomunidadesespeciales-2294682/|title=Evoluciona el proyecto de Comunidades Especiales|date=24 February 2017|newspaper=El Nuevo Dia|language=es|access-date=24 June 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.elvocero.com/gobierno/ya-es-ley-oficina-para-el-desarrollo-socioecon-mico-y/article_54769072-382d-5156-9acf-9e8c0b4f4d82.html|title=Ya es ley Oficina para el Desarrollo Socioeconómico y Comunitario|newspaper=El Vocero de Puerto Rico|language=es|access-date=24 June 2019}}</ref>
