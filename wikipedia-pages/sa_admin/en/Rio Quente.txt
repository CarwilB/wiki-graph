'''Rio Quente''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈʁi.u ˈkẽtʃi|lang}}; "Hot River") is a municipality in the south of the state of [[Goiás]], [[Brazil]].  It is the site of a hot water spa known all over the country.

==Location==
[[File:Rio Quente 16 (27822967745).jpg|thumb|Hot water springs in Rio Quente]]
Rio Quente is 178&nbsp;km. south of the state capital Goiânia and 360&nbsp;km. southwest of Brasília.  Nearby cities are [[Morrinhos, Goiás|Morrinhos]], 53&nbsp;km. to the west, and [[Caldas Novas]], 28&nbsp;km. to the east.  Connections with [[Goiânia]] are made by BR-352 / [[Bela Vista de Goiás]] / GO-147 / [[Piracanjuba]] / GO-217 / GO-139 / GO-213 / GO-507.  For a complete list of distances see [https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Seplan]

It became a municipality in 1989, dismembering itself from [[Caldas Novas]].  Rio Quente is in the [[Meia Ponte Microregion]].

Neighboring municipalities are:
*North: [[Caldas Novas]]
*South: [[Água Limpa]]
*East: [[Caldas Novas]] and [[Mairipotaba]]
*West: [[Morrinhos, Goiás|Morrinhos]]

Rio Quente is famous for its hot water springs, which were discovered by [[Bartolomeu Bueno da Silva]] (son), in 1722 and received the name of Caldas Velhas.  It is situated in a region of tropical climate—hot and humid—with rains in the months of November to March.  The average annual temperature is 23&nbsp;°C.

Rio Quente is served by [[Caldas Novas Airport|Nelson Ribeiro Guimarães]] airport located in the adjoining municipality of Caldas Novas.

==Profile of the City==
Despite the small territorial dimension of the municipality (257.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>) there is a developed agriculture with production of rice and corn and raising of dairy cattle.  Nevertheless, the main source of income is in the touristic complex called [http://www.rioquenteresorts.com.br/home/home.asp, Rio Quente Resorts] which attracts thousands of tourists all year round.  It has 7 hotels, including the famous Hotel Pousada, the first to be built.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|12,315|ha}} [[Caldas Novas State Park]] which protects a plateau region that is the main source of water for the geothermal aquifers of the region.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra de Caldas Novas – SECIMA}}|title=Parque Estadual da Serra de Caldas Novas|publisher=SECIMA|url=http://www.secima.go.gov.br/post/ver/197706/parque-estadual-da-serra-de-caldas-novas-pescan|access-date=2017-01-16|archive-date=2017-01-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170118033509/http://www.secima.go.gov.br/post/ver/197706/parque-estadual-da-serra-de-caldas-novas-pescan|url-status=dead}}</ref>
One of the attractions of the resort is the constant renewal of all the water of the pools due to the great volume that springs from its main thermal springs.  The volume of water produced by the springs results in a constant flow of 6,228,000 liters per hour, reaching a figure over 149 million liters every 24 hours. Attractions are the hotel complex and the only river with hot-water rapids in the Americas.  This river is called the Ribeirão das Aguas Quentes.

Rio Quente has one of the highest standards of living in the state of [[Goiás]].  The United Nations [[Human Development Index]] of 2000 ranks it in 7th place in the state (out of a total of 242 municipalities) and 460 nationally out of a total of 5,507 municipalities.  On a scale of 0.000 to 1.000 it had a rating of 0.806.
