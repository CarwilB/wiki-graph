'''Itabaiana''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|itabaˈjɐnɐ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 103,440 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) and its area is {{cvt|337.348|km2}}.<ref name="IBGE" />

The municipality contains part of the [[Serra de Itabaiana National Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional da Serra de Itabaiana|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente|url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=147|access-date=2016-05-03}}</ref>

The city of Itabaiana has a museum of local history and art named the ''Museu Artístico E Histórico De Itabaiana, Antônio Nogueira''.
