'''Empalme''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title='''—''' |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 31, 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 }}</ref> In 2015, the municipality had a total population of 56,177,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/son/poblacion/|title=Número de habitantes. Sonora|website=Cuentame.inegi.org.mx|access-date=9 July 2018}}</ref> which had fallen to 51,431 by 2020.<ref>{{cite web |title=Empalme: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/empalme |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

The municipal seat is the [[homonymous]] [[Empalme, Sonora|city]].
