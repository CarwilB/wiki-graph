'''Santo Domingo de los Colorados'''<ref>[http://www.santodomingo.gov.ec/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=15&Itemid=19&limitstart=1 Santo Domingo de los Colorados]{{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} at www.santodomingo.gob.ec</ref> or simply known as '''Santo Domingo''', is the biggest [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] in the [[Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas Province]],<ref>[http://www.gptsachila.gov.ec/ Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100724052024/http://www.gptsachila.gov.ec/ |date=2010-07-24 }} at www.gptsachila.gob.ec</ref> after La Concordia officially became part of the province on May 31, 2013.<ref>{{Cite web|title = Cantones y Parroquias - Prefectura de Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas|url = http://www.gptsachila.gob.ec/index.php/la-provincia/parroquias|website = www.gptsachila.gob.ec|access-date = 2015-12-30}}</ref> The canton is named after its seat, the town of [[Santo Domingo de los Colorados|Santo Domingo]]. The canton partially occupies the Toachi [[river basin]]. The Toachi river flows into the [[Daule (river)|Daule]] river.

===Political divisions===
[[File:Mapa urbano de Santo Domingo.jpg|300px|thumb|Urban parishes of Santo Domingo Canton]]
Santo Domingo Canton is divided into 7 urban parishes and 7 rural parishes.

* Urban parishes: Santo Domingo, Chiguilpe, Río Verde, Bombolí, Zaracay, Abraham Calazacón, Río Toachi
* Rural parishes: San José de Alluriquín, El Esfuerzo, Luz de América, Puerto Limón, San Jacinto del Búa, Santa María del Toachi, Valle Hermoso
