'''Bernardino Bilbao''' (or: ''General Bernardino Bilbao Rioja'') is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the Northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Potosí Department|Potosí]]. It has its name after [[Bernardino Bilbao Rioja]], Bolivian general and politician.

==Location==
Bernardino Bilbao province is one of sixteen provinces in the Potosí Department. It is located between 17° 49' und 18° 10' [[Latitude|South]] and between 65° 53' und 66° 16' [[Longitude|West]]. It borders [[Cochabamba Department]] in the North, [[Alonso de Ibáñez Province]] in the Southwest, and [[Charcas Province]] in the South.

The province extends over {{Convert|55|km|abbr=on}} from east to west and {{Convert|60|km|abbr=on}} from north to south.

==Population==
The main [[idiom]] of the province is [[Quechua languages|Quechua]], spoken by 99% of the population, while 40% also speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]]. The population increased from 10,045 inhabitants (1992 census) to 10,623 (2001 census), an increase of 5.8%. The capital of the province is [[Arampampa]] (1,703 inhabitants).

99% of the population have no access to electricity, while 94% have no sanitary facilities. 62% of the population are employed in agriculture, 0.5% in mining, 17.5% in industry, and 20% in general services. 92% of the population are Catholics, and 5% are Protestants.

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
