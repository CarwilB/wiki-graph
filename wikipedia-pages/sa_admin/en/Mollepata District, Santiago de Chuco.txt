'''Mollepata''' or '''Mullipata''' ([[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''mulli'' [[Schinus molle|Peruvian pepper tree]] ''pata'' elevated place / edge, bank (of a river), shore) is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Santiago de Chuco Province|Santiago de Chuco]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
