'''Chihuahua''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is the [[Chihuahua, Chihuahua|city of Chihuahua]], which is also the capital of the [[Chihuahua (state)|state of Chihuahua]]. 

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 937, 674,<ref name=INEGI>{{cite web|title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Chihuahua. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía]] (INEGI)|accessdate=22 July 2021|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825197810.pdf}}</ref> up from  819,543 in 2010.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Chihuahua|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=019|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=17 April 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150417170910/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=019|url-status=dead}}</ref> It covers an area of 9,219.30&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2020, the city of Chihuahua had a population of 925, 762,<ref name=INEGI/> up from 809,232 in 2020. Other than the city of Chihuahua, the municipality had 888 localities, the largest of which  was [[El Sauz, Chihuahua|El Sauz]] (2010 population: 1,474), classified as rural.<ref name=INEGI/>
