'''Teya Municipality''' (In the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “place of [[Manilkara zapota|sapodilla]]”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing  65.15&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 65&nbsp;km northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
Before the arrival of the Spanish, the area belonged to the chieftainship of Ah Kin Chel. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system. The encomienda was established in 1565 for Alonso de Castro. By 1678, the encomendero was Anastasio Chacón de Azcorran and in 1690, the encomendera passed to María Barbosa e Ignacia Salazar.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the [[Izamal Municipality]].<ref name=municipios /> By April 1921 it was designated as its own municipality.<ref name="1921 is Teya">{{cite web|title=Núm. 183.|url=http://www.poresto.net/ver_nota.php?zona=yucatan&idSeccion=1&idTitulo=74258|website=Por Esto!|publisher=Por Esto!|accessdate=5 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304180340/http://poresto.net/ver_nota.php?idseccion=1&idtitulo=74258&zona=yucatan|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of health, public services and public sanitation.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Teya|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=5 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Local festivals==
Every year at the end of April there is a feast in honor of the town's patron saint, St. Barnabas, and on 11 June the town holds a fiesta for San Bernardino.<ref name=municipios />
