'''Distracción''' ({{IPA|es|distɾaɣˈsjon}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[La Guajira Department|La Guajira]]. Founded in 1845 by a man from neighboring [[Barrancas, La Guajira|Barrancas]] named [[Antonio María Vidal]] on the right margin of the [[Ranchería River]].<ref name="laguajira.gov.co">[http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/distraccion.htm {{in lang|es}} Government of La Guajira: Distraccion] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928001812/http://www.laguajira.gov.co/municipios/distraccion.htm |date=September 28, 2007 }}</ref>

The town celebrates the [[Festival de los Laureles]] (Spanish for Festival of the [[Lauraceae|Laurels]]). The town's economy relies mostly in [[agriculture]], [[farming]] and the rudimentary exploitation of a [[limestone]] mine.<ref name="laguajira.gov.co"/>

==Geography==

The municipality of Distracción is located in the [[Guajira Peninsula]], on the Valley of Upar formed by the mountains of the [[Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta]] and [[Serranía del Perijá]]. It has a total area of 220&nbsp;km² bordering to the north with the municipality of [[Riohacha]]; to the east with the municipality of [[Fonseca, La Guajira|Fonseca]]; to the south with the municipalities of [[San Juan del Cesar]] and Fonseca and to the west with the municipality of San Juan del Cesar.<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20071013060901/http://distraccion-laguajira.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m-I1--&m=f&s=m {{in lang|es}} Distraccion: Geography]</ref>
