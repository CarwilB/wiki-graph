'''Gustavo A. Madero''' is the northernmost [[Boroughs of Mexico City|borough]] (''demarcación territorial'') of [[Mexico City]].

==History==
Founded as "Villa de Guadalupe" in 1563, it became the city of "Villa de Guadalupe Hidalgo" in 1828, and finally a ''delegación'' in 1931. It was named after [[Gustavo A. Madero]], the brother and fellow revolutionary of [[List of Presidents of Mexico|President]] [[Francisco I. Madero]].

The [[Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo]], which ended the [[Mexican–American War]] of 1846–1848, was signed in Gustavo A. Madero.

==Points of interest==
The area houses the [[Basílica de Guadalupe]], the shrine of [[Our Lady of Guadalupe]] at the foot of [[Tepeyac|Tepeyac Hill]], where [[Roman Catholicism|Roman Catholics]] believe the [[Blessed Virgin Mary|Virgin Mary]] appeared to the [[Indigenous peoples of Mexico|indigenous Mexican]] [[Juan Diego|Juan Diego Cuauhtlatoatzin]] in 1531.

[[File:Basílica de Guadalupe desde el aire 7.jpg|thumb|Basílica de Guadalupe in 2024]]

Being the northernmost borough, the Northern Central Bus Station (''Terminal Central del Norte'') is located here, providing constant bus service to all major cities in the northern and western part of the country.

On Sundays, the [[San Felipe de Jesús Tianguis]] in the neighborhood of the same name, is Latin America's largest [[tianguis]] or street market, with 30,000 vendors and stretching seven kilometers.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.chilango.com/general/los-mejores-tianguis-para-chacharear-en-la-ciudad/|title=Los mejores tianguis para chacharear en la Ciudad |date=28 July 2016|website=Chilango.com|access-date=26 July 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.maspormas.com/ciudad/tianguis-la-san-felipe-no-podras-recorrerlo-dia/|title=Tianguis de la San Felipe: no podrás recorrerlo en un día - Máspormás|date=23 January 2017|website=Madpormas.com|access-date=26 July 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.excelsior.com.mx/comunidad/2017/04/26/1160037|title=Tianguis de San Felipe de Jesús tendrá mayor seguridad|date=27 April 2017|website=Excelsior.com.mx|access-date=26 July 2018 |author1=Notimex }}</ref>
