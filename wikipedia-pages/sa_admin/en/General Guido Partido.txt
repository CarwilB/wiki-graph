'''General Guido Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the eastern part of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref name="Lechuga1949">{{cite book

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 3,000 inhabitants in an area of {{Convert|2340|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[General Guido]].
