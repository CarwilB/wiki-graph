'''El Rosal''' ({{IPA|es|el roˈsal}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Western Savanna Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. El Rosal is situated on the [[Bogotá savanna]] with its urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2685|m|ft}} and a distance of {{convert|20|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. It is part of the [[Metropolitan Area of Bogotá]]. El Rosal borders [[Subachoque]] in the northeast, [[San Francisco, Cundinamarca|San Francisco]] in the northwest, [[Madrid, Cundinamarca|Madrid]] in the southeast and [[Facatativá]] in the southwest.<ref name=websiteElRosal>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150520155716/http://www.elrosal-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website El Rosal]</ref>

== History ==
The area of Subachoque, of which El Rosal was a part until 1903, in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The ''[[zipa]]'' of [[Bogotá|Bacatá]] [[Muisca rulers|ruled]] over Subachoque. The [[Panche people|Panche]] lived to the west of Subachoque and when the [[conquistador]]es arrived, they took advantage of the situation and entered the Muisca territories. Subachoque and El Rosal were submitted by [[Nicolaus Federmann|Nicolás de Federman]] in 1538.<ref name=websiteElRosal/>

The [[Muisca agriculture]] consisted of [[potato]]es, [[maize]], [[coca]], [[arracacha]], [[sweet potato]]es, [[tuber]]s and [[Ullucus tuberosus|chungua]].<ref name=websiteElRosal/>

Modern El Rosal was founded on March 28, 1903 by Ana Vicenta González.<ref name=websiteElRosal/>

== Economy ==
A large percentage of this agricultural community is employed by large scale flower growers in the production of roses, carnations, [[Alstroemeria|Peruvian lilies]] and other varieties of flowers. Other crops which contribute to the economy are potatoes, carrots, corn, sweetpeas, string beans and herbs. Wheat was a major crop until the 1930s when the price fell and made it economically unfeasible to continue planting it. Dairy farms, meat cattle, goats and horses are also vital parts of the El Rosal economy.<ref name=websiteElRosal/>
