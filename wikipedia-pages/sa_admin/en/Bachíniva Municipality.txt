'''Bachíniva''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Bachíniva]]. The municipality covers an area of 1,691.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 6,011,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Bachíniva|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=006|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> down from 7,242 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Bachiniva |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08006a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=August 18, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070519094729/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08006a.htm |archivedate=May 19, 2007 }}</ref>

As of 2010, the town of Bachíniva had a population of 2,109.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Bachíniva, the municipality had 117 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) was: [[El Porvenir, Chihuahua|El Porvenir]] (1,071), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

The head of the municipality was founded in 1660 by the [[Franciscan|Orden de San Francisco de Asís]], and was originally named Santa María de Nativitas de Bachimba, which was changed years later to the current Bachíniva.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.bachiniva.gob.mx/Contenido/plantilla5.asp?cve_canal=5704&Portal=bachiniva |title=Municipio de Bachíniva |accessdate=2008-11-08 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110722224734/http://www.bachiniva.gob.mx/Contenido/plantilla5.asp?cve_canal=5704&Portal=bachiniva |archivedate=2011-07-22 }}</ref>

Bachíniva is self-named the Apple Paradise, as it is home to many apple orchards. Many of the jobs in the municipality come from these orchards.
