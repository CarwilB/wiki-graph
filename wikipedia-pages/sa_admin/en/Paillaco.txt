''' Paillaco''' is a city and commune in southern [[Chile]]. It is located in [[Valdivia Province]] of [[Los Ríos Region]], and is about 48 km southeast of [[Valdivia, Chile | Valdivia]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Paillaco spans an area of {{convert|896|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 19,237 inhabitants (9,620 men and 9,617 women). Of these, 9,973 (51.8%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 9,264 (48.2%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 6% (1,085 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Paillaco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an alcalde who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde was [[Ramona Reyes]]. The council has the following members:<ref name="Official"/>
* José Teobaldo Aravena Pérez ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Ruth Lucy Castillo Prieto ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]])
* Sergio Orlando Castro Barrera ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Héctor Alberto Avilés Fuentes ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Gabriel Iván Medina Alarcón ([[Radical Party (Chile)|PR]])
* Gonzalo Rubén Galilea García (RN)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Paillaco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by [[Enrique Jaramillo]]  (PDC) and [[Gastón von Mühlenbrock]] (UDI) as part of the 54th electoral district, together with [[Panguipulli, Chile|Panguipulli]], [[Los Lagos, Chile|Los Lagos]], [[Futrono]], [[Lago Ranco, Chile|Lago Ranco]], [[Río Bueno, Chile|Río Bueno]] and [[La Unión, Chile|La Unión]]. The commune is represented in the {{Current senators (16th senatorial constituency of Chile)}} as part of the 16th senatorial constituency (Los Ríos Region).
