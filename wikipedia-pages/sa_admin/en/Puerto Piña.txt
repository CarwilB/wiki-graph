'''Puerto Piña''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,113 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 558; its population as of 2000 was 813.<ref name=inec/> The town is served by [[Bahía Piña Airport]], which has [[Air Panama]] flights to [[Panama City]].

This small coastal community is located near the border with Colombia. It is the home for a tourism consortium named "Tropi" by the locals, which forms the only business of the village. The remainder of the economy consists of fishing and subsistence farming.
