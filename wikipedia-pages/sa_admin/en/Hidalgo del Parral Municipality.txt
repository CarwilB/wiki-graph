''' Hidalgo del Parral ''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Hidalgo del Parral]] (Parral City). The municipality covers an area of 1,751&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 107,061,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Hidalgo del Parral|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=032|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=17 April 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150417170901/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=032|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 103,519 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Hidalgo del Parral|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08030a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223203436/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08030a.htm|archivedate=February 23, 2007}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the city of Hidalgo del Parral had a population of 104,836.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Hidalgo del Parral, the municipality had 192 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
