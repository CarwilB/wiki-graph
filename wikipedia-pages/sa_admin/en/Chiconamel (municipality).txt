'''Chiconamel''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. The municipal seat is the village of [[Chiconamel, Veracruz]].

In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 6,811, of whom 1,517 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chiconamel|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30056a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=2008-11-02|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110617080459/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30056a.htm|archive-date=17 June 2011|url-status=dead}}</ref> 
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 3,902 (62%) spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Nahuatl]].

The municipality of Chiconamel covers a total surface area of 133.25&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Unusually, however, the municipality is divided into two by an intervening portion of the municipality of [[Chalma (municipality)|Chalma]], with the eastern portion of its territory an [[exclave]].<ref>{{cite web |url= http://app.comsocialver.gob.mx/boletin/municipios/mapas/30056.jpg |title= Map of Chiconamel |publisher= Veracruz State Govt. |access-date= 2008-11-04 |url-status= dead |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20110722224819/http://app.comsocialver.gob.mx/boletin/municipios/mapas/30056.jpg |archive-date= 22 July 2011 }}</ref>
