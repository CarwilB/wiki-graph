'''Diriomo''' is a town and a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Granada (department)|Granada]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

Diriomo is located on a plateau near the [[Mombacho Volcano Nature Reserve|Mombacho Volcano]], between [[Masatepe]] and [[Granada, Nicaragua|Granada]],<ref>{{Cite web|url = https://vianica.com/nicaragua/granada/diriomo/6.2|title = Diriomo, Nicaragua - ViaNica.com|website = vianica.com|access-date = 2016-04-17}}</ref> 48 kilometers from the capital, Managua.The population of Diriomo Municipality is approximately 34,000.

== History ==
The name of Diriomo comes from the native language of Nahuatlaca, meaning foreign.<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.laprensa.com.ni/2000/12/24/departamentales/752993-diriomo-el-pueblo-ms-brujo|title = Diriomo, el pueblo más brujo|website = La Prensa|access-date = 2016-04-17}}</ref> Before the arrival of the Spaniards, the town belonged to a tribe belonging to the Chorotegas, which also belonged to the neighboring areas of Diriangén, Nicarao and Diriá.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.laprensa.com.ni/2000/12/24/departamentales/752993-diriomo-el-pueblo-ms-brujo|title=Diriomo, el pueblo más brujo|website=La Prensa|access-date=2016-04-18}}</ref>

Diriomo is also considered part of the White Towns, or Pueblos Blancos, along with Nindiri, San Juan de Oriente, San Marcos, Niquinohomo, Masatepe, Catarina, and [[Diria]]. These towns take this name due to the whitewash on their houses and buildings, made from water, salt and lime.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.nicaragua-guide.com/pueblos-blancos.html|title=Pueblos Blancos|website=Nicaragua Guide - The Nica Sagas|access-date=2016-04-18}}</ref> The White Towns are known to represent strong Nicaraguan [[Pre-Columbian era|pre-Columbian]] traditions, dating to before the arrival of the European colonizers.

== Festivals and events ==
[[File:Virgen De Candelaria.jpg|thumb|The Virgen de Candelaria in the February 2nd celebrations in Diriomo]]
Diriomo celebrates the patron festivities from January 21- February 9, with February 2nd as the main day, every year in honor of the [[Virgin of Candelaria|Virgen de Candelaria]]. The Virgin de Candelaria holds a candle in her right hand and baby Jesus on the other. The image of the Virgen is taken to La Raya, the place where it is believed she made one of her biggest miracle of saving the town from death during the volcanic eruption of the Cosigüina in 1835.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.laprensa.com.ni/2016/02/03/departamentales/1979890-celebran-a-virgen-de-candelaria-en-diriomo|title=Celebran a virgen de Candelaria en Diriomo|website=La Prensa|access-date=2016-04-18}}</ref> During the mass on February 2 there is a tradition to bless the candles people bring, which are a representation of the light of Christ.<ref>{{Cite web|url = http://www.laprensa.com.ni/2012/01/21/nacionales/87882-diriomo-celebra-a-la-virgen-de-candelaria|title = Diriomo celebra a la Virgen de Candelaria|website = La Prensa|access-date = 2016-04-17}}</ref>

During this time the pacific environment turns into a busy time with thousands of people coming from all over the country, nearby municipalities and as a tourist destination. People attending can enjoy the traditional drink known as chicha bruja, an alcoholic corn drink. Other traditional foods for the occasion include rosquillas, a corn masa cookie, and cajetas, a local fruit based sweet. During the celebration there are traditional dances offered to the Virgen, including Las Inditas, Los Diablitos y la Sirena.
