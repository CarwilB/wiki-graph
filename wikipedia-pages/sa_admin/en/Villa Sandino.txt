'''Villa Sandino''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|676.5|km2}}, and as per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 15,189 individuals. The municipality was officially created as Villa Somoza on 27 August 1942, and was renamed as Villa Sandino in 1979.

==History==
The origin of the town dates back to 1886 when immigrants from other parts of Nicaragua came in search of land suitable for setting up cattle ranches and settled in the region. These settlers established the village of Pueblo Viejo along the route connecting Chontales with El Rama and Muelle de los Bueyes. Don Dionisio Dávila is regarded as the founder of the settlement. Economic activities such as cattle ranching, [[timber]] logging, and [[Carapichea ipecacuanha|raicilla]] and [[rubber]] extraction contributed to the economic growth of the area, and the construction of the road to El Rama encouraged further migration and settlement. As the population expanded, local authorities petitioned the national government to elevate the settlement to municipal status. The municipality was officially created under the name Villa Somoza by an official decree issued on 27 August 1942. Following the [[Nicaraguan Revolution]] in 1979, the municipality was renamed as Villa Sandino.<ref name="Cul">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/culture-of-villa-sandino/|title=Culture of Villa Sandino|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/municipality-of-villa-sandino/|title=Municipio de Villa Sandino|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Villa Sandino is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|676.5|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/ |title=Administrative Divisions of Nicaragua |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.el19digital.com/articulos/ver/166341-quinto-comando-militar-regional-sostiene-con-reunion-con-productores-de-las-comunidades-del-municipio-de-villa-sandino-chontales|title=Quinto Comando Militar Regional sostiene con reunión con productores de las comunidades del municipio de Villa Sandino, Chontales|work=[[El 19]]|date=14 July 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> The municipality is bordered by the municipalities of [[Santo Tomás, Nicaragua|Santo Tomás]] and [[Muelle de los Bueyes]] to the east, [[Acoyapa]] to the south, and [[Santo Tomás, Nicaragua|Santo Tomás]] to the north and Santo Tomás and Acoyapa to the west. It is located {{cvt|191|km}} from the Nicaraguan capital of [[Managua]].<ref name="Hist"/>

Villa Sandino is located in the southeastern part of the department. The municipality contains several hills and elevated areas. The Mico River crosses the territory and forms a number of pools and recreation areas. It later joins the Rama and Siquia rivers to form the Escondido River.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/nature-of-villa-sandino/|title=Nature of Villa Sandino|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 

== Demographics and economy ==
As per 2023 estimate, Villa Sandino has a population of 15,189 individuals of whom 7,536 were male and 7,653 were female. About 55.7% of the population lived in urban areas, and rest of the population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP">{{cite web |title=Villa Sandino (Municipality, Nicaragua) |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/chontales/6540__villa_sandino/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |work=City Population.de}}</ref>

The economy of the municipality is based on [[livestock]] rearing. Limited [[agriculture]] is practiced and agricultural produce include food grains, [[bananas]], and tubers.<ref name="Hist"/>
