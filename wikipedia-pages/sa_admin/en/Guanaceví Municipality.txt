'''Guanaceví''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat is at [[Guanaceví]]. The municipality covers an area of 5246.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 10,149,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guanaceví|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=009|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=25 August 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160825183426/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=009|url-status=dead}}</ref> down from 10,224 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Guanaceví|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10009a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=November 10, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061124110015/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10009a.htm|archive-date=November 24, 2006|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2010, the town of Guanaceví had a population of 2,908.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Guanaceví, the municipality had 348 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
