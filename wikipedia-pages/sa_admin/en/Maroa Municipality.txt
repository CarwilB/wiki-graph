The '''Maroa Municipality''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Maroa}})  is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the southern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Amazonas (Venezuelan state)|Amazonas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality had a population of 2,029.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref>  The town of [[Maroa, Amazonas|Maroa]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Maroa Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Amazonas.zip{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
Maroa was founded by the [[Cacique]] Maruwa in 1760.<ref>[http://www.amazonas.gob.ve/amazonas/municipios.html Municipios del Estado Amazonas<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100421163113/http://www.amazonas.gob.ve/amazonas/municipios.html |date=2010-04-21 }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Maroa Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, had a population of 1,900 (up from 1,706 in 2000).  This amounted to 1.3% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.1|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=June 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Maroa Municipality is Thaimir Briceño Angulo, elected on October 31, 2004, with 61% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D22%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |title=Archived copy |access-date=2018-10-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303183110/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D22%26mun%3D3%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html |archive-date=2016-03-03 |url-status=bot: unknown }}</ref>  She replaced Antonio Briceño shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Victorino and Comunidad (previous to December 18, 1997, the Maroa Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
