'''Bellavista''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Callao]], [[Peru]]. It is the province's second oldest district, having been created in 1915.

The current [[mayor]] of the district is Daniel Malpartida.

== History ==
The district was created in 1915.

The Navy's Medical Centre (CMST), named after surgeon Major Santiago Távara, was inaugurated in 1956 to service leaders and subordinates of the [[Peruvian Navy]].<ref>{{Cite book |title=Historia de la ortopedia y de la traumatología en el Perú |last=Zaldívar Sobrado |first=César |publisher=[[Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos]] |year=2002 |isbn=9789972461637 |pages=119 |language=es |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=t0xKogP-BEkC}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 4.56 km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 34 meters above [[sea level]].
===Boundaries===
Bellavista is the only district in the [[Callao]] to have an almost perfect geometrical shape, as it is a rectangle where the southern border with both the [[Lima Province]] and [[La Perla District|La Perla]] is marked by [[Avenida Venezuela]].

On the north, Avenida Oscar Benavides (formerly known as Avenida Colonial) marks the district's border with the [[Callao District]], which it also borders on the west in Jr. Andrés Santiago Vigil. On the east, the district ends at the Santiago Tábara Naval Hospital or the western perimeter of the [[National University of San Marcos]], where the Lima Province (or more specifically, the [[Lima District]]) begins.

Bellavista can be divided into four sections, from west to east: Bellavista Cercado (downtown, the historical section), Ciudad del Pescador ("Fisherman City"), Jardines de Virú ("Virů Gardens"), and San Joaquín-San José. [[Estadio Miguel Grau (Callao)|Estadio Miguel Grau]], the largest stadium in Callao Province, is located between Cercado and Ciudad del Pescador.

== Law and government ==

=== Crime ===
Callao is one of the areas of Peru that experiences the most crime due to its designation as the main port city in Peru, it is known as one of the largest exit points of [[cocaine]] and is rife with organized crime that results with violence.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Yagoub |first=Mimi |date=2017-03-27 |title=Peru's New Homicide Index Shows Spiking Violence in Drug Port |url=https://insightcrime.org/news/brief/peru-first-official-murder-stats-show-spiking-violence-in-drug-port/ |access-date=2023-01-15 |website=[[InSight Crime]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Mistler-Ferguson |first=Scott |date=2023-01-12 |title=How Important Is Peru’s Port of Callao for Cocaine Trade? |url=https://insightcrime.org/news/how-important-port-callao-peru-cocaine-trade/ |access-date=2023-01-15 |website=[[InSight Crime]] |language=en-US}}</ref> In 2016, the [[murder rate]] in Callao was double the national average.<ref name=":0" /> In 2023, a raid by the [[Peruvian National Police]] found 3 tons of cocaine located in a home in Bellavista.<ref>{{Cite web |date=7 January 2023 |title=Callao: hallan más de 3 toneladas de cocaína debajo de una casa |url=https://www.americatv.com.pe/noticias/actualidad/callao-hallan-mas-3-toneladas-cocaina-debajo-casa-n466286 |access-date=2023-01-15 |website=[[América Televisión]]}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the [[Peru 2005 Census|2005 census]] by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district had 72,761 inhabitants, and a population density of 15,956.4 persons/km<sup>2</sup>.

The official see of the Callao Regional Government is located in Bellavista.
