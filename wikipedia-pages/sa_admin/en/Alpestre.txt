'''Alpestre''' ({{IPA|pt|awˈpɛs.tɾi|lang|Br-Alpestre.ogg}}) is the northernmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The city lies near the [[Uruguay River]], at its northernmost point. A landmark called Ponto Extremo Norte (''Extreme North Point'') marks the northernmost point of the state.
