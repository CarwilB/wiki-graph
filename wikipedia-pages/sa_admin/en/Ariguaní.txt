'''Ariguaní''' ({{IPA|es|aɾiɣwaˈni}}) is a municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Department of Magdalena|Magdalena]]. The municipality seat is the town of [[El Difícil]]. It is bordered by the municipalities of [[Nueva Granada, Magdalena|Nueva Granada]], [[Sabanas de San Ángel]], [[Pijiño]] and the Municipality of [[Bosconia]] (Department of [[Cesar Department|Cesar]]). According to the last national census (2005), the population is 31,047 Ariguaní inhabitants, of whom 18,313 live in the urban center (El Difícil), and the remaining 12,734 live in rural areas (districts and villages).
