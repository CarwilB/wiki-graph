'''Santo Antônio do Amparo''' is a [[city]] in the southern part of the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 18,613 (2020 est.) in an area of 488.89&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/121J Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The municipality was founded in 1938.
