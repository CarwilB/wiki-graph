[[File:MinasGerais Municip Taiobeiras.svg|thumb|right|250px| Location of Taiobeiras in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]]]
'''Taiobeiras''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. As of 2020 the population was 34,397 in an area of 1,194&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/taiobeiras/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation of the municipal seat is 821 meters.  It became a municipality in 1953.  The postal code (CEP) is  39550-000 and it belongs to the statistical microregion of [[Salinas, Minas Gerais|Salinas]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/taiobeiras/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-23 |archive-date=2009-07-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090727165745/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/taiobeiras/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Taiobeiras is located 44&nbsp;km. north of Salinas on highway MG-404.  It is 704&nbsp;km. to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.estradas.com.br/new/header_sites/mapas.asp |title=Mapa de Estradas |access-date=2008-05-23 |archive-date=2008-05-12 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080512043839/http://www.estradas.com.br/new/header_sites/mapas.asp |url-status=dead }}</ref>
Neighboring municipalities are:
*West: [[Rio Pardo de Minas]],
*North: [[Indaiabira]] and [[São João do Paraíso]]
*East: [[Berizal]] and [[Curral de Dentro]],
*South: [[Santa Cruz de Salinas]] and [[Salinas, Minas Gerais|Salinas]]<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.taiobeiras.mg.gov.br/v1/modulos/upload/paginas/PaginasArquivos_34_63.PDF |title=Map of Region |access-date=2008-05-23 |archive-date=2022-10-05 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221005093724/http://www.taiobeiras.mg.gov.br/v1/modulos/upload/paginas/PaginasArquivos_34_63.PDF |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The economy is based on agriculture with some cattle raising. There were 18,000 head in 2006. The main agricultural crops were coffee, bananas, citrus fruits, mangoes, tomatoes, and corn. The GDP was R$100,657,000 in 2005. In 2006, there were 1,221 rural establishments employing 1,200 salaried workers. The total agricultural land area was 90,819 hectares. There were 2 banking agencies in the urban area.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

This municipality is isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.
*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: .700 (2000)
*State ranking: 544 out of 853 municipalities as of 2000
*National ranking: 2,998 out of 5,138 municipalities as of 2000 <ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>
*Health clinics and hospitals: 2 basic clinics, 8 health centers, and 1 hospital with 65 beds <ref name="Indicators">{{Cite web |url=http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/TAIOBEIRAS.pdf |title=Indicators |access-date=2008-05-23 |archive-date=2011-07-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706160309/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/TAIOBEIRAS.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>
 
The social and health indicators were: degree of [[urbanization]]—79.7%; Illiteracy rate: 26.8%; coverage of sewage system in the urban area—0.60%; coverage of garbage collection in the urban area—85.4; and infant mortality—19.60.<ref name="Indicators"/>
