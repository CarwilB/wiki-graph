'''Antón  ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Antón District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It is located near the north-western shore of the [[Gulf of Panama]]. It is the seat of Antón District.<ref name=inec/> It has a land area of {{convert|106.3|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 9,790 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|92.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 7,220; its population as of 2000 was 8,360.<ref name=inec/>
