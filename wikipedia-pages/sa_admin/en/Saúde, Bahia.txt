'''Saúde''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2013">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2013/dtb_2013.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=September 27, 2013 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=November 2, 2013|language=pt}}</ref> Its population estimated in 2020 is 12,943.
