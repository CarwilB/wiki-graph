'''Asunción Mita''' ({{IPA|es|asunˈsjom ˈmita}}) is a town and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|506.5|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 53,944 inhabitants. The municipality lies close to the border with [[El Salvador]].

==History==
Asunción Mita is described as derived from the [[Nahuatl]] word Mictlan, meaning "place of the dead". Before the Spanish colonialization, it served as the capital of the kingdom of Mita and the chiefdom of Mictlán. After the Spanish arrival, a new town was founded near the ruins of the previous indigenous settlement. The municipality declined during Spanish colonial rule, partly due to restrictions on indigo cultivation, which shifted economic activity to other regions and reduced its former importance.<ref name="Hist"/>

Mita was one of the districts of the [[Chiquimula department]] formed by the government decree on 4 November 1825. On 8 May 1852, Mita district was divided into two districts&ndash;Asunción Mita and [[Santa Catarina Mita]], and both of them were incorporated as a part of [[Jutiapa department|Jutiapa]]. However, it was re-incorporated later into Chiquimula, and on 19 November 1853, it was again segregated from Chiquimula and annexed to Jutiapa. It was listed as villa on 11 February 1915.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Asunción Mita|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Asunción Mita municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/historia-del-municipio-de-asuncion-mita-jutiapa/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Asunción Mita is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|506.5|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Asunción Mita (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jutiapa/2205__asunci%C3%B3n_mita/|access-date=23 May 2026}}</ref> It is situated about {{cvt|146|km}} from [[Guatemala City]]. It borders [[Santa Catarina Mita]] and [[Agua Blanca, Jutiapa|Agua Blanca]] to the north, [[Atescatempa]], and [[Yupiltepeque]] to the south, and [[Jutiapa, Jutiapa|Jutiapa]] and Yupiltepeque to the west. It shares a land border with [[El Salvador]] to the east and south.<ref>{{cite web|title=Geografía del municipio de Asunción Mita, Jutiapa|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jutiapa/geografia-del-municipio-de-asuncion-mita-jutiapa/|trans-title=Geography of Asunción Mita municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> The municipality is located in a fertile plains bound by the Ostúa and Tamasulapa rivers, and considered one of the best agricultural zones on Guatemala’s coastal region.<ref name="Hist"/>

==Climate==
Asuncion Mita is located at an elevation of {{cvt|878.11|m}} above sea level. The district has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|25.17|C}}. The district receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|7.84|cm}} and has 123.78 rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jutiapa/asuncion-mita|title=Asunción Mita|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
As of 1850, Asuncion Mita had a population of approximately 3,300.<ref>{{cite book|last=Baily|first=John|title=Central America; Describing Each of the States of Guatemala, Honduras, Salvador, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica|year=1850|publisher=Trelawney Saunders|location=London|page=88|url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/7306/view/1/88/}}</ref> It had an estimated population of 53,944 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 26,791 males and 27,153 females. About 24.3% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 9.1% was over the age of 65 years. About 56.7% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (43.3%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (82.6%) were born in the same municipality. The city had a literacy rate of 85.8%. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (95.9%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Xinca people|Xinca]] (1.8%) forming a minor minority. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.1%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
