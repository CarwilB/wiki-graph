'''Camasca''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Intibucá department|Intibucá]].

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Camasca municipality had a population of 6,781. Of these, 94.41% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (93.88% [[Lenca]]), 5.28% [[Mestizo]], 0.25% [[Afro-Honduran|Afro-Honduran or Black]], 0.01% [[white Latin Americans|White]] and 0.04% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
