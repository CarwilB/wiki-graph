'''Palotina''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in western [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]] state, located around 60 kilometers from the border with [[Paraguay]].<ref name=F120>Foweraker, p. 120.</ref>

==Location==

The population is 32,121 inhabitants, as of the 2020 estimate. Owing to highly fertile land,<ref name=F120/> the city is a flourishing agricultural hub and a base for two major agricultural companies: C. Vale and I. Rieddi. Another highlight of Palotina is the campus of the [[Federal University of Paraná]] (UFPR). There is also one private university, the UESPAR (União de Ensino Superior do Paraná).

The municipality contains  the {{convert|385|ha|adj=on}} [[São Camilo State Park]], a conservation unit originally created as a biological reserve in 1990.<ref>{{citation|language= pt |title=Parque Estadual de São Camilo |publisher=IAP: Instituto Ambiental do Paraná
 |url=http://www.iap.pr.gov.br/modules/ucps/aviso.php?codigo=120|access-date=2016-04-29}}</ref>

==History==

Settlement in the area started in 1953 when [[Pinho e Terras]] started to colonize the area. The settlement began to grow and on  July 25, 1960, attained the status of municipality. Pinho e Terras approach to colonization was called exemplary,<ref name=F120/> but in the early 1960s the colonists were besieged by gunmen hired by the "new landowners" affiliated with state governor and [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 1945-65)|Socialist Democratic Party]] boss [[Moises Lupion]].<ref>Lupion, according to Foweraker, p. 108, practiced "double baptism" of land, awarding the title of already occupied farmland to the highest bidder.</ref> The federal government moved in a regiment of border guards to pacify the area and in 1963 re-nationalized the disputed lands. The colonists had to buy back their own land.<ref name=F120/>

The name of the municipality comes from [[Vincent Pallotti|Saint Vincent Pallotti]], because some priests, who were members of the [[Pallottines|Society of Catholic Apostolate (SAC)]], were present during the colonization period. The priests of this society are known to this day as "Pallotines"; hence, the name "Palotina" (feminine form, in Portuguese, of "Pallottine").<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/palotina/historico|access-date=2021-11-09|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|title=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística, Palotina, História & Fotos}}</ref> Saint Vincent Pallotti is the patron saint of Palotina; a statue of his can be found in the central square of the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Palotina celebra padroeiro São Vicente Pallotti no dia 22|url=http://www.folhadaterraweb.com.br/noticias/geralregiao/capa/2017/01/palotina-celebra-padroeiro-sao-vicente-pallotti-no-dia-22|access-date=2021-11-09|website=www.folhadaterraweb.com.br}}</ref> The only parish in the municipality, which is made up only of Pallottine priests, is also named after the Saint: in Portuguese, Paróquia São Vicente Pallotti.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Paróquia São Vicente Palotti - Palotina|url=https://www.diocesetoledo.org/paroquias/ler/287|access-date=2021-11-09|website=www.diocesetoledo.org}}</ref> The parish belongs to the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Toledo, Brazil|Diocese of Toledo]].

The major part of the population is catholic.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/palotina/panorama|access-date=2021-11-09|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|title=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística, Palotina, Panorama}}</ref>

Agriciltural economy of Palotina relies on production of corn, wheat and soybean. A 1969 survey placed Palotina in the cluster of most efficient agricultural communities in Brazil.<ref name="F46" /> According to authors of the study, higher productivity in this cluster stemmed from raising pigs, which accounts for 68% of its output. 82% of lands were allotted to fodder crops rather than traditional rice, bean and cotton.<ref name="F46">Foweraker, p 46.</ref>

Nowadays, Palotina is a near 30k inhabitants municipality with an agroindustrial economy and a hopeful future. Most of the population, around 68%, have Italian ancestry and about 13% of the community have German ancestry.

On the morning of 26 July 2023, at the grain cooperative C.Vale exploded, causing 9 employees dead and 11 injuries. Investigation is still ongoing.

==Climate==
[[Humid subtropical climate]] of Palotina (''Cfa'' in [[Köppen climate classification]]) is characterized by humid and hot summers, while winters are cool. Precipitation is moderate all year long.
