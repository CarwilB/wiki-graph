'''El Calvario''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]]. It was the epicenter of the [[2008 Colombia earthquake]].

==Climate==
El Calvario has a borderline [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Cfb'')/[[tropical rainforest climate]] (''Af'') with mild mornings, warm afternoons and heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
