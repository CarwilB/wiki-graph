'''Contralmirante Villar''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Tumbes Region]] in [[Peru]]. Its capital is the town of [[Zorritos]].

The [[Cerros de Amotape National Park]] is partially located in this province, which is the largest in the Tumbes Region.<ref>{{Cite web |title= Provincia de Tumbes|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070501102521/http://www.tumbes.com/departamento/provincias/index.html |url=http://www.tumbes.com/departamento/provincias/index.html|language=es |archive-date=2007-05-01|website=tumbes.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title= Cerros de Amotape|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160530080825/http://www.sernanp.gob.pe/cerros-de-amotape |url=http://www.sernanp.gob.pe/cerros-de-amotape|language=es |archive-date=2016-05-30|website=sernanp.gob.pe}}</ref>

==Political division==
The province of Contralmirante Villar is divided into three [[Districts of Peru|district]]s ({{Langx|es|distritos}}, singular: ''distrito''), each of which is headed by a mayor (''[[alcalde]]''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:
* [[Casitas District|Casitas]] ([[Cañaveral, Peru|Cañaveral]])
* [[Zorritos District|Zorritos]] ([[Zorritos]])
* [[Canoas de Punta Sal District|Canoas de Punta Sal]] ([[Canoas de Punta Sal]])
