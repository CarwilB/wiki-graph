'''Rawson Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 115,829 inhabitants in an area of 3,922&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Rawson, Chubut|Rawson]], which is located around 1,404&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
