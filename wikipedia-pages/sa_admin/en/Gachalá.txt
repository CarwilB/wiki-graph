'''Gachalá''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Guavio Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The urban centre of Gachalá is situated at a distance of {{convert|148|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]] at an altitude of {{convert|1712|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. The municipality borders the western portion of the split municipality [[Ubalá]] and the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]] in the north, the eastern part of Ubalá and [[Medina, Cundinamarca|Medina]] in the east, [[Fómeque]], [[Junín, Cundinamarca|Junín]] and [[Gama, Cundinamarca|Gama]] in the west and Fómeque, Medina and the department of [[Meta Department|Meta]] in the south.<ref name=websiteGachala>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150516191709/http://www.gachala-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Gachalá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name of Gachalá is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "clay vessel of the night" or "defeat of the night".<ref name=websiteGachala/><ref name=Gachala>{{in lang|es}} [http://institucionmurcagachala.blogspot.com.co/2012/05/gachala-cundinamarca-informacion-de.html Gachalá, Cundinamarca]</ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], Gachalá was inhabited by the Chío tribe, belonging to the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. As of 1548, the terrain of Gachalá belonged to the ''[[cacique]]'' of [[Guatavita]].<ref name=Gachala/>

Modern Gachalá was founded on February 22, 1810, by Mariano de Mendoza y Bueno.<ref name=websiteGachala/>

== Economy ==
The main economical activity in Gachalá is the hydroelectric plant of the [[Guavio Reservoir]].<ref name=Gachala/> [[Colombian emeralds|Emerald mining]] is also an important source of income. The {{convert|858|carat|g}} [[Gachalá Emerald]] was found in and named after Gachalá.
