'''Zetaquira'''{{efn|Also sometimes spelled ''Zetaquirá''}} ({{IPA|es|setaˈkiɾa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Lengupá Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Zetaquira is located at {{convert|67|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] and borders [[Pesca]] in the north, [[Miraflores, Boyacá|Miraflores]] in the south, in the east [[Berbeo]], [[San Eduardo, Boyacá|San Eduardo]] and [[Aquitania, Boyacá|Aquitania]] and in the west [[Ramiriquí]] and [[Chinavita]]. The municipality stretches over an area of {{convert|262|km2|mi2}} on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at altitudes between {{convert|1875|m|ft}} and {{convert|3600|m|ft}}.<ref name=websiteZetaquira>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.zetaquira-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Zetaquirá] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310085303/http://www.zetaquira-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2014-03-10 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Zetaquira in [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] means "Land of the snake" or "City of the snake".<ref name=websiteZetaquira/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Municipalities Boyacá] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180707011243/http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html |date=2018-07-07 }} - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The central highlands of the Colombian [[Andes]] in the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] were inhabited by various indigenous peoples. The predominant culture was the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in a loose [[Muisca Confederation|confederation]]. Zetaquira was part of the reign of the ''[[zaque]]'', based in [[Tunja|Hunza]], present-day Tunja. Other indigenous people in the vicinity of Zetaquira were the [[Achagua people|Achagua]] and [[Tegua people|Tegua]].

Modern Zetaquira was not founded until May 21, 1765, by Pedro López.<ref name=websiteZetaquira/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Zetaquira are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Predominant agricultural products are [[coffee]], [[sugar cane]], [[maize]], [[banana]]s, [[bean]]s and [[arracacha]].<ref name=websiteZetaquira/> Tourism, mainly the [[thermal bath]]s in the municipality, is another source of income.
