'''Angostura Municipality''' (also '''Bolivariano Angostura''', prior to June 2009 called '''Raúl Leoni Municipality''') is one of the eleven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 40,927. <ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |accessdate=2016-06-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archivedate=2016-03-15 }}</ref> The town of [[Ciudad Piar]] is the [[County seat|administrative seat]] of Angostura Municipality.<ref name="Bolivar-data">http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Bolivar.zip{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality was formerly named for the Venezuelan president [[Raúl Leoni]], who served 1964 to 1969.

==Demographics==
Angostura Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, had a population of 47,167 (up from 36,654 in 2000).  This amounts to 3.1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|0.83|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
