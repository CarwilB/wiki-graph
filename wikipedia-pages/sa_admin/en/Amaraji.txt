'''Amaraji'''  is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2025]]) population of 18,335 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Chã Grande]] ('''N'''); [[Ribeirão, Pernambuco|Ribeirão]] ('''S'''); [[Primavera, Pernambuco|Primavera]] ('''E'''); [[Gravatá]] and [[Cortês]] ('''W''')
* Area - 234.78&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 289m
* Hydrography - [[Sirinhaém River|Sirinhaém]] and [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 24.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 101.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Amaraji''' are based in food & beverage industry and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s, [[manioc]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
