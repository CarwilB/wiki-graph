'''La Palma''' ({{IPA|es|la ˈpal.ma}}) is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 4,205 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Chepigana District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 11,632; its population as of 2000 was 3,884.<ref name=inec/>
It is located at the mouth of the [[Tuira River|Tuira river]], where the wide river meets the [[Golfo de San Miguel]]. The Chucunaque joins the Tuira further south.

==Economy==
La Palma is the principal trading center for the large sparsely populated region that surrounds it. Plantains, corn, and rice are cultivated, and livestock is raised; there is also some sawmilling. The town is isolated, with no highway linking it to other communities in central and western Panama.{{fact|date=July 2025}}
