'''Calvas''' is a [[Cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of the [[Loja Province|Province of Loja]], [[Ecuador]], located in the south of the province, bordering [[Paltas Canton|Paltas]] and [[Gonzanamá]] in the north, [[Quilanga]] and [[Espíndola]] in the east, [[Peru]] in the south, and [[Sozoranga]] in the west.  It covers 851.2 km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of about 1,932 m.  The total population is 26,042, and the capital city of the canton is [[Cariamanga]].

==Attractions==
* Ahuaca Hill - 2463m tall, it is made up of two peaks.  A cross is placed at 2454m as a symbol of local faith.
