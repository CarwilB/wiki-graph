'''San Jerónimo Tecuanipan''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Puebla]].<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/puebla/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=January 4, 2010}}</ref> 
In 2020, the municipality reported a total population of 6,597, an increase of 13.2% from 2010.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Jerónimo Tecuanipan: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/san-jeronimo-tecuanipan |publisher=Secretaría de Economía |access-date=24 January 2024}}</ref>
