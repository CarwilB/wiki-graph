'''Guaymas''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 31, 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 }}</ref> In 2015, the municipality had a total population of 158,046.<ref name="inegi.org.mx"/> The municipal seat is the city of [[Guaymas]].
