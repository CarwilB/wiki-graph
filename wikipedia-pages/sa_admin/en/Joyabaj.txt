'''Joyabaj''' ({{IPA|es|xoʝaˈβax}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Quiché Department|El Quiché]].  It is located about 50 kilometers from [[Santa Cruz del Quiché]], in the [[Sierra de Chuacús]] mountains.

Joyabaj was an important part of the royal route to Mexico during the Spanish time.  On February 4, 1976, the town was almost destroyed by an [[1976 Guatemala earthquake|earthquake]]. Most of its beautiful colonial houses and buildings were lost and hundreds of people died. Joyabaj was also hit hard by the civil war that lasted about 30 years in Guatemala. The majority of the ''ladinos'' (people with mixed Spanish and Indian heritage) fled to [[Guatemala City]].

The town's ''fiesta'' is celebrated from August 8–15. Many activities take place every day. One of the most important and fantastic celebrations is [[Danza de los Voladores de Papantla|El Palo Volador]]. This is a traditional dance in which flying angels (ángeles) spin to the ground from a huge wooden pole. The '''Palo Volador''' reenacts the descend of the angels into the underworld to battle the forces of darkness. This a pre-conquest ritual that has survived many centuries.

The people from Joyabaj are called ''Xoyes'' or ''Joyabatecos''.
