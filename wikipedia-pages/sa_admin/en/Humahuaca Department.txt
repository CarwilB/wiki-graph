'''Humahuaca Department''' is a department located in the [[Jujuy Province]] of Argentina.  Its capital city is [[Humahuaca]]. Part of the [[Quebrada de Humahuaca]] extends through the length of the department.

== Size, borders and access ==
Humahuaca Department has a [[surface area]] of {{convert|3792|km2|mi2}}, and as of the 2010 [[census]] had a [[population density]] of 4.6 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=10 December 2016 |archive-date=28 August 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160828122414/http://dipec.jujuy.gov.ar/dc_poblacion/2.2.5.pdf |language=es |title=Población. Superficie y Densidad Poblacional, según Departamentos. Años 1991-2001-2010 |url=http://www.dipec.jujuy.gov.ar/dc_poblacion/2.2.5.pdf |work=Dirección Provincial de Estadística y Censos - Provincia de Jujuy}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> The department is bordered on the east by the province of [[Salta (province)|Salta]], on the west by [[Cochinoca Department]], and on the south by the [[Tumbaya Department|Tumbaya]], [[Tilcara Department|Tilcara]], and [[Valle Grande Department]]s.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=11 December 2016 |archive-date=14 November 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161114215039/http://www.dipec.jujuy.gov.ar/mapasgeoreferenciados.html |language=es |title=Provincia de Jujuy por departamento. Mapa político. |url=http://www.dipec.jujuy.gov.ar/mapasgeoreferenciados.html |work=Dirección Provincial de Estadística y Censos - Provincia de Jujuy}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

The primary means of accessing the department is via [[National Route 9 (Argentina)|National Route 9]].<ref>{{cite web|access-date=11 December 2016 |archive-date=21 December 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161221115052/http://localidades.cfi.org.ar/Jujuy/Humahuaca/Bienvenidos/1821/3234 |language=es |title=Humahuaca |url=http://localidades.cfi.org.ar/Jujuy/Humahuaca/Bienvenidos/1821/3234 |work=Consejo Federal de Inversiones}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>
