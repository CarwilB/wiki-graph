'''Pitimbu''' is a [[city]] in the state of [[Paraíba]] (Brazil), located in the microregion of the South Coast and metropolitan area of [[João Pessoa, Paraíba|João Pessoa]]. According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] (Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics), in 2020 its population was estimated at 19,275 inhabitants, 46% in urban areas. Its area is {{cvt|136|km2}} representing 0.241% of the state.

The municipality contains a small part of the {{convert|6677|ha|adj=on}} [[Acaú-Goiana Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use conservation unit created in 2007.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Acaú-Goiana – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Acaú-Goiana|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/581550|accessdate=2016-06-06}}</ref>
