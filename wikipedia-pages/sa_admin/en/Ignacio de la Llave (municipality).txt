'''Ignacio de la Llave''', previously known as San Cristobal de la Llave, is a municipality located in the region of La Mixtequilla, which is near the western limit of the [[Papaloapan River|Papaloapan]] river, going through the [[Blanco river (Mexico)|Blanco]], [[Limón river (Mexico)|Limón]] and [[Las Pozas (mexico)|Las Pozas]] rivers. The climate of the region varies between hot-humid and tropical-humid, with frequent summer rains between July and September. The average temperature is from 64 and 72 [[Fahrenheit|°F]], with a maximum registered in the high 70s [[Fahrenheit|°F]]{{quantify|date=May 2023}} (24-26°C) and the lowest in the high 40{{quantify|date=May 2023}} [[Fahrenheit|°F]] (7-9°C).<ref name=":0">{{Cite book|title=La muerte en El Zapotal: el simbolismo de sus esculturas.|last=Suzan Morales|first=Adelina|publisher=|year=1997|isbn=|location=|pages=|quote=|via=}}</ref> Ignacio de la Llave is a neighbor to and has deep political, social and trading relationships with: [[Alvarado (municipality)|Alvarado]], [[Acula (municipality)|Acula]], [[Ixmatlahuacan]], [[Tlalixcoyan]] and&nbsp;[[Tierra Blanca, Veracruz|Tierra Blanca]].<ref name=":0" />

The municipality celebrates its carnival in the month of March were music, dances and joy are spread by its inhabitants.

Mayor Juan José Flores Lira died of a heart attack on April 24, 2017.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://cronicadelpoder.com/2017/04/24/fallece-de-un-infarto-alcalde-de-ignacio-de-la-llave/|title = Fallece, de un infarto, alcalde de Ignacio de la Llave|date = 24 April 2017}}</ref>

== Toponymy ==
The municipality's is named after General [[Ignacio de la Llave]],<ref name=":1">{{Cite book|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|last=|first=|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipales|year=2005|isbn=|location=|pages=|quote=|via=}}</ref> who was [[governor]] for the state of [[Veracruz]] from 1861 to 1862. It shares its name with the state of [[Veracruz]], just in a different order.

== Physical medium ==

=== Location ===
It's located in the central zone of the state of Veracruz, in the previously known zone named ''Las llanuras del sotavento,''<ref name=":1" /> in the economic-administrative region called the [[Papaloapan Region (Veracruz)|Papaloapan Region]].<ref>{{Cite book|title=Estudios Regionales Para la Planeación Información básica|last=|first=|publisher=Secretaría de finanzas y planeación del estado de Veracruz|year=2005|isbn=|location=|pages=|quote=|via=}}</ref>

=== Borders ===
It borders on the north with the municipalities of Tlalixcoyan and Alvarado, to the south with Ixmatlahuacan and Tierra Blanca, to the west with Tierra Blanca and Tlalixcoyan and to the east with Alvarado and Ixmatlahuacan.<ref name=":2">{{Cite book|title=Sistema de información municipal; cuadernillos municipales, 2016|last=|first=|publisher=Secretaria de finanzas y planeacion del estado de Veracruz.|year=2016|isbn=|location=|pages=|quote=|via=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book|title=Compendio de información geográfica municipal Tierra Blanca, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave|last=|first=|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|year=2010|isbn=|location=|pages=|quote=|via=}}</ref>
