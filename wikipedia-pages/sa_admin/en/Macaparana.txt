'''Macaparana''' is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located {{convert|120|km|abbr=off}} from [[Recife]], the capital of Pernambuco. As of the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], it had a population of 23,879.<ref name="IBGEMacaparana2025" />

==Geography==
* State – [[Pernambuco]]
* Region – [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries – [[Paraíba]] state   ('''N''' and '''W''');  [[São Vicente Ferrer, Pernambuco|São Vicente Ferrer]] and [[Vicência]]   ('''S''');  [[Timbaúba]]   ('''E''').
* Area – {{convert|126.35|km2|2|lk=on|abbr=off}}
* Elevation – {{convert|350|m|ft|lk=out|abbr=off}}
* Hydrography – [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation – [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate – Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature – {{convert|23.1|C|F|1|lk=on|abbr=off}}
* Distance to [[Recife]] – {{convert|120|km|mi|lk=out|abbr=off}}

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Macaparana''' are based in industry, [[commerce]] and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
