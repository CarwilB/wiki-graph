'''Arraial do Cabo''' ({{IPA|pt|ɐʁɐjˈaw du ˈkabu}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 30,593 as of 2020 [[census]] and its total area is {{convert|160|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="IBGE">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php | title = Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics Website | access-date = November 20, 2013 }}</ref>

It was founded in 1503 by the conqueror [[Amerigo Vespucci]]. In 1960 a documentary film was made directed by Mário Carneiro and Paulo Cesar Saraceni about the local fishing industry.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ubWPrKfAAyg Arraial do Cabo, film by Mário Carneiro and Paulo Cesar Saraceni on YouTube]</ref>

Its geography made Arraial do Cabo an important and dangerous point in the age of sail. From the arrival of the Portuguese Navy in 1503 through the nineteenth century, many shipwrecks occurred. Due to this fact (and other biological factors) Arraial do Cabo is well known as the "Dive Capital".

The municipality operates the [[Ilha do Cabo Frio Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit on an Atlantic island in the south east of the municipality.<ref>{{citation|title=Unidades de Conservação da Região dos Lagos |publisher=Petrobras |language=pt |url=http://licenciamento.ibama.gov.br/Petroleo/Reservat%F3rio%20Membro%20Siri%20-%20%20Badejo/Mapas/MAPA%20II.5.2-1a%20Unidades%20de%20Conserva%E7%E3o%20da%20Regi%E3o%20dos%20Lagos.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160509163337/http://licenciamento.ibama.gov.br/Petroleo/Reservat%F3rio%20Membro%20Siri%20-%20%20Badejo/Mapas/MAPA%20II.5.2-1a%20Unidades%20de%20Conserva%E7%E3o%20da%20Regi%E3o%20dos%20Lagos.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-date=May 9, 2016 |access-date=2016-04-26 }}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|56769|ha}} [[Arraial do Cabo Marine Extractive Reserve]], created in 1997.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Marinha do Arraial do Cabo – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX Marinha do Arraial do Cabo|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/590409|access-date=2016-09-13}}</ref>
