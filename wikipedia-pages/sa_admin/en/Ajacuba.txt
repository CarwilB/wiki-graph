''' Ajacuba ''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|192.7|km2}}.

As of 2005, the municipality's population was 16,111.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ajacuba |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13005a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=December 27, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071019234745/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/hidalgo/municipios/13005a.htm |archive-date=October 19, 2007 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
It is located between the parallels  99° 27’ 51” and 99° 07’ 32”  west longitude, and 20° 05’ 35” and 18° 55’ 22” north latitude. Ajacuba borders. It covers a total surface area of {{cvt|192.7|km2}} at an altitude of 6,634&nbsp;ft.  In the year 2010 census by [[INEGI]], it reported a population of 17,055.<ref>[http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=13&mun=005 Ajacuba municipality] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160617182427/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=13&mun=005 |date=2016-06-17 }} SEDESOL, Catálogo de localidades.</ref>

The town of Ajacuba, as the municipal seat, has governing jurisdiction over the following communities: Santiago Tezontlale, Vicente Guerrero, Tecomatlan, and Tulancalco.<ref name="INEGI">{{cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |author=INEGI |title=Link to tables of population data from Census of 2005 INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |access-date=2008-10-25 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110613183420/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2011-06-13 }}</ref> The total municipality extends 96.37 and borders with the municipalities of [[Tetepango]], [[San Agustín Tlaxiaca]], [[Atitalaquia]], [[Atotonilco de Tula]], [[Progreso de Obregón]] and the [[state of México]] ([[Hueypoxtla]] and [[Apaxco]]).

The municipal seat is in a small, elongated valley but most of the municipality is inside of the [[Mezquital Valley]].<ref name="EncMuc">[]</ref> The highest [[mountain]] the Picacho in the [[Sierra Tezontlalpa]], it rises {{convert|2800|m}} above sea level,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tequixquiac.gob.mx/medio-fisico/|title=Medio Físico – Ayuntamiento de Tequixquiac|website=www.tequixquiac.gob.mx|access-date=2016-06-04|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161129171743/http://www.tequixquiac.gob.mx/medio-fisico/|archive-date=2016-11-29|url-status=dead}}</ref> on the border between the municipalities of San Agustín Tlaxiaca and [[Atitalaquia]].

== Tourism ==
Ajacuba is a tourist destination, with 6 balnearios:<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oY0XK6TMaDw |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211215/oY0XK6TMaDw |archive-date=2021-12-15 |url-status=live|title=Ajacuba, Hidalgo|last=Paco5|date=20 October 2016|via=YouTube}}{{cbignore}}</ref> "Ejidal Las Lumbreras", "Ajacuba", "La Carreta", "Los Arcos", "El Sol" and "Los Toboganes". The people who visit Ajacuba usually eat the regional dishes such as ''barbacoa'', ''tlacoyos'', ''tacos'', etc.
