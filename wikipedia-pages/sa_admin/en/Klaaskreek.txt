'''Klaaskreek''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Brokopondo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 2,124.<ref name="census"/> Located northeast of Brokopondo, the main town is Reinsdorp. In 2007, a technical training centre in biological agriculture was established at Klaaskreek to teach locals about enhancing agriculture in the area.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://sgp.undp.org/web/projects/10544/planning_grant_technical_training_centre_in_biological_agriculture_at_klaaskreek.html |title=United Nations Development Programme |access-date=2010-01-26 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110723111936/http://sgp.undp.org/web/projects/10544/planning_grant_technical_training_centre_in_biological_agriculture_at_klaaskreek.html |archive-date=2011-07-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==
Around 1860 an expedition led by J. Rosenberg set out to discover [[gold]]. Even though some gold was discovered near Victoria, the government decided not to pursue the matter. Rosenberg however persisted, established the ''New-York and Surinam Company'', and finally received a concession in 1870. The company failed to meet its objectives, and the concession was nullified in 1874.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië - Page 312 - Goudindustrie|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01.pdf|date=1916|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

Klaaskreek is situated in the proximity of the [[Brokopondo Reservoir]]. In 1958 Klaaskreek was designated as one of the transmigration towns for the inhabitants of the area that was flooded after the construction of the [[Afobaka Dam]],<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Onderzoek-Regionaal-Plan-Brokopondo-2015-2040.pdf|title=Onderzoek Regionaal plan Bronsweg 2015-2040|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=12 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> and the main settlement for people from [[Ganzee]].<ref name="herald">{{cite news|url=https://www.srherald.com/ingezonden/2019/02/01/nostalgie-en-toch-toekomstgericht-denken-en-handelen/ |title=Nostalgie en toch toekomstgericht denken en handelen|website=Suriname Herald|access-date=15 February 2021|language=nl}}</ref> Klaaskreek is a [[tribe|tribal]]<ref name="planning"/> area mainly inhabited by [[Maroon (people)|Maroons]].<ref name="census"/>

Klaaskreek has a school, police station,<ref name="planning"/> [[civil engineering]] company.,<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.waterkant.net/suriname/2012/08/17/surinaams-dorpje-baalt-van-smerig-bedrijf/|title=Surinaams dorpje baalt van ‘smerig’ bedrijf|website=Waterkant.net|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> river [[resort]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tripadvisor.nl/Hotel_Review-g4682833-d1806958-Reviews-New_Babunhol_River_Resort-Klaaskreek_Brokopondo_District.html|title=New Babunhol River Resort|website=Trip Advisor|access-date=15 May 2020}}</ref> and a beach called Benastrand.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surivision.nl/klaaskreek/|title=Klaaskreek|website=Surivision|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> [[Medische Zending]] operates a clinic in the town.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|title=Zorggebied|website=Medische Zending.sr|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=30 September 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220930070719/http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Towns==
Other villages in the resort include [[Berg en Dal (Suriname)|Berg en Dal]], Kapasikélé, [[Njun Lombe]], Mujékiki, and Reinsdorp.<ref name="planning"/> In 1960, an agriculture pilot project was started in Brokobaka, [[Centrum, Brokopondo|Centrum]].<ref name="suriname_nu">{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname.nu/175alg/brokopondo01.html|title=Distrikt Brokopondo|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname.nu}}</ref> The project led to a [[palm oil]] plantation and factory in the former plantation of Victoria.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/p/phedra|title=Plantages / P / Phedra |website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=15 May 2020}}</ref> however the [[Surinamese Interior War]] and [[lethal yellowing]] resulted in the closure of the factory in 1996.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2016/11/14/oliepalmproductie-zou-geen-prioriteit-moeten-zijn/|title=Oliepalmproductie zou geen prioriteit moeten zijn|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
