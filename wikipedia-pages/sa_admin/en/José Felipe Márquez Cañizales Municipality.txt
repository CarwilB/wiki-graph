'''José Felipe Márquez Cañizales''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of {{convert|648|km2|abbr=on}} with a population of 6,492 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following three parishes, with the seat of each in parentheses:

* [[Antonio José de Sucre, Trujillo|Antonio José de Sucre]] ([[La Placita, Venezuela|La Placita]]) ;
* [[El Socorro, Trujillo|El Socorro]] ([[El Paradero]]) ;
* [[Los Caprichos (civil parish)|Los Caprichos]] ([[Los Caprichos, Venezuela|Los Caprichos]])
