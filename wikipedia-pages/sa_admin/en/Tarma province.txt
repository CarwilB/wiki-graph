'''Tarma''' is a [[Peru|Peruvian province]], making up one of the nine provinces that conform the [[Junín Region]].  To the north it borders with the province of [[Junín Province|Junín]], to the east with the province of [[Chanchamayo Province|Chanchamayo]], to the south with the province of [[Jauja Province|Jauja]] and to the west with the province of [[Yauli Province|Yauli]].

The region has an area of {{convert|2749.16|km2|sp=us}}, which represents the 6.2% of the departmental territory. It is located in a position privileged, touristy and economic, halfway between the capital and the high forest, and point of convergence of various routes towards the central forest. The climate is temperate and mild during most of months predominates.
