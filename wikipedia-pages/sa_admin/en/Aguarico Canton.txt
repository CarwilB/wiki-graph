'''Aguarico Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Orellana Province]].  Its capital is the town of [https://web.archive.org/web/20171117052239/http://anda.inec.gob.ec/anda/index.php/catalog/270 Nuevo Rocafuerte].  Its population at the 2010 census was 4,658 INEC 2010.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://anda.inec.gob.ec/anda/index.php/catalog/270|title=Ecuador - VII Censo de Población y VI de Vivienda 2010 - Información general|website=anda.inec.gob.ec|access-date=2017-11-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171117052239/http://anda.inec.gob.ec/anda/index.php/catalog/270|archive-date=2017-11-17|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

Aguarico is part of the [[Cuyabeno Wildlife Reserve|Cuyabeno Natural Reserve]] and [[Yasuni National Park]].

==History==
Aguarico dates from the late nineteenth century during an estate boom on the banks of the [[Napo River]] in the Amazon region.  During the invasion of Peru in Ecuador in 1941, Rocafuerte was taken by the Peruvian army after defeating the Ecuadorian military garrison on 11 August of that year. Following the armed conflict, almost all rubber plantations were abandoned.

On 22 February 1945, by order of President [[Jose Maria Velasco Ibarra]], the town of [[Nuevo Rocafuerte]] officially became the new county seat of the Canton of Aguarico.
