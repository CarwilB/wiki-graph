'''Ayapel''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Córdoba Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

Ayapel is a Colombian municipality located in the far eastern department of Cordoba and bathed by the waters of San Jorge and Ayapel swamp.
Bounded on the north by San Marcos, [[San Benito Abad]], and Majagual, on the east by Guaranda and Achi, on the west by Buenavista and [[Montelíbano|Montelibano]], Pueblo Nuevo and south to the department of Antioquia. According to 2020 estimates, the population of Ayapel was 47,247.
