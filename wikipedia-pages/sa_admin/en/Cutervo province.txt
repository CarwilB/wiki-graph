'''Cutervo''' is one of the thirteen [[provinces of Peru|provinces]] that make up the [[Cajamarca Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-04-16 |title=Health Alert: Updates to Government of Peru Quarantine and Movement Restrictions (April 16, 2021) |url=https://pe.usembassy.gov/health-alert-updates-to-government-of-peru-quarantine-and-movement-restrictions-april-16-2021/ |access-date=2022-06-07 |website=U.S. Embassy in Peru |language=en-US}}</ref> It is the home of the [[Cutervo National Park]]. It has a population of 162,686 and an area {{Convert|3028.46|km2|sp=us}}.
==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[Jaén Province, Peru|Province of Jaén]]
*'''East''': [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]]
*'''South''': [[Chota Province]]
*'''West''': [[Lambayeque Region]]
