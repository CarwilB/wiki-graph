'''Guazapares''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Témoris]]. The municipality covers an area of 2,145.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 8,998,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Guazapares|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=030|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=4 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304140544/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=030|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from  8,010 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Guazapares|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08030a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal|accessdate=September 28, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070223203436/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08030a.htm|archivedate=February 23, 2007}}</ref>

The municipality had 492 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) was: [[Témoris, Chihuahua|Témoris]] (2,053), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

== History ==

As a pueblo, Guazapares was home to the '''Guazapare people''',<ref>Also transcribed as Guacapane, Guacapare, Guarapare, Guasapare, Guasapari, Guaspare, Guazapari, or Gucapari</ref> a tribal group who spoke a dialect of the [[Tarahumara language]].<ref name="thomas">{{cite book |last1=Thomas |first1=Cyrus |title=Indian Languages of Mexico and Central America and Their Geographical Distribution |isbn=978-0-8466-4042-4 |url=https://www.loc.gov/item/11035495/ |access-date=9 May 2024 |language=English |page=36}}</ref> After [[Jesuit]] missionaries [[Julio Pascual]] and [[Manuel Martinez (Jesuit)|Manuel Martinez]] were killed in a 1632 uprising, {{ill|Pedro de Perea|es}} led a punitive expedition against native groups including the Guazapare, killing about 800 natives. The surviving Guazapare were [[reductions|reduced]] into the [[Spanish missions in the Sonoran Desert|Jesuit missions]].<ref name="barr">{{cite book |last1=Barr |first1=Juliana |last2=Countryman |first2=Edward |title=Contested Spaces of Early America |date=7 March 2014 |publisher=University of Pennsylvania Press |isbn=978-0-8122-0933-4 |pages=130–131 |language=en}}</ref>
