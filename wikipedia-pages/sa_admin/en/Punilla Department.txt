The '''Punilla Department''' is an administrative division of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]], [[Argentina]]. It covers large parts of the name-giving [[Punilla Valley|Punilla valley]] and is located west of the provincial capital [[Córdoba, Argentina|Córdoba]]. In 2022, the department had over 173,000 inhabitants, with a population density of almost 90 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>.

== Geography ==
The department is located between the two mountain ranges of the [[Sierras de Córdoba]], the Sierras Grandes to the west, and the Sierras Chicas to the east. The department is crossed in a north-south direction by national road RN38, which connects the department with [[Córdoba, Argentina|Córdoba]], [[Cruz del Eje]] and further north the province of [[La Rioja Province, Argentina|La Rioja]].

== History ==
Prior to the [[Spanish Empire|Spanish conquest]], the area was inhabited by the [[Comechingón|Comechingones]]. 

The population of the department grew towards the end of the 19th and early 20th century, when the Argentine railway network reached it, leading to an increase in tourism and health institutions like [[Sanatorium|tuberculosis sanatoriums]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=La historia milenaria del Valle de Punilla, una tierra de leyendas |url=https://www.eldiariodecarlospaz.com.ar/sociedad/2025/8/30/la-historia-milenaria-del-valle-de-punilla-una-tierra-de-leyendas-242371.html |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=www.eldiariodecarlospaz.com.ar |language=spanish}}</ref>

== Economy ==
Tourism is one of the main and growing branches of the local economy,<ref>{{Cite web |last=Córdoba |first=Gobierno de |date=2023-05-19 |title=Punilla, un departamento en permanente crecimiento |url=https://www.lavoz.com.ar/espacio-institucional/punilla-un-departamento-en-permanente-crecimiento_0_YPNThxCYGz.html |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=www.lavoz.com.ar |language=es-LA}}</ref> including the production and sale of regional food varieties such as Cordobés [[Alfajor|alfajores]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-29 |title=Región Punilla – Córdoba Turismo |url=https://cordobaturismo.gov.ar/experiencias/region-punilla/ |access-date=2026-05-29 |language=es}}</ref>

Compared to other departments in the province, the raising of livestock plays a minor role in the local economy.<ref>{{Cite news |last=IDECOR |date=2024-06-26 |title=La producción ganadera de Córdoba, actualizada en 8 mapas |url=https://www.idecor.gob.ar/la-produccion-ganadera-de-cordoba-actualizada-en-8-mapas/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20260314091058/https://www.idecor.gob.ar/la-produccion-ganadera-de-cordoba-actualizada-en-8-mapas/ |archive-date=2026-03-14 |access-date=2026-05-29 |work=IDECOR |language=es-ES}}</ref>

== Tourism ==
The department includes a number of national and international tourist attractions, like the city of [[Villa Carlos Paz]] with its lake, the cities of [[Capilla del Monte]], [[La Cumbre, Argentina|La Cumbre]] and [[La Falda]] in the mountains. With [[Uritorco|Cerro Uritorco]], it also includes the highest peak of the Sierras Chicas with hiking trails.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Punilla Valley - Tourism in CÃ³rdoba |url=https://www.welcomeargentina.com/provincia-cordoba/punilla-valley.html |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=www.welcomeargentina.com}}</ref> The rivers of the department offer beaches and balnearios, and more active tourism options like para gliding, horseback riding and biking.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Valle de Punilla - Destino Punilla - Guía turística argentina |url=https://destinopunilla.com.ar/ |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=Destino Punilla |language=es}}</ref>

The city of [[Cosquín, Argentina|Cosquín]] is home to two annual festivals that attract large numbers of visitors: The [[Cosquín Festival]] for folk music, which started in 1961,<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cosquín |url=http://turismo-argentina.net/cosquin/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110724114028/http://turismo-argentina.net/cosquin/ |archive-date=2011-07-24 |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=turismo-argentina.net}}</ref> and [[Cosquín Rock]], which takes place since 2001.<ref>{{Cite web |title=La Historia – Cosquin Rock |url=https://cosquinrock.net/historia/ |archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20250718143059/https://cosquinrock.net/historia/ |archive-date=2025-07-18 |access-date=2026-05-29 |website=cosquinrock.net |language=es-AR}}</ref>
