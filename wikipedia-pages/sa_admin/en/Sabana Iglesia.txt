'''Sabana Iglesia''' is a [[town]] and [[municipality]] in the [[Santiago Province (Dominican Republic)|Santiago]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]]. Santiago is part of the northern valley of the country, otherwise known as the [[Cibao]]. Sabana Iglesia is located south of [[Santiago de los Caballeros]], the second-largest city in the [[Dominican Republic]], and sits just north of the country's second-tallest dam, the '''Bao Dam,''' surrounded by the mountains of the [[Cordillera Central, Dominican Republic|Cordillera Central]].

== History ==
Sabana Iglesia's founding date is unclear, however, for long it was a rural section of the municipality of [[Santiago de los Caballeros|Santiago]] that was eventually converted into a municipal district in November 1987. In February 2007 this municipal district was elevated to a municipality that includes the rural sections of Sabana Iglesia, Los Ranchos de Babosico and La Zanja.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Historia : Ayuntamiento Municipal de Sabana Iglesia |url=https://ayuntamientosabanaiglesia.gob.do/historia/#:~:text=Sabana%20Iglesia%20era%20una%20secci%C3%B3n,Los%20Ranchos%20y%20La%20Zanja. |access-date=2022-07-07 |website=ayuntamientosabanaiglesia.gob.do}}</ref> Much of the town's earliest development is attributed to '''Generosa Ferreira''', who established Sabana Iglesia's first church, school, library and clinic which are still in operation today.<ref>{{Citation |title=Generosa de Jesus Ferreira, activist of Sabana Iglesias |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xapdjLDEWbg |language=en |access-date=2022-07-10}}</ref>
