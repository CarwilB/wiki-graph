'''Jari''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 3,349 (2022 census) in an area of 856.46&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23KS6 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 441 m. It is located west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
