'''Valle Fértil''' is a department of the province of [[San Juan Province (Argentina)|San Juan]] ([[Argentina]]).<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.canal13sanjuan.com/san-juan/el-peronismo-gano-en-12-de-los-19-departamentos-de-san-juan-_a68fec2cb3c9bd063e329c15f|title=El peronismo ganó en 12 de los 19 departamentos de San Juan|work=Diario 13 San Juan|date=26 October 2025|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> Characterized by highly fertile landscapes and contains the [[Ischigualasto Provincial Park]], also known as the ''Valley of the Moon'', which is visited by tourists from around the world.

== Origin of the name ==
The Spaniards, upon their arrival, found the indigenous people referred to the area as "Chaj Nai--Paj", which means "green or fertile country of many rivers".

== Tourism ==
[[Image:Ischigualasto.jpg|thumb | left | Ischigualasto]]
The main attraction is [[Ischigualasto]], a strange landscape, in what may have been one of the most interesting stages of the earth's history, the Triassic period, the place known as the Valley of the Moon, for the apparent resemblance to a lunar landscape. It is a protected area for the preservation and study of strange geological formations, [[wildlife]], [[flora]] and [[fossil]]s.
