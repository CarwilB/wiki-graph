'''San Marcos''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|222.8|km2}} and had a population of 15,229 inhabitants according to the 2013 national census. The VII Festival of Traditional Games was held in San Marcos on 31 April 2006.

== History ==
In the 1791 census, San Marcos was listed as part of [[Petoa]], and in the 1896, it was already listed as a municipality under the Quimistán district.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de San Marcos, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/San-Marcos.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-san-marcos-santa-barbara/|title=Municipality of San Marcos|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San Marcos is located in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Quimistán]] to the north, Petoa to the east, [[San Luis, Santa Bárbara|San Luis]] and [[Trinidad, Santa Bárbara|Trinidad]] to the south, and [[Macuelizo]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> It is located about {{cvt|56|km}} from the city of [[San Pedro Sula]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|222.2|km2}} and has an average elevation of {{cvt|267|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/>

== Demographics ==
As per the 2013 census, San Marcos had a total population of 15,229 inhabitants, of whom 7,762 (51.0%) were male and 7,467 (49.0%) were female. Of the total population, 5,660 (37.2%) was classified as urban and 9,568 (62.8%) lived in the rural area. The intercensal population growth rate between 2001 and 2013 was 1.2% per year.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}<ref name="CP1">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1621__san_marcos/|title=San Marcos|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=16 May 2026}}</ref>

Of the population, 0.3% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] and the rest were non-indigenous population.<ref name="CP1"/> By broad age group, 36.2% of the population were aged 0–14, 57.7% were aged 15–64, and 6.1% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 21.2 years and the mean age was 26.2 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (9,714 persons), the illiteracy rate was 21.1%, slightly lower than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} The municipality had 3,558 occupied private dwellings, with an average household size of 4.2 persons.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=23,27}}

==Culture==
The VII Festival of Traditional Games was held in San Marcos on 31 April 2006. Athlete Gaby Flores and  journalist Neida Sandoval were honored during the Games.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>
