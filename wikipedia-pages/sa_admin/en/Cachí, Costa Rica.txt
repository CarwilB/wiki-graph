'''Cachí''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Paraíso (canton)|Paraíso]] canton, in the [[Cartago Province|Cartago]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Cachí has an area of {{formatnum:41.24}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:1049}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
It lies near the eastern bank of the man-made [[Lake Cachí]], created by the damming of the [[Reventazon River]] which before the 1970s flowed past the town. The town is connected to [[Ujarrás]], on the other side the lake.

==Landmarks==
The town lies in an area rich with green coffee plantations, with a sugar mill dated to the early twentieth century. Of note is a wooden house built by [[Macedonio Quesada]], the noted Costa Rican wood carver and sculptor who named the house {{lang|es|Casa del Soñador}} (Dreamer's House), made entirely from coffee branches and bamboo.<ref name="GVCR">{{cite web|url=http://www.govisitcostarica.com/region/city.asp?cID=194|title=Cachí|publisher=Go Visit Costa Rica|accessdate=26 June 2011}}</ref> The main restaurant of note is La Casona del Cafetal Restaurant.<ref name="GVCR"/> Cachí' contains an Evangelical Church called Espiritu de Vida. The town is divided into several neighborhoods including Calle Boza, Pueblo Nuevo, Loaisa, Peñas Blancas, Primavera, and Volio. There are three elementary schools and one high school.
