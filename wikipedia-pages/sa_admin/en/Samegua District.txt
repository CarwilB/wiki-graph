'''Samegua District''' is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Mariscal Nieto Province|Mariscal Nieto]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Samegua District was created by Law 9940 (8 November 1894).
