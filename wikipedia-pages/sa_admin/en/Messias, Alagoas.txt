'''Messias''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[miˈsiɐ(s)]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 18,031 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/messias/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|113|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 1% of the {{convert|6116|ha}} [[Murici Ecological Station]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC de Murici (ESEC) – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=ESEC de Murici (ESEC)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591553|accessdate=2016-11-06}}</ref>


The city was founded on September 6, 1962.
