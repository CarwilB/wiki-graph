'''Puerto Morelos''' is one of the eleven [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|municipalities]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Quintana Roo]], on the [[Yucatán Peninsula]]. It was formed in 2016 from the localities of [[Puerto Morelos]], [[Leona Vicario, Quintana Roo|Leona Vicario]] and [[Central Vallarta]] previously belonging to the municipality of [[Benito Juárez Municipality, Quintana Roo|Benito Juárez]]. In the 2010 census, these three localities recorded a total of 15,725 inhabitants.<ref name="INEGI">[http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 2010 census tables: INEGI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130502000000/http://mapserver.inegi.org.mx/mgn2k/?s=geo&c=1223 |date=2013-05-02 }}</ref>

The municipal seat is the town of Puerto Morelos, which is located about {{convert|13|km|mi}} southwest of [[Cancún]] on the [[Caribbean Sea]] and is the easternmost municipal seat in Mexico.

[[Joaquín Zetina Gasca, Quintana Roo|Joaquín Zetina Gasca]] is one of the communities in the municipality and the location of the municipal offices.

Ignacio Sánchez Cordero, who sought the [[2021 Mexican local elections|candidacy]] of [[Morena (political party)|MORENA]] and [[Ecologist Green Party of Mexico|PVEM]] for mayor, was assassinated on February 24, 2021.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Vázquez |first1=Patricia |title=Asesinan a precandidato de Morena y PVEM a edil de Puerto Morelos |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/02/25/estados/asesinan-a-precandidato-de-morena-y-pvem-a-edil-de-puerto-morelos/ |access-date=February 25, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=February 25, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>
