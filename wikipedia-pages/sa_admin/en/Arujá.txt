'''Arujá''' is a city in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. It is part of the [[Metropolitan Region of São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |title=Lei Complementar nº 1.139, de 16 de junho de 2011 |access-date=2015-09-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151001191247/http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |archive-date=2015-10-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The population is 91,157 (2020 est.) in an area of {{Cvt|96.17|km2}}.<ref name=ibge>[https://archive.today/20150907124325/http://cod.ibge.gov.br/234WS Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its boundaries are [[Santa Isabel, São Paulo|Santa Isabel]] in the north and the northeast, [[Mogi das Cruzes]] in the southeast, [[Itaquaquecetuba]] in the south and [[Guarulhos]] to the west and the northwest.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|292000|ha}} [[Mananciais do Rio Paraíba do Sul Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1982 to protect the sources of the [[Paraíba do Sul]] river.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_de_Telecomunicações_do_Estado_de_São_Paulo|Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo]] until 1975, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Companhia_Telefônica_da_Borda_do_Campo|Companhia Telefônica da Borda do Campo]]. In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
