'''Duas Barras''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈduɐz ˈbaʁɐs}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 11,563 in 2021, and its area is {{cvt|379.619|km2}}.<ref name=igbe>{{cite web|title=Duas Barras|publisher=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE)| url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rj/duas-barras/panorama | language=pt | access-date=18 April 2023}}</ref>

The town has a historic centre with colourful painted homes around its main square.
