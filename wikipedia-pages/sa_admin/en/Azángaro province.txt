'''Azángaro''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Puno Region]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. [http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}, Retrieved November 8, 2007</ref>

== Languages ==
According to the [[Peru 2007 Census|2007 census]], [[Quechua language|Quechua]] was spoken by 81.0% of the population as their first language, while 18.4% spoke [[Spanish language|Spanish]], 0.3% spoke [[Aymara language|Aymara]], 0.0% spoke [[Asháninka language|Asháninka]], 0.1% spoke other indigenous languages and 0.0% spoke foreign languages.
