'''Tena''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Tequendama Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The urban centre is located at an altitude of {{convert|1384|m|ft}} at a distance of {{convert|42|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. The municipality borders [[Bojaca]] in the north, [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] in the west, [[San Antonio del Tequendama]] in the east and [[El Colegio]] in the south. The southern border of Tena is formed by the [[Bogotá River]].<ref name=websiteTena>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tena-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Tena] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20140310084931/http://www.tena-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2014-03-10 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
In the [[Chibcha language]], Tena means "Place of rest for the ''zipa''".<ref name=websiteTena/>

== History ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Tena was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The area of Tena formed the western border region with the territories of the [[Panche people|Panche]].

Modern Tena was founded on December 11, 1607 by Borja.<ref name=websiteTena/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Tena are [[agriculture]], [[livestock]] farming and fishing.<ref name=websiteTena/>
