'''Calçado''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[kɐwˈsadu]}}) is a city located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 200&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. Has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 11,093 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Lajedo, Pernambuco|Lajedo]]   ('''N''' and '''E''');  [[Canhotinho]]    ('''S''');  [[Angelim]] and [[Jupi]]    ('''W''')
* Area - 423.08&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 643 m
* Hydrography - [[Mundaú River|Mundaú]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Clima - Hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 22.1 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 200&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Calçado''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[beans]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]].
