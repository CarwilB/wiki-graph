'''Thomonde''' ({{IPA|fr|tɔmɔ̃d}}; {{langx|ht|Tomonn}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Hinche Arrondissement]], in the [[Centre (department)|Centre]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It is located in the Centre or "Plateau Central", arrondissement Hinche. Its sections are {{ill|Cabral, Thomonde|lt=Cabral|es|Cabral (Thomonde)}}, {{ill|Tierra Muscady|es}}, {{ill|Baille Tourrible|es}}, and  {{ill|La Hoye|es}}. The geo-coordinates are 19.02N and 71.97W.

Since 2004 until at least 2012, Organization for the Development of the Peasants of Salmadère (ODPSA) facilitated the growth of [[maize]] and [[millet]] as well as improving soil development. In March 2012, it received $89,596 from [[Japan]].<ref>{{cite news |title=Haiti - Japan : Donation of more than $300,000 for agriculture, education and health |url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-5066-haiti-japan-donation-of-more-than-$300-000-for-agriculture-education-and-health.html |work=[[HaitiLibre]] |date=1 March 2012}}</ref> Like other municipalities of the [[Centre Department]], the town suffered a drought in 2012.<ref>{{cite news |title=Haiti - Agriculture : Food Outlook (March-June 2013) |url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-8344-haiti-agriculture-food-outlook-march-june-2013.html |work=HaitiLibre |date=15 April 2012}}</ref> In 2014, [[Ministry of Economy and Finance (Haiti)|Minister of Economy and Finance]] [[Wilson Laleau]] accompanied by Counsellor for Legal Affairs Wolff Dubique, Abel Metelus, and other members of his cabinet agreed to launch an Integrated Agricultural Development Project centered in Thomonde with Compagnie de Promotion Immobilière et de Ressources Agricoles.<ref>{{cite news |title=Haiti - Agriculture : Project of Agricultural and Ecological city in Thomonde |url=https://www.haitilibre.com/en/news-10735-haiti-agriculture-project-of-agricultural-and-ecological-city-in-thomonde.html |work=HaitiLibre |date=17 March 2014}}</ref>
