'''Jilotzingo''' is one of [[Municipalities of the State of Mexico|125]] [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico (state)|State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The [[municipal seat]] is the town of Santa Ana Jilotzingo, which is the 4th largest town in the municipality.  The municipality covers an area of  143.66&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 13,825.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jilotzingo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15046a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=June 6, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070527203708/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15046a.htm |archive-date=May 27, 2007 }}</ref>

In 2016, the mayor Juan Antonio Mayen Saucedo was murdered<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.excelsior.com.mx/comunidad/2016/04/22/1088212|title=Asesinan a edil de Santa Ana Jilotzingo, Edomex|date=22 April 2016}}</ref> and the body of TV presenter [[Renato López]] was found.
