'''Mariápolis''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in Brazil. The population is 4,091 (2020 est.) in an area of 187&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/33Z Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 410 m.
The town was founded in 1943 by [[Jan Antonín Baťa]] as part of his expansion of the [[Bata Corporation|Bata shoe company]], founded by his brother [[Tomáš Baťa|Tomáš]].<ref>{{cite news|title=Bata's World – Brazil |url=http://world.tomasbata.org/america/brazil/ |website=world.tomasbata.org |access-date=8 September 2023}}</ref>

Other towns in Brazil linked to Baťa include [[Anaurilândia]], [[Batatuba]], [[Bataguassu]], and [[Batayporã]].
