[[File:Bandera_Oxapampa.png | thumb|right | Banner of the District of Oxapampa - Province of Oxapampa - Pasco Region]]
'''Oxapampa District''' is one of eight  [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of [[Oxapampa Province]], one of three provinces in [[Pasco Department]] in [[Peru]]. The town of [[Oxapampa]] is in the district and is the capital of the province. The population of the province was 16,565 in 2017 of which more than 14,000 lived in the town of Oxapampa.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]].
