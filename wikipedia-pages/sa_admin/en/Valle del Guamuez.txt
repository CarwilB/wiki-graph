'''Valle del Guamuez''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Putumayo Department]], in the [[Republic of Colombia]].

==Crime==
The Sinaloa Mafia is a criminal organisation that has an alliance with La Constru, a criminal groups that controls microtrafficking activities, principally of [[cocaine]] in the municipalities of Valle del Guamuez.<ref name="y241">{{cite web | last=Navarrete | first=Maria Alejandra | last2=Alonso | first2=Laura | title=Overview of Violence Against Social Leaders in Colombia | website=InSight Crime | date=2020-02-18 | url=https://insightcrime.org/news/overview-violence-social-leaders-colombia/ | access-date=2025-02-17}}</ref>

==Visitors==
As of 2025, the UK [[Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office]] advises against all but essential travel to Valle del Guamuez.<ref name="p485">{{cite web | title=Colombia travel advice | website=GOV.UK | date=2013-03-21 | url=https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/colombia/regional-risks | access-date=2025-02-17}}</ref>
