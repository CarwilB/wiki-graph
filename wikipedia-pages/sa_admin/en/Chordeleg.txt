'''Chordeleg''' ({{IPA|es|tʃoɾˈðeleɣ}}) is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] and seat of [[Chordeleg Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of {{convert|16.3|km2|abbr=on}} and according to the 2022 Ecuadorian census it had a population of 6,907.<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/ecuador/parish/admin/azuay/011150__chordeleg/ Citypopulation.de] Population and area of Chordeleg parish</ref>

Chordeleg is well known for its jewelry industry, especially gold and silver [[filigree]] jewelry.
