'''Yanahuanca District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Daniel Alcides Carrión Province]] in the mountains of central [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital]''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
