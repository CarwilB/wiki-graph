'''São Valentim do Sul''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It was raised to municipality status in 1992, the area being taken out of the municipality of [[Dois Lajeados]].<ref name="lei9554">{{Cite web|url=http://leisestaduais.com.br/rs/lei-ordinaria-n-9554-1992-rio-grande-do-sul-cria-o-municipio-de-sao-valentim-do-sul|title=Lei Nº 9554: Cria o Município de São Valentim do Sul|publisher=Leis Estaduais|lang=pt|date=20 March 1992}}</ref>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 2,245.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/sao-valentim-do-sul/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
