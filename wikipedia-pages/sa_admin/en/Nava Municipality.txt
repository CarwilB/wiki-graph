'''Nava''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Nava, Mexico|Nava]]. The municipality covers an area of 804.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
