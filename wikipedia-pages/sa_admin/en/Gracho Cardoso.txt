'''Gracho Cardoso''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈɡɾakʊ kɐɦˈdozu}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. It covers {{convert|242.5|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 5,824 with a population density of 24 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i">{{cite web | url = http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8YS | title = Gracho Cardoso | year = 2016 | publisher = Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística | location = Brasília, Brazil | accessdate = 28 October 2016 | language = Portuguese}}</ref>
