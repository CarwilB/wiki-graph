'''Juramento''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. {{as of|2020}} the population was 4,345 in a total area of 432&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/juramento/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1953.

Juramento is located 42&nbsp;km to the southeast of [[Montes Claros]] on BR-308 at an elevation of 682 meters.  It belongs to the statistical microregion of Montes Claros.  Neighboring municipalities are [[Francisco Sá]], [[Montes Claros]], [[Glaucilândia]], and [[Botumirim]].

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and [[subsistence agriculture]].  The GDP in 2005 was R$16,756,000.  Juramento is in the bottom tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  It suffers from isolation, poor soils, and periodic drought.  {{as of|2007}} there were no banking agencies in the town.  There was a small retail commerce serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  In the rural area there were 310 establishments employing about 800 workers.  Only 29 of the farms had tractors.   There were 57 automobiles in all of the municipality.   There were 25,500 head of cattle in 2006.  The crops with a planted area of more than 100 hectares were beans, sugarcane and corn.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.680 (2000)
*State ranking: 635 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 3,292 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*Literacy rate: 79%
*Life expectancy: 67 (average of males and females)
*Urbanization rate: 48.01 (2000)
*Percentage of urban houses served by sewage system: 22.90
*Infant mortality rate: 19.23<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/JURAMENTO.pdf |title=Health indicators |access-date=2008-06-10 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706160823/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/JURAMENTO.pdf |archive-date=2011-07-06 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo state with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.<ref>[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In the health sector there were 5 clinics and one hospital with 18 beds.  In the educational sector there were 4 primary schools and 2 middle schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>
