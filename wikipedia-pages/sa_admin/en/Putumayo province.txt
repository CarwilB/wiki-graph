'''Putumayo''' is one of the eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Loreto Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is the  town of [[San Antonio del Estrecho]].

== History ==
It was created by Law N° 30186, on May 5, 2014, in President [[Ollanta Humala]]'s term.

==Political division==
The province is divided into four districts.
