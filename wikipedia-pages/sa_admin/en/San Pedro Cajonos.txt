'''San Pedro Cajonos''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in southeastern [[Mexico]], being the fourth smallest municipality in Oaxaca, but is still bigger than [[Santa Inés Yatzeche]], [[Santa Cruz Amilpas]], and [[Natividad, Oaxaca|Natividad]]. The municipality covers an area of  km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Villa Alta District]] in the center of the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca|Sierra Norte Region]]. It was founded on September 16, 1700 and given the name of San Pedro Cajonos. The language spoken in the town is a mix of Spanish and a lesser amount of spoken indigenous language called [[Cajonos Zapotec]].<ref>Archived at [https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211210/xdynyR-iSsA Ghostarchive]{{cbignore}} and the [https://web.archive.org/web/20151218114530/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdynyR-iSsA Wayback Machine]{{cbignore}}: {{cite AV media| url = https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xdynyR-iSsA#t=16| title = Words of Life ZAPOTEC, CAJONOS People/Language Movie Trailer | website=[[YouTube]]}}{{cbignore}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news|url=http://globalrecordings.net/en/language/2732|title = Zapotec, Cajonos language}}</ref>

As of March 2020, the municipality had a total population of 1,081.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Pedro Cajonos (Municipality, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://citypopulation.de/en/mexico/admin/oaxaca/20303__san_pedro_cajonos/ |access-date=2024-07-17 |website=citypopulation.de}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20303a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=2023-06-10 |lang=es |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040517171313/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20303a.htm |archive-date=May 17, 2004 |title=San Pedro Cajonos}}</ref>
