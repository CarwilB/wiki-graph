'''Chaglla District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Pachitea Province|Pachitea]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

==Populated centers==
In the district there are 22 towns, one of which is urban and the 21 remaining rural.
