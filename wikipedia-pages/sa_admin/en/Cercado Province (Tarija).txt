'''Cercado''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the central parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] [[Tarija Department|Tarija]].

==Location==
''Cercado'' province is one of six provinces in the Tarija Department. It is located between 21° 15' and 21° 51' [[Latitude|south]] and between 64° 20' and 65° 00' [[Longitude|west]].

The province borders [[Eustaquio Méndez Province]] in the northwest, [[José María Avilés Province]] in the south-west, [[Aniceto Arce Province]] in the south, and [[Burnet O'Connor Province]] in the east.

The province extends over 80&nbsp;km from north to south, and 80&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Population==
The main language used in the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 99.4%, and 0.6% [[Guarani language|Guaraní]].

The population increased from 108,241 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 153,457 (2001 census), an increase of 41.8%. - 39.4% of the population are younger than 15 years old. In the census 2012 the population increased to 205,346 inhabitants, migration mainly driven by gas extraction boom of the region.

26.0% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 39.4% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

12.2% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.1% in [[mining]], 11.5% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 76.2% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

89.6% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 6.3% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
In contrast to the neighbouring provinces, the province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]] '''Tarija Municipality'''. It is identical to the Cercado Province.
