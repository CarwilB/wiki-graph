'''Santo Domingo''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|681.7|km2}}, and as per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 14,460 individuals. It is predominantly known as a mining town, as there are several small gold mines that operate in the region.

==History==
Archaeological remains and indigenous place names suggest that the original inhabitants of the region were indigenous people of Mayan-Caribbean origin who coexisted with other Chontal groups. The modern settlement of Santo Domingo developed during the mining boom in the 1870s. While the earliest miners were indigenous people, the [[Mestizo people|Mestizos]] arrived in 1872. The first settlement was established near the Artigua River, and consisted of huts built from straw and wood. Until 1913, Santo Domingo was part of the municipality of La Libertad. On 17 March 1913, it was granted the category of town, and on 17 October 1951, it was officially granted city status.<ref name="Cul">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/Santo-Domingo-culture/|title=Culture of Santo Domingo|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/Municipality-of-Santo-Domingo/|title=Municipio de Santo Domingo|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Santo Domingo is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|681.7|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/ |title=Administrative Divisions of Nicaragua |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.el19digital.com/articulos/ver/titulo:148476-inauguran-casa-materna-maria-salome-centeno-chavarria-en-chontales|title=Inauguran Casa Materna María Salomé Centeno Chavarría en Chontales|work=[[El 19]]|date=19 January 2024|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es|trans-title=The María Salomé Centeno Chavarría Maternity Home is inaugurated in Chontales}}</ref> The municipality is bordered by the municipalities of [[Santo Tomás, Nicaragua|Santo Tomás]] and [[San Pedro de Lóvago]] to the south, [[El Ayote]] to the east, and [[La Libertad, Comayagua|La Libertad]] to the north and west. It is located {{cvt|190|km}} from the Nicaraguan capital of [[Managua]].<ref name="Hist"/>

The municipality is located in the northeastern part of the department, and extends from the [[Amerrisque Mountains]] range towards the plains of the Caribbean coast. The municipality is historically associated with gold mining, and there are several small mines and dredging sites where small-scale miners extract and process gold. One of the municipality’s notable natural landmarks is Peña Blanca, a large white [[monolith]] located near the town. The Siquia River is a major water source in the municipality, and serves for recreation and fishing.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Geo">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/nature-of-santo-domingo/|title=Nature of Santo Domingo|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 

== Demographics and economy ==
As per 2023 estimate, Santo Domingo has a population of 14,460 individuals of whom 7,179 were male and 7,281 were female. About 44.6% of the population lived in rural areas, and rest was classified as urban.<ref name="CP">{{cite web |title=Santo Domingo (Municipality, Nicaragua) |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/chontales/6520__santo_domingo/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |work=City Population.de}}</ref>

The economy of the municipality is based on [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing. Major agricultural produce include [[maize]], beans, [[sorghum]], and fruits like [[mangoes]] and [[citrus fruits]]. The livestock rearing dates back to centuries, with several old large ranches.<ref name="Hist"/>
