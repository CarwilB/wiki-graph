The '''San Justo Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento San Justo''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-north of the province. Starting from the north and going clockwise, it limits with the departments of [[Vera Department|Vera]], [[San Javier Department, Santa Fe|San Javier]], [[Garay Department|Garay]], [[La Capital Department, Santa Fe|La Capital]], [[Las Colonias Department|Las Colonias]], and [[San Cristóbal Department|San Cristóbal]]. San Justo is thus one of only three provincial departments that do not share a border with another province.

The department has about 40,000 inhabitants, which are distributed in 18 districts. The head town is [[San Justo, Santa Fe|San Justo]] (population 22,000). The others are [[Angeloni, Argentina|Angeloni]], [[Cayastacito]], [[Colonia Dolores]], [[Colonia Esther]], [[Gobernador Crespo]], [[La Camila]], [[La Criolla, Santa Fe|La Criolla]], [[La Penca y Caraguatá]], [[Marcelino Escalada]], [[Naré]], [[Pedro Gómez Cello, Santa Fe|Pedro Gómez Cello]], [[Ramayón]], [[San Bernardo, San Justo|San Bernardo]], [[San Martín Norte]], [[Silva, Argentina|Silva]], [[Vera y Pintado]], and [[Videla, Argentina|Videla]].
