'''São Roque do Canaã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 12,510 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|342|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/sao-roque-do-canaa/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The capital of the municipality is located at an altitude of 120 meters above sea level.
