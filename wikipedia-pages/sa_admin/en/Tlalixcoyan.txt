'''Tlalixcoyan''' (pronounced Tlah-leeks-KOH-yan) is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].<ref>[https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/tlalixcoyan] Tlalixcoyan
MUNICIPALITY OF VERACRUZ DE IGNACIO DE LA LLAVE ''Data Mexico''</ref>
In Tlalixcoyan the main indigenous language is the [[Chinantec]].

==Geography==
Tlalixcoyan is bordered by: [[Alvarado (municipality)|Alvarado]], [[Medellín Municipality, Veracruz|Medellín]], and [[Cotaxtla (municipality)|Cotaxtla]] and is on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Mexican Federal Highways 180]] and [[Mexican Federal Highway|190]].
==Notable people==
[[Lorenzo Barcelata]], composer, was born in Tlalixcoyan, Veracruz, and he died in [[Mexico City]] from [[cholera]].
