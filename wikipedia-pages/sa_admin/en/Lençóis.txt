'''Lençóis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Bahia]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 11,499 (2020 est.) in an area of 1277&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/28L Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The town has a well-preserved colonial atmosphere and is the starting point for treks into [[Chapada Diamantina National Park|Chapada Diamantina]].

==Transportation==
The city is served by [[lençóis Airport|Horácio de Mattos Airport]].

[[File:Cachoeira dos Mosquitos - Chapada Diamantina.jpg|thumb|left|Cachoeira do Mosquito in Lençois - Bahia]]

==Climate==
Lençóis has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with a hot, moderately rainy [[wet season]] and a warm drier season.
