'''Cachoeira do Arari''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 24,064 people. The area of the municipality is 3,102.080&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Arari''. The town of Cachoeira do Arari is located on the [[Arari River]].

Cachoeira do Arari is the location of the Museum of the Marajó, founded in 1972 by the Catholic priest and museologician Giovanni Gallo.<ref>{{cite web |last= |first= |date= |title=Museu do Marajó |url=https://museus.pa.gov.br/museus/163/museu-do-maraj |website= |location= |publisher=Government of Pará |access-date=}}</ref>

The [[Cambu River]] has its source in the municipality.<ref name="lisboa">{{cite book | last = Lisboa | first = P.L.B. | title = A Terra dos Aruã: Uma história ecológica do arquipelágo do Marajó | year = 2012 | publisher = Museu Paraense Emílio Goeldi | location = Belém}}</ref>

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
