'''Morales''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in [[Izabal Department]] of [[Guatemala]].  The municipality was created in 1920<ref>The municipality was created by ''Acuerdo Gubernativo'' 24-06-1920.</ref> and is formed by the town of Morales, 9 villages and 56 rural communities.<ref name="Basterrechea">{{cite web |author=Asesoría Manuel Basterrechea Asociados |url=http://www.crid.or.cr/digitalizacion/pdf/spa/doc13929/doc13929-2a.pdf |title=Plan Municipal para la Reducción de Desastres para el Municipio Morales, Izabal |access-date=2009-07-27 |year=2000 |publisher=UNEPAR/UNICEF |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120222222008/http://www.crid.or.cr/digitalizacion/pdf/spa/doc13929/doc13929-2a.pdf |archive-date=2012-02-22 }}</ref>  The [[Cuevas del Silvino National Park]] is located a few kilometers northeast of Morales.

== Northern Railroad of Guatemala ==

{{Main|Northern Railroad of Guatemala}}

[[File:Tramoselvatico.jpg|200px|thumb|Railways through the jungle in [[Izabal Department]] in 1896.  Photographs by ''[[La Ilustración Guatemalteca]]''.]]

On 22 November 1896 the Northern Railroad [[Zacapa, Zacapa]]-[[Puerto Barrios]] connection, was opened to the public;  it was the most important infrastructure project of general [[José María Reina Barrios]] given the economic crisis that loomed over Guatemala if he was not able to finish the railroad on time for the [[Exposición Centroamericana|Central American Expo]] scheduled for March 1897.<ref name=ilustracion154/> The new line had a length of {{convert|101|mi|km|order=flip|sp=us}}.<ref name=ilustracion154>{{cite journal|author=La Ilustración Guatemalteca|title=La Línea del Norte|date=15 December 1896|journal=[[La Ilustración Guatemalteca]]|url=https://archive.org/stream/ilustracionguate00guat#page/230/mode/2up|location=Guatemala|publisher=Síguere, Guirola y Cía.|language=es|page=154}}
</ref> In those days, the Northern region of Guatemala was practically a new and unexplored area, a newfound place where natural resources could be exploited and the railroad construction finally gave way to the commercial and industrial exploration of the area.<ref name=ilustracion154/> The complete line [[Puerto Barrios]]-[[Guatemala City]] -through Panajax- had {{convert|197|mi|km|order=flip|sp=us}} and was built in stages (which later became train stops): from [[Puerto Barrios]] to Tenedores -in Morales-, {{convert|18|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}; from Tenedores to Los Amates, {{convert|41|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}; from Los Amates to [[Gualán]], {{convert|21|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}; from [[Zacapa Department|Zacapa]] to [[San Agustín Acasaguastlán|El Rancho de San Agustín]], {{convert|34|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}; from El Rancho to Panajax, {{convert|30|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}; and finally, from Panajax to [[Guatemala city]], {{convert|32|mi|km|order=flip|abbr=on}}.<ref name=ilustracion154/>

== United Fruit Company ==

{{Main|United Fruit Company}}

In early 1920, after the Unionist party deposed [[Manuel Estrada Cabrera]], the [[United Fruit Company]] -which had received considerable concessions from the Guatemalan president in the past two decades in [[Izabal Department|Izabal]]- was suddenly confronted with a large strike and did not have the help from the government to repress it due to the [[Tragic Week (Guatemala)|fighting taking place in Guatemala City]].<ref>{{cite book|author=Prins Wilhelm|author-link=Prince Wilhelm, Duke of Södermanland|pages=148–209|url=https://archive.org/stream/journeycentral00wilhrich|title=Between two continents, notes from a journey in Central America, 1920 |year=1922|publisher=E. Nash and Grayson, Ltd.|location=London, UK}}</ref>  New labor chapters opened both in Morales and [[Los Amates]] banana plantations, while [[Puerto Barrios]] had more than 250 members.  The strike grew stronger and upon the resistance of the Unionists to help it, UFCO supported a coup d'état led by general [[José María Orellana]] in 1921, who swiftly repressed the union members and gave tranquility to the United Fruit Company operations in Izabal.<ref>{{cite book|author=Colby, Jason M.|title=The business of empire: United Fruit, race and U.S. expansion in Central America|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=cIrLdVsnyTkC&pg=PA143|page=143|publisher=Cornell University Press|year=2011|isbn=9780801462726}}</ref>

==Franja Transversal del Norte==

{{Main|Franja Transversal del Norte}}

In the 1960s, the importance of the region known as Franja Transversal del Norte was in livestock, exploitation of precious export wood and archaeological wealth.  Timber contracts we granted to multinational companies such as Murphy Pacific Corporation from California, which invested US$30 million for the colonization of southern Petén and Alta Verapaz, and formed the North Impulsadora Company. Colonization of the area was made through a process by which inhospitable areas of the Franja Transversal del Norte (FTN) were granted to native peasants.{{sfn|Solano|2012|p=12}}

In 1964, the National Institute for Agrarian Transformation (INTA) defined the geography of the FTN as the northern part of the departments of Huehuetenango, Quiché, Alta Verapaz and Izabal and that same year priests of the [[Maryknoll]] order and the Order of the Sacred Heart began the first process of colonization, along with INTA, carrying settlers from Huehuetenango to the [[Ixcán]] sector in Quiché.{{sfn|Solano|2012|p=13}}

The Northern Transversal Strip was officially created during the government of General Carlos Arana Osorio in 1970, by Decree 60-70 in the Congress, for agricultural development.<ref name=wikiguate>{{cite journal|url=http://wikiguate.com.gt/wiki/Franja_Transversal_del_Norte|journal=Wikiguate|title=Franja Transversal del Norte|access-date=30 October 2014|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140414054657/http://wikiguate.com.gt/wiki/Franja_Transversal_del_Norte|archive-date=14 April 2014}}</ref>  The area included within the municipalities: San Ana Huista, San Antonio Huista, Nentón, Jacaltenango, [[San Mateo Ixtatán]], and Santa Cruz Barillas in [[Huehuetenango (department)|Huehuetenango]]; [[Chajul]] and [[Uspantán|San Miguel Uspantán]] in Quiché; [[Cobán]], [[Chisec]], [[San Pedro Carchá]], Lanquín, [[Senahú]], [[Cahabón]] and [[Chahal, Guatemala|Chahal]], in Alta Verapaz and the entire department of [[Izabal Department|Izabal]].{{sfn|Solano|2012|p=15}}

==Sports==
The town boasted a [[Liga Nacional de Fútbol de Guatemala|Guatemalan top division]] football side in [[Heredia Jaguares de Peten|Deportivo Heredia]] before it was relocated to [[San José, El Petén]] Department in summer 2008 due to a lack of support.

==Climate==
Morales has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Af'') and its municipal capital is 48 m above sea level.<ref name="Climate-Data.org"/>
