'''Humuya''' ({{IPA|es|uˈmuʝa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua (department)|Comayagua]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|54.19|km2}} and had a population of 1,319 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
The settlement was originally known as the village of Tambla, which was inhabited by indigenous natives. "Tambla" literally means "abundance of corn". It later became part of the municipality of [[San Sebastian, Comayagua|San Sebastian]] in 1896. On 26 January 1897, it was elevated to the rank of municipality, and was given the name "Humuya", which means "place of two waters" in the [[Mayan languages|Mayan language]].<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Humuya, Comayagua|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Comayagua/Humuya.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Humuya|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-humuya/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Humuya is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Comayagua Department|Comayagua]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Cane, La Paz|Cane]] to the south, [[Lamaní]] to the east, and [[San Sebastián, Comayagua|San Sebastian]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|54.19|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/comayagua/0305__humuya/|title=Humuya, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> The Naranjo, El Cacalote, and Los Cerritos mountains surround the municipality, and the aldea of Humuya, the municipal seat, is located in the northeastern part of the municipality.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>

Humuya has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|22.76|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|263.35|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/comayagua/humuya|title=Humuya, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> 

== Administrative divisions ==
The municipality comprises three aldeas (villages) and their associated caseríos (hamlets).<ref name="INE">{{cite report|url=https://temp.ine.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/023-Comayagua-Humuya.pdf|work=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|title=XVII Censo Nacional de Población y VI de Vivienda 2013: Tomo 23, Municipio de Humuya, Departamento de Comayagua|date=February 2025|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Humuya had a total population of 1,319 inhabitants, of whom 666 (50.5%) were men and 653 (49.5%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 414 individuals (31.4%) were aged 0–14 years, 803 individuals (60.9%) were aged 15–64, and 102 individuals (7.7%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 23.9 years and the mean age was 28.6 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 14.3%, lower than the departmental average of 14.6%. The municipality had 310 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.2 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
