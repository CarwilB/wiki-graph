'''La Libertad''' ({{IPA|es|la liβeɾˈtað}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]]. It is situated at 1720 metres above [[sea level]]. The municipality has a population of 38,234 (2018 census) and covers an area of 231&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
