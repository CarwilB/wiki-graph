'''Río Senguer Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

This provincial subdivision has a population of about 6,194 inhabitants in an area of 22,335 km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Alto Río Senguer]], which is located around 2,021&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
