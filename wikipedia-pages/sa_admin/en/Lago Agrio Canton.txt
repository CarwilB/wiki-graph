'''Lago Agrio Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Sucumbíos Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Nueva Loja]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 66,788.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> The region contains the [[Lago Agrio oil field]] which is at the heart of a high-profile court case against [[Chevron Corporation|Chevron]].
