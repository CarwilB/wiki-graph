'''Lurigancho'''{{efn|name=Lurigancho}} is a [[List of districts of Lima|district of Lima]], [[Peru]]. It is located in the valley of the [[Rímac River]], which it shares with neighboring [[Chaclacayo]] and [[Ate District|Ate]] districts. Its capital is the town of [[Chosica]], located {{cvt|861|m}} above [[sea level]].

== Etymology ==
The district's official name is "Lurigancho," a name with two possible origins. It is either the changed name of a pre-Columbian culture (''Ruricancho''), or taken from ''Hurin Huancho'' ({{lit|Huancho of the Valley}}), a term used to describe the Huancho, an ethnic group that established a ''[[Cacique|cacicazgo]]'' of the same name.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Historia del distrito de San Juan De Lurigancho |url=https://www.gob.pe/40165-municipalidad-distrital-de-san-juan-de-lurigancho-historia-del-distrito-de-san-juan-de-lurigancho |date=2024-01-14 |website=[[Gob.pe]]}}</ref>

In order to distinguish it from [[San Juan de Lurigancho]], a district that separated from Lurigancho District in 1967, it is commonly known as '''Lurigancho-Chosica''', or simply as '''Chosica''', after the [[Chosica|populated centre of the same name]].<ref name=LPDerecho>{{Cite news |title=Proponen que distrito de «Lurigancho» se denomine Chosica |date=2022-05-26 |url=https://lpderecho.pe/proponen-distrito-lurigancho-denomine-chosica/ |work=Escuela de Derecho LP}}</ref> The district's official name has often caused confusion between both districts, leading to the prevalence of its unofficial names.<ref name=LPDerecho/><ref>{{Cite news |title=¿SJL no es el único? La razón por la que 2 distritos de Lima Metropolitana se llaman LURIGANCHO |last=Nomberto |first=Isabel |date=2024-01-08 |url=https://larepublica.pe/datos-lr/respuestas/2024/01/07/por-que-en-lima-hay-dos-distritos-que-se-llaman-lurigancho-esta-es-la-verdadera-razon-san-juan-de-lurigancho-sjl-chosica-evat-498001 |work=[[La República]]}}</ref>

== History ==
The district was created on January 2, 1857,<ref>Alberto Tauro del Pino, ''Enciclopedia Ilustrada del Perú'', vol. X, p. 1534.</ref> with the town of Lurigancho (in the current [[San Juan de Lurigancho]] district, split off in 1967) as its capital. It had a population of 1248 inhabitants, most of them dedicated to agricultural activities, according to the census of 1876.

== Politics ==
Its capital, Chosica, is located in the extreme east of the district, near the border with the [[Huarochirí Province|Province of Huarochirí]].

=== Subdivisions ===
Lurigancho has one [[Populated centres of Peru|minor populated centre]] inside the urban core of Lima, which is [[Santa María de Huachipa]] which is located in the extreme west of the district and adjacent to [[San Juan de Lurigancho]]. Other notable urban areas in this zone are Jicamarca and Cajamarquilla, where one of the principal zinc refineries of the country is located. In the mountain zone in proximity to the refinery is located the [[Jicamarca Radio Observatory]].

== Demographics ==
According to a 2002 estimate by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 125,088 inhabitants and a population density of 529 persons/km². In 1999, there were 32,327 households in the district.
