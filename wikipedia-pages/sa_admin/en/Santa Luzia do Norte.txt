'''Santa Luzia do Norte''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃tɐ luˈzjɐ ˈdu ˈnɔɦtɪ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 7,320 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/santa-luzia-do-norte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 29&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>,<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> which makes it the smallest municipality of Alagoas.
