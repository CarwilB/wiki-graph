'''Magdalena Tequisistlán''' is a town and municipality in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Oaxaca]], located {{convert|470|km|mi|abbr=on}} southeast of [[Mexico City]].
It is part of the [[Tehuantepec District, Oaxaca|Tehuantepec District]] in the west of the [[Istmo de Tehuantepec, Oaxaca|Istmo Region]].
The town was founded in 1410 under the present name. In the [[Tequistlatec language|Chontal language]] it was known as "Maadú".<ref name=INF>{{cite web

Older spelling variations include '''Tequixistlán'''.<ref name="de-Angulo"/>

==History==
The [[Tequistlatec language]], now extinct, used to be spoken in Tequisistlán.<ref name="de-Angulo">de Angulo, Jaime and Lucy S. Freeland. 1925. [http://www.jstor.org/stable/40444929 The Chontal Language (Dialect of Tequixistlan)]. ''Anthropos'' 20: 1032-1052.</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of 599.64&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 190 meters above sea level. The climate is warm sub humid, with summer rains.
===Flora and fauna===
Flora include [[chepil]], [[epazote]], [[purslane]], pine, oak, [[tepehuaje]], [[Ceiba aesculifolia|pochote]], and coconut. Wild fauna include deer, skunk, opossum, coyote, armadillo, and wild boar.<ref name=INF/>

==Demography==

As of 2005, the municipality had 1,524 households with a total population of 6,014 of whom 232 who spoke an indigenous language.<ref name=INF/>
Some speak the highland version of [[Oaxacan Chontal]].<ref name=goaxchontal>{{cite web
 |url=http://www.go-oaxaca.com/overview/chontales.html
 |title=Chontales
 |work=Go Oaxaco
 |accessdate=2010-07-21}} {{Dead link|date=October 2010|bot=H3llBot}}</ref>
The people grow corn, coffee and vegetables for personal consumption.
About 30% of the population is engaged in raising cattle. 
Hunting and fishing are practiced for personal consumption.
There are large deposits of marble that are exploited by local cooperatives, employing 40% of the economically active population. Mezcal is made also to a lesser extent.<ref name=INF/>
