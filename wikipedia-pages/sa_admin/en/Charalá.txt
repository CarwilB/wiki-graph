'''Charalá''' is a town and municipality in the south of the [[departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Santander Department|Santander]] in northeastern [[Colombia]]. Its [[antipodes|antipode]] is located within the capital of [[Indonesia]], [[Jakarta]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.antipodemap.com/|title=Antipodes Map - Antipodal location for any map point|date=2006|publisher=Solution Realm Software Inc|accessdate=3 November 2012|url-status=usurped|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20121106090110/http://www.antipodemap.com/|archivedate=6 November 2012}}</ref>

The municipality borders the municipalities [[Encino, Santander|Encino]] and [[Coromoro]] in the east, [[Oiba]], [[Confines]] and [[Suaita]] in the west, [[Páramo, Santander|Páramo]], [[Ocamonte]] and [[Mogotes]] in the north and in the south [[Gámbita]] and [[Duitama]], the latter in the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]].<ref name=websiteCharala>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150605153006/http://www.charala-santander.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Charalá]</ref>

== Climate ==
Charalá has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Af'') with consistent temperatures, cool nights, and abundant rainfall.

== Etymology ==
The name Charalá is [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]], the language of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] and was given to honour the Guane ''[[cacique]]'' of the village; "Chalala".<ref name=websiteCharala/>

== History ==
Before the arrival of the Spanish in the area, the Santander department was inhabited by the [[Guane people|Guane]]. Charalá was located at the border of Guane territory and the [[Muisca Confederation]] of which it formed an independent unity.

Modern Charalá was founded by one of the [[List of conquistadors in Colombia|conquistadors]] who was part in the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]]; [[Martín Galeano]], on July 23, 1540.<ref name=websiteCharala/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in the municipality are agriculture ([[coffee]], [[sugar cane]] and [[maize]]) and [[marble]] mining.<ref name=websiteCharala/>

== Born in Charalá ==
* [[José Acevedo y Gómez]], independence hero of Colombia
* [[José Antonio Galán]], was a Neogranadine historical. He was the leader of the Comuneros insurrection in 1781.

== Trivia ==
