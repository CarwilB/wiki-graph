'''Quijos Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]] located in the [[Napo Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Baeza, Ecuador|Baeza]]. It includes the village of [[Chalpi]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 5,505.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
