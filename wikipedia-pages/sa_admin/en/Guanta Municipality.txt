The '''Guanta Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 30,891.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=8 November 2015 |archive-date=15 March 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Guanta]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Guanta Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
Guanta dates from the completion of the railway to the [[coal]] mines of [[Naricual]] and [[Capiricual]] nearly {{convert|19|km|mi}} beyond [[Barcelona, Anzoátegui|Barcelona]], and was created for the shipment of coal.<ref>{{EB1911 |wstitle=Guanta |volume=12 |page=651 |inline=1}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Guanta Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, had a population of 31,629 (up from 28,542 in 2000). This amounted to 2.1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|472.07|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Guanta Municipality is Jhonnathan Marín, elected on 23 November 2008 with 58% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> He replaced Luis Alfredo Cardozo Belizario shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into two parishes; Guanta and Chorrerón (previous to 27 June 1995, the Guanta Municipality contained only a single).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
