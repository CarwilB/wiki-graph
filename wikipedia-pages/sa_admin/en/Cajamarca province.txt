'''Cajamarca''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] of the [[Cajamarca Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of the province is [[Cajamarca]].

== Geography ==
One of the highest elevations of the province is the Llusk'a Qullpa mountain range at approximately {{convert|4000|m|ft|abbr=on}} on the border of the districts of [[Encañada District|Encañada]] and [[Namora District|Namora]].
