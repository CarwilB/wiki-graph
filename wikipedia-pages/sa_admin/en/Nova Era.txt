'''Nova Era''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the microregion of [[Itabira]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 17,551.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/nova-era/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
