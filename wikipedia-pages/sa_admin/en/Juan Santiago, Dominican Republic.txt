'''Juan Santiago''' is a [[Dominican Republic|Dominican]] [[List of municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] in the [[Elías Piña Province|Elías Piña]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]].

==History==
Juan Santiago was elevated to the category of [[Municipality|municipal district]] of [[Comendador, Elías Piña|Comendador]] by the law 916 of 12 August 1978. Then it was elevated to the category of municipality.

==Economy==
The main economic activity of the municipality is [[agriculture]].
