'''Berazategui''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. With an area of {{convert|188|sqkm|abbr=on}} and a population of 320,224 ({{census-ar|2010}}),<ref name=Cen2010 /> it is at the southeast of the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] urban conglomerate, and its capital is [[Berazategui]] city.

It was part of the [[Quilmes Partido]] until 1960.

The majority of the population are concentrated in the city of [[Berazategui]], which has an important commercial district with a pedestrian centre.

Berazategui is also home to [[Asociación Deportiva Berazategui]], a football club that play in the [[Primera C Metropolitana|regionalised 4th division]] of Argentine football.
