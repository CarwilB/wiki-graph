'''Paraguachón''' ({{langx|guc|Paalüwachon}}) is a corregimiento and community located {{convert|8|km|mi|abbr=on}} east of [[Maicao]], the municipality in which it is located. It is located in the [[La Guajira Department]], in northern Colombia. It is a town that is located on the margin of the [[Colombia–Venezuela border|border with Venezuela]] and is where the Transversal del Caribe ends.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.elheraldo.co/region/guajira/en-un-39-aumentaron-viajeros-por-paraguachon-104882 |title=En un 69% aumentaron viajeros por Paraguachón |accessdate= 15 April 2013 |date=27 March 2013 |publisher=El Heraldo |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20160815104305/http://www.elheraldo.co/region/guajira/en-un-39-aumentaron-viajeros-por-paraguachon-104882|archivedate=15 August 2016}}</ref>
