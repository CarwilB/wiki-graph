'''Lábrea''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈlabɾjɐ}}) is the southernmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]].

==Economy==

The  population of the Lábrea municipality was 46,882 as of 2020.
Its area is {{convert|68508.6|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>
This makes it the sixth largest municipality in Amazonas by area and the tenth largest in Brazil.  
The town is the seat of the [[Territorial Prelature of Lábrea]].
[[Lábrea Airport]] is served by scheduled regular air connections.

==Geography==
===Conservation===

The municipality contains the [[Iquiri National Forest]], a {{convert|1472599|ha|adj=on}} sustainable use conservation unit created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional do Iquiri|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=1612|accessdate=2016-06-02}}</ref>
The municipality also contains part of the [[Mapinguari National Park]], a {{convert|1776914|ha}} conservation unit created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA Mapinguari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PARNA Mapinguari|publisher= ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4234|accessdate=2016-06-02}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|776940|ha|adj=on}} [[Ituxi Extractive Reserve]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Ituxi – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Ituxi|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3142|accessdate=2016-08-30}}</ref>
It contains 91% of the {{convert|604209|ha|adj=on}} [[Médio Purus Extractive Reserve]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Médio Purus – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Médio Purus|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/en/uc/4548|accessdate=2016-09-03}}</ref>

===Climate===
Lábrea has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with consistently hot temperatures and very oppressive humidity. Rainfall is heavy for most of the year with the annual average being close to {{convert|2,400|mm|in|-1|disp=or}}; however a short dry season in June and July when monthly rainfall averages less than {{convert|50|mm|in|0|disp=or}} places the climate in the tropical monsoon rather than [[tropical rainforest climate|tropical rainforest]] category.
