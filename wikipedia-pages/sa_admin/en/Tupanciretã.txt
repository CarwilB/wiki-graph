'''Tupanciretã''' is a [[municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 20,005 (2022 census) in an area of 2251.86&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/Y99 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 465 m.  The name comes from the [[Tupi language]].  It is located west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]] and northeast of [[Alegrete]].
