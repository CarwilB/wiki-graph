'''El Cristo''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Aguadulce District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|113|sqkm|sqmi}} and a population of 4,017 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|35.5|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,393; its population as of 2000 was 3,852.<ref name=inec/>

El Cristo is one of the oldest towns in the region. In pre-Columbian times it was inhabited by an aboriginal community for a period of several centuries. According to studies by the archaeologist Cook, this community is similar to the pre-Columbian site of the Sierra at the edge of [[Santa María District, Panama|Santa Maria]]. The history of El Cristo goes back to the founding of [[Nata, Coclé|Nata]], by which time El Cristo was a cattle farm of the Spanish. The town is home to a Christmas tree, renowned for being the highest in Central America.{{Citation needed|date=March 2009}}
