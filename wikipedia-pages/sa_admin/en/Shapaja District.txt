'''Shapaja District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[San Martín Province|San Martín]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The name comes from [[Attalea phalerata|Shapaja palm tree]] that is local to the region. The district is known for its beaches and fisheries which employ many of the locals.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.peru.travel/en/attractions/district-of-shapaja |access-date=2025-10-05 |website=www.peru.travel}}</ref>
