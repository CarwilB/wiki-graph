'''Eldorado do Sul''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in the [[metropolitan area]] of [[Porto Alegre]], the state's capital and largest city, opposite the capital on the right bank of [[Guaíba River]]. Population: 41,902 (est. 2020).

Eldorado do Sul was hit badly by the [[2024 Rio Grande do Sul floods]], being completely engulfed. The mayor said that "the city has been 100% destroyed by these floods."<ref>{{Cite AV media |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OHYtXKr2PME |title=Cidade está 100% destruída", diz prefeito de Eldorado do Sul |date=5 May 2024 |language=en |access-date=9 May 2024 |via=[[YouTube]] |archive-date=8 May 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240508011148/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OHYtXKr2PME&feature=youtu.be |url-status=live }}</ref>
