'''Oviedo''' is a city and municipality in the [[Pedernales Province|Pedernales]] [[Provinces of the Dominican Republic|province]] of the [[Dominican Republic]]. It is the southernmost city of the Dominican Republic and of the [[Hispaniola]] island.
