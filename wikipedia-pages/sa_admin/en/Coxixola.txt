'''Coxixola''' is the smallest municipality located in [[Paraíba]] state, [[Brazil]]. The population is 1,935 people (2020). The total area is 113.6&nbsp;km². Coxixola was a district firstly of [[São João do Cariri]]-[[Paraíba|PB]] and after of [[Serra Branca]]-PB until 1994 when it became a municipality itself.

The word ''coxixola'' means to build a small house using clay bricks in [[Old Tupi language|the Tupi-Guarany]] Indian language.
