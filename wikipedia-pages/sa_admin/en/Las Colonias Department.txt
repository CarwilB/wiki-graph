The '''Las Colonias Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Las Colonias''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], Argentina. It is located in the center of the province. Starting from the east and going clockwise, it limits with the departments of [[La Capital Department, Santa Fe|La Capital]], [[San Jerónimo Department|San Jerónimo]], [[San Martín Department, Santa Fe|San Martín]], [[Castellanos Department|Castellanos]], [[San Cristóbal Department|San Cristóbal]], and [[San Justo Department, Santa Fe|San Justo]]. Las Colonias is thus one of only three provincial departments that do not share a border with another province.

The department has about 95,000 inhabitants, which are distributed in 37 districts (mostly small municipalities and communes). The head town is [[Esperanza, Santa Fe|Esperanza]] (population 36,000). Other cities and towns are [[Colonia Cavour]], [[Colonia San José, Santa Fe|Colonia San José]], [[Cululú]], [[Elisa, Santa Fe|Elisa]], [[Empalme San Carlos]], [[Felicia, Argentina|Felicia]], [[Franck, Argentina|Franck]], [[Grutly]], [[Hipatia, Argentina|Hipatia]], [[Humboldt, Argentina|Humboldt]], [[Ituzaingó, Santa Fe|Ituzaingó]], [[Jacinto L. Arauz]], [[La Pelada]], [[Las Tunas, Santa Fe|Las Tunas]], [[María Luisa, Santa Fe|María Luisa]], [[Matilde, Argentina|Matilde]], [[Nuevo Torino]], [[Pilar, Santa Fe|Pilar]], [[Progreso, Argentina|Progreso]], [[Providencia, Argentina|Providencia]], [[Pujato Norte]], [[Rivadavia, Santa Fe|Rivadavia]], [[Sa Pereira]], [[San Agustín, Santa Fe|San Agustín]], [[San Carlos Centro]], [[San Carlos Norte]], [[San Carlos Sud]], [[San Jerónimo del Sauce]], [[San Jerónimo Norte]], [[San Mariano, Argentina|San Mariano]], [[Santa Clara de Buena Vista]], [[Santa María Centro]], [[Santa María Norte]], [[Santo Domingo, Santa Fe|Santo Domingo]], [[Sarmiento, Santa Fe|Sarmiento]], and [[Soutomayor]].

The name of this department (which means "The Colonies") derives from the fact that it was settled mostly by [[immigration in Argentina|immigrant]] families under the system of [[agricultural colonies in Argentina|agricultural colonies]], of which Esperanza was the first formally organized in Argentina.
