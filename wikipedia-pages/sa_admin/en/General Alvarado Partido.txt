'''General Alvarado Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic Coast]] of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 34,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1599|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Miramar, Buenos Aires|Miramar]], which is {{convert|450|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

== Economy ==

Like many of the partidos on the Atlantic coast of Buenos Aires Province, the economy of General Alvarado Partido is dominated by seasonal tourism.

The vast majority of the tourists come from [[Gran Buenos Aires]] in the summer holiday season (December–February).
