'''Douradina''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 5,975 (2020) and its area is 281&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, which makes it the smallest municipality in the state.
