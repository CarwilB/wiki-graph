'''Matias Cardoso''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]].

==Geography==
Matias Cardoso is located on the right bank of the [[São Francisco River]]. It is 10 km. south of [[Manga, Minas Gerais|Manga]] and is 72 km. northwest of [[Jaíba]]. 

As of 2020 the population was 11,260 in an area of 1,938 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/matias-cardoso/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  
*The elevation of the municipal seat is 472 meters.  
*It became a municipality in 1993  
*The postal code (CEP) is 39478-000
*Statistical microregion:  [[Januária]]
 
The municipality contains the {{convert|6358|ha}} [[Jaíba Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|title=Sistema de Áreas Protegidas - SAP |language=pt |year=2005 |author=Josefina Ivonete Fagundes |publisher=IEF - Instituto Estadual de Florestal - MG |url=http://servicos.meioambiente.mg.gov.br/regionais/reg08.asp |access-date=2016-04-27 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170216050339/http://servicos.meioambiente.mg.gov.br/regionais/reg08.asp |archive-date=2017-02-16 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The economy is based on agriculture with emphasis on cattle raising.  There were 46,000 head in 2006.  The main agricultural crops were cotton, peanuts, corn, manioc, sorghum, sugarcane, and [[Ricinus|mamona]]—castor oil plant. The GDP was R$37,034,000 in 2005.

==Health==

This municipality is extremely isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.  It is one of the poorest in the state and in the country.

== Festivity ==
Since 2011 in the municipality it is celebrated annually on December 8 (day of the [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] [[Calendar of saints|liturgical feast]] [[Immaculate Conception|Immaculate Conception of Our Lady]]) the '''Day of the ''Gerais'''''. Matias Cardoso was founded by the [[bandeirantes]] of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] Januário Almeida and his son, Matias Cardoso de Almeida. The date was the initiative of state representative Paulo Guedes (not the [[Paulo Guedes|minister]]). The date was chosen because it is the day of the [[patron saint]] of the municipal [[parish church]], built in 1664.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Minas |first=Estado de |date=2014-07-12 |title=História de Minas Gerais é celebrada em duas cidades que tiveram os primeiros povoados |url=https://www.em.com.br/app/noticia/gerais/2014/07/12/interna_gerais,547565/historia-de-minas-gerais-e-celebrada-em-duas-cidades-que-tiveram-os-primeiros-povoados.shtml |access-date=2023-05-15 |website=Estado de Minas |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

Inhabitants of the region are called ''[[Geraizeiro|geraizeiros]]''.
