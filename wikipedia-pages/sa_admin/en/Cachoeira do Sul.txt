'''Cachoeira do Sul''' ({{IPA|pt|kaʃuˈe(j)ɾɐ  du ˈsuw}}) is a [[municipality]] in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], southernmost [[Brazil]].

Its Marian Catedral da Nossa Senhora da Conceição <ref>http://www.gcatholic.org/churches/brazil/2666.htm GCatholic</ref> is the episcopal see of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Cachoeira do Sul]].

== Geography ==
It is located at a [[latitude]] of 30º02'21" S and a [[longitude]] of 52º53'38" W, at an approximate elevation of 68 meters above sea level. Its population in 2020 was approximately 81,869 and its area is 3715.5 square kilometers.

=== Climate ===
Cachoeira do Sul has a [[humid subtropical]] [[climate]] and its annual average temperature is {{convert|18.9|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. In January, the warmest month, highs frequently surpass {{convert|30|°C|°F|abbr=on}} with the average low dropping to {{convert|19|°C|°F|abbr=on}}. In June, the coldest month, highs reach {{convert|19|°C|°F|abbr=on}} and lows usually go below {{convert|9|°C|°F|abbr=on}}, reaching up to {{convert|−5|°C}}, but snow is a rare occurrence. Rainfall is distributed evenly throughout the year with a monthly average of {{convert|140|mm|in}}.

== Sports ==
There are two soccer teams in Cachoeira do Sul, Grêmio Esportivo São José and Cachoeira Futebol Clube they both play at the Joaquim Vidal Stadium.
