'''Coronel Pringles''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] of the [[Buenos Aires Province|Province of Buenos Aires]] in [[Argentina]]. In the southern part of the province, it was founded on 10 July 1882 by the provincial government when they divided the territory of Tres Arroyos into the partidos of [[Coronel Suárez Partido|Coronel Suárez]], [[Tres Arroyos Partido|Tres Arroyos]], and Coronel Pringles.

The partido has a population of 23,794 (Census INDEC 2001) in an area of {{convert|5257|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Coronel Pringles]], which is {{convert|597|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
