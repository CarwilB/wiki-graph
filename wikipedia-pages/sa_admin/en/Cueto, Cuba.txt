'''Cueto''' is a [[municipality]] and [[town]] in [[Holguín Province]], [[Cuba]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located southwest of the Holguín Province, next to the border with [[Santiago de Cuba Province]]. It borders with the municipalities of [[Báguanos]], [[Mayarí]], [[Mella, Cuba|Mella]] and [[Urbano Noris]] (San Germán). In addition to Cueto itself, the municipal territory includes the villages of Alto Cedro, Barajagua, [[Birán]],<ref name="OSM">{{OSM|r|5904273|Cueto}}</ref><ref>{{OSM|n|1336109283|Birán}}</ref> Marcané, and other minor localities.<ref name="OSM" />

Birán, best known as the birthplace of [[Fidel Castro|Fidel]], [[Raúl Castro|Raúl]] and [[Ramón Castro Ruz|Ramón Castro]], was part of the neighboring municipality of Mayarí until the 1976 reform.<ref>[http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/omayari/index.htm Mayarí on guije.com]</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2018, the municipality of Cueto had a population of 31,552, of which 16,308 were men and 15,244 were women<ref name="atenas">{{Cite web|title=Anuario Estadístico Cueto 2018|url=http://www.onei.gob.cu/sites/default/files/anuario_est_municipal/10_cueto_.pdf|author=|first=|year=2019|website=|language=es|archiveurl=|archivedate=|accessdate=}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|329|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref>{{Cite web|title=Cuba Municipalities|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|last=Statoids|date=|website=www.statoids.com|url-status=live|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20040225085955/http://www.statoids.com:80/ycu.html |archive-date=2004-02-25 |access-date=2020-05-15}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|95.9|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. The town of Cueto, as of the 2017 census, had a registered population of 15,111 living in just over 11,000 homes.

==Popular culture==
The name of the town, along with its municipal villages of '''Alto Cedro''' and '''Marcané''', figures in the following lyrics of the song ''[[Chan Chan (song)|Chan Chan]]'', composed in 1984 and released in 1996 by [[Compay Segundo]], and also performed by the [[Buena Vista Social Club (album)|Buena Vista Social Club]]:
{|
| style="padding-right:3em;" |''De Alto Cedro voy para Marcané''

''Llego a Cueto voy para Mayarí''
|
|From Alto Cedro I go to Marcané

I get to Cueto, then head for Mayarí
|}
The same line is repeated in a chorus of the 2011 song "''Buena''" by a Polish band [[Blue Cafe]].
