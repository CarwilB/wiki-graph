'''Jayuya''' ({{IPA|es|xaˈʝuʝa}}) is a [[Jayuya barrio-pueblo|town]] and [[Municipalities of Puerto Rico|municipality]] of [[Puerto Rico]] located in the [[Cordillera Central (Puerto Rico)|mountainous center region]] of the island, north of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico|Ponce]]; east of [[Utuado, Puerto Rico|Utuado]]; and west of [[Ciales, Puerto Rico|Ciales]]. Jayuya is spread over 10 [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrios]] and [[Jayuya barrio-pueblo|Jayuya Pueblo]]<ref>[http://boricuaonline.com/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=70&Itemid=1 Jayuya... El Pueblo de Los Tres Picachos] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120925120005/http://boricuaonline.com/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=70&Itemid=1 |date=September 25, 2012 }} – Retrieved September 24, 2012</ref><ref>[https://jayuya-puertorico.blogspot.com/2009/11/localizacion.html Mi pueblo del tomate, Jayuya] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130727180431/http://jayuya-puertorico.blogspot.com/2009/11/localizacion.html |date=July 27, 2013 }} – Retrieved September 24, 2012</ref> (the downtown and administrative center of the city). It is the principal city of the Jayuya [[Micropolitan Statistical Area]].

Named after the [[Taíno]] tribal leader, [[Hayuya]], Jayuya is known for its skilled [[Wood carving|wood carvers]]. Among its monuments are the statue of [[Nemesio Canales]] and the bust of Hayuya, both by the Puerto Rican sculptor [[Tomás Batista]].

==History==
The town of Jayuya was founded in 1911, but history traces non-indigenous settlers living in the region as far back as 1878. At that time, a small community was established there, separated from the larger cities of the coasts with little to no communication. Near the end of the 19th century, the town focused on the production of coffee, which greatly boosted the local economy.

In 1911, the town was officially declared a municipality with a population of more than 9,000.

In 1950, the town was the setting of the [[Jayuya Uprising]], in which [[Nationalist Party of Puerto Rico|Nationalists]] started a [[revolt]] against the Government of the United States led by [[Blanca Canales]], a social worker, and her cousins, Elio and Doris Torresola. Canales led the group into the main town square and gave a speech, declaring Puerto Rico an [[Independence movement in Puerto Rico|independent republic]]. The police station was later attacked, telephone lines cut, and the post office burned to the ground. One police officer was killed and three others wounded in these attacks. The group held the town for three days, until it was bombed by U.S. planes supporting a ground attack by the [[Puerto Rico National Guard]]. Even though an extensive part of the town was destroyed, the news of the American bombing was not reported outside of Puerto Rico. Instead, it was reported by the American media stateside as an incident between Puerto Ricans.

==Geography==
Jayuya is located in the center of the island of Puerto Rico, in a valley in the middle of the [[Cordillera Central (Puerto Rico)|Cordillera Central]]. Due to this location, its terrain is mostly rough and steep. Some of the highest mountains of the island are located in this region, including [[Monte Jayuya]], [[Cerro Rosa]], [[Tres Picachos]] and the highest mountain of the island, [[Cerro de Punta|Cerro Punta]], at {{convert|4389|ft|abbr=on}}.<ref name="PR_Ency">{{cite web|url=https://enciclopediapr.org/en/encyclopedia/jayuya-municipality/|title=Jayuya Municipality|publisher=Fundación Puertorriqueña de las Humanidades (FPH)|website=enciclopediapr.org|access-date=2019-03-20|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190823194037/https://enciclopediapr.org/en/encyclopedia/jayuya-municipality/|archive-date=2019-08-23|url-status=live}}</ref>

The town's total area is estimated at {{convert|39.4|mi2|km2}}{{Citation needed|date=July 2009}}. Part of the [[Toro Negro State Forest]] is located within the municipality of Jayuya. Rivers like [[Río Naranjito]] and [[Río Yunes]], as well as the [[Río Grande de Jayuya]] and its various affluents, flow through this municipality.

===Climate===
As in most municipalities located in the mountain region, the climate is mostly cool with temperatures in the {{convert|80|to|70|F|C}} range. However, they are known to go as low as {{convert|50|or|60|F|C}} during some winters.

==Festivals and events==
Jayuya celebrates its [[Fiestas patronales in Puerto Rico|patron saint]] festival in September. The {{lang|es|Fiestas Patronales Nuestra Señora de la Monserrate}} is a religious and cultural celebration that generally features parades, games, artisans, amusement rides, regional food, and live entertainment.<ref name="PR_Ency" /><ref>{{cite web | title=Puerto Rico Festivales, Eventos y Actividades en Puerto Rico | website=Puerto Rico Hoteles y Paradores | url=https://www.puertoricohotelesparadores.com/festivales-eventos-actividades | language=es | access-date=2020-07-17 | archive-date=2020-02-26 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200226163727/https://www.puertoricohotelesparadores.com/festivales-eventos-actividades | url-status=live }}</ref> The festival has featured live performances by well-known artists such as [[Victor Manuelle]].<ref name="Fiestas">{{cite web |title=Fiestas Patronales en honor a la Virgen de la Monserrate |url=https://sondeaquiprnet.files.wordpress.com/2013/08/1150919_590697014303021_2118448371_n.jpg |website=sondeaquiprnet |publisher=El Gobierno Municipal de Jayuya |access-date=14 August 2023 |archive-date=July 3, 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220703001113/https://sondeaquiprnet.files.wordpress.com/2013/08/1150919_590697014303021_2118448371_n.jpg |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Tourism==
Due to its location, Jayuya is usually mentioned as the obligatory municipality to visit when visiting the center of the island of [[Puerto Rico]]. There are several landmarks for tourists to consider like the [[Nemesio Canales]] Museum at his [[Casa Nemesio Canales|historic home]] and El Cemí Museum,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.atlasobscura.com/places/cemi-museum|title=Cemi Museum|website=Atlas Obscura|access-date=June 15, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190510141259/https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/cemi-museum|archive-date=May 10, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref> both located in the same area on a small valley to the west of the [[Jayuya barrio-pueblo|downtown district]].<ref>{{Cite magazine|url=https://archive.archaeology.org/1103/trenches/la_piedra_escrita_puerto_rico.html|title=From The Trenches – Off the Grid – La Piedra Escrita|magazine=archive.archaeology.org|access-date=2019-11-05|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191105145619/https://archive.archaeology.org/1103/trenches/la_piedra_escrita_puerto_rico.html|archive-date=2019-11-05|url-status=live}}</ref> Also, the [[Taíno people|Taíno]] tomb in the city's downtown has the remains of a Taíno Indian found in an excavation. Nearby, there's a monument to Chief [[Hayuya]], [[cacique]] of the region.

In the outskirts of the city, tourists can visit the [[Cerro de Punta]], highest peak in the island of Puerto Rico, as well as the other mountains mentioned ([[Tres Picachos]], for example). The [[Toro Negro State Forest]] can also be reached through the city.[[File:CasaMuseoBC.jpg|250px|thumb|Casa Museo Canales|left]]Jayuya is home to the first [[aerostat]] in Puerto Rico and one of the world's largest. It is located in barrio Mameyes, at 3,200 feet above sea level. ==Economy==
===Agriculture===
Jayuya's economy has always depended greatly on agriculture. Products like coffee, tomato, and other vegetables are produced and distributed in the region. Livestock is another part of the city's economy.

===Industry===
In the middle of the 20th century, several manufacturing industries established in Jayuya, aiming to revitalize the economy as well as provide work for the residents. Some of the companies currently in the city are [[Baxter Healthcare]] and [[Abbott Laboratories]]. A bridge used by these companies for the delivery of their materials to their plants was destroyed by [[Hurricane Maria]]. Work was set to begin on the bridge in 2019.<ref name="Femmy_2019">{{cite news |last1=Irizarry Álvarez |first1=Femmy |title=Comenzarán trabajos del puente sobre río Viví en Utuado |url=https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/nota/comenzarantrabajosdelpuentesobreriovivienutuado-1319994/ |access-date=January 7, 2019 |work=Primera Hora |language=es |date=January 6, 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190219225746/https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/nota/comenzarantrabajosdelpuentesobreriovivienutuado-1319994/ |archive-date=February 19, 2019 |url-status=live }}</ref>

==Government==
{{Main|Mayoralty in Puerto Rico}}
Like all municipalities in [[Puerto Rico]], Jayuya is administered by a mayor. The current mayor is [[Jorge González Otero]], from the [[Popular Democratic Party of Puerto Rico|Popular Democratic Party]] (PPD). González was first elected at the [[Puerto Rican general election, 1996|1996 general election]].

The city belongs to the [[Puerto Rico Senatorial district III]], which is represented by two Senators. In [[2024 Puerto Rican general election|2024]], [[Brenda Pérez Soto]] and [[Gabriel González (Puerto Rican politician)|Gabriel González]] were elected as District Senators.<ref>[https://elecciones2024.ceepur.org/Escrutinio_General_121/index.html#es/default/SENADORES_POR_DISTRITO_Arecibo_III.xml]</ref>

==Transportation==
There are 34 bridges in Jayuya.<ref name="bridge">{{cite web|title=Jayuya Bridges|url=http://bridgereports.com/pr/jayuya/|website=National Bridge Inventory Data|publisher=US Dept. of Transportation|access-date=February 20, 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190221224303/http://bridgereports.com/pr/jayuya/|archive-date=February 21, 2019|url-status=live}}</ref>

==Education==
As all other municipalities in the island, Jayuya is part of the Puerto Rico Department of Education. It has several elementary schools distributed throughout the city's barrios. However, there's currently only one public high school, Josefina León Zayas High School, located in the city's downtown. The high school is [[LEED]] certified, for having a green-building design.<ref>{{cite web | title=JOSEFINA LEÓN ZAYAS HS | website=ROSSILUGO | date=December 16, 2021 | url=http://rossilugo.com/portfolio/jayuya/ | access-date=January 18, 2022 | archive-date=January 18, 2022 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220118014427/http://rossilugo.com/portfolio/jayuya/ | url-status=live }}</ref>

The Metropolitan University, a private institution, has a campus in the municipality's downtown area.

==Symbols==
The {{lang|es|municipio}} has an official flag and coat of arms.<ref>{{cite web | title=Ley Núm. 70 de 2006 -Ley para disponer la oficialidad de la bandera y el escudo de los setenta y ocho (78) municipios. | website=LexJuris de Puerto Rico | url=https://www.lexjuris.com/LEXLEX/Leyes2006/lexl2006070.htm | language=es | access-date=2021-06-15 | archive-date=January 20, 2021 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210120152958/http://www.lexjuris.com/LEXLEX/Leyes2006/lexl2006070.htm | url-status=live }}</ref>

===Flag===
The flag is rectangular in shape and derives its symbolism from the coat of arms. It symbolizes the spirit of people proud of their [[Taíno people|Taíno]] heritage and their hospitality.<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020">{{cite web | title=JAYUYA | website=LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico | date=February 19, 2020 | url=http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/jayuya.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200219205049/http://www.lexjuris.com/pueblos/pueblos_files/jayuya.html | archive-date=February 19, 2020 | url-status=live | language=es | access-date=September 17, 2020}}</ref>

===Coat of arms===
Jayuya's coat of arms consists of a shield with a three tower crown above, which is standard for municipalities. Inside the shield, centered at the top, a [[Heraldic crown|crown]] representing Chief [[Hayuya]]'s monarchy. In the center a green band with a white border representing the [[Tres Picachos]] (Three Peaks) mountains. The green color symbolizes the countryside and the town's matron, [[Our Lady of Montserrat|La Virgen de la Monserrate]]. At the bottom of the shield, is a [[petroglyph]] known as the ''Taíno Sun'' found in Jayuya.<ref name="LexJuris (Leyes y Jurisprudencia) de Puerto Rico 2020" />
