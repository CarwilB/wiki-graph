'''Río Cuarto''' is a city in the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]], Argentina. Located in the south of the province, it has about 157,000 inhabitants ({{census-ar|2010}}) and is an important commercial and agricultural hub.

==Overview==
The [[Río Cuarto River]] flows through the province of Córdoba; its central location in the [[Humid Pampas]] favored the city's development as a transport hub for much of the surrounding [[Agriculture in Argentina|agriculture]], and several [[slaughterhouse]]s and [[food processing]] plants opened in Río Cuarto during the twentieth century.

Río Cuarto was founded on November 11, 1786, as ''Villa de la Concepción del Río Cuarto'', by the colonial Governor [[Rafael de Sobremonte]]. Its first rail connection was by way of the former [[Andean Railway]] in 1870, after which the village grew rapidly with the influx of [[Italian Argentine|Italian]] and [[Spanish Argentine|Spanish]] immigrants (mainly as [[tenant farmer]]s). The municipal government charter establishing the modern system of elected mayors and city council was enacted in 1883; the city's first elected mayor was Moysés Irusta. 
{{stack|[[File:Puente Colgante Río Cuarto..JPG|thumb|The Bicentennial Bridge]][[File:Iglesia Estancia El Durazno.JPG|thumb|The chapel at Estancia El Durazno.]]}}
Father Antonio Cardarelli commissioned the construction of the city's principal Roman Catholic church, the Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception, built between 1883 and 1886.
The [[National University of Río Cuarto]], established in 1971, is located here. The city's [[football (soccer)|football]] teams include [[Estudiantes de Rio Cuarto|Estudiantes]] and [[Sportivo y Biblioteca Atenas de Rio Cuarto|Atenas]]. Río Cuarto hosted the International Biology Olympiad in 2006.

The city is served by National Routes 8, 36, and 158, as well as [[Las Higueras Airport]], inaugurated in 1954.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://prensa.cba.gov.ar/informacion-general/rio-cuarto-regresa-la-conectividad-aerea-con-buenos-aires/ |title=Río Cuarto: regresa la conectividad aérea con Buenos Aires |website=Prensa Gobierno de Córdoba |publisher=Gobierno de la Provincia de Córdoba |date= 2025-10-01 |language=es |access-date=2026-01-28}}</ref>

The [[cable-stayed]] Bicentennial Bridge was inaugurated in 2010. Some of the city's principal tourist draws include the [[Autódromo Parque Ciudad de Río Cuarto|Río Cuarto Racetrack]], which hosts [[Turismo Carretera]] touring car racing events, and [[Estancia]] El Durazno, an estate built in 1917 belonging to former Governor Ambrosio Olmos. The [[Rio Cuarto craters|Río Cuarto craters]], a group of geologically unusual [[impact crater]]s, are located nearby. The city's [[UN/LOCODE]] is ARRCU.

==University of Río Cuarto==

The [[National University of Río Cuarto]] (UNRC) was founded on May 1, 1971, through a decree signed by President [[Alejandro Agustín Lanusse]]. This represented an acceleration of the [[Taquini Plan]] from the previous year, which aimed to extend [[higher education in Argentina]] by establishing national universities in more than the largest cities.<ref>{{cite web |title=La creación de la UNRC implicó la movilización de instituciones y personas |url=https://www.unrc.edu.ar/inc/n_completa.php?nota=37336 |website=Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto |date=26 April 2023 |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite journal |last=Rovelli |first=Laura Inés |title=Del plan a la política de creación de nuevas universidades nacionales en Argentina: la expansión institucional de los años 70 revisitada |journal=Question |year=2009 |url=https://www.memoria.fahce.unlp.edu.ar/art_revistas/pr.9832/pr.9832.pdf |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref>

As of 2013, UNRC had a student body of approximately 14,000 and a teaching staff of around 2,000.<ref>{{Cite book |author=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |title=Anuario Estadístico de la República Argentina 2014 |edition=1st |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos (INDEC) |location=Buenos Aires |year=2015 |isbn=978-950-896-468-7 |language=es |url=https://www.indec.gov.ar/ftp/cuadros/sociedad/Anuario_Estadistico_2014.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305033754/https://www.indec.gov.ar/ftp/cuadros/sociedad/Anuario_Estadistico_2014.pdf |archive-date=2016-03-05 |access-date=2026-01-28 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Fast-forwarding to 2021, according to publications by the university itself, the student body consists of over 20,000 students distributed across five faculties: Agronomy and Veterinary Sciences; Economic Sciences; Exact, Physical-Chemical, and Natural Sciences; Humanities; and Engineering.<ref>{{cite web |title=La Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto celebra el 50 aniversario de su creación |url=https://www.unrc.edu.ar/unrc/n_comp.cdc?nota=35633 |website=Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto |date=29 April 2021 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref name="teaching">{{cite web |title=La enseñanza en la UNRC|url=https://www.unrc.edu.ar/unrc/launiversidad/ensenianza.php |website=Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> In an external assessment by the national accreditation agency, there are approximately 55 undergraduate programs and 28 postgraduate programs.<ref>{{cite web |title=Informe de evaluación externa – Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto |url=https://www.unrc.edu.ar/descargar/informe-UNRC-coneau.pdf |website=CONEAU/UNRC |date=17 March 2022 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> The university’s own publications also mention more than forty advanced-level programs—doctorates, master’s degrees, and specializations—coordinated by the faculties and the Graduate Studies office.<ref name="teaching"/>

UNRC is engaged in several areas: research, extension, and community service. According to the university itself, there are more than 500 active research and extension projects on an annual basis, with the support of 1,100 teaching-research staff and over 400 research fellows distributed across 11 research institutes. This includes three UNRC institutes and eight joint institutes with [[CONICET]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Secretaría de Ciencia y Técnica |url=https://www.unrc.edu.ar/unrc/cienciaytecnica/ |website=Universidad Nacional de Río Cuarto |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Historia |url=https://imico.conicet.gov.ar/historia/ |website=IMICO–CONICET |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> 

The city is also home to [[Siglo 21 University]], a private university that opened a branch campus in Río Cuarto in 2001;<ref>{{cite web |title=Resolución CONEAU 375/04 – Universidad Empresarial Siglo 21 |url=https://www.coneau.gob.ar/archivos/resoluciones/375-04.pdf |website=CONEAU |date=2004 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Quiénes somos – Hitos institucionales |url=https://21.edu.ar/la-universidad/quienes-somos |website=Universidad Siglo 21 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> it forms part of a nationwide network of learning centers (over 320 “Centros de Apoyo Universitario” according to the university; reported as 350+ in 2019 press coverage).<ref>{{cite web |title=Presencia territorial: +320 Centros de Apoyo Universitario |url=https://21.edu.ar/la-universidad/infraestructura/presencia-territorial |website=Universidad Siglo 21 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Universidad Siglo 21, una opción disponible en todo el país |url=https://www.lavoz.com.ar/espacio-institucional/universidad-siglo-21-una-opcion-disponible-en-todo-pais/ |work=La Voz |date=28 September 2019 |language=es |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref>

==Climate==

The climate is temperate, typical of the humid Pampas, with four marked seasons. Summers are warm with frequent thunderstorms; however, the heat is often "cut" by periods of southerly winds, so that the average high is a comfortable 29&nbsp;°C, even though it can reach 38&nbsp;°C.

Fall arrives slowly in March, and winter arrives in May with the first frosts. During the very dry winters, temperatures vary according to wind patterns: northwesterly, downsloping winds can bring temperatures of 25&nbsp;°C (but with cool nights) even in midwinter, whereas southerly winds can leave daytime highs around 6&nbsp;°C. Temperatures descend to -5&nbsp;°C sometimes, and snow is uncommon, but not extremely so: in 2007, over 15&nbsp;cm of snow covered the city, and temperatures plummeted to -11&nbsp;°C, reaching highs of only 0&nbsp;°C on one occasion.

Spring brings violent thunderstorms and wide temperature swings, with heat waves followed by frost or cool periods. In some years, the normal winter drought extends into the spring, posing a threat to agriculture; however, at 846&nbsp;mm, Rio Cuarto's climate is generally favourable for crops.

Thunderstorms are very common during the summer, and can last for hours, and even days.
