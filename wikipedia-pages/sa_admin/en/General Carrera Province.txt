'''General Carrera Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de General Carrera}}) is a [[Provinces of Chile|province]] in the [[Aysén Region]] of Chile. Its capital is [[Chile Chico]]. Covering an area of {{cvt|11919.5|km2}}, it had a population of 7,628 inhabitants to the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
The [[Aysén Region]] was established on July 10, 1974, by Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> The region is divided into four provinces&ndash;General Carrera, [[Aysén Province|Aysén]], [[Coyhaique Province|Coyhaique]] and [[Capitán Prat Province|Capitán Prat]], which are further divided into ten [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_de_aysen_del_general_carlos_ibanez_del_campo/|lang=es|title=Region de Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|trans-title=Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
General Carrera Province is one of the four provinces of the [[Aysén Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It is located in the east-central part of the region along the Argentinian border,<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|url=http://www.gobernaciongeneralcarrera.gov.cl/geografia/|title=Ubicación geográfica|trans-title=Geographical location|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> and covers an area of {{cvt|11919.5|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> The majority (96.2%) of the province's land belongs to the [[Baker River (Chile)|Baker River]] basin.<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/6|lang=es|title=Provincia details, General Carrera Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, General Carrera Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> [[Monte San Valentín]] (San Valentin peak), at {{cvt|4070|m}} is the highest peak in the province.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://peakvisor.com/adm/provincia-de-general-carrera.html|title=General Carrera Provine|work=Peak visor|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

[[Chile Chico]], the province capital is, located on the shores of [[General Carrera Lake]] in the [[Chilean Patagonia]].<ref name="Geo"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://ripioturismo.com/travel-guide/chile/aysen-carretera-austral/the-carretera-austral-what-to-see-what-to-do/|title=Carretera Austral|work=Ripio|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> Chile Chico has a warm [[Mediterranean climate]] ([[Koppen classification]]: Csb) with an average annual temperature of {{cvt|8.39|C}}. The region receives approximately {{cvt|3.08|cm}} of rainfall annually on average.<ref name="Geo"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/aysen/chile-chico|title=Chile Chico|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
Genera Carrera is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a provincial governor.<ref name="Gobernadores">{{cite web|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |access-date=18 March 2011 |title=Gobierno de Chile: Gobernadores |work=[[Government of Chile]] |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110707010623/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/w3-article-67516.html |archive-date=7 July 2011 |df=dmy }}</ref> It is further subdivided into two [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas''), [[Río Ibáñez, Chile|Río Ibáñez]] and [[Chile Chico]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MN"/>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 7,628 inhabitants. The population consisted of 3,888 males (51.0%) and 3,740 females (49.0%). Appproximately 16.8% of the population was below the age of 15, 66.0% was between the ages of 15 and 64 years, and 17.2% was aged 65 or older. The province had a rural population of 4,546 inhabitants (59.6%) and an urban population of 3,082 inhabitants (40.4%). Most residents were born in Chile, accounting for 7,271 inhabitants (95.3%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population, with 5,470 inhabitants (71.7%), while 2,156 inhabitants (28.3%) identified as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group, 3,521 adherents (55.7%), followed by 1,725 inhabitants (27.3%) indicating no religious affiliation, and [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]], with 919 adherents (14.5%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/114__general_carrera/|title=General Carrera Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=https://storage.googleapis.com/bktdescargascenso2024/viviendas_censo2024.zip|title=Chilean Census 2024, Results|work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
