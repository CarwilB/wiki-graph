'''Quemado de Güines''' ({{IPA|es|keˈmaðo ðe ˈɣwines}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Villa Clara Province]] of [[Cuba]].
It was founded in 1667.<ref name=guije/>

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the poblados of Caguaguas, Carahatas, Güines, Paso Cavado, Poblado, San Valentín and Zambumbia and Jose R. Riquelme.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vquemado/index.htm|title=Quemado de Güines|author=Guije.com|access-date=2007-10-09|language=es}}</ref>

It borders with the municipalities of [[Corralillo]] in the west, [[Sagua la Grande]] in the east, and [[Santo Domingo, Cuba|Santo Domingo]] in south.

==Demographics==
In 2004, the municipality of Quemado de Güines had a population of 22,590.<ref name=atenas>{{Cite web| url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm| title=2004 Population trends, by Province and Municipality| author= Atenas.cu| year= 2004| access-date=2007-10-09 |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20070927111826/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm <!-- Bot retrieved archive --> |archive-date = 2007-09-27|language=es}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|338|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-09}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|66.8|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. In 2022 the population number had dropped to 20,533.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
