'''Santa Ana Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Manabí Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Santa Ana, Ecuador|Santa Ana]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 45,287.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>

==Demographics==
Ethnic groups as of the [[Ecuadorian census]] of 2010:<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/|title = Resultados}}</ref>
*[[Mestizo]]  50.3%
*[[Montubio]]  42.1%
*[[Afro-Ecuadorian]]  3.9%
*[[White Latin American|White]]  3.6%
*[[Indigenous peoples in Ecuador|Indigenous]]  0.1%
*Other  0.1%
