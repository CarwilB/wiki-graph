'''Belo Monte''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbɛlʊ ˈmõti]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the western of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 6,710 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/belo-monte/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|334|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality was designated a priority area for conservation and sustainable use when the [[Caatinga Ecological Corridor]] was created in 2006.<ref>{{citation|language=pt
 |last=Mattos|first=Marluza|title=Ministério cria Corredor Ecológico da Caatinga|year=2006|publisher=Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://www.mma.gov.br/informma/item/3274-ministerio-cria-corredor-ecologico-da-caatinga|accessdate=2016-11-01}}</ref>
