'''La Uvita''' is a town and municipality in the [[Northern Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is located at an altitude of {{convert|2700|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. La Uvita borders [[San Mateo, Boyacá|San Mateo]] in the north, [[El Cocuy]] and [[Chita, Boyacá|Chita]] in the east, Chita and [[Jericó, Boyacá|Jericó]] in the south and [[Boavita]] in the west.<ref name=websiteLaUvita>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150602084847/http://www.lauvita-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website La Uvita]</ref>

== Etymology ==
La Uvita is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]], meaning "meadow of the fertile farmlands".<ref name=websiteLaUvita/>

== History ==
La Uvita was founded by Vicente Ferrer del Río de Loza on December 24, 1758, as a place for the colonial inhabitants of Boavita to flee the indigenous people in Boavita.<ref name=websiteLaUvita/>

== Economy ==
Main economic activity of La Uvita is the manufacturing of cheese. It also serves as a touristic entry to the [[El Cocuy National Park]].<ref name=websiteLaUvita/>
