'''Villeta''' is a municipality and town in [[Cundinamarca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is located in [[Gualivá Province]], approximately {{convert|84|km|mi}} northwest of [[Bogotá]], considered the capital of the province. Its name means 'little village'. The municipality borders [[Quebradanegra]] and [[Nimaima]] in the north, [[Nocaima]] and [[Sasaima]] in the east, [[Albán, Cundinamarca|Albán]] and [[Vianí]] in the south and [[Guaduas]] in the west. It is situated at an altitude of {{convert|850|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]<ref name=WebsiteVilleta>[http://www.villeta-cundinamarca.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Informacion-del-Municipio.aspx Official website Villeta]</ref>

== History ==
The region of Villeta before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Panche people|Panche]]. Modern Villeta was founded as Villa de San Miguel on September 29, 1551, by Alonso de Olalla and Hernando de Alcocer.<ref name=HistoryVilleta>[http://www.villeta-cundinamarca.gov.co/MiMunicipio/Paginas/Pasado-Presente-y-Futuro.aspx History Villeta]</ref>

Main economic activities in Villeta are related with sugar cane derivatives and as an important tourist center for people from Bogotá due to its warm climate, including ecologic trails, cascades and hotels.

== Geology ==
The [[Villeta Group]] comprising the [[Conejo Formation|Conejo]], [[La Frontera Formation|La Frontera]], [[Simijaca Formation|Simijaca]], [[Hiló Formation|Hiló]], [[Capotes Formation|Capotes]], [[Socotá Formation|Socotá]],  [[El Peñón Formation|El Peñón]] and [[Trincheras Formation]]s is named after Villeta.<ref name=Acosta2001_p39>Acosta & Ulloa, 2001, p.39</ref>
