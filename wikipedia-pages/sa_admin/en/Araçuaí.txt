'''Araçuaí''' ({{IPA|pt|aɾɐswa'i}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in the [[Jequitinhonha River]] valley. The [[Araçuaí River]], a tributary of the Jequitinhonha, flows through it. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was estimated to be 36,712 people living in a total area of 2,235&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion of Jequitinhonha and the microregion of Araçuaí. The city is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Araçuaí]]. The elevation of the municipal seat is 307 meters. It became a municipality in 1870.

Between the municipalities of Araçuaí and [[Itinga]] is located the largest [[lithium]] mine in Brazil,<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Lítio pode impulsionar o desenvolvimento do Vale do Jequitinhonha - Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |url=https://www.almg.gov.br/comunicacao/noticias/arquivos/Litio-pode-impulsionar-o-desenvolvimento-do-Vale-do-Jequitinhonha/#:~:text=Polo%20da%20extra%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20de%20l%C3%ADtio,para%20reverter%20essa%20contradi%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20hist%C3%B3rica. |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Portal da Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-19 |title=A corrida pela riqueza do lítio na pequena Araçuaí - revista piauí |url=https://piaui.folha.uol.com.br/a-corrida-pela-riqueza-do-litio-na-pequena-aracuai/ |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=revista piauí - _pra quem tem um clique a mais}}</ref> explored and extracted by Sigma Lithium Resources.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Sigma Lithium Resources |url=https://sigmalithiumresources.com/ |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Sigma Lithium |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The origin of the name "Araçuaí" has two versions: according to [[Augustin Saint-Hilaire|Auguste de Saint-Hilaire]], the name was given by the ''[[paulistas]]'' to the river because they had found a large quantity of gold in it. They exclaimed: "''ouro só ali''" ("only gold there"), and from this phrase Araçuaí was created. According to philologist [[Eduardo de Almeida Navarro]], Araçuaí comes from the [[Tupi language]], through the composition of the words ''araso'iá'' ([[:pt:Araçoia|araçoia]]) and ''<nowiki/>'y'' (river), therefore meaning "river of the araçoias".<ref>{{cite book|author-link=Eduardo de Almeida Navarro |date=2013 |edition=1.ª |first=Eduardo de Almeida |isbn=9788526019331 |last=Navarro |location=São Paulo |publisher=[[Global Editora|Global]] |title=[[Dicionário de Tupi Antigo|Dicionário de tupi antigo: a língua indígena clássica do Brasil]]}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

Until 1857, the place was called Calhau, due to the large quantity of [[Pebble|pebbles]] (''calhais'') in the region.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Portal IFNMG - Historia da cidade de Araçuai |url=https://www.ifnmg.edu.br/programas-ara/47-portal/aracuai/aracuai-institucional/1204-historia-da-cidade-de-aracuai |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=www.ifnmg.edu.br}}</ref>

== History ==
Before the arrival of the Europeans, the current municipality of Araçuaí was inhabited by the Tocoió and [[Aimoré|Botocudo]] indigenous.

The [[priest]] Carlos Pereira de Moura had founded the Aldeia do Pontal, currently [[:pt:Itira|Itira]]. The village was located next to the confluence of two large rivers: the [[Jequitinhonha River|Jequitinhonha]] and the [[Araçuaí River|Araçuaí]]. The village had a set of qualities to evolve into a city, such as easy access to [[Canoe|canoes]], used at the time for navigating rivers, but the priest was excessively demanding and authoritarian, prohibiting alcoholic beverages and prostitutes there. So, the prostitutes emigrated up the Araçuaí River, and the canoeists changed ports, attracted by them.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=História - Prefeitura de Araçuaí |url=https://www.aracuai.mg.gov.br/a-cidade/historia#:~:text=Ara%C3%A7ua%C3%AD%20-%20Nossa%20Hist%C3%B3ria,%20Nossa%20Origem&text=A%20instala%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20sob%20a%20denomina%C3%A7%C3%A3o,dizer%20Rio%20das%20Araras%20Grandes. |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=www.aracuai.mg.gov.br}}</ref>

The prostitutes were sheltered by Luciana Teixeira, owner of the Fazenda da Boa Vista da Barra do Calhau, on her lands on the right bank of the Calhau and Araçuaí streams. This became the landing point for the canoes that went up the Jequitinhonha. Luciana started a village on her land in 1817.<ref name=":0" />

Over time, the place gained importance. It was established under the name Vila de Arassuahy on July 1, 1871, and on September 21 of the same year it was elevated to the category of city, with the name Araçuaí.<ref name=":0" />

Until 1911, the city was the capital of the entire northeast of Minas Gerais. In 1882, the [[Bahia-Minas Railway|Bahia and Minas Railway]] was built, connecting the Bahian city of [[Caravelas]] to Araçuaí, but it was deactivated and closed in 1966.<ref name=":0" /> 

Today, Araçuaí is considered one of the commercial and educational centers of the [[Jequitinhonha River|Jequitinhonha valley]].

== Geography ==
According to the regional division in force since 2017, established by the IBGE, the municipality belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]] and [[Immediate Geographic Region of Araçuaí]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2017">{{cite web|access-date=30 October 2017 |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2017 |title=Base de dados por municípios das Regiões Geográficas Imediatas e Intermediárias do Brasil |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/divisao_regional/divisao_regional_do_brasil/divisao_regional_do_brasil_em_regioes_geograficas_2017/tabelas/regioes_geograficas_composicao_por_municipios_2017_20180911.xls}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Until then, with the divisions into microregions and mesoregions in force, it was part of the microregion of Araçuaí, which in turn was included in the [[Jequitinhonha (mesoregion)|mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2016">{{cite web|access-date=30 October 2017 |author=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |date=2016 |title=Divisão Territorial Brasileira 2016 |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2016/DTB_2016_v2.zip}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

== Economy ==
The city's [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] is around R$520 million [[Brazilian real|reais]], with 50.3% of the added value coming from services, followed by public administration (33.6%), industry (11%) and agriculture (5.1%).<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Economia de Araçuaí - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/ara%C3%A7ua%C3%AD---mg |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref> With this structure, the [[GDP per capita]] of Araçuaí is R$ 14.2 thousand, a value lower than the state average (R$ 40.1 thousand) and the region of Teófilo Otoni (R$ 14.9 thousand), but higher than the average of the municipalities in the region of Araçuaí (R$ 11.4 thousand).<ref name=":1" />

== Culture ==
In the city, the Araçuaí Museum was created to house the collection of objects and documents that record the religiosity, customs and activities that make up the history of Araçuaí and the region.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |last=Gerais |first=Portal Minas |title=Turismo em Minas Gerais {{!}} Museu de Araçuaí |url=https://www.minasgerais.com.br/pt/atracoes/aracuai/cultura/museu-de-aracuai |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Portal Minas Gerais |language=pt-br}}</ref> The museum was created by the artist Lira Marques and Frei Chico, a Dutch [[Franciscans|Franciscan friar]] who worked as a researcher of Brazilian popular culture and religiosity.<ref name=":2" /> Every year, in September, the city holds the Micareta de Araçuaí, an [[Micareta|off-season carnival]] that attracts many people from the region.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2023-06-26 |title=Prefeitura de Araçuai anuncia realização da Micareta, com Wesley Safadão entre as atrações |url=https://gazetadearacuai.com.br/noticia/494/prefeitura-de-aracuai-anuncia-realizacao-da-micareta-com-wesley-safadao-entre-as-atracoes |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Gazeta de Araçuaí |language=pt-br}}</ref>
