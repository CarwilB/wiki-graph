'''Santa María''' is a  north eastern [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 22,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|5,740|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Santa María, Catamarca|Santa María]].
