'''Río Claro''' is a [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of the [[Talca Province]] in [[Chile]]'s [[Maule Region]]. The municipal seat is the [[List of towns in Chile|town]] of [[Cumpeo]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Río Claro spans an area of {{convert|430.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 12,698 inhabitants (6,716 men and 5,982 women). Of these, 2,651 (20.9%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 10,047 (79.1%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 0.8% (107 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Río Claro is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Claudio Guajardo Oyarce.<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Río Claro is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Empedrado, Chile|Empedrado]], [[Pencahue]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], [[Pelarco]] and [[San Rafael, Chile|San Rafael]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
