'''Sarakreek''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] (≈ municipality) in the gold mining region<ref>{{cite news|url=https://unitednews.sr/kassa-rinkelt-voor-dorpen-sarakreek-dankzij-skalians/|title=KASSA RINKELT VOOR DORPEN SARAKREEK DANKZIJ SKALIANS|website=United News|access-date=19 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> of [[Brokopondo District]] in [[Suriname]].<ref>[https://www.bing.com/maps?cp=4.3197~-54.966&style=a&lvl=15 Bing Maps - Sarakreek]</ref>  Its population at the 2012 census was 3,076.<ref name="census"/> It is served by the [[Sarakreek Airstrip]]. The resort is named after the eponymous [[Sara Creek (river)|creek]].

A large part of Sarakreek was flooded after the construction of the [[Afobaka Dam]], and the villages which were lost in the [[Brokopondo Reservoir]] were resettled in [[Marshallkreek]] and [[Centrum, Brokopondo|Tapoeripa]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/binn011sran01_01/binn011sran01_01_0005.php|title=Sranan. Cultuur in Suriname|language=nl|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|access-date=16 May 2020}}</ref>

Villages in the resort include: [[Baikutu]], Duwatra, [[Lebidoti]],<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Onderzoek-Regionaal-Plan-Brokopondo-2015-2040.pdf|title=Onderzoek Regionaal Plan Brokopondo 2015-2040|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=19 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> and [[Zoewatta]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.maplandia.com/suriname/brokopondo/sarakreek/zoewatta/|title=Sarakreek Zoewatta|website=Map Landia|access-date=19 May 2020}}</ref>

Flooded villages include: [[Abontjeman]] and [[Ganzee]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://werkgroepcaraibischeletteren.nl/rond-het-sterfbed-van-mijn-dorp/ |title=Rond het sterfbed van mijn dorp |author=Jerry Dewnarain|website=Werkgroep Caraïbische Letteren|access-date=16 January 2022|language=nl}}</ref>

==Koffiekamp==
The [[federation]] of Koffiekamp was among the lost villages, and consisted of three settlements: Maipa-ondo of the misidjan lo of clan founded in 1793, Baka Mbuju of the Njanfai-lo of clan, and [[Maria Hartmann]] founded Koffiekamp in 1851. Koffiekamp was located at the confluence of the Sara Creek and the [[Suriname River]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.gfcnieuws.com/koffiekamp-in-historisch-perspectief/|title=Koffiekamp in historisch perspectief|access-date=19 May 2020|language=nl|website=GFC Nieuws|url-status=dead}}</ref>
