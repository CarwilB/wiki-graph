'''Tepakán''' (in the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] language, “place where pakán fruit [similar to [[Opuntia ficus-indica|tuna]]] is found”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]]. Containing {{Convert|134.13|km2|abbr=on}} of land, it is roughly {{Convert|70|km|abbr=on}} east of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
During pre-Hispanic times, the area was part of the chieftainship of Ah-Kin-Chel. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with Cristóbal Sánchez as the encomendero in 1581. Subsequent holders of the trusteeship Esteban Tello Aguilar in 1700, Ana de Varreda Villegas in 1705, Antonia Pacheco and Juan Nepomuceno Calderón.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821.<ref name="municipios"/> On 24 July 1867 a decree passed to reorganize the divisions within the territory and Tepakán was assigned to [[Izamal Municipality]].<ref name="Municipalities organazation">{{cite book|title=División territorial del Estado de Yucatán de 1810 a 1995.|date=1997|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|isbn=970-13-1518-9|pages=109–110|edition=1.|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|accessdate=10 June 2015|language=Spanish|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=2015-12-23}}</ref> In 1988, it was made its own municipality.<ref>División territorial del Estado de Yucatán de 1810 a 1995, pp 123-124</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of ecology, public works, ecology, roads and markets, and cemeteries.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Tepakán|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=10 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />
