'''Santana do São Francisco''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern Portuguese pronunciation: </small>[sɐ̃ˈtɐ̃nɐ ˈdʊ ˈsɐ̃w fɾɐ̃ˈsisku]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]].
