'''Santo Antônio das Missões''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning "Saint Anthony of the [[Misiones Orientales|missions]]") is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the western part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 10,050 (2020 est.) in an area of 1710.87&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2I26 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Distance from the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]] is 534&nbsp;km west and tens of kilometres east from the [[Argentina|Argentine]] border but the municipality does not border since it is close.

The municipality would be partially flooded by the proposed [[Garabí Dam]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos}}|language=pt |title=Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos|publisher=Eletrobras CGTEE|date=7 April 2014
