'''Lagoa do Itaenga''' (also written as '''Lagoa de Itaenga''') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located 78&nbsp;km from [[Recife]], the capital of Pernambuco. As of the [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 census]], it had a population of 19,003.<ref name="IBGELagoaDeItaenga2025" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Carpina]] and [[Lagoa do Carro]] ('''N'''); [[Glória do Goitá]] ('''S'''); [[Paudalho]] ('''E'''); [[Feira Nova, Pernambuco|Feira Nova]] and [[Limoeiro]] ('''W''')
* Area - 57.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 183 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] and Caducifólia forests
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 24.6 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 78&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Lagoa de Itaenga''' are largely dominated by the food & [[drink industry|beverage industry]] (77%) and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]] and [[cattle]].
