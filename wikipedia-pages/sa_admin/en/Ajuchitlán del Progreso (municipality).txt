'''Ajuchitlán''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Ajuchitlán]]. The municipality covers an area of 1983.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2020, the municipality had a total population of 37,655,<ref>{{cite web |title=Ajuchitlán del Progreso |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/ajuchitlan-del-progreso |website=Secretaría de Economía}}</ref> compared to 37,475 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ajuchitlán |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12003a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=December 23, 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927205604/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12003a.htm |archive-date=September 27, 2007 }}</ref>
