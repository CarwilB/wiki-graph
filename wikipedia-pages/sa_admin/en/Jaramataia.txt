'''Jaramataia''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒarɐmɐˈtɐjɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 5,761 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/jaramataia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|104|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>
