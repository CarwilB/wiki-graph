'''Sullana''' is a landlocked province in the [[Piura Region]] in northwestern [[Peru]]. It is the northernmost province in the [[Piura Region]].

== Boundaries ==
*'''North''': [[Contralmirante Villar province|province of Contralmirante Villar]] ([[Tumbes Region]])
*'''East''': [[Ecuador]], [[Ayabaca province|province of Ayabaca]]
*'''South''': [[Piura province|province of Piura]]
*'''West''': provinces of [[Paita province|Paita]] and [[Talara Province|Talara]]

== Political division ==
The province has an area of {{convert|5423.61|km2|sp=us}} and is divided into eight [[districts of Peru|districts]]

*'''[[Sullana District|Sullana]]''' 
*[[Bellavista District, Sullana|Bellavista]] 
*[[Marcavelica District|Marcavelica]] 
*[[Miguel Checa District|Miguel Checa]]
*[[Ignacio Escudero District|Ignacio Escudero]]
*[[Lancones District|Lancones]]
*[[Querecotillo District|Querecotillo]]
*[[Salitral District, Sullana|Salitral]]

== Population ==
The province has an approximate population of 250,000 residents.

== Capital ==
The capital of the province is the city of [[Sullana]] which is the second largest city in the [[Piura Region]].
