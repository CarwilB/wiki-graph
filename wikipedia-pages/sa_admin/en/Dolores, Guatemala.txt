'''Dolores''' is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Petén Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|1374|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 30,546 inhabitants.

==History==
Archaeological evidence from the area suggests that the region was once part of the [[Mayan civilization]] of [[Mopan territory|Mopán]], and was known as "Ixtanché" in the Mayan language meaning "wooden bank to rest". In 1695, the Spanish military expedition, led by Juan Díaz de Velasco, established a military presence in the area. A small garrison was left in the area under the command Nicolás Cuevas, which marked the arrival of the first Europeans and [[Mestizos]]. Later, this, [[Franciscan]] missionaries carried out evangelization of the local population. The name "Dolores" first appears in 1708, when Juan Antonio Ruiz y Bustamante organized the population in the settlement.<ref name="Hist"/>

After the Guatemalan independence, Dolores appears in administrative records during the early 19th century. It was included administrative divisions during the subsequent reorganization of the territory by the constitution of 1825. In 1836, the settlement was attached to the Santa Ana circuit.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/peten/historia-del-municipio-de-dolores-peten/|title=Historia del municipio de Dolores|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Dolores municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Dolores is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Petén Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|1374|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Dolores (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/pet%C3%A9n/1708__dolores/|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It is located in the southeastern part of the department, lying approximately {{cvt|78|km}} from the departmental capital of [[Flores]] and about {{cvt|401|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]]. The municipality borders the municipalities of [[Melchor de Mencos]], [[Santa Ana, Petén|Santa Ana]], and [[San Francisco, Petén|San Francisco]] to the east, [[Poptún]] to the south, and [[Sayaxché]] to the west. It shares land border with Belize to the south-east.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/peten/geografia-del-municipio-de-dolores-peten/|title=Geografia del municipio de Dolores|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Dolores municipality|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|138.11|m}} above sea level, Dolores has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|26.77|C}} and receives about {{cvt|77.09|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/peten/doloress|title=Dolores climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 30,546 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 15,652 males and 14,894 females. About 34.8% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.3% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (71.8%) was classified as urban, while 28.2% lived in rural areas. About 35.1% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (90.3%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (about 9.7%) forming a minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 79.9%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (91.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
