'''Mojuí dos Campos''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Pará]] in the [[Northern Region, Brazil|Northern region]] of [[Brazil]]. The city had already emancipated from Santarém in 1999, but since that date no municipal elections were run in the city until the [[Brazilian municipal elections, 2012|2012 one]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.jusbrasil.com.br/noticias/2115476/tre-suspende-eleicoes-de-mojui-dos-campos|title=TRE suspende eleições de Mojuí dos Campos|language=pt-br}}</ref> Consequently, the city was established on January 1, 2013, with the inauguration of the city's first mayor and councilors.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://oglobo.globo.com/politica/com-5-novos-municipios-brasil-agora-tem-5570-cidades-7235803|title=Com 5 novos municípios, Brasil agora tem 5.570 cidades|language=pt-br}}</ref>
