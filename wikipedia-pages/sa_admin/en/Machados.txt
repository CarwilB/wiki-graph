'''Machados''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[maˈʃɐdu(s)]}}) (population 11.802) is a city in northeastern [[Brazil]], in the State of [[Pernambuco]]. It lies in the mesoregion of [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco and has an area of {{convert|56.96|km2|2|lk=on|abbr=off}}. It is the largest producer of [[banana]]s of the state, with over 21.000 ton.

==Geography==
* State – [[Pernambuco]]
* Region – [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries – [[São Vicente Ferrer, Pernambuco|São Vicente Ferrer]]  ('''N''');  [[Bom Jardim, Pernambuco|Bom Jardim]]   ('''S''' and '''E''');  [[Orobó]]  ('''W''').
* Area – {{convert|56.96|km2|2|lk=on|abbr=off}}
* Elevation – {{convert|320|m|ft|lk=on|abbr=off}}
* Hydrography – [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation – [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Annual average temperature – {{convert|23.9|C|F|1|lk=on|abbr=off}}
* Distance to [[Recife]] – {{convert|320|km|mi|lk=on|abbr=off}}

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Machados''' are related with commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[banana]]s, [[sugarcane]] and  [[cattle]].
