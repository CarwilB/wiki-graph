'''Praia Grande''' is the southernmost municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. Despite its name (which means ''Great Beach'' in [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]]), the city is landlocked.

The nearby canyons, which include [[Serra Geral National Park]] and [[Aparados da Serra National Park]], make it a destination for [[hot air ballooning]] activities.<ref name=g1>{{Cite web|first=John|last=Pacheco|url=https://g1.globo.com/sc/santa-catarina/noticia/2025/06/21/balao-pega-fogo-durante-passeio-mortos-feridos-praia-grande-santa-catarina.ghtml|title=Oito pessoas morrem em queda de balão que pegou fogo em Praia Grande (SC)|trans-title=Eight people die in hot air balloon crash that caught fire in Praia Grande (SC)|date=21 June 2025|website=G1|accessdate=21 June 2025|language=pt-br}}</ref> On 21 June 2025, a [[2025 Santa Catarina hot air balloon crash|hot air balloon crashed in the town]], killing 8 of the 21 people on board.<ref name=bbc> {{cite news|first1=Rute|last1=Pina|first2=Jaroslav|last2=Lukiv|title=At least eight dead in hot air balloon accident in Brazil |url=https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c17w04wxwpxo |work=[[BBC]] |date=21 June 2025}}</ref><ref name=cnn>{{Cite web|url=https://www.cnn.com/2025/06/21/americas/dead-hot-air-balloon-accident-brazil-intl|title=At least 8 dead after hot air balloon accident in southern Brazil|first1=Duarte|last1=Mendonca|first2=Eve|last2=Brennan|date=21 June 2025|website=[[CNN]]|accessdate=21 June 2025}}</ref>
