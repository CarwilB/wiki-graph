'''San Pedro de Larcay''' is one of 11 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Sucre Province]] in the [[Ayacucho Region|Ayacucho region]] in [[Peru]].

==Population==
The population of San Pedro de Larcay ([[Peru 2005 Census|2005 census]]) is 1,175 people, 600 men and 575 women.<ref name=inei>[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ INEI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601020523/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ |date=2007-06-01 }}</ref>
