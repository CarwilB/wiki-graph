'''Teapa''' is a [[Municipalities of Tabasco|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tabasco]],<ref>{{Cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tabasco/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=January 4, 2010}}</ref> located in the southeastern part of the country near the [[Gulf of Mexico]] and bordering the state of [[Chiapas]]. Declared a {{lang|es|[[Pueblos Mágicos|Pueblo Mágico]]}} (Magic Town) for its landscape and traditions, its main attractions include the {{ill|Grutas de Coconá|es}} caves and the historic Estación del Ferrocarril. It is also a significant center for banana production.<ref>{{Cite web |title=These are Mexico's newest Magical Towns |url=https://www.travelweekly.com/Mexico-Travel/Insights/Mexico-names-new-Magical-Towns |access-date=2025-09-15 |website=www.travelweekly.com |language=en}}</ref>

==Etymology==
The name {{lang|es|Teapa}} comes from the [[Nahuatl]] words {{lang|nah|tetl}} and {{lang|nah|apan}}, which mean "river over stones" or "stone's river". It refers to one of the rivers that crosses the town.

==Geography==
The municipal seat is the city of Teapa. The municipality is divided into 18 ejidos, 15 ranches, 1 populated, 6 neighborhoods and 1 villa.{{Clarify|date=September 2010}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_tabasco |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México Estado de Tabasco|accessdate=2008-01-30}}</ref>  Its territorial extension is {{convert|679.78|km2|sqmi}}, which corresponds to 2.76% of the state total; this places the municipality 16th in territorial extension.  There is a border to the north with the municipalities of Centro and Jalapa; and in the south, east and west with the state of Chiapas.  According to the results presented for the 2nd population and home count from 2005, the municipality has a population of 49,262 people.

===Climate===
The weather is warm and humid with rain all year round; it has an annual average temperature of {{convert|27.8|°C|°F}}.  The monthly highest average is in June from and the lowest average is in December.
