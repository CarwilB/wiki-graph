'''Acatic ''' is a [[town]] and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]], in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 339.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The town produces mainly Spanish style shingles, brick, adobe floor tile, chia, [[maize|corn]], and [[tequila]]. Pueblo Viejo Tequila is bottled near by. The town is the entry "gate" to Los Altos of Jalisco.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 18,551.<ref>{{cite web|title=Acatic |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14001a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=April 13, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070526105634/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14001a.htm |archive-date=May 26, 2007 }}</ref>
