'''Dom Joaquim''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|Brazilian municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Conceição do Mato Dentro]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 4,451.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/dom-joaquim/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
