'''Paraíso''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Pocrí District]], [[Los Santos Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 597 as of 2010. Its population as of 1990 was 574; its population as of 2000 was 593.<ref name=inec/>
