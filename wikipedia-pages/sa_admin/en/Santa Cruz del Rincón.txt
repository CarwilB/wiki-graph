'''Santa Cruz del Rincón''' ([[Tlapanec language|Me'phaa]]: '''Xkuaa''', "flat place"<ref name=rodrigo>{{cite thesis |last=Armada Ramírez |first=Rodrigo |year=2012 |title=Alimentación, jóvenes y violencia en Santa Cruz el Rincón, comunidad me'phaa de Malinaltepec, Guerrero |url=https://mediateca.inah.gob.mx/islandora_74/islandora/object/tesis:526 |degree=MSc |pages=39–64 |publisher=[[National School of Anthropology and History|ENAH]] |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. It is located about {{convert|100|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Chilpancingo]]. Its creation from the municipality of [[Malinaltepec (municipality)|Malinaltepec]] was approved in 2021 and went into force on 21 May 2022.

==Geography==
The municipality of Santa Cruz del Rincón is located at the headwaters of the [[Marquelia River]] in the transition zone between the [[Costa Chica of Guerrero|Costa Chica]] and the [[Sierra Madre del Sur]] of eastern Guerrero.<ref name=rodrigo/> It borders the municipalities of Malinaltepec to the north, [[Iliatenco (municipality)|Iliatenco]] to the northeast, and [[San Luis Acatlán (municipality)|San Luis Acatlán]] to the south and west. The highest point of Santa Cruz del Rincón is its tripoint with Malinaltepec and Iliatenco at Cerro Ardilla, {{convert|1763|m}} above sea level.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://peakvisor.com/peak/cerro-ardilla.html |title=Cerro Ardilla |website=PeakVisor |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|89.55|km2}}.<ref name=creation/> Since 2011, {{convert|23.8|km2}} of the part of the municipality east of the main north–south highway has been protected as a Voluntary Conservation Area.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.protectedplanet.net/555599601 |title=Area de Conservación Ecológica de Santa Cruz del Rincón |website=[[World Database on Protected Areas]] |publisher=[[UNEP-WCMC]] |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref>

Santa Cruz del Rincón has a [[subhumid temperate climate]] with rain in the summer. Average temperatures range between {{convert|22|and(-)|26|C}}.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/12/12041.pdf |title=Malinaltepec, Guerrero |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref>

==History==
Due to a lack of basic infrastructure and services, Santa Cruz del Rincón and its surrounding communities had been demanding the creation of an independent municipal government since 1964.<ref>{{cite news |last=Ramírez Hernández |first=Sofío |date=11 February 2014 |title=PROPOSICIÓN CON PUNTO DE ACUERDO POR EL QUE SE EXHORTA AL CONGRESO DEL ESTADO DE GUERRERO PARA QUE EN PLENO EJERCICIO DE SUS FACULTADES, CONSIDERE Y FACILITE LA CREACIÓN DEL MUNICIPIO DE SANTA CRUZ DEL RINCÓN EN EL ESTADO DE GUERRERO |language=es |newspaper=Gaceta del Senado |url=https://www.senado.gob.mx/65/gaceta_del_senado/documento/45629 |number=LXII/2SPO-79-1493/45629 |publisher=[[Senate of the Republic (Mexico)|Senate of Mexico]] |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref> On 31 August 2021, the [[Congress of Guerrero|Guerrero state congress]] approved the formation of the municipality of Santa Cruz del Rincón from nineteen localities previously belonging to Malinaltepec.<ref name=creation/> The state constitutional amendment establishing the municipality was passed on 13 January 2022 and went into force on 21 May 2022.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=20 May 2022 |title=DECRETO NÚMERO 161 POR EL QUE SE APRUEBA LA ADICIÓN DE LOS NUEVOS MUNICIPIOS DE SANTA CRUZ DEL RINCÓN, SAN NICOLÁS, ÑUU SAVI Y LAS VIGAS AL ARTÍCULO 27 DE LA CONSTITUCIÓN POLÍTICA DEL ESTADO LIBRE Y SOBERANO DE GUERRERO. EN EL LUGAR QUE CONFORME AL ORDEN ALFABÉTICO LES CORRESPONDE. |url=https://periodicooficial.guerrero.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/P.O-40-20-MAYO-20221.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Guerrero |pages=9–24 |access-date=21 November 2023}}</ref>

==Administration==
Santa Cruz del Rincón will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2024. An interim administration was appointed in September 2023, led by [[municipal president]] Godofredo Avilés Mendoza.<ref>{{cite web |last=Bautista |first=Yonathan |date=25 September 2023 |url=https://www.elfarodelacostachica.com.mx/2023/09/instalan-cabildo-instituyente-de-santa-cruz-del-rincon/ |title=Instalan Cabildo instituyente de Santa Cruz del Rincón |language=es |website=Congress of Guerrero |accessdate=28 November 2023}}</ref> The municipal government of Santa Cruz del Rincón consists of a municipal president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and six trustees (''regidores'').<ref>{{cite news |last=Chávez |first=Lourdes |date=16 November 2023 |url=https://suracapulco.mx/impreso/2/aprueba-iepc-composicion-de-ayuntamientos-incluidos-los-cuatro-nuevos-municipios/ |title=Aprueba IEPC composición de ayuntamientos, incluidos los cuatro nuevos municipios |language=es |newspaper=El Sur |location=Chilpancingo |accessdate=24 November 2023}}</ref>

The {{ill|Coordinadora Regional de Autoridades Comunitarias-Policía Comunitaria|es|lt=CRAC–PC}}, a [[Grupos de autodefensa comunitaria|community self-defence group]] serving indigenous communities in eastern Guerrero, operates in the area.<ref name=rodrigo/>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Santa Cruz del Rincón recorded a population of 6851 inhabitants.<ref name=creation/> The municipal seat, also named Santa Cruz del Rincón or just El Rincón, recorded a population of 1677 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=cabecera/> Other localities in the municipality with over a thousand people are El Potrerillo and Tierra Colorada, which recorded populations of 1393 and 1020 inhabitants respectively in the 2020 Census.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=120120016 |title=El Potrerillo (Potrerillo del Rincón), Malinaltepec, Guerrero (120410016) |language=es |website=México en Cifras |publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=28 November 2023}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=120410025 |title=Tierra Colorada, Malinaltepec, Guerrero (120410025) |language=es |website=México en Cifras |publisher=[[INEGI]] |accessdate=28 November 2023}}</ref> According to the 2010 Census, 70% of the population in Santa Cruz del Rincón spoke the Me'phaa or [[Tlapanec language]].<ref name=rodrigo/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The economy of Santa Cruz del Rincón is based on agriculture: corn, coffee and hibiscus are the main crops.<ref name=rodrigo/> 

A paved highway runs north to south through the municipality, connecting it with [[Tlapa de Comonfort]] to the north and [[Marquelia]] to the south.<ref name=rodrigo/>

Several educational institutions operate in Santa Cruz del Rincón, including a public library, a junior high school and a high school, a branch of the [[National Pedagogic University (Mexico)|National Pedagogic University]],<ref name=creation/> and a branch of UNISUR ([[Universidad Intercultural de los Pueblos del Sur]]), which offers informal education for indigenous youth.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Alonso Guzmán |first1=Lorena |last2=Hernández Alarcón |first2=Victor Manuel |last3=Solís Carmona |first3=Edgardo |date=January–March 2014 |title=La Universidad Intercultural de los Pueblos del Sur: una opción de educación no formal para la población indígena en el estado de Guerrero, México |language=es |trans-title=Universidad Intercultural de los Pueblos del Sur: An option in Informal Education for the Indigenous Population in the State of Guerrero, Mexico |journal=Revista mexicana de investigación educativa |url=https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?script=sci_arttext&pid=S1405-66662014000100006 |volume=19 |issue=60 |pages=103–128 |access-date=28 November 2023}}</ref>
