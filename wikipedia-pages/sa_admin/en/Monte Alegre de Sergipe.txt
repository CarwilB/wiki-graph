'''Monte Alegre de Sergipe''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈmõtʃi aˈlɛɡɾi dʒi seʁˈʒipi|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 15,175 (2020). Monte Alegre de Sergipe covers {{convert|407|km2|mi2|abbr=on}} with a population density of 37 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>
