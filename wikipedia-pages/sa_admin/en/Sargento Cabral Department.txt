'''Sargento Cabral''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]], named after [[Juan Bautista Cabral]] (Sergeant Cabral), a soldier in the Independence Wars.

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 15,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1651|km2|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Colonia Elisa]], which is located about {{convert|1100|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
