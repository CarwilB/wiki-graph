It also has a population of 48 294 people <ref name=IBGE1></ref>

== Etymology ==
The name ''Araci'' is derived from [[José de Alencar]]'s novel [[Ubirajara (novel)|''Ubirajara'']]. It combines ''ara'' (day) with ''cy'' (mother), thus meaning ''Mother of the Day''.<ref>https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/araci/historico</ref> The meaning inspired the composition of the municipality's anthem by musician Ramos Freirense. The name also inspired writer and poet Antônio Fernando Peltier in writing multiple poems for the city
