'''Camaná''' is a [[province]] in the [[Arequipa Region]], [[Peru]]. It borders the provinces of [[Caravelí Province|Caravelí]], [[Condesuyos Province|Condesuyos]], [[Castilla Province|Castilla]], [[Caylloma Province|Caylloma]], [[Arequipa Province|Arequipa]] and [[Islay Province|Islay]].

The province was struck by the [[2001 southern Peru earthquake]], which occurred on June 23, 2001.

==Political division==
The province is divided into eight [[district]]s (Spanish: ''distritos'', singular: ''distrito''):

* [[Camaná District|Camaná]] ([[Camaná]])
* [[José María Quimper District|José María Quimper]] ([[El Cardo]])
* [[Mariano Nicolás Valcárcel District|Mariano Nicolás Valcárcel]] ([[Urasqui]])
* [[Mariscal Cáceres District, Camaná|Mariscal Cáceres]] ([[San José, Camaná Province|San José]])
* [[Nicolás de Piérola District|Nicolás de Piérola]] ([[San Gregorio, Camaná Province|San Gregorio]])
* [[Ocoña District|Ocoña]] ([[Ocoña]])
* [[Quilca District|Quilca]] (Quilca)
* [[Samuel Pastor District|Samuel Pastor]] ([[La Pampa, Peru|La Pampa]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (84.02%) learnt to speak in childhood, 13.30% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language]] and 2.57% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
