'''Guayabal''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Boquerón District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|57.6|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,420 as of 2023, giving it a population density of {{convert|42.0|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2010 was 2,111; its population as of 2000 was 1,797.<ref name=inec/>
