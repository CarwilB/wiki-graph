The [[Cordillera Apolobamba|Apolobamba]] mountain range traverses the province. [[Chawpi Urqu|Wisk'achani]] (Chawpi Urqu), the highest mountain of the range, lies on the border with [[Bolivia]]. == Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (60.23%) learnt to speak in childhood, 30.24% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language and  9.37% using [[Aymara language|Aymara]]  ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
