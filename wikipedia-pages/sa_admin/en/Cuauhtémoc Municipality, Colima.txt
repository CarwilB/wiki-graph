'''Cuauhtémoc''' is a [[municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[state (Mexico)|state]] of [[Colima]]. Its municipal seat is the city of [[Cuauhtémoc, Colima]].

The municipality of Cuauhtémoc covers a total surface area of 373.2  km<sup>2</sup>. In the 2005 [[INEGI]] Census, it reported a total population of 25,576 (down from 26,771 five year earlier),
of whom 7,513 lived in the municipal seat.

The municipality was created on 15 January 1915. Its name honours the last ''[[tlatoani]]'' of the [[Aztec]] people, [[Cuauhtemoc]].
