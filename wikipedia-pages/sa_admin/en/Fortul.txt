'''Fortul''' is one of the seven municipalities of the Colombian [[Arauca Department|department of Arauca]]. It limits to the north with [[Saravena]]; to the south with [[Tame, Arauca|Tame]]; to the east with [[Arauquita]] and to the west with [[Güicán]]. It was founded on January 7, 1920. According to the census conducted by the DANE in 2005, Fortul has a population of 25,379 inhabitants, of which 13,081 live in the municipal seat making it 49.5% women and 50.5% men.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fortul-arauca.gov.co/|title=Alcaldia de Fortul, personal de la Alcaldia (In Spanish)|accessdate=2018-06-26|archive-date=2018-06-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180627010031/http://www.fortul-arauca.gov.co/|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fortul-arauca.gov.co/municipio/informacion-general|title=Información general|website=www.fortul-arauca.gov.co|language=es-es|access-date=2018-10-26|archive-date=2018-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181027061506/http://www.fortul-arauca.gov.co/municipio/informacion-general|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Economy==
The economic activities in the municipality are given by the sale of basic primary services and goods, as well as by some transformation of raw materials. The town has an organized trade of goods and consumption (stores, stores, restaurants, workshops, supplies and others).
Three important activities in the town are livestock, agriculture, and the production of double cream cheese by some lactic micro-enterprises. The area of direct influence of the urban area on the territory is considered approximately 30% of its territory, since the neighboring municipalities, [[Arauquita]], [[Tame, Arauca|Tame]] and [[Saravena]], cover the demand for services and remaining goods; thus, the urban dynamics of this town is very scarce as people from rural areas go to the villages.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dane.gov.co/files/censo2005/PERFIL_PDF_CG2005/81300T7T000.PDF|title=Boletín Censo General 2005|date=2010-09-10}}</ref>
