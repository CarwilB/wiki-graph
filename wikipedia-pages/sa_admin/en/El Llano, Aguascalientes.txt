'''El Llano''' is a [[Municipalities of Aguascalientes|municipality]] in the Mexican [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Aguascalientes]]. It stands at {{coord|21|55|N|101|58|W|}}.

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 18,828,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=El Llano|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=010|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=24 February 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180224112915/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=01&mun=010|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 17,115 in 2005.

The municipality had 244 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: the municipal seat of [[Palo Alto, Aguascalientes|Palo Alto]] (5,399), classified as urban, and [[Los Conos, Aguascalientes|Los Conos]] (1,108), [[Ojo de Agua de Crucitas, Aguascalientes|Ojo de Agua de Crucitas]] (1,078), and [[Santa Rosa (El Huizache), Aguascalientes|Santa Rosa (El Huizache)]] (1,050), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
