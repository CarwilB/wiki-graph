'''Limay Mahuida Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department of Argentina]] in [[La Pampa Province]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.minutouno.com/sociedad/granizo/alerta-lluvias-y-una-provincia-que-precauciones-tomar-n5578389|title=Alerta por lluvias y granizo en una provincia: qué precauciones tomar|work={{ill|Minutouno.com|es}}|date=8 November 2022|access-date=2 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital city of the department is [[Limay Mahuida]]. With a population of 423 in 2022, it is the least populated department in Argentina, excluding Antarctic territories.
