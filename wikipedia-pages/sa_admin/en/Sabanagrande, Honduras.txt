'''Sabanagrande''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://proceso.hn/sabanagrande-en-situacion-alarmante-por-covid-19-y-se-toman-medidas-de-restriccion/|title=Sabanagrande en situación “alarmante” por COVID-19 y se toman medidas de restricción|work={{ill|Proceso Digital|es}}|date=15 July 2021|access-date=23 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

People make a living by planting corn and beans and selling them on the market. Many people also sell rosquillas. Cattle is also raised and cheese and other products are sold.{{cn|date=November 2025}}
