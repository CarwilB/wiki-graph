'''Pedras Altas''' ([[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning "Tall Stones") is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 1,954 (2020 est.) in an area of 1377.37&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/GQJ Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The [[Jaguarão River]], which forms the border with [[Uruguay]] here, flows along the southwestern part of the municipality.
