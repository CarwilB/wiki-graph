'''Florida''' is one of the twelve districts of the [[Bongará Province]] in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref name="Amazonas">{{cite web|url=http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|title=Provincias y Distritos|date=n.d.|language=es|website=Gobierno Regional de Amazonas|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130615121036/http://www.regionamazonas.gob.pe/sede/detalle_directorio.php?id=35|archive-date=15 June 2013|url-status=dead|access-date=15 November 2014}}</ref> Its seat is Pomacochas, also named Florida. It is located approximately  90&nbsp;km from [[Bagua Grande]], at the route of the Peruvian forest for the marginal highway of the forest, today called Fernando Belaunde Terry.

The borders of the district are:

*To the north: [[Yambrasbamba District]]
*To the south: [[Shipasbamba District]] and [[Cuispes District]].
*To the east: [[Jumbilla District]] and Cuispes.
*To the west: [[Utcubamba Province]].

The access to the capital of the district is by asphalted highway of the Forest Fernando Belaunde Terry. It is an intermediate step of Chachapoyas to [[Lima]].

The highway of the (Pan-American) coast (Fernando Belaunde Terry) joins to that of the forest doing a whole of 1,525&nbsp;km, which is asphalted for its entire length.

In accordance with the census conducted in the district in the year 2003, it has a population of 5,117 inhabitants, 2,839 or (56%) belong to rural population and 2,275 inhabitants or (44%) are urban population.

[[Lake Pomacochas]] is situated within the district.
