'''San Carlos''' is a western [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 28,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|11,578|km2}}, and its capital city is [[San Carlos, Mendoza|San Carlos]], which is located around {{cvt|1200|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].
