'''São Caetano''', also known as  ''São Caitano''  is a city in the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Brejo da Madre de Deus]]   ('''N''');  [[Altinho, Pernambuco|Altinho]]   ('''S''');  [[Caruaru]]   ('''E''');  [[Tacaimbó]] and [[Cachoeirinha, Pernambuco|Cachoeirinha]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 382.48&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 552 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]], [[Ipojuca River|Ipojuca]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)]] rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] hot
* Annual average temperature - 22.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 148&nbsp;km
* Population - 37,368 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/sao-caitano/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''São Caetano''' are based on the textile industry, commerce  and [[agribusiness]] especially plantations of [[manioc]];  and farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]] and [[pig]]s.
