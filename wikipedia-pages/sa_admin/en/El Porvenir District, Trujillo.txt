'''El Porvenir District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Trujillo Province, Peru|Trujillo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

==Festivals==
*[[International Calzaferia El Porvenir]] is an annual footwear fair and in 2012 had its 10th.  It is held in El Porvenir city.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.rpp.com.pe/2012-11-04-trujillo-x-calzaferia-llego-a-su-fin-con-exito-noticia_537185.html|title =Trujillo: X Calzaferia llegó a su fin con éxito|access-date =November 25, 2012}}</ref>
