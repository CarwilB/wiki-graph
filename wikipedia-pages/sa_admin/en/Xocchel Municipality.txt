'''Xocchel Municipality''' ({{IPA|myn|ʃokˈtʃʼel|pron}}, in the [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] Language: “counting [[jay]]s”) is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing  53.65&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and located roughly 45&nbsp;km southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
During pre-Hispanic times, the area was part of the chieftainship of [[Ah Kin Chel]]. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system. In 1753, two women were the encomenderas for Xocchel, Catalina Guerrero y Ulbarri and María Enríquez de Novoa, responsible for 207 indigenous people.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821.<ref name="municipios"/> On 30 November 1840 a partition was made assigning Xocchel to the [[Izamal Municipality]].<ref name="Municipalities organazation">{{cite book|title=División territorial del Estado de Yucatán de 1810 a 1995.|date=1997|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|isbn=970-13-1518-9|pages=101–102|edition=1.|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|access-date=10 June 2015|language=es}}</ref> In 1988, it was made its own municipality.<ref>División territorial del Estado de Yucatán de 1810 a 1995, pp 123-124</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public service, nomenclature, cemeteries, and parks.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Xocchel|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=10 June 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>
