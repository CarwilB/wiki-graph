== Toponym ==
The toponym "Camboriú" is a reference to the [[Camboriú River]]. The name originates from the [[Tupi language]], meaning "river of the [[common snook]]", from ''kamuri'' (snook) and ''<nowiki/>'y'' (river). Another hypothesis is that the [[Indigenous peoples|indigenous]] origin was inspired by the relief of the Pedra Branca, a hill that resembles a female breast and is visible from several points in the municipality. According to Patrianova, in ''Pequeno Livro'', Cambu means "to nurse", and Ryry, Ruru or Riú means nursing container, i.e. breast.
