'''Colina''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]], [[capital (political)|capital]] of the [[Chacabuco Province]], in the northern part of the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]], approximately 30 kilometers north of Santiago Centro.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Colina spans an area of {{convert|52769|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 77,815 inhabitants (41,004 men and 36,811 women). Of these, 62,811 (80.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 15,004 (19.3%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 47.5% (25,046 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> Average household income: US$19,783 (PPP, 2006).{{Citation needed|date=December 2010}}

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], Colina is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by a mayor who is directly elected every four years. Since 2021, the mayor has been Isabel Valenzuela Ahumada ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref>{{cite web |title=Alcaldesa |url=https://www.colina.cl/alcaldesa |website=Municipality of Colina |access-date=4 June 2026 |language=es-CL}}</ref> The 2024-2028 municipal council has the following members:<ref>{{cite web |title=Concejales |url=https://www.colina.cl/concejales/ |website=Municipality of Colina |access-date=4 June 2026 |language=es-CL}}</ref>
* Carlos Andrews Cruz ([[Republican Party of Chile|REP]])
* Federico Koch Valenzuela ([[Broad Front (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Gonzalo Torres Ferrari ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Rafael Goni del Río (RN)
* Orlando Melo Inostroza ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]])
* Angélica Antiman Palman (UDI)
* Lorena Rojas Mendoza (RN)
* Soledad Vial Cummins (REP)

The regional [[intendant]], appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]], is Fernando Echeverría.<ref name="Intendants">{{Cite web |url=http://www.gobiernodechile.cl/intendentes/ |title=Gobierno de Chile: Intendentes |accessdate=3 December 2010 |language=es |archive-date=15 June 2013 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130615025111/http://www.gobiernodechile.cl/intendentes/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> Belonging to Province of Chacabuco, whose actual governor is Mrs. Angélica Antimán.

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Colina is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Mr. Patricio Melero ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mr. Gabriel Silber ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 16th electoral district (together with [[Lampa, Chile|Lampa]], [[Tiltil]], [[Quilicura]] and [[Pudahuel]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
