'''Aguanqueterique''' ({{IPA|es|aɣwaŋketeˈɾike}}) is a village and [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[La Paz department, Honduras|La Paz]], situated in the southwest of the country, with the principal village about {{convert|53|km}} by road south of [[La Paz, Honduras|La Paz]] town. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|187|km2}} and contains another village, Barrancaray, and 70 hamlets. As of 2001 it had a population of 4620.

Aguanqueterique is a producer of [[grains]], [[sugarcane]] and [[coffee]], and involved with cattle and poultry farming. Its patronal festival is the day of Our Lady of Mercy, held on September 24.

==History==
The Municipality of Aguanqueterique has been cited as being founded in around 1500, and in 1694 it was listed as one of the towns in the Province of Comayagua.<ref name="Edu"/> In the [[Mesoamerican language]], the name means "place of the avocados".<ref name="Red"/>

At the time of the ecclesiastical census of 1791, it was chartered under the name San Pedro de Aguanqueterique,<ref name="Edu"/> under the curacy of Comayagua.<ref name="Red">{{Cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-aguanqueterique/|title=Municipality of Aguanqueterique|website=Redhonduras.hn|accessdate=22 May 2026}}</ref> In 1825 it was documented to be part of the Parish of Goascorán, under Comayagua, and in 1889 it became a municipality of the District of San Antonio.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/12-La-Paz/Aguanqueterique.html|title=Aguanqueterique|website=Edu-honduras.info|accessdate=22 May 2026|language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Geography==
Aguanqueterique is a large village and municipality covering an area of {{convert|187|km2}} in the [[La Paz department, Honduras|La Paz Department]] of southwest Honduras. By road it is about {{convert|53|km}} by road south of [[La Paz, Honduras|La Paz]], and {{convert|5.5|km}} by road southeast of [[San Juan, La Paz|San Juan]].<ref name="GM">{{Google maps | url =https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Aguanqueterique,+La+Paz,+Honduras/14.3204325,-87.6731034/@14.2170611,-87.6538668,12.46z/data=!4m14!4m13!1m10!1m1!1s0x8f65642752d1e597:0x8a1d09f27653966f!2m2!1d-87.6667436!2d13.9511364!3m4!1m2!1d-87.6741654!2d14.300294!3s0x8f6582eb14996789:0x46c13545270d5cfc!1m0!3e0?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MDUyMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D  | accessdate =22 May 2026}}</ref> Municipally, Aguanqueterique borders [[Lamani]] to the north, [[Curaren]] and [[Lepaterique]] to the east, [[San Antonio del Norte]] and [[Lauterique]] to the south, and [[Guajiquiro]], [[San Juan, La Paz|San Juan]], and San Antonio del Norte to the west. The other main village in the municipality is [[Barrancaray]], and there are 70 hamlets.The average elevation is {{convert|1486|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/>

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Aguanqueterique municipality had a population of 4,738. Of these, 65.23% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (65.09% [[Lenca]]), 32.92%, [[Mestizo]], 1.58% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.25% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.02% others.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/|title=Baseline|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)|accessdate=22 May 2026|language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Economy==
The municipality is a producer of [[grains]], [[sugarcane]] and [[coffee]], and cattle and poultry farming.<ref name="Red"/>

==Landmarks==
The main village of Aguanqueterique contains the Plaza San Isidro, a central park, a football ground, the Hogar Materno Infantil hospital and the Monasterio Anuciasion de Las Siervas to the southeast.<ref name="GM"/>

==Culture==
The patronal festival of Aguanqueterique is the day of Our Lady of Mercy, held on September 24.<ref name="Red"/>
