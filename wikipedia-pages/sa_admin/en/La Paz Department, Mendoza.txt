'''La Paz ''' is an eastern [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|7,105|km2}}, and its capital city is [[La Paz, Mendoza|La Paz]].
