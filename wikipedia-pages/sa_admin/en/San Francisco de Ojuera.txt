'''San Francisco de Ojuera''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|179.0|km2}} and had a population of 7,000 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
During the population census of 1791, 
San Francisco de Ojuera is mentioned as "Ojuera". The municipality was officially established in 1895, as a part of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]].<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=San Francisco de Ojuera, Santa Bárbara|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/San-Francisco-de-Ojuera.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of San Francisco de Ojuera|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-san-francisco-ojuera/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
San Francisco de Ojuera is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Concepción del Sur]] to the north, the department of [[Intibucá Department|Intibucá]] to the south, [[San Pedro Zacapa]] to the east, and [[Ceguaca]] and [[Santa Rita, Santa Bárbara|Santa Rita]] to the west. The Winse and [[Gualcarque River]]s pass through the municipality.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|179|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1619__san_francisco_de_ojuera/|title=San Francisco de Ojuera|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|784.3|m}} above sea level, San Francisco de Ojuera has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Aw). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|21.04|C}} and receives about {{cvt|288.39|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/santa-barbara/san-francisco-de-ojuera|title=San Francisco de Ojuera, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, San Francisco de Ojuera had a total population of 7,000 inhabitants, of whom 3,595 (51.3%) were men and 3,404 (48.7%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

About 1.9% of the population were classified as Indigenous and the rest as non-indigenous.<ref name="CP"/> By broad age group, 39.5% of the population were aged 0–14 years, 54.5% were aged 15–64, and 6% were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 19.7 years and the mean age was 25.4 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 19%, lower than the departmental average of 22.2%. The municipality had 1,602 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 4.3 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19}}

The major economic activity is [[agriculture]], and major crops include [[coffee]], [[maize]], [[bean]]s, and vegetables.<ref name="Red"/>
