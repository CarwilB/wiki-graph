The '''Simón Rodríguez Municipality''' is one of the twenty-nine municipalities that composes the [[Táchira]] state in the [[Andes]] of [[Venezuela]]. Its [[municipal seat]] is the town of [[San Simón, Táchira|San Simón]] and its mayor is Carlos Márquez of [[COPEI]] since November 2021. According to the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], the population was 2,526 people.

== History ==
The historical origins of the Simón Rodríguez municipality trace back to 1657, when the region was discovered by [[Cristóbal Rosales]] and [[Manuel de Villalobos]], who named it the Valley of the Yeguines and Buroquias (Spanish: ''Valle Yeguines y Buroquias'') after groups of indigenous people. In 1850, the town [[San Simón, Táchira|San Simón]] was founded near the [[Escalante River (Venezuela)|Escalante River]]. In 1874 it was designated as a municipality and in 1995 elevated to an [[autonomous municipality]], adopting its name in honor of the prominent Venezuelan philosopher and educator [[Simón Rodríguez]].<ref name="Buenas Tareas">{{cite web|url=https://www.buenastareas.com/ensayos/Municipio-Simon-Rodriguez/44865796.html|title=Municipio Simon Rodriguez|trans-title=Simon Rodriguez Municipality|lang=es|website=Buenas Tareas|date=26 November 2013|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Simón Rodríguez Municipality was 2,526 people, accounting for 0.21% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref> By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Simón Rodríguez as 2,658 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.64% since 2011 and showing a population density of 38.52 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2025__sim%C3%B3n_rodr%C3%ADguez/|title=Simón Rodríguez (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 52.1% men (1,275) and 47.9% women (1,170). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 64.7% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 26.5% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 8.8%. The municipality is completely rural, with 100% of the population living in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (59.9%) and [[Mestizo]] (38.7%). There was also a minority groups of 34 [[Afro-Venezuelans]] individuals (1.4%). The [[literacy rate]] was 88.7%, with 231 inhabitants of Simón Rodríguez not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Government==
The first mayor of the Simón Rodríguez Municipality was Francisco Márquez, who was elected in 1995.<ref name="Buenas Tareas"/> The current mayor is Carlos Márquez of the [[COPEI]] party who was first elected in November 2021.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/regional/oposicion-en-el-tachira-gano-16-alcaldias/|title=Oposición en el Táchira ganó 16 alcaldías|trans-title=Opposition in Táchira Wins 16 Mayoral Races|lang=es|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|first=Rosecny|last=Zambrano|date=23 November 2021|access-date=18 May 2026|quote=Municipio Simón Rodríguez por la oposición gana Carlos Márquez con 981 votos. [In the Simón Rodríguez Municipality, Carlos Márquez of the opposition won with 981 votes.]}}</ref> He was re-elected for another term in July 2025.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/politica/oficialismo-gana-21-alcaldias-en-tachira-oposicion-obtiene-8/|title=Oficialismo gana 21 alcaldías en Táchira, oposición obtiene 8|trans-title=Ruling party wins 21 mayoralties in Táchira, opposition wins 8|lang=es|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|first=Maryory|last=Bustamante|date=30 July 2025|access-date=18 May 2026|quote=Simón Rodríguez: Carlos Márquez (reelección) [Simón Rodríguez: Carlos Márquez (re-election)]}}</ref>

The Simón Rodríguez Municipality is divided into seven ''[[barrio]]s'', with the town of [[San Simón, Táchira|San Simón]] serving as the [[municipal seat]] and administrative center. The other six are Buroquia, Capacho, El Alto, Loma del Rodeo, Mesa Grande, and Mesa Seca.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://callejero-venezuela.openalfa.com/municipio-simon-rodriguez|title= Callejero de Municipio Simón Rodríguez, Estado Táchira |trans-title=Street map of Simón Rodríguez Municipality, Táchira State|lang=es|website=Callejero Venezuela|access-date=18 May 2026}}</ref>
