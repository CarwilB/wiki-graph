'''Rojas Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 23,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2050|sqkm|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Rojas, Buenos Aires|Rojas]], which is around {{convert|220|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
