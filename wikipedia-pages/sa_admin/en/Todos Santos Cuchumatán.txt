'''Todos Santos Cuchumatán''' ({{IPA|es|ˈtoðos ˈsantos kutʃumaˈtan}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]]. It is situated in the [[Sierra de los Cuchumatanes]] at an elevation of 2,500 [[metre|m]] (about 8,000&nbsp;ft). The municipality covers an area of approximately 269&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is formed by the town of Todos Santos Cuchumatán, 6 villages, and 69 smaller rural communities, called ''caserios'', ''parajes'', and ''cantones''.<ref name="diagnostico">{{cite web |url=http://www.inforpressca.com/todossantos/diagostico_socioeconomico_todos_santos.pdf |title=Todos Santos Cuchumatán - Diagnóstico Socioeconómico, potencialidades productivas y propuestas de inversión |accessdate=2009-02-02 |author= |year=2004 |publisher=Universidad de San Carlos, Facultad de Ciencias Sociales, EPS}}</ref>  The village of [[San Martin Cuchumatán]] is the second largest urbanized centre in the municipality of Todos Santos. San Martin is also the agricultural heart of the Cuchumateca valley which produces crops like potatoes, broccoli and large scale cultivation of coffee on the lower mountain slopes of the municipality.

The population of Todos Santos is predominantly [[Indigenous people of the Americas|indigenous]], of [[Maya peoples|Mayan]] descent, most of whom still speak the [[Mayan language]] of [[Mam language|Mam]]. The town is one of few places in Guatemala where indigenous men still wear their traditional clothing, along with the women.<ref>{{cite book

An annual festival is celebrated on October 31–November 2, and is centered on [[All Saints' Day]] on November 1 ("Todos Santos" translates to "all saints" in English). Festivities include traditional dances, [[marimba]] music and the famous horse races.  The horse races are often the scene of mayhem and bloodshed due to the riders' penchant to drink alcohol for days leading up to the races even though the mayor banned the selling of hard alcohol in May 2008.<ref>{{cite news| url=http://in.reuters.com/article/lifestyleMolt/idINTRE5A14XN20091102?sp=true | archive-url=https://archive.today/20120710113357/http://in.reuters.com/article/lifestyleMolt/idINTRE5A14XN20091102?sp=true | url-status=dead | archive-date=July 10, 2012 | work=Reuters | title=Moonshine turns Mayan horse race in Guatemala rowdy | date=November 2, 2009}}</ref>

In May 2000, a group of tourists from Japan and a bus driver were attacked by a large mob<ref>{{Cite web |last=Miranda |first=Ricardo |date=August 4, 2000 |title=Ugly lynchings in Guatemala a legacy of war |url=https://www.theglobeandmail.com/news/world/ugly-lynchings-in-guatemala-a-legacy-of-war/article1041564/ |website=The Globe And Mail}}</ref> after members of the group took pictures of local children. The Guatemalan bus driver was doused with gasoline and burned to death, and one member of the Japanese tour group was stoned to death. Two other tourists were injured along with two police officers.<ref>{{Cite web |last=City |first=Ibon Villelabeitia in Guatemala |date=2000-05-01 |title=Mayan mob kills two 'baby stealers' |url=http://www.theguardian.com/world/2000/may/02/5 |access-date=2022-12-08 |website=the Guardian |language=en}}</ref>

While visiting the market in Todos Santos, travelers are advised to exercise discretion when taking photographs. It is important to respect the privacy and cultural sensitivities of the local Mayan community. Photography of individuals—particularly children—should only be done with explicit permission. A notable incident occurred in 2000, when a misunderstanding involving a tourist resulted in a fatality. Persistent local rumors regarding child abduction have contributed to heightened concerns, and as a result, residents are understandably protective of their families. To prevent miscommunication or unintended offense, visitors are encouraged to seek consent before taking photographs.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Traditions of Todos Santos Cuchumatan - Travel Guatemala {{!}} Anywhere |url=https://www.anywhere.com/guatemala/attractions/todos-santos-cuchumatanes-traditional-market |access-date=2025-05-21 |website=www.anywhere.com |language=en}}</ref>
