'''Rivadavia''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northwest of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], on the border with [[La Pampa Province]]. It has an area of {{convert|3940|sqkm|abbr=out}}, and a population of 15,452 ({{census-ar|2001}}).

[[File:Palacio Municipal de Rivadavia (Argentina).jpg|thumb|left|200px|Palacio municipal]]

The partido, created in 1910, has the town of [[América, Buenos Aires|América]] as its capital, and its main economic activities are [[cattle]], and the cultivation of [[wheat]], [[maize]] and [[soya bean]]s.

==Name==
[[File:Busto de Rivadavia en América (Argentina).jpg|thumb|left|170px|Statue of Bernardino Rivadavia in América]]

The partido is named in honour of [[Bernardino Rivadavia]], a veteran of the [[Argentine War of Independence]] and [[President of Argentina]] 1826–1827.
