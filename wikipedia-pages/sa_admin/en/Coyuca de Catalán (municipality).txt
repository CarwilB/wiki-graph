''' Coyuca de Catalán ''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]], being the largest municipality in Guerrero. The municipal seat lies at [[Coyuca de Catalán]]. The municipality covers an area of 921.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 41,975.<ref>{{cite web|title=Coyuca de Benítez|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12022a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930021813/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12022a.htm|archive-date=September 30, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>

There were 234 confirmed cases and 24 deaths related to the [[COVID-19 pandemic in Mexico|COVID-19]] as of February 24, 2021.<ref>{{cite web |title=Coronavirus (COVID-19) en Coyuca de Catalán, Guerrero |url=http://www.gncys.com/covid19/covid19-municipio.aspx?edoid=GR&munid=022 |website=gncys.com |access-date=February 26, 2021 |language=es-mx |date=1 February 2020}}</ref>

==Organized crime==
A group of gunmen entered ''Puerto El Bálsamo'', burning 60 houses and looting 15 {{lang|es|vinatas}} ([[mezcal]] factories) in 2020. 
320 mezcal producers left the town of ''Zihuaquio'' on January 26, 2020 after attacks by criminal groups operating in the area. Mezcal provides income in several towns, but due to constant confrontations between alleged drug traffickers, people have had to leave and cannot harvest their crops.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Ocampo Arista |first1=Sergio |title=Destruye la delincuencia la industria del mezcal en Zihuaquio |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/03/01/estados/destruye-la-delincuencia-la-industria-del-mezcal-en-zihuaquio/ |access-date=March 1, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=March 1, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>

Two women who had been kidnapped by ''[[La Familia Michoacana]]'' and eight members of the cartel were killed in a confrontation between members of the cartel and townspeople of Hacienda de Dolores on February 25, 2021.<ref>{{cite news |title=Enfrenta pueblo guerrerense a La Familia Michoacana; ocho muertos |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/02/26/estados/enfrenta-pueblo-guerrerense-a-la-familia-michoacana-ocho-muertos/ |access-date=February 26, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=February 26, 2021 |language=es}}</ref>

Residents of Los Guajes de Ayala used social media in June 2021 to ask for federal help after three days of being held hostage by ''La Familia Michoacana''. The area is in the middle of the gold and the drug trade.<ref>{{cite web |title=Sitiados por el crimen organizado, piden auxilio pobladores de Coyuca de Catalán |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/estados/2021/6/10/sitiados-por-el-crimen-organizado-piden-auxilio-pobladores-de-coyuca-de-catalan-265753.html |website=www.proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |access-date=June 10, 2021 |language=spanish}}</ref>
