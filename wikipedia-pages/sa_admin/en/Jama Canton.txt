'''Jama Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Manabí Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Jama, Ecuador|Jama]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 20,230.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
