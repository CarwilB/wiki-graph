The '''Atures Municipality''' ({{langx|es|Municipio Atures}})  is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the southern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Amazonas (Venezuelan state)|Amazonas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 104,228.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city of [[Puerto Ayacucho]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Atures Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Amazonas.zip {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The city of Puerto Ayacucho was founded to facilitate the transport of goods past the [[Atures Rapids]] on the [[Orinoco|Orinoco River]] in the late 19th century (mostly [[rubber]]).{{cn|date=December 2022}}

==Demographics==
The Atures Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 91,386 (up from 74,066 in 2000). This amounts to 64.3% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality's population density is {{convert|20.3|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/amazonas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
[[File:Venezuela-tobdelaselva.JPG|thumb|Tobogan de la Selva, Atures Municipality]]

==Government==
The mayor of the Atures Municipality is Mireya Labrador, elected on October 31, 2004, with 39% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=1&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/22/alcaldedis=0&edo=22&mun=1&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |website=www.cne.gob.ve}}</ref> She replaced Angel Rodriguez shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Archived copy |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref> The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Fernando Girón Tovar, Luis Alberto Gómez, Parhueña, and Platanillal (previous to December 18, 1997, the Atures Municipality contained only a single parish).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
