'''Saint-Raphaël''' ({{langx|ht|Sen Rafayèl}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord (Haitian department)|Nord]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 169,867 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017|archive-date=6 November 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151106110552/http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|url-status=live}}</ref> Postal codes in the Saint-Raphaël Arrondissement start with the number 14.

==Communes==
The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Dondon]]
* [[La Victoire, Haiti|La Victoire]]
* [[Pignon]]
* [[Ranquitte]]
* [[Saint-Raphaël, Haiti|Saint-Raphaël]]

==History==
In the wake of the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]], food had become short in supply. On 31 January 2010, a food shipment sent through the Pignon Airport, for an orphanage in the commune of [[Pignon]], was looted just after leaving the airport gates. A negotiation between aide shippers and the local mayor lead to a partitioning of food aide between the orphanage and the rest of the population.<ref>New York Times, [https://www.nytimes.com/2010/02/05/world/americas/05planes.html "Volunteers Fly Supplies Into Hard-to-Reach Areas"] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100521142810/http://www.nytimes.com/2010/02/05/world/americas/05planes.html |date=2010-05-21 }}, '''Shaila Dewan''', ''4 February 2010'' (accessed 4 February 2010)</ref>
