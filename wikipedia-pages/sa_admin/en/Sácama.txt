'''Sácama''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsakama}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Sacama has a very wet [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af''), although it borders very closely on the [[tropical monsoon climate]] (''Am'') found further east.
