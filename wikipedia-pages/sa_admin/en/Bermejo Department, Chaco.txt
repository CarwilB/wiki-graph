'''Bermejo''' is the easternmost [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 24,000 inhabitants in an area of  2,562&nbsp;km², and its capital city is [[La Leonesa]], which is located around 100&nbsp;km from the provincial capital.
