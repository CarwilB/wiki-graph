'''Aracitaba''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 2,059 people living in a total area of {{cvt|105.885|km2}}. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of [[Juiz de Fora]].
