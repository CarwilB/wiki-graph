'''Tocumen''' is a city and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Panamá District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 74,952 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 47,032; its population as of 2000 was 83,187.<ref name=inec/> It is the site of the [[Tocumen International Airport]].

== Climate ==
According to the [[Köppen Climate Classification]] system, Tocumen has a [[tropical savanna climate]], abbreviated ''Aw'' on climate maps.
