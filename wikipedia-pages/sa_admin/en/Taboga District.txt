'''Taboga''' ({{IPA|es|taˈβoɣa}}) is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Panamá Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 1,402;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (for 2019) is 1,387.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|12.1|km2}}. The capital lies at the town of [[Taboga, Panama|Taboga]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

Taboga is an island district that includes the islands of Taboga, Taboguilla, Otoque, Urabá, Melones, Chamá, and Estivá. Taboga itself is {{convert|8|mi}} long and is {{convert|12|mi}} away from the mainland.

It was the home of [[Francisco Pizarro]], where he built boats for his conquest of the Incas in 1539.

==Administrative divisions==
Taboga District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[Taboga, Panama|Taboga]] (capital)
*[[Otoque Occidente]]
*[[Otoque Oriente]]
