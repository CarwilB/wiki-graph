'''Medellín''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. The municipal seat is the town of [[Medellín, Veracruz|Medellín]].

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the central zone of the state, about 100&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 398.2 km2.  The municipality of Medellín is delimited to the north by [[Veracruz, Veracruz|Veracruz]], to the north-east by [[Boca del Río, Veracruz|Boca del Río]], to the east by [[Alvarado (municipality)|Alvarado]], to the south by [[Tlalixcoyan]] and to the west by [[Jamapa]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30105a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20130111235857/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30105a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2013-01-11|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Medellín|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-10}}</ref>

The weather in Medellín is warm all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Demographics==

{{As of|2020}}, Medellín Municipality had a population of 95,202, with over 155 localities. However only 3% lives in the municipal seat (2,820 hab.). The biggest localities as 2020 are Puente Moreno (34,913 hab.), El Tejar (11,144 hab.), Arboledas San Ramón (8,894 hab.), Paso del Toro (5,787 hab.) and Arboledas San Miguel (4,159 hab.).<ref name="MEDELLINCENSUS2020">{{cite web |url=http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Medell%C3%ADn.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf|title=Censo Medellín de Bravo 2020 |language=es |access-date=August 1, 2023 }}</ref>

==Agriculture==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[green chile]], [[rice]], [[watermelon]], [[mango]] and [[pineapple]].

==Culture==
In Medellín, in September takes place the celebration in honor to San Miguel Arcangel, Patron of the town.
