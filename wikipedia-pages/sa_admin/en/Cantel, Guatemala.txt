'''Cantel''' is a municipality in the [[Quetzaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is located {{Convert|11|km|mi|abbr=on}} east of the city of [[Quetzaltenango]] and has an area of {{Convert|52.6|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}. The town of Cantel has a population of 47,941 (2023 census),<ref>{{Cite web |title=Cantel (Municipality, Guatemala) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/quetzaltenango/0914__cantel/ |access-date=2023-11-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> and is situated at an altitude of {{Convert|2,370|m|ft|abbr=on}}.

Cantel includes the small [[Maya civilization|Maya]] archaeological sites of [[Chojolom]] and [[Cerro Quiac]].
