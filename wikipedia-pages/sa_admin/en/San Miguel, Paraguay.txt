'''San Miguel''' is one of the ten districts of the [[Misiones Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. San Miguel is known as the ''Wool Capital''.

== Geography ==

San Miguel is located 178&nbsp;km south from [[Asunción]], the capital city of Paraguay. it is reached by [[Route 1 (Paraguay)|Route Number 1]] from Asuncion.
