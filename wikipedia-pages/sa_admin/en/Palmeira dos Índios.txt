'''Palmeira dos Índios''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[pɐwˈmeɾɐ dʊˈzĩdʒʊ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]].  {{as of|2010}}, it has a population of around 70,000.

The city is situated in the interior of [[Alagoas]]. The Brazilian writer [[Graciliano Ramos]] was its mayor in 1927. It is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Palmeira dos Índios]].

== History ==
Palmeira dos Índios was originally inhabited by the [[Xukuru people]]. It was established as a [[Freguesia|parish]] in 1798 and transformed into a town in 1835.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Palmeiras dos Índios |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/palmeira-dos-indios/historico |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191020101623/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/palmeira-dos-indios/historico |archive-date=2019-10-20 |access-date=2026-06-13 |website=[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

==Languages==
[[Xocó language|Xukuru-Kariri]] was spoken in Palmeira dos Índios.<ref name="Meader-Nordeste">{{cite book |last=Meader |first=Robert E. |url=https://www.silbrazil.org/resources/archives/16988 |title=Indios do Nordeste: Levantamento sobre os remanescentes tribais do nordeste brasileiro |year=1978 |publisher=[[SIL International]] |location=Brasilia |language=pt |archive-date=2024-10-08 |access-date=2020-01-23 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241008181259/https://www.silbrazil.org/resources/archives/16988 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
