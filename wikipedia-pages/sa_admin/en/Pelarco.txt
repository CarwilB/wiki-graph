'''Pelarco''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Talca Province]], [[Maule Region]] of [[Chile]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|331.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, Pelarco had 7,266 inhabitants; of these, 1,822 (25.1%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 5,444 (74.9%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 3,714 men and 3,552 women. The population fell 5.0% (382 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pelarco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Alfredo Pérez Leiva ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Munitel"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pelarco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pablo Lorenzini ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Pedro Pablo Alvarez-Salamanca ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 38th electoral district, together with [[Curepto]], [[Constitución, Chile|Constitución]], [[Empedrado, Chile|Empedrado]], [[Pencahue]], [[Maule, Chile|Maule]], [[San Clemente, Chile|San Clemente]], [[Río Claro, Chile|Río Claro]] and [[San Rafael, Chile|San Rafael]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Juan Antonio Coloma Correa (UDI) and [[Andrés Zaldívar|Andrés Zaldívar Larraín]] (PDC) as part of the 10th senatorial constituency (Maule-North).
