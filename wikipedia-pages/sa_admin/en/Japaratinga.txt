'''Japaratinga''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ʒɐpɐɾɐˈtĩɡɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 8,403 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/japaratinga/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is {{cvt|86|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Cidades@ |access-date=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Japaratinga is located along the coast, near the eastern-most point of the main South American continent.

===Climate===
Japaratinga has a [[tropical forest]], characterized by high rainfall, with definitions setting minimum normal annual rainfall between 2,000&nbsp;mm (about 78&nbsp;inches or 2 meters) and 1,700&nbsp;mm (about 67&nbsp;inches). The soil can be poor due to high rainfall leaching out soluble nutrients.

==Demographics==
As of the census of 2005, there were around 6,727 people living in Japaratinga. According to the census, the racial makeup of the city was majority [[White people|White]] and [[Multiracial]]; minority [[Black people|Black]] or [[African American]]. People of [[Portuguese people|Portuguese]], [[Italian people|Italian]], [[Spanish people|Spanish]], [[French people|French]], [[United States|American]] and [[English people|English]] descent form the largest ethnic groups in the city.

==Economy==
Japaratinga has a number of Hotels and Pousadas (small hotels). Tourism is the most important economic activity in the city.
