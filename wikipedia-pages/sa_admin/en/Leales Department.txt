'''Leales Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 51,090 (2001) and an area of 2027 km². The seat of the department is in [[Bella Vista, Tucumán|Bella Vista]].
