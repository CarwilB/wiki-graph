'''San Lorenzo District''' is one of the districts of [[Central Department]], Paraguay. It was officially established in 1775. As per the 2022 census, the district had a population of 225,395 inhabitants.

==History==
The early settlements were established in 1604, when people started populating the agricultural lands of Ñu Guazú between the San Lorenzo river and [[Fernando de la Mora|Tapyipery]], then known as Balsequillo.<ref name="San">{{cite web|title=SanLo - Reseña Histórica|work=Municipalidad de San Lorenzo|url=https://www.sanlo.gov.py/index.php/contacto/mi-ciudad-mi-pueblo|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=SanLo - Historical Review}}</ref><ref name="ABC">{{cite web|title=La ciudad se originó en 1604|work=ABC Color|url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/comercial/aniversario-san-lorenzo-2025/2025/08/10/la-ciudad-se-origino-en-1604/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=The city originated in 1604}}</ref> During the 17th century, the population gradually increased, with encomiendas established by settlers such as Francisco Cuevas and Juan Vanguelles. In 1698, the [[Jesuit]] priest Martín de Yegros donated his lands in the region to the Jesuit College of Asunción, which would result in land disputes between the Jesuits and local inhabitants during the early 18th century. In 1704, [[Governorate of Paraguay|Governor]] Antonio Escobar y Gutiérrez conducted a registration of the residents. Historical accounts state that the locals supported [[José de Antequera y Castro]] during the Communer Revolution in 1724.<ref name="ABC"/> During the early years, the local economy relied heavily on the sale of [[firewood]] used by steamships navigating the [[Paraguay River|Paraguay]] and [[Paraná River]]s.<ref name="San"/>

After the [[Suppression of the Society of Jesus|expulsion of the Jesuits]] in 1767, Agustín Fernando de Pinedo officially founded the town in 1775 under the authority of the Spanish Crown, which later became the district of San Lorenzo del Campo Grande.<ref name="San"/><ref name="ABC"/> As there is no definitive founding date that exists, 10 August, the feast day of the patron saint, [[Saint Lawrence|San Lorenzo]], was later adopted as the official anniversary date.<ref name="San"/><ref name="ABC"/><ref name="UHP">{{cite web|title=San Lorenzo alista un fin de semana de patronales con jornadas repletas de actividades|work=Última Hora|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/san-lorenzo-alista-un-fin-de-semana-de-patronales-con-jornadas-repletas-de-actividades|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=San Lorenzo prepares a patron saint festival weekend packed with activities}}</ref>  In the 20th century, the city was linked to [[Asunción]] by the famous Lechero Train.<ref name="San"/>

==Geography==
San Lorenzo is a [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] located in the [[Central Department]] in [[Paraguay]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|54.08|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/central/1114__san_lorenzo/|title=San Lorenzo |work=City population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The city of San Lorenzo is known as the "University City" as it hosts the main campus of the [[National University of Asunción]].<ref name="ABC"/><ref name="San"/>

==Demographics==
As per the 2022 census, San Lorenzo had a population of 225,395 inhabitants of which 110,085 were males and 115,310 were females. The entire population was classified as urban. About 21.3% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 9.1% was more than 65 years of age.<ref name="CP"/>
