'''Sobradinho''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil.

== History ==

<!-- Do not copy anything to Wikipedia, it is not allowed. Even from official websites! Read: Wikipedia:Things not to do! Do not remove this notice! -->
The history of Sobradinho begins in 1825, with the arrival of [[São Paulo (state)|Paulista]] João Lopes, who climbed the [[Serra Geral (southern Brazil)|Serra Geral]] and settled on the banks of a stream, where he built a wooden two-story house (sobrado) on the road connecting [[Rio Pardo]] to [[Soledade (Rio Grande do Sul)|Soledade]]. There, he established a [[trading post]] to serve travelers. The house also served as a reference point for the [[muleteers]] passing through.

On December 3, 1927, it became emancipated from the municipality of Soledade, of which it was part as the Fourth District. Initially named Jacuhy, it was later renamed Sobradinho.

== Geography ==
The municipality of Sobradinho is located in the [[Centro-Serra region]]. It borders the municipality of [[Arroio do Tigre]] to the north; [[Lagoa Bonita do Sul]] to the south; [[Passa Sete]] and [[Segredo (Rio Grande do Sul)|Segredo]] to the east; and [[Ibarama]] to the west.

The altitude of the municipal seat is 427 meters; longitude −53.029 and latitude −29.421. It covers an area of 130 square kilometers, and its estimated population in 2008 was 14,675 inhabitants.<ref name=autogenerated1 />

== Economy ==
The main industrial products manufactured in Sobradinho are upholstery, dairy products, leather clothing, fabric and knitwear, footwear, wood processing, wooden shoe lasts, wooden pegs for tobacco drying in kilns, [[metallurgy]], meat, and wine.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.centroserra.com.br/site/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=64&Itemid=208 Centro-Serra Online – Main products]{{dead link|date=March 2025}}</ref>

The main agricultural products are tobacco (2,000 ha), beans (270 ha), corn (1,800 ha), soybeans (1,000 ha), and grapes (70 ha), while the primary livestock product is milk (1,440,000 liters per year).<ref name=autogenerated1 />

== Tourism ==
The main tourist attractions in Sobradinho are the Bean Festival<ref>[http://www.indoviajar.com.br/brasil/rs/sobradinho/festa-do-feijao.htm IndoViajar – Bean Festival]</ref> and House of Culture<ref>[http://www.indoviajar.com.br/brasil/rs/sobradinho/casa-da-cultura.htm IndoViajar – House of Culture]</ref>
== Education ==
The municipality has four state-run elementary schools, seven municipal elementary schools, one high school, and one college.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
