'''Tolhuin Department''' ({{langx|es|Departamento Tolhuin}}) is a [[Departments of Argentina|department of Argentina]] in [[Tierra del Fuego Province, Argentina|Tierra del Fuego Province]], [[Argentina]]. Previously part of the [[Río Grande Department]], it was established as a separate department on October 27, 2017, by law of the provincial legislature.<ref>[http://www.legistdf.gov.ar/blog/post.php/3450 LEGISLATURA: El Parlamento sancionó la creación del departamento Tolhuin] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171219133019/http://www.legistdf.gov.ar/blog/post.php/3450 |date=2017-12-19 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.lalicuadoratdf.com.ar/2017/10/legislatura-parlamento-sanciono-la-creacion-del-departamento-tolhuin/|title=LEGISLATURA: El Parlamento sancionó la creación del departamento Tolhuin|date=27 October 2017}}</ref> [[Tolhuin]] is the capital city of the department.<ref>[https://ushuaia24.com.ar/noticia/2198/la-legislatura-aprobo-la-creacion-del-departamento-de-tolhuin La Legislatura aprobó la creación del departamento de Tolhuin]</ref>
