'''Campechuela''' is a municipality and town in [[Granma Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located on the southern shore of the [[Gulf of Guacanayabo]].

==History==
The town of Campechuela was founded in 1869. It became the seat of the municipality in 1912.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Cabecera (Campechuela town), Ceiba Hueca, Cienaguilla, La Gloria, [[San Ramón (Campechuela)|San Ramón]] and Tana.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/ocampechuela/index.htm|title=Campechuela|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-06|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Campechuela had a population of 41,719.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|577|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|72|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Economy==
The economy is based on [[agriculture]] (with [[sugarcane]] and fruit farms) and [[Livestock|stock]] raising.
