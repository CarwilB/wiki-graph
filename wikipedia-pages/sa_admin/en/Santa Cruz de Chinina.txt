'''Santa Cruz de Chinina''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chepo District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,572 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,954; its population as of 2000 was 1,715.<ref name=inec/>

In 2013, the corregimiento Río Lagarto was created by splitting in two the corregimiento Santa Cruz de Chinina.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Navarro |first=Dayana |date=9 April 2013 |title=Chepo tendrá ahora 10 corregimientos |url=https://www.laestrella.com.pa/panama/nacional/10-chepo-tendra-corregimientos-GLLE315641 |access-date=2025-11-24 |website=www.laestrella.com.pa |language=es-PA}}</ref>

In 2025, the construction of a $29-million bridge crossing the [[Bayano River|Bayano river]] in Chinina was finalized.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Arcia |first=José |date=27 June 2025 |title=Agricultores y ganaderos reclaman apertura de puente vehicular sobre el río Bayano |url=https://www.laestrella.com.pa/panama/agricultores-y-ganaderos-reclaman-apertura-de-puente-vehicular-sobre-el-rio-bayano-BB14044764 |access-date=2025-11-24 |website=www.laestrella.com.pa |language=es-PA}}</ref>
