'''Tamasopo''' is a [[Municipalities of San Luis Potosí|municipality]] and town in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[San Luis Potosí]]. The town is located at {{coord|21|55|21|N|99|23|33|W}}. The municipality has an area of {{convert|1329|sqkm|sqmi}} and a population of 28,848 in 2010, including the population of the town of Tamasopo with 4,326 people.<ref>SEDESOL,  http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/zap/datGenerales.aspx?entra=nacion&ent=24&mun=036, accessed 4 Apr 2013</ref>
[[File:Plaza tamasopo.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The main plaza in the town of Tamasopo.]]

==Geography==

Tamasopo is located in the foothills of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]].  The town has an elevation of {{convert|1,200|ft|m}}.  The surrounding mountains rise to about 5,000 feet (1,500&nbsp;m) above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>   Tamasopo is famous for its waterfalls in a lush rain forest

'''El Puente de Dios''' is two miles (3&nbsp;km) northwest of Tamasopo town.  It consists of waterfalls into a narrow gorge and cavern beneath an arch through which the Gallinas river runs rapidly.  Blue and clear water pools for swimming are at the top and bottom of the cavern. The '''Cascada de Tamasopo''' is two miles (3&nbsp;km) north of the town.  It features three cascades tumbling about {{convert|20|m|ft}} into pools divided by [[travertine]] ledges and shelves. The character of the waterfalls reminds some travelers of [[Havasu Falls]] in the [[Grand Canyon]].<ref>"Waterfalls of the Huasteca"http://trans-americas.com/blog/2010/04/huasteca-waterfalls/, accessed 7 Apr 2013</ref>  Nearby is '''El Trampolin''' which consists of small waterfalls falling into clear, deep pools in the midst of a tropical forest.<ref>Conaway, William J. ''Driving Adventures in the Mexican Huasteca'' William J. Conaway, 1995</ref>

The climate of Tamasopo is classified as sub-tropical Cwa (Koppen) or Cwal (Trewartha).

==History==

Tamasopo is located in the [[Huasteca]] region, named after the indigenous people of the area. [[Indigenous people of the Americas|Indigenous people]] number 4,461 people in the municipality of whom 2,950 speak an indigenous language.<ref>"Tamasopo" http://www.en.nuestro-mexico.com/San-Luis-Potosi/Tamasopo/, accessed 4 Apr 2013</ref>  The indigenous population belongs mostly to two groups: the [[Huastec people|Huastecas]] who traditionally lived in the southern portion of the municipality and the [[Pame people|Pame]] who lived in the northern part of the municipality.<ref>"Enciclopedia de Municipios" {{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_sanluispotosi |title=E-Local -San Luis Potos? |accessdate=2007-04-01 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070401165115/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_sanluispotosi |archivedate=2007-04-01 }}, accessed 4 Apr 2013</ref>
