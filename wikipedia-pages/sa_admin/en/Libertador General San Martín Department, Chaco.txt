'''Libertador General San Martín''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located on the north eastern border of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 54,500 inhabitants in an area of  7,800&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[General José de San Martín, Chaco|General José de San Martín]], which is located around 1,125&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

The department and its ''cabecera'' (capital) are named after [[José de San Martín]], the General that led Argentina to victory over the [[Spanish Empire]] in the [[Argentine War of Independence]].
