'''Huayllay District''' (Quechua ''Wayllay'' recreation meadow, lush grove, [[Jarava ichu|ichu]] variety) is one of thirteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Pasco Province|Pasco]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
