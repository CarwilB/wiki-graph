'''Itaporanga d'Ajuda''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|itapoˈɾɐ̃ɡɐ daˈʒudɐ|lang}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeastern]] state of [[Sergipe]]. It covers {{convert|757|km2|mi2}}, has a population of 34,709 (2020) and a population density of 46 residents per km<sup>2</sup> (120/sq mi).<ref name="i"/> It is home to the [[Tejupeba House and the Chapel of the Colégio Sugar Plantation]], a plantation house and chapel cited as the first Jesuits settlement in Portuguese Brazil.

The municipality contains part of the [[Serra de Itabaiana National Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional da Serra de Itabaiana|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=147|accessdate=2016-05-03}}</ref>

== Toponym ==

"Itaporanga" is a [[Tupi language]] term meaning "beautiful stone". It is formed by the word ''itá'', meaning stone, and ''poranga'', meaning beautiful. "D'Ajuda" is a reference to the patron of the municipality, [[Our Lady of Help]] (''Nossa Senhora da Ajuda'').<ref name="na"/>
