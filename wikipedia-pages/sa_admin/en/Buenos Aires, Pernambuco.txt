'''Buenos Aires''' is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located 78&nbsp;km from the state capital of [[Recife]], it has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2025]]) population of 13,276 people.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]] (Coastal zone)
* Boundaries - [[Vicência]]    ('''N''');  [[Carpina]]   ('''S''');  [[Limoeiro]]   ('''W'''); [[Nazaré da Mata]]   ('''E''')
* Area - 96.69&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 149&nbsp;m
* [[Drainage basin]] - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.1&nbsp;°C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 78&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Buenos Aires''' are based in industry, [[commerce]] and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[poultry]] and [[cattle]].
