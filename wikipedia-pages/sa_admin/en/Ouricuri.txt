'''Ouricuri''' is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in the mesoregion of ''Sertão Pernambucano''. According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], in 2025, Ouricuri has a total area of 2,381 square kilometers and had an estimated population of 68,489 inhabitants  .<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Araripina]], [[Trindade, Pernambuco|Trindade]] and [[Ipubi]] ('''N'''), [[Santa Cruz, Pernambuco|Santa Cruz]] and [[Santa Filomena, Pernambuco|Santa Filomena]] ('''S'''), [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]] and [[Bodocó]]  ('''E'''), [[Piauí]] state ('''W''')
* Area - 2,423&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 451 m
* Hydrography - [[Brigida River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]
* Climate - Semi-arid ([[Sertao]]) hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 25.5 c
* Main road -  BR 232 and BR 316
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 621&nbsp;km

==Economy==

{{Further|Largest Cities of Northeast Region, Brazil}}
The main economic activities in '''Ouricuri''' are based in extraction of gypsum and no metallic minerals, and primary sector especially creation of goats, donkeys, pigs and farms with beans, manioc and corn. Ouricuri is located in the micro region of [[Araripina]] which contains 95% of the Brazilian reserves of [[Gypsum]].
