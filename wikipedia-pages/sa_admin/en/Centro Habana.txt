'''Centro Habana''' is one of the 15 [[Municipio|municipalities]] in the city of [[Havana]], Cuba. A [[chinatown]], ''Barrio Chino'', is also located here. It is a smaller municipality of Havana, and it has the highest [[population density]].

Centro Habana is divided into five ''consejos populares'' (wards): [[Cayo Hueso, Havana|Cayo Hueso]], [[Colón, Havana|Colón]], [[Dragones, Havana|Dragones]], [[Los Sitios]] and [[Pueblo Nuevo, Havana|Pueblo Nuevo]].

It is the part of the city located in the polygon bounded by the [[Malecón, Havana|Malecón]], the [[Paseo del Prado, Havana|Paseo del Prado]], Maximo Gómez, Arroyo and Infanta streets.

==History==
The infrastructure of the city, built 450 years ago, heavily deteriorated during the 1990s after the collapse of the [[Cuba–Soviet Union relations|Cuban–Soviet]] trade partnership. In 1996, restoration projects were started to improve housing and infrastructure in the Cayo Hueso community.<ref>[http://www.idrc.ca/es/ev-5486-201-1-DO_TOPIC.html Restoration Project] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070311033912/http://www.idrc.ca/es/ev-5486-201-1-DO_TOPIC.html |date=March 11, 2007 }} - Canada International Development Research Centre</ref>

Centro Habana was established as an administrative division in 1963 and later formally made its own ''municipio'' in 1976.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Bartolomé Bárguez |first1=Carlos |title=Ciudad de La Habana. La identidad de la provincia y sus municipios |url=http://www.polgalvan.sld.cu/contenido/historiamcpio.htm |language=es |date=2006 |access-date=2018-12-03 |archive-date=2018-12-18 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181218203839/http://www.polgalvan.sld.cu/contenido/historiamcpio.htm |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2004, the municipality of Centro Habana had a population of 158,151.<ref name=atenas>{{Cite web |url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060714001016/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm |archive-date=2006-07-14 |title=2004 Population trends, by Province and Municipality |author=Atenas.cu |year=2004 |access-date=2007-10-06 |language=es |url-status=dead }}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|4|km2|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| access-date=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|39537.8|PD/km2}}. In 2023 Centro Habana had a population of 126.105.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.onei.gob.cu/anuario-estadistico-de-cuba-2023 |format=pdf |title=Anuario Estadístico de Cuba 2023 |language=es |date=2024 |access-date=2025-04-24 }}</ref> It has a population density of {{convert|36872|PD/km2}}.
