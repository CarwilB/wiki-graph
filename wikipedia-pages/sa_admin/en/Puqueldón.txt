'''Puqueldón''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chiloé Archipelago]] which is part of [[Chiloé Province]] and [[Los Lagos Region]].  The commune is located on [[Lemuy Island]] and consists of eight villages the biggest of which is Puqueldón. Puqueldón was one of the towns to receive the [[Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation]]s [[scholarship]] in 2002.

==History==
Puqueldón which gained town ("Villa") status at the end of the 18th century was mentioned as Pucolón in a document written in 1776.<ref>Juan Mancilla Pérez: ''Pueblos de Chiloé'', p. 54. Castro 2008</ref> The place is known for the strong faith of its people. Contemporary Spanish documents speak of a chapel here in 1785 and of a rustic village in 1787. In the later year a census established the population as 1,521 [[Spaniard]]s and 74 natives. In the [[Viceroyalty of Peru|colonial period]] Puqueldón was under military government and had 4 towns of which Ichuac had the greatest population. Finally, in 1826, it became a department of [[Chiloé Province|Chiloé]]. By 1924 the town had a chapel, town square, post office and telegraph services, a civil registry and public schools. The present church at Plaza de Armas was built in 1940.

== Architecture ==
[[Image:Iglesia de Puqueldón.jpg|thumb|240px|left|[[Christian Church|Church]]]]
As in the rest of the archipelago, the population of Puqueldón were inspired by a deep religious faith and built 8 [[Christian Church|churches]], each special in its own way. Some, like [[Church of Ichuac|Ichuac]], [[Church of Aldachildo|Aldachildo]] and [[Church of Detif|Detif]] are more than 100 years old and have recently been declared [[World Heritage Sites]], along with 15 other [[Chiloé Province|Chilote churches]]. Some have picturesque cemeteries with little shingled [[mausoleums]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Puqueldón spans an area of {{convert|97.3|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 4,160 inhabitants (2,006 men and 2,154 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population fell by 2.1% (88 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Puqueldón is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Elson Carcamo Barría ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Puqueldón is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gabriel Ascencio ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and Alejandro Santana ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 58th electoral district, together with [[Castro, Chile|Castro]], [[Ancud]], [[Quemchi]], [[Dalcahue]], [[Curaco de Vélez]], [[Quinchao]], [[Chonchi]], [[Queilén]], [[Quellón]], [[Chaitén]], [[Hualaihué]], [[Futaleufú, Chile|Futaleufú]] and [[Palena, Chile|Palena]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Kuschel Silva (RN) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
