'''El Monte''' is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Talagante Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. As of 2007, it had a population of 29,568.

==Geography==
El Monte can be found on the north bank of the [[Mapocho River]] in the [[Chilean Central Valley]] approximately {{convert|55|km|abbr=on}} southwest of the metropolitan area of [[Santiago, Chile|Santiago]]. The commune spans an area of {{convert|118.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], El Monte spans an area of {{convert|118.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and had 26,459 inhabitants (13,334 men and 13,125 women). Of these, 22,284 (84.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 4,175 (15.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 20.9% (4,577 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, El Monte is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012–2016 alcalde is Francisco Gómez Ramírez ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> The communal council has the following members:
* Juan Pablo Gómez Ramírez ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Rossana Sanhueza Muñoz (IND)
* Mario Varela Montero ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Guisela Rosales Reyes (IND)
* Andres Maass Reyes ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Rolando Ortega Valdivia ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], El Monte is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[Alhué]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
