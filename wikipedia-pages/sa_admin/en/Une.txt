'''Une''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Cundinamarca Department]], [[Colombia]], located in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]]. It is situated 27 miles (43 km) southeast of [[Bogotá]], the country's capital.<ref name="Semana2025">{{cite web |url=https://www.semana.com/turismo/articulo/el-pueblo-de-cundinamarca-con-el-nombre-mas-corto-tiene-solo-tres-letras-y-es-una-joya-oculta-por-descubrir/202540/ |title=The town of Cundinamarca with the shortest name |publisher=Semana Magazine |date=2025-07-27 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

Considered one of the oldest municipalities in Cundinamarca, Une is notable for having the shortest name in the department, consisting of only three letters.<ref name="ElPais2025">{{cite web |url=https://www.elpais.com.co/turismo/los-encantos-del-pueblo-con-el-nombre-mas-corto-de-cundinamarca-un-destino-que-enamora-con-sus-espectaculares-paisajes-2814.html |title=The charms of the town with the shortest name in Cundinamarca |publisher=El País |date=2025-07-27 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

== Etymology ==

=== Linguistic origin ===
* Language family: [[Chibchan languages|Chibchan]]
* Language: [[Muisca language|Muysccubun]]

=== Meaning ===
The [[toponym]] "Une" in ''Muysccubun'' ([[Muisca language]]) has several meanings according to historical sources:
# "Pot" or "meeting center of the chiefdom" of [[Ubaque]].<ref name="ColTurismo">{{cite web |url=http://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/CUNDINAMARCA/MUNICIPIOS/UNE/UNE.htm |title=Une Cundinamarca |publisher=Colombia Tourism Web |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>
# "Good thing," according to Acosta Ortegón's dictionary.<ref name="Municipality">{{cite web |url=http://www.une-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |title=General information about Une |publisher=Municipality of Une |access-date=2015-05-01 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150517055638/http://www.une-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |archive-date=2015-05-17 }}</ref>
# "Sky," according to other interpretations of the Chibchan language.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

The [[toponym]] "Queca," name of a village (''vereda'') in the municipality, in the [[Muisca language]] (Quyca) means "earth," "world," or "territory." It is also related to the idea of "people" or "homeland" in the context of their culture.<ref name="Municipality"/>

== History ==

=== Pre-Columbian era ===
The territory of Une was part of the [[Ubaque]] Chiefdom in [[Muisca]] times, which belonged to the [[Muisca Confederation]] of [[Bacatá]].<ref name="HistoriaUne">{{cite web |url=https://ruborpuebloscundinamarca.blogspot.com/2011/02/une-apuntes-de-historia.html |title=Une - Historical Notes |publisher=Towns of Cundinamarca |date=2011-02-06 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref> The [[Muisca people|Muisca]] left important archaeological remains in the region, including [[rock art]] and [[petroglyphs]] that are still preserved in several villages throughout the municipality.<ref name="RockArt">{{cite web |url=https://rupestreweb.tripod.com/arguello.html |title=Rock art in southwestern Cundinamarca, Colombia |publisher=Rupestre Web |date=2003 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Colonial foundation (1538-1600) ===
The municipality was founded on February 22, 1538 by Diego Romero de Aguilar, a member of [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]]'s expedition.<ref name="Municipality"/> He also served as its first [[encomienda|encomendero]].

In 1594, [[oidor]] Miguel de Ibarra recorded a population of 1,058 [[indigenous peoples|indigenous people]].<ref name="ColTurismo"/> On June 19, 1600, Oidor Luis Enríquez determined through an order issued in [[Bogotá|Santafé]] to settle the indigenous people of Queca and Une in a single town, unifying the two communities.<ref name="Archive">{{cite web |url=http://censoarchivos.mcu.es/CensoGuia/archivodetail.htm?id=1746704 |title=Municipal Archive of Une Cundinamarca |publisher=Archive Census-Guide |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Colonial development (1600-1779) ===
During this period, the first church was built with the help of mason Juan Robles, which was erected as a parish in 1692, with Juan de Dios Ramos as its first priest.<ref name="Archive"/> The church was repaired in 1629 and 1630 at the request of visitor Lesmes de Espinosa, repairs that were approved by the [[Council of Castile]] counselor Juan de Valcárcel on January 12, 1630.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

In 1779, according to the statistical report by Francisco Antonio Moreno y Escandón, the population consisted of 181 whites and 675 indigenous people. During this time, a hospital house was developed for the care of indigenous people.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

=== Municipal status (1779) ===
On January 3, 1779, Une was officially established as a municipality, separating administratively from other colonial jurisdictions.<ref name="FamilySearch">{{cite web |url=https://www.familysearch.org/en/wiki/Une,_Oriente,_Cundinamarca,_Colombia_-_Genealogy |title=Une, Oriente, Cundinamarca, Colombia - Genealogy |publisher=FamilySearch |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== 19th century and early modernization ===
In 1898, the [[telegraph]] was inaugurated, marking the beginning of modern communications in the municipality.<ref name="ColTurismo"/> On July 26, 1936, the [[telephone]] was installed, significantly improving communications with the outside world.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

The first night school dates from 1912, directed by Adriano Beltrán Pérez, established through City Council Agreement No. 1 of 1912.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

==== Infrastructure construction ====
* 1916: Construction of the current church began on August 15, with the first stone laid by parish priest Luis Francisco Luque. The work was directed by master builder Antonio Camargo.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>
* 1922: On July 20, the municipal slaughterhouse was inaugurated.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>
* 1924: On June 13, [[electric lighting]] service was activated at 6 PM, produced by a municipal plant built by a Society of neighbors.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>
* 1928: Construction of the towers of the current church was completed, under the direction of Father Eugenio Celis.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>
* 1927–1932: The road to Caraza was built. The work was surveyed in 1927 by engineers Hernando Parra Lleras and Ezequiel Sánchez, with Dr. Ruperto Melo as Governor. It began on October 12, 1928, and was inaugurated on December 17, 1932.<ref name="ColTurismo"/>

=== 20th century educational development ===
In February 1941, the school was founded by professor Fidel Leal, an illustrious son of the municipality. The institution was private until 1946 and became municipal in 1947.<ref name="IEDHistory">{{cite web |url=https://fidelealybernabe-edu.wixsite.com/fidelealybernabe-edu/institucion |title=IED Fidel Leal y Bernabé Riveros - History |publisher=Educational Institution |date=2021 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

==== Educational timeline ====
* 1952: Primary sections and the first two years of high school were approved.
* 1961 and 1963: Third and fourth years of high school were approved respectively.
* 1963: Bernabé Riveros School was founded by Dr. Bernabé Riveros.
* 1968: On November 26, it merged with Bernabé Riveros School, becoming the "Integrated Departmental School Fidel Leal and Bernabé Riveros" of Une.
* 1973: On June 13, La Inmaculada School was integrated, founded in 1942 by the Discalced Carmelite Missionary Sisters.
* 1972: On July 4, basic secondary and vocational secondary education was officially approved.

=== Une in national context ===
Une participated in national historical events, being known during the [[Colombian Conservative Party|Conservative]]-[[Colombian Liberal Party|Liberal]] [[bipartisanship]] period as the "Red Star of the East" due to its liberal political tradition in a predominantly conservative region.<ref name="ColombiaB">{{cite web |url=https://colombiabacana.com/un-pueblo-liberal-rodeado-de-conservadores/ |title=A liberal town surrounded by conservatives |publisher=Colombia Bacana |date=2018-11-30 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref> During the [[Thousand Days' War]], the municipality played an important role due to its strategic location on routes to the [[Llanos|Eastern Plains]].<ref name="ColombiaB"/>

== Geography ==
Une is located in the [[Eastern Province, Cundinamarca|Eastern Province]], on the foothills of the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Cordillera]]. Its geographic position is approximately {{coord|4|24|7|N|74|1|31|W}}.<ref name="WeatherSpark">{{cite web |url=https://en.weatherspark.com/y/23339/Average-Weather-in-Une-Colombia-Year-Round |title=Weather in Une, Colombia |publisher=Weather Spark |date=2018 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Boundaries ===
Une is bounded by [[Chipaque]] to the north; [[Cáqueza]], [[Fosca, Cundinamarca|Fosca]], and [[Gutiérrez, Cundinamarca|Gutiérrez]] to the east; Gutiérrez and [[Bogotá]] to the south; and Bogotá and Chipaque to the west.<ref name="Municipality"/>

=== Physical characteristics ===
* Elevation: 7,795 feet (2,376 meters) above sea level<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Area: 81.5 square miles (211 km²)<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Average temperature: 61°F (16°C)<ref name="Climate">{{cite web |url=https://www.clima.com/colombia/cundinamarca/une |title=Climate in Une |publisher=Clima.com |date=2024 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>
* Climate: Temperate mountain climate with two rainy seasons
* Hydrography: The municipality is crossed by the Negro River and has several water bodies, including the famous Chocolate Reservoir

== Economy ==

=== Agriculture ===
Une's economy is mainly based on [[agriculture]], standing out as one of the municipalities with the highest [[potato]] production in Colombia.<ref name="YouTubeEcon">{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5_yryz1f6W4 |title=Une Cundinamarca - Agricultural Economy |publisher=YouTube |date=2024-07-18 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref> Sixty percent of its area is dedicated to crops, with potato being the main product, followed by:

==== Main crops ====
* Potato: The product that supplies several municipalities in eastern Cundinamarca and part of Bogotá.<ref name="YouTubeEcon"/>
* Cilantro
* Onions
* Carrots
* Peas
* Corn
* Various vegetables<ref name="ColombiaAS">{{cite web |url=https://colombia.as.com/actualidad/este-es-el-pueblo-de-cundinamarca-que-solo-tiene-3-letras-y-ofrece-estos-sitios-de-interes-y-actividades-n/ |title=This is the town in Cundinamarca that has only 3 letters |publisher=AS Colombia |date=2025-03-13 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Primary sector ===
According to the National Federation of Potato Growers, Une is among the Cundinamarca municipalities with the greatest aptitude for potato cultivation, especially the Diacol Capiro variety destined for the processing industry.<ref name="FEDEPAPA">{{cite web |url=https://fedepapa.com/home/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Regional-Cundinamarca.pdf |title=Regional Cundinamarca - Potato Sector |publisher=FEDEPAPA |date=2024 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Livestock ===
Livestock and dairy production are also important economic activities, especially the raising of [[Normandy cattle|Norman cattle]], recognized at annual livestock fairs.<ref name="ColombiaAS"/>

=== Tourism ===
In recent years,{{when?|date=September 2025}} tourism has gained relevance as an economic activity, attracting visitors interested in:
* Rural and ecological tourism
* Cultural and heritage tourism  
* Religious tourism
* Gastronomic tourism<ref name="ColombiaAS"/>

== Cultural heritage ==

=== Rock art and archaeology ===
Une preserves important vestiges of Muisca [[rock art]], including [[petroglyphs]] located in different villages of the municipality.<ref name="RockArt"/> Among the most important sites are:
* Timasita village petroglyphs: Three stones with red pictograms showing Muisca occupation of the territory.<ref name="Timasita"/>
* Petroglyphs in various villages, which form part of the archaeological legacy of the Cundiboyacense highlands.<ref name="RockArt"/>

=== Royal roads ===
Une preserves vestiges of the old colonial [[Wiktionary:camino real|Royal Roads]] that connected [[Bogotá|Santafé de Bogotá]] with the [[Llanos|Eastern Plains]], constituting an important [[cultural heritage]] of the region.<ref name="RoyalRoads">{{cite web |url=https://www.elespectador.com/turismo/rutas-historicas-de-colombia-asi-puede-visitar-un-camino-real/ |title=Historical routes of Colombia: how to visit a Royal Road |publisher=El Espectador |date=2025-08-13 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Handicrafts ===
The municipality maintains artisanal traditions typical of Cundinamarca, especially:
* Textile in sheep wool
* Handicrafts in wood
* Traditional jewelry
* Leather work<ref name="Crafts">{{cite web |url=https://artesaniasdecolombia.com.co/PortalAC/Publicacion/laboratorio-de-innovacion-y-diseno---cundinamarca_8931 |title=Handicrafts of Cundinamarca |publisher=Handicrafts of Colombia |date=2024-10-15 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

== Municipal symbols ==

=== Coat of arms ===
The municipal coat of arms comprises a circle divided by two oblique lines, which when joined at the center represent the meaning of the municipality's name:
* Upper section: Sun's radiance and a tractor, symbolizing wealth, work, and agricultural mechanization. The blue borders represent the sky.
* Lower section: A gear representing industry and progress. It includes elements of rural life that provide development and sustenance to the municipality.
* Left side: A star and scales. The red background represents love for the homeland, and the scales symbolize equity and justice.
* Right side: A family representing rural lineage and family unity.
* Motto: "''Land of Peace and Progress''"<ref name="Municipality"/>

=== Flag ===
Consists of three horizontal stripes:
* Red: Love for the homeland
* White: Tranquility and peace  
* 'Green: Goodness and fertility of its fields<ref name="Municipality"/>

== Tourist attractions ==

[[File:Plaza de toros de Une (01).jpg|left|thumb|200px|Leopoldo Romero Cubillos Bullring]]
[[File:Municipio de Une-Cundinamarca.jpg|thumb|167px|Gustavo Moya Ángel Sports Complex]]

Une offers various tourist attractions that combine nature, history, and culture:

=== Natural sites ===
* La Chorrera [[Waterfall]]: Crystal-clear waterfall born in the [[Sumapaz Páramo]], in the San Salvador sector, with two tiers, the first 66 feet (20 meters) high<ref name="LaChorrera">{{cite web |url=https://www.une-cundinamarca.gov.co/turismo/la-chorrera-une |title=La Chorrera Waterfall |publisher=Municipality of Une |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>
* Chocolate Reservoir: Magical water body surrounded by lush vegetation, perfect for landscape contemplation<ref name="Semana2025"/>
* Sanctuary Cave: Rock formation of speleological interest<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Devil's Stone: Geological formation surrounded by popular legends<ref name="ColombiaAS"/>

=== Historical and cultural sites ===
* Our Lady of Conception Parish: Construction began in 1916 and finished in 1928<ref name="ColTurismo"/>
* Calvary Hill: [[Scenic viewpoint|Viewpoint]] and [[Christian pilgrimage|pilgrimage]] site located in Villa Natalia neighborhood<ref name="Semana2025"/>
* Old Royal Roads: Vestiges of colonial routes to the Plains<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Cultural Center: Hub for cultural and educational activities in the municipality<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Petroglyphs and indigenous rock art: In various villages throughout the municipality<ref name="RockArt"/>

=== Sports and recreation infrastructure ===
* Leopoldo Romero Cubillos Bullring: Coliseum where bullfighting and cultural events are held<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Gustavo Moya Ángel Sports Complex: Municipal sports complex<ref name="Municipality"/>
* Central park: Public space for recreation and citizen gatherings

== Festivals and events ==

=== Annual fair and festival ===
From December 12-16, the traditional Une Fair and Festival is celebrated, which includes:<ref name="FairUne">{{cite web |url=https://www.flotalamacarena.com/inicio/ferias-y-fiestas-en-une-2024-tradicion-cultura-y-diversion/ |title=Fair and Festival in Une 2024 |publisher=Flota La Macarena |date=2024-12-09 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

* Musical shows with renowned artists
* Norman Cattle Exhibition: Livestock fair of the Norman breed
* Traditional events: Cavalcades, livestock market, and cultural activities
* Ox Team Exhibition: Traditional parades and competitions
* VIII Expo-Oriente Art: Regional art exhibition

=== Religious festivals ===
* Patron Saint Festival (December 7): In honor of the Immaculate Conception<ref name="PatronFest">{{cite web |url=https://www.une-cundinamarca.gov.co/calendario-de-actividades/fiestas-patronales-une-cundinamarca |title=Une Patron Saint Festival |publisher=Municipality of Une |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>
* Festivals in honor of Saint Anthony of Padua
* Our Lady of Mount Carmel festivities<ref name="Semana2025"/>

== Health services ==

=== Health services ===
The municipality has the Timoteo Riveros Cubillos Health Center ESE of Une, which provides first-level care services.<ref name="ESEUne">{{cite web |url=http://esecentrodesaludtrcune.gov.co |title=Timoteo Riveros Cubillos Health Center ESE |publisher=ESE Une |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Doctor in Your Territory Program ===
Since 2024, Une benefits from the departmental program "Doctor in Your Territory" of the Cundinamarca Governorate, which guarantees:
* 24-hour medical care 7 days a week
* Specialized consultations in gynecology, pediatrics, and internal medicine
* Telemedicine services with higher complexity hospitals<ref name="DoctorTerritory">{{cite web |url=https://www.cundinamarca.gov.co/noticias/servicio-medico-24-horas-llegara-a-55-municipios-de-cundinamarca-que-nunca-lo-han-tenido |title=24-hour medical service will reach 55 municipalities |publisher=Cundinamarca Governorate |date=2024-07-18 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

== Public services ==

=== Administration ===
The public services of water supply, sewerage, and waste management are handled by the Office of Public Services for Water Supply, Sewerage, and Waste Management of the Municipality of Une Cundinamarca, directed by Leidy Mariela Castro Sierra.<ref name="PublicServices">{{cite web |url=https://www.une-cundinamarca.gov.co/directorio-institucional/oficina-servicios-publicos |title=Public Services Office |publisher=Municipality of Une |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

=== Energy services ===
Electricity is supplied by the Cundinamarca Energy Company S.A. ESP, which is part of the departmental public services network.<ref name="EPC">{{cite web |url=https://epc.com.co |title=Public Companies of Cundinamarca |publisher=EPC |date=2024 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

== Climate ==

Une enjoys a temperate [[mountain climate]], characterized by:

=== Climate ===
* Average temperature: 61°F (16°C)<ref name="Climate"/>
* Average maximum temperature: 66°F (19°C)
* Average minimum temperature: 50°F (10°C)<ref name="WeatherSpark"/>
* Elevation: 7,795 feet (2,376 meters) above sea level
* Rainfall pattern: Bimodal (two rainy seasons per year)
* Average relative humidity: 76%<ref name="Climate"/>

=== Seasons ===
* Dry season: December to April (most recommended time for tourism)
* Rainy seasons: April-June and September-November
* Cloud cover: The sky remains partly cloudy during most of the year<ref name="WeatherSpark"/>

== Gastronomy ==

Une offers traditional Cundiboyacense cuisine that includes:

=== Typical dishes ===
* Potato preparations (being the main local product)
* Traditional homemade kumis from the region<ref name="Crafts"/>
* Artisanal dairy products from local Norman cattle
* Preparations with local horticultural products
* Traditional rural cuisine from the eastern Cundinamarca region

A complete gastronomic inventory of the municipality was conducted in 2018 as part of academic studies on local culinary heritage.<ref name="Gastronomy">{{cite web |url=https://repositorio.uniagustiniana.edu.co/bitstreams/e40828a0-8e52-44af-ae80-019e8b5e3457/download |title=Gastronomic inventory of Une municipality |publisher=Agustiniana University |date=2018 |access-date=14 September 2025}}</ref>

== Notable personalities ==

=== Founders and early settlers ===
* Diego Romero de Aguilar: Founder and first encomendero (1538)
* Juan de Dios Ramos: First priest of the parish established in 1692

=== Educators and benefactors ===
* Fidel Fulgencio Leal Cruz: Founder of the municipal school (1941), considered an illustrious son of the municipality
* Bernabé Riveros: Founder of the school that bears his name (1963)
* Timoteo Riveros Cubillos: Important figure in local health development (the health center bears his name)

=== Politicians and administrators ===
* Leopoldo Romero Cubillos: Mayor 2004-2007, the bullring bears his name
* Urías Romero: Mentioned in the municipal anthem as a local historical figure
