'''El Parral''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]], located approximately {{convert|45|km}} south of the state capital of [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez]].

==Geography==
The municipality of El Parral is located in the [[Chiapas Depression dry forests|Chiapas Depression]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Chiapa de Corzo, Chiapas|Chiapa de Corzo]] to the north, [[Venustiano Carranza, Chiapas|Venustiano Carranza]] to the east, [[Villa Corzo]] to the south, and [[Villaflores, Chiapas|Villaflores]] to the west. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|365.50|km2}}.<ref name=area/>

The generally flat terrain of El Parral mostly consists of farmland and pastureland, although isolated patches of forest and jungle remain.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/biblioteca/ficha.html?upc=702825292942 |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Villa Corzo, Chiapas |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref> The southeastern part of the municipality borders the Angostura Reservoir, the largest reservoir in Mexico in terms of total capacity,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/territorio/agua/presas.aspx?tema=T |title=Presas |language=es |website=Cuéntame |publisher=INEGI |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref> created by the [[Angostura Dam (Mexico)|Angostura Dam]] (officially called the Belisario Domínguez Dam) on the [[Grijalva River]].

==History==
José Félix Flores Arellano is credited with founding the community of El Parral. He worked as a manager on a farm in the area in 1927 and was asked by locals to serve as President of the local Executive Agrarian Committee, which oversaw the construction of the area's first basic infrastructure such as roads, water supply, a telephone office and a school. El Parral was part of the municipality of Villa Corzo until 23 November 2011, when the decree raising El Parral to the level of an independent municipality was gazetted.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=23 November 2011 |title=Decreto Número 008 |url=http://www.sgg.chiapas.gob.mx/po2006/archivos/descargas.php?f=P.O.%20337%20SEGUNDA%20SECCION.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Chiapas, Segunda Sección |access-date=2 November 2020 }}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of El Parral recorded a total population of 14,171 inhabitants.<ref name=ageeml/>

There are 157 localities in the municipality, of which only the municipal seat, also known as El Parral, is designated as urban. It recorded a population of 10,865 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=ageeml/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
Economic activities in El Parral include agriculture and fish farming.<ref>{{cite news |date=13 October 2019 |url=https://www.chiapasencontacto.com/entrega-rutilio-escandon-apoyos-agricolas-y-acuicolas-a-productores-de-el-parral/ |title=Entrega Rutilio Escandón apoyos agrícolas y acuícolas a productores de El Parral |language=es |magazine=Chiapas en Contacto |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref>

The paved Chiapas Highway 157 runs north–south through the municipality, connecting El Parral with the municipalities of Chiapa de Corzo to the north and Villa Corzo to the south.<ref>{{cite map |url=http://www.ceieg.chiapas.gob.mx/productos/files/MAPASMUNDC/Base_El_parral.pdf |title=Municipio de El Parral |year=2019 |scale=1:100,000 |publisher=CEIEG |access-date=2 November 2020}}</ref> [[Tuxtla Gutiérrez International Airport]] is located about {{convert|20|km}} north of El Parral.
