'''Andradas''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020 its population is estimated to be 41,396.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/andradas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is known as "Land of Wine", thanks to Italian immigrants who brought viticulture to the city. It is also known for extreme sports like paragliding, hang-gliding, parachute, and more. Every year athletes from all over the world go to city practice their sport.
