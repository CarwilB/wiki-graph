'''Gros-Morne''' ({{langx|ht|Gwo Mòn}}) is an [[arrondissement]] in the [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Adegbosin |first1=Adeyinka |last2=Warnken |first2=Jan |last3=Sun |first3=Ying |title=Mapping the quality of basic and comprehensive emergency obstetric care services in Haiti |journal=International Journal for Quality in Health Care |date=October 20, 2021 |volume=33 |issue=4 |doi= |url=https://academic.oup.com/intqhc/article/33/4/mzab143/6406584 |access-date=February 6, 2024}}</ref>

It includes three [[List of communes of Haiti|communes]]: [[Anse-Rouge]], [[Gros-Morne, Haiti|Gros-Morne]], and [[Terre-Neuve, Haiti|Terre-Neuve]].
