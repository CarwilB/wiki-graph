'''Taquaritinga do Norte''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˌtak͡wɐɾiˈtĩɡɐ ˈdu ˈnɔhti]}}) is a city in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]].

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Paraíba]]  ('''N'''); [[Caruaru]], [[Toritama]] and [[Brejo da Madre de Deus]]  ('''S'''); [[Vertentes]]  ('''E'''); [[Santa Cruz do Capibaribe]]  ('''W''')
* Area - 475.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 774 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[Hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - semi [[arid]] - hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 20.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 164&nbsp;km
* Population - 24,736 ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]])<ref name="IBGE" />

==Economy==
[[Image:Igreja de Santo Amaro (Taquaritinga do Norte).jpg|thumb|right|240px|Santo Amaro Church]]
The main economic activities in '''Taquaritinga''' are based in textile industry, general commerce and [[agribusiness]].
