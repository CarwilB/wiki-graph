'''Floresta''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]], part of the [[Tundama Province]], a subregion of Boyacá. Floresta is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. It borders [[Santa Rosa de Viterbo, Boyacá|Santa Rosa de Viterbo]], [[Cerinza]] and [[Betéitiva]] in the north, in the east [[Busbanzá]] and [[Corrales, Boyacá|Corrales]] in the west Santa Rosa de Viterbo and [[Nobsa]] in the south.

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] of the highlands of Colombia, the area of Floresta was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], organized in their [[Muisca Confederation]]. In those days Floresta, together with Santa Rosa de Viterbo, was called '''Tobasía''' which is still the name of a [[corregimiento]] of the village. Tobasía was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' loyal to the ruler of [[Duitama|Tundama]], present-day Duitama.

Modern Floresta was founded in 1818, three years before the independence of Colombia from the Spanish Empire. Floresta means "between the flowers".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Floresta] - accessed 07-05-2016</ref>

== Economy ==
Main economical activity of Floresta is [[agriculture]], predominantly [[potato]]es, [[maize]] and [[barley]]. Also the Colombian fruit [[uchuva]] is produced in Floresta. The municipality grows grapes, races [[Riesling]] and [[Pinot Noir]] for wine production, uncommon in tropical Colombia. [[Livestock]] and [[dairy]] farming are other production areas.

== Geology ==
The [[List of fossiliferous stratigraphic units of Colombia|fossiliferous]] [[Devonian]] [[Floresta Formation]] is named after Floresta and has provided many [[trilobite]]s and [[brachiopod]] fossils.
