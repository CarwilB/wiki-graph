'''Valencia de Jesús''' is a Colombian town and corregimiento of [[Valledupar]] in the [[Department of Cesar]]. The village is known for preserving one of the oldest churches in the Americas.<ref>[http://www.elpilon.com.co/noticias/not00000769.htm {{in lang|es}} El Pilon: Iglesia de Valencia de Jesús: Un cofre repleto de valores]</ref>

==History==

The village was founded by Captain Antonio Florez in c. 1590 with the name ''Dulce Nombre de Jesus'' but due to constant attacks from the indigenous [[Chimila people|Chimila]], the town was moved closer to Valledupar. The old village was named; Pueblo Viejo (named changed to [[Pueblo Bello]] in the 20th Century) and the new town; Nueva Valencia.<ref>{{Harvnb|Gutierrez Hinojosa|2000|p=119}}</ref>

During the [[Spanish conquest of the Americas|Spanish conquest and colonization of the Americas]] Valencia was a Spanish enclave similar in importance to [[Valledupar]], functioning as seat of its municipality and its territory extended from [[Nabusimake]] to [[El Paso, Cesar|El Paso]].<ref name="GutierrezHinojosa">{{Harvnb|Gutierrez Hinojosa|2000|p=120}}</ref>

Valencia was also one of the most important religious centers in the region for the [[Dominican Order|Community of the Dominicans]] which imposed on the Christian faith on the indigenous. The Parish of Valencia de Jesus back then limited to the north with Valledupar, [[Azúcar Buena I]], to the west with the [[Garupal River]], to the south with [[Codazzi|Espiritu Santo]] in between the [[Cesar River]] up to a locality named Alto Minas.<ref>[http://www.elpilon.com.co/noticias/not00011278.htm {{in lang|es}} El PIlon: Semana Santa en Valencia de Jesús]{{Dead link|date=October 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }} (Archivo Diocesano de Santa Marta)</ref>

During the independence movement in the 19th Century, Valencia was considered a powerful village with an aristocrat base of inhabitants owners of large ''[[hacienda]]s''. After the revolt in Valledupar against the Spanish monarchical authorities, Valencia de Jesus sided with the [[King of Spain]], [[Fernando VII]] organized and confronted the people of Valledupar, who defeated them. After the independence the village decayed and was added to Valledupar.<ref name="GutierrezHinojosa" />
