'''Marshallkreek''' (also '''Marchallkreek'''<ref name="planning"/><!-- both spellings are used in the document-->) is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Brokopondo District]]. Its population at the 2012 census was 1,171.<ref name="census"/> The resort and town are named after Captain Marshall who first settled Suriname in 1630.<ref name="Marshall's">{{cite book|accessdate=16 May 2020|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=paQMAAAAYAAJ&q=marshall's+creek+suriname+river&pg=PA253|title=Encyclopædia Britannica: A Dictionary of ARTS,SCIENCES, and General LITERATURE, Volume XI|year = 1888|edition=Ninth Edition&mdash;Popular Reprint}}</ref>

The resort contains the following villages: Koinakondre, Laizang Kondre, Marshallkreek, Phedra, and Rama.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Onderzoek-Regionaal-Plan-Brokopondo-2015-2040.pdf|title=Onderzoek Regional Plan Brokopondo 2015-2040|access-date=16 May 2020|language=nl|website=Planning Office Suriname}}</ref> There are two schools: one in Marshallkreek and one near the [[Avobakaweg]].<ref name="planning"/> [[Medische Zending]] operates a clinic in Marshallkreek and Phedra.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|title=Zorggebied|website=Medische Zending.sr|access-date=16 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=30 September 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220930070719/http://www.medischezending.sr/mz/zorggebied/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The former plantation Phedra is the agricultural centre of the resort.<ref name="planning"/> In 1960, a [[palm oil]] plantation was based in Phedra,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/p/phedra|title=Plantages / P / Phedra |website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=16 May 2020}}</ref> however the [[Surinamese Interior War]] and [[lethal yellowing]] resulted in the closure of the factory in Victoria, [[Klaaskreek]] in 1996.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2016/11/14/oliepalmproductie-zou-geen-prioriteit-moeten-zijn/|title=Oliepalmproductie zou geen prioriteit moeten zijn|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=15 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Marshallkreek was one of the transmigration villages for the inhabitants of [[Sarakreek]] which were flooded after the construction of the [[Afobaka Dam]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/binn011sran01_01/binn011sran01_01_0005.php|title=Sranan. Cultuur in Suriname|language=nl|website=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|access-date=16 May 2020}}</ref>
