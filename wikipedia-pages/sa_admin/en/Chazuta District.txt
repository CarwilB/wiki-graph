'''Chazuta District''' is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[San Martín Province|San Martín]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> San Martín Province is located in north central Peru, in [[San Martín Region]], a highland area on the edge of the [[Amazon Basin]]. Immediately to the east of San Martín Region is the large lowland region of [[Loreto Region|Loreto]], in the northeast corner of Peru. The area is home to members of the [[Kichwa-Lamista people|Kichwa-Lamista]] ethnicity, who migrated to the area from [[Lamas Province|province of Lamas]] between 1850 and 1920.<ref name="Chaparro 2">{{cite book |last1=Chaparro |first1=Anahí |editor1-last=Ministerio de Desarrollo Agrario y Riego Dirección General de Saneamiento de la Propiedad Agraria y Catastro Rural |title=TERRITORIOS Y RECURSOS NATURALES DE LOS PUEBLOS INDÍGENAS EN LA AMAZONÍA PERUANA |date=2021 |publisher=Ministerio de Desarrollo Agrario y Riego Dirección General de Saneamiento de la Propiedad Agraria y Catastro Rural |location=Lima |pages=107–120 |url=https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/2798646/La%20situación%20de%20tierras%2C%20territorios%20y%20recursos%20naturales%20de%20los%20pueblos%20ind%C3%ADgenas%20en%20la%20Amazon%C3%ADa%20Peruana.pdf |access-date=4 June 2023 |chapter=Los Kichwa de San Martín: impactos de las dinámicas territoriales en las cuencas de los ríos Mayo y Huallaga}}</ref>
