'''Sucre Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and one of five the municipalities that make up the [[Metropolitan District of Caracas]]. According to a 2016 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 691,317.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>  The parish of [[Petare]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Sucre Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |access-date=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archive-date=2007-12-21 }}</ref>

It includes the neighborhood of [[La Carlota, Miranda]] near the [[Generalissimo Francisco de Miranda Air Base]] and the former presidential residence [[La Casona]].

== Name ==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]].

==Demographics==
The Sucre Municipality, according to a 2016 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 691,317 (up from 591,414 in 2000).  This amounts to 2.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>  The municipality's population density  (2016) is about 11,000 people per square mile (4,200/km<sup>2</sup>).<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>
