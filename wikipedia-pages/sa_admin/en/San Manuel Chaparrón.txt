'''San Manuel Chaparrón''' ({{IPA|es|san maˈnwel tʃapaˈron}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jalapa Department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|127.5|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 9,239 inhabitants.

==History== 
The name "Chaparrón" is derived from the name of a tree that bore a yellow fruit situated on the banks of the Chaparrón River.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=San Manuel Chaparrón|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/san-manuel-chaparron/|access-date=24 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> The prefix "San Manuel" is the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for Saint Manuel or [[Saint Emmanuel]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.wisdomlib.org/cities/san-manuel-10266|title=San Manuel|work=Wisdom Library|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The settlement originated from a hacienda, which later became a village called Espinal. The municipality was officially founded by government decree 106 dated 24 November 1873.<ref name="Hist"/>

==Geography==
San Manuel Chaparrón is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Jalapa Department|Jalapa]] in [[Guatemala]]. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|127.5|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=San Manuel Chaparrón (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jalapa/2104__san_manuel_chaparr%C3%B3n/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> Recreational areas are located in the villages of La Ventana, La Peña and Agua Tibia in the municipality.<ref name="Hist"/>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1379.63|m}} above sea level, the municipality has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The average annual temperature is {{cvt|19.55|C}}. The municipality receives an average annual rainfall of {{cvt|13.57|cm}} and has 178.18 average rainy days in a year.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/jalapa/san-manuel-chaparron|title=San Manuel Chaparron|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
San Manuel Chaparrón had an estimated population of 9,239 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 4,511 males and 4,728 females. About 25% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 8.4% was over the age of 65 years. About 59.8% of the population was classified as rural, and the rest (40.2%) lived in urban areas. Most of the residents (79.5%) were born in the same municipality. The municipality had a literacy rate of 81.1%.<ref name="CP"/>

San Manuel Chaparrón has the lowest indigenous population in the department of Jalapa with [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (99.1%) forming the major ethnic group. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (99.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="CP"/> The predominant religion is [[Catholic Church|Catholic]], 
with the annual fair held between 9 and 12 March in commemoration of [[Saint Emmanuel]] (San Manuel).<ref name="Hist"/>
