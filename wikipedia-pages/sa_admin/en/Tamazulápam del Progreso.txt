'''Tamazulapam del Progreso''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]], Mexico.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Pérez García |first=Carina |date=April 2, 2018 |title=Tamazulápam: oasis de la Mixteca, Oaxaca |url=https://www.nvinoticias.com/agenda/cultura/tamazulapam-oasis-de-la-mixteca-oaxaca/27002 |work=NVI Noticias |language=Spanish}}</ref> It is located about {{Convert|150|km|mi|abbr=on}} from Oaxaca City, the state capital.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Flores |first=Rocío |date=2021 |title=Tamazulápam del Progreso apuesta a la conservación de los recursos naturales |url=https://oaxaca.media/2021/09/tamazulapam-del-progreso-apuesta-a-la-conservacion-de-los-recursos-naturales/ |website=Oaxaca Media |language=Spanish}}</ref> In 2010, it had a population of 7,059.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2010 |title=Villa de Tamazulápam del Progreso, Oaxaca |url=https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/34876/Oaxaca_540.pdf |publisher=[[National Council for the Evaluation of Social Development Policy]] |language=Spanish}}</ref>

It is part of the [[Teposcolula District]] in the center of the [[Mixteca Region]].
