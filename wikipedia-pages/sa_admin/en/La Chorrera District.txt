'''La Chorrera''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Panamá Oeste Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 124,656;<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> the latest official estimate (2019) is 199,708.<ref name="ReferenceA">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censo, Panama.</ref> The district covers a total area of {{cvt|770|km2}}. The capital lies at the city of [[La Chorrera, Panama|La Chorrera]].<ref name="Statoids"/>
