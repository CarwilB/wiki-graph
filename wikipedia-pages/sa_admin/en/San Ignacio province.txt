'''San Ignacio''' is one of thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] located in the [[Cajamarca Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of San Ignacio.

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[Ecuador]]
*'''East''': [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]]
*'''South''': [[Jaén Province, Peru|province of Jaén]]
*'''West''': [[Piura Region]]

==Political division==
The province extends over an area of {{convert|4990.30|km2|sp=us}} and is divided into seven districts:

*[[San Ignacio District, Peru|San Ignacio]]
*[[Chirinos District|Chirinos]]
*[[Huarango District|Huarango]]
*[[La Coipa District|La Coipa]]
*[[Namballe District|Namballe]]
*[[San José de Lourdes District|San José de Lourdes]]
*[[Tabaconas District|Tabaconas]]

==Population==
The province has a population of 127,523 inhabitants as of [[Peru 2005 Census|census 2005]].
