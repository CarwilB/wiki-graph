'''Marañón''' is one of eleven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Huánuco Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of Huacrachuco. The province has a population of 23,000 inhabitants as of 2002.

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[La Libertad Region]], [[San Martín Region]]
*'''East''': [[Leoncio Prado Province|province of Leoncio Prado]]
*'''South''': [[Huacaybamba Province|province of Huacaybamba]]
*'''West''': [[Ancash Region]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into three districts, which are:

* [[Cholón District|Cholón]] ([[San Pedro de Chonta]])
* [[Huacrachuco District|Huacrachuco]] ([[Huacrachuco]])
* [[San Buenaventura District, Marañón|San Buenaventura]] ([[San Buenaventura, Peru|San Buenaventura]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (74.84%) learned to speak in childhood, 24.75% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
