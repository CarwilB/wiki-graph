'''Ibimirim''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 28,825 inhabitants and the total area is 1929.34&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />
[[File:Bandeira de Ibimirim.jpg|thumb|right|160px|City Flag]]

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Custódia]] and [[Sertânia]]    ('''N''');  [[Inajá, Pernambuco|Inajá]] and [[Manari, Pernambuco|Manari]]    ('''S''');  [[Tupanatinga]] ('''E''');   [[Floresta, Pernambuco|Floresta]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 2033.59&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 401 m
* Hydrography - [[Moxotó River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]  [[hiperxerófila]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot
* Annual average temperature - 24.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 331.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Ibimirim''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pigs]]s, [[cattle]], [[donkey]]s, [[horse]]s, [[chicken]]s;  and plantations of [[tomato]]es, [[bean]]s, [[mango]]es and [[banana]]s.
