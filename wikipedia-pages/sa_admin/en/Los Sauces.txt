'''Los Sauces''' ({{IPA|es|los ˈsawses}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in the [[Malleco Province]], [[Araucanía Region]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Los Sauces spans an area of {{convert|849.8|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 7,581 inhabitants (3,847 men and 3,734 women). Of these, 3,638 (48%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,943 (52%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population fell by 15.7% (1,414 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Los Sauces is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Ramón Vilches Álvarez ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]), and the council has the following members:<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>
* Jaime Badilla E. ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]/[[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Nelda Gallegos (RN)	
* Pedro Carrillo F. ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Eva Calderón M. ([[Christian Democrat Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Victor Gonzalez (UDI)
* Jose Ortega E. (PDC)

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Los Sauces is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gonzalo Arenas (UDI) and Mario Venegas (PDC) as part of the 48th electoral district, together with [[Angol]], [[Renaico]], [[Collipulli]], [[Ercilla, Chile|Ercilla]], [[Purén]], [[Lumaco]] and [[Traiguén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alberto Espina Otero (RN) and Jaime Quintana Leal ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 14th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-North).
