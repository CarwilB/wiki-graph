'''Machadinho''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It was raised to municipality status in 1959, the area being taken out of the municipality of [[Lagoa Vermelha]].<ref name="lei3716">{{Cite web|url=http://leisestaduais.com.br/rs/lei-ordinaria-n-3716-1959-rio-grande-do-sul-cria-o-municipio-de-machadinho|title=Lei Nº 3716: Cria o Município de Machadinho|publisher=Leis Estaduais|lang=pt|date=16 February 1959}}</ref>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 5,427.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/machadinho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
