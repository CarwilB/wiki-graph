'''Belén''' is one of 11 [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Sucre Province]] in the [[Ayacucho Region|Ayacucho region]] in [[Peru]].

== History ==
Belén District was created by Law No.15231 in Nov 23, 1964, during first term of President [[Fernando Belaúnde]]

==Administrative division==
The populated places in the district are:<ref name=inei/>
* Belen
* Socos
* Cochayocc
* Cuije

==Population==
The population of  Belén is 611 people, 292 men and 319 women.<ref name=inei>[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ INEI] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070601020523/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/ |date=2007-06-01 }}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (79.32%) learnt to speak in childhood, 20.52% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013  }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
