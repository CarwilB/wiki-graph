'''Itaguaçu''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 14,023 (2020) and its area is {{convert|532|km2|mi2}}. Itaguaçu has a population density of 26.6 per square kilometer. Itaguaçu has three districts: Itaimbé, Palmeira and Itaçu.<ref name="i"/>

==Origin of the name==

The name of the municipality is of [[Tupi language|Tupi]] origin: ''ita'', meaning "rock", and ''guaçu'', meaning "large".<ref name="i"/>

==Geography==

Itaguaçu is characterized by several rocky peaks, including Pedra Paulista at {{Convert|600|m|ft}}, Pico do Caparaó at {{Convert|850|m|ft}}, Pedra do Barro Preto, the symbol of the municipality, and several others. Several waterfalls dot the town, notably the Cachoeira do Christófari.<ref name="t"/> Itaguaçu borders the municipalities of [[Baixo Guandu]] to the northeast, [[Colatina]] to the north, [[São Roque do Canaã]] to the east, [[Itarana]] to the south, [[Laranja da Terra]] to the southeast, and [[Santa Teresa, Espírito Santo|Santa Teresa]] to the southeast.<ref name="t"/>

==History==

Itaguaçu was first colonized in the second half of the 14th century by Francisco da Silva Coutinho, Antônio Gonçalves Ferreira, Major José Vieira de Carvalho and José Teodoro de Andrade. The first settlement was on the [[Santa Joana River]], and also called Santa Joana. It was later known as Boa Família and finally as Itaguaçu.<ref name="i"/>

A later wave of settlement came with the [[Italian Brazilian|Italian immigration to Brazil]] of the late 19th century. Several families from San Cassiano de Treviso in the Italian [[province of Treviso]]. They worked for another native of Treviso in Santa Catarina for three years before twelve families moved to Boa Família in 1882.<ref name="i"/>

==Noted places==

The Church of Matriz Nossa Senhora Medianeira de todas as Graças was built in the seat of the municipality in 1953. It was built in the [[Gothic Revival architecture|Gothic Revival]] style with twin [[spire]]s that rise to {{Convert|48|m|ft}}. The Lutheran Church of Palmeira de Santa Joana (''Templo de Palmeira de Santa Joana''), located in the Palmeira district, was built in 1902. It is built in a style typical of Lutheran churches in the region with one tower and three bells.<ref name="t"/>
