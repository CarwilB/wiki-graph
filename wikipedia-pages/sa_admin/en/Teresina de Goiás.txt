'''Teresina de Goiás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in northern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location==
It is part of the region of the statistical micro-region of [[Chapada dos Veadeiros Microregion|Chapada dos Veadeiros]] and is located north of the [[Federal District]] and [[Brasília]].  It is 67&nbsp;km north of [[Alto Paraíso de Goiás]].  The nearest major population center is [[Formosa, Goiás|Formosa]].  Neighboring municipalities are [[Alto Paraíso de Goiás]], [[Cavalcante]], and [[Nova Roma, Goiás]].

The distance to the state capital, [[Goiânia]], is 488&nbsp;km.  Highway connections are made by BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / BR-060 / [[Alexânia]] / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / GO-118 / BR-010 / [[São João d'Aliança]] / [[Alto Paraíso de Goiás]] / BR-010 / GO-118.

Teresina is near the [[Chapada dos Veadeiros National Park]].  Communities of descendants of runaway slaves, called [[Kalunga]], are found nearby.

==The economy==
The economy is based on [[subsistence agriculture]], cattle raising (10,700 in 2006), services, public administration, and small transformation industries.  There were no banking institutions or hospitals in 2007.  There were 50 automobiles, 8 trucks, and 9 pick-up trucks in all of the municipality in 2007.  [http://www.ibge.gov.br/ IBGE]

==Health and education==
*Adult literacy rate: 74.2% (2000) (national average was 86.4%)
*Infant mortality rate: 26.50 (2000) (national average was 33.0).  The rate was 47.7 in 1990.
*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]:  0.672
*State ranking:  227 (out of 242 municipalities)
*National ranking:  3,430 (out of 5,507 municipalities) [http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhgo.htm Frigoletto]
*Schools:  7 with 1,221 students (2006)
*Hospitals: none, but there was one public ambulatory clinic  [https://web.archive.org/web/20060625175338/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/ Seplan]

Teresina was first created as a district of the municipality of [[Cavalcante]] in 1968, becoming dismembered in 1988.
