'''Ixhuatlancillo''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the central zone of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 165&nbsp;km from [[Xalapa]], the state capital. It has a surface of 39.48 km<sup>2</sup> and is located at {{coord|18|54|N|97|09|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Ixhuatlancillo is delimited to the north by [[La Perla, Veracruz|La Perla]], to the east by [[Mariano Escobedo, Veracruz|Mariano Escobedo]], to the south by [[Orizaba]], [[Río Blanco, Veracruz|Río Blanco]] and [[Nogales, Veracruz|Nogales]] and to the west by [[Maltrata]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30081a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Ixhuatlancillo|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070308131732/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30081a.htm|archive-date=2007-03-08|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The weather in Ixhuatlancillo is cool all year with rains in summer and autumn.
==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[coffee]] and [[sugarcane]].
==Culture==
On February 2, each year, there is a celebration in honor of [[Virgen de la Candelaria]], patron of the town.
