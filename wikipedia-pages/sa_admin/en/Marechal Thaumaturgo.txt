'''Marechal Thaumaturgo''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|maɾeˈʃaw tawmɐˈtuʁɡu}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 19,299 and its area is 7,744&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Geography==
The municipality contains 5% of the {{convert|846633|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Divisor National Park]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA da Serra do Divisor – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PARNA da Serra do Divisor|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/pt-br/uc/6170|accessdate=2016-08-08}}</ref>

It contains most of the [[Alto Juruá Extractive Reserve]], created in 1990, and 5% of the [[Alto Tarauacá Extractive Reserve]], created in 2000.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Alto Tarauacá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Alto Tarauacá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/282|accessdate=2016-08-07}}</ref>
