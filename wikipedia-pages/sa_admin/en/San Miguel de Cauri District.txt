== Geography ==
The [[Rawra mountain range|Rawra]] mountain range traverses the district. Some of the highest mountains of the district are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe - UGEL map of the Daniel Alcides Carrión Province (Huánuco Region)</ref>

* Parya Qayqu
* [[Puywanqucha]]
* Qayqu Anka
