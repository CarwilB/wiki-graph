'''San Pedro de Totora''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]].

==Location==
San Pedro de Totora province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 41' and 18° 06' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 33' and 67° 58' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in the north, [[Sajama Province]] in the west and southwest, [[Carangas Province]] in the southeast, and [[Nor Carangas Province]] in the east.

The province extends over 50&nbsp;km from north to south, and 45&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Population==
Main [[idiom]] of the province is [[Aymara language|Aymara]], spoken by 90%, while 72.5% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 3% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992).

The population increased from 4,040 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 4,941 (2001 census), an increase of 22.3%. - 43% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).

99% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 94% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

83.5% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 4% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 12.5% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

64% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 25% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises only one [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]], '''Totora Municipality'''. It is identical to San Pedro de Totora Province.
