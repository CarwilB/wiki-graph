'''Rangel''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) of the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]]. Its capital is the town of [[Mucuchíes]]. It has four parishes: Cacute, La Toma, [[Mucurubá]], and San Rafael.<ref>{{cite web
