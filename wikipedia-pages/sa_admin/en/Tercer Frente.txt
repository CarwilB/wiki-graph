'''Tercer Frente''' ({{IPA|es|teɾˈseɾ ˈfɾente}}, [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "Third Front") is a municipality in the [[Santiago de Cuba Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is centered on the town, and municipal seat, of '''Cruce de los Baños'''.

==Geography==
The municipality is located in the western part of the province, neighboring the province of [[Granma Province|Granma]], and borders with the municipalities of [[Guisa]], [[Jiguaní]], [[Contramaestre]], [[Palma Soriano]] and [[Guamá, Cuba|Guamá]].

It includes the town of Cruce de los Baños and the villages of El Yayal, Manaca and Matias.<ref>{{OSM|r|5919056|Tercer Frente}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Tercer Frente had a population of 30,237.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|364|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}, it has a population density of {{convert|83|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
