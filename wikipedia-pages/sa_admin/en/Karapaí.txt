'''Karapaí''' (locally known as '''Estrella del Norte''') is a municipality in [[Paraguay]], located in the [[Amambay|Amambay Department]]. It is the fifth and newest district created in Amambay.<ref>{{Cite web|last=|first=|date=|title=Ley Nº 5085 / CREA EL MUNICIPIO DE KARAPAÍ EN EL XIII DEPARTAMENTO AMAMBAY Y UNA MUNICIPALIDAD CON ASIENTO EN EL PUEBLO DE KARAPAÍ.|url=https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/4717/ley-n-5085-crea-el-municipio-de-karapai-en-el-xiii-departamento-amambay-y-una-municipalidad-con-asiento-en-el-pueblo-de-karapai|archive-url=|archive-date=|access-date=2020-07-27|website=[[Central Archives and Library of Congress]]|location=[[Asunción]]|language=es}}</ref>
