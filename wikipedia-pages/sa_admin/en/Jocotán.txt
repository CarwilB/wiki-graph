'''Jocotán''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref name=FERN/>

Radio Chortis, a Roman Catholic radio station funded by Belgian and German Catholics, is located in the town. It broadcasts primarily in Spanish, but there are a few hours a week in the [[Ch'orti' language]], which is still spoken in some isolated areas.

On 3 August 2001, Jocotán municipality declared yellow code in the area when it learned about the desperate situation that the rural communities were facing imminent famine; the root cause of the crisis were the short raining season, and the decline in the international coffee price.<ref name=industriall>{{cite web|url=http://www.industriall-union.org/es/archive/imf/la-hambruna-y-la-sequia-golpean-a-guatemala-ya-hay-48-muertos |title=La hambruna y la sequía golpean a Guatemala: ya hay 48 muertos |website=Industriall global union |date=14 September 2001 |language=es |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151020192444/http://www.industriall-union.org/es/archive/imf/la-hambruna-y-la-sequia-golpean-a-guatemala-ya-hay-48-muertos |archive-date=October 20, 2015 }}</ref> [[Alfonso Portillo]]'s government decreed State of Calamity to get international help; officially, 48 deceased were reported, but there were rumors of hundreds of casualties.<ref name=industriall/>
