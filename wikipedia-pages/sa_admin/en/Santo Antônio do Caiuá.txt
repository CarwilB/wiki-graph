'''Santo Antônio do Caiuá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northwestern part of the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,626 (2020 est.) in an area of 219.07&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The municipality was founded in 1961.
