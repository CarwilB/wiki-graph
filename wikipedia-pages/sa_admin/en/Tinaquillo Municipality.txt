The '''Tinaquillo Municipality''' (was known as '''Falcón Municipality''' before 2011) is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to a according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 97.687.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Tinaquillo]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Tinaquillo Municipality.{{Citation needed | date=August 2024}}

==Demographics==
The Tinaquillo Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 90,773 (up from 74,872 in 2000).  This amounts to 30.2% of the state's population. The municipality's population density is {{convert|127.13|PD/sqkm}}.{{Citation needed | date=August 2024}}

==Government==
The mayor of the Tinaquillo Municipality is José Gonzalo Mujica Herrera, elected on October 31, 2004 with 42% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=2&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | title=Alcalde de Municipio - FALCON | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303181545/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=2&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2016-03-03}}</ref>   He replaced Dimas Ramos shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones 30 de Julio de 2000 - Directorio de Alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - Directory of elected Mayors |accessdate=2008-03-15 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]]; Tinaquillo.{{Citation needed | date=August 2024}}
