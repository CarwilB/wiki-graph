'''San Lorenzo''' ({{IPA|es|san loˈɾenso}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It was founded in 1812 year by Spanish families.

This is the birthplace of [[Justo Rufino Barrios]]-who served as [[President of Guatemala]] from 1873 until his death in 1885 and whose native house has been declared as a National Heritage.<ref name=barrios>{{cite journal|title=Estatua del general don Justo Rufino Barrios|date=15 May 1896|location=Guatemala|volume=I|issue=20|journal=[[La Ilustración Guatemalteca]]|publisher=Síguere, Guirola & Cía.|url=https://archive.org/stream/ilustracionguate00guat#page/464/mode/2up|language=es}}</ref>

==Tourism==
{{See also|La Aurora International Airport|Tapachula International Airport}}

San Lorenzo's most representative historic landmark is "Justo Rufino Barrios" hacienda, birthplace of general Barrios and named National Heritage by the government.<ref name=barrios/>

==Geographic location==

San Lorenzo has an area of {{convert|25|km2|abbr=on}}, which makes it one of the smallest municipalities of both [[San Marcos Department]] and [[Guatemala]]. It is located 23&nbsp;km West of [[San Marcos, San Marcos|San Marcos]], the department capital, and is surrounded by San Marcos municipalities.<ref name=segeplan/>
