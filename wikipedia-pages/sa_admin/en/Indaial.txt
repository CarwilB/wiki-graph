'''Indaial''' is a city in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], [[Brazil]]. It is located on the left bank of [[Itajaí-Açu River|Itajaí River]], and 160&nbsp;km from the capital of the state, [[Florianópolis]].

The city is of European origin, with many textiles industries and strong agricultural activity. It was colonized about 1860 by [[Germans]], [[Italians]] and [[Polish people|Poles]]. Before the colonization it was populated by Tapajós and Carijós Indians, that are now names of two big neighborhoods in Indaial.
