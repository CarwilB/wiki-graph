'''Berilo''' ({{IPA|pt|beˈɾilu}}) is a municipality in the northeast of the Brazilian state of [[Minas Gerais]].  {{as of|2020}} the population was 11,872 in a total area of 586&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/berilo/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation is 401 meters.  It is part of the IBGE{{clarify|What is IBGE?|date=December 2019}} statistical meso-region of [[Jequitinhonha]] and the micro-region of [[Capelinha]].  It became a municipality in 1963.

The economy is based on cattle raising and agriculture, with the main crops being coffee, pineapple, sugarcane, and corn.  There were plantations of eucalyptus trees for charcoal production.  In 2005 there were 1830 rural producers but only 1 tractor.  {{as of|2005}} there was one hospital and five public health clinics.  Educational needs were met by 19 primary schools and 2 middle schools.  There were 304 automobiles in 2006, giving a ratio of 40 inhabitants per automobile (there were 813 motorcycles).  There was one bank in 2007.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=January 9, 2007 }}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities are [[Francisco Badaró]], [[Chapada do Norte]], [[Virgem da Lapa]], and [[José Gonçalves de Minas]].  The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 545&nbsp;km.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.aondefica.com/centerleft_mg.asp |title=Distancias Minas Gerais |access-date=2008-07-21 |archive-date=2010-07-07 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100707145655/http://www.aondefica.com/centerleft_mg.asp |url-status=usurped }}</ref> The nearest major population center, [[Minas Novas]] (pop. 30,578 in 2007), is 42&nbsp;km. on secondary roads.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/capelinha/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-07-21 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080426213918/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/capelinha/ |archive-date=2008-04-26 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Social indicators==
Berilo is ranked low on the MHDI{{clarify|What is MHDI?|date=December 2019}} and was one of the poorest municipalities in the state and in the country in 2000.  
*MHDI: .680 (2000)
*State ranking: 636 out of 853 municipalities
*National ranking: 3,298 out of 5,138 municipalities in 2000
*Life expectancy: 70
*Literacy rate: 73 
*Combined primary, secondary and tertiary gross enrollment ratio: .787
*Per capita income (monthly): R$92.00<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>
*Urbanization rate: 23.35 (national rate was 81.25)
*Percentage of urban residences connected to sewage system: 49.50 (national rate was 69.52)
*Infant mortality rate: 10.20 (the national average was 18.91).<ref>[http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/BERILO.pdf Indicadores de Saúde] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161105/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/BERILO.pdf |date=July 6, 2011 }}</ref>

The above figures can be compared with those of [[Poços de Caldas]], which had an MHDI of 0.841, the highest in the state of [[Minas Gerais]].  The highest in the country was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in the state of [[São Paulo]] with an MHDI of 0.919.  The lowest was [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] with an MHDI of 0.467 out of a total of 5504 municipalities in the country {{as of|2004|lc=y}}.  At last count Brazil had 5,561 municipalities so this might have changed at the time of this writing.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>
