'''Cushamen''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the north west of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 17,000 inhabitants in an area of  16,250&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Cushamen]].
The name means ''loneliness'' in the [[Tehuelche language]].
