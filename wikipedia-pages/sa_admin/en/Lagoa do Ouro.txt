'''Lagoa do Ouro''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[laˈɡoˑɐ ˈdu ˈoɾu]}}) (''Golden Pond'') is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]], located {{convert|263|km|abbr=on}} west from [[Recife]].

==History==
In early 1902, Captain Amador José Monteiro of the Brazilian [[National Guard (Brazil)|National Guard]] presented a manifesto to the Municipal Council of the municipality [[Correntes, Pernambuco|Correntes]]. The council gathered on February 9, 1902, and authorized the first fair of the village. The council also approved of the town's name of Igatauá during this meeting. On December 9, 1938, the name was changed to Lagoa do Ouro due to an [[urban legend]] that arose in the village, where [[Gold nugget|nuggets]] or [[gold bars]] were believed to be in a local pond belonging to the estate of João Alves da Silva, who became known as John Gold.<ref>{{cite web |title=Lagoa do Ouro História|language=pt |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/lagoa-do-ouro/historico|website=Cidades.ibge.gov.br |access-date=October 24, 2018}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality contains part of the [[Pedra Talhada Biological Reserve]], a fully protected conservation unit created in 1989.<ref>{{citation |title=Plano Operativo de Prevenção e Combate aos Incêndios Florestais da Reserva Biológica de Pedra Talhada |date=November 2006 |author=Helaeson de Almeida |language=pt |url=http://www.ibama.gov.br/phocadownload/prevfogo/plano_operativo_reserva_biolgica_de_pedra_talhada.pdf |access-date=April 16, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120824182250/http://www.ibama.gov.br/phocadownload/prevfogo/plano_operativo_reserva_biolgica_de_pedra_talhada.pdf |archive-date=2012-08-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

===Climate===
Lagoa do Ouro has a [[tropical savanna climate]] (''Aw'') according to the [[Köppen climate classification]].

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Lagoa do Ouro are based in [[agribusiness]]. [[Beans]], [[manioc]], [[maize|corn]] and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[sheep]] and [[poultry]] are the main agricultural resources within the municipality.
