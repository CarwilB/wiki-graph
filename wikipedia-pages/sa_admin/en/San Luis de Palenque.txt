'''San Luis de Palenque''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]], [[Colombia]]. The town is on a bend of the [[Pauto River]], a tributary of the [[Meta River]].

It is served by [[San Luis de Palenque Airport]].
