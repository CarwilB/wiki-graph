''' Huazalingo ''' is a town and one of the 84 [[municipalities of Hidalgo]], in central-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|113.1|km2}}.

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 12,766.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Huazalingo (Municipality, Hidalgo, Mexico) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/mexico/hidalgo/13026__huazalingo/|access-date=2022-02-25|website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
