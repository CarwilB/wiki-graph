'''Sucre Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 114,509.<ref>http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx {{Dead link|date=August 2022}}</ref> The town of [[Cagua]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Sucre Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip {{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]].

==History==
{{Unreferenced section|date=February 2025}}
The town of Cagua was first established as ''Cagua la Vieja'' in 1620 and was originally made up of Spaniards.  The origin of the name ''Cagua'', comes from the native word ''Cahiua'', which means ''snail''.  Cagua was rebuilt in its current location in 1622 under the new name of ''Nuestra Señora del Rosario de Cagua'', which was later changed to ''San Jose de Cagua'' during the 18th century.  The city is now known as just ''Cagua''.

Cagua is a small city with several squares and has a tropical climate.  It's ones of the most important cities of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] because of its proximity to [[Maracay]], the state capital and [[Caracas]]. Cagua has been, for a long time, an important industrial zone.

One of its most important places are Casa Guipuzcoana and the Mountain "El Empalado", where original natives were burned alive by Spanish colonizers; this mountain is rich in marble and typical plants such as Araguaney, Samán, Bucare, and Apamate.

==Demographics==
The Sucre Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 120,302 (up from 109,723 in 2000).  This amounts to 7.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1582.92|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{Dead link|date=June 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=no }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Sucre Municipality is Wilson Coy, elected on November 21, 2021, with 39.58% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Noticias |first=Últimas |date=2021-11-23 |title=Regiones ya tienen sus gobernadores y alcaldes |url=https://ultimasnoticias.com.ve/noticias/general/regiones-ya-tienen-sus-gobernadores-y-alcaldes/ |access-date=2023-07-02 |website=Últimas Noticias |language=es}}</ref> He replaced Miriam Pardo shortly after the elections.  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Capital Sucre and Bella Vista.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
