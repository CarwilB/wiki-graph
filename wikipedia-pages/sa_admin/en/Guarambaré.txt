'''Guarambaré''' ({{IPA|es|ɡwaɾambaˈɾe}}) is a city and district in the [[Central Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. According to the 2022 national census, the district had a population of 27,695 inhabitants. Guarambaré was the name of a [[Guaraní people|Guaraní]] tribal chief in the 16th century.

==History==
Guarambaré was first established in 1539 as an indigenous reduction (''reducción de indios'') named "Todos los Santos de Guarambaré" (All saints of Guarambare), named after a local chief Pedro Guarambaré of the [[Guaraní people]].<ref name="UH2011">{{cite news|url=https://www.ultimahora.com/guarambare-celebro-los-329-anos-su-segunda-fundacion-n426481|title=Guarambaré celebró los 329 años de su segunda fundación|work=[[Última Hora (Paraguay)|Última Hora]]|date=6 May 2011|access-date=20 May 2026|language=es|trans-title=Guarambaré celebrated the 329 years of his second foundation}}</ref><ref name="ADN">{{cite web|url=https://www.adndigital.com.py/guarambare-la-ciudad-conocida-bello-jardin-vya-renda/|title=Guarambaré, la ciudad conocida como el "bello jardín vy'a renda"|work=ADN Digital|date=10 September 2017|access-date=20 May 2026|language=es|archive-url=http://web.archive.org/web/20250125033831/https://www.adndigital.com.py/guarambare-la-ciudad-conocida-bello-jardin-vya-renda/|archive-date=25 January 2025|trans-title=Guarambaré, the city known as the "beautiful garden of happiness"}}</ref> A Franciscan reduction was later founded between 1580 and 1600 in the location south of the Aquidabán river, and was relocated in 1673 to the current location, south-east of [[Asunción]].<ref>{{cite book|last=Torres|first=Dionisio González|title=Origen e Historia de los pueblos del Paraguay|year=2010|publisher=Servilibro|location=Asunción|isbn=978-99953-0-198-9|pages=132}}</ref> The Franciscan architecture is preserved in several parts of the city.<ref name="UH2011"/> Guarambaré was officially founded on 6 May 1682 during the rule of [[Governorate of Paraguay|Governor of Paraguay]], Juan Diez de Andino ([[:es:Juan Diez de Andino|es]]) as confirmed by the document held in the Archivo Nacional de Asunción.<ref name="UH2011"/><ref name="ADN"/> Paraguayan president [[Carlos Antonio López]] signed the official decree of declaration on 7 December 1848.<ref name="ADN"/>

==Geography and demographics==
Guarambaré is located in the [[Central Department]] of Paraguay. It occupies an area of {{cvt|2.78|km2}}. According to the 2022 national census, the district had a population of 27,695 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/paraguay/admin/central/1104__guarambar%C3%A9/|title=Guarambaré|work=City Population|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Economy==
The principal economic activities of the city are [[sugarcane]] cultivation and [[sugar]] production. The city has two sugar factories: the Azucarera Guarambaré and the Ingenio Azucarero La Felsina. Other agricultural activities include the cultivation of [[tomatoes]], [[watermelons]], [[Bell peppers|peppers]], [[cotton]], and [[maize]].<ref name="UH2011"/><ref name="ADN"/>

==Culture==
The patron saint feast of Guarambaré is celebrated on 8 September in honour of the Nativity of the Virgin Mary (''Natividad de María''). A notable tradition during the feast is the ''vito de dinero'', in which banknotes are thrown from the top of the church for the children on the streets below.<ref name="ADN"/> The city is associated with the poet and musician [[Emiliano R. Fernández]], who was born in the city in 1894.<ref name="ADN"/>
