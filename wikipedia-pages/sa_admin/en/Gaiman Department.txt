'''Gaiman Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,612 inhabitants in an area of 11,076 km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Gaiman, Chubut|Gaiman]], which is located around 1,398 km from the [[Capital federal]].
