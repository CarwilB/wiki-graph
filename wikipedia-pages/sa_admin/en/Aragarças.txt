'''Aragarças''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in southwest [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]].

==Location==
Aragarças is the most important town in the [[Aragarças Microregion]]. It is 410&nbsp;km from the state capital, [[Goiânia]] on the confluence of two important rivers, the Garças and the [[Araguaia River]].

Highway connections with [[Goiânia]] are made by GO-060 / [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]] / [[Turvânia]] / [[Israelândia]] / [[Iporá]] / [[Piranhas, Goiás|Piranhas]] / BR-158 / [[Bom Jardim de Goiás]].

Municipal boundaries are with:
*North and Northwest: [[Araguaia River]]
*South and West: [[Baliza]] and [[Bom Jardim de Goiás]]
*East:  [[Montes Claros de Goiás]] and [[Bom Jardim de Goiás]]

It is separated from [[Barra do Garças]] in [[Mato Grosso]] by a bridge over the [[Araguaia River]].  The main highway connections are made by BR-158, BR-70, GO-194 and GO-060.

==Transport==

[[Aragarças Airport]] is a small airfield that serves mainly private aircraft with one single paved runway. The airfield has no terminal building with just a small hangar. The closest airport is Barra do Garcas Airport [[SBBW]].

The municipality is served by three major roads:

* [[BR-070|Federal Highway 70]]
* [[Goiás State Highway GO-194]]
* [[BR-158|Federal Highway 158]]

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and tourism.
The population was employed in transformation industries, commerce (174 units), hotels and restaurants, and public administration (297 workers).  The informal economy is also very strong.{{citation needed|date=July 2017}}
In recent years{{when|date=July 2017}} tourism has become one of the main sources of income, driven by the natural environment of the area.{{citation needed|date=July 2017}}  It is one of the most visited touristic points in the state, especially in the months of June and July, when enormous sandbanks are exposed on the Araguaia River, creating kilometers of pristine beaches.{{citation needed|date=July 2017}} The Araguaia is a sought after location for sport fishing.{{citation needed|date=July 2017}}

There were 26,500 cattle registered in 2006. The main agricultural products were rice and corn.  There was also production of charcoal and hardwoods.

==History==

The first penetration into the region now occupied by Aragarças occurred in 1872 by a group of prospectors seeking diamonds from [[Mato Grosso]].  They were massacred by the Bororó Indians who lived in the area.  In 1933 the news that an enormous diamond had been discovered brought droves of prospectors.  In the 1950s Aragarça, the name given to the settlement and made up of the name of the Araguaia River and its tributary the Garças, was the center of the government campaign to open up the east of Mato Grosso.  A hotel was built to house visitors and a residential area to house the government workers involved in the project.  Other improvements were the building of an airstrip and a concrete bridge over the Araguaia River.   Araguaia became a district of [[Baliza]] in 1951 and a municipality in 1953.

==Climate==
Aragarças experiences a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with hot temperatures and distinct wet and dry seasons.

The city experiences a [[wet season]] from October to March. The [[dry season]] extends from May to September.<ref name="WMONormals"/>

Aragarças has hot conditions throughout the year. September and October, before the wet season, are the hottest months, with average highs around {{convert|36|C|abbr=on}}.<ref name="WMONormals"/>
