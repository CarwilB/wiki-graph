'''Tortugo''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] in the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico]]. It is the third smallest [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] of San Juan in land area and had a population of 4,543 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name= "2010 Census">{{cite book|title=Puerto Rico: 2010:population and housing unit counts | url = https://permanent.access.gpo.gov/gpo35934/cph-2-53.pdf|year=2010|publisher=U.S. Dept. of Commerce, Economics and Statistics Administration, U.S. Census Bureau}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Of the 4,543 inhabitants, 67.6% were white, 19.63% were [[African-American]], 0.42% were [[Amerindian]], 0.15% were [[Asian people|Asian]], 8.3% were from other races and 3.9% were from a mixture of races. Of the total population, 99.32% identified as Hispanic or Latino descent.<ref>{{cite web | url = https://geonames.usgs.gov/pls/gnispublic/f?p=gnispq:3:::NO::P3_FID:2416074 | title = Geography and population demographics from Tortugo in San Juan | date = 11 November 2015}}</ref>
