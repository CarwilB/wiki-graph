''' Zozocolco de Hidalgo ''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in northwest of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about {{convert|100|km|mi|-1}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface area of {{convert|106.11|km2|mi2}} and is located at {{coord|20|08|N|97|35|W|}}.

== Name ==
The name comes from the {{langx|nah|Xoxo-colco}}, which means "In the potter of clay".

==Geography==

The municipality of ''' Zozocolco ''' is bordered to the north by [[Coxquihui]], to the east by [[Gulf of Mexico]], to the northeast by [[Espinal, Veracruz|Espinal]], and to the west, south and southeast by [[Puebla]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30203a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Zozocolco de Hidalgo|publisher= Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-18}}</ref>

===Climate===
The climate in Zozocolco is warm-humid with an average temperature of {{convert|22|C|F}}, with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==
[[File:Danza_del_toro.jpg|right|thumb|San Miguel Arcángel festival at september]]
Every September, a festival is held to celebrate St Michael ({{lang|es|San Miguel}}), patron of the town, and in December there is a festival held in honor of [[Our Lady of Guadalupe]].
