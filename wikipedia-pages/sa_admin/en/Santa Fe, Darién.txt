'''Santa Fé ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] and town in [[Chepigana District]], [[Darién Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 6,923 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 58 of July 29, 1998, owing to the Declaration of Unconstitutionality of Law 1 of 1982.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 5,764.<ref name=inec/> It is approximately 200&nbsp;km east of [[Panama City]] on the [[Inter-American Highway]].<ref name=moon/> The town contains a hospital. It is served by bus.<ref name=moon>Friar, William. ''Panama''. [[Moon Publications]] (2008), p. 422. {{ISBN|1-59880-085-X}}.</ref>
