'''Piracema''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=315060|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Minas Gerais {{!}} Piracema|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-01}}</ref>  As of 2020, the estimated population was 6,398.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/piracema/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
