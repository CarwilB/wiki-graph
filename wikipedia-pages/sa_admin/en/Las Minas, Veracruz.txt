'''Las Minas''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: 'the mines') is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located {{convert|28|km}} northeast of the state capital [[Xalapa]].<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://siglo.inafed.gob.mx/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30107a.html |title=Las Minas |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Las Minas is located in central Veracruz at an altitude between {{convert|700|and(-)|2400|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Altotonga]] to the west and north, [[Tatatila]] to the east, [[Las Vigas de Ramírez, Veracruz|Las Vigas de Ramírez]] to the south, and [[Villa Aldama]] to the southwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/geo/contenidos/topografia/compendio.aspx |title=Compendio de Información Geográfica Municipal 2010: Las Minas, Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave |language=es |publisher=INEGI |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|51.157|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.1% of the state's area.<ref name=survey>{{cite book |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/Productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/inter_censal/panorama/702825082420.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave 2015 |page=220 |language=es |publisher=[[INEGI]] |date=2016 |access-date=27 February 2018 |isbn=978-607-739-871-4}}</ref>

The land in Las Minas is mostly either forested (47%) or used as farmland (46%).<ref name=compendium/> The municipality is located in the [[Nautla River]] basin and the main stem of the Nautla flows through it from south to north. Known as the ''[[Arroyo (creek)|arroyo]]'' Borregos as it enters the municipality, it changes its name to the Trinidad River as it flows past the hydroelectric plant near the municipal seat, and then becomes the [[Bobos River (Mexico)|Bobos River]] as its course bends northwest on the municipality's border with Tatatila.<ref name=atlas>{{cite encyclopedia |title=Hidrología |encyclopedia=Atlas del patrimonio natural, histórico y cultural de Veracruz |language=es |year=2010 |publisher=Government of Veracruz |url=http://cdigital.uv.mx/bitstream/123456789/9650/1/04HIDROLOGIA.pdf |pages=100–101 |volume=1}}</ref>

Las Minas's climate is generally temperate and humid. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|1100|and(-)|1600|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
The original name for the area was Zomelahuacan ([[Nahuatl]]: "slippery place of elderberries").<ref name=encyclopedia/> The discovery of rich mineral deposits in the area of Zomelahuacan and Tatatila dates back to 1680 and gold and copper mining took place in the 18th and 19th centuries.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sgm.gob.mx/pdfs/VERACRUZ.pdf |title=Panorama Minero del Estado de Veracruz |language=es |page=2 |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=December 2016 |access-date=27 February 2018 |archive-date=28 February 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180228100734/http://www.sgm.gob.mx/pdfs/VERACRUZ.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> In 1803 the town of Las Minas de Zomelahuacan was founded; its name was eventually shortened to Las Minas.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

On 1 December 1868, Las Minas became a municipality in the canton of Jalacingo in the state of Veracruz. On 28 October 1881 it was separated from Jalacingo and attached to the canton of Xalapa. It became a free municipality on 15 January 1918.<ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Veracruz-Llave. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=112, 218 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222574/702825222574_1.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1517-0}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and a trustee (''regidor'').<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is María Magdalena Hernández Condado.<ref>{{cite news |last=Rasgado |first=Gabriela |url=https://www.presencia.mx/nota.aspx?id=124567&s=4 |title=Así quedan las alcaldías para las mujeres en Veracruz |language=es |newspaper=Presencia.MX |date=5 June 2017 |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref>

In the 2020 Mexican Census, the municipality of Las Minas recorded a population of 2,934 inhabitants living in 614 households.<ref name=2020CENSUSLASMINAS>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Las-Minas.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Las Minas CENSUS]</ref>

There are 12 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> all of the classified as rural. The municipal seat, Las Minas, recorded a population of 219 inhabitants.<ref name=2020CENSUSLASMINAS/> Other localities include Rinconada (539 hab.), Molinillo (439 hab.), Landaco (348 hab.), Quiahuixcuautla (304 hab.) and Pueblo Nuevo (242 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSLASMINAS/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The main economic activity in Las Minas is farming. Corn, beans and coffee are the main crops grown, and pastures are maintained for the grazing of goats and sheep.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://lasminas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/Plan-de-Desarrollo-Municipal-2014-2017.pdf |title=Plan Municipal de Desarrollo 2014–2017 |language=es |page=17 |publisher=Municipal council of Las Minas, Veracruz |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref> Mineral exploration in the area is ongoing as of 2018.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.mexicangold.ca/las-minas/projects-overview/ |title=Las Minas - Overview |publisher=Mexican Gold Corp |access-date=27 February 2018}}</ref> The Las Minas [[hydroelectric plant]] is located near the municipal seat and has a capacity of 15 MW.<ref name=atlas/>
