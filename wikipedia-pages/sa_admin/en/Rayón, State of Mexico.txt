'''Rayón''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in [[Toluca Region]], the northeastern part of the [[state of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]], the municipal seat is [[Santa María Rayón]], formerly named ''Cuauhtenco''. The municipality is located at a southern pass leading out of the [[Toluca Valley]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Rayón|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15072a.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160819061138/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15072a.html|url-status=dead|archive-date=August 19, 2016|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=June 6, 2009}} </ref>

==Geography==
The total municipality extends 84.37&nbsp;km{{sup|2}} and borders with the municipalities of [[Tenango del Valle]], [[Calimaya]] and [[Almoloya del Río]].

== Communication and transport ==
The [[Lerma-Tenango Highway]], is a main highway that cross by Rayón, connect with Toluca to Tenango del Valle.

== Economy ==
The economy is principally farming, cattle raising and small businesses, concentrating on the production of corn, beans and fruit.
