'''Cajamarca''' is a town and municipality in the [[Tolima Department]] of [[Colombia]].  The population of the municipality was 17,309 as of the 2018 census.<ref name="Censo"></ref> The municipality has two populated centres, the town centre of Cajamarca and the locality of Anaime. It is located along the [[Pan-American Highway]]. 

In 1886, colonisers from [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]] colonised the area currently known as Anaime, and started to develop the land for agriculture. In 1913, the Bishop of Ibagué Ismael Perdomo bought the land of the municipality and officially founded the municipality of Cajamarca, which stands for 'cold land' in [[Quechuan languages|Quechua]].<ref>Alcaldía de Cajamarca - Tolima. 2019. "Caracterización Poblacional." https://cajamarcatolima.micolombiadigital.gov.co/sites/cajamarcatolima/content/files/000267/13340_caracterizacion-cajamarca-2019.pdf</ref>

The main economic activity is agriculture, and the town is also known as the 'agricultural larder' of Colombia.<ref>El Tiempo. 1997. "Cajamarca despensa agrícola del Tolima." ''El Tiempo'', 21 May.  https://www.eltiempo.com/archivo/documento/MAM-600830</ref> 

Internationally, the town is best known as the location of the La Colosa gold mining project of [[AngloGold Ashanti]].
