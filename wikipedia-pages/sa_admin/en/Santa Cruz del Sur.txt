'''Santa Cruz del Sur''' is a town and municipality in [[Cuba]]. It is located in [[Camagüey Province]] south of the provincial capital of [[Camagüey]]. It lies on the [[Caribbean Sea|Caribbean]] coast.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Buenaventura, Doce Leguas, El Junco, Gonzalo de Quesada, Guaicanamar, Guayabal, La Calzada, Playa Bonita, San Pedro and Yaguabo.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/csanta/index.htm|title=Santa Cruz del Sur|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-05|language=es}}</ref>

==History==
The city has a special place in history due to its total destruction by the [[storm surge]] during the [[1932 Cuba Hurricane|catastrophic hurricane of 1932]] beginning late on November 8, 1932.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.cadenagramonte.cubaweb.cu/historia/santa_cruz_sur.asp Testimonies of the disaster] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070922151043/http://www.cadenagramonte.cubaweb.cu/historia/santa_cruz_sur.asp |date=2007-09-22 }}</ref> In a few hours, approximately at 10:00 am of November 9, the city disappeared under the sea, and more than 3,000 of its inhabitants drowned or were crushed by flying debris carried by winds in excess of 135&nbsp;mph (215&nbsp;km/h). Why a select number of survivors escaped the storm, and why the town was not evacuated prior to landfall, persist as questions. The hurricane was rumored to be destined for landfall far east of Santa Cruz, and when notification arrived about the town's peril, landfall was imminent. The last departing trains for evacuation failed to depart due to the storm surge. Fishermen of Santa Cruz were said to have predicted the event, apparently suspecting that "something wrong was brewing in the atmosphere." Survivors provided horrifying testimonies in the wake of a storm that literally drowned people in their homes.<ref>{{Cite book|title=La furia de los vientos|last=Junco|first=Pedro Armando|publisher=Editoria Acana|year=1989|isbn=9592671834|location=Camaguey}}</ref> [[Hurricane Paloma]] also made landfall there 76 years later on the same night in 2008 with 100&nbsp;mph (160&nbsp;km/h) winds.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Santa Cruz del Sur had a population of 39,919.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1122|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|36|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}} In 2022 the urban population was 22,430.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Santa Cruz del Sur (Municipality, Cuba) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/camag%C3%BCey/3013__santa_cruz_del_sur/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
