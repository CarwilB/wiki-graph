'''Santa Cruz''' is a canton in the [[Provinces of Ecuador|Ecuadorian province]] of the [[Galápagos Province|Galápagos]]. Its area covers the islands of [[Baltra Island|Baltra]], [[Bartolomé Island|Bartolomé]], [[Marchena Island|Marchena]], [[North Seymour Island|North Seymour]], [[Pinta Island|Pinta]], [[Pinzón Island|Pinzón]], [[Rábida Island|Rábida]], [[Santa Cruz Island (Galápagos)| Santa Cruz]], and [[Santiago Island (Galápagos)|Santiago]]. As of the 2006 Galápagos census, it has a population of 11,262. <ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inec.gov.ec/web/guest/ecu_est/est_soc/cen_pob_viv Censo de Población y Vivienda, Galápagos 2006] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090919204951/http://www.inec.gov.ec/web/guest/ecu_est/est_soc/cen_pob_viv |date=2009-09-19 }}. Instituto Nacional de Estadistica y Censos (INEC).</ref>
