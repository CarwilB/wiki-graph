'''San Ignacio''' is a [[Municipalities of Sinaloa|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sinaloa]] in northwestern [[Mexico]], being the most sparsely populated municipality in Sinaloa.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sinaloa/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=January 11, 2010}}</ref>

It stands at {{coord|23|56|12|N|106|25|39|W|}}.<!-- 23° 56'12" N. and 106° 25'39" W. -->

== Political subdivision ==
San Ignacio Municipality is subdivided in 8 ''sindicaturas'':
*Central de San Ignacio
*Estación Dimas
*Contraestaca
*Ixpalino
*Coyotitán
*San Juan
*Ajoya
*San Javier
