'''Pesca''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the [[Sugamuxi Province]], a subregion of Boyacá. The town is located in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]] at altitudes between {{convert|2540|m|ft}} and {{convert|4000|m|ft}}. Pesca is {{convert|108|km|mi}} west from the department capital [[Tunja]] and borders [[Firavitoba]] in the north, [[Iza, Boyacá|Iza]] in the northeast, [[Tuta, Boyacá|Tuta]] in the northwest, in the east [[Tota, Boyacá|Tota]], [[Zetaquirá]] in the south, [[Rondón, Boyacá|Rondón]] and [[Siachoque]] in the southwest and [[Toca, Boyacá|Toca]] in the west.

== Etymology ==
In the [[Chibcha language]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]], Pesca means "strong enclosure".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Pesca]</ref>

== History ==
Before the arrival of the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish]] in the 1530s, Pesca was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a confederation of different [[Muisca rulers|rulers]]; ''[[zaque]]s'' based in [[Tunja|Hunza]], ''[[zipa]]s'' ruling from [[Bacatá|Muyquytá]] and ''[[cacique]]s'' in other territories. Pesca was reigned by the ''[[iraca]]'' of sacred City of the Sun [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]], now called Sogamoso.

Modern Pesca was founded by [[Juan de Sanct Martín]] on December 20, 1548.<ref name=ColombiaTurismo>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.colombiaturismoweb.com/DEPARTAMENTOS/BOYACA/MUNICIPIOS/PESCA/PESCA.htm Pesca]</ref>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Pesca are [[agriculture]] ([[potato]]es, [[maize]], [[wheat]], [[pea]]s, [[bean]]s and [[Oxalis tuberosa|ibias]]) and [[livestock]] farming.
