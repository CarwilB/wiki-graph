'''Bojacá''' is a municipality and town of the [[Western Savanna Province]], [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The urban centre is situated at an altitude of {{convert|2598|m|ft}} on the [[Bogotá savanna]] at {{convert|40|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. The municipality borders [[Zipacón]], [[Madrid, Cundinamarca|Madrid]] and [[Facatativá]] in the north, Madrid and [[Mosquera, Cundinamarca|Mosquera]] in the east, [[Soacha]] and [[San Antonio del Tequendama]] in the south and [[Tena, Cundinamarca|Tena]], [[La Mesa, Cundinamarca|La Mesa]] and Zipacón in the west.<ref name=websiteBojaca>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.bojaca-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Bojacá] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171011080747/http://www.bojaca-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2017-10-11 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Bojacá comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Purple enclosure".<ref name=websiteBojaca/><ref name=TiempoCundin>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.eltiempo.com/archivo/documento/CMS-7783403 Etymology municipalities Cundinamarca] - [[El Tiempo (Colombia)|El Tiempo]]</ref>

== History ==
The area around Bojacá was inhabited at least since 3410 years [[Before Present|BP]] (1400 BCE), evidenced by [[archaeology|archaeological]] excavations from the [[Herrera Period]] performed in [[Aguazuque]], Zipacón and around [[Lake Herrera]], Mosquera.<ref>{{citation |last=[[Gonzalo Correal Urrego|Correal Urrego]] |first=Gonzalo |year=1990 |title=Aguazuque: Evidence of hunter-gatherers and growers on the high plains of the Eastern Ranges |location=Bogotá, Colombia |publisher=Banco de la República: Fundación de Investigaciones Arqueológicas Nacionales |url=http://admin.banrepcultural.org/sites/default/files/aguazuque_completo_terminado_baja.pdf |pages=1–316 |language=es |accessdate=2016-07-08}}</ref> The territories were already important in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] when Bojacá was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]]; a loose confederation of [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]].

On his expedition to ''[[El Dorado]]'' and after conquering the Muisca, [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] founded modern Bojacá on October 16, 1537.<ref name=websiteBojaca/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Bojacá are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Among the agricultural products cultivated are [[potato]]es, [[carrot]]s, [[maize]], [[lettuce]] and fruits as [[blackberry|blackberries]], [[strawberry|strawberries]], [[prune]]s and the typical Colombian fruits [[uchuva]], [[tree tomato]] and [[Passiflora ligularis|granadilla]].<ref name=websiteBojaca/>
