'''Soledad Atzompa''' ({{coord|18|45|N|97|09|W|}}) is a village in the mountainous central zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 90&nbsp;km south-west of the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of 65.80 km<sup>2</sup> and stands at an elevation of 2260 metres above sea level.

==Name==
Its name, Atzompa, comes from the [[nahuatl]] language and refers to the small river valleys on the mountain looking like a scalp<ref name = "encmex">{{cite web
 | title = Soledad Atzompa
 | publisher = Encyclopedia of the Towns and Delegations of Mexico
 | url = http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM30veracruz/municipios/30147a.html
 | access-date = 9 Jan 2014
 }}</ref> In time it took the other part of its name from the Soledad river which runs through the town. This river is a tributary of the River Blanco.

==Geography==

The municipality of Soledad Atzompa is delimited to the north by [[Huiloapan de Cuauhtémoc]], to the east by [[Atlahuilco]], to the southeast by [[Xoxocotla, Veracruz|Xoxocotla]], and to the west and northwest by [[Acultzingo (municipality)|Acultzingo]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30147a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Soledad Atzompa |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-15}}</ref> There had been a number of border disputes in the past settled by a decree in 1982.<ref name = "encmex"/>

The climate of Soledad Atzompa is temperate-humid, with an average temperature of 12&nbsp;°C; annual average rainfall is 887&nbsp;mm.<ref name = "encmex"/>

==Agriculture==

The local soil is composed of sandy rock and only 50% can be used for agriculture. The area produces principally [[maize]].

==Celebrations==

In February there are celebrations in honor of "Virgen de la Candelaria", patron of the town, and in December there are celebrations in honor to [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].<ref name = "encmex"/>
