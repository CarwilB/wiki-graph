'''Southern Chalatenango''' ({{langx|es|Chalatenango Sur}}) is one of the 44 municipalities of [[El Salvador]].<ref>"Municipio de Chalatenango Sur" https://www.asamblea.gob.sv/node/12806#:~:text=Municipio%20de%20Chalatenango%20Sur,San%20Luis%20del%20Carmen</ref> This municipality is made up of 20 districts [[Arcatao]], [[Azacualpa, El Salvador|Azacualpa]], [[San José Cancasque]], [[Chalatenango, El Salvador|Chalatenango]], [[Comalapa, El Salvador|Comalapa]], 
[[Concepción Quezaltepeque]], [[El Carrizal, El Salvador|El Carrizal]], [[La Laguna, El Salvador|La Laguna]], [[Las Vueltas]], [[Las Flores, El Salvador|Las Flores]], [[Nombre de Jesús]], [[Nueva Trinidad]], [[Ojos de Agua, El Salvador|Ojos de Agua]], [[Potonico]], [[San Antonio de la Cruz]], [[San Antonio Los Ranchos]], [[San Francisco Lempa]], [[San Isidro Labrador, El Salvador|San Isidro Labrador]], [[San Luis del Carmen]]
and [[San Miguel de Mercedes]]. The municipality comprises the largest amount of districts within the entire country.<ref> "Chalatenango Sur tiene 20 distritos" https://www.tcsahora.com/chalatenango-sur-tiene-20-distritos-es-un-reto-grande-milton-serrano/</ref> The municipality arise as part of reducing the amount of municipalities in El Salvador. Its mayor has been disputed since 13 April 2026 between Milton Serrano and Víctor Hernández.
