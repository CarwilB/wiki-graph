'''José Sixto Verduzco''' is a municipality in northern [[Michoacán]].
It is part of the [[Bajío]] region. In the 2020 [[INEGI]] census, it reported 26,213 inhabitants. The seat is at [[Pastor Ortiz]].

It is named for {{ill|José Sixto Verduzco|es}}, one of the signatories of the 1813 [[Solemn Act of the Declaration of Independence of Northern America]]. 
The municipality was created in 1974.<ref>{{cite web |url= http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16113a.html |title=José Sixto Verduzco |work=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |access-date=25 May 2025 |archive-date=25 February 2021 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20210225040946/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM16michoacan/municipios/16113a.html |url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Geography==
It is bordered to the north by [[Guanajuato]] ([[Huanimaro]] and [[Abasolo, Guanajuato|Abasolo]] municipalities), to the east and south by [[Puruándiro]], Michoacán, and to the west by [[Pénjamo]] and [[Angamacutiro]] (Mich.).
