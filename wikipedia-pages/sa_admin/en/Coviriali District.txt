'''Coviriali District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of [[Satipo Province]] in the [[Department of Junín]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The town of Coviriali, the capital of the district, had a population of 338 in 2017<ref>{{cite web |last1=Coviriali |title=Peru: Junin Region |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/peru/junin/1206__satipo |accessdate=7 Aug 2020}}</ref> and is located {{convert|4|km|mile}} south of the provincial capital of [[Satipo (town)|Satipo]].

Coviriali has a [[tropical rainforest]] climate similar to that of nearby Satipo.  The annual average temperature in Satipo, which varies little from month to month, is {{convert|22.9|C|F}} and annual precipitation is {{convert|1879|mm|inch}}. Precipitation is adequate in all months although less is received in the austral winter months of June through August.<ref name="6 Aug 2010">{{cite web |title=Satipo |url=https://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather.php3?s=604563&cityname=Satipo%2C+Junin%2C+Peru |website=Weatherbase}}</ref> Coviriali's climate under the [[Köppen Classification]] is Af (borderline Am, tropical monsoon). In the mountains of the district the climate above about {{convert|1500|m|ft}} in elevation becomes Cfb (sub-tropical, warm but not hot year round).
