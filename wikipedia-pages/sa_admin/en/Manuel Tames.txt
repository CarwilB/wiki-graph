'''Manuel Tames''' is a municipality and town in the [[Guantánamo Province]] of [[Cuba]]. Its administrative seat is located in the town of [[Jamaica, Cuba|Jamaica]].<ref name=statoids/>

==Geography==
The town is located {{convert|15|km|mi}} east of the provincial capital, [[Guantánamo, Cuba|Guantánamo]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Manuel Tames had a population of 36,067.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|526|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|69|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}. The urban population was 19,790.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Manuel Tames (Municipality, Cuba) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/guant%C3%A1namo/3502__manuel_tames/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
