'''Turpo''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''T'urpu''', meaning "pointed") is one of the nineteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Andahuaylas Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Digital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100501234242/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2010-05-01 }}''. Retrieved April 4, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
It is elevated at 3,297 m above sea level.<ref>{{cite web |title=TURPO - District in Apurímac Region |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/peru-apurimac-admin.php?adm2id=030218 |website=City Population |accessdate=17 May 2019}}</ref> One of the highest peaks of the district is Pisti at approximately {{convert|4200|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Andahuaylas Province (Apurímac Region)</ref>

* Anta Qaqa
* Apankayniyuq
* Illa Rumi
* Liq'i Yakana Pampa
* Qucha Pampa

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (95.22%) learnt to speak in childhood, 4.57% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población</ref>
