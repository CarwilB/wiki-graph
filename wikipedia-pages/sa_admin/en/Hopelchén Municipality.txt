'''Hopelchén''' ([[Yucatec Maya]]: "place of five wells") is one of the 13 [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipalities]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[Mexican state|state]] of [[Campeche]]. It is situated inland in the north of the state. The municipal seat, and largest settlement, is the city of [[Hopelchén]].

==Demographics==
The 2005 INEGI census reported a population of 34,687, down from 36,271 in 1990. Of the 1990 figure, 17,664 spoke one of several indigenous languages, predominantly [[Yucatec Maya]] with 14,983 and [[Ch'ol language|Ch'ol]] with  2,039; the total number of indigenous language speakers had fallen to 15,416 by 2005. As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 37,777.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Hopelchén|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=006|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=September 30, 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210930205334/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=006|url-status=dead}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of Hopelchén had a population of 7,295.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the city of Hopelchén, the municipality had 269 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Bolonchén de Rejón, Campeche|Bolonchén de Rejón]] (3,975), [[Vicente Guerrero, Campeche|Vicente Guerrero]] (3,198), classified as urban, and [[Dzibalchén, Campeche|Dzibalchén]] (2,340), [[Ukum, Campeche|Ukum]] (2,019), [[Xmabén, Campeche|Xmabén]] (1,228), [[Suc-Tuc, Campeche|Suc-Tuc]] (1,179), and [[Chunchintok, Campeche|Chunchintok]] (1,086), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

===Mennonites===
The 2020 Mexican census found that of the municipality's 42,170 residents, about 6,000 or 15% were [[Mennonites in Mexico|Mennonites]] (a group of German and Dutch descent who speak the [[Plautdietsch]] German dialect). They were concentrated in the communities of Nuevo Progreso, El Temporal, Nuevo Durango, La Nueva Trinidad, Las Flores, Las Palmas, Santa Rosa, Santa Fe, La Estrella, and La Esperanza.<ref>{{cite news|lang=es|url=https://www.poresto.net/campeche/2024/7/12/en-hopelchen-el-15-de-la-poblacion-es-menonita-segun-inegi.amp.html |title= En Hopelchén, el 15% de la población es menonita, según Inegi|trans-title= In Hopelchén, 15% of the population is Mennonite, according to Inegi|  work=Poresto.net|date=July 1, 2024}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Strochlic |first=Nina |date=12 April 2019 |title=An Unlikely Feud Between Beekeepers and Mennonites Simmers in Mexico |url=https://www.nationalgeographic.com/environment/article/unlikely-feud-beekeepers-mennonites-simmers-mexico |url-access=registration |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210228071115/https://www.nationalgeographic.com/environment/article/unlikely-feud-beekeepers-mennonites-simmers-mexico |archive-date=28 February 2021 |access-date=5 November 2024 |work=[[National Geographic]]}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ldPSIjH4a7c  |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211221/ldPSIjH4a7c |archive-date=2021-12-21 |url-status=live|title=Mennonites Colony of Hopelchen, Mexico |website=Youtube.com |access-date=July 5, 2019}}{{cbignore}}</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality of Hopelchén is bordered to the north and east by the state of [[Yucatán (state)|Yucatán]]; to the south by the municipality of [[Calakmul]], and to the west by the municipalities of [[Champotón Municipality|Champotón]], [[Campeche, Campeche|Campeche]], [[Tenabo Municipality|Tenabo]] and [[Hecelchakán Municipality|Hecelchakán]].

It covers 7,460.27&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, accounting for 13% of the state's total surface area.

==Heritage==
The [[Maya civilization|Maya]] archaeological sites of [[Chunan-Tunich]], [[Dzibilnocac]], [[Santa Rosa Xtampak]], [[Hochob]]  and [[Pak-Chén]] are located in the municipality. It is also home to two cave complexes: [[Chuncedro]] and [[Xtacumbilxuna’an]]
