'''Santa Rosa da Serra''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northwest of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.mapnall.com/pt/Mapa-Santa-Rosa-da-Serra_1122512.html |title=Santa Rosa da Serra Map on Mapnall}}</ref> Its population as of 2020 was 3,357 people living in a total area of 296&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-rosa-da-serra/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaíba (mesoregion)|Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaiba]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Patos de Minas]].  It became a municipality in 1962.

==Geography==
The urban center is located at an elevation of 1,040 meters southeast of [[Patos de Minas]] in the upper Indaiá River valley.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[São Gotardo]] (N), [[Estrela do Indaiá]] (E), [[Córrego Danta]] (S), and [[Campos Altos]] (W).<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/patosdeminas/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080223224756/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/patosdeminas/ |date=2008-02-23 }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$28 million, with 10 million generated by services, 1 million generated by industry, and 16 million generated by agriculture.  Santa Rosa da Serra is in the middle tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  As of 2007 there were no banking agencies in the town.  There was a modest retail infrastructure serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  There were 325 automobiles in all of the municipality (2007), about one for every 10 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

Santa Rosa da Serra has a small municipal area and therefore little available land for agriculture.  In the rural area there were 494 establishments (2006) occupying 25,000 hectares (planted area—2,000 ha, and natural pasture—16,500 ha.).  About 2,000 persons were dependent on agriculture. 62 of the farms had tractors, a ratio of one in 8 farms.  There were 9,700 head of cattle in 2006.  The main crops were: 
*coffee: 2,000 hectares
*corn: 1,500 hectares <ref name="IBGE"/>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 3 health clinics and no hospitals.  In the educational sector there were 2 pre-primary schools, 3 primary schools, and no middle schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>
