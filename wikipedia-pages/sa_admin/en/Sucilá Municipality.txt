'''Sucilá Municipality''' ({{IPA|es|sukiˈla}}, [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "zacate (grass) water")<ref name=etemology>{{cite web|title=El Significado de los Nombre de los Pueblos de Yucatán: Agua|url=http://www.meridadeyucatan.com/el-significado-de-los-nombre-de-los-pueblos-de-yucatan-agua/|website=Merida de Yucatan|publisher=Crónicas de la Ciudad Blanca|accessdate=8 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 256.76&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (99.14 sq mi) of land and is located roughly {{convert|150|km}} northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], it was part of the chieftainship of Cupules.<ref name="municipios"/> At colonization, Sucilá became part of the [[encomienda]] system and though an encomienda was established by the seventeenth century,<ref name="Cook (1974)">{{cite book|last1=Cook|first1=Sherburne F.|last2=Borah|first2=Woodrow|title=Essays in population history|date=1974|publisher=University of California Press|location=Berkeley, California|isbn=978-0-520-02272-0|url=https://archive.org/details/primerlibrodela01camagoog|page=[https://archive.org/details/primerlibrodela01camagoog/page/n128 101]|accessdate=8 August 2015}}</ref> no names were discovered before the encomendera Josefa Chacón y Salazar, who served in 1745.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821, and in 1825 the area was assigned to the [[Tizimín Municipality]]. In 1837, it was assigned to the [[Espita Municipality]].<ref name="municipios"/> As with other areas of the state, Sucilá was depopulated during the [[Caste War of Yucatán]].<ref name="Reed (2001)">{{cite book|last1=Reed|first1=Nelson A.|title=The Caste War of Yucatán|date=2001|publisher=Stanford University Press|location=Stanford, California|isbn=978-0-804-74001-2|pages=93–94| edition=Revised|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=e2KhyOCebJkC&pg=PA92|accessdate=8 August 2015}}</ref> In 1988, the area was confirmed as its own municipality.<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|accessdate=7 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=Spanish|date=1996|pages=110, 116, 124|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archivedate=2015-12-23}}</ref>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public services, public security, urban development and ecology.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Sucilá | url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=8 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish}}</ref>

==Communities==
The head of the municipality is [[Sucilá]], Yucatán. The municipality has 63 populated places<ref name=governance /> besides the seat including La Hacienda Xmabalam, San Antonio, San Pedro II, San Miguelito, San Román, Santa Rosa, Santa Teresa, Tierra Blanca, Xmihuan,  and Yohches.
