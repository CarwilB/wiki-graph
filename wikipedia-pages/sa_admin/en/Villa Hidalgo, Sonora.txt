'''Villa Hidalgo''' is a city, and the surrounding [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] of the same name, in the north-east of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Sonora]].

==Municipal seat==
It was founded by the [[Jesuit]] missionary [[Marcos del Río]] in 1644 as '''(San Ignacio de) Oputo'''. On 1 April 1967 the [[Congress of Sonora|State Congress]] ordered that it change its name from Oputo to its current name in honor of [[Father of the Nation]] [[Miguel Hidalgo]].

==Area==
The municipal area is 951.17&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Population==
The population was 1,986 inhabitants (2.05 inhab./km<sup>2</sup>) in 2000. 

==Economy==
The main economic activities are agriculture and cattle raising.
