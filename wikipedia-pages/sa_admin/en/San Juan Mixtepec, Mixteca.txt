'''San Juan Mixtepec''' ([[Mixtec language|Mixtec]]: ''Ñuu Snuviko'')<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Gomez |first=Melissa |date=2022-12-27 |title=I’m Mexican American. But the L.A. City Council audio leak reminded me that I’m Oaxacan too |url=https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2022-12-27/los-angeles-city-council-oaxacans-essay |access-date=2022-12-27 |website=Los Angeles Times |language=en-US}}</ref> is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 209.24 km<sup>2</sup>, and is part of the [[Juxtlahuaca District]] of the [[Mixteca Region]].

Its Mixtepec name, Ñuu Snuviko, translates to "place where the clouds descend."<ref name=":0" />

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 7,423.<ref>{{cite web|title=San Juan Mixtepec Distrito 8|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20208a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 12, 2009}}</ref>
