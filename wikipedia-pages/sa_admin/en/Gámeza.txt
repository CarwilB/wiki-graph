'''Gámeza''' ({{IPA|es|ˈɣamesa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the [[Sugamuxi Province]], a subregion of Boyacá. The town center is located at {{convert|18|km|mi}} from Sogamoso and the municipality borders [[Tasco, Boyacá|Tasco]] and [[Corrales, Boyacá|Corrales]] in the north, [[Tópaga, Boyacá|Tópaga]] and [[Mongua]] in the south, in the east [[Socotá]] and westward of Gámeza Corrales and Tópaga.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.gameza-boyaca.gov.co/index.shtml Official website Gámeza] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170112061711/http://www.gameza-boyaca.gov.co/index.shtml |date=2017-01-12 }} - accessed 04-05-2016</ref>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] in the 1530s, Gámeza was inhabited first by indigenous groups during the [[Herrera Period]] and later part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], the former country of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] in the central highlands ([[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]]) of Colombia. The confederation was ruled by ''[[zaque]]s'' in [[Tunja|Hunza]] (present-day Tunja), ''[[zipa]]s'' in [[Bogotá|Bacatá]] and ''[[cacique]]s'' of other villages. Gámeza was part of the reign of the ''[[iraca]]'' of [[Sogamoso|Sugamuxi]], currently known as Sogamoso.

With the election of a new ruler of Sugamuxi, the ''cacique'' of Gámeza was consulted, together with the leaders of [[Busbanzá]], [[Toca, Boyacá]] and [[Pesca]] among the nobles of [[Firavitoba]] and [[Floresta, Boyacá|Tobasía]]. In case of conflict, the ruler of [[Duitama|Tundama]] would intervene.<ref name=website/>

The first Europeans encountering the Muisca were the troops led by [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] in 1537. The last ''zaque'' beaten by the Spanish soldiers was [[Aquiminzaque]] whose primary wife was the daughter of the ''cacique'' of Gámeza.<ref name=website/>

Gámeza was conquered and Spanish missionaries were sent to convert the indigenous people to [[catholicism]]. On November 4, 1585 Gámeza was properly founded.<ref name=website/>

The name Gámeza comes from Gamza, name of the ''cacique'' of the village. ''Gá'' and ''za'' in the [[Chibcha language]] of the Muisca mean "serf of the Sun" and "night" respectively.<ref name=website/><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Gámeza] - accessed 04-05-2016</ref>

== Geography ==
Gámeza is located in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]]. In the area [[gold]] deposits are present and the [[Chicamocha River]] originates in Gámeza.<ref name=website/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Gámeza are [[dairy]] farming and [[agriculture]]; [[potato]]es, [[pea]]s, [[maize]], [[wheat]] and [[barley]]. [[Coal mining]] forms another important source of income for the villagers.<ref name=website/>
