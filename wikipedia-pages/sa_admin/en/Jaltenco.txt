'''Jaltenco''' is the [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in [[Zumpango Region]], a small municipality in this region, covers an area of  4.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, this territory is in the northeastern part of the [[state of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is [[San Andrés Jaltenco]], although both are commonly called only San Andrés. The municipality is located at a northern pass leading out of the [[Valley of Mexico]] to 60 kilometers north of [[Mexico City]] and about 200&nbsp;km northeast of the state capital of [[Toluca]].

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 26,359.<ref>{{cite web|title=Jaltenco |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15044a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=June 6, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070304120252/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15044a.htm |archive-date=March 4, 2007 }}</ref> The municipality has an [[exclave]], Alborada. 

==Geography==
The location of the municipality is north of Mexico City, and is located in the low extreme geographical coordinates of [[Greenwich]], north latitude 19º45'23" minimum, 19º46'28" maximum, west longitude 99°05'30" minimum, 99°06'35" maximum.<ref name="Tequixquiac municipality">[http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/datos-geograficos/15/15044.pdf Jaltenco municipality] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160304113835/http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/datos-geograficos/15/15044.pdf |date=2016-03-04 }} INEGI, 2009.</ref>

The town of [[San Andrés Jaltenco]], a municipal seat, has governing jurisdiction over the following to Alborada Jaltenco. The total municipality extends 4,7&nbsp;km{{sup|2}} and borders with the municipalities of [[Zumpango]] and [[Nextlalpan]]. The area of this municipality is 15&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (59.85 sq mi).

=== Flora and fauna ===
90% of Jaltenco municipality is urbanized; all territory is plane lands, live here small mammals as mouse, rabbit, bat and gopher, birds as sparrow. The flora is century plants, prickly pears, chollas and others.<ref>[http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15044a.html Jaltenco] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161009231745/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15044a.html |date=2016-10-09 }} INAFED.</ref>
