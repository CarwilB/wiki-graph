'''Santa Filomena''' is a city  in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 12,360 inhabitants and the total area is 1004.561&nbsp;km².<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Ouricuri]]   ('''N''');  [[Dormentes]]   ('''S''');  [[Santa Cruz, Pernambuco|Santa Cruz]]  ('''E''');  [[Betânia do Piauí]], [[Piauí]] state  ('''W''')
* Area - 1005.06&nbsp;km²
* Elevation - 630 m
* Hydrography - Brigida and Garças rivers
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]]- hot and dry
* Annual average temperature - 23.9 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 717&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activity in '''Santa Filomena''' is [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[goat]]s, [[donkeys]], [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[manioc]] and [[bean]]s.
