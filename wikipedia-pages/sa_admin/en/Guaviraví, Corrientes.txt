'''Guaviraví''', is an Argentinean municipality, located in [[San Martín Department, Corrientes|San Martín Department]] in the [[Corrientes Province]]. Its capital city is called Villa Escobar. It is located on [[National Route 14 (Argentina)|National Route 14]], 5 km from the [[Uruguay River]].

== Communication routes ==
The main communication route is [[National Route 14 (Argentina)|National Route 14]], which links it to the south with [[Paso de los Libres]] and to the north with [[La Cruz, Corrientes|La Cruz]]. In the locality there is the station 25 de Febrero of the [[General Urquiza Railway]].
