'''Lagoa Formosa''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the northwest of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 18,111 people living in a total area of {{cvt|844|km2}}. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of [[Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaíba (mesoregion)|Triângulo Mineiro e Alto Paranaiba]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Patos de Minas]].  It became a municipality in 1962.

==Geography==
The urban center  is located at an elevation of 902 meters 19&nbsp;km southeast of [[Patos de Minas]] on federal highway BR-354.  Neighboring municipalities are: [[Patos de Minas]] (N), [[Carmo do Paranaíba]] (E), [[Carmo do Paranaíba]] (S), and [[Patos de Minas]] (W).

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and agriculture, especially the growing of coffee.  The GDP in 2005 was R$ R$103 million, of which 50 million came from services, 06 million came from industry, and 42 million came from agriculture.  Lagoa Formosa is in the top tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  {{as of|2007}} there was 01 banking agency in the town.  There was a modest retail infrastructure serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  There were 2,647 automobiles in all of the municipality (2007), about one for every 06 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

In the rural area there were 1,666 establishments occupying 62,000 hectares (planted area, 9,500 ha, and natural pasture, 45,500 ha.) (2006).  About 4,400 persons were employed in agriculture.  114 of the farms had tractors, a ratio of one in 12 farms.  There were 64,000 head of cattle in 2006.  The main crops were coffee, soybeans, beans and corn.<ref name="IBGE"/>
