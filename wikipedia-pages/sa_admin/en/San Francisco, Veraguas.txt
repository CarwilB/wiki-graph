'''San Francisco de la Montaña '''is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[San Francisco District, Panama|San Francisco District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 2,283 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of San Francisco District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,851; its population as of 2000 was 2,221.<ref name=inec/>
