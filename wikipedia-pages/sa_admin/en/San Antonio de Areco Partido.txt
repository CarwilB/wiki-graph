'''San Antonio de Areco''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] (county) in the north of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]]. 

The partido has an area of {{convert|852|sqkm|abbr=out}}, and a population of 21,333 ({{census-ar|2001}}). It is {{convert|113|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]] city, and {{convert|175|km|abbr=on}} from [[La Plata]], and its ''cabecera'' or capital is the city of [[San Antonio de Areco]].
