'''Magdalena Partido''' ({{IPA|es|maɣðaˈlena}}) is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeastern part of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 16,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1785|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Magdalina, Buenos Aires|Magdalena]], which is {{convert|87|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
