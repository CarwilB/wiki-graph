'''Atílio Vivacqua'''{{efn|Due to orthographic changes and [[Portuguese name#Spelling|Portuguese naming conventions]], the first 'a' in Vivacqua sometimes has an acute accent (´).}} is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 12,105 (2020) and its area is 223&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/5P2]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|10459|ha}} [[Serra das Torres Natural Monument]], created in 2010 to protect the mountain peaks in the area.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese|last=Hartung Gomes|first=Paulo Cesar|title=Lei Nº 9.463|date=11 June 2010|url=http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|accessdate=2016-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161028020315/http://www.al.es.gov.br/antigo_portal_ales/images/leis/html/9.463.htm|archive-date=28 October 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==History==
Until 10 April 1964, Atílio Vivacqua was a district of [[Cachoeiro de Itapemirim]] called Marapé.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?lang=&codmun=320070&search=%7C%7Cinfogr%25E1ficos:-hist%25F3rico|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Espírito Santo {{!}} Atilio Vivacqua {{!}} Histórico|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-11}}</ref> The municipality is named for Senator Atílio Vivácqua (1894–1961).<ref>{{cite web|title=História|url=http://www.pmav.es.gov.br/historia.php|website=Município de Atílio Vivacqua|accessdate=11 May 2016|language=pt|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170114165744/http://www.pmav.es.gov.br/historia.php|archive-date=14 January 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref>
