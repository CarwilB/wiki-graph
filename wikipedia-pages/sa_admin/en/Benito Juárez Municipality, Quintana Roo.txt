'''Benito Juárez''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Quintana Roo|eleven municipalities]] of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Quintana Roo]].  Most of its population reside in the municipal seat, [[Cancún]]. It is named after the 19th century [[President of Mexico|president]] and statesman [[Benito Juárez]].

== Geography ==
Benito Juárez is located in the north of the state with the [[Caribbean Sea]] as its eastern border. It borders on the municipalities of [[Isla Mujeres Municipality|Isla Mujeres]] to the north, [[Puerto Morelos Municipality|Puerto Morelos]] to the south, and [[Lázaro Cárdenas, Quintana Roo|Lázaro Cárdenas]] to the west. The municipality covers an area {{Convert|1664|km2}} which is 3.72% of the territory of the state.

[[Puerto Juárez, Quintana Roo|Puerto Juárez]], the primary location of the original population of the area before the founding of the city of [[Cancún]], was considered a separate location from 1960 until 1990, when it was abolished as such and incorporated as one of Cancún's districts.

==History==
The [[Mayan culture]] occupied the territory and they are still subsisting. In Cancún are different types of archeological monuments like "Ruinas de el Rey". At the time the town of Cancún was founded (1902) the majority of its business activity was the production and export gum and "palo de tinte". They transported it to [[Puerto Morelos]] for the translation to [[Cozumel]] and from there it was exported to other countries.

After World War II gum production dropped. In the 1960s, other commercial activities were started. In the 1960s the "island of Cancún" was only occupied at certain times of the year,<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cancun.gob.mx/cancun/index.php?option=com_content&task=view&id=50&Itemid=9|title=Benito Juárez History|accessdate=2009-11-14}}</ref> however by 1974 the first hotel was opened in what is now known as the [[Hotel Zone (Cancún)|Hotel Zone]]. This was also the year Quintana Roo that was recognized as a state.  At the time Benito Juarez was founded, it was part the [[Isla Mujeres (municipality)|Isla Mujeres territory]] and received municipality status one year later in 1975.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM23quintanaroo/municipios/23005a.html|title=Quintana Roo - Benito Juárez|access-date=2021-11-05|archive-date=2018-01-12|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180112234515/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM23quintanaroo/municipios/23005a.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2016, the [[Puerto Morelos (municipality)|municipality of Puerto Morelos]] was split off from Benito Juárez.
