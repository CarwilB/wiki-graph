'''Mejía''' is a municipality of [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]], [[Venezuela]]. Its only parish, [[San Antonio del Golfo]], is also the [[municipal seat]]. As of 2021, it has a population of 18,144.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones Regionales y Municipales – Municipio Mejía |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/web/mapas/mapas_cargos_a_elegir/documentos/17_ce_sucre_concejales_2021.pdf |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral |access-date=5 February 2024 |pages=11–12 |date=30 November 2021}}</ref>
