'''Licey al Medio''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Santiago Province (Dominican Republic)|Santiago province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]]. Within the municipality there is one [[municipal district]] (''distrito municipal''): Las Palomas.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113 |title=Listado de Codigos de Provincias, Municipio y Distritos Municipales, Actualizada a Junio 20 del 2006 |author=Oficina Nacional de Estadistica, Departamento de Cartografia, Division de Limites y Linderos |language=Spanish |accessdate=2007-01-24 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070803184926/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman |archivedate=August 3, 2007 }}</ref> The [[Cibao International Airport]] is located here.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Ponce |first=Miguel |date=2018-07-07 |title=Licey al Medio: entre lucha social y puntal de desarrollo |url=https://www.elcaribe.com.do/panorama/region-norte/licey-al-medio-entre-lucha-social-y-puntal-de-desarrollo/ |access-date=2024-04-21 |website=www.elcaribe.com.do |language=es}}</ref>

== Economy ==
The main economic activities of Licey al Medio are the swine and poultry production, as well as a robust metalworking industry. Licey holds economic exchange links with [[Tamboril, Dominican Republic|Tamboril]], [[Santiago de los Caballeros|Santiago]], [[Moca, Dominican Republic|Moca]] and [[Santo Domingo]], mainly for its production of chickens and eggs. Licey has an industrial park zone containing 7 companies, with approximately 275 employees in total.
