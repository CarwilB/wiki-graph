'''Tiburon''' ({{IPA|fr|tibyʁɔ̃}}; {{langx|ht|Tibiwon}}) is a coastal [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Chardonnières Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 21,170 inhabitants.<ref>{{cite web|title=IHSI|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/POPTOTAL&MENAGDENS_ESTIM2009.pdf|website=INSTITUT HAÏTIEN DE STATISTIQUE ET D’INFORMATIQUE|access-date=30 August 2014}}</ref> It is situated in a valley, at the mouth of the Tiburon River.

== Geology ==
The valley of the Tiburon River and the town of Tiburon sit exactly on the [[Enriquillo–Plantain Garden fault zone|Enriquillo–Plantain Garden fault]].
