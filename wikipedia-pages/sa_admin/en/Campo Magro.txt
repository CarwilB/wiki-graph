'''Campo Magro''' is a town and suburb of [[Curitiba]] in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]]. It is a very young municipality, and its economy is growing around the road that links Curitiba to [[Castro, Paraná|Castro]], known as "Estrada do Cerne", a two way road.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 29,740.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/campo-magro/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

[[Ecotourism]] is a flourishing industry but its inconsistent growth has encouraged the inhabitants of the city to seek employment at Curitiba.

A very important source of income to the town are monthly payments from the federal and state governments for [[potable water]] provided to Curitiba.

Curitiba is expanding towards Campo Magro and is attracting real estate investment activity.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.xn--paran-1qa.org/|title=Archived copy|access-date=2018-09-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170911165654/http://xn--paran-1qa.org/|archive-date=2017-09-11|url-status=dead}}</ref>
