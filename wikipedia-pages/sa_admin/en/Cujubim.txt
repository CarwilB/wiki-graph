'''Cujubim''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 26,183 (2020) and its area is 3,864&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cod.ibge.gov.br/HX5]</ref>

The municipality contains 22% of the [[Angelim Extractive Reserve]] and 21% of the [[Ipê Extractive Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Angelim – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Angelim|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/627|accessdate=2016-08-30}}</ref><ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Ipê – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Ipê|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/3033 |accessdate=2016-08-31}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|660|ha}} [[Tucano Sustainable Yield State Forest]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLORSU Tucano – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLORSU Tucano|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6791|accessdate=2016-10-13}}</ref>
