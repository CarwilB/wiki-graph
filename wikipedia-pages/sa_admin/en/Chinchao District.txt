'''Chinchao District''' is one of twelve [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Huánuco Province|Huánuco]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its seat is Acomayo.
