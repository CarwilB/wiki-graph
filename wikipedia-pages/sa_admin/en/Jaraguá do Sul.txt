'''Jaraguá do Sul''' is a city in the north of the Brazilian state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].

==History==
The city was founded on July 25, 1876, by engineer and [[Brazilian Army]] colonel Emílio Carlos Jourdan and his family. The city was originally named Jaraguá, but was later renamed to Jaraguá do Sul due to an older city already named [[Jaraguá, Goiás|Jaraguá]] in Goiás state.<ref name="sc">{{in lang|pt}} [http://www.sc.gov.br/clipping_governo/noticia_int.asp?str_data=13/07/2006&cd_noticia=134218&str_retorno=clipping.asp  Os prós e os contras de Jaraguá – Government of Santa Catarina] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110524050605/http://www.sc.gov.br/clipping_governo/noticia_int.asp?str_data=13%2F07%2F2006&cd_noticia=134218&str_retorno=clipping.asp |date=May 24, 2011 }}</ref>

==Etymology==
''Jaraguá'' means ''Lord of the Valley'' in a [[Tupi–Guarani languages|Tupi–Guarani language]]. It was the name given by the indigenous population of the city to the Boa Vista Hill (Morro da Boa Vista).<ref>{{in lang|pt}} [https://ocp.news/entretenimento/por-que-jaragua-do-sul-tem-esse-nome – Por que Jaraguá do Sul tem esse nome? 4 curiosidades sobre a história da cidade]</ref>

==Geography==
It borders [[Campo Alegre, Santa Catarina|Campo Alegre]] and [[São Bento do Sul]] to the north; [[Blumenau]], [[Massaranduba, Santa Catarina|Massaranduba]], [[Pomerode]] and [[Rio dos Cedros]] to the south; [[Guaramirim]], [[Joinville]] and [[Schroeder, Santa Catarina|Schroeder]] to the east and [[Corupá]] to the west.

The [[Rio Itapocu|Itapocu River]] crosses the city and is considered Jaraguá do Sul's most important river, alongside [[Jaraguá River]] and [[Itapocuzinho River]], who are also the main affluents of the former.

The [[climate]] is considered [[temperate]], with average annual temperature around 21&nbsp;°C. In summer temperatures often exceed 35&nbsp;°C, although they may reach 40&nbsp;°C in a few days. The winter is relatively cold for Brazilian standards, with an average minimum temperature around 12&nbsp;°C in the months of June and July. [[Frost]] occurs almost every winter. Temperatures of zero or below are rare, and were most recently reached on July 14, 2000. The lowest temperature occurred on July 19, 1975, when thermometers recorded −2&nbsp;°C. The highest temperature ever recorded was 42.5&nbsp;°C on January 3, 1973.

==Sports==
[[Malwee/Jaraguá]], formerly known as Associação Desportiva Jaraguá, is a successful [[futsal]] club, which has won the [[Liga Futsal|national league]] twice. In 2007, the city's arena, named [[Arena Jaraguá]], hosted the final matches of the [[Grand Prix de Futsal]].<ref name="grandprixdefutsal">{{in lang|pt}} [http://www.futsalbrasil.com.br/noticias/noticia.php?cd_noticia=3926 3º Grand Prix de Futsal: Definidos os quatro grupos da competição – Futsal Brasil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090707230518/http://www.futsalbrasil.com.br/noticias/noticia.php?cd_noticia=3926 |date=July 7, 2009 }}</ref> The arena also hosted two [[Ultimate Fighting Championship]] events, [[UFC on FX: Belfort vs. Rockhold|UFC on FX 8]] in 2013 and [[UFC Fight Night: Machida vs. Mousasi|UFC Fight Night 36]] in 2014.

The city's [[association football|football (soccer)]] club is [[Grêmio Esportivo Juventus]]. The retired goalkeeper [[Eduardo Roberto Stinghen]], nicknamed Ado, was born in the city in 1946. [[Filipe Luís]], who previously played for [[Flamengo]], was also born in the city.

==Industry==
The local economy is mainly based in metallurgy and clothing industries. The city was the origin for several worldwide companies, like [[WEG Industries]] (electric motors and industrial electronics), Marisol (clothing), Duas Rodas (food seasoning), Malwee (clothing), Menegotti (construction equipment) and many others. The city is the 7th largest economy of the state.<ref>{{in lang|pt}} [https://www.jdv.com.br/sc-tem-cinco-entre-os-maiores-pibs-do-brasil-jaragua-tem-a-setima-economia – SC tem cinco entre os maiores PIBs do Brasil; Jaraguá tem a sétima economia]</ref>

==FEMUSC==
Jaraguá do Sul is home to the Festival de Música de Santa Catarina (FEMUSC) which was created by [[Alex Klein]].  The festival is held in late January and attracts students and faculty from all over the world. The program involves faculty and students recitals, orchestral and band performances, lessons and masterclasses.
<ref>{{in lang|pt}} [https://www.alexoboeklein.com/femusc – FEMUSC]</ref>

==Schützenfest==
The [[Schützenfest]] (also known in Brazil as ''Festa dos Atiradores'') is a traditional festival held annually since 1988, celebrated in October in Jaraguá do Sul. It is part of the popular festivals celebrated in that month in Santa Catarina state due to the German colonization in the region.<ref name="schutzenfest">{{in lang|pt}} [http://turismo.jaraguadosul.com.br/modules/xt_conteudo/index.php?id=240 Schützenfest  – Turismo de Jaraguá do Sul] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080316090837/http://turismo.jaraguadosul.com.br/modules/xt_conteudo/index.php?id=240 |date=March 16, 2008 }}</ref>

==Demographics==

===Ethnic groups===
The first inhabitants of the area were the [[Indigenous peoples in Brazil|indigenous people]] of the [[Xokleng people|Xokleng]] and the [[Kaingang people|Kaingang]].<ref name="guia">{{in lang|pt}} [http://www.guiasantacatarina.com.br/jaraguadosul/cidade.php3 Jaraguá do Sul – Guia Santa Catarina]</ref> The following ethnic groups later immigrated to the city: Germans, Hungarians, Italians, Poles, and [[Afro-Brazilian|African]]s, who were brought to the city as slaves.<ref name="prefeitura">{{in lang|pt}} [http://cultura.jaraguadosul.com.br/modules/xt_conteudo/index.php?id=352 Etnias – Prefeitura Municipal de Jaraguá do Sul] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110728172628/http://cultura.jaraguadosul.com.br/modules/xt_conteudo/index.php?id=352 |date=July 28, 2011 }}</ref>
By 2002 City Hall research concluded that 45% of the people from Jaraguá do Sul are descended from Germans. The other main groups were Italians (25%), Poles (6%), and Hungarians (3%); 21% had other ancestry.[33]

== Infrastructure ==

=== Transports ===

==== Airports ====
Jaraguá do Sul has a municipal aerodrome ([[ICAO airport code|ICAO]]: '''SIH4'''), used for particular or business flights.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-11-27 |title=AISWEB - Informações Aeronáuticas do Brasil |url=https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=aerodromos&codigo=SIH4 |website=aisweb.decea.mil.br}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> For commercial flights, inhabitants use the [[Joinville-Lauro Carneiro de Loyola Airport|Joinville Airport]], located about 50km from the city, and the [[Navegantes Airport|Navegantes International Airport]].<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-11-27 |date=2019-06-10 |first=Elissandro |language=pt-BR |last=Sutil |title=Veja quais são os aeroportos que ficam mais perto de Jaraguá do Sul e região |url=https://ocp.news/cotidiano/veja-quais-sao-os-aeroportos-que-ficam-mais-perto-de-jaragua-do-sul-e-regiao |website=OCP News {{!}} As melhores notícias e histórias de Santa Catarina}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

==== Urban ====
Jaraguá do Sul's urban terminal is located in the Getúlio Vargas Avenue, in the city's downtown, and has a non-integrated municipal transport system currently operated by Viação Canarinho.

==== Intermunicipal bus system and Highways ====
The city has an intermunicipal bus station. In operation since 1992, it is located in the Vila Baependi neighbourhood and currently receives about 600 people per day. The building also hosts the Instituto de Meio Ambiente de Santa Catarina (IMA), the Association of Councilors of the Itapocu Valley, The Rã Bugio Institute, and the Centro de Valorização da Vida (CVV).<ref>{{cite web|access-date=2024-09-15 |archive-date=2024-11-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241127200357/https://www.jaraguadosul.sc.gov.br/terminal-rodoviario |title=Prefeitura de Jaraguá do Sul |url=https://www.jaraguadosul.sc.gov.br/terminal-rodoviario |website=www.jaraguadosul.sc.gov.br}}<!-- auto-translated from Portuguese by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> Some of the companies that operate the terminal are: Auto Viação Catarinense, Reunidas S.A. Transportes Coletivos, Empresa União de Transportes, Viação Nossa Senhora dos Navegantes, Empresa União Cascavel de Transporte e Turismo Ltda., Viação Itapemirim S.A., Brasil Sul Linhas Rodoviárias, Expresso São Bento, Auto Viação Venâncio Aires, Empresa de Ônibus Nossa Senhora da Penha and Viação Canarinho.

The city can be accessed through the following highways:

* [[BR-280 (Brazil highway)|BR-280]] – Accesses other regions of Santa Catarina and the neighbouring state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]].
* SC-110 – Accesses the neighbouring towns of [[Pomerode]] and [[Rio dos Cedros]].
* SC-108 – Accesses the cities of [[Joinville]] and [[Blumenau]].
