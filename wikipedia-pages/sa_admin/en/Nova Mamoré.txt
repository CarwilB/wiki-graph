'''Nova Mamoré''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. In 2020, the population of Nova Mamoré was 31,392. Its area is 10,072&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/C8E]</ref>

==Economy==
The [[economy]] of Nova Mamoré is based on mainly [[agriculture]] and [[wood]]. Its main agriculture comes from the [[chestnut]], [[cupuaçu]], [[acerola]], [[açaí]], [[guava]], [[mango]] and [[pineapple]] plants. There are approximately 325,000 [[cattle]] in Nova Mamoré. In the 1970s and 1980s, the town produced a large amount of [[gold]], but the main mineral source now is water. There are around 27 [[factories]], most of them producing [[timber]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.novamamore.ro.gov.br/informacoes.asp |title=PREFEITURA MUNICIPAL DE NOVA MAMORÉ |website=www.novamamore.ro.gov.br |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100223181145/http://www.novamamore.ro.gov.br/informacoes.asp |archive-date=2010-02-23}} </ref>

==Reserves==
There are three protected areas for indigenous groups in the municipality: Terras Indígenas Igarapé Ribeirão, Reserva dos Karipunas and Terras Indígenas Laje<ref name="municip">{{cite web |url=http://www.novamamore.ro.gov.br/municipio/localizacao |title=Localização |website=www.novamamore.ro.gov.br |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305082348/http://www.novamamore.ro.gov.br/municipio/localizacao |archive-date=2016-03-05}} </ref>
Ninety-five percent of the area of ''{{Lang|pt|Parque Estadual Guajará-Mirim}}'' ("[[Guajará-Mirim State Park]]") is located within Nova Mamoré; the remainder is in neighboring [[Guajará-Mirim]].<ref name="municip"/>
Nova Mamoré contains 27% of the {{convert|204632|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Ouro Preto Extractive Reserve]], created in 1990.{{sfn|RESEX do Rio Ouro Preto – ISA|loc=Informações gerais}}
The municipality contains 13% of the {{convert|197364|ha|adj=on}} [[Jaci Paraná Extractive Reserve]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Jaci Paraná – ISA}}|language=pt
