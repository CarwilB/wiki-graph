'''Andamarca''' (from [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Anta Marka''', meaning "copper village") is one of fifteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Concepción Province, Peru|Concepción Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]].
