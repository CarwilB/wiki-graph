'''Chetilla District''' is one of twelve [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Cajamarca Province|Cajamarca]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Along with Porcón ([[Cajamarca District]]) and [[Los Baños del Inca District|Los Baños del Inca]] but in contrast to the rest of the Cajamarca Region, Chetilla is a traditional [[Quechua languages|Quechua]]-speaking area. However, [[Cajamarca Quechua]] is under strong pressure from [[Spanish language|Spanish]].<ref>Ethnologue.com: Cajamarca Quechua. http://www.ethnologue.com/show_language.asp?code=qvc</ref>
