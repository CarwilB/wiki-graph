'''Cacaulândia ''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. 
It has an area of {{convert|1,961.778|km2|sqmi}}.
It has a [[tropical savanna climate]].
As of 2019 the estimated population was 6230.

==Geography==

Cacaulândia is in the state of Rondônia, Brazil.{{sfn|Geonames}} It has an area of {{convert|1,961.778|km2}} as of 2018.{{sfn|IBGE}} The elevation above sea level is about {{convert|142|m}}.{{sfn|Geonames}}

===Climate===

The [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen climate type]] is Aw : [[Tropical savanna climate]].{{sfn|mindat}}
The average annual temperature is {{convert|24|C}}.{{sfn|NASAearth}}
The average annual rainfall is {{convert|1,890|mm}}.{{sfn|NASArain}}

==History==

Cacaulândia has its origins in a Northwest Brazil Integrated Development Program (Polonoroeste) project to support farmers in the region through a Núcleo Urbano de Apoio Rural (NUAR: Urban Center of Rural Support).
It was named Cacaulândia because it was a large cocoa producing region.
{{convert|40|ha}} of land were donated for the NUAR from lots at the intersection of TB-65 with Line C-15.
The region was split off from the municipality of [[Ariquemes]] on 13 February 1992, and became a separate municipality and district named Cacaulândia.
As of 2007 it remained a municipality with one district.{{sfn|História : IBGE}}

==Demographics==

The population in the 2010 census was 5,736.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
The estimated population as of 2020 was 6,269.{{sfn|IBGE}}
Population density as of 2010 was {{convert|2.92|PD/km2|PD/sqmi}}.{{sfn|IBGE}}
As of 2010, 95.5% of the population had attended school between the ages of 6 and 14. 
Also as of 2010, the municipal [[Human Development Index]] was 0.646.
This compares to 0.283 in 1991 and 0.454 in 2000.{{sfn|IBGE}}

On the 2010 census religion was reported as [[Catholic Church|Catholic]] by 3,071 people, [[Evangelicalism|Evangelical]] by 1,607 people and [[Animism]] by 0 people.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}

In 2017, the average monthly salary of formal workers was 2.1 minimum wages. 
Formally employed people were 7.6% of the total population. 
Households with monthly income of up to half a minimum wage per person represent 41.2% of the population.{{sfn|IBGEpan}}
