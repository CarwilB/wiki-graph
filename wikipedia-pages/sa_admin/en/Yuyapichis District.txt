'''Yuyapichis District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Puerto Inca Province|Puerto Inca]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

==Languages==
According to the [[Peru 2007 Census|2007 census]], [[Spanish language|Spanish]] was spoken by 90.3% of the population as their first language, while 4.5% spoke [[Asháninka language|Asháninka]], 4.3% spoke [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 0.5% spoke [[Aymara language|Aymara]]. 0.1% spoke other indigenous languages and 0.0% spoke foreign languages.
