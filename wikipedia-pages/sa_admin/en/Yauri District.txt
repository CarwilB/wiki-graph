'''Yauri District''', also known as '''Espinar''' or '''Kiskachay District''', is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Espinar Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its seat is [[Yauri, Peru|Yauri]] (Yawri).

== Geography ==
