'''Morpará''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Morpará covers {{convert|2.090,926|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 8,497 with a population density of 1.28 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>

== History ==
Historically, Morpará developed as a small rural settlement in the interior of Bahia, and over time became a local center for agriculture and livestock.<ref>{{cite web |title=Morpará — Cidades e Estados |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/ba/morpara.html
 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=15 September 2025}}</ref> Its economy is primarily based on farming, cattle raising, and small-scale commerce that serves the surrounding rural communities.<ref>{{cite web |title=Perfil dos Municípios Brasileiros |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/estatisticas/downloads-estatisticas.html
 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=15 September 2025}}</ref> Despite its small size, the town has basic educational and healthcare facilities and functions as an important support hub for nearby villages.<ref>{{cite web |title=Rede de Saúde da Bahia |url=http://www.saude.ba.gov.br
 |publisher=Secretaria de Saúde do Estado da Bahia (SESAB) |access-date=15 September 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Escolas no Município de Morpará |url=http://escolas.educacao.ba.gov.br
 |publisher=Secretaria de Educação do Estado da Bahia (SEC-BA) |access-date=15 September 2025}}</ref>
