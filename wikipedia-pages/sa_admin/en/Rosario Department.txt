The '''Rosario Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Rosario''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the {{province|Santa Fe|Argentina}}. It is located on the south of the province, with its eastern border coinciding with the provincial border along the [[Paraná River]]. It has an area of {{convert|1,890|km2}} and a population of 1.3 million, with a population density of about 700 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>. This department is the most populated in the province (comprising more than one third of the total population).

The department's head town is the city of [[Rosario]]; most of the population is concentrated in its [[metropolitan area]] ([[Greater Rosario]]), which reaches north into the neighboring [[San Lorenzo Department]]. Other important cities and towns are [[Ibarlucea]], [[Granadero Baigorria]], [[Funes, Santa Fe|Funes]], [[Pérez, Santa Fe|Pérez]], [[Piñero (Santa Fe)|Piñero]], [[Zavalla, Santa Fe|Zavalla]], [[Pueblo Nuevo, Santa Fe|Pueblo Nuevo]], [[Villa Diego, Santa Fe|Villa Diego]], [[Álvarez, Santa Fe|Álvarez]], [[Villa Amelia, Santa Fe|Villa Amelia]], [[Acebal, Santa Fe|Acebal]], [[Uranga, Santa Fe|Uranga]], [[Arroyo Seco, Santa Fe|Arroyo Seco]], [[Fighiera, Santa Fe|Fighiera]], and [[Coronel Bogado, Santa Fe|Coronel Bogado]].

Limiting the Rosario Department to the south is the [[Constitución Department, Santa Fe|Constitución Department]] (whose head town is [[Villa Constitución, Santa Fe|Villa Constitución]]) and to the east and north, the [[San Lorenzo Department]] (head town [[San Lorenzo, Santa Fe|San Lorenzo]]). On the eastern border, along the Paraná River, is the heart of the Industrial Corridor, a productive area with a concentration of light industries (such as soybean oil and pod processing) and major ports.
