'''Mariano Roque Alonso''' ({{IPA|es|maˈɾja.no ˈro.ke aˈlon.so}}) is a district and city located in the [[Central Department]] of [[Paraguay]], in the [[Gran Asunción]] metropolitan area. The city has a population of approximately 85,000 people.

The city was founded in 1944 and is located between the [[Paraguay River]] and the Transchaco Road, approximately 18&nbsp;kilometers away from the city of [[Asunción]]. This optimal location has allowed the city to experience rapid commercial, industrial, and social development.

The town is known for the fact that, on the afternoon of February 4, 1996, [[LAC Colombia Flight 028]] crashed while taking off from [[Silvio Pettirossi International Airport]], killing four crew members and eighteen people on the ground. With a total of 22 people dead, it is the worst air accident in the history of Paraguay.

The economy of Mariano Roque Alonso is primarily supported by commercial, port and fishing activities.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Comercial, portuaria y pesquera – 73° Aniversario Mariano Roque Alonso – ABC Color |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/comercial/73-aniversario-mariano-roque-alonso/comercial-portuaria-y-pesquera-1735964.html |access-date=June 12, 2025 |website=www.abc.com.py |language=es}}</ref> It is also the home of the Mariano Roque Alonso International Expo Fair,<ref>{{Cite web |last=DIGITAL |first=ADN |date=July 5, 2018 |title=Ultiman detalles para la gran Expo Mariano Roque Alonso |url=https://www.adndigital.com.py/ultiman-detalles-la-gran-expo-mariano-roque-alonso/ |access-date=June 12, 2025 |website=ADN Digital |language=es}}</ref> Paraguay's largest annual [[Trade fair|Expo]] show, where companies and industries showcase their latest products.
