'''Carapebus''' ({{IPA|pt|kɐɾapeˈbu(j)ʃ}}, {{IPA|pt|kɐɾapiˈbu(j)ʃ|}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 16,586 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|306|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

''Carapebus Esporte Clube'' and ''Associação Atlética Carapebus'' are the municipality's [[association football|football (soccer)]] clubs.
