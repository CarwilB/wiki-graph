'''Ulcumayo''' (from local [[Quechua language|Quechua]] '''Ullqu Mayu''' or '''Ullqumayu''', "male river") is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Junín Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
