'''Consolación del Sur''' is a municipality and town in the [[Pinar del Río Province]] of [[Cuba]]. Also called the ''Athens of Vueltabajo'', it was founded in 1690.<ref name="guije">{{Cite web |author=Guije.com |title=Consolación del Sur |url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/psur/index.htm |accessdate=2007-10-07 |language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
It is located in a major [[rice]]-growing area; [[tobacco]] is also cultivated as in much of the [[Pinar del Río Province]].

Wards ({{lang|es|consejos populares}}) of the municipality include [[Villa I (Consolación del Sur)|Villa I]], [[Villa II (Consolación del Sur)|Villa II]], [[Pueblo Nuevo (Consolación del Sur)|Pueblo Nuevo]], [[Arroyo de Agua (Consolación del Sur)|Arroyo de Agua]],  [[Crucero de Echevarría]], [[El Canal (Cuba)|El Canal]], [[Entronque de Herradura]], [[Herradura (Cuba)| Herradura]], [[Entronque de Pilotos]], [[Pilotos (Cuba)|Pilotos]], [[Puerta de Golpe]], and [[Alonso Rojas (Cuba)|Alonso Rojas]].

==Demographics==
In 2004, the municipality of Consolación del Sur had a population of 87,500.<ref name=atenas>{{Cite web| url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm| archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20060714001016/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm| url-status=dead| archive-date=2006-07-14| title=2004 Population trends, by Province and Municipality| author=Atenas.cu| year=2004| accessdate=2007-10-07| language=es}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1112|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|78.7|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Attractions==
There are three churches in this town, one [[Roman Catholic]] parish (dedicated to "[[Nuestra Señora de la Candelaria]]", or Our Lady of the Purification) and two [[Protestant church]]es. The town has two parks; the [[Catholic church]] is near the central park. There is also the [[Consolación del Sur Municipal Museum]].
