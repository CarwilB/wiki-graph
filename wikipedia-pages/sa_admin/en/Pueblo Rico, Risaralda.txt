'''Pueblo Rico''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Risaralda Department|Risaralda]], [[Colombia]]. About 97 km away from the capital [[Pereira, Colombia|Pereira]]. In 2023 the town had an estimated population of 12,423.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Pueblo Rico, Risaralda en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/risaralda/pueblo-rico |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
Founded in 1904 by Hilarión Pinzón, Leandro Tamayo, Justo Grajales, Sinforoso Leyva, Bibiano Chalarca, José Terán, Luis Ángel and Segundo Ramos. In 1884 by a group of [[Antioquia Department|Antioqueños]], from [[El Carmen de Atrato|Carmen del Atrato]], [[Chocó Department|Chocó]] and it was officially founded in the year 1907. It belonged to [[Chocó Department|Chocó]] until 1912, when it was annexed to Caldas through Law 31. 

In 1925, the construction of the Cintó penal colony began, on the left bank of the San Juan River, which is now closed. Around it, in 1953, the town of Santa Cecilia emerged, which was declared a township that same year. By that time, the first [[Chocó Department|Chocoan]] families had already reached this place. In 1940, the township of Villa Claret was established, a small hamlet formed northeast of the municipal seat. With these three population centers the Municipality of Pueblo Rico is created.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Risaralda |first=Gobernación de |date=2016-09-28 |title=Pueblo Rico |url=https://www.risaralda.gov.co/publicaciones/100241/pueblo-rico/ |access-date=2023-09-28 |website=Gobernación de Risaralda |language=spanish}}</ref>

== Climate ==
