'''Puerto Peñasco''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-date=July 31, 2012 }}</ref> As of 2015, the municipality had a total population of 62,177 inhabitants.<ref name="inegi.org.mx"/> The only locality with a significant population is the municipal seat, also named [[Puerto Peñasco]], which contains almost 99% of the municipality's population. 

Most of the municipality consists of empty stretches of the [[Gran Desierto de Altar|Gran Desierto de Altar (Altar Desert)]] adjacent to and south of the international border with the U.S. state of [[Arizona]].

==Geography==
The municipality lies at the northern end of the [[Gulf of California|Gulf of California (Sea of Cortez)]]. 

As municipal seat, the city of Puerto Peñasco has governing jurisdiction over 27 other named communities,<ref name="INEGI">{{cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=INEGI Census 2010 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110613183420/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2011-06-13 }}</ref> which cover a territory of {{convert|9,774.5|km2}}.<ref name="encmuc">{{Cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_sonora |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México Sonora  Puerto Peñasco |publisher=INAFED |location=Mexico |language=es |access-date=December 15, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081201232501/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_sonora |archive-date=December 1, 2008 }}</ref> The municipality has a total population of 62,177 inhabitants.<ref name="inegi.org.mx"/> The municipality borders with the municipalities of [[San Luis Río Colorado Municipality]], [[Plutarco Elías Calles Municipality]], and [[Caborca Municipality]]. To its north-northwest is [[Yuma County, Arizona]], in the United States, and to the south is the Gulf of California.<ref name="encmuc"/>

==The municipality==
[[Image:20091214 - Puerto Penasco -335.jpg|thumb|right|200px|alt=View to the Pinacate peaks from the sand dunes|Pinacate sand dunes]]

The land is generally flat with the small mountain range of Sonoyta to the north and east of the area. Most of the territory is the Altar Desert, which extends north and east of the city, and a volcanic zone called [[Pinacate Peaks|Sierra El Pinacate]].<ref name="encmuc"/><ref name="romo">{{cite journal |last=Romo Cedano |first=Luis |date=June 2007 |title=Puerto Peñasco: Mar y desierto al extremo |trans-title=Puerto Peñasco:Sea and desert to the extreme |journal=Revista Travesias |location=Mexico |url=http://www.revistatravesias.com/numero-65/fin-de-semana/puerto-penasco-mar-y-desierto-al-extremo.html |language=es |access-date=December 15, 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090916190727/http://www.revistatravesias.com/numero-65/fin-de-semana/puerto-penasco-mar-y-desierto-al-extremo.html |archive-date=September 16, 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality has 110&nbsp;km of beaches.<ref name="songob">{{cite web |url=http://www.sonoraturismo.gob.mx/puerto-penasco-sonora.htm |title=Destinos de Sonora-Puerto Peñasco |publisher=Government of Sonora |location=Mexico |language=es |trans-title=Sonora Destinations – Puerto Peñasco |access-date=2009-12-15 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100306042148/http://sonoraturismo.gob.mx/puerto-penasco-sonora.htm |archive-date=2010-03-06 }}</ref>  Near the city are fields of sand dunes of the Altar Desert, which is one of the driest in the world.<ref name="encmuc"/> ATVs and motocross are permitted on the sand dunes.<ref name="explorando">{{cite web |url= http://www.explorandomexico.com.mx/city/69/Puerto-Penasco/about/ |title= Sobre Puerto Peñasco |publisher=Explorando Mexico |location=Mexico |language=es |trans-title=About Puerto Peñasco |access-date=2009-12-15 }}</ref> Most vegetation and animal life is typical for sandy deserts, scrub brush, reptiles (including the [[Gila monster]]) and small mammals.<ref name="encmuc"/>

Farming and livestock is nearly non-existent due to the dry conditions. Some industry exists, associated with fishing such as canning and freezing. The commercial sector of the economy support 57% of the population selling food, clothes, pharmaceuticals and other items to both the local population and to tourists. The second most important economic sector is fishing, especially shrimp fishing.<ref name="encmuc"/> Tourism drives most of the commercial sector. The municipality receives about 1,000 visitors a day, with about 85% coming from the U.S. state of Arizona.<ref name="songob"/>
[[File:Pinacate volcanic field.jpg|thumb|View of the Pinacate volcanic area from the Elegante Crater]]

'''[[El Pinacate y Gran Desierto de Altar Biosphere Reserve|El Pinacate]]''' is a volcanic region and a biosphere reserve that covers 714,556.6 hectares which covers parts of the municipalities of Puerto Peñasco, [[San Luis Río Colorado Municipality|San Luis Río Colorado]] and [[General Plutarco Elías Calles Municipality|Plutarco Elías Calles]]. This area has been considered one of the most arid and inhospitable of the Sonoran Desert. In spite of this, the area contains a wide variety of wildlife and evidence of human habitation from ancient times, which has attracted the attention of both tourists and researchers. It contains 553 species of plants with cactuses such as [[saguaro]]s, [[cylindropuntia|chollas]] and [[ocotillo]]s dominating. With the rainy season, the area explodes with short-lived wildflowers. The area also contains 41 species of mammals, 237 species of birds, 45 species of reptiles, and four types of amphibians. It was declared a protected biosphere reserve by the Mexican government in 1993. The entrance to the biosphere park is located 50&nbsp;km from the city of Puerto Peñasco. The nucleus of the biosphere consists of the Sierra el Pinacate, Adair Bay and Sierra del Rosario which cannot be developed under any circumstances. The main attraction for tourists is the volcanic craters. The major ones are named Badillo, Molina (or Trébol), McDougal (the largest) and Caravajales. In addition, there is the Grande Volcano which stands at 3,200 feet tall, and has a depth of 950&nbsp;ft.<ref name="pinagob">{{cite web |url=http://www.sonoraturismo.gob.mx/el-pinacate.htm |title=El Pinacate y Gran Desierto de Altar - Reservas Ecológicas de Sonora |publisher=Government of Sonora |location=Mexico |language=es |trans-title=El Pinacate and the Great Altar Desert – Ecological Reserves of Sonora |access-date=2009-12-15 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090202045527/http://sonoraturismo.gob.mx/el-pinacate.htm |archive-date=2009-02-02 }}</ref>

'''San Jorge Island''', also known as '''Bird Island''', is really a small group of rocky outcroppings that are just above the surface of the sea about 40&nbsp;km southeast of the city. The rocks are capped in white like the Alps, not with snow, but with the [[guano]] of innumerable sea birds such as [[seagull]]s and [[pelican]]s, which live here. Below the birds live approximately 3,000 sea lions very close to the water. It is the largest concentration of [[sea lion]]s in Mexico. It is possible to [[kayak]] and/or [[Snorkeling|snorkel]] here.<ref name="romo"/>

Nine native cultures can be found in the municipality. The Guarijíos have a wide variety of crafts such as figures made with natural materials such as palm fronds and clay. They also make hats from a variety of natural fibers. The Mayos, who call themselves the Yoreme, have a rich oral tradition. Other groups include the Opatas, the Papagos, the Pimas, the Seris, the Yaquis, the Cucapa, and the Kikapu, all of whom are noted for basketmaking using desert plants.<ref name="buenviaje">{{cite web |url=http://www.revistabuenviaje.com/conocemexico/destinos/sonora/puertopenasco/puertopenasco.html |title=Puerto Peñasco |publisher=Buen Viaje Diario Turistico on line |language=es |access-date=2009-12-15 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100103041051/http://www.revistabuenviaje.com/conocemexico/destinos/sonora/puertopenasco/puertopenasco.html |archive-date=2010-01-03 }}</ref>
