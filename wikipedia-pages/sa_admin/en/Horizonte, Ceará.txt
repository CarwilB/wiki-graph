'''Horizonte''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Ceará]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of Brazil.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=230523|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Ceará {{!}} Horizonte|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-11}}</ref>

==Automotive==
Among some Enterprises locally, there was the [[Troller]], which was closed by Ford and bought by State Government who resold to Comexport. This together [[General Motors do Brasil]] planned to the import the vehicles [[Chevrolet Captiva]] EV and [[Chevrolet Spark EUV]] to be assembled there with parts in CKD of China, started on 3 Dezember 2025, an investment of R$ 400 million of US$ 73,7 million since 2026. 10 Units a day, 5 each Model, 100 Monthly each.<ref>https://gmauthority.com/blog/2025/09/chevy-captiva-ev-to-be-assembled-in-brazil-in-2026/</ref><ref>https://gmauthority.com/blog/2025/09/brand-new-chevy-spark-euv-to-be-assembled-locally-in-brazil/</ref>
