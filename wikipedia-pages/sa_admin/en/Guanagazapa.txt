'''Guanagazapa''' ({{IPA|es|ɡwanaɣaˈsapa}}) is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department|Escuintla]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|386.2|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 
17,565 inhabitants.

==History==
As per a document from the government archives, the original settlement of Guanagazapa was destroyed by a fire in 1808, and the inhabitants were forced to move to a new location. The population, which included 157 indigenous people and 25 [[Ladinos]], settled in on a new place known as Los Apantes or Los Jobales, as recorded by the mayor of [[Escuintla]] and the head priest of the parish. This location is located about {{cvt|2|km}} from the current location, and no official document is available that refers to the establishment of the municipality itself. As per a government notice on 16 June 1915, the municipality of Guanagazapa was restored along with other municipalities in the region.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Guanagazapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Guanagazapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-del-municipio-de-guanagazapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Guanagazapa is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|386.2|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Guanagazapa (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0508__guanagazapa/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the southeastern part of the department, about {{cvt|25|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla. It borders the municipalities of [[San Vicente Pacaya]] to the north, [[Iztapa]] to the south, [[Pueblo Nuevo Viñas]] and [[Taxisco]] to the east, and Escuintla  and [[Masagua]] to the west.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Guanagazapa|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Guanagazapa municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-guanagazapa-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref> The northern part of the municipality consists of mountainous terrain, the central part is the populated urban area, while the south and south east form the plains, which consist of agricultural and pastoral lands.<ref name="Geo"/>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|342.74|m}} above sea level, Guanagazapa has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|24.76|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|117.21|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/guanagazapa|title=Guanagazapa|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 17,565 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 9,130 males and 8,435 females. About 33.2% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.0% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (76.6%) was classified as rural, while 23.4% lived in urban areas.  About 68.9% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (82.2%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (17.5%) forming a significant minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 81.0%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (92.7%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
