'''Olho d'Água das Flores''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈojʊ ˈdagwɐ ˈdɐɦ ˈflo]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the west of [[Alagoas]], a [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]]. Its population is 21,738 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/olho-dagua-das-flores/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 183&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
