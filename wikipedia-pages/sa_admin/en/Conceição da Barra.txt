'''Conceição da Barra''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. The city is the northernmost coastal city of the state. Its population was 31,273 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|1188.044|km2}}.

==Geography==
The municipality contains the {{convert|3481|ha}} [[Itaúnas State Park]], created in 1991, which protects the coast up to the border with [[Bahia]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual de Itaúnas – IEMA}}|language=pt|title=Parque Estadual de Itaúnas|publisher=IEMA: Instituto Estadual de Meio Ambiente e Recursos Hídricos|url=http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/default.asp?pagina=16707|accessdate=2016-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161027192913/http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/default.asp?pagina=16707|archive-date=2016-10-27|url-status=dead}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|2817|ha}} [[Rio Preto National Forest]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional de Rio Preto|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=122|accessdate=2016-05-20}}</ref>
It contains part of the {{convert|1,503.75|ha}} [[Córrego Grande Biological Reserve]].

The climate is tropical, warm and mostly humid, with one or two dry months.
Average annual temperature is {{convert|22|to|24|C}}, and average annual rainfall is {{convert|1250|to|1500|mm}}.<ref>{{citation|url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-grande/|title=Reserva Biológica do Córrego Grande|work=Viarural|accessdate=2016-04-15|language=Portuguese|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160423125035/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/reservas-biologicas/reserva-biologica-do-corrego-grande/|archive-date=2016-04-23|url-status=usurped}}</ref>
