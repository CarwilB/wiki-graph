'''Cañasgordas''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. The population was 13,595 at the 2018 census.

==Climate==
Cañasgordas has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) with heavy rainfall year-round.
