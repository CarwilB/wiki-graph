'''Arequipa''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of the [[Arequipa Province]] in [[Peru]]. The [[downtown]] area of the city of [[Arequipa]] is located in this district. The current mayor of the district is Omar Julio Candia Aguilar.<ref>[http://documentos.jne.gob.pe/conaut/wf_reg_mun_det.aspx?arrcodigo=040000X4&ID_PERIODO=28 JNE Autoridades regionales y municipales] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131103223130/http://documentos.jne.gob.pe/conaut/wf_reg_mun_det.aspx?arrcodigo=040000X4&ID_PERIODO=28 |date=November 3, 2013 }}</ref>
