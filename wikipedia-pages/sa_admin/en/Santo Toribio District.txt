The '''Santo Toribio District''' ({{langx|es|'''Distrito de Santo Toribio'''}}) is one of 10 districts of the [[Huaylas Province]] in the [[Ancash Region]] of [[Peru]]. The capital of the district is the village of [[Santo Toribio]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.munisantotoribio.gob.pe/ |title=Official website of the Santo Toribio district |access-date=2007-05-17 |archive-date=2007-06-25 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070625165135/http://www.munisantotoribio.gob.pe/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Location==
The district is located in the north-western part of the province at an elevation of 2,860m.
