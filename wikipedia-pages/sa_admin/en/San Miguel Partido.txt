'''San Miguel Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] urban area of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

Provisional results of the 2010 census report that the  provincial subdivision has a population of about 281,120 inhabitants<ref name=Cen2010 /> in an area of {{convert|83|sqkm|abbr=on}}. Its capital city is [[San Miguel, Buenos Aires|San Miguel]], which is around {{convert|32|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
