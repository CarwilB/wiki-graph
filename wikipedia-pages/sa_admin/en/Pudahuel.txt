'''Pudahuel''' ({{IPA|es|puðaˈwel}}, [[Mapudungun language|Mapudungun]] "place of pools/water" or "place where seagulls gather") is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]]. Santiago's [[Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport]] is located in Pudahuel.

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Pudahuel spans an area of {{convert|197.4|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 230.293 inhabitants (112,412 men and 117,881 women). The population grew by 18% (34,713 persons) between the 2002 and 2017 census.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Chile |first=Library of the National Congress of Chile |date=June 2020 |title=Pudahuel Statistical Reports 2021, Library of the National Congress of Chile. |url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/reportescomunales/comunas_v.html?anno=2021&idcom=13124 |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=bcn.cl}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pudahuel is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (Mayor) who is directly elected every four years.

The 2021–2024 mayor is Ítalo Bravo Lizana ([[Equality Party (Chile)|Equality Party]]). The communal council has the following members:<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipal Council of Pudahuel 2021-2024 |url=https://www.mpudahuel.cl/portal/concejo-municipal-2021-2024/ |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=www.mpudahuel.cl}}</ref>

* Cinthya Muñoz Escanilla ([[Equality Party (Chile)|PI]])
* Sandy Muñoz Poblete ([[Equality Party (Chile)|PI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pudahuel is part of electoral [[Electoral divisions of Chile|district No. 8]] together with the [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Colina, Chile|Colina]], [[Lampa, Chile|Lampa]], Pudahuel, [[Maipú, Chile|Maipú]], [[Tiltil|Til Til]], [[Cerrillos, Chile|Cerrillos]] and [[Estación Central]]. On the other hand, the commune belongs to the [[Electoral divisions of Chile|VII Senatorial District]] that represents the entire [[Santiago Metropolitan Region|Metropolitan Region of Santiago.]]<ref>{{Cite web |last=rbenavente |title=Library of the National Congress {{!}} SIIT {{!}} Conformation of new districts and constituencies, reform of the Binominal System |url=https://www.bcn.cl/siit/actualidad-territorial/conformacion-de-nuevos-distritos-y-circunscripciones |access-date=2022-08-27 |website=bcn.cl |language=en}}</ref>
