The '''Biruaca Municipality''' is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Apure]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 54,323.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geohive.com/|title=registered through Argeweb|website=www.geohive.com}}</ref> The town of [[Biruaca]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Biruaca Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Apure.zip{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Biruaca Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 54,233 (up from 45,180 in 2000).  This amounts to 11.4% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|42.34|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/apure/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=July 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Biruaca Municipality is Daniel Blanco, re-elected November 23, 2008 with 38% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=03&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 |title=Divulgaciﾃｳn Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 |publisher=Cne.gob.ve |date= |accessdate=2019-07-17}}</ref> The municipality contains one [[parish]]; Urbana Biruaca.<ref name=autogenerated1 />

==Climate==
Minimum monthly temperature in the Biruaca municipality lies between 21.2 and 23.9&nbsp;°C, while maximum temperature is between 29.2 and 35.3&nbsp;°C. Total annual precipitation fluctuates between 1336 and 1820&nbsp;mm. Most rain falls between June and August, while the driest quarter comprises January to March.<ref>{{cite journal|last1=Ferrer-Paris|first1=José R.|title=Caracterización ambiental de la ruta de NeoMapas: NM13 ''San Fernando'', estado Apure (CNEB i12)|journal=Figshare|date=2017|doi=10.6084/m9.figshare.4745578}}</ref>
