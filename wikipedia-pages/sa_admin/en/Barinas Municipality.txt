'''Barinas Municipality''' is a [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipality]] in the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]]. The town of [[Barinas, Barinas|Barinas]] serves as the seat of the state. It is spread over an area of {{cvt|3304|km2}}, and lies on the banks of the [[Santo Domingo River]] in the foothills of the [[Cordillera]] mountains in northwestern Venezuela. As per the 2011 census, the municipality has a population of 361,678 inhabitants.

==Geography==
Barinas is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] in the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/|title=Venezuela administration|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Venezuela: administrative units |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |archive-date=15 March 2016 |access-date=8 November 2015}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|3304|km2}}.<ref name="Local">{{cite web|url=https://citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/barinas/0604__barinas/|title=Localities of Barinas|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> It lies on the banks of the [[Santo Domingo River]] in the foothills of the [[Cordillera]] mountains in northwestern Venezuela, and marks the start of the Venezuelan plains down from the [[Andes]].<ref name="Brit">{{cite web|url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Barinas-Venezuela|title=Barinas|work=[[Britannica]]|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref><ref name="Info">{{cite web|url=https://en.ultimasnoticias.com.ve/barinas/they-celebrate-445-years-of-the-foundation-of-barinas/|title=They celebrate 445 years of the foundation of Barinas|date=30 June 2022|access-date=1 June 2025|work=Ultimas Noticias}}</ref>

==Administration==
The town of [[Barinas, Barinas]] is the seat of the state of Barinas, and is the largest urban center in the Venezuelan plains.<ref name="Brit"/><ref name="Info"/> Also known as Ciudad Marquesa, it was officially established in 1758, from a city originally founded by  Spanish captain Juan Andrés Varela as Altamira de Cáceres.<ref name="Info"/> The local administration is headed by a mayor.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=2&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231407/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D5%26mun%3D2%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html|url-status=dead|title=Alcalde de Municipio Barinas|archive-date=29 February 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones 30 de Julio 2000 Directerio Alcado Electos |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archive-date=2 October 2006 |access-date=15 March 2008 |lang=es}}</ref> The municipality is divided into seven localities&ndash; Barinas, El Corozo, La Caramuca, La Mula, Quebrada Seca, San Silvestre, and Torunos.<ref name="Local"/>

==Demographics and economy==
As per the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 361,678 inhabitants. The population consisted of 174,859 males and 178,992 females. About 99,899 inhabitants (28.2%) were below the age of fourteen. Majority (92.7%) of the population was classified as urban, and the rest (7.3%) as rural. The municipality had a literacy rate of 95.9%. [[Mestizos]] (53%) formed the major ethnic group, followed by [[White people|Whites]] (47%).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/barinas/0604__barinas/|title=Barinas Municipality|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

[[Agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing are major contributors to the economy. [[Tobacco]] and [[Cocoa bean|cocoa]] was extensively cultivated earlier, while the major food crops include [[rice]], [[coffee]], [[bananas]], and [[millets]].<ref name="Brit"/>
