'''Jaquirana''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,662.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/jaquirana/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|6655|ha|adj=on}} [[Tainhas State Park]], created in 1975.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual de Tainhas – cambaradosul}}|title=Parque Estadual de Tainhas|work=cambaradosul.tur.br|url=http://www.cambaradosul.tur.br/pousada/parque-estadual-de-tainhas/|access-date=2016-12-09|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160214210659/http://www.cambaradosul.tur.br/pousada/parque-estadual-de-tainhas/|archive-date=2016-02-14}}</ref>
