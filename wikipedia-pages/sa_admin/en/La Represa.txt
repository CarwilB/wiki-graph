'''La Represa''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[La Chorrera District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 681 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 650; its population as of 2000 was 696.<ref name=inec/> Most recently, its population was recorded as 796 in 2015.<ref> {{cite web|title=La Represa |url=https://www.city-facts.com/la-represa-panam%C3%A1-oeste |language=English|publisher=City Facts|accessdate=9 November 2022}}</ref>
