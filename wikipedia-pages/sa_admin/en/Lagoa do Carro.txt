'''Lagoa do Carro''' is a city in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]] region at 60&nbsp;km of the state capital [[Recife]].

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Carpina]]  ('''N''' and '''E''');  [[Lagoa do Itaenga]]   ('''S''');  [[Limoeiro]]   ('''W''')
* Area - 68.87&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 128 m
* Hydrography - [[Capibaribe River|Capibaribe]] and [[Goiana River]]s
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]] forest
* Climate - Hot tropical and humid
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 60&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Lagoa do Carro''' are based in industry and [[agribusiness]]; especially  [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[pig]]s and [[chicken]]s.
