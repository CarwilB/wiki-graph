'''Barra de Santo Antônio''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbaɦɐ ˈdɪ sɐ̃tɐ̃ˈtõj(ʊ)]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Alagoas]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 16,068 (2020 est.)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/barra-de-santo-antonio/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> in an area of {{cvt|138.43|km2}}.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/234AO Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]{{dead link|date=October 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The elevation is 10 m. It is situated on the [[Atlantic Ocean|Atlantic coast]], northeast of [[Maceió]].
