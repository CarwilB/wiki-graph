'''Rio Piracicaba''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte and to the microregion of Itabira. It has two rural districts: Conceição de Piracicaba (better known as "Jorge") and Padre Pinto (better known as "Caxambu").{{Cn|date=September 2024}} As of 2020, the estimated population was 14,332.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/rio-piracicaba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
