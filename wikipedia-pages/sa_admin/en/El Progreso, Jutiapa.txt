'''El Progreso''' ({{IPA|es|el pɾoˈɣɾeso}}), also '''El Progreso, Achuapa''', is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Jutiapa Department|Jutiapa department]] of [[Guatemala]]. In 2018 the municipality had a population of 22,114<ref>{{cite web|url=http://wikiguate.com.gt/el-progreso-municipio/|title=El Progreso (municipio)|publisher=wikiguate|access-date=3 February 2017 |language=Spanish}}</ref> The [[Pan American Highway]] passes to the south of the main town, connecting it with [[Jutiapa]], {{convert|11|km}}  to the southwest. The [[Morán River|Morán]], [[Chiquito River (Guatemala)|Chiquito]] and [[Ostúa River|Ostúa]] rivers flow through the municipality. The main items produced locally include cigars, leather products, furniture, tiles and mud bricks.

==History==
El Progreso municipality was created on 6 October 1884 under the government of General [[Justo Rufino Barrios]]. It appears as if there may well already have been a settlement called Achuapa on the site which then changed its name to El Progreso.<ref name="Mijutiapa"/> The first settlers were the indigenous [[Pipile people|Pipiles]], who grew maize, cacao and above all [[annatto]] in the Achuapa, Morán and Chiquito valleys.<ref name="muni"/> The name "Achuapa" appears to be derived from "achioti", the annatto-bearing achiote tree, and "pan" meaning place. Remains of the early settlers in the Achuapa Valley have been found along the banks of the [[Morán River|Morán]] and [[Chiquito River (Guatemala)|Chiquito Rivers]].<ref name="Mijutiapa"/> On 24 January 1939, the government agreed to establish a Catholic Church feast day in early February to honor to the [[Virgin of Lourdes]].<ref name="muni">{{cite web|url=http://www.munielprogreso.gob.gt/el-progreso-jutiapa.html|title=El Progreso, Jutiapa|publisher=Municipio El Progreso|language=Spanish|access-date=1 February 2017}}</ref> The modern municipality was established by Article 3 of a government decree on 20 September 1987.<ref name="Mijutiapa">{{cite web|url=http://www.mijutiapa.com/el-progreso-achuapa/|title=El Progreso|publisher=Mijutiapa.com|language=Spanish|access-date=1 February 2017}}</ref>

The local fiestas were originally held in connection with [[Trinity Sunday]] in May or June but are now celebrated around 11 February in remembrance of [[Our Lady of Lourdes]].<ref name="Mijutiapa"/>

==Geography==
[[File:Parquesito_El_Progreso.JPG|thumb|left|The local park]]
The municipality of El Progreso is situated {{convert|128|km}} from [[Guatemala City]] and {{convert|11|km}} northeast of [[Jutiapa]], the departmental head.<ref name="muni"/> Jutiapa department is located within the Eastern Plains, notable for being Central America's only desert.{{sfn|McNally|2008|p=405}}

Situated at a height of {{convert|970|meters}} above sea level, El Progreso borders the municipality of [[Monjas]] to the north, the municipality of [[Jutiapa]] to the south and west, and the municipalities of [[Santa Catarina Mita]] and [[Asunción Mita]] in the department of Jutiapa to the east. The western part of the municipality contains part of the river basin of the [[Ostúa River]] and the Las Aradas hills.<ref name="muni"/><ref name="Mijutiapa"/> Other rivers include the Apantes, Chiquito, Morán and San Pedro rivers. The municipality contains the main town of El Progreso, 8 villages and 17 hamlets.<ref name="Mijutiapa"/>  The [[Suchitan]] volcano is nearby and the [[Pan American Highway]] passes to the south of the main town, connecting it with Jutiapa to the southwest.<ref name="GM">{{Google maps | url =https://www.google.com/maps/place/Volcan+Suchitan/@14.4,-89.785522,1047m/data=!3m2!1e3!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x8f6264184efd3dc5:0x47cf64fc384be5!8m2!3d14.4!4d-89.7833333?hl=en  | accessdate =1 February 2017}}</ref>

==Economy==
[[File:Cultivo de maiz de El Progreso.JPG|thumb|left|Maize cultivation in El Progreso]]
Historically, maize, cacao and achiote were the main crops produced in the municipality<ref name="muni"/> while today crops also include rice, beans and broccoli. The main items produced locally today include cigars, leather products, furniture, tiles and mud bricks.<ref name="Mijutiapa"/> The La Cooperativa de Retana en El Progreso, a cooperative of landowners, is based in El Progreso.<ref>{{cite book|title=Jutiapa: en la historia y la leyenda|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=0LhqAAAAMAAJ|year=1994|publisher=CENALTEX, Ministerio de Educación|language=Spanish|page=278}}</ref>

==Facilities==
[[File:Municipalidad El Progreso jutiapa.JPG|thumb|City hall]]
The main town contains several restaurants and churches. El Progreso has a well-designed park and a sports and recreation centre which attracts visitors not only from the surrounding areas but also from further afield. There are a number of primary schools and pre-school institutions as well as private educational centres,<ref name="Mijutiapa"/> including Colegio Evangelico Horeb,<ref name="GM"/> Colegio Parroquial Mixto Nuestra Señora De Lourdes, Colegio Particular Mixto Liceo Progreso, Cpmi Liceo Progreso, Escuela Regional De Hoteleria Y Turismo Ecologico, Liceo Bilingüe, and Fray Amadeo Ruano Najarro.<ref name="amarillasya.com">{{cite web|title=Colegios en El Progreso Jutiapa Guatemala|url=http://amarillasya.com/category/educacion-e-instruccion/colegios/location/guatemala/jutiapa/el-progreso/|website=amarillasya.com|access-date=1 February 2017}}</ref>

==Sports==
{{Commons|El Progreso (Jutiapa)}}
The football club, "Oriental Progresista" was formed in 1932.<ref name="munielprogreso.gob.gt">{{cite web|title=Historia del Deportivo Achuapa|url=http://www.munielprogreso.gob.gt/historia-del-deportivo-achuapa.html|publisher=La Municipalidad El Progreso|access-date=1 February 2017}}</ref> [[Deportivo Achuapa]] football club play in the [[Primera División de Ascenso|second tier of Guatemalan football]]. The city's stadium, [[Estadio Municipal Manuel Ariza]], is surfaced in grass and offers seating for 1,500 attendees.<ref name="soccerway.com">{{cite web|title=Estadio Municipal Manuel Ariza|url=https://int.soccerway.com/venues/guatemala/estadio-municipal-manuel-ariza/v9141/|publisher=Soccer Way|access-date=1 February 2017}}</ref>
