'''General Pinto Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the northern border of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 11,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2540|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[General Pinto]], which is around {{convert|355|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The partido and its district capital are named after General [[Manuel Guillermo Pinto]], who fought in the defence of Buenos Aires against the English and in the [[Argentine War of Independence]]. He later went on to serve as [[Governor of Buenos Aires]].

The partido was reduced in size in 1991 due to the creation of [[Florentino Ameghino Partido]].
