'''Santa Cruz do Arari''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 10,314 people. The area of the municipality is 1,074.854&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and the microregion of Arari.

In 2010, the mayor was Marcelo José Pamplona Beltrão.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/municipio/dados_politicos.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |title=:: Site Oficial de Santa Cruz do Arari :: |accessdate=2011-07-06 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706155857/http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/municipio/dados_politicos.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |archivedate=2011-07-06 }}</ref> The [[United Nations]] HDI, or [[Human Development Index]], score for Santa Cruz do Arari is 0.63.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |title=:: Site Oficial de Santa Cruz do Arari :: |accessdate=2011-07-06 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706155948/http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/dado_geral/mumain.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |archivedate=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

Nearby [[Lake Arari]] is a large lake located on the island [[Marajó]], which is a tourist attraction and the home of a wide variety of avian life.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/municipio/ponto_turistico.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |title=:: Site Oficial de Santa Cruz do Arari :: |access-date=2010-04-11 |archive-date=2011-07-06 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706155957/http://www.pmsantacruz.pa.gov.br/portal1/municipio/ponto_turistico.asp?iIdMun=100115108 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable-use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
