'''Campestre da Serra''' (literally ''a field surrounded by forest and mountains'') is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southern [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is 2526 feet above sea level.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 3,395.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/campestre-da-serra/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
