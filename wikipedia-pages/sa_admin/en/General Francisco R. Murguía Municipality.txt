'''General Francisco R. Murguía''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|140|km}} north of the state capital of [[Zacatecas City]]. Its municipal seat is located in Nieves.

==Geography==
The municipality of Francisco R. Murguía is located at an elevation between {{convert|1500|and(-)|2700|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in northern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Mazapil Municipality|Mazapil]] to the northeast, [[Villa de Cos]] to the southeast, [[Río Grande Municipality, Zacatecas|Río Grande]] to the southwest, and [[Juan Aldama Municipality|Juan Aldama]] to the west.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32014.pdf |title=General Francisco R. Murguía, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref> It also borders three municipalities of [[Durango]] to the north and northwest: [[Santa Clara Municipality, Durango|Santa Clara]], [[General Simón Bolívar Municipality|General Simón Bolívar]], and [[San Juan de Guadalupe Municipality|San Juan de Guadalupe]]. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|5034.261|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 6.6% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Francisco R. Murguía comprises [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (53%), grassland (24%), and [[mesquite|mezquital]] (2%). Another 21% of the land is used for agriculture.<ref name=compendium/> The municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of the [[Aguanaval River]], which flows south to north through the municipality.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32014a.html |title=General Francisco R. Murguía |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref> The small mountain range called the Sierra de Guadalupe rises in the south-central part of the municipality, and was formerly exploited for its deposits of copper, antimony and mercury.<ref name=minerals>{{cite report |url=https://mapserver.sgm.gob.mx/InformesTecnicos/InventariosMinerosWeb/T3211OOAF0001_01.PDF |title=Inventario físico de recursos minerales de la carta Sierra de Guadalupe G13-D87 |language=es |pages=7, 18 |publisher=[[Mexican Geological Survey]] |date=December 2011 |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref>

===Climate===
Francisco R. Murguía has a semi-arid climate with dry winters.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|12|and(-)|20|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|200|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Prior to the arrival of the Spanish, the area in what is now the municipality of General Francisco R. Murguía was inhabited by [[Chichimeca]] peoples. The expedition of [[Francisco de Ibarra]] arrived in the area on 5 August 1558, naming it Santa Maria de las Nieves after the [[Dedication of the Basilica of Saint Mary Major|feast on that day]]. In the 18th century it took the name of Real de Minas in reference to the local mining activities, but was renamed Nieves later in the same century.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

After the independence of Mexico, Nieves was one of the original ''partidos'' named in Zacatecas's state constitution first enacted on 17 January 1825. It became a free municipality on 19 August 1916. In 1964, the municipality was renamed in honour of General {{ill|Francisco Murguía|es}}, who was born in Mazapil and fought in the [[Mexican Revolution]].<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Francisco R. Murguía comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and thirteen trustees (''regidores''), eight elected by [[relative majority]] and five by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Javier Garcia (2021).<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=16 October 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-zacatecas-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Zacatecas 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Francisco R. Murguía recorded a population of 21,974 inhabitants living in 5427 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=014 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de General Francisco R. Murguía |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=22 January 2021}}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 21,809 inhabitants in Francisco R. Murguía.<ref name=survey/>

[[INEGI]] lists 76 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Nieves is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 5653 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Francisco R. Murguía is agriculture.<ref name=encyclopedia/> The main crops grown are forage oats, beans, and corn.<ref name=plan>{{cite news |author=General Francisco R. Murguía Municipal Council |url=http://www.presidencianieves.org.mx/pages/transparencia/Soporte/4101/PMD_2017_2018.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=33 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Gobierno del Estado de Zacatecas |volume=127 |issue=32 |publisher=Government of Zacatecas |date=27 April 2017 |accessdate=22 January 2021}}</ref> Cattle, sheep and goats are also raised.<ref name=minerals/>
