'''Bonfim''' ({{IPA|pt|bõˈfĩ}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the mideast of the state of [[Roraima]] in [[Brazil]]. Its population is 12,557 and its area is 8,095&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rr/bonfim/panorama |title=Bonfim|website=[[IBGE]]|access-date=9 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref> The city lies opposite the [[Takutu River]] from [[Lethem, Guyana]]. The [[Takutu River Bridge]] links Bonfim and Roraima with the town of Lethem and the Atlantic port of [[Georgetown, Guyana]].<ref>Stabroek News at [https://archive.today/20110516134117/http://gosouthamerica.about.com/b/a/257968.htm] as of 16 March 2007</ref>

==Overview==
In 1775, the Portuguese built Fort São Joaquim at the confluence of the [[Uraricoera River|Uraricoera]] and the [[Takutu River]] to prevent the [[Dutch Republic|Dutch]] from advancing into the area.<ref name="socio">{{cite web|url=https://pib.socioambiental.org/en/Povo:Macuxi |title=Macuxi|website=Socio Ambiental|access-date=9 April 2021}}</ref> The ruins of the fort can still be seen.<ref name="hist"/> In 1790, the Amerindians revolted, and the Portuguese retreated from the region.<ref name="socio"/>

Bonfim started with the compound of a religious mission from the United States.<ref name="hist">{{cite web|url=http://www.bonfim.rr.gov.br/pagina/192_Historia-da-Cidade.html |title=Historia da Cidade|website=Government of Bonfim|access-date=9 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>
On 1 July 1982 it became a municipality<ref name="hist"/> by Federal Bill Nº 7.009.
On 28 March 2007 it was declared a free port for international trade by President [[Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_Ato2007-2010/2008/Lei/L11732.htm |title=LEI Nº 11.732, DE 30 DE JUNHO DE 2008.|website=Planalto.gov.br|access-date=9 April 2021|language=pt}}</ref>

[[File:Moscou em Bonfim.JPG|thumb|left|The indigenous village of Moscou]]
The municipality is home to Amerindians of the [[Wapishana]] and [[Macushi]] tribes who live in the Wapixana, Macuxi, and Jabuti [[Indigenous territory (Brazil)|Indigenous Territories]]. Many of the indigenous community are tri-lingual speaking their own language, [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] and [[English language|English]].<ref name="hist"/>

The economy is mainly based on livestock in particular [[cattle ranching]] and agriculture which an emphasis on cassava, bananas and cashew.<ref name="hist"/>

==Transport==
Bonfim is connected to the [[BR-401]] which links the road network of Guyana to [[Boa Vista, Roraima|Boa Vista]], the capital of Roraima, located 115 kilometres from Bonfim.<ref>{{cite journal|url=https://www.academia.edu/11926356 |page=125|author1=Reinaldo Imbrozio Barbosa |author2=Ciro Campos |title=Detection and geographical distribution of clearing areas in the savannas ('lavrado') of Roraima using Google Earth web tool|year=2001|journal=Journal of Geography and Regional Planning|issn=2070-1845}}</ref>
