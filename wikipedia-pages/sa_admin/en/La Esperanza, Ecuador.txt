'''La Esperanza''' (the name is [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "hope") is a town and parish in northern [[Ecuador]] in [[Imbabura Province]], [[Ibarra Canton]]. It lies at the north-eastern foot of [[Mount Imbabura]] [[volcano]] about {{convert|7|km|mi}} in straight line distance south of the city of [[Ibarra, Ecuador|Ibarra]].

La Esperanza parish had a population of 6,677 in the 2001 census and 7,363 in the 2010 census.<ref>"Ecuador: Municipal Division", https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 14 Jul 2017</ref>  The town has an elevation of {{convert|2448|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref> 

In the late 1960s and 1970s, La Esperanza became known to [[hippie]]s and celebrities for the [[magic mushrooms]] which grew there in profusion.<ref>"Everyday Musings from across the Equator", https://jenainecuador.wordpress.com/2011/09/05/la-esperanza-ecuador/, accessed 14 Jul 2017</ref>
