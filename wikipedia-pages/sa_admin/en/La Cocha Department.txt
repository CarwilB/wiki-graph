'''La Cocha Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 17,683 (2001) and an area of 917&nbsp;km². The seat of the department is in [[La Cocha]].

*Yánima
