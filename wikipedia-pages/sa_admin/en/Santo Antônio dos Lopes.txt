'''Santo Antônio dos Lopes''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the central part of the state of [[Maranhão]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 14,522 (2020 est.) in an area of 770.92&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23NNK Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref>
