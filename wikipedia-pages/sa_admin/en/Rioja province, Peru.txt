'''Rioja''' is one of ten [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[San Martín Region]] in northern [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-san-martin-por-es-tan-cautivante-esta-region-y-fiestas-se-celebran-junio-1032192.aspx|title=San Martín: ¿Por qué es tan cautivante esta región y qué fiestas se celebran en junio?|publisher=[[Andina (news agency)|Andina]]|date=7 June 2025|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Location==
The province is bordered to the north and east by the province of [[Moyobamba Province|Moyobamba]] and to the south and west by the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]].
The province has a population of 90,000 inhabitants. Its capital is [[Rioja, Peru|Rioja]]. The area of the province is {{Convert|2535.04|km2|sp=us}}.

==Political division==
The province is divided into nine districts.

* [[Awajun District|Awajun]] ([[Bajo Naranjillo]])
* [[Elias Soplin Vargas District|Elias Soplin Vargas]] ([[Segunda Jerusalén]])
* [[Nueva Cajamarca District|Nueva Cajamarca]] ([[Nueva Cajamarca]])
* [[Pardo Miguel District|Pardo Miguel]] ([[Naranjos (Peru)|Naranjos]])
* [[Posic District|Posic]] ([[Posic]])
* [[Rioja District|Rioja]] ([[Rioja, Peru|Rioja]])
* [[San Fernando District|San Fernando]] ([[San Fernando, Rioja Province|San Fernando]])
* [[Yorongos District|Yorongos]] ([[Yorongos]])
* [[Yuracyacu District|Yuracyacu]] ([[Yuracyacu, Rioja Province|Yuracyacu]])
