'''Aysén Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Aysén}}) is a [[Provinces of Chile|province]] in the [[Aysén Region]] of Chile. Its capital is [[Puerto Aysén]]. Spread over an area of {{cvt|46588.8|km2}}, it had a population of 29,905 inhabitants as per the [[2024 Chilean census]].

==History==
In 1520, the region was discovered by [[Spanish people|Spanish]] explorer [[Ferdinand Magellan]], who named it as "Tierras de Diciembre" (Land of December). In the 19th century, migrants from Argentina and Germany, settled in the region, and engaged in [[agriculture]], fishing and logging. In the 20th century, the Government of Chile gave land grants to encourage settlement in the region, and the city of [[Puerto Aysen]] was established in 1913.<ref name="VC">{{cite web|url=https://www.visitchile.com/en/tourist-guides/coyhaique-and-puerto-aysen/|title=Coyhaique and Puerto Aisén|work=Visit Chile|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[Aysén Region]] was established on 10 July 1974, as per Law No. 575, which reorganised Chile into thirteen regions.<ref name="BCN">{{cite web|url=https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?i=6210|lang=es|title=Decreto Ley 575 del 14 de julio de 1974|trans-title=Decree Law 575 of 14 July 1974|work=[[Biblioteca del Congreso Nacional de Chile]]|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It was named is divided into four provinces&ndash;Aysén, [[Coyhaique Province|Coyhaique]], [[General Carrera Province|General Carrera]] and [[Capitán Prat Province|Capitán Prat]], which are further divided into ten [[Communes of Chile|communes]].<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.descentralizachile.cl/region_de_aysen_del_general_carlos_ibanez_del_campo/|lang=es|title=Region de Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|trans-title=Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Geography==
Aysén Province is one of the four provinces of the [[Aysén Region]] in Chile.<ref name="Hist"/><ref name="Admin">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/|title=Administrative division of Chile|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 May 2026}}</ref> It spans an area of {{cvt|46588.8|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/> The province is situated in the [[Chilean Patagonia]]. [[Puerto Aysén]], located on the banks of the [[Aysén River]], serves as the capital of the province.<ref name="VC"/><ref name="Info">{{cite web|url=https://www.interpatagonia.com/puertoaisen/index_i.html|title=Puerto Aisen|work=Inter Patagonia|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> Majority of the land area is occupies by coastal plains along the Aysen and Baker rivers. About 20% of the land area forms part of the [[Guaitecas Archipelago]].<ref name="MN">{{cite web|url=https://simbio.mma.gob.cl/DPA/DetailsProvincia/23|title=Provincia details, Aisen Provincia|trans-title=Details of the Province, Aisen Province|work=[[Ministry of the Interior (Chile)]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The province consists of various [[fjord]]s, mountains, and eroded river valleys.<ref name="Info"/>

===Climate===
The province has a maritime climate with average annual temperature of {{Cvt|9.36|C}}. The region receives only about {{cvt|3.4|cm}} rainfall on average annually.<ref name="VC"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/chile/aysen/puerto-aisen|title=Puerto Aisen|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref>

==Administration==
Coyhaique is a second-level [[administrative division of Chile]], governed by a governor.<ref name="Municip"/> It is further sub-divided into the [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas'') of [[Aisén, Chile|Aisén]], [[Cisnes]], and [[Guaitecas]].<ref name="Admin"/><ref name="MN"/>

==Demographics==
According to the [[2024 Chilean census]], the province had a population of 29,905 inhabitants. The population consisted of 14,977 males (50.1%) and 14,928 females (49.9%). About 19.0% of the population was below the age of 15 years, 69.2% belonged to the age group of 15–64 years, and 11.7% was aged 65 years or older. The province had an urban population of 24,926 inhabitants (83.4%) and a rural population of 4,979 inhabitants (16.6%). Most of the residents were born in Chile, accounting for 28,984 inhabitants (96.9%). Non-indigenous people formed the majority of the population with 19,132 inhabitants (64%), while 10,767 inhabitants (36%) identified themselves as belonging to indigenous groups. [[Roman Catholics]] formed the largest religious group with 13,160 adherents (54.6%), followed by 6,540 inhabitants (27.1%) indicating no religious affiliation, and [[Evangelicals]] or [[Protestants]] with 3,769 adherents (15.6%).<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/112__ays%C3%A9n/|title=Aysen Province|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite report|url=
