'''Arroio do Padre''' ({{IPA|pt|aˈʁoju du ˈpadɾi}}, [[Portuguese language|Portuguese]] meaning Priest's Stream) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southeastern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. Its 2020 population was 2,951.

Much of the population have [[Pomeranians (German people)|Pomeranian]] origin and are [[Lutheranism|Evangelical Lutheran]].
