'''Colniza''' is a municipality in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]], Brazil.

==Demographics==

Colniza has the highest [[homicide rate]] in Brazil with 165 deaths per year per 100 thousand inhabitants.{{sfn|Tom Phillips|2008}}{{sfn|Lisandra Paraguassú|2007}}
It is the westernmost and largest (by area) municipality of the state.

==Conservation==

The municipality of Colniza has a mosaic of conservation units consisting of the [[Rio Madeirinha Ecological Station]], the [[Rio Roosevelt Ecological Station]], the [[Tucumã State Park]] and the [[Guariba-Roosevelt Extractive Reserve]].

Satellite monitoring showed that Colniza had the highest level of deforestation in Mato Grosso in the six years from 2010 to 2016, with {{convert|78556|ha}} cleared, most of it illegally. Rates of deforestation rose successively in 2013, 2014 and 2015.{{sfn|Câmara Setorial mantém área da Resex ...}}

The {{convert|13683|ha|adj=on}} Rio Madeirinha Ecological Station is a fully protected environmental unit created in 1997.{{sfn|ESEC do Rio Madeirinha – ISA}}
The {{convert|96925|ha|adj=on}} Rio Roosevelt Ecological Station is a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1997.{{sfn|ESEC do Rio Roosevelt – ISA}}
The {{convert|164224|ha|adj=on}} Guariba-Roosevelt Extractive Reserve is a sustainable use unit created in 1996.{{sfn|RESEX Guariba-Roosevelt – ISA}}
The municipality also contains {{convert|3641|ha|adj=on}} of the [[Campos Amazônicos National Park]], a {{convert|961318|ha|adj=on}} protected area created in 2006 that protects an unusual enclave of [[cerrado]] vegetation in the Amazon rainforest.{{sfn|PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos – ISA}}
It contains 44% of the {{convert|227817|ha|adj=on}} [[Igarapés do Juruena State Park]], created in 2002.{{sfn|PES Igarapés do Juruena – ISA}}
