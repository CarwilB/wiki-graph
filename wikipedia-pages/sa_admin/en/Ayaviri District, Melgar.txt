'''Ayaviri''' or '''Ayawiri''' ([[Aymara language|Aymara]]) is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Melgar Province|Melgar]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>
