'''Morelos''' is a semi-urban [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Zacatecas (city)|Zacatecas metropolitan area]] in the [[Mexican state]] of [[Zacatecas]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.gob.mx/conapo/documentos/delimitacion-de-las-zonas-metropolitanas-de-mexico-2015 |title=Delimitación de las zonas metropolitanas de México 2015 |trans-title=Delimitation of Mexico's Metropolitan Areas 2015 |language=es |pages=256–257 |publisher=CONAPO |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality of Morelos is located at an elevation between {{convert|2100|and(-)|2600|m}} in central Zacatecas. It borders the municipalities of [[Calera de Víctor Rosales|Calera]] to the northwest, [[Pánuco Municipality, Zacatecas|Pánuco]] to the northeast, [[Vetagrande]] to the east, and [[Zacatecas (city)|the city of Zacatecas]] to the south.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32032.pdf |title=Morelos, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|180.9|km2}}<ref name=cifras/> and comprises 0.2% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 71% of the land in Morelos is used for agriculture. The remainder is covered by [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (13.7%), grassland (12.6%) and urban areas (2.4%).<ref name=compendium/> The municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of [[Llanos el Salado|El Salado]]. The highest point of the municipality is Cerro San Gil in the Sierra de Zacatecas southeast of the town of Hacienda Nueva, with an elevation of {{convert|2603|m}} above sea level.<ref name=region/><ref name=compendium/><ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32032a.html |title=Morelos |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref>

Morelos has a temperate semi-arid climate with dry winters.<ref name=compendium/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|400|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In pre-Hispanic times, the Morelos area was inhabited by the Cagihua tribe of the [[Zacateco]] people. [[Francisco de Ibarra]] passed through the area on 1 September 1554, giving it the name of Arroyo de los Chupaderos. Between 1610 and 1620, Antonio de Figueroa built and operated the first ranch in the area.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

After independence, Chupaderos was administered by the municipality of Vetagrande. It was declared a separate municipality on 8 February 1869 and its first municipal assembly convened in 1871. On 5 May 1894, the municipality changed its name from Chupaderos to Morelos in honour of [[José María Morelos]].<ref name=anniversary>{{cite web |year=2019 |url=http://moreloszacatecas.org/documentos/Gaceta_Aniversario_Morelos.pdf |title=Morelos: Aniversario |language=es |publisher=Municipality of Morelos |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref> The municipal seat inaugurated its first power plant in 1955 and its first potable water supply in 1966.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Morelos comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and ten trustees (''regidores''), six elected by [[relative majority]] and four by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Margarita Robles de Santiago.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Census, the municipality of Morelos recorded a population of 13,207 inhabitants living in 3614 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=78–79 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2016 |accessdate=12 June 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 11,493 inhabitants in Morelos.<ref name=snim/>

[[INEGI]] lists 28 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which two are classified as urban:<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=032 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Morelos |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref>
*the municipal seat, also called Morelos, which recorded a population of 7538 inhabitants in the 2020 Census; and
*Hacienda Nueva, located {{convert|2|km}} south of the municipal seat, which recorded a population of 4029 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
[[File:Zac airport o1.jpg|thumb|General Leobardo C. Ruiz International Airport, also known as Zacatecas International Airport, is located in the municipality of Morelos.]]
Economic activities in Morelos include agriculture and small businesses. Crops grown include corn, bean, barley, oats and chiles.<ref name=encyclopedia/> CRUCEN (''Centro Regional Universitario Centro Norte''), a regional centre of the agriculturally focused [[Chapingo Autonomous University]], is located in Morelos.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://scru.chapingo.mx/somos-crucen/ |title=¿Qué es el CRUCEN? |language=es |publisher=[[Chapingo Autonomous University]] |access-date=20 June 2021}}</ref>

Federal Highways [[Mexican Federal Highway 45|45]], [[Mexican Federal Highway 45D|45D]] (the Zacatecas bypass), and [[Mexican Federal Highway 54|54]] all intersect in the municipality. [[Zacatecas International Airport]] is located in the northern part of the municipality.

[[Peñoles]] operated the Francisco I. Madero zinc mine in the southwestern part of the municipality from 2001 to 2020.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite web |last=Serrano |first=Miriam |date=17 May 2020 |url=http://ntrzacatecas.com/2020/05/17/cierra-en-morelos-la-mina-madero/ |title=Cierra en Morelos la mina Madero |language=es |website=NTR Zacatecas |access-date=12 June 2021}}</ref>
