'''Presidente Bernardes''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 5,341 (2020 est.) in an area of 237&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The elevation is 591 m. Presidente Bernardes is named after [[President of Brazil|President]] [[Artur Bernardes]].
