'''Barreiros''' (''Barriers'') is a municipality in [[Pernambuco]] with 42,083 inhabitants (2025).<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Zona da Mata (coastal)|Zona da mata Pernambucana]]
* Boundaries - [[Tamandaré]] ('''N'''); [[Alagoas]] state and [[São José da Coroa Grande]] ('''S'''); [[Água Preta]] ('''W'''); [[Atlantic Ocean]] ('''E''')
* Area - 233.37&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 22m
* Vegetation - Forest [[Subperenifólia]]
* Clima - Hot tropical and humid
* Annual average temperature - 24.9C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 106&nbsp;km

==Beaches==
{{See also|List of beaches in Pernambuco#Barreiros}}

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Barreiros''' are based in [[commerce]], [[tourism]] and [[agribusiness]]. Especially [[coconut]]s, [[manioc]], [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and creations of [[cattle]] and [[water buffalo|buffalos]].
