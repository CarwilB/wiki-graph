'''Padre Abad''' is the smallest of four [[provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Ucayali Region]], in the central [[Amazon rainforest]] of [[Peru]].

==Languages==
According to the [[Peru 2007 Census|2007 census]], 89.4% of the population spoke [[Spanish language|Spanish]] as their first language, while 6.3% spoke [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 0.1% spoke [[Asháninka language|Asháninka]], 0.1% spoke [[Aymara language|Aymara]], 3.9% spoke other indigenous languages and 0.0% spoke foreign languages.

==Political division==
The province is divided into three [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: {{lang|es|distrito}}), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Curimaná District|Curimaná]] ([[Curimaná]])
* [[Irazola District|Irazola]] ([[San Alejandro]])
* [[Padre Abad District|Padre Abad]] ([[Aguaytía]])
