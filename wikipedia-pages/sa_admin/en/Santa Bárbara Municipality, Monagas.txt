'''Santa Bárbara''' is one of the 13 municipalities of the state of [[Monagas]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality's capital is [[Santa Bárbara, Monagas|Santa Bárbara]].

== History ==
In December 2021, Carmen Tillero was elected mayor of the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web|last=admin|title=Juramentada Carmen Tillero como alcaldesa de Santa Bárbara|url=https://laverdaddemonagas.com/2021/12/07/juramentada-carmen-tillero-como-alcaldesa-de-santa-barbara/|access-date=2022-01-02|language=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
The municipality Santa Bárbara is located to the west of the Monagas State. Has a tropical dry forest vegetation and another tropical humid forest, has an average annual temperature of 26.8&nbsp;°C and rainfall of 1,092&nbsp;mm (annual average).{{citation needed|date=January 2023}}

== Economy ==
The economy is farm, among the main crops are sugarcane, cotton, maize, soy and sorghum.{{citation needed|date=January 2023}}
