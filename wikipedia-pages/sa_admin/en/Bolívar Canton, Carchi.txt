'''Bolívar Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Carchi Province]].  Its capital is the town of [[Bol%C3%ADvar, Carchi|Bolívar]].  Its population at the 2001 census was 13,898<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> and in 2010 was 14,347.<ref>"Ecuador: Cantons and Parishes", https://www.citypopulation.de/php/ecuador-parish-admin.php, accessed 12 Jun 2017</ref>

Bolivar is located in the [[Andes]] and the town of Bolivar has an elevation of {{convert|2600|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref> Aside from Bolívar itself, the canton contains the rural parishes of García Moreno, Los Andes, Monte Olivo, San Rafael and San Vicente de Pusir.
