'''Saint-Louis-du-Nord''' ({{langx|ht|Sen Lwi dinò}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord-Ouest (department)|Nord-Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 146,567 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Saint-Louis-du-Nord Arrondissement start with the number 32.

The arrondissement consists of the following municipalities:
* [[Saint-Louis-du-Nord]]
* [[Anse-à-Foleur]]
