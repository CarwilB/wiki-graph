'''Lonya Chico''' is a district of the province of [[Luya Province|Luya]], in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas region]] of [[Peru]]. Several ruins of the [[Chachapoya culture]] are located in the area.

The capital of the district, also called Lonya Chico, celebrates the day of its patron saint, [[Saint Lucy]], on August 15.{{cn|date=June 2024}}

In the north, the district borders the districts of [[Luya District|Luya]] and [[Luya Viejo]], in the east the district of [[Chachapoyas Province|Chachapoyas]], in the south the district of [[Inguilpata]], and in the west the district of [[Conila]].
