'''Chacsinkín Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "firewood reddened by time or plant of the red flowers") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing 158.40&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> of land and is located roughly {{convert|120|km}} southeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and a part of the chieftainship of Tutul Xiú. After colonization, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with Lorenzo de Ávila Carranza and Isabel de la Cerda serving as {{lang|es|encomenderos}} in 1704.<ref name="municipios"/>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821,<ref name="municipios"/> and in 1825 the area was assigned to the High Sierra partition with headquarters in [[Tekax Municipality]]. In 1867, it was moved to the jurisdiction of the [[Peto Municipality]].<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995 |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |website=inegi |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=7 August 2015 |location=Aguascalientes, Mexico |language=Spanish |date=1996 |pages=83, 116, 124 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf |archivedate=23 December 2015 }}</ref> It was transferred again to the [[Tzucacab Municipality]] in 1910 and finally became its own municipality in 1918.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has four councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, education and health, recruitment and potable water.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Chacsinkín|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|accessdate=8 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=Spanish|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The Municipal Council administers the business of the municipality. It is responsible for budgeting and expenditures and producing all required reports for all branches of the municipal administration. Annually it determines educational standards for schools.<ref name=governance />

The Police Commissioners ensure public order and safety. They are tasked with enforcing regulations, distributing materials and administering rulings of general compliance issued by the council.<ref name=governance />
