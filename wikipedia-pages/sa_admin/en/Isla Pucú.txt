'''Isla Pucú''' is a small [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Cordillera Department]] of [[Paraguay]].<ref>{{cite web |last1=Aguero |first1=Faustina |title=En Isla Pucú celebrarán 82 años de vida institucional |url=https://www.abc.com.py/nacionales/2023/05/16/en-isla-pucu-celebraran-82-anos-de-vida-institucional/ |website=ABC Paraguay |publisher=ABC Paraguay |access-date=6 February 2024}}</ref> Its people are usually farmers. Technology is taking place rapidly and it is getting to be a more sophisticated place, because many of the young people are migrating to North America and Spain, to help their families economically.
