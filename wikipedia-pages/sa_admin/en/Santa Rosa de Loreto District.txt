'''Santa Rosa de Loreto''' is one of the five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of [[Mariscal Ramón Castilla Province]] in [[Peru]]. From the administrative point of view of the [[Catholic Church in Peru]], it forms part of the [[Apostolic Vicariate of San José de Amazonas]]. It is located near [[Tres Fronteras]], the triple border between Peru, [[Colombia]] and [[Brazil]].

==History==

Proposals to create the district date back to 2010, when efforts took place until their approval in 2021.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Así es la isla Santa Rosa: comercio, identidad y diplomacia frente a las tensiones entre Gustavo Petro y Perú |last=Giraldo |first=Clara |date=2025-08-25 |url=https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/08/05/asi-es-la-isla-santa-rosa-comercio-identidad-y-diplomacia-tras-las-acusaciones-de-gustavo-petro-contra-el-peru/ |work=[[Infobae]]}}</ref> The district was officially created on July 3, 2025, through Law N° 32403, approved by the [[Congress of Peru]] following the initiative of President [[Dina Boluarte]]. Formerly part of [[Yavari District]],<ref>{{Cite news |title=¿En qué consiste el conflicto territorial entre Perú y Colombia por la isla Santa Rosa? |last=Bazzani |first=Diego |date=2025-08-09 |url=https://www.france24.com/es/am%C3%A9rica-latina/20250809-en-qu%C3%A9-consiste-el-conflicto-territorial-entre-per%C3%BA-y-colombia-por-la-isla-santa-rosa |work=[[France 24]]}}</ref> its capital is [[Santa Rosa de Yavarí]], one of three border crossings of [[Tres Fronteras]].<ref name=Giner>{{Cite news |title=Santa Rosa, la historia detrás del distrito que crispó la relación entre Perú y Colombia |last=Giner Vásquez |first=Renzo |date=2025-08-07 |url=https://elcomercio.pe/peru/loreto-iquitos-colombia-santa-rosa-del-amazonas-la-historia-detras-del-distrito-que-crispo-la-relacion-entre-peru-y-colombia-tlc-gustavo-petro-noticia/ |work=[[El Comercio (Peru)|El Comercio]] |url-access=subscription}}</ref>

The district was provisionally administered by Jack Iván Yovera Peña, provincial mayor of Mariscal Ramón Castilla,<ref>{{Cite news |title=Canciller (e) Rosa Villavicencio denunció que Perú “está violando” acuerdos fronterizos, por lo que envío dos notas de protesta |last=Lancheros |first=Katherine |date=2025-08-05 |url=https://www.infobae.com/colombia/2025/08/05/canciller-e-rosa-villavicencio-denuncio-que-peru-esta-violando-acuerdos-fronterizos-por-lo-que-envio-dos-notas-de-protesta/ |work=[[Infobae]]}}</ref> until Max Ortiz Rubio, who had been mayor of the populated centre back in 2018, was chosen as interim mayor in August.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Distrito fronterizo de Santa Rosa espera confirmación oficial sobre sus primeras elecciones distritales |date=2025-09-22 |url=https://radiolasoberana.com/2025/09/22/distrito-fronterizo-de-santa-rosa-espera-confirmacion-oficial-sobre-sus-primeras-elecciones-distritales/ |work=Radio La Soberana}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |title=Loreto: empadronan a familias del distrito Santa Rosa para facilitar su acceso a programas sociales |date=2025-08-29 |url=https://elperuano.pe/noticia/277840-loreto-empadronan-a-familias-del-distrito-santa-rosa-para-facilitar-su-acceso-a-programas-sociales |work=[[El Peruano]]}}</ref> A temporary board of neighbourhood delegates was established until the election of authorities in the local elections of September 2025.<ref>{{Cite news |title=LEY Nº 32403: Ley de creación del distrito de Santa Rosa de Loreto en la provincia de Mariscal Ramón Castilla del departamento de Loreto |last=Boluarte Zegarra |first=Dina Ercilia |date=2025-07-03 |url=https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2415565-1 |work=[[El Peruano]] |author-link=Dina Boluarte}}</ref>

Its creation and inclusion of [[Santa Rosa Island (Amazon River)|Santa Rosa Island]] led to the protest of president of Colombia [[Gustavo Petro]], who claimed that [[Colombian–Peruvian territorial dispute (2024–present)|Peru had usurped Colombian territory]], and announced that the celebrations of the [[Battle of Boyacá]] would be moved to nearby [[Leticia, Amazonas|Leticia]] in response.<ref name=Giner/><ref>{{Cite news |title=La postura de Gustavo Petro genera rechazo unánime en el Perú: la historia y las claves del impasse por la isla Santa Rosa |last=Ortiz Martínez |first=Sebastian |date=2025-08-06 |url=https://elcomercio.pe/politica/gobierno/isla-santa-rosa-la-postura-de-gustavo-petro-genera-rechazo-unanime-en-el-peru-la-historia-y-las-claves-del-impasse-por-la-isla-santa-rosa-noticia/ |work=[[El Comercio (Peru)|El Comercio]]}}</ref>
