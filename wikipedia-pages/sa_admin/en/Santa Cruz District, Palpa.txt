'''Santa Cruz District''' is one of five [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Palpa Province|Palpa]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> A large Pre-Comlumbian archaeological site called the Lost City of [[Huayuri]] is located in the district.

== Tourism ==
The Santa Cruz District is a common destination for hikers, interested in Punta Union (peak) and [[Cordillera Blanca]]. The hiking tracks offer an elevation of up to 4,760 meters above sea level. The majority of the trekking companies are located in [[Huaraz]], one of the biggest towns in the [[Andes|Peruvian Andes]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Santa Cruz Trek, Peru - A Guide to Hiking the Cordillera Blanca|url=https://www.cleverhiker.com/blog/santa-cruz-trek-peru-a-guide-to-hiking-in-the-cordillera-blanca|access-date=2021-05-06|website=CleverHiker|language=en-US}}</ref> The 4 and 5-day passing treks are in the Andes are considered one of the most beautiful in [[South America]].<ref>{{Cite web|title=Santa Cruz Trek|url=https://ecoice-peru.com/trekking-santa-cruz/|access-date=2021-05-06|website=Eco Ice Peru|language=en-US}}</ref>
