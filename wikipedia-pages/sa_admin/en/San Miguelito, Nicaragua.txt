'''San Miguelito''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Río San Juan (department)|Río San Juan]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupied an area of {{cvt|1097|km2}}, and as per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 17,031 individuals. The settlement was founded in the mid 19th century by [[rubber]] tappers and [[Carapichea ipecacuanha|Ipecacuanha]] gatherers, and was a major commercial center in the 20th century.

==History==
The first settlement in the area, to the east of the current location, was known as "Las Aldeas" ("Villages") and was founded between the years 1850 and 1855 by [[rubber]] tappers and [[Carapichea ipecacuanha|Ipecacuanha]] gatherers (raicilleros). In the 20th century, San Miguelito became a major commercial center due to its rubber and raicilla based industries. A railway line was built to transport the produce to the port city of Punta Mico on the Atlantic coast.<ref name="San">{{cite web|url=https://www.riosanjuan.com.ni/en/el-almendro-2/|title=El Almendro – Río San Juan|work=Instituto Nicaragüense de turismo (INTUR)|access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref><ref name="MAPA">{{cite web|url=https://mapanicaragua.com/en/municipality-of-the-almond-tree/|title=Municipality of El Almendro|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref>

==Geography ==
San Miguelito is a municipality in the [[Río San Juan Department]] of southeastern Nicaragua. It occupies an area of {{cvt|1097|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/ |title=Administrative Divisions of Nicaragua |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref> It shares boundaries with the municipalities of [[Acoyapa]], [[Villa Sandino]], and [[El Coral]] to the north, [[Nueva Guinea]] to the east, [[Morrito]] to the west, and [[San Miguelito, Río San Juan|San Miguelito]] and Nueva Guinea to the south.<ref name="MAPA"/> It is situated on the eastern shore of [[Lake Nicaragua]],<ref name="San"/> about {{cvt|250|km}} from the national capital of [[Managua]].<ref name="MAPA"/>

==Demographics and economy==
As per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 19,543 individuals of whom 10,013 were males and 9,530 were females. The rural population was 15,254 (78.1%) and the rest of the population of 4,289 (21.9%) was classified as urban.<ref name="CP">{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/r%C3%ADo_san_juan/8515__san_miguelito/ |title=San Miguelito |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref> 

While it was a major center of rubber production earlier, the major economic activity now is [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] rearing. The municipality is called "The white water pool", due to its dairy industry.<ref name="MAPA"/>
