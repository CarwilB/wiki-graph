'''San Pedro''' ({{IPA|es|sam ˈpeðɾo}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Sucre Department]], northern [[Colombia]].

The original town was developed in 1684 by settlers from Sincé, Pileta, Corozal, Betulia, Sincelejo, Ovejas, El Salado, [[El Carmen de Bolívar]], Tacamocho, Magangue, Cascajal and [[Buenavista, Sucre|Buenavista]].
