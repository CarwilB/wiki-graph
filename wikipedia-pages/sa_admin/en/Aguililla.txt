'''Aguililla ''' ({{IPA|es|aɣ̞iˈliʝa}}) is a city and municipality in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. The municipality has a population of roughly 15,000. It is located in the western part of the state.

Starting in late 2019, Aguililla has been the center of a conflict between two of the most notorious drug cartels in the country—[[Jalisco New Generation Cartel]] (CJNG) and [[La Resistencia (gang)|Cárteles Unidos]].<ref>{{cite news |title=El CJNG entregó juguetes de Día de Reyes en el Aguaje, zona donde ha sembrado el terror por años |url=https://www.infobae.com/america/mexico/2021/01/07/el-cjng-entrego-juguetes-de-dia-de-reyes-en-el-aguaje-zona-donde-ha-sembrado-el-terror-por-anos/ |access-date=January 7, 2021 |work=infobae |publisher=Infobae |date=January 7, 2021 |language=es-ES}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Aguililla: The Mexican town in the crossfires of a cartel war |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2021/4/22/aguililla-the-mexican-town-in-the-crossfires-of-a-cartel-war |agency=Al Jazeera |date=April 22, 2021}}</ref>
