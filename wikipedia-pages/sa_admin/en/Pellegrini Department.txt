'''Pellegrini Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department of Argentina]] in [[Santiago del Estero Province]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.nuevodiarioweb.com.ar/policiales/327179-un-sismo-abrio-profundas-grietas-de-mas-de-dos-kilometros-en-pellegrini.htm|title=Un sismo abrió profundas grietas de más de dos kilómetros en Pellegrini|work=Nuevo Diario Web|date=25 January 2022|access-date=28 November 2025|language=es}}</ref> The capital city of the department is in [[Nueva Esperanza]].

==Location==
Pellegrini is located in the far northwest corner of [[Santiago del Estero Province|Santiago del Estero]]. It borders [[Salta Province]] and [[Tucumán Province]].
