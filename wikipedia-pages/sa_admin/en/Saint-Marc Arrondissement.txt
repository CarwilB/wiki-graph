'''Saint-Marc''' ({{langx|ht|Sen Mak}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 268,499 inhabitants.<ref>''Institut Haïtien de Statistique et d'Informatique'' (IHSI), 7 August 2003.</ref>
Postal codes in the Saint-Marc Arrondissement start with the number 43.
[[File:Aerial 2.jpeg|300px|thumb|Saint-Marc Arrondissement]]

The arrondissement consists of the following municipalities:
* [[Saint-Marc]]
* [[La Chapelle, Artibonite|La Chapelle]]
* [[Verrettes]]
* [[Montrouis]]
*[[Liancourt, Haiti|Liancourt]]
