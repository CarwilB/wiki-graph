'''Coacalco de Berriozábal''' ({{pronunciation|es-mx-Coacalco de Berriozábal.wav|Spanish pronunciation|help=yes}}; simply known as '''Coacalco''') is one of 125 [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipalities]] in the [[State of Mexico]], [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is the city of San Francisco Coacalco.  The municipality lies in the [[Greater Mexico City]] [[conurbation]], north of [[Mexico City]]. The municipal seat is '''San Francisco Coacalco''' and the municipality is named after [[Felipe Berriozábal]] (1829–1900), a Mexican politician and military leader.

The word ''Coacalco'' comes from the [[Nahuatl language|Nahuatl]] {{lang|nah|cōātl}} ("snake"), {{lang|nah|calli}} ("home"), and the suffix -{{lang|nah|co}} ("at"), thus meaning "at the house of the snake", a name that was first recorded in 1320.<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web |url=http://e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM15mexico/municipios/15020a.html |language=es |publisher= INEGI |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de Mexico Estado de Mexico Coacalco |access-date=20 April 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120405034717/http://e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM15mexico/municipios/15020a.html |archive-date=5 April 2012 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==
Coacalco de Berriozábal is part of the [[Valley of Mexico]]. It is located at the site of what was once the city-state of [[Xaltocan]]. Between 850 and 1521, the municipality was inhabited by [[Toltec]] people.<ref name="EncMuc"/> In the 18th and 19th centuries, the main economic activities were [[agriculture]], [[Animal husbandry|husbandry]] and [[salt]] harvesting. On 12 February 1862, General [[Felipe Berriozábal]], then-governor of the state, signed an order that declared Coacalco an independent municipality, ending a 343-year-old dependency of [[Ecatepec de Morelos|Ecatepec]].<ref name="EncMuc"/>

==Economy==
The economy of Coacalco has changed since the 1970s, having switched from being primarily agricultural and rural to an urban industrial area. Since the 2000s, the municipality has become one of the principal commercial districts of the northern part of the metropolitan area.

==Population==
[[File:Evolucion Demografica Coacalco2.png|thumb|right|Increase of the population from the 1970s to the 2000s]]
Located near Mexico City, Coacalco's population has increased by more than 200,000 since 1970. In 2005, the city had a population of 252,555 people;<ref name="INEGI">{{cite web |url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/tabuladosbasicos/tabulados/cpv2000/15po04.pdf |title=Población total, edad, edad mediana e índice de masculinidad |year=2005 |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática]] |access-date= 20 April 2010 |language=es|url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120305183822/http://www.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/tabuladosbasicos/tabulados/cpv2000/15po04.pdf |archive-date=5 March 2012}}</ref> by 2010, the population grew to 278,064 inhabitants.<ref name="inegi10">{{cite web |url=http://cuentame.inegi.org.mx/monografias/informacion/mex/territorio/div_municipal.aspx?tema=me&e=15 |title=División municipal.
