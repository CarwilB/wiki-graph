'''Niquinohomo''' ({{IPA|es|nikinoˈomo}}) is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Masaya (department)|Masaya]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].
It is home to the oldest extant church in Nicaragua; Church of Saint Ann (Iglesia de Santa Ana). The city's patron saint, as well as the birthplace of [[Augusto César Sandino]], after whom the [[Sandinista National Liberation Front|Sandinista]] movement was named.
