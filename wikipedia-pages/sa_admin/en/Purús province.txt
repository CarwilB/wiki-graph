'''Purús''' is one of four [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Ucayali Region]], in the central [[Amazon rainforest]] of [[Peru]]. In 2022 it had a population of 3,270.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Peru: Administrative Division (Regions and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/peru/admin/ |access-date=2025-06-19 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> The [[Purus River]] runs through it.
