'''Pataz''' is one of twelve [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[La Libertad Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Tayabamba]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into thirteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

* [[Buldibuyo District|Buldibuyo]]
* [[Chillia District|Chillia]]
* [[Huancaspata District|Huancaspata]]
* [[Huaylillas District|Huaylillas]]
* [[Huayo District|Huayo]]
* [[Ongón District|Ongón]]
* [[Parcoy District|Parcoy]]
* [[Pataz District|Pataz]]
* [[Pías District|Pías]]
* [[Santiago de Challas District|Santiago de Challas]]
* [[Taurija District|Taurija]]
* [[Tayabamba District|Tayabamba]]
* [[Urpay District|Urpay]]
