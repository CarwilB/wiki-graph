'''Corinto''' is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].

Yordan Guetio of Corinto, a social activist, was murdered on February 2, 2021. He was the twentieth such human rights activist murdered, along with five massacres and one signer of a peace agreement who is missing as of this date in 2021.<ref>{{cite news |title=Asesinaron a otro líder social, esta vez fue en Corinto, Cauca |url=https://www.infobae.com/america/colombia/2021/02/03/asesinaron-a-otro-lider-social-esta-vez-fue-en-corinto-cauca/ |access-date=February 3, 2021 |work=infobae |publisher=Infobae |date=February 3, 2021 |language=es-ES}}</ref>

A car bomb exploded on March 27, 2021, injuring eleven.<ref>{{cite news |title=At least 16 injured in Colombia car bomb blast |url=https://www.yahoo.com/news/least-16-injured-colombia-car-060018499.html |access-date=March 27, 2021 |work=news.yahoo.com |agency=Reuters |date=March 27, 2021}}</ref>
