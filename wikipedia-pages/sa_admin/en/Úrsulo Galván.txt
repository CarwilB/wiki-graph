'''Úrsulo Galván''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central coastal zone of the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 76&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 123.92&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The name of this municipality is in honor of {{ill|Úrsulo Galván Reyes|es}}, an early 20th-century agrarian activist and revolutionary.

==Geography==
The municipality of Úrsulo Galván is bordered to the north by [[Actopan (municipality)|Actopan]], to the east by [[Gulf of Mexico]] and to the south by [[Puente Nacional, Veracruz|Puente Nacional]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web | url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30191a.htm | title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Úrsulo Galván | publisher=Gobierno Estatal | accessdate=2009-06-17 | language=Spanish | website=veracruz.gob.mx}}</ref>

The weather in Úrsulo Galván is hot and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==History==
Gilberto Ortiz Parra, precandidate for mayor ([[Morena (political party)|MORENA]]) and two police officers died on February 11, 2021, after being shot.<ref>{{cite news |title=Asesinan a tiros a precandidato de Morena en Veracruz |url=https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/estados/asesinan-tiros-precandidato-de-morena-en-veracruz |access-date=February 12, 2021 |work=El Universal |date=11 February 2021 |language=es}}</ref>

==Agriculture==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[watermelon]], [[sugarcane]], [[rice]] and [[green chile]].

==Celebrations==
In November there is a celebration in honor of San Carlos, patron of the town, and in December that in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].
