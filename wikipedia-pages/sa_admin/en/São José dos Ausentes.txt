'''São José dos Ausentes''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] with an average altitude of 1200 meters, in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. Its population was approximately 3,543 in 2020. It has an area of approximately 1176&nbsp;km². [[Pico do Monte Negro]] is located in São José dos Ausentes, with its peak 1403 meters above sea level. It is the highest point in [[Rio Grande do Sul]] State. The town is 220&nbsp;km away from the state's capital, [[Porto Alegre]].

==Geography==
===Climate===
São José dos Ausentes features a [[Oceanic climate|temperate oceanic climate]] (type ''Cfb''), It features an annual uniform precipitation. The Annual average temperature is 13&nbsp;°C
The city is one of the coldest in Brazil and snow precipitation is not uncommon.
