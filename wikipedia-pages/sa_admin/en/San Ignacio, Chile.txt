'''San Ignacio''' ({{IPA|es|san iɣˈnasjo}}) is a Chilean town and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Diguillín Province]], Ñuble Region. It has an area of {{convert|363.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.<ref name="INE"/>

==Demographics==
According to data from the 2002 Census of Population and Housing, San Ignacio had 16,106 inhabitants; of these, 4,873 (30.3%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 11,233 (69.7%) in [[rural area]]s. At that time, there were 8,192 men and 7,914 women.<ref name="INE"/> ''Sanignacino'' is the [[demonym]] for a man, and ''Sanignacina'' is that of a woman.

==Administration==
As a [[Communes of Chile|commune]], San Ignacio is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="SUBDERE"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], San Ignacio is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] as a part of the 41st electoral district (together with [[Chillán]], [[Coihueco]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[El Carmen, Chile|El Carmen]], [[Pemuco]], [[Yungay, Chile|Yungay]] and [[Chillán Viejo]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]]
