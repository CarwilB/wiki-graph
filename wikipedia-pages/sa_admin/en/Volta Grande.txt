'''Volta Grande''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020 its population was estimated to be 5,261.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/volta-grande/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
Volta Grande is located in the [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] mesoregion of Minas Gerais near the border with the state of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. The city is {{convert|352|km|abbr=on}} by road from the state capital of [[Belo Horizonte]]. It lies at an altitude of {{convert|210|m|abbr=on}}.

===Climate===
The climate is tropical with rains during the summer and mean annual temperature around {{convert|19|C|abbr=on}}, with variations between {{convert|15|C|abbr=on}} (average minimum) and {{convert|23|C|abbr=on}} (mean maximum).

===Hydrography===
The rivers [[Paraiba do Sul]] and [[Rio Angu|Angu]] drain the municipality of Volta Grande which is part of the Paraiba do Sul river basin.

==History==
The town originated from a village formed in 1835 and was elevated to a district of the municipality of [[Alem Paraíba]] in 1891. Emancipation came in 1938.

==Notable people==
It is the birthplace of film director [[Humberto Mauro]].
