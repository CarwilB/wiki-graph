'''Chiautempan''' is one of the [[Municipalities of Tlaxcala|municipalities]] of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tlaxcala]]. The municipal seat is at [[Santa Ana Chiautempan]].

As of 2020, the location had a population of 73,125 people.<ref>{{cite web | title=México en cifras | website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía (INEGI) | date=March 4, 2020 | url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/?ag=29010#collapse-Resumen | language=es | access-date=March 26, 2026}}</ref>
