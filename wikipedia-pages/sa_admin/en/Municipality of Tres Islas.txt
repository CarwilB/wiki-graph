The '''municipality of Tres Islas''' is one of the municipalities of [[Cerro Largo Department]], [[Uruguay]], established on 30 October 2018. Its seat is the town of [[Tres Islas, Uruguay|Tres Islas]].

== History ==
This municipality was created on 30 October 2018 by Departmental Board of Cerro Largo Decree No. 29/2018, covering the same territory of the electoral constituency identified by the Electoral Board of Cerro Largo as series GFA.<ref name=DIGESTO />

== Location ==
The municipality is located at the west area of Cerro Largo Department. Its seat is the town of Tres Islas.

== Authorities ==
The authority of the municipality is the Municipal Council, integrated by the Mayor (who presides it) and four Councilors.
