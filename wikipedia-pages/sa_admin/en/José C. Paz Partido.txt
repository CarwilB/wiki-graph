'''José C. Paz Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the [[Greater Buenos Aires]] urban area of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 263,094 inhabitants<ref name=Cen2010 /> in an area of {{convert|50.11|km2|sqmi|1|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[José C. Paz, Buenos Aires|José Clemente Paz]], which is {{convert|35|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==Economy==
The main industries in J.C. Paz Partido are centered on the production of textiles, ceramics and food.{{cn|date=February 2024}}
