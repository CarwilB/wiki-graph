'''El Tallán District''' is one of ten [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Piura Province|Piura]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 17, 2008.</ref> The district was created by the law Ley N° 15434 of February 19, 1965 during the first government of Peruvian President [[Fernando Belaúnde]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Ley N° 15434|url=http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/15434.pdf|access-date=April 21, 2019|language=es}}</ref>
