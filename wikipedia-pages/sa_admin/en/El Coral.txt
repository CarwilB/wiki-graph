'''El Coral''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the southeastern portion of [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|306|km2}}, and as per 2023 estimate, the municipality has a population of 8,427 individuals. 

==History==
The name "El Coral" originates from "La Quebrada del Coral", a stream in the region that was named after the [[coral snake]] by the early settlers in the region. Till the middle of the 19th century, the population was dominated by indigenous people. Later, outside settlers came to the region to set up cattle ranches, and [[timber]] yards. In the mid 20th century, further migration increased the population.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/municipality-of-el-coral/|title=Municipio de El Coral|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Cul">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/coral-culture/|title=Culture of El Coral|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

El Coral was officially established as a municipality on 20 February 1997.<ref name="Hist"/><ref>{{cite web|url=http://legislacion.asamblea.gob.ni/normaweb.nsf/b34f77cd9d23625e06257265005d21fa/bd14b25a8639c058062570a1005778d6?OpenDocument|title=LEY N°. 251, Ley Creadora De Los Municipios de San Francisco Cuapa y El Coral|publisher=[[National Assembly (Nicaragua)|National Assembly]]|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es|trans-title=Law Creating the Municipalities of San Francisco Cuapa and El Croal}}</ref>

==Geography==
El Coral is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Chontales Department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It occupies an area of {{cvt|306|km2}}.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/ |title=Administrative Divisions of Nicaragua |work=City Population.de |access-date=19 May 2026}}</ref> It is located in the southeastern part of the department.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|url=https://www.mapanicaragua.com/en/nature-of-the-coral/|title=Nature of El Coral|work=MAPA Nicaragua|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The municipality is bordered by the municipalities of [[Nueva Guinea]] and [[El Almendro]] to the south, [[Villa Sandino]] and [[Muelle de los Bueyes]] to the north, Muelle de los Bueyes and Nueva Guinea to the east, and [[Acoyapa]] and Villa Sandino to the west. It is located {{cvt|239|km}} from the Nicaraguan capital of [[Managua]].<ref name="Hist"/>

The topography is predominantly flat, with a few small peaks along the northern border. The highest peak is the Cerro Las Paces at {{cvt|545|m}}. There are several rivers flowing through the municipality, the major of which is the El Rama River. The vegetation consists of [[tropical rainforests]] with a high precipitation throughout the year.<ref name="Geo"/>

== Demographics and economy ==
As per 2023 estimate, El Coral has a population of 8,427 individuals of whom 4,176 males and 4,251 females. The urban population was 4,554 (54%) and the rest of the population (46%) was classified as living in rural areas.<ref name="CP">{{cite web |title=El Coral (Municipality, Nicaragua) |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/nicaragua/admin/chontales/6545__el_coral/ |access-date=3 April 2026 |work=City Population.de}}</ref>

The economy of the municipality is mainly based on [[livestock]] rearing and associated industries. There is limited [[agriculture]], and the major agricultural produce include [[maize]], beans, [[cassava]], and [[sugarcane]].<ref name="Hist"/> The soil is poor, and due to the dense vegetation, agroforestry systems are common, especially [[banana]] and [[coffee]] plantations.<ref name="Geo"/> The municipality's climate supports year round grazing and attracts ranchers from other parts of the department during the dry season.<ref name="Cul"/>
