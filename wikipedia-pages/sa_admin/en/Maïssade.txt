'''Maïssade''' ({{IPA|fr|maisad}}; {{langx|ht|Mayisad}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Hinche Arrondissement]], in the [[Centre (department)|Centre]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.travelsradiate.com/north-america/republic-of-haiti/departement-du-centre/maissade/3721711|title=Maissade|publisher=travelsradiate.com|access-date=28 January 2010|location=travelsradiate}}</ref> of [[Haiti]].
It has 58,942 inhabitants as of 2015.
