'''Vista Alegre do Prata''' (lit. Joyous View of Silver) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the northeastern part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 1,557 (2020 est.) in an area of 119.33&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1D70 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 562 m.
