'''Alto Caparaó''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Manhuaçu. {{as of|2020}} its population is estimated to be 5,894.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/alto-caparao/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
