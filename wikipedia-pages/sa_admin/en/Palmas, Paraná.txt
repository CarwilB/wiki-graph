'''Palmas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the south of the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]]. Palmas is located in the mountainous area of Paraná and is in the [[mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] of South-Central Paraná. Palmas is the capital of the [[microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of the same name. The population is 51,755 (2020 est.) in an area of 1557.90&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/235KP Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 1,056 m.
