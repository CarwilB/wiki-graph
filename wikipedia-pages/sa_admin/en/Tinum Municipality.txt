'''Tinum Municipality''' ([[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]]: "crippled [[Acanthocereus tetragonus|numtzutzuy]]{{#tag:ref|"cereus pentagonus: Numtzutzuy (Gaumer); reported also as "nuntzutzuy." Sp. Tuna silvestre. Common. Stems climbing, 3-5-angled, rarely 6-8-angled,  the spines 4 cm. long or less; flowers 14-20 cm. long, white; fruit large,  red. The fruit is edible. The spines are sometimes used as a substitute for pins".<ref name=numtzutzuy>{{cite web|last1=Standley|first1=Paul C.|title=Flora of Yucatán|url=https://archive.org/stream/floraofyucatanfistan#page/366/mode/2up|publisher=Field Museum of Natural History|access-date=15 August 2015|location=Chicago, Illinois|page=366|date=1930}}</ref>|group="n"}}")<ref name="name meaning">{{cite web|title=El Significado de los Nombres de los Pueblos de Yucatán: Plantas|url=http://www.meridadeyucatan.com/el-significado-de-los-nombres-de-los-pueblos-de-yucatan-plantas/|website=Merida de Yucatan|publisher=Crónicas de la Ciudad Blanca|access-date=15 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es}}</ref> is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]] containing {{convert|393.44|km2|abbr=on}} of land and is located roughly {{convert|140|km}} east of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/>

==History==
There is no accurate data on when the town was founded, but it was a settlement before the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]] and was located in the chieftainship of  Cupules. Within the municipality is [[Chichen Itza]], a city built in the Post Classic Maya period, which reached its apex between the 11th and 12th centuries.

After colonization by the Spanish, the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system with various encomenderos,<ref name="municipios"/> beginning with Juan García de Llanos in 1549 and passing to the crown in 1551. In 1607, it passed to Baltasar Pacheco Dorantes.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5|page=515| url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA503|access-date=15 August 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821. In 1825<ref name="state divisions">{{cite web|title=Estado de Yucatán. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995|url=http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|website=inegi|publisher=Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática|access-date=15 August 2015|location=Aguascalientes, Mexico|language=es|date=1996|page=15|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151223101757/http://www.inegi.org.mx/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/yuc/YUCATAN.pdf|archive-date=2015-12-23}}</ref> the Mexican government assigned this community to the [[Valladolid Municipality, Yucatan|Valladolid Municipality]]. In 1918 Tinum was designated as an independent municipality.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has seven councilpersons, who serve as Secretary and councilors of public works, police commissaries, education, ecology, public monuments and sports.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Tinum | url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=15 August 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es}}</ref>

==Communities==
The seat of the municipality is Tinum. The municipality has 37 populated places<ref name=governance /> besides the seat, including Balantún, Chichén Itzá, Chichil, Dzulotok, Macuchén, [[Pisté, Yucatán|Pisté]], San Francisco, San Felipe, San Felipe Nuevo, San José, San Nicolás, Santa María, Tohopkú, and X-Calakoop. ==Local festivals==
Every year on 12 June, the town celebrates the feast of [[Saint Anthony of Padua]], its patron saint.<ref name="municipios"/>

==Tourist attractions==
* Church of Saint Anthony of Padua, built during the colonial era
* Church of Conception, built during the colonial era
* archeological sites at Bacancú, Canahum, [[Chichen Itza]], Dzibiac, Halacal, Joya de Erik Thompson, Pisté, San Francisco Semé, San Juan Holtún, Tikincab, La Venta and Xnabá.
* [[Sacred Cenote]]
* [[Hacienda Chichén]]
