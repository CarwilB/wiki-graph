'''San Justo Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba Province]] in [[Argentina]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lmdiario.com.ar/contenido/395211/el-domingo-25-de-junio-seran-las-elecciones-provinciales-en-cordoba|title=El domingo 25 de junio serán las elecciones provinciales en Córdoba|work=La Nueva Mañana|date=20 March 2023|access-date=4 March 2026|language=es}}</ref>

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 190,182 inhabitants in an area of 13,677&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[San Francisco, Córdoba|San Francisco]], which is located around 670&nbsp;km from [[Buenos Aires]]. The department is home to [[Mar Chiquita (Córdoba)|Mar Chiquita]], the largest inland body of water in Argentina.
