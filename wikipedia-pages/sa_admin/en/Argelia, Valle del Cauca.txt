'''Argelia''' is a town and  municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Valle del Cauca Department|Valle del Cauca]], [[Colombia]]. The main means of income for this region is coffee.{{Fact|date=October 2008}} 

Argelia was founded by the Ospina-Agi brothers, and are now recognized by a donkey statue for their great efforts in transporting goods by donkey up the jungle terrain and steep hills.
