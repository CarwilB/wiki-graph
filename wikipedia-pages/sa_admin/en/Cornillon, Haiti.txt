'''Cornillon''' ({{IPA|fr|kɔʁnijɔ̃}}; {{langx|ht|Kòniyon}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Croix-des-Bouquets Arrondissement]], in the [[Ouest (department)|Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 54,254 inhabitants.<ref name="IHSI"/>
