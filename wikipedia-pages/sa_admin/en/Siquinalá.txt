'''Siquinalá''' is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|103.4|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 26,645 inhabitants.

==History==
The origin of Siquinalá dates to the [[pre-Columbian period]], where it was located between the settlements of Cotzumalguapa and Itzcuintlan, and was probably occupied by the [[Kaqchikel people]]. The population was later forced to migrate due to volcanic eruptions.<ref name="Hist"/>

On 11 October 1825, when the Constitution of Guatemala was adopted,  Siquinalá was part of the District No. 2 in the circuit of Escuintla. On 6 March 1867, it was elevated as a municipality. While the municipality might have been abolished later, it was restored by a government order on 4 August 1910.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de Siquinala|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of Siquinala municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-del-municipio-de-siquinala-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
Siquinala is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|103.4|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=Siquinala (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0504__siquinal%C3%A1/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the north western part of the department, about {{cvt|82|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|170|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of 
[[Santa Lucía Cotzumalguapa]] to the north and west, Escuintla to the east, and [[La Democracia, Escuintla|La Democracia]] south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de Siquinala |work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of Siquinala municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-siquinala-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|336.58|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> La Gomera has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|26.44|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|125.13|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/siquinala |title=Siquinala |work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251118201506/https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/escuintla/siquinala|archive-date=18 November 2025}}</ref>

The town holds an annual festival on November 25, in honor of [[Saint Catherine of Alexandria]] (''Santa Catalina de Alejandria'').

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 26,645 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 13,685 males and 12,960 females. About 30.1% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 5.1% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (79%) was classified as urban, while 21% lived in rural areas. About 53.6% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (92.9%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (5.9%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 86.4%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (94.4%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
