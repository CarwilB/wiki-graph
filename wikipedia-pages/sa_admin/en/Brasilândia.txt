'''Brazlândia''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 11,853&nbsp;(2020) and its area is {{Nowrap|5,807 km}}<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality contains the {{convert|3858|ha}} [[Cisalpina Private Natural Heritage Reserve]], created in 2016.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|last=Verruck|first=Jaime Elias|title=RESOLUÇÃO SEMADE Nº 35, de 06 de junho de 2016|date=6 June 2016|url=http://www.imasul.ms.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2015/06/Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o-SEMADE-n.-35-RPPN-CISALPINA-assinatura-2.pdf|accessdate=2016-11-14|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161114235747/http://www.imasul.ms.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/74/2015/06/Resolu%C3%A7%C3%A3o-SEMADE-n.-35-RPPN-CISALPINA-assinatura-2.pdf|archive-date=14 November 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
