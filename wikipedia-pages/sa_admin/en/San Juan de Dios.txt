'''San Juan de Dios''' is a ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]]'' in [[Antón District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|55.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 4,797 it 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|86.1|/sqkm|/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> The population in 1990 was 6,199 and in 2000 was 4,214.<ref name=inec/>
