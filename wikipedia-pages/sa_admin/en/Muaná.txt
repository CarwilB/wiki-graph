'''Muaná''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 40,906 people. The area of the municipality is 3,765.524&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of [[Arari]].

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=pt
