'''Sucre''' is a municipality of [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]], [[Venezuela]]. It has seven [[Parishes of Venezuela|parishes]] and the capital is [[Cumaná]], which is also the capital of the state. As of 2021, it has a population of 451,794.<ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones Regionales y Municipales – Municipio Sucre |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/web/mapas/mapas_cargos_a_elegir/documentos/17_ce_sucre_concejales_2021.pdf |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral |access-date=5 February 2024 |pages=17–18 |date=30 November 2021}}</ref>

== Name ==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Sucre Municipality (disambiguation)|Sucre Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio José de Sucre]].{{cn|date=February 2024}}
