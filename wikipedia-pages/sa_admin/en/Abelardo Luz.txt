'''Abelardo Luz''' is a Brazilian [[municipality]] in the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. Until 1958 it was part of the municipality of [[Xanxerê]]. It is the birthplace of Brazilian former football player [[Paulo Roberto Falcão]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|6566|ha}} [[Mata Preta Ecological Station]], a fully protected area created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC da Mata Preta – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=ESEC da Mata Preta|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591215|access-date=2016-07-18}}</ref>
