'''Génova''' ({{IPA|es|ˈxenoβa}}) is a municipality in the southern part of the department of [[Quindío Department|Quindío]], [[Colombia]].  It's located 52&nbsp;km south of the departmental capital [[Armenia, Colombia|Armenia]].

Located within the [[Colombian coffee growing axis]], the municipality was made part of the "Coffee Cultural Landscape" [[UNESCO]] [[World Heritage Site]] in 2011.<ref name="unesco">{{cite web |title = Coffee Cultural Landscape |url = http://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1121 |website = UNESCO World Heritage List |publisher = United Nations Educational, Scientific, and Cultural Organization |access-date = 25 Apr 2011}}</ref> In 2023 Génova had an estimated population of 7,726.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Génova, Quindío en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/quindio/genova |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==

Génova was founded by Segundo Henao Patiño, who had founded [[Calarcá, Quindío|Calarcá]] in 1886. During the Thousand Day War, Henao was told by mule drivers arriving from [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]] and [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] that the government war ministry was executing liberal guerrillas, with or without trial. In order to hide from these potential reprisals, he organized a group of liberals from [[Calarcá]] to explore the mountains to the south.

The expedition, headed by Henao, included the families Patiño, Ossa, Ospina, Restrepo, Aria, Herrera, Aguilar, Cardona, González, Orozco and Giraldo.  The group planted out land along the Gris and Azul rivers.  There were already several settlers living in the region, near the Gris River.

Henao was well known throughout the region for his role in the foundation of Calarcá, and all of the settlers enthusiastically supported him when he announced his intention to form a new town. He was placed in charge of choosing a site and designing the street plans. Henao chose a site near the joining of the Gris and San Juan rivers, on the land of Luis Ossa.

The official foundation took place on October 12, 1903. Génova became a ''corregimiento'' of [[Calarcá]] in 1906, and was later included in the municipality of [[Pijao, Quindío|Pijao]] when this was created in 1927. In 1937 the town became a separate municipality, with Fución Londoño the first mayor.

== Climate ==
Génova has a [[subtropical highland climate]] with an average of 21°C.
