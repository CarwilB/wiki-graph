'''Nueva Cajamarca District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Rioja Province|Rioja]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://www.inei.gob.pe INEI Peru]''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== History ==
Nueva Cajamarca was established as District on December 26, 1984, during [[Fernando Belaúnde Terry]] second term.
