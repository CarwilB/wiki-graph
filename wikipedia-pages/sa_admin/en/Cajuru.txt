'''Cajuru''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 26,393 (2020 est.) in an area of 660&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/384 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 775 m.
