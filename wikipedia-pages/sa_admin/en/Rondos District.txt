'''Rondos District''' is one of seven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Lauricocha Province|Lauricocha]] in the [[Huanuco Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The district had an area of {{convert|172.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and a population of 3,798 in 2017.
<ref name="Rondos">{{cite web |title=Rondos |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/peru/huanuco/admin/lauricocha/101005__rondos/ |website=City Population |access-date=7 August 2021}}</ref> The town of Rondos is the capital of the district and had a population of 826 in 2017. The town is situated on a mesa overlooking the junction of the [[Lauricocha River|Lauricocha]] and [[Nupe River|Nupe]] Rivers, {{convert|250|m|ft}} in elevation below Rondos, to form the [[Marañon River]]. The headwaters of both the Lauricocha and Nupe rivers have been proposed as [[Source of the Amazon River|sources of the Amazon River]].<ref name="Britannica">{{cite EB1911|wstitle= Amazon |volume= 01 | pages = 783&ndash;790 |last1= Church |first1= George Earl }}</ref>

==Climate==
[[File:Río Marañón 00050.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A view from the town of Rondos of the confluence of the Nupe and Lauricocha rivers forming the Marañon River.]]
The location within the tropics and the high elevation of Rondos impacts the climate which is typically cool and cloudy. Daily temperatures vary little during the course of a year and usually fall within a range of {{convert|2|C|F}} to {{convert|17|C|F}} degrees. Rarely does the temperature rise to above {{cvt|19|C|F}} or fall to below {{cvt|-1|C|F}}. The nearest weather station at [[San Miguel de Cauri District|San Miguel de Cauri]], {{cvt|30|km|mile}} distant from Rondos, gets {{cvt|872|mm|inch}} of precipitation annually.  Most is between October and April with a distinct dry season from May to September.<ref name="Weatherspark">{{cite web |title=Average Weather in Rondos |url=https://weatherspark.com/y/21372/Average-Weather-in-Rondos-Peru-Year-Round |website=Weatherspark |access-date=7 August 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=San Miguel de Cauri, Peru |url=https://www.weatherbase.com/weather/weather.php3?s=604546&cityname=San-Miguel-de-Cauri-Peru |website=Weatherbase |access-date=10 August 2021}}</ref>
