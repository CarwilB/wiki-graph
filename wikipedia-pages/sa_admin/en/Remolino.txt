'''Remolino''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. Founded between 1752 and 1776 by [[Fernando de Mier y Guerra]]. Erected municipality in 1814.
