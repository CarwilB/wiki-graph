'''Ricaurte''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. A law issued on 14 November  1857 created the Peñalisa District and December of the same year the name was changed to Ricaurte; in 1937 it was annexed to the Girardot municipality but it recovered its condition of municipality in 1968.

== Other Facts ==
* Market Day: Thursday and Sunday
* Distance from Bogotá: 142&nbsp;km
* Foundation: 1813 as Peñalisa
* Created: Departamental Decree No. 409 of 11 March 1968
* Median temperature: 27&nbsp;°C

== Veredas ==
The Municipality of Ricaurte has 16 Veredas, 17 within the Urban Zone.  The Veredas are:

*Callejón, Casablanca, Cumaca, El Paso, El Portal, La Carrera, La Tetilla, La Virginia, Las Veras, Llano del Pozo, Limoncito, Manuel Norte, Manuel Sur, Pto Peñalisa, San Francisco, San Martin and Zona Urbana
