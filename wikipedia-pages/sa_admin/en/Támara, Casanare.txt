'''Támara''' is a town and municipality in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]], [[Colombia]]. The urban centre is located at an altitude of {{convert|1156|m|ft}} at a distance of {{convert|95|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Yopal]]. It borders in the north [[Sacama]], in the east [[Pore, Casanare|Pore]] and [[Paz de Ariporo]], in the west [[Socotá]] of the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]] and in the south [[Nunchia]] and [[Paya, Boyacá]].<ref name=websiteTamara>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.tamara-casanare.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Támara]</ref>

== History ==
Támara before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]] was an important [[cotton]] producing village.<ref name=websiteTamara/>

Modern Támara was founded on August 6, 1628, by José Dadey Pey.<ref name=websiteTamara/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Támara are [[agriculture]]; [[coffee]], [[yuca]], [[maize]], [[banana]]s and [[sugarcane]] and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteTamara/>
