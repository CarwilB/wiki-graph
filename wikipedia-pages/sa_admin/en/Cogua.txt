'''Cogua''' ({{IPA|es|ˈkoɣwa}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. It is situated on northern part of the [[Bogotá savanna]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2600|m|ft}} at {{convert|50|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]]. Cogua borders [[Tausa]] in the north, [[Nemocón]] in the east, [[Pacho]] in the west and [[Zipaquirá]] in the south.<ref name=websiteCogua>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.cogua-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Cogua] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150520064443/http://www.cogua-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-05-20 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Cogua is derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Support of the hill".<ref name=websiteCogua/>

== History ==
Cogua in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] who lived on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] and had established an advanced civilization. Cogua is located between two important sources of [[halite|salt]] in the [[Muisca economy]]; Zipaquirá and Nemocón.

Modern Cogua was founded on August 23, 1604 by Lorenzo de Terrones.<ref name=websiteCogua/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Cogua are [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming.<ref name=websiteCogua/> The [[Neusa Reservoir]] is located within the boundaries of Cogua.
