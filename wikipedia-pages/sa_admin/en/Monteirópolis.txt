'''Monteirópolis''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[mõte(j)ˈɾɔpɔlis]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 7,165 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/monteiropolis/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 86&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/monteiropolis/panorama]</ref>
