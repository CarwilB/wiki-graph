'''Catuípe''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the western part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 8,701 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|583.26|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1KCV Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its nickname is ''Land of Spring Water'' for the springs located around the municipality. It is located 419&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
