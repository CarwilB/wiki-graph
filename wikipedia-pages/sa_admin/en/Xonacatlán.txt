'''Xonacatlán''' ({{IPA|es|xonakaˈtlan|-|}}) is one of 125 [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipalities]] in [[Mexico (state)|Mexico State]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|32.87|km2}}.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 45,274. [[San Francisco Xonacatlán|Xonacatlán de Vicencio]] is located in the municipality of Xonacatlán.<ref>{{Cite web

==Etymology==
According to the ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México'', published by the Mexican government, the name Xonacatlán is [[Nahuatl language|Nahuatl]] in origin and means "among the onions" ("onion" in Nahuatl is "xonacatl").<ref name=EMM>[https://archive.today/20110718113347/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15115a.htm  Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Xonacatlán]</ref>

==History==
The date of the town's foundation is not historically recorded. However, it was conquered in 1476 by [[Axayácatl]] during the conquest of the valley of [[Toluca, Mexico|Toluca]].<ref name=EMM /> The town fell to the invading Spanish forces in 1521.<ref name=EMM /> The independence of Mexico in 1821 contributed to the town's prosperity.<ref name=EMM /> The town was originally assigned to part of another municipality, but Celso Vicencio Hernández advocated for its municipal status in the State of Mexico's legislature, ultimately successfully, and municipal status was conferred on October 18, 1870.<ref name=EMM /> Afterward, political power was held by a few dominated by the Vicencio family.<ref name=EMM /> Upon Celso Vicencio's death on February 19, 1908, the city government proclaimed the renaming of the town to ''Xonacatlán de Vicencio''.<ref name=EMM />  The city hall was built from 1931 to 1945.<ref name=EMM />
