==History==
Garzas (Spanish for '[[Heron|herons]]') was one of the original six ''barrios'' of Adjuntas as evident in the 1818 administrative rural census.<ref>{{cite web |title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881|website=Biblioteca Nacional de España|url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587|language=es|page=1614|access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> Its population grew in size, first due to the establishment of ''[[Citron|Citrus medica]]'' crops (locally known as ''cidra'') during the end of the 19th century. Construction of a dam between 1936 and 1943 further developed the area as the establishment of the [[Garzas Lake]] solidified the infrastructure and fortified both the agricultural and recreational opportunities in the area.<ref name=":0">"Barrio Guilarte, Adjuntas." ''80 Grados''. San Juan, Puerto Rico. Available at: <nowiki>https://www.80grados.net/barrio-guilarte-adjuntas/</nowiki> (accessed June 15, 2026).</ref>

== Demographics ==
Garzas was in Spain's [[gazetteer]]s<ref>{{cite web | title=Anuario del comercio, de la industria, de la magistratura y de la administración. 1881 | website=Biblioteca Nacional de España | url=https://hemerotecadigital.bne.es/hd/es/viewer?id=9047075f-3c7e-411c-9e1e-41a2f650e1ba&page=1587 | language=es |page=1614| access-date=4 April 2023}}</ref> until Puerto Rico was ceded by [[Spain]] in the aftermath of the [[Spanish–American War]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Paris of 1898]] and became an [[Insular area|unincorporated territory of the United States]]. == Landmarks and places of interest ==

* '''[[Villa Sotomayor|Hacienda Villa Sotomayor]]''' is a [[Paradores in Puerto Rico|''parador'']] located along the Vacas River near its confluence with the [[Cidra River]].<ref name="Primera Hora 2018">{{cite web |date=December 3, 2018 |title=Gigante seducción en Adjuntas |url=https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/notas/gigante-seduccion-en-adjuntas/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200719152940/https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/notas/gigante-seduccion-en-adjuntas/ |archive-date=2020-07-19 |access-date=2020-07-18 |website=Primera Hora |language=es}}</ref>
* '''[[Garzas Lake]]''' is a reservoir established in 1943 by the damming of the Vacas River. One of the highest reservoirs of its size in the island, it is popular for fishing and boating.<ref name=":0" />
* '''''Salto Ataúd''''' (Spanish for 'coffin falls') is a tiered waterfall also located along the Vacas River, downstream from Garzas Lake. It is a popular site for swimming and is located at GPS coordinates {{coord|18.15385|-66.74065|display=inline|format=dec}}, which can be accessed from PR-522. The area also contains other ''charcas'' ([[Plunge pool|plunge pools]]) such as ''Las Losetas'' and ''Las Garzas''.<ref>{{cite book |title=Guía de Saltos y Caídas de Agua de Puerto Rico |publisher=Estado Libre Asociado de Puerto Rico, Departamento de Recursos Naturales y Ambientales |page=4 |url=https://www.drna.pr.gov/wp-content/uploads/formidable/Gu%c3%ada-de-Saltos-y-Ca%c3%addas-de-Agua-de-Puerto-RicoRes.pdf |access-date=18 September 2021 |year=2016 |archive-date=24 December 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161224104227/http://drna.pr.gov/wp-content/uploads/formidable/Gu%C3%ADa-de-Saltos-y-Ca%C3%ADdas-de-Agua-de-Puerto-RicoRes.pdf |url-status=live }}</ref>
* A portion of the '''[[Guilarte State Forest]]''' is also located within the boundaries of barrio Garzas.
