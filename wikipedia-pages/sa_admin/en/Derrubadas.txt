'''Derrubadas''' is a municipality of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]].

==Geography==
It is located at 27º15'53" South, 53º51'39" West, at an altitude of 485 meters above sea level. In 2020, its population was estimated at 2,761 people.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/derrubadas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Occupying an area of 365.44 square kilometers, it is bounded in the north by the [[Uruguay River]], where it is opposite [[Argentina]] and the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|17491|ha}} [[Turvo State Park]], created in 1947.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES do Turvo – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=PES do Turvo|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/597608|access-date=2016-11-11}}</ref>
The state park covers almost half of the municipality.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque do Turvo – Municipio de Derrubadas}}|language=pt|title=Parque do Turvo|publisher=Municipio de Derrubadas|url=http://www.derrubadas-rs.com.br/index.php/parque-do-turvo|access-date=2016-11-11|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161112080718/http://www.derrubadas-rs.com.br/index.php/parque-do-turvo|archive-date=2016-11-12|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The municipality would be partially flooded by the proposed [[Panambi Dam]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos}}|language=pt |title=Eletrobras apresenta Garabi-Panambi a prefeitos|publisher=Eletrobras CGTEE|date=7 April 2014
