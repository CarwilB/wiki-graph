'''Arequipa''' is a [[province]] in the [[Arequipa Region]], [[Peru]]. Its capital, [[Arequipa]], is Peru's second most populous city. It borders the provinces of [[Islay Province|Islay]], [[Camaná Province|Camaná]], and [[Caylloma Province|Caylloma]]. According to [[INEI]], in the year 2017 it had a population of 1,080,635 people.<ref name=poblacionestimada>{{Cite web |url = http://es.scribd.com/doc/111455706/Peru-Estimaciones-y-Proyecciones-de-poblacion-por-sexo-segun-departamento-provincia-y-distrito-2012-2015-INEI|title= Perú:Estimaciones y proyecciones de Población Por sexo según Departamento, Provincia y Distrito 2012-2015 - INEI (page 129)}}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is mainly inhabited by Mestizos of Spanish and Indigenous American ancestry. [[Spanish language|Spanish]], is the language which the majority of the population (85.67%) learnt to speak in childhood, 12.35% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua language]] and 1.64% using the [[Aymara language]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).
