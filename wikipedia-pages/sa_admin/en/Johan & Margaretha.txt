'''Johan & Margaretha''',{{efn|{{langx|srn|Kerkigron Magrita}}, {{langx|hns|Margiṯa}}, {{langx|jvn|Margita}}<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/_oso001198701_01/_oso001198701_01_0006.php|title=Verjavaanste toponiemen in Suriname en hun herkomst|trans-title=Javanese-influenced place names in Suriname and their origin|first=Hein|last=Vruggink|publisher=[[Digital Library for Dutch Literature]]|date=1987|lang=nl}}</ref>}} also simply known as '''Margaretha''' ({{langx|srn|Magrita}}), is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Commewijne District]].  Its population at the 2012 [[census]] was 756.<ref name="census"/> It is located along a peninsula northeast of [[Paramaribo]]. It is named after the [[coffee]] plantation Johan & Margaretha which was founded in 1745 by Johan Knöffel.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/j/johanenmargaretha|title=Plantage Johan en Margaretha|website=Suriname Plantages|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> It is safe to assume that his wife was called Margaretha. 

The resort has a clinic and a school.<ref name="planning"/> The [[nature reserve]] of [[Braamspunt]] is located in the Johan & Margaretha resort. The resort of Margaretha can only be reached by boat.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN 2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

==Frederiksdorp==
{{anchor|Frederikdorp}}Frederiksdorp ({{langx|srn|Pikin Knuffel}})<ref name="plantage">{{cite web|url=https://www.surinameplantages.com/archief/f/frederiksdorp|title=Plantage Frederiksdorp|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl|website=Suriname Plantages}}</ref> is a village and former [[coffee]] and [[Cocoa bean|cocoa]]<ref name="united"/> plantation<ref name="plantage"/> located next to Johan & Margareta. Both plantations were owned by Johan Frederik Knöffel.<ref name="plantage"/> 

In 2004, the plantation became a historical [[monument]].<ref name="united">{{cite news|url=https://unitednews.sr/frederiksdorp-herleving-van-een-lang-vergane-glorietijd/|title=FREDERIKSDORP; HERLEVING VAN EEN LANG VERGANE GLORIETIJD|website=United News.sr|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> Frederikdorp is located on the [[Commewijne River]] and has a ferry connection to [[Marienburg, Suriname|Mariënburg]]. Frederiksdorp covers an area of 300 [[hectare]]s, has beaches, and [[mangrove]] forests.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.frederiksdorp.com/nl/over-ons/|title=Over Ons|website=Frederiksdorp.com|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=2 December 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20221202151512/https://frederiksdorp.com/nl/over-ons/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
