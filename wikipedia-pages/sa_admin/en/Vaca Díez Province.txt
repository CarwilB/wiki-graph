'''Vaca Díez''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Beni Department]], [[Bolivia]]. The two largest settlements in Vaca Díez are [[Guayaramerín]] with a population of 35,764 in 2012, along with [[Riberalta]] with a population of 78,754. Vaca Díez measures 16,228&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (10,083 miles) in size.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.citypopulation.de/php/bolivia-beni.php?adm2id=0802|title=Vaca Díez (Province, Beni, Bolivia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location|website=www.citypopulation.de|access-date=2020-03-21}}</ref>
