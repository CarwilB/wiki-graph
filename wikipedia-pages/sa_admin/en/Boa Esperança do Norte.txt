<p>'''Boa Esperança do Norte''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]] in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West Region]] of [[Brazil]], the creation of the municipality was approved in March of 2000, however it was only recognized by the [[Supreme Federal Court]] in October 2023.<ref name="G1-2023-10-13">{{Cite web |title=Conheça Boa Esperança do Norte e saiba o que tem no novo município de MT |date=13 October 2023 |url=https://g1.globo.com/mt/mato-grosso/noticia/2023/10/13/conheca-boa-esperanca-do-norte-e-saiba-o-que-tem-no-novo-municipio-de-mt.ghtml |publisher=G1 |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=STF valida a criação de Boa Esperança do Norte (MT), o 5.569º município brasileiro |url=https://www.cartacapital.com.br/justica/stf-valida-a-criacao-de-boa-esperanca-do-norte-mt-o-5-569o-municipio-brasileiro/ |website=CartaCapital |access-date=27 October 2023 |language=pt-BR |date=16 October 2023}}</ref> It was established on 1 January 2025.<ref name="G1-2023-10-13"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gazetadigital.com.br/editorias/politica-de-mt/novo-municipio-do-estado-boa-esperanca-no-norte-enfrenta-dificuldades-em-seu-pirmeiro-ano/794765|title=Novo município do Estado, Boa Esperança no Norte enfrenta dificuldades em seu pirmeiro ano |date=5 January 2025 |publisher=Gazeta Digital |language=pt-BR  |access-date=10 January 2025}}</ref></p> 

Prior to its municipal status, Boa Esperança was a district of [[Sorriso]], however 80% of the land of the new municipality previously belonged to [[Nova Ubiratã]].<ref name="G1-2023-10-13" />
