'''Soledade''' is a municipality in [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The city is nicknamed the ''Cidade das Pedras Preciosas'' meaning the ''City of Precious Stones'' or ''Rare Gems''. Much of the [[amethyst]] sold in the world comes from the mines of Rio Grande do Sul near Soledade.  Most of the factories in Soledade process and sell these stones internationally.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 31,035.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/soledade/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Its other famous export apart from its precious stones, is [[Fábio Rochemback]] who played football for [[Grêmio]]. 

Another notable citizen was Joaquim Mauricio Cardoso, a lawyer, professor, and politician. He was the governor for the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], having a public school<ref>{{Cite web |title=ESCOLA NORMAL MAURÍCIO CARDOSO em Soledade, RS: (54) 3381... |url=https://eguias.net/empresa/escola-normal-mauricio-cardoso/soledade/rs/1209839 |access-date=2022-12-27 |website=eGuias.net |language=pt-br}}</ref> in Soledade founded after him.
