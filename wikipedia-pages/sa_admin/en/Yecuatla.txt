''' Yecuatla ''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the north zone in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 40&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 135.72 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|52|N|96|47|W|}}.

==Name==
The name comes from the language Náhuatl, Yec-uauh-tlan; that means "Place of three snakes".

==Geography==

The municipality of ''' Yecuatla ''' is bordered to the north-east by [[Colipa (municipality)|Colipa]], to the south by [[Chiconquiaco]], to the west by [[Misantla (municipality)|Misantla]] and to the north-west by [[Colipa (municipality)|Colipa]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30197a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Yecuatla |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-18}}</ref>

The climate in Yecuatla is warm-regular with an average temperature of 22.5°C, with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[bean]], [[green chile]], [[orange fruit]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

Every Augusts, a festival is held to celebrate Virgen de la Asunción, patron of the town and in December there is a festival held in honor of the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]].
