The '''San Carlos Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 106,760.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The city of [[San Carlos, Cojedes|San Carlos]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the San Carlos Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{Dead link|date=August 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
Father Capuchino Fray Pedro de Berja founded the city of San Carlos de Austria on April 27, 1678.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://sancarlos-cojedes.gov.ve/portal-alcaldias/ |title=Alcaldías Digitales<!-- Bot generated title --> |access-date=2008-03-14 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070606100556/http://sancarlos-cojedes.gov.ve/portal-alcaldias/ |archive-date=2007-06-06 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The San Carlos Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 97,860 (up from 85,402 in 2000).  This amounts to 32.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{Dead link|date=August 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|53.1|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{Dead link|date=August 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the San Carlos Municipality is José Jesús Betancourt Sanoja, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 52% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - San Carlos | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120206165103/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis=0&edo=8&mun=6&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-06}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 Directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-14 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; “San Carlos de Austria, Juan Angel Bravo, and Manuel Manrique.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
