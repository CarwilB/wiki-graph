'''Caxambu''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population in 2021 was estimated at 21,566.

Caxambu is renowned for its [[spa]] which has twelve sources of mineral sparkling water flowing 24 hours a day
and a [[cold-water geyser]].<ref>{{cite journal | last=Bonotto | first=Daniel Marcos | title=Hydrogeochemical study of spas groundwaters from southeast Brazil | journal=Journal of Geochemical Exploration | publisher=Elsevier BV | volume=169 | year=2016 | pages=60–72 | doi=10.1016/j.gexplo.2016.07.016 | hdl=11449/173240 | hdl-access=free }}</ref>
