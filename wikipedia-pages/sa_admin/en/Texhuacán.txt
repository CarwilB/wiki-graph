{{Short description|Municipality in Mexico}}
'''Texhuacán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the montane central zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about 205&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 32.90 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|37|N|97|02|W|}}.

==Geography==

The municipality of Texhuacán is delimited to the north by [[Los Reyes, Veracruz|Los Reyes]], to the east by [[Zongolica]] to the south by [[Mixtla de Altamirano]], to the west by [[Astacinga]] and [[Tlaquilpa]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30171a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Texhuacán |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=Spanish|accessdate=2009-06-16}}</ref>

It is watered by the river Talcoco and several permanent or temporary creeks, which are tributaries of the Silly, affluent Rio of the Papaloapan.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]] and [[coffee]].

==Celebrations==

The celebration in honor of San Juan Bautista, Patron of the town, takes place in Texhuacán in June, while the celebration in honor of Virgen de Guadalupe takes place in December.
