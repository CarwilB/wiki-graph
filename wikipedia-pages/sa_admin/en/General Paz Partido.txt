'''General Paz Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the northeast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 10,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1197|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Ranchos, Buenos Aires|Ranchos]], which is {{convert|110|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The partido is named in honour of Brigadier General [[José María Paz]], a veteran of the [[Argentine War of Independence]].
