'''Datem del Marañón''' is one of the eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Loreto Region]] of [[Peru]]. It was created on August 2, 2005, during the presidency of [[Alejandro Toledo]].

==Political division==
The province is divided into six districts.

* [[Andoas District|Andoas]] ([[Alianza Cristiana]])
* [[Barranca District, Datem del Marañón|Barranca]] ([[San Lorenzo, Loreto|San Lorenzo]])
* [[Cahuapanas District|Cahuapanas]] ([[Santa María de Cahuapanas]])
* [[Manseriche District|Manseriche]] ([[Sarameriza (Peru)|Saramiriza]])
* [[Morona District|Morona]] ([[Puerto Alegría, Peru|Puerto Alegría]])
* [[Pastaza District|Pastaza]] ([[Ullpayacu]])
