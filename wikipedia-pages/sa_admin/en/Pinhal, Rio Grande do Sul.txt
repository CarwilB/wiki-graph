'''Pinhal''' ({{coord|27|30|39|S|53|12|54|W}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. It is situated at 368 m above [[sea level]] and its estimated population in 2020 was 2,580.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/rs/pinhal/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It has an area of 72,6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
