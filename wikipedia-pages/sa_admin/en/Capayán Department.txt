'''Capayán''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] located in the south of [[Catamarca Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 6,358 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|4,284|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Chumbicha]].
