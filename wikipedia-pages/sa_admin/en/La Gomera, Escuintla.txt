'''La Gomera''' ({{IPA|es|la ɣoˈmeɾa}})  is a city and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla department|Escuintla]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|480.3|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 54,301 inhabitants.

==History==
La Gomera was founded in 1611 by Don Antonio Peraza de Ayala y Rojas, then [[Captaincy General of Guatemala|Captain General of Guatemala]], who was took up the name of Count of La Gomera. The settlement was established to segregate the indigenous and the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] population at [[Zapotitlán, Jutiapa|Zapotitlán]]. It was named as "Villa de la Gomera", and as per the municipal archives from 1740, the town had a population of 250 [[Mulattos]], six [[Mestizos]] and two Spaniards. As per 1770 records attributed to Archbishop Pedro Cortés Larraz, who visited the region, La Gomera had 49 families with 276 people, of which the one fourth was indigenous population, and largely spoke [[Spanish language]]. The region was dependent on [[agriculture]], and grew [[maize]], [[sugercane]], [[cotton]], apart from [[livestock]] rearing and [[salt]] production.<ref name="Hist"/>

As per government decree enacted on 17 August 1896, La Gomera became part of the department of Escuintla, and was established as a municipality on 16 June 1915. On 27 July 1913, Texcuaco was integrated with La Gomera.<ref name="Hist">{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de La Gomera|work=Deguate|trans-title=History of La Gomera municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/historia-del-municipio-de-la-gomera-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
La Gomera is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Escuintla Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|480.3|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=La Gomera (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/escuintla/0507__la_gomera/|access-date=24 May 2026}}</ref> It lies in the northern part of the department, about {{cvt|57|km}} from the departmental capital of Escuintla and {{cvt|112|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala city]]. It borders the municipalities of [[Santa Lucia Cotzumalguapa]] to the north,[[Puerto San José]] and [[Escuintla]] to the east, [[Nueva Concepción]] to the west, and Pacific Ocean to the south.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de La Gomera|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of La Gomera municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/escuintla/geografia-del-municipio-de-la-gomera-escuintla/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of {{cvt|35|m}} above sea level,<ref name="Geo"/> La Gomera has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|24.24|C}}, and receives about {{cvt|114.72|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/la-gomera|title=La Gomera|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=1 April 2025}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 54,301 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 27,949 males and 26,352 females. About 28.5% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 6.0% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (56.9%) was classified as urban, while 43.1% lived in rural areas. About 67.9% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (96.7%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (2.8%) forming a small minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 83.5%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (97.5%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
