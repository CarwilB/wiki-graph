'''La Concordia Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]]. It shares borders with the cantons of [[El Carmen Canton|El Carmen]], [[Chone Canton|Chone]], [[Pedernales Canton|Pedernales]], [[Puerto Quito Canton|Puerto Quito]], [[Quinindé Canton|Quinindé]], and [[Santo Domingo Canton (Ecuador)|Santo Domingo]], which are part of the provinces of [[Manabí Province|Manabí]], [[Pichincha Province|Pichincha]], [[Esmeraldas Province|Esmeraldas]], and [[Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas Province|Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas]], respectively.  Its capital is the town of [[La Concordia, Ecuador|La Concordia]]. Elections on whether to form part of one of those two provinces took place on February 5, 2012. The final resolution was to become part of [[Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas Province]], thus becoming the 2nd Canton of that province.
