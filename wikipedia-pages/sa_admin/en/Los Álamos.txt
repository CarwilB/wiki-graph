'''Los Álamos''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of cities in Chile|city]] in [[Arauco Province]], [[Biobío Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Los Álamos spans an area of {{convert|599.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 18,632 inhabitants (9,456 men and 9,176 women). Of these, 16,394 (88%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,238 (12%) in [[rural area]]s. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 10.4% (1,762 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

The commune includes the locality of [[Antihuala]].

=== Religion ===
The 2024 census reported that among people aged 15 or older in Los Álamos, 62.3% were [[evangelical Christians]], 20.7% did not practice any religion, and 14.4% were [[Catholic]]. None of the remaining religions reached 1%. As a result, Los Álamos is the commune in Chile with the highest percentage of evangelical Christians, accounting for 10,918 people.<ref name="2024census">{{cite web |url=https://censo2024.ine.gob.cl/estadisticas/ |title=Resultados |access-date=15 May 2026 |work=[[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] |language=es}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Los Álamos is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Lautaro Melita Vinett ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> Since 2013 and until the present the alcalde of Los Álamos is Jorge Fuentes Fetis

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Los Álamos is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Manuel Monsalve (PS) and Iván Norambuena ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 46th electoral district, together with [[Lota, Chile|Lota]], [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], [[Arauco, Chile|Arauco]], [[Curanilahue]], [[Cañete, Chile|Cañete]], [[Contulmo]] and [[Tirúa]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Victor Pérez Varela (UDI) and Mariano Ruiz-Esquide Jara ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).

== Culture ==
In the literary field, Los Álamos stands out for its poets. Among these, one can name René Briones Sandoval, Maribel Castro Vergara, Olga Ester Garrido, Marta Díaz Pereira, María Rocha, Manuel Castro, Mac Karo, who were anthologized in the magazine ''Chonchón'' n°39, published in September 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Chonchón n°39 Especial Los ÁLamos |url=https://www.calameo.com/read/005682847adf51021e421 |access-date=2022-08-16 |website=calameo.com}}</ref> In addition, During the years 2018 and 2019, the poets of Los Álamos participated in the I and II Latin American Meeting of Poets and Narrators in [[Lebu, Chile|Lebu]], events that included the presence of authors from the [[Arauco, Chile|Province of Arauco]], such as [[Lidia Mansilla Valenzuela]] and Alejandro Concha M., and from abroad, such as Jorge Canales and Amapola, from [[El Salvador]], [[Chary Gumeta]] and [[José Baroja]], from [[Mexico]], and Felicidad Batista, from [[Spain]], among other participants.<ref>{{Cite web |title=I Encuentro latinoamericano de poetas y narradores en Lebu |url=https://estirpepoesia.blogspot.com/2018/11/encuentro-latinoamericano-de-poetas-en.html |access-date=2022-08-16 |website=Poeta Alejandro Concha M. @estirpepoesia, Lota.}}</ref>
