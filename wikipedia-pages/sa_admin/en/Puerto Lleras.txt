'''Puerto Lleras''' is a town and municipality in the [[Meta Department]], [[Colombia]].

The town<ref>{{cite web |title=Puerto Lleras |url=https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/3.2703/-73.3737 |website=OpenStreetMap |accessdate=26 January 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Puerto Lleras |url=https://www.google.com/maps/@3.2687841,-73.3746524,4926m/data=!3m1!1e3 |website=Google Maps |publisher=Google |accessdate=26 January 2019}}</ref> is on the [[Ariari River]], and is served by [[Puerto Lleras Airport]].
