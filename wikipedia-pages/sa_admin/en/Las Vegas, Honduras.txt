'''Las Vegas''' is a [[Municipality (Honduras)|municipality]] in the department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]], Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|106.1|km2}} and had a population of 23,980 inhabitants as per the 2013 national census. It is situated near the shores of [[Lake Yojoa]]. It is one of the principal commercial centres of the Santa Bárbara department.

== History ==
The municipality of Las Vegas was created by Executive Decree No. 38-87 MGJ, signed on 8 September 1987 by president [[José Azcona del Hoyo]]. The decree was issued in response to a petition filed by the Patronato Pro-Mejoramiento Comunal de Las Vegas (Pro-Communal Improvement Board of Las Vegas) on 8 September 1986. The municipality was carved out of the territory previously belonging to the municipality of [[San Pedro Zacapa]], in the same department of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]].<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Las Vegas, Santa Bárbara|url=https://www.edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Las-Vegas.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=1 April 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-las-vegas-santa-barbara/|title=Department of Santa Barbara|work=Red Honduras|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Las Vegas is located in the department of Santa Bárbara, Honduras. It is bound by the municipalities of San Pedro Zacapa in the south, [[Concepción del Sur]] in the northwest, [[Santa Cruz, Honduras|Santa Cruz]] in the east, and the shores of [[Lake Yojoa]] in the west. It covers an area of {{cvt|106.1|km2}} and has a mean elevation of {{cvt|895|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Edu"/> The municipality is situated in a small valley surrounded by mountainous terrain. It has a temperate climate with maximum temperatures of {{cvt|28-33|C}} in the summer, and {{cvt|6-27|C}} in the winter.<ref name="Red"/>

== Demographics ==
At the time of the formation, the population of the municipality was recorded as 8,826 inhabitants.<ref name="Edu"/> As per the 2013 census, Las Vegas had a total population of 23,980 inhabitants, of whom 12,005 (50.1%) were male and 11,976 (49.9%) were female.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1627__las_vegas/|title=Las Vegas|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} The municipality is the most densely populated in the department, with a density of 226.02 inhabitants per km².<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}} Of the total population, 11,708 (48.8%) lived in urban and 12,273 (51.2%) in the rural areas.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=20}} By broad age group, 37.0% of the population were aged 0–14, 57.7% were aged 15–64, and 5.3% were aged 65 and over. The median age was 20.6 years and the mean age was 25.4 years. Among the population aged 15 and over (15,112 persons), the illiteracy rate of 15.9%, which is lower than the departmental average of 22.2%.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=19}}

The 2013 census recorded 6,447 private dwellings in the municipality, of which 5,349 were occupied.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=27}} Of the occupied dwellings, 4,305 (80.5%) were owner-occupied and 523 (9.8%) were rented.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=34}} Of the occupied private dwellings, 4,368 (81.7%) had access to water from the public distribution system, and 4,679 dwellings (87.5%) had access to electricity, of which 4,373 were connected to the public electricity system.<ref name="INE2013"/>{{rp|page=32}}

== Economy ==
Las Vegas is one of the principal commercial centres of Santa Bárbara department. The main economic activities of the municipality are [[agriculture]], [[livestock]] rearing raising, and commerce. A major source of employment is the [[El Mochito mine]], located close to the aldea of El Mochito o Mocho Arriba.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/>
