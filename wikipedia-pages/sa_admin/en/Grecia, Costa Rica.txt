== Geography ==
Grecia has an area of {{Convert|7.51|km2|abbr=on}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead}}</ref> and an elevation of {{Convert|999|m|abbr=on}}.<ref name="divadm" /> It is in the foothills of the [[Cordillera Central (Costa Rica)|Cordillera Central]] on the eastern edge of the [[Costa Rican Central Valley|Central Valley]]. The city, which was once named "the cleanest city in Latin America,"<ref name="SE">{{Cite web |title=Concejo Municipal de Grecia Sesión Extraordinaria N 121 |url=https://docplayer.es/147954949-Concejo-municipal-de-grecia-sesion-extraordinaria-n-121.html |language=es}}</ref> is {{Convert|20|km|abbr=on}} northwest of the provincial capital city of Alajuela, {{Convert|27|km|abbr=on}} from [[Juan Santamaría International Airport]], and {{Convert|45|km|abbr=on}} from the national capital city of [[San José, Costa Rica|San José]].

== History ==

=== Pre-Columbian Era ===
Grecia, and its neighboring districts, was previously inhabited by [[indigenous peoples]] belonging to the [[Western Huetar Kingdom]]. This presence occurred during the reign of [[King Garabito]].<ref name="IB"/>

=== 19th Century ===
Grecia was petitioned to be founded as a town as early as October 1828.<ref name="GC">{{Cite book |last=Nunez |first=Francisco |title=Grecia en su Centenario |publisher=Imprenta Nacional |year=1939 |location=San Jose, Costa Rica |pages=1 |language=Spanish |trans-title=Grecia in its centenary}}</ref>  The first group of settlers, who later became bosses to future settlers, found the unestablished land to be very fertile. Furthermore, the [[state government]] motivated these travelers to discover pathways up North by offering them incentives.<ref name="IB"/> These discoveries and rewards led to an increase of migration of settlers into the northwest of the Central Valley, looking for unclaimed land to work on.<ref name="IB"/><ref name="GC"/>

Eventually, the increase of settlements and construction of homes in the territory motivated residents to look for permission from the local government to officially establish the town. Their request, named File Number 21, was not approved until ten years later.<ref name="GC"/>

Legislation Number 69 was signed and enacted on April 27, 1838. The act allowed travelers to explore past the "other side of the Poas" and construct a settlement they would call Grecia.<ref name="GC"/>

==Economy==
Grecia is part of the tourist route of the western region of the Central Valley of Costa Rica, given the existence in the canton of waterfalls, protected areas, and the Catholic church of Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes, which is part of Costa Rica's historical-architectural heritage.

==Culture==
===Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes===
{{More citations needed|section|date=March 2024}}
[[File:Iglesia Nuestra Senora de las Mercedes.png|thumb|Interior of Church of Our Lady of Mercy, located in Grecia, Costa Rica.]]
Grecia is noted for its unique church, {{Ill|Church of Our Lady of Mercy (Grecia)|lt=Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes|es|Iglesia de Nuestra Señora de las Mercedes (Grecia)}}, made entirely of pre-fabricated steel plates painted red. The building was constructed in the 19th century to represent the practice of Catholic religion in Grecia and honor the [[Virgin Mary]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Municipalidad de Grecia |url=http://www.grecia.go.cr/ruta-categorias/22/iglesia-de-nuestra-senora-de-las-mercedes |access-date=2024-03-16 |website=www.grecia.go.cr}}</ref>

Initially approved to be designed as a doric style brick temple, the Church of Our Lady of Mercy is built with [[Neoclassicism|neoclassical]] influence due to Angel Miguel Velasquez, secretary of [[public works]] at the time who introduced the design to the district.<ref name="CR">{{Cite book |last=Sanou |first=Ofelia |title=Arquitectura e historia en Costa Rica: templos parroquiales en el Valle Central, Grecia, San Ramon, y Palmares (1860-1914) |date=2001 |publisher=Editorial de la Universidad de Costa Rica |isbn=9789977676432 |edition=1st |location=San Jose, Costa Rica |pages=57–58 |language=es |trans-title=Architecture and history in Costa Rica: parish temples in the Central Valley, Grecia, San Ramon, and Palmares (1860-1914)}}</ref>

There are several urban legends about this church. One recounts how the church was donated by some foreign country, and sent to [[Greece]] as a gift, but was wrongly shipped to Grecia, Costa Rica. Another legend states that the final destination of the church was the city of [[Punta Arenas]] in [[Chile]] but was disembarked, by mistake, in the port of [[Puntarenas]], Costa Rica, and later sent to the city of Grecia, where it was assembled.

However, records clearly show that the instruction, shipment, and construction of the church were a coordinated effort of Grecia's population, the Catholic Church, the Costa Rican government, and [[:es:Alejo E. Jiménez Bonnefil|Alejo E. Jiménez Bonnefil (1858–1922)]], a Costa Rican coffee producer and exporter who was in charge of commanding and importing the church from the manufacturer {{Lang|fr|Ateliers de la Société de Couvillet|italic=no}} in [[Belgium]], in the late 19th century.<ref name="CR"/>

== Parks and recreation ==

=== Grecia Central Park ===
[[File:Grecia Central Park.png|thumb|[[Grecia Central Park]] in Downtown Grecia]]
Located in front of Our Lady of Mercy Church, [[Grecia Central Park]] is one of the many parks in the district.<ref name="SE"/> The park has undergone numerous changes over the past few years, including the remodeling of its entire electrical system.<ref name="PB"/> The [[recreation]] ground is primarily used for walking. After numerous closures, however, the local government made an effort to promote the use of the space for its residents.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-08-14 |title=Municipalidad de Grecia reabre parque central {{!}} Periódico Mi Tierra |url=https://www.periodicomitierra.com/index.php/actualidad/municipalidad-de-grecia-reabre-parque-central |access-date=2024-03-15 |website=www.periodicomitierra.com |language=es}}</ref> The Vamos al Parque program, in collaboration with the [[University of Costa Rica]], developed events such as free art, education, and recreation activities for its residents.<ref>{{Cite web |title=La UCR llenará el Parque de Grecia con música, arte y recreación |url=https://www.ucr.ac.cr/noticias/2023/5/05/la-ucr-llenara-el-parque-de-grecia-con-musica-arte-y-recreacion/imprimir.html?empotrar=true&ruta=https://www.ucr.ac.cr/noticias/2023/5/05/la-ucr-llenara-el-parque-de-grecia-con-musica-arte-y-recreacion.html |access-date=2024-03-15 |website=www.ucr.ac.cr}}</ref>

== Government and politics ==
[[File:Francisco Murillo Quesada.jpg|thumb|Francisco Murillo Quesada, the Current Mayor of Grecia]]
The [[local government]] of the district of Grecia is that of the Grecia canton—Grecia is a district in the Grecia canton. The municipality serves every district in the canton and its main responsibility is to administer local services and satisfy the interests of its residents.<ref name="IB">{{Cite book |last=Departamento de Asistencia Tecnica |url= |title=Informacion basica de la municipalidad de Grecia |collaboration=Gerardo Rodriguez Sibaja |publisher=Instituto de Fomento y Asesoria Municipal |year=1990 |edition=1st |location=San Jose, Costa Rica |pages=17–19, 23 |language=Spanish |trans-title=Basic information of the municipality of Grecia}}</ref> In 2020, [[Francisco Murillo Quesada]], a [[lawyer]], became the [[mayor]] of the [[Grecia (canton)|Grecia canton]].<ref>''[https://www.ungl.or.cr/images/centro_de_informacion/documentacion_e_infomacion_general/Perfiles%20de%20Alcaldias%20e%20Intendencias%202020-2024.pdf Perfiles de Alcaldías e Intendencias 2020-2024] (PDF'') (in Spanish). UNGL. Retrieved 14 March 2024.</ref> This also made him the mayor of the district of Grecia.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde |url=http://www.grecia.go.cr/micrositio/articulo/209/alcalde |access-date=2024-03-13 |website=www.grecia.go.cr |language=es}}</ref> He succeeded [[Minor Molina]] in May 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde electo de Grecia ya salió del país sin haber tomado posesión |url=https://www.encuentromunicipal.com/index.php/actualidad/3346-alcalde-electo-de-grecia-ya-salio-del-pais-sin-haber-tomado-posesion |access-date=2024-03-13 |website=www.encuentromunicipal.com}}</ref><ref name="TS"/> The first and second vice mayors of Grecia are [[Nora Maria Suarez Cruz]] and [[Francisco Rodriguez Gutierrez]], respectively.<ref name="TS">[https://www.tse.go.cr/juris/relevantes/1494-E11-2020.pdf ''Numero 1494-E11-2020. Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones,''] (PDF) Republica de Costa Rica. Retrieved 12 March 2024.</ref>

The district of Grecia is one of the seven districts that belong to the local government of the Grecia canton.<ref name="EC"/> The municipality consists of a popularly elected [[council]], a [[trustee]] for each district, and a mayor.<ref name="IB"/> Currently, the trustee of the Grecia district is [[Luz María Jiménez Arrieta]]. She succeeded Roy José Rodríguez Corrales, who resigned in 2023.<ref>''[https://www.tse.go.cr/juris/municipales/5509-M-2023.html Numero 5509-M-2023 Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones]'' (PDF) Republica de Costa Rica.
