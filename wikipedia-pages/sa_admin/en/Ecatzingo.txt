'''Ecatzingo''' is one of 125 [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipalities]], in the [[Mexico (state)|State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is the town of Ecatzingo de Hidalgo.  The municipality covers an area of 54.71 km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 8247.<ref>{{cite web|title=Ecatzingo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15034a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=June 5, 2009 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070129040357/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15034a.htm |archive-date=January 29, 2007 }}</ref>
