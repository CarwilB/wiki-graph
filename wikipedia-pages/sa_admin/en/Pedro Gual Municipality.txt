'''Pedro Gual''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 22,579.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[Cúpira]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Pedro Gual Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{cite web |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 }}</ref> The municipality is named for 19th century Venezuelan President [[Pedro Gual Escandón]].

==Demographics==
The Pedro Gual Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 22,579 (up from 19,379 in 2000).  This amounts to 0.8% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|24.41|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Pedro Gual Municipality is Manuel Alvarez, elected on October 31, 2004, with 47% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Pedro Gual | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D13%26mun%3D21%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231712/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D13%26mun%3D21%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-23 | url-status=live }}</ref>  He replaced Lenis Landaeta shortly after the elections.<ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | accessdate=March 15, 2008 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | archivedate=October 2, 2006}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Cúpira and Machurucuto.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
