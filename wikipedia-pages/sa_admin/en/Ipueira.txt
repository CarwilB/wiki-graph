'''Ipueira''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the interior of the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Norte]] in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast region]] of [[Brazil]], in the region of [[Seridó]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |date=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |accessdate=December 17, }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, to the southwest of [[Natal, Rio Grande do Norte|Natal]], the state capital, and has an area of 127&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and is located at the midway point between [[Caicó]] (in [[Rio Grande do Norte]]) and [[Patos, Brazil|Patos]] (in [[Paraíba|Paraiba]]). It has a population (as estimated in 2020 by [[IBGE]]) of 2,253 people, making it the [[:pt:Lista de municípios do Rio Grande do Norte por população|third least populous municipal in the state]].

Ipueira was separated from the municipality [[São João do Sabugi]] in the 1960s. The name is formed from combining the words ''iapo'' (meaning [[:pt:Igapó|igapó]]) with the Portuguese suffix ''-eira''. It has a [[Human Development Index|human development index]] of 0.679 (2010), the [[:pt:Lista de municípios do Rio Grande do Norte por IDH-M|eighth highest in Rio Grande do Norte]], and considered average by the [[United Nations Development Programme]].
