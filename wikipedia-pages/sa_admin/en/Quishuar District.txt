'''Quishuar District''' is one of sixteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Tayacaja Province|Tayacaja]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Its [[Quechua languages|Quechua]] name ''kiswar'' is the name of the tree ''[[Buddleja incana]]''.

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (87.40%) learnt to speak in childhood, 12.60% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>

== History ==
The Quishuar district was created on March 6, 1957, by law No.12816, during the government of President Manuel Prado Ugarteche. <ref>{{Cite web| title=Creando el Distrito de Quishuar en la Provincia de Tayacaja | language=es | trans-title=Creating the District of Quishuar in the Province of Tayacaja | url=https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/12816.pdf | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190419185446/http://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/12816.pdf | archive-date=2019-04-19}}</ref>
