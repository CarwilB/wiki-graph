'''Villa González''' is a [[Municipalities of the Dominican Republic|municipality]] (''[[municipio]]'') of the [[Santiago Province (Dominican Republic)|Santiago province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]]. Within the municipality there are two [[municipal district]]s (''distritos municipal''): [[El Limón, Santiago, Dominican Republic|El Limón]] and [[Palmar Arriba]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman&task=cat_view&gid=113 |title=Listado de Codigos de Provincias, Municipio y Distritos Municipales, Actualizada a Junio 20 del 2006 |author=Oficina Nacional de Estadistica, Departamento de Cartografia, Division de Limites y Linderos |language=es |access-date=2007-01-24 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070803184926/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?option=com_docman |archive-date=August 3, 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The town is located {{convert|20|mi|km}} from [[Santiago de los Caballeros]].
