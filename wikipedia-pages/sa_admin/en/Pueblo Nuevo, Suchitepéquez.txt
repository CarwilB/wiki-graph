Pueblo Nuevo is a municipality in the department of [[Suchitepéquez Department|Suchitepéquez]],<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/inauguran-mejoras-en-tramo-carretero-de-pueblo-nuevo-suchitepequez/|title=Inauguran mejoras en tramo carretero de Pueblo Nuevo, Suchitepéquez|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=24 March 2021|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> in the south-west region of the Republic of [[Guatemala]].

== Geography ==
Pueblo Nuevo is located in the department of Suchitepéquez at a distance of 14 km from the departmental capital [[Mazatenango, Suchitepéquez|Mazatenango]].
