'''Cravo Norte''' is a town and municipality in the [[Arauca Department]], [[Colombia]]. It is thought to have been founded in 1538 by the [[Jesuit]] missionary [[Joseph Gumilla]]. The first census was conducted in May 1797 and counted a population of 205. <ref>[http://www.cravonorte-arauca.gov.co/nuestromunicipio.shtml?apc=m1I1--&s=m&m=I&prev=]{{dead link|date=December 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Climate==
Cravo Norte has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with little rainfall from December to March and heavy to very heavy rainfall from April to November.
