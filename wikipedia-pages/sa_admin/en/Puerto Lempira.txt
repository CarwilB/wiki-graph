'''Puerto Lempira''' or '''Auhya Yari''' is the Miskito capital of the [[Gracias a Dios department]] in northeastern [[Honduras]], located on the shores of the [[Caratasca Lagoon]].  Though it does not have paved roads, it is the largest town in the [[Gracias a Dios Department]].<ref>Chandler, Gary & Prado, Liza. ''Honduras & the Bay Islands'' (2007) p. 297 ({{ISBN|978-1740591508}})</ref>

The town is named for the 16th century leader of the indigenous [[Lenca people|Lenca]] peoples, [[Lempira (Lenca)|Lempira]], who directed an ultimately unsuccessful resistance against the Spanish ''[[conquistador]]'' forces in the 1530s.

Puerto Lempira became the departmental capital in 1975, prior to which it was [[Brus Laguna]].  In the 1980s, the town became a centre for [[CIA]] operations against the [[Sandinista National Liberation Front|Sandinistas]].<ref>Gill, Nicholas.  ''[[Frommer's]] Honduras'' (2009) p.260 ({{ISBN|978-0470159439}})</ref>

Its population is 22,860 (2023 calculation).<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/cities/ Citypopulation.de] Population of cities in Honduras</ref>

Puerto Lempira is [[sister city]] to [[Plattsburgh, New York]], US, and has been since 2011.

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Puerto Lempira municipality had a population of 47,528. Of these, 82.41% were Indigenous (81.98% Miskito), 14.86% Mestizo, 1.34% Afro-Honduran or Black, 1.27% White and 0.13% others.

By 2018, the municipality had a population of 54,554 inhabitants, composed of 26,440 men and 28,115 women. Of the total population, 34,140 people lived in rural areas, while 20,414 resided in urban areas.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>

==Culture==
[[Miskito people|Miskito]] culture is dominant, though not exclusive.

==Language==
Miskito language is dominant and official in the region, followed by [[Mayangna language|Mayangna]] and [[Spanish language|Spanish]].

==Transportation==

The city is served by [[Puerto Lempira Airport]], which operates several flights a week on a regular schedule.

==Climate==
Like most of the Caribbean side of Central America, Puerto Lempira has a warm, humid and rainy [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af''). Its driest month of March, when the relentlessly wet [[trade wind]]s are weakest, is only marginally rainy enough to avoid a [[tropical monsoon climate|tropical monsoon]] classification; however, between June and December monthly rainfall consistently averages above or near {{convert|300|mm|in|0|disp=or}}.
