'''Pilcomayo''' is a [[departments of Argentina|department]] in the [[Argentina|Argentine]] [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Formosa Province|Formosa]]. Its capital is [[Clorinda, Argentina|Clorinda]], the second most populous city in the province. Pilcomayo has a surface of 5,342 km<sup>2</sup> and a population of 78,114 according to the {{census-ar|2001}}. Other communities in Pilcomayo include [[Laguna Blanca, Formosa|Laguna Blanca]], [[Laguna Naick Neck]], [[Riacho He-Hé]] and [[Siete Palmas]]. The [[Río Pilcomayo National Park|Pilcomayo River National Park]] (''Parque Nacional Río Pilcomayo'') is about 45 kilometers west of Clorinda, crossed by the [[Pilcomayo River]].
