''' La Pintada''' ({{IPA|es|la pinˈtaða}}) is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Coclé Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 23,202.<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 10, 2009}}</ref> The district covers a total area of 1,030&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the town of [[La Pintada, Panamá|La Pintada]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
La Pintada District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[La Pintada, Panama|La Pintada]] (capital)
*[[El Harino]]
*[[El Potrero, Coclé|El Potrero]]
*[[Llano Grande, Coclé|Llano Grande]]
*[[Piedras Gordas]]
*[[Las Lomas, Coclé|Las Lomas]]
*[[Llano Norte, Coclé|Llano Norte]]
