'''Monte Carmelo''' is one of the 20 municipalities of the state of [[Trujillo (state)|Trujillo]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality occupies an area of 386&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> with a population of 16,174 inhabitants according to the 2011 census.

==Parishes==
The municipality consists of the following three parishes:

*Buena Vista
*Monte Carmelo
*Santa María del Horcón
