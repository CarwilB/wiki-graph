'''San Roque González de Santa Cruz''' is a ''[[district|distrito]]'' in the [[Paraguarí Department]] of [[Paraguay]], it is located 97&nbsp;km from [[Asunción]], the capital of the country.

The [[Route 1 (Paraguay)|National Route 1]], crosses through this city.
