'''Choró''' is a [[municipality]] located in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Ceara]]. It is at an altitude of 243m, located on {{Coord|04|50|36|S|39|08|28|W|}}. Its population was 13,565 (2020). The municipality has a surface area of approximately 792,7&nbsp;km².
