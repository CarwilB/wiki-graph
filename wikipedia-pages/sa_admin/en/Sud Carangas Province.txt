'''Sud Carangas''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the central parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]].

==Location==
Sud Carangas province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 18° 38' and 19° 09' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 37' and 67° 34' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders [[Saucarí Province]] in the North, [[Carangas Province]] in the Northwest, [[Litoral Province (Bolivia)|Litoral Province]] in the West, [[Ladislao Cabrera Province]] in the South, [[Sebastián Pagador Province]] in the Southeast, [[Eduardo Avaroa Province]] in the East, and [[Poopó Province]] in the Northeast. The province borders [[Poopó Lake]] in the East.

The province extends over 60&nbsp;km from North to South, and 100&nbsp;km from East to West.

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Aymara language|Aymara]], spoken by 96.5%, while 78.1% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 46.0% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992).

The population increased from 4,028 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 6,136 (2001 census), an increase of 52%. - 41.1% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).

99.9% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 98.7% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

77.9% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 4.1% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 18.0% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

74.8% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 18.9% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
