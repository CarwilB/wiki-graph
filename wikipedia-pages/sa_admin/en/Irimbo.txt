'''Irimbo''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] located in the northeastern part of the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. The municipality has an area of 125.54 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan Michoacán] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110517162648/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_Michoacan |date=17 May 2011 }} ''Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México''. Retrieved on 12 July 2018</ref> 

The seat of the municipality is also named [[Irimbo (town)|Irimbo]].

As of 2010, it has a population of 14,766.
