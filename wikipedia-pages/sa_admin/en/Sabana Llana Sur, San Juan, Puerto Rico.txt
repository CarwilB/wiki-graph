==Location==
Sabana Llana Sur is located east of [[El Cinco, San Juan, Puerto Rico|El Cinco]] [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]], north of the municipality of [[Trujillo Alto]] and west of the municipality of [[Carolina, Puerto Rico|Carolina]]. It is bordered by the [[Sabana Llana Norte, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Sabana Llana Norte]] and [[Oriente, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Oriente]] barrios to the north.
