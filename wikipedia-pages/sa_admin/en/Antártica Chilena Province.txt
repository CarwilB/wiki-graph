'''Antártica Chilena Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia Antártica Chilena}}) is the southernmost of the four [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] in [[Chile]]'s southernmost [[Regions of Chile|region]], [[Magallanes and Antártica Chilena Region]] (XII). The capital is [[Puerto Williams]]. The province comprises the extreme southern part of [[Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego]] (south of the [[Cordillera Darwin]]), the islands south and west of Isla Grande ([[Diego Ramírez Islands]] and its National Park, [[Diego Ramirez Islands & Drake Passage National Park]]), and Chile's claims in [[Antarctica]].<ref>[http://www.gobernacionantartica.gov.cl/geografia.html Gobernación Provincia Antártica Chilena: Información Geográfica] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120402142859/http://www.gobernacionantartica.gov.cl/geografia.html |date=April 2, 2012 }} (in Spanish)</ref>  The province is [[Administrative divisions of Chile|administratively divided]] into two [[Communes of Chile|communes]] (''comunas''):  [[Cabo de Hornos, Chile|Cabo de Hornos]], located at the southern tip of South America, and [[Antártica]], a wedge-shaped [[Territorial claims in Antarctica|claim of Antarctica]], which is not internationally recognized. Its total area of {{convert|1,265,853.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> makes it almost twice as large as all other provinces of Chile combined.

==Communes==
# [[Cabo de Hornos, Chile|Commune of Cabo de Hornos]] (until 2001 called Navarino, capital Puerto Williams), area {{convert|14146|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, population as of census 2002: 2,262, of which 1,952 are in the capital.<ref>[http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-25031.html Gobierno de Chile: SUBDERE - Cabo de Hornos<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070220183330/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-25031.html |date=February 20, 2007 }}</ref> As a commune, Cabo de Hornos is a third-level administrative division of Chile administered by a municipal council, headed by an alcalde who is directly elected every four years. The 2016-2020 alcalde is Patricio Fernández (PDC).The communal council has the following members:
#:Daniel Fernando Valdebenito Contreras (PS) Ángela Barría Barrientos (RN) Juan Velásquez (PS) Carolina Guenel González (DC) Francis Delgado Ibaceta (RN) Paola Speake Ojeda (DC)
# [[Antártica (Commune)|Commune of Antártica]], area {{convert|1250000|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, population as of census 2002: 130. This territory as a whole is not recognized internationally. (''See'' [[Antarctic territorial claims]].)

The Municipality of Cabo de Hornos governs both Antártica and Cabo de Hornos from Cabo de Hornos, making it the only Chilean municipality to currently administer more than one commune.

==Demography==
According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), it had a population of 2,392 inhabitants (1,518 men and 874 women), giving it a population density of {{convert|0.0019|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.  Of these, 1,952 (81.6%) lived in urban areas and 440 (18.4%) in rural areas.  The province is the largest in Chile, yet it is also the least and most sparsely populated province in the country.  Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population grew by 23% (447 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

== Territory recognition ==
[[File:182 - Punta Arenas - Revendications territoriales chiliennes - Janvier 2010.JPG|thumb|Antarctica claim by Chile, as seen on a monument in [[Punta Arenas]]]]

The South American portion of the territory is internationally recognized as part of Chile. However, the Commune of [[Antártica]], which encompasses most of the area of the province, is disputed.  It forms the Chilean claim to [[Antarctica]], stretching south of the [[60th parallel south|60th parallel]] to the [[South Pole]]. This region of Antarctica claimed by Chile overlaps with [[Territorial claims in Antarctica|land claims]] by [[Argentina]] and the [[United Kingdom]].

The Commune of Antártica is practically uninhabited except for research stations of various countries, although Chile maintains a permanent civilian population at [[Villa Las Estrellas]] (near [[Base Presidente Eduardo Frei Montalva|Frei Montalva Station]]), including women and children, a small school, and even a bank, in order to support its territorial claim of the area. The official population for the area as of the census 2002 is 130 (115 male, 15 female). This does not include the staff of non-Chilean bases in the area.<ref>[http://foresight.stanford.edu:3455/SouthPole/736 The South Pole: Eduardo Frei Montalva Station (Chile)<!-- Bot generated title -->] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111002032024/http://foresight.stanford.edu:3455/SouthPole/736 |date=October 2, 2011 }}</ref>

According to the Chilean argument, its claim to Antártica Chilena is as old as Chile itself. The [[Tordesillas Line]] established a west–east division of colonial territories between Portugal and Spain.<ref>{{Cite web |title=The Treaty of Tordesillas |url=https://www.thoughtco.com/the-treaty-of-tordesillas-4090126 |access-date=2023-01-01 |website=ThoughtCo |language=en}}</ref> On the first organization of the new conquered territories, [[Carlos V, Holy Roman Emperor|Charles V]], ruler of the Spanish Empire assigned to [[Pedro Sánchez de la Hoz|Pedro Sancho de la Hoz]] control over Terra Australis, which included the Southern part of South America, Tierra del Fuego Archipelago, and all southern undiscovered territories (Terra Australis Ignota). This {{Lang|es|gobernación}} eventually merged with other territories in the [[Captaincy General]] of the Kingdom of Chile.

==Prehistory==
Early settlement by prehistoric man is known to have occurred from [[archaeological]] recovery within this Province. Yaghans established many settlements on the islands of the present day province of the Antártica espana Province; for example, there is a significant early [[Yaghan people|Yaghan]] [[archaeological]] site at [[Wulaia Bay]], which C. Michael Hogan terms the ''Bahia Wulaia Dome Middens''.<ref>C. Michael Hogan (2008) [http://www.megalithic.co.uk/article.php?sid=18795 ''Bahia Wulaia Dome Middens'', ''Megalithic Portal'', ed. Andy Burnham]</ref>

==History==
Puerto Williams and Porvenir were established in the late 19th century, mainly by [[Western Europe]]an immigrants (esp. from [[British Isles]], [[Scandinavia]] and the former [[Yugoslavia]]) and navigators in cruise ships and fish boats. The Chilean government transplanted Chilean settlers from the central part of the country in the 20th century to increase the province's sparse population.
