'''San Mateo District''' is one of thirty-two [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Huarochirí Province]], located in the [[Department of Lima]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It was one of eleven districts that formed the Huarochirí Province after it was created by decree on August 4, 1821, during the [[Protectorate of San Martín]].
