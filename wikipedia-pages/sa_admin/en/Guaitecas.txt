'''Guaitecas''' is [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in [[Guaitecas Archipelago]] which are part of [[Aysén Province]] and [[Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region|Region]]. The administrative centre is [[Melinka]], the only port and town in the wider [[Chonos Archipelago]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Guaitecas spans an area of {{convert|787|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 1,539 inhabitants (913 men and 626 women). Of these, 1,411 (91.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 128 (8.3%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 20% (256 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> Much of the population of Guaitecas is of [[Chiloé Archipelago|Chilote]]-[[Huilliche]] background.<ref name=Gonz>{{cite conference |url=https://www.aacademica.org/vi.congreso.chileno.de.antropologia/160.pdf |title=Prácticas Económicas en las Costas Insulares de Aisén. ==Administration==
As a commune, Guaitecas is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] (Mayor) who is directly elected every four years. In December 2018 Cristián Alvarado Oyarzo ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) who was then the mayor was [[preventive detention|preventively detained]] as part of an investigation where he was accused of a [[fraud]] against the state ({{langx|es|fraude al fisco}}) for at least 340 million [[Chilean pesos|pesos]].<ref>[https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/nacional/region-de-aysen/2018/12/19/alcalde-de-guaitecas-queda-en-prision-preventiva-tras-ser-formalizado-por-millonario-fraude-al-fisco.shtml Alcalde de Guaitecas queda en prisión preventiva tras ser formalizado por millonario fraude al fisco]</ref> This led to his deposition as mayor and his replacement by municipal councillor Marcos Silva Miranda ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]), who afterwards won the [[2021 Chilean municipal elections|2021 municipal elections]], being ratified as mayor.{{citation needed|date=December 2021}} In December 2021 Cristián Alvarado was condemned to 10 years of prison and two of his collaborators to seven years of prison each.<ref>{{Cite news|title=Condenan a exalcalde de Guaitecas a 10 años de cárcel por malversación de caudales públicos|url=https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/nacional/region-de-aysen/2021/12/21/exalcalde-de-guaitecas-cristian-alvarado-condenado-a-10-anos-de-carcel.shtml|last=Hermosilla|first=Ignacio|date=2021-12-21|access-date=2021-12-29|work=[[Radio Bío-Bío]]|language=Spanish}}</ref>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Guaitecas is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Alinco Bustos (Ind-PPD), Miguel Ángel Calisto Águila (PDC) and Aracely Leuquén Uribe (RN), as part of the 27th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Aysén del General Carlos Ibáñez del Campo Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Ximena Órdenes (PPD) and David Sandoval (UDI),  as part of the 14th senatorial constituency, identical to the 27th district.
