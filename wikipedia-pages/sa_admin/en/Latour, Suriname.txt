'''Latour''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Paramaribo District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 29,526.<ref name="census"/> Latour is a low income neighbourhood with large unemployment.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vakantiearena.nl/suriname/paramaribo/paramaribo-zuid|title=Paramaribo-Zuid|access-date=29 May 2020|website=Vakantie Arena|language=nl}}</ref> Latour is the most populous resort of Paramaribo.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Structuur Analyse|access-date=29 May 2020|language=nl|website=Planning Office Suriname}}</ref>

One of the most active NGOs in Latour is Stibula,<ref>{{Cite web|date=2021-03-06|title=Stibula werkt keihard aan ontspanning jongeren|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2021/03/06/stibula-werkt-keihard-aan-ontspanning-jongeren/|access-date=2021-05-07|website=Dagblad Suriname|language=nl}}</ref> which works to provide development to the area's youth.
