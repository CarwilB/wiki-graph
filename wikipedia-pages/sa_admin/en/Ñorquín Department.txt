'''Ñorquín''' is a department located in the north of [[Neuquén Province]], [[Argentina]].

==Geography==
The Department limits with [[Minas Department, Neuquén|Minas Department]] at the Northwest, [[Chos Malal Department]] at the northeast, [[Pehuenches Department]] at east, [[Loncopué Department]] at south and [[Chile]] at east.
