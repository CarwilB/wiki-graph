'''Coacoatzintla''' in a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the mountainous central zone of the state, about 11.5&nbsp;km from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 43.9 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|39|N|96|56|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Coacoatzintla is delimited to the north by [[Tonayan]], to the north-east by [[Miahuatlán (Veracruz)|Miahuatlán]], to the east by [[Naolinco de Victoria|Naolinco]], to the south-east by [[Jilotepec, Veracruz|Jilotepec]], to the south by [[Banderilla, Veracruz|Banderilla]] and to the west by [[Tlacolulan]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30036a.htm|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Coacoatzintla|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070307113947/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30036a.htm|archive-date=2007-03-07|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The climate in Coacoatzintla is wet and cold with rains all year round.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 11,018 inhabitants in 20 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSCOACOATZINTLA>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Coacoatzintla.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Coacoatzintla CENSUS]</ref> More than 70% resides in the municipal seat; other localities include Los Planes (616 hab.), Pueblo Viejo (325 hab.) and Tlachinola (265 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSCOACOATZINTLA/>

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]] and [[potatoes]].

==Culture==
The celebration in honor to Santiago Apostol, patron of the town, occurs in July.{{cn|date=September 2023}}
