== Geography ==
Barina barrio is located in the southernmost part of the municipality of Yauco, immediately south of [[Yauco barrio-pueblo|Yauco Pueblo]] (downtown Yauco) and the barrios of [[Susúa Baja, Yauco, Puerto Rico|Susúa Baja]] and [[Jácana, Yauco, Puerto Rico|Jácana]]. It has a small southern coast facing the Caribbean Sea. The [[Yauco River]] crosses it from north to south, forming a flat valley that is used for agriculture. The rest of the barrio is hilly and forms part of the Southern Karst region of Puerto Rico.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Geographic Names Information System |url=https://edits.nationalmap.gov/apps/gaz-domestic/public/summary/1612625 |access-date=2022-11-30 |website=edits.nationalmap.gov}}</ref>

== Landmarks and attractions ==

The [[Guánica State Forest]] is the largest and one of the best-preserved [[Tropical and subtropical dry broadleaf forests|dry forests]] in Puerto Rico and the Caribbean.<ref>Ewel, J.L. & J.L. Whitmore. 1973. The ecological life zones of Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. USDA For. Serv. Res. Pap. ITF-18.</ref>

The Guánica State Forest is also home to several beaches. Atolladora Beach, also known as Yauco Beach, in the Ballena Bay is the largest beach of the municipality. It is located in the Guánica State Forest bordering Ballena Beach (in the municipality of [[Guánica, Puerto Rico|Guánica]]) and Tamarindo Beach (in the municipality of [[Guayanilla, Puerto Rico|Guayanilla]]).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Playa Atolladora, Yauco |url=http://www.callejeandopr.com/3/post/2020/03/playa-atolladora-yauco.html |access-date=2022-11-30 |website=www.callejeandopr.com |language=en}}</ref> It can be accessed by road PR-333.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bahía de la Ballena |url=https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/profile/bahia-de-la-ballena/8843 |access-date=2022-11-30 |website=Discover Puerto Rico |language=en}}</ref>
