'''Villarino Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the south of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 26,500 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|11400|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Médanos, Buenos Aires|Médanos]], which is located {{convert|735|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]] and that is known for its onion and premium wine production.
