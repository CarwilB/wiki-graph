'''Cheto''' is a district of the [[Chachapoyas Province]] in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]] in [[Peru]]. Its seat is Cheto located at an altitude of 2,500 m.s.n.m..

During the 2005 Peru census the district had 686 inhabitants.
