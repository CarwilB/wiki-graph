'''Perches''' ({{IPA|fr|pɛʁʃ|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-WikiLucas00-perches.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Pèch}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Fort-Liberté Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It had 11,556 inhabitants as of 2015.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref>

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of two communal sections, namely:
* [[Haut des Perches]], rural
* [[Bas des Perches]], urban and rural, containing the town of Perches
