'''Astolfo Dutra''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} is estimated to be 14,270 people living in an elevation of 260 meters.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/astolfo-dutra/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The area of the municipality is {{cvt|159.139|km2}}. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of [[Ubá]].
