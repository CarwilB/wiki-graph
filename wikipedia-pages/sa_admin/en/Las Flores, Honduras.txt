'''Las Flores''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Lempira department|Lempira]].

Las Flores is one of the municipalities of the [[Lempira Department|Lempira department]]. It is located {{convert|17|km|mi|abbr=off}} from [[Gracias]] on the paved road that leads to [[Santa Rosa de Copán]]. The town is {{convert|200|m|ft|abbr=off}} away from the paved road.

== History ==
The very first settlers were from [[La Paz Department (Honduras)|La Paz department]]. It was first called "Las Flores de Santa Bárbara", honoring the image of the saint of that place. It was granted the category of municipality on 1 January 1869.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

== Geography ==
This municipality is in a valley, near to a river. The vegetation corresponds to dry sub-tropical forests. The weather is hot since it is in a valley. There are some hills around it but they are not so high or steep.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

== Boundaries ==
Its boundaries are: 
* North : [[Lepaera]] municipality .
* South : [[Gracias]] municipality.
* East  : [[Gracias]] municipality .
* West  : [[Talgua]] municipality and [[Copán]] department.

== Resources ==
{{Unreferenced section|date=January 2023}}
The most important economical activity is commerce due to its location near to [[Gracias]] city and [[Santa Rosa de Copán]] city. On both sides of the road are several restaurants, stores, and car workshops. Cattle and Milk products are for local consumption. Another popular activity is to collect gravel, sand and construction materials from the two nearby rivers. Most of this material is required by [[Gracias]] city. Recently, the material has been used in the highway extension between Gracias, Lempira, and La Esperanza, Intibucá. Most of the people that live in the town only make their school in there. After that most of the people move to the nearest city, Santa Rosa de Copan, where education is more efficient. Because of the peace that the inhabitants experience it is very normal for them to gather at the central park or on streets very late at night.

== Population ==
In this case 90% of the people are cross-breed of native Indians and Spanish settlers.
*'''Population''': the figure for 2001 was 7,509. And for 2015 it's estimated at 9,838.
*'''Villages''': 10
*'''Settlements''': 40

===Demographics===
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Las Flores municipality had a population of 9,512. Of these, 97.43% were [[Mestizo]], 1.45% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.83% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.27% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.01% others.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ |title=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea |access-date=2024-12-22 |archive-date=2021-06-16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210616083136/https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ |url-status=live }}</ref>
