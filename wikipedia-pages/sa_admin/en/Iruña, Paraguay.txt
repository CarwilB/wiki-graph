'''Iruña''' is a district located in the [[Alto Paraná Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. Previously it was part of the district of [[Ñacunday]], and it was created as a district in 1993. It is located about {{convert|112|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from the departmental capital [[Ciudad del Este]] and {{convert|379|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Asunción]]. The commercial activity of the area is agriculture.
