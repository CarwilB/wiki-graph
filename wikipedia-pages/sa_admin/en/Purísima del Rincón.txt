'''Purísima del Rincón''' is a Mexican [[Municipalities of Guanajuato|municipality]] located in the state of [[Guanajuato]], founded on January 1, 1603. Its municipal seat is the town of Purísima de Bustos.

The municipality has an area of 288.44 square kilometres (0.95% of the surface of the state), and is bordered to the north and east by [[San Francisco del Rincón]], to the south by [[Manuel Doblado, Guanajuato|Manuel Doblado]], and to the west by the state of [[Jalisco]]. The municipality had 68,795 inhabitants according to the 2010 census.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=2007-03-22 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archivedate=2013-04-06 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070609120601/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |archive-date=2007-06-09 |accessdate=2007-03-22 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México}}</ref>

Purísima del Rincón is named after the [[Immaculate Conception]]. In 1954, Purísima de Bustos was named after [[Hermenegildo Bustos]], an artist born in the city.

The municipal president of Purísima del Rincón and its many smaller outlying communities is Tomás Torres Montañez.<ref>[http://purisimadelrincon.mx/Purisima/h-ayuntamiento Información General] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140222040032/http://purisimadelrincon.mx/Purisima/h-ayuntamiento |date=2014-02-22 }} Detailed information about the municipal president.</ref>
