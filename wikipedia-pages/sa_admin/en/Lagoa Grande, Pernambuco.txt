'''Lagoa Grande''' is a city in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Pernambuco]], 665&nbsp;km away from the state's capital, [[Recife]]. The population in 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 24,951 inhabitants, and the total area is 1844.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

== Economy ==
The main economic activities in '''Lagoa Grande''' are based on commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pig]]s, [[donkey]]s;  and plantations of [[grape]]s (more than 32400 tons), [[tomato]]es, [[onion]]s, [[apple guava|guava]], and [[mango]]es.{{cn|date=October 2023}}
