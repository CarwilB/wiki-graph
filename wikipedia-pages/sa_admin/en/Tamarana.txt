'''Tamarana''' is a small [[municipality]] in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]]. This small town's main activities are crop cultivation, animal husbandry and tourism.<ref>{{Cite web |date=August 2023 |title=CADERNO ESTATÍSTICO MUNICÍPIO DE TAMARANA |url=http://www.ipardes.gov.br/cadernos/MontaCadPdf1.php?Municipio=86125&btOk=ok |access-date=2023-08-08 |website=IPARDES |language=pt-br}}</ref> The current mayor is Luzia Suzukawa.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2021-01-01 |title=Prefeita e vereadores de Tamarana tomam posse; veja lista de eleitos |url=https://g1.globo.com/pr/norte-noroeste/noticia/2021/01/01/prefeita-e-vereadores-de-tamarana-tomam-posse-veja-lista-de-eleitos.ghtml |access-date=2023-08-08 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref> Edison Siena was the first mayor of the municipality, in 1996.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Okamura |first=Lucilia |date=1996-12-06 |title=Prefeito e vereadores são diplomados |language=pt-br |work=Folha de Londrina |url=https://www.folhadelondrina.com.br/politica/prefeito-e-vereadores-sao-diplomados-2324.html?d=1 |access-date=2023-08-08}}</ref> The second mayor was Paulo Mitio Nakaoka, succeeded by Edison Siena's son Roberto Siena, then Suzukawa. The nature in Tamarana is notable, with many rivers and tourist spots, including Estância Tamarana. It is also home to the Apucaraninha [[Indigenous territory (Brazil)|Indian Reservation]]. Tamarana is near [[Londrina]], [[Ortigueira, Paraná|Ortigueira]] and other municipalities.
