'''Anse-à-Pitres''' ({{IPA|fr|ɑ̃s a pitʁ}}; {{langx|ht|Ansapit}}), or '''''Anse-à-Pitre'''''<ref>{{cite web|title=National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency|url=http://geonames.nga.mil/namesgaz/ |website=GeoNames Search|access-date=3 June 2017}}</ref> is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Belle-Anse Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. Its [[Dominican Republic–Haiti border|border]] crossing to [[Pedernales, Dominican Republic|Pedernales]] is one of the four chief land crossings to the [[Dominican Republic]].
