'''El Junquito''' is one of the 22 [[Parishes of Venezuela|parishes]] located in the [[Libertador Bolivarian Municipality]] and one of 32 of [[Caracas]], [[Venezuela]].

== Raid ==
{{main|El Junquito raid}}
On January 15, 2018, the parish was the place of a [[El Junquito raid|raid]], which killed [[Óscar Alberto Pérez]] and other members after he [[Caracas helicopter incident|crashed a MBB Bo 105]] helicopter after he was fired gunfire and having thrown several grenades and firing at a government building in June of 2017. <ref name="DWmassacre">{{cite news |last=Romero-Castillo |first=Evan |date=19 January 2018 |title=Maduro and the "El Junquito massacre" |language=es |work=[[Deutsche Welle]] |url=http://www.dw.com/es/maduro-y-la-masacre-de-el-junquito/a-42213860 |access-date=20 January 2018}}</ref><ref>{{cite news|title=Four days after the El Junquito massacre, relatives await the release of the bodies|url=https://www.lapatilla.com/site/2018/01/19/a-cuatro-dias-de-la-masacre-de-el-junquito-familiares-esperan-que-entreguen-los-cadaveres/|access-date=20 January 2018|work=[[La Patilla]]|date=19 January 2018|language=es-ES}}</ref>
