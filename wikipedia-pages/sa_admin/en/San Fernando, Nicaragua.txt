'''San Fernando''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Nueva Segovia Department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

San Fernando is located 24 kilometers from the Department Capital, [[Ocotal]], and 250 kilometers from [[Managua]], the Capital of the Republic.

The town was the site of [[Battle of San Fernando|battle]] that pitted [[United States Marine Corps|American Marines]] and [[National Guard (Nicaragua)|Nicaraguan Provisional Guardsmen]] against [[Augusto César Sandino|Sandinista]] rebels on July 25, 1927, during the [[Sandino Rebellion]].

==Coffee==
Within the entire municipality of San Fernando there are more than 10,000 residents,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.manfut.org/segovia/fernando.html|title=SAN FERNANDO NUEVA SEGOVIA NICARAGUA MANFUT.ORG|website=www.manfut.org}}</ref> most of whom are coffee farmers.  It is estimated that there are approximately 200 coffee farms within the municipality.

The highest point in Nicaragua, [[Mogotón]], which measures 2,106 meters above sea level, is located just 20 kilometers from the town of San Fernando.
