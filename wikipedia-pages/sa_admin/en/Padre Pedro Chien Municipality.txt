The '''Padre Pedro Chien Municipality''' is one of the 11 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 15,488.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The town of [[El Palmar, Bolívar|El Palmar]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Padre Pedro Chien Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Bolivar.zip {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Padre Pedro Chien Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 14,749 (up from 12,810 in 2000).  This amounts to 1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|6.48|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/bolivar/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Padre Pedro Chien Municipality is Franklin Gonzalez, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 76% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Padre Pedro Chien | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D6%26mun%3D11%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231507/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/6/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D6%26mun%3D11%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-18 | url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-18 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]] (Capital Padre Pedro Chien).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
