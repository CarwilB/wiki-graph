'''La Jalca''' is a district of the [[Chachapoyas Province]] in the [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]], [[Peru]]. Its capital is [[La Jalca (Peru)|La Jalca]], also known as ''Jalca'', ''Jalca Grande'' or ''La Jalca Grande''. It was the first Spanish foundation of the region. The current city of [[Chachapoyas, Peru|Chachapoyas]] was established here. Founded in 1538 as ''San Juan de la Frontera de los Chachapoyas'' ("Saint John of the Frontier of the Chachapoyas") Chachapoyas was later moved to its present location.

The famous ''[[juane]]s'', a typical plate of the forest, prepared and invented for the festivity, also date back to this epoch. In contrast to the current ''juanes iquiteños'' they were prepared by means of [[yuca]].
