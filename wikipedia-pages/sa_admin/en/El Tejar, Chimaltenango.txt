'''El Tejar''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] and town in the [[Chimaltenango Department|Chimaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of approximately {{cvt|13.51|km2}}. As per 2023 estimates, it has a population of about 24,363 inhabitants.

==History==
Tejar was occupied by [[Kaqchikel people]] and belonged to the Sacatepéquez region. It was known as Tejar de Ortiz in the 16th century, as per documents from the general archives, it is mentioned in a will granted by Bartolomé de Archila dated on 12 December 1567. It is mentioned as a municipality in the Sacatepéquez district in the circuit of Chimaltenango when the Guatemalan constitution was adopted in 1825.<ref>{{cite web|title=Historia del municipio de El Tejar|work=Deguate|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/historia-del-municipio-de-el-tejar-chimaltenango/|access-date=30 May 2026|language=es}}</ref>

==Geography==
El Tejar is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chimaltenango Department]] in [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Administrative divisions, Guatemala|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin//|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/ygt.html|title=Municipalities of Guatemala|work=Statoids|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is spread over an area of {{cvt|13.51|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|title=El Tejar (Municipality, Guatemala)|work=Citypopulation.de|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/chimaltenango/0416__el_tejar/|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Chimaltenango]] to the north and west, [[Santo Domingo Xenacoj]] to the north and east, and [[Parramos]] and [[Pastores]] to the south. It is located about {{cvt|48|km}} from the national capital of [[Guatemala City]] and {{cvt|5|km}} from the departmental capital of Chimaltenango.<ref name="Geo">{{cite web|title=Geografia del municipio de El Tejar|work=Deguate|trans-title=Geography of El Tejar municipality|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/chimaltenango/geografia-del-municipio-de-el-tejar-chimaltenango/|access-date=23 May 2026|language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|title=Municipios de Chimaltenango|year=2010 |location=Guatemala|url=http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|access-date=8 June 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150608155133/http://www.segeplan.gob.gt/2.0/index.php?option=com_k2&view=itemlist&task=category&id=51:comalapa&Itemid=333|archive-date=8 June 2015|language=es}}</ref>

===Climate===
Located at an elevation of {{cvt|1810.62|m}} above sea level, El Tejar has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Koppen Classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|20.85|C}} and receives about {{cvt|144.76|mm}} of rainfall annually.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/guatemala/chimaltenango/el-tejar|title=El Tejar climate data|work=Weather and Climate|access-date=30 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The municipality had an estimated population of 24,363 inhabitants in 2023. The population consisted of 12,311 males and 12,052 females. About 25.9% of the population was below the age of fourteen, and 4.4% was over the age of 65 years. Majority of the population (80.2%) was classified as urban, while 19.8% lived in rural areas. About 33.2% of the inhabitants were born in the same municipality, while 66.2% were born in another municipality in Guatemala and 0.6% were born in other countries. [[Ladino people|Ladinos]] (66.1%) formed the major ethnic group, with [[Maya peoples|Maya]] (32.8%) forming a significant minority. The municipality had a literacy rate of 93.4%, and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] (96.2%) was the most spoken language.<ref name="CP"/>
