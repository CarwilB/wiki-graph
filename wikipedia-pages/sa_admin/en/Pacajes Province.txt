'''Pacajes''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz]]. Its capital is [[Coro Coro, Bolivia|Coro Coro]].

The most important river of the province is the [[Desaguadero River (Bolivia)|Desaguadero River]]. Other rivers in the province are  [[Ch'alla Jawira]] (in Aroma and Pacajes), [[Ch'alla Jawira (Callapa)|Ch'alla Jawira]] (in Callapa), [[Jach'a Jawira (Calacoto)|Jach'a Jawira]] (in Calacoto), [[Jach'a Jawira (Caquiaviri)|Jach'a Jawira]] (in Caquiaviri), [[Llallawa Jawira]], [[Qala Jawira]], [[Qullpa Jawira (La Paz)|Qullpa Jawira]] and [[Thujsa Jawira (Pacajes)|Thujsa Jawira]].

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into eight [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into cantons.
