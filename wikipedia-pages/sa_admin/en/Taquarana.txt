'''Taquarana''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[takwɐˈɾɐ̃nɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population was 20,072 (2020)<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/taquarana/panorama |title=IBGE 2020 |access-date=4 January 2021 |archive-date=19 October 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211019073128/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/taquarana/panorama |url-status=live }}</ref> and its area is 166&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=11 June 2008 }}</ref>
