'''Governador Lindenberg''' is the name of a [[Brazil]]ian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southeastern state of [[Espírito Santo]].  Its population was 12,880 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|360|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/es/governador-lindenberg/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

As of 2001, an official flag was adopted, and the coat of arms designed by Andreia Freitas dos Santos won a school competition to choose the city's coat of arms, in which the prize was a television.{{cn|date=August 2024}}

Located in the north of the state, roughly 200km from the capital of [[Vitória, Espírito Santo|Vitória]], this municipality gained independence from [[Colatina]] on 29 July 1997. Its colonization was made primarily through the access of the [[Doce River]], by [[Italian immigration to Brazil |Italian immigrants]], who settled there during the 19th century.
