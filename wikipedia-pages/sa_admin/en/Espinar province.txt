== History ==
On 21 May 2012, agricultural leadership in Espinar Province announced a strike against the planned expansion of [[Tintaya mine|Tintaya]] mine, a [[copper mine]] owned by the Swiss corporation [[Xstrata]]. The leaders' demands included higher environmental standards, more money for area development, and independent oversight of the mine.<ref name=AJ186 /> Strikers occupied the roads to the mine over the following week, blocking all access. In response, President [[Ollanta Humala]] declared a [[state of emergency]] in the province, suspending constitutional rights,<ref name=LAT>{{cite web |url=http://latimesblogs.latimes.com/world_now/2012/05/peru-mayor-protests-mining-project.html |title=Peru police arrest mayor who led mine protests |date=30 May 2012 |work=The Los Angeles Times |access-date=26 June 2012}}</ref> and deployed police commandos against the strikers.<ref name=AJ186 /> Two civilians were killed in the resulting clashes, and seventy police officers were injured.<ref name=LAT/> On 30 May, provincial mayor [[Oscar Mollohuanca]] was arrested by the [[Government of Peru|national government]] and accused of inciting protests against an expansion of a copper mine owned by [[Xstrata]].<ref name=BBC26>{{cite web |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/world-latin-america-18312047 |title=Peru: Espinar protest mayor in 'preventative' detention |date=2 June 2012 |work=BBC News |access-date=26 June 2012}}</ref> He was conditionally released on 13 July.<ref name=AJ186>{{cite web |url=http://www.aljazeera.com/indepth/opinion/2012/06/2012614113713713304.html |title=Peru's great transformation |author=Stephanie Boyd |date=18 June 2012 |publisher=Al Jazeera |access-date=26 June 2012}}</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (68.90%) learnt to speak in childhood, 30.75% of the residents started speaking in [[Spanish language|Spanish]] ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
