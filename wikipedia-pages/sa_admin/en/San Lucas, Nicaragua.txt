'''San Lucas''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Madriz (department)|Madriz]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]]. It is located 227 kilometres from the capital [[Managua]] and almost 8,5 kilometres from [[Somoto]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://info-nicaragua.com/madriz-somoto/san-lucas/ |title=San Lucas, Madriz |website=Info-Nicaragua |access-date=7 November 2024}}</ref>
