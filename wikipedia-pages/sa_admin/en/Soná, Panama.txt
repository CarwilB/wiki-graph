'''Soná''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Soná District]], [[Veraguas Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 10,802 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Soná District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 9,094; its population as of 2000 was 10,104.<ref name=inec/> [[File:Sona calle.jpg|thumb|Soná, 2024]]
