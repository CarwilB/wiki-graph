'''Aquila''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located about 136 km from state capital [[Xalapa]] to the south-west. It has a surface of 35.37 km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|18|48|N|97|18|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Aquila is delimited to the north by the state of [[Puebla (state)|Puebla]], to the east by [[Maltrata]], to the south by [[Acultzingo Municipality|Acultzingo]] and to the west by Puebla.<ref name="elocal">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30018a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Aquila |publisher=Gobierno Estatal |language=es |access-date=2009-05-22 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070307113601/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30018a.htm |archive-date=2007-03-07 }}</ref>

==Products==
It produces [[maize]].
