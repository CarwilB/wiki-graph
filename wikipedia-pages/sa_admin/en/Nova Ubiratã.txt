'''Nova Ubiratã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]] in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West Region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1, 2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref><ref name="IBGE_Pop_2009">{{cite web | url = http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/populacao/estimativa2009/POP2009_DOU.pdf | title = Estimativas da população para 1º de julho de 2009| publisher = Estimativas de População, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) | date = August 14, 2009 | access-date=December 17, 2009 | format = [[PDF]]|language=pt }}</ref><ref name="PNUD_IDH_2000">{{cite web |url=http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20(pelos%20dados%20de%202000).htm |title=Ranking decrescente do IDH-M dos municípios do Brasil |year=2000 |publisher=Atlas do Desenvolvimento Humano, Programa das Nações Unidas para o Desenvolvimento (PNUD) |access-date=December 17, 2009 |language=pt |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091003103229/http://www.pnud.org.br/atlas/ranking/IDH-M%2091%2000%20Ranking%20decrescente%20%28pelos%20dados%20de%202000%29.htm |archive-date=October 3, 2009 |url-status=dead }}</ref><ref name="IBGE_PIB_2005">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/home/estatistica/economia/pibmunicipios/2005/tab01.pdf |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios 2002-2005 |date=December 19, 2007 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref>

The municipality holds the {{convert|131795|ha}} [[Rio Ronuro Ecological Station]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 1998.<ref>{{citation |title=ESEC Rio Ronuro|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5650|access-date=2016-05-25}}</ref>

== History ==
Manoel Pinheiro was the founder of Nova Ubiratã and also the one who chose the name Ubiratã, which, according to the oldest residents, refers to the name of the farm where the settlement developed. Another possible origin of the name is that it is a tribute to the [[Ubiratã | city of the same name]] in  [[Paraná (state) | Paraná]], from where the first settlers came.

Among the families who first arrived at the settlement were the Feijó, the Setter, and the Ross. The colonizing company was Comércio de Imóveis Pinheiro Ltda. (COMIPIL), whose main shareholder was Manoel Pinheiro.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |url=https://impactomt.com.br/imprensa/noticia/nova-ubirata-completa-27-anos-conheca-um-pouco-dessa-historia/871/20240420_170637 |title =  Nova Ubiratã Completa 27 Anos: Conheça Um Pouco Dessa História  |trans-title=Nova Ubiratã Turns 27: Learn a Little about its History |access-date= 19 January 2025 |website=Impacto MT |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== Politics ==
The municipality of ''Nova Ubiratã'' has a political and administrative structure composed of the [[Executive (government)|executive branch]], headed by a [[Mayors in Brazil|mayor]] elected by direct vote and the [[Legislature|legislative branch]], embodied by the ''Câmara Municipal de Nova Ubiratã'' (Municipal Chamber of Nova Ubiratã), a collegial body representing the municipality's residents, composed of elected [[councillor]]s  who are also elected by direct vote.<ref>{{Cite book|last=Meirelles|first=Hely Lopes|title=Direito municipal brasileiro |trans-title=Brazilian Municipal Law  |edition=18th|location=São Paulo|publisher=Malheiros Editores|year=2017|language=pt-BR|isbn=978-85-392-0356-7|others=Updated by Giovani da Silva Corralo}}</ref>

As of January 2025, the mayor of Nova Ubiratã is Edegar José Bernardi,<ref name = mayor/> the vice-mayor is Eder Leandro, Setter<ref name = vice_mayor/> and the President of the Municipal Chamber is Leonildo Antônio.<ref name = president/>
