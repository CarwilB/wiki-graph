'''Chapadinha''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Maranhão]]. It is located 235&nbsp;km south-east of the state capital [[São Luís, Maranhão|São Luís]]. The population is 80,195&nbsp;(2020) and the total area is 3,247&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

The municipality lies in the [[Munim River]] basin.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Comitê de Bacia Hidrográfica do Rio Munim...}}|title=Comitê de Bacia Hidrográfica do Rio Munim convoca primeira assembleia|date=14 September 2015|publisher=Governo do Maranhão|url=http://www.ma.gov.br/comite-de-bacia-hidrografica-do-rio-munim-convoca-primeira-assembleia/|access-date=2017-02-09|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170211155122/http://www.ma.gov.br/comite-de-bacia-hidrografica-do-rio-munim-convoca-primeira-assembleia/|archive-date=11 February 2017|url-status=dead}}</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|11971|ha}} [[Chapada Limpa Extractive Reserve]], created in 2007.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Chapada Limpa – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=RESEX Chapada Limpa|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/585384|access-date=2016-08-31}}</ref>

==Climate==
Chapadinha experiences a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with hot temperatures and distinct wet and dry seasons. The city experiences a [[wet season]] from January to May. The [[dry season]] extends from July to November.
