'''Candeias do Jamari''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 27,388 in 2020 and its area is {{convert|6844|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 76% of the fully protected {{convert|71161|ha|adj=on}} [[Samuel Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Samuel Estação Ecológica|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/5936|accessdate=2016-05-14}}</ref>
It also contains part of the {{convert|221218|ha|adj=on}} [[Jacundá National Forest]], a sustainable use conservation unit.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional de Jacundá|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=97|accessdate=2016-05-16}}</ref>
