'''Saint-Jean-du-Sud''' ({{IPA|fr|sɛ̃ ʒɑ̃ dy syd}}; {{langx|ht|Sen Jan disid}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Port-Salut Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. In 2015, it had 25,567 inhabitants.
