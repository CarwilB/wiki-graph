'''Villa María del Triunfo''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of the [[Lima Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is located in the [[Cono Sur (Lima)|Cono Sur]] area of the city of [[Lima]].
It was officially established as a district on December 28, 1961. The current [[mayor]] (''[[alcalde]]'') of Villa María del Triunfo is Eloy Chávez Hernández.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.munivmt.gob.pe/alcaldesa.php|title=Municipalidad de Villa María del Triunfo|access-date=6 September 2011|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120124163707/http://munivmt.gob.pe/alcaldesa.php|archive-date=24 January 2012|url-status=dead|df=dmy-all}}</ref>

== Politics ==
Villa María del Triunfo is under the jurisdiction of its own [[Municipalities of Peru|district municipality]], as well as that of the [[Metropolitan Municipality of Lima]].

==Geography==
The district has a total land [[area]] of 70.57&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its administrative center is located 158 meters above [[sea level]].

===Boundaries===
* North: [[La Molina District|La Molina]]
* East: [[Pachacámac District|Pachacámac]]
* South: [[Villa El Salvador]] and [[Lurín District|Lurín]]
* West: [[San Juan de Miraflores]]

==Demographics==
According to a 2002 estimate by the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática|INEI]], the district has 329,057 inhabitants and a population density of 4662.8 persons/km<sup>2</sup>. In 1999, there were 71,889 households in the district.<br>
According to Propoli (http://www.propoli.org/quehacemos.htm){{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} :  <br>
the principal economic activities in the region are timber, restaurants, footwear, clothing, metalworking, tourism.<br>
Undernourishment rate is 14.76%<br>
Population without access to drinking water is 34.05%<br>
Population without electricity is 22.9%
