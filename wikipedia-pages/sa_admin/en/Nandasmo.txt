'''Nandasmo''' is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]] in the [[Masaya Department|Masaya]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].

== Economy ==
'''Nandasmo''' is largely driven by agriculture and handicrafts. The town's artisans produce a variety of handcrafted goods.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nandasmo, Nicaragua - ViaNica.com |url=https://vianica.com/nicaragua/masaya/nandasmo/11.5 |access-date=2024-12-21 |website=vianica.com}}</ref>
