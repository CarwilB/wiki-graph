'''Pucón''' ([[Mapudungun]]: "entrance to the [[cordillera]]") is a [[Chile]]an [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] administered by the municipality of Pucón. It is located in the [[Cautín Province|Province of Cautín]], [[Araucanía Region]], 100&nbsp;km to the southeast of [[Temuco]] and 780&nbsp;km to the south of [[Santiago]]. It is on the eastern shore of [[Villarrica Lake|Lake Villarrica]], and [[Villarrica (volcano)|Villarrica volcano]] is located roughly 17&nbsp;km to the south.
 
Pucón's location by a lake and volcano, along with its relatively stable climate, especially in summer,<ref>[http://www.letramedia.cl/?p=990 Welcome to Pucón] Revista Letra Media. Retrieved 24 August 2013.</ref> make it a popular destination for tourists. It offers a variety of sports and activities for tourists, including [[water skiing]], [[snow skiing]], [[backpacking (wilderness)|backpacking]], white water [[rafting]] and [[kayaking]], [[horse back riding]], natural [[hot springs]], [[zip line|zip line rides]], [[skydiving]] and guided ascents of [[Villarrica (volcano)|Villarrica volcano]].

==History==
During the [[conquest of Chile]] the Spanish founded the city of [[Villarrica, Chile|Villarrica]] in 1552 in the area of what is now Pucón.<ref name=Petit2004>{{cite book |last=Petit-Breuilh Sepúlveda|first=María Eugenia |date=2004 |title=La historia eruptiva de los volcanes hispanoamericanos (Siglos XVI al XX): El modelo chileno|chapter=Los volcanes y la evolución del poblamiento |location=Huelva, Spain |publisher=Casa de los volcanes|language=es |pages=48–50 |isbn=84-95938-32-4 }}</ref> The Spanish carried out [[history of mining in Chile|significant mining of gold]] in [[placer deposit]]s around this city.<ref name=Petit2004/> However at some point in the 16th century it is presumed the gold placers were buried by [[lahar]]s flowing down from nearby [[Villarrica (volcano)|Villarrica Volcano]].<ref name=Petit2004/> This prompted settlers to relocate the city further west at its modern location, abandoning this the site of Pucón.<ref name=Petit2004/>

The modern settlement of Pucón was established in 1883 as a fort in the aftermath of the [[Occupation of Araucanía]] when the Chilean state subdued the native population of [[Araucanía Region]]. It was a strategically advantageous spot because of its location at entrance of the [[Trancura River|Trancura Valley]], where the Argentinean army had once pursued a group of Mapuches through [[Mamuil Malal Pass]] during the [[Conquest of the Desert]].
 
The first hotel in Pucón was the Gudenschwager, established in 1923, but it was in 1934 with the establishment of Gran Hotel Pucón that tourism became a popular activity in the area. In 1940 the first road between Villarrica and Pucón was built and in the 1970s the road to [[Caburgua Lake]] was finished.

==Climate==
According to the [[Köppen climate classification]], Pucón has a [[oceanic climate]] (Cfb), on summer, it reaches 22°C and 25°C, rarely reaching above 30°C, on winter, the temperature reaches to 2°C and 4°C.

According to the 2002 [[census]] carried out by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|Chilean National Statistics Institute]], Pucón spans an area of {{convert|1248.5|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 21,107 inhabitants (10,705 men and 10,402 women). Of these, 13,837 (65.6%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 7,270 (34.4%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 47% (6,751 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Tourism==
Pucón is a major center of [[adventure travel|adventure tourism]] in Chile, attracting national and foreigner visitors alike. Due to its striking natural surroundings which include volcanoes, lakes, waterfalls, nature reserves and hot springs, Pucón attracts tourism all year round.During the summer, popular tourist activities include [[Hiking in Chile|hiking]], [[rafting]], horseback riding, bird watching, fishing, canopy trails, and canyoning. In winter and spring, it is possible to go skiing or snowboarding on the slopes of Villarrica volcano or visit one of the several thermal baths scattered around the nearby [[Valdivian temperate rain forest]]. These activities are easily arranged from the city itself, which harbours several tourist offices and travel agencies. [[File:Volcan villarica desde la ciudad de pucon.JPG|thumb|left|350px|Villarrica volcano as seen from a main Pucón street]]
Despite its relatively small urban area, accommodation infrastructure in Pucón is well developed, ranging from backpacker hostels to luxury hotels. Chilean and international cuisine can be found in one of the town's several restaurants and cafes. More recently, Pucón has become a major destination for [[Holiday cottage|second home]] investments and amenity migration.<ref>[http://www.jsedimensions.org/wordpress/content/amenity-migration-a-comparative-study-of-the-italian-alps-and-the-chil-ean-andes_2012_03/ Borsdorf, A., Hidalgo, R., Zunino, H. (2012): Amenity Migration: a comparative study of the Italian Alps and the Chilean Andes. ''Journal of Sustainability Education'' 3, pp. 1-10] [http://www.jsedimensions.org/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2012/03/BorsdorfHidalgoZuninoJSE2012.pdf PDF].</ref>

===Tourist attractions===

[[Villarrica (volcano)|Villarrica]] is the closest volcano to Pucón and is frequently climbed from the town. Other volcanoes in the area include [[Mocho-Choshuenco]], [[Quetrupillán]] and [[Lanín]]. Villarrica has had more than 82 eruptions since 1558, the most recent of which occurred on March 3, 2015. It is possible to climb it throughout the year, if weather and volcanic activity permit. During winter, the summit is completely covered by snow, and offers views of at least seven of the surrounding lakes.

El Cañi, meaning “sight that transforms”, is a private protected area of 500 hectares belonging to a non-profit organization.<ref>[http://www.thisischile.cl/8495/2/hike-el-cani-near-pucon-chile-for-great-views-of-the-region/News.aspx Hike El Cañi near Pucón, Chile for great views of the region] www.thisischile.cl Thursday, February 21, 2013, retrieved 29 August 2013</ref> It contains [[Hiking in Chile|hiking trails]] through ancient [[araucaria]]s, [[Lenga Beech|lengas]], [[Nothofagus dombeyi|coigues]], old volcanic craters and lagoons. It is administered by a group of farmers. The park is home to foxes, [[Cougar|pumas]], [[pudú]]s (a small kind of deer), wild ducks and [[condor]]s, among many other species. A view point located at 1,550 meters altitude provides views of four nearby volcanoes: [[Villarrica (volcano)|Villarrica]], [[Quetrupillán]], [[Lanín]] and [[Llaima]].

[[List of national parks of Chile|National parks]] in the area include [[Villarrica National Park]], [[Huerquehue National Park]] and [[Villarrica National Reserve]]. Some of the main lakes near Pucón are [[Villarrica Lake|Villarrica]], [[Caburgua Lake|Caburgua]] and [[Tinquilco Lake|Tinquilco]]. The main river is the [[Trancura River|Trancura]], which is used for kayaking and rafting. There are different [[International Scale of River Difficulty|levels of difficulty]] on the river, from easy family rides through Level III rapids to more difficult rides through Level V rapids.

There are several hot springs and spas in the [[Communes of Chile|commune]] of Pucón and the surrounding area, including: Palguín, Rio Blanco, Huife, Quimey-Co and Los Pozones. The nearby commune of [[Curarrehue, Chile|Curarrehue]] also contains popular spas, including: Menetúe, San Luis, Trancura, Panqui and Ancamil.

==Lahar risk zones==
Volcanic mudflows known as [[lahar]]s are common in the history of Pucón and the vicinity of Villarrica volcano. In 1964, a lahar destroyed much of the woodlogging town of [[Coñaripe]]. A 2014 study determined that the international road just east of the [[Pucón Airport]] lies in a zone of high danger, as does the road connecting to the west with the town of Villarrica. The airport is in a low danger zone, while most of the urbanized area of the city was outside the possible paths for lahars.<ref>{{cite thesis |last=Bono Troncoso |first=Laura Andrea del Carmen |date=2014 |title=Modelación de los lahares del volcán Villarrica en el sector de Pucón, Región de La Araucanía |chapter=Discusiones |publisher=[[Universidad de Chile]] |language=es }}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Pucón is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Pucón is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by René Manuel García ([[National Renewal (Chile)|National Renewal]]) and Fernando Meza ([[Social Democrat Radical Party]]) as part of the 52nd electoral district (together with [[Cunco, Chile|Cunco]], [[Curarrehue]], [[Villarrica, Chile|Villarrica]], [[Loncoche]], [[Gorbea, Chile|Gorbea]] and [[Toltén]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by José Garcia Ruminot (National Renewal) and Eugenio Tuma Zedan (PPD) as part of the 15th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-South).

==Transportation==
The main road to access Pucón is [[Chile Route 199]], which begins in [[Freire, Chile|Freire]] at the intersection of the highway [[Ruta de la Araucanía]], a section of [[Chile Route 5]]. With respect to intercity public transit, there are bus terminals with daily service to [[Temuco]], [[Villarrica, Chile|Villarica]], [[Cunco, Chile|Cunco]], [[Concepción, Chile|Concepción]], and [[Santiago]], with additional stops along the way. The city is also served by [[Pucón Airport]], which can handle aircraft up to the size of a [[Boeing 737]] jet.
