'''Abejorral''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Part of the subregion of [[Eastern Antioquia]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.abejorral-antioquia.gov.co/municipio/nuestro-municipio|title=Alcaldia Municipal de Abejorral|language=Spanish|access-date=2018-06-09}}</ref> It borders to the north with the municipalities of [[Montebello, Antioquia|Montebello]], [[La Ceja, Antioquia|La Ceja]] and [[La Unión, Antioquia|La Unión]], to the east with municipality of [[Sonsón]], to the south with the department of [[Caldas Department|Caldas]] and to the west with the municipalities of [[Santa Bárbara, Antioquia|Santa Bárbara]] y [[Montebello, Antioquia|Montebello]]. The population was 17,599 at the 2018 census.

It was founded in 1805 by [[José Antonio Villegas]], although some believe it was in 1811.<ref name=":1">{{Cite journal|date=2009-07-29|title=Abejorral : generalidades|publisher=Medellín |hdl=10495/423|language=es}}</ref> The latter was the year when the titles of the land were officially donated to the then existing residents.<ref name=":1" />

Abejorral is one of the oldest municipalities in the Antioquia Subdivision and Colombia, It is known for its colonial times homes which are part of the Historical National Registry. Its topography is ideal for hiking  and cycling activities with beautiful panoramic views of river and valleys;  some of them  will provide beautiful conditions for wild camping specially along the Arma River or the Aures River. Abejorral has some touristic features highlighted by foreigners as unique like " La Casa en el Aire<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.elespectador.com/publicaciones/buen-viaje/aventura-de-pasar-noche-una-casa-el-aire-articulo-611489|title=La aventura de pasar la noche en una casa en el aire|date=2016-01-19|website=ELESPECTADOR.COM|language=ES|access-date=2019-08-28}}</ref>"  and the" Aures River Waterfall ".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.bing.com/videos/search?q=El+salto+del+rio+Aures&view=detail&mid=A8C01E17338D410ED69AA8C01E17338D410ED69A&FORM=VIRE|title=El salto del rio Aures - Bing video|website=www.bing.com|access-date=2019-08-28}}</ref> Another ideas about the options to visit and recommendations are part of this new blog "Orientese"<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://orientese.co/abejorral-turistico/|title=Abejorral Turístico|date=2016-04-08|website=Orientese co|language=es|access-date=2019-08-28}}</ref>

==Sites of interest==

Most of the town is a historical site; the architecture that has survived through the years gives Abejorral the aspect of a city "suspended" in the colonial era, which is the reason that a part of the municipality was declared National monument. Sites of interest include:<ref name=":0" />

* Nuestra Señora del Carmen parish church.
* Nuestra Señora de los Dolores chapel.
* Hospital chapel.
* Municipal Cemetery.
* The Ancient Presbytery (Antigua Casa Cural).
* The founder's house (Casa del fundador).
* The Orange House (La Casa Naranjada).
