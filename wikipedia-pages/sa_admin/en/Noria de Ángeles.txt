'''Noria de Ángeles''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] state of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|75|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Zacatecas City]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Noria de Ángeles is located at an elevation between {{convert|2000|and(-)|2500|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in southeastern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Villa Hidalgo, Zacatecas|Villa Hidalgo]] to the east, [[Pinos, Zacatecas|Pinos]] to the southeast, [[Loreto, Zacatecas|Loreto]] to the south, [[Luis Moya, Zacatecas|Luis Moya]] to the west, [[Ojocaliente, Zacatecas|Ojocaliente]] to the northwest, and [[Villa González Ortega]] to the north.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32035.pdf |title=Noria de Ángeles, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=[[INEGI]] |year=2009 |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref> It also borders the municipality of [[Salinas de Hidalgo|Salinas]] in the state of [[San Luis Potosí]] to the northeast. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|408.5|km2}} and comprises 0.5% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 60.8% of the land in Noria de Ángeles is used for agriculture. [[Meseta Central matorral|Matorral]] (33.1%) and grassland (3.5%) cover much of the remainder, while the abandoned Real de Ángeles mine and its [[tailings]] area cover 1.9% of the municipality's land area. Most of the municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of [[Llanos el Salado|El Salado]], while a portion of the south is drained by the Chicalote River, a tributary of the San Pedro or Aguascalientes River, itself a branch of the [[Río Verde (Jalisco)|Río Verde]].<ref name=compendium/>

Noria de Ángeles has a temperate semi-arid climate with dry winters. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|300|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Noria de Ángeles is situated in an area that was originally inhabited by the [[Guachichil]], one of the [[Chichimeca]] nations. The settlement of Noria de Ángeles was first registered on 15 January 1621,<ref name=nomination>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/uploads/nominations/1351.pdf |title=Camino Real de Tierra Adentro |at=Vol. Zacatecas, pp. 12–13 (pp. 831–832 of the PDF) |website=World Heritage List |publisher=[[UNESCO]] |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref> and the mining settlement of Real de Ángeles was recorded in 1705.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32035a.html |title=Noria de Ángeles |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=18 June 2021 |archive-date=1 July 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200701120656/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32035a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The municipality of Angeles was established in the ''partido'' of Pinos on 29 October 1833. It became a free municipality under the name of [[Noria]] de Ángeles on 19 August 1916. From 1935 to 1941, the municipality was named after [[Álvaro Obregón]].<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |pages=59, 117 |location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=18 June 2021 }}{{Dead link|date=February 2026 |bot=InternetArchiveBot }}</ref>

The Temple of Nuestra Señora de los Ángeles was built in Noria de Ángeles from 24 June 1870 to August 1872. It was inscribed on the [[UNESCO]] list of [[World Heritage Site]]s in 2010 as part of the [[Camino Real de Tierra Adentro]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1351/multiple=1&unique_number=1691 |title=Camino Real de Tierra Adentro |website=World Heritage List |publisher=UNESCO |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/document/106973 |title=Temple of Nuestra Señora de los Angeles of the Town of Noria de Angeles; Scale: 1:1 073 |website=World Heritage List |publisher=UNESCO |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

In 1979, the village of Real de Ángeles was demolished and rebuilt {{convert|5|km}} to the east to make way for an [[Open-pit mining|open-pit]] silver mine.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Cervantes |first1=Berenice |last2=Garcia Perez |first2=Pedro |last3=Mora |first3=Adair |date=10 September 2019 |url=http://tropicozacatecas.com/2019/09/10/san-nicolas-tolentino-300-anos-de-fe-y-cultura-en-real-de-angeles/ |title=San Nicolás Tolentino, 300 años de fe y cultura en Real de Ángeles |language=es |website=Trópico de Cáncer Noticias |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref> The world's largest such mine in the 1980s, it was operated from 1982 to 1998 by Grupo Frisco, a mining company owned by [[Carlos Slim]]. Grupo Frisco has been repeatedly sanctioned for failing to comply with environmental regulations during the mine's operation and closure. The full extent of the mine's impact on the local environment and the health of the local population has yet to be thoroughly investigated.<ref>{{cite news |last=Valadez Rodríguez |first=Alfredo |date=13 November 2018 |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/2018/11/13/estados/026n1est |title=Tierras yermas y contaminadas, legado de Minera Real de Ángeles |language=es |newspaper=[[La Jornada]] |page=26 |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |last=Guzmán |first=Federico |date=18 August 2019 |url=https://ejatlas.org/conflict/minera-real-de-angeles-zacatecas |title=Minera Real de Angeles, Zacatecas, Mexico |language=en,es |website=[[Environmental Justice Atlas]] |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Noria de Ángeles comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and ten trustees (''regidores''), six elected by [[relative majority]] and four by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Manuel Becerra de la Rosa.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Noria de Ángeles recorded a population of 16,284 inhabitants living in 4126 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=84–85 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=18 June 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 15,607 inhabitants in Noria de Ángeles.<ref name=snim/>

There are 36 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which only the municipal seat, also called Noria de Ángeles, is classified as urban.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=035 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Noria de Ángeles |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=18 June 2021 |archive-date=16 November 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211116002103/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=035 |url-status=dead }}</ref> It recorded a population of 1633 inhabitants in the 2020 Census. The most populated locality in the municipality is Maravillas, located {{convert|12|km}} southwest of the municipal seat. It recorded a population of 2809 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy==
The main economic activities in Noria de Ángeles are agriculture and cattle farming.<ref name=encyclopedia/> The main crops grown are alfalfa and vegetables such as onions, lettuce and tomatillos.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Noria-de-Angeles.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 035 Noria de Ángeles |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |access-date=18 June 2021}}</ref>
