'''Humaitá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 56,144 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/humaita/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 33,072&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=2007-02-20 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=2008-06-11 }}</ref> The city is located on the banks of the [[Madeira River]].

The city is served by [[Humaitá Airport]]. The [[Trans-Amazonian Highway]] passes through the city.

==Geography==
The municipality contains part of the strictly-protected [[Cuniã Ecological Station]], an area of [[cerrado]] savanna parkland.<ref>{{citation|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Cuniã|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=53|access-date=2016-05-16}}</ref>
It contains 5% of the [[Campos Amazônicos National Park]], a {{convert|961318|ha|adj=on}} protected area created in 2006 that protects an unusual enclave of [[cerrado]] vegetation in the Amazon rainforest.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos – ISA}}|language=pt |title=PARNA dos Campos Amazônicos|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1482|access-date=2016-06-03}}</ref>
It also contains the [[Humaitá National Forest]], a {{convert|473155|ha|adj=on}} sustainable use conservation unit created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Floresta Nacional de Humaitá|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=92|access-date=2016-06-03}}</ref>
