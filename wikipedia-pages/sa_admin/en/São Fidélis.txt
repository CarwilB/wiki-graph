'''São Fidélis''' ({{IPA|pt|ˈsɐ̃w fiˈdɛliʃ}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 38,710 (2020) and its area is 1,028&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|21444|ha|adj=on}} [[Desengano State Park]], created in 1970.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Upam Desengano flagra corte de madeira nativa ... Inea}}
 |title=Upam Desengano flagra corte de madeira nativa em Santa Maria Madalena|date=14 October 2015|publisher=Inea: Instituto Estadual do Ambiente
 |url=http://200.20.53.3:8081/Portal/Noticias/INEA0110557|accessdate=2017-01-12}}</ref>
