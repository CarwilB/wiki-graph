'''Nazaré da Mata''' is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located  at 62&nbsp;km from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. It has an estimated ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE 2020]]) population of 32,573 people, and it is well known as the town of ''[[maracatu|Maracatu Rural]]''  and its ''engenhos de cana-de-açúcar'' (sugarcane plantations houses). It is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Nazaré]].

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Nazaré da Mata''' are based in food and beverage industry, [[commerce]] and [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]]; and [[livestock]] such as [[poultry]], [[cattle]], [[goat]]s and sheep.
