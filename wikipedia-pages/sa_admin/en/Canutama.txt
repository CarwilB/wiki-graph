'''Canutama''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]], considered third largest city of the Purus Medium after [[Lábrea]] and [[Tapauá]].

Its population was 15,807 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/canutama/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 29,820&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] – {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
===Conservation===

The municipality contains part of the strictly-protected [[Cuniã Ecological Station]], an area of savannah parkland.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica de Cuniã|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=53|accessdate=2016-05-16}}</ref>
The municipality contains most of the [[Balata-Tufari National Forest]], a {{convert|1079670|ha|adj=on}} sustainable use conservation unit created in 2005.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Balata-Tufari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=FLONA de Balata-Tufari|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1184|accessdate=2016-06-03}}</ref>
It contains part of the [[Mapinguari National Park]], a {{convert|1776914|ha|adj=on}} conservation unit created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PARNA Mapinguari – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=PARNA Mapinguari|publisher= ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/4234|accessdate=2016-06-02}}</ref>
It also contains the {{convert|197986|ha|adj=on}} [[Canutama Extractive Reserve]], created in 2009.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Canutama – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RESEX Canutama|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1575|accessdate=2016-09-02}}</ref>
It contains 2% of the {{convert|881704|ha|adj=on}} [[Tapauá State Forest]], also created in 2009.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FES Tapauá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=FES Tapauá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6607|accessdate=2016-09-02}}</ref>

== History ==

During its beginning, it received some denominations: in 1874, it was established with the name of New Colony of Beautiful Sight, and in 1891, Ville of Our Lady of Nazare. From 1895 it only received the name for which today we know it: Canutama.
