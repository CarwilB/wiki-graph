'''Quirihue''' ({{IPA|es|kiˈɾiwe}}) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] in [[Itata Province]] in the [[Ñuble Region]] of [[Chile]]. It is the administrative capital of the province. As per 2017 census, it had a population of 11,746 inhabitants.

==Geography==
Quirihue is located in [[Itata Province]] in the [[Ñuble Region]] of [[Chile]].<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/chile/mun/admin/itata/16201__quirihue/|title=Quirihue|work=City population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref><ref name="Min">{{cite web|url=https://www.mindat.org/feature-3873958.html|title=Quirihue|work=Mindat|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It spans an area of {{cvt|589|km2}}.<ref name="INE"/><ref name="CP"/> It is the administrative capital of the Itata Province.<ref name="CR">{{cite web|url=https://www.crwflags.com/fotw/flags/cl-qrh.html|title=Quirihue|work=CRW|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> The city has a [[Mediterranean climate]].<ref name="Min"/> The region is prone to [[earthquakes]], and the [[2010 Chile earthquake]], was one of the strongest in the 21st century and measured 8.8 on the [[Richter scale]]. The earthquake and the subsequent [[Tsunami]], which originated off the coast of Quirihue, caused significant destruction and killed 523 people.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.newington.nsw.edu.au/student-magazine/2025/08/29/a-brief-history-of-earthquakes/|title=A brief history of earthquakes|work=Newington|date=29 August 2025|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

==Administration==
As a commune, Quirihue is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years.<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> The city's flag consists of two equal halves of white and blue. There is a red cross on the white half on the left, and a yellow colored lion and an eight pointed star on the blue half.<ref name="CR"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Quirihue is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] as part of the [[District 19 (Chamber of Deputies of Chile)|19th electoral district]]. The electoral district is formed by 22 communes and elects five members to the chamber. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by the 16th senatorial constituency of Nuble, which elects two members to the Senate.<ref>{{cite web |title= Law no. 18.700, Constitutional Law on Popular Votes and Scruinty |publisher=Library of National Congress  |url=https://aceproject.org/ero-en/regions/americas/CL/leyes-electorales/chile-ley-18700-organica-escrutinios-2016-desactualizada |access-date=23 December 2024 |work=Ley Chile |language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
According to the 2017 census conducted by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Quirihue had a population of 11,746 inhabitants. The population consisted of 5,685 males and 6,061 females. About 80% of the population was classified as urban and rest as rural. About 1,915 (19%) of the population was below the age of fourteen. Majority (99.4%) of the population of the city were Chilean-born. [[Roman Catholics]] (68.5%) formed the major religious group with a significant minority (17.9%) of [[protestants]].<ref name= "CP"/>
