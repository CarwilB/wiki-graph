'''Yaxkukul Municipality''' (in the [[Yucatec Maya language]]: "where he first worships God") is a [[Municipalities of Yucatán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Yucatán]], containing {{Convert|43.43|km|2}} of land and located roughly {{Convert|25|km}} northeast of the city of [[Mérida, Yucatán|Mérida]].<ref name="municipios"/> Survey maps of Yaxkukul cite its full name as "Santa Cruz de Mayo Yaxkukul".{{sfn|Wachte|Andes|1996|p=262}}

==History==
It is unknown which chieftainship the area was under prior to the arrival of the Spanish. An ancient tradition is that a prince from Zaci (now [[Valladolid]]) sent a scouting party, which founded a town at the site. After the [[Spanish conquest of Yucatán|conquest]], the area became part of the [[encomienda]] system.<ref name="municipios"/> In 1607 the encomienda of Nabalam was joined with Yaxkukul, still later Yaxkukul was joined with Tahcab, and in 1667 was granted to the encomendero Francisco Menéndez Morán.<ref name="García Bernal (1978)">{{cite book|last1=García Bernal|first1=Manuela Cristina|title=Población y encomienda en Yucatán bajo los Austrias|date=1978|publisher=Escuela de Estudios Hispano-Americanos|location=Sevilla|isbn=978-8-400-04399-5|page=341| url=https://books.google.com/books?id=HYfY8H8P4VUC&pg=PA337|access-date=3 July 2015|language=es}}</ref>

Yucatán declared its independence from the Spanish Crown in 1821 and in 1825, the area was assigned to the coastal region with its headquarters in [[Izamal Municipality]]. Still later, it passed to the [[Tixkokob Municipality]] and in 1918, was designated as its own municipality.<ref name=municipios />

Yucatán architecture evolved in towns like Yaxkukul, Tixpehual, and Euan on the "preconquest platform foundation" and in the sixteenth century the features of churches consisted of an open view of chapel, the elongated nave and facade of [[Bell-gable|espadana]] which finally "complemented these buildings in the eighteenth century".{{Sfn|Edgerton|Lara|2001|p=78}}

==Governance==
The municipal president is elected for a three-year term. The town council has seven councilpersons, who serve as secretary and councilors of public works, ecology, public monuments, and nomenclature.<ref name=governance>{{cite web|title=Yaxkukul|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|website=inafed|publisher=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México|access-date=4 July 2015|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=4 July 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220704100544/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM31yucatan/index.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==Local festivals==
Every year from 16 January to 10 February the town holds a celebration for the Virgin of Candelaria.<ref name=festival>{{cite news|last1=Islas Chuc|first1=José A.|title=El lunes 10 acaba la fiesta de Yaxkukul|url=http://yucatan.com.mx/yucatan/el-lunes-10-acaba-la-fiesta-de-yaxkukul|access-date=5 July 2015|publisher=El Diario de Yucatán|date=6 February 2014|location=Mérida, Mexico|language=es|archive-date=7 February 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140207071437/http://yucatan.com.mx/yucatan/el-lunes-10-acaba-la-fiesta-de-yaxkukul|url-status=dead}}</ref>
