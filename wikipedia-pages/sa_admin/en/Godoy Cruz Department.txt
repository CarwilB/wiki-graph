'''Godoy Cruz''' is a  central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 183,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|75|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Godoy Cruz, Mendoza|Godoy Cruz]], which is located around {{cvt|1,110|km}} from the [[Capital federal]]. Its borders are [[Mendoza Department]] in the north, [[Guaymallén Department|Guaymallén]] in the northeast, [[Maipú Department, Mendoza|Maipú]] in the southeast, [[Luján de Cuyo Department|Luján de Cuyo]] in the south and [[Las Heras Department|Las Heras]] in the southwest and to the west.

The department and its ''cabecera'' (head town) are named in honour of Argentine statesman and businessman [[Tomás Godoy Cruz]] (1791-1852).

==Economy==

Despite its relatively tiny size (0.1% of Mendoza Province), Godoy Cruz is home to 182,977 inhabitants (11.6%), and accounts for around 36% of Mendoza's economy.

The main activity of the department is the commerce (specially auto sales companies) and the services sector. There are some [[wineries]] but there are not [[vineyards]]. The Casino of Mendoza is in the border of [[Godoy Cruz, Mendoza|Godoy Cruz]] and [[Mendoza, Argentina|Mendoza City]].

==Population==

Considering its urban population (99.97%), it's the most densely populated department of Mendoza Province.

==Sport==

The city of Godoy Cruz is home to [[Godoy Cruz Antonio Tomba]], a [[football (soccer)|football]] club, which became in 2006 the first club from Mendoza Province ever to compete in the [[Primera Division Argentina|Primera Division]]. Other significant sport clubs are Andes Talleres, noteworthy in [[artistic roller skating]], and YPF.
