'''Pueblo Viejo''' is a [[municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Veracruz]]. 
It is located in the state's [[Huasteca Alta]] region. 
The municipal seat is the city of [[Ciudad Cuauhtémoc, Veracruz|Ciudad Cuauhtémoc]].

==Geography==
The municipality covers a total area of {{cvt|286.24|km2}} and  is located on [[List of Mexican Federal Highways|Federal Highway 123]]. 
It borders with the state of [[Tamaulipas]] and the municipality of [[Pánuco (municipality of Veracruz)|Pánuco]] to the north and west, with the [[Gulf of Mexico]] to the east, and with [[Tampico Alto (municipality)|Tampico Alto]] to the south.

==Demographics==
In the 2005 INEGI Census, the municipality reported a total population of 52,593 (up from 48,054 in 1995), 
of whom 8,950 lived in the municipal seat.<ref>
{{cite web |title=Pueblo Viejo |url=http://portal.veracruz.gob.mx/portal/page?_pageid=1645,4150684&_dad=portal&_schema=PORTAL&ciudad=30133 |work=Portal del Gobierno de Veracruz |publisher=Veracruz State Govt. |access-date=4 November 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110721170534/http://portal.veracruz.gob.mx/portal/page?_pageid=1645,4150684&_dad=portal&_schema=PORTAL&ciudad=30133 |archive-date=21 July 2011 }}</ref> 
Of the municipality's inhabitants, 553  (1.2%) spoke an [[Languages of Mexico|indigenous language]], primarily [[Nahuatl]]; 0.32% of the total population spoke no Spanish.<ref>

==Settlements==
The municipal seat of Pueblo Viejo is not its largest centre of population.
Of the municipality's 73 settlements, the largest are:
*[[Benito Juárez, Pánuco, Veracruz|Benito Juárez]] (2005 population: 14,015)
*[[Anáhuac, Veracruz|Anáhuac]] (13,657)
*[[Cd. Cuauhtémoc, Veracruz|Cd. Cuauhtémoc]] (municipal seat; 8,950)
*[[Hidalgo, Veracruz|Hidalgo]] (6,159)
*[[Primero de Mayo (Los Mangos)]] (5,068)
