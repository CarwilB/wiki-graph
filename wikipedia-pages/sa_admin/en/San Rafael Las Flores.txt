{{Short description|Municipality of Santa Rosa, Guatemala}}
'''San Rafael Las Flores''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Santa Rosa Department, Guatemala|Santa Rosa Department]] in southeastern [[Guatemala]]. As of 2020, it had a population of 13,620.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Rafael Las Flores |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/santa_rosa/0605__san_rafael_las_flores/ |website=City Population |access-date=13 December 2021}}</ref>

The [[Escobal mine|El Escobal silver mine]], operated by [[Pan American Silver]], is nearby. The community of San Rafael Las Flores has been heavily involved in the [[Escobal mine protests]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite book|last=editor.|first=Bull, Benedicte, 1969- editor. Aguilar-Stoen, Mariel|url=http://worldcat.org/oclc/1100656471|title=Environmental politics in Latin America : elite dynamics, the left tide and sustainable development|date=13 November 2014|isbn=978-1-317-65379-0|oclc=1100656471}}</ref>
