'''Acala''' is a [[Municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] in the [[List of states in Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Chiapas]], in southern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat is [[Acala, Chiapas|Acala]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 28,947,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Acala|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=002|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=11 March 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180311140643/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=002|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 24,800 as of 2005.<ref name=Gob>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07002a.htm |title=Acala: ''Enciclopedia de los municipios de México'' |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] / Gobierno del Estado de Chiapas |accessdate=July 23, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927210646/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chiapas/municipios/07002a.htm |archivedate=September 27, 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==
As of 2010, the city of Acala had a population of 13,889,<ref name=SEDESOL/> up from 12,686 as of 2005.<ref name=Gob/> Other than the city of Acala, the municipality had 258 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[20 de Noviembre, Chiapas|20 de Noviembre]] (4,636), classified as urban, and [[Nuevo Vicente Guerrero (El Chichonal), Chiapas|Nuevo Vicente Guerrero (El Chichonal)]] (2,088) and [[Unión Buenavista, Chiapas|Unión Buenavista]] (1,847), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

It covers an area of 295.6&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
