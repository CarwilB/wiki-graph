'''Moro District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Santa Province]] in [[Peru]] (not to be confused with the [[Moro District, Riau Islands|Moro District]] in the [[Riau Islands Province]] of Indonesia).<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> It covers an area of 356.9 km<sup>2</sup> and had a population of 8,311 at the 2017 Census; the projected population in mid 2022 was 8,791.
