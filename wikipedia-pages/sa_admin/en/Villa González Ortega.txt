'''Villa González Ortega''' is a [[Municipalities of Zacatecas|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Zacatecas]], located approximately {{convert|70|km}} southeast of the state capital of [[Zacatecas City]]. It is named after [[Jesús González Ortega]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Villa González Ortega is located at an elevation between {{convert|2000|and(-)|2400|m}} on the [[Mexican Plateau]] in southeastern Zacatecas. It borders the Zacatecan municipalities of [[Noria de Ángeles]] to the south, [[Ojocaliente, Zacatecas|Ojocaliente]] to the southwest, and [[General Pánfilo Natera Municipality|General Pánfilo Natera]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/32/32053.pdf |title=Villa González Ortega, Zacatecas |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |year=2009 |access-date=17 June 2021}}</ref> It also borders the municipalities of [[Villa de Ramos]] and [[Salinas de Hidalgo|Salinas]] in the state of [[San Luis Potosí]] to the north and east respectively. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|431.9|km2}} and comprises 0.6% of the state's area.<ref name=panorama/>

As of 2009, 45.7% of the land in Villa González Ortega is used for agriculture. The remainder of the land comprises [[Meseta Central matorral|matorral]] (51.8%), urban areas (1.3%), and grassland (1.0%). The municipality lies in the [[endorheic basin]] of [[Llanos el Salado|El Salado]]. Its generally flat terrain is interrupted by small hills and plateaus.<ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite web |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32053a.html |title=Villa González Ortega |language=es |website=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=17 June 2021 |archive-date=1 July 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200701120734/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM32zacatecas/municipios/32053a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> There are no permanent rivers in the municipality.<ref name=compendium/>

Villa González Ortega has a temperate semi-arid climate with dry winters. Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|300|and(-)|500|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
Villa González Ortega is situated in an area that was originally inhabited by the Salinero tribe of the [[Guachichil]], one of the [[Chichimeca]] nations. The ''[[hacienda]]'' of El Carro, a cattle ranch, was established in the area in the late 16th century. After Mexican independence, the ranch prospered under the ownership of Juan Nepomuceno de Moncada y Berrio, {{ill|Count of San Mateo de Valparaíso|es|Condado de San Mateo de Valparaíso}} and {{ill|Marquess of Jaral de Berrio|es|Marquesado de Jaral del Berrio}}, whose testament funded the construction of the neoclassical Church of St. Teresa in El Carro between 1850 and 1855.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref name=nomination>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/uploads/nominations/1351.pdf |title=Camino Real de Tierra Adentro |at=Vol. Zacatecas, pp. 13–15 (pp. 832–834 of the PDF) |website=World Heritage List |publisher=[[UNESCO]] |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref> This church, also known as the Temple of Nuestra Señora de los Dolores, was inscribed on the [[UNESCO]] list of [[World Heritage Site]]s in 2010 as part of the [[Camino Real de Tierra Adentro]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1351/multiple=1&unique_number=1691 |title=Camino Real de Tierra Adentro |website=World Heritage List |publisher=UNESCO |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://whc.unesco.org/document/106974 |title=Temple of Nuestra Señora de los Dolores in Villa Gonzalez Ortega; Scale: 1:1 651 |website=World Heritage List |publisher=UNESCO |access-date=19 June 2021}}</ref>

In 1857, the municipality of Ojocaliente, to which the ranch of El Carro belonged, was transferred from the state of San Luis Potosí to Zacatecas. El Carro was briefly established as a municipality in 1869 before being suppressed in 1870. It regained its municipal status on 19 July 1890,<ref name=division>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Zacatecas. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/zac/ZACATECAS.pdf |language=es |pages=59, 117, 141|location=Mexico |publisher=[[INEGI]] |isbn=970-13-1519-7 |access-date=17 June 2021}}</ref> and its prosperity peaked during the [[Porfiriato]]. The dismantling of the ''hacienda'' after the [[Mexican Revolution]] led to a 50% decrease in the population of the municipality.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

On 29 November 1922,<ref name=division/> the municipality was renamed in honour of Jesús González Ortega, commander of the [[Liberal Party (Mexico)|Liberal]] forces in the [[Reform War]], and governor of Zacatecas from 1858 to 1864.<ref>{{cite book |last=Rodríguez Valadez |first=Juan Manuel |title=Zacatecas: Historia de las instituciones jurídicas |url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/2.pdf |publisher=[[Senate of the Republic (Mexico)]] |location=Mexico |date=16 August 2010 |chapter-url=https://archivos.juridicas.unam.mx/www/bjv/libros/6/2863/11.pdf |chapter=Gobernantes de Zacatecas |language=es |access-date=17 June 2021}}</ref> In 1967, a water supply, electrical grid and health clinic were built in the municipal seat.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

==Administration==
The municipal government of Villa González Ortega comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and ten trustees (''regidores''), six elected by [[relative majority]] and four by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The municipal presidency is located in the main house of the former ''hacienda''.<ref name=nomination/> The current president of the municipality is Ronal García Reyes.<ref>{{cite web |last=Alvarado |first=Silvia |date=8 June 2021 |url=http://portico.mx/2021/06/08/quienes-ganaron-en-los-municipios-de-zacatecas |title=Va por Zacatecas gana más municipios |language=es |website=Pórtico |access-date=17 June 2021}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2020 Mexican Census, Villa González Ortega recorded a population of 13,208 inhabitants living in 3492 households.<ref name=panorama>{{cite book |url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825198053.pdf |title=Panorama sociodemográfico de Zacatecas. Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 |pages=124–125 |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2021 |accessdate=17 June 2021}}</ref> The 2010 Census recorded a population of 12,893 inhabitants in Villa González Ortega.<ref name=snim/>

There are 31 inhabited localities in the municipality,<ref name=panorama/> of which two are classified as urban:<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=053 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Villa González Ortega |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |access-date=17 June 2021 |archive-date=14 November 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211114233902/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=32&mun=053 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
* the municipal seat, also called Villa González Ortega, which recorded a population of 6611 inhabitants in the 2020 Census; and
* [[Estancia de Animas]], located {{convert|9|km}} west of the municipal seat, which recorded a population of 2598 inhabitants in the 2020 Census.<ref name=panorama/>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The main economic activities in Villa González Ortega are agriculture and livestock farming. The main crops grown are corn, beans, grapes, and alfalfa.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://coepla.zacatecas.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Villa-Gonzalez-Ortega.docx |title=Información estadística básica. Municipio: 053 Villa González Ortega |language=es |publisher=Coordinación Estatal de Planeación |access-date=17 June 2021}}</ref>

[[Mexican Federal Highway 49|Federal Highway 49]] runs through the northern part of the municipality, connecting it to Zacatecas City in the west and [[San Luis Potosí City]] in the southeast.
