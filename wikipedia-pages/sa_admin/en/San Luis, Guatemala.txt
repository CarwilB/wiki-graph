'''San Luis''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[El Petén]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://agn.gt/avanzan-gestiones-de-certeza-juridica-en-el-municipio-de-san-luis-peten/|title=Avanzan gestiones de certeza jurídica en el municipio de San Luis, Petén|first=Julio|last=Morales|publisher=Agencia Guatemalteca de Noticias|date=2 March 2025|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref> The municipality is rectangular and covers approximately 2,915 km<sup>2</sup>. It contains 44,903 people. There are about 140 hamlets in the municipality, with the capital being the pueblo of San Luis itself.

==Overview==
Its economy is primarily of agriculture, but also contains minor manufacturing services like personalised furniture and custom building services. The pueblo itself consists of several neighbourhoods, including El Centro, Tikajal, Bethel, Vista Hermosa, La Florida, El Estadio, La Cruce and El Paraiso. It contains a public school in every neighbourhood and is home to a few private schools, including ''Centro Maya Asuncion'', a school for Mayan teenaged adolescent females from remote villages. It is administered by Jesus Claros Aliaraza and the Catholic community is represented by the [[Comboni Missionaries of the Heart of Jesus|Comboni Missionaries]]. Though it is surrounded by steep and hilly forests, deforestation remains the problem of the degradation for the surrounding environment.

Languages spoken in San Luis include [[Spanish language|Spanish]], [[Q'eqchi' language|Q'eqchi']], and [[Mopan Maya language|Mopan Maya]].
