'''Pivijay''' is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. Founded in 1774 by José Flores, Julian Valera y Antonio Sánchez. Promoted to municipality in 1912.

*Piñuelas
*Placitas
*Salaminita
