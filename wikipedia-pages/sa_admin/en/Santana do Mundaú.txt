'''Santana do Mundaú''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[sɐ̃ˈtɐ̃nɐ ˈdu mũdɐˈuː]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population peaked at 11,235 in 2005 and was 10,687 in 2020.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/santana-do-mundau/panorama |title=IBGE 2020 |access-date=2021-01-04 |archive-date=2021-06-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210627105620/https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/santana-do-mundau/panorama |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its area is {{convert|224|km²}}.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics |access-date=2006-12-02 |archive-date=2008-06-11 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>
