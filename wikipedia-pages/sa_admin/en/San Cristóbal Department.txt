The '''San Cristóbal Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento San Cristóbal''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the center-northwest of the province, limiting with the departments of [[Nueve de Julio Department, Santa Fe|Nueve de Julio]] and [[Vera Department|Vera]] in the north, [[San Justo Department, Santa Fe|San Justo]] in the east, and [[Las Colonias Department|Las Colonias]] and [[Castellanos Department|Castellanos]] in the south; to the west it shares a short border with the province of [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]] and a longer one with [[Santiago del Estero Province|Santiago del Estero]].

The department is the third largest in the province and has about 65,000 inhabitants. The head town is [[San Cristóbal, Santa Fe|San Cristóbal]] (population 14,000). The municipalities and communes in the department total 32: [[Aguará Grande]], [[Ambrosetti, Argentina|Ambrosetti]], [[Arrufó]], [[Capivara, Argentina|Capivara]], [[Ceres, Santa Fe|Ceres]], [[Colonia Ana]], [[Colonia Bossi]], [[Colonia Dos Rosas y La Legua]], [[Colonia Rosa]], [[Constanza, Argentina|Constanza]], [[Curupaity, Argentina|Curupaity]], [[Hersilia, Argentina|Hersilia]], [[Huanqueros]], [[La Cabral]], [[Colonia Clara]], [[La Lucila, Santa Fe|La Lucila]], [[La Rubia, Argentina|La Rubia]], [[Las Avispas, Argentina|Las Avispas]], [[Las Palmeras, Argentina|Las Palmeras]], [[Moisés Ville]], [[Monigotes]], [[Monte Oscuridad]], [[Ñanducita]], [[Palacios, Argentina|Palacios]], [[Portugalete, Argentina|Portugalete]], San Cristóbal, [[San Guillermo, Argentina|San Guillermo]], [[Santurce, Argentina|Santurce]], [[Soledad, Argentina|Soledad]], [[Suardi, Argentina|Suardi]], [[Villa Saralegui]], [[Villa Trinidad, Santa Fe|Villa Trinidad]].
