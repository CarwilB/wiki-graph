The '''Sir Arthur McGregor Municipality''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that make up the eastern [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Anzoátegui]], and according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of [[Venezuela]], the municipality has a population of 9,768.<ref>http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx {{Dead link|date=August 2022}}</ref> The town of El Chaparro is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Sir Arthur McGregor Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Anzoategui.zip {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Sir Arthur McGregor Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 9,434 (up from 8,590 in 2000). This amounts to 0.6% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|8.46|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/anzoategui/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=May 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

== Geography ==
Sir Arthur McGregor Municipality is located in the central‑western portion of Anzoátegui State in eastern Venezuela and is one of the state’s 21 municipalities.{{cn|date=April 2026}}

For administrative purposes, it is divided into two parishes: El Chaparro, which functions as the municipal seat and principal settlement, and Tomás Alfaro Calatrava.{{cn|date=April 2026}} The municipality is bordered to the north by Cajigal Municipality, to the south by Miranda Municipality, to the east by Aragua Municipality, and to the west by the adjacent state of Guárico.{{cn|date=April 2026}}

==Government==
The mayor of the Sir Arthur McGregor Municipality is María Agustina Rondón de Salazar, re-elected November 23, 2008, with 66% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=02&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02|title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008}}</ref> The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; El Chaparral and Tomás Alfaro Calatrava.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
