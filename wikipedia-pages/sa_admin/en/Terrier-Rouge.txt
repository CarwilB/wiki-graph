'''Terrier-Rouge''' ({{IPA|fr|tɛʁje ʁuʒ}}; {{langx|ht|Tèrye Wouj}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Trou-du-Nord Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 21,328 inhabitants.

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of two [[communal section]]s, namely:
* Fond Blanc, urban and rural, containing the town of Terrier Rouge
* Grand Bassin, urban and rural, containing the town of Grand Bassin
