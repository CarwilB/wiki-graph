'''Mapimí''' is a [[Municipalities of Durango|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Durango]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Mapimí, Durango|Mapimí]]. The municipality covers an area of 7,126.7&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

In 2010, the municipality had a total population of 25,137,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Mapimí|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=013|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=25 January 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200125232353/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=10&mun=013|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 22,940 in 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Mapimí|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10013a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=November 10, 2008|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927205746/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/durango/mpios/10013a.htm|archivedate=September 27, 2007}}</ref>

In 2010, the town of Mapimí had a population of 5,623.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Mapimí, the municipality had 283 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Bermejillo, Durango|Bermejillo]] (9,149) and [[Ceballos, Durango|Ceballos]] (3,730), classified as urban.<ref name=SEDESOL/>

Located in the northeastern portion of the state, it borders the municipalities of [[Lerdo (municipality)|Lerdo]], [[Gómez Palacio (municipality)|Gómez Palacio]], [[Tlahualilo (municipality)|Tlahualilo]], [[Villa Hidalgo (municipality of Durango)|Villa Hidalgo]], [[San Pedro del Gallo (municipality)|San Pedro del Gallo]], [[San Luis del Cordero (municipality)|San Luis del Cordero]] and [[Nazas (municipality)|Nazas]].
