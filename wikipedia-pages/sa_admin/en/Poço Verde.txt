'''Poço Verde''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|ˈposu ˈveʁdʒi|lang}}) is the westernmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 23,867 {{as of|2020|lc=y}} and its area is 431 square kilometers (166.4 square miles).<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
