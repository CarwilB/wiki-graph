'''Yantalo District''' is one of six [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Moyobamba Province|Moyobamba]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital]''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

Yantalo is a "[[Quechuan languages|quechua]]" word meaning "wood carrier". As of today the "Yantalinos" walk every day to the forest to collect dry wood. They cut each piece of almost equal size, bundle them up and carry the wood on their backs, usually tied with a cloth around their forehead. Main use is for fire / cooking.
