'''Iguaba Grande''' ({{IPA|pt|iˈgwabɐ ˈgɾɐ̃dʒi}}) is a [[List of municipalities in Rio de Janeiro|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]]. Its population was 28,837 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|54|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/painel.php?lang=&codmun=330187&search=%7Ciguaba-grande|title=Archived copy |access-date=2017-07-09}}</ref>
