'''Galeana''' is both a [[Municipalities of Nuevo León|municipality]] and a city in the Mexican state of [[Nuevo León]]. It is named after [[Hermenegildo Galeana]],{{citation needed|date=April 2026}} a lieutenant involved in the country's movement towards independence. Galeana shares borders with the states of [[Coahuila]], [[San Luis Potosí]] and Zacatecas to the west; with the municipalities of Aramberri and Doctor Arroyo to the south; [[Rayones]] to the north; and with Linares, Montemorelos, [[Rayones]] and [[Iturbide, Nuevo León|Iturbide]] to the east.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/nuevoleon/municipios/19017a.htm |title="Galeana", Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |access-date=2007-03-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070208214127/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/nuevoleon/municipios/19017a.htm |archive-date=2007-02-08 |url-status=dead }}</ref> Galeana is the largest [[municipality]] in the state, totaling {{cvt|7154.6|km2}}; however, it is thinly populated with only 38,930 inhabitants,<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://cuentame.inegi.gob.mx/monografias/informacion/nl/territorio/div_municipal.aspx?tema=me&e=19 |title=INEGI Información Municipal |access-date=2007-03-29 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070220044544/http://cuentame.inegi.gob.mx/monografias/informacion/nl/territorio/div_municipal.aspx?tema=me&e=19 |archive-date=2007-02-20 |url-status=dead }}</ref> resulting in a population density of 0.18 persons per square kilometer. Galeana, the municipal seat, is the best developed town in the region. It has services such as hospitals, accommodations, restaurants, bars and the largest [[drink]] distributor in the zone. However, it remains as a low per-capita income city.

==Geography==
Galeana is the largest municipality in the state, totaling {{cvt|7154.6|km2}}.
===Climate===
Climate in Galeana is usually much colder than the rest of the state.  In summer it is pleasantly cool and during winter, temperatures may reach down to -9°C. Annual average temperature is 17°C, and the average rain precipitation is around 446&nbsp;mm. The winter cold blends with a noticeable lack of air humidity. Predominants winds comes from the north.

===Crops===
The main crop in Galeana is the [[potato]], which is exported to different states and is also internationally shipped.{{cn|date=April 2023}}

==Tourist attractions==

[[Image:Galeana Laguna de Labradores.jpg|thumb|150px|left|Laguna de Labradores]]The municipality is known for its numerous tourist attractions. Probably the best known attraction is the ''[[Cerro del Potosí]]'' (Potosi's Mount), the highest mountain in the whole state, which rises over 3,600 meters above sea level.  On the Rayones-Galeana highway lies El Puente de Dios (''God's Bridge''), a majestic natural archway that lies on top of a small creek. ''El Pozo del Gavilán'' is an enormous, deep natural [[cenote]], where diverse species live.  The ''Laguna de Labradores'', in the capital, is a small lake connected to the ''Pozo del Gavilán''.{{cn|date=April 2023}}
