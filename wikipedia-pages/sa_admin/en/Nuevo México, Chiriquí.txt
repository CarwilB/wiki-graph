'''Nuevo México''' ({{IPA|es|ˈnweβo ˈmexiko}}) is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Alanje District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|74.0|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,497 as of 2023, giving it a population density of {{convert|28.9|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 41 of April 30, 2003.<ref name=inec/> The name of the subdivision references both the country of [[Mexico]] and the [[United States|U.S.]] [[US state|state]] of [[New Mexico]].
