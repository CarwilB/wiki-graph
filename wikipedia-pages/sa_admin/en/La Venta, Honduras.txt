'''La Venta''' (or '''La Venta del Sur''') is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the south of the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Francisco Morazán department|Francisco Morazán]].

It is approximately 52&nbsp;km from the national capital, [[Tegucigalpa]], and lies just off the [[Pan-American Highway]].

The town has valuable forestry assets, and the main form of employment locally is agriculture, particularly of plums and other soft fruits.
