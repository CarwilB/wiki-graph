'''San Zenón''' ({{IPA|es|san seˈnon}}) is a town and municipality of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Magdalena Department|Magdalena]]. It was founded in 1751 by [[Antonio de Mier y Guerra]]. On April 30, 1950, it was elevated to the category of [[municipality]].
