'''Guabiruba''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] situated in the state of [[Santa Catarina, Brazil]], with an estimated 24,382 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/guabiruba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It is  located in 27°05′09″ S and 48°58′52″ W and 60 m above [[sea level]]. It was colonized principally by people from [[Baden]], [[Germany]].

Guabiruba is known as "Pelznickelland".<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.pelznickel.com.br/ |title=Home |website=pelznickel.com.br}}</ref>
