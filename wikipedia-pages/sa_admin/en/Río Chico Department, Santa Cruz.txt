'''Río Chico Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Santa Cruz Province, Argentina|Santa Cruz Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has an area of 34,262&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> and had a population of 6,314 at the 2022 Census.<ref name="Census 2022">Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos de la Republica Argentine, Census of 16 May 2022.</ref> [[Cardiel Lake]] is located in this department. The seat of the department is in [[Gobernador Gregores]].
