'''Chaves''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the state of [[Pará]]. Its population as of 2020 is estimated to be 23,948 people. The area of the municipality is 13,084.879&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Marajó]] and to the microregion of ''Arari''.

Chaves is located on the island [[Marajó]] in the mouth of the [[Amazon River]]. A number of islands are part of the municipality, the largest of which are [[Caviana]] and [[Mexiana]]. The [[Tartarugas River]] forms the border with the municipality of [[Soure, Pará|Soure]].<ref name="lisboa">{{cite book | last = Lisboa | first = P.L.B. | title = A Terra dos Aruã: Uma história ecológica do arquipelágo do Marajó | year = 2012 | publisher = Museu Paraense Emílio Goeldi | location = Belém}}</ref>

The municipality is contained in the {{convert|59985|km2|adj=on}} [[Marajó Archipelago Environmental Protection Area]], a sustainable use conservation unit established in 1989 to protect the environment of the delta region.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Arquipélago do Marajó – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=APA Arquipélago do Marajó|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/951|accessdate=2016-06-27}}</ref>

In the 17th and 18th Century, the area was inhabited by the [[Aruã people|Aruã]], mainly around the [[Ganhoão River]]. Most of them migrated to [[Amapá]] and [[French Guiana]] after the [[Treaty of the Mapuá]]. In 1793, the [[Kingdom of Portugal|Portuguese]] transferred the ones who had stayed to the lower [[Tocantins River]].<ref name="steward">{{cite book |last=Steward |first=J.H. |date=1948 |title=Handbook of South American Indians, Volume 3: The Tropical Forest Tribes |url=https://etnolinguistica.wdfiles.com/local--files/hsai:vol3p193-198/vol3p193-198_turiwara.pdf |location= |publisher=Smithsonian Institution |page=194–196 |isbn= |access-date=}}</ref> On the coast of Chaves, ceramic fragments related to them could still be found, but the advance of the [[Vieira Grande Bay]] washed them away.<ref>{{cite web |author-last=Carvalho |author-first=P.M. |date=2010 |title=Tem quilombo na Amazônia |url=https://www.academia.edu/50997045/Carvalho_2010_Tem_quilombo_na_Amazonia}}</ref>
