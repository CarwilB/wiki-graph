'''Minas''' is a municipality and town in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Geography==
The municipality borders with [[Sierra de Cubitas]], [[Camagüey]], [[Sibanicú]], [[Guáimaro]] and [[Nuevitas]]. Its territory includes the villages of Anguila, Fomento, Gurugú, Las Piedras, [[Lugareño]], Monte Oscuro, Redención and [[Senado (Minas)|Senado]].<ref>{{OSM|r|5892226|Minas}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Minas had a population of 35,966.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1015|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=https://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|35|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
