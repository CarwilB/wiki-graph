'''Mundo Novo''' is the southernmost [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 18,473 (2020) and its area is 479.327&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

It is situated on the frontier to [[Paraguay]], opposite of [[Saltos del Guairá]] and the [[Paraná river]].
