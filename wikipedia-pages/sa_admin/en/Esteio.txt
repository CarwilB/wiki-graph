'''Esteio''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] situated in the Brazilian state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], in the metropolitan region of [[Porto Alegre]], the state capital. About 9.3 miles from Porto Alegre, and neighboring [[Canoas]] and [[Sapucaia do Sul]], it has about 83,000 inhabitants.

Esteio is known for being the city where the [[Expointer]] takes place. Expointer is one of the most important [[agriculture]]/[[farming]] fairs of [[South America]].
