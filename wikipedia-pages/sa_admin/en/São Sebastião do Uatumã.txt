'''São Sebastião do Uatumã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 14,352 (2020) and its area is 10,741&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>

==Geography==

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|938720|ha|adj=on}} [[Uatumã Biological Reserve]], a strictly protected conservation unit created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Biológica do Uatumã|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=213|accessdate=2016-04-18}}</ref>
It contains about 60% of the {{convert|424430|ha|adj=on}} [[Uatumã Sustainable Development Reserve]], which protects the lower part of the [[Uatumã River]] basin.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS do Uatumã – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RDS do Uatumã|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6832|accessdate=2016-07-25}}</ref>
