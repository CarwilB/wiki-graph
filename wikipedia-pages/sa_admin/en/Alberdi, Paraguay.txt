'''Alberdi''' is a town in the [[Ñeembucú Department|Ñeembucú department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is {{convert|144|km|abbr=on}} south of the capital city, [[Asunción]]. == History ==
Alberdi was founded on 29 August, 1930 under president [[José Patricio Guggiari]].<ref name=":1">Riveros Montiel, Tania Inocencia; Olmedo de Ríos, Shirley Lorena (2019). "[https://www.conacyt.gov.py/sites/default/files/upload_editores/u454/ALTAS-WEB_0.pdf Atlas Turístico del Ñeembucú]". ''Universidad Nacional de Pilar, Facultad de Ciencias, Tecnologías y Artes'' (in Spanish). Retrieved 2023-07-23.</ref> The town was named after [[Juan Bautista Alberdi]], an [[Argentina|Argentine]] diplomat and writer who voiced his support of Paraguay during the [[Paraguayan War]].<ref name=":1" /><ref name=":2">{{Cite web |title=Aniversario de Fundación de Alberdi |url=https://visitaparaguay.com.py/lugar/486/aniversario-de-fundacion-de-alberdi |access-date=2023-07-26 |website=Visita Paraguay |language=en}}</ref> 

== Geography ==
Alberdi is located along the [[Paraguay River]] in northern Ñeembucú, a department in southeast Paraguay. It is neighbored by the Paraguayan towns of [[Villa Oliva]] to the north and [[Villa Franca]] to the south, as well as the city of [[Formosa, Argentina|Formosa]], Argentina across the Paraguay River to the west.<ref name=":1" /> 

Most of the town is located within the region of [[Ypoá National Park|Lake Ypoá]], which is marked by landscapes of estuaries, streams and rivers that contribute to the region's cool and humid climate. Many of the riparian areas of Alberdi are prone to significant flooding following periods of heavy rain.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mancuello |first=Rodolfo |date=2019-05-20 |title=Formosa: El río Paraguay sigue creciendo y se ubica cerca de la cota de contención {{!}} CHACO DÍA POR DÍA |url=https://www.chacodiapordia.com/2019/05/20/formosa-el-rio-paraguay-sigue-creciendo-y-se-ubica-cerca-de-la-cota-de-contencion/ |access-date=2023-07-26 |language=es-AR}}</ref><ref name=":1" />

According to the department of Ñeembucú, Alberdi's 2021 population of 9,679 accounted for 11% of the department's total population. Alberdi is the second most populated city behind [[Pilar, Paraguay|Pilar]], the department’s capital.<ref name=":1" /> 

== Economy ==

=== Commerce destination ===
Being at the [[Argentina–Paraguay border]], a popular characteristic of the town is its commerce with the Argentine city of Formosa, which sits across the river. A large number of Argentines travel through the Formosa-Alberdi Port<ref>{{Cite web |title=Argentina.gob.ar |url=https://www.argentina.gob.ar/ |access-date=2023-07-29 |website=Argentina.gob.ar |language=es}}</ref> in order to shop for merchandise, mainly electronics and clothing, in Alberdi due to prices being markedly lower than those found in Argentina. The local Alberdi municipality encourages the activity and assists in organizing travel to and from Formosa by offering speedboats to shuttle tourists across the Paraguay River every half hour, as the [[Villeta]]–Alberdi–[[Pilar, Paraguay|Pilar]] [[List of highways in Paraguay|Route]] passes through the city.<ref>{{Cite web |title=La Nación / Comercio fronterizo de Alberdi tuvo buena recepción con más de 1.000 personas que cruzaron |url=https://www.lanacion.com.py/negocios/2022/04/16/comercio-fronterizo-de-alberdi-tuvo-buena-recepcion-con-mas-de-1000-personas-que-cruzaron/ |access-date=2023-07-26 |website=www.lanacion.com.py |language=es}}</ref><ref name=":1" />

=== Agriculture ===
In addition to commercial tourism, Alberdi relies to a lesser extent on agriculture and fishing. The primary crops are [[cassava]], [[lettuce]], and [[Cucurbita|squash]].<ref name=":1" />
