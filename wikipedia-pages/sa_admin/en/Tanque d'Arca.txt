'''Tanque d'Arca''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈtɐ̃jki ˈdɐʁkɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. In 2020,<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/tanque-darca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> its population was 6,138. Its area is {{cvt|156|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref>
