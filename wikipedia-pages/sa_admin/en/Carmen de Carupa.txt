'''Carmen de Carupa''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Ubaté Province]], part of the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. The municipality, located in the [[Ubaté-Chiquinquirá Valley]] on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] borders [[San Cayetano, Cundinamarca|San Cayetano]] in the west, [[Tausa]] and [[Sutatausa]] in the south, [[Ubaté]] and [[Susa, Cundinamarca|Susa]] in the east and [[Simijaca]] and [[Buenavista, Boyacá|Buenavista]] and [[Coper, Boyacá|Coper]] ([[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]) in the north.<ref name=websiteCarmen>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.carmendecarupa-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Carmen de Carupa]{{Dead link|date=June 2019 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==History==
The area around Carmen de Carupa was before the arrival of the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish]] [[conquistador]]s inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The ''[[cacique]]'' of Ubaté ruled over the territories of Carmen de Carupa. The western and northern neighbouring indigenous group of Carmen de Carupa was the [[Muzo people|Muzo]].

Modern Carmen de Carupa was founded on July 20, 1808 by José Joaquín Urdaneta and Doña Ventura.<ref name=websiteCarmen/>

==Economy==
Main economical activity in Carmen de Carupa is [[agriculture]], predominantly [[potato]]es.<ref name=websiteCarmen/>
