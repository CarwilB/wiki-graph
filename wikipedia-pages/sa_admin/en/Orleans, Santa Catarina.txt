'''Orleans''' is a Brazilian municipality in the state of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. The town is located in the south of the state.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 23,038.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/orleans/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|1330|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra Furada State Park]], created in 1980.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual da Serra Furada – FATMA}}|title=Parque Estadual da Serra Furada|publisher=FATMA: Fundação do Meio Ambiente|url=http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-da-serra-furada|access-date=2017-02-04|archive-date=2018-02-11|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180211123601/http://www.fatma.sc.gov.br/conteudo/parque-estadual-da-serra-furada|url-status=dead}}</ref>

==History==
Named after emperor [[Dom Pedro II]]'s son-in-law, [[Gaston, Count of Eu]], from the House of Orléans on December 26, 1884.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://orleans.sc.gov.br/2013/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=76&Itemid=517|title=História da Prefeitura municipal de Orleans|publisher=orleans.sc.gov.br|access-date=19 October 2016}}</ref>
It was first colonized by Italians, then Germans, Austrians, Poles, Norwegians, Latvians, Dutch and other nationalities from Europe.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://orleans.sc.gov.br/2013/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=76&Itemid=517|title=História da Prefeitura municipal de Orleans|publisher=orleans.sc.gov.br|access-date=19 October 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://hafemann.com.br/site/imigracao-catarinense/|title=Imigração da Europa a Santa Catarina|publisher=hafemann.br|access-date=19 October 2016}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.weber-ruiz.com/imigracao.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091213031938/http://www.weber-ruiz.com/imigracao.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=December 13, 2009|title=Imigração no Brasil|publisher=weber-ruiz.com|access-date=19 October 2016}}</ref>
