'''Hidalgo Municipality''' (also, '''Villa Hidalgo''') is a [[Municipalities of Tamaulipas|municipality]] located in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Tamaulipas]]. [[El Chorrito]] is a centre of pilgrimage in the municipality.
{{coord|24|14|53|N|99|26|16|W|display=title}}

==Geography==
===Climate===
The prevailing climate in Hidalgo is sub-humid and warm. Average rainfall is 700 millimeters, the minimum temperature is 2&nbsp;°C and maximum of 41&nbsp;°C.
