'''Uspantán''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Quiché Department|El Quiché]]. It is one of the largest municipalities of [[El Quiché]] and stretches from the mountainous highlands in the South to the tropical lowlands in the North.  The municipal seat is in Villa de San Miguel Uspantán with a population of 2,800. The birthplace of [[Nobel Peace Prize]] winner [[Rigoberta Menchú]], a community named [[Laj Chimel]], is located Uspantán not far from the municipal seat.  Completion of paving on the road in from [[Chichicastenango]] has brought a small tourist boom to the town.

The municipality includes the [[pre-Columbian era|pre-Columbian]] [[Maya civilization|Maya]] archaeological site of [[Chitinamit]], believed to be Jakawitz, the first capital of the [[K'iche' people|K'iche' Maya]].<ref>Sharer & Traxler 2006, p.622.</ref><ref>Ministerio de Cultura y Deportes. Item 1005.</ref>
