'''Alegre''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 29,975 (2020) and its area is {{convert|772|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/4Z7]</ref>
