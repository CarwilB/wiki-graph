'''Sarmiento Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chubut Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 8,724 inhabitants in an area of 14,563 km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Sarmiento, Chubut|Sarmiento]], which is located around 1,909 km from the [[Capital federal]].
