'''Primero de Mayo''' is a south eastern [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,000 inhabitants in an area of  1,864&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Margarita Belén]], which is located around 1,035&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
