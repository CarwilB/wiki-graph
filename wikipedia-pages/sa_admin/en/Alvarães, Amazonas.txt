'''Alvarães''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 16,220 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/alvaraes/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 5,912&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2007-01-09 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109005631/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2007-01-09 }}</ref>

The municipality contains about 37% of the [[Tefé National Forest]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Tefé – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
