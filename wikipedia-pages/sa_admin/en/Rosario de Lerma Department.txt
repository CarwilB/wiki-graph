'''Rosario de Lerma''' is a department located in [[Salta Province]], [[Argentina]]. Its main settlements are [[Rosario de Lerma]] (the capital) and [[Campo Quijano]].

== Geography ==
===Overview===
The department is located in the north-central side of the province, near the [[Andes]], and includes part of the [[Puna de Atacama]]. It borders with [[Jujuy Province]] and the departments of [[La Caldera Department|La Caldera]], [[Capital Department, Salta|Capital]], [[Cerrillos Department|Cerrillos]], [[Chicoana Department|Chicoana]], [[Cachi Department|Cachi]] and [[La Poma Department|La Poma]]. The territorial strip linking the northern and southern side of La Poma Department separates Rosario de Lerma from [[Los Andes Department]].
