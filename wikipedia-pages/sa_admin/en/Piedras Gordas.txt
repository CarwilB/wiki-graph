'''Piedras Gordas''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[La Pintada District]], [[Coclé Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|137.8|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 4,164 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|30.2|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 3,648; its population as of 2000 was 3,638.<ref name=inec/>

Part of the [[La Pintada, Panama|La Pintada]] district, Piedras Gordas is nestled in the Tabasará Mountains.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Panama {{!}} History, Map, Flag, Capital, Population, & Facts {{!}} Britannica |url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Panama |access-date=2022-12-30 |website=www.britannica.com |language=en}}</ref>
