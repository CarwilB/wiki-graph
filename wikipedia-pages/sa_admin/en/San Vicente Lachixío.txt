'''San Vicente Lachixío''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of 93.13&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. 
It is part of the [[Sola de Vega District]] in the [[Sierra Sur de Oaxaca|Sierra Sur Region]].

The town is named for the catholic patron saint San Vicente Ferrer. The town of '''San Vicente Lachixio''' was founded in 1776.<ref>{{cite web|title=Encyclopedia of the Municipalities of Mexico|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20535a.htm|accessdate=1 April 2011|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120327111727/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20535a.htm|archivedate=27 March 2012}}</ref>

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 3,154 and in 2010 it was 2976 of which the great majority is from indigenous descent.<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20535a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=June 15, 2009}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=Catálogo Localidades|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=20&mun=535|access-date=2020-11-30|website=www.microrregiones.gob.mx}}</ref>
