'''San Estanislao''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Bolívar Department]], northern [[Colombia]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sanestanislao-bolivar.gov.co/ |title=Home |website=sanestanislao-bolivar.gov.co}}</ref>

San Estanislao is also informally known as '''Arenal''' by the locals, Arenal is [[Spanish language|Spanish]] for "[[sand]]", which earned its nickname in result that the town accumulates high amounts of sand during rain and wind.  A mission was established on the present site in 1650.  Access to the town can be challenging as the roads that access the town are unpaved and often water-logged after long periods of rain. Inhabitants of San Estanislao generally support themselves through family farms yielding a variety of produce and meat products, which are they often trade amongst themselves.

== Location ==
San Estanislao is located alongside the northeastern side of the [[Canal del Dique|Dique Canal]] 16 meters (55&nbsp;feet) above sea level. Nearest major metropolitan municipalities are Barranquilla to the north by northeast, and Cartagena to the west. The town of [[Soplaviento]] is located across the canal from San Estanislao.

== Climate ==
The average temperature is approximately 35.0'C (95.0'F) during the day, 23.9'C (75.0'F) at night, and is generally the same most of the calendar year.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.sanestanislao-bolivar.gov.co/ |title=Home |website=sanestanislao-bolivar.gov.co}}</ref>
