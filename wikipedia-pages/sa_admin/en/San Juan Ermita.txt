'''San Juan Ermita''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Chiquimula Department|Chiquimula department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It has a population of 16,418 (2018 census)<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/ Citypopulation.de] Population of departments and municipalities in Guatemala</ref> and cover an area of 80.5 km<sup>2</sup>.
