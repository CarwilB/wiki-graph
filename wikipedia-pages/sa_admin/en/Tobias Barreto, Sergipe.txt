'''Tobias Barreto''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|toˈbi.ɐz baˈʁetu|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population is 52,530 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|1033|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/tobias-barreto/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

It was formerly known as '''Vila de Campos do Rio Real''', but was rechristened '''Tobias Barreto''' in order to honor the [[Tobias Barreto|homonymous poet]] who was born in there.
