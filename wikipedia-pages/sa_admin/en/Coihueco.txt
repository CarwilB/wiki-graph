'''Coihueco''' ({{IPA|es|kojˈweko}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and [[List of cities in Chile|city]] in [[Punilla Province]], [[Ñuble Region]]. It is located near [[Chillán]], the provincial capital. Coihueco borders [[San Carlos, Chile|San Carlos]] and [[San Fabián, Chile|San Fabián]] on the north, [[Argentina]] on the east, [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]] on the South, and Chillán on the west.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Coihueco spans an area of {{convert|1776.6|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 23,583 inhabitants (12,211 men and 11,372 women). Of these, 7,230 (30.7%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 16,353 (69.3%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 4.4% (998 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Coihueco is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Arnoldo Manuel Jiménez Venegas ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Coihueco is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Carlos Abel Jarpa ([[Social Democrat Radical Party|PRSD]]) and Rosauro Martínez ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 41st electoral district, together with [[Chillán]], [[Pinto, Chile|Pinto]], [[San Ignacio, Chile|San Ignacio]], [[El Carmen, Chile|El Carmen]], [[Pemuco]], [[Yungay, Chile|Yungay]] and [[Chillán Viejo]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Victor Pérez Varela ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mariano Ruiz-Esquide Jara ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 13th senatorial constituency (Biobío-Coast).
