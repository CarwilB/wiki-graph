'''Magüí Payán''' ({{IPA|es|maˈɣwi}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Magüí Payán has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with very heavy rainfall year round.
