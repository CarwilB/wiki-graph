'''Aiuruoca''' is a city in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. In 2020 its population was estimated to be 5,976.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/aiuruoca/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

Aiuruoca was founded in 1706.

In the 1920s, [[Scandinavian Brazilians|Danish immigrants]] in rural parts of Aiuruoca laid the foundation for the production of the [[:Category:Brazilian cheeses|Brazilian cheese]] ''[[queijo prato]]''.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://revistagloborural.globo.com/GloboRural/0,6993,EEC1708312-1641-1,00.html|title=Reportagens|website=revistagloborural.globo.com|access-date=2016-04-25|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160531170422/http://revistagloborural.globo.com/GloboRural/0,6993,EEC1708312-1641-1,00.html|archive-date=2016-05-31|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The municipality contains 15.76% of the {{convert|22917|ha}} [[Serra do Papagaio State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES da Serra do Papagaio – ISA}}|language=pt
