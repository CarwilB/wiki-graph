'''Ocosingo''' is a city and its surrounding [[municipalities of Chiapas|municipality]] of the same name in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Chiapas]].

==Overview==
The northeastern boundary of the municipality is the [[Usumacinta River]], along a portion where the river forms [[Mexico-Guatemala border|the international border]] with [[Guatemala]].

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 198,877,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Ocosingo|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=059|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|access-date=23 April 2014|archive-date=24 October 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211024181012/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=07&mun=059|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 170,280 as of 2005.

As of 2010, the city of Ocosingo had a population of 41,878,<ref name=SEDESOL/> up from 35,065 as of 2005. Other than the city of Ocosingo, the municipality had 1,387 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Nueva Palestina, Chiapas|Nueva Palestina]] (10,588), [[Frontera Corozal, Chiapas|Frontera Corozal]] (5,184), [[Tenango, Chiapas|Tenango]] (4,436), [[Abasolo, Chiapas|Abasolo]] (2,884), classified as urban, and [[Damasco, Chiapas|Damasco]] (2,380), [[Taniperla, Chiapas|Taniperla]] (2,106), [[San Quintín, Chiapas|San Quintín]] (1,732), [[Cristóbal Colón, Chiapas|Cristóbal Colón]] (1,623), [[Santo Domingo, Chiapas|Santo Domingo]] (1,584), [[Cintalapa, Ocosingo|Cintalapa]] (1,571), [[Perla de Acapulco, Chiapas|Perla de Acapulco]] (1,527), [[Santa Elena, Chiapas|Santa Elena]] (1,508), [[El Censo, Chiapas|El Censo]] (1,500), [[El Sibal, Chiapas|El Sibal]] (1,472), [[Nuevo Francisco León, Chiapas|Nuevo Francisco León]] (1,420), [[El Zapotal, Chiapas|El Zapotal]] (1,385), [[Las Tazas, Chiapas|Las Tazas]] (1,381), [[Sibaca, Chiapas|Sibaca]] (1,359), [[Arroyo Granizo, Chiapas|Arroyo Granizo]] (1,349), [[Ubilio García, Chiapas|Ubilio García]] (1,318), [[El Limonar, Chiapas|El Limonar]] (1,289), [[Patria Nueva (San José el Contento), Chiapas|Patria Nueva (San José el Contento)]] (1,282), [[Zaragoza, Chiapas|Zaragoza]] (1,163), [[Los Pinos, Chiapas|Los Pinos]] (1,073), [[Ramón F. Several notable [[Maya civilization|Maya]] [[List of Maya sites|archaeological sites]] are located in the municipality, including [[Bonampak]], [[Yaxchilan]], and [[Toniná]].

The western portion of the municipality is in the [[Chiapas Highlands]]. The [[Lacandon Forest]] covers the central and eastern foothills and lowlands. Protected areas in the municipality include [[Montes Azules Biosphere Reserve|Montes Azules]] and [[Nahá–Metzabok]] biosphere reserves.

==History==
Ocosingo was given city status on 31 July 1979.<ref name="HSJ">{{cite web|title=OCOSINGO, CHIAPAS, MÉXICO|url=http://www.sanjoseocosingo.com/ocosingo.html|website=Hotel San Jose Ocosingo|access-date=30 April 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080617004041/http://www.sanjoseocosingo.com/ocosingo.html|archive-date=17 June 2008|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Ocosingo rose to national prominence during the Zapatista uprising of 1994. It was occupied by the EZLN along with several other towns in Chiapas (including San Cristóbal). The EZLN retreated from most towns before the arrival of the Mexican army but not so in Ocosingo. Thus, the town saw several days of intense fighting, leaving dozens of rebels, soldiers and civilians dead.{{citation needed|date=February 2021}}

Se busca que caso de Mariana se investigue con visión de género: Sánchez Cordero |url=https://www.jornada.com.mx/notas/2021/02/01/politica/se-busca-que-caso-de-mariana-se-investigue-con-vision-de-genero-sanchez-cordero/ |access-date=February 1, 2021 |work=jornada.com.mx |publisher=La Jornada |date=February 1, 2021 |language=es}}</ref> Mariana's body was cremated on February 2 without the family's authorization, impeding a complete investigation into her death.<ref>{{cite news |title=La familia de Mariana asegura que no autorizó su cremación |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/estados/2021/2/2/la-familia-de-mariana-asegura-que-no-autorizo-su-cremacion-257505.html |access-date=February 6, 2021 |work=proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |date=February 2, 2021 |language=spanish}}</ref> The director of the health center where Mariana worked was arrested on February 6 for her possible participation in the case.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Mandujano |first1=Isain |title=Detienen a directora del centro de salud de Ocosingo donde asesinaron a Mariana |url=https://www.proceso.com.mx/nacional/estados/2021/2/6/detienen-directora-del-centro-de-salud-de-ocosingo-donde-asesinaron-mariana-257787.html |access-date=February 6, 2021 |work=proceso.com.mx |publisher=Proceso |date=February 6, 2021 |language=spanish}}</ref>
