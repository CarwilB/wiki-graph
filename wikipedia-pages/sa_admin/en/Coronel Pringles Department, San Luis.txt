''''Coronel Pringles''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[San Luis Province]], [[Argentina]].

With an area of {{convert|4484|sqkm|sqmi|0|abbr=on|sp=us}} it borders to the north with the departments of [[Ayacucho Department, San Luis|Ayacucho]] and [[Libertador General San Martín Department, San Luis|San Martín]], to the east with [[Chacabuco Department, San Luis|Chacabuco]] and [[General Pedernera Department|General Pedernera]], to the south with Pedernera, and to the west with [[Juan Martín de Pueyrredón Department|Juan Martín de Pueyrredón]], and [[Belgrano Department, San Luis|Belgrano]].
