'''Comandante Fernández Department''' is a central [[departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 88,000 inhabitants in an area of  1,500&nbsp;km², and its capital city is [[Presidencia Roque Sáenz Peña]], which is located around 1,120&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].
