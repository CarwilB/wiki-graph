'''Costa Marques''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. Its population was 18,798 (2020) and its area is 4,987&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/6BS]</ref>

The city is located in the [[Guaporé River]]'s right bank and the main attraction is [[Forte Príncipe da Beira]] (named after the [[Prince of Beira]]), which was built in 1786 by the Portuguese Kingdom to protect that region from the Spanish Crown.

The municipality contains 91% of the {{convert|36442|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra dos Reis State Park]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Serra dos Reis – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PES Serra dos Reis|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6322|access-date=2016-08-06}}</ref>
It contains 14% of the {{convert|2244|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra dos Reis A State Park]], created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|PES Serra dos Reis A – ISA}}
 |title=PES Serra dos Reis A|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6334|access-date=2016-12-20}}</ref>
It also contains 52.5% of the {{convert|146400|ha|adj=on}} [[Rio Cautário State Extractive Reserve]], created in 1995.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Rio Cautário (Estadual) – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX Rio Cautário (Estadual)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
