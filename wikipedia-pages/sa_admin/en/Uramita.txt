''' Uramita''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Uramita has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Am'') with moderate rainfall from January to March and heavy rainfall in the remaining months. The average temperature is around 28.4&nbsp;°C (83.1&nbsp;°F), and the average annual rainfall is 2,330&nbsp;mm (91.6 inches).
