'''Santa María Jacatepec''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] located in the state of [[Oaxaca]] 11&nbsp;km north of the [[Valle Nacional]].  "Jacatepec" comes from [[Nahuatl]] meaning 'on jackal hill.' 
It is part of the [[Tuxtepec District]] of the [[Papaloapan Region]], and is only 30 meters above sea level.<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20417a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México Oaxaca Santa Maria Jacatepec |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930021340/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20417a.htm |archivedate=30 September 2007 }}</ref>

==The town==

The town had its origins when the residents of San Juan Palantla needed to escape an epidemic that devastated the village. First they established a village they called Valle Real; however, because of the place's bad location, they moved on to several farming areas called San Pedro Ozumasín, San Mateo Yetla and Santa María Jacatepec. The last was established as a town in 1650 and then as at municipality in 1896.<ref name="EncMuc"/>

Near the town are 3 springs called '''Tzul Zul,''' '''Paraíso Escondido''' and '''Arroyo Blanco''' which have been converted into recreational parks.<ref name="MexDesOax">{{cite journal |last= Quintanar Hinojosa |first=Beatriz  |date=August 2007 |title=Ciudad de Oaxaca |journal=Guía México Desconocido: Oaxaca |volume= 137  |pages=87  |url= http://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx }}</ref>

==The municipality==
As municipal seat, Jacatepec has governing authority over the following communities:

Boca de Perro, Cerro Caballo, Cerro Concha, Cerro de Viento, Cinco de Oro, Colonia Emiliano Zapata, Ejido Adalberto Vélez, El Diamante, El Fortín II, El Guayabo, El Hule (La Hulera), El Porvenir, Emiliano Zapata, La Florida, La Joya de Santa María, Lacatepec, La Nueva Esperanza, La Nueva Soledad, La Reforma, La Soledad, Loma del Carmen, Macedonio Alcalá, Nuevo Faisán, Nuevo Málzaga, Rancho Alegre, Rancho Faisán, Rancho Gavilán, Rancho Manantial, San Agustín, San Lorenzo, San Martín, Santa Sofía, Vega del Sol, and Vuelta Abajo (Lucero Alto)
