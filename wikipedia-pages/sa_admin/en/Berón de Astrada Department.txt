'''Berón de Astrada Department''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Corrientes Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 2,294 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|804|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Berón de Astrada]], which is located around {{cvt|1,116|km}} from [[Capital Federal]].
