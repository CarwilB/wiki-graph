'''Santo Antônio de Leverger''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the southern part of the state of [[Mato Grosso]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 16,999 (2020 est.) in an area of {{convert|12261.29|km2|abbr=on}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/233JR Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is {{convert|141|m|abbr=on}}. 

The municipality contains the {{convert|1487|ha|adj=on}} [[Águas Quentes State Park]], the first protected area in Mato Grosso, which is known for the healing powers of its thermal waters.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|PES Águas Quentes – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=PES Águas Quentes|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/55|accessdate=2016-08-01}}</ref>
