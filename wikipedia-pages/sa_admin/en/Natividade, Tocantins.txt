'''Natividade''' (meaning the [[Nativity of Jesus|nativity]]) is a municipality ''([[Municipalities of Brazil|município]])'' in the state of [[Tocantins (state)|Tocantins]] in [[Brazil]]. 

== Geography ==
The estimated population in 2020 was 9,250, the area is 3,240&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The elevation is 323 m.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=171420|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Tocantins {{!}} Natividade|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-16}}</ref>

== Commemoration ==
The Roman Catholic commemoration of ''Senhor do Bonfim'' is the most traditional in the state of Tocantins.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Fé e tradição marcam Romaria do Senhor do Bonfim, maior evento religioso do Tocantins |url=https://www.to.gov.br/secom/noticias/fe-e-tradicao-marcam-romaria-do-senhor-do-bonfim-maior-evento-religioso-do-tocantins/4ylu56xk3t81 |access-date=2023-05-15 |website=www.to.gov.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref>
