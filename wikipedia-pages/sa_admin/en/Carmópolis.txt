'''Carmópolis''' is a ({{IPA|pt-BR|kɐɦˈmɔpolis}}) [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 16,937 (2020). Carmópolis covers {{convert|45.66|km2|mi2|abbr=on}} and has a population density of 294.15 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located {{convert|30.6|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/> Carmópolis borders on the municipalities of [[Japaratuba]], [[Rosário do Catete]], [[General Maynard]], and [[Santo Amaro das Brotas]], all in the state of Sergipe.
