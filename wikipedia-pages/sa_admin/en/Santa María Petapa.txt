'''Santa María Petapa ''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]].   
It is part of the [[Juchitán District]] in the west of the [[Istmo de Tehuantepec|Istmo de Tehuantepec region]].

==Name==
The name comes from the [[Zapotec language]] words meaning "four winds".
The town was given this name in 1525.<ref name=inf>{{cite web
 |title=Santa María Petapa
 |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/oaxaca/municipios/20427a.htm
 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México
 |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]
 |access-date=2010-07-15}}</ref>

After [[Coatzacoalcos|Espiritu Santu]] (now Coatzacoalcos) it is the oldest Spanish town in the [[Isthmus of Tehuantepec]].<ref name=williams>{{cite book
 |page=243
 |title=The isthmus of Tehuantepec: being the results of a survey for a railroad to connect the Atlantic and Pacific oceans, made by the scientific commission under the direction of Major J.G. Barnard, U.S. engineers. With a résumé of the geology, climate, local geography, productive industry, fauna and ..
 |author=John Jay Williams
 |publisher=D. Appleton & Company
 |year=1852}}</ref> 
Guigo or guego and petlatl means "river" and daa or petlatl means petate. It is original name of Petapa.

==Geography==
The town is located on a plain, bounded to the north and west by high mountains.<ref name=williams/>
It is at an elevation of 260 meters above sea level. The municipality covers an area of 145.44&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
The land is irrigated by the [[Petapa River]]. 
The climate is warm and humid, with abundant rainfall in the spring, summer and autumn.
===Flora and fauna===
The forests contain oak, mahogany, cedar, guaiac, rosewood, spring, Nopo, mamey, avocado, pine and cedar.
Wild fauna include Toucan, boar, deer, tepeizcuinte, coyote, raccoon, armadillo and skunk.<ref name=inf/>

An 1852 description said the town had once contained a population of 5,000 but had by that time been reduced to 1,300.<ref name=williams/>

As of 2005, the municipality had 3,476 households with a total population of 13,867 of which 3,294 spoke an indigenous language.
==Economy==
The main economic activity is agriculture, with crops of corn, beans, coffee and vegetables.  
Animal husbandry is also important, including cattle, pigs, goats, sheep, horses and poultry.
Logging is also practiced, with fine wood marketed for furniture manufacturing.<ref name=inf/>
