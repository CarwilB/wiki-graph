'''Pavão''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population as of 2020 was estimated to be 8,419 people living in a total area of 599&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/pavao/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the mesoregion of Vale do Mucuri and to the micro-region of Teófilo Otoni. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Geography==
Pavão is located at an elevation of 228 meters, 108&nbsp;km. northeast of [[Teófilo Otoni]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 545&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: [[Novo Oriente de Minas]], [[Crisólita]], [[Carlos Chagas, Minas Gerais|Carlos Chagas]], and [[Teófilo Otoni]].<ref>[http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ Citybrazil] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080613202846/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/teofilootoni/ |date=2008-06-13 }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, small industries, and agriculture. A large percentage of the population lives in the rural area and is engaged in subsistence farming. The GDP in 2005 was approximately {{BRLConvert|25|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 14 million Reais from services, 1 million Reais from industry, and 8 million Reais from agriculture. There were 651 rural producers on 79,000 hectares of land. Only 29 farms had tractors (2006). Approximately 2,000 persons were dependent on agriculture. Sugarcane, beans and corn were grown on a small scale. There were 45,000 head of cattle (2006). There was one bank (2007) and 295 automobiles (181 motorcycles), giving a ratio of 29 inhabitants per automobile.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>

==Health and education==
There were 5 health clinics and 1 hospital with 44 beds. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Teófilo Otoni, although highway connections are poor. Educational needs were met by 6 primary schools, 2 middle schools, and 3 pre-primary schools.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE]</ref>
