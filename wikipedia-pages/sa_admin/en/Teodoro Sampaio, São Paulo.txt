'''Teodoro Sampaio''' is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 23,273 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|1556|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/23ZNM Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 321 m.

== Geography ==
The municipality contains part of the {{convert|246800|ha|adj=on}} [[Great Pontal Reserve]], created in 1942.<ref>{{citation|accessdate=2016-11-13|ref={{harvid|Pontal do Paranapanema – IBAMA}}|language=pt
 |title=Pontal do Paranapanema|publisher=IBAMA: Instituto Brasileiro do Meio Ambiente e dos Recursos Naturais Renováveis
 |url=http://www.mpsp.mp.br/portal/page/portal/cao_urbanismo_e_meio_ambiente/bacias_hidrograficas/bh_22pontal_paranapanema/bh_21_vegetacao/Uni_cons_Est_Fed-muni22.htm}}</ref>
It contains the {{convert|33845|ha|adj=on}} [[Morro do Diabo State Park]], created in 1986.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Sobre o Parque – SAP}}|language=pt|title=Sobre o Parque|publisher=SAP: Sistema Ambiental Paulista|url=http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-morro-do-diabo/|accessdate=2016-11-07|url-status=dead|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20161027190357/http://www.ambiente.sp.gov.br/parque-morro-do-diabo|archivedate=2016-10-27}}</ref>
It also contains 36% of the {{convert|6677|ha|adj=on}} [[Mico Leão Preto Ecological Station]], established in 2002.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC Mico Leão Preto – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=ESEC Mico Leão Preto|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/591309|accessdate=2016-09-06}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-06-14|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012. The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-06-14|website=Telefônica}}</ref>

== Religion ==
{{see also|Religion in Brazil|Protestantism in Brazil|Roman Catholic Church in Brazil}}
[[Christianity]] is present in the city as follows:

=== Catholic Church ===
The Catholic church in the municipality is part of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Presidente Prudente]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.catholic-hierarchy.org/country/r10br.html|title=Sul 1 Region of Brazil [Catholic-Hierarchy]|access-date=2026-05-18|website=www.catholic-hierarchy.org}}</ref>

=== Protestant Church ===
The most diverse evangelical beliefs are present in the city, mainly [[Pentecostalism|Pentecostal]], including the [[Assembleias de Deus|Assemblies of God in Brazil]] (the largest evangelical church in the country),<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://confradesp.com.br/campos-eclesiasticos/|title=Campos Eclesiásticos|date=2018-12-10|access-date=2026-05-18|website=CONFRADESP|language=Portuguese}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://ad.org.br/locations/|title=Arquivos: Locais|access-date=2026-05-18|website=Assembleia de Deus Belém – Sede|language=Portuguese}}</ref> [[Christian Congregation in Brazil]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br/localidade|title=Localidade - Congregação Cristã no Brasil|access-date=2026-05-18|website=congregacaocristanobrasil.org.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> among others. These denominations are growing more and more throughout Brazil.
