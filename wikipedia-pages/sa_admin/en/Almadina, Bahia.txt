'''Almadina, Bahia''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]]. Almadina covers {{convert|245.27|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}, and has a population of 5,366 with a population density of 25 inhabitants per square kilometer.<ref name="i"/>

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|157,745|ha|adj=on}} [[Lagoa Encantada e Rio Almada Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1993.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA Lagoa Encantada e Rio Almada – INEMA}}|language=pt|title=APA Lagoa Encantada e Rio Almada|publisher=INEMA|url=http://www.inema.ba.gov.br/gestao-2/unidades-de-conservacao/apa/apa-lagoa-encantada-e-rio-almada/|access-date=2016-10-26|archive-date=2016-10-27|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161027123948/http://www.inema.ba.gov.br/gestao-2/unidades-de-conservacao/apa/apa-lagoa-encantada-e-rio-almada/|url-status=dead}}</ref>
