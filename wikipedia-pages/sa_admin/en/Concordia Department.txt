The '''Concordia Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento Concordia''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Entre Ríos Province|Entre Ríos]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the north-east of the province, beside the [[Uruguay River]]. The head city and largest settlement is [[Concordia, Entre Ríos|Concordia]].

The [[Salto Grande Dam|Salto Grande dam]] is located in the Concordia Department.

== Districts and municipalities ==
Concordia Department is divided into four districts: Moreira, Suburbios, Yeruá and Yuquerí. There are six municipalities: [[Concordia, Entre Ríos|Concordia]], [[Colonia Ayuí]], [[Estancia Grande, Entre Ríos|Estancia Grande]], [[La Criolla]], [[Los Charrúas]], and [[Puerto Yeruá]].<ref>{{Cite web | title=Archived copy}}</ref>

== Population ==
As of 2022, out of the 198,902 people who live in Concordia Department, 179,203 live in the head city of Concordia.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-07-16 |title=CENSO 2022: La poblaciòn de Entre Rios es de 1.415.097 y la de Concordia 179.203 |url=https://www.diariojunio.com.ar/censo-2022-la-poblacion-de-entre-rios-es-de-1-415-097-y-la-de-concordia-179-203/ |access-date=2025-03-23 |website=Diario Junio |language=es}}</ref>
