'''Veinticinco de Mayo''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of the province of [[Río Negro  Province|Río Negro ]] ([[Argentina]]).<ref name=":cp">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/php/argentina-admin.php|title=Argentina: Administrative Division|publisher=citypopulation}}</ref><ref>{{Cite book|url=http://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/441/download/13645|title=Cenro Latinoamericano y Caribeño de Demografía (CELADE)|publisher=World Bank|year=1970}}</ref> Its name translates to "Twenty Fifth of May," a national holday in Argentina cellabrating the [[May Revolution]].<ref>{{cite web |title=25 de mayo: Día de la Patria |url=https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/25-de-mayo-dia-de-la-patria |website=Gobierno de Argentina |access-date=June 20, 2025 |language=es |trans-title=May 25: Day of the Homeland}}</ref>
