'''Palestina de Los Altos''' (English: ''[[Palestine (region)|Palestine]] of the Highlands'') is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. Its inhabitants speak [[Mam language|Mam]] and Spanish.

Palestina de los Altos was originally called Toj Suj.  It is located between [[San Juan Ostuncalco]] and [[San Marcos, Guatemala|San Marcos]].

The land is mainly used for farming, primarily corn and potatoes, and pasture.
