'''Huaniqueo''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|40|km}} northwest of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Huaniqueo is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northern Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|2000|and(-)|2900|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Morelos, Michoacán|Morelos]] to the north, [[Chucándiro]] to the east, Morelia to the south, [[Coeneo]] to the southwest, and [[Jiménez, Michoacán|Jiménez]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16037.pdf |title=Huaniqueo, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|201.129|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.3% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Huaniqueo consists of tropical forest (23%), temperate forest (21%), and grassland (17%). Another 36% of the land is used for agriculture and 1% consists of urban areas.<ref name=compendium/> Huaniqueo is drained by seasonal streams: about 94% of the municipality is in the basin of the [[Ángulo River]], a tributary of the [[Lerma River]], while the easternmost 6% is in the basin of [[Lake Cuitzeo]].

Huaniqueo has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|14|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In the [[Purépecha language]], Huaniqueo has been translated to mean either "place where corn is roasted" or "place of those who give or bring a sign". It is believed that the first settlement was of [[Otomi]] origin and was conquered by the [[Tarascan state|Tarascans]] by 1450. After the arrival of the Spanish, the place was given the name Santa Marta Huaniqueo after its patron saint.<ref name=encyclopedia/>

Huaniqueo was made a ''partido'' of Michoacán in 1825 and one of its original municipalities in 1831. On 23 April 1861, {{ill|Epitacio Huerta|es}}, governor of Michoacán, renamed the municipal seat Huaniqueo de Morales in honour of the parish priest Juan José Pastor Morales, a supporter of the struggle for Mexican independence.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=81, 138–139 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4}}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is María Trinidad Vázquez Herrera.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Huaniqueo recorded a population of 7983 inhabitants living in 2369 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=037 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Huaniqueo |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=26 December 2020 |archive-date=17 August 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210817194658/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=037 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 8093 inhabitants in Huaniqueo.<ref name=survey/>

There are 27 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Huaniqueo de Morales is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 2566 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Huaniqueo is agriculture. The municipality is responsible for 15% of Mexico's total [[lentil]] production, behind only Coeneo which accounts for 57%.<ref>{{cite news |date=13 May 2020 |url=https://www.brunoticias.com/coeneo-y-huaniqueo-producen-la-mayoria-de-la-lenteja-mexicana/ |title=Coeneo y Huaniqueo producen la mayoría de la lenteja mexicana |language=es |publisher=Brunoticias |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref> There is low-level production of other crops and livestock for local consumption.<ref>{{cite news |author=Huaniqueo Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/2a-7219.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=12 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 2a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=25 January 2019 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>
