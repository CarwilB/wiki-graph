'''Trujillo District''' is one of eleven [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Trujillo Province, Peru|Trujillo]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>  This district is the heart of the city of [[Trujillo, Peru|Trujillo]], because it is the commercial and financial center of the city. The most important [[shopping mall|mall]]s, [[bank]]s and other business of the city are located in this district.
