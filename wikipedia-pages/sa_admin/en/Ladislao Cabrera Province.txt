'''Ladislao Cabrera''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the southern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[Oruro Department|Oruro]].  It is named after [[Ladislao Cabrera]] (1830 – 1921). Its seat is [[Salinas de Garci Mendoza]].

==Location==
Ladislao Cabrera is one of sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 19° 02' and 19° 56' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 35' and 67° 43' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders [[Sud Carangas Province]] in the north, [[Atahuallpa Province]] in the west, [[Potosí Department]] in the south, [[Eduardo Avaroa Province]] in the east, and [[Sebastián Pagador Province]] in the northeast.

The province extends over 100&nbsp;km from north to south, and 125&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Demographics==
The main language of the province is [[Aymara language|Aymara]], spoken by 90.5% of inhabitants, while 86.4% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 19.7% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]].

The population increased from 7,363 inhabitants (1992 census) to 11,698 (2001 census), an increase of 59%. 42.5% of the population are younger than 15 years old.

99.9% of the population have no access to electricity and 90.0% have no sanitary facilities.

82.4% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.1% in mining, 1.8% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], and 15.7% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

85.4% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 10.2% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]], which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
