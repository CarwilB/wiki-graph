'''Galibi''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Marowijne District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 741. Galibi is a [[tribe|tribal]] area inhabited by an [[Indigenous people|Indigenous]] population<ref name="census"/> of [[Kalina people|Kalina]] Amerindians.<ref name="vids">{{cite web|url=https://vids.sr/about/dorpsbesturen/|title=Dorpen en Dorpsbesturen|website=Vereniging van Inheemse Dorpshoofden|access-date=25 February 2021|language=nl|archive-date=22 June 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200622170430/https://vids.sr/about/dorpsbesturen/|url-status=dead}}</ref>

The town of Alusiaka used to be located near the banks of the [[Marowijne River]]. The [[Dutch West India Company]] was given 500 [[hectare]]s to grow [[coconut]]s; but, because of their mistreatment of the workers, the town was abandoned.<ref name="geschiedenis">{{cite web|url=https://dbnl.org/tekst/kemp009gesc02_01/kemp009gesc02_01_0007.php|title=Een geschiedenis van de Surinaamse literatuur. Deel 2|website=Digital Library for Dutch Literature|date=2002|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> In 1871 a 60-metre [[lighthouse]] was built near the entrance of the Marowijne; it was operated until 2012.<ref>{{Cite rowlett|sur|access-date=21 May 2020}}</ref>

==Christiaankondre==
{{anchor|Christiaankondre}}The village of Christiaankondre is located along the estuary of the [[Marowijne River]]. Together with neighbouring Langamankondre, it is often referred to as Galibi.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://dbnl.nl/tekst/helm003cult01_01/helm003cult01_01_0034.php |title=Cultureel mozaïek van Suriname|year=1977|author=[[Albert Helman]]|page=350|language=nl|location=Zutphen|publisher=Walburg Pers|isbn=9060110730}}</ref> The village has been named after Christiaan Pané whose father founded the village in the late 17th century. The economy is based on fishing, [[subsistence farming]], and tourism.<ref name="bibi">{{cite web|url=https://www.bibionderweg.nl/suriname/district-marowijne/ |title=District Marowijne|website=Bibi Onderweg|access-date=25 February 2021|language=nl}}</ref> The village has a school, a clinic,<ref name="plan"/> and a little zoo.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.columbusmagazine.nl/zuid-amerika/suriname/marowijne-en-galibi/foto/475774/galibi-zoo |title=Galibi Zoo|website=Columbus Magazine|access-date=25 February 2021|language=nl}}</ref> Corneliskondre is the larger of the two settlements.<ref name="vids"/> The village chief as of 2020 is Richardo Pané.<ref name="vids"/>

==Langamankondre==
{{anchor|Langamankondre}}Langamankondre is located next to Christiaankondre. The name is a mix of Dutch and [[Sranan Tongo]] and means "Long man's village" because the chief was very tall.<ref name="bibi"/> In 2016, Selowin Alamijawari was elected village chief.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.dbsuriname.com/2016/02/20/langamankondre-installeert-nieuw-dorpshoofd/ |title=Langamankondre installeert nieuw dorpshoofd|website=Dagblad Suriname|access-date=25 February 2021|language=nl}}</ref>

==Galibi Nature Reserve==
{{anchor|Galibi}}The main attraction is the Galibi Nature Reserve. There is a beach at the mouth of the Marowijne River on the Atlantic Ocean. This section also has a small zoo and tourist shop. Tourists come to Galibi largely to see the [[Leatherback sea turtle]]. Its females migrate here from all over the world (including places as distant as [[Costa Rica]], to lay their eggs in Suriname.<ref name="galibi_suriname"/> Almost half of the known population of leatherback turtles, lay their eggs in this area.<ref name="plan">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=STRUCTUUR ANALYSE DISTRICTEN  2009-2013|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=22 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>

Other turtles that frequent the beach are the [[Green sea turtle]], the [[Olive ridley sea turtle]], and the [[Hawksbill sea turtle]]<ref name="galibi_suriname">{{cite web|url=https://www.galibi-suriname.com/|title=Galibi Tour naar Galibi Zeeschildpadden|website=Galibi Suriname|access-date=19 May 2020|language=nl,en}}</ref> The area can only visited under strict supervision between February and June.<ref name="plan"/>

==Wia Wia Nature Reserve==
{{anchor|Wia Wia}}Founded in 1961, the Wia Wia Nature Reserve is located to the west of the Galibi Nature Reserve. It was also intended for the protection of the turtles; however, the focus has shifted to [[bird]] species,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://yellow.place/en/stichting-natuurbehoud-suriname-paramaribo-suriname|title=Stichting Natuurbehoud Suriname|website=Stichting Natuurbehoud Suriname via Yellow.place|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl|archive-date=6 May 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210506205759/https://yellow.place/en/stichting-natuurbehoud-suriname-paramaribo-suriname|url-status=dead}}</ref> because the beach has shifted to the west.<ref>{{cite web|last1=Ouboter|first1=Paul E.|year=2001 |url=https://www.researchgate.net/publication/262972705_Directory_of_protected_areas_of_Suriname |title=Directory of protected areas of Suriname|publisher=IBER|page=11}}</ref> The Wia Wia Nature Reserve spans an area of 36,000 hectares.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://surinameweetjes.weebly.com/natuur.html|title=Natuur|website=Suriname Weetjes|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}
</ref>

==Transport==
Galibi has a small boat (''korjaal'') connection to [[Albina, Suriname|Albina]]. The journey across the water takes about 1.5 hrs, and boats land near the villages of Christiaankondre and Langamankondre. There is a road connection to some parts of the resort Galibi, but not to the two main villages near the nature reserve. The residents believed that a road would have disturbed their peace and quiet.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.suriname-vakantiereis.nl/galibi-suriname|title=Galibi Suriname|website=Suriname Vakantiereis|access-date=21 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref>
