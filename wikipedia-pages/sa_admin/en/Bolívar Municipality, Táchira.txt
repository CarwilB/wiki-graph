'''Bolívar Municipality''' is one of the 29 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] that makes up the western [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Táchira (state)|Táchira]] and, according to a 2019 population projection by the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]], the municipality has a population of 66,081. The city of [[San Antonio del Táchira]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Bolívar Municipality.

==Name==
The municipality is one of several in Venezuela named "[[Bolívar Municipality]]" in honour of Venezuelan independence hero [[Simón Bolívar]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://bibliofep.fundacionempresaspolar.org/media/1038/gv_t5_c38_p240_343_lres_single_preview.pdf|title=Estado Anzoátegui|trans-title=Anzoátegui|lang=es|first=Ninoska|last=Cilento|website=BiblioFEP|publisher=[[Empresas Polar]]|date=|access-date=15 May 2026}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Based on the [[2011 Venezuelan census]], The population of the Bolívar Municipality was 62,923 people, accounting for 5.25% of the total population of the state of [[Táchira]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/20__t%C3%A1chira/|title=Táchira (State in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref> The majority (74.7%) of the municipality's population is in the city [[San Antonio del Táchira]], the [[municipal seat]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/tachira/2004__bol%C3%ADvar/|title=Urban Localities in Bolívar (Táchira, Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=25 June 2017|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>

By June 2019, official projections from the [[Statistics National Institute (Venezuela)|Venezuelan Statistics National Institute]] estimated the population of Bolívar as 66,081 people, representing an [[annual growth rate]] of 0.61% since 2011 and showing a population density of 323.9 inhabitants/km². However, these projections do not account for the impact of emigration linked to the country's recent economic and political circumstances.<ref name="CityPopulation.de">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/venezuela/admin/t%C3%A1chira/2004__bol%C3%ADvar/|title=Bolívar (Municipality in Venezuela)|website=CityPopulation.de|date=11 May 2019|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>

The [[gender]] distribution of the population was 49.4% men (30,431) and 50.6% women (31,199). The [[age distribution]] showed that the largest segment of the population was aged 15 to 64, comprising 67.9% of the people. Younger people aged 0 to 14 made up 26.3% of the population, while those aged 65 and older accounted for 5.8%. The municipality is mostly urbanized, with 91.3% of inhabitants (56,289) living in [[urban centers]] compared to just 8.7% (5,341) in [[rural areas]].<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

Ethnically, the municipality identified as predominantly [[White people]] (49.9%) and [[Mestizo]] (42.2%). Minority groups included 1.1% [[Afro-Venezuelans]] and 6.8% belonging to other ethnic groups. The [[literacy rate]] was 96.5%, with 1,810 inhabitants of Bolívar not able to read or write.<ref name="CityPopulation.de"/>

==Government==
The mayor of the Bolívar Municipality is Sandra Sánchez Prada of the [[United Socialist Party of Venezuela]] (PSUV), who was elected in November 2021 and sworn-in the following month as the first female mayor of the municipality.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://lanacionweb.com/politica/sandra-sanchez-toma-posesion-de-su-cargo-como-alcaldesa-de-bolivar/|title=Sandra Sánchez toma posesión de su cargo como alcaldesa de Bolívar|trans-title=Sandra Sánchez Takes Office as Mayor of Bolívar|lang=es|first=Jonathan|last=Maldonado|newspaper=[[La Nación]]|date=3 December 2021}}</ref> The municipality is divided into five [[parish]]es: Bolívar, Angarita, Palotal, Isaías Medina, and Juan Vicente Gómez.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://callejero-venezuela.openalfa.com/municipio-bolivar_tachira|title=  Callejero de Municipio Bolívar, Estado Táchira |trans-title=Street map of Bolívar Municipality, Táchira State|lang=es|website=Callejero Venezuela|access-date=17 May 2026}}</ref>
