'''Boa Esperança''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Its population was 15,092 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|429|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/A6U]</ref> Its average altitude is 140 meters above sea level.
