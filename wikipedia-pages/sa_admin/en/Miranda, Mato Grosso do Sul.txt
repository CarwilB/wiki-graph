'''Miranda''' is a [[List of municipalities in Mato Grosso do Sul|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]], [[Brazil]], named for its location on the [[Miranda River|river by the same name]]. Its population was 28,220 in 2020 and its area is 5,479&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
