'''Petoa''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in Honduras. Covering approximately {{cvt|203.5|km²}}, it had a population of 12,135 individuals in 2013.

== History ==
In the population census of 1791, Petoa is mentioned as the head of a curacy and it is represented as a municipality in the Quimistán District in the 1889 political maps.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Municipio de Petoa, Santa Bárbara|url=https://www.edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/16-Santa-Barbara/Petoa.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Petoa, Santa Bárbara|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-Petoa/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=16 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Petoa is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Santa Bárbara Department, Honduras|Santa Bárbara]] in northern Honduras.<ref name="Info">{{cite web|url=https://www.municipio.hn/municipio-petoa.html|title=Municipio de Petoa – ubicación y superficie|work=Municipio.hn|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref><ref name="Dat">{{cite web|url=https://www.mindat.org/feature-3603799.html|title=Petoa|work=Mineral Data|access-date=1 June 2024}}</ref> Covering approximately {{cvt|203.5|km²}}, it borders the municipalities of [[Quimistán]] to the north, [[Villanueva, Cortés]] to the east, and [[San Marcos, Santa Bárbara|San Marcos]] to the west.<ref name="Info"/><ref name="Red"/><ref name="Pop">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/santa_b%C3%A1rbara/1616__petoa/|title=Petoa|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> The municipality has a mean elevation of approximately {{cvt|253|m}} above sea level.<ref name="Info"/> The Chamelecón and Cacaulapa rivers pass through the region.<ref name="Red"/> The region has a [[tropical monsoon climate]].<ref name="Dat"/> The soil is prone to erosion particularly along the river banks, where embankments have been constructed.<ref>{{cite report|url=https://www.worldbank.org/en/results/2022/12/21/strengthening-climate-and-disaster-resilience-in-honduras|title=Disaster resilience in Honduras|date=21 December 2022|work=[[United Nations]]|access-date=1 June 2025}}</ref> The town is located about {{cvt|44|km}} from the [[Ramón Villeda Morales International Airport]].<ref name="Info"/>

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Petoa municipality had a population of 12,135. Of these, 83.87% were [[Mestizo]], 15.18% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.68% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.20% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.07% others.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ |title=Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea |access-date=2022-07-19 |archive-date=2021-06-16 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210616083136/https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ |url-status=live }}</ref> According to the INE projection, Petoa had a population of 13,121 in 2022, marking gradual growth since the 2013 census of 12,135. Population growth rate averaged around 1.92 % annually between 1950 and 2013.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://oee.unah.edu.hn/assets/Perfiles-Sociodemograficos/Santa-Barbara-16/Reporte-de-1615-Santa-Barbara-Petoa.pdf|title=Perfil sociodemográfico de Petoa (INE/UNAH, 2013–2022)|work=UNAH/INE|access-date=17 June 2025}}</ref> Gender distribution was almost equal and nearly 86% of the population was rural.<ref name="Pop"/>
