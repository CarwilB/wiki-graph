'''La Esperanza''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Quetzaltenango Department|Quetzaltenango department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It covers an area of 15.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 2465 metres. This municipality was founded on April 7, 1910.
