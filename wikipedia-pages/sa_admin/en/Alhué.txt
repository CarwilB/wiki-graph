'''Alhué''' ({{IPA|es|alˈwe}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] located in [[Melipilla Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]].

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Alhué spans an area of {{convert|845.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 4,435 inhabitants (2,343 men and 2,092 women). Of these, 2,593 (58.5%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 1,842 (41.5%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 10.5% (422 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Alhué is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcalde is Roberto Torres Huerta ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]). The communal council has the following members:<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>
* Danilo Salazar Morales ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])
* Hugo Lazo Segovia ([[Communist Party of Chile|PC]])     
* Roberto Aravena Miranda ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Mario Huerta Mora ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Luis Núñez Pérez ([[Humanist Party (Chile)|PH]])
* Nancy Cofré Quiroz ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Alhué is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Denise Pascal ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Gonzalo Uriarte ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 31st electoral district, (together with [[Talagante]], [[Peñaflor, Chile|Peñaflor]], [[El Monte, Chile|El Monte]], [[Isla de Maipo]], [[Melipilla]], [[María Pinto]], [[Curacaví]], [[San Pedro, Chile|San Pedro]] and [[Padre Hurtado]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
