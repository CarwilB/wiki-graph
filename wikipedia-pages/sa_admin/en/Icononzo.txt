'''Icononzo''' ({{IPA|es|ikoˈnonso}}) is a municipality located in the [[Tolima Department]] in [[Colombia]].

== Climate ==
The average temperature is 21&nbsp;°C.

== History ==
In 1875, the community of Guamitos was inherited by Don Vicente Reyes Daza.{{citation needed|date=July 2018}}

During the colonial era and by the year 1888, Adrian Lords Escobar, Guillermo Quijano, Alberto Williamson and others arrived and created a small community along the road leading to Guamitos. By Ordinance No. 3 of July 16, 1888, it was instituted as "Corregimiento Icononzo".{{citation needed|date=July 2018}} 

By 1892, Reyes Daza and Williamson began the assembly of the estates Canada and Scotland. Labor supply in the region was worsened by housing shortages. This situation led the ranch owners to donate land to address the problem, beginning the construction of 17 new homes. This resulted in the formation of a new town, which, because of their increasing wealth and development, was upgraded to a municipality by Ordinance No. 1915 of April 21, after it was returned to the department of Tolima in territories under the jurisdiction of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]].

Icononzo is known for a [[natural bridge]] over a deep canyon used in the civil turmoil known as [[La Violencia]] following the [[Bogotazo]]. The arch was painted by the Italian painter Gerolamo Fumagalli in the 19th century.<ref name="fumagalli">{{cite web |url=http://www.bridgemanart.com/asset/116140/Fumagalli-Gerolamo-19th-century/Natural-Bridge-Valle-d'Icononzo-colour-litho |title=Natural Bridge, Valle d'Icononzo | publisher=Bridgeman Art Library Ltd | access-date=10 January 2013}}</ref>

In 1989, the [[Atlantis (commune)|Atlantis commune]], founded in [[Burtonport]], Ireland, by Jenny James, relocated near to Icononzo.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Martinez |first1=Margarita |title=Killings End Commune's Innocence |url=https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2001-feb-11-mn-23995-story.html |access-date=2 August 2018 |work=Los Angeles Times |agency=Associated Press |date=11 February 2001}}</ref>
