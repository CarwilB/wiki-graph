'''La Reina''' ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: "The Queen"; distorted from Larraín) is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]] located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]], created in 1963 from an eastern portion of the [[Ñuñoa]] commune. It belongs to the [[Northeastern zone of Santiago de Chile]].

La Reina is a residential commune inhabited by mostly mid- to upper-mid income families and high-income groups. The [[Eulogio Sánchez Airport]] and Military Hospital are both located in the southern part of the commune.

==Demographics==
According to the 1999 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], La Reina spans an area of {{convert|23.4|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 96,762 inhabitants (44,293 men and 52,469 women), and the commune is an entirely [[urban area]]. The population grew by 4.7% (4,352 persons) between the 1990 and 1999 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/> The 2003 projected population was 96,551.<ref name="sir">{{cite web|url=http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |title=System of Regional Information |accessdate=13 September 2010 |work=Ministry of Planning of Chile |language=es |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20100823144707/http://sir.mideplan.cl/ |archivedate=23 August 2010 }}</ref>{{Failed verification|date=September 2010}}

==Administration==
As a commune, La Reina is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 mayor is José Manuel Palacios Parra ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]). The communal council has the following members:<ref>{{cite web|title=Concejales de La Reina|url=https://www.lareina.cl/concejales2021/|access-date=11 October 2024|website=lareina.cl|language=es}}</ref>

* Ursus Trotter Lecaros ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Luis Acevedo Sánchez (Ind/[[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* Rodolfo del Real Mihovilovic ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])

===Parliamentary Representation===  
La Reina belongs to Chile's [[Electoral divisions of Chile|Electoral District]] No. 11, along with the communes of [[Lo Barnechea]], [[Vitacura]], [[Las Condes]], and [[Peñalolén]], and to the [[Electoral divisions of Chile|VII Senatorial Constituency]] (Metropolitan Region of Santiago).

In the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile]], it is represented (2022-2026 term) by:  
* [[Catalina del Real|Catalina Del Real Mihovilovic]] ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* [[Guillermo Ramírez Diez]] ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])
* [[Francisco Undurraga|Francisco Undurraga Gazitúa]] ([[Political Evolution|EVOPOLI]])
* [[Tomás Hirsch|Tomás Hirsch Goldschmidt]] ([[Humanist Action|AH]])
* [[Cristián Araya Lerdo de Tejada]] ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* [[Gonzalo de la Carrera|Gonzalo De la Carrera Correa]] (Ind)

In the [[Senate of Chile]], it was represented by [[Carlos Montes Cisternas|Carlos Montes]] from the [[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]] and [[Manuel José Ossandón]] from [[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]] during the 2014-2022 period.
