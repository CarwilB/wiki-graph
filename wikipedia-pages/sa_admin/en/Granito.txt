'''Granito''' (''Granite'') is a city  in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. The population in 2025, according with [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 7,217 inhabitants and the total area is {{convert|521.69|km2|mi2}}.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Sertão]] Pernambucano
* Boundaries - [[Exu, Pernambuco|Exu]] and [[Moreilândia]]    ('''N''');  [[Parnamirim, Pernambuco|Parnamirim]]    ('''S''');  [[Serrita]]   ('''E''');   [[Bodocó]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 521.86&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 447 m
* Hydrography - [[Brigida River]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]]
* Climate - [[semi arid]] - ([[Sertão]]) hot
* Annual average temperature - 25.4 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 592&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Granito''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially creation of [[cattle]], [[sheep]], [[goats]], [[donkeys]], [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[maize|corn]].
