'''Tecate''' is a [[Municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] in the Mexican [[States of Mexico|state]] of [[Baja California]]. Its municipal seat is located in the city of [[Tecate]]. According to the 2020 census, it had a population of 108,440 inhabitants.<ref name="México en cifras">{{Cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/#tabMCcollapse-Indicadores|title=México en cifras|date=January 2016}}</ref> The municipality has an area of 3,079.0&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (1,188.8 sq mi).

==Boroughs==
The municipality is administratively subdivided into 6 boroughs, in addition to the municipal seat, [[Tecate]].
* [[Nueva Colonia Hindú, Baja California|Nueva Colonia Hindú]]
* [[Colonia Luis Echeverría]]
* [[Heroes del Desierto, Baja California|Heroes del Desierto]]
* [[Mi Ranchito, Baja California|Mi Ranchito]]
* [[Valle de Las Palmas]]
* [[La Rumorosa]]

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 108,440.<ref name="México en cifras">{{Cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/#tabMCcollapse-Indicadores|title=México en cifras|date=January 2016}}</ref>

As of 2010, the city of [[Tecate]] had a population of 64,764.<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Tecate|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=02&mun=003|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=April 23, 2014|archive-date=October 9, 2017|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171009143812/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=02&mun=003|url-status=dead}}</ref> Other than the city of Tecate, the municipality had 964 localities, the largest of which (with 2010 populations in parentheses) were: [[Lomas de Santa Anita, Baja California|Lomas de Santa Anita]] (6,604), [[Nueva Colonia Hindú, Baja California|Nueva Colonia Hindú]] (4,431), classified as urban, and [[Cereso del Hongo, Baja California|Cereso del Hongo]] (4,278), [[Luis Echeverría Álvarez (El Hongo), Baja California|Luis Echeverría Álvarez (El Hongo)]] (2,411), classified as urban, and [[Hacienda Tecate, Baja California|Hacienda Tecate]] (1,871), [[Valle de las Palmas, Baja California|Valle de las Palmas]] (1,860), [[La Rumorosa]] (1,836), [[Maclovio Herrera (Colonia Aviación), Baja California|Maclovio Herrera (Colonia Aviación)]] (1,219), [[Alfonso Garzón (Granjas Familiares), Baja California|Alfonso Garzón (Granjas Familiares)]] (1,188), and [[El Mirador, Baja California|El Mirador]] (1,171), classified as rural.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
