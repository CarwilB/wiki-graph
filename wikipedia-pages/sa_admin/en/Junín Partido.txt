'''Junín Partido''' is a county (''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'') in the {{province|Buenos Aires|Argentina}}. Its population is 88,664 inhabitants ({{census-ar|2001}}) and the population density reaches 39.4 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>. Its [[administrative seat]] is the city of [[Junín, Buenos Aires|Junín]].

==Geography==
Junín occupies an area of {{convert|2263|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}},

It is bounded on the northwest by General Arenales, on the northeast by Rojas, on the east by Chacabuco, on the southeast by Bragado, on the south by General Viamonte, on the southwest by Lincoln and on the west by Leandro N. Alem.
