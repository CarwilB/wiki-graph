'''Morazán''' ({{IPA|es|moɾaˈsan}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[El Progreso Department]] of [[Guatemala]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.eleconomista.net/economia/Guatemala-Canicula-afectara-a-agricultores-de-tres-regiones-20170721-0130.html|title=Guatemala: Canícula afectará a agricultores de tres regiones|first=Carlos|last=Álvarez|work=ElEconomista.net|date=21 July 2017|access-date=28 February 2026|language=es}}</ref>

Morazán is situated at 349.5 m above [[sea level]], and covers a terrain of 329&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
Employment is overgrown now of many opportunities there are in Morazán. Agriculture is the dominant trade, and much of the population works family-owned fields. Opportunities for education have now been more available its higher from kindergarten to learning being a teacher. There are many sports to be done. Friendly people and many stored to search around.

One annual festival "Feria del pueblo"is celebrated from December 22 to December 26 each year.
