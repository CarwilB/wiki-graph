'''Juruá''' ({{IPA|pt|ʒuɾuˈa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 15,106 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/jurua/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 19,400&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2014-08-03 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

The municipality contains 62% of the {{convert|187982|ha|adj=on}} [[Baixo Juruá Extractive Reserve]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX do Baixo Juruá – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=RESEX do Baixo Juruá|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/1151|accessdate=2016-10-22}}</ref>
The municipality contains about 12% of the [[Tefé National Forest]], created in 1989.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|FLONA de Tefé – ISA}}|language=Portuguese
 |title=FLONA de Tefé|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/6656|accessdate=2016-10-19}}</ref>
