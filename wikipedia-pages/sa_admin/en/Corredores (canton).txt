'''Corredores''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Puntarenas Province|Puntarenas]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is Ciudad Neily in [[Corredor District|Corredor]] district.

== History ==
Corredores was created on 19 October, 1973 by decree 5373.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

In October 2023, [[Temporary Migrant Care Center|CATEM]], a migrant detention and processing facility opened in Corredores.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-09-27 |title=Child-Friendly Safe Spaces for migrant children and adolescents |url=https://www.unicef.org/lac/en/blog/child-friendly-spaces-for-migrant-children-adolescents-in-costa-rica |access-date=2025-04-20 |website=UNICEF}}</ref> In February 2025, it was used to detain migrants [[Rendition (law)|renditioned]] to Costa Rica from the [[United States]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-02-20 |title=Foreigners from  Asia and India deported from the U.S. to stay in Costa Rica for up to six weeks |url=https://amcostarica.com/Foreigners%20from%20%20Asia%20and%20India%20deported%20from%20the%20U.S.%20to%20stay%20in%20Costa%20Rica%20for%20up%20to%20six%20weeks%20022025.html |access-date=2025-04-20 |website=AM Costa Rica}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Corredores has an area of {{formatnum:620.6}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:58}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The lowland canton shares its eastern border with [[Panama]]. The Conte River marks the western limit. The northern boundary runs through the Zapote Ridge, one of the coastal mountain ranges.
