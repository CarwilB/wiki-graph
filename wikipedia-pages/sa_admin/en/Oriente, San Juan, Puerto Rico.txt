'''Oriente''' is one of the 18 [[Barrios of San Juan, Puerto Rico|barrios]] of the municipality of [[San Juan, Puerto Rico|San Juan]], [[Puerto Rico]]. It is composed of 3 subbarrios: Borinquen, López Sicardó, and San José. Before 1951, it was a [[Barrios of Puerto Rico|barrio]] of the former [[municipality]] of [[Rio Piedras, Puerto Rico|Rio Piedras]]. In [[2010 United States Census|2010]], it had a population of 31,374. It is surrounded by the barrios of [[Sabana Llana Norte, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Sabana Llana Norte]] to the east, [[Sabana Llana Sur, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Sabana Llana Sur]] to the south, [[Hato Rey Central, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Central]] and [[Hato Rey Norte, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Hato Rey Norte]] to the west, and [[Santurce, San Juan, Puerto Rico|Santurce]] barrio and the [[Laguna San José (Puerto Rico)|San Jose Lagoon]] to the north.<ref name="Pico_1969">{{cite book |last1=Picó |first1=Rafael |last2=Buitrago de Santiago |first2=Zayda |last3=Berrios |first3=Hector H. |title=Nueva geografía de Puerto Rico: física, económica, y social, por Rafael Picó.
