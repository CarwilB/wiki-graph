'''Gualberto Villarroel''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[La Paz Department, Bolivia]]. Its capital is [[San Pedro de Curahuara de Carangas]].

== Subdivision ==
The province is divided into three [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into cantons.
