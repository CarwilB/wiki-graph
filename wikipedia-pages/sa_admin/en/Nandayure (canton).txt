'''Nandayure''' is a [[Cantons of Costa Rica|canton]] in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref> The head city is in [[Carmona District|Carmona]] district.

== History ==
Nandayure was created on 9 October 1961 by decree 2826.<ref name="crevoterritorial" />

On September 5, 2012, Nandayure was struck by a [[2012 Costa Rica earthquake|magnitude 7.6 earthquake]], destroying houses in the canton.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/eventpage/usc000cfsd |title=M7.6 - 12km ENE of Hojancha, Costa Rica |date=September 5, 2012 |publisher=[[United States Geological Survey]] |access-date=October 3, 2012}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Sismo Destruyó Viviendas en Varios Cantones de Guanacaste y Alajuela |author=Mata Blanco, Alonso |url=http://www.nacion.com/2012-09-06/ElPais/sismo-destruyo-viviendas-en-varios-cantones-de-guanacaste-y-alajuela--.aspx |newspaper=[[La Nación (San José)|La Nación]] |date=September 6, 2012 |access-date=October 3, 2012 |language=es |trans-title=Earthquake Destroyed Homes in Several Cantons of Guanacaste and Alajuela |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120925071449/http://www.nacion.com/2012-09-06/ElPais/sismo-destruyo-viviendas-en-varios-cantones-de-guanacaste-y-alajuela--.aspx |archive-date=September 25, 2012 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
Nandayure has an area of {{cvt|565.59|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and a mean elevation of {{formatnum:215}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

The canton encompasses a piece of the coastline of the [[Gulf of Nicoya]] near the mouth of the [[Tempisque River]], including Berrugate Island. It cuts across the center of the [[Nicoya Peninsula]] to the Pacific coast between the Ora River to the north and the Bongo River to the south.
