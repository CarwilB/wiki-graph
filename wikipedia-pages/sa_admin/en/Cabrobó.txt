'''Cabrobó''' is a city in the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Pernambuco]], 536&nbsp;km away from the state's capital, [[Recife]]. The city is located just to the north of a section of the [[São Francisco River]] that contains many archipelagos.

==History==
The [[Truká language|Truká]] people had historically occupied the [[:pt:Ilha da Assunção|Ilha da Assunção]] archipelago of the [[São Francisco River]] in Cabrobó municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://pib.socioambiental.org/pt/Povo:Truk%C3%A1 |title=Truká |website=Povos Indígenas no Brasil |language=pt |access-date=29 January 2020}}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in '''Cabrobó''' are based in general commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[cattle]], [[pig]]s; and plantations of [[onion]]s, [[cannabis]], [[water melon]]s and [[rice]].
