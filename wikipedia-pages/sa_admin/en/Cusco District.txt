'''Cusco''' is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] in the northern [[Cusco Province]] within the [[Cusco Region]] of [[Peru]]. It is bordered by districts of [[Ccorca District|Ccorca]] and [[Poroy District|Poroy]] on the west, the provinces of [[Anta Province|Anta]], the [[Calca Province|Calca]], and [[Urubamba Province|Urubamba]] on the north, the [[San Jerónimo District, Cusco|San Jerónimo District]] on the east, and the districts of [[Santiago District, Cusco|Santiago]] and [[San Sebastián District, Cusco|San Sebastián]] to the south.
