'''Queilén''' is a [[List of towns in Chile|town]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] in [[Chiloé Province]], in the [[Archipelago of Chiloé]] in southern [[Chile]]. It covers a portion of the southeast of the Greater [[Chiloé Island|Island of Chiloé]], between the communes of [[Chonchi]], [[Quellón]], [[Tranqui Island]] and [[Acuy Island]]. For the 2002 census, the population of the town had grown to 1,912 inhabitants, according to INE; the municipality had a population of 5,138.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.viajes-a.net/ciudad/Queilen-8446.htm La ciudad de Queilén] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090414082653/http://www.viajes-a.net/ciudad/Queilen-8446.htm |date=April 14, 2009 }}</ref>

Its capital is the town of the same name, known on the island for its beaches and views of the [[Corcovado National Park (Chile)|Gulf of Corcovado]] and the [[Andes]]. It has a quieter atmosphere than the larger towns of the province ([[Ancud]], [[Castro, Chile|Castro]] and [[Quellón]]). It was founded by Anthony Cipriano in the early nineteenth century.

This community has with the Public Library No. 333, which since 2002 joined the program Biblioredes [[Web service|services]], allowing free access to Internet and [[Information technology|technology]] services closer to all inhabitants from the community.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-24998.html Antecedentes de Queilén] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081204192802/http://www.subdere.gov.cl/1510/propertyvalue-24998.html |date=December 4, 2008 }}</ref>

==History==
The exact date of the foundation of Queilén is unknown. In 1703 there was an [[Arab diaspora|Arab community]] with a chapel and in 1771 a new church was built. [[Jesuit missionaries]] and [[Spania|Spanish]] settlers, granted encomiendas in the area, gradually occupied Queilén.

Between 1818 and 1821 the [[Spanish Chilean|Spanish]] cavalry [[Captain (land)|captain]] Antonio Cipriano, explored the coastline and drew up maps, preparing the population for military resistance to the expected [[Chile]]an landings. In 1890, in recognition of his accomplishments, the area was named after captain Grille. It seems that the community never accepted the name "Puerto Grille" and simply continued to use Queilén.

==Architecture==
In 1867, Queilén was a little Chilote port with a church. Around 1924 it comprised a few houses, [[chapel]], [[post office]], [[customs]], [[telegraph]], [[civil registry]] and state schools. In 1937 a fire destroyed 15 houses, this, added to the isolation of the community, contributed to emigration towards [[Castro, Chile|Castro]] and [[Ancud]]. There was a gradual degeneration of the visual quality of the town, which is now being reversed by the incentive of tourism.

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Queilén spans an area of {{convert|223.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 5,138 inhabitants (2,663 men and 2,475 women). Of these, 1,912 (37.2%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 3,226 (62.8%) in [[rural area]]s. The population grew by 3.8% (186 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Services==
Queilen city have basic services like hotel, cash dispenser, hospital, cultural center, museum, library, gas station, restaurants, supermarkets, telephone service, police office, aerodrome, port and pier.

==Administration==
As a commune, Queilén is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Carlos Gómez Miranda ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Queilén is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gabriel Ascencio (PDC) and Alejandro Santana ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) as part of the 58th electoral district, (together with [[Castro, Chile|Castro]], [[Ancud]], [[Quemchi]], [[Dalcahue]], [[Curaco de Vélez]], [[Quinchao]], [[Puqueldón]], [[Chonchi]], [[Quellón]], [[Chaitén]], [[Hualaihué]], [[Futaleufú, Chile|Futaleufú]] and [[Palena, Chile|Palena]]). The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Camilo Escalona Medina ([[Socialist Party of Chile|PS]]) and Carlos Kuschel Silva (RN) as part of the 17th senatorial constituency (Los Lagos Region).
