'''Chaitén''' ({{IPAc-en|tʃ|aɪ|ˈ|t|ɛ|n}}, {{respell|chy|TEN}}) is a [[Chile]]an [[List of towns in Chile|town]], [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and former capital of the [[Palena Province]] in [[Los Lagos Region]]. The town is north of the mouth of [[Yelcho River]], on the east coast of the [[Gulf of Corcovado]]. The town is strategically close to the northern end of the [[Carretera Austral]], where the highway goes inland. The [[Desertores Islands]] are part of the commune.

==Evacuation==

[[File:Vista aérea Chaitén.jpg|thumb|250px|left|The river's new course shown in the center of the image. North is to the left.]]
[[Image:Chaitén.jpg|thumb|left|Following the eruption of the [[Chaitén (volcano)|Chaitén volcano]], a [[lahar]] destroyed much of the town of Chaitén.]]

The town was evacuated in May 2008 when the [[Chaitén (volcano)|Chaitén volcano]] erupted for the first time in more than 9,000 years.<ref>The [[Global Volcanism Program]] reports that previously assessed radiocarbon dating of the last lava flow from Chaitén has suggested that the volcano last erupted in 7420&nbsp;BC, plus or minus 75 years. ([http://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/NaturalHazards/natural_hazards_v2.php3?img_id=14815 NASA, "Earth Observatory Natural Hazards] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080510093549/http://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/NaturalHazards/natural_hazards_v2.php3?img_id=14815 |date=May 10, 2008 }})</ref><ref>{{cite journal

The town was completely flooded on May 12, 2008, after a [[lahar]] caused the banks of the Blanco River to overflow about {{convert|200|m|abbr=on}} on each side.<ref>[http://www.volcano.si.edu/world/volcano.cfm?vnum=1508-041&volpage=weekly Global Volcanism Program | Chaitén | SI / USGS Weekly Volcanic Activity Reports<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> Over the subsequent weeks, the river excavated a new course through Chaitén, completely destroying a significant part of it by July 2008. This process is still ongoing; it is unclear how extensive the damage will ultimately be. Some defensive work has been undertaken by the government, but they ultimately decided to abandon the town, relocating and compensating all residents.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.lanacion.cl/prontus_noticias_v2/site/artic/20090129/pags/20090129222600.html |title=La Nacion "Gobierno no reconstruirá Chaitén y prepara relocalización", 2009 Jan 30 |access-date=2009-01-30 |archive-date=2016-01-21 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160121131117/http://www.lanacion.cl/prontus_noticias_v2/site/artic/20090129/pags/20090129222600.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> The provincial capital was provisionally moved to [[Futaleufú, Chile|Futaleufú]] after the eruption breakout, but the capital shift was later on declared permanent. The future of the town has become a matter of political controversy in Chile.

==Reconstruction of Chaitén==
On February 25, 2009, the government announced plans to rebuild Chaitén {{convert|10|km|abbr=on}} north of its current location in a coastal place known as Santa Bárbara or "Fandango Norte".<ref>[http://diario.elmercurio.com/2009/02/26/nacional/nacional/noticias/9B9D95FC-E45A-4ED1-9A23-47CC95C6A148.htm ] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090301094806/http://diario.elmercurio.com/2009/02/26/nacional/nacional/noticias/9B9D95FC-E45A-4ED1-9A23-47CC95C6A148.htm |date=March 1, 2009 }}</ref> On March 3, 2009, construction work on the new town's administrative facilities started.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://latercera.com/contenido/654_105748_9.shtml |title=Comienzan los trabajos para la construcción de centro cívico de nueva Chaitén |publisher=Latercera.com |date=2010-02-25 |accessdate=2010-03-01 |archive-date=2011-07-13 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110713185812/http://latercera.com/contenido/654_105748_9.shtml |url-status=dead }}</ref>

On April 9, 2011, president [[Sebastián Piñera]] announced a program to rebuild the town on its existing northern area, reversing plans by the previous administration to move the town to a different location farther north.<ref>[http://www.emol.com/noticias/nacional/2011/04/09/475139/pinera-oficializa-refundacion-de-chaiten-en-sector-norte-de-la-ciudad.html Piñera oficializa refundación de Chaitén en sector norte de la ciudad], El Mercurio, April 9, 2011.</ref>
