'''Ataco''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Tolima Department|Tolima]] department of [[Colombia]]. The population of the municipality was 13,470 in the 2018 census.<ref name="Censo"></ref>

The Colombian state has been accused of human rights violations in Ataco[http://www.nadir.org/nadir/initiativ/agp/free/colombia/txt/2003/1030mining_resources.htm].
