'''Nova Canaã''' is a [[municipality (Brazil)|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Bahia]] in the [[Nordeste|North-East region]] of [[Brazil]].<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2008">{{cite web |url=https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/divisao_territorial/2008/dtb_2008.zip |title=Divisão Territorial do Brasil |date=July 1,  2008 |publisher=Divisão Territorial do Brasil e Limites Territoriais, Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística (IBGE) |access-date=December 17, 2009|language=pt}}</ref> The population is 16,472 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|805|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/nova-canaa/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
