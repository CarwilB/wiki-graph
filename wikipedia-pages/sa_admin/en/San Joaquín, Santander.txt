'''San Joaquín''' is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]]. It covers an area of 143.1 square kilometers, at an average elevation of 2,000 meters above the sea level.<ref>{{Cite web |title=San Joaquín topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-gb.topographic-map.com/map-n75t51/San-Joaqu%C3%ADn/?center=6.46839,-72.8671&zoom=12 |access-date=2024-12-22 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref> In the year 2018, it had a population of 2,241.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Urban Localities in San Joaquín (Santander, Colombia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map, Location, Weather and Web Information |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/colombia/santander/68682__san_joaqu%C3%ADn/ |access-date=2024-12-22 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
