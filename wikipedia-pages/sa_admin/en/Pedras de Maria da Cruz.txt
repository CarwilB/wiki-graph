'''Pedras de Maria da Cruz''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]] in [[Brazil]].  As of 2020 the population was 12,212 in an area of {{cvt|1520|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/pedras-de-maria-da-cruz/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation of the municipal seat is 476 meters.  It became a municipality in 1993.  The postal code (CEP) is 39492-000 and it belonged to the statistical microregion of [[Januária]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/pedrasmariacruz/|title=CityBrazil - Minas Gerais - Pedras de Maria da Cruz|access-date=2008-05-23|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204090413/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/pedrasmariacruz/|archive-date=2008-02-04|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Pedras de Maria da Cruz is located on the right bank of the [[São Francisco River]].  It is connected [[Januária]] by paved BR-135.  The distance is 20&nbsp;km.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.estradas.com.br/new/header_sites/mapas.asp |title=Mapa de Estradas |access-date=2008-05-23 |archive-date=2008-05-12 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080512043839/http://www.estradas.com.br/new/header_sites/mapas.asp |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The economy is based on agriculture with emphasis on cattle raising.  There were 51,000 head in 2006.  The main agricultural crops were pineapple, sugarcane, bananas, mangoes, coconuts, and corn.  The GDP was R$27,897,000 in 2005.  Irrigation from the São Francisco River allows for production of tropical fruits.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php|title=IBGE - Cidades}}</ref>

This municipality is extremely isolated from major population centers and suffers from drought and poor soils.  It is one of the poorest in the state and in the country.  Like many impoverished districts it fought for municipal autonomy to apply for government assistance programs.
