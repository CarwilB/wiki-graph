'''Córdoba''' is a municipality in the eastern part of the department of [[Quindío Department|Quindío]], [[Colombia]].  It's located 24&nbsp;km southeast of the departmental capital [[Armenia, Colombia|Armenia]].

== History ==
Córdoba was founded in 1927 by Jesús García and Jesús Buitrago, and became a municipality in 1966 when it split from [[Calarcá]]. In 2023 it had an estimated population of 5,888.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Telefónicas |first=Telencuestas-Encuestas |title=Cuántos habitantes tiene Córdoba, Quindío en 2023 |url=https://telencuestas.com/censos-de-poblacion/colombia/2023/quindio/cordoba |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=Telencuestas |language=es}}</ref> By population it's the third smallest municipality in Quindío, after [[Buenavista, Quindío|Buenavista]] and [[Pijao, Quindío|Pijao]].

== Geography ==
Córdoba is bounded to the north by the municipality of [[Calarcá, Quindío|Calarcá]], to the south by [[Pijao, Quindío|Pijao]], to the west by [[Buenavista, Quindío|Buenavista]], and to the east by the department of [[Tolima Department|Tolima]].

=== Climate ===
The average temperature of Córdoba is 20&nbsp;°C with a [[subtropical highland climate]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Córdoba - Gobernación del Quindio |url=https://quindio.gov.co/cordoba |access-date=2023-09-27 |website=quindio.gov.co}}</ref>

== Tourism ==
Córdoba is home to the '''National Bamboo and Guadua Investigation Center''' ({{Langx|es|Centro Nacional para el Estudio Bambú-Guadua}}). Open to the public, the center shows the potential uses of [[guadua]] as a material for construction, furniture and decoration.  It undertakes research on the cultivation of new varieties of guadua.

The '''Bamboo and Guadua Festival''' (Spanish: ''Fiesta del Bambú y la Guadua'') is held in Córdoba in June.[[Image:Guadua furniture 2005-06-16.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Guadua furniture on display at the National Bamboo and Guadua Investigation Center in Córdoba, [[Quindío Department|Quindío]].]]
