'''Carapo District''' is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the  [[Huanca Sancos Province]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is Wayta Wayta at {{convert|4800|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Huancasancos Province (Ayacucho Region)</ref>

* Misa Rumi
* Parya Muqu
* Pincha Urqu
* Qala Qala
* Urqu Pata

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. 84.37% of the population speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]] as a first language, while the remaining 15.46% are first language [[Spanish language|Spanish]] speakers, according to the [[2007 Peru Census]].<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población: Idioma o lengua con el que aprendió hablar (in Spanish)</ref>
