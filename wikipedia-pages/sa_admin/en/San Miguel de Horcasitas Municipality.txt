'''San Miguel de Horcasitas''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=-|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=4 January 2010}}</ref>
The municipal seat is at [[San Miguel de Horcasitas]].

The municipal area is 1,768.45 km<sup>2</sup>. The population was in 2020 was 10,729.<ref>{{cite web |title=San Miguel de Horcasitas: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/san-miguel-de-horcasitas |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=3 January 2025}}</ref>

Neighboring municipalities are:  [[Rayón Municipality, Sonora|Rayón]] to the northeast, [[Ures Municipality|Ures]] to the east, [[Hermosillo Municipality|Hermosillo]] to the south, and [[Carbó Municipality|Carbó]] to the northwest.
