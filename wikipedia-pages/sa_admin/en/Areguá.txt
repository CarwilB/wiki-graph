It is known for its production of [[strawberry]] products, [[pottery]], [[colonial architecture]] and historic [[cobblestone]] streets.

The district lies between the [[Ypacaraí Lake]] and the basin it forms with the [[Salado River (Paraguay)|Salado River]] to the East and hills to the West.

==Etymology==
[[File:Cerro koi3.jpg|thumb|250px|right|Koi Hill]]
[[File:Playa aregua.jpg|thumb|250px|Areguá Beach]]
Areguá is thought to come from [[Classical Guarani]] ''ariguâ·r'' ("[person/people] from above, from up there"), refererring to the area's high altitude and its first inhabitants from the [[Mbyá Guaraní people|Mbya Guarani]] tribes.<ref>{{Cite web |last=lanaciongn |date=2020-01-19 |title=Diario HOY {{!}} Areguá, herencia de ancestros, donde del barro se hace arte |url=https://www.hoy.com.py/arte/aregua-herencia-de-ancestros-donde-del-barro-se-hace-arte |access-date=2023-06-03 |website=Diario HOY |language=es}}</ref>

==History==
Areguá was founded by [[Domingo Martínez de Irala]] in 1538.<ref>Macgregor J. [https://books.google.com/books?id=nEw6AAAAcAAJ&dq=aregua+paraguay&pg=PA1028 ''The Progress of America from the Discovery by Columbus to the Year 1846''] Whittaker & Company, 1847 p. 1028</ref> Early settlers, numbering about 200, lived close to the church. They were [[Order of the Blessed Virgin Mary of Mercy|Mercedarian]]s who arrived with the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spanish]] to raise cattle. African slaves were put to work on these farms.<ref>Walker S. S. [https://books.google.com/books?id=EJzHiqBPJCoC&dq=aregua+paraguay&pg=PA320 ''African Roots/American Cultures: Africa in the Creation of the Americas''] Rowman & Littlefield, 2001 p. 320 {{ISBN|0742501655}}</ref>

It was promoted to district status on November 12, 1862 by [[Francisco Solano López]].

By the end of the 19th century, Areguá had become a tourist destination to which many writers, artists and intellectuals from nearby areas retreated in the summer.

In 2019, it was designed as a [[City of Crafts and Folk Arts|city of crafts and folk arts]] by the [[UNESCO]] as part of its [[Creative Cities Network]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Portal Oficial del Ministerio de Relaciones Exteriores de la República del Paraguay :: Areguá fue designada por la UNESCO como Ciudad Creativa |url=https://www.mre.gov.py/index.php/noticias-de-embajadas-y-consulados/aregua-fue-designada-por-la-unesco-como-ciudad-creativa |access-date=2023-05-29 |website=www.mre.gov.py}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Areguá {{!}} Creative Cities Network |url=https://en.unesco.org/creative-cities/aregua |access-date=2023-05-29 |website=en.unesco.org}}</ref>

===Climate===
Areguá's climate is [[temperate climate|temperate]]. Maximum summer temperatures reach 40°C. Average winter temperatures are 0°C. The average annual rainfall is 1377mm distributed throughout the year.

==Demography==
Areguá's population is about 67,847. Of these residents, 33,870 are female and 33,977 are male.<ref>[http://countryeconomy.com/demography/population/paraguay Paraguay population] Country Economy website. 2008.</ref>

==Culture==
Areguá's economy is based on [[strawberry]] farming. The town is also known for its pottery, which is an emergent industry in the region.

Every year, the [[Strawberry Expo]] takes place in Areguá. It consists of a month-long strawberry festival where farmers and producers sell strawberries and strawberry products (from juice, cakes and liquors to ice cream, [[Empanada|empanadas]] and [[Pastafrola|pastafrolas]]).<ref>{{Cite web |title=Expo Frutilla |url=https://visitaparaguay.com.py/lugar/462/expo-frutilla |access-date=2023-05-24 |website=Visita Paraguay |language=en}}</ref>

The district's [[Patronal festivals in Paraguay|patronal festival]] is February 2 ([[Virgin of Candelaria]]). Celebrations last around ten days, these include: a novena, a folk festival, serenatas and a procession that takes the image of the Virgin of Candelaria to schools, businesses and chapels, among other places.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Luego de larga ausencia, la Virgen reúne a devotos - Fiesta Patronal San Blas y Virgen de la Candelaria 2022 - ABC Color |url=https://www.abc.com.py/edicion-impresa/suplementos/comercial/fiesta-patronal-ita-aregua-capiata/2022/02/02/luego-de-larga-ausencia-la-virgen-reune-a-devotos/ |access-date=2023-06-03 |website=www.abc.com.py |language=es}}</ref>

==Places of interest==
Areguá has a historic area centred on ''Avenida del Lago'' (Lake Avenue) which runs from a hilltop church to the [[Ypacaraí Lake]]. In 1997, the Paraguayan parliament declared it a National Heritage Area.

Isla Valle, the local ecological organization, regulates fishing in the lake.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://islavalle.com.py/ |title=Islavalle |access-date=2017-07-20 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170911141804/http://islavalle.com.py/ |archive-date=2017-09-11 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

Another attraction is the Centro Artesanal de la Cuenca. At the Center, craftsmen from the region display locally made pottery and crafts. Aregua also has art galleries, including Guggiari Arte, Luis Cogliolo Galería de Arte, Paseo La Candelaria, Areguá pesebres, El Cántaro, and Museo las Margaritas.

The Koi and Chorori hills are a geologic attraction. Koi Hill is known for its formations of hexagonal sandstone.<ref>Eckel E. B. and Milton C. [https://books.google.com/books?id=6D9SAQAAMAAJ ''Geological and Mineral Resources of Paraguay''] U.S. Government Printing Office, 1959 p. 34</ref> The formations are a protected heritage site and the hill was declared a Natural Monument in 1993.
