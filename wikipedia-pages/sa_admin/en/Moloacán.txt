'''Moloacán''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the south-east zone of the state, about {{convert|456|km|mi|0}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of {{convert|261.57|sqkm|sqmi|2}}. It is located at {{coord|17|59|N|94|21|W|}}.

==Geography==
The municipality of Moloacán is delimited to the north by [[Coatzacoalcos (municipality)|Coatzacoalcos]], to the east by [[Las Choapas]], to the south by [[Minatitlán, Veracruz|Minatitlán]], and to the west by [[Ixhuatlán del Sureste]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30111a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20070626042748/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30111a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2007-06-26|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Moloacán|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-11}}</ref>

The weather in Moloacán is hot all year round with rains in summer and autumn.

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]] and [[orange fruit]].
==Culture==
In May, a celebration takes place to honor to [[James the Great|Santiago Apóstol]], patron of the town.
