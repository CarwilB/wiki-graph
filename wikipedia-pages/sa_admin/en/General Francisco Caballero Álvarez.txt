'''General Francisco Caballero Álvarez''' or '''General Francisco Álvarez''' (also known as '''Puente Kyhá''') is a [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] and [[Districts of Paraguay|district]] in the [[Canendiyú]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is located about 500&nbsp;km (310&nbsp;mi) away from [[Asunción]], the capital of the country, by [[Route 3 (Paraguay)|Route Number 3]].
