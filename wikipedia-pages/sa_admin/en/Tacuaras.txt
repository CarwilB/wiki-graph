'''Tacuaras''' is a [[List of cities and towns in Paraguay|town]] in the [[Ñeembucú]] [[Department (country subdivision)|department]] of [[Paraguay]]. Tacuaras is a good fertile area for farming; it used to be lush green tropical forest, but now it has a population of about 300 people. The land in that territory is not very expensive, selling for about $100 per hectare.{{cn|date=April 2024}}
