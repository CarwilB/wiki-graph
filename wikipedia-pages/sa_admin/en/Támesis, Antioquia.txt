'''Támesis''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Antioquia Department|Antioquia]]. Part of the subregion of [[Southwestern Antioquia]]. Located at an elevation of 1,638 m (5,374 ft) above sea level, it was established in 1858. The local economy is based on agriculture.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.tamesis-antioquia.gov.co/index.shtml |title=Sitio oficial de Támesis en Antioquia, Colombia |publisher=Administración Municipal |access-date=1 March 2014}}</ref>

[[File:No a la mineria, Tamesis.JPG|thumb|"No to the mine" sign in Támesis]]

==Environmental issues==
The mountains in the area, including the Cerro de Cristo Rey mountain overlooking Támesis and a sacred mountain to the Indians, contain valuable mineral deposits. There are plans to develop these resources, but these plans have met local opposition. Some locals are against mining in general, whereas others are questioning the regulation of mining activities that is seen as protecting the multinational companies.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.eltiempo.com/colombia/medellin/ARTICULO-WEB-NEW_NOTA_INTERIOR-12391001.html |title=Caminata contra la minería pasará por siete pueblos antioqueños |author=Carlos Mario Cano R. |date=20 November 2012 |work=El Tiempo |access-date=1 March 2014}}</ref>
