'''Xapuri''' ({{IPA|pt|ʃapuˈɾi}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the southeast of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. 
It was the scene of an early bloodless victory during the war to make Acre independent of Bolivia. The town is known as the birthplace of the [[Rubber tapping|rubber tapper]] and environmentalist [[Chico Mendes]] and of the surgeon and professor [[Adib Jatene]].

==Geography==

Xapuri is at the point where the [[Xapuri River]] meets the [[Acre River]].
Its name is said to come from the Indigenous word ''Chapury'', meaning "river meeting".{{sfn|História de Xapuri – Prefeitura}}
Another explanation is that its name comes from the indigenous tribe of "Xapury" people.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}
The town is about {{convert|12|km}} northwest of the [[BR-317]] highway which leads from [[Rio Branco, Acre|Rio Branco]], {{convert|241|km}} to the east, to [[Brasiléia]], {{convert|74|km}} to the west. It has broad streets and wooden houses.{{sfn|Welcome to Xapuri – Lonely Planet}}
The area of the municipality is {{convert|5347.468|km2}}.{{sfn|Acre » Xapuri – IBGE}}
It is 12th largest in area in Acre.
It is bounded by the municipality of [[Sena Madureira]] to the west, Rio Branco to the north, [[Capixaba, Acre|Capixaba]] to the east, [[Epitaciolândia]] to the south,  and [[Brasiléia]] to the southwest.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|931537|ha|adj=on}} [[Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use environmental unit created in 1990.{{sfn|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}
The reserve is fairly well maintained apart from a few locations of deforestation, but its surroundings are quite deforested, compromising its buffer zone.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}

== History ==

Xapuri was born in 1883 shortly after Volta da Empreza (today's [[Rio Branco, Acre|Rio Branco]]) was founded.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}
The first Europeans came to the region in the first rubber boom, a period of uncontrolled [[land grabbing]] and extraction of forest resources.{{sfn|História de Xapuri – Prefeitura}}
The village of Xapuri became one of Acre's main rubber trading posts, and the region was an important producer of rubber and Brazil nuts.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}
Until the [[Acre War]] of 1902–03 it was part of [[Bolivia]], although most of the new colonists were Brazilian.{{sfn|Senador Jorge Viana}}
At the time of the Acre War the Bolivians called the post Mariscal Sucre.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}

At the start of 1902 [[José Plácido de Castro]] accepted an invitation to lead a revolt against Bolivia.{{sfn|Senador Jorge Viana}}
Although he argued for immediately attacking the garrison of 230 troops at Puerto Alonso (now [[Porto Acre]]), Plácido de Castro was persuaded to first take the outpost at Xapuri.{{sfn|Pedraja|2006|p=63}}
He entered Xapuri with 33 men in the early morning of 6 August 1902 and captured the sleeping garrison without spilling blood.
On 7 August 1902 he issued a manifesto proclaiming that Acre was independent.{{sfn|Pedraja|2006|p=64}}
After further fighting the last Bolivian forces surrender at what is now Porto Acre on 24 January 1903.{{sfn|Pedraja|2006|p=68}}

The village of Xapuri was officially elevated to the status of a town on 22 March 1904 by the prefect of Alto Acre, Colonel Augusto da Cunha Matos.
On 22 March 1905 it was elevated to the status of city by the interim prefect Captain Odilon Pratagi Brasiliense.{{sfn|História de Xapuri – Prefeitura}}
Xapuri was officially made a municipality on 23 October 1912.
Infrastructure was soon built, including trading houses and schools.
For many years Xapuri was known as the Little Princess of Acre (''Princesinha do Acre'') due to its great wealth from rubber.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}

The city became famous after the 1988 assassination of the rubber tapper and environmentalist [[Chico Mendes]], who was born in Xapuri.
His house has been preserved as one of the attractions of the town.{{sfn|História de Xapuri – Prefeitura}}

==People and economy==

The municipality of Xapuri has the 9th largest population in Acre.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}
As of 2010 the population was 16,091.
The estimated population in 2020 was 19,596.
The population density of 2010 was 3.01 people per square kilometre.{{sfn|Acre » Xapuri – IBGE}}

The town is an important Acre tourist destination for its monuments to the [[Acre War]] and the house of [[Chico Mendes]], and has an eco-lodge at the Seringal Waterfall. 
Apart from cattle ranching and subsistence farming, there is a condom factory, wood flooring factory and furniture workshops.
There are several settlement projects for extraction of nuts and rubber, subsistence agriculture and forestry.
The people also raise livestock, hunt and fish.
The Xapuri I and II projects are trying to bring back extractors and farmers who had moved to the city and to promote recovery of areas of pasturage and [[slash-and-burn]] agriculture by replanting fruit trees and timber trees.{{sfn|Xapuri – Governo do Estado do Acre}}
