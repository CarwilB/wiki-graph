'''Chincha''' is one of five [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Ica Region]] of [[Peru]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-pisco-sera-anfitriona-por-dia-de-identidad-e-integracion-cultural-region-ica-1032364.aspx|title=Pisco será la anfitriona por el Día de la Identidad e Integración Cultural de región Ica|work=[[Andina (news agency)|Andina]]|date=6 June 2025|access-date=7 March 2026|language=es}}</ref> The capital of the province is the city of [[Chincha Alta]], a center where the culture of the [[Black Peruvians]] developed. It is characterized by its schools and fabric textiles.
