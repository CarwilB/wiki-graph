'''Ilo''' is one of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] that make up the [[Moquegua Region]] in [[Peru]]. The province is the largest [[mining]] center in all of southern Peru.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.inei.gob.pe INEI] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/19970412040139/http://www.inei.gob.pe/ |date=1997-04-12 }}</ref><ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.muniperu.net IPAM]</ref>

== Geography ==

The province borders the province of [[Mariscal Nieto province|Mariscal Nieto]] to the north, the province of [[Jorge Basadre province|Jorge Basadre]] to the east and southeast, the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the south and west, and the province of [[Islay Province|Islay]] to the northwest.

It extends north–south through the region's [[coast]]al area, forming a [[bay]].

== Demographics ==

=== Population ===

According to the [[Peru 1993 Census|1993 Census]], the province has a population of 51,481 inhabitants, 52.4% of whom (26,964) are male and 47.6% (24,517) are female.

As of 2005, the [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]] estimates the province's population to be 68,363.

=== Languages ===

[[Spanish language|Spanish]] is spoken at home by 82% of the population; while others speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]] (10.6%), [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (6.1%), other [[Indigenous peoples of Europe|indigenous]] languages (0.1%) and foreign languages (0.3%).

=== Immigration ===

Persons originating from other regions of the country make up 51.6% of the population and 0.3% of residents were born abroad.

The largest immigrant groups come from the [[Puno Region]] (17.4% of the total population) and the [[Arequipa Region]] (15.3%).

=== Age ===

The age distribution is 
*42.4% under the age of 20, 
*10.9% from 20 to 24, 
*31.2% from 25 to 44, 
*12.9% from 45 to 64, and 
*2.6% who are 65 years of age or older.

The province of Ilo is divided into three [[district]]s ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: {{lang|es|distrito}}), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[El Algarrobal District|El Algarrobal]] ([[El Algarrobal]])
* [[Ilo District|Ilo]] ([[Ilo, Peru|Ilo]])
