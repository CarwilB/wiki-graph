'''Barra de Guabiraba''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbaha ˈdi ɡ͡wɐbiˈɾabɐ]}}) (''Guabiraba Bar'') is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located 132.6&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco. The city has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 12,263 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Gravatá]] and [[Sairé]] ('''N'''); [[Bonito, Pernambuco|Bonito]] ('''S''' and '''W'''); [[Cortês]] ('''E''')
* Area - 114.22&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 482 m
* Hydrography - [[Sirinhaém River|Sirinhaém]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Subperenifólia]] forest
* Clima - hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.0 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 132.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''Barra de Guabiraba''' are based in industry and [[agribusiness]], especially [[manioc]], [[sugarcane]], [[passion fruit]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[poultry]].
