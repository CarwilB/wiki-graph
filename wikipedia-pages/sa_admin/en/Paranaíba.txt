'''Paranaíba''' is the easternmost [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Mato Grosso do Sul]]. Its population was 42,276 (2020) and its area is 5,402.778&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.
