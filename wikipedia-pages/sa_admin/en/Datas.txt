'''Datas''' is a Brazilian municipality in the north-center of the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 5,421 living in a total area of 309&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/datas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Diamantina]]. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="IBGE">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070109000000/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=9 January 2007 }}</ref>

==Geography==
Datas is at an elevation of 1340 meters in the upper [[Jequitinhonha River]] valley. It is south of [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]] to which it is connected by federal highway BR-367. The nearest major population center is [[Diamantina, Minas Gerais|Diamantina]]. The distance to Diamantina is 27&nbsp;km; and the distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 272&nbsp;km. Neighboring municipalities are: Diamantina (N); [[Santo Antônio do Itambé]] and [[Presidente Kubitschek]] (E); [[Conceição do Mato Dentro]] (S); and [[Gouveia, Minas Gerais|Gouveia]] (W).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/diamantina/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=16 July 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080204111902/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/diamantina/ |archive-date=4 February 2008 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy==
The main economic activities are services, and agriculture. The GDP in 2005 was {{BRLConvert|13|m|year=2005|showdate=no}}, with 10 million from services, 1&nbsp;million from industry, and 1&nbsp;million from agriculture. There were 442 rural producers on 10,000 hectares of land. Only 5 farms had tractors (2006). The main crops were sugarcane, beans, corn, and soybeans. There were 2,000 head of cattle (2006).<ref name="IBGE"/>

==Social indicators==
The social indicators rank it in the bottom tier of municipalities in the state.
*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.694 (2000)
*State ranking: 572 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 3094 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*Literacy rate: 86%
*Life expectancy: 66 (average of males and females)<ref name="Frigoletto">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto]</ref>
*Infant mortality: 24.39
*Percentage of houses in urban area connected to sewers: 93.20
*Degree of urbanization: 52.02% <ref>{{Cite web |url=http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/DATAS.pdf |title=Indicadores de Datas |access-date=16 July 2008 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706161042/http://dtr2002.saude.gov.br/caadab/indicadores/minas/DATAS.pdf |archive-date=6 July 2011 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In terms of HDI, the highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568. Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha. In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0.467—putting it in last place.<ref name="Frigoletto"/>

There were 7 health clinics and one small private hospital with 18 beds in 2005. Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to Diamantina. Educational needs were met by 7 primary schools and 2 middle schools.<ref name="IBGE"/>
