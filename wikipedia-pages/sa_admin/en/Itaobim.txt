'''Itaobim''' is a municipality in the northeast of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population in 2020 was 21,029 inhabitants in a total area of {{cvt|680|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/itaobim/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The city is part of the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. The elevation of the municipal seat is 180 meters. It became a municipality in 1962.<ref name="Estação: ITAOBIM (A550)">{{cite web |last= |first= |date=23 October 2020 |title=Estação: ITAOBIM A550 |url=https://tempo.inmet.gov.br/TabelaEstacoes/A550 |access-date=23 November 2023 |website=portal.inmet.gov.br |publisher=[[INMET]] |quote=}}</ref> Neighboring municipalities are [[Medina]], [[Jequitinhonha]], [[Ponto dos Volantes]] and [[Itinga]]. The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 604 kilometers.

== Etymology ==
The word Itaobim comes from the [[Tupi language]] and means ''Itá'' (rock) and ''oby'' (green). This toponym was chosen by the inhabitants because there is a mountain range made up of green-toned rocks near the city.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |title=Câmara Municipal de Itaobim |url=https://www.camaraitaobim.mg.gov.br/cidade#:~:text=Inicialmente%20o%20lugar%20chamava-se,=%20pedra%20+%20obim%20=%20verde. |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=www.camaraitaobim.mg.gov.br |language=pt-BR}}</ref>

== History ==
In the 1910s, the town of São Roque existed, located between the [[Jequitinhonha River]] and the São Roque stream. The town was formed by people from the Brazilian northeast, mostly from Bahia, who were fleeing the drought. According to reports, the location was chosen because it had fertile and flat land, suitable for agriculture and livestock farming. After a flood that occurred in January 1928, when the Jequitinhonha River overflowed and destroyed the town, the inhabitants sought refuge on a nearby plateau, forming the city of Itaobim.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Assessoria |title=História de Itaobim |url=https://www.itaobim.mg.gov.br/detalhe-da-materia/info/historia-de-itaobim/6504 |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Prefeitura Municipal de Itaobim |language=pt-br}}</ref>

The municipality was officially formed on December 30, 1962.<ref name=":0" />

== Geography ==
According to the regional division in force since 2017, instituted by the IBGE, the municipality belongs to the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni|Intermediate]] and [[Immediate Geographic Region of Teófilo Otoni]]. Until then, with the divisions into microregions and mesoregions in force, it was part of the microregion of Pedra Azul, which in turn was included in the [[Jequitinhonha (mesoregion)|mesoregion of Jequitinhonha]]. Located 620 km from Belo Horizonte, the city, an old route of the [[bandeirantes]], is at a strategic junction of the [[BR-116 (Brazil highway)|BR-116]] (Rio-Bahia), [[BR-367 (Brazil highway)|BR-367]] (North of Minas) and BR-327 (South of Bahia) highways.

== Economy ==
The city's [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] is around R$308.7 million [[Brazilian real|reais]], with 54.8% of the added value coming from services, followed by public administration (36.5%), industry (5.6%) and agriculture (3.1%).<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Economia de Itaobim - MG |url=https://www.caravela.info/regional/itaobim---mg#:~:text=Entre%202006%20a%202021,%20o,foi%20de%2018,7%25. |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Caravela |language=pt}}</ref> The GDP per capita of Itaobim is R$14.7 thousand.<ref name=":1" />

== Culture ==
In the city, the Itaobim Art and Culture Museum has old objects and stuffed animals by Brasiliano Pereira Reis, better known as Mestre Canjira, a [[Taxidermy|taxidermist]] who stuffed animals and birds killed in accidents donated by drivers and Forest Police officers.<ref>{{Cite web |last= |first= |date=2014-02-12 |title=Morre Canjira, o homem que criou o Museu de Arte e Cultura de Itaobim {{!}} Aconteceu no Vale |url=https://aconteceunovale.com.br/portal/?p=21078 |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=Aconteceu no Vale |language=pt-BR}}</ref> Every year, Itaobim holds the Festa da Manga, a festival that brings together many people from the region and where nationally famous artists perform.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Festa da Manga: Turismo Em Itaobim: História, Geografia, Cultura e Turismo {{!}} Projeto REdescobrindo os Vales |url=https://www.redescobrindoosvales.tur.br/jequitinhonha/microrregiao-de-pedra-azul/itaobim/festa-de-manga.php |access-date=2024-08-16 |website=www.redescobrindoosvales.tur.br}}</ref>
