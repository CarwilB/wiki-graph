'''Alto Parnaíba''' is a Brazilian municipality in the southernmost tip of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Maranhão]]. In 2020, the city's population was 11,212.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|724324|ha}} [[Nascentes do Rio Parnaíba National Park]], created in 2002.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=pt |title=Unidade de Conservação: Parque Nacional das Nascentes do Rio Parnaíba|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=156|access-date=2016-05-01}}</ref>

==Climate==
Alto Parnaíba experiences a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') with hot temperatures and distinct wet and dry seasons. 

The city experiences a [[wet season]] from November to April. The [[dry season]] extends from May to September, with June through August having almost no rainfall.<ref name="WMONormals"/>

Alto Parnaíba has hot and humid conditions throughout the year. September and October, before the wet season, are the hottest months, with average highs around {{convert|36|C|abbr=on}}.<ref name="WMONormals"/>
