'''Chiconquiaco''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]]. It is located in the [[Sierra de Chiconquiaco]] in the middle of the State of Veracruz. It has a surface of 68.27&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It is located at {{coord|19|45|N|96|49|W|}}.

==Demographics==
{{As of|2020}}, the municipality had a population of 13,881 inhabitants in 52 localities.<ref name=2020CENSUSCHICONQUIACO>[http://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2022/09/Chiconquiaco.CM_.Ver_.2022.4.pdf 2020 Chiconquiaco CENSUS]</ref> About 3,500 inhabitants reside in the municipal seat. Other localities include El Huérfano (1,694 hab.), El Capulín (937 hab.), La Sombra (901 hab.) and Las Paredes (694 hab.).<ref name=2020CENSUSCHICONQUIACO/>

==Economy==
It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]] and [[coffee]].

==Culture==
In '''Chiconquiaco''', in June takes place the celebration in honor to San Pedro and San Pablo Patrons  of the place.

==Geography==
The municipality of ''' Chiconquiaco ''' is delimited to the north-west by [[Misantla]], to the north by [[Yecuatla]], to the east by [[Juchique de Ferrer]], to the south-east by [[Tepetlán]], to the south by [[Acatlán, Veracruz|Acatlán]] and to the west by [[Landero y Coss]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30057a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20070626042748/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30057a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=2007-06-26|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Chiconquiaco|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-05}}</ref>

===Climate===
According to the [[Köppen climate classification]] system, Chiconquiaco has a [[Oceanic climate#Subtropical highland variety (Cfb, Cwb)|subtropical highland climate]] (''Cfb''). Fog occurs on an average of 120.6 days per year, while lightning occurs on 3.6 days. The record high and record low temperatures of {{convert|35.0|°C}} and {{convert|-6.0|°C}} were recorded on September 20, 1967, and January 4, 1979, respectively.
