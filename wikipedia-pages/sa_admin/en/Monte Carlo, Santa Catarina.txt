'''Monte Carlo''' is a [[city]] in [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]], in the [[Southern Brazil|Southern Region]] of [[Brazil]]. It is part of the metropolitan region of the city of Fraiburgo.

==Climate==
Monte Carlo has [[Oceanic climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]] Cfb) in general, the temperature varies from 2°C to 31°C and is rarely lower than -3°C or higher than 35°C. [[Frost]] are common on winter.
