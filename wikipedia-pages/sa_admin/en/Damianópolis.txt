'''Damianópolis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in eastern [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]]. The population was 3,597 (2007) in a total area of 415.3&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Location==
Damianópolis is located to the east of the important BR-020 highway and west of the border with the state of [[Bahia]]. Connections with this highway are made by paved road by way of [[Mambaí]].

[[Goiânia]], the capital city of Goiás state, is 528&nbsp;km away. Highway connections are made by BR-153 / [[Anápolis]] / GO-060 / [[Alexânia]] / [[Planaltina, Goiás|Planaltina]] / [[Formosa, Goiás|Formosa]] / GO-020 / BR-030 / [[Vila Boa, Goiás|Vila Boa]] / [[Alvorada do Norte]] / GO-236 / [[Buritinópolis]] / [[Mambaí]] / GO-108.<ref>[http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm Sepin Distancias a Goiânia] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090205080546/http://www.seplan.go.gov.br/sepin/pub/anuario/2005/transporte/tab01_transporte.htm |date=February 5, 2009 }}</ref>

Municipal boundaries are with:
*north:  [[Buritinópolis]] and [[Mambaí]]
*west:  [[Alvorada do Norte]]
*south:  [[Sítio d'Abadia]]

==Economy==
The economy is based on cattle raising (24,400 head in 2006) and agriculture, especially the growing of soybeans and corn.
