'''Forquilhinha''' is a municipality in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Santa Catarina (state)|Santa Catarina]]. In 2020, it had a population of 27,211 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/sc/forquilhinha/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> It covers an area of 184,557&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> at an altitude of 42 meters above sea level. It is the native town of cardinal Paulo Evaristo Arns and his sister [[Zilda Arns]].

The city is served by [[Diomício Freitas Airport]].
