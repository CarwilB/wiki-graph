'''Bolívar''' is a [[Provinces of Peru|province]] located in the [[La Libertad Region]] of Peru. It is one of the twelve provinces that make up that region.

==Boundaries==
*'''North:''' [[Amazonas (Peruvian department)|Amazonas Region]]
*'''East:''' [[San Martín Region]]
*'''South:''' the province of [[Pataz Province|Pataz]]
*'''West:''' the province of [[Sánchez Carrión Province|Sánchez Carrión]] and [[Cajamarca Region]]

==Political division==
The province has an area of {{Convert|1718.86|km2|sp=us}} and is divided into six districts:

*[[Bolívar District, Bolívar|Bolívar]]
*[[Bambamarca District, Bolívar|Bambamarca]]
*[[Condormarca District|Condormarca]]
*[[Longotea District|Longotea]]
*[[Uchumarca District|Uchumarca]]
*[[Ucuncha District|Ucuncha]]

==Capital==
The capital of this province is the city of [[Bolívar, Peru|Bolívar]].
