'''Uberaba''' ({{IPA|pt|ubeˈɾabɐ}}) is a [[municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]], Brazil. It is located in the [[Brazilian Highlands]] at an elevation of 823 metres (2,700&nbsp;ft) above sea level on the Uberaba River, and is situated 418 kilometres (260&nbsp;mi) from the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Uberaba {{!}} Brazil {{!}} Britannica |url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Uberaba |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240428063018/https://www.britannica.com/place/Minas-Gerais |archive-date=2024-04-28 |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=www.britannica.com |language=en}}</ref> The city was granted its status in 1856 and derives its name from the [[Tupi language]], meaning "bright water".<ref>{{Cite web |last=giftnily.com |date=7 March 2022 |title=There is tupi in Portuguese spoken in Brazil |url=https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/brasil/2022/03/07/curitiba-ubatuba-pipoca-quotchorar-as-pitangas-quot-o-tupi-presente-no-portugues-falado-no-brasil.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240428062908/https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/brasil/2022/03/07/curitiba-ubatuba-pipoca-quotchorar-as-pitangas-quot-o-tupi-presente-no-portugues-falado-no-brasil.html |archive-date=2024-04-28 |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=Giftnily |language=en-US}}</ref> As of 2022, the population was 337,836 inhabitants, making it the 7th-most populous city in Minas Gerais.<ref name="IBGE_Uberaba" />

==History==
[[File:Rua do comercio 1930.jpg|thumb|Uberaba circa 1930.]]
The history of Uberaba traces its origins back to 1810, when Captain Major Eustaquio founded a settlement near the ancient [[Rodovia Anhanguera|Anhanguera route]], which was more commonly referred to back then as the "Goyazes road". This settlement served as a resting point for both locals and travelers who passed through or arrived there during the 19th century. Its strategic location and function as a crossroads prompted local farmers to engage in crop cultivation and cattle domestication for commercial purposes, leading to the establishment of a robust livestock network in the region.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Urban land: the relationship between religious and public institutions in the control over the land heritage for founding the city of Buritizal / SP |url=https://cadernos.proarq.fau.ufrj.br/public/docs/Proarq18_UrbanLand_IvoneJunior.pdf}}</ref>

Initially a part of the [[Captaincies of Brazil|Captaincy]] of [[Goias]], the territory of Uberaba was incorporated into the Captaincy of Minas Gerais in 1816. It gained recognition as a parish in 1820 and was officially designated as a city in 1856.<ref>{{Cite web |title=IFTM {{!}} Congresso de Internacionalização e Educação Profissional e Tecnológica |url=http://www.iftm.edu.br/coninterept/ |access-date=2022-06-29 |website=www.iftm.edu.br |language=pt-br}}</ref>

In 1889, the inaugural railway station was established due to the expansion of the Mogiana Railway from the neighboring municipality of [[Sacramento, Minas Gerais|Sacramento]]. The introduction of the railway system boosted the economy of Uberaba in the subsequent years.<ref>{{Cite web |title=THE TRAMWAYS OF SACRAMENTO |url=http://tramz.com/br/sa/sa.html |access-date=17 March 2022 |website=tramz.com}}</ref>

Currently, Uberaba is known for its lively livestock and [[agribusiness]] events. As an industrial hub, the city functions as a regional center for culture, commerce, and entertainment, hosting a diverse array of institutions, markets, and entertainment venues.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Santana |first1=M. L. |last2=Pereira |first2=R. J. |last3=Bignardi |first3=A. B. |last4=Filho |first4=A. E. Vercesi |last5=Menéndez-Buxadera |first5=A. |last6=El Faro |first6=L. |date=1 December 2015 |title=Detrimental effect of selection for milk yield on genetic tolerance to heat stress in purebred Zebu cattle: Genetic parameters and trends |journal=Journal of Dairy Science |language=en |volume=98 |issue=12 |pages=9035–9043 |doi=10.3168/jds.2015-9817 |issn=0022-0302 |pmid=26476953 |doi-access=free}}</ref>

The municipality includes the rural district of [[Peirópolis]], known for its paleontological site. This area features the Paleontological Research Center Llewellyn Ivor Price and a museum dedicated to local fossil discoveries.

On 27 March 2024, Uberaba gained recognition from UNESCO as a [[UNESCO Global Geoparks|global geopark]].<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2024-04-01 |title=UNESCO names 18 new Geoparks {{!}} UNESCO |url=https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-names-18-new-geoparks |access-date=2024-04-01 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240401025255/https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/unesco-names-18-new-geoparks |archive-date=1 April 2024 }}</ref>

== Geography ==
=== Climate ===
Uberaba has a [[Tropical savanna climate|Tropical climate]] (Aw) with an average annual temperature of 21.9&nbsp;°C (71.4&nbsp;°F). The highest temperature ever recorded was 40.2&nbsp;°C (105.2&nbsp;°F) in October, while the lowest recorded temperature was −2.2&nbsp;°C (28.0&nbsp;°F) in July.

Precipitation occurs on an average of 86 days per year, with the highest amount in December and the lowest in July.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Uberaba Climate, Weather By Month, Average Temperature (Brazil) – Weather Spark |url=https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/brazil/uberaba |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=weatherspark.com |language=en}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
Uberaba is the primary municipality in the [[Intermediate Geographic Region of Uberaba]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Divisões Regionais do Brasil {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/geociencias/cartas-e-mapas/redes-geograficas/15778-divisoes-regionais-do-brasil.html |access-date=2022-06-29 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

In 2021, the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Uberaba]] comprised 10 municipalities spanning an area of 14,281.652&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (5,514.17 sq mi).

The [[2022 Brazilian census|2022 Brazilian Census]] reported that Uberaba had a population of approximately 337,836 people, reflecting a 14% increase from the [[2010 Brazilian census|2010 census]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-31 |title=As 200 cidades mais populosas do Brasil, segundo Censo 2022 |url=https://valor.globo.com/brasil/artigo/as-cidades-mais-populosas-do-brasil.ghtml |access-date=2025-10-03 |website=Valor Econômico |language=pt-br}}</ref> The population density was 74.68 people per square kilometer. Ethnically, 52.24% of Uberaba's population identified as [[White Brazilians|white]], 36.15% as [[Pardo Brazilians|mixed-race]], and 11.21% as [[Afro-Brazilians|black]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Uberaba (Municipality, Brazil) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/brazil/regiaosudeste/admin/minas_gerais/3170107__uberaba/ |access-date=2025-10-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

Regarding household composition, 96.3% of the population lived in private households, while smaller percentages resided in non-institutionalized group quarters or were institutionalized.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Uberaba MG |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/ranking-resumido/download.html?cod=3170107 |access-date=2025-08-12 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

==Economy==
[[File:78 ExpoZebu 2012 (7) (7198159706).jpg|thumb|Cattle exhibition during the Expozebu fair.]]
Uberaba is an important grain producer in Minas Gerais, particularly known for [[Maize|corn]], [[soy]]bean, [[Economics of coffee|coffee]], [[cotton]], and [[sugarcane]]. The city is also recognized for hosting [[Expozebu]], one of the largest [[Agricultural show|cattle fairs]] in the world, held annually since 1935.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Iglesias |first1=Carlos |last2=Sesmero |first2=Juan P. |date=1 June 2015 |title=Economic Analysis of Supplementing Sugarcane with Corn for Ethanol Production in Brazil: A Case Study in Uberaba |url=https://doi.org/10.1007/s12155-014-9551-4 |journal=BioEnergy Research |language=en |volume=8 |issue=2 |pages=627–643 |doi=10.1007/s12155-014-9551-4 |bibcode=2015BioER...8..627I |issn=1939-1242 |s2cid=15362034|url-access=subscription }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Intelligence |first=fDi |title=From zebu to tattoos, Uberaba's pioneering spirit lives on |url=https://www.fdiintelligence.com/article/72650 |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=www.fdiintelligence.com |date=16 August 2018 |language=en}}</ref> From 2019 to 2024, Uberaba secured positions among the top 100 Brazilian cities with the highest [[Agriculture|agricultural production value]], ranking 30th by 2024 according to the 2023 report from the [[Ministry of Agriculture (Brazil)|Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock]] (MAPA).<ref>{{Cite web |title=The 100 Wealthiest Cities in Brazilian Agribusiness |url=https://exame.com/agro/as-100-cidades-mais-ricas-do-agronegocio-brasileiro-em-2023-saiba-quais/ |access-date=2024-10-30 |website=Exame |language=pt-br}}</ref>

The service sector employs slightly over half of the city's population, followed by the manufacturing industry, and agriculture ranking third. The industrial sector encompasses various fields such as agribusiness, food processing, textile manufacturing, steel production, mining, chemical manufacturing, electronic devices, home appliances, and more.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Intelligence |first=fDi |title=Uberaba thinks beyond the crop |url=https://www.fdiintelligence.com/article/72649 |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=www.fdiintelligence.com |date=16 August 2018 |language=en}}</ref>

The chemical manufacturing sector is concentrated in the local industrial park, with processing plants operated by chemical companies, including [[The Mosaic Company|Mosaic]], [[FMC Corporation]], Sipcam Nichino ([[UPL (company)|UPL]]), and [[Yara International|Yara]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=24 July 2019 |title=Acid Plant Database October 21, 2019 |url=http://www.sulphuric-acid.com/sulphuric-acid-on-the-web/acid%20plants/Vale-Fertilizantes-Uberaba.htm |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=Agência de Notícias Brasil-Árabe |language=en-US}}</ref> In 2021, the city ranked 62nd among the 100 largest economies in Brazil, with a [[Gross domestic product|GDP]] of [[Brazilian real|R$]] 20.3 billion and a GDP per capita of R$59,943.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-10-08 |title=Uberlândia termina ano entre as 30 cidades com maior PIB do Brasil {{!}} Triângulo Mineiro {{!}} G1 |url=https://g1.globo.com/mg/triangulo-mineiro/noticia/2023/12/31/uberlandia-termina-ano-entre-as-30-cidades-com-maior-pib-do-brasil.ghtml |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241008050257/https://g1.globo.com/mg/triangulo-mineiro/noticia/2023/12/31/uberlandia-termina-ano-entre-as-30-cidades-com-maior-pib-do-brasil.ghtml |archive-date=8 October 2024 |access-date=2024-10-08}}</ref>

In 2011, [[Petrobras]] selected Uberaba as the site for its UFN-5 (Nitrogen Fertilizer Unit 5), which was designed to connect to the [[GASBOL|Gasbol]] pipeline and produce 1,500 metric tons of [[ammonia]] daily.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Bailey |first=Mary |date=2014-05-07 |title=Toyo awarded contract for Petrobras ammonia plant |url=https://www.chemengonline.com/toyo-awarded-contract-for-petrobras-ammonia-plant/ |website=chemengonline.com}}</ref> Construction of UFN-5 began in 2014 as part of the [[Programa de Aceleração do Crescimento|PAC-2]], but was suspended in 2015 at 30% completion due to Petrobras' financial crisis, influenced by [[Operation Car Wash]], and the company's redirection of resources to [[Pre-salt layer|pre-salt]] oil fields.<ref>{{Cite web |last=admin |date=2022-07-20 |title=Brazil Agrochemical Update 07/20 - Agrochemicals in Brazil |url=https://agribrasilis.com/2022/07/20/agrochemicals-in-brazil/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241202190914/https://agribrasilis.com/2022/07/20/agrochemicals-in-brazil/ |archive-date=2024-12-02 |access-date=2024-11-14 |website=Agribrasilis - Inside Agribusiness |language=pt-BR}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Pooler |first=Michael |date=2022-09-28 |title='It cannot be the madness that it is today': what's next for Petrobras? |url=https://www.ft.com/content/d379f8be-e61e-4e39-986c-f8d3bdbf8e75 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241202190730/https://www.ft.com/content/d379f8be-e61e-4e39-986c-f8d3bdbf8e75 |archive-date=2024-12-02 |access-date=2024-12-02 |work=Financial Times}}</ref> After listing the site for sale without a successful auction, the Government of Minas Gerais transferred it back to Uberaba in 2021 under a 20-year lease. In 2022, Atlas Agro signed a letter of intent to establish a sustainable [[nitrogen fertilizer]] facility at the location.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-03-15 |title=Atlas Agro begins engineering on its green nitrogen fertilizer plant in Brazil — Fertilizer Daily |url=https://www.fertilizerdaily.com/20240315-atlas-agro-initiates-engineering-of-its-green-nitrogen-fertilizer-plant-in-brazil/ |access-date=2024-11-14 |website=www.fertilizerdaily.com |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Paleontology ==
[[File:Replica de Titanossauro em Peirópolis.jpg|thumb|Replica of a species of titanosaur next to the Dinosaur Museum in [[Peirópolis]].]]
Uberaba is recognized for housing a significant paleontological site in Brazil, boasting fossil records dating back approximately 80 to 66 million years, from the [[Late Cretaceous|Late Cretaceous period]].

The first paleontological discoveries in the territory were made by chance in 1945 when workers in the rural district of Peirópolis accidentally came across well-preserved fossils. Subsequently, paleontologist [[Llewellyn Ivor Price]], associated with the [[National Department of Mineral Production]] (DNPM), conducted extensive scientific research in the region from 1947 until 1974, with ''[[Peirosaurus]]'' being one of Price's early findings in the area of the [[Serra da Galga Formation]].<ref name="CCRA09">{{cite journal |last=Candeiro |first=C. R. A. |year=2009 |title=Vertebrates of the Marília Formation (late Maastrichtian) from the Peirópolis paleontological site: Toward a better understanding |journal=Earth Sciences Research Journal |volume=13 |issue=1 |pages=6–15}}</ref> The specimens collected during this period were later transferred to the [[Earth Sciences Museum|Museum of Earth Sciences]] in [[Rio de Janeiro]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Smith |first=Sebastian |title=Brazil's biggest dinosaur found after passing 60 years in cupboard |url=https://phys.org/news/2016-10-brazil-biggest-dinosaur-years-cupboard.html |access-date=2024-01-29 |website=phys.org |language=en}}</ref>

In 1991, it was established in Peirópolis, the Paleontological Research Center Llewellyn Ivor Price, locally known as the Centro de Pesquisas Paleontológicas Llewellyn Ivor Price. Currently, the research center collaborates with various national and international research institutions, and serves as a hub for paleontology in Brazil, focusing on understanding the fossil fauna and environments of the Late Cretaceous in the region, maintaining its own technical team for fossil preservation.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Paleophilatelie.eu – Brazil 1995 |url=https://www.paleophilatelie.eu/description/stamps/brazil_1995.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240425203154/https://www.paleophilatelie.eu/description/stamps/brazil_1995.html |archive-date=2024-04-25 |access-date=7 March 2022 |website=www.paleophilatelie.eu}}</ref> As an integral part of the Research Center, The Dinosaur Museum aims to disseminate paleontological knowledge to the general public through interactive and educational exhibitions. The museum attracts an average of 100,000 visitors annually.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-07-25 |title=Sesc São Paulo - Em Minas, um ninho de dinossauros - Revistas - Online |url=https://portal.sescsp.org.br/online/artigo/3897_EM+MINAS+UM+NINHO+DE+DINOSSAUROS |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230725000608/https://portal.sescsp.org.br/online/artigo/3897_EM+MINAS+UM+NINHO+DE+DINOSSAUROS |archive-date=25 July 2023 |access-date=2023-07-25}}</ref>

In 2022, a geological survey identified 31 [[Geoheritage|geosites]] and sites in Uberaba.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-03-31 |title=Repositório Institucional de Geociências: Mapa do patrimônio geológico do Geoparque Uberaba: terra de gigantes | url=https://rigeo.sgb.gov.br/handle/doc/22944 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240331035435/https://rigeo.sgb.gov.br/handle/doc/22944 | archive-date=2024-03-31 | url-status=live | language=pt}}</ref> Across the territory, over 10,000 prehistoric animal fossils have been documented, which includes [[Sauropoda|sauropods]], [[Theropoda|theropods]], and [[velociraptor]]s, along with various other specimens such as fishes, frogs, [[Crocodyliformes|crocodyliforms]], turtles, birds, mammals, mollusks, and the presence of [[trace fossil]]s.<ref>{{Cite journal |last1=Martinelli |first1=Agustín |last2=Teixeira |first2=Vicente |last3=Ferraz |first3=Mara |last4=Cavellani |first4=Camila |last5=Pérez |first5=Cecilia |last6=Marinho |first6=Thiago |last7=Ribeiro |first7=Luiz |date=2017-08-22 |title=Curadoria da coleção do Centro de Pesquisas Paleontólogicas L. == Transportation ==
Road access to Uberaba is provided by [[BR-050 (Brazil highway)|BR-050]], connecting the city to the federal capital [[Brasília]] and [[São Paulo]], as well as by [[BR-262 (Brazil highway)|BR-262]], linking it to the state capital Belo Horizonte.

The railway system is managed by VLI Multimodal S.A., which operates the rail network linking the local [[Intermodal freight transport|intermodal terminal]] to the southeastern corridor of [[Ferrovia Centro-Atlântica]]. The corridor ultimately leads to the [[Port of Santos]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2024-04-07 |title=VLI inaugurates new fertilizer corridor at Brazil's FCA railway |url=https://williams.com.br/vli-inaugurates-new-fertilizer-corridor-at-brazils-fca-railway/ |access-date=2024-04-25 |website=Williams |language=en}}</ref>

Air access is provided by [[Uberaba Airport|Mário de Almeida Franco Airport]].
