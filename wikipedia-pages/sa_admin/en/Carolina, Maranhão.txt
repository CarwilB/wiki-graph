Carolina is served by [[Carolina (Maranhão) Airport|Brig. Lysias Augusto Rodrigues Airport]].

==Climate==
Carolina features a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Aw'') characterized by hot temperatures and distinct wet and dry seasons. The city experiences a wet season from October to April, with January through March being the rainiest period. The dry season is from June to September, and experiences little rain.
