'''Porecatu''' is a city in the state of [[Paraná (state)|Paraná]], [[Brazil]], located at around {{coord|22|45|21|S|51|22|44|W|display=title}}, at the margins of the [[Paranapanema River]]. Its [[economy]] is mainly based on [[agriculture]] with the [[sugarcane]] being the most important product.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 12,748.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pr/porecatu/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

The city is served by [[Porecatu Airport]].

==History==

'''Porecatu''' was the main place of a violent conflict between [[government]] forces and [[Landless Workers' Movement|landless workers]] during the decade of 1940, before the official creation of the [[city]]. This conflict was called [[Porecatu War]] (Portuguese: "Guerra de Porecatu").
