'''Báguanos''' ({{IPA|es|ˈbaɣwanos}}) is a municipality and town in the [[Holguín Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Geography==
Located in the middle of the province, Báguanos is {{Convert|34|km}} far from [[Holguín]] and {{Convert|52|km}} from [[Banes, Cuba|Banes]]. The municipal territory includes several villages. The most populated one is Tacajó.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Báguanos had a population of 47,851.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|806|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|59|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
