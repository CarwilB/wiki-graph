The '''Oropesa District''' is one of the twelve [[Districts of Peru|districts]] in the [[Quispicanchi Province]] in [[Peru]]. Its capital is the town of [[Oropesa, Quispicanchi|Oropesa]].

== Geography ==
One of the highest peaks of the district is [[Pachatusan]] at {{convert|4842|m|ft|abbr=on}}. Other mountains are listed below:<ref>escale.minedu.gob.pe/ UGEL map Cusco Province (Cusco Region)</ref>

* Maransirayuq
* [[Pachatusan]]
* Quri Qalla
* Quriwayrachina
* Sinchi Q'umirniyuq
* [[Waypun]]
