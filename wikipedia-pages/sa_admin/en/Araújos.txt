'''Araújos''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the center of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. Its population {{as of|2020|lc=y}} was 9,401 people living in a total area of {{cvt|245|km2}}. The city belongs to the meso-region of Central Mineira and to the micro-region of Bom Despacho.  It became a municipality in 1954.<ref name="ibge.gov.br">[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref><ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/araujos/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>

==Geography==
The city center of Araújos is located at an elevation of 821 meters about halfway between [[Divinópolis]] and [[Bom Despacho, Minas Gerais|Bom Despacho]].   Neighboring  municipalities are:  [[Bom Despacho, Minas Gerais|Bom Despacho]] (N) and (NW),  [[Leandro Ferreira]] (NE), [[Nova Serrana]] and [[Pedigão]] (E), [[Santo Antônio do Monte]] (S) and (SW).<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/bomdespacho/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-08-22 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080501121858/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/bomdespacho/ |archive-date=2008-05-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is 163&nbsp;km.  The distance to regional center [[Bom Despacho, Minas Gerais|Bom Despacho]] is 32&nbsp;km.  Connections are made by state highway MG-252 and MG-164.  The distance to [[Divinópolis]] is 49&nbsp;km.<ref>[http://www.der.mg.gov.br/index.php?option=com_wrapper&Itemid=179 DERMG]</ref>

==Economy==
Services and agriculture are the most important economic activities.  In 2005 there were 158 small transformation industries employing 821 workers.    The GDP in 2005 was approximately R$40 million, with 23 million reais from services, 5 million reais from industry, and 10 million reais from agriculture.  There were 351 rural producers on 16,000 hectares of land.  46 farms had tractors (2006).  Approximately 700 persons were employed in agriculture.  The main crops are beans and corn.  There were 18,000 head of cattle (2006).<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

There was one bank (2007).  In the vehicle fleet there were 1,089 automobiles, 92 trucks, 149 pickup trucks, and 542 motorcycles (2007).<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

==Health and education==
In the health sector there were 8 public health clinics, 4 of which were specialized. (2005).  Patients with more serious health conditions are transported to [[Divinópolis]] or [[Belo Horizonte]].  Educational needs of 1,900 students were met by 6 primary schools, 1 middle school, and 2 pre-primary schools.<ref name="ibge.gov.br"/>

*Municipal [[Human Development Index]]: 0.755 (2000)
*State ranking: 244 out of 853 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*National ranking: 1718 out of 5,138 municipalities {{as of|2000|lc=y}}
*Literacy rate: 87%
*Life expectancy: 72 (average of males and females)<ref name="frigoletto.com.br">[http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm Frigoletto] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706152853/http://www.frigoletto.com.br/GeoEcon/idhmg.htm |date=2011-07-06 }}</ref>

In 2000 the per capita monthly income of R$213.00 below the state and national average of R$276.00 and R$297.00 respectively.  [[Poços de Caldas]] had the highest per capita monthly income in 2000 with R$435.00.  The lowest was [[Setubinha]] with R$73.00.

The highest ranking municipality in Minas Gerais in 2000 was [[Poços de Caldas]] with 0.841, while the lowest was [[Setubinha]] with 0.568.  Nationally the highest was [[São Caetano do Sul]] in São Paulo with 0.919, while the lowest was Setubinha.  In more recent statistics (considering 5,507 municipalities) [[Manari, Brazil|Manari]] in the state of [[Pernambuco]] has the lowest rating in the country—0,467—putting it in last place.<ref name="frigoletto.com.br"/>
