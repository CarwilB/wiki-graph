'''Grand-Gosier''' ({{IPA|fr|ɡʁɑ̃ ɡozje}}; {{langx|ht|Gran Gozye}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Belle-Anse Arrondissement]], in the [[Sud-Est (department)|Sud-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 17,059 inhabitants.

It is the birthplace of celebrated Haitian poet and [[Haitian Creole]] language advocate [[Félix Morisseau-Leroy]].
