'''Puracé''' ({{IPA|es|puɾaˈse}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Cauca Department]], [[Colombia]].

An 1853 watercolor by [[Manuel María Paz Delgado|Manuel María Paz]] is an early depiction of four indigenous people in Puracé wearing straw hats and [[ruana]]s.<ref>{{Cite web

| first = Manuel María

}}</ref>
