'''San Luis Río Colorado''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in northwestern Mexico, being the northwesternmost municipality in Sonora and covering an area of 8412.75&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=January 4, 2010|archive-date=July 31, 2012|archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="SLRC">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/municipios/26055a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México. The municipality is the location of the city of [[San Luis Rio Colorado]] (its municipal seat), the community of [[El Golfo de Santa Clara]], and the [[Bahia Adair]] biosphere preserve.

==Geography==
The municipality is in the [[Lower Colorado River Valley]], with the [[Colorado River]] along its west,  the [[U.S.-Mexico border]] along the north, and to the [[Colorado River Delta]] on the southwest, and the [[Gulf of California|Gulf of California (Sea of Cortez)]] along the south.

===Adjacent jurisdictions===
* [[Yuma County, Arizona|Yuma County]], [[Arizona]] — north
* [[Puerto Peñasco (municipality)|Municipality of Puerto Peñasco]], [[Sonora]] — east
* [[Mexicali Municipality|Municipality of Mexicali]] and [[Colorado River]], [[Baja California]]  — west
* [[Gulf of California|Gulf of California (Sea of Cortes)]] — south
