'''Grão Mogol''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the state of [[Minas Gerais]].  In 2020 the population was 15,890 in a total area of 3,890&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/grao-mogol/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation is 829 meters.  It became a municipality in 1840.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/graomogol/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080823043305/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/graomogol/ |archive-date=2008-08-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
Grão Mogol is located in an isolated area between [[Montes Claros]] and the [[Jequitinhonha River]].  The distance to the state capital, [[Belo Horizonte]], is 551&nbsp;km.  Other distances are: Brasília, 930; Montes Claros, 157; Rio de Janeiro, 1,080; Salvador, 894; and São Paulo, 1,215.

Grão Mogol is also an IBGE statistical microregion including the following municipalities: [[Botumirim]], [[Cristália]], Grão Mogol, [[Itacambira]], [[Josenópolis]], and [[Padre Carvalho]].  The population of this region was 39,406 in 2000 and the area was 9,108.00&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080224162737/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |archive-date=2008-02-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==History==

The settlement of Serra de Santo Antônio do Itacambiraçu, present-day Grão Mogol, had it origins in the discovery of diamonds at the end of the eighteenth century.  In 1839 the place was called Arraial da Serra de Grão Mogol and soon began to attract people from all over the country including foreigners to search for diamonds.

The site began to be noticed by the Portuguese crown for its commerce of diamonds and soon the government took over the mining and the commercialization.  In 1840 what was only a "arraial" was made a "Vila Provincial" and in the same year it became a district.

In 1858, Grão Mogol received the category of city. For decades, it was considered the most important city in the north of the state of Minas Gerais.    When the diamonds ran out the city began to lose population, especially after 1960, coinciding with the loss of territory to form the municipalities of [[Itacambira]], [[Cristália]] and [[Botumirim]].

Today the colonial buildings and the popular traditions make Grão Mogol an important touristic attraction.<ref>[http://www.graomogol.mg.gov.br/portal1/municipio/historia.asp?iIdMun=100131317 City government site]</ref>

==Economic activities==

The most important economic activities are cattle raising (19,000 head in 2006) and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$50,489,000.  The most important agricultural crops are bananas, coffee, tropical and citrus fruits, peanuts, rice, sugarcane, and corn.  In the rural area there were 1,737 producers.  The total area of agricultural land was 183,695 hectares in 2006.  Pasture made up 56,000 ha. and 76,000 ha. were in woodland.  In the urban area there was 1 banking agency in 2006.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

==Tourism==

Besides the old historical buildings of the town there is an interesting church, Igreja Matriz de Santo Antônio, built in the nineteenth century by slaves for the white population. In the rural area there are many natural attractions to please the visitor.  The most important is the Canyon do Extrema, made up of deep pools of water and several waterfalls.<ref>[http://www.graomogol.mg.gov.br/portal1/municipio/ponto_turistico.asp?iIdMun=100131317 City site]</ref>
The municipality contains the {{convert|28404|ha|adj=on}} [[Grão Mogol State Park]], created in 1998.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual de Grão Mogol – IEF}}|title=Parque Estadual de Grão Mogol|publisher=IEF: Instituto Estadual de Florestas|url=http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/areas-protegidas/202?task=view|access-date=2017-01-17|archive-date=2017-04-01|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170401171654/http://www.ief.mg.gov.br/areas-protegidas/202?task=view|url-status=dead}}</ref>
