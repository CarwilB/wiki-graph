'''Jauja''' is a [[Peru]]vian province. It is one of the nine provinces of the [[Junín Region]]. To the north it borders with the provinces of [[Yauli Province|Yauli]], [[Tarma Province|Tarma]] and [[Chanchamayo Province|Chanchamayo]]. To the east with the province of [[Satipo Province|Satipo]], to the south with the province of  [[Concepción Province, Peru|Concepción]] and to the west with the [[Lima Region]]. The capital of the province is the city of [[Jauja]] (Quechua: Shausha or Sausa). The city was founded by [[Francisco Pizarro]] as the first capital of Peru.

Before the arrival of the [[Spain|Spaniards]] the province was called ''Hatun Xauxa'' (Quechua spelling variants: Hatun Shawsha or Hatun Sausa) and it was the main center of the nation ''Hatunwanka Xauxa'', that consisted of Tawantinsuyu after the expansion of Pachakutiq [[Inca]].

== Geography ==
The province lies in the [[Nor Yauyos-Cochas Landscape Reserve]]. The [[Paryaqaqa mountain range|Paryaqaqa]] mountain range traverses the province. One of the highest mountains of the province is [[Paryaqaqa]] (Tulluqutu) at {{convert|5750|m|ft|0}}.
