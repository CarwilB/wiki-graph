'''Paute Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in the [[Azuay Province]]. It was established on February 26, 1860.  Its capital is the town of [[Paute]].  It has a population of 25,494 as per the 2010 census.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref>
Its current mayor is Raul Delgado and Freddy Gonzales as vice-mayor.
