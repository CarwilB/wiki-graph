'''Lajes''' is a municipality in [[Rio Grande do Norte]], [[Brazil]]. [[Pico do Cabugi]] is located there. On January 1, 1929, [[Alzira Soriano]] was sworn mayor of the city, becoming the first female mayor in Brazil and in all [[South America]].<ref>[http://www.tre-rn.gov.br/nova/inicial/institucional/historico/a_mulher_na_politica_nacional/alzirabi.php Tribunal Regional Eleitoral do Rio Grande do Norte] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090313010246/http://www.tre-rn.gov.br/nova/inicial/institucional/historico/a_mulher_na_politica_nacional/alzirabi.php |date=2009-03-13 }}</ref>
