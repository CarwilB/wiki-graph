'''Valle de Zaragoza ''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Valle de Zaragoza]]. 

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 5,105,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Valle de Zaragoza|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=067|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014|archive-date=5 March 2016|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160305011327/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=067|url-status=dead}}</ref> up from 4,341 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title=Valle de Zaragoza|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08067a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 13, 2008}}</ref>

==Geography==
As of 2010, the town of Valle de Zaragoza had a population of 2,223.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Valle de Zaragoza, the municipality had 156 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
