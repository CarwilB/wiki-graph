The '''San Javier Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento San Javier''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the {{province|Santa Fe|Argentina}}. It is located on the northeast of the province, with its eastern border coinciding with the provincial border along the [[Paraná River]]. 

The department's head town is the city of [[San Javier, Santa Fe|San Javier]], which has over 15,000 inhabitants (more than half the population of the department). The department also includes the towns of [[Alejandra, Argentina|Alejandra]], [[Cacique Ariacaiquín]], [[Colonia Durán]], [[La Brava, Argentina|La Brava]], and [[Romang, Santa Fe|Romang]].

San Javier Department borders the departments of [[Garay Department|Garay]] (south), [[San Justo Department, Santa Fe|San Justo]] (southwest), [[Vera Department|Vera]] (west), and [[General Obligado Department|General Obligado]] (north).
