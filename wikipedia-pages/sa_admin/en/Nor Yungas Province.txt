'''Nor Yungas''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[Yungas]] area of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Departments of Bolivia|department]] of [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz]].<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091027220541/http://www.ine.gov.bo/ |date=2009-10-27 }} (Spanish)</ref> During the presidency of [[José Manuel Pando]] the Yungas Province was divided into two parts, the Nor Yungas and the [[Sud Yungas Province]], by law of January 12, 1899. Its administrative seat is the town of [[Coroico]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.gobernacionlapaz.gob.bo/index.php/conoce/la-paz/estructura/estructura-fisica/286-provincia-nor-yungas.html |title=Official website |access-date=2012-04-14 |archive-date=2012-09-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120928001514/http://www.gobernacionlapaz.gob.bo/index.php/conoce/la-paz/estructura/estructura-fisica/286-provincia-nor-yungas.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>
