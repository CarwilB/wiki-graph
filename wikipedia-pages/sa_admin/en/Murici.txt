'''Murici''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[mʊɾiˈsiː]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the east of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Alagoas]]. Its population is 28,333 (2020) and its area is {{cvt|424|km2}}.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php]</ref> According to the Veja magazine,<ref>Jimenez, Gabriele (2013), "Parada no Tempo". Abril press, April 17th, 2013, pg. 88.</ref> despite receiving generous public transfers, the city still lags behind most municipalities in Brazil in human development. 30.6% of its inhabitants are illiterate and 65% rely on Federal funds to survive, which contribute for an HDI of just 0.58, slightly below Iraq's HDI of 0.59.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://veja.abril.com.br/politica/murici-a-cidade-onde-a-familia-calheiros-se-reveza-no-poder/|title=Murici, a cidade onde a família Calheiros se reveza no poder|website=VEJA.com}}</ref>

The municipality contains 61% of the {{convert|6116|ha}} [[Murici Ecological Station]], created in 2001.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|ESEC de Murici (ESEC) – ISA}}|language=pt
