'''Salazar de las Palmas''' ({{IPA|es|salaˈsaɾ ðe las ˈpalmas}}) is a Colombian [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] and town located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Norte de Santander Department|North of Santander]].

== Etymology ==
The municipality bears the name of Salazar in honor of the [[oidor]] {{Ill|Alonso Pérez de Salazar|lt=Alonso Pérez de Salazar|es|Alonso Pérez de Salazar}}, in addition to the fact that "Las Palmas" refers to the fact that when the town was established it was characterized by a large vegetation of palm trees.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Mohalem |first=José de Jesús Acosta |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=Mb4RAQAAIAAJ&q=1583+Salazar+de+las+Palmas |title=Historia de la Iglesia en Pamplona, siglos XVI, XVII y XVIII |date=2000 |publisher=Arquidiocesis de Nueva Pamplona |language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
In 1583, Captain Alonso Esteban Rangel founded Salazar de las Palmas.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Verea |first=Francisco Pérez de Salazar |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=jaloAAAAMAAJ&q=1583+Salazar+de+las+Palmas |title=Semblanzas e historia de una familia en la Puebla de los Angeles |date=1998 |publisher=F.P. de Salazar Verea |isbn=978-970-92073-0-9 |language=es}}</ref> The village was established to facilitate access to the [[Magdalena River|Madgalena River]] in the [[New Kingdom of Granada]], in addition to creating a new frontier with the [[Barí people|Motilones]] natives.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Grafenstein |first=Johanna von |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=sZXtAAAAMAAJ&q=1583+Salazar+de+las+Palmas |title=El Golfo-Caribe y sus puertos: 1600-1850 |date=2006 |publisher=Instituto Mora |isbn=978-970-684-134-6 |language=es}}</ref>

== Heritage sites ==
The construction of the parish church of San Pablo began in 1889, it was blessed by the bishop of the [[Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Nueva Pamplona|Diocese of Nueva Pamplona]], Evaristo Blanco in 1915.<ref name=":0">{{Cite book |last=Silva |first=Ramón Cárdenas |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=ztxrAAAAMAAJ&q=%22G%C3%A9nesis+de+Salazar+de+las+Palmas%22+%22doscientos+cincuenta+mil%22 |title=Génesis de Salazar de las Palmas, 1583-1983 |date=1983 |publisher=Tipograf. Unión |page=116 |language=es}}</ref> It is estimated that the cost of the construction of the church was two hundred and fifty thousand dollars.<ref name=":0" /> As of 1953, the municipal seat had two churches, the parish of San Pablo with a modern style architecture and the old chapel of Belén, made of adobe and tapia materials.<ref name=":1">{{Cite book |last=Ramírez |first=Jesús Emilio |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=KxO0AAAAIAAJ&q=Salazar+de+las+Palmas+San+Pablo |title=El terremoto de Arboledas, Cucutilla y Salazar de Las Palmas, 8 de julio, 1950 |date=1953 |publisher=Instituto Geofísico de los Andes Colombianos |page=39 |language=es}}</ref> The Belén chapel suffered damage to one of its towers after the [[1875 Cúcuta earthquake|earthquake of 1875]].<ref name=":1" /> The chapel has an [[retable]] of Nuestra Señora de Belén, which is believed to have been found by a woman named Lucía in one of the stones of the Trinidad Creek in the 1600s.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Villamizar |first=Néstor Cuadros |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=xDsUAQAAIAAJ&q=%22Salazar+de+las+Palmas%22+Capilla+Retablo |title=Luis Elberto Uribe Bueno: su tierra, vida y obra, memorias |date=1999 |publisher=Alcaldía de Salazar de las Palmas |isbn=978-958-33-1041-6 |page=59 |language=es}}</ref>

== Nature reserves ==
The {{Ill|Santurban Regional Natural Park in Salazar de las Palmas|lt=Santurban Regional Natural Park in Salazar de las Palmas|es|Parque natural regional Santurbán-Salazar de las Palmas}} comprises approximately 19,088 hectares delimited by CORPONOR.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Corponor declara 19.088 hectáreas como Parque Natural Regional Santurbán- Salazar de las Palmas. |url=https://corponor.gov.co/sitioanterior/index.php/comunicaciones/historico-de-noticias/1688-corponor-declara-19088-hectareas-como-parque-natural-regional-santurban-salazar-de-las-palmas |access-date=2022-08-28 |website=CORPONOR}}</ref> The municipality also has several lagoons such as Los Bueyes, Peroneo and Triaca. Salazar has the northern lagoon complex of Cáchira (Spanish: ''Complejo Lagunar Norte Cáchira''), which also includes parts of the Arboledas and Cáchira municipalities.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2009 |title=Estudio del estado actual y plan de manejo del Páramo de Santurbán en el departamento de Norte de Santander |url=https://corponor.gov.co/areasnaturalesestrategicas/descargas/Resumen%20Ejecutivo%20EA%20y%20PMA%20%20Santurban_Corponor_2009.pdf |access-date=2022-08-28 |website=CORPONOR}}</ref> The Regional Natural Park, abbreviated as PNR, was declared by agreement number 020 of 2013 and it is located in the northern lagoon complex.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2015-11-19 |title=Estaticos |url=https://corponor.gov.co/dev/index.php/es/estaticos/60-estaticos?start=10 |access-date=2022-08-28 |website=CORPONOR}}</ref>
