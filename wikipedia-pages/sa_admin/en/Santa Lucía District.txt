'''Santa Lucía''' is the fifth [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Barva canton]], in the [[Heredia Province|Heredia province]] of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |lang=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== Toponymy ==

The name alludes to [[Saint Lucy|Saint Lucia of Syracuse]].

== History ==

=== 1998 Tornado ===

On September 22, 1998 at 13:30, the district and its surroundings were impacted for 10 minutes by the most damaging tornado recorded in the country, with 179 damaged houses, affecting 600 people. The {{lang|es|Instituto Meteorológico Nacional}} (National Weather Institute) still catalogued the event as weak.<ref>{{cite news |title=Santa Lucía solidaria |url=https://www.nacion.com/el-pais/santa-lucia-solidaria/L3DBWCMB3VFOBOYIXI4VCUTKOM/story/ |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=24 September 1998}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Tornado embistió barrio de Heredia |url=http://wvw.nacion.com/ln_ee/1998/septiembre/23/pais1.html |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=23 September 1998}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |title=Condiciones aptas para más tornados |url=https://www.nacion.com/el-pais/condiciones-aptas-para-mas-tornados/LH32XFGNQRGBVBWF4RPNDHWZ3U/story/ |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=12 June 2002}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Santa Lucía has an area of {{formatnum:2.87}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |lang=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:1159}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It is located in the [[Greater Metropolitan Area (Costa Rica)|Greater Metropolitan Area]], in the central valley of the country, its borders are to the north with the Barva and [[San Pablo District, Barva|San Pablo]] districts of the Barva canton, to the east the [[Ángeles District, San Rafael|Ángeles]] district of [[San Rafael (canton)|San Rafael]] canton, to the south the [[San Josécito District, San Rafael|San Josecito]] district of San Rafael canton, and to the west the [[Mercedes District, Heredia|Mercedes]] district of the [[Heredia (canton)|Heredia]] canton.

Three {{lang|es|quebradas}} (creeks, small rivers) cross the district from east to west: Seca creek, Burío creek and Palmar creek, the last two converge as tributaries that form the Burío river in the southern limit of the district.

=== Residential areas ===

There are several residential area completed projects: Urbanización Jardines de Santa Lucía, Urbanización Antiesquivo, Residencial Jardines del Beneficio, Residencial Malinche Real, Urbanización Villa Esmeralda, Residencial La Ronda, Urbanización Don Álvaro, Urbanización Doña Iris, Urbanización El Tajo.

== Economy ==

The district is mostly a residential area, with small businesses to attend the necessities of its inhabitants.  There is an still operating coffee receiving and processing plant, the {{lang|es|Beneficio de Café Juan León e Hijos}}, and small coffee orchards.

== Education ==

=== Public education ===

There is one {{lang|es|Centro de Cuido y Desarrollo Infantil (CECUDI)}} (daycare center) named {{lang|es|Sueños de Algodón}} (Cotton Dreams),<ref>{{cite news |title=Municipalidad de Barva abrió "Sueños de Algodón" |url=http://elflorense.com/provincial/municipalidad-de-barva-abrio-suenos-de-algodon/ |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=18 September 2014}}</ref> and also one public primary school named {{lang|es|Escuela Domingo González Pérez}}.<ref>{{cite web |title=Escuela Domingo González Pérez |url=https://es-la.facebook.com/EscuelaDomingoGonzalezPerez/}}</ref>

There are offsite research facilities of the {{lang|es|Escuela de Ciencias Agrarias}} (Agrarian Sciences School) of [[National University of Costa Rica]] (UNA), called the {{lang|es|Finca Experimental Santa Lucía}} (Santa Lucía Experimental Farm).<ref>{{cite web |title=Finca Experimental Santa Lucía |url=http://www.directorio.una.ac.cr/?wpbdp_listing=finca-experimental-santa-lucia-escuela-de-ciencias-agrarias}}</ref> Also the {{lang|es|Instituto de Investigación y Servicios Forestales}} of UNA is located in the district.<ref>{{cite web |title=Instituto de Investigación y Servicios Forestales |url=http://www.inisefor.una.ac.cr/}}</ref>

=== Private education ===
There is a private preschool and daycare center, {{lang|es|Centro Educativo Bilingüe Cosquillitas}}.

There are two private education centers, {{lang|es|Centro Educativo Montealto}},<ref>{{cite web |title=Centro Educativo Montealto |url=http://www.montealto.cr/}}</ref> that teaches preschool, primary school, and high school, and {{lang|es|Centro Educativo Shkénuk Montessori}}<ref>{{cite web |title=Centro Educativo Shkénuk Montessori |url=https://www.slmontessorishkenuk.com/}}</ref>

== Culture ==

=== Museum ===

The {{lang|es|[[Museo de Cultura Popular]]}} (Popular Culture Museum) is located in the district.

=== Cultural center ===

There are plans to build the {{lang|es|Centro Cultural de Bandas}} (Musical bands cultural center) in the district, however there are allegations of corruption in the contracting procedures.<ref>{{cite news |title=Diputados denuncian piñatas en el ICE |url=https://delfino.cr/2019/03/diputados-denuncian-pinatas-en-el-ice |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=7 March 2019}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Centro de Bandas de Heredia |url=http://lacayoarquitectos.com/?portfolio=centro-de-bandas-de-heredia |website=Arquitecto Daniel Lacayo y Asoc. |accessdate=5 October 2019}}</ref>

=== Leisure ===

There is a {{lang|es|Salón Comunal}} (community center), several urban parks and a public soccer pitch.

The {{lang|es|Centro de Recreo}} (leisure center) of the workers union of the National University of Costa Rica is also located in the district.<ref>{{cite web |title=Centro de Recreo FOBESO |url=https://centroderecreo.fobeso.com/index.php/acerca-de}}</ref>

=== Religion ===

In 2002 the Catholic church of the district, {{lang|es|Iglesia de Santa Lucía Virgen y Mártir}} which was a [[filial church]] of the {{lang|es|Parroquia de San Bartolomé Apóstol}} in the Barva district, became the {{lang|es|Parroquia de Santa Lucía Virgen y Mártir}}, a parish with its own filial church of {{lang|es|Iglesia de la Medalla Milagrosa}} in the neighboring Barrio Peralta of San Josecito district, in the San Rafael canton.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Araya Campos |first1=Ana Priscilla |title=Barva: Identidad, Patrimonio y Tradición |url=http://www.kerwa.ucr.ac.cr/bitstream/handle/10669/75184/Barva.%20Identidad%2C%20Patrimonio%20y%20Tradici%C3%B3n.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y |accessdate=5 October 2019}}</ref>

== Health Care ==

There is one {{lang|en|Equipos Básicos de Atención Integral en Salud}} (Ebais) which provides basic first line health care, managed by the [[Costa Rican Social Security Fund]].

Also a {{lang|es|Hogar Crea}} for the rehabilitation and treatment of young persons with addiction issues.

== Public utilities ==

The {{lang|es|Empresa de Servicios Públicos de Heredia}} (Heredia Public Utilities Company), ESPH, is the main provider of electricity and potable water in the district. There are two [[Well|water wells]] to extract water for their users in this and other districts, the {{lang|es|Pozo Santa Lucía}} and {{lang|es|Pozo Malinche Real}}, and two water tanks of their network, {{lang|es|Tanque Antiesquivo}} and {{lang|es|Tanque Santa Lucía}}.<ref>{{cite web |title=ESPH Mapa |url=https://www.esph-sa.com/sites/default/files/inline-files/Mapa%20Barva%20Santa%20Luc%C3%ADa.pdf }}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

There are plans by ESPH for a sewage treatment plant for this and neighboring districts, as the community relies on residential [[septic tank]]s, which is common in the country.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Córdoba |first1=Juan Diego |title=Construcción de sistema para tratar aguas residuales en Heredia comenzará en 2020 |url=https://www.nacion.com/el-pais/servicios/construccion-de-sistema-para-tratar-aguas/GOCJOVCIEVFHRKU53CHUBCO3LQ/story/ |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=2 October 2019}}</ref>

Telecommunications include [[Fiber to the x|fiber optic to the home]] (Provided by ICE Kölbi),<ref>{{cite news |last1=Mora |first1=María José |title=Vecinos de Heredia y Alajuela ya cuentan con fibra óptica del ICE |url=https://www.larepublica.net/noticia/vecinos-de-heredia-y-alajuela-ya-cuentan-con-fibra-optica-del-ice |accessdate=5 October 2019 |date=30 August 2019}}</ref> cable television and full cellular telephony coverage by ICE Kölbi, Claro and Movistar.
