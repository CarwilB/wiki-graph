'''Trancas''' is a department located in the northern part of [[Tucumán Province]], Argentina. 
It is bounded to the west and north by [[Salta Province]], on the east by the Department of [[Burruyacú]], to the south by the Department of [[Tafi Viejo]] and to the southwest by the Department of [[Tafí del Valle]].  The headquarters is the town of [[Trancas, Argentina|Trancas]].

==Population==

According to the 2010 Census, 17,541 people lived in the apartment people. This number made it the third least populated department of the province, 
after Barns and Tafi del Valle.<ref>[http://www.censo2010.indec.gov.ar/preliminares/cuadro_tucuman.asp "Provincia de Tucumán.
