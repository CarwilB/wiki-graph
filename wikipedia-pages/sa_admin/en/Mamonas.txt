'''Mamonas''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. As of 2020 the population was 6,554 in a total area of 290&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/mamonas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> The elevation is 681 meters. It became a municipality in 1993. The postal code (CEP) is 39516-000.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|53264|ha|adj=on}} [[Caminho dos Gerais State Park]], created in 2007.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Caminho das Gerais – Via Rural}}|title=Parque Estadual Caminho das Gerais|publisher=Via Rural|url=http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-caminho-dos-gerais/default.htm|access-date=2017-01-18|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170131182959/http://br.viarural.com/servicos/turismo/parques-estaduais/parque-estadual-caminho-dos-gerais/default.htm|archive-date=2017-01-31|url-status=usurped}}</ref>
Mamonas is part of the statistical microregion of [[Janaúba]]. It is connected by poor roads to the regional center of Janaúba to the south. 
Neighboring municipalities are:  [[Espinosa, Minas Gerais|Espinosa]], [[Monte Azul]], and [[Gameleiras]]. 

With irrigation Jaíba has managed to produce a range of agricultural products.  The main economic activities are cattle raising (12,000 head in 2006) and farming with production of cotton, citrus fruits, beans (1,220 ha.), corn (4,600 ha.), and sorghum. In 2006 there were 1,205 rural producers with a total area of 15,958 hectares. Cropland made up 1,800 hectares and natural pasture 10,000 hectares. There were only 5 tractors. In the urban area there were no financial institutions as of 2006. There were 240 automobiles, giving a ratio of about one automobile for every 30 inhabitants. The Gross Domestic Product was R$ 15,529,000 (2005). Health care was provided by 4 public health clinics. There were no hospitals (2005).
