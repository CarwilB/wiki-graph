'''Ciénega''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsjeneɣa}}) is a [[municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in the [[Márquez Province]], part of [[Boyacá Department]], [[Colombia]]. The urban centre of Ciénega is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at a distance of {{convert|27|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] at an elevation of {{convert|2460|m|ft}}. The elevation ranges within the municipality from {{convert|2200|m|ft}} to {{convert|3400|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[Viracachá]] and [[Soracá]] in the north, [[Rondón, Boyacá|Rondón]] in the east and south and [[Ramiriquí]] in the south and west.<ref name=websiteCienega>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150530104152/http://www.cienega-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Ciénega]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Ciénega comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "Place of water".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Ciénega] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
The area around Ciénega in the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] was part of the [[Muisca Confederation]], a loose confederation of different [[Muisca rulers|rulers]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. Ciénega was ruled by the ''[[zaque]]'' based in [[Tunja|Hunza]]. On his way to the legendary ''[[El Dorado]]'', [[conquistador]] [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] passed through Ciénega.

Modern Ciénega was founded on October 22, 1818 by José Cayetano Vasquez, son of the owner of the main [[hacienda]] in the village.<ref name=websiteCienega/>

== Economy ==
Main economical activities of Ciénega are [[livestock]] farming (66 %) and [[agriculture]] (31 %). Among the agricultural products cultivated are [[potato]]es, [[maize]], [[pea]]s and [[arracacha]], [[tree tomato]] and [[uchuva]].<ref name=websiteCienega/>
