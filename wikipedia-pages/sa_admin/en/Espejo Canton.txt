'''Espejo''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Carchi Province]]. Its capital is the town of [[El Ángel, Ecuador|El Ángel]].  Its population in the 2001 census was 13,515 and in the 2010 census was 13,364.<ref>[http://www.statoids.com/yec.html Cantons of Ecuador] at statoids.com</ref> Espejo is located in the [[Andes]] and El Ángel is situated at an elevation of {{convert|3010|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref>Google Earth</ref>
[[File:El Angel.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The canton capital of El Ángel.]] 
Espejo canton contains the parishes of El Ángel (Anhil), El Goaltal, La Libertad (Alizo), and San Isidro.
