'''Jordânia''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the northeast of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Immediate Geographic Region of Almenara]]. {{as of|2020}} the population was 10,842 in a total area of 549&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/jordania/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Jordânia is located on the state boundary with [[Bahia]]. It is linked to the major population center of Almenara by poor roads. The elevation is 198 meters. It became a municipality in 1948.

==Economy==
The main economic activities are cattle raising and subsistence farming. The GDP was R$27,629,000 in 2005. The main cash crop was coffee. There were 143 automobiles in 2007. No banking agencies were listed for that year. In the rural zone there were 611 producers with a total planted area of 1,500 hectares. Most of the land was in natural pasture or woodland. Only 10 of the farms had tractors in 2006.
