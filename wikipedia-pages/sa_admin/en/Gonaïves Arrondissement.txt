'''Gonaïves''' ({{langx|ht|Gonayiv}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Artibonite (department)|Artibonite]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].
It has 263,858 inhabitants.<ref>''Institut Haïtien de Statistique et d'Informatique'' (IHSI), 7 August 2003.</ref>
Postal codes in the Gonaïves Arrondissement start with the number 41.

The arrondissement consists of the following municipalities:
* [[Gonaïves]]
* [[Ennery, Artibonite|Ennery]]
* [[L'Estère]]
