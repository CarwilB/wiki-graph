'''Anse-à-Veau''' ({{IPA|fr|ɑ̃s a vo}}; {{langx|ht|Ansavo}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Anse-à-Veau Arrondissement]], in the [[Nippes]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. The postal code is HT 7510.

In the aftermath of the [[2010 Haiti earthquake]], Anse-a-Veau was swollen by refugees from the afflicted areas. Aid to the community after the quake have been airdropped. As of 12 March 2010, 6 weeks after the quake, [[HMAS Choules (L100)|RFA Largs Bay]] arrived off the coast and made a large aid drop.<ref>Relief Web, [http://www.reliefweb.int/rw/rwb.nsf/db900SID/SKEA-83GJBJ?OpenDocument "RFA Largs Bay delivers aid to isolated Haitian village"], '''Government of the United Kingdom''', ''12 March 2010'' (accessed 12 March 2010)</ref>
