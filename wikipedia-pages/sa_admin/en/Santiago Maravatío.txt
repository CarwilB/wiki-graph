'''Santiago Maravatío''' is a Mexican city (and [[Municipalities of Guanajuato|municipality]]) located in the [[Bajío]] (''lowlands'') of the state of [[Guanajuato]]. With an area of 91.760 square kilometres, Santiago Maravatío accounts for less than 1% of the surface of the state.  It is bordered to the north, east and southeast by [[Salvatierra, Guanajuato|Salvatierra]] and to the west by [[Yuriria]].

Santiago Maravatío was founded on July 25, 1540 and was named after [[James, son of Zebedee|St. James]] (Apóstol Santiago el Mayor), the [[patron saint]] of [[Spain]], in what had been a [[Chichimeca]] community (Maravatío in Purepecha means "Precious Place").

The municipality had a total of 7,151 inhabitants as of 2006.<ref>[http://www.guanajuato.gob.mx/index.php?option=com_wrapper&Itemid=63&indicadores_id=135 Gobierno del Estado de Guanajuato – Detalle de Indicador<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref> The municipal president of Santiago Maravatío and its ten microregions is Leonel Flores.  The economy of the municipality is mostly based on agriculture and [[ranching]].<ref>[http://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Estatal/GUANAJUATO/Municipios/Santiago%20Maravatio/SanBan01.pdf Microsoft Word - SanBan01.doc<!-- Bot generated title -->]</ref>
