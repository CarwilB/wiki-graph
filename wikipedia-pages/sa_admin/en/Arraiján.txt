'''Arraiján  ''' is a city and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Arraiján District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 41,041 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Arraiján District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 24,665; its population as of 2000 was 64,772.<ref name=inec/>
