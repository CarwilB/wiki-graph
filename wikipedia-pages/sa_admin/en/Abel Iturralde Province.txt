'''Abel Iturralde''' is one of the twenty provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]]. It is situated in its northern part. Its name honors [[Abel Iturralde Palacios]], a Bolivian politician. [[Madidi|Madidi National Park]] is partially in this province.

== Location ==

Abel Iturralde province is located between 11° 48' and 14° 35' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 55' and 69° 05' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 300&nbsp;km from North to South, and 200&nbsp;km from east to west. 

The province is situated on the northern edges of the [[Cordillera Central (Bolivia)|Cordillera Central]]. It is bordered to the west  by the [[Madre de Dios River|Madre de Dios]] and [[Heath River]]s and to the east by the [[Beni River|Río Beni]]. The province borders [[Pando Department]] in the north, [[Peru]] in the west, [[Franz Tamayo Province]] in the south, and [[Beni Department|Beni]] in the east.

== Population ==
The population of Abel Iturralde Province has increased by 100% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 8,226 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 11,828 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 14,060 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2012: 18,073 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosLaPaz.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística - Proyecciones] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080911211201/http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosLaPaz.pdf |date=2008-09-11 }}</ref>
*2024: 22,602 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Bolivia: Administrative Division (Departments and Provinces) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>
 
98.3% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 12.1% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], 6.7% [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 9.8% other [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] languages. ''(2001)''  

The [[Literacy|literacy rate]] of the province's population is 83.5%. ''(1992)'' 

83.8% of the population have no access to electricity, 58.7% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

87.0% of the population are Catholics, 10.6% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]]:
*[[Ixiamas Municipality]] - 5,625 inhabitants ''(2005)''
*[[San Buenaventura Municipality, La Paz|San Buenaventura Municipality]] - 6,203 inhabitants
