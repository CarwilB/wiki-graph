'''Arcoverde''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located in the [[Mesoregion (geography)|mesoregion]] of ''[[Sertão Pernambucano]]''.<ref>{{Cite web |title=BDE Mesorregiões |url=http://www.bde.pe.gov.br/estruturacaogeral/mesorregioes.aspx}}</ref> Arcoverde has a total area of 344 km²<ref>{{Cite web |title=IBGE |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/pe/arcoverde/panorama |access-date=2024-02-24 |website=cidades.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and lies west of the capital Recife by 256 km. Arcoverde has boundaries with [[Paraíba]] to the north, [[Buíque]] and [[Pedra, Pernambuco|Pedra]] to the south, [[Pesqueira, Pernambuco|Pesqueira]] to the east, and [[Sertânia]] to the west.

In the [[2022 Brazilian census|census of 2022]], Arcoverde had a population of 77,742 people,<ref>{{Cite web |date=2023-06-28 |title=População de Arcoverde (PE) é de 77.586 pessoas, aponta o Censo do IBGE |url=https://g1.globo.com/pe/caruaru-regiao/noticia/2023/06/28/populacao-de-arcoverde-pe-e-de-77-586-pessoas-aponta-o-censo-do-ibge.ghtml |access-date=2024-02-24 |website=G1 |language=pt-br}}</ref> making it the 22nd most populous municipality in Pernambuco and the third most populous in the ''Sertão Pernambucano'' region, trailing only [[Serra Talhada]]<ref>{{Cite web |title=Serra Talhada (PE) {{!}} Cidades e Estados {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/pe/serra-talhada.html |access-date=2024-02-24 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref> and [[Araripina]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Araripina (PE) {{!}} Cidades e Estados {{!}} IBGE |url=https://www.ibge.gov.br/cidades-e-estados/pe/araripina.html |access-date=2024-02-24 |website=www.ibge.gov.br}}</ref>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Arcoverde are based in general [[commerce]], services and primary sector.
