'''Cortazar''' is a city and its surrounding [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in the southeastern quadrant of the state of [[Guanajuato]] in [[Mexico]]. It is bordered to the north by [[Villagrán, Guanajuato|Villagrán]], to the north and east by [[Celaya]], to the southeast by [[Tarimoro]],  to the south by [[Salvatierra, Guanajuato|Salvatierra]], and to the west by [[Jaral del Progreso]] and [[Salamanca, Guanajuato|Salamanca]]. The city had a 2005 census population of 57,748, while the municipality had a population of 83,175.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |title=2005 Census |work=INEGI: Instituto Nacional de Estadística, Geografía e Informática |accessdate=2007-03-22 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130406130712/http://www.inegi.gob.mx/est/contenidos/espanol/sistemas/conteo2005/localidad/iter/ |archive-date=2013-04-06 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |title=Mexican Municipality Encyclopedia |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |accessdate=2007-03-22 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070317205520/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/wb2/ELOCAL/EMM_guanajuato |archive-date=2007-03-17 }}</ref> The municipality has an area of 335.2&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup> (129.4 sq mi) and includes many smaller outlying communities, the largest of which are Tierra Fría to the west and [[Cañada, Guanajuato, Mexico|La Cañada de Caracheo]] to the south.

In [[pre-Columbian]] times the region was inhabited primarily by [[Otomi people|Otomí]] and [[Nahua]]s people. Founded in 1721 by [[Franciscan|Franciscan friars]], the village of San José de los Amoles was under the order of the congress of Guanajuato given the present name of Cortázar in 1857 after [[Luis Cortazar y Rábago]], a Mexican patriot and leader of Mexico's war of independence against [[Spain]].

The municipal president of the city and its microregions is [[Elías Ruiz Ramírez]] of the National Action Party, since 2009.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://v2.pan.org.mx/pics/gobierno/edo_gto/cortazar.htm |title=Información General |accessdate=2007-06-23 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070703095806/http://v2.pan.org.mx/pics/gobierno/edo_gto/cortazar.htm |archive-date=2007-07-03 }}</ref>
