'''Aripuanã''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[states of Brazil|state]] of [[Mato Grosso]] in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West Region]] of [[Brazil]]. It is located on the banks of the [[Aripuanã River]].

The city is served by [[Aripuanã Airport]].

The municipality contains the {{convert|8517|ha}} [[Rio Flor do Prado Ecological Station]], a fully protected environmental unit created in 2003.<ref>{{citation|access-date=2016-06-05|language=pt|author=Blairo Borges Maggi|date=9 December 2003|title=Decreto 2.124 de 09 de Dezembro de 2003|url=http://www.sad-legislacao.mt.gov.br/Aplicativos/Sad-Legislacao/legislacaosad.nsf/709f9c981a9d9f468425671300482be0/affae9616cc70ce28425706f00585aba?OpenDocument|archive-date=2016-08-21|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160821100152/http://www.sad-legislacao.mt.gov.br/Aplicativos/Sad-Legislacao/legislacaosad.nsf/709f9c981a9d9f468425671300482be0/affae9616cc70ce28425706f00585aba?OpenDocument|url-status=dead}}</ref>
It also holds part of the {{convert|164224|ha}} [[Guariba-Roosevelt Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use unit created in 1996.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RESEX Guariba-Roosevelt – ISA}}|language=pt |title=RESEX Guariba-Roosevelt|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/2831|access-date=2016-06-07}}</ref>
