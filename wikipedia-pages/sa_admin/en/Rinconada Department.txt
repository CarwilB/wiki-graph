'''Rinconada''' is a department in the [[province of Jujuy]] in the [[Argentina]]. According to INDEC its population in 2010 was 2,489 inhabitants. Its main town is [[Rinconada, Jujuy|Rinconada]]. The town of [[Mina Pirquitas]] represents the highest point in Argentina, standing at 4,271&nbsp;m above sea level.

==Seismicity==
The seismicity of the area of Jujuy is frequent and low intensity, and mean to severe seismic silence every 40 years.

* 1863 earthquake: This earthquake on January 4, 1863,<ref name="Scielo">{{Cite web |url=http://www.scielo.org.ar/scielo.php?pid=S0004-48222006000400011&script=sci_arttext |title=Fenómenos de licuefacción asociados a terremotos históricos. Su análisis en la evaluación del peligro sísmico en la Argentina |accessdate=24 September 2012}}</ref> measuring 6.4 on the Richter scale, indicated an important milestone in the history of seismic events in Jujuy. It highlighted the need to impose stricter construction codes.<ref name="Historicos">{{Cite web |url=http://www.inpres.gov.ar/seismology/historicos.php |title=Terremotos históricos ocurridos en la República Argentina |accessdate=25 May 2013}}</ref>
* 1948 earthquake: The August 25, 1848, measuring 7.0 on the Richter scale, destroyed buildings and opened numerous cracks in wide areas.<ref name="Scielo"/><ref name="Historicos"/>
* 2009 earthquake: November 6, 2009, measuring 5.6 on the Richter scale.
