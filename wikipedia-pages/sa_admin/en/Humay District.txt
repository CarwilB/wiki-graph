'''Humay District''' is one of eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Pisco Province|Pisco]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=April 23, 2008 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

The '''Lily Mine''', about {{convert|40|km|abbr=on}} east of [[Pisco, Peru]], is a well-known producer of [[mineral collecting|mineral specimens]] for collectors, and rough material for decorative [[stone carving|stone-carving]].
<ref>[http://www.mindat.org/loc-6987.html Lily Mine] at [[Mindat.org]]</ref>
