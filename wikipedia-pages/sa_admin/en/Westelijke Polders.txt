'''Westelijke Polders''' is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Nickerie District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 8,616.<ref name="census"/> Its Dutch place name (in English 'western [[polders]]') reflects Suriname's colonial past. Its western boundary is the [[Courantyne River]], between Suriname and [[Guyana]].

Since 1998, the Canawaina ferry connects [[South Drain, Suriname|South Drain]], located in the resort Westelijke Polders, with [[Moleson Creek]] in [[Guyana]].<ref>Ministerie van Transport, Communicatie en Toerisme - [http://mintct.sr/parasta/transport.htm De Canawaima Ferry Service Incorporated] {{Webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20120530200131/http://mintct.sr/parasta/transport.htm |date=2012-05-30 }}</ref> This is the only legal connection between the two countries, but before the repavement of the road many travelers preferred to take a back-track route.<ref>Kaieteur News - [http://www.kaieteurnewsonline.com/2009/07/24/canawaima-ferry-service-seeks-to-boost-travel/ Canawaima Ferry Service seeks to boost travel]</ref>

The uninhabited [[nature reserve]] Papagaaieneiland is located in the Courantyne. The route of the planned bridge between Suriname and Guyana will run through the island.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.starnieuws.com/index.php/welcome/index/nieuwsitem/4332|title=Corantijnbrug zal lopen over Papagaaien Eiland |website=Star Nieuws|access-date=18 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> As of 2020, the construction has not started.
