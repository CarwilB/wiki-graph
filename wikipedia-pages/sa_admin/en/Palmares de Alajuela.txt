'''Palmares''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Palmares (canton)|Palmares]] canton, in the [[Alajuela Province|Alajuela]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |access-date=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Palmares has an area of {{formatnum:1.14}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |access-date=26 September 2020 |archive-date=16 September 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200916045223/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:1017}} metres.<ref name="divadm" /> It is in the [[Costa Rican Central Valley|Central Valley]] (Valle Central), 6 kilometers southeast of the city of [[San Ramón de Alajuela|San Ramón]], 38 kilometers northwest of the provincial capital city of [[Alajuela]], and 56 kilometers from the national [[capital city]] of [[San José, Costa Rica|San Jose]]. It is located with the [[Montes Del Aguacate]] (Avocado Mountains) and is part of the Corredor Biológico Montes del Aguacate (Montes del Aguacate Biological Corridor).<ref>{{Cite web |last=Sistema Nacional de Áreas de Conservación |title=CORREDOR BIOLÓGICO MONTES DEL AGUACATE: PLAN PARA LA GESTIÓN ESTRATÉGICA 2015-2021 |url=https://conservationcorridor.org/cpb/SINAC_2015.pdf |website=Conservation Corridor}}</ref>

==Culture==
===Fiestas de Palmares===
During January the city hosts their 'Fiestas', a large fair organized by a local committee which have become along with Zapote in December, and Liberia in July, one of the largest fairs in the country. This fair lasts for two weeks, with two days of special note: the 'Tope' (a massive horseback riding parade through town) and the 'Carnaval' (carnival); it is  said that during these days you can find over 500,000 people in Palmares.
