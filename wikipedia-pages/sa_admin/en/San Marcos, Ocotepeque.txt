'''San Marcos''' is a town, with a population of 6,836 (2013 census),<ref>[https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/ocotepeque/ Citypopulation.de] Population of all localities in the department of Ocotepeque with at least 200 inhabitants.</ref> and a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Ocotepeque department|Ocotepeque]].

This municipality of 22,000 people is located in Western [[Honduras]] bordering [[Guatemala]] and [[El Salvador]]. It's situated between the highest point in Honduras, [[Mount Meredon]], and the [[Rio Grande (Honduras)|Rio Grande]] and [[Suntulin River]]s. It was founded by Spanish and Belgian gold miners. San Marcos de Ocotepeque is a bustle of cultural activity ranging from plays to literary events.{{cn|date=January 2023}}

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, San Marcos municipality had a population of 19,978. Of these, 98.27% were [[Mestizo]], 0.89% [[White Latin Americans|White]], 0.50% [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]], 0.30% [[Afro-Hondurans|Black or Afro-Honduran]] and 0.05% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
