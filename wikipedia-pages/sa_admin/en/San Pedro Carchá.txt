'''San Pedro Carchá''', usually referred to as '''Carchá''', is a town and a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Guatemala]]n department of [[Alta Verapaz]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|1310|km2}} and is situated at 1,282 m above [[sea level]].<ref name="MUNI_2">{{cite web |title=San Pedro Carchá - Ubicación |url=http://www.inforpressca.com/sanpedrocarcha/ubicacion.php |publisher=inforpressca.com |access-date=2009-10-01 }}</ref>

At the 2018 census, the population of the municipality was 235,275 and that of the town of Carchá was 16,353.<ref name="MUNI_1">{{cite web |title=San Pedro Carchá |url=http://www.inforpressca.com/sanpedrocarcha/ |access-date=2009-10-01 |publisher=inforpressca.com}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Guatemala: Departments, Major Cities & Towns - Population Statistics, Maps, Charts, Weather and Web Information |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/cities/ |access-date=2024-06-14 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

==Sports==
[[C.S.D. Carchá]] is a [[football club]] that plays their home games at the [[Juan Ramón Ponce Stadium|Estadio Juan Ramon Ponce]]. They have played in [[Liga Nacional de Guatemala|Guatemala's top division]] from 1998 to 2001.{{Citation needed|date=February 2024}}

The Northern Transversal Strip was officially created during the government of General Carlos Arana Osorio in 1970, by Legislative Decree 60–70, for agricultural development.<ref name=wikiguate>{{cite web|url=http://wikiguate.com.gt/wiki/Franja_Transversal_del_Norte|website=Wikiguate|title=Franja Transversal del Norte|access-date=30 October 2014|location=Guatemala|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141031093752/http://wikiguate.com.gt/wiki/Franja_Transversal_del_Norte|archive-date=31 October 2014}}</ref> The decree literally said: "It is of public interest and national emergency, the establishment of Agrarian Development Zones in the area included within the municipalities: San Ana Huista, San Antonio Huista, [[Nentón]], Jacaltenango, [[San Mateo Ixtatán]], and Santa Cruz Barillas in [[Huehuetenango (department)|Huehuetenango]]; [[Chajul]] and San Miguel [[Uspantán]] in Quiché; [[Cobán]], [[Chisec]], San Pedro Carchá, [[Lanquín]], [[Senahú]], [[Cahabón]] and Chahal, in Alta Verapaz and the entire department of Izabal."{{sfn|Solano|2012|p=15}}
