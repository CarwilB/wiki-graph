'''Santa Isabel''' is one of the 67 [[municipalities of Chihuahua]], in northern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Santa Isabel, Chihuahua|Santa Isabel]]. The municipality covers an area of 1,040.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

Between 1932 and 1993 the municipality was called '''General Trías''', in honour of General {{ill|Ángel Trías Álvarez|es}}, who served eight terms as [[governor of Chihuahua]] in the 19th century.<ref>{{cite web |title=024 Santa Isabel |url=https://chihuahuagrande.com/024-santa-isabel/ |website=Chihuahua Grande |access-date=23 August 2024 |date=4 January 2018}}</ref>

As of 2010, the municipality had a total population of 3,937,<ref name=SEDESOL>{{cite web|title=Santa Isabel|url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=08&mun=024|work=Catálogo de Localidades|publisher=[[Secretaría de Desarrollo Social]] (SEDESOL)|accessdate=23 April 2014}}</ref> up from 3,820 as of 2005.<ref>{{cite web|title= Santa Isabel |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/chihuahua/Mpios/08024a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=October 25, 2008}}</ref> 
By 2020, it had fallen to 3,791.<ref>{{cite web |title=Santa Isabel: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/santa-isabel |website=Data México |publisher=Secretariat of the Economy |access-date=23 August 2024}}</ref> 

As of 2010, the town of Santa Isabel had a population of 1,378.<ref name=SEDESOL/> Other than the town of Santa Isabel, the municipality had 52 localities, none of which had a population over 1,000.<ref name=SEDESOL/>
