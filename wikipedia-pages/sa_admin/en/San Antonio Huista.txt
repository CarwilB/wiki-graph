'''San Antonio Huista''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the Guatemalan [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Huehuetenango Department|Huehuetenango]]. It is located 362 kilometers from Guatemala City. The predominant language is Spanish, while approximately 20% of the population speak indigenous languages, the majority speaking [[Mam language|Mam]]. The municipality of San Antonio Huista is situated at 1240 metres above sea level, with a population of 16,697 (2018 census). It covers an area of 60.4&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The annual festival is December 8–12 dedicated to the [[Virgin of Guadalupe]] and the patron saint's day ([[Saint Anthony of Padua]]) is celebrated June 11–13.
