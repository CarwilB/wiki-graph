'''Guateque''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Eastern Boyacá Province]]. Guateque's urban center is located at an altitude of {{convert|1815|m|ft}} on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at distances of {{convert|125|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] and {{convert|112|km|mi}} from [[Bogotá]], capital of Colombia. It borders the municipalities of [[La Capilla]], [[Tenza]], [[Sutatenza]], [[Somondoco]] of Boyacá and [[Tibiritá]] and [[Manta, Cundinamarca|Manta]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]].<ref name=websiteGuateque>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.guateque-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Guateque] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20150602140055/http://www.guateque-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-06-02 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
Guateque is derived from the original name ''Guatoc'', meaning "Stream of the ravine" or "Lord of the winds".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Municipalities Boyacá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
In the centuries before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], the central highlands of Colombia were inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]]. The Muisca practiced different rituals related to their [[Muisca religion|religion]]. On the hill within the municipality that they called ''Guatoc'', the people organized religious festivities.<ref name=websiteGuateque/> The ''[[cacique]]'' of Guateque was an important leader within the loose [[Muisca Confederation]], as [[gold]] mining was executed in Guateque.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.nemosto.net/quimbaya/Muisca.pdf Gold mining in Guateque] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160807224143/http://www.nemosto.net/quimbaya/Muisca.pdf |date=2016-08-07 }}</ref>

While [[Catholicism|Catholic]] missionaries were already working in Guateque since 1556, the modern town was founded on January 28, 1636, by a group of Spanish leaders.<ref name=websiteGuateque/>

== Economy ==
Despite the urban character of Guateque, almost half of its economy is based on [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Predominant agricultural products are [[maize]] and [[tomato]]es.<ref name=websiteGuateque/>
