'''Aguanil''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[Brazilian state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. It was founded on 30 December 1962 and, in 2020, its population was estimated to be 4,522.<ref name="ibge">{{cite web |url=  https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/aguanil|title= Aguanil|language=Portuguese |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística ([[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]]) | place = Brasília, Brazil |date=2024 |accessdate=2024-02-01}}</ref>
