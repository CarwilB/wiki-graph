'''Villeda Morales''' is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Honduras|Honduran]] [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Gracias a Dios department|Gracias a Dios]].
It was named after a former president named [[Ramón Villeda Morales]]

==Demographics==
At the time of the 2013 Honduras census, Ramón Villeda Morales municipality had a population of 10,314. Of these, 87.90% were [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] (87.78% [[Miskito people|Miskito]]), 11.69% [[Mestizo]], 0.21% [[Afro-Hondurans|Afro-Honduran or Black]], 0.11% [[White Latin Americans|White]] and 0.09% others.<ref>[https://www.ine.gob.hn/V3/baseine/ Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE), bases de datos en línea]</ref>
