'''Herval''' is a Brazilian municipality in the southern part of the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]]. The population is 6,814 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|1757.84|km2}}.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/1GJD Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 287&nbsp;m. Its main industry is agriculture.

Herval is situated south of the [[Piratini River (São Gonçalo Channel)|Piratini River]], which was defined in 1777 as the border between the [[Spain|Spanish]] and [[Portugal|Portuguese]] possessions. The current border between [[Uruguay]] and Brazil lies further south, at the [[Jaguarão River]].

Herval is famous for its international [[rodeo]] festival, organized every January.
