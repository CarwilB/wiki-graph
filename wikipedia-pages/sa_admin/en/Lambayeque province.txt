'''Lambayeque''' '''province''' is the oldest and largest of three [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in [[Peru]]'s [[Department of Lambayeque|Lambayeque Region]]. The province was established on February 12, 1821''',''' by [[José de San Martín]], originating from the former administrative entity known as the {{ill|Partido of Saña |lt=Partido of Saña or Lambayeque|es|Partido de Saña}} under the [[Intendancy of Trujillo]]. As such, it originally spanned the entire modern-day Department of Lambayeque, along with the provinces of [[Chepén province|Chepén]] and [[Pacasmayo province|Pacasmayo]], until the [[Chiclayo province|province of Chiclayo]] was definitively created in March 1839 (with a brief period in April 1835).<ref>{{Cite news |year={{date|1839-03-22}} |title=Decreto del 22 de marzo de 1839 |url=https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/LeyesXIX/1839020.pdf |url-status=live |access-date={{date|2026-03-10}} |location=Lima |language=es}}</ref>

== Boundaries ==
*'''North''', '''Northwest''' and '''Northeast''': [[Piura Region]]
*'''East:''' A tiny border with the [[Department of Cajamarca|Cajamarca region]]
*'''Southeast''' provinces of [[Ferreñafe province|Ferreñafe]] and [[Chiclayo province|Chiclayo]]
*'''Southwest''' [[Pacific Ocean]]
