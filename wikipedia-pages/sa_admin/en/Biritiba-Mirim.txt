'''Biritiba Mirim''' is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] of the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]]. It is part of the [[Metropolitan Region of São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |title=Lei Complementar nº 1.139, de 16 de junho de 2011 |access-date=2015-09-30 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151001191247/http://governo-sp.jusbrasil.com.br/legislacao/1028148/lei-complementar-1139-11 |archive-date=2015-10-01 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The population is 32,936 (2020 est.) in an area of {{cvt|317.41|km2}}.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/8EW Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its limits are [[Guararema]] in the north, [[Salesópolis]] in the east, [[Bertioga]] to the south and [[Mogi das Cruzes]] in the west and northwest.

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_de_Telecomunicações_do_Estado_de_São_Paulo|Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo]] until 1973, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Companhia_Telefônica_da_Borda_do_Campo|Companhia Telefônica da Borda do Campo]]. In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
