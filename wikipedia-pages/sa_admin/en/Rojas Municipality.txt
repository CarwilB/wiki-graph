The '''Rojas Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 40,126.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Libertad, Barinas|Libertad]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Rojas Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Barinas.zip {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Rojas Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 40,264 (up from 34,132 in 2000).  This amounts to 5.3% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|25.3|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=April 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Rojas Municipality is Orlando Leonel Gómez Mendoza, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 48% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web| title=Alcalde de Municipio - Rojas | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231118/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=7&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 - directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-17 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Libertad, Dolores, Palacios Fajardo, and Santa Rosa.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
