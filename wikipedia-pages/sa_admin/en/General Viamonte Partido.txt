'''General Viamonte Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the north of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 17,600 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|2150|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Los Toldos]], which is {{convert|310|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The town of Los Toldos is home to an [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|Indigenous]] [[Mapuche]] colony.

==Name==
The partido was originally named after a settlement that had built up around a train station on the [[Buenos Aires Western Railway]] called ''Los Toldos''. In 1910 the name of the partido was changed from ''Los Toldos'' to General Viamonte although the town has kept the name Los Toldos.

The partido is now named in honour of General [[Juan José Viamonte]], a hero of the [[Argentine War of Independence]], governor of Buenos Aires and [[List of Heads of State of Argentina|Argentine head of state]] for 3 days (April 18, 1815 to April 20, 1815).

There is some debate about the name of the district capital: some sources claim that the capital is also known as General Viamonte, but the population of the district still call it Los Toldos.{{citation needed|date=December 2017}}
