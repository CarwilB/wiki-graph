'''Renaico''' ({{IPA|es|reˈnajko}}) is a [[List of cities in Chile|city]] and [[Communes of Chile|commune]] forming part of the [[Malleco Province]] in the [[Araucanía Region]] in southern [[Chile]]. The city is located on the south bank of the [[Renaico River]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Renaico spans an area of {{convert|267.4|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 9,128 inhabitants (4,508 men and 4,620 women). Of these, 6,878 (75.4%) lived in [[urban area]]s and 2,250 (24.6%) in [[rural area]]s. The population fell by 0.8% (69 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Renaico is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2008-2012 alcalde is Ivonne Morales Urra ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/> 

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Renaico is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Gonzalo Arenas ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) and Mario Venegas ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 48th electoral district, together with [[Angol]], [[Collipulli]], [[Ercilla, Chile|Ercilla]], [[Los Sauces]], [[Purén]], [[Lumaco]] and [[Traiguén]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Alberto Espina Otero ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]]) and Jaime Quintana Leal ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) as part of the 14th senatorial constituency (Araucanía-North).
