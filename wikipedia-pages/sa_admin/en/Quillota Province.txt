'''Quillota Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Quillota}}) is one of eight [[Provinces of Chile|province]]s of the central [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Valparaíso Region|Valparaíso]] (V). Its capital is the city of [[Quillota]] (pop. 75,916).<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a [[province]], Quillota is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]], governed by a provincial delegate who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]].

===Communes===
The province comprises five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''comunas''), each governed by a municipality consisting of an [[alcalde]] and [[municipal council]]:
*[[Quillota]] (capital)
*[[La Calera, Chile|La Calera]]
*[[Nogales, Chile|Nogales]]
*[[Hijuelas]]
*[[La Cruz, Chile|La Cruz]]

==History==
On March 11, 2010, the communes of [[Limache]] and [[Olmué]] were transferred to [[Marga Marga Province]] under Law 20,368 (signed August 25, 2009).

==Geography and demography==
The province spans a landlocked area of {{convert|1,113.1|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, the smallest in the Valparaíso Region with the exception of [[Isla de Pascua]] (Easter Island). According to the 2002 census, Quillota is the third most populous province in the region with a population of 175,917. At that time, there were 151,366 people living in [[urban area]]s, 24,551 people living in [[rural area]]s, 86,620 men and 89,297 women.<ref name="INE"/>
