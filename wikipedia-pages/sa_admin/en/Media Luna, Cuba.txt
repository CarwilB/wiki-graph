'''Media Luna''' ({{IPA|es|ˈmeðja luna}}) is a town and [[municipality]] in the [[Granma Province]] of [[Cuba]]. It is located on the coastal region of the province, bordering the [[Gulf of Guacanayabo]], between [[Niquero, Cuba|Niquero]] and [[Campechuela, Cuba|Campechuela]].

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Media Luna had a population of 31,589.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|376|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-06}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|94.0|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Transport==
The town is crossed by the state highway "[[Circuito Sur de Oriente]]" (CSO).
