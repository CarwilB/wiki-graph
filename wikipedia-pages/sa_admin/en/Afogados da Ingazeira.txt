'''Afogados da Ingazeira''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] municipality in the state of [[Pernambuco]]. The estimated population in 2025 according to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] was 42,672, and the total area is {{convert|378.031|km2|mi2}}.<ref name="IBGE" /> It has a population density of 97 inhabitants per square kilometer. In 2000, Afogados da Ingazeira had the second highest [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) in the Alto do Pajeú [[sertão]] region of Pernambuco state.  Nonetheless, its score (0.683) was still below the state (0.718) and national (0.789) averages. The city is the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Afogados da Ingazeira]].

Afogados da ingazeira have [[one]] [[sister city]]
* {{flagicon|Brazil}} [[Tabira, Pernambuco|Tabira]], [[Brazil]]<ref name="Cidade-irmã"/>

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Afogados da Ingazeira are industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially farming of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[chickens]];  and plantations of [[beans]], [[Maize|corn]] and [[manioc]].
