'''Argelia''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Part of the subregion of [[Eastern Antioquia]]. The population was 8,911 in 2005. 

==Climate==
Argelia has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) which closely borders a [[subtropical highland climate]]. It has very heavy rainfall year round.
