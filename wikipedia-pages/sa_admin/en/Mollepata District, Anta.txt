'''Mollepata District''' is one of nine [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[Anta Province|Anta]], in the [[Department of Cuzco]], in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> The Mollepata trek or [[wikivoyage:Salkantay trail|Salkantay trek]], an alternative route to the [[Inca Trail to Machu Picchu]], passes through the [[Vilcabamba mountain range|Vilcabamba Range]] from Mollepata to [[Santa Teresa District|Santa Teresa]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=2011-07-07 |title=The High Road to Ruins |url=https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/the-high-road-to-ruins |access-date=2024-08-02 |website=Travel |language=en}}</ref> The Mollepata trek is considered harder than the ''Classic'' trek because of its height and remoteness.<ref>Walkopedia Ltd., [https://www.walkopedia.net/best-world-walks/Peru/Mollepata---Salkantay-Trek Mollepata / Salkantay Trek], accessed on 10 October 2025</ref>

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the district are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the language which the majority of the population (74.13%) learnt to speak in childhood, 24.83% of the residents started speaking using the [[Spanish language|Spanish]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007, Frequencias: Preguntas de Población</ref>
