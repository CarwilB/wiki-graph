'''Maisí''' is a municipality and town in the [[Guantánamo Province]] of [[Cuba]]. Its administrative seat is located in the town of [[La Máquina, Cuba|La Máquina]].

==Geography==
The easternmost point of Cuba, ''Punta Maisí'' (called "Baitiquiri"<ref name=taino>{{Cite web|url=http://www.taino-tribe.org/terms1.htm |title= Taino Dictionary |author=Taino-Tribe.org |accessdate=2007-10-18|language=Spanish}}</ref>{{better|date=May 2026}} by the [[Taíno]] in pre-colonial times) is located in this municipality.

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Maisí had a population of 28,276.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|525|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|55|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
