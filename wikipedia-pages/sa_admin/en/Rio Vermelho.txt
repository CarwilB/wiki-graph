'''Rio Vermelho''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the [[Mesoregion (Brazil)|mesoregion]] Metropolitana de [[Belo Horizonte]] and to the [[Microregion (Brazil)|microregion]] of [[Conceição do Mato Dentro]].  As of 2020, the estimated population was 12,739.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/rio-vermelho/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
