'''Abasolo''' is one of the 38 [[municipalities of Coahuila]], in north-eastern [[Mexico]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Abasolo, Coahuila|Abasolo]]. The municipality covers an area of 645.9&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 991.<ref>{{cite web|title=Abasolo |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05001a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=November 8, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930023813/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/coahuila/mpios/05001a.htm |archivedate=September 30, 2007 }}</ref>
