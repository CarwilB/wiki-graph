'''Paita''' is one of eight [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Piura Region]] in northwestern [[Peru]].

== Location ==
The province is located along the [[Pacific Ocean|pacific]] coast. Its capital city, [[Paita]], is one of the main ports of Peru.

=== Boundaries ===
*'''North''': provinces of  [[Talara Province|Talara]] and [[Sullana Province|Sullana]]
*'''East''': province of [[Sullana Province|Sullana]]
*'''South''': province of [[Piura Province|Piura]]
*'''West''' [[Pacific Ocean|Sea of Grau]].

== Political division ==
The province is divided into seven [[districts of Peru|districts]], which are:

:*[[Paita District|Paita]]
:*[[Amotape District|Amotape]]
:*[[Colan District|Colan]]
:*[[La Huaca District|La Huaca]]
:*[[Vichayal District|Vichayal]]
:*[[Arenal District, Paita|Arenal]]
:*[[Tamarindo District|Tamarindo]]

== Capital ==
The capital of the province is the city of [[Paita]].
