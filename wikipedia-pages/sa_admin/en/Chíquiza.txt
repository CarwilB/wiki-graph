'''Chíquiza''' ({{IPA|es|tʃiˈkisa}}) is a town and municipality in the [[Central Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. Chíquiza was called '''San Pedro de Iguaque''' until July 17, 2003, when the name was changed to Chíquiza.<ref name=websiteChiquiza>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150529032622/http://www.chiquiza-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chíquiza]</ref> The municipality is situated on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] with the urban centre at an altitude of {{convert|2900|m|ft}} and {{convert|24|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. Chíquiza borders [[Arcabuco]] in the north, [[Sora, Boyacá|Sora]] and [[Sáchica]] in the south, [[Villa de Leyva]] in the west and [[Motavita]] in the east.<ref name=websiteChiquiza/>

== Etymology ==
The name Chíquiza comes from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]] and means "only" or "alone", "Chiqui" means priest.<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Chíquiza] - Excelsio.net</ref> Another origin is given from the word ''chequiza''; "hairy field".<ref name=websiteChiquiza/>

== History ==
Before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]], Chíquiza was part of the loose [[Muisca Confederation]] and [[Muisca rulers|ruled]] by a ''[[cacique]]'' who was loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of [[Tunja|Hunza]]. The village was important in the [[Muisca religion|religion]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] as it was close to the [[Sacred lake|sacred]] [[Lake Iguaque]]; the birthplace of [[Bachué]], mother goddess of the Muisca.<ref name=websiteChiquiza/>

Modern Chíquiza was founded in 1556 and became a part of Tunja until 1982. In 2003 the name was changed from San Pedro de Iguaque to Chíquiza.<ref name=websiteChiquiza/>

== Economy ==
The economy of Chíquiza is centered around [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Main agricultural products are [[maize]], [[potato]]es, [[pea]]s, [[wheat]], [[Brassica napus|nabo]], [[ibia]], [[Ullucus|ruba]] and [[onion]]s.<ref name=websiteChiquiza/>
