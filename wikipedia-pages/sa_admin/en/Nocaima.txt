'''Nocaima''' ({{IPA|es|noˈkajma}}) is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. It is the oldest European settlement in Colombia, and therefore of historical significance. Founded on June 8, 1605, it sits in the hills of the Andes, about 60&nbsp;km outside Bogota, the current Capital of Colombia.
