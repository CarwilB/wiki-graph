'''Cuautitlán de García Barragán''' is a town and [[municipalities of Jalisco|municipality]] in [[Jalisco]] in central-western [[Mexico]]. The municipality covers an area of  1,391&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 16,408.<ref>{{cite web|title=Cuautitlán de García Barragán|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14027a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|accessdate=April 13, 2009|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070530190931/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/jalisco/mpios/14027a.htm|archive-date=May 30, 2007|url-status=dead}}</ref>
