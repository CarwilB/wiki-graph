'''Malvinas Argentinas''' is a municipality in the [[Colón Department, Córdoba|Department of Colón]] of [[Córdoba Province, Argentina]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://snop-ppo.obraspublicas.gob.ar/localities/details/af1c05b4-8788-4a4d-96c9-adf9ffac77dd|title=Localidad de MALVINAS ARGENTINAS|work=Unidades Territoriales|publisher=[[Secretariat of Public Works (Argentina)]]|access-date=7 July 2025|language=es}}</ref> Its population is  8,628 (2001 Census), showing a 67% increase since the previous census (1991). 
It is a working-class suburb of [[Córdoba, Argentina|Córdoba City]], one of the poorest places in the Province. More than 26% of the people over 15 have not completed their elementary schooling.

The municipality's name reflects Argentina's claims of [[Falkland Islands sovereignty dispute|sovereignty]] over the [[Falkland Islands]] (''Islas Malvinas'' in Spanish), and could be translated as "Argentine Falklands".
