'''Tlilapan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] located in the central zone in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about {{convert|85|km|mi}} from the state capital of [[Xalapa]]. It has an area of {{convert|23.85|km2|sqmi}}. It is located at {{coord|18|48|N|97|06|W}}. The name comes from the [[Nahuatl]] language, meaning "Black creek". The village has existed since the 16th century and, following national independence, and was designated a municipality along with [[Orizaba]], [[San Andrés Tenejapan]], San Francisco Necoxtla, and San Juan del Rio. The municipal cemetery was established in 1880 and, in 1916 the first Municipal President was named.

==Geography==
The municipality of Tlilapan is bordered to the north by [[Rafael Delgado, Veracruz|Rafael Delgado]], to the south by [[Huiloapan de Cuauhtémoc]] and to the west by [[Nogales (municipality of Veracruz)|Nogales]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30185a.htm|archive-url=https://archive.today/20090617162439/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30185a.htm|url-status=dead|archive-date=June 17, 2009|title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tlilapan|publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> It is watered by small rivers such as the Río Blanco.

The climate of Tlilapan is temperate, with abundant rainfall in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==
The chief agricultural products of Tlilapan are [[maize]], [[coffee]] and [[sugarcane]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Comité de Información Estadística y Geográfica del Estado de Veracruz |url=https://ceieg.veracruz.gob.mx/wp-content/uploads/sites/21/2023/08/Tlilapan.CM_.Ver_.2023.2.pdf |access-date=2026-04-27 |website=Sistema de Información Estadística y Geográfica del Estado de Veracruz de Ignacio de la Llave (SIEGVER), CUADERNILLOS MUNICIPALES, Edición 2023, Tlilapan |language=es-ES}}</ref>

==Celebrations==
In July, Tlilapan festival takes place the celebration of [[John the Apostle|Santiago Apostol]], patron of the town, and in December a celebration takes place in honor of the [[Our Lady of Guadalupe|Virgen de Guadalupe]].
