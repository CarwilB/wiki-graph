'''San Lorenzo''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 14,000 inhabitants in an area of  {{convert|2,135|km²|sqmi|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Villa Berthet]], which is located around {{convert|1,190|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the [[Capital federal]].
