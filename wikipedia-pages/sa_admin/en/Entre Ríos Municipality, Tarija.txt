}}'''Entre Ríos Municipality''' is the only [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipal section]] of the [[Burdett O'Connor Province]] in the [[Tarija Department]] in central [[Bolivia]]. Its seat, [[Entre Ríos, Tarija|Entre Ríos]], dates to 1616 and has taken the four names of Ciudad de Las Vegas de la Nueva Granada, Villa de San Carlos, San Luis, and finally San Luis de Entre Ríos.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Entre Ríos, el misterio del pueblo de los 4 nombres |url=https://elpais.bo/tarija/20181004_entre-rios-el-misterio-del-pueblo-de-los-4-nombres.html |access-date=2026-05-05 |website=El País Tarija |language=es}}</ref> It is one of the 11 municipalities of Tarija Department.
