'''Caimito''' is a municipality and town in [[Artemisa Province]] of [[Cuba]].
The town was founded in 1820. The municipality of '''''Caimito del Guayabal''''' was created in 1910, based on the previously existing (1879–1902) municipality of Guayabal in the [[Pinar del Río Province]].<ref name=guije/> Since 1976, the official name is Caimito.

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the villages of Caimito, Guayabal, Ceiba del Agua, Pueblo Nuevo, Rancho Grande, Capellanías, Vereda Nueva, Los Naranjos, Aguacate, Banes, Habana Libre and Menelao Mora.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/hcaimito/index.htm|title=Caimito|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-08|language=es}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Caimito had a population of 42,552.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|238|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-08}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|180|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Transport==
Caimito, crossed by the [[Carretera Central (Cuba)|Carretera Central]], is served by [[Autopista A4 (Cuba)|A4 motorway]], passing some km north. From 2014, it counts a railway station on the new [[Havana]]-[[Guanajay]]-[[Artemisa]]/[[Mariel, Cuba|Mariel]] line, part of the [[Havana–Pinar del Río railway|Havana-Artemisa-Pinar del Río line]]. [[Playa Baracoa Airport]], serving Havana, is located in the municipal territory, and lies in front of [[Playa Baracoa]], a village belonging to the neighboring municipality of [[Bauta, Cuba|Bauta]].<ref>{{OSM|n|455624731|Playa Baracoa}}</ref><ref>{{OSM|n|259849770|Playa Baracoa Airport}}</ref>
