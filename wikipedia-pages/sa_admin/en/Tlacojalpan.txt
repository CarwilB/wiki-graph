'''Tlacojalpan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], about {{convert|175|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It has a surface of 91.30 km2. It is located at {{coord|18|14|N|95|57|W|}}.

==Name==
The name comes from the language Náhuatl, Tlahco-xal-pan; that means "In the half of the sandbank".

==Geography==

The municipality of Tlacojalpan is delimited to the north by [[Cosamaloapan de Carpio|Cosamaloapan]], to the east by [[Tuxtilla]], to the south by [[Oaxaca]] State and to the west by [[Otatitlán]].<ref name="e-local">{{cite web|url= http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/veracruz/municipios/30176a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México: Tlacojalpan |publisher=Gobierno Estatal|language=es|access-date=2009-06-16}}</ref> It is watered by creeks that are tributaries of the [[Papaloapan River]].

The weather in Tlacojalpan is very warm and wet all year with rains in summer and autumn.

==Agriculture==

It produces principally [[maize]], [[beans]], [[rice]], [[sugarcane]] and [[mango]].

==Celebrations==

In Tlacojalpan, in January takes place the celebration in honor to San Cristobal, Patron of the town, and in December takes place the celebration in honor to Virgen de Guadalupe.
