'''Leoncio Prado''' is one of eleven [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[Huánuco Region]] in [[Peru]]. The capital of this province is the city of [[Tingo María]].

The province was named after the Peruvian mariner [[Leoncio Prado Gutiérrez]].

==Boundaries==
*'''North''': [[San Martín Region]]
*'''East''': [[Ucayali Region]]
*'''South''': provinces of [[Puerto Inca Province|Puerto Inca]], [[Pachitea Province|Pachitea]] and [[Huánuco Province|Huánuco]]
*'''West''': provinces of [[Marañón Province|Marañón]], [[Dos de Mayo Province|Dos de Mayo]], [[Huacaybamba Province|Huacaybamba]] and [[Huamalíes Province|Huamalíes]]

==Political division==
The province is divided into six districts, which are:

* [[Daniel Alomías Robles District|Daniel Alomías Robles]] ([[Pumahuasi, Tingo María|Pumahuasi]])
* [[Hermilio Valdizán District|Hermilio Valdizán]] ([[Hermilio Valdizán]])
* [[José Crespo y Castillo District|José Crespo y Castillo]] ([[Aucayacu]])
* [[Luyando District|Luyando]] ([[Naranjillo, Tingo Maria|Naranjillo]])
* [[Mariano Dámaso Beraun District|Mariano Dámaso Beraun]] ([[las Palmas, Leoncio Prado Province|las Palmas]])
* [[Rupa-Rupa District|Rupa-Rupa]] ([[Tingo María]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The province is inhabited by [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] is the language which the majority of the population (91.42%) learnt to speak in childhood, 8.23% of the residents started speaking using the [[Quechua language|Quechua]] language ([[2007 Peru Census]]).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=2013-01-27 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
