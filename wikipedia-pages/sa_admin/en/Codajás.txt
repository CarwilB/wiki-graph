'''Codajás''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Amazonas (Brazilian state)|Amazonas]]. Its population was 29,168 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/am/codajas/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 18,712&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |accessdate=2008-06-11 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archivedate=2008-06-11 }}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality contains part of the [[Amanã Sustainable Development Reserve]].<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|RDS Amanã – ISA}}|language=Portuguese |title=RDS Amanã|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/337|accessdate=2016-05-05}}</ref>
