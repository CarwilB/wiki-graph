'''Sauce''' (hispanicized spelling of [[Quechua language|Quechua]] ''sawsi'' [[willow]])<ref>{{Ref Simi}}</ref> is one of fourteen [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the province [[San Martín Province|San Martín]] in [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Geography ==
Located in the foothills of the [[Cordillera Oriental (Peru)]], the town is 614 m a.s.l. and 51 [[km]] south of the [[Tarapoto]] city, across the [[Huallaga River]], 6°42’12” south latitude and 76°15’15” west longitude.
