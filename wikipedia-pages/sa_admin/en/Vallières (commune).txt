'''Vallières''' ({{IPA|fr|valjɛʁ|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Mathieu Kappler-Vallières.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Valyè}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Vallières Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 17,470 inhabitants.

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of three [[communal section]]s, namely:
* '''Trois Palmistes,''' urban and rural, containing the village of Vallières
* '''Ecrevisse or Grosse Roche,''' urban and rural, containing the village of '''Grosse Roche'''
* '''Corosse,''' rural
