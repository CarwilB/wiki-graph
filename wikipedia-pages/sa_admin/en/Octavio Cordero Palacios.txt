'''Octavio Cordero Palacios''' is a town and [[Parishes of Ecuador|parish]] in [[Cuenca Canton]], [[Azuay Province]], [[Ecuador]]. The parish covers an area of {{Convert|20.4|km2|sqmi}}. In 2010, its total population was 2,271.<ref>{{Cite web |year=2019 |title=Actualización del plan de desarrollo y ordenamiento territorial de la parroquia Octavio Cordero Palacios |trans-title=Update of the development and territorial planning plan of the Octavio Cordero Palacios parish |url=http://octaviocordero.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/PDOT-Octavio-Cordero-Palacios.pdf |access-date=8 February 2024 |website=GAD Parroquial Rural de Octavio Cordero Palacios |language=es}}</ref> The town is named after the Ecuadorian writer and inventor [[Octavio Cordero Palacios (writer)|Octavio Cordero Palacios]].
