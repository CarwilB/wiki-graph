'''Santana do Araguaia''' is the southernmost city in the [[Brazil]]ian [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Pará]]. The city lies near the [[Araguaia River]], which marks the border with the state of [[Tocantins]] and [[Mato Grosso]]. The town was established on December 20, 1961, by then-governor of Pará, [[Aurélio do Carmo]], after being split off from the municipality of [[Conceição do Araguaia]].<ref>{{cite web | title = Santana do Araguaia | url = http://www.santanadoaraguaia.com/santana-do-araguaia/ | year = 2010 | access-date = December 8, 2010 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20100605014906/http://www.santanadoaraguaia.com/santana-do-araguaia/ | archive-date = 2010-06-05 }}</ref>

The city is served by [[Santana do Araguaia Airport]].
