'''Tibes''' (''Barrio Tibes'') is one of the 31 [[barrio]]s in the [[municipality]] of [[Ponce, Puerto Rico]].  Together with [[Magueyes]], [[Portugués Rural|Portugués]], [[Montes Llanos]], [[Maragüez]], [[Machuelo Arriba]], [[Sabanetas (Ponce)|Sabanetas]], and [[Cerrillos (Ponce)|Cerrillos]], barrio Tibes is one of the municipality's eight rural interior barrios.  Tibes attracted attention recently when, in 1975, what was to become the discovery of the oldest [[cemetery]] in the [[West Indies]] came about as a result of rainstorms. The name of this barrio is of native Indian origin.<ref>Sunny A. Cabrera Salcedo. ''Hacia un Estudio Integral de la Toponimia del Municipio de Ponce, Puerto Rico.'' Ph. D. dissertation. May 1999. University of Massachusetts Amherst. Graduate School. Department of Spanish and Portuguese. Page 54.</ref> It was organized in 1831.<ref>[http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html ''Barrios de Ponce.''] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150930083458/http://freepages.genealogy.rootsweb.ancestry.com/~poncepr/barrios.html |date=30 September 2015 }} Antepasados Esclavos.(From: Pedro Tomás de Córdoba. Memorias geográficas, históricas, económicas y estadísticas de la Isla de Puerto Rico.) Retrieved 28 November 2014.</ref>

==Location==
[[Image:Paisaje en el Barrio Tibes (PR-10 Norte cerca de la PR-503), Ponce, PR (DSC02996A).jpg|thumb|left|200px|Landscape scene in Barrio Tibes at [[PR-10]] northbound approaching [[PR-503]]]]

Tibes is a mountainous rural barrio located in the central section of the municipality, north of the city of Ponce.<ref>[http://www.citymelt.com/city/Puerto-Rico/Tibes+Barrio-PR.html City Melt] Retrieved, February 26, 2010.</ref> Tibes is located on the foothills of the [[Cordillera Central, Puerto Rico|Cordillera Central]].  The [[toponymy]], or origin of the name, is related to the hard, smooth stones plentiful in the rivers of this barrio and used by the indigenous peoples of the area as a weapon and the [[Jíbaro (Puerto Rico)|jíbaro]] countrymen for sharpening agricultural tools such as the [[machete]].<ref>Government of the Municipality of Ponce. Periódico "El Señorial".  Special issue: Carnaval Ponceño 2013. February 2013. Page 17. Ponce, Puerto Rico.</ref> [[Hurricane Maria]] hit Puerto Rico on September 20, 2017 causing catastrophic damages. Some residents of Tibes were cut off for 3 months, unable to find passage from their homes due to blocked roadways.<ref>{{cite web | last=Guzmán | first=Sandra Torres | title=Huracán María: Vecinos del barrio Tibes tardaron tres meses en abrir caminos | website=Primera Hora | date=2021-09-20 | url=https://www.primerahora.com/noticias/puerto-rico/notas/huracan-maria-vecinos-del-barrio-tibes-tardaron-tres-meses-en-abrir-caminos/ | language=es | access-date=2021-09-21}}</ref>

==Boundaries==

Tibes is bounded on the North by the hills just north of El Principe Road, on the South by Tibes Road, on the West by the hills west of [[PR-10]], and on the East by Pastillo Road, the hills west of [[Río Chiquito]].<ref>[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=12 August 2010 }} Retrieved 20 February  2010.</ref><ref>[http://www.trails.com/topo.aspx?panerror=true&z=19&e=759173.152588761&n=1993318.14420084&s=50&size=s&style=drgsr Ponce Topographic Map. Map Styles: Map and Shaded. Trails.com. From: United States Geological Survey. Topo Map. Projection: NAD83/WGS84.] Retrieved 22 February 2010.</ref>

In terms of barrio-to-barrio boundaries, Tibes is bounded in the North by Barrio [[San Patricio (Ponce)|San Patricio]], in the South by [[Portugués Rural|Portugués]], in the West by [[Guaraguao]] and [[Magueyes]], and in the East by Barrios [[Monte Llano]] and Portugués.<ref name="gis.otg.gobierno.pr">[http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ General Purpose Population Data, Census 2000. Unidad de Sistemas de Información Geográfica, Área de Tecnología de Información Gubernamental, Oficina de Gerencia y Presupuesto. Gobierno de Puerto Rico.] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100812171159/http://www.gis.otg.gobierno.pr/webcensus/ |date=12 August 2010 }} Retrieved 27 February 2010.</ref><ref>[http://www.ponceweb.org/Anuncio/Barriosdeponce.pdf Mapa de Localizacion Limite de Barrios de Ponce.] Retrieved 27 February 2010.</ref>

==Features and demographics==

In 2010, Tibes had {{convert|6.99|sqmi|km2|sigfig=2}} of land area and no water area.
