'''Río Verde''' is a [[Chile]]an [[Communes of Chile|commune]] and settlement in [[Magallanes Province]] and [[Magallanes Region|Region]]. The commune is located in the area surrounding [[Seno Skyring]], a sea inlet north of [[Riesco Island]].

According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Río Verde spans an area of {{convert|9975.2|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and has 358 inhabitants (295 men and 63 women), making the commune an entirely [[rural area]]. The population grew by 6.9% (23 persons) between the 1992 and 2002 censuses.<ref name= "INE"/>

==Administration==
[[image:Riesco Insel.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Pto.Curtze]]
As a commune, Río Verde is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2012-2016 alcaldesa is Tatiana Vásquez Barrientos ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]).<ref name="Munitel"/><ref name="Official"/>

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Río Verde is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Juan Morano ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) and [[Gabriel Boric]] ([[Independent (politician)|Ind.]]) as part of the 60th electoral district, which includes the entire [[Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region]]. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Carlos Bianchi Chelech (Ind.) and Carolina Goic ([[Christian Democratic Party|PDC]]) as part of the 19th senatorial constituency (Magallanes y la Antártica Chilena Region).
