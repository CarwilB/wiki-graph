'''Guamote''' is a town in the [[Chimborazo Province]], [[Ecuador]]. It is the seat of the [[Guamote Canton]].

Guamote is located on [[Ecuador Highway 35]], between [[Riobamba]] and [[Alausí]]. Ecuador Highway 46 branches in Guamote to the east and connects it with [[Macas (city)|Macas]], crossing [[Sangay National Park]].
