'''Florida''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Siquirres (canton)|Siquirres]] canton, in the [[Limón Province|Limón]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Florida was created on 19 September 1911 by Ley 11.<ref name="dta2017" />

== Geography ==
Florida has an area of {{formatnum:82.76}} km²<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:160}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

==Locations==
* Neighborhoods (''Barrios''): El Alto

==Economy==
It shares with [[Siquirres District]] the biggest hydroelectrical dam of Central America,<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://es.sustainablehydro.net/ph-reventazon/ |title=Aplicación del Protocolo al PH Reventazón – Taller regional :: Aplicación de la sostenibilidad hidroeléctrica :: Protocolo de evaluación :: Experiencia y beneficios |access-date=2020-03-27 |archive-date=2020-03-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200327092004/http://es.sustainablehydro.net/ph-reventazon/ |url-status=dead }}</ref> the Proyecto Hidroeléctrico Reventazón, built over the Reventazón River by the [[Instituto Costarricense de Electricidad]]. Impressive views of the dam are a main tourist attraction in the district.
