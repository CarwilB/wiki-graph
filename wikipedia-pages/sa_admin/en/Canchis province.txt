'''Qanchi''' is one of thirteen [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] in the [[Department of Cusco]] in the southern highlands of [[Peru]].

== Geography ==
The [[Urubamba River|Willkanuta River]] or Willkamayu is one of the largest rivers of the province. [[Siwinaqucha]], the biggest lake in the province,<ref name=escale/> is also one of the biggest lakes in Peru.

The [[Willkanuta mountain range|Willkanuta]] and [[La Raya mountain range|La Raya]] mountain ranges traverse the province. One of the highest peaks of the province is [[Ausangate]]. ==History==
After independence, the province was created as the province of Tinta. On October 14, 1833, it was divided into two new provinces: Canchis and [[Canas Province|Canas]]. On August 29, 1834, the city of [[Sicuani]] became the official capital of the province of Canchis.

==Political division==
The province is divided into eight [[Districts of Peru|districts]] ({{langx|es|distritos}}, singular: ''distrito''), each of which is headed by a mayor (''alcalde''). The districts, with their capitals in parentheses, are:

* [[Checacupe District|Checacupe]] ([[Checacupe]])
* [[Combapata District|Combapata]] ([[Combopata]])
* [[Marangani District|Marangani]] ([[Marangani]])
* [[Pitumarca District|Pitumarca]] ([[Pitumarca]])
* [[San Pablo District, Canchis|San Pablo]] ([[San Pablo, Cusco|San Pablo]])
* [[San Pedro District, Canchis|San Pedro]] ([[San Pedro, Peru|San Pedro]])
* [[Sicuani District|Sicuani]] ([[Sicuani]])
* [[Tinta District|Tinta]] ([[Tinta, Peru|Tinta]])

== Ethnic groups ==
The people in the province are mainly [[Indigenous peoples of the Americas|indigenous]] citizens of [[Quechua people|Quechua]] descent. [[Quechua language|Quechua]] is the first language of the majority of the population (58.59%), and [[Spanish language|Spanish]] of almost all the rest (41.14%).<ref>[http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales?ori=C inei.gob.pe]  {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130127005551/http://iinei.inei.gob.pe/iinei/RedatamCpv2007.asp?id=ResultadosCensales%3Fori%3DC |date=January 27, 2013 }} INEI, Peru, Censos Nacionales 2007</ref>
