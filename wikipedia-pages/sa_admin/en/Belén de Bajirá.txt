'''Belén de Bajirá''' is a [[Municipalities of Colombia]] in [[Chocó Department|Chocó]] Department. It is located in the [[Urabá Antioquia|Urabá]] subregion. 

The place is rich in gold, nickel, oil, copper, petrol and oil palm, but its population is evidently poor.

In December 2022, New Belén de Bajirá becoming the municipality separates from the [[Riosucio, Chocó|Riosucio]].<ref>[https://www.radionacional.co/noticias-colombia/nuevo-belen-de-bajira-municipio-31-de-choco {{in lang|es}} Nuevo Belén de Bajirá, municipio número 31 del departamento del Chocó]</ref>
