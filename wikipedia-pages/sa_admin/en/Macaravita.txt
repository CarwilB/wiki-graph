'''Macaravita''' is a municipality in the province of [[García Rovira Province|García Rovira]] within the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]].<ref>{{Cite journal |last=Estevan Camacho |first=Claudia Liliana |date=2004 |title=Demografia historica en malaga, macaravita y molagavita 1800-1830 |url=https://noesis.uis.edu.co/handle/20.500.14071/16857 |journal=Facultity of Human Sciences, History school, Dir Jairo Gutierrez Ramos}}</ref>

== History ==
The municipality of Macaravita was founded on 22 December, 1857 by Hernán Pérez de Estrada. 

Before the [[Spanish conquest of Colombia|Spanish conquest]], the territory of  Macaravita was inhabited by indigenous people of the Tuneba or Lache ethnic group.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=2019-12-14 |title=CARACTERIZACIÓN DE LA POBLACIÓN DE MUNICIPIO DE MACARAVITA SANTANDER |url=https://www.macaravita-santander.gov.co/estudios-e-investigaciones/caracterizacion-de-la-poblacion |access-date=2026-01-15 |website=Municipality of Macaravita, Santander}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Macaravita is located in the southeast of the province of García Rovira, east of the department of Santander. 

It borders the municipality of [[Carcasí]] to the north; [[Chiscas]] and [[El Espino, Spain|El Espino]] of the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacáto]] the east; [[Boavita]] and [[Guacamayas, Boyacá|Guacamayas]] of the department of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]] to the south; and [[Capitanejo, Santander|Capitanejo]] to the west.<ref name=":0" />
