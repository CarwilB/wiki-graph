'''Inquisivi''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in [[Bolivia]]. During the presidency of [[José Ballivián]] it was created on November 2, 1844. The capital of the province is [[Inquisivi]].

== Geography ==
The [[Kimsa Cruz mountain range]] traverses the province.
