'''San Fernando''' is a  [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Chaco Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 365,000 inhabitants in an area of  3,489&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and its capital city is [[Resistencia, Chaco|Resistencia]], which is also the provincial capital. It located around 1,020&nbsp;km from the [[Capital federal]].

==Municipalities==

The department consists of 5 first-level municipalities:
*[[Barranqueras]]
*[[Basail]]
*[[Comandante Fontana, Chaco|Fontana]]
*[[Puerto Vilelas]]
*[[Resistencia, Chaco|Resistencia]] (capital)
