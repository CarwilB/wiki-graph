'''Roldanillo''' is a town and municipality located in the [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Valle del Cauca Department|Valle del Cauca]], [[Colombia]].

It is also a tourist hot spot and known as Colombia's flying capital. It has hosted a range of international competitions, including the paragliding world cup superfinals.{{Cn|date=March 2023}}

Roldanillo's Occidente bus terminal has service to Cali, Rozo, Tulua, Buga, Armenia, Cartago, and Pereira, as well as nearby towns such as [[Zarzal]], [[El Dovio]], Naranjal, La Union, Sevilla, Andalucia, Primavera, and La Tulia.
