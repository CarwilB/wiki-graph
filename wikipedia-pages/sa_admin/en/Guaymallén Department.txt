'''Guaymallén''' is a  central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].
[[File:Sagrada Familia Villa Nueva.jpg|thumb|Sagrada Familia Parish in Villa Nueva]]
The provincial subdivision has a population of about 250,000 inhabitants in an area of {{cvt|164|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Villa Nueva, Mendoza|Villa Nueva]], which is located around {{cvt|1,090|km}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

==History==

*1858, on May 14 Guaymallén Department was created.
*1896, on May 19 Villa Nueva was declared as cabecera (capital) of the department.

==Economy==

The economy of the Guaymallén Department, represented 8.7% of the economy of Mendoza Province.

The three main sectors of the economy are banks and financial institutions, the service sector, and industry and manufacturing.
