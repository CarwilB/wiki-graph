'''Barra do Garças''' is a city with a population of 61,135 located in the Brazilian state of [[Mato Grosso]], around {{convert|550|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the capital city of [[Cuiabá]]. It was founded on 13 June 1924, but it became politically independent on 15 September 1948. Nowadays, Barra do Garças is the 8th biggest city in [[Mato Grosso]]. The city is situated on the border between the [[Mato Grosso]] and [[Goiás]] states and due to this the area is considered a [[geodesic]] center.

A small but developed [[tourist city]] due to high agriculture exportation and a strong military presence, Barra do Garças also has a Cristo Redentor (Christ the Redeemer) statue and is known as ''Rio de Janeiro do Oeste'' (Rio de Janeiro of the West.)

The municipality contains the {{convert|11002|ha}} [[Serra Azul State Park]], created in 1994.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|publisher=ICBS – Campus Universitário do Araguaia – UFMT|year=2015|page=1|last1=Pedroni|last2=Sanchez|first1=Fernando|first2=Maryland|title=Parque Estadual Da Serra Azul – PESA|url=http://www.comcerrado.com.br/sitios/serra-azul.pdf|access-date=2016-12-11|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161220100047/http://www.comcerrado.com.br/sitios/serra-azul.pdf|archive-date=20 December 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
Barra do Garças has many other tourist attractions: a Discoporto, (UFOport), water parks, as well as hot springs, mountains, and fresh water beaches.

The city is served by [[Barra do Garças Airport]].
