'''Assis Brasil''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|aˈsiz bɾaˈziw}}) is a  [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the south of the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Acre (state)|Acre]]. Its population is 7,534 (2020 est) and its area is {{convert|4974|km2}}.

The municipality contains part of the [[Rio Acre Ecological Station]].<ref>{{citation|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Estação Ecológica Rio Acre|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=68|accessdate=2016-04-23}}</ref>
It also contains part of the {{convert|931537|ha|adj=on}} [[Chico Mendes Extractive Reserve]], a sustainable use environmental unit created in 1990.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Unidade de Conservação ... MMA}}|language=Portuguese |title=Unidade de Conservação: Reserva Extrativista Chico Mendes|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/cnuc/index.php?ido=relatorioparametrizado.exibeRelatorio&relatorioPadrao=true&idUc=222|accessdate=2016-06-04}}</ref>

It is located near the [[Brazil–Peru Integration Bridge]]. It is the closest Brazilian city to [[Chile]].

==Name==
The municipality was named after [[Joaquim Francisco de Assis Brasil]], a diplomat who was involved in the transference of [[Acre (state)|Acre]] from Bolivian to Brazilian control after the [[Acre War]].
