'''São Benedito do Sul''' is a city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. It is located  172.5&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], the capital of the state of Pernambuco. As of 2025, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] estimates, it has a population of 13,456 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />
[[File:Bandeira_de_São_Benedito_do_Sul_Pernambuco.jpg|thumb|right|160px|[[Lists of city flags|City Flag]]]]

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''São Benedito do Sul''' are based in [[agribusiness]], especially [[sugarcane]], [[banana]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]] and [[chickens]].
