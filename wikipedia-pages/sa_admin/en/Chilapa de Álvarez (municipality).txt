''' Chilapa de Álvarez''' is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Chilapa de Álvarez]]. The municipality covers an area of 566.8&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.

==Demographics==
As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 105,146.<ref>{{cite web|title=Chilapa de Álvarez |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12028a.htm |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=December 23, 2008 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070930024330/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12028a.htm |archivedate=September 30, 2007 }}</ref>

The 2000 [[census]] counted 102,853 persons in the municipality.
