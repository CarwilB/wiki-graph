'''Manhumirim''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Manhuaçu. The city is considered a Transit city. The Omnibus connects Manhumirim to other neighboring cities such as Alto Jequitiba, Alto Caparao, Reducto, and Manhuacu.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 22,802.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/manhumirim/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
