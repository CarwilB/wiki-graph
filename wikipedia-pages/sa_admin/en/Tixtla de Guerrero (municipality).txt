''' Tixtla de Guerrero ''' ({{IPA|es|ˈtiɣstla ðe ɣeˈreɾo|lang}}, {{IPA|nah|ˈtiʃt͡ɬa|lang}}) is a [[Municipalities of Guerrero|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Guerrero]]. The municipal seat lies at [[Tixtla de Guerrero]]. The municipality covers an area of {{Convert|290|km2|sqmi|abbr=}}.{{Citation needed|date=March 2025}}

As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 37,300.<ref>{{cite web|title=Tixtla de Guerrero |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/guerrero/municipios/12061a.htm|work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México|publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]]|access-date=December 23, 2008}}</ref>
