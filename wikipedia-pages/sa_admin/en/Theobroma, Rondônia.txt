'''Theobroma''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Rondônia]]. As of 2020, it had a population of 10,395 and covers an area of 2,197 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://cod.ibge.gov.br/N8P]</ref>

The municipality is named after the plant genus [[Theobroma]], which includes the economically significant [[Theobroma cacao]].
