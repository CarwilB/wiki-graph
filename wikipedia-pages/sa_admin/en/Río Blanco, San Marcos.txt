'''Río Blanco''' is a mountainous [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[San Marcos Department|San Marcos department]] of [[Guatemala]] at 2650 altitude. [[Mam language|Mam]] and Spanish are spoken there.

==Climate==

Río Blanco has temperate climate ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cwb'').
