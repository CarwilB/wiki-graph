'''Botumirim''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the north of the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  In 2020 the population was 6,288 in a total area of 1,572&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/botumirim/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  The elevation is 948 meters.  It became a municipality in 1962.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/graomogol/ |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080823043305/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/graomogol/ |archive-date=2008-08-23 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Geography==
Botumirim is located in the Serra do Espinhaço mountains east of  [[Montes Claros]].  It is 60&nbsp;km south of the BR-251 highway and is connected to [[Grão Mogol]], which is 42&nbsp;km. to the northeast, by dirt roads.   The distance to [[Belo Horizonte]] is {{convert|512|km|mi|abbr=on}}.

Botumirim is part of the statistical microregion of Grão Mogol.  Neighboring municipalities are:  [[Grão Mogol]], [[Itacambira]], [[Bocaiúva]], [[Turmalina]], [[Leme do Prado]], [[José Gonçalves de Minas]], and [[Cristália]].<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |title=Citybrazil |access-date=2008-05-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080224162737/http://www.citybrazil.com.br/mg/regioes/graomogol/index.htm |archive-date=2008-02-24 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The climate is semi-arid, meaning it has slightly more rain than an arid climate.<ref>{{cite book |doi=10.1016/B978-0-12-812128-3.00023-9 |chapter=Climate-Smart Soil Management in Semiarid Regions |title=Soil Management and Climate Change |date=2018 |last1=Garcia-Franco |first1=Noelia |last2=Hobley |first2=Eleanor |last3=Hübner |first3=Rico |last4=Wiesmeier |first4=Martin |pages=349–368 |isbn=978-0-12-812128-3 }}</ref>  The average annual temperature is 22.4 C.  The average annual maximum temperature is 29.3 C and the average annual minimum temperature is 16.7 C.  More than half of the area is considered mountainous with elevations between 1,625 meters and 640 meters.  The main rivers are the Rio do Peixe and the Ribeirão da Onça.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://botumirim.com/secoes.php?id=16 |title=Botumirim.com |access-date=2008-05-24 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080116030355/http://botumirim.com/secoes.php?id=16 |archive-date=2008-01-16 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising (8,000 head in 2006) and agriculture.  The GDP in 2005 was R$ 18,247,000.  The most important agricultural crops are bananas, coffee, tropical and citrus fruits, peanuts, sugarcane, and corn.  There was also production of charcoal from eucalyptus plantations.  In the rural area there were 699 producers.  The total area of agricultural land was 36,346 hectares in 2006.  In the urban area there were no banking agencies in 2006.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php IBGE] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |date=June 11, 2008 }}</ref>
