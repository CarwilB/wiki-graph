'''Parás''' is a rural municipal town in the [[Administrative divisions of Mexico|state]] of [[Nuevo León]],<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-01-13 |title=Este es el pueblo más pequeño de Nuevo León que tienes que visitar |url=https://abcnoticias.mx/tendencia/2025/1/13/este-es-el-pueblo-mas-pequeno-de-nuevo-leon-que-tienes-que-visitar-237027.html |access-date=2025-06-06 |website=ABC Noticias |language=es}}</ref> [[Mexico]], founded on February 17, 1851. It lies southwest of the [[Falcón Reservoir]] in [[Tamaulipas]]. It was founded in what was known as ''Rancho Huizachal de los Canales''. The name ''Parás'' is derived from {{Interlanguage link|Jose María Parás y Ballesteros|es|José María Parás}}, the first constitutional governor of [[Nuevo León]].
