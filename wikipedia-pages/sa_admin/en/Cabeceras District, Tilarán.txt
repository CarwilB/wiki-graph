'''Cabeceras''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Tilarán (canton)|Tilarán]] canton, in the [[Guanacaste Province|Guanacaste]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
Cabeceras was created on 11 June 2020 by Ley 20965.<ref name="dta2017" /> Originally part of [[Quebrada Grande District|Quebrada Grande]] district in the same Tilarán canton, of which it was allocated {{convert|51.25|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}}.

The district has all the basic services such as schools, a high school, [[Healthcare in Costa Rica#EBAIS|EBAIS]] (health services), ASADA (community managed potable water services), cemetery, soccer pitch, local business, coffee producers cooperative and a milk producers association.

== Geography ==
Cabeceras has an area of {{cvt|51.25|km2}}<ref name="inecarea">{{cite web |language=es |title=Área en kilómetros cuadrados, según provincia, cantón y distrito administrativo |url=https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |website=Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos |accessdate=26 September 2020 |archive-date=24 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201024225203/https://www.inec.cr/anuario-estadistico/anuario-estadistico-geografia |url-status=dead }}</ref> and an elevation of {{formatnum:725}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />
