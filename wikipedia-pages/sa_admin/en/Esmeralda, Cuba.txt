'''Esmeralda''' is a municipality and town in the [[Camagüey Province]] of [[Cuba]].

==Geography==
The municipality is divided into the [[barrio]]s of Brasil (also known as Jaronú), Caonao, Guanaja, Quemado and Tabor.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/cesmeralda/index.htm|title=Esmeralda|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-05|language=es}}</ref>

[[Cayo Romano]], one of the [[cay]]s of [[Jardines del Rey]] archipelago is located north of Esmeralda, across the Bay of la Jiguey (''Bahia de Jiguey'').

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Esmeralda had a population of 29,203.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|1480|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids| accessdate=2007-10-05}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|20|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.

==Economy==
The economy is based on crops of  [[sugarcane]], [[coconut]]s, [[pineapple]], [[orange (fruit)|orange]]s and [[tobacco]].
