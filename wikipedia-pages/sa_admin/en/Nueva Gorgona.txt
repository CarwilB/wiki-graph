'''Nueva Gorgona''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chame District]], [[Panamá Oeste Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 4,075 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,980; its population as of 2000 was 3,140.<ref name=inec/>

Nueva Gorgona is 79&nbsp;km west from Panama City on the Pan American Highway (about one hour drive). It is located between the towns of Chame and Coronado, 5 minute drive from either town. Gorgona's beaches and proximity to Panama city have attracted interest from developers.  Gorgona has 4.2&nbsp;km long beach with  white / black sand and turquoise waters.  Gorgona possesses one of the best surfing beaches (Malibu) in the area, and a fish market. The beach is mostly calm, but can have an undertow in some areas.

It has become a tourist destination for locals and ex-pats as described in more detail in WikiVoyage.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Nueva Gorgona – Travel guide at Wikivoyage |url=https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Nueva_Gorgona |access-date=2023-10-10 |website=en.wikivoyage.org |language=en}}</ref>
