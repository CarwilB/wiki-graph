'''Angelim''' (''Angelinne'') ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ɐ̃ʒeˈlĩ]}}) is a municipality/city in the [[Agreste]] region of the state of Pernambuco, Brazil. The population in 2022, according to [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]] by [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]], was 10,241 inhabitants and the total area is 118.72&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />
