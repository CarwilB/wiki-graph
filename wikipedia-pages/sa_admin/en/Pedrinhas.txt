'''Pedrinhas''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|pɛˈdɾij̃ɐs}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Sergipe]] in [[Brazil]]. Its area is {{convert|33.144|km2|abbr=on}}, and its population is 9,665 inhabitants (2020 estimate).<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/se/pedrinhas/panorama Pedrinhas - Panorama], [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], accessed 7 May 2019</ref> It has an elevation of 165 m.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - [http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/link.php?codmun=280510]</ref>
