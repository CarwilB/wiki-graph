'''Inhacorá''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the northern part of the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 2,215 (2020 est.) in an area of 114.11 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/246JD Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Its elevation is 358 m. It is located 482&nbsp;km west of the state capital of [[Porto Alegre]], northeast of [[Alegrete]].
