'''Água Boa''' (English: ''Good Water'') is a town in the Brazilian state of [[Mato Grosso]].

In August 2007 an important Italian [[wind orchestra]] has performed there: the ''Orchestra Fiati Giovanile Italiana e Coro "I Music Piemonteis"'' conducted by Ugo Bairo, and his [[choir]], conducted by [[Carmelo Luca Sambataro]]. There he premiered his wind composition dedicated to the local government: ''The Mato Grosso [[March (music)|March]]''.<ref>{{Cite web|title=Orquestra italiana conhece projetos sociais no interior do Mato Grosso|url=https://www.oriundi.net/orquestra-italiana-conhece-projetos-sociais-no-interior-do-mato-grosso|access-date=2021-09-21|website=ORIUNDI - Giornalismo fatto con passione|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

It is served by [[Água Boa Airport]].
