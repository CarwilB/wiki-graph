'''Santa Luzia''' is a Brazilian municipality located in the state of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion [[Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte (mesoregion)|Metropolitana de Belo Horizonte]] and to the microregion of Belo Horizonte. Its population in 2025 was 230,382.<ref name="IBGE" />

==History==
The first European settlement was established in the modern-day town in 1692 by explorers looking for gold. Santa Luzia became a village in 1847 and received its modern name in 1924.<ref>{{cite web |title=História do Município |url=https://www.cmsantaluzia.mg.gov.br/pagina/ler/1000/historia |publisher=Câmara Municipal De Santa Luzia |access-date=7 February 2026}}</ref>

A church, now known as the Igreja Matriz de Santa Luzia, was established in what would become Santa Luzia around 1721, which was gradually expanded through the rest of the 18th century.<ref>{{cite web |title=Igreja Matriz Santa Luzia |url=https://www.santaluzia.mg.gov.br/v2/index.php/cultura/igreja-matriz-santa-luzia/ |publisher=Prefeitura de Santa Luzia |access-date=7 February 2026}}</ref> Several other churches were established in the city in the 1800s.

==Economy==
In 2023, the [[GDP per capita]] of Santa Luzia was around R$30,155.<ref>{{cite web |title=Produto Interno Bruto dos Municípios (2021) |url=https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/santa-luzia/pesquisa/38/46996 |publisher=IBGE |access-date=7 February 2026}}</ref>
