'''Maipú''' is a [[communes of Chile|commune]] of [[Chile]], located in [[Santiago Province, Chile|Santiago Province]], within the [[Santiago Metropolitan Region]] and forming part of the [[Greater Santiago]] conurbation. It was founded on 16 February 1821 and is historically significant as the site of the [[Battle of Maipú]] (5 April 1818), where Chile's independence was consolidated. Maipú is the second largest commune in the country and is predominantly inhabited by a middle-class population.

==Demographics==
According to the 2002 [[census]] of the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]], Maipú spans {{convert|133|sqkm|0|abbr=on}} and had a population of 479,911, comprising 233,000 men and 247,000 women. The vast majority of people, 476,552 or 99.3%, lived in [[urban area]]s while 3,359 (0.7%) lived in [[rural area]]s. From 1992 to 2002, the population grew by 82.6%, an increase of 211,840 people.<ref name="INE"/>

==Administration==
As a commune, Maipú is a third-level [[administrative division of Chile]] administered by a [[municipal council]], headed by an [[alcalde]] who is directly elected every four years. The 2024-2028 alcalde is [[Tomás Vodanovic]] ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]]).<ref>{{Cite web|title=Alcaldesa Cathy Barriga Guerra|url=http://www.municipalidadmaipu.cl/alcaldesa-cathy-barriga-guerra/|access-date=16 November 2020}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|title=CATHERINE BARRIGA GUERRA (INDEP. UDI)|url=http://www.subdere.gov.cl/autoridades-nacionales/catherine-barriga-guerra-indep-udi}}</ref> The communal council had the following members:
* Ka Quiroz Viveros ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Nicolas Carrancio Fuentes ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Bladymir Muñoz Acevedo ([[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Felipe Farías López (Ind/[[Frente Amplio (Chilean political party)|FA]])
* Ariel Ramos Stocker ([[Communist Party of Chile|PCCh]])
* Graciela Arochas Felber ([[Christian Democratic Party (Chile)|PDC]])
* Edison Aguilera Salazar ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Karen Garrido Neira ([[Republican Party (Chile, 2019)|REP]])
* Juan Carlos Prado Chacón ([[National Renewal (Chile)|RN]])
* Horacio Saavedra ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]])

Within the [[electoral divisions of Chile]], Maipú is represented in the [[Chamber of Deputies of Chile|Chamber of Deputies]] by Pepe Auth ([[Party for Democracy (Chile)|PPD]]) and Mónica Zalaquett ([[Independent Democratic Union|UDI]]) as part of the 20th electoral district, which consists entirely of the Santiago commune. The commune is represented in the [[Senate of Chile|Senate]] by Guido Girardi Lavín (PPD) and Jovino Novoa Vásquez (UDI) as part of the 7th senatorial constituency (Santiago-West).
