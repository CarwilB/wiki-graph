'''Verdelândia''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the north of the [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]].  As of 2020 the population was 9,443 in a total area of {{cvt|1452|km2}}.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/verdelandia/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>  It became a municipality in 1997.

==Geography==
Verdelândia is located at an elevation of 485 meters in the valley of the Rio Verde, a tributary of the [[São Francisco River]].  It is connected by state highway MG-401 to [[Janaúba]] and [[Jaíba]].  It belongs to the statistical microregion of [[Januária]].  Neighboring municipalities are [[Varzelândia]], [[São João da Ponte]], [[Janaúba]], and [[Jaíba]].

*[[Janaúba]]: 40&nbsp;km.

==Economic activities==
The most important economic activities are cattle raising, commerce, and [[subsistence agriculture]].  The GDP in 2005 was R$ 37,844,000.  Verdelândia is in the bottom tier of municipalities in the state with regard to economic and social development.  It suffers from isolation, poor soils, and periodic drought.  As of 2007 there were no banking agencies in the town.  There was a small retail commerce serving the surrounding area of cattle and agricultural lands.  In the rural area there were 722 establishments employing about 3,000 workers.  Only 68 of the farms had tractors.   There were 67 automobiles in all of the municipality, less than 1 for every 100 inhabitants.   There were 61,000 head of cattle in 2006.  The crops with a planted area of more than 100 hectares were beans, sugarcane, manioc, and corn.

In the health sector there were 04 clinics and no hospitals.  In the educational sector there were 11 primary schools and 01 middle school.<ref name="IBGE"/>
