'''General Arenales Partido ''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] on the northern border of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in Argentina.

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 15,000 inhabitants in an area of {{convert|1522|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[General Arenales, Buenos Aires|General Arenales]], which is located {{convert|320|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from Buenos Aires.

==Economy==
The economy of General Arenales Partido is dominated by [[agriculture]]. The main products are [[agronomy|arable]] crops, beef, pork, honey and dairy products.

There are also small scale metal and textile industries as well as numerous small businesses.
