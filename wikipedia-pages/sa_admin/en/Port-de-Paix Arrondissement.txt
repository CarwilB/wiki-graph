'''Port-de-Paix''' ({{langx|ht|Podpè}}) is an [[Arrondissements of Haiti|arrondissement]] in the [[Nord-Ouest (department)|Nord-Ouest]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. As of 2015, the population was 336,650 inhabitants.<ref name="ht2015est">{{Cite web|url=http://www.ihsi.ht/pdf/projection/Estimat_PopTotal_18ans_Menag2015.pdf|title=Population totale, population de 18 ans et plus ménages et densités estimés en 2015|date=March 2015|website=IHSI|language=fr|trans-title=Total Population, Population of 18 and over, Households and Densities Estimated in 2015|access-date=8 May 2017}}</ref> Postal codes in the Port-de-Paix Arrondissement start with the number 31.

The arrondissement consists of the following communes:
* [[Port-de-Paix]]
* [[Bassin-Bleu]]
* [[Chansolme]]
* [[Lapointe, Nord-Ouest|Lapointe]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://lenouvelliste.com/lenouvelliste/article/148647/Creation-de-cinq-nouvelles-communes-par-decret-presidentiel|title=Création de cinq nouvelles communes par décret présidentiel|date=14 August 2015|publisher=Le Nouvelliste|language=fr|editor=Olivier, Louis-Joseph|access-date=9 May 2017}}</ref>
* Île de la Tortue (the island [[Tortuga (Haiti)|Tortuga]])
