'''El Guapo''' is a small town in [[Páez Municipality, Miranda|Páez Municipality]], [[Miranda (state)|Miranda State]], [[Venezuela]]. It was founded by [[:es:Juan Francisco de León|Juan Francisco de León]] on May 16, 1777. 

El Guapo began as a town where vegetables and cacao were grown. It is now known for its natural beauty; the local rivers Río Guapo and Río Chuspita attract tourists. 

Each year on 31 January, El Guapo residents celebrate the visit of an image of the [[Christ Child|Child Jesus]] to [[Tacarigua Lagoon]]. <ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=1 February 2019 |title=Niño Jesús de El Guapo: más de 100 años de fe y tradición |url=http://www.miranda.gob.ve/index.php/nino-jesus-de-el-guapo-mas-de-100-anos-de-fe-y-tradicion/ |access-date=2024-07-27 |website=Miranda Gobernación Bolivariana |language=es}}</ref> The tradition dates back to colonial times.<ref name=":0" />
