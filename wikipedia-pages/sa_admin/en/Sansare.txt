'''Sansare''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[El Progreso Department|El Progreso department]] of [[Guatemala]]. It is situated at 790 m above [[sea level]]. It contains 11,100 people. It covers a terrain of 118&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. Its annual festival is September 23-September 25. Sansare is known for its annual bicycle race, the "Carretera Valle del Sare".<ref>{{Cite news|first=Héctor |last=Contreras |date=28 June 2015 |title=Ochenta personas participan en la tercera carrera Valle del Sare |newspaper=[[Prensa Libre (Guatemala)|Prensa Libre]] |url=http://www.prensalibre.com/guatemala/el-progreso/ochenta-personas-participan-en-la-tercera-carrera-valle-del-sare |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150702043647/http://www.prensalibre.com/guatemala/el-progreso/ochenta-personas-participan-en-la-tercera-carrera-valle-del-sare |archive-date=2 July 2015 |url-status=live |df=dmy}}</ref>

==History==
The municipality and the valley are named after the ''sare'' tree (''[[Acaciella angustissima]]'').<ref name="Historia">{{Cite web|title=Historia del Municipio de Sansare |language=es |publisher=deGUATE |url=http://www.deguate.com/municipios/pages/el-progreso/sansare/historia.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180623221940/http://www.deguate.com/municipios/pages/el-progreso/sansare/historia.php |archive-date=23 June 2018 |url-status=live |df=dmy}}</ref>

The area was already settled by [[Maya peoples|Mayan]]s, associated with the cultures of [[Copán]] and [[Quiriguá]], when the [[Spanish Empire|Spanish]] arrived. The valley was mentioned as early as 1769 in Spanish records, and the municipality was included in those mentioned in the creation of the department of Zacapa in 1871.<ref name="Historia" /> In 1908 it was transferred to El Progresso Department.
