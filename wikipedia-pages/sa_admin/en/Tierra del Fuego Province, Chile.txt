'''Tierra del Fuego Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Tierra del Fuego}}) is one of four [[Provinces of Chile|provinces]] in the southern [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Magallanes and Antártica Chilena Region|Magallanes and Antártica Chilena]] (XII). It includes the Chilean or western part of the [[Isla Grande de Tierra del Fuego|main island of Tierra del Fuego]], except for the part south of the [[Cordillera Darwin]], which is in [[Antártica Chilena Province]]. 

Chilean Tierra del Fuego has two towns, [[Porvenir, Chile|Porvenir]], capital of the province, and [[Cerro Sombrero]], and a number of small villages. A key geographical feature is [[Inútil Bay|Bahía Inútil]] ("Useless Bay"), so named by British geographers in the late 19th century because the bay is not useful as a port.<ref>{{cite book|title=Voyages of the Adventure and Beagle|last = King|first=P.P.|authorlink=Phillip Parker King|year=1839|page=125|url=http://darwin-online.org.uk/content/frameset?viewtype=text&itemID=F10.1&keywords=useless+bay&pageseq=190}}</ref>

==Geology and hydrography==
Gold-bearing sands have been recorded at a number of sites within the Fuegan shores of the Tierra del Fuego Province.<ref>[[Jorge Rabassa|J. Rabassa]], 2008</ref> Chilean Tierra del Fuego is known for numerous small lakes, including Lago Blanco and [[Lago Deseado]]. The climate is [[Subpolar Oceanic]], bordering on a [[Tundra climate]].

==Demography==
According to the 2002 census by the [[National Statistics Institute (Chile)|National Statistics Institute]] (''INE''), the province spans an area of {{convert|22592.7|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}<ref name="INE"/> and had a population of 6,904 inhabitants (4,418 men and 2,486 women), giving it a population density of {{convert|0.3|PD/sqkm|0|abbr=on}}.  It is the third most sparsely populated province in the country after [[Antártica Chilena Province|Antártica Chilena]] and [[Capitan Prat Province|Capitan Prat]], and it is the fifth least-populated province in the country. Of these, 4,734 (68.6%) lived in urban areas and 2,170 (31.4%) in rural areas. Between the 1992 and 2002 censuses, the population fell by 1.2% (81 persons).<ref name= "INE"/>

==History==
The shores among the islands have provided settlement locations for thousands of years of early humans in the Americas. In particular, the [[Wulaia Bay]] area of [[Navarino Island]] (south of the province) has yielded evidence of settlement by the [[Yaghan people]] more than 10,000 years ago.<ref>C. Michael Hogan, 2008</ref>

A [[Tierra del Fuego gold rush|gold rush]] occurred in the late 19th and early 20th century.
