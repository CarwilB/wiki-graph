'''Caatiba''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Bahia]], [[Brazil]]. In 2020 it had 6,488 inhabitants.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/ba/caatiba/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> Until 1961 it belonged to the municipality of [[Vitória da Conquista]] when it became independent. In 1962 the first election took place and the mayor elected, Iris Geraldo Silveira, started his term in 1963.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/dtbs/bahia/caatiba.pdf|title=IBGE historic summary about Caatiba|website=IBGE.gov.br|access-date=December 22, 2017}}</ref>
