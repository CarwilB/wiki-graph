'''Ixtenco''' is a [[Municipalities of Tlaxcala|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|Mexican state]] of [[Tlaxcala]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tlaxcala/ |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |accessdate=January 4, 2009 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070311233823/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/tlaxcala/ |archivedate=March 11, 2007 }}</ref>

Ixtenco covers a territory of roughly {{cvt|43.5|km2}}. Apart from the town of [[Ixtenco]], which is the seat of the local government, the municipality contains only one other community, called Miguel Muñoz León.<ref name=geografia>{{cite web |title=Geografía |publisher=State of Tlaxcala |language=Spanish |url= http://www.tlaxcala.gob.mx/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=847 |accessdate=April 11, 2014 }}</ref><ref name=sedesol>{{cite web |title=Informe Anual Sobre La Situación de Pobreza y Rezago Social |publisher=SEDESOL |language=Spanish |url=http://www.sedesol.gob.mx/work/models/SEDESOL/Informes_pobreza/2014/Municipios/Tlaxcala/Tlaxcala_016.pdf |accessdate=April 11, 2014 |archive-date=February 26, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140226181340/http://www.sedesol.gob.mx/work/models/SEDESOL/Informes_pobreza/2014/Municipios/Tlaxcala/Tlaxcala_016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> The municipality borders the municipalities of Huamantla and Trinidad Sánchez Santos, as well as the [[State of Puebla]].<ref name=geografia/> The municipal government consists of a municipal president, one syndic and seven representatives called regidors.<ref name=encmuc>{{cite web |title=Estado de Tlaxcala  Ixtenco |work=Enciclopedia de Los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=INAFED |year=2010 |language=Spanish |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM29tlaxcala/municipios/29016a.html |accessdate=April 11, 2014 |archive-date=April 13, 2014 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140413142806/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/EMM29tlaxcala/municipios/29016a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The terrain sits on an old lava flow which determines the areas soil composition and underground water flows.<ref name=xalapasco>{{cite web |author=Pedro Morales |title= 
Espectacular cerro de Xalapasco en Ixtenco |publisher=State of Tlaxcala  |date=December 29, 2013 |language=Spanish |url= http://e-tlaxcala.mx/nota/2013-12-29/municipios/espectacular-cerro-de-xalapasco-en-ixtenco |accessdate=April 11, 2014 }}</ref>

From its past, the municipality contains the ruins of two major [[hacienda]]s from the 18th century. The San Antonio Cuauhtla Hacienda is still largely intact, while the San Cristóbal Haciendia is in ruins.<ref name=monhis>{{cite web |title=Monumentos históricos |publisher=State of Tlaxcala |language=Spanish |url= http://www.tlaxcala.gob.mx/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=847 |accessdate=April 11, 2014 }}</ref>

==Geography and environment==
The municipality is on the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]], almost entirely on the lower slopes of volcano [[Malinche (volcano)|La Malinche]] with an average altitude of 2500 m [[AMSL]].<ref name=geografia/><ref name=encmuc/> It is on an old solidified lava flow, which accounts for the area's volcanic soil.<ref name=sanchezverin>{{cite web  |author=Carlos Arturo Giordano Sanchez Verin |title= 
Agricultura y Tradición en un Pueblo Otomí de Tlaxcala: San Juan Ixtenco |publisher=UNAM  |language=Spanish |url= http://www.economia.unam.mx/amhe/pdfs/giordano_pub_01.pdf|accessdate=April 11, 2014 }}</ref> About thirty percent of the territory is rugged, mostly in the west and center. The rest of the territory is semi-flat, especially in the northwest and southeast.<ref name=geografia/><ref name=encmuc/> One notable geographic landmark is Xalapasco hill (2750 m AMSL), a small extinct volcano which contain nine [[maar]]-like craters.<ref name=xalapasco/>

[[File:Cerro Xalapasco cima.jpg|thumb|200px|right|Summit of Xalapasco hill]]
Most of the municipality has a temperate climate with a rainy season in the summer. Average annual temperatures vary between 8-16&nbsp;°C. The average annual rainfall varies between 600–1100&nbsp;mm.<ref name=Prontuario>{{cite web |author=INEGI |title=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos: Ixtenco, Tlaxcala |publisher= |language=Spanish |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/datos-geograficos/29/29016.pdf |accessdate=September 4, 2016 |archive-date=October 3, 2016 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161003173314/http://www3.inegi.org.mx/sistemas/mexicocifras/datos-geograficos/29/29016.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> There is no surface water except for streams that run during the rainy season and a freshwater spring on the side of La Malinche Volcano. Potable water for the municipality comes from this spring and wells.<ref name=encmuc/>

Only vestiges of the area's original [[Madrean pine-oak woodlands]] and [[zacatonal|grassland]] survive, mostly on Xalapasco hill and in the extreme west of the municipality. Most of the area is inhabited or otherwise modified by human activity.<ref name=geografia/> Little wildlife remains as well but includes [[coyote]]s, [[Mexican cottontail|rabbits]], squirrels, [[opossum]]s, skunks, as well as multiple [[bird]]s, [[reptile]]s and [[insect]]s.<ref name=sanchezverin/><ref name=geografia/>

Urbanization of the area has led to problems with garbage and water pollution, but the most serious issue is soil erosion due to [[deforestation]] and [[overgrazing]]. There have been reforestation efforts, mostly [[pine]] species, but these have not been sufficient to stop further environmental damage from unchecked running water and wind coming down from La Malinche.<ref name=geografia/><ref name=sanchezverin/>
