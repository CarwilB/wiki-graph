'''Villa Talea de Castro''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. It is part of the [[Villa Alta District]] in the center of the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca|Sierra Norte Region]].

As of 2013, the municipality had a population of 2,500.<ref>{{cite news|last=Varma|first=Subodh|title=Ignored by big companies, Villa Talea De Castro creates its own mobile service.
