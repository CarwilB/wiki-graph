'''Santo Antônio do Aventureiro''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in the state of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. The population is 3,602 (2020 est.) in an area of 202.03 km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name=ibge>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/HPN Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> The elevation is 385 m. It became an independent municipality in 1962.
