'''Jalapa''' is a [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] and the seat of the [[Jalapa Department]] in [[Guatemala]]. The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|686|km2}}, making it the largest municipality by area in the Jalapa department. As per 2023 estimate, the municipality of Jalapa had a population of 195,321 inhabitants.

== Etymology ==
"Jalapa" is derived from the [[Nahuatl]] words "xal" and "apan" roughly translating to "place in sandy water". The settlement was earlier known as Santa María Xalapán and Santa María Jalapa.<ref name="Deguate">{{cite web|author=Rodriguez, Mireya|title=Historia del municipio de Jalapa|trans-title=History of municipality of Jalapa|work=Deguate.com|date=21 November 2016|url=https://departamentos.deguate.com/jalapa/historia-del-municipio-de-jalapa-jalapa/|access-date=1 June 2025|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="PDD"/>{{rp|page=22}}

== History ==
The settlement of Jalapa was populated by the [[Poqomam people]], who migrated from the foothills of the [[Jumay Volcano]], due to its volcanic activity in the early 15th century. In 1528, the [[Spanish people|Spanish]] conquered the region after a battle.<ref name="PDD">{{cite report|url=https://portal.segeplan.gob.gt/segeplan/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/2100_PDD_JALAPA.pdf|title=Plan de Desarrollo Departamental, Departamento de Jalapa|lang=es|trans-title=Departmental Development Plan, Department of Jalapa|work=Secretaría de Planificación y Programación de la Presidencia|year=2011|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>{{rp|page=19}} As of 1850, the population of Jalapa was approximately 3,500.<ref>{{cite book|last=Baily|first=John|title=Central America; Describing Each of the States of Guatemala, Honduras, Salvador, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica|year=1850|publisher=Trelawney Saunders|location=London|page=88|url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/7306/view/1/88/}}</ref> The [[Jalapa Department]] was established on 24 November 1873. On 26 August 1878, Jalapa was elevated to the category of a city.<ref name="Deguate"/> The first patron feast was established by government agreement of 26 August 1881 fro 29 to 31 December. The date was subsequently changed on several occasions and was set as 15 September in 2010.<ref name="Deguate"/>

== Geography ==
Jalapa is one of the seven [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipalities]] in the Jalapa Department in [[Guatemala]]. It also serves as the seat of the department.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/21__jalapa/|title=Jalapa Department|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> It is located in the highlands of southeastern Guatemala at an elevation of {{cvt|1362|m}}.<ref name="Brit">{{cite web|url=https://www.britannica.com/place/Jalapa-Guatemala|title=Jalapa, Guatemala|work=[[Britannica]]|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|686|km2}}, making it the largest municipality by area in the Jalapa department.<ref name="PDD"/>{{rp|page=20}} It is located to the north the [[Pan American Highway]], and serves as the commercial and administrative centre for the Jalapa department.<ref name="Brit"/> 

== Demographics ==
As per 2023 estimate, the municipality of Jalapa had a population of 195,321 inhabitants. The population consists of 96,083 males and 99,238 females. The entire population was classified as urban. The municipality had a literacy rate of 81.4%. [[Spanish language|Spanish]] was the most spoken language, with almost 99% of the population indicating it as their mother tongue. The [[Ladino people|Ladino]] (49.4%) and [[Xinka people|Xinka]] (48.9%) people formed the majority of the population. About 91.6% of the population were born locally.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/guatemala/admin/jalapa/2101__jalapa/|title=Jalapa|work=City Population.de|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>
