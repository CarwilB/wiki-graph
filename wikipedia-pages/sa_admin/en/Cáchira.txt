'''Cáchira''' is a [[Colombian municipality]] located in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Norte de Santander]]. The urban centre is situated at an altitude of {{convert|2025|m|ft}} in the [[Cordillera Oriental (Colombia)|Eastern Ranges]] of the Colombian [[Andes]].<ref name=WebsiteCachira>{{in lang|es}} {{URL|http://www.cachira-nortedesantander.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml|Official website Cáchira}}</ref>
