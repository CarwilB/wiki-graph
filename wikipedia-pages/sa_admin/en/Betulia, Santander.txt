'''Betulia''' is a town and municipality in the [[Santander Department]] in northeastern [[Colombia]].

==Biodiversity==
Frog ''[[Hypodactylus adercus]]'' is only known from Betulia, its [[Type locality (biology)|type locality]].<ref name=Frost/><ref name=LAC/>
