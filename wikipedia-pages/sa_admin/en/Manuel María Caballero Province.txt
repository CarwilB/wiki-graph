'''Manuel María Caballero Province''' is one of the fifteen provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz]] [[Departments of Bolivia|Department]] and is situated in the department's western parts. The province name honors deputy [[Manuel María Caballero]] from [[Vallegrande]], one of the signatories of the Bolivian constitution (Constitución política) of 5 August 1861.

== Location ==
''Manuel María Caballero Province'' is located between 17° 26' and 18° 15' [[Latitude|South]] and between 64° 07' and 64° 50' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 90&nbsp;km from North to South, and 75&nbsp;km from West to East.

The province is situated on the western edge of the Bolivian lowlands and borders [[Cochabamba Department]] in the West, [[Vallegrande Province]] in the South, [[Florida Province]] in the East, and [[Ichilo Province]] in the Northeast.

== Population ==
The population of Manuel María Caballero Province has increased by circa 35% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 16,074 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2001: 20,562 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 21,517 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=2007-08-25 }}</ref>
*2010: 22,916 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosSantaCruz.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística - Proyecciones] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081118162334/http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosSantaCruz.pdf |date=2008-11-18 }}</ref>
*2024: 23,189 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>{{Cite web |title=Manuel María Caballero (Province, Bolivia) - Population Statistics, Charts, Map and Location |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/bolivia/dept/admin/santa_cruz/0713__manuel_mar%C3%ADa_caballero/ |access-date=2024-09-11 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref>

20.0% of the population is urban population. ''(1992)''

44.9% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

95.4% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 43.8% speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]], and 1.6 speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]]. ''(1992)''

70.1% of the population have no access to electricity, 70.8% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

90.1% of the population are Catholics, 8.4% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipios]]:
*[[Comarapa Municipality]] – 14,660 inhabitants ''(2001)''
*[[Saipina Municipality]] – 5,350 inhabitants ''(2001)''
