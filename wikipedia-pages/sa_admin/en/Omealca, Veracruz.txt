'''Omealca''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://oem.com.mx/elsoldecordoba/local/padres-de-familia-toman-la-primaria-agustin-melgar-en-omealca-en-demanda-de-un-maestro-26153907|title=Familias toman primaria en Omealca; piden un maestro|work=[[El Sol de Córdoba]]|first=Adriana|last=Luna|date=6 October 2025|access-date=25 November 2025|language=es}}</ref> The municipal seat is [[Omealca]].

==Etymology==
Omealca means place between two rivers in [[nahuatl]], due to it being next to Blanco river and above a subterranean one. 

==Geography==
===Climate===
Omealca's has a very diverse range of climates, as it is next to the state of [[Puebla]] and [[Oaxaca]], meaning that besides having [[Veracruz]]'s tropical climate, it also has Puebla's mountainous climate and Oaxaca's [[arid climate]].
