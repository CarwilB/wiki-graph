'''Gómez Farías Municipality''' is situated in the Mexican state of [[Tamaulipas]]. The town of Gómez Farías serves as the municipal seat of government. Covering an area of 433 square kilometres (167 sq mi), Gómez Farías Municipality had a population of 8,786 as of 2010. The town of Gómez Farías, specifically, had a population of 883 in the same year.<ref name="gomez-farias">{{cite web |title=MUNICIPIO DE GÓMEZ FARÍAS |url=http://www.campotamaulipas.gob.mx/oeidrus/pdfs/basica/gomez_farias.pdf |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141221190618/http://www.campotamaulipas.gob.mx/oeidrus/pdfs/basica/gomez_farias.pdf |archive-date=2014-12-21 |access-date=19 Dec 2014 |website=www.campotamaulipas.gob.mx}}</ref> Additionally, within the municipal area, a portion of 23,124 hectares (57,140 acres) (equivalent to 231 square kilometers) is encompassed by the [[El Cielo Biosphere Reserve]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Comisión Nacional para el Conocimiento y Uso de la Biodiversidad |url=http://www2.inecc.gob.mx/publicaciones/libros/2/cielo.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160213201104/http://www2.inecc.gob.mx/publicaciones/libros/2/cielo.html |archive-date=2016-02-13 |access-date=23 Dec 2014 |website=Instituto Nacional de Ecología}}</ref>

==History==
In 1749, a settlement was established on the site of Gómez Farías to concentrate the semi-nomadic indigenous peoples of the area into a single community.  On 10 January 1869, the municipality was created and named after [[Valentín Gómez Farías]], a former President of Mexico.<ref name="gomez-farias"/>

==Geography==
===Climate===
Most of the State of [[Tamaulipas]] is semi-arid and typically covered in a thorny scrub vegetation called [[Tamaulipan Mezquital]].  Gómez Farías and a few other areas on the eastern slopes of the [[Sierra Madre Oriental]] are the exception, with high precipitation and luxuriant vegetation. [[El Cielo Biosphere Reserve]] is the most northerly area in Mexico of tropical [[Veracruz moist forests]]. Temperate [[cloud forest]]s are found at higher elevations.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.botany.si.edu/projects/cpd/ma/ma9.htm |title=Gomez Farias Region and El Cielo Biosphere Reserve |website=Botany.si.edu |accessdate=2014-12-18 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20150305003818/http://botany.si.edu/projects/cpd/ma/ma9.htm |archivedate=2015-03-05 }}</ref>

==Economy==

[[Sugar cane]], mostly grown with irrigation, is by far the most important agricultural product of the municipality.  [[Cattle]], [[citrus]], and [[mango]] are also important. In the mountainous part of the municipality, [[maize]] is the principal crop, grown mostly by small-scale farmers. Gómez Farías attracts a sizable number of tourists, including bird watchers and botanists, to the [[El Cielo Biosphere Reserve]] and scenic areas along the [[Sabinas River|Sabinas]] and Frio rivers.<ref name="gomez-farias"/>
