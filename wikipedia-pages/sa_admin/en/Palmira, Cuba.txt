'''Palmira''' is a municipality and town in the [[Cienfuegos Province]] of [[Cuba]]. The whole municipality has 31,813 inhabitants.<ref name="atenas">{{Cite web |author=Atenas.cu |year=2004 |title=2004 Population trends, by Province and Municipality |url=http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20070927111826/http://www.atenas.inf.cu/todo/Estadisticas/TABLA%20No_3balance.htm <!-- Bot retrieved archive --> |archivedate=2007-09-27 |accessdate=2007-10-07 |language=es}}</ref>

==History==
It was founded in 1879<ref name=guije/> under the name ''Las Casas''. The name was changed to Palmira and the community was incorporated in 1940.

==Geography==
[[File:Palmira (Cuba - town's church).jpg|thumb|left|150px|Town's church]]
Located in the middle of its province, Palmira is 12&nbsp;km far from [[Cienfuegos]], 16 from [[Cruces, Cuba|Cruces]], and 52 from [[Santa Clara, Cuba|Santa Clara]]. It is bordered by the municipalities of [[Rodas]], [[Lajas, Cuba|Lajas]], Cruces, [[Cumanayagua]] and Cienfuegos.

In 1943 the municipality was divided into the [[barrio]]s of Primero, Segundo, Arango y Escarza.<ref name=guije>{{Cite web|url=http://www.guije.com/pueblo/municipios/vpalmira/index.htm|title=Palmira|author=Guije.com|accessdate=2007-10-07|language=es}}</ref>

The thermal water resort of Arriete-Ciego Montero, {{convert|12|km|mi|abbr=on}} form the town of Palmira, is also famous for the bottled water plant named "[[Ciego Montero]]".

==Demographics==
In 2022, the municipality of Palmira had a population of 31,813.<ref name="citypopulation">{{Cite web |title=Cuba: Administrative Division (Provinces and Municipalities) - Population Statistics, Charts and Map |url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/cuba/admin/ |access-date=2024-02-03 |website=www.citypopulation.de}}</ref> With a total area of {{convert|318|km2|sqmi|abbr=on}},<ref name=statoids>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ycu.html|title=Municipios of Cuba|author=Statoids|date=July 2003| accessdate=2007-10-07}}</ref> it has a population density of {{convert|104.3|/km2|/sqmi|abbr=on}}.
