'''Bom Jardim''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈbõ ʒaɦˈdĩ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]]/city in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Pernambuco]] in [[Brazil]]. The population in [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]] was 37,629 inhabitants and the total area is 226,639&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==

* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste]] of Pernambuco
* Boundaries - [[Orobó]] and [[Machados]]   ('''N''');  [[João Alfredo]]   ('''S''');  [[Vicência]] and [[Limoeiro]]   ('''E''');   [[Surubim]] and [[Casinhas]]   ('''W''').
* Area - 218.43&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 333 m
* Hydrography - [[Goiana River]]
* Vegetation - [[Subcaducifólia]]{{clarify|date=October 2012}} forest
* Climate  -  [[Tropical]] hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 23.9&nbsp;°C
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 115&nbsp;km

==Economy==

The main economic activities in Bom Jardim are related with extractive industry, commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially breeding of [[cattle]], [[goat]]s, [[sheep]], [[pig]]s, [[chickens]]; and plantations of [[banana]]s and [[pineapple]]s.
