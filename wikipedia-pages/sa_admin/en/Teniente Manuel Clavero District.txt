'''Teniente Manuel Clavero District'''  ([[Spanish language|Spanish]] ''teniente'' [[lieutenant]])  is one of four [[Districts of Peru|districts]] of the [[Putumayo Province]] in [[Peru]]. It is also the northernmost district in Peru.<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080423164200/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-04-23 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref>

== Authorities ==
The current [[mayor]] of the district is Luis Enrique Calderón Aspajo (Fuerza Loretana).<ref>[http://aplicaciones007.jne.gob.pe/sraepublico/wf_inicio.aspx JNE 2015] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20151215190905/http://aplicaciones007.jne.gob.pe/sraepublico/wf_inicio.aspx |date=2015-12-15 }}</ref>
