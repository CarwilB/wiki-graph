'''Pocrí District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Los Santos Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 3,397.<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> The district covers a total area of 280&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. The capital lies at the city of [[Pocrí, Los Santos]].<ref name="Statoids"/>

==Administrative divisions==
Pocrí District is divided administratively into the following ''[[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimientos]]'':

*[[Pocrí, Los Santos|Pocrí]] (capital)
*[[El Cañafístulo]]
*[[Lajamina]]
*[[Paraíso, Los Santos|Paraíso]]
*[[Paritilla]]
