'''Plaza''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2016 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 238,750.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The city of [[Guarenas]] is the [[County seat|administrative centre]] of the Plaza Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>{{Cite web| url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | title=Estado Miranda.- División político territorial, según municipios y parroquias, 2003 | format=ZIP | accessdate=2008-03-22 | url-status=dead | archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip | archivedate=2007-12-21}}</ref>

==History==
The city of Guarenas was established in 1621 as Nuestra Señora de Copacabana de los Guarenas.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Notitarde |title=Municipios Plaza y Zamora, estado Miranda: Patrimonio Cultural de Venezuela |url=https://www.notitarde.com.ve/pais/64973/municipios-plaza-y-zamora-estado-miranda-patrimonio-cultural-de-venezuela |access-date=2026-01-16 |website=Notitarde |language=es}}</ref> Today, Guarenas has virtually merged with its neighbor, [[Guatire]].

The Curupao Power Plant, which was inaugurated in 1933, still provides electricity to Guarenas and Guatire.<ref>{{Cite web |last=actualidad-24.com |title=Historia de la Electricidad en Venezuela |url=https://www.actualidad-24.com/2018/03/historia-luz-electrica-Venezuela.html |access-date=2026-01-16}}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Plaza Municipality, according to a 2016 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 238,750 (up from 203,590 in 2000).  This amounts to 8.3% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|1318.77|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/miranda/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=March 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Plaza Municipality is Willian Eduardo Páez Sosa, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 41% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio - Plaza | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D13%26mun%3D8%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120229231754/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D13%26mun%3D8%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-29 | access-date=2008-03-23 | url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web| url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | title=Elecciones 30 de julio de 2000 directorio de alcaldes electos | language=es | trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 directory of elected mayors | access-date=2008-03-23 | archive-date=2006-10-02 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf | url-status=dead}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]] (Guarenas).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
