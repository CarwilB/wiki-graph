'''Tacna''' ({{IPA|es|ˈtaɣna}}; [[Aymara language|Aymara]] and [[Quechuan languages|Quechua]]: ''Taqna'') is a [[Districts of Peru|district]] of [[Tacna Province|Tacna]], a province of the [[department of Tacna]], [[Peru]].<ref>{{in lang|es}} [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística e Informática]]. ''[http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ Banco de Información Distrital] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080102023316/http://desa.inei.gob.pe/mapas/bid/ |date=2008-01-02 }}''. Retrieved April 11, 2008.</ref> Its capital is [[Tacna|San Pedro de Tacna]].

== Etymology ==
The term ''Tacna'' is derived from two [[Quechua languages|Quechua]] words: ''Taka'', meaning 'to hit', and ''na'', which means 'place'. Thus, the full name means "I hit this place" or "I rule this place".<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.munitacna.gob.pe/pagina/sf/tacna/historia|title=Historia|website=Municipalidad Provincial de Tacna}}</ref>
