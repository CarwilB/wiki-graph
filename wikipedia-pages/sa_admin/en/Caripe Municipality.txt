'''Caripe''' is one of the 13 municipalities of the state of [[Monagas]], [[Venezuela]]. The municipality's capital is [[Caripe]], and the [[Cueva del Guácharo National Park]] is its main tourist attraction. In the north it borders the state of [[Sucre (state)|Sucre]], to the east is the municipality of [[Piar Municipality, Monagas|Piar]], to the south are the municipalities of [[Bolivar Municipality, Monagas|Bolivar]] and [[Punceres Municipality, Monagas|Punceres]] and to the west is the municipality of [[Acosta Municipality, Monagas|Acosta]].<ref name=alcaldia/>

== Culture ==
=== Public holidays ===
Carnivals: In February or March.

=== Cuisine ===
The dishes typical in Caripe are arepa and hot chocolate. The people drink rum of petals of roses.{{cn|date=January 2023}}
