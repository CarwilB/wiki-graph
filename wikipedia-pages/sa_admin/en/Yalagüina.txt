'''Yalagüina''' ({{IPA|es|ʝalaˈɣwina}}) is a [[Municipalities of Nicaragua|municipality]]{{cn|date=August 2024}} in the [[Madriz (department)|Madriz]] [[Departments of Nicaragua|department]] of [[Nicaragua]].<ref>{{cite web | url=https://www.google.com/maps/place//@13.483333,-86.483333,12z/data=!5m1!1e4?hl=en&entry=ttu | title=Google Maps }}</ref>

== {{Coord|13|29|N|86|29|W|region:NI_type:city|display=title}}Geography ==
The municipal limits reach [[Totogalpa]] in the North, and [[Pueblo Nuevo, Estelí|Pueblo Nuevo]] to the South. Bordering to the East is [[Palacagüina]] and to the West is [[Somoto, Madriz|Somoto]]. Yalagüina is 205km from [[Managua]].{{Madriz Department}}
