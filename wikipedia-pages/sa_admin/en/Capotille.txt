'''Capotille''' ({{IPA|fr|kapɔtij}}; {{langx|ht|Kapotiy}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Ouanaminthe Arrondissement]], in the [[Nord-Est (department)|Nord-Est]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]]. It has 15,086 inhabitants and is adjacent to the [[Dominican Republic–Haiti border]].

== Communal Sections ==
The commune consists of two [[communal section]]s, namely:
* [[Capotille (communal section)|Capotille]], urban and rural, contains the town of Capotille
* [[Lamine (communal section)|Lamine]], rural
