'''Bellavista''' is one of ten [[Provinces of Peru|provinces]] of the [[San Martín Region]] in northern [[Peru]].

It was created on 31 May 1984.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.leyes.congreso.gob.pe/Documentos/Leyes/23844.pdf|title=Ley N° 23844|publisher=[[Congress of the Republic of Peru]]|access-date=25 September 2025|language=es}}</ref>

==Political division==
The province is divided into six districts.
* [[Alto Biavo District|Alto Biavo]] ([[Cuzco, Bellavista|Cuzco]])
* [[Bajo Biavo District|Bajo Biavo]] ([[Nuevo Lima]])
* [[Bellavista District, Bellavista|Bellavista]] ([[Bellavista, Bellavista|Bellavista]])
* [[Huallaga District|Huallaga]] ([[Ledoy]])
* [[San Pablo District, Bellavista|San Pablo]] ([[San Pablo, Bellavista|San Pablo]])
* [[San Rafael District, Bellavista|San Rafael]] ([[San Rafael, Bellavista|San Rafael]])
