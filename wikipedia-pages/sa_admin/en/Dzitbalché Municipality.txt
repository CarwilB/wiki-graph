'''Dzitbalché''' (from [[Yucatec Maya language|Yucatec Maya]] ''jun tsʼíit báalcheʼ'', "a stick or large stem of [[balché]]"<ref name=intro>{{cite book |last=Gómez Montejo |first=Alicia E. |chapter=Dzitbalché, "Árbol de la Pitarrilla" |title=Las H. Juntas Municipales del Estado de Campeche. Una breve descripción |language=es |date=October 2002 |place=Campeche |url=https://www.calkini.net/municipio/dzitas1.htm |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref>) is a [[Municipalities of Campeche|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Campeche]], located {{convert|73|km}} northeast of the state capital of [[Campeche City]]. Its creation from the municipality of [[Calkiní Municipality|Calkiní]] was approved in 2019 and went into force on 1 January 2021.

==Geography==
The municipality of Dzitbalché is located in northern Campeche. It borders the municipalities of Calkiní to the north and west, [[Hecelchakán Municipality|Hecelchakán]] to the south, [[Hopelchén Municipality|Hopelchén]] to the southeast, and [[Santa Elena Municipality|Santa Elena]] in [[Yucatán]] to the northeast. The municipality covers an area of {{convert|366.79|km2}}.<ref name=creation/>

Dzitbalché has a [[tropical savanna climate]] with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures range between {{convert|23.7|and(-)|27.7|C}}, and average annual precipitation is about {{convert|1070|mm}}.<ref>{{cite web| url = https://smn.conagua.gob.mx/tools/RESOURCES/Normales5110/NORMAL04070.TXT | title = Normales climatológicas | publisher = [[Servicio Meteorológico Nacional (Mexico)|Servicio Meteorológico Nacional]] | language = es | accessdate = 17 October 2020}}</ref>

==History==
The [[Maya peoples|Maya]] founded Dzitbalché around 1443–1445. The ''[[Songs of Dzitbalché]]'' originate from this pre-Hispanic period. The area was ruled by the Canché family when the Spanish arrived.<ref name=intro/><ref name=historia>{{cite book |last=Canto Sosa |first=Santiago |chapter=Reseña histórica |title=Los ojos de Ah-Canul. Notas, Reseñas y Comentarios, 2000-2017 |language=es |date=2017 |place=Calkiní |url=https://www.calkini.net/municipio/dzitas1.htm |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref>

When Campeche first organized its administrative divisions in 1861, Dzitbalché was made a ''municipalidad'' in the ''partido'' of Hecelchakán. When the municipality of Calkiní was established in 1916, Dzitbalché became a municipal section (''sección municipal'') with its own board (''junta'') within Calkiní.<ref>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/gobierno.html |title=Gobierno |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Campeche |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |access-date=17 October 2020 |archive-date=10 October 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191010174010/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/gobierno.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> On 31 March 2019 the Campeche state legislature approved the dissolution of the municipal section and the creation of the municipality of Dzitbalché.<ref>{{cite news |date=1 April 2019 |url=https://www.novedadescampeche.com.mx/estado/campeche/dzitbalche-el-municipio-numero-13/ |title=Dzitbalché, el municipio número 13 |language=es |newspaper=Novedades Campeche |accessdate=17 October 2020}}</ref> The decree establishing the municipality was gazetted on 26 April 2019 and went into force on 1 January 2021.<ref name=decree>{{cite news |date=26 April 2019 |title=Decreto Número 45 |url=http://periodicooficial.campeche.gob.mx/sipoec/public/periodicos/201904/PO0919SS26042019.pdf |language=es |work=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Campeche, Segunda Sección |pages=3–4 |access-date=17 October 2020}}</ref>

==Administration==
Dzitbalché will hold its first elections as an independent municipality in 2021. Until then, its administration is being carried out by the members of the former municipal board of Dzitbalché, elected when it was still subordinate to Calkiní.<ref name=creation/> The current president is José Carlos Sánchez Flores.<ref>{{cite news |date=16 October 2019 |url=https://www.elsur.mx/sanchez-flores-rinde-primer-informe-actividades/ |title=Sánchez Flores rinde primer informe de actividades |language=es |newspaper=El Sur de Campeche |accessdate=17 October 2020 |archive-date=19 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201019033534/https://www.elsur.mx/sanchez-flores-rinde-primer-informe-actividades/ |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the localities that now comprise the municipality of Dzitbalché recorded a population of 14,387 inhabitants.<ref name=creation/>

Two of the localities in the municipality are classified as urban:
*The municipal seat also named [[Dzitbalché]], which recorded a population of 11,686 inhabitants in the 2010 Census, and
*Bacabchén, which recorded a population of 2,527 inhabitants in 2010.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=001 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Calkiní |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=[[SEDESOL]] |accessdate=17 October 2020 |archive-date=28 October 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201028013107/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=04&mun=001 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Economy and infrastructure==
The municipal seat of Dzitbalché functions as a market town for agricultural and artisanal products from the surrounding area. Handicrafts practised include products made from the ''palma de guano'' (''[[Sabal]]'' spp.), woodworking, embroidery, and gold jewelry.<ref name=creation/><ref name=historia/><ref name=encyclopedia>{{cite encyclopedia |url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/municipios/04001a.html |title=Calkiní |language=es |encyclopedia=Estado de Campeche |series=Enciclopedia de los Municipios y Delegaciones de México |publisher=[[INAFED]] |accessdate=17 October 2020 |archive-date=15 September 2018 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180915102231/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM04campeche/municipios/04001a.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> Manufacturing is underdeveloped apart from a ''[[maquiladora]]'' operated by [[Augusta Sportswear]].<ref name=creation/>

The municipality has eighteen schools including a [[National College of Professional Technical Education|CONALEP]] school, and one health centre. [[Mexican Federal Highway 180]] and the Campeche–Mérida railway (which will become part of the [[Mayan Train]]) run north–south through the municipality.<ref name=creation/>
