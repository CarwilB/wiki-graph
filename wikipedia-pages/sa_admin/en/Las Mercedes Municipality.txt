The '''Las Mercedes Municipality''' is one of the 15 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the central [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Guárico]] and, according to a 2010 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 31,825.<ref>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/guarico/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls Population data at ocei.gov.ve]{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Overview==
The town of [[Las Mercedes del Llano]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Las Mercedes Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>[http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Guarico.zip Infos at ocei.gov.ve]{{Dead link|date=November 2018 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

The Municipality includes the 4.8&nbsp;km Third Orinoco River Crossing, between [[Cabruta]] and [[Caicara del Orinoco]] in [[Bolívar (state)|Bolívar]].
