'''Cavaillon''' ({{IPA|fr|kavajɔ̃|-|LL-Q150 (fra)-Sartus85-cavaillon.wav}}; {{langx|ht|Kavayon}}) or '''Cavaellon''' is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] and a rural town located in the [[Aquin Arrondissement]] of the [[Sud (department)|Sud]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].

==Geography==
Its nearest neighboring towns/cities are [[Nan Cotie]] (12&nbsp;km north), [[Saint-Louis-du-Sud]] (20&nbsp;km southeast), and [[Les Cayes]] (16&nbsp;km west).

===Villages===
[[Balou]], [[Boileau, Cavaillon, Haiti|Boileau]], [[Bedo, Cavaillon, Haiti|Bedo]], [[Martineau, Haiti|Martineau]], [[Cassignol]], [[Bonne Fin]], [[Digo Salo]], [[Fevre]], [[Grand Dieu]], [[Grand Place, Cavaillon, Haiti|Grand Place]], [[Grand-Blois]], [[Gross Mary]], [[L'ellet]], [[La Marche, Cavaillon, Haiti|La Marche]], [[Nan Cotie]], [[Rosseau, Cavaillon, Haiti|Rosseau]] and [[Volbrune]].

==Personalities==
The town is most famous for being the birthplace of both [[Benito Martínez (longevity claimant)|Benito Martínez Abrogán]],<ref>[http://www.economist.com/obituary/displaystory.cfm?story_id=8049523 Economist - "Benito Martínez Abrogán, possibly the world's oldest man"]. Retrieved October 27, 2006</ref> Cuba's oldest-ever person, and [[George Valris]], a famous Caribbean artist.
