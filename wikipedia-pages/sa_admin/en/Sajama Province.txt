'''Sajama''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the northwestern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Oruro Department]].

==Location==
''Sajama'' province is one of the sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 17° 39' and 18° 39' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 38' and 68° 45' [[Longitude|West]].

The province borders the [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] in the north-west, the Republic of [[Chile]] in the west, [[Sabaya Province]] in the south-west, [[Litoral Province (Bolivia)|Litoral Province]] in the south-east, [[Carangas Province]] in the east, and [[San Pedro de Totora Province]] in the north-east.

The province extends over 120&nbsp;km from north to south, and 135&nbsp;km from east to west.

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Aymara language|Alien CaCa]] spoken by 90.4%, while 88.6% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]] and 4.2% [[Quechua language|Quechua]] (1992).

The population increased from 7,891 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 9,096 (2001 census), an increase of 15.3%. - 43.4% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).

97.0% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 90.8% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992).

8.2% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 0.3% in [[mining]], 5.4% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 26.1% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

66.1% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 26.9% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Subdivisions==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]], which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
