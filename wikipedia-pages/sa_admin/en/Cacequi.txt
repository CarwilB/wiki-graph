'''Cacequi''' is a municipality in the state of [[Rio Grande do Sul]], Brazil, 407&nbsp;km from [[Porto Alegre]]. As of 2020 its population was estimated at 12,423. The town is situated at an elevation of 103 m, with an area of 2,441&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>, and is known as the ''capital of the watermelon''.

== History ==

The town was emancipated on 23 December 1944. Its [[Patron Saint]] is [[Blessed Virgin Mary (Roman Catholic)|Our Lady]] of Victory. Its name derives from the [[Tupi language]]: Cacequi, (bean's water) from ''cumandá'', bean; ''í'', water. Other definition say the name of the city means: ''indigenous chief water'' or ''indigenous chief River''. The mayor of the city is Francisco Fonseca Franco.
