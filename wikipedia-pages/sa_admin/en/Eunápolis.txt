'''Eunápolis''' is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Bahia]] with 113,709 people ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]).<ref name="IBGE" /> The town was founded in 1988. In 1996, the city was made the seat of the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of Eunápolis]].
