'''Vicente Guerrero''' is a [[Municipalities of Puebla|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Puebla]]. The municipal seat is [[Santa María del Monte]].

==Geography==
The municipality had 20,391 inhabitants in 2005 (according to [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|INEGI]]). From this total, 9,646 were men and 10,745 women. Its total area is 234.73 km<sup>2</sup>. 

The geographical coordinates of the municipality are 18° 32′ N and 97° 12′ W. Its [[altitude]] is 2600 meters above the [[sea level]].

Source: Statistics from [[Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Geografía|INEGI]].
