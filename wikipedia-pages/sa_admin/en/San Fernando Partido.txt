'''San Fernando''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' of [[Buenos Aires Province]], [[Argentina]], in the north of [[Greater Buenos Aires]]. Its capital is [[San Fernando, Buenos Aires|San Fernando]]. It is twenty-eight kilometers from the city of [[Buenos Aires]].

==Population distribution (2010)==
Of the 163,240 inhabitants recorded in the 2010 census, the distribution is:<ref name=SanFerPoblación/>
*[[San Fernando de la Buena Vista|San Fernando]]: 76,726
*[[Victoria, Buenos Aires|Victoria]]: 44,959
*[[Virreyes, Buenos Aires|Virreyes]]: 38,599
