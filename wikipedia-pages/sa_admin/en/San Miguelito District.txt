'''San Miguelito''' is a city and [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Panamá Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the 2023 Panamanian census was 280,777.<ref name="_2023"/> The district covers an area of 50.1&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. San Miguelito district is completely [[enclaved]] within Panama District (which completely surrounds it) and it is included in the Panama City Metropolitan Area.<ref name="_2023"/>

[[Association football|Football]] player [[Luis Tejada]] was born in San Miguelito, and both [[Blas Pérez]] and [[Kevin Kurányi]] were once residents of the district.
