'''Miracema do Tocantins''' ({{langx|xer|Krikahâ dawanã hã}} {{IPA|xer|kɾikahə dawanə̃ hə̃|}}<ref name="Cotrim-2016">{{cite thesis|last=Cotrim |first=Rodrigo Guimarães Prudente Marquez |date=2016 |url=https://repositorio.unb.br/bitstream/10482/22780/1/2016_RodrigoGuimar%C3%A3esPrudenteMarquezCotrim.pdf |title=Uma descrição da morfologia e de aspectos morfossintaxe da língua Akwê-Xerente (Jê Central) |type=Ph.D. dissertation |location=Brasília |publisher=Universidade de Brasília}}</ref>{{rp|44}}) is a municipality of the [[Brazil]]ian state of [[Tocantins]]. Its estimated population in 2020 was 17,936 inhabitants. It has an area of {{cvt|2,656|km2}} and an average elevation of {{cvt|197|m}}.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/perfil.php?codmun=171320|title=IBGE {{!}} Cidades {{!}} Tocantins {{!}} Miracema do Tocantins|website=cidades.ibge.gov.br|access-date=2016-05-19}}</ref>

It was the state capital until 1990.
