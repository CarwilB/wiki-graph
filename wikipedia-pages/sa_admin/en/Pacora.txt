'''Pacora''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Panamá District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] located near [[Tocumen International Airport]].

It is regarded as the industrial area of Panama where many multinationals are moving into for their logistics and industrial hub.<ref>http://www.mici.gob.pa/detalle.php?cid=20&id=4953<nowiki/>{{Dead link|date=December 2023}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://laestrella.com.pa/economia/consejo-aprueba-crear-zona-franca-pacora/23974198|title=Consejo aprueba crear zona franca en Pacora|access-date=2017-06-27|archive-date=2016-12-03|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161203154111/http://laestrella.com.pa/economia/consejo-aprueba-crear-zona-franca-pacora/23974198|url-status=dead}}</ref> More than 250 multinationals from manufacturing to IT services are moving there.<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.presidencia.gob.pa/en/News/Government-backs-Free-Trade-Zone-in-the-corregimiento-of-Pacora-that-will-generate-6-thousand-jobs |access-date=2023-12-28 |website=www.presidencia.gob.pa}}</ref>
