'''Caracolí''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]], It has an extension of 260&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It has unique woodlands, with many species of animals and plants.<ref>{{Cite book |last=Posada Carbó |first=Eduardo |title=The Colombian Caribbean: a regional history, 1870-1950 |date=1996 |publisher=Clarendon Press |isbn=978-0-19-820628-6 |series=Oxford historical monographs |location=Oxford [England; New York]}}</ref>

==History==
