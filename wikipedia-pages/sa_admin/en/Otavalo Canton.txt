'''Otavalo Canton''' is a [[cantons of Ecuador|canton]] of [[Ecuador]], located in [[Imbabura Province]].  Its capital is the city of [[Otavalo (city)|Otavalo]].  The Canton's population in the 2010 census was 104,874 compared to 90,188 in the 2001 census.<ref>"Resultados del Censo, 2010", http://www.ecuadorencifras.gob.ec/resultados/, accessed 12 May 2017</ref> Its area is {{convert|530.5|sqkm|sqmi}}.  [[Indigenous people of Ecuador|Indigenous people]], especially the [[Otavalo people]], make up 57 percent of the total population.
