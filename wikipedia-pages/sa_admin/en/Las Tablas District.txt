'''Las Tablas District''' is a [[Districts of Panama|district]] (''distrito'') of [[Los Santos Province]] in [[Panama]]. The population according to the [[2000 Panamanian census|2000 census]] was 24,298.<ref name="Statoids">{{cite web|url=http://www.statoids.com/ypa.html|title=Districts of Panama|publisher=Statoids|access-date=April 12, 2009}}</ref> The district covers a total area of {{convert|698|km²}}. The capital lies at the city of [[Las Tablas, Los Santos|Las Tablas]].<ref name="Statoids"/>
