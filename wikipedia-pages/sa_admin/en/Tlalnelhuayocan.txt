'''Tlalnelhuayocan''' is a [[Municipalities of Veracruz|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Veracruz]], located 2 kilometres from the state capital [[Xalapa]]. It stands on the [[Mexico City]]&ndash;[[Veracruz, Veracruz|Veracruz]] railway and on [[Mexican Federal Highway|Federal Highways 180 and 125]]. Its name was San Andrés Tlalnelhuayocan, who is the first name was used to the [[Nahuatl]] and the people of Jilotepec. Tlalnelhuayocan is formed by various localities, being, San Andres, Otilpan, Palenquillo, San Antonio, Xocotla and Xolostla the most known of them.

== Vegetation ==
There are reported more than 123 species, of which 95 are [[epiphytes]]. These numbers represents the 24% of the epiphyitic flora of central Veracruz.<ref>{{Cite book|title = Comparación florística de epífitas vasculares entre un bosque mesófilo de montaña y un acahual en el municipio de Tlalnelhuayocan, Ver.|last = Gómez Díaz|first = Jorge Antonio|publisher = Universidad Veracruzana|year = 2010|location = Xalapa, Veracruz}}</ref>
