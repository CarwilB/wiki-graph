'''Marcos Castellanos''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]].<ref>{{cite web |title=Marcos Castellanos: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/marcos-castellanos |website=Data México |publisher=Government of Mexico |access-date=14 November 2024 |language=en}}</ref> It is located near the southern shore of [[Lake Chapala]]. The municipal seat is the city of [[San José de Gracia, Michoacán|San José de Gracia]].{{Citation needed|date=November 2024}}
