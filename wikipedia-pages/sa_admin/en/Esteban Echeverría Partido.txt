'''Esteban Echeverría Partido''' is a [[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]] in the [[Gran Buenos Aires]] urban area, in [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of 300,959 inhabitants <ref name=Cen2010Updated>{{Cite web |url=http://www.estadistica.ec.gba.gov.ar/dpe/images/Revista%20Poblacio%CC%81n%20III.pdf |title=Estudios de POBLACIÓN de la provincia de Buenos Aires |access-date=2020-05-13 |archive-date=2025-11-01 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251101093645/http://www.estadistica.ec.gba.gov.ar/dpe/images/Revista%20Poblacio%CC%81n%20III.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> in an area of {{convert|120|km2|sqmi|0|abbr=on}}, and its capital city is [[Monte Grande]], which is {{convert|29|km|mi|0|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].

The partido is named after the poet and [[novelist]] [[Esteban Echeverría]].
