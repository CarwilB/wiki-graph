'''Buriticá''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]].<ref>[http://planeacion.gobant.gov.co/antioquia/mapas_veredales/buritica.jpg Mapa de la Gobernación de Antioquia del municipio de Buriticá, sus corregimientos y veredas]{{dead link|date=January 2017|bot=medic}}{{cbignore|bot=medic}}</ref>

== Demography ==
An 1852 watercolor by [[Henry Price (painter)|Henry Price]] depicts an Indian man and woman from Buriticá wearing blue and white clothing. It bears the notation, "Colonel Codazzi believes that this (pure) ethnic group is almost extinct."<ref name=indiancouple>{{cite web|last=Price|first=henry|author-link=Henry Price (painter)|title=Buriticá Indian Male and Female, Province of Antioquia|url=http://www.wdl.org/en/item/8998/#contributors=Colombia.+Comisi%C3%B3n+Corogr%C3%A1fica|work=[[World Digital Library]]|access-date=7 May 2014}}</ref>

According to the 2005 census data from the [[National Administrative Department of Statistics (Colombia)|National Administrative Department of Statistics]], the town's ethnic composition is:

* [[Mestizo]] & [[White people|White]] (98.4%)
* [[Afro-Colombian]] (1.6%) <ref>[http://www.dane.gov.co/files/investigaciones/poblacion/proyepobla06_20/ProyeccionMunicipios2005_2009.xls Proyecciones de Población Municipales por área]</ref>
