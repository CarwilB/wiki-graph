'''Valparaíso''' ({{IPA|es|balpaɾaˈiso}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Caquetá Department]], [[Colombia]].

==Climate==
Valparaíso has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
