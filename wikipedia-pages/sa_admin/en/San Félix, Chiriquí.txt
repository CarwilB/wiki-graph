'''San Félix''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[San Félix District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|55.7|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 2,972 as of 2010, giving it a population density of {{convert|53.4|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,680; its population as of 2000 was 2,443.<ref name=inec/>
