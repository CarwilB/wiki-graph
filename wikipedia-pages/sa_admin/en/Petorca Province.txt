'''Petorca Province''' ({{langx|es|Provincia de Petorca}}) is one of eight [[Provinces of Chile|province]]s of the central [[Chile]]an [[Regions of Chile|region]] of [[Valparaíso Region|Valparaíso]] (V). Its capital is the city of [[La Ligua]].

==Administration==
As a province, Petorca is a second-level [[administrative divisions of Chile|administrative division]], governed by a provincial delegate who is appointed by the [[President of Chile|president]].

===Communes===
The province comprises five [[Communes of Chile|communes]] ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''comunas''), each governed by a municipality consisting of an [[alcalde]] and [[municipal council]]:
*[[La Ligua]]
*[[Cabildo, Chile|Cabildo]]
*[[Zapallar]]
*[[Papudo]]
*[[Petorca]]

==Geography and demography==
The province spans an area of {{convert|4588.9|sqkm|0|abbr=on}}, the largest in the Valparaíso Region. According to the 2002 census, Petorca is the second least populous province in the region under Isla de Pascua (Easter Island) with a population of 70,610. At that time, there were 50,289 people living in [[urban area]]s, 20,321 people living in [[rural area]]s, 35,647 men and 34,963 women.<ref name="INE"/>
