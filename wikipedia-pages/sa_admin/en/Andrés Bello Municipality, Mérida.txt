The '''Andrés Bello Municipality''' is one of the 23 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Mérida (state)|Mérida]] and, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 14,029.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion6.xls{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The town of [[La Azulita]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Andrés Bello Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Merida.zip{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is one of a number in Venezuela named "[[Andrés Bello Municipality (disambiguation)|Andrés Bello Municipality]]", in honour of the writer [[Andrés Bello]].

==Demographics==
The Andrés Bello Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 14,029 (up from 11,962 in 2000).  This amounts to 1.7% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|35.2|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/merida/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls{{dead link|date=January 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Andrés Bello Municipality is Maria Elena Villasmil, elected on November 21, 2021, with 55% of the vote.  The municipality is divided into one [[Parish]] (Capital Andrés Bello).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
