'''Alianza''' ({{IPA|es|aˈljansa}}) is a [[Municipalities of Honduras|municipality]] in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle (department)|Valle]] in Honduras. It covers an area of {{cvt|205.7|km2}} and had a population of 7,491 inhabitants according to the 2013 census.

== History ==
Alianza was earlier inhabited by the [[Chʼortiʼ people]]. It was known as "Hacienda de Mongoya", and was part of the municipality of [[Goascorán]]. It was officially elevated as a municipality under the name Alianza in 1847.<ref name="Edu">{{cite web|title=Alianza, Valle|url=https://edu-honduras.info/Los-Departamentos/17-Valle/Alianza.html|work=Edu-Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026|lang=es}}</ref><ref name="Red">{{cite web|title=Municipality of Alianza|url=https://redhonduras.hn/en/geography/municipality-alianza/|work=Red Honduras|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

== Geography ==
Alianza is located in the [[Departments of Honduras|department]] of [[Valle Department|Valle]] in Honduras.<ref name="Red"/><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/|title=Administrative divisions of Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://statoids.com/yhn.html|title=Municipalities of Honduras|work=Statoids|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref> It borders the municipalities of [[Goascorán]] to the north, and [[Nacaome]] to the east. It borders the [[Gulf of Fonseca]] to the south and shares international border with [[El Salvador]] to the west.<ref name="Edu"/><ref name="Red"/> The municipality covers an area of {{cvt|205.7|km2}}.<ref name="CP">{{cite web|url=https://www.citypopulation.de/en/honduras/admin/valle/1702__alianza/|title=Alianza, Honduras|work=Citypopulation.de|access-date=31 May 2026}}</ref>

Located at an elevation of approximately {{cvt|118.94|m}} above sea level, Alianza has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: Am). The municipality has an average annual temperature of {{cvt|28.64|C}} and typically receives about {{cvt|186.53|mm}} of annual precipitation.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://weatherandclimate.com/honduras/valle/alianza|title=Alianza, Climate|work=Weather and climate|access-date=1 April 2026}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
According to the 2013 census, Alianza had a total population of 7,491 inhabitants, of whom 3,590 (47.9%) were men and 3,901 (52.1%) were women. The entire population was classified as rural.<ref name="CP"/><ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}

By broad age group, 2,400 individuals (32.0%) were aged 0–14 years, 4,306 individuals (57.5%) were aged 15–64, and 785 individuals (10.5%) were aged 65 years and over. The median age was 23.5 years and the mean age was 30.0 years. Among the population aged 15 and over, the municipality recorded an illiteracy rate of 22.4%, higher than the departmental average of 17.7%. The municipality had 1,897 occupied private dwellings, with an average of 3.9 persons per occupied dwelling.<ref name="INE"/>{{rp|page=19, 20}}
