'''Salcedo''' is the capital city of the [[Hermanas Mirabal Province]] in the [[Dominican Republic]]. It is the birthplace of the Dominican heroines, the [[Mirabal sisters]], who died in the struggle against the [[dictator]] [[Rafael Trujillo]].  A museum in the town commemorates three of the sisters; it was tended to by the remaining sister, Bélgica (Dedé) Mirabal, until her death on February 1, 2014.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.huffingtonpost.com/franklin-garcia/last-surviving-mirabal-si_b_4713662.html|title=Last Surviving Mirabal Sister, Doña Dede, Dead at 88|last=Garcia|first=Franklin|date=2014-02-03|website=Huffington Post|language=en-US|access-date=2018-08-01}}</ref>

The city is named after [[Francisco Antonio Salcedo]] who fought in the northwestern part of the country against the [[Haiti]]an [[army]] during the Dominican-Haitian War after the Dominican [[independence]] from [[Haiti]] in 1844.

== Geography ==
Salcedo is located in the [[Cibao Valley]], south of the ''Cordillera Septentrional'' (in [[English language|English]], "Northern mountain range"). It has a total area of 432.95&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref name="listado" /> It has only one municipal district (a subdivision of a municipality): [[Jamao Afuera]].<ref name="division">{{cite web|url=http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?module=uploads&func=download&fileId=1098|format=PDF|title=División Territorial 2008|author=Oficina Nacional de Estadística|language=es|access-date=2009-10-01|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100414191548/http://www.one.gob.do/index.php?module=uploads&func=download&fileId=1098|archive-date=2010-04-14}}</ref>

== History ==
In the place where is now the city of Salcedo there was a very small town with the name of ''Juana Núñez''. It was made a ''Puesto cantonal'' (an old category that now is called [[Government of the Dominican Republic#Provincial and municipal government|Municipal District]]) in 1880 as part of the old [[La Vega Province|La Vega]] province.<ref name="pouerie">{{cite book
  | last =Pouerié Cordero
  | first =M.M
  | title =Síntesis de ciudades, pueblos e islas del país
  | publisher =Impresora Mary
  | year =1997
  | location =Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic
  | language =es }}</ref>

With the creation of the [[Espaillat Province|Espaillat]] province in 1885, Juana Núñez was made part of this new province. In 1891, its name was changed from Juana Núñez to the present one, Salcedo.

Salcedo was made a municipality in 1905 and, when the Salcedo Province (now Hermanas Mirabal Province) was created in 1952, the city became the head municipality of the new province.

==Economy==
[[Farming]] is the only economic activity in the municipality, except for some very small [[Industry (economics)|industries]]; the main products are [[Plantain (cooking)|plantain]], [[Manioc|cassava]] and [[Cacao tree|cacao]].
==Sports==
As of the [[2025 Copa Dominicana de Fútbol]] season, Salcedo's [[Estadio Domingo Polonia]] (Also known as Complejo Deportivo Salcedo) has been the home of the city's first ever [[Professional sports team]] in the [[Hermanas Mirabal Province]].<ref>{{cite web |title=La LDF arranca este viernes en Santiago |url=https://www.ligadominicanadefutbol.com/2025/08/06/la-ldf-arranca-este-viernes-en-santiago/ |website=Liga Dominicana de fútbol |publisher=Prensa LDF |access-date=3 June 2026}}</ref> In the club's first season in the [[Liga Dominicana de Fútbol|Liga Dominicana de futbol]], they displayed a huge upset to the 4-in-a-row 5-time Champion [[Cibao FC]] in the Finals.<ref>{{cite web |title=*Salcedo fc hace historia, Campeón LDF 2025-2026* |url=https://www.ligadominicanadefutbol.com/2026/05/25/salcedo-fc-hace-historia-campeon-ldf-2025-2026/ |website=Liga Dominicana de fútbol |publisher=Prensa LDF |access-date=3 June 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Salcedo fc y Cibao fc listos para la final de la LDF 2025-2026 |url=https://www.ligadominicanadefutbol.com/2026/05/13/salcedo-fc-y-cibao-fc-listos-para-la-final-de-la-ldf-2025-2026/ |website=Liga Dominicana de fútbol |publisher=Prensa LDF |access-date=3 June 2026}}</ref>

==Interesting facts==
The city became widely known outside of the Dominican Republic due to the YouTube video with an unusual request from the listener of a local radio station to play a song with the words "Esas son Reebok o son Nike" (meaning "Are they Reebok or Nike?"). The song in reference was actually Corona's "[[The Rhythm of the Night]]".
