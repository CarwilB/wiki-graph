The '''Francisco Linares Alcántara Municipality''' is one of the 18 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Aragua (state)|Aragua]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 123,122.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Santa Rita, Aragua|Santa Rita]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Francisco Linares Alcántara Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/secciones/division/Aragua.zip {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Demographics==
The Francisco Linares Alcántara Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 135,873 (up from 118,146 in 2000).  This amounts to 8.2% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|5661.38|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ocei.gov.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/aragua/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=October 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Francisco Linares Alcántara Municipality is Raiza Márquez, elected on November 23, 2008, with 56% of the vote.<ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/divulgacion_regionales_2008/index.php?e=04&m=00&p=00&c=00&t=00&ca=00&v=02 | title=Divulgación Elecciones Regionales - 23 de Noviembre de 2008 }}</ref> She replaced Hugo R. Peña A. shortly after the elections. The municipality is divided into three [[parish]]es; Capital Francisco Linares Alcántara, Francisco de Miranda (separated from Capital Francisco Linares Alcantara parish effective January 30, 1995), and Monseñor Feliciano González (separated from Francisco de Miranda parish effective December 16, 1997).<ref name=autogenerated1 />
