'''Anse-d'Hainault''' ({{IPA|fr|ɑ̃s dɛno}}; also '''Anse-d'Ainault'''; {{langx|ht|Ansdeno}}) is a [[List of communes of Haiti|commune]] in the [[Anse-d'Hainault Arrondissement]], in the [[Grand'Anse (department)|Grand'Anse]] [[Departments of Haiti|department]] of [[Haiti]].

Towns and villages in Anse-d'Hainault include: [[Anse-d'Hainault, Haiti|Anse-d'Hainault]], [[Decotelette]], [[Dossous]], [[Duchanino]], [[Escamel]] and [[Peligrin]].
