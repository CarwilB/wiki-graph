'''Baco ''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Barú District, Chiriquí|Barú District]], [[Chiriquí Province]], [[Panama]]. It has a land area of {{convert|75.5|sqkm|sqmi}} and had a population of 8,008 as of 2023, giving it a population density of {{convert|106.1|PD/sqkm|PD/sqmi}}.<ref name=inec/> It was created by Law 10 of March 7, 1997; this measure was complemented by Law 5 of January 19, 1998 and Law 69 of October 28, 1998.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 2000 was 7,101.<ref name=inec/>
