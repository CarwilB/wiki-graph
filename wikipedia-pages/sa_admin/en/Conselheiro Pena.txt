'''Conselheiro Pena''', formerly known as "Lajao", is located in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]], [[Brazil]]. This town of 20,000+ inhabitants, was formed alongside the "[[Rio Doce]]", or Sweet River.
