'''Fronteras''' is a [[Municipalities of Sonora|municipality]] in the [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Sonora]] in north-western [[Mexico]].<ref>{{cite web|title=- |url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120731104257/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/sonora/ |url-status=dead |archive-date=July 31, 2012 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México |publisher=[[Instituto Nacional para el Federalismo y el Desarrollo Municipal]] |access-date=January 4, 2010 }}</ref><!-- The municipality covers an area of  km².--->
In the 2020 Census, the municipality reported a total population of 9,041.<ref>{{cite web |title=Fronteras: Economy, employment, equity, quality of life, education, health and public safety |website=Data México |url=https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/en/profile/geo/fronteras |publisher=[[Secretaría de Economía]] |access-date=2 January 2025}}</ref>

The municipal seat stands at [[Fronteras]].
