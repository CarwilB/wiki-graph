==Climate==
Jauro has a [[tropical savanna climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification]]: ''Aw'').

==History==
During the time of the former Captaincy of [[Mato Grosso]], there was intense activity on the Jauru River because it was one of the rivers that served as a transportation route to the former Mato Grosso capital of Vila Bela da Santíssima Trindade.<ref>Disponible en: [https://web.archive.org/web/20071023035250/http://www.coisasdematogrosso.com.br/site/cidades/cidade.asp?cod=118]. Consultado el 13 de junio de 2009.</ref>
This river was one of the concrete and best-defined boundaries between the Viceroyalty of the Río de la Plata (Spanish) and Portuguese Brazil. A significant [[masonry]] boundary marker was erected for this purpose, the location of which is now entirely within Brazilian territory.<ref>{{cite web|access-date=22 September 2008 |archive-date=14 June 2009 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090614070515/http://www.exercito.gov.br/03ativid/operacoes/jauru/indice.htm |author=Exército Brasileito |title=Histórico-Marco do Jauru |url=http://www.exercito.gov.br/03ativid/operacoes/jauru/indice.htm}}<!-- auto-translated from Spanish by Module:CS1 translator --></ref> After 1811, and at least until the end of Gaspar Rodríguez de Francia's administration, the former boundary of the viceroyalty was maintained by the [[Republic of Paraguay]], but the Brazilian state contested the previous agreements. In any case, Paraguayan claims were maintained until 1870.
