'''Punta Indio Partido''' is a ''[[Partidos of Buenos Aires|partido]]'' on the Atlantic coast of [[Buenos Aires Province]] in [[Argentina]].

It was created in 1994, as an excision from the old [[Magdalena Partido]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 9,000 inhabitants<ref>{{Cite web |title=Wayback Machine |url=http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/07000LC121.xls |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070928010656/http://www.indec.gov.ar/censo2001s2_2/Datos/07000LC121.xls |archive-date=2007-09-28 |access-date=2026-03-31 |website=www.indec.gov.ar}}</ref> in an area of {{convert|1,610|km²}}, and its capital city is [[Verónica, Buenos Aires|Verónica]], which is around {{convert|140|km|abbr=on}} from [[Buenos Aires]].
