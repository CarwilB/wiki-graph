'''Briceño''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. Part of the subregion of [[Northern Antioquia]], it lies at an altitude of 1,200 m (3,900 ft) above sea level.

==Climate==
Briceño has a relatively cool [[tropical rainforest climate]] (Af) due to altitude. It has very heavy rainfall year round.
