'''Soracá''' is a town and municipality in the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|Department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]], part of the subregion of the [[Central Boyacá Province]]. Soracá borders the municipalities of [[Chivatá]] in the north, [[Siachoque]], [[Viracacha]] and [[Ramiriquí]] in the east, [[Boyacá, Boyacá|Boyacá]] in the south and the department capital [[Tunja]] in the west.<ref name=website>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.soraca-boyaca.gov.co/index.shtml Official website Soracá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
In [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]], Soracá means "ruling mansion of the sovereign".<ref>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Soracá]</ref>

== History ==
The area of Soracá was part of the ''[[zaque|zacazgo]]'' of Hunza, presently known as Tunja in the time before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]]. The [[Muisca people|Muisca]] inhabiting the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] and their [[Muisca Confederation|confederation]] were an advanced civilization who spoke [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]. In the 1530s the Spanish [[conquistador]]es led by [[Gonzalo Jiménez de Quesada]] entered the territories of the Muisca.

== Economy ==
Main economical activities in Soracá are [[dairy]] production, [[mining]] and production of bricks.<ref name=website/>
