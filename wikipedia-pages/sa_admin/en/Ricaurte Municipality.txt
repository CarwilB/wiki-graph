The '''Ricaurte Municipality''' is one of the nine [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Cojedes (state)|Cojedes]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 12,657.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=Venezuela: administrative units, extended |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref> The town of [[Libertad, Cojedes|Libertad]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Ricaurte Municipality.<ref name=autogenerated1>http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Cojedes.zip {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref> The municipality is named for Venezuelan independence hero [[Antonio Ricaurte]].

==Demographics==
The Ricaurte Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 12,340 (up from 10,883 in 2000). This amounts to 4.1% of the state's population.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion5.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|13.64|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>http://www.ine.gob.ve/sintesisestadistica/estados/cojedes/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls {{dead link|date=July 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Ricaurte Municipality is Ramón A Peralta Ruíz, elected on October 31, 2004, with 54% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web | title=Alcalde de Municipio | url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D5%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120217163452/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/8/alcaldedis%3D0%26edo%3D8%26mun%3D5%26par%3D0%26cen%3D0%26mesa%3D0%26cua%3D0.html | archive-date=2012-02-17 | access-date=2008-03-14 | url-status=live }}</ref>   He replaced Violeta Montoya shortly after the elections.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |title=Elecciones 30 De Julio De 2000 Directorio De Alcaldes Electos |accessdate=March 15, 2008 |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=October 2, 2006 }}</ref>  The municipality is divided into two [[parish]]es; Libertad de Cojedes and El Amparo.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
