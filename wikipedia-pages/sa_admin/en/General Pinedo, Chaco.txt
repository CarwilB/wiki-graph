'''General Pinedo''' is a town in the south of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Chaco Province|Chaco]], Argentina. It has about 16,000 inhabitants as per the {{census-ar|2001}}, and is the head town of the 12 de Octubre Department.
