'''Sericita''' is a [[Brazil|Brazilian]] [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[Federative units of Brazil|state]] of [[Minas Gerais]]. The city belongs to the mesoregion of [[Zona da Mata (Minas Gerais)|Zona da Mata]] and to the microregion of Ponte Nova.  As of 2020, the estimated population was 7,333.<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/mg/sericita/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref>
