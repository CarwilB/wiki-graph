'''Rivadavia''' is a central [[Departments of Argentina|department]] of [[Mendoza Province]] in [[Argentina]].

The provincial subdivision has a population of about 52,500 inhabitants in an area of  {{cvt|2,141|km2}}, and its capital city is [[Rivadavia, Mendoza|Rivadavia]], which is located around {{cvt|1,075|km}} from the [[Capital federal]].

==Sport==

The city of Rivadavia is home to [[Club Sportivo Independiente Rivadavia]], a [[football (soccer)|football]] club that play in the regionalised [[Torneo Argentino A|3rd Division]].
