'''El Peñol''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Colombia|municipality]] in [[Antioquia Department]], [[Colombia]]. The population was 16,223 at the 2018 census. It is part of the subregion of [[Eastern Antioquia]]. The town is nationally known for the nearby monolith known also as [[El Peñón de Guatapé|El Peñol]], which is a tourist attraction.

==History==
The town was originally founded along the Negro-Nare River in 1714 by Fray Miguel de Castro y Rivadeneiro. The area was inhabited by the indigenous [[Tahamí people]] prior to the [[Spanish colonization of the Americas|Spanish]] arrival and colonization of the area. <ref>{{cite web |last1=BnB |first1=Isabella |title=Guatape History |url=https://bnbcolombia.com/guatape-history/ |website=bnbcolombia.com |access-date=29 November 2023}}</ref>

In 1971, [[Empresas Públicas de Medellín]] determined that more water and power was needed to support the rapidly growing city of [[Medellín]], located approximately 50mi/30km to the west, and decided that the 
Negro-Nare River should be dammed at Guatape, flooding the Negro-Nare River valley in which El Peñol was located. The old town seized by eminent domain and a new town was built several miles west, disrupting the lives of several thousand residents who were then forced to move to the new town without recourse.<ref name="Portal">{{cite news |last1=Arango Lopez |first1=Cindia |title=A Town amid the Waters: The Building of a Hydroelectric Dam in Eastern Antioquia, Colombia |url=https://sites.utexas.edu/llilas-benson-magazine/2022/09/27/a-town-amid-the-waters-the-building-of-a-hydroelectric-dam-in-eastern-antioquia-colombia/ |work=Portal |publisher=Llilas Benson Latin American Studies at The University of Texas |date=27 September 2022}}</ref>

The new dam was completed and the valley flooded in 1978. The El Peñol-Guatapé Reservoir now sits over the former location of El Peñol. A cross now marks the location in the reservoir above the former [[Parish#Catholic_Church|parish]] of El Viejo Peñol that fronted the town plaza. Some rural parts of Guatape were also flooded and lost.<ref name="Portal"/><ref>{{cite news |last1=Ehrlich |first1=David |title=Cross over the flooded town of El Peñol |url=https://photocontest.smithsonianmag.com/photocontest/detail/cross-over-the-flooded-town-of-el-penol-colombia/ |work=Smithsonian Magazine |date=February 2017}}</ref>

==Climate==
El Peñol has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> consistently warm temperatures and abundant rainfall.
