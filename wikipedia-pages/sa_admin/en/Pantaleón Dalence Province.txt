'''Pantaleón Dalence''' is a [[Provinces of Bolivia|province]] in the eastern parts of the [[Bolivia]]n [[Oruro Department]]. Its seat is [[Huanuni]]. The province was named after the jurist Pantaleón Dalence Jiménez.

==Location==
Pantaleón Dalence province is one of sixteen provinces in the Oruro Department. It is located between 18° 05' and 18° 35' [[Latitude|South]] and between 66° 10' and 66° 45' [[Longitude|West]]. 

The province borders [[Cercado Province, Oruro|Cercado Province]] in the northwest, [[Poopó Province]] in the southwest, [[Potosí Department]] in the east, and [[Cochabamba Department]] in the northeast. 

The province extends over 55&nbsp;km from north to south, and 65&nbsp;km from east to west.

== Geography ==
The province lies at the [[Uru Uru Lake]], one of the largest lakes of Bolivia.

The Asanaki mountain range traverses the province. Some of the highest mountains of the province are listed below:<ref>BIGM map 1:50,000 6239-III Huanuni</ref>

==Population==
The main language of the province is [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], spoken by 93.2%, while 69.8% of the population speak [[Quechua language|Quechua]] and 21.2% [[Aymara language|Aymara]] (1992). 

The population decreased from 24,892 inhabitants (1992 [[census]]) to 23,608 (2001 census), a decrease of 5.2%. - 45% of the population are younger than 15 years old (1992).  

27.7% of the population have no access to [[Electricity generation|electricity]], 87.4% have no [[Tap water|sanitary facilities]] (1992). 

21.7% of the population are employed in [[Primary sector of industry|agriculture]], 38.2% in [[mining]], 5.9% in [[Secondary sector of industry|industry]], 34.2% in [[Tertiary sector of industry|general services]] (2001).

82.3% of the population are [[Roman Catholicism in Bolivia|Catholics]], 14.3% are [[Protestantism in South America|Protestants]] (1992).

==Division==
The province comprises two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]] which are further subdivided into [[Cantons of Bolivia|cantons]].
