'''São Mateus''' is the eighth oldest [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Brazil]] and the seventh most populous in the [[States of Brazil|state]] of [[Espírito Santo]]. Founded on September 21, 1544, it gained municipal autonomy in 1764. Originally named ''Povoado do Cricaré'', it was renamed São Mateus in 1566 by Father [[Joseph of Anchieta]]. According to [[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]], its population is approximately 123,750 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" /> São Mateus is considered a milestone in the colonization of Espírito Santo's territory.<ref name="Sitios_historicos_governo_ES"/>

It has the largest Afro-descendant population in the state, a legacy of the Port of São Mateus, which, until the mid-19th century, was a major entry point for enslaved Africans in Brazil. The municipality also includes descendants of [[Italian Brazilians|Italian immigrants]], who contributed to the colonization of its rural hinterlands.

Its economy is driven by service provision and the exploration and production of [[petroleum]]. Petroleum fields were discovered in the 1970s, with further expansion in the 1980s. In the 2000s, the North Capixaba Terminal was established in the Campo Grande region to handle the region's production output.

Located at a [[latitude]] of 18º42'58" [[South]] and a [[longitude]] of 39º51'21" [[West]], São Mateus sits at an altitude of 36 meters. Its total area is {{convert|2338.727|km2|sqmi}}, representing 5.12% of Espírito Santo's territory. It borders [[Boa Esperança, Espírito Santo|Boa Esperança]], [[Pinheiros, Espírito Santo|Pinheiros]], and [[Conceição da Barra]] to the [[north]]; [[São Gabriel da Palha]], [[Vila Valério]], [[Jaguaré]], and [[Linhares]] to the [[south]]; the [[Atlantic Ocean]] to the [[east]]; and [[Nova Venécia]] to the [[west]]. It is {{convert|215|km|mi}} from the state capital, [[Vitória, Espírito Santo|Vitória]]. São Mateus is also known for its strong tourism appeal, both historical and seasonal. The carnival in Guriri, the municipality's main beach resort, is one of the liveliest in the state and is nationally recognized, attracting many tourists, particularly from [[Minas Gerais]].

== Etymology ==
From its founding in 1544 until the mid-16th century, the small settlement along the banks of the [[São Mateus River]] was known as ''Povoação do Cricaré''. The name São Mateus honors the evangelist [[Matthew the Apostle|Matthew]], as Father [[Joseph of Anchieta]], during one of his pilgrimages through the then [[Captaincy of Espírito Santo]] in 1566, celebrated a mass on September 21, Saint Matthew's Day. Following the custom of naming places and geographical features after the saint of the day, Anchieta renamed the ''Povoação do Cricaré'' to São Mateus.<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa">{{cite web |author=Prefeitura de São Mateus |title=The First Colonizers |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/historia-sao-mateus.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090926184314/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/historia-sao-mateus.php |archive-date=2009-09-26 |access-date=October 27, 2009}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver&id=7&id_departamento=3 |title=Aldeia de São Mateus - 16th and 17th Centuries |access-date=July 7, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140725205248/http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver&id=7&id_departamento=3 |archive-date=2014-07-25 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

== History ==
=== Pre-Cabral period ===
[[File:E11 p9.jpg|thumb|left|upright=1.0|{{center|''Botocudo Family on the Move'', <small>by [[Jean-Baptiste Debret]], 1834</small>}}]]

Before Portuguese colonization, the São Mateus region was inhabited by Aimorés, also known as [[Botocudos]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.hjobrasil.com/ordem.asp?secao=44&categoria=341&subcategoria=0&id=3766 |title=Aimorés Indians |date=August 14, 2009 |access-date=July 7, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20130731191112/http://www.hjobrasil.com/ordem.asp?secao=44&categoria=341&subcategoria=0&id=3766 |archive-date=2013-07-31 |url-status=dead}}</ref> Funerary urns found in the Barra Nova region in the 1960s, along with ceramic pieces unearthed near the Roberto Silvares Hospital in 1998, are attributed to the [[Tupiniquim|Tupi]] ethnic group, to which the Aimorés do not belong, and are dated from the 10th century to the 16th century.<ref name="Grupos indígenas pré-colombianos">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Prehistory in Northern Espírito Santo |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=19 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

Manuscripts from the early colonization period report the presence of [[Human cannibalism|cannibalistic]] Indians in the region. Unlike other Tupi Indians, these did not know how to swim but were skilled at rowing and working with clay.<ref name="Grupos indígenas pré-colombianos"/>

Efforts by [[Jesuits|Jesuit priests]] to catechize the indigenous population were unsuccessful. Afonso Brás, the first missionary in the [[Captaincy of Espírito Santo]], wrote in a 1551 letter that after receiving baptism, the Indians fled, returning to their beliefs and customs.<ref name="Grupos indígenas pré-colombianos"/>

=== Arrival of the first colonizers ===
[[File:Padre Anchieta.jpg|thumb|left|upright=1.0|{{center|Father [[Joseph of Anchieta]]}}]]

There is no precise date or record of the first settlers' names, but oral tradition suggests Portuguese colonizers arrived in São Mateus around 1544. Alarmed by frequent indigenous attacks, settlers from [[Vasco Fernandes Coutinho, captain of Espírito Santo|Vasco Fernandes Coutinho]]'s group reportedly split up, abandoning the [[Captaincy of Espírito Santo]]. Some fled to neighboring captaincies or moved inland, possibly heading north toward the [[São Mateus River]].<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa"/>

The lack of records from the early colonization years leads to speculative hypotheses. One suggests São Mateus may have been settled by shipwreck survivors. Father [[Joseph of Anchieta]]'s accounts note that in 1596, while passing through the São Mateus River, he celebrated mass for shipwrecked individuals, though no documentation confirms this as historical fact.<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa"/><ref name="Colonização Portuguesa - Nardoto">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Portuguese Colonization |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=27 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

More likely, the first colonists came from the neighboring Captaincy of Porto Seguro, led by Pero do Campo Tourinho. The earliest documented Portuguese presence in the region relates to the Battle of Cricaré, which occurred in late January 1558. Another record is a letter describing a Jesuit mission by Father Fernão Cardin, who visited the Village of São Mateus in September 1583.<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa - Nardoto"/>

=== Slave trade ===
[[File:Kenneth Lu - Slave ship model ( (4811223749).jpg|thumb|right|upright=1.0|{{center|Model of the interior of a slave ship}}]]

The entry of [[Slavery in Brazil|enslaved Africans]] into the municipality occurred through the Port, beginning in the [[Colonial Brazil|colonial period]] and intensifying in the 19th century, particularly after the 1850 ban on the transatlantic slave trade until the [[Abolition of slavery in Brazil|abolition of slavery]]. At its peak, 16 companies at the port were exclusively engaged in this trade.<ref name="Maria_do_carmo">{{cite web |author=Maria do Carmo de Oliveira Russo |title=Slavery in the maintenance of agrarian structures and the socio-economic context of São Mateus/ES (1850 - 1888) |url=http://www.ichs.ufop.br/cadernosdehistoria/ojs/index.php/cadernosdehistoria/article/view/70 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150206223257/http://www.ichs.ufop.br/cadernosdehistoria/ojs/index.php/cadernosdehistoria/article/view/70 |archive-date=2015-02-06 |access-date=January 26, 2015 |work=Cadernos de História}}</ref>

The arrival of slave ships at the Port was eagerly anticipated by the population, especially buyers, hoping to select the best ones. Onboard, enslaved Africans were prepared: men and women were oiled, wounds and tumors covered with rust and gunpowder, and in cases of intestinal infections, their anuses were stuffed with tow. Once disembarked, chained in single file, they were driven to the market, where their physical condition and tribal origins were examined. Preference was given to those with slender shins, heels set back, and small buttocks, deemed ideal for fieldwork.<ref name="Comercio_negreiro_porto_Nardoto">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Slave Trade in São Mateus |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=55 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

Notably, São Mateus recorded the seizure of the last clandestine [[slave ship]] on the Brazilian coast in 1856, following the 1850 law banning the African slave trade.<ref name="Maria_do_carmo"/> Some ships carried over 300 captives, who arrived naked, malnourished, and chained, enduring voyages lasting over 90 days. Many succumbed to the harsh conditions and were thrown overboard.<ref name="Comercio_negreiro_porto_Nardoto"/>

Between 1863 and 1887, 606 enslaved Africans were traded in the city, including 326 men and 269 women. Prices varied based on factors like sex, age, occupation, and physical condition, and were influenced by events such as the 1850 transatlantic trade ban.<ref name="Maria_do_carmo_Conferência">{{cite web |url=http://cihe.fflch.usp.br/sites/cihe.fflch.usp.br/files/Maria%20do%20Carmo%20de%20Oliveira%20Russo_1.pdf |title=The slave as merchandise: purchase, sale, and other commercial activities involving captives in São Mateus/ES in the second half of the 19th century |author=Maria do Carmo de Oliveira Russo |work=IV International Conference on Economic History |access-date=January 26, 2015}}</ref> According to municipal registry records, slave prices rose significantly from 1868, reaching 1,028,500 [[Brazilian real (old)|réis]] for men and 1,018,750 réis for women, a sharp increase from the previous year's averages of 375,000 réis for men and 637,916 réis for women.<ref name="Maria_do_carmo_Conferência"/>

=== Italian immigration ===
[[File:Gravura do Barão de Aymorés - 1860.JPG|thumb|right|upright=1.0|{{center|Baron of Aimorés}}]]

The state of [[Espírito Santo]] was the first to receive a significant wave of [[Italian immigration to Brazil|Italian immigrants]] around 1874, driven by major political changes in [[Europe]] during the early 19th century.<ref name="Portal_Itália">{{cite web |url=http://www.portalitalia.com.br/historia/es/comunidade_mapa_esvejamais.asp |title=Italian immigration in the state of Espírito Santo |work=Portal Itália |access-date=January 13, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121194049/http://www.portalitalia.com.br/historia/es/comunidade_mapa_esvejamais.asp |archive-date=2015-01-21 |url-status=dead}}</ref> These changes caused internal conflicts in the [[Kingdom of Italy]], severely impacting its northern regions' populations.<ref name="Nardoto_Crise_Itália">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=Italian Immigration in São Mateus |chapter=Italy - A country facing a severe economic and social collapse |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=Editora Tribuna do Cricaré |year=2009 |page=15 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

Simultaneously, the [[Lei Áurea|1888 abolition of slavery]] led to a shortage of skilled labor for coffee plantations.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.saocarlosoficial.com.br/_fonte/canais.asp?c=181 |title=Abolition and European Immigration |publisher=São Carlos Oficial |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref>

In São Mateus, the first group of Italians arrived at the Port in 1887, at the request of the Baron of Aimorés, who, anticipating the abolition of slavery, requested immigrants from the Kingdom of Italy's consulate in Brazil to work on his farm along the Cravo Waterfall on the [[São Mateus River]].<ref name="Nardoto_Barão">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=Italian Immigration in São Mateus |chapter=The Baron of Aymorés and Immigration |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=Editora Tribuna do Cricaré |year=2009 |page=25 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

Successive arrivals of Italian immigrants continued at the old port until the end of the 19th century. Upon arrival, they were treated similarly to enslaved Africans, displayed in public squares for landowners to select. They were forced to show their hands for inspection; those with rough, calloused hands were chosen for farm work, while those with soft hands were labeled lazy and left to fend for themselves at the port.<ref name="Nardoto_Epopéia">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=Italian Immigration in São Mateus |chapter=The Epic of the Colonists on Land |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=Editora Tribuna do Cricaré |year=2009 |page=18 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

=== Elevation to village status ===
With the discovery of gold in [[Minas Gerais]] in the second half of the 17th century, the Portuguese government, fearing loss of control, took measures to prevent adventurers from accessing the mines.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://educaterra.terra.com.br/voltaire/500br/br_ouro.htm |title=The Gold Century |author= |access-date=July 7, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110812232032/http://educaterra.terra.com.br/voltaire/500br/br_ouro.htm |archive-date=2011-08-12 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 1764, Thomé Couceiro de Abreu, the ombudsman of the Captaincy of Porto Seguro, following orders from the [[List of monarchs of Portugal|Portuguese Crown]], extended beyond his captaincy's territorial limits to elevate the settlement to village status. This was deemed necessary to prevent intrusions via the São Mateus River into the newly discovered gold mines.<ref name="A Criação da Vila de São Mateus">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Creation of the Village of São Mateus |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=35 |volume=single |id=}}</ref> The chosen name, Villa Nova do Rio de São Mateus, was the same given by [[Joseph of Anchieta|Anchieta]].<ref>{{cite web |author=Apontador |title=São Mateus - ES |url=http://www.apontador.com.br/local/es/sao_mateus.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100210160306/http://www.apontador.com.br/local/es/sao_mateus.html |archive-date=2010-02-10 |access-date=July 8, 2010}}</ref> This occurred on September 27, 1764.<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa"/><ref name="Colonização Portuguesa - Nardoto"/>

At the time of its elevation to village status, the settlement of São Mateus, located on a high plateau, had two streets flanking the Main Church. Four alleys extended from these streets to Córrego da Bica, then called Córrego do Mato. The two main streets had a few masonry houses where wealthier landowners lived, while less affluent residents, slaves, servants, and dependents lived in mud houses along the alleys.<ref name="A Criação da Vila de São Mateus"/>

During this period, the [[Captaincy of Espírito Santo]] was directly administered by the [[List of monarchs of Portugal|Portuguese Crown]], as its [[Donatary captain|donataries]] struggled with the challenging task. Consequently, the village was subordinated to the Captaincy of Porto Seguro until January 1823.<ref name="A Criação da Vila de São Mateus" /> Measures included street measurements, the construction of a town hall and jail, and the installation of a [[whipping post]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver&id=7&id_departamento=3 |title=The Creation of the Village of São Mateus - 18th Century |access-date=July 7, 2010 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140725205248/http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver&id=7&id_departamento=3 |archive-date=2014-07-25 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

=== Creation of the municipality ===
On April 3, 1848, by decree of the president of the then [[Espírito Santo|Province of Espírito Santo]], Dr. Luiz Pedreira de Couto Ferraz, Villa Nova do Rio São Mateus was elevated to city status, retaining the name São Mateus given by the first colonizers.<ref name="Política de São Mateus">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Creation of the Municipality of São Mateus |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=48 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

The people of São Mateus learned of this news after April 13, 1848, when correspondence was sent to the municipal council. To celebrate, a grand festival was held on April 21, 22, and 23 of the same year. Upon becoming a municipality, São Mateus' territory spanned 13,588&nbsp;km², equivalent to 29.8% of Espírito Santo's territory. The ''[[comarca]]'' was established on March 23, 1853.<ref name="Política de São Mateus"/>

The first district created in the municipality was Serra dos Aimorés in 1886, later renamed [[Nova Venécia]].<ref name="História IBGE"/> In 1891, the first territorial division created the municipality of [[Conceição da Barra]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/painel/historico.php?codmun=320160 |title=History/Conceição da Barra - ES |work=IBGE Cities |publisher=IBGE |access-date=December 18, 2015}}</ref> In 1935, the district of [[Barra de São Francisco]] was created, becoming a municipality through Decree-Law 15,177 on December 31, 1943. In 1949, the districts of Barra Nova, Boa Esperança, Nestor Gomes, and Nova Verona were established, with Nova Venécia gaining autonomy via State Law No. 767 on December 11, 1953. [[Boa Esperança, Espírito Santo|Boa Esperança]] became a municipality through State Law No. 1912 on December 28, 1963. In 1964, the districts of [[Barra Seca]], Itauninhas, and [[Jaguaré]] were created, with the latter becoming a municipality on December 13, 1981, via Law No. 3445.<ref name="Política de São Mateus"/><ref name="História IBGE">{{cite web |url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/painel/painel.php?codmun=320490# |title=History/São Mateus - ES |work=IBGE Cities |publisher=IBGE |access-date=December 18, 2015}}</ref>

== Geography ==
According to the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, the municipality's area is {{convert|2338.727|km2|sqmi}}, making it the second largest municipality by area in [[Espírito Santo]]. Of this, {{convert|2332.3007|km2|sqmi}} is [[rural area]] and {{convert|155.49|km2|sqmi}} is [[urban area]].<ref name="Embrapa_Área_Urbana"/> Located in the northeast of Brazil's [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]], in the North Coast Mesoregion of Espírito Santo (São Mateus Microregion),<ref name="IBGE_DTB_2013" /> it is {{convert|64|km|mi}} from the border with [[Bahia]], at a [[latitude]] of 18º43’15” South and a [[longitude]] of 39º51’46” West.<ref name="Embrapa_Área_Urbana"/> Its neighboring municipalities are [[Conceição da Barra]] to the north, [[Pinheiros, Espírito Santo|Pinheiros]] and [[Boa Esperança, Espírito Santo|Boa Esperança]] to the northwest, [[Nova Venécia]] to the west, [[São Gabriel da Palha]] and [[Vila Valério]] to the southwest, [[Jaguaré]] and [[Linhares]] to the south, with the [[Atlantic Ocean]] to the east.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/uf.php?lang=&coduf=32&search=espirito-santo |title=Espírito Santo |author=Cidades@ - IBGE |access-date=January 10, 2015}}</ref>

=== Topography ===
The topography of São Mateus is predominantly flat, with the municipal seat at {{convert|37.7|m|ft}} above sea level.<ref name="Aspectos_Geográficos_SM_ES">{{cite web |url=http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=6 |title=Introduction - Geographical Aspects |author=São Mateus ES |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-date=January 21, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193735/http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=6 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The central region consists of [[Tertiary period|Tertiary]] plateaus with a gentle slope toward the coast, ranging from 30 to 100 meters in height. The western part features [[Granite|granitic]] formations up to 350 meters, while the coastal area is flat with flood-prone regions and dunes, not exceeding 4 meters in altitude.<ref>{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Topography |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=152 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

Three distinct soil types are found in the municipality:<ref name="Atlas_ES">{{cite web |url=http://www.ijsn.es.gov.br/Sitio/images/files/Atlas_Visualizacao8_8.pdf |title=Historical and Geographical Atlas of Espírito Santo |author=Instituto Jones do Santos Neves |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193604/http://www.ijsn.es.gov.br/Sitio/images/files/Atlas_Visualizacao8_8.pdf |archive-date=2015-01-21 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

[[File:Vista do Rio São Mateus a partir da Praça do Mirante 03.JPG|thumb|upright=1.0|{{center|View of the [[São Mateus River]] valley from Mirante Square}}]]
[[File:Cachoeira da Jararaca.jpg|thumb|upright=1.0|{{center|Jararaca Waterfall on the São Mateus River}}]]
* [[Precambrian|Precambrian Zone]]: Predominantly [[gneiss]]ic, part of the Brazilian Crystalline Shield, extending from the Nestor Gomes district to the border with [[Nova Venécia]]. This soil type is also found in the bed of the [[São Mateus River]].<ref name="Nardoto_Solo">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Soil |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=152 |volume=single |id=}}</ref><ref name="inventario">{{cite web |last1=FONSECA |first1=Maria Angélica |last2=MOREIRA |first2=João Vicente Pedrosa |last3=SIMÕES |first3=Eduardo Rodrigo Donatelli |last4=VICENTE |first4=Célia Regina Bigossi |year=2005 |title=Tourism Offer Inventory of the Municipality of São Mateus |url=http://vix.sebraees.com.br/arquivos/biblioteca/invent_s%C3%A3o_mateus.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193913/http://vix.sebraees.com.br/arquivos/biblioteca/invent_s%C3%A3o_mateus.pdf |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 14, 2015 |publisher=Sebrae}}</ref>
* [[Tertiary period|Tertiary Zone]]: The predominant soil, characterized as dystrophic and [[podzol]]ic red-yellow [[latosol]], with medium to low fertility and a [[pH]] around {{formatnum:5.0}}.<ref name="Aspectos_Geográficos_SM_ES"/> Composed of sedimentary plateaus with [[argillite]]s and [[sandstone]], these plateaus, ranging from {{convert|30|m|ft}} to {{convert|100|m|ft}}, start in the Pedra d'Água neighborhood and extend to the [[Precambrian|Precambrian Zone]] in Nestor Gomes.<ref name="Nardoto_Solo"/>
* [[Quaternary|Quaternary Sediment Zone]]: Found along valleys, riverways, and coastal plains, forming the sandy strip from Pedra D'Água to the [[Atlantic Ocean]]. It includes the Barra Nova district with [[sandstone]] [[reef]] formations, as well as the [[mangrove]]s of Nativo and Campo Grande, and the [[swamp]]s of Suruaca.<ref name="Atlas_ES"/><ref name="Nardoto_Solo"/>

=== Hydrography ===
The municipality contains three hydrographic basins. The [[Doce River Basin]] covers a small area in the Suruaca Valley region. The [[Itaúnas River|Itaúnas River Basin]] encompasses a small portion of the Itauninhas district, while the [[São Mateus River|São Mateus River Basin]] is the most extensive, draining over 90% of the municipality's area.<ref name="Atlas_ES"/><ref name="inventario"/><ref name="Nardoto_Bacias">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Hydrographic Basin |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=152 |volume=single |id=}}</ref>

The São Mateus River Basin, also known locally as the Cricaré River, spans approximately {{convert|103351|km2|sqmi}}. It drains ten municipalities across [[Minas Gerais]] and Espírito Santo, emptying into the [[Atlantic Ocean]] in [[Conceição da Barra]]. Its main tributaries include the Cotaxé River, also known as the ''North Branch of the São Mateus River'', Preto, Mingal da Vovó River, Panela Velha River, and Pirapococa River. Notably, this river has a rare [[distributary]], the [[Mariricu River]].<ref name="Nardoto_Bacias"/><ref name="Hidrografia_Prefeitura">{{cite web |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/hidrografia-sao-mateus.php |title=Hydrography of São Mateus |author=Prefeitura de São Mateus |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-date=January 1, 2012 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120101202616/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/hidrografia-sao-mateus.php |url-status=dead }}</ref>

The city also has {{convert|43|km|mi}} of coastline,<ref name="Hidrografia_Prefeitura"/> featuring beaches such as Abricó, Aldeia do Coco, Barra Nova, Bosque, Brejo Velho, Caramujo, Gameleira, Guriri, Campo Grande, Oitizeiro, Ranchinho, and Urussuquara, with Guriri being the most famous.<ref name="Arena_Ao_Mar">{{cite web |url=http://www.arenaaomar.com.br/ilha_guriri |title=The Island |author=Arena Ao Mar |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121192858/http://www.arenaaomar.com.br/ilha_guriri |archive-date=2015-01-21 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |author=Terra |title=São Mateus, Espírito Santo, blends beaches and colonial constructions |url=http://vidaeestilo.terra.com.br/turismo/noticias/0,,OI3785490-EI14071,00-Sao+Mateus+no+Espirito+Santo+mistura+praias+e+construcoes+coloniais.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193451/http://vidaeestilo.terra.com.br/turismo/noticias/0,,OI3785490-EI14071,00-Sao+Mateus+no+Espirito+Santo+mistura+praias+e+construcoes+coloniais.html |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 14, 2015}}</ref>

The climate of São Mateus is classified by the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] as a [[Tropical savanna climate|tropical wet and dry climate]] (type ''Aw'' according to the [[Köppen climate classification|Köppen classification]]),<ref name="vetmedunivienna">{{cite web |url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/62twqml5g?url=http://koeppen-geiger.vu-wien.ac.at/pics/kottek_et_al_2006.gif |archive-date=2011-11-02 |title=World Map of the Köppen-Geiger climate classification |publisher=Institute for Veterinary Public Health |access-date=January 14, 2015 |url-status=live}}</ref> with an average annual temperature of around 24&nbsp;°C, featuring rainy [[summer]]s with high [[temperature]]s and milder [[winter]]s.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/62twu0PtQ?url=http://www.brasil.gov.br/sobre/geografia/mapas/clima |archive-date=2011-11-02 |title=Climate |author=Portal Brasil |date=January 6, 2010 |access-date=January 14, 2015 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111102175041/http://biblioteca.ibge.gov.br/visualizacao/mapas/GEBIS%20-%20RJ/BrasilClimas.jpg |archive-date=2011-11-02 |title=Brazil - Climates |author=IBGE Library |access-date=January 23, 2013 |url-status=dead}}</ref> The annual [[precipitation (meteorology)|precipitation]] index is approximately {{convert|1350|mm|in}}, with November and December being the wettest months. [[Humidity|Air humidity]] is relatively high, with an [[insolation]] time of {{convert|2140|hours|abbr=on}} per year.<ref name="NCB_1981-2010"/>

According to the [[National Institute of Meteorology]] (INMET), since 1971, the lowest recorded temperature in São Mateus was {{convert|8|°C|°F}} on August 12, 1997,<ref name="Temp-min-INMET"/> and the highest was {{convert|38.7|°C|°F}} on February 25, 2006.<ref name="Temp-max-INMET"/> The highest 24-hour precipitation accumulation was {{convert|175.3|mm|in}} on November 22, 2008.<ref name="Precipitação-INMET"/> The wettest month was November 2000, with {{convert|573.1|mm|in}}, followed by November 2008 ({{convert|526.8|mm|in}}) and November 2001 ({{convert|504.8|mm|in}}).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.inmet.gov.br/projetos/rede/pesquisa/gera_serie_txt_mensal.php?&mRelEstacao=83550&btnProcesso=serie&mRelDtInicio=01/01/1971&mRelDtFim=31/12/2019&mAtributos=,,,,,,,,,1,,,,,,, |title=BDMEP - historical series - monthly data - total precipitation (mm) - São Mateus |publisher=Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia |access-date=October 13, 2015}}</ref>

=== Ecology and environment ===
[[File:Guruçá.JPG|thumb|left|upright=1.0|{{center|[[Crab]] emerging from its burrow}}]]

In São Mateus, the coastal region was historically dominated by [[restinga]].<ref name="Geog-ES"/> The remaining restinga is part of the [[Atlantic Forest Biosphere Reserve]],<ref name="Areas_Protegidas_do_ES"/> and the entire São Mateus coastline is within the buffer zone of the [[Abrolhos Marine National Park]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2012/05/noticias/a_gazeta/economia/1236887-criacao-de-areas-ambientais-inviabiliza-petroleo-e-pesca.html |title=Creation of environmental areas makes oil and fishing unfeasible |author=Gazeta Online |access-date=January 14, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.conservation.org.br/onde/ecossistemas/index.php?id=204 |title=Conservation Units |author=Conservação Internacional |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193546/http://www.conservation.org.br/onde/ecossistemas/index.php?id=204 |archive-date=2015-01-21 |url-status=dead}}</ref>

In the plateaus and river valleys, the original cover was [[Atlantic Forest]], rich in [[hardwood]]s.<ref name="Geog-ES">{{cite book |author=Cícero Moraes |title=Geography of Espírito Santo |location=Vitória |publisher=Fundação Cultura do Espírito Santo |year=174 |page=125 |volume=single |id=}}</ref> Most of the Atlantic Forest has been replaced by monoculture reforestation ([[eucalyptus]]), [[livestock farming]], and crops such as [[coffee]], [[coconut]], and [[black pepper]].<ref name="Atlas_ES"/><ref name="vegetação">{{cite book |author=Eliezer Nardoto |title=History of São Mateus |chapter=Vegetation Cover |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=152 |volume=single |id=}}</ref> However, some biodiversity persists in undisturbed patches, including [[Bromeliaceae|bromeliads]], [[orchid]]s, [[Attalea dubia|indaiá palms]], [[Tabebuia ochracea|golden trumpet trees]], [[Cecropia|pumpwoods]], [[Tibouchina granulosa|purple glory trees]], and [[fern]]s.<ref name="vegetação"/>

[[File:Allagoptera arenaria in Guriri Beach, Brazil.jpg|thumb|upright=1.0|{{center|''Allagoptera arenaria'' palm, endemic to the region}}]]

The only ecological station in the municipality is Barra Nova.<ref name="Atlas_ES"/> Its creation was a condition imposed on [[Petrobras]] for the establishment of the North Capixaba Terminal in the Barra Nova district. This area is considered extremely important for [[fish]], [[amphibian]]s, [[bird]]s, and [[mammal]]s, and serves as a spawning ground for four of the seven species of [[sea turtle]]s worldwide.<ref name="Areas_Protegidas_do_ES">{{cite web |url=http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/download/MAPA_UC_ES.pdf |title=Protected natural areas of Espírito Santo |author=State Secretariat for the Environment |access-date=January 14, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193443/http://www.meioambiente.es.gov.br/download/MAPA_UC_ES.pdf |archive-date=2015-01-21 |url-status=dead}}</ref><ref name="Petro_Barra_Tartaruga">{{cite web |url=http://www.pres.mpf.mp.br/site/noticias/ler_noticia.zul?noticia=1077 |title=MPF/ES wants Petrobras, Transpetro, and São Mateus City Hall to jointly create a conservation unit in the Barra Nova District |author=Federal Public Ministry |access-date=January 14, 2015}}</ref>

According to the [[National Institute for Space Research]] (INPE), in 2013, only {{convert|15.425|km2|sqmi}} (7%) of the municipality's area was covered by native vegetation, including {{convert|9.899|km2|sqmi}} of [[Atlantic Forest]], {{convert|2.066|km2|sqmi}} of [[restinga]], {{convert|1.452|km2|sqmi}} of [[mangrove]]s, and {{convert|2.008|km2|sqmi}} of floodplain vegetation.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.dropbox.com/sh/586xr2nom7kts9u/AACoqS4YdSndqrW2PUrjFF6Ma/Atlas_municipios2014_anobase2013_PDF.pdf?dl=0 |title=Atlas of the Atlantic Forest municipalities |author=[[National Institute for Space Research]] |publisher=2014 |access-date=January 24, 2015}}</ref> Preserved areas are home to animals such as [[spider]]s, [[crab]]s, [[butterfly|butterflies]], and [[dragonfly|dragonflies]] among [[invertebrate]]s; [[snake]]s, [[tortoise]]s, and [[lizard]]s among [[reptile]]s; [[frog]]s and [[toad]]s among [[amphibian]]s; [[parakeet]]s, [[pigeon]]s, [[Thrush (bird)|thrushes]], [[tanager]]s, and [[toucan]]s among [[bird]]s; and [[giant otter]]s, [[capybara]]s, [[otter]]s, and [[marmoset]]s among [[mammal]]s.<ref name="inventario"/>

== Demographics ==
In 2010, the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE) recorded a population of {{formatnum:109028}} inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010">{{cite web |url=http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/listabl.asp?z=cd&o=19&i=P&c=200 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150125003108/http://www.sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=200&i=P&nome=on&qtu8=137&qtu14=3&notarodape=on&tab=200&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&poc2=2&opc1=2&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&nivt=0&poc1=2&sec58=0&orp=6&qtu3=29&qtu13=47&opv=2&opc2=2&sec1=0&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&orc2=3&opc58=1&qtu2=5&qtu15=3&sev=93&sec2=0&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5566&opn13=0&orc1=4&poc58=1&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on&orc58=5&opn7=0&decm=99&ascendente=on&sep=36960&orn=1&qtu7=36&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=S%C3%A3o+Mateus&OpcCara=44&proc=1 |archive-date=January 25, 2015 |title=Resident population by sex, situation, and age groups - Sample - General Population Characteristics |author=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (SIDRA) |date=2010 |access-date=January 25, 2015 |url-status=live }}</ref> According to the census that year, {{formatnum:53930}} were men and {{formatnum:55098}} were women. Of the total, {{formatnum:84541}} lived in the [[urban area]] and {{formatnum:24487}} in the [[rural area]].<ref name="IBGE_Pop_2010"/> In the first decade of the 21st century, São Mateus' population grew by 20.56%, making it one of the fastest-growing municipalities in Espírito Santo during this period.<ref>{{cite web |date=November 29, 2010 |title=Espírito Santo's population exceeds 3.5 million inhabitants |url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2010/11/707619-populacao+do+espirito+anto+ultrapassa+3+5+milhoes+de+habitantes.html |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121194310/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2010/11/707619-populacao+do+espirito+anto+ultrapassa+3+5+milhoes+de+habitantes.html |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref> According to 2014 statistics, the population was {{formatnum:122668}}, ranking as the seventh most populous in the state. In 2010, {{formatnum:28124}} inhabitants (25.80%) were under 15 years old, {{formatnum:74752}} (68.56%) were aged 15 to 64, and {{formatnum:6152}} (5.64%) were over 65. The [[life expectancy]] at birth was 75.6 years, and the [[total fertility rate]] was 2.0 children per woman.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/>

The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI-M) of São Mateus is considered high, at 0.735 in 2010 according to the [[United Nations Development Programme]] (UNDP), ranking as the eighth highest in Espírito Santo and 897th in Brazil.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> The education index was 0.655, the longevity index was 0.843, and the income index was 0.719.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> From 2000 to 2010, the proportion of people with a per capita household income of up to half the minimum wage decreased by 49.7%. In 2010, 84.6% of the population lived above the [[poverty line]], 9.7% were at the poverty line, and 5.7% were below it.<ref name="ODM_1">{{cite web |url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150125004110/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/1-acabar-com-a-fome-e-a-miseria/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es |archive-date=January 25, 2015 |title=1 - End hunger and poverty |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=January 25, 2015 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The [[Gini coefficient]], measuring [[social inequality]], was 0.577, where 1.00 is the worst and 0.00 is the best.<ref name="ODM_Perfil">{{cite web |url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es |archive-url=https://archive.today/20150125004216/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/perfil/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es |archive-date=January 25, 2015 |title=Municipal profile |author=Portal ODM |date=2012 |access-date=January 25, 2015 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The wealthiest 20% of the population accounted for 60.9% of the municipal income, 19.9 times higher than the 3.1% share of the poorest 20%.<ref name="ODM_1"/>

=== Ethnicities and migration ===
São Mateus is a [[multiracial]] city, originally populated by [[Indigenous peoples of Brazil|Indians]], [[Portuguese people|Portuguese]], [[Africans]], and, from the late 19th century, [[Italian Brazilians|Italians]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.saomateus-es.com.br/site/index.php?p=paginas_ver&id=7&id_departamento=3 |title=History of São Mateus |publisher=São Mateus |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref> According to the 2010 IBGE census, based on self-declaration, the population consisted of {{formatnum:62464}} ''[[Pardo Brazilians|pardos]]'' (57.29%), {{formatnum:30717}} [[White people|whites]] (28.17%), {{formatnum:14842}} [[Black people|blacks]] (13.61%), {{formatnum:905}} [[Asian people|Asians]] (0.83%), and {{formatnum:101}} [[Indigenous peoples of Brazil|indigenous people]] (0.09%).<ref name="SIDRA_Etnias">{{cite web |url=http://sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=2094&i=P&nome=on&qtu8=137&qtu14=3¬arodape=on&tab=2094&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&poc133=1&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&nivt=0&orc86=3&orp=5&qtu3=27&qtu13=47&opv=2&poc86=2&opc133=1&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&orc133=4&qtu2=5&qtu15=3&sev=93&opc86=2&sec133=0&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5565&opn13=0&sec86=0&ascendente=on&sep=38559&orn=1&qtu7=36&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=Sao+Mateus&OpcCara=44&proc=1&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on&opn7=0&decm=99 |title=Table 2094 - Resident population by color or race and religion |publisher=Sistema IBGE de Recuperação Automática (SIDRA) |date=2010 |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref> In the same year, {{formatnum:108897}} inhabitants were [[Brazilians|Brazilian]] (99.88%), of whom {{formatnum:108835}} were [[Brazilian nationality law|native-born]] (99.82%) and {{formatnum:62}} were naturalized Brazilians (0.06%), and {{formatnum:131}} were foreigners (0.12%).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=1497&i=P&orc302=3&nome=on&qtu8=137&qtu14=3¬arodape=on&tab=1497&opc302=2&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&nivt=0&orp=4&qtu3=27&qtu13=47&opv=2&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&qtu2=5&qtu15=3&sev=93&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5565&opn13=0&poc302=2&opn7=0&decm=99&sec302=0&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on&sep=33141&orn=1&qtu7=36&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=Sao+Mateus&OpcCara=44&proc=1 |title=Table 1497 - Resident population by nationality - General Sample Results |date=2010 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref>

By [[Regions of Brazil|region]] of birth, {{formatnum:96784}} were born in the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]] (88.77%), {{formatnum:10324}} in the [[Northeast Region, Brazil|Northeast]] (9.47%), {{formatnum:658}} in the [[North Region, Brazil|North]] (0.60%), {{formatnum:378}} in the [[South Region, Brazil|South]] (0.35%), and {{formatnum:298}} in the [[Central-West Region, Brazil|Central-West]] (0.27%). {{formatnum:85647}} were natives of Espírito Santo (78.56%), with {{formatnum:59017}} born in the municipality (54.13%). Among the {{formatnum:23381}} from other states (27.30%), [[Minas Gerais]] had the largest presence with {{formatnum:7784}} residents (7.14%), followed by [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] with {{formatnum:2208}} (2.02%), and [[Rio de Janeiro (state)|Rio de Janeiro]] with {{formatnum:1144}} (1.05%).<ref>{{cite web |url=http://sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=631&i=P&opc183=2&nome=on&qtu8=137&qtu14=3¬arodape=on&tab=631&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&poc2=1&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&nivt=0&orp=5&qtu3=27&qtu13=47&opv=2&opc2=1&orc183=4&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&orc2=3&qtu2=5&qtu15=3&sev=93&sec2=0&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5565&opn13=0&poc183=2&ascendente=on&sep=24929&orn=1&qtu7=36&sec183=0&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=Sao+Mateus&OpcCara=44&proc=1&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on&opn7=0&decm=99 |title=Table 631 - Resident population by sex and place of birth |date=2010 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=http://sidra.ibge.gov.br/bda/tabela/protabl.asp?c=1505&i=P&nome=on&qtu8=137&qtu14=3¬arodape=on&tab=1505&opn8=0&opn14=0&unit=0&pov=3&OpcTipoNivt=1&opn1=0&nivt=0&orp=4&qtu3=27&qtu13=47&opv=2&pop=1&opn2=0&opn15=0&orv=2&qtu2=5&qtu15=3&sev=93&sec12017=0&opp=f1&opn3=0&qtu6=5565&opn13=0&opc12017=2&poc12017=2&sep=33142&orn=1&qtu7=36&orc12017=3&opn7=0&decm=99&pon=1&qtu9=558&opn6=3&digt6=Sao+Mateus&OpcCara=44&proc=1&qtu1=1&opn9=0&cabec=on |title=Table 1505 - Resident population by nativity in relation to the municipality and federation unit - General Sample Results |date=2010 |publisher=Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística |access-date=January 13, 2015}}</ref> Studies indicate that economic inequality disproportionately affects the black population, particularly women, who have lower education levels and wages. According to IBGE, the black population grew from 10,680 (1970 Census) to 77,112 (76.31% of 101,051) by 2006.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://apublica.org/especial-quilombolas/ |title=Quilombola Special |date=September 2016 |access-date=October 1, 2016 |publisher=Pública: Investigative Journalism Agency |last=Pública |first=Pública}}</ref>

According to the 2010 census by the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE), São Mateus' population is predominantly composed of {{formatnum:59867}} [[Catholic Church|Roman Catholics]] (57.41%), {{formatnum:36227}} [[Protestantism|Protestants]] (33.23%), {{formatnum:9468}} people [[Irreligion|without religion]] (8.68%), and {{formatnum:1297}} [[Jehovah's Witnesses]] (1.19%). There are also {{formatnum:637}} [[Kardecist spiritism|Spiritists]] (0.58%), {{formatnum:51}} followers of new Eastern religions (0.05%), {{formatnum:50}} [[Candomblé]] practitioners (0.05%), {{formatnum:42}} [[Umbanda]] practitioners (0.04%), {{formatnum:20}} [[Western esotericism|esoterics]] (0.02%), and {{formatnum:12}} [[Buddhism|Buddhists]] (0.01%). [[File:Catedral de São Mateus 2015.jpg|thumb|left|upright=1.0|{{center|[[St. Matthew Cathedral, São Mateus|São Mateus Cathedral]]}}]]

== Evangelical Churches ==

Although the city developed within a predominantly Catholic social framework, it is now possible to find the presence of various Protestant denominations. The city is home to a wide range of Protestant or Reformed faiths, with notable representation from the [[Assemblies of God]] with {{formatnum:9843}} members, the [[Baptist Church]] with {{formatnum:7908}}, the [[Universal Church of the Kingdom of God]] with {{formatnum:1630}} members, and the Maranatha Christian Church with {{formatnum:1315}} members.<ref name="IBGE_Religião"/>

== Politics and administration ==
[[File:Prédio da Prefeitura de São Mateus-ES.JPG|thumb|right|upright=1.0|alt=City Hall of São Mateus|City Hall of São Mateus]]

According to the municipal [[organic law]], enacted on April 5, 1990, municipal administration is carried out by the [[Executive Branch|executive]] and [[Legislative Branch|legislative]] powers.<ref name="Lei_Organica_Municipal">{{cite web|url=http://www.camarasaomateus.es.gov.br/estrutura/LEI_ORGANICA_DO_MUNICIPIO_DE_SAO_MATEUS-ES.pdf|title=Lei Orgânica do Município de São Mateus|date=April 5, 1990|publisher=São Mateus Municipal Chamber|access-date=January 16, 2014|archive-date=January 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000228/http://www.camarasaomateus.es.gov.br/estrutura/LEI_ORGANICA_DO_MUNICIPIO_DE_SAO_MATEUS-ES.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref> João dos Santos Neves, the then President of the Municipal Government, was the first representative of the executive power. Prior to this, the City Council managed the administration entirely.<ref name="História_Camara_Municipal_Site_Nardoto">{{cite web|url=http://www.saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver_pg&id=269|title=Câmara Municipal de São Mateus|publisher=São Mateus|access-date=January 16, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121194106/http://www.saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=paginas_ver_pg&id=269|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Amadeu Boroto, from the [[Brazilian Socialist Party]] (PSB), was elected mayor in the [[2012 Brazilian municipal elections|2012 municipal elections]], securing a total of {{formatnum:38742}} votes (69.27% of voters),<ref>{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/eleicoes/2012/noticia/2012/10/amadeu-boroto-e-reeleito-prefeito-de-sao-mateus.html|title=Amadeu Boroto é reeleito prefeito de São Mateus|date=7 October 2012 |publisher=G1|access-date=January 16, 2014}}</ref> with Keydson Quaresma Gomes as [[Deputy Mayor|deputy mayor]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://eleicoes.uol.com.br/2012/candidatos/2012/vice-prefeito/es/01021972-keydson-quaresma.htm|title=Keydson Quaresma|publisher=UOL Elections 2012|access-date=January 16, 2014}}</ref>

The legislative power is exercised by the [[Câmara municipal|municipal chamber]], composed of eleven councilors elected for four-year terms, in accordance with Article 29 of the [[Constitution of Brazil|Constitution]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm|archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/61tYzN9Fe?url=http://www.dji.com.br/constituicao_federal/cf029a031.htm|archive-date=September 22, 2011|author=DJI|title=Constituição Federal - CF - 1988 / Art. 29|access-date=January 16, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> As of January 2015, the chamber consisted of four seats held by the [[Brazilian Socialist Party]] (PSB), two by the [[National Mobilization|National Mobilization Party]] (PMN), one by the [[Social Democratic Party (Brazil, 2011)|Social Democratic Party]] (PSD), one by the [[Communist Party of Brazil]] (PCdoB), one by the [[Humanist Party of Solidarity]] (PHS), one by the [[Workers' Party (Brazil)|Workers' Party]] (PT), and one by the [[Democratic Labour Party (Brazil)|Democratic Labour Party]] (PDT).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://g1.globo.com/es/espirito-santo/apuracao/sao-mateus.html|title=Apuração São Mateus-ES|publisher=G1|access-date=January 16, 2014}}</ref> The chamber is responsible for drafting and voting on fundamental laws for the administration and the executive, particularly the participatory budget (Budget Guidelines Law).<ref name="Lei_Organica_Municipal"/>

The city is the seat of the São Mateus Judicial District, classified as a third-tier jurisdiction, covering only the municipality itself.<ref name="CGJ_Comarcas">{{cite web|url=http://www.cgj.es.gov.br/Comarcas/|archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6NvZhFAB7?url=http://www.cgj.es.gov.br/Comarcas/|archive-date=March 8, 2014|title=Comarcas|author=Espírito Santo General Inspectorate of Justice (CGJ)|access-date=January 25, 2015|url-status=live}}</ref> In December 2014, there were {{formatnum:77697}} registered voters, representing 2.923% of the total in the state of Espírito Santo.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/consulta-quantitativo|title=Consulta Quantitativo|date=April 12, 2013|author=[[Superior Electoral Court]] (TSE)|access-date=January 25, 2015|archive-date=June 10, 2014|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140610023817/http://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/estatisticas/consulta-quantitativo|url-status=dead}}</ref> São Mateus has two sister cities: [[Sondrio]] ([[Italy]] {{flagicon|Italy}})<ref>{{cite web|url=http://diocesedesaomateus.org.br/portal/noticias/359-dom-zanoni-vista-sondrio-na-italia.html|title=Dom Zanoni vista Sondrio na Itália|access-date=July 10, 2010|author=Diocese of São Mateus|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101224110207/http://www.diocesedesaomateus.org.br/portal/noticias/359-dom-zanoni-vista-sondrio-na-italia.html|archive-date=December 24, 2010|url-status=dead}}</ref> and [[Luoyang]] ([[China]] {{flagicon|China}}).<ref name="Luoyang">{{cite web|url=https://noticias.uol.com.br/politica/escandalos-no-congresso/camara-gasta-r-500-mil-com-diarias-no-exterior.htm |title=45. Câmara gasta R$ 500 mil com diárias no exterior |date=2011-05-29|access-date=July 10, 2010|publisher=UOL}}</ref> The initiative to adopt Sondrio as a sister city originated from the [[Roman Catholic Diocese of São Mateus|Diocese of São Mateus]], as Bishop Emeritus Dom Aldo Gerna hails from that city. The partnership with Luoyang, initiated by the municipal executive, aims to foster commercial cooperation between the two cities.<ref name="Luoyang"/>

In 2012, São Mateus's [[gross domestic product]] (GDP) was approximately one billion four hundred million [[Brazilian real|reais]], accounting for 49.8% of the total GDP of its microregion, making it the largest in the region and the eleventh in the state.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> Of the total GDP in that year, R$278,692,000 came from the [[Primary sector of the economy|primary sector]], R$245,387,000 from the [[Secondary sector of the economy|secondary sector]], R$854,086,000 from the [[Tertiary sector of the economy|tertiary sector]], and R$96,320,000 were collected from [[tax]]es on products net of subsidies at current prices. The per capita GDP was R$13,184.81.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=134&codmun=320490|title=Produto Interno Bruto de São Mateus-ES em 2012|publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE)|access-date=January 8, 2015}}</ref>

In 2010, 71.3% of the population over 18 years old was [[Workforce|economically active]], with an unemployment rate of 8.7%.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/>

;Primary sector

In 2012, the primary sector generated approximately 280 million reais in São Mateus, making it the second-largest contributor to the GDP.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> In 2010, of the employed population aged 18 or older, 22.75% worked in agriculture and 2.82% in extractive industries.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> Among the primary activities, notable are the extraction of [[petroleum]] and [[natural gas]], [[silviculture]], and the cultivation of [[coconut]]. Other significant crops include [[macadamia]], [[coffee]], [[black pepper]]. [[Horticulture]] and [[livestock farming]] are also important, though to a lesser extent.

*'''[[Petroleum]] and [[natural gas]]''': The first productive [[Oil field|oil and gas fields]] in Espírito Santo were discovered in São Mateus in 1967.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.es.gov.br/EspiritoSanto/paginas/petroleo_gas.aspx|title=Petróleo e Gás Natural|publisher=Government of the State of Espírito Santo|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref><ref name="Petroleo_e_Silvicultura">{{cite web|url=http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=7|title=O eucalipto e o petróleo: a redenção do norte|publisher=São Mateus - ES|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-date=January 9, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150109162833/http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=7|url-status=dead}}</ref> According to the Espírito Santo Institute for Research, Technical Assistance, and Rural Extension (INCAPER), in 2013, an average of 2,800 [[Barrel (unit)|barrels]] of oil were produced daily from 150 onshore wells and the Cação Platform, with the municipality accounting for 23% of the state's oil production.<ref name="PROATER">{{cite web|url=http://www.incaper.es.gov.br/proater/municipios/Nordeste/Sao_Mateus.pdf|title=PROATER 2011 - 2013|publisher=Espírito Santo Institute for Research, Technical Assistance, and Rural Extension (INCAPER)|access-date=January 25, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303171120/http://www.incaper.es.gov.br/proater/municipios/Nordeste/Sao_Mateus.pdf|archive-date=March 3, 2016|url-status=dead}}</ref>
*'''[[Silviculture]]''': [[Eucalyptus]] is the main crop in the municipality, introduced by [[Aracruz Celulose]] in the early 1970s.<ref name="Petroleo_e_Silvicultura"/> In 2013, 745,124 [[cubic meter]]s of this wood were harvested, with 700,581 [[Cubic meter|m³]] used for [[Pulp (paper)|pulp]] production, 15,537 [[Cubic meter|m³]] for [[charcoal]], and 29,006 [[Cubic meter|m³]] for various other purposes.<ref name="IBGE_Silvicultura">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=138&codmun=320490|title=Produção da Extração Vegetal e da Silvicultura 2013|publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE)|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref>
*'''[[Coconut]]''': São Mateus is the third-largest producer of [[coconut]] in Brazil. In 2010, the municipality produced approximately 75 million fruits annually, representing 3.66% of national production, 25% of the [[Southeast Region, Brazil|Southeast Region]], and about 48% of Espírito Santo's production, using 3,740 [[Hectare|ha]] of planted area.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cpatc.embrapa.br/publicacoes_2011/doc_164.pdf|title=Evolução da produção de coco no Brasil e o comércio internacional - Panorama 2010|publisher=Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation (Embrapa)|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref>
*'''[[Macadamia]]''': Macadamia production makes São Mateus the second-largest producer of this fruit in Brazil. Introduced in the second half of the 1980s, it yielded just over 300 [[Tonne|tons]] annually in 2012, using approximately 500 [[Hectare|ha]] of planted area. Notably, about 98% of this production is exported to the [[United States]] and [[China]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://jornais.fivepress.com.br/eshoje/201207131200/PDF/edio39200007.pdf|title=São Mateus é o segundo maior produtor de macadâmia do pais|publisher=ES Hoje Newspaper|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150109162902/http://jornais.fivepress.com.br/eshoje/201207131200/PDF/edio39200007.pdf|archive-date=January 9, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vidanocampoonline.com/index.php/arquivo-de-noticias/39-noticias/1102-espirito-santo-cresce-cultivo-da-noz-macadamia-grande-parte-da-producao-segue-para-a-exportacao|title=Espírito Santo: Cresce cultivo da noz macadâmia; grande parte da produção segue para a exportação|date=April 21, 2011|publisher=Vida no Campo|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150109162506/http://www.vidanocampoonline.com/index.php/arquivo-de-noticias/39-noticias/1102-espirito-santo-cresce-cultivo-da-noz-macadamia-grande-parte-da-producao-segue-para-a-exportacao|archive-date=January 9, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref>
*'''[[Coffee]]''': The municipality is notable for producing [[Coffea canephora|conilon coffee]], being the sixth-largest producer in the state.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.cetcaf.com.br/Links/cafeicultura%20capixaba.htm|title=Breve histórico do Café Arábica no Espírito Santo|publisher=Coffee Technology Development Center (CETAF)|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-date=March 20, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150320220328/http://cetcaf.com.br/Links/cafeicultura%20capixaba.htm|url-status=dead}}</ref> According to IBGE, in 2013, 21,000 tons of this grain were produced on 12,500 [[Hectare|ha]], with an average yield of 1,620 kilograms per hectare, generating approximately 82 million [[Brazilian real|reais]] in revenue.<ref name="IBGE_Agrícola">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=136&codmun=320490|title=Produção Agrícola Municipal de 2013|publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE)|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref>
*'''[[Black pepper]]''': Espírito Santo is the second-largest producer of black pepper in Brazil, with São Mateus as its largest producer.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.revistacampoenegocios.com.br/pimenta-do-reino-alta-rentabilidade-atrai-produtores-para-a-atividade/|title=Pimenta-do-reino: Alta rentabilidade atrai produtores para a atividade|publisher=Campo & Negócios|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150109163115/http://www.revistacampoenegocios.com.br/pimenta-do-reino-alta-rentabilidade-atrai-produtores-para-a-atividade/|archive-date=January 9, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> In 2013, IBGE estimated a production of 4,480 tons on 1,600 [[Hectare|ha]], with an average yield of 2,880 kilograms per hectare, generating approximately 52 million reais in revenue.<ref name="IBGE_Agrícola"/>
*'''[[Horticulture]]''': Apart from [[coconut]], no other fruit crop stands out significantly. However, in 2013, the municipality produced 6,720 tons of [[banana]]s, 900 tons of [[lemon]]s, 56,700 tons of [[papaya]]s, 75 tons of [[mango]]es, 6,000 tons of [[passion fruit]], and 210 tons of [[grape]]s.<ref name="IBGE_Agrícola"/>
*'''[[Animal husbandry]]''': In 2013, IBGE estimated a herd of 88,732 [[cattle]], 5,100 [[pigs]], 2,080 [[sheep]], 227 [[Bubalus|buffalo]], 4,520 [[horses]], 408 [[goats]], and 49,220 [[birds]], including 9,610 [[Chicken|hens]]. The city produced 9,406 liters of milk from 8,509 [[cows]], 27,000 dozen chicken eggs, and 108,000 kilograms of [[honey]]. Additionally, 85,000 kilograms of [[tilapia]] were produced in captivity.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=135&codmun=320490|title=Espírito Santo: Produção da Pecuária Municipal 2013|publisher=Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics (IBGE)|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref>

;Secondary and tertiary sectors

In 2010, 6.45% of the employed population worked in the manufacturing industry,<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> with the secondary sector contributing 245,387,000 reais to the municipal GDP, currently the smallest contributor among the three production sectors.<ref name="IBGE_PIB" /> Nevertheless, the industrial sector has shown the most growth, driven by the establishment of industrial plants such as the North Espírito Santo Terminal,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.petrobras.com.br/pt/nossas-atividades/principais-operacoes/terminais-e-oleodutos/terminal-norte-capixaba.htm|title=Terminal Norte Capixaba|publisher=Petrobras|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref> as well as automobile factories, such as those of Volare<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.valor.com.br/empresas/3653660/marcopolo-nova-fabrica-no-es-deve-comecar-operar-em-outubro|title=Marcopolo: Nova fábrica no ES deve começar a operar em Outubro|date=14 August 2014 |publisher=Valor|access-date=January 9, 2015}}</ref> and [[Agrale]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.amanha.com.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=7031:agrale-construira-fabrica-em-sao-mateus-no-espirito-santo&catid=37:home-4&Itemid=134|title=Marcopolo: Agrale construirá fábrica em São Mateus, no Espírito Santo|publisher=Revista Amanhã|access-date=January 9, 2015|archive-date=January 21, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193805/http://www.amanha.com.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=7031:agrale-construira-fabrica-em-sao-mateus-no-espirito-santo&catid=37:home-4&Itemid=134|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2010, among the economically active population, 8.80% worked in construction, 1.06% in public utilities, 15.06% in commerce, and 37.21% in services,<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/> with the tertiary sector generating the largest share of the municipal GDP in 2012: R$854,086,000.<ref name="IBGE_PIB"/>

== Infrastructure ==
=== Healthcare ===

In 2009, the municipality had 63 healthcare facilities, including [[hospital]]s, [[Emergency department|emergency services]], [[Community health center|health centers]], and [[Dentistry|dental services]], with 32 public and 31 private facilities. These provided 165 [[Hospital bed|beds]] for hospitalization, with 123 in public facilities and 42 in private ones, the latter also serving the [[Sistema Único de Saúde|Unified Health System]] (SUS).<ref name="IBGE_Saúde">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=5&codmun=320490|title=Serviços de Saúde 2009|author=Cidades@ - IBGE|date=2009|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

In 2012, 68.8% of births in the municipality were [[Caesarean section|C-sections]], and 1% of children were born without [[Prenatal care|prenatal exams]]. There was one maternal death among 1,748 births, representing a rate of 57.2 deaths per 100,000 births, and 20.5% of children were born to teenage mothers (under 20 years old).<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/5-melhorar-a-saude-das-gestantes/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|title=Melhorar a saúde das gestantes|author=Portal ODM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 21, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121192810/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/5-melhorar-a-saude-das-gestantes/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|url-status=dead}}</ref> In 2013, 96.1% of children under one year old had up-to-date vaccination records.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|title=Reduzir a mortalidade infantil|author=Portal ODM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122042413/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/4-reduzir-a-mortalidade-infantil/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|url-status=dead}}</ref>

In 2012, 21 cases of [[HIV/AIDS|AIDS]] were reported, with nine in men and twelve in women. In 2011, there were 648 cases of [[Dengue fever|dengue]], two of [[malaria]], and one of [[leishmaniasis]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/6-combater-a-aids-a-malaria-e-outras-doencas/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|title=Combater AIDS, malária e outras doenças|author=Portal ODM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122035357/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/6-combater-a-aids-a-malaria-e-outras-doencas/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|url-status=dead}}</ref>

According to the [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics]] (IBGE), in 2012, there were 512 deaths in São Mateus hospitals, with 384 men and 242 women. The most frequent causes included 167 deaths from [[respiratory disease]]s, 124 from [[Cardiovascular disease|circulatory diseases]], 72 from [[digestive disease]]s, and 58 from external causes such as accidents, injuries, and poisonings.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=114&codmun=320490|title=Morbidades Hospitalares 2012|author=Cidades@ - IBGE|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

The city has a public municipal pharmacy providing free medications.<ref>{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Farmácia Popular |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/farmacia-popular-sao-mateus.php |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090925164327/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/farmacia-popular-sao-mateus.php |archive-date=2009-09-25 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> Additionally, the municipality conducts vaccination campaigns against [[influenza]] in May<ref>{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Cronograma Campanha contra Influenza |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/download/saude/cronograma-de-vacinacao-Influenza.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193600/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/download/saude/cronograma-de-vacinacao-Influenza.pdf |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> and against [[rabies]] in the same month.<ref>{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Profilaxia da Raiva |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/download/saude/campanha-de-profilaxia-da-raiva-animal.pdf |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193740/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/download/saude/campanha-de-profilaxia-da-raiva-animal.pdf |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

=== Education ===

[[File:Polivalente São Mateus 2015.jpg|thumb|left|upright=1.0|alt=Polivalente School|Polivalente School]]

In 2013, the average Basic Education Development Index (IDEB) for São Mateus public schools was 5.4 for early years and 4.3 for final years. The municipality ranked {{formatnum:2171}} among Brazil's {{formatnum:5565}} municipalities for early years and {{formatnum:2054}} for final years. Among Espírito Santo's 78 municipalities, São Mateus ranked 36th for early years and 30th for final years.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/2-educacao-basica-de-qualidade-para-todos/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|title=Oferecer educação básica de qualidade a todos|author=Portal ODM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 22, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122042408/http://www.relatoriosdinamicos.com.br/portalodm/2-educacao-basica-de-qualidade-para-todos/BRA003032068/sao-mateus---es|url-status=dead}}</ref> The [[Human Development Index]] (HDI) for education was 0.655 in 2010, compared to Brazil's 0.849, up from 0.251 in 1991.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/>

In 2012, the municipality had approximately {{formatnum:26582}} enrollments in public and private schools. According to IBGE, of the 81 preschool institutions, 71 were public municipal schools, and 10 were private. Of the 90 [[Primary school|elementary schools]], 69 were public municipal schools, 11 were public state schools, and 10 were private schools. Of the 13 [[high school]]s, six were public state schools, one was a public federal school, and six were private schools.<ref name="Educação_IBGE"/> There were 282 preschool teachers, {{formatnum:1060}} elementary school teachers, and 306 high school teachers.<ref name="Educação_IBGE"/>

In 2010, 55.68% of youths over 18 had completed elementary education, and 43.49% had completed high school. Additionally, 96.28% of children aged 5 to 6 were attending school.<ref name="PNUD_IDH_2010"/>

In 2015, São Mateus had the Espírito Santo Federal Institute (IFES) offering technical education<ref name="IFES">{{cite web|url=http://www.sm.ifes.edu.br/site/index.php|title=IFES - Campus São Mateus ES|author=Espírito Santo Federal Institute|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122054001/http://www.sm.ifes.edu.br/site/index.php|archive-date=January 22, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> and two federal higher education institutions: the North Espírito Santo University Center (CEUNES), part of the [[Federal University of Espírito Santo]] (UFES)<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ceunes.ufes.br/|title=Centro Universitário Norte do Espírito Santo|author=Federal University of Espírito Santo|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> and a campus of the Espírito Santo Federal Institute (IFES).<ref name="IFES"/>

=== Public safety and crime ===

[[File:Câmera de monitoramento em São Mateus.JPG|thumb|right|upright=1.0|alt=Camera monitoring service in São Mateus|Camera monitoring service in São Mateus]]

As in most medium and large Brazilian municipalities, crime remains a concern in São Mateus.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://globotv.globo.com/tv-gazeta-es/estv-2a-edicao/v/alto-numero-de-homicidios-em-sao-mateus-es-preocupa-populacao-local/2276762/|title=Alto número de homicídios em São Mateus, ES, preocupa população local|author=Globo.TV|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> In 2012, the homicide rate was 68.9 per 100,000 inhabitants, making it the sixth most violent municipality in Espírito Santo and the 93rd in Brazil.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://exame.abril.com.br/brasil/noticias/as-500-cidades-mais-violentas-do-brasil-versao-2014|title=As 500 cidades mais perigosas do Brasil – versão 2014|author=Exame|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> Considering citizens identifying as Black or White, the homicide rate was 93.3 per 100,000 for those identifying as Black and 9.5 for those identifying as White.<ref>{{cite web |author=Sangari |date=2011 |title=Tabela CT. Número e taxas (por 100 mil) de homicídio segundo raça/cor nos municípios |url=http://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_cor_total.xlsx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000418/http://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_cor_total.xlsx |archive-date=2015-01-22 |access-date=January 19, 2015 |format=xls}}</ref> The suicide rate was 3.6 per 100,000 inhabitants, ranking 21st in the state and 1008th nationally.<ref>{{cite web |author=Sangari |date=2011 |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de suicídio nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |url=http://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_Suicidio_total.xlsx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121235940/https://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_Suicidio_total.xlsx |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 19, 2015 |format=xls}}</ref> The traffic accident mortality rate was 63.5 per 100,000 inhabitants, the second highest in the state and 54th nationally.<ref>{{cite web |author=Sangari |date=2011 |title=Número e taxas (em 100 mil) de Óbitos Ac.Transporte nos municípios com 10.000 habitantes ou mais |url=http://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_Transp_total.xlsx |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122000424/http://www.mapadaviolencia.net.br/tabelas2014/2014_Transp_total.xlsx |archive-date=2015-01-22 |access-date=January 19, 2015 |format=xls}}</ref>

The municipality has two prison facilities. The São Mateus Provisional Detention Center, opened in 2009, has {{formatnum:350}} spaces for temporary detainees or those awaiting trial.<ref>{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Governo do Estado inaugura CDP de São Mateus |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=126 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131225063040/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=126 |archive-date=2013-12-25 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> The São Mateus Regional Penitentiary, managed privately, is recognized as a model for prison management. Opened in 2011, it houses 534 inmates, including 76 women.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.sindspem-ma.com.br/index.php?mnID=16&ID=2519&Espirito%20Santo,%20tem-seis-presidios%20com%20cogestao|title=Espírito Santo tem seis presídios com cogestão|author=SINDSPEM|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

In 2011, a Municipal Civil Guard, named Citizen Guard, was established to protect and defend municipal public property and its users.<ref>{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Prefeitura de São Mateus implanta Guarda Cidadã |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=548 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131225072514/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=548 |archive-date=2013-12-25 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

São Mateus is home to the 13th Battalion of the [[Military Police of Espírito Santo State]]. Established on June 22, 2010, from the Fifth Independent Company, it covers São Mateus, [[Conceição da Barra]], [[Jaguaré]], and [[Pedro Canário]]. As of January 2015, Lieutenant Alex Voney de Almeida was its commander.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pm.es.gov.br/ome/default.aspx|title=Décimo Terceiro Batalhão de Polícia Militar do Espírito Santo|author=Espírito Santo Military Police|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193442/http://www.pm.es.gov.br/ome/default.aspx|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> The city is also home to the Eighteenth Regional Police Station of the Civil Police<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.pc.es.gov.br/telefones-e-enderecos/18-dpj-sao-mateus|title=18º Delegacia Regional|author=Espírito Santo Civil Police|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193744/http://www.pc.es.gov.br/telefones-e-enderecos/18-dpj-sao-mateus|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> and a [[Federal Police of Brazil|Federal Police]] station.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.dpf.gov.br/institucional/pf-pelo-brasil/espirito-santo/superintendencia-regional-do-espirito-santo|title=Superintendência Regional do Espírito Santo|author=[[Federal Police (Brazil)|Federal Police]]|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

=== Housing, services, and communications ===
[[File:EDP Escelsa em São Mateus.jpg|thumb|right|upright=1.0|alt=Escelsa substation in São Mateus|[[Substation]] of Escelsa]]

In 2010, according to [[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]], São Mateus had {{formatnum:32729}} permanent private households, with {{formatnum:25714}} in urban areas and {{formatnum:7015}} in rural areas. Of these, {{formatnum:28851}} were built with coated masonry, {{formatnum:3456}} with uncoated masonry, 167 with reused wood, 97 with prepared wood, 66 with coated stucco, 26 with uncoated stucco, 11 with straw, and 52 with other materials.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=94&codmun=320490|title=Censo Demográfico 2010: Características da População e dos Domicílios: Resultados do Universo|author=Cidades@ - IBGE|date=2010|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

[[File:Torre da Oi em São Mateus 01.JPG|thumb|left|upright=1.0|alt=Oi Telecommunications tower in São Mateus|Tower of [[Oi (telecommunications)|Oi Telecommunications]]]]

In 2010, of the {{formatnum:32729}} households, {{formatnum:30891}} were houses, {{formatnum:1779}} were apartments, 73 were rooms or tenements, and 50 were houses in villages or condominiums. Regarding occupancy, {{formatnum:23647}} households were owned, {{formatnum:5858}} were rented, {{formatnum:3236}} were loaned, and 52 were in other conditions. For water supply, {{formatnum:26154}} households had access to the general network, {{formatnum:6103}} used wells, springs, or cisterns, 223 relied on water trucks, 165 used rainwater stored in cisterns, 24 used rivers, lakes, streams, or creeks, and 124 had other sources. For waste disposal, {{formatnum:27299}} households had garbage collected, {{formatnum:5142}} burned their waste, 221 dumped it on vacant lots, 87 buried it, two disposed of it in rivers or the sea, and 42 used other methods. Finally, {{formatnum:32664}} households had electricity, while 129 did not.<ref name="IBGE_Domicílios">{{cite web|url=http://cidades.ibge.gov.br/xtras/csv.php?lang=&idtema=79&codmun=320490|title=Censo demográfico 2010: resultados do universo - indicadores sociais municipais|author=Cidades@ - IBGE|date=2010|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

The Autonomous Water and Sewage Service (SAAE) is responsible for water supply and sewage treatment.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.saaesma.com.br/|title=SAAE São Mateus|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> Currently, SAAE serves 80% of households with treated water.<ref name="IBGE_Domicílios"/> The municipality has two [[water treatment plant]]s: one in the seat, which draws water from the [[São Mateus River]], and another in Guriri, which draws from the [[Mariricu River]] and has equipment for [[Desalination|desalinating]] brackish water during dry periods.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.saaesma.com.br/agua/tratamento|title=Tratamento|author=SAAE São Mateus|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

Espírito Santo Centrais Elétricas S.A. (Escelsa), a subsidiary of [[EDP Brasil]], supplies electricity to São Mateus, serving 67 of Espírito Santo’s 78 municipalities.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.edp.com.br/distribuicao/edp-escelsa/conheca/area-concessao/Paginas/default.aspx|title=Área de concessão|publisher=EDP Escelsa|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6H0SqepkD?url=http://www.edp.com.br/distribuicao/edp-escelsa/conheca/area-concessao/Paginas/default.aspx|archive-date=May 30, 2013|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Internet]] services, including dial-up and [[broadband]] ([[Asymmetric Digital Subscriber Line|ADSL]]), are offered by various free and paid [[Internet service provider|providers]]. Mobile phone services are provided by [[Claro Company|Claro]],<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.claro.com.br/celular/cobertura/regiao/ddd27/ES/cidade/|title=Cobertura Claro|author=Claro|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150122005311/http://www.claro.com.br/celular/cobertura/regiao/ddd27/ES/cidade/|archive-date=January 22, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> [[Oi (telecommunications)|Oi]],<ref>{{cite web |author=Oi |title=Cobertura Oi |url=https://www.oi.com.br/portal-oi-cobertura/ |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> [[TIM Group|Tim]],<ref>{{cite web |author=Tim |title=Cobertura Tim |url=https://www.tim.com.br/para-voce/cobertura-e-roaming/mapa-de-cobertura |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> and [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.vivo.com.br/portalweb/appmanager/env/web?_nfls=false&_nfpb=true&_pageLabel=vivoVcCobNacCoberturaVivoPage&WT.ac=portal.movel.coberturaeroaming.coberturanacional.coberturavivo&cliente=0&cltype=c291Y2xpZW50ZQ®ional=RS#|title=Cobertura Vivo|author=Vivo|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> The area code ([[Direct distance dialing|DDD]]) for São Mateus is 027,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://dddcidade.com.br/ddd-sao-mateus-es/|title=DDD|author=DDD Cidade|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> and the [[postal code]] ([[Código de Endereçamento Postal|CEP]]) ranges from 29930-210 to 29949-990.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.correios.com.br/|title=CEP de cidades brasileiras|author=[[Correios]]|access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref>

In January 2015, São Mateus had two newspapers: Tribuna do Cricaré<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.telelistas.net/locais/es/sao+mateus/jornais+editores+e+representantes/225565244/tribuna+do+cricare|title=Tribuna do Cricaré|author=Telelistas|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 21, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121192938/http://www.telelistas.net/locais/es/sao+mateus/jornais+editores+e+representantes/225565244/tribuna+do+cricare|url-status=dead}}</ref> and Folha Acadêmica.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.folhaacademica.com.br/|title=Folha Acadêmica|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-date=January 21, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121192956/http://www.folhaacademica.com.br/|url-status=dead}}</ref> It also had five radio stations, including Rádio Cricaré at 1120 [[Amplitude modulation|AM]]<ref>{{cite web|url=http://simnoticias.com.br/site/index.php/radios-sim/sim-am|title=Rádios AM|author=Rede SIM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193803/http://simnoticias.com.br/site/index.php/radios-sim/sim-am|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> and four [[Frequency modulation|FM]] stations: Rádio Ilha 87.9 FM,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.ilhafmguriri.com.br/|title=Ilha FM Guriri|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121235812/http://www.ilhafmguriri.com.br/|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Rádio Kairós 94.7 FM,<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.radiokairos.com.br/radio/|title=Rádio Kairós FM|access-date=January 19, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121235929/http://www.radiokairos.com.br/radio/|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref> Rádio Musical 105.1 FM,<ref name="Radios_SIM">{{cite web |title=SIM FM |url=https://www.radios.com.br/aovivo/radio-musical-1051-fm/14009 |access-date=January 19, 2015}}</ref> and Rádio SIM 105.9 FM.<ref name="Radios_SIM"/>

=== Transportation ===
;Air

São Mateus is served by the Tancredo de Almeida Neves Airport. During the 1950s, this [[aerodrome]] received regular flights from the now-defunct [[Navegação Aérea Brasileira|NAB]]. Currently, it has no commercial routes but is equipped to handle helicopters and aircraft with up to 50 passengers.<ref name="Aeroporto_Liberado_Prefeitura">{{cite web |author=São Mateus City Hall |title=Aeroporto de São Mateus é liberado |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=1129 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121193407/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=1129 |archive-date=2015-01-21 |access-date=January 21, 2015}}</ref> Plans exist to extend its runway from {{formatnum:1350|m}} m to {{formatnum:1650|m}} m and replace its nighttime lighting, which is currently activated only on request.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://smnoticias.com.br/noticia/536/aeroporto-de-sao-mateus-tera-pista-ampliada-para-receber-aviao-de-medio-porte|title=Aeroporto de São Mateus terá pista ampliada para receber avião de médio porte|author=SM Notícias|access-date=January 21, 2015|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150121194312/http://smnoticias.com.br/noticia/536/aeroporto-de-sao-mateus-tera-pista-ampliada-para-receber-aviao-de-medio-porte|archive-date=January 21, 2015|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |author= |title=ES recebe R$ 200 milhões para investir em quatro aeroportos |url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/noticia/2013/01/es-recebe-r-200-milhoes-para-investir-em-quatro-aeroportos.html |access-date=January 21, 2015 |website=G1 |date=26 January 2013 }}</ref>

;Road

'''Railway'''

From 1923 to 1941, São Mateus was served by the São Mateus Railway, which connected it to [[Nova Venécia]], then a settlement under São Mateus’s jurisdiction.

The railway had a 60&nbsp;cm gauge and facilitated the transport of timber and coffee from the region to the city’s former river port, as well as local passenger services. It spanned 68&nbsp;km of track without connecting to other railways.

Its operation was short-lived, ceasing in 1941 when it was dismantled, and its rails and materials were sold by the State Government. The proceeds funded the construction of a water tank in the city, now located near the Municipal Historical Museum.

Few traces of the old railway remain in São Mateus, except for its former headquarters building, repurposed for other uses. The railway’s former trackbed was replaced by the Miguel Curry Carneiro Highway (ES-381), now part of BR-381, with this section owned by the State Government, explaining the name change.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br/es_outras/saomateus.htm|title=São Mateus -- Estações Ferroviárias do Estado do Espírito Santo|access-date=August 26, 2020|website=www.estacoesferroviarias.com.br}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|last=Gavinha|url=https://www.gazetadonorte.com/governo-entrega-obras-de-pavimentacao-asfaltica-na-es-381-em-sao-mateus/|title=Governo entrega obras de pavimentação asfáltica na ES-381 em São Mateus |date=13 February 2020 | publisher=Gazeta do Norte|access-date=August 26, 2020|language=pt-BR}}</ref>

=== Tourist attractions ===

São Mateus is among Brazil’s [[List of oldest continuously inhabited cities|oldest continuously inhabited cities]]<ref name="Colonização Portuguesa"/> and boasts one of Espírito Santo’s most significant colonial architectural ensembles, the Port buildings, designated a heritage site by the State Culture Council in 1976.<ref name="Sitios_historicos_governo_ES"/> In the upper city, the municipality’s oldest heritage assets, built between the 18th and 19th centuries, are located. The municipality also features beaches, rivers, waterfalls, dunes, and mangroves.<ref name="Pontos_Turisticos_Nardoto">{{cite book|author=Eliezer Nardoto|title=História de São Mateus|chapter=Pontos Turísticos|edition=1st|location=São Mateus|publisher=EDAL|year=1999|page=270|volume=single|id=}}</ref> Notable attractions include:

===Cultural Attractions===

*'''Old Church''': The ruins of the Old Church are the symbol of the municipality and its main postcard.<ref name="PROATER"/> Contrary to common belief, it never functioned as a church.<ref name="Linhares_em_Dia">{{cite web |url=http://www.linharesemdia.com.br/noticias/geral/8820-sao-mateus-ponto-historicoturistico-a-igreja-velha-sera-protegida-por-grade-metalica.html |title=São Mateus: ponto histórico/turístico, a Igreja Velha, será protegida por grade metálica |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=Linhares em dia |archive-date=January 23, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150123213438/http://www.linharesemdia.com.br/noticias/geral/8820-sao-mateus-ponto-historicoturistico-a-igreja-velha-sera-protegida-por-grade-metalica.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its construction began in the early years of the 19th century at the request of the [[Jesuits]], designed to exceed 300 meters in length.<ref name="Dicas_da_Gisa">{{cite web |url=http://parceiros.gazetaonline.com.br/dicasdagisa/?p=1696 |title=Igreja Velha |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=Gazeta on line}}</ref> The funding for its construction came from a 1% tax on everything exported through the former Port.<ref name="Linhares_em_Dia"/> However, the project was halted in August 1853 by a decision of the Municipal Council, which argued that expanding the Parish Church would cost five times less and that completing the Old Church would require over 50 years.<ref name="Dicas_da_Gisa"/> The construction used stones brought as [[ballast]] on ships docking at the port, laid with mortar made from lime and [[whale oil]].<ref>{{cite web |url=http://ecoviagem.uol.com.br/brasil/espirito-santo/sao-mateus/ |title=São Mateus-ES |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=Eco Viagem}}</ref>
*'''Houses in the Port''': The Houses in the Port of São Mateus are a group of buildings constructed along the banks of the [[São Mateus River]], starting from the late 18th century. Originally, most of these mansions were built with stone masonry, with internal and side walls made of [[plaster]]. The construction of these buildings gave rise to a large cluster of houses around a square used for loading and unloading ships docking in São Mateus.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=1 |title=Porto de São Mateus |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=São Mateus |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150109231547/http://saomateus-es.com.br/site/?p=guias_ver&id=1 |archive-date=January 9, 2015 |url-status=dead}}</ref> With the opening of the first highways, starting in 1938, when the road connecting São Mateus to [[Linhares]] was inaugurated, the economic activities at the port began to decline. Ship transport waned, and the old port lost its major commercial houses, which relocated to the upper city. The abandoned mansions were then occupied by prostitutes, leading to architectural modifications in several of them. In 1968, following numerous crimes related to prostitution, the expulsion of prostitutes was ordered, and the mansions were designated as heritage sites by the State Culture Council in 1976.<ref name="Sitios_historicos_governo_ES"/>
*'''Biquinha''': This is a water reservoir built in 1880, with a system to capture water from springs located at the base of the São Mateus River valley slope, below the cathedral grounds, to supply drinking water to the port’s fountain. It is part of São Mateus’ Historical Heritage.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=473 |title=Secretarias fazem reforma da Biquinha |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=Prefeitura de São Mateus |archive-date=January 23, 2015 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150123173534/http://www.saomateus.es.gov.br/site/noticia-detalhe.aspx?id=473 |url-status=dead }}</ref>
*'''[[Projeto TAMAR]]''': The research base of TAMAR in Guriri was established in 1988, housing a visitor center that also serves as the Guriri Open Sea Turtle Museum. Key attractions include an aquarium, two turtle observation tanks, and an exhibition of life-sized replicas and silhouettes of the five sea turtle species. During the reproductive season in the summer, the release of hatchlings is organized in the late afternoons.<ref name="Tamar"/><ref>{{cite web |url=http://g1.globo.com/espirito-santo/noticia/2014/09/desova-de-tartarugas-comeca-no-es-e-biologa-alerta-para-riscos.html |title=Desova de tartarugas começa no ES e bióloga alerta para riscos |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=G1 |date=11 September 2014 }}</ref>

===Cuisine===

[[File:Moqueca capixaba 18 de janeiro de 2014 (2).JPG|left|thumb|upright=1.0|{{center|A [[moqueca capixaba]] prepared at home in a [[clay pot]]}}]]

The proximity of São Mateus to other states and the origins of the municipality’s settlers have contributed to a highly diverse cuisine, with a strong [[Bahia]]n influence, characterized by heavily [[seasoning|seasoned]] dishes, including [[vatapá]], [[acarajé]], [[Canjica (dish)|mungunzá]], [[Caruru (food)|caruru]], [[Quibebé|quibebe]], [[moqueca]], and seafood from the region.<ref>{{cite book |last=Sossai |first=Edilene Cristina Rodrigues |title=Gostinho Cultural do Norte do Espírito Santo |publisher=SINCADES |year=2011 |edition=1st |volume=single |location=Vila Pavão |page=53 |chapter=Culinária Africana}}</ref> Regarding moqueca, São Mateus offers both the [[moqueca|Bahian moqueca]], prepared with [[palm oil]], [[coconut milk]], and [[black pepper]], and the traditional [[moqueca capixaba]], which does not use these ingredients and may or may not be accompanied by [[shrimp sauce]].<ref name="Gastronomia">{{cite book |last=Nardoto |first=Eliezer |title=História de São Mateus |chapter=Gastronomia |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=393 |volume=single}}</ref>

One of the most traditional [[delicacy|delicacies]] in the municipality is the [[Menticirrhus americanus|southern kingcroaker]] moqueca, a small freshwater fish. This moqueca is not sold in restaurants, being a privilege of the city’s traditional families. Additionally, the municipality consumes various freshwater and marine fish, including [[Centropomus|snook]], [[hake]], [[catfish]], [[Leporinus|piau]], [[Stellifer|stardrum]], and ''[[hoplias]]''. Shellfish and crustaceans, such as [[crab]]s and [[mussel]]s, are also highly appreciated.<ref name="Gastronomia"/>

Products made from [[cassava]], such as cassava flour, [[beiju]], and [[tapioca]], are also characteristic foods of the municipality, prepared artisanally by families descended from [[quilombola|''quilombolas'']].<ref name="Gastronomia"/><ref name="Pratos_Doces">{{cite book |last=Nardoto |first=Eliezer |title=História de São Mateus |chapter=Pratos Doces |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=394 |volume=single}}</ref>

The Italian colonizers contributed to the popularity of pasta dishes, with the municipality widely consuming dishes such as [[spaghetti]], [[agnolini]], [[pizza]], [[polenta]], [[gnocchi]], and [[pancake]]s, among others. Some families descended from Italian immigrants still maintain the tradition of preparing a homemade pasta known as [[tagliatelle]].<ref name="Gastronomia"/>

===Sports===

[[File:Sernamby.jpg|thumb|left|upright=1.0|{{center|Sernamby Stadium}}]]

São Mateus currently has the [[Associação Atlética São Mateus]]<ref name="AASM">{{cite web |url=http://www.arquivodeclubes.com/es/saomateus.htm |title=Centro Educativo e Recreativo Associação Atlética São Mateus |access-date=July 8, 2010 |website=Acervo de Clubes |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20090531121952/http://www.arquivodeclubes.com/es/saomateus.htm |archive-date=May 31, 2009 |url-status=dead}}</ref> as its only active professional [[Association football|football]] club, having previously hosted the activities of the now-defunct Matheense Football Club.<ref name="Mateense">{{cite book |last=Nardoto |first=Eliezer |title=História de São Mateus |chapter=Esporte em São Mateus |edition=1st |location=São Mateus |publisher=EDAL |year=1999 |page=XX |volume=single}}</ref> The Associação Atlética São Mateus was founded on December 13, 1963, and among its notable campaigns, it participated in the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série D|2011 Brazilian Football Championship – Série D]] and the [[Campeonato Capixaba|1st Division of the Campeonato Capixaba]], winning the championship in 2009 and 2011.<ref>{{cite web |url=http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2009/06/101980-sao+mateus+e+o+campeao+capixaba+de+2009.html |title=São Mateus é o Campeão Capixaba de 2009 |access-date=July 8, 2010 |website=Gazeta Online |archive-date=June 25, 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210625075648/http://gazetaonline.globo.com/_conteudo/2009/06/101980-sao+mateus+e+o+campeao+capixaba+de+2009.html |url-status=dead }}</ref> Its stadium is the Manoel Moreira Sobrinho Stadium, better known as Sernamby Stadium, with a capacity of {{formatnum:7500}} people.<ref name="AASM"/>

The Matheense Football Club's founding date is uncertain, but the club was established before 1922. Its best performance in state championships was runner-up in the [[Campeonato Capixaba Série B|1994 Campeonato Capixaba Série B]]. The club's stadium, "Othovarino Duarte dos Santos," is more commonly known as the "Campo do Matheense." It has a capacity of about 1,000 people and is located in the Sernamby neighborhood.<ref name="Mateense"/>

===Holidays===

In São Mateus, there are four municipal holidays and eight national holidays, in addition to optional holidays. According to the city hall, the municipal holidays are: [[Good Friday]]; [[Feast of Corpus Christi|Corpus Christi]]; the city’s anniversary and the day of [[Matthew the Apostle|Saint Matthew the Evangelist]] on September 21; and the day of Saint Benedict on December 27.<ref name="Lei_Organica_Municipal"/> According to federal law No. 9,093, approved on September 12, 1995, municipalities may have a maximum of four municipal holidays with a religious scope, including Good Friday.<ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaCarnaval">{{cite web |url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |title=Carnaval - é ou não feriado? folga automática pode gerar alteração contratual |author=Sérgio Ferreira Pantaleão |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website=Guia Trabalhista |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6338F8rTG?url=http://www.guiatrabalhista.com.br/tematicas/carnaval.htm |archive-date=November 8, 2011 |url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="GuiaTrabalhistaLei">{{cite web |url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |title=Lei n.º 9.093, de 12 de setembro de 1995 |author=Presidência da República |access-date=January 23, 2015 |website= |archive-url=https://www.webcitation.org/6338H85jV?url=http://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/Leis/L9093.htm |archive-date=November 8, 2011 |url-status=live}}</ref>
