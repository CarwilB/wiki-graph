'''Temascaltepec''' is a [[municipalities of Mexico|municipality]] located in the [[Ixtapan Region]] of the [[State of Mexico]] in [[Mexico]]. Temascaltepec has an area of 547.5&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>. It borders the municipalities of [[Valle de Bravo]], [[Amanalco de Becerra]], [[Tejupilco]], [[San Simón de Guerrero]], [[Texcaltitlán]], [[Zinacantepec]], [[Coatepec Harinas]], and [[Zacazonapan]].<ref name="EncMuc">{{cite web|url=http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15086a.htm |title=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de Mexico Estado de Mexico Temascaltepec |access-date=2008-03-14 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070608021932/http://www.e-local.gob.mx/work/templates/enciclo/mexico/mpios/15086a.htm |archive-date=June 8, 2007 }}</ref> Temascaltepec's population was 26,968 in 1990, but rose to 30,336 by 2005, according to INEGI.<ref>{{cite book |author=Ayuntamiento de Temascaltepec |title=Enciclopedia de los municipios de México, Centro Nacional de Estudios Municipales de la Secretaria de Gobernación, Talleres Gráficos de la Nación, México |year=1988 |publisher=Talleres Gráficos de la Nación, México |location=Gobierno del Estado de México }}</ref>

==History==
This area was home to [[Nahuas]], [[Matlatzinca people|Matlatzincas]] and [[Mazahua people|Mazahuas]]. Temascaltepec may have originally been a subject town of nearby [[Texcaltitlán]]. It was conquered by [[Aztec]] emperor [[Axayacatl]] and was designated the capital of a strategic province. It fought wars with the neighboring [[Purepecha Empire]] to the west.<ref>{{cite book |last1=Berdan |first1=Frances |title=Aztec imperial strategies |date=1996 |publisher=Dumbarton Oaks Research Library and Collection |location=Washington, D.C |isbn=9780884022114 |pages=269-270}}</ref>

==Geography==
The Mountain of Temascaltepec is a prolongation of Nevado de Toluca and crosses the municipality. The most significant elevations are the hills of Temeroso, Soledad, Fortin, Peñas del Diablo, Peñon, Tres Reyes and Juan Luis. The three main rivers of Temascaltepec are Verde or De la Presa, Vado, and Temascaltepec.<ref>{{Citation|title=Temascaltepec River|date=2018-03-02|url=https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Temascaltepec_River&oldid=828411117|work=Wikipedia|language=en|access-date=2019-10-08}}</ref> All three rivers experience water flow year-round. In addition, there are 41 freshwater [[Spring (hydrosphere)|springs]]. The municipality is warm and [[Subtropics|subtropical]] in the north and east, and [[Semi-arid climate|semi-arid]] in the south and west. Average temperatures vary between {{convert|18|and|22|C|F}} and annual precipitation is from {{convert|800|to|1600|mm|in|abbr=on}}.

===Flora and fauna===
The area hosts a diverse array of flora and fauna. Plant life include: ash trees (''[[Fraxinus]]'' spp.), [[pine]] (''Pinus'' spp.), oyamel (''[[Abies religiosa]]''), holm oak (''[[Quercus ilex]]''), cedar, sabino, guaje (''[[Leucaena]] [[Leucaena esculenta|esculenta]]''),
tepegauje (''[[Lysiloma]] [[Lysiloma acapulcense|acapulcense]]''), madroño (''[[Texas Madrone|Arbutus xalapensis]]''), mahogany (''[[Swietenia]]'' spp.), ''[[Jacaranda]]'' spp., capulín (''[[Capulin|Prunus salicifolia]]''), [[guava]]s (''Psidium'' spp.), such as arrayán (''[[Psidium sartorianum|P. sartorianum]]''), [[mango]]s (''Mangifera'' spp.), copal (''[[Copaifera]]'' spp.) and [[peanut]] (''Arachis hypogaea'').<ref name=":0">{{Cite web|url=http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15086a.html|title=Estado de México - Temascaltepec|website=www.inafed.gob.mx|access-date=2019-10-08|archive-date=2019-09-17|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190917080705/http://www.inafed.gob.mx/work/enciclopedia/EMM15mexico/municipios/15086a.html|url-status=dead}}</ref>

Animal life include [[Felidae|cats]], [[deer]], [[collared peccary]] (''Pecari tajacu''), [[squirrel]]s, cuinique (''[[Notocitellus adocetus]]''), [[long-tailed weasel]] (''Mustela frenata''), [[hare]]s, [[nine-banded armadillo]], tejón (''[[white-nosed coati|Nasua narica]]''), tigrillo, [[bat]]s, [[pocket gopher]]s, [[skunk]]s, cacomiztle (''[[Ring-tailed cat|Bassariscus astutus]]''), [[gray fox]] (''Urocyon cinereoargenteus''), sparrowhawks (''[[Accipiter]]'' spp.), [[eagle]]s, [[New World vultures|vulture]], [[common raven]] (''Corvus corax''), quebrantahueso (''[[crested caracara|Caracara cheriway]]''), [[owl]]s, magpie-jays (''[[Calocitta]]'' spp.), [[chachalaca]], [[New World quail|quail]], huilota (''[[mourning dove|Zenaida macroura]]''), corn birds, turtledove, bobo, multo birds, chichicuilote (possibly ''[[Charadrius wilsonia]]''), tortoises, cincuantes, [[scorpion]]s, alicante, [[toad]]s, [[lizard]]s, [[frog]]s and [[axolotl]]s (''Ambystoma mexicanum'').

The principal natural resources, aside from minerals, are the forests, being present on 68% of the territory. The remaining territory is distributed amongst agricultural use, urban zones, breeding grounds, ways and water-bearing areas.<ref name=":0" />
