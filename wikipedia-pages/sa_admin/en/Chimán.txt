'''Chimán  ''' is a town and [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Chimán District]], [[Panamá Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 1,205 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> It is the seat of Chimán District.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 2,221; its population as of 2000 was 1,334.<ref name=inec/>
