'''São Joaquim do Monte''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈsɐ̃w ʒuɐˈkĩ ˈdu ˈmõti]}}) is a city located in the state of [[Pernambuco]], [[Brazil]]. Located 124.6&nbsp;km away from [[Recife]], capital of the state of Pernambuco, it has an estimated ([[2022 Brazilian Census|2022 Census]]) population of 20,037 inhabitants.<ref name="IBGE" />

==Geography==
* State - [[Pernambuco]]
* Region - [[Agreste|Agreste Pernambucano]]
* Boundaries - [[Bezerros]] and [[Camocim de São Félix]]   ('''N''');  [[Cupira, Pernambuco|Cupira]]    ('''S''');  [[Bonito, Pernambuco|Bonito]] and [[Belém de Maria]]   ('''E''');   [[Agrestina]]    ('''W''').
* Area - 242.63&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>
* Elevation - 463 m
* Hydrography - [[Sirinhaém River|Sirinhaém]] and [[Una River (Pernambuco)|Una rivers]]
* Vegetation - [[Caatinga]] [[hipoxerófila]]
* Climate - Hot and humid
* Annual average temperature - 22.7 c
* Distance to [[Recife]] - 124.6&nbsp;km

==Economy==
The main economic activities in '''São Joaquim do Monte''' are based in commerce and [[agribusiness]], especially [[tomato]]es, [[manioc]], [[passion fruit]]s; and [[livestock]] such as [[cattle]], [[pig]]s and [[poultry]].
