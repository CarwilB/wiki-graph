The '''Ezequiel Zamora Municipality''' is one of the 12 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Barinas (state)|Barinas]] and, according to the 2011 census by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 53,580.<ref>{{Cite web |url=http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |title=GeoHive - Venezuela extended population statistics |access-date=2015-11-08 |archive-date=2016-03-15 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160315201648/http://www.geohive.com/cntry/venezuela_ext.aspx |url-status=dead }}</ref>  The town of [[Santa Bárbara, Barinas|Santa Bárbara]] is the [[County seat|shire town]] of the Ezequiel Zamora Municipality.<ref name="autogenerated1">{{Cite web |title=MUNICIPIO EZEQUIEL ZAMORA |url=http://www.cleb.gob.ve/directorios/zamora.php |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250124211749/http://www.cleb.gob.ve/directorios/zamora.php |archive-date=2025-01-24 |language=es}}</ref> The municipality is named for the 19th century Venezuelan soldier [[Ezequiel Zamora]].

==Demographics==
The Ezequiel Zamora Municipality, according to a 2007 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, has a population of 51,436 (up from 43,367 in 2000).  This amounts to 6.8% of the state's population.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Proyecciones de población por año, según sexo y municipio, 2000 - 2010 |trans-title=Population projections by year, by sex and municipality, 2000 - 2010 |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/see/sintesisestadistica2007/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190403093504/http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/see/sintesisestadistica2007/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls |archive-date=2019-04-03 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=www.ine.gob.ve |language=es}}</ref>  The municipality's population density is {{convert|12.7|PD/sqkm}}.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Densidad poblacional según municipio, 2007 |trans-title=Population density by municipality, 2007 |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/see/sintesisestadistica2007/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190403093504/http://www.ine.gob.ve/documentos/see/sintesisestadistica2007/estados/barinas/cuadros/Poblacion4.xls |archive-date=2019-04-03 |access-date=2025-12-18 |website=www.ine.gob.ve |language=es}}</ref>

==Government==
The mayor of the Ezequiel Zamora Municipality is Levid Emilio Méndez, re-elected on October 31, 2004, with 38% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio Zamora |trans-title=Mayor of the Municipality of Zamora |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=4&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303213237/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/5/alcaldedis=0&edo=5&mun=4&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |archive-date=2016-03-03 |website=cne.gob.ve |language=es}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=Elecciones 30 de Julio de 2000 Directorio de Acaldes Electos |trans-title=Elections July 30, 2000 - Directory of Elected Mayors |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 |accessdate=2008-03-15 |website=Consejo Nacional Electoral |language=es}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into four [[parish]]es; Santa Bárbara, José Ignacio del Pumar, Pedro Briceño Méndez, and Ramón Ignacio Méndez.<ref name=autogenerated1 />
