== Toponymy ==
"Ibiporã" comes from the [[Tupi language]] and means "pretty land", from ''yby'' ("land") and ''porang'' ("pretty").<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm |title=Curso de Tupi Antigo |access-date=2012-09-10 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20120809053512/http://www.fflch.usp.br/dlcv/tupi/vocabulario.htm |archive-date=2012-08-09 }}</ref>
