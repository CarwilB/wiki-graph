'''Kampong Baroe''' ([[Javanese script|Javanese]]: ꦏꦩ꧀ꦥꦸꦁ​​ꦧꦫꦸ; [[Standard Malay]]: ''Kampung Baru'') is a [[resorts of Suriname|resort]] in [[Suriname]], located in the [[Saramacca District]].  Its population at the 2012 census was 2,248, the majority being [[Javanese Surinamese|Javanese]], and [[Indo-Surinamese|East Indian]].<ref name="census"/> The main agricultural crop is [[peanut]]s. Kampong Baroe also contains cattle, pig, and chicken farms.<ref name="planning">{{cite web|url=https://www.planningofficesuriname.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/STRUCTUUR-ANALYSE-IV.pdf|title=Structuur Analyse|website=Planning Office Suriname|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> "Kampong Baroe" in [[Malay language|Malay]] language literally means "New Village" ([[Kampong]]: Village and ''Baru/Baroe'': New). 

Kampong Baroe has a clinic,<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.gfcnieuws.com/uitbreiding-rgd-poli-kampong-baroe/|title=Uitbreiding RGD-poli Kampong Baroe |website=GFC Nieuws|access-date=26 May 2020|language=nl}}</ref> and a school.<ref name="planning"/> On 23 March 2020, construction of a multi functional centre started.<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.suriname.nu/surinamenieuws/updates-bouw-multifunctionele-zaal-kampong-baroe-per-maandag-23-maart-2020/|title=Updates: bouw multifunctionele zaal Kampong Baroe per maandag 23 maart 2020.|website=Suriname.nu|access-date=25 May 2020|language=nl}}{{Dead link|date=August 2025 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>

Other villages located in the resort are [[Boston, Suriname|Boston]], [[Uitkijk]], [[Dam Parra]], [[Totikamp]], and [[Maho, Suriname|Maho]].
