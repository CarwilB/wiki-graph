The '''General López Department''' (in [[Spanish language|Spanish]], '''''Departamento General López''''') is an administrative subdivision (''[[Departments of Argentina|departamento]]'') of the [[provinces of Argentina|province]] of [[Santa Fe Province|Santa Fe]], [[Argentina]]. It is located in the south of the province. Its head town is [[Melincué]] (population 2,200), and its largest city is [[Venado Tuerto]] (population 70,000).

It is bordered by the [[Caseros Department]] in the north, and by the [[Constitución Department, Santa Fe|Constitución Department]] in the north-east; the rest of its borders coincide with interprovincial limits (with [[Córdoba Province (Argentina)|Córdoba]] in the west and with [[Buenos Aires Province|Buenos Aires]] in the south and east).

The towns and cities in this department are (in alphabetical order): [[Aarón Castellanos, Santa Fe|Aarón Castellanos]], [[Amenábar, Argentina|Amenábar]], [[Cafferatta, Santa Fe|Cafferata]], [[Cañada del Ucle]], [[Carmen, Argentina|Carmen]], [[Carreras, Argentina|Carreras]], [[Chapuy]], [[Chovet, Argentina|Chovet]], [[Christophersen, Argentina|Christophersen]], [[Diego de Alvear, Santa Fe|Diego de Alvear]], [[Elortondo]], [[Firmat]], [[Hughes, Argentina|Hughes]], [[La Chispa]], [[Labordeboy]], [[Lazzarino, Argentina|Lazzarino]], [[Maggiolo, Argentina|Maggiolo]], [[María Teresa, Argentina|María Teresa]], Melincué, [[Miguel Torres, Argentina|Miguel Torres]], [[Murphy, Santa Fe|Murphy]], [[Rufino, Santa Fe|Rufino]], [[San Eduardo, Argentina|San Eduardo]], [[San Francisco de Santa Fe]], [[San Gregorio, Argentina|San Gregorio]], [[Sancti Spiritu, Argentina|Sancti Spiritu]], [[Santa Isabel, Argentina|Santa Isabel]], [[Teodelina]], Venado Tuerto, [[Villa Cañás]], [[Wheelwright, Argentina|Wheelwright]].

==Economy==
The General López Department's economy is primarily based on [[intensive farming]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.senadosantafe.gob.ar/departamentos/general-lopez |title=General López |website=[[Senate of Santa Fe Province]] |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> Venado Tuerto, the department's most populous city, is home to the La Victoria Industrial Park, which houses approximately 74 companies in sectors including agroindustry, metalworking, and construction.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.parqueflandria.com.ar/complejo-industrial-venado-tuerto/ |title=Parque Industrial La Victoria: Eje Productivo |website=Parque Flandria |access-date=12 November 2025|language=es}}</ref> Companies operating in the region include Agritec Global, which produces liquid fertilizers.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.infocampo.com.ar/los-fertilizantes-liquidos-una-tendencia-que-crece-y-sumo-una-nueva-inversion-en-venado-tuerto/ |title=Los fertilizantes líquidos, una tendencia que crece y sumó una nueva inversión en Venado Tuerto |website=Infocampo |date=24 August 2025 |access-date=12 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>

In the oilseed sector, Aceitera General Deheza (AGD), owned by [[Roberto Urquía]], operates storage facilities in Rufino, located in the soybean-producing region of southern Santa Fe Province.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.agd.com.ar/es/rufino-acopio |title=Rufino (Acopio) |website=AGD |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref> In June 2022, [[MSU Argentina]], owned by [[Manuel Santos Uribelarrea Balcarce]], announced plans for a peanut processing plant.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.sur24.com.ar/noticias/msu-agro-desembarca-en-rufino-con-una-planta-modelo-procesadora-de-mani |title=Desembarca en Rufino una planta modelo procesadora de maní |website=Sur54 |access-date=12 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://reporteasia.com/noticias/2024/10/31/que-es-msu |title=¿Qué es MSU? El grupo que transforma el agro y la energía en Argentina |last=Lapaglia |first=Felipe |website=Reporte Asia |access-date=12 November 2025|language=es}}</ref>
