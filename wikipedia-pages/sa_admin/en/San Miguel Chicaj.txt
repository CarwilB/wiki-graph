'''San Miguel Chicaj''' ({{IPA|es|ˈsam miˈɣel tʃiˈkax}}) is a town and [[Municipalities of Guatemala|municipality]] in the [[Baja Verapaz]] [[Departments of Guatemala|department]] of [[Guatemala]]. San Miguel Chicaj has an area of 280 [[Km²]], which makes one of the largest municipality of [[Baja Verapaz Department]]. It has a population of 33,131 (2018 census), mostly of achí background.{{sfn|Font|2004|p=}}

== History ==
According to an old local legend, the name "San Miguel Chicaj" came from the time that [[Michael (archangel)|Saint Michael Archangel]] descended from Heaven and landed on the region.

On the Municipality of [[Salamá]] minutes, San Miguel Chicaj was founded on 23 October 1803 and raised to fourth category municipality in 1877, by the government of general [[Justo Rufino Barrios]].{{sfn|Blog de San Miguel Chicaj|2010|p=}}

== Commercial activities ==
San Miguel Chijal is a farming community; its main produces are corn, beans and sugar cane, while its people also works with chickens and livestock, producing eggs, meat and several dairy products.
