'''Viçosa''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[viˈsɔzɐ]}}) is a Brazilian [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] in [[Alagoas]] state. Located inland on the zone of a former portion of the [[Mata Atlântica|Atlantic Rain Forest]], it is 86&nbsp;km away from the state capital [[Maceió]]. Its population was 25,693 (2020)<ref>[https://cidades.ibge.gov.br/brasil/al/vicosa/panorama IBGE 2020]</ref> and its area is 354.76&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[[Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics|IBGE]] - {{cite web|url=http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |title=Archived copy |access-date=11 June 2008 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080611095922/http://www.ibge.gov.br/cidadesat/default.php |archive-date=11 June 2008 }}</ref>
