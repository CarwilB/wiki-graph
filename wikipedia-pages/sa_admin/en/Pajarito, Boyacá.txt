'''Pajarito''' is a town and municipality in the [[La Libertad Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The municipality, situated in the southeast of the department borders [[Labranzagrande]] and [[Aquitania, Boyacá|Aquitania]] of the department of Boyacá and [[Yopal]], [[Recetor]] and [[Aguazul]] of the department of [[Casanare Department|Casanare]]. The urban centre is located at a distance of {{convert|147|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]] at an altitude of {{convert|800|m|ft}} in a tight valley, not allowing much expansion of the urban area. The centre is experiencing periodic landslides.<ref name=websitePajarito>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150530080842/http://www.pajarito-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Pajarito]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Pajarito in [[Spanish language|Spanish]] means "little bird", yet the origin of the name is thought to be from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]: ''Pa'' is "father", ''ja''; "load, role", ''rito''; "dog".<ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Pajarito] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== Geology ==
The area of Pajarito is characterized by an [[intrusive rock|intrusive]] complex into the surrounding [[Lower Cretaceous]] sediments (Macanal Formation). The [[petrology]] of the igneous body is alkaline to sub-alkaline and low in SiO<sub>2</sub>. Secondary minerals are [[apatite]] and hydrothermal [[chlorite]].<ref>Moreno Murillo et al., 2007</ref>

== History ==
The first inhabitants of the village may have been [[indigenous peoples of Colombia|indigenous]] people of the Golconda tribe, short in stature and bandy-legged. The Golcondenses were cultivators of maize and hunters. They spoke a version of Chibcha, giving rise to the name of the town.

Modern Pajarito was founded on January 1, 1853 by soldiers of [[Gran Colombia]]. The first mayors were military men.<ref name=websitePajarito/>

== Economy ==
Pajarito is a rural community centered around [[agriculture]] and [[livestock]] farming. Main agricultural products cultivated in the municipality are [[coffee]], [[maize]], [[sugarcane]], [[yuca]], [[bean]]s and [[potato]]es. Hot springs are present outside the urban centre.<ref name=websitePajarito/>

==Climate==
Pajarito has a [[tropical monsoon climate]] (Am) with moderate to little rainfall from December to March and heavy to very heavy rainfall in the remaining months.
