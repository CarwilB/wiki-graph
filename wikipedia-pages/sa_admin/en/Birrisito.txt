'''Birrisito''' is a [[Districts of Costa Rica|district]] of the [[Paraíso (canton)|Paraíso]] canton, in the [[Cartago Province|Cartago]] province of [[Costa Rica]].<ref name="divadm">{{cite web |language=es |title=Declara oficial para efectos administrativos, la aprobación de la División Territorial Administrativa de la República N°41548-MGP |url=http://www.pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?param1=NRTC&nValor1=1&nValor2=88416&nValor3=115607&param2=1&strTipM=TC&lResultado=1&strSim=simp |website=Sistema Costarricense de Información Jurídica |accessdate=26 September 2020 |date=19 March 2019}}</ref><ref name="dta2017">{{cite book |language=es |title=División Territorial Administrativa de la República de Costa Rica|date=8 March 2017|publisher=Editorial Digital de la Imprenta Nacional|isbn=978-9977-58-477-5|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/editorialdigital/libros/historiaygeografia/division_17.pdf}}</ref>

== History ==
The district was proposed on 13 July 2020 by the deputy Paola Valladares ([[National Liberation Party (Costa Rica)|PLN]]-[[Cartago Province|Cartago]]) under the file 22076 of the Legislative Assembly, which was approved in a second debate on 12 July 2021 and eventually created under Law No.° 10004, signed on 28 July 2021 and published in La Gaceta No.° 158 of 18 August 2021.<ref name="gaceta">{{cite journal|title=La Gaceta N° 158|journal=La Gaceta|date=18 August 2021|number=158|page=2|url=https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/pub/2021/08/18/COMP_18_08_2021.pdf|accessdate=30 December 2021|language=es}}</ref>

Due to the [[2022 Costa Rican general election|general elections of 2022]], the district would be incorporated until the after that event.

It was segregated from [[Paraíso, Costa Rica|Paraíso]], the first district of the canton.

== Geography ==
Birrisito has an area of {{formatnum:7.5}} km<sup>2</sup><ref name="gaceta"/> and an elevation of {{formatnum:1325}} metres.<ref name="divadm" />

It is located 8&nbsp;km southeast of [[Cartago, Costa Rica|Cartago]].

== Demographics ==
As of the [[Costa Rica 2011 Census|2011 census]], the district hasn't been created and its population was part of Paraíso district.<ref name="censo2011">{{cite web |language=es |title=Censo. 2011.
