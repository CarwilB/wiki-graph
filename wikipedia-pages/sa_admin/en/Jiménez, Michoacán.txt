'''Jiménez''' is a [[Municipalities of Michoacán|municipality]] in the [[Mexico|Mexican]] [[List of states of Mexico|state]] of [[Michoacán]]. It is located approximately {{convert|65|km}} west of the state capital of [[Morelia]].

==Geography==
The municipality of Jiménez is located in the [[Trans-Mexican Volcanic Belt]] in northern Michoacán at an elevation between {{convert|1900|and(-)|2500|m}}. It borders the municipalities of [[Puruándiro]] to the north, [[Morelos, Michoacán|Morelos]] to the northeast, [[Huaniqueo]] to the east, [[Coeneo]] to the southeast, [[Zacapu]] to the southwest, and [[Panindícuaro]] to the northwest.<ref name=compendium>{{cite web |url=http://www3.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/app/mexicocifras/datos_geograficos/16/16044.pdf |title=Jiménez, Michoacán de Ocampo |work=Prontuario de información geográfica municipal de los Estados Unidos Mexicanos |language=es |publisher=INEGI |date=2009 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref> The municipality covers an area of {{convert|195.057|km2}}<ref name=infocard/> and comprises 0.33% of the state's area.<ref name=compendium/>

As of 2009, the land cover in Jiménez consists of grassland (16%), temperate forest (10%), [[matorral]] (5%) and tropical forest (4%). Another 56% of the land is used for agriculture and 2% consists of urban areas.<ref name=compendium/> Jiménez is drained by the [[Ángulo River]], a tributary of the [[Lerma River]] which flows south to north through the municipality. There are a few small reservoirs in the municipality, the largest of which are the Aristeo Mercado Reservoir with a capacity of {{convert|27000000|m3}}, and the Copándaro Reservoir with a capacity of {{convert|4000000|m3}}.<ref>{{cite journal |last=Rionda |first=Luis Miguel |year=1986 |title=Agricultura campesina y migración: El impacto de un cultivo comercial en un pueblo de migrantes |journal=Relaciones |language=es |url=https://www.colmich.edu.mx/relaciones25/files/revistas/026/LuisMiguelRionda.pdf |volume=7 |issue=26 |pages=69–93 |access-date=26 December 2020 |archive-date=21 July 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210721161327/https://www.colmich.edu.mx/relaciones25/files/revistas/026/LuisMiguelRionda.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>
===Climate===
Jiménez has a temperate climate with rain in the summer.<ref name=encyclopedia/> Average temperatures in the municipality range between {{convert|16|and(-)|18|C}}, and average annual precipitation ranges between {{convert|800|and(-)|1000|mm}}.<ref name=compendium/>

==History==
In the mid-19th century, stagecoaches running between [[Zamora, Michoacán|Zamora]] and Morelia forded the Ángulo River at what is now Villa de Jiménez. A man named Patrocinio Aguilar lived there and supplied travellers with provisions, so that the place became known as Vado de Aguilar ("Aguilar's Ford"). On May 30, 1910, the settlement was granted the status of a town (''villa'') and renamed after [[Mariano Jiménez]], governor of Michoacán from 1885 to 1892.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |last=Ramírez Sevilla |first=Luis |title=Villa Jiménez en la lente de Martiniano Mendoza |language=es |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=8XlxnRaDsy0C&pg=PA32 |publisher={{ill|El Colegio de Michoacán|es}} |page=32 |year=2002 |isbn=9789706790767 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>

Originally part of the municipality of Zacapu, Jiménez became an independent municipality on 1 May 1921.<ref name=encyclopedia/><ref>{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Michoacán de Ocampo. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995. |pages=81, 138–139 |url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1501-4 |archive-date=2019-04-12 |access-date=2020-12-27 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190412021950/http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/historicos/2104/702825222420/702825222420_5.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref>

==Administration==
The municipal government comprises a president, a councillor ([[Spanish language|Spanish]]: ''síndico''), and seven trustees (''regidores''), four elected by [[relative majority]] and three by [[proportional representation]].<ref name=encyclopedia/> The current president of the municipality is Arturo León Balvanera.<ref>{{cite news |last=Aparicio |first=Beatriz |date=25 September 2018 |url=https://www.alcaldesdemexico.com/notas-principales/configuracion-politica-de-michoacan-2018-2021/ |title=Configuración política de Michoacán 2018-2021 |language=es |magazine=Alcaldes de México |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>

==Demographics==
In the 2010 Mexican Census, the municipality of Jiménez recorded a population of 13,275 inhabitants living in 3851 households.<ref name=catalog>{{cite web |url=http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=044 |title=Resumen municipal: Municipio de Jiménez |language=es |website=Catálogo de Localidades |publisher=SEDESOL |access-date=26 December 2020 |archive-date=21 August 2021 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210821234650/http://www.microrregiones.gob.mx/catloc/LocdeMun.aspx?tipo=clave&campo=loc&ent=16&mun=044 |url-status=dead }}</ref> The 2015 Intercensal Survey estimated a population of 12,426 inhabitants in Jiménez.<ref name=survey/>

There are 25 localities in the municipality,<ref name=snim/> of which only the municipal seat Villa Jiménez is classified as urban. It recorded a population of 4249 inhabitants in the 2010 Census.<ref name=catalog/>

==Economy==
The main economic activity in Jiménez is agriculture. Corn is the main crop, followed by sorghum and forage oats. Chicken, cattle and pigs are also raised.<ref>{{cite news |author=Jiménez Municipal Council |url=http://congresomich.gob.mx/file/5a-6719.pdf |title=Plan de Desarrollo Municipal |language=es |page=20 |newspaper=Periódico Oficial del Estado de Michoacán, 5a. Secc. |publisher=Government of Michoacán |date=18 January 2019 |access-date=26 December 2020}}</ref>
