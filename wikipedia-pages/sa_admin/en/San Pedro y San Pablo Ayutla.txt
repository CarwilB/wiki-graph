'''San Pedro y San Pablo Ayutla''' is a town and [[Municipalities of Oaxaca|municipality]] in [[Oaxaca]] in south-western [[Mexico]]. 
It is part of the [[Sierra Mixe]] district within the [[Sierra Norte de Oaxaca]] Region.<ref name=INF>{{cite web

 |work=Enciclopedia de los Municipios de México

 |accessdate=2010-07-14}}</ref>

==Geography==
The municipality covers an area of {{convert|108.45|km2|abbr=on}} at an altitude of {{convert|2180|m|sp=us}} above sea level. The climate is temperate to cool, with warmer micro-regions in the lowlands and ravines. The rainy season begins in May and ends in October with drizzle in the rest of the year. The average temperature is {{convert|16|C|abbr=on}}, varying from a maximum of {{convert|31|C|abbr=on}} to a minimum of {{convert|2|C|abbr=on}}. Forest cover is pine-oak.<ref name=INF/>
==Population==
As of 2005, the municipality had 1,014 households with a total population of 4,319 of whom 3,639 spoke an indigenous language.
The main economic activity is logging.<ref name=INF/>
