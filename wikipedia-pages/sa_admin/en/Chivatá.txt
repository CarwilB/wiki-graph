'''Chivatá''' is a town and municipality in the [[Central Boyacá Province]], part of the [[Colombia]]n [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Boyacá Department|Boyacá]]. The urban centre is situated at an altitude of {{convert|2903|m|ft}} on the [[Altiplano Cundiboyacense]] at {{convert|5|km|mi}} from the department capital [[Tunja]]. It borders [[Oicatá]], [[Toca, Boyacá|Toca]] and [[Tuta, Boyacá|Tuta]] in the north, Toca and [[Siachoque]] in the east, Siachoque and [[Soracá]] in the south and Tunja in the west.<ref name=websiteChivata>{{in lang|es}} [https://archive.today/20150529032900/http://www.chivata-boyaca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Chivatá]</ref>

== Etymology ==
The name Chivatá is derived from the [[Chibcha language]] of the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] who inhabited the central highlands of Colombia before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca|Spanish conquest]] in the 1530s. It means "Our outside farmfields".<ref name=websiteChivata/><ref name=excelsio>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.excelsio.net/2011/11/sabe-el-origen-del-nombre-de-su.html Etymology Chivatá] - Excelsio.net</ref>

== History ==
Chivatá was ruled by a ''[[cacique]]'' named Chipatá, loyal to the ''[[zaque]]'' of nearby [[Tunja|Hunza]] before the arrival of the Spanish [[conquistador]]es. Modern Chivatá was founded on March 5, 1556.<ref name=websiteChivata/>
