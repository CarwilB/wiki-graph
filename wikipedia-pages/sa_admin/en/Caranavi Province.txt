'''Caranavi Province''' is one of the twenty provinces of the [[Bolivia]]n [[La Paz Department, Bolivia|La Paz Department]] and is situated in the department's eastern parts. The province was created by Law 1401 on 16 December 1992 from a portion of what was then [[Nor Yungas Province]].<ref name=Ley1401>{{cite web|title=Ley Nº 1401 de 16 de diciembre de 1992|url=http://www.lexivox.org/norms/BO-L-1401.xhtml|work=Lexivox}}</ref> The creation of the province had been a local cause embraced by Ramiro Revuelta, a Deputy in the national legislature who was assassinated on November 28, 1992.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.eldiario.net/noticias/2014/2014_12/nt141216/nuevoshorizontes.php?n=107&|title=Caranavi de tercera sección a provincia|date=2014-12-16|work=El Diario|access-date=2018-01-20}}</ref>

== Location ==
''Caranavi Province'' is located between 15° 20' and 16° 03' [[Latitude|South]] and between 67° 07' and 67° 42' [[Longitude|West]]. It extends over 55&nbsp;km from West to East, and 75&nbsp;km from North to South.

The province is situated on the Bolivian [[Altiplano]] east of [[Lake Titicaca]], on the headwaters of [[Beni River|Río Beni]], and borders [[Larecaja Province]] and in the Northwest, [[Pedro Domingo Murillo Province]] in the Southwest, [[Nor Yungas Province]] in the South, and [[Sud Yungas Province]] in the East and Northeast.

== Population ==
The population of Caranavi Province has increased by circa 40% over the recent two decades:
*1992: 43,093 inhabitants ([[census]])<ref>[http://www.santacruz.gov.bo/websites/provincias/ichilo.html Santa Cruz Website] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081202105420/http://www.santacruz.gov.bo/websites/provincias/ichilo.html |date=2008-12-02 }}</ref>
*2001: 51,153 inhabitants ([[census]])
*2005: 56,167 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070825045345/http://www.ine.gov.bo/pdf/boletin/np_2005_75.pdf |date=August 25, 2007 }}</ref>
*2010: 59,090 inhabitants (est.)<ref>[http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosSantaCruz.pdf Instituto Nacional de Estadística - Proyecciones] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081118162334/http://www.ine.gov.bo/PDF/DIFD/ProyeccionesPoblacionProvinciasMunicipiosSantaCruz.pdf |date=2008-11-18 }}</ref>

40.6% of the population are younger than 15 years old. ''(1992)''

The [[literacy rate]] of the population is 83.1%. ''(1992)''

92.7% of the population speak [[Spanish Language|Spanish]], 71.6% speak [[Aymara language|Aymara]], and 11.1% [[Quechua languages|Quechua]]. ''(1992)''

88.7% of the population have no access to electricity, 65.6% have no sanitary facilities. ''(1992)''

68.0% of the population are Catholics, 22.9% are Protestants. ''(1992)''

== Division ==
Since 23 December 2009, the province is divided into two [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipalities]]<ref>[http://www.bolivia.com/noticIAS/AutoNoticias/DetalleNoticia42293.asp www.bolivia.com] (Spanish)</ref><ref>[http://www.planguarani.com/noticias/pagina.php?sip=246 www.planguarani.com] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110715092055/http://www.planguarani.com/noticias/pagina.php?sip=246 |date=2011-07-15 }} (Spanish)</ref><ref>[http://www.enlared.org.bo/portal/default.asp?cg2=6543 www.enlared.org.bo] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100107231401/http://www.enlared.org.bo/portal/default.asp?cg2=6543 |date=January 7, 2010 }} (Spanish)</ref><ref>Alto Beni municipality was created by Law 4131 on 23 December 2009. {{cite news|title=Alto Beni nace como municipio de Caranavi|url=http://www.cambio.bo/noticia.php?fecha=2010-01-05&idn=12910|access-date=17 September 2010|newspaper=Cambio|date=January 5, 2010|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110706150403/http://www.cambio.bo/noticia.php?fecha=2010-01-05&idn=12910|archive-date=6 July 2011}}</ref> which are further subdivided into 21 cantons.
