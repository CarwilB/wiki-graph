'''Nossa Senhora Aparecida''' ({{IPA|<small>Central northeastern portuguese pronunciation: </small>[ˈnɔsɐ sĩˈj̃ɔɾɐ ɐpɐɾiˈsidɐ]}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 8,809 (2020) and its area is {{convert|341|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Nossa Senhora Aparecida has a population density of 26 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located {{convert|99.1|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/>
