'''Acapulco de Juárez''' is one of the 81 [[municipalities of Guerrero]], in southwestern [[Mexico]], along [[Acapulco Bay]] on the Pacific coast, being both the most populous and most densely populated municipality in Guerrero. The municipal seat lies at [[Acapulco]]. 

Forty percent of the municipality is mountainous terrain; another forty percent is semi-flat; and the other twenty percent is flat. Altitude varies from sea level to {{convert|1699|m|ft|0|abbr=off}}. The highest peaks are Potrero, San Nicolás, and Alto Camarón.

One major river runs through the municipality, the [[Papagayo River|Papagayo]], along with a number of ''arroyos'' (streams). There are also two small lagoons, Tres Palos and Coyuca, along with a number of thermal springs.<ref>{{cite map |author1=Comisión de estudios del territorio nacional (CETENAL) |author2=Instituto nacional de estadística geografía e informática (INEGI) |year=1991 |title=Acapulco: Guerrero |edition=Primera |scale=1:50,000 |publisher=CETENAL, INEGI |oclc=494416658}}</ref>

It was incorporated on {{dts|August 6, 1824}}, as part of the [[State of Mexico]], and became part of the new state of [[Guerrero]] upon its creation on {{dts|October 27, 1849}}. ''De Juárez'' was officially added to its name on {{dts|June 20, 1873}}.<ref name="Incorporationdate">{{cite book |date=1996 |title=Estado de Guerrero. División Territorial de 1810 a 1995.|url=http://internet.contenidos.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/integracion/pais/divi_terri/1810-1985/gro/GUERRERO.pdf|language=es |location=Mexico |publisher=INEGI |isbn=970-13-1491-3|access-date=February 2, 2021}}</ref>

As of 2020, the municipality had a total population of 779,566,<ref name="census2020">{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/scitel/Default?ev=9|title=Censo de Población y Vivienda 2020 - SCITEL|language=es|publisher=INEGI|access-date=January 27, 2021}}</ref> covering an area of {{convert|1727.3|km2|sqmi}}.<ref name="Landarea">
{{cite web|url=https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/areasgeograficas/default.aspx|title=México en cifras - Medio Ambiente - Guerrero|publisher=INEGI|language= es| access-date=February 12, 2021}}</ref>

Its current [[municipal president]] is Abelina López Rodríguez, from the [[Morena (political party)|Morena]] political party.{{cn|date=October 2023}}
