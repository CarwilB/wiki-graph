'''Pacho''' is a municipality and town of [[Colombia]] in the [[Departments of Colombia|department]] of [[Cundinamarca Department|Cundinamarca]]. Pacho is part of the [[Rionegro Province]] and the urban centre is situated at a distance of {{convert|88|km|mi}} from the capital [[Bogotá]] at an altitude of {{convert|2136|m|ft}}, while the altitude ranges from {{convert|1000|m|ft}} to {{convert|3700|m|ft}}. The municipality borders [[San Cayetano, Cundinamarca|San Cayetano]], [[Villagómez]] and [[Topaipí]] in the north, [[Supatá]] and [[Subachoque]] in the south, [[Vergara, Cundinamarca|Vergara]] and [[El Peñón, Cundinamarca|El Peñón]] in the west and in the east [[Zipaquirá]], [[Tausa]] and [[Cogua]].<ref name=websitePacho>{{in lang|es}} [http://www.pacho-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml Official website Pacho] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20150924063041/http://www.pacho-cundinamarca.gov.co/informacion_general.shtml |date=2015-09-24 }}</ref>

== Etymology ==
In the times before the [[Spanish conquest of the Muisca]], Pacho was called ''Guataque'' or ''Gotaque''; ''Gua''; "mountain", ''Tha''; "strong" and ''Que''; "elevated", so "strong elevated mountain". The modern name is also derived from [[Chibcha language|Chibcha]]; ''Pa'' is "father" and ''chó'' is "good"; "good father".<ref name=websitePacho/><ref>* {{cite book| author=Espejo Olaya, Maria Bernarda | year=1999 | url=http://cvc.cervantes.es/lengua/thesaurus/pdf/54/TH_54_003_424_0.pdf | title=Notas sobre toponimia en algunas coplas colombianas |trans-title=Notes about toponomy of some Colombian ballads - Thesaurus |volume=Tomo LIV, Núm. 3. | page=1121 | language=es}}</ref>

== History ==
The area of Pacho before becoming part of the [[New Kingdom of Granada]] was inhabited by the [[Muisca people|Muisca]] where the area of Pacho formed the western boundary of the [[Muisca Confederation]], bordering the [[Muzo people|Muzo]] in the northwest and the [[Panche people|Panche or Colima]] in the west and southwest.

Modern Pacho was founded on August 25, 1604, by Lorencio de Terrones.<ref name=websitePacho/>

==Climate==
Pacho has a [[subtropical highland climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]]: ''Cfb'')<ref>{{Cite journal |date=23 October 2023 |title=Table 1 Overview of the Köppen-Geiger climate classes including the defining criteria. |url=https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02549-6/tables/1 |journal=Nature: Scientific Data |language=en}}</ref> with consistently mild temperatures.

== Economy ==
Pacho was already an [[Muisca agriculture|agricultural]] area in the times of the Muisca. Today the main agricultural products are [[coffee]], [[orange (fruit)|oranges]], [[strawberry|strawberries]], [[papaya]]s, [[pineapple]]s, [[banana]]s, [[Cooking banana|plantain]]s, [[potato]]es, [[yuca]] and flowers. The town also as an important [[dairy]] farming industry.<ref name=websitePacho/>
