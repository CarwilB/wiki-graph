'''Buroz''' is one of the 21 [[Municipalities of Venezuela|municipalities]] ([[municipio]]s) that makes up the [[Venezuela]]n state of [[Miranda (state)|Miranda]] and, according to a 2011 population estimate by the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela, the municipality has a population of 27,515.<ref>{{Cite book|page=56 |title=IXV Censo Nacional de Población y Vivienda |lang=es|trans-title= XIV National Census of Population and Housing |url=https://ine.gob.ve/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Censo-Nacional-2011.pdf  |publisher= Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela, National Institute of Statistics|date= May 2014|access-date=November 6, 2025}}</ref> The town of [[Mamporal]] is the [[municipal seat]] of the Buroz Municipality.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Alcaldía del Municipio Bolivariano Buroz |url=https://alcaldias.com/alcaldias-de-venezuela/alcaldias-del-estado-miranda/alcaldia-del-municipio-buroz/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251104193111/https://alcaldias.com/alcaldias-de-venezuela/alcaldias-del-estado-miranda/alcaldia-del-municipio-buroz/ |archive-date=2025-11-04 |access-date=2025-11-04 |work=Alcaldias.com |language=es-ES}}</ref>

==Demographics==
Venezuela has not collected or reported complete population statistics since 2011.<ref>{{Cite news |date=2023-06-22 |title=Venezuela's New Trick For Killing Democracy: Make Official Statistics "Disappear" |url=https://worldcrunch.com/world-affairs/venezuela-corruption-statistics/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230705203422/https://worldcrunch.com/world-affairs/venezuela-corruption-statistics/ |archive-date=2023-07-05 |access-date=2025-11-04 |work=Worldcrunch |language=en}}</ref> In 2019, President Maduro directed the National Institute of Statistics of Venezuela to conduct the census. <ref>{{Cite web |title=The 2020 round of population and housing censuses in Latin America and the  Caribbean amid the pandemic |url=https://repositorio.cepal.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/ca9cdde8-b156-4e26-b5ec-306fd8e02862/content |archive-url= |archive-date= |access-date=2025-11-04 |website=repositorio.cepal.org}}</ref> However, only about 16% of the population was counted.

==Government==
The mayor of the Buroz Municipality in 2004 was Ramón Gómez Serrano, re-elected on October 31 with 68% of the vote.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Alcalde de Municipio |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=20&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20160303180745/http://www.cne.gob.ve/regionales2004/13/alcaldedis=0&edo=13&mun=20&par=0&cen=0&mesa=0&cua=0.html |archive-date=2016-03-03}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |title=ELECCIONES 30 DE JULIO DE 2000, DIRECTORIO DE ALCALDES ELECTOS |url=http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20061002063906/http://www.cne.gob.ve/estadisticas/e016.pdf |archivedate=2006-10-02 |accessdate=2008-03-15}}</ref>  The municipality is divided into one [[parish]] (Mamporal).<ref name="autogenerated1">{{cite web |title=Archived copy |url=http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |url-status=dead |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20071221102920/http://www.ine.gob.ve/secciones/division/Miranda.zip |archivedate=2007-12-21 |accessdate=2008-03-22}}</ref>
