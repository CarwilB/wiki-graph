'''El Tablón''' is a town and municipality in the [[Nariño Department]], [[Colombia]]. 
It contains "mystical stone statues" from the pre-Columbian era<ref>https://www.megalithic.co.uk/article.php?sid=28054</ref><ref>{{cite web | url=http://www.globespots.com/places.php?continentID=0&countryID=53&subjectID=0&categoryID=0/ | title=Redirecting }}</ref> and other archaeological sites. The [[Aponte Indigenous Reserve]] is located within the municipality.<ref>{{Cite web |date=22 March 2015 |title=Donde se encuentra el resguardo indígena de aponte |trans-title=Where is the Aponte indigenous reservation located? |url=https://cocupo.com/donde-se-encuentra-el-resguardo-indigena-de-aponte/ |access-date=25 August 2024 |website=Cocupo |language=es}}</ref>
