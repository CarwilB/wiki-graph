'''Areia Branca''' ({{IPA|pt-BR|aˈɾejɐ ˈbɾɐ̃kɐ}}, {{Translation|White Sand}}) is a [[Municipalities of Brazil|municipality]] located in the [[States of Brazil|Brazilian state]] of [[Sergipe]]. Its population was 18,686 (2020) and it covers {{convert|148.13|km2|mi2|abbr=on}}. Areia Branca has a population density of 120 inhabitants per square kilometer. It is located {{convert|36|km|mi|abbr=on}} from the state capital of Sergipe, [[Aracaju]].<ref name="i"/> The municipality contains part of the [[Serra de Itabaiana National Park]].<ref name="h"/>
