'''San José del General''' is a [[Corregimientos of Panama|corregimiento]] in [[Omar Torrijos Herrera District]], [[Colón Province]], [[Panama]] with a population of 2,248 as of 2010.<ref name=inec/> Its population as of 1990 was 1,422; its population as of 2000 was 1,207.<ref name=inec/>

On February 20, 2018 San José del General was separated from [[Donoso District]] to form the new Omar Torrijos Herrera District. The new district is divided into three corregimientos. One of these is still called San José del General, while the others are called San Juan de Turbe and Nueva Esperanza.<ref name=Gaceta>{{Cite web|url=https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/28469_B/66175.pdf|title=Ley 11 del 20 de febrero de 2018|author=Gaceta Oficial de Panamá|date=February 21, 2013}}</ref>
