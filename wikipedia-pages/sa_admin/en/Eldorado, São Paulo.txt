'''Eldorado''' ('''Xiririca''' before 1948<ref name=hist>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/11FB IBGE], history</ref>) is a municipality in the state of [[São Paulo (state)|São Paulo]] in [[Brazil]]. The population is 15,544 (2020 est.) in an area of 1654&nbsp;km<sup>2</sup>.<ref>[http://cod.ibge.gov.br/2Z4 Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística]</ref> Eldorado is one of 29 cities in São Paulo officially recognized as a touristic city, which gives it extra funding.

==Geography==
Eldorado is situated on the [[Ribeira de Iguape River]]. The state highways [[SP-165]] and [[SP-193]] pass through the town.

The municipality contains part of the {{convert|488865|ha|adj=on}} [[Serra do Mar Environmental Protection Area]], created in 1984.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA da Serra do Mar – ISA}}|language=pt
 |title=APA da Serra do Mar (APA)|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/595509|access-date=2016-12-08}}</ref>
It contains part of the {{convert|41704|ha|adj=on}} [[Intervales State Park]], created in 1995.<ref>{{citation|language=pt|page=33|last1=Furlan|last2=Leite|first1=Sueli Ângelo|first2=Sandra Aparecida|year=2008|title=Parque Estadual Intervales: Plano de Manejo|chapter=Resumo Executivo|publisher=Fundação Florestal|url=http://fflorestal.sp.gov.br/files/2012/01/2.%20Resumo%20Executivo/pag%2001_134%20RESUMO%20EXECUTIVO.pdf|access-date=2016-12-06}}{{Dead link|date=March 2024 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }}</ref>
It contains part of the {{convert|40175|ha|adj=on}} [[Caverna do Diabo State Park]], created in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|Parque Estadual Caverna do Diabo – MMA}}|language=pt
 |title=Parque Estadual Caverna do Diabo|publisher=MMA: Ministério do Meio Ambiente
 |url=http://sistemas.mma.gov.br/portalcnuc/rel/index.php?fuseaction=portal.exibeUc&idUc=1675|access-date=2016-11-21}}</ref>
This park contains the largest cave in São Paulo, [[Caverna da Tapagem|Caverna do Diabo]] (The Devil's Cave).
The municipality contains 41% of the {{convert|64625|ha|adj=on}} [[Quilombos do Médio Ribeira Environmental Protection Area]], established in 2008.<ref>{{citation|ref={{harvid|APA dos Quilombos do Médio Ribeira – ISA}}
 |title=APA dos Quilombos do Médio Ribeira|publisher=ISA: Instituto Socioambiental
 |url=https://uc.socioambiental.org/uc/593131|access-date=2016-11-21}}</ref>

== Media ==
In telecommunications, the city was served by [[:pt:Companhia_de_Telecomunicações_do_Estado_de_São_Paulo|Companhia de Telecomunicações do Estado de São Paulo]] until 1975, when it began to be served by [[:pt:Telecomunicações_de_São_Paulo|Telecomunicações de São Paulo]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.imprensaoficial.com.br/DO/BuscaDO2001Documento_11_4.aspx?link=/1973/ineditoriais/abril/24/p1/pag_0054_DUI6O3SN7V5CNe7E7ANSRTTG04F.pdf&pagina=54&data=24/04/1973&caderno=Ineditoriais&paginaordenacao=100054|title=Creation of Telesp - April 12, 1973|access-date=2024-05-26|website=www.imprensaoficial.com.br|language=Portuguese}}</ref> In July 1998, this company was acquired by Telefónica, which adopted the [[Vivo (telecommunications company)|Vivo]] brand in 2012.<ref name=":vivo" />

The company is currently an operator of cell phones, fixed lines, internet (fiber optics/4G) and television (satellite and cable).<ref name=":vivo">{{Cite web|url=https://ri.telefonica.com.br/en/the-company/our-history/|title=Our History - Telefônica RI|access-date=2024-05-26|website=Telefônica}}</ref>
