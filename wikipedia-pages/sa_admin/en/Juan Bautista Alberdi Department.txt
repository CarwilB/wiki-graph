'''Juan Bautista Alberdi Department''' is a [[Departments of Argentina|department]] in [[Tucumán Province]], [[Argentina]]. It has a population of 28,206 (2001) and an area of 730&nbsp;km². The seat of the department is in [[Juan Bautista Alberdi, Tucumán|Juan Bautista Alberdi]]. The department is named after [[Juan Bautista Alberdi]].
