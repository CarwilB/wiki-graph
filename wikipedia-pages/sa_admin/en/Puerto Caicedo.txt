'''Puerto Caicedo''' ({{IPA|es|ˈpweɾto kajˈseðo}}) is a town and municipality located in the [[Putumayo Department]], [[Republic of Colombia]].

==Climate==
Puerto Caicedo has a [[tropical rainforest climate]] ([[Köppen climate classification|Köppen]] ''Af'') with heavy to very heavy rainfall year-round.
