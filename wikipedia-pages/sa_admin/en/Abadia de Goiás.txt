'''Abadia de Goiás''' is a [[municipality]] in central [[Goiás]] state, [[Brazil]], located on the western edge of the [[Goiânia]] metropolitan area.<ref>{{Cite web|last=Latitude.to|title=GPS coordinates of Abadia de Goiás, Brazil. Latitude: -16.7587 Longitude: -49.4355|url=https://latitude.to/articles-by-country/br/brazil/218192/abadia-de-goias|access-date=2020-10-06|website=Latitude.to, maps, geolocated articles, latitude longitude coordinate conversion.|language=en}}</ref> The residues from the [[Goiânia incident]] are stored within its territory and, according to the [[National Nuclear Energy Commission]], do not present any level of [[radioactivity]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Carvalho |first=Humberta |url=http://g1.globo.com/goias/noticia/2012/09/deposito-de-rejeitos-do-cesio-137-em-abadia-de-goias-foi-alvo-de-polemica.html |title=Depósito de rejeitos do césio-137 em Abadia de Goiás foi alvo de polêmica |publisher=G1 |date=2012-09-13 |accessdate=2021-10-09 |website=Goiás |lang=pt-br}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Alves |first=Renato |url=https://www.correiobraziliense.com.br/app/noticia/ciencia-e-saude/2011/03/18/interna_ciencia_saude,243245/abadia-de-goias-abriga-ha-24-anos-lixo-radioativo-do-desastre-com-cesio-137.shtml |title=Abadia de Goiás abriga há 24 anos lixo radioativo do desastre com césio 137 |publisher=Correio Braziliense |date=2011-03-18 |accessdate=2021-10-09 |website=Acervo |lang=pt-BR}}</ref>

==Geographical Data==
The distance to [[Goiânia]] is 27&nbsp;km. and highway connections are made by BR-060.

Neighboring municipalities are:
*north and east:  [[Trindade, Goiás|Trindade]]
*northwest:  [[Goiânia]]
*east:  [[Guapó]]
*south:  [[Aragoiânia]]

==Demographic and Political Data==
*Population density: 40.07 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup> (2007)
*Urban population: 3,963 (2007)
*Rural population: 1,905 (2007)
*Eligible voters in 2007: 4683
*City government in 2005: mayor (Antomar Moreira dos Santos), vice-mayor (Maria Lúcia das Graças Matias), and 09 councilpersons
*Households: 1,398 (2000)
*Households earning less than 01 minimum salary: 872 (2000)

==Economy==
The economy is based on services, government jobs, small industries, cattle raising, poultry, and agriculture.

===Main Enterprises===
*agriculture: 4 units employing 42 workers
*transformation industry: 13 units employing 53 workers
*construction: 7 units employing 21 workers
*commerce: 57 units employing 142 workers
*real estate: 31 units employing 62 workers
*education: 3 units employing 12 workers
*health: 4 units employing 07 workers
*public administration: 3 units employing 168 workers
([[IBGE]] 2003)

The cattle herd consisted of 17,460 head, of which 1,930 were milking cows (2006).  The main agricultural products were rice, manioc, and corn.

==Health and education==
*Infant mortality rate in 2000: 27.70 (28.50 in 1990)
*Literacy rate in 2000: 89.2
There were no hospitals in 2007.  There were 06 schools with an enrollment of 1,810.<ref>[http://www.ibge.gov.br/ IBGE]</ref>

[[Human Development Index]]:  0.742
*State ranking:  101 (out of 242 municipalities)
*National ranking:  2,112 (out of 5,507 municipalities)
