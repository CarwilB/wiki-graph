'''Yuty''' is a [[town]] in the [[Caazapá Department]] of [[Paraguay]]. It is located 98km to the south of the department capital [[Caazapá|Caazapa]] and 330km to the South East of the national capital [[Asunción|Asuncion]]. 

Its name origin has two theories. The first claims it comes from the [[Guarani language|Guarani]] terms ''yu'' + ''ty'' (place of thorns). The second asserts that as the place used to be a reunion site and the natives when asked where they were coming from responded. "Ayu aty hagui" which means "I come from the meeting place" the contraction of that phrase supposedly evolved into the current name Yuty.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |title=Yuty Tierra del Itakuru |url=https://www.municipios.gov.py/yuty/ |access-date=September 18, 2024 |website=Gobernaciones y Municipios}}</ref>

It was founded on October 4, 1611 by [[Spain|Spanish]] missionary [[Luis de Bolaños]] with the name of San Francisco de Yuty (''Saint Francis of Yuty)''<ref name=":0" />

The main local crops are cotton and rice.<ref name=":0" />
