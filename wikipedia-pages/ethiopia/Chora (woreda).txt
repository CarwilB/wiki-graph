{{Short description|Woreda or district of Ethiopia}}
'''Chora''' is one of the [[woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Illubabor Zone]], Chora is bordered on the south by the [[Jimma Zone]], on the west by [[Yayu (woreda)|Yayu]], on the northwest by [[Supena Sodo]], on the north by [[Dega (woreda)|Dega]], and on the east by [[Bedele (woreda)|Bedele]]. The major town in Chora is [[Kumbabe]].

[[Coffee]] is an important cash crop of this woreda. Over 50 square kilometers are planted with this crop.<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20160815124929/http://www.oromiacoffeeunion.org/production.htm "Coffee Production"]}} Oromia Coffee Cooperative Union website</ref>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 100,506, of whom 49,784 were men and 50,722 were women; 7,715 or 7.68% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 61.1% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 25.11% practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 13.66% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 318,483, of whom 160,504 are men and 157,979 are women; 9,545 or 8.06% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 12%. With an estimated area of 947.19 square kilometers, Chora has an estimated population density of 125.1 people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of 72.3.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061123000000/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=November 23, 2006 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 84,617 in 18,027 households, of whom 41,558 were men and 43,059 women; 5,337 or 6.31% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Chora were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (86.74%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (10.52%), and the [[Tigrayans|Tigrayan]] (1.87%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.87% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]] was spoken as a first language by 88.07%, 9.82% [[Amharic language|Amharic]], and 1.71% [[Tigrinya language|Tigrinya]]; the remaining 0.4% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 73.88% of the population reporting they practiced that belief, while 21.24% of the population said they embraced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 3.98% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=November 15, 2009 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009)</ref>
