'''Guba Koricha''' ([[Oromo language|Oromo]]: ''Gubbaa Qorichaa'') is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|zone woreda]] in [[Oromia Regional State]], [[Ethiopia]].  Located in the [[West Hararghe Zone]], [[Guba Koricha, ]].according to the [[OCHA]] map (2005) is bordered on the south by [[Darolebu]], on the southwest by the [[Arsi Zone]], on the west by the [[Afar Region]], on the north by [[Mieso, Oromia (woreda)|Mieso]], on the northeast by [[Chiro (woreda)|Chiro]], on the east by [[Habro]], and on the southeast by [[Boke (woreda)|Boke]].<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20080530003828/http://www.ocha-eth.org/Maps/downloadables/OROMIYA.pdf Map of Somali Region at UN-OCHA]}} ([[Portable Document Format|PDF]] file)</ref> Woreda of [[Anchar]] was separated from Guba Koricha.

Although [[coffee]] is an important cash crop of this District, less than 20 square kilometers are planted with this crop.<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20160815124929/http://www.oromiacoffeeunion.org/production.htm "Coffee Production"]}} Oromia Coffee Cooperative Union website</ref>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported this District's total population as 122,335, of whom 62,633 were men and 59,702 were women; 2,875 or 2.35% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants (93.26%) said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], while 6.43% of the population practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this district has an estimated total population of 192,846, of whom 94,136 were males and 98,710 were females; 7,033 or 3.65% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 9.6%. With an estimated area of 1,477.66 square kilometers, Guba Koricha has an estimated population density of 130.5 people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of 101.8.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061123121716/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=2006-11-23 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this District of 139,198, of whom 71,625 were men and 67,573 women; 3,930 or 2.82% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Guba Koricha were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (81.52%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (11.86%) and the [[Argobba people|Argobba]] (6.16%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.46% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromo]] was spoken as a first language by 80.49%, and 19.06% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 0.45% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 86.11% of the population reporting they practiced that belief, while 12.02% of the population said they professed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]] and 1.8% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=2009-11-15 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009)</ref>
