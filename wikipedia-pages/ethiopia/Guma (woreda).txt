{{Short description|Woreda or district of Ethiopia}}
'''Guma''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of [[Gomma (woreda)|Gomma]] woreda. The administrative center of this woreda is [[Toba, Ethiopia|Toba]].

== Population ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 60,490, of whom 30,284 were men and 30,206 were women; 5,269 or 8.71% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 94.97% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 4.37% of the population said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
