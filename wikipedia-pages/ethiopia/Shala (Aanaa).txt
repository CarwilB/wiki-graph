'''Shala''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|Aanaa]]s in the [[Oromia]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was separated from [[Seraro]] woreda. Part of the [[West Arsi Zone]] located in the [[Great Rift Valley, Ethiopia|Great Rift Valley]], Shala is bordered on the south by [[Seraro]], on the west by the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities and Peoples' Region]], on the north by [[Shala Lake]] which separates it from [[Arsi Negele (woreda)|Arsi Negele]], and on the east by [[Naannawa Shashamane (woreda)|Naannawa Shashamane]]; its western boundary is defined by the course of the [[Bilate River]]. The administrative center of this woreda is [[Aje, Ethiopia|Aje]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 149,804, of whom 74,930 were men and 74,874 were women; 7,680 or 5.13% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 94.81% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 2.5% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], and 2.12% of the population practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
