{{Short description|District in Oromia, Ethiopia}}
'''Sinana''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Sinanana Dinsho]] woreda. It is part of the [[Bale Zone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 118,594, of whom 61,968 were men and 56,626 were women; none of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 59.99% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 38.93% of the population practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
