'''Gewane''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Afar Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 3 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 3]], Gewane is bordered on the south by [[Amibara]], on the west by [[Bure Mudaytu]] and [[Administrative Zone 5 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 5]], on the north by the [[Administrative Zone 1 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 1]], and on the east by the [[Somali Region]]; the [[Awash River]] defines parts of the boundary with Administrative Zone 5. The administrative centre is [[Gewane]]; other towns in Gewane include [[Meteka]].

The highest point in this woreda is [[Mount Ayalu]] (2145 m); other important peaks include [[Mount Yangudi]]. Bodies of water include [[Lake Kadabassa]], which lies in the swampy lowlands that stretch alongside the Awash and serve as an important pasture for nomadic pastoralists.<ref>[http://www.africa.upenn.edu/eue_web/Afar0905.htm "Afar Pastoralists Face Consequences of Poor Rains"] UNDP Emergencies Unit for Ethiopia report, dated May 2000 (accessed 29 December 2008)</ref> {{As of|2008}}, Ayesha has 56 kilometers of all-weather gravel road and 45&nbsp;km of community roads; about 41% of the total population has access to drinking water.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, [http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf ''Baseline Survey of 55 Weredas of PCDP Phase II, Part I''] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151420/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf |date=July 20, 2011 }} (Addis Ababa: August 2008), Annexes 16, 17</ref> A notable local landmark is the [[Yangudi Rassa National Park]], which occupies the northeastern corner of the woreda. There are known [[diatomite]] deposits near the village of [[Adamitulu]], but these have not yet been developed.<ref>[http://www.geology.gov.et/current%20exploration%20and%20mining.html Current Exploration and Mining] {{webarchive|url=https://archive.today/20070422062521/http://www.geology.gov.et/current%20exploration%20and%20mining.html |date=2007-04-22 }}, Geological Survey of Ethiopia Web Page (accessed 26 January 2009)</ref> An archeological site near the village of [[Aramis, Ethiopia|Aramis]] in this woreda produced remains of ''[[Australopithecus]]''.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 31,318, of whom 17,171 are men and 14,147 women; with an area of 967.85 square km, Gewane has a population density of 32.36. While 5,986 or 19.11% are urban inhabitants, a further 1,280 or 4.09% are pastoralists. A total of 6,191 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 5.1 persons to a household, and 6,708 housing units. 92.87% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 6.42% were [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity|Orthodox Christians]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
