{{Short description|District located in Oromia state of Ethiopia}}
'''Dugda Dawa''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]]s in the [[Oromia]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Hagere Mariam (woreda)|Bule Hora]] woreda. It is part of the [[Borena Zone|West Guji Zone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 147,327, of whom 75,114 were men and 72,213 were women; 5,560 or 3.77% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], with 69.89% of the population reporting they observed these beliefs, while 22.3% of the population practiced traditional beliefs, 5.06% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]] and 1.15% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
