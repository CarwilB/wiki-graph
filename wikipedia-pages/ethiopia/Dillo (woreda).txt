'''Dillo''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]] of [[Borena Zone]] in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]].<ref name=rispa2013>{{Cite web|url=http://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/drought/docs/REPORT%20OF%20TRADITIONAL%20COMMUNITY%20INSTITUTIONS.PDF|title=Report of the Traditional / Community Institutions Roles and Opportunities for Engagement in the planning, Programming and Investment process and Actions.|website=RISPA|access-date=12 June 2018|url-status=dead|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180612210744/http://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/drought/docs/REPORT%20OF%20TRADITIONAL%20COMMUNITY%20INSTITUTIONS.PDF|archive-date=2018-06-12|date=September 2013}}</ref>

{{As of|2013}}, the population was estimated at 43,853 (21,543 female and 22,310 male) with 97% rural and 3% urban. The dominant climate condition is arid and the population relies on hand-dug shallow wells and ponds for water sources. The residents engage in livestock production, predominantly cattle, sheep and goats, but also equines, camel and poultry.<ref name=rispa2013 />
