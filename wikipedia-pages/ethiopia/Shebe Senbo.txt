{{Short description|Woreda or district of Ethiopia}}
'''Shebe Senbo''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of [[Seka Chekorsa (woreda)|Seka Chekorsa]] woreda. The major town is Shebe.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 112,068, of whom 56,737 were men and 55,331 were women; 5,265 or 4.7% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 76.83% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 21.26% of the population said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 1.77% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
