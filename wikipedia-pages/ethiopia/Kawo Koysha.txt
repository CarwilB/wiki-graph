'''Kawo Koysha''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woredas]] in the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]] of Ethiopia and part of the [[Wolayita Zone]]. Kawo Koysha is bordered on the south by [[Offa (woreda)|Offa]] woreda, on the west by the [[Kindo Didaye]], on the north and east by [[Kindo Koysha]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://codeaddis.com/wash-social-kindo-didaye-kawo-koysha-program-officer-assistant-at-inter-aide-france?__cf_chl_managed_tk__=pmd_upDW7DigcOzujXxUjGxgORXQeg0gLp9i6i.k6FRDhtQ-1631010291-0-gqNtZGzNAyWjcnBszQil|title=Wash Social Kindo Didaye – Kawo Koysha Program Officer Assistant at Inter Aide France|accessdate=7 September 2021|date=27 November 2020|publisher=Code Addis|archive-date=7 September 2021|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210907102603/https://codeaddis.com/wash-social-kindo-didaye-kawo-koysha-program-officer-assistant-at-inter-aide-france?__cf_chl_managed_tk__=pmd_upDW7DigcOzujXxUjGxgORXQeg0gLp9i6i.k6FRDhtQ-1631010291-0-gqNtZGzNAyWjcnBszQil|url-status=dead}}</ref> Kawo Koysha woreda was established in 2019 from the surrounding woredas.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23|title=Weredas&#124;&#124;Towns &#124; Wolaita Zone Administrations|access-date=2021-09-07|archive-date=2023-03-01|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230301123037/http://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23|url-status=dead}}</ref> The administrative center of this woreda is [[Lasho]] Town.
