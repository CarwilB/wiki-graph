'''Boloso Bombe''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]] of [[Ethiopia]] part of the [[Wolayita Zone]]. Boloso Bombe is bordered on the south by [[Kindo Koysha]], on the west by the [[Dawro Zone]], on the north by the [[Kembata Zone]], on the east by [[Boloso Sore]], northwest by [[Tembaro Special Woreda]] and on the southeast by [[Damot Sore]]. The administrative center of this woreda is [[Bombe (Wolaita)|Bombe]], which has a latitude and longitude of 7°08′15.1"N 37° 34'54.1"E.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2019 population projection conducted by the CSA,<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |title=Projected population of Ethiopia |access-date=2021-07-28 |archive-date=2021-07-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210728101857/https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> this woreda has a total population of 109,789, of whom 53,460 are men and 56,329 women; 1,057 or 1.2% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 69.27% of the population reporting that belief, 26.86% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 1.26% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
