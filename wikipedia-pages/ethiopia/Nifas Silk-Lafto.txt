'''Nifas Silk-Lafto''',<ref>Lafto is sometimes spelled "Lafito"</ref> also spelled '''Niffassilk Lafto''' or '''Nefassilk Lafto''' ([[Amharic]]: ንፋስ ስልክ ላፍቶ ክፍለ ከተማ), is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was of 335,740.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in the southwestern suburb of the city. It borders with the districts of [[Kolfe Keranio]], [[Lideta]], [[Kirkos]] and [[Bole Medhanealem|Bole]] and [[Akaky Kaliti]].
