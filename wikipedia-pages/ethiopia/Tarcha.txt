'''Tarcha''' is town and separate district in southwestern [[Ethiopia]]. Tarcha serves as the seat of [[Dawro Zone]] of [[South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region|Southwestern Ethiopia Regional State]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ena.et/web/amh/w/am_80960|title=ጠቅላይ ሚኒስትር አቢይ አህመድ ዳውሮ ዞን ታርጫ ከተማ ገብተዋል|website=www.ena.et}}</ref>
Tarcha located in 507 km southwest from [[Addis Ababa]], and also 169 km away from nearby city [[Sodo|Wolaita Sodo]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ethiopia-insight.com/2021/08/23/ክልል-የመሆን-ልዩ-ጥያቄ-በደቡብ-ምዕራብ-ኢ/|title=ወደ ተርጫ የሚያመራው መንገድ|website=www.ethiopia-insight.com}}</ref>

==Infrastructures==
Tarcha has 24 hours electricity, one government hospital, one polytechnic college and there is also higher education called Dawro Tarcha campus; one of the branches of [[Wolaita Sodo University]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://southwestcomunications.gov.et/dawuro/|title=About Dawro Zone|website=southwestcomunications.gov.et}}</ref>
