'''Diga''' is one of the [[woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Misraq Welega Zone]] and it is part of former [[Diga Leka]] woreda.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 66,689, of whom 33,896 were men and 32,793 were women; 8,377 or 12.56% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants observed [[P'ent'ay|Protestantism]], with 50.28% reporting that as their religion, while 36.53% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 11.87% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
