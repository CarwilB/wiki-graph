'''Ayra''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woredas]] in the [[Oromia|Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[West Welega Zone]]. It was separated from former [[Aira Gulliso]] woreda in November 1999. It is bounded by Gulliso in the north, Yubdo in southeast and [[Kelam Welega Zone|Kelem Wollega Zone]] in the south and west. [[Aira, Ethiopia|Aira]] is the administrative center.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 47,537 in 9,293 households, of whom 23,470 were men and 24,067 women; 6,079 or 12.79% of its population were urban dwellers. The overwhelming majority of the inhabitants (88.37%) observed [[P'ent'ay|Protestantism]], while 6.96% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]] and 4.42% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
