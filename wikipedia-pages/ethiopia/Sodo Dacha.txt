{{Short description|District in Oromia Region, Ethiopia}}
'''Sodo Dachi''' is one of the district in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Its capital city is Terre. it was part of [[Kersana Malima|Kersa Malima]] district. It is part of the south west [[Debub Mirab Shewa Zone|Shewa Zone]].

==Demographics==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 43,607, of whom 22,178 were men and 21,429 were women; 2,732 or 6.23% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 97.4% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 1.53% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].most population is Gurage ethnic backgrounds <ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
