'''Boricha''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Sidama Zone]] located in the [[Great Rift Valley, Ethiopia|Great Rift Valley]], Boricha is bordered on the south by [[Loko Abaya]], on the west by the [[Wolayita Zone]], on the northwest by the [[Oromia Region]], on the northeast by [[Awasa Zuria]], on the east by [[Shebedino]], and on the southeast by [[Dale (woreda)|Dale]]. Boricha was separated from Shebedino woreda.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 450,260, of whom 225,524 are men and 224,736 women; 10,402 or 4.16% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 77.9% of the population reporting that belief, 8.94% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]], 8.22% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 1.81% observed traditional religions, and 1.14% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].
