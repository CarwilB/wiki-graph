'''Maale''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. It is named after the [[Maale people]] who are living at this woreda. Part of the [[Debub Omo Zone]], Maale is bordered on the south by [[Bena Tsemay]], on the west by [[Bako Gazer]], on the north by the [[Basketo special woreda]] and [[Gelila (woreda)|Gelila]], on the north and east by the [[Gamo Gofa Zone]], and on the southeast by the [[Dirashe special woreda]]. Maale was separated from Bako Gazer woreda. The administrative seat of this district is Lemogento.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda had a total population of 84,693, of whom 42,871 were men and 41,822  were women; 914 or 1.08% of its population were urban dwellers.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Population-and-Housing-Census-2007-SNNPR-Statistical |url=https://ess.gov.et/download/population-and-housing-census-2007-snnpr-statistical/ |access-date=2026-03-21 |website=Ethiopian Statistics Services |language=en-GB}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Ababa |first=Addis |year=2008 |title=Summary and statistical report of the 2007 population and housing census |url=https://www.ethiopianreview.com/pdf/001/Cen2007_firstdraft(1).pdf |publisher=Federal democratic republic of Ethiopia population census commission}}</ref> The majority of the inhabitants practiced traditional beliefs, with 68.84% of the population reporting that belief, 19.01% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], and 3.89% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=The 2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia |url=http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |archive-date=2012-11-13 |access-date=2026-03-21 |website=www.csa.gov.et}}</ref>
