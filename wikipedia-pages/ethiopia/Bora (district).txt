'''Bora''' is one of the [[Aanaa]]s in the [[Oromia Regiona State]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of the former Aanaa of [[Dugda Bora]]. Part of the [[East Shewa Zone]]  it is located in the [[Great Rift Valley, Ethiopia|Great Rift Valley]]. The administrative center of Bora is [[Bote (Alem Tena)]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 58,748, of whom 30,487 were men and 28,261 were women; 11,403 or 19.41% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 86% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 6.01% of the population practiced traditional beliefs, 4.47% of the population were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 3.11% of the population were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
