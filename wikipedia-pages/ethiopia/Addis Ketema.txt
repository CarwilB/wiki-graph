'''Addis Ketema''' ({{langx|am|አዲስ ከተማ}} or ክፍለ ከተማ, meaning "new city") is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was 271,664.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in the northwestern area of the city, not far from its centre. It borders with the districts of [[Gullele]] in the north, [[Arada (Addis Ababa)|Arada]] in the east, [[Lideta]] in the south and [[Kolfe Keranio]] in the west. [[Addis Mercato]], Africa's largest open-air [[marketplace]], is in Addis Ketema.
