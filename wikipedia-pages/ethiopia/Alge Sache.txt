{{Short description|District in Oromia, Ethiopia}}
'''Alge Sachi''' is one of the [[woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It used to be called [[Supena Sodo]] woreda. The major town is [[Alge, Ethiopia|Alge]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 76,611, of whom 37,914 were men and 38,697 were women; 7,354 or 9.6% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], with 46.13% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 27.54% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], and 25.62% practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
