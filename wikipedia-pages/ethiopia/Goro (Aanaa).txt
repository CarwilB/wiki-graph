'''Goro''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|District]]s in the [[Oromia]] region of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Walisona Goro]] district. It is part of the [[Southwest Shewa Zone]]. The [[Alaba-K’abeena language|Kebena people]], who are also found in neighboring [[Kebena]] woreda, form the majority of inhabitants in this district. 

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 45,486, of whom 22,912 were men and 22,574 were women; 3,714 or 8.17% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 70.23% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 26.75% of the population practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 2.16% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
