'''Dera''' ([[Oromo language|Oromo]]: ''Deeraa,'' [[Amharic]]: ደራ) is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in the [[Oromia Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Kaba Shewa Zone (Oromia)|Kaba Shewa Zone]], Dera is bordered on the south by the [[Jamma River]] which separates it from [[Hidabu Abote]] and [[Wara Jarso]], on the west, north and east by the [[Amhara Region]]; the [[Abay River]] defines the western boundary, and its tributary the [[Walaqa River]] the northern. The administrative center of Dera is [[Gundo Meskel]].

The woreda government announced March 2008 the completion of infrastructure improvements, which consisted of 77 kilometers of new road and an additional 366 kilometers renovated at a cost of 2.6 million [[Ethiopian Birr|Birr]].<ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20090331135701/http://www.oromiagov.org/newsdetail.asp?Page=5 "77 K.M. new road built, 366 K.M. renovated with public participation"] (2 April 2008) Oromia State Government website (accessed 12 Feb 2016)</ref>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 141,426, of whom 70,787 were men and 70,639 were women; 10,531 or 14.89% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 64.87% of the population reporting they practised that belief, and 34.92% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 193,214, of whom 99,085 are men and 94,129 are women; 5,719 or 2.96% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 9.5%. With an estimated area of 1,550.47 square kilometers, Dera has an estimated population density of 124.6 people per square kilometer, which is less than the Zone average of 143.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061123121716/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=November 23, 2006 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 139,661, of whom 68,474 were men and 71,187 women; 3,210 or 2.3% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. Dera is almost evenly divided between the two largest ethnic groups reported in this woreda: the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (50.18%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (49.76%). Further, [[Amharic language|Amharic]] was spoken as a first language by 50.69%, and 49.28% spoke [[Oromo language|Oromo]]. The majority of the inhabitants professed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 64.49% of the population reporting they practiced that belief, while 35.45% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=November 15, 2009 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009)</ref>
