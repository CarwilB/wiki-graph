'''Dale Lalo''' (also known as '''Dale Sedi''') was one of the 180 [[Aanaa]]s in the [[Oromia]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[West Welega Zone]], Dale Lalo was bordered on the south by the [[Illubabor Zone]], on the west by [[Gawo Dale]], on the north by [[Ayra Guliso]], on the northeast by [[Yubdo (woreda)|Yubdo]], and on the east by [[Nole Kaba]]. Towns in Dale Lalo included [[Alem, Ethiopia|Alem]] and [[Teferi, Ethiopia|Teferi]]. It was separated for [[Dale Sedi]] and [[Lalo Kile]] woredas.

A survey of the land in Dale Lalo shows that 46.53% is cultivated or arable, 15.96% pasture, 5.1% forest, and 32.4% infrastructure or other uses.<ref>Span Consultants Ltd, ''et al.'' [http://www-wds.worldbank.org/external/default/main?pagePK=64193027&piPK=64187937&theSitePK=523679&menuPK=64187510&searchMenuPK=57313&theSitePK=523679&entityID=000333038_20090324021236&searchMenuPK=57313&theSitePK=523679 "Mekenajo - Dembidolo Road Upgrading Project, Revised Final EIA Report"], World Bank website (March 2009), p. 28</ref> There are 30 primary schools, and 1 secondary education schools in this woreda. [[Health in Ethiopia|Health services]] are provided by one health center, four clinics, and five health posts; most of these facilities are located in urban areas.<ref>"Mekenajo - Dembidolo Road Upgrading Project", pp. 30f</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 132,554, of whom 67,743 are men and 64,811 are women; 7,741 or 5.84% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 10.9%. With an estimated area of 1,089.22 square kilometers, Dale Lalo has an estimated [[population density]] of 121.7 people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of 91.7.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061123121716/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=November 23, 2006 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 95,154 in 17,534 households, of whom 46,706 were men and 48,448 were women; 4,323 or 4.54% of its population were urban dwellers. The two largest ethnic groups reported in Dale Lalo were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (96.72%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (2.94%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.34% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]] was spoken as a first language by 97.27%, and 2.52% [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 0.21% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], with 41.98% reporting that as their religion, while 41.14% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 15.52% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]] and 1.06% [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=November 15, 2009 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.16, 2.20, Annex II.2 (accessed 9 April 2009)</ref>
