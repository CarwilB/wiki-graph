{{short description|District in Sidama Region, Ethiopia}}
'''Bursa''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Sidama National Regional state]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Southern Sidama Zone]], Bursa is bordered on the south by [[Hula (woreda)|Hula]], on the west by [[Aleta Wendo (woreda)|Aleta Wendo]], on the northwest by [[Wensho]], on the northeast by [[Arbegona (woreda)|Arbegona]], and on the southeast by [[Bona Zuria]]. Bursa was separated from Hula woreda.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 103,631, of whom 51,731 are men and 51,900 women; 2,304 or 2.22% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 88.63% of the population reporting that belief, 6.25% observed traditional religions, 2.18% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]], and 1.77% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
