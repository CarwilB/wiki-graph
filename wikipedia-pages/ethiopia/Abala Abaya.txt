'''Abala Abaya''' is a district in [[Wolayita Zone]] of [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. The district is composed of 16 kebele administrations, of which 13 are rural and 3 are urban. It is situated between 6°38′ N latitude and 37 ° 42′ E longitude and located at a distance of 419&nbsp;km south from [[Addis Ababa]]. The district was established in 2019 from the surrounding districts.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23|title=Abala Abaya woreda|website=snnprswolaitazone.gov.et|access-date=2021-11-04|archive-date=2023-06-10|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230610213658/http://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23}}</ref> Abala Abaya is bordered on the south by [[Lake Abaya]],<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://wolaitatimes.com.et/?p=493|title=direction to Abala Abaya district|website=wolaitatimes.com.et|access-date=2021-09-07|archive-date=2021-09-08|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210908053135/https://wolaitatimes.com.et/?p=493}}</ref> on the west by the [[Humbo]] district, on the north by the [[Sodo Zuria]] district, on the east  by [[Hobicha]] district.  The administrative center of this district is [[Faracho|Paracho]] Town.

==Population==
The total population of the district is about 56,812. Total households of the district are 27,627 of which 16,981 male and 10,646 female households.<ref>{{cite journal |last1=Yesuph |first1=Dessalegn Shiferaw |last2=Bedeke |first2=Sisay Belay |last3=Didana |first3=Habtamu Lemma |title=Assessing climate change-induced poverty of mixed crop-livestock smallholders in Wolaita zone |journal=Research in Globalization |date=December 2023 |volume=7 |article-number=100158 |doi=10.1016/j.resglo.2023.100158 |doi-access=free |hdl=10419/331085 |hdl-access=free }}</ref>
