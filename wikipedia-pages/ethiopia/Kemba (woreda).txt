'''Kemba''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Gamo Gofa Zone]], Kemba is bordered on the southwest by the [[Debub Omo Zone]], on the west by [[Uba Debretsehay]], on the northwest by [[Zala (woreda)|Zala]], on the northeast by [[Deramalo]], on the east by [[Bonke]], and on the southeast by the [[Dirashe special woreda]]; the [[Weito River]] defines the boundary with Bonke and Dirashe. The major town in this woreda is [[Kemba, Estonia|Kemba]].

Kemba is part of a region known for hilly and undulating midland and upper lowland terrain; due to terrain and weather patterns, less than one in five households is food secure. Food crops include [[maize]], [[enset]], sweet potatoes, [[taro]], [[teff]], and yams; income sources include butter and selling firewood.<ref>[http://www.fews.net/livelihood/et/Baseline.pdf "Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region, Ethiopia Livelihood Profiles: January 2006"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110723130716/http://www.fews.net/livelihood/et/Baseline.pdf |date=2011-07-23 }}, USAID/FEWSNET, p. 27 (accessed 11 January 2011)</ref> According to a 2004 report, Kemba had 31 kilometers of all-weather roads and 8 kilometers of dry-weather roads, for an average road density of 34 kilometers per 1000 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls "Detailed statistics on roads"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151415/http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls |date=2011-07-20 }}, SNNPR Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 15 September 2009)</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 155,979, of whom 79,273 are men and 76,706 women; 4,702 or 3.02% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 46.42% of the population reporting that belief, 28.35% practiced traditional beliefs, and 15.92% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 99,674 of whom 51,387 were men and 48,287 were women; 2,149 or 2.16% of its population were urban dwellers. The two largest ethnic groups reported in Kemba were the [[Gamo people|Gamo]] (96.94%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (2.49%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.57% of the population. [[Gamo language|Gamo]] is spoken as a first language by 89.97%, 5.75% [[Male language (Ethiopia)|Male]], and 1.44% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 2.84% spoke all other primary languages reported.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.15 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref>
