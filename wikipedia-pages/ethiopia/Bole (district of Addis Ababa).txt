'''Bole''' ({{langx|am|ቦሌ ክፍለ ከተማ}}) is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was of 328,900.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in the southeastern suburb of the city. It borders with the districts of [[Yeka]], [[Kirkos]], [[Nifas Silk-Lafto]] and [[Akaky Kaliti]].
