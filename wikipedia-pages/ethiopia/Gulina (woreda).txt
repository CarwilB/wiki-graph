'''Gulina''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia]], or ''woredas'', in the [[Afar Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 4 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 4]], Gulina is located near the base of the eastern escarpment of the [[Ethiopian Highlands]], and bordered on the south by [[Ewa (Ethiopian District)|Ewa]], on the west by the [[Amhara Region]], on the north by [[Yalo (Ethiopian District)|Yalo]], on the northeast by [[Teru (Ethiopian District)|Teru]], and on the east by [[Aura (Ethiopian District)|Aura]]. The administrative center of Gulina, and Zone 4, is [[Kelewina|Kaluwan]]

The highest peak in this woreda is the active volcano [[Dalaffilla]] (613 meters). Rivers include the Fokissa, Galbate, and Gulina Rivers. Gulina has one of the higher rates of tree cover of any woreda in the Afar Region—4.2%.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, [http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf ''Baseline Survey of 55 Weredas of PCDP Phase II, Part I''] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151420/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf |date=2011-07-20 }} (Addis Ababa: August 2008), Annex 1</ref> {{As of|2008}}, Gulina has 123 kilometers of all-weather road; about 29% of the total population has access to drinking water.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, ''Baseline Survey'', Annexes 16, 17</ref> However, the all-weather road would be more useful there were also a bridge over the Gulina river; local merchants complain that they had to transport goods on the shoulders of people over the river, which increased the cost of every [[quintal]] of goods by 8 [[Ethiopian Birr|Birr]].<ref>[http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/Research/Participatory%20Research%20&%20%20Social%20Analysis%20-%20The%20case%20of%20Afar.pdf "Social Analysis and Indigenous Livelihood Strategies in Afar Pastoral Communities"] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151457/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/Research/Participatory%20Research%20%26%20%20Social%20Analysis%20-%20The%20case%20of%20Afar.pdf |date=2011-07-20 }} (Addis Ababa: Pastoral Community Development Project website, 2005), p. 63</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 49,794, of whom 28,259 are men and 21,535 women; with an area of 805.40 square kilometers, Gulina has a population density of 61.83. While 3,925 or 7.88% are urban inhabitants, a further 22,300 or 44.78% are pastoralists. A total of 7,552 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 6.6 persons to a household, and 7,820 housing units. 99.54% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region], Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
