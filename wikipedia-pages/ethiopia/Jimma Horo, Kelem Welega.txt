'''Jimma Horo''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Oromia Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Kelem Welega Zone]]. The administrative center of this woreda is Nunu. Jimma Horo was separated from former [[Jimma Gidami]] woreda.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 45,889, of whom 23,128 were men and 22,761 were women; 2,845 or 6.2% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 40.52% reporting that as their religion, while 29.86% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 29.44% observed [[Islam in Ethiopia|Islam]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
