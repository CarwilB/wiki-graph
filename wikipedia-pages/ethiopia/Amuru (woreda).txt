'''Amuru''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|Ethiopian woredas (districts)]] located in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]].

==Geography==
It is part of the [[Horo Gudru Welega Zone]]. It was part of former [[Amuru Jarte]] woreda.

The administrative center is [[Obora, Ethiopia|Obora]].  The town of [[Amuru, Ethiopia|Amuru]] is in the ''woreda''.

The Amuru woreda is known for its production of [[teff]], barley, maize and other cereal crops.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 52,633.

4,892 or 9.3% of its population were urban dwellers.

26,490 were women, and 26,143 were men.

The majority of the inhabitants observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 41.77% reporting that as their religion, while 38.53% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], 14.8% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], and 4.35% observed traditional beliefs.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
The two major ethnic groups living in the woreda are Oromo people and Amhara people.
