'''Damot Sore''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[South Ethiopia Regional State]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Wolayita Zone]] Damot Sore is bordered on the southeast by [[Sodo Zuria]], on the west by [[Kindo Koysha]], on the northwest by [[Boloso Bombe]], and on the north by [[Boloso Sore]]. The administrative town of the wereda is [[Gununo]]. Damot Sore was separated from Boloso Sore woreda.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2019 population projection conducted by the CSA,<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |title=Projected population of Ethiopia, 2011–2019 |access-date=2021-07-28 |archive-date=2021-07-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210728101857/https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> this woreda has a total population of 131,078, of whom 63,845 are men and 67,233 women; 6,124 or 6.08% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 62.47% of the population reporting that belief, 31.15% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 5.47% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
