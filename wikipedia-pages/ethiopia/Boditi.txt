'''Boditi''', ([[Amharic]]: ቦዲቲ) or ([[Wolaitta language |Wolayttattuwa]]: '''Bodditte''') is a city and separate district in south central Ethiopia. The city is located in the [[Wolaita Zone]] of the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. This city has a latitude and longitude of {{Coord|6|55|N|37|52|E}} with an average elevation of 2050 meters above sea level. It is the administrative center of [[Damot Gale]] district. The city is bounded by Shasha-Gale Kebele in the north, Ade-Koisha Kebele in the south, Chawkare Kebele in the east and Sibaye-Korke kebele in the west. It is located in East Rift valley at a distance of {{cvt|180|miles}} to the south of Addis Ababa and at about {{cvt|86|miles}} to the west of [[Hawassa]].

As of 2006 permanent postal service is available, as well as electricity and telephone service, more colleges and higher educational institutions are currently being developed as the number of school-aged children increases.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Infrastructure.xls|title=Infrastracture in Boditi|website=www.snnprbofed.gov.et}}. Southern Nations, Nationalities and People's Region, Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 27 September 2009)</ref>
The map attached to C. W. Gwynn's account of his 1908/09 triangulation survey of southern Ethiopia shows Boditi, with the note that it had a market.<ref>Gwynn, [https://www.jstor.org/stable/1778701 "A Journey in Southern Abyssinia", ''Geographical Journal''], 38 (1911), pp. 113-139</ref> During the early 1930s, the market was held on Tuesdays and was very important. In 1969, a group of Swedish architectural students surveyed Boditi, and presented their findings to the University of Lund.<ref>Summary at [http://130.238.24.99/library/resources/dossiers/local_history_of_ethiopia/B/ORTBIT.pdf "Local History in Ethiopia"]{{dead link|date=November 2016 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} The Nordic Africa Institute website (accessed 6 June 2008)</ref>

==Demographics==
The 1994 national census reported this city had a total population of 13,400 of whom 6,479 were men and 6,921 were women. Boditi is one of densely populated areas in [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. Total population of the city as conducted by central statistical agency of Ethiopia in 2023 is 67,861. Among these males count 32,941 and females count 34,920.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Population-Size-of-Towns-by-Sex-as-of-July-2023.pdf|title=Population size of towns by sex as of July 2023|website=www.statsethiopia.gov.et}}</ref>
