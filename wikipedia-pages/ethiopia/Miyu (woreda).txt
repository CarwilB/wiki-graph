'''Miyu''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was separated from [[Dire (woreda)|Dire]] woreda. It is part of the [[Borena Zone]]. The administrative center of Miyu is [[Hidi Lola]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for Miyu woreda of 50,601, of whom 25,082 were men and 25,519 were women; 3,941 or 7.79% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practiced traditional beliefs, with 71.59% of the population reporting they observed these beliefs, while 17.14% of the population were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 7% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], 3.83% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]] and 1.79% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
