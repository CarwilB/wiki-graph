'''Shanto''' ([[Ge'ez]]: '''ሻንቶ''', [[Wolaitta language|Wolayttattuwa]]: '''Shantto''')<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.google.com/maps/place/Shanito+Town/@7.0242601,37.8457112,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x17b198e56a912703:0x9cc70f8b5c5e87c!8m2!3d7.0209338!4d37.8644948|title=Shanito Town · Ethiopia|website=Shanito Town · Ethiopia}}</ref> is a town and separate district in southern [[Ethiopia]]. Located in the [[Wolayita Zone|Wolaita Zone]] of the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]], This town has a latitude and longitude of 7°01′ 23"N 37°51′49"E with an elevation of 1953 meters above sea level.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://elevationmap.net/|title=Shanito Town, Damot Pulasa, Wolayita, Ethiopia on the Elevation Map. Topographic Map of Shanito Town, Damot Pulasa, Wolayita, Ethiopia.|website=elevationmap.net}}</ref> The town is the administrative center of [[Damot Pulasa]] woreda. There are various development works in Shanto town, including 24-hour electricity services, health facilities, schools, public libraries and other services.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://wolaitatimes.com/?p=3261|title=የሻንቶ (ዎላይታ) የማህበረሰብ ቤተ-መጻሕፍትን የሼፕ ኢትዮጵያ አጋር ድርጅቶች ጎበኙ።|website=wolaitatimes.com|language=amharic}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.eeu.gov.et/news/detail/1206|title=ለመልሶ ግንባታና አቅም ማሰደግ ሥራ ትኩረት ተሰጥቶ እየተሰራ ነው|website=www.eeu.gov.et|language=amharic}}
</ref>
==Demography==
Total population of the town as conducted by central statistical agency of Ethiopia in 2023 is 15,020. Among this figures male population constitutes 7,434 and Females counted 7,586 from total population.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Population-Size-of-Towns-by-Sex-as-of-July-2023.pdf|title=Population size of towns by sex as of July 2023|website=www.statsethiopia.gov.et|access-date=2024-01-06|archive-date=2024-02-07|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240207122825/http://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Population-Size-of-Towns-by-Sex-as-of-July-2023.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>
