{{Short description|Woreda (district) in Ethiopia}}'''Diksis''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Arsi Zone]] and was separated from the [[Tena (woreda)|Tena]] woreda.

== Demographics ==
According to the 2007 national census, Diksis had a total population of 72,301, comprising 35,970 men and 36,331 women. Approximately 7,854 or 10.86% of the population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 62.92% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 36.71% of the population practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
