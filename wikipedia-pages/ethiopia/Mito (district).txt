'''Mito''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]] in [[Ethiopia]]. The district is one of ten districts in [[Silt'e Zone]] of [[Central Ethiopia Regional State]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://siltezonecommunication.gov.et/silte-woreda|website=siltezonecommunication.gov.et|access-date=2026-02-14|title=Administrative structure of Silte zone}}</ref> The district is one of main areas of agricultural products in Ethiopia and known for its fertile soil and favorable climate for agriculture.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://siltezonecommunication.gov.et/post/wheat-farming-in-silte-zones-mito-woreda-a-growing-hub-of-ethiopias-agricultural-future|website=siltezonecommunication.gov.et|title=wheat farming in silte zone mito woreda}}</ref>
The Koshe-Mito-[[Worabe]] asphalt road that connects Mito district to [[Mareko special woreda]], is one of the roads that connects the district to other parts of Ethiopia.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.fanamc.com/archives/263428|website=www.fanamc.com|title=የቆሼ-ሚቶ-ወራቤ መንገድ ፕሮጀክት አስፓልት የማንጠፍ ሥራ እየተከናወነ መሆኑ ተገለጸ|language=amharic}}</ref>
