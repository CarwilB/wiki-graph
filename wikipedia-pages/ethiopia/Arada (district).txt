'''Arada''' ([[Amharic]]: አራዳ ክፍለ ከተማ) is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 Arada's population was of 226,000.<ref name=a/> Arada is one of 10 districts of Addis Ababa, the capital of [[Ethiopia]]. Arada is a center of culture and education, with numerous schools, cultural establishments and annual cultural events.

==Geography==
The district is located in the northern area of the city, nearby the centre. It borders with the districts of [[Gullele]], [[Yeka]], [[Kirkos]], [[Lideta]] and [[Addis Ketema]]. It has an average elevation of nearly 2444m. <ref>{{Cite web |title=Arada topographic map, elevation, terrain |url=https://en-us.topographic-map.com/map-st32f3/Arada/?center=9.10512,38.70522&zoom=12 |access-date=2025-06-17 |website=Topographic maps |language=en}}</ref> 

==Main sights==
Arada is known as the center of the old and the new generation artistic, social and urban life style. Its main sights include [[St. George's Cathedral, Addis Ababa|St. George's Cathedral]], the great [[Menelik II]] Square, the [[Hager Fikir Theatre]], the [[Taitu Hotel]], [[Charles de Gaulle]] Square and many other historical areas and structures.
