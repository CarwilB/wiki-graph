'''Tach Armachiho''' ({{langx|am|ታች አርማጭሆ}}) is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Amhara Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. This woreda is named after "Armachiho", a province in northwestern Ethiopia along the [[Ethiopia–Sudan border|border with Sudan]] and south of the [[Tekezé River]].<ref>Antony Mockler uses this name in this sense in his book, ''Haile Selassie's War'' (New York: Olive Branch Press, 2003)</ref> Part of the [[Central Gondar Zone]], Tach Armachiho is bordered on the south by [[Lay Armachiho]] and [[Chilga]], on the southwest by [[Metemma (woreda)|Metemma]], on the west by [[Mirab Armachiho]], on the north by the [[Tegeda]], on the east by [[Dabat (woreda)|Dabat]],  and on the southeast by [[Wegera (woreda)|Wegera]]. [[Sanja (woreda)|Sanja]] town is the administrative center of Tach Armachiho Woreda.

==Demographics==
Based on the 2007 national census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 89,115, of whom 45,874 are men and 43,241 women; 12,258 or 13.8% are urban inhabitants. The majority of the inhabitants practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 97.8% reporting that as their religion, while 1.4% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=266&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Amhara Region] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101114004005/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=266&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2010-11-14 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
