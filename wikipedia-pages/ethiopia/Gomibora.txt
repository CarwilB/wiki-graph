'''Gomibora''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Hadiya Zone]], Gomibora is bordered on the south by [[Soro (woreda)|Soro]], on the west by the [[Yem special woreda]], on the north by [[Gibe (woreda)|Gibe]], on the northeast by [[Misha (woreda)|Misha]], and on the east by [[Limo (woreda)|Limo]]. Gomibora was part of Soro woreda.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 93,141, of whom 46,624 are men and 46,517 women; none of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 91.53% of the population reporting that belief, 5.16% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]], and 1.58% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
