'''Chinaksen''' ({{langx|om|'''Aanaa cinaaksan'''}}) is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|districts]] or ''woredas'' in the [[East Hararghe Zone]] in the [[Oromia]] region of [[Ethiopia]]. The administrative center of this Aanaa is [[Chinaksen]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 87,063, of whom 44,925 were men and 42,138 were women; 12,261 or 14.08% of its population were urban dwellers. 99.9% of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with less than 0.1% of the population practising [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
