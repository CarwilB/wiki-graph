'''Bicho''' is one of the [[woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the former [[Metu (woreda)|Metu]], it is now part of the [[Illubabor Zone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 24,947, of whom 12,512 were men and 12,435 were women; 1,555 or 6.25% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 72.68% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 19.1% of the population said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 8.1% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
