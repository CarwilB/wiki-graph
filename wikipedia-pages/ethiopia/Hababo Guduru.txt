{{Short description|District in Oromia Region, Ethiopia}}
{{Coord|format=dms|display=title}}''' Hababo Guduru''' is one of [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Horo Gudru Welega Zone]]. It was separated from [[Guduru (woreda)|Guduru]] woreda.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 45,325, of whom 22,744 were men and 22,581 were women; 2,364 or 5.22% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 42.15% reporting that as their religion, while 40.19% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], and 16.93% observed traditional beliefs.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
