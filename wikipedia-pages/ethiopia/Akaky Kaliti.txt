'''Akaky Kaliti''' ([[Amharic]]: አቃቂ ቃሊቲ ክፍለ ከተማ), also spelled '''Akaki Kality''', is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was of 195,273.<ref name=a/> Formerly, Akaky Kaliti was known as Woreda 26. 

Many industries are found in this sub-city of Addis Ababa.

==Geography==
The district is the southernmost suburb of the city and borders with the districts of [[Nifas Silk-Lafto]] and [[Bole Medhanealem|Bole]]. The towns of Galan and [[Dukem]] in the [[Oromia Special Zone Surrounding Addis Ababa]] lie to the east along the [[Addis Ababa–Adama Expressway]].
