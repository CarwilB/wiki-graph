'''Kirkos''' ([[Amharic]]: ቂርቆስ ክፍለ ከተማ), also spelled '''Kerkos''', '''Kirikos''' or '''Cherkos''', is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was 235,441.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in the city centre, and borders the districts of [[Arada (Addis Ababa)|Arada]], [[Yeka]], [[Bole Medhanealem|Bole]], [[Nifas Silk-Lafto]] and [[Lideta]].
