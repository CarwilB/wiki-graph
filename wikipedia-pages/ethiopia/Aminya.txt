'''Aminya''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|Aanaa]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Arsi Zone]], Amigna is bordered on the south by [[Seru (woreda)|Seru]], on the southwest by [[Robe (woreda)|Robe]], on the west by [[Sude, Ethiopia|Sude]], on the northwest by [[Chole (woreda)|Chole]], on the north by [[Gololcha]], and on the east by the [[West Hararghe Zone]]. Towns include [[Adele, Ethiopia|Adele]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 73,245, of whom 36,618 were men and 36,627 were women; 6,425 or 8.77% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 58.91% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 40.54% of the population practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 76,061, of whom 37,890 are men and 38,171 are women; 6,429 or 8.45% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 12.3%. With an estimated area of 1,345.32 square kilometers, Amigna has an estimated population density of 56.5 people per square kilometer, which is less than the Zone average of 132.2.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20061123121716/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=November 23, 2006 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

=== 1994 national census ===
The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 54,280, of whom 27,354 were men and 26,926 women; 3,596 or 6.62% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. The two largest ethnic groups reported in Amigna were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (89.95%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (9.24%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.81% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromo]] was spoken as a first language by 89.52%, and 10.24% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 0.24% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 55.63% of the population having reported they practiced that belief, while 44.01% of the population said they professed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=November 15, 2009 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009).</ref>
