'''Kersa Dula''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Somali Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the   liibaan zone Kersa Dula is bounded on the south, west and north by the [[Oromia Region]], and on the east by welmal river 

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda is primary inhabited by the karanle  of the Somali people and it has a total population of 42,774, of whom 23,541 are men and 19,233 women. While 3,631 or 8.49% are urban inhabitants, a further 33,114 or 77.42% are pastoralists. 99.36% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=274&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Somali Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121114110154/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=274&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-14 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 3.1 and 3.4.</ref>
