'''Lideta''' ([[Amharic]]: ልደታ ክፍለ ከተማ) is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was of 214,769.<ref name=a>"[http://www.addisababacity.gov.et/index.php/en/sub-cities/lideta Lideta] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20131203151026/http://www.addisababacity.gov.et/index.php/en/sub-cities/lideta |date=2013-12-03 }}". City Government of Addis Ababa.</ref>

==Geography==
The district is located in the central-western area of the city, nearby the centre. It borders with the districts of [[Addis Ketema]], [[Arada (Addis Ababa)|Arada]], [[Kirkos]], [[Nifas Silk-Lafto]] and [[Kolfe Keranio]].
