'''Tebela''' ([[Geʽez]]: ጠበላ) or '''([[Wolaitta language|Wolayttattuwa]]: Xabala)''' is a city in Wolaita Zone [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. Tebela is an administrative capital of [[Humbo]] district of [[Wolayita Zone]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://reliefweb.int/sites/reliefweb.int/files/resources/21_adm_snp_081517_a0.pdf|title=Location of Tebela in Humbo, within the map of SNNPR}}</ref> Tebela is located about 345&nbsp;km away from [[Addis Ababa]] to the south. And also, Tebela is located 20&nbsp;km, South from [[Sodo]], the capital of the [[South Ethiopia Regional State|region]] and also [[Wolayita Zone]]. The coordinate point of the city in map is 6°42′24″N 37°46′10″E.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tebela+Town,+Ethiopia/@6.7005086,37.7535164,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x17b04d1c85a30f5d:0x13ddc157c110b9e5!8m2!3d6.7005597!4d37.77425|title= Tebela , Ethiopia|website=www.google.com}}</ref> The amenities in the city are; 24 hours electricity, pure public water, banks, primary and secondary schools, postal service, telecommunications services health centre, private clinics, drugs store, public market, public road light around high ways, internal and town crossing asphalt roads and others.

== Demographics ==
[[File:Tebela.jpg|Tebela entrance from Sodo|thumb]]
Tebela is one of densely populated areas in [[South Ethiopia Regional State]]. Total population of the city as conducted by central statistical agency of Ethiopia in 2020 is 17,538. From this figure female population counts 8,689 and male population counts 8,849.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Population-Size-of-Towns-by-Sex-as-of-July-2023.pdf|title=Population size of towns by sex as of July 2023|website=www.statsethiopia.gov.et|access-date=January 6, 2024|archive-date=February 7, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240207122825/http://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Population-Size-of-Towns-by-Sex-as-of-July-2023.pdf|url-status=dead}}</ref>
