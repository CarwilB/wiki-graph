'''Wadera''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Adolana Wadera]] woreda. It is part of the [[Guji Zone]]. The largest town is [[Wadera, Ethiopia|Wadera]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 50,554, of whom 25,670 were men and 24,884 were women; 4,729 or 9.35% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 64.18% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 26.62% of the population were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], 4.78% practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 2.62% practiced traditional beliefs, and 1.11% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
