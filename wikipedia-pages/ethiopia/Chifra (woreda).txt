'''Chifra''' (or '''Sifra''') is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Afar Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 1 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 1]], Chifra is located  In 40.016562, 11.603945 Altitude and longitude coordinations, near the base of the eastern escarpment of the [[Ethiopian Highlands]] and bordered on the south by [[Mille (Ethiopian District)|Mille]], on the west by the [[Amhara Region]], on the north by the [[Administrative Zone 4 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 4]], and on the east by [[Dubti (Ethiopian District)|Dubti]]; the [[Logiya River]] defines part of the boundary with Zone 4.

== Overview ==
The average elevation in this woreda is 825 meters above sea level;<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, [http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf ''Baseline Survey of 55 Weredas of PCDP Phase II, Part I''] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151420/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf |date=July 20, 2011 }} (Addis Ababa: August 2008), Annex 1 (accessed 23 March 2009)</ref>
the highest peak is [[Mount Groppo]] (900 meters). Rivers include the [[Mille River|Mille]].<ref name=APDA>Afar Pastoralist Development Association, [http://www.utviklingsfondet.no/filestore/Afar_Dev_Conf_Document.pdf "Document of Afar Development Conference Aysaita, December 15-30, 2004"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110728175401/http://www.utviklingsfondet.no/filestore/Afar_Dev_Conf_Document.pdf |date=2011-07-28 }} (accessed 13 January 2009)</ref> There are two roads in Chifra, connecting its administrative center to other towns. One runs from Chifra to [[Mille, Ethiopia|Mille]], which is 105 kilometers in length; it was constructed in two segments between February 1999 and February 2001 by [[SUR Construction]].<ref>[http://www.sur.com.et/MajProjExp.html "SUR Construction PLC - Major Projects"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20100226180507/http://www.sur.com.et/MajProjExp.html |date=2010-02-26 }}, SUR Construction webpage (accessed 6 May 2010</ref> The other goes south to [[Garsa Gita]] where it joins the all-weather road to [[Bati (Ethiopian District)|Bati]].<ref name=APDA/> {{As of|2008}}, about 22.33% of the total population of Chifra has access to drinking water.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, ''Baseline Survey'', Annex 16</ref>

[[Education in Ethiopia|Education]] in this woreda is in three forms: formal, non-formal, and Koranic. Formal education goes to Grade 8 and non-formal education is implemented in 4 [[kebele]]s by the Afar Pastoralist Development Association. The non-formal education is in the [[Afar language]] and includes mathematics; a total of 843 students have achieved literacy. {{As of|2004}}, there were 223 people (30 women, 31 men, and the rest children) in non-formal education taught by 6 teachers. Details about the local Koranic education is not available, but at one village children had been learning Koranic verses there for 117 years.<ref name=APDA/>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 91,080, of whom 50,861 are men and 40,219 women; with an area of 1,519.32 square kilometers, Chifra has a population density of 59.95. While 9,132 or 10.03% are urban inhabitants, a further 38,234 or 41.98% are pastoralists. A total of 14,518 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 6.3 persons to a household, and 14,937 housing units. 98.88% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 1.09% were [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity|Orthodox Christians]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
