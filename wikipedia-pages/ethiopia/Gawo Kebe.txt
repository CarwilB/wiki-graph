'''Gawo Qebe''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Kelem Welega Zone]] and is bordered on the east and north by [[Mirab Welega Zone]], on the west by [[Jimma Horo, Kelem Welega|Jimma Horo]], on the south by [[Yemalogi Welele]] and on the southeast by [[Dale Wabera]]. The administrative center of this woreda is [[Kebe, Ethiopia|Kebe]]. Gawo Qebe was separated from former [[Gawo Dale]] woreda.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 65,396, of whom 32,966 were men and 32,430 were women; 1,731 or 2.65% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants observed [[Islam in Ethiopia|Islam]], with 39.77% reporting that as their religion, while 38.79% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], and 21.27% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
