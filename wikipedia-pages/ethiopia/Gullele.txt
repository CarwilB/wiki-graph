'''Gullele''' ([[Amharic]]: ጉለሌ ክፍለ ከተማ), also spelled '''Gulele''', is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was of 248,865.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in northern suburb of the city, near the [[Mount Entoto]] and [[Entoto Natural Park]]. It borders with the districts of [[Kolfe Keranio]], [[Addis Ketema]], [[Arada (Addis Ababa)|Arada]] and [[Yeka]].
