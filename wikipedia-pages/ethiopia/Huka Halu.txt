{{Short description|Woreda in the Oromia region of Ethiopia}}
'''Huka Halu''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of [[Bure, Oromia (woreda)|Bure]]. It is part of the [[Illubabor Zone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 16,881, of whom 8,481 were men and 8,400 were women; 1,685 or 9.98% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 65% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 23.52% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], and 11.04% of the population were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
