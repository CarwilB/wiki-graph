{{Short description|District of Oromia, Ethiopia}}
'''Setema''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Located in the western part of the [[Jimma Zone]], Setema is bordered on the south by [[Gera (woreda)|Gera]], on the west by [[Sigmo (woreda)|Sigmo]], on the north by the [[Illubabor Zone]], and on the southeast by [[Gomma (woreda)|Gomma]]. The administrative center of the woreda is [[Gatira]].

== Overview ==
The altitude of this woreda ranges from {{convert|2,250 to 3,010|m|ft}} above sea level. The highest points are in the Damu Siga mountain range. Perennial rivers include the Onja, Salako, Gidache and [[Gebba River|Gebba]]. A survey of the land in this woreda shows that 27.2% is arable or cultivable (20.8% was under annual crops), 13.1% pasture, 55.1% [[Forestry in Ethiopia|forest]], and the remaining 4.6% is considered degraded, builtup or otherwise unusable. The majority of the Sigmo-Geba State Forest, about {{convert|100|km2|sqmi}} in size, is located in Setema. [[Teff]], [[maize|corn]], and sheep are important cash crops.<ref name=Oromia>[http://oromiagov.org/Socio%20Economic%20Profile/Djimma%20Zone/Djimma%20Zone.pdf ''Socio-economic profile of the Djimma (sic) Zone''] Government of Oromia Region (last accessed 1 August 2006).</ref> Although [[coffee]] is also an important cash crop in this woreda, less than {{convert|20|km2|sqmi}} are planted with this crop.<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20160815124929/http://www.oromiacoffeeunion.org/production.htm "Coffee Production"]}} Oromia Coffee Cooperative Union website</ref>

Industry in the woreda includes 32 [[grain mill]]s. There were 18 Farmers Associations with 17,623 members and 5 Farmers Service Cooperatives with 7,562 members. Setema has {{convert|35|km|mi}} of all-weather road, for an average road density of 31.6&nbsp;kilometres per {{nowrap|1,000 square kilometres}} ({{nowrap|5 mi / 100 sq mi}}). About 60% of the urban and 9.6% of the rural population has access to [[water supply and sanitation in Ethiopia|drinking water]].<ref name=Oromia/>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 103,221, of whom 50,744 were men and 52,477 were women; 4,729 or 4.58% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]], with 96.91% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 2.67% of the population said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 117,275, of whom 59,069 are men and 58,206 are women; 4,147 or 3.54% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 12.3%. With an estimated area of 1,106.10 square kilometers, Setema has an estimated population density of 106 people per square kilometer, which is less than the Zone average of 150.6.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics], Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 84,655, of whom 42,234 were men and 42,421 women; 2,318 or 2.74% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Setema were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (96.48%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (2.22%), and the [[Tigrayans|Tigray]] (1.0%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.3% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]] was spoken as a first language by 97.17%, 1.75% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]], and 0.97% spoke [[Tigrinya language|Tigrinya]]; the remaining 0.11% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 96.84% of the population having reported they practiced that belief, while 2.89% of the population said they professed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=2009-11-15 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009)</ref>
