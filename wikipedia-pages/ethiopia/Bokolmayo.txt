'''Bokolmayo''' (Boqolmayo in Somali, Bokolmayo English) is one of the 96 districts in the [[Somali Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. The district is located in the [[Liben Zone]], situated between [[Filtu]] and [[Dollo Ado]], the largest town in the [[Liben Zone]]. Bokolmayo hosts the highest number of refugees in Ethiopia—nearly 200,000. These refugees are Somalis who fled the [[Somali Civil War|civil war in Somalia]] around early 2009.<ref>{{cite web |title=Bokolmanyo Camp profile |url=https://data2.unhcr.org/en/documents/details/64816 |website=unhcr.org |publisher=UNHCR |accessdate=21 September 2020}}</ref> There are five refugee camps in the Liben zone, three of which are found in Bokolmayo.<ref>{{cite web |title=Situation of horn of Africa Somali refugees |url=https://data2.unhcr.org/en/situations/horn?id=7&country=65 |website=unhcr.org |publisher=UNHCR |accessdate=21 September 2020}}</ref> These refugee camps include:

Bokolmayo is approximately 95&nbsp;km west of [[Dolo Odo|Dollo Ado]], and 140&nbsp;km east of [[Filtu]]. The district lies between two rivers: [[Dawa River|Dawa]] and [[Ganale Doria River|Ganale River]]. 

==Demographics==
The majority of the population in Bokolmayo are Somalis (99.9%), with the remaining 0.001% consisting of individuals from other Ethiopian nationalities, who have come for professional work or business. Bokolmayo has a population of nearly 110,000, of which: 40% are [[Pastoralism|pastoralists]], 20% are [[Pastoral farming|agropastoralist]], 20% are farmers and 20% are urban dwellers according to [[Central Statistical Agency|Ethiopia's central statistical agency]].<ref>{{cite web |last= mohamed abdulahi Bilow |first=Bokolmayo residence |date=August 2024 |title=Population of Somali region |url=http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php/census-report/census-tables/category/301-census-tables |accessdate=21 September 2020 |website=CSA |publisher=Central statistical Agency}}</ref> 

The majority ethnic group is Abrisha subclan of [[Degoodi|dagodia]] clan from larger [[Gugundhabe Hawiye ]] clan. 

Bokolmayo is the center of culture for dagodia clan and the king of dagodia, [[Wabar Abdille]], resides there.
