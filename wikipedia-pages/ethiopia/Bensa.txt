'''Bensa''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in [[Sidama Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Daye is the capital city of Eastern Sidaama zone (Soojjaati zoone), also known as Bensa zone. Bensa is one of the more populous woredas in Sidama, extending into the [[Oromia Region]] like a peninsula, Bensa is bordered on the south and north by the Oromia Region, with [[Bona Zuria]] on the west, [[Arbegona (woreda)|Arbegona]] on the northwest, [[Chire (woreda)|Chire]] on the east, and [[Aroresa]] on the southeast. The major town in Bensa is [[Daye, Ethiopia|Daye]].

According to a 2004 report, Bensa had 101 kilometers of all-weather roads and no kilometers of dry-weather roads, for an average road density of 125 kilometers per 1000 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls "Detailed statistics on roads"] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151415/http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls |date=July 20, 2011 }}, SNNPR Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 15 September 2009)</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 250,727, of whom 126,959 are men and 123,768 women; 11,588 or 4.62% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 92.8% of the population reporting that belief, 2.67% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 1.89% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

In the 1994 Census this woreda had a population of 186,343, of whom 94,823 were men and 91,520 women; 5,897 or 3.16% of its population were urban dwellers. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Bensa were the [[Sidama people|Sidama]] (96.18%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (2.2%), and the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (0.96%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.66% of the population. [[Sidamo language|Sidamo]] is spoken as a first language by 97.64% of the inhabitants, 1.46% speak [[Amharic language|Amharic]], and 0.77% [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]]; the remaining 0.13% spoke all other primary languages reported. 77.76% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], 7.59% observed traditional religions, 5.58% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 3.21% were [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity|Ethiopian Orthodox]], and 2.51% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=November 19, 2008 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.7, 2.11, 2.15, 2.19 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref> Concerning [[Education in Ethiopia|education]], 18.18% of the population were considered literate; 6.96% of children aged 7–12 were in primary school; 1.70% of the children aged 13–14 were in junior secondary school; and 0.93% of the inhabitants aged 15–18 were in senior secondary school. Concerning [[Water supply and sanitation in Ethiopia|sanitary conditions]], about 62.36% of the urban houses and 13.61% of all houses had access to safe drinking water at the time of the census, while about 65.72% of the urban and 5.1% of the total had toilet facilities.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=November 19, 2008 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.19, 3.5, 3.7, 6.3, 6.11, 6.13 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref>
