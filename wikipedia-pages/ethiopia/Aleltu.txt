'''Aleltu''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Bereh]] woreda. It is part of the [[Shawaa Kaabaa|North Shewa Zone]]. Towns located in this woreda include Tale, Bakkee, Digare, Sant'e and Galata. The closest major cities include [[Addis Ababa]], [[Adama]] and [[Bishoftu]].<ref>{{cite web|title=Go Mapper|url=http://www.gomapper.com/travel/where-is/aleltu-located.html|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20141221215140/http://www.gomapper.com/travel/where-is/aleltu-located.html|url-status=usurped|archive-date=December 21, 2014|accessdate=20 October 2013}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 53,414, of whom 27,109 were men and 26,305 were women; 3,851 or 7.21% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 99.15% of the population reporting they practised that belief, 0.75% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
