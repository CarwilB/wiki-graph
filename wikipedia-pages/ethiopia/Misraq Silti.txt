'''Misraq Silti''', ([[Amharic]]: '''ምስራቅ ስልጢ''') is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]] in [[Ethiopia]]. The district is one of ten districts in [[Silt'e Zone]] of [[Central Ethiopia Regional State]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://siltezonecommunication.gov.et/silte-woreda|website=siltezonecommunication.gov.et|title=Administrative structure of Silte zone}}</ref> 
The district has a favorable ecosystem that allows for irrigation-based farming three times in a year.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.ena.et/web/amh/w/amh_5616028|website=www.ena.et|title=በገንዘብ ሚኒስትሩ አቶ አህመድ ሺዴ የተመራ የሥራ ኃላፊዎች ቡድን በስልጤ ዞን የተለያዩ የልማት ስራዎችን ተመለከተ|language=amharic}}</ref>

==Population==
The total population of the district is about 60,578.  Of these numbers males count 37,221 and females count 23,357.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://siltezonecommunication.gov.et/silte-population|website=siltezonecommunication.gov.et|title=Population Size and Distribution in Silte zone}}</ref>
