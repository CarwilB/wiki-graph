'''Hadale'ela Afar, Ethiopia''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Afar Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 5 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 5]], Hadale-Ela is located near the base of the eastern escarpment of the [[Ethiopian Highlands]], and bordered on the south by [[Simurobi Gele'alo]], on the west by the [[Amhara Region]], on the north by [[Dalifage]], on the northeast by the [[Borkana River]] which separates it from [[Dewe (woreda)|Dewe]], and on the east by [[Administrative Zone 3 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 3]]. Information is lacking on the towns of this woreda.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 35,255, of whom 19,467 are men and 15,788 women. While 2,119 or 6.01% are urban inhabitants, a further 5,951 or 16.89% are pastoralists. 99.41% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref> Although the predominant ethnic group in this woreda are the [[Afar people|Afar]], there is a significant [[Argobba people|Argobba]] minority.
