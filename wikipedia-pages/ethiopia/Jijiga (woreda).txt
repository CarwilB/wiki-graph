{{short description|District in Somali Region, Ethiopia}}
'''Jijiga''' ({{langx|so|Jigjiga}}) is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Somali Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Jijiga Zone]], Jijiga is bordered on the south by [[Kebri Beyah (woreda)|Kebri Beyah]], on the southwest by [[Gursum, Somali (woreda)|Gursum]], on the southeast by Ajersagora, on the northwest by the [[Sitti Zone]], and on the north by [[Awbare (woreda)|Awbare]]. Towns and cities in this woreda include [[Jijiga]].

The average elevation in this woreda is 1803 meters above sea level.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, [http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf ''Baseline Survey of 55 Weredas of PCDP Phase II, Part I''] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151420/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/pcdpII%20-%20One.pdf |date=2011-07-20 }} (Addis Ababa: August 2008), Annex 1 (accessed 23 March 2009)</ref> The only perennial rivers in this woreda are the [[Fafen River|Fafen]] and the [[Jerer River|Jerer]]. {{As of|2008}}, Jijiga has 80 kilometers of asphalt road and 60 kilometers of all-weather gravel road; about 34.1% of the total population has access to drinking water.<ref>Hailu Ejara Kene, ''Baseline Survey'', Annexes 16, 17</ref>
The Karamara mountain to the west of the city of Jijiga were thoroughly mined during the [[Ogaden War]], and there are still dangerous areas which have been marked off limits.<ref name=NAI-web>[http://130.238.24.99/library/resources/dossiers/local_history_of_ethiopia/j/ORTJI.pdf "Local History in Ethiopia"]{{dead link|date=November 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} The Nordic Africa Institute website (accessed 31 May 2008)</ref>

Prior to the 2004 October referendum, which established the disputed boundary between the Oromia and Somali Regions, a large section in the north of this woreda became the [[Chinaksen (woreda)|Chinaksen]] woreda, which was transferred to the Oromia Region.<ref>[http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/oromia-final.pdf "Pastoral and Agro Pastoral Livelihood baseline profile - Oromiya (7 Weredas)"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151752/http://www.pcdp.org.et/Docs/baseline/oromia-final.pdf |date=2011-07-20 }}, Pastoral Community Development Project (2004), p. 45</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 277,560, of whom 149,292 are men and 128,268 women. While 125,876 or 45.35% are urban inhabitants, a further 6,956 or 2.51% are pastoralists. 91.41% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 6.97% were [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity|Orthodox Christian]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=274&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Somali Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121114110154/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=274&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-14 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 3.1 and 3.4.</ref>
This diverse woreda is primarily inhabited by the [[Somali people|Somali]] the [[Gadabuursi]], [[Ogaden]], Abaskuul, Bartire and Yabare subclans of the [[Jidwaq]], Geri Koombe, [[Habr Awal]] and [[Garhajis]] of the [[Isaaq]] and [[Sheekhaal]]. 
The 1997 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 269,096, of whom 138,483 were men and 130,613 were women; 73,548 or 27.33% of its population were urban dwellers. The largest ethnic group reported in Jijiga was the [[Somali people|Somali]] 138,483 (99.9%).<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck05%5Ck05.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Somali Region, Vol. 1''] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119230720/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck05%5Ck05.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }} Tables 2.1, 2.12 (accessed 10 January 2009). The results of the 1994 census in the Somali Region were not satisfactory, so the census was repeated in 1997.</ref>

== Agriculture ==
A sample enumeration performed by the CSA in 2001 interviewed 37,413 farmers in this woreda, who held an average of 1.05 hectares of land. Of the 39.37 square kilometers of private land surveyed, 81.21% was under cultivation, 10.6% pasture, 3.72% fallow, and 1.8% was devoted to other uses; the percentage in woodland is missing. For the land under cultivation in Jijiga, 66.08% is planted in cereals like [[teff]], [[sorghum]] and [[maize]], 1.61% in pulses, 1.61% in root crops, and 0.07% in vegetables. Permanent crops included 4108 hectares planted in [[khat]], 1 in [[enset]], and 14.55 in fruit trees. 78.33% of the farmers both raise crops and livestock, while 19.88% only grow crops and 1.79% only raise livestock. Land tenure in this woreda is distributed amongst 94.28% owned their land, 1.29% rented, and the remaining 4.43% held their land under other forms of tenure.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Agricultural_Sample_Enumeration_2001/Agricultural_Sample_Enumeration_2001 "Central Statistical Authority of Ethiopia. Agricultural Sample Survey (AgSE2001). Report on Area and Production - Somali Region. Version 1.1 - December 2007"]{{dead link|date=April 2017 |bot=InternetArchiveBot |fix-attempted=yes }} (accessed 26 January 2009)</ref>
