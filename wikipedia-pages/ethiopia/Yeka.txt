'''Yeka''' ([[Amharic]]: የካ ክፍለ ከተማ) is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]].<ref name=a>"[http://www.addisababacity.gov.et/index.php/en/sub-cities/yeka  demamit Yeka]". City Government of Addis Ababa.</ref>

==Geography==
The district is located in northeastern suburb of the city. It borders with the districts of [[Gullele]], [[Arada (Addis Ababa)|Arada]], [[Kirkos]] and [[Bole Medhanealem|Bole]].

==Chaka Project==
{{main article|Chaka Project}}
On 21 May 2022, Prime Minister [[Abiy Ahmed]] launched a project known as the [[Chaka Project]] that aims to clear forest areas and build national palaces, houses, artificial lakes, roads and other infrastructure. Comprising 503 hectares, it is the largest project after the [[Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam]] (GERD), costing 49 billion [[Ethiopian birr|ETB]] funded by the [[United Arab Emirates]].
