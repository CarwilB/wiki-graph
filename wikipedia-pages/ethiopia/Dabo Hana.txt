{{Short description|Woreda or district of Ethiopia}}
'''Dabo Hana''' is one of the [[woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was separated from [[Bedele (woreda)|Bedele]] woreda. The major town is [[Kone, Ethiopia|Kone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 41,285, of whom 20,471 were men and 20,814 were women; 4,169 or 10.1% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 61.66% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 29.13% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], and 7.51% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Moslem]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
