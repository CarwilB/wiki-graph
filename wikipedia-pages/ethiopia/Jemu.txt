'''Jemu''' is town and separate district in southwestern [[Ethiopia]]. Jemu serves as the administrative seat of [[West Omo Zone]] and [[Meinit Shasha]] district of [[South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region|Southwestern Ethiopia Regional State]]. The town is located at 6° 38′ 24″ N, 35° 46′ 58″ E, and its altitude is 1,500 meters above sea level.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://elevationmap.net/jemu-town-menit-shasha-bench-maji-et-1011143081|title=jemu-town-menit-shasha-bench-maji|website=elevationmap.net}}</ref>Jemu is a home of [[Me'en people]] and located in 662 km southwest from [[Addis Ababa]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://southwestcomunications.gov.et/westomo/|title=የደቡብ ምዕራብ ኢትዮጵያ ክልልን ምን ያህል ያውቃሉ|website=southwestcomunications.gov.et}}</ref>

==Infrastructures==
Jemu has infrastructures as [[Road surface|paved roads]] and electric service, public market services, hotels and restaurant services, banks and telecommunication services.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://southwestcomunications.gov.et/dsvsd/|title=ከተሞች ለነዋሪዎች ምቹ እንዲሆኑ የተጀመሩ ጥረቶች ለውጥ እያመጡ መሆናቸውን የደካ እና የጀሙ ከተማ አስተዳደር ከንቲባዎች ገለፁ|website=southwestcomunications.gov.et}}</ref>
