'''Gursum''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Somali Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Fafan Zone]], Gursum (Somali: Dagmada Gursum) is one of the districts (woredas) in the Somali Region of Ethiopia.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2017-12-01 |title=Rapid Assessment Report on IDPS settled in Gursum Woreda, Fafan zone, Somali region December, 2017 - Ethiopia {{!}} ReliefWeb |url=https://reliefweb.int/report/ethiopia/rapid-assessment-report-idps-settled-gursum-woreda-fafan-zone-somali-region-december |access-date=2026-05-09 |website=reliefweb.int |language=en}}</ref> It is part of the Fafan Zone, located in the eastern part of the region.Gursum is bordered by:North: Gursum District of the Oromia Region West: Babile District East: Jijiga District South: Goljanno District in the Somali Region<ref>{{Cite book |last=Conference |first=Ethiopian Society of Animal Production Annual |url=https://books.google.co.in/books?id=_0AeAQAAIAAJ&q=Gursum,+Somali+(woreda)&dq=Gursum,+Somali+(woreda)&hl=en&newbks=1&newbks_redir=0&source=gb_mobile_search&ovdme=1&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjyoc7XgquUAxVMzjgGHVHWJ60Q6AF6BAgPEAM#Gursum |title=Participatory Innovation and Research: Lessons for Livestock Development |date=2005 |publisher=Ethiopian Society of Animal Production |language=en}}</ref>

== Demographics ==
Gursum district has a total population of 211,593 people, of whom 105,509 are men and 106,084 are women.

Urban dwellers: 15,478 individuals, representing 7.31% of the population, which is similar to the Fafan Zone average of 6.9%.

Area: 876.57 square kilometers.

Population density: 241.4 people per square kilometer.

The district is primarily inhabited by the [[Arap]] clan, with  maturity populations of [[Arap|Muuse Celi]] and [[Arap|Subeer Celi]] and [[Arap|Mahamed Celi]]

2007 Census

The 2007 Ethiopian national census recorded a population of 27,400 people for Gursum district.
However, this figure did not include the 17 village centers of Boraale, which are largely inhabited by members of the Akisho clan.

Estimated Boraale population in 2007: 81,000 people.

Combined total for Gursum in 2007: 108,400 people (district + Boraale).

Demographics

The population of Gursum is 100% Somali.

The [[Arap]] clan makes up approximately 100% of the population, residing mainly in Bombas, Boraale, Dhagaxle and the surrounding villages. Boraale is considered the stronghold of the [[Arap]] clan, known for its fertile farmland and high population density.
