'''Wancii''', also spelled '''[[Wonchi (volcano)|Wancii]]''' in [[Oromo language|Oromo]], is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in the central [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is located in the [[Southwest Shewa Zone]]; bordered on the southwest by [[Amaya (woreda)|Amaya]], on the north by [[West Shewa Zone]] ([[Naannawa Ambo|Ambo]] district), and on the southeast by [[Waliso (woreda)|Waliso district]] and [[Goro (Aanaa)|Goro District]]. The administrative centre of Wanchi is [[Chitu, Ethiopia|Chitu]]. Dariyan and Haroo-Wancii have emerged as rapidly growing rural towns. Specially Haroo-Wancii town, which is located at a distance of 5&nbsp;km from Wancii Crater lake, is attracting the attention of many investors and booming.

The highest point in Wanchi is [[Wonchi (volcano)|Wonchi volcano]], which is about {{convert|3450|m}}<ref>{{cite web |url=http://volcano.oregonstate.edu/vwdocs/volc_images/africa/Wonchi.html |title=Wonchi, Ethiopia |website=volcano.oregonstate.edu |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081110084549/http://volcano.oregonstate.edu/vwdocs/volc_images/africa/Wonchi.html |archive-date=2008-11-10}} </ref> above sea level. The volcano contains a caldera and crater lake with islands and an ancient monastery, all which makes the caldera a popular tourist destination. Warqee (also known as [[Ensete ventricosum|Enset]]) is by far the most important staple crop in the area.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of {{formatnum:93624}}, of whom {{formatnum:46915}} were men and {{formatnum:46709}} were women; {{formatnum:1905}} or 2.04% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 70.12% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 28.41% of the population were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], and 1.28% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1], Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of {{formatnum:108550}}, of whom {{formatnum:55090}} are men and {{formatnum:53460}} are women; {{formatnum:1574}} or 1.45% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 12.3%. With an estimated area of {{formatnum:457.51}} square kilometers, Wonchi has an estimated population density of {{formatnum:237.3}} people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of {{formatnum:152.8}}.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics], Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of {{formatnum:78736}}, of whom {{formatnum:39009}} were men and {{formatnum:39727}} women; {{formatnum:880}} or 1.12% of its population were urban dwellers at the time. The two largest ethnic groups reported in Wonchi were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (98.87%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (0.84%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.29% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromo]] was spoken as a first language by 99.46%. The majority of the inhabitants professed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 93.18% of the population reporting they practiced that belief, while 5.23% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], and 1.1% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=2009-11-15 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.13, 2.16, 2.20 (accessed 6 April 2009)</ref>
