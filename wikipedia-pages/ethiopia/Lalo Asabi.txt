{{Short description|District in Oromia, Ethiopia}}
'''Lalo Asabi''' is one of the 180 [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]]s in the [[Oromia]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[West Welega Zone]], Lalo Asabi is bordered on the south by [[Yubdo (woreda)|Yubdo]], on the west by [[Ayra Guliso]], on the north by [[Boji]], on the east by the [[Benishangul-Gumuz Region]], and on the southeast by [[Gimbi (woreda)|Gimbi]]. The administrative center of this woreda is [[Inango]]; other towns in Lalo Asabi include [[Dongoro]].

== Overview ==
A survey of the land in Lalo Asabi shows that 80.39% is cultivated or arable, 5.26% pasture, 9.08% forest, and 5.26% infrastructure or other uses.<ref>Span Consultants Ltd, ''et al.'' [https://archive.today/20120221234059/http://go.worldbank.org/X1Q3J28GA0 "Mekenajo - Dembidolo Road Upgrading Project, Revised Final EIA Report"], World Bank website (March 2009), p. 28</ref> [[Coffee]] is an important cash crop of this woreda. Over 50 square kilometers are planted with this crop.<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20160815124929/http://www.oromiacoffeeunion.org/production.htm "Coffee Production"]}} Oromia Coffee Cooperative Union website</ref>

There are 22 primary schools, and 2 secondary education schools in this woreda. [[Health in Ethiopia|Health services]] are provided by two clinics, and eight health posts; most of these facilities are located in urban areas.<ref>"Mekenajo - Dembidolo Road Upgrading Project", pp. 30f</ref>

== Demographics ==
At the 2007 national census, this woreda's population was 75,584 in 15,164 households, of whom 37,086 were men and 38,498 women; 10,370 or 13.71% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants (85.83%) observed [[P'ent'ay|Protestantism]], while 12.79% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 1.08% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 82,987, of whom 42,845 are men and 40,142 are women; 10,090 or 12.16% of its population are urban dwellers, which is greater than the Zone average of 10.9%. With an estimated area of 376.57 square kilometers, Lalo Asabi has an estimated population density of 220.4 people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of 91.7.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080731152750/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=2008-07-31 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 58,695 in 9,526 households, of whom 28,473 were men and 30,222 were women; 5,647 or 9.62% of its population were urban dwellers. The two largest ethnic groups reported in Lalo Asabi were the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (98.39%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (1.27%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.34% of the population. [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]] was spoken as a first language by 98.64%, and 1.09% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 0.27% spoke all other primary languages reported. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], with 64.51% reporting that as their religion, while 32.84% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 1.28% [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20091115040247/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf |date=2009-11-15 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.16, 2.20, Annex II.2 (accessed 9 April 2009)</ref>
