'''Kori''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia]], or ''woreda'', in the [[Afar Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 1 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 1]], Kori is bordered on the south by [[Dubti (Ethiopian District)|Dubti]], on the west the [[Administrative Zone 4 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 4]], on the north by the [[Administrative Zone 2 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 2]], on the east by [[Elidar (Ethiopian District)|Elidar]]. The administrative center is [[Ayrorta]].

Kori was created by a decision of the Afar Regional Cabinet on 4 February 2007, from the northern part of [[Dubti (Ethiopian District)|Dubti]].<ref>[http://www.afarfriends.org/Dok%20t%20websida/APDA/apda20070204.pdf "Three new woredas decided in Afar Region"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20140729214201/http://www.afarfriends.org/Dok%20t%20websida/APDA/apda20070204.pdf |date=2014-07-29 }}</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 30,652, of whom 16,821 are men and 13,831 women; with an area of 2,869.56 square kilometers, Kori has a population density of 10.68. While 0 or 0.00% are urban inhabitants, a further 16,951 or 55.30% are pastoralists. A total of 5,024 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 6.1 persons to a household, and 5,245 housing units. 99.8% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
