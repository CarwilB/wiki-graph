'''Damot Pulasa''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]] in [[South Ethiopia Regional State|South Ethiopia]], Part of the [[Wolayita Zone]]. Damot Pulasa is bordered on the east and south by [[Damot Gale]], on the west by the [[Boloso Sore]], and on the north by the [[Hadiya Zone]]. Damot Pulasa was separated from Damo Gale district. The administrative center of the district is [[Shanto, Wolaita|Shanto]].

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2019 population projection conducted by the CSA,<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |title=Projected population of Ethiopia |access-date=2021-07-28 |archive-date=2021-07-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210728101857/https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> this woreda has a total population of 135,760, of whom 66,463 are men and 69,297 women; 5,346 or 5.08% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 73.72% of the population reporting that belief, 17.1% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Roman Catholic]], and 8.17% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
