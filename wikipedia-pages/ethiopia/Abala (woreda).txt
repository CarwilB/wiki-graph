'''Abala''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Afar Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 2 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 2]], Abala is located at the base of the eastern escarpment of the [[Ethiopian Highlands]], and bordered on the south by [[Megale]], on the west by the [[Tigray Region]], on the north by [[Berhale (woreda)|Berhale]], on the northeast by [[Afdera (woreda)|Afdera]], and on the east by [[Erebti]]. The major town in Abala is [[Abala, Abala, Ethiopia|Abala]].

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 37,963, of whom 20,486 are men and 17,477 women; with an area of 1,188.72 square kilometers, Abala has a population density of 31.94. While 10,301 or 27.13% are urban inhabitants, a further 5,552 or 14.62% are pastoralists. A total of 6,703 households were counted in this woreda, which results in an average of 5.7 persons to a household, and 6,855 housing units. 80.53% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 19.35% were [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity|Orthodox Christians]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref> The predominant ethnic group in this woreda are the [[Afar people|Afar]].
