'''Shebedino''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Sidama Zone]] located in the [[Great Rift Valley, Ethiopia|Great Rift Valley]], Shebedino is bordered on the south by [[Dale (woreda)|Dale]], on the west by [[Boricha (woreda)|Boricha]], on the north by [[Awasa Zuria]], on the east by [[Gorche]], and on the southeast by [[Wensho]]. Towns in Shebedino include [[Leku, Ethiopia|Leku]]. Boricha and Gorche woredas were separated from Shebedino woreda.

According to a 2004 report, Shebedino had 17 kilometers of asphalt roads, 50 kilometers of all-weather roads and 58 kilometers of dry-weather roads, for an average road density of 121 kilometers per 1000 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls "Detailed statistics on roads"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151415/http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls |date=2011-07-20 }}, SNNPR Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 15 September 2009)</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 233,922, of whom 118,026 are men and 115,896 women; 11,831 or 5.06% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 81.94% of the population reporting that belief, 8.61% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 4.31% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 2.75% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region], Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

In the 1994 Census, this woreda had a population of 420,976, of whom 214,000 were men and 206,976 women; 10,669 or 2.53% of its population were urban dwellers. The four largest ethnic groups reported in Shebedino were the [[Sidama people|Sidama]] (91.43%), the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (2.67%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (2.3%), and the [[Welayta people|Welayta]] (2.16%); all other ethnic groups made up 1.44% of the population. [[Sidamo language|Sidamo]] is spoken as a first language by 93.36% of the inhabitants, 2.25% speak [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]], 2.13% [[Amharic language|Amharic]], and 1.74% [[Welayta language|Welayta]]; the remaining 0.28% spoke all other primary languages reported. 61.74% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], 15.46% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 8.6% observed traditional religions, 6.24% embraced [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholicism]], and 4.45% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1], Tables 2.1, 2.7, 2.11, 2.15, 2.19 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref>
