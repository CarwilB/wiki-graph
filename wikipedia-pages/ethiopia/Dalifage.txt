'''Dalifage''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia]], or ''woredas'', in the [[Afar Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Administrative Zone 5 (Afar)|Administrative Zone 5]], Dalifage is a triangle-shaped district located near the base of the eastern escarpment of the [[Ethiopian Highlands]], and bordered on the south by [[Hadele Ele|Hadale'ela]], on the west by the [[Amhara Region]], and on the north by the [[Borkana River]] which separates it from [[Dewe (woreda)|Dewe]].

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] of Ethiopia (CSA), this woreda has a total population of 36,154, of whom 21,372 are men and 14,782 women. While 4,979 or 13.77% are urban inhabitants, a further 2,088 or 5.78% are pastoralists. 99.03% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Afar Region] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113215726/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=267&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2012 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.4, 2.5, 3.1, 3.2 and 3.4.</ref>
