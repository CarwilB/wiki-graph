'''Loodee Heexosaa''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|aanaa]]s in [[Oromia Regional State]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Arsi Zone]]. It was separated from [[Hitosa|Heexosaa]] Aanaa. [[Huruta]] is the main town of Aanaa.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 107,133, of whom 53,522 were men and 53,611 were women; 15,298 or 14.28% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 53.49% of the population reporting they observed this belief, while 45.22% of the population were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 1.22% of the population were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
