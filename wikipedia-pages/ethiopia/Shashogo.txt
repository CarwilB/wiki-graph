'''Shashogo''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Hadiya Zone]], Shashogo is bordered on the south by the [[Kembata Tembaro Zone]], on the west by [[Limo (woreda)|Limo]], on the northwest by [[Ana Lemo]], on the northeast by the [[Silt'e Zone]], and on the southeast by the [[Alaba special woreda]].

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 103,722, of whom 52,435 are men and 51,287 women; 8,219 or 7.92% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 51.96% of the population reporting that belief, 42.48% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 4.96% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region], Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
