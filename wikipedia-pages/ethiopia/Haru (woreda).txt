{{Short description|District of Oromia Region, Ethiopia}}
'''Haru''' is one of the 180 [[Aanaa]]s in [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[West Welega Zone]], Haru is bordered on the south by [[Nole Kaba]], on the southwest by [[Dale Lalo]], on the west by [[Yubdo (woreda)|Yubdo]], on the north by [[Gimbi (woreda)|Gimbi]], on the east by an exclave of the [[Benishangul-Gumuz Region]], and on the southeast by the [[Illubabor Zone]]. Towns in Haru include [[Guyi, Ethiopia|Guyi]], Chonge and [[Guyi Abo]].

[[Coffee]] is an important [[cash crop]] of this woreda. Over 50 square kilometers are planted with this crop.<ref>{{usurped|1=[https://web.archive.org/web/20160815124929/http://www.oromiacoffeeunion.org/production.htm "Coffee Production"]}} Oromia Coffee Cooperative Union website</ref>

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 67,262 in 13,332 households, of whom 33,178 were men and 34,084 women; 4,870 or 7.24% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants observed [[P'ent'ay|Protestantism]], with 72.09% reporting that as their religion, while 25.06% observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], and 2.42% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>

Based on figures published by the [[Central Statistical Agency (Ethiopia)|Central Statistical Agency]] in 2005, this woreda has an estimated total population of 74,657, of whom 38,375 are men and 36,282 are women; 5,988 or 8.02% of its population are urban dwellers, which is less than the Zone average of 10.9%. With an estimated area of 448.75 square kilometers, Haru has an estimated population density of 166.4 people per square kilometer, which is greater than the Zone average of 91.7.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm CSA 2005 National Statistics] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20070813054932/http://www.csa.gov.et/text_files/2005_national_statistics.htm |date=2007-08-13 }}, Tables B.3 and B.4</ref>

The 1994 national census reported this  woreda's population at 53,324 in 9,492 households, of whom 26,031 were men and 27,293 women; 3,359 or 6.3% of its population were urban dwellers. The largest ethnic groups reported in Haru was the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (99.26%). [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]] was spoken as the first language by 99.61%. The majority of the inhabitants observed [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 54.33% reporting that as their religion, while 40.21% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestant]], 2.95% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 1.81% observed traditional beliefs.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck04%5Ck04_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119200333/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs/report/Statistical_Report/k04/k04_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.16, 2.20, Annex II.2 (accessed 9 April 2009)</ref>
