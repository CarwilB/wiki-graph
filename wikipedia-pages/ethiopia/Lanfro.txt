'''Lanfro''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Until it was included into the Silte Zone, Lanfro formed part of the [[Gurage Zone]]. Lanfro is bordered on the south by the [[Alaba special woreda]], on the southwest by [[Sankurra]], on the west by [[Dalocha (woreda)|Dalocha]], on the north by [[Silte (woreda)|Silte]], and on the east by the [[Oromia Region]]. The major town in Lanfro is [[Torra]].

== Overview ==
Cash crops in Lanfro include pepper, which accounts for up to 60% of the cash income for many households, and [[teff]]. Butter is also an important income source, which can account for as much as 10% of the income for poor households.<ref>[http://www.fews.net/livelihood/et/Baseline.pdf "Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region, Ethiopia Livelihood Profiles: January 2006"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110723130716/http://www.fews.net/livelihood/et/Baseline.pdf |date=2011-07-23 }}, USAID/FEWSNET, p. 20 (accessed 11 January 2011)</ref> This woreda has 7 kilometers of all-weather roads and 3 kilometers of dry-weather roads, for an average road density of 19 kilometers per 1000 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls " Detailed statistics on roads"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151415/http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls |date=2011-07-20 }}, SNNPR Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 3 September 2009)</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 116,114, of whom 58,848 are men and 57,266 women; 12,457 or 10.71% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 97.62% of the population reporting that belief, and 1.93% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 80,072 of whom 40,145 were males and 39,927 were females; 5,096 or 6.36% of its population were urban dwellers. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Lanfro were the Silte (89.92%), the [[Alaba people|Alaba]] (7.1%), and the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (1.46%); all other ethnic groups made up 1.52% of the population. [[Silte language|Silte]] was spoken as a first language by 89.98%, 7.05% [[Alaba language|Alaba]], and 1.82% spoke [[Amharic language|Amharic]]; the remaining 1.15% spoke all other primary languages reported. 97.57% of the population said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 2.32% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.15, 2.19 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref> Concerning [[Education in Ethiopia|education]], 10.41% of the population were considered literate, which is less than the Zone average of 20.62%.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partII.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia'', Vol. 1, part 2] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119202355/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partII.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Table 3.7 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref> Concerning [[Water supply and sanitation in Ethiopia|sanitary conditions]], 64.65% of the urban houses and 9.81% of all houses had access to safe drinking water at the time of the census; 27.93% of the urban and 4.11% of all houses had toilet facilities.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partIV.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia'', Vol. 1, part 4] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119193032/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partIV.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 6.11, 6.13 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref>
