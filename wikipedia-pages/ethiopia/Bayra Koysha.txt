'''Bayra Koysha''' is the woredas in [[Wolayita Zone]] of the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]] in Ethiopia.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://wolaitatimes.com.et/?p=323|title=የዎላይታ ልማት ማህበር የንፁህ መጠጥ ውሃ ችግር ባለበት አከባቢ ያስገነባው የንጹህ መጠጥ ውሃ ለአገልግሎት ዝግጁ ሆነ – Wolaita Times|access-date=2021-09-07|archive-date=2021-09-07|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210907133436/https://wolaitatimes.com.et/?p=323|url-status=dead}}</ref> Bayra Koysha was established in 2019 from the other surrounding woredas. Bayra Koysha is bordered by [[Sodo Zuria]] on east, [[Damot Sore]] on north, [[Kawo Koysha]] on west and on south [[Humbo]] woredas.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23|title=Weredas&#124;&#124;Towns &#124; Wolaita Zone Administrations|access-date=2021-09-07|archive-date=2023-03-01|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230301123037/http://snnprswolaitazone.gov.et/node/23|url-status=dead}}</ref> The administrative town of this woreda is 
[[Beklo Segno]].
