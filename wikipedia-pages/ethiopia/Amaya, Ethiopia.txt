'''Amaya''' or Ameya is town and separate district in southwestern [[Ethiopia]]. Amaya serves as the administrative seat of [[Konta Zone]] and [[Amaya Zuria]] district of [[South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region|Southwestern Ethiopia Regional State]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://www.ena.et/web/amh/w/amh_6134083|title=የሴቶችን ተጠቃማነት በማሳደግ አካታችና ሁለንተዊ ብልፅግናን ለማረጋገጥ የተጀመሩ ስራዎች ተጠናክረው ይቀጥላል|website=www.ena.et}}</ref>
Amaya is a home of [[Konta people]] and located in 467 km southwest from [[Addis Ababa]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://southwestcomunications.gov.et/konta/|title=የኮንታ ዞን መሠረታዊ ዳራ|website=southwestcomunications.gov.et}}</ref>

==Infrastructures==
Amaya has various infrastructures; such as pure drinking water, health institutions and electric service, public market services, hotels and restaurant services, banks and telecommunication services.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://imisethiopia.gov.et/redatam/pryesp/SWsisppi/Abstract2014.pdf|title=SWEPR Finance & Economic Development Bureau Annual Statistical Abstract 2022|website=imisethiopia.gov.et}}</ref>
