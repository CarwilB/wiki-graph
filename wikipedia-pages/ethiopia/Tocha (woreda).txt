'''Tocha''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[South West Ethiopia Peoples' Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Part of the [[Dawro Zone]], Tocha is bordered on the south by [[Isara (woreda)|Isara]] and Kechi woreda, on the west by the Kechi and Tarcha zuria woreda, on the north by Tarcha zuria woreda and on the east by [[Mareka]]. Towns in Tocha include [[Tocha, Ethiopia|Tocha]]. Tocha was disintegrated in to Kechi and Tarcha zuria woredas.

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 102,848, of whom 52,481 are men and 50,367 women; 6,614 or 6.43% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 51.31% of the population reporting that belief, 44.35% were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], and 3.71% practiced traditional beliefs.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region], Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>
