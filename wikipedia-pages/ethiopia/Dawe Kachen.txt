{{Short description|Ethiopian administrative division}}
'''Dawe Kachen''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s (an administrative division) in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of [[Goro, Oromia (woreda)|Goro]] woreda. It is part of the [[Bale Zone]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 30,849, of whom 16,181 were men and 14,668 were women; 693 or 2.25% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], with 99.47% of the population reporting they observed this belief.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive |url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=November 13, 2011 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
