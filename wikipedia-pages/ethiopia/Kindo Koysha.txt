'''Kindo Koysha''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|district]] in the [[South Ethiopia Regional State]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is a Part of the [[Wolayita Zone]], Kindo Koysha is bordered on the south by [[Offa (woreda)|Offa]], on the southwest by [[Kindo Didaye]], on the west by the [[Dawro Zone]], on the north by [[Boloso Bombe]], on the west by [[Damot Sore]], on the southwest by [[Kindo Didaye]] and on the southeast by [[Bayra Koysha]]. The administrative center of Kindo Koysha is [[Bale Hawassa]]. Kindo Didaye woreda was separated from Kindo Koysha.

According to a 2004 report, Kindo Koysha had 86 kilometers of all-weather roads and 39 kilometers of dry-weather roads, for an average road density of 161 kilometers per 1000 square kilometers.<ref>[http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls "Detailed statistics on roads"] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110720151415/http://www.snnprbofed.gov.et/Reports/Roads.xls |date=2011-07-20 }}, SNNPR Bureau of Finance and Economic Development website (accessed 15 September 2009)</ref>

== Demographics ==
Based on the 2019 population projection conducted by the CSA,<ref>{{Cite web |url=https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |title=Projected population of Ethiopia |access-date=2021-07-28 |archive-date=2021-07-28 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210728101857/https://www.statsethiopia.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Projected-Population-of-Ethiopia-20112019.pdf |url-status=dead }}</ref> this woreda has a total population of 136,412, of whom 66,546 are men and 69,866 women; 6,590 or 6.3% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 79.82% of the population reporting that belief, 16.73% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 1.52% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]], and 1.18% observed traditional religions.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

The 1994 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 140,687 of whom 69,980 were men and 70,707 were women; 3,606 or 2.56% of its population were urban dwellers. The largest ethnic group reported in Damot Weyde was the [[Welayta people|Welayta]] (99.46%); all other ethnic groups made up 0.54% of the population. [[Welayta language|Welayta]] was the dominant first language, spoken by 99.66% of the inhabitants; the remaining 0.34% spoke all other primary languages reported. Concerning religious beliefs, the 1994 census reported that 39.74% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], 32.49% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 13.83% observed traditional religions, 12.86% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 1.08% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Roman Catholic]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.15, 2.19 (Retrieved 30 December 2008)</ref>
