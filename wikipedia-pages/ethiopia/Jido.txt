'''Jido''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]] in [[Oromia Region]], [[Ethiopia]]. It was part of former [[Wuchalena Jido]] woreda. Part of the [[Kaba Shewa Zone (Oromia)|Kaba Shewa Zone]], Jido is bordered on the north west by [[Wuchale (woreda)|Wuchale]], on the north by [[Amhara Region]], on the north east by [[Abichu (woreda)|Abichu]], on the east by [[Kembibit]] and on the south by [[Bereh]] and [[Aleltu]].

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 53,658, of whom 26,889 were men and 26,769 were women; none of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants said they practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], with 97.58% of the population reporting they practised that belief, and 1.6% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
