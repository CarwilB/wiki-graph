{{Short description|District in Oromia Region, Ethiopia}}
'''Guna''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Oromia Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. It is part of the [[Arsi Zone]]. It was separated from the [[Merti]] woreda{{When|date=May 2026}}.

== Demographics ==
The 2007 national census reported a total population for this woreda of 76,365, of whom 38,481 were men and 37,884 were women; 5,558 or 7.28% of its population were urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants, 59.89%, said they were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]] while 39.99% of the population practised [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 ''2007 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Oromia Region'', Vol. 1] {{Webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20111113202722/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=272&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2011-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.5, 3.4 (accessed 13 January 2012)</ref>
