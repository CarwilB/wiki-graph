'''Araarso''' is a [[Districts of Ethiopia|town]] of [[Somali Region]] in [[Ethiopia]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://rasaasa.com/?page_id=6986|title=The Somali Regional State of Ethiopia [Ogaden]|website=rasaasa news|accessdate=7 June 2018|archive-date=12 June 2018|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180612141141/http://rasaasa.com/?page_id=6986|url-status=dead}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=http://allafrica.com/stories/201412293279.html|title=Somalia: The Ogaden Region of Ethiopia - the Source of Heroes, Heroines and Scholars of Somalia|website=allafrica|access-date=7 June 2018}}</ref>

It is about 70 kilometers southeast of Jijiga.
