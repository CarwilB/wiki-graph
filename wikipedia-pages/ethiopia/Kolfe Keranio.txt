'''Kolfe Keranio''' ([[Amharic]]: ኮልፌ ቀራኒዮ ክፍለ ከተማ), also spelled '''Kolfe Keraneo''' or simply '''Kolfe''', is a [[Districts of Addis Ababa|district]] (sub-city) of [[Addis Ababa]], [[Ethiopia]]. As of 2011 its population was 546,219.<ref name=a/>

==Geography==
The district is located in the western suburb of the city, near the [[Gefersa Reservoir]]. It borders the districts of [[Gullele]], [[Addis Ketema]], [[Lideta]] and [[Nifas Silk-Lafto]].
