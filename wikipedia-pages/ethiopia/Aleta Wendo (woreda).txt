'''Aleta Wendo''' is one of the [[Districts of Ethiopia|woreda]]s in the [[Sidama Region]] of [[Ethiopia]]. Aleta Wendo is bordered on the south by [[Dara (woreda)|Dara]], on the west by [[Chuko (woreda)|Chuko]], on the north by [[Dale (woreda)|Dale]] and [[Wensho]], on the east by [[Bursa (woreda)|Bursa]], and on the southeast by [[Hula (woreda)|Hula]]. The administrative center is [[Aleta Wendo]]. Chuko woreda was separated from Aleta Wendo.

== Population ==
Based on the 2007 Census conducted by the CSA, this woreda has a total population of 188,976, of whom 96,640 are men and 92,336 women; 22,093 or 11.69% of its population are urban dwellers. The majority of the inhabitants were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], with 72.78% of the population reporting that belief, 7.38% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 3.91% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], 3.65% were [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholic]], and 3.54% observed traditional religions.
<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 Census 2007 Tables: Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20121113220306/http://www.csa.gov.et/index.php?option=com_rubberdoc&view=doc&id=273&format=raw&Itemid=521 |date=2012-11-13 }}, Tables 2.1, and 3.4.</ref>

In the 1994 Census this woreda had a population of 271,446, of whom 139,213 were men and 132,233 women; 15,904 or 5.86% of its population were urban dwellers. The three largest ethnic groups reported in Aleta Wendo were the [[Sidama people|Sidama]] (92.14%), the [[Amhara people|Amhara]] (4.36%), and the [[Oromo people|Oromo]] (0.92%); all other ethnic groups made up 2.58% of the population. [[Sidamo language|Sidamo]] is spoken as a first language by 94.12% of the inhabitants, 4.73% speak [[Amharic language|Amharic]], and 0.26% [[Oromo language|Oromiffa]]; the remaining 0.89% spoke all other primary languages reported. 61.57% of the population said they were [[P'ent'ay|Protestants]], 17.31% observed traditional religions, 8.82% practiced [[Ethiopian Orthodox Christianity]], 5.21% were [[Islam in Ethiopia|Muslim]], and 4.15% embraced [[Roman Catholicism in Ethiopia|Catholicism]].<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples'Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.7, 2.11, 2.15, 2.19 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref> Concerning [[Education in Ethiopia|education]], 36.98% of the population were considered literate; statistics on school attendance in this woreda are missing. Concerning [[Water supply and sanitation in Ethiopia|sanitary conditions]], at the time of the 1994 national census about 91.29% of the urban houses and 24.17% of all houses had access to safe drinking water at the time of the census, while about 76.50% of the urban and 12.83% of the total had toilet facilities.<ref>[http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf ''1994 Population and Housing Census of Ethiopia: Results for Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region'', Vol. 1, part 1] {{webarchive|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20081119232405/http://www.csa.gov.et/surveys/Population%20and%20Housing%20Census%201994/survey0/data/docs%5Creport%5CStatistical_Report%5Ck07%5Ck07_partI.pdf |date=2008-11-19 }}, Tables 2.1, 2.12, 2.19, 3.5, 3.7, 6.3, 6.11, 6.13 (accessed 30 December 2008)</ref> However, according to a 2004 survey, none of the inhabitants have access to drinkable water; as a result 20% use unprotected river water, 60% unprotected springs, 1% unprotected ponds, and 19% unprotected wells.<ref>''Final Report'', p. 71</ref>
