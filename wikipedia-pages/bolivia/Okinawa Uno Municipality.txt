'''Okinawa Uno''', is a [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipality]] of [[Bolivia]], located in [[Ignacio Warnes Province]] in [[Santa Cruz Department (Bolivia)|Santa Cruz Department]]. Its shares its name with the town of [[Okinawa Uno|Okinawa I]], which lies between the [[Río Grande (Bolivia)|Río Grande]] to the east and the Pailón River to the west. The municipality has a population of 10,508 inhabitants, according to the [[2024 Bolivian census]].<ref name="ine">{{cite web |date=2014 |title=Listado de Comunidades |url=http://fichacomunidad.ine.gob.bo/ |access-date=12 Jan 2023 |publisher=[[National Institute of Statistics of Bolivia]] |language=es}}<!-- auto-translated by Module:CS1 translator --></ref>

== History ==
The town of Okinawa I was established by [[Okinawan diaspora|Okinawan immigrants]] after the end of the [[Second World War]], and during its peak in the mid-1960s consisted of 565 families and over 3,000 Okinawans in total.{{Sfnp|Miyahira|Petrucci|2015|p=557}}<ref>{{Cite web |last=Mar 2014 |first=Akemi Kikumura Yano / 14 |title=Bolivia - Migration Historical Overview |url=http://www.discovernikkei.org/en/journal/2014/3/14/bolivia/ |access-date=2023-01-12 |website=Discover Nikkei |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Okinawa, Bolivia |url=https://theworld.org/stories/2009-07-06/okinawa-bolivia |access-date=2023-01-12 |website=The World from PRX |language=en}}</ref>

The municipality was established on 6 April 1991, separating it from [[Warnes Municipality]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=1991 |title=Bolivia: Ley Nº 1837, 6 de abril de 1991 |url=https://www.lexivox.org/norms/BO-L-1837.html |access-date=2026-05-11}}</ref>

== Cantons and communities ==
Okinawa Uno is divided into two cantons at its creation: Okinawa Uno and Ignacia Zeballos, with Okinawa Uno and Nuevo Horizonte as their respective capitals.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1991 |title=Bolivia: Ley Nº 1837, 6 de abril de 1991 |url=https://www.lexivox.org/norms/BO-L-1837.html |access-date=2026-05-11}}</ref> They contain the following communities.<ref>{{Cite |last=Instituto Nacional de Estadística |title=Bolivia- Encuesta de Presupuestos Familiares 2015-2016: Clasificaciones |date=2015 |url=https://catalog.ihsn.org/catalog/8033/related-materials |location=https://catalog.ihsn.org//catalog/8033/download/88221 |publisher=International Household Survey Network}}</ref>

Okinawa Uno Canton includes the communities of:

* Nueva Esperanza
* El Carmen
* El Retiro
* Puerto Perez
* Valle Chico Los Limones
* Junta Pailon
* Las Mercedes
* Okinawa 2
* Puerto Nuevo
* Rancho Chico
* Los Angeles
* Monte Verde
* Nueva Aurora
* Okinawa 1
* San Antonio
* Sumuque
* Villa Reyes

Ignacia Zeballos Canton includes the communities of:

* Nuevo Horizonte
* Virgen De Guadalupe
* Okinawa 3
* San Miguel
* San Miguel II
