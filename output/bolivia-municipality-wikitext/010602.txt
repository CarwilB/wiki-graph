'''Yamparáez Municipality''' is the second [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipal section]] of the [[Yamparáez Province]] in the [[Chuquisaca Department]] in [[Bolivia]]. Yamparáez Municipality is the smaller and less populous of the two municipalities in [[Yamparáez Province]].

==Government==
The Mayor of Yamparáez is Grover Yucra Arancibia, who was elected in the [[2026 Bolivian regional elections|March 2026 elections]].<ref>{{Cite report| publisher = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last1 = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last2 = Tribunal Supremo Electoral| title = Publicación de resultados Elecciones Subnacionales 2026: Elección de Autoridades Políticas Departamentales Regionales y Municipales 2026 (Primera y Segunda Vuelta Electoral)| series = Separata de información pública| date = 2026-04-23 | page = 26 }}</ref>

Yamparáez has a municipal council with five seats, as do all municipalities with populations up to 15,000.<ref>{{Cite news| title = A más población, los municipios tienen más concejales, según la ley| work = Los Tiempos| access-date = 2026-06-19| date = 2026-02-08| url = https://www.lostiempos.com/actualidad/pais/20260208/mas-poblacion-municipios-tienen-mas-concejales-ley}}</ref>
