'''Shinahota Municipality''' is the second [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipal section]] of the [[Tiraque Province]] in the [[Cochabamba Department]] in [[Bolivia]]. Shinahota Municipality is the more populous of the two municipalities in [[Tiraque Province]], but it covers a smaller area.

==Government==
The Mayor of Shinahota is Luis Arce Clemente, who was elected in the [[2026 Bolivian regional elections|March 2026 elections]].<ref>{{Cite report| publisher = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last1 = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last2 = Tribunal Supremo Electoral| title = Publicación de resultados Elecciones Subnacionales 2026: Elección de Autoridades Políticas Departamentales Regionales y Municipales 2026 (Primera y Segunda Vuelta Electoral)| series = Separata de información pública| date = 2026-04-23 | page = 51 }}</ref>

Shinahota has a municipal council with seven seats, as do all municipalities with populations between 15,001 and 50,000.<ref>{{Cite news| title = A más población, los municipios tienen más concejales, según la ley| work = Los Tiempos| access-date = 2026-06-19| date = 2026-02-08| url = https://www.lostiempos.com/actualidad/pais/20260208/mas-poblacion-municipios-tienen-mas-concejales-ley}}</ref>
