'''San Julián Municipality''' is the fourth [[Municipalities of Bolivia|municipal section]] of the [[Ñuflo de Chaves Province]] in the [[Santa Cruz Department]] in [[Bolivia]]. It is the most populous municipality in [[Ñuflo de Chaves Province]].<ref>{{Cite| last = Instituto Nacional de Estadística| title = Población censada e indicadores demográficos: Tabulados por Municipio/TIOC| url = https://nube.ine.gob.bo/index.php/s/0OrfPjzMB16fATQ/download| date = 2025}}</ref>

==Government==
The Mayor of San Julián is Carlos Vaca García, who was elected in the [[2026 Bolivian regional elections|March 2026 elections]].<ref>{{Cite report| publisher = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last1 = Órgano Electoral Plurinacional| last2 = Tribunal Supremo Electoral| title = Publicación de resultados Elecciones Subnacionales 2026: Elección de Autoridades Políticas Departamentales Regionales y Municipales 2026 (Primera y Segunda Vuelta Electoral)| series = Separata de información pública| date = 2026-04-23 | page = 74 }}</ref>

San Julián has a municipal council with seven seats, as do all municipalities with populations between 15,001 and 50,000.<ref>{{Cite news| title = A más población, los municipios tienen más concejales, según la ley| work = Los Tiempos| access-date = 2026-06-19| date = 2026-02-08| url = https://www.lostiempos.com/actualidad/pais/20260208/mas-poblacion-municipios-tienen-mas-concejales-ley}}</ref> One of those seats is an Indigenous seat assigned by the Guaraní according to its own norms and procedures.
